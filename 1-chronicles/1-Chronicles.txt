1 CHRONICLES 2

2:1  LA: Filii autem Israël : Ruben, Simeon, Levi, Juda, Issachar, et Zabulon,
     EN: The sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun,

2:2  LA: Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Nephthali, Gad, et Aser.
     EN: Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.

2:3  LA: Filii Juda : Her, Onan, et Sela : hi tres nati sunt ei de filia Sue Chananitide. Fuit autem Her primogenitus Juda malus coram Domino, et occidit eum.
     EN: The sons of Judah: Er, Onan, and Shelah. These three were born to him by the daughter of Shua, a Canaanite woman. Er, Judah's firstborn, was evil before the Lord, and He killed him.

2:4  LA: Thamar autem nurus ejus peperit ei Phares et Zara : omnes ergo filii Juda, quinque.
     EN: Tamar his daughter-in-law bore him Perez and Zerah. Thus Judah had five sons in all.

2:5  LA: Filii autem Phares : Hesron et Hamul.
     EN: The sons of Perez: Hezron and Hamul.

2:6  LA: Filii quoque Zaræ : Zamri, et Ethan, et Eman, Chalchal quoque, et Dara, simul quinque.
     EN: The sons of Zerah: Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calcol, and Dara, five in all.

2:7  LA: Filii Charmi : Achar, qui turbavit Israël, et peccavit in furto anathematis.
     EN: The sons of Carmi: Achar, who troubled Israel and sinned by stealing what was under the ban.

2:8  LA: Filii Ethan : Azarias.
     EN: The sons of Ethan: Azariah.

2:9  LA: Filii autem Hesron qui nati sunt ei : Jerameel, et Ram, et Calubi.
     EN: The sons born to Hezron: Jerahmeel, Ram, and Chelubai.

2:10  LA: Porro Ram genuit Aminadab. Aminadab autem genuit Nahasson, principem filiorum Juda.
      EN: Ram fathered Amminadab. Amminadab fathered Nahshon, leader of the children of Judah.

2:11  LA: Nahasson quoque genuit Salma, de quo ortus est Booz.
      EN: Nahshon fathered Salma, from whom Boaz was born.

2:12  LA: Booz vero genuit Obed, qui et ipse genuit Isai.
      EN: Boaz fathered Obed, who in turn fathered Jesse.

2:13  LA: Isai autem genuit primogenitum Eliab, secundum Abinadab, tertium Simmaa,
      EN: Jesse fathered Eliab his firstborn, Abinadab the second, Shimea the third,

2:14  LA: quartum Nathanaël, quintum Raddai,
      EN: Nethanel the fourth, Raddai the fifth,

2:15  LA: sextum Asom, septimum David.
      EN: Ozem the sixth, and David the seventh.

2:16  LA: Quorum sorores fuerunt Sarvia et Abigail. Filii Sarviæ : Abisai, Joab, et Asaël, tres.
      EN: Their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. Zeruiah's sons were Abishai, Joab, and Asahel, three.

2:17  LA: Abigail autem genuit Amasa, cujus pater fuit Jether Ismahelites.
      EN: Abigail bore Amasa, whose father was Jether the Ishmaelite.

2:18  LA: Caleb vero filius Hesron accepit uxorem nomine Azuba, de qua genuit Jerioth : fueruntque filii ejus Jaser, et Sobab, et Ardon.
      EN: Caleb son of Hezron took a wife named Azubah, by whom he fathered Jerioth. His sons were Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon.

2:19  LA: Cumque mortua fuisset Azuba, accepit uxorem Caleb Ephratha, quæ peperit ei Hur.
      EN: When Azubah died, Caleb took Ephrath as his wife, and she bore him Hur.

2:20  LA: Porro Hur genuit Uri, et Uri genuit Bezeleel.
      EN: Hur fathered Uri, and Uri fathered Bezalel.

2:21  LA: Post hæc ingressus est Hesron ad filiam Machir patris Galaad, et accepit eam cum esset annorum sexaginta : quæ peperit ei Segub.
      EN: After this Hezron went in to the daughter of Machir, Gilead's father, and married her when he was sixty years old. She bore him Segub.

2:22  LA: Sed et Segub genuit Jair, et possedit viginti tres civitates in terra Galaad.
      EN: Segub fathered Jair, who possessed twenty-three cities in the land of Gilead.

2:23  LA: Cepitque Gessur et Aram oppida Jair, et Canath, et viculos ejus sexaginta civitatum : omnes isti filii Machir patris Galaad.
      EN: Geshur and Aram took Jair's towns, along with Kenath and its villages, sixty cities. All these were sons of Machir, Gilead's father.

2:24  LA: Cum autem mortuus esset Hesron, ingressus est Caleb ad Ephratha. Habuit quoque Hesron uxorem Abia, quæ peperit ei Assur patrem Thecuæ.
      EN: After Hezron died, Caleb went in to Ephrath. Hezron also had a wife, Abijah, who bore him Ashhur, Tekoa's father.

2:25  LA: Nati sunt autem filii Jerameel primogeniti Hesron, Ram primogenitus ejus, et Buna, et Aram, et Asom, et Achia.
      EN: The sons born to Jerahmeel, Hezron's firstborn, were Ram his firstborn, Bunah, Aram, Ozem, and Ahijah.

2:26  LA: Duxit quoque uxorem alteram Jerameel, nomine Atara, quæ fuit mater Onam.
      EN: Jerahmeel also took another wife, named Atarah, who was Onam's mother.

2:27  LA: Sed et filii Ram primogeniti Jerameel fuerunt Moos, Jamin, et Achar.
      EN: The sons of Ram, Jerahmeel's firstborn, were Maaz, Jamin, and Eker.

2:28  LA: Onam autem habuit filios Semei et Jada. Filii autem Semei : Nadab et Abisur.
      EN: Onam had sons, Shammai and Jada. Shammai's sons were Nadab and Abishur.

2:29  LA: Nomen vero uxoris Abisur, Abihail, quæ peperit ei Ahobban et Molid.
      EN: Abishur's wife was named Abihail; she bore him Ahban and Molid.

2:30  LA: Filii autem Nadab fuerunt Saled et Apphaim. Mortuus est autem Saled absque liberis.
      EN: Nadab's sons were Seled and Appaim. Seled died without children.

2:31  LA: Filius vero Apphaim, Jesi : qui Jesi genuit Sesan. Porro Sesan genuit Oholai.
      EN: Appaim's son was Ishi, who fathered Sheshan. Sheshan fathered Ahlai.

2:32  LA: Filii autem Jada fratris Semei : Jether, et Jonathan. Sed et Jether mortuus est absque liberis.
      EN: The sons of Jada, Shammai's brother, were Jether and Jonathan. Jether also died without children.

2:33  LA: Porro Jonathan genuit Phaleth, et Ziza. Isti fuerunt filii Jerameel.
      EN: Jonathan fathered Peleth and Zaza. These were Jerahmeel's sons.

2:34  LA: Sesan autem non habuit filios, sed filias : et servum ægyptium nomine Jeraa.
      EN: Sheshan had no sons, but daughters, and an Egyptian servant named Jarha.

2:35  LA: Deditque ei filiam suam uxorem : quæ peperit ei Ethei.
      EN: He gave his daughter to him as a wife, and she bore him Attai.

2:36  LA: Ethei autem genuit Nathan, et Nathan genuit Zabad.
      EN: Attai fathered Nathan, and Nathan fathered Zabad.

2:37  LA: Zabad quoque genuit Ophlal, et Ophlal genuit Obed.
      EN: Zabad fathered Ephlal, and Ephlal fathered Obed.

2:38  LA: Obed genuit Jehu, Jehu genuit Azariam,
      EN: Obed fathered Jehu; Jehu fathered Azariah.

2:39  LA: Azarias genuit Helles, et Helles genuit Elasa.
      EN: Azariah fathered Helez, and Helez fathered Eleasah.

2:40  LA: Elasa genuit Sisamoi, Sisamoi genuit Sellum,
      EN: Eleasah fathered Sismai; Sismai fathered Shallum.

2:41  LA: Sellum genuit Icamiam, Icamia autem genuit Elisama.
      EN: Shallum fathered Jekamiah, and Jekamiah fathered Elishama.

2:42  LA: Filii autem Caleb fratris Jerameel : Mesa primogenitus ejus ; ipse est pater Ziph : et filii Maresa patris Hebron.
      EN: The sons of Caleb, Jerahmeel's brother: Mesha his firstborn, who was Ziph's father; and the sons of Mareshah, Hebron's father.

2:43  LA: Porro filii Hebron, Core, et Taphua, et Recem, et Samma.
      EN: The sons of Hebron: Korah, Tappuah, Rekem, and Shema.

2:44  LA: Samma autem genuit Raham, patrem Jercaam, et Recem genuit Sammai.
      EN: Shema fathered Raham, Jorkeam's father, and Rekem fathered Shammai.

2:45  LA: Filius Sammai, Maon : et Maon pater Bethsur.
      EN: Shammai's son was Maon, and Maon was Beth-zur's father.

2:46  LA: Epha autem concubina Caleb peperit Haran, et Mosa, et Gezez. Porro Haran genuit Gezez.
      EN: Ephah, Caleb's concubine, bore Haran, Moza, and Gazez. Haran fathered Gazez.

2:47  LA: Filii autem Jahaddai, Regom, et Joathan, et Gesan, et Phalet, et Epha, et Saaph.
      EN: The sons of Jahdai: Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah, and Shaaph.

2:48  LA: Concubina Caleb Maacha, peperit Saber, et Tharana.
      EN: Maacah, Caleb's concubine, bore Sheber and Tirhanah.

2:49  LA: Genuit autem Saaph pater Madmena Sue, patrem Machbena et patrem Gabaa. Filia vero Caleb fuit Achsa.
      EN: Shaaph, Madmannah's father, fathered Sheva, the father of Machbenah and the father of Gibeah. Caleb's daughter was Achsah.

2:50  LA: Hi erant filii Caleb, filii Hur primogeniti Ephratha, Sobal pater Cariathiarim.
      EN: These were the sons of Caleb, son of Hur, Ephrath's firstborn: Shobal, the father of Kiriath-jearim,

2:51  LA: Salma pater Bethlehem, Hariph pater Bethgader.
      EN: Salma, the father of Bethlehem, and Hareph, the father of Beth-gader.

2:52  LA: Fuerunt autem filii Sobal patris Cariathiarim, qui videbat dimidium requietionum.
      EN: Shobal, the father of Kiriath-jearim, who saw half the resting places, had sons.

2:53  LA: Et de cognatione Cariathiarim, Jethrei, et Aphuthei, et Semathei, et Maserei. Ex his egressi sunt Saraitæ, et Esthaolitæ.
      EN: The families of Kiriath-jearim were the Ithrites, Puthites, Shumathites, and Mishraites. From these came the Zorathites and Eshtaolites.

2:54  LA: Filii Salma, Bethlehem, et Netophathi, coronæ domus Joab, et dimidium requietionis Sarai :
      EN: The sons of Salma: Bethlehem, the Netophathites, the crowns of the house of Joab, and half the resting place of Zorah;

      EN: also the families of scribes living in Jabez, singing and sounding forth and dwelling in tents. These are the Kenites who came from the heat of the father of the house of Rechab.
