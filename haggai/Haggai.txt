HAGGAI 1

1:1  LA: In anno secundo Darii regis, in mense sexto, in die una mensis, factum est verbum Domini in manu Aggæi prophetæ, ad Zorobabel, filium Salathiel, ducem Juda, et ad Jesum, filium Josedec, sacerdotem magnum, dicens :
     EN: In the second year of King Darius, in the sixth month, on the first day of the month, the word of the Lord came through Haggai the prophet to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, saying:

1:2  LA: Hæc ait Dominus exercituum, dicens : Populus iste dicit : Nondum venit tempus domus Domini ædificandæ.
     EN: Thus says the Lord of hosts: This people says, “The time has not yet come to build the house of the Lord.”

1:3  LA: Et factum est verbum Domini in manu Aggæi prophetæ, dicens :
     EN: Then the word of the Lord came through Haggai the prophet, saying:

1:4  LA: [Numquid tempus vobis est ut habitetis in domibus laqueatis, et domus ista deserta ?
     EN: Is it time for you to dwell in paneled houses while this house lies deserted?

1:5  LA: Et nunc hæc dicit Dominus exercituum : Ponite corda vestra super vias vestras.
     EN: Now, therefore, thus says the Lord of hosts: Set your hearts upon your ways.

1:6  LA: Seminastis multum, et intulistis parum ; comedistis, et non estis satiati ; bibistis, et non estis inebriati ; operuistis vos, et non estis calefacti ; et qui mercedes congregavit, misit eas in sacculum pertusum.
     EN: You have sown much and brought in little. You have eaten but have not been satisfied; you have drunk but have not had your fill. You have clothed yourselves but have not become warm, and whoever gathered wages put them into a bag full of holes.

1:7  LA: Hæc dicit Dominus exercituum : Ponite corda vestra super vias vestras ;
     EN: Thus says the Lord of hosts: Set your hearts upon your ways.

1:8  LA: ascendite in montem, portate ligna, et ædificate domum : et acceptabilis mihi erit, et glorificabor, dicit Dominus.
     EN: Go up to the mountain, bring timber, and build the house. It will be pleasing to Me, and I will be glorified, says the Lord.

1:9  LA: Respexistis ad amplius, et ecce factum est minus ; et intulistis in domum, et exsufflavi illud : quam ob causam ? dicit Dominus exercituum : quia domus mea deserta est, et vos festinatis unusquisque in domum suam.
     EN: You looked for more, and see, it turned out to be less. You brought it home, and I blew it away. Why? says the Lord of hosts. Because My house lies deserted while each of you hurries to his own house.

1:10  LA: Propter hoc super vos prohibiti sunt cæli ne darent rorem, et terra prohibita est ne daret germen suum :
      EN: Because of this the heavens above you have been restrained from giving dew, and the earth has been restrained from giving its produce.

1:11  LA: et vocavi siccitatem super terram, et super montes, et super triticum, et super vinum, et super oleum, et quæcumque profert humus, et super homines, et super jumenta, et super omnem laborem manuum.]
      EN: I called for drought upon the land, the mountains, the wheat, the wine, the oil, everything the ground produces, human beings, beasts of burden, and all the labor of your hands.

1:12  LA: Et audivit Zorobabel, filius Salathiel, et Jesus, filius Josedec, sacerdos magnus, et omnes reliquiæ populi, vocem Domini Dei sui, et verba Aggæi prophetæ, sicut misit eum Dominus Deus eorum ad eos, et timuit populus a facie Domini.
      EN: Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Joshua son of Jehozadak the high priest, and all the remnant of the people listened to the voice of the Lord their God and the words of Haggai the prophet, whom the Lord their God had sent to them. The people feared before the Lord.

1:13  LA: Et dixit Aggæus, nuntius Domini de nuntiis Domini, populo dicens : Ego vobiscum sum, dicit Dominus.
      EN: Then Haggai, the Lord's messenger, spoke to the people with the Lord's messages, saying, “I am with you, says the Lord.”

1:14  LA: Et suscitavit Dominus spiritum Zorobabel, filii Salathiel, ducis Juda, et spiritum Jesu, filii Josedec, sacerdotis magni, et spiritum reliquorum de omni populo : et ingressi sunt, et faciebant opus in domo Domini exercituum, Dei sui.
      EN: The Lord stirred up the spirit of Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, the spirit of Joshua son of Jehozadak the high priest, and the spirit of the remnant of all the people. They came and worked on the house of the Lord of hosts, their God,

1:15  LA: In die vigesima et quarta mensis, in sexto mense, in anno secundo Darii regis.
      EN: on the twenty-fourth day of the month, in the sixth month, in the second year of King Darius.

HAGGAI 2

2:1  LA: In septimo mense, vigesima et prima mensis, factum est verbum Domini in manu Aggæi prophetæ, dicens :
     EN: In the seventh month, on the twenty-first day of the month, the word of the Lord came through Haggai the prophet, saying:

2:2  LA: Loquere ad Zorobabel, filium Salathiel, ducem Juda, et ad Jesum, filium Josedec, sacerdotem magnum, et ad reliquos populi, dicens :
     EN: Speak to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, to Joshua son of Jehozadak the high priest, and to the remnant of the people, saying:

2:3  LA: [Quis in vobis est derelictus, qui vidit domum istam in gloria sua prima ? et quid vos videtis hanc nunc ? numquid non ita est, quasi non sit in oculis vestris ?
     EN: Who among you is left who saw this house in its former glory? And how do you see it now? Is it not as though it were nothing in your eyes?

2:4  LA: Et nunc confortare, Zorobabel, dicit Dominus ; et confortare, Jesu, fili Josedec, sacerdos magne ; et confortare, omnis populus terræ, dicit Dominus exercituum : et facite (quoniam ego vobiscum sum, dicit Dominus exercituum)
     EN: But now take courage, Zerubbabel, says the Lord; take courage, Joshua son of Jehozadak, high priest; take courage, all you people of the land, says the Lord of hosts. And carry out—for I am with you, says the Lord of hosts—

2:5  LA: verbum quod pepigi vobiscum cum egrederemini de terra Ægypti : et spiritus meus erit in medio vestrum : nolite timere.
     EN: the word that I covenanted with you when you came out of the land of Egypt. My Spirit will be in your midst; do not fear.

2:6  LA: Quia hæc dicit Dominus exercituum : Adhuc unum modicum est, et ego commovebo cælum, et terram, et mare, et aridam.
     EN: For thus says the Lord of hosts: There is still a little while, and I will shake heaven and earth, the sea and the dry land.

2:7  LA: Et movebo omnes gentes, et veniet desideratus cunctis gentibus : et implebo domum istam gloria, dicit Dominus exercituum.
     EN: I will shake all nations, and the one desired by all nations will come. I will fill this house with glory, says the Lord of hosts.

2:8  LA: Meum est argentum, et meum est aurum, dicit Dominus exercituum.
     EN: The silver is Mine, and the gold is Mine, says the Lord of hosts.

2:9  LA: Magna erit gloria domus istius novissimæ plus quam primæ, dicit Dominus exercituum : et in loco isto dabo pacem, dicit Dominus exercituum.]
     EN: The glory of this latter house will be greater than that of the former, says the Lord of hosts. And in this place I will give peace, says the Lord of hosts.

2:10  LA: In vigesima et quarta noni mensis, in anno secundo Darii regis, factum est verbum Domini ad Aggæum prophetam, dicens :
      EN: On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of King Darius, the word of the Lord came to Haggai the prophet, saying:

2:11  LA: Hæc dicit Dominus exercituum : Interroga sacerdotes legem, dicens :
      EN: Thus says the Lord of hosts: Ask the priests about the law, saying:

2:12  LA: Si tulerit homo carnem sanctificatam in ora vestimenti sui, et tetigerit de summitate ejus panem, aut pulmentum, aut vinum, aut oleum, aut omnem cibum, numquid sanctificabitur ? Respondentes autem sacerdotes, dixerunt : Non.
      EN: If a man carries consecrated meat in the fold of his garment, and with its edge touches bread, stew, wine, oil, or any food, will it become consecrated? The priests answered, “No.”

2:13  LA: Et dixit Aggæus : Si tetigerit pollutus in anima ex omnibus his, numquid contaminabitur ? Et responderunt sacerdotes, et dixerunt : Contaminabitur.
      EN: Then Haggai said, “If someone made unclean by a dead body touches any of these things, will it become unclean?” The priests answered, “It will become unclean.”

2:14  LA: Et respondit Aggæus, et dixit : [Sic populus iste, et sic gens ista ante faciem meam, dicit Dominus, et sic omne opus manuum eorum : et omnia quæ obtulerunt ibi, contaminata erunt.
      EN: Haggai answered and said: So it is with this people, and so with this nation before Me, says the Lord, and so with every work of their hands; everything they have offered there will be unclean.

2:15  LA: Et nunc ponite corda vestra a die hac et supra, antequam poneretur lapis super lapidem in templo Domini.
      EN: Now set your hearts upon the time before this day, before stone was laid upon stone in the Lord's temple.

2:16  LA: Cum accederetis ad acervum viginti modiorum, et fierent decem ; et intraretis ad torcular, ut exprimeretis quinquaginta lagenas, et fiebant viginti.
      EN: When you came to a heap of twenty modii, there were ten; and when you entered the winepress to draw out fifty jars, there were twenty.

2:17  LA: Percussi vos vento urente, et aurugine, et grandine omnia opera manuum vestrarum : et non fuit in vobis qui reverteretur ad me, dicit Dominus.
      EN: I struck you with scorching wind and blight, and all the works of your hands with hail; yet there was no one among you who returned to Me, says the Lord.

2:18  LA: Ponite corda vestra ex die ista, et in futurum, a die vigesima et quarta noni mensis : a die qua fundamenta jacta sunt templi Domini, ponite super cor vestrum.
      EN: Set your hearts upon this day and the future, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month. From the day when the foundations of the Lord's temple were laid, set your heart upon it.

2:19  LA: Numquid jam semen in germine est, et adhuc vinea, et ficus, et malogranatum, et lignum olivæ non floruit ? ex die ista benedicam.]
      EN: Is the seed already sprouting, while the vine, fig tree, pomegranate, and olive tree have still not blossomed? From this day I will bless you.

2:20  LA: Et factum est verbum Domini secundo ad Aggæum in vigesima et quarta mensis, dicens :
      EN: The word of the Lord came a second time to Haggai on the twenty-fourth day of the month, saying:

2:21  LA: Loquere ad Zorobabel ducem Juda, dicens : [Ego movebo cælum pariter et terram,
      EN: Speak to Zerubbabel, governor of Judah, saying: I will shake both heaven and earth,

2:22  LA: et subvertam solium regnorum, et conteram fortitudinem regni gentium : et subvertam quadrigam et ascensorem ejus, et descendent equi, et ascensores eorum, vir in gladio fratris sui.
      EN: and overturn the throne of kingdoms and shatter the strength of the kingdom of the nations. I will overturn the chariot and its rider; horses and their riders will fall, each man by his brother's sword.

2:23  LA: In die illa, dicit Dominus exercituum, assumam te, Zorobabel, fili Salathiel, serve meus, dicit Dominus : et ponam te quasi signaculum, quia te elegi, dicit Dominus exercituum.]
      EN: On that day, says the Lord of hosts, I will take you, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, My servant, says the Lord, and make you like a signet, for I have chosen you, says the Lord of hosts.
