MALACHI — LATIN–ENGLISH (CLEMENTINE VULGATE)

MALACHI 1

1:1  LA: Onus verbi Domini ad Israël in manu Malachiæ.
     EN: The burden of the word of the Lord to Israel through Malachi.

1:2  LA: Dilexi vos, dicit Dominus, et dixistis : In quo dilexisti nos ? Nonne frater erat Esau Jacob ? dicit Dominus : et dilexi Jacob,
     EN: I have loved you, says the Lord. But you have said, “How have You loved us?” Was not Esau Jacob's brother? says the Lord. Yet I loved Jacob,

1:3  LA: Esau autem odio habui, et posui montes ejus in solitudinem, et hæreditatem ejus in dracones deserti.
     EN: but hated Esau, and made his mountains a wilderness and his inheritance a home for the dragons of the desert.

1:4  LA: Quod si dixerit Idumæa : Destructi sumus, sed revertentes ædificabimus quæ destructa sunt : hæc dicit Dominus exercituum : Isti ædificabunt, et ego destruam : et vocabuntur termini impietatis, et populus cui iratus est Dominus usque in æternum.
     EN: If Edom says, “We have been destroyed, but we will return and rebuild what has been destroyed,” thus says the Lord of hosts: They will build, and I will tear down. They will be called the territory of wickedness and the people with whom the Lord is angry forever.

1:5  LA: Et oculi vestri videbunt, et vos dicetis : Magnificetur Dominus super terminum Israël.
     EN: Your eyes will see, and you will say, “May the Lord be magnified beyond the borders of Israel.”

1:6  LA: Filius honorat patrem, et servus dominum suum. Si ergo pater ego sum, ubi est honor meus ? et si Dominus ego sum, ubi est timor meus ? dicit Dominus exercituum. Ad vos, o sacerdotes, qui despicitis nomen meum, et dixistis : In quo despeximus nomen tuum ?
     EN: A son honors his father, and a servant his master. If, then, I am a father, where is My honor? And if I am the Lord, where is the fear due to Me? says the Lord of hosts to you, priests who despise My name. Yet you have said, “How have we despised Your name?”

1:7  LA: Offertis super altare meum panem pollutum, et dicitis : In quo polluimus te ? in eo quod dicitis : Mensa Domini despecta est.
     EN: You offer polluted bread upon My altar, and you say, “How have we polluted You?” By saying, “The Lord's table is contemptible.”

1:8  LA: Si offeratis cæcum ad immolandum, nonne malum est ? et si offeratis claudum et languidum, nonne malum est ? offer illud duci tuo, si placuerit ei, aut si susceperit faciem tuam, dicit Dominus exercituum.
     EN: If you offer a blind animal for sacrifice, is it not evil? And if you offer one that is lame and sick, is it not evil? Offer it to your governor: will it please him, or will he receive you favorably? says the Lord of hosts.

1:9  LA: Et nunc deprecamini vultum Dei ut misereatur vestri (de manu enim vestra factum est hoc), si quomodo suscipiat facies vestras, dicit Dominus exercituum.
     EN: Now entreat God's favor, that He may have mercy on you—for this has come from your hands—to see whether He will receive you favorably, says the Lord of hosts.

1:10  LA: Quis est in vobis qui claudat ostia, et incendat altare meum gratuito ? non est mihi voluntas in vobis, dicit Dominus exercituum, et munus non suscipiam de manu vestra.
      EN: Who among you will close the doors and kindle My altar without payment? I take no pleasure in you, says the Lord of hosts, and I will accept no offering from your hands.

1:11  LA: Ab ortu enim solis usque ad occasum, magnum est nomen meum in gentibus, et in omni loco sacrificatur : et offertur nomini meo oblatio munda, quia magnum est nomen meum in gentibus, dicit Dominus exercituum.
      EN: For from the rising of the sun to its setting, My name is great among the nations, and in every place sacrifice is offered; a pure offering is presented to My name, for My name is great among the nations, says the Lord of hosts.

1:12  LA: Et vos polluistis illud in eo quod dicitis : Mensa Domini contaminata est, et quod superponitur contemptibilis est, cum igne qui illud devorat.
      EN: But you have polluted it by saying, “The Lord's table is defiled, and what is placed upon it is contemptible, together with the fire that devours it.”

1:13  LA: Et dixistis : Ecce de labore, et exsufflastis illud, dicit Dominus exercituum : et intulistis de rapinis claudum et languidum, et intulistis munus : numquid suscipiam illud de manu vestra ? dicit Dominus.
      EN: You have said, “Look, what toil!” and you have blown upon it in contempt, says the Lord of hosts. You have brought in stolen goods, the lame and the sick, and presented them as an offering. Shall I accept it from your hands? says the Lord.

1:14  LA: Maledictus dolosus qui habet in grege suo masculum, et votum faciens immolat debile Domino : quia rex magnus ego, dicit Dominus exercituum, et nomen meum horribile in gentibus.
      EN: Cursed is the deceiver who has a male in his flock and makes a vow, but sacrifices a defective animal to the Lord. For I am a great King, says the Lord of hosts, and My name is feared among the nations.

MALACHI 2

2:1  LA: Et nunc ad vos mandatum hoc, o sacerdotes.
     EN: And now this command is for you, priests.

2:2  LA: Si nolueritis audire, et si nolueritis ponere super cor, ut detis gloriam nomini meo, ait Dominus exercituum, mittam in vos egestatem, et maledicam benedictionibus vestris, et maledicam illis, quoniam non posuistis super cor.
     EN: If you will not listen and will not take it to heart to give glory to My name, says the Lord of hosts, I will send want upon you and curse your blessings. I will indeed curse them, because you have not taken it to heart.

2:3  LA: Ecce ego projiciam vobis brachium, et dispergam super vultum vestrum stercus solemnitatum vestrarum, et assumet vos secum.
     EN: Look, I will throw the shoulder at you and scatter the dung of your festivals over your faces, and it will carry you away with it.

2:4  LA: Et scietis quia misi ad vos mandatum istud, ut esset pactum meum cum Levi, dicit Dominus exercituum.
     EN: You will know that I sent you this command so that My covenant with Levi might stand, says the Lord of hosts.

2:5  LA: Pactum meum fuit cum eo vitæ et pacis : et dedi ei timorem, et timuit me, et a facie nominis mei pavebat.
     EN: My covenant with him was one of life and peace. I gave him fear, and he feared Me and trembled before My name.

2:6  LA: Lex veritatis fuit in ore ejus, et iniquitas non est inventa in labiis ejus : in pace et in æquitate ambulavit mecum, et multos avertit ab iniquitate.
     EN: The law of truth was in his mouth, and iniquity was not found upon his lips. He walked with Me in peace and uprightness and turned many away from iniquity.

2:7  LA: Labia enim sacerdotis custodient scientiam, et legem requirent ex ore ejus, quia angelus Domini exercituum est.
     EN: For the lips of a priest will guard knowledge, and people will seek the law from his mouth, because he is the messenger of the Lord of hosts.

2:8  LA: Vos autem recessistis de via, et scandalizastis plurimos in lege : irritum fecistis pactum Levi, dicit Dominus exercituum.
     EN: But you have turned aside from the way and caused many to stumble in the law. You have made void the covenant of Levi, says the Lord of hosts.

2:9  LA: Propter quod et ego dedi vos contemptibiles, et humiles omnibus populis, sicut non servastis vias meas, et accepistis faciem in lege.
     EN: Therefore I too have made you contemptible and low before all peoples, because you have not kept My ways and have shown partiality in the law.

2:10  LA: Numquid non pater unus omnium nostrum ? numquid non Deus unus creavit nos ? quare ergo despicit unusquisque nostrum fratrem suum, violans pactum patrum nostrorum ?
      EN: Have we not all one Father? Has not one God created us? Why, then, does each of us despise his brother, violating the covenant of our fathers?

2:11  LA: Transgressus est Juda, et abominatio facta est in Israël et in Jerusalem, quia contaminavit Juda sanctificationem Domini, quam dilexit, et habuit filiam dei alieni.
      EN: Judah has transgressed, and an abomination has been committed in Israel and Jerusalem, for Judah has defiled the holiness of the Lord, which He loved, and has taken the daughter of a foreign god.

2:12  LA: Disperdet Dominus virum qui fecerit hoc, magistrum et discipulum, de tabernaculis Jacob, et offerentem munus Domino exercituum.
      EN: May the Lord destroy from the tents of Jacob the man who does this, teacher and disciple, and the one who presents an offering to the Lord of hosts.

2:13  LA: Et hoc rursum fecistis : operiebatis lacrimis altare Domini, fletu et mugitu, ita ut non respiciam ultra ad sacrificium, nec accipiam placabile quid de manu vestra.
      EN: This too you have done: you covered the Lord's altar with tears, weeping, and groaning, so that I no longer regard the sacrifice or accept anything pleasing from your hands.

2:14  LA: Et dixistis : Quam ob causam ? Quia Dominus testificatus est inter te et uxorem pubertatis tuæ, quam tu despexisti : et hæc particeps tua, et uxor fœderis tui.
      EN: You have said, “Why?” Because the Lord has been a witness between you and the wife of your youth, whom you have despised, although she is your companion and the wife of your covenant.

2:15  LA: Nonne unus fecit, et residuum spiritus ejus est ? et quid unus quærit, nisi semen Dei ? custodite ergo spiritum vestrum, et uxorem adolescentiæ tuæ noli despicere.
      EN: Did not the One make them, and is not the remnant of the spirit His? And what does the One seek except offspring for God? Guard your spirit, then, and do not despise the wife of your youth.

2:16  LA: Cum odio habueris, dimitte, dicit Dominus Deus Israël : operiet autem iniquitas vestimentum ejus, dicit Dominus exercituum. Custodite spiritum vestrum, et nolite despicere.
      EN: When you have come to hate her, send her away, says the Lord, the God of Israel; but iniquity will cover his garment, says the Lord of hosts. Guard your spirit, and do not despise her.

2:17  LA: Laborare fecistis Dominum in sermonibus vestris, et dixistis : In quo eum fecimus laborare ? In eo quod dicitis : Omnis qui facit malum bonus est in conspectu Domini, et tales ei placent : aut certe ubi est Deus judicii ?
      EN: You have wearied the Lord with your words, and you have said, “How have we wearied Him?” By saying, “Everyone who does evil is good in the Lord's sight, and such people please Him,” or else, “Where is the God of judgment?”

MALACHI 3

3:1  LA: Ecce ego mitto angelum meum, et præparabit viam ante faciem meam : et statim veniet ad templum suum Dominator quem vos quæritis, et angelus testamenti quem vos vultis. Ecce venit, dicit Dominus exercituum.
     EN: Look, I am sending My messenger, and he will prepare the way before Me. Suddenly the Ruler whom you seek will come to His temple, and the messenger of the covenant whom you desire. Look, he is coming, says the Lord of hosts.

3:2  LA: Et quis poterit cogitare diem adventus ejus, et quis stabit ad videndum eum ? ipse enim quasi ignis conflans, et quasi herba fullonum :
     EN: Who will be able to contemplate the day of his coming, and who will stand to see him? For he is like a refiner's fire and like a fuller's cleansing herb.

3:3  LA: et sedebit conflans, et emundans argentum : et purgabit filios Levi, et colabit eos quasi aurum et quasi argentum, et erunt Domino offerentes sacrificia in justitia.
     EN: He will sit refining and cleansing silver; he will purify the sons of Levi and refine them like gold and silver, and they will offer sacrifices to the Lord in justice.

3:4  LA: Et placebit Domino sacrificium Juda et Jerusalem, sicut dies sæculi, et sicut anni antiqui.
     EN: The sacrifice of Judah and Jerusalem will please the Lord, as in the days of old and as in ancient years.

3:5  LA: Et accedam ad vos in judicio, et ero testis velox maleficis, et adulteris, et perjuris, et qui calumniantur mercedem mercenarii, viduas et pupillos, et opprimunt peregrinum, nec timuerunt me, dicit Dominus exercituum.
     EN: I will draw near to you in judgment and be a swift witness against sorcerers, adulterers, perjurers, those who defraud hired workers of their wages, wrong widows and orphans, and oppress the foreigner, and who have not feared Me, says the Lord of hosts.

3:6  LA: Ego enim Dominus, et non mutor : et vos filii Jacob, non estis consumpti.
     EN: For I am the Lord, and I do not change; and you, children of Jacob, have not been consumed.

3:7  LA: A diebus enim patrum vestrorum recessistis a legitimis meis, et non custodistis : revertimini ad me, et revertar ad vos, dicit Dominus exercituum. Et dixistis : In quo revertemur ?
     EN: From the days of your fathers you have turned aside from My statutes and have not kept them. Return to Me, and I will return to you, says the Lord of hosts. But you have said, “How shall we return?”

3:8  LA: Si affliget homo Deum, quia vos configitis me ? Et dixistis : In quo configimus te ? In decimis et in primitiis.
     EN: Can a human being afflict God? Yet you are piercing Me. You have said, “How are we piercing You?” In tithes and firstfruits.

3:9  LA: Et in penuria vos maledicti estis, et me vos configitis gens tota.
     EN: You are cursed with want, and you are piercing Me, the whole nation of you.

3:10  LA: Inferte omnem decimam in horreum, et sit cibus in domo mea : et probate me super hoc, dicit Dominus : si non aperuero vobis cataractas cæli, et effudero vobis benedictionem usque ad abundantiam :
      EN: Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse, so that there may be food in My house. Test Me in this, says the Lord: see whether I will not open the floodgates of heaven for you and pour out a blessing upon you in abundance.

3:11  LA: et increpabo pro vobis devorantem, et non corrumpet fructum terræ vestræ, nec erit sterilis vinea in agro, dicit Dominus exercituum.
      EN: I will rebuke the devourer for you, and it will not ruin the fruit of your land, nor will the vine in the field be barren, says the Lord of hosts.

3:12  LA: Et beatos vos dicent omnes gentes : eritis enim vos terra desiderabilis, dicit Dominus exercituum.
      EN: All nations will call you blessed, for you will be a desirable land, says the Lord of hosts.

3:13  LA: Invaluerunt super me verba vestra, dicit Dominus.
      EN: Your words have grown strong against Me, says the Lord.

3:14  LA: Et dixistis : Quid locuti sumus contra te ? Dixistis : Vanus est qui servit Deo : et quod emolumentum quia custodivimus præcepta ejus, et quia ambulavimus tristes coram Domino exercituum ?
      EN: You have said, “What have we spoken against You?” You have said, “The person who serves God labors in vain. What profit is there in our having kept His commands and walked in mourning before the Lord of hosts?

3:15  LA: Ergo nunc beatos dicimus arrogantes : siquidem ædificati sunt facientes impietatem, et tentaverunt Deum, et salvi facti sunt.
      EN: So now we call the arrogant blessed, for those who practice wickedness have prospered; they have put God to the test and escaped unharmed.”

3:16  LA: Tunc locuti sunt timentes Dominum, unusquisque cum proximo suo : et attendit Dominus, et audivit, et scriptus est liber monumenti coram eo timentibus Dominum, et cogitantibus nomen ejus.
      EN: Then those who feared the Lord spoke, each with his neighbor. The Lord paid attention and heard, and a book of remembrance was written before Him for those who fear the Lord and meditate upon His name.

3:17  LA: Et erunt mihi, ait Dominus exercituum, in die qua ego facio, in peculium : et parcam eis, sicut parcit vir filio suo servienti sibi.
      EN: They will be Mine, says the Lord of hosts, as a treasured possession on the day when I act. I will spare them as a man spares his son who serves him.

3:18  LA: Et convertemini, et videbitis quid sit inter justum et impium, et inter servientem Deo et non servientem ei.
      EN: You will turn and see the difference between the just and the wicked, between the one who serves God and the one who does not serve Him.

3:19  LA: Ecce enim dies veniet succensa quasi caminus : et erunt omnes superbi et omnes facientes impietatem stipula : et inflammabit eos dies veniens, dicit Dominus exercituum, quæ non derelinquet eis radicem et germen.
      EN: For look, a day will come burning like a furnace, and all the proud and all who practice wickedness will be stubble. The coming day will set them ablaze, says the Lord of hosts, leaving them neither root nor shoot.

3:20  LA: Et orietur vobis timentibus nomen meum sol justitiæ, et sanitas in pennis ejus : et egrediemini, et salietis sicut vituli de armento.
      EN: But for you who fear My name, the sun of justice will rise, with healing in its wings. You will go out and leap like calves from the herd.

3:21  LA: Et calcabitis impios, cum fuerint cinis sub planta pedum vestrorum, in die qua ego facio, dicit Dominus exercituum.
      EN: You will tread down the wicked, for they will be ashes beneath the soles of your feet on the day when I act, says the Lord of hosts.

3:22  LA: Ecce ego mittam vobis Eliam prophetam, antequam veniat dies Domini magnus et horribilis.
      EN: Look, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the great and terrible day of the Lord comes.

3:23  LA: Et convertet cor patrum ad filios, et cor filiorum ad patres eorum : ne forte veniam, et percutiam terram anathemate.
      EN: He will turn the hearts of fathers to their children and the hearts of children to their fathers, lest I come and strike the land with a ban of destruction.

3:24  LA: Mementote legis Moysi servi mei, quam mandavi ei in Horeb ad omnem Israël, præcepta et judicia.
      EN: Remember the law of Moses My servant, which I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel, its precepts and judgments.
