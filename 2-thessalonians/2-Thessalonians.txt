2 THESSALONIANS 1

1:1  LA: Paulus , et Sylvanus, et Timotheus, ecclesiæ Thessalonicensium in Deo Patre nostro, et Domino Jesu Christo.
     EN: Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy, to the church of the Thessalonians in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ:

1:2  LA: Gratia vobis, et pax a Deo Patre nostro, et Domino Jesu Christo.
     EN: grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

1:3  LA: Gratias agere debemus semper Deo pro vobis, fratres, ita ut dignum est, quoniam supercrescit fides vestra, et abundat caritas uniuscujusque vestrum in invicem:
     EN: We ought always to thank God for you, brothers, as is fitting, because your faith is growing greatly and the love of each of you for one another is increasing.

1:4  LA: ita ut et nos ipsi in vobis gloriemur in ecclesiis Dei, pro patientia vestra, et fide, et in omnibus persecutionibus vestris, et tribulationibus, quas sustinetis
     EN: Thus we ourselves boast about you in the churches of God, about your endurance and faith amid all the persecutions and afflictions you bear,

1:5  LA: in exemplum justi judicii Dei, ut digni habeamini in regno Dei, pro quo et patimini.
     EN: as a sign of God’s just judgment, that you may be counted worthy of the kingdom of God, for which you also suffer.

1:6  LA: Si tamen justum est apud Deum retribuere tribulationem iis qui vos tribulant:
     EN: For it is indeed just with God to repay affliction to those who afflict you,

1:7  LA: et vobis, qui tribulamini, requiem nobiscum in revelatione Domini Jesu de cælo cum angelis virtutis ejus,
     EN: and to give you who are afflicted rest with us, when the Lord Jesus is revealed from heaven with the angels of his power,

1:8  LA: in flamma ignis dantis vindictam iis qui non noverunt Deum, et qui non obediunt Evangelio Domini nostri Jesu Christi,
     EN: in flaming fire, bringing retribution upon those who do not know God and do not obey the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ.

1:9  LA: qui pœnas dabunt in interitu æternas a facie Domini, et a gloria virtutis ejus:
     EN: They will pay the penalty of eternal destruction, away from the face of the Lord and the glory of his power,

1:10  LA: cum venerit glorificari in sanctis suis, et admirabilis fieri in omnibus, qui crediderunt, quia creditum est testimonium nostrum super vos in die illo.
      EN: when he comes to be glorified in his saints and marveled at in all who have believed, because our testimony was believed among you, on that day.

1:11  LA: In quo etiam oramus semper pro vobis: ut dignetur vos vocatione sua Deus noster, et impleat omnem voluntatem bonitatis, et opus fidei in virtute,
      EN: To this end we also pray always for you, that our God may count you worthy of his calling and fulfill with power every desire for goodness and work of faith,

1:12  LA: ut clarificetur nomen Domini nostri Jesu Christi in vobis, et vos in illo secundum gratiam Dei nostri, et Domini Jesu Christi.
      EN: so that the name of our Lord Jesus Christ may be glorified in you, and you in him, according to the grace of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.

2 THESSALONIANS 2

2:1  LA: Rogamus autem vos, fratres, per adventum Domini nostri Jesu Christi, et nostræ congregationis in ipsum:
     EN: Now we appeal to you, brothers, concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering to him,

2:2  LA: ut non cito moveamini a vestro sensu, neque terreamini, neque per spiritum, neque per sermonem, neque per epistolam tamquam per nos missam, quasi instet dies Domini.
     EN: not to be quickly shaken from your judgment or frightened by a spirit, a spoken word, or a letter supposedly sent by us, as though the day of the Lord were at hand.

2:3  LA: Ne quis vos seducat ullo modo: quoniam nisi venerit discessio primum, et revelatus fuerit homo peccati filius perditionis,
     EN: Let no one deceive you in any way. For that day will not come unless the rebellion comes first and the man of sin is revealed, the son of perdition,

2:4  LA: qui adversatur, et extollitur supra omne, quod dicitur Deus, aut quod colitur, ita ut in templo Dei sedeat ostendens se tamquam sit Deus.
     EN: who opposes and exalts himself above everything called God or worshiped, so that he sits in God’s temple, displaying himself as though he were God.

2:5  LA: Non retinetis quod cum adhuc essem apud vos, hæc dicebam vobis?
     EN: Do you not remember that I told you these things while I was still with you?

2:6  LA: et nunc quid detineat scitis, ut reveletur in suo tempore.
     EN: And now you know what restrains him, so that he may be revealed in his own time.

2:7  LA: Nam mysterium jam operatur iniquitatis: tantum ut qui tenet nunc, teneat, donec de medio fiat.
     EN: For the mystery of lawlessness is already at work; only let the one who now restrains continue to restrain, until he is removed from the midst.

2:8  LA: Et tunc revelabitur ille iniquus, quem Dominus Jesus interficiet spiritu oris sui, et destruet illustratione adventus sui eum:
     EN: Then that lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord Jesus will kill with the breath of his mouth and destroy by the radiance of his coming.

2:9  LA: cujus est adventus secundum operationem Satanæ in omni virtute, et signis, et prodigiis mendacibus,
     EN: His coming is according to the working of Satan, with every kind of power, signs, and lying wonders,

2:10  LA: et in omni seductione iniquitatis iis qui pereunt: eo quod caritatem veritatis non receperunt ut salvi fierent.
      EN: and with every deception of lawlessness for those who are perishing, because they did not receive the love of the truth so that they might be saved.

2:11  LA: Ideo mittet illis Deus operationem erroris ut credant mendacio,
      EN: Therefore God will send them a working of error, so that they believe the lie,

2:12  LA: ut judicentur omnes qui non crediderunt veritati, sed consenserunt iniquitati.
      EN: and so that all who did not believe the truth but consented to lawlessness may be judged.

2:13  LA: Nos autem debemus gratias agere Deo semper pro vobis, fratres dilecti a Deo, quod elegerit vos Deus primitias in salutem in sanctificatione spiritus, et in fide veritatis:
      EN: But we ought always to thank God for you, brothers beloved by God, because God chose you as firstfruits for salvation through sanctification of the spirit and faith in the truth.

2:14  LA: in qua et vocavit vos per Evangelium nostrum in acquisitionem gloriæ Domini nostri Jesu Christi.
      EN: To this he also called you through our gospel, to obtain the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.

2:15  LA: Itaque fratres, state: et tenete traditiones, quas didicistis, sive per sermonem, sive per epistolam nostram.
      EN: Therefore, brothers, stand firm and hold to the traditions you learned, whether through spoken word or through our letter.

2:16  LA: Ipse autem Dominus noster Jesus Christus, et Deus et Pater noster, qui dilexit nos, et dedit consolationem æternam, et spem bonam in gratia,
      EN: May our Lord Jesus Christ himself, and God our Father, who loved us and gave us eternal comfort and good hope in grace,

2:17  LA: exhortetur corda vestra, et confirmet in omni opere et sermone bono.
      EN: comfort your hearts and strengthen them in every good work and word.

2 THESSALONIANS 3

3:1  LA: De cetero fratres, orate pro nobis ut sermo Dei currat, et clarificetur, sicut et apud vos:
     EN: Finally, brothers, pray for us, that the word of God may run its course and be glorified, as it is among you,

3:2  LA: et ut liberemur ab importunis, et malis hominibus: non enim omnium est fides.
     EN: and that we may be delivered from troublesome and evil people, for not everyone has faith.

3:3  LA: Fidelis autem Deus est, qui confirmabit vos, et custodiet a malo.
     EN: But God is faithful; he will strengthen you and guard you from evil.

3:4  LA: Confidimus autem de vobis, in Domino, quoniam quæ præcepimus, et facitis, et facietis.
     EN: We have confidence in you in the Lord that you are doing and will do what we have commanded.

3:5  LA: Dominus autem dirigat corda vestra in caritate Dei, et patientia Christi.
     EN: May the Lord direct your hearts into the love of God and the endurance of Christ.

3:6  LA: Denuntiamus autem vobis, fratres, in nomine Domini nostri Jesu Christi, ut subtrahatis vos ab omni fratre ambulante inordinate, et non secundum traditionem, quam acceperunt a nobis.
     EN: We command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to withdraw from every brother who lives disorderly and not according to the tradition they received from us.

3:7  LA: Ipsi enim scitis quemadmodum oporteat imitari nos: quoniam non inquieti fuimus inter vos:
     EN: For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us, because we did not behave disorderly among you,

3:8  LA: neque gratis panem manducavimus ab aliquo, sed in labore, et in fatigatione, nocte et die operantes, ne quem vestrum gravaremus.
     EN: nor did we eat anyone’s bread without paying for it. Rather, we worked with labor and toil night and day, so that we might not burden any of you.

3:9  LA: Non quasi non habuerimus potestatem, sed ut nosmetipsos formam daremus vobis ad imitandum nos.
     EN: This was not because we lacked the right, but to offer ourselves to you as an example to imitate.

3:10  LA: Nam et cum essemus apud vos, hoc denuntiabamus vobis: quoniam si quis non vult operari, nec manducet.
      EN: For even when we were with you, we commanded you this: if anyone is unwilling to work, neither should he eat.

3:11  LA: Audivimus enim inter vos quosdam ambulare inquiete, nihil operantes, sed curiose agentes.
      EN: For we hear that some among you live disorderly, doing no work but meddling in other people’s affairs.

3:12  LA: Iis autem, qui ejusmodi sunt, denuntiemus, et obsecramus in Domino Jesu Christo, ut cum silentio operantes, suum panem manducent.
      EN: We command and urge such people in the Lord Jesus Christ to work quietly and eat their own bread.

3:13  LA: Vos autem, fratres, nolite deficere benefacientes.
      EN: But you, brothers, do not grow weary in doing good.

3:14  LA: Quod si quis non obedit verbo nostro per epistolam, hunc notate, et ne commisceamini cum illo ut confundatur:
      EN: If anyone does not obey our word through this letter, take note of him and do not associate with him, so that he may be ashamed.

3:15  LA: et nolite quasi inimicum existimare, sed corripite ut fratrem.
      EN: Yet do not regard him as an enemy, but correct him as a brother.

3:16  LA: Ipse autem Dominus pacis det vobis pacem sempiternam in omni loco. Dominus sit cum omnibus vobis.
      EN: May the Lord of peace himself give you everlasting peace in every place. May the Lord be with all of you.

3:17  LA: Salutatio, mea manu Pauli: quod est signum in omni epistola, ita scribo.
      EN: This greeting is in my own hand, Paul’s. It is the sign in every letter; this is how I write.

3:18  LA: Gratia Domini nostri Jesu Christi cum omnibus vobis. Amen.
      EN: The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you. Amen.
