MARK — LATIN–ENGLISH (CLEMENTINE VULGATE)
Captured from the Saints.Bible staging Parallel Reader, 1 November 2026.
Verse numbers follow the Saints (Greek/Hebrew) column; 'vulgate_reference' in verses.json gives the Vulgate's own number where it differs.

MARK 1

1:1  LA: Initium Evangelii Jesu Christi, Filii Dei.
     EN: The beginning of the Gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God.

1:2  LA: Sicut scriptum est in Isaia propheta: Ecce ego mitto angelum meum ante faciem tuam, qui præparabit viam tuam ante te.
     EN: As it is written in Isaiah the prophet: Behold, I send my messenger before your face, who will prepare your way before you.

1:3  LA: Vox clamantis in deserto: Parate viam Domini, rectas facite semitas ejus.
     EN: A voice crying in the wilderness: Prepare the way of the Lord; make his paths straight.

1:4  LA: Fuit Joannes in deserto baptizans, et prædicans baptismum pœnitentiæ in remissionem peccatorum.
     EN: John was in the wilderness, baptizing and proclaiming a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins.

1:5  LA: Et egrediebatur ad eum omnis Judææ regio, et Jerosolymitæ universi, et baptizabantur ab illo in Jordanis flumine, confitentes peccata sua.
     EN: The whole region of Judea and all the people of Jerusalem went out to him, and were baptized by him in the Jordan River, confessing their sins.

1:6  LA: Et erat Joannes vestitus pilis cameli, et zona pellicea circa lumbos ejus, et locustas et mel silvestre edebat.
     EN: John was clothed in camel’s hair, with a leather belt around his waist, and ate locusts and wild honey.

1:7  LA: Et prædicabat dicens: Venit fortior me post me, cujus non sum dignus procumbens solvere corrigiam calceamentorum ejus.
     EN: And he proclaimed: One mightier than I is coming after me, whose sandal strap I am not worthy to stoop down and untie.

1:8  LA: Ego baptizavi vos aqua, ille vero baptizabit vos Spiritu Sancto.
     EN: I have baptized you with water, but he will baptize you with the Holy Spirit.

1:9  LA: Et factum est: in diebus illis venit Jesus a Nazareth Galilææ: et baptizatus est a Joanne in Jordane.
     EN: And it happened in those days that Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee and was baptized by John in the Jordan.

1:10  LA: Et statim ascendens de aqua, vidit cælos apertos, et Spiritum tamquam columbam descendentem, et manentem in ipso.
      EN: Immediately on coming up from the water, he saw the heavens opened and the Spirit descending like a dove and remaining upon him.

1:11  LA: Et vox facta est de cælis: Tu es Filius meus dilectus, in te complacui.
      EN: And a voice came from heaven: You are my beloved Son; in you I am well pleased.

1:12  LA: Et statim Spiritus expulit eum in desertum.
      EN: And immediately the Spirit drove him into the wilderness.

1:13  LA: Et erat in deserto quadraginta diebus, et quadraginta noctibus: et tentabatur a Satana: eratque cum bestiis, et angeli ministrabant illi.
      EN: He was in the wilderness forty days and forty nights, and was tempted by Satan; he was with the wild beasts, and the angels ministered to him.

1:14  LA: Postquam autem traditus est Joannes, venit Jesus in Galilæam, prædicans Evangelium regni Dei,
      EN: After John was handed over, Jesus came into Galilee, proclaiming the Gospel of the kingdom of God,

1:15  LA: et dicens: Quoniam impletum est tempus, et appropinquavit regnum Dei: pœnitemini, et credite Evangelio.
      EN: and saying: The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God has drawn near. Repent and believe the Gospel.

1:16  LA: Et præteriens secus mare Galilææ, vidit Simonem, et Andream fratrem ejus, mittentes retia in mare (erant enim piscatores),
      EN: Passing beside the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew his brother casting nets into the sea, for they were fishermen.

1:17  LA: et dixit eis Jesus: Venite post me, et faciam vos fieri piscatores hominum.
      EN: And Jesus said to them: Come after me, and I will make you become fishers of people.

1:18  LA: Et protinus relictis retibus, secuti sunt eum.
      EN: Immediately they left their nets and followed him.

1:19  LA: Et progressus inde pusillum, vidit Jacobum Zebedæi, et Joannem fratrem ejus, et ipsos componentes retia in navi:
      EN: Going a little farther from there, he saw James the son of Zebedee and John his brother, also mending their nets in a boat.

1:20  LA: et statim vocavit illos. Et relicto patre suo Zebedæo in navi cum mercenariis, secuti sunt eum.
      EN: Immediately he called them, and they left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired workers and followed him.

1:21  LA: Et ingrediuntur Capharnaum: et statim sabbatis ingressus in synagogam, docebat eos.
      EN: They entered Capernaum, and immediately on the Sabbath he entered the synagogue and taught them.

1:22  LA: Et stupebant super doctrina ejus: erat enim docens eos quasi potestatem habens, et non sicut scribæ.
      EN: They were astonished at his teaching, for he taught them as one having authority, and not as the scribes.

1:23  LA: Et erat in synagoga eorum homo in spiritu immundo: et exclamavit,
      EN: In their synagogue was a man with an unclean spirit, and he cried out,

1:24  LA: dicens: Quid nobis et tibi, Jesu Nazarene? venisti perdere nos? scio qui sis, Sanctus Dei.
      EN: saying: What have we to do with you, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are: the Holy One of God.

1:25  LA: Et comminatus est ei Jesus, dicens: Obmutesce, et exi de homine.
      EN: Jesus rebuked him, saying: Be silent, and come out of the man.

1:26  LA: Et discerpens eum spiritus immundus, et exclamans voce magna, exiit ab eo.
      EN: The unclean spirit convulsed him, cried out with a loud voice, and came out of him.

1:27  LA: Et mirati sunt omnes, ita ut conquirerent inter se dicentes: Quidnam est hoc? quænam doctrina hæc nova? quia in potestate etiam spiritibus immundis imperat, et obediunt ei.
      EN: They were all amazed, so that they asked one another: What is this? What is this new teaching? For with authority he commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey him.

1:28  LA: Et processit rumor ejus statim in omnem regionem Galilææ.
      EN: And the report of him immediately spread throughout the whole region of Galilee.

1:29  LA: Et protinus egredientes de synagoga, venerunt in domum Simonis et Andreæ, cum Jacobo et Joanne.
      EN: Immediately on leaving the synagogue, they came into the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John.

1:30  LA: Decumbebat autem socrus Simonis febricitans: et statim dicunt ei de illa.
      EN: Simon’s mother-in-law lay sick with a fever, and immediately they told him about her.

1:31  LA: Et accedens elevavit eam, apprehensa manu ejus: et continuo dimisit eam febris, et ministrabat eis.
      EN: He approached, took her by the hand, and raised her up. Immediately the fever left her, and she served them.

1:32  LA: Vespere autem facto cum occidisset sol, afferebant ad eum omnes male habentes, et dæmonia habentes:
      EN: When evening came, after the sun had set, they brought to him all who were ill and those who had demons.

1:33  LA: et erat omnis civitas congregata ad januam.
      EN: The whole city was gathered at the door.

1:34  LA: Et curavit multos, qui vexabantur variis languoribus, et dæmonia multa ejiciebat, et non sinebat ea loqui, quoniam sciebant eum.
      EN: He cured many who were afflicted with various illnesses, and cast out many demons. He did not allow them to speak, because they knew him.

1:35  LA: Et diluculo valde surgens, egressus abiit in desertum locum, ibique orabat.
      EN: Rising very early, he went out to a deserted place and prayed there.

1:36  LA: Et prosecutus est eum Simon, et qui cum illo erant.
      EN: Simon and those who were with him followed after him.

1:37  LA: Et cum invenissent eum, dixerunt ei: Quia omnes quærunt te.
      EN: When they found him, they said to him: Everyone is looking for you.

1:38  LA: Et ait illis: Eamus in proximos vicos, et civitates, ut et ibi prædicem: ad hoc enim veni.
      EN: He said to them: Let us go to the neighboring villages and towns, so that I may preach there also; for this is why I have come.

1:39  LA: Et erat prædicans in synagogis eorum, et in omni Galilæa, et dæmonia ejiciens.
      EN: He was preaching in their synagogues throughout all Galilee and casting out demons.

1:40  LA: Et venit ad eum leprosus deprecans eum: et genu flexo dixit ei: Si vis, potes me mundare.
      EN: A leper came to him, imploring him, and knelt down and said to him: If you will, you can make me clean.

1:41  LA: Jesus autem misertus ejus, extendit manum suam: et tangens eum, ait illi: Volo: mundare.
      EN: Jesus, moved with compassion for him, stretched out his hand, touched him, and said: I will; be clean.

1:42  LA: Et cum dixisset, statim discessit ab eo lepra, et mundatus est.
      EN: When he had spoken, immediately the leprosy left him, and he was cleansed.

1:43  LA: Et comminatus est ei, statimque ejecit illum,
      EN: He sternly warned him and immediately sent him away,

1:44  LA: et dicit ei: Vide nemini dixeris: sed vade, ostende te principi sacerdotum, et offer pro emundatione tua, quæ præcepit Moyses in testimonium illis.
      EN: and said to him: See that you tell no one, but go, show yourself to the chief priest, and offer for your cleansing what Moses commanded, as a testimony to them.

1:45  LA: At ille egressus cœpit prædicare, et diffamare sermonem, ita ut jam non posset manifeste introire in civitatem, sed foris in desertis locis esset, et conveniebant ad eum undique.
      EN: But he went out and began to proclaim and spread the account, so that Jesus could no longer enter a city openly, but remained outside in deserted places; and people came to him from everywhere.

MARK 2

2:1  LA: Et iterum intravit Capharnaum post dies,
     EN: And again he entered into Capernaum after some days.

2:2  LA: et auditum est quod in domo esset, et convenerunt multi, ita ut non caperet neque ad januam, et loquebatur eis verbum.
     EN: And it was heard that he was in the house. And many came together, so that there was no room: no, not even at the door. And he spoke to them the word.

2:3  LA: Et venerunt ad eum ferentes paralyticum, qui a quatuor portabatur.
     EN: And they came to him, bringing one sick of the palsy, who was carried by four.

2:4  LA: Et cum non possent offerre eum illi præ turba, nudaverunt tectum ubi erat: et patefacientes submiserunt grabatum in quo paralyticus jacebat.
     EN: And when they could not offer him to him for the multitude, they uncovered the roof where he was: and opening it, they let down the bed wherein the man sick of the palsy lay.

2:5  LA: Cum autem vidisset Jesus fidem illorum, ait paralytico: Fili, dimittuntur tibi peccata tua.
     EN: When Jesus saw their faith, he said to the paralyzed man: Son, your sins are forgiven you.

2:6  LA: Erant autem illic quidam de scribis sedentes, et cogitantes in cordibus suis:
     EN: And there were some of the scribes sitting there and thinking in their hearts:

2:7  LA: Quid hic sic loquitur? blasphemat. Quis potest dimittere peccata, nisi solus Deus?
     EN: Why does this man speak thus? He blasphemes. Who can forgive sins, but God only?

2:8  LA: Quo statim cognito Jesus spiritu suo, quia sic cogitarent intra se, dicit illis: Quid ista cogitatis in cordibus vestris?
     EN: Which Jesus immediately knowing in his spirit that they so thought within themselves, says to them: Why think you these things in your hearts?

2:9  LA: Quid est facilius dicere paralytico: Dimittuntur tibi peccata: an dicere: Surge, tolle grabatum tuum, et ambula?
     EN: Which is easier: to say to the paralyzed man, Your sins are forgiven, or to say, Rise, take up your mat, and walk?

2:10  LA: Ut autem sciatis quia Filius hominis habet potestatem in terra dimittendi peccata (ait paralytico),
      EN: But so that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins—he said to the paralyzed man—

2:11  LA: tibi dico: Surge, tolle grabatum tuum, et vade in domum tuam.
      EN: I say to you: Rise, take up your mat, and go to your house.

2:12  LA: Et statim surrexit ille: et, sublato grabato, abiit coram omnibus, ita ut mirarentur omnes, et honorificent Deum, dicentes: Quia numquam sic vidimus.
      EN: And immediately he arose and, taking up his bed, went his way in the sight of all: so that all wondered and glorified God, saying: We never saw the like.

2:13  LA: Et egressus est rursus ad mare, omnisque turba veniebat ad eum, et docebat eos.
      EN: And he went forth again to the sea side: and all the multitude came to him. And he taught them.

2:14  LA: Et cum præteriret, vidit Levi Alphæi sedentem ad telonium, et ait illi: Sequere me. Et surgens secutus est eum.
      EN: And when he was passing by, he saw Levi, the son of Alphaeus, sitting at the receipt of custom; and he says to him: Follow me. And rising up, he followed him.

2:15  LA: Et factum est, cum accumberet in domo illius, multi publicani et peccatores simul discumbebant cum Jesu et discipulis ejus: erant enim multi, qui et sequebantur eum.
      EN: And it happened as he sat at table in his house, many tax collectors and sinners sat down together with Jesus and his disciples. For they were many, who also followed him.

2:16  LA: Et scribæ et pharisæi videntes quia manducaret cum publicanis et peccatoribus, dicebant discipulis ejus: Quare cum publicanis et peccatoribus manducat et bibit Magister vester?
      EN: And the scribes and the Pharisees, seeing that he ate with tax collectors and sinners, said to his disciples: Why does your master eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?

2:17  LA: Hoc audito Jesus ait illis: Non necesse habent sani medico, sed qui male habent: non enim veni vocare justos, sed peccatores.
      EN: Jesus hearing this, says to them: They that are well do not need a physician, but they that are sick. For I came not to call the just, but sinners.

2:18  LA: Et erant discipuli Joannis et pharisæi jejunantes: et veniunt, et dicunt illi: Quare discipuli Joannis et pharisæorum jejunant, tui autem discipuli non jejunant?
      EN: And the disciples of John and the Pharisees used to fast. And they come and say to him: Why do the disciples of John and of the Pharisees fast; but your disciples do not fast?

2:19  LA: Et ait illis Jesus: Numquid possunt filii nuptiarum, quamdiu sponsus cum illis est, jejunare? Quanto tempore habent secum sponsum, non possunt jejunare.
      EN: And Jesus says to them: Can the children of the marriage fast, as long as the bridegroom is with them? As long as they have the bridegroom with them, they cannot fast.

2:20  LA: Venient autem dies cum auferetur ab eis sponsus: et tunc jejunabunt in illis diebus.
      EN: But the days will come when the bridegroom shall be taken away from them: and then they shall fast in those days.

2:21  LA: Nemo assumentum panni rudis assuit vestimento veteri: alioquin aufert supplementum novum a veteri, et major scissura fit.
      EN: No man sews a piece of raw cloth to an old garment: otherwise the new piecing takes away from the old, and there is made a greater rent.

2:22  LA: Et nemo mittit vinum novum in utres veteres: alioquin dirumpet vinum utres, et vinum effundetur, et utres peribunt: sed vinum novum in utres novos mitti debet.
      EN: And no one puts new wine into old bottles: otherwise the wine will burst the bottles, and both the wine will be spilled and the bottles will be lost. But new wine must be put into new bottles.

2:23  LA: Et factum est iterum Dominus sabbatis ambularet per sata, et discipuli ejus cœperunt progredi, et vellere spicas.
      EN: And it happened again, as the Lord walked through the grain fields on the sabbath, that his disciples began to go forward and to pluck the ears of grain.

2:24  LA: Pharisæi autem dicebant ei: Ecce, quid faciunt sabbatis quod non licet?
      EN: And the Pharisees said to him: Behold, why do they on the sabbath day that which is not lawful?

2:25  LA: Et ait illis: Numquam legistis quid fecerit David, quando necessitatem habuit, et esuriit ipse, et qui cum eo erant?
      EN: And he said to them: Have you never read what David did when he had need and was hungry, himself and they that were with him?

2:26  LA: quomodo introivit in domum Dei sub Abiathar principe sacerdotum, et panes propositionis manducavit, quos non licebat manducare, nisi sacerdotibus, et dedit eis qui cum eo erant?
      EN: How he went into the house of God, under Abiathar the high priest, and did eat the loaves of proposition, which was not lawful to eat but for the priests, and gave to them who were with him?

2:27  LA: Et dicebat eis: Sabbatum propter hominem factum est, et non homo propter sabbatum.
      EN: And he said to them: The Sabbath was made for humanity, and not humanity for the Sabbath.

2:28  LA: Itaque Dominus est Filius hominis, etiam sabbati.
      EN: Therefore the Son of Man is Lord even of the Sabbath.

MARK 3

3:1  LA: Et introivit iterum in synagogam: et erat ibi homo habens manum aridam.
     EN: And he entered again into the synagogue: and there was a man there who had a withered hand.

3:2  LA: Et observabant eum, si sabbatis curaret, ut accusarent illum.
     EN: And they watched him whether he would heal on the sabbath days, that they might accuse him.

3:3  LA: Et ait homini habenti manum aridam: Surge in medium.
     EN: And he said to the man who had the withered hand: Stand up in the midst.

3:4  LA: Et dicit eis: Licet sabbatis benefacere, an male? animam salvam facere, an perdere? At illi tacebant.
     EN: And he says to them: Is it lawful to do good on the sabbath days, or to do evil? To save life, or to destroy? But they held their peace.

3:5  LA: Et circumspiciens eos cum ira, contristatus super cæcitate cordis eorum, dicit homini: Extende manum tuam. Et extendit, et restituta est manus illi.
     EN: And looking round about on them with anger, being grieved for the blindness of their hearts, he says to the man: Stretch forth your hand. And he stretched it forth: and his hand was restored to him.

3:6  LA: Exeuntes autem pharisæi, statim cum Herodianis consilium faciebant adversus eum quomodo eum perderent.
     EN: And the Pharisees going out, immediately made a consultation with the Herodians against him, how they might destroy him.

3:7  LA: Jesus autem cum discipulis suis secessit ad mare: et multa turba a Galilæa et Judæa secuta est eum,
     EN: But Jesus retired with his disciples to the sea; and a great multitude followed him from Galilee and Judea,

3:8  LA: et ab Jerosolymis, et ab Idumæa, et trans Jordanem: et qui circa Tyrum et Sidonem multitudo magna, audientes quæ faciebat, venerunt ad eum.
     EN: And from Jerusalem, and from Idumea and from beyond the Jordan. And they about Tyre and Sidon, a great multitude, hearing the things which he did, came to him.

3:9  LA: Et dicit discipulis suis ut navicula sibi deserviret propter turbam, ne comprimerent eum:
     EN: And he spoke to his disciples that a small boat should wait on him, because of the multitude, lest they should throng him.

3:10  LA: multos enim sanabat, ita ut irruerent in eum ut illum tangerent, quotquot habebant plagas.
      EN: For he healed many, so that they pressed upon him for to touch him, as many as had evils.

3:11  LA: Et spiritus immundi, cum illum videbant, procidebant ei: et clamabant, dicentes:
      EN: And the unclean spirits, when they saw him, fell down before him: and they cried, saying:

3:12  LA: Tu es Filius Dei. Et vehementer comminabatur eis ne manifestarent illum.
      EN: You are the Son of God. And he strictly charged them that they should not make him known.

3:13  LA: Et ascendens in montem vocavit ad se quos voluit ipse: et venerunt ad eum.
      EN: And going up into a mountain, he called to him whom he would himself: and they came to him.

3:14  LA: Et fecit ut essent duodecim cum illo: et ut mitteret eos prædicare.
      EN: And he made that twelve should be with him, and that he might send them to preach.

3:15  LA: Et dedit illis potestatem curandi infirmitates et ejiciendi dæmonia.
      EN: And he gave them power to heal sicknesses, and to cast out demons.

3:16  LA: Et imposuit Simoni nomen Petrus:
      EN: And to Simon he gave the name Peter:

3:17  LA: et Jacobum Zebedæi, et Joannem fratrem Jacobi, et imposuit eis nomina Boanerges, quod est, Filii tonitrui:
      EN: And James the son of Zebedee, and John the brother of James; and he named them Boanerges, which is, The sons of thunder.

3:18  LA: et Andream, et Philippum, et Bartholomæum, et Matthæum, et Thomam, et Jacobum Alphæi, et Thaddæum, et Simonem Cananæum,
      EN: And Andrew and Philip, and Bartholomew and Matthew, and Thomas and James of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus and Simon the Cananean:

3:19  LA: et Judam Iscariotem, qui et tradidit illum.
      EN: And Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him.

3:20  LA: Et veniunt ad domum: et convenit iterum turba, ita ut non possent neque panem manducare.
      EN: And they come to a house, and the multitude comes together again, so that they could not so much as eat bread.

3:21  LA: Et cum audissent sui, exierunt tenere eum: dicebant enim: Quoniam in furorem versus est.
      EN: And when his friends had heard of it, they went out to seize him. For they said: He has become mad.

3:22  LA: Et scribæ, qui ab Jerosolymis descenderant, dicebant: Quoniam Beelzebub habet, et quia in principe dæmoniorum ejicit dæmonia.
      EN: And the scribes who had come down from Jerusalem, said: He has Beelzebub, and by the prince of demons he casts out demons.

3:23  LA: Et convocatis eis in parabolis dicebat illis: Quomodo potest Satanas Satanam ejicere?
      EN: And after he had called them together, he said to them in parables: How can Satan cast out Satan?

3:24  LA: Et si regnum in se dividatur, non potest regnum illud stare.
      EN: And if a kingdom be divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand.

3:25  LA: Et si domus super semetipsam dispertiatur, non potest domus illa stare.
      EN: And if a house be divided against itself, that house cannot stand.

3:26  LA: Et si Satanas consurrexerit in semetipsum, dispertitus est, et non poterit stare, sed finem habet.
      EN: And if Satan be risen up against himself, he is divided, and cannot stand, but has an end.

3:27  LA: Nemo potest vasa fortis ingressus in domum diripere, nisi prius fortem alliget, et tunc domum ejus diripiet.
      EN: No man can enter into the house of a strong man and rob him of his goods, unless he first bind the strong man, and then shall he plunder his house.

3:28  LA: Amen dico vobis, quoniam omnia dimittentur filiis hominum peccata, et blasphemiæ quibus blasphemaverint:
      EN: Amen, I say to you that all sins shall be forgiven to the sons of men, and the blasphemies wherewith they shall blaspheme:

3:29  LA: qui autem blasphemaverit in Spiritum Sanctum, non habebit remissionem in æternum, sed reus erit æterni delicti.
      EN: But he that shall blaspheme against the Holy Spirit, shall never have forgiveness, but shall be guilty of an everlasting sin.

3:30  LA: Quoniam dicebant: Spiritum immundum habet.
      EN: Because they said: He has an unclean spirit.

3:31  LA: Et veniunt mater ejus et fratres: et foris stantes miserunt ad eum vocantes eum,
      EN: And his mother and his brothers came; and standing without, sent to him, calling him.

3:32  LA: et sedebat circa eum turba: et dicunt ei: Ecce mater tua et fratres tui foris quærunt te.
      EN: And the multitude sat about him; and they say to him: Behold your mother and your brothers without seek for you.

3:33  LA: Et respondens eis, ait: Quæ est mater mea et fratres mei?
      EN: And answering them, he said: Who is my mother and my brothers?

3:34  LA: Et circumspiciens eos, qui in circuitu ejus sedebant, ait: Ecce mater mea et fratres mei.
      EN: And looking round about on them who sat about him, he says: Behold my mother and my brothers.

3:35  LA: Qui enim fecerit voluntatem Dei, hic frater meus, et soror mea, et mater est.
      EN: For whoever shall do the will of God, he is my brother, and my sister, and mother.

MARK 4

4:1  LA: Et iterum cœpit docere ad mare: et congregata est ad eum turba multa, ita ut navim ascendens sederet in mari, et omnis turba circa mare super terram erat:
     EN: And again he began to teach by the sea side; and a great multitude was gathered together to him, so that he went up into a boat and sat in the sea: and all the multitude was upon the land by the sea side.

4:2  LA: et docebat eos in parabolis multa, et dicebat illis in doctrina sua:
     EN: And he taught them many things in parables, and said to them in his doctrine:

4:3  LA: Audite: ecce exiit seminans ad seminandum.
     EN: Hear you: Behold, the sower went out to sow.

4:4  LA: Et dum seminat, aliud cecidit circa viam, et venerunt volucres cæli, et comederunt illud.
     EN: And while he sowed, some fell by the way side, and the birds of the air came and ate it up.

4:5  LA: Aliud vero cecidit super petrosa, ubi non habuit terram multam: et statim exortum est, quoniam non habebat altitudinem terræ:
     EN: And other some fell upon stony ground, where it had not much earth; and it shot up immediately, because it had no depth of earth.

MARK 16

16:1  LA: Et cum transisset sabbatum, Maria Magdalene, et Maria Jacobi, et Salome emerunt aromata ut venientes ungerent Jesum.
      EN: When the Sabbath had passed, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought spices so that they might come and anoint Jesus.

16:2  LA: Et valde mane una sabbatorum, veniunt ad monumentum, orto jam sole.
      EN: Very early on the first day of the week, they came to the tomb, the sun having already risen.

16:3  LA: Et dicebant ad invicem: Quis revolvet nobis lapidem ab ostio monumenti?
      EN: And they said to one another: Who will roll back the stone for us from the entrance of the tomb?

16:4  LA: Et respicientes viderunt revolutum lapidem. Erat quippe magnus valde.
      EN: Looking up, they saw that the stone had been rolled back, for it was very large.

16:5  LA: Et introëuntes in monumentum viderunt juvenem sedentem in dextris, coopertum stola candida, et obstupuerunt.
      EN: Entering the tomb, they saw a young man sitting on the right, clothed in a white robe, and they were astonished.

16:6  LA: Qui dicit illis: Nolite expavescere: Jesum quæritis Nazarenum, crucifixum: surrexit, non est hic, ecce locus ubi posuerunt eum.
      EN: He said to them: Do not be alarmed. You seek Jesus of Nazareth, who was crucified. He has risen; he is not here. Behold the place where they laid him.

16:7  LA: Sed ite, dicite discipulis ejus, et Petro, quia præcedit vos in Galilæam: ibi eum videbitis, sicut dixit vobis.
      EN: But go, tell his disciples and Peter that he goes before you into Galilee. There you will see him, as he told you.

16:8  LA: At illæ exeuntes, fugerunt de monumento: invaserat enim eas tremor et pavor: et nemini quidquam dixerunt: timebant enim.
      EN: They went out and fled from the tomb, for trembling and terror had seized them. They said nothing to anyone, for they were afraid.

16:9  LA: Surgens autem mane prima sabbati, apparuit primo Mariæ Magdalene, de qua ejecerat septem dæmonia.
      EN: Rising early on the first day of the week, he appeared first to Mary Magdalene, from whom he had cast out seven demons.

16:10  LA: Illa vadens nuntiavit his, qui cum eo fuerant, lugentibus et flentibus.
       EN: She went and told those who had been with him, as they mourned and wept.

16:11  LA: Et illi audientes quia viveret, et visus esset ab ea, non crediderunt.
       EN: When they heard that he was alive and had been seen by her, they did not believe.

16:12  LA: Post hæc autem duobus ex his ambulantibus ostensus est in alia effigie, euntibus in villam:
       EN: After these things he appeared in another form to two of them as they were walking into the countryside.

16:13  LA: et illi euntes nuntiaverunt ceteris: nec illis crediderunt.
       EN: They went and told the others, but they did not believe them either.

16:14  LA: Novissime recumbentibus illis undecim apparuit: et exprobravit incredulitatem eorum et duritiam cordis: quia iis, qui viderant eum resurrexisse, non crediderunt.
       EN: Finally he appeared to the eleven as they reclined at table, and reproached them for their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they had not believed those who had seen him risen.

16:15  LA: Et dixit eis: Euntes in mundum universum prædicate Evangelium omni creaturæ.
       EN: And he said to them: Go into the whole world and proclaim the Gospel to every creature.

16:16  LA: Qui crediderit, et baptizatus fuerit, salvus erit: qui vero non crediderit, condemnabitur.
       EN: Whoever believes and is baptized will be saved; but whoever does not believe will be condemned.

16:17  LA: Signa autem eos qui crediderint, hæc sequentur: in nomine meo dæmonia ejicient: linguis loquentur novis:
       EN: These signs will follow those who believe: in my name they will cast out demons; they will speak in new tongues;

16:18  LA: serpentes tollent: et si mortiferum quid biberint, non eis nocebit: super ægros manus imponent, et bene habebunt.
       EN: they will take up serpents; and if they drink anything deadly, it will not harm them; they will lay hands upon the sick, and they will be well.

16:19  LA: Et Dominus quidem Jesus postquam locutus est eis, assumptus est in cælum, et sedet a dextris Dei.
       EN: So the Lord Jesus, after speaking to them, was taken up into heaven and sits at the right hand of God.

16:20  LA: Illi autem profecti prædicaverunt ubique, Domino cooperante, et sermonem confirmante, sequentibus signis.
       EN: They went out and preached everywhere, the Lord working with them and confirming the word by the signs that followed.
