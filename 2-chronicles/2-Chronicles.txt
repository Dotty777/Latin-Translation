2 CHRONICLES 1

1:1  LA: Confortatus est ergo Salomon filius David in regno suo, et Dominus Deus ejus erat cum eo, et magnificavit eum in excelsum.
     EN: Solomon son of David grew strong in his kingdom. The Lord his God was with him and exalted him greatly.

1:2  LA: Præcepitque Salomon universo Israëli, tribunis, et centurionibus, et ducibus, et judicibus omnis Israël, et principibus familiarum :
     EN: Solomon gave orders to all Israel, to the commanders of thousands and hundreds, the leaders, the judges of all Israel, and the heads of families.

1:3  LA: et abiit cum universa multitudine in excelsum Gabaon, ubi erat tabernaculum fœderis Dei, quod fecit Moyses famulus Dei in solitudine.
     EN: He went with the whole multitude to the high place at Gibeon, where the tabernacle of God's covenant stood, which Moses the servant of God had made in the wilderness.

1:4  LA: Arcam autem Dei adduxerat David de Cariathiarim in locum quem præparaverat ei, et ubi fixerat illi tabernaculum, hoc est, in Jerusalem.
     EN: David had brought the ark of God from Kiriath-jearim to the place he had prepared for it, where he had pitched a tent for it, in Jerusalem.

1:5  LA: Altare quoque æneum quod fabricatus fuerat Beseleel filius Uri filii Hur, ibi erat coram tabernaculo Domini : quod et requisivit Salomon, et omnis ecclesia.
     EN: The bronze altar made by Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, was there before the Lord's tabernacle. Solomon and the whole assembly sought it out.

1:6  LA: Ascenditque Salomon ad altare æneum, coram tabernaculo fœderis Domini, et obtulit in eo mille hostias.
     EN: Solomon went up to the bronze altar before the tabernacle of the Lord's covenant and offered a thousand victims upon it.

1:7  LA: Ecce autem in ipsa nocte apparuit ei Deus, dicens : Postula quod vis, ut dem tibi.
     EN: That very night God appeared to him, saying, “Ask what you wish Me to give you.”

1:8  LA: Dixitque Salomon Deo : Tu fecisti cum David patre meo misericordiam magnam, et constituisti me regem pro eo.
     EN: Solomon said to God, “You showed great mercy to my father David and made me king in his place.

1:9  LA: Nunc ergo Domine Deus, impleatur sermo tuus quem pollicitus es David patri meo : tu enim me fecisti regem super populum tuum multum, qui tam innumerabilis est quam pulvis terræ.
     EN: Now, Lord God, let the word You promised my father David be fulfilled, for You have made me king over Your great people, as countless as the dust of the earth.

1:10  LA: Da mihi sapientiam et intelligentiam, ut ingrediar et egrediar coram populo tuo : quis enim potest hunc populum tuum digne, qui tam grandis est, judicare ?
      EN: Give me wisdom and understanding, so that I may go in and out before Your people. For who can worthily judge this people of Yours, so great as it is?”

1:11  LA: Dixit autem Deus ad Salomonem : Quia hoc magis placuit cordi tuo, et non postulasti divitias, et substantiam, et gloriam, neque animas eorum qui te oderant, sed nec dies vitæ plurimos : petisti autem sapientiam et scientiam, ut judicare possis populum meum super quem constitui te regem :
      EN: God said to Solomon, “Because this pleased your heart more, and you did not ask for riches, possessions, glory, the lives of those who hated you, or even many days of life, but asked for wisdom and knowledge to judge My people over whom I made you king,

1:12  LA: sapientia et scientia data sunt tibi : divitias autem et substantiam et gloriam dabo tibi, ita ut nullus in regibus nec ante te nec post te fuerit similis tui.
      EN: wisdom and knowledge are given to you. I will also give you riches, possessions, and glory, so that no king before or after you will be like you.”

1:13  LA: Venit ergo Salomon ab excelso Gabaon in Jerusalem coram tabernaculo fœderis, et regnavit super Israël.
      EN: Solomon came from the high place at Gibeon, from before the tabernacle of the covenant, to Jerusalem, and reigned over Israel.

1:14  LA: Congregavitque sibi currus et equites, et facti sunt ei mille quadringenti currus, et duodecim millia equitum : et fecit eos esse in urbibus quadrigarum, et cum rege in Jerusalem.
      EN: He gathered chariots and horsemen and had fourteen hundred chariots and twelve thousand horsemen. He stationed them in the chariot cities and with the king in Jerusalem.

1:15  LA: Præbuitque rex argentum et aurum in Jerusalem quasi lapides, et cedros quasi sycomoros quæ nascuntur in campestribus multitudine magna.
      EN: The king made silver and gold in Jerusalem as plentiful as stones, and cedars as plentiful as the sycamores growing in great numbers on the plains.

1:16  LA: Adducebantur autem ei equi de Ægypto et de Coa a negotiatoribus regis, qui ibant et emebant pretio,
      EN: Horses were brought to him from Egypt and Kue by the king's merchants, who went and bought them at a price:

1:17  LA: quadrigam equorum sexcentis argenteis, et equum centum quinquaginta : similiter de universis regnis Hethæorum, et a regibus Syriæ emptio celebrabatur.
      EN: a team of four horses for six hundred silver pieces and a horse for one hundred fifty. Likewise, purchases were made from all the kingdoms of the Hittites and from the kings of Syria.

1:18  LA: Decrevit autem Salomon ædificare domum nomini Domini, et palatium sibi.
      EN: Solomon decided to build a house for the Lord's name and a palace for himself.

2 CHRONICLES 2

2:1  LA: Et numeravit septuaginta millia virorum portantium humeris, et octoginta millia qui cæderent lapides in montibus, præpositosque eorum tria millia sexcentos.
     EN: He counted seventy thousand men to carry loads on their shoulders, eighty thousand to cut stones in the mountains, and thirty-six hundred overseers.

2:2  LA: Misit quoque ad Hiram regem Tyri, dicens : Sicut egisti cum David patre meo, et misisti ei ligna cedrina ut ædificaret sibi domum, in qua et habitavit :
     EN: He sent to Hiram king of Tyre, saying, “As you dealt with my father David and sent him cedar timber to build a house in which to live,

2:3  LA: sic fac mecum ut ædificem domum nomini Domini Dei mei, ut consecrem eam ad adolendum incensum coram illo, et fumiganda aromata, et ad propositionem panum sempiternam, et ad holocautomata mane, et vespere, sabbatis quoque, et neomeniis, et solemnitatibus Domini Dei nostri in sempiternum, quæ mandata sunt Israëli.
     EN: so deal with me, that I may build a house for the name of the Lord my God. I will consecrate it for burning incense and fragrant spices before Him, for the continual setting out of bread, and for burnt offerings morning and evening, on sabbaths, new moons, and the festivals of the Lord our God, commanded to Israel forever.

2:4  LA: Domus enim quam ædificare cupio, magna est : magnus est enim Deus noster super omnes deos.
     EN: The house I desire to build is great, for our God is great above all gods.

2:5  LA: Quis ergo poterit prævalere, ut ædificet ei dignam domum ? si cælum, et cæli cælorum, capere eum nequeunt, quantus ego sum, ut possim ædificare ei domum ? sed ad hoc tantum, ut adoleatur incensum coram illo.
     EN: Who could succeed in building Him a worthy house? If heaven and the heaven of heavens cannot contain Him, who am I to build Him a house, except solely for burning incense before Him?

2:6  LA: Mitte ergo mihi virum eruditum, qui noverit operari in auro, et argento, ære, et ferro, purpura, coccino, et hyacintho : et qui sciat sculpere cælaturas cum his artificibus quos mecum habeo in Judæa, et Jerusalem, quos præparavit David pater meus.
     EN: Send me a skilled man who knows how to work in gold, silver, bronze, iron, purple, scarlet, and blue, and how to engrave carvings with the craftsmen I have in Judah and Jerusalem, whom my father David provided.

2:7  LA: Sed et ligna cedrina mitte mihi, et arceuthina, et pinea de Libano : scio enim quod servi tui noverint cædere ligna de Libano : et erunt servi mei cum servis tuis,
     EN: Send me also cedar, juniper, and pine timber from Lebanon. I know that your servants know how to cut timber from Lebanon, and my servants will work with yours

2:8  LA: ut parentur mihi ligna plurima. Domus enim quam cupio ædificare, magna est nimis, et inclyta.
     EN: to prepare abundant timber for me, for the house I desire to build is exceedingly great and splendid.

2:9  LA: Præterea operariis qui cæsuri sunt ligna, servis tuis, dabo in cibaria tritici coros viginti millia, et hordei coros totidem, et vini viginti millia metretas, olei quoque sata viginti millia.
     EN: For the food of your servants, the workers who will cut the timber, I will give twenty thousand cors of wheat, as many cors of barley, twenty thousand metretes of wine, and twenty thousand seahs of oil.”

2:10  LA: Dixit autem Hiram rex Tyri per litteras quas miserat Salomoni : Quia dilexit Dominus populum suum, idcirco te regnare fecit super eum.
      EN: Hiram king of Tyre replied in a letter sent to Solomon: “Because the Lord loved His people, He made you king over them.”

2:11  LA: Et addidit, dicens : Benedictus Dominus Deus Israël, qui fecit cælum et terram : qui dedit David regi filium sapientem et eruditum et sensatum atque prudentem, ut ædificaret domum Domino, et palatium sibi.
      EN: He added, “Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who made heaven and earth, who gave King David a wise, learned, sensible, and prudent son to build a house for the Lord and a palace for himself.

2:12  LA: Misi ergo tibi virum prudentem et scientissimum Hiram patrem meum,
      EN: I have therefore sent you a prudent and highly skilled man, Hiram my father,

2:13  LA: filium mulieris de filiabus Dan, cujus pater fuit Tyrius, qui novit operari in auro, et argento, ære, et ferro, et marmore, et lignis, in purpura quoque, et hyacintho, et bysso, et coccino : et qui scit cælare omnem sculpturam, et adinvenire prudenter quodcumque in opere necessarium est cum artificibus tuis, et cum artificibus domini mei David patris tui.
      EN: the son of a woman of the daughters of Dan, whose father was a Tyrian. He knows how to work in gold, silver, bronze, iron, marble, wood, purple, blue, fine linen, and scarlet; he knows how to carve every kind of engraving and skillfully devise whatever is needed for the work, with your craftsmen and those of my lord David your father.

2:14  LA: Triticum ergo, et hordeum, et oleum, et vinum, quæ pollicitus es, domine mi, mitte servis tuis.
      EN: Now send your servants the wheat, barley, oil, and wine you promised, my lord.

2:15  LA: Nos autem cædemus ligna de Libano, quot necessaria habueris, et applicabimus ea ratibus per mare in Joppe : tuum autem erit transferre ea in Jerusalem.
      EN: We will cut as much timber from Lebanon as you need and bring it by sea on rafts to Joppa. You will then transport it to Jerusalem.”

2:16  LA: Numeravit igitur Salomon omnes viros proselytos qui erant in terra Israël, post dinumerationem quam dinumeravit David pater ejus, et inventi sunt centum quinquaginta millia, et tria millia sexcenti.
      EN: Solomon counted all the resident foreigners in the land of Israel after the census his father David had taken, and 153,600 were found.

2:17  LA: Fecitque ex eis septuaginta millia qui humeris onera portarent, et octoginta millia qui lapides in montibus cæderent : tria autem millia et sexcentos præpositos operum populi.
      EN: He appointed seventy thousand of them to carry loads on their shoulders, eighty thousand to cut stones in the mountains, and thirty-six hundred as overseers of the people's work.

2 CHRONICLES 3

3:1  LA: Et cœpit Salomon ædificare domum Domini in Jerusalem in monte Moria, qui demonstratus fuerat David patri ejus, in loco quem paraverat David in area Ornan Jebusæi.
     EN: Solomon began to build the Lord's house in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, which had been shown to his father David, at the place David had prepared on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

3:2  LA: Cœpit autem ædificare mense secundo, anno quarto regni sui.
     EN: He began building in the second month of the fourth year of his reign.

3:3  LA: Et hæc sunt fundamenta quæ jecit Salomon, ut ædificaret domum Dei : longitudinis cubitos in mensura prima sexaginta, latitudinis cubitos viginti.
     EN: These were the foundations Solomon laid for building God's house: sixty cubits long by the former measure and twenty cubits wide.

3:4  LA: Porticum vero ante frontem, quæ tendebatur in longum juxta mensuram latitudinis domus, cubitorum viginti : porro altitudo centum viginti cubitorum erat : et deauravit eam intrinsecus auro mundissimo.
     EN: The porch across the front extended twenty cubits along the width of the house; its height was one hundred twenty cubits. He overlaid its interior with purest gold.

3:5  LA: Domum quoque majorem texit tabulis ligneis abiegnis, et laminas auri obrizi affixit per totum : sculpsitque in ea palmas, et quasi catenulas se invicem complectentes.
     EN: He covered the larger chamber with fir boards and fastened plates of refined gold over all of it. He carved palm trees and interlocking chains upon it.

3:6  LA: Stravit quoque pavimentum templi pretiosissimo marmore, decore multo.
     EN: He also paved the temple floor with very precious marble of great beauty.

3:7  LA: Porro aurum erat probatissimum, de cujus laminis texit domum, et trabes ejus, et postes, et parietes, et ostia : et cælavit cherubim in parietibus.
     EN: The gold was of the finest quality. With its plates he covered the house, its beams, doorposts, walls, and doors, and carved cherubim on the walls.

3:8  LA: Fecit quoque domum Sancti sanctorum : longitudinem juxta latitudinem domus cubitorum viginti : et latitudinem similiter viginti cubitorum : et laminis aureis texit eam, quasi talentis sexcentis.
     EN: He made the chamber of the Holy of Holies, twenty cubits long across the width of the house and likewise twenty cubits wide. He covered it with gold plates, about six hundred talents.

3:9  LA: Sed et clavos fecit aureos, ita ut singuli clavi siclos quinquagenos appenderent : cœnacula quoque texit auro.
     EN: He made gold nails, each nail weighing fifty shekels. He also covered the upper rooms with gold.

3:10  LA: Fecit etiam in domo Sancti sanctorum cherubim duos, opere statuario : et texit eos auro.
      EN: He made two cherubim in the Holy of Holies as sculpted figures and covered them with gold.

3:11  LA: Alæ cherubim viginti cubitis extendebantur, ita ut una ala haberet cubitos quinque et tangeret parietem domus : et altera quinque cubitos habens, alam tangeret alterius cherub.
      EN: The wings of the cherubim extended twenty cubits. One wing was five cubits and touched the wall of the house; the other, five cubits long, touched the wing of the other cherub.

3:12  LA: Similiter cherub alterius ala, quinque habebat cubitos, et tangebat parietem : et ala ejus altera quinque cubitorum, alam cherub alterius contingebat.
      EN: Likewise, one wing of the other cherub was five cubits and touched the wall; its other wing of five cubits touched the wing of the other cherub.

3:13  LA: Igitur alæ utriusque cherubim expansæ erant et extendebantur per cubitos viginti : ipsi autem stabant erectis pedibus, et facies eorum erant versæ ad exteriorem domum.
      EN: The wings of the two cherubim were thus spread out and extended twenty cubits. They stood upright on their feet, with their faces turned toward the outer chamber.

3:14  LA: Fecit quoque velum ex hyacintho, purpura, cocco, et bysso : et intexuit ei cherubim.
      EN: He made the veil of blue, purple, scarlet, and fine linen, and wove cherubim into it.

3:15  LA: Ante fores etiam templi duas columnas, quæ triginta et quinque cubitos habebant altitudinis : porro capita earum, quinque cubitorum.
      EN: Before the temple doors he also made two pillars thirty-five cubits high, with capitals five cubits high.

3:16  LA: Necnon et quasi catenulas in oraculo, et superposuit eas capitibus columnarum : malogranata etiam centum, quæ catenulis interposuit.
      EN: He made chainlike ornaments in the inner sanctuary and placed them upon the capitals of the pillars, and a hundred pomegranates, which he placed among the chains.

3:17  LA: Ipsas quoque columnas posuit in vestibulo templi, unam a dextris, et alteram a sinistris : eam quæ a dextris erat, vocavit Jachin : et quæ ad lævam, Booz.
      EN: He set the pillars in the temple porch, one on the right and the other on the left. He called the one on the right Jachin and the one on the left Boaz.

2 CHRONICLES 4

4:1  LA: Fecit quoque altare æneum viginti cubitorum longitudinis, et viginti cubitorum latitudinis, et decem cubitorum altitudinis.
     EN: He made a bronze altar twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and ten cubits high.

4:2  LA: Mare etiam fusile decem cubitis a labio usque ad labium, rotundum per circuitum : quinque cubitos habebat altitudinis, et funiculus triginta cubitorum ambiebat gyrum ejus.
     EN: He also made the cast sea, ten cubits from rim to rim, circular all around. It was five cubits high, and a cord of thirty cubits encircled it.

4:3  LA: Similitudo quoque boum erat subter illud, et decem cubitis quædam extrinsecus cælaturæ, quasi duobus versibus alvum maris circuibant. Boves autem erant fusiles :
     EN: Beneath it were figures of oxen, with carvings outside extending ten cubits, surrounding the body of the sea in two rows. The oxen were cast.

4:4  LA: et ipsum mare super duodecim boves impositum erat, quorum tres respiciebant ad aquilonem, et alii tres ad occidentem : porro tres alii meridiem, et tres qui reliqui erant, orientem, habentes mare superpositum : posteriora autem boum erant intrinsecus sub mari.
     EN: The sea rested on twelve oxen: three faced north, three west, three south, and the remaining three east, with the sea resting above them. The oxen's hindquarters were inward beneath the sea.

4:5  LA: Porro vastitas ejus habebat mensuram palmi, et labium illius erat quasi labium calicis, vel repandi lilii : capiebatque tria millia metretas.
     EN: Its thickness measured a handbreadth, and its rim was like the rim of a cup or an open lily. It held three thousand metretes.

4:6  LA: Fecit quoque conchas decem : et posuit quinque a dextris, et quinque a sinistris, ut lavarent in eis omnia quæ in holocaustum oblaturi erant : porro in mari sacerdotes lavabantur.
     EN: He made ten basins and placed five on the right and five on the left, to wash in them everything to be offered as a burnt offering. The priests washed in the sea.

4:7  LA: Fecit autem et candelabra aurea decem secundum speciem qua jussa erant fieri : et posuit ea in templo, quinque a dextris, et quinque a sinistris.
     EN: He made ten gold lampstands according to the pattern commanded and placed them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left.

4:8  LA: Necnon et mensas decem : et posuit eas in templo, quinque a dextris, et quinque a sinistris : phialas quoque aureas centum.
     EN: He also made ten tables and placed them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left, and a hundred gold bowls.

4:9  LA: Fecit etiam atrium sacerdotum, et basilicam grandem : et ostia in basilica, quæ texit ære.
     EN: He made the priests' court and a great court, with doors in the great court, which he covered with bronze.

4:10  LA: Porro mare posuit in latere dextro contra orientem ad meridiem.
      EN: He placed the sea on the right side, toward the southeast.

4:11  LA: Fecit autem Hiram lebetes, et creagras, et phialas : et complevit omne opus regis in domo Dei :
      EN: Hiram made the pots, forks, and bowls, and completed all the king's work in God's house:

4:12  LA: hoc est, columnas duas, et epistylia, et capita, et quasi quædam retiacula, quæ capita tegerent super epistylia.
      EN: the two pillars, the architraves and capitals, and the latticework covering the capitals above the architraves;

4:13  LA: Malogranata quoque quadringenta, et retiacula duo ita ut bini ordines malogranatorum singulis retiaculis jungerentur, quæ protegerent epistylia, et capita columnarum.
      EN: also four hundred pomegranates and two lattices, with two rows of pomegranates attached to each lattice covering the architraves and capitals of the pillars.

4:14  LA: Bases etiam fecit, et conchas, quas superposuit basibus :
      EN: He made the stands and the basins placed upon the stands;

4:15  LA: mare unum, boves quoque duodecim sub mari,
      EN: the one sea and the twelve oxen beneath the sea;

4:16  LA: et lebetes, et creagras, et phialas. Omnia vasa fecit Salomoni Hiram pater ejus in domo Domini ex ære mundissimo.
      EN: and the pots, forks, and bowls. Hiram his father made all the vessels for Solomon in the Lord's house from purest bronze.

4:17  LA: In regione Jordanis, fudit ea rex in argillosa terra inter Sochot et Saredatha.
      EN: The king cast them in the clay soil of the Jordan region between Succoth and Zeredah.

4:18  LA: Erat autem multitudo vasorum innumerabilis, ita ut ignoraretur pondus æris.
      EN: The multitude of vessels was beyond counting, so that the weight of bronze was not known.

4:19  LA: Fecitque Salomon omnia vasa domus Dei, et altare aureum, et mensas, et super eas panes propositionis :
      EN: Solomon made all the vessels of God's house, the gold altar, and the tables with the bread of the Presence upon them;

4:20  LA: candelabra quoque cum lucernis suis ut lucerent ante oraculum juxta ritum ex auro purissimo :
      EN: the lampstands with their lamps of purest gold, to burn before the inner sanctuary according to the rite;

4:21  LA: et florentia quædam, et lucernas, et forcipes aureos : omnia de auro mundissimo facta sunt.
      EN: the flower ornaments, lamps, and gold tongs: all were made of purest gold.

4:22  LA: Thymiateria quoque, et thuribula, et phialas, et mortariola ex auro purissimo. Et ostia cælavit templi interioris, id est, in Sancta sanctorum : et ostia templi forinsecus aurea. Sicque completum est omne opus quod fecit Salomon in domo Domini.
      EN: The incense vessels, censers, bowls, and small mortars were also of purest gold. He carved the doors of the inner temple, the Holy of Holies, and the outer temple doors were gold. Thus all Solomon's work in the Lord's house was completed.

2 CHRONICLES 5

5:1  LA: Intulit igitur Salomon omnia quæ voverat David pater suus : argentum, et aurum, et universa vasa posuit in thesauris domus Dei.
     EN: Solomon brought in everything his father David had dedicated. He placed the silver, gold, and all the vessels in the treasuries of God's house.

5:2  LA: Post quæ congregavit majores natu Israël, et cunctos principes tribuum, et capita familiarum de filiis Israël in Jerusalem, ut adducerent arcam fœderis Domini de civitate David, quæ est Sion.
     EN: After this he gathered Israel's elders, all the tribal leaders, and the heads of families of the children of Israel in Jerusalem, to bring the ark of the Lord's covenant from the city of David, which is Zion.

5:3  LA: Venerunt itaque ad regem omnes viri Israël in die solemni mensis septimi.
     EN: All the men of Israel came to the king at the festival in the seventh month.

5:4  LA: Cumque venissent cuncti seniorum Israël, portaverunt Levitæ arcam,
     EN: When all the elders of Israel had arrived, the Levites carried the ark

5:5  LA: et intulerunt eam, et omnem paraturam tabernaculi. Porro vasa sanctuarii, quæ erant in tabernaculo, portaverunt sacerdotes cum Levitis.
     EN: and brought it in, together with all the furnishings of the tabernacle. The priests and Levites carried the sacred vessels that had been in the tabernacle.

5:6  LA: Rex autem Salomon, et universus cœtus Israël, et omnes qui fuerunt congregati ante arcam, immolabant arietes et boves absque ullo numero : tanta enim erat multitudo victimarum.
     EN: King Solomon, the entire assembly of Israel, and everyone gathered before the ark sacrificed rams and oxen without number, so great was the multitude of victims.

5:7  LA: Et intulerunt sacerdotes arcam fœderis Domini in locum suum, id est, ad oraculum templi, in Sancta sanctorum subter alas cherubim :
     EN: The priests brought the ark of the Lord's covenant to its place, into the temple's inner sanctuary, the Holy of Holies, beneath the wings of the cherubim.

5:8  LA: ita ut cherubim expanderent alas suas super locum in quo posita erat arca, et ipsam arcam tegerent cum vectibus suis.
     EN: The cherubim spread their wings above the place where the ark stood and covered the ark and its poles.

5:9  LA: Vectium autem quibus portabatur arca, quia paululum longiores erant, capita parebant ante oraculum : si vero quis paululum fuisset extrinsecus, eos videre non poterat. Fuit itaque arca ibi usque in præsentem diem.
     EN: Because the poles used to carry the ark were a little longer, their ends could be seen before the inner sanctuary, but anyone standing a little farther outside could not see them. The ark remained there to the present day.

5:10  LA: Nihilque erat aliud in arca, nisi duæ tabulæ quas posuerat Moyses in Horeb, quando legem dedit Dominus filiis Israël egredientibus ex Ægypto.
      EN: There was nothing in the ark except the two tablets Moses had placed there at Horeb, when the Lord gave the law to the children of Israel as they came out of Egypt.

5:11  LA: Egressis autem sacerdotibus de sanctuario (omnes enim sacerdotes qui ibi potuerant inveniri, sanctificati sunt : nec adhuc in illo tempore vices et ministeriorum ordo inter eos divisus erat),
      EN: When the priests came out of the sanctuary—for all the priests who could be found there had consecrated themselves, and at that time the courses and order of service had not yet been divided among them—

12:16  LA: Et dormivit Roboam cum patribus suis, sepultusque est in civitate David : et regnavit Abia filius ejus pro eo.
       EN: Rehoboam rested with his fathers and was buried in the city of David. His son Abijah reigned in his place.

2 CHRONICLES 13

13:1  LA: Anno octavodecimo regis Jeroboam, regnavit Abia super Judam.
      EN: In the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam, Abijah began to reign over Judah.

13:2  LA: Tribus annis regnavit in Jerusalem, nomenque matris ejus Michaia filia Uriel de Gabaa : et erat bellum inter Abiam et Jeroboam.
      EN: He reigned three years in Jerusalem. His mother was Micaiah daughter of Uriel from Gibeah. There was war between Abijah and Jeroboam.

13:3  LA: Cumque iniisset Abia certamen, et haberet bellicosissimos viros, et electorum quadringenta millia : Jeroboam instruxit econtra aciem octingenta millia virorum, qui et ipsi electi erant, et ad bella fortissimi.
      EN: Abijah entered the conflict with four hundred thousand chosen, highly experienced warriors. Jeroboam drew up opposite him eight hundred thousand men, also chosen and very mighty in battle.

13:4  LA: Stetit ergo Abia super montem Semeron, qui erat in Ephraim, et ait : Audi, Jeroboam, et omnis Israël.
      EN: Abijah stood on Mount Zemaraim in Ephraim and said, “Hear me, Jeroboam and all Israel!

13:5  LA: Num ignoratis quod Dominus Deus Israël dederit regnum David super Israël in sempiternum, ipsi et filiis ejus in pactum salis ?
      EN: Do you not know that the Lord God of Israel gave David the kingship over Israel forever, to him and his sons by a covenant of salt?

13:6  LA: Et surrexit Jeroboam filius Nabat, servus Salomonis filii David, et rebellavit contra dominum suum.
      EN: Jeroboam son of Nebat, the servant of Solomon son of David, rose up and rebelled against his master.

13:7  LA: Congregatique sunt ad eum viri vanissimi, et filii Belial, et prævaluerunt contra Roboam filium Salomonis : porro Roboam erat rudis, et corde pavido, nec potuit resistere eis.
      EN: Worthless men and sons of Belial gathered to him and prevailed against Rehoboam son of Solomon. Rehoboam was inexperienced and timid of heart and could not resist them.

13:8  LA: Nunc ergo vos dicitis quod resistere possitis regno Domini, quod possidet per filios David, habetisque grandem populi multitudinem, atque vitulos aureos quos fecit vobis Jeroboam in deos.
      EN: Now you say you can resist the Lord's kingdom, which He holds through David's sons. You have a great multitude of people and the gold calves Jeroboam made as gods for you.

13:9  LA: Et ejecistis sacerdotes Domini, filios Aaron, atque Levitas, et fecistis vobis sacerdotes sicut omnes populi terrarum : quicumque venerit, et initiaverit manum suam in tauro de bobus, et in arietibus septem, fit sacerdos eorum qui non sunt dii.
      EN: You expelled the Lord's priests, Aaron's sons, and the Levites, and made yourselves priests like all the peoples of the lands. Whoever comes and consecrates his hand with a bull from the herd and seven rams becomes a priest of those that are no gods.

13:10  LA: Noster autem Dominus, Deus est, quem non relinquimus, sacerdotesque ministrant Domino, de filiis Aaron, et Levitæ sunt in ordine suo :
       EN: But the Lord is our God, whom we do not abandon. Priests of Aaron's sons minister to the Lord, and the Levites are in their appointed order.

13:11  LA: holocausta quoque offerunt Domino per singulos dies mane et vespere, et thymiama juxta legis præcepta confectum, et proponuntur panes in mensa mundissima, estque apud nos candelabrum aureum, et lucernæ ejus, ut accendantur semper ad vesperam : nos quippe custodimus præcepta Domini Dei nostri, quem vos reliquistis.
       EN: They offer burnt offerings to the Lord every morning and evening, and incense prepared according to the law's commands. Bread is set upon the purest table, and we have the gold lampstand with its lamps to be lit every evening. We keep the commands of the Lord our God, whom you have abandoned.

13:12  LA: Ergo in exercitu nostro dux Deus est, et sacerdotes ejus, qui clangunt tubis, et resonant contra vos : filii Israël, nolite pugnare contra Dominum Deum patrum vestrorum, quia non vobis expedit.
       EN: God is the leader in our army, with His priests sounding trumpets and raising the alarm against you. Children of Israel, do not fight the Lord God of your fathers, for it will not go well for you.”

13:13  LA: Hæc illo loquente, Jeroboam retro moliebatur insidias. Cumque ex adverso hostium staret, ignorantem Judam suo ambiebat exercitu.
       EN: While he spoke, Jeroboam prepared an ambush from behind. As he stood facing his enemies, he surrounded unsuspecting Judah with his army.

13:14  LA: Respiciensque Judas, vidit instare bellum ex adverso et post tergum, et clamavit ad Dominum, ac sacerdotes tubis canere cœperunt.
       EN: Judah looked and saw battle pressing from the front and behind. They cried to the Lord, and the priests began sounding trumpets.

13:15  LA: Omnesque viri Juda vociferati sunt : et ecce illis clamantibus, perterruit Deus Jeroboam, et omnem Israël qui stabat ex adverso Abia et Juda.
       EN: All the men of Judah shouted. As they cried out, God terrified Jeroboam and all Israel standing opposite Abijah and Judah.

13:16  LA: Fugeruntque filii Israël Judam, et tradidit eos Deus in manu eorum.
       EN: The children of Israel fled from Judah, and God delivered them into their hands.

13:17  LA: Percussit ergo eos Abia et populus ejus plaga magna : et corruerunt vulnerati ex Israël quingenta millia virorum fortium.
       EN: Abijah and his people struck them with a great blow, and five hundred thousand mighty men of Israel fell wounded.

13:18  LA: Humiliatique sunt filii Israël in tempore illo, et vehementissime confortati filii Juda, eo quod sperassent in Domino Deo patrum suorum.
       EN: The children of Israel were humbled at that time, and the children of Judah grew exceedingly strong because they trusted in the Lord God of their fathers.

13:19  LA: Persecutus est autem Abia fugientem Jeroboam, et cepit civitates ejus, Bethel et filias ejus, et Jesana cum filiabus suis, Ephron quoque et filias ejus :
       EN: Abijah pursued the fleeing Jeroboam and captured his cities: Bethel and its dependent towns, Jeshanah with its dependent towns, and Ephron with its dependent towns.

13:20  LA: nec valuit ultra resistere Jeroboam in diebus Abia : quem percussit Dominus, et mortuus est.
       EN: Jeroboam could no longer resist in Abijah's days. The Lord struck him, and he died.

13:21  LA: Igitur Abia, confortato imperio suo, accepit uxores quatuordecim : procreavitque viginti duos filios, et sedecim filias.
       EN: Abijah strengthened his rule and took fourteen wives. He fathered twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters.

13:22  LA: Reliqua autem sermonum Abia, viarumque et operum ejus, scripta sunt diligentissime in libro Addo prophetæ.
       EN: The rest of Abijah's words, ways, and deeds are carefully recorded in the book of Iddo the prophet.

13:23  LA: Dormivit autem Abia cum patribus suis, et sepelierunt eum in civitate David : regnavitque Asa filius ejus pro eo, in cujus diebus quievit terra annis decem.
       EN: Abijah rested with his fathers and was buried in the city of David. His son Asa reigned in his place. In his days the land rested ten years.

2 CHRONICLES 14

14:1  LA: Fecit autem Asa quod bonum et placitum erat in conspectu Dei sui, et subvertit altaria peregrini cultus, et excelsa.
      EN: Asa did what was good and pleasing before his God, and overthrew the altars of foreign worship and the high places.

14:2  LA: Et confregit statuas, lucosque succidit :
      EN: He broke the statues and cut down the sacred groves.

14:3  LA: et præcepit Judæ ut quæreret Dominum Deum patrum suorum, et faceret legem, et universa mandata :
      EN: He commanded Judah to seek the Lord God of their fathers and observe the law and all the commandments.

14:4  LA: et abstulit de cunctis urbibus Juda aras et fana, et regnavit in pace.
      EN: He removed altars and shrines from all the cities of Judah and reigned in peace.

14:5  LA: Ædificavit quoque urbes munitas in Juda, quia quietus erat, et nulla temporibus ejus bella surrexerant, pacem Domino largiente.
      EN: He built fortified cities in Judah, because he was at rest and no wars arose in his time, as the Lord granted peace.

14:6  LA: Dixit autem Judæ : Ædificemus civitates istas, et vallemus muris, et roboremus turribus, et portis, et seris, donec a bellis quieta sunt omnia, eo quod quæsierimus Dominum Deum patrum nostrorum, et dederit nobis pacem per gyrum. Ædificaverunt igitur, et nullum in exstruendo impedimentum fuit.
      EN: He said to Judah, “Let us build these cities, surround them with walls, and strengthen them with towers, gates, and bolts, while everything is at rest from war. We have sought the Lord God of our fathers, and He has given us peace on every side.” They built, and nothing hindered the construction.

14:7  LA: Habuit autem Asa in exercitu suo portantium scuta et hastas de Juda trecenta millia, de Benjamin vero scutariorum et sagittariorum ducenta octoginta millia : omnes isti viri fortissimi.
      EN: Asa's army had three hundred thousand men of Judah carrying shields and spears and two hundred eighty thousand shield-bearers and archers from Benjamin. All were mighty men.

14:8  LA: Egressus est autem contra eos Zara Æthiops cum exercitu suo, decies centena millia, et curribus trecentis : et venit usque Maresa.
      EN: Zerah the Ethiopian came against them with an army of one million men and three hundred chariots, and advanced as far as Mareshah.

14:9  LA: Porro Asa perrexit obviam ei, et instruxit aciem ad bellum in valle Sephata, quæ est juxta Maresa :
      EN: Asa went out to meet him and drew up his battle line in the Valley of Zephathah near Mareshah.

14:10  LA: et invocavit Dominum Deum, et ait : Domine, non est apud te ulla distantia, utrum in paucis auxilieris, an in pluribus. Adjuva nos, Domine Deus noster : in te enim, et in tuo nomine habentes fiduciam, venimus contra hanc multitudinem. Domine, Deus noster tu es : non prævaleat contra te homo.
       EN: He called upon the Lord God, saying, “Lord, it makes no difference to You whether You help with few or with many. Help us, Lord our God, for trusting in You and Your name we have come against this multitude. Lord, You are our God; let no human being prevail against You.”

14:11  LA: Exterruit itaque Dominus Æthiopes coram Asa et Juda : fugeruntque Æthiopes.
       EN: The Lord terrified the Ethiopians before Asa and Judah, and the Ethiopians fled.

14:12  LA: Et persecutus est eos Asa, et populus qui cum eo erat, usque Gerara : et ruerunt Æthiopes usque ad internecionem, quia Domino cædente contriti sunt, et exercitu illius præliante. Tulerunt ergo spolia multa,
       EN: Asa and the people with him pursued them as far as Gerar. The Ethiopians fell until they were annihilated, crushed as the Lord struck and His army fought. They took much spoil,

14:13  LA: et percusserunt civitates omnes per circuitum Geraræ : grandis quippe cunctos terror invaserat : et diripuerunt urbes, et multam prædam asportaverunt.
       EN: struck all the cities around Gerar, and plundered them, carrying away much booty, for great terror had fallen upon everyone.

14:14  LA: Sed et caulas ovium destruentes, tulerunt pecorum infinitam multitudinem, et camelorum : reversique sunt in Jerusalem.
       EN: They destroyed the sheepfolds as well and took an immense multitude of livestock and camels, then returned to Jerusalem.

2 CHRONICLES 15

15:1  LA: Azarias autem filius Oded, facto in se spiritu Dei,
      EN: The Spirit of God came upon Azariah son of Oded,

15:2  LA: egressus est in occursum Asa, et dixit ei : Audite me, Asa, et omnis Juda et Benjamin : Dominus vobiscum, quia fuistis cum eo. Si quæsieritis eum, invenietis : si autem dereliqueritis eum, derelinquet vos.
      EN: and he went out to meet Asa and said, “Hear me, Asa and all Judah and Benjamin! The Lord is with you because you have been with Him. If you seek Him, you will find Him; if you abandon Him, He will abandon you.

15:3  LA: Transibant autem multi dies in Israël absque Deo vero, et absque sacerdote doctore, et absque lege.
      EN: Many days passed in Israel without the true God, without a priest to teach, and without the law.

15:4  LA: Cumque reversi fuerint in angustia sua ad Dominum Deum Israël, et quæsierint eum, reperient eum.
      EN: When they return in their distress to the Lord God of Israel and seek Him, they will find Him.

15:5  LA: In tempore illo, non erit pax egredienti et ingredienti, sed terrores undique in cunctis habitatoribus terrarum :
      EN: At that time there will be no peace for those going out and coming in, but terrors on every side among all the inhabitants of the lands.

15:6  LA: pugnavit enim gens contra gentem, et civitas contra civitatem, quia Dominus conturbabit eos in omni angustia.
      EN: Nation fought against nation and city against city, for the Lord will trouble them with every distress.

15:7  LA: Vos ergo confortamini, et non dissolvantur manus vestræ : erit enim merces operi vestro.
      EN: But you, be strong and do not let your hands grow weak, for your work will have its reward.”

15:8  LA: Quod cum audisset Asa, verba scilicet, et prophetiam Azariæ filii Oded prophetæ, confortatus est, et abstulit idola de omni terra Juda et de Benjamin, et ex urbibus quas ceperat, montis Ephraim : et dedicavit altare Domini quod erat ante porticum Domini.
      EN: When Asa heard these words and the prophecy of Azariah son of Oded the prophet, he took courage and removed the idols from all Judah and Benjamin and the cities he had captured in the hill country of Ephraim. He dedicated the Lord's altar before the Lord's porch.

15:9  LA: Congregavitque universum Judam et Benjamin, et advenas cum eis de Ephraim, et de Manasse, et de Simeon : plures enim ad eum confugerant ex Israël, videntes quod Dominus Deus illius esset cum eo.
      EN: He gathered all Judah and Benjamin, and the foreigners with them from Ephraim, Manasseh, and Simeon, for many had fled to him from Israel, seeing that the Lord his God was with him.

15:10  LA: Cumque venissent in Jerusalem mense tertio, anno decimoquinto regni Asa,
       EN: They came to Jerusalem in the third month of the fifteenth year of Asa's reign.

15:11  LA: immolaverunt Domino in die illa de manubiis et præda quam adduxerant, boves septingentos, et arietes septem millia.
       EN: That day they sacrificed to the Lord seven hundred oxen and seven thousand rams from the spoils and booty they had brought.

15:12  LA: Et intravit ex more ad corroborandum fœdus ut quærerent Dominum Deum patrum suorum in toto corde, et in tota anima sua.
       EN: He entered according to custom to confirm the covenant, that they would seek the Lord God of their fathers with all their heart and all their soul.

15:13  LA: Si quis autem, inquit, non quæsierit Dominum Deum Israël, moriatur, a minimo usque ad maximum, a viro usque ad mulierem.
       EN: He said, “Whoever does not seek the Lord God of Israel shall die, from the least to the greatest, man or woman.”

15:14  LA: Juraveruntque Domino voce magna in jubilo, et in clangore tubæ, et in sonitu buccinarum,
       EN: They swore to the Lord with a loud voice, joyful shouting, the blast of the trumpet, and the sound of horns,

15:15  LA: omnes qui erant in Juda, cum execratione : in omni enim corde suo juraverunt, et in tota voluntate quæsierunt eum, et invenerunt : præstititque eis Dominus requiem per circuitum.
       EN: all who were in Judah, with a curse. They swore with all their heart and sought Him with their whole will, and found Him. The Lord gave them rest on every side.

15:16  LA: Sed et Maacham matrem Asa regis ex augusto deposuit imperio, eo quod fecisset in luco simulacrum Priapi : quod omne contrivit, et in frustra comminuens combussit in torrente Cedron.
       EN: He also removed Maacah, King Asa's mother, from her exalted authority, because she had made an image of Priapus in a sacred grove. He crushed it, broke it to pieces, and burned it in the Kidron torrent.

15:17  LA: Excelsa autem derelicta sunt in Israël : attamen cor Asa erat perfectum cunctis diebus ejus,
       EN: The high places remained in Israel, but Asa's heart was whole all his days.

15:18  LA: eaque quæ voverat pater suus, et ipse, intulit in domum Domini, argentum, et aurum, vasorumque diversam supellectilem.
       EN: He brought into the Lord's house the things his father and he himself had dedicated: silver, gold, and various furnishings and vessels.

15:19  LA: Bellum vero non fuit usque ad trigesimum quintum annum regni Asa.
       EN: There was no war until the thirty-fifth year of Asa's reign.

2 CHRONICLES 16

16:1  LA: Anno autem trigesimo sexto regni ejus, ascendit Baasa rex Israël in Judam, et muro circumdabat Rama, ut nullus tute posset egredi et ingredi de regno Asa.
      EN: In the thirty-sixth year of his reign, Baasha king of Israel advanced into Judah and surrounded Ramah with a wall, so that no one could safely go out from or enter Asa's kingdom.

16:2  LA: Protulit ergo Asa argentum et aurum de thesauris domus Domini, et de thesauris regis, misitque ad Benadad regem Syriæ, qui habitabat in Damasco, dicens :
      EN: Asa brought silver and gold from the treasuries of the Lord's house and the king's treasuries and sent them to Ben-hadad king of Syria, who lived in Damascus, saying,

16:3  LA: Fœdus inter me et te est ; pater quoque meus et pater tuus habuere concordiam : quam ob rem misi tibi argentum et aurum, ut rupto fœdere quod habes cum Baasa rege Israël, facias eum a me recedere.
      EN: “There is a covenant between me and you, as there was an agreement between my father and yours. Therefore I have sent you silver and gold. Break your covenant with Baasha king of Israel, and make him withdraw from me.”

16:4  LA: Quo comperto, Benadad misit principes exercituum suorum ad urbes Israël : qui percusserunt Ahion, et Dan, et Abelmaim, et universas urbes Nephthali muratas.
      EN: When he learned this, Ben-hadad sent his army commanders against Israel's cities. They struck Ijon, Dan, Abel-maim, and all the walled cities of Naphtali.

16:5  LA: Quod cum audisset Baasa, desiit ædificare Rama, et intermisit opus suum.
      EN: When Baasha heard, he stopped building Ramah and abandoned his work.

16:6  LA: Porro Asa rex assumpsit universum Judam, et tulerunt lapides de Rama, et ligna quæ ædificationi præparaverat Baasa, ædificavitque ex eis Gabaa et Maspha.
      EN: King Asa took all Judah, and they carried away the stones and timber Baasha had prepared for building Ramah. With them he built Geba and Mizpah.

16:7  LA: In tempore illo venit Hanani propheta ad Asa regem Juda, et dixit ei : Quia habuisti fiduciam in rege Syriæ, et non in Domino Deo tuo, idcirco evasit Syriæ regis exercitus de manu tua.
      EN: At that time Hanani the prophet came to Asa king of Judah and said, “Because you trusted in the king of Syria and not in the Lord your God, the king of Syria's army has escaped your hand.

16:8  LA: Nonne Æthiopes et Libyes multo plures erant quadrigis, et equitibus, et multitudine nimia, quos cum Domino credidisses, tradidit in manu tua ?
      EN: Were not the Ethiopians and Libyans far greater in chariots, horsemen, and immense numbers? Yet because you trusted the Lord, He delivered them into your hand.

16:9  LA: Oculi enim Domini contemplantur universam terram, et præbent fortitudinem his qui corde perfecto credunt in eum. Stulte igitur egisti, et propter hoc ex præsenti tempore adversum te bella consurgent.
      EN: The Lord's eyes survey all the earth and give strength to those who trust Him with a whole heart. You have acted foolishly, and from now on wars will arise against you.”

16:10  LA: Iratusque Asa adversus videntem, jussit eum mitti in nervum : valde quippe super hoc fuerat indignatus : et interfecit de populo in tempore illo plurimos.
       EN: Asa grew angry with the seer and ordered him put in the stocks, for he was greatly enraged by this. At that time he killed very many of the people.

16:11  LA: Opera autem Asa prima et novissima scripta sunt in libro regum Juda et Israël.
       EN: Asa's deeds, first and last, are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.

16:12  LA: Ægrotavit etiam Asa anno trigesimo nono regni sui, dolore pedum vehementissimo, et nec in infirmitate sua quæsivit Dominum, sed magis in medicorum arte confisus est.
       EN: In the thirty-ninth year of his reign Asa suffered very severe pain in his feet. Even in his illness he did not seek the Lord but trusted instead in the physicians' art.

16:13  LA: Dormivitque cum patribus suis, et mortuus est anno quadragesimo primo regni sui.
       EN: He rested with his fathers and died in the forty-first year of his reign.

16:14  LA: Et sepelierunt eum in sepulchro suo quod foderat sibi in civitate David : posueruntque eum super lectum suum plenum aromatibus et unguentibus meretriciis, quæ erant pigmentariorum arte confecta, et combusserunt super eum ambitione nimia.
       EN: They buried him in the tomb he had cut for himself in the city of David. They laid him upon his bed, filled with spices and seductive ointments prepared by the perfumers' art, and made a burning over him with exceedingly lavish display.

2 CHRONICLES 17

17:1  LA: Regnavit autem Josaphat filius ejus pro eo, et invaluit contra Israël.
      EN: His son Jehoshaphat reigned in his place and grew strong against Israel.

17:2  LA: Constituitque militum numeros in cunctis urbibus Juda quæ erant vallatæ muris. Præsidiaque disposuit in terra Juda, et in civitatibus Ephraim quas ceperat Asa pater ejus.
      EN: He stationed companies of soldiers in all Judah's walled cities and placed garrisons throughout Judah and in the cities of Ephraim his father Asa had captured.

17:3  LA: Et fuit Dominus cum Josaphat, quia ambulavit in viis David patris sui primis : et non speravit in Baalim,
      EN: The Lord was with Jehoshaphat because he walked in the earlier ways of David his father. He did not trust in the Baals,

17:4  LA: sed in Deo patris sui : et perrexit in præceptis illius, et non juxta peccata Israël.
      EN: but in his father's God, and followed His commands, not Israel's sins.

17:5  LA: Confirmavitque Dominus regnum in manu ejus, et dedit omnis Juda munera Josaphat : factæque sunt ei infinitæ divitiæ, et multa gloria.
      EN: The Lord established the kingdom in his hand. All Judah brought gifts to Jehoshaphat, and he acquired immense wealth and great glory.

17:6  LA: Cumque sumpsisset cor ejus audaciam propter vias Domini, etiam excelsa et lucos de Juda abstulit.
      EN: His heart took courage in the Lord's ways, and he also removed the high places and sacred groves from Judah.

17:7  LA: Tertio autem anno regni sui misit de principibus suis Benhail, et Obdiam, et Zachariam, et Nathanaël, et Michæam, ut docerent in civitatibus Juda :
      EN: In the third year of his reign he sent his officials Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah to teach in Judah's cities.

17:8  LA: et cum eis Levitas Semeiam, et Nathaniam, et Zabadiam, Asaël quoque, et Semiramoth, et Jonathan, Adoniamque et Thobiam, et Thobadoniam Levitas, et cum eis Elisama, et Joran sacerdotes :
      EN: With them were the Levites Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-adonijah, and with them the priests Elishama and Jehoram.

17:9  LA: docebantque populum in Juda, habentes librum legis Domini, et circuibant cunctas urbes Juda, atque erudiebant populum.
      EN: They taught the people in Judah, having the book of the Lord's law. They went through all Judah's cities and instructed the people.

17:10  LA: Itaque factus est pavor Domini super omnia regna terrarum quæ erant per gyrum Juda, nec audebant bellare contra Josaphat.
       EN: Dread of the Lord fell upon all the kingdoms of the lands surrounding Judah, and they did not dare wage war against Jehoshaphat.

17:11  LA: Sed et Philisthæi Josaphat munera deferebant, et vectigal argenti : Arabes quoque adducebant pecora, arietum septem millia septingenta, et hircorum totidem.
       EN: The Philistines brought Jehoshaphat gifts and tribute in silver. The Arabs brought livestock: seventy-seven hundred rams and as many male goats.

17:12  LA: Crevit ergo Josaphat, et magnificatus est usque in sublime : atque ædificavit in Juda domos ad instar turrium, urbesque muratas.
       EN: Jehoshaphat grew and was greatly exalted. He built towerlike houses and walled cities in Judah.

17:13  LA: Et multa opera paravit in urbibus Juda : viri quoque bellatores et robusti erant in Jerusalem,
       EN: He prepared many works in Judah's cities. There were mighty warriors in Jerusalem,

17:14  LA: quorum iste numerus per domos atque familias singulorum : in Juda principes exercitus, Ednas dux, et cum eo robustissimi viri trecenta millia.
       EN: whose numbers by their houses and families were these: among the army leaders in Judah, Adnah the commander, with three hundred thousand mighty men;

17:15  LA: Post hunc Johanan princeps, et cum eo ducenta octoginta millia.
       EN: after him Jehohanan the commander, with two hundred eighty thousand;

17:16  LA: Post istum quoque Amasias filius Zechri, consecratus Domino, et cum eo ducenta millia virorum fortium.
       EN: after him Amasiah son of Zichri, consecrated to the Lord, with two hundred thousand mighty men.

       EN: the Lord will strike you with a great plague, together with your people, your children, your wives, and all your possessions.

       EN: They invaded Judah, devastated it, and plundered all the property found in the king's house, as well as his sons and wives. No son remained to him except Jehoahaz, his youngest.

       EN: Beyond all this, the Lord struck him with an incurable bowel disease.

Mortuusque est in infirmitate pessima, et non fecit ei populus secundum morem combustionis exequias, sicut fecerat majoribus ejus.
       EN: Day followed day and time passed until two years were complete. Wasted away by prolonged decay, he discharged even his entrails and lost both his illness and his life. He died of a terrible disease, and the people did not hold a funeral burning for him according to custom as they had for his ancestors.

21:20  LA: Triginta duorum annorum fuit cum regnare cœpisset, et octo annis regnavit in Jerusalem. Ambulavitque non recte, et sepelierunt eum in civitate David, verumtamen non in sepulchro regum.
       EN: He was thirty-two when he began to reign and reigned eight years in Jerusalem. He did not walk rightly. They buried him in the city of David, but not in the royal tombs.

2 CHRONICLES 22

      EN: Jerusalem's inhabitants made his youngest son Ahaziah king in his place, for Arab raiders who attacked the camp had killed all those older than he. Thus Ahaziah son of Jehoram king of Judah reigned.

22:2  LA: Quadraginta duorum annorum erat Ochozias cum regnare cœpisset, et uno anno regnavit in Jerusalem : et nomen matris ejus Athalia filia Amri.
      EN: Ahaziah was forty-two when he began to reign and reigned one year in Jerusalem. His mother was Athaliah daughter of Omri.

22:3  LA: Sed et ipse ingressus est per vias domus Achab : mater enim ejus impulit eum ut impie ageret.
      EN: He also followed the ways of Ahab's house, for his mother urged him to act wickedly.

22:4  LA: Fecit igitur malum in conspectu Domini, sicut domus Achab : ipsi enim fuerunt ei consiliarii post mortem patris sui, in interitum ejus :
      EN: He did evil in the Lord's sight like Ahab's house, for after his father's death they became his advisers, to his destruction.

22:5  LA: ambulavitque in consiliis eorum. Et perrexit cum Joram filio Achab rege Israël in bellum contra Hazaël regem Syriæ in Ramoth Galaad : vulneraveruntque Syri Joram.
      EN: He followed their advice and went with Joram son of Ahab, king of Israel, to war against Hazael king of Syria at Ramoth-gilead. The Syrians wounded Joram,

22:6  LA: Qui reversus est ut curaretur in Jezrahel : multas enim plagas acceperat in supradicto certamine. Igitur Ochozias filius Joram rex Juda descendit ut inviseret Joram filium Achab in Jezrahel ægrotantem.
      EN: who returned to Jezreel to recover, for he had received many wounds in that battle. Ahaziah son of Jehoram, king of Judah, went down to visit Joram son of Ahab while he was ill at Jezreel.

22:7  LA: Voluntatis quippe fuit Dei adversus Ochoziam, ut veniret ad Joram : et cum venisset, et egrederetur cum eo adversum Jehu filium Namsi, quem unxit Dominus ut deleret domum Achab.
      EN: It was God's will against Ahaziah that he should come to Joram and, on arriving, go out with him against Jehu son of Nimshi, whom the Lord had anointed to destroy Ahab's house.

22:8  LA: Cum ergo everteret Jehu domum Achab, invenit principes Juda, et filios fratrum Ochoziæ, qui ministrabant ei, et interfecit illos.
      EN: While Jehu was overthrowing Ahab's house, he found Judah's leaders and the sons of Ahaziah's brothers who served him, and killed them.

22:9  LA: Ipsum quoque perquirens Ochoziam, comprehendit latitantem in Samaria : adductumque ad se, occidit : et sepelierunt eum, eo quod esset filius Josaphat, qui quæsierat Dominum in toto corde suo. Nec erat ultra spes aliqua ut de stirpe quis regnaret Ochoziæ :
      EN: He also searched for Ahaziah and captured him hiding in Samaria. He was brought to him and killed. They buried him because he was a son of Jehoshaphat, who had sought the Lord with all his heart. There was no longer any hope that someone of Ahaziah's line would reign,

22:10  LA: siquidem Athalia mater ejus, videns quod mortuus esset filius suus, surrexit, et interfecit omnem stirpem regiam domus Joram.
       EN: for his mother Athaliah, seeing that her son was dead, rose and killed all the royal offspring of Jehoram's house.

22:11  LA: Porro Josabeth filia regis tulit Joas filium Ochoziæ, et furata est eum de medio filiorum regis, cum interficerentur : absconditque eum cum nutrice sua in cubiculo lectulorum : Josabeth autem, quæ absconderat eum, erat filia regis Joram, uxor Jojadæ pontificis, soror Ochoziæ : et idcirco Athalia non interfecit eum.
       EN: But Jehosheba the king's daughter took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him from among the king's sons as they were being killed. She hid him and his nurse in a bedroom. Jehosheba, who hid him, was King Jehoram's daughter, the wife of Jehoiada the high priest, and Ahaziah's sister. Thus Athaliah did not kill him.

22:12  LA: Fuit ergo cum eis in domo Dei absconditus sex annis, quibus regnavit Athalia super terram.
       EN: He remained hidden with them in God's house for six years, while Athaliah reigned over the land.

2 CHRONICLES 23

23:1  LA: Anno autem septimo, confortatus Jojada, assumpsit centuriones, Azariam videlicet filium Jeroham, et Ismahel filium Johanan, Azariam quoque filium Obed, et Maasiam filium Adaiæ, et Elisaphat filium Zechri : et iniit cum eis fœdus.
      EN: In the seventh year Jehoiada took courage and gathered the commanders of hundreds: Azariah son of Jeroham, Ishmael son of Jehohanan, Azariah son of Obed, Maaseiah son of Adaiah, and Elishaphat son of Zichri. He made a covenant with them.

23:2  LA: Qui circumeuntes Judam, congregaverunt Levitas de cunctis urbibus Juda, et principes familiarum Israël, veneruntque in Jerusalem.
      EN: They traveled through Judah, gathered the Levites from all Judah's cities and the heads of Israel's families, and came to Jerusalem.

      EN: The Levites and all Judah did everything Jehoiada the high priest commanded. Each took the men under him who were coming on duty for the sabbath, along with those whose sabbath service was finished and who were to depart, for Jehoiada the high priest had not dismissed the companies that normally relieved one another each week.

23:9  LA: Deditque Jojada sacerdos centurionibus lanceas, clypeosque et peltas regis David, quas consecraverat in domo Domini.
      EN: Jehoiada the priest gave the commanders of hundreds King David's spears and large and small shields, which he had consecrated in the Lord's house.

23:10  LA: Constituitque omnem populum tenentium pugiones a parte templi dextra, usque ad partem templi sinistram, coram altari et templo, per circuitum regis.
       EN: He stationed all the people holding daggers from the right side of the temple to the left, before the altar and temple, around the king.

23:11  LA: Et eduxerunt filium regis, et imposuerunt ei diadema et testimonium, dederuntque in manu ejus tenendam legem, et constituerunt eum regem : unxit quoque illum Jojada pontifex, et filii ejus : imprecatique sunt ei, atque dixerunt : Vivat rex.
       EN: They brought out the king's son, put the crown and the testimony upon him, placed the law in his hand to hold, and made him king. Jehoiada the high priest and his sons anointed him, prayed for him, and said, “Long live the king!”

23:12  LA: Quod cum audisset Athalia, vocem scilicet currentium atque laudantium regem, ingressa est ad populum in templum Domini.
       EN: When Athaliah heard the sound of people running and praising the king, she went to the people in the Lord's temple.

       EN: She saw the king standing on the step at the entrance, with the leaders and troops around him, and all the people of the land rejoicing, sounding trumpets, playing instruments of various kinds, and raising their voices in praise. She tore her clothes and cried, “Treason! Treason!”

       EN: They laid their hands upon her neck, and when she entered the Horse Gate of the king's house, they killed her there.

       EN: Jehoiada made a covenant between himself, all the people, and the king, that they would be the Lord's people.

       EN: All the people entered Baal's house and destroyed it, breaking its altars and images. They killed Mattan, Baal's priest, before the altars.

       EN: Jehoiada appointed overseers in the Lord's house under the priests and Levites whom David had arranged in the Lord's house, to offer burnt offerings to the Lord as written in Moses' law, with joy and songs according to David's arrangement.

       EN: He appointed gatekeepers at the gates of the Lord's house, so that no one unclean in any respect could enter.

23:20  LA: Assumpsitque centuriones, et fortissimos viros, ac principes populi, et omne vulgus terræ, et fecerunt descendere regem de domo Domini, et introire per medium portæ superioris in domum regis, et collocaverunt eum in solio regali.
       EN: He took the commanders of hundreds, mighty men, leaders of the people, and all the people of the land. They brought the king down from the Lord's house through the Upper Gate into the king's house and seated him on the royal throne.

23:21  LA: Lætatusque est omnis populus terræ, et urbs quievit : porro Athalia interfecta est gladio.
       EN: All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet. Athaliah had been killed with the sword.

Et congregata est infinita pecunia,

       EN: The king and Jehoiada gave it to those overseeing the work on the Lord's house. They used it to hire stonecutters and craftsmen of every trade to restore the Lord's house, and workers in iron and bronze to support what had begun to fall.

       EN: The workers labored diligently, and through their hands the breaches in the walls were repaired. They restored the Lord's house to its former condition and made it stand firm.

24:14  LA: Cumque complessent omnia opera, detulerunt coram rege et Jojada reliquam partem pecuniæ : de qua facta sunt vasa templi in ministerium, et ad holocausta, phialæ quoque, et cetera vasa aurea et argentea : offerebantur holocausta in domo Domini jugiter cunctis diebus Jojadæ.

       EN: Jehoiada grew old, full of days, and died at one hundred thirty years of age.

       EN: They buried him in the city of David with the kings, because he had done good for Israel and for his house.

       EN: After Jehoiada died, Judah's leaders came and paid homage to the king. Flattered by their attentions, he yielded to them.

       EN: They abandoned the temple of the Lord God of their fathers and served sacred groves and carved images. Wrath came upon Judah and Jerusalem because of this sin.

24:19  LA: Mittebatque eis prophetas ut reverterentur ad Dominum, quos protestantes illi audire nolebant.
       EN: He sent prophets to bring them back to the Lord, but although they warned them, they refused to listen.

       EN: The Spirit of God clothed Zechariah the priest, son of Jehoiada. He stood before the people and said, “Thus says the Lord God: Why do you transgress the Lord’s command? It will not benefit you. You have abandoned the Lord, so that He has abandoned you.”

24:21  LA: Qui congregati adversus eum, miserunt lapides juxta regis imperium in atrio domus Domini.
       EN: They gathered against him and stoned him at the king's command in the court of the Lord's house.

24:22  LA: Et non est recordatus Joas rex misericordiæ quam fecerat Jojada pater illius secum, sed interfecit filium ejus. Qui cum moreretur, ait : Videat Dominus, et requirat.
       EN: King Joash did not remember the mercy that Zechariah’s father Jehoiada had shown him, but killed his son. As he died, he said, “May the Lord see and demand an account.”

24:23  LA: Cumque evolutus esset annus, ascendit contra eum exercitus Syriæ : venitque in Judam et Jerusalem, et interfecit cunctos principes populi, atque universam prædam miserunt regi in Damascum.
       EN: When the year had passed, the Syrian army came against him. It entered Judah and Jerusalem, killed all the people's leaders, and sent all the booty to the king in Damascus.

24:24  LA: Et certe cum permodicus venisset numerus Syrorum, tradidit Dominus in manibus eorum infinitam multitudinem, eo quod dereliquissent Dominum Deum patrum suorum : in Joas quoque ignominiosa exercuere judicia.
       EN: Although very few Syrians had come, the Lord delivered an immense multitude into their hands because they had abandoned the Lord God of their fathers. They also inflicted humiliating judgments upon Joash.

24:25  LA: Et abeuntes dimiserunt eum in languoribus magnis : surrexerunt autem contra eum servi sui in ultionem sanguinis filii Jojadæ sacerdotis, et occiderunt eum in lectulo suo, et mortuus est : sepelieruntque eum in civitate David, sed non in sepulchris regum.
       EN: They departed, leaving him gravely ill. His servants rose against him to avenge the blood of Jehoiada the priest's son and killed him in his bed. He died, and they buried him in the city of David, but not in the royal tombs.

24:26  LA: Insidiati vero sunt ei Zabad filius Semaath Ammanitidis, et Jozabad filius Semmarith Moabitidis.
       EN: Those who plotted against him were Zabad son of Shimeath the Ammonite woman and Jehozabad son of Shimrith the Moabite woman.

24:27  LA: Porro filii ejus, ac summa pecuniæ quæ adunata fuerat sub eo, et instauratio domus Dei, scripta sunt diligentius in libro regum : regnavit autem Amasias filius ejus pro eo.
       EN: His sons, the total money gathered under him, and the restoration of God's house are carefully recorded in the book of the kings. His son Amaziah reigned in his place.

2 CHRONICLES 25

25:1  LA: Viginti quinque annorum erat Amasias cum regnare cœpisset, et viginti novem annis regnavit in Jerusalem : nomen matris ejus Joadan de Jerusalem.
      EN: Amaziah was twenty-five when he began to reign and reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jehoaddan from Jerusalem.

25:2  LA: Fecitque bonum in conspectu Domini, verumtamen non in corde perfecto.
      EN: He did good in the Lord's sight, but not with a whole heart.

25:3  LA: Cumque roboratum sibi videret imperium, jugulavit servos qui occiderant regem patrem suum,
      EN: When he saw his rule firmly established, he executed the servants who had killed the king his father,

25:4  LA: sed filios eorum non interfecit, sicut scriptum est in libro legis Moysi, ubi præcepit Dominus, dicens : Non occidentur patres pro filiis, neque filii pro patribus suis, sed unusquisque in suo peccato morietur.
      EN: but did not kill their sons, as written in the book of Moses' law, where the Lord commanded, “Fathers shall not be killed for their sons, nor sons for their fathers. Each shall die for his own sin.”

25:5  LA: Congregavit igitur Amasias Judam, et constituit eos per familias, tribunosque et centuriones in universo Juda et Benjamin : et recensuit a viginti annis supra, invenitque trecenta millia juvenum qui egrederentur ad pugnam, et tenerent hastam et clypeum :
      EN: Amaziah gathered Judah and arranged them by families under commanders of thousands and hundreds throughout Judah and Benjamin. He counted those twenty years old and above and found three hundred thousand young men able to go to battle and carry spear and shield.

25:6  LA: mercede quoque conduxit de Israël centum millia robustorum, centum talentis argenti.
      EN: He also hired one hundred thousand mighty men from Israel for a hundred talents of silver.

25:7  LA: Venit autem homo Dei ad illum, et ait : O rex, ne egrediatur tecum exercitus Israël : non est enim Dominus cum Israël, et cunctis filiis Ephraim :
      EN: A man of God came to him and said, “King, do not let Israel's army go out with you, for the Lord is not with Israel or any of Ephraim's sons.

25:8  LA: quod si putas in robore exercitus bella consistere, superari te faciet Deus ab hostibus : Dei quippe est et adjuvare, et in fugam convertere.
      EN: If you think warfare depends on the strength of an army, God will make your enemies defeat you, for it belongs to God both to help and to put to flight.”

25:9  LA: Dixitque Amasias ad hominem Dei : Quid ergo fiet de centum talentis, quæ dedi militibus Israël ? Et respondit ei homo Dei : Habet Dominus unde tibi dare possit multo his plura.
      EN: Amaziah said to the man of God, “What about the hundred talents I gave Israel's soldiers?” The man of God answered, “The Lord has the means to give you far more than these.”

25:10  LA: Separavit itaque Amasias exercitum qui venerat ad eum ex Ephraim, ut reverteretur in locum suum : at illi contra Judam vehementer irati, reversi sunt in regionem suam.
       EN: Amaziah dismissed the army that had come to him from Ephraim, to return home. They returned to their region greatly enraged against Judah.

25:11  LA: Porro Amasias confidenter eduxit populum suum, et abiit in vallem Salinarum, percussitque filios Seir decem millia :
       EN: Amaziah confidently led his people out to the Valley of Salt and struck down ten thousand of the sons of Seir.

25:12  LA: et alia decem millia virorum ceperunt filii Juda, et adduxerunt ad præruptum cujusdam petræ, præcipitaveruntque eos de summo in præceps : qui universi crepuerunt.
       EN: The children of Judah captured another ten thousand men, brought them to the edge of a cliff, and threw them headlong from the summit. All of them were broken apart.

25:13  LA: At ille exercitus quem remiserat Amasias ne secum iret ad prælium, diffusus est in civitatibus Juda, a Samaria usque ad Bethoron, et interfectis tribus millibus, diripuit prædam magnam.
       EN: The army Amaziah had sent back from accompanying him to battle spread through Judah's cities from Samaria to Beth-horon, killed three thousand people, and took much plunder.

25:14  LA: Amasias vero post cædem Idumæorum, et allatos deos filiorum Seir, statuit illos in deos sibi, et adorabat eos, et illis adolebat incensum.
       EN: After slaughtering the Edomites, Amaziah brought the gods of the sons of Seir, set them up as his own gods, worshiped them, and burned incense to them.

25:15  LA: Quam ob rem iratus Dominus contra Amasiam misit ad illum prophetam, qui diceret ei : Cur adorasti deos qui non liberaverunt populum suum de manu tua ?
       EN: The Lord grew angry with Amaziah and sent a prophet to tell him, “Why have you worshiped gods who could not rescue their own people from your hand?”

25:16  LA: Cumque hæc ille loqueretur, respondit ei : Num consiliarius regis es ? quiesce, ne interficiam te. Discedensque propheta : Scio, inquit, quod cogitaverit Deus occidere te quia fecisti hoc malum, et insuper non acquievisti consilio meo.
       EN: While he was speaking, Amaziah answered, “Are you the king's counselor? Stop, or I will kill you.” As the prophet departed, he said, “I know God has resolved to kill you, because you have done this evil and also refused my counsel.”

25:17  LA: Igitur Amasias rex Juda inito pessimo consilio, misit ad Joas filium Joachaz filii Jehu regem Israël, dicens : Veni, videamus nos mutuo.
       EN: Amaziah king of Judah, taking very bad advice, sent to Joash son of Jehoahaz, son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying, “Come, let us face one another.”

25:18  LA: At ille remisit nuntios, dicens : Carduus qui est in Libano misit ad cedrum Libani, dicens : Da filiam tuam filio meo uxorem : et ecce bestiæ quæ erant in silva Libani, transierunt, et conculcaverunt carduum.
       EN: He sent messengers back, saying, “The thistle in Lebanon sent to Lebanon's cedar, saying, ‘Give your daughter to my son as his wife.’ But beasts in Lebanon's forest passed by and trampled the thistle.

25:19  LA: Dixisti : Percussi Edom, et idcirco erigitur cor tuum in superbiam : sede in domo tua : cur malum adversum te provocas, ut cadas et tu, et Juda tecum ?
       EN: You say, ‘I struck Edom,’ and your heart rises in pride. Stay in your house. Why provoke disaster against yourself, so that you fall, and Judah with you?”

25:20  LA: Noluit audire Amasias, eo quod Domini esset voluntas ut traderetur in manus hostium propter deos Edom.
       EN: Amaziah refused to listen, for it was the Lord's will that he be delivered into his enemies' hands because of Edom's gods.

25:21  LA: Ascendit igitur Joas rex Israël, et mutuos sibi præbuere conspectus : Amasias autem rex Juda erat in Bethsames Juda :
       EN: Joash king of Israel went up, and they faced one another. Amaziah king of Judah was at Beth-shemesh in Judah.

25:22  LA: corruitque Juda coram Israël, et fugit in tabernacula sua.
       EN: Judah fell before Israel and fled to their tents.

25:23  LA: Porro Amasiam regem Juda, filium Joas filii Joachaz, cepit Joas rex Israël in Bethsames, et adduxit in Jerusalem : destruxitque murum ejus a porta Ephraim usque ad portam anguli quadringentis cubitis.
       EN: Joash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, son of Joash, son of Jehoahaz, at Beth-shemesh and brought him to Jerusalem. He demolished four hundred cubits of its wall from the Ephraim Gate to the Corner Gate.

25:24  LA: Omne quoque aurum et argentum, et universa vasa quæ repererat in domo Dei, et apud Obededom in thesauris etiam domus regiæ, necnon et filios obsidum, reduxit in Samariam.
       EN: He took back to Samaria all the gold, silver, and vessels he found in God's house and with Obed-edom, the treasures of the royal house as well, and the hostages' sons.

25:25  LA: Vixit autem Amasias filius Joas rex Juda, postquam mortuus est Joas filius Joachaz rex Israël, quindecim annis.
       EN: Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, lived fifteen years after the death of Joash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel.

25:26  LA: Reliqua autem sermonum Amasiæ priorum et novissimorum scripta sunt in libro regum Juda et Israël.
       EN: The rest of Amaziah's deeds, first and last, are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.

25:27  LA: Qui postquam recessit a Domino, tetenderunt ei insidias in Jerusalem. Cumque fugisset in Lachis, miserunt, et interfecerunt eum ibi.
       EN: After he turned away from the Lord, they plotted against him in Jerusalem. He fled to Lachish, but they sent and killed him there.

25:28  LA: Reportantesque super equos, sepelierunt eum cum patribus suis in civitate David.
       EN: They brought him back on horses and buried him with his fathers in the city of David.

2 CHRONICLES 26

26:1  LA: Omnis autem populus Juda filium ejus Oziam, annorum sedecim, constituit regem pro Amasia patre suo.
      EN: All the people of Judah made his son Uzziah, sixteen years old, king in place of his father Amaziah.

26:2  LA: Ipse ædificavit Ailath, et restituit eam ditioni Juda, postquam dormivit rex cum patribus suis.
      EN: He rebuilt Elath and restored it to Judah's rule after the king rested with his fathers.

26:3  LA: Sedecim annorum erat Ozias cum regnare cœpisset, et quinquaginta duobus annis regnavit in Jerusalem : nomen matris ejus Jechelia de Jerusalem.
      EN: Uzziah was sixteen when he began to reign and reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jecoliah from Jerusalem.

26:4  LA: Fecitque quod erat rectum in oculis Domini, juxta omnia quæ fecerat Amasias pater ejus.
      EN: He did what was right in the Lord's eyes, according to everything his father Amaziah had done.

26:5  LA: Et exquisivit Dominum in diebus Zachariæ intelligentis et videntis Deum : cumque requireret Dominum, direxit eum in omnibus.
      EN: He sought the Lord in the days of Zechariah, who understood and saw God. As long as he sought the Lord, He guided him in everything.

26:6  LA: Denique egressus est, et pugnavit contra Philisthiim, et destruxit murum Geth, et murum Jabniæ, murumque Azoti : ædificavit quoque oppida in Azoto et in Philisthiim.
      EN: He went out to fight the Philistines and demolished the walls of Gath, Jabneh, and Ashdod. He also built towns in Ashdod and among the Philistines.

26:7  LA: Et adjuvit eum Deus contra Philisthiim, et contra Arabes qui habitabant in Gurbaal, et contra Ammonitas.
      EN: God helped him against the Philistines, the Arabs living in Gur-baal, and the Ammonites.

26:8  LA: Appendebantque Ammonitæ munera Oziæ : et divulgatum est nomen ejus usque ad introitum Ægypti propter crebras victorias.
      EN: The Ammonites paid tribute to Uzziah, and his fame spread as far as the entrance of Egypt because of his frequent victories.

26:9  LA: Ædificavitque Ozias turres in Jerusalem super portam anguli, et super portam vallis, et reliquas in eodem muri latere, firmavitque eas.
      EN: Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem at the Corner Gate and Valley Gate, and others along the same side of the wall, and fortified them.

26:10  LA: Exstruxit etiam turres in solitudine, et effodit cisternas plurimas, eo quod haberet multa pecora tam in campestribus quam in eremi vastitate : vineas quoque habuit et vinitores, in montibus et in Carmelo : erat quippe homo agriculturæ deditus.
       EN: He built towers in the wilderness and dug many cisterns, for he had much livestock both in the plains and in the vast wilderness. He also had vineyards and vinegrowers in the mountains and on Carmel, for he was devoted to agriculture.

26:11  LA: Fuit autem exercitus bellatorum ejus, qui procedebant ad prælia sub manu Jehiel scribæ, Maasiæque doctoris, et sub manu Hananiæ, qui erat de ducibus regis.
       EN: His army of warriors went out to battle under Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the instructor, and under Hananiah, one of the king's commanders.

26:12  LA: Omnisque numerus principum per familias, virorum fortium duorum millium sexcentorum.
       EN: The total number of heads of families, mighty men, was twenty-six hundred.

26:13  LA: Et sub eis universus exercitus trecentorum et septem millium quingentorum, qui erant apti ad bella, et pro rege contra adversarios dimicabant.
       EN: Under them was an entire army of 307,500 men fit for war, who fought for the king against his enemies.

26:14  LA: Præparavit quoque eis Ozias, id est, cuncto exercitui, clypeos, et hastas, et galeas, et loricas, arcusque et fundas ad jaciendos lapides.
       EN: Uzziah prepared for them, for the whole army, shields, spears, helmets, armor, bows, and slings for throwing stones.

26:15  LA: Et fecit in Jerusalem diversi generis machinas, quas in turribus collocavit et in angulis murorum, ut mitterent sagittas, et saxa grandia : egressumque est nomen ejus procul, eo quod auxiliaretur ei Dominus, et corroborasset illum.
       EN: In Jerusalem he made machines of different kinds and placed them on the towers and wall corners to shoot arrows and large stones. His fame spread far, because the Lord helped and strengthened him.

26:16  LA: Sed cum roboratus esset, elevatum est cor ejus in interitum suum, et neglexit Dominum Deum suum : ingressusque templum Domini, adolere voluit incensum super altare thymiamatis.
       EN: But when he was strong, his heart rose up to his destruction, and he neglected the Lord his God. He entered the Lord's temple, intending to burn incense on the incense altar.

26:17  LA: Statimque ingressus post eum Azarias sacerdos, et cum eo sacerdotes Domini octoginta, viri fortissimi,
       EN: Azariah the priest immediately followed him, with eighty priests of the Lord, very brave men.

26:18  LA: restiterunt regi, atque dixerunt : Non est tui officii, Ozia, ut adoleas incensum Domino, sed sacerdotum, hoc est, filiorum Aaron, qui consecrati sunt ad hujuscemodi ministerium : egredere de sanctuario, ne contempseris : quia non reputabitur tibi in gloriam hoc a Domino Deo.
       EN: They opposed the king and said, “It is not your duty, Uzziah, to burn incense to the Lord, but the priests', the sons of Aaron consecrated for this service. Leave the sanctuary and do not act contemptuously, for this will not be reckoned to your glory by the Lord God.”

26:19  LA: Iratusque Ozias, tenens in manu thuribulum ut adoleret incensum, minabatur sacerdotibus. Statimque orta est lepra in fronte ejus coram sacerdotibus, in domo Domini super altare thymiamatis.
       EN: Uzziah grew angry. Holding a censer in his hand to burn incense, he threatened the priests. Immediately leprosy broke out on his forehead before the priests, in the Lord's house beside the incense altar.

26:20  LA: Cumque respexisset eum Azarias pontifex, et omnes reliqui sacerdotes, viderunt lepram in fronte ejus, et festinato expulerunt eum. Sed et ipse perterritus, acceleravit egredi, eo quod sensisset illico plagam Domini.
       EN: Azariah the high priest and all the other priests looked at him and saw the leprosy on his forehead. They quickly drove him out. Terrified, he himself hurried to leave, because he immediately recognized the Lord's stroke.

26:21  LA: Fuit igitur Ozias rex leprosus usque ad diem mortis suæ, et habitavit in domo separata plenus lepra, ob quam ejectus fuerat de domo Domini. Porro Joatham filius ejus rexit domum regis, et judicabat populum terræ.
       EN: King Uzziah remained leprous until his death. He lived in a separate house, covered with leprosy, because of which he had been expelled from the Lord's house. His son Jotham governed the king's house and judged the people of the land.

26:22  LA: Reliqua autem sermonum Oziæ priorum et novissimorum scripsit Isaias filius Amos propheta.
       EN: Isaiah the prophet, son of Amoz, wrote the rest of Uzziah's deeds, first and last.

26:23  LA: Dormivitque Ozias cum patribus suis, et sepelierunt eum in agro regalium sepulchrorum, eo quod esset leprosus : regnavitque Joatham filius ejus pro eo.
       EN: Uzziah rested with his fathers. They buried him in the field of the royal tombs because he was leprous. His son Jotham reigned in his place.

2 CHRONICLES 27

27:1  LA: Viginti quinque annorum erat Joatham cum regnare cœpisset, et sedecim annis regnavit in Jerusalem : nomen matris ejus Jerusa filia Sadoc.
      EN: Jotham was twenty-five when he began to reign and reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jerushah daughter of Zadok.

27:2  LA: Fecitque quod rectum erat coram Domino, juxta omnia quæ fecerat Ozias pater suus, excepto quod non est ingressus templum Domini : et adhuc populus delinquebat.
      EN: He did what was right before the Lord, according to everything his father Uzziah had done, except that he did not enter the Lord's temple. The people still acted wickedly.

27:3  LA: Ipse ædificavit portam domus Domini excelsam, et in muro Ophel multa construxit.
      EN: He built the Upper Gate of the Lord's house and did much building on the wall of Ophel.

27:4  LA: Urbes quoque ædificavit in montibus Juda, et in saltibus castella et turres.
      EN: He built cities in Judah's mountains and fortresses and towers in the forests.

27:5  LA: Ipse pugnavit contra regem filiorum Ammon, et vicit eos, dederuntque ei filii Ammon in tempore illo centum talenta argenti, et decem millia coros tritici, ac totidem coros hordei : hæc ei præbuerunt filii Ammon in anno secundo et tertio.
      EN: He fought the king of the Ammonites and defeated them. At that time the Ammonites gave him a hundred talents of silver, ten thousand cors of wheat, and as many cors of barley. They gave him these in the second and third years as well.

27:6  LA: Corroboratusque est Joatham, eo quod direxisset vias suas coram Domino Deo suo.
      EN: Jotham grew strong because he directed his ways before the Lord his God.

27:7  LA: Reliqua autem sermonum Joatham, et omnes pugnæ ejus et opera, scripta sunt in libro regum Israël et Juda.
      EN: The rest of Jotham's deeds, all his battles and works, are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah.

27:9  LA: Dormivitque Joatham cum patribus suis, et sepelierunt eum in civitate David : et regnavit Achaz filius ejus pro eo.
      EN: Jotham rested with his fathers and was buried in the city of David. His son Ahaz reigned in his place.

2 CHRONICLES 28

28:1  LA: Viginti annorum erat Achaz cum regnare cœpisset, et sedecim annis regnavit in Jerusalem. Non fecit rectum in conspectu Domini sicut David pater ejus,
      EN: Ahaz was twenty when he began to reign and reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. He did not do what was right in the Lord's sight as David his father had done,

28:2  LA: sed ambulavit in viis regum Israël, insuper et statuas fudit Baalim.
      EN: but walked in the ways of Israel's kings and even cast statues of the Baals.

28:3  LA: Ipse est qui adolevit incensum in valle Benennom, et lustravit filios suos in igne juxta ritum gentium quas interfecit Dominus in adventu filiorum Israël.
      EN: He burned incense in the Valley of Ben-hinnom and passed his sons through fire in a purification rite, according to the custom of the nations the Lord killed when the children of Israel came.

28:4  LA: Sacrificabat quoque, et thymiama succendebat in excelsis, et in collibus, et sub omni ligno frondoso.
      EN: He sacrificed and burned incense at the high places, on the hills, and beneath every leafy tree.

28:5  LA: Tradiditque eum Dominus Deus ejus in manu regis Syriæ, qui percussit eum, magnamque prædam cepit de ejus imperio, et adduxit in Damascum : manibus quoque regis Israël traditus est, et percussus plaga grandi.
      EN: The Lord his God delivered him into the hand of the king of Syria, who struck him, took great spoil from his kingdom, and brought it to Damascus. He was also delivered into the hands of the king of Israel and struck with a great blow.

28:6  LA: Occiditque Phacee filius Romeliæ, de Juda centum viginti millia in die uno, omnes viros bellatores : eo quod reliquissent Dominum Deum patrum suorum.
      EN: Pekah son of Remaliah killed one hundred twenty thousand warriors of Judah in one day, because they had abandoned the Lord God of their fathers.

28:7  LA: Eodem tempore occidit Zechri, vir potens ex Ephraim, Maasiam filium regis, et Ezricam ducem domus ejus, Elcanam quoque secundum a rege.
      EN: At that time Zichri, a mighty man of Ephraim, killed Maaseiah the king's son, Azrikam the governor of his house, and Elkanah, second to the king.

28:8  LA: Ceperuntque filii Israël de fratribus suis ducenta millia mulierum, puerorum, et puellarum, et infinitam prædam : pertuleruntque eam in Samariam.
      EN: The children of Israel took from their brothers two hundred thousand women, boys, and girls as captives, and immense plunder, which they brought to Samaria.

28:9  LA: Ea tempestate erat ibi propheta Domini, nomine Oded : qui egressus obviam exercitui venienti in Samariam, dixit eis : Ecce iratus Dominus Deus patrum vestrorum contra Juda, tradidit eos in manibus vestris, et occidistis eos atrociter, ita ut ad cælum pertingeret vestra crudelitas.
      EN: A prophet of the Lord named Oded was there at that time. He went out to meet the army coming to Samaria and said, “The Lord God of your fathers, angry with Judah, delivered them into your hands. You killed them so savagely that your cruelty reached heaven.

28:10  LA: Insuper filios Juda et Jerusalem vultis vobis subjicere in servos et ancillas : quod nequaquam facto opus est : peccastis enim super hoc Domino Deo vestro.
       EN: Moreover, you wish to subject the children of Judah and Jerusalem as your male and female slaves. This must not be done, for you have sinned in this against the Lord your God.

28:11  LA: Sed audite consilium meum, et reducite captivos quos adduxistis de fratribus vestris, quia magnus furor Domini imminet vobis.
       EN: Hear my counsel and return the captives you have taken from your brothers, for the Lord's great fury hangs over you.”

28:12  LA: Steterunt itaque viri de principibus filiorum Ephraim, Azarias filius Johanan, Barachias filius Mosollamoth, Ezechias filius Sellum, et Amasa filius Adali, contra eos qui veniebant de prælio,
       EN: Some leaders of the sons of Ephraim—Azariah son of Jehohanan, Berechiah son of Meshillemoth, Hezekiah son of Shallum, and Amasa son of Hadlai—stood against those returning from battle

28:13  LA: et dixerunt eis : Non introducetis huc captivos, ne peccemus Domino. Quare vultis adjicere super peccata nostra, et vetera cumulare delicta ? grande quippe peccatum est, et ira furoris Domini imminet super Israël.
       EN: and said, “You shall not bring captives here, lest we sin against the Lord. Why do you wish to add to our sins and heap up our former offenses? Our sin is great, and the Lord's fierce wrath hangs over Israel.”

28:14  LA: Dimiseruntque viri bellatores prædam, et universa quæ ceperant, coram principibus, et omni multitudine.
       EN: The warriors left the booty and everything they had captured before the leaders and the whole multitude.

28:15  LA: Steteruntque viri quos supra memoravimus, et apprehendentes captivos, omnesque qui nudi erant, vestierunt de spoliis : cumque vestissent eos, et calceassent, et refecissent cibo ac potu, unxissentque propter laborem, et adhibuissent eis curam : quicumque ambulare non poterant, et erant imbecillo corpore, imposuerunt eos jumentis, et adduxerunt Jericho civitatem palmarum ad fratres eorum, ipsique reversi sunt in Samariam.
       EN: The men named above took charge of the captives and clothed all who were naked from the spoils. They clothed them, gave them sandals, refreshed them with food and drink, anointed them because of their weariness, and cared for them. They placed the weak who could not walk on animals and brought them to their brothers at Jericho, the city of palms, then returned to Samaria.

28:16  LA: Tempore illo misit rex Achaz ad regem Assyriorum, postulans auxilium.
       EN: At that time King Ahaz sent to the king of the Assyrians, asking for help.
