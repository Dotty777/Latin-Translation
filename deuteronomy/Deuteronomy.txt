DEUTERONOMY 1

1:1  LA: Hæc sunt verba quæ locutus est Moyses ad omnem Israël trans Jordanem in solitudine campestri, contra mare Rubrum, inter Pharan et Tophel et Laban et Haseroth, ubi auri est plurimum :
     EN: These are the words Moses spoke to all Israel beyond the Jordan, in the wilderness plain opposite the Red Sea, between Paran, Tophel, Laban, and Hazeroth, where gold is very plentiful.

1:2  LA: undecim diebus de Horeb per viam montis Seir usque ad Cadesbarne.
     EN: It is eleven days from Horeb by the Mount Seir road to Kadesh-barnea.

1:3  LA: Quadragesimo anno, undecimo mense, prima die mensis, locutus est Moyses ad filios Israël omnia quæ præceperat illi Dominus, ut diceret eis,
     EN: In the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, Moses told the children of Israel everything the Lord had commanded him to tell them,

1:4  LA: postquam percussit Sehon regem Amorrhæorum, qui habitabat in Hesebon, et Og regem Basan, qui mansit in Astaroth, et in Edrai,
     EN: after he had struck down Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth and Edrei,

1:5  LA: trans Jordanem in terra Moab. Cœpitque Moyses explanare legem, et dicere :
     EN: beyond the Jordan in the land of Moab. Moses began to explain the law, saying:

1:6  LA: Dominus Deus noster locutus est ad nos in Horeb, dicens : Sufficit vobis quod in hoc monte mansistis :
     EN: The Lord our God spoke to us at Horeb, saying: You have stayed at this mountain long enough.

1:7  LA: revertimini, et venite ad montem Amorrhæorum, et ad cetera quæ ei proxima sunt campestria atque montana et humiliora loca contra meridiem, et juxta littus maris, terram Chananæorum, et Libani usque ad flumen magnum Euphraten.
     EN: Turn and go to the hill country of the Amorites and all its neighboring plains, hills, and lowlands to the south and beside the seacoast, the land of the Canaanites and Lebanon, as far as the great river Euphrates.

1:8  LA: En, inquit, tradidi vobis : ingredimini et possidete eam, super qua juravit Dominus patribus vestris Abraham, Isaac, et Jacob, ut daret illam eis, et semini eorum post eos.
     EN: See, He said, I have handed it over to you. Enter and possess it—the land the Lord swore to your fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob that He would give to them and their offspring after them.

1:9  LA: Dixique vobis illo in tempore :
     EN: At that time I said to you:

1:10  LA: Non possum solus sustinere vos : quia Dominus Deus vester multiplicavit vos, et estis hodie sicut stellæ cæli, plurimi.
     EN: I cannot bear you alone, because the Lord your God has multiplied you, and today you are as numerous as the stars of heaven.

1:11  LA: (Dominus Deus patrum vestrorum addat ad hunc numerum multa millia, et benedicat vobis sicut locutus est.)
     EN: May the Lord, the God of your fathers, add many thousands to this number and bless you as He has said!

1:12  LA: Non valeo solus negotia vestra sustinere, et pondus ac jurgia.
     EN: I cannot bear your affairs, your burden, and your disputes alone.

1:13  LA: Date ex vobis viros sapientes et gnaros, et quorum conversatio sit probata in tribubus vestris, ut ponam eos vobis principes.
     EN: Choose from among you wise, knowledgeable men whose conduct is proven in your tribes, so that I may appoint them as your leaders.

1:14  LA: Tunc respondistis mihi : Bona res est, quam vis facere.
     EN: Then you answered me: What you intend to do is good.

1:15  LA: Tulique de tribubus vestris viros sapientes et nobiles, et constitui eos principes, tribunos, et centuriones, et quinquagenarios ac decanos, qui docerent vos singula.
     EN: I took wise and distinguished men from your tribes and appointed them as your leaders: commanders of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens, to instruct you in each matter.

1:16  LA: Præcepique eis, dicens : Audite illos, et quod justum est judicate : sive civis sit ille, sive peregrinus.
     EN: I commanded them: Hear them and judge justly, whether the person is a fellow citizen or a foreigner.

1:17  LA: Nulla erit distantia personarum : ita parvum audietis ut magnum, nec accipietis cujusquam personam, quia Dei judicium est. Quod si difficile vobis visum aliquid fuerit, referte ad me, et ego audiam.
     EN: There shall be no distinction between persons. Hear the small just as the great; show no one partiality, because judgment belongs to God. If anything seems too difficult for you, refer it to me, and I will hear it.

1:18  LA: Præcepique omnia quæ facere deberetis.
     EN: I commanded everything you were to do.

1:19  LA: Profecti autem de Horeb, transivimus per eremum terribilem et maximam, quam vidistis, per viam montis Amorrhæi, sicut præceperat Dominus Deus noster nobis. Cumque venissemus in Cadesbarne,
     EN: Setting out from Horeb, we passed through that vast and terrible wilderness you saw, by the road to the Amorite hill country, as the Lord our God had commanded us. When we reached Kadesh-barnea,

1:20  LA: dixi vobis : Venistis ad montem Amorrhæi, quem Dominus Deus noster daturus est nobis :
     EN: I said to you: You have reached the Amorite hill country, which the Lord our God is going to give us.

1:21  LA: vide terram, quam Dominus Deus tuus dat tibi : ascende et posside eam, sicut locutus est Dominus Deus noster patribus tuis : noli timere, nec quidquam paveas.
     EN: Look at the land the Lord your God gives you. Go up and possess it, as the Lord our God told your fathers. Do not fear or be at all alarmed.

1:22  LA: Et accessistis ad me omnes, atque dixistis : Mittamus viros qui considerent terram : et renuntient per quod iter debeamus ascendere, et ad quas pergere civitates.
     EN: You all approached me and said: Let us send men to examine the land and report which road we should take up and which cities we should approach.

1:23  LA: Cumque mihi sermo placuisset, misi ex vobis duodecim viros, singulos de tribubus suis.
     EN: The proposal pleased me, and I sent twelve men from among you, one from each tribe.

1:24  LA: Qui cum perrexissent, et ascendissent in montana, venerunt usque ad Vallem botri : et considerata terra,
     EN: They set out, went up into the hill country, and reached the Valley of the Cluster. After examining the land,

1:25  LA: sumentes de fructibus ejus, ut ostenderent ubertatem, attulerunt ad nos, atque dixerunt : Bona est terra, quam Dominus Deus noster daturus est nobis.
     EN: they took some of its fruit to show its fertility and brought it to us, saying: The land the Lord our God is going to give us is good.

1:26  LA: Et noluistis ascendere, sed increduli ad sermonem Domini Dei nostri,
     EN: But you would not go up. Distrusting the word of the Lord our God,

1:27  LA: murmurastis in tabernaculis vestris, atque dixistis : Odit nos Dominus, et idcirco eduxit nos de terra Ægypti, ut traderet nos in manu Amorrhæi, atque deleret.
     EN: you grumbled in your tents and said: The Lord hates us. That is why He brought us out of Egypt, to hand us over to the Amorites and destroy us.

1:28  LA: Quo ascendemus ? nuntii terruerunt cor nostrum, dicentes : Maxima multitudo est, et nobis statura procerior ; urbes magnæ, et ad cælum usque munitæ : filios Enacim vidimus ibi.
     EN: Where shall we go up? The messengers have terrified our hearts, saying: The people are extremely numerous and taller than we are; the cities are large and fortified up to heaven. We saw descendants of the Anakim there.

1:29  LA: Et dixi vobis : Nolite metuere, nec timeatis eos :
     EN: I said to you: Do not be alarmed or afraid of them.

1:30  LA: Dominus Deus, qui ductor est vester, pro vobis ipse pugnabit, sicut fecit in Ægypto cunctis videntibus.
     EN: The Lord God, your leader, will Himself fight for you, as He did in Egypt before everyone's eyes.

1:31  LA: Et in solitudine (ipse vidisti) portavit te Dominus Deus tuus, ut solet homo gestare parvulum filium suum, in omni via per quam ambulastis, donec veniretis ad locum istum.
     EN: And in the wilderness—you yourself saw it—the Lord your God carried you, as a man carries his little son, all along the way you traveled until you reached this place.

1:32  LA: Et nec sic quidem credidistis Domino Deo vestro,
     EN: Even so, you did not trust the Lord your God,

1:33  LA: qui præcessit vos in via, et metatus est locum in quo tentoria figere deberetis, nocte ostendens vobis iter per ignem, et die per columnam nubis.
     EN: who went ahead of you on the road and marked out a place for you to pitch your tents, showing you the way by fire at night and by a pillar of cloud during the day.

1:34  LA: Cumque audisset Dominus vocem sermonum vestrorum, iratus juravit, et ait :
     EN: When the Lord heard your words, He became angry and swore:

1:35  LA: Non videbit quispiam de hominibus generationis hujus pessimæ terram bonam, quam sub juramento pollicitus sum patribus vestris,
     EN: None of the men of this most wicked generation shall see the good land I promised your fathers under oath,

1:36  LA: præter Caleb filium Jephone : ipse enim videbit eam, et ipsi dabo terram, quam calcavit, et filiis ejus, quia secutus est Dominum.
     EN: except Caleb son of Jephunneh. He shall see it, and I will give him and his children the land on which he walked, because he followed the Lord.

1:37  LA: Nec miranda indignatio in populum, cum mihi quoque iratus Dominus propter vos dixerit : Nec tu ingredieris illuc :
     EN: Nor is His anger against the people surprising, since the Lord was also angry with me because of you and said: You too shall not enter there.

1:38  LA: sed Josue filius Nun minister tuus, ipse intrabit pro te. Hunc exhortare et robora, et ipse sorte terram dividet Israëli.
     EN: Joshua son of Nun, your attendant, shall enter in your place. Encourage and strengthen him, for he will divide the land by lot for Israel.

1:39  LA: Parvuli vestri, de quibus dixistis quod captivi ducerentur, et filii qui hodie boni ac mali ignorant distantiam, ipsi ingredientur : et ipsis dabo terram, et possidebunt eam.
     EN: Your little ones, whom you said would be taken captive, and your children who today do not know the difference between good and evil—they shall enter. I will give them the land, and they shall possess it.

1:40  LA: Vos autem revertimini, et abite in solitudinem per viam maris Rubri.
     EN: But you, turn back and go into the wilderness by the Red Sea road.

1:41  LA: Et respondistis mihi : Peccavimus Domino : ascendemus et pugnabimus, sicut præcepit Dominus Deus noster. Cumque instructi armis pergeretis in montem,
     EN: You answered me: We have sinned against the Lord. We will go up and fight, as the Lord our God commanded. But when you had armed yourselves and were heading toward the mountain,

1:42  LA: ait mihi Dominus : Dic ad eos : Nolite ascendere, neque pugnetis : non enim sum vobiscum : ne cadatis coram inimicis vestris.
     EN: the Lord said to me: Tell them: Do not go up or fight, for I am not with you; otherwise you will fall before your enemies.

1:43  LA: Locutus sum, et non audistis : sed adversantes imperio Domini, et tumentes superbia, ascendistis in montem.
     EN: I spoke, but you would not listen. Defying the Lord's command and swollen with pride, you went up into the hill country.

1:44  LA: Itaque egressus Amorrhæus, qui habitabat in montibus, et obviam veniens, persecutus est vos, sicut solent apes persequi : et cecidit de Seir usque Horma.
     EN: Then the Amorites who lived in the mountains came out to meet you and pursued you as bees pursue, striking you down from Seir to Hormah.

1:45  LA: Cumque reversi ploraretis coram Domino, non audivit vos, nec voci vestræ voluit acquiescere.
     EN: You returned and wept before the Lord, but He did not listen to you or consent to heed your voice.

1:46  LA: Sedistis ergo in Cadesbarne multo tempore.
     EN: So you remained at Kadesh-barnea a long time.

DEUTERONOMY 2

2:1  LA: Profectique inde, venimus in solitudinem, quæ ducit ad mare Rubrum, sicut mihi dixerat Dominus : et circuivimus montem Seir longo tempore.
     EN: Setting out from there, we entered the wilderness leading to the Red Sea, as the Lord had told me, and traveled around Mount Seir for a long time.

2:2  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad me :
     EN: Then the Lord said to me:

2:3  LA: Sufficit vobis circuire montem istum : ite contra aquilonem :
     EN: You have traveled around this mountain long enough. Go north,

2:4  LA: et populo præcipe, dicens : Transibitis per terminos fratrum vestrorum filiorum Esau, qui habitant in Seir, et timebunt vos.
     EN: and command the people: You will pass through the territory of your brothers, the descendants of Esau who live in Seir, and they will fear you.

2:5  LA: Videte ergo diligenter ne moveamini contra eos. Neque enim dabo vobis de terra eorum quantum potest unius pedis calcare vestigium, quia in possessionem Esau dedi montem Seir.
     EN: Take great care, therefore, not to rise against them. I will not give you any of their land, not even enough for a single footprint, because I have given Mount Seir to Esau as a possession.

2:6  LA: Cibos emetis ab eis pecunia, et comedetis : aquam emptam haurietis, et bibetis.
     EN: Buy food from them with money and eat; draw the water you have bought and drink.

2:7  LA: Dominus Deus tuus benedixit tibi in omni opere manuum tuarum : novit iter tuum, quomodo transieris solitudinem hanc magnam, per quadraginta annos habitans tecum Dominus Deus tuus, et nihil tibi defuit.
     EN: The Lord your God has blessed you in all the work of your hands. He knows your journey, how you have crossed this great wilderness. For forty years the Lord your God has lived with you, and you have lacked nothing.

2:8  LA: Cumque transissemus fratres nostros filios Esau, qui habitabant in Seir, per viam campestrem de Elath, et de Asiongaber, venimus ad iter quod ducit in desertum Moab.
     EN: After passing our brothers, the descendants of Esau who lived in Seir, by the plain road from Elath and Ezion-geber, we came to the road leading into the wilderness of Moab.

2:9  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad me : Non pugnes contra Moabitas, nec ineas adversus eos prælium : non enim dabo tibi quidquam de terra eorum, quia filiis Loth tradidi Ar in possessionem.
     EN: The Lord said to me: Do not fight the Moabites or begin a battle against them. I will not give you any of their land, because I have given Ar to the descendants of Lot as a possession.

2:10  LA: Emim primi fuerunt habitatores ejus, populus magnus, et validus, et tam excelsus ut de Enacim stirpe,
     EN: The Emim were its first inhabitants, a great and powerful people, so tall that they were thought to belong to the line of the Anakim,

2:11  LA: quasi gigantes crederentur, et essent similes filiorum Enacim. Denique Moabitæ appellant eos Emim.
     EN: as though they were giants, resembling the descendants of the Anakim. The Moabites call them Emim.

2:12  LA: In Seir autem prius habitaverunt Horrhæi : quibus expulsis atque deletis, habitaverunt filii Esau, sicut fecit Israël in terra possessionis suæ, quam dedit illi Dominus.
     EN: The Horites formerly lived in Seir, but the descendants of Esau drove them out and destroyed them and settled there, just as Israel did in the land of its possession, which the Lord gave it.

2:13  LA: Surgentes ergo ut transiremus torrentem Zared, venimus ad eum.
     EN: So we set out to cross the Zered torrent and came to it.

2:14  LA: Tempus autem, quo ambulavimus de Cadesbarne usque ad transitum torrentis Zared, triginta et octo annorum fuit : donec consumeretur omnis generatio hominum bellatorum de castris, sicut juraverat Dominus :
     EN: The time we traveled from Kadesh-barnea until we crossed the Zered torrent was thirty-eight years, until the whole generation of fighting men had perished from the camp, as the Lord had sworn.

2:15  LA: cujus manus fuit adversum eos, ut interirent de castrorum medio.
     EN: His hand was against them, so that they perished from the midst of the camp.

2:16  LA: Postquam autem universi ceciderunt pugnatores,
     EN: After all the fighting men had fallen,

2:17  LA: locutus est Dominus ad me, dicens :
     EN: the Lord spoke to me, saying:

2:18  LA: Tu transibis hodie terminos Moab, urbem nomine Ar :
     EN: Today you will cross the territory of Moab, at the city called Ar.

2:19  LA: et accedens in vicina filiorum Ammon, cave ne pugnes contra eos, nec movearis ad prælium : non enim dabo tibi de terra filiorum Ammon, quia filiis Loth dedi eam in possessionem.
     EN: When you approach the neighborhood of the Ammonites, take care not to fight them or rise up for battle. I will not give you any of the Ammonites' land, because I have given it to the descendants of Lot as a possession.

2:20  LA: Terra gigantum reputata est : et in ipsa olim habitaverunt gigantes, quos Ammonitæ vocant Zomzommim,
     EN: It was considered a land of giants. Giants once lived there, whom the Ammonites call Zamzummim,

2:21  LA: populus magnus, et multus, et proceræ longitudinis, sicut Enacim, quos delevit Dominus a facie eorum : et fecit illos habitare pro eis,
     EN: a great and numerous people, as tall as the Anakim. The Lord destroyed them before the Ammonites and made the Ammonites live in their place,

2:22  LA: sicut fecerat filiis Esau, qui habitant in Seir, delens Horrhæos, et terram eorum illis tradens, quam possident usque in præsens.
     EN: as He had done for the descendants of Esau who live in Seir, destroying the Horites and handing over their land, which they possess to this day.

2:23  LA: Hevæos quoque, qui habitabant in Haserim usque Gazan, Cappadoces expulerunt : qui egressi de Cappadocia deleverunt eos, et habitaverunt pro illis.
     EN: The Cappadocians also drove out the Hivites who lived in Hazerim as far as Gaza; they came from Cappadocia, destroyed them, and settled in their place.

2:24  LA: Surgite, et transite torrentem Arnon : ecce tradidi in manu tua Sehon regem Hesebon Amorrhæum, et terram ejus incipe possidere, et committe adversus eum prælium.
     EN: Rise and cross the Arnon torrent. See, I have handed over to you Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon. Begin to possess his land and engage him in battle.

2:25  LA: Hodie incipiam mittere terrorem atque formidinem tuam in populos, qui habitant sub omni cælo : ut audito nomine tuo paveant, et in morem parturientium contremiscant, et dolore teneantur.
     EN: Today I will begin to spread dread and fear of you among the peoples who live under all heaven, so that when they hear your name they will be terrified, tremble like women in labor, and be gripped with anguish.

2:26  LA: Misi ergo nuntios de solitudine Cademoth ad Sehon regem Hesebon verbis pacificis, dicens :
     EN: So I sent messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon with peaceful words, saying:

2:27  LA: Transibimus per terram tuam : publica gradiemur via ; non declinabimus neque ad dexteram, neque ad sinistram.
     EN: We will pass through your land. We will follow the public road, turning neither right nor left.

2:28  LA: Alimenta pretio vende nobis, ut vescamur : aquam pecunia tribue, et sic bibemus. Tantum est ut nobis concedas transitum,
     EN: Sell us provisions for a price, so that we may eat; give us water for money, so that we may drink. Only grant us passage,

2:29  LA: sicut fecerunt filii Esau, qui habitant in Seir, et Moabitæ, qui morantur in Ar : donec veniamus ad Jordanem, et transeamus ad terram, quam Dominus Deus noster daturus est nobis.
     EN: as the descendants of Esau who live in Seir and the Moabites who dwell in Ar have done, until we reach the Jordan and cross into the land the Lord our God is going to give us.

2:30  LA: Noluitque Sehon rex Hesebon dare nobis transitum : quia induraverat Dominus Deus tuus spiritum ejus, et obfirmaverat cor illius, ut traderetur in manus tuas, sicut nunc vides.
     EN: But Sihon king of Heshbon would not grant us passage, because the Lord your God had hardened his spirit and made his heart stubborn, so that he would be handed over to you, as you now see.

2:31  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad me : Ecce cœpi tibi tradere Sehon, et terram ejus : incipe possidere eam.
     EN: The Lord said to me: See, I have begun to hand Sihon and his land over to you. Begin to possess it.

2:32  LA: Egressusque est Sehon obviam nobis cum omni populo suo ad prælium in Jasa.
     EN: Sihon came out with all his people to meet us in battle at Jahaz.

2:33  LA: Et tradidit eum Dominus Deus noster nobis : percussimusque eum cum filiis suis et omni populo suo.
     EN: The Lord our God handed him over to us, and we struck him down with his sons and all his people.

2:34  LA: Cunctasque urbes in tempore illo cepimus, interfectis habitatoribus earum, viris ac mulieribus et parvulis : non reliquimus in eis quidquam,
     EN: At that time we captured all his cities and killed their inhabitants—men, women, and little children. We left nothing in them,

2:35  LA: absque jumentis, quæ in partem venere prædantium : et spoliis urbium, quas cepimus
     EN: except the livestock, which fell to the plunderers, and the spoils of the cities we captured,

2:36  LA: ab Aroër, quæ est super ripam torrentis Arnon, oppido quod in valle situm est, usque Galaad. Non fuit vicus et civitas, quæ nostras effugeret manus : omnes tradidit Dominus Deus noster nobis,
     EN: from Aroer on the bank of the Arnon torrent, the town situated in the valley, as far as Gilead. No village or city escaped our hands; the Lord our God handed them all over to us,

2:37  LA: absque terra filiorum Ammon, ad quam non accessimus : et cunctis quæ adjacent torrenti Jeboc, et urbibus montanis, universisque locis, a quibus nos prohibuit Dominus Deus noster.
     EN: except the land of the Ammonites, which we did not approach, and everything beside the Jabbok torrent, the mountain cities, and all the places the Lord our God had forbidden us.

DEUTERONOMY 3

3:1  LA: Itaque conversi ascendimus per iter Basan : egressusque est Og rex Basan in occursum nobis cum populo suo ad bellandum in Edrai.
     EN: Then we turned and went up by the road to Bashan. Og king of Bashan came out with his people to meet us in battle at Edrei.

3:2  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad me : Ne timeas eum : quia in manu tua traditus est cum omni populo ac terra sua : faciesque ei sicut fecisti Sehon regi Amorrhæorum, qui habitavit in Hesebon.
     EN: The Lord said to me: Do not fear him, for he has been handed over to you with all his people and his land. Do to him as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon.

3:3  LA: Tradidit ergo Dominus Deus noster in manibus nostris etiam Og regem Basan, et universum populum ejus : percussimusque eos usque ad internecionem,
     EN: So the Lord our God also handed Og king of Bashan and all his people over to us, and we struck them down to complete destruction,

3:4  LA: vastantes cunctas civitates illius uno tempore. Non fuit oppidum, quod nos effugeret : sexaginta urbes, omnem regionem Argob regni Og in Basan.
     EN: laying waste all his cities at one time. No town escaped us: sixty cities, the entire region of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

3:5  LA: Cunctæ urbes erant munitæ muris altissimis, portisque et vectibus, absque oppidis innumeris, quæ non habebant muros.
     EN: All these cities were fortified with very high walls, gates, and bars, besides countless towns without walls.

3:6  LA: Et delevimus eos, sicut feceramus Sehon regi Hesebon, disperdentes omnem civitatem, virosque ac mulieres et parvulos :
     EN: We destroyed them as we had done to Sihon king of Heshbon, destroying every city, its men, women, and little children.

3:7  LA: jumenta autem et spolia urbium diripuimus.
     EN: But we seized the livestock and the spoils of the cities.

3:8  LA: Tulimusque illo in tempore terram de manu duorum regum Amorrhæorum, qui erant trans Jordanem : a torrente Arnon usque ad montem Hermon,
     EN: At that time we took the land from the two Amorite kings beyond the Jordan, from the Arnon torrent to Mount Hermon,

3:9  LA: quem Sidonii Sarion vocant, et Amorrhæi Sanir :
     EN: which the Sidonians call Sirion and the Amorites Senir:

3:10  LA: omnes civitates, quæ sitæ sunt in planitie, et universam terram Galaad et Basan usque ad Selcha, et Edrai civitates regni Og in Basan.
     EN: all the cities on the plain, and all the land of Gilead and Bashan as far as Salecah and Edrei, cities of Og's kingdom in Bashan.

3:11  LA: Solus quippe Og rex Basan restiterat de stirpe gigantum. Monstratur lectus ejus ferreus, qui est in Rabbath filiorum Ammon, novem cubitos habens longitudinis, et quatuor latitudinis ad mensuram cubiti virilis manus.
     EN: For Og king of Bashan alone remained of the line of the giants. His iron bed is displayed at Rabbah of the Ammonites, nine cubits long and four cubits wide, measured by the cubit of a man's forearm.

3:12  LA: Terramque possedimus tempore illo ab Aroër, quæ est super ripam torrentis Arnon, usque ad mediam partem montis Galaad : et civitates illius dedi Ruben et Gad.
     EN: At that time we possessed the land from Aroer on the bank of the Arnon torrent to half the hill country of Gilead; I gave its cities to Reuben and Gad.

3:13  LA: Reliquam autem partem Galaad, et omnem Basan regni Og, tradidi mediæ tribui Manasse, omnem regionem Argob : cunctaque Basan vocatur Terra gigantum.
     EN: The remaining part of Gilead and all Bashan, Og's kingdom, I gave to half the tribe of Manasseh—the whole region of Argob. All Bashan is called the Land of Giants.

3:14  LA: Jair filius Manasse possedit omnem regionem Argob usque ad terminos Gessuri et Machati. Vocavitque ex nomine suo Basan, Havoth Jair, id est, Villas Jair, usque in præsentem diem.
     EN: Jair son of Manasseh possessed the whole region of Argob as far as the borders of the Geshurites and Maacathites. He called Bashan after his own name, Havvoth-jair, that is, Villages of Jair, to this day.

3:15  LA: Machir quoque dedi Galaad.
     EN: I also gave Gilead to Machir.

3:16  LA: Et tribubus Ruben et Gad dedi de terra Galaad usque ad torrentem Arnon medium torrentis, et confinium usque ad torrentem Jeboc, qui est terminus filiorum Ammon :
     EN: To the tribes of Reuben and Gad I gave land from Gilead to the Arnon torrent, with the middle of the torrent as the boundary, and as far as the Jabbok torrent, the border of the Ammonites;

3:17  LA: et planitiem solitudinis, atque Jordanem, et terminos Cenereth usque ad mare deserti, quod est salsissimum, ad radices montis Phasga contra orientem.
     EN: also the wilderness plain, and the Jordan, and the borders of Chinnereth as far as the sea of the wilderness, which is exceedingly salty, at the foot of Mount Pisgah to the east.

3:18  LA: Præcepique vobis in tempore illo, dicens : Dominus Deus vester dat vobis terram hanc in hæreditatem : expediti præcedite fratres vestros filios Israël omnes viri robusti,
     EN: At that time I commanded you: The Lord your God gives you this land as an inheritance. All your strong men shall go armed ahead of your brothers, the children of Israel,

3:19  LA: absque uxoribus, et parvulis, atque jumentis. Novi enim quod plura habeatis pecora, et in urbibus remanere debebunt, quas tradidi vobis,
     EN: leaving your wives, little children, and livestock. I know that you have many herds, and they must remain in the cities I have given you,

3:20  LA: donec requiem tribuat Dominus fratribus vestris, sicut vobis tribuit : et possideant ipsi etiam terram, quam daturus est eis trans Jordanem : tunc revertetur unusquisque in possessionem suam, quam dedi vobis.
     EN: until the Lord grants rest to your brothers as He has to you, and they too possess the land He is going to give them beyond the Jordan. Then each of you shall return to the possession I have given you.

3:21  LA: Josue quoque in tempore illo præcepi, dicens : Oculi tui viderunt quæ fecit Dominus Deus vester duobus his regibus : sic faciet omnibus regnis, ad quæ transiturus es.
     EN: At that time I also commanded Joshua: Your eyes have seen what the Lord your God did to these two kings. He will do the same to all the kingdoms into which you are about to cross.

3:22  LA: Ne timeas eos : Dominus enim Deus vester pugnabit pro vobis.
     EN: Do not fear them, for the Lord your God will fight for you.

3:23  LA: Precatusque sum Dominum in tempore illo, dicens :
     EN: At that time I prayed to the Lord, saying:

3:24  LA: Domine Deus, tu cœpisti ostendere servo tuo magnitudinem tuam, manumque fortissimam : neque enim est alius deus vel in cælo, vel in terra, qui possit facere opera tua, et comparari fortitudini tuæ.
     EN: Lord God, You have begun to show Your servant Your greatness and Your mighty hand. There is no other god in heaven or on earth who can do Your works or compare with Your strength.

3:25  LA: Transibo igitur, et videbo terram hanc optimam trans Jordanem, et montem istum egregium, et Libanum.
     EN: Let me therefore cross over and see this excellent land beyond the Jordan, that splendid mountain, and Lebanon.

3:26  LA: Iratusque est Dominus mihi propter vos, nec exaudivit me, sed dixit mihi : Sufficit tibi : nequaquam ultra loquaris de hac re ad me.
     EN: But the Lord was angry with me because of you and did not hear me. He said to me: Enough! Speak to Me no further about this matter.

3:27  LA: Ascende cacumen Phasgæ, et oculos tuos circumfer ad occidentem, et ad aquilonem, austrumque et orientem, et aspice ; nec enim transibis Jordanem istum.
     EN: Go up to the summit of Pisgah and turn your eyes west, north, south, and east, and look, for you will not cross this Jordan.

3:28  LA: Præcipe Josue, et corrobora eum atque conforta : quia ipse præcedet populum istum, et dividet eis terram quam visurus es.
     EN: Commission Joshua, strengthen and encourage him, because he will go ahead of this people and divide for them the land you will see.

3:29  LA: Mansimusque in valle contra fanum Phogor.
     EN: We remained in the valley opposite the shrine of Peor.

DEUTERONOMY 4

4:1  LA: Et nunc, Israël, audi præcepta et judicia, quæ ego doceo te : ut faciens ea, vivas, et ingrediens possideas terram, quam Dominus Deus patrum vestrorum daturus est vobis.
     EN: And now, Israel, hear the precepts and judgments I teach you, so that by doing them you may live and enter and possess the land the Lord, the God of your fathers, is going to give you.

4:2  LA: Non addetis ad verbum, quod vobis loquor, nec auferetis ex eo : custodite mandata Domini Dei vestri, quæ ego præcipio vobis.
     EN: You shall not add to the word I speak to you or take away from it. Keep the commandments of the Lord your God that I command you.

4:3  LA: Oculi vestri viderunt omnia quæ fecit Dominus contra Beelphegor, quomodo contriverit omnes cultores ejus de medio vestri.
     EN: Your eyes have seen everything the Lord did against Baal-peor, how He crushed all its worshipers from among you.

4:4  LA: Vos autem qui adhæretis Domino Deo vestro, vivitis universi usque in præsentem diem.
     EN: But all of you who hold fast to the Lord your God are alive to this day.

4:5  LA: Scitis quod docuerim vos præcepta atque justitias, sicut mandavit mihi Dominus Deus meus : sic facietis ea in terra, quam possessuri estis :
     EN: You know that I have taught you precepts and righteous ordinances, as the Lord my God commanded me. You shall practice them in the land you are going to possess,

4:6  LA: et observabitis et implebitis opere. Hæc est enim vestra sapientia, et intellectus coram populis, ut audientes universa præcepta hæc, dicant : En populus sapiens et intelligens, gens magna.
     EN: observing them and carrying them out. This is your wisdom and understanding before the peoples, who will hear all these precepts and say: See, this great nation is a wise and understanding people.

4:7  LA: Nec est alia natio tam grandis, quæ habeat deos appropinquantes sibi, sicut Deus noster adest cunctis obsecrationibus nostris.
     EN: There is no other nation so great that it has gods as close to it as our God is present to all our prayers.

4:8  LA: Quæ est enim alia gens sic inclyta, ut habeat cæremonias, justaque judicia, et universam legem, quam ego proponam hodie ante oculos vestros ?
     EN: What other nation is so renowned that it has rites, just judgments, and an entire law like this which I will set before your eyes today?

4:9  LA: Custodi igitur temetipsum, et animam tuam sollicite. Ne obliviscaris verborum, quæ viderunt oculi tui, et ne excidant de corde tuo cunctis diebus vitæ tuæ. Docebis ea filios ac nepotes tuos,
     EN: Therefore guard yourself and your soul carefully. Do not forget the things your eyes have seen or let them slip from your heart all the days of your life. Teach them to your children and grandchildren,

4:10  LA: a die in quo stetisti coram Domino Deo tuo in Horeb, quando Dominus locutus est mihi, dicens : Congrega ad me populum, ut audiant sermones meos, et discant timere me omni tempore quo vivunt in terra, doceantque filios suos.
     EN: beginning with the day you stood before the Lord your God at Horeb, when the Lord said to me: Gather the people to Me, so that they may hear My words and learn to fear Me all the time they live on earth, and teach their children.

4:11  LA: Et accessistis ad radices montis, qui ardebat usque ad cælum : erantque in eo tenebræ, et nubes, et caligo.
     EN: You approached the foot of the mountain, which burned up to heaven; darkness, cloud, and gloom were upon it.

4:12  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad vos de medio ignis. Vocem verborum ejus audistis, et formam penitus non vidistis.
     EN: The Lord spoke to you from the midst of the fire. You heard the sound of His words but saw no form at all.

4:13  LA: Et ostendit vobis pactum suum, quod præcepit ut faceretis, et decem verba, quæ scripsit in duabus tabulis lapideis.
     EN: He declared His covenant to you, which He commanded you to carry out, and the ten words, which He wrote on two stone tablets.

4:14  LA: Mihique mandavit in illo tempore ut docerem vos cæremonias et judicia, quæ facere deberetis in terra, quam possessuri estis.
     EN: At that time He commanded me to teach you the rites and judgments you were to practice in the land you are going to possess.

4:15  LA: Custodite igitur sollicite animas vestras. Non vidistis aliquam similitudinem in die, qua locutus est vobis Dominus in Horeb de medio ignis :
     EN: So guard your souls carefully. You saw no likeness on the day the Lord spoke to you at Horeb from the midst of the fire,

4:16  LA: ne forte decepti faciatis vobis sculptam similitudinem, aut imaginem masculi vel feminæ :
     EN: lest you be deceived and make yourselves a carved likeness or image of a male or female,

4:17  LA: similitudinem omnium jumentorum, quæ sunt super terram, vel avium sub cælo volantium,
     EN: a likeness of any animal on earth, or bird flying under heaven,

4:18  LA: atque reptilium, quæ moventur in terra, sive piscium qui sub terra morantur in aquis :
     EN: or creeping thing moving on the earth, or fish dwelling in the waters beneath the earth;

4:19  LA: ne forte elevatis oculis ad cælum, videas solem et lunam, et omnia astra cæli, et errore deceptus adores ea, et colas quæ creavit Dominus Deus tuus in ministerium cunctis gentibus, quæ sub cælo sunt.
     EN: and lest you lift your eyes to heaven, see the sun, moon, and all the stars of heaven, and be misled into worshiping and serving them, which the Lord your God created to serve all the nations under heaven.

4:20  LA: Vos autem tulit Dominus, et eduxit de fornace ferrea Ægypti, ut haberet populum hæreditarium, sicut est in præsenti die.
     EN: But the Lord took you and brought you out of Egypt's iron furnace to have a people as His inheritance, as you are today.

4:21  LA: Iratusque est Dominus contra me propter sermones vestros, et juravit ut non transirem Jordanem, nec ingrederer terram optimam, quam daturus est vobis.
     EN: The Lord was angry with me because of your words and swore that I would not cross the Jordan or enter the excellent land He is going to give you.

4:22  LA: Ecce morior in hac humo ; non transibo Jordanem : vos transibitis, et possidebitis terram egregiam.
     EN: See, I am dying in this land; I will not cross the Jordan. You will cross and possess that splendid land.

4:23  LA: Cave nequando obliviscaris pacti Domini Dei tui, quod pepigit tecum, et facias tibi sculptam similitudinem eorum, quæ fieri Dominus prohibuit :
     EN: Take care never to forget the covenant the Lord your God made with you, and never to make yourself a carved likeness of anything the Lord has forbidden to be made.

4:24  LA: quia Dominus Deus tuus ignis consumens est, Deus æmulator.
     EN: For the Lord your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God.

4:25  LA: Si genueritis filios ac nepotes, et morati fueritis in terra, deceptique feceritis vobis aliquam similitudinem, patrantes malum coram Domino Deo vestro, ut eum ad iracundiam provocetis :
     EN: If you have children and grandchildren and remain in the land, and are deceived into making yourselves some likeness, doing evil before the Lord your God and provoking Him to anger,

4:26  LA: testes invoco hodie cælum et terram, cito perituros vos esse de terra, quam transito Jordane possessuri estis : non habitabitis in ea longo tempore, sed delebit vos Dominus,
     EN: I call heaven and earth as witnesses today that you will quickly perish from the land you are going to possess across the Jordan. You will not live there long; the Lord will destroy you

4:27  LA: atque disperget in omnes gentes, et remanebitis pauci in nationibus, ad quas vos ducturus est Dominus.
     EN: and scatter you among all the nations, and you will remain few among the peoples to whom the Lord will lead you.

4:28  LA: Ibique servietis diis, qui hominum manu fabricati sunt, ligno et lapidi qui non vident, nec audiunt, nec comedunt, nec odorantur.
     EN: There you will serve gods fashioned by human hands, wood and stone, which neither see nor hear nor eat nor smell.

4:29  LA: Cumque quæsieris ibi Dominum Deum tuum, invenies eum : si tamen toto corde quæsieris, et tota tribulatione animæ tuæ.
     EN: But when you seek the Lord your God there, you will find Him, provided you seek with all your heart and all the anguish of your soul.

4:30  LA: Postquam te invenerint omnia quæ prædicta sunt, novissimo tempore reverteris ad Dominum Deum tuum, et audies vocem ejus.
     EN: After all these foretold things have overtaken you, in the latter time you will return to the Lord your God and hear His voice.

4:31  LA: Quia Deus misericors Dominus Deus tuus est : non dimittet te, nec omnino delebit, neque obliviscetur pacti, in quo juravit patribus tuis.
     EN: For the Lord your God is a merciful God. He will not abandon you or destroy you utterly or forget the covenant He swore to your fathers.

4:32  LA: Interroga de diebus antiquis, qui fuerunt ante te ex die quo creavit Deus hominem super terram, a summo cælo usque ad summum ejus, si facta est aliquando hujuscemodi res, aut umquam cognitum est,
     EN: Ask about the ancient days before you, from the day God created humankind on earth; ask from one end of heaven to the other whether anything like this has ever happened or ever been known:

4:33  LA: ut audiret populus vocem Dei loquentis de medio ignis, sicut tu audisti, et vixisti :
     EN: that a people heard God's voice speaking from the midst of the fire, as you heard, and lived;

4:34  LA: si fecit Deus ut ingrederetur, et tolleret sibi gentem de medio nationum, per tentationes, signa atque portenta, per pugnam et robustam manum, extentumque brachium, et horribiles visiones juxta omnia quæ fecit pro vobis Dominus Deus vester in Ægypto, videntibus oculis tuis :
     EN: whether God ever went and took for Himself a people from among the nations, through trials, signs, wonders, battle, a mighty hand, an outstretched arm, and terrifying sights, like everything the Lord your God did for you in Egypt before your eyes,

4:35  LA: ut scires quoniam Dominus ipse est Deus, et non est alius præter eum.
     EN: so that you might know that the Lord Himself is God, and there is no other besides Him.

4:36  LA: De cælo te fecit audire vocem suam, ut doceret te, et in terra ostendit tibi ignem suum maximum, et audisti verba illius de medio ignis :
     EN: From heaven He made you hear His voice to instruct you; on earth He showed you His great fire, and you heard His words from the midst of the fire,

4:37  LA: quia dilexit patres tuos, et elegit semen eorum post eos. Eduxitque te præcedens in virtute sua magna ex Ægypto,
     EN: because He loved your fathers and chose their offspring after them. He went before you and brought you out of Egypt by His great power,

4:38  LA: ut deleret nationes maximas et fortiores te in introitu tuo : et introduceret te, daretque tibi terram earum in possessionem, sicut cernis in præsenti die.
     EN: to destroy nations greater and stronger than you as you entered, and to bring you in and give you their land as a possession, as you see today.

4:39  LA: Scito ergo hodie, et cogitato in corde tuo quod Dominus ipse sit Deus in cælo sursum, et in terra deorsum, et non sit alius.
     EN: Therefore know today and consider in your heart that the Lord Himself is God in heaven above and on earth below, and there is no other.

4:40  LA: Custodi præcepta ejus atque mandata, quæ ego præcipio tibi : ut bene sit tibi, et filiis tuis post te, et permaneas multo tempore super terram, quam Dominus Deus tuus daturus est tibi.
     EN: Keep His precepts and commandments that I command you, so that it may go well with you and your children after you, and you may remain a long time in the land the Lord your God is going to give you.

4:41  LA: Tunc separavit Moyses tres civitates trans Jordanem ad orientalem plagam,
     EN: Then Moses set apart three cities beyond the Jordan to the east,

4:42  LA: ut confugiat ad eas qui occiderit nolens proximum suum, nec sibi fuerit inimicus ante unum et alterum diem, et ad harum aliquam urbium possit evadere :
     EN: so that anyone who unintentionally killed his neighbor, without having been his enemy previously, could flee to them and escape to one of these cities:

4:43  LA: Bosor in solitudine, quæ sita est in terra campestri de tribu Ruben : et Ramoth in Galaad, quæ est in tribu Gad : et Golan in Basan, quæ est in tribu Manasse.
     EN: Bezer in the wilderness, situated in the plain of the tribe of Reuben; Ramoth in Gilead, in the tribe of Gad; and Golan in Bashan, in the tribe of Manasseh.

4:44  LA: Ista est lex, quam proposuit Moyses coram filiis Israël :
     EN: This is the law Moses set before the children of Israel,

4:45  LA: et hæc testimonia et cæremoniæ atque judicia, quæ locutus est ad filios Israël, quando egressi sunt de Ægypto,
     EN: and these are the testimonies, rites, and judgments he spoke to the children of Israel after they came out of Egypt,

4:46  LA: trans Jordanem in valle contra fanum Phogor in terra Sehon regis Amorrhæi, qui habitavit in Hesebon, quem percussit Moyses. Filii quoque Israël egressi ex Ægypto
     EN: beyond the Jordan, in the valley opposite the shrine of Peor, in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon and whom Moses struck down. The children of Israel, after coming out of Egypt,

4:47  LA: possederunt terram ejus, et terram Og regis Basan, duorum regum Amorrhæorum, qui erant trans Jordanem ad solis ortum :
     EN: possessed his land and the land of Og king of Bashan, the two Amorite kings beyond the Jordan to the east,

4:48  LA: ab Aroër, quæ sita est super ripam torrentis Arnon, usque ad montem Sion, qui est et Hermon,
     EN: from Aroer on the bank of the Arnon torrent to Mount Sion, also called Hermon,

4:49  LA: omnem planitiem trans Jordanem ad orientalem plagam, usque ad mare solitudinis, et usque ad radices montis Phasga.
     EN: and the entire plain beyond the Jordan to the east, as far as the sea of the wilderness and the foot of Mount Pisgah.

DEUTERONOMY 5

5:1  LA: Vocavitque Moyses omnem Israëlem, et dixit ad eum : Audi, Israël, cæremonias atque judicia, quæ ego loquor in auribus vestris hodie : discite ea, et opere complete.
     EN: Moses summoned all Israel and said: Hear, Israel, the rites and judgments I speak in your hearing today. Learn them and carry them out.

5:2  LA: Dominus Deus noster pepigit nobiscum fœdus in Horeb.
     EN: The Lord our God made a covenant with us at Horeb.

5:3  LA: Non cum patribus nostris iniit pactum, sed nobiscum qui in præsentiarum sumus, et vivimus.
     EN: He did not make this covenant with our fathers, but with us who are here and alive today.

5:4  LA: Facie ad faciem locutus est nobis in monte de medio ignis.
     EN: He spoke to us face to face on the mountain from the midst of the fire.

5:5  LA: Ego sequester et medius fui inter Dominum et vos in tempore illo, ut annuntiarem vobis verba ejus : timuistis enim ignem, et non ascendistis in montem. Et ait :
     EN: At that time I stood as mediator between the Lord and you, to proclaim His words to you, because you feared the fire and did not go up the mountain. He said:

5:6  LA: Ego Dominus Deus tuus, qui eduxi te de terra Ægypti, de domo servitutis.
     EN: I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.

5:7  LA: Non habebis deos alienos in conspectu meo.
     EN: You shall have no foreign gods before Me.

5:8  LA: Non facies tibi sculptile, nec similitudinem omnium, quæ in cælo sunt desuper, et quæ in terra deorsum, et quæ versantur in aquis sub terra.
     EN: You shall not make yourself a carved image or a likeness of anything in heaven above, on earth below, or in the waters beneath the earth.

5:9  LA: Non adorabis ea, et non coles. Ego enim sum Dominus Deus tuus : Deus æmulator, reddens iniquitatem patrum super filios in tertiam et quartam generationem his qui oderunt me :
     EN: You shall not worship or serve them. For I am the Lord your God, a jealous God, repaying the fathers' iniquity upon the children to the third and fourth generation of those who hate Me,

5:10  LA: et faciens misericordiam in multa millia diligentibus me, et custodientibus præcepta mea.
     EN: and showing mercy to many thousands who love Me and keep My precepts.

5:11  LA: Non usurpabis nomen Domini Dei tui frustra : quia non erit impunitus qui super re vana nomen ejus assumpserit.
     EN: You shall not misuse the name of the Lord your God, for whoever invokes His name for a worthless purpose will not go unpunished.

5:12  LA: Observa diem sabbati, ut sanctifices eum, sicut præcepit tibi Dominus Deus tuus.
     EN: Observe the Sabbath day and keep it holy, as the Lord your God commanded you.

5:13  LA: Sex diebus operaberis, et facies omnia opera tua.
     EN: For six days you shall labor and do all your work.

5:14  LA: Septimus dies sabbati est, id est, requies Domini Dei tui. Non facies in eo quidquam operis tu, et filius tuus, et filia, servus et ancilla, et bos, et asinus, et omne jumentum tuum, et peregrinus qui est intra portas tuas : ut requiescat servus tuus, et ancilla tua, sicut et tu.
     EN: The seventh day is the Sabbath, that is, the rest of the Lord your God. On it you shall do no work—you, your son or daughter, your male or female slave, your ox or donkey or any of your livestock, or the foreigner within your gates—so that your male and female slaves may rest as you do.

5:15  LA: Memento quod et ipse servieris in Ægypto, et eduxerit te inde Dominus Deus tuus in manu forti, et brachio extento. Idcirco præcepit tibi ut observares diem sabbati.
     EN: Remember that you too served as a slave in Egypt, and the Lord your God brought you out with a strong hand and an outstretched arm. Therefore He commanded you to observe the Sabbath day.

5:16  LA: Honora patrem tuum et matrem, sicut præcepit tibi Dominus Deus tuus, ut longo vivas tempore, et bene sit tibi in terra, quam Dominus Deus tuus daturus est tibi.
     EN: Honor your father and mother, as the Lord your God commanded you, so that you may live a long time and it may go well with you in the land the Lord your God is going to give you.

5:17 (Vg 5:18)  LA: neque mœchaberis,
     EN: You shall not commit adultery.

5:18 (Vg 5:17)  LA: Non occides,
     EN: You shall not kill.

5:19  LA: furtumque non facies :
     EN: You shall not steal.

5:20  LA: nec loqueris contra proximum tuum falsum testimonium.
     EN: You shall not speak false testimony against your neighbor.

5:21  LA: Non concupisces uxorem proximi tui : non domum, non agrum, non servum, non ancillam, non bovem, non asinum, et universa quæ illius sunt.
     EN: You shall not covet your neighbor's wife, or his house, field, male or female slave, ox, donkey, or anything that belongs to him.

5:22  LA: Hæc verba locutus est Dominus ad omnem multitudinem vestram in monte de medio ignis et nubis, et caliginis, voce magna, nihil addens amplius : et scripsit ea in duabus tabulis lapideis, quas tradidit mihi.
     EN: The Lord spoke these words in a loud voice to your entire assembly on the mountain, from the midst of the fire, cloud, and darkness, adding nothing further. He wrote them on two stone tablets, which He gave me.

5:23  LA: Vos autem postquam audistis vocem de medio tenebrarum, et montem ardere vidistis, accessistis ad me omnes principes tribuum et majores natu, atque dixistis :
     EN: After you heard the voice from the midst of the darkness and saw the mountain burning, all your tribal leaders and elders approached me and said:

5:24  LA: Ecce ostendit nobis Dominus Deus noster majestatem et magnitudinem suam : vocem ejus audivimus de medio ignis, et probavimus hodie, quod loquente Deo cum homine, vixerit homo.
     EN: See, the Lord our God has shown us His majesty and greatness. We have heard His voice from the midst of the fire, and today we have learned by experience that a human being can live when God speaks with him.

5:25  LA: Cur ergo moriemur, et devorabit nos ignis hic maximus ? si enim audierimus ultra vocem Domini Dei nostri, moriemur.
     EN: Why then should we die and this great fire devour us? If we hear the voice of the Lord our God any longer, we will die.

5:26  LA: Quid est omnis caro, ut audiat vocem Dei viventis, qui de medio ignis loquitur sicut nos audivimus, et possit vivere ?
     EN: What mortal can hear the voice of the living God speaking from the midst of the fire, as we have heard, and live?

5:27  LA: Tu magis accede : et audi cuncta quæ dixerit Dominus Deus noster tibi : loquerisque ad nos, et nos audientes faciemus ea.
     EN: You go near instead and hear everything the Lord our God tells you. Speak to us, and we will listen and do it.

6:5  LA: Diliges Dominum Deum tuum ex toto corde tuo, et ex tota anima tua, et ex tota fortitudine tua.

Dominum Deum tuum timebis, et illi soli servies, ac per nomen illius jurabis.

Fear the Lord your God, serve Him alone, and swear by His name.

6:18  LA: et fac quod placitum est et bonum in conspectu Domini, ut bene sit tibi : et ingressus possideas terram optimam, de qua juravit Dominus patribus tuis,

     EN: The Lord commanded us to practice all these ordinances and fear the Lord our God, so that it may go well with us all the days of our life, as it does today.

6:25  LA: Eritque nostri misericors, si custodierimus et fecerimus omnia præcepta ejus coram Domino Deo nostro, sicut mandavit nobis.
     EN: He will be merciful to us if we keep and carry out all His precepts before the Lord our God, as He commanded us.

DEUTERONOMY 7

7:1  LA: Cum introduxerit te Dominus Deus tuus in terram, quam possessurus ingredieris, et deleverit gentes multas coram te, Hethæum, et Gergezæum, et Amorrhæum, Chananæum, et Pherezæum, et Hevæum, et Jebusæum, septem gentes multo majoris numeri quam tu es, et robustiores te :
     EN: When the Lord your God brings you into the land you are entering to possess, and destroys many nations before you—the Hittites, Girgashites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, seven nations far more numerous and stronger than you—

7:2  LA: tradideritque eas Dominus Deus tuus tibi, percuties eas usque ad internecionem. Non inibis cum eis fœdus, nec misereberis earum,
     EN: and the Lord your God hands them over to you, strike them down to complete destruction. Make no covenant with them and show them no mercy.

7:3  LA: neque sociabis cum eis conjugia. Filiam tuam non dabis filio ejus, nec filiam illius accipies filio tuo :
     EN: Do not join in marriage with them. Do not give your daughter to his son or take his daughter for your son,

7:4  LA: quia seducet filium tuum, ne sequatur me, et ut magis serviat diis alienis : irasceturque furor Domini, et delebit te cito.
     EN: because he will turn your son away from following Me to serve foreign gods instead. Then the Lord's fury will blaze and quickly destroy you.

7:5  LA: Quin potius hæc facietis eis : aras eorum subvertite, et confringite statuas, lucosque succidite, et sculptilia comburite :
     EN: Instead, do this to them: overthrow their altars, break their pillars, cut down their sacred groves, and burn their carved images.

7:6  LA: quia populus sanctus es Domino Deo tuo. Te elegit Dominus Deus tuus, ut sis ei populus peculiaris de cunctis populis, qui sunt super terram.
     EN: For you are a people holy to the Lord your God. The Lord your God chose you from all the peoples on earth to be a people specially His own.

7:7  LA: Non quia cunctas gentes numero vincebatis, vobis junctus est Dominus, et elegit vos, cum omnibus sitis populis pauciores :
     EN: The Lord joined Himself to you and chose you, not because you outnumbered all the nations—for you are fewer than all the peoples—

7:8  LA: sed quia dilexit vos Dominus, et custodivit juramentum, quod juravit patribus vestris : eduxitque vos in manu forti, et redemit de domo servitutis, de manu Pharaonis regis Ægypti.
     EN: but because the Lord loved you and kept the oath He swore to your fathers. He brought you out with a strong hand and redeemed you from the house of slavery, from the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt.

7:9  LA: Et scies, quia Dominus Deus tuus, ipse est Deus fortis et fidelis, custodiens pactum et misericordiam diligentibus se, et his qui custodiunt præcepta ejus in mille generationes :
     EN: Know that the Lord your God is Himself God, mighty and faithful, keeping covenant and mercy for a thousand generations with those who love Him and keep His precepts,

7:10  LA: et reddens odientibus se statim, ita ut disperdat eos, et ultra non differat, protinus eis restituens quod merentur.
     EN: and immediately repaying those who hate Him by destroying them. He delays no longer, promptly repaying them what they deserve.

7:11  LA: Custodi ergo præcepta et cæremonias atque judicia, quæ ego mando tibi hodie ut facias.
     EN: Therefore keep the precepts, rites, and judgments that I command you today to practice.

7:12  LA: Si postquam audieris hæc judicia, custodieris ea, et feceris, custodiet et Dominus Deus tuus pactum tibi, et misericordiam quam juravit patribus tuis :
     EN: If, after hearing these judgments, you keep and carry them out, the Lord your God will also keep with you the covenant and mercy He swore to your fathers.

7:13  LA: et diliget te, ac multiplicabit, benedicetque fructui ventris tui, et fructui terræ tuæ, frumento tuo, atque vindemiæ, oleo, et armentis, gregibus ovium tuarum super terram, pro qua juravit patribus tuis ut daret eam tibi.
     EN: He will love and multiply you and bless the fruit of your womb and of your land, your grain, vintage, oil, herds, and flocks of sheep, in the land He swore to your fathers to give you.

7:14  LA: Benedictus eris inter omnes populos. Non erit apud te sterilis utriusque sexus, tam in hominibus quam in gregibus tuis.
     EN: You will be blessed among all peoples. Neither male nor female shall be barren among you, whether among humans or your flocks.

7:15  LA: Auferet Dominus a te omnem languorem : et infirmitates Ægypti pessimas, quas novisti, non inferet tibi, sed cunctis hostibus tuis.
     EN: The Lord will remove every sickness from you. He will not inflict on you the terrible diseases of Egypt that you have known, but on all your enemies.

7:16  LA: Devorabis omnes populos, quos Dominus Deus tuus daturus est tibi. Non parcet eis oculus tuus, nec servies diis eorum, ne sint in ruinam tui.
     EN: You shall consume all the peoples the Lord your God is going to give you. Your eye shall not spare them, and you shall not serve their gods, lest they bring about your ruin.

7:17  LA: Si dixeris in corde tuo : Plures sunt gentes istæ quam ego : quomodo potero delere eas ?
     EN: If you say in your heart: These nations are more numerous than I; how can I destroy them?

7:18  LA: noli metuere, sed recordare quæ fecerit Dominus Deus tuus Pharaoni, et cunctis Ægyptiis,
     EN: do not be afraid. Remember what the Lord your God did to Pharaoh and all the Egyptians,

7:19  LA: plagas maximas, quas viderunt oculi tui, et signa atque portenta, manumque robustam, et extentum brachium, ut educeret te Dominus Deus tuus : sic faciet cunctis populis, quos metuis.
     EN: the great plagues your eyes saw, the signs and wonders, the mighty hand and outstretched arm with which the Lord your God brought you out. He will do the same to all the peoples you fear.

7:20  LA: Insuper et crabrones mittet Dominus Deus tuus in eos, donec deleat omnes atque disperdat qui te fugerint, et latere potuerint.
     EN: Moreover, the Lord your God will send hornets among them until He destroys and wipes out all who flee from you and manage to hide.

7:21  LA: Non timebis eos, quia Dominus Deus tuus in medio tui est, Deus magnus et terribilis :
     EN: Do not fear them, because the Lord your God is in your midst, a great and terrible God.

7:22  LA: ipse consumet nationes has in conspectu tuo paulatim atque per partes. Non poteris eas delere pariter : ne forte multiplicentur contra te bestiæ terræ.
     EN: He will consume these nations before you little by little and part by part. You cannot destroy them all at once, lest the wild animals of the land multiply against you.

7:23  LA: Dabitque eos Dominus Deus tuus in conspectu tuo : et interficiet illos, donec penitus deleantur.
     EN: The Lord your God will deliver them before you and kill them until they are utterly destroyed.

7:24  LA: Tradetque reges eorum in manus tuas, et disperdes nomina eorum sub cælo : nullus poterit resistere tibi, donec conteras eos.
     EN: He will hand their kings over to you, and you will wipe out their names from under heaven. No one will be able to resist you until you crush them.

7:25  LA: Sculptilia eorum igne combures : non concupisces argentum et aurum, de quibus facta sunt, neque assumes ex eis tibi quidquam, ne offendas, propterea quia abominatio est Domini tui :
     EN: Burn their carved images with fire. Do not covet the silver and gold from which they are made or take any of it for yourself, lest you fall into sin, because it is an abomination to your Lord.

7:26  LA: nec inferes quippiam ex idolo in domum tuam, ne fias anathema, sicut et illud est. Quasi spurcitiam detestaberis, et velut inquinamentum ac sordes abominationi habebis, quia anathema est.
     EN: Do not bring anything from an idol into your house, lest you become devoted to destruction as it is. Detest it as filth and abhor it as pollution and dirt, because it is devoted to destruction.

DEUTERONOMY 8

8:1  LA: Omne mandatum, quod ego præcipio tibi hodie, cave diligenter ut facias, ut possitis vivere, et multiplicemini, ingressique possideatis terram, pro qua juravit Dominus patribus vestris.
     EN: Take great care to carry out every commandment I command you today, so that you may live and multiply and enter and possess the land the Lord swore to your fathers.

8:2  LA: Et recordaberis cuncti itineris, per quod adduxit te Dominus Deus tuus quadraginta annis per desertum, ut affligeret te, atque tentaret, et nota fierent quæ in tuo animo versabantur, utrum custodires mandata illius, an non.
     EN: Remember the whole journey along which the Lord your God led you through the wilderness for forty years, to afflict and test you and make known what was in your heart—whether you would keep His commandments or not.

8:3  LA: Afflixit te penuria, et dedit tibi cibum manna, quod ignorabas tu et patres tui : ut ostenderet tibi quod non in solo pane vivat homo, sed in omni verbo quod egreditur de ore Dei.
     EN: He afflicted you with hunger and gave you manna as food, which neither you nor your fathers had known, to show you that a human being lives not by bread alone, but by every word that comes from the mouth of God.

8:4  LA: Vestimentum tuum, quo operiebaris, nequaquam vetustate defecit, et pes tuus non est subtritus, en quadragesimus annus est :
     EN: The clothing covering you never wore out with age, and your foot was not worn sore: see, this is the fortieth year.

8:5  LA: ut recogites in corde tuo, quia sicut erudit filium suum homo, sic Dominus Deus tuus erudivit te,
     EN: Consider in your heart that, as a man disciplines his son, so the Lord your God has disciplined you,

8:6  LA: ut custodias mandata Domini Dei tui, et ambules in viis ejus, et timeas eum.
     EN: so that you may keep the commandments of the Lord your God, walk in His ways, and fear Him.

8:7  LA: Dominus enim Deus tuus introducet te in terram bonam, terram rivorum, aquarumque et fontium, in cujus campis et montibus erumpunt fluviorum abyssi :
     EN: For the Lord your God will bring you into a good land, a land of streams, waters, and springs, where deep sources of rivers burst forth in its plains and mountains;

8:8  LA: terram frumenti, hordei ac vinearum, in qua ficus, et malogranata, et oliveta nascuntur : terram olei ac mellis,
     EN: a land of wheat, barley, and vineyards, where figs, pomegranates, and olive groves grow; a land of oil and honey,

8:9  LA: ubi absque ulla penuria comedes panem tuum, et rerum omnium abundantia perfrueris : cujus lapides ferrum sunt, et de montibus ejus æris metalla fodiuntur :
     EN: where you will eat your bread without any shortage and enjoy an abundance of everything; its stones are iron, and copper ore is dug from its mountains.

8:10  LA: ut cum comederis, et satiatus fueris, benedicas Domino Deo tuo pro terra optima, quam dedit tibi.
     EN: When you have eaten and are satisfied, bless the Lord your God for the excellent land He has given you.

8:11  LA: Observa, et cave nequando obliviscaris Domini Dei tui, et negligas mandata ejus atque judicia et cæremonias, quas ego præcipio tibi hodie :
     EN: Be watchful and take care never to forget the Lord your God or neglect His commandments, judgments, and rites that I command you today.

8:12  LA: ne postquam comederis et satiatus fueris, domos pulchras ædificaveris, et habitaveris in eis,
     EN: Otherwise, after you have eaten and are satisfied, built beautiful houses and lived in them,

8:13  LA: habuerisque armenta boum, et ovium greges, argenti et auri, cunctarumque rerum copiam,
     EN: and acquired herds of cattle, flocks of sheep, and an abundance of silver, gold, and everything,

8:14  LA: elevetur cor tuum, et non reminiscaris Domini Dei tui, qui eduxit te de terra Ægypti, de domo servitutis,
     EN: your heart may be lifted up, and you may fail to remember the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery,

8:15  LA: et ductor tuus fuit in solitudine magna atque terribili, in qua erat serpens flatu adurens, et scorpio, ac dipsas, et nullæ omnino aquæ : qui eduxit rivos de petra durissima,
     EN: and was your guide in the great and terrible wilderness, where there were serpents with scorching breath, scorpions, and thirst-causing snakes, and no water at all. He brought streams out of the hardest rock

8:16  LA: et cibavit te manna in solitudine, quod nescierunt patres tui. Et postquam afflixit ac probavit, ad extremum misertus est tui,
     EN: and fed you in the wilderness with manna, which your fathers had not known. After afflicting and testing you, He finally had mercy on you,

8:17  LA: ne diceres in corde tuo : Fortitudo mea, et robur manus meæ, hæc mihi omnia præstiterunt :
     EN: lest you say in your heart: My strength and the might of my hand have provided all this for me.

8:18  LA: sed recorderis Domini Dei tui, quod ipse vires tibi præbuerit, ut impleret pactum suum, super quo juravit patribus tuis, sicut præsens indicat dies.
     EN: Instead, remember the Lord your God, for He Himself gave you strength to fulfill His covenant, which He swore to your fathers, as this day shows.

8:19  LA: Sin autem oblitus Domini Dei tui, secutus fueris deos alienos, coluerisque illos et adoraveris : ecce nunc prædico tibi quod omnino dispereas.
     EN: But if you forget the Lord your God and follow foreign gods, serving and worshiping them, see, I warn you now that you will utterly perish.

8:20  LA: Sicut gentes, quas delevit Dominus in introitu tuo, ita et vos peribitis, si inobedientes fueritis voci Domini Dei vestri.
     EN: Like the nations the Lord destroyed when you entered, you too will perish if you disobey the voice of the Lord your God.

DEUTERONOMY 9

9:1  LA: Audi , Israël : tu transgredieris hodie Jordanem, ut possideas nationes maximas et fortiores te, civitates ingentes, et ad cælum usque muratas,
     EN: Hear, Israel: today you will cross the Jordan to possess nations greater and stronger than you, enormous cities walled up to heaven,

9:2  LA: populum magnum atque sublimem, filios Enacim, quos ipse vidisti et audisti, quibus nullus potest ex adverso resistere.
     EN: a great and lofty people, descendants of the Anakim, whom you yourself have seen and heard of, and whom no one can withstand.

9:3  LA: Scies ergo hodie quod Dominus Deus tuus ipse transibit ante te, ignis devorans atque consumens, qui conterat eos, et deleat atque disperdat ante faciem tuam velociter, sicut locutus est tibi :
     EN: Know today, therefore, that the Lord your God will Himself cross before you, a devouring and consuming fire, to crush them, destroy them, and quickly wipe them out before you, as He told you.

9:4  LA: ne dicas in corde tuo, cum deleverit eos Dominus Deus tuus in conspectu tuo : Propter justitiam meam introduxit me Dominus ut terram hanc possiderem, cum propter impietates suas istæ deletæ sint nationes.
     EN: When the Lord your God has destroyed them before you, do not say in your heart: Because of my righteousness the Lord brought me in to possess this land, whereas these nations have been destroyed because of their own wickedness.

9:5  LA: Neque enim propter justitias tuas, et æquitatem cordis tui ingredieris, ut possideas terras earum : sed quia illæ egerunt impie, introëunte te deletæ sunt : et ut compleret verbum suum Dominus, quod sub juramento pollicitus est patribus tuis, Abraham, Isaac, et Jacob.
     EN: It is not because of your righteous deeds or the uprightness of your heart that you will enter and possess their lands. They are destroyed when you enter because they have acted wickedly, and so that the Lord may fulfill the word He promised under oath to your fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

9:6  LA: Scito ergo quod non propter justitias tuas Dominus Deus tuus dederit tibi terram hanc optimam in possessionem, cum durissimæ cervicis sis populus.
     EN: Know, then, that the Lord your God has not given you this excellent land as a possession because of your righteous deeds, for you are a very stiff-necked people.

9:7  LA: Memento, et ne obliviscaris, quomodo ad iracundiam provocaveris Dominum Deum tuum in solitudine. Ex eo die, quo egressus es ex Ægypto usque ad locum istum, semper adversum Dominum contendisti.
     EN: Remember and do not forget how you provoked the Lord your God to anger in the wilderness. From the day you came out of Egypt until this place, you have continually contended against the Lord.

9:8  LA: Nam et in Horeb provocasti eum, et iratus delere te voluit,
     EN: At Horeb too you provoked Him, and in His anger He wanted to destroy you,

9:9  LA: quando ascendi in montem, ut acciperem tabulas lapideas, tabulas pacti quod pepigit vobiscum Dominus : et perseveravi in monte quadraginta diebus ac noctibus, panem non comedens, et aquam non bibens.
     EN: when I went up the mountain to receive the stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant the Lord made with you. I remained on the mountain forty days and nights, eating no bread and drinking no water.

9:10  LA: Deditque mihi Dominus duas tabulas lapideas scriptas digito Dei, et continentes omnia verba quæ vobis locutus est in monte de medio ignis, quando concio populi congregata est.
     EN: The Lord gave me two stone tablets written with the finger of God, containing all the words He spoke to you on the mountain from the midst of the fire when the people's assembly was gathered.

9:11  LA: Cumque transissent quadraginta dies, et totidem noctes, dedit mihi Dominus duas tabulas lapideas, tabulas fœderis,
     EN: When forty days and as many nights had passed, the Lord gave me the two stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant,

9:12  LA: dixitque mihi : Surge, et descende hinc cito : quia populus tuus, quem eduxisti de Ægypto, deseruerunt velociter viam, quam demonstrasti eis, feceruntque sibi conflatile.
     EN: and said to me: Rise and go down from here quickly, because your people whom you brought out of Egypt have quickly abandoned the way you showed them and made themselves a cast image.

9:13  LA: Rursumque ait Dominus ad me : Cerno quod populus iste duræ cervicis sit :
     EN: The Lord said to me again: I see that this is a stiff-necked people.

9:14  LA: dimitte me ut conteram eum, et deleam nomen ejus de sub cælo, et constituam te super gentem, quæ hac major et fortior sit.
     EN: Let Me crush them and wipe out their name from under heaven, and establish you over a nation greater and stronger than this one.

9:15  LA: Cumque de monte ardente descenderem, et duas tabulas fœderis utraque tenerem manu,
     EN: When I came down from the burning mountain, holding the two tablets of the covenant in my two hands,

9:16  LA: vidissemque vos peccasse Domino Deo vestro, et fecisse vobis vitulum conflatilem, ac deseruisse velociter viam ejus, quam vobis ostenderat :
     EN: I saw that you had sinned against the Lord your God, made yourselves a cast calf, and quickly abandoned His way that He had shown you.

9:17  LA: projeci tabulas de manibus meis, confregique eas in conspectu vestro.
     EN: I threw the tablets from my hands and broke them before your eyes.

9:18  LA: Et procidi ante Dominum sicut prius, quadraginta diebus et noctibus panem non comedens, et aquam non bibens, propter omnia peccata vestra quæ gessistis contra Dominum, et eum ad iracundiam provocastis :
     EN: I fell down before the Lord as before, for forty days and nights, eating no bread and drinking no water, because of all the sins you had committed against the Lord, provoking Him to anger.

9:19  LA: timui enim indignationem et iram illius, qua adversum vos concitatus, delere vos voluit. Et exaudivit me Dominus etiam hac vice.
     EN: For I feared His indignation and anger, which had blazed against you so that He wanted to destroy you. But the Lord heard me this time too.

9:20  LA: Adversum Aaron quoque vehementer iratus, voluit eum conterere, et pro illo similiter deprecatus sum.
     EN: He was also very angry with Aaron and wanted to crush him, and I likewise prayed for him.

9:21  LA: Peccatum autem vestrum quod feceratis, id est, vitulum, arripiens, igne combussi, et in frusta comminuens, omninoque in pulverem redigens, projeci in torrentem, qui de monte descendit.
     EN: I took your sin that you had made, the calf, burned it with fire, broke it into pieces, ground it entirely to dust, and threw it into the torrent flowing down the mountain.

9:22  LA: In incendio quoque, et in tentatione, et in Sepulchris concupiscentiæ provocastis Dominum :
     EN: At the Burning, at the Testing, and at the Graves of Craving you also provoked the Lord.

9:23  LA: et quando misit vos de Cadesbarne, dicens : Ascendite, et possidete terram, quam dedi vobis, et contempsistis imperium Domini Dei vestri, et non credidistis ei, neque vocem ejus audire voluistis :
     EN: And when He sent you from Kadesh-barnea, saying: Go up and possess the land I have given you, you despised the command of the Lord your God, did not trust Him, and would not listen to His voice.

9:24  LA: sed semper fuistis rebelles a die qua nosse vos cœpi.
     EN: You have always been rebellious from the day I first knew you.

9:25  LA: Et jacui coram Domino quadraginta diebus ac noctibus, quibus eum suppliciter deprecabar, ne deleret vos ut fuerat comminatus :
     EN: So I lay before the Lord forty days and nights, humbly begging Him not to destroy you as He had threatened.

9:26  LA: et orans dixi : Domine Deus, ne disperdas populum tuum, et hæreditatem tuam, quam redemisti in magnitudine tua, quos eduxisti de Ægypto in manu forti.
     EN: I prayed: Lord God, do not destroy Your people and Your inheritance, whom You redeemed in Your greatness and brought out of Egypt with a strong hand.

9:27  LA: Recordare servorum tuorum Abraham, Isaac, et Jacob : ne aspicias duritiam populi hujus, et impietatem atque peccatum :
     EN: Remember Your servants Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. Do not regard this people's stubbornness, wickedness, and sin,

9:28  LA: ne forte dicant habitatores terræ, de qua eduxisti nos : Non poterat Dominus introducere eos in terram, quam pollicitus est eis, et oderat illos : idcirco eduxit, ut interficeret eos in solitudine :
     EN: lest the inhabitants of the land from which You brought us say: The Lord could not bring them into the land He promised them, and He hated them; therefore He brought them out to kill them in the wilderness.

9:29  LA: qui sunt populus tuus et hæreditas tua, quos eduxisti in fortitudine tua magna, et in brachio tuo extento.
     EN: They are Your people and Your inheritance, whom You brought out by Your great strength and outstretched arm.

DEUTERONOMY 10

10:1  LA: In tempore illo dixit Dominus ad me : Dola tibi duas tabulas lapideas, sicut priores fuerunt, et ascende ad me in montem : faciesque arcam ligneam,
     EN: At that time the Lord said to me: Cut yourself two stone tablets like the first ones and come up to Me on the mountain. Make a wooden ark,

10:2  LA: et scribam in tabulis verba quæ fuerunt in his qui ante confregisti : ponesque eas in arca.
     EN: and I will write on the tablets the words that were on the former ones you broke. Place them in the ark.

10:3  LA: Feci igitur arcam de lignis setim. Cumque dolassem duas tabulas lapideas instar priorum, ascendi in montem, habens eas in manibus.
     EN: So I made an ark of acacia wood. After cutting two stone tablets like the first ones, I went up the mountain with them in my hands.

10:4  LA: Scripsitque in tabulis, juxta id quod prius scripserat, verba decem, quæ locutus est Dominus ad vos in monte de medio ignis, quando populus congregatus est : et dedit eas mihi.
     EN: He wrote on the tablets, as He had written before, the ten words the Lord spoke to you on the mountain from the midst of the fire when the people assembled, and He gave them to me.

10:5  LA: Reversusque de monte, descendi, et posui tabulas in arcam, quam feceram, quæ hucusque ibi sunt, sicut mihi præcepit Dominus.
     EN: I turned and came down the mountain and placed the tablets in the ark I had made. They are still there, as the Lord commanded me.

10:6  LA: Filii autem Israël moverunt castra ex Beroth filiorum Jacan in Mosera, ubi Aaron mortuus ac sepultus est, pro quo sacerdotio functus est Eleazar filius ejus.
     EN: The children of Israel moved their camp from Beeroth of the sons of Jaakan to Moserah, where Aaron died and was buried. His son Eleazar served as priest in his place.

10:7  LA: Inde venerunt in Gadgad : de quo loco profecti, castrametati sunt in Jetebatha, in terra aquarum atque torrentium.
     EN: From there they came to Gudgodah; departing from that place, they camped at Jotbathah, a land of waters and torrents.

10:8  LA: Eo tempore separavit tribum Levi, ut portaret arcam fœderis Domini, et staret coram eo in ministerio, ac benediceret in nomine illius usque in præsentem diem.
     EN: At that time He set apart the tribe of Levi to carry the ark of the Lord's covenant, to stand before Him in ministry, and to bless in His name, to this day.

10:9  LA: Quam ob rem non habuit Levi partem, neque possessionem cum fratribus suis : quia ipse Dominus possessio ejus est, sicut promisit ei Dominus Deus tuus.
     EN: Therefore Levi had no share or possession with his brothers, because the Lord Himself is his possession, as the Lord your God promised him.

10:10  LA: Ego autem steti in monte, sicut prius, quadraginta diebus ac noctibus : exaudivitque me Dominus etiam hac vice, et te perdere noluit.
     EN: I remained on the mountain, as before, forty days and nights. The Lord heard me this time too and would not destroy you.

10:11  LA: Dixitque mihi : Vade, et præcede populum, ut ingrediatur, et possideat terram, quam juravi patribus eorum ut traderem eis.
     EN: He said to me: Go and lead the people, so that they may enter and possess the land I swore to their fathers to give them.

10:12  LA: Et nunc Israël, quid Dominus Deus tuus petit a te, nisi ut timeas Dominum Deum tuum, et ambules in viis ejus, et diligas eum, ac servias Domino Deo tuo in toto corde tuo, et in tota anima tua :
     EN: And now, Israel, what does the Lord your God ask of you except to fear the Lord your God, walk in His ways, love Him, and serve the Lord your God with all your heart and all your soul,

10:13  LA: custodiasque mandata Domini, et cæremonias ejus, quas ego hodie præcipio tibi, ut bene sit tibi ?
     EN: and keep the Lord's commandments and His rites, which I command you today for your good?

10:14  LA: En Domini Dei tui cælum est, et cælum cæli, terra, et omnia quæ in ea sunt :
     EN: See, heaven and the heaven of heaven belong to the Lord your God, the earth and everything in it.

10:15  LA: et tamen patribus tuis conglutinatus est Dominus, et amavit eos, elegitque semen eorum post eos, id est, vos, de cunctis gentibus, sicut hodie comprobatur.
     EN: Yet the Lord bound Himself to your fathers and loved them, and chose their offspring after them—you—from all the nations, as is shown today.

10:16  LA: Circumcidite igitur præputium cordis vestri, et cervicem vestram ne induretis amplius :
     EN: Therefore circumcise the foreskin of your heart and stiffen your neck no longer.

10:17  LA: quia Dominus Deus vester ipse est Deus deorum, et Dominus dominantium, Deus magnus, et potens, et terribilis, qui personam non accipit, nec munera.
     EN: For the Lord your God is Himself God of gods and Lord of lords, the great, mighty, and terrible God, who shows no partiality and accepts no bribes.

10:18  LA: Facit judicium pupillo et viduæ ; amat peregrinum, et dat ei victum atque vestitum.
     EN: He administers justice for the orphan and widow, loves the foreigner, and gives him food and clothing.

10:19  LA: Et vos ergo amate peregrinos, quia et ipsi fuistis advenæ in terra Ægypti.
     EN: Therefore you too shall love foreigners, for you yourselves were strangers in the land of Egypt.

10:20  LA: Dominum Deum tuum timebis, et ei soli servies : ipsi adhærebis, jurabisque in nomine illius.
     EN: Fear the Lord your God and serve Him alone. Hold fast to Him and swear by His name.

10:21  LA: Ipse est laus tua, et Deus tuus, qui fecit tibi hæc magnalia et terribilia, quæ viderunt oculi tui.
     EN: He is your praise and your God, who has done for you these great and terrible things your eyes have seen.

10:22  LA: In septuaginta animabus descenderunt patres tui in Ægyptum, et ecce nunc multiplicavit te Dominus Deus tuus sicut astra cæli.
     EN: Your fathers went down to Egypt as seventy persons, and now, see, the Lord your God has multiplied you like the stars of heaven.

DEUTERONOMY 11

11:1  LA: Ama itaque Dominum Deum tuum, et observa præcepta ejus et cæremonias, judicia atque mandata, omni tempore.
     EN: Therefore love the Lord your God and keep His precepts, rites, judgments, and commandments at all times.

11:2  LA: Cognoscite hodie quæ ignorant filii vestri, qui non viderunt disciplinam Domini Dei vestri, magnalia ejus et robustam manum, extentumque brachium,
     EN: Recognize today what your children do not know, for they did not see the discipline of the Lord your God, His great deeds, mighty hand, and outstretched arm,

11:3  LA: signa et opera quæ fecit in medio Ægypti Pharaoni regi, et universæ terræ ejus,
     EN: the signs and works He did in the midst of Egypt to Pharaoh the king and his entire land,

11:4  LA: omnique exercitui Ægyptiorum, et equis ac curribus : quomodo operuerint eos aquæ maris Rubri, cum vos persequerentur, et deleverit eos Dominus usque in præsentem diem :
     EN: and to the whole Egyptian army, its horses and chariots: how the waters of the Red Sea covered them when they pursued you, and the Lord destroyed them to this day;

11:5  LA: vobisque quæ fecerit in solitudine donec veniretis ad hunc locum :
     EN: what He did for you in the wilderness until you reached this place;

11:6  LA: et Dathan atque Abiron filiis Eliab, qui fuit filius Ruben : quos aperto ore suo terra absorbuit, cum domibus et tabernaculis, et universa substantia eorum, quam habebant in medio Israël.
     EN: and what He did to Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab son of Reuben, when the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them, their households and tents, and all the possessions they had among Israel.

11:7  LA: Oculi vestri viderunt opera Domini magna quæ fecit,
     EN: Your eyes have seen the great works the Lord has done,

11:8  LA: ut custodiatis universa mandata illius, quæ ego hodie præcipio vobis, et possitis introire, et possidere terram, ad quam ingredimini,
     EN: so that you may keep all His commandments that I command you today and be able to enter and possess the land into which you are going,

11:9  LA: multoque in ea vivatis tempore : quam sub juramento pollicitus est Dominus patribus vestris, et semini eorum, lacte et melle manantem.
     EN: and live there a long time, the land flowing with milk and honey that the Lord promised under oath to your fathers and their offspring.

11:10  LA: Terra enim, ad quam ingrederis possidendam, non est sicut terra Ægypti, de qua existi, ubi jacto semine in hortorum morem aquæ ducuntur irriguæ :
     EN: For the land you are entering to possess is not like the land of Egypt from which you came, where, after sowing the seed, irrigation water is channeled as in gardens.

11:11  LA: sed montuosa est et campestris, de cælo expectans pluvias,
     EN: It has mountains and plains and waits for rain from heaven,

11:12  LA: quam Dominus Deus tuus semper invisit, et oculi illius in ea sunt a principio anni usque ad finem ejus.
     EN: a land the Lord your God continually visits; His eyes are on it from the beginning of the year to its end.

11:13  LA: Si ergo obedieritis mandatis meis, quæ ego hodie præcipio vobis, ut diligatis Dominum Deum vestrum, et serviatis ei in toto corde vestro, et in tota anima vestra :
     EN: If, then, you obey my commandments that I command you today, loving the Lord your God and serving Him with all your heart and all your soul,

11:14  LA: dabit pluviam terræ vestræ temporaneam et serotinam, ut colligatis frumentum, et vinum, et oleum,
     EN: He will give your land early and late rain, so that you may gather grain, wine, and oil,

11:15  LA: fœnumque ex agris ad pascenda jumenta, et ut ipsi comedatis ac saturemini.
     EN: and grass from the fields to feed your livestock, and so that you yourselves may eat and be satisfied.

11:16  LA: Cavete ne forte decipiatur cor vestrum, et recedatis a Domino, serviatisque diis alienis, et adoretis eos :
     EN: Take care lest your heart be deceived and you turn away from the Lord to serve foreign gods and worship them,

11:17  LA: iratusque Dominus claudat cælum, et pluviæ non descendant, nec terra det germen suum, pereatisque velociter de terra optima, quam Dominus daturus est vobis.
     EN: and the Lord become angry and shut the heavens so that no rain falls and the earth yields no produce, and you quickly perish from the excellent land the Lord is going to give you.

11:18  LA: Ponite hæc verba mea in cordibus et in animis vestris, et suspendite ea pro signo in manibus, et inter oculos vestros collocate.
     EN: Place these words of mine in your hearts and souls; hang them as a sign on your hands and set them between your eyes.

11:19  LA: Docete filios vestros ut illa meditentur : quando sederis in domo tua, et ambulaveris in via, et accubueris atque surrexeris.
     EN: Teach your children to meditate on them, when you sit in your house, walk along the road, lie down, and rise.

11:20  LA: Scribes ea super postes et januas domus tuæ,
     EN: Write them on the doorposts and doors of your house,

11:21  LA: ut multiplicentur dies tui, et filiorum tuorum in terra, quam juravit Dominus patribus tuis, ut daret eis quamdiu cælum imminet terræ.
     EN: so that your days and your children's days may be multiplied in the land the Lord swore to your fathers to give them, as long as heaven stands over the earth.

11:22  LA: Si enim custodieritis mandata quæ ego præcipio vobis, et feceritis ea, ut diligatis Dominum Deum vestrum, et ambuletis in omnibus viis ejus, adhærentes ei,
     EN: For if you keep and carry out the commandments I command you, loving the Lord your God, walking in all His ways, and holding fast to Him,

11:23  LA: disperdet Dominus omnes gentes istas ante faciem vestram, et possidebitis eas, quæ majores et fortiores vobis sunt.
     EN: the Lord will destroy all these nations before you, and you will possess nations greater and stronger than yourselves.

11:24  LA: Omnis locus, quem calcaverit pes vester, vester erit. A deserto, et a Libano, a flumine magno Euphrate usque ad mare occidentale erunt termini vestri.
     EN: Every place your foot treads shall be yours. Your borders shall extend from the wilderness and Lebanon, from the great river Euphrates to the western sea.

11:25  LA: Nullus stabit contra vos : terrorem vestrum et formidinem dabit Dominus Deus vester super omnem terram quam calcaturi estis, sicut locutus est vobis.
     EN: No one will stand against you. The Lord your God will spread dread and fear of you over all the land you will tread, as He told you.

11:26  LA: En propono in conspectu vestro hodie benedictionem et maledictionem :
     EN: See, today I set before you a blessing and a curse:

11:27  LA: benedictionem, si obedieritis mandatis Domini Dei vestri, quæ ego hodie præcipio vobis :
     EN: a blessing if you obey the commandments of the Lord your God that I command you today,

11:28  LA: maledictionem, si non obedieritis mandatis Domini Dei vestri, sed recesseritis de via, quam ego nunc ostendo vobis, et ambulaveritis post deos alienos, quos ignoratis.
     EN: a curse if you do not obey the commandments of the Lord your God, but turn away from the way I now show you and follow foreign gods you do not know.

11:29  LA: Cum vero introduxerit te Dominus Deus tuus in terram, ad quam pergis habitandam, pones benedictionem super montem Garizim, maledictionem super montem Hebal :
     EN: When the Lord your God brings you into the land where you are going to live, place the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal.

11:30  LA: qui sunt trans Jordanem, post viam quæ vergit ad solis occubitum in terra Chananæi, qui habitat in campestribus contra Galgalam, quæ est juxta vallem tendentem et intrantem procul.
     EN: They are beyond the Jordan, behind the road toward the setting sun, in the land of the Canaanites who live in the plains opposite Gilgal, beside the valley extending far inland.

11:31  LA: Vos enim transibitis Jordanem, ut possideatis terram quam Dominus Deus vester daturus est vobis, ut habeatis et possideatis illam.
     EN: For you will cross the Jordan to possess the land the Lord your God is going to give you, so that you may hold and possess it.

11:32  LA: Videte ergo ut impleatis cæremonias atque judicia, quæ ego hodie ponam in conspectu vestro.
     EN: Therefore take care to carry out the rites and judgments I will set before you today.

DEUTERONOMY 12

12:1  LA: Hæc sunt præcepta atque judicia, quæ facere debetis in terra, quam Dominus Deus patrum tuorum daturus est tibi, ut possideas eam cunctis diebus, quibus super humum gradieris.
     EN: These are the precepts and judgments you must practice in the land the Lord, the God of your fathers, is going to give you, so that you may possess it all the days you walk on the earth.

12:2  LA: Subvertite omnia loca, in quibus coluerunt gentes, quas possessuri estis, deos suos super montes excelsos, et colles, et subter omne lignum frondosum.
     EN: Overthrow all the places where the nations you will dispossess worshiped their gods, on high mountains, on hills, and beneath every leafy tree.

12:3  LA: Dissipate aras eorum, et confringite statuas : lucos igne comburite, et idola comminuite : disperdite nomina eorum de locis illis.
     EN: Tear down their altars and break their pillars; burn their sacred groves with fire and smash their idols. Wipe out their names from those places.

12:4  LA: Non facietis ita Domino Deo vestro :
     EN: You shall not do this to the Lord your God.

12:5  LA: sed ad locum, quem elegerit Dominus Deus vester de cunctis tribubus vestris, ut ponat nomen suum ibi, et habitet in eo, venietis :
     EN: Instead, come to the place the Lord your God chooses from all your tribes to establish His name there and dwell in it.

12:6  LA: et offeretis in loco illo holocausta et victimas vestras, decimas et primitias manuum vestrarum, et vota atque donaria, primogenita boum et ovium.
     EN: In that place offer your burnt offerings and sacrifices, your tithes and the first offerings of your hands, your vows and gifts, and the firstborn of your cattle and sheep.

12:7  LA: Et comedetis ibi in conspectu Domini Dei vestri : ac lætabimini in cunctis, ad quæ miseritis manum vos et domus vestræ, in quibus benedixerit vobis Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: There you shall eat before the Lord your God and rejoice, you and your households, in every undertaking in which the Lord your God has blessed you.

12:8  LA: Non facietis ibi quæ nos hic facimus hodie, singuli quod sibi rectum videtur :
     EN: There you shall not do as we do here today, each doing what seems right to himself,

12:9  LA: neque enim usque in præsens tempus venistis ad requiem, et possessionem, quam Dominus Deus vester daturus est vobis.
     EN: for you have not yet come to the rest and possession the Lord your God is going to give you.

12:10  LA: Transibitis Jordanem, et habitabitis in terra, quam Dominus Deus vester daturus est vobis, ut requiescatis a cunctis hostibus per circuitum : et absque ullo timore habitetis
     EN: You will cross the Jordan and live in the land the Lord your God is going to give you, resting from all the enemies around you and dwelling without any fear

12:11  LA: in loco, quem elegerit Dominus Deus vester, ut sit nomen ejus in eo : illuc omnia, quæ præcipio, conferetis, holocausta, et hostias, ac decimas, et primitias manuum vestrarum : et quidquid præcipuum est in muneribus, quæ vovebitis Domino.
     EN: in the place the Lord your God chooses for His name to be there. Bring there everything I command: burnt offerings, sacrifices, tithes, the first offerings of your hands, and all the choicest gifts you vow to the Lord.

12:12  LA: Ibi epulabimini coram Domino Deo vestro, vos et filii ac filiæ vestræ, famuli et famulæ, atque Levites qui in urbibus vestris commoratur : neque enim habet aliam partem et possessionem inter vos.
     EN: There you shall feast before the Lord your God—you, your sons and daughters, male and female servants, and the Levite living in your cities, for he has no other share or possession among you.

12:13  LA: Cave ne offeras holocausta tua in omni loco, quem videris :
     EN: Take care not to offer your burnt offerings in any place you see,

12:14  LA: sed in eo, quem elegerit Dominus, in una tribuum tuarum offeres hostias, et facies quæcumque præcipio tibi.
     EN: but offer sacrifices in the place the Lord chooses in one of your tribes, and there do everything I command you.

12:15  LA: Sin autem comedere volueris, et te esus carnium delectaverit, occide, comede juxta benedictionem Domini Dei tui, quam dedit tibi in urbibus tuis : sive immundum fuerit, hoc est, maculatum et debile : sive mundum, hoc est, integrum et sine macula, quod offerri licet, sicut capream et cervum, comedes :
     EN: But if you wish to eat and desire meat, slaughter and eat according to the blessing the Lord your God has given you in your cities. Whether it is unclean, that is, blemished and weak, or clean, that is, sound and unblemished and fit to be offered, you may eat it as you eat gazelle and deer,

12:16  LA: absque esu dumtaxat sanguinis, quem super terram quasi aquam effundes.
     EN: except that you shall not eat the blood; pour it on the ground like water.

12:17  LA: Non poteris comedere in oppidis tuis decimam frumenti, et vini, et olei tui, primogenita armentorum et pecorum, et omnia quæ voveris, et sponte offerre volueris, et primitias manuum tuarum :
     EN: You may not eat in your towns the tithe of your grain, wine, or oil, the firstborn of your cattle or flocks, anything you have vowed or wish to offer freely, or the first offerings of your hands.

12:18  LA: sed coram Domino Deo tuo comedes ea in loco, quem elegerit Dominus Deus tuus, tu et filius tuus, et filia tua, et servus et famula, atque Levites qui manet in urbibus tuis : et lætaberis et reficieris coram Domino Deo tuo in cunctis ad quæ extenderis manum tuam.
     EN: Eat them before the Lord your God in the place the Lord your God chooses—you, your son and daughter, your male and female servant, and the Levite dwelling in your cities. Rejoice and refresh yourselves before the Lord your God in everything you undertake.

12:19  LA: Cave ne derelinquas Levitem in omni tempore quo versaris in terra.
     EN: Take care not to abandon the Levite all the time you live in the land.

12:20  LA: Quando dilataverit Dominus Deus tuus terminos tuos, sicut locutus est tibi, et volueris vesci carnibus, quas desiderat anima tua :
     EN: When the Lord your God enlarges your borders, as He told you, and you wish to eat the meat your soul desires,

12:21  LA: locus autem, quem elegerit Dominus Deus tuus ut sit nomen ejus ibi, si procul fuerit, occides de armentis et pecoribus, quæ habueris, sicut præcepi tibi, et comedes in oppidis tuis, ut tibi placet.
     EN: if the place the Lord your God chooses for His name to be there is far away, slaughter from the cattle and flocks you have, as I commanded you, and eat in your towns as you please.

12:22  LA: Sicut comeditur caprea et cervus, ita vesceris eis : et mundus et immundus in commune vescentur.
     EN: Eat them as gazelle and deer are eaten; the clean and unclean may eat together.

12:23  LA: Hoc solum cave, ne sanguinem comedas : sanguis enim eorum pro anima est, et idcirco non debes animam comedere cum carnibus :
     EN: Only take care not to eat the blood, for their blood stands for their life; therefore you must not eat the life with the meat.

12:24  LA: sed super terram fundes quasi aquam,
     EN: Pour it on the ground like water,

12:25  LA: ut bene sit tibi et filiis tuis post te, cum feceris quod placet in conspectu Domini.
     EN: so that it may go well with you and your children after you, when you do what pleases the Lord.

12:26  LA: Quæ autem sanctificaveris, et voveris Domino, tolles, et venies ad locum, quem elegerit Dominus :
     EN: But take the things you have consecrated and vowed to the Lord and come to the place the Lord chooses.

12:27  LA: et offeres oblationes tuas carnem et sanguinem super altare Domini Dei tui : sanguinem hostiarum fundes in altari ; carnibus autem ipse vesceris.
     EN: Offer your offerings, their flesh and blood, on the altar of the Lord your God. Pour the blood of the sacrifices on the altar, but eat the meat yourself.

12:28  LA: Observa et audi omnia quæ ego præcipio tibi, ut bene sit tibi et filiis tuis post te in sempiternum, cum feceris quod bonum est et placitum in conspectu Domini Dei tui.
     EN: Observe and hear everything I command you, so that it may go well with you and your children after you forever, when you do what is good and pleasing before the Lord your God.

12:29  LA: Quando disperdiderit Dominus Deus tuus ante faciem tuam gentes, ad quas ingredieris possidendas, et possederis eas, atque habitaveris in terra earum :
     EN: When the Lord your God destroys before you the nations whose lands you enter to possess, and you dispossess them and live in their land,

12:30  LA: cave ne imiteris eas, postquam te fuerint introëunte subversæ, et requiras cæremonias earum, dicens : Sicut coluerunt gentes istæ deos suos, ita et ego colam.
     EN: take care not to imitate them after they have been overthrown at your coming. Do not inquire into their rites, saying: As these nations worshiped their gods, so will I worship.

12:31  LA: Non facies similiter Domino Deo tuo. Omnes enim abominationes, quas aversatur Dominus, fecerunt diis suis, offerentes filios et filias, et comburentes igni.
     EN: You shall not do the same to the Lord your God. For they have done for their gods every abomination the Lord detests, offering their sons and daughters and burning them with fire.

DEUTERONOMY 13

13:1 (Vg 12:32)  LA: Quod præcipio tibi, hoc tantum facito Domino : nec addas quidquam, nec minuas.
     EN: Do for the Lord only what I command you; neither add anything nor take anything away.

13:2 (Vg 13:1)  LA: Si surrexerit in medio tui prophetes, aut qui somnium vidisse se dicat, et prædixerit signum atque portentum,
     EN: If a prophet arises among you, or someone who says he has seen a dream, and predicts a sign or wonder,

13:3 (Vg 13:2)  LA: et evenerit quod locutus est, et dixerit tibi : Eamus, et sequamur deos alienos quos ignoras, et serviamus eis :
     EN: and what he said comes to pass, and he says to you: Let us go and follow foreign gods you do not know, and serve them,

13:4 (Vg 13:3)  LA: non audies verba prophetæ illius aut somniatoris : quia tentat vos Dominus Deus vester, ut palam fiat utrum diligatis eum an non, in toto corde, et in tota anima vestra.
     EN: do not listen to the words of that prophet or dreamer. For the Lord your God is testing you, to make clear whether you love Him with all your heart and all your soul or not.

13:5 (Vg 13:4)  LA: Dominum Deum vestrum sequimini, et ipsum timete, et mandata illius custodite, et audite vocem ejus : ipsi servietis, et ipsi adhærebitis.
     EN: Follow the Lord your God and fear Him; keep His commandments and hear His voice. Serve Him and hold fast to Him.

13:6 (Vg 13:5)  LA: Propheta autem ille aut fictor somniorum interficietur : quia locutus est ut vos averteret a Domino Deo vestro, qui eduxit vos de terra Ægypti, et redemit vos de domo servitutis : ut errare te faceret de via, quam tibi præcepit Dominus Deus tuus : et auferes malum de medio tui.
     EN: But that prophet or fabricator of dreams shall be killed, because he spoke to turn you away from the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt and redeemed you from the house of slavery, making you stray from the way the Lord your God commanded you. Remove the evil from your midst.

13:7 (Vg 13:6)  LA: Si tibi voluerit persuadere frater tuus filius matris tuæ, aut filius tuus vel filia, sive uxor quæ est in sinu tuo, aut amicus, quem diligis ut animam tuam, clam dicens : Eamus, et serviamus diis alienis, quos ignoras tu, et patres tui,
     EN: If your brother, your mother's son, or your son or daughter, the wife in your embrace, or your friend whom you love as your own soul tries to persuade you secretly, saying: Let us go and serve foreign gods, unknown to you and your fathers,

13:8 (Vg 13:7)  LA: cunctarum in circuitu gentium, quæ juxta vel procul sunt, ab initio usque ad finem terræ,
     EN: the gods of any surrounding nations, near or far, from one end of the earth to the other,

13:9 (Vg 13:8)  LA: non acquiescas ei, nec audias, neque parcat ei oculus tuus ut miserearis et occultes eum,
     EN: do not yield to him or listen to him. Your eye shall not spare him by showing mercy or concealing him.

13:10 (Vg 13:9)  LA: sed statim interficies : sit primum manus tua super eum, et postea omnis populus mittat manum.
     EN: Instead, kill him at once. Your hand shall be first against him, and afterward all the people shall lay their hands on him.

13:11 (Vg 13:10)  LA: Lapidibus obrutus necabitur : quia voluit te abstrahere a Domino Deo tuo, qui eduxit te de terra Ægypti, de domo servitutis :
     EN: He shall be put to death by stoning because he sought to draw you away from the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery,

13:12 (Vg 13:11)  LA: ut omnis Israël audiens timeat, et nequaquam ultra faciat quippiam hujus rei simile.
     EN: so that all Israel may hear and fear and never again do anything like this.

13:13 (Vg 13:12)  LA: Si audieris in una urbium tuarum, quas Dominus Deus tuus dabit tibi ad habitandum, dicentes aliquos :
     EN: If you hear people saying in one of the cities the Lord your God gives you to live in:

13:14 (Vg 13:13)  LA: Egressi sunt filii Belial de medio tui, et averterunt habitatores urbis suæ, atque dixerunt : Eamus, et serviamus diis alienis quos ignoratis :
     EN: Sons of Belial have gone out from among you and turned aside the inhabitants of their city, saying: Let us go and serve foreign gods you do not know,

13:15 (Vg 13:14)  LA: quære sollicite et diligenter, rei veritate perspecta, si inveneris certum esse quod dicitur, et abominationem hanc opere perpetratam,
     EN: inquire carefully and diligently. Examine the truth of the matter, and if you find that the report is certain and this abomination has actually been committed,

13:16 (Vg 13:15)  LA: statim percuties habitatores urbis illius in ore gladii, et delebis eam ac omnia quæ in illa sunt, usque ad pecora.
     EN: immediately strike the inhabitants of that city with the edge of the sword and destroy it and everything in it, including the livestock.

13:17 (Vg 13:16)  LA: Quidquid etiam supellectilis fuerit, congregabis in medio platearum ejus, et cum ipsa civitate succendes, ita ut universa consumas Domino Deo tuo, et sit tumulus sempiternus. Non ædificabitur amplius,
     EN: Gather all its belongings in the midst of its squares and burn them with the city, consuming everything for the Lord your God. It shall be a perpetual mound of ruins, never rebuilt.

13:18 (Vg 13:17)  LA: et non adhærebit de illo anathemate quidquam in manu tua : ut avertatur Dominus ab ira furoris sui, et misereatur tui, multiplicetque te sicut juravit patribus tuis,
     EN: None of the things devoted to destruction shall cling to your hand, so that the Lord may turn from His furious anger, have mercy on you, and multiply you as He swore to your fathers,

13:19 (Vg 13:18)  LA: quando audieris vocem Domini Dei tui custodiens omnia præcepta ejus, quæ ego præcipio tibi hodie, ut facias quod placitum est in conspectu Domini Dei tui.
     EN: when you hear the voice of the Lord your God, keeping all His precepts that I command you today and doing what pleases the Lord your God.

DEUTERONOMY 14

14:1  LA: Filii estote Domini Dei vestri : non vos incidetis, nec facietis calvitium super mortuo :
     EN: Be children of the Lord your God. Do not cut yourselves or shave yourselves bald for the dead,

14:2  LA: quoniam populus sanctus es Domino Deo tuo, et te elegit ut sis ei in populum peculiarem de cunctis gentibus, quæ sunt super terram.
     EN: because you are a people holy to the Lord your God, and He chose you from all the nations on earth to be a people specially His own.

14:3  LA: Ne comedatis quæ immunda sunt.
     EN: Do not eat unclean things.

14:4  LA: Hoc est animal quod comedere debetis : bovem, et ovem, et capram,
     EN: These are the animals you may eat: cattle, sheep, goats,

14:5  LA: cervum et capream, bubalum, tragelaphum, pygargum, orygem, camelopardalum.
     EN: deer, gazelles, buffalo, goat-stags, white-rumped antelopes, oryxes, and giraffes.

14:6  LA: Omne animal, quod in duas partes findit ungulam, et ruminat, comedetis.
     EN: You may eat every animal that has a hoof split in two and chews the cud.

14:7  LA: De his autem, quæ ruminant, et ungulam non findunt, comedere non debetis, ut camelum, leporem, chœrogryllum : hæc, quia ruminant et non dividunt ungulam, immunda erunt vobis.
     EN: But you must not eat those that chew the cud without having a split hoof, such as the camel, hare, and rock badger. These are unclean for you because they chew the cud but do not divide the hoof.

14:8  LA: Sus quoque, quoniam dividat ungulam et non ruminat, immunda erit. Carnibus eorum non vescemini, et cadavera non tangetis.
     EN: The pig is also unclean, because it divides the hoof but does not chew the cud. Do not eat their flesh or touch their carcasses.

14:9  LA: Hæc comedetis ex omnibus quæ morantur in aquis : quæ habent pinnulas et squamas, comedite :
     EN: Of everything dwelling in the waters, these you may eat: eat those that have fins and scales.

14:10  LA: quæ absque pinnulis et squamis sunt, ne comedatis, quia immunda sunt.
     EN: Do not eat those without fins and scales, because they are unclean.

14:11  LA: Omnes aves mundas comedite.
     EN: Eat all clean birds.

14:12  LA: Immundas ne comedatis : aquilam scilicet, et gryphem, et haliæetum,
     EN: Do not eat the unclean: the eagle, griffon, and sea eagle,

14:13  LA: ixion et vulturem ac milvum juxta genus suum :
     EN: the ixion, vulture, and kite according to its kind,

14:14  LA: et omne corvini generis,
     EN: and every kind of raven,

14:15  LA: et struthionem, ac noctuam, et larum, atque accipitrem juxta genus suum :
     EN: the ostrich, owl, gull, and hawk according to its kind,

14:16  LA: herodium ac cygnum, et ibin,
     EN: the heron, swan, and ibis,

14:17  LA: ac mergulum, porphyrionem, et nycticoracem,
     EN: the small diving bird, purple swamphen, and night raven,

14:18  LA: onocrotalum, et charadrium, singula in genere suo : upupam quoque et vespertilionem.
     EN: the pelican and plover, each according to its kind, and also the hoopoe and bat.

14:19  LA: Et omne quod reptat et pennulas habet, immundum erit, et non comedetur.
     EN: Every winged creeping thing shall be unclean and shall not be eaten.

14:20  LA: Omne quod mundum est, comedite.
     EN: Eat everything that is clean.

14:21  LA: Quidquid autem morticinum est, ne vescamini ex eo. Peregrino, qui intra portas tuas est, da ut comedat, aut vende ei : quia tu populus sanctus Domini Dei tui es. Non coques hædum in lacte matris suæ.
     EN: Do not eat anything that has died of itself. Give it to the foreigner within your gates to eat, or sell it to him, because you are a people holy to the Lord your God. Do not boil a young goat in its mother's milk.

14:22  LA: Decimam partem separabis de cunctis fructibus tuis qui nascuntur in terra per annos singulos,
     EN: Set apart a tenth of all the produce that grows in your land each year.

14:23  LA: et comedes in conspectu Domini Dei tui in loco quem elegerit, ut in eo nomen illius invocetur, decimam frumenti tui, et vini, et olei, et primogenita de armentis et ovibus tuis : ut discas timere Dominum Deum tuum omni tempore.
     EN: Eat before the Lord your God, in the place He chooses for His name to be invoked there, the tithe of your grain, wine, and oil, and the firstborn of your cattle and sheep, so that you may learn to fear the Lord your God at all times.

14:24  LA: Cum autem longior fuerit via, et locus quem elegerit Dominus Deus tuus, tibique benedixerit, nec potueris ad eum hæc cuncta portare,
     EN: But if the road is too long and the place the Lord your God chooses is too far away, and He has blessed you so that you cannot carry all these things there,

14:25  LA: vendes omnia, et in pretium rediges, portabisque manu tua, et proficisceris ad locum quem elegerit Dominus Deus tuus :
     EN: sell everything and turn it into money; carry it in your hand and go to the place the Lord your God chooses.

14:26  LA: et emes ex eadem pecunia quidquid tibi placuerit, sive ex armentis, sive ex ovibus, vinum quoque et siceram, et omne quod desiderat anima tua : et comedes coram Domino Deo tuo, et epulaberis tu et domus tua :
     EN: With that money buy whatever pleases you, whether cattle or sheep, wine or strong drink, or anything your soul desires. Eat before the Lord your God and feast, you and your household.

14:27  LA: et Levites qui intra portas tuas est, cave ne derelinquas eum, quia non habet aliam partem in possessione tua.
     EN: Take care not to abandon the Levite within your gates, because he has no other share in your possession.

14:28  LA: Anno tertio separabis aliam decimam ex omnibus quæ nascuntur tibi eo tempore, et repones intra januas tuas.
     EN: In the third year set apart another tithe of everything that grows for you at that time and store it within your gates.

14:29  LA: Venietque Levites qui aliam non habet partem nec possessionem tecum, et peregrinus ac pupillus et vidua, qui intra portas tuas sunt, et comedent et saturabuntur : ut benedicat tibi Dominus Deus tuus in cunctis operibus manuum tuarum quæ feceris.
     EN: Then the Levite, who has no other share or possession with you, and the foreigner, orphan, and widow within your gates shall come and eat and be satisfied, so that the Lord your God may bless you in all the work your hands do.

DEUTERONOMY 15

15:1  LA: Septimo anno facies remissionem,
     EN: In the seventh year grant a release,

15:2  LA: quæ hoc ordine celebrabitur. Cui debetur aliquid ab amico vel proximo ac fratre suo, repetere non poterit, quia annus remissionis est Domini.
     EN: to be observed in this way: anyone owed something by his friend, neighbor, or brother may not demand it back, because it is the Lord's year of release.

15:3  LA: A peregrino et advena exiges : civem et propinquum repetendi non habebis potestatem.
     EN: You may exact payment from a foreigner or stranger, but you shall have no right to demand it from a fellow citizen or kinsman.

15:4  LA: Et omnino indigens et mendicus non erit inter vos : ut benedicat tibi Dominus Deus tuus in terra, quam traditurus est tibi in possessionem.
     EN: There shall be no destitute person or beggar at all among you, so that the Lord your God may bless you in the land He is going to give you as a possession.

15:5  LA: Si tamen audieris vocem Domini Dei tui, et custodieris universa quæ jussit, et quæ ego hodie præcipio tibi, benedicet tibi, ut pollicitus est.
     EN: Provided you hear the voice of the Lord your God and keep everything He has ordered and I command you today, He will bless you as He promised.

15:6  LA: Fœnerabis gentibus multis, et ipse a nullo accipies mutuum. Dominaberis nationibus plurimis, et tui nemo dominabitur.
     EN: You will lend to many nations and borrow from no one. You will rule over many nations, and no one will rule over you.

15:7  LA: Si unus de fratribus tuis, qui morantur intra portas civitatis tuæ in terra quam Dominus Deus tuus daturus est tibi, ad paupertatem venerit, non obdurabis cor tuum, nec contrahes manum,
     EN: If one of your brothers living within the gates of your city in the land the Lord your God is going to give you falls into poverty, do not harden your heart or close your hand,

15:8  LA: sed aperies eam pauperi, et dabis mutuum, quo eum indigere perspexeris.
     EN: but open it to the poor man and lend him what you see he needs.

15:9  LA: Cave ne forte subrepat tibi impia cogitatio, et dicas in corde tuo : Appropinquat septimus annus remissionis : et avertas oculos tuos a paupere fratre tuo, nolens ei quod postulat mutuum commodare : ne clamet contra te ad Dominum, et fiat tibi in peccatum.
     EN: Take care lest a wicked thought creep upon you and you say in your heart: The seventh year, the year of release, is approaching, and turn your eyes away from your poor brother, refusing to lend him what he asks. Otherwise he will cry to the Lord against you, and it will become a sin for you.

15:10  LA: Sed dabis ei : nec ages quippiam callide in ejus necessitatibus sublevandis, ut benedicat tibi Dominus Deus tuus in omni tempore, et in cunctis ad quæ manum miseris.
     EN: Instead, give to him. Do not act craftily in relieving his needs, so that the Lord your God may bless you at all times and in everything you undertake.

15:11  LA: Non deerunt pauperes in terra habitationis tuæ : idcirco ego præcipio tibi, ut aperias manum fratri tuo egeno et pauperi, qui tecum versatur in terra.
     EN: There will always be poor people in the land where you live. Therefore I command you to open your hand to your needy and poor brother living with you in the land.

15:12  LA: Cum tibi venditus fuerit frater tuus Hebræus aut Hebræa, et sex annis servierit tibi, in septimo anno dimittes eum liberum :
     EN: If your fellow Hebrew, male or female, is sold to you and serves you six years, in the seventh year you shall set that person free.

15:13  LA: et quem libertate donaveris, nequaquam vacuum abire patieris :
     EN: When you grant someone freedom, never let that person go away empty-handed.

15:14  LA: sed dabis viaticum de gregibus, et de area, et torculari tuo, quibus Dominus Deus tuus benedixerit tibi.
     EN: Give provisions from your flocks, threshing floor, and winepress, with which the Lord your God has blessed you.

15:15  LA: Memento quod et ipse servieris in terra Ægypti, et liberaverit te Dominus Deus tuus, et idcirco ego nunc præcipio tibi.
     EN: Remember that you too served as a slave in the land of Egypt, and the Lord your God freed you. Therefore I command you this now.

15:16  LA: Sin autem dixerit : Nolo egredi : eo quod diligat te, et domum tuam, et bene sibi apud te esse sentiat :
     EN: But if he says: I do not wish to leave, because he loves you and your household and feels that he fares well with you,

15:17  LA: assumes subulam, et perforabis aurem ejus in janua domus tuæ, et serviet tibi usque in æternum. Ancillæ quoque similiter facies.
     EN: take an awl and pierce his ear at the door of your house, and he shall serve you forever. Do the same for a female slave.

15:18  LA: Non avertas ab eis oculos tuos, quando dimiseris eos liberos, quoniam juxta mercedem mercenarii per sex annos servivit tibi : ut benedicat tibi Dominus Deus tuus in cunctis operibus quæ agis.
     EN: Do not turn your eyes away from them when you set them free, because for six years he has served you at the worth of a hired worker's wages; then the Lord your God will bless you in all the work you do.

15:19  LA: De primogenitis, quæ nascuntur in armentis, et in ovibus tuis, quidquid est sexus masculini, sanctificabis Domino Deo tuo. Non operaberis in primogenito bovis, et non tondebis primogenita ovium.
     EN: Consecrate to the Lord your God every firstborn male born among your cattle and sheep. Do not put the firstborn of your cattle to work or shear the firstborn of your sheep.

15:20  LA: In conspectu Domini Dei tui comedes ea per annos singulos in loco quem elegerit Dominus, tu et domus tua.
     EN: Eat them before the Lord your God each year in the place the Lord chooses, you and your household.

15:21  LA: Sin autem habuerit maculam, vel claudum fuerit, vel cæcum, aut in aliqua parte deforme vel debile, non immolabitur Domino Deo tuo :
     EN: But if it has a blemish, is lame or blind, or is deformed or weak in any part, it shall not be sacrificed to the Lord your God.

15:22  LA: sed intra portas urbis tuæ comedes illud : tam mundus quam immundus similiter vescentur eis, quasi caprea et cervo.
     EN: Eat it within your city's gates; the clean and unclean alike may eat it, as gazelle or deer.

15:23  LA: Hoc solum observabis, ut sanguinem eorum non comedas, sed effundes in terram quasi aquam.
     EN: Only observe this: do not eat their blood, but pour it on the ground like water.

DEUTERONOMY 16

16:1  LA: Observa mensem novarum frugum, et verni primum temporis, ut facias Phase Domino Deo tuo : quoniam in isto mense eduxit te Dominus Deus tuus de Ægypto nocte.
     EN: Observe the month of new crops, the first month of spring, and celebrate the Passover for the Lord your God, because in that month the Lord your God brought you out of Egypt by night.

16:2  LA: Immolabisque Phase Domino Deo tuo de ovibus, et de bobus, in loco quem elegerit Dominus Deus tuus, ut habitet nomen ejus ibi.
     EN: Sacrifice the Passover to the Lord your God from sheep and cattle in the place the Lord your God chooses for His name to dwell there.

16:3  LA: Non comedes in eo panem fermentatum : septem diebus comedes absque fermento afflictionis panem, quoniam in pavore egressus es de Ægypto : ut memineris diei egressionis tuæ de Ægypto, omnibus diebus vitæ tuæ.
     EN: You shall not eat leavened bread with it. For seven days eat unleavened bread, the bread of affliction, because you came out of Egypt in dread, so that you may remember the day you came out of Egypt all the days of your life.

16:4  LA: Non apparebit fermentum in omnibus terminis tuis septem diebus, et non remanebit de carnibus ejus, quod immolatum est vespere in die primo, usque mane.
     EN: No leaven shall be seen within any of your borders for seven days, and none of the meat sacrificed on the evening of the first day shall remain until morning.

16:5  LA: Non poteris immolare Phase in qualibet urbium tuarum, quas Dominus Deus tuus daturus est tibi,
     EN: You may not sacrifice the Passover in just any of your cities that the Lord your God is going to give you,

16:6  LA: sed in loco quem elegerit Dominus Deus tuus, ut habitet nomen ejus ibi : immolabis Phase vespere ad solis occasum, quando egressus es de Ægypto.
     EN: but in the place the Lord your God chooses for His name to dwell there. Sacrifice the Passover in the evening at sunset, at the time you came out of Egypt.

16:7  LA: Et coques, et comedes in loco quem elegerit Dominus Deus tuus, maneque consurgens vades in tabernacula tua.
     EN: Cook it and eat it in the place the Lord your God chooses; in the morning, rise and return to your tents.

16:8  LA: Sex diebus comedes azyma : et in die septima, quia collecta est Domini Dei tui, non facies opus.
     EN: For six days eat unleavened bread; on the seventh day do no work, because it is an assembly of the Lord your God.

16:9  LA: Septem hebdomadas numerabis tibi ab ea die qua falcem in segetem miseris.
     EN: Count seven weeks from the day you put the sickle to the standing grain.

16:10  LA: Et celebraris diem festum hebdomadarum Domino Deo tuo, oblationem spontaneam manus tuæ, quam offeres juxta benedictionem Domini Dei tui :
     EN: Celebrate the Feast of Weeks for the Lord your God with a freewill offering from your hand, which you shall offer according to the blessing of the Lord your God.

16:11  LA: et epulaberis coram Domino Deo tuo, tu, filius tuus et filia tua, servus tuus et ancilla tua, et Levites qui est intra portas tuas, advena ac pupillus et vidua, qui morantur vobiscum : in loco quem elegerit Dominus Deus tuus, ut habitet nomen ejus ibi.
     EN: Feast before the Lord your God—you, your son and daughter, your male and female slave, the Levite within your gates, and the stranger, orphan, and widow living with you—in the place the Lord your God chooses for His name to dwell there.

16:12  LA: Et recordaberis quoniam servus fueris in Ægypto : custodiesque ac facies quæ præcepta sunt.
     EN: Remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and observe and carry out what is commanded.

16:13  LA: Solemnitatem quoque tabernaculorum celebrabis per septem dies, quando collegeris de area et torculari fruges tuas :
     EN: Also celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles for seven days when you have gathered your produce from the threshing floor and winepress.

16:14  LA: et epulaberis in festivitate tua, tu, filius tuus et filia, servus tuus et ancilla, Levites quoque et advena, pupillus et vidua qui intra portas tuas sunt.
     EN: Feast at your festival—you, your son and daughter, your male and female slave, and the Levite, stranger, orphan, and widow within your gates.

16:15  LA: Septem diebus Domino Deo tuo festa celebrabis in loco quem elegerit Dominus : benedicetque tibi Dominus Deus tuus in cunctis frugibus tuis, et in omni opere manuum tuarum, erisque in lætitia.
     EN: For seven days celebrate the feast for the Lord your God in the place the Lord chooses. The Lord your God will bless you in all your produce and all the work of your hands, and you shall be joyful.

16:16  LA: Tribus vicibus per annum apparebit omne masculinum tuum in conspectu Domini Dei tui in loco quem elegerit : in solemnitate azymorum, in solemnitate hebdomadarum, et in solemnitate tabernaculorum. Non apparebit ante Dominum vacuus :
     EN: Three times a year all your males shall appear before the Lord your God in the place He chooses: at the Feast of Unleavened Bread, the Feast of Weeks, and the Feast of Tabernacles. No one shall appear before the Lord empty-handed,

16:17  LA: sed offeret unusquisque secundum quod habuerit juxta benedictionem Domini Dei sui, quam dederit ei.
     EN: but each shall offer according to what he has, in keeping with the blessing the Lord his God has given him.

16:18  LA: Judices et magistros constitues in omnibus portis tuis, quas Dominus Deus tuus dederit tibi, per singulas tribus tuas : ut judicent populum justo judicio,
     EN: Appoint judges and officers at all your gates that the Lord your God gives you, in each of your tribes, to judge the people with just judgment,

16:19  LA: nec in alteram partem declinent. Non accipies personam, nec munera : quia munera excæcant oculos sapientum, et mutant verba justorum.
     EN: without leaning to either side. Show no partiality and take no bribes, because bribes blind the eyes of the wise and change the words of the righteous.

16:20  LA: Juste quod justum est persequeris : ut vivas, et possideas terram, quam Dominus Deus tuus dederit tibi.
     EN: Pursue what is just with justice, so that you may live and possess the land the Lord your God gives you.

16:21  LA: Non plantabis lucum, et omnem arborem juxta altare Domini Dei tui.
     EN: Do not plant a sacred grove or any tree beside the altar of the Lord your God.

16:22  LA: Nec facies tibi, neque constitues statuam : quæ odit Dominus Deus tuus.
     EN: Do not make or erect a pillar for yourself, which the Lord your God hates.

DEUTERONOMY 17

17:1  LA: Non immolabis Domino Deo tuo ovem, et bovem, in quo est macula, aut quippiam vitii : quia abominatio est Domino Deo tuo.
     EN: Do not sacrifice to the Lord your God a sheep or ox with a blemish or any defect, because it is an abomination to the Lord your God.

17:2  LA: Cum reperti fuerint apud te intra unam portarum tuarum, quas Dominus Deus tuus dabit tibi, vir aut mulier qui faciant malum in conspectu Domini Dei tui, et transgrediantur pactum illius,
     EN: If a man or woman is found among you within one of the gates the Lord your God gives you, doing evil before the Lord your God and transgressing His covenant

17:3  LA: ut vadant et servant diis alienis, et adorent eos, solem et lunam, et omnem militiam cæli, quæ non præcepi :
     EN: by going to serve foreign gods and worshiping them—the sun, moon, or any of the host of heaven—which I have not commanded,

17:4  LA: et hoc tibi fuerit nuntiatum, audiensque inquisieris diligenter et verum esse repereris, et abominatio facta est in Israël :
     EN: and this is reported to you, hear it and investigate diligently. If you find it true and this abomination has been committed in Israel,

17:5  LA: educes virum ac mulierem, qui rem sceleratissimam perpetrarunt, ad portas civitatis tuæ, et lapidibus obruentur.
     EN: bring the man or woman who committed this wicked deed to your city's gates, and they shall be stoned.

17:6  LA: In ore duorum aut trium testium peribit qui interficietur. Nemo occidatur, uno contra se dicente testimonium.
     EN: The person sentenced to death shall die on the testimony of two or three witnesses. No one shall be killed on the testimony of one witness against him.

17:7  LA: Manus testium prima interficiet eum, et manus reliqui populi extrema mittetur : ut auferas malum de medio tui.
     EN: The witnesses' hands shall be first to kill him; afterward the rest of the people shall lay their hands on him. Thus remove the evil from your midst.

17:8  LA: Si difficile et ambiguum apud te judicium esse perspexeris inter sanguinem et sanguinem, causam et causam, lepram et lepram : et judicum intra portas tuas videris verba variari : surge, et ascende ad locum, quem elegerit Dominus Deus tuus.
     EN: If you perceive a judgment to be difficult and uncertain, between one case of bloodshed and another, one lawsuit and another, or one leprous condition and another, and see that the judges within your gates disagree, rise and go up to the place the Lord your God chooses.

17:9  LA: Veniesque ad sacerdotes Levitici generis, et ad judicem qui fuerit illo tempore : quæresque ab eis, qui indicabunt tibi judicii veritatem.
     EN: Go to the priests of the line of Levi and to the judge serving at that time. Consult them, and they will declare the true judgment to you.

17:10  LA: Et facies quodcumque dixerint qui præsunt loco quem elegerit Dominus, et docuerint te
     EN: Do whatever those presiding in the place the Lord chooses tell you and teach you

17:11  LA: juxta legem ejus, sequerisque sententiam eorum, nec declinabis ad dexteram neque ad sinistram.
     EN: according to His law. Follow their decision, turning neither right nor left.

17:12  LA: Qui autem superbierit, nolens obedire sacerdotis imperio, qui eo tempore ministrat Domino Deo tuo, et decreto judicis, morietur homo ille, et auferes malum de Israël :
     EN: But the person who acts arrogantly, refusing to obey the command of the priest who ministers to the Lord your God at that time and the judge's decision, shall die. Remove the evil from Israel,

17:13  LA: cunctusque populus audiens timebit, ut nullus deinceps intumescat superbia.
     EN: and all the people will hear and fear, so that no one will swell with pride afterward.

17:14  LA: Cum ingressus fueris terram, quam Dominus Deus tuus dabit tibi, et possederis eam, habitaverisque in illa, et dixeris : Constituam super me regem, sicut habent omnes per circuitum nationes :
     EN: When you enter the land the Lord your God gives you, possess it and live in it, and say: I will appoint a king over me, as all the surrounding nations have,

17:15  LA: eum constitues, quem Dominus Deus tuus elegerit de numero fratrum tuorum. Non poteris alterius gentis hominem regem facere, qui non sit frater tuus.
     EN: appoint the one the Lord your God chooses from among your brothers. You may not make a man of another nation king, one who is not your brother.

17:16  LA: Cumque fuerit constitutus, non multiplicabit sibi equos, nec reducet populum in Ægyptum, equitatus numero sublevatus, præsertim cum Dominus præceperit vobis ut nequaquam amplius per eamdem viam revertamini.
     EN: Once appointed, he shall not multiply horses for himself or lead the people back to Egypt, relying on the size of his cavalry, especially since the Lord has commanded you never to return by that road again.

17:17  LA: Non habebit uxores plurimas, quæ alliciant animum ejus, neque argenti et auri immensa pondera.
     EN: He shall not have many wives to entice his heart, nor enormous quantities of silver and gold.

17:18  LA: Postquam autem sederit in solio regni sui, describet sibi Deuteronomium legis hujus in volumine, accipiens exemplar a sacerdotibus Leviticæ tribus,
     EN: After sitting on the throne of his kingdom, he shall write out for himself this repetition of the law in a scroll, receiving a master copy from the priests of the tribe of Levi.

17:19  LA: et habebit secum, legetque illud omnibus diebus vitæ suæ, ut discat timere Dominum Deum suum, et custodire verba et cæremonias ejus, quæ in lege præcepta sunt.
     EN: He shall keep it with him and read it all the days of his life, so that he may learn to fear the Lord his God and keep His words and rites commanded in the law.

17:20  LA: Nec elevetur cor ejus in superbiam super fratres suos, neque declinet in partem dexteram vel sinistram, ut longo tempore regnet ipse et filii ejus super Israël.
     EN: His heart shall not be lifted in pride above his brothers, nor shall he turn to the right or left, so that he and his sons may reign over Israel a long time.

DEUTERONOMY 18

18:1  LA: Non habebunt sacerdotes et Levitæ, et omnes qui de eadem tribu sunt, partem et hæreditatem cum reliquo Israël, quia sacrificia Domini, et oblationes ejus comedent,
     EN: The priests and Levites, and all who belong to that tribe, shall have no share or inheritance with the rest of Israel, because they shall eat the Lord's sacrifices and offerings.

18:2  LA: et nihil aliud accipient de possessione fratrum suorum : Dominus enim ipse est hæreditas eorum, sicut locutus est illis.
     EN: They shall receive nothing else from their brothers' possession, for the Lord Himself is their inheritance, as He told them.

18:3  LA: Hoc erit judicium sacerdotum a populo, et ab his qui offerunt victimas : sive bovem, sive ovem immolaverint, dabunt sacerdoti armum ac ventriculum :
     EN: This shall be the priests' due from the people and from those offering sacrifices: whether they sacrifice an ox or a sheep, they shall give the priest the shoulder and the stomach,

18:4  LA: primitias frumenti, vini, et olei, et lanarum partem ex ovium tonsione.
     EN: the firstfruits of grain, wine, and oil, and a share of the wool from the shearing of the sheep.

18:5  LA: Ipsum enim elegit Dominus Deus tuus de cunctis tribubus tuis, ut stet, et ministret nomini Domini, ipse, et filii ejus in sempiternum.
     EN: For the Lord your God chose him from all your tribes to stand and minister in the Lord's name, he and his sons forever.

18:6  LA: Si exierit Levites ex una urbium tuarum ex omni Israël in qua habitat, et voluerit venire, desiderans locum quem elegerit Dominus,
     EN: If a Levite leaves one of your cities anywhere in Israel where he lives and wishes to come, longing for the place the Lord chooses,

18:7  LA: ministrabit in nomine Domini Dei sui, sicut omnes fratres ejus Levitæ, qui stabunt eo tempore coram Domino.
     EN: he shall minister in the name of the Lord his God like all his brother Levites who stand before the Lord at that time.

18:8  LA: Partem ciborum eamdem accipiet, quam et ceteri : excepto eo, quod in urbe sua ex paterna ei successione debetur.
     EN: He shall receive the same portion of food as the others, apart from what is due to him in his own city through his paternal inheritance.

18:9  LA: Quando ingressus fueris terram, quam Dominus Deus tuus dabit tibi, cave ne imitari velis abominationes illarum gentium.
     EN: When you enter the land the Lord your God gives you, take care not to desire to imitate the abominations of those nations.

18:10  LA: Nec inveniatur in te qui lustret filium suum, aut filiam, ducens per ignem : aut qui ariolos sciscitetur, et observet somnia atque auguria, nec sit maleficus,
     EN: Let no one among you purify his son or daughter by leading them through fire, or consult soothsayers, observe dreams and omens, or practice sorcery,

18:11  LA: nec incantator, nec qui pythones consulat, nec divinos, aut quærat a mortuis veritatem.
     EN: or cast spells, consult mediums or diviners, or seek the truth from the dead.

18:12  LA: Omnia enim hæc abominatur Dominus, et propter istiusmodi scelera delebit eos in introitu tuo.
     EN: For the Lord abhors all these things, and because of such crimes He will destroy them when you enter.

18:13  LA: Perfectus eris, et absque macula cum Domino Deo tuo.
     EN: You shall be perfect and without blemish before the Lord your God.

18:14  LA: Gentes istæ, quarum possidebis terram, augures et divinos audiunt : tu autem a Domino Deo tuo aliter institutus es.
     EN: These nations whose land you will possess listen to augurs and diviners; but you have been instructed otherwise by the Lord your God.

18:15  LA: Prophetam de gente tua et de fratribus tuis, sicut me, suscitabit tibi Dominus Deus tuus : ipsum audies,
     EN: The Lord your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from your nation and from your brothers. Listen to him,

18:16  LA: ut petisti a Domino Deo tuo in Horeb, quando concio congregata est, atque dixisti : Ultra non audiam vocem Domini Dei mei, et ignem hunc maximum amplius non videbo, ne moriar.
     EN: as you asked of the Lord your God at Horeb, when the assembly gathered and you said: Let me no longer hear the voice of the Lord my God or see this great fire anymore, lest I die.

18:17  LA: Et ait Dominus mihi : Bene omnia sunt locuti.
     EN: The Lord said to me: Everything they have said is good.

18:18  LA: Prophetam suscitabo eis de medio fratrum suorum similem tui : et ponam verba mea in ore ejus, loqueturque ad eos omnia quæ præcepero illi.
     EN: I will raise up for them a prophet like you from among their brothers. I will put My words in his mouth, and he will tell them everything I command him.

18:19  LA: Qui autem verba ejus, quæ loquetur in nomine meo, audire noluerit, ego ultor existam.
     EN: If anyone refuses to hear his words which he speaks in My name, I Myself will exact retribution.

18:20  LA: Propheta autem qui arrogantia depravatus voluerit loqui in nomine meo, quæ ego non præcepi illi ut diceret, aut ex nomine alienorum deorum, interficietur.
     EN: But a prophet corrupted by arrogance who presumes to speak in My name what I did not command him to say, or speaks in the name of foreign gods, shall be killed.

18:21  LA: Quod si tacita cogitatione responderis : Quomodo possum intelligere verbum, quod Dominus non est locutus ?
     EN: If you reply in your silent thoughts: How can I recognize a word the Lord has not spoken?

18:22  LA: hoc habebis signum : quod in nomine Domini propheta ille prædixerit, et non evenerit : hoc Dominus non est locutus, sed per tumorem animi sui propheta confinxit : et idcirco non timebis eum.
     EN: this shall be your sign: if that prophet predicts something in the Lord's name and it does not happen, the Lord did not speak it; the prophet invented it in his pride. Therefore do not fear him.

DEUTERONOMY 19

19:1  LA: Cum disperdiderit Dominus Deus tuus gentes, quarum tibi traditurus est terram, et possederis eam, habitaverisque in urbibus ejus et in ædibus :
     EN: When the Lord your God destroys the nations whose land He is going to hand over to you, and you possess it and live in its cities and houses,

19:2  LA: tres civitates separabis tibi in medio terræ, quam Dominus Deus tuus dabit tibi in possessionem,
     EN: set apart three cities in the midst of the land the Lord your God gives you as a possession.

19:3  LA: sternens diligenter viam : et in tres æqualiter partes totam terræ tuæ provinciam divides : ut habeat e vicino qui propter homicidium profugus est, quo possit evadere.
     EN: Prepare the road carefully and divide the whole territory of your land into three equal parts, so that anyone fleeing because of a killing may have a nearby place to escape to.

19:4  LA: Hæc erit lex homicidæ fugientis, cujus vita servanda est : qui percusserit proximum suum nesciens, et qui heri et nudiustertius nullum contra eum odium habuisse comprobatur :
     EN: This is the law for the fleeing killer whose life is to be preserved: one who struck his neighbor unknowingly and is shown to have had no hatred against him previously,

19:5  LA: sed abiisse cum eo simpliciter in silvam ad ligna cædenda, et in succisione lignorum securis fugerit manu, ferrumque lapsum de manubrio amicum ejus percusserit, et occiderit : hic ad unam supradictarum urbium confugiet, et vivet :
     EN: but went with him innocently into the forest to cut wood, and as he cut, the axe slipped from his hand and the iron head flew from its handle, striking and killing his companion. He shall flee to one of the cities mentioned above and live,

19:6  LA: ne forsitan proximus ejus, cujus effusus est sanguis, dolore stimulatus, persequatur, et apprehendat eum si longior via fuerit, et percutiat animam ejus, qui non est reus mortis : quia nullum contra eum, qui occisus est, odium prius habuisse monstratur.
     EN: lest the relative of the man whose blood was shed, driven by grief, pursue him, catch him if the road is too long, and take his life, though he is not deserving of death, since he is shown to have had no previous hatred for the man killed.

19:7  LA: Idcirco præcipio tibi, ut tres civitates æqualis inter se spatii dividas.
     EN: Therefore I command you to set apart three cities at equal distances from one another.

19:8  LA: Cum autem dilataverit Dominus Deus tuus terminos tuos, sicut juravit patribus tuis, et dederit tibi cunctam terram, quam eis pollicitus est
     EN: When the Lord your God enlarges your borders as He swore to your fathers, and gives you all the land He promised them,

19:9  LA: (si tamen custodieris mandata ejus, et feceris, quæ hodie præcipio tibi, ut diligas Dominum Deum tuum, et ambules in viis ejus omni tempore), addes tibi tres alias civitates, et supradictarum trium urbium numerum duplicabis :
     EN: provided you keep His commandments and do what I command you today, loving the Lord your God and walking in His ways at all times, add three other cities and double the number of the three cities already mentioned,

19:10  LA: ut non effundatur sanguis innoxius in medio terræ, quam Dominus Deus tuus dabit tibi possidendam, ne sis sanguinis reus.
     EN: so that innocent blood may not be shed in the midst of the land the Lord your God gives you to possess, and you may not be guilty of bloodshed.

19:11  LA: Si quis autem, odio habens proximum suum, insidiatus fuerit vitæ ejus, surgensque percusserit illum, et mortuus fuerit, fugeritque ad unam de supradictis urbibus,
     EN: But if someone hates his neighbor, lies in wait for his life, rises and strikes him so that he dies, and flees to one of those cities,

19:12  LA: mittent seniores civitatis illius, et arripient eum de loco effugii, tradentque in manu proximi, cujus sanguis effusus est, et morietur.
     EN: the elders of his city shall send and seize him from his refuge, hand him over to the relative of the man whose blood was shed, and he shall die.

19:13  LA: Non miseraberis ejus, et auferes innoxium sanguinem de Israël, ut bene sit tibi.
     EN: Do not pity him. Remove the guilt of innocent blood from Israel, so that it may go well with you.

19:14  LA: Non assumes, et transferes terminos proximi tui, quos fixerunt priores in possessione tua, quam Dominus Deus tuus dabit tibi in terra quam acceperis possidendam.
     EN: Do not take up and move your neighbor's boundary markers that earlier generations established in the possession the Lord your God will give you in the land you receive to possess.

19:15  LA: Non stabit testis unus contra aliquem, quidquid illud peccati, et facinoris fuerit : sed in ore duorum aut trium testium stabit omne verbum.
     EN: One witness shall not suffice against anyone, whatever the sin or crime may be. Every charge shall stand on the testimony of two or three witnesses.

19:16  LA: Si steterit testis mendax contra hominem, accusans eum prævaricationis,
     EN: If a lying witness rises against a man, accusing him of wrongdoing,

19:17  LA: stabunt ambo, quorum causa est, ante Dominum in conspectu sacerdotum et judicum qui fuerint in diebus illis.
     EN: both parties to the case shall stand before the Lord, in the presence of the priests and judges serving in those days.

19:18  LA: Cumque diligentissime perscrutantes, invenerint falsum testem dixisse contra fratrem suum mendacium,
     EN: After investigating most carefully, if they find that the false witness has spoken a lie against his brother,

19:19  LA: reddent ei sicut fratri suo facere cogitavit, et auferes malum de medio tui :
     EN: they shall do to him as he intended to do to his brother. Remove the evil from your midst,

19:20  LA: ut audientes ceteri timorem habeant, et nequaquam talia audeant facere.
     EN: so that the others may hear and fear and never dare to do such things.

19:21  LA: Non misereberis ejus, sed animam pro anima, oculum pro oculo, dentem pro dente, manum pro manu, pedem pro pede exiges.
     EN: Do not pity him, but exact life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.

DEUTERONOMY 20

20:1  LA: Si exieris ad bellum contra hostes tuos, et videris equitatus et currus, et majorem quam tu habeas adversarii exercitus multitudinem, non timebis eos : quia Dominus Deus tuus tecum est, qui eduxit te de terra Ægypti.
     EN: If you go out to war against your enemies and see cavalry, chariots, and an opposing army more numerous than yours, do not fear them, because the Lord your God, who brought you out of Egypt, is with you.

20:2  LA: Appropinquante autem jam prælio, stabit sacerdos ante aciem, et sic loquetur ad populum :
     EN: When battle approaches, the priest shall stand before the battle line and say to the people:

20:3  LA: Audi, Israël : vos hodie contra inimicos vestros pugnam committitis : non pertimescat cor vestrum, nolite metuere, nolite cedere, nec formidetis eos :
     EN: Hear, Israel: today you engage in battle against your enemies. Do not let your hearts be terrified. Do not be afraid, do not retreat, and do not dread them,

20:4  LA: quia Dominus Deus vester in medio vestri est, et pro vobis contra adversarios dimicabit, ut eruat vos de periculo.
     EN: because the Lord your God is in your midst and will fight for you against your adversaries, to rescue you from danger.

20:5  LA: Duces quoque per singulas turmas audiente exercitu proclamabunt : Quis est homo qui ædificavit domum novam, et non dedicavit eam ? vadat, et revertatur in domum suam, ne forte moriatur in bello, et alius dedicet eam.
     EN: The officers shall also proclaim to each division in the army's hearing: Is there a man who has built a new house and has not dedicated it? Let him go and return home, lest he die in battle and another dedicate it.

20:6  LA: Quis est homo qui plantavit vineam, et necdum fecit eam esse communem, de qua vesci omnibus liceat ? vadat, et revertatur in domum suam, ne forte moriatur in bello, et alius homo ejus fungatur officio.
     EN: Is there a man who has planted a vineyard and not yet made its fruit available for everyone to eat? Let him go and return home, lest he die in battle and another man perform that duty.

20:7  LA: Quis est homo, qui despondit uxorem, et non accepit eam ? vadat, et revertatur in domum suam, ne forte moriatur in bello, et alius homo accipiat eam.
     EN: Is there a man who is betrothed to a wife but has not taken her in marriage? Let him go and return home, lest he die in battle and another man take her.

20:8  LA: His dictis addent reliqua, et loquentur ad populum : Quis est homo formidolosus, et corde pavido ? vadat, et revertatur in domum suam, ne pavere faciat corda fratrum suorum, sicut ipse timore perterritus est.
     EN: After saying this, they shall add these words to the people: Is there a man who is fearful and fainthearted? Let him go and return home, lest he make his brothers' hearts afraid as he himself is terrified by fear.

20:9  LA: Cumque siluerint duces exercitus, et finem loquendi fecerint, unusquisque suos ad bellandum cuneos præparabit.
     EN: When the army's officers fall silent and finish speaking, each shall prepare his ranks for battle.

20:10  LA: Siquando accesseris ad expugnandam civitatem, offeres ei primum pacem.
     EN: Whenever you approach a city to attack it, first offer it peace.

20:11  LA: Si receperit, et aperuerit tibi portas, cunctus populus, qui in ea est, salvabitur, et serviet tibi sub tributo.
     EN: If it accepts and opens its gates to you, all the people in it shall be spared and serve you under tribute.

20:12  LA: Sin autem fœdus inire noluerit, et cœperit contra te bellum, oppugnabis eam.
     EN: But if it refuses to make a covenant and begins war against you, besiege it.

20:13  LA: Cumque tradiderit Dominus Deus tuus illam in manu tua, percuties omne quod in ea generis masculini est, in ore gladii,
     EN: When the Lord your God hands it over to you, strike every male in it with the edge of the sword,

20:14  LA: absque mulieribus et infantibus, jumentis et ceteris quæ in civitate sunt. Omnem prædam exercitui divides, et comedes de spoliis hostium tuorum, quæ Dominus Deus tuus dederit tibi.
     EN: but spare the women, infants, livestock, and other things in the city. Divide all the plunder among the army, and eat from the spoils of your enemies that the Lord your God gives you.

20:15  LA: Sic facies cunctis civitatibus, quæ a te procul valde sunt, et non sunt de his urbibus, quas in possessionem accepturus es.
     EN: Do this to all cities very far from you, which are not among the cities you will receive as a possession.

20:16  LA: De his autem civitatibus, quæ dabuntur tibi, nullum omnino permittes vivere :
     EN: But in the cities that will be given to you, allow no one at all to live.

20:17  LA: sed interficies in ore gladii, Hethæum videlicet, et Amorrhæum, et Chananæum, Pherezæum, et Hevæum, et Jebusæum, sicut præcepit tibi Dominus Deus tuus :
     EN: Kill them with the edge of the sword—the Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites—as the Lord your God commanded you,

20:18  LA: ne forte doceant vos facere cunctas abominationes, quas ipsi operati sunt diis suis, et peccetis in Dominum Deum vestrum.
     EN: lest they teach you to practice all the abominations they have performed for their gods, and you sin against the Lord your God.

20:19  LA: Quando obsederis civitatem multo tempore, et munitionibus circumdederis ut expugnes eam, non succides arbores, de quibus vesci potest, nec securibus per circuitum debes vastare regionem : quoniam lignum est, et non homo, nec potest bellantium contra te augere numerum.
     EN: When you besiege a city for a long time and surround it with siegeworks to attack it, do not cut down trees that provide food or lay waste the surrounding region with axes. For a tree is not a human being and cannot increase the number fighting against you.

20:20  LA: Si qua autem ligna non sunt pomifera, sed agrestia, et in ceteros apta usus, succide, et instrue machinas, donec capias civitatem, quæ contra te dimicat.
     EN: But if trees bear no fruit and are wild trees fit for other uses, cut them down and build siege engines until you capture the city fighting against you.

DEUTERONOMY 21

21:1  LA: Quando inventum fuerit in terra, quam Dominus Deus tuus daturus est tibi, hominis cadaver occisi, et ignorabitur cædis reus,
     EN: If the body of a slain person is found in the land the Lord your God is going to give you and the killer is unknown,

21:2  LA: egredientur majores natu, et judices tui, et metientur a loco cadaveris singularum per circuitum spatia civitatum :
     EN: your elders and judges shall go out and measure the distance from the body to each of the surrounding cities.

21:3  LA: et quam viciniorem ceteris esse perspexerint, seniores civitatis illius tollent vitulam de armento, quæ non traxit jugum, nec terram scidit vomere,
     EN: The elders of the city they find nearest shall take a heifer from the herd that has neither pulled a yoke nor broken the soil with a plow,

21:4  LA: et ducent eam ad vallem asperam atque saxosam, quæ numquam arata est, nec sementem recepit : et cædent in ea cervices vitulæ :
     EN: and lead it to a rough, rocky valley that has never been plowed or sown. There they shall cut the heifer's neck.

21:5  LA: accedentque sacerdotes filii Levi, quos elegerit Dominus Deus tuus ut ministrent ei, et benedicant in nomine ejus, et ad verbum eorum omne negotium, et quidquid mundum, vel immundum est, judicetur.
     EN: The priests, the sons of Levi, shall approach, whom the Lord your God has chosen to minister to Him and bless in His name; by their word every dispute and everything clean or unclean shall be judged.

21:6  LA: Et venient majores natu civitatis illius ad interfectum, lavabuntque manus suas super vitulam, quæ in valle percussa est,
     EN: The elders of that city shall come to the slain person and wash their hands over the heifer struck down in the valley,

21:7  LA: et dicent : Manus nostræ non effuderunt sanguinem hunc, nec oculi viderunt :
     EN: saying: Our hands have not shed this blood, nor have our eyes seen it.

21:8  LA: propitius esto populo tuo Israël, quem redemisti, Domine, et ne reputes sanguinem innocentem in medio populi tui Israël. Et auferetur ab eis reatus sanguinis :
     EN: Be gracious to Your people Israel, whom You redeemed, Lord, and do not charge innocent blood to Your people Israel. Then the guilt of bloodshed shall be removed from them.

21:9  LA: tu autem alienus eris ab innocentis cruore, qui fusus est, cum feceris quod præcepit Dominus.
     EN: When you have done what the Lord commanded, you shall be free of the innocent blood that was shed.

21:10  LA: Si egressus fueris ad pugnam contra inimicos tuos, et tradiderit eos Dominus Deus tuus in manu tua, captivosque duxeris,
     EN: If you go out to fight your enemies and the Lord your God hands them over to you, and you take captives,

21:11  LA: et videris in numero captivorum mulierem pulchram, et adamaveris eam, voluerisque habere uxorem,
     EN: and among the captives you see a beautiful woman, fall in love with her, and wish to have her as your wife,

21:12  LA: introduces eam in domum tuam : quæ radet cæsariem, et circumcidet ungues,
     EN: bring her into your house. She shall shave her hair and trim her nails,

21:13  LA: et deponet vestem, in qua capta est : sedensque in domo tua, flebit patrem et matrem suam uno mense : et postea intrabis ad eam, dormiesque cum illa, et erit uxor tua.
     EN: and remove the clothing in which she was captured. She shall stay in your house and mourn her father and mother for one month. Afterward you may go in to her and sleep with her, and she shall be your wife.

21:14  LA: Si autem postea non sederit animo tuo, dimittes eam liberam, nec vendere poteris pecunia, nec opprimere per potentiam : quia humiliasti eam.
     EN: But if afterward she does not please you, let her go free. You may not sell her for money or oppress her by force, because you have violated her.

21:15  LA: Si habuerit homo uxores duas, unam dilectam, et alteram odiosam, genuerintque ex eo liberos, et fuerit filius odiosæ primogenitus,
     EN: If a man has two wives, one loved and the other hated, and both bear him children, and the hated wife's son is the firstborn,

21:16  LA: volueritque substantiam inter filios suos dividere, non poterit filium dilectæ facere primogenitum, et præferre filio odiosæ :
     EN: when he wishes to divide his property among his sons, he may not make the loved wife's son the firstborn and prefer him to the hated wife's son.

21:17  LA: sed filium odiosæ agnoscet primogenitum, dabitque ei de his quæ habuerit cuncta duplicia : iste est enim principium liberorum ejus, et huic debentur primogenita.
     EN: He shall acknowledge the hated wife's son as firstborn and give him a double share of everything he has, for he is the beginning of his children, and the firstborn's rights belong to him.

21:18  LA: Si genuerit homo filium contumacem et protervum, qui non audiat patris aut matris imperium, et coërcitus obedire contempserit :
     EN: If a man has a stubborn and insolent son who will not listen to his father's or mother's command and, though disciplined, scorns obedience,

21:19  LA: apprehendent eum, et ducent ad seniores civitatis illius, et ad portam judicii,
     EN: they shall seize him and bring him to the elders of his city, to the gate of judgment,

21:20  LA: dicentque ad eos : Filius noster iste protervus et contumax est : monita nostra audire contemnit, comessationibus vacat, et luxuriæ atque conviviis :
     EN: and say to them: This son of ours is insolent and stubborn. He scorns listening to our warnings and devotes himself to revelry, excess, and feasting.

21:21  LA: lapidibus eum obruet populus civitatis, et morietur, ut auferatis malum de medio vestri, et universus Israël audiens pertimescat.
     EN: The city's people shall stone him, and he shall die. Thus you shall remove the evil from your midst, and all Israel will hear and be afraid.

21:22  LA: Quando peccaverit homo quod morte plectendum est, et adjudicatus morti appensus fuerit in patibulo :
     EN: If a man commits a sin punishable by death and, after being condemned to death, is hung on a gibbet,

21:23  LA: non permanebit cadaver ejus in ligno, sed in eadem die sepelietur : quia maledictus a Deo est qui pendet in ligno : et nequaquam contaminabis terram tuam, quam Dominus Deus tuus dederit tibi in possessionem.
     EN: his body shall not remain on the wood, but shall be buried the same day, because whoever hangs on wood is cursed by God. Do not defile the land the Lord your God gives you as a possession.

DEUTERONOMY 22

22:1  LA: Non videbis bovem fratris tui, aut ovem errantem, et præteribis : sed reduces fratri tuo,
     EN: If you see your brother's ox or sheep straying, do not pass by; bring it back to your brother.

22:2  LA: etiamsi non est propinquus frater tuus, nec nosti eum : duces in domum tuam, et erunt apud te quamdiu quærat ea frater tuus, et recipiat.
     EN: Even if your brother is not nearby or you do not know him, bring it to your house, and it shall remain with you until your brother searches for it and receives it back.

22:3  LA: Similiter facies de asino, et de vestimento, et de omni re fratris tui, quæ perierit : si inveneris eam, ne negligas quasi alienam.
     EN: Do the same for his donkey, clothing, or anything your brother has lost. If you find it, do not neglect it as though it were someone else's concern.

22:4  LA: Si videris asinum fratris tui aut bovem cecidisse in via, non despicies, sed sublevabis cum eo.
     EN: If you see your brother's donkey or ox fallen on the road, do not ignore it, but help him lift it up.

22:5  LA: Non induetur mulier veste virili, nec vir utetur veste feminea : abominabilis enim apud Deum est qui facit hæc.
     EN: A woman shall not wear men's clothing, nor shall a man use women's clothing, for whoever does these things is abhorrent to God.

22:6  LA: Si ambulans per viam, in arbore vel in terra nidum avis inveneris, et matrem pullis vel ovis desuper incubantem : non tenebis eam cum filiis,
     EN: If, while walking along the road, you find a bird's nest in a tree or on the ground, with the mother sitting on the chicks or eggs, do not take her with her young.

22:7  LA: sed abire patieris, captos tenens filios : ut bene sit tibi, et longo vivas tempore.
     EN: Let her go, keeping the young you have taken, so that it may go well with you and you may live a long time.

22:8  LA: Cum ædificaveris domum novam, facies murum tecti per circuitum : ne effundatur sanguis in domo tua, et sis reus labente alio, et in præceps ruente.
     EN: When you build a new house, make a wall around the roof, so that blood may not be shed in your house and you become guilty if someone slips and falls headlong.

22:9  LA: Non seres vineam tuam altero semine : ne et sementis quam sevisti, et quæ nascuntur ex vinea, pariter sanctificentur.
     EN: Do not sow your vineyard with a second kind of seed, lest both the seed you sow and the vineyard's produce become consecrated together.

22:10  LA: Non arabis in bove simul et asino.
     EN: Do not plow with an ox and a donkey together.

22:11  LA: Non indueris vestimento, quod ex lana linoque contextum est.
     EN: Do not wear a garment woven of wool and linen together.

22:12  LA: Funiculos in fimbriis facies per quatuor angulos pallii tui, quo operieris.
     EN: Make cords as fringes on the four corners of the cloak with which you cover yourself.

22:13  LA: Si duxerit vir uxorem, et postea odio habuerit eam,
     EN: If a man marries a wife and afterward hates her,

22:14  LA: quæsieritque occasiones quibus dimittat eam, objiciens ei nomen pessimum, et dixerit : Uxorem hanc accepi, et ingressus ad eam non inveni virginem :
     EN: and seeks grounds to dismiss her by bringing a vile accusation against her, saying: I married this wife, but when I went in to her, I did not find her a virgin,

22:15  LA: tollent eam pater et mater ejus, et ferent secum signa virginitatis ejus ad seniores urbis qui in porta sunt :
     EN: her father and mother shall take her and bring the signs of her virginity with them to the city's elders at the gate.

22:16  LA: et dicet pater : Filiam meam dedi huic uxorem : quam quia odit,
     EN: Her father shall say: I gave my daughter to this man as a wife. Because he hates her,

22:17  LA: imponit ei nomen pessimum, ut dicat : Non inveni filiam tuam virginem : et ecce hæc sunt signa virginitatis filiæ meæ. Expandent vestimentum coram senioribus civitatis :
     EN: he brings a vile accusation against her, saying: I did not find your daughter a virgin. But here are the signs of my daughter's virginity. They shall spread out the cloth before the city's elders.

22:18  LA: apprehendentque senes urbis illius virum, et verberabunt illum,
     EN: The elders of that city shall seize the man and beat him,

22:19  LA: condemnantes insuper centum siclis argenti, quos dabit patri puellæ, quoniam diffamavit nomen pessimum super virginem Israël : habebitque eam uxorem, et non poterit dimittere eam omnibus diebus vitæ suæ.
     EN: and additionally fine him one hundred shekels of silver, which he shall give to the young woman's father, because he has spread a vile accusation against a virgin of Israel. He shall keep her as his wife and may not dismiss her all the days of his life.

22:20  LA: Quod si verum est quod objicit, et non est in puella inventa virginitas,
     EN: But if his accusation is true and virginity is not found in the young woman,

22:21  LA: ejicient eam extra fores domus patris sui, et lapidibus obruent viri civitatis illius, et morietur : quoniam fecit nefas in Israël, ut fornicaretur in domo patris sui : et auferes malum de medio tui.
     EN: they shall bring her outside the door of her father's house, and the men of that city shall stone her to death, because she committed an outrage in Israel by engaging in sexual immorality in her father's house. Remove the evil from your midst.

22:22  LA: Si dormierit vir cum uxore alterius, uterque morietur, id est, adulter et adultera : et auferes malum de Israël.
     EN: If a man sleeps with another man's wife, both shall die, the adulterer and the adulteress. Remove the evil from Israel.

22:23  LA: Si puellam virginem desponderit vir, et invenerit eam aliquis in civitate, et concubuerit cum ea,
     EN: If a man is betrothed to a young virgin and someone finds her in the city and lies with her,

22:24  LA: educes utrumque ad portam civitatis illius, et lapidibus obruentur : puella, quia non clamavit, cum esset in civitate : vir, quia humiliavit uxorem proximi sui : et auferes malum de medio tui.
     EN: bring both to that city's gate, and they shall be stoned: the young woman because she did not cry out though she was in the city, and the man because he violated his neighbor's wife. Remove the evil from your midst.

22:25  LA: Sin autem in agro repererit vir puellam, quæ desponsata est, et apprehendens concubuerit cum ea, ipse morietur solus :
     EN: But if the man finds the betrothed young woman in the countryside and seizes her and lies with her, he alone shall die.

22:26  LA: puella nihil patietur, nec est rea mortis : quoniam sicut latro consurgit contra fratrem suum, et occidit animam ejus, ita et puella perpessa est.
     EN: The young woman shall suffer nothing and is not deserving of death, because what she suffered is like a robber rising against his brother and taking his life.

22:27  LA: Sola erat in agro : clamavit, et nullus affuit qui liberaret eam.
     EN: She was alone in the countryside; she cried out, and no one was there to rescue her.

22:28  LA: Si invenerit vir puellam virginem, quæ non habet sponsum, et apprehendens concubuerit cum illa, et res ad judicium venerit :
     EN: If a man finds a young virgin who is not betrothed, seizes her and lies with her, and the matter comes to judgment,

22:29  LA: dabit qui dormivit cum ea, patri puellæ quinquaginta siclos argenti, et habebit eam uxorem, quia humiliavit illam : non poterit dimittere eam cunctis diebus vitæ suæ.
     EN: the man who slept with her shall give her father fifty shekels of silver and have her as his wife, because he violated her. He may not dismiss her all the days of his life.

DEUTERONOMY 23

23:1 (Vg 22:30)  LA: Non accipiet homo uxorem patris sui, nec revelabit operimentum ejus.
     EN: A man shall not take his father's wife or uncover his covering.

23:2 (Vg 23:1)  LA: Non intravit eunuchus, attritis vel amputatis testiculis et abscisso veretro, ecclesiam Domini.
     EN: A eunuch whose testicles are crushed or cut off and whose penis is severed shall not enter the assembly of the Lord.

23:3 (Vg 23:2)  LA: Non ingredietur mamzer, hoc est, de scorto natus, in ecclesiam Domini, usque ad decimam generationem.
     EN: A mamzer, that is, one born of a prostitute, shall not enter the assembly of the Lord, even to the tenth generation.

23:4 (Vg 23:3)  LA: Ammonites et Moabites etiam post decimam generationem non intrabunt ecclesiam Domini in æternum :
     EN: An Ammonite or Moabite shall never enter the assembly of the Lord, even after the tenth generation,

23:5 (Vg 23:4)  LA: quia noluerunt vobis occurrere cum pane et aqua in via quando egressi estis de Ægypto : et quia conduxerunt contra te Balaam filium Beor de Mesopotamia Syriæ, ut malediceret tibi :
     EN: because they would not meet you with bread and water on the road when you came out of Egypt, and because they hired Balaam son of Beor from Mesopotamia in Syria against you to curse you.

23:6 (Vg 23:5)  LA: et noluit Dominus Deus tuus audire Balaam, vertitque maledictionem ejus in benedictionem tuam, eo quod diligeret te.
     EN: But the Lord your God would not listen to Balaam and turned his curse into a blessing for you, because He loved you.

23:7 (Vg 23:6)  LA: Non facies cum eis pacem, nec quæras eis bona cunctis diebus vitæ tuæ in sempiternum.
     EN: Do not make peace with them or seek their welfare all the days of your life, forever.

23:8 (Vg 23:7)  LA: Non abominaberis Idumæum, quia frater tuus est : nec Ægyptium, quia advena fuisti in terra ejus.
     EN: Do not abhor an Edomite, because he is your brother, or an Egyptian, because you were a stranger in his land.

23:9 (Vg 23:8)  LA: Qui nati fuerint ex eis, tertia generatione intrabunt in ecclesiam Domini.
     EN: Their descendants in the third generation may enter the assembly of the Lord.

23:10 (Vg 23:9)  LA: Quando egressus fueris adversus hostes tuos in pugnam, custodies te ab omni re mala.
     EN: When you go out to battle against your enemies, keep yourself from every evil thing.

23:11 (Vg 23:10)  LA: Si fuerit inter vos homo, qui nocturno pollutus sit somnio, egredietur extra castra,
     EN: If there is a man among you who has been defiled by a nocturnal emission, he shall go outside the camp

23:12 (Vg 23:11)  LA: et non revertetur, priusquam ad vesperam lavetur aqua : et post solis occasum regredietur in castra.
     EN: and not return until he washes with water toward evening. After sunset he may return to the camp.

23:13 (Vg 23:12)  LA: Habebis locum extra castra, ad quem egrediaris ad requisita naturæ,
     EN: Have a place outside the camp where you go to relieve yourself,

23:14 (Vg 23:13)  LA: gerens paxillum in balteo : cumque sederis, fodies per circuitum, et egesta humo operies
     EN: carrying a digging peg on your belt. When you sit down, dig around and cover your excrement with earth

23:15 (Vg 23:14)  LA: quo revelatus es : Dominus enim Deus tuus ambulat in medio castrorum, ut eruat te, et tradat tibi inimicos tuos : et sint castra tua sancta, et nihil in eis appareat fœditatis, ne derelinquat te.
     EN: where you have relieved yourself. For the Lord your God walks in the midst of the camp to rescue you and hand your enemies over to you. Let your camp be holy, and let nothing filthy appear in it, lest He abandon you.

23:16 (Vg 23:15)  LA: Non trades servum domino suo, qui ad te confugerit.
     EN: Do not hand over to his master a slave who has fled to you.

23:17 (Vg 23:16)  LA: Habitabit tecum in loco, qui ei placuerit, et in una urbium tuarum requiescet : ne contristes eum.
     EN: He shall live with you in the place he chooses and settle in one of your cities. Do not distress him.

23:18 (Vg 23:17)  LA: Non erit meretrix de filiabus Israël, nec scortator de filiis Israël.
     EN: There shall be no prostitute among the daughters of Israel or fornicator among the sons of Israel.

23:19 (Vg 23:18)  LA: Non offeres mercedem prostibuli, nec pretium canis in domo Domini Dei tui, quidquid illud est quod voveris : quia abominatio est utrumque apud Dominum Deum tuum.
     EN: Do not bring a prostitute's wages or a dog's price into the house of the Lord your God for any vow you have made, because both are an abomination before the Lord your God.

23:20 (Vg 23:19)  LA: Non fœnerabis fratri tuo ad usuram pecuniam, nec fruges, nec quamlibet aliam rem :
     EN: Do not lend your brother money, crops, or anything else at interest,

23:21 (Vg 23:20)  LA: sed alieno. Fratri autem tuo absque usura id quo indiget, commodabis : ut benedicat tibi Dominus Deus tuus in omni opere tuo in terra, ad quam ingredieris possidendam.
     EN: though you may lend to a foreigner. Lend your brother what he needs without interest, so that the Lord your God may bless all your work in the land you are entering to possess.

23:22 (Vg 23:21)  LA: Cum votum voveris Domino Deo tuo, non tardabis reddere : quia requiret illud Dominus Deus tuus, et si moratus fueris, reputabitur tibi in peccatum.
     EN: When you make a vow to the Lord your God, do not delay fulfilling it, because the Lord your God will require it. If you delay, it will be counted as sin for you.

23:23 (Vg 23:22)  LA: Si nolueris polliceri, absque peccato eris.
     EN: If you choose not to make a promise, you will be without sin.

23:24 (Vg 23:23)  LA: Quod autem semel egressum est de labiis tuis, observabis, et facies sicut promisisti Domino Deo tuo, et propria voluntate et ore tuo locutus es.
     EN: But keep what has once passed your lips, and do as you promised the Lord your God, what you spoke willingly with your own mouth.

23:25  LA: Si intraveris in segetem amici tui, franges spicas, et manu conteres : falce autem non metes.
     EN: If you enter your friend's standing grain, pluck the ears and rub them in your hand, but do not harvest with a sickle.

23:26 (Vg 23:24)  LA: Ingressus vineam proximi tui, comede uvas, quantum tibi placuerit : foras autem ne efferas tecum.
     EN: When you enter your neighbor's vineyard, eat as many grapes as you please, but do not carry any away with you.

DEUTERONOMY 24

24:1  LA: Si acceperit homo uxorem, et habuerit eam, et non invenerit gratiam ante oculos ejus propter aliquam fœditatem : scribet libellum repudii, et dabit in manu illius, et dimittet eam de domo sua.
     EN: If a man takes a wife and has her, and she does not find favor in his eyes because of some indecency, he shall write a certificate of divorce, put it in her hand, and send her from his house.

24:2  LA: Cumque egressa alterum maritum duxerit,
     EN: When she has left and married another husband,

24:3  LA: et ille quoque oderit eam, dederitque ei libellum repudii, et dimiserit de domo sua, vel certe mortuus fuerit :
     EN: and he too hates her, gives her a certificate of divorce, and sends her from his house, or if he dies,

24:4  LA: non poterit prior maritus recipere eam in uxorem : quia polluta est, et abominabilis facta est coram Domino : ne peccare facias terram tuam, quam Dominus Deus tuus tradiderit tibi possidendam.
     EN: the first husband may not receive her again as his wife, because she has been defiled and become abhorrent before the Lord. Do not cause the land the Lord your God gives you to possess to incur sin.

24:5  LA: Cum acceperit homo nuper uxorem, non procedet ad bellum, nec ei quippiam necessitatis injungetur publicæ, sed vacabit absque culpa domi suæ, ut uno anno lætetur cum uxore sua.
     EN: When a man has recently married a wife, he shall not go out to war or be assigned any public duty. He shall be free at home without blame, to rejoice with his wife for one year.

24:6  LA: Non accipies loco pignoris inferiorem, et superiorem molam : quia animam suam opposuit tibi.
     EN: Do not take a lower or upper millstone as a pledge, for he has pledged his very life to you.

24:7  LA: Si deprehensus fuerit homo sollicitans fratrem suum de filiis Israël, et vendito eo acceperit pretium, interficietur, et auferes malum de medio tui.
     EN: If a man is caught enticing away his brother from among the children of Israel and receiving money from selling him, he shall be killed. Remove the evil from your midst.

24:8  LA: Observa diligenter ne incurras plagam lepræ, sed facies quæcumque docuerint te sacerdotes Levitici generis, juxta id quod præcepi eis, et imple sollicite.
     EN: Take great care not to incur the affliction of leprosy, but do whatever the priests of the line of Levi teach you according to what I commanded them, and carry it out carefully.

24:9  LA: Mementote quæ fecerit Dominus Deus vester Mariæ in via cum egrederemini de Ægypto.
     EN: Remember what the Lord your God did to Miriam on the road when you came out of Egypt.

24:10  LA: Cum repetes a proximo tuo rem aliquam, quam debet tibi, non ingredieris domum ejus ut pignus auferas :
     EN: When you demand from your neighbor anything he owes you, do not enter his house to take a pledge.

24:11  LA: sed stabis foris, et ille tibi proferet quod habuerit.
     EN: Stand outside, and he shall bring out to you what he has.

24:12  LA: Sin autem pauper est, non pernoctabit apud te pignus,
     EN: But if he is poor, the pledge shall not stay with you overnight.

24:13  LA: sed statim reddes ei ante solis occasum : ut dormiens in vestimento suo, benedicat tibi, et habeas justitiam coram Domino Deo tuo.
     EN: Return it promptly before sunset, so that he may sleep in his garment and bless you, and you may have righteousness before the Lord your God.

24:14  LA: Non negabis mercedem indigentis, et pauperis fratris tui, sive advenæ, qui tecum moratur in terra, et intra portas tuas est :
     EN: Do not withhold the wages of your needy and poor brother, or of the stranger living with you in your land and within your gates.

24:15  LA: sed eadem die reddes ei pretium laboris sui ante solis occasum, quia pauper est, et ex eo sustentat animam suam : ne clamet contra te ad Dominum, et reputetur tibi in peccatum.
     EN: Pay him for his labor that same day before sunset, because he is poor and sustains his life from it, lest he cry to the Lord against you and it be counted as sin for you.

24:16  LA: Non occidentur patres pro filiis, nec filii pro patribus, sed unusquisque pro peccato suo morietur.
     EN: Fathers shall not be killed for their children, nor children for their fathers. Each shall die for his own sin.

24:17  LA: Non pervertes judicium advenæ et pupilli, nec auferes pignoris loco viduæ vestimentum.
     EN: Do not pervert justice for the stranger or orphan, or take a widow's garment as a pledge.

24:18  LA: Memento quod servieris in Ægypto, et eruerit te Dominus Deus tuus inde. Idcirco præcipio tibi ut facias hanc rem.
     EN: Remember that you served as a slave in Egypt and the Lord your God rescued you from there. Therefore I command you to do this.

24:19  LA: Quando messueris segetem in agro tuo, et oblitus manipulum reliqueris, non reverteris, ut tollas illum : sed advenam, et pupillum, et viduam auferre patieris, ut benedicat tibi Dominus Deus tuus in omni opere manuum tuarum.
     EN: When you harvest the grain in your field and forget a sheaf, leaving it behind, do not return to take it. Let the stranger, orphan, and widow take it, so that the Lord your God may bless all the work of your hands.

DEUTERONOMY 32

     EN: Hear, heavens, what I speak; let the earth hear the words of my mouth.

32:3  LA: Quia nomen Domini invocabo : date magnificentiam Deo nostro.]
     EN: For I will invoke the Lord's name; ascribe greatness to our God.

32:4  LA: [Dei perfecta sunt opera, et omnes viæ ejus judicia : Deus fidelis, et absque ulla iniquitate, justus et rectus.
     EN: God's works are perfect, and all His ways are justice: a faithful God, without any iniquity, just and upright.

     EN: But the Lord's portion is His people; Jacob is His allotted inheritance.

     EN: The Lord alone was his guide, and no foreign god was with him.

     EN: They provoked Him with foreign gods and stirred Him to anger with abominations.

     EN: They provoked Me with what was no god and angered Me with their vanities. I will provoke them with what is no people and anger them with a foolish nation.

     EN: If only they were wise and understood, and foresaw their end!

     EN: Are these things not stored up with Me and sealed in My treasuries?

     EN: the fruits brought forth by sun and moon,

33:15  LA: de vertice antiquorum montium, de pomis collium æternorum :
     EN: the summit of the ancient mountains, the fruits of the everlasting hills,

33:16  LA: et de frugibus terræ, et de plenitudine ejus. Benedictio illius qui apparuit in rubo, veniat super caput Joseph, et super verticem nazaræi inter fratres suos.
     EN: the earth's produce and its fullness. May the blessing of Him who appeared in the bush come upon Joseph's head, upon the crown of the one consecrated among his brothers.

33:17  LA: Quasi primogeniti tauri pulchritudo ejus, cornua rhinocerotis cornua illius : in ipsis ventilabit gentes usque ad terminos terræ. Hæ sunt multitudines Ephraim : et hæc millia Manasse.]
     EN: His beauty is like that of a firstborn bull; his horns are the horns of a rhinoceros. With them he will toss the nations to the ends of the earth. These are the multitudes of Ephraim, and these the thousands of Manasseh.

33:18  LA: Et Zabulon ait : [Lætare, Zabulon, in exitu tuo, et Issachar in tabernaculis tuis.
     EN: Of Zebulun he said: Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out, and Issachar, in your tents.

33:19  LA: Populos vocabunt ad montem : ibi immolabunt victimas justitiæ. Qui inundationem maris quasi lac sugent, et thesauros absconditos arenarum.]
     EN: They will call peoples to the mountain; there they will sacrifice righteous offerings. They will suck the sea's abundance like milk, and the hidden treasures of the sands.

33:20  LA: Et Gad ait : [Benedictus in latitudine Gad : quasi leo requievit, cepitque brachium et verticem.
     EN: Of Gad he said: Blessed is Gad in his broad territory. He rested like a lion and seized the arm and the crown of the head.

33:21  LA: Et vidit principatum suum, quod in parte sua doctor esset repositus : qui fuit cum principibus populi, et fecit justitias Domini, et judicium suum cum Israël.]
     EN: He saw his preeminence, because in his portion a teacher was laid away, who was with the people's leaders and carried out the Lord's righteous ordinances and His judgment with Israel.

33:22  LA: Dan quoque ait : [Dan catulus leonis, fluet largiter de Basan.]
     EN: Of Dan he also said: Dan is a lion's cub; he will stream forth abundantly from Bashan.

33:23  LA: Et Nephthali dixit : [Nephthali abundantia perfruetur, et plenus erit benedictionibus Domini : mare et meridiem possidebit.]
     EN: Of Naphtali he said: Naphtali will enjoy abundance and be filled with the Lord's blessings. He will possess the sea and the south.

33:24  LA: Aser quoque ait : [Benedictus in filiis Aser, sit placens fratribus suis, et tingat in oleo pedem suum :
     EN: Of Asher he also said: Blessed among sons is Asher. May he please his brothers and dip his foot in oil.

33:25  LA: ferrum et æs calceamentum ejus. Sicut dies juventutis tuæ, ita et senectus tua.
     EN: Iron and bronze shall be his footwear. As the days of your youth, so shall your old age be.

33:26  LA: Non est deus alius ut Deus rectissimi, ascensor cæli, auxiliator tuus. Magnificentia ejus discurrunt nubes,
     EN: There is no other god like the God of the most upright, who rides the heavens, your helper. By His majesty the clouds race on;

33:27  LA: habitaculum ejus sursum, et subter brachia sempiterna ejiciet a facie tua inimicum, dicetque : Conterere.
     EN: His dwelling is above, and beneath are everlasting arms. He will drive the enemy from before you and say: Be crushed.

33:28  LA: Habitabit Israël confidenter, et solus. Oculus Jacob in terra frumenti et vini, cælique caligabunt rore.
     EN: Israel shall dwell securely and alone. Jacob's eye shall look upon a land of grain and wine, and the heavens shall grow heavy with dew.

33:29  LA: Beatus es tu, Israël : quis similis tui, popule, qui salvaris in Domino ? Scutum auxilii tui, et gladius gloriæ tuæ : negabunt te inimici tui, et tu eorum colla calcabis.]
     EN: Blessed are you, Israel! Who is like you, a people saved in the Lord? He is the shield of your help and the sword of your glory. Your enemies will deny you, and you will tread upon their necks.

DEUTERONOMY 34

34:1  LA: Ascendit ergo Moyses de campestribus Moab super montem Nebo, in verticem Phasga contra Jericho : ostenditque ei Dominus omnem terram Galaad usque Dan,
     EN: Then Moses went up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the summit of Pisgah opposite Jericho. The Lord showed him all the land of Gilead as far as Dan,

34:2  LA: et universum Nephthali, terramque Ephraim et Manasse, et omnem terram Juda usque ad mare novissimum,
     EN: all Naphtali, the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, and all the land of Judah as far as the farthest sea,

34:3  LA: et australem partem, et latitudinem campi Jericho civitatis palmarum usque Segor.
     EN: the southern region and the broad plain of Jericho, the city of palms, as far as Zoar.

34:4  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad eum : Hæc est terra, pro qua juravi Abraham, Isaac, et Jacob, dicens : Semini tuo dabo eam. Vidisti eam oculis tuis, et non transibis ad illam.
     EN: The Lord said to him: This is the land I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying: I will give it to your offspring. You have seen it with your eyes, but you will not cross over into it.

34:5  LA: Mortuusque est ibi Moyses servus Domini, in terra Moab, jubente Domino :
     EN: So Moses, the Lord's servant, died there in the land of Moab at the Lord's command.

34:6  LA: et sepelivit eum in valle terræ Moab contra Phogor : et non cognovit homo sepulchrum ejus usque in præsentem diem.
     EN: He buried him in the valley in the land of Moab opposite Peor, and no one has known his grave to this day.

34:7  LA: Moyses centum et viginti annorum erat quando mortuus est : non caligavit oculus ejus, nec dentes illius moti sunt.
     EN: Moses was one hundred twenty years old when he died. His eye had not grown dim, nor had his teeth become loose.

34:8  LA: Fleveruntque eum filii Israël in campestribus Moab triginta diebus : et completi sunt dies planctus lugentium Moysen.
     EN: The children of Israel wept for him on the plains of Moab for thirty days, and the days of lamentation for those mourning Moses were completed.

34:9  LA: Josue vero filius Nun repletus est spiritu sapientiæ, quia Moyses posuit super eum manus suas. Et obedierunt ei filii Israël, feceruntque sicut præcepit Dominus Moysi.
     EN: Joshua son of Nun was filled with the spirit of wisdom because Moses had laid his hands on him. The children of Israel obeyed him and did as the Lord had commanded Moses.

34:10  LA: Et non surrexit ultra propheta in Israël sicut Moyses, quem nosset Dominus facie ad faciem,
     EN: No prophet has arisen again in Israel like Moses, whom the Lord knew face to face,

34:11  LA: in omnibus signis atque portentis, quæ misit per eum, ut faceret in terra Ægypti Pharaoni, et omnibus servis ejus, universæque terræ illius,
     EN: in all the signs and wonders the Lord sent him to perform in the land of Egypt against Pharaoh, all his servants, and his entire land,

34:12  LA: et cunctam manum robustam, magnaque mirabilia, quæ fecit Moyses coram universo Israël.
     EN: and in all the mighty power and great wonders Moses performed before all Israel.
