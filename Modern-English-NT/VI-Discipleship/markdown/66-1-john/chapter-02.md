## Text

**2:1** My little children, I am writing these things to you so that you may not sin. Yet even if anyone sins, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous.

**2:2** And He Himself is the propitiation for our sins, yet not for ours only, but also for those of the entire world.

**2:3** And by this we know that we have come to know Him, if we observe His commandments.

**2:4** The one who says that he knows Him and does not keep His commandments is a liar, and the truth is not in him.

**2:5** But whoever keeps His word, truly in him the love of God is perfected; and by this we know that we are in Him.

**2:6** The one who says that he remains in Him ought himself also to walk just as He walked.

**2:7** Beloved, I am not writing a new commandment to you, but an old commandment that you have had from the beginning. The old commandment is the word that you have heard.

**2:8** Again, I am writing a new commandment to you, which is true both in Him and in you, because the darkness has passed away and the true light is already shining.

**2:9** The one who says that he is in the light and hates his brother is in darkness even until now.

**2:10** The one who loves his brother remains in the light, and there is no stumbling block in him.

**2:11** But the one who hates his brother is in darkness, and walks in darkness, and does not know where he is going, because the darkness has blinded his eyes.

**2:12** I am writing to you, little children, because your sins are forgiven for the sake of His name.

**2:13** I am writing to you, fathers, because you have come to know Him who is from the beginning. I am writing to you, youths, because you have overcome the evil one.

**2:14** I am writing to you, young children, because you have come to know the Father. I am writing to you, young men, because you are strong, and the word of God remains in you, and you have overcome the evil one.

**2:15** Do not love the world, nor the things that are in the world. If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father is not in him.

**2:16** For everything that is in the world is the craving of the flesh, and the craving of the eyes, and the pride of life, which is not from the Father, but is from the world.

**2:17** And the world is passing away, and its craving; yet the one who does the will of God remains forever.

**2:18** Little children, it is the last hour; and just as you heard that antichrist is coming, even now many antichrists have arisen; from this we know that it is the last hour.

**2:19** They went out from us, but they were not of us; for if they had been of us, they would certainly have remained with us; rather, so that they might be clearly revealed, that they are not all of us.

**2:20** But you have an anointing from the Holy One, and you know all things.

**2:21** I have not written to you as to those ignorant of the truth, but as to those who know it, and that no lie is from the truth.

**2:22** Who is the liar except the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ? This is the antichrist, the one who denies the Father and the Son.

**2:23** Everyone who denies the Son does not have the Father either; the one who confesses the Son has the Father also.

**2:24** As for you, let what you heard from the beginning continue to remain in you. If what you heard from the beginning continues to remain in you, you also will remain in the Son and in the Father.

**2:25** And this is the promise that He Himself promised us: eternal life.

**2:26** I have written these things to you concerning those who would deceive you.

**2:27** And as for you, let the anointing that you received from Him remain in you. And you have no need for anyone to teach you; rather, as His anointing teaches you concerning all things, it is both true and not a lie. And just as it has taught you, remain in Him.

**2:28** And now, little children, remain in Him, so that when He appears we may have confidence and not be put to shame by Him at His coming.

**2:29** If you know that He is righteous, know also that everyone who does righteousness has been born of Him.

## Translator Notes

- **2:8** — The Vulgate uses the perfect tense transierunt ('has passed away'), contrasting with the Greek present paragetai ('is passing away') and the Vulgate's own present transit in 2:17.
- **2:14** — The Clementine Vulgate maintains the present tense Scribo ('I am writing') across 2:12-14 and omits the second address to 'fathers' found in the Greek text.
- **2:20** — The Vulgate reads nostis omnia ('you know all things'), reflecting the textual variant panta rather than pantes ('you all know').
- **2:27** — The Vulgate renders the ambiguous Greek verb menete unambiguously as an imperative, manete ('remain').
