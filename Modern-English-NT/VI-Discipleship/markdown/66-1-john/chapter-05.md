## Text

**5:1** Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Christ has been born of God. And everyone who loves Him who brought forth also loves the one who has been born of Him.

**5:2** By this we know that we love the children of God: when we love God and do His commandments.

**5:3** For this is the love of God: that we observe His commandments, and His commandments are not heavy.

**5:4** For everything that has been born of God conquers the world; and this is the victory that conquers the world: our faith.

**5:5** Who is the one who conquers the world, except the one who believes that Jesus is the Son of God?

**5:6** This is He who came through water and blood, Jesus Christ: not in water only, but in water and blood. And it is the Spirit who testifies that Christ is the truth.

**5:7** For there are three who give testimony in heaven: the Father, the Word, and the Holy Spirit; and these three are one.

**5:8** And there are three who give testimony on earth: the spirit, and the water, and the blood; and these three are one.

**5:9** If we accept the testimony of men, the testimony of God is greater, because this is the testimony of God, which is greater: that He has testified concerning His Son.

**5:10** The one who believes in the Son of God has the testimony of God in himself. The one who does not believe the Son makes Him a liar, because he does not believe in the testimony that God has testified concerning His Son.

**5:11** And this is the testimony: that God has given us eternal life, and this life is in His Son.

**5:12** The one who has the Son has life; the one who does not have the Son does not have life.

**5:13** I write these things to you so that you may know that you have eternal life, you who believe in the name of the Son of God.

**5:14** And this is the confidence that we have toward Him: that whatever we ask according to His will, He hears us.

**5:15** And we know that He hears us, whatever we ask; we know that we have the petitions that we request from Him.

**5:16** Let the one who knows that his brother is committing a sin not leading to death ask, and life will be given to him, to the one sinning not leading to death. There is a sin leading to death; I do not say that anyone should pray for that.

**5:17** All iniquity is sin, and there is a sin leading to death.

**5:18** We know that everyone who has been born of God does not sin, but the generation of God preserves him, and the evil one does not touch him.

**5:19** We know that we are from God, and the whole world lies in the evil one.

**5:20** And we know that the Son of God has come and has given us understanding so that we may know the true God and may be in His true Son. This is the true God and eternal life.

**5:21** Little children, guard yourselves from idols. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **5:6** — The Vulgate reads 'quoniam Christus est veritas' ('that Christ is the truth'), whereas the Greek manuscripts read 'that the Spirit is the truth.'
- **5:7** — Verses 7-8 contain the Johannine Comma ('in heaven: the Father, the Word, and the Holy Spirit; and these three are one. And there are three who give testimony on earth'), a hallmark reading of the Clementine Vulgate absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts.
- **5:10** — The Vulgate reads 'non credit Filio' ('does not believe the Son') and adds 'Dei' ('of God') after the first 'testimony,' whereas the Greek text reads 'does not believe God' and omits 'of God.'
- **5:17** — The Clementine Vulgate omits 'non' ('not'), reading 'et est peccatum ad mortem' ('and there is a sin leading to death'), whereas the Greek text reads 'and there is sin not leading to death.'
- **5:18** — The Vulgate renders the Greek participle 'the One born of God' abstractly as 'generatio Dei' ('the generation of God,' i.e., being born of God) preserving the believer.
- **5:20** — The Vulgate explicitly supplies 'Deum' ('the true God') and reads the subjunctive 'simus in vero Filio ejus' ('may be in His true Son'), without the closing name 'Jesus Christ' found in Greek manuscripts.
