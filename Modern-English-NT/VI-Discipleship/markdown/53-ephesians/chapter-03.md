## Text

**3:1** For this reason, I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus on behalf of you Gentiles,

**3:2** if indeed you have heard of the administration of the grace of God, which was given to me among you,

**3:3** that according to revelation the mystery was made known to me, just as I wrote above in brief,

**3:4** as you are able, when reading, to understand my insight into the mystery of Christ,

**3:5** which in other generations was not known to the sons of men, as it has now been revealed to His holy apostles and prophets in the Spirit,

**3:6** that the Gentiles are co-heirs, and of the same body, and joint partakers of His promise in Christ Jesus through the Gospel,

**3:7** of which I became a minister according to the gift of the grace of God, which was given to me according to the working of His power.

**3:8** To me, the least of all the saints, this grace was given, to proclaim among the Gentiles the good news of the unsearchable riches of Christ,

**3:9** and to enlighten all as to what is the administration of the mystery hidden from the ages in God, who created all things,

**3:10** so that the manifold wisdom of God might become known through the church to the principalities and authorities in the heavenly places,

**3:11** according to the predetermination of the ages, which He carried out in Christ Jesus our Lord,

**3:12** in whom we have boldness and access in confidence through faith in Him.

**3:13** Therefore I ask that you do not lose heart in my tribulations for you, which is your glory.

**3:14** For this reason I bend my knees to the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ,

**3:15** from whom every fatherhood in the heavens and on earth is named,

**3:16** that He may grant you, according to the riches of His glory, to be strengthened with power through His Spirit in the inner man,

**3:17** for Christ to dwell through faith in your hearts, rooted and founded in love,

**3:18** so that you may be able to comprehend with all the saints what is the width and length and height and depth,

**3:19** and to know also the love of Christ, surpassing knowledge, so that you may be filled to all the fullness of God.

**3:20** Now to Him who is able to do all things far more abundantly than we ask or understand, according to the power that works in us,

**3:21** to Him be glory in the church and in Christ Jesus throughout all generations of the age of ages. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — The Vulgate uses sacramentum ("sacred secret" or "mystery") here and in 1:9 and 3:9, alternating with mysterium in 3:4 to denote the same hidden divine plan.
- **3:11** — Latin praefinitionem saeculorum literally means "predetermination of the ages" (often rendered "eternal purpose").
- **3:14** — The Vulgate includes "of our Lord Jesus Christ" (Domini nostri Jesu Christi) after "the Father," a reading absent from some Greek manuscripts.
- **3:15** — Latin paternitas literally means "fatherhood" (preserving the wordplay with Patrem, "Father," in verse 14), whereas Greek patria denotes "family" or "lineage."
- **3:20** — Latin omnia facere superabundanter quam makes "all things" (omnia) the direct object of "do," and intelligimus specifically means "understand" (where Greek has "think").
