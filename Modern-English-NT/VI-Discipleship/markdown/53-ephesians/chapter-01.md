## Text

**1:1** Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ through the will of God, to all the saints who are at Ephesus, and to the faithful in Christ Jesus.

**1:2** Grace to you, and peace from God our Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ.

**1:3** Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who blessed us in every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places in Christ,

**1:4** just as He chose us in Him before the founding of the world, that we might be holy and unblemished in His sight in love.

**1:5** He predestined us for adoption as sons through Jesus Christ to Himself, according to the purpose of His will,

**1:6** to the praise of the glory of His grace, in which He favored us in His beloved Son.

**1:7** In Him we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of His grace,

**1:8** which abounded exceedingly in us in all wisdom and insight,

**1:9** so that He might make known to us the mystery of His will, according to His good pleasure, which He purposed in Him,

**1:10** in the administration of the fullness of times, to restore all things in Christ, the things that are in the heavens and the things that are on earth, in Him;

**1:11** in whom we also were called by lot, having been predestined according to the purpose of the One who works all things according to the counsel of His will,

**1:12** so that we might be to the praise of His glory, we who hoped beforehand in Christ;

**1:13** in whom you also, when you had heard the word of truth, the Gospel of your salvation, in whom also, believing, you were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise,

**1:14** who is the pledge of our inheritance, for the redemption of the acquisition, to the praise of His glory.

**1:15** For this reason I also, hearing of your faith that is in the Lord Jesus and love toward all the saints,

**1:16** do not cease giving thanks for you, making remembrance of you in my prayers,

**1:17** that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may grant you a spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of Him,

**1:18** with the eyes of your heart enlightened, so that you may know what is the hope of His calling, and what are the riches of the glory of His inheritance in the saints,

**1:19** and what is the surpassing greatness of His power toward us who believe, according to the working of the might of His power,

**1:20** which He worked in Christ, raising Him from the dead and setting Him at His right hand in the heavenly places,

**1:21** above every principality and authority and power and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this age, but also in the future one.

**1:22** And He subjected all things under His feet, and gave Him as head over the whole church,

**1:23** which is His body, and the fullness of the One who is filled all in all.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — In the Clementine Vulgate, "in love" (in caritate) closes verse 4 rather than introducing verse 5, which begins a new sentence (Qui praedestinavit).
- **1:6** — The Vulgate adds Filio ("in His beloved Son"), whereas Greek reads simply "in the Beloved."
- **1:10** — Latin instaurare literally means "to restore" or "renew," a distinctive Vulgate rendering where Greek uses a verb meaning "to sum up" or "bring together under one head."
- **1:22** — Latin supra omnem ecclesiam means "over the whole church" (with feminine omnem modifying ecclesiam), whereas Greek reads "head over all things to the church."
- **1:23** — Latin adimpletur is passive in form ("is filled"); the Vulgate also includes "and" (et) before "the fullness."
