## Text

**2:1** And you, when you were dead in your offenses and sins,

**2:2** in which you once walked according to the age of this world, according to the prince of the authority of this air, of the spirit that now works in the sons of unbelief,

**2:3** among whom we all also once lived in the desires of our flesh, doing the will of the flesh and of the thoughts, and were by nature sons of wrath, just as the rest also;

**2:4** but God, who is rich in mercy, because of His exceeding love with which He loved us,

**2:5** even when we were dead in sins, made us alive together in Christ (by whose grace you have been saved),

**2:6** and raised us up together, and made us sit together in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus,

**2:7** so that He might show in the coming ages the abundant riches of His grace, in goodness toward us in Christ Jesus.

**2:8** For by grace you have been saved through faith, and this is not from yourselves, for it is God's gift,

**2:9** not from works, so that no one may boast.

**2:10** For we are His handiwork, created in Christ Jesus in good works, which God prepared so that we might walk in them.

**2:11** Therefore remember that once you, Gentiles in the flesh, who are called uncircumcision by that which is called circumcision in the flesh, made by hand,

**2:12** that at that time you were without Christ, alienated from the citizenship of Israel and strangers to the covenants, having no hope of the promise, and without God in this world.

**2:13** But now in Christ Jesus, you who once were far off have been made near in the blood of Christ.

**2:14** For He Himself is our peace, who made both one, and breaking down the dividing wall of partition, the hostilities in His flesh,

**2:15** nullifying the law of commandments by decrees, so that He might create the two in Himself into one new man, making peace,

**2:16** and might reconcile both in one body to God through the cross, putting to death the hostilities in Himself.

**2:17** And coming, He proclaimed the good news of peace to you who were far off, and peace to those who were near.

**2:18** For through Him both of us have access in one Spirit to the Father.

**2:19** So then you are no longer strangers and sojourners, but you are citizens with the saints and members of God's household,

**2:20** built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, with Christ Jesus Himself as the chief cornerstone,

**2:21** in whom the entire building, assembled together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord,

**2:22** in whom you also are being built together into a dwelling place of God in the Spirit.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The Vulgate adds hujus ("this air") and renders the final phrase filios diffidentiae, literally "sons of unbelief" or "distrust" (where Greek has "disobedience").
- **2:5** — The Vulgate reads in Christo ("in Christ," rather than "with Christ") and adds the relative pronoun cujus ("by whose grace you have been saved") in the parenthetical clause.
- **2:8** — The Vulgate includes a second enim ("for") in Dei enim donum est ("for it is God's gift").
- **2:12** — Clementine Vulgate punctuation attaches promissionis to spem ("having no hope of the promise") rather than to testamentorum ("covenants"), and adds hoc ("in this world").
- **2:15** — Latin decretis is an unadorned ablative ("by decrees"), which Latin interpreters have understood either instrumentally (nullifying the law through Christ's decrees) or descriptively (the law consisting in decrees).
