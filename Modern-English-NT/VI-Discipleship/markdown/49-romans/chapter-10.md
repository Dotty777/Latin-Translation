## Text

**10:1** Brothers, my heart's desire indeed, and my supplication to God, is made on their behalf for salvation.

**10:2** For I bear witness concerning them that they have zeal for God, yet not according to knowledge.

**10:3** For, not knowing God's righteousness and seeking to set up their own, they have not submitted to God's righteousness.

**10:4** For the end of the law is Christ, for righteousness to everyone who believes.

**10:5** For Moses wrote that the person who does the righteousness that is from the law will live by it.

**10:6** But the righteousness that is from faith speaks in this way: "Do not say in your heart, 'Who will ascend into heaven?' (that is, to bring Christ down),

**10:7** or, 'Who will descend into the abyss?'" (that is, to call Christ back from the dead).

**10:8** But what does Scripture say? "The word is near, in your mouth and in your heart." That is the word of faith that we preach.

**10:9** Because if you confess the Lord Jesus with your mouth, and believe in your heart that God has raised Him from the dead, you will be saved.

**10:10** For with the heart one believes for righteousness, and with the mouth confession is made for salvation.

**10:11** For Scripture says, "Everyone who believes in Him will not be brought to shame."

**10:12** For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek; for the same Lord of all is rich toward all who call upon Him.

**10:13** For everyone who calls upon the name of the Lord will be saved.

**10:14** How then will they call upon Him in whom they have not believed? Or how will they believe Him whom they have not heard? And how will they hear without someone preaching?

**10:15** And how will they preach unless they are sent? Just as it is written: "How beautiful are the feet of those who proclaim good news of peace, of those who proclaim good news of good things!"

**10:16** Yet not all obey the gospel. For Isaiah says, "Lord, who has believed our report?"

**10:17** Therefore faith is from hearing, and hearing is through the word of Christ.

**10:18** But I say: Have they not heard? Yes indeed: "Into all the earth their sound has gone out, and to the ends of the world their words."

**10:19** But I say: Did Israel not know? First Moses says, "I will lead you to jealousy by what is not a nation; by a foolish nation I will drive you to anger."

**10:20** And Isaiah is bold and says, "I was found by those who were not seeking Me; I appeared openly to those who did not ask for Me."

**10:21** But to Israel he says, "All day long I have stretched out My hands to an unbelieving and contradicting people."

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — Latin 'finis' (like Greek 'telos') encompasses both "goal/fulfillment" and "termination."
- **10:8** — The Vulgate explicitly supplies 'Scriptura' ("Scripture"), where the Greek reads simply "what does it say?", and has 'Prope est verbum' ("The word is near") without "you."
- **10:15** — The Vulgate retains the fuller reading 'evangelizantium pacem, evangelizantium bona' ("who proclaim good news of peace, of those who proclaim good news of good things"), whereas critical Greek editions omit the clause about peace.
- **10:16** — Latin 'auditus' ("report" here, "hearing" in 10:17) forms a direct verbal link between Isaiah's question and Paul's conclusion in the next verse.
- **10:21** — The Vulgate reads 'non credentem' ("unbelieving") where the Greek text reads 'apeithounta' ("disobedient").
