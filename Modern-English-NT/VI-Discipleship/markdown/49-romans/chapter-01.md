## Text

**1:1** Paul, a servant of Jesus Christ, a called apostle, set apart for the Gospel of God,

**1:2** which He had promised beforehand through His prophets in the holy Scriptures

**1:3** concerning His Son, who was made for Him from the seed of David according to the flesh,

**1:4** who was predestined as the Son of God in power according to the Spirit of sanctification from the resurrection of the dead, Jesus Christ our Lord,

**1:5** through whom we have received grace and apostleship for obedience to the faith among all the nations for the sake of His name,

**1:6** among whom you also are the called of Jesus Christ:

**1:7** to all who are in Rome, beloved by God, called saints. Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**1:8** First, I give thanks to my God through Jesus Christ for all of you, because your faith is proclaimed throughout the entire world.

**1:9** For God is my witness, whom I serve in my spirit in the Gospel of His Son, that unceasingly I make mention of you

**1:10** always in my prayers, imploring that somehow at long last I may have a prosperous journey in the will of God to come to you.

**1:11** For I long to see you, so that I may impart to you some spiritual grace to strengthen you,

**1:12** that is, to be comforted together among you through the faith that is mutual, both yours and mine.

**1:13** Now I do not want you to be unaware, brothers, that I have often planned to come to you (and have been prevented until now), so that I may have some fruit among you also, just as among the rest of the nations.

**1:14** To Greeks and to barbarians, to the wise and to the foolish, I am a debtor;

**1:15** so, as far as lies in me, I am ready to proclaim the Gospel to you also who are in Rome.

**1:16** For I am not ashamed of the Gospel. For it is God's power for salvation to every believer, to the Jew first and to the Greek.

**1:17** For the righteousness of God is revealed in it from faith to faith, just as it is written: "But the righteous one lives by faith."

**1:18** For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of those men who hold back the truth of God in unrighteousness,

**1:19** because what is known of God is evident in them. For God has made it evident to them.

**1:20** For His invisible realities, from the creation of the world, are clearly perceived, being understood through the things that have been made, His everlasting power also and His divinity, so that they are without excuse.

**1:21** Because although they had known God, they did not glorify Him as God or give thanks, but they became empty in their thoughts, and their foolish heart was darkened.

**1:22** For claiming themselves to be wise, they became fools.

**1:23** And they changed the glory of the incorruptible God into the likeness of an image of corruptible man, and of birds, of four-footed creatures, and of reptiles.

**1:24** For this reason God handed them over to the desires of their heart, to impurity, so that they dishonor their own bodies in themselves,

**1:25** who traded the truth of God for a lie, and revered and served the creature rather than the Creator, who is blessed throughout the ages. Amen.

**1:26** Therefore God handed them over to passions of disgrace; for their females changed the natural use into that use which is contrary to nature.

**1:27** Likewise also the males, leaving the natural use of the female, burned in their desires toward one another, males committing indecency with males, and receiving in themselves the recompense for their error that was due.

**1:28** And just as they did not approve of having God in their knowledge, God handed them over to a reprobate mind, so that they do the things that are not fitting,

**1:29** being filled with all iniquity, malice, fornication, greed, wickedness; full of envy, murder, contention, deceit, spitefulness; whisperers,

**1:30** slanderers, hateful to God, insolent, proud, haughty, inventors of evil things, disobedient to parents,

**1:31** foolish, disorderly, without affection, without covenant, without mercy.

**1:32** Although they had known the righteousness of God, they did not understand that those who practice such things are worthy of death, and not only those who do them, but also those who consent to those who do them.

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — The Clementine Vulgate includes "ei" ("for Him"), specifying that the Son was made from David's seed for God; this dative is absent from the Greek text.
- **1:4** — The Vulgate renders the Greek "horisthentos" ("declared" or "designated") as "praedestinatus est" ("was predestined"), a notable Latin reading reflected throughout Western exegesis.
- **1:17** — The Vulgate renders the quotation from Habakkuk 2:4 in the present tense ("vivit", "lives") rather than the Greek future tense ("will live").
- **1:30** — Latin "Deo odibiles" uses a passive adjective with the dative ("hateful to God"), whereas the Greek "theostygeis" can also be taken actively ("haters of God").
- **1:32** — The Clementine Vulgate adds "non intellexerunt" ("they did not understand") and links both those who do such things and those who consent to them as worthy of death, differing from the Greek clause structure.
