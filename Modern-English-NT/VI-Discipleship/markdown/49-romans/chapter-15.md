## Text

**15:1** Now we who are stronger ought to bear the weaknesses of the weak, and not to please ourselves.

**15:2** Let each one of you please his neighbor for good, toward edification.

**15:3** For indeed Christ did not please Himself, but just as it is written: "The reproaches of those who reproach You fell upon Me."

**15:4** For whatever things were written were written for our teaching, so that through patience and the consolation of the Scriptures we may have hope.

**15:5** Now may the God of patience and comfort grant you to be of the same mind toward one another according to Jesus Christ,

**15:6** so that, of one mind and with one mouth, you may honor the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**15:7** For this reason, welcome one another, just as Christ also welcomed you, to the honor of God.

**15:8** For I say that Christ Jesus was a minister of the circumcision because of the truth of God, to confirm the promises of the fathers,

**15:9** and that the Gentiles honor God on account of mercy, just as it is written: "For this reason I will praise You among the Gentiles, Lord, and I will sing to Your name."

**15:10** And again it says: "Rejoice, Gentiles, with His people."

**15:11** And again: "Praise the Lord, all you Gentiles, and magnify Him, all you peoples."

**15:12** And again Isaiah says: "There will be the root of Jesse, and He who will rise up to rule the Gentiles; in Him the Gentiles will hope."

**15:13** Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that you may abound in hope and the power of the Holy Spirit.

**15:14** Now I myself also am certain about you, my brothers, that you yourselves also are full of love, filled with all knowledge, so that you are able to admonish one another.

**15:15** However, I have written to you rather boldly in part, brothers, as reminding you again, because of the grace that was given to me by God,

**15:16** that I may be a minister of Christ Jesus among the Gentiles, sanctifying the gospel of God, so that the offering of the Gentiles may become acceptable and sanctified in the Holy Spirit.

**15:17** Therefore I have glory in Christ Jesus toward God.

**15:18** For I do not dare to speak anything of those things which Christ does not accomplish through me for the obedience of the Gentiles, by word and deeds,

**15:19** in the power of signs and wonders, in the power of the Holy Spirit, so that from Jerusalem and all around as far as Illyricum I have fulfilled the gospel of Christ.

**15:20** And in this way I have preached this gospel, not where Christ was named, so that I would not build upon another's foundation,

**15:21** but just as it is written: "Those to whom it has not been announced concerning Him will see, and those who have not heard will understand."

**15:22** For this reason also I was greatly hindered from coming to you, and I have been prevented until now.

**15:23** But now, having no further place in these regions, and having a longing to come to you already for many preceding years,

**15:24** when I begin to set out for Spain, I hope that in passing through I may see you, and be escorted there by you, if first I have enjoyed your company in part.

**15:25** Now, therefore, I will set out for Jerusalem to minister to the saints.

**15:26** For Macedonia and Achaia have approved making some contribution for the poor among the saints who are in Jerusalem.

**15:27** For it pleased them, and they are their debtors. For if the Gentiles have been made sharers of their spiritual things, they ought also to minister to them in material things.

**15:28** Therefore, when I have completed this and consigned this fruit to them, I will set out for Spain by way of you.

**15:29** Now I know that when I come to you, I will come in the abundance of the blessing of the gospel of Christ.

**15:30** Therefore I implore you, brothers, through our Lord Jesus Christ and through the love of the Holy Spirit, that you help me in your prayers for me to God,

**15:31** that I may be delivered from the unbelievers who are in Judea, and that the offering of my service may become acceptable to the saints in Jerusalem,

**15:32** so that I may come to you in joy through the will of God and be refreshed with you.

**15:33** Now may the God of peace be with all of you. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — The Clementine Vulgate reads 'vestrum' ('of you') rather than the Greek 'of us.'
- **15:13** — The Vulgate coordinates 'in spe, et virtute Spiritus Sancti' ('in hope and the power of the Holy Spirit') with 'et' ('and'), whereas the Greek reads 'in hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.'
- **15:14** — The Clementine Vulgate reads 'dilectione' ('love') where the Greek text reads 'agathosynes' ('goodness').
- **15:20** — The Vulgate reads 'praedicavi Evangelium hoc' ('I have preached this gospel') rather than the Greek 'making it my ambition to preach the gospel'; in 15:16 'sanctificans' ('sanctifying') likewise renders the Greek 'serving as a priest of.'
- **15:22** — The clause 'et prohibitus sum usque adhuc' ('and I have been prevented until now') is a Vulgate-distinct addition not in the Greek; similarly, in 15:31 'obsequii mei oblatio' ('the offering of my service') expands the Greek 'my service.'
