## Text

**14:1** Now receive the one who is weak in faith, not in disputes over thoughts.

**14:2** For one believes that he may eat all things; but let the one who is weak eat vegetables.

**14:3** Let the one who eats not scorn the one who does not eat, and let the one who does not eat not judge the one who eats, for God has received him.

**14:4** Who are you, who judge another's servant? To his own master he stands or falls; yet he will stand, for God is able to make him stand.

**14:5** For one judges between one day and another, while another judges every day; let each one abound in his own mind.

**14:6** The one who regards the day, for the Lord he regards it, and the one who eats, for the Lord he eats, for he gives thanks to God. And the one who does not eat, for the Lord he does not eat, and he gives thanks to God.

**14:7** For none of us lives for himself, and no one dies for himself.

**14:8** For whether we live, we live for the Lord, or whether we die, we die for the Lord. Therefore, whether we live or whether we die, we are the Lord's.

**14:9** For to this purpose Christ died and rose again, so that He might be Lord of both the dead and the living.

**14:10** But you, why do you judge your brother? Or you, why do you scorn your brother? For we will all stand before the judgment seat of Christ.

**14:11** For it is written: "As I live," says the Lord, "surely every knee will bow to Me, and every tongue will confess to God."

**14:12** Therefore, each one of us will render an account for himself to God.

**14:13** Therefore let us no longer judge one another, but rather make this your judgment: that you not place a stumbling block or a snare before a brother.

**14:14** I know and am confident in the Lord Jesus that nothing is unclean through Him; except to the one who considers something to be unclean, to him it is unclean.

**14:15** For if your brother is grieved because of food, you are no longer walking according to love. Do not by your food destroy the one for whom Christ died.

**14:16** Therefore, let our good not be slandered.

**14:17** For the kingdom of God is not food and drink, but righteousness, and peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit.

**14:18** For the one who serves Christ in this pleases God and is approved by people.

**14:19** Therefore let us pursue the things of peace, and let us guard the things of edification toward one another.

**14:20** Do not destroy the work of God on account of food. Indeed, all things are clean, but it is evil for the person who eats with stumbling.

**14:21** It is good not to eat meat and not to drink wine, nor to do anything by which your brother stumbles, or is ensnared, or is weakened.

**14:22** Do you have faith? Keep it within yourself before God. Blessed is the one who does not judge himself in what he approves.

**14:23** But the one who makes a distinction, if he eats, is condemned, because it is not from faith. And everything that is not from faith is sin.

## Translator Notes

- **14:2** — The Clementine Vulgate reads the jussive subjunctive 'manducet' ('let him eat') rather than the indicative ('eats') found in the Greek.
- **14:5** — Latin 'judicat diem inter diem' literally means 'judges day between day' (distinguishing one day from another, while another regards every day alike). 'In suo sensu abundet' literally means 'let him abound in his own mind,' expressing full personal conviction.
- **14:14** — Latin 'commune' literally means 'common' (i.e., ritually unclean or profane). Vulgate 'per ipsum' ('through Him') naturally refers to Christ, whereas the Greek 'di heautou' means 'in itself.'
- **14:16** — The Vulgate reads 'bonum nostrum' ('our good') rather than the Greek 'your good'; similarly, it reads 'Deus' ('God') in 14:4 and 'Christi' ('of Christ') in 14:10 where critical Greek editions read 'Lord' and 'God,' and adds 'custodiamus' ('let us guard') in 14:19.
- **14:23** — Latin 'discernit' literally means 'distinguishes' or 'makes a distinction' (having scruples over foods), rendering the Greek middle participle 'diakrinomenos' ('doubting').
