## Text

**12:1** Therefore I urge you, brothers, by the mercy of God, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy, pleasing to God—your reasonable service.

**12:2** And do not be conformed to this age, but be reformed in the newness of your mind, so that you may discern what the will of God is: what is good, and well-pleasing, and perfect.

**12:3** For through the grace that has been given to me, I say to everyone among you not to think more highly than one ought to think, but to think with sober judgment, and to each person according as God has apportioned a measure of faith.

**12:4** For just as we have many members in one body, yet all the members do not have the same function,

**12:5** so we, being many, are one body in Christ, and individually members of one another.

**12:6** Now having differing gifts according to the grace that has been given to us: whether prophecy, in proportion to faith;

**12:7** or service, in serving; or the one who teaches, in teaching;

**12:8** the one who exhorts, in exhorting; the one who gives, in simplicity; the one who leads, in diligence; the one who shows mercy, in cheerfulness.

**12:9** Let love be without pretense: hating what is evil, clinging to what is good;

**12:10** loving one another with brotherly affection, outdoing one another in showing honor;

**12:11** not sluggish in diligence, fervent in spirit, serving the Lord;

**12:12** rejoicing in hope, patient in tribulation, steadfast in prayer;

**12:13** sharing in the needs of the saints, pursuing hospitality.

**12:14** Bless those who are persecuting you; bless, and do not curse.

**12:15** Rejoice with those who rejoice, weep with those who weep;

**12:16** being of the same mind toward one another; not setting your mind on lofty things, but associating with the lowly. Do not be wise in your own estimation;

**12:17** repaying no one evil for evil; taking care for what is honorable not only before God, but also before all people.

**12:18** If it can be done, as far as it depends on you, being at peace with all people;

**12:19** not avenging yourselves, dearly beloved, but leave room for wrath. For it is written: "Vengeance belongs to Me; I will repay," says the Lord.

**12:20** Rather, if your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him a drink; for by doing this you will heap coals of fire upon his head.

**12:21** Do not be conquered by evil, but conquer evil with good.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — Latin misericordiam is singular ("mercy"), and rationabile obsequium literally means "reasonable service" or "rational worship."
- **12:2** — Latin reformamini in novitate sensus vestri literally means "be reformed in the newness of your mind/understanding."
- **12:11** — The Vulgate reads Domino servientes ("serving the Lord") rather than the variant "serving the time."
- **12:17** — The Vulgate includes non tantum coram Deo, sed etiam ("not only before God, but also"), reflecting an expanded textual tradition.
- **12:19** — Latin non vosmetipsos defendentes literally means "not defending/avenging yourselves."
