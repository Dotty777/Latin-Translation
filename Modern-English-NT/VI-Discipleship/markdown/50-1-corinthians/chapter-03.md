## Text

**3:1** And I, brothers, could not speak to you as to spiritual people, but as to fleshly ones. As to infants in Christ,

**3:2** I gave you milk to drink, not food, for you were not yet able; nor indeed are you able even now, for you are still fleshly.

**3:3** For since there is jealousy and contention among you, are you not fleshly, and do you not walk according to man?

**3:4** For when one says, "I indeed belong to Paul," and another, "I to Apollos," are you not men? What then is Apollos? And what is Paul?

**3:5** Servants of Him whom you believed, and to each one just as the Lord gave.

**3:6** I planted, Apollos watered, yet God gave the growth.

**3:7** Therefore neither the one who plants is anything, nor the one who waters, but God, who gives the growth.

**3:8** Now the one who plants and the one who waters are one. Yet each one will receive his own reward according to his own labor.

**3:9** For we are God's helpers; you are God's field, you are God's building.

**3:10** According to the grace of God that was given to me, as a wise architect I laid a foundation, and another builds upon it. Yet let each one see how he builds upon it.

**3:11** For no one can lay another foundation besides the one that has been laid, which is Christ Jesus.

**3:12** Now if anyone builds upon this foundation gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, straw,

**3:13** each one's work will be manifest, for the day of the Lord will disclose it, because it will be revealed in fire, and the fire will test what sort each one's work is.

**3:14** If anyone's work that he built upon it remains, he will receive a reward.

**3:15** If anyone's work burns up, he will suffer loss; but he himself will be saved, yet so as through fire.

**3:16** Do you not know that you are the temple of God, and the Spirit of God dwells in you?

**3:17** Now if anyone violates the temple of God, God will destroy him. For the temple of God is holy, which you are.

**3:18** Let no one deceive himself: if anyone among you seems to be wise in this age, let him become foolish so that he may be wise.

**3:19** For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God. For it is written, "I will catch the wise in their craftiness."

**3:20** And again, "The Lord knows the thoughts of the wise, that they are empty."

**3:21** Therefore let no one boast in men.

**3:22** For all things are yours, whether Paul, or Apollos, or Cephas, or the world, or life, or death, or things present, or things to come; for all things are yours,

**3:23** yet you belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.

## Translator Notes

- **3:4** — In the Clementine Vulgate the questions "What then is Apollos? And what is Paul?" close verse 4, whereas modern Greek-based editions place them at the beginning of verse 5.
- **3:5** — The Vulgate reads ministri ejus, cui credidistis ("Servants of Him whom you believed"), a Vulgate-distinct reading where the Greek text has "servants through whom you believed."
- **3:9** — The Vulgate renders Greek synergoi ("fellow workers") as adjutores ("helpers" or "assistants").
- **3:13** — The Clementine Vulgate adds Domini ("of the Lord") to "the day," whereas the Greek text reads simply "the day."
- **3:19** — The Vulgate renders the quotation of Job 5:13 in the first-person future (Comprehendam, "I will catch") as direct divine speech, whereas the Greek and Hebrew use a third-person participle ("He who catches").
