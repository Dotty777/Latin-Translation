## Text

**6:1** Does anyone among you, having a case against another, dare to be judged before the unrighteous and not before the saints?

**6:2** Or do you not know that the saints will judge this world? And if the world will be judged by you, are you unworthy to judge the smallest matters?

**6:3** Do you not know that we will judge angels? How much more secular matters?

**6:4** Therefore, if you have secular lawsuits, appoint those who are despised in the church to judge.

**6:5** I speak to your shame. Is it so that there is not any wise person among you who can judge between his brothers?

**6:6** Instead, brother contends in a lawsuit with brother, and this before unbelievers?

**6:7** Already, indeed, it is altogether a fault in you that you have lawsuits among yourselves. Why do you not rather accept wrong? Why do you not rather suffer fraud?

**6:8** Rather, you yourselves do wrong and defraud, and this to brothers.

**6:9** Or do you not know that the unrighteous will not possess the kingdom of God? Do not be misled: neither sexually immoral people, nor those who serve idols, nor adulterers,

**6:10** nor the effeminate, nor those who lie with males, nor thieves, nor the greedy, nor drunkards, nor revilers, nor extortioners will possess the kingdom of God.

**6:11** And such were some of you; yet you have been washed, yet you have been sanctified, yet you have been justified in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ and in the Spirit of our God.

**6:12** All things are permitted for me, yet not all things are beneficial; all things are permitted for me, yet I myself will not be brought under the power of anything.

**6:13** Food is for the stomach, and the stomach is for foods; yet God will destroy both it and them. The body, however, is not for sexual immorality, but for the Lord, and the Lord is for the body.

**6:14** Moreover, God both raised up the Lord and will raise us up through His power.

**6:15** Do you not know that your bodies are members of Christ? Taking the members of Christ, then, shall I make them members of a prostitute? Far from it.

**6:16** Or do you not know that the one who clings to a prostitute becomes one body? For it says, "The two will be in one flesh."

**6:17** But the one who clings to the Lord is one spirit.

**6:18** Flee sexual immorality. Every sin, whatever a person commits, is outside the body; but the one who commits sexual immorality sins against his own body.

**6:19** Or do you not know that your members are a temple of the Holy Spirit, who is in you, whom you have from God, and you are not your own?

**6:20** For you were bought at a great price. Glorify and carry God in your body.

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — Latin constituite ("appoint") is an imperative; the Greek form kathizete can be read either as an imperative or as an indicative.
- **6:5** — The Latin literally reads the singular "between his brother" (inter fratrem suum), a compressed idiom meaning between one brother and another.
- **6:9** — Latin possidebunt ("will possess") renders the Greek verb for "inherit" in vv. 9-10, and the Latin verse break falls before neque molles; in v. 10, molles literally means "soft ones" and masculorum concubitores "those who lie with males."
- **6:19** — A distinctly Vulgate plural reading: "your members are a temple" (membra vestra, templum sunt), where the Greek text has singular "your body."
- **6:20** — The Vulgate preserves two distinctive readings absent from the critical Greek text: "at a great price" (pretio magno) and the added command "and carry/bear" (et portate) before "God."
