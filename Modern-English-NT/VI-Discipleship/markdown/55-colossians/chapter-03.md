## Text

**3:1** Therefore, if you have risen together with Christ, seek the things that are above, where Christ is, sitting at God's right hand.

**3:2** Keep your minds set on the things that are above, not on the things on the earth.

**3:3** For you died, and your life is hidden with Christ in God.

**3:4** When Christ, your life, appears, then you also will appear with Him in glory.

**3:5** Put to death, therefore, your members that are on the earth: fornication, impurity, lust, evil desire, and greed, which is servitude to idols.

**3:6** On account of these things the wrath of God comes upon the sons of unbelief.

**3:7** In these things you also once walked, when you were living in them.

**3:8** But now, you too, lay aside all of them: wrath, indignation, malice, blasphemy, foul speech from your mouth.

**3:9** Do not lie to one another, stripping off the old man with his deeds,

**3:10** and clothing yourselves with the new one, who is being renewed into knowledge according to the image of Him who created him,

**3:11** where there is not Gentile and Jew, circumcision and uncircumcision, barbarian and Scythian, slave and free; but Christ is all things, and in all.

**3:12** Clothe yourselves, therefore, as God's chosen ones, holy and beloved, with a heart of mercy, kindness, humility, modesty, patience,

**3:13** bearing with one another and forgiving one another if anyone has a complaint against another; just as the Lord also forgave you, so too must you.

**3:14** Over all these things, however, have love, which is the bond of perfection;

**3:15** and let the peace of Christ exult in your hearts, in which you were also called in one body; and be thankful.

**3:16** Let the word of Christ dwell abundantly in you, in all wisdom, teaching and admonishing one another with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, singing in grace to God in your hearts.

**3:17** Everything, whatever you do in word or in deed, do all things in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, giving thanks to God and the Father through Him.

**3:18** Wives, be submissive to your husbands, as is proper, in the Lord.

**3:19** Husbands, love your wives, and do not be bitter toward them.

**3:20** Children, obey your parents in all things, for this is pleasing in the Lord.

**3:21** Fathers, do not provoke your children to indignation, so that they do not become disheartened.

**3:22** Slaves, obey your masters in the flesh in all things, not serving for the eye, as if pleasing people, but in singleness of heart, fearing God.

**3:23** Whatever you do, work from the heart, as for the Lord and not for people,

**3:24** knowing that you will receive the reward of the inheritance from the Lord. Serve the Lord Christ.

**3:25** For the one who does wrong will receive back what he has done unjustly, and there is no partiality with God.

## Translator Notes

- **3:6** — The Vulgate includes the phrase "upon the sons of unbelief" (super filios incredulitatis), which is omitted in some early Greek manuscripts; where present in Greek, the noun means "disobedience" rather than the Vulgate's "unbelief."
- **3:14** — The Vulgate supplies the explicit imperative verb "have" (habete), whereas the Greek text omits a verb here and relies on "put on" carried over from verse 12.
- **3:15** — The Vulgate reads "exult" (exsultet) rather than the Greek "rule" or "act as umpire," and uses the ablative "in which" (in qua) rather than the Greek "to which."
- **3:24** — Latin servite is unambiguously imperative ("Serve the Lord Christ"), whereas the corresponding Greek form can be read as either imperative or indicative ("You are serving the Lord Christ").
- **3:25** — The Vulgate adds "with God" (apud Deum) at the end of the verse, a phrase absent from the Greek text here.
