## Text

**3:1** Therefore, holy brothers, partakers of a heavenly calling, consider the Apostle and high priest of our confession, Jesus,

**3:2** who is faithful to Him who made Him, just as Moses also was in all His house.

**3:3** For this One has been counted worthy of greater glory than Moses, by as much as he who built it has greater honor than the house.

**3:4** For every house is built by someone, but He who created all things is God.

**3:5** And Moses indeed was faithful in His entire house as a servant, for a testimony of the things that were to be spoken;

**3:6** but Christ as a Son in His own house, which house we are, if we hold confidence and the glory of hope firm to the end.

**3:7** For this reason, just as the Holy Spirit says, "Today, if you hear His voice,

**3:8** do not harden your hearts, as in the provocation, according to the day of testing in the desert,

**3:9** where your fathers tested Me, tried Me, and saw My works

**3:10** for forty years. Because of this I was angry with this generation and said, 'They always go astray in heart. And they themselves have not known My ways,'

**3:11** just as I swore in My wrath, 'They will never enter into My rest.'"

**3:12** See to it, brothers, that there does not happen to be in any one of you an evil heart of unbelief, in departing from the living God;

**3:13** but encourage one another every single day, as long as it is called "Today," so that no one of you may be hardened by the deceit of sin.

**3:14** For we have become partakers of Christ, if indeed we hold the beginning of His substance firm to the end.

**3:15** While it is said, "Today, if you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts, as in that provocation."

**3:16** For some, on hearing, gave provocation, yet not all who set out from Egypt through Moses.

**3:17** And with whom was He angry for forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose corpses fell prostrate in the desert?

**3:18** And to whom did He swear that they would not enter into His rest, except to those who were unbelieving?

**3:19** And we see that they were unable to enter because of unbelief.

## Translator Notes

- **3:6** — The Vulgate reads "in domo sua" ("in His own house," where the Greek has "over"), nominative "quae domus" ("which house," where most Greek texts have "whose house"), and "gloriam spei" ("the glory of hope," where the Greek has "boasting/pride of hope").
- **3:11** — Latin literally reads "Si introibunt in requiem meam" ("If they will enter into My rest"), reproducing the Hebrew elliptical oath formula for an emphatic negative (cf. 3:18).
- **3:14** — The Vulgate adds "ejus" in "initium substantiae ejus" ("the beginning of His substance," matching "substantiae ejus" in 1:3), whereas the Greek lacks "his" and "hypostasis" is often translated "confidence" or "assurance."
- **3:16** — The Vulgate reads declarative statements ("Quidam ... sed non universi," "For some ... yet not all"), whereas modern critical Greek editions punctuate the verse as two rhetorical questions ("Who ...? Was it not all ...?").
- **3:18** — The Vulgate reads "increduli fuerunt" ("were unbelieving"), tying the verse lexically to "incredulitatis" and "incredulitatem" ("unbelief") in 3:12 and 3:19, whereas the Greek uses a term meaning "disobedient."
