## Text

**10:1** For the law, having a shadow of the good things to come, not the very image of the things, can never, with those very same sacrifices that they offer unceasingly year by year, make those who approach perfect;

**10:2** otherwise they would have ceased to be offered, because the worshipers, having been cleansed once, would no longer have any consciousness of sin;

**10:3** but in them a remembrance of sins is made year by year.

**10:4** For it is impossible for sins to be taken away by the blood of bulls and goats.

**10:5** Therefore, entering the world, He says, "Sacrifice and offering You did not want, but a body You prepared for Me;

**10:6** burnt offerings for sin did not please You.

**10:7** Then I said, 'Look, I come (at the head of the book it is written about Me) to do Your will, God.'"

**10:8** Saying above, "Sacrifices and offerings and burnt offerings for sin You did not want, nor were they pleasing to You" (which are offered according to the law),

**10:9** "Then I said, 'Look, I come to do Your will, God,'" He takes away the first so that He may establish what follows.

**10:10** In this will we have been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once.

**10:11** And every priest indeed stands ready daily, ministering and often offering the same sacrifices, which can never take away sins;

**10:12** but this One, offering one sacrifice for sins, sits forever at the right hand of God,

**10:13** waiting from then on until His enemies are placed as a footstool for His feet.

**10:14** For by one offering He has perfected forever those who have been sanctified.

**10:15** Moreover, the Holy Spirit also bears witness to us; for after He said,

**10:16** "Now this is the covenant that I will make with them after those days, says the Lord, by giving My laws in their hearts, and on their minds I will inscribe them,

**10:17** and their sins and their iniquities I will not remember anymore."

**10:18** Now where there is forgiveness of these, there is no longer an offering for sin.

**10:19** Therefore, brothers, having confidence in the entrance to the holy places by the blood of Christ,

**10:20** which He initiated for us as a new and living way through the veil, that is, His flesh,

**10:21** and a great priest over the house of God,

**10:22** let us approach with a true heart in fullness of faith, with our hearts sprinkled from an evil conscience and our body washed with clean water;

**10:23** let us hold the confession of our hope unswerving (for He is faithful who promised),

**10:24** and let us consider one another for the stirring up of love and good works,

**10:25** not deserting our gathering, as is the custom of some, but offering encouragement, and all the more as you see the day approaching.

**10:26** For if we sin willfully after having received the knowledge of the truth, there is no longer left a sacrifice for sins,

**10:27** but a certain terrifying expectation of judgment, and a zeal of fire that is about to consume the adversaries.

**10:28** Anyone who makes the law of Moses void dies without any compassion by two or three witnesses;

**10:29** how much more do you suppose that one deserves worse punishments, who has trampled on the Son of God, and has counted the blood of the covenant in which he was sanctified as defiled, and has insulted the Spirit of grace?

**10:30** For we know the One who said, "To Me belongs vengeance, and I will repay." And again, "The Lord will judge His people."

**10:31** It is dreadful to fall into the hands of the living God.

**10:32** But remember the earlier days, in which, having been enlightened, you endured a great struggle of sufferings,

**10:33** both on the one hand made a spectacle by reproaches and tribulations, and on the other hand becoming companions of those living in such a way.

**10:34** For you both sympathized with those in chains and accepted with joy the plundering of your goods, knowing that you have a better and enduring possession.

**10:35** Therefore, do not lose your confidence, which has a great reward.

**10:36** For endurance is necessary for you, so that, doing the will of God, you may obtain the promise.

**10:37** For, "Yet a very little while, He who is to come will come and will not delay;

**10:38** and My righteous one lives by faith; yet if he withdraws himself, he will not please My soul."

**10:39** But we are not sons of withdrawal to destruction, but of faith to the gaining of the soul.

## Translator Notes

- **10:2** — The Clementine Vulgate reads this clause as a declarative statement (alioquin cessassent offerri, without a negative) rather than the rhetorical question found in most Greek manuscripts ("would they not have ceased to be offered?").
- **10:6** — Latin holocautomata pro peccato ("burnt offerings for sin," also in v. 8) omits the conjunction "and" present in the Greek ("burnt offerings and [offerings] for sin").
- **10:9** — The Clementine Vulgate has first-person tunc dixi ("Then I said"), quoting the introductory words from v. 7 directly, whereas the Greek has third-person "then he has said"; Latin sequens also literally means "what follows" rather than Greek "the second."
- **10:19** — The Clementine Vulgate reads in sanguine Christi ("by the blood of Christ"), whereas the Greek text has "by the blood of Jesus."
- **10:39** — Latin subtractionis filii ("sons of withdrawal") includes filii ("sons"), a Vulgate-distinct addition not present in the Greek text.
