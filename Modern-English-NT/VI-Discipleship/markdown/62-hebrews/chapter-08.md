## Text

**8:1** Now the main point regarding the things being said is this: we have such a high priest, who sat down at the right hand of the throne of Majesty in the heavens,

**8:2** a minister of the holy places and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, and not man.

**8:3** For every high priest is appointed to offer gifts and sacrifices; therefore it is necessary that this One also have something to offer.

**8:4** If therefore He were on earth, He would not even be a priest, since there are those who offer gifts according to the law,

**8:5** who serve a copy and shadow of the heavenly things. Just as an answer was given to Moses when he was completing the tabernacle: "See," He says, "make all things according to the pattern that was shown to you on the mountain."

**8:6** But now He has obtained a better ministry, by as much as He is also the mediator of a better covenant, which has been enacted upon better promises.

**8:7** For if that first one had been free of fault, surely no place would be sought for a second.

**8:8** For finding fault with them, He says, "Look, days will come, says the Lord, and I will accomplish a new covenant over the house of Israel and over the house of Judah,

**8:9** not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers on the day when I took hold of their hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt; because they themselves did not continue in My covenant, and I disregarded them, says the Lord.

**8:10** For this is the covenant that I will establish for the house of Israel after those days, says the Lord: giving My laws into their mind, and upon their heart I will inscribe them; and I will be God to them, and they themselves will be a people to Me;

**8:11** and each one will not teach his neighbor, and each one his brother, saying, 'Know the Lord,' because all will know Me, from the least to the greatest of them;

**8:12** for I will be merciful toward their iniquities, and their sins I will remember no longer."

**8:13** Now in saying "new," He has made the first obsolete. And what is becoming antiquated and growing old is close to perishing.

## Translator Notes

- **8:5** — The Vulgate uses the same noun "exemplar" both for the earthly copy (Greek "hypodeigma") and for the heavenly pattern shown to Moses (Greek "typos"), and "responsum est" ("an answer was given") renders Greek "kechrematistai" ("was divinely instructed").
- **8:8** — The Vulgate uses the future tense "venient" ("will come"), whereas the Greek text has the present "erchontai" ("are coming").
- **8:11** — The Vulgate reads "proximum" ("neighbor"), aligning with a Septuagint and Greek manuscript variant, where the critical Greek text has "politen" ("fellow citizen").
