## Text

**3:1** Now know this, that in the last days perilous times will be at hand:

**3:2** people will be lovers of themselves, greedy, haughty, arrogant, blasphemous, not obedient to parents, unthankful, wicked,

**3:3** without affection, without peace, accusers, unrestrained, fierce, without kindness,

**3:4** betrayers, reckless, swollen with pride, and lovers of pleasures more than of God,

**3:5** having indeed an appearance of godliness, yet denying its power. And avoid these people:

**3:6** for among them are those who make their way into houses and lead captive weak women burdened with sins, who are led along by various desires,

**3:7** always learning and never arriving at the knowledge of the truth.

**3:8** Now just as Jannes and Mambres resisted Moses, so these also resist the truth, men corrupted in mind, disqualified regarding the faith;

**3:9** yet they will advance no further, for their foolishness will be manifest to all, just as theirs also was.

**3:10** You, however, have closely followed my teaching, way of life, purpose, faith, forbearance, love, patience,

**3:11** persecutions, sufferings, the sorts of things that befell me in Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra: what persecutions I endured, and the Lord rescued me from all of them.

**3:12** And all who wish to live in a godly manner in Christ Jesus will suffer persecution.

**3:13** But evil men and deceivers will advance to what is worse, erring and leading into error.

**3:14** You, however, remain in the things that you have learned and that have been entrusted to you, knowing from whom you learned them,

**3:15** and that from infancy you have known the sacred writings, which are able to instruct you for salvation through the faith that is in Christ Jesus.

**3:16** All Scripture, divinely inspired, is useful for teaching, for reproving, for correcting, and for training in righteousness,

**3:17** so that the man of God may be perfect, equipped for every good work.

## Translator Notes

- **3:7** — Vulgate reads simply "never arriving," omitting "able" found in the Greek text.
- **3:8** — Vulgate preserves the name "Mambres" (also attested in Western witnesses), whereas most Greek manuscripts read "Jambres."
- **3:13** — Vulgate renders Greek "deceiving and being deceived" with the wordplay "erring and leading into error" (errantes, et in errorem mittentes).
- **3:14** — Vulgate reads "have been entrusted to you" (credita sunt tibi) rather than the Greek "have become convinced of."
- **3:16** — Unlike the Greek (which includes "and" between the two adjectives), the Vulgate omits "and," making "divinely inspired" (divinitus inspirata) modify "Scripture" directly.
