## Text

**1:1** Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ through the will of God, according to the promise of life that is in Christ Jesus,

**1:2** to Timothy, dearest son: grace, mercy, peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

**1:3** I give thanks to God, whom I serve from my ancestors with a pure conscience, that without interruption I keep remembrance of you in my prayers, night and day,

**1:4** longing to see you, mindful of your tears, so that I may be filled with joy,

**1:5** receiving a reminder of that faith which is in you without pretense, which also dwelt first in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am certain that it is in you as well.

**1:6** For this reason I remind you to revive the grace of God that is in you through the laying on of my hands.

**1:7** For God did not give us a spirit of fear, but of power, of love, and of sober-mindedness.

**1:8** Therefore do not be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me, His prisoner, but labor together for the gospel according to the power of God,

**1:9** who delivered us and called us with His holy calling, not according to our works, but according to His own purpose and the grace that was given to us in Christ Jesus before the times of the ages.

**1:10** Now, however, it has been manifested through the illumination of our Savior Jesus Christ, who indeed destroyed death and illuminated life and incorruption through the gospel,

**1:11** in which I was appointed a preacher, an apostle, and a teacher of the Gentiles.

**1:12** For this reason I also suffer these things, yet I am not put to shame. For I know whom I have trusted, and I am certain that He is able to preserve my deposit until that day.

**1:13** Keep the pattern of sound words that you heard from me, in faith and in love in Christ Jesus.

**1:14** Guard the good deposit through the Holy Spirit, who dwells in us.

**1:15** You know this, that all who are in Asia have turned away from me, among whom are Phygelus and Hermogenes.

**1:16** May the Lord grant mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, because he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chain;

**1:17** but when he had come to Rome, he diligently searched for me and found me.

**1:18** May the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord on that day. And how much he served me in Ephesus, you yourself know better.

## Translator Notes

- **1:6** — Vulgate reads "revive the grace" (resuscites gratiam) rather than the Greek "rekindle the gift."
- **1:8** — Vulgate renders Greek "share in suffering" as "labor together" (collabora), part of a consistent Vulgate motif of "labor" here and in 2:3 and 2:9.
- **1:10** — Vulgate renders Greek "appearing" as "illumination" (illuminationem), creating a direct lexical link with "illuminated" (illuminavit) later in the verse.
- **1:12** — Literally "my deposit" (depositum meum), which may refer either to what Paul has entrusted to God for safekeeping or to the gospel charge God has entrusted to Paul (cf. 1:14).
- **1:18** — Vulgate explicitly includes "to me" (mihi), absent from most critical Greek manuscripts.
