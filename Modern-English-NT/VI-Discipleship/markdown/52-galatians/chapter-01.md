## Text

**1:1** Paul, an apostle not from men, nor through a man, but through Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised Him up from the dead,

**1:2** and all the brothers who are with me, to the churches of Galatia.

**1:3** Grace to you and peace from God the Father and our Lord Jesus Christ,

**1:4** who gave Himself for our sins, so that He might rescue us from the present wicked age, according to the will of our God and Father,

**1:5** to whom belongs glory forever and ever. Amen.

**1:6** I am amazed that you are so very quickly turning away from Him who called you into the grace of Christ to another gospel,

**1:7** which is not another, except that there are some who are disturbing you and want to overturn the gospel of Christ.

**1:8** But even if we or an angel from heaven should proclaim a gospel to you other than what we proclaimed to you, let him be accursed.

**1:9** Just as we said before, now I also say again: if anyone proclaims a gospel to you other than what you received, let him be accursed.

**1:10** For am I now trying to persuade men, or God? Or am I seeking to please men? If I were still pleasing men, I would not be a servant of Christ.

**1:11** For I make known to you, brothers, that the gospel which was proclaimed by me is not according to man;

**1:12** for I myself neither received it from a man nor learned it, but through a revelation of Jesus Christ.

**1:13** For you have heard of my former way of life in Judaism: how beyond measure I was persecuting the church of God and trying to overthrow it,

**1:14** and I was advancing in Judaism beyond many of my contemporaries among my own people, being more exceedingly zealous for my ancestral traditions.

**1:15** But when it pleased Him who separated me from my mother's womb and called me through His grace,

**1:16** to reveal His Son in me, so that I might proclaim Him among the Gentiles, immediately I did not consent to flesh and blood,

**1:17** nor did I go to Jerusalem to my predecessors, the apostles; but I went away into Arabia, and returned again to Damascus.

**1:18** Then after three years I went to Jerusalem to see Peter, and I stayed with him for fifteen days;

**1:19** but I saw no other of the apostles, except James, the brother of the Lord.

**1:20** Now the things that I am writing to you—look, before God, I am not lying.

**1:21** Afterward I went into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.

**1:22** Now I was not known by face to the churches of Judea that were in Christ;

**1:23** they only had the report, "The one who once persecuted us is now proclaiming the faith that he once tried to overthrow";

**1:24** and in me they were glorifying God.

## Translator Notes

- **1:6** — The Vulgate reads in gratiam (accusative, "into the grace") rather than "in the grace," and uses the same word aliud ("another") in both verses 6 and 7, whereas the Greek distinguishes heteron ("different") and allo ("another").
- **1:12** — The Vulgate uses the active verb didici ("nor learned it"), whereas the Greek has the passive edidachthen ("nor was I taught it").
- **1:16** — The Vulgate translates the Greek "did not consult with" with non acquievi ("did not consent to" or "did not yield to").
- **1:18** — The Clementine Vulgate reads Petrum ("Peter") here and Petro in 2:7-8, while using Cephas in 2:9, 11, and 14; many critical Greek editions read "Cephas" here as well.
