## Text

**5:1** Stand firm, and do not be held fast again by a yoke of slavery.

**5:2** Look, I, Paul, tell you that if you are circumcised, Christ will not benefit you at all.

**5:3** Now I testify again to every man who circumcises himself that he is obligated to do the entire law.

**5:4** You have been separated from Christ, you who are justified in the law; you have fallen from grace.

**5:5** For by the Spirit, from faith, we await the hope of righteousness.

**5:6** For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision has any value, but faith that works through love.

**5:7** You were running well; who prevented you from obeying the truth?

**5:8** This persuasion does not come from Him who is calling you.

**5:9** A little leaven corrupts the entire lump.

**5:10** I am confident in you in the Lord that you will be of no other mind; but the one who unsettles you will bear the judgment, whoever he is.

**5:11** But I, brothers, if I still preach circumcision, why do I still suffer persecution? Then the stumbling block of the cross has been nullified.

**5:12** I wish that those who unsettle you would even be cut off.

**5:13** For you were called to freedom, brothers; only do not give freedom as an opportunity for the flesh, but through the love of the Spirit serve one another.

**5:14** For the entire law is fulfilled in one statement: "You shall love your neighbor as yourself."

**5:15** But if you bite and devour one another, see to it that you are not consumed by one another.

**5:16** Now I say: walk by the Spirit, and you will not accomplish the desires of the flesh.

**5:17** For the flesh desires against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh; for these oppose one another, so that whatever things you wish, you do not do them.

**5:18** Now if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the law.

**5:19** Now the works of the flesh are plain, which are fornication, impurity, indecency, debauchery,

**5:20** slavery to idols, sorceries, enmities, contentions, jealousies, rages, quarrels, dissensions, sects,

**5:21** envies, murders, bouts of drunkenness, revelries, and things similar to these, about which I am forewarning you, just as I forewarned you, that those who practice such things will not obtain the kingdom of God.

**5:22** But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, forbearance,

**5:23** meekness, faith, modesty, self-control, chastity. Against things of this kind there is no law.

**5:24** And those who belong to Christ have crucified their flesh with its vices and cravings.

**5:25** If by the Spirit we live, by the Spirit let us also walk.

**5:26** Let us not become eager for empty glory, provoking one another, envying one another.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — In the Clementine Vulgate, the clause concerning the freedom with which Christ set us free concludes 4:31, so 5:1 opens directly with "Stand firm." The passive verb contineri literally means "to be held fast" or "confined."
- **5:9** — The Vulgate reads corrumpit ("corrupts," reflecting an early Western reading) where most Greek manuscripts read "leavens."
- **5:13** — The Clementine Vulgate reads per caritatem Spiritus ("through the love of the Spirit"), whereas the standard Greek text reads simply "through love."
- **5:19** — Across 5:19-21 the Clementine Vulgate lists seventeen works of the flesh, including both impudicitia ("indecency") and luxuria ("debauchery") in 5:19 and homicidia ("murders") in 5:21, and uses consequentur ("obtain") rather than "inherit."
- **5:22** — Across 5:22-23 the Clementine Vulgate enumerates twelve fruits of the Spirit, including longanimitas ("forbearance"), modestia ("modesty"), and castitas ("chastity") alongside the nine found in the standard Greek text.
