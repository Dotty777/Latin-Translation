## Text

**5:1** Now concerning the times and the moments, brothers, you have no need for us to write to you.

**5:2** For you yourselves know thoroughly that, just like a thief in the night, so the day of the Lord will come.

**5:3** For when they say, "Peace and security," then sudden destruction will come upon them, like pain upon a woman with child, and they will not escape.

**5:4** But you, brothers, are not in darkness, for that day to overtake you like a thief.

**5:5** For all of you are sons of light and sons of the day; we are not of the night, nor of darkness.

**5:6** Therefore, let us not sleep as the rest also do, but let us stay awake and be sober.

**5:7** For those who sleep, sleep at night; and those who are drunk, are drunk at night.

**5:8** But let us who are of the day be sober, wearing the breastplate of faith and love, and as a helmet the hope of salvation,

**5:9** because God did not appoint us to wrath, but to the obtaining of salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,

**5:10** who died for us, so that whether we stay awake or sleep, we may live together with Him.

**5:11** For this reason, comfort one another and build each other up, just as you are also doing.

**5:12** Now we ask you, brothers, that you recognize those who labor among you, and are over you in the Lord, and admonish you,

**5:13** that you hold them more abundantly in love because of their work. Have peace with them.

**5:14** And we ask you, brothers: reprove the unruly, comfort the fainthearted, support the weak, be patient toward all.

**5:15** See that no one repays evil for evil to anyone, but always pursue what is good toward one another and toward all.

**5:16** Rejoice at all times.

**5:17** Pray without interruption.

**5:18** Give thanks in all things, for this is God's will in Christ Jesus concerning all of you.

**5:19** Do not extinguish the Spirit.

**5:20** Do not spurn prophecies.

**5:21** But test all things; hold fast to what is good.

**5:22** Abstain from every evil appearance.

**5:23** Now may the God of peace Himself sanctify you in every respect, so that your whole spirit, and soul, and body may be kept without blame at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**5:24** Faithful is He who called you, who will also do it.

**5:25** Brothers, pray for us.

**5:26** Greet all the brothers with a holy kiss.

**5:27** I put you under oath by the Lord that this letter be read to all the holy brothers.

**5:28** May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **5:13** — The Vulgate reads "pacem habete cum eis" ("Have peace with them," referring to the leaders), whereas critical Greek texts read "be at peace among yourselves."
- **5:18** — The Vulgate reads "in omnibus vobis" ("concerning all of you"), including "omnibus" ("all") where standard Greek texts read simply "for you."
- **5:22** — Latin "specie mala" uses an adjective ("evil appearance" or "evil form") rather than a genitive; "species" carries both the sense of outward "appearance" and that of "form" or "kind."
- **5:23** — The Vulgate links the second clause with "ut" ("so that ... may be kept") as a purpose or result clause rather than a second coordinate wish, and "per omnia" literally means "through all things" or "in every respect."
- **5:27** — The Vulgate includes "sanctis" ("holy") in "omnibus sanctis fratribus" ("all the holy brothers"), and v. 28 concludes with "Amen," reflecting the traditional Vulgate text rather than the shorter critical Greek text.
