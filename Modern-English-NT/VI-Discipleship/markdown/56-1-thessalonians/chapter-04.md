## Text

**4:1** Furthermore, then, brothers, we ask and implore you in the Lord Jesus that, just as you received from us how you ought to walk and to please God, so also you should walk, in order that you may abound even more.

**4:2** For you know what commands I gave you through the Lord Jesus.

**4:3** For this is God's will, your sanctification: that you abstain from fornication,

**4:4** that each one of you know how to possess his own vessel in sanctification and honor,

**4:5** not in the passion of desire, as the Gentiles also do, who do not know God,

**4:6** and that no one transgress or defraud his brother in a matter, because the Lord is an avenger concerning all these things, just as we told you beforehand and bore witness.

**4:7** For God did not call us to impurity, but to sanctification.

**4:8** Therefore, the one who spurns these things does not spurn man, but God, who also gave His Holy Spirit in us.

**4:9** Now concerning brotherly love, we have no need to write to you, for you yourselves have learned from God to love one another.

**4:10** Indeed, you do this toward all the brothers throughout all of Macedonia. Yet we ask you, brothers, that you abound even more,

**4:11** and strive to be quiet, and attend to your own business, and work with your hands, just as we commanded you,

**4:12** and that you walk honorably toward those who are outside, and desire nothing from anyone.

**4:13** Now we do not want you to be unaware, brothers, concerning those who are asleep, so that you may not grieve as the rest also do, who have no hope.

**4:14** For if we believe that Jesus died and rose again, so also God will bring with Him those who have fallen asleep through Jesus.

**4:15** For we say this to you in the word of the Lord, that we who are alive, who remain until the coming of the Lord, will not precede those who have fallen asleep.

**4:16** Because the Lord Himself will descend from heaven with a command, and with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet of God; and the dead who are in Christ will rise first.

**4:17** Afterward we who are alive, who are left, will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet Christ in the air; and thus we will always be with the Lord.

**4:18** So then, comfort one another with these words.

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — The Vulgate uses the subjunctive "sic et ambuletis" ("so also you should walk") as part of the exhortation, whereas critical Greek texts read an indicative ("just as you are walking").
- **4:2** — The Clementine Vulgate reads first-person singular "dederim" ("I gave"), whereas Greek manuscripts read the plural ("we gave").
- **4:4** — Literally "possess his own vessel" ("vas suum possidere"); Latin "possidere" ("possess, control") translates Greek ktasthai ("acquire, take"), and "vessel" is variously understood as one's own body or one's wife.
- **4:8** — The Vulgate includes "haec" ("these things"), uses the past tense "dedit" ("gave"), and reads first-person "in nobis" ("in us"), whereas critical Greek texts omit "these things," use a present participle ("gives"), and read second-person "to you."
- **4:17** — The Vulgate reads "obviam Christo" ("to meet Christ"), whereas standard Greek texts read "to meet the Lord."
