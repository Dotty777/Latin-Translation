## Text

**3:1** Finally, brothers, pray for us, that the word of God may run and be glorified, just as it also is among you,

**3:2** and that we may be rescued from unreasonable and evil men, for faith does not belong to all.

**3:3** But God is faithful; He will strengthen you and guard you from evil.

**3:4** And we have confidence about you in the Lord, that what we instructed, you are both doing and will do.

**3:5** And may the Lord direct your hearts in the love of God and the patience of Christ.

**3:6** Now we command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to withdraw yourselves from every brother walking in a disorderly way and not according to the tradition that they received from us.

**3:7** For you yourselves know how one ought to imitate us, because we were not restless among you,

**3:8** nor did we eat bread for free from anyone, but in labor and in fatigue, working night and day, so that we might not burden any of you.

**3:9** Not as though we did not have authority, but so that we might give ourselves to you as a pattern for imitating us.

**3:10** For even when we were with you, we kept commanding you this, that if anyone does not want to work, neither should he eat.

**3:11** For we have heard that some among you are walking restlessly, working at nothing, but acting meddlesomely.

**3:12** Now let us command those who are of this kind, and we urge them in the Lord Jesus Christ, that working with quietness they eat their own bread.

**3:13** But you, brothers, do not lose heart in doing good.

**3:14** And if anyone does not obey our word through the letter, take note of him, and do not associate with him, so that he may be ashamed;

**3:15** and do not regard him as an enemy, but reprove him as a brother.

**3:16** Now may the Lord of peace Himself give you everlasting peace in every place. The Lord be with all of you.

**3:17** The greeting, with my own hand, Paul's, which is the sign in every letter; this is how I write.

**3:18** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Vulgate reads sermo Dei ('the word of God'), whereas Greek manuscripts read 'the word of the Lord'.
- **3:3** — Vulgate reads Deus ('God'), whereas most Greek manuscripts read 'the Lord'. Latin a malo can be understood as either neuter ('from evil') or masculine ('from the evil one').
- **3:6** — Vulgate reads third-person plural acceperunt ('they received'), referring collectively to 'every brother', and shifts from inordinate ('in a disorderly way') here to inquieti/inquiete ('restless/restlessly') in 3:7 and 3:11.
- **3:12** — The Clementine Vulgate reads the present subjunctive denuntiemus ('let us command') alongside the indicative obsecramus ('and we urge'), where Greek has two indicatives.
- **3:16** — Vulgate reads pacem sempiternam ('everlasting peace', where Greek has 'peace at all times') and in omni loco ('in every place', reflecting the Greek variant en panti topo rather than en panti tropo, 'in every way').
