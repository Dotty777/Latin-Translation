## Text

**7:1** Now the high priest said, "Are these matters so?"

**7:2** And he said, "Men, brothers and fathers, listen: The God of glory appeared to our father Abraham while he was in Mesopotamia, before he stayed in Haran,

**7:3** and said to him, 'Go out from your land and from your relatives, and come into the land that I will show you.'

**7:4** Then he went out from the land of the Chaldeans and lived in Haran. And from there, after his father died, He moved him into this land in which you now live.

**7:5** And He did not give him an inheritance in it, not even a foot's step; but He promised to give it to him as a possession, and to his seed after him, although he had no son.

**7:6** Now God spoke to him, that his seed would be a resident alien in a foreign land, and they would subject them to servitude and mistreat them for four hundred years.

**7:7** 'And the nation that they serve, I Myself will judge,' said the Lord, 'and after these things they will come out and serve Me in this place.'

**7:8** And He gave him the covenant of circumcision; and so he fathered Isaac and circumcised him on the eighth day, and Isaac fathered Jacob, and Jacob the twelve patriarchs.

**7:9** And the patriarchs, being jealous, sold Joseph into Egypt; and God was with him,

**7:10** and rescued him from all his distresses, and gave him favor and wisdom in the sight of Pharaoh, king of Egypt; and he appointed him governor over Egypt and over all his household.

**7:11** Now a famine came over all Egypt and Canaan, and great distress, and our fathers were finding no food.

**7:12** But when Jacob heard that there was grain in Egypt, he sent our fathers the first time;

**7:13** and the second time Joseph was recognized by his brothers, and his family was made known to Pharaoh.

**7:14** Then Joseph sent and summoned his father Jacob and all his relatives, seventy-five souls.

**7:15** And Jacob went down into Egypt; and he died, he and our fathers.

**7:16** And they were carried over to Shechem and laid in the tomb that Abraham bought for a price of silver from the sons of Hamor, the son of Shechem.

**7:17** Now as the time of the promise that God had declared to Abraham was approaching, the people grew and multiplied in Egypt,

**7:18** until another king arose in Egypt who did not know Joseph.

**7:19** This man, dealing craftily with our people, afflicted our fathers so that they would expose their infants, that they might not be kept alive.

**7:20** At that same time Moses was born, and he was pleasing to God; and he was nurtured for three months in his father's house.

**7:21** Now when he had been exposed, Pharaoh's daughter took him up and nurtured him for herself as a son.

**7:22** And Moses was educated in all the wisdom of the Egyptians, and he was powerful in his words and in his works.

**7:23** Now when a period of forty years was fulfilled for him, it rose in his heart to visit his brothers, the sons of Israel.

**7:24** And when he saw someone suffering wrong, he defended him and executed vengeance for the one who was enduring the wrong, by striking down the Egyptian.

**7:25** Now he supposed that his brothers understood that God was giving them deliverance through his hand, but they did not understand.

**7:26** And on the following day he appeared to them as they were quarreling, and tried to reconcile them in peace, saying, 'Men, you are brothers; why are you harming one another?'

**7:27** But the one who was doing wrong to his neighbor pushed him away, saying, 'Who appointed you ruler and judge over us?

**7:28** You do not want to kill me just as you killed the Egyptian yesterday, do you?'

**7:29** And Moses fled at this word and became a foreigner in the land of Midian, where he fathered two sons.

**7:30** And when forty years were completed, an angel appeared to him in the desert of Mount Sinai in the fire of a flame of a bush.

**7:31** Now Moses, seeing it, was amazed at the sight. And as he drew near to look closely, the voice of the Lord came to him, saying,

**7:32** 'I am the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.' And Moses, trembling, did not dare to look closely.

**7:33** Then the Lord said to him, 'Loosen the footwear from your feet, for the place on which you are standing is holy ground.

**7:34** Seeing, I have seen the affliction of My people who are in Egypt, and I have heard their groaning, and I have come down to free them. And now come, and I will send you into Egypt.'

**7:35** This Moses, whom they disowned, saying, 'Who appointed you ruler and judge?' this very one God sent as ruler and redeemer, with the hand of the angel who appeared to him in the bush.

**7:36** This man led them out, performing wonders and signs in the land of Egypt, and in the Red Sea, and in the desert for forty years.

**7:37** This is the Moses who said to the sons of Israel, 'God will raise up a prophet for you from among your brothers, like me; to him you will listen.'

**7:38** This is the one who was in the assembly in the wilderness with the angel who spoke to him on Mount Sinai, and with our fathers, the one who received words of life to give to us.

**7:39** Our fathers were unwilling to obey him, but pushed him away and turned back in their hearts to Egypt,

**7:40** saying to Aaron, 'Make gods for us who will go before us; for as for this Moses who led us out from the land of Egypt, we do not know what has happened to him.'

**7:41** And they made a calf in those days, and offered a sacrifice to the idol, and were rejoicing in the works of their hands.

**7:42** But God turned away and handed them over to serve the host of heaven, just as it is written in the book of the prophets: 'Did you offer Me slaughtered animals and sacrifices for forty years in the desert, house of Israel?

**7:43** And you took up the tabernacle of Moloch and the star of your god Rempham, the images that you made in order to worship them; and I will carry you away beyond Babylon.'

**7:44** The tabernacle of the testimony was with our fathers in the desert, just as God ordained for them, speaking to Moses, that he should make it according to the pattern that he had seen.

**7:45** This also our fathers, having received it, brought in with Joshua into the possession of the nations whom God drove out from before the face of our fathers, until the days of David,

**7:46** who found favor before God and asked that he might find a tabernacle for the God of Jacob.

**7:47** Yet Solomon built a house for Him.

**7:48** But the Most High does not dwell in things made by hand, as the prophet says:

**7:49** 'Heaven is My throne, and the earth is the footstool of My feet. What house will you build for Me?' says the Lord, 'or what is the place of My rest?

**7:50** Has not My hand made all these things?'

**7:51** Stiff-necked and uncircumcised in hearts and ears, you always resist the Holy Spirit; just as your fathers did, so too do you.

**7:52** Which of the prophets did your fathers not persecute? And they killed those who announced beforehand concerning the coming of the Righteous One, of whom you have now been the betrayers and murderers,

**7:53** you who received the law by the ordinance of angels and did not keep it."

**7:54** Now on hearing these things, they were cut apart in their hearts, and they were gnashing their teeth at him.

**7:55** But he, being full of the Holy Spirit, gazing intently into heaven, saw the glory of God, and Jesus standing at the right hand of God. And he said, "Look, I see the heavens opened, and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God."

**7:57** Then crying out with a loud voice, they covered their ears and rushed upon him with one accord.

**7:58** And driving him out of the city, they were stoning him; and the witnesses laid down their garments beside the feet of a young man who was called Saul.

**7:59** And they were stoning Stephen as he called out and said, "Lord Jesus, receive my spirit."

**7:60** Then kneeling down, he cried out with a loud voice, saying, "Lord, do not charge this sin against them." And when he had said this, he fell asleep in the Lord. Now Saul was consenting to his killing.

## Translator Notes

- **7:6** — The Clementine Vulgate reads ei ("to him") where the Greek text has houtos ("thus"), introducing indirect discourse with Quia ("that") before shifting to direct speech in 7:7.
- **7:7** — The Clementine Vulgate reads Dominus ("the Lord") where the Greek text reads ho theos ("God").
- **7:16** — The Clementine Vulgate distinctly reads filii Sichem ("the son of Shechem"), whereas the Greek has tou en Sychem ("in Shechem," or in some manuscripts tou Sychem), and Genesis 33:19 calls Hamor the father of Shechem.
- **7:30** — The Clementine Vulgate reads in igne flammae rubi ("in the fire of a flame of a bush"), reversing the Greek noun order ("in a flame of fire of a bush").
- **7:38** — The Clementine Vulgate reads verba vitae ("words of life") where the Greek text has logia zonta ("living oracles").
- **7:43** — The Clementine Vulgate preserves the spelling Rempham for this deity's name, whereas critical Greek editions read Rephan (Rhaiphan).
- **7:45** — Latin Jesu here (like Greek Iesou) refers to Joshua, son of Nun, and is rendered with the standard English spelling "Joshua."
- **7:55** — In Clementine Vulgate versification, 7:55 includes Stephen's exclamation ("And he said..."), which modern editions number separately as 7:56.
- **7:60** — The Clementine Vulgate distinctly adds in Domino ("in the Lord") after obdormivit ("he fell asleep"), and includes the closing sentence about Saul, which modern editions place at 8:1a.
