## Text

**4:1** Now while they were speaking to the people, the priests and the magistrate of the temple and the Sadducees came upon them,

**4:2** being distressed because they were teaching the people and announcing in Jesus the resurrection from the dead;

**4:3** and they laid hands on them and placed them in custody until the next day, for it was already evening.

**4:4** Yet many of those who had heard the word believed; and the number of the men came to be five thousand.

**4:5** Now it came about on the next day that their rulers and elders and scribes were gathered together in Jerusalem,

**4:6** and Annas the high priest, and Caiaphas, and John, and Alexander, and as many as were of the priestly family.

**4:7** And setting them in the center, they questioned them, "By what power, or in what name, did you yourselves do this?"

**4:8** Then Peter, filled with the Holy Spirit, said to them, "Rulers of the people and elders, listen:

**4:9** if we today are being examined concerning a good deed done to an infirm man, by what means this man has been made well,

**4:10** let it be known to all of you, and to all the people of Israel, that in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ the Nazarene, whom you crucified, whom God raised from the dead, by Him this man stands before you well.

**4:11** This is the stone that was rejected by you builders, which has become the head of the corner;

**4:12** and salvation is not in anyone else; nor indeed is there any other name under heaven given to people in which we must be saved."

**4:13** Now seeing the courage of Peter and John, and having discovered that they were unlettered and ordinary men, they marveled, and they recognized them, that they had been with Jesus;

**4:14** and seeing also the man who had been healed standing with them, they could say nothing against it.

**4:15** So they ordered them to withdraw outside the council, and they conferred with one another,

**4:16** saying, "What shall we do with these men? For indeed a notable sign has been done through them; it is evident to all who live in Jerusalem, and we cannot deny it.

**4:17** But so that it may not spread further among the people, let us threaten them not to speak any longer in this name to any person."

**4:18** And summoning them, they charged them not to speak at all nor to teach in the name of Jesus.

**4:19** But Peter and John, answering, said to them, "Judge whether it is right in the sight of God to listen to you rather than to God.

**4:20** For we cannot keep from speaking the things we have seen and heard."

**4:21** And threatening them, they released them, finding no way to punish them because of the people, because all were glorifying what had been done in that which had happened.

**4:22** For the man on whom this sign of healing had been done was more than forty years old.

**4:23** And having been released, they came to their own people and reported to them all that the chief priests and elders had said to them.

**4:24** When they heard this, with one mind they lifted up their voice to God and said, "Lord, You are the One who made heaven and earth, the sea, and all things that are in them,

**4:25** who by the Holy Spirit through the mouth of our father David, Your servant, said, 'Why did the nations rage, and the peoples devise futile things?

**4:26** The kings of the earth took their stand, and the rulers gathered together as one against the Lord and against His Christ.'

**4:27** For truly in this city there gathered together against Your holy Servant Jesus, whom You anointed, Herod and Pontius Pilate, with the nations and the peoples of Israel,

**4:28** to do what Your hand and Your counsel decreed to take place.

**4:29** And now, Lord, look upon their threats, and grant to Your servants to speak Your word with all boldness,

**4:30** while You stretch out Your hand for healings, and for signs and wonders to be done through the name of Your holy Son Jesus."

**4:31** And when they had prayed, the place in which they were gathered together was shaken; and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit, and they spoke the word of God with boldness.

**4:32** Now the multitude of those who believed had one heart and one soul; and not one said that anything of what he possessed was his own, but everything was common to them.

**4:33** And with great power the apostles were giving testimony to the resurrection of Jesus Christ our Lord, and great grace was in all of them.

**4:34** For there was not anyone needy among them; for as many as were owners of fields or houses, selling them, brought the proceeds of what they were selling

**4:35** and placed them before the feet of the apostles; and it was distributed to each one just as each had need.

**4:36** Now Joseph, who was surnamed Barnabas by the apostles (which is translated, Son of Consolation), a Levite, a Cypriot by birth,

**4:37** since he owned a field, sold it, and brought the proceeds, and placed them before the feet of the apostles.

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The Vulgate gives the figure quinque millia ("five thousand") without the Greek qualifier "about."
- **4:10** — The Clementine Vulgate includes Domini nostri ("our Lord") before "Jesus Christ the Nazarene," an addition not found in the standard Greek text.
- **4:12** — The Latin salus ("salvation") and salvos fieri ("be saved") directly echo salvus factus est ("has been made well") in 4:9, linking physical restoration and spiritual salvation.
- **4:21** — The Vulgate reads clarificabant id quod factum fuerat in eo quod acciderat ("were glorifying what had been done in that which had happened"), without "God," whereas the Greek reads "were glorifying God for what had happened."
- **4:27** — Here the Vulgate translates Greek pais as puerum ("Servant," matching David pueri tui in 4:25), whereas in 4:30 it translates the same Greek title for Jesus as filii ("Son").
