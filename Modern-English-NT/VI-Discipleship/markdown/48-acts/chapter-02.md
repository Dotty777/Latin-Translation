## Text

**2:1** And when the days of Pentecost were being fulfilled, they were all together in the same place;

**2:2** and suddenly a sound came from heaven, as of a violent wind approaching, and it filled the whole house where they were sitting.

**2:3** And divided tongues, as of fire, appeared to them, and it sat on each one of them;

**2:4** and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit, and began to speak in various tongues, just as the Holy Spirit was giving them to speak out.

**2:5** Now there were Jews living in Jerusalem, devout men from every nation that is under heaven.

**2:6** And when this sound occurred, the crowd came together and was confused in mind, because each one was hearing them speaking in his own tongue.

**2:7** And they were all astounded and marveled, saying, "Look, are not all these who are speaking Galileans?

**2:8** And how have we each heard our own tongue in which we were born?

**2:9** Parthians, and Medes, and Elamites, and those who live in Mesopotamia, Judea, and Cappadocia, Pontus, and Asia,

**2:10** Phrygia, and Pamphylia, Egypt, and the parts of Libya around Cyrene, and visitors from Rome,

**2:11** Jews also, and proselytes, Cretans and Arabs: we have heard them speaking in our tongues the great deeds of God."

**2:12** And they were all astounded and marveled to one another, saying, "What can this mean?"

**2:13** But others, mocking, were saying, "These men are full of new wine."

**2:14** But Peter, standing with the eleven, raised his voice and spoke to them, "Jewish men, and all of you who live in Jerusalem, let this be known to you, and receive my words with your ears.

**2:15** For these men are not drunk, as you assume, since it is the third hour of the day;

**2:16** but this is what was spoken through the prophet Joel:

**2:17** 'And it will be in the last days,' says the Lord, 'I will pour out of My Spirit on all flesh; and your sons and your daughters will prophesy, and your young men will see visions, and your old men will dream dreams.

**2:18** And indeed, on My male servants and on My female servants in those days I will pour out of My Spirit, and they will prophesy;

**2:19** and I will give wonders in heaven above, and signs on the earth below: blood, and fire, and vapor of smoke;

**2:20** the sun will be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the great and manifest day of the Lord comes.

**2:21** And it will be that everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.'

**2:22** Men of Israel, listen to these words: Jesus the Nazarene, a man approved by God among you by mighty works, and wonders, and signs, which God performed through Him in your midst, just as you also know;

**2:23** Him, handed over by the determined plan and foreknowledge of God, you afflicted through the hands of wicked men and put to death;

**2:24** whom God raised up, having loosed the pains of the underworld, because it was impossible for Him to be held by it.

**2:25** For David says concerning Him, 'I kept seeing the Lord in My sight always, because He is at My right hand, so that I may not be shaken;

**2:26** for this reason My heart was glad, and My tongue exulted; moreover, My flesh also will rest in hope,

**2:27** because You will not abandon My soul in the underworld, nor will You allow Your Holy One to see decay.

**2:28** You have made known to Me the ways of life; and You will fill Me with gladness with Your face.'

**2:29** Men, brothers, may I be permitted to speak boldly to you concerning the patriarch David, that he died and was buried, and his tomb is with us to this present day.

**2:30** Therefore, since he was a prophet and knew that God had sworn to him with an oath that one from the fruit of his loins would sit on his throne,

**2:31** foreseeing this, he spoke concerning the resurrection of Christ, that He was neither abandoned in the underworld, nor did His flesh see decay.

**2:32** This Jesus God raised up, of whom all of us are witnesses.

**2:33** Therefore, having been exalted by the right hand of God, and having received the promise of the Holy Spirit from the Father, He has poured out this One, whom you see and hear.

**2:34** For David did not ascend into heaven, but he himself said, 'The Lord said to my Lord: Sit at My right hand,

**2:35** until I set Your enemies as a footstool for Your feet.'

**2:36** Therefore, let all the house of Israel know most certainly that God has made Him both Lord and Christ, this Jesus whom you crucified."

**2:37** Now when they heard these things, they were pierced in heart, and said to Peter and to the rest of the apostles, "What shall we do, men, brothers?"

**2:38** But Peter said to them, "Repent, and let each one of you be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.

**2:39** For the promise is for you, and for your children, and for all who are far away, as many as the Lord our God will call."

**2:40** With very many other words also he testified and was exhorting them, saying, "Be saved from this perverse generation."

**2:41** Therefore, those who received his word were baptized, and about three thousand souls were added on that day.

**2:42** And they were persevering in the teaching of the apostles, and in the communion of the breaking of bread, and in prayers.

**2:43** And fear was coming upon every soul; many wonders and signs also were being done through the apostles in Jerusalem, and there was great dread among all of them.

**2:44** Also all who believed were together and had all things in common.

**2:45** They were selling possessions and goods, and were dividing them to all, just as each one had need.

**2:46** Daily also, continuing steadfastly with one mind in the temple, and breaking bread from house to house, they were taking food with exultation and simplicity of heart,

**2:47** praising God and having favor with all the people. And the Lord was daily increasing together those who were being saved.

## Translator Notes

- **2:23** — The Clementine Vulgate reads 'affligentes' ("afflicting") rather than 'affigentes' ("fastening/nailing"), differing from the Greek 'prospexantes' ("having nailed [to a cross]").
- **2:24** — The Vulgate reads 'doloribus inferni' ("pains of the underworld," where Latin 'infernus' denotes the realm of the dead/hell), differing from the Greek text's "pangs of death."
- **2:33** — The Vulgate reads masculine 'hunc, quem' ("this One, whom"), referring personally to the Holy Spirit, whereas the Greek text uses the neuter "this which."
- **2:42** — The Vulgate links 'communicatione' with the genitive 'fractionis' ("the communion of the breaking of bread"), combining two terms that appear as separate coordinate nouns ("fellowship" and "the breaking of bread") in Greek.
- **2:43** — The Vulgate includes 'in Jerusalem' ("in Jerusalem") and the closing clause 'et metus erat magnus in universis' ("and there was great dread among all of them"), phrases absent from standard Greek editions.
