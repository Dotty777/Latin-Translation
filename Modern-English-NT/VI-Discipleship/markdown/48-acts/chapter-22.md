## Text

**22:1** "Men, brothers, and fathers, hear the defense that I now give to you."

**22:2** Now when they had heard that he was speaking to them in the Hebrew language, they kept silence all the more.

**22:3** And he said, "I am a Jewish man, born in Tarsus of Cilicia, yet brought up in this city, educated at the feet of Gamaliel according to the truth of the ancestral law, zealous for the law, just as you all are today;

**22:4** who persecuted this Way even to death, binding and handing over into prisons men and women,

**22:5** as the high priest bears witness for me, and all the elders; from whom also receiving letters to the brothers, I was proceeding to Damascus so that I might bring those who were bound from there to Jerusalem to be punished.

**22:6** Now it came about that as I was going and approaching Damascus at midday, suddenly an abundant light from heaven flashed around me;

**22:7** and falling to the ground, I heard a voice saying to me, 'Saul, Saul, why are you persecuting Me?'

**22:8** And I answered, 'Who are You, Lord?' And He said to me, 'I am Jesus the Nazarene, whom you are persecuting.'

**22:9** And those who were with me indeed saw the light, but they did not hear the voice of Him who was speaking with me.

**22:10** And I said, 'What shall I do, Lord?' And the Lord said to me, 'Get up and go to Damascus, and there you will be told about all the things that you must do.'

**22:11** And since I could not see because of the brightness of that light, being led by the hand by my companions, I came to Damascus.

**22:12** Now a certain Ananias, a man according to the law, attested by all the Jews living there,

**22:13** coming to me and standing beside me, said to me, 'Brother Saul, look up.' And in that same hour I looked up at him.

**22:14** And he said, 'The God of our fathers has preordained you to know His will, and to see the Just One, and to hear a voice from His mouth;

**22:15** because you will be His witness to all people of the things that you have seen and heard.

**22:16** And now why do you delay? Rise up and be baptized, and wash away your sins, calling upon His name.'

**22:17** Now it came about, when I returned to Jerusalem and was praying in the temple, that I fell into a trance

**22:18** and saw Him saying to me, 'Hurry, and go out quickly from Jerusalem, because they will not accept your testimony about Me.'

**22:19** And I said, 'Lord, they themselves know that I was locking up in prison and beating throughout the synagogues those who believed in You;

**22:20** and when the blood of Stephen Your witness was being shed, I myself was standing by and consenting, and guarding the garments of those who were killing him.'

**22:21** And He said to me, 'Go, because I will send you far away to the nations.'"

**22:22** Now they were listening to him up to this word, and they raised their voice, saying, "Remove such a man from the earth, for it is not right for him to live!"

**22:23** And as they were shouting loudly, and throwing off their garments, and tossing dust into the air,

**22:24** the tribune ordered him to be led into the barracks, and to be beaten with whips and tortured, so that he might know for what reason they were shouting at him in this way.

**22:25** And when they had bound him tightly with leather straps, Paul said to the centurion standing beside him, "Is it lawful for you to flog a man who is a Roman and uncondemned?"

**22:26** Hearing this, the centurion approached the tribune and reported to him, saying, "What are you about to do? For this man is a Roman citizen."

**22:27** And the tribune approached and said to him, "Tell me, are you a Roman?" And he said, "Yes."

**22:28** And the tribune answered, "With a large sum I acquired this citizenship." And Paul said, "But I was even born one."

**22:29** Immediately, therefore, those who were about to torture him withdrew from him. The tribune also became afraid after he learned that he was a Roman citizen, and because he had bound him.

**22:30** Now on the next day, wishing to know more accurately for what reason he was being accused by the Jews, he unbound him and ordered the priests and the whole council to assemble; and bringing Paul out, he set him among them.

## Translator Notes

- **22:3** — Vulgate distinctly reads 'juxta veritatem paternae legis' ('according to the truth of the ancestral law') and 'aemulator legis' ('zealous for the law'), where the Greek reads 'strictness' ('akribeian') and 'zealous for God' ('tou theou').
- **22:12** — Vulgate 'vir secundum legem' ('a man according to the law') omits the adjective 'devout' ('eulabes') present in the Greek text.
- **22:24** — Vulgate renders Greek 'mastixin anetazesthai' ('to be examined by scourging') with two explicit infinitives, 'flagellis caedi, et torqueri' ('to be beaten with whips and tortured'), echoed by 'torturi' in 22:29.
- **22:30** — Vulgate reads 'sacerdotes' ('the priests') rather than 'the chief priests' (Greek 'tous archiereis').
