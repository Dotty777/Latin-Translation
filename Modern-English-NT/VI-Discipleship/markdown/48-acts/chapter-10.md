## Text

**10:1** Now there was a certain man in Caesarea named Cornelius, a centurion of the cohort that is called Italian,

**10:2** devout, and fearing God with all his household, giving many charitable gifts to the people, and praying to God always.

**10:3** He saw clearly in a vision, about the ninth hour of the day, an angel of God coming in to him and saying to him, "Cornelius."

**10:4** And gazing intently at him, seized with fear, he said, "What is it, lord?" And he said to him, "Your prayers and your charitable gifts have ascended as a memorial in the sight of God.

**10:5** And now send men to Joppa and summon a certain Simon, who is surnamed Peter;

**10:6** he is lodging with a certain Simon, a tanner, whose house is beside the sea; he will tell you what you must do."

**10:7** And when the angel who was speaking to him had departed, he called two of his household servants and a soldier fearing the Lord from those who obeyed him.

**10:8** And when he had recounted everything to them, he sent them to Joppa.

**10:9** Now on the next day, while they were making their journey and approaching the city, Peter went up to the upper level to pray, about the sixth hour.

**10:10** And when he became hungry, he wanted to eat; but while they were making preparations, a trance fell upon him,

**10:11** and he saw heaven opened, and a certain vessel descending, like a large linen sheet, being lowered by four corners from heaven to the earth,

**10:12** in which were all four-footed animals and creeping things of the earth and birds of the sky.

**10:13** And a voice came to him, "Rise, Peter; kill and eat."

**10:14** But Peter said, "Far from it, Lord, because I have never eaten anything common and unclean."

**10:15** And a voice came to him again a second time, "What God has purified, you must not call common."

**10:16** Now this happened three times, and immediately the vessel was taken back into heaven.

**10:17** And while Peter was perplexed within himself as to what the vision that he had seen might be, look, the men who had been sent by Cornelius, inquiring for the house of Simon, stood at the gate.

**10:18** And when they had called out, they were asking whether Simon, who is surnamed Peter, had lodging there.

**10:19** Now while Peter was thinking about the vision, the Spirit said to him, "Look, three men are seeking you.

**10:20** Rise therefore, go down, and go with them, doubting nothing, because I have sent them."

**10:21** And Peter, going down to the men, said, "Look, I am the one whom you are seeking; what is the reason for which you have come?"

**10:22** And they said, "Cornelius, a centurion, a righteous man and fearing God, and having testimony from the entire nation of the Jews, received an answer from a holy angel to summon you into his house and to hear words from you."

**10:23** Bringing them in therefore, he received them as guests. And on the following day, rising up, he set out with them, and some of the brothers from Joppa accompanied him.

**10:24** And on the next day he entered Caesarea. Now Cornelius was waiting for them, having called together his relatives and close friends.

**10:25** And it happened that when Peter had entered, Cornelius came to meet him, and falling down at his feet, worshiped.

**10:26** But Peter lifted him up, saying, "Stand up; I myself am also a man."

**10:27** And speaking with him, he went inside and found many who had gathered together,

**10:28** and he said to them, "You know how abominable it is for a Jewish man to associate with or approach a foreigner; yet God has shown me to call no person common or unclean.

**10:29** For this reason I came without hesitation when summoned. I ask therefore, for what reason have you summoned me?"

**10:30** And Cornelius said, "Four days ago until this hour, I was praying at the ninth hour in my house, and look, a man stood before me in a bright white garment and said,

**10:31** 'Cornelius, your prayer has been heard, and your charitable gifts have been remembered in the sight of God.

**10:32** Send therefore to Joppa and summon Simon, who is surnamed Peter; he is lodging in the house of Simon the tanner beside the sea.'

**10:33** Immediately therefore I sent to you, and you have done well in coming. Now therefore we are all present in your sight to hear all things that have been commanded to you by the Lord."

**10:34** And Peter, opening his mouth, said, "In truth I have learned that God is not partial to persons;

**10:35** but in every nation, the one who fears Him and works righteousness is acceptable to Him.

**10:36** God sent the word to the sons of Israel, announcing peace through Jesus Christ (He is Lord of all).

**10:37** You know the word that came about throughout all Judea, for beginning from Galilee after the baptism that John preached,

**10:38** Jesus from Nazareth: how God anointed Him with the Holy Spirit and with power, who went about doing good and healing all who were oppressed by the devil, because God was with Him.

**10:39** And we are witnesses of all things that He did in the region of the Jews and in Jerusalem, whom they killed, hanging Him on a tree.

**10:40** God raised Him up on the third day and granted Him to become visible,

**10:41** not to all the people, but to witnesses appointed beforehand by God: to us, who ate and drank with Him after He rose from the dead.

**10:42** And He commanded us to preach to the people and to testify that He Himself is the One who has been appointed by God as judge of the living and the dead.

**10:43** To Him all the prophets bear witness that all who believe in Him receive remission of sins through His name."

**10:44** While Peter was still speaking these words, the Holy Spirit fell upon all who were hearing the word.

**10:45** And the believers from the circumcision who had come with Peter were astonished, because the grace of the Holy Spirit had been poured out even upon the nations.

**10:46** For they were hearing them speaking in tongues and magnifying God.

**10:47** Then Peter answered, "Can anyone withhold water so that these should not be baptized, who have received the Holy Spirit just as we also have?"

**10:48** And he commanded them to be baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ. Then they asked him to remain with them for some days.

## Translator Notes

- **10:6** — The Clementine Vulgate includes the closing clause "he will tell you what you must do" (hic dicet tibi quid te oporteat facere), which is absent from critical Greek editions.
- **10:28** — The Vulgate reads "abominable" (abominatum) rather than "unlawful" (Greek athemiton), expressing the strong ritual repulsion toward associating with a foreigner.
- **10:33** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "in your sight" (in conspectu tuo, referring to Peter), whereas most Greek manuscripts read "before God."
- **10:45** — The Vulgate reads "the grace of the Holy Spirit" (gratia Spiritus Sancti), whereas the Greek text reads "the gift of the Holy Spirit."
- **10:48** — The Clementine Vulgate preserves the full title "in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ" (in nomine Domini Jesu Christi), whereas critical Greek editions read "in the name of Jesus Christ" and the Byzantine tradition reads "in the name of the Lord."
