## Text

**6:1** In those days, as the number of the disciples was growing, a murmuring of the Greeks arose against the Hebrews, because their widows were being slighted in the daily ministry.

**6:2** So the twelve, summoning the multitude of the disciples, said, "It is not right for us to forsake the word of God and serve at tables.

**6:3** Therefore, brothers, look carefully for seven men from among you of good reputation, full of the Holy Spirit and of wisdom, whom we may appoint over this work.

**6:4** But we will remain devoted to prayer and to the ministry of the word."

**6:5** And the statement was pleasing before the whole multitude. And they chose Stephen, a man full of faith and of the Holy Spirit, and Philip, and Prochorus, and Nicanor, and Timon, and Parmenas, and Nicolaus, a proselyte from Antioch.

**6:6** These they set before the presence of the apostles, and praying, they laid hands on them.

**6:7** And the word of the Lord was growing, and the number of the disciples in Jerusalem was multiplying greatly; a large crowd of the priests was also obeying the faith.

**6:8** Now Stephen, full of grace and strength, was performing great wonders and signs among the people.

**6:9** But some from the synagogue which is called that of the Freedmen, and of the Cyrenians, and of the Alexandrians, and of those who were from Cilicia and Asia, rose up disputing with Stephen,

**6:10** and they were not able to resist the wisdom and the Spirit who was speaking.

**6:11** Then they secretly put forward men to say that they had heard him speaking words of blasphemy against Moses and against God.

**6:12** And so they stirred up the people, and the elders, and the scribes; and rushing together, they seized him and brought him into the council,

**6:13** and they set up false witnesses who said, "This man does not cease speaking words against the holy place and the law;

**6:14** for we have heard him saying that this Jesus of Nazareth will destroy this place and will change the traditions that Moses handed down to us."

**6:15** And gazing intently at him, all who were sitting in the council saw his face as the face of an angel.

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — Vulgate renders Greek "Hellēnistōn" (Greek-speaking Jews) literally as "Græcorum" ("of the Greeks") and uses the stronger verb "despicerentur" ("were being slighted" or "looked down upon") for Greek "paretheōrounto" ("were being overlooked").
- **6:5** — Vulgate renders Greek "prosēlyton" with the Latin calque "advenam" (lit. "newcomer" or "stranger"), the standard Vulgate biblical term for a Gentile convert or proselyte.
- **6:7** — Vulgate reads "verbum Domini" ("the word of the Lord"), where the Greek text reads "ho logos tou theou" ("the word of God").
- **6:10** — Vulgate reads nominative "qui loquebatur" ("the Spirit who was speaking"), making the Spirit the grammatical subject, whereas the Greek reads dative "hō elalei" ("the Spirit by which he [Stephen] was speaking").
