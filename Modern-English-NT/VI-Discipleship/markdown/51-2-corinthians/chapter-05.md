## Text

**5:1** For we know that if our earthly house of this dwelling is dissolved, we have a building from God, a house not made by hand, eternal in the heavens.

**5:2** For indeed in this we groan, longing to be clothed over with our dwelling that is from heaven,

**5:3** if indeed we are found clothed, not naked.

**5:4** For indeed we who are in this tent groan, weighed down, because we do not want to be stripped, but to be clothed over, so that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life.

**5:5** Now He who fashions us for this very thing is God, who has given us the pledge of the Spirit.

**5:6** Therefore, being always bold, knowing that while we are in the body we are away from the Lord

**5:7** (for we walk by faith, and not by appearance);

**5:8** we are indeed bold, and we have a ready will rather to be away from the body and to be present with the Lord.

**5:9** And therefore we strive, whether absent or present, to please Him.

**5:10** For we must all be manifested before the tribunal of Christ, so that each one may receive back the things proper to the body, according to what he has done, whether good or evil.

**5:11** Knowing, therefore, the fear of the Lord, we persuade people, yet to God we are manifest. And I hope that we are manifest in your consciences as well.

**5:12** We are not commending ourselves to you again, but giving you an occasion to boast on our behalf, so that you may have a response for those who boast in outward appearance, and not in heart.

**5:13** For whether we are out of our mind, it is for God; or whether we are sober-minded, it is for you.

**5:14** For the love of Christ impels us, judging this: that if One died for all, then all died;

**5:15** and Christ died for all, so that those who live as well may no longer live for themselves, but for Him who died for them and rose again.

**5:16** Consequently, from now on we know no one according to the flesh. Even if we have known Christ according to the flesh, yet now we no longer know Him so.

**5:17** If, therefore, anyone is a new creature in Christ, the old things have passed away; look, all things have become new.

**5:18** And all things are from God, who has reconciled us to Himself through Christ and has given to us the ministry of reconciliation,

**5:19** since indeed God was in Christ, reconciling the world to Himself, not counting their offenses against them, and He placed in us the word of reconciliation.

**5:20** On behalf of Christ, therefore, we serve as ambassadors, as though God were exhorting through us. We plead on behalf of Christ: be reconciled to God.

**5:21** Him who did not know sin, He made to be sin for us, so that we might be made the righteousness of God in Him.

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — The Vulgate reads vestiti ("clothed"), aligning with the textual tradition "having put it on" rather than the variant "unclothed."
- **5:7** — Latin species literally denotes "visible appearance" or "form" (often translated "sight"), contrasting outward visible reality with faith.
- **5:10** — The Vulgate reads propria corporis ("the things proper to the body"), reflecting an ancient textual variant rather than the standard Greek "the things done through the body."
- **5:14** — The Clementine Vulgate includes si ("if") in 5:14 ("if One died for all") and explicitly names Christus ("Christ") in 5:15, both absent from critical Greek texts.
- **5:17** — The Vulgate punctuates Si qua ergo in Christo nova creatura as a single conditional clause ("If, therefore, anyone is a new creature in Christ") and adds omnia ("all things") in the closing clause.
