## Text

**6:1** And as helpers, we exhort you not to receive the grace of God in vain.

**6:2** For He says, "At an accepted time I heard you, and on the day of salvation I helped you." Look, now is the acceptable time; look, now is the day of salvation.

**6:3** Not giving any offense to anyone, so that our ministry may not be censured,

**6:4** but in all things let us present ourselves as ministers of God: in much endurance, in afflictions, in hardships, in distresses,

**6:5** in beatings, in prisons, in riots, in labors, in vigils, in fastings,

**6:6** in chastity, in knowledge, in patience, in gentleness, in the Holy Spirit, in love without pretense,

**6:7** in the word of truth, in the power of God, through the weapons of righteousness on the right and on the left,

**6:8** through glory and dishonor, through bad reputation and good reputation; as deceivers, yet truthful; as those who are unknown, yet known;

**6:9** as dying, and look, we live; as disciplined, yet not put to death;

**6:10** as sorrowful, yet always rejoicing; as needy, yet enriching many; as having nothing, yet possessing all things.

**6:11** Our mouth stands open to you, Corinthians; our heart has been opened wide.

**6:12** You are not constricted in us, but you are constricted in your own inward affections.

**6:13** Now having the same recompense (I speak as to children), be opened wide yourselves as well.

**6:14** Do not bear the yoke with unbelievers. For what sharing does righteousness have with iniquity? Or what companionship does light have with darkness?

**6:15** And what agreement does Christ have with Belial? Or what share does a believer have with an unbeliever?

**6:16** And what harmony does the temple of God have with idols? For you are the temple of the living God, just as God says: "I will dwell in them, and I will walk among them, and I will be their God, and they will be a people to Me."

**6:17** "For this reason, come out from their midst and be separated," says the Lord, "and do not touch what is unclean,

**6:18** and I will receive you; and I will be a Father to you, and you will be sons and daughters to Me," says the Lord Almighty.

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The Vulgate renders the opening participle as Adjuvantes ("as helpers" or "assisting") without specifying an object, whereas the Greek means "working together."
- **6:4** — The Vulgate shifts to a hortatory subjunctive exhibeamus ("let us present") where the Greek continues with a participle ("commending").
- **6:14** — The Vulgate idiom Nolite jugum ducere ("Do not bear the yoke") omits the element "unequally" present in the Greek compound verb.
- **6:16** — The Clementine Vulgate reads second-person plural vos enim estis ("For you are") where critical Greek texts read first-person plural ("For we are").
