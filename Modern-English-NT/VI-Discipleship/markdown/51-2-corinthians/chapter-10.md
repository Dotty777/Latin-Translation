## Text

**10:1** Now I myself, Paul, implore you by the gentleness and moderation of Christ, I who am indeed lowly among you face to face, yet while absent am confident toward you.

**10:2** And I ask you that when present I may not be bold with that confidence by which I am thought to be bold against certain ones, who regard us as though we walk according to the flesh.

**10:3** For while walking in the flesh, we do not wage war according to the flesh.

**10:4** For the weapons of our warfare are not fleshly, but powerful before God for the destruction of strongholds, destroying plans,

**10:5** and every height exalting itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing every understanding into captivity for obedience to Christ,

**10:6** and being in readiness to punish every disobedience, once your obedience has been fulfilled.

**10:7** Look at the things that are according to appearance. If anyone is confident in himself that he is Christ's, let him consider this again within himself: that just as he is Christ's, so also are we.

**10:8** For even if I should boast somewhat more about our authority, which the Lord gave us for building up and not for your destruction, I will not be ashamed.

**10:9** Yet so that I may not be thought as though to terrify you through letters:

**10:10** for indeed, "The letters," they say, "are weighty and strong, yet the bodily presence is weak, and the speech contemptible":

**10:11** let such a person consider this, that what we are in word through letters while absent, such we also are when present in deed.

**10:12** For we do not dare to rank or compare ourselves with certain ones who commend themselves; instead, we ourselves, measuring ourselves in ourselves and comparing ourselves with ourselves.

**10:13** Yet we will not boast beyond measure, but according to the measure of the rule by which God has measured to us, a measure of reaching all the way to you.

**10:14** For we do not overextend ourselves as though not reaching to you, for we arrived all the way to you in the gospel of Christ,

**10:15** not boasting beyond measure in others' labors, yet having hope of your growing faith, to be magnified among you according to our rule to abundance,

**10:16** to preach the gospel even in those places that are beyond you, not to boast within another's rule in the things that have been prepared.

**10:17** Yet whoever boasts, let him boast in the Lord.

**10:18** For it is not the one who commends himself who is approved, but the one whom God commends.

## Translator Notes

- **10:2** — Latin reads passive existimor ("I am thought"), whereas the Greek has middle logizomai ("I reckon" or "plan").
- **10:10** — Vulgate reads plural inquiunt ("they say"), whereas most Greek witnesses have singular phesin ("one says").
- **10:12** — Following the Western and Vulgate textual tradition, the Latin uses first-person plural pronouns ("we ourselves, measuring ourselves in ourselves") and omits the Greek clause "they are without understanding."
- **10:18** — Vulgate reads Deus ("God"), whereas the Greek reads ho Kyrios ("the Lord").
