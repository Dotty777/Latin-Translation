## Text

**14:1** "Do not let your heart be troubled. You believe in God; believe also in Me.

**14:2** In My Father's house there are many dwelling places; if it were not so, I would have told you, because I am going to prepare a place for you.

**14:3** And if I go away and prepare a place for you, I am coming again and will receive you to Myself, so that where I am, you also may be.

**14:4** And you know where I am going, and you know the way."

**14:5** Thomas said to Him, "Lord, we do not know where You are going; and how can we know the way?"

**14:6** Jesus said to him, "I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father unless through Me.

**14:7** If you had known Me, you would certainly have known My Father as well; and from now on you will know Him, and you have seen Him."

**14:8** Philip said to Him, "Lord, show us the Father, and it is enough for us."

**14:9** Jesus said to him, "Have I been with you for so long a time, and you have not known Me? Philip, the one who sees Me sees the Father also. How do you say, 'Show us the Father'?

**14:10** Do you not believe that I am in the Father, and the Father is in Me? The words that I speak to you I do not speak from Myself; rather, the Father remaining in Me, He Himself has done the works.

**14:11** Do you not believe that I am in the Father, and the Father is in Me?

**14:12** Otherwise, believe because of the works themselves. Amen, amen, I tell you, the one who believes in Me will also do the works that I do, and he will do greater than these, because I am going to the Father.

**14:13** And whatever you ask the Father in My name, this I will do, so that the Father may be glorified in the Son.

**14:14** If you ask Me anything in My name, this I will do.

**14:15** If you love Me, keep My commandments;

**14:16** and I will ask the Father, and He will give you another Paraclete, so that He may remain with you forever,

**14:17** the Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot receive, because it neither sees Him nor knows Him; but you will know Him, because He will remain with you and will be in you.

**14:18** I will not leave you orphans; I will come to you.

**14:19** A little while longer, and the world sees Me no longer. But you see Me; because I live, you also will live.

**14:20** On that day you will know that I am in My Father, and you in Me, and I in you.

**14:21** The one who has My commandments and keeps them is the one who loves Me. And the one who loves Me will be loved by My Father, and I will love him and manifest Myself to him."

**14:22** Judas, not the Iscariot, said to Him, "Lord, what has happened that You are about to manifest Yourself to us, and not to the world?"

**14:23** Jesus answered and said to him, "If anyone loves Me, he will keep My word, and My Father will love him, and We will come to him and make a dwelling place with him;

**14:24** the one who does not love Me does not keep My words. And the word that you have heard is not Mine, but His who sent Me, the Father.

**14:25** These things I have spoken to you while remaining with you.

**14:26** But the Paraclete, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in My name, He will teach you all things and remind you of everything that I have said to you.

**14:27** Peace I leave to you; My peace I give to you. I do not give to you in the way the world gives. Do not let your heart be troubled, nor be frightened.

**14:28** You have heard that I said to you, 'I am going away, and I am coming to you.' If you loved Me, you would certainly rejoice because I am going to the Father, for the Father is greater than I.

**14:29** And now I have told you before it takes place, so that when it has taken place, you may believe.

**14:30** I will no longer speak many things with you, for the ruler of this world is coming, and he has nothing in Me.

**14:31** But so that the world may know that I love the Father, and just as the Father gave Me a commandment, so I do. Rise, let us go from here."

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — Unlike the ambiguous Greek verb form, the Vulgate explicitly distinguishes indicative Creditis ("You believe") from imperative credite ("believe").
- **14:2** — Latin mansiones ("dwelling places") is cognate with manere ("remain") and mansionem ("a dwelling place") in 14:23. Following Clementine Vulgate punctuation, the second half reads as a statement ("if it were not so, I would have told you, because...") rather than a rhetorical question.
- **14:10** — The Clementine Vulgate reads the perfect tense fecit ("has done") with unadorned opera ("the works"), and uses plural creditis ("do you not believe") in both 14:10 and 14:11.
- **14:11** — The Clementine Vulgate repeats the question of 14:10a (Non creditis, "Do you not believe") rather than an imperative ("Believe Me"), and places alioquin propter opera ipsa credite ("Otherwise, believe because of the works themselves") at the beginning of 14:12.
- **14:16** — The Vulgate retains the Greek loanword Paraclitus ("Paraclete," meaning Advocate, Helper, or Comforter) here and in 14:26, unlike its use of advocatus in 1 John 2:1.
