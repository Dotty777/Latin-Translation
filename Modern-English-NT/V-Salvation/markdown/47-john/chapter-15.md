## Text

**15:1** "I am the true vine, and My Father is the farmer.

**15:2** Every branch in Me not bearing fruit, He will take it away, and every one that bears fruit, He will prune it, so that it may yield more fruit.

**15:3** You are clean already on account of the word that I have spoken to you.

**15:4** Remain in Me, and I in you. Just as a branch cannot bear fruit from itself unless it remains in the vine, so neither can you unless you remain in Me.

**15:5** I am the vine; you are the branches. The one who remains in Me, and I in him, he bears much fruit, because without Me you can do nothing.

**15:6** If anyone does not remain in Me, he will be thrown out like a branch and will wither; and they will gather him and throw him into the fire, and he burns.

**15:7** If you remain in Me, and My words remain in you, you will ask for whatever you wish, and it will be done for you.

**15:8** In this My Father has been glorified, that you yield very much fruit and become My disciples.

**15:9** Just as the Father loved Me, I also loved you. Remain in My love.

**15:10** If you keep My commandments, you will remain in My love, just as I also have kept My Father's commandments and remain in His love.

**15:11** These things I have spoken to you, so that My joy may be in you and your joy may be made full.

**15:12** This is My commandment: that you love each other as I loved you.

**15:13** No one has greater love than this: that a person lay down his life for his friends.

**15:14** You are My friends if you do the things that I command you.

**15:15** I will no longer call you servants, because a servant does not know what his master is doing. But I have called you friends, because all things that I have heard from My Father I have made known to you.

**15:16** You did not choose Me; rather, I chose you and appointed you so that you may go and yield fruit, and your fruit may remain, so that whatever you ask the Father in My name, He may give to you.

**15:17** I command you these things: that you love each other.

**15:18** If the world hates you, know that it hated Me before you.

**15:19** If you had been of the world, the world would love what was its own; yet because you are not of the world, but I chose you out of the world, for this reason the world hates you.

**15:20** Remember My word that I told you: 'A servant is not greater than his master.' If they persecuted Me, they will persecute you as well; if they kept My word, they will keep yours as well.

**15:21** But they will do all these things to you on account of My name, because they do not know Him who sent Me.

**15:22** If I had not come and spoken to them, they would have no sin; yet now they have no excuse for their sin.

**15:23** Whoever hates Me hates My Father as well.

**15:24** If I had not done among them the works that no one else did, they would have no sin; yet now they have both seen and hated both Me and My Father.

**15:25** But this is so that the word written in their law may be fulfilled: 'They hated Me without cause.'

**15:26** But when the Paraclete comes, whom I will send to you from the Father, the Spirit of truth who proceeds from the Father, He will bear witness about Me;

**15:27** and you will bear witness, because you have been with Me from the beginning."

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — Latin tollet ("will take away") and purgabit ("will prune" or "will cleanse") are future tense in the Vulgate, whereas the Greek uses present-tense verbs. Latin purgabit denotes both viticultural pruning and cleansing, connecting conceptually with mundi ("clean") in verse 3.
- **15:6** — The Vulgate uses future verbs (mittetur, arescet, colligent, mittent) with the singular pronoun eum ("him," or "it" referring to the branch, where the Greek has plural "them"), followed by an abrupt shift to the present tense ardet ("burns").
- **15:15** — Latin dicam ("I will... call") is future tense in the Vulgate, differing from the present indicative ("I call") in the Greek text.
- **15:26** — Latin Paraclitus retains the Greek loanword Parakletos, meaning "Advocate," "Counselor," or "Helper" (likewise in 16:7).
