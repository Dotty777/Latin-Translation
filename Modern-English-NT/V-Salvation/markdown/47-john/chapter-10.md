## Text

**10:1** "Amen, amen, I tell you, the one who does not enter through the door into the fold of the sheep, but climbs up from another place, that one is a thief and a robber.

**10:2** But the one who enters through the door is the shepherd of the sheep.

**10:3** For him the doorkeeper opens, and the sheep hear his voice, and he calls his own sheep by name and brings them out.

**10:4** And when he has sent out his own sheep, he goes ahead of them, and the sheep follow him, because they know his voice.

**10:5** A stranger, however, they do not follow, but flee from him, because they do not know the voice of strangers."

**10:6** Jesus told them this proverb, but they did not understand what He was speaking to them.

**10:7** Therefore Jesus said to them again, "Amen, amen, I tell you that I am the door of the sheep.

**10:8** All, as many as came, are thieves and robbers, and the sheep did not listen to them.

**10:9** I am the door. If anyone enters through Me, he will be saved, and he will go in and go out, and he will find pastures.

**10:10** The thief does not come except to steal and slaughter and destroy. I have come so that they may have life, and have it more abundantly.

**10:11** I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd gives his life for his sheep.

**10:12** But the hired worker, who is not a shepherd and whose own the sheep are not, sees the wolf coming and abandons the sheep and flees, and the wolf snatches and scatters the sheep.

**10:13** Now the hired worker flees because he is a hired worker, and the sheep are of no concern to him.

**10:14** I am the good shepherd; and I know My own, and My own know Me.

**10:15** Just as the Father knows Me, I also know the Father; and I lay down My life for My sheep.

**10:16** And I have other sheep that are not from this fold; I must bring those also, and they will hear My voice, and there will be one fold and one shepherd.

**10:17** Therefore the Father loves Me, because I lay down My life so that I may take it up again.

**10:18** No one takes it away from Me, but I lay it down of Myself; and I have authority to lay it down, and I have authority to take it up again. I received this command from My Father."

**10:19** Again a division arose among the Jews on account of these words.

**10:20** Now many among them were saying, "He has a demon and is insane; why are you listening to Him?"

**10:21** Others were saying, "These words are not those of one who has a demon. Can a demon really open the eyes of the blind?"

**10:22** Now the Festival of Dedication took place in Jerusalem, and it was winter.

**10:23** And Jesus was walking in the temple, in Solomon's portico.

**10:24** Therefore the Jews surrounded Him and were saying to Him, "How long are You keeping our soul in suspense? If You are the Christ, tell us plainly."

**10:25** Jesus answered them, "I speak to you, and you do not believe. The works that I do in My Father's name, these bear witness about Me.

**10:26** But you do not believe, because you are not among My sheep.

**10:27** My sheep hear My voice, and I know them, and they follow Me;

**10:28** and I give them eternal life, and they will never perish, and no one will snatch them from My hand.

**10:29** What My Father has given Me is greater than all, and no one can snatch it from the hand of My Father.

**10:30** I and the Father are one."

**10:31** Therefore the Jews picked up stones to stone Him.

**10:32** Jesus answered them, "Many good works I have shown you from My Father; on account of which of those works are you stoning Me?"

**10:33** The Jews answered Him, "We are not stoning You for a good work, but for blasphemy, and because You, though You are a man, make Yourself God."

**10:34** Jesus answered them, "Is it not written in your law, 'I have said, you are gods'?

**10:35** If he called them gods, to whom the word of God came, and the Scripture cannot be broken,

**10:36** of Him whom the Father sanctified and sent into the world, do you say, 'You are blaspheming,' because I said, 'I am the Son of God'?

**10:37** If I am not doing the works of My Father, do not believe Me.

**10:38** But if I am doing them, even if you are not willing to believe Me, believe the works, so that you may know and believe that the Father is in Me, and I in the Father."

**10:39** Therefore they were seeking to seize Him, and He escaped from their hands.

**10:40** And He went away again across the Jordan, to that place where John was baptizing at first, and He stayed there.

**10:41** And many came to Him and were saying, "John indeed performed no sign.

**10:42** Yet all things that John said about this man were true." And many believed in Him.

## Translator Notes

- **10:8** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "All, as many as came" (Omnes quotquot venerunt), omitting the phrase "before Me" found in many Greek manuscripts.
- **10:11** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "gives" (dat) and "his sheep" (ovibus suis), whereas the Greek uses the same verb "lays down" as in verses 15, 17, and 18 and reads "the sheep."
- **10:16** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "one fold" (unum ovile), repeating ovile from earlier in the verse, whereas the Greek distinguishes "fold" from "one flock."
- **10:24** — Literally "How long are You lifting up (or taking away) our soul?" (Quousque animam nostram tollis?), a literal Latin rendering of the Greek idiom for holding someone in suspense.
- **10:29** — The Clementine Vulgate uses the neuter singular "What My Father has given Me is greater than all" (quod ... majus), matching Codex Vaticanus rather than the masculine variant "My Father, who has given them to Me, is greater than all."
