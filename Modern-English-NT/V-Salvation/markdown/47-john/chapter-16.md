## Text

**16:1** "These things I have spoken to you so that you may not be made to stumble.

**16:2** They will exclude you from the synagogues; but an hour is coming when everyone who kills you will suppose that he is rendering service to God.

**16:3** And they will do these things to you, because they have not known the Father, nor Me.

**16:4** But these things I have spoken to you so that when their hour comes, you may remember that I told you.

**16:5** Yet I did not tell you these things from the beginning, because I was with you. And now I am going to Him who sent Me, and none of you questions Me, 'Where are You going?'

**16:6** But because I have spoken these things to you, sorrow has filled your heart.

**16:7** Yet I tell you the truth: it is to your advantage that I go; for if I do not depart, the Paraclete will not come to you, but if I depart, I will send Him to you.

**16:8** And when He comes, He will convict the world concerning sin, and concerning righteousness, and concerning judgment.

**16:9** Concerning sin, because they have not believed in Me.

**16:10** Concerning righteousness, because I am going to the Father, and you will no longer see Me.

**16:11** And concerning judgment, because the ruler of this world has already been judged.

**16:12** I still have many things to tell you, yet you cannot bear them at present.

**16:13** But when He, the Spirit of truth, comes, He will teach you all truth; for He will not speak from Himself, but whatever He hears He will speak, and He will declare to you the things that are to come.

**16:14** He will glorify Me, because He will receive from what is Mine and declare it to you.

**16:15** All things that the Father has are Mine. For this reason I said that He will receive from what is Mine and declare it to you.

**16:16** A little while, and you will no longer see Me; and again a little while, and you will see Me, because I am going to the Father."

**16:17** Then some of His disciples said to each other, "What is this that He is saying to us, 'A little while, and you will not see Me; and again a little while, and you will see Me,' and, 'Because I am going to the Father'?"

**16:18** So they were saying, "What is this that He says, 'A little while'? We do not know what He is speaking about."

**16:19** Now Jesus knew that they wanted to question Him, and He said to them, "You are inquiring among yourselves about this, because I said, 'A little while, and you will not see Me; and again a little while, and you will see Me.'

**16:20** Amen, amen, I tell you, you will wail and weep, while the world will rejoice; you will be sorrowful, yet your sorrow will be turned into joy.

**16:21** When a woman is giving birth, she has sorrow, because her hour has come; but when she has given birth to the child, she no longer remembers the distress on account of the joy that a human being has been born into the world.

**16:22** Therefore you also have sorrow now; yet I will see you again, and your heart will rejoice, and no one will take your joy from you.

**16:23** And on that day you will not ask Me anything. Amen, amen, I tell you, if you ask the Father for anything in My name, He will give it to you.

**16:24** Up to now you have not asked for anything in My name; ask, and you will receive, so that your joy may be full.

**16:25** These things I have spoken to you in proverbs. An hour is coming when I will no longer speak to you in proverbs, but will declare to you plainly concerning the Father.

**16:26** On that day you will ask in My name; and I do not say to you that I will ask the Father on your behalf,

**16:27** for the Father Himself loves you, because you have loved Me and have believed that I came forth from God.

**16:28** I came forth from the Father and came into the world; again, I am leaving the world and going to the Father."

**16:29** His disciples say to Him, "Look, now You are speaking plainly and saying no proverb;

**16:30** now we know that You know all things and have no need for anyone to question You; by this we believe that You came forth from God."

**16:31** Jesus replied to them, "Do you believe now?

**16:32** Look, an hour is coming, and has already come, when you will be scattered, each one to his own home, and will leave Me alone; yet I am not alone, because the Father is with Me.

**16:33** These things I have spoken to you so that in Me you may have peace. In the world you will have distress; but take courage, I have conquered the world."

## Translator Notes

- **16:5** — The opening sentence ("Yet I did not tell you these things from the beginning, because I was with you") begins verse 5 in the Clementine Vulgate, whereas modern Greek editions place it at the end of verse 4.
- **16:9** — The Vulgate reads the perfect tense non crediderunt ("they have not believed"), contrasting with the present tense ("they do not believe") in the Greek text.
- **16:13** — The Vulgate reads docebit vos omnem veritatem ("He will teach you all truth"), a distinctive Latin rendering compared to the Greek "He will guide you into all the truth."
- **16:16** — The Vulgate includes the closing clause quia vado ad Patrem ("because I am going to the Father"), which is omitted in many modern critical Greek editions here though echoed by the disciples in verse 17.
- **16:25** — Latin proverbia (translating Greek paroimiai) refers here to veiled or figurative sayings rather than simple maxims; similarly in verse 29.
