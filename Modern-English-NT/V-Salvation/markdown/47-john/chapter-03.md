## Text

**3:1** Now there was a man from the Pharisees, Nicodemus by name, a ruler of the Jews.

**3:2** This man came to Jesus by night and said to Him, "Rabbi, we know that You have come from God as a teacher, for no one can perform these signs that You perform unless God is with him."

**3:3** Jesus answered and said to him, "Amen, amen, I tell you, unless someone is reborn anew, he cannot see the kingdom of God."

**3:4** Nicodemus said to Him, "How can a man be born when he is old? Can he really enter into his mother's womb a second time and be reborn?"

**3:5** Jesus answered, "Amen, amen, I tell you, unless someone is reborn from water and the Holy Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God.

**3:6** What has been born from the flesh is flesh, and what has been born from the Spirit is spirit.

**3:7** Do not be astonished that I said to you, 'You must be born anew.'

**3:8** The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, yet you do not know where it comes from or where it goes; so is everyone who has been born from the Spirit."

**3:9** Nicodemus answered and said to Him, "How can these things happen?"

**3:10** Jesus answered and said to him, "You are a teacher in Israel, and you do not know these things?

**3:11** Amen, amen, I tell you that we speak what we know, and we testify to what we have seen, and you do not receive our testimony.

**3:12** If I have spoken earthly things to you and you do not believe, how will you believe if I speak heavenly things to you?

**3:13** And no one has ascended into heaven except the One who descended from heaven, the Son of Man, who is in heaven.

**3:14** And just as Moses lifted up the serpent in the desert, in the same way the Son of Man must be lifted up,

**3:15** so that everyone who believes in Him may not perish, but may have eternal life.

**3:16** For God loved the world in such a way that He gave His only-begotten Son, so that everyone who believes in Him may not perish, but may have eternal life.

**3:17** For God did not send His Son into the world that He might judge the world, but that the world might be saved through Him.

**3:18** The one who believes in Him is not judged; the one who does not believe, however, has already been judged, because he does not believe in the name of the only-begotten Son of God.

**3:19** Now this is the judgment: that the light has come into the world, and people loved the darkness more than the light, for their works were evil.

**3:20** For everyone who does evil hates the light and does not come to the light, so that his works may not be reproved;

**3:21** yet the one who does the truth comes to the light, so that his works may be made manifest, because they have been done in God."

**3:22** After these things Jesus and His disciples came into the land of Judea, and there He was staying with them and was baptizing.

**3:23** Now John also was baptizing in Aenon, near Salim, because many waters were there, and people were coming and being baptized.

**3:24** For John had not yet been put into prison.

**3:25** Now a dispute arose on the part of John's disciples with the Jews concerning purification.

**3:26** And they came to John and said to him, "Rabbi, the One who was with you across the Jordan, to whom you bore witness, look, He is baptizing, and everyone is coming to Him."

**3:27** John answered and said, "A man cannot receive anything unless it has been given to him from heaven.

**3:28** You yourselves bear witness for me that I said, 'I am not the Christ,' but that I have been sent ahead of Him.

**3:29** The one who has the bride is the bridegroom; the friend of the bridegroom, however, who stands and hears him, rejoices with joy because of the bridegroom's voice. Therefore this joy of mine has been fulfilled.

**3:30** He must increase, while I must decrease.

**3:31** The One who comes from above is above all. The one who is from the earth is from the earth, and speaks from the earth. The One who comes from heaven is above all.

**3:32** And what He has seen and heard, to this He testifies, and no one receives His testimony.

**3:33** The one who has received His testimony has set his seal that God is truthful.

**3:34** For the One whom God has sent speaks the words of God, for God does not give the Spirit by measure.

**3:35** The Father loves the Son and has given everything into His hand.

**3:36** The one who believes in the Son has eternal life; yet the one who disbelieves the Son will not see life, but the wrath of God remains upon him."

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — The Vulgate retains the Hebrew affirmation "Amen, amen" (meaning "truly, truly") and renders Greek "anothen" in its temporal sense as "denuo" ("anew/again"), paired with "renatus" ("reborn"), rather than "from above."
- **3:5** — The Vulgate explicitly reads "Spiritu Sancto" ("the Holy Spirit"), whereas most modern Greek editions read simply "Spirit."
- **3:8** — Latin "Spiritus ... spirat ... vocem" preserves a deliberate wordplay that can be read both as "the wind blows ... its sound" and as "the Spirit breathes ... His voice," echoing "born from the Spirit" at the end of the verse.
- **3:11** — The first-person plurals (scimus, loquimur, testamur, nostrum) are left lowercase because the text does not state whom Jesus includes with Himself.
- **3:13** — The Vulgate includes the closing clause "qui est in caelo" ("who is in heaven"), which is omitted in many modern Greek critical editions.
- **3:31** — No change of speaker is marked in the Latin text here; while John the Baptist's speech is kept open through 3:36, many interpreters end his direct speech at 3:30 and read 3:31-36 as the Evangelist's commentary.
