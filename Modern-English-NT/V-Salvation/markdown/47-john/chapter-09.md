## Text

**9:1** And passing by, Jesus saw a man blind from birth.

**9:2** And His disciples asked Him, "Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, so that he was born blind?"

**9:3** Jesus answered, "Neither did this man sin nor his parents, but so that the works of God may be manifested in him.

**9:4** I must work the works of Him who sent Me while it is day; night is coming, when no one is able to work.

**9:5** As long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world."

**9:6** When He had said these things, He spat on the ground, and made mud from the saliva, and spread the mud over his eyes.

**9:7** And He said to him, "Go, wash in the pool of Siloam" (which is interpreted "Sent"). Therefore he went away, and washed, and came back seeing.

**9:8** Accordingly, the neighbors and those who had previously seen that he was a beggar were saying, "Is this not the one who used to sit and beg?" Others were saying, "This is he."

**9:9** Others, however, were saying, "Not at all, but he resembles him." Yet he kept saying, "I am he."

**9:10** Therefore they were saying to him, "How were your eyes opened?"

**9:11** He answered, "That man who is called Jesus made mud, and anointed my eyes, and said to me, 'Go to the pool of Siloam and wash.' And I went away, and washed, and I see."

**9:12** And they said to him, "Where is He?" He said, "I do not know."

**9:13** They bring the one who had been blind to the Pharisees.

**9:14** Now it was the Sabbath when Jesus made the mud and opened his eyes.

**9:15** Therefore the Pharisees were asking him again how he had seen. And he said to them, "He placed mud on my eyes, and I washed, and I see."

**9:16** Therefore some of the Pharisees were saying, "This man who does not keep the Sabbath is not from God." Others, however, were saying, "How can a sinful man do these signs?" And there was a division among them.

**9:17** Therefore they say to the blind man again, "What do you say about Him who opened your eyes?" And he said, "He is a prophet."

**9:18** Therefore the Jews did not believe concerning him that he had been blind and had seen, until they called the parents of the one who had seen.

**9:19** And they asked them, saying, "Is this your son, whom you say was born blind? How then does he see now?"

**9:20** His parents replied to them and said, "We know that this is our son and that he was born blind;

**9:21** but how he sees now, we do not know; or who opened his eyes, we ourselves do not know. Ask him; he is of age; let him speak for himself."

**9:22** His parents said these things because they feared the Jews; for the Jews had already conspired that if anyone confessed Him to be the Christ, he would be put out of the synagogue.

**9:23** Because of this, his parents said, "He is of age; ask him."

**9:24** Therefore they again called the man who had been blind, and said to him, "Give glory to God; we know that this man is a sinner."

**9:25** Therefore he said to them, "Whether He is a sinner, I do not know; one thing I know, that although I was blind, I now see."

**9:26** Therefore they said to him, "What did He do to you? How did He open your eyes?"

**9:27** He answered them, "I have already told you, and you heard; why do you want to hear it again? You do not also want to become His disciples, do you?"

**9:28** Therefore they cursed him and said, "You be His disciple; we, however, are disciples of Moses.

**9:29** We know that God spoke to Moses, yet as for this one, we do not know where He is from."

**9:30** That man answered and said to them, "Indeed, in this is an astonishing thing, that you do not know where He is from, and He opened my eyes.

**9:31** Now we know that God does not hear sinners; rather, if anyone is a worshiper of God and does His will, He listens to him.

**9:32** Since time began, it has not been heard that anyone opened the eyes of one born blind.

**9:33** If He were not from God, He could not do anything."

**9:34** They answered and said to him, "You were born completely in sins, and are you teaching us?" And they threw him outside.

**9:35** Jesus heard that they had thrown him outside, and when He had found him, He said to him, "Do you believe in the Son of God?"

**9:36** He answered and said, "Who is He, Lord, so that I may believe in Him?"

**9:37** And Jesus said to him, "You have both seen Him, and the one who speaks with you is He Himself."

**9:38** And he said, "I believe, Lord." And falling prostrate, he worshiped Him.

**9:39** And Jesus said, "For judgment I have come into this world, so that those who do not see may see, and those who see may become blind."

**9:40** And some of the Pharisees who were with Him heard and said to Him, "We are not also blind, are we?"

**9:41** Jesus said to them, "If you were blind, you would not have sin. But now you say, 'We see'; your sin remains."

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The Clementine Vulgate reads the singular "Me oportet" ("I must"), whereas many modern Greek critical editions read the plural ("We must").
- **9:11** — The Vulgate reads the present tense "et video" ("and I see") and includes "ad natatoria" ("to the pool of"), and similarly uses simple "videre" ("see") in 9:15 and 9:18 where the Greek uses "anablepo" ("receive sight").
- **9:27** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "et audistis" ("and you heard") without the negation found in the Greek ("and you did not listen").
- **9:28** — The Vulgate uses the subjunctive "sis" ("You be His disciple"), matching the imprecatory force of "Maledixerunt" ("cursed"), whereas the Greek uses the indicative ("You are his disciple").
- **9:35** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "Filium Dei" ("the Son of God"), whereas many modern Greek critical editions read "the Son of Man."
