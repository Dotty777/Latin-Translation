## Text

**8:1** But Jesus proceeded to the Mount of Olives.

**8:2** And at daybreak He came again into the temple, and all the people came to Him; and sitting down, He was teaching them.

**8:3** Then the scribes and the Pharisees bring a woman caught in adultery, and they stood her in the middle,

**8:4** and said to Him, "Teacher, this woman has just now been caught in adultery.

**8:5** Now in the law Moses commanded us to stone such women. What therefore do You say?"

**8:6** Yet they were saying this to test Him, so that they might be able to accuse Him. But Jesus, stooping downward, was writing with His finger on the ground.

**8:7** So when they persisted in questioning Him, He stood upright and said to them, "Let the one among you who is without sin cast a stone at her first."

**8:8** And stooping down again, He was writing on the ground.

**8:9** Then hearing this, they went out one after another, beginning with the elders; and Jesus remained alone, with the woman standing in the middle.

**8:10** Then raising Himself up, Jesus said to her, "Woman, where are those who were accusing you? Has no one condemned you?"

**8:11** She said, "No one, Lord." Then Jesus said, "Neither will I condemn you; go, and from now on sin no more."

**8:12** So Jesus spoke to them again, saying, "I am the light of the world; the one who follows Me does not walk in darkness, but will have the light of life."

**8:13** So the Pharisees said to Him, "You bear testimony about Yourself; Your testimony is not true."

**8:14** Jesus answered and said to them, "Even if I bear testimony about Myself, My testimony is true, because I know where I came from and where I am going; yet you do not know where I come from or where I am going.

**8:15** You judge according to the flesh; I do not judge anyone;

**8:16** and if I do judge, My judgment is true, because I am not alone, but I and the One who sent Me, the Father.

**8:17** And it is written in your law that the testimony of two men is true.

**8:18** I am the One who bears testimony about Myself, and the One who sent Me, the Father, bears testimony about Me."

**8:19** So they were saying to Him, "Where is Your Father?" Jesus answered, "You know neither Me nor My Father; if you knew Me, perhaps you would also know My Father."

**8:20** Jesus spoke these words in the treasury, teaching in the temple, and no one seized Him, because His hour had not yet come.

**8:21** Then Jesus said to them again, "I am going away, and you will seek Me, and in your sin you will die. Where I am going, you are not able to come."

**8:22** So the Jews were saying, "Surely He will not kill Himself, because He said, 'Where I am going, you are not able to come'?"

**8:23** And He was saying to them, "You are from below; I am from above. You are from this world; I am not from this world.

**8:24** Therefore I told you that you will die in your sins; for if you do not believe that I am, you will die in your sin."

**8:25** So they were saying to Him, "Who are You?" Jesus said to them, "The Beginning, who also speak to you.

**8:26** I have many things to speak and to judge about you, yet the One who sent Me is truthful; and the things I heard from Him, these I speak in the world."

**8:27** And they did not understand that He was calling God His Father.

**8:28** So Jesus said to them, "When you have lifted up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am, and I do nothing from Myself, but just as the Father taught Me, I speak these things;

**8:29** and the One who sent Me is with Me, and He has not left Me alone, because I always do the things pleasing to Him."

**8:30** While He was speaking these things, many believed in Him.

**8:31** So Jesus was saying to those Jews who believed Him, "If you remain in My word, you will truly be My disciples,

**8:32** and you will come to know the truth, and the truth will set you free."

**8:33** They answered Him, "We are Abraham's offspring, and we have never been slaves to anyone; how do You say, 'You will be free'?"

**8:34** Jesus answered them, "Amen, amen, I tell you that everyone who commits sin is a slave of sin.

**8:35** Now a slave does not remain in the house forever, but a son remains forever.

**8:36** Therefore, if the Son sets you free, you will truly be free.

**8:37** I know that you are sons of Abraham, yet you seek to kill Me, because My word finds no room in you.

**8:38** I speak what I saw with My Father, and you do the things you saw with your father."

**8:39** They answered and said to Him, "Our father is Abraham." Jesus said to them, "If you are sons of Abraham, do the works of Abraham.

**8:40** But now you seek to kill Me, a man who has spoken to you the truth that I heard from God; Abraham did not do this.

**8:41** You do the works of your father." Therefore they said to Him, "We were not born from fornication; we have one Father, God."

**8:42** Jesus therefore said to them, "If God were your Father, you would certainly love Me as well; for I proceeded from God and came; for I did not come from Myself either, but He sent Me.

**8:43** Why do you not understand My speech? Because you are not able to hear My word.

**8:44** You are from your father the devil, and you want to do the desires of your father. He was a murderer from the beginning, and he did not stand in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaks a lie, he speaks from his own, because he is a liar and the father of it.

**8:45** But if I speak the truth, you do not believe Me.

**8:46** Which of you will convict Me of sin? If I speak the truth to you, why do you not believe Me?

**8:47** The one who is from God hears the words of God. For this reason you do not hear, because you are not from God."

**8:48** The Jews therefore answered and said to Him, "Do we not rightly say that You are a Samaritan and have a demon?"

**8:49** Jesus answered, "I do not have a demon, but I honor My Father, and you have dishonored Me.

**8:50** I, however, do not seek My glory; there is One who seeks and judges.

**8:51** Amen, amen, I tell you, if anyone keeps My word, he will never see death."

**8:52** Therefore the Jews said, "Now we know that You have a demon. Abraham died, and so did the prophets; and You say, 'If anyone keeps My word, he will never taste death.'

**8:53** You are not greater than our father Abraham, who died, are You? And the prophets died. Who do You make Yourself?"

**8:54** Jesus answered, "If I glorify Myself, My glory is nothing; it is My Father who glorifies Me, about whom you say that He is your God,

**8:55** and you have not come to know Him, yet I know Him. And if I say that I do not know Him, I will be like you, a liar. But I know Him, and I keep His word.

**8:56** Abraham, your father, exulted to see My day; he saw it and was glad."

**8:57** The Jews therefore said to Him, "You are not yet fifty years old, and You have seen Abraham?"

**8:58** Jesus said to them, "Amen, amen, I tell you, before Abraham came into being, I am."

**8:59** They therefore took up stones to throw at Him, yet Jesus hid Himself and went out from the temple.

## Translator Notes

- **8:9** — The Vulgate reads simply Audientes autem ("Then hearing this") without the expansion "convicted by their conscience."
- **8:10** — The Vulgate reads qui te accusabant ("those who were accusing you"), whereas shorter Greek manuscripts read simply "where are they?"
- **8:11** — Latin condemnabo is future tense ("Neither will I condemn you"), differing from Greek present-tense katakrino ("Neither do I condemn you").
- **8:19** — The Vulgate renders the Greek conditional particle with forsitan ("perhaps"), yielding the distinctively Vulgate phrasing "perhaps you would also know My Father."
- **8:24** — The Vulgate shifts from plural in peccatis vestris ("in your sins") to singular in peccato vestro ("in your sin"), where Greek texts use the plural in both clauses; quia ego sum is translated literally as "that I am" without supplying "He" (as also in 8:28).
- **8:25** — Principium can be nominative or accusative; it is rendered here as a noun ("The Beginning, who also speak to you"), as the first-person qui et loquor vobis suggests, whereas the corresponding Greek ten archen is usually taken adverbially.
- **8:27** — Vulgate quia Patrem ejus dicebat Deum ("that He was calling God His Father") reads ejus ("His") and explicitly includes Deum ("God"), whereas standard Greek texts read that He was speaking to them about the Father.
- **8:45** — The Vulgate reads conditional si ("if") rather than causal hoti ("because") found in Greek manuscripts.
