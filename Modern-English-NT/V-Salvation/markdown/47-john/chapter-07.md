## Text

**7:1** After these things, Jesus was walking about in Galilee, for He did not want to walk about in Judea, because the Jews were seeking to kill Him.

**7:2** Now the feast of the Jews, Tabernacles, was close at hand.

**7:3** Then His brothers said to Him, "Depart from here and go into Judea, so that Your disciples too may see Your works that You are doing.

**7:4** For indeed no one does anything in secret and himself seeks to be in the open. If You do these things, reveal Yourself to the world."

**7:5** For even His brothers were not believing in Him.

**7:6** Therefore Jesus said to them, "My time has not yet arrived, but your time is always ready.

**7:7** The world cannot hate you, yet it hates Me, because I bear witness concerning it that its works are evil.

**7:8** You go up to this feast; I, however, am not going up to this feast, because My time has not yet been fulfilled."

**7:9** When He had said these things, He Himself remained in Galilee.

**7:10** Yet once His brothers went up, then He Himself also went up to the feast, not publicly, but as though in secret.

**7:11** Therefore the Jews were seeking Him at the feast and kept saying, "Where is He?"

**7:12** And there was much murmuring in the crowd about Him. For some were saying, "He is good." Others, however, were saying, "No, instead He leads the crowds astray."

**7:13** Still, no one was speaking openly about Him because of fear of the Jews.

**7:14** Now when the feast was already halfway through, Jesus went up into the temple and was teaching.

**7:15** And the Jews were amazed, saying, "How does this man know letters, though He has not studied?"

**7:16** Jesus answered them and said, "My teaching is not Mine, but that of Him who sent Me.

**7:17** If anyone wills to do His will, he will know about the teaching, whether it is from God or I am speaking from Myself.

**7:18** The one who speaks from himself seeks his own glory; yet the one who seeks the glory of the one who sent him, this one is truthful, and there is no unrighteousness in him.

**7:19** Did Moses not give you the law, and yet not one of you does the law?

**7:20** Why are you seeking to kill Me?" The crowd answered and said, "You have a demon; who is seeking to kill You?"

**7:21** Jesus answered and said to them, "I did one work, and all of you are amazed.

**7:22** Therefore Moses gave you circumcision (not that it comes from Moses, but from the fathers), and you circumcise a man on the Sabbath.

**7:23** If a man receives circumcision on the Sabbath so that the law of Moses is not broken, are you indignant with Me because I made a whole man well on the Sabbath?

**7:24** Do not judge by appearance; rather, judge a righteous judgment."

**7:25** Therefore some from Jerusalem were saying, "Is this not the one whom they are seeking to kill?

**7:26** And look, He is speaking openly, and they say nothing to Him. Could the rulers truly have come to know that this is the Christ?

**7:27** Yet we know where this man is from; when the Christ comes, however, no one knows where He is from."

**7:28** Therefore Jesus was crying out as He taught in the temple, saying, "You know Me, and you also know where I am from; and I did not come from Myself, yet He who sent Me is true, whom you do not know.

**7:29** I know Him, for I am from Him, and He Himself sent Me."

**7:30** Therefore they were seeking to seize Him, yet no one laid hands on Him, because His hour had not yet arrived.

**7:31** Yet many from the crowd believed in Him and were saying, "When the Christ comes, will He really do more signs than the ones this man is doing?"

**7:32** The Pharisees heard the crowd murmuring these things about Him, and the rulers and the Pharisees sent officers to seize Him.

**7:33** Therefore Jesus said to them, "I am still with you for a short time, and I am going to Him who sent Me.

**7:34** You will seek Me, and you will not find Me; and where I am, you are not able to come."

**7:35** Therefore the Jews said among themselves, "Where is this man about to go, that we will not find Him? Is He really about to go to the dispersion of the Gentiles and teach the Gentiles?

**7:36** What is this saying that He spoke, 'You will seek Me, and you will not find Me; and where I am, you are not able to come'?"

**7:37** Now on the last, great day of the festival, Jesus was standing and crying out, saying, "If anyone is thirsty, let him come to Me and drink.

**7:38** Whoever believes in Me, just as Scripture says, rivers of living water will flow from his belly."

**7:39** Now He said this concerning the Spirit, whom those believing in Him were about to receive; for the Spirit had not yet been given, because Jesus had not yet been glorified.

**7:40** Therefore some from that crowd, when they had heard these sayings of His, were saying, "This man is truly the Prophet."

**7:41** Others were saying, "This man is the Christ." Yet some were saying, "Does the Christ really come from Galilee?

**7:42** Does Scripture not say that the Christ comes from the seed of David, and from the village of Bethlehem, where David was?"

**7:43** Thus a dissension arose in the crowd on account of Him.

**7:44** Now some from among them wished to seize Him, yet no one laid hands upon Him.

**7:45** Therefore the officers came to the chief priests and the Pharisees. And they said to them, "Why did you not bring Him?"

**7:46** The officers answered, "Never has any man spoken like this man."

**7:47** Therefore the Pharisees answered them, "Have you also been led astray?

**7:48** Has anyone among the rulers believed in Him, or among the Pharisees?

**7:49** Yet this crowd, which does not know the law, is cursed."

**7:50** Nicodemus, the one who came to Him by night, who was one of them, said to them,

**7:51** "Does our law really judge a man unless it first hears from him and learns what he is doing?"

**7:52** They answered and said to him, "Are you a Galilean too? Search the Scriptures and see that no prophet arises from Galilee."

**7:53** And each one returned to his own house.

## Translator Notes

- **7:8** — The Vulgate reads "I am not going up" (non ascendo), whereas many Greek manuscripts read "not yet" (oupo).
- **7:22** — The Vulgate places "Therefore" (propterea) at the start of verse 22 linked to Moses giving circumcision, whereas many modern editions attach the corresponding Greek phrase to the end of verse 21.
- **7:32** — The Vulgate reads "the rulers" (principes) here as in verses 26 and 48, distinct from "the chief priests" (pontifices) in verse 45 and the Greek archiereis ("chief priests").
- **7:35** — The Vulgate reads "of the Gentiles" and "the Gentiles" (gentium / gentes, also "nations"), whereas Greek manuscripts read "Greeks" (Hellenon / Hellenas).
- **7:39** — The Vulgate explicitly includes "given" (datus), whereas many early Greek witnesses read simply "for the Spirit was not yet."
- **7:53** — Unlike the Saints Greek Critical Text, the Vulgate includes 7:53-8:11 without double text-critical brackets.
