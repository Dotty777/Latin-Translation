## Text

**2:1** And on the third day a wedding took place in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there.

**2:2** Now Jesus and His disciples were invited to the wedding as well.

**2:3** And when the wine gave out, the mother of Jesus said to Him, "They have no wine."

**2:4** And Jesus said to her, "What is it to Me and to you, woman? My hour has not yet come."

**2:5** His mother said to the attendants, "Whatever He tells you, do it."

**2:6** Now six stone water jars were placed there according to the purification of the Jews, each holding two or three measures.

**2:7** Jesus said to them, "Fill the water jars with water." And they filled them all the way to the top.

**2:8** And Jesus said to them, "Draw some out now, and bring it to the banquet master." And they brought it.

**2:9** Now when the banquet master tasted the water that had become wine, and did not know where it was from (though the attendants who had drawn the water knew), the banquet master called the bridegroom,

**2:10** and said to him, "Every man sets out the good wine first, and once they have become drunk, then that which is inferior. You, however, have saved the good wine up to now."

**2:11** Jesus performed this, the beginning of signs, in Cana of Galilee; and He made His glory manifest, and His disciples believed in Him.

**2:12** After this He Himself, and His mother, and His brothers, and His disciples went down to Capernaum; and there they remained not many days.

**2:13** And the Passover of the Jews was near, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem,

**2:14** and He found in the temple those selling oxen, and sheep, and doves, and the money changers seated.

**2:15** And when He had made something like a whip out of cords, He drove them all out of the temple, the sheep also, and the oxen; and He poured out the coins of the money changers, and overturned the tables.

**2:16** And to those who were selling doves He said, "Take these things away from here, and do not make the house of My Father a house of commerce."

**2:17** Then His disciples remembered that it is written, "Zeal for Your house has consumed Me."

**2:18** So the Jews answered and said to Him, "What sign do You show us, since You do these things?"

**2:19** Jesus answered and said to them, "Tear down this temple, and in three days I will raise it up."

**2:20** Therefore the Jews said, "In forty-six years this temple was built, and will You raise it up in three days?"

**2:21** He, however, was speaking of the temple of His body.

**2:22** Therefore, when He had risen from the dead, His disciples remembered that He had been saying this, and they believed the Scripture and the word that Jesus spoke.

**2:23** Now while He was in Jerusalem at the Passover, on the feast day, many believed in His name, seeing His signs that He was performing.

**2:24** Jesus Himself, however, was not entrusting Himself to them, because He Himself knew all people,

**2:25** and because He had no need that anyone bear witness concerning man, for He Himself knew what was within man.

## Translator Notes

- **2:4** — Latin "Quid mihi et tibi est, mulier?" ("What is it to Me and to you, woman?") renders a Semitic idiom distancing the speaker from the interlocutor's concern, with "woman" used as a respectful form of address.
- **2:6** — Latin "metretas" refers to the Greek liquid measure metretes, roughly nine to ten gallons (about 39 liters) each.
- **2:10** — Latin "inebriati fuerint" literally means "have become drunk," though in biblical usage it can also convey drinking freely or to the full.
- **2:17** — The Vulgate reads the perfect/present "comedit" ("has consumed"), matching its rendering of Psalm 68:10 (69:9), whereas most modern Greek critical editions read the future "will consume."
