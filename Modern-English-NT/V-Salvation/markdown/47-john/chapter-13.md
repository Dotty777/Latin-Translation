## Text

**13:1** Before the feast day of the Passover, knowing that His hour had come to pass from this world to the Father, since He had loved His own who were in the world, Jesus loved them to the end.

**13:2** And when supper had been made, the devil having already put it into the heart that Judas, son of Simon Iscariot, should betray Him,

**13:3** knowing that the Father had given all things into His hands, and that He had come forth from God and was going to God,

**13:4** He rose from supper and laid aside His garments, and after He had taken a linen towel, He wrapped it around His waist.

**13:5** Then He poured water into a basin and began to wash the feet of the disciples and to wipe them with the linen towel that was wrapped around His waist.

**13:6** So He came to Simon Peter. And Peter said to Him, "Lord, are You washing my feet?"

**13:7** Jesus answered and said to him, "What I am doing you do not know now, but you will know afterward."

**13:8** Peter said to Him, "You will never wash my feet." Jesus answered him, "If I do not wash you, you will have no share with Me."

**13:9** Simon Peter said to Him, "Lord, not only my feet, but also my hands and my head."

**13:10** Jesus said to him, "The one who has been washed has no need except to wash his feet, but is entirely clean. And you are clean, but not all of you."

**13:11** For He knew who it was who would betray Him; for this reason He said, "You are not all clean."

**13:12** So after He had washed their feet and taken His garments, when He had reclined again, He said to them, "Do you know what I have done to you?

**13:13** You call Me Teacher and Lord, and you speak rightly, for indeed I am.

**13:14** Therefore, if I, the Lord and Teacher, have washed your feet, you also should wash the feet of one another.

**13:15** For I have given you an example, so that just as I have done to you, you also should do.

**13:16** Amen, amen, I tell you: a servant is not greater than his master, nor is an apostle greater than the one who sent him.

**13:17** If you know these things, you will be blessed if you do them.

**13:18** I am not speaking about all of you; I know those whom I have chosen, but so that the Scripture may be fulfilled: 'The one who eats bread with Me will lift up his heel against Me.'

**13:19** From now on I am telling you before it takes place, so that when it has taken place, you may believe that I am.

**13:20** Amen, amen, I tell you: the one who receives anyone whom I send receives Me; and the one who receives Me receives Him who sent Me."

**13:21** When Jesus had said these things, He was troubled in spirit, and He testified and said, "Amen, amen, I tell you that one of you will betray Me."

**13:22** So the disciples were looking at one another, uncertain about whom He was speaking.

**13:23** Now one of His disciples was reclining at the bosom of Jesus, the one whom Jesus loved.

**13:24** So Simon Peter motioned to him and said to him, "Who is it about whom He is speaking?"

**13:25** Therefore, when he had leaned back upon the chest of Jesus, he said to Him, "Lord, who is it?"

**13:26** Jesus answered, "It is the one to whom I hand the dipped bread." And when He had dipped the bread, He gave it to Judas, son of Simon Iscariot.

**13:27** And after the morsel, Satan entered into him. And Jesus said to him, "What you are doing, do more quickly."

**13:28** Now none of those reclining knew for what purpose He said this to him.

**13:29** For some were thinking, because Judas had the moneybox, that Jesus had said to him, "Buy the things that we need for the feast day," or that he should give something to the needy.

**13:30** So when he had received the morsel, he went out immediately. And it was night.

**13:31** So when he had gone out, Jesus said, "Now the Son of Man has been glorified, and God has been glorified in Him.

**13:32** If God has been glorified in Him, God will also glorify Him in Himself, and He will glorify Him immediately.

**13:33** Little children, I am with you a little while longer. You will seek Me; and just as I said to the Jews, 'Where I am going, you cannot come,' I also tell you now.

**13:34** I give you a new commandment: that you love one another; just as I have loved you, that you also love one another.

**13:35** By this everyone will know that you are My disciples, if you have love for one another."

**13:36** Simon Peter said to Him, "Lord, where are You going?" Jesus answered, "Where I am going you cannot follow Me now, but you will follow afterward."

**13:37** Peter said to Him, "Why can I not follow You now? I will lay down my life for You."

**13:38** Jesus answered him, "Will you lay down your life for Me? Amen, amen, I tell you: the rooster will not crow until you deny Me three times."

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — Latin coena facta literally means "supper having been made/done" (understood either as supper having been prepared/served or as supper having ended, contrasting with Greek critical texts' "during supper"). The Vulgate also reads in cor ("into the heart") without a possessive pronoun, with "Judas, son of Simon Iscariot" as subject of the betrayal clause.
- **13:16** — The Vulgate retains the loanword apostolus ("apostle"), literally meaning "one sent" or "messenger."
- **13:18** — The Vulgate reads the future tense levabit ("will lift up") and mecum panem ("bread with Me"), whereas many Greek manuscripts read a past tense ("has lifted up") and "My bread."
- **13:31** — Here and in 13:32 the Vulgate uses clarificare (an Ecclesiastical Latin synonym for glorificare, used in 14:13) for "glorify."
