## Text

**4:1** Therefore, when Jesus came to know that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus makes and baptizes more disciples than John

**4:2** (although Jesus was not baptizing, but His disciples were),

**4:3** He left Judea and went away again into Galilee.

**4:4** Now it was necessary for Him to pass through Samaria.

**4:5** Therefore He came to a city of Samaria, which is called Sychar, near the plot of land that Jacob gave to Joseph his son.

**4:6** Now Jacob's spring was there. Therefore Jesus, tired from the journey, was sitting thus by the spring. It was about the sixth hour.

**4:7** A woman from Samaria came to draw water. Jesus said to her, "Give Me a drink."

**4:8** (For His disciples had gone away into the city to buy food.)

**4:9** Therefore that Samaritan woman said to Him, "How do You, being a Jew, ask a drink from me, who am a Samaritan woman?" For Jews do not associate with Samaritans.

**4:10** Jesus answered and said to her, "If you knew the gift of God, and who it is who says to you, 'Give Me a drink,' you would perhaps have asked Him, and He would have given you living water."

**4:11** The woman said to Him, "Sir, You have nothing with which to draw, and the well is deep; where then do You get living water?

**4:12** Surely You are not greater than our father Jacob, who gave us the well and himself drank from it, and his sons, and his livestock?"

**4:13** Jesus answered and said to her, "Everyone who drinks from this water will be thirsty again; but the one who drinks from the water that I will give him will never be thirsty;

**4:14** instead, the water that I will give him will become in him a spring of water leaping up into eternal life."

**4:15** The woman said to Him, "Sir, give me this water, so that I may not be thirsty, nor come here to draw."

**4:16** Jesus said to her, "Go, call your husband, and come here."

**4:17** The woman answered and said, "I have no husband." Jesus said to her, "You have spoken well, 'I have no husband';

**4:18** for you have had five husbands, and the one you have now is not your husband; you have spoken this truthfully."

**4:19** The woman said to Him, "Sir, I see that You are a prophet.

**4:20** Our fathers worshiped on this mountain, and you say that in Jerusalem is the place where one must worship."

**4:21** Jesus said to her, "Woman, believe Me that the hour is coming when neither on this mountain nor in Jerusalem will you worship the Father.

**4:22** You worship what you do not know; we worship what we know, because salvation is from the Jews.

**4:23** Yet the hour is coming, and now is, when true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth. For the Father also seeks such people to worship Him.

**4:24** God is spirit, and it is necessary for those who worship Him to worship in spirit and truth."

**4:25** The woman said to Him, "I know that Messiah is coming (who is called Christ); so when He comes, He will declare all things to us."

**4:26** Jesus said to her, "I am He, the One speaking with you."

**4:27** And immediately His disciples came, and they were marveling that He was speaking with a woman. Yet no one said, "What are You seeking?" or, "Why are You speaking with her?"

**4:28** Therefore the woman left her water jar and went away into the city, and said to those people,

**4:29** "Come and see a man who told me everything I have done; could He be the Christ?"

**4:30** Therefore they went out from the city and were coming to Him.

**4:31** Meanwhile the disciples were urging Him, saying, "Rabbi, eat."

**4:32** But He said to them, "I have food to eat that you do not know."

**4:33** So the disciples were saying to one another, "Surely no one brought Him anything to eat?"

**4:34** Jesus said to them, "My food is to do the will of Him who sent Me, to complete His work.

**4:35** Do you not say that there are still four months, and the harvest comes? Look, I say to you, lift up your eyes and see the fields, for they are already white for harvest.

**4:36** And the one who reaps receives wages and gathers fruit for eternal life, so that both the one who sows and the one who reaps may rejoice together.

**4:37** For in this the saying is true: it is one who sows, and another who reaps.

**4:38** I sent you to reap what you did not labor for; others have labored, and you have entered into their labors."

**4:39** Now from that city many of the Samaritans believed in Him because of the word of the woman testifying, "He told me everything I have done."

**4:40** Therefore, when the Samaritans came to Him, they urged Him to stay there. And He stayed there two days.

**4:41** And many more believed in Him because of His word.

**4:42** And they were saying to the woman, "No longer do we believe because of your speech; for we ourselves have heard, and we know that this One is truly the Savior of the world."

**4:43** Now after two days He went out from there and went away into Galilee.

**4:44** For Jesus Himself testified that a prophet has no honor in his own homeland.

**4:45** Therefore, when He came into Galilee, the Galileans received Him, since they had seen all the things that He had done in Jerusalem at the feast; for they themselves had also come to the feast.

**4:46** So He came again to Cana of Galilee, where He made the water wine. And there was a certain royal official whose son was sick at Capernaum.

**4:47** When this man heard that Jesus had arrived from Judea into Galilee, he went to Him and was urging Him to come down and heal his son, for he was beginning to die.

**4:48** Therefore Jesus said to him, "Unless you see signs and wonders, you do not believe."

**4:49** The royal official said to Him, "Sir, come down before my son dies."

**4:50** Jesus said to him, "Go; your son lives." The man believed the word that Jesus said to him, and set out.

**4:51** Now while he was already going down, the servants met him and reported, saying that his son was living.

**4:52** Therefore he inquired from them the hour at which he had become better. And they said to him, "Yesterday at the seventh hour the fever left him."

**4:53** Therefore the father realized that it was that hour at which Jesus said to him, "Your son lives"; and he himself believed, and his entire household.

**4:54** This again Jesus did as the second sign, when He had come from Judea into Galilee.

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — The Clementine Vulgate reads 'cognovit Jesus' ("Jesus came to know"), whereas many Greek manuscripts read "the Lord." In verse 2 the Vulgate also reads simply 'quamquam Jesus non baptizaret', without 'ipse' ("Himself").
- **4:6** — The Vulgate distinguishes 'fons' ("spring," used here and in 4:14 for living water) from 'puteus' ("well," used by the Samaritan woman in 4:11-12 for the deep dug shaft).
- **4:10** — The Vulgate translates the Greek contrary-to-fact particle with 'forsitan' ("perhaps"), yielding the distinctively Vulgate phrasing "you would perhaps have asked Him."
- **4:11** — Latin 'Domine' (vocative of 'Dominus') here and in 4:15, 4:19, and 4:49 is translated "Sir" as respectful address, though it is the same word used for "Lord."
- **4:35** — In the Clementine Vulgate, 'jam' ("already") belongs to the end of 4:35 ("already white for harvest") rather than the start of 4:36, which begins simply with 'Et' ("And").
