## Text

**9:1** And stepping into the boat, He crossed over the water and came into His own city.

**9:2** And look, they were bringing to Him a paralytic lying on a bed. And seeing their faith, Jesus said to the paralytic, "Have courage, son; your sins are forgiven you."

**9:3** And look, some of the scribes said within themselves, "This man is blaspheming."

**9:4** And when Jesus had seen their thoughts, He said, "Why do you think evil things in your hearts?

**9:5** Which is easier, to say, 'Your sins are forgiven you,' or to say, 'Rise and walk'?

**9:6** But so that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins," then He said to the paralytic, "Rise, pick up your bed, and go to your house."

**9:7** And he rose and went away to his house.

**9:8** Now when the crowds saw this, they were afraid, and they glorified God, who gave such authority to men.

**9:9** And as Jesus was passing on from there, He saw a man sitting at the tax office, Matthew by name. And He said to him, "Follow Me." And rising, he followed Him.

**9:10** And it happened that, as He was reclining at table in the house, look, many tax collectors and sinners came and were reclining at table with Jesus and His disciples.

**9:11** And seeing this, the Pharisees were saying to His disciples, "Why does your teacher eat with tax collectors and sinners?"

**9:12** But Jesus, hearing this, said, "A physician is not needed by the healthy, but by those who are ill.

**9:13** But go and learn what this means: 'Mercy I desire, and not sacrifice.' For I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners."

**9:14** Then the disciples of John approached Him, saying, "Why do we and the Pharisees fast frequently, but Your disciples do not fast?"

**9:15** And Jesus said to them, "Can the sons of the bridegroom mourn as long as the bridegroom is with them? Yet days will come when the bridegroom will be taken away from them, and then they will fast.

**9:16** Moreover, no one puts a patch of raw cloth onto an old garment; for it takes away its fullness from the garment, and a worse tear is made.

**9:17** Nor do they put new wine into old wineskins; otherwise the wineskins burst, and the wine is poured out, and the wineskins perish. But they put new wine into new wineskins, and both are preserved."

**9:18** While He was speaking these things to them, look, a certain ruler approached and worshiped Him, saying, "Lord, my daughter has just now died; but come, lay Your hand on her, and she will live."

**9:19** And rising, Jesus was following him, and so were His disciples.

**9:20** And look, a woman who had been suffering a flow of blood for twelve years approached from behind and touched the fringe of His garment.

**9:21** For she was saying within herself, "If I only touch His garment, I will be made whole."

**9:22** But Jesus, turning around and seeing her, said, "Have courage, daughter; your faith has made you whole." And the woman was made whole from that hour.

**9:23** And when Jesus had come into the ruler's house and had seen the flute players and the crowd making an uproar, He said:

**9:24** "Withdraw, for the girl is not dead, but is sleeping." And they were laughing at Him.

**9:25** And when the crowd had been driven out, He went in and took hold of her hand, and the girl rose.

**9:26** And this report went out into all that land.

**9:27** And as Jesus was passing on from there, two blind men followed Him, crying out and saying, "Have mercy on us, Son of David!"

**9:28** Now when He had come into the house, the blind men approached Him. And Jesus said to them, "Do you believe that I am able to do this for you?" They said to Him, "Yes, Lord."

**9:29** Then He touched their eyes, saying, "According to your faith, let it be done for you."

**9:30** And their eyes were opened; and Jesus sternly warned them, saying, "See to it that no one knows."

**9:31** But going out, they spread His fame in all that land.

**9:32** Now when they had gone out, look, they brought to Him a mute man who had a demon.

**9:33** And when the demon had been cast out, the mute man spoke; and the crowds marveled, saying, "Never has anything like this appeared in Israel."

**9:34** But the Pharisees were saying, "By the ruler of demons He casts out demons."

**9:35** And Jesus was going about all the cities and villages, teaching in their synagogues, and proclaiming the Gospel of the kingdom, and curing every sickness and every infirmity.

**9:36** Now seeing the crowds, He had compassion on them, because they were harassed and lying prostrate, like sheep having no shepherd.

**9:37** Then He said to His disciples, "The harvest indeed is plentiful, but the laborers are few.

**9:38** Therefore ask the Lord of the harvest to send laborers into His harvest."

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The Vulgate reads "vidisset" ("had seen") rather than "knowing," reflecting the Greek textual variant "idon" instead of "eidos."
- **9:15** — Latin "filii sponsi" ("sons of the bridegroom") literally preserves a Semitic idiom referring to the bridegroom's attendants or wedding guests.
- **9:16** — In the Vulgate, "plenitudinem" ("fullness") is accusative as the direct object of "tollit," indicating that the patch pulls away the garment's substance and creates a worse tear.
- **9:18** — The Vulgate includes the address "Domine" ("Lord"), which is absent from the Greek text of Matthew 9:18.
- **9:28** — The Vulgate adds "vobis" ("for you") to Jesus's question, whereas the Greek text omits it.
