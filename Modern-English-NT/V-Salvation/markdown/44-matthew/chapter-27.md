## Text

**27:1** Now when morning had come, all the chief priests and the elders of the people held a consultation against Jesus, so that they might hand Him over to death.

**27:2** And having bound Him, they brought Him and handed Him over to Pontius Pilate, the governor.

**27:3** Then when Judas, who had handed Him over, saw that He had been condemned, he was moved by repentance and brought back the thirty silver coins to the chief priests and the elders,

**27:4** saying, "I have sinned in handing over righteous blood." But they said, "What is that to us? You see to it."

**27:5** And having thrown down the silver coins in the temple, he withdrew; and going away, he hanged himself with a noose.

**27:6** Now the chief priests, having taken the silver coins, said, "It is not permitted to put them into the treasury, because it is the price of blood."

**27:7** And having held a consultation, they bought the potter's field with them for the burial of foreigners.

**27:8** For this reason that field has been called Akeldama, that is, Field of Blood, down to the present day.

**27:9** Then what was spoken through Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled, saying, "And they took the thirty silver coins, the price of Him who was valued, whom they valued from the sons of Israel,

**27:10** and they gave them for the potter's field, just as the Lord appointed for me."

**27:11** Now Jesus stood before the governor, and the governor questioned Him, saying, "Are You the King of the Jews?" Jesus said to him, "You say so."

**27:12** And while He was being accused by the chief priests and the elders, He answered nothing.

**27:13** Then Pilate said to Him, "Do You not hear how many testimonies they are speaking against You?"

**27:14** And He did not answer him regarding a single word, so that the governor marveled greatly.

**27:15** Now during the feast day the governor was accustomed to release to the people one prisoner whom they wished;

**27:16** and at that time he had a notorious prisoner who was called Barabbas.

**27:17** Therefore when they had assembled, Pilate said, "Whom do you want me to release to you: Barabbas, or Jesus, who is called Christ?"

**27:18** For he knew that they had handed Him over through envy.

**27:19** Now while he was sitting on the judgment seat, his wife sent word to him, saying, "Have no dealings with that righteous man, for I have suffered many things today in a vision because of Him."

**27:20** But the chief priests and the elders persuaded the crowds that they should ask for Barabbas, but destroy Jesus.

**27:21** Now the governor answered and said to them, "Which of the two do you want to be released to you?" But they said, "Barabbas."

**27:22** Pilate said to them, "What then should I do with Jesus, who is called Christ?"

**27:23** They all said, "Let Him be crucified!" The governor said to them, "What evil, then, has He done?" But they kept shouting all the more, saying, "Let Him be crucified!"

**27:24** Now when Pilate saw that he was making no headway, but rather that an uproar was arising, he took water and washed his hands before the people, saying, "I am innocent of the blood of this righteous man; you yourselves see to it."

**27:25** And the entire people answered and said, "His blood be on us and on our children!"

**27:26** Then he released Barabbas to them; but after Jesus had been flogged, he handed Him over to them to be crucified.

**27:27** Then the soldiers of the governor, taking Jesus into the praetorium, assembled the entire cohort around Him;

**27:28** and stripping Him, they placed a scarlet cloak around Him,

**27:29** and weaving a crown of thorns, they set it on His head, and a reed in His right hand. And bending the knee before Him, they were mocking Him, saying, "Hail, King of the Jews!"

**27:30** And spitting on Him, they took the reed and kept striking His head.

**27:31** And after they had mocked Him, they stripped Him of the cloak and dressed Him in His garments, and they led Him away so that they might crucify Him.

**27:32** Now as they were going out, they found a Cyrenian man, Simon by name; they forced this man to bear His cross.

**27:33** And they came to a place that is called Golgotha, which is the Place of a Skull.

**27:34** And they gave Him wine mixed with gall to drink. And after tasting it, He refused to drink.

**27:35** Now after they had crucified Him, they divided His garments, casting lots, so that what was spoken through the prophet might be fulfilled, saying, "They divided My garments among themselves, and over My garment they cast lots."

**27:36** And sitting down, they kept watch over Him.

**27:37** And they placed above His head the written charge against Him: "This is Jesus, the King of the Jews."

**27:38** Then two bandits were crucified with Him, one on the right and one on the left.

**27:39** Now those passing by were blaspheming Him, shaking their heads,

**27:40** and saying, "Aha! You who destroy the temple of God and rebuild it in three days, save Yourself! If You are the Son of God, come down from the cross!"

**27:41** Likewise the chief priests also, mocking with the scribes and the elders, were saying,

**27:42** "He saved others; He is not able to save Himself. If He is the King of Israel, let Him come down now from the cross, and we believe Him.

**27:43** He trusts in God; let Him deliver Him now, if He desires Him; for He said, 'I am the Son of God.'"

**27:44** Now the bandits who had been crucified with Him were also reproaching Him with the very same thing.

**27:45** Now from the sixth hour, darkness came over the entire earth until the ninth hour.

**27:46** And around the ninth hour, Jesus cried out with a loud voice, saying, "Eli, Eli, lamma sabacthani?" that is, "My God, My God, why have You abandoned Me?"

**27:47** Now some of those standing there, hearing it, were saying, "This man is calling Elijah."

**27:48** And at once one of them ran, took a sponge and filled it with vinegar, and placed it on a reed, and was offering it to Him to drink.

**27:49** But the rest were saying, "Wait, let us see whether Elijah comes to deliver Him."

**27:50** Now Jesus, crying out again with a loud voice, released His spirit.

**27:51** And look, the veil of the temple was torn into two parts from top to bottom; and the earth shook, and the rocks were split,

**27:52** and the tombs were opened, and many bodies of the saints who had fallen asleep rose up.

**27:53** And going out from the tombs after His resurrection, they came into the holy city and appeared to many.

**27:54** Now the centurion and those who were with him guarding Jesus, having seen the earthquake and the things that were happening, feared greatly, saying, "Truly this man was the Son of God!"

**27:55** Now many women were there at a distance, who had followed Jesus from Galilee, serving Him,

**27:56** among whom was Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James and Joseph, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee.

**27:57** Now when evening had come, a certain rich man from Arimathea came, Joseph by name, who himself was also a disciple of Jesus;

**27:58** this man approached Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus. Then Pilate ordered the body to be handed over.

**27:59** And having taken the body, Joseph wrapped it in a clean linen cloth,

**27:60** and placed it in his new tomb, which he had carved out in the rock. And he rolled a large stone up to the doorway of the tomb, and departed.

**27:61** Now Mary Magdalene and the other Mary were there, sitting across from the grave.

**27:62** Now on the following day, which is after the Preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees came together to Pilate,

**27:63** saying, "Sir, we remember that while still alive, that deceiver said, 'After three days I will rise again.'

**27:64** Therefore order the grave to be guarded until the third day; otherwise His disciples might come and steal Him away and say to the people, 'He has risen from the dead,' and the final deception will be worse than the first."

**27:65** Pilate said to them, "You have a guard; go, guard it as you know how."

**27:66** So they departed and secured the grave, sealing the stone, together with the guards.

## Translator Notes

- **27:4** — The Vulgate reads sanguinem justum ("righteous blood"), whereas critical Greek editions read "innocent blood"; the Vulgate likewise includes justi ("righteous") in v. 24, which modern critical Greek texts omit.
- **27:8** — The Vulgate distinctively includes Haceldama, hoc est ("Akeldama, that is"), a reading absent from the Greek text of Matthew 27:8 and parallel to Acts 1:19.
- **27:16** — The Vulgate uses the singular verb habebat ("he had," referring to Pilate the governor), whereas the Greek text has the plural ("they had").
- **27:33** — Latin Calvariae locus literally means "Place of a Skull" (calvaria = skull), translating Aramaic Golgotha; this Latin term is the origin of the traditional English name "Calvary."
- **27:35** — The fulfillment quotation ("so that what was spoken... they cast lots," citing Psalm 22:18 / Vulg. 21:19) is present in the Clementine Vulgate (parallel to John 19:24) but absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts and modern critical Greek editions of Matthew.
- **27:40** — The Vulgate includes both the scornful interjection Vah! ("Aha!", parallel to Mark 15:29) and Dei ("of God") after templum, neither of which appears in modern critical Greek editions of Matthew 27:40.
- **27:42** — The Vulgate reads si ("if") before rex Israel est and uses the present indicative credimus ("we believe"), whereas critical Greek editions omit "if" and read the future ("we will believe").
