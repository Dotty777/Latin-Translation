## Text

**8:1** Now when He had come down from the mountain, large crowds followed Him.

**8:2** And look, a leper came up and worshiped Him, saying, "Lord, if You wish, You are able to cleanse me."

**8:3** And stretching out His hand, Jesus touched him, saying, "I wish it; be cleansed." And at once his leprosy was cleansed.

**8:4** And Jesus said to him, "See to it that you tell no one, but go, show yourself to the priest, and offer the gift that Moses prescribed, as a testimony to them."

**8:5** Now when He had entered Capernaum, a centurion approached Him, pleading with Him,

**8:6** and saying, "Lord, my boy lies paralyzed in the house and is severely tormented."

**8:7** And Jesus said to him, "I will come and cure him."

**8:8** And answering, the centurion said, "Lord, I am not worthy for You to enter under my roof, but only speak a word, and my boy will be healed.

**8:9** For I also am a man placed under authority, having soldiers under me; and I say to this one, 'Go,' and he goes; and to another, 'Come,' and he comes; and to my servant, 'Do this,' and he does it."

**8:10** Now hearing this, Jesus marveled, and He said to those following Him, "Amen, I tell you, I have not found such great faith in Israel.

**8:11** And I say to you that many will come from east and west, and will recline with Abraham and Isaac and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven,

**8:12** but the sons of the kingdom will be cast out into the outer darkness; there will be weeping and grinding of teeth."

**8:13** And Jesus said to the centurion, "Go, and just as you have believed, let it be done for you." And the boy was healed in that hour.

**8:14** And when Jesus had come into Peter's house, He saw his mother-in-law lying down and having a fever.

**8:15** And He touched her hand, and the fever left her; and she rose and was serving them.

**8:16** Now when evening had come, they brought to Him many who had demons; and He was casting out the spirits with a word, and He cured all who were ill,

**8:17** so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, saying, "He Himself took our infirmities and carried our illnesses."

**8:18** Now Jesus, seeing large crowds around Him, gave orders to go across the water.

**8:19** And one scribe approached and said to Him, "Teacher, I will follow You wherever You go."

**8:20** And Jesus said to him, "Foxes have dens and the birds of the sky have nests, but the Son of Man has nowhere to rest His head."

**8:21** And another of His disciples said to Him, "Lord, permit me first to go and bury my father."

**8:22** But Jesus said to him, "Follow Me, and let the dead bury their own dead."

**8:23** And as He stepped into the boat, His disciples followed Him.

**8:24** And look, a great upheaval arose on the sea, so that the boat was being covered by the waves; but He Himself was sleeping.

**8:25** And His disciples approached Him and roused Him, saying, "Lord, save us; we are perishing!"

**8:26** And Jesus said to them, "Why are you fearful, you of little faith?" Then rising, He commanded the winds and the sea, and a great calm came about.

**8:27** Moreover, the men marveled, saying, "What sort of man is this, that the winds and the sea obey Him?"

**8:28** And when He had come across the water into the region of the Gerasenes, two men who had demons met Him, coming out of the tombs, exceedingly fierce, so that no one could pass along that road.

**8:29** And look, they cried out, saying, "What is there between us and You, Jesus, Son of God? Have You come here before the time to torment us?"

**8:30** Now not far from them there was a herd of many pigs feeding.

**8:31** And the demons were pleading with Him, saying, "If You cast us out from here, send us into the herd of pigs."

**8:32** And He said to them, "Go." And coming out, they went into the pigs; and look, the whole herd rushed down the steep slope into the sea, and they died in the waters.

**8:33** Now the herdsmen fled, and coming into the city, they reported everything, and about those who had had demons.

**8:34** And look, the whole city went out to meet Jesus; and upon seeing Him, they pleaded that He would depart from their borders.

## Translator Notes

- **8:6** — Latin "puer" literally means "boy" and was commonly used for a young household servant, distinct from "servus" ("servant") in 8:9.
- **8:9** — The Vulgate includes the participle "constitutus" ("placed/set") after "sub potestate," which is absent from most Greek manuscripts of Matthew 8:9 (compare Luke 7:8).
- **8:15** — The Vulgate reads plural "eis" ("them") rather than singular "Him," which appears in many Greek manuscripts.
- **8:28** — The Vulgate reads "Gerasenorum" ("of the Gerasenes"), whereas Greek manuscripts of Matthew commonly read "Gadarenes" or "Gergesenes."
- **8:30** — The Vulgate distinctly reads "non longe ab illis" ("not far from them"), contrasting with the Greek reading "at a distance from them."
