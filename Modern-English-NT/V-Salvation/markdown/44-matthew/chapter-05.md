## Text

**5:1** Now seeing the crowds, Jesus went up onto the mountain, and when He had sat down, His disciples approached Him.

**5:2** And opening His mouth, He was teaching them, saying:

**5:3** "Blessed are the poor in spirit, because the kingdom of heaven is theirs.

**5:4** Blessed are those who mourn, because they will be comforted.

**5:5** Blessed are the meek, because they will possess the earth.

**5:6** Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, because they will be satisfied.

**5:7** Blessed are the merciful, because they will obtain mercy.

**5:8** Blessed are the pure in heart, because they will see God.

**5:9** Blessed are the peacemakers, because they will be called sons of God.

**5:10** Blessed are those who suffer persecution on account of righteousness, because the kingdom of heaven is theirs.

**5:11** Blessed are you when they revile you, and persecute you, and falsely say all evil against you on account of Me.

**5:12** Rejoice and exult, because your reward is abundant in heaven. For in this way they persecuted the prophets who were before you.

**5:13** You are the salt of the earth. But if the salt loses its strength, with what will it be salted? It is good for nothing any longer, except to be thrown outside and trampled by people.

**5:14** You are the light of the world. A city placed on a mountain cannot be hidden;

**5:15** nor do they light a lamp and put it under a grain measure, but on a lampstand, so that it may shine for all who are in the house.

**5:16** In this way let your light shine before people, so that they may see your good works and glorify your Father who is in heaven.

**5:17** Do not suppose that I have come to annul the law or the prophets; I have not come to annul, but to fulfill.

**5:18** For amen, I tell you, until heaven and earth pass away, not one iota or one stroke will pass from the law until all things take place.

**5:19** Therefore, whoever annuls one of these least commandments and teaches people so will be called least in the kingdom of heaven; but whoever does and teaches them, this one will be called great in the kingdom of heaven.

**5:20** For I tell you that unless your righteousness abounds more than that of the scribes and Pharisees, you will not enter into the kingdom of heaven.

**5:21** You have heard that it was said to the ancients, 'You shall not kill, and whoever kills will be liable to judgment.'

**5:22** But I tell you that everyone who is angry with his brother will be liable to judgment. And whoever says to his brother, 'Raca,' will be liable to the council. And whoever says, 'Fool,' will be liable to the Gehenna of fire.

**5:23** Therefore, if you are offering your gift at the altar, and there you remember that your brother has something against you,

**5:24** leave your gift there before the altar, and go first to be reconciled to your brother; and then come and offer your gift.

**5:25** Be in agreement with your adversary quickly while you are on the way with him, or else the adversary might hand you over to the judge, and the judge hand you over to the officer, and you be thrown into prison.

**5:26** Amen, I tell you, you will not come out from there until you pay the last quadrans.

**5:27** You have heard that it was said to the ancients, 'You shall not commit adultery.'

**5:28** But I tell you that everyone who looks at a woman to lust after her has already committed adultery with her in his heart.

**5:29** Now if your right eye causes you to stumble, tear it out and throw it away from you; for it is better for you that one of your members perish than for your whole body to be thrown into Gehenna.

**5:30** And if your right hand causes you to stumble, cut it off and throw it away from you; for it is better for you that one of your members perish than for your whole body to go into Gehenna.

**5:31** Now it was said, 'Whoever divorces his wife must give her a document of divorce.'

**5:32** But I tell you that everyone who divorces his wife, except for a cause of sexual immorality, causes her to commit adultery; and whoever marries a woman who has been divorced commits adultery.

**5:33** Again, you have heard that it was said to the ancients, 'You shall not swear falsely, but you shall fulfill your oaths to the Lord.'

**5:34** But I tell you not to swear at all: neither by heaven, because it is the throne of God,

**5:35** nor by the earth, because it is the footstool of His feet; nor by Jerusalem, because it is the city of the great King;

**5:36** nor shall you swear by your head, because you cannot make one hair white or black.

**5:37** But let your speech be, 'Yes, yes,' 'No, no'; and whatever is more than these is from evil.

**5:38** You have heard that it was said, 'An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.'

**5:39** But I tell you not to resist evil; rather, if anyone strikes you on your right cheek, offer him the other also.

**5:40** And to the one who wants to contend with you in court and take your tunic, let him have your cloak as well.

**5:41** And whoever compels you to go one mile, go with him two more.

**5:42** Give to whoever asks from you, and do not turn away from the one who wishes to borrow from you.

**5:43** You have heard that it was said, 'You shall love your neighbor, and you shall hate your enemy.'

**5:44** But I tell you: love your enemies, do good to those who hate you, and pray for those who persecute and slander you,

**5:45** so that you may be sons of your Father who is in heaven, who makes His sun rise on the good and the evil, and sends rain on the righteous and the unrighteous.

**5:46** For if you love those who love you, what reward will you have? Do not even the tax collectors do this?

**5:47** And if you greet your brothers only, what more do you do? Do not even the Gentiles do this?

**5:48** Therefore, you must be perfect, just as your heavenly Father also is perfect."

## Translator Notes

- **5:5** — Latin possidebunt ("will possess") reflects the Vulgate wording rather than the Greek verb meaning "inherit."
- **5:21** — Latin occides ... occiderit means "kill," rendered the same way as non occides in Mark 10:19 and Luke 18:20; in Matthew 19:18 the Vulgate instead reads homicidium ("murder").
- **5:22** — Raca is an Aramaic term of contempt, and gehennae ignis ("the Gehenna of fire") retains the Latin loanword referring to the Valley of Hinnom as an image of fiery judgment.
- **5:26** — A quadrans was the smallest Roman bronze coin, worth one-quarter of an as.
- **5:37** — Latin a malo can be understood either as neuter "from evil" or masculine "from the evil one," and the same ambiguity occurs with malo in 5:39.
- **5:44** — The Vulgate preserves the fuller clauses benefacite his qui oderunt vos ("do good to those who hate you") and et calumniantibus vos ("and slander you"), which are absent from most modern critical Greek editions; in 5:45 the Vulgate also reads super bonos et malos ("on the good and the evil") rather than the Greek order "the evil and the good."
