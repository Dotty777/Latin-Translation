## Text

**12:1** At that time Jesus went through the sown fields on the Sabbath, and His disciples, being hungry, began to pluck heads of grain and eat.

**12:2** Now the Pharisees, seeing this, said to Him, "Look, Your disciples are doing what is not lawful to do on the Sabbath."

**12:3** But He said to them, "Have you not read what David did when he was hungry, and those who were with him:

**12:4** how he entered into the house of God and ate the loaves of presentation, which it was not lawful for him to eat, nor for those who were with him, except for the priests alone?

**12:5** Or have you not read in the Law that on the Sabbath the priests in the temple violate the Sabbath and are without guilt?

**12:6** Yet I say to you that One greater than the temple is here.

**12:7** But if you knew what this means, 'I desire mercy and not sacrifice,' you would never have condemned the innocent.

**12:8** For the Son of Man is the Lord even of the Sabbath."

**12:9** And when He had moved on from there, He came into their synagogue.

**12:10** And look, a man was there who had a withered hand; and they questioned Him, saying, "Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath?" so that they might accuse Him.

**12:11** But He said to them, "What man will there be among you who has one sheep, and if it falls into a pit on the Sabbath, will he not take hold of it and lift it up?

**12:12** How much better indeed is a man than a sheep? Therefore it is lawful to do good on the Sabbath."

**12:13** Then He said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." And he stretched it out, and it was restored to health like the other.

**12:14** Now going out, the Pharisees were taking counsel against Him as to how they might destroy Him.

**12:15** But Jesus, knowing this, withdrew from there; and many followed Him, and He healed all of them,

**12:16** and He ordered them not to make Him known,

**12:17** so that what was spoken through the prophet Isaiah might be fulfilled, saying:

**12:18** "Here is My servant, whom I have chosen, My beloved, in whom My soul is thoroughly pleased. I will place My Spirit upon Him, and He will announce judgment to the nations.

**12:19** He will not contend, nor cry out, nor will anyone hear His voice in the streets;

**12:20** a crushed reed He will not break, and smoldering flax He will not extinguish, until He brings forth judgment to victory;

**12:21** and in His name the nations will hope."

**12:22** Then a man who had a demon, blind and mute, was brought to Him, and He healed him, so that he spoke and saw.

**12:23** And all the crowds were amazed and were saying, "Could this man be the Son of David?"

**12:24** But the Pharisees, hearing this, said, "This man does not cast out demons except by Beelzebub, the ruler of the demons."

**12:25** And Jesus, knowing their thoughts, said to them, "Every kingdom divided against itself will be laid waste, and every city or house divided against itself will not stand.

**12:26** And if Satan casts out Satan, he has become divided against himself; how, therefore, will his kingdom stand?

**12:27** And if I cast out demons by Beelzebub, by whom do your sons cast them out? Therefore they themselves will be your judges.

**12:28** But if I, by the Spirit of God, cast out demons, then the kingdom of God has reached you.

**12:29** Or how can anyone enter into the house of a strong man and plunder his belongings, unless he first binds the strong man? And then he will plunder his house.

**12:30** The one who is not with Me is against Me, and the one who does not gather with Me scatters.

**12:31** For this reason I say to you: Every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven people, but blasphemy against the Spirit will not be forgiven.

**12:32** And whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man, it will be forgiven him; but the one who speaks against the Holy Spirit, it will not be forgiven him, neither in this age nor in the age to come.

**12:33** Either make the tree good and its fruit good, or make the tree bad and its fruit bad; since by the fruit a tree is recognized.

**12:34** Offspring of vipers, how can you speak good things when you are evil? For from the overflow of the heart the mouth speaks.

**12:35** A good person brings out good things from a good treasury, and an evil person brings out evil things from an evil treasury.

**12:36** Now I say to you that for every idle word that people speak, they will render an account of it on the day of judgment.

**12:37** For from your words you will be justified, and from your words you will be condemned."

**12:38** Then some of the scribes and Pharisees answered Him, saying, "Teacher, we wish to see a sign from You."

**12:39** And answering, He said to them, "An evil and adulterous generation seeks a sign, and a sign will not be given to it except the sign of Jonah the prophet.

**12:40** For just as Jonah was in the belly of the sea monster for three days and three nights, so the Son of Man will be in the heart of the earth for three days and three nights.

**12:41** The men of Nineveh will rise up in the judgment with this generation and will condemn it, because they repented at the preaching of Jonah; and look, something greater than Jonah is here.

**12:42** The Queen of the South will rise up in the judgment with this generation and will condemn it, because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and look, something greater than Solomon is here.

**12:43** Now when an unclean spirit has gone out from a person, it walks through dry places, seeking rest, and finds none.

**12:44** Then it says, 'I will return to my house from which I came out.' And coming, it finds it unoccupied, swept clean, and adorned.

**12:45** Then it goes and takes along with itself seven other spirits more wicked than itself, and entering, they dwell there; and the last state of that person becomes worse than the former. So it will also be for this most wicked generation."

**12:46** While He was still speaking to the crowds, look, His mother and brothers were standing outside, seeking to speak to Him.

**12:47** Now someone said to Him, "Look, Your mother and Your brothers are standing outside, seeking You."

**12:48** But answering the one who was speaking to Him, He said, "Who is My mother, and who are My brothers?"

**12:49** And stretching out His hand toward His disciples, He said, "Here are My mother and My brothers.

**12:50** For whoever does the will of My Father who is in heaven, that one is My brother, and sister, and mother."

## Translator Notes

- **12:6** — The Vulgate uses the masculine comparative adjective major ("One greater"), whereas critical Greek texts read the neuter meizon ("something greater"); contrast the neuter plus ("something greater") in 12:41-42.
- **12:8** — The Vulgate includes etiam ("even"), reflecting the traditional Byzantine Greek reading (kai tou sabbatou) rather than modern critical Greek editions that omit the word.
- **12:24** — The Vulgate retains the spelling Beelzebub (here and in 12:27), echoing the Old Testament Baal-zebub (2 Kings 1:2), whereas critical Greek manuscripts read Beelzeboul (Beelzebul).
- **12:41** — The Latin idiom poenitentiam egerunt (literally "did penance" or "practiced repentance") is Jerome's standard rendering of the Greek verb metanoeo ("repent").
- **12:47** — The Vulgate reads simply quaerentes te ("seeking You"), shorter than most Greek manuscripts that add "to speak to you"; several early Greek manuscripts omit verse 47 altogether.
