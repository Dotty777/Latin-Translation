## Text

**10:1** And having summoned His twelve disciples, He gave them authority over unclean spirits, to drive them out and to heal every disease and every infirmity.

**10:2** Now the names of the twelve apostles are these: first, Simon, who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother;

**10:3** James son of Zebedee, and John his brother; Philip and Bartholomew; Thomas and Matthew the tax collector; James son of Alphaeus and Thaddaeus;

**10:4** Simon the Cananaean, and Judas Iscariot, who also handed Him over.

**10:5** These twelve Jesus sent out, instructing them and saying, "Do not go away onto the road of the Gentiles, and do not enter the cities of the Samaritans;

**10:6** but rather go to the sheep of the house of Israel that have been lost.

**10:7** And as you go, proclaim, saying, 'The kingdom of heaven has drawn near.'

**10:8** Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse the lepers, drive out demons; without cost you have received, without cost give.

**10:9** Do not possess gold, nor silver, nor money in your belts;

**10:10** no bag on the way, nor two tunics, nor sandals, nor a staff; for the worker is worthy of his food.

**10:11** Now into whatever city or village you enter, inquire who in it is worthy, and stay there until you depart.

**10:12** And as you enter the house, greet it, saying, 'Peace to this house.'

**10:13** And if indeed that house is worthy, your peace will come upon it; but if it is not worthy, your peace will return to you.

**10:14** And whoever does not receive you, nor hear your words, as you go outside from the house or city, shake off the dust from your feet.

**10:15** Amen, I tell you: It will be more bearable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrah on the day of judgment than for that city.

**10:16** Look, I am sending you like sheep in the midst of wolves. Therefore be prudent like serpents, and innocent like doves.

**10:17** Now be on guard against people. For they will hand you over in councils, and in their synagogues they will flog you;

**10:18** and you will be led before governors and before kings on account of Me, as a testimony to them and to the Gentiles.

**10:19** But when they hand you over, do not think about how or what you are to speak; for it will be given to you in that hour what you are to speak;

**10:20** for it is not you who are speaking, but the Spirit of your Father who speaks in you.

**10:21** Now brother will hand over brother to death, and a father his son; and children will rise up against parents and put them to death;

**10:22** and you will be hated by all on account of My name; but the one who perseveres all the way to the end will be saved.

**10:23** Now when they persecute you in this city, flee into another. Amen, I tell you, you will not finish the cities of Israel until the Son of Man comes.

**10:24** A disciple is not above the teacher, nor a servant above his master;

**10:25** it is enough for the disciple to be like his teacher, and for the servant to be like his master. If they have called the head of the household Beelzebub, how much more those of his household?

**10:26** Therefore do not fear them. For nothing is covered that will not be revealed, nor hidden that will not be known.

**10:27** What I tell you in the darkness, speak in the light; and what you hear in the ear, proclaim upon the rooftops.

**10:28** And do not fear those who kill the body, yet cannot kill the soul; but rather fear Him who is able to destroy both soul and body in Gehenna.

**10:29** Are not two sparrows sold for a copper coin? Yet not one of them will fall to the ground without your Father.

**10:30** Now all the hairs of your head are numbered.

**10:31** Therefore do not fear: you are better than many sparrows.

**10:32** Therefore everyone who confesses Me in the presence of people, I too will confess him in the presence of My Father who is in heaven.

**10:33** But whoever denies Me in the presence of people, I too will deny him in the presence of My Father who is in heaven.

**10:34** Do not suppose that I have come to send peace onto the earth; I have not come to send peace, but a sword;

**10:35** for I have come to separate a man against his father, and a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;

**10:36** and a man's enemies are those of his household.

**10:37** The one who loves father or mother more than Me is not worthy of Me; and the one who loves son or daughter above Me is not worthy of Me.

**10:38** And the one who does not take his cross and follow Me is not worthy of Me.

**10:39** The one who finds his life will lose it, and the one who loses his life on account of Me will find it.

**10:40** The one who receives you receives Me, and the one who receives Me receives Him who sent Me.

**10:41** The one who receives a prophet in the name of a prophet will obtain the reward of a prophet, and the one who receives a righteous person in the name of a righteous person will obtain the reward of a righteous person.

**10:42** And whoever gives one of these least ones only a cup of cold water to drink in the name of a disciple, amen, I tell you, he will not lose his reward."

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — Latin "Chananæus" transliterates Greek "Kananaios" (from Aramaic for "zealot") with the Vulgate spelling for "Canaanite"; it is rendered "Cananaean" here rather than harmonized to "Zealot" from Luke 6:15.
- **10:5** — The Vulgate has the plural "civitates" ("cities"), whereas the Greek text has the singular "city."
- **10:12** — The Clementine Vulgate includes "dicentes: Pax huic domui" ("saying, 'Peace to this house'"), a clause absent from critical Greek editions.
- **10:25** — The Vulgate preserves the spelling "Beelzebub" (echoing 2 Kings 1:2) rather than the Greek form "Beelzebul."
- **10:42** — The Vulgate uses the superlative "minimis" ("least ones"), whereas the Greek uses the positive adjective "little ones."
