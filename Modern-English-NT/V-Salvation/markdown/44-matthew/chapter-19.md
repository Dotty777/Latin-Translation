## Text

**19:1** And it happened that, when Jesus had completed these discourses, He departed from Galilee and came into the borders of Judea across the Jordan,

**19:2** and large crowds followed Him, and He healed them there.

**19:3** And Pharisees approached Him, testing Him and saying, "Is it lawful for a man to send away his wife for any cause whatever?"

**19:4** Answering, He said to them, "Have you not read that He who made man from the beginning made them male and female? And He said,

**19:5** 'For this reason a man will leave father and mother and cling to his wife, and the two will be in one flesh.'

**19:6** Consequently, they are no longer two, but one flesh. Therefore, what God has joined together, man must not separate."

**19:7** They said to Him, "Why then did Moses command one to give a certificate of divorce and send her away?"

**19:8** He said to them, "Because Moses, on account of the hardness of your heart, permitted you to send away your wives; from the beginning, however, it was not like this.

**19:9** Now I tell you that whoever sends away his wife, except on account of fornication, and marries another commits adultery; and the one who marries a woman who has been sent away commits adultery."

**19:10** His disciples said to Him, "If the case of a man with a wife is like this, it is not advantageous to marry."

**19:11** He said to them, "Not all receive this word, but those to whom it has been given.

**19:12** For there are eunuchs who were born that way from their mother's womb; and there are eunuchs who were made so by men; and there are eunuchs who have castrated themselves for the sake of the kingdom of heaven. Whoever can receive it, let him receive it."

**19:13** Then little children were brought to Him, so that He might lay hands on them and pray. But the disciples were rebuking them.

**19:14** But Jesus said to them, "Leave the little children alone, and do not prevent them from coming to Me, for it is to such as these that the kingdom of heaven belongs."

**19:15** And when He had laid hands on them, He went away from there.

**19:16** And look, someone approached and said to Him, "Good Teacher, what good thing should I do so that I may have eternal life?"

**19:17** He said to him, "Why do you question Me about what is good? One is good: God. Now if you want to enter into life, keep the commandments."

**19:18** He said to Him, "Which ones?" And Jesus said, "You shall not commit murder; you shall not commit adultery; you shall not commit theft; you shall not give false testimony;

**19:19** honor your father and your mother; and you shall love your neighbor as yourself."

**19:20** The young man said to Him, "All these things I have observed from my youth; what do I still lack?"

**19:21** Jesus said to him, "If you want to be perfect, go, sell what you have, and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven; and come, follow Me."

**19:22** Now when the young man had heard the word, he went away sorrowful, for he had many possessions.

**19:23** And Jesus said to His disciples, "Amen, I tell you that a rich man will enter the kingdom of heaven with difficulty.

**19:24** And again I tell you, it is easier for a camel to pass through a needle's eye than for a rich man to enter the kingdom of heaven."

**19:25** Now on hearing these things, the disciples were greatly amazed, saying, "Who, then, will be able to be saved?"

**19:26** Looking steadily, Jesus said to them, "With human beings this is impossible; with God, however, everything is possible."

**19:27** Then, answering, Peter said to Him, "Look, we have left everything and followed You; what then will there be for us?"

**19:28** And Jesus said to them, "Amen, I tell you that you who have followed Me, in the regeneration, when the Son of Man sits on the throne of His majesty, you also will sit upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.

**19:29** And everyone who has left house, or brothers, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or fields for the sake of My name will receive a hundredfold and will possess eternal life.

**19:30** However, many who are first will be last, and the last will be first."

## Translator Notes

- **19:4** — The Clementine Vulgate explicitly reads qui fecit hominem ab initio ("He who made man from the beginning"), whereas Greek manuscripts omit "man" here.
- **19:17** — Combining Magister bone ("Good Teacher") in verse 16 with Quid me interrogas de bono? ("Why do you question Me about what is good?"), the Vulgate explicitly adds Deus ("God") in Unus est bonus, Deus ("One is good: God").
- **19:24** — The Clementine Vulgate reads in regnum caelorum ("into the kingdom of heaven"), whereas Greek manuscripts read "the kingdom of God" here.
- **19:28** — The Vulgate renders the phrase in sede majestatis suae ("on the throne of His majesty"), where the Greek text has "glory."
- **19:29** — The Vulgate has singular domum ("house"), includes aut uxorem ("or wife"), and renders possidebit ("will possess") where the Greek has "will inherit."
