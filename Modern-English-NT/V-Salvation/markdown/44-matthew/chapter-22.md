## Text

**22:1** And answering, Jesus spoke to them again in parables, saying,

**22:2** "The kingdom of heaven has become like a man who was a king, who held a wedding feast for his son.

**22:3** And he sent his servants to call those who had been invited to the wedding feast, and they did not want to come.

**22:4** Again he sent other servants, saying, 'Tell those who have been invited, "Look, I have prepared my dinner; my bulls and fattened animals have been slaughtered, and everything is ready; come to the wedding feast."'

**22:5** But they disregarded it and went away, one to his farm and another to his business,

**22:6** while the rest laid hold of his servants, subjected them to abuse, and killed them.

**22:7** Now when the king heard this, he grew angry; and having sent his armies, he destroyed those murderers and set fire to their city.

**22:8** Then he said to his servants, 'The wedding feast is indeed ready, but those who had been invited were not worthy.

**22:9** Go therefore to the outlets of the roads, and whoever you find, call to the wedding feast.'

**22:10** And his servants went out into the roads and gathered together all whom they found, bad and good; and the wedding feast was filled with those reclining at table.

**22:11** Now the king entered to see those reclining at table, and he saw there a man not clothed in a wedding garment.

**22:12** And he said to him, 'Friend, how did you enter here without having a wedding garment?' But he fell silent.

**22:13** Then the king said to the attendants, 'Bind his hands and feet and cast him into the outer darkness; there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.'

**22:14** For many are called, yet few are chosen."

**22:15** Then going away, the Pharisees took counsel so that they might catch Him in speech.

**22:16** And they sent their disciples to Him with the Herodians, saying, "Teacher, we know that You are truthful and teach the way of God in truth, and You are not concerned about anyone, for You do not look to the person of men;

**22:17** tell us therefore what You think: is it lawful to pay the census tax to Caesar, or not?"

**22:18** But Jesus, recognizing their wickedness, said, "Why are you testing Me, hypocrites?

**22:19** Show Me the coin of the census tax." Then they presented a denarius to Him.

**22:20** And Jesus said to them, "Whose is this image and inscription?"

**22:21** They said to Him, "Caesar's." Then He said to them, "Give back therefore what is Caesar's to Caesar, and what is God's to God."

**22:22** When they heard this, they marveled, and having left Him, they went away.

**22:23** On that day, Sadducees, who say that there is no resurrection, approached Him and questioned Him,

**22:24** saying, "Teacher, Moses said that if anyone dies without having a son, his brother should marry his wife and raise up seed for his brother.

**22:25** Now there were seven brothers among us; and the first, having taken a wife, died, and having no seed, left his wife to his brother.

**22:26** Similarly, the second and the third, all the way to the seventh.

**22:27** And last of all, the woman died as well.

**22:28** In the resurrection, therefore, whose wife among the seven will she be? For they all had her."

**22:29** But answering, Jesus said to them, "You are in error, not knowing the Scriptures nor the power of God.

**22:30** For in the resurrection they will neither marry nor be married, but will be like the angels of God in heaven.

**22:31** Now concerning the resurrection of the dead, have you not read what was spoken by God, saying to you,

**22:32** 'I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob'? He is not a God of the dead, but of the living."

**22:33** And hearing this, the crowds were marveling at His teaching.

**22:34** Now when the Pharisees heard that He had imposed silence on the Sadducees, they came together as one,

**22:35** and one of them, a teacher of the law, questioned Him, testing Him,

**22:36** "Teacher, what is the great commandment in the law?"

**22:37** Jesus said to him, "You shall love the Lord your God from your whole heart, and in your whole soul, and in your whole mind.

**22:38** This is the greatest and foremost commandment.

**22:39** And the second is like this one: 'You shall love your neighbor just as yourself.'

**22:40** Upon these two commandments depends the entire law, and the prophets."

**22:41** Now when the Pharisees had gathered together, Jesus questioned them,

**22:42** saying, "What do you think about the Christ? Whose son is He?" They said to Him, "David's."

**22:43** He said to them, "How therefore does David, in the Spirit, call Him Lord, saying,

**22:44** 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at My right hand, until I place Your enemies as a footstool for Your feet"'?

**22:45** If therefore David calls Him Lord, how is He his son?"

**22:46** And no one could answer Him a word, nor did anyone dare from that day forward to question Him any further.

## Translator Notes

- **22:7** — The Clementine Vulgate includes the clause "when he heard this" (cum audisset), reflecting Byzantine and Western Greek witnesses, whereas modern critical Greek editions omit it.
- **22:24** — The Vulgate reads singular "a son" (filium), whereas the Greek text reads plural "children" (tekna).
- **22:30** — The Vulgate retains "of God" (angeli Dei) and uses future-tense verbs throughout (nubent, nubentur, erunt), whereas modern critical Greek editions omit "of God."
- **22:37** — The Vulgate shifts from "from" (ex) with the heart to "in" (in) with the soul and mind, whereas the Greek text repeats a single preposition.
- **22:44** — The Vulgate reads "a footstool for Your feet" (scabellum pedum tuorum, agreeing with Psalm 109:1 [110:1] and majority Greek witnesses), whereas modern critical Greek editions read "underneath Your feet."
