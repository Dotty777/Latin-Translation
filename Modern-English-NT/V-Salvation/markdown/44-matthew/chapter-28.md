## Text

**28:1** Now in the evening of the Sabbath, as it was growing light toward the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to see the tomb.

**28:2** And look, a great earthquake took place. For an angel of the Lord descended from heaven and, approaching, rolled back the stone and was sitting on it.

**28:3** Now his appearance was like lightning, and his garment like snow.

**28:4** And out of fear of him, the guards were terrified and became like dead men.

**28:5** But the angel answered and said to the women, "As for you, do not be afraid, for I know that you are seeking Jesus, who was crucified.

**28:6** He is not here; for He has risen, just as He said. Come and see the place where the Lord had been laid.

**28:7** And going quickly, tell His disciples that He has risen. And look, He is going ahead of you into Galilee; there you will see Him. Look, I have told you beforehand."

**28:8** And they went out quickly from the tomb with fear and great joy, running to bring word to His disciples.

**28:9** And look, Jesus met them, saying, "Greetings!" And they approached and grasped His feet and worshiped Him.

**28:10** Then Jesus said to them, "Do not be afraid. Go, bring word to My brothers that they should go into Galilee; there they will see Me."

**28:11** Now when they had departed, look, some of the guards came into the city and reported to the chief priests all the things that had taken place.

**28:12** And having assembled with the elders and taken counsel, they gave a large amount of money to the soldiers,

**28:13** saying, "Say, 'His disciples came at night and stole Him while we were sleeping.'

**28:14** And if this is heard by the governor, we will persuade him and make you secure."

**28:15** So they, having accepted the money, did just as they had been instructed. And this account has been spread widely among the Jews up to this present day.

**28:16** Now the eleven disciples departed into Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus had directed them.

**28:17** And seeing Him, they worshiped; but some doubted.

**28:18** And approaching, Jesus spoke to them, saying, "All power has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.

**28:19** Therefore go and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit,

**28:20** teaching them to keep everything that I have commanded you. And look, I am with you every day, even to the consummation of the age."

## Translator Notes

- **28:1** — The Vulgate reads Vespere ("in the evening") for the close of the Sabbath day, followed by feminine quæ and prima sabbati (literally "first of the Sabbath," an idiom for the first day of the week) with "day" or "hour" implied.
- **28:3** — Unlike Greek manuscripts that describe the angel's garment as "white as snow," the Clementine Vulgate reads simply sicut nix ("like snow").
- **28:6** — The Clementine Vulgate reads ubi positus erat Dominus ("where the Lord had been laid"), using a pluperfect passive verb and explicitly naming "the Lord" where critical Greek texts read simply "where he lay."
- **28:7** — The Clementine Vulgate reads simply quia surrexit ("that He has risen"), without the phrase "from the dead" present in Greek manuscripts, and uses prædixi ("I have told you beforehand") at the close of the angel's speech.
- **28:19** — The Vulgate renders the command as docete omnes gentes ("teach all nations") rather than "make disciples of all nations" (the sense of the Greek verb), and uses in nomine ("in the name of") with the ablative case.
