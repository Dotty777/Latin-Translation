## Text

**21:1** And when they had drawn near to Jerusalem and had come to Bethphage at the Mount of Olives, then Jesus sent two disciples,

**21:2** saying to them, "Go into the village that lies opposite you, and immediately you will find a donkey tied up, and a colt with her; untie them and bring them to Me.

**21:3** And if anyone says anything to you, say, 'The Lord has need of these,' and at once he will let them go."

**21:4** Now all this happened so that what was spoken through the prophet might be fulfilled, saying,

**21:5** "Say to the daughter of Zion, 'Look, your king is coming to you, gentle, sitting upon a donkey, and a colt, the foal of a beast under the yoke.'"

**21:6** Now the disciples went and did as Jesus had commanded them.

**21:7** And they brought the donkey and the colt, and placed their garments on them, and had Him sit on top.

**21:8** Now a very great crowd spread their garments on the road, while others were cutting branches from the trees and spreading them on the road.

**21:9** And the crowds that were going ahead and that were following were crying out, saying, "Hosanna to the Son of David! Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord! Hosanna in the highest!"

**21:10** And when He had entered Jerusalem, the entire city was thrown into commotion, saying, "Who is this?"

**21:11** And the people were saying, "This is Jesus, the prophet from Nazareth of Galilee."

**21:12** And Jesus entered the temple of God and was driving out all those selling and buying in the temple, and He overturned the tables of the moneychangers and the seats of those selling doves.

**21:13** And He said to them, "It is written, 'My house will be called a house of prayer,' but you have made it a cave of robbers."

**21:14** And the blind and the lame approached Him in the temple, and He healed them.

**21:15** But when the chief priests and the scribes saw the marvelous things that He did, and the children crying out in the temple and saying, "Hosanna to the Son of David!" they became indignant,

**21:16** and said to Him, "Do You hear what these are saying?" And Jesus said to them, "Yes. Have you never read, 'From the mouth of infants and those who are nursing You have perfected praise'?"

**21:17** And leaving them, He went out beyond the city to Bethany and stayed there.

**21:18** Now in the morning, returning into the city, He grew hungry.

**21:19** And seeing a single fig tree beside the road, He came to it and found nothing on it except leaves alone, and He said to it, "May fruit never grow from you forever." And instantly the fig tree dried up.

**21:20** And seeing it, the disciples marveled, saying, "How did it dry up instantly?"

**21:21** And Jesus answered and said to them, "Amen, I tell you, if you have faith and do not hesitate, you will not only do what happened to the fig tree, but even if you say to this mountain, 'Lift yourself up and throw yourself into the sea,' it will be done.

**21:22** And all things, whatever you ask in prayer, believing, you will receive."

**21:23** And when He had come into the temple, the chief priests and the elders of the people approached Him as He was teaching, saying, "By what authority do You do these things? And who gave You this authority?"

**21:24** Answering, Jesus said to them, "I too will ask you one thing; if you tell it to Me, I too will tell you by what authority I do these things.

**21:25** Where was the baptism of John from? From heaven, or from men?" But they were reasoning among themselves, saying,

**21:26** "If we say, 'From heaven,' He will say to us, 'Why then did you not believe him?' But if we say, 'From men,' we fear the crowd, for all regarded John as a prophet."

**21:27** And answering Jesus, they said, "We do not know." He Himself also said to them, "Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things.

**21:28** Now what do you think? A certain man had two sons, and approaching the first, he said, 'Son, go today, work in my vineyard.'

**21:29** But he answered and said, 'I will not.' Afterward, however, moved by repentance, he went.

**21:30** And approaching the other, he spoke in the same way. But he answered and said, 'I am going, sir,' and he did not go.

**21:31** Which of the two did the will of the father?" They said to Him, "The first." Jesus said to them, "Amen, I tell you that tax collectors and prostitutes will go ahead of you into the kingdom of God.

**21:32** For John came to you in the way of righteousness, and you did not believe him, whereas tax collectors and prostitutes believed him; and you, seeing this, did not even repent afterward so as to believe him.

**21:33** Hear another parable: There was a man, a householder, who planted a vineyard, and put a hedge around it, and dug a winepress in it, and built a tower, and leased it to farmers, and departed abroad.

**21:34** Now when the time of the fruits had drawn near, he sent his servants to the farmers so that they might receive its fruits.

**21:35** And the farmers, having seized his servants, beat one, killed another, and stoned still another.

**21:36** Again he sent other servants, more than the earlier ones, and they did to them in the same way.

**21:37** And last of all he sent his son to them, saying, 'They will respect my son.'

**21:38** But the farmers, seeing the son, said among themselves, 'This is the heir; come, let us kill him, and we will have his inheritance.'

**21:39** And seizing him, they threw him outside the vineyard and killed him.

**21:40** Therefore, when the master of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those farmers?"

**21:41** They said to Him, "He will miserably destroy those wicked men and will lease his vineyard to other farmers, who will deliver the fruit to him in its seasons."

**21:42** Jesus said to them, "Have you never read in the Scriptures, 'The stone that the builders rejected, this one has been made the head of the corner; by the Lord this was done, and it is marvelous in our eyes'?

**21:43** Therefore I say to you that the kingdom of God will be taken away from you and given to a nation producing its fruits.

**21:44** And the one who falls on this stone will be shattered; but on whomever it falls, it will crush him."

**21:45** And when the chief priests and the Pharisees had heard His parables, they realized that He was speaking about them.

**21:46** And while seeking to lay hold of Him, they feared the crowds, because they regarded Him as a prophet.

## Translator Notes

- **21:5** — Latin 'filium subjugalis' is literally 'the son of one under the yoke,' referring to the young of a yoked draft animal or beast of burden.
- **21:7** — The Vulgate reads 'eum desuper sedere fecerunt' ('had Him sit on top'), using a plural causative verb rather than the singular 'He sat' found in most modern critical Greek editions.
- **21:21** — Latin 'Tolle, et jacta te' uses active and reflexive imperatives ('Lift yourself up and throw yourself') where Greek manuscripts have passive imperatives ('Be taken up and be thrown').
- **21:30** — The Vulgate renders the second son's reply as 'Eo, domine' ('I am going, sir'), using the verb 'to go' where the Greek text has the pronoun 'Ego' ('I [will], sir').
- **21:41** — Latin 'Malos male perdet' preserves a sharp wordplay on 'malus' (literally 'the wicked ones he will wickedly/miserably destroy').
