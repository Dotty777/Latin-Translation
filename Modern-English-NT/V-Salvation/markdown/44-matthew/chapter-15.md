## Text

**15:1** Then scribes and Pharisees from Jerusalem approached Him, saying,

**15:2** "Why do Your disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? For they do not wash their hands when they eat bread."

**15:3** But He answered and said to them, "Why do you also transgress the commandment of God because of your tradition? For God said,

**15:4** 'Honor father and mother,' and, 'He who curses father or mother must certainly die.'

**15:5** But you say, 'Whoever says to father or mother, "Whatever gift is from me will benefit you,"

**15:6** and he will not honor his father or his mother.' And you have nullified the commandment of God because of your tradition.

**15:7** Hypocrites! Isaiah prophesied well concerning you, saying,

**15:8** 'This people honors Me with their lips, but their heart is far from Me.

**15:9** Yet they worship Me in vain, teaching the doctrines and commandments of men.'"

**15:10** And having summoned the crowds to Himself, He said to them, "Listen and understand.

**15:11** What enters into the mouth does not defile a man; rather, what comes forth from the mouth, this defiles a man."

**15:12** Then His disciples approached and said to Him, "Do You know that the Pharisees took offense when they heard this saying?"

**15:13** But He answered and said, "Every planting that My heavenly Father did not plant will be uprooted.

**15:14** Leave them alone; they are blind, and guides of the blind. And if a blind man guides a blind man, both fall into a pit."

**15:15** But Peter answered and said to Him, "Explain this parable to us."

**15:16** And He said, "Are you also still without understanding?

**15:17** Do you not understand that everything that enters into the mouth goes into the stomach and is discharged into the latrine?

**15:18** But the things that come forth from the mouth come out from the heart, and those defile a man.

**15:19** For from the heart come out evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false testimonies, blasphemies.

**15:20** These are the things that defile a man. But to eat with unwashed hands does not defile a man."

**15:21** And going out from there, Jesus withdrew into the districts of Tyre and Sidon.

**15:22** And look, a Canaanite woman, coming out from those borders, cried out, saying to Him, "Have mercy on me, Lord, Son of David! My daughter is severely tormented by a demon."

**15:23** But He did not answer her a word. And His disciples approached and were asking Him, saying, "Send her away, because she is crying out after us."

**15:24** But He answered and said, "I have been sent only to the sheep of the house of Israel that have been lost."

**15:25** Yet she came and worshiped Him, saying, "Lord, help me."

**15:26** And He answered and said, "It is not good to take the bread of the children and throw it to the dogs."

**15:27** But she said, "Yes, Lord; for even the puppies eat from the crumbs that fall from the table of their masters."

**15:28** Then Jesus answered and said to her, "Woman, great is your faith! Let it be done for you as you wish." And her daughter was healed from that hour.

**15:29** And when Jesus had moved on from there, He came beside the Sea of Galilee; and going up onto the mountain, He was sitting there.

**15:30** And great crowds approached Him, having with them the mute, the blind, the lame, the crippled, and many others; and they laid them down at His feet, and He healed them,

**15:31** so that the crowds were amazed as they saw the mute speaking, the lame walking, and the blind seeing; and they magnified the God of Israel.

**15:32** Then Jesus, having summoned His disciples, said, "I have compassion on the crowd, because for three days now they have remained with Me and have nothing to eat; and I do not wish to send them away fasting, so that they do not faint on the way."

**15:33** And the disciples said to Him, "Where then can we get so many loaves in the wilderness to satisfy so great a crowd?"

**15:34** And Jesus said to them, "How many loaves do you have?" And they said, "Seven, and a few small fish."

**15:35** And He commanded the crowd to recline on the ground.

**15:36** And taking the seven loaves and the fish, and giving thanks, He broke them and gave them to His disciples, and the disciples gave them to the people.

**15:37** And they all ate and were satisfied. And they picked up what was left over of the fragments, seven large baskets full.

**15:38** Now those who ate were four thousand men, besides little children and women.

**15:39** And having sent the crowd away, He boarded a boat and came into the borders of Magadan.

## Translator Notes

- **15:5** — The Vulgate renders the elliptical Greek clause as a complete statement, "Whatever gift is from me will benefit you" (Latin Munus, quodcumque est ex me, tibi proderit), whereas Greek-based translations usually read "What you would have gained from me is an offering [to God]." In 15:6, the Clementine Vulgate also includes "or his mother" and reads "commandment" (mandatum) rather than "word."
- **15:9** — The Clementine Vulgate includes "and" (et) in docentes doctrinas et mandata hominum ("teaching the doctrines and commandments of men"), whereas the Greek reads "teaching as doctrines the commandments of men."
- **15:26** — Unlike the Greek, which uses the diminutive kynaria ("little dogs") in both 15:26 and 15:27, the Vulgate distinguishes canibus ("dogs") in Jesus's reply from the diminutive catelli ("puppies" or "little dogs") in the woman's response.
- **15:31** — Unlike most Greek manuscripts, the Clementine Vulgate omits "the crippled made whole" from the list of miracles witnessed by the crowd, naming only the mute speaking, the lame walking, and the blind seeing.
- **15:39** — The Vulgate reads Magedan (Magadan), agreeing with the earliest Greek witnesses against the later Textus Receptus reading "Magdala."
