## Text

**24:1** And having gone out from the temple, Jesus was walking away. And His disciples approached to show Him the buildings of the temple.

**24:2** But He answered and said to them, "Do you see all these things? Amen, I tell you, not a stone will be left here upon a stone that will not be torn down."

**24:3** Now while He was sitting on the Mount of Olives, the disciples approached Him privately, saying, "Tell us, when will these things be? And what will be the sign of Your coming and of the consummation of the age?"

**24:4** And Jesus answered and said to them, "Watch out that no one leads you astray;

**24:5** for many will come in My name, saying, 'I am the Christ,' and they will lead many astray.

**24:6** For you are going to hear of wars and reports of wars. Watch out that you are not troubled; for these things must take place, but the end is not yet;

**24:7** for nation will rise up against nation, and kingdom against kingdom, and there will be plagues, and famines, and earthquakes in various places;

**24:8** but all these things are the beginnings of pains.

**24:9** Then they will hand you over to tribulation and kill you, and you will be an object of hatred to all nations on account of My name.

**24:10** And then many will stumble, and they will hand one another over, and will hate one another.

**24:11** And many false prophets will rise up, and they will lead many astray.

**24:12** And since iniquity has abounded, the love of many will grow cold;

**24:13** but the one who perseveres to the very end, this one will be saved.

**24:14** And this gospel of the kingdom will be proclaimed in the entire world as a testimony to all nations, and then the consummation will come.

**24:15** Therefore, when you see the abomination of desolation, which was spoken of by the prophet Daniel, standing in the holy place (let the one who reads understand),

**24:16** then those who are in Judea, let them flee to the mountains;

**24:17** and the one on the roof, let him not go down to take anything from his house;

**24:18** and the one in the field, let him not turn back to take his tunic.

**24:19** But woe to those who are pregnant and to those who are nursing in those days!

**24:20** But pray that your flight may not take place in winter or on a Sabbath;

**24:21** for then there will be great tribulation, such as has not taken place from the beginning of the world up to now, nor will take place.

**24:22** And unless those days had been shortened, no flesh would be saved; but on account of the elect, those days will be shortened.

**24:23** Then if anyone says to you, 'Look, here is the Christ,' or 'There,' do not believe it.

**24:24** For false christs and false prophets will rise up, and they will give great signs and wonders, so that (if it is possible) even the elect might be led into error.

**24:25** Look, I have told you in advance.

**24:26** Therefore, if they say to you, 'Look, He is in the desert,' do not go out; 'Look, in the inner rooms,' do not believe it.

**24:27** For just as lightning goes out from the east and appears all the way to the west, so also will the coming of the Son of Man be.

**24:28** Wherever the body is, there the eagles also will be gathered.

**24:29** Now immediately after the tribulation of those days, the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light, and the stars will fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens will be shaken;

**24:30** and then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in heaven; and then all the tribes of the earth will lament, and they will see the Son of Man coming in the clouds of heaven with great power and majesty.

**24:31** And He will send His angels with a trumpet and a loud voice, and they will gather His elect from the four winds, from the heights of the heavens all the way to their boundaries.

**24:32** Now learn a parable from the fig tree: when its branch has already become tender and leaves have sprung forth, you know that summer is near;

**24:33** so you also, when you see all these things, know that it is near, at the doors.

**24:34** Amen, I tell you that this generation will not pass away until all these things take place.

**24:35** Heaven and earth will pass away; My words, however, will not pass away.

**24:36** Now concerning that day and hour no one knows, not even the angels of the heavens, except the Father alone.

**24:37** Now just as in the days of Noah, so also will the coming of the Son of Man be;

**24:38** for just as in the days before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, up to that day on which Noah entered the ark,

**24:39** and they did not know until the flood came and carried them all away; so also will the coming of the Son of Man be.

**24:40** Then two men will be in the field: one will be taken, and one will be left behind.

**24:41** Two women will be grinding at the mill: one will be taken, and one will be left behind.

**24:42** Keep watch, therefore, because you do not know at what hour your Lord is coming.

**24:43** Now know this, that if the head of the household knew at what hour the thief was going to come, he would certainly keep watch and would not allow his house to be broken into.

**24:44** For this reason, you also be ready, because at an hour you do not know, the Son of Man is coming.

**24:45** Who, do you think, is the faithful and prudent servant whom his master appointed over his household to give them food at the proper time?

**24:46** Blessed is that servant whom his master, when he comes, will find doing so.

**24:47** Amen, I tell you that he will appoint him over all his possessions.

**24:48** But if that wicked servant says in his heart, 'My master has delayed in coming,'

**24:49** and begins to beat his fellow servants, and also eats and drinks with drunkards,

**24:50** the master of that servant will come on a day he does not expect and at an hour of which he is unaware,

**24:51** and he will divide him and place his portion with the hypocrites; there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth in that place."

## Translator Notes

- **24:2** — Latin Videtis haec omnia? ("Do you see all these things?") is an affirmative question without the negation ("Do you not see...?") found in the Greek text.
- **24:7** — The Clementine Vulgate includes pestilentiae ("plagues") and places it before fames ("famines"), whereas critical Greek editions omit "plagues" and Byzantine manuscripts list "famines" first.
- **24:31** — Latin cum tuba, et voce magna ("with a trumpet and a loud voice") coordinates two nouns with et, whereas Greek critical texts read "with a loud trumpet" (and Byzantine texts read "with a trumpet of a great sound").
- **24:36** — Following the Latin and Byzantine textual tradition, the Clementine Vulgate omits "nor the Son," reading only neque angeli caelorum, nisi solus Pater ("not even the angels of the heavens, except the Father alone").
- **24:42** — Both here (qua hora, "at what hour") and in verse 43 (qua hora), the Clementine Vulgate reads "hour," whereas critical Greek texts read "what day" in verse 42 and "what watch" in verse 43.
