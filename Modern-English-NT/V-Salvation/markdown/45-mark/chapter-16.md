## Text

**16:1** And when the Sabbath had passed, Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought spices so that they might come and anoint Jesus.

**16:2** And very early in the morning on the first day of the week, they came to the tomb, when the sun had already risen.

**16:3** And they were saying to each other, "Who will roll back the stone for us from the entrance of the tomb?"

**16:4** And looking, they saw the stone rolled back. For indeed it was very large.

**16:5** And entering the tomb, they saw a young man sitting on the right side, clothed in a bright white robe, and they were astounded.

**16:6** And he said to them, "Do not be alarmed. You are seeking Jesus the Nazarene, who was crucified. He has risen; He is not here. Here is the place where they laid Him.

**16:7** But go, tell His disciples and Peter that He is going ahead of you into Galilee; there you will see Him, just as He said to you."

**16:8** And going out, they fled from the tomb, for trembling and terror had overtaken them; and they told no one anything, for they were afraid.

**16:9** Now rising early in the morning on the first day of the week, He appeared first to Mary Magdalene, from whom He had driven out seven demons.

**16:10** She went and brought word to those who had been with Him, as they were mourning and weeping.

**16:11** And hearing that He was alive and had been seen by her, they did not believe.

**16:12** After these things, He was manifested in a different form to two of them as they were walking, going into the countryside;

**16:13** and they went and brought word to the rest, nor did they believe them.

**16:14** Finally, He appeared to those eleven as they were reclining at table, and He reproached their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they did not believe those who had seen that He had risen.

**16:15** And He said to them, "Going into the entire world, preach the Gospel to every creature.

**16:16** The one who believes and is baptized will be saved, but the one who does not believe will be condemned.

**16:17** And these signs will follow those who believe: in My name they will drive out demons; they will speak in new tongues;

**16:18** they will take up serpents, and if they drink anything deadly, it will not harm them; they will lay hands on the sick, and they will be well."

**16:19** And indeed the Lord Jesus, after He had spoken to them, was taken up into heaven and sits at the right hand of God.

**16:20** They, however, set out and preached everywhere, the Lord working alongside them and confirming the word with the signs that followed.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — Vulgate specifies Jesum ("Jesus") rather than a pronoun ("him") as in most Greek manuscripts.
- **16:2** — Latin una sabbatorum (literally "on one of the Sabbaths") is a Semitic idiom meaning "on the first day of the week" (compare prima sabbati in 16:9).
- **16:8** — Vulgate renders Greek ekstasis ("astonishment/bewilderment") with pavor ("terror/dread").
- **16:19** — The Vulgate uses the present tense sedet ("sits") where the Greek has the aorist ("sat down").
