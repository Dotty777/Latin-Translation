## Text

**15:1** And immediately in the morning, the chief priests, taking counsel with the elders, the scribes, and the entire council, bound Jesus, led Him, and handed Him over to Pilate.

**15:2** And Pilate questioned Him, "Are You the King of the Jews?" But answering, He said to him, "You say so."

**15:3** And the chief priests kept accusing Him of many things.

**15:4** Then Pilate questioned Him again, saying, "Do You not answer anything? See how many things they are accusing You of."

**15:5** Yet Jesus answered nothing further, so that Pilate was astonished.

**15:6** Now on the feast day he was accustomed to release to them one of the prisoners, whomever they requested.

**15:7** Now there was one called Barabbas, who was bound along with the insurgents and who had committed murder in the insurrection.

**15:8** And when the crowd had gone up, they began to ask, just as he always did for them.

**15:9** But Pilate answered them and said, "Do you want me to release the King of the Jews to you?"

**15:10** For he knew that the chief priests had handed Him over through envy.

**15:11** But the high priests incited the crowd so that he would rather release Barabbas to them.

**15:12** And Pilate, answering again, said to them, "What then do you want me to do to the King of the Jews?"

**15:13** But they shouted again, "Crucify Him!"

**15:14** Yet Pilate was saying to them, "Why, what evil did He do?" But they kept shouting all the more, "Crucify Him!"

**15:15** Now Pilate, wishing to satisfy the people, released Barabbas to them, and handed over Jesus, scourged with whips, to be crucified.

**15:16** Then the soldiers led Him into the courtyard of the praetorium and called together the entire cohort,

**15:17** and clothed Him in purple, and weaving a crown of thorns, they placed it upon Him.

**15:18** And they began to salute Him, "Hail, King of the Jews!"

**15:19** And they kept striking His head with a reed and spitting on Him, and kneeling down, they were worshiping Him.

**15:20** And after they had mocked Him, they stripped Him of the purple and clothed Him in His own garments; and they led Him out to crucify Him.

**15:21** And they forced a certain man passing by into service, Simon the Cyrenian, coming from the countryside, the father of Alexander and Rufus, so that he might carry His cross.

**15:22** And they brought Him to the place Golgotha, which is translated, Place of a Skull.

**15:23** And they were giving Him wine mixed with myrrh to drink, and He did not accept it.

**15:24** And crucifying Him, they divided His garments, casting lots over them as to who would take what.

**15:25** Now it was the third hour, and they crucified Him.

**15:26** And the notice of His charge was inscribed: "King of the Jews."

**15:27** And along with Him they crucified two robbers, one on His right and the other on His left.

**15:29** And those passing by were blaspheming Him, shaking their heads and saying, "Aha! You who destroy the temple of God and rebuild it in three days,

**15:30** save Yourself, coming down from the cross!"

**15:31** In the same way, the chief priests also, mocking, were saying to one another along with the scribes, "Others He saved; He is not able to save Himself.

**15:32** Let the Christ, the King of Israel, come down now from the cross, so that we may see and believe." And those who had been crucified with Him were reviling Him.

**15:33** And when the sixth hour had arrived, darkness came across the entire earth until the ninth hour.

**15:34** And at the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a loud voice, saying, "Eloi, Eloi, lamma sabacthani?" which is translated, "My God, My God, why have You abandoned Me?"

**15:35** And some of those standing around, hearing this, were saying, "Look, He is calling Elijah."

**15:36** Then one man, running and filling a sponge with vinegar and putting it around a reed, was giving Him a drink, saying, "Wait, let us see whether Elijah comes to take Him down."

**15:37** But Jesus, letting out a loud cry, breathed His last.

**15:38** And the veil of the temple was torn in two, from the top all the way down.

**15:39** Now the centurion, who was standing directly opposite, seeing that He had breathed His last while crying out in this manner, said, "Truly this man was the Son of God."

**15:40** Now there were also women watching from a distance, among whom were Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James the younger and of Joseph, and Salome;

**15:41** and when He was in Galilee, they used to follow Him and serve Him; and there were many other women who had gone up together with Him to Jerusalem.

**15:42** And when evening had already come (because it was the Preparation, which is the day before the Sabbath),

**15:43** Joseph of Arimathea came, a distinguished councilor who himself was also awaiting the kingdom of God, and he boldly went in to Pilate and requested the body of Jesus.

**15:44** Now Pilate wondered whether He had already passed away; and having summoned the centurion, he questioned him whether He was already dead.

**15:45** And when he had learned this from the centurion, he granted the body to Joseph.

**15:46** Then Joseph, having purchased a linen cloth and taking Him down, wrapped Him in the linen cloth and laid Him in a tomb that had been cut out of rock, and rolled a stone to the entrance of the tomb.

**15:47** Now Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joseph were watching where He was laid.

## Translator Notes

- **15:7** — Vulgate reads singular qui ... fecerat ("who had committed"), explicitly attributing the murder during the insurrection to Barabbas himself, whereas the Greek uses a plural verb referring to the insurgents generally.
- **15:22** — Latin Calvariae locus literally means "Place of a Skull" (from calvaria, "skull"), translating the Aramaic name Golgotha and giving rise to the English name "Calvary."
- **15:27** — Verse 28 ("And the Scripture was fulfilled which says, 'He was numbered with the transgressors'") is absent from the Clementine Vulgate, so the verse numbering passes directly from 15:27 to 15:29.
- **15:29** — Vulgate adds Dei ("the temple of God"), where most modern Greek editions read simply "the temple."
- **15:40** — Vulgate reads Joseph here and in 15:47 (literally Maria Joseph, "Mary [the mother] of Joseph"), where most Greek manuscripts read the shorter form Joses.
