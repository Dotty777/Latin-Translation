## Text

**9:1** And after six days, Jesus took along Peter and James and John, and led them onto a high mountain apart by themselves, and He was transfigured before them.

**9:2** And His garments became gleaming and exceedingly white like snow, such as a launderer on earth cannot make white.

**9:3** And Elijah appeared to them with Moses, and they were speaking with Jesus.

**9:4** And Peter, answering, said to Jesus, "Rabbi, it is good for us to be here; and let us make three tents, one for You, and one for Moses, and one for Elijah."

**9:5** For he did not know what he should say, for they were terrified with fear.

**9:6** And a cloud formed, overshadowing them, and a voice came out of the cloud, saying, "This is My most beloved Son; listen to Him."

**9:7** And immediately, looking around, they no longer saw anyone except Jesus alone with them.

**9:8** And as they were coming down from the mountain, He commanded them not to recount to anyone the things they had seen, except when the Son of Man has risen from the dead.

**9:9** And they kept the saying to themselves, debating what "when He has risen from the dead" meant.

**9:10** And they were asking Him, saying, "Why then do the Pharisees and scribes say that Elijah must come first?"

**9:11** And He, answering, said to them, "Elijah, when he comes first, will restore all things; and as it is written concerning the Son of Man, that He should suffer many things and be despised.

**9:12** But I tell you that Elijah has also come (and they did to him whatever they wished), just as it is written about him."

**9:13** And coming to His disciples, He saw a great crowd around them, and scribes debating with them.

**9:14** And immediately all the people, seeing Jesus, were astounded and struck with fear, and running up, they were greeting Him.

**9:15** And He asked them, "What are you debating among yourselves?"

**9:16** And one from the crowd, answering, said, "Teacher, I have brought to You my son, who has a mute spirit;

**9:17** and wherever it seizes him, it dashes him down, and he foams, and grinds his teeth, and wastes away; and I told Your disciples to cast it out, and they could not."

**9:18** And He, answering them, said, "O unbelieving generation, how long will I be with you? How long will I bear with you? Bring him to Me."

**9:19** And they brought him. And when it saw Him, the spirit immediately convulsed him; and dashed to the ground, he was rolling around, foaming.

**9:20** And He asked his father, "How much time has it been since this happened to him?" And he said, "From infancy;

**9:21** and frequently it has thrown him into the fire and into the waters to destroy him; but if You can do anything, help us, having compassion on us."

**9:22** And Jesus said to him, "If you are able to believe, all things are possible for the one who believes."

**9:23** And immediately the father of the boy, crying out, was saying with tears, "I believe, Lord; help my unbelief."

**9:24** And when Jesus saw a crowd running together, He rebuked the unclean spirit, saying to it, "Deaf and mute spirit, I command you, come out of him, and do not enter into him anymore."

**9:25** And crying out and greatly wrenching him, it came out of him, and he became like one dead, so that many were saying, "He is dead."

**9:26** But Jesus, taking hold of his hand, lifted him up, and he stood up.

**9:27** And when He had entered the house, His disciples were asking Him privately, "Why could we not cast it out?"

**9:28** And He said to them, "This kind can come out by nothing except by prayer and fasting."

**9:29** And departing from there, they were passing through Galilee, and He did not want anyone to know.

**9:30** Now He was teaching His disciples, and He was saying to them, "The Son of Man will be delivered into the hands of men, and they will kill Him; and having been killed, on the third day He will rise."

**9:31** But they did not understand the saying, and they were afraid to ask Him.

**9:32** And they came to Capernaum. And when they were in the house, He was asking them, "What were you discussing on the way?"

**9:33** But they kept silent, for on the way they had disputed among themselves who among them was greater.

**9:34** And sitting down, He called the twelve and said to them, "If anyone wants to be first, he will be the last of all and the servant of all."

**9:35** And taking a child, He set him in their midst; and when He had embraced him, He said to them,

**9:36** "Whoever receives one of such children in My name receives Me; and whoever welcomes Me does not welcome Me, but Him who sent Me."

**9:37** John answered Him, saying, "Teacher, we saw someone casting out demons in Your name who does not follow us, and we forbade him."

**9:38** But Jesus said, "Do not forbid him, for there is no one who performs a mighty work in My name and can quickly speak evil of Me.

**9:39** For the one who is not against you is for you.

**9:40** For whoever gives you a cup of water to drink in My name, because you belong to Christ, amen, I tell you, he will not lose his reward.

**9:41** And whoever causes one of these little ones who believe in Me to stumble, it would be better for him if a donkey's millstone were placed around his neck and he were thrown into the sea.

**9:42** And if your hand causes you to stumble, cut it off; it is better for you to enter into life maimed than, having two hands, to go into Gehenna, into the unquenchable fire.

**9:43** Where their worm does not die, and the fire is not quenched.

**9:44** And if your foot causes you to stumble, cut it off; it is better for you to enter into eternal life lame than, having two feet, to be thrown into the Gehenna of unquenchable fire.

**9:45** Where their worm does not die, and the fire is not quenched.

**9:46** And if your eye causes you to stumble, cast it out; it is better for you to enter into the kingdom of God one-eyed than, having two eyes, to be thrown into the Gehenna of fire,

**9:47** where their worm does not die, and the fire is not quenched.

**9:48** For everyone will be salted with fire, and every sacrifice will be salted with salt.

**9:49** Salt is good; but if the salt becomes saltless, with what will you season it? Have salt in yourselves, and have peace among yourselves."

## Translator Notes

- **9:9** — The Vulgate renders the disciples' question with a temporal clause ("quid esset, cum a mortuis resurrexerit"), echoing Jesus's exact words in 9:9, rather than an infinitive ("what rising from the dead meant").
- **9:10** — The Vulgate reads "pharisæi et scribæ" ("the Pharisees and scribes"), whereas the Greek textual tradition mentions only "the scribes."
- **9:11** — The Latin punctuates this as a statement, with quomodo in its sense "as"; the Greek is commonly read as a question ("and how is it written...?").
- **9:14** — The Vulgate translates the crowd's reaction with two verbs ("stupefactus est, et expaverunt," "were astounded and struck with fear") where the Greek has a single verb.
- **9:28** — Following the broader Latin and Byzantine tradition, the Vulgate includes "et jejunio" ("and fasting") alongside prayer; likewise, it retains "credere" ("to believe") in 9:23, "ignis inextinguibilis" ("of unquenchable fire") in 9:45, and "et omnis victima sale salietur" ("and every sacrifice will be salted with salt") in 9:49.
- **9:42** — Native Vulgate Mark 8:39 corresponds to Saints.Bible 9:1, shifting native Vulgate Mark 9:1-49 one number lower than Saints.Bible 9:2-50 (with the refrain 'where their worm does not die...' at native 9:43, 9:45, 9:47).
