## Text

**10:1** And rising up from there, He came into the territory of Judea beyond the Jordan; and crowds gathered to Him again, and as He was accustomed, He was teaching them again.

**10:2** And Pharisees approached and were questioning Him whether it is lawful for a man to send away a wife, testing Him.

**10:3** But answering, He said to them, "What did Moses command you?"

**10:4** They said, "Moses permitted writing a certificate of divorce and sending her away."

**10:5** Answering them, Jesus said, "Because of the hardness of your heart, he wrote this commandment for you;

**10:6** yet from the beginning of creation, God made them male and female.

**10:7** For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and will cling to his wife,

**10:8** and the two will be in one flesh. And so they are no longer two, but one flesh.

**10:9** Therefore, what God has united, let no human being separate."

**10:10** And in the house, His disciples questioned Him again about the same matter.

**10:11** And He said to them, "Whoever sends away his wife and marries another commits adultery against her.

**10:12** And if a wife sends away her husband and marries another, she commits adultery."

**10:13** And they were bringing little children to Him so that He might touch them. But the disciples were rebuking those who were bringing them.

**10:14** When Jesus saw them, He was indignant and said to them, "Allow the little children to come to Me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of God belongs to such ones.

**10:15** Amen, I tell you: whoever does not receive the kingdom of God like a little child will not enter into it."

**10:16** And embracing them and laying hands on them, He was blessing them.

**10:17** And when He had gone out onto the road, a certain man ran up and, kneeling before Him, was asking Him, "Good Teacher, what should I do so that I may obtain eternal life?"

**10:18** But Jesus said to him, "Why do you call Me good? No one is good except the one God.

**10:19** You know the commandments: do not commit adultery, do not kill, do not steal, do not give false testimony, do not commit fraud, honor your father and mother."

**10:20** But answering, he said to Him, "Teacher, I have observed all these things from my youth."

**10:21** And Jesus, looking intently at him, loved him and said to him, "One thing is lacking to you: go, sell whatever you have, and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven; and come, follow Me."

**10:22** And saddened at the word, he went away grieving, for he had many possessions.

**10:23** And looking around, Jesus said to His disciples, "With what difficulty will those who have riches enter into the kingdom of God!"

**10:24** And the disciples were astounded at His words. But Jesus, answering again, said to them, "Little children, how difficult it is for those who trust in riches to enter into the kingdom of God!

**10:25** It is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a wealthy person to enter into the kingdom of God."

**10:26** And they were even more amazed, saying to themselves, "And who can be saved?"

**10:27** And looking intently at them, Jesus said, "With human beings it is impossible, but not with God; for all things are possible with God."

**10:28** And Peter began to say to Him, "Look, we have left everything and have followed You."

**10:29** Answering, Jesus said, "Amen, I tell you: there is no one who has left house, or brothers, or sisters, or father, or mother, or children, or fields on account of Me and on account of the Gospel,

**10:30** who will not receive a hundred times as much now in this time: houses, and brothers, and sisters, and mothers, and children, and fields, with persecutions, and in the age to come, eternal life.

**10:31** Yet many who are first will be last, and the last will be first."

**10:32** Now they were on the road going up to Jerusalem, and Jesus was going ahead of them, and they were astonished, and those following were afraid. And taking the twelve to Himself again, He began to tell them what things were about to happen to Him:

**10:33** "Look, we are going up to Jerusalem, and the Son of Man will be handed over to the chief priests, and to the scribes, and to the elders, and they will condemn Him to death and will hand Him over to the Gentiles;

**10:34** and they will mock Him, and spit on Him, and flog Him, and kill Him; and on the third day He will rise again."

**10:35** And James and John, the sons of Zebedee, came up to Him, saying, "Teacher, we want You to do for us whatever we ask."

**10:36** And He said to them, "What do you want Me to do for you?"

**10:37** And they said, "Grant us that we may sit, one at Your right hand and the other at Your left hand, in Your glory."

**10:38** But Jesus said to them, "You do not know what you are asking. Are you able to drink the cup that I drink, or to be baptized with the baptism with which I am baptized?"

**10:39** And they said to Him, "We are able." And Jesus said to them, "Indeed, the cup that I drink, you will drink; and with the baptism with which I am baptized, you will be baptized;

**10:40** but to sit at My right hand or at My left is not Mine to give to you, but it is for those for whom it has been prepared."

**10:41** And when the ten heard, they began to be indignant about James and John.

**10:42** But Jesus, calling them, said to them, "You know that those who are regarded as ruling over the Gentiles exercise dominion over them, and their rulers hold authority over them.

**10:43** Yet it is not this way among you; instead, whoever wishes to become greater will be your servant,

**10:44** and whoever wishes to be first among you will be the slave of all.

**10:45** For indeed, the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve and to give His life as a redemption for many."

**10:46** And they came to Jericho; and as He and His disciples and a very large crowd were departing from Jericho, the son of Timaeus, Bartimaeus, a blind man, was sitting beside the road, begging.

**10:47** And when he had heard that it was Jesus the Nazarene, he began to cry out and say, "Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me."

**10:48** And many were rebuking him so that he would be silent. But he was crying out all the more, "Son of David, have mercy on me."

**10:49** And stopping, Jesus commanded him to be called. And they called the blind man, saying to him, "Take heart; get up, He is calling you."

**10:50** Throwing off his cloak and leaping up, he came to Him.

**10:51** And answering, Jesus said to him, "What do you want Me to do for you?" And the blind man said to Him, "Rabboni, that I may see."

**10:52** And Jesus said to him, "Go, your faith has made you whole." And immediately he saw, and he was following Him on the road.

## Translator Notes

- **10:7** — The Clementine Vulgate includes "and will cling to his wife" (et adhærebit ad uxorem suam), a clause omitted in several early Greek manuscripts.
- **10:19** — The Clementine Vulgate places "do not commit adultery" (ne adulteres) before "do not kill" (ne occidas), reversing the order found in most Greek manuscripts of Mark.
- **10:24** — The Clementine Vulgate includes "for those who trust in riches" (confidentes in pecuniis), reflecting the longer Byzantine textual tradition rather than the shorter Alexandrian Greek text.
- **10:33** — The Clementine Vulgate adds "and to the elders" (et senioribus) to the chief priests and scribes, a distinctive reading absent from most Greek witnesses.
- **10:40** — The Clementine Vulgate includes "to you" (vobis) in "is not Mine to give to you," whereas most critical Greek editions omit the pronoun.
