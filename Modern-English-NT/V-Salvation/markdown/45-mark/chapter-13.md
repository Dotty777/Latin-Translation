## Text

**13:1** And as He was going out of the temple, one of His disciples said to Him, "Teacher, look what stones and what structures!"

**13:2** And answering, Jesus said to him, "Do you see all these great buildings? Not a stone will be left upon a stone that will not be torn down."

**13:3** And as He was sitting on the Mount of Olives opposite the temple, Peter, James, John, and Andrew were questioning Him privately:

**13:4** "Tell us, when will these things happen? And what will be the sign when all these things begin to be completed?"

**13:5** And answering, Jesus began to say to them, "Watch out that nothing misleads you;

**13:6** for many will come in My name, saying, 'I am,' and they will mislead many.

**13:7** Now when you hear of wars and reports of wars, do not be afraid; for these things must happen, but the end is not yet.

**13:8** For nation will rise up against nation, and kingdom against kingdom, and there will be earthquakes from place to place, and famines. These are the beginning of pains.

**13:9** But watch out for yourselves. For they will hand you over to councils, and in synagogues you will be beaten, and you will stand before governors and kings on account of Me, as a testimony to them.

**13:10** And among all nations the Gospel must first be proclaimed.

**13:11** And when they lead you away, handing you over, do not think beforehand about what you should speak, but whatever is given to you in that hour, speak that; for it is not you who are speaking, but the Holy Spirit.

**13:12** And brother will hand over brother to death, and a father his son; and children will rise up against parents and put them to death.

**13:13** And you will be hated by everyone on account of My name. But the one who endures to the end, this one will be saved.

**13:14** Now when you see the abomination of desolation standing where it should not be (let the one who reads understand), then let those who are in Judea flee into the mountains;

**13:15** and let the one who is on the roof not go down into the house, nor enter to take anything out of his house;

**13:16** and let the one who will be in the field not turn back to take his garment.

**13:17** But woe to those who are pregnant and to those who are nursing in those days.

**13:18** And pray that these things may not happen in winter.

**13:19** For those days will be tribulations such as have not been from the beginning of creation, which God made, until now, nor will come to be.

**13:20** And if the Lord had not shortened the days, no flesh would have been saved; but on account of the chosen ones, whom He chose, He shortened the days.

**13:21** And then, if anyone says to you, 'Look, here is the Christ; look, there!' do not believe it.

**13:22** For false christs and false prophets will rise up and will give signs and wonders to mislead, if it is possible, even the chosen ones.

**13:23** You, therefore, watch out; look, I have told you all things beforehand.

**13:24** But in those days, after that tribulation, the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its brightness,

**13:25** and the stars of heaven will be falling down, and the powers that are in the heavens will be shaken.

**13:26** And then they will see the Son of Man coming in clouds with great power and glory.

**13:27** And then He will send His angels and will gather His chosen ones from the four winds, from the farthest point of the earth all the way to the farthest point of heaven.

**13:28** Now learn a parable from the fig tree. When its branch has already become tender and leaves have sprouted, you recognize that summer is near;

**13:29** so you also, when you see these things happening, know that it is near, at the doors.

**13:30** Amen, I tell you that this generation will not pass away until all these things happen.

**13:31** Heaven and earth will pass away, yet My words will not pass away.

**13:32** But concerning that day or hour no one knows, neither the angels in heaven nor the Son, except the Father.

**13:33** Watch out, stay awake, and pray, for you do not know when the time is.

**13:34** Just as a man who, having gone on a journey abroad, left his house and gave his servants authority over each task, and commanded the doorkeeper to stay awake,

**13:35** stay awake, therefore (for you do not know when the master of the house is coming: in the evening, or at midnight, or at the rooster's crow, or in the morning),

**13:36** so that, when he comes suddenly, he may not find you sleeping.

**13:37** Now what I say to you, I say to everyone: Stay awake."

## Translator Notes

- **13:5** — The Clementine Vulgate reads the neuter pronoun quid ("anything" / "nothing") rather than the masculine quis ("anyone"), broadening the warning beyond human deceivers to anything that might mislead.
- **13:19** — The Vulgate uses the plural predicate nominative tribulationes tales ("tribulations such as..."), directly equating those days with the tribulations themselves, where the Greek text uses a singular noun.
- **13:25** — The Vulgate reads the genitive stellae caeli ("the stars of heaven") with the periphrastic erunt decidentes ("will be falling down"), rather than "falling from heaven" as in the Greek text.
- **13:29** — Latin sit has no expressed subject; it may be understood impersonally ("it is near") or as referring to the Son of Man of verse 26 ("He is near").
- **13:34** — The Vulgate joins cujusque operis ("of each task") as a genitive modifying potestatem ("authority"), rather than separating "authority" and "to each his work" as in the Greek text.
