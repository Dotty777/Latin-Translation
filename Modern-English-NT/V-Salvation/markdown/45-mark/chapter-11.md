## Text

**11:1** And as they were approaching Jerusalem and Bethany, at the Mount of Olives, He sent two of His disciples,

**11:2** and said to them, "Go into the village that is opposite you, and immediately upon entering it, you will find a colt tied, on which no person has yet sat. Untie it and bring it.

**11:3** And if anyone says to you, 'What are you doing?' say that it is needed by the Lord, and at once he will send it here."

**11:4** And going away, they found a colt tied in front of a door, outside where two roads met, and they untied it.

**11:5** And some of those standing there were saying to them, "What are you doing, untying the colt?"

**11:6** They spoke to them just as Jesus had instructed them, and they permitted them.

**11:7** And they led the colt to Jesus, and placed their garments on it, and He sat on it.

**11:8** And many spread their garments on the road, while others were cutting leafy branches from the trees and spreading them on the road.

**11:9** And those who were going ahead and those who were following were crying out, saying, "Hosanna! Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord!

**11:10** Blessed is the kingdom of our father David that comes! Hosanna in the highest!"

**11:11** And He entered Jerusalem, into the temple; and having looked around at everything, since it was already the evening hour, He went out to Bethany with the twelve.

**11:12** And on the following day, as they were going out from Bethany, He became hungry.

**11:13** And when He saw from a distance a fig tree having leaves, He went to see if perhaps He might find anything on it; and when He came to it, He found nothing except leaves, for it was not the time for figs.

**11:14** And answering, He said to it, "May no one eat fruit from you anymore, forever." And His disciples were listening.

**11:15** And they came into Jerusalem. And when He had entered the temple, He began to drive out those selling and buying in the temple, and He overturned the tables of the money changers and the seats of those selling doves.

**11:16** And He would not allow anyone to carry a vessel through the temple.

**11:17** And He was teaching, saying to them, "Is it not written, 'My house will be called a house of prayer for all nations'? But you have made it a cave of robbers."

**11:18** When the chief priests and the scribes heard this, they were seeking how they might destroy Him; for they feared Him, because the entire crowd was astonished at His teaching.

**11:19** And when evening had come, He went out from the city.

**11:20** And as they were passing by in the morning, they saw the fig tree withered from the roots.

**11:21** And Peter, remembering, said to Him, "Rabbi, look, the fig tree that You cursed has withered!"

**11:22** And answering, Jesus said to them, "Have faith in God.

**11:23** Amen, I tell you that whoever says to this mountain, 'Be lifted up and be thrown into the sea,' and does not waver in his heart, but believes that whatever he says will happen, it will happen for him.

**11:24** Therefore I say to you, all things that you ask for while praying, believe that you will receive them, and they will come about for you.

**11:25** And when you stand to pray, forgive, if you hold anything against anyone, so that your Father also, who is in heaven, may forgive you your sins."

**11:27** And they came again to Jerusalem. And as He was walking in the temple, the chief priests and the scribes and the elders came to Him,

**11:28** and said to Him, "By what authority do You do these things? And who gave You this authority to do these things?"

**11:29** But Jesus, answering, said to them, "I also will ask you one question, and answer Me, and I will tell you by what authority I do these things.

**11:30** The baptism of John, was it from heaven or from men? Answer Me."

**11:31** But they were reasoning among themselves, saying, "If we say, 'From heaven,' He will say, 'Why then did you not believe him?'

**11:32** If we say, 'From men,' we fear the people; for all held that John was truly a prophet."

**11:33** And answering, they said to Jesus, "We do not know." And answering, Jesus said to them, "Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things."

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**11:26** *(after `11:25`)* But if you do not forgive, neither will your Father, who is in heaven, forgive you your sins.

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — The Vulgate reads "Jerusalem and Bethany" (Jerosolymæ et Bethaniæ), omitting Bethphage, which appears in most Greek manuscripts.
- **11:19** — The Vulgate uses the singular verb egrediebatur ("He went out"), whereas many critical Greek texts read the plural ("they went out").
- **11:24** — The Vulgate reads the future accipietis ("you will receive"), whereas the earliest Greek manuscripts read the aorist ("you have received").
- **11:25** — The Latin text reads si quis habetis (a textual variant for si quid habetis, "if you hold anything") and peccata ("sins," where the Greek has "trespasses").
- **11:32** — The Vulgate reads the first-person timemus populum ("we fear the people") as part of the leaders' direct speech, whereas most Greek manuscripts shift to third-person narration ("they feared the crowd").
