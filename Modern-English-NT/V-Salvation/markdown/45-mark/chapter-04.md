## Text

**4:1** And again He began to teach by the sea, and a large crowd gathered to Him, so that boarding a boat He sat on the sea, and the entire crowd was along the sea on the land.

**4:2** And He was teaching them many things in parables, and He said to them in His teaching:

**4:3** "Listen! Look, the sower went out to sow.

**4:4** And while he was sowing, some fell along the road, and the birds of the sky came and ate it.

**4:5** Yet some fell on rocky places, where it did not have much soil, and immediately it sprang up, because it did not have depth of soil.

**4:6** And when the sun rose, it was scorched, and because it did not have a root, it dried up.

**4:7** And some fell among thorns, and the thorns came up and choked it, and it produced no fruit.

**4:8** And some fell into good soil, and produced fruit rising up and growing, and brought forth, one thirty, one sixty, and one a hundred."

**4:9** And He said, "The one who has ears for hearing, let him hear."

**4:10** And when He was alone, those who were with Him, the twelve, asked Him about the parable.

**4:11** And He said to them, "To you it has been given to know the mystery of the kingdom of God; but to those who are outside, all things happen in parables,

**4:12** so that seeing they may see and not see, and hearing they may hear and not understand, that they may never turn and sins be forgiven them."

**4:13** And He said to them, "Do you not know this parable? And how will you understand all the parables?

**4:14** The one who sows, sows the word.

**4:15** Now these are the ones along the road, where the word is sown; and when they have heard, Satan comes at once and takes away the word that has been sown in their hearts.

**4:16** And similarly, these are the ones who are sown on rocky places: when they have heard the word, they immediately receive it with joy,

**4:17** and they do not have a root in themselves, but are temporary; then, when tribulation and persecution arise on account of the word, at once they stumble.

**4:18** And others are those who are sown among thorns; these are the ones who hear the word,

**4:19** and the troubles of the age, and the deception of riches, and desires concerning other things entering in choke the word, and it becomes without fruit.

**4:20** And these are the ones who were sown on good soil, who hear the word and welcome it and bear fruit, one thirty, one sixty, and one a hundred."

**4:21** And He said to them, "Does a lamp come to be placed under a grain measure or under a bed? Is it not to be placed on a lampstand?

**4:22** For there is nothing hidden that will not be revealed, nor has anything been made secret except that it may come into the open.

**4:23** If anyone has ears for hearing, let him hear."

**4:24** And He said to them, "Watch what you hear. With whatever measure you measure, it will be measured back to you, and more will be added to you.

**4:25** For it will be given to the one who has, and even what he has will be taken away from the one who does not have."

**4:26** And He was saying, "This is how the kingdom of God is: just as if a man should cast seed onto the earth,

**4:27** and sleep and rise night and day, and the seed should sprout and grow while he does not know.

**4:28** For of its own accord the earth bears fruit: first the blade, then the head, then the full grain in the head.

**4:29** And when it has brought forth the fruits, immediately he sends in the sickle, because the harvest is here."

**4:30** And He was saying, "To what should we liken the kingdom of God? Or to what parable should we compare it?

**4:31** Like a mustard seed, which, when it has been sown in the earth, is smaller than all the seeds that are on the earth;

**4:32** and when it has been sown, it comes up and becomes greater than all garden plants, and makes large branches, so that the birds of the sky can dwell under its shade."

**4:33** And He was speaking the word to them with many such parables, to the extent that they could hear.

**4:34** And without a parable He was not speaking to them, but in private He was explaining all things to His disciples.

**4:35** And He said to them on that day, when evening had come, "Let us cross over to the opposite side."

**4:36** And dismissing the crowd, they took Him just as He was in the boat, and other boats were with Him.

**4:37** And a great storm of wind arose, and it was throwing the waves into the boat, so that the boat was filling up.

**4:38** And He Himself was in the stern, sleeping on a pillow; and they woke Him and said to Him, "Teacher, does it not matter to You that we are perishing?"

**4:39** And rising up, He rebuked the wind and said to the sea, "Be silent, be mute." And the wind ceased, and a great calm came about.

**4:40** And He said to them, "Why are you fearful? Do you not yet have faith?" And they feared with a great fear, and were saying to one another, "Who, do you think, is this, that both the wind and the sea obey Him?"

## Translator Notes

- **4:10** — The Vulgate reads hi qui cum eo erant duodecim ("those who were with Him, the twelve") without a preposition separating two groups, and has singular parabolam ("the parable") where critical Greek texts read plural "the parables."
- **4:12** — The Vulgate repeats the verb video three times (videntes videant, et non videant) and includes peccata ("sins") at the end of the verse, whereas modern critical Greek editions distinguish seeing from perceiving and omit "sins."
- **4:27** — The Vulgate reads dum nescit ille ("while he does not know") with temporal dum, rather than Greek hos ("how he does not know").
- **4:37** — The Vulgate uses singular mittebat with procella ("storm") as subject throwing fluctus ("the waves") into the boat, and omits "already" before impleretur navis.
- **4:40** — Native Clementine Vulgate Mark 4:40 combines Saints.Bible 4:40 and 4:41. The Latin includes necdum ("not yet") in Jesus' rebuke and the parenthetical putas ("do you think") in the disciples' question.
