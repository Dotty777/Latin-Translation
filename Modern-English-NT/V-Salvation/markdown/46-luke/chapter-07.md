## Text

**7:1** Now when He had completed all His words in the ears of the people, He entered Capernaum.

**7:2** Now a certain centurion's slave, being ill, was about to die, and he was precious to him.

**7:3** And when he had heard about Jesus, he sent elders of the Jews to Him, asking Him to come and save his slave.

**7:4** And when they had come to Jesus, they asked Him earnestly, saying to Him, "He is worthy that You should grant him this,

**7:5** for he loves our nation, and he himself built the synagogue for us."

**7:6** And Jesus was going with them. And when He was now not far from the house, the centurion sent friends to Him, saying, "Lord, do not trouble Yourself, for I am not worthy that You should enter under my roof;

**7:7** for which reason I did not even consider myself worthy to come to You; but speak with a word, and my servant will be healed.

**7:8** For I too am a man placed under authority, having soldiers under me; and I say to this one, 'Go,' and he goes; and to another, 'Come,' and he comes; and to my slave, 'Do this,' and he does it."

**7:9** When Jesus heard this, He was amazed, and turning to the crowds following Him, He said, "Amen, I tell you, not even in Israel have I found so great a faith."

**7:10** And when those who had been sent returned to the house, they found the slave, who had been sick, well.

**7:11** And it happened afterward that He was going to a city that is called Nain, and His disciples and a large crowd were going with Him.

**7:12** Now as He drew near to the gate of the city, look, a dead man was being carried out, the only son of his mother, and she was a widow; and a large crowd from the city was with her.

**7:13** And when the Lord saw her, moved with compassion for her, He said to her, "Do not weep."

**7:14** And He came up and touched the bier, and those who were carrying it stood still. And He said, "Young man, I say to you, rise."

**7:15** And the one who was dead sat up and began to speak. And He gave him to his mother.

**7:16** Now fear seized all of them, and they were magnifying God, saying, "A great prophet has risen among us, and God has visited His people."

**7:17** And this report about Him went out into all Judea and into all the surrounding region.

**7:18** And John's disciples reported to him about all these things.

**7:19** And John summoned two of his disciples and sent them to Jesus, saying, "Are You the One who is to come, or are we waiting for another?"

**7:20** And when the men had come to Him, they said, "John the Baptist sent us to You, saying, 'Are You the One who is to come, or are we waiting for another?'"

**7:21** Now in that very hour He cured many of illnesses and afflictions and evil spirits, and to many who were blind He granted sight.

**7:22** And answering, He said to them, "Go and report to John what you have heard and seen: that the blind see, the lame walk, lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead rise, the poor have good news proclaimed to them;

**7:23** and blessed is whoever is not caused to stumble because of Me."

**7:24** And when the messengers of John had departed, He began to speak to the crowds about John: "What did you go out into the desert to see? A reed shaken by the wind?

**7:25** But what did you go out to see? A man dressed in soft clothing? Look, those who are in costly clothing and luxury are in the houses of kings.

**7:26** But what did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, I say to you, and more than a prophet:

**7:27** this is the one about whom it is written, 'Look, I send My messenger before Your face, who will prepare Your way before You.'

**7:28** For I say to you, among those born of women there is no prophet greater than John the Baptist; yet the one who is least in the kingdom of God is greater than he."

**7:29** And all the people who heard, and the tax collectors, justified God, having been baptized with the baptism of John.

**7:30** But the Pharisees and the experts in the law rejected the counsel of God for themselves, not having been baptized by him.

**7:31** And the Lord said, "What, then, should I say the people of this generation are like, and what are they like?

**7:32** They are like children sitting in the marketplace and speaking to one another and saying, 'We played the flute for you, and you did not dance; we lamented, and you did not weep.'

**7:33** For John the Baptist has come neither eating bread nor drinking wine, and you say, 'He has a demon.'

**7:34** The Son of Man has come eating and drinking, and you say, 'Look, a gluttonous man and one who drinks wine, a friend of tax collectors and sinners.'

**7:35** And wisdom has been justified by all her children."

**7:36** Now one of the Pharisees asked Him to eat with him. And having entered the Pharisee's house, He reclined at table.

**7:37** And look, a woman who was a sinner in the city, when she learned that He was reclining at table in the Pharisee's house, brought an alabaster jar of ointment;

**7:38** and standing behind Him at His feet, she began to wet His feet with tears, and she was wiping them with the hair of her head, and kissing His feet, and anointing them with the ointment.

**7:39** Now when the Pharisee who had invited Him saw this, he spoke within himself, saying, "If this man were a prophet, He would surely know who and what kind of woman this is who is touching Him, that she is a sinner."

**7:40** And answering, Jesus said to him, "Simon, I have something to say to you." And he said, "Teacher, say it."

**7:41** "A certain moneylender had two debtors: one owed five hundred denarii, and the other fifty.

**7:42** Since they had nothing with which to repay, he forgave both of them. Which of them, then, loves him more?"

**7:43** Answering, Simon said, "I suppose the one to whom he forgave more." And He said to him, "You have judged correctly."

**7:44** And turning toward the woman, He said to Simon, "Do you see this woman? I entered your house; you gave no water for My feet, but she has wet My feet with tears and wiped them with her hair.

**7:45** You gave Me no kiss, but she, from the time she entered, has not stopped kissing My feet.

**7:46** You did not anoint My head with oil, but she has anointed My feet with ointment.

**7:47** Therefore I say to you, many sins are forgiven her, for she has loved much. But the one to whom less is forgiven loves less."

**7:48** Then He said to her, "Sins are forgiven you."

**7:49** And those who were reclining at table together began to say within themselves, "Who is this who even forgives sins?"

**7:50** And He said to the woman, "Your faith has saved you; go in peace."

## Translator Notes

- **7:9** — The Clementine Vulgate includes Amen ("Truly") before "I say to you" (paralleling Matt 8:10), whereas the Greek text of Luke omits it.
- **7:22** — The Vulgate reverses the Greek order ("what you have seen and heard") to "what you have heard and seen" (quae audistis et vidistis), paralleling Matt 11:4.
- **7:31** — The Clementine Vulgate includes the narrative introduction "And the Lord said" (Ait autem Dominus), marking verses 29-30 as Luke's commentary rather than Jesus's speech; many early Greek manuscripts omit this clause.
- **7:45** — The Vulgate reads third-person "from the time she entered" (ex quo intravit), reflecting an ancient textual variant where most Greek manuscripts read first-person ("since I came in").
- **7:47** — Latin quoniam ("for") carries the same debated causal-or-evidential range as the Greek, indicating either the ground or the evidence of her forgiveness; the Vulgate also uses comparative minus ("less") rather than Greek "little."
