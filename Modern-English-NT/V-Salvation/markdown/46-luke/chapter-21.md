## Text

**21:1** Now looking up, He saw those who were putting their offerings into the treasury, the rich.

**21:2** And He also saw a certain very poor widow putting in two tiny copper coins.

**21:3** And He said, "Truly I tell you that this poor widow put in more than everyone.

**21:4** For all these put into the offerings of God from what was abundant to them; yet she, from what is lacking to her, put in all of her livelihood that she had."

**21:5** And as some were speaking about the temple, that it had been adorned with fine stones and gifts, He said,

**21:6** "These things that you see, days will come in which not a stone will be left upon a stone that will not be demolished."

**21:7** And they questioned Him, saying, "Teacher, when will these things be, and what will be the sign when they begin to take place?"

**21:8** And He said, "Watch out that you are not led astray; for many will come in My name, saying, 'I am,' and, 'The time has drawn near.' Therefore do not go after them.

**21:9** And when you hear of wars and uprisings, do not be terrified; these things must take place first, but the end is not yet immediate."

**21:10** Then He was saying to them, "Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom.

**21:11** And great earthquakes will occur from place to place, and plagues and famines, and there will be terrors from heaven and great signs.

**21:12** But before all these things, they will lay their hands upon you and persecute you, handing you over to synagogues and prisons, dragging you to kings and governors on account of My name;

**21:13** and it will turn out for you as a testimony.

**21:14** Settle it therefore in your hearts not to prepare beforehand how you are to answer;

**21:15** for I will give you a mouth and wisdom, which all your adversaries will be unable to resist or contradict.

**21:16** And you will be handed over by parents, brothers, relatives, and friends, and they will put some of you to death;

**21:17** and you will be hated by everyone on account of My name;

**21:18** and not a hair from your head will perish.

**21:19** In your endurance you will possess your souls.

**21:20** Now when you see Jerusalem surrounded by an army, then know that its desolation has drawn near;

**21:21** then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains, and let those in its midst depart, and let those in the countryside not enter it,

**21:22** because these are days of vengeance, so that all things that have been written may be fulfilled.

**21:23** But woe to those who are pregnant and those who are nursing in those days! For there will be great distress upon the land, and wrath against this people.

**21:24** And they will fall by the edge of the sword, and they will be led as captives into all nations, and Jerusalem will be trampled by the Gentiles until the times of the nations are fulfilled.

**21:25** And there will be signs in the sun, moon, and stars, and on the earth distress of the nations because of bewilderment at the roar of the sea and the waves,

**21:26** with people withering away from fear and expectation of the things that will come upon the entire world; for the powers of the heavens will be shaken;

**21:27** and then they will see the Son of Man coming in a cloud with great power and majesty.

**21:28** Now as these things begin to take place, look up and raise your heads, because your redemption is drawing near."

**21:29** And He spoke a parable to them: "Look at the fig tree and all the trees:

**21:30** when they already produce fruit from themselves, you know that summer is near.

**21:31** So you also, when you see these things taking place, know that the kingdom of God is near.

**21:32** Amen, I tell you that this generation will not pass away until all things take place.

**21:33** Heaven and earth will pass away, yet My words will not pass away.

**21:34** Now watch yourselves, so that your hearts may not be weighed down with carousing, drunkenness, and the anxieties of this life, and that day come upon you unexpectedly;

**21:35** for like a snare it will come upon all who sit upon the face of the entire earth.

**21:36** Stay awake, therefore, praying at all times, so that you may be deemed worthy to escape all these things that are about to happen, and to stand before the Son of Man."

**21:37** Now by day He was teaching in the temple, but at night, going out, He would stay on the mountain that is called Olivet.

**21:38** And all the people would come to Him early in the morning in the temple to hear Him.

## Translator Notes

- **21:19** — Latin possidebitis animas vestras uses the future indicative of possideo ("you will possess your souls"), contrasting with Greek traditions that read an imperative or emphasize gaining one's life.
- **21:20** — Latin ab exercitu is singular ("by an army"), whereas the Greek text uses the plural ("armies" or "encampments").
- **21:26** — Latin arescentibus hominibus literally means "with people withering away" (drying up), a distinctive Vulgate rendering of the Greek participle meaning "fainting" or "expiring."
- **21:30** — The Vulgate (cum producunt jam ex se fructum, scitis) explicitly includes "fruit" (fructum), connects ex se ("from themselves") to the trees rather than the observers, and omits the Greek participle "seeing."
- **21:36** — Latin ut digni habeamini ("so that you may be deemed worthy") follows the textual reading preserved in the Vulgate and Majority Greek tradition, whereas several older Greek manuscripts read "so that you may have strength."
