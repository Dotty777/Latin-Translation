## Text

**17:1** And He said to His disciples, "It is impossible for stumbling blocks not to come, yet woe to the one through whom they come.

**17:2** It is more beneficial for him if a millstone is placed around his neck and he is thrown into the sea than for him to cause one of these little ones to stumble.

**17:3** Watch yourselves: if your brother sins against you, rebuke him; and if he repents, forgive him.

**17:4** And if he sins against you seven times in a day, and seven times in a day turns back to you, saying, 'I repent,' forgive him."

**17:5** And the apostles said to the Lord, "Add to our faith."

**17:6** But the Lord said, "If you have faith like a mustard seed, you will say to this mulberry tree, 'Be uprooted and be transplanted into the sea,' and it will obey you.

**17:7** Now who among you, having a servant plowing or herding, would say to him when he has returned from the field, 'Come over at once, recline at table,'

**17:8** and would not say to him, 'Prepare what I may dine on, and fasten your belt, and serve me while I eat and drink, and after these things you will eat and drink'?

**17:9** Is he grateful to that servant because he did what he had commanded him?

**17:10** I do not think so. So you also, when you have done all the things that have been commanded you, say, 'We are useless servants; what we were obligated to do, we have done.'"

**17:11** And it happened, as He was going to Jerusalem, that He was passing through the midst of Samaria and Galilee.

**17:12** And as He was entering a certain village, ten leprous men met Him, who stood at a distance;

**17:13** and they raised their voice, saying, "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us."

**17:14** When He saw them, He said, "Go, show yourselves to the priests." And it happened, as they were going, that they were cleansed.

**17:15** Now one of them, when he saw that he was cleansed, turned back, magnifying God with a loud voice,

**17:16** and fell on his face before His feet, giving thanks; and this man was a Samaritan.

**17:17** And Jesus answered and said, "Were not ten cleansed? And where are the nine?

**17:18** No one was found to return and give glory to God except this foreigner."

**17:19** And He said to him, "Rise, go; because your faith has saved you."

**17:20** Now when He was asked by the Pharisees, "When does the kingdom of God come?" He answered them and said, "The kingdom of God does not come with observation;

**17:21** nor will they say, 'Look, here!' or 'Look, there!' For look, the kingdom of God is within you."

**17:22** And He said to His disciples, "Days will come when you will long to see one day of the Son of Man, and you will not see it.

**17:23** And they will say to you, 'Look, here!' and 'Look, there!' Do not go, nor follow after them;

**17:24** for just as lightning, flashing from under heaven to the places that are under heaven, shines, so will the Son of Man be in His day.

**17:25** First, however, He must suffer many things, and be rejected by this generation.

**17:26** And just as it happened in the days of Noah, so will it also be in the days of the Son of Man:

**17:27** they were eating and drinking, taking wives and being given in marriage, up to the day on which Noah entered the ark; and the flood came and destroyed them all.

**17:28** In the same way, just as it happened in the days of Lot: they were eating and drinking, buying and selling, planting and building;

**17:29** but on the day Lot went out from Sodom, it rained fire and sulfur from heaven and destroyed them all;

**17:30** just like this it will be on the day when the Son of Man is revealed.

**17:31** In that hour, let the one who is on the housetop, whose belongings are in the house, not come down to take them; and likewise, let the one who is in the field not turn back.

**17:32** Be mindful of Lot's wife.

**17:33** Whoever seeks to save his life will lose it, and whoever loses it will keep it alive.

**17:34** I tell you, in that night two will be in one bed: one will be taken up, and the other will be left behind;

**17:35** two women will be grinding together: one will be taken up, and the other will be left behind; two in the field: one will be taken up, and the other will be left behind."

**17:37** And He said to them, "Wherever the body is, there the eagles will also be gathered."

## Translator Notes

- **17:6** — The Clementine Vulgate uses future indicatives (habueritis ... dicetis ... obediet, 'If you have ... you will say ... it will obey'), casting the saying as a future condition rather than the contrary-to-fact conditional ('would say ... would obey') of the Greek text.
- **17:10** — The Clementine Vulgate begins verse 10 with non puto ('I do not think so'), a phrase omitted in most modern critical Greek texts and placed at the end of verse 9 in the Textus Receptus.
- **17:21** — Latin intra vos explicitly renders the debated Greek expression entos hymon as 'within you' rather than 'among you' or 'in your midst'.
- **17:31** — Latin In illa hora ('In that hour') is a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Greek text has 'on that day'.
- **17:35** — The Latin text includes the clause 'two in the field: one will be taken up, and the other will be left behind' at the end of verse 35; it is traditionally numbered as verse 36, and the line is omitted in most modern critical Greek texts.
