## Text

**9:1** Now after summoning the twelve apostles, He gave them power and authority over all demons and to cure illnesses.

**9:2** And He sent them to proclaim the kingdom of God and to heal the sick.

**9:3** And He said to them, "Take nothing on the road: neither a staff, nor a bag, nor bread, nor money; nor are you to have two tunics.

**9:4** And into whatever house you enter, remain there, and do not go out from there.

**9:5** And whoever does not receive you, when you go out from that city, shake off even the dust of your feet as a testimony against them."

**9:6** So having gone out, they went around through the villages, proclaiming the gospel and curing everywhere.

**9:7** Now Herod the tetrarch heard all the things that were being done by Him, and he was perplexed because it was being said

**9:8** by some that John had risen from the dead, and by some that Elijah had appeared, and by others that one of the ancient prophets had risen.

**9:9** And Herod said, "I myself beheaded John; yet who is this man about whom I hear such things?" And he was seeking to see Him.

**9:10** And when the apostles returned, they recounted to Him everything they had done; and taking them along, He withdrew privately into a deserted place that belongs to Bethsaida.

**9:11** When the crowds learned of this, they followed Him; and He welcomed them, and was speaking to them about the kingdom of God, and was healing those who were in need of a cure.

**9:12** Now the day had begun to decline, and the twelve came up and said to Him, "Send the crowds away, so that going into the villages and farms that are nearby, they may lodge and find provisions, for we are here in a deserted place."

**9:13** But He said to them, "You yourselves give them something to eat." And they said, "We do not have more than five loaves and two fish, unless perhaps we go and buy provisions for this entire crowd."

**9:14** Now there were about five thousand men. And He said to His disciples, "Have them recline in dining groups of fifty each."

**9:15** And they did so, and had them all recline.

**9:16** Now having taken the five loaves and the two fish, He looked up to heaven and blessed them; and He broke and distributed them to His disciples to set before the crowds.

**9:17** And all of them ate and were filled. And what remained over for them was taken up, twelve baskets of fragments.

**9:18** And it happened, while He was alone praying, that the disciples also were with Him; and He asked them, saying, "Who do the crowds say that I am?"

**9:19** But they answered and said, "John the Baptist; yet others, Elijah; and others, that one of the former prophets has risen."

**9:20** Then He said to them, "But you, who do you say I am?" Answering, Simon Peter said, "The Christ of God."

**9:21** But He, rebuking them, ordered them not to tell this to anyone,

**9:22** saying, "It is necessary for the Son of Man to suffer many things, and to be rejected by the elders and chief priests and scribes, and to be killed, and on the third day to rise again."

**9:23** Now He was saying to everyone, "If anyone wants to come after Me, let him renounce himself, and take up his cross every day, and follow Me.

**9:24** For the one who wants to save his life will lose it; for the one who loses his life on account of Me will save it.

**9:25** For how does a man benefit if he gains the entire world, yet loses himself and forfeits himself?

**9:26** For whoever is ashamed of Me and of My words, of this one the Son of Man will be ashamed when He comes in His majesty, and that of the Father, and of the holy angels.

**9:27** Now I say to you in truth: there are some standing here who will not taste death until they see the kingdom of God."

**9:28** Now it happened, about eight days after these words, that He took along Peter and James and John, and went up onto a mountain to pray.

**9:29** And while He was praying, the look of His face became different, and His attire became white and dazzling.

**9:30** And look, two men were talking with Him. Now they were Moses and Elijah,

**9:31** seen in majesty, and they were speaking of His departure, which He was about to fulfill in Jerusalem.

**9:32** Now Peter and those who were with him were weighed down with sleep; and waking up, they saw His majesty and the two men who were standing with Him.

**9:33** And it happened, as they were departing from Him, that Peter said to Jesus, "Master, it is good for us to be here, and let us make three tents, one for You, and one for Moses, and one for Elijah," not knowing what he was saying.

**9:34** Now while he was speaking these things, a cloud formed and overshadowed them; and they were afraid as they were entering into the cloud.

**9:35** And a voice came from the cloud, saying, "This is My beloved Son; listen to Him."

**9:36** And while the voice was sounding, Jesus was found alone. And they themselves remained silent, and in those days told no one anything of the things they had seen.

**9:37** Now it happened on the following day, as they were coming down from the mountain, that a large crowd met them.

**9:38** And look, a man from the crowd cried out, saying, "Teacher, I implore You, look upon my son, because he is my only child;

**9:39** and look, a spirit seizes him, and suddenly he cries out, and it dashes him down and convulses him with foaming, and scarcely departs, tearing at him;

**9:40** and I asked Your disciples to drive it out, and they were not able."

**9:41** And answering, Jesus said, "O unbelieving and perverse generation, how long will I be with you and endure you? Bring your son here."

**9:42** And as he was approaching, the demon dashed him down and convulsed him.

**9:43** And Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit, and healed the boy, and returned him to his father.

**9:44** Now all were astonished at the greatness of God; and while everyone was marveling at all the things He was doing, He said to His disciples, "You, place these words in your hearts; for the Son of Man is about to be handed over into the hands of men."

**9:45** Yet they did not comprehend this saying, and it was veiled before them so that they would not perceive it; and they were afraid to question Him about this saying.

**9:46** Now a thought entered into them as to which of them was the greatest.

**9:47** But Jesus, seeing the thoughts of their heart, took a child and placed him beside Himself,

**9:48** and said to them, "Whoever welcomes this child in My name receives Me, and whoever receives Me receives Him who sent Me. For whoever is least among all of you, this one is the greatest."

**9:49** Then John answered and said, "Master, we saw someone driving out demons in Your name, and we forbade him, because he does not follow with us."

**9:50** And Jesus said to him, "Do not forbid him, for whoever is not against you is for you."

**9:51** Now it happened, as the days of His being taken up were being fulfilled, that He Himself firmly set His face to go to Jerusalem.

**9:52** And He sent messengers ahead of Him; and as they went, they entered into a city of the Samaritans to make ready for Him.

**9:53** And they did not receive Him, because His face was that of one going to Jerusalem.

**9:54** Now when His disciples James and John saw this, they said, "Lord, do You want us to command that fire descend from heaven and consume them?"

**9:55** And turning around, He rebuked them, saying, "You do not know what spirit you are of.

**9:56** The Son of Man did not come to destroy lives, but to save them." And they went away to another village.

**9:57** Now it happened, as they were walking on the road, that someone said to Him, "I will follow You wherever You go."

**9:58** Jesus said to him, "Foxes have dens, and the birds of the sky have nests, but the Son of Man has nowhere to rest His head."

**9:59** Then He said to another, "Follow Me." Yet he said, "Lord, allow me first to go and bury my father."

**9:60** And Jesus said to him, "Let the dead bury their own dead; but you, go and announce the kingdom of God."

**9:61** And another said, "I will follow You, Lord, but allow me first to say farewell to what is at home."

**9:62** Jesus said to him, "No one setting his hand to the plow and looking back is suited for the kingdom of God."

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The Vulgate reads "et inde ne exeatis" ("and do not go out from there"), instructing the apostles to stay in the same house rather than move about, whereas the Greek text reads positively ("and depart from there").
- **9:10** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "in locum desertum, qui est Bethsaidæ" ("into a deserted place that belongs to Bethsaida"), whereas critical Greek editions read "to a city called Bethsaida."
- **9:26** — Here and in 9:31 the Vulgate renders Greek "doxa" ("glory") as "majestas" ("majesty").
- **9:35** — The Vulgate reads "Filius meus dilectus" ("My beloved Son"), whereas critical Greek editions read "My Chosen One."
- **9:42** — Native Vulgate Luke 9 divides the exorcism across 9:42-43 and places the crowd's astonishment and Jesus's prediction together in 9:44 (mapping to Saints.Bible 9:43-44); it also reads "in cordibus vestris" ("in your hearts"), whereas the Greek text reads "into your ears."
- **9:44** — Native Vulgate Luke 9 divides the exorcism across 9:42-43 and places the crowd's astonishment and Jesus's prediction together in 9:44 (mapping to Saints.Bible 9:43-44); it also reads "in cordibus vestris" ("in your hearts"), whereas the Greek text reads "into your ears."
- **9:55** — Here and in 9:56 the Clementine Vulgate includes Jesus's words ("You do not know what spirit you are of. The Son of Man did not come to destroy lives, but to save them."), which are omitted in critical Greek editions.
