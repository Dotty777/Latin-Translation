## Text

**23:1** And rising up, the entire multitude of them led Him to Pilate.

**23:2** And they began to accuse Him, saying, "We found this man subverting our nation, and forbidding taxes to be given to Caesar, and saying that He is the Christ, a king."

**23:3** Now Pilate questioned Him, saying, "Are You the king of the Jews?" But answering, He said, "You say so."

**23:4** Then Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowds, "I find no ground for a charge in this man."

**23:5** But they grew more insistent, saying, "He stirs up the people, teaching throughout all of Judea, beginning from Galilee all the way to here."

**23:6** Now Pilate, hearing of Galilee, asked whether the man was a Galilean.

**23:7** And when he learned that He was under Herod's authority, he sent Him over to Herod, who himself also was in Jerusalem in those days.

**23:8** Now when Herod saw Jesus, he rejoiced greatly. For he had been desiring for a long time to see Him, because he had heard many things about Him, and he was hoping to see some sign performed by Him.

**23:9** And he was questioning Him with many words. But He Himself answered him nothing.

**23:10** And the chief priests and the scribes were standing there, persistently accusing Him.

**23:11** Then Herod with his troops scorned and mocked Him after clothing Him in a white garment, and sent Him back to Pilate.

**23:12** And Herod and Pilate became friends on that very day; for previously they were enemies toward one another.

**23:13** Now Pilate, having called together the chief priests, and the magistrates, and the people,

**23:14** said to them, "You brought this man to me as one turning the people away, and look, questioning Him before you, I have found no ground for a charge in this man among the things of which you accuse Him.

**23:15** But neither has Herod; for I sent you to him, and look, nothing deserving of death has been done by Him.

**23:16** Therefore, after punishing Him, I will release Him."

**23:18** But the entire crowd cried out all together, saying, "Take this man away, and release to us Barabbas,"

**23:19** who had been thrown into prison because of a certain insurrection that had taken place in the city, and for murder.

**23:20** Then Pilate spoke to them again, wanting to release Jesus.

**23:21** But they kept shouting back, saying, "Crucify, crucify Him!"

**23:22** And he said to them a third time, "Why, what evil has this man done? I find no ground for death in Him; therefore I will chastise Him and release Him."

**23:23** But they kept pressing on with loud voices, demanding that He be crucified; and their voices were prevailing.

**23:24** And Pilate decreed that their petition be carried out.

**23:25** And he released to them the one who had been thrown into prison because of murder and insurrection, whom they were asking for; but Jesus he handed over to their will.

**23:26** And while they were leading Him, they seized a certain Simon of Cyrene, coming from the countryside, and they placed the cross upon him to carry behind Jesus.

**23:27** And a large crowd of the people and of women was following Him, who were wailing and lamenting Him.

**23:28** But Jesus, turning to them, said, "Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep over Me, but weep over yourselves and over your children.

**23:29** For look, days will come in which they will say, 'Blessed are the barren, and the wombs that have not given birth, and the breasts that have not nursed.'

**23:30** Then they will begin to say to the mountains, 'Fall upon us,' and to the hills, 'Cover us.'

**23:31** For if they do these things in the green wood, what will happen in the dry?"

**23:32** And two other wicked men were also being led with Him to be put to death.

**23:33** And after they came to the place that is called Calvary, there they crucified Him, and the robbers, one on the right and the other on the left.

**23:34** Now Jesus was saying, "Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they do." And dividing His garments, they cast lots.

**23:35** And the people stood watching, and the rulers along with them were ridiculing Him, saying, "He saved others; let Him save Himself, if this man is the Christ of God, the chosen one."

**23:36** And the soldiers also were mocking Him, coming up and offering Him vinegar,

**23:37** and saying, "If You are the king of the Jews, save Yourself."

**23:38** Now there was also an inscription written above Him in Greek, and Latin, and Hebrew letters: "This is the king of the Jews."

**23:39** And one of the robbers who were hanging there was blaspheming Him, saying, "If You are the Christ, save Yourself and us."

**23:40** But the other, answering, was rebuking him, saying, "Do you not even fear God, since you are under the same condemnation?

**23:41** And we indeed justly, for we are receiving what our deeds deserve; but this man has done nothing evil."

**23:42** And he was saying to Jesus, "Lord, remember me when You come into Your kingdom."

**23:43** And Jesus said to him, "Amen, I tell you, today you will be with Me in paradise."

**23:44** Now it was about the sixth hour, and darkness came over the whole earth until the ninth hour.

**23:45** And the sun was darkened, and the veil of the temple was torn down the middle.

**23:46** And crying out with a loud voice, Jesus said, "Father, into Your hands I entrust My spirit." And saying these things, He breathed His last.

**23:47** Now the centurion, seeing what had happened, glorified God, saying, "Truly this man was righteous."

**23:48** And the entire crowd of those who were present together at this spectacle and were seeing the things that were happening returned, beating their breasts.

**23:49** And all His acquaintances were standing at a distance, and the women who had followed Him from Galilee, seeing these things.

**23:50** And look, there was a man named Joseph, who was a council member, a good and righteous man.

**23:51** This man had not consented to their counsel and deeds; he was from Arimathea, a city of Judea, and he himself was also waiting for the kingdom of God.

**23:52** This man went to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus,

**23:53** and having taken Him down, he wrapped Him in a linen cloth and laid Him in a hewn tomb, in which no one had yet been laid.

**23:54** And it was the day of Preparation, and the Sabbath was dawning.

**23:55** Now following closely, the women who had come with Him from Galilee saw the tomb and how His body was laid.

**23:56** And returning, they prepared spices and ointments; and on the Sabbath they indeed kept still according to the commandment.

## Translator Notes

- **23:11** — The Vulgate renders the Greek for "splendid/bright garment" specifically as veste alba ("a white garment").
- **23:15** — The Clementine Vulgate reads nam remisi vos ad illum ("for I sent you to him"), contrasting with critical Greek texts that read "for he sent him back to us"; ei ("by Him") functions as a dative of agent with actum est.
- **23:33** — Latin Calvariae is the genitive of calvaria ("skull") and the origin of the English name "Calvary"; here and in v. 39 the Vulgate shifts to latrones ("robbers"), whereas v. 32 uses nequam ("wicked men").
- **23:42** — The Clementine Vulgate retains the address Domine ("Lord") rather than "Jesus," and translates "into Your kingdom" (in regnum tuum, accusative) rather than "in Your kingdom."
- **23:56** — Latin siluerunt literally means "kept silent" or "kept still," reflecting the Vulgate's rendering of Sabbath stillness and cessation from work.
