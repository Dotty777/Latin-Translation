## Text

**8:1** And it happened afterward that He Himself was traveling through cities and villages, preaching and proclaiming the good news of the kingdom of God, and the twelve were with Him,

**8:2** and certain women who had been cured of evil spirits and illnesses: Mary, who is called Magdalene, out of whom seven demons had gone,

**8:3** and Joanna the wife of Chuza, Herod's steward, and Susanna, and many others, who were ministering to Him from their own means.

**8:4** Now when a very large crowd was gathering and people were hurrying to Him from the cities, He spoke through a comparison:

**8:5** "The one who sows went out to sow his seed. And while he was sowing, some fell alongside the road, and it was trampled underfoot, and the birds of the sky ate it.

**8:6** And some fell on the rock, and when it sprouted, it dried up, because it had no moisture.

**8:7** And some fell among thorns, and the thorns sprang up together and choked it.

**8:8** And some fell into good soil, and growing up, it produced fruit a hundredfold." Saying these things, He cried out, "The one who has ears to hear, let him hear."

**8:9** Now His disciples were asking Him what this parable was.

**8:10** To them He said, "To you it has been given to know the mystery of the kingdom of God, but to the rest in parables, so that seeing they may not see, and hearing they may not understand.

**8:11** Now this is the parable: the seed is the word of God.

**8:12** And those alongside the road, these are the ones who hear; then the devil comes and takes away the word from their heart, so that they may not believe and be saved.

**8:13** For those on the rock are the ones who, when they hear, receive the word with joy; and these have no roots; they believe for a time, and in a time of testing they draw back.

**8:14** Now what fell into thorns, these are the ones who have heard, and as they go along, they are choked by anxieties, and riches, and pleasures of life, and they do not yield fruit.

**8:15** But what fell into good soil, these are the ones who, hearing the word in a good and excellent heart, hold it fast and bear fruit in patience.

**8:16** Now no one who lights a lamp covers it with a vessel or puts it under a bed, but puts it on a lampstand, so that those who enter may see the light.

**8:17** For there is nothing concealed that will not be revealed, nor anything hidden that will not be known and come into the open.

**8:18** Watch therefore how you hear. For whoever has, to him it will be given; and whoever does not have, even what he thinks he has will be taken away from him."

**8:19** Now His mother and brothers came to Him, and they could not reach Him because of the crowd.

**8:20** And it was reported to Him, "Your mother and Your brothers are standing outside, wanting to see You."

**8:21** Answering, He said to them, "My mother and My brothers are these, who hear the word of God and do it."

**8:22** Now it happened on one of the days that He Himself got into a boat with His disciples, and He said to them, "Let us cross over the lake." And they embarked.

**8:23** And as they were sailing, He fell asleep; and a windstorm came down onto the lake, and they were being flooded, and they were in danger.

**8:24** And approaching, they woke Him up, saying, "Master, we are perishing!" And rising up, He rebuked the wind and the tempest of the water, and it ceased, and there came a calm.

**8:25** Then He said to them, "Where is your faith?" And being afraid, they marveled to one another, saying, "Who do you suppose this is, that He commands even the winds and the sea, and they obey Him?"

**8:26** And they sailed to the region of the Gerasenes, which is opposite Galilee.

**8:27** And when He had stepped out onto the land, a certain man met Him who had already had a demon for a long time, and he wore no clothing, nor did he stay in a house, but in the tombs.

**8:28** When he saw Jesus, he fell down before Him, and crying out with a loud voice, he said, "What is there between me and You, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I implore You, do not torment me."

**8:29** For He was commanding the unclean spirit to come out of the man. For many times it would seize him, and he was bound with chains and kept under guard with shackles. And having broken the bonds, he would be driven by the demon into deserted places.

**8:30** Then Jesus asked him, saying, "What is your name?" And he said, "Legion," because many demons had entered into him.

**8:31** And they were begging Him not to command them to go into the abyss.

**8:32** Now a herd of many pigs was feeding on the mountain there, and they begged Him to permit them to enter into them. And He permitted them.

**8:33** So the demons came out of the man and entered into the pigs, and the herd rushed headlong down the steep slope into the lake and was drowned.

**8:34** When the herdsmen saw what had happened, they fled and reported it in the city and in the countryside.

**8:35** And they went out to see what had happened, and they came to Jesus and found the man from whom the demons had gone out sitting, clothed and of sound mind, at His feet, and they were afraid.

**8:36** Those who had seen it also reported to them how he had been made well from the legion;

**8:37** and the whole multitude of the region of the Gerasenes begged Him to depart from them, because they were gripped by great fear. And getting into the boat, He Himself returned.

**8:38** And the man from whom the demons had gone out was begging Him that he might be with Him. But Jesus sent him away, saying,

**8:39** "Return to your house, and tell what great things God has done for you." And he went away throughout the whole city, proclaiming what great things Jesus had done for him.

**8:40** Now it happened that when Jesus had returned, the crowd welcomed Him, for they were all waiting for Him.

**8:41** And look, a man came whose name was Jairus, and he himself was a ruler of the synagogue; and he fell at the feet of Jesus, begging Him to come into his house,

**8:42** because he had an only daughter, about twelve years old, and she was dying. And it happened that while He was going, He was being pressed in on by the crowd.

**8:43** And a certain woman had a flow of blood for twelve years, who had spent all her livelihood on physicians and could not be cured by anyone;

**8:44** she came up from behind and touched the fringe of His garment, and immediately her flow of blood stopped.

**8:45** And Jesus said, "Who is it that touched Me?" And while all were denying it, Peter and those who were with him said, "Master, the crowds are pressing in on You and jostling You, and You say, 'Who touched Me?'"

**8:46** And Jesus said, "Someone touched Me, for I know that power has gone out from Me."

**8:47** Now when the woman saw that she had not escaped notice, she came trembling and fell down before His feet, and she declared in the presence of all the people for what reason she had touched Him, and how she had been healed immediately.

**8:48** And He said to her, "Daughter, your faith has saved you; go in peace."

**8:49** While He was still speaking, someone came to the ruler of the synagogue, saying to him, "Because your daughter has died, do not trouble Him."

**8:50** But Jesus, having heard this word, answered the girl's father, "Do not be afraid; only believe, and she will be saved."

**8:51** And when He had come to the house, He did not permit anyone to enter with Him except Peter, James, and John, and the father and mother of the girl.

**8:52** Now all were weeping and mourning for her. But He said, "Do not weep; the girl is not dead, but is sleeping."

**8:53** And they were ridiculing Him, knowing that she had died.

**8:54** But He Himself, taking hold of her hand, called out, saying, "Girl, rise."

**8:55** And her spirit returned, and she rose at once. And He ordered that she be given something to eat.

**8:56** And her parents were astonished, and He commanded them not to tell anyone what had happened.

## Translator Notes

- **8:10** — The Vulgate reads singular mysterium ("mystery"), whereas the Greek text of Luke has plural "mysteries."
- **8:25** — The Vulgate reads mari ("the sea") rather than Greek "the water," and uses the parenthetical putas ("do you suppose") to render the Greek inferential particle.
- **8:36** — Distinctively, the Vulgate reads a legione ("from the legion"), whereas the Greek text reads "the demon-possessed man."
- **8:40** — The source Latin text prints future erunt, here understood in the past narrative context as the standard Vulgate imperfect erant ("were").
- **8:49** — The Vulgate reads ad principem synagogae ("to the ruler of the synagogue") rather than Greek "from the synagogue ruler's house," and illum ("Him") rather than "the Teacher."
