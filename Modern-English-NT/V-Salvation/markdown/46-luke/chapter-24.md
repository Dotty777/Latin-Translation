## Text

**24:1** Now on the first day of the week, very early at dawn, they came to the tomb, carrying the spices that they had prepared.

**24:2** And they found the stone rolled back from the tomb.

**24:3** And having entered, they did not find the body of the Lord Jesus.

**24:4** And it happened, while they were dismayed in mind about this, look, two men stood beside them in gleaming clothing.

**24:5** Now while they were afraid and lowered their faces to the ground, they said to them, "Why are you seeking the living one among the dead?

**24:6** He is not here, but He has risen; remember how He spoke to you when He was still in Galilee,

**24:7** saying that the Son of Man must be handed over into the hands of sinful men, and be crucified, and rise again on the third day."

**24:8** And they remembered His words.

**24:9** And having returned from the tomb, they reported all these things to those eleven and to all the rest.

**24:10** Now it was Mary Magdalene, and Joanna, and Mary of James, and the other women who were with them, who were saying these things to the apostles.

**24:11** And before them those words seemed like nonsense, and they did not believe them.

**24:12** But Peter, rising, ran to the tomb; and stooping down, he saw the linen cloths placed by themselves, and he departed, marveling within himself at what had happened.

**24:13** And look, two of them were going on that very day to a village that was at a distance of sixty stadia from Jerusalem, named Emmaus.

**24:14** And they themselves were speaking with one another about all these things that had occurred.

**24:15** And it happened, while they were conversing and inquiring with one another, that Jesus Himself, drawing near, was walking with them.

**24:16** Yet their eyes were held so that they would not recognize Him.

**24:17** And He said to them, "What are these conversations that you are exchanging with one another while walking, and you are sad?"

**24:18** And answering, one of them, whose name was Cleopas, said to Him, "Are You the only stranger in Jerusalem, and have You not known the things that have happened in it during these days?"

**24:19** To them He said, "What things?" And they said, "Concerning Jesus the Nazarene, who was a man, a prophet, powerful in deed and word before God and all the people,

**24:20** and how our chief priests and rulers handed Him over to a sentence of death, and crucified Him.

**24:21** Yet we were hoping that He Himself was about to redeem Israel; and now, in addition to all these things, today is the third day since these things took place.

**24:22** Moreover, certain women from among us frightened us, who were at the tomb before daylight,

**24:23** and when His body was not found, they came, saying that they had also seen a vision of angels, who say that He lives.

**24:24** And some from among us departed to the tomb and found it just as the women said, yet Him they did not find."

**24:25** And He said to them, "Oh, foolish ones, and slow in heart to believe in everything that the prophets have spoken!

**24:26** Was it not necessary that the Christ suffer these things and thus enter into His glory?"

**24:27** And beginning from Moses and all the prophets, He was explaining to them in all the Scriptures the things that were about Himself.

**24:28** And they drew near to the village to which they were going, and He made it appear that He was going farther.

**24:29** And they pressed Him, saying, "Stay with us, because evening is coming on, and the day has already declined." And He went in with them.

**24:30** And it happened, while He was reclining at table with them, that He took the bread, and blessed and broke it, and was handing it to them.

**24:31** And their eyes were opened, and they recognized Him; and He Himself vanished from their eyes.

**24:32** And they said to one another, "Was not our heart burning inside us while He spoke on the road and opened the Scriptures to us?"

**24:33** And rising at that same hour, they returned to Jerusalem, and they found the eleven gathered together and those who were with them,

**24:34** saying, "The Lord has truly risen, and has appeared to Simon."

**24:35** And they themselves were recounting what had taken place on the road, and how they recognized Him in the breaking of the bread.

**24:36** Now while they were speaking these things, Jesus stood in the midst of them and said to them, "Peace to you; it is I, do not be afraid."

**24:37** Yet troubled and terrified, they supposed that they were seeing a spirit.

**24:38** And He said to them, "Why are you troubled, and why do thoughts rise up in your hearts?

**24:39** Look at My hands and feet, that it is I Myself; feel and see, because a spirit does not have flesh and bones, as you see that I have."

**24:40** And after He had said this, He showed them His hands and feet.

**24:41** Now while they were still not believing and were marveling because of joy, He said, "Do you have anything here to eat?"

**24:42** And they offered Him a portion of roasted fish and a honeycomb.

**24:43** And when He had eaten in their presence, taking what remained, He gave it to them.

**24:44** And He said to them, "These are the words that I spoke to you while I was still with you, that it is necessary for all things written in the Law of Moses, and the Prophets, and the Psalms concerning Me to be fulfilled."

**24:45** Then He opened their mind so that they might understand the Scriptures,

**24:46** and He said to them, "Thus it is written, and thus it was necessary for the Christ to suffer and to rise again from the dead on the third day,

**24:47** and for repentance and forgiveness of sins to be preached in His name among all nations, beginning from Jerusalem.

**24:48** Now you are witnesses of these things.

**24:49** And I am sending the promise of My Father upon you; as for you, remain in the city until you are clothed with power from on high."

**24:50** Then He led them outside to Bethany, and having raised His hands, He blessed them.

**24:51** And it happened, while He was blessing them, that He withdrew from them and was being carried into heaven.

**24:52** And worshiping, they returned to Jerusalem with great joy,

**24:53** and they were continually in the temple, praising and blessing God. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **24:17** — In the Clementine Vulgate, 'et estis tristes?' ('and you are sad?') is part of Jesus's question, whereas modern critical Greek texts close the question after 'while walking' and add a narrative clause ('and they stood still, looking sad').
- **24:36** — The Clementine Vulgate includes 'ego sum, nolite timere' ('it is I, do not be afraid') after 'Peace to you,' a reading attested in several Greek and Old Latin witnesses but omitted in modern critical Greek editions.
- **24:42** — The Clementine Vulgate includes 'et favum mellis' ('and a honeycomb') alongside the roasted fish, whereas modern critical Greek editions omit this phrase.
- **24:43** — The Clementine Vulgate adds 'sumens reliquias dedit eis' ('taking what remained, He gave it to them'), a reading rooted in Latin tradition and a few Greek and Syriac witnesses but absent from most Greek manuscripts.
- **24:53** — The Clementine Vulgate includes both 'laudantes et benedicentes' ('praising and blessing') and the concluding 'Amen,' whereas modern critical Greek editions omit 'praising and' as well as 'Amen.'
