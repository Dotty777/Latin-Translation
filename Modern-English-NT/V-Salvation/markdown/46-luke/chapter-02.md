## Text

**2:1** Now it came about in those days that a decree went out from Caesar Augustus that the entire world should be registered.

**2:2** This first registration was carried out by Quirinius, governor of Syria.

**2:3** And all were going to register, each one to his own city.

**2:4** Now Joseph also went up from Galilee, from the city of Nazareth, into Judea, into the city of David, which is called Bethlehem, since he was from the house and family of David,

**2:5** to register with Mary, his betrothed wife, who was pregnant.

**2:6** Now it came about, while they were there, that the days were fulfilled for her to give birth.

**2:7** And she gave birth to her firstborn son, wrapped Him in strips of cloth, and laid Him down in a manger, because there was no place for them in the inn.

**2:8** And there were shepherds in that same region staying awake and guarding the watches of the night over their flock.

**2:9** And look, an angel of the Lord stood beside them, and the brightness of God shone around them, and they feared with a great fear.

**2:10** And the angel said to them, "Do not be afraid, for look, I announce to you good news of a great joy that will be for all the people,

**2:11** because today a Savior has been born for you, who is Christ the Lord, in the city of David.

**2:12** And this is a sign for you: you will find an infant wrapped in strips of cloth and placed in a manger."

**2:13** And suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly army, praising God and saying,

**2:14** "Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace to people of good will."

**2:15** And it came about, when the angels departed from them into heaven, that the shepherds were speaking to one another, "Let us cross over to Bethlehem and see this word that has come about, which the Lord has shown us."

**2:16** And they came hurrying, and they found Mary and Joseph, and the infant placed in the manger.

**2:17** Now upon seeing, they came to know about the word that had been told to them concerning this child.

**2:18** And all who heard marveled, even at the things that had been told to them by the shepherds.

**2:19** Mary, however, was keeping all these words, weighing them in her heart.

**2:20** And the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God in all that they had heard and seen, just as it was spoken to them.

**2:21** And after eight days were completed for the child to be circumcised, His name was called Jesus, which had been named by the angel before He was conceived in the womb.

**2:22** And after the days of her purification were fulfilled according to the law of Moses, they brought Him to Jerusalem to present Him to the Lord,

**2:23** just as it is written in the law of the Lord, "Every male opening the womb will be called holy to the Lord,"

**2:24** and to offer a sacrifice according to what was stated in the law of the Lord, a pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons.

**2:25** And look, there was a man in Jerusalem whose name was Simeon; and this man was righteous and devout, waiting for the consolation of Israel, and the Holy Spirit was in him.

**2:26** And he had received an answer from the Holy Spirit that he would not see death unless he first saw the Christ of the Lord.

**2:27** And he came in the Spirit into the temple. And when His parents brought in the child Jesus to do for Him according to the custom of the law,

**2:28** he himself took Him into his arms, and blessed God, and said,

**2:29** "Now You release Your servant, Lord, according to Your word, in peace,

**2:30** because my eyes have seen Your salvation,

**2:31** which You have prepared before the face of all peoples:

**2:32** a light for the revelation of the nations, and the glory of Your people Israel."

**2:33** And His father and mother were marveling at the things that were being spoken about Him.

**2:34** And Simeon blessed them and said to Mary His mother, "Look, this one is set for the downfall and for the rising of many in Israel, and for a sign that will be spoken against,

**2:35** and a sword will pierce through your own soul, so that thoughts from many hearts may be revealed."

**2:36** And there was Anna, a prophetess, daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher; she had advanced in many days, and had lived with her husband seven years from her virginity.

**2:37** And she was a widow up to eighty-four years, who did not depart from the temple, serving with fastings and supplications night and day.

**2:38** And she, coming up at that very hour, was giving praise to the Lord, and was speaking about Him to all who were waiting for the redemption of Israel.

**2:39** And when they had completed everything according to the law of the Lord, they returned to Galilee, to their own city, Nazareth.

**2:40** Now the child was growing and becoming strong, full of wisdom, and the grace of God was in Him.

**2:41** And His parents went every year to Jerusalem on the solemn day of Passover.

**2:42** And when He had become twelve years old, as they went up to Jerusalem according to the custom of the feast day,

**2:43** and when the days were completed, as they were returning, the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem, and His parents did not know it.

**2:44** Supposing, however, that He was in the company, they went a day's journey, and were searching for Him among their relatives and acquaintances.

**2:45** And not finding Him, they went back to Jerusalem, searching for Him.

**2:46** And it came about that after three days they found Him in the temple, sitting in the middle of the teachers, listening to them and questioning them.

**2:47** Now all who were listening to Him were astounded at His understanding and His answers.

**2:48** And when they saw Him, they were astonished. And His mother said to Him, "Son, why have You treated us like this? Look, Your father and I have been searching for You in sorrow."

**2:49** And He said to them, "Why is it that you were searching for Me? Did you not know that I must be in the things that belong to My Father?"

**2:50** And they themselves did not understand the word that He spoke to them.

**2:51** And He went down with them and came to Nazareth, and remained subject to them. And His mother was keeping all these words in her heart.

**2:52** And Jesus was advancing in wisdom, in age, and in favor before God and people.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The Vulgate reads 'a præside Syriæ Cyrino' ("by Quirinius, governor of Syria"), expressing Quirinius as the agent of the registration rather than the Greek temporal clause ("while Quirinius was governing Syria").
- **2:14** — The Vulgate reads 'hominibus bonæ voluntatis' ("to people of good will"), taking the Greek genitive ('eudokias') as a genitive of quality rather than "those with whom He is pleased."
- **2:17** — The Vulgate reads 'cognoverunt' ("they came to know"), reflecting the variant 'egnosan', whereas most Greek editions read 'egnorisan' ("they made known").
- **2:22** — The Vulgate reads the singular 'purgationis ejus' ("her purification," referring to Mary under Leviticus 12, though Latin 'ejus' is grammatically gender-neutral), whereas critical Greek editions read the plural "their purification."
- **2:49** — Literally "in those [things] that are of My Father" ('in his quæ Patris mei sunt'), a neuter plural expression preserving the Vulgate's openness between "My Father's affairs" and "My Father's house."
