## Text

**22:1** Now the feast day of Unleavened Bread, which is called Passover, was approaching.

**22:2** And the chief priests and the scribes were seeking how they might kill Jesus, yet they feared the people.

**22:3** Then Satan entered Judas, who was called by the surname Iscariot, one of the twelve.

**22:4** And he went away and spoke with the chief priests and the magistrates about how he might hand Him over to them.

**22:5** And they rejoiced and made an agreement to give him money.

**22:6** And he gave his promise, and was seeking an opportunity to hand Him over away from the crowds.

**22:7** Then the day of Unleavened Bread came, on which it was necessary for the Passover to be killed.

**22:8** And He sent Peter and John, saying, "Go, prepare the Passover for us so that we may eat."

**22:9** But they said, "Where do You wish us to prepare it?"

**22:10** And He said to them, "Look, as you enter the city, a certain man carrying a pitcher of water will meet you; follow him into the house that he enters,

**22:11** and you will say to the master of the house, 'The Teacher says to you: Where is the guest room where I may eat the Passover with My disciples?'

**22:12** And he himself will show you a large upper room, furnished, and prepare it there."

**22:13** So they went and found it as He had said to them, and they prepared the Passover.

**22:14** And when the hour had arrived, He reclined at table, and the twelve apostles with Him.

**22:15** And He said to them, "With longing I have longed to eat this Passover with you before I suffer.

**22:16** For I tell you that from now on I will not eat it until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God."

**22:17** And having taken a cup, He gave thanks and said, "Take it and divide it among yourselves.

**22:18** For I tell you that I will not drink from the produce of the vine until the kingdom of God comes."

**22:19** And having taken bread, He gave thanks, and broke it, and gave it to them, saying, "This is My body, which is given for you; do this in memory of Me."

**22:20** Likewise the cup also, after He had dined, saying, "This is the cup, the new covenant in My blood, which will be poured out for you.

**22:21** Yet look, the hand of the one handing Me over is with Me on the table.

**22:22** And indeed the Son of Man goes according to what has been determined; yet woe to that man through whom He will be handed over."

**22:23** And they began to ask among themselves which of them it was who was about to do this.

**22:24** Now a dispute also arose among them about which of them seemed to be the greatest.

**22:25** But He said to them, "The kings of the nations exercise dominion over them, and those who hold authority over them are called benefactors.

**22:26** With you, however, it is not so; instead, let the one who is greatest among you become like the least, and the one who is leader like the one who serves.

**22:27** For who is greater: the one reclining at table, or the one who serves? Is it not the one reclining? Yet I am in your midst like one who serves.

**22:28** And you are the ones who have remained with Me in My trials.

**22:29** And I bestow on you, just as My Father bestowed on Me, a kingdom,

**22:30** so that you may eat and drink at My table in My kingdom, and may sit on thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel."

**22:31** And the Lord said, "Simon, Simon, look, Satan has demanded you so that he might sift you like wheat;

**22:32** yet I Myself have prayed for you so that your faith will not fail; and you, once you have turned back, strengthen your brothers."

**22:33** And he said to Him, "Lord, I am prepared to go with You both into prison and to death."

**22:34** But He said, "I tell you, Peter, a rooster will not crow today before you three times deny knowing Me." And He said to them,

**22:35** "When I sent you without a purse, a satchel, or sandals, did you lack anything?"

**22:36** And they said, "Nothing." Therefore He said to them, "But now let the one who has a purse take it, and likewise a satchel; and let the one who has none sell his tunic and buy a sword.

**22:37** For I tell you that this which is written must still be fulfilled in Me: 'And He was counted with the wicked.' For indeed those things concerning Me have their end."

**22:38** And they said, "Lord, look, there are two swords here." But He said to them, "That is enough."

**22:39** And having gone out, He went according to custom to the Mount of Olives, and the disciples also followed Him.

**22:40** And when He had arrived at the place, He said to them, "Pray so that you do not enter into temptation."

**22:41** And He Himself drew away from them as far as a stone's throw, and kneeling down, He was praying,

**22:42** saying, "Father, if You wish, take this cup away from Me; yet let not My will, but Yours, be done."

**22:43** Then an angel from heaven appeared to Him, strengthening Him. And being in agony, He was praying at greater length.

**22:44** And His sweat became like drops of blood running down onto the ground.

**22:45** And when He had risen from prayer and come to His disciples, He found them sleeping from sorrow.

**22:46** And He said to them, "Why are you sleeping? Rise, pray, so that you do not enter into temptation."

**22:47** While He was still speaking, look, there was a crowd, and the one who was called Judas, one of the twelve, was going ahead of them, and he approached Jesus to kiss Him.

**22:48** But Jesus said to him, "Judas, are you handing over the Son of Man with a kiss?"

**22:49** Now when those who were around Him saw what was about to happen, they said to Him, "Lord, should we strike with the sword?"

**22:50** And one of them struck the slave of the high priest and severed his right ear.

**22:51** But Jesus responded and said, "Allow it this far." And when He touched his ear, He healed him.

**22:52** Then Jesus said to those who had come to Him, the chief priests and the magistrates of the temple and the elders, "As if against a bandit, have you come out with swords and clubs?

**22:53** When I was with you daily in the temple, you did not extend your hands against Me; but this is your hour and the authority of darkness."

**22:54** Then seizing Him, they led Him to the house of the high priest; Peter, however, was following from a distance.

**22:55** Now when a fire had been lit in the middle of the courtyard and they were sitting around it, Peter was in their midst.

**22:56** When a certain servant girl saw him sitting by the light and looked intently at him, she said, "This one also was with Him."

**22:57** But he denied Him, saying, "Woman, I do not know Him."

**22:58** And after a little while, another man, seeing him, said, "You too are one of them." Peter, however, said, "Man, I am not."

**22:59** And after an interval of about one hour, a certain other man was insisting, saying, "Truly, this one also was with Him, for he is also a Galilean."

**22:60** And Peter said, "Man, I do not know what you are saying." And at once, while he was still speaking, a rooster crowed.

**22:61** And turning around, the Lord looked at Peter, and Peter recalled the word of the Lord, as He had said, "Before a rooster crows, you will deny Me three times."

**22:62** And going outside, Peter wept bitterly.

**22:63** And the men who were holding Him kept mocking Him, beating Him.

**22:64** And they blindfolded Him and kept striking His face, and they were questioning Him, saying, "Prophesy! Who is the one who struck You?"

**22:65** And blaspheming, they kept speaking many other things against Him.

**22:66** And when day arrived, the elders of the people, the chief priests, and the scribes assembled, and they led Him into their council, saying, "If You are the Christ, tell us."

**22:67** And He said to them, "If I tell you, you will not believe Me;

**22:68** and if I also question you, you will not answer Me, nor will you release Me.

**22:69** Yet from now on the Son of Man will be sitting at the right hand of the power of God."

**22:70** Then they all said, "Are You then the Son of God?" And He said, "You yourselves say that I am."

**22:71** And they said, "Why do we still require testimony? For we ourselves have heard it from His mouth."

## Translator Notes

- **22:20** — Vulgate reads future passive "fundetur" ("will be poured out") and third-person singular "postquam coenavit" ("after He had dined"), where critical Greek texts have a present participle ("being poured out") and an infinitive ("after supper"). The Latin masculine relative "qui" ("which") can refer to either "calix" ("cup") or "sanguine" ("blood").
- **22:26** — Vulgate pairs "major" ("greater/greatest") directly with "minor" ("lesser/least," which can also mean "younger"), corresponding to the Greek "neoteros" ("younger").
- **22:31** — Vulgate explicitly introduces a new speech with "Ait autem Dominus" ("And the Lord said"), which is omitted in critical Greek editions. Here "vos" ("you") is plural, shifting to singular "te" and "tu" ("you") addressed to Peter alone in verse 32.
- **22:36** — Vulgate leaves the object of "qui non habet" ("the one who has none") unexpressed and reads "tunicam" ("tunic") rather than "cloak." Jesus's speech continues directly into verse 37.
- **22:43** — Vulgate includes verses 43-44 in full, places "Et factus in agonia, prolixius orabat" in verse 43 rather than verse 44, and uses "prolixius" ("at greater length") where the Greek has "ektenesteron" ("more earnestly").
- **22:51** — Latin "Sinite usque huc" ("Allow it this far") is concise and can be understood either as telling the disciples to stop resisting or as asking the arresting party to permit Him to heal the wounded slave.
- **22:68** — Vulgate includes both "mihi" ("Me") and "neque dimittetis" ("nor will you release Me"), which are omitted in many critical Greek editions. Likewise, Vulgate places the council's question ("If You are the Christ, tell us") at the end of verse 66 rather than the beginning of verse 67.
