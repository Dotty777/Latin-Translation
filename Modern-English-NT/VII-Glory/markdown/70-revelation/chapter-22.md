## Text

**22:1** And he showed me a river of the water of life, shining like crystal, issuing from the throne of God and of the Lamb.

**22:2** In the middle of its street, and on either side of the river, was the tree of life, bearing twelve fruits month by month, yielding its fruit; and the leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations.

**22:3** And there will be no curse any longer, but the thrones of God and of the Lamb will be in it, and His servants will serve Him.

**22:4** And they will see His face, and His name will be on their foreheads.

**22:5** And there will be no more night, and they will not need the light of a lamp nor the light of the sun, because the Lord God will illuminate them, and they will reign forever and ever.

**22:6** And he said to me, "These words are most faithful and true. And the Lord God of the spirits of the prophets has sent His angel to show His servants the things that must happen soon.

**22:7** And look, I am coming swiftly. Blessed is the one who guards the words of the prophecy of this book."

**22:8** And I, John, am the one who heard and saw these things. And after I had heard and seen, I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel who was showing me these things.

**22:9** And he said to me, "See that you do not do it, for I am a fellow servant of yours, and of your brothers the prophets, and of those who keep the words of the prophecy of this book. Worship God."

**22:10** And he says to me, "Do not seal the words of the prophecy of this book, for the time is near.

**22:11** Let the one who does harm do harm still, and let the one who is in filth grow filthy still, and let the one who is righteous be justified still, and let the holy one be sanctified still.

**22:12** Look, I am coming soon, and My reward is with Me, to repay each one according to his works.

**22:13** I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end.

**22:14** Blessed are those who wash their robes in the blood of the Lamb, so that the right to the tree of life may be theirs and they may enter through the gates into the city.

**22:15** Outside are the dogs, and the sorcerers, and the sexually immoral, and the murderers, and those who serve idols, and everyone who loves and practices a lie.

**22:16** I, Jesus, have sent My angel to testify about these things to you in the churches. I am the root and the offspring of David, the shining morning star."

**22:17** And the Spirit and the bride say, "Come." And let the one who hears say, "Come." And let the one who thirsts come, and let the one who wishes receive the water of life freely.

**22:18** "For I solemnly testify to everyone who hears the words of the prophecy of this book: if anyone adds to these things, God will add upon him the plagues written in this book.

**22:19** And if anyone takes away from the words of the book of this prophecy, God will remove his share from the book of life, and from the holy city, and from the things that are written in this book,"

**22:20** says the One who bears witness to these things. "Yes, I am coming soon." Amen. Come, Lord Jesus.

**22:21** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you. Amen.

## Translator Notes

- **22:3** — The Clementine Vulgate uses the plural verb erunt with sedes ("the thrones of God and of the Lamb will be in it"), whereas the Greek has the singular ("the throne ... will be"), though the following pronouns ejus and illi ("His ... Him") remain singular.
- **22:11** — The Vulgate reads the passive justificetur ("be justified still"), reflecting a Greek variant, whereas most modern critical Greek texts read "still do righteousness."
- **22:14** — The Clementine Vulgate includes in sanguine Agni ("in the blood of the Lamb"), echoing Revelation 7:14, whereas Greek manuscripts either omit this phrase or read "do His commandments."
- **22:19** — The Vulgate reads de libro vitæ ("from the book of life") and includes et de his ("and from the things"), whereas the critical Greek text reads "from the tree of life" without the following "and from."
- **22:20** — Following the Clementine punctuation (a colon at the end of v. 19 and a period after istorum), dicit qui testimonium perhibet istorum concludes the warning of vv. 18-19, whereas Greek editions link this clause forward to introduce "Yes, I am coming soon."
