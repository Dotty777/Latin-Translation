## Text

**9:1** And the fifth angel sounded the trumpet, and I saw a star that had fallen from heaven onto the earth, and the key of the pit of the abyss was given to him.

**9:2** And he opened the pit of the abyss, and smoke of the pit ascended, like the smoke of a great furnace, and the sun and the air were darkened from the smoke of the pit.

**9:3** And from the smoke of the pit locusts came out onto the earth, and power was given to them, just as the scorpions of the earth have power.

**9:4** And it was commanded to them not to injure the grass of the earth, nor any green thing, nor any tree, except only the people who do not have the seal of God on their foreheads.

**9:5** And it was given to them not to kill them, but to torment them for five months; and their torment was like the torment of a scorpion when it strikes a person.

**9:6** And in those days people will seek death and will not find it; and they will long to die, and death will flee from them.

**9:7** And the likenesses of the locusts were like horses prepared for battle; and on their heads were things like crowns resembling gold, and their faces were like the faces of human beings.

**9:8** And they had hair like the hair of women. And their teeth were like the teeth of lions.

**9:9** And they had breastplates like iron breastplates, and the sound of their wings was like the sound of chariots of many horses running into war.

**9:10** And they had tails like those of scorpions, and stingers were in their tails, and their power was to harm people for five months.

**9:11** And they had over themselves as king the angel of the abyss, whose name in Hebrew is Abaddon, and in Greek Apollyon, having in Latin the name Exterminans.

**9:12** One woe has passed, and look, two woes are still coming after these things.

**9:13** And the sixth angel sounded the trumpet, and I heard one voice from the four horns of the golden altar that is before the eyes of God,

**9:14** saying to the sixth angel who had the trumpet, "Release the four angels who are bound in the great river Euphrates."

**9:15** And the four angels were released, who were prepared for the hour, and day, and month, and year, to kill a third of the people.

**9:16** And the number of the mounted army was twenty thousand times ten thousand. And I heard their number.

**9:17** And thus I saw the horses in the vision: and those who sat on them had fiery, and hyacinth, and sulfurous breastplates, and the heads of the horses were like the heads of lions; and from their mouths proceeds fire, and smoke, and sulfur.

**9:18** And by these three plagues a third of the people were killed, from the fire, and from the smoke, and the sulfur that were proceeding from their mouths.

**9:19** For the power of the horses is in their mouths and in their tails; for their tails are like serpents, having heads, and with these they do harm.

**9:20** And the rest of the people, who were not killed in these plagues, did not even repent of the works of their hands, so that they would not worship demons, and images of gold, and of silver, and of bronze, and of stone, and of wood, which can neither see, nor hear, nor walk;

**9:21** and they did not repent from their murders, nor from their sorceries, nor from their fornication, nor from their thefts.

## Translator Notes

- **9:3** — The Clementine Vulgate explicitly reads de fumo putei ('from the smoke of the pit'), specifying 'of the pit' where most Greek manuscripts read simply 'from the smoke.'
- **9:11** — The closing clause latine habens nomen Exterminans ('having in Latin the name Exterminans,' meaning 'Destroyer' or 'Exterminator') is a distinctive Vulgate addition absent from the Greek text.
- **9:13** — Vulgate ante oculos Dei ('before the eyes of God') is a distinctive reading where the Greek text reads simply 'before God.'
- **9:16** — Latin vicies millies dena millia literally means 'twenty thousand times ten thousand,' which equals two hundred million.
- **9:20** — Latin poenitentiam agere (here and in 9:21) is the standard Vulgate idiom for 'to repent,' literally meaning 'to perform repentance.'
