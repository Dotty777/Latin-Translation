## Text

**19:1** After these things I heard something like the voice of many crowds in heaven, saying, "Hallelujah! Salvation and glory and power belong to our God,

**19:2** because true and just are the judgments of Him who passed judgment on the great prostitute who corrupted the earth through her prostitution, and avenged the blood of His servants from her hands."

**19:3** And again they said, "Hallelujah!" And her smoke rises forever and ever.

**19:4** And the twenty-four elders and the four living beings fell down and worshiped God sitting on the throne, saying, "Amen! Hallelujah!"

**19:5** And a voice came out from the throne, saying, "Give praise to our God, all His servants, and you who fear Him, small and great."

**19:6** And I heard something like the voice of a great crowd, and like the sound of many waters, and like the sound of great thunders, saying, "Hallelujah! For the Lord our God, the Almighty, has reigned.

**19:7** Let us rejoice and exult, and let us give glory to Him, because the wedding of the Lamb has come, and His wife has prepared herself."

**19:8** And it was given to her to clothe herself with fine linen, shining and bright white; for fine linen is the just deeds of the saints.

**19:9** And he said to me, "Write: 'Blessed are those who are called to the wedding supper of the Lamb.'" And he said to me, "These words of God are true."

**19:10** And I fell before his feet to worship him. And he says to me, "See that you do not do that! I am a fellow servant of yours and of your brothers who have the testimony of Jesus. Worship God. For the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy."

**19:11** And I saw heaven opened, and look, a white horse, and He who sat on it was called Faithful and True, and with justice He judges and fights.

**19:12** Now His eyes were like a flame of fire, and on His head were many diadems, and He had a name written that no one knows except Himself.

**19:13** And He was clothed in a garment sprinkled with blood, and His name is called the Word of God.

**19:14** And the armies that are in heaven were following Him on white horses, clothed in fine linen, white and clean.

**19:15** And from His mouth proceeds a sword sharp on both sides, so that with it He may strike the nations. And He Himself will rule them with an iron rod; and He Himself treads the winepress of the wine of the fury of the wrath of God Almighty.

**19:16** And on His garment and on His thigh He has written, "King of kings and Lord of those who rule."

**19:17** And I saw one angel standing in the sun, and he cried out with a loud voice, saying to all the birds that were flying through the middle of heaven, "Come and gather together for the great supper of God,

**19:18** so that you may eat the flesh of kings, and the flesh of tribunes, and the flesh of the mighty, and the flesh of horses and of those who sit on them, and the flesh of all, free and slave, both small and great."

**19:19** And I saw the beast and the kings of the earth and their armies gathered together to wage battle against Him who sat on the horse and against His army.

**19:20** And the beast was seized, and with it the false prophet who performed signs in its presence, by which he led astray those who received the mark of the beast and those who worshiped its image. These two were thrown alive into the lake of fire burning with sulfur.

**19:21** And the rest were killed by the sword of Him who sat on the horse, which proceeds from His mouth; and all the birds were filled with their flesh.

## Translator Notes

- **19:1** — The Clementine Vulgate reads the plural "turbarum multarum" ("of many crowds"), whereas Greek manuscripts have the singular "a great crowd."
- **19:2** — The Vulgate uses the relative pronoun "qui" ("of Him who passed judgment") rather than the causal conjunction "because" found in the Greek, and has the plural "manibus" ("hands") rather than singular "hand."
- **19:13** — The Vulgate reads "aspersa sanguine" ("sprinkled with blood"), reflecting the Latin textual tradition rather than the Greek majority reading "dipped in blood."
- **19:15** — The Vulgate specifies "ex utraque parte acutus" ("sharp on both sides") where critical Greek texts have simply "sharp," and renders Greek "poimanei" ("shepherd") as "reget" ("will rule").
- **19:16** — The Vulgate omits the word "name" before "scriptum" ("written") and translates Greek "Lord of lords" with the participle "Dominus dominantium" ("Lord of those who rule").
