## Text

**21:1** And I saw a new heaven and a new earth. For the first heaven and the first earth passed away, and the sea no longer exists.

**21:2** And I, John, saw the holy city, the new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God, prepared like a bride adorned for her husband.

**21:3** And I heard a loud voice from the throne, saying, "Look, the tabernacle of God is with people, and He will dwell with them. And they themselves will be His people, and God Himself will be with them, their God,

**21:4** and God will wipe away every tear from their eyes; and death will be no more, nor will mourning, nor outcry, nor pain exist any longer, because the first things have passed away."

**21:5** And He who sat on the throne said, "Look, I am making all things new." And He said to me, "Write, because these words are most faithful and true."

**21:6** And He said to me, "It is done! I am the Alpha and the Omega, the beginning and the end. To the one who is thirsty I will give from the spring of the water of life, freely.

**21:7** The one who conquers will possess these things, and I will be God to him, and he will be a son to Me.

**21:8** But as for the cowardly, and the unbelieving, and the abominable, and murderers, and fornicators, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all liars, their portion will be in the lake burning with fire and sulfur, which is the second death."

**21:9** And one of the seven angels who had the bowls full of the seven last plagues came and spoke with me, saying, "Come, and I will show you the bride, the wife of the Lamb."

**21:10** And he carried me up in spirit to a great and high mountain, and showed me the holy city Jerusalem coming down out of heaven from God,

**21:11** having the radiance of God; and its light was like a precious stone, like a jasper stone, like crystal.

**21:12** And it had a great and high wall, having twelve gates, and at the gates twelve angels, and names inscribed, which are the names of the twelve tribes of the sons of Israel:

**21:13** on the east three gates, and on the north three gates, and on the south three gates, and on the west three gates.

**21:14** And the wall of the city had twelve foundations, and on them were the twelve names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.

**21:15** And the one who was speaking with me had a golden measuring reed to measure the city, and its gates, and the wall.

**21:16** And the city is laid out as a square, and its length is as great as its width; and he measured the city with the golden reed at twelve thousand stadia, and its length and height and width are equal.

**21:17** And he measured its wall: one hundred forty-four cubits, by human measurement, which is that of an angel.

**21:18** And the structure of its wall was of jasper stone, while the city itself was pure gold, like pure glass.

**21:19** And the foundations of the wall of the city were adorned with every precious stone. The first foundation was jasper; the second, sapphire; the third, chalcedony; the fourth, emerald;

**21:20** the fifth, sardonyx; the sixth, sardius; the seventh, chrysolite; the eighth, beryl; the ninth, topaz; the tenth, chrysoprase; the eleventh, hyacinth; the twelfth, amethyst.

**21:21** And the twelve gates are twelve pearls, one for each; and each single gate was made of a single pearl. And the street of the city was pure gold, like transparent glass.

**21:22** And I saw no temple in it, for the Lord God Almighty is its temple, and the Lamb.

**21:23** And the city has no need of the sun or the moon to shine in it, for the radiance of God has illuminated it, and its lamp is the Lamb.

**21:24** And the nations will walk in its light, and the kings of the earth will bring their glory and honor into it.

**21:25** And its gates will not be closed by day, for there will be no night there.

**21:26** And they will bring the glory and the honor of the nations into it.

**21:27** Nothing defiled will enter into it, nor anyone who practices abomination and falsehood, except those who are written in the book of life of the Lamb.

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — The Vulgate includes "Et ego Joannes" ("And I, John"), a reading absent from most modern critical Greek editions.
- **21:5** — Latin "fidelissima" uses the superlative ("most faithful") where the Greek has the positive adjective ("trustworthy/faithful").
- **21:7** — Latin "possidebit" literally means "will possess" rather than "will inherit" (Greek "kleronomesei").
- **21:11** — Here and in 21:23 the Vulgate uses "claritas" ("radiance") rather than "gloria" ("glory," used in 21:24, 26), and renders the final comparison simply as "sicut crystallum" ("like crystal").
- **21:16** — The Vulgate specifies "aurea" ("golden") again for the reed and lists the equal dimensions in the order "length and height and width"; twelve thousand stadia is roughly 1,400 miles (2,200 kilometers).
