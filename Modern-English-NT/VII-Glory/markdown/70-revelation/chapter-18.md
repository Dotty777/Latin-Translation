## Text

**18:1** And after these things I saw another angel descending from heaven, having great authority, and the earth was illuminated by his glory.

**18:2** And he cried out with strength, saying, "Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great, and she has become a dwelling of demons, and a place of confinement for every unclean spirit, and a place of confinement for every unclean and detestable bird,

**18:3** because all nations have drunk from the wine of the wrath of her fornication, and the kings of the earth have fornicated with her, and the merchants of the earth have become rich from the power of her luxury."

**18:4** And I heard another voice from heaven saying, "Come out from her, My people, so that you do not share in her offenses, and do not receive any of her plagues.

**18:5** For her sins have reached as far as heaven, and the Lord has remembered her iniquities.

**18:6** Repay her just as she herself has repaid you, and pay back double according to her deeds; in the cup in which she mixed, mix double for her.

**18:7** As much as she glorified herself and was in luxury, just as much give her torment and mourning, because in her heart she says, 'I sit as a queen, and I am not a widow, and I will not see mourning.'

**18:8** Therefore in one day her plagues will come: death, and mourning, and famine, and she will be burned up with fire, because mighty is God, who will judge her."

**18:9** And the kings of the earth, who fornicated with her and lived in luxury, will weep and lament over her when they see the smoke of her burning,

**18:10** standing far off because of fear of her torments, saying, "Woe, woe, that great city Babylon, that mighty city, because in one hour your judgment has come."

**18:11** And the traders of the earth will weep and mourn over her, because no one will buy their merchandise any longer:

**18:12** merchandise of gold, and silver, and precious stone, and pearl, and fine linen, and purple, and silk, and scarlet, and all thyine wood, and all vessels of ivory, and all vessels of precious stone, and of bronze, and of iron, and of marble;

**18:13** and cinnamon, and spices, and ointment, and frankincense, and wine, and oil, and fine flour, and wheat, and cattle, and sheep, and horses, and carriages, and slaves, and the souls of people.

**18:14** And the fruits of the desire of your soul have departed from you, and all rich and splendid things have perished from you, and they will not find them any longer.

**18:15** The merchants of these things, who became rich, will stand far off from her because of fear of her torments, weeping and mourning,

**18:16** and saying, "Woe, woe, that great city, which was dressed in fine linen, and purple, and scarlet, and was gilded with gold, and precious stone, and pearls,

**18:17** because in one hour such great riches have been laid waste." And every helmsman, and everyone who sails on a lake, and sailors, and those who work on the sea, stood far off,

**18:18** and cried out, seeing the place of her burning, saying, "What is like this great city?"

**18:19** And they threw dust upon their heads, and cried out, weeping and mourning, saying, "Woe, woe, that great city, in which all who had ships on the sea became rich from her prices, because in one hour she has been made desolate."

**18:20** Rejoice over her, heaven, and holy apostles and prophets, because God has judged your judgment on her.

**18:21** And one mighty angel lifted up a stone like a great millstone and hurled it into the sea, saying, "With this force Babylon, that great city, will be hurled, and will no longer be found.

**18:22** And the sound of harpists, and of musicians, and of those playing the flute and the trumpet will not be heard in you any longer; and no craftsman of any craft will be found in you any longer; and the sound of a millstone will not be heard in you any longer;

**18:23** and the light of a lamp will not shine in you any longer; and the voice of bridegroom and bride will not be heard in you anymore; because your merchants were the princes of the earth, because in your sorceries all nations went astray.

**18:24** And in her was found the blood of prophets and of saints, and of all who have been killed on the earth."

## Translator Notes

- **18:5** — The Vulgate reads 'Dominus' ('the Lord') where Greek manuscripts read 'God,' and 'pervenerunt' ('have reached') where critical Greek texts read 'have been heaped up.'
- **18:8** — The Vulgate reads simply 'Deus' ('God') with the future tense 'judicabit' ('will judge'), whereas Greek manuscripts read 'the Lord God' with a past participle ('who has judged').
- **18:12** — The Vulgate reads 'de lapide pretioso' ('of precious stone'), whereas Greek manuscripts read 'of most costly wood.'
- **18:17** — The Vulgate reads 'in lacum navigat' ('sails on a lake'), a distinctive Latin reading contrasting with the Greek 'sails to a place' (i.e., voyagers or passengers).
- **18:18** — The Vulgate reads 'locum incendii ejus' ('the place of her burning'), contrasting with the Greek reading 'the smoke of her burning.'
