## Text

**17:1** And one of the seven angels who had the seven bowls came and spoke with me, saying, "Come, I will show you the condemnation of the great prostitute who sits upon many waters,

**17:2** with whom the kings of the earth fornicated, and those who inhabit the earth became drunk from the wine of her prostitution."

**17:3** And he carried me away in spirit into a desert. And I saw a woman sitting upon a scarlet beast, full of names of blasphemy, having seven heads and ten horns.

**17:4** And the woman was clothed in purple and scarlet, and gilded with gold and precious stone and pearls, having a golden cup in her hand, full of abomination and the uncleanness of her fornication.

**17:5** And upon her forehead was written a name: "Mystery: Babylon the great, mother of fornications and of the abominations of the earth."

**17:6** And I saw the woman drunk from the blood of the saints and from the blood of the martyrs of Jesus. And when I saw her, I marveled with great amazement.

**17:7** And the angel said to me, "Why do you marvel? I will tell you the mystery of the woman, and of the beast that carries her, which has seven heads and ten horns.

**17:8** The beast that you saw was, and is not, and is about to ascend from the abyss, and will go into destruction; and those who inhabit the earth (whose names are not written in the book of life from the foundation of the world) will marvel, seeing the beast, which was and is not.

**17:9** And here is the mind that has wisdom. The seven heads are seven mountains on which the woman sits, and they are seven kings.

**17:10** Five have fallen, one is, and the other has not yet come; and when he comes, he must remain a short time.

**17:11** And the beast that was, and is not, it itself is also the eighth, and is of the seven, and goes into destruction.

**17:12** And the ten horns that you saw are ten kings, who have not yet received a kingdom, but they will receive authority as kings for one hour after the beast.

**17:13** These have one purpose, and they will hand over their power and authority to the beast.

**17:14** These will fight against the Lamb, and the Lamb will conquer them, because He is Lord of lords and King of kings, and those who are with Him are called, chosen, and faithful."

**17:15** And he said to me, "The waters that you saw, where the prostitute sits, are peoples, and nations, and languages.

**17:16** And the ten horns that you saw on the beast, these will hate the prostitute, and will make her desolate and naked, and will eat her flesh, and will burn her up with fire.

**17:17** For God has put into their hearts to do what is pleasing to Him, to give their kingdom to the beast, until the words of God are fulfilled.

**17:18** And the woman whom you saw is the great city that holds kingship over the kings of the earth."

## Translator Notes

- **17:7** — Latin 'sacramentum' here renders Greek 'mysterion' (shifting from 'mysterium' in 17:5) in its biblical Latin sense of a sacred mystery or hidden meaning.
- **17:8** — The Clementine Vulgate reads 'quæ erat, et non est' ('which was and is not'), omitting the third clause 'and is to come' found in Greek manuscripts.
- **17:12** — The Vulgate reads 'post bestiam' ('after the beast') with the future tense 'accipient' ('will receive'), whereas the Greek text reads 'with the beast' in the present tense.
- **17:16** — The Vulgate reads 'in bestia' ('on the beast'), making only the ten horns the subject of the hatred, whereas critical Greek texts read 'and the beast' as a joint subject.
- **17:17** — The Vulgate reads 'quod placitum est illi' ('what is pleasing to Him') and omits the Greek clause 'and to act with one purpose' before 'to give their kingdom to the beast.'
