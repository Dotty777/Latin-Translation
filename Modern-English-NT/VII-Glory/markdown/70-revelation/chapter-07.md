## Text

**7:1** After these things I saw four angels standing on the four corners of the earth, holding the four winds of the earth so that they would not blow on the earth, nor on the sea, nor against any tree.

**7:2** And I saw another angel ascending from the rising of the sun, having the seal of the living God; and he cried out with a loud voice to the four angels to whom it was given to harm the earth and the sea,

**7:3** saying, "Do not harm the earth and the sea, nor the trees, until we seal the servants of our God on their foreheads."

**7:4** And I heard the number of those sealed: one hundred forty-four thousand who were sealed, from every tribe of the sons of Israel.

**7:5** From the tribe of Judah, twelve thousand sealed; from the tribe of Reuben, twelve thousand sealed; from the tribe of Gad, twelve thousand sealed;

**7:6** from the tribe of Asher, twelve thousand sealed; from the tribe of Naphtali, twelve thousand sealed; from the tribe of Manasseh, twelve thousand sealed;

**7:7** from the tribe of Simeon, twelve thousand sealed; from the tribe of Levi, twelve thousand sealed; from the tribe of Issachar, twelve thousand sealed;

**7:8** from the tribe of Zebulun, twelve thousand sealed; from the tribe of Joseph, twelve thousand sealed; from the tribe of Benjamin, twelve thousand sealed.

**7:9** After these things I saw a great crowd, which no one could count, from all nations, and tribes, and peoples, and languages, standing before the throne and in the sight of the Lamb, clothed in white robes, and palm branches were in their hands;

**7:10** and they were crying out with a loud voice, saying, "Salvation to our God, who sits on the throne, and to the Lamb."

**7:11** And all the angels were standing around the throne, and the elders, and the four living beings; and they fell on their faces in the sight of the throne and worshiped God,

**7:12** saying, "Amen. Blessing, and splendor, and wisdom, and thanksgiving, honor, and might, and strength to our God forever and ever. Amen."

**7:13** And one of the elders answered and said to me, "These who are clothed in white robes, who are they? And where have they come from?"

**7:14** And I said to him, "My lord, you know." And he said to me, "These are the ones who have come out of the great tribulation, and they have washed their robes and whitened them in the blood of the Lamb.

**7:15** Therefore they are before the throne of God, and they serve Him day and night in His temple; and He who sits on the throne will dwell over them.

**7:16** They will not hunger, nor thirst any longer, nor will the sun fall upon them, nor any scorching heat,

**7:17** because the Lamb, who is in the center of the throne, will guide them and lead them to the springs of the waters of life, and God will wipe away every tear from their eyes."

## Translator Notes

- **7:5** — Unlike many Greek manuscripts that include 'sealed' only on the first and last tribes, the Clementine Vulgate repeats signati ('sealed') for all twelve tribes in 7:5-8.
- **7:15** — Vulgate habitabit super illos literally reads 'will dwell over them,' where the Greek text uses a verb meaning 'will spread His tent/tabernacle over them.'
- **7:17** — Vulgate reget ('will guide/rule') corresponds to Greek poimanei ('will shepherd'), and vitae fontes aquarum preserves the plural 'waters' ('springs of the waters of life').
