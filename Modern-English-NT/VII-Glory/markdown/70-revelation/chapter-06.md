## Text

**6:1** And I saw that the Lamb had opened one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four living beings saying, as with a voice of thunder, "Come and see."

**6:2** And I looked, and see, a white horse; and the one who sat on it had a bow, and a crown was given to him, and he went out conquering so that he might conquer.

**6:3** And when He had opened the second seal, I heard the second living being saying, "Come and see."

**6:4** And another horse, a red one, went out; and to the one who sat on it, it was given to take peace from the earth and that they should kill one another, and a great sword was given to him.

**6:5** And when He had opened the third seal, I heard the third living being saying, "Come and see." And look, a black horse; and the one who sat on it had a pair of scales in his hand.

**6:6** And I heard something like a voice in the midst of the four living beings saying, "A two-pound measure of wheat for a denarius, and three two-pound measures of barley for a denarius; and do not harm the wine and the oil."

**6:7** And when He had opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living being saying, "Come and see."

**6:8** And look, a pale horse; and the one who sat on it, his name was Death, and the underworld was following him; and authority was given to him over the four parts of the earth, to kill with the sword, with famine, and with death, and by the beasts of the earth.

**6:9** And when He had opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of those who had been killed because of the word of God and because of the testimony that they had.

**6:10** And they were crying out with a loud voice, saying, "How long, Lord, holy and true, do You not judge and avenge our blood on those who dwell on the earth?"

**6:11** And a white robe was given to each of them, and it was told to them that they should rest a little while longer, until their fellow servants and their brothers, who are to be killed just as they also were, are made complete.

**6:12** And I looked when He had opened the sixth seal, and see, a great earthquake occurred, and the sun became black like sackcloth of hair, and the whole moon became like blood.

**6:13** And the stars from the sky fell onto the earth, just as a fig tree casts off its unripe figs when it is shaken by a great wind.

**6:14** And the sky receded like a scroll rolled up, and every mountain and the islands were moved from their places.

**6:15** And the kings of the earth, and the princes, and the tribunes, and the rich, and the strong, and every slave and free person hid themselves in the caves and in the rocks of the mountains.

**6:16** And they say to the mountains and to the rocks, "Fall on us and hide us from the face of Him who sits on the throne and from the wrath of the Lamb,

**6:17** because the great day of Their wrath has come, and who will be able to stand?"

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The Vulgate reads "that the Lamb had opened" (quod aperuisset) rather than "when," and includes "and see" (et vide) here and in vv. 3, 5, and 7, which critical Greek editions omit.
- **6:6** — Latin bilibris is literally a two-pound measure (rendering Greek choinix), and a denarius was a silver coin roughly equal to a day's wage; note also the Vulgate order "the wine and the oil" (Greek: "the oil and the wine").
- **6:8** — Latin infernus ("hell") denotes the underworld or realm of the dead (Greek Hades). Notably, the Clementine Vulgate reads "over the four parts of the earth" (quatuor partes) and singular "to him" (illi), whereas the Greek reads "over a fourth of the earth" and (in critical editions) plural "to them."
- **6:17** — The Vulgate reads plural "Their wrath" (irae ipsorum), referring jointly to God and the Lamb, whereas critical Greek editions read singular "His wrath."
