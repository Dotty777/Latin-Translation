## Text

**2:1** "To the angel of the church of Ephesus write: These things says the One who holds the seven stars in His right hand, who walks in the midst of the seven golden lampstands:

**2:2** I know your works, and labor, and your endurance, and that you cannot bear evil people, and you have tested those who say that they are apostles and are not, and have found them to be liars;

**2:3** and you have endurance, and have borne up for the sake of My name, and have not grown faint.

**2:4** But I have this against you, that you have abandoned your first love.

**2:5** Remember therefore from where you have fallen, and repent, and do the first works; but if not, I am coming to you, and I will move your lampstand from its place, unless you repent.

**2:6** But you have this, that you hate the deeds of the Nicolaitans, which I also hate.

**2:7** Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches: To the one who conquers, I will grant to eat from the tree of life, which is in the paradise of My God.

**2:8** And to the angel of the church of Smyrna write: These things says the first and the last, who was dead and lives:

**2:9** I know your tribulation and your poverty, yet you are rich; and you are slandered by those who say that they are Jews and are not, but are a synagogue of Satan.

**2:10** Fear none of these things that you are about to suffer. Look, the devil is about to cast some of you into prison so that you may be tested, and you will have tribulation for ten days. Be faithful even to death, and I will give you the crown of life.

**2:11** Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches: The one who conquers will not be harmed by the second death.

**2:12** And to the angel of the church of Pergamum write: These things says the One who has the broadsword sharp on both sides:

**2:13** I know where you dwell, where the seat of Satan is; and you hold fast to My name, and did not deny My faith. And in those days Antipas was My faithful witness, who was killed among you, where Satan dwells.

**2:14** But I have a few things against you: because you have there those who hold the teaching of Balaam, who taught Balak to put a stumbling block before the sons of Israel, to eat and to commit fornication;

**2:15** so you also have those who hold the teaching of the Nicolaitans.

**2:16** Likewise repent; otherwise I will come to you soon, and I will fight against them with the sword of My mouth.

**2:17** Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches: To the one who conquers, I will give the hidden manna, and I will give him a white stone, and on the stone a new name written, which no one knows except the one who receives it.

**2:18** And to the angel of the church of Thyatira write: These things says the Son of God, who has eyes like a flame of fire, and His feet are like fine brass:

**2:19** I know your works, and faith, and your love, and service, and your endurance, and that your last works are more than the first.

**2:20** But I have a few things against you: because you allow the woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess, to teach and to lead My servants astray, to commit fornication and to eat of things sacrificed to idols.

**2:21** And I gave her time to repent, and she is unwilling to repent of her fornication.

**2:22** Look, I will cast her onto a bed, and those who commit adultery with her will be in the greatest tribulation, unless they repent of their works.

**2:23** And I will kill her children with death, and all the churches will know that I am the One who searches the inward parts and hearts, and I will give to each one of you according to his works. Now to you I say,

**2:24** and to the rest who are in Thyatira, all who do not have this teaching, and who have not known the depths of Satan, as they say: I will not lay upon you any other burden;

**2:25** nevertheless, hold fast to what you have until I come.

**2:26** And the one who conquers and keeps My works until the end, to him I will give authority over the nations,

**2:27** and he will rule them with an iron rod, and like a potter's vessel they will be shattered,

**2:28** just as I also have received from My Father; and I will give him the morning star.

**2:29** Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches."

## Translator Notes

- **2:5** — Latin age poenitentiam (literally "do penance" or "exercise repentance") is the standard Vulgate idiom for repenting, paired with the verb poenitere in 2:21.
- **2:7** — The Vulgate reads "of My God" (Dei mei) rather than simply "of God" as in most modern critical Greek texts.
- **2:13** — The Vulgate clause Et in diebus illis Antipas testis meus fidelis is elliptical and lacks a main verb before the relative clause; "was" is supplied to complete the sentence.
- **2:14** — The Vulgate reads simply "to eat and to commit fornication" (edere, et fornicari), omitting "things sacrificed to idols," which appears in the Greek text and in 2:20.
- **2:22** — The Vulgate reflexive pronoun suis ("their works") refers to those who commit adultery with Jezebel, whereas the Greek text reads "her works" (autes).
