## Text

**1:1** The revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave Him to make plain to His servants, the things that must take place soon; and He signified it, sending through His angel to His servant John,

**1:2** who bore witness to the word of God, and to the testimony of Jesus Christ, whatever he saw.

**1:3** Blessed is the one who reads and hears the words of this prophecy, and keeps the things that are written in it, for the time is near.

**1:4** John to the seven churches that are in Asia. Grace to you and peace from Him who is, and who was, and who is to come, and from the seven spirits who are in the presence of His throne,

**1:5** and from Jesus Christ, who is the faithful witness, the firstborn of the dead, and the prince of the kings of the earth, who loved us and washed us from our sins in His blood,

**1:6** and made us a kingdom and priests to God and His Father: to Him be glory and dominion forever and ever. Amen.

**1:7** Look, He is coming with the clouds, and every eye will see Him, and those who pierced Him. And all the tribes of the earth will lament over Him. Yes, amen.

**1:8** "I am the Alpha and the Omega, the beginning and the end," says the Lord God, who is, and who was, and who is to come, the Almighty.

**1:9** I, John, your brother and sharer in the tribulation and kingdom and endurance in Christ Jesus, was on the island that is called Patmos on account of the word of God and the testimony of Jesus.

**1:10** I was in spirit on the Lord's day, and I heard behind me a loud voice like that of a trumpet,

**1:11** saying, "Write in a book what you see, and send it to the seven churches that are in Asia: to Ephesus, and to Smyrna, and to Pergamum, and to Thyatira, and to Sardis, and to Philadelphia, and to Laodicea."

**1:12** And I turned to see the voice that was speaking with me; and having turned, I saw seven golden lampstands,

**1:13** and in the midst of the seven golden lampstands, one like a Son of Man, clothed in a full-length robe and belted across the chest with a golden sash.

**1:14** Now His head and hair were bright white like white wool, and like snow, and His eyes were like a flame of fire;

**1:15** and His feet were like fine brass, as in a burning furnace, and His voice was like the sound of many waters;

**1:16** and He had seven stars in His right hand, and from His mouth came a sword sharp on both sides, and His face was as the sun shines in its strength.

**1:17** And when I saw Him, I fell at His feet as one dead. And He placed His right hand on me, saying, "Do not be afraid; I am the first and the last,

**1:18** and the living One; and I was dead, and look, I am living forever and ever, and I have the keys of death and of the underworld.

**1:19** Write therefore what you have seen, and the things that are, and the things that must take place after these.

**1:20** The mystery of the seven stars that you saw in My right hand, and the seven golden lampstands: the seven stars are the angels of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands are the seven churches."

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — The Vulgate uses singular verbs ("reads and hears ... and keeps") rather than switching to the plural ("those who hear ... and keep") as in the Greek text.
- **1:5** — The Vulgate reads "loved" (perfect tense, dilexit) and "washed us" (lavit nos, corresponding to the Greek variant lousanti) rather than "loves" and "freed us" (lysanti).
- **1:8** — The Vulgate includes "the beginning and the end" (principium et finis), a clause omitted in most modern critical Greek editions.
- **1:15** — Latin auricalco denotes a gleaming gold-copper alloy or fine brass; the Vulgate also reads simply "as in a burning furnace" (sicut in camino ardenti) without the Greek participle "refined."
- **1:18** — Latin inferni ("of the underworld," rendering Greek Hades) denotes the realm of the dead, rendered consistently as "the underworld" across Revelation (1:18, 6:8, 20:13-14) and Acts 2:24, 27, 31.
- **1:20** — Latin sacramentum ("sacrament" or "sacred mystery") renders Greek mysterion and here signifies the hidden symbolic meaning of the seven stars and lampstands.
