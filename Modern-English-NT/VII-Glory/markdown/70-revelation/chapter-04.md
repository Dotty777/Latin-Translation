## Text

**4:1** After these things I looked, and see, an open door in heaven, and the first voice, which I heard like that of a trumpet speaking with me, saying, "Come up here, and I will show you the things that must take place after these things."

**4:2** And immediately I was in the Spirit, and look, a throne was set in heaven, and One seated upon the throne.

**4:3** And He who was seated was like the appearance of a jasper stone and a sardius, and a rainbow was around the throne, like the appearance of an emerald.

**4:4** And around the throne were twenty-four seats, and on the thrones twenty-four elders seated, robed in white garments, and on their heads were golden crowns.

**4:5** And from the throne were proceeding flashes of lightning, and voices, and peals of thunder; and seven lamps were burning before the throne, which are the seven spirits of God.

**4:6** And in front of the throne was something like a sea of glass, resembling crystal; and in the midst of the throne and around the throne were four living beings, full of eyes in front and behind.

**4:7** And the first living being was like a lion, and the second living being was like a calf, and the third living being had a face like that of a man, and the fourth living being was like a flying eagle.

**4:8** And the four living beings, each one of them had six wings, and all around and within they are full of eyes; and they had no rest day and night, saying, "Holy, Holy, Holy, the Lord God Almighty, who was, and who is, and who is to come."

**4:9** And whenever those living beings gave glory, and honor, and blessing to Him who sits on the throne, who lives forever and ever,

**4:10** the twenty-four elders fell down before Him who sits on the throne, and worshiped Him who lives forever and ever, and cast their crowns before the throne, saying,

**4:11** "Worthy are You, Lord our God, to receive glory, and honor, and power, because You created all things, and because of Your will they existed and were created."

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The Vulgate varies the Latin terminology here between sedes ("throne"), sedilia ("seats"), and thronos ("thrones"), whereas the Greek repeats thronos throughout.
- **4:6** — Latin animalia literally denotes "living beings" (creatures endowed with life, anima), rendering Greek zoa ("living creatures").
- **4:9** — Vulgate-distinct reading: "blessing" (benedictionem), whereas the Greek text reads "thanksgiving" (eucharistian).
- **4:11** — Latin Domine Deus noster reads literally "Lord our God," without a conjunction between "Lord" and "God" (contrast Greek "our Lord and our God").
