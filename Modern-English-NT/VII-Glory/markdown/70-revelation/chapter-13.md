## Text

**13:1** And I saw a beast rising out of the sea, having seven heads and ten horns, and on its horns ten diadems, and on its heads names of blasphemy.

**13:2** And the beast that I saw was like a leopard, and its feet were like the feet of a bear, and its mouth like the mouth of a lion. And the dragon gave to it his power and great authority.

**13:3** And I saw one of its heads as if killed to death, and the wound of its death was healed. And the whole earth marveled after the beast.

**13:4** And they worshiped the dragon, who gave authority to the beast, and they worshiped the beast, saying, "Who is like the beast? And who will be able to fight against it?"

**13:5** And a mouth was given to it, speaking great things and blasphemies, and authority was given to it to act for forty-two months.

**13:6** And it opened its mouth in blasphemies against God, to blaspheme His name and His tabernacle and those who dwell in heaven.

**13:7** And it was granted to it to make war against the saints and to conquer them. And authority was given to it over every tribe and people and language and nation.

**13:8** And all who dwell on the earth worshiped it, whose names have not been written in the book of life of the Lamb, who was killed from the beginning of the world.

**13:9** If anyone has an ear, let him hear.

**13:10** Whoever leads into captivity will go into captivity; whoever kills with the sword must be killed by the sword. Here is the endurance and faith of the saints.

**13:11** And I saw another beast rising out of the earth, and it had two horns like those of a lamb, and it spoke like a dragon.

**13:12** And it exercised all the authority of the former beast in its sight, and it made the earth and those who dwell in it worship the first beast, whose wound of death was healed.

**13:13** And it performed great signs, so that it even made fire descend from heaven to the earth in the sight of people.

**13:14** And it deceived those who dwell on the earth because of the signs that were given to it to perform in the sight of the beast, telling those who dwell on the earth to make an image of the beast that has the wound of the sword and lived.

**13:15** And it was granted to it to give breath to the image of the beast, and so that the image of the beast might speak and cause whoever would not worship the image of the beast to be killed.

**13:16** And it will cause all, small and great, and rich and poor, and free and slave, to have a mark on their right hand or on their foreheads,

**13:17** and so that no one may be able to buy or sell except the one who has the mark, or the name of the beast, or the number of its name.

**13:18** Here is wisdom. Let the one who has understanding calculate the number of the beast, for it is the number of a man, and its number is six hundred sixty-six.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — The Clementine Vulgate reads 'his power and great authority' (virtutem suam, et potestatem magnam), omitting 'and his throne' found in the Greek text.
- **13:8** — The Vulgate uses the past tense adoraverunt ('worshiped') where the Greek has a future tense, and unambiguously links ab origine mundi ('from the beginning of the world') to the Lamb who was killed.
- **13:10** — The Vulgate reads Qui in captivitatem duxerit ('Whoever leads into captivity'), matching the parallel retribution of the sword clause, whereas modern critical Greek editions read 'If anyone is destined for captivity.'
- **13:16** — The Vulgate uses the future tense faciet ('will cause') and habere caracterem ('to have a mark'), where the Greek uses the present tense and 'that they be given a mark.'
