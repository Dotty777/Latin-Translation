## Text

**5:1** And I saw in the right hand of Him who sits on the throne a scroll written inside and outside, sealed with seven seals.

**5:2** And I saw a strong angel proclaiming with a loud voice, "Who is worthy to open the scroll and break its seals?"

**5:3** And no one in heaven, or on earth, or under the earth, was able to open the scroll or to look at it.

**5:4** And I was weeping greatly, because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or to see it.

**5:5** And one of the elders said to me, "Do not weep; look, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has conquered so as to open the scroll and break its seven seals."

**5:6** And I saw, and look, in the midst of the throne and of the four living beings, and in the midst of the elders, a Lamb standing as though killed, having seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven spirits of God, sent into all the earth.

**5:7** And He came and took the scroll from the right hand of Him who sits on the throne.

**5:8** And when He had opened the scroll, the four living beings and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb, each one having a harp and golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of the saints.

**5:9** And they were singing a new song, saying, "Worthy are You, Lord, to take the scroll and to open its seals, because You were killed, and You redeemed us to God by Your blood from every tribe, and language, and people, and nation,

**5:10** and You made us a kingdom and priests to our God, and we will reign on the earth."

**5:11** And I looked, and I heard the voice of many angels around the throne, and the living beings, and the elders, and their number was thousands of thousands,

**5:12** saying with a loud voice, "Worthy is the Lamb who was killed to receive power, and divinity, and wisdom, and strength, and honor, and glory, and blessing."

**5:13** And every creature that is in heaven, and on the earth, and under the earth, and those that are in the sea, and those in it, I heard them all saying, "To Him who sits on the throne and to the Lamb be blessing, and honor, and glory, and authority forever and ever."

**5:14** And the four living beings were saying, "Amen." And the twenty-four elders fell down on their faces and worshiped Him who lives forever and ever.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Latin liber denotes an ancient book-roll or scroll, sealed along its margin and rolled up (cf. 6:14).
- **5:8** — Vulgate-distinct reading: "when He had opened the scroll" (aperuisset), whereas the Greek text reads "when He had taken the scroll" (elaben).
- **5:9** — Notable Vulgate readings in vv. 9-10: the Latin includes "Lord" (Domine), has "open" (aperire) rather than "break/loose" its seals, and reads first-person "us ... we will reign" (nos ... regnabimus) where critical Greek texts omit "us" in v. 9 and read third-person "them ... they will reign" in v. 10.
- **5:11** — Vulgate-distinct reading: "thousands of thousands" (millia millium), omitting the preceding "myriads of myriads" present in the Greek text.
- **5:12** — Notable Vulgate-distinct reading: "divinity" (divinitatem), where the Greek text reads "wealth/riches" (plouton, Latin divitias).
