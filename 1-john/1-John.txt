1 JOHN — LATIN–ENGLISH (CLEMENTINE VULGATE)
Captured from the Saints.Bible staging Parallel Reader, 1 November 2026.
Verse numbers follow the Saints (Greek/Hebrew) column; 'vulgate_reference' in verses.json gives the Vulgate's own number where it differs.

1 JOHN 1

1:1  LA: Quod fuit ab initio, quod audivimus, quod vidimus oculis nostris, quod perspeximus, et manus nostræ contrectaverunt de verbo vitæ:
     EN: What was from the beginning, what we have heard, what we have seen with our eyes, what we have looked upon and our hands have touched, concerning the Word of life—

1:2  LA: et vita manifestata est, et vidimus, et testamur, et annuntiamus vobis vitam æternam, quæ erat apud Patrem, et apparuit nobis:
     EN: and the life was made manifest, and we have seen and bear witness and announce to you the eternal life that was with the Father and appeared to us—

1:3  LA: quod vidimus et audivimus, annuntiamus vobis, ut et vos societatem habeatis nobiscum, et societas nostra sit cum Patre, et cum Filio ejus Jesu Christo.
     EN: what we have seen and heard we announce to you also, so that you too may have fellowship with us, and our fellowship may be with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ.

1:4  LA: Et hæc scribimus vobis ut gaudeatis, et gaudium vestrum sit plenum.
     EN: We write these things to you so that you may rejoice and your joy may be complete.

1:5  LA: Et hæc est annuntiatio, quam audivimus ab eo, et annuntiamus vobis: quoniam Deus lux est, et tenebræ in eo non sunt ullæ.
     EN: And this is the message we have heard from him and announce to you: God is light, and there is no darkness at all in him.

1:6  LA: Si dixerimus quoniam societatem habemus cum eo, et in tenebris ambulamus, mentimur, et veritatem non facimus.
     EN: If we say that we have fellowship with him and walk in darkness, we lie and do not practice the truth.

1:7  LA: Si autem in luce ambulamus sicut et ipse est in luce, societatem habemus ad invicem, et sanguis Jesu Christi, Filii ejus, emundat nos ab omni peccato.
     EN: But if we walk in the light, as he himself is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus Christ his Son cleanses us from every sin.

1:8  LA: Si dixerimus quoniam peccatum non habemus, ipsi nos seducimus, et veritas in nobis non est.
     EN: If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us.

1:9  LA: Si confiteamur peccata nostra: fidelis est, et justus, ut remittat nobis peccata nostra, et emundet nos ab omni iniquitate.
     EN: If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive our sins and cleanse us from all lawlessness.

1:10  LA: Si dixerimus quoniam non peccavimus, mendacem facimus eum, et verbum ejus non est in nobis.
      EN: If we say that we have not sinned, we make him a liar, and his word is not in us.

1 JOHN 2

2:1  LA: Filioli mei, hæc scribo vobis, ut non peccetis. Sed et si quis peccaverit, advocatum habemus apud Patrem, Jesum Christum justum:
     EN: My little children, I write these things to you so that you may not sin. But if anyone does sin, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the just.

2:2  LA: et ipse est propitiatio pro peccatis nostris: non pro nostris autem tantum, sed etiam pro totius mundi.
     EN: He is the propitiation for our sins, and not for ours alone, but also for those of the whole world.

2:3  LA: Et in hoc scimus quoniam cognovimus eum, si mandata ejus observemus.
     EN: By this we know that we have come to know him: if we observe his commandments.

2:4  LA: Qui dicit se nosse eum, et mandata ejus non custodit, mendax est, et in hoc veritas non est.
     EN: Whoever says that he knows him but does not keep his commandments is a liar, and the truth is not in him.

2:5  LA: Qui autem servat verbum ejus, vere in hoc caritas Dei perfecta est: et in hoc scimus quoniam in ipso sumus.
     EN: But whoever keeps his word, in him the love of God is truly made complete. By this we know that we are in him.

2:6  LA: Qui dicit se in ipso manere, debet, sicut ille ambulavit, et ipse ambulare.
     EN: Whoever says that he remains in him ought himself to walk as he walked.

2:7  LA: Carissimi, non mandatum novum scribo vobis, sed mandatum vetus, quod habuistis ab initio. Mandatum vetus est verbum, quod audistis.
     EN: Beloved, I am not writing a new commandment to you, but an old commandment that you have had from the beginning. The old commandment is the word you have heard.

2:8  LA: Iterum mandatum novum scribo vobis, quod verum est et in ipso, et in vobis: quia tenebræ transierunt, et verum lumen jam lucet.
     EN: Yet I am writing a new commandment to you, which is true both in him and in you, because the darkness has passed away and the true light is already shining.

2:9  LA: Qui dicit se in luce esse, et fratrem suum odit, in tenebris est usque adhuc.
     EN: Whoever says that he is in the light and hates his brother is still in darkness.

2:10  LA: Qui diligit fratrem suum, in lumine manet, et scandalum in eo non est.
      EN: Whoever loves his brother remains in the light, and there is no cause of stumbling in him.

2:11  LA: Qui autem odit fratrem suum, in tenebris est, et in tenebris ambulat, et nescit quo eat: quia tenebræ obcæcaverunt oculos ejus.
      EN: But whoever hates his brother is in darkness and walks in darkness, and does not know where he is going, because the darkness has blinded his eyes.

2:12  LA: Scribo vobis, filioli, quoniam remittuntur vobis peccata propter nomen ejus.
      EN: I write to you, little children, because your sins are forgiven for his name’s sake.

2:13  LA: Scribo vobis, patres, quoniam cognovistis eum, qui ab initio est. Scribo vobis, adolescentes, quoniam vicistis malignum.
      EN: I write to you, fathers, because you have known him who is from the beginning. I write to you, young men, because you have overcome the evil one.

2:14  LA: Scribo vobis, infantes, quoniam cognovistis patrem. Scribo vobis juvenes, quoniam fortes estis, et verbum Dei manet in vobis, et vicistis malignum.
      EN: I write to you, children, because you have known the Father. I write to you, young men, because you are strong, and the word of God remains in you, and you have overcome the evil one.

2:15  LA: Nolite diligere mundum, neque ea quæ in mundo sunt. Si quis diligit mundum, non est caritas Patris in eo:
      EN: Do not love the world or the things in the world. If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father is not in him.

2:16  LA: quoniam omne quod est in mundo, concupiscentia carnis est, et concupiscentia oculorum, et superbia vitæ: quæ non est ex Patre, sed ex mundo est.
      EN: For all that is in the world is the desire of the flesh, the desire of the eyes, and the pride of life; this is not from the Father but from the world.

2:17  LA: Et mundus transit, et concupiscentia ejus: qui autem facit voluntatem Dei manet in æternum.
      EN: And the world is passing away, along with its desire; but whoever does the will of God remains forever.

2:18  LA: Filioli, novissima hora est: et sicut audistis quia antichristus venit, et nunc antichristi multi facti sunt; unde scimus, quia novissima hora est.
      EN: Little children, it is the last hour. And just as you heard that the antichrist is coming, so now many antichrists have arisen. By this we know that it is the last hour.

2:19  LA: Ex nobis prodierunt, sed non erant ex nobis, nam, si fuissent ex nobis, permansissent utique nobiscum: sed ut manifesti sint quoniam non sunt omnes ex nobis.
      EN: They went out from us, but they were not of us. For if they had been of us, they would certainly have remained with us. But they went out so that it might be made clear that not all are of us.

2:20  LA: Sed vos unctionem habetis a Sancto, et nostis omnia.
      EN: But you have an anointing from the Holy One, and you know all things.

2:21  LA: Non scripsi vobis quasi ignorantibus veritatem, sed quasi scientibus eam: et quoniam omne mendacium ex veritate non est.
      EN: I have not written to you as people who do not know the truth, but as people who know it, and because no lie comes from the truth.

2:22  LA: Quis est mendax, nisi is qui negat quoniam Jesus est Christus? Hic est antichristus, qui negat Patrem, et Filium.
      EN: Who is the liar but the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ? This is the antichrist: the one who denies the Father and the Son.

2:23  LA: Omnis qui negat Filium, nec Patrem habet: qui confitetur Filium, et Patrem habet.
      EN: Everyone who denies the Son does not have the Father either. Whoever confesses the Son has the Father also.

2:24  LA: Vos quod audistis ab initio, in vobis permaneat: si in vobis permanserit quod audistis ab initio, et vos in Filio et Patre manebitis.
      EN: As for you, let what you heard from the beginning remain in you. If what you heard from the beginning remains in you, you too will remain in the Son and the Father.

2:25  LA: Et hæc est repromissio, quam ipse pollicitus est nobis, vitam æternam.
      EN: And this is the promise he himself made to us: eternal life.

2:26  LA: Hæc scripsi vobis de his, qui seducant vos.
      EN: I have written these things to you about those who would deceive you.

2:27  LA: Et vos unctionem, quam accepistis ab eo, maneat in vobis. Et non necesse habetis ut aliquis doceat vos: sed sicut unctio ejus docet vos de omnibus, et verum est, et non est mendacium. Et sicut docuit vos: manete in eo.
      EN: As for you, let the anointing you received from him remain in you. You have no need for anyone to teach you; rather, his anointing teaches you about all things, and it is true and is no lie. Just as it has taught you, remain in him.

2:28  LA: Et nunc, filioli, manete in eo: ut cum apparuerit, habeamus fiduciam, et non confundamur ab eo in adventu ejus.
      EN: And now, little children, remain in him, so that when he appears we may have confidence and not be put to shame before him at his coming.

2:29  LA: Si scitis quoniam justus est, scitote quoniam et omnis, qui facit justitiam, ex ipso natus est.
      EN: If you know that he is just, know also that everyone who practices righteousness has been born of him.

1 JOHN 3

3:1  LA: Videte qualem caritatem dedit nobis Pater, ut filii Dei nominemur et simus. Propter hoc mundus non novit nos: quia non novit eum.
     EN: See what love the Father has given us, that we should be called children of God—and be so. For this reason the world does not know us, because it did not know him.

3:2  LA: Carissimi, nunc filii Dei sumus: et nondum apparuit quid erimus. Scimus quoniam cum apparuerit, similes ei erimus: quoniam videbimus eum sicuti est.
     EN: Beloved, we are now children of God, and what we shall be has not yet appeared. We know that when he appears, we shall be like him, because we shall see him as he is.

3:3  LA: Et omnis qui habet hanc spem in eo, sanctificat se, sicut et ille sanctus est.
     EN: And everyone who has this hope in him makes himself holy, just as he is holy.

3:4  LA: Omnis qui facit peccatum, et iniquitatem facit: et peccatum est iniquitas.
     EN: Everyone who commits sin also commits lawlessness, and sin is lawlessness.

3:5  LA: Et scitis quia ille apparuit ut peccata nostra tolleret: et peccatum in eo non est.
     EN: You know that he appeared to take away our sins, and there is no sin in him.

3:6  LA: Omnis qui in eo manet, non peccat: et omnis qui peccat, non vidit eum, nec cognovit eum.
     EN: Everyone who remains in him does not sin; everyone who sins has neither seen him nor known him.

3:7  LA: Filioli, nemo vos seducat. Qui facit justitiam, justus est, sicut et ille justus est.
     EN: Little children, let no one deceive you. Whoever practices righteousness is just, as he is just.

3:8  LA: Qui facit peccatum, ex diabolo est: quoniam ab initio diabolus peccat. In hoc apparuit Filius Dei, ut dissolvat opera diaboli.
     EN: Whoever commits sin is from the devil, because the devil sins from the beginning. For this the Son of God appeared: to undo the works of the devil.

3:9  LA: Omnis qui natus est ex Deo, peccatum non facit: quoniam semen ipsius in eo manet, et non potest peccare, quoniam ex Deo natus est.
     EN: Everyone born of God does not commit sin, because his seed remains in him; and he cannot sin, because he has been born of God.

3:10  LA: In hoc manifesti sunt filii Dei, et filii diaboli. Omnis qui non est justus, non est ex Deo, et qui non diligit fratrem suum:
      EN: By this the children of God and the children of the devil are made evident: everyone who is not just is not from God, nor is the one who does not love his brother.

3:11  LA: quoniam hæc est annuntiatio, quam audistis ab initio, ut diligatis alterutrum.
      EN: For this is the message you heard from the beginning: that you should love one another.

3:12  LA: Non sicut Cain, qui ex maligno erat, et occidit fratrem suum. Et propter quid occidit eum? Quoniam opera ejus maligna erant: fratris autem ejus, justa.
      EN: We must not be like Cain, who was from the evil one and killed his brother. And why did he kill him? Because his own works were evil, but his brother’s were just.

3:13  LA: Nolite mirari, fratres, si odit vos mundus.
      EN: Do not be surprised, brothers, if the world hates you.

3:14  LA: Nos scimus quoniam translati sumus de morte ad vitam, quoniam diligimus fratres. Qui non diligit, manet in morte:
      EN: We know that we have passed from death to life because we love the brothers. Whoever does not love remains in death.

3:15  LA: omnis qui odit fratrem suum, homicida est. Et scitis quoniam omnis homicida non habet vitam æternam in semetipso manentem.
      EN: Everyone who hates his brother is a murderer. And you know that no murderer has eternal life remaining within him.

3:16  LA: In hoc cognovimus caritatem Dei, quoniam ille animam suam pro nobis posuit: et nos debemus pro fratribus animas ponere.
      EN: By this we have known the love of God: he laid down his life for us, and we too ought to lay down our lives for the brothers.

3:17  LA: Qui habuerit substantiam hujus mundi, et viderit fratrem suum necessitatem habere, et clauserit viscera sua ab eo: quomodo caritas Dei manet in eo?
      EN: If someone has this world’s goods and sees his brother in need but closes his heart to him, how does the love of God remain in him?

3:18  LA: Filioli mei, non diligamus verbo neque lingua, sed opere et veritate:
      EN: My little children, let us love not in word or with the tongue, but in deed and truth.

3:19  LA: in hoc cognoscimus quoniam ex veritate sumus: et in conspectu ejus suadebimus corda nostra.
      EN: By this we know that we are from the truth, and we shall reassure our hearts before him.

3:20  LA: Quoniam si reprehenderit nos cor nostrum: major est Deus corde nostro, et novit omnia.
      EN: For if our heart reproaches us, God is greater than our heart and knows all things.

3:21  LA: Carissimi, si cor nostrum non reprehenderit nos, fiduciam habemus ad Deum:
      EN: Beloved, if our heart does not reproach us, we have confidence before God,

3:22  LA: et quidquid petierimus, accipiemus ab eo: quoniam mandata ejus custodimus, et ea, quæ sunt placita coram eo, facimus.
      EN: and whatever we ask we shall receive from him, because we keep his commandments and do what is pleasing before him.

3:23  LA: Et hoc est mandatum ejus: ut credamus in nomine Filii ejus Jesu Christi: et diligamus alterutrum, sicut dedit mandatum nobis.
      EN: And this is his commandment: that we believe in the name of his Son Jesus Christ and love one another, as he commanded us.

3:24  LA: Et qui servat mandata ejus, in illo manet, et ipse in eo: et in hoc scimus quoniam manet in nobis, de Spiritu quem dedit nobis.
      EN: Whoever keeps his commandments remains in him, and he in that person. By this we know that he remains in us: by the Spirit he has given us.

1 JOHN 4

4:1  LA: Carissimi , nolite omni spiritui credere, sed probate spiritus si ex Deo sint: quoniam multi pseudoprophetæ exierunt in mundum.
     EN: Beloved, do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits to see whether they are from God, because many false prophets have gone out into the world.

4:2  LA: In hoc cognoscitur Spiritus Dei: omnis spiritus qui confitetur Jesum Christum in carne venisse, ex Deo est:
     EN: By this the Spirit of God is known: every spirit that confesses Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is from God.

4:3  LA: et omnis spiritus qui solvit Jesum, ex Deo non est, et hic est antichristus, de quo audistis quoniam venit, et nunc jam in mundo est.
     EN: And every spirit that divides Jesus is not from God. This is the antichrist, of whom you heard that he is coming, and he is now already in the world.

4:4  LA: Vos ex Deo estis filioli, et vicistis eum, quoniam major est qui in vobis est, quam qui in mundo.
     EN: You are from God, little children, and have overcome him, because the one who is in you is greater than the one who is in the world.

4:5  LA: Ipsi de mundo sunt: ideo de mundo loquuntur, et mundus eos audit.
     EN: They are from the world; therefore they speak from the world, and the world listens to them.

4:6  LA: Nos ex Deo sumus. Qui novit Deum, audit nos; qui non est ex Deo, non audit nos: in hoc cognoscimus Spiritum veritatis, et spiritum erroris.
     EN: We are from God. Whoever knows God listens to us; whoever is not from God does not listen to us. By this we recognize the Spirit of truth and the spirit of error.

4:7  LA: Carissimi, diligamus nos invicem: quia caritas ex Deo est. Et omnis qui diligit, ex Deo natus est, et cognoscit Deum.
     EN: Beloved, let us love one another, because love is from God. Everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God.

4:8  LA: Qui non diligit, non novit Deum: quoniam Deus caritas est.
     EN: Whoever does not love does not know God, because God is love.

4:9  LA: In hoc apparuit caritas Dei in nobis, quoniam Filium suum unigenitum misit Deus in mundum, ut vivamus per eum.
     EN: By this the love of God appeared among us: God sent his only-begotten Son into the world so that we might live through him.

4:10  LA: In hoc est caritas: non quasi nos dilexerimus Deum, sed quoniam ipse prior dilexit nos, et misit Filium suum propitiationem pro peccatis nostris.
      EN: In this is love: not that we loved God, but that he first loved us and sent his Son as the propitiation for our sins.

4:11  LA: Carissimi, si sic Deus dilexit nos: et nos debemus alterutrum diligere.
      EN: Beloved, if God loved us in this way, we too ought to love one another.

4:12  LA: Deum nemo vidit umquam. Si diligamus invicem, Deus in nobis manet, et caritas ejus in nobis perfecta est.
      EN: No one has ever seen God. If we love one another, God remains in us, and his love is made complete in us.

4:13  LA: In hoc cognoscimus quoniam in eo manemus, et ipse in nobis: quoniam de Spiritu suo dedit nobis.
      EN: By this we know that we remain in him and he in us: he has given us of his Spirit.

4:14  LA: Et nos vidimus, et testificamur quoniam Pater misit Filium suum Salvatorem mundi.
      EN: And we have seen and bear witness that the Father sent his Son as Savior of the world.

4:15  LA: Quisquis confessus fuerit quoniam Jesus est Filius Dei, Deus in eo manet, et ipse in Deo.
      EN: Whoever confesses that Jesus is the Son of God, God remains in him and he in God.

4:16  LA: Et nos cognovimus, et credidimus caritati, quam habet Deus in nobis. Deus caritas est: et qui manet in caritate, in Deo manet, et Deus in eo.
      EN: We have come to know and believe the love God has for us. God is love, and whoever remains in love remains in God, and God in him.

4:17  LA: In hoc perfecta est caritas Dei nobiscum, ut fiduciam habeamus in die judicii: quia sicut ille est, et nos sumus in hoc mundo.
      EN: In this the love of God is made complete with us, that we may have confidence on the day of judgment, because as he is, so are we in this world.

4:18  LA: Timor non est in caritate: sed perfecta caritas foras mittit timorem, quoniam timor pœnam habet: qui autem timet, non est perfectus in caritate.
      EN: There is no fear in love; rather, perfect love casts out fear, because fear involves punishment. Whoever fears has not been made perfect in love.

4:19  LA: Nos ergo diligamus Deum, quoniam Deus prior dilexit nos.
      EN: Let us therefore love God, because God first loved us.

4:20  LA: Si quis dixerit: Quoniam diligo Deum, et fratrem suum oderit, mendax est. Qui enim non diligit fratrem suum quem vidit, Deum, quem non vidit, quomodo potest diligere?
      EN: If someone says, “I love God,” and hates his brother, he is a liar. For whoever does not love his brother whom he has seen, how can he love God whom he has not seen?

4:21  LA: Et hoc mandatum habemus a Deo: ut qui diligit Deum, diligat et fratrem suum.
      EN: And we have this commandment from God: whoever loves God must also love his brother.

1 JOHN 5

5:1  LA: Omnis qui credit quoniam Jesus est Christus, ex Deo natus est. Et omnis qui diligit eum qui genuit, diligit et eum qui natus est ex eo.
     EN: Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Christ has been born of God. And everyone who loves the one who gave him birth loves also the one born of him.

5:2  LA: In hoc cognoscimus quoniam diligamus natos Dei, cum Deum diligamus, et mandata ejus faciamus.
     EN: By this we know that we love the children of God: when we love God and do his commandments.

5:3  LA: Hæc est enim caritas Dei, ut mandata ejus custodiamus: et mandata ejus gravia non sunt.
     EN: For this is the love of God: that we keep his commandments. And his commandments are not burdensome.

5:4  LA: Quoniam omne quod natum est ex Deo, vincit mundum: et hæc est victoria, quæ vincit mundum, fides nostra.
     EN: For everything born of God overcomes the world. And this is the victory that overcomes the world: our faith.

5:5  LA: Quis est, qui vincit mundum, nisi qui credit quoniam Jesus est Filius Dei?
     EN: Who is it that overcomes the world except the one who believes that Jesus is the Son of God?

5:6  LA: Hic est, qui venit per aquam et sanguinem, Jesus Christus: non in aqua solum, sed in aqua et sanguine. Et Spiritus est, qui testificatur quoniam Christus est veritas.
     EN: This is the one who came through water and blood, Jesus Christ—not in water alone, but in water and blood. And it is the Spirit who bears witness that Christ is the truth.

5:7  LA: Quoniam tres sunt, qui testimonium dant in cælo: Pater, Verbum, et Spiritus Sanctus: et hi tres unum sunt.
     EN: For there are three who bear witness in heaven: the Father, the Word, and the Holy Spirit; and these three are one.

5:8  LA: Et tres sunt, qui testimonium dant in terra: spiritus, et aqua, et sanguis: et hi tres unum sunt.
     EN: And there are three who bear witness on earth: the spirit, the water, and the blood; and these three are one.

5:9  LA: Si testimonium hominum accipimus, testimonium Dei majus est: quoniam hoc est testimonium Dei, quod majus est, quoniam testificatus est de Filio suo.
     EN: If we receive human testimony, the testimony of God is greater. For this is the testimony of God, which is greater: that he has testified about his Son.

5:10  LA: Qui credit in Filium Dei, habet testimonium Dei in se. Qui non credit Filio, mendacem facit eum: quia non credit in testimonium quod testificatus est Deus de Filio suo.
      EN: Whoever believes in the Son of God has the testimony of God within himself. Whoever does not believe the Son makes him a liar, because he does not believe the testimony God has given about his Son.

5:11  LA: Et hoc est testimonium, quoniam vitam æternam dedit nobis Deus: et hæc vita in Filio ejus est.
      EN: And this is the testimony: God has given us eternal life, and this life is in his Son.

5:12  LA: Qui habet Filium, habet vitam: qui non habet Filium, vitam non habet.
      EN: Whoever has the Son has life; whoever does not have the Son does not have life.

5:13  LA: Hæc scribo vobis ut sciatis quoniam vitam habetis æternam, qui creditis in nomine Filii Dei.
      EN: I write these things to you who believe in the name of the Son of God, so that you may know that you have eternal life.

5:14  LA: Et hæc est fiducia, quam habemus ad eum: quia quodcumque petierimus, secundum voluntatem ejus, audit nos.
      EN: And this is the confidence we have toward him: if we ask anything according to his will, he hears us.

5:15  LA: Et scimus quia audit nos quidquid petierimus: scimus quoniam habemus petitiones quas postulamus ab eo.
      EN: And we know that he hears us in whatever we ask; we know that we have the requests we have asked of him.

5:16  LA: Qui scit fratrem suum peccare peccatum non ad mortem, petat, et dabitur ei vita peccanti non ad mortem. Est peccatum ad mortem: non pro illo dico ut roget quis.
      EN: Whoever knows that his brother is committing a sin not leading to death should pray, and life will be given to the one sinning not unto death. There is a sin unto death; I do not say that anyone should pray for that.

5:17  LA: Omnis iniquitas, peccatum est: et est peccatum ad mortem.
      EN: All lawlessness is sin, and there is a sin unto death.

5:18  LA: Scimus quia omnis qui natus est ex Deo, non peccat: sed generatio Dei conservat eum, et malignus non tangit eum.
      EN: We know that everyone born of God does not sin; rather, birth from God preserves him, and the evil one does not touch him.

5:19  LA: Scimus quoniam ex Deo sumus: et mundus totus in maligno positus est.
