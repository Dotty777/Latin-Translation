NUMBERS 2

2:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen et Aaron, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:

2:2  LA: Singuli per turmas, signa, atque vexilla, et domos cognationum suarum, castrametabuntur filii Israël, per gyrum tabernaculi fœderis.
     EN: The children of Israel shall camp around the tabernacle of the covenant, each according to their companies, standards, banners, and ancestral households.

2:3  LA: Ad orientem Judas figet tentoria per turmas exercitus sui : eritque princeps filiorum ejus Nahasson filius Aminadab.
     EN: Judah shall pitch its tents on the east, according to the companies of its army; the leader of its sons shall be Nahshon son of Amminadab.

2:4  LA: Et omnis de stirpe ejus summa pugnantium, septuaginta quatuor millia sexcenti.
     EN: The total of the fighting men from his stock was seventy-four thousand six hundred.

2:5  LA: Juxta eum castrametati sunt de tribu Issachar, quorum princeps fuit Nathanaël filius Suar.
     EN: Beside him camped those of the tribe of Issachar, whose leader was Nethanel son of Zuar.

2:6  LA: Et omnis numerus pugnatorum ejus quinquaginta quatuor millia quadringenti.
     EN: The total number of his fighting men was fifty-four thousand four hundred.

2:7  LA: In tribu Zabulon princeps fuit Eliab filius Helon.
     EN: The leader in the tribe of Zebulun was Eliab son of Helon.

2:8  LA: Omnis de stirpe ejus exercitus pugnatorum, quinquaginta septem millia quadringenti.
     EN: The entire army of fighting men from his stock was fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

2:9  LA: Universi qui in castris Judæ enumerati sunt, fuerunt centum octoginta sex millia quadringenti : et per turmas suas primi egredientur.
     EN: All those numbered in Judah's camp were one hundred eighty-six thousand four hundred; they shall set out first, by their companies.

2:10  LA: In castris filiorum Ruben ad meridianam plagam erit princeps Elisur filius Sedeur.
     EN: In the camp of the sons of Reuben, on the southern side, the leader shall be Elizur son of Shedeur.

     EN: His entire army of fighting men who were numbered was forty-six thousand five hundred.

2:12  LA: Juxta eum castrametati sunt de tribu Simeon : quorum princeps fuit Salamiel filius Surisaddai.
     EN: Beside him camped those of the tribe of Simeon, whose leader was Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai.

2:13  LA: Et cunctus exercitus pugnatorum ejus qui numerati sunt, quinquaginta novem millia trecenti.
     EN: His entire army of fighting men who were numbered was fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

2:14  LA: In tribu Gad princeps fuit Eliasaph filius Duel.
     EN: The leader in the tribe of Gad was Eliasaph son of Deuel.

2:15  LA: Et cunctus exercitus pugnatorum ejus, qui numerati sunt, quadraginta quinque millia sexcenti quinquaginta.
     EN: His entire army of fighting men who were numbered was forty-five thousand six hundred fifty.

     EN: All those recorded in Reuben's camp were one hundred fifty-one thousand four hundred fifty, according to their companies. They shall depart in the second position.

     EN: The tabernacle of the testimony shall be taken up through the duties of the Levites and their companies. As it is set up, so shall it be taken down. Each shall depart in their own place and order.

2:18  LA: Ad occidentalem plagam erunt castra filiorum Ephraim, quorum princeps fuit Elisama filius Ammiud.
     EN: On the western side shall be the camp of the sons of Ephraim, whose leader was Elishama son of Ammihud.

     EN: His entire army of fighting men who were numbered was forty thousand five hundred.

2:20  LA: Et cum eis tribus filiorum Manasse, quorum princeps fuit Gamaliel filius Phadassur.
     EN: With them shall be the tribe of the sons of Manasseh, whose leader was Gamaliel son of Pedahzur.

2:21  LA: Cunctusque exercitus pugnatorum ejus, qui numerati sunt, triginta duo millia ducenti.
     EN: His entire army of fighting men who were numbered was thirty-two thousand two hundred.

2:23  LA: Et cunctus exercitus pugnatorum ejus, qui recensiti sunt, triginta quinque millia quadringenti.
     EN: His entire army of fighting men who were recorded was thirty-five thousand four hundred.

     EN: All those numbered in Ephraim's camp were one hundred eight thousand one hundred, according to their companies. They shall depart third.

     EN: On the northern side camped the sons of Dan, whose leader was Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.

     EN: Beside him those of the tribe of Asher pitched their tents; their leader was Pagiel son of Ochran.

     EN: His entire army of fighting men who were numbered was forty-one thousand five hundred.

     EN: His entire army of fighting men was fifty-three thousand four hundred.

NUMBERS 3

3:1  LA: Hæ sunt generationes Aaron et Moysi in die qua locutus est Dominus ad Moysen in monte Sinai.
     EN: These are the descendants of Aaron and Moses at the time when the Lord spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai.

3:2  LA: Et hæc nomina filiorum Aaron : primogenitus ejus Nadab, deinde Abiu, et Eleazar, et Ithamar.
     EN: These are the names of Aaron's sons: Nadab his firstborn, then Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

3:3  LA: Hæc nomina filiorum Aaron sacerdotum qui uncti sunt, et quorum repletæ et consecratæ manus ut sacerdotio fungerentur.
     EN: These are the names of Aaron's sons, the priests who were anointed and whose hands were filled and consecrated to exercise the priesthood.

3:4  LA: Mortui sunt enim Nadab et Abiu cum offerrent ignem alienum in conspectu Domini in deserto Sinai, absque liberis : functique sunt sacerdotio Eleazar et Ithamar coram Aaron patre suo.
     EN: For Nadab and Abihu died when they offered unauthorized fire before the Lord in the wilderness of Sinai, without children; Eleazar and Ithamar exercised the priesthood in the presence of Aaron their father.

3:5  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

3:6  LA: Applica tribum Levi, et fac stare in conspectu Aaron sacerdotis ut ministrent ei, et excubent,
     EN: Bring forward the tribe of Levi and make them stand before Aaron the priest to serve him, keep watch,

3:7  LA: et observent quidquid ad cultum pertinet multitudinis coram tabernaculo testimonii,
     EN: and attend to everything belonging to the worship of the multitude before the tabernacle of the testimony,

3:8  LA: et custodiant vasa tabernaculi, servientes in ministerio ejus.
     EN: and guard the vessels of the tabernacle, serving in its ministry.

3:9 (Vg 3:9, 3:10)  LA: Dabisque dono Levitas
     EN: You shall give the Levites as a gift to Aaron and his sons, to whom they have been handed over by the children of Israel. You shall appoint Aaron and his sons over the service of the priesthood. An outsider who approaches to minister shall die.

3:11  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

3:12  LA: Ego tuli Levitas a filiis Israël pro omni primogenito, qui aperit vulvam in filiis Israël, eruntque Levitæ mei.
     EN: I have taken the Levites from the children of Israel in place of every firstborn who opens the womb among the children of Israel, and the Levites shall be Mine.

3:13  LA: Meum est enim omne primogenitum : ex quo percussi primogenitos in terra Ægypti, sanctificavi mihi quidquid primum nascitur in Israël : ab homine usque ad pecus, mei sunt. Ego Dominus.
     EN: For every firstborn is Mine. From the time I struck down the firstborn in the land of Egypt, I consecrated to Myself everything born first in Israel, from human beings to livestock. They are Mine. I am the Lord.

3:14  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen in deserto Sinai, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying:

3:15  LA: Numera filios Levi per domos patrum suorum et familias, omnem masculum ab uno mense et supra.
     EN: Number the sons of Levi by their fathers' households and families, every male from one month old and upward.

3:16  LA: Numeravit Moyses, ut præceperat Dominus,
     EN: Moses numbered them as the Lord had commanded,

3:17  LA: et inventi sunt filii Levi per nomina sua, Gerson et Caath et Merari.
     EN: and the sons of Levi were found by their names: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

3:18  LA: Filii Gerson : Lebni et Semei.
     EN: The sons of Gershon: Libni and Shimei.

3:19  LA: Filii Caath : Amram et Jesaar, Hebron et Oziel.
     EN: The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.

3:20  LA: Filii Merari : Moholi et Musi.
     EN: The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi.

3:21  LA: De Gerson fuere familiæ duæ, Lebnitica, et Semeitica :
     EN: From Gershon came two families, the Libnites and the Shimeites.

3:22  LA: quarum numeratus est populus sexus masculini ab uno mense et supra, septem millia quingenti.
     EN: Their male population, from one month old and upward, was numbered at seven thousand five hundred.

3:23  LA: Hi post tabernaculum metabuntur ad occidentem,
     EN: They shall camp behind the tabernacle, on the west,

3:24  LA: sub principe Eliasaph filio Laël.
     EN: under the leader Eliasaph son of Lael.

3:25  LA: Et habebunt excubias in tabernaculo fœderis,
     EN: They shall have charge in the tabernacle of the covenant

3:26  LA: ipsum tabernaculum et operimentum ejus, tentorium quod trahitur ante fores tecti fœderis, et cortinas atrii : tentorium quoque quod appenditur in introitu atrii tabernaculi, et quidquid ad ritum altaris pertinet, funes tabernaculi et omnia utensilia ejus.
     EN: of the tabernacle itself and its covering, the curtain drawn before the entrance to the tent of the covenant, and the hangings of the court; also the curtain hanging at the entrance to the tabernacle's court, everything belonging to the service of the altar, the tabernacle's ropes, and all its equipment.

3:27  LA: Cognatio Caath habebit populos Amramitas et Jesaaritas et Hebronitas et Ozielitas. Hæ sunt familiæ Caathitarum recensitæ per nomina sua.
     EN: The clan of Kohath shall comprise the Amramites, the Izharites, the Hebronites, and the Uzzielites. These are the families of the Kohathites recorded by their names.

3:28  LA: Omnes generis masculini ab uno mense et supra, octo millia sexcenti habebunt excubias sanctuarii,
     EN: All their males from one month old and upward, eight thousand six hundred, shall have charge of the sanctuary

3:29  LA: et castrametabuntur ad meridianam plagam.
     EN: and camp on the southern side.

3:30  LA: Princepsque eorum erit Elisaphan filius Oziel :
     EN: Their leader shall be Elizaphan son of Uzziel.

3:31  LA: et custodient arcam, mensamque et candelabrum, altaria et vasa sanctuarii, in quibus ministratur, et velum, cunctamque hujuscemodi supellectilem.
     EN: They shall guard the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, the sanctuary's vessels used in ministry, the veil, and all equipment of this kind.

3:32  LA: Princeps autem principum Levitarum Eleazar filius Aaron sacerdotis, erit super excubitores custodiæ sanctuarii.
     EN: The chief over the leaders of the Levites, Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, shall be over those keeping charge of the sanctuary.

3:33  LA: At vero de Merari erunt populi Moholitæ et Musitæ recensiti per nomina sua :
     EN: From Merari shall come the Mahlites and the Mushites, recorded by their names:

3:34  LA: omnes generis masculini ab uno mense et supra, sex millia ducenti.

     EN: The Lord said to Aaron: You, your sons, and your father's house with you shall bear the iniquity of the sanctuary, and you and your sons together shall bear the sins of your priesthood.

18:2  LA: Sed et fratres tuos de tribu Levi, et sceptrum patris tui sume tecum, præstoque sint, et ministrent tibi : tu autem et filii tui ministrabitis in tabernaculo testimonii.
     EN: Take with you also your brothers from the tribe of Levi, your father's stock. Let them be at hand and minister to you; but you and your sons shall minister in the tabernacle of the testimony.

18:3  LA: Excubabuntque Levitæ ad præcepta tua, et ad cuncta opera tabernaculi : ita dumtaxat ut ad vasa sanctuarii et ad altare non accedant, ne et illi moriantur, et vos pereatis simul.
     EN: The Levites shall keep watch according to your commands and attend to all the work of the tabernacle, provided that they do not approach the vessels of the sanctuary or the altar, lest they die and you perish with them.

18:4  LA: Sint autem tecum, et excubent in custodiis tabernaculi, et in omnibus cæremoniis ejus. Alienigena non miscebitur vobis.
     EN: They shall be with you and keep charge of the tabernacle and all its ceremonies. No outsider shall join you.

18:5  LA: Excubate in custodia sanctuarii, et in ministerio altaris : ne oriatur indignatio super filios Israël.
     EN: Keep charge of the sanctuary and the ministry of the altar, lest wrath arise against the children of Israel.

18:6  LA: Ego dedi vobis fratres vestros Levitas de medio filiorum Israël, et tradidi donum Domino, ut serviant in ministeriis tabernaculi ejus.
     EN: I have given you your brothers the Levites from among the children of Israel and handed them over as a gift to the Lord, to serve in the ministries of His tabernacle.

18:7  LA: Tu autem et filii tui custodite sacerdotium vestrum : et omnia quæ ad cultum altaris pertinent, et intra velum sunt, per sacerdotes administrabuntur : si quis externus accesserit, occidetur.
     EN: You and your sons shall guard your priesthood. Everything belonging to the service of the altar and everything within the veil shall be administered by the priests. If any outsider approaches, he shall be put to death.

18:8  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Aaron : Ecce dedi tibi custodiam primitiarum mearum. Omnia quæ sanctificantur a filiis Israël, tradidi tibi et filiis tuis pro officio sacerdotali legitima sempiterna.
     EN: The Lord spoke to Aaron: Behold, I have given you charge of My firstfruits. Everything consecrated by the children of Israel I have handed over to you and your sons for the priestly office, as a lasting ordinance.

18:9  LA: Hæc ergo accipies de his, quæ sanctificantur et oblata sunt Domino. Omnis oblatio, et sacrificium, et quidquid pro peccato atque delicto redditur mihi, et cedit in Sancta sanctorum, tuum erit, et filiorum tuorum.
     EN: You shall therefore receive these things from what is consecrated and offered to the Lord: every offering and sacrifice, and whatever is rendered to Me for sin and guilt and becomes most holy, shall belong to you and your sons.

18:10  LA: In sanctuario comedes illud : mares tantum edent ex eo, quia consecratum est tibi.
     EN: You shall eat it in the sanctuary; only males shall eat from it, because it is consecrated to you.

18:11  LA: Primitias autem, quas voverint et obtulerint filii Israël, tibi dedi, et filiis tuis, ac filiabus tuis, jure perpetuo : qui mundus est in domo tua, vescetur eis.
     EN: The firstfruits the children of Israel vow and offer I have given to you, your sons, and your daughters as a perpetual right. Whoever is clean in your household shall eat them.

18:12  LA: Omnem medullam olei, et vini, ac frumenti, quidquid offerunt primitiarum Domino, tibi dedi.
     EN: All the best of the oil, wine, and grain, whatever firstfruits they offer to the Lord, I have given to you.

18:13  LA: Universa frugum initia, quas gignit humus, et Domino deportantur, cedent in usus tuos : qui mundus est in domo tua, vescetur eis.
     EN: All the first produce the soil brings forth that is brought to the Lord shall be for your use. Whoever is clean in your household shall eat it.

18:14  LA: Omne quod ex voto reddiderint filii Israël, tuum erit.
     EN: Everything the children of Israel render by vow shall be yours.

18:15  LA: Quidquid primum erumpit e vulva cunctæ carnis, quam offerunt Domino, sive ex hominibus, sive de pecoribus fuerit, tui juris erit : ita dumtaxat ut pro hominis primogenito pretium accipias, et omne animal quod immundum est redimi facias,
     EN: Whatever first emerges from the womb of all flesh offered to the Lord, whether human beings or livestock, shall be your due, provided that you receive a price for a human firstborn and have every unclean animal redeemed.

18:16  LA: cujus redemptio erit post unum mensem, siclis argenti quinque, pondere sanctuarii. Siclus viginti obolos habet.
     EN: Its redemption after one month shall be five silver shekels according to the sanctuary's weight. A shekel has twenty obols.

18:17  LA: Primogenitum autem bovis, et ovis, et capræ, non facies redimi, quia sanctificata sunt Domino. Sanguinem tantum eorum fundes super altare, et adipes adolebis in suavissimum odorem Domino.
     EN: But you shall not have the firstborn of an ox, sheep, or goat redeemed, because they are consecrated to the Lord. You shall only pour their blood on the altar and burn their fat as a most pleasing fragrance to the Lord.

18:18  LA: Carnes vero in usum tuum cedent, sicut pectusculum consecratum, et armus dexter : tua erunt.
     EN: Their meat shall be for your use, like the consecrated breast and the right shoulder; they shall be yours.

18:19  LA: Omnes primitias sanctuarii, quas offerunt filii Israël Domino, tibi dedi, et filiis, ac filiabus tuis, jure perpetuo. Pactum salis est sempiternum coram Domino, tibi ac filiis tuis.
     EN: All the firstfruits of the sanctuary that the children of Israel offer to the Lord I have given to you, your sons, and your daughters as a perpetual right. It is an everlasting covenant of salt before the Lord for you and your children.

18:20  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad Aaron : In terra eorum nihil possidebitis, nec habebitis partem inter eos : ego pars et hæreditas tua in medio filiorum Israël.
     EN: The Lord said to Aaron: You shall possess nothing in their land or have a share among them. I am your portion and inheritance among the children of Israel.

18:21  LA: Filiis autem Levi dedi omnes decimas Israëlis in possessionem, pro ministerio, quo serviunt mihi in tabernaculo fœderis :
     EN: To the sons of Levi I have given all Israel's tithes as a possession for the ministry by which they serve Me in the tabernacle of the covenant,

18:22  LA: ut non accedant ultra filii Israël ad tabernaculum, nec committant peccatum mortiferum,
     EN: so that the children of Israel may no longer approach the tabernacle and commit a sin bringing death,

18:23  LA: solis filiis Levi mihi in tabernaculo servientibus, et portantibus peccata populi. Legitimum sempiternum erit in generationibus vestris. Nihil aliud possidebunt,
     EN: while the sons of Levi alone serve Me in the tabernacle and bear the people's sins. This shall be a lasting ordinance throughout your generations. They shall possess nothing else,

18:24  LA: decimarum oblatione contenti, quas in usus eorum et necessaria separavi.
     EN: being content with the offering of tithes, which I have set apart for their use and needs.

18:25  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

18:26  LA: Præcipe Levitis, atque denuntia : Cum acceperitis a filiis Israël decimas, quas dedi vobis, primitias earum offerte Domino, id est, decimam partem decimæ,
     EN: Command the Levites and declare: When you receive from the children of Israel the tithes I have given you, offer their firstfruits to the Lord, that is, a tenth part of the tithe,

18:27  LA: ut reputetur vobis in oblationem primitivorum, tam de areis quam de torcularibus :
     EN: so that it may be counted for you as an offering of firstfruits, both from threshing floors and from winepresses.

18:28  LA: et universis quorum accipitis primitias, offerte Domino, et date Aaron sacerdoti.
     EN: From all the things whose firstfruits you receive, offer to the Lord and give to Aaron the priest.

18:29  LA: Omnia quæ offeretis ex decimis, et in donaria Domini separabitis, optima et electa erunt.
     EN: Everything you offer from the tithes and set apart as gifts for the Lord shall be the best and choicest.

18:30  LA: Dicesque ad eos : Si præclara et meliora quæque obtuleritis ex decimis, reputabitur vobis quasi de area, et torculari dederitis primitias :
     EN: Say to them: If you offer the finest and best of everything from the tithes, it shall be counted for you as though you had given firstfruits from the threshing floor and winepress.

18:31  LA: et comedetis eas in omnibus locis vestris, tam vos quam familiæ vestræ : quia pretium est pro ministerio, quo servitis in tabernaculo testimonii.
     EN: You and your households shall eat them in all your dwelling places, because this is the payment for the ministry by which you serve in the tabernacle of the testimony.

18:32  LA: Et non peccabitis super hoc, egregia vobis et pinguia reservantes, ne polluatis oblationes filiorum Israël, et moriamini.
     EN: You shall not sin in this by reserving the excellent and rich portions for yourselves, lest you defile the offerings of the children of Israel and die.

NUMBERS 19

19:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen et Aaron, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:

19:2  LA: Ista est religio victimæ, quam constituit Dominus. Præcipe filiis Israël, ut adducant ad te vaccam rufam ætatis integræ, in qua nulla sit macula, nec portaverit jugum :
     EN: This is the sacrificial rite the Lord has established. Command the children of Israel to bring you a red heifer of full age, with no blemish, that has never borne a yoke.

19:3  LA: tradetisque eam Eleazaro sacerdoti, qui eductam extra castra, immolabit in conspectu omnium :
     EN: Hand it over to Eleazar the priest, who shall lead it outside the camp and sacrifice it in everyone's sight.

19:4  LA: et tingens digitum in sanguine ejus, asperget contra fores tabernaculi septem vicibus,
     EN: Dipping his finger in its blood, he shall sprinkle toward the tabernacle's entrance seven times

19:5  LA: comburetque eam cunctis videntibus, tam pelle et carnibus ejus quam sanguine et fimo flammæ traditis.
     EN: and burn it in everyone's sight, committing its hide, flesh, blood, and dung to the flames.

19:6  LA: Lignum quoque cedrinum, et hyssopum, coccumque bis tinctum sacerdos mittet in flammam, quæ vaccam vorat.
     EN: The priest shall also throw cedar wood, hyssop, and twice-dyed scarlet into the flame consuming the heifer.

19:7  LA: Et tunc demum, lotis vestibus et corpore suo, ingredietur in castra, commaculatusque erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: Only then, after washing his clothes and body, shall he enter the camp. He shall remain defiled until evening.

19:8  LA: Sed et ille qui combusserit eam, lavabit vestimenta sua et corpus, et immundus erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: The one who burned it shall also wash his clothes and body and remain unclean until evening.

19:9  LA: Colliget autem vir mundus cineres vaccæ, et effundet eos extra castra in loco purissimo, ut sint multitudini filiorum Israël in custodiam, et in aquam aspersionis : quia pro peccato vacca combusta est.
     EN: A clean man shall gather the heifer's ashes and pour them out in a very clean place outside the camp, to be kept for the multitude of the children of Israel for water of sprinkling, because the heifer was burned for sin.

19:10  LA: Cumque laverit qui vaccæ portaverat cineres vestimenta sua, immundus erit usque ad vesperum. Habebunt hoc filii Israël, et advenæ qui habitant inter eos, sanctum jure perpetuo.
     EN: The one who carried the heifer's ashes shall wash his clothes and remain unclean until evening. The children of Israel and the sojourners dwelling among them shall hold this sacred as a perpetual ordinance.

19:11  LA: Qui tetigerit cadaver hominis, et propter hoc septem diebus fuerit immundus,
     EN: Anyone who touches a human corpse and is therefore unclean for seven days

19:12  LA: aspergetur ex hac aqua die tertio et septimo, et sic mundabitur. Si die tertio aspersus non fuerit, septimo non poterit emundari.
     EN: shall be sprinkled with this water on the third and seventh days, and thus be cleansed. If not sprinkled on the third day, that person cannot be cleansed on the seventh.

19:13  LA: Omnis qui tetigerit humanæ animæ morticinum, et aspersus hac commistione non fuerit, polluet tabernaculum Domini et peribit ex Israël : quia aqua expiationis non est aspersus, immundus erit, et manebit spurcitia ejus super eum.
     EN: Anyone who touches the dead body of a human being and is not sprinkled with this mixture shall defile the Lord's tabernacle and perish from Israel. Because that person has not been sprinkled with the water of purification, that person shall be unclean, and the uncleanness shall remain upon them.

19:14  LA: Ista est lex hominis qui moritur in tabernaculo : omnes qui ingrediuntur tentorium illius, et universa vasa quæ ibi sunt, polluta erunt septem diebus.
     EN: This is the law concerning a person who dies in a tent: everyone who enters that person's tent and all the vessels there shall be defiled for seven days.

19:15  LA: Vas, quod non habuerit operculum nec ligaturam desuper, immundum erit.
     EN: A vessel without a cover or fastening over it shall be unclean.

19:16  LA: Si quis in agro tetigerit cadaver occisi hominis, aut per se mortui, sive os illius, vel sepulchrum, immundus erit septem diebus.
     EN: If anyone in the open country touches the corpse of someone killed or naturally dead, or that person's bone or grave, that person shall be unclean for seven days.

19:17  LA: Tollentque de cineribus combustionis atque peccati, et mittent aquas vivas super eos in vas :
     EN: They shall take some of the ashes of the burning for sin and pour fresh water over them in a vessel.

19:18  LA: in quibus cum homo mundus tinxerit hyssopum, asperget ex eo omne tentorium, et cunctam supellectilem, et homines hujuscemodi contagione pollutos :
     EN: A clean man shall dip hyssop into it and sprinkle the whole tent, all its furnishings, and the people defiled by contact of this kind.

19:19  LA: atque hoc modo mundus lustrabit immundum tertio et septimo die : expiatusque die septimo, lavabit et se et vestimenta sua, et immundus erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: In this way the clean person shall purify the unclean on the third and seventh days. Purified on the seventh day, the person shall wash both body and clothes and remain unclean until evening.

19:20  LA: Si quis hoc ritu non fuerit expiatus, peribit anima illius de medio ecclesiæ : quia sanctuarium Domini polluit, et non est aqua lustrationis aspersus.
     EN: If anyone is not purified by this rite, that person shall perish from the midst of the assembly, because that person has defiled the Lord's sanctuary and has not been sprinkled with the water of purification.

19:21  LA: Erit hoc præceptum legitimum sempiternum. Ipse quoque qui aspergit aquas, lavabit vestimenta sua. Omnis qui tetigerit aquas expiationis, immundus erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: This command shall be a lasting ordinance. The one who sprinkles the water shall also wash his clothes. Anyone who touches the water of purification shall be unclean until evening.

19:22  LA: Quidquid tetigerit immundus, immundum faciet : et anima, quæ horum quippiam tetigerit, immunda erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: Whatever an unclean person touches becomes unclean, and anyone who touches any of these things shall be unclean until evening.

NUMBERS 20

20:1  LA: Veneruntque filii Israël et omnis multitudo in desertum Sin, mense primo, et mansit populus in Cades. Mortuaque est ibi Maria, et sepulta in eodem loco.
     EN: The children of Israel and the whole multitude came into the wilderness of Zin in the first month, and the people stayed in Kadesh. Miriam died there and was buried in that same place.

20:2  LA: Cumque indigeret aqua populus, convenerunt adversum Moysen et Aaron :
     EN: When the people lacked water, they gathered against Moses and Aaron.

20:3  LA: et versi in seditionem, dixerunt : Utinam periissemus inter fratres nostros coram Domino.
     EN: Turning to rebellion, they said: If only we had perished among our brothers before the Lord!

20:4  LA: Cur eduxistis ecclesiam Domini in solitudinem, ut et nos et nostra jumenta moriamur ?
     EN: Why have you brought the Lord's assembly into the wilderness, so that both we and our animals should die?

20:5  LA: quare nos fecistis ascendere de Ægypto, et adduxistis in locum istum pessimum, qui seri non potest, qui nec ficum gignit, nec vineas, nec malogranata, insuper et aquam non habet ad bibendum ?
     EN: Why have you made us come up from Egypt and brought us to this wretched place, which cannot be sown, produces neither figs nor vines nor pomegranates, and even has no water to drink?

20:6  LA: Ingressusque Moyses et Aaron, dimissa multitudine, tabernaculum fœderis, corruerunt proni in terram, clamaveruntque ad Dominum, atque dixerunt : Domine Deus, audi clamorem hujus populi, et aperi eis thesaurum tuum fontem aquæ vivæ, ut satiati, cesset murmuratio eorum. Et apparuit gloria Domini super eos.
     EN: Moses and Aaron left the multitude and entered the tabernacle of the covenant. They fell face down on the ground and cried to the Lord, saying: Lord God, hear this people's cry and open to them Your treasury, a spring of living water, so that, being satisfied, they may cease their grumbling. The glory of the Lord appeared above them.

20:7  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

20:8  LA: Tolle virgam, et congrega populum, tu et Aaron frater tuus, et loquimini ad petram coram eis, et illa dabit aquas. Cumque eduxeris aquam de petra, bibet omnis multitudo et jumenta ejus.
     EN: Take the staff and assemble the people, you and Aaron your brother. Speak to the rock before them, and it shall give water. When you bring water from the rock, the whole multitude and their animals shall drink.

20:9  LA: Tulit igitur Moyses virgam, quæ erat in conspectu Domini, sicut præceperat ei,
     EN: Moses took the staff that was before the Lord, as He had commanded him.

20:10  LA: congregata multitudine ante petram : dixitque eis : Audite, rebelles et increduli : num de petra hac vobis aquam poterimus ejicere ?
     EN: Having assembled the multitude before the rock, he said to them: Listen, rebels and unbelievers! Can we bring water out of this rock for you?

20:11  LA: Cumque elevasset Moyses manum, percutiens virga bis silicem, egressæ sunt aquæ largissimæ, ita ut populus biberet et jumenta.
     EN: Moses raised his hand and struck the flint rock twice with the staff. Water came out abundantly, so that the people and their animals drank.

20:12  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad Moysen et Aaron : Quia non credidistis mihi, ut sanctificaretis me coram filiis Israël, non introducetis hos populos in terram, quam dabo eis.
     EN: The Lord said to Moses and Aaron: Because you did not believe Me, to manifest My holiness before the children of Israel, you shall not bring these peoples into the land I will give them.

20:13  LA: Hæc est aqua contradictionis, ubi jurgati sunt filii Israël contra Dominum, et sanctificatus est in eis.
     EN: This is the water of contradiction, where the children of Israel disputed against the Lord, and He manifested His holiness among them.

20:14  LA: Misit interea nuntios Moyses de Cades ad regem Edom, qui dicerent : Hæc mandat frater tuus Israël : Nosti omnem laborem qui apprehendit nos,
     EN: Meanwhile Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom to say: Thus says your brother Israel. You know all the hardship that has overtaken us,

20:15  LA: quomodo descenderint patres nostri in Ægyptum, et habitaverimus ibi multo tempore, afflixerintque nos Ægyptii, et patres nostros :
     EN: how our fathers went down to Egypt, how we dwelt there a long time, and how the Egyptians afflicted us and our fathers;

20:16  LA: et quomodo clamaverimus ad Dominum, et exaudierit nos, miseritque angelum, qui eduxerit nos de Ægypto. Ecce in urbe Cades, quæ est in extremis finibus tuis, positi,
     EN: how we cried to the Lord, and He heard us and sent an angel who brought us out of Egypt. Now we are stationed in the city of Kadesh, at the edge of your territory,

20:17  LA: obsecramus ut nobis transire liceat per terram tuam. Non ibimus per agros, nec per vineas ; non bibemus aquas de puteis tuis : sed gradiemur via publica, nec ad dexteram nec ad sinistram declinantes, donec transeamus terminos tuos.
     EN: and ask that we be allowed to pass through your land. We shall not go through fields or vineyards or drink water from your wells. We shall travel along the public road, turning neither right nor left, until we have passed through your territory.

20:18  LA: Cui respondit Edom : Non transibis per me, alioquin armatus occurram tibi.
     EN: Edom answered him: You shall not pass through me; otherwise I will come out armed against you.

20:19  LA: Dixeruntque filii Israël : Per tritam gradiemur viam : et si biberimus aquas tuas, nos et pecora nostra, dabimus quod justum est : nulla erit in pretio difficultas, tantum velociter transeamus.
     EN: The children of Israel said: We shall travel along the beaten road. If we and our livestock drink your water, we shall pay what is fair. There will be no difficulty over the price; only let us pass through quickly.

20:20  LA: At ille respondit : Non transibis. Statimque egressus est obvius, cum infinita multitudo, et manu forti,
     EN: But he answered: You shall not pass through. At once he came out to meet them with an immense multitude and a strong force,

20:21  LA: nec voluit acquiescere deprecanti, ut concederet transitum per fines suos. Quam ob rem divertit ab eo Israël.
     EN: refusing to grant their plea for passage through his territory. Therefore Israel turned away from him.

20:22  LA: Cumque castra movissent de Cades, venerunt in montem Hor, qui est in finibus terræ Edom :
     EN: Moving camp from Kadesh, they came to Mount Hor, at the borders of the land of Edom.

20:23  LA: ubi locutus est Dominus ad Moysen :
     EN: There the Lord spoke to Moses:

20:24  LA: Pergat, inquit, Aaron ad populos suos : non enim intrabit terram, quam dedi filiis Israël, eo quod incredulus fuerit ori meo, ad aquas contradictionis.
     EN: Let Aaron go to his people, He said, for he shall not enter the land I have given the children of Israel, because he did not believe the word of My mouth at the waters of contradiction.

20:25  LA: Tolle Aaron et filium ejus cum eo, et duces eos in montem Hor.
     EN: Take Aaron and his son with him, and lead them up Mount Hor.

20:26  LA: Cumque nudaveris patrem veste sua, indues ea Eleazarum filium ejus : Aaron colligetur, et morietur ibi.
     EN: After removing the father's garments, put them on Eleazar his son. Aaron shall be gathered to his people and die there.

20:27  LA: Fecit Moyses ut præceperat Dominus : et ascenderunt in montem Hor coram omni multitudine.
     EN: Moses did as the Lord commanded. They went up Mount Hor before the whole multitude.

20:28 (Vg 20:28, 20:29, 20:30)  LA: Cumque Aaron spoliasset vestibus suis, induit eis Eleazarum filium ejus.
     EN: After removing Aaron's garments, he put them on Eleazar his son. When Aaron had died on the mountaintop, Moses came down with Eleazar. The whole multitude, seeing that Aaron had died, wept over him for thirty days throughout all their families.

NUMBERS 21

21:1  LA: Quod cum audisset Chananæus rex Arad, qui habitabat ad meridiem, venisse scilicet Israël per exploratorum viam, pugnavit contra illum, et victor existens, duxit ex eo prædam.
     EN: When the Canaanite king of Arad, who dwelt in the south, heard that Israel had come by the explorers' road, he fought against Israel and, gaining victory, carried away plunder from them.

21:2  LA: At Israël voto se Domino obligans, ait : Si tradideris populum istum in manu mea, delebo urbes ejus.
     EN: Israel bound itself by a vow to the Lord, saying: If You deliver this people into my hand, I will destroy their cities.

21:3  LA: Exaudivitque Dominus preces Israël, et tradidit Chananæum, quem ille interfecit subversis urbibus ejus : et vocavit nomen loci illius Horma, id est, anathema.
     EN: The Lord heard Israel's prayers and delivered the Canaanites over to them. Israel killed them and overthrew their cities, and named that place Hormah, that is, Devoted to Destruction.

21:4  LA: Profecti sunt autem et de monte Hor, per viam quæ ducit ad mare Rubrum, ut circumirent terram Edom. Et tædere cœpit populum itineris ac laboris :
     EN: They departed from Mount Hor by the road leading to the Red Sea, to go around the land of Edom. The people began to grow weary of the journey and hardship.

21:5  LA: locutusque contra Deum et Moysen, ait : Cur eduxisti nos de Ægypto, ut moreremur in solitudine ? deest panis, non sunt aquæ : anima nostra jam nauseat super cibo isto levissimo.
     EN: Speaking against God and Moses, they said: Why did you bring us out of Egypt to die in the wilderness? There is no bread and no water; our soul is already sick of this insubstantial food.

21:6  LA: Quam ob rem misit Dominus in populum ignitos serpentes, ad quorum plagas et mortes plurimorum,
     EN: Therefore the Lord sent fiery serpents among the people. At their bites and the deaths of very many,

21:7  LA: venerunt ad Moysen, atque dixerunt : Peccavimus, quia locuti sumus contra Dominum et te : ora ut tollat a nobis serpentes. Oravitque Moyses pro populo,
     EN: the people came to Moses and said: We have sinned, because we spoke against the Lord and you. Pray that He may take the serpents away from us. Moses prayed for the people,

21:8  LA: et locutus est Dominus ad eum : Fac serpentem æneum, et pone eum pro signo : qui percussus aspexerit eum, vivet.
     EN: and the Lord said to him: Make a bronze serpent and set it up as a sign. Anyone bitten who looks at it shall live.
