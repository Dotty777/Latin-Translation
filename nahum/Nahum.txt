NAHUM — LATIN–ENGLISH (CLEMENTINE VULGATE)

Verse numbers follow the Saints (Greek/Hebrew) column; 'vulgate_reference' in verses.json gives the Vulgate's own number where it differs.

NAHUM 1

1:1  LA: Onus Ninive. Liber visionis Nahum Elcesæi.
     EN: The burden concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.

1:2  LA: [Deus æmulator, et ulciscens Dominus : ulciscens Dominus, et habens furorem : ulciscens Dominus in hostes suos, et irascens ipse inimicis suis.
     EN: The Lord is a jealous and avenging God; the Lord avenges and is filled with fury. The Lord avenges Himself on His foes, and He is angry with His enemies.

1:3  LA: Dominus patiens, et magnus fortitudine, et mundans non faciet innocentem. Dominus in tempestate et turbine viæ ejus, et nebulæ pulvis pedum ejus.
     EN: The Lord is patient and great in strength, but when He cleanses, He will not declare the guilty innocent. The Lord's way is in storm and whirlwind, and clouds are the dust of His feet.

1:4  LA: Increpans mare, et exsiccans illud, et omnia flumina ad desertum deducens. Infirmatus est Basan et Carmelus, et flos Libani elanguit.
     EN: He rebukes the sea and dries it up, and turns all the rivers into desert. Bashan and Carmel have withered, and the flower of Lebanon has faded.

1:5  LA: Montes commoti sunt ab eo, et colles desolati sunt : et contremuit terra a facie ejus, et orbis, et omnes habitantes in eo.
     EN: The mountains quake before Him, and the hills are laid waste. The earth trembles at His presence—the world and all who inhabit it.

1:6  LA: Ante faciem indignationis ejus quis stabit ? et quis resistet in ira furoris ejus ? Indignatio ejus effusa est ut ignis, et petræ dissolutæ sunt ab eo.
     EN: Who will stand before His indignation, and who will withstand His furious anger? His indignation is poured out like fire, and rocks are shattered by Him.

1:7  LA: Bonus Dominus, et confortans in die tribulationis, et sciens sperantes in se.
     EN: The Lord is good, giving strength in the day of distress, and He knows those who hope in Him.

1:8  LA: Et in diluvio prætereunte consummationem faciet loci ejus, et inimicos ejus persequentur tenebræ.
     EN: With an overflowing flood He will make an end of its place, and darkness will pursue His enemies.

1:9  LA: Quid cogitatis contra Dominum ? Consummationem ipse faciet : non consurget duplex tribulatio,
     EN: What are you plotting against the Lord? He Himself will make an end; distress will not rise a second time.

1:10  LA: quia sicut spinæ se invicem complectuntur, sic convivium eorum pariter potantium ; consumentur quasi stipula ariditate plena.
      EN: For as thorns intertwine, so is the feast of those who drink together; they will be consumed like utterly dry stubble.

1:11  LA: Ex te exibit cogitans contra Dominum malitiam, mente pertractans prævaricationem.
      EN: From you will come one who plots evil against the Lord, turning treachery over in his mind.

1:12  LA: Hæc dicit Dominus : Si perfecti fuerint, et ita plures, sic quoque attondentur, et pertransibit : afflixi te, et non affligam te ultra.
      EN: Thus says the Lord: Even if they are at full strength and so numerous, they too will be shorn away, and he will pass away. I have afflicted you, but I will afflict you no more.

1:13  LA: Et nunc conteram virgam ejus de dorso tuo, et vincula tua disrumpam.
      EN: And now I will break his rod from your back and tear apart your bonds.

1:14  LA: Et præcipiet super te Dominus ; non seminabitur ex nomine tuo amplius : de domo Dei tui interficiam sculptile, et conflatile ; ponam sepulchrum tuum, quia inhonoratus es.
      EN: The Lord will command concerning you: No more offspring will be sown to bear your name. From the house of your god I will destroy the carved and cast images. I will prepare your grave, for you are dishonored.

NAHUM 2

2:1  LA: Ecce super montes pedes evangelizantis, et annuntiantis pacem. Celebra, Juda, festivitates tuas, et redde vota tua, quia non adjiciet ultra ut pertranseat in te Belial : universus interiit.]
     EN: Look! On the mountains are the feet of one bringing good news and announcing peace. Celebrate your festivals, Judah, and fulfill your vows, for Belial will no longer pass through you; he has utterly perished.

2:2  LA: Ascendit qui dispergat coram te, qui custodiat obsidionem : contemplare viam, conforta lumbos, robora virtutem valde.
     EN: One who scatters has advanced before you, one who maintains the siege. Watch the road, brace your loins, strengthen your might greatly.

2:3  LA: Quia reddidit Dominus superbiam Jacob, sicut superbiam Israël ; quia vastatores dissipaverunt eos, et propagines eorum corruperunt.
     EN: For the Lord has restored the pride of Jacob, like the pride of Israel, because ravagers have scattered them and ruined their branches.

2:4  LA: Clypeus fortium ejus ignitus, viri exercitus in coccineis ; igneæ habenæ currus in die præparationis ejus, et agitatores consopiti sunt.
     EN: The shield of his warriors glows red; the men of his army are dressed in scarlet. The reins of the chariot flash with fire on the day of his preparation, and the drivers have fallen asleep.

2:5  LA: In itineribus conturbati sunt ; quadrigæ collisæ sunt in plateis : aspectus eorum quasi lampades, quasi fulgura discurrentia.
     EN: They rush in confusion along the roads; chariots collide in the squares. Their appearance is like torches, like lightning darting about.

2:6  LA: Recordabitur fortium suorum ; ruent in itineribus suis : velociter ascendent muros ejus, et præparabitur umbraculum.
     EN: He will remember his warriors; they will stumble along their roads. They will quickly climb its walls, and the protective shelter will be prepared.

2:7  LA: Portæ fluviorum apertæ sunt, et templum ad solum dirutum.
     EN: The river gates have been opened, and the temple has been razed to the ground.

2:8  LA: Et miles captivus abductus est, et ancillæ ejus minabantur gementes ut columbæ, murmurantes in cordibus suis.
     EN: The soldier has been led away captive, and her maidservants were driven along, moaning like doves, murmuring in their hearts.

2:9  LA: Et Ninive quasi piscina aquarum aquæ ejus ; ipsi vero fugerunt. State, state ! et non est qui revertatur.
     EN: Nineveh's waters are like a pool of water, yet its people have fled. “Stop! Stop!” But there is no one who turns back.

2:10  LA: Diripite argentum, diripite aurum : et non est finis divitiarum ex omnibus vasis desiderabilibus.
      EN: Plunder the silver! Plunder the gold! There is no end to the wealth of every desirable vessel.

2:11  LA: Dissipata est, et scissa, et dilacerata ; et cor tabescens, et dissolutio geniculorum, et defectio in cunctis renibus, et facies omnium eorum sicut nigredo ollæ.
      EN: She is shattered, split apart, and torn to pieces: hearts melt, knees give way, every waist loses strength, and all their faces are like the blackness of a pot.

2:12  LA: Ubi est habitaculum leonum, et pascua catulorum leonum, ad quam ivit leo ut ingrederetur illuc catulus leonis, et non est qui exterreat ?
      EN: Where is the lions' dwelling and the feeding ground of the young lions, where the lion went so that the lion's cub might enter there, with no one to frighten them?

2:13  LA: Leo cepit sufficienter catulis suis, et necavit leænis suis, et implevit præda speluncas suas, et cubile suum rapina.
      EN: The lion caught enough for his cubs and killed for his lionesses; he filled his caves with prey and his lair with plunder.

2:14  LA: Ecce ego ad te, dicit Dominus exercituum, et succendam usque ad fumum quadrigas tuas, et leunculos tuos comedet gladius, et exterminabo de terra prædam tuam, et non audietur ultra vox nuntiorum tuorum.]
      EN: Look, I am against you, says the Lord of hosts. I will burn your chariots until they go up in smoke, and the sword will devour your young lions. I will cut off your prey from the earth, and the voice of your messengers will be heard no more.

NAHUM 3

3:1  LA: Væ civitas sanguinum, universa mendacii dilaceratione plena ! non recedet a te rapina.
     EN: Woe, city of bloodshed, wholly filled with lies and ravaging! Plunder will not depart from you.

3:2  LA: Vox flagelli, et vox impetus rotæ, et equi frementis, et quadrigæ ferventis, et equitis ascendentis,
     EN: The crack of the whip, the rumble of rushing wheels, the snorting horse, the bounding chariot, the charging horseman,

3:3  LA: et micantis gladii, et fulgurantis hastæ, et multitudinis interfectæ, et gravis ruinæ ; nec est finis cadaverum, et corruent in corporibus suis.
     EN: and the flashing sword, and the glittering spear, and the multitude of the slain, and the heavy ruin! There is no end to the corpses, and they will stumble over their bodies.

3:4  LA: Propter multitudinem fornicationum meretricis speciosæ, et gratæ, et habentis maleficia, quæ vendidit gentes in fornicationibus suis, et familias in maleficiis suis.
     EN: This is because of the many acts of prostitution of the beautiful and pleasing prostitute, the mistress of sorceries, who sold nations through her prostitution and families through her sorceries.

3:5  LA: Ecce ego ad te, dicit Dominus exercituum, et revelabo pudenda tua in facie tua ; et ostendam gentibus nuditatem tuam, et regnis ignominiam tuam.
     EN: Look, I am against you, says the Lord of hosts. I will uncover your private parts before your face, and I will show the nations your nakedness and kingdoms your disgrace.

3:6  LA: Et projiciam super te abominationes, et contumeliis te afficiam, et ponam te in exemplum.
     EN: I will throw abominations upon you, subject you to insults, and make an example of you.

3:7  LA: Et erit : omnis qui viderit te resiliet a te, et dicet : Vastata est Ninive. Quis commovebit super te caput ? unde quæram consolatorem tibi ?
     EN: And everyone who sees you will recoil from you and say, “Nineveh has been laid waste.” Who will shake his head in sorrow over you? Where shall I seek a comforter for you?

3:8  LA: Numquid melior es Alexandria populorum, quæ habitat in fluminibus ? aquæ in circuitu ejus ; cujus divitiæ, mare ; aquæ, muri ejus.
     EN: Are you better than populous Alexandria, which dwells among the rivers, with water all around her, whose wealth is the sea, whose walls are waters?

3:9  LA: Æthiopia fortitudo ejus, et Ægyptus, et non est finis ; Africa et Libyes fuerunt in auxilio tuo.
     EN: Ethiopia was her strength, and Egypt, without limit; Africa and the Libyans were your helpers.

3:10  LA: Sed et ipsa in transmigrationem ducta est in captivitatem : parvuli ejus elisi sunt in capite omnium viarum, et super inclytos ejus miserunt sortem, et omnes optimates ejus confixi sunt in compedibus.
      EN: Yet she too was carried into exile as a captive. Her little children were dashed to pieces at the head of every street; they cast lots for her honored men, and all her nobles were bound in shackles.

3:11  LA: Et tu ergo inebriaberis, et eris despecta : et tu quæres auxilium ab inimico.
      EN: You too, then, will become drunk and be despised; you too will seek help against the enemy.

3:12  LA: Omnes munitiones tuæ sicut ficus cum grossis suis : si concussæ fuerint, cadent in os comedentis.
      EN: All your fortifications are like fig trees with their early figs: if they are shaken, the figs fall into the eater's mouth.

3:13  LA: Ecce populus tuus mulieres in medio tui : inimicis tuis adapertione pandentur portæ terræ tuæ, devorabit ignis vectes tuos.
      EN: Look, your people are women in your midst. The gates of your land will be flung wide open to your enemies; fire will devour your bars.

3:14  LA: Aquam propter obsidionem hauri tibi : exstrue munitiones tuas, intra in lutum, et calca, subigens tene laterem.
      EN: Draw water for the siege; strengthen your fortifications. Go into the clay and tread it; knead it and take up the brick.

3:15  LA: Ibi comedet te ignis, peribis gladio, devorabit te ut bruchus : congregare ut bruchus, multiplicare ut locusta.
      EN: There fire will devour you; you will perish by the sword. It will devour you like the locust grub. Gather together like the locust grub; multiply like the locust.

3:16  LA: Plures fecisti negotiationes tuas quam stellæ sint cæli ; bruchus expansus est, et avolavit.
      EN: You have multiplied your trading beyond the stars of heaven; the locust grub has spread its wings and flown away.

3:17  LA: Custodes tui quasi locustæ, et parvuli tui quasi locustæ locustarum, quæ considunt in sepibus in die frigoris : sol ortus est, et avolaverunt, et non est cognitus locus earum ubi fuerint.
      EN: Your guards are like locusts, and your little ones like swarms of locusts that settle in hedges on a cold day. The sun has risen, and they have flown away; the place where they were is unknown.

3:18  LA: Dormitaverunt pastores tui, rex Assur, sepelientur principes tui : latitavit populus tuus in montibus, et non est qui congreget.
      EN: Your shepherds have fallen asleep, king of Assyria; your princes will be buried. Your people have hidden in the mountains, and there is no one to gather them.

3:19  LA: Non est obscura contritio tua ; pessima est plaga tua. Omnes qui audierunt auditionem tuam compresserunt manum super te : quia super quem non transiit malitia tua semper ?]
      EN: Your crushing is plain to see; your wound is grievous. All who have heard the report about you have clapped their hands over you, for upon whom has your wickedness not passed continually?
