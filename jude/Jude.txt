JUDE 1

1:1  LA: Judas Jesu Christi servus, frater autem Jacobi, his qui sunt in Deo Patre dilectis, et Christo Jesu conservatis, et vocatis.
     EN: Jude, a servant of Jesus Christ and brother of James, to those who are beloved in God the Father, kept safe for Christ Jesus, and called:

1:2  LA: Misericordia vobis, et pax, et caritas adimpleatur.
     EN: may mercy, peace, and love be yours in fullness.

1:3  LA: Carissimi, omnem sollicitudinem faciens scribendi vobis de communi vestra salute, necesse habui scribere vobis: deprecans supercertari semel traditæ sanctis fidei.
     EN: Beloved, while making every effort to write to you about your common salvation, I found it necessary to write and urge you to contend earnestly for the faith once handed down to the saints.

1:4  LA: Subintroierunt enim quidam homines (qui olim præscripti sunt in hoc judicium) impii, Dei nostri gratiam transferentes in luxuriam, et solum Dominatorem, et Dominum nostrum Jesum Christum negantes.
     EN: For certain people have slipped in unnoticed, who were long ago designated for this judgment: ungodly people who turn the grace of our God into licentiousness and deny our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ.

1:5  LA: Commonere autem vos volo, scientes semel omnia, quoniam Jesus populum de terra Ægypti salvans, secundo eos, qui non crediderunt, perdidit:
     EN: I wish to remind you, though you once knew all this, that Jesus, after saving a people from the land of Egypt, afterward destroyed those who did not believe.

1:6  LA: angelos vero, qui non servaverunt suum principatum, sed dereliquerunt suum domicilium, in judicium magni diei, vinculis æternis sub caligine reservavit.
     EN: And the angels who did not keep their own dominion but abandoned their dwelling, he has kept in everlasting chains under darkness for the judgment of the great day.

1:7  LA: Sicut Sodoma, et Gomorrha, et finitimæ civitates simili modo exfornicatæ, et abeuntes post carnem alteram, factæ sunt exemplum, ignis æterni pœnam sustinentes.
     EN: Likewise Sodom and Gomorrah and the neighboring cities, which committed sexual immorality in a similar way and went after other flesh, have become an example, undergoing the punishment of eternal fire.

1:8  LA: Similiter et hi carnem quidem maculant, dominationem autem spernunt, majestatem autem blasphemant.
     EN: In the same way these people defile the flesh, reject dominion, and blaspheme majesty.

1:9  LA: Cum Michaël Archangelus cum diabolo disputans altercaretur de Moysi corpore, non est ausus judicium inferre blasphemiæ: sed dixit: Imperet tibi Dominus.
     EN: But when Michael the archangel, disputing with the devil, argued about the body of Moses, he did not dare to bring a blasphemous judgment against him, but said, “May the Lord command you.”

1:10  LA: Hi autem quæcumque quidem ignorant, blasphemant: quæcumque autem naturaliter, tamquam muta animalia, norunt, in his corrumpuntur.
      EN: Yet these people blaspheme whatever they do not understand; and in what they know by nature, like speechless animals, they corrupt themselves.

1:11  LA: Væ illis, quia in via Cain abierunt, et errore Balaam mercede effusi sunt, et in contradictione Core perierunt!
      EN: Woe to them! They have gone the way of Cain, poured themselves into Balaam’s error for a reward, and perished in Korah’s rebellion.

1:12  LA: Hi sunt in epulis suis maculæ, convivantes sine timore, semetipsos pascentes, nubes sine aqua, quæ a ventis circumferentur, arbores autumnales, infructuosæ, bis mortuæ, eradicatæ,
      EN: These people are stains at their feasts, dining without fear and feeding themselves: clouds without water, driven about by winds; autumn trees without fruit, twice dead, uprooted;

1:13  LA: fluctus feri maris, despumantes suas confusiones, sidera errantia: quibus procella tenebrarum servata est in æternum.
      EN: wild waves of the sea, foaming out their own shame; wandering stars, for whom a storm of darkness has been reserved forever.

1:14  LA: Prophetavit autem et de his septimus ab Adam Enoch, dicens: Ecce venit Dominus in sanctis millibus suis
      EN: Enoch, the seventh from Adam, also prophesied about them, saying, “Behold, the Lord has come with his holy thousands

1:15  LA: facere judicium contra omnes, et arguere omnes impios de omnibus operibus impietatis eorum, quibus impie egerunt, et de omnibus duris, quæ locuti sunt contra Deum peccatores impii.
      EN: to execute judgment against all and convict all the ungodly of all the ungodly deeds they have wickedly done, and of all the harsh things ungodly sinners have spoken against God.”

1:16  LA: Hi sunt murmuratores querulosi, secundum desideria sua ambulantes, et os eorum loquitur superba, mirantes personas quæstus causa.
      EN: These people are grumblers and complainers, walking according to their own desires. Their mouths speak arrogant things, and they flatter people for gain.

1:17  LA: Vos autem carissimi, memores estote verborum, quæ prædicta sunt ab apostolis Domini nostri Jesu Christi,
      EN: But you, beloved, remember the words spoken beforehand by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ,

1:18  LA: qui dicebant vobis, quoniam in novissimo tempore venient illusores, secundum desideria sua ambulantes in impietatibus.
      EN: who told you that in the last time mockers would come, walking according to their own desires in ungodliness.

1:19  LA: Hi sunt, qui segregant semetipsos, animales, Spiritum non habentes.
      EN: These are the people who separate themselves, living by natural impulses and not having the Spirit.

1:20  LA: Vos autem carissimi superædificantes vosmetipsos sanctissimæ vestræ fidei, in Spiritu Sancto orantes,
      EN: But you, beloved, building yourselves up in your most holy faith and praying in the Holy Spirit,

1:21  LA: vosmetipsos in dilectione Dei servate, exspectantes misericordiam Domini nostri Jesu Christi in vitam æternam.
      EN: keep yourselves in the love of God, awaiting the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal life.

1:22  LA: Et hos quidem arguite judicatos:
      EN: Rebuke some who have been judged;

1:23  LA: illos vero salvate, de igne rapientes. Aliis autem miseremini in timore: odientes et eam, quæ carnalis est, maculatam tunicam.
      EN: but save others, snatching them from the fire. Have mercy on still others with fear, hating even the garment stained by the flesh.

1:24  LA: Ei autem qui potens est vos conservare sine peccato et constituere ante conspectum gloriæ suæ immaculatos in exsultatione in adventu Domini nostri Jesu Christi,
      EN: Now to him who is able to keep you without sin and to set you unblemished and rejoicing before the presence of his glory at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ,

1:25  LA: soli Deo Salvatori nostro, per Jesum Christum Dominum nostrum, gloria et magnificentia, imperium et potestas ante omne sæculum, et nunc, et in omnia sæcula sæculorum. Amen.
      EN: to the only God our Savior, through Jesus Christ our Lord, be glory and magnificence, dominion and power, before every age, now, and through all ages of ages. Amen.
