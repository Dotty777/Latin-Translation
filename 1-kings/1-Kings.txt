1 KINGS 1

1:1  LA: Et rex David senuerat, habebatque ætatis plurimos dies : cumque operiretur vestibus, non calefiebat.
     EN: King David had grown old and was advanced in years; although they covered him with clothes, he could not get warm.

1:2  LA: Dixerunt ergo ei servi sui : Quæramus domino nostro regi adolescentulam virginem, et stet coram rege, et foveat eum, dormiatque in sinu suo, et calefaciat dominum nostrum regem.
     EN: So his servants said to him, “Let us look for a young virgin for our lord the king. Let her stand before the king and care for him, and sleep in his embrace, to warm our lord the king.”

1:3  LA: Quæsierunt igitur adolescentulam speciosam in omnibus finibus Israël, et invenerunt Abisag Sunamitidem, et adduxerunt eam ad regem.
     EN: They therefore searched for a beautiful young woman throughout the territory of Israel, found Abishag the Shunammite, and brought her to the king.

1:4  LA: Erat autem puella pulchra nimis, dormiebatque cum rege, et ministrabat ei : rex vero non cognovit eam.
     EN: The girl was very beautiful; she slept with the king and attended to him, but the king did not know her sexually.

1:5  LA: Adonias autem filius Haggith elevabatur, dicens : Ego regnabo. Fecitque sibi currus et equites, et quinquaginta viros qui currerent ante eum.
     EN: Now Adonijah son of Haggith exalted himself, saying, “I will be king.” He provided himself with chariots and horsemen and fifty men to run before him.

1:6  LA: Nec corripuit eum pater suus aliquando, dicens : Quare hoc fecisti ? Erat autem et ipse pulcher valde, secundus natu post Absalom.
     EN: His father had never rebuked him by saying, “Why have you done this?” He too was very handsome, born next after Absalom.

1:7  LA: Et sermo ei cum Joab filio Sarviæ, et cum Abiathar sacerdote, qui adjuvabant partes Adoniæ.
     EN: He conferred with Joab son of Zeruiah and with Abiathar the priest, who supported Adonijah's side.

1:8  LA: Sadoc vero sacerdos, et Banaias filius Jojadæ, et Nathan propheta, et Semei et Rei, et robur exercitus David, non erat cum Adonia.
     EN: But Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei and Rei, and the strength of David's army were not with Adonijah.

1:9  LA: Immolatis ergo Adonias arietibus et vitulis, et universis pinguibus, juxta lapidem Zoheleth, qui erat vicinus fonti Rogel, vocavit universos fratres suos filios regis, et omnes viros Juda servos regis.
     EN: Adonijah slaughtered rams, calves, and all kinds of fattened animals beside the stone of Zoheleth, near the spring of Rogel, and invited all his brothers, the king's sons, and all the men of Judah who served the king.

1:10  LA: Nathan autem prophetam, et Banaiam, et robustos quosque, et Salomonem fratrem suum non vocavit.
      EN: But he did not invite Nathan the prophet, Benaiah, the mighty men, or his brother Solomon.

1:11  LA: Dixit itaque Nathan ad Bethsabee matrem Salomonis : Num audisti quod regnaverit Adonias filius Haggith, et dominus noster David hoc ignorat ?
      EN: Then Nathan said to Bathsheba, Solomon's mother, “Have you heard that Adonijah son of Haggith has become king, while our lord David knows nothing about it?

1:12  LA: Nunc ergo veni, accipe consilium a me, et salva animam tuam, filiique tui Salomonis.
      EN: Now come, take my advice, and save your own life and the life of your son Solomon.

1:13  LA: Vade, et ingredere ad regem David, et dic ei : Nonne tu, domine mi rex, jurasti mihi ancillæ tuæ, dicens : Salomon filius tuus regnabit post me, et ipse sedebit in solio meo ? quare ergo regnat Adonias ?
      EN: Go in to King David and say to him, ‘Did you not swear to me, your servant, my lord the king, saying: Your son Solomon will reign after me, and he will sit on my throne? Why, then, is Adonijah reigning?’

1:14  LA: Et adhuc ibi te loquente cum rege, ego veniam post te, et complebo sermones tuos.
      EN: While you are still there speaking with the king, I will come in after you and confirm your words.”

1:15  LA: Ingressa est itaque Bethsabee ad regem in cubiculum : rex autem senuerat nimis, et Abisag Sunamitis ministrabat ei.
      EN: So Bathsheba went to the king in his chamber. The king was very old, and Abishag the Shunammite was attending to him.

1:16  LA: Inclinavit se Bethsabee, et adoravit regem. Ad quam rex : Quid tibi, inquit, vis ?
      EN: Bathsheba bowed down and paid homage to the king. The king said to her, “What do you want?”

1:17  LA: Quæ respondens, ait : Domine mi, tu jurasti per Dominum Deum tuum ancillæ tuæ : Salomon filius tuus regnabit post me, et ipse sedebit in solio meo.
      EN: She answered, “My lord, you swore to your servant by the Lord your God: ‘Your son Solomon will reign after me, and he will sit on my throne.’

1:18  LA: Et ecce nunc Adonias regnat, te, domine mi rex, ignorante.
      EN: But now Adonijah is reigning, and you, my lord the king, know nothing about it.

1:19  LA: Mactavit boves, et pinguia quæque, et arietes plurimos, et vocavit omnes filios regis, Abiathar quoque sacerdotem, et Joab principem militiæ : Salomonem autem servum tuum non vocavit.
      EN: He has slaughtered oxen, fattened animals, and many rams, and invited all the king's sons, Abiathar the priest, and Joab the commander of the army; but he did not invite your servant Solomon.

1:20  LA: Verumtamen, domine mi rex, in te oculi respiciunt totius Israël, ut indices eis quis sedere debeat in solio tuo, domine mi rex, post te.
      EN: Yet, my lord the king, the eyes of all Israel look to you to tell them who should sit on your throne after you, my lord the king.

1:21  LA: Eritque, cum dormierit dominus meus rex cum patribus suis, erimus ego et filius meus Salomon peccatores.
      EN: When my lord the king sleeps with his fathers, my son Solomon and I will be treated as offenders.”

1:22  LA: Adhuc illa loquente cum rege, Nathan propheta venit.
      EN: While she was still speaking with the king, Nathan the prophet came.

1:23  LA: Et nuntiaverunt regi, dicentes : Adest Nathan propheta. Cumque introisset in conspectu regis, et adorasset eum pronus in terram,
      EN: They announced to the king, “Nathan the prophet is here.” He entered the king's presence and bowed before him with his face to the ground.

1:24  LA: dixit Nathan : Domine mi rex, tu dixisti : Adonias regnet post me, et ipse sedeat super thronum meum ?
      EN: Nathan said, “My lord the king, did you say, ‘Let Adonijah reign after me, and let him sit on my throne’?

1:25  LA: Quia descendit hodie, et immolavit boves, et pinguia, et arietes plurimos, et vocavit universos filios regis et principes exercitus, Abiathar quoque sacerdotem, illisque vescentibus et bibentibus coram eo, et dicentibus : Vivat rex Adonias :
      EN: For today he has gone down and slaughtered oxen, fattened animals, and many rams, and invited all the king's sons, the army commanders, and Abiathar the priest. They are eating and drinking before him and saying, ‘Long live King Adonijah!’

1:26  LA: me servum tuum, et Sadoc sacerdotem, et Banaiam filium Jojadæ, et Salomonem famulum tuum non vocavit.
      EN: But he did not invite me, your servant, or Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, or your servant Solomon.

1:27  LA: Numquid a domino meo rege exivit hoc verbum, et mihi non indicasti servo tuo quis sessurus esset super thronum domini mei regis post eum ?
      EN: Has this order come from my lord the king, without your telling me, your servant, who is to sit on my lord the king's throne after him?”

1:28  LA: Et respondit rex David, dicens : Vocate ad me Bethsabee. Quæ cum fuisset ingressa coram rege, et stetisset ante eum,
      EN: King David answered, “Call Bathsheba to me.” She came into the king's presence and stood before him.

1:29  LA: juravit rex, et ait : Vivit Dominus, qui eruit animam meam de omni angustia,
      EN: The king swore, saying, “As the Lord lives, who has rescued my life from every distress,

1:30  LA: quia sicut juravi tibi per Dominum Deum Israël, dicens : Salomon filius tuus regnabit post me, et ipse sedebit super solium meum pro me : sic faciam hodie.
      EN: just as I swore to you by the Lord, the God of Israel, saying, ‘Your son Solomon will reign after me, and he will sit on my throne in my place,’ so I will do today.”

1:31  LA: Summissoque Bethsabee in terram vultu, adoravit regem, dicens : Vivat dominus meus David in æternum.
      EN: Bathsheba lowered her face to the ground and paid homage to the king, saying, “May my lord David live forever!”

1:32  LA: Dixit quoque rex David : Vocate mihi Sadoc sacerdotem, et Nathan prophetam, et Banaiam filium Jojadæ. Qui cum ingressi fuissent coram rege,
      EN: King David also said, “Call Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada to me.” When they had come before the king,

1:33  LA: dixit ad eos : Tollite vobiscum servos domini vestri, et imponite Salomonem filium meum super mulam meam, et ducite eum in Gihon.
      EN: he said to them, “Take your lord's servants with you, set my son Solomon on my mule, and lead him to Gihon.

1:34  LA: Et ungat eum ibi Sadoc sacerdos et Nathan propheta in regem super Israël : et canetis buccina, atque dicetis : Vivat rex Salomon.
      EN: There let Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anoint him king over Israel. Sound the trumpet and say, ‘Long live King Solomon!’

1:35  LA: Et ascendetis post eum, et veniet, et sedebit super solium meum, et ipse regnabit pro me : illique præcipiam ut sit dux super Israël et super Judam.
      EN: Then go up after him; he shall come and sit on my throne and reign in my place. I will appoint him ruler over Israel and Judah.”

1:36  LA: Et respondit Banaias filius Jojadæ regi, dicens : Amen : sic loquatur Dominus Deus domini mei regis.
      EN: Benaiah son of Jehoiada answered the king, “Amen! May the Lord, the God of my lord the king, speak so.

1:37  LA: Quomodo fuit Dominus cum domino meo rege, sic sit cum Salomone, et sublimius faciat solium ejus a solio domini mei regis David.
      EN: As the Lord has been with my lord the king, so may He be with Solomon, and make his throne greater than the throne of my lord King David.”

1:38  LA: Descendit ergo Sadoc sacerdos, et Nathan propheta, et Banaias filius Jojadæ, et Cerethi, et Phelethi : et imposuerunt Salomonem super mulam regis David, et adduxerunt eum in Gihon.
      EN: So Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites and Pelethites went down. They set Solomon on King David's mule and brought him to Gihon.

1:39  LA: Sumpsitque Sadoc sacerdos cornu olei de tabernaculo, et unxit Salomonem : et cecinerunt buccina, et dixit omnis populus : Vivat rex Salomon.
      EN: Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the tabernacle and anointed Solomon. They sounded the trumpet, and all the people said, “Long live King Solomon!”

1:40  LA: Et ascendit universa multitudo post eum, et populus canentium tibiis, et lætantium gaudio magno : et insonuit terra a clamore eorum.
      EN: The whole multitude went up after him, playing pipes and rejoicing with great joy, and the earth resounded with their shouting.

1:41  LA: Audivit autem Adonias, et omnes qui invitati fuerant ab eo : jamque convivium finitum erat : sed et Joab, audita voce tubæ, ait : Quid sibi vult clamor civitatis tumultuantis ?
      EN: Adonijah and all those he had invited heard it, just as the feast had ended. Joab too heard the trumpet and said, “What does this uproar in the city mean?”

1:42  LA: Adhuc illo loquente, Jonathas filius Abiathar sacerdotis venit : cui dixit Adonias : Ingredere, quia vir fortis es, et bona nuntians.
      EN: While he was still speaking, Jonathan son of Abiathar the priest arrived. Adonijah said to him, “Come in, for you are a valiant man and bring good news.”

1:43  LA: Responditque Jonathas Adoniæ : Nequaquam : dominus enim noster rex David regem constituit Salomonem :
      EN: Jonathan answered Adonijah, “By no means! Our lord King David has made Solomon king.

1:44  LA: misitque cum eo Sadoc sacerdotem, et Nathan prophetam, et Banaiam filium Jojadæ, et Cerethi, et Phelethi, et imposuerunt eum super mulam regis.
      EN: He sent Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites and Pelethites with him, and they set him on the king's mule.

1:45  LA: Unxeruntque eum Sadoc sacerdos et Nathan propheta regem in Gihon : et ascenderunt inde lætantes, et insonuit civitas : hæc est vox quam audistis.
      EN: Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anointed him king at Gihon. They came up from there rejoicing, and the city resounded. That is the sound you heard.

1:46  LA: Sed et Salomon sedet super solium regni.
      EN: Moreover, Solomon is sitting on the royal throne.

1:47  LA: Et ingressi servi regis benedixerunt domino nostro regi David, dicentes : Amplificet Deus nomen Salomonis super nomen tuum, et magnificet thronus ejus super thronum tuum. Et adoravit rex in lectulo suo :
      EN: The king's servants also came in to bless our lord King David, saying, ‘May God make Solomon's name greater than your name, and his throne more glorious than your throne.’ And the king bowed in worship on his bed

1:48  LA: et locutus est : Benedictus Dominus Deus Israël, qui dedit hodie sedentem in solio meo, videntibus oculis meis.
      EN: and said, ‘Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, who today has provided someone to sit on my throne while my own eyes can see it.’”

1:49  LA: Territi sunt ergo, et surrexerunt omnes qui invitati fuerant ab Adonia, et ivit unusquisque in viam suam.
      EN: Then all those invited by Adonijah were terrified and got up, and each went his own way.

1:50  LA: Adonias autem timens Salomonem, surrexit, et abiit, tenuitque cornu altaris.
      EN: But Adonijah, afraid of Solomon, got up and went away and took hold of the horn of the altar.

1:51  LA: Et nuntiaverunt Salomoni, dicentes : Ecce Adonias timens regem Salomonem, tenuit cornu altaris, dicens : Juret mihi rex Salomon hodie, quod non interficiat servum suum gladio.
      EN: It was reported to Solomon, “Adonijah, afraid of King Solomon, has taken hold of the horn of the altar, saying, ‘Let King Solomon swear to me today that he will not kill his servant with the sword.’”

1:52  LA: Dixitque Salomon : Si fuerit vir bonus, non cadet ne unus quidem capillus ejus in terram : sin autem malum inventum fuerit in eo, morietur.
      EN: Solomon said, “If he proves a good man, not one hair of his head will fall to the ground; but if evil is found in him, he will die.”

1:53  LA: Misit ergo rex Salomon, et eduxit eum ab altari : et ingressus adoravit regem Salomonem : dixitque ei Salomon : Vade in domum tuam.
      EN: So King Solomon sent for him and had him brought away from the altar. He came in and paid homage to King Solomon, and Solomon said to him, “Go to your house.”

1 KINGS 2

2:1  LA: Appropinquaverunt autem dies David ut moreretur : præcepitque Salomoni filio suo, dicens :
     EN: As the time of David's death drew near, he charged his son Solomon, saying:

2:2  LA: Ego ingredior viam universæ terræ : confortare, et esto vir.
     EN: “I am going the way of all the earth. Be strong and be a man.

2:3  LA: Et observa custodias Domini Dei tui, ut ambules in viis ejus : ut custodias cæremonias ejus, et præcepta ejus, et judicia, et testimonia, sicut scriptum est in lege Moysi : ut intelligas universa quæ facis, et quocumque te verteris :
     EN: Keep the observances of the Lord your God, walking in His ways and keeping His ceremonies, commandments, judgments, and testimonies, as written in the law of Moses, so that you may understand everything you do and wherever you turn,

2:4  LA: ut confirmet Dominus sermones suos quos locutus est de me, dicens : Si custodierint filii tui vias suas, et ambulaverint coram me in veritate, in omni corde suo et in omni anima sua, non auferetur tibi vir de solio Israël.
     EN: and so that the Lord may confirm the words He spoke concerning me: ‘If your sons guard their ways and walk before Me faithfully with all their heart and all their soul, you will never lack a man on the throne of Israel.’

2:5  LA: Tu quoque nosti quæ fecerit mihi Joab filius Sarviæ, quæ fecerit duobus principibus exercitus Israël, Abner filio Ner, et Amasæ filio Jether : quos occidit, et effudit sanguinem belli in pace, et posuit cruorem prælii in balteo suo qui erat circa lumbos ejus, et in calceamento suo quod erat in pedibus ejus.
     EN: You also know what Joab son of Zeruiah did to me, what he did to the two commanders of Israel's army, Abner son of Ner and Amasa son of Jether. He killed them, shedding the blood of war in peacetime and putting the blood of battle on the belt around his waist and the sandals on his feet.

2:6  LA: Facies ergo juxta sapientiam tuam, et non deduces canitiem ejus pacifice ad inferos.
     EN: Act, therefore, according to your wisdom, and do not let his gray hair go down peacefully to the underworld.

2:7  LA: Sed et filiis Berzellai Galaaditis reddes gratiam, eruntque comedentes in mensa tua : occurrerunt enim mihi quando fugiebam a facie Absalom fratris tui.
     EN: But show kindness to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite, and let them eat at your table, for they came to meet me when I fled from your brother Absalom.

2:8  LA: Habes quoque apud te Semei filium Gera filii Jemini de Bahurim, qui maledixit mihi maledictione pessima quando ibam ad castra : sed quia descendit mihi in occursum cum transirem Jordanem, et juravi ei per Dominum, dicens : Non te interficiam gladio :
     EN: You also have with you Shimei son of Gera, son of Jemini, from Bahurim, who cursed me with a most bitter curse when I was going to the camp. But because he came down to meet me when I crossed the Jordan, I swore to him by the Lord, saying, ‘I will not kill you with the sword.’

2:9  LA: tu noli pati eum esse innoxium. Vir autem sapiens es, ut scias quæ facies ei : deducesque canos ejus cum sanguine ad inferos.
     EN: Do not let him go unpunished. You are a wise man and will know what to do to him; bring his gray hair down to the underworld in blood.”

2:10  LA: Dormivit igitur David cum patribus suis, et sepultus est in civitate David.
      EN: Then David slept with his fathers and was buried in the City of David.

2:11  LA: Dies autem quibus regnavit David super Israël, quadraginta anni sunt : in Hebron regnavit septem annis ; in Jerusalem, triginta tribus.
      EN: The time David reigned over Israel was forty years: he reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three in Jerusalem.

2:12  LA: Salomon autem sedit super thronum David patris sui, et firmatum est regnum ejus nimis.
      EN: Solomon sat on the throne of his father David, and his kingdom was firmly established.

2:13  LA: Et ingressus est Adonias filius Haggith ad Bethsabee matrem Salomonis. Quæ dixit ei : Pacificusne est ingressus tuus ? Qui respondit : Pacificus.
      EN: Adonijah son of Haggith came to Bathsheba, Solomon's mother. She said to him, “Do you come in peace?” He answered, “In peace.”

2:14  LA: Addiditque : Sermo mihi est ad te. Cui ait : Loquere. Et ille :
      EN: He added, “I have something to say to you.” She said, “Speak.” And he said,

2:15  LA: Tu, inquit, nosti, quia meum erat regnum, et me præposuerat omnis Israël sibi in regem : sed translatum est regnum, et factum est fratris mei : a Domino enim constitutum est ei.
      EN: “You know the kingdom was mine, and all Israel had chosen me to be their king. But the kingdom has been transferred and has become my brother's, for the Lord has appointed it to him.

2:16  LA: Nunc ergo petitionem unam precor a te : ne confundas faciem meam. Quæ dixit ad eum : Loquere.
      EN: Now I ask one favor of you; do not put me to shame.” She said to him, “Speak.”

2:17  LA: Et ille ait : Precor ut dicas Salomoni regi (neque enim negare tibi quidquam potest) ut det mihi Abisag Sunamitidem uxorem.
      EN: He said, “Please ask King Solomon—he cannot refuse you anything—to give me Abishag the Shunammite as my wife.”

2:18  LA: Et ait Bethsabee : Bene : ego loquar pro te regi.
      EN: Bathsheba said, “Very well; I will speak to the king for you.”

2:19  LA: Venit ergo Bethsabee ad regem Salomonem ut loqueretur ei pro Adonia : et surrexit rex in occursum ejus, adoravitque eam, et sedit super thronum suum : positusque est thronus matri regis, quæ sedit ad dexteram ejus.
      EN: So Bathsheba came to King Solomon to speak to him for Adonijah. The king rose to meet her, bowed before her, and sat on his throne. A throne was placed for the king's mother, and she sat at his right hand.

2:20  LA: Dixitque ei : Petitionem unam parvulam ego deprecor a te : ne confundas faciem meam. Et dixit ei rex : Pete, mater mea : neque enim fas est ut avertam faciem tuam.
      EN: She said to him, “I ask one small favor of you; do not put me to shame.” The king said to her, “Ask, my mother, for it would not be right for me to turn you away.”

2:21  LA: Quæ ait : Detur Abisag Sunamitis Adoniæ fratri tuo uxor.
      EN: She said, “Let Abishag the Shunammite be given as a wife to your brother Adonijah.”

2:22  LA: Responditque rex Salomon, et dixit matri suæ : Quare postulas Abisag Sunamitidem Adoniæ ? postula ei et regnum : ipse est enim frater meus major me, et habet Abiathar sacerdotem, et Joab filium Sarviæ.
      EN: King Solomon answered his mother, “Why do you ask for Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? Ask for the kingdom for him as well! He is my older brother, and has Abiathar the priest and Joab son of Zeruiah.”

2:23  LA: Juravit itaque rex Salomon per Dominum, dicens : Hæc faciat mihi Deus, et hæc addat, quia contra animam suam locutus est Adonias verbum hoc.
      EN: Then King Solomon swore by the Lord, saying, “May God do these things to me, and add to them, if Adonijah has not spoken this word at the cost of his life!

2:24  LA: Et nunc vivit Dominus, qui firmavit me, et collocavit me super solium David patris mei, et qui fecit mihi domum, sicut locutus est, quia hodie occidetur Adonias.
      EN: And now, as the Lord lives, who has established me and set me on the throne of my father David, and made me a house as He promised, Adonijah shall be killed today.”

2:25  LA: Misitque rex Salomon per manum Banaiæ filii Jojadæ, qui interfecit eum, et mortuus est.
      EN: King Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, who struck him down, and he died.

2:26  LA: Abiathar quoque sacerdoti dixit rex : Vade in Anathoth ad agrum tuum : equidem vir mortis es : sed hodie te non interficiam, quia portasti arcam Domini Dei coram David patre meo, et sustinuisti laborem in omnibus in quibus laboravit pater meus.
      EN: The king also said to Abiathar the priest, “Go to your estate at Anathoth. You certainly deserve death, but I will not put you to death today, because you carried the ark of the Lord God before my father David and shared the hardships in everything my father endured.”

2:27  LA: Ejecit ergo Salomon Abiathar ut non esset sacerdos Domini, ut impleretur sermo Domini quem locutus est super domum Heli in Silo.
      EN: So Solomon expelled Abiathar from being the Lord's priest, fulfilling the word the Lord had spoken concerning the house of Eli at Shiloh.

2:28  LA: Venit autem nuntius ad Joab, quod Joab declinasset post Adoniam, et post Salomonem non declinasset : fugit ergo Joab in tabernaculum Domini, et apprehendit cornu altaris.
      EN: The news came to Joab, who had sided with Adonijah and had not sided with Solomon. So Joab fled to the Lord's tabernacle and took hold of the horn of the altar.

2:29  LA: Nuntiatumque est regi Salomoni quod fugisset Joab in tabernaculum Domini, et esset juxta altare : misitque Salomon Banaiam filium Jojadæ, dicens : Vade, interfice eum.
      EN: King Solomon was told that Joab had fled to the Lord's tabernacle and was beside the altar. Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, saying, “Go, kill him.”

2:30  LA: Et venit Banaias ad tabernaculum Domini, et dixit ei : Hæc dicit rex : Egredere. Qui ait : Non egrediar, sed hic moriar. Renuntiavit Banaias regi sermonem, dicens : Hæc locutus est Joab, et hæc respondit mihi.
      EN: Benaiah came to the Lord's tabernacle and said to him, “The king says: Come out.” He answered, “I will not come out; I will die here.” Benaiah reported this to the king, saying, “This is what Joab said, and this is how he answered me.”

2:31  LA: Dixitque ei rex : Fac sicut locutus est, et interfice eum, et sepeli : et amovebis sanguinem innocentem qui effusus est a Joab, a me, et a domo patris mei.
      EN: The king said to him, “Do as he has said: kill him and bury him. Thus you will remove from me and my father's house the innocent blood Joab shed.

1 KINGS 3

3:1  LA: Confirmatum est igitur regnum in manu Salomonis, et affinitate conjunctus est Pharaoni regi Ægypti : accepit namque filiam ejus, et adduxit in civitatem David, donec compleret ædificans domum suam, et domum Domini, et murum Jerusalem per circuitum.
     EN: Thus the kingdom was established in Solomon's hand. He allied himself by marriage with Pharaoh king of Egypt, taking his daughter and bringing her to the City of David until he finished building his own house, the Lord's house, and the wall around Jerusalem.

3:2  LA: Attamen populus immolabat in excelsis : non enim ædificatum erat templum nomini Domini usque in diem illum.
     EN: The people, however, were sacrificing at the high places, for no temple had yet been built for the Lord's name.

3:3  LA: Dilexit autem Salomon Dominum, ambulans in præceptis David patris sui, excepto quod in excelsis immolabat, et accendebat thymiama.
     EN: Solomon loved the Lord, walking in the commandments of his father David, except that he sacrificed and burned incense at the high places.

3:4  LA: Abiit itaque in Gabaon, ut immolaret ibi : illud quippe erat excelsum maximum : mille hostias in holocaustum obtulit Salomon super altare illud in Gabaon.
     EN: He went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, for that was the greatest high place. Solomon offered a thousand victims as burnt offerings on that altar at Gibeon.

3:5  LA: Apparuit autem Dominus Salomoni per somnium nocte, dicens : Postula quod vis ut dem tibi.
     EN: The Lord appeared to Solomon in a dream at night, saying, “Ask what you wish Me to give you.”

3:6  LA: Et ait Salomon : Tu fecisti cum servo tuo David patre meo misericordiam magnam, sicut ambulavit in conspectu tuo in veritate et justitia, et recto corde tecum : custodisti ei misericordiam tuam grandem, et dedisti ei filium sedentem super thronum ejus, sicut est hodie.
     EN: Solomon said, “You showed great mercy to Your servant David my father, as he walked before You in faithfulness and righteousness and with an upright heart toward You. You preserved Your great mercy for him and gave him a son to sit on his throne, as it is today.

3:7  LA: Et nunc Domine Deus, tu regnare fecisti servum tuum pro David patre meo : ego autem sum puer parvulus, et ignorans egressum et introitum meum.
     EN: And now, Lord God, You have made Your servant king in place of my father David. But I am a little child and do not know how to go out or come in.

3:8  LA: Et servus tuus in medio est populi quem elegisti, populi infiniti, qui numerari et supputari non potest præ multitudine.
     EN: Your servant is among the people You have chosen, a people without number, who cannot be counted or reckoned because of their multitude.

3:9  LA: Dabis ergo servo tuo cor docile, ut populum tuum judicare possit, et discernere inter bonum et malum. Quis enim poterit judicare populum istum, populum tuum hunc multum ?
     EN: Give Your servant, therefore, a teachable heart, so that he may judge Your people and distinguish between good and evil. For who can judge this people, this great people of Yours?”

3:10  LA: Placuit ergo sermo coram Domino, quod Salomon postulasset hujuscemodi rem.
      EN: It pleased the Lord that Solomon had asked for such a thing.

3:11  LA: Et dixit Dominus Salomoni : Quia postulasti verbum hoc, et non petisti tibi dies multos, nec divitias, aut animas inimicorum tuorum, sed postulasti tibi sapientiam ad discernendum judicium :
      EN: The Lord said to Solomon, “Because you have asked for this, and have not asked for long life for yourself, or riches, or the lives of your enemies, but have asked for wisdom to discern judgment,

3:12  LA: ecce feci tibi secundum sermones tuos, et dedi tibi cor sapiens et intelligens, in tantum ut nullus ante te similis tui fuerit, nec post te surrecturus sit.
      EN: behold, I have done according to your words and given you a wise and understanding heart, so that no one like you has existed before you, nor will arise after you.

3:13  LA: Sed et hæc quæ non postulasti, dedi tibi : divitias scilicet, et gloriam, ut nemo fuerit similis tui in regibus cunctis retro diebus.
      EN: I have also given you what you did not ask for: riches and glory, so that no one among all the kings in days past has been like you.

3:14  LA: Si autem ambulaveris in viis meis, et custodieris præcepta mea et mandata mea, sicut ambulavit pater tuus, longos faciam dies tuos.
      EN: And if you walk in My ways and keep My commandments and commands, as your father walked, I will lengthen your days.”

3:15  LA: Igitur evigilavit Salomon, et intellexit quod esset somnium : cumque venisset Jerusalem, stetit coram arca fœderis Domini, et obtulit holocausta, et fecit victimas pacificas, et grande convivium universis famulis suis.
      EN: Solomon awoke and realized it had been a dream. When he came to Jerusalem, he stood before the ark of the Lord's covenant, offered burnt offerings and peace offerings, and made a great feast for all his servants.

3:16  LA: Tunc venerunt duæ mulieres meretrices ad regem, steteruntque coram eo :
      EN: Then two women who were prostitutes came to the king and stood before him.

3:17  LA: quarum una ait : Obsecro, mi domine : ego et mulier hæc habitabamus in domo una, et peperi apud eam in cubiculo.
      EN: One of them said, “Please, my lord, this woman and I lived in one house, and I gave birth while she was there in the room.

3:18  LA: Tertia autem die postquam ego peperi, peperit et hæc : et eramus simul, nullusque alius nobiscum in domo, exceptis nobis duabus.
      EN: On the third day after I gave birth, this woman gave birth too. We were together, and no one else was in the house with us, only the two of us.

3:19  LA: Mortuus est autem filius mulieris hujus nocte : dormiens quippe oppressit eum.
      EN: This woman's son died in the night, for she lay on him while sleeping.

3:20  LA: Et consurgens intempestæ noctis silentio, tulit filium meum de latere meo, ancillæ tuæ dormientis, et collocavit in sinu suo : suum autem filium, qui erat mortuus, posuit in sinu meo.
      EN: She rose in the silence of the dead of night, took my son from my side while I, your servant, was sleeping, and placed him in her embrace. But her own son, who was dead, she laid in my embrace.

3:21  LA: Cumque surrexissem mane ut darem lac filio meo, apparuit mortuus : quem diligentius intuens clara luce, deprehendi non esse meum quem genueram.
      EN: When I got up in the morning to nurse my son, he appeared dead; but examining him more closely in the daylight, I discovered that he was not the son I had borne.”

3:22  LA: Responditque altera mulier : Non est ita ut dicis, sed filius tuus mortuus est, meus autem vivit. E contrario illa dicebat : Mentiris : filius quippe meus vivit, et filius tuus mortuus est. Atque in hunc modum contendebant coram rege.
      EN: The other woman answered, “It is not as you say. Your son is dead, and mine is alive.” The first insisted, “You are lying! My son is alive, and your son is dead.” In this way they disputed before the king.

3:23  LA: Tunc rex ait : Hæc dicit : Filius meus vivit, et filius tuus mortuus est : et ista respondit : Non, sed filius tuus mortuus est, meus autem vivit.
      EN: Then the king said, “This one says, ‘My son is alive, and your son is dead,’ and that one answers, ‘No, your son is dead, and mine is alive.’”

3:24  LA: Dixit ergo rex : Afferte mihi gladium. Cumque attulissent gladium coram rege,
      EN: So the king said, “Bring me a sword.” When they brought a sword before the king,

3:25  LA: Dividite, inquit, infantem vivum in duas partes, et date dimidiam partem uni, et dimidiam partem alteri.
      EN: he said, “Divide the living infant in two, and give half to one and half to the other.”

3:26  LA: Dixit autem mulier, cujus filius erat vivus, ad regem (commota sunt quippe viscera ejus super filio suo) : Obsecro, domine, date illi infantem vivum, et nolite interficere eum. E contrario illa dicebat : Nec mihi nec tibi sit, sed dividatur.
      EN: The woman whose son was alive spoke to the king, for her deepest feelings were stirred for her son: “Please, my lord, give her the living infant, and do not kill him.” But the other said, “Let him be neither mine nor yours; divide him.”

3:27  LA: Respondit rex, et ait : Date huic infantem vivum, et non occidatur : hæc est enim mater ejus.
      EN: The king answered, “Give this woman the living infant, and do not kill him, for she is his mother.”

3:28  LA: Audivit itaque omnis Israël judicium quod judicasset rex, et timuerunt regem, videntes sapientiam Dei esse in eo ad faciendum judicium.
      EN: All Israel heard the judgment the king had given, and they stood in awe of the king, seeing that God's wisdom was in him to administer justice.

      EN: Ahimaaz, in Naphtali; he too had a daughter of Solomon, Basemath, as his wife;

      EN: Baana son of Hushai, in Asher and Bealoth;

      EN: Shimei son of Ela, in Benjamin;

1 KINGS 5

5:1  LA: Salomon autem erat in ditione sua, habens omnia regna a flumine terræ Philisthiim usque ad terminum Ægypti : offerentium sibi munera, et servientium ei cunctis diebus vitæ ejus.
     EN: Solomon held in his dominion all the kingdoms from the river of the land of the Philistines to the border of Egypt. They brought him gifts and served him all the days of his life.

5:2  LA: Erat autem cibus Salomonis per dies singulos triginta cori similæ, et sexaginta cori farinæ,
     EN: Solomon's daily provisions were thirty cors of fine flour and sixty cors of flour,

5:3  LA: decem boves pingues, et viginti boves pascuales, et centum arietes, excepta venatione cervorum, caprearum, atque bubalorum, et avium altilium.
     EN: ten fattened oxen, twenty pasture-fed oxen, and a hundred rams, besides hunted deer, gazelles, wild oxen, and fattened birds.

5:4  LA: Ipse enim obtinebat omnem regionem quæ erat trans flumen, a Thaphsa usque ad Gazan, et cunctos reges illarum regionum : et habebat pacem ex omni parte in circuitu.
     EN: For he ruled the entire region beyond the river, from Tiphsah to Gaza, and all the kings of those regions, and he had peace on every side around him.

5:5  LA: Habitabatque Juda et Israël absque timore ullo, unusquisque sub vite sua et sub ficu sua, a Dan usque Bersabee, cunctis diebus Salomonis.
     EN: Judah and Israel lived without any fear, each under his own vine and fig tree, from Dan to Beersheba, all the days of Solomon.

5:6  LA: Et habebat Salomon quadraginta millia præsepia equorum currilium, et duodecim millia equestrium.
     EN: Solomon had forty thousand stalls for chariot horses and twelve thousand riding horses.

5:9  LA: Dedit quoque Deus sapientiam Salomoni, et prudentiam multam nimis, et latitudinem cordis quasi arenam quæ est in littore maris.
     EN: God also gave Solomon wisdom, very great discernment, and a breadth of heart like the sand on the seashore.

5:10  LA: Et præcedebat sapientia Salomonis sapientiam omnium Orientalium et Ægyptiorum,
      EN: Solomon's wisdom surpassed the wisdom of all the people of the East and of the Egyptians.

5:11  LA: et erat sapientior cunctis hominibus : sapientior Ethan Ezrahita, et Heman, et Chalcol, et Dorda filiis Mahol : et erat nominatus in universis gentibus per circuitum.
      EN: He was wiser than all people, wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, Heman, Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol; and he was renowned among all the surrounding nations.

5:12  LA: Locutus est quoque Salomon tria millia parabolas : et fuerunt carmina ejus quinque et mille.
      EN: Solomon spoke three thousand proverbs, and his songs numbered one thousand and five.

5:13  LA: Et disputavit super lignis a cedro quæ est in Libano, usque ad hyssopum quæ egreditur de pariete : et disseruit de jumentis, et volucribus, et reptilibus, et piscibus.
      EN: He discussed trees, from the cedar in Lebanon to the hyssop growing from the wall; he also spoke about animals, birds, reptiles, and fish.

5:14  LA: Et veniebant de cunctis populis ad audiendam sapientiam Salomonis, et ab universis regibus terræ qui audiebant sapientiam ejus.
      EN: People came from all nations to hear Solomon's wisdom, and from all the kings of the earth who heard of his wisdom.

5:14a  LA: Confirmatum est igitur regnum in manu Salomonis, et affinitate conjunctus est Pharaoni regi Ægypti : accepit namque filiam ejus, et adduxit in civitatem David, donec compleret ædificans domum suam, et domum Domini, et murum Jerusalem per circuitum.
       EN: Thus the kingdom was established in Solomon's hand. He allied himself by marriage with Pharaoh king of Egypt, taking his daughter and bringing her to the City of David until he finished building his own house, the Lord's house, and the wall around Jerusalem.

5:14b  LA: Pharao rex Ægypti ascendit, et cepit Gazar, succenditque eam igni, et Chananæum, qui habitabat in civitate, interfecit : et dedit eam in dotem filiæ suæ uxori Salomonis.
       EN: Pharaoh king of Egypt had gone up and taken Gezer, burned it with fire, and killed the Canaanites living in the city. He gave it as a dowry to his daughter, Solomon's wife.

5:15  LA: Misit quoque Hiram rex Tyri servos suos ad Salomonem : audivit enim quod ipsum unxissent regem pro patre ejus : quia amicus fuerat Hiram David omni tempore.
      EN: Hiram king of Tyre sent his servants to Solomon, for he had heard that they had anointed him king in his father's place. Hiram had always been David's friend.

5:16  LA: Misit autem Salomon ad Hiram, dicens :
      EN: Solomon sent to Hiram, saying,

5:17  LA: Tu scis voluntatem David patris mei, et quia non potuerit ædificare domum nomini Domini Dei sui propter bella imminentia per circuitum, donec daret Dominus eos sub vestigio pedum ejus.
      EN: “You know the wish of my father David, and that he could not build a house for the name of the Lord his God because of the wars pressing upon him on every side, until the Lord put his enemies beneath his feet.

5:18  LA: Nunc autem requiem dedit Dominus Deus meus mihi per circuitum, et non est satan, neque occursus malus.
      EN: But now the Lord my God has given me rest on every side; there is no adversary or harmful encounter.

5:19  LA: Quam ob rem cogito ædificare templum nomini Domini Dei mei, sicut locutus est Dominus David patri meo, dicens : Filius tuus, quem dabo pro te super solium tuum, ipse ædificabit domum nomini meo.
      EN: For this reason I intend to build a temple for the name of the Lord my God, as the Lord told my father David: ‘Your son, whom I will set on your throne in your place, shall build a house for My name.’

5:20  LA: Præcipe igitur ut præcidant mihi servi tui cedros de Libano, et servi mei sint cum servis tuis : mercedem autem servorum tuorum dabo tibi quamcumque petieris : scis enim quomodo non est in populo meo vir qui noverit ligna cædere sicut Sidonii.
      EN: Therefore command your servants to cut cedars from Lebanon for me. My servants will be with yours, and I will pay you whatever wages you ask for your servants. You know that among my people there is no one skilled in cutting timber like the Sidonians.”

5:21  LA: Cum ergo audisset Hiram verba Salomonis, lætatus est valde, et ait : Benedictus Dominus Deus hodie, qui dedit David filium sapientissimum super populum hunc plurimum.
      EN: When Hiram heard Solomon's words, he rejoiced greatly and said, “Blessed be the Lord God today, who has given David a very wise son over this great people!”

5:22  LA: Et misit Hiram ad Salomonem, dicens : Audivi quæcumque mandasti mihi : ego faciam omnem voluntatem tuam in lignis cedrinis et abiegnis.
