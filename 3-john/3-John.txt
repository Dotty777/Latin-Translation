3 JOHN 1

1:1  LA: Senior Gajo carissimo, quem ego diligo in veritate.
     EN: The elder to the dearly loved Gaius, whom I love in truth.

1:2  LA: Carissime, de omnibus orationem facio prospere te ingredi, et valere sicut prospere agit anima tua.
     EN: Beloved, I pray that in every respect you may prosper and be well, just as your soul prospers.

1:3  LA: Gavisus sum valde venientibus fratribus, et testimonium perhibentibus veritati tuæ, sicut tu in veritate ambulas.
     EN: I rejoiced greatly when brothers came and testified to your truth, to the way you walk in truth.

1:4  LA: Majorem horum non habeo gratiam, quam ut audiam filios meos in veritate ambulare.
     EN: I have no greater joy than this: to hear that my children are walking in truth.

1:5  LA: Carissime, fideliter facis quidquid operaris in fratres, et hoc in peregrinos,
     EN: Beloved, you act faithfully in whatever you do for the brothers, even when they are strangers.

1:6  LA: qui testimonium reddiderunt caritati tuæ in conspectu ecclesiæ: quos, benefaciens, deduces digne Deo.
     EN: They have testified to your love before the church. You will do well to send them on their way in a manner worthy of God.

1:7  LA: Pro nomine enim ejus profecti sunt, nihil accipientes a gentibus.
     EN: For they set out for his name, accepting nothing from the Gentiles.

1:8  LA: Nos ergo debemus suscipere hujusmodi, ut cooperatores simus veritatis.
     EN: We therefore ought to receive such people, so that we may be fellow workers for the truth.

1:9  LA: Scripsissem forsitan ecclesiæ: sed is qui amat primatum gerere in eis, Diotrephes, non recipit nos:
     EN: I would perhaps have written to the church, but Diotrephes, who loves to hold first place among them, does not receive us.

1:10  LA: propter hoc si venero, commonebo ejus opera, quæ facit, verbis malignis garriens in nos: et quasi non ei ista sufficiant, neque ipse suscipit fratres: et eos qui suscipiunt, prohibet, et de ecclesia ejicit.
      EN: Therefore, if I come, I will call attention to the deeds he is doing, chattering against us with malicious words. And as though that were not enough for him, he neither receives the brothers himself nor allows those who do receive them to do so, and he throws them out of the church.

1:11  LA: Carissime, noli imitari malum, sed quod bonum est. Qui benefacit, ex Deo est: qui malefacit, non vidit Deum.
      EN: Beloved, do not imitate evil, but what is good. Whoever does good is from God; whoever does evil has not seen God.

1:12  LA: Demetrio testimonium redditur ab omnibus, et ab ipsa veritate, sed et nos testimonium perhibemus: et nosti quoniam testimonium nostrum verum est.
      EN: Everyone bears witness to Demetrius, and so does the truth itself. We too bear witness, and you know that our testimony is true.

1:13  LA: Multa habui tibi scribere: sed nolui per atramentum et calamum scribere tibi.
      EN: I had much to write to you, but I did not wish to write to you with ink and pen.

1:14  LA: Spero autem protinus te videre, et os ad os loquemur. Pax tibi. Salutant te amici. Saluta amicos nominatim.
      EN: I hope to see you soon, and we will speak face to face. Peace to you. The friends greet you. Greet the friends by name.
