GALATIANS 1

1:1  LA: Paulus , Apostolus non ab hominibus, neque per hominem, sed per Jesum Christum, et Deum Patrem, qui suscitavit eum a mortuis:
     EN: Paul, an apostle, neither from men nor through a man, but through Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised him from the dead,

1:2  LA: et qui mecum sunt omnes fratres, ecclesiis Galatiæ.
     EN: and all the brothers who are with me, to the churches of Galatia.

1:3  LA: Gratia vobis, et pax a Deo Patre, et Domino nostro Jesu Christo,
     EN: Grace to you and peace from God the Father and from our Lord Jesus Christ,

1:4  LA: qui dedit semetipsum pro peccatis nostris, ut eriperet nos de præsenti sæculo nequam, secundum voluntatem Dei et Patris nostri,
     EN: who gave himself for our sins to rescue us from this present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father,

1:5  LA: cui est gloria in sæcula sæculorum. Amen.
     EN: to whom belongs glory forever and ever. Amen.

1:6  LA: Miror quod sic tam cito transferimini ab eo qui vos vocavit in gratiam Christi in aliud Evangelium:
     EN: I am astonished that you are turning away so quickly from him who called you into the grace of Christ, to another gospel.

1:7  LA: quod non est aliud, nisi sunt aliqui qui vos conturbant, et volunt convertere Evangelium Christi.
     EN: There is no other gospel. There are only some who trouble you and want to distort the gospel of Christ.

1:8  LA: Sed licet nos aut angelus de cælo evangelizet vobis præterquam quod evangelizavimus vobis, anathema sit.
     EN: But even if we or an angel from heaven should preach to you a gospel other than the one we preached to you, let him be accursed.

1:9  LA: Sicut prædiximus, et nunc iterum dico: si quis vobis evangelizaverit præter id quod accepistis, anathema sit.
     EN: As we said before, so now I say again: if anyone preaches to you a gospel other than the one you received, let him be accursed.

1:10  LA: Modo enim hominibus suadeo, an Deo? an quæro hominibus placere? si adhuc hominibus placerem, Christi servus non essem.
     EN: For am I now trying to persuade men, or God? Or am I seeking to please men? If I were still pleasing men, I would not be Christ’s servant.

1:11  LA: Notum enim vobis facio, fratres, Evangelium, quod evangelizatum est a me, quia non est secundum hominem:
     EN: For I make known to you, brothers, that the gospel I preached is not of human origin.

1:12  LA: neque enim ego ab homine accepi illud, neque didici, sed per revelationem Jesu Christi.
     EN: For I neither received it from a man nor learned it, except through a revelation of Jesus Christ.

1:13  LA: Audistis enim conversationem meam aliquando in Judaismo: quoniam supra modum persequebar Ecclesiam Dei, et expugnabam illam,
     EN: For you have heard of my former way of life in Judaism, how I persecuted the church of God beyond measure and ravaged it.

1:14  LA: et proficiebam in Judaismo supra multos coætaneos meos in genere meo, abundantius æmulator existens paternarum mearum traditionum.
     EN: And I advanced in Judaism beyond many of my own people who were my age, being all the more zealous for the traditions of my fathers.

1:15  LA: Cum autem placuit ei, qui me segregavit ex utero matris meæ, et vocavit per gratiam suam,
     EN: But when he who set me apart from my mother’s womb and called me by his grace was pleased

1:16  LA: ut revelaret Filium suum in me, ut evangelizarem illum in gentibus: continuo non acquievi carni et sanguini,
     EN: to reveal his Son in me so that I might proclaim him among the Gentiles, immediately I did not yield to flesh and blood,

1:17  LA: neque veni Jerosolymam ad antecessores meos Apostolos: sed abii in Arabiam, et iterum reversus sum Damascum:
     EN: nor did I go to Jerusalem to the apostles who preceded me. Instead I went away to Arabia and returned again to Damascus.

1:18  LA: deinde post annos tres veni Jerosolymam videre Petrum, et mansi apud eum diebus quindecim:
     EN: Then, after three years, I went to Jerusalem to see Peter, and I stayed with him for fifteen days.

1:19  LA: alium autem Apostolorum vidi neminem, nisi Jacobum fratrem Domini.
     EN: But I saw none of the other apostles except James, the brother of the Lord.

1:20  LA: Quæ autem scribo vobis, ecce coram Deo, quia non mentior.
     EN: As for what I am writing to you, behold, before God I am not lying.

1:21  LA: Deinde veni in partes Syriæ, et Ciliciæ.
     EN: Then I went into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.

1:22  LA: Eram autem ignotus facie ecclesiis Judææ, quæ erant in Christo:
     EN: But I was still unknown by sight to the churches of Judea that were in Christ.

1:23  LA: tantum autem auditum habebant quoniam qui persequebatur nos aliquando, nunc evangelizat fidem, quam aliquando expugnabat:
     EN: They had only heard this: “The man who once persecuted us now proclaims the faith he once tried to destroy.”

1:24  LA: et in me clarificabant Deum.
     EN: And they glorified God in me.


GALATIANS 2

2:1  LA: Deinde post annos quatuordecim, iterum ascendi Jerosolymam cum Barnaba, assumpto et Tito.
     EN: Then, after fourteen years, I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, taking Titus also with me.

2:2  LA: Ascendi autem secundum revelationem: et contuli cum illis Evangelium, quod prædico in gentibus, seorsum autem iis qui videbantur aliquid esse: ne forte in vacuum currerem, aut cucurrissem.
     EN: I went up in accordance with a revelation, and I laid before them the gospel I preach among the Gentiles. I did so privately before those who seemed to be of standing, lest I might be running, or might have run, in vain.

2:3  LA: Sed neque Titus, qui mecum erat, cum esset gentilis, compulsus est circumcidi:
     EN: Yet even Titus, who was with me, was not compelled to be circumcised, though he was a Gentile.

2:4  LA: sed propter subintroductos falsos fratres, qui subintroierunt explorare libertatem nostram, quam habemus in Christo Jesu, ut nos in servitutem redigerent.
     EN: This matter arose because of false brothers who had been slipped in. They slipped in to spy on the freedom we have in Christ Jesus, so that they might bring us into slavery.

2:5  LA: Quibus neque ad horam cessimus subjectione, ut veritas Evangelii permaneat apud vos:
     EN: We did not submit to them for even an hour, so that the truth of the gospel might remain with you.

2:6  LA: ab iis autem, qui videbantur esse aliquid (quales aliquando fuerint, nihil mea interest: Deus personam hominis non accipit): mihi enim qui videbantur esse aliquid, nihil contulerunt.
     EN: As for those who seemed to be people of standing, what they once were makes no difference to me. God shows no partiality. Those who seemed to be people of standing added nothing to me.

2:7  LA: Sed e contra cum vidissent quod creditum est mihi Evangelium præputii, sicut et Petro circumcisionis
     EN: On the contrary, they saw that I had been entrusted with the gospel for the uncircumcised, just as Peter had been for the circumcised.

2:8  LA: (qui enim operatus est Petro in apostolatum circumcisionis, operatus est et mihi inter gentes):
     EN: For he who worked in Peter for his apostleship to the circumcised also worked in me among the Gentiles.

2:9  LA: et cum cognovissent gratiam, quæ data est mihi, Jacobus, et Cephas, et Joannes, qui videbantur columnæ esse, dextras dederunt mihi, et Barnabæ societatis: ut nos in gentes, ipsi autem in circumcisionem:
     EN: And when they recognized the grace given to me, James and Cephas and John, who seemed to be pillars, gave Barnabas and me their right hands in fellowship, so that we would go to the Gentiles and they to the circumcised.

2:10  LA: tantum ut pauperum memores essemus, quod etiam sollicitus fui hoc ipsum facere.
     EN: They asked only that we remember the poor, the very thing I was eager to do.

2:11  LA: Cum autem venisset Cephas Antiochiam, in faciem ei restiti, quia reprehensibilis erat.
     EN: But when Cephas came to Antioch, I opposed him to his face, because he was at fault.

2:12  LA: Prius enim quam venirent quidam a Jacobo, cum gentibus edebat: cum autem venissent, subtrahebat, et segregabat se, timens eos qui ex circumcisione erant.
     EN: For before certain people came from James, he used to eat with the Gentiles. But when they came, he began to withdraw and separate himself, fearing those of the circumcision.

2:13  LA: Et simulationi ejus consenserunt ceteri Judæi, ita ut et Barnabas duceretur ab eis in illam simulationem.
     EN: The rest of the Jews joined him in his hypocrisy, so that even Barnabas was led by them into the same hypocrisy.

2:14  LA: Sed cum vidissem quod non recte ambularent ad veritatem Evangelii, dixi Cephæ coram omnibus: Si tu, cum Judæus sis, gentiliter vivis, et non judaice: quomodo gentes cogis judaizare?
     EN: But when I saw that they were not walking straight in line with the truth of the gospel, I said to Cephas in front of them all, “If you, though a Jew, live like a Gentile and not like a Jew, how can you compel the Gentiles to live like Jews?

2:15  LA: Nos natura Judæi, et non ex gentibus peccatores.
     EN: We are Jews by birth, and not sinners from among the Gentiles.

2:16  LA: Scientes autem quod non justificatur homo ex operibus legis, nisi per fidem Jesu Christi: et nos in Christo Jesu credimus, ut justificemur ex fide Christi, et non ex operibus legis: propter quod ex operibus legis non justificabitur omnis caro.
     EN: Yet we know that a person is not justified by works of the law but through faith in Jesus Christ. We too have believed in Christ Jesus so that we may be justified by faith in Christ and not by works of the law, for by works of the law no flesh will be justified.”

2:17  LA: Quod si quærentes justificari in Christo, inventi sumus et ipsi peccatores, numquid Christus peccati minister est? Absit.
     EN: But if, in seeking to be justified in Christ, we ourselves were also found to be sinners, is Christ then a servant of sin? Certainly not!

2:18  LA: Si enim quæ destruxi, iterum hæc ædifico: prævaricatorem me constituo.
     EN: For if I rebuild what I tore down, I make myself a transgressor.

2:19  LA: Ego enim per legem, legi mortuus sum, ut Deo vivam: Christo confixus sum cruci.
     EN: For through the law I died to the law, so that I might live for God. I have been crucified with Christ.

2:20  LA: Vivo autem, jam non ego: vivit vero in me Christus. Quod autem nunc vivo in carne: in fide vivo Filii Dei, qui dilexit me, et tradidit semetipsum pro me.
     EN: And I live, yet no longer I, but Christ lives in me. The life I now live in the flesh, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave himself up for me.

2:21  LA: Non abjicio gratiam Dei. Si enim per legem justitia, ergo gratis Christus mortuus est.
     EN: I do not set aside the grace of God. For if righteousness comes through the law, then Christ died in vain.


GALATIANS 3

3:1  LA: O insensati Galatæ, quis vos fascinavit non obedire veritati, ante quorum oculos Jesus Christus præscriptus est, in vobis crucifixus?
     EN: O foolish Galatians, who has bewitched you into disobeying the truth? Before your very eyes Jesus Christ was set forth, crucified among you.

3:2  LA: Hoc solum a vobis volo discere: ex operibus legis Spiritum accepistis, an ex auditu fidei?
     EN: I want to learn only this from you: did you receive the Spirit by works of the law, or by the hearing of faith?

3:3  LA: sic stulti estis, ut cum Spiritu cœperitis, nunc carne consummemini?
     EN: Are you so foolish? Having begun with the Spirit, are you now being brought to completion by the flesh?

3:4  LA: tanta passi estis sine causa? si tamen sine causa.
     EN: Did you suffer so much in vain, if indeed it was in vain?

3:5  LA: Qui ergo tribuit vobis Spiritum, et operatur virtutes in vobis: ex operibus legis, an ex auditu fidei?
     EN: So does he who gives you the Spirit and works miracles among you do so by works of the law, or by the hearing of faith?

3:6  LA: Sicut scriptum est: Abraham credidit Deo, et reputatum est illi ad justitiam:
     EN: As it is written, “Abraham believed God, and it was counted to him as righteousness.”

3:7  LA: cognoscite ergo quia qui ex fide sunt, ii sunt filii Abrahæ.
     EN: Know then that those who are of faith are the sons of Abraham.

3:8  LA: Providens autem Scriptura quia ex fide justificat gentes Deus, prænuntiavit Abrahæ: Quia benedicentur in te omnes gentes.
     EN: And Scripture, foreseeing that God justifies the Gentiles by faith, announced beforehand to Abraham, “In you all nations will be blessed.”

3:9  LA: Igitur qui ex fide sunt, benedicentur cum fideli Abraham.
     EN: So those who are of faith will be blessed with faithful Abraham.

3:10  LA: Quicumque enim ex operibus legis sunt, sub maledicto sunt. Scriptum est enim: Maledictus omnis qui non permanserit in omnibus quæ scripta sunt in libro legis ut faciat ea.
     EN: For all who are of works of the law are under a curse. For it is written, “Cursed is everyone who does not continue in all that is written in the book of the law, to do it.”

3:11  LA: Quoniam autem in lege nemo justificatur apud Deum, manifestum est: quia justus ex fide vivit.
     EN: Now it is clear that no one is justified before God by the law, because “the righteous person lives by faith.”

3:12  LA: Lex autem non est ex fide, sed: Qui fecerit ea, vivet in illis.
     EN: But the law is not of faith. Rather, “The one who does these things will live by them.”

3:13  LA: Christus nos redemit de maledicto legis, factus pro nobis maledictum: quia scriptum est: Maledictus omnis qui pendet in ligno:
     EN: Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us, for it is written, “Cursed is everyone who hangs on a tree.”

3:14  LA: ut in gentibus benedictio Abrahæ fieret in Christo Jesu, ut pollicitationem Spiritus accipiamus per fidem.
     EN: This was so that the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles in Christ Jesus, and that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.

3:15  LA: Fratres (secundum hominem dico) tamen hominis confirmatum testamentum nemo spernit, aut superordinat.
     EN: Brothers, I speak in human terms. Even when a man’s covenant has been ratified, no one sets it aside or adds to it.

3:16  LA: Abrahæ dictæ sunt promissiones, et semini ejus. Non dicit: Et seminibus, quasi in multis: sed quasi in uno: Et semini tuo, qui est Christus.
     EN: The promises were spoken to Abraham and to his seed. It does not say, “And to seeds,” as though speaking of many, but as of one, “And to your seed,” who is Christ.

3:17  LA: Hoc autem dico, testamentum confirmatum a Deo: quæ post quadringentos et triginta annos facta est lex, non irritum facit ad evacuandam promissionem.
     EN: What I mean is this: the law, which came four hundred and thirty years later, does not annul the covenant God ratified and so nullify the promise.

3:18  LA: Nam si ex lege hæreditas, jam non ex promissione. Abrahæ autem per repromissionem donavit Deus.
     EN: For if the inheritance comes from the law, it no longer comes from a promise. But God gave it to Abraham through a promise.

3:19  LA: Quid igitur lex? Propter transgressiones posita est donec veniret semen, cui promiserat, ordinata per angelos in manu mediatoris.
     EN: Why, then, the law? It was laid down because of transgressions until the seed should come to whom God had made the promise. It was ordained through angels by the hand of a mediator.

3:20  LA: Mediator autem unius non est: Deus autem unus est.
     EN: Now a mediator is not for one party alone, but God is one.

3:21  LA: Lex ergo adversus promissa Dei? Absit. Si enim data esset lex, quæ posset vivificare, vere ex lege esset justitia.
     EN: Is the law, then, against God’s promises? Certainly not! For if a law had been given that could give life, righteousness would indeed come from the law.

3:22  LA: Sed conclusit Scriptura omnia sub peccato, ut promissio ex fide Jesu Christi daretur credentibus.
     EN: But Scripture confined all things under sin, so that the promise through faith in Jesus Christ might be given to those who believe.

3:23  LA: Prius autem quam veniret fides, sub lege custodiebamur conclusi in eam fidem quæ revelanda erat.
     EN: Before faith came, we were held in custody under the law, shut up until the faith that was to be revealed.

3:24  LA: Itaque lex pædagogus noster fuit in Christo, ut ex fide justificemur.
     EN: So the law was our guardian in Christ, so that we might be justified by faith.

3:25  LA: At ubi venit fides, jam non sumus sub pædagogo.
     EN: But now that faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian.

3:26  LA: Omnes enim filii Dei estis per fidem, quæ est in Christo Jesu.
     EN: For you are all sons of God through the faith that is in Christ Jesus.

3:27  LA: Quicumque enim in Christo baptizati estis, Christum induistis.
     EN: For all of you who were baptized into Christ have put on Christ.

3:28  LA: Non est Judæus, neque Græcus: non est servus, neque liber: non est masculus, neque femina. Omnes enim vos unum estis in Christo Jesu.
     EN: There is neither Jew nor Greek, neither slave nor free, neither male nor female; for you are all one in Christ Jesus.

3:29  LA: Si autem vos Christi, ergo semen Abrahæ estis, secundum promissionem hæredes.
     EN: And if you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham’s seed, heirs according to the promise.


GALATIANS 4

4:1  LA: Dico autem: quanto tempore hæres parvulus est, nihil differt a servo, cum sit dominus omnium:
     EN: Now I say that as long as the heir is a child, he is no different from a slave, though he is master of everything.

4:2  LA: sed sub tutoribus et actoribus est usque ad præfinitum tempus a patre:
     EN: He is under guardians and managers until the time his father has appointed.

4:3  LA: ita et nos cum essemus parvuli, sub elementis mundi eramus servientes.
     EN: So too, when we were children, we were in bondage under the elements of the world.

4:4  LA: At ubi venit plenitudo temporis, misit Deus Filium suum factum ex muliere, factum sub lege,
     EN: But when the fullness of time came, God sent his Son, born of a woman, born under the law,

4:5  LA: ut eos, qui sub lege erant, redimeret, ut adoptionem filiorum reciperemus.
     EN: to redeem those who were under the law, so that we might receive adoption as sons.

4:6  LA: Quoniam autem estis filii, misit Deus Spiritum Filii sui in corda vestra, clamantem: Abba, Pater.
     EN: And because you are sons, God sent the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, “Abba, Father.”

4:7  LA: Itaque jam non est servus, sed filius: quod si filius, et hæres per Deum.
     EN: So he is no longer a slave but a son. And if a son, then also an heir through God.

4:8  LA: Sed tunc quidem ignorantes Deum, iis, qui natura non sunt dii, serviebatis.
     EN: Formerly, when you did not know God, you served those who by nature are not gods.

4:9  LA: Nunc autem cum cognoveritis Deum, immo cogniti sitis a Deo: quomodo convertimini iterum ad infirma et egena elementa, quibus denuo servire vultis?
     EN: But now that you have come to know God, or rather have been known by God, how can you turn back to the weak and impoverished elements? Do you want to serve them all over again?

4:10  LA: Dies observatis, et menses, et tempora, et annos.
     EN: You observe days and months and seasons and years.

4:11  LA: Timeo vos, ne forte sine causa laboraverim in vobis.
     EN: I am afraid for you, lest I have labored among you in vain.

4:12  LA: Estote sicut ego, quia et ego sicut vos: fratres, obsecro vos. Nihil me læsistis.
     EN: Become as I am, because I too have become as you are. I beg you, brothers. You did me no wrong.

4:13  LA: Scitis autem quia per infirmitatem carnis evangelizavi vobis jampridem: et tentationem vestram in carne mea
     EN: You know that it was through bodily weakness that I preached the gospel to you before. And the trial you faced in my flesh

4:14  LA: non sprevistis, neque respuistis: sed sicut angelum Dei excepistis me, sicut Christum Jesum.
     EN: you did not despise or reject. Instead, you welcomed me as an angel of God, as Christ Jesus.

4:15  LA: Ubi est ergo beatitudo vestra? testimonium enim perhibeo vobis, quia, si fieri posset, oculos vestros eruissetis, et dedissetis mihi.
     EN: What, then, has become of your blessedness? For I bear you witness that, if it had been possible, you would have plucked out your eyes and given them to me.

4:16  LA: Ergo inimicus vobis factus sum, verum dicens vobis?
     EN: Have I then become your enemy by telling you the truth?

4:17  LA: Æmulantur vos non bene: sed excludere vos volunt, ut illos æmulemini.
     EN: They are eager to win you over, but for no good purpose. They want to shut you out so that you will be eager for them.

4:18  LA: Bonum autem æmulamini in bono semper: et non tantum cum præsens sum apud vos.
     EN: But be zealous for what is good, in what is good, always, and not only when I am with you.

4:19  LA: Filioli mei, quos iterum parturio, donec formetur Christus in vobis:
     EN: My little children, for whom I am again in labor until Christ is formed in you,

4:20  LA: vellem autem esse apud vos modo, et mutare vocem meam: quoniam confundor in vobis.
     EN: I wish I could be with you now and change my tone, because I am at a loss about you.

4:21  LA: Dicite mihi qui sub lege vultis esse: legem non legistis?
     EN: Tell me, you who want to be under the law, have you not read the law?

4:22  LA: Scriptum est enim: Quoniam Abraham duos filios habuit: unum de ancilla, et unum de libera.
     EN: For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one by a slave woman and one by a free woman.

4:23  LA: Sed qui de ancilla, secundum carnem natus est: qui autem de libera, per repromissionem:
     EN: The son of the slave woman was born according to the flesh, but the son of the free woman through a promise.

4:24  LA: quæ sunt per allegoriam dicta. Hæc enim sunt duo testamenta. Unum quidem in monte Sina, in servitutem generans, quæ est Agar:
     EN: These things are spoken as an allegory. For these are two covenants. One is on Mount Sinai, bearing children for slavery. This is Hagar.

4:25  LA: Sina enim mons est in Arabia, qui conjunctus est ei quæ nunc est Jerusalem, et servit cum filiis suis.
     EN: For Sinai is a mountain in Arabia. It is joined to the present Jerusalem, which is in slavery with her children.

4:26  LA: Illa autem, quæ sursum est Jerusalem, libera est, quæ est mater nostra.
     EN: But the Jerusalem above is free, and she is our mother.

4:27  LA: Scriptum est enim: Lætare, sterilis, quæ non paris; erumpe et clama, quæ non parturis: quia multi filii desertæ, magis quam ejus quæ habet virum.
     EN: For it is written, “Rejoice, barren woman who does not bear children. Break out and cry aloud, you who do not labor in childbirth. For the children of the desolate woman are many, more than those of the woman who has a husband.”

4:28  LA: Nos autem, fratres, secundum Isaac promissionis filii sumus.
     EN: Now we, brothers, are children of promise, as Isaac was.

4:29  LA: Sed quomodo tunc is, qui secundum carnem natus fuerat, persequebatur eum qui secundum spiritum: ita et nunc.
     EN: But just as then the one born according to the flesh persecuted the one born according to the spirit, so it is now.

4:30  LA: Sed quid dicit Scriptura? Ejice ancillam, et filium ejus: non enim hæres erit filius ancillæ cum filio liberæ.
     EN: But what does Scripture say? “Cast out the slave woman and her son, for the son of the slave woman will not inherit with the son of the free woman.”

4:31  LA: Itaque, fratres, non sumus ancillæ filii, sed liberæ: qua libertate Christus nos liberavit.
     EN: So then, brothers, we are not children of the slave woman but of the free woman. It is with this freedom that Christ has set us free.


GALATIANS 5

5:1  LA: State , et nolite iterum jugo servitutis contineri.
     EN: Stand firm, and do not let yourselves be held again under a yoke of slavery.

5:2  LA: Ecce ego Paulus dico vobis: quoniam si circumcidamini, Christus vobis nihil proderit.
     EN: Look, I, Paul, tell you that if you are circumcised, Christ will be of no benefit to you.

5:3  LA: Testificor autem rursus omni homini circumcidenti se, quoniam debitor est universæ legis faciendæ.
     EN: And I testify again to every man who has himself circumcised that he is bound to keep the whole law.

5:4  LA: Evacuati estis a Christo, qui in lege justificamini: a gratia excidistis.
     EN: You who are being justified by the law are cut off from Christ. You have fallen from grace.

5:5  LA: Nos enim spiritu ex fide, spem justitiæ exspectamus.
     EN: For through the Spirit, by faith, we await the hope of righteousness.

5:6  LA: Nam in Christo Jesu neque circumcisio aliquid valet, neque præputium: sed fides, quæ per caritatem operatur.
     EN: For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision has any power, but faith working through love.

5:7  LA: Currebatis bene: quis vos impedivit veritati non obedire?
     EN: You were running well. Who hindered you from obeying the truth?

5:8  LA: persuasio hæc non est ex eo, qui vocat vos.
     EN: This persuasion does not come from him who calls you.

5:9  LA: Modicum fermentum totam massam corrumpit.
     EN: A little leaven spoils the whole batch of dough.

5:10  LA: Ego confido in vobis in Domino, quod nihil aliud sapietis: qui autem conturbat vos, portabit judicium, quicumque est ille.
     EN: I have confidence in you in the Lord that you will be of no other mind. But he who troubles you will bear the judgment, whoever he may be.

5:11  LA: Ego autem, fratres, si circumcisionem adhuc prædico: quid adhuc persecutionem patior? ergo evacuatum est scandalum crucis.
     EN: But if I still preach circumcision, brothers, why am I still persecuted? In that case the stumbling block of the cross has been removed.

5:12  LA: Utinam et abscindantur qui vos conturbant.
     EN: I wish those who trouble you would even be cut off.

5:13  LA: Vos enim in libertatem vocati estis, fratres: tantum ne libertatem in occasionem detis carnis, sed per caritatem Spiritus servite invicem.
     EN: For you were called to freedom, brothers. Only do not give the flesh an opportunity through your freedom, but through the love of the Spirit serve one another.

5:14  LA: Omnis enim lex in uno sermone impletur: Diliges proximum tuum sicut teipsum.
     EN: For the whole law is fulfilled in one saying: “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.”

5:15  LA: Quod si invicem mordetis, et comeditis: videte ne ab invicem consumamini.
     EN: But if you bite and devour one another, see that you are not consumed by one another.

5:16  LA: Dico autem: Spiritu ambulate, et desideria carnis non perficietis.
     EN: But I say, walk by the Spirit, and you will not carry out the desires of the flesh.

5:17  LA: Caro enim concupiscit adversus spiritum, spiritus autem adversus carnem: hæc enim sibi invicem adversantur, ut non quæcumque vultis, illa faciatis.
     EN: For the flesh desires what is against the Spirit, and the Spirit what is against the flesh. They oppose each other, so that you do not do whatever you want.

5:18  LA: Quod si Spiritu ducimini, non estis sub lege.
     EN: But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the law.

5:19  LA: Manifesta sunt autem opera carnis, quæ sunt fornicatio, immunditia, impudicitia, luxuria,
     EN: Now the works of the flesh are plain: sexual immorality, uncleanness, shamelessness, sexual excess,

5:20  LA: idolorum servitus, veneficia, inimicitiæ, contentiones, æmulationes, iræ, rixæ, dissensiones, sectæ,
     EN: idolatry, sorceries, hostilities, disputes, rivalries, fits of anger, quarrels, divisions, factions,

5:21  LA: invidiæ, homicidia, ebrietates, comessationes, et his similia, quæ prædico vobis, sicut prædixi: quoniam qui talia agunt, regnum Dei non consequentur.
     EN: envy, murders, drunkenness, revelries, and things like these. I warn you now as I warned you before: those who do such things will not obtain the kingdom of God.

5:22  LA: Fructus autem Spiritus est caritas, gaudium, pax, patientia, benignitas, bonitas, longanimitas,
     EN: But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, long-suffering,

5:23  LA: mansuetudo, fides, modestia, continentia, castitas. Adversus hujusmodi non est lex.
     EN: gentleness, faith, modesty, self-control, and chastity. Against such things there is no law.

5:24  LA: Qui autem sunt Christi, carnem suam crucifixerunt cum vitiis et concupiscentiis.
     EN: Those who belong to Christ have crucified their flesh with its vices and desires.

5:25  LA: Si Spiritu vivimus, Spiritu et ambulemus.
     EN: If we live by the Spirit, let us also walk by the Spirit.

5:26  LA: Non efficiamur inanis gloriæ cupidi, invicem provocantes, invicem invidentes.
     EN: Let us not become eager for empty glory, provoking one another and envying one another.


GALATIANS 6

6:1  LA: Fratres , etsi præoccupatus fuerit homo in aliquo delicto, vos, qui spirituales estis, hujusmodi instruite in spiritu lenitatis, considerans teipsum, ne et tu tenteris.
     EN: Brothers, if anyone is caught in a fault, you who are spiritual should set such a person right in a spirit of gentleness. Each of you should watch yourself, so that you too are not tempted.

6:2  LA: Alter alterius onera portate, et sic adimplebitis legem Christi.
     EN: Bear one another’s burdens, and so you will fulfill the law of Christ.

6:3  LA: Nam si quis existimat se aliquid esse, cum nihil sit, ipse se seducit.
     EN: For if anyone thinks he is something when he is nothing, he deceives himself.

6:4  LA: Opus autem suum probet unusquisque, et sic in semetipso tantum gloriam habebit, et non in altero.
     EN: Let each person test his own work. Then he will have cause to boast in himself alone and not in another.

6:5  LA: Unusquisque enim onus suum portabit.
     EN: For each person will carry his own burden.

6:6  LA: Communicet autem is qui catechizatur verbo, ei qui se catechizat, in omnibus bonis.
     EN: Let the one who is taught the word share all good things with the one who teaches him.

6:7  LA: Nolite errare: Deus non irridetur.
     EN: Do not be deceived: God is not mocked.

6:8  LA: Quæ enim seminaverit homo, hæc et metet. Quoniam qui seminat in carne sua, de carne et metet corruptionem: qui autem seminat in spiritu, de spiritu metet vitam æternam.
     EN: For whatever a man sows, that is what he will reap. Whoever sows in his flesh will also reap corruption from the flesh, but whoever sows in the Spirit will reap eternal life from the Spirit.

6:9  LA: Bonum autem facientes, non deficiamus: tempore enim suo metemus non deficientes.
     EN: Let us not grow weary in doing good, for in due season we will reap if we do not grow weary.

6:10  LA: Ergo dum tempus habemus, operemur bonum ad omnes, maxime autem ad domesticos fidei.
     EN: So then, while we have time, let us do good to everyone, especially to those of the household of faith.

6:11  LA: Videte qualibus litteris scripsi vobis mea manu.
     EN: See what kind of letters I have written to you in my own hand.

6:12  LA: Quicumque enim volunt placere in carne, hi cogunt vos circumcidi, tantum ut crucis Christi persecutionem non patiantur.
     EN: Those who want to make a good impression in the flesh are the ones compelling you to be circumcised, only so that they will not suffer persecution for the cross of Christ.

6:13  LA: Neque enim qui circumciduntur, legem custodiunt: sed volunt vos circumcidi, ut in carne vestra glorientur.
     EN: For even those who are circumcised do not keep the law. They want you to be circumcised so that they may boast in your flesh.

6:14  LA: Mihi autem absit gloriari, nisi in cruce Domini nostri Jesu Christi: per quem mihi mundus crucifixus est, et ego mundo.
     EN: But may I never boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.

6:15  LA: In Christo enim Jesu neque circumcisio aliquid valet, neque præputium, sed nova creatura.
     EN: For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision counts for anything, but a new creation.

6:16  LA: Et quicumque hanc regulam secuti fuerint, pax super illos, et misericordia, et super Israël Dei.
     EN: And as for all who follow this rule, peace and mercy be upon them and upon the Israel of God.

6:17  LA: De cetero, nemo mihi molestus sit: ego enim stigmata Domini Jesu in corpore meo porto.
     EN: From now on let no one trouble me, for I bear the marks of the Lord Jesus on my body.

6:18  LA: Gratia Domini nostri Jesu Christi cum spiritu vestro, fratres. Amen.
     EN: The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brothers. Amen.
