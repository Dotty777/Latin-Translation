# Saints Latin Critical Text: anomalies found during the Modern English review (24 November 2026)

These are problems in the critical text (`Saints-Latin-Critical-Text/`), not in the English. Where they apply, the English translates the critical text as it stands and keeps Saints.Bible verse slots; `verse-map.json` records English verses that translate a critical-text verse filed under another number. Items marked *(unverified)* rest on reviewers' knowledge of the printed Clementine Vulgate, which is not in this repository, and should be checked in the Studio.

## Found during the review

### Text missing or placed elsewhere
- **Sirach.** The critical text lacks several Clementine lines that have no Saints verse slot *(unverified)*: Clem 2:10a, 2:12, 2:17, 21:3, 21:13, 21:15, 22:22, 27:24, 28:7, 28:9, 28:16, 37:10, 37:13–14, 48:12 and 49:1b. The notes that said "the Clementine Vulgate omits" these now say "this Latin text".
- **Sirach 1:15, 22:17, 42:1.** These carry the placeholder text "No aligned Latin source passage." The English additional verses repeat the placeholder.
- **1 Maccabees 1.** Clementine clauses may be missing *(unverified)*: 1:5 *et obtinuit regiones gentium*, 1:32 *Et irruit super civitatem repente* and 1:49 *et prohiberent celebrari sabbatum*. The notes at 1:4, 1:30 and 1:45 say "the Vulgate omits" them, which is true only of this text.
- **2 Maccabees 15.** The Clementine date clause in 15:37 *(unverified)* has no critical-text verse, so the critical text's 15:37–39 may be Clementine 15:38–40.
- **Psalm 108:16.** The clause *et persecutus est hominem inopem et mendicum, et compunctum corde mortificare* is missing.
- **Psalm 12:3, 129:6.** These clauses are missing: *usquequo exaltabitur inimicus meus super me?* and *A custodia matutina usque ad noctem*.
- **Proverbs 16:9.** *Cor hominis disponit viam suam…* is missing, and the critical text puts Clementine 16:4 at 16:9.
- **Isaiah 63:19 / 64:1.** *Utinam dirumperes cælos, et descenderes; a facie tua montes defluerent* is missing, so 64:1 "they would melt" has no antecedent.
- **Matthew 17:21.** *Hoc autem genus non ejicitur nisi per orationem et jejunium* is missing.
- **Hosea 6:2.** *quia ipse cepit, et sanabit nos; percutiet, et curabit nos* is missing.
- **1 Kings 7.** The critical text lacks Clementine 7:13, 7:36 and 7:45, so Saints 7:1 and 7:22 have no English and English 7:2 opens mid-sentence.
- **Esther 5:2.** The critical text lacks Clementine 5:1 (the golden scepter); Saints 5:2 has no English.
- **Exodus 37–39.** Several Clementine verses are absent, and the critical text's order differs from the Saints slots. See the structural list below.

### Possible typos or unusual readings
These are translated as they stand, unless noted:
- Genesis 32:23 *vadum Jacob* (Jaboc?); 37:36 *Mazianitis* (Madianitis?); 30:33 *furtim* (*furti me arguent*?); 49:9 *a prædam* (*ad prædam*?); 41:54 *non erat panis*; 42:20 *possimus*.
- Deuteronomy 5:17–18: *neque mœchaberis* stands at 17 and *Non occides* at 18, the reverse of the Clementine order.
- Ruth 3:10 *est secuta* (*es*?) and 1 Samuel 4:21 *vocabit*: the English follows the expected reading, with a note.
- 2 Kings 8:11 *Stetique* (Stetitque).
- 1 Chronicles 16:36 *omnis populo*; 26:6 *Semei*, where 26:4 and 26:7 have Semeias.
- Isaiah 1:19 *comeditis* (*comedetis*?); 2:6 *augeres* (*augures*?); 9:4 *violentia* (*violenta*?); 5:25 *adversus* against *aversus* at 9:11 and elsewhere.
- Wisdom 18:21 *Prosperans* (*properans*?).
- Acts 16:5 *abundabunt* (*abundabant*?).
- 2 Corinthians 1:23 *fidei vestæ* (*vestræ*).
- Ezekiel 20:45 (an additional verse) duplicates 21:1.
- Two keys are used both as a regular verse and as an additional verse: Sirach 19:6 and Acts 9:6. Both are deliberate.

## Found during the structural repair (verbatim from the repair reports)

- 1 Kings: the CT files under Vulgate keys content that the Saints (3 Kingdoms) slots hold elsewhere; English kept at the Saints slots and mapped: 3:1 -> 5:14; 4:20, 5:1, 5:5 -> 2:46; 4:27-28 -> 5:1; 5:6 -> 10:26; 5:31, 6:37-38 -> 6:1; 7:1 -> 7:38; 7:22 -> 7:8; 8:12-13 -> 8:53; 9:15, 9:17-22 -> 10:22; 9:16 -> 5:14; 9:23, 9:25 -> 2:35; 9:24 -> 9:9; 11:3 -> 11:1; 11:23-24 -> 11:14; 12:2 -> 11:43; 14:1-6, 10-13, 17-18 -> 12:24; 22:47-50 -> 16:28.
- 1 Kings 7: the CT lacks Vulgate 7:13 (Solomon fetches Hiram from Tyre; Saints 7:1), 7:36 (engraved cherubim, lions and palms on the stands; Saints 7:22) and 7:45 (cauldrons, shovels and bowls of bronze; Saints 7:31) - its own versification notes say 'not collated'. CT keys 7:1, 7:22 hold Vulgate 7:1, 7:22 instead (content of Saints 7:38/7:50 and 7:8), and CT 7:31 holds Vulgate 7:31 (the basin's mouth), so the English at 7:1, 7:22 and 7:31 does not hold the Saints-slot content, and English 7:2 opens mid-sentence ('the son of a widowed woman...') because its lead-in (Vulgate 7:13) is missing.
- 1 Kings notes outside the assigned chapters that misdescribe the Latin (not edited): 2:35 says the Clementine Vulgate 'retains' the officers/sacrifices summary here - it is Vulgate 9:23, 25 (CT 9:23, 9:25) placed at the Saints slot; 2:46 says the Vulgate 'appends' the prosperity summary - it is Vulgate 4:20-21, 25 (CT 4:20, 5:1, 5:5); 16:28 calls the Jehoshaphat notices an addition 'in this Latin text' parallel to '22:46-49' - they are CT/Vulgate 22:47-50 placed here; 15:7 says 'Verse 6 is absent in this Clementine Vulgate text' and 15:31 says 'Verse 32 ... is absent' - the CT has 15:6 and 15:32 and the English carries them as additional verses.
- Nehemiah 7:42-48: the CT keeps the Clementine verse division (Levites split over CT 7:43-44; singers CT 7:45; gatekeepers CT 7:46; Nethinim CT 7:47; Keros...Shalmai in one verse CT 7:48), one key behind the Saints slots. English stays at the Saints slots (mapped); CT 7:48 is split across English 7:47 (Keros, Sia, Padon) and 7:48 (Lebana, Hagaba, Shalmai) to fill the empty Saints slot 7:48. The headings 'The Levites:'/'The singers:' remain at the ends of 7:42/7:43, as in the Latin.
- Nehemiah 7:68 (horses and mules) is not a Saints slot (absent from the Hebrew- and Greek-based editions); converted from a regular verse to an additional verse after 7:67.
- Nehemiah 12:33-34: the CT has no 12:34; CT 12:33 covers Saints 12:33 and 12:34. English 12:33 split at the Saints boundary; 12:34 mapped to CT 12:33.
- Esther 5: CT key 5:2 holds Vulgate 5:1 (Esther in royal robes in the inner court), whose content is Saints 5:1; English kept at 5:1 and mapped. Vulgate 5:2 (the king sees her and extends the golden scepter, which she kisses) is absent from the CT ('not collated'), so Saints 5:2 has no English.
- Esther 10:4-13: Saints slot 10:3 (Greek-based 10:3) holds Addition F, so the existing English 10:3, which translates CT 10:3-13, was kept and mapped; the note no longer claims the Vulgate text appends these verses to 10:3.
- 1 Maccabees 12:53-54: the Vulgate splits Saints 12:53 into two verses; CT 12:54 (the nations' words) is already translated at the end of English 12:53, whose Greek-based slot holds it - mapped, no text change.
- 2 Maccabees 12:45-46: the Vulgate splits Saints 12:45 into two verses; CT 12:46 ('It is a holy and salutary thought to pray for the dead...') is already translated at the end of English 12:45, whose Greek-based slot holds it - mapped, no text change.
- Daniel 3:98-100 (the king's letter, Vulgate numbering) have no slot in ch. 3, but Saints slot 4:37 (Hebrew-based 4:37) holds this content and the English 4:37 already translates CT 3:98-100 followed by CT 4:37 - mapped, no text change.
- Daniel 3:67-70 (cold and heat; dews and hoarfrost; frost and cold; ice and snows) are not Saints slots (the Greek-based edition omits 67-70 from its numbering); converted from regular verses to additional verses after 3:66.
- Daniel 3: the CT keeps the Vulgate order of the canticle where the Greek order differs (CT 3:54/3:55 and 3:58/3:59 and 3:77/3:78 are swapped relative to the Greek-based slots; 3:39-42 have different boundaries). English follows the CT keys; not re-ordered.
- Daniel 5:17/5:25: CT 5:25 (the inscription) is translated at the end of English 5:17, where the Hebrew-based Saints slot holds it - mapped; note corrected (it claimed the Latin text itself ends 5:17 with this sentence).
- Daniel 1:22-60 (left as arranged): the CT's chapter-1 additional keys are not all Susanna - CT 1:24-27 and 1:42 are Bel and the Dragon (Vulgate 14:24-27, 14:42: the king bids Daniel worship the dragon; the pitch cakes; the accusers thrown to the lions), so the additional verses after 1:21 interleave Bel text with Susanna. No verse missing or duplicated within Daniel.
- Daniel 4 (not assigned): additional verse src 4:3 (the decree summoning the wise men) has after '4:37'; in reading order it belongs after 4:5 (before additional 4:6). The CT ch. 4 also lacks Vulgate 4:4-5 (the diviners come in; Daniel comes in) - not collated per its versification notes.
- Sirach 1:15, 22:17, 42:1: CT text is the placeholder "No aligned Latin source passage." at Saints slots; the English additional items carrying the same placeholder are left as they are (42:1 only re-anchored after 41:23 because English 41:27 was removed).
- Sirach: the CT keeps many pieces of Saints-slot verses under leftover Vulgate numbers that are not Saints slots: 1:32, 1:35, 1:39-40, 2:23, 3:25, 5:17, 6:1, 9:19-20, 13:27, 13:29, 18:3, 22:8, 22:29, 23:28, 24:43, 25:30, 25:32, 25:36, 26:20, 26:26-27, 31:33, 31:35, 31:39, 31:42, 33:16, 33:33, 34:27, 35:11, 35:18-19, 35:23, 38:39, 41:24-26, 43:34, 44:26, 45:29, 47:27-28, 47:30, 49:18. Each is translated inside the English verse at the Saints slot whose content it holds; pairings are in map.
- Sirach 7:9, 20:3, 51:3: CT Vulgate-numbered verses sit on Saints slot keys but hold the content of the neighbouring slot (7:9 = Saints 7:10a; 20:3 = Saints 20:4b; 51:3 = Saints 51:2b). English stays at 7:10, 20:4, 51:2 (map); Saints slots 7:9 ("Do not say...") and 51:3 ("redeemed me ... from gnashing jaws") have no Latin in the CT.
- Sirach 9:15-16, 12:5-7, 17:2-3: CT keys follow the Vulgate order, not the Saints-slot content (9:15/9:16 swapped; CT 12:5 = Saints 12:7, 12:6 = 12:5, 12:7 = 12:6; 17:2/17:3 swapped). English kept at the Saints slots; pairings in map.
- Sirach 6:34, 14:24, 18:9, 19:5, 24:10, 33:14, 37:17, 45:25, 47:15: one CT verse holds two Saints slots (37:17 holds 37:17-19). English was at the second slot only; it is now split across both slots and the second slot is mapped to the CT key.
- Sirach 20:3-4: CT order is reversed against the Vulgate sentence (20:3 "sic qui facit per vim judicium iniquum", 20:4 "Concupiscentia spadonis..."); English 20:4 gives both in sense order (eunuch clause first).
- Sirach 26:26-28: CT 26:28 ("In duobus contristatum est cor meum...") is the head of the list filed under 26:26-27; English 26:28 now gives the heading first, then the list.
- Sirach 19:6: CT key is a Saints slot but holds Vulgate-only content; it stays an additional item (src 19:6) after English 19:6, which now carries the end of CT 19:5 (Saints 19:6 content).
- Sirach 31:34 (Vulgate-only) stands between CT 31:33 and 31:35, which are both translated within English 31:27; the additional item stays after 31:27.
- Sirach 51:11: no Saints slot (the Saints Greek has no 51:11); split out of English 51:10 as an additional item.
- Sirach 33:16: the Saints Greek places "And I was the last to keep watch" at 36:10 (manuscript-order seam); in the Latin it is CT 33:16 and is translated in English 33:17 (map).
- Genesis 31:55-32:32: CT files Saints 32:1-33 under Vulgate numbers 31:55, 32:1-32 (English kept at Saints slots 32:1-33 and mapped).
- Genesis 50:22: CT 50:22 holds the content of Saints 50:22-23 and there is no CT 50:23; English divided by content, 50:23 mapped to CT 50:22.
- Exodus 36:26-38: CT keys Clementine 39:19-31 at a fixed offset (Saints n = Clem 39:n-7), but by content CT 36:26 = Saints 36:28, CT 36:28-32 = Saints 36:30-34, CT 36:33-34 = Saints 36:35, CT 36:35-37 = Saints 36:36-38, and CT 36:38 (Clem 39:31, 'Perfectum est igitur omne opus') = Saints 39:10. English kept at the Saints slots and mapped.
- Exodus 36:27: CT has Clementine 36:27 ('Contra occidentem vero ... fecit sex tabulas', the west boards of the tabernacle) in the middle of the priestly-garments passage; Clementine 39:20 ('Fecerunt quoque tunicam superhumeralis totam hyacinthinam', = Saints 36:29) is missing from the CT, so Saints 36:29 has no Latin. The boards sentence stays an additional verse after 36:28.
- Exodus 37-38: CT files several Clementine verses under their own Vulgate numbers at Saints slots whose content differs: CT 37:1 (ark = Saints 38:1), 37:4 (ark poles = Saints 38:11), 38:1 (altar of burnt offering = Saints 38:22), 38:20 (pegs = Saints 37:18), 38:2 and 38:6-7 (altar of burnt offering; kept as additional verses after 38:22/38:23); 37:22 and 37:24-28 have no slot.
- Exodus 37-38: Clementine 36:8, 36:36, 37:2, 37:7-8, 37:11-15, 37:20-21 and 38:4-5 are absent from the CT, so Saints 37:1, 37:4, 38:2, 38:6-7, 38:10, 38:14 and 38:24 have no Latin (EN 37:2 therefore opens with a dangling 'of which').
- Exodus 39: CT keys Clementine 38:25-31 and 39:32-43 in an order that differs from the Saints slots; by content (Hebrew-based slots) CT 39:2 = Saints 39:3, CT 39:3-4 = 39:4, CT 39:7 = 39:9, CT 39:10 = 39:13, CT 39:13 = 39:20, CT 39:14 = 39:17, CT 39:16+39:15 = 39:15, CT 39:17 = 39:16, CT 39:20 = 39:14. Saints 39:2 (amount of silver) has no Latin counterpart.
- Exodus 40:31-32 (Clem 40:29-30, the washing of Moses, Aaron and his sons): no Saints slot in ch. 40; the content is Saints 38:27, where the English stands (mapped).
- Leviticus 15:19-23: CT keeps Vulgate numbering; by content CT 15:19-20 = Saints 15:19 and CT 15:21-23 = Saints 15:20-22; Saints 15:23 has no Latin counterpart (English kept at Saints slots and mapped).
- Numbers 3:9, 5:23, 35:13, 35:22, 36:7: each CT verse (Clementine 3:10, 5:25, 35:15, 35:25, 36:8) holds the condensed content of several Saints slots (3:9-10, 5:23-26, 35:13-15, 35:22-25, 36:7-10) and the CT has no verse at the later slots; English divided by content and the later slots mapped to the one CT verse (Saints 35:23 has no Latin counterpart).
- 1 Samuel 20:43: CT files Saints 21:1 ('Et surrexit David, et abiit...') under the Vulgate number 20:43; CT chapter 21 begins at 21:2 (English kept at 21:1 and mapped).
- Psalms 12:1-3: CT 12:1 holds both the superscription (Saints 12:1) and Saints 12:2 ("Usquequo, Domine, oblivisceris me in finem? ..."); CT has no 12:2. CT 12:3 also lacks the Clementine clause "usquequo exaltabitur inimicus meus super me?" (end of Saints 12:3); no English given for it.
- Psalms 15:10: CT 15:10 holds Saints 15:10 and 15:11 ("Notas mihi fecisti vias vitæ ..."); CT has no 15:11.
- Psalms 43:22: CT 43:22 holds Saints 43:22 and 43:23 ("Quoniam propter te mortificamur tota die ..."); CT has no 43:23.
- Psalms 108:16-18: CT 108:17 holds Saints 108:17 and 108:18 ("Et induit maledictionem sicut vestimentum ..."); CT has no 108:18. CT 108:16 ends at "facere misericordiam," and lacks the Clementine continuation "et persecutus est hominem inopem et mendicum, et compunctum corde mortificare" (rest of Saints 108:16); no English given for it.
- Psalms 129:4-6: CT 129:4 holds Saints 129:4 and 129:5 (split after "propter legem tuam"); CT has no 129:5. The CT omits Clementine 129:6 "A custodia matutina usque ad noctem, speret Israël in Domino" (Saints 129:6b-7a); no English given for it.
- Psalms 2:13 and 4:10: CT keeps the Vulgate verses 2:13 and 4:10, which have no Saints slot (their content is the end of Saints 2:12 and 4:9); now given as additional verses.
- Psalms 72:22-24: CT (Vulgate) verse division runs about half a verse behind Saints 72:22-24 ("ut jumentum factus sum apud te" in 72:23, "Tenuisti manum dexteram meam" in 72:24); English follows the CT keys.
- Proverbs 15:28-29 / 16:7-8: CT files the second halves of Saints 15:28 and 15:29 (Hebrew 16:7, 16:8) under the Vulgate numbers 16:7 and 16:8. Those keys are Saints slots holding different Septuagint content (Saints 16:7 = "The beginning of a good way..."; 16:8 = Septuagint-only). English keeps the text at 15:28/15:29 (mapped).
- Proverbs 16:5: the second sentence of CT 16:5 ("Initium viæ bonæ facere justitiam ... immolare hostias") is the content of Saints 16:7. It is left in English 16:5 with the CT.
- Proverbs 16:9: CT 16:9 holds Clementine 16:4 ("Universa propter semetipsum operatus est Dominus..."), and CT has no 16:4. Clementine 16:9 "Cor hominis disponit viam suam, sed Domini est dirigere gressus ejus" (end of Saints 15:29) is missing from the CT; no English given for it.
- Proverbs 20:9 / 20:20-22: CT files the last three proverbs of Saints 20:9 under the Vulgate numbers 20:20-22 (no Saints slot). English keeps them in 20:9 (mapped).
- Proverbs 31:25-28: CT 31:25 = Clementine 31:26 "Os suum aperuit sapientiæ..." and CT 31:26 = Clementine 31:25 "Fortitudo et decor...". The Hebrew-based edition puts "opens her mouth with wisdom" in Saints 31:28 and has no 31:25, while the Greek-based 31:25 holds "strength and splendor". English keeps CT 31:25 in 31:28 (mapped). The Greek- and Hebrew-based editions disagree on the content of 31:25-27.
- Hosea 6:1-3: the CT lacks Clementine 6:2 "quia ipse cepit, et sanabit nos; percutiet, et curabit nos" (second half of Saints 6:1), so no English is given for it. CT 6:2 (= Clementine 6:3) holds Saints 6:2 and 6:3; CT has no 6:3.
- Matthew 17:14: CT 17:14 holds Saints 17:14 and 17:15; CT has no 17:15.
- Matthew 17:21: the CT has no verse for the Clementine "Hoc autem genus non ejicitur nisi per orationem et jejunium" (Clementine 17:20, traditional 17:21; Saints has no slot). No English exists for it (not flagged; left as is).
- Mark 4:40: CT 4:40 holds Saints 4:40 and 4:41; CT has no 4:41.
- Luke 17:35-37: CT 17:35 also contains the traditional verse 17:36 ("duo in agro: unus assumetur, et alter relinquetur"), and CT 17:36 holds "Respondentes dicunt illi: Ubi Domine?", which is the opening of Saints 17:37 (the second half of Clementine 17:37). Translated as the CT stands, with 17:36 as an additional verse after 17:35. If the CT is re-keyed, 17:36 should become "duo in agro..." and "Respondentes..." should rejoin 17:37.
- Acts 9:6-7: CT 9:6 has only "Et tremens ac stupens dixit: Domine, quid me vis facere?" (no Greek counterpart; kept as the existing additional verse, src 9:6, after 9:5). CT files Saints 9:6 ("Et Dominus ad eum: Surge, et ingredere civitatem...") under 9:7 together with Saints 9:7. English 9:6 is kept at its Saints slot and mapped to CT 9:7. Note that the additional-verse src 9:6 has the same key as the regular verse 9:6.
- 2 Corinthians 1:23: CT 1:23 holds Saints 1:23 and 1:24; CT has no 1:24.
- 2 Corinthians 1:23: CT reads "fidei vestæ", a typo for "vestræ".
- 2 Timothy 4:8-10: CT follows the Vulgate division. CT 4:8 ends with Saints 4:9 ("Festina ad me venire cito"), and CT 4:9 ("Demas enim me reliquit...") and 4:10 ("Crescens in Galatiam...") together make up Saints 4:10.
- Revelation 20:7-10: CT follows the Vulgate division. CT 20:7 = Saints 20:7-8; CT 20:8 = Saints 20:9a; CT 20:9 = Saints 20:9b-10a ("Et descendit ignis ... ubi et bestia"); CT 20:10 = Saints 20:10b ("et pseudopropheta cruciabuntur...").
