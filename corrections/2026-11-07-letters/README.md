# Clementine Latin–English review · 7 November 2026

All 351 readings across Galatians, 1 Thessalonians, and 1 Timothy were compared with the displayed Clementine Latin. The pass revised 312 English readings for fidelity and spoken cadence and repaired three glued Latin initials. It kept Vulgate verse boundaries and characteristic readings. An independent Latin specialist and human read-aloud review remain pending.

| Book | Readings reviewed | English revised | Latin spacing repair |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Galatians | 149 | 139 | 3:1 |
| 1 Thessalonians | 89 | 74 | 1:8 |
| 1 Timothy | 113 | 99 | 1:6 |

The saved source blocks matched the prepared content exactly. The English matched on all three staging comparison pages and in every one of the 17 Parallel Reader chapters. Card order, verse identifiers, source links, and the Greek comparison wording were preserved. Existing comparison marks were recalculated for the revised English, and stale wording cues were removed when their quoted phrase no longer appeared. The comparison ratings are separate from the Latin translation review.

Rainbow highlighting uses saved positions tied to exact wording. After the update the comparison pages showed no generated Rainbow marks in the Latin column, and 45 of 351 Latin–English rows in the Parallel Reader still had Rainbow marks. The affected highlight data still needs re-alignment.
