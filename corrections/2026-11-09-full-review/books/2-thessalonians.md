## Proposed English corrections (1)

**2 Thessalonians 2:1** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Rogamus autem vos, fratres, per adventum Domini nostri Jesu Christi, et nostræ congregationis in ipsum:
- Now: Now we appeal to you, brothers, concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering to him,
- Proposed: Now we appeal to you, brothers, by the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering to him,
- Why: "per adventum" with "rogamus" is an adjuration, "by the coming"; "concerning" renders the Greek preposition (hyper), not the Latin "per".

## Notes on the recorded Latin (1)

"Same as reference" means the recorded Latin has the same words as the reference Clementine transcription, so a suspected typo was not introduced by the site.

- **2 Thessalonians 3:12** · suspected-typo · same as reference: "denuntiemus" (subjunctive) appears to be a typo for the Clementine "denuntiamus", indicative parallel to "obsecramus"; the English "We command" already renders the indicative.

## Withdrawn on second reading (1)

- **2 Thessalonians 2:17**: "exhortetur corda vestra": in Christian Latin "exhortari" covers hearten, console; "comfort your hearts" is within its range.
