## Proposed English corrections (1)

**1 John 1:3** · other-text · minor

- Latin: quod vidimus et audivimus, annuntiamus vobis, ut et vos societatem habeatis nobiscum, et societas nostra sit cum Patre, et cum Filio ejus Jesu Christo.
- Now: what we have seen and heard we announce to you also, so that you too may have fellowship with us, and our fellowship may be with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ.
- Proposed: what we have seen and heard we announce to you, so that you too may have fellowship with us, and our fellowship may be with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ.
- Why: Latin has only "annuntiamus vobis, ut et vos": one "et", rendered by "you too". The extra "also" after "to you" reflects the Greek (kai hymin), not this Latin.
