## Proposed English corrections (2)

**Ezra 2:1** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Hi sunt autem provinciæ filii, qui ascenderunt de captivitate, quam transtulerat Nabuchodonosor rex Babylonis in Babylonem, et reversi sunt in Jerusalem et Judam, unusquisque in civitatem suam.
- Now: These are the people of the province who came up from the captivity into which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had carried them in Babylon. They returned to Jerusalem and Judah, each to his own city.
- Proposed: These are the people of the province who came up from the captivity that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had carried away to Babylon. They returned to Jerusalem and Judah, each to his own city.
- Why: "quam transtulerat ... in Babylonem": in with the accusative is motion, "had carried away to Babylon"; the English "had carried them in Babylon" makes it a location and reads as a slip.

**Ezra 8:19** · meaning · minor

- Latin: et Hasabiam, et cum eo Isaiam de filiis Merari, fratresque ejus, et filios ejus viginti :
- Now: and Hashabiah, and with him Jeshaiah from the descendants of Merari, with his kinsmen and their sons, twenty.
- Proposed: and Hashabiah, and with him Jeshaiah from the descendants of Merari, with his kinsmen and his sons, twenty.
- Why: "fratresque ejus, et filios ejus" is "his brothers and his sons"; English "their sons" follows the Hebrew pronoun, not the Latin singular "ejus".

## Notes on the recorded Latin (2)

"Same as reference" means the recorded Latin has the same words as the reference Clementine transcription, so a suspected typo was not introduced by the site.

- **Ezra 6:11** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Latin has "de domum ipsius"; "de" takes the ablative and the Clementine reads "tollatur lignum de domo ipsius". Apparent typo; English is unaffected.
- **Ezra 8:14** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Latin has "De filii Begui"; should read "De filiis Begui, Uthai et Zachur" (ablative plural, as in every parallel verse of the list). English is unaffected.
