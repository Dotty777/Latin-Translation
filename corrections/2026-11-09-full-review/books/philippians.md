## Proposed English corrections (2)

**Philippians 4:6** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Nihil solliciti sitis: sed in omni oratione, et obsecratione, cum gratiarum actione petitiones vestræ innotescant apud Deum.
- Now: Be nothing anxious: but in every thing, by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, let your petitions be made known to God.
- Proposed: Be nothing anxious: but in all prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, let your petitions be made known to God.
- Why: 'in omni oratione, et obsecratione' = in all prayer and supplication ('omni' qualifies 'oratione'); 'in every thing, by prayer' follows the Greek/KJV construction (en panti, te proseuche).

**Philippians 4:18** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: Habeo autem omnia, et abundo: repletus sum, acceptis ab Epaphrodito quæ misistis odorem suavitatis, hostiam acceptam, placentem Deo.
- Now: But I have all and abound: I am filled, having received from Epaphroditus the things you sent, an fragrance of sweetness, an acceptable sacrifice, pleasing to God.
- Proposed: But I have all and abound: I am filled, having received from Epaphroditus the things you sent, a fragrance of sweetness, an acceptable sacrifice, pleasing to God.
- Why: 'an fragrance' is ungrammatical (article left from 'an odour' when the noun was modernised).
