## Proposed English corrections (3)

**Wisdom of Solomon 1:14** · other-text · major

- Latin: Creavit enim ut essent omnia, et sanabiles fecit nationes orbis terrarum : et non est in illis medicamentum exterminii, nec inferorum regnum in terra.
- Now: For He created all things to exist, and made the kinds of things throughout the world capable of health. There is no deadly poison in them, nor is the dominion of the realm of the dead upon the earth.
- Proposed: For He created all things to exist, and made the nations of the world capable of health. There is no deadly poison in them, nor is the dominion of the realm of the dead upon the earth.
- Why: 'nationes orbis terrarum' is 'the nations of the world'; 'the kinds of things throughout the world' follows the Greek (geneseis), not the Latin.

**Wisdom of Solomon 6:14 (Vulgate 6:15)** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Qui de luce vigilaverit ad illam non laborabit ; assidentem enim illam foribus suis inveniet.
- Now: Whoever rises early to seek her will not labor in vain, for he will find her sitting at his doors.
- Proposed: Whoever rises early to seek her will not labor, for he will find her sitting at his doors.
- Why: 'non laborabit' is simply 'will not labor' (he is spared toil, finding her at his door); 'in vain' is added and shifts the sense to labor that succeeds.

**Wisdom of Solomon 7:27** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Et cum sit una, omnia potest ; et in se permanens omnia innovat : et per nationes in animas sanctas se transfert ; amicos Dei et prophetas constituit.
- Now: Although she is one, she can do all things, and remaining in herself, she renews all things. Through the generations she passes into holy souls and makes friends of God and prophets.
- Proposed: Although she is one, she can do all things, and remaining in herself, she renews all things. Through the nations she passes into holy souls and makes friends of God and prophets.
- Why: 'per nationes' is 'through the nations'; 'through the generations' renders the Greek (kata geneas), not the Latin shown.
