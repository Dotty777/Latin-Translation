# 2 Corinthians · Latin–English review, 9 November 2026

255 distinct readings read against the Clementine Latin (255 reader rows). [Method and limits](../README.md). Nothing below has been applied to the staging site.

## Proposed English corrections (9)

**2 Corinthians 2:11** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: ut non circumveniamur a Satana: non enim ignoramus cogitationes ejus.
- Now: That we do not be overreached by Satan. For we are not ignorant of his devices.
- Proposed: That we be not overreached by Satan. For we are not ignorant of his devices.
- Why: 'That we do not be overreached' is broken English (faulty modernisation of 'be not overreached') for 'ut non circumveniamur'.

**2 Corinthians 4:8** · meaning · minor

- Latin: In omnibus tribulationem patimur, sed non angustiamur: aporiamur, sed non destituimur:
- Now: In all things we suffer tribulation: but are not distressed. We are constrained: but are not destitute.
- Proposed: In all things we suffer tribulation: but are not distressed. We are perplexed: but are not destitute.
- Why: 'aporiamur' = we are perplexed, at a loss (DR 'straitened'); 'constrained' (forced, restricted) is not a sense of the verb.

**2 Corinthians 4:17** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Id enim, quod in præsenti est momentaneum et leve tribulationis nostræ, supra modum in sublimitate æternum gloriæ pondus operatur in nobis,
- Now: For that which is at present momentary and light of our tribulation works for us above measure, exceedingly an eternal weight of glory.
- Proposed: For that which is at present momentary and light of our tribulation works in us above measure, exceedingly an eternal weight of glory.
- Why: 'operatur in nobis' = works in us; 'for us' follows the Greek dative, not the Latin 'in nobis'.

**2 Corinthians 5:17** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Si qua ergo in Christo nova creatura, vetera transierunt: ecce facta sunt omnia nova.
- Now: Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, there is a new creation. The old things have passed away; behold, all things have become new.
- Proposed: Therefore, if there is any new creature in Christ, the old things have passed away; behold, all things have become new.
- Why: 'Si qua ... nova creatura' (qua agrees with creatura) = if there is any new creature in Christ; EN follows the Greek 'if anyone is in Christ, new creation', making it the main clause.

**2 Corinthians 6:3** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: Nemini dantes ullam offensionem, ut non vituperetur ministerium nostrum:
- Now: Giving no offence to any man, that our ministry do not be blamed.
- Proposed: Giving no offence to any man, that our ministry be not blamed.
- Why: 'that our ministry do not be blamed' is broken English (faulty modernisation of 'be not blamed') for 'ut non vituperetur'.

**2 Corinthians 7:15** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: et viscera ejus abundantius in vobis sunt, reminiscentis omnium vestrum obedientiam: quomodo cum timore et tremore excepistis illum.
- Now: And his heart are more abundantly towards you: remembering the obedience of you all, how with fear and trembling you received him.
- Proposed: And his heart is more abundantly towards you: remembering the obedience of you all, how with fear and trembling you received him.
- Why: 'his heart are' is ungrammatical (verb left plural after 'bowels' was changed to 'heart' for 'viscera ejus ... sunt').

**2 Corinthians 9:3** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: Misi autem fratres: ut ne quod gloriamur de vobis, evacuetur in hac parte, ut (quemadmodum dixi) parati sitis:
- Now: Now I have sent the brothers, that the thing which we boast of concerning you do not be made void in this behalf, that (as I have said) you may be ready:
- Proposed: Now I have sent the brothers, that the thing which we boast of concerning you be not made void in this behalf, that (as I have said) you may be ready:
- Why: 'do not be made void' is broken English (faulty modernisation of 'be not made void') for 'ut ne ... evacuetur'.

**2 Corinthians 11:11** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Quare? quia non diligo vos? Deus scit.
- Now: Therefore? Because I love you not? God knows it.
- Proposed: Why? Because I love you not? God knows it.
- Why: 'Quare?' is the interrogative 'Why?' (DR 'Wherefore?'); 'Therefore?' is a wrong modernisation and gives no sense.

**2 Corinthians 12:2** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Scio hominem in Christo ante annos quatuordecim, sive in corpore nescio, sive extra corpus nescio, Deus scit, raptum hujusmodi usque ad tertium cælum.
- Now: I know a man in Christ: above fourteen years ago (whether in the body, I know not, or out of the body, I know not: God knows), such a one caught up to the third heaven.
- Proposed: I know a man in Christ: fourteen years ago (whether in the body, I know not, or out of the body, I know not: God knows), such a one caught up to the third heaven.
- Why: 'ante annos quatuordecim' = fourteen years ago; 'above' (more than) is added and has no basis in the Latin.

## English and Latin cover different amounts of text (2)

- **2 Corinthians 8:14**: The Latin verse ends with 'sicut scriptum est:' but the English stops short; 'As it is written:' is printed at the head of the next unit (8:15).
- **2 Corinthians 8:15**: English opens with 'As it is written:', which renders 'sicut scriptum est:' at the end of the previous Latin verse (8:14), not anything in this Latin.

## Notes on the recorded Latin (3)

"Same as reference" means the recorded Latin has the same words as the reference Clementine transcription, so a suspected typo was not introduced by the site.

- **2 Corinthians 1:1** · spacing: Stray space before the comma in the Latin: 'Paulus , Apostolus' should read 'Paulus, Apostolus'.
- **2 Corinthians 1:23** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Typo in the Latin: 'fidei vestæ' should read 'fidei vestræ'.
- **2 Corinthians 12:17** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Apparent typo in the Latin: 'eorum, quod misi ad vos' should read 'eorum, quos misi ad vos' (relative agreeing with eorum; EN 'whom I sent' is right).

## Withdrawn on second reading (1)

- **2 Corinthians 4:1**: Article-less "administrationem" refers back to the ministry just described; "this" is a legitimate determiner choice, as in Douay.
