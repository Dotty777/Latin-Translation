## Proposed English corrections (37)

**Acts 1:7** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Dixit autem eis: Non est vestrum nosse tempora vel momenta quæ Pater posuit in sua potestate:
- Now: He said to them, “It is not for you to know the times or seasons that the Father has set by his own authority.
- Proposed: He said to them, “It is not for you to know the times or moments that the Father has set by his own authority.
- Why: "tempora vel momenta" = times or moments; "seasons" is the Greek/KJV wording, not the Latin "momenta".

**Acts 1:13** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Et cum introissent in cœnaculum, ascenderunt ubi manebant Petrus, et Joannes, Jacobus, et Andreas, Philippus, et Thomas, Bartholomæus, et Matthæus, Jacobus Alphæi, et Simon Zelotes, et Judas Jacobi.
- Now: And when they had come in, they went up into an upper room, where abode Peter and John, James and Andrew, Philip and Thomas, Bartholomew and Matthew, James of Alphaeus and Simon Zelotes and Jude the brother of James.
- Proposed: And when they had come into the upper room, they went up where abode Peter and John, James and Andrew, Philip and Thomas, Bartholomew and Matthew, James of Alphaeus and Simon Zelotes and Jude of James.
- Why: "cum introissent in cœnaculum, ascenderunt ubi manebant": the upper room goes with "entered", not "went up" (Greek order). "Judas Jacobi" = Jude of James; "the brother" is added.

**Acts 1:18** · meaning · minor

**Acts 2:23** · other-text · major

## English and Latin cover different amounts of text (5)

- **Acts 8:7**: English stops short: "Multi autem paralytici et claudi curati sunt" is not rendered here; its English appears under 8:8.
- **Acts 8:8**: English renders the end of the previous Latin verse (paralytici et claudi curati sunt), not "Factum est ergo gaudium magnum in illa civitate", whose English is under 8:9.
- **Acts 8:9**: English begins with "There was therefore great joy in that city", which renders the Latin of 8:8 ("Factum est ergo gaudium magnum"), not this verse.
- **Acts 23:25**: English ends with "And he wrote a letter after this manner:", which renders "Scribens epistolam continentem hæc" at the head of the next Latin verse (23:26), not anything in this verse.
- **Acts 23:26**: English stops short: "Scribens epistolam continentem hæc" (writing a letter containing this) is not rendered here; it is displayed under 23:25 instead.

## Notes on the recorded Latin (4)

"Same as reference" means the recorded Latin has the same words as the reference Clementine transcription, so a suspected typo was not introduced by the site.

- **Acts 2:9** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Typo in the Latin: "Mespotamiam" should read "Mesopotamiam".
- **Acts 5:1** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Latin typo: "cum Saphira uxore suo" should read "cum Saphira uxore sua" (feminine ablative agreeing with uxore).
- **Acts 8:5** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Latin typo: plural "prædicabant" with singular subject Philippus; should read "prædicabat illis Christum".
- **Acts 16:5** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Latin typo: future "abundabunt" beside imperfect "confirmabantur"; should read "abundabant numero quotidie" (as the English "increased" renders).
