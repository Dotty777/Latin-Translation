# 2 Maccabees · Latin–English review, 9 November 2026

555 distinct readings read against the Clementine Latin (555 reader rows). [Method and limits](../README.md). Nothing below has been applied to the staging site.

## Proposed English corrections (1)

**2 Maccabees 12:24** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Ipse vero Timotheus incidit in partes Dosithei et Sosipatris : et multis precibus postulabat ut vivus dimitteretur, eo quod multorum ex Judæis parentes haberet ac fratres, quos morte ejus decipi eveniret.
- Now: Timothy himself fell into the hands of Dositheus's and Sosipater's troops. With many entreaties he asked to be released alive, because he held the parents and brothers of many Jews, who would come to harm through his death.
- Proposed: Timothy himself fell into the hands of Dositheus's and Sosipater's troops. With many entreaties he asked to be released alive, because he held the parents and brothers of many Jews, who would be deceived through his death.
- Why: "quos morte ejus decipi eveniret": decipi means to be deceived or cheated (of their hope), not to "come to harm".

## Notes on the recorded Latin (11)

"Same as reference" means the recorded Latin has the same words as the reference Clementine transcription, so a suspected typo was not introduced by the site.

- **2 Maccabees 3:7** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Latin has "illæ accitum Heliodorum"; "illæ" (feminine plural) has no construction. Read "ille accitum Heliodorum ... misit" (he, the king, summoned and sent Heliodorus). English already renders "ille".
- **2 Maccabees 3:8** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Apparent misspelling in the Latin: "iter est agressus" should read "iter est aggressus" (set out on the journey). English is unaffected.
- **2 Maccabees 4:19** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Typo in the Latin: "in sacrificum Herculis" should read "in sacrificium Herculis" (for the sacrifice of Hercules). English is unaffected.
- **2 Maccabees 4:28** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Number slip in the Latin: "ad hunc exactio vectigalium pertinebant" has a plural verb with singular subject "exactio"; read "pertinebat". English is unaffected.
- **2 Maccabees 5:5** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Apparent misspelling in the Latin: "repente agressus est civitatem" should read "aggressus est" (same spelling recurs at 3:8). English is unaffected.
- **2 Maccabees 8:8** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Latin has "res ei cedere propere" (hastily); the Clementine reads "cedere prospere" (turn out prosperously). The English "going swiftly in his favor" follows the defective "propere" and should be rechecked once the Latin is corrected.
- **2 Maccabees 9:26** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Typo in the Latin: "Ora itaque vos, et peto" should read "Oro itaque vos, et peto" (I beg and ask you). English already renders "Oro".
- **2 Maccabees 10:1** · suspected-typo · differs from reference: Split word in the Latin: "Machab æus autem" should read "Machabæus autem" (the unit's own note acknowledges the split). English is unaffected.
- **2 Maccabees 11:36** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Latin has the infinitive "confestim aliquem mittere", which has no construction; the Clementine reads the imperative "confestim aliquem mittite" (send someone at once). English already renders the imperative.
- **2 Maccabees 14:1** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Latin has "per portam Tripolis" (through the gate); the Clementine reads "per portum Tripolis" (through the harbor), which the English "through the harbor of Tripolis" already renders.
- **2 Maccabees 15:36** · coverage · same as reference: Vulgate 15:37 is missing between this unit and the next (Vg 15:38): "habere autem celebritatem tertiadecima die mensis Adar, quod dicitur voce syriaca, pridie Mardochæi diei." Latin and English both break off at "præterire :" / "celebration,".
