## Proposed English corrections (1)

**Nahum 3:11** · other-text · major

- Latin: Et tu ergo inebriaberis, et eris despecta : et tu quæres auxilium ab inimico.
- Now: You too, then, will become drunk and be despised; you too will seek help against the enemy.
- Proposed: You too, then, will become drunk and be despised; you too will seek help from the enemy.
- Why: 'quæres auxilium ab inimico' is 'you will seek help from the enemy'; 'against the enemy' gives the Hebrew sense (refuge from), not the Latin 'ab inimico'.
