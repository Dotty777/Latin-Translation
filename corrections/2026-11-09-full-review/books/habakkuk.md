## Proposed English corrections (4)

**Habakkuk 1:13** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Mundi sunt oculi tui, ne videas malum, et respicere ad iniquitatem non poteris. Quare respicis super iniqua agentes, et taces devorante impio justiorem se ?
- Now: Your eyes are pure, so that You do not look upon evil, and You cannot look approvingly on iniquity. Why do You look upon those who act wickedly and remain silent while the wicked devours one more just than himself?
- Proposed: Your eyes are pure, so that You do not look upon evil, and You cannot look on iniquity. Why do You look upon those who act wickedly and remain silent while the wicked devours one more just than himself?
- Why: 'respicere ad iniquitatem non poteris' is simply 'You cannot look upon iniquity'; 'approvingly' is added with nothing in the Latin behind it.

**Habakkuk 2:8** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Quia tu spoliasti gentes multas, spoliabunt te omnes qui reliqui fuerint de populis, propter sanguinem hominis, et iniquitatem terræ, civitatis, et omnium habitantium in ea.
- Now: Because you have plundered many nations, all the peoples who remain will plunder you, because of human bloodshed and the violence done to the land, the city, and all who dwell in it.
- Proposed: Because you have plundered many nations, all the peoples who remain will plunder you, because of human bloodshed and the iniquity done to the land, the city, and all who dwell in it.
- Why: 'iniquitatem terræ' is iniquity/wrong; 'violence' is the Hebrew word, not a sense of 'iniquitas'.

**Habakkuk 2:14** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Quia replebitur terra, ut cognoscant gloriam Domini, quasi aquæ operientes mare.
- Now: For the earth will be filled with knowledge of the Lord's glory, like waters covering the sea.
- Proposed: For the earth will be filled, so that they may know the Lord's glory, like waters covering the sea.
- Why: Latin has a purpose clause, 'replebitur terra, ut cognoscant gloriam Domini' (that they may know); 'filled with knowledge of the Lord's glory' is the Hebrew/KJV construction.

**Habakkuk 2:17** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Quia iniquitas Libani operiet te, et vastitas animalium deterrebit eos de sanguinibus hominum, et iniquitate terræ, et civitatis, et omnium habitantium in ea.
- Now: For the violence done to Lebanon will cover you, and the devastation of animals will terrify them, because of human bloodshed and the violence done to the land, the city, and all who dwell in it.
- Proposed: For the iniquity done to Lebanon will cover you, and the devastation of animals will terrify them, because of human bloodshed and the iniquity done to the land, the city, and all who dwell in it.
- Why: 'iniquitas Libani' and 'iniquitate terræ' are iniquity/wrong; 'violence' (twice) is the Hebrew word, not a sense of 'iniquitas'.
