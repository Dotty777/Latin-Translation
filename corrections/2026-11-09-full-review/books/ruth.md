## Proposed English corrections (1)

**Ruth 2:1** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Erat autem viro Elimelech consanguineus, homo potens, et magnarum opum, nomine Booz.
- Now: Elimelech had a kinsman named Boaz, a powerful man of great wealth.
- Proposed: Her husband Elimelech had a kinsman named Boaz, a powerful man of great wealth.
- Why: "viro Elimelech" = (her) husband Elimelech; the English leaves "viro" untranslated.

## Notes on the recorded Latin (2)

"Same as reference" means the recorded Latin has the same words as the reference Clementine transcription, so a suspected typo was not introduced by the site.

- **Ruth 4:9** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Latin has "quod possederim omnis quæ fuerunt"; "omnis" does not agree with neuter plural "quæ fuerunt": read "omnia quæ fuerunt Elimelech". The English "everything that belonged" is correct.
- **Ruth 4:17** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Latin has "Vicinæ autem mulieris congratulantes"; genitive singular "mulieris" is a slip for nominative plural: read "Vicinæ autem mulieres congratulantes ei". The English "The neighboring women" is correct.
