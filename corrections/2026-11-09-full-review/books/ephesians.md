## Proposed English corrections (5)

**Ephesians 1:2** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Gratia vobis, et pax a Deo Patre nostro, et Domino Jesu Christo.
- Now: Grace be to you and peace, from God the Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ.
- Proposed: Grace be to you and peace, from God our Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ.
- Why: 'a Deo Patre nostro' = from God our Father; 'nostro' is left untranslated ('God the Father').

**Ephesians 1:3** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Benedictus Deus et Pater Domini nostri Jesu Christi, qui benedixit nos in omni benedictione spirituali in cælestibus in Christo,
- Now: Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with spiritual blessings in heavenly places, in Christ:
- Proposed: Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with every spiritual blessing in heavenly places, in Christ:
- Why: 'in omni benedictione spirituali' = with every spiritual blessing; 'omni' is dropped ('with spiritual blessings').

**Ephesians 1:7** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: In quo habemus redemptionem per sanguinem ejus, remissionem peccatorum secundum divitias gratiæ ejus,
- Now: In whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his, grace,
- Proposed: In whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his grace,
- Why: Stray comma inside the phrase: 'the riches of his, grace' ('divitias gratiæ ejus').

**Ephesians 2:12** · meaning · minor

- Latin: quia eratis illo in tempore sine Christo, alienati a conversatione Israël, et hospites testamentorum, promissionis spem non habentes, et sine Deo in hoc mundo.
- Now: That you were at that time without Christ, being aliens from the conversation of Israel and strangers to the testament, having no hope of the promise and without God in this world.
- Proposed: That you were at that time without Christ, being aliens from the conversation of Israel and strangers to the testaments, having no hope of the promise and without God in this world.
- Why: 'hospites testamentorum' is plural, strangers to the testaments (covenants); the English has the singular 'the testament'.

**Ephesians 5:2** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: et ambulate in dilectione, sicut et Christus dilexit nos, et tradidit semetipsum pro nobis, oblationem et hostiam Deo in odorem suavitatis.
- Now: And walk in love, as Christ also has loved us and has delivered himself for us, an oblation and a sacrifice to God for an fragrance of sweetness.
- Proposed: And walk in love, as Christ also has loved us and has delivered himself for us, an oblation and a sacrifice to God for a fragrance of sweetness.
- Why: 'an fragrance' is ungrammatical (article left from 'an odour' when the noun was modernised).
