## Proposed English corrections (3)

**Hebrews 5:12** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: Etenim cum deberetis magistri esse propter tempus, rursum indigetis ut vos doceamini quæ sint elementa exordii sermonum Dei: et facti estis quibus lacte opus sit, non solido cibo.
- Now: For whereas for the time you ought to be masters, you have need to be taught again what are the first elements of the words of God: and you are become such as need milk and not of strong food.
- Proposed: For whereas for the time you ought to be masters, you have need to be taught again what are the first elements of the words of God: and you are become such as have need of milk and not of strong food.
- Why: "such as need milk and not of strong food" is broken grammar left by modernising "have need of milk, and not of"; Latin "quibus lacte opus sit, non solido cibo".

**Hebrews 11:23** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Fide Moyses, natus, occultatus est mensibus tribus a parentibus suis, eo quod vidissent elegantem infantem, et non timuerunt regis edictum.
- Now: By faith Moses, when he was born, was hid three months by his parents: because they saw he was a fitting babe, and they feared not the king’s edict.
- Proposed: By faith Moses, when he was born, was hid three months by his parents: because they saw he was a comely babe, and they feared not the king’s edict.
- Why: "elegantem infantem" means a fine, handsome child (Douay "comely"); "fitting" does not render "elegantem" and gives no sense here.

**Hebrews 12:3** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: Recogitate enim eum qui talem sustinuit a peccatoribus adversum semetipsum contradictionem: ut ne fatigemini, animis vestris deficientes.
- Now: For think diligently upon him that endured such opposition from sinners against himself that you do not be wearied, fainting in your minds.
- Proposed: For think diligently upon him that endured such opposition from sinners against himself that you be not wearied, fainting in your minds.
- Why: "that you do not be wearied" is ungrammatical, a slip in modernising "that you be not wearied" for "ut ne fatigemini".
