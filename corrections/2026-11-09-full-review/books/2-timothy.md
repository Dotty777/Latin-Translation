## Proposed English corrections (1)

**2 Timothy 2:22** · other-text · major

- Latin: Juvenilia autem desideria fuge, sectare vero justitiam, fidem, spem, caritatem, et pacem cum iis qui invocant Dominum de corde puro.
- Now: But flee you youthful desires, and pursue justice, faith, love and peace with them that call on the Lord out of a pure heart.
- Proposed: But flee you youthful desires, and pursue justice, faith, hope, love and peace with them that call on the Lord out of a pure heart.
- Why: The Latin list is "justitiam, fidem, spem, caritatem, et pacem"; "spem" (hope) is missing from the English, which keeps the Douay-Rheims list without it.
