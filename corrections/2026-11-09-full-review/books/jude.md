## Proposed English corrections (1)

**Jude 1:9** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Cum Michaël Archangelus cum diabolo disputans altercaretur de Moysi corpore, non est ausus judicium inferre blasphemiæ: sed dixit: Imperet tibi Dominus.
- Now: But when Michael the archangel, disputing with the devil, argued about the body of Moses, he did not dare to bring a blasphemous judgment against him, but said, “May the Lord rebuke you.”
- Proposed: But when Michael the archangel, disputing with the devil, argued about the body of Moses, he did not dare to bring a blasphemous judgment against him, but said, “May the Lord command you.”
- Why: "Imperet tibi Dominus" is "May the Lord command you" (impero = command). "Rebuke" renders the Greek epitimesai (Latin would be increpet), not this Latin.
