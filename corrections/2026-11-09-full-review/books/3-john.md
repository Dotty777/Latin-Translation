## Proposed English corrections (1)

**3 John 1:4** · other-text · major

- Latin: Majorem horum non habeo gratiam, quam ut audiam filios meos in veritate ambulare.
- Now: I have no greater joy than this: to hear that my children are walking in truth.
- Proposed: I have no greater grace than this: to hear that my children are walking in truth.
- Why: "gratiam" is grace/favour; "joy" follows the Greek reading charan (Latin would be gaudium). The Vulgate's distinctive "Majorem horum non habeo gratiam" should be kept.
