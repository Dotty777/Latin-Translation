## Proposed English corrections (4)

**Hosea 2:20 (Vulgate 2:18)** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Et percutiam cum eis fœdus in die illa, cum bestia agri, et cum volucre cæli, et cum reptili terræ ; et arcum, et gladium, et bellum conteram de terra, et dormire eos faciam fiducialiter.
- Now: On that day I will make a covenant for them with the wild beast, the bird of the sky, and the creeping creature of the earth. I will break bow and sword and warfare from the land and make them sleep in safety.
- Proposed: On that day I will make a covenant with them, with the wild beast, the bird of the sky, and the creeping creature of the earth. I will break bow and sword and warfare from the land and make them sleep in safety.
- Why: Latin 'percutiam cum eis fœdus' is 'I will make a covenant with them'; English 'for them' follows the Hebrew, not 'cum eis'.

**Hosea 7:1** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Cum sanare vellem Israël, revelata est iniquitas Ephraim, et malitia Samariæ, quia operati sunt mendacium ; et fur ingressus est spolians, latrunculus foris.
- Now: When I wished to heal Israel, Ephraim's iniquity and Samaria's wickedness were uncovered, for they practiced deceit. A thief entered to plunder, a robber band outside.
- Proposed: When I wished to heal Israel, Ephraim's iniquity and Samaria's wickedness were uncovered, for they practiced deceit. A thief entered to plunder, a robber outside.
- Why: 'latrunculus foris' is singular, 'a robber outside'; 'robber band' adds a collective sense taken from the Hebrew, not in the Latin.

**Hosea 11:4** · other-text · minor

- Latin: In funiculis Adam traham eos, in vinculis caritatis ; et ero eis quasi exaltans jugum super maxillas eorum, et declinavi ad eum ut vesceretur.
- Now: With human cords I will draw them, with bonds of love. I will be to them like one lifting the yoke over their jaws, and I bent down to him so that he might eat.
- Proposed: With the cords of Adam I will draw them, with bonds of love. I will be to them like one lifting the yoke over their jaws, and I bent down to him so that he might eat.
- Why: Latin keeps the name: 'In funiculis Adam' (cords of Adam). 'Human cords' renders the Hebrew common noun, not the Vulgate's proper name (cf. 6:7 'sicut Adam' kept as Adam).

**Hosea 12:9 (Vulgate 12:8)** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Et dixit Ephraim : Verumtamen dives effectus sum ; inveni idolum mihi : omnes labores mei non invenient mihi iniquitatem quam peccavi.
- Now: Ephraim said, “Yet I have become rich; I have found an idol for myself. In all my labors they will find no iniquity in me in which I have sinned.”
- Proposed: Ephraim said, “Yet I have become rich; I have found an idol for myself. All my labors will find for me no iniquity in which I have sinned.”
- Why: 'omnes labores mei non invenient mihi iniquitatem': 'labores' is the subject (my labours will not find me iniquity). 'In all my labors they will find… in me' follows the Hebrew/KJV construction.

## Notes on the recorded Latin (3)

"Same as reference" means the recorded Latin has the same words as the reference Clementine transcription, so a suspected typo was not introduced by the site.

- **Hosea 2:25 (Vulgate 2:23)** · coverage · same as reference: Unit stops at Vg 2:23; Vg 2:24 (second half of Heb 2:25) is absent from Latin and English: 'Et dicam Non populo meo : Populus meus es tu ; et ipse dicet : Deus meus es tu.'
- **Hosea 4:13** · suspected-typo · same as reference: 'ascendebant thymiama' appears to be a slip for the Vulgate's 'accendebant thymiama' (they burned incense); English 'sent incense up' follows the faulty form. Verify against a printed Clementine.
- **Hosea 6:1** · coverage · same as reference: Vg 6:2 is missing from Latin and English between this unit and the next (Vg 6:3): 'quia ipse cepit, et sanabit nos ; percutiet, et curabit nos.' The unit breaks off at 'revertamur ad Dominum,'.
