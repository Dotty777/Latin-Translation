## Proposed English corrections (6)

**1 Maccabees 1:13 (Vulgate 1:14)** · meaning · major

- Latin: Et destinaverunt aliqui de populo, et abierunt ad regem : et dedit illis potestatem ut facerent justitiam gentium.
- Now: Some of the people were appointed and went to the king, and he gave them authority to practice the customs of the nations.
- Proposed: Some of the people resolved on this and went to the king, and he gave them authority to practice the customs of the nations.
- Why: "destinaverunt aliqui de populo" is active: some of the people determined/resolved (to do this). The English passive "were appointed" makes them chosen by others.

**1 Maccabees 2:7** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Et dixit Mathathias : Væ mihi ! ut quid natus sum videre contritionem populi mei, et contritionem civitatis sanctæ, et sedere illic, cum datur in manibus inimicorum ?
- Now: Mattathias said, “Woe to me! Why was I born to see the crushing of my people and the crushing of the holy city, and to sit here while it is given into the hands of enemies?
- Proposed: Mattathias said, “Woe to me! Why was I born to see the crushing of my people and the crushing of the holy city, and to sit there while it is given into the hands of enemies?
- Why: "sedere illic" is "to sit there" (in the holy city); English has "here".

**1 Maccabees 2:48** · meaning · minor

- Latin: et obtinuerunt legem de manibus gentium, et de manibus regum, et non dederant cornu peccatori.
- Now: They rescued the law from the hands of the nations and from the hands of the kings, and did not give the sinner a horn of strength.
- Proposed: They rescued the law from the hands of the nations and from the hands of the kings, and did not give a horn to the sinner.
- Why: "non dederant cornu peccatori": the Latin has only "cornu", a horn. "of strength" is an explanatory addition not in the Latin (acknowledged in the note).

**1 Maccabees 4:59** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Et statuit Judas, et fratres ejus, et universa ecclesia Israël, ut agatur dies dedicationis altaris in temporibus suis ab anno in annum per dies octo a quinta et vigesima die mensis Casleu, cum lætitia et gaudio.
- Now: Judas, his brothers, and the whole assembly of Israel decreed that the days of the altar's dedication should be observed in their season, year by year for eight days, from the twenty-fifth day of the month of Casleu, with joy and gladness.
- Proposed: Judas, his brothers, and the whole assembly of Israel decreed that the day of the altar's dedication should be observed in its season, year by year for eight days, from the twenty-fifth day of the month of Casleu, with joy and gladness.
- Why: "ut agatur dies dedicationis altaris": singular verb, so "the day of the dedication ... should be kept"; English has plural "the days ... in their season" (as the Greek).

**1 Maccabees 7:34** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Et irridens sprevit eos, et polluit : et locutus est superbe,
- Now: He mocked and despised them, treated them with contempt, and spoke arrogantly.
- Proposed: He mocked and despised them, defiled them, and spoke arrogantly.
- Why: "et polluit" means he defiled/polluted (them); "treated them with contempt" merely repeats "sprevit" and loses the defilement.

**1 Maccabees 9:13** · meaning · major

- Latin: exclamaverunt autem et hi qui erant ex parte Judæ etiam ipsi, et commota est terra a voce exercituum : et commissum est prælium a mane usque ad vesperam.
- Now: Those on Judas's side also sounded their trumpets, and the earth shook at the sound of the armies. Battle was joined from morning until evening.
- Proposed: Those on Judas's side also cried out, and the earth shook at the sound of the armies. Battle was joined from morning until evening.
- Why: "exclamaverunt autem et hi ... etiam ipsi": they too cried out. The Latin has no trumpets here; "sounded their trumpets" matches the Greek, not this Latin.
