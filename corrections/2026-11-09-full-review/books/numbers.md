## Proposed English corrections (22)

**Numbers 3:4** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Mortui sunt enim Nadab et Abiu cum offerrent ignem alienum in conspectu Domini in deserto Sinai, absque liberis : functique sunt sacerdotio Eleazar et Ithamar coram Aaron patre suo.
- Now: For Nadab and Abihu died when they offered unauthorized fire before the Lord in the wilderness of Sinai, without children; Eleazar and Ithamar exercised the priesthood in the presence of Aaron their father.
- Proposed: For Nadab and Abihu died when they offered strange fire before the Lord in the wilderness of Sinai, without children; Eleazar and Ithamar exercised the priesthood in the presence of Aaron their father.
- Why: "ignem alienum" is strange/foreign fire; "unauthorized" is an interpretive gloss (modern versions of the Hebrew), not a sense of "alienum".

**Numbers 3:38** · meaning · major

- Latin: Castrametabuntur ante tabernaculum fœderis, id est, ad orientalem plagam, Moyses et Aaron cum filiis suis, habentes custodiam sanctuarii in medio filiorum Israël. Quisquis alienus accesserit, morietur.
- Now: Before the tabernacle of the covenant, that is, on the eastern side, Moses and Aaron shall camp with Aaron's sons, keeping charge of the sanctuary among the children of Israel. Any outsider who approaches shall die.
- Proposed: Before the tabernacle of the covenant, that is, on the eastern side, Moses and Aaron shall camp with their sons, keeping charge of the sanctuary among the children of Israel. Any outsider who approaches shall die.
- Why: "cum filiis suis" = with their sons (of Moses and Aaron, the subject); English narrows it to "Aaron's sons", as in the Hebrew.

**Numbers 5:18** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Cumque steterit mulier in conspectu Domini, discooperiet caput ejus, et ponet super manus illius sacrificium recordationis, et oblationem zelotypiæ : ipse autem tenebit aquas amarissimas, in quibus cum execratione maledicta congessit.
- Now: When the woman stands before the Lord, he shall uncover her head and place in her hands the sacrifice of remembrance and offering of jealousy. He himself shall hold the bitter water into which he has gathered the curses with an imprecation.
- Proposed: When the woman stands before the Lord, he shall uncover her head and place in her hands the sacrifice of remembrance and offering of jealousy. He himself shall hold the most bitter water into which he has gathered the curses with an imprecation.
- Why: "aquas amarissimas" is superlative, most bitter waters; English has only "bitter" (as the Hebrew).

- Proposed: He shall put her under oath and say: If no other man has slept with you, and you have not been defiled by forsaking your husband's bed, this most bitter water into which I have gathered curses shall not harm you.

**Numbers 10:13** · other-text · major

- Now: They moved camp for the first time according to the Lord's command through Moses.

**Numbers 26:62** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Fueruntque omnes qui numerati sunt, viginti tria millia generis masculini ab uno mense et supra : quia non sunt recensiti inter filios Israël, nec eis cum ceteris data possessio est.
- Now: All those numbered were twenty-three thousand males from one month old and upward. They were not recorded among the children of Israel, nor was a possession given them with the others.
- Proposed: All those numbered were twenty-three thousand males from one month old and upward, for they were not recorded among the children of Israel, nor was a possession given them with the others.
- Why: "quia non sunt recensiti" gives the reason (because they were not counted among Israel); the English drops "quia" and makes it a bare statement.

**Numbers 30:8** · meaning · minor

- Latin: quo die audierit vir, et non contradixerit, voti rea erit, reddetque quodcumque promiserat.
- Now: and her husband hears it that day and does not object, she shall be bound by the vow and render whatever she promised.
