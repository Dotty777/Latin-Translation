## Proposed English corrections (1)

**Lamentations 3:37** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Quis est iste qui dixit ut fieret, Domino non jubente ?
- Now: Mem. Who is this who spoke and it came to pass, without the Lord commanding it?
- Proposed: Mem. Who is this who said that a thing should be done, without the Lord commanding it?
- Why: 'qui dixit ut fieret' is a command clause (who said that it should be done); 'who spoke and it came to pass' renders the Hebrew, asserting a fulfilment the Latin does not state.
