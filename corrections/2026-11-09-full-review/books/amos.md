## Proposed English corrections (5)

**Amos 3:9** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Auditum facite in ædibus Azoti, et in ædibus terræ Ægypti, et dicite : Congregamini super montes Samariæ, et videte insanias multas in medio ejus, et calumniam patientes in penetralibus ejus.
- Now: Make it heard in the buildings of Ashdod and in the buildings of the land of Egypt, and say: ‘Gather on the mountains of Samaria and see the many outrages in her midst, and those suffering oppression in her inner chambers.’
- Proposed: Make it heard in the buildings of Ashdod and in the buildings of the land of Egypt, and say: ‘Gather on the mountains of Samaria and see the many acts of madness in her midst, and those suffering oppression in her inner chambers.’
- Why: 'insanias multas' means many madnesses/follies; 'outrages' is not a sense of 'insania'.

**Amos 4:4** · other-text · minor

- Latin: [Venite ad Bethel, et impie agite ; ad Galgalam, et multiplicate prævaricationem : et afferte mane victimas vestras, tribus diebus decimas vestras.
- Now: “Come to Bethel and act wickedly; to Gilgal and multiply transgression. Bring your victims in the morning and your tithes every three days.
- Proposed: “Come to Bethel and act wickedly; to Gilgal and multiply transgression. Bring your victims in the morning and your tithes in three days.
- Why: 'tribus diebus decimas vestras' is 'your tithes in three days'; 'every three days' is a reading of the Hebrew, not of the Latin ablative.

**Amos 5:12** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Quia cognovi multa scelera vestra, et fortia peccata vestra : hostes justi, accipientes munus, et pauperes deprimentes in porta.
- Now: For I know your many crimes and your mighty sins: enemies of the righteous, accepting bribes and thrusting aside the poor at the gate.
- Proposed: For I know your many crimes and your mighty sins: enemies of the righteous, accepting bribes and oppressing the poor at the gate.
- Why: 'pauperes deprimentes in porta' is pressing down/oppressing the poor; 'thrusting aside' renders the Hebrew verb, not 'deprimentes'.

**Amos 7:1** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Hæc ostendit mihi Dominus Deus : et ecce fictor locustæ in principio germinantium serotini imbris, et ecce serotinus post tonsionem regis.
- Now: The Lord God showed me this: see, One forming locusts at the beginning of the growth after the late rain; and see, the late growth was after the king's mowing.
- Proposed: The Lord God showed me this: see, One forming locusts at the beginning of the growth after the late rain; and see, the late rain was after the king's mowing.
- Why: 'et ecce serotinus post tonsionem regis': 'serotinus' (masc.) is the late rain (imber) just named; 'late growth' follows the Hebrew.

**Amos 9:6** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Qui ædificat in cælo ascensionem suam, et fasciculum suum super terram fundavit ; qui vocat aquas maris, et effundit eas super faciem terræ : Dominus nomen ejus.
- Now: He builds His ascent in heaven and has founded His vault upon the earth; He calls the waters of the sea and pours them over the earth's surface—the Lord is His name.
- Proposed: He builds His ascent in heaven and has founded His bundle upon the earth; He calls the waters of the sea and pours them over the earth's surface—the Lord is His name.
- Why: 'fasciculum suum' means His bundle; 'vault' is the modern rendering of the Hebrew word, not a sense of 'fasciculus'.

## Notes on the recorded Latin (2)

- **Amos 6:10** · coverage · same as reference: Vg 6:11 is missing from Latin and English between this unit (Vg 6:10) and the next (Vg 6:12): 'Et respondebit : Finis est. Et dicet ei : Tace, et non recorderis nominis Domini.'
