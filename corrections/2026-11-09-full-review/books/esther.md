## Proposed English corrections (1)

**Esther 6:8** · meaning · minor

- Latin: debet indui vestibus regiis, et imponi super equum, qui de sella regis est, et accipere regium diadema super caput suum :
- Now: ought to be dressed in royal garments, placed upon a horse from the king's own stable, and receive the royal diadem upon his head.
- Proposed: ought to be dressed in royal garments, placed upon a horse the king himself rides, and receive the royal diadem upon his head.
- Why: "equum, qui de sella regis est" is the horse "of the king's saddle", one the king himself rides; "sella" is saddle or seat, not "stable".
