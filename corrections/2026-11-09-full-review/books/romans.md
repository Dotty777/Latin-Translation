## Proposed English corrections (9)

**Romans 2:5** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: Secundum autem duritiam tuam, et impœnitens cor, thesaurizas tibi iram in die iræ, et revelationis justi judicii Dei,
- Now: But according to your hardness and impenitent heart, you store up up to yourself wrath, against the day of wrath and revelation of the just judgment of God:
- Proposed: But according to your hardness and impenitent heart, you store up to yourself wrath, against the day of wrath and revelation of the just judgment of God:
- Why: Doubled word "up up" in the rendering of "thesaurizas tibi iram".

**Romans 2:26** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Si igitur præputium justitias legis custodiat, nonne præputium illius in circumcisionem reputabitur?
- Now: If then, the uncircumcised keep the justices of the law, shall not this uncircumcision be counted for circumcision?
- Proposed: If then, the uncircumcised keep the justices of the law, shall not his uncircumcision be counted for circumcision?
- Why: "præputium illius" is "his uncircumcision" (genitive of ille); "this uncircumcision" loses the possessive.

**Romans 4:17** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: (sicut scriptum est: Quia patrem multarum gentium posui te) ante Deum, cui credidit, qui vivificat mortuos, et vocat ea quæ non sunt, tamquam ea quæ sunt:
- Now: (As it is written: I have made you a father of many nations), before God, whom he believed: who gives life the dead and calls those things that are not, as those that are.
- Proposed: (As it is written: I have made you a father of many nations), before God, whom he believed: who gives life to the dead and calls those things that are not, as those that are.
- Why: Missing word: "who gives life the dead" for "qui vivificat mortuos" needs "to".

**Romans 6:18** · meaning · major

- Latin: Liberati autem a peccato, servi facti estis justitiæ.
- Now: Being then freed from sin, we have been made servants of justice.
- Proposed: Being then freed from sin, you have been made servants of justice.
- Why: "servi facti estis justitiæ" is second person plural (you have been made); the English has "we have been made", changing who is addressed.

**Romans 8:16** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Ipse enim Spiritus testimonium reddit spiritui nostro quod sumus filii Dei.
- Now: The Spirit himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God.
- Proposed: The Spirit himself bears witness to our spirit that we are children of God.
- Why: "testimonium reddit spiritui nostro" is dative, gives testimony to our spirit; "with our spirit" follows the Greek compound verb, not the Latin.

**Romans 10:8** · other-text · minor · amended on second reading

- Latin: Sed quid dicit Scriptura? Prope est verbum in ore tuo, et in corde tuo: hoc est verbum fidei, quod prædicamus.
- Now: But what says the scripture? The word is near you; even in your mouth and in your heart. This is the word of faith, which we preach.
- Proposed: But what says the scripture? The word is near, in your mouth and in your heart. This is the word of faith, which we preach.
- Why: "Prope est verbum in ore tuo, et in corde tuo" has no "te"; "near you; even" follows the Greek/Douay wording, adding a pronoun the Latin lacks.

**Romans 13:12** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: Nox præcessit, dies autem appropinquavit. Abjiciamus ergo opera tenebrarum, et induamur arma lucis.
- Now: The night is passed And the day is near. Let us, therefore cast off the works of darkness and put on the armour of light.
- Proposed: The night is passed and the day is near. Let us, therefore cast off the works of darkness and put on the armour of light.
- Why: Stray capital mid-sentence: "is passed And the day" ("Nox præcessit, dies autem appropinquavit").

**Romans 15:33** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: Deus autem pacis sit cum omnibus vobis. Amen.
- Now: Now the God of peace be with, you all. Amen.
- Proposed: Now the God of peace be with you all. Amen.
- Why: Stray comma splitting "with, you all" ("sit cum omnibus vobis").

**Romans 16:11** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: Salutate eos qui sunt ex Aristoboli domo. Salutate Herodionem cognatum meum. Salutate eos qui sunt ex Narcisi domo, qui sunt in Domino.
- Now: Greet them that are of Aristobulus’ household. Greet Herodian, my kinsman. Greet them that are of Narcissus’ household, who are in the Lord.
- Proposed: Greet them that are of Aristobulus’ household. Greet Herodion, my kinsman. Greet them that are of Narcissus’ household, who are in the Lord.
- Why: Name misspelt: "Herodionem" is the accusative of Herodion; "Herodian" is not the name (same person, wrong spelling).

## Notes on the recorded Latin (5)
