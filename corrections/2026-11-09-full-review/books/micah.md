## Proposed English corrections (1)

**Micah 7:1** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Væ mihi, quia factus sum sicut qui colligit in autumno racemos vindemiæ ! non est botrus ad comedendum, præcoquas ficus desideravit anima mea.
- Now: Woe to me, for I have become like one gathering clusters after the autumn vintage! There is no bunch to eat; my soul has longed for early figs.
- Proposed: Woe to me, for I have become like one gathering in autumn the clusters of the vintage! There is no bunch to eat; my soul has longed for early figs.
- Why: 'qui colligit in autumno racemos vindemiæ' is one who gathers in autumn the clusters of the vintage; 'after the autumn vintage' adds 'after' and reattaches 'autumno' to the vintage.

## Notes on the recorded Latin (2)

"Same as reference" means the recorded Latin has the same words as the reference Clementine transcription, so a suspected typo was not introduced by the site.

- **Micah 3:1** · suspected-typo · same as reference: 'Audite, princeps Jacob' looks like a slip for the Vulgate's plural 'principes Jacob' (plural 'Audite', 'vestrum'; cf. 3:9 'principes domus Jacob'). English 'prince of Jacob' follows the singular. Verify against a printed Clementine.
- **Micah 3:7** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Typo in the Latin: 'omnes vultos suos' should be 'omnes vultus suos' (fourth-declension accusative plural).
