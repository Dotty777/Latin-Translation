## Proposed English corrections (6)

**Nehemiah 3:1** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Et surrexit Eliasib sacerdos magnus, et fratres ejus sacerdotes, et ædificaverunt portam gregis : ipsi sanctificaverunt eam, et statuerunt valvas ejus, et usque ad turrim centum cubitorum sanctificaverunt eam, usque ad turrim Hananeel.
- Now: Eliashib the high priest and his fellow priests rose and built the Sheep Gate. They consecrated it and set up its doors, and consecrated the wall as far as the tower of a hundred cubits, as far as the Tower of Hananel.
- Proposed: Eliashib the high priest and his fellow priests rose and built the Sheep Gate. They consecrated it and set up its doors, and consecrated it as far as the tower of a hundred cubits, as far as the Tower of Hananel.
- Why: "usque ad turrim centum cubitorum sanctificaverunt eam": "eam" is the same "it" (portam) as before; English supplies "the wall", a noun the Latin does not have.

**Nehemiah 3:20** · other-text · major

- Latin: Post eum in monte ædificavit Baruch filius Zachai mensuram secundam, ab angulo usque ad portam domus Eliasib sacerdotis magni.
- Now: After him Baruch son of Zabbai built a second section on the hill, from the corner to the entrance of the house of Eliashib the high priest.
- Proposed: After him Baruch son of Zaccai built a second section on the hill, from the corner to the entrance of the house of Eliashib the high priest.
- Why: Latin "Baruch filius Zachai" is Zaccai; English "Zabbai" is the Hebrew written reading, not the name the Vulgate gives.

**Nehemiah 5:4** · meaning · minor · amended on second reading

- Latin: Et alii dicebant : Mutuo sumamus pecunias in tributa regis, demusque agros nostros et vineas :
- Now: Still others said, “Let us borrow money for the king's taxes and give our fields and vineyards as security.
- Proposed: Still others said, “Let us borrow money for the king's taxes and give our fields and vineyards.
- Why: "demusque agros nostros et vineas" is simply "and let us give our fields and vineyards"; "as security" is an interpretive addition with no Latin behind it.

**Nehemiah 8:4** · other-text · major

- Latin: Stetit autem Esdras scriba super gradum ligneum, quem fecerat ad loquendum : et steterunt juxta eum Mathathias, et Semeia, et Ania, et Uria, et Helcia, et Maasia, ad dexteram ejus : et ad sinistram, Phadaia, Misaël, et Melchia, et Hasum, et Hasbadana, Zacharia, et Mosollam.
- Now: Ezra the scribe stood on a wooden platform he had made for speaking. Beside him on his right stood Mattithiah, Shema, Anaiah, Uriah, Hilkiah, and Maaseiah; on his left were Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchijah, Hashum, Hashbaddanah, Zechariah, and Meshullam.
- Proposed: Ezra the scribe stood on a wooden platform he had made for speaking. Beside him on his right stood Mattithiah, Shemaiah, Anaiah, Uriah, Hilkiah, and Maaseiah; on his left were Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchijah, Hashum, Hashbaddanah, Zechariah, and Meshullam.
- Why: Latin "Semeia" is the name Shemaiah (as rendered elsewhere in this book); English "Shema" is the Hebrew form of the name here, not the Vulgate's.

**Nehemiah 9:5** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Et dixerunt Levitæ Josue, et Cedmihel, Bonni, Hasebnia, Serebia, Odaia, Sebnia, Phathathia : Surgite, benedicite Domino Deo vestro ab æterno usque in æternum : et benedicant nomini gloriæ tuæ excelso in omni benedictione et laude.
- Now: The Levites Jeshua, Kadmiel, Bunni, Hashabneiah, Sherebiah, Hodiah, Shebaniah, and Pethahiah said, “Rise and bless the Lord your God from everlasting to everlasting. Let them bless Your glorious name, exalted above every blessing and praise.
- Proposed: The Levites Jeshua, Kadmiel, Bunni, Hashabneiah, Sherebiah, Hodiah, Shebaniah, and Pethahiah said, “Rise and bless the Lord your God from everlasting to everlasting. Let them bless Your glorious and exalted name with every blessing and praise.
- Why: "benedicant nomini gloriæ tuæ excelso in omni benedictione et laude": "excelso" qualifies the name, and "in omni" is "with all"; "exalted above every blessing" is the Hebrew sense, not the Latin.

**Nehemiah 9:6** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Tu ipse, Domine, solus, tu fecisti cælum, et cælum cælorum, et omnem exercitum eorum : terram, et universa quæ in ea sunt : maria, et omnia quæ in eis sunt : et tu vivificas omnia hæc, et exercitus cæli te adorat.
- Now: You alone are the Lord. You made heaven, the heaven of heavens, and all their host; the earth and everything upon it; the seas and everything in them. You give life to all these, and the host of heaven worships You.
- Proposed: You Yourself, Lord, You alone made heaven, the heaven of heavens, and all their host; the earth and everything upon it; the seas and everything in them. You give life to all these, and the host of heaven worships You.
- Why: "Tu ipse, Domine, solus, tu fecisti cælum": "Domine" is vocative and "solus" goes with the making; "You alone are the Lord" is the Hebrew statement, not in the Latin.
