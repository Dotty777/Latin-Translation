## Proposed English corrections (2)

**Colossians 3:20** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Filii, obedite parentibus per omnia: hoc enim placitum est in Domino.
- Now: Children, obey your parents in all things: for this is well pleasing to the Lord.
- Proposed: Children, obey your parents in all things: for this is well pleasing in the Lord.
- Why: 'placitum est in Domino' = is well pleasing in the Lord; 'to the Lord' follows the Greek received text (to Kyrio), not the Latin 'in Domino'.

**Colossians 4:3** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: orantes simul et pro nobis, ut Deus aperiat nobis ostium sermonis ad loquendum mysterium Christi (propter quod etiam vinctus sum),
- Now: Praying also for us also, that God may open to us a door of speech to speak the mystery of Christ (for which also I am bound):
- Proposed: Praying at the same time for us also, that God may open to us a door of speech to speak the mystery of Christ (for which also I am bound):
- Why: Doubled word: 'Praying also for us also' (DR 'withal' modernised to a second 'also'); 'simul et pro nobis' = at the same time for us also.
