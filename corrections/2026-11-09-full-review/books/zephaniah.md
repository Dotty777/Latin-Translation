## Proposed English corrections (2)

**Zephaniah 1:13** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Et erit fortitudo eorum in direptionem, et domus eorum in desertum : et ædificabunt domos, et non habitabunt ; et plantabunt vineas, et non bibent vinum earum.
- Now: Their wealth will become plunder and their houses a wasteland. They will build houses but not inhabit them; they will plant vineyards but not drink their wine.
- Proposed: Their strength will become plunder and their houses a wasteland. They will build houses but not inhabit them; they will plant vineyards but not drink their wine.
- Why: 'fortitudo eorum' is their strength; 'wealth' renders the Hebrew word, not 'fortitudo'.

**Zephaniah 3:4 (Vulgate 3:5)** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Prophetæ ejus vesani, viri infideles ; sacerdotes ejus polluerunt sanctum, injuste egerunt contra legem.
- Now: Her prophets are reckless, faithless men. Her priests have defiled the holy place and acted unjustly against the law.
- Proposed: Her prophets are mad, faithless men. Her priests have defiled the holy place and acted unjustly against the law.
- Why: 'vesani' means mad, senseless; 'reckless' is the usual rendering of the Hebrew word, not of 'vesani'.
