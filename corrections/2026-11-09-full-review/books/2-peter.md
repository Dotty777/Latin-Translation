## Proposed English corrections (4)

**2 Peter 1:11** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: Sic enim abundanter ministrabitur vobis introitus in æternum regnum Domini nostri et Salvatoris Jesu Christi.
- Now: For so an entrance shall be ministered to you abundantly into the ever-lasting kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.
- Proposed: For so an entrance shall be ministered to you abundantly into the everlasting kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.
- Why: "ever-lasting" carries a stray hyphen; "æternum regnum" is "everlasting kingdom".

**2 Peter 1:19** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: Et habemus firmiorem propheticum sermonem: cui benefacitis attendentes quasi lucernæ lucenti in caliginoso loco donec dies elucescat, et lucifer oriatur in cordibus vestris:
- Now: And we have the more firm prophetical word: to what you do well to attend, as to a light that shines in a dark place, until the day dawn and the day star arise in your hearts.
- Proposed: And we have the more firm prophetical word: to which you do well to attend, as to a light that shines in a dark place, until the day dawn and the day star arise in your hearts.
- Why: "to what you do well to attend" is a faulty modernisation of "whereunto"; the relative "cui" needs "to which".

**2 Peter 2:4** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: Si enim Deus angelis peccantibus non pepercit, sed rudentibus inferni detractos in tartarum tradidit cruciandos, in judicium reservari.
- Now: For if God spared not the angels that sinned, but delivered them, drawn down by infernal ropes to the lower the underworld, to torments, to be reserved to judgment:
- Proposed: For if God spared not the angels that sinned, but delivered them, drawn down by infernal ropes to the lower underworld, to torments, to be reserved to judgment:
- Why: "to the lower the underworld" has a doubled article left by replacing "hell"; Latin "in tartarum".

**2 Peter 2:8** · english-typo · minor

- Latin: aspectu enim, et auditu justus erat: habitans apud eos, qui de die in diem animam justam iniquis operibus cruciabant.
- Now: For in sight and hearing he was just, dwelling among them who from day today vexed the just soul with unjust works.
- Proposed: For in sight and hearing he was just, dwelling among them who from day to day vexed the just soul with unjust works.
- Why: "from day today" is a typo for "from day to day" ("de die in diem").

## Notes on the recorded Latin (1)

"Same as reference" means the recorded Latin has the same words as the reference Clementine transcription, so a suspected typo was not introduced by the site.

- **2 Peter 2:19** · suspected-typo · same as reference: "servi sint corruptionem" is a typo for the Clementine "servi sint corruptionis" (genitive, "slaves of corruption"), as the English has it.
