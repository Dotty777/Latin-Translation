## Proposed English corrections (9)

**Daniel 8:2** · other-text · minor

- Latin: vidi in visione mea, cum essem in Susis castro, quod est in Ælam regione : vidi autem in visione esse me super portam Ulai.
- Now: saw in my vision that I was in the citadel of Susa, in the province of Elam. In the vision I saw myself above the gate of Ulai.
- Proposed: saw in my vision, when I was in the citadel of Susa, in the province of Elam. In the vision I saw myself above the gate of Ulai.
- Why: 'cum essem in Susis castro' is 'when I was in the citadel of Susa' (his location while seeing); 'that I was' makes it the content of the vision, as in the Hebrew.

**Daniel 8:10** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Et magnificatum est usque ad fortitudinem cæli : et dejecit de fortitudine, et de stellis, et conculcavit eas.
- Now: It grew great as far as the host of heaven and cast down some of the host and of the stars and trampled them.
- Proposed: It grew great as far as the strength of heaven and cast down some of the strength and of the stars and trampled them.
- Why: 'fortitudinem cæli … de fortitudine' is 'the strength of heaven … of the strength'; 'host' renders the Hebrew word, not 'fortitudo'.

**Daniel 8:11** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Et usque ad principem fortitudinis magnificatum est : et ab eo tulit juge sacrificium, et dejecit locum sanctificationis ejus.
- Now: It grew great even against the prince of the host. It took the continual sacrifice away from him and cast down the place of his sanctuary.
- Proposed: It grew great even against the prince of the strength. It took the continual sacrifice away from him and cast down the place of his sanctuary.
- Why: 'principem fortitudinis' is 'the prince of the strength'; 'host' follows the Hebrew, not 'fortitudo'.

**Daniel 8:13** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Et audivi unum de sanctis loquentem : et dixit unus sanctus alteri nescio cui loquenti : Usquequo visio, et juge sacrificium, et peccatum desolationis quæ facta est : et sanctuarium, et fortitudo conculcabitur ?
- Now: I heard one of the holy ones speaking, and one holy one said to another who was speaking—I do not know who—‘How long will the vision last, concerning the continual sacrifice and the sin of desolation that has occurred? How long will the sanctuary and the host be trampled?’
- Proposed: I heard one of the holy ones speaking, and one holy one said to another who was speaking—I do not know who—‘How long will the vision last, concerning the continual sacrifice and the sin of desolation that has occurred? How long will the sanctuary and the strength be trampled?’
- Why: 'sanctuarium, et fortitudo conculcabitur': 'fortitudo' is 'the strength'; 'host' follows the Hebrew.

**Daniel 9:17** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Nunc ergo exaudi, Deus noster, orationem servi tui, et preces ejus : et ostende faciem tuam super sanctuarium tuum, quod desertum est propter temetipsum.
- Now: Now, therefore, our God, hear the prayer and supplications of Your servant, and for Your own sake let Your face shine upon Your desolate sanctuary.
- Proposed: Now, therefore, our God, hear the prayer and supplications of Your servant, and for Your own sake show Your face upon Your desolate sanctuary.
- Why: 'ostende faciem tuam super sanctuarium' is 'show Your face upon'; 'let Your face shine' is the Hebrew idiom, not 'ostende'.

**Daniel 9:19** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Exaudi, Domine ; placare Domine : attende et fac : ne moreris propter temetipsum, Deus meus, quia nomen tuum invocatum est super civitatem et super populum tuum.
- Now: Hear, Lord; forgive, Lord; attend and act. Do not delay, for Your own sake, my God, because Your name is invoked over Your city and Your people.”
- Proposed: Hear, Lord; be appeased, Lord; attend and act. Do not delay, for Your own sake, my God, because Your name is invoked over Your city and Your people.”
- Why: 'placare, Domine' is 'be appeased, Lord'; 'forgive' follows the Hebrew, not the Latin verb.

**Daniel 9:24** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Septuaginta hebdomades abbreviatæ sunt super populum tuum et super urbem sanctam tuam, ut consummetur prævaricatio, et finem accipiat peccatum, et deleatur iniquitas, et adducatur justitia sempiterna, et impleatur visio et prophetia, et ungatur Sanctus sanctorum.
- Now: Seventy weeks have been shortened for your people and your holy city, so that transgression may be brought to completion, sin come to an end, iniquity be wiped away, everlasting justice be brought in, vision and prophecy be fulfilled, and the Holy of Holies be anointed.
- Proposed: Seventy weeks have been shortened for your people and your holy city, so that transgression may be brought to completion, sin come to an end, iniquity be wiped away, everlasting justice be brought in, vision and prophecy be fulfilled, and the Holy One of holy ones be anointed.
- Why: 'ungatur Sanctus sanctorum' is masculine, a person ('the Saint of saints'); 'the Holy of Holies' suggests the place (Sanctum sanctorum) and matches the Hebrew rather than this Latin.

**Daniel 11:10** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Filii autem ejus provocabuntur, et congregabunt multitudinem exercituum plurimorum : et veniet properans, et inundans : et revertetur, et concitabitur, et congredietur cum robore ejus.
- Now: His sons will be provoked and gather a multitude of very great armies. He will come swiftly, sweeping in like a flood; he will return, be stirred up, and engage his stronghold.
- Proposed: His sons will be provoked and gather a multitude of very great armies. He will come swiftly, sweeping in like a flood; he will return, be stirred up, and engage his forces.
- Why: 'congredietur cum robore ejus' is 'will join battle with his strength/forces'; 'stronghold' follows the Hebrew ('fortress'), not 'robur'.

**Daniel 11:36** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Et faciet juxta voluntatem suam rex, et elevabitur, et magnificabitur adversus omnem deum : et adversus Deum deorum loquetur magnifica, et dirigetur, donec compleatur iracundia : perpetrata quippe est definitio.
- Now: The king will do as he wills. He will exalt himself and magnify himself against every god; against the God of gods he will speak monstrous things. He will prosper until wrath is completed, for the determination has been made.
- Proposed: The king will do as he wills. He will exalt himself and magnify himself against every god; against the God of gods he will speak great things. He will prosper until wrath is completed, for the determination has been made.
- Why: 'loquetur magnifica' is 'will speak great (boastful) things'; 'monstrous' is not a sense of 'magnifica'.
