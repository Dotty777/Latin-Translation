## Proposed English corrections (2)

**Malachi 2:12** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Disperdet Dominus virum qui fecerit hoc, magistrum et discipulum, de tabernaculis Jacob, et offerentem munus Domino exercituum.
- Now: May the Lord destroy from the tents of Jacob the man who does this, teacher and disciple, and the one who presents an offering to the Lord of hosts.
- Proposed: The Lord will destroy from the tents of Jacob the man who does this, teacher and disciple, and the one who presents an offering to the Lord of hosts.
- Why: "Disperdet Dominus" is future indicative, a statement that the Lord will destroy; "May the Lord destroy" turns it into a wish (that would be "disperdat").

**Malachi 3:1** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Ecce ego mitto angelum meum, et præparabit viam ante faciem meam : et statim veniet ad templum suum Dominator quem vos quæritis, et angelus testamenti quem vos vultis. Ecce venit, dicit Dominus exercituum.
- Now: Look, I am sending My messenger, and he will prepare the way before Me. Suddenly the Ruler whom you seek will come to His temple, and the messenger of the covenant whom you desire. Look, he is coming, says the Lord of hosts.
- Proposed: Look, I am sending My messenger, and he will prepare the way before Me. Immediately the Ruler whom you seek will come to His temple, and the messenger of the covenant whom you desire. Look, he is coming, says the Lord of hosts.
- Why: "statim veniet" = will come at once, immediately; "Suddenly" renders the Hebrew adverb, not "statim".
