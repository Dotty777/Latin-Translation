## Proposed English corrections (1)

**Philemon 1:15** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Forsitan enim ideo discessit ad horam a te, ut æternum illum reciperes:
- Now: For perhaps he was separated from you for a time so that you might receive him forever,
- Proposed: For perhaps he departed from you for a time so that you might receive him forever,
- Why: "discessit" is active, "he departed"; the passive "was separated" renders the Greek passive verb, not the Latin.
