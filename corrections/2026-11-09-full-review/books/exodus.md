## Proposed English corrections (29)

**Exodus 1:11** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Præposuit itaque eis magistros operum, ut affligerent eos oneribus : ædificaveruntque urbes tabernaculorum Pharaoni, Phithom et Ramesses.
- Now: So he appointed taskmasters over them to afflict them with burdens. They built storage cities for Pharaoh: Pithom and Rameses.
- Proposed: So he appointed taskmasters over them to afflict them with burdens. They built cities of tabernacles for Pharaoh: Pithom and Rameses.
- Why: "urbes tabernaculorum" is "cities of tabernacles (tents)"; "storage cities" renders the Hebrew, not this Latin.

**Exodus 1:19** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Quæ responderunt : Non sunt Hebreæ sicut ægyptiæ mulieres : ipsæ enim obstetricandi habent scientiam, et priusquam veniamus ad eas, pariunt.
- Now: They answered, “The Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women. They themselves know how to give birth, and they deliver before we come to them.”
- Proposed: They answered, “The Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women. They themselves have the skill of midwifery, and they deliver before we come to them.”
- Why: "obstetricandi habent scientiam" means they have the knowledge of acting as midwife (obstetricare), not knowledge of "how to give birth".

**Exodus 4:10** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Ait Moyses : Obsecro, Domine, non sum eloquens ab heri et nudiustertius : et ex quo locutus es ad servum tuum, impeditioris et tardioris linguæ sum.
- Now: Moses said, “Please, Lord, I have not been eloquent, either yesterday or the day before. Even since You spoke to Your servant, my tongue has been hindered and slow.”
- Proposed: Moses said, “Please, Lord, I have not been eloquent, either yesterday or the day before. And since You spoke to Your servant, my tongue has been more hindered and slower.”
- Why: "et ex quo locutus es... impeditioris et tardioris linguæ sum": comparatives, "more hindered and slower" since God spoke; English drops the comparison and turns "et ex quo" into "Even since".

**Exodus 17:16** · meaning · minor

## Notes on the recorded Latin (4)

"Same as reference" means the recorded Latin has the same words as the reference Clementine transcription, so a suspected typo was not introduced by the site.

- **Exodus 28:4** · suspected-typo · same as reference: "vestimenta quæ faciet" appears to be a slip for "quæ facient" (plural, the wise-hearted of v.3; next sentence 'Facient vestimenta sancta'). English 'they shall make' matches facient. Verify against the Clementine.
- **Exodus 29:17** · suspected-typo · same as reference: "secabis in frustra" (frustra = 'in vain') is a typo for "secabis in frusta" ('into pieces'), which the English correctly renders.
- **Exodus 29:29** · suspected-typo · same as reference: "ut ungantur in ea, et consecrantur manus eorum": the indicative appears to be a slip for the subjunctive "consecrentur", parallel to ungantur after ut. Verify against the Clementine; English is unaffected.
- **Exodus 30:6** · suspected-typo · same as reference: "quod ante arcum pendet testimonii" (arcum = 'bow') is a typo for "ante arcam pendet testimonii" ('before the ark of the testimony'), as the English has it.
