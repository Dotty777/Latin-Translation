## Proposed English corrections (1)

**1 Timothy 6:10** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Radix enim omnium malorum est cupiditas: quam quidam appetentes erraverunt a fide, et inseruerunt se doloribus multis.
- Now: For greed is the root of all evils. In pursuing it, some have wandered from the faith and pierced themselves with many sorrows.
- Proposed: For greed is the root of all evils. In pursuing it, some have wandered from the faith and entangled themselves in many sorrows.
- Why: "inseruerunt se doloribus multis" means they thrust or entangled themselves in many sorrows; "pierced themselves" renders the Greek verb (as in the KJV), not "inseruerunt".
