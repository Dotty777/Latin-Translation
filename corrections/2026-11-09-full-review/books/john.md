# John · Latin–English review, 9 November 2026

## Proposed English corrections (5)

**John 1:23** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Ait: Ego vox clamantis in deserto: Dirigite viam Domini, sicut dixit Isaias propheta.
- Now: He said: I am a voice crying in the wilderness, Make straight the way of the Lord, as Isaiah the prophet said.
- Proposed: He said: I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness, Make straight the way of the Lord, as Isaiah the prophet said.
- Why: 'vox clamantis' is 'the voice of one crying' (genitive participle); 'a voice crying' drops the person who cries and makes the voice itself the crier.

**John 4:29** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Venite, et videte hominem qui dixit mihi omnia quæcumque feci: numquid ipse est Christus?
- Now: Come, and see a man who has told me all things whatever I have done. Is not he the Christ?
- Proposed: Come, and see a man who has told me all things whatever I have done. Is he perhaps the Christ?
- Why: 'numquid ipse est Christus?' is a tentative question ('can this be the Christ?'); 'Is not he the Christ?' turns it into a question expecting yes, as if the Latin were 'nonne'.

**John 19:12** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Et exinde quærebat Pilatus dimittere eum. Judæi autem clamabant dicentes: Si hunc dimittis, non es amicus Cæsaris. Omnis enim qui se regem facit, contradicit Cæsari.
- Now: And from now on Pilate sought to release him. But the Jews cried out, saying: If you release this man, you are not Caesar’s friend. For whoever makes himself a king speaks against Caesar.
- Proposed: And from then on Pilate sought to release him. But the Jews cried out, saying: If you release this man, you are not Caesar’s friend. For whoever makes himself a king speaks against Caesar.
- Why: "exinde quærebat" = from then on he sought (narrative past); "from now on" misplaces the time reference.

**John 19:28** · meaning · minor

- Latin: Postea sciens Jesus quia omnia consummata sunt, ut consummaretur Scriptura, dixit: Sitio.
- Now: Afterwards, Jesus knowing that all things were now accomplished, that the scripture might be fulfilled, said: I thirst.
- Proposed: Afterwards, Jesus knowing that all things were accomplished, that the scripture might be fulfilled, said: I thirst.
- Why: "quia omnia consummata sunt" has no "jam"; the English adds "now" (as in the Greek).

**John 20:8** · other-text · minor

- Latin: Tunc ergo introivit et ille discipulus qui venerat primus ad monumentum: et vidit, et credidit:
- Now: Then the other disciple, who had come first to the tomb, also entered; and he saw and believed.
- Proposed: Then that disciple, who had come first to the tomb, also entered; and he saw and believed.
- Why: "et ille discipulus" = that disciple also; "the other disciple" follows the Greek wording, not this Latin.

## Notes on the recorded Latin (3)

"Same as reference" means the recorded Latin has the same words as the reference Clementine transcription, so a suspected typo was not introduced by the site.

- **John 5:5** · coverage · same as reference: Clementine John 5:4 ('Angelus autem Domini descendebat secundum tempus in piscinam, et movebatur aqua. Et qui prior descendisset ... sanus fiebat a quacumque detinebatur infirmitate') is absent: units pass from 5:3 to 5:5.
- **John 10:1** · spacing: Stray space before the comma in the recorded Latin: 'Amen , amen dico vobis' should read 'Amen, amen dico vobis'. English is unaffected.
- **John 14:10** · suspected-typo · same as reference: Recorded 'ipse fecit opera' (perfect) appears to be a typo for the Clementine 'ipse facit opera' (present: 'he does the works'), which the English already renders. Verify against a printed Clementine.

## Withdrawn on second reading (1)

- **John 19:39**: 'libras centum': the libra is the Roman pound, so 'Roman pounds' is an accurate rendering of the unit, not a departure.
