# Genesis (Latin) · points left for a specialist (13 November 2026)

Not changed in this pass.

## Wording

- 2:3 “which God created to be made” (*quod creavit Deus ut faceret*) — clumsy aloud; no clearer literal wording found.
- 18:10, 18:14 “life continuing” for *vita comite* — accurate but stiff; one decision covers both.
- 21:7 — the 9 November reading (“Who would have believed Abraham, on hearing it, …”) takes *Abraham* as dative; the syntax allows other readings (compare Douay-Rheims).
- 17:14 — this pass changed “cut off” to “blotted out” for *delebitur*, building on the 9 November edit; please confirm.
- 14:20 — “Abram” is supplied as the one who gives the tithe; the Latin does not name him.
- 19:27 — “came to the place” supplies an ellipsis that runs into 19:28.
- 7:14 — “every wild animal” adds “wild” to *omne animal*.
- 41:9 *peccatum* “fault”, 47:9 *mali* “troubled”, 43:11 *terebinthi* “terebinth nuts” — possible nuances.
- 48:16 — capital “He” for the angel.
- 32:29 — lowercase “you” in Jacob’s question beside capitalised pronouns in the answer.

## Data

- 6:6 — the Latin reads *quod hominum fecisset*; the Clementine has *hominem* (transcription slip; English unaffected).
- Rows sharing one Vulgate verse repeat its text: 5:31/5:32 (vg-genesis-5-31), 35:22a/35:22b (vg-genesis-35-22), 49:32 repeats 49:30 (vg-genesis-49-30), 50:23 repeats 50:22 (vg-genesis-50-22). Decide whether such rows should be blank or merged.
