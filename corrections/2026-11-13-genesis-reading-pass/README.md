# Genesis Latin–English reading pass · 13 November 2026

All 1,534 rows of Genesis were read against the Clementine Latin for fidelity first and clarity when read aloud second. This pass starts from the [9 November editorial revision](../2026-11-09-latin-editorial/README.md), whose 17 Genesis readings were checked and kept. 33 English readings were revised: none major, 23 minor and 10 for spoken flow.

| Chapters | Rows reviewed | Revised |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1–10 | 267 | 5 |
| 11–20 | 247 | 8 |
| 21–30 | 360 | 10 |
| 31–40 | 323 | 3 |
| 41–50 | 337 | 7 |

The English now follows the Latin where it had drifted toward the Hebrew or a common English Bible: 17:14 *delebitur* “blotted out”, 26:20 the well *Calumniam* “Calumny”, 7:21 *animantium* “animals, wild beasts”, 1:26 singular *hominem … praesit* (matching the 9 November change to 1:27). Other revisions restore dropped words (3:3 *ne forte* “lest perhaps”, 13:13 the second intensifier, 21:12 “listen to her voice”, 49:11 *vineam* and *vitem*), correct tense (15:2, 16:5), put *congreget* back in 41:35 where the Latin has it, and name a speaker where the old wording pointed to the wrong person (5:10, 30:10, 37:14). Genesis 38:16 *ingrediens ad eam* now reads “He approached her”, since “went in to her” elsewhere in the column means intercourse.

The review was AI-assisted (five parallel readers working from a shared brief, then checked together). Every changed reading was matched against its previous English in `genesis/verses.json` and `genesis/Genesis.txt`; card IDs, verse references and Vulgate references are unchanged. Specialist sign-off remains open.
