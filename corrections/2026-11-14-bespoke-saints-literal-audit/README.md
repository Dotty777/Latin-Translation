## Major Source & Policy Fixes Flagged for Vince's Ruling

1. **`1 Maccabees 1:59` (`verse-59`, Major):** Current English adds unattested `"of God"` at the end (*"opposite the altar of God"*), whereas Clementine Latin reads simply `contra altare` (no `Dei`, unlike `altare Dei` in `1:54`). Candidate removes `"of God"` while keeping the attached `contra altare` note intact.
2. **`1 Maccabees 1:13` (`verse-13`, Major):** Current English reads *"to follow the customs of the nations"*, replacing both verb and singular noun of Clementine `ut facerent justitiam gentium` (*"that they might do the justice of the nations"*).
3. **`1 Maccabees 1:47` (`verse-47`, Major):** Current English renders `pecora communia` as *"unclean animals"*, collapsing its lexical distinction from `immundis` (*"unclean"*) in `1:48` and `1:62`; candidate restores *"common cattle"*.
4. **`John 3:7` (`john-3-7`, Major):** Removes prohibited insertion `"all"` (*"You all must be born again"*) for simple plural `vos` (`oportet vos nasci denuo`), matching `TRANSLATION-STANDARD.md` §1 & §3.
5. **Douay-Rheims Scaffold & Omissions Cleaned:** Removes unattested `"the manner of"` (`John 2:6`), `"have well drunk"` (`2:10`), `"his"` before `signorum` (`2:11`), `"was ... in building"` (`2:20`), and `"risen again"` (`2:22`); restores omitted `Ipse ... ipse` (`2:24–25`), `et dixit` (`2:19; 3:3, 9, 10, 27`), `ibi` (`Ps 22:2`), `ipsa` (`Ps 22:4`), `Et ... et` (`Ps 22:6`), `Cethim` (`1 Macc 1:1`), and singular divine `Thou / Thy` in direct prayer to God (`Ps 22:4–6`, `John 2:17`), while strictly preserving every distinct Clementine Vulgate reading (`Dominus regit me`, `calix meus`, `renatus fuerit denuo`, `Spiritu Sancto`, `qui est in cælo`, `quasi leprosum`, `percussi eum`, `Antiochus illustris`, and locked rows `1:22`, `1:49`).
