# Latin–English editorial revision · 9 November 2026

This revision updates the GitHub edition of the Saints.Bible Latin–English Parallel Reader. It follows the Clementine Latin and keeps the existing English register where the wording is accurate and clear when spoken.

The English pass adjudicated all 481 proposals from the [full 70-book review](../2026-11-09-full-review/README.md). The independent wording pass applied 464 proposals, including revisions where a direct gloss would mislead when spoken. It also revised 109 additional readings for spoken flow, fixed seven readings whose English crossed an incorrect verse boundary, resolved three English discrepancies against the selected Clementine transcription, and adjusted the wording on one restored group to retain the Latin emphasis. The additional spoken review focused on selected passages. It did not cover every line independently.

The text of the grouped restorations was checked against the [Clementine Vulgate Project transcription](https://github.com/BibleGet-I-O/Clementine-Vulgate/tree/d57e2cde0cceda0d073ea9efc1fee616bcfeb2c1/src/utf8). Exodus 18:6 also restores the possessive in *filii tui* and reflects it in English. The three earlier source questions are resolved for the selected electronic transcription. Other printings may differ.

Every changed reading was matched to its original Latin and English in the archive and in the corresponding book text export. The check preserved all 34,575 reader rows, card IDs, verse references, Vulgate references, and captured site classifications. The capture dates still refer to the source snapshot. Staging publication and remaining source coverage work are separate. The [November 9 gap list](../2026-11-09-full-review/vulgate-verses-not-in-record.json) records a mix of grouped archive omissions and material that needs a separate reader alignment decision.
