TITUS 1

1:1  LA: Paulus servus Dei, Apostolus autem Jesu Christi secundum fidem electorum Dei, et agnitionem veritatis, quæ secundum pietatem est
     EN: Paul, a servant of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the faith of God’s chosen ones and the knowledge of the truth that accords with godliness,

1:2  LA: in spem vitæ æternæ, quam promisit qui non mentitur, Deus, ante tempora sæcularia:
     EN: in the hope of eternal life, which God, who does not lie, promised before the ages began.

1:3  LA: manifestavit autem temporibus suis verbum suum in prædicatione, quæ credita est mihi secundum præceptum Salvatoris nostri Dei:
     EN: At the appointed times he made his word known through the preaching entrusted to me by the command of God our Savior.

1:4  LA: Tito dilecto filio secundum communem fidem, gratia, et pax a Deo Patre, et Christo Jesu Salvatore nostro.
     EN: To Titus, my beloved son in our common faith: grace and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Savior.

1:5  LA: Hujus rei gratia reliqui te Cretæ, ut ea quæ desunt, corrigas, et constituas per civitates presbyteros, sicut et ego disposui tibi,
     EN: For this reason I left you in Crete: to set right what remains unfinished and appoint presbyters in every city, as I directed you.

1:6  LA: si quis sine crimine est, unius uxoris vir, filios habens fideles, non in accusatione luxuriæ, aut non subditos.
     EN: A man must be without reproach, the husband of one wife, with believing children who are not accused of reckless living or insubordination.

1:7  LA: Oportet enim episcopum sine crimine esse, sicut Dei dispensatorem: non superbum, non iracundum, non vinolentum, non percussorem, non turpis lucri cupidum:
     EN: For a bishop, as God’s steward, must be without reproach: not arrogant, quick-tempered, given to wine, violent, or greedy for shameful gain,

1:8  LA: sed hospitalem, benignum, sobrium, justum, sanctum, continentem,
     EN: but hospitable, kind, sober, just, holy, and self-controlled,

1:9  LA: amplectentem eum, qui secundum doctrinam est, fidelem sermonem: ut potens sit exhortari in doctrina sana, et eos qui contradicunt, arguere.
     EN: holding firmly to the trustworthy word that accords with the teaching, so that he can both encourage others in sound teaching and refute those who oppose it.

1:10  LA: Sunt enim multi etiam inobedientes, vaniloqui, et seductores: maxime qui de circumcisione sunt:
      EN: For there are also many who are disobedient, empty talkers and deceivers, especially those of the circumcision.

1:11  LA: quos oportet redargui: qui universas domos subvertunt, docentes quæ non oportet, turpis lucri gratia.
      EN: They must be refuted, for they overturn entire households, teaching what they should not for the sake of shameful gain.

1:12  LA: Dixit quidam ex illis, proprius ipsorum propheta: Cretenses semper mendaces, malæ bestiæ, ventres pigri.
      EN: One of their own, a prophet of theirs, said, “Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, lazy bellies.”

1:13  LA: Testimonium hoc verum est. Quam ob causam increpa illos dure, ut sani sint in fide,
      EN: This testimony is true. For this reason rebuke them sharply, so that they may be sound in the faith,

1:14  LA: non intendentes judaicis fabulis, et mandatis hominum, aversantium se a veritate.
      EN: paying no attention to Jewish fables or the commands of people who turn away from the truth.

1:15  LA: Omnia munda mundis: coinquinatis autem et infidelibus, nihil est mundum, sed inquinatæ sunt eorum et mens et conscientia.
      EN: All things are clean to the clean; but to the defiled and unbelieving nothing is clean. Both their mind and their conscience are defiled.

1:16  LA: Confitentur se nosse Deum, factis autem negant: cum sint abominati, et incredibiles, et ad omne opus bonum reprobi.
      EN: They profess to know God, but deny him by their deeds. They are detestable, unbelieving, and unfit for any good work.

TITUS 2

2:1  LA: Tu autem loquere quæ decent sanam doctrinam:
     EN: But you, speak what is fitting for sound teaching:

2:2  LA: senes ut sobrii sint, pudici, prudentes, sani in fide, in dilectione, in patientia:
     EN: older men are to be sober, chaste, prudent, and sound in faith, love, and endurance;

2:3  LA: anus similiter in habitu sancto, non criminatrices, non multo vino servientes, bene docentes:
     EN: older women likewise are to be holy in their conduct, not slanderers or enslaved to much wine, but teaching well,

2:4  LA: ut prudentiam doceant adolescentulas, ut viros suos ament, filios suos diligant,
     EN: so that they may teach the younger women prudence: to love their husbands and their children,

2:5  LA: prudentes, castas, sobrias, domus curam habentes, benignas, subditas viris suis, ut non blasphemetur verbum Dei.
     EN: to be prudent, chaste, sober, attentive to their households, kind, and subject to their husbands, so that the word of God may not be blasphemed.

2:6  LA: Juvenes similiter hortare ut sobrii sint.
     EN: Likewise, urge the younger men to be sober.

2:7  LA: In omnibus teipsum præbe exemplum bonorum operum, in doctrina, in integritate, in gravitate,
     EN: In everything show yourself an example of good works, with integrity and dignity in your teaching

2:8  LA: verbum sanum, irreprehensibile: ut is qui ex adverso est, vereatur, nihil habens malum dicere de nobis.
     EN: and sound speech beyond reproach, so that an opponent may be afraid, having nothing evil to say about us.

2:9  LA: Servos dominis suis subditos esse, in omnibus placentes, non contradicentes,
     EN: Slaves are to be subject to their masters, pleasing them in everything, not talking back

2:10  LA: non fraudantes, sed in omnibus fidem bonam ostendentes: ut doctrinam Salvatoris nostri Dei ornent in omnibus.
      EN: or stealing, but showing complete good faith, so that in everything they may adorn the teaching of God our Savior.

2:11  LA: Apparuit enim gratia Dei Salvatoris nostri omnibus hominibus,
      EN: For the grace of God our Savior has appeared to all people,

2:12  LA: erudiens nos, ut abnegantes impietatem, et sæcularia desideria, sobrie, et juste, et pie vivamus in hoc sæculo,
      EN: training us to renounce ungodliness and worldly desires and to live soberly, justly, and devoutly in this age,

2:13  LA: exspectantes beatam spem, et adventum gloriæ magni Dei, et Salvatoris nostri Jesu Christi:
      EN: awaiting the blessed hope and the coming of the glory of our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ,

2:14  LA: qui dedit semetipsum pro nobis, ut nos redimeret ab omni iniquitate, et mundaret sibi populum acceptabilem, sectatorem bonorum operum.
      EN: who gave himself for us to redeem us from all lawlessness and cleanse for himself an acceptable people, eager for good works.

2:15  LA: Hæc loquere, et exhortare, et argue cum omni imperio. Nemo te contemnat.
      EN: Speak these things, encourage, and rebuke with all authority. Let no one despise you.

TITUS 3

3:1  LA: Admone illos principibus, et potestatibus subditos esse, dicto obedire, ad omne opus bonum paratos esse:
     EN: Remind them to be subject to rulers and authorities, to obey, and to be ready for every good work,

3:2  LA: neminem blasphemare, non litigiosos esse, sed modestos, omnem ostendentes mansuetudinem ad omnes homines.
     EN: to slander no one, to avoid quarrels, to be considerate, and to show complete gentleness toward everyone.

3:3  LA: Eramus enim aliquando et nos insipientes, increduli, errantes, servientes desideriis, et voluptatibus variis, in malitia et invidia agentes, odibiles, odientes invicem.
     EN: For we too were once foolish, unbelieving, wandering astray, enslaved to various desires and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful and hating one another.

3:4  LA: Cum autem benignitas et humanitas apparuit Salvatoris nostri Dei,
     EN: But when the kindness and love for humanity of God our Savior appeared,

3:5  LA: non ex operibus justitiæ, quæ fecimus nos, sed secundum suam misericordiam salvos nos fecit per lavacrum regenerationis et renovationis Spiritus Sancti,
     EN: he saved us, not because of righteous works we had done, but according to his mercy, through the washing of rebirth and renewal by the Holy Spirit,

3:6  LA: quem effudit in nos abunde per Jesum Christum Salvatorem nostrum:
     EN: whom he poured out richly upon us through Jesus Christ our Savior,

3:7  LA: ut justificati gratia ipsius, hæredes simus secundum spem vitæ æternæ.
     EN: so that, justified by his grace, we might be heirs according to the hope of eternal life.

3:8  LA: Fidelis sermo est: et de his volo te confirmare: ut curent bonis operibus præesse qui credunt Deo. Hæc sunt bona, et utilia hominibus.
     EN: This saying is trustworthy, and I want you to insist on these things, so that those who believe God may take care to excel in good works. These things are good and useful to people.

3:9  LA: Stultas autem quæstiones, et genealogias, et contentiones, et pugnas legis devita: sunt enim inutiles, et vanæ.
     EN: But avoid foolish questions, genealogies, disputes, and quarrels about the law, for they are useless and empty.

3:10  LA: Hæreticum hominem post unam et secundam correptionem devita:
      EN: After a first and second correction, avoid a heretical person,

3:11  LA: sciens quia subversus est, qui ejusmodi est, et delinquit, cum sit proprio judicio condemnatus.
      EN: knowing that such a person is perverted and sins, condemned by his own judgment.

3:12  LA: Cum misero ad te Artemam, aut Tychicum, festina ad me venire Nicopolim: ibi enim statui hiemare.
      EN: When I send Artemas or Tychicus to you, hurry to join me at Nicopolis, for I have decided to spend the winter there.

3:13  LA: Zenam legisperitum et Apollo sollicite præmitte, ut nihil illis desit.
      EN: Send Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their way with care, so that they lack nothing.

3:14  LA: Discant autem et nostri bonis operibus præesse ad usus necessarios: ut non sint infructuosi.
      EN: Let our people also learn to devote themselves to good works to meet pressing needs, so that they may not be unfruitful.

3:15  LA: Salutant te qui mecum sunt omnes: saluta eos qui nos amant in fide. Gratia Dei cum omnibus vobis. Amen.
      EN: All who are with me greet you. Greet those who love us in the faith. The grace of God be with all of you. Amen.
