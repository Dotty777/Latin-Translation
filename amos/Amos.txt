AMOS 1

1:1  LA: Verba Amos, qui fuit in pastoribus de Thecue, quæ vidit super Israël in diebus Oziæ, regis Juda, et in diebus Jeroboam, filii Joas, regis Israël, ante duos annos terræmotus.
     EN: The words of Amos, who was among the shepherds of Tekoa, which he saw concerning Israel in the days of Uzziah king of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel, two years before the earthquake.

1:2  LA: Et dixit : [Dominus de Sion rugiet, et de Jerusalem dabit vocem suam ; et luxerunt speciosa pastorum, et exsiccatus est vertex Carmeli.
     EN: He said: “The Lord will roar from Zion and raise His voice from Jerusalem; the shepherds' lovely pastures have mourned, and Carmel's summit has dried up.”

1:3  LA: Hæc dicit Dominus : Super tribus sceleribus Damasci, et super quatuor non convertam eum, eo quod trituraverint in plaustris ferreis Galaad.
     EN: Thus says the Lord: “For three crimes of Damascus, and for four, I will not turn it back, because they threshed Gilead with iron carts.

1:4  LA: Et mittam ignem in domum Azaël, et devorabit domos Benadad.
     EN: I will send fire into the house of Hazael, and it will devour the houses of Ben-hadad.

1:5  LA: Et conteram vectem Damasci : et disperdam habitatorem de campo idoli, et tenentem sceptrum de domo voluptatis : et transferetur populus Syriæ Cyrenen, dicit Dominus.
     EN: I will break the bar of Damascus, destroy the inhabitant from the plain of the idol and the scepter-bearer from the house of delight. The people of Syria will be deported to Cyrene,” says the Lord.

1:6  LA: Hæc dicit Dominus : Super tribus sceleribus Gazæ, et super quatuor non convertam eum, eo quod transtulerint captivitatem perfectam, ut concluderent eam in Idumæa.
     EN: Thus says the Lord: “For three crimes of Gaza, and for four, I will not turn it back, because they deported an entire captive population to shut them up in Edom.

1:7  LA: Et mittam ignem in murum Gazæ, et devorabit ædes ejus.
     EN: I will send fire upon Gaza's wall, and it will devour her buildings.

1:8  LA: Et disperdam habitatorem de Azoto, et tenentem sceptrum de Ascalone : et convertam manum meam super Accaron, et peribunt reliqui Philisthinorum, dicit Dominus Deus.
     EN: I will destroy the inhabitant from Ashdod and the scepter-bearer from Ashkelon; I will turn My hand against Ekron, and the remnant of the Philistines will perish,” says the Lord God.

1:9  LA: Hæc dicit Dominus : Super tribus sceleribus Tyri, et super quatuor non convertam eum, eo quod concluserint captivitatem perfectam in Idumæa, et non sint recordati fœderis fratrum.
     EN: Thus says the Lord: “For three crimes of Tyre, and for four, I will not turn it back, because they shut up an entire captive population in Edom and did not remember the covenant of brothers.

1:10  LA: Et mittam ignem in murum Tyri, et devorabit ædes ejus.
      EN: I will send fire upon Tyre's wall, and it will devour her buildings.”

1:11  LA: Hæc dicit Dominus : Super tribus sceleribus Edom, et super quatuor non convertam eum, eo quod persecutus sit in gladio fratrem suum, et violaverit misericordiam ejus, et tenuerit ultra furorem suum, et indignationem suam servaverit usque in finem.
      EN: Thus says the Lord: “For three crimes of Edom, and for four, I will not turn it back, because he pursued his brother with the sword, violated his compassion, prolonged his fury, and kept his indignation to the end.

1:12  LA: Mittam ignem in Theman, et devorabit ædes Bosræ.
      EN: I will send fire into Teman, and it will devour Bozrah's buildings.”

1:13  LA: Hæc dicit Dominus : Super tribus sceleribus filiorum Ammon, et super quatuor non convertam eum, eo quod dissecuerit prægnantes Galaad ad dilatandum terminum suum.
      EN: Thus says the Lord: “For three crimes of the children of Ammon, and for four, I will not turn it back, because he ripped open the pregnant women of Gilead to enlarge his border.

1:14  LA: Et succendam ignem in muro Rabba, et devorabit ædes ejus in ululatu in die belli, et in turbine in die commotionis.
      EN: I will kindle fire upon Rabbah's wall, and it will devour her buildings amid howling on the day of battle, amid whirlwind on the day of upheaval.

1:15  LA: Et ibit Melchom in captivitatem, ipse et principes ejus simul, dicit Dominus.]
      EN: Milcom will go into captivity, he and his princes together,” says the Lord.

AMOS 2

2:1  LA: Hæc dicit Dominus : Super tribus sceleribus Moab, et super quatuor non convertam eum, eo quod incenderit ossa regis Idumææ usque ad cinerem.
     EN: Thus says the Lord: “For three crimes of Moab, and for four, I will not turn it back, because he burned the bones of the king of Edom to ashes.

2:2  LA: Et mittam ignem in Moab, et devorabit ædes Carioth : et morietur in sonitu Moab, in clangore tubæ.
     EN: I will send fire into Moab, and it will devour Kerioth's buildings. Moab will die amid uproar, amid the trumpet's blast.

2:3  LA: Et disperdam judicem de medio ejus, et omnes principes ejus interficiam cum eo, dicit Dominus.]
     EN: I will destroy the judge from his midst and kill all his princes with him,” says the Lord.

2:4  LA: [Hæc dicit Dominus : Super tribus sceleribus Juda, et super quatuor non convertam eum, eo quod abjecerit legem Domini et mandata ejus non custodierit : deceperant enim eos idola sua, post quæ abierant patres eorum.
     EN: Thus says the Lord: “For three crimes of Judah, and for four, I will not turn it back, because he rejected the Lord's law and did not keep His commandments. Their idols, which their fathers followed, had deceived them.

2:5  LA: Et mittam ignem in Juda, et devorabit ædes Jerusalem.
     EN: I will send fire into Judah, and it will devour Jerusalem's buildings.”

2:6  LA: Hæc dicit Dominus : Super tribus sceleribus Israël, et super quatuor non convertam eum, pro eo quod vendiderit pro argento justum, et pauperem pro calceamentis.
     EN: Thus says the Lord: “For three crimes of Israel, and for four, I will not turn it back, because he sold the righteous for silver and the poor for footwear.

2:7  LA: Qui conterunt super pulverem terræ capita pauperum, et viam humilium declinant : et filius ac pater ejus ierunt ad puellam, ut violarent nomen sanctum meum.
     EN: They crush the heads of the poor into the dust of the earth and turn aside the way of the humble. A son and his father went to the same girl, to profane My holy name.

2:8  LA: Et super vestimentis pignoratis accubuerunt juxta omne altare, et vinum damnatorum bibebant in domo Dei sui.
     EN: They reclined beside every altar on garments taken in pledge, and in the house of their God they drank the wine of those condemned.

2:9  LA: Ego autem exterminavi Amorrhæum a facie eorum, cujus altitudo, cedrorum altitudo ejus, et fortis ipse quasi quercus ; et contrivi fructum ejus desuper, et radices ejus subter.
     EN: Yet I destroyed the Amorite before them, whose height was the height of cedars, who was strong as an oak. I crushed his fruit above and his roots below.

2:10  LA: Ego sum qui ascendere vos feci de terra Ægypti, et duxi vos in deserto quadraginta annis, ut possideretis terram Amorrhæi.
      EN: I am the One who brought you up from the land of Egypt and led you in the wilderness forty years, so that you might possess the land of the Amorite.

2:11  LA: Et suscitavi de filiis vestris in prophetas, et de juvenibus vestris nazaræos. Numquid non ita est, filii Israël ? dicit Dominus.
      EN: I raised up some of your sons as prophets and some of your young men as Nazirites. Is this not so, children of Israel?” says the Lord.

2:12  LA: Et propinabitis nazaræis vinum, et prophetis mandabitis, dicentes : Ne prophetetis.
      EN: “But you will give the Nazirites wine to drink and command the prophets, saying, ‘Do not prophesy.’

2:13  LA: Ecce ego stridebo subter vos, sicut stridet plaustrum onustum fœno.
      EN: See, I will creak beneath you as a cart laden with hay creaks.

2:14  LA: Et peribit fuga a veloce, et fortis non obtinebit virtutem suam, et robustus non salvabit animam suam :
      EN: Escape will fail the swift, the strong will not maintain his strength, and the mighty will not save his life.

2:15  LA: et tenens arcum non stabit, et velox pedibus suis non salvabitur, et ascensor equi non salvabit animam suam :
      EN: The bowman will not stand, the swift of foot will not be saved, and the rider of a horse will not save his life.

2:16  LA: et robustus corde inter fortes nudus fugiet in illa die, dicit Dominus.]
      EN: Even the stouthearted among the mighty will flee naked on that day,” says the Lord.

AMOS 3

3:1  LA: Audite verbum quod locutus est Dominus super vos, filii Israël, super omnem cognationem quam eduxi de terra Ægypti, dicens :
     EN: Hear the word that the Lord has spoken against you, children of Israel, against the whole family that I brought out of the land of Egypt, saying:

3:2  LA: Tantummodo vos cognovi ex omnibus cognationibus terræ ; idcirco visitabo super vos omnes iniquitates vestras.
     EN: “You alone have I known among all the families of the earth; therefore I will call you to account for all your iniquities.

3:3  LA: Numquid ambulabunt duo pariter, nisi convenerit eis ?
     EN: Will two walk together unless they have agreed?

3:4  LA: numquid rugiet leo in saltu, nisi habuerit prædam ? numquid dabit catulus leonis vocem de cubili suo, nisi aliquid apprehenderit ?
     EN: Will a lion roar in the forest unless it has prey? Will a lion cub raise its voice from its den unless it has caught something?

3:5  LA: numquid cadet avis in laqueum terræ absque aucupe ? numquid auferetur laqueus de terra antequam quid ceperit ?
     EN: Will a bird fall into a snare on the ground without a birdcatcher? Will a snare be lifted from the ground before it has caught anything?

3:6  LA: si clanget tuba in civitate, et populus non expavescet ? si erit malum in civitate, quod Dominus non fecerit ?
     EN: If a trumpet sounds in a city, will the people not be terrified? Will there be a calamity in a city that the Lord has not brought about?

3:7  LA: Quia non facit Dominus Deus verbum, nisi revelaverit secretum suum ad servos suos prophetas.
     EN: For the Lord God does nothing without revealing His secret to His servants the prophets.

3:8  LA: Leo rugiet, quis non timebit ? Dominus Deus locutus est, quis non prophetabit ?
     EN: A lion will roar—who will not fear? The Lord God has spoken—who will not prophesy?

3:9  LA: Auditum facite in ædibus Azoti, et in ædibus terræ Ægypti, et dicite : Congregamini super montes Samariæ, et videte insanias multas in medio ejus, et calumniam patientes in penetralibus ejus.
     EN: Make it heard in the buildings of Ashdod and in the buildings of the land of Egypt, and say: ‘Gather on the mountains of Samaria and see the many acts of madness in her midst, and those suffering oppression in her inner chambers.’

3:10  LA: Et nescierunt facere rectum, dicit Dominus, thesaurizantes iniquitatem et rapinas in ædibus suis.
      EN: They do not know how to do right,” says the Lord, “storing up injustice and plunder in their buildings.”

3:11  LA: Propterea hæc dicit Dominus Deus : Tribulabitur et circuietur terra : et detrahetur ex te fortitudo tua, et diripientur ædes tuæ.
      EN: Therefore thus says the Lord God: “The land will be afflicted and surrounded; your strength will be stripped from you, and your buildings will be plundered.”

3:12  LA: Hæc dicit Dominus : Quomodo si eruat pastor de ore leonis duo crura, aut extremum auriculæ, sic eruentur filii Israël, qui habitant in Samaria in plaga lectuli, et in Damasci grabato.
      EN: Thus says the Lord: “As a shepherd might rescue two legs or the tip of an ear from a lion's mouth, so the children of Israel will be rescued, those who dwell in Samaria on the edge of a bed and on a couch in Damascus.

3:13  LA: Audite, et contestamini in domo Jacob, dicit Dominus Deus exercituum ;
      EN: Hear and bear witness in the house of Jacob,” says the Lord God of hosts,

3:14  LA: quia in die cum visitare cœpero prævaricationes Israël, super eum visitabo, et super altaria Bethel ; et amputabuntur cornua altaris, et cadent in terram.
      EN: “for on the day when I begin to call Israel's transgressions to account, I will visit judgment upon him and upon the altars of Bethel. The horns of the altar will be cut off and fall to the ground.

3:15  LA: Et percutiam domum hiemalem cum domo æstiva, et peribunt domus eburneæ, et dissipabuntur ædes multæ, dicit Dominus.]
      EN: I will strike the winter house together with the summer house; the ivory houses will perish, and many buildings will be torn down,” says the Lord.

AMOS 4

4:1  LA: Audite verbum hoc, vaccæ pingues, quæ estis in monte Samariæ, quæ calumniam facitis egenis et confringitis pauperes ; quæ dicitis dominis vestris : Afferte, et bibemus.
     EN: Hear this word, fat cows on the mountain of Samaria, who oppress the needy and crush the poor, who say to your lords, “Bring us drink.”

4:2  LA: Juravit Dominus Deus in sancto suo, quia ecce dies venient super vos, et levabunt vos in contis, et reliquias vestras in ollis ferventibus.
     EN: The Lord God has sworn by His holiness: “See, days will come upon you when they will lift you on poles and your remains in boiling pots.

4:3  LA: Et per aperturas exibitis altera contra alteram, et projiciemini in Armon, dicit Dominus.]
     EN: You will go out through breaches, one opposite another, and you will be cast into Armon,” says the Lord.

4:4  LA: [Venite ad Bethel, et impie agite ; ad Galgalam, et multiplicate prævaricationem : et afferte mane victimas vestras, tribus diebus decimas vestras.
     EN: “Come to Bethel and act wickedly; to Gilgal and multiply transgression. Bring your sacrificial victims in the morning and your tithes after three days.

4:5  LA: Et sacrificate de fermentato laudem, et vocate voluntarias oblationes, et annuntiate ; sic enim voluistis, filii Israël, dicit Dominus Deus.
     EN: Offer a thanksgiving sacrifice from leavened bread; call for freewill offerings and announce them, for this is what you wanted, children of Israel,” says the Lord God.

4:6  LA: Unde et ego dedi vobis stuporem dentium in cunctis urbibus vestris, et indigentiam panum in omnibus locis vestris ; et non estis reversi ad me, dicit Dominus.
     EN: “Therefore I too gave you numbness of teeth in all your cities and lack of bread in all your places; yet you have not returned to Me,” says the Lord.

4:7  LA: Ego quoque prohibui a vobis imbrem, cum adhuc tres menses superessent usque ad messem : et plui super unam civitatem, et super alteram civitatem non plui ; pars una compluta est, et pars super quam non plui, aruit.
     EN: “I also withheld rain from you when there were still three months until harvest. I rained upon one city but not upon another; one portion received rain, and the portion upon which I did not rain dried up.

4:8  LA: Et venerunt duæ et tres civitates ad unam civitatem ut biberent aquam, et non sunt satiatæ ; et non redistis ad me, dicit Dominus.
     EN: Two or three cities came to one city to drink water and were not satisfied; yet you have not returned to Me,” says the Lord.

4:9  LA: Percussi vos in vento urente, et in aurugine : multitudinem hortorum vestrorum et vinearum vestrarum, oliveta vestra et ficeta vestra comedit eruca : et non redistis ad me, dicit Dominus.
     EN: “I struck you with scorching wind and blight; the caterpillar ate the abundance of your gardens and vineyards, your olive groves and fig orchards; yet you have not returned to Me,” says the Lord.

4:10  LA: Misi in vos mortem in via Ægypti ; percussi in gladio juvenes vestros, usque ad captivitatem equorum vestrorum, et ascendere feci putredinem castrorum vestrorum in nares vestras : et non redistis ad me, dicit Dominus.
      EN: “I sent death among you in the manner of Egypt. I struck your young men with the sword, even to the capture of your horses, and made the stench of your camps rise into your nostrils; yet you have not returned to Me,” says the Lord.

4:11  LA: Subverti vos sicut subvertit Deus Sodomam et Gomorrham, et facti estis quasi torris raptus ab incendio : et non redistis ad me, dicit Dominus.
      EN: “I overthrew you as God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah, and you became like a brand snatched from a fire; yet you have not returned to Me,” says the Lord.

4:12  LA: Quapropter hæc faciam tibi, Israël : postquam autem hæc fecero tibi, præparare in occursum Dei tui, Israël.
      EN: “Therefore I will do these things to you, Israel; after I have done these things to you, prepare to meet your God, Israel.

4:13  LA: Quia ecce formans montes, et creans ventum, et annuntians homini eloquium suum, faciens matutinam nebulam, et gradiens super excelsa terræ : Dominus Deus exercituum nomen ejus.]
      EN: For see, He who forms the mountains and creates the wind, who declares His utterance to humankind, who makes the morning mist and treads upon the earth's heights—the Lord God of hosts is His name.”

AMOS 5

5:1  LA: Audite verbum istud, quod ego levo super vos planctum : domus Israël cecidit, et non adjiciet ut resurgat.
     EN: Hear this word, the lament that I raise over you: “The house of Israel has fallen and will rise no more.

5:2  LA: Virgo Israël projecta est in terram suam, non est qui suscitet eam.
     EN: Virgin Israel has been cast upon her own land; there is no one to raise her.”

5:3  LA: Quia hæc dicit Dominus Deus : Urbs de qua egrediebantur mille, relinquentur in ea centum ; et de qua egrediebantur centum, relinquentur in ea decem in domo Israël.
     EN: For thus says the Lord God: “The city from which a thousand went out will have a hundred left; and the one from which a hundred went out will have ten left in the house of Israel.”

5:4  LA: Quia hæc dicit Dominus domui Israël : Quærite me, et vivetis.
     EN: For thus says the Lord to the house of Israel: “Seek Me, and you will live.

5:5  LA: Et nolite quærere Bethel, et in Galgalam nolite intrare, et in Bersabee non transibitis, quia Galgala captiva ducetur, et Bethel erit inutilis.
     EN: Do not seek Bethel or enter Gilgal or cross over to Beer-sheba, for Gilgal will be led captive, and Bethel will become useless.”

5:6  LA: Quærite Dominum, et vivite (ne forte comburatur ut ignis domus Joseph, et devorabit, et non erit qui extinguat Bethel :
     EN: Seek the Lord and live, lest the house of Joseph burn like fire and it devour, with no one to quench Bethel,

5:7  LA: qui convertitis in absinthium judicium, et justitiam in terra relinquitis) :
     EN: you who turn judgment into wormwood and abandon righteousness on the ground.

5:8  LA: facientem Arcturum et Orionem, et convertentem in mane tenebras, et diem in noctem mutantem ; qui vocat aquas maris, et effundit eas super faciem terræ ; Dominus nomen est ejus :
     EN: Seek Him who makes Arcturus and Orion, turns darkness into morning, and changes day into night; who calls the waters of the sea and pours them over the earth's surface—the Lord is His name—

5:9  LA: qui subridet vastitatem super robustum, et depopulationem super potentem affert.
     EN: who with a smile brings devastation upon the strong and destruction upon the powerful.

5:10  LA: Odio habuerunt corripientem in porta, et loquentem perfecte abominati sunt.
      EN: They have hated the one who reproves at the gate and abhorred the one who speaks with integrity.

5:11  LA: Idcirco, pro eo quod diripiebatis pauperem, et prædam electam tollebatis ab eo, domos quadro lapide ædificabitis, et non habitabitis in eis ; vineas plantabis amantissimas, et non bibetis vinum earum.
      EN: Therefore, because you plundered the poor and took choice spoil from him, you will build houses of dressed stone but will not dwell in them. You will plant delightful vineyards but will not drink their wine.

5:12  LA: Quia cognovi multa scelera vestra, et fortia peccata vestra : hostes justi, accipientes munus, et pauperes deprimentes in porta.
      EN: For I know your many crimes and your mighty sins: enemies of the righteous, accepting bribes and oppressing the poor at the gate.

5:13  LA: Ideo prudens in tempore illo tacebit, quia tempus malum est.
      EN: Therefore the prudent will be silent at that time, for it is an evil time.

5:14  LA: Quærite bonum, et non malum, ut vivatis ; et erit Dominus Deus exercituum vobiscum, sicut dixistis.
      EN: Seek good and not evil, that you may live, and the Lord God of hosts will be with you, as you have said.

5:15  LA: Odite malum et diligite bonum, et constituite in porta judicium : si forte misereatur Dominus Deus exercituum reliquiis Joseph.
      EN: Hate evil and love good, and establish justice at the gate. Perhaps the Lord God of hosts will have mercy on the remnant of Joseph.

5:16  LA: Propterea hæc dicit Dominus Deus exercituum, dominator : In omnibus plateis planctus ; et in cunctis quæ foris sunt, dicetur : Væ, væ ! et vocabunt agricolam ad luctum, et ad planctum eos qui sciunt plangere.
      EN: Therefore thus says the Lord, the God of hosts, the Ruler: “In all the squares there will be lamentation, and in all the open places they will say, ‘Alas, alas!’ They will call the farmer to mourning, and those skilled in lamenting to lamentation.

5:17  LA: Et in omnibus vineis erit planctus, quia pertransibo in medio tui, dicit Dominus.]
      EN: In every vineyard there will be lamentation, for I will pass through your midst,” says the Lord.

5:18  LA: [Væ desiderantibus diem Domini ! ad quid eam vobis ? Dies Domini ista, tenebræ, et non lux.
      EN: Woe to those who desire the day of the Lord! What is it to you? That day of the Lord is darkness and not light.

5:19  LA: Quomodo si fugiat vir a facie leonis, et occurrat ei ursus ; et ingrediatur domum, et innitatur manu sua super parietem, et mordeat eum coluber.
      EN: It is as though a man fled from a lion and a bear met him, or went into a house and leaned his hand against the wall, and a snake bit him.

5:20  LA: Numquid non tenebræ dies Domini, et non lux ; et caligo, et non splendor in ea ?
      EN: Is the day of the Lord not darkness and not light, gloom with no brightness in it?

5:21  LA: Odi, et projeci festivitates vestras, et non capiam odorem cœtuum vestrorum.
      EN: “I hate and reject your feasts; I will not delight in the fragrance of your assemblies.

5:22  LA: Quod si obtuleritis mihi holocautomata, et munera vestra, non suscipiam ; et vota pinguium vestrorum non respiciam.
      EN: Even if you offer Me burnt offerings and your gifts, I will not accept them, nor look upon the vowed offerings of your fattened beasts.

5:23  LA: Aufer a me tumultum carminum tuorum ; et cantica lyræ tuæ non audiam.
      EN: Take the clamor of your songs away from Me; I will not listen to the music of your lyre.

5:24  LA: Et revelabitur quasi aqua judicium, et justitia quasi torrens fortis.
      EN: Judgment will be revealed like water, and righteousness like a mighty torrent.

5:25  LA: Numquid hostias et sacrificium obtulistis mihi in deserto quadraginta annis, domus Israël ?
      EN: Did you offer Me victims and sacrifice in the wilderness for forty years, house of Israel?

5:26  LA: et portastis tabernaculum Moloch vestro, et imaginem idolorum vestrorum, sidus dei vestri, quæ fecistis vobis.
      EN: You carried the tent for your Moloch and the image of your idols, the star of your god, which you made for yourselves.

5:27  LA: Et migrare vos faciam trans Damascum, dicit Dominus : Deus exercituum nomen ejus.]
      EN: I will make you go into exile beyond Damascus,” says the Lord; the God of hosts is His name.

AMOS 6

6:1  LA: Væ qui opulenti estis in Sion, et confiditis in monte Samariæ : optimates capita populorum, ingredientes pompatice domum Israël !
     EN: Woe to you who are wealthy in Zion and trust in the mountain of Samaria, nobles, heads of the peoples, entering the house of Israel with pomp!

6:2  LA: Transite in Chalane, et videte, et ite inde in Emath magnam, et descendite in Geth Palæstinorum, et ad optima quæque regna horum : si latior terminus eorum termino vestro est.
     EN: Cross over to Calneh and see; go from there to great Hamath, and go down to Gath of the Philistines and to all their best kingdoms. See whether their territory is broader than yours.

6:3  LA: Qui separati estis in diem malum, et appropinquatis solio iniquitatis ;
     EN: You who are set apart for an evil day and draw near the throne of iniquity,

6:4  LA: qui dormitis in lectis eburneis, et lascivitis in stratis vestris ; qui comeditis agnum de grege, et vitulos de medio armenti ;
     EN: who sleep on ivory beds and revel on your couches, who eat the lamb from the flock and calves from the midst of the herd,

6:5  LA: qui canitis ad vocem psalterii, sicut David putaverunt se habere vasa cantici,
     EN: who sing to the sound of the harp—they imagined they had musical instruments like David—

6:6  LA: bibentes vinum in phialis, et optimo unguento delibuti, et nihil patiebantur super contritione Joseph.
     EN: who drink wine from bowls and are smeared with the finest ointment: they felt no suffering over the breaking of Joseph.

6:7  LA: Quapropter nunc migrabunt in capite transmigrantium, et auferetur factio lascivientium.
     EN: Therefore now they will go into exile at the head of the exiles, and the band of revelers will be taken away.

6:8  LA: Juravit Dominus Deus in anima sua, dicit Dominus Deus exercituum : Detestor ego superbiam Jacob, et domos ejus odi, et tradam civitatem cum habitatoribus suis.
     EN: The Lord God has sworn by His own life, says the Lord God of hosts: “I detest Jacob's pride and hate his houses; I will surrender the city with its inhabitants.”

6:9  LA: Quod si reliqui fuerint decem viri in domo una, et ipsi morientur.
     EN: If ten men remain in one house, they too will die.

6:10  LA: Et tollet eum propinquus suus, et comburet eum, ut efferat ossa de domo ; et dicet ei, qui in penetralibus domus est : Numquid adhuc est penes te ?
      EN: His relative will take him up and burn him, to bring the bones out of the house. He will say to the one in the house's inner chambers, “Is anyone still with you?”

6:11  LA: Quia ecce Dominus mandabit, et percutiet domum majorem ruinis, et domum minorem scissionibus.
      EN: For see, the Lord will command, and He will strike the greater house into ruins and the smaller house into fragments.

6:12  LA: Numquid currere queunt in petris equi, aut arari potest in bubalis ? quoniam convertistis in amaritudinem judicium, et fructum justitiæ in absinthium.
      EN: Can horses run upon rocks, or can one plow with wild oxen? For you have turned judgment into bitterness and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood.

6:13  LA: Qui lætamini in nihilo ; qui dicitis : Numquid non in fortitudine nostra assumpsimus nobis cornua ?
      EN: You who rejoice in nothing, who say, “Have we not taken horns for ourselves by our own strength?”

6:14  LA: Ecce enim suscitabo super vos, domus Israël, dicit Dominus Deus exercituum, gentem, et conteret vos ab introitu Emath usque ad torrentem deserti.]
      EN: “For see, I will raise up a nation against you, house of Israel,” says the Lord God of hosts, “and it will crush you from the entrance of Hamath to the torrent of the wilderness.”

AMOS 7

7:1  LA: Hæc ostendit mihi Dominus Deus : et ecce fictor locustæ in principio germinantium serotini imbris, et ecce serotinus post tonsionem regis.
     EN: The Lord God showed me this: see, One forming locusts at the beginning of the growth after the late rain; and see, the late rain was after the king's mowing.

7:2  LA: Et factum est, cum consummasset comedere herbam terræ, dixi : Domine Deus, propitius esto, obsecro ; quis suscitabit Jacob, quia parvulus est ?
     EN: When it had finished eating the land's grass, I said, “Lord God, be merciful, I beg You! Who will raise Jacob, for he is small?”

7:3  LA: Misertus est Dominus super hoc : Non erit, dixit Dominus.
     EN: The Lord relented over this. “It will not happen,” said the Lord.

7:4  LA: Hæc ostendit mihi Dominus Deus : et ecce vocabat judicium ad ignem Dominus Deus ; et devoravit abyssum multam, et comedit simul partem.
     EN: The Lord God showed me this: see, the Lord God was calling judgment to the fire, and it devoured the great deep and consumed the allotted portion as well.

7:5  LA: Et dixi : Domine Deus, quiesce, obsecro ; quis suscitabit Jacob, quia parvulus est ?
     EN: I said, “Lord God, stop, I beg You! Who will raise Jacob, for he is small?”

7:6  LA: Misertus est Dominus super hoc : Sed et istud non erit, dixit Dominus Deus.
     EN: The Lord relented over this. “This too will not happen,” said the Lord God.

7:7  LA: Hæc ostendit mihi Dominus : et ecce Dominus stans super murum litum, et in manu ejus trulla cæmentarii.
     EN: The Lord showed me this: see, the Lord stood upon a plastered wall, with a mason's trowel in His hand.

7:8  LA: Et dixit Dominus ad me : Quid tu vides, Amos ? Et dixi : Trullam cæmentarii. Et dixit Dominus : [Ecce ego ponam trullam in medio populi mei Israël ; non adjiciam ultra superinducere eum.
     EN: The Lord said to me, “What do you see, Amos?” I said, “A mason's trowel.” The Lord said, “See, I will place a trowel in the midst of My people Israel; I will no longer plaster him over.

7:9  LA: Et demolientur excelsa idoli, et sanctificationes Israël desolabuntur, et consurgam super domum Jeroboam in gladio.]
     EN: The high places of the idol will be demolished, Israel's sanctuaries will be desolated, and I will rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword.”

7:10  LA: Et misit Amasias, sacerdos Bethel, ad Jeroboam, regem Israël, dicens : Rebellavit contra te Amos in medio domus Israël ; non poterit terra sustinere universos sermones ejus.
      EN: Amaziah, priest of Bethel, sent to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, “Amos has rebelled against you in the midst of the house of Israel; the land cannot bear all his words.

7:11  LA: Hæc enim dicit Amos : In gladio morietur Jeroboam, et Israël captivus migrabit de terra sua.
      EN: For Amos says this: ‘Jeroboam will die by the sword, and Israel will go captive into exile from his own land.’”

7:12  LA: Et dixit Amasias ad Amos : Qui vides, gradere : fuge in terram Juda, et comede ibi panem, et prophetabis ibi.
      EN: Amaziah said to Amos, “Seer, go! Flee to the land of Judah, eat bread there, and prophesy there.

7:13  LA: Et in Bethel non adjicies ultra ut prophetes, quia sanctificatio regis est, et domus regni est.
      EN: But you shall no longer prophesy at Bethel, for it is the king's sanctuary and the royal house.”

7:14  LA: Responditque Amos, et dixit ad Amasiam : Non sum propheta, et non sum filius prophetæ : sed armentarius ego sum vellicans sycomoros.
      EN: Amos answered Amaziah, “I am not a prophet, nor am I a prophet's son; I am a herdsman, plucking sycamore figs.

7:15  LA: Et tulit me Dominus cum sequerer gregem, et dixit Dominus ad me : Vade, propheta ad populum meum Israël.
      EN: The Lord took me as I followed the flock, and the Lord said to me, ‘Go, prophesy to My people Israel.’

7:16  LA: Et nunc audi verbum Domini : [Tu dicis : Non prophetabis super Israël, et non stillabis super domum idoli.
      EN: Now hear the word of the Lord. You say, ‘You shall not prophesy against Israel or pour out speech against the house of the idol.’

7:17  LA: Propter hoc hæc dicit Dominus : Uxor tua in civitate fornicabitur, et filii tui et filiæ tuæ in gladio cadent, et humus tua funiculo metietur : et tu in terra polluta morieris, et Israël captivus migrabit de terra sua.]
      EN: Therefore thus says the Lord: ‘Your wife will prostitute herself in the city; your sons and daughters will fall by the sword; your ground will be measured out by a cord. You will die in a polluted land, and Israel will go captive into exile from his own land.’”

AMOS 8

8:1  LA: Hæc ostendit mihi Dominus Deus : et ecce uncinus pomorum.
     EN: The Lord God showed me this: see, a hook for fruit.

8:2  LA: Et dixit : Quid tu vides, Amos ? Et dixi : Uncinum pomorum. Et dixit Dominus ad me : [Venit finis super populum meum Israël ; non adjiciam ultra ut pertranseam eum.
     EN: He said, “What do you see, Amos?” I said, “A hook for fruit.” The Lord said to me, “The end has come upon My people Israel; I will no longer pass over him.

8:3  LA: Et stridebunt cardines templi in die illa, dicit Dominus Deus : multi morientur ; in omni loco projicietur silentium.]
     EN: The temple's hinges will creak on that day,” says the Lord God. “Many will die; silence will be cast over every place.”

8:4  LA: [Audite hoc, qui conteritis pauperem, et deficere facitis egenos terræ,
     EN: Hear this, you who crush the poor and bring the needy of the land to an end,

8:5  LA: dicentes : Quando transibit mensis, et venundabimus merces ? et sabbatum, et aperiemus frumentum, ut imminuamus mensuram, et augeamus siclum, et supponamus stateras dolosas,
     EN: who say, “When will the month pass, so we may sell merchandise, and the sabbath, so we may open up the grain, reducing the measure, increasing the shekel, and substituting deceitful balances,

8:6  LA: ut possideamus in argento egenos et pauperes pro calceamentis, et quisquilias frumenti vendamus ?
     EN: so that we may possess the needy for silver and the poor for footwear, and sell the refuse of the grain?”

8:7  LA: Juravit Dominus in superbiam Jacob : Si oblitus fuero usque ad finem omnia opera eorum.
     EN: The Lord has sworn against Jacob's pride: “Never will I forget any of their deeds.”

8:8  LA: Numquid super isto non commovebitur terra, et lugebit omnis habitator ejus, et ascendet quasi fluvius universus, et ejicicetur, et defluet, quasi rivus Ægypti ?
     EN: Will the land not shake over this, and every inhabitant of it mourn? It will rise up, all of it, like a river, be cast out, and sink down like the stream of Egypt.

8:9  LA: Et erit in die illa, dicit Dominus Deus : occidet sol in meridie, et tenebrescere faciam terram in die luminis :
     EN: “On that day,” says the Lord God, “the sun will set at noon, and I will darken the land in the light of day.

8:10  LA: et convertam festivitates vestras in luctum, et omnia cantica vestra in planctum, et inducam super omne dorsum vestrum saccum, et super omne caput calvitium : et ponam eam quasi luctum unigeniti, et novissima ejus quasi diem amarum.
      EN: I will turn your feasts into mourning and all your songs into lamentation. I will put sackcloth upon every back of yours and baldness upon every head; I will make it like mourning for an only son and its end like a bitter day.

8:11  LA: Ecce dies veniunt, dicet Dominus, et mittam famem in terram : non famem panis, neque sitim aquæ, sed audiendi verbum Domini.
      EN: See, days are coming,” the Lord will say, “when I will send a famine upon the land—not a famine of bread or a thirst for water, but of hearing the word of the Lord.

8:12  LA: Et commovebuntur a mari usque ad mare, et ab aquilone usque ad orientem : circuibunt quærentes verbum Domini, et non invenient.
      EN: They will be driven from sea to sea and from north to east; they will roam seeking the word of the Lord and will not find it.

8:13  LA: In die illa deficient virgines pulchræ et adolescentes in siti,
      EN: On that day beautiful virgins and young men will faint from thirst,

8:14  LA: qui jurant in delicto Samariæ, et dicunt : Vivit Deus tuus, Dan, et vivit via Bersabee ; et cadent, et non resurgent ultra.]
      EN: those who swear by Samaria's sin and say, ‘As your god lives, Dan,’ and, ‘As the way of Beer-sheba lives.’ They will fall and rise no more.”

AMOS 9

9:1  LA: Vidi Dominum stantem super altare, et dixit : [Percute cardinem, et commoveantur superliminaria : avaritia enim in capite omnium, et novissimum eorum in gladio interficiam ; non erit fuga eis. Fugient, et non salvabitur ex eis qui fugerit.
     EN: I saw the Lord standing upon the altar, and He said, “Strike the hinge, and let the lintels shake, for greed is at the head of them all; I will kill the last of them with the sword. There will be no escape for them. They will flee, and none who flees will be saved.

9:2  LA: Si descenderint usque ad infernum, inde manus mea educet eos ; et si ascenderint usque in cælum, inde detraham eos.
     EN: If they descend to the underworld, from there My hand will bring them out; if they ascend to heaven, from there I will bring them down.

9:3  LA: Et si absconditi fuerint in vertice Carmeli, inde scrutans auferam eos ; et si celaverint se ab oculis meis in profundo maris, ibi mandabo serpenti, et mordebit eos.
     EN: If they hide on Carmel's summit, from there I will search them out and take them away; if they conceal themselves from My eyes in the depths of the sea, there I will command the serpent, and it will bite them.

9:4  LA: Et si abierint in captivitatem coram inimicis suis, ibi mandabo gladio, et occidet eos : et ponam oculos meos super eos in malum, et non in bonum.
     EN: If they go into captivity before their enemies, there I will command the sword, and it will kill them. I will set My eyes upon them for harm and not for good.”

9:5  LA: Et Dominus Deus exercituum, qui tangit terram, et tabescet, et lugebunt omnes habitantes in ea : et ascendet sicut rivus omnis, et defluet sicut fluvius Ægypti.
     EN: The Lord God of hosts touches the earth, and it melts, and all who dwell in it will mourn. It will all rise like a stream and sink down like the river of Egypt.

9:6  LA: Qui ædificat in cælo ascensionem suam, et fasciculum suum super terram fundavit ; qui vocat aquas maris, et effundit eas super faciem terræ : Dominus nomen ejus.
     EN: He builds His ascent in heaven and set His bundle firmly upon the earth; He calls the waters of the sea and pours them over the earth's surface—the Lord is His name.

9:7  LA: Numquid non ut filii Æthiopum vos estis mihi, filii Israël ? ait Dominus. Numquid non Israël ascendere feci de terra Ægypti, et Palæstinos de Cappadocia, et Syros de Cyrene ?]
     EN: “Are you not like the children of the Ethiopians to Me, children of Israel?” says the Lord. “Did I not bring Israel up from the land of Egypt, the Philistines from Cappadocia, and the Syrians from Cyrene?

9:8  LA: [Ecce oculi Domini Dei super regnum peccans : et conteram illud a facie terræ ; verumtamen conterens non conteram domum Jacob, dicit Dominus.
     EN: See, the eyes of the Lord God are upon the sinful kingdom, and I will crush it from the face of the earth. Yet I will not utterly crush the house of Jacob,” says the Lord.

9:9  LA: Ecce enim mandabo ego, et concutiam in omnibus gentibus domum Israël, sicut concutitur triticum in cribro, et non cadet lapillus super terram.
     EN: “For see, I will command and shake the house of Israel among all nations, as wheat is shaken in a sieve, and no small stone will fall to the ground.

9:10  LA: In gladio morientur omnes peccatores populi mei, qui dicunt : Non appropinquabit, et non veniet super nos malum.
      EN: All the sinners of My people will die by the sword, those who say, ‘Disaster will not approach or come upon us.’

9:11  LA: In die illa suscitabo tabernaculum David, quod cecidit : et reædificabo aperturas murorum ejus, et ea quæ corruerant instaurabo : et reædificabo illud sicut in diebus antiquis,
      EN: On that day I will raise up David's tent that has fallen; I will rebuild the breaches in its walls and restore what had collapsed. I will rebuild it as in the days of old,

9:12  LA: ut possideant reliquias Idumææ, et omnes nationes : eo quod invocatum sit nomen meum super eos, dicit Dominus faciens hæc.
      EN: so that they may possess the remnant of Edom and all nations, because My name has been called upon them,” says the Lord who does these things.

9:13  LA: Ecce dies veniunt, dicit Dominus, et comprehendet arator messorem, et calcator uvæ mittentem semen : et stillabunt montes dulcedinem, et omnes colli culti erunt.
      EN: “See, days are coming,” says the Lord, “when the plowman will overtake the reaper and the grape treader the sower of seed. The mountains will drip sweetness, and all the hills will be cultivated.

9:14  LA: Et convertam captivitatem populi mei Israël ; et ædificabunt civitates desertas, et inhabitabunt ; et plantabunt vineas, et bibent vinum earum, et facient hortos, et comedent fructus eorum.
      EN: I will reverse the captivity of My people Israel; they will build the deserted cities and dwell in them, plant vineyards and drink their wine, make gardens and eat their fruit.

9:15  LA: Et plantabo eos super humum suam, et non evellam eos ultra de terra sua, quam dedi eis, dicit Dominus Deus tuus.]
      EN: I will plant them upon their own soil and no longer uproot them from the land that I gave them,” says the Lord your God.
