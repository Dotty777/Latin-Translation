EXODUS 1

1:1  LA: Hæc sunt nomina filiorum Israël qui ingressi sunt in Ægyptum cum Jacob : singuli cum domibus suis introierunt :
     EN: These are the names of the sons of Israel who entered Egypt with Jacob; each entered with his household:

1:2  LA: Ruben, Simeon, Levi, Judas,
     EN: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah,

1:3  LA: Issachar, Zabulon et Benjamin,
     EN: Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin,

1:4  LA: Dan et Nephthali, Gad et Aser.
     EN: Dan and Naphtali, Gad and Asher.

1:5  LA: Erant igitur omnes animæ eorum qui egressi sunt de femore Jacob, septuaginta : Joseph autem in Ægypto erat.
     EN: All the persons who came from Jacob's thigh numbered seventy. Joseph, however, was in Egypt.

1:6  LA: Quo mortuo, et universis fratribus ejus, omnique cognatione illa,
     EN: After he died, along with all his brothers and that entire generation,

1:7  LA: filii Israël creverunt, et quasi germinantes multiplicati sunt : ac roborati nimis, impleverunt terram.
     EN: the children of Israel increased and multiplied as though sprouting up. They grew exceedingly strong and filled the land.

1:8  LA: Surrexit interea rex novus super Ægyptum, qui ignorabat Joseph.
     EN: Meanwhile, a new king arose over Egypt who did not know Joseph.

1:9  LA: Et ait ad populum suum : Ecce, populus filiorum Israël multus, et fortior nobis est.
     EN: He said to his people, “Look, the people of the children of Israel are numerous and stronger than we are.

1:10  LA: Venite, sapienter opprimamus eum, ne forte multiplicetur : et si ingruerit contra nos bellum, addatur inimicis nostris, expugnatisque nobis egrediatur de terra.
     EN: Come, let us oppress them shrewdly, lest they multiply and, if war breaks out against us, join our enemies, defeat us, and leave the land.”

1:11  LA: Præposuit itaque eis magistros operum, ut affligerent eos oneribus : ædificaveruntque urbes tabernaculorum Pharaoni, Phithom et Ramesses.
     EN: So he appointed taskmasters over them to afflict them with burdens. They built storage cities for Pharaoh: Pithom and Rameses.

1:12  LA: Quantoque opprimebant eos, tanto magis multiplicabantur, et crescebant :
     EN: But the more they oppressed them, the more they multiplied and increased.

1:13  LA: oderantque filios Israël Ægyptii, et affligebant illudentes eis,
     EN: The Egyptians hated the children of Israel and afflicted them, treating them contemptuously.

1:14  LA: atque ad amaritudinem perducebant vitam eorum operibus duris luti et lateris, omnique famulatu, quo in terræ operibus premebantur.
     EN: They made their lives bitter with hard labor in clay and brick, and with every kind of service by which they were oppressed in working the land.

1:15  LA: Dixit autem rex Ægypti obstetricibus Hebræorum, quarum una vocabatur Sephora, altera Phua,
     EN: The king of Egypt spoke to the Hebrew midwives, one named Shiphrah and the other Puah,

1:16  LA: præcipiens eis : Quando obstetricabitis Hebræas, et partus tempus advenerit : si masculus fuerit, interficite eum : si femina, reservate.
     EN: commanding them, “When you serve the Hebrew women as midwives and the time of birth comes, if it is a boy, kill him; if a girl, preserve her.”

1:17  LA: Timuerunt autem obstetrices Deum, et non fecerunt juxta præceptum regis Ægypti, sed conservabant mares.
     EN: But the midwives feared God. They did not follow the king of Egypt's command but kept the boys alive.

1:18  LA: Quibus ad se accersitis, rex ait : Quidnam est hoc quod facere voluistis, ut pueros servaretis ?
     EN: The king summoned them and said, “Why have you chosen to do this, keeping the boys alive?”

1:19  LA: Quæ responderunt : Non sunt Hebreæ sicut ægyptiæ mulieres : ipsæ enim obstetricandi habent scientiam, et priusquam veniamus ad eas, pariunt.
     EN: They answered, “The Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women. They themselves know how to give birth, and they deliver before we come to them.”

1:20  LA: Bene ergo fecit Deus obstetricibus : et crevit populus, confortatusque est nimis.
     EN: So God dealt well with the midwives, and the people increased and grew exceedingly strong.

1:21  LA: Et quia timuerunt obstetrices Deum, ædificavit eis domos.
     EN: Because the midwives feared God, He established households for them.

1:22  LA: Præcepit ergo Pharao omni populo suo, dicens : Quidquid masculini sexus natum fuerit, in flumen projicite : quidquid feminini, reservate.
     EN: Then Pharaoh commanded all his people, saying, “Every male child that is born, throw into the river; every female, preserve.”

EXODUS 2

2:1  LA: Egressus est post hæc vir de domo Levi : et accepit uxorem stirpis suæ.
     EN: After this, a man of the house of Levi went and took a wife of his own lineage.

2:2  LA: Quæ concepit, et peperit filium : et videns eum elegantem, abscondit tribus mensibus.
     EN: She conceived and bore a son. Seeing that he was beautiful, she hid him for three months.

2:3  LA: Cumque jam celare non posset, sumpsit fiscellam scirpeam, et linivit eam bitumine ac pice : posuitque intus infantulum, et exposuit eum in carecto ripæ fluminis,
     EN: When she could no longer conceal him, she took a basket of rushes and coated it with bitumen and pitch. She placed the little child inside and set him among the reeds by the riverbank,

2:4  LA: stante procul sorore ejus, et considerante eventum rei.
     EN: while his sister stood at a distance, watching to see what would happen.

2:5  LA: Ecce autem descendebat filia Pharaonis ut lavaretur in flumine : et puellæ ejus gradiebantur per crepidinem alvei. Quæ cum vidisset fiscellam in papyrione, misit unam e famulabus suis : et allatam
     EN: Then Pharaoh's daughter came down to bathe in the river, while her young women walked along its bank. Seeing the basket among the papyrus, she sent one of her servants. When it was brought,

2:6  LA: aperiens, cernensque in ea parvulum vagientem, miserta ejus, ait : De infantibus Hebræorum est hic.
     EN: she opened it and saw a little child crying inside. Taking pity on him, she said, “This is one of the Hebrew children.”

2:7  LA: Cui soror pueri : Vis, inquit, ut vadam, et vocem tibi mulierem hebræam, quæ nutrire possit infantulum ?
     EN: The boy's sister said to her, “Would you like me to go and call a Hebrew woman for you who can nurse the little child?”

2:8  LA: Respondit : Vade. Perrexit puella et vocavit matrem suam.
     EN: She answered, “Go.” The girl went and called her mother.

2:9  LA: Ad quam locuta filia Pharaonis : Accipe, ait, puerum istum, et nutri mihi : ego dabo tibi mercedem tuam. Suscepit mulier, et nutrivit puerum : adultumque tradidit filiæ Pharaonis.
     EN: Pharaoh's daughter said to her, “Take this boy and nurse him for me, and I will give you your wages.” The woman took the boy and nursed him; when he had grown, she handed him over to Pharaoh's daughter.

2:10  LA: Quem illa adoptavit in locum filii, vocavitque nomen ejus Moyses, dicens : Quia de aqua tuli eum.
     EN: She adopted him as her son and called his name Moses, saying, “Because I drew him out of the water.”

2:11  LA: In diebus illis postquam creverat Moyses, egressus est ad fratres suos : viditque afflictionem eorum, et virum ægyptium percutientem quemdam de Hebræis fratribus suis.
     EN: In those days, after Moses had grown, he went out to his brothers. He saw their affliction and an Egyptian man striking one of his Hebrew brothers.

2:12  LA: Cumque circumspexisset huc atque illuc, et nullum adesse vidisset, percussum Ægyptium abscondit sabulo.
     EN: He looked this way and that, and when he saw no one present, he struck down the Egyptian and hid him in the sand.

2:13  LA: Et egressus die altero conspexit duos Hebræos rixantes : dixitque ei qui faciebat injuriam : Quare percutis proximum tuum ?
     EN: Going out the next day, he saw two Hebrews quarreling. He said to the one doing wrong, “Why are you striking your neighbor?”

2:14  LA: Qui respondit : Quis te constituit principem et judicem super nos ? num occidere me tu vis, sicut heri occidisti Ægyptium ? Timuit Moyses, et ait : Quomodo palam factum est verbum istud ?
     EN: He answered, “Who appointed you ruler and judge over us? Do you intend to kill me, as you killed the Egyptian yesterday?” Moses was afraid and said, “How has this matter become known?”

2:15  LA: Audivitque Pharao sermonem hunc, et quærebat occidere Moysen : qui fugiens de conspectu ejus, moratus est in terra Madian, et sedit juxta puteum.
     EN: Pharaoh heard of it and sought to kill Moses. But Moses fled from his presence and stayed in the land of Midian, where he sat beside a well.

2:16  LA: Erant autem sacerdoti Madian septem filiæ, quæ venerunt ad hauriendam aquam : et impletis canalibus adaquare cupiebant greges patris sui.
     EN: The priest of Midian had seven daughters. They came to draw water and, after filling the troughs, wished to water their father's flocks.

2:17  LA: Supervenere pastores, et ejecerunt eas : surrexitque Moyses, et defensis puellis, adaquavit oves earum.
     EN: Shepherds came and drove them away, but Moses rose, defended the young women, and watered their sheep.

2:18  LA: Quæ cum revertissent ad Raguel patrem suum, dixit ad eas : Cur velocius venistis solito ?
     EN: When they returned to their father Reuel, he said to them, “Why have you come back sooner than usual?”

2:19  LA: Responderunt : Vir ægyptius liberavit nos de manu pastorum : insuper et hausit aquam nobiscum, potumque dedit ovibus.
     EN: They answered, “An Egyptian man rescued us from the shepherds' hands. He even drew water with us and gave the sheep a drink.”

2:20  LA: At ille : Ubi est ? inquit : quare dimisistis hominem ? vocate eum ut comedat panem.
     EN: He said, “Where is he? Why did you leave the man? Call him to eat bread.”

2:21  LA: Juravit ergo Moyses quod habitaret cum eo. Accepitque Sephoram filiam ejus uxorem :
     EN: Moses therefore swore that he would live with him, and he took his daughter Zipporah as his wife.

2:22  LA: quæ peperit ei filium, quem vocavit Gersam, dicens : Advena fui in terra aliena. Alterum vero peperit, quem vocavit Eliezer, dicens : Deus enim patris mei adjutor meus eripuit me de manu Pharaonis.
     EN: She bore him a son, whom he called Gershom, saying, “I have been a stranger in a foreign land.” She bore another, whom he called Eliezer, saying, “For the God of my father, my Helper, rescued me from Pharaoh's hand.”

2:23  LA: Post multum vero tempore mortuus est rex Ægypti : et ingemiscentes filii Israël, propter opera vociferati sunt : ascenditque clamor eorum ad Deum ab operibus.
     EN: After a long time, the king of Egypt died. The children of Israel groaned and cried out because of their labor, and their cry because of their labor rose to God.

2:24  LA: Et audivit gemitum eorum, ac recordatus est fœderis quod pepigit cum Abraham, Isaac et Jacob.
     EN: He heard their groaning and remembered the covenant He had made with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

2:25  LA: Et respexit Dominus filios Israël et cognovit eos.
     EN: The Lord looked upon the children of Israel and acknowledged them.

EXODUS 3

3:1  LA: Moyses autem pascebat oves Jethro soceri sui sacerdotis Madian : cumque minasset gregem ad interiora deserti, venit ad montem Dei Horeb.
     EN: Moses was tending the sheep of his father-in-law Jethro, the priest of Midian. Driving the flock into the interior of the wilderness, he came to Horeb, the mountain of God.

3:2  LA: Apparuitque ei Dominus in flamma ignis de medio rubi : et videbat quod rubus arderet, et non combureretur.
     EN: The Lord appeared to him in a flame of fire from the midst of a bush. He saw that the bush was burning but was not being consumed.

3:3  LA: Dixit ergo Moyses : Vadam, et videbo visionem hanc magnam, quare non comburatur rubus.
     EN: Moses said, “I will go and see this great sight, why the bush is not consumed.”

3:4  LA: Cernens autem Dominus quod pergeret ad videndum, vocavit eum de medio rubi, et ait : Moyses, Moyses. Qui respondit : Adsum.
     EN: When the Lord saw that he was coming to look, He called to him from the midst of the bush, saying, “Moses, Moses!” He answered, “Here I am.”

3:5  LA: At ille : Ne appropies, inquit, huc : solve calceamentum de pedibus tuis : locus enim, in quo stas, terra sancta est.
     EN: He said, “Do not come near here. Remove the sandals from your feet, for the place where you stand is holy ground.”

3:6  LA: Et ait : Ego sum Deus patris tui, Deus Abraham, Deus Isaac et Deus Jacob. Abscondit Moyses faciem suam : non enim audebat aspicere contra Deum.
     EN: He said, “I am the God of your father, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.” Moses hid his face, for he did not dare look toward God.

3:7  LA: Cui ait Dominus : Vidi afflictionem populi mei in Ægypto, et clamorem ejus audivi propter duritiam eorum qui præsunt operibus :
     EN: The Lord said to him, “I have seen the affliction of My people in Egypt, and I have heard their cry because of the harshness of their taskmasters.

3:8  LA: et sciens dolorem ejus, descendi ut liberem eum de manibus Ægyptiorum, et educam de terra illa in terram bonam, et spatiosam, in terram quæ fluit lacte et melle, ad loca Chananæi et Hethæi, et Amorrhæi, et Pherezæi, et Hevæi, et Jebusæi.
     EN: Knowing their suffering, I have come down to deliver them from the Egyptians' hands and bring them up from that land into a good and spacious land, a land flowing with milk and honey, to the places of the Canaanite, Hittite, Amorite, Perizzite, Hivite, and Jebusite.

3:9  LA: Clamor ergo filiorum Israël venit ad me : vidique afflictionem eorum, qua ab Ægyptiis opprimuntur.
     EN: The cry of the children of Israel has reached Me, and I have seen the affliction with which the Egyptians oppress them.

3:10  LA: Sed veni, et mittam te ad Pharaonem, ut educas populum meum, filios Israël, de Ægypto.
     EN: But come, and I will send you to Pharaoh, so that you may lead My people, the children of Israel, out of Egypt.”

3:11  LA: Dixitque Moyses ad Deum : Quis sum ego ut vadam ad Pharaonem, et educam filios Israël de Ægypto ?
     EN: Moses said to God, “Who am I to go to Pharaoh and lead the children of Israel out of Egypt?”

3:12  LA: Qui dixit ei : Ego ero tecum : et hoc habebis signum, quod miserim te : cum eduxeris populum meum de Ægypto, immolabis Deo super montem istum.
     EN: He said to him, “I will be with you. This will be your sign that I have sent you: when you have led My people out of Egypt, you will sacrifice to God on this mountain.”

3:13  LA: Ait Moyses ad Deum : Ecce ego vadam ad filios Israël, et dicam eis : Deus patrum vestrorum misit me ad vos. Si dixerint mihi : Quod est nomen ejus ? quid dicam eis ?
     EN: Moses said to God, “Look, I will go to the children of Israel and tell them, ‘The God of your fathers has sent me to you.’ If they say to me, ‘What is His name?’ what shall I tell them?”

3:14  LA: Dixit Deus ad Moysen : Ego sum qui sum. Ait : Sic dices filiis Israël : Qui est, misit me ad vos.
     EN: God said to Moses, “I am who I am.” He said, “Thus you shall tell the children of Israel: ‘He who is has sent me to you.’”

3:15  LA: Dixitque iterum Deus ad Moysen : Hæc dices filiis Israël : Dominus Deus patrum vestrorum, Deus Abraham, Deus Isaac et Deus Jacob, misit me ad vos : hoc nomen mihi est in æternum, et hoc memoriale meum in generationem et generationem.
     EN: God said again to Moses, “You shall tell the children of Israel this: ‘The Lord, the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has sent me to you.’ This is My name forever, and this is My memorial from generation to generation.

3:16  LA: Vade, et congrega seniores Israël, et dices ad eos : Dominus Deus patrum vestrorum apparuit mihi, Deus Abraham, Deus Isaac et Deus Jacob, dicens : Visitans visitavi vos : et vidi omnia quæ acciderunt vobis in Ægypto.
     EN: Go, gather the elders of Israel, and tell them: ‘The Lord, the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, appeared to me, saying: I have indeed visited you and seen everything that has happened to you in Egypt.

3:17  LA: Et dixi ut educam vos de afflictione Ægypti in terram Chananæi, et Hethæi, et Amorrhæi, et Pherezæi, et Hevæi, et Jebusæi, ad terram fluentem lacte et melle.
     EN: And I have declared that I will bring you out of the affliction of Egypt into the land of the Canaanite, Hittite, Amorite, Perizzite, Hivite, and Jebusite, into a land flowing with milk and honey.’

3:18  LA: Et audient vocem tuam : ingredierisque tu, et seniores Israël, ad regem Ægypti, et dices ad eum : Dominus Deus Hebræorum vocavit nos : ibimus viam trium dierum in solitudinem, ut immolemus Domino Deo nostro.
     EN: They will listen to your voice. You and the elders of Israel shall go to the king of Egypt and say to him, ‘The Lord, the God of the Hebrews, has called us. We will go a three-day journey into the wilderness to sacrifice to the Lord our God.’

3:19  LA: Sed ego scio quod non dimittet vos rex Ægypti ut eatis nisi per manum validam.
     EN: But I know that the king of Egypt will not let you go except under a mighty hand.

3:20  LA: Extendam enim manum meam, et percutiam Ægyptum in cunctis mirabilibus meis, quæ facturus sum in medio eorum : post hæc dimittet vos.
     EN: For I will stretch out My hand and strike Egypt with all My wonders that I will perform among them. After this he will let you go.

3:21  LA: Daboque gratiam populo huic coram Ægyptiis : et cum egrediemini, non exibitis vacui :
     EN: I will give this people favor before the Egyptians, and when you depart, you will not go empty-handed.

3:22  LA: sed postulabit mulier a vicina sua et ab hospita sua, vasa argentea et aurea, ac vestes : ponetisque eas super filios et filias vestras, et spoliabitis Ægyptum.
     EN: Each woman shall ask her neighbor and the woman lodging with her for articles of silver and gold and for clothing. You shall put these on your sons and daughters, and you will despoil Egypt.”

EXODUS 4

4:1  LA: Respondens Moyses ait : Non credent mihi, neque audient vocem meam, sed dicent : Non apparuit tibi Dominus.
     EN: Moses answered, “They will not believe me or listen to my voice, but will say, ‘The Lord has not appeared to you.’”

4:2  LA: Dixit ergo ad eum : Quid est quod tenes in manu tua ? Respondit : Virga.
     EN: He therefore said to him, “What is that you hold in your hand?” He answered, “A staff.”

4:3  LA: Dixitque Dominus : Projice eam in terram. Projecit, et versa est in colubrum, ita ut fugeret Moyses.
     EN: The Lord said, “Throw it on the ground.” He threw it, and it turned into a snake, so that Moses fled.

4:4  LA: Dixitque Dominus : Extende manum tuam, et apprehende caudam ejus. Extendit, et tenuit, versaque est in virgam.
     EN: The Lord said, “Stretch out your hand and grasp its tail.” He stretched it out and grasped it, and it turned into a staff.

4:5  LA: Ut credant, inquit, quod apparuerit tibi Dominus Deus patrum suorum, Deus Abraham, Deus Isaac et Deus Jacob.
     EN: “This is so they may believe,” He said, “that the Lord, the God of their fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has appeared to you.”

4:6  LA: Dixitque Dominus rursum : Mitte manum tuam in sinum tuum. Quam cum misisset in sinum, protulit leprosam instar nivis.
     EN: The Lord said again, “Put your hand into your bosom.” He put it into his bosom and brought it out leprous, like snow.

4:7  LA: Retrahe, ait, manum tuam in sinum tuum. Retraxit, et protulit iterum, et erat similis carni reliquæ.
     EN: “Put your hand back into your bosom,” He said. He put it back and brought it out again, and it was like the rest of his flesh.

4:8  LA: Si non crediderint, inquit, tibi, neque audierint sermonem signi prioris, credent verbo signi sequentis.
     EN: “If they do not believe you,” He said, “or heed the message of the first sign, they will believe the message of the next sign.

4:9  LA: Quod si nec duobus quidem his signis crediderint, neque audierint vocem tuam : sume aquam fluminis, et effunde eam super aridam, et quidquid hauseris de fluvio, vertetur in sanguinem.
     EN: But if they do not believe even these two signs or listen to your voice, take water from the river and pour it on dry ground. Whatever you draw from the river will turn into blood.”

4:10  LA: Ait Moyses : Obsecro, Domine, non sum eloquens ab heri et nudiustertius : et ex quo locutus es ad servum tuum, impeditioris et tardioris linguæ sum.
     EN: Moses said, “Please, Lord, I have not been eloquent, either yesterday or the day before. Even since You spoke to Your servant, my tongue has been hindered and slow.”

4:11  LA: Dixit Dominus ad eum : Quis fecit os hominis ? aut quis fabricatus est mutum et surdum, videntem et cæcum ? nonne ego ?
     EN: The Lord said to him, “Who made the human mouth? Or who made the mute and deaf, the seeing and the blind? Was it not I?

4:12  LA: Perge, igitur, et ego ero in ore tuo : doceboque te quid loquaris.
     EN: Go, therefore, and I will be in your mouth and teach you what to say.”

4:13  LA: At ille : Obsecro, inquit, Domine, mitte quem missurus es.
     EN: But he said, “Please, Lord, send the one You are going to send.”

4:14  LA: Iratus Dominus in Moysen, ait : Aaron frater tuus Levites, scio quod eloquens sit : ecce ipse egreditur in occursum tuum, vidensque te lætabitur corde.
     EN: The Lord grew angry with Moses and said, “I know that Aaron, your brother the Levite, is eloquent. Look, he is coming out to meet you, and when he sees you, his heart will rejoice.

4:15  LA: Loquere ad eum, et pone verba mea in ore ejus : et ego ero in ore tuo, et in ore illius, et ostendam vobis quid agere debeatis.
     EN: Speak to him and put My words in his mouth. I will be in your mouth and in his mouth, and I will show you what you must do.

4:16  LA: Ipse loquetur pro te ad populum, et erit os tuum : tu autem eris ei in his quæ ad Deum pertinent.
     EN: He will speak to the people for you and be your mouth; you will be his guide in the things pertaining to God.

4:17  LA: Virgam quoque hanc sume in manu tua, in qua facturus es signa.
     EN: Take this staff in your hand as well; with it you will perform the signs.”

4:18  LA: Abiit Moyses, et reversus est ad Jethro socerum suum, dixitque ei : Vadam et revertar ad fratres meos in Ægyptum, ut videam si adhuc vivant. Cui ait Jethro : Vade in pace.
     EN: Moses went back to his father-in-law Jethro and said to him, “I will go back to my brothers in Egypt to see whether they are still alive.” Jethro said to him, “Go in peace.”

4:19  LA: Dixit ergo Dominus ad Moysen in Madian : Vade, et revertere in Ægyptum, mortui sunt enim omnes qui quærebant animam tuam.
     EN: Then the Lord said to Moses in Midian, “Go back to Egypt, for all those seeking your life have died.”

4:20  LA: Tulit ergo Moyses uxorem suam, et filios suos, et imposuit eos super asinum : reversusque est in Ægyptum, portans virgam Dei in manu sua.
     EN: Moses took his wife and sons, set them on a donkey, and returned to Egypt, carrying the staff of God in his hand.

4:21  LA: Dixitque ei Dominus revertenti in Ægyptum : Vide ut omnia ostenta quæ posui in manu tua, facias coram Pharaone : ego indurabo cor ejus, et non dimittet populum.
     EN: As he returned to Egypt, the Lord said to him, “See that you perform before Pharaoh all the wonders I have placed in your hand. I will harden his heart, and he will not release the people.

4:22  LA: Dicesque ad eum : Hæc dicit Dominus : Filius meus primogenitus Israël.
     EN: You shall say to him, ‘Thus says the Lord: Israel is My firstborn son.

4:23  LA: Dixi tibi : Dimitte filium meum ut serviat mihi ; et noluisti dimittere eum : ecce ego interficiam filium tuum primogenitum.
     EN: I told you: Release My son, so that he may serve Me. But you refused to release him. Look, I will kill your firstborn son.’”

4:24  LA: Cumque esset in itinere, in diversorio occurrit ei Dominus, et volebat occidere eum.
     EN: While he was on the journey, the Lord met him at a lodging place and sought to kill him.

4:25  LA: Tulit idcirco Sephora acutissimam petram, et circumcidit præputium filii sui, tetigitque pedes ejus, et ait : Sponsus sanguinum tu mihi es.
     EN: So Zipporah took a very sharp stone, cut off her son's foreskin, touched his feet, and said, “You are a bridegroom of blood to me.”

4:26  LA: Et dimisit eum postquam dixerat : Sponsus sanguinum ob circumcisionem.
     EN: He let him go after she had said, “A bridegroom of blood,” because of the circumcision.

4:27  LA: Dixit autem Dominus ad Aaron : Vade in occursum Moysi in desertum. Qui perrexit obviam ei in montem Dei, et osculatus est eum.
     EN: The Lord said to Aaron, “Go into the wilderness to meet Moses.” He went to meet him at the mountain of God and kissed him.

4:28  LA: Narravitque Moyses Aaron omnia verba Domini quibus miserat eum, et signa quæ mandaverat.
     EN: Moses told Aaron all the Lord's words with which He had sent him, and the signs He had commanded.

4:29  LA: Veneruntque simul, et congregaverunt cunctos seniores filiorum Israël.
     EN: They came together and gathered all the elders of the children of Israel.

4:30  LA: Locutusque est Aaron omnia verba quæ dixerat Dominus ad Moysen : et fecit signa coram populo,
     EN: Aaron spoke all the words the Lord had spoken to Moses, and he performed the signs before the people.

4:31  LA: et credidit populus. Audieruntque quod visitasset Dominus filios Israël, et respexisset afflictionem illorum : et proni adoraverunt.
     EN: The people believed. They heard that the Lord had visited the children of Israel and looked upon their affliction, and they bowed down in worship.

EXODUS 5

5:1  LA: Post hæc ingressi sunt Moyses et Aaron, et dixerunt Pharaoni : Hæc dicit Dominus Deus Israël : Dimitte populum meum ut sacrificet mihi in deserto.
     EN: After this, Moses and Aaron went in and said to Pharaoh, “Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: Release My people, so that they may sacrifice to Me in the wilderness.”

5:2  LA: At ille respondit : Quis est Dominus, ut audiam vocem ejus, et dimittam Israël ? nescio Dominum, et Israël non dimittam.
     EN: But he answered, “Who is the Lord, that I should listen to His voice and release Israel? I do not know the Lord, and I will not release Israel.”

5:3  LA: Dixeruntque : Deus Hebræorum vocavit nos, ut eamus viam trium dierum in solitudinem, et sacrificemus Domino Deo nostro : ne forte accidat nobis pestis aut gladius.
     EN: They said, “The God of the Hebrews has called us to go a three-day journey into the wilderness and sacrifice to the Lord our God, lest plague or sword fall upon us.”

5:4  LA: Ait ad eos rex Ægypti : Quare Moyses et Aaron sollicitatis populum ab operibus suis ? ite ad onera vestra.
     EN: The king of Egypt said to them, “Moses and Aaron, why are you drawing the people away from their work? Go to your burdens.”

5:5  LA: Dixitque Pharao : Multus est populus terræ : videtis quod turba succreverit : quanto magis si dederitis eis requiem ab operibus ?
     EN: Pharaoh said, “The people of the land are numerous. You see how the multitude has grown; how much more if you give them rest from their work?”

5:6  LA: Præcepit ergo in die illo præfectis operum et exactoribus populi, dicens :
     EN: That day Pharaoh commanded the taskmasters and the people's overseers, saying,

5:7  LA: Nequaquam ultra dabitis paleas populo ad conficiendos lateres, sicut prius : sed ipsi vadant, et colligant stipulas.
     EN: “You shall no longer give the people straw to make bricks, as before. Let them go themselves and gather stubble.

5:8  LA: Et mensuram laterum, quam prius faciebant, imponetis super eos, nec minuetis quidquam : vacant enim, et idcirco vociferantur, dicentes : Eamus, et sacrificemus Deo nostro.
     EN: Impose on them the same quantity of bricks they made before, without reducing it at all. They are idle, and therefore they cry out, saying, ‘Let us go and sacrifice to our God.’

5:9  LA: Opprimantur operibus, et expleant ea : ut non acquiescant verbis mendacibus.
     EN: Let them be weighed down with work and complete it, so that they do not yield to lying words.”

5:10  LA: Igitur egressi præfecti operum et exactores ad populum, dixerunt : Sic dicit Pharao : Non do vobis paleas :
     EN: The taskmasters and overseers went out and told the people, “Thus says Pharaoh: I am not giving you straw.

5:11  LA: ite, et colligite sicubi invenire poteritis, nec minuetur quidquam de opere vestro.
     EN: Go and gather it wherever you can find it, but none of your work will be reduced.”

5:12  LA: Dispersusque est populus per omnem terram Ægypti ad colligendas paleas.
     EN: The people scattered throughout all the land of Egypt to gather straw.

5:13  LA: Præfecti quoque operum instabant, dicentes : Complete opus vestrum quotidie, ut prius facere solebatis quando dabantur vobis paleæ.
     EN: The taskmasters pressed them, saying, “Complete your work each day, as you used to do when straw was given to you.”

5:14  LA: Flagellatique sunt qui præerant operibus filiorum Israël, ab exactoribus Pharaonis, dicentibus : Quare non impletis mensuram laterum sicut prius, nec heri, nec hodie ?
     EN: The overseers of the work of the children of Israel were whipped by Pharaoh's taskmasters, who said, “Why have you not completed the same quantity of bricks as before, either yesterday or today?”

5:15  LA: Veneruntque præpositi filiorum Israël, et vociferati sunt ad Pharaonem dicentes : Cur ita agis contra servos tuos ?
     EN: The overseers of the children of Israel came and cried out to Pharaoh, saying, “Why do you treat your servants this way?

5:16  LA: paleæ non dantur nobis, et lateres similiter imperantur : en famuli tui flagellis cædimur, et injuste agitur contra populum tuum.
     EN: We are given no straw, yet the same bricks are demanded. Look, we your servants are beaten with whips, and your people are treated unjustly.”

5:17  LA: Qui ait : Vacatis otio, et idcirco dicitis : Eamus, et sacrificemus Domino.
     EN: He said, “You are sitting idle, and therefore you say, ‘Let us go and sacrifice to the Lord.’

5:18  LA: Ite ergo, et operamini : paleæ non dabuntur vobis, et reddetis consuetum numerum laterum.
     EN: Go and work, then. You will not be given straw, but you shall deliver the usual number of bricks.”

5:19  LA: Videbantque se præpositi filiorum Israël in malo, eo quod diceretur eis : Non minuetur quidquam de lateribus per singulos dies.
     EN: The overseers of the children of Israel saw that they were in trouble, because they were told, “Your daily bricks will not be reduced at all.”

5:20  LA: Occurreruntque Moysi et Aaron, qui stabant ex adverso, egredientibus a Pharaone :
     EN: As they came out from Pharaoh, they met Moses and Aaron, who stood opposite them.

5:21  LA: et dixerunt ad eos : Videat Dominus et judicet, quoniam fœtere fecistis odorem nostrum coram Pharaone et servis ejus, et præbuistis ei gladium, ut occideret nos.
     EN: They said to them, “May the Lord look and judge, because you have made our odor foul before Pharaoh and his servants, and have put a sword in his hand to kill us.”

5:22  LA: Reversusque est Moyses ad Dominum, et ait : Domine, cur afflixisti populum istum ? quare misisti me ?
     EN: Moses returned to the Lord and said, “Lord, why have You afflicted this people? Why did You send me?

5:23  LA: ex eo enim quo ingressus sum ad Pharaonem ut loquerer in nomine tuo, afflixit populum tuum : et non liberasti eos.
     EN: For ever since I went to Pharaoh to speak in Your name, he has afflicted Your people, and You have not delivered them.”

EXODUS 6

6:1  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad Moysen : Nunc videbis quæ facturus sim Pharaoni : per manum enim fortem dimittet eos, et in manu robusta ejiciet illos de terra sua.
     EN: The Lord said to Moses, “Now you will see what I will do to Pharaoh. Under a strong hand he will release them, and under a mighty hand he will drive them from his land.”

6:2  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen dicens : Ego Dominus
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying, “I am the Lord,

6:3  LA: qui apparui Abraham, Isaac et Jacob in Deo omnipotente : et nomen meum Adonai non indicavi eis.
     EN: who appeared to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as God Almighty; but My name Adonai I did not make known to them.

6:4  LA: Pepigique fœdus cum eis, ut darem eis terram Chanaan, terram peregrinationis eorum, in qua fuerunt advenæ.
     EN: I made a covenant with them to give them the land of Canaan, the land of their sojourn, in which they lived as strangers.

6:5  LA: Ego audivi gemitum filiorum Israël, quo Ægyptii oppresserunt eos : et recordatus sum pacti mei.
     EN: I have heard the groaning of the children of Israel whom the Egyptians have oppressed, and I have remembered My covenant.

6:6  LA: Ideo dic filiis Israël : Ego Dominus qui educam vos de ergastulo Ægyptiorum, et eruam de servitute, ac redimam in brachio excelso et judiciis magnis.
     EN: Therefore tell the children of Israel: I am the Lord, who will bring you out of the Egyptians' prison of labor, rescue you from slavery, and redeem you with an uplifted arm and great judgments.

6:7  LA: Et assumam vos mihi in populum, et ero vester Deus : et scietis quod ego sum Dominus Deus vester qui eduxerim vos de ergastulo Ægyptiorum,
     EN: I will take you as My people and be your God. You will know that I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the Egyptians' prison of labor

6:8  LA: et induxerim in terram, super quam levavi manum meam ut darem eam Abraham, Isaac et Jacob : daboque illam vobis possidendam. Ego Dominus.
     EN: and brought you into the land concerning which I raised My hand to give it to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. I will give it to you as a possession. I am the Lord.”

6:9  LA: Narravit ergo Moyses omnia filiis Israël : qui non acquieverunt ei propter angustiam spiritus, et opus durissimum.
     EN: Moses told all this to the children of Israel, but they did not heed him because of their anguish of spirit and exceedingly hard labor.

6:10  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

6:11  LA: Ingredere, et loquere ad Pharaonem regem Ægypti, ut dimittat filios Israël de terra sua.
     EN: “Go in and tell Pharaoh, king of Egypt, to release the children of Israel from his land.”

6:12  LA: Respondit Moyses coram Domino : Ecce filii Israël non audiunt me : et quomodo audiet Pharao, præsertim cum incircumcisus sim labiis ?
     EN: Moses answered before the Lord, “Look, the children of Israel do not listen to me. How will Pharaoh listen, especially since my lips are uncircumcised?”

6:13  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen et Aaron, et dedit mandatum ad filios Israël, et ad Pharaonem regem Ægypti ut educerent filios Israël de terra Ægypti.
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron and gave them a command for the children of Israel and for Pharaoh, king of Egypt, to lead the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt.

6:14  LA: Isti sunt principes domorum per familias suas. Filii Ruben primogeniti Israëlis : Henoch et Phallu, Hesron et Charmi :
     EN: These are the heads of the houses according to their families. The sons of Reuben, Israel's firstborn: Hanoch and Pallu, Hezron and Carmi.

6:15  LA: hæ cognationes Ruben. Filii Simeon : Jamuel, et Jamin, et Ahod, et Jachin, et Soar, et Saul filius Chananitidis : hæ progenies Simeon.
     EN: These are the clans of Reuben. The sons of Simeon: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, the son of a Canaanite woman. These are the descendants of Simeon.

6:16  LA: Et hæc nomina filiorum Levi per cognationes suas : Gerson, et Caath, et Merari. Anni autem vitæ Levi fuerunt centum triginta septem.
     EN: These are the names of Levi's sons according to their clans: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. The years of Levi's life were one hundred thirty-seven.

6:17  LA: Filii Gerson : Lobni et Semei, per cognationes suas.
     EN: The sons of Gershon: Libni and Shimei, according to their clans.

6:18  LA: Filii Caath : Amram, et Isaar, et Hebron, et Oziel ; anni quoque vitæ Caath, centum triginta tres.
     EN: The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. The years of Kohath's life were one hundred thirty-three.

6:19  LA: Filii Merari : Moholi et Musi : hæ cognationes Levi per familias suas.
     EN: The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These are Levi's clans according to their families.

6:20  LA: Accepit autem Amram uxorem Jochabed patruelem suam : quæ peperit ei Aaron et Moysen. Fueruntque anni vitæ Amram, centum triginta septem.
     EN: Amram took as wife Jochebed, his father's brother's daughter. She bore him Aaron and Moses. The years of Amram's life were one hundred thirty-seven.

6:21  LA: Filii quoque Isaar : Core, et Nepheg, et Zechri.
     EN: The sons of Izhar: Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri.

6:22  LA: Filii quoque Oziel : Misaël, et Elisaphan, et Sethri.
     EN: The sons of Uzziel: Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri.

6:23  LA: Accepit autem Aaron uxorem Elisabeth filiam Aminadab, sororem Nahason, quæ peperit ei Nadab, et Abiu, et Eleazar, et Ithamar.
     EN: Aaron took as wife Elisheba, daughter of Amminadab and sister of Nahshon. She bore him Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

6:24  LA: Filii quoque Core : Aser, et Elcana, et Abiasaph : hæ sunt cognationes Coritarum.
     EN: The sons of Korah: Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph. These are the clans of the Korahites.

6:25  LA: At vero Eleazar filius Aaron accepit uxorem de filiabus Phutiel : quæ peperit ei Phinees. Hi sunt principes familiarum Leviticarum per cognationes suas.
     EN: Eleazar, Aaron's son, took a wife from the daughters of Putiel. She bore him Phinehas. These are the heads of the Levite families according to their clans.

6:26  LA: Iste est Aaron et Moyses, quibus præcepit Dominus ut educerent filios Israël de terra Ægypti per turmas suas.
     EN: These are the Aaron and Moses whom the Lord commanded to lead the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt by their companies.

6:27  LA: Hi sunt, qui loquuntur ad Pharaonem regem Ægypti, ut educant filios Israël de Ægypto : iste est Moyses et Aaron,
     EN: These are the ones who spoke to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, to lead the children of Israel out of Egypt: this Moses and Aaron,

6:28  LA: in die qua locutus est Dominus ad Moysen, in terra Ægypti.
     EN: on the day when the Lord spoke to Moses in the land of Egypt.

6:29  LA: Et locutus est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens : Ego Dominus : loquere ad Pharaonem regem Ægypti, omnia quæ ego loquor tibi.
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying, “I am the Lord. Tell Pharaoh, king of Egypt, everything I tell you.”

6:30  LA: Et ait Moyses coram Domino : En incircumcisus labiis sum, quomodo audiet me Pharao ?
     EN: Moses said before the Lord, “Look, my lips are uncircumcised. How will Pharaoh listen to me?”

EXODUS 7

7:1  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad Moysen : Ecce constitui te Deum Pharaonis : et Aaron frater tuus erit propheta tuus.
     EN: The Lord said to Moses, “Look, I have appointed you as God to Pharaoh, and your brother Aaron will be your prophet.

7:2  LA: Tu loqueris ei omnia quæ mando tibi : et ille loquetur ad Pharaonem, ut dimittat filios Israël de terra sua.
     EN: You shall tell him everything I command you, and he shall tell Pharaoh to release the children of Israel from his land.

7:3  LA: Sed ego indurabo cor ejus, et multiplicabo signa et ostenta mea in terra Ægypti,
     EN: But I will harden his heart and multiply My signs and wonders in the land of Egypt,

7:4  LA: et non audiet vos : immittamque manum meam super Ægyptum, et educam exercitum et populum meum filios Israël de terra Ægypti per judicia maxima.
     EN: and he will not listen to you. I will lay My hand upon Egypt and lead My army and My people, the children of Israel, out of the land of Egypt through very great judgments.

7:5  LA: Et scient Ægyptii quia ego sum Dominus qui extenderim manum meam super Ægyptum, et eduxerim filios Israël de medio eorum.
     EN: The Egyptians will know that I am the Lord, who stretched out My hand upon Egypt and led the children of Israel out from among them.”

7:6  LA: Fecit itaque Moyses et Aaron sicut præceperat Dominus : ita egerunt.
     EN: Moses and Aaron did as the Lord commanded; so they acted.

7:7  LA: Erat autem Moyses octoginta annorum, et Aaron octoginta trium, quando locuti sunt ad Pharaonem.
     EN: Moses was eighty years old and Aaron eighty-three when they spoke to Pharaoh.

7:8  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad Moysen et Aaron :
     EN: The Lord said to Moses and Aaron,

7:9  LA: Cum dixerit vobis Pharao, Ostendite signa : dices ad Aaron : Tolle virgam tuam, et projice eam coram Pharaone, ac vertetur in colubrum.
     EN: “When Pharaoh tells you, ‘Show signs,’ say to Aaron: ‘Take your staff and throw it before Pharaoh, and it will turn into a snake.’”

7:10  LA: Ingressi itaque Moyses et Aaron ad Pharaonem, fecerunt sicut præceperat Dominus : tulitque Aaron virgam coram Pharaone et servis ejus, quæ versa est in colubrum.
     EN: Moses and Aaron went in to Pharaoh and did as the Lord commanded. Aaron took his staff before Pharaoh and his servants, and it turned into a snake.

7:11  LA: Vocavit autem Pharao sapientes et maleficos : et fecerunt etiam ipsi per incantationes ægyptiacas et arcana quædam similiter.
     EN: Pharaoh called the wise men and sorcerers, and they too did the same through Egyptian incantations and certain secret arts.

7:12  LA: Projeceruntque singuli virgas suas, quæ versæ sunt in dracones : sed devoravit virga Aaron virgas eorum.
     EN: Each threw down his staff, and they turned into serpents, but Aaron's staff swallowed their staffs.

7:13  LA: Induratumque est cor Pharaonis, et non audivit eos, sicut præceperat Dominus.
     EN: Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he did not listen to them, as the Lord had directed.

7:14  LA: Dixit autem Dominus ad Moysen : Ingravatum est cor Pharaonis : non vult dimittere populum.
     EN: The Lord said to Moses, “Pharaoh's heart has grown heavy; he refuses to release the people.

7:15  LA: Vade ad eum mane, ecce egredietur ad aquas : et stabis in occursum ejus super ripam fluminis : et virgam quæ conversa est in draconem, tolles in manu tua.
     EN: Go to him in the morning. He will be going out to the water. Stand to meet him on the riverbank, and take in your hand the staff that turned into a serpent.

7:16  LA: Dicesque ad eum : Dominus Deus Hebræorum misit me ad te, dicens : Dimitte populum meum ut sacrificet mihi in deserto : et usque ad præsens audire noluisti.
     EN: Say to him: ‘The Lord, the God of the Hebrews, sent me to you, saying: Release My people, so that they may sacrifice to Me in the wilderness. But until now you have refused to listen.

7:17  LA: Hæc igitur dicit Dominus : In hoc scies quod sim Dominus : ecce percutiam virga, quæ in manu mea est, aquam fluminis, et vertetur in sanguinem.
     EN: Therefore thus says the Lord: By this you will know that I am the Lord. Look, with the staff in My hand I will strike the river's water, and it will turn into blood.

7:18  LA: Pisces quoque, qui sunt in fluvio, morientur, et computrescent aquæ, et affligentur Ægyptii bibentes aquam fluminis.
     EN: The fish in the river will die, the waters will become foul, and the Egyptians will suffer when drinking the river's water.’”

7:19  LA: Dixit quoque Dominus ad Moysen : Dic ad Aaron : Tolle virgam tuam, et extende manum tuam super aquas Ægypti, et super fluvios eorum, et rivos ac paludes, et omnes lacus aquarum, ut vertantur in sanguinem : et sit cruor in omni terra Ægypti, tam in ligneis vasis quam in saxeis.
     EN: The Lord also said to Moses, “Tell Aaron: ‘Take your staff and stretch out your hand over the waters of Egypt, over their rivers, streams, marshes, and all their pools of water, so that they turn into blood. Let there be blood throughout the land of Egypt, in wooden vessels as well as stone ones.’”

7:20  LA: Feceruntque Moyses et Aaron sicut præceperat Dominus : et elevans virgam percussit aquam fluminis coram Pharaone et servis ejus : quæ versa est in sanguinem.
     EN: Moses and Aaron did as the Lord commanded. Raising the staff, he struck the river's water before Pharaoh and his servants, and it turned into blood.

7:21  LA: Et pisces, qui erant in flumine, mortui sunt : computruitque fluvius, et non poterant Ægyptii bibere aquam fluminis, et fuit sanguis in tota terra Ægypti.
     EN: The fish in the river died, and the river became foul. The Egyptians could not drink the river's water, and blood was throughout the land of Egypt.

7:22  LA: Feceruntque similiter malefici Ægyptiorum incantationibus suis : et induratum est cor Pharaonis, nec audivit eos, sicut præceperat Dominus.
     EN: The Egyptian sorcerers did the same with their incantations. Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he did not listen to them, as the Lord had directed.

7:23  LA: Avertitque se, et ingressus est domum suam, nec apposuit cor etiam hac vice.
     EN: He turned away and went into his house, without taking even this to heart.

7:24  LA: Foderunt autem omnes Ægyptii per circuitum fluminis aquam ut biberent : non enim poterant bibere de aqua fluminis.
     EN: All the Egyptians dug around the river for drinking water, because they could not drink the river's water.

7:25  LA: Impletique sunt septem dies, postquam percussit Dominus fluvium.
     EN: Seven days passed after the Lord struck the river.

7:26 (Vg 8:1)  LA: Dixit quoque Dominus ad Moysen : Ingredere ad Pharaonem, et dices ad eum : Hæc dicit Dominus : Dimitte populum meum, ut sacrificet mihi :
     EN: The Lord also said to Moses, “Go in to Pharaoh and tell him: ‘Thus says the Lord: Release My people, so that they may sacrifice to Me.

7:27 (Vg 8:2)  LA: sin autem nolueris dimittere, ecce ego percutiam omnes terminos tuos ranis,
     EN: But if you refuse to release them, look, I will strike all your territory with frogs.

7:28 (Vg 8:3)  LA: et ebulliet fluvius ranas : quæ ascendent, et ingredientur domum tuam, et cubiculum lectuli tui, et super stratum tuum, et in domos servorum tuorum, et in populum tuum, et in furnos tuos, et in reliquias ciborum tuorum :
     EN: The river will swarm with frogs. They will come up and enter your house, your bedchamber, and your bed, your servants' houses and your people, your ovens and the remnants of your food.

7:29 (Vg 8:4)  LA: et ad te, et ad populum tuum, et ad omnes servos tuos intrabunt ranæ.
     EN: The frogs will come upon you, your people, and all your servants.’”

EXODUS 8

8:1 (Vg 8:5)  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad Moysen : Dic ad Aaron : Extende manum tuam super fluvios ac super rivos et paludes, et educ ranas super terram Ægypti.
     EN: The Lord said to Moses, “Tell Aaron: ‘Stretch out your hand over the rivers, streams, and marshes, and bring up frogs upon the land of Egypt.’”

8:2 (Vg 8:6)  LA: Et extendit Aaron manum super aquas Ægypti, et ascenderunt ranæ, operueruntque terram Ægypti.
     EN: Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt. The frogs came up and covered the land of Egypt.

8:3 (Vg 8:7)  LA: Fecerunt autem et malefici per incantationes suas similiter, eduxeruntque ranas super terram Ægypti.
     EN: The sorcerers did the same through their incantations and brought up frogs upon the land of Egypt.

8:4 (Vg 8:8)  LA: Vocavit autem Pharao Moysen et Aaron, et dixit eis : Orate Dominum ut auferat ranas a me et a populo meo, et dimittam populum ut sacrificet Domino.
     EN: Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron and said to them, “Pray to the Lord to remove the frogs from me and my people, and I will release the people to sacrifice to the Lord.”

8:5 (Vg 8:9)  LA: Dixitque Moyses ad Pharaonem : Constitue mihi quando deprecer pro te, et pro servis tuis, et pro populo tuo, ut abigantur ranæ a te, et a domo tua, et a servis tuis, et a populo tuo : et tantum in flumine remaneant.
     EN: Moses said to Pharaoh, “Appoint for me when I should plead for you, your servants, and your people, so that the frogs may be driven away from you, your house, your servants, and your people, and remain only in the river.”

8:6 (Vg 8:10)  LA: Qui respondit : Cras. At ille : Juxta, inquit, verbum tuum faciam : ut scias quoniam non est sicut Dominus Deus noster.
     EN: He answered, “Tomorrow.” Moses said, “I will do according to your word, so that you may know that there is none like the Lord our God.

8:7 (Vg 8:11)  LA: Et recedent ranæ a te, et a domo tua, et a servis tuis, et a populo tuo : et tantum in flumine remanebunt.
     EN: The frogs will depart from you, your house, your servants, and your people, and remain only in the river.”

8:8 (Vg 8:12)  LA: Egressique sunt Moyses et Aaron a Pharaone : et clamavit Moyses ad Dominum pro sponsione ranarum quam condixerat Pharaoni.
     EN: Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh, and Moses cried to the Lord concerning the promise about the frogs that he had made to Pharaoh.

8:9 (Vg 8:13)  LA: Fecitque Dominus juxta verbum Moysi : et mortuæ sunt ranæ de domibus, et de villis, et de agris.
     EN: The Lord acted according to Moses' word. The frogs died in the houses, villages, and fields.

8:10 (Vg 8:14)  LA: Congregaveruntque eas in immensos aggeres, et computruit terra.
     EN: They gathered them into enormous heaps, and the land stank.

10:1  LA: Et dixit Dominus ad Moysen : Ingredere ad Pharaonem : ego enim induravi cor ejus, et servorum illius, ut faciam signa mea hæc in eo :
     EN: The Lord said to Moses, “Go in to Pharaoh, for I have hardened his heart and those of his servants, so that I may perform these signs of Mine in his midst,

     EN: and you may recount in the hearing of your son and your grandchildren how often I crushed the Egyptians and performed My signs among them, and you may know that I am the Lord.”

10:3  LA: Introierunt ergo Moyses et Aaron ad Pharaonem, et dixerunt ei : Hæc dicit Dominus Deus Hebræorum : Usquequo non vis subjici mihi ? dimitte populum meum, ut sacrificet mihi.
     EN: Moses and Aaron went in to Pharaoh and said to him, “Thus says the Lord, the God of the Hebrews: How long will you refuse to submit to Me? Release My people, so that they may sacrifice to Me.

10:4  LA: Sin autem resistis, et non vis dimittere eum : ecce ego inducam cras locustam in fines tuos :
     EN: But if you resist and refuse to release them, look, tomorrow I will bring locusts into your territory.

     EN: They brought Moses and Aaron back to Pharaoh, who said to them, “Go, sacrifice to the Lord your God. Who are those who will go?”

     EN: He made a very strong west wind blow, which swept up the locusts and cast them into the Red Sea. Not even one remained in all the territory of Egypt.

     EN: The Lord hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he did not release the children of Israel.

     EN: The Lord said to Moses, “Stretch out your hand toward heaven, and let darkness be over the land of Egypt, so dense that it can be felt.”

     EN: Moses stretched out his hand toward heaven, and dreadful darkness covered all the land of Egypt for three days.

10:23  LA: Nemo vidit fratrem suum, nec movit se de loco in quo erat : ubicumque autem habitabant filii Israël, lux erat.
     EN: No one saw his brother or moved from the place where he was. But wherever the children of Israel lived, there was light.

     EN: Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron and said to them, “Go, sacrifice to the Lord. Only your sheep and herds shall remain; your little ones may go with you.”

     EN: But the Lord hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he refused to release them.

     EN: Pharaoh said to Moses, “Leave me, and take care never to see my face again. On whatever day you appear before me, you will die.”

10:29  LA: Respondit Moyses : Ita fiet ut locutus es : non videbo ultra faciem tuam.
     EN: Moses answered, “It shall be as you have said. I will not see your face again.”

     EN: But among all the children of Israel, not a dog will utter a sound, against either person or beast, so that you may know with what a wonder the Lord distinguishes between the Egyptians and Israel.

11:8  LA: Descendentque omnes servi tui isti ad me, et adorabunt me, dicentes : Egredere tu, et omnis populus qui subjectus est tibi : post hæc egrediemur.
     EN: All these servants of yours will come down to me and bow before me, saying, ‘Go out, you and all the people subject to you.’ After this we will depart.”

11:9  LA: Et exivit a Pharaone iratus nimis. Dixit autem Dominus ad Moysen : Non audiet vos Pharao ut multa signa fiant in terra Ægypti.
     EN: He went out from Pharaoh exceedingly angry. The Lord said to Moses, “Pharaoh will not listen to you, so that many signs may be done in the land of Egypt.”

11:10  LA: Moyses autem et Aaron fecerunt omnia ostenta, quæ scripta sunt, coram Pharaone. Et induravit Dominus cor Pharaonis, nec dimisit filios Israël de terra sua.
     EN: Moses and Aaron performed before Pharaoh all the wonders that are written. The Lord hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he did not release the children of Israel from his land.

EXODUS 12

12:1  LA: Dixit quoque Dominus ad Moysen et Aaron in terra Ægypti :
     EN: The Lord said to Moses and Aaron in the land of Egypt,

12:2  LA: Mensis iste, vobis principium mensium : primus erit in mensibus anni.
     EN: “This month shall be the beginning of months for you; it shall be the first among the months of the year.

12:3  LA: Loquimini ad universum cœtum filiorum Israël, et dicite eis : Decima die mensis hujus tollat unusquisque agnum per familias et domos suas.
     EN: Speak to the entire assembly of the children of Israel and tell them: On the tenth day of this month, each shall take a lamb according to their families and households.

12:4  LA: Sin autem minor est numerus ut sufficere possit ad vescendum agnum, assumet vicinum suum qui junctus est domui suæ, juxta numerum animarum quæ sufficere possunt ad esum agni.
     EN: If the number is too small to eat a lamb, he shall take his next-door neighbor along with him, according to the number of persons sufficient to eat the lamb.

12:5  LA: Erit autem agnus absque macula, masculus, anniculus : juxta quem ritum tolletis et hædum.
     EN: The lamb shall be without blemish, a male one year old. Under the same rule you may also take a young goat.

12:6  LA: Et servabitis eum usque ad quartamdecimam diem mensis hujus : immolabitque eum universa multitudo filiorum Israël ad vesperam.
     EN: You shall keep it until the fourteenth day of this month, and the whole multitude of the children of Israel shall sacrifice it in the evening.

12:7  LA: Et sument de sanguine ejus, ac ponent super utrumque postem, et in superliminaribus domorum, in quibus comedent illum.
     EN: They shall take some of its blood and put it on both doorposts and on the lintels of the houses where they eat it.

12:8  LA: Et edent carnes nocte illa assas igni, et azymos panes cum lactucis agrestibus.
     EN: That night they shall eat the flesh roasted over fire, and unleavened bread with wild lettuces.

12:9  LA: Non comedetis ex eo crudum quid, nec coctum aqua, sed tantum assum igni : caput cum pedibus ejus et intestinis vorabitis.
     EN: You shall not eat any of it raw or boiled in water, but only roasted over fire. You shall eat its head with its feet and entrails.

12:10  LA: Nec remanebit quidquam ex eo usque mane ; si quid residuum fuerit, igne comburetis.
     EN: None of it shall remain until morning; whatever is left, you shall burn with fire.

12:11  LA: Sic autem comedetis illum : renes vestros accingetis, et calceamenta habebitis in pedibus, tenentes baculos in manibus, et comedetis festinanter : est enim Phase (id est, transitus) Domini.
     EN: This is how you shall eat it: with your loins girded, sandals on your feet, and staffs held in your hands. You shall eat hurriedly, for it is the Lord's Passover—that is, the passing over.

12:12  LA: Et transibo per terram Ægypti nocte illa, percutiamque omne primogenitum in terra Ægypti ab homine usque ad pecus : et in cunctis diis Ægypti faciam judicia. Ego Dominus.
     EN: I will pass through the land of Egypt that night and strike every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from person to beast. Upon all the gods of Egypt I will execute judgments. I am the Lord.

12:13  LA: Erit autem sanguis vobis in signum in ædibus in quibus eritis : et videbo sanguinem, et transibo vos : nec erit in vobis plaga disperdens quando percussero terram Ægypti.
     EN: The blood shall be a sign for you on the houses where you are. I will see the blood and pass over you, and no destroying plague will be among you when I strike the land of Egypt.

12:14  LA: Habebitis autem hunc diem in monimentum : et celebrabitis eam solemnem Domino in generationibus vestris cultu sempiterno.
     EN: You shall keep this day as a memorial and celebrate it as a festival to the Lord throughout your generations, with an everlasting observance.

12:15  LA: Septem diebus azyma comedetis : in die primo non erit fermentum in domibus vestris : quicumque comederit fermentatum, peribit anima illa de Israël, a primo die usque ad diem septimum.
     EN: For seven days you shall eat unleavened bread. On the first day there shall be no leaven in your houses. Whoever eats anything leavened from the first day through the seventh, that person shall perish from Israel.

12:16  LA: Dies prima erit sancta atque solemnis, et dies septima eadem festivitate venerabilis : nihil operis facietis in eis, exceptis his, quæ ad vescendum pertinent.
     EN: The first day shall be holy and solemn, and the seventh day honored with the same festivity. You shall do no work on them except what pertains to eating.

12:17  LA: Et observabitis azyma : in eadem enim ipsa die educam exercitum vestrum de terra Ægypti, et custodietis diem istum in generationes vestras ritu perpetuo.
     EN: You shall observe the unleavened bread, for on this very day I will lead your army out of the land of Egypt. You shall keep this day throughout your generations as a perpetual rite.

12:18  LA: Primo mense, quartadecima die mensis ad vesperam, comedetis azyma usque ad diem vigesimam primam ejusdem mensis ad vesperam.
     EN: In the first month, from the evening of the fourteenth day of the month until the evening of the twenty-first day of the same month, you shall eat unleavened bread.

12:19  LA: Septem diebus fermentum non invenietur in domibus vestris : qui comederit fermentatum, peribit anima ejus de cœtu Israël, tam de advenis quam de indigenis terræ.
     EN: For seven days no leaven shall be found in your houses. Whoever eats anything leavened, that person shall perish from the assembly of Israel, whether a stranger or a native of the land.

12:20  LA: Omne fermentatum non comedetis : in cunctis habitaculis vestris edetis azyma.
     EN: You shall eat nothing leavened; in all your dwellings you shall eat unleavened bread.”

12:21  LA: Vocavit autem Moyses omnes seniores filiorum Israël, et dixit ad eos : Ite tollentes animal per familias vestras, et immolate Phase.
     EN: Moses called all the elders of the children of Israel and said to them, “Go, take an animal for each of your families, and sacrifice the Passover.

12:22  LA: Fasciculumque hyssopi tingite in sanguine qui est in limine, et aspergite ex eo superliminare, et utrumque postem : nullus vestrum egrediatur ostium domus suæ usque mane.
     EN: Dip a bundle of hyssop in the blood at the threshold and sprinkle some of it on the lintel and both doorposts. None of you shall go out the door of his house until morning.

12:23  LA: Transibit enim Dominus percutiens Ægyptios : cumque viderit sanguinem in superliminari, et in utroque poste, transcendet ostium domus, et non sinet percussorem ingredi domos vestras et lædere.
     EN: For the Lord will pass through, striking the Egyptians. When He sees the blood on the lintel and both doorposts, He will pass over the doorway of the house and will not allow the destroyer to enter your houses and harm you.

12:24  LA: Custodi verbum istud legitimum tibi et filiis tuis usque in æternum.
     EN: Keep this word as a statute for yourself and your children forever.

12:25  LA: Cumque introieritis terram, quam Dominus daturus est vobis ut pollicitus est, observabitis cæremonias istas.
     EN: When you enter the land the Lord will give you, as He promised, you shall observe these ceremonies.

12:26  LA: Et cum dixerint vobis filii vestri : Quæ est ista religio ?
     EN: When your children ask you, ‘What is this religious observance?’

12:27  LA: Dicetis eis : Victima transitus Domini est, quando transivit super domos filiorum Israël in Ægypto, percutiens Ægyptios, et domos nostras liberans. Incurvatusque populus adoravit.
     EN: you shall tell them, ‘It is the sacrifice of the Lord's passing over, when He passed over the houses of the children of Israel in Egypt, striking the Egyptians and delivering our houses.’” The people bowed down and worshiped.

12:28  LA: Et egressi filii Israël fecerunt sicut præceperat Dominus Moysi et Aaron.
     EN: The children of Israel went and did as the Lord had commanded Moses and Aaron.

12:29  LA: Factum est autem in noctis medio, percussit Dominus omne primogenitum in terra Ægypti, a primogenito Pharaonis, qui in solio ejus sedebat, usque ad primogenitum captivæ quæ erat in carcere, et omne primogenitum jumentorum.
     EN: At midnight the Lord struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from Pharaoh's firstborn, who sat on his throne, to the firstborn of the captive woman in prison, and every firstborn of the animals.

12:30  LA: Surrexitque Pharao nocte, et omnes servi ejus, cunctaque Ægyptus : et ortus est clamor magnus in Ægypto : neque enim erat domus in qua non jaceret mortuus.
     EN: Pharaoh rose in the night, as did all his servants and all Egypt. A great cry arose in Egypt, for there was no house in which someone did not lie dead.

12:31  LA: Vocatisque Pharao Moyse et Aaron nocte, ait : Surgite et egredimini a populo meo, vos et filii Israël : ite, immolate Domino sicut dicitis.
     EN: Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron in the night and said, “Rise and go out from among my people, you and the children of Israel. Go, sacrifice to the Lord, as you say.

12:32  LA: Oves vestras et armenta assumite ut petieratis, et abeuntes benedicite mihi.
     EN: Take your sheep and herds, as you requested, and go; bless me also.”

12:33  LA: Urgebantque Ægyptii populum de terra exire velociter, dicentes : Omnes moriemur.
     EN: The Egyptians urged the people to leave the land quickly, saying, “We will all die.”

12:34  LA: Tulit igitur populus conspersam farinam antequam fermentaretur : et ligans in palliis, posuit super humeros suos.
     EN: So the people took their kneaded dough before it was leavened, bound it in cloaks, and placed it on their shoulders.

12:35  LA: Feceruntque filii Israël sicut præceperat Moyses : et petierunt ab Ægyptiis vasa argentea et aurea, vestemque plurimam.
     EN: The children of Israel did as Moses commanded. They asked the Egyptians for articles of silver and gold and much clothing.

12:36  LA: Dominus autem dedit gratiam populo coram Ægyptiis ut commodarent eis : et spoliaverunt Ægyptios.
     EN: The Lord gave the people favor before the Egyptians, so that they lent to them, and they despoiled the Egyptians.

12:37  LA: Profectique sunt filii Israël de Ramesse in Socoth, sexcenta fere millia peditum virorum, absque parvulis.
     EN: The children of Israel set out from Rameses for Succoth, about six hundred thousand men on foot, besides little ones.

12:38  LA: Sed et vulgus promiscuum innumerabile ascendit cum eis, oves et armenta et animantia diversi generis multa nimis.
     EN: An innumerable mixed multitude also went up with them, along with sheep, herds, and exceedingly many animals of different kinds.

12:39  LA: Coxeruntque farinam, quam dudum de Ægypto conspersam tulerant : et fecerunt subcinericios panes azymos : neque enim poterant fermentari, cogentibus exire Ægyptiis, et nullam facere sinentibus moram : nec pulmenti quidquam occurrerat præparare.
     EN: They baked the dough they had brought kneaded from Egypt and made unleavened cakes baked under the ashes. It could not be leavened, because the Egyptians compelled them to leave and allowed no delay; nor had they had time to prepare any cooked food.

12:40  LA: Habitatio autem filiorum Israël qua manserunt in Ægypto, fuit quadringentorum triginta annorum.
     EN: The stay of the children of Israel during which they remained in Egypt was four hundred thirty years.

12:41  LA: Quibus expletis, eadem die egressus est omnis exercitus Domini de terra Ægypti.
     EN: When these were completed, on that very day all the Lord's army went out from the land of Egypt.

12:42  LA: Nox ista est observabilis Domini, quando eduxit eos de terra Ægypti : hanc observare debent omnes filii Israël in generationibus suis.
     EN: This is a night to be observed for the Lord, when He led them out of the land of Egypt. All the children of Israel must observe it throughout their generations.

12:43  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad Moysen et Aaron : Hæc est religio Phase : omnis alienigena non comedet ex eo.
     EN: The Lord said to Moses and Aaron, “This is the observance of the Passover: no foreigner shall eat of it.

12:44  LA: Omnis autem servus emptitius circumcidetur, et sic comedet.
     EN: Every purchased slave shall be circumcised, and then he may eat.

12:45  LA: Advena et mercenarius non edent ex eo.
     EN: A stranger and a hired worker shall not eat of it.

12:46  LA: In una domo comedetur, nec efferetis de carnibus ejus foras, nec os illius confringetis.
     EN: It shall be eaten in one house. You shall not carry any of its flesh outside, nor break a bone of it.

12:47  LA: Omnis cœtus filiorum Israël faciet illud.
     EN: The entire assembly of the children of Israel shall keep it.

12:48  LA: Quod si quis peregrinorum in vestram voluerit transire coloniam, et facere Phase Domini, circumcidetur prius omne masculinum ejus, et tunc rite celebrabit : eritque sicut indigena terræ : si quis autem circumcisus non fuerit, non vescetur ex eo.
     EN: If any foreigner wishes to join your settlement and celebrate the Lord's Passover, every male of his household shall first be circumcised; then he may duly celebrate it and shall be like a native of the land. But anyone uncircumcised shall not eat of it.

12:49  LA: Eadem lex erit indigenæ et colono qui peregrinatur apud vos.
     EN: The same law shall apply to the native and the settler who sojourns among you.”

12:50  LA: Feceruntque omnes filii Israël sicut præceperat Dominus Moysi et Aaron.
     EN: All the children of Israel did as the Lord commanded Moses and Aaron.

12:51  LA: Et eadem die eduxit Dominus filios Israël de terra Ægypti per turmas suas.
     EN: On that very day the Lord led the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt by their companies.

EXODUS 13

13:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

13:2  LA: Sanctifica mihi omne primogenitum quod aperit vulvam in filiis Israël, tam de hominibus quam de jumentis : mea sunt enim omnia.
     EN: “Consecrate to Me every firstborn that opens the womb among the children of Israel, both of people and of animals, for they are all Mine.”

13:3  LA: Et ait Moyses ad populum : Mementote diei hujus in qua egressi estis de Ægypto et de domo servitutis, quoniam in manu forti eduxit vos Dominus de loco isto : ut non comedatis fermentatum panem.
     EN: Moses said to the people, “Remember this day on which you came out of Egypt and the house of slavery, because with a strong hand the Lord led you out of this place. You shall eat no leavened bread.

13:4  LA: Hodie egredimini mense novarum frugum.
     EN: Today you depart, in the month of new crops.

13:5  LA: Cumque introduxerit te Dominus in terram Chananæi, et Hethæi, et Amorrhæi, et Hevæi, et Jebusæi, quam juravit patribus tuis ut daret tibi, terram fluentem lacte et melle, celebrabis hunc morem sacrorum mense isto.
     EN: When the Lord brings you into the land of the Canaanite, Hittite, Amorite, Hivite, and Jebusite, which He swore to your fathers to give you, a land flowing with milk and honey, you shall celebrate this sacred observance in this month.

13:6  LA: Septem diebus vesceris azymis : et in die septimo erit solemnitas Domini.
     EN: For seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day there shall be a festival to the Lord.

13:7  LA: Azyma comedetis septem diebus : non apparebit apud te aliquid fermentatum, nec in cunctis finibus tuis.
     EN: You shall eat unleavened bread for seven days. Nothing leavened shall appear among you or throughout all your territory.

13:8  LA: Narrabisque filio tuo in die illo, dicens : Hoc est quod fecit mihi Dominus quando egressus sum de Ægypto.
     EN: On that day you shall tell your son, ‘This is what the Lord did for me when I came out of Egypt.’

13:9  LA: Et erit quasi signum in manu tua, et quasi monimentum ante oculos tuos : et ut lex Domini semper sit in ore tuo, in manu enim forti eduxit te Dominus de Ægypto.
     EN: It shall be like a sign on your hand and a memorial before your eyes, so that the Lord's law may always be in your mouth; for with a strong hand the Lord brought you out of Egypt.

13:10  LA: Custodies hujuscemodi cultum statuto tempore a diebus in dies.
     EN: You shall keep this observance at its appointed time, year after year.

13:11  LA: Cumque introduxerit te Dominus in terram Chananæi, sicut juravit tibi et patribus tuis, et dederit tibi eam :
     EN: When the Lord brings you into the Canaanite's land, as He swore to you and your fathers, and gives it to you,

13:12  LA: separabis omne quod aperit vulvam Domino, et quod primitivum est in pecoribus tuis : quidquid habueris masculini sexus, consecrabis Domino.
     EN: you shall set apart for the Lord everything that opens the womb and every firstborn of your livestock. Whatever male offspring you have, you shall consecrate to the Lord.

13:13  LA: Primogenitum asini mutabis ove : quod si non redemeris, interficies. Omne autem primogenitum hominis de filiis tuis, pretio redimes.
     EN: You shall exchange the firstborn of a donkey for a sheep; if you do not redeem it, you shall kill it. But every human firstborn among your sons you shall redeem for a price.

13:14  LA: Cumque interrogaverit te filius tuus cras, dicens : Quid est hoc ? respondebis ei : In manu forti eduxit nos Dominus de terra Ægypti, de domo servitutis.
     EN: When your son asks you in time to come, ‘What is this?’ you shall answer him, ‘With a strong hand the Lord led us out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.

13:15  LA: Nam cum induratus esset Pharao, et nollet nos dimittere, occidit Dominus omne primogenitum in terra Ægypti, a primogenito hominis usque ad primogenitum jumentorum : idcirco immolo Domino omne quod aperit vulvam masculini sexus, et omnia primogenita filiorum meorum redimo.
     EN: For when Pharaoh was hardened and refused to release us, the Lord killed every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of people to the firstborn of animals. Therefore I sacrifice to the Lord every male that opens the womb, and I redeem all the firstborn of my sons.’

13:16  LA: Erit igitur quasi signum in manu tua, et quasi appensum quid, ob recordationem, inter oculos tuos : eo quod in manu forti eduxit nos Dominus de Ægypto.
     EN: It shall therefore be like a sign on your hand and something hung between your eyes as a reminder, because with a strong hand the Lord led us out of Egypt.”

13:17  LA: Igitur cum emisisset Pharao populum, non eos duxit Deus per viam terræ Philisthiim quæ vicina est : reputans ne forte pœniteret eum, si vidisset adversum se bella consurgere, et reverteretur in Ægyptum.
     EN: When Pharaoh had released the people, God did not lead them by the road through the land of the Philistines, although it was near. He considered that the people might regret it if they saw wars rising against them and return to Egypt.

13:18  LA: Sed circumduxit per viam deserti, quæ est juxta mare Rubrum : et armati ascenderunt filii Israël de terra Ægypti.
     EN: Instead He led them around by the wilderness road beside the Red Sea. The children of Israel went up armed from the land of Egypt.

13:19  LA: Tulit quoque Moyses ossa Joseph secum : eo quod adjurasset filios Israël, dicens : Visitabit vos Deus ; efferte ossa mea hinc vobiscum.
     EN: Moses also took Joseph's bones with him, because Joseph had bound the children of Israel by an oath, saying, “God will visit you; carry my bones away from here with you.”

13:20  LA: Profectique de Socoth castrametati sunt in Etham, in extremis finibus solitudinis.
     EN: Setting out from Succoth, they camped at Etham, at the far edge of the wilderness.

13:21  LA: Dominus autem præcedebat eos ad ostendendam viam per diem in columna nubis, et per noctem in columna ignis : ut dux esset itineris utroque tempore.
     EN: The Lord went before them to show the way, by day in a pillar of cloud and by night in a pillar of fire, to guide their journey at both times.

13:22  LA: Numquam defuit columna nubis per diem, nec columna ignis per noctem, coram populo.
     EN: The pillar of cloud by day and the pillar of fire by night never failed before the people.

EXODUS 14

14:1  LA: Locutus est autem Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

14:2  LA: Loquere filiis Israël : Reversi castrametentur e regione Phihahiroth, quæ est inter Magdalum et mare contra Beelsephon : in conspectu ejus castra ponetis super mare.
     EN: “Tell the children of Israel to turn back and camp opposite Pi-hahiroth, which is between Migdol and the sea, facing Baal-zephon. You shall camp opposite it beside the sea.

14:3  LA: Dicturusque est Pharao super filiis Israël : Coarctati sunt in terra ; conclusit eos desertum.
     EN: Pharaoh will say of the children of Israel, ‘They are hemmed in by the land; the wilderness has shut them in.’

14:4  LA: Et indurabo cor ejus, ac persequetur vos : et glorificabor in Pharaone, et in omni exercitu ejus ; scientque Ægyptii quia ego sum Dominus. Feceruntque ita.
     EN: I will harden his heart, and he will pursue you. I will be glorified in Pharaoh and all his army, and the Egyptians will know that I am the Lord.” They did so.

14:5  LA: Et nuntiatum est regi Ægyptiorum quod fugisset populus : immutatumque est cor Pharaonis et servorum ejus super populo, et dixerunt : Quid voluimus facere ut dimitteremus Israël, ne serviret nobis ?
     EN: The king of the Egyptians was told that the people had fled. Pharaoh's heart and those of his servants changed toward the people, and they said, “What have we chosen to do, releasing Israel from serving us?”

14:6  LA: Junxit ergo currum, et omnem populum suum assumpsit secum.
     EN: He harnessed his chariot and took all his people with him.

14:7  LA: Tulitque sexcentos currus electos, et quidquid in Ægypto curruum fuit : et duces totius exercitus.
     EN: He took six hundred chosen chariots and every other chariot in Egypt, with the commanders of the whole army.

14:8  LA: Induravitque Dominus cor Pharaonis regis Ægypti, et persecutus est filios Israël : at illi egressi sunt in manu excelsa.
     EN: The Lord hardened the heart of Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and he pursued the children of Israel. But they had gone out with an uplifted hand.

14:9  LA: Cumque persequerentur Ægyptii vestigia præcedentium, repererunt eos in castris super mare : omnis equitatus et currus Pharaonis, et universus exercitus, erant in Phihahiroth contra Beelsephon.
     EN: The Egyptians followed the tracks of those who had gone ahead and found them camped beside the sea. All Pharaoh's cavalry and chariots and the whole army were at Pi-hahiroth, facing Baal-zephon.

14:10  LA: Cumque appropinquasset Pharao, levantes filii Israël oculos, viderunt Ægyptios post se, et timuerunt valde : clamaveruntque ad Dominum,
     EN: As Pharaoh approached, the children of Israel lifted their eyes and saw the Egyptians behind them. They were greatly afraid and cried out to the Lord.

14:11  LA: et dixerunt ad Moysen : Forsitan non erant sepulchra in Ægypto, ideo tulisti nos ut moreremur in solitudine : quid hoc facere voluisti, ut educeres nos ex Ægypto ?
     EN: They said to Moses, “Were there perhaps no graves in Egypt, that you brought us to die in the wilderness? Why did you choose to do this, leading us out of Egypt?

14:12  LA: nonne iste est sermo, quem loquebamur ad te in Ægypto, dicentes : Recede a nobis, ut serviamus Ægyptiis ? multo enim melius erat servire eis, quam mori in solitudine.
     EN: Is this not what we told you in Egypt, saying, ‘Leave us alone to serve the Egyptians’? It was far better to serve them than to die in the wilderness.”

14:13  LA: Et ait Moyses ad populum : Nolite timere : state, et videte magnalia Domini quæ facturus est hodie : Ægyptios enim, quos nunc videtis, nequaquam ultra videbitis usque in sempiternum.
     EN: Moses said to the people, “Do not fear. Stand firm and see the mighty deeds the Lord will do today. For the Egyptians you see now, you will never see again, forever.

14:14  LA: Dominus pugnabit pro vobis, et vos tacebitis.
     EN: The Lord will fight for you, and you shall keep silent.”

14:15  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad Moysen : Quid clamas ad me ? loquere filiis Israël ut proficiscantur.
     EN: The Lord said to Moses, “Why do you cry to Me? Tell the children of Israel to set out.

14:16  LA: Tu autem eleva virgam tuam, et extende manum tuam super mare, et divide illud : ut gradiantur filii Israël in medio mari per siccum.
     EN: Lift your staff and stretch out your hand over the sea, dividing it so that the children of Israel may walk through the midst of the sea on dry ground.

14:17  LA: Ego autem indurabo cor Ægyptiorum ut persequantur vos : et glorificabor in Pharaone, et in omni exercitu ejus, et in curribus et in equitibus illius.

EXODUS 16

16:1  LA: Profectique sunt de Elim, et venit omnis multitudo filiorum Israël in desertum Sin, quod est inter Elim et Sinai, quintodecimo die mensis secundi, postquam egressi sunt de terra Ægypti.

     EN: The entire congregation of the children of Israel murmured against Moses and Aaron in the wilderness.

     EN: The Lord said to Moses, “Look, I will rain bread from heaven for you. Let the people go out and gather enough for each day, so that I may test whether they will walk in My law or not.

     EN: On the sixth day, let them prepare what they bring in, and let it be twice what they usually gather each day.”

     EN: Moses said, “The Lord will give you meat to eat in the evening and bread to the full in the morning, because He has heard the complaints you have murmured against Him. For what are we? Your murmuring is against the Lord, not against us.”

     EN: While Aaron was speaking to the whole assembly of the children of Israel, they looked toward the wilderness, and the Lord's glory appeared in the cloud.

     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

     EN: You shall not steal.

20:15 (Vg 20:13)  LA: Non occides.
     EN: You shall not kill.

20:16  LA: Non loqueris contra proximum tuum falsum testimonium.
     EN: You shall not speak false testimony against your neighbor.

EXODUS 21

21:1  LA: Hæc sunt judicia quæ propones eis.
     EN: “These are the judgments you shall set before them.

     EN: If you buy a Hebrew slave, he shall serve you for six years; in the seventh he shall go free without payment.

21:3  LA: Cum quali veste intraverit, cum tali exeat : si habens uxorem, et uxor egredietur simul.
     EN: With whatever clothing he entered, with that he shall leave. If he entered with a wife, his wife shall leave with him.

     EN: But if his master gave him a wife and she bore sons and daughters, the woman and her children shall belong to her master; he himself shall leave with his clothing.

21:5  LA: Quod si dixerit servus : Diligo dominum meum et uxorem ac liberos ; non egrediar liber :
     EN: If the slave says, ‘I love my master, my wife, and my children; I will not go free,’

     EN: his master shall bring him before the judges, and he shall be brought to the door and doorposts. His master shall pierce his ear with an awl, and he shall be his slave forever.

21:7  LA: Si quis vendiderit filiam suam in famulam, non egredietur sicut ancillæ exire consueverunt.
     EN: If someone sells his daughter as a servant, she shall not leave as female slaves customarily leave.

21:8  LA: Si displicuerit oculis domini sui cui tradita fuerat, dimittet eam : populo autem alieno vendendi non habebit potestatem, si spreverit eam.
     EN: If she displeases the master to whom she was handed over, he shall release her. He shall have no power to sell her to a foreign people if he has rejected her.

21:9  LA: Sin autem filio suo desponderit eam, juxta morem filiarum faciet illi.
     EN: If he betroths her to his son, he shall treat her according to the custom for daughters.

21:10  LA: Quod si alteram ei acceperit, providebit puellæ nuptias, et vestimenta, et pretium pudicitiæ non negabit.
     EN: If he takes another woman for him, he shall provide the young woman with marriage and clothing, and shall not deny the price of her chastity.

21:11  LA: Si tria ista non fecerit, egredietur gratis absque pecunia.
     EN: If he does not do these three things, she shall go free without payment of money.

21:12  LA: Qui percusserit hominem volens occidere, morte moriatur.
     EN: Whoever strikes a person intending to kill shall surely die.

21:13  LA: Qui autem non est insidiatus, sed Deus illum tradidit in manus ejus, constituam tibi locum in quem fugere debeat.
     EN: But if he did not lie in wait, and God delivered the person into his hands, I will appoint you a place to which he must flee.

21:14  LA: Si quis per industriam occiderit proximum suum, et per insidias : ab altari meo evelles eum, ut moriatur.
     EN: If someone kills his neighbor deliberately and by treachery, you shall tear him away even from My altar to die.

21:15  LA: Qui percusserit patrem suum aut matrem, morte moriatur.
     EN: Whoever strikes his father or mother shall surely die.

21:16 (Vg 21:17)  LA: Qui maledixerit patri suo, vel matri, morte moriatur.
     EN: Whoever curses his father or mother shall surely die.

21:17 (Vg 21:16)  LA: Qui furatus fuerit hominem, et vendiderit eum, convictus noxæ, morte moriatur.
     EN: Whoever steals a person and sells him, being convicted of the crime, shall surely die.

21:18  LA: Si rixati fuerint viri, et percusserit alter proximum suum lapide vel pugno, et ille mortuus non fuerit, sed jacuerit in lectulo :
     EN: If men quarrel and one strikes his neighbor with a stone or fist, and he does not die but takes to his bed,

     EN: if he rises and walks outside with his staff, the one who struck him shall be free of guilt, provided he compensates for his lost work and medical expenses.

21:20  LA: Qui percusserit servum suum, vel ancillam virga, et mortui fuerint in manibus ejus, criminis reus erit.
     EN: Whoever strikes his male or female slave with a rod, and they die under his hands, shall be guilty of a crime.

21:21  LA: Sin autem uno die vel duobus supervixerit, non subjacebit pœnæ, quia pecunia illius est.
     EN: But if the slave survives a day or two, he shall not be subject to punishment, because the slave is his money.

21:22  LA: Si rixati fuerint viri, et percusserit quis mulierem prægnantem, et abortivum quidem fecerit, sed ipsa vixerit : subjacebit damno quantum maritus mulieris expetierit, et arbitri judicaverint.
     EN: If men quarrel and someone strikes a pregnant woman and causes a miscarriage, but she herself lives, he shall be liable to a fine as much as the woman's husband demands and the arbitrators decide.

21:23  LA: Sin autem mors ejus fuerit subsecuta, reddet animam pro anima,
     EN: But if her death follows, he shall give life for life,

21:24  LA: oculum pro oculo, dentem pro dente, manum pro manu, pedem pro pede,
     EN: eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,

21:25  LA: adustionem pro adustione, vulnus pro vulnere, livorem pro livore.
     EN: burn for burn, wound for wound, bruise for bruise.

21:26  LA: Si percusserit quispiam oculum servi sui aut ancillæ, et luscos eos fecerit, dimittet eos liberos pro oculo quem eruit.
     EN: If someone strikes the eye of his male or female slave and makes them blind in one eye, he shall release them free for the eye he destroyed.

21:27  LA: Dentem quoque si excusserit servo vel ancillæ suæ, similiter dimittet eos liberos.
     EN: If he knocks out a tooth of his male or female slave, he shall likewise release them free.

21:28  LA: Si bos cornu percusserit virum aut mulierem, et mortui fuerint, lapidibus obruetur : et non comedentur carnes ejus, dominus quoque bovis innocens erit.
     EN: If an ox gores a man or woman to death, it shall be stoned, and its flesh shall not be eaten; the ox's owner shall be innocent.

21:29  LA: Quod si bos cornupeta fuerit ab heri et nudiustertius, et contestati sunt dominum ejus, nec recluserit eum, occideritque virum aut mulierem : et bos lapidibus obruetur, et dominum ejus occident.
     EN: But if the ox has been accustomed to gore for some time, and its owner has been warned but has not confined it, and it kills a man or woman, the ox shall be stoned and its owner put to death.

21:30  LA: Quod si pretium fuerit ei impositum, dabit pro anima sua quidquid fuerit postulatus.
     EN: If a payment is imposed on him, he shall give whatever is demanded for his life.

21:31  LA: Filium quoque et filiam si cornu percusserit, simili sententiæ subjacebit.
     EN: If it gores a son or daughter, it shall be subject to the same judgment.

21:32  LA: Si servum ancillamque invaserit, triginta siclos argenti domino dabit, bos vero lapidibus opprimetur.
     EN: If it attacks a male or female slave, the owner shall give thirty shekels of silver to their master, and the ox shall be stoned.

21:33  LA: Si quis aperuerit cisternam, et foderit, et non operuerit eam, cecideritque bos aut asinus in eam,
     EN: If someone opens or digs a cistern and does not cover it, and an ox or donkey falls into it,

21:34  LA: reddet dominus cisternæ pretium jumentorum : quod autem mortuum est, ipsius erit.
     EN: the cistern's owner shall repay the value of the animals, and the dead animal shall be his.

21:35  LA: Si bos alienus bovem alterius vulneraverit, et ille mortuus fuerit : vendent bovem vivum, et divident pretium, cadaver autem mortui inter se dispertient.
     EN: If one person's ox wounds another's ox and it dies, they shall sell the living ox and divide its price, and divide the dead animal between them.

21:36  LA: Sin autem sciebat quod bos cornupeta esset ab heri et nudiustertius, et non custodivit eum dominus suus : reddet bovem pro bove, et cadaver integrum accipiet.
     EN: But if it was known that the ox had been accustomed to gore for some time and its owner did not restrain it, he shall repay ox for ox and receive the whole carcass.

21:37 (Vg 22:1)  LA: Si quis furatus fuerit bovem aut ovem, et occiderit vel vendiderit : quinque boves pro uno bove restituet, et quatuor oves pro una ove.
     EN: If someone steals an ox or sheep and kills or sells it, he shall restore five oxen for one ox and four sheep for one sheep.

EXODUS 22

22:1 (Vg 22:2)  LA: Si effringens fur domum sive suffodiens fuerit inventus, et accepto vulnere mortuus fuerit, percussor non erit reus sanguinis.
     EN: If a thief is found breaking into a house or digging through it, and dies from a blow, the one who struck him shall not be guilty of bloodshed.

22:2 (Vg 22:3)  LA: Quod si orto sole hoc fecerit, homicidium perpetravit, et ipse morietur. Si non habuerit quod pro furto reddat, ipse venundabitur.
     EN: But if he does this after sunrise, he has committed homicide and shall himself die. If the thief has nothing with which to make restitution for the theft, he shall be sold.

22:3 (Vg 22:4)  LA: Si inventum fuerit apud eum quod furatus est, vivens : sive bos, sive asinus, sive ovis, duplum restituet.
     EN: If what he stole is found alive with him, whether ox, donkey, or sheep, he shall restore double.

22:4 (Vg 22:5)  LA: Si læserit quispiam agrum vel vineam, et dimiserit jumentum suum ut depascatur aliena : quidquid optimum habuerit in agro suo, vel in vinea, pro damni æstimatione restituet.
     EN: If someone damages a field or vineyard by letting his animal graze on another's property, he shall make restitution from the best of his own field or vineyard, according to the assessed damage.

22:5 (Vg 22:6)  LA: Si egressus ignis invenerit spinas, et comprehenderit acervos frugum, sive stantes segetes in agris, reddet damnum qui ignem succenderit.
     EN: If fire spreads, catches thorns, and consumes stacks of grain or standing crops in the fields, whoever kindled the fire shall repay the damage.

22:6 (Vg 22:7)  LA: Si quis commendaverit amico pecuniam aut vas in custodiam, et ab eo, qui susceperat, furto ablata fuerint : si invenitur fur, duplum reddet :
     EN: If someone entrusts money or an article to a friend for safekeeping, and it is stolen from the one who received it, the thief, if found, shall repay double.

22:7 (Vg 22:8)  LA: si latet fur, dominus domus applicabitur ad deos, et jurabit quod non extenderit manum in rem proximi sui,
     EN: If the thief remains hidden, the householder shall be brought before the judges and swear that he has not laid his hand on his neighbor's property

22:8 (Vg 22:9)  LA: ad perpetrandam fraudem, tam in bove quam in asino, et ove ac vestimento, et quidquid damnum inferre potest : ad deos utriusque causa perveniet, et si illi judicaverint, duplum restituet proximo suo.
     EN: to commit fraud, whether concerning an ox, donkey, sheep, clothing, or anything that may involve a loss. Both parties' case shall come before the judges; if they give judgment against him, he shall repay his neighbor double.

22:9 (Vg 22:10)  LA: Si quis commendaverit proximo suo asinum, bovem, ovem, et omne jumentum ad custodiam, et mortuum fuerit, aut debilitatum, vel captum ab hostibus, nullusque hoc viderit :
     EN: If someone entrusts a donkey, ox, sheep, or any animal to his neighbor for safekeeping, and it dies, is injured, or is captured by enemies without anyone seeing,

22:10 (Vg 22:11)  LA: jusjurandum erit in medio, quod non extenderit manum ad rem proximi sui : suscipietque dominus juramentum, et ille reddere non cogetur.
     EN: an oath shall settle the matter, that he did not lay his hand on his neighbor's property. The owner shall accept the oath, and the other shall not be compelled to repay.

22:11 (Vg 22:12)  LA: Quod si furto ablatum fuerit, restituet damnum domino ;
     EN: But if it was stolen, he shall repay the loss to the owner.

22:12 (Vg 22:13)  LA: si comestum a bestia, deferat ad eum quod occisum est, et non restituet.
     EN: If it was devoured by a beast, he shall bring him what was killed and shall not make restitution.

22:13 (Vg 22:14)  LA: Qui a proximo suo quidquam horum mutuo postulaverit, et debilitatum aut mortuum fuerit domino non præsente, reddere compelletur.
     EN: Whoever borrows any of these from his neighbor shall be compelled to repay if it is injured or dies while its owner is absent.

22:14 (Vg 22:15)  LA: Quod si impræsentiarum dominus fuerit, non restituet, maxime si conductum venerat pro mercede operis sui.
     EN: But if the owner is present, he shall not repay, especially if it had been hired for the wages of its work.

22:15 (Vg 22:16)  LA: Si seduxerit quis virginem necdum desponsatam, dormieritque cum ea : dotabit eam, et habebit eam uxorem.
     EN: If someone seduces a virgin who is not yet betrothed and sleeps with her, he shall provide her marriage payment and take her as his wife.

22:16 (Vg 22:17)  LA: Si pater virginis dare noluerit, reddet pecuniam juxta modum dotis, quam virgines accipere consueverunt.
     EN: If the virgin's father refuses to give her, he shall pay money according to the marriage payment virgins customarily receive.

22:17 (Vg 22:18)  LA: Maleficos non patieris vivere.
     EN: You shall not allow sorcerers to live.

22:18 (Vg 22:19)  LA: Qui coierit cum jumento, morte moriatur.
     EN: Whoever has intercourse with an animal shall surely die.

22:19 (Vg 22:20)  LA: Qui immolat diis, occidetur, præterquam Domino soli.
     EN: Whoever sacrifices to gods, except to the Lord alone, shall be killed.

22:20 (Vg 22:21)  LA: Advenam non contristabis, neque affliges eum : advenæ enim et ipsi fuistis in terra Ægypti.
     EN: You shall not distress or afflict a stranger, for you yourselves were strangers in the land of Egypt.

22:21 (Vg 22:22)  LA: Viduæ et pupillo non nocebitis.
     EN: You shall not harm a widow or orphan.

22:22 (Vg 22:23)  LA: Si læseritis eos, vociferabuntur ad me, et ego audiam clamorem eorum :
     EN: If you harm them, they will cry out to Me, and I will hear their cry.

22:23 (Vg 22:24)  LA: et indignabitur furor meus, percutiamque vos gladio, et erunt uxores vestræ viduæ, et filii vestri pupilli.
     EN: My wrath will blaze, and I will strike you with the sword. Your wives will become widows and your children orphans.

22:24 (Vg 22:25)  LA: Si pecuniam mutuam dederis populo meo pauperi qui habitat tecum, non urgebis eum quasi exactor, nec usuris opprimes.
     EN: If you lend money to one of My poor people living with you, you shall not press him like a debt collector or oppress him with interest.

22:25 (Vg 22:26)  LA: Si pignus a proximo tuo acceperis vestimentum, ante solis occasum reddes ei.
     EN: If you take your neighbor's garment as a pledge, return it to him before sunset.

22:26 (Vg 22:27)  LA: Ipsum enim est solum, quo operitur, indumentum carnis ejus, nec habet aliud in quo dormiat : si clamaverit ad me, exaudiam eum, quia misericors sum.
     EN: For it is his only covering, the clothing for his body, and he has nothing else in which to sleep. If he cries to Me, I will hear him, for I am merciful.

22:27 (Vg 22:28)  LA: Diis non detrahes, et principi populi tui non maledices.
     EN: You shall not revile the judges or curse the ruler of your people.

22:28 (Vg 22:29)  LA: Decimas tuas et primitias tuas non tardabis reddere : primogenitum filiorum tuorum dabis mihi.
     EN: You shall not delay giving your tithes and firstfruits. You shall give Me the firstborn of your sons.

22:29 (Vg 22:30)  LA: De bobus quoque, et ovibus similiter facies : septem diebus sit cum matre sua, die octava reddes illum mihi.
     EN: You shall do likewise with your cattle and sheep. It shall remain with its mother seven days; on the eighth day you shall give it to Me.

22:30 (Vg 22:31)  LA: Viri sancti eritis mihi : carnem, quæ a bestiis fuerit prægustata, non comedetis, sed projicietis canibus.
     EN: You shall be holy men to Me. You shall not eat flesh that beasts have already fed upon; throw it to the dogs.

EXODUS 23

23:1  LA: Non suscipies vocem mendacii, nec junges manum tuam ut pro impio dicas falsum testimonium.
     EN: You shall not accept a lying report or join hands to give false testimony for a wicked person.

23:2  LA: Non sequeris turbam ad faciendum malum : nec in judicio, plurimorum acquiesces sententiæ, ut a vero devies.
     EN: You shall not follow a crowd to do evil, nor yield to the majority's opinion in judgment so as to turn aside from the truth.

23:3  LA: Pauperis quoque non misereberis in judicio.
     EN: Neither shall you favor a poor person in judgment.

23:4  LA: Si occurreris bovi inimici tui, aut asino erranti, reduc ad eum.
     EN: If you meet your enemy's ox or donkey wandering, return it to him.

23:5  LA: Si videris asinum odientis te jacere sub onere, non pertransibis, sed sublevabis cum eo.
     EN: If you see the donkey of someone who hates you lying under its load, you shall not pass by but help him lift it.

23:6  LA: Non declinabis in judicium pauperis.
     EN: You shall not distort a poor person's judgment.

23:7  LA: Mendacium fugies. Insontem et justum non occides : quia aversor impium.
     EN: Shun falsehood. Do not kill the innocent and righteous, for I reject the wicked.

23:8  LA: Nec accipies munera, quæ etiam excæcant prudentes, et subvertunt verba justorum.
     EN: You shall not accept gifts, which blind even the prudent and overturn the words of the righteous.

23:9  LA: Peregrino molestus non eris. Scitis enim advenarum animas : quia et ipsi peregrini fuistis in terra Ægypti.
     EN: You shall not trouble a foreigner. You know the souls of strangers, because you yourselves were foreigners in the land of Egypt.

23:10  LA: Sex annis seminabis terram tuam, et congregabis fruges ejus :
     EN: For six years you shall sow your land and gather its produce.

23:11  LA: anno autem septimo dimittes eam, et requiescere facies, ut comedant pauperes populi tui : et quidquid reliquum fuerit, edant bestiæ agri : ita facies in vinea et in oliveto tuo.
     EN: But in the seventh year you shall leave it and let it rest, so that the poor of your people may eat. Whatever remains, let the beasts of the field eat. You shall do likewise with your vineyard and olive grove.

23:12  LA: Sex diebus operaberis : septimo die cessabis, ut requiescat bos et asinus tuus, et refrigeretur filius ancillæ tuæ, et advena.
     EN: For six days you shall work; on the seventh day you shall cease, so that your ox and donkey may rest and your female slave's son and the stranger may be refreshed.

23:13  LA: Omnia quæ dixi vobis, custodite. Et per nomen externorum deorum non jurabitis, neque audietur ex ore vestro.
     EN: Keep everything I have told you. You shall not swear by the name of foreign gods, nor shall it be heard from your mouth.

23:14  LA: Tribus vicibus per singulos annos mihi festa celebrabitis.
     EN: Three times each year you shall celebrate festivals for Me.

23:15  LA: Solemnitatem azymorum custodies. Septem diebus comedes azyma, sicut præcepi tibi, tempore mensis novorum, quando egressus es de Ægypto : non apparebis in conspectu meo vacuus.
     EN: You shall keep the festival of Unleavened Bread. For seven days you shall eat unleavened bread as I commanded you, at the appointed time in the month of new crops, when you came out of Egypt. You shall not appear before Me empty-handed.

23:16  LA: Et solemnitatem messis primitivorum operis tui, quæcumque seminaveris in agro : solemnitatem quoque in exitu anni, quando congregaveris omnes fruges tuas de agro.
     EN: Also keep the harvest festival of the firstfruits of your labor, whatever you have sown in the field, and the festival at the year's end, when you have gathered all your produce from the field.

23:17  LA: Ter in anno apparebit omne masculinum tuum coram Domino Deo tuo.
     EN: Three times a year every male among you shall appear before the Lord your God.

23:18  LA: Non immolabis super fermento sanguinem victimæ meæ, nec remanebit adeps solemnitatis meæ usque mane.
     EN: You shall not offer the blood of My sacrifice with leaven, nor shall the fat of My festival remain until morning.

23:19  LA: Primitias frugum terræ tuæ deferes in domum Domini Dei tui. Non coques hædum in lacte matris suæ.
     EN: You shall bring the firstfruits of your land's produce into the house of the Lord your God. You shall not boil a young goat in its mother's milk.

23:20  LA: Ecce ego mittam angelum meum, qui præcedat te, et custodiat in via, et introducat in locum quem paravi.
     EN: Look, I will send My angel to go before you, guard you on the way, and bring you into the place I have prepared.

23:21  LA: Observa eum, et audi vocem ejus, nec contemnendum putes : quia non dimittet cum peccaveris, et est nomen meum in illo.
     EN: Attend to him and listen to his voice. Do not consider him worthy of contempt, for he will not pardon you when you sin, and My name is in him.

23:22  LA: Quod si audieris vocem ejus, et feceris omnia quæ loquor, inimicus ero inimicis tuis, et affligam affligentes te.
     EN: If you listen to his voice and do everything I say, I will be an enemy to your enemies and afflict those who afflict you.

23:23  LA: Præcedetque te angelus meus, et introducet te ad Amorrhæum, et Hethæum, et Pherezæum, Chananæumque, et Hevæum, et Jebusæum, quos ego conteram.
     EN: My angel will go before you and bring you to the Amorite, Hittite, Perizzite, Canaanite, Hivite, and Jebusite, whom I will crush.

23:24  LA: Non adorabis deos eorum, nec coles eos : non facies opera eorum, sed destrues eos, et confringes statuas eorum.
     EN: You shall not worship their gods or serve them. You shall not do their works but destroy them and break their statues.

23:25  LA: Servietisque Domino Deo vestro, ut benedicam panibus tuis et aquis, et auferam infirmitatem de medio tui.
     EN: You shall serve the Lord your God, so that I may bless your bread and water and remove sickness from your midst.

23:26  LA: Non erit infœcunda, nec sterilis in terra tua : numerum dierum tuorum implebo.
     EN: There shall be no one unfruitful or barren in your land. I will fulfill the number of your days.

23:27  LA: Terrorem meum mittam in præcursum tuum, et occidam omnem populum, ad quem ingredieris : cunctorumque inimicorum tuorum coram te terga vertam :
     EN: I will send My terror before you and kill every people whose land you enter. I will make all your enemies turn their backs before you,

23:28  LA: emittens crabrones prius, qui fugabunt Hevæum, et Chananæum, et Hethæum, antequam introëas.
     EN: sending hornets ahead to drive away the Hivite, Canaanite, and Hittite before you enter.

23:29  LA: Non ejiciam eos a facie tua anno uno : ne terra in solitudinem redigatur, et crescant contra te bestiæ.
     EN: I will not drive them away from before you in one year, lest the land become a wilderness and beasts multiply against you.

23:30  LA: Paulatim expellam eos de conspectu tuo, donec augearis, et possideas terram.
     EN: Little by little I will drive them from your sight until you increase and possess the land.

23:31  LA: Ponam autem terminos tuos a mari Rubro usque ad mare Palæstinorum, et a deserto usque ad fluvium : tradam in manibus vestris habitatores terræ, et ejiciam eos de conspectu vestro.
     EN: I will set your borders from the Red Sea to the sea of the Philistines, and from the wilderness to the river. I will deliver the inhabitants of the land into your hands and drive them from your sight.

23:32  LA: Non inibis cum eis fœdus, nec cum diis eorum.
     EN: You shall make no covenant with them or with their gods.

23:33  LA: Non habitent in terra tua, ne forte peccare te faciant in me, si servieris diis eorum : quod tibi certe erit in scandalum.
     EN: They shall not dwell in your land, lest they make you sin against Me by serving their gods; this will certainly become a stumbling block for you.”

EXODUS 24

24:1  LA: Moysi quoque dixit : Ascende ad Dominum tu, et Aaron, Nadab et Abiu, et septuaginta senes ex Israël, et adorabitis procul.
     EN: He also said to Moses, “Come up to the Lord, you and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy elders of Israel, and worship at a distance.

24:2  LA: Solusque Moyses ascendet ad Dominum, et illi non appropinquabunt : nec populus ascendet cum eo.
     EN: Moses alone shall come up to the Lord. The others shall not approach, nor shall the people ascend with him.”

24:3  LA: Venit ergo Moyses et narravit plebi omnia verba Domini, atque judicia : responditque omnis populus una voce : Omnia verba Domini, quæ locutus est, faciemus.
     EN: Moses came and recounted to the people all the Lord's words and judgments. All the people answered with one voice, “All the words the Lord has spoken, we will do.”

24:4  LA: Scripsit autem Moyses universos sermones Domini : et mane consurgens, ædificavit altare ad radices montis, et duodecim titulos per duodecim tribus Israël.
     EN: Moses wrote all the Lord's words. Rising in the morning, he built an altar at the foot of the mountain and twelve pillars for Israel's twelve tribes.

24:5  LA: Misitque juvenes de filiis Israël, et obtulerunt holocausta, immolaveruntque victimas pacificas Domino, vitulos.
     EN: He sent young men of the children of Israel, and they offered whole burnt offerings and sacrificed young bulls as peace offerings to the Lord.

24:6  LA: Tulit itaque Moyses dimidiam partem sanguinis, et misit in crateras : partem autem residuam fudit super altare.
     EN: Moses took half the blood and put it into bowls; the remaining part he poured upon the altar.

24:7  LA: Assumensque volumen fœderis, legit audiente populo : qui dixerunt : Omnia quæ locutus est Dominus, faciemus, et erimus obedientes.
     EN: Taking the scroll of the covenant, he read it in the people's hearing. They said, “Everything the Lord has spoken, we will do, and we will be obedient.”

24:8  LA: Ille vero sumptum sanguinem respersit in populum, et ait : Hic est sanguis fœderis quod pepigit Dominus vobiscum super cunctis sermonibus his.
     EN: He took the blood, sprinkled it upon the people, and said, “This is the blood of the covenant the Lord has made with you concerning all these words.”

24:9  LA: Ascenderuntque Moyses et Aaron, Nadab et Abiu, et septuaginta de senioribus Israël :
     EN: Moses and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of Israel's elders went up.

24:10  LA: et viderunt Deum Israël : et sub pedibus ejus quasi opus lapidis sapphirini, et quasi cælum, cum serenum est.
     EN: They saw the God of Israel. Under His feet was something like a work of sapphire stone, like the sky when it is clear.

24:11  LA: Nec super eos qui procul recesserant de filiis Israël, misit manum suam, videruntque Deum, et comederunt, ac biberunt.
     EN: He did not stretch out His hand against those of the children of Israel who had withdrawn to a distance. They saw God, and ate and drank.

24:12  LA: Dixit autem Dominus ad Moysen : Ascende ad me in montem, et esto ibi : daboque tibi tabulas lapideas, et legem, ac mandata quæ scripsi : ut doceas eos.
     EN: The Lord said to Moses, “Come up to Me on the mountain and remain there. I will give you stone tablets, and the law and commandments I have written, so that you may teach them.”

24:13  LA: Surrexerunt Moyses et Josue minister ejus : ascendensque Moyses in montem Dei,
     EN: Moses and his attendant Joshua rose, and as Moses ascended the mountain of God,

24:14  LA: senioribus ait : Expectate hic donec revertamur ad vos. Habetis Aaron et Hur vobiscum : si quid natum fuerit quæstionis, referetis ad eos.
     EN: he said to the elders, “Wait here until we return to you. You have Aaron and Hur with you; if any dispute arises, refer it to them.”

24:15  LA: Cumque ascendisset Moyses, operuit nubes montem,
     EN: When Moses went up, a cloud covered the mountain,

24:16  LA: et habitavit gloria Domini super Sinai, tegens illum nube sex diebus : septimo autem die vocavit eum de medio caliginis.
     EN: and the Lord's glory dwelt upon Sinai, covering it with a cloud for six days. On the seventh day He called to him from the midst of the darkness.

24:17  LA: Erat autem species gloriæ Domini quasi ignis ardens super verticem montis in conspectu filiorum Israël.
     EN: The appearance of the Lord's glory was like a burning fire on the mountain's summit in the sight of the children of Israel.

24:18  LA: Ingressusque Moyses medium nebulæ, ascendit in montem : et fuit ibi quadraginta diebus, et quadraginta noctibus.
     EN: Moses entered the midst of the cloud and ascended the mountain. He was there forty days and forty nights.

EXODUS 25

25:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

25:2  LA: Loquere filiis Israël, ut tollant mihi primitias : ab omni homine qui offeret ultroneus, accipietis eas.
     EN: “Tell the children of Israel to take firstfruits for Me. You shall receive them from every person who offers willingly.

25:3  LA: Hæc sunt autem quæ accipere debeatis : aurum, et argentum, et æs,
     EN: These are what you shall receive: gold, silver, and bronze;

25:4  LA: hyacinthum et purpuram, coccumque bis tinctum, et byssum, pilos caprarum,
     EN: blue and purple, twice-dyed scarlet, fine linen, goats' hair;

25:5  LA: et pelles arietum rubricatas, pellesque janthinas, et ligna setim :
     EN: rams' skins dyed red, violet-colored skins, and acacia wood;

25:6  LA: oleum ad luminaria concinnanda : aromata in unguentum, et thymiamata boni odoris :
     EN: oil for preparing the lamps, spices for anointing oil, and sweet-smelling incense;

25:7  LA: lapides onychinos, et gemmas ad ornandum ephod, ac rationale.
     EN: onyx stones and gems for adorning the ephod and breastpiece.

25:8  LA: Facientque mihi sanctuarium, et habitabo in medio eorum :
     EN: They shall make Me a sanctuary, and I will dwell in their midst,

25:9  LA: juxta omnem similitudinem tabernaculi quod ostendam tibi, et omnium vasorum in cultum ejus. Sicque facietis illud :
     EN: according to the entire pattern of the tabernacle that I will show you and all the furnishings for its service. Thus you shall make it:

25:10  LA: arcam de lignis setim compingite, cujus longitudo habeat duos et semis cubitos : latitudo, cubitum et dimidium : altitudo, cubitum similiter ac semissem.
     EN: Construct an ark of acacia wood, two and a half cubits long, a cubit and a half wide, and likewise a cubit and a half high.

25:11  LA: Et deaurabis eam auro mundissimo intus et foris : faciesque supra, coronam auream per circuitum :

25:30  LA: Et pones super mensam panes propositionis in conspectu meo semper.
     EN: You shall place the bread of presentation on the table before Me always.

25:31  LA: Facies et candelabrum ductile de auro mundissimo, hastile ejus, et calamos, scyphos, et sphærulas, ac lilia ex ipso procedentia.
     EN: Make a lampstand of hammered work from the purest gold, with its shaft, branches, cups, knobs, and lilies proceeding from it.

     EN: On the lampstand itself shall be four nut-shaped cups, each with knobs and lilies.

EXODUS 26

26:1  LA: Tabernaculum vero ita facies : decem cortinas de bysso retorta, et hyacintho, ac purpura, coccoque bis tincto, variatas opere plumario facies.
     EN: You shall make the tabernacle this way: make ten curtains of twisted fine linen, blue, purple, and twice-dyed scarlet, patterned with embroidered work.

26:2  LA: Longitudo cortinæ unius habebit viginti octo cubitos : latitudo, quatuor cubitorum erit. Unius mensuræ fient universa tentoria.
     EN: Each curtain shall be twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide. All the curtains shall have one measure.

26:3  LA: Quinque cortinæ sibi jungentur mutuo, et aliæ quinque nexu simili cohærebunt.
     EN: Five curtains shall be joined to one another, and the other five shall be linked in the same way.

26:4  LA: Ansulas hyacinthinas in lateribus ac summitatibus facies cortinarum, ut possint invicem copulari.
     EN: Make blue loops along the sides and ends of the curtains, so that they can be joined to one another.

26:5  LA: Quinquagenas ansulas cortina habebit in utraque parte, ita insertas ut ansa contra ansam veniat, et altera alteri possit aptari.
     EN: The curtain shall have fifty loops on each side, fitted so that loop faces loop and each can be joined to the other.

26:6  LA: Facies et quinquaginta circulos aureos quibus cortinarum vela jungenda sunt, ut unum tabernaculum fiat.
     EN: Make fifty gold rings with which the curtain panels are to be joined, so that the tabernacle becomes one.

26:7  LA: Facies et saga cilicina undecim, ad operiendum tectum tabernaculi.
     EN: Make eleven coverings of goats' hair to cover the tabernacle's roof.

26:8  LA: Longitudo sagi unius habebit triginta cubitos, et latitudo, quatuor : æqua erit mensura sagorum omnium.
     EN: Each covering shall be thirty cubits long and four wide; all the coverings shall have the same measure.

26:9  LA: E quibus quinque junges seorsum, et sex sibi mutuo copulabis, ita ut sextum sagum in fronte tecti duplices.
     EN: Join five of them separately and link six to one another, folding the sixth covering double at the front of the roof.

26:10  LA: Facies et quinquaginta ansas in ora sagi unius, ut conjungi cum altero queat, et quinquaginta ansas in ora sagi alterius, ut cum altero copuletur.
     EN: Make fifty loops on the edge of one covering, so that it can be joined to the other, and fifty loops on the edge of the other covering to join it to the first.

26:11  LA: Facies et quinquaginta fibulas æneas quibus jungantur ansæ, ut unum ex omnibus operimentum fiat.
     EN: Make fifty bronze clasps to join the loops, so that all form one covering.

26:12  LA: Quod autem superfuerit in sagis quæ parantur tecto, id est unum sagum quod amplius est, ex medietate ejus operies posteriora tabernaculi.
     EN: Of what remains in the coverings prepared for the roof—that is, the extra covering—you shall use half to cover the back of the tabernacle.

26:13  LA: Et cubitus ex una parte pendebit, et alter ex altera qui plus est in sagorum longitudine, utrumque latus tabernaculi protegens.
     EN: A cubit on one side and a cubit on the other, the excess in the coverings' length, shall hang down to protect both sides of the tabernacle.

26:14  LA: Facies et operimentum aliud tecto de pellibus arietum rubricatis : et super hoc rursum aliud operimentum de janthinis pellibus.

     EN: each ten cubits long and a cubit and a half wide.

26:20  LA: In latere quoque secundo tabernaculi quod vergit ad aquilonem, viginti tabulæ erunt,

26:22  LA: Ad occidentalem vero plagam tabernaculi facies sex tabulas,

     EN: and two others to stand at the corners at the back of the tabernacle.

26:25  LA: Et erunt simul tabulæ octo, bases earum argenteæ sedecim, duabus basibus per unam tabulam supputatis.

     EN: five others on the other side, and the same number on the western side.

     EN: They shall be passed through the middle of the boards from end to end.

26:29  LA: Ipsas quoque tabulas deaurabis, et fundes in eis annulos aureos per quos vectes tabulata contineant : quos operies laminis aureis.
     EN: Overlay the boards themselves with gold, and cast gold rings for them through which the bars may hold the framework together. Cover the bars with gold plates.

     EN: Overlay five pillars of acacia wood with gold, before which the screen shall be drawn. Their capitals shall be gold and their bases bronze.

27:12  LA: In latitudine vero atrii, quod respicit ad occidentem, erunt tentoria per quinquaginta cubitos, et columnæ decem, basesque totidem.
     EN: Across the width of the courtyard facing west, there shall be hangings fifty cubits long, ten pillars, and as many bases.

     EN: The width of the courtyard facing east shall also be fifty cubits.

27:14  LA: In quibus quindecim cubitorum tentoria lateri uno deputabuntur, columnæque tres et bases totidem :
     EN: Of these, hangings of fifteen cubits shall be assigned to one side, with three pillars and as many bases.

     EN: For the courtyard entrance make a screen twenty cubits long of blue, purple, twice-dyed scarlet, and twisted fine linen, the work of an embroiderer. It shall have four pillars with as many bases.

27:17  LA: Omnes columnæ atrii per circuitum vestitæ erunt argenteis laminis, capitibus argenteis, et basibus æneis.
     EN: All the pillars around the courtyard shall be covered with silver plates, with silver capitals and bronze bases.

27:18  LA: In longitudine occupabit atrium cubitos centum, in latitudine quinquaginta, altitudo quinque cubitorum erit : fietque de bysso retorta, et habebit bases æneas.

27:19  LA: Cuncta vasa tabernaculi in omnes usus et cæremonias, tam paxillos ejus quam atrii, ex ære facies.
     EN: Make all the tabernacle's utensils for every use and ceremony, its pegs as well as those of the courtyard, of bronze.

27:20  LA: Præcipe filiis Israël ut afferant tibi oleum de arboribus olivarum purissimum, piloque contusum, ut ardeat lucerna semper
     EN: Command the children of Israel to bring you the purest oil from olive trees, beaten with a pestle, so that a lamp may burn continually

     EN: in the tabernacle of the testimony, outside the veil spread before the testimony. Aaron and his sons shall set it in place to shine before the Lord until morning. It shall be a perpetual observance from the children of Israel throughout their generations.

EXODUS 28

28:1  LA: Applica quoque ad te Aaron fratrem tuum cum filiis suis de medio filiorum Israël, ut sacerdotio fungantur mihi : Aaron, Nadab, et Abiu, Eleazar, et Ithamar.

28:2  LA: Faciesque vestem sanctam Aaron fratri tuo in gloriam et decorem.
     EN: Make holy clothing for your brother Aaron, for glory and beauty.

28:3  LA: Et loqueris cunctis sapientibus corde quos replevi spiritu prudentiæ, ut faciant vestes Aaron, in quibus sanctificatus ministret mihi.
     EN: Speak to all the wise of heart whom I have filled with the spirit of understanding, to make Aaron's garments in which he may be consecrated to minister to Me.

     EN: These are the garments they shall make: a breastpiece and ephod, a robe and fitted linen tunic, a turban and sash. They shall make holy garments for your brother Aaron and his sons, to serve Me as priests.

28:5  LA: Accipientque aurum, et hyacinthum, et purpuram, coccumque bis tinctum, et byssum.
     EN: They shall take gold, blue, purple, twice-dyed scarlet, and fine linen.

28:6  LA: Facient autem superhumerale de auro et hyacintho et purpura, coccoque bis tincto, et bysso retorta, opere polymito.
     EN: They shall make the ephod of gold, blue, purple, twice-dyed scarlet, and twisted fine linen, in skillfully woven work.

28:7  LA: Duas oras junctas habebit in utroque latere summitatum, ut in unum redeant.
     EN: It shall have two joined edges at the top on either side, so that they form one piece.

28:8  LA: Ipsa quoque textura et cuncta operis varietas erit ex auro et hyacintho, et purpura, coccoque bis tincto, et bysso retorta.
     EN: Its fabric and all the varied workmanship shall likewise be of gold, blue, purple, twice-dyed scarlet, and twisted fine linen.

28:9  LA: Sumesque duos lapides onychinos, et sculpes in eis nomina filiorum Israël :
     EN: Take two onyx stones and engrave on them the names of the children of Israel:

28:10  LA: sex nomina in lapide uno, et sex reliqua in altero, juxta ordinem nativitatis eorum.
     EN: six names on one stone and the remaining six on the other, according to their birth order.

28:11  LA: Opere sculptoris et cælatura gemmarii, sculpes eos nominibus filiorum Israël, inclusos auro atque circumdatos :
     EN: With a sculptor's work and a gem-cutter's engraving, engrave them with the names of the children of Israel, enclosed and surrounded by gold.

28:12  LA: et pones in utroque latere superhumeralis, memoriale filiis Israël. Portabitque Aaron nomina eorum coram Domino super utrumque humerum, ob recordationem.
     EN: Place them on either side of the ephod as a memorial for the children of Israel. Aaron shall carry their names before the Lord on both shoulders as a remembrance.

28:13  LA: Facies et uncinos ex auro,
     EN: Make gold hooks,

28:14  LA: et duas catenulas ex auro purissimo sibi invicem cohærentes, quas inseres uncinis.
     EN: and two little chains of the purest gold linked together, which you shall attach to the hooks.

28:15  LA: Rationale quoque judicii facies opere polymito juxta texturam superhumeralis, ex auro, hyacintho, et purpura, coccoque bis tincto, et bysso retorta.
     EN: Make the breastpiece of judgment in skillfully woven work, like the fabric of the ephod, of gold, blue, purple, twice-dyed scarlet, and twisted fine linen.

28:16  LA: Quadrangulum erit et duplex : mensuram palmi habebit tam in longitudine quam in latitudine.
     EN: It shall be square and doubled, a span in length and a span in width.

28:17  LA: Ponesque in eo quatuor ordines lapidum : in primo versu erit lapis sardius, et topazius, et smaragdus :
     EN: Set four rows of stones in it. In the first row shall be sardius, topaz, and emerald;

28:18  LA: in secundo carbunculus, sapphirus, et jaspis :
     EN: in the second, carbuncle, sapphire, and jasper;

28:19  LA: in tertio ligurius, achates, et amethystus :
     EN: in the third, ligure, agate, and amethyst;

28:20  LA: in quarto chrysolithus, onychinus, et beryllus. Inclusi auro erunt per ordines suos.
     EN: in the fourth, chrysolite, onyx, and beryl. They shall be enclosed in gold in their rows.

28:21  LA: Habebuntque nomina filiorum Israël : duodecim nominibus cælabuntur, singuli lapides nominibus singulorum per duodecim tribus.
     EN: They shall bear the names of the children of Israel, engraved with twelve names, each stone with the name of one of the twelve tribes.

28:22  LA: Facies in rationali catenas sibi invicem cohærentes ex auro purissimo,
     EN: Make linked chains of the purest gold on the breastpiece,

28:23  LA: et duos annulos aureos, quos pones in utraque rationalis summitate :
     EN: and two gold rings, which you shall place at the two upper ends of the breastpiece.

28:24  LA: catenasque aureas junges annulis, qui sunt in marginibus ejus,
     EN: Join the gold chains to the rings at its edges.

EXODUS 32

     EN: Aaron said to them, “Take the gold earrings from the ears of your wives, sons, and daughters, and bring them to me.”

     EN: The people did what he commanded and brought the earrings to Aaron.

     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying, “Go down. Your people, whom you led out of the land of Egypt, have sinned.

     EN: They have quickly turned from the way you showed them. They made themselves a molten calf, worshiped it, and sacrificed offerings to it, saying, ‘These are your gods, Israel, who led you out of the land of Egypt.’”

     EN: The Lord said again to Moses, “I see that this people is stiff-necked.

     EN: Leave Me, so that My wrath may blaze against them and I may destroy them; then I will make you into a great nation.”

     EN: But Moses prayed to the Lord his God, saying, “Why, Lord, does Your wrath blaze against Your people, whom You led out of the land of Egypt with great strength and a mighty hand?

32:13  LA: Recordare Abraham, Isaac, et Israël servorum tuorum, quibus jurasti per temetipsum, dicens : Multiplicabo semen vestrum sicut stellas cæli ; et universam terram hanc, de qua locutus sum, dabo semini vestro, et possidebitis eam semper.

     EN: The Lord was appeased and did not do the harm He had spoken against His people.

     EN: They were made by God's work, and the writing engraved on the tablets was also God's.

     EN: The Lord therefore struck the people for the guilt of the calf Aaron had made.

EXODUS 33

33:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens : Vade, ascende de loco isto tu, et populus tuus quem eduxisti de terra Ægypti, in terram quam juravi Abraham, Isaac et Jacob, dicens : Semini tuo dabo eam :

     EN: The Lord said to Moses, “Tell the children of Israel: ‘You are a stiff-necked people. Were I to go up among you for a moment, I would destroy you. Now remove your ornaments, so that I may know what to do with you.’”

     EN: So the children of Israel laid aside their ornaments from Mount Horeb onward.

33:7  LA: Moyses quoque tollens tabernaculum, tetendit extra castra procul, vocavitque nomen ejus, Tabernaculum fœderis. Et omnis populus, qui habebat aliquam quæstionem, egrediebatur ad tabernaculum fœderis, extra castra.
     EN: Moses took the tent and pitched it far outside the camp, calling it the Tabernacle of the Covenant. Anyone among the people who had a question went out to the tabernacle of the covenant outside the camp.

33:8  LA: Cumque egrederetur Moyses ad tabernaculum, surgebat universa plebs, et stabat unusquisque in ostio papilionis sui, aspiciebantque tergum Moysi, donec ingrederetur tentorium.
     EN: Whenever Moses went out to the tabernacle, all the people rose, each standing at the entrance of his tent. They watched Moses' back until he entered the tent.

33:9  LA: Ingresso autem illo tabernaculum fœderis, descendebat columna nubis, et stabat ad ostium, loquebaturque cum Moyse,
     EN: When he entered the tabernacle of the covenant, the pillar of cloud descended and stood at the entrance, and He spoke with Moses.

33:10  LA: cernentibus universis quod columna nubis staret ad ostium tabernaculi. Stabantque ipsi, et adorabant per fores tabernaculorum suorum.
     EN: Everyone saw the pillar of cloud standing at the tabernacle's entrance. They stood and worshiped at the doors of their own tents.

33:11  LA: Loquebatur autem Dominus ad Moysen facie ad faciem, sicut solet loqui homo ad amicum suum. Cumque ille reverteretur in castra, minister ejus Josue filius Nun, puer, non recedebat de tabernaculo.
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses face to face, as a man usually speaks to his friend. When Moses returned to the camp, his young attendant Joshua son of Nun did not leave the tabernacle.

33:12  LA: Dixit autem Moyses ad Dominum : Præcipis ut educam populum istum : et non indicas mihi quem missurus es mecum, præsertim cum dixeris : Novi te ex nomine, et invenisti gratiam coram me.
     EN: Moses said to the Lord, “You command me to lead this people, but You do not tell me whom You will send with me, although You have said, ‘I know you by name, and you have found favor before Me.’

33:13  LA: Si ergo inveni gratiam in conspectu tuo, ostende mihi faciem tuam, ut sciam te, et inveniam gratiam ante oculos tuos : respice populum tuum gentem hanc.
     EN: If, then, I have found favor in Your sight, show me Your face, so that I may know You and find favor in Your eyes. Look upon this nation as Your people.”

33:14  LA: Dixitque Dominus : Facies mea præcedet te, et requiem dabo tibi.
     EN: The Lord said, “My face will go before you, and I will give you rest.”

33:15  LA: Et ait Moyses : Si non tu ipse præcedas, ne educas nos de loco isto.
     EN: Moses said, “If You Yourself do not go before us, do not lead us away from this place.

33:16  LA: In quo enim scire poterimus ego et populus tuus invenisse nos gratiam in conspectu tuo, nisi ambulaveris nobiscum, ut glorificemur ab omnibus populis qui habitant super terram ?
     EN: For how can we know, I and Your people, that we have found favor in Your sight unless You walk with us, so that we may be distinguished in glory from all peoples dwelling upon the earth?”

33:17  LA: Dixit autem Dominus ad Moysen : Et verbum istud, quod locutus es, faciam : invenisti enim gratiam coram me, et teipsum novi ex nomine.
     EN: The Lord said to Moses, “I will also do this thing you have spoken, for you have found favor before Me, and I know you by name.”

33:18  LA: Qui ait : Ostende mihi gloriam tuam.
     EN: He said, “Show me Your glory.”

33:19  LA: Respondit : Ego ostendam omne bonum tibi, et vocabo in nomine Domini coram te : et miserebor cui voluero, et clemens ero in quem mihi placuerit.
     EN: He answered, “I will show you all goodness and proclaim in the name of the Lord before you. I will have mercy on whom I will, and be gracious to whom I please.”

33:20  LA: Rursumque ait : Non poteris videre faciem meam : non enim videbit me homo et vivet.
     EN: He said again, “You cannot see My face, for no human shall see Me and live.”

33:21  LA: Et iterum : Ecce, inquit, est locus apud me, et stabis supra petram.
     EN: Again He said, “Look, there is a place beside Me, and you shall stand upon the rock.

33:22  LA: Cumque transibit gloria mea, ponam te in foramine petræ, et protegam dextera mea, donec transeam :
     EN: When My glory passes, I will put you in a cleft of the rock and shelter you with My right hand until I have passed.

     EN: I will remove My hand, and you shall see My back; but My face you cannot see.”

EXODUS 34

34:1  LA: Ac deinceps : Præcide, ait, tibi duas tabulas lapideas instar priorum, et scribam super eas verba, quæ habuerunt tabulæ quas fregisti.
     EN: Then He said, “Cut two stone tablets like the former ones, and I will write upon them the words that were on the tablets you broke.

     EN: Be ready in the morning to ascend Mount Sinai at once, and stand with Me on the mountain's summit.

34:3  LA: Nullus ascendat tecum, nec videatur quispiam per totum montem : boves quoque et oves non pascantur e contra.
     EN: Let no one ascend with you or be seen anywhere on the mountain. Neither cattle nor sheep shall graze opposite it.”

34:4  LA: Excidit ergo duas tabulas lapideas, quales antea fuerant : et de nocte consurgens ascendit in montem Sinai, sicut præceperat ei Dominus, portans secum tabulas.
     EN: He cut two stone tablets like the former ones. Rising while it was still night, he ascended Mount Sinai as the Lord commanded him, carrying the tablets with him.

34:5  LA: Cumque descendisset Dominus per nubem, stetit Moyses cum eo, invocans nomen Domini.
     EN: When the Lord descended in the cloud, Moses stood with Him, invoking the Lord's name.

34:6  LA: Quo transeunte coram eo, ait : Dominator Domine Deus, misericors et clemens, patiens et multæ miserationis, ac verax,
     EN: As He passed before him, Moses said, “Sovereign Lord God, merciful and gracious, patient, abundant in compassion, and true,

34:7  LA: qui custodis misericordiam in millia ; qui aufers iniquitatem, et scelera, atque peccata, nullusque apud te per se innocens est ; qui reddis iniquitatem patrum filiis, ac nepotibus in tertiam et quartam progeniem.
     EN: who keep mercy for thousands, who take away iniquity, crimes, and sins, and before whom no one is innocent of himself; who repay the iniquity of fathers upon children and grandchildren, to the third and fourth generation.”

34:8  LA: Festinusque Moyses, curvatus est pronus in terram, et adorans
     EN: Moses quickly bowed low to the ground and worshiped,

34:9  LA: ait : Si inveni gratiam in conspectu tuo, Domine, obsecro ut gradiaris nobiscum (populus enim duræ cervicis est) et auferas iniquitates nostras atque peccata, nosque possideas.
     EN: saying, “If I have found favor in Your sight, Lord, I beg You to walk with us, for this is a stiff-necked people. Take away our iniquities and sins, and possess us.”

34:10  LA: Respondit Dominus : Ego inibo pactum videntibus cunctis : signa faciam quæ numquam visa sunt super terram, nec in ullis gentibus, ut cernat populus iste, in cujus es medio, opus Domini terribile quod facturus sum.
     EN: The Lord answered, “I will make a covenant in everyone's sight. I will work signs never seen upon the earth or among any nations, so that this people among whom you are may behold the fearsome work of the Lord that I will do.

34:11  LA: Observa cuncta quæ hodie mando tibi : ego ipse ejiciam ante faciem tuam Amorrhæum, et Chananæum, et Hethæum, Pherezæum quoque, et Hevæum, et Jebusæum.
     EN: Observe everything I command you today. I Myself will drive out before you the Amorite, Canaanite, Hittite, Perizzite, Hivite, and Jebusite.

34:12  LA: Cave ne umquam cum habitatoribus terræ illius jungas amicitias, quæ sint tibi in ruinam :
     EN: Take care never to enter friendships with the inhabitants of that land that may become your ruin.

34:13  LA: sed aras eorum destrue, confringe statuas, lucosque succide :
     EN: Instead, destroy their altars, break their statues, and cut down their sacred groves.

34:14  LA: noli adorare deum alienum. Dominus zelotes nomen ejus ; Deus est æmulator.
     EN: Do not worship a foreign god. The Lord—Jealous is His name—is a jealous God.

34:15  LA: Ne ineas pactum cum hominibus illarum regionum : ne, cum fornicati fuerint cum diis suis, et adoraverint simulacra eorum, vocet te quispiam ut comedas de immolatis.
     EN: Do not make a covenant with the people of those regions, lest, when they prostitute themselves with their gods and worship their images, someone invite you to eat what has been sacrificed.

34:16  LA: Nec uxorem de filiabus eorum accipies filiis tuis : ne, postquam ipsæ fuerint fornicatæ, fornicari faciant et filios tuos in deos suos.
     EN: Nor shall you take wives from their daughters for your sons, lest, after prostituting themselves, they make your sons also prostitute themselves to their gods.

34:17  LA: Deos conflatiles non facies tibi.
     EN: You shall not make molten gods for yourself.

34:18  LA: Solemnitatem azymorum custodies. Septem diebus vesceris azymis, sicut præcepi tibi, in tempore mensis novorum : mense enim verni temporis egressus es de Ægypto.
     EN: Keep the festival of Unleavened Bread. For seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, as I commanded you, at the appointed time in the month of new crops; for in the springtime month you came out of Egypt.

34:19  LA: Omne quod aperit vulvam generis masculini, meum erit. De cunctis animantibus, tam de bobus, quam de ovibus, meum erit.
     EN: Every male that opens the womb shall be Mine. Among all animals, both cattle and sheep, it shall be Mine.

34:20  LA: Primogenitum asini redimes ove : sin autem nec pretium pro eo dederis, occidetur. Primogenitum filiorum tuorum redimes : nec apparebis in conspectu meo vacuus.
     EN: Redeem a donkey's firstborn with a sheep. If you do not give a price for it, it shall be killed. Redeem your sons' firstborn, and do not appear before Me empty-handed.

34:21  LA: Sex diebus operaberis ; die septimo cessabis arare et metere.

40:35 (Vg 40:33)  LA: Nec poterat Moyses ingredi tectum fœderis, nube operiente omnia, et majestate Domini coruscante, quia cuncta nubes operuerat.
     EN: Moses could not enter the tent of the covenant, because the cloud covered everything and the Lord's majesty shone forth, for the cloud had covered all things.

40:36 (Vg 40:34)  LA: Siquando nubes tabernaculum deserebat, proficiscebantur filii Israël per turmas suas :
     EN: Whenever the cloud left the tabernacle, the children of Israel set out by their companies.

40:37 (Vg 40:35)  LA: si pendebat desuper, manebant in eodem loco.
     EN: If it remained above, they stayed in the same place.

40:38 (Vg 40:36)  LA: Nubes quippe Domini incubabat per diem tabernaculo, et ignis in nocte, videntibus cunctis populis Israël per cunctas mansiones suas.
     EN: For the Lord's cloud rested upon the tabernacle by day, and fire by night, in the sight of all the people of Israel throughout all their encampments.
