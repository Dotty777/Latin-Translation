2 KINGS 1

1:1  LA: Prævaricatus est autem Moab in Israël, postquam mortuus est Achab.
     EN: Moab rebelled against Israel after Ahab died.

1:2  LA: Ceciditque Ochozias per cancellos cœnaculi sui, quod habebat in Samaria, et ægrotavit : misitque nuntios, dicens ad eos : Ite, consulite Beelzebub deum Accaron, utrum vivere queam de infirmitate mea hac.
     EN: Ahaziah fell through the lattice of his upper room in Samaria and became ill. He sent messengers, telling them, “Go and consult Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron, about whether I can survive this illness.”

1:3  LA: Angelus autem Domini locutus est ad Eliam Thesbiten, dicens : Surge, et ascende in occursum nuntiorum regis Samariæ, et dices ad eos : Numquid non est Deus in Israël, ut eatis ad consulendum Beelzebub deum Accaron ?
     EN: But the angel of the Lord spoke to Elijah the Tishbite, saying, “Rise and go up to meet the messengers of the king of Samaria. Tell them, ‘Is there no God in Israel, that you go to consult Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron?

1:4  LA: Quam ob rem hæc dicit Dominus : De lectulo, super quem ascendisti, non descendes, sed morte morieris. Et abiit Elias.
     EN: Therefore thus says the Lord: You will not come down from the bed you have climbed onto; you will certainly die.’” And Elijah departed.

1:5  LA: Reversique sunt nuntii ad Ochoziam. Qui dixit eis : Quare reversi estis ?
     EN: The messengers returned to Ahaziah, who asked them, “Why have you returned?”

1:6  LA: At illi responderunt ei : Vir occurrit nobis, et dixit ad nos : Ite, et revertimini ad regem qui misit vos, et dicetis ei : Hæc dicit Dominus : Numquid quia non erat Deus in Israël, mittis ut consulatur Beelzebub deus Accaron ? idcirco de lectulo, super quem ascendisti, non descendes, sed morte morieris.
     EN: They answered him, “A man met us and told us, ‘Go back to the king who sent you and tell him: Thus says the Lord: Is it because there was no God in Israel that you send to consult Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron? Therefore you will not come down from the bed you have climbed onto; you will certainly die.’”

1:7  LA: Qui dixit eis : Cujus figuræ et habitus est vir ille, qui occurrit vobis, et locutus est verba hæc ?
     EN: He asked them, “What did the man who met you and spoke these words look like, and how was he dressed?”

1:8  LA: At illi dixerunt : Vir pilosus, et zona pellicea accinctus renibus. Qui ait : Elias Thesbites est.
     EN: They said, “He was a hairy man, with a leather belt fastened around his waist.” He said, “It is Elijah the Tishbite.”

1:9  LA: Misitque ad eum quinquagenarium principem, et quinquaginta qui erant sub eo. Qui ascendit ad eum : sedentique in vertice montis, ait : Homo Dei, rex præcepit ut descendas.
     EN: He sent to him a captain of fifty, with the fifty men under him. The captain went up to Elijah, who was sitting on the summit of a mountain, and said, “Man of God, the king has commanded you to come down.”

1:10  LA: Respondensque Elias, dixit quinquagenario : Si homo Dei sum, descendat ignis de cælo, et devoret te, et quinquaginta tuos. Descendit itaque ignis de cælo, et devoravit eum, et quinquaginta qui erant cum eo.
      EN: Elijah answered the captain of fifty, “If I am a man of God, let fire come down from heaven and consume you and your fifty men.” Fire came down from heaven and consumed him and the fifty men with him.

1:11  LA: Rursumque misit ad eum principem quinquagenarium alterum, et quinquaginta cum eo. Qui locutus est illi : Homo Dei, hæc dicit rex : Festina, descende.
      EN: Again he sent to him another captain of fifty, with fifty men. This one said to him, “Man of God, thus says the king: Hurry, come down!”

1:12  LA: Respondens Elias, ait : Si homo Dei ego sum, descendat ignis de cælo, et devoret te, et quinquaginta tuos. Descendit ergo ignis de cælo, et devoravit illum, et quinquaginta ejus.
      EN: Elijah answered, “If I am a man of God, let fire come down from heaven and consume you and your fifty men.” Fire came down from heaven and consumed him and his fifty men.

1:13  LA: Iterum misit principem quinquagenarium tertium, et quinquaginta qui erant cum eo. Qui cum venisset, curvavit genua contra Eliam, et precatus est eum, et ait : Homo Dei, noli despicere animam meam, et animas servorum tuorum qui mecum sunt.
      EN: He sent a third captain of fifty, with fifty men. When this one arrived, he bent his knees before Elijah and pleaded with him, saying, “Man of God, do not despise my life or the lives of your servants who are with me.

1:14  LA: Ecce descendit ignis de cælo, et devoravit duos principes quinquagenarios primos, et quinquagenos qui cum eis erant : sed nunc obsecro ut miserearis animæ meæ.
      EN: Look, fire has come down from heaven and consumed the first two captains of fifty and the fifty men with each of them. But now, I beg you, have mercy on my life.”

1:15  LA: Locutus est autem angelus Domini ad Eliam, dicens : Descende cum eo : ne timeas. Surrexit igitur, et descendit cum eo ad regem,
      EN: The angel of the Lord spoke to Elijah, saying, “Go down with him; do not be afraid.” So he rose and went down with him to the king,

1:16  LA: et locutus est ei : Hæc dicit Dominus : Quia misisti nuntios ad consulendum Beelzebub deum Accaron, quasi non esset Deus in Israël a quo posses interrogare sermonem, ideo de lectulo super quem ascendisti, non descendes, sed morte morieris.
      EN: and said to him, “Thus says the Lord: Because you sent messengers to consult Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron, as though there were no God in Israel from whom you could inquire for a word, therefore you will not come down from the bed you have climbed onto; you will certainly die.”

1:17  LA: Mortuus est ergo juxta sermonem Domini quem locutus est Elias, et regnavit Joram frater ejus pro eo, anno secundo Joram filii Josaphat regis Judæ : non enim habebat filium.
      EN: He died according to the word of the Lord that Elijah had spoken. His brother Joram reigned in his place, in the second year of Joram son of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, for Ahaziah had no son.

1:18  LA: Reliqua autem verborum Ochoziæ quæ operatus est, nonne hæc scripta sunt in libro sermonum dierum regum Israël ?
      EN: The rest of the acts of Ahaziah, what he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?

2 KINGS 2

2:1  LA: Factum est autem cum levare vellet Dominus Eliam per turbinem in cælum, ibant Elias et Eliseus de Galgalis.
     EN: When the Lord was about to take Elijah up into heaven in a whirlwind, Elijah and Elisha were traveling from Gilgal.

2:2  LA: Dixitque Elias ad Eliseum : Sede hic, quia Dominus misit me usque in Bethel. Cui ait Eliseus : Vivit Dominus, et vivit anima tua, quia non derelinquam te. Cumque descendissent Bethel,
     EN: Elijah said to Elisha, “Stay here, for the Lord has sent me as far as Bethel.” Elisha said to him, “As the Lord lives and as your soul lives, I will not abandon you.” When they had gone down to Bethel,

2:3  LA: egressi sunt filii prophetarum qui erant in Bethel, ad Eliseum, et dixerunt ei : Numquid nosti quia hodie Dominus tollet dominum tuum a te ? Qui respondit : Et ego novi : silete.
     EN: the sons of the prophets who were in Bethel came out to Elisha and said to him, “Do you know that today the Lord will take your master away from you?” He answered, “I know it too. Be silent.”

2:4  LA: Dixit autem Elias ad Eliseum : Sede hic, quia Dominus misit me in Jericho. Et ille ait : Vivit Dominus, et vivit anima tua, quia non derelinquam te. Cumque venissent Jericho,
     EN: Elijah said to Elisha, “Stay here, for the Lord has sent me to Jericho.” He said, “As the Lord lives and as your soul lives, I will not abandon you.” When they came to Jericho,

2:5  LA: accesserunt filii prophetarum qui erant in Jericho, ad Eliseum, et dixerunt ei : Numquid nosti quia Dominus hodie tollet dominum tuum a te ? Et ait : Et ego novi : silete.
     EN: the sons of the prophets in Jericho approached Elisha and said to him, “Do you know that today the Lord will take your master away from you?” He said, “I know it too. Be silent.”

2:6  LA: Dixit autem ei Elias : Sede hic, quia Dominus misit me usque ad Jordanem. Qui ait : Vivit Dominus, et vivit anima tua, quia non derelinquam te. Ierunt igitur ambo pariter,
     EN: Elijah said to him, “Stay here, for the Lord has sent me as far as the Jordan.” He said, “As the Lord lives and as your soul lives, I will not abandon you.” So the two went on together.

2:7  LA: et quinquaginta viri de filiis prophetarum secuti sunt eos, qui et steterunt e contra, longe : illi autem ambo stabant super Jordanem.
     EN: Fifty men from the sons of the prophets followed them and stood opposite at a distance, while the two stood beside the Jordan.

2:8  LA: Tulitque Elias pallium suum, et involvit illud, et percussit aquas : quæ divisæ sunt in utramque partem, et transierunt ambo per siccum.
     EN: Elijah took his cloak, rolled it up, and struck the waters. They divided to either side, and both men crossed on dry ground.

2:9  LA: Cumque transissent, Elias dixit ad Eliseum : Postula quod vis ut faciam tibi, antequam tollar a te. Dixitque Eliseus : Obsecro ut fiat in me duplex spiritus tuus.
     EN: When they had crossed, Elijah said to Elisha, “Ask what you wish me to do for you before I am taken away from you.” Elisha said, “I ask that a double measure of your spirit may be in me.”

2:10  LA: Qui respondit : Rem difficilem postulasti : attamen si videris me quando tollar a te, erit tibi quod petisti : si autem non videris, non erit.
      EN: He answered, “You have asked a difficult thing. Yet if you see me when I am taken away from you, what you have asked will be yours. But if you do not see me, it will not be.”

2:11  LA: Cumque pergerent, et incedentes sermocinarentur, ecce currus igneus, et equi ignei diviserunt utrumque : et ascendit Elias per turbinem in cælum.
      EN: As they continued on, walking and speaking together, suddenly a fiery chariot and fiery horses separated the two, and Elijah went up into heaven in a whirlwind.

2:12  LA: Eliseus autem videbat, et clamabat : Pater mi, pater mi, currus Israël, et auriga ejus. Et non vidit eum amplius : apprehenditque vestimenta sua, et scidit illa in duas partes.
      EN: Elisha saw it and cried out, “My father, my father! The chariot of Israel and its charioteer!” He saw him no more. He took hold of his clothes and tore them into two pieces.

2:13  LA: Et levavit pallium Eliæ, quod ceciderat ei : reversusque stetit super ripam Jordanis,
      EN: He picked up Elijah's cloak, which had fallen from him, returned, and stood on the bank of the Jordan.

2:14  LA: et pallio Eliæ, quod ceciderat ei, percussit aquas, et non sunt divisæ : et dixit : Ubi est Deus Eliæ etiam nunc ? Percussitque aquas, et divisæ sunt huc atque illuc, et transiit Eliseus.
      EN: He struck the waters with Elijah's cloak, which had fallen from him, but they did not divide. He said, “Where is the God of Elijah, even now?” He struck the waters, and they divided to this side and that, and Elisha crossed.

2:15  LA: Videntes autem filii prophetarum qui erant in Jericho e contra, dixerunt : Requievit spiritus Eliæ super Eliseum. Et venientes in occursum ejus, adoraverunt eum proni in terram,
      EN: The sons of the prophets at Jericho, watching from the opposite side, said, “The spirit of Elijah has rested upon Elisha.” They came to meet him and bowed facedown to the ground before him.

2:16  LA: dixeruntque illi : Ecce cum servis tuis sunt quinquaginta viri fortes qui possunt ire, et quærere dominum tuum, ne forte tulerit eum spiritus Domini, et projecerit eum in unum montium, aut in unam vallium. Qui ait : Nolite mittere.
      EN: They said to him, “Look, among your servants are fifty strong men who can go and search for your master. Perhaps the Spirit of the Lord has taken him up and cast him onto one of the mountains or into one of the valleys.” He said, “Do not send them.”

2:17  LA: Coëgeruntque eum donec acquiesceret, et diceret : Mittite. Et miserunt quinquaginta viros : qui cum quæsissent tribus diebus, non invenerunt.
      EN: They pressed him until he consented and said, “Send them.” They sent fifty men, who searched for three days but did not find him.

2:18  LA: Et reversi sunt ad eum : at ille habitabat in Jericho, et dixit eis : Numquid non dixi vobis : Nolite mittere ?
      EN: They returned to him while he was staying in Jericho. He said to them, “Did I not tell you, ‘Do not send them’?”

2:19  LA: Dixerunt quoque viri civitatis ad Eliseum : Ecce habitatio civitatis hujus optima est, sicut tu ipse, domine, perspicis : sed aquæ pessimæ sunt, et terra sterilis.
      EN: The men of the city also said to Elisha, “Look, this city is very well situated, as you yourself can see, my lord, but the water is very bad and the land is barren.”

2:20  LA: At ille ait : Afferte mihi vas novum, et mittite in illud sal. Quod cum attulissent,
      EN: He said, “Bring me a new vessel and put salt in it.” When they had brought it,

2:21  LA: egressus ad fontem aquarum misit in illum sal, et ait : Hæc dicit Dominus : Sanavi aquas has, et non erit ultra in eis mors, neque sterilitas.
      EN: he went out to the spring, threw the salt into it, and said, “Thus says the Lord: I have healed these waters. No longer will there be death or barrenness in them.”

2:22  LA: Sanatæ sunt ergo aquæ usque in diem hanc, juxta verbum Elisei quod locutus est.
      EN: The waters have remained healed to this day, according to the word Elisha spoke.

2:23  LA: Ascendit autem inde in Bethel : cumque ascenderet per viam, pueri parvi egressi sunt de civitate, et illudebant ei, dicentes : Ascende calve, ascende calve.
      EN: He went up from there to Bethel. As he was going up along the road, small boys came out of the city and mocked him, saying, “Go up, baldhead! Go up, baldhead!”

2:24  LA: Qui cum respexisset, vidit eos, et maledixit eis in nomine Domini : egressique sunt duo ursi de saltu, et laceraverunt ex eis quadraginta duos pueros.
      EN: He turned, saw them, and cursed them in the name of the Lord. Two bears came out of the woods and tore forty-two of the boys.

2:25  LA: Abiit autem inde in montem Carmeli, et inde reversus est in Samariam.
      EN: He went from there to Mount Carmel and then returned to Samaria.

2 KINGS 3

3:1  LA: Joram vero filius Achab regnavit super Israël in Samaria anno decimooctavo Josaphat regis Judæ : regnavitque duodecim annis.
     EN: Joram son of Ahab became king over Israel in Samaria in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned twelve years.

3:2  LA: Et fecit malum coram Domino, sed non sicut pater suus et mater : tulit enim statuas Baal quas fecerat pater ejus.
     EN: He did evil before the Lord, but not like his father and mother, for he removed the statues of Baal that his father had made.

3:3  LA: Verumtamen in peccatis Jeroboam filii Nabat, qui peccare fecit Israël, adhæsit, nec recessit ab eis.
     EN: Nevertheless, he clung to the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who made Israel sin, and did not turn away from them.

3:4  LA: Porro Mesa rex Moab nutriebat pecora multa, et solvebat regi Israël centum millia agnorum, et centum millia arietum cum velleribus suis.
     EN: Mesha king of Moab raised many sheep and paid the king of Israel a hundred thousand lambs and a hundred thousand rams with their fleeces.

3:5  LA: Cumque mortuus fuisset Achab, prævaricatus est fœdus quod habebat cum rege Israël.
     EN: When Ahab died, he broke the covenant he had with the king of Israel.

3:6  LA: Egressus est igitur rex Joram in die illa de Samaria, et recensuit universum Israël.
     EN: King Joram went out from Samaria that day and mustered all Israel.

3:7  LA: Misitque ad Josaphat regem Juda, dicens : Rex Moab recessit a me : veni mecum contra eum ad prælium. Qui respondit : Ascendam : qui meus est, tuus est : populus meus, populus tuus, et equi mei, equi tui.
     EN: He sent to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, saying, “The king of Moab has rebelled against me. Come with me to battle against him.” He answered, “I will go up. What is mine is yours, my people are your people, and my horses are your horses.”

3:8  LA: Dixitque : Per quam viam ascendemus ? At ille respondit : Per desertum Idumææ.
     EN: He asked, “By what road shall we go up?” The other answered, “Through the wilderness of Edom.”

3:9  LA: Perrexerunt igitur rex Israël, et rex Juda, et rex Edom, et circuierunt per viam septem dierum, nec erat aqua exercitui et jumentis quæ sequebantur eos.
     EN: The king of Israel, the king of Judah, and the king of Edom set out and made a circuit of seven days' journey. There was no water for the army or the animals following them.

3:10  LA: Dixitque rex Israël : Heu ! heu ! heu ! congregavit nos Dominus tres reges ut traderet in manus Moab.
      EN: The king of Israel said, “Alas, alas, alas! The Lord has gathered us three kings to hand us over to Moab.”

3:11  LA: Et ait Josaphat : Estne hic propheta Domini, ut deprecemur Dominum per eum ? Et respondit unus de servis regis Israël : Est hic Eliseus filius Saphat, qui fundebat aquam super manus Eliæ.
      EN: Jehoshaphat said, “Is there a prophet of the Lord here, through whom we may entreat the Lord?” One of the servants of the king of Israel answered, “Elisha son of Shaphat is here, who used to pour water over Elijah's hands.”

3:12  LA: Et ait Josaphat : Est apud eum sermo Domini. Descenditque ad eum rex Israël, et Josaphat rex Juda, et rex Edom.
      EN: Jehoshaphat said, “The word of the Lord is with him.” The king of Israel, Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and the king of Edom went down to him.

3:13  LA: Dixit autem Eliseus ad regem Israël : Quid mihi et tibi est ? vade ad prophetas patris tui et matris tuæ. Et ait illi rex Israël : Quare congregavit Dominus tres reges hos ut traderet eos in manus Moab ?
      EN: Elisha said to the king of Israel, “What have you to do with me? Go to the prophets of your father and mother.” The king of Israel said to him, “Why has the Lord gathered these three kings to hand them over to Moab?”

3:14  LA: Dixitque ad eum Eliseus : Vivit Dominus exercituum, in cujus conspectu sto, quod si non vultum Josaphat regis Judæ erubescerem, non attendissem quidem te, nec respexissem.
      EN: Elisha said to him, “As the Lord of hosts lives, before whom I stand, if I did not have regard for Jehoshaphat king of Judah, I would not pay attention to you or even look at you.

3:15  LA: Nunc autem adducite mihi psaltem. Cumque caneret psaltes, facta est super eum manus Domini, et ait :
      EN: But now bring me a musician.” As the musician played, the hand of the Lord came upon him, and he said:

3:16  LA: Hæc dicit Dominus : Facite alveum torrentis hujus fossas et fossas.
      EN: “Thus says the Lord: Make this torrent bed full of trench upon trench.

3:17  LA: Hæc enim dicit Dominus : Non videbitis ventum, neque pluviam : et alveus iste replebitur aquis, et bibetis vos, et familiæ vestræ, et jumenta vestra.
      EN: For thus says the Lord: You will see neither wind nor rain, yet this bed will be filled with water, and you, your households, and your livestock will drink.

3:18  LA: Parumque est hoc in conspectu Domini : insuper tradet etiam Moab in manus vestras.
      EN: This is a small thing in the Lord's sight; He will also hand Moab over to you.

3:19  LA: Et percutietis omnem civitatem munitam, et omnem urbem electam, et universum lignum fructiferum succidetis, cunctosque fontes aquarum obturabitis, et omnem agrum egregium operietis lapidibus.
      EN: You will strike every fortified city and every choice city, cut down every fruit-bearing tree, stop up all the springs of water, and cover every fine field with stones.”

3:20  LA: Factum est igitur mane, quando sacrificium offerri solet, et ecce aquæ veniebant per viam Edom, et repleta est terra aquis.
      EN: In the morning, at the customary time for offering sacrifice, water came by way of Edom, and the land was filled with water.

3:21  LA: Universi autem Moabitæ audientes quod ascendissent reges ut pugnarent adversum eos, convocaverunt omnes qui accincti erant balteo desuper, et steterunt in terminis.
      EN: All the Moabites heard that the kings had come up to fight against them. They summoned everyone who was girded with a sword belt and took their stand at the borders.

3:22  LA: Primoque mane surgentes, et orto jam sole ex adverso aquarum, viderunt Moabitæ e contra aquas rubras quasi sanguinem,
      EN: They rose early in the morning, and when the sun had risen opposite the waters, the Moabites saw the waters across from them looking red as blood.

3:23  LA: dixeruntque : Sanguis gladii est : pugnaverunt reges contra se, et cæsi sunt mutuo : nunc perge ad prædam, Moab.
      EN: They said, “It is blood from the sword! The kings have fought against one another and slaughtered each other. Now, Moab, go to the spoil!”

3:24  LA: Perrexeruntque in castra Israël : porro consurgens Israël, percussit Moab : at illi fugerunt coram eis. Venerunt igitur qui vicerant, et percusserunt Moab,
      EN: They went into Israel's camp, but Israel rose and struck Moab, and the Moabites fled before them. The victors came on and struck Moab.

3:25  LA: et civitates destruxerunt : et omnem agrum optimum, mittentes singuli lapides, repleverunt : et universos fontes aquarum obturaverunt : et omnia ligna fructifera succiderunt, ita ut muri tantum fictiles remanerent : et circumdata est civitas a fundibulariis, et magna ex parte percussa.
      EN: They destroyed the cities and filled every good field with stones, each man throwing one. They stopped up all the springs of water and cut down all the fruit-bearing trees, until only the earthen walls remained. The city was surrounded by slingers and largely battered down.

3:26  LA: Quod cum vidisset rex Moab, prævaluisse scilicet hostes, tulit secum septingentos viros educentes gladium, ut irrumperent ad regem Edom : et non potuerunt.
      EN: When the king of Moab saw that the enemies had prevailed, he took seven hundred men who drew the sword with him, to break through to the king of Edom, but they could not.

3:27  LA: Arripiensque filium suum primogenitum, qui regnaturus erat pro eo, obtulit holocaustum super murum : et facta est indignatio magna in Israël, statimque recesserunt ab eo, et reversi sunt in terram suam.
      EN: He seized his firstborn son, who was to reign in his place, and offered him as a burnt offering upon the wall. Great indignation arose in Israel, and they immediately withdrew from him and returned to their land.

2 KINGS 4

4:1  LA: Mulier autem quædam de uxoribus prophetarum clamabat ad Eliseum, dicens : Servus tuus vir meus mortuus est, et tu nosti quia servus tuus fuit timens Dominum : et ecce creditor venit ut tollat duos filios meos ad serviendum sibi.
     EN: A woman, one of the prophets' wives, cried out to Elisha, saying, “Your servant, my husband, has died. You know that your servant feared the Lord. Now a creditor has come to take my two sons to serve him.”

4:2  LA: Cui dixit Eliseus : Quid vis ut faciam tibi ? dic mihi, quid habes in domo tua ? At illa respondit : Non habeo ancilla tua quidquam in domo mea, nisi parum olei quo ungar.
     EN: Elisha said to her, “What do you wish me to do for you? Tell me, what do you have in your house?” She answered, “Your servant has nothing in my house except a little oil with which to anoint myself.”

4:3  LA: Cui ait : Vade, pete mutuo ab omnibus vicinis tuis vasa vacua non pauca,
     EN: He said to her, “Go and borrow empty vessels from all your neighbors, and not just a few.

4:4  LA: et ingredere, et claude ostium tuum cum intrinsecus fueris tu, et filii tui : et mitte inde in omnia vasa hæc, et cum plena fuerint, tolles.
     EN: Then go inside and shut your door when you and your sons are within. Pour some of the oil into all these vessels, and set them aside when they are full.”

4:5  LA: Ivit itaque mulier, et clausit ostium super se, et super filios suos : illi offerebant vasa, et illa infundebat.
     EN: The woman went and shut the door behind herself and her sons. They brought her the vessels, and she poured.

4:6  LA: Cumque plena fuissent vasa, dixit ad filium suum : Affer mihi adhuc vas. Et ille respondit : Non habeo. Stetitque oleum.
     EN: When the vessels were full, she said to her son, “Bring me another vessel.” He answered, “I have none.” And the oil stopped.

4:7  LA: Venit autem illa, et indicavit homini Dei. Et ille : Vade, inquit, vende oleum, et redde creditori tuo : tu autem, et filii tui vivite de reliquo.
     EN: She came and told the man of God. He said, “Go, sell the oil, and pay your creditor. You and your sons can live on what remains.”

     EN: One of the officers on whose hand the king leaned answered the man of God, saying, “Even if the Lord were to make floodgates in heaven, could what you say happen?” He said, “You will see it with your own eyes, but you will not eat any of it.”

     EN: For the Lord had caused the Syrian camp to hear the sound of chariots, horses, and a very great army. They had said to one another, “Look, the king of Israel has hired the kings of the Hittites and the Egyptians against us, and they have come upon us!”

       EN: She saw the king standing on the platform according to custom, with the singers and trumpets near him, and all the people of the land rejoicing and sounding trumpets. She tore her clothes and cried, “Conspiracy! Conspiracy!”

11:15  LA: Præcepit autem Jojada centurionibus qui erant super exercitum, et ait eis : Educite eam extra septa templi, et quicumque eam secutus fuerit, feriatur gladio. Dixerat enim sacerdos : Non occidatur in templo Domini.
       EN: Jehoiada commanded the captains of hundreds who were over the army, saying, “Bring her out beyond the temple enclosure, and let whoever follows her be struck with the sword.” For the priest had said, “She must not be killed in the Lord's temple.”

11:16  LA: Imposueruntque ei manus, et impegerunt eam per viam introitus equorum, juxta palatium, et interfecta est ibi.
       EN: They laid hands on her and drove her along the horses' entrance road beside the palace, and she was killed there.

11:17  LA: Pepigit ergo Jojada fœdus inter Dominum, et inter regem, et inter populum, ut esset populus Domini : et inter regem et populum.
       EN: Jehoiada made a covenant between the Lord, the king, and the people, that they should be the Lord's people, and between the king and the people.

11:18  LA: Ingressusque est omnis populus terræ templum Baal, et destruxerunt aras ejus, et imagines contriverunt valide : Mathan quoque sacerdotem Baal occiderunt coram altari. Et posuit sacerdos custodias in domo Domini.
       EN: All the people of the land entered Baal's temple, destroyed his altars, and thoroughly shattered his images. They also killed Mattan, Baal's priest, before the altar. The priest stationed guards at the Lord's house.

11:19  LA: Tulitque centuriones, et Cerethi et Phelethi legiones, et omnem populum terræ, deduxeruntque regem de domo Domini : et venerunt per viam portæ scutariorum in palatium, et sedit super thronum regum.
       EN: He took the captains of hundreds, the companies of Cherethites and Pelethites, and all the people of the land. They brought the king down from the Lord's house and came to the palace by the road through the Gate of the Guards. He sat on the throne of the kings.

11:20  LA: Lætatusque est omnis populus terræ, et civitas conquievit : Athalia autem occisa est gladio in domo regis.
       EN: All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet. Athaliah had been killed with the sword in the king's house.

2 KINGS 12

12:1  LA: Septemque annorum erat Joas, cum regnare cœpisset.
      EN: Joash was seven years old when he began to reign.

12:2  LA: Anno septimo Jehu, regnavit Joas : et quadraginta annis regnavit in Jerusalem. Nomen matris ejus Sebia de Bersabee.
      EN: In the seventh year of Jehu, Joash became king, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Zibiah of Beersheba.

12:3  LA: Fecitque Joas rectum coram Domino cunctis diebus quibus docuit eum Jojada sacerdos.
      EN: Joash did what was right before the Lord throughout all the days that Jehoiada the priest instructed him.

12:4  LA: Verumtamen excelsa non abstulit : adhuc enim populus immolabat, et adolebat in excelsis incensum.
      EN: Yet he did not remove the high places, for the people still sacrificed and burned incense at the high places.

12:5  LA: Dixitque Joas ad sacerdotes : Omnem pecuniam sanctorum, quæ illata fuerit in templum Domini a prætereuntibus, quæ offertur pro pretio animæ, et quam sponte et arbitrio cordis sui inferunt in templum Domini :
      EN: Joash said to the priests, “All the money for sacred purposes brought into the Lord's temple by those passing through, the money offered as the price of a life, and what people willingly bring into the Lord's temple as their hearts decide,

12:6  LA: accipiant illam sacerdotes juxta ordinem suum, et instaurent sartatecta domus, si quid necessarium viderint instauratione.
      EN: let the priests receive it according to their order, and let them repair the fabric of the house wherever they see repairs are needed.”

12:7  LA: Igitur usque ad vigesimum tertium annum regis Joas, non instauraverunt sacerdotes sartatecta templi.
      EN: But until the twenty-third year of King Joash, the priests had not repaired the fabric of the temple.

12:8  LA: Vocavitque rex Joas Jojadam pontificem et sacerdotes, dicens eis : Quare sartatecta non instauratis templi ? nolite ergo amplius accipere pecuniam juxta ordinem vestrum, sed ad instaurationem templi reddite eam.
      EN: King Joash called Jehoiada the high priest and the priests, and said to them, “Why are you not repairing the fabric of the temple? No longer receive money according to your order, but hand it over for repairing the temple.”

12:9  LA: Prohibitique sunt sacerdotes ultra accipere pecuniam a populo, et instaurare sartatecta domus.
      EN: The priests were forbidden to receive any more money from the people or to repair the fabric of the house.

12:10  LA: Et tulit Jojada pontifex gazophylacium unum, aperuitque foramen desuper, et posuit illud juxta altare ad dexteram ingredientium domum Domini : mittebantque in eo sacerdotes qui custodiebant ostia, omnem pecuniam quæ deferebatur ad templum Domini.
       EN: Jehoiada the high priest took a chest, made a hole in its top, and placed it beside the altar, to the right of those entering the Lord's house. The priests guarding the doors put into it all the money brought to the Lord's temple.

12:11  LA: Cumque viderent nimiam pecuniam esse in gazophylacio, ascendebat scriba regis, et pontifex, effundebantque et numerabant pecuniam quæ inveniebatur in domo Domini :
       EN: When they saw that there was a great deal of money in the chest, the king's scribe and the high priest came up, poured out the money found in the Lord's house, and counted it.

12:12  LA: et dabant eam juxta numerum atque mensuram in manu eorum qui præerant cæmentariis domus Domini : qui impendebant eam in fabris lignorum et in cæmentariis, iis qui operabantur in domo Domini,
       EN: They handed it over by number and weight to those supervising the builders of the Lord's house. These spent it on the carpenters and masons working in the Lord's house

12:13  LA: et sartatecta faciebant : et in iis qui cædebant saxa, et ut emerent ligna, et lapides, qui excidebantur, ita ut impleretur instauratio domus Domini in universis quæ indigebant expensa ad muniendam domum.
       EN: and making repairs, on the stonecutters, and on buying timber and cut stone, so that the repair of the Lord's house could be completed with everything requiring expenditure to strengthen it.

12:14  LA: Verumtamen non fiebant ex eadem pecunia hydriæ templi Domini, et fuscinulæ, et thuribula, et tubæ, et omne vas aureum et argenteum, de pecunia quæ inferebatur in templum Domini.
       EN: But no water jars for the Lord's temple, forks, censers, trumpets, or any gold or silver vessel were made from that money brought into the Lord's temple.

12:15  LA: Iis enim qui faciebant opus, dabatur ut instauraretur templum Domini :
       EN: It was given to those doing the work, so that the Lord's temple could be repaired.

12:16  LA: et non fiebat ratio iis hominibus qui accipiebant pecuniam ut distribuerent eam artificibus, sed in fide tractabant eam.
       EN: No accounting was required from the men who received the money to distribute to the craftsmen, for they handled it faithfully.

12:17  LA: Pecuniam vero pro delicto, et pecuniam pro peccatis non inferebant in templum Domini, quia sacerdotum erat.
       EN: But the money for guilt and the money for sins was not brought into the Lord's temple, because it belonged to the priests.

12:18  LA: Tunc ascendit Hazaël rex Syriæ, et pugnabat contra Geth, cepitque eam : et direxit faciem suam ut ascenderet in Jerusalem.
       EN: Then Hazael king of Syria came up and fought against Gath and captured it. He set his face toward going up to Jerusalem.

12:19  LA: Quam ob rem tulit Joas rex Juda omnia sanctificata quæ consecraverant Josaphat, et Joram, et Ochozias, patres ejus reges Juda, et quæ ipse obtulerat : et universum argentum quod inveniri potuit in thesauris templi Domini et in palatio regis : misitque Hazaëli regi Syriæ, et recessit ab Jerusalem.
       EN: Therefore Joash king of Judah took all the sacred gifts consecrated by his fathers Jehoshaphat, Joram, and Ahaziah, kings of Judah, and those he himself had offered, together with all the silver that could be found in the treasuries of the Lord's temple and the king's palace. He sent it to Hazael king of Syria, who withdrew from Jerusalem.

12:20  LA: Reliqua autem sermonum Joas, et universa quæ fecit, nonne hæc scripta sunt in libro verborum dierum regum Juda ?
       EN: The rest of the acts of Joash, and everything he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

12:21  LA: Surrexerunt autem servi ejus, et conjuraverunt inter se, percusseruntque Joas in domo Mello in descensu Sella.
       EN: His servants rose and conspired together and struck Joash in the house of Millo, on the descent to Silla.

12:22  LA: Josachar namque filius Semaath, et Jozabad filius Somer servi ejus, percusserunt eum, et mortuus est : et sepelierunt eum cum patribus suis in civitate David : regnavitque Amasias filius ejus pro eo.
       EN: His servants Jozacar son of Shimeath and Jehozabad son of Shomer struck him, and he died. They buried him with his fathers in the City of David, and his son Amaziah reigned in his place.

2 KINGS 13

13:1  LA: Anno vigesimo tertio Joas filii Ochoziæ regis Juda, regnavit Joachaz filius Jehu super Israël in Samaria decem et septem annis.
      EN: In the twenty-third year of Joash son of Ahaziah, king of Judah, Jehoahaz son of Jehu became king over Israel in Samaria for seventeen years.

13:2  LA: Et fecit malum coram Domino, secutusque est peccata Jeroboam filii Nabat, qui peccare fecit Israël, et non declinavit ab eis.
      EN: He did evil before the Lord and followed the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who made Israel sin, and did not turn away from them.

13:3  LA: Iratusque est furor Domini contra Israël, et tradidit eos in manu Hazaël regis Syriæ, et in manu Benadad filii Hazaël, cunctis diebus.
      EN: The Lord's fury burned against Israel, and He handed them over to Hazael king of Syria and to Ben-hadad son of Hazael throughout those days.

13:4  LA: Deprecatus est autem Joachaz faciem Domini, et audivit eum Dominus : vidit enim angustiam Israël, quia attriverat eos rex Syriæ :
      EN: But Jehoahaz entreated the Lord's favor, and the Lord heard him, for He saw Israel's distress, because the king of Syria had crushed them.

13:5  LA: et dedit Dominus salvatorem Israëli, et liberatus est de manu regis Syriæ : habitaveruntque filii Israël in tabernaculis suis sicut heri et nudiustertius.
      EN: The Lord gave Israel a deliverer, and Israel was freed from the hand of the king of Syria. The children of Israel lived in their tents as they had before.

13:6  LA: Verumtamen non recesserunt a peccatis domus Jeroboam, qui peccare fecit Israël, sed in ipsis ambulaverunt : siquidem et lucus permansit in Samaria.
      EN: Yet they did not turn away from the sins of the house of Jeroboam, who made Israel sin, but walked in them. The sacred grove also remained in Samaria.

13:7  LA: Et non sunt derelicti Joachaz de populo nisi quinquaginta equites, et decem currus, et decem millia peditum : interfecerat enim eos rex Syriæ, et redegerat quasi pulverem in tritura areæ.
      EN: No more than fifty horsemen, ten chariots, and ten thousand infantry were left to Jehoahaz from the people, for the king of Syria had killed them and made them like dust at the threshing on the threshing floor.

13:8  LA: Reliqua autem sermonum Joachaz, et universa quæ fecit, et fortitudo ejus, nonne hæc scripta sunt in libro sermonum dierum regum Israël ?
      EN: The rest of the acts of Jehoahaz, everything he did, and his might, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?

13:9  LA: Dormivitque Joachaz cum patribus suis, et sepelierunt eum in Samaria : regnavitque Joas filius ejus pro eo.
      EN: Jehoahaz slept with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria. His son Joash reigned in his place.

13:10  LA: Anno trigesimo septimo Joas regis Juda, regnavit Joas filius Joachaz super Israël in Samaria sedecim annis.
       EN: In the thirty-seventh year of Joash king of Judah, Joash son of Jehoahaz became king over Israel in Samaria for sixteen years.

13:11  LA: Et fecit quod malum est in conspectu Domini : non declinavit ab omnibus peccatis Jeroboam filii Nabat, qui peccare fecit Israël, sed in ipsis ambulavit.
       EN: He did what was evil in the Lord's sight. He did not turn away from any of the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who made Israel sin, but walked in them.

13:12  LA: Reliqua autem sermonum Joas, et universa quæ fecit, et fortitudo ejus, quomodo pugnaverit contra Amasiam regem Juda, nonne hæc scripta sunt in libro sermonum dierum regum Israël ?
       EN: The rest of the acts of Joash, everything he did, and his might, how he fought against Amaziah king of Judah, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?

13:13  LA: Et dormivit Joas cum patribus suis : Jeroboam autem sedit super solium ejus. Porro Joas sepultus est in Samaria cum regibus Israël.
       EN: Joash slept with his fathers, and Jeroboam sat on his throne. Joash was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel.

13:14  LA: Eliseus autem ægrotabat infirmitate, qua et mortuus est : descenditque ad eum Joas rex Israël, et flebat coram eo, dicebatque : Pater mi, pater mi, currus Israël et auriga ejus.
       EN: Elisha was ill with the sickness from which he died. Joash king of Israel went down to him and wept before him, saying, “My father, my father! The chariot of Israel and its charioteer!”

13:15  LA: Et ait illi Eliseus : Affer arcum et sagittas. Cumque attulisset ad eum arcum et sagittas,
       EN: Elisha said to him, “Bring a bow and arrows.” When he had brought him a bow and arrows,

13:16  LA: dixit ad regem Israël : Pone manum tuam super arcum. Et cum posuisset ille manum suam, superposuit Eliseus manus suas manibus regis,
       EN: he said to the king of Israel, “Place your hand on the bow.” When he placed his hand on it, Elisha laid his own hands over the king's hands

13:17  LA: et ait : Aperi fenestram orientalem. Cumque aperuisset, dixit Eliseus : Jace sagittam. Et jecit. Et ait Eliseus : Sagitta salutis Domini, et sagitta salutis contra Syriam : percutiesque Syriam in Aphec, donec consumas eam.
       EN: and said, “Open the eastern window.” When he opened it, Elisha said, “Shoot an arrow.” He shot it, and Elisha said, “The Lord's arrow of deliverance, the arrow of deliverance against Syria! You will strike Syria at Aphek until you consume it.”

13:18  LA: Et ait : Tolle sagittas. Qui cum tulisset, rursum dixit ei : Percute jaculo terram. Et cum percussisset tribus vicibus, et stetisset,
       EN: He said, “Take the arrows.” When he took them, Elisha said to him again, “Strike the ground with an arrow.” He struck three times and stopped.

13:19  LA: iratus est vir Dei contra eum, et ait : Si percussisses quinquies, aut sexies, sive septies, percussisses Syriam usque ad consumptionem : nunc autem tribus vicibus percuties eam.
       EN: The man of God became angry with him and said, “If you had struck five or six or seven times, you would have struck Syria until it was consumed. But now you will strike it three times.”

13:20  LA: Mortuus est ergo Eliseus, et sepelierunt eum. Latrunculi autem de Moab venerunt in terram in ipso anno.
       EN: Elisha died, and they buried him. Raiding bands from Moab came into the land in that very year.

13:21  LA: Quidam autem sepelientes hominem, viderunt latrunculos, et projecerunt cadaver in sepulchro Elisei. Quod cum tetigisset ossa Elisei, revixit homo, et stetit super pedes suos.
       EN: Some men burying a man saw the raiders and threw the corpse into Elisha's tomb. When it touched Elisha's bones, the man came back to life and stood on his feet.

13:22  LA: Igitur Hazaël rex Syriæ afflixit Israël cunctis diebus Joachaz :
       EN: Hazael king of Syria afflicted Israel throughout all the days of Jehoahaz.

13:23  LA: et misertus est Dominus eorum, et reversus est ad eos propter pactum suum, quod habebat cum Abraham, et Isaac, et Jacob : et noluit disperdere eos, neque projicere penitus usque in præsens tempus.
       EN: But the Lord had mercy on them and turned toward them because of His covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. He was unwilling to destroy them or cast them away completely, even to the present time.

13:24  LA: Mortuus est autem Hazaël rex Syriæ, et regnavit Benadad filius ejus pro eo.
       EN: Hazael king of Syria died, and his son Ben-hadad reigned in his place.

13:25  LA: Porro Joas filius Joachaz tulit urbes de manu Benadad filii Hazaël, quas tulerat de manu Joachaz patris sui jure prælii : tribus vicibus percussit eum Joas, et reddidit civitates Israël.
       EN: Joash son of Jehoahaz recovered from Ben-hadad son of Hazael the cities he had taken from his father Jehoahaz by conquest. Joash struck him three times and restored the cities to Israel.

2 KINGS 14

14:1  LA: In anno secundo Joas filii Joachaz regis Israël, regnavit Amasias filius Joas regis Juda.
      EN: In the second year of Joash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel, Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, began to reign.

14:2  LA: Viginti quinque annorum erat cum regnare cœpisset : viginti autem et novem annis regnavit in Jerusalem. Nomen matris ejus Joadan de Jerusalem.
      EN: He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Jehoaddan of Jerusalem.

14:3  LA: Et fecit rectum coram Domino, verumtamen non ut David pater ejus. Juxta omnia quæ fecit Joas pater suus, fecit :
      EN: He did what was right before the Lord, though not like his father David. He acted according to everything his father Joash had done,

14:4  LA: nisi hoc tantum, quod excelsa non abstulit : adhuc enim populus immolabat, et adolebat incensum in excelsis.
      EN: except only that he did not remove the high places, for the people still sacrificed and burned incense at the high places.

14:5  LA: Cumque obtinuisset regnum, percussit servos suos, qui interfecerant regem patrem suum :
      EN: When he had secured the kingdom, he struck down the servants who had killed his father the king.

14:6  LA: filios autem eorum qui occiderant, non occidit, juxta quod scriptum est in libro legis Moysi, sicut præcepit Dominus, dicens : Non morientur patres pro filiis, neque filii morientur pro patribus : sed unusquisque in peccato suo morietur.
      EN: But he did not kill the murderers' sons, according to what is written in the scroll of the law of Moses, as the Lord commanded: “Fathers must not die for their sons, nor sons for their fathers; each must die for his own sin.”

14:7  LA: Ipse percussit Edom in valle Salinarum decem millia, et apprehendit petram in prælio, vocavitque nomen ejus Jectehel usque in præsentem diem.
      EN: He struck down ten thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt, captured the Rock in battle, and called it Joktheel, its name to this day.

14:8  LA: Tunc misit Amasias nuntios ad Joas filium Joachaz filii Jehu regis Israël, dicens : Veni, et videamus nos.
      EN: Then Amaziah sent messengers to Joash son of Jehoahaz, son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying, “Come, let us face one another.”

14:9  LA: Remisitque Joas rex Israël ad Amasiam regem Juda, dicens : Carduus Libani misit ad cedrum quæ est in Libano, dicens : Da filiam tuam filio meo uxorem. Transieruntque bestiæ saltus quæ sunt in Libano, et conculcaverunt carduum.
      EN: Joash king of Israel sent back to Amaziah king of Judah, saying, “The thistle of Lebanon sent to the cedar in Lebanon, saying, ‘Give your daughter to my son as a wife.’ But the wild beasts of Lebanon passed by and trampled the thistle.

14:10  LA: Percutiens invaluisti super Edom, et sublevavit te cor tuum : contentus esto gloria, et sede in domo tua : quare provocas malum, ut cadas tu et Judas tecum ?
       EN: You have struck Edom and prevailed, and your heart has lifted you up. Be content with your glory and stay at home. Why provoke disaster, so that you fall, and Judah with you?”

14:11  LA: Et non acquievit Amasias. Ascenditque Joas rex Israël, et viderunt se, ipse et Amasias rex Juda, in Bethsames oppido Judæ.
       EN: Amaziah would not agree. Joash king of Israel came up, and he and Amaziah king of Judah faced one another at Beth-shemesh, a town of Judah.

14:12  LA: Percussusque est Juda coram Israël, et fugerunt unusquisque in tabernacula sua.
       EN: Judah was defeated before Israel, and each man fled to his tent.

14:13  LA: Amasiam vero regem Juda, filium Joas filii Ochoziæ, cepit Joas rex Israël in Bethsames, et adduxit eum in Jerusalem : et interrupit murum Jerusalem, a porta Ephraim usque ad portam anguli, quadringentis cubitis.
       EN: Joash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, son of Joash, son of Ahaziah, at Beth-shemesh and brought him to Jerusalem. He broke down four hundred cubits of Jerusalem's wall, from the Gate of Ephraim to the Corner Gate.

14:14  LA: Tulitque omne aurum et argentum, et universa vasa quæ inventa sunt in domo Domini et in thesauris regis, et obsides, et reversus est in Samariam.
       EN: He took all the gold, silver, and vessels found in the Lord's house and in the king's treasuries, together with hostages, and returned to Samaria.

14:15  LA: Reliqua autem verborum Joas quæ fecit, et fortitudo ejus qua pugnavit contra Amasiam regem Juda, nonne hæc scripta sunt in libro sermonum dierum regum Israël ?
       EN: The rest of the acts of Joash, what he did, and his might in fighting against Amaziah king of Judah, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?

14:16  LA: Dormivitque Joas cum patribus suis, et sepultus est in Samaria cum regibus Israël, et regnavit Jeroboam filius ejus pro eo.
       EN: Joash slept with his fathers and was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel. His son Jeroboam reigned in his place.

14:17  LA: Vixit autem Amasias filius Joas rex Juda postquam mortuus est Joas filius Joachaz regis Israël, quindecim annis.
       EN: Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, lived fifteen years after the death of Joash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel.

14:18  LA: Reliqua autem sermonum Amasiæ, nonne hæc scripta sunt in libro sermonum dierum regum Juda ?
       EN: The rest of the acts of Amaziah, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

14:19  LA: Factaque est contra eum conjuratio in Jerusalem : at ille fugit in Lachis. Miseruntque post eum in Lachis, et interfecerunt eum ibi :
       EN: A conspiracy was formed against him in Jerusalem, and he fled to Lachish. They sent after him to Lachish and killed him there.

14:20  LA: et asportaverunt in equis, sepultusque est in Jerusalem cum patribus suis in civitate David.
       EN: They carried him back on horses, and he was buried in Jerusalem with his fathers in the City of David.

14:21  LA: Tulit autem universus populus Judæ Azariam annos natum sedecim, et constituerunt eum regem pro patre ejus Amasia.
       EN: All the people of Judah took Azariah, who was sixteen years old, and made him king in place of his father Amaziah.

14:22  LA: Ipse ædificavit Ælath, et restituit eam Judæ, postquam dormivit rex cum patribus suis.
       EN: He built Elath and restored it to Judah after the king had slept with his fathers.

14:23  LA: Anno quintodecimo Amasiæ filii Joas regis Juda, regnavit Jeroboam filius Joas regis Israël in Samaria, quadraginta et uno anno.
       EN: In the fifteenth year of Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel, began to reign in Samaria for forty-one years.

14:24  LA: Et fecit quod malum est coram Domino : non recessit ab omnibus peccatis Jeroboam filii Nabat, qui peccare fecit Israël.
       EN: He did what was evil before the Lord. He did not turn away from any of the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who made Israel sin.

14:25  LA: Ipse restituit terminos Israël ab introitu Emath usque ad mare solitudinis, juxta sermonem Domini Dei Israël quem locutus est per servum suum Jonam filium Amathi prophetam, qui erat de Geth, quæ est in Opher.
       EN: He restored Israel's borders from the entrance to Hamath as far as the Sea of the Wilderness, according to the word the Lord, the God of Israel, had spoken through His servant Jonah son of Amittai, the prophet from Gath, which is in Hepher.

14:26  LA: Vidit enim Dominus afflictionem Israël amaram nimis, et quod consumpti essent usque ad clausos carcere et extremos, et non esset qui auxiliaretur Israëli.
       EN: For the Lord saw Israel's very bitter affliction, that they had been consumed, even those shut in prison and the last remaining, and that there was no one to help Israel.

14:27  LA: Nec locutus est Dominus ut deleret nomen Israël de sub cælo, sed salvavit eos in manu Jeroboam filii Joas.
       EN: The Lord had not spoken of blotting out Israel's name from under heaven, but saved them through Jeroboam son of Joash.

14:28  LA: Reliqua autem sermonum Jeroboam, et universa quæ fecit, et fortitudo ejus qua præliatus est, et quomodo restituit Damascum et Emath Judæ in Israël, nonne hæc scripta sunt in libro sermonum dierum regum Israël ?

15:31  LA: Reliqua autem sermonum Phacee, et universa quæ fecit, nonne hæc scripta sunt in libro sermonum dierum regum Israël ?
       EN: The rest of the acts of Pekah, and everything he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?

2 KINGS 16

16:1  LA: Anno decimoseptimo Phacee filii Romeliæ, regnavit Achaz filius Joatham regis Juda.
      EN: In the seventeenth year of Pekah son of Remaliah, Ahaz son of Jotham, king of Judah, began to reign.

16:2  LA: Viginti annorum erat Achaz cum regnare cœpisset, et sedecim annis regnavit in Jerusalem. Non fecit quod erat placitum in conspectu Domini Dei sui sicut David pater ejus,
      EN: Ahaz was twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. He did not do what was pleasing in the sight of the Lord his God, as his father David had done,

16:3  LA: sed ambulavit in via regum Israël : insuper et filium suum consecravit, transferens per ignem secundum idola gentium, quæ dissipavit Dominus coram filiis Israël.
      EN: but walked in the way of the kings of Israel. He even consecrated his son by passing him through fire, according to the idols of the nations the Lord had scattered before the children of Israel.

16:4  LA: Immolabat quoque victimas, et adolebat incensum in excelsis, et in collibus, et sub omni ligno frondoso.
      EN: He also offered sacrifices and burned incense at the high places, on the hills, and under every leafy tree.

16:5  LA: Tunc ascendit Rasin rex Syriæ, et Phacee filius Romeliæ rex Israël, in Jerusalem ad præliandum : cumque obsiderent Achaz, non valuerunt superare eum.
      EN: Then Rezin king of Syria and Pekah son of Remaliah, king of Israel, came up to Jerusalem to fight. They besieged Ahaz but could not overcome him.

16:6  LA: In tempore illo restituit Rasin rex Syriæ, Ailam Syriæ, et ejecit Judæos de Aila : et Idumæi venerunt in Ailam, et habitaverunt ibi usque in diem hanc.
      EN: At that time Rezin king of Syria restored Elath to Syria and drove the Jews out of Elath. The Edomites came to Elath and have lived there to this day.

16:7  LA: Misit autem Achaz nuntios ad Theglathphalasar regem Assyriorum, dicens : Servus tuus, et filius tuus ego sum : ascende, et salvum me fac de manu regis Syriæ, et de manu regis Israël, qui consurrexerunt adversum me.
      EN: Ahaz sent messengers to Tiglath-pileser king of the Assyrians, saying, “I am your servant and your son. Come up and save me from the hand of the king of Syria and the king of Israel, who have risen against me.”

16:8  LA: Et cum collegisset argentum et aurum quod inveniri potuit in domo Domini et in thesauris regis, misit regi Assyriorum munera.
      EN: He gathered the silver and gold that could be found in the Lord's house and the king's treasuries and sent gifts to the king of the Assyrians.

16:9  LA: Qui et acquievit voluntati ejus : ascendit enim rex Assyriorum in Damascum, et vastavit eam, et transtulit habitatores ejus Cyrenen : Rasin autem interfecit.
      EN: He agreed to his request. The king of the Assyrians went up to Damascus, devastated it, and deported its inhabitants to Cyrene. He killed Rezin.

16:10  LA: Perrexitque rex Achaz in occursum Theglathphalasar regis Assyriorum in Damascum : cumque vidisset altare Damasci, misit rex Achaz ad Uriam sacerdotem exemplar ejus, et similitudinem juxta omne opus ejus.
       EN: King Ahaz went to Damascus to meet Tiglath-pileser king of the Assyrians. When he saw the altar in Damascus, King Ahaz sent Uriah the priest a model of it and a likeness of all its workmanship.

16:11  LA: Exstruxitque Urias sacerdos altare : juxta omnia quæ præceperat rex Achaz de Damasco, ita fecit sacerdos Urias donec veniret rex Achaz de Damasco.
       EN: Uriah the priest built an altar. According to everything King Ahaz had commanded from Damascus, Uriah the priest made it before King Ahaz returned from Damascus.

16:12  LA: Cumque venisset rex de Damasco, vidit altare, et veneratus est illud : ascenditque et immolavit holocausta et sacrificium suum,
       EN: When the king came from Damascus, he saw the altar and revered it. He went up and offered burnt offerings and his sacrifice,

16:13  LA: et libavit libamina, et fudit sanguinem pacificorum quæ obtulerat super altare.
       EN: poured out drink offerings, and poured the blood of the peace offerings he had offered upon the altar.

16:14  LA: Porro altare æreum quod erat coram Domino, transtulit de facie templi, et de loco altaris, et de loco templi Domini : posuitque illud ex latere altaris ad aquilonem.
       EN: He moved the bronze altar that stood before the Lord away from the front of the temple, from the place of the altar and the place of the Lord's temple, and put it on the north side of the altar.

16:15  LA: Præcepit quoque rex Achaz Uriæ sacerdoti, dicens : Super altare majus offer holocaustum matutinum, et sacrificium vespertinum, et holocaustum regis, et sacrificium ejus, et holocaustum universi populi terræ, et sacrificia eorum, et libamina eorum : et omnem sanguinem holocausti, et universum sanguinem victimæ super illud effundes : altare vero æreum erit paratum ad voluntatem meam.
       EN: King Ahaz commanded Uriah the priest, “On the larger altar offer the morning burnt offering, the evening sacrifice, the king's burnt offering and sacrifice, and the burnt offerings, sacrifices, and drink offerings of all the people of the land. Pour all the blood of the burnt offering and all the blood of the sacrifice upon it. But the bronze altar will be kept ready at my pleasure.”

16:16  LA: Fecit igitur Urias sacerdos juxta omnia quæ præceperat rex Achaz.
       EN: Uriah the priest did everything King Ahaz commanded.

16:17  LA: Tulit autem rex Achaz cælatas bases, et luterem qui erat desuper : et mare deposuit de bobus æreis qui sustentabant illud, et posuit super pavimentum stratum lapide.
       EN: King Ahaz removed the carved bases and the basin that was above them. He took the sea down from the bronze oxen supporting it and placed it upon a stone pavement.

16:18  LA: Musach quoque sabbati quod ædificaverat in templo : et ingressum regis exterius convertit in templum Domini propter regem Assyriorum.
       EN: He also altered the Sabbath Musach, which he had built in the temple, and the king's outer entrance into the Lord's temple, because of the king of the Assyrians.

16:19  LA: Reliqua autem verborum Achaz quæ fecit, nonne hæc scripta sunt in libro sermonum dierum regum Juda ?
       EN: The rest of the acts of Ahaz, what he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

16:20  LA: Dormivitque Achaz cum patribus suis, et sepultus est cum eis in civitate David : et regnavit Ezechias filius ejus pro eo.
       EN: Ahaz slept with his fathers and was buried with them in the City of David. His son Hezekiah reigned in his place.

2 KINGS 17

17:1  LA: Anno duodecimo Achaz regis Juda, regnavit Osee filius Ela in Samaria super Israël novem annis.
      EN: In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah, Hoshea son of Elah began to reign in Samaria over Israel for nine years.

17:2  LA: Fecitque malum coram Domino, sed non sicut reges Israël qui ante eum fuerant.
      EN: He did evil before the Lord, but not like the kings of Israel before him.

17:3  LA: Contra hunc ascendit Salmanasar rex Assyriorum, et factus est ei Osee servus, reddebatque illi tributa.
      EN: Shalmaneser king of the Assyrians came up against him, and Hoshea became his servant and paid him tribute.

17:4  LA: Cumque deprehendisset rex Assyriorum Osee, quod rebellare nitens misisset nuntios ad Sua regem Ægypti ne præstaret tributa regi Assyriorum sicut singulis annis solitus erat, obsedit eum, et vinctum misit in carcerem.
      EN: The king of the Assyrians discovered that Hoshea was seeking to rebel and had sent messengers to So king of Egypt, so as not to pay tribute to the king of the Assyrians as he had done each year. He besieged him, bound him, and put him in prison.

17:5  LA: Pervagatusque est omnem terram : et ascendens Samariam, obsedit eam tribus annis.
      EN: He ranged through the whole land, came up to Samaria, and besieged it for three years.

17:6  LA: Anno autem nono Osee, cepit rex Assyriorum Samariam, et transtulit Israël in Assyrios : posuitque eos in Hala et in Habor juxta fluvium Gozan, in civitatibus Medorum.
      EN: In Hoshea's ninth year the king of the Assyrians captured Samaria and deported Israel to the Assyrians. He settled them in Halah and Habor, beside the river of Gozan, in the cities of the Medes.

17:7  LA: Factum est enim, cum peccassent filii Israël Domino Deo suo, qui eduxerat eos de terra Ægypti, de manu Pharaonis regis Ægypti, coluerunt deos alienos.
      EN: This happened because the children of Israel had sinned against the Lord their God, who had brought them out of the land of Egypt from the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and had worshiped foreign gods.

17:8  LA: Et ambulaverunt juxta ritum gentium quas consumpserat Dominus in conspectu filiorum Israël et regum Israël, quia similiter fecerant.
      EN: They walked according to the customs of the nations the Lord had destroyed before the children of Israel, and of the kings of Israel, who had acted in the same way.

17:9  LA: Et offenderunt filii Israël verbis non rectis Dominum Deum suum : et ædificaverunt sibi excelsa in cunctis urbibus suis, a turre custodum usque ad civitatem munitam.
      EN: The children of Israel offended the Lord their God with words that were not right. They built high places for themselves in all their towns, from the watchtower to the fortified city.

17:10  LA: Feceruntque sibi statuas et lucos in omni colle sublimi, et subter omne lignum nemorosum :
       EN: They made statues and sacred groves for themselves on every high hill and under every shady tree.

17:11  LA: et adolebant ibi incensum super aras in morem gentium quas transtulerat Dominus a facie eorum : feceruntque verba pessima irritantes Dominum.
       EN: There they burned incense on the altars in the manner of the nations the Lord had removed from before them. They did very wicked things, provoking the Lord.

17:12  LA: Et coluerunt immunditias de quibus præcepit eis Dominus ne facerent verbum hoc.
       EN: They worshiped filthy things, though the Lord had commanded them not to do this thing.

17:13  LA: Et testificatus est Dominus in Israël et in Juda per manum omnium prophetarum et videntium, dicens : Revertimini a viis vestris pessimis, et custodite præcepta mea et cæremonias, juxta omnem legem quam præcepi patribus vestris, et sicut misi ad vos in manu servorum meorum prophetarum.
       EN: The Lord warned Israel and Judah through all the prophets and seers, saying, “Turn back from your very wicked ways and keep My commands and ordinances, according to the whole law I commanded your fathers and sent to you through My servants the prophets.”

17:14  LA: Qui non audierunt, sed induraverunt cervicem suam juxta cervicem patrum suorum, qui noluerunt obedire Domino Deo suo.
       EN: They did not listen but stiffened their necks like their fathers, who were unwilling to obey the Lord their God.

17:15  LA: Et abjecerunt legitima ejus, et pactum quod pepigit cum patribus eorum, et testificationes quibus contestatus est eos : secutique sunt vanitates, et vane egerunt : et secuti sunt gentes quæ erant per circuitum eorum, super quibus præceperat Dominus eis ut non facerent sicut et illæ faciebant.
       EN: They rejected His statutes, the covenant He had made with their fathers, and the warnings by which He had admonished them. They followed vanities and acted vainly. They followed the nations around them, concerning whom the Lord had commanded them not to act as they did.

17:16  LA: Et dereliquerunt omnia præcepta Domini Dei sui : feceruntque sibi conflatiles duos vitulos, et lucos, et adoraverunt universam militiam cæli : servieruntque Baal,
       EN: They abandoned all the commands of the Lord their God. They made two cast-metal calves and sacred groves for themselves, worshiped the whole host of heaven, and served Baal.

17:17  LA: et consecraverunt filios suos et filias suas per ignem : et divinationibus inserviebant, et auguriis : et tradiderunt se ut facerent malum coram Domino, ut irritarent eum.
       EN: They consecrated their sons and daughters through fire, devoted themselves to divination and omens, and gave themselves over to doing evil before the Lord, provoking Him.

17:18  LA: Iratusque est Dominus vehementer Israëli, et abstulit eos a conspectu suo, et non remansit nisi tribus Juda tantummodo.
       EN: The Lord became very angry with Israel and removed them from His sight. Nothing remained except the tribe of Judah alone.

17:19  LA: Sed nec ipse Juda custodivit mandata Domini Dei sui : verum ambulavit in erroribus Israël, quos operatus fuerat.
       EN: But even Judah did not keep the commandments of the Lord its God; it walked in the errors Israel had practiced.

17:20  LA: Projecitque Dominus omne semen Israël, et afflixit eos, et tradidit eos in manu diripientium, donec projiceret eos a facie sua :
       EN: The Lord rejected all Israel's offspring, afflicted them, and handed them over to plunderers, until He cast them away from His presence,

17:21  LA: ex eo jam tempore quo scissus est Israël a domo David, et constituerunt sibi regem Jeroboam filium Nabat : separavit enim Jeroboam Israël a Domino, et peccare eos fecit peccatum magnum.
       EN: from the very time when Israel was torn away from David's house and they made Jeroboam son of Nebat their king. For Jeroboam separated Israel from the Lord and made them commit a great sin.

17:22  LA: Et ambulaverunt filii Israël in universis peccatis Jeroboam quæ fecerat : et non recesserunt ab eis,
       EN: The children of Israel walked in all the sins Jeroboam had committed and did not turn away from them,

17:23  LA: usquequo Dominus auferret Israël a facie sua, sicut locutus fuerat in manu omnium servorum suorum prophetarum : translatusque est Israël de terra sua in Assyrios, usque in diem hanc.
       EN: until the Lord removed Israel from His presence, as He had spoken through all His servants the prophets. Israel was deported from its land to the Assyrians, where it remains to this day.

17:24  LA: Adduxit autem rex Assyriorum de Babylone, et de Cutha, et de Avah, et de Emath, et de Sepharvaim : et collocavit eos in civitatibus Samariæ pro filiis Israël : qui possederunt Samariam, et habitaverunt in urbibus ejus.
       EN: The king of the Assyrians brought people from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim and settled them in the cities of Samaria in place of the children of Israel. They took possession of Samaria and lived in its cities.

17:25  LA: Cumque ibi habitare cœpissent, non timebant Dominum : et immisit in eos Dominus leones, qui interficiebant eos.
       EN: When they began to live there, they did not fear the Lord. The Lord sent lions among them, which killed them.

17:26  LA: Nuntiatumque est regi Assyriorum, et dictum : Gentes quas transtulisti, et habitare fecisti in civitatibus Samariæ, ignorant legitima Dei terræ : et immisit in eos Dominus leones, et ecce interficiunt eos, eo quod ignorent ritum Dei terræ.
       EN: It was reported to the king of the Assyrians: “The nations you have deported and settled in the cities of Samaria do not know the laws of the God of the land. The Lord has sent lions among them, and they are killing them, because they do not know the worship of the God of the land.”

17:27  LA: Præcepit autem rex Assyriorum, dicens : Ducite illuc unum de sacerdotibus quos inde captivos adduxistis, et vadat, et habitet cum eis : et doceat eos legitima Dei terræ.
       EN: The king of the Assyrians commanded, “Take there one of the priests you brought away captive from that place. Let him go and live with them and teach them the laws of the God of the land.”

17:28  LA: Igitur cum venisset unus de sacerdotibus his qui captivi ducti fuerant de Samaria, habitavit in Bethel, et docebat eos quomodo colerent Dominum.
       EN: One of the priests who had been taken captive from Samaria came and lived in Bethel and taught them how to worship the Lord.

17:29  LA: Et unaquæque gens fabricata est deum suum : posueruntque eos in fanis excelsis quæ fecerant Samaritæ, gens et gens in urbibus suis, in quibus habitabat.
       EN: Each nation made its own god and placed them in the high-place shrines the Samaritans had made, each nation in the cities where it lived.

17:30  LA: Viri enim Babylonii fecerunt Sochothbenoth : viri autem Cuthæi fecerunt Nergel : et viri de Emath fecerunt Asima.
       EN: The Babylonians made Succoth-benoth; the men of Cuthah made Nergal; and the men of Hamath made Ashima.

17:31  LA: Porro Hevæi fecerunt Nebahaz et Tharthac. Hi autem qui erant de Sepharvaim, comburebant filios suos igni, Adramelech et Anamelech diis Sepharvaim,
       EN: The Hivites made Nibhaz and Tartak. Those from Sepharvaim burned their sons in fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim,

17:32  LA: et nihilominus colebant Dominum. Fecerunt autem sibi de novissimis sacerdotes excelsorum, et ponebant eos in fanis sublimibus.
       EN: and nevertheless also worshiped the Lord. They made priests of the high places for themselves from the lowest ranks and appointed them in the lofty shrines.

17:33  LA: Et cum Dominum colerent, diis quoque suis serviebant juxta consuetudinem gentium, de quibus translati fuerant Samariam.
       EN: While they worshiped the Lord, they also served their own gods according to the custom of the nations from which they had been deported to Samaria.

17:34  LA: Usque in præsentem diem morem sequuntur antiquum : non timent Dominum, neque custodiunt cæremonias ejus, judicia, et legem, et mandatum, quod præceperat Dominus filiis Jacob, quem cognominavit Israël :
       EN: To this day they follow their former custom. They do not fear the Lord or keep His ordinances, judgments, law, and commandment, which the Lord commanded the children of Jacob, whom He named Israel.

17:35  LA: et percusserat cum eis pactum, et mandaverat eis, dicens : Nolite timere deos alienos, et non adoretis eos, neque colatis eos, et non immoletis eis :
       EN: He had made a covenant with them and commanded them, saying, “Do not fear foreign gods, bow down to them, worship them, or sacrifice to them.

17:36  LA: sed Dominum Deum vestrum, qui eduxit vos de terra Ægypti in fortitudine magna et in brachio extento, ipsum timete, et illum adorate, et ipsi immolate.
       EN: But the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt with great strength and an outstretched arm—fear Him, worship Him, and sacrifice to Him.

17:37  LA: Cæremonias quoque, et judicia, et legem, et mandatum, quod scripsit vobis, custodite ut faciatis cunctis diebus : et non timeatis deos alienos.
       EN: Observe the ordinances, judgments, law, and commandment He wrote for you, so that you carry them out all your days. Do not fear foreign gods.

17:38  LA: Et pactum quod percussit vobiscum, nolite oblivisci : nec colatis deos alienos,
       EN: Do not forget the covenant He made with you, and do not worship foreign gods.

17:39  LA: sed Dominum Deum vestrum timete, et ipse eruet vos de manu omnium inimicorum vestrorum.
       EN: Fear the Lord your God, and He will rescue you from the hands of all your enemies.”

17:40  LA: Illi vero non audierunt, sed juxta consuetudinem suam pristinam perpetrabant.
       EN: But they did not listen; they continued acting according to their former custom.

17:41  LA: Fuerunt igitur gentes istæ timentes quidem Dominum, sed nihilominus et idolis suis servientes : nam et filii eorum, et nepotes, sicut fecerunt patres sui, ita faciunt usque in præsentem diem.
       EN: So these nations feared the Lord but nevertheless served their idols as well. Their sons and grandsons do as their fathers did, even to this day.

2 KINGS 18

18:1  LA: Anno tertio Osee filii Ela regis Israël, regnavit Ezechias filius Achaz regis Juda.
      EN: In the third year of Hoshea son of Elah, king of Israel, Hezekiah son of Ahaz, king of Judah, began to reign.

18:2  LA: Viginti quinque annorum erat cum regnare cœpisset, et viginti novem annis regnavit in Jerusalem : nomen matris ejus Abi filia Zachariæ.
      EN: He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Abi, daughter of Zechariah.

18:3  LA: Fecitque quod erat bonum coram Domino, juxta omnia quæ fecerat David pater ejus.
      EN: He did what was good before the Lord, according to everything his father David had done.

18:4  LA: Ipse dissipavit excelsa, et contrivit statuas, et succidit lucos, confregitque serpentem æneum quem fecerat Moyses : siquidem usque ad illud tempus filii Israël adolebant ei incensum : vocavitque nomen ejus Nohestan.
      EN: He demolished the high places, shattered the statues, cut down the sacred groves, and broke apart the bronze serpent Moses had made, for until that time the children of Israel had burned incense to it. He called its name Nehushtan.

18:5  LA: In Domino Deo Israël speravit : itaque post eum non fuit similis ei de cunctis regibus Juda, sed neque in his qui ante eum fuerunt :
      EN: He trusted in the Lord, the God of Israel. Among all the kings of Judah after him there was none like him, nor among those before him.

18:6  LA: et adhæsit Domino, et non recessit a vestigiis ejus, fecitque mandata ejus, quæ præceperat Dominus Moysi.
      EN: He held fast to the Lord and did not depart from His footsteps. He kept His commandments, which the Lord had commanded Moses.

18:7  LA: Unde et erat Dominus cum eo, et in cunctis ad quæ procedebat, sapienter se agebat. Rebellavit quoque contra regem Assyriorum, et non servivit ei.
      EN: Therefore the Lord was with him, and he acted wisely in everything he undertook. He also rebelled against the king of the Assyrians and did not serve him.

18:8  LA: Ipse percussit Philisthæos usque ad Gazam, et omnes terminos eorum, a turre custodum usque ad civitatem munitam.
      EN: He struck the Philistines as far as Gaza and throughout their territory, from the watchtower to the fortified city.

18:9  LA: Anno quarto regis Ezechiæ, qui erat annus septimus Osee filii Ela regis Israël, ascendit Salmanasar rex Assyriorum in Samariam, et oppugnavit eam,
      EN: In King Hezekiah's fourth year, which was the seventh year of Hoshea son of Elah, king of Israel, Shalmaneser king of the Assyrians came up to Samaria, besieged it,

18:10  LA: et cepit. Nam post annos tres, anno sexto Ezechiæ, id est nono anno Osee regis Israël, capta est Samaria :
       EN: and captured it. For after three years, in Hezekiah's sixth year, that is the ninth year of Hoshea king of Israel, Samaria was captured.

18:11  LA: et transtulit rex Assyriorum Israël in Assyrios, collocavitque eos in Hala et in Habor fluviis Gozan in civitatibus Medorum :
       EN: The king of the Assyrians deported Israel to the Assyrians and settled them in Halah and Habor, the rivers of Gozan, in the cities of the Medes,

18:12  LA: quia non audierunt vocem Domini Dei sui, sed prætergressi sunt pactum ejus : omnia quæ præceperat Moyses servus Domini non audierunt, neque fecerunt.
       EN: because they had not listened to the voice of the Lord their God, but had transgressed His covenant. Everything that Moses, the Lord's servant, had commanded, they neither heard nor did.

18:13  LA: Anno quartodecimo regis Ezechiæ, ascendit Sennacherib rex Assyriorum ad universas civitates Juda munitas, et cepit eas.
       EN: In King Hezekiah's fourteenth year, Sennacherib king of the Assyrians came up against all the fortified cities of Judah and captured them.

18:14  LA: Tunc misit Ezechias rex Juda nuntios ad regem Assyriorum in Lachis, dicens : Peccavi : recede a me, et omne quod imposueris mihi, feram. Indixit itaque rex Assyriorum Ezechiæ regi Judæ trecenta talenta argenti, et triginta talenta auri.
       EN: Hezekiah king of Judah sent messengers to the king of the Assyrians at Lachish, saying, “I have sinned. Withdraw from me, and I will bear whatever you impose upon me.” The king of the Assyrians imposed upon Hezekiah king of Judah three hundred talents of silver and thirty talents of gold.

18:15  LA: Deditque Ezechias omne argentum quod repertum fuerat in domo Domini et in thesauris regis.
       EN: Hezekiah gave all the silver found in the Lord's house and the king's treasuries.

18:16  LA: In tempore illo confregit Ezechias valvas templi Domini, et laminas auri quas ipse affixerat, et dedit eas regi Assyriorum.
       EN: At that time Hezekiah broke apart the doors of the Lord's temple and the gold plates he himself had fastened to them, and gave them to the king of the Assyrians.

18:17  LA: Misit autem rex Assyriorum Tharthan, et Rabsaris, et Rabsacen de Lachis ad regem Ezechiam cum manu valida Jerusalem : qui cum ascendissent, venerunt Jerusalem, et steterunt juxta aquæductum piscinæ superioris, quæ est in via Agrifullonis.
       EN: The king of the Assyrians sent the Tartan, the Rab-saris, and the Rabshakeh from Lachish to King Hezekiah at Jerusalem with a strong force. They came up to Jerusalem and stood beside the aqueduct of the upper pool, on the road to the Fuller's Field.

18:18  LA: Vocaveruntque regem : egressus est autem ad eos Eliacim filius Helciæ præpositus domus, et Sobna scriba, et Joahe filius Asaph a commentariis.
       EN: They called for the king. Eliakim son of Hilkiah, the overseer of the household, Shebna the scribe, and Joah son of Asaph, the recorder, came out to them.

18:19  LA: Dixitque ad eos Rabsaces : Loquimini Ezechiæ : Hæc dicit rex magnus, rex Assyriorum : Quæ est ista fiducia, qua niteris ?
       EN: The Rabshakeh said to them, “Tell Hezekiah: Thus says the great king, the king of the Assyrians: What is this confidence on which you rely?

18:20  LA: forsitan inisti consilium, ut præpares te ad prælium. In quo confidis, ut audeas rebellare ?
       EN: Perhaps you have devised a plan to prepare yourself for battle. In whom do you trust, that you dare to rebel?

18:21  LA: an speras in baculo arundineo atque confracto Ægypto, super quem, si incubuerit homo, comminutus ingredietur manum ejus, et perforabit eam ? sic est Pharao rex Ægypti omnibus qui confidunt in se.
       EN: Do you trust in Egypt, that broken reed staff, which, if a man leans on it, splinters into his hand and pierces it? That is what Pharaoh king of Egypt is to all who trust in him.

18:22  LA: Quod si dixeritis mihi : In Domino Deo nostro habemus fiduciam : nonne iste est, cujus abstulit Ezechias excelsa et altaria, et præcepit Judæ et Jerusalem : Ante altare hoc adorabitis in Jerusalem ?
       EN: But if you tell me, ‘We trust in the Lord our God,’ is He not the one whose high places and altars Hezekiah removed, commanding Judah and Jerusalem, ‘You must worship before this altar in Jerusalem’?

18:23  LA: Nunc igitur transite ad dominum meum regem Assyriorum, et dabo vobis duo millia equorum, et videte an habere valeatis ascensores eorum.
       EN: Now, then, come over to my master, the king of the Assyrians. I will give you two thousand horses, and see whether you can find riders for them.

18:24  LA: Et quomodo potestis resistere ante unum satrapam de servis domini mei minimis ? an fiduciam habes in Ægypto propter currus et equites ?
       EN: How can you resist even one governor from among my master's least servants? Do you trust in Egypt for chariots and horsemen?

18:25  LA: Numquid sine Domini voluntate ascendi ad locum istum, ut demolirer eum ? Dominus dixit mihi : Ascende ad terram hanc, et demolire eam.
       EN: Have I come up to this place to demolish it without the Lord's will? The Lord told me, ‘Go up against this land and demolish it.’”

18:26  LA: Dixerunt autem Eliacim filius Helciæ, et Sobna, et Joahe Rabsaci : Precamur ut loquaris nobis servis tuis syriace : siquidem intelligimus hanc linguam : et non loquaris nobis judaice, audiente populo qui est super murum.
       EN: Eliakim son of Hilkiah, Shebna, and Joah said to the Rabshakeh, “Please speak to us, your servants, in Syriac, for we understand that language. Do not speak to us in the language of Judah within the hearing of the people on the wall.”

18:27  LA: Responditque eis Rabsaces, dicens : Numquid ad dominum tuum, et ad te misit me dominus meus, ut loquerer sermones hos, et non potius ad viros qui sedent super murum, ut comedant stercora sua, et bibant urinam suam vobiscum ?
       EN: The Rabshakeh answered them, “Has my master sent me to your master and to you to speak these words, and not rather to the men sitting on the wall, who will eat their own dung and drink their own urine with you?”

18:28  LA: Stetit itaque Rabsaces, et exclamavit voce magna judaice, et ait : Audite verba regis magni, regis Assyriorum.
       EN: The Rabshakeh stood and cried out in a loud voice in the language of Judah, saying, “Hear the words of the great king, the king of the Assyrians.

18:29  LA: Hæc dicit rex : Non vos seducat Ezechias : non enim poterit eruere vos de manu mea :
       EN: Thus says the king: Do not let Hezekiah deceive you, for he cannot rescue you from my hand.

18:30  LA: neque fiduciam vobis tribuat super Dominum, dicens : Eruens liberabit nos Dominus, et non tradetur civitas hæc in manu regis Assyriorum.
       EN: Do not let him give you confidence in the Lord, saying, ‘The Lord will surely rescue us, and this city will not be handed over to the king of the Assyrians.’

18:31  LA: Nolite audire Ezechiam. Hæc enim dicit rex Assyriorum : Facite mecum quod vobis est utile, et egredimini ad me : et comedet unusquisque de vinea sua, et de ficu sua : et bibetis aquas de cisternis vestris,
       EN: Do not listen to Hezekiah, for thus says the king of the Assyrians: Do what is advantageous for you with me, and come out to me. Each of you will eat from his own vine and fig tree, and you will drink water from your own cisterns,

18:32  LA: donec veniam, et transferam vos in terram quæ similis est terræ vestræ, in terram fructiferam, et fertilem vini, terram panis et vinearum, terram olivarum et olei ac mellis : et vivetis, et non moriemini. Nolite audire Ezechiam, qui vos decipit, dicens : Dominus liberabit nos.
       EN: until I come and transfer you to a land like your own land, a fruitful land rich in wine, a land of bread and vineyards, a land of olives, oil, and honey. You will live and not die. Do not listen to Hezekiah, who deceives you by saying, ‘The Lord will rescue us.’

18:33  LA: Numquid liberaverunt dii gentium terram suam de manu regis Assyriorum ?
       EN: Have the gods of the nations rescued their own lands from the hand of the king of the Assyrians?

18:34  LA: ubi est deus Emath, et Arphad ? ubi est deus Sepharvaim, Ana, et Ava ? numquid liberaverunt Samariam de manu mea ?
       EN: Where is the god of Hamath and Arpad? Where is the god of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah? Have they rescued Samaria from my hand?

18:35  LA: Quinam illi sunt in universis diis terrarum, qui eruerunt regionem suam de manu mea, ut possit eruere Dominus Jerusalem de manu mea ?
       EN: Who among all the gods of the lands has rescued his territory from my hand, that the Lord should be able to rescue Jerusalem from my hand?”

18:36  LA: Tacuit itaque populus, et non respondit ei quidquam : siquidem præceptum regis acceperant ut non responderent ei.
       EN: The people remained silent and answered him nothing, for they had received the king's command not to answer him.

18:37  LA: Venitque Eliacim filius Helciæ, præpositus domus, et Sobna scriba, et Joahe filius Asaph a commentariis ad Ezechiam scissis vestibus, et nuntiaverunt ei verba Rabsacis.
       EN: Eliakim son of Hilkiah, the overseer of the household, Shebna the scribe, and Joah son of Asaph, the recorder, came to Hezekiah with torn clothes and reported the Rabshakeh's words to him.

2 KINGS 19

19:1  LA: Quæ cum audisset Ezechias rex, scidit vestimenta sua, et opertus est sacco, ingressusque est domum Domini.
      EN: When King Hezekiah heard these things, he tore his clothes, covered himself with sackcloth, and entered the Lord's house.

19:2  LA: Et misit Eliacim præpositum domus, et Sobnam scribam, et senes de sacerdotibus, opertos saccis, ad Isaiam prophetam filium Amos.
      EN: He sent Eliakim, the overseer of the household, Shebna the scribe, and the elders of the priests, covered in sackcloth, to Isaiah the prophet, son of Amoz.

19:3  LA: Qui dixerunt : Hæc dicit Ezechias : Dies tribulationis, et increpationis, et blasphemiæ dies iste : venerunt filii usque ad partum, et vires non habet parturiens.
      EN: They said, “Thus says Hezekiah: This is a day of distress, rebuke, and blasphemy. The children have come to the point of birth, but the woman in labor has no strength.

19:4  LA: Si forte audiat Dominus Deus tuus universa verba Rabsacis, quem misit rex Assyriorum dominus suus ut exprobraret Deum viventem et argueret verbis, quæ audivit Dominus Deus tuus : et fac orationem pro reliquiis quæ repertæ sunt.
      EN: Perhaps the Lord your God will hear all the words of the Rabshakeh, whom his master, the king of the Assyrians, sent to taunt the living God and rebuke Him with words that the Lord your God has heard. Offer a prayer for the remnant that is left.”

19:5  LA: Venerunt ergo servi regis Ezechiæ ad Isaiam.
      EN: The servants of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah.

19:6  LA: Dixitque eis Isaias : Hæc dicetis domino vestro : Hæc dicit Dominus : Noli timere a facie sermonum quos audisti, quibus blasphemaverunt pueri regis Assyriorum me.
      EN: Isaiah said to them, “Tell your master: Thus says the Lord: Do not be afraid of the words you have heard, with which the servants of the king of the Assyrians have blasphemed Me.

19:7  LA: Ecce ego immittam ei spiritum, et audiet nuntium, et revertetur in terram suam, et dejiciam eum gladio in terra sua.
      EN: Look, I will put a spirit into him, and he will hear a report and return to his land. I will strike him down with the sword in his own land.”

19:8  LA: Reversus est ergo Rabsaces, et invenit regem Assyriorum expugnantem Lobnam : audierat enim quod recessisset de Lachis.
      EN: The Rabshakeh returned and found the king of the Assyrians attacking Libnah, for he had heard that he had withdrawn from Lachish.

19:9  LA: Cumque audisset de Tharaca rege Æthiopiæ, dicentes : Ecce egressus est ut pugnet adversum te : et iret contra eum, misit nuntios ad Ezechiam, dicens :
      EN: When he heard the report about Tirhakah king of Ethiopia, “He has come out to fight against you,” and was going to confront him, he sent messengers to Hezekiah, saying:

19:10  LA: Hæc dicite Ezechiæ regi Juda : Non te seducat Deus tuus in quo habes fiduciam, neque dicas : Non tradetur Jerusalem in manus regis Assyriorum.
       EN: “Tell Hezekiah king of Judah: Do not let your God, in whom you trust, deceive you, and do not say, ‘Jerusalem will not be handed over to the king of the Assyrians.’

19:11  LA: Tu enim ipse audisti quæ fecerunt reges Assyriorum universis terris, quomodo vastaverunt eas : num ergo solus poteris liberari ?
       EN: You yourself have heard what the kings of the Assyrians have done to all the lands, how they have devastated them. Will you alone be able to escape?

19:12  LA: Numquid liberaverunt dii gentium singulos quos vastaverunt patres mei, Gozan videlicet, et Haran, et Reseph, et filios Eden qui erant in Thelassar ?
       EN: Have the gods of the nations rescued each of those my fathers devastated: Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, and the children of Eden who were in Telassar?

19:13  LA: ubi est rex Emath, et rex Arphad, et rex civitatis Sepharvaim, Ana, et Ava ?
       EN: Where are the king of Hamath, the king of Arpad, and the king of the city of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?”

19:14  LA: Itaque cum accepisset Ezechias litteras de manu nuntiorum, et legisset eas, ascendit in domum Domini, et expandit eas coram Domino,
       EN: Hezekiah took the letter from the messengers' hands and read it. He went up to the Lord's house and spread it before the Lord,

19:15  LA: et oravit in conspectu ejus, dicens : Domine Deus Israël, qui sedes super cherubim, tu es Deus solus regum omnium terræ : tu fecisti cælum et terram.
       EN: and prayed in His presence, saying, “Lord, God of Israel, who sit above the cherubim, You alone are God of all the kings of the earth. You made heaven and earth.

19:16  LA: Inclina aurem tuam, et audi : aperi, Domine, oculos tuos, et vide : audi omnia verba Sennacherib, qui misit ut exprobraret nobis Deum viventem.
       EN: Incline Your ear and hear. Open Your eyes, Lord, and see. Hear all the words of Sennacherib, who has sent to us to taunt the living God.

19:17  LA: Vere, Domine, dissipaverunt reges Assyriorum gentes, et terras omnium.
       EN: Truly, Lord, the kings of the Assyrians have laid waste the nations and all their lands.

19:18  LA: Et miserunt deos eorum in ignem : non enim erant dii, sed opera manuum hominum, ex ligno et lapide : et perdiderunt eos.
       EN: They have thrown their gods into the fire, for they were not gods but the work of human hands, wood and stone, and they destroyed them.

19:19  LA: Nunc igitur Domine Deus noster, salvos nos fac de manu ejus, ut sciant omnia regna terræ quia tu es Dominus Deus solus.
       EN: Now, Lord our God, save us from his hand, so that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that You alone are the Lord God.”

19:20  LA: Misit autem Isaias filius Amos ad Ezechiam, dicens : Hæc dicit Dominus Deus Israël : Quæ deprecatus es me super Sennacherib rege Assyriorum, audivi.
       EN: Isaiah son of Amoz sent to Hezekiah, saying, “Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: I have heard what you have prayed to Me concerning Sennacherib king of the Assyrians.

19:21  LA: Iste est sermo, quem locutus est Dominus de eo : [Sprevit te, et subsannavit te, virgo filia Sion : post tergum tuum caput movit, filia Jerusalem.
       EN: This is the word the Lord has spoken concerning him: The virgin daughter of Zion despises you and mocks you; the daughter of Jerusalem shakes her head behind your back.

19:22  LA: Cui exprobrasti, et quem blasphemasti ? contra quem exaltasti vocem tuam, et elevasti in excelsum oculos tuos ? Contra Sanctum Israël.
       EN: Whom have you taunted and blasphemed? Against whom have you raised your voice and lifted your eyes on high? Against the Holy One of Israel.

19:23  LA: Per manum servorum tuorum exprobrasti Domino, et dixisti : In multitudine curruum meorum ascendi excelsa montium in summitate Libani, et succidi sublimes cedros ejus, et electas abietes illius. Et ingressus sum usque ad terminos ejus, et saltum Carmeli ejus
       EN: Through your servants you have taunted the Lord and said, ‘With my multitude of chariots I have climbed the mountain heights on the summit of Lebanon. I have cut down its lofty cedars and choice fir trees. I have entered as far as its borders, and its forest of Carmel

19:24  LA: ego succidi. Et bibi aquas alienas, et siccavi vestigiis pedum meorum omnes aquas clausas.
       EN: I have cut down. I have drunk foreign waters and dried up all the enclosed waters with the soles of my feet.’

       EN: Have you not heard what I did from the beginning? From ancient days I shaped it, and now I have brought it about: fortified cities of fighting men will become ruined heaps.

23:3  LA: Stetitque rex super gradum : et fœdus percussit coram Domino, ut ambularent post Dominum, et custodirent præcepta ejus, et testimonia, et cæremonias in omni corde, et in tota anima, et suscitarent verba fœderis hujus, quæ scripta erant in libro illo : acquievitque populus pacto.
      EN: The king stood on the step and made a covenant before the Lord, to walk after the Lord and keep His commands, testimonies, and ordinances with all their heart and all their soul, and to uphold the words of this covenant written in that book. The people assented to the covenant.

23:4  LA: Et præcepit rex Helciæ pontifici, et sacerdotibus secundi ordinis, et janitoribus, ut projicerent de templo Domini omnia vasa quæ facta fuerant Baal, et in luco, et universæ militiæ cæli : et combussit ea foris Jerusalem in convalle Cedron, et tulit pulverem eorum in Bethel.
      EN: The king commanded Hilkiah the high priest, the priests of the second rank, and the doorkeepers to cast out of the Lord's temple all the vessels made for Baal, for the sacred grove, and for the whole host of heaven. He burned them outside Jerusalem in the Valley of Kidron and carried their ashes to Bethel.

23:5  LA: Et delevit aruspices quos posuerant reges Juda ad sacrificandum in excelsis per civitates Juda, et in circuitu Jerusalem : et eos qui adolebant incensum Baal, et soli, et lunæ, et duodecim signis, et omni militiæ cæli.
      EN: He removed the soothsayers whom the kings of Judah had appointed to sacrifice at the high places in the cities of Judah and around Jerusalem, and those who burned incense to Baal, the sun, the moon, the twelve signs, and the whole host of heaven.

23:6  LA: Et efferri fecit lucum de domo Domini foras Jerusalem in convalle Cedron, et combussit eum ibi, et redegit in pulverem, et projecit super sepulchra vulgi.
      EN: He had the sacred grove carried out from the Lord's house, outside Jerusalem to the Valley of Kidron. He burned it there, reduced it to dust, and scattered it over the graves of the common people.

23:7  LA: Destruxit quoque ædiculas effeminatorum quæ erant in domo Domini, pro quibus mulieres texebant quasi domunculas luci.
      EN: He also demolished the small buildings of the effeminate men in the Lord's house, for whom the women wove what resembled little houses for the sacred grove.

23:8  LA: Congregavitque omnes sacerdotes de civitatibus Juda, et contaminavit excelsa ubi sacrificabant sacerdotes de Gabaa usque Bersabee, et destruxit aras portarum in introitu ostii Josue principis civitatis, quod erat ad sinistram portæ civitatis.
      EN: He gathered all the priests from the cities of Judah and defiled the high places where the priests sacrificed, from Geba to Beersheba. He destroyed the altars of the gates at the entrance of the gate of Joshua, the city governor, which was to the left of the city gate.

23:9  LA: Verumtamen non ascendebant sacerdotes excelsorum ad altare Domini in Jerusalem : sed tantum comedebant azyma in medio fratrum suorum.
      EN: Yet the priests of the high places did not go up to the Lord's altar in Jerusalem; they only ate unleavened bread among their brothers.

23:10  LA: Contaminavit quoque Topheth, quod est in convalle filii Ennom, ut nemo consecraret filium suum aut filiam per ignem, Moloch.
       EN: He also defiled Topheth in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, so that no one might consecrate his son or daughter to Molech through fire.

23:11  LA: Abstulit quoque equos quos dederant reges Juda soli in introitu templi Domini juxta exedram Nathanmelech eunuchi, qui erat in Pharurim : currus autem solis combussit igni.
       EN: He removed the horses that the kings of Judah had dedicated to the sun at the entrance of the Lord's temple, beside the chamber of Nathan-melech the eunuch in Parurim. He burned the chariots of the sun with fire.

23:12  LA: Altaria quoque quæ erant super tecta cœnaculi Achaz, quæ fecerant reges Juda, et altaria quæ fecerat Manasses in duobus atriis templi Domini, destruxit rex, et cucurrit inde, et dispersit cinerem eorum in torrentem Cedron.
       EN: The king destroyed the altars on the roofs of Ahaz's upper room that the kings of Judah had made, and the altars Manasseh had made in the two courts of the Lord's temple. He hurried away from there and scattered their ashes into the torrent of Kidron.

23:13  LA: Excelsa quoque, quæ erant in Jerusalem ad dexteram partem montis offensionis, quæ ædificaverat Salomon rex Israël Astaroth idolo Sidoniorum, et Chamos offensioni Moab, et Melchom abominationi filiorum Ammon, polluit rex.
       EN: The king also defiled the high places in Jerusalem on the right side of the Mount of Offense, which Solomon king of Israel had built for Ashtoreth, the idol of the Sidonians, Chemosh, the offense of Moab, and Milcom, the abomination of the children of Ammon.

23:14  LA: Et contrivit statuas, et succidit lucos : replevitque loca eorum ossibus mortuorum.
       EN: He shattered the statues and cut down the sacred groves, filling their sites with the bones of the dead.

23:15  LA: Insuper et altare quod erat in Bethel, et excelsum quod fecerat Jeroboam filius Nabat, qui peccare fecit Israël : et altare illud, et excelsum destruxit, atque combussit, et comminuit in pulverem, succenditque etiam lucum.
       EN: Moreover, the altar at Bethel and the high place made by Jeroboam son of Nebat, who made Israel sin—he destroyed that altar and high place, burned them, and crushed them to dust. He also burned the sacred grove.

23:16  LA: Et conversus Josias, vidit ibi sepulchra quæ erant in monte : misitque et tulit ossa de sepulchris, et combussit ea super altare, et polluit illud juxta verbum Domini quod locutus est vir Dei, qui prædixerat verba hæc.
       EN: Josiah turned and saw the tombs on the mountain. He sent and took the bones from the tombs and burned them on the altar, defiling it according to the Lord's word spoken by the man of God who had foretold these things.

23:17  LA: Et ait : Quis est titulus ille, quem video ? Responderuntque ei cives urbis illius : Sepulchrum est hominis Dei, qui venit de Juda, et prædixit verba hæc, quæ fecisti super altare Bethel.
       EN: He asked, “What is that monument I see?” The citizens of that city answered, “It is the tomb of the man of God who came from Judah and foretold these things you have done to the altar of Bethel.”

23:18  LA: Et ait : Dimitte eum : nemo commoveat ossa ejus. Et intacta manserunt ossa illius cum ossibus prophetæ qui venerat de Samaria.
       EN: He said, “Leave him alone. Let no one disturb his bones.” His bones remained untouched, together with the bones of the prophet who had come from Samaria.

23:19  LA: Insuper et omnia fana excelsorum quæ erant in civitatibus Samariæ, quæ fecerant reges Israël ad irritandum Dominum, abstulit Josias : et fecit eis secundum omnia opera quæ fecerat in Bethel.
       EN: Josiah also removed all the high-place shrines in the cities of Samaria that the kings of Israel had made to provoke the Lord. He did to them everything he had done at Bethel.

23:20  LA: Et occidit universos sacerdotes excelsorum qui erant ibi super altaria, et combussit ossa humana super ea : reversusque est Jerusalem.
       EN: He killed all the priests of the high places who were there upon the altars and burned human bones on them. Then he returned to Jerusalem.

23:21  LA: Et præcepit omni populo, dicens : Facite Phase Domino Deo vestro, secundum quod scriptum est in libro fœderis hujus.
       EN: He commanded all the people, saying, “Celebrate the Passover for the Lord your God, according to what is written in this Book of the Covenant.”

23:22  LA: Nec enim factum est Phase tale a diebus judicum qui judicaverunt Israël, et omnium dierum regum Israël et regum Juda,
       EN: No such Passover had been celebrated from the days of the judges who judged Israel, or throughout all the days of the kings of Israel and Judah,

23:23  LA: sicut in octavodecimo anno regis Josiæ factum est Phase istud Domino in Jerusalem.
       EN: as this Passover was celebrated for the Lord in Jerusalem in the eighteenth year of King Josiah.

23:24  LA: Sed et pythones, et ariolos, et figuras idolorum, et immunditias, et abominationes, quæ fuerant in terra Juda et Jerusalem, abstulit Josias : ut statueret verba legis quæ scripta sunt in libro quem invenit Helcias sacerdos in templo Domini.
       EN: Josiah also removed the spirit mediums, diviners, images of idols, filthy things, and abominations that had been in the land of Judah and Jerusalem, to establish the words of the law written in the book Hilkiah the priest had found in the Lord's temple.

23:25  LA: Similis illi non fuit ante eum rex, qui reverteretur ad Dominum in omni corde suo, et in tota anima sua, et in universa virtute sua juxta omnem legem Moysi : neque post eum surrexit similis illi.
       EN: There had been no king like him before him who returned to the Lord with all his heart, all his soul, and all his strength, according to the whole law of Moses, nor did one like him arise afterward.

23:26  LA: Verumtamen non est aversus Dominus ab ira furoris sui magni quo iratus est furor ejus contra Judam propter irritationes quibus provocaverat eum Manasses.
       EN: Yet the Lord did not turn from His great and furious anger that burned against Judah because of the provocations with which Manasseh had provoked Him.

23:27  LA: Dixit itaque Dominus : Etiam Judam auferam a facie mea, sicut abstuli Israël : et projiciam civitatem hanc quam elegi Jerusalem, et domum de qua dixi : Erit nomen meum ibi.
       EN: The Lord said, “I will also remove Judah from My presence, as I removed Israel. I will reject this city, Jerusalem, which I chose, and the house of which I said, ‘My name will be there.’”

23:28  LA: Reliqua autem sermonum Josiæ, et universa quæ fecit, nonne hæc scripta sunt in libro verborum dierum regum Juda ?
       EN: The rest of the acts of Josiah, and everything he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

23:29  LA: In diebus ejus ascendit Pharao Nechao rex Ægypti contra regem Assyriorum ad flumen Euphraten, et abiit Josias rex in occursum ejus : et occisus est in Mageddo cum vidisset eum.
       EN: In his days Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt went up against the king of the Assyrians at the river Euphrates. King Josiah went to meet him and was killed at Megiddo when he saw him.

23:30  LA: Et portaverunt eum servi sui mortuum de Mageddo : et pertulerunt in Jerusalem, et sepelierunt eum in sepulchro suo. Tulitque populus terræ Joachaz filium Josiæ : et unxerunt eum, et constituerunt eum regem pro patre suo.
       EN: His servants carried him dead from Megiddo, brought him to Jerusalem, and buried him in his tomb. The people of the land took Josiah's son Jehoahaz, anointed him, and made him king in place of his father.

23:31  LA: Viginti trium annorum erat Joachaz cum regnare cœpisset, et tribus mensibus regnavit in Jerusalem : nomen matris ejus Amital filia Jeremiæ de Lobna.
       EN: Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Hamutal, daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah.

23:32  LA: Et fecit malum coram Domino, juxta omnia quæ fecerant patres ejus.
       EN: He did evil before the Lord, according to everything his fathers had done.

23:33  LA: Vinxitque eum Pharao Nechao in Rebla, quæ est in terra Emath, ne regnaret in Jerusalem : et imposuit mulctam terræ centum talentis argenti, et talento auri.
       EN: Pharaoh Neco bound him at Riblah in the land of Hamath, so that he could not reign in Jerusalem, and imposed a tribute of a hundred talents of silver and one talent of gold upon the land.

23:34  LA: Regemque constituit Pharao Nechao Eliacim filium Josiæ pro Josia patre ejus : vertitque nomen ejus Joakim. Porro Joachaz tulit, et duxit in Ægyptum, et mortuus est ibi.
       EN: Pharaoh Neco made Eliakim son of Josiah king in place of his father Josiah and changed his name to Jehoiakim. He took Jehoahaz and brought him to Egypt, where he died.

23:35  LA: Argentum autem et aurum dedit Joakim Pharaoni, cum indixisset terræ per singulos, ut conferretur juxta præceptum Pharaonis : et unumquemque juxta vires suas exegit, tam argentum quam aurum, de populo terræ, ut daret Pharaoni Nechao.
       EN: Jehoiakim gave the silver and gold to Pharaoh, levying contributions from everyone in the land according to Pharaoh's command. He exacted both silver and gold from the people of the land, from each according to his means, to give it to Pharaoh Neco.

23:36  LA: Viginti quinque annorum erat Joakim cum regnare cœpisset, et undecim annis regnavit in Jerusalem : nomen matris ejus Zebida filia Phadaia de Ruma.
       EN: Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Zebidah, daughter of Pedaiah of Rumah.

23:37  LA: Et fecit malum coram Domino juxta omnia quæ fecerant patres ejus.
       EN: He did evil before the Lord, according to everything his fathers had done.

2 KINGS 24

24:1  LA: In diebus ejus ascendit Nabuchodonosor rex Babylonis, et factus est ei Joakim servus tribus annis : et rursum rebellavit contra eum.
      EN: In his days Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up, and Jehoiakim became his servant for three years. Then he rebelled against him again.

24:2  LA: Immisitque ei Dominus latrunculos Chaldæorum, et latrunculos Syriæ, et latrunculos Moab, et latrunculos filiorum Ammon : et immisit eos in Judam ut disperderent eum, juxta verbum Domini quod locutus fuerat per servos suos prophetas.
      EN: The Lord sent against him raiding bands of Chaldeans, Syrians, Moabites, and children of Ammon. He sent them against Judah to destroy it, according to the word the Lord had spoken through His servants the prophets.

24:3  LA: Factum est autem hoc per verbum Domini contra Judam, ut auferret eum coram se propter peccata Manasse universa quæ fecit,
      EN: This happened against Judah by the Lord's word, to remove it from His presence because of all the sins Manasseh had committed,

24:4  LA: et propter sanguinem innoxium quem effudit, et implevit Jerusalem cruore innocentium : et ob hanc rem noluit Dominus propitiari.
      EN: and because of the innocent blood he had shed, filling Jerusalem with the blood of the innocent. For this reason the Lord was unwilling to be appeased.

24:5  LA: Reliqua autem sermonum Joakim, et universa quæ fecit, nonne hæc scripta sunt in libro sermonum dierum regum Juda ? Et dormivit Joakim cum patribus suis :
      EN: The rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, and everything he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? Jehoiakim slept with his fathers,

24:6  LA: et regnavit Joachin filius ejus pro eo.
      EN: and his son Jehoiachin reigned in his place.

24:7  LA: Et ultra non addidit rex Ægypti ut egrederetur de terra sua : tulerat enim rex Babylonis, a rivo Ægypti usque ad fluvium Euphraten, omnia quæ fuerant regis Ægypti.
      EN: The king of Egypt no longer came out of his land, for the king of Babylon had taken everything belonging to the king of Egypt, from the stream of Egypt to the river Euphrates.

24:8  LA: Decem et octo annorum erat Joachin cum regnare cœpisset, et tribus mensibus regnavit in Jerusalem : nomen matris ejus Nohesta filia Elnathan de Jerusalem.
      EN: Jehoiachin was eighteen years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Nehushta, daughter of Elnathan of Jerusalem.

24:9  LA: Et fecit malum coram Domino, juxta omnia quæ fecerat pater ejus.
      EN: He did evil before the Lord, according to everything his father had done.

24:10  LA: In tempore illo ascenderunt servi Nabuchodonosor regis Babylonis in Jerusalem, et circumdata est urbs munitionibus.
       EN: At that time the servants of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up to Jerusalem, and the city was surrounded with siegeworks.

24:11  LA: Venitque Nabuchodonosor rex Babylonis ad civitatem cum servis suis ut oppugnarent eam.
       EN: Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to the city with his servants to attack it.

24:12  LA: Egressusque est Joachin rex Juda ad regem Babylonis, ipse et mater ejus, et servi ejus, et principes ejus, et eunuchi ejus : et suscepit eum rex Babylonis anno octavo regni sui.
       EN: Jehoiachin king of Judah went out to the king of Babylon, with his mother, servants, leaders, and eunuchs. The king of Babylon took him in the eighth year of his reign.

24:13  LA: Et protulit inde omnes thesauros domus Domini, et thesauros domus regiæ : et concidit universa vasa aurea quæ fecerat Salomon rex Israël in templo Domini juxta verbum Domini.
       EN: He carried out all the treasures of the Lord's house and the royal house, and cut apart all the gold vessels Solomon king of Israel had made in the Lord's temple, according to the Lord's word.

24:14  LA: Et transtulit omnem Jerusalem, et universos principes, et omnes fortes exercitus, decem millia, in captivitatem, et omnem artificem et clusorem : nihilque relictum est, exceptis pauperibus populi terræ.
       EN: He deported all Jerusalem, all the leaders, and all the army's strong men, ten thousand captives, with every craftsman and smith. Nothing was left except the poorest people of the land.

24:15  LA: Transtulit quoque Joachin in Babylonem, et matrem regis, et uxores regis, et eunuchos ejus : et judices terræ duxit in captivitatem de Jerusalem in Babylonem.
       EN: He deported Jehoiachin to Babylon, together with the king's mother, his wives, and his eunuchs, and took the judges of the land captive from Jerusalem to Babylon.

24:16  LA: Et omnes viros robustos, septem millia, et artifices, et clusores mille, omnes viros fortes et bellatores : duxitque eos rex Babylonis captivos in Babylonem.
       EN: All the strong men, seven thousand, and the craftsmen and smiths, a thousand, all mighty fighting men—the king of Babylon took them captive to Babylon.

24:17  LA: Et constituit Matthaniam patruum ejus pro eo : imposuitque nomen ei Sedeciam.
       EN: He appointed Jehoiachin's uncle Mattaniah in his place and gave him the name Zedekiah.

24:18  LA: Vigesimum et primum annum ætatis habebat Sedecias cum regnare cœpisset, et undecim annis regnavit in Jerusalem : nomen matris ejus erat Amital filia Jeremiæ de Lobna.
       EN: Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Hamutal, daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah.

24:19  LA: Et fecit malum coram Domino, juxta omnia quæ fecerat Joakim.
       EN: He did evil before the Lord, according to everything Jehoiakim had done.

24:20  LA: Irascebatur enim Dominus contra Jerusalem et contra Judam, donec projiceret eos a facie sua : recessitque Sedecias a rege Babylonis.
       EN: For the Lord was angry with Jerusalem and Judah until He cast them away from His presence. Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.

2 KINGS 25

25:1  LA: Factum est autem anno nono regni ejus, mense decimo, decima die mensis, venit Nabuchodonosor rex Babylonis, ipse et omnis exercitus ejus, in Jerusalem, et circumdederunt eam : et exstruxerunt in circuitu ejus munitiones.
      EN: In the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came against Jerusalem with his whole army. They surrounded it and built siegeworks all around it.

25:2  LA: Et clausa est civitas atque vallata usque ad undecimum annum regis Sedeciæ,
      EN: The city remained shut up and besieged until the eleventh year of King Zedekiah,

25:3  LA: nona die mensis : prævaluitque fames in civitate, nec erat panis populo terræ.
      EN: on the ninth day of the month. The famine grew severe in the city, and the people of the land had no bread.

25:4  LA: Et interrupta est civitas : et omnes viri bellatores nocte fugerunt per viam portæ quæ est inter duplicem murum ad hortum regis. Porro Chaldæi obsidebant in circuitu civitatem. Fugit itaque Sedecias per viam quæ ducit ad campestria solitudinis.
      EN: The city was breached, and all the fighting men fled by night along the road through the gate between the double wall beside the king's garden. The Chaldeans were besieging the city all around. Zedekiah fled along the road leading to the plains of the wilderness.

25:5  LA: Et persecutus est exercitus Chaldæorum regem, comprehenditque eum in planitie Jericho : et omnes bellatores qui erant cum eo, dispersi sunt, et reliquerunt eum.
      EN: The Chaldean army pursued the king and caught him in the plain of Jericho. All the fighting men with him scattered and abandoned him.

25:6  LA: Apprehensum ergo regem duxerunt ad regem Babylonis in Reblatha : qui locutus est cum eo judicium.
      EN: They captured the king and brought him to the king of Babylon at Riblah, who pronounced judgment upon him.

25:7  LA: Filios autem Sedeciæ occidit coram eo, et oculos ejus effodit, vinxitque eum catenis, et adduxit in Babylonem.
      EN: He killed Zedekiah's sons before his eyes, gouged out his eyes, bound him with chains, and brought him to Babylon.

25:8  LA: Mense quinto, septima die mensis, ipse est annus nonusdecimus regis Babylonis, venit Nabuzardan princeps exercitus, servus regis Babylonis, in Jerusalem.
      EN: In the fifth month, on the seventh day of the month, in the nineteenth year of the king of Babylon, Nebuzaradan, the army commander and servant of the king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem.

25:9  LA: Et succendit domum Domini, et domum regis : et domos Jerusalem, omnemque domum combussit igni.
      EN: He burned the Lord's house and the king's house, and the houses of Jerusalem; every house he burned with fire.

25:10  LA: Et muros Jerusalem in circuitu destruxit omnis exercitus Chaldæorum, qui erat cum principe militum.
       EN: The whole Chaldean army with the commander of the soldiers tore down Jerusalem's walls all around.

25:11  LA: Reliquam autem populi partem quæ remanserat in civitate, et perfugas qui transfugerant ad regem Babylonis, et reliquum vulgus transtulit Nabuzardan princeps militiæ.
       EN: Nebuzaradan, the army commander, deported the rest of the people who remained in the city, the deserters who had gone over to the king of Babylon, and the rest of the common people.

25:12  LA: Et de pauperibus terræ reliquit vinitores et agricolas.
       EN: He left some of the poor of the land as vine growers and farmers.

25:13  LA: Columnas autem æreas quæ erant in templo Domini, et bases, et mare æreum quod erat in domo Domini, confregerunt Chaldæi, et transtulerunt æs omne in Babylonem.
       EN: The Chaldeans broke apart the bronze pillars in the Lord's temple, the bases, and the bronze sea in the Lord's house, and carried all the bronze to Babylon.

25:14  LA: Ollas quoque æreas, et trullas, et tridentes, et scyphos, et mortariola, et omnia vasa ærea, in quibus ministrabant, tulerunt.
       EN: They also took the bronze pots, ladles, forks, cups, small mortars, and all the bronze vessels used in service.

25:15  LA: Necnon et thuribula, et phialas : quæ aurea, aurea, et quæ argentea, argentea tulit princeps militiæ,
       EN: The army commander also took the censers and bowls, those of gold as gold and those of silver as silver,

25:16  LA: id est, columnas duas, mare unum, et bases quas fecerat Salomon in templo Domini : non erat pondus æris omnium vasorum.
       EN: that is, the two pillars, the one sea, and the bases Solomon had made in the Lord's temple. The bronze of all these vessels could not be weighed.

25:17  LA: Decem et octo cubitos altitudinis habebat columna una : et capitellum æreum super se altitudinis trium cubitorum : et retiaculum, et malogranata super capitellum columnæ, omnia ærea : similem et columna secunda habebat ornatum.
       EN: One pillar was eighteen cubits high, with a bronze capital three cubits high upon it. The latticework and pomegranates above the pillar's capital were all bronze. The second pillar had similar decoration.

25:18  LA: Tulit quoque princeps militiæ Saraiam sacerdotem primum, et Sophoniam sacerdotem secundum, et tres janitores.
       EN: The army commander also took Seraiah the chief priest, Zephaniah the second priest, and three doorkeepers.

25:19  LA: Et de civitate eunuchum unum, qui erat præfectus super bellatores viros : et quinque viros de his qui steterant coram rege, quos reperit in civitate : et Sopher principem exercitus, qui probabat tyrones de populo terræ : et sexaginta viros e vulgo, qui inventi fuerant in civitate.
       EN: From the city he took one eunuch who was in charge of the fighting men, five men who had stood before the king and were found in the city, Sopher, the army commander who examined recruits from the people of the land, and sixty men from the common people who were found in the city.

25:20  LA: Quos tollens Nabuzardan princeps militum, duxit ad regem Babylonis in Reblatha.
       EN: Nebuzaradan, the commander of the soldiers, took them and brought them to the king of Babylon at Riblah.

25:21  LA: Percussitque eos rex Babylonis, et interfecit eos in Reblatha in terra Emath : et translatus est Juda de terra sua.
       EN: The king of Babylon struck and killed them at Riblah in the land of Hamath. Thus Judah was deported from its land.

25:22  LA: Populo autem qui relictus erat in terra Juda, quem dimiserat Nabuchodonosor rex Babylonis, præfecit Godoliam filium Ahicam filii Saphan.
       EN: Over the people who were left in the land of Judah, whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had left behind, he appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan.

25:23  LA: Quod cum audissent omnes duces militum, ipsi et viri qui erant cum eis, videlicet quod constituisset rex Babylonis Godoliam, venerunt ad Godoliam in Maspha, Ismahel filius Nathaniæ, et Johanan filius Caree, et Saraia filius Thanehumeth Netophathites, et Jezonias filius Maachathi, ipsi et socii eorum.
       EN: When all the army commanders and the men with them heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah, they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah: Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan son of Kareah, Seraiah son of Tanhumeth the Netophathite, and Jaazaniah son of the Maacathite, they and their companions.

25:24  LA: Juravitque Godolias ipsis et sociis eorum, dicens : Nolite timere servire Chaldæis : manete in terra, et servite regi Babylonis, et bene erit vobis.
       EN: Gedaliah swore to them and their companions, saying, “Do not be afraid to serve the Chaldeans. Stay in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it will be well with you.”

25:25  LA: Factum est autem in mense septimo, venit Ismahel filius Nathaniæ filii Elisama de semine regio, et decem viri cum eo : percusseruntque Godoliam, qui et mortuus est : sed et Judæos et Chaldæos qui erant cum eo in Maspha.
       EN: In the seventh month Ishmael son of Nethaniah, son of Elishama, of royal descent, came with ten men. They struck Gedaliah, and he died, and also the Jews and Chaldeans with him at Mizpah.

25:26  LA: Consurgensque omnis populus a parvo usque ad magnum, et principes militum, venerunt in Ægyptum timentes Chaldæos.
       EN: All the people, from small to great, and the army commanders rose and went to Egypt, because they feared the Chaldeans.

25:27  LA: Factum est vero in anno trigesimo septimo transmigrationis Joachin regis Juda, mense duodecimo, vigesima septima die mensis : sublevavit Evilmerodach rex Babylonis, anno quo regnare cœperat, caput Joachin regis Juda de carcere.
       EN: In the thirty-seventh year of the exile of Jehoiachin king of Judah, in the twelfth month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, Evil-merodach king of Babylon, in the year he began to reign, raised the head of Jehoiachin king of Judah from prison.

25:28  LA: Et locutus est ei benigne, et posuit thronum ejus super thronum regum qui erant cum eo in Babylone.
       EN: He spoke kindly to him and placed his throne above the thrones of the kings who were with him in Babylon.

25:29  LA: Et mutavit vestes ejus quas habuerat in carcere, et comedebat panem semper in conspectu ejus cunctis diebus vitæ suæ.
       EN: He changed the clothes Jehoiachin had worn in prison, and Jehoiachin ate bread regularly in his presence all the days of his life.

25:30  LA: Annonam quoque constituit ei sine intermissione, quæ et dabatur ei a rege per singulos dies omnibus diebus vitæ suæ.
       EN: He also appointed him an uninterrupted allowance, which was given to him by the king each day, all the days of his life.
