## Text

**6:1** Then King Darius gave a command, and they searched in the library of books that were stored in Babylon.

**6:2** And in Ecbatana, which is a fortress in the province of Media, one scroll was found, and such a record was written in it:

**6:3** "In the first year of King Cyrus, King Cyrus decreed that the house of God, which is in Jerusalem, be built in the place where they offer sacrifices, and that they lay foundations supporting a height of sixty cubits and a width of sixty cubits,

**6:4** three courses of unpolished stones, and likewise courses of new timber; and the expenses will be given from the house of the king.

**6:5** Moreover, let the gold and silver vessels of the temple of God, which Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the temple of Jerusalem and brought into Babylon, be restored and carried back into the temple in Jerusalem to their own place, which were also placed in the temple of God."

**6:6** "Now therefore, Tattenai, governor of the region that is beyond the river, Shethar-bozenai, and your counselors the Apharsachites, who are beyond the river, stay far away from them,

**6:7** and allow that temple of God to be made by the governor of the Jews and by their elders, so that they may build that house of God in its own place.

**6:8** Moreover, it has been commanded by me what ought to be done by those elders of the Jews so that the house of God may be built: namely, that from the king's coffer, that is, from the tributes that are given from the region beyond the river, expenses be carefully given to those men, so that the work may not be hindered.

**6:9** And if it is necessary, calves, and lambs, and young goats for a burnt offering to the God of heaven, wheat, salt, wine, and oil, according to the rite of the priests who are in Jerusalem, let it be given to them day by day, so that there may be no complaint in anything.

**6:10** And let them offer offerings to the God of heaven, and pray for the life of the king and of his sons.

**6:11** Therefore a decree has been laid down by me: as for every man who alters this order, let a beam be taken from his own house and set upright, and let him be impaled on it, and let his house be confiscated.

**6:12** And may God, who caused His name to dwell there, destroy all kingdoms and the people that stretches out its hand to resist and destroy that house of God which is in Jerusalem. I, Darius, have established a decree, which I wish to be carefully fulfilled."

**6:13** Therefore Tattenai, governor of the region beyond the river, and Shethar-bozenai, and his counselors thus diligently carried it out, according to what King Darius had commanded.

**6:14** And the elders of the Jews were building and prospering according to the prophecy of Haggai the prophet and of Zechariah the son of Iddo; and they built and constructed by the command of the God of Israel, and by the command of Cyrus, and Darius, and Artaxerxes, kings of the Persians,

**6:15** and they completed this house of God by the third day of the month of Adar, which is the sixth year of the reign of King Darius.

**6:16** And the sons of Israel, the priests and the Levites, and the rest of the sons of the exile celebrated the dedication of the house of God with joy.

**6:17** And they offered for the dedication of the house of God one hundred calves, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs, and twelve male goats for the sin of all Israel, according to the number of the tribes of Israel.

**6:18** And they appointed the priests in their divisions, and the Levites in their courses, over the works of God in Jerusalem, just as it is written in the book of Moses.

**6:19** And the sons of Israel of the exile celebrated the Passover on the fourteenth day of the first month.

**6:20** For the priests and the Levites had been purified as one, all of them clean to sacrifice the Passover for all the sons of the exile, and for their brothers the priests, and for themselves.

**6:21** And the sons of Israel who had returned from the exile ate, and all who had separated themselves to them from the defilement of the nations of the land, to seek the Lord God of Israel.

**6:22** And they celebrated the festival of unleavened bread for seven days with gladness, because the Lord had made them glad and had turned the heart of the king of Assyria toward them, to help their hands in the work of the house of the Lord God of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — The Vulgate reads "unpolished stones" (lapidibus impolitis) and plural "likewise courses of new timber" (et sic ordines de lignis novis), whereas the Aramaic Masoretic Text refers to large stones and a single course of new timber.
- **6:5** — The closing clause "which were also placed in the temple of God" (quæ et posita sunt in templo Dei) is a Vulgate perfect passive indicative statement, whereas the Aramaic expresses a command to deposit them in the house of God.
- **6:9** — The Vulgate lists "young goats" (hædos) where the Aramaic lists rams, and translates "according to the word of the priests" as "according to the rite of the priests" (secundum ritum sacerdotum).
- **6:11** — The Vulgate translates the penalty on the offender's house with the Roman legal term publicetur ("be confiscated" to the public treasury), whereas the Aramaic specifies that it be turned into a refuse heap.
- **6:19** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "the sons of Israel of the exile" (filii Israël transmigrationis), including "Israel" where the Hebrew reads simply "the sons of the exile."
