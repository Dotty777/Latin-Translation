## Text

**10:1** So then, while Ezra was praying in this way, and while he was imploring and weeping and lying before the temple of God, an exceedingly great assembly of men, women, and children was gathered to him from Israel, and the people wept with much weeping.

**10:2** And Shecaniah the son of Jehiel, of the sons of Elam, answered and said to Ezra, "We have acted unfaithfully against our God and have married foreign wives from the peoples of the land; and now, if there is repentance in Israel concerning this,

**10:3** let us make a covenant with the Lord our God to cast out all the wives and those who have been born of them, according to the will of the Lord and of those who fear the command of the Lord our God; let it be done according to the law.

**10:4** Arise, it is yours to decide, and we will be with you; be strong, and act."

**10:5** So Ezra arose and made the leaders of the priests and of the Levites, and all Israel, swear that they would act according to this word; and they swore.

**10:6** And Ezra arose before the house of God and went to the chamber of Jehohanan the son of Eliashib, and entered there; he did not eat bread and did not drink water, for he was mourning the transgression of those who had come from the captivity.

**10:7** And a proclamation was sent in Judah and in Jerusalem to all the sons of the exile, that they should be gathered together in Jerusalem;

**10:8** and as for everyone who does not come within three days, according to the counsel of the leaders and the elders, all his property will be taken away, and he himself will be cast out from the assembly of the exile.

**10:9** Therefore all the men of Judah and Benjamin assembled in Jerusalem within three days; it was the ninth month, on the twentieth day of the month; and all the people sat in the open square of the house of God, trembling because of the sin and the rains.

**10:10** And Ezra the priest arose and said to them, "You have transgressed and have married foreign wives, so as to add to the guilt of Israel.

**10:11** And now make confession to the Lord, the God of your fathers, and do His pleasure, and separate yourselves from the peoples of the land and from the foreign wives."

**10:12** And the entire multitude answered and said with a loud voice, "According to your word to us, so let it be done.

**10:13** However, because the people are many, and it is the time of rain, and we cannot bear to stand outside, and the work is not of one day or two (for indeed we have sinned greatly in this matter),

**10:14** let leaders be appointed in the entire multitude; and let all in our cities who have married foreign wives come at appointed times, and with them the elders, city by city, and the judges of each, until the anger of our God is turned away from us over this sin."

**10:15** Therefore Jonathan the son of Asahel and Jahzeiah the son of Tekoa stood over this, and Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite helped them;

**10:16** and the sons of the exile did so. And Ezra the priest and the men, the leaders of the families, went into the houses of their fathers, and all of them by their names; and they sat down on the first day of the tenth month to investigate the matter.

**10:17** And all the men who had married foreign wives were dealt with by the first day of the first month.

**10:18** And among the sons of the priests there were found those who had married foreign wives. Of the sons of Jeshua the son of Jozadak, and his brothers: Maaseiah, Eliezer, Jarib, and Gedaliah.

**10:19** And they gave their hands that they would put away their wives, and that for their guilt they would offer a ram from the sheep.

**10:20** And of the sons of Immer: Hanani and Zebadiah.

**10:21** And of the sons of Harim: Maaseiah, Elijah, Shemaiah, Jehiel, and Uzziah.

**10:22** And of the sons of Pashhur: Elioenai, Maaseiah, Ishmael, Nethanel, Jozabad, and Elasah.

**10:23** And of the sons of the Levites: Jozabad, Shimei, Kelaiah (that is, Kelita), Pethahiah, Judah, and Eliezer.

**10:24** And of the singers: Eliashib. And of the gatekeepers: Shallum, Telem, and Uri.

**10:25** And from Israel, of the sons of Parosh: Ramiah, Izziah, Malchijah, Mijamin, Eliezer, Malchijah, and Benaiah.

**10:26** And of the sons of Elam: Mattaniah, Zechariah, Jehiel, Abdi, Jeremoth, and Elijah.

**10:27** And of the sons of Zattu: Elioenai, Eliashib, Mattaniah, Jeremoth, Zabad, and Aziza.

**10:28** And of the sons of Bebai: Jehohanan, Hananiah, Zabbai, Athlai.

**10:29** And of the sons of Bani: Meshullam, Malluch, Adaiah, Jashub, Sheal, and Ramoth.

**10:30** And of the sons of Pahath-moab: Adna, Chelal, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattaniah, Bezalel, Binnui, and Manasseh.

**10:31** And of the sons of Harim: Eliezer, Jeshua, Malchijah, Shemaiah, Simeon,

**10:32** Benjamin, Malluch, Shemariah.

**10:33** And of the sons of Hashum: Mattenai, Mattattah, Zabad, Eliphelet, Jeremai, Manasseh, Shimei.

**10:34** Of the sons of Bani: Maadai, Amram, Uel,

**10:35** Benaiah, Bedeiah, Cheluhi,

**10:36** Vaniah, Meremoth, Eliashib,

**10:37** Mattaniah, Mattenai, Jaasai,

**10:38** Bani, Binnui, Shimei,

**10:39** Shelemiah, Nathan, Adaiah,

**10:40** Machnadebai, Shashai, Sharai,

**10:41** Azarel, Shelemiah, Shemariah,

## Translator Notes

- **10:2** — The Vulgate reads "if there is repentance" (si est pœnitentia), forming a conditional clause with verse 3, whereas the Hebrew text reads "there is hope."
- **10:9** — Both here (pro peccato, "because of the sin") and in 10:14 (super peccato hoc, "over this sin"), the Vulgate specifies "sin" where the Hebrew text reads "matter."
- **10:15** — The Vulgate reads "Therefore ... stood over this" (Igitur ... steterunt super hoc), portraying these men as presiding over the measure rather than opposing it, and has Thecue ("Tekoa," matching the Septuagint) where the Hebrew reads "Tikvah."
- **10:16** — The Vulgate reads "went into the houses of their fathers" (abierunt ... in domos patrum suorum), whereas the Hebrew text states that Ezra selected men according to their fathers' houses.
- **10:25** — The Vulgate reads "Eliezer" (matching the Septuagint), whereas the Hebrew text reads "Eleazar."
- **10:31** — The Vulgate reads Josue ("Jeshua," matching 10:18 and the Septuagint), whereas the Hebrew text reads "Isshijah."
- **10:44** — The Vulgate renders the difficult Hebrew ending cleanly as "there were women among them who had borne children" (fuerunt ex eis mulieres, quæ pepererant filios).
