## Text

**7:1** Now after these matters, in the reign of Artaxerxes, king of the Persians, Ezra the son of Seraiah, the son of Azariah, the son of Hilkiah,

**7:2** the son of Shallum, the son of Zadok, the son of Ahitub,

**7:3** the son of Amariah, the son of Azariah, the son of Meraioth,

**7:4** the son of Zerahiah, the son of Uzzi, the son of Bukki,

**7:5** the son of Abishua, the son of Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron, the priest from the beginning.

**7:6** This Ezra went up from Babylon, and he was a swift scribe in the law of Moses, which the Lord God gave to Israel; and the king gave him all his request, according to the hand of the Lord his God upon him.

**7:7** And some of the sons of Israel, and of the sons of the priests, and of the sons of the Levites, and of the singers, and of the gatekeepers, and of the Nethinim went up to Jerusalem in the seventh year of King Artaxerxes.

**7:8** And they came to Jerusalem in the fifth month; that is the seventh year of the king.

**7:9** For on the first day of the first month he began to go up from Babylon, and on the first day of the fifth month he came to Jerusalem, according to the good hand of his God upon him.

**7:10** For Ezra prepared his heart to investigate the law of the Lord, and to do and to teach precept and judgment in Israel.

**7:11** Now this is the copy of the letter of the edict which King Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest, a scribe instructed in the words and precepts of the Lord, and in His ceremonies in Israel:

**7:12** "Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, most learned scribe of the law of the God of heaven, greetings.

**7:13** It has been decreed by me that whoever in my kingdom, of the people of Israel, and of its priests, and of the Levites, chooses to go to Jerusalem may go with you.

**7:14** For from the presence of the king and of his seven counselors you have been sent to inspect Judea and Jerusalem in the law of your God, which is in your hand,

**7:15** and to carry the silver and gold which the king and his counselors have voluntarily offered to the God of Israel, whose tabernacle is in Jerusalem.

**7:16** And all the silver and gold, whatever you find in the entire province of Babylon and the people wish to offer, and from the priests the things they voluntarily offer for the house of their God, which is in Jerusalem,

**7:17** freely receive, and carefully buy with this money calves, rams, lambs, and sacrifices and their drink offerings, and offer them upon the altar of the temple of your God, which is in Jerusalem.

**7:18** Moreover, if it pleases you and your brothers to do anything with the remaining silver and gold, do it according to the will of your God.

**7:19** The vessels also, which are given to you for the service of the house of your God, deliver in the sight of God in Jerusalem.

**7:20** Moreover, the other things that are needed for the house of your God, however much it is necessary for you to spend, will be given from the treasury and from the king's exchequer,

**7:21** and from me. I, King Artaxerxes, have established and decreed to all the keepers of the public coffer who are beyond the river, that whatever Ezra the priest, scribe of the law of the God of heaven, requests from you, you give without delay,

**7:22** up to one hundred talents of silver, and up to one hundred kors of wheat, and up to one hundred baths of wine, and up to one hundred baths of oil, and salt without measure.

**7:23** Everything that pertains to the rite of the God of heaven, let it be diligently provided in the house of the God of heaven, so that He may not perhaps become angry against the kingdom of the king and of his sons.

**7:24** We also make known to you concerning all the priests, and the Levites, and the singers, and the gatekeepers, the Nethinim, and the servants of this house of God, that you have no authority to impose tax, tribute, or annual dues upon them.

**7:25** And you, Ezra, according to the wisdom of your God, which is in your hand, appoint judges and magistrates, so that they may judge all the people who are beyond the river, namely, those who know the law of your God; and also freely teach the uninstructed.

**7:26** And as for everyone who does not diligently do the law of your God and the law of the king, judgment will be upon him, whether to death, or to banishment, or to the condemnation of his property, or indeed to prison."

**7:27** Blessed be the Lord God of our fathers, who put this in the heart of the king, to glorify the house of the Lord which is in Jerusalem,

**7:28** and inclined His mercy toward me before the king and his counselors, and all the mighty leaders of the king; and I, strengthened by the hand of the Lord my God which was in me, gathered from Israel leaders to go up with me.

## Translator Notes

- **7:5** — The Vulgate renders Aaron's title as "the priest from the beginning" (sacerdotis ab initio), taking the Hebrew word for "chief" (rosh) in its temporal sense of "beginning."
- **7:6** — The Vulgate construes "Israel" as the indirect object ("which the Lord God gave to Israel"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "which the Lord, the God of Israel, gave."
- **7:12** — The Vulgate renders the Aramaic honorific and epistolary transition ("and now") into classical Latin letter form as "most learned ... greetings" (doctissimo, salutem).
- **7:21** — In the Clementine Vulgate punctuation, "and from me" (et a me.) concludes the list of funding sources begun in verse 20, whereas the Aramaic joins it to the opening of King Artaxerxes's decree.
