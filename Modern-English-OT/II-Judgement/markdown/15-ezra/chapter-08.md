## Text

**8:1** These, therefore, are the leaders of the families, and the genealogy of those who went up with me from Babylon in the reign of King Artaxerxes.

**8:2** Of the sons of Phinehas, Gershom. Of the sons of Ithamar, Daniel. Of the sons of David, Hattush.

**8:3** Of the sons of Shecaniah, the sons of Parosh, Zechariah; and with him one hundred fifty men were counted.

**8:4** Of the sons of Pahath-moab, Eliehoenai the son of Zerahiah, and with him two hundred men.

**8:5** Of the sons of Shecaniah, the son of Ezekiel, and with him three hundred men.

**8:6** Of the sons of Adin, Ebed the son of Jonathan, and with him fifty men.

**8:7** Of the sons of Elam, Jeshaiah the son of Athaliah, and with him seventy men.

**8:8** Of the sons of Shephatiah, Zebadiah the son of Michael, and with him eighty men.

**8:9** Of the sons of Joab, Obadiah the son of Jehiel, and with him two hundred eighteen men.

**8:10** Of the sons of Shelomith, the son of Josiphiah, and with him one hundred sixty men.

**8:11** Of the sons of Bebai, Zechariah the son of Bebai, and with him twenty-eight men.

**8:12** Of the sons of Azgad, Johanan the son of Hakkatan, and with him one hundred ten men.

**8:13** Of the sons of Adonikam, who were the last; and these are their names: Eliphelet, and Jeiel, and Shemaiah, and with them sixty men.

**8:14** Of the sons of Bigvai, Uthai and Zaccur, and with them seventy men.

**8:15** Now I gathered them at the river that flows to Ahava, and we remained there three days; and I searched among the people and among the priests for any of the sons of Levi, and I found none there.

**8:16** Therefore I sent Eliezer, and Ariel, and Shemaiah, and Elnathan, and Jarib, and another Elnathan, and Nathan, and Zechariah, and Meshullam, leaders; and Joiarib and Elnathan, wise men.

**8:17** And I sent them to Iddo, who is the chief in the place of Casiphia, and I put words in their mouth that they should speak to Iddo and his brothers, the Nethinim, in the place of Casiphia, so that they might bring to us ministers of the house of our God.

**8:18** And they brought to us, by the good hand of our God upon us, a most learned man of the sons of Mahli, the son of Levi, the son of Israel, and Sherebiah, and his sons and his brothers, eighteen,

**8:19** and Hashabiah, and with him Jeshaiah of the sons of Merari, and his brothers and his sons, twenty;

**8:20** and of the Nethinim, whom David and the leaders had given for the services of the Levites, two hundred twenty Nethinim; all these were called by their own names.

**8:21** And I proclaimed a fast there beside the river Ahava, so that we might afflict ourselves before the Lord our God, and ask from Him a straight way for us and our children, and for all our possessions.

**8:22** For I was ashamed to ask the king for help and horsemen who would defend us from the enemy on the way, because we had said to the king, "The hand of our God is upon all who seek Him in goodness, and His dominion, and His strength, and wrath are upon all who abandon Him."

**8:23** So we fasted and petitioned our God for this, and it turned out favorably for us.

**8:24** And I separated twelve from the leaders of the priests: Sherebiah and Hashabiah, and with them ten of their brothers;

**8:25** and I weighed out to them the silver and the gold, and the consecrated vessels of the house of our God, which the king, and his counselors, and his leaders, and all Israel among those who had been found, had offered;

**8:26** and I weighed out into their hands six hundred fifty talents of silver, and one hundred silver vessels, one hundred talents of gold,

**8:27** and twenty golden bowls, which were worth a thousand solidi each, and two vessels of the finest gleaming bronze, as beautiful as gold.

**8:28** And I said to them, "You are holy to the Lord, and the vessels are holy, and the silver and gold that was freely offered to the Lord God of our fathers;

**8:29** watch and guard them until you weigh them out before the leaders of the priests and of the Levites, and the heads of the families of Israel in Jerusalem, into the treasury of the house of the Lord."

**8:30** So the priests and the Levites received the weight of the silver, and the gold, and the vessels, to carry them to Jerusalem, into the house of our God.

**8:31** Therefore we set out from the river Ahava on the twelfth day of the first month to go to Jerusalem; and the hand of our God was upon us, and He delivered us from the hand of the enemy and of the ambusher on the way.

**8:32** And we came to Jerusalem, and we remained there three days.

**8:33** Now on the fourth day, the silver, and the gold, and the vessels were weighed out in the house of our God by the hand of Meremoth the son of Uriah the priest, and with him was Eleazar the son of Phinehas, and with them were Jozabad the son of Joshua and Noadiah the son of Binnui, Levites,

**8:34** according to the number and weight of everything; and all the weight was written down at that time.

**8:35** Moreover, those who had come from captivity, the sons of the exile, offered burnt offerings to the God of Israel: twelve young bulls for all the people of Israel, ninety-six rams, seventy-seven lambs, twelve male goats for sin; all as a burnt offering to the Lord.

**8:36** And they delivered the king's edicts to the satraps who were from the king's presence, and to the governors beyond the river, and they lifted up the people and the house of God.

## Translator Notes

- **8:3** — The Vulgate places "Of the sons of Shecaniah" at the start of verse 3 in apposition to "the sons of Parosh," whereas the Hebrew attaches "of the sons of Shecaniah" to Hattush in verse 2.
- **8:5** — The Vulgate reads "Ezekiel" (Ezechiel) where the Hebrew text reads "Jahaziel," and like the Hebrew omits an individual name before "the son of Ezekiel."
- **8:22** — The Vulgate reads "help" (auxilium) rather than "a force of soldiers," connects "in goodness" (in bonitate) to those who seek God rather than to God's hand ("for good"), and lists three attributes ("His dominion, and His strength, and wrath") where the Hebrew has two.
- **8:26** — The Vulgate reads "one hundred silver vessels" (vasa argentea centum), without the weight unit "talents" that accompanies the silver vessels in the Hebrew text.
- **8:27** — The Vulgate translates the Persian darics of the Hebrew as Roman gold solidi (solidos) and uses the distributive numeral millenos ("a thousand each").
