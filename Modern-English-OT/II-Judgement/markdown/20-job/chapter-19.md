## Text

**19:1** Then Job answered and said:

**19:2** "How long do you afflict my soul and crush me with words?

**19:3** Look, ten times you humiliate me, and you are not ashamed while oppressing me.

**19:4** Indeed, even if I was ignorant, my ignorance will be with me.

**19:5** But you lift yourselves up against me and reprove me with my disgraces.

**19:6** At least now understand that God has afflicted me with no fair judgment and has surrounded me with His scourges.

**19:7** Look, I will call out, enduring violence, and no one will hear; I will shout aloud, and there is no one who judges.

**19:8** He has fenced in my path, and I cannot pass through; and on my footpath He has placed darkness.

**19:9** He has stripped me of my glory and removed the crown from my head.

**19:10** He has torn me down on every side, and I am perishing; and as from an uprooted tree He has removed my hope.

**19:11** His fury has raged against me, and thus He has regarded me as His enemy.

**19:12** His raiders have come together and made a way for themselves through me, and they have besieged my tent all around.

**19:13** He has removed my brothers far from me, and my acquaintances have withdrawn from me like strangers.

**19:14** My relatives have abandoned me, and those who knew me have forgotten me.

**19:15** The lodgers of my house and my maidservants have regarded me as a stranger, and I have been like a foreigner in their eyes.

**19:16** I called my servant, and he did not answer; with my own mouth I kept imploring him.

**19:17** My wife has shuddered at my breath, and I kept pleading with the children of my womb.

**19:18** Fools also despised me; and when I had withdrawn from them, they spoke ill of me.

**19:19** Those who were formerly my counselors have detested me, and the one whom I loved most has turned away from me.

**19:20** With my flesh consumed, my bone has clung to my skin, and only the lips have been left around my teeth.

**19:21** Have mercy on me, have mercy on me, at least you, my friends, for the hand of the Lord has touched me.

**19:22** Why do you pursue me as God does and satisfy yourselves with my flesh?

**19:23** Who would grant me that my words be written? Who would give me that they be inscribed in a book

**19:24** with an iron stylus and a plate of lead, or be carved with a chisel in flint?

**19:25** For I know that my Redeemer lives, and on the last day I will rise from the earth;

**19:26** and once more I will be clothed in my skin, and in my flesh I will see my God,

**19:27** whom I myself will see, and my eyes will gaze upon, and not another: this hope of mine has been stored up in my breast.

**19:28** Why then do you now say, 'Let us pursue him, and let us find the root of the matter against him'?

**19:29** Flee therefore from the face of the sword, since the sword is the avenger of iniquities; and know that there is a judgment."

## Translator Notes

- **19:6** — Vulgate reads "flagellis suis" ("with His scourges") where the Hebrew has "His net," and "non aequo judicio" ("with no fair judgment") where the Hebrew speaks of God wronging or subverting Job.
- **19:18** — Vulgate reads "Stulti" ("Fools"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "young boys."
- **19:20** — Vulgate renders the closing clause "derelicta sunt tantummodo labia circa dentes meos" ("only the lips have been left around my teeth"), differing from the Hebrew idiom "I have escaped by the skin of my teeth."
- **19:24** — Latin "celte" ("with a chisel") is a famously rare Vulgate term (from which the archaeological word "celt" derives), corresponding to the Hebrew "forever" and perhaps arising from an early textual variant for "certe" ("surely").
- **19:25** — Verses 25-27 present one of the most notable Vulgate-distinct passages in the Old Testament: Jerome renders Job's confession explicitly in terms of bodily resurrection ("on the last day I will rise from the earth," "clothed in my skin," "in my flesh I will see my God," and "this hope of mine has been stored up in my breast"), differing markedly from the more elliptical Hebrew Masoretic Text.
