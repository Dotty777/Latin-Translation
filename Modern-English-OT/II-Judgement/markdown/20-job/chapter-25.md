## Text

**25:1** Now Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

**25:2** "Dominion and terror are with Him, who makes harmony in His heights.

**25:3** Is there any number to His soldiers? And upon whom will His light not rise?

**25:4** Can a man be justified when compared with God? Or can one born of a woman appear clean?

**25:5** Look, even the moon does not shine, and the stars are not clean in His sight:

**25:6** how much less man, who is decay, and the son of man, who is a worm?"

## Translator Notes

- **25:4** — Vulgate-distinct phrasing: Latin explicitly renders the Hebrew preposition ("with/before God") with the participle comparatus ("compared with God").
- **25:6** — Latin literally reads quanto magis ("how much more [is this true of]"), functioning idiomatically after the preceding negative as "how much less"; Latin putredo literally means "decay/rottenness" where the Hebrew reads "maggot."
