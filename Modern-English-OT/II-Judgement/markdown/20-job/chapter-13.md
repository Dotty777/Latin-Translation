## Text

**13:1** Look, my eye has seen all these things, and my ear has heard, and I have understood each one.

**13:2** In accordance with your knowledge, I too know, and I am not inferior to you.

**13:3** Yet still I will speak to the Almighty, and I long to dispute with God,

**13:4** first showing you to be fabricators of falsehood and devotees of perverse doctrines.

**13:5** And if only you would keep silent, so that you might be considered wise.

**13:6** Hear, then, my rebuke, and pay attention to the judgment of my lips.

**13:7** Does God really need your falsehood, that you should speak deceptions for Him?

**13:8** Do you really show partiality to Him, and strive to judge for God?

**13:9** Or will it please Him, from whom nothing can be hidden? Or will He be deceived, like a man, by your frauds?

**13:10** He Himself will rebuke you, because in secret you show partiality to Him.

**13:11** As soon as He stirs Himself, He will throw you into turmoil, and His terror will rush upon you.

**13:12** Your memory will be likened to ashes, and your necks will be reduced to mud.

**13:13** Keep silent for a short while, so that I may speak whatever my mind suggests to me.

**13:14** Why am I tearing my flesh with my teeth, and carrying my soul in my hands?

**13:15** Even if He kills me, in Him I will hope; even so, I will argue my ways in His presence.

**13:16** And He Himself will be my savior, for not a single hypocrite will come into His presence.

**13:17** Hear my speech, and grasp the riddles with your ears.

**13:18** If I am judged, I know that I will be found righteous.

**13:19** Who is there who would contend in judgment with me? Let him come. Why am I consumed while keeping silent?

**13:20** Just two things do not do to me, and then I will not be hidden from Your face:

**13:21** keep Your hand far away from me, and do not let Your dread terrify me.

**13:22** Call me, and I will answer You, or at least let me speak, and You answer me.

**13:23** How many iniquities and sins do I have? Show me my crimes and offenses.

**13:24** Why are You hiding Your face and regarding me as Your enemy?

**13:25** Against a leaf that is snatched away by the wind, You display Your power, and You pursue dry stubble:

**13:26** for You write bitter things against me, and You wish to consume me by the sins of my youth.

**13:27** You have placed my foot in the stocks, and You have watched all my paths, and You have examined the prints of my feet:

**13:28** I, who am to be consumed like decay, and like a garment that is eaten by a moth.

## Translator Notes

- **13:4** — Vulgate "cultores perversorum dogmatum" ("devotees of perverse doctrines") is a distinctive Latin rendering; the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "worthless physicians."
- **13:8** — The Latin idiom "faciem ejus accipitis" (literally "you accept His face," repeated in 13:10) is a biblical expression for showing partiality or favoritism.
- **13:12** — Vulgate "cervices vestræ" ("your necks") is a distinctive reading where the Hebrew refers to "your defenses" or "your mounds."
- **13:15** — Vulgate "in ipso sperabo" ("in Him I will hope") follows the Hebrew Qere tradition ("in Him") rather than the Ketiv ("not"); likewise in 13:16, Vulgate uses the personal noun "salvator" ("savior") rather than "salvation."
