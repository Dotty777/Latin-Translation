## Text

**12:1** Then Job answered and said:

**12:2** "So then, are you the only people, and will wisdom die with you?

**12:3** I also have a heart just as you do, nor am I inferior to you; for who is ignorant of these things that you know?

**12:4** The one who is mocked by his friend, as I am, will call upon God, and He will hear him; for the simplicity of the righteous man is mocked.

**12:5** A lamp held in contempt in the thoughts of the rich is prepared for the appointed time.

**12:6** The tents of robbers abound, and they boldly provoke God, although He Himself has given all things into their hands.

**12:7** Indeed, question the beasts, and they will teach you; and the birds of the sky, and they will tell you.

**12:8** Speak to the earth, and it will answer you, and the fish of the sea will recount it.

**12:9** Who is unaware that the hand of the Lord has made all these things?

**12:10** In His hand is the soul of every living thing, and the spirit of all human flesh.

**12:11** Does not the ear judge words, and the palate of the one who eats, flavor?

**12:12** In the aged is wisdom, and in much time is prudence.

**12:13** With Him are wisdom and strength; He Himself has counsel and understanding.

**12:14** If He tears down, there is no one who can build; if He shuts a man in, there is no one who can open.

**12:15** If He holds back the waters, all things will dry up; and if He lets them out, they will overturn the earth.

**12:16** With Him are strength and wisdom; He Himself knows both the deceiver and the one who is deceived.

**12:17** He leads counselors to a foolish end, and judges into stupor.

**12:18** He unfastens the belt of kings, and binds their waists with a rope.

**12:19** He leads priests without honor, and supplants the nobles:

**12:20** changing the lip of the truthful, and taking away the teaching of the elders.

**12:21** He pours out contempt upon princes, raising up those who had been oppressed.

**12:22** He reveals deep things out of darkness, and brings the shadow of death out into the light.

**12:23** He multiplies nations and destroys them, and restores them completely after they have been overturned.

**12:24** He changes the heart of the princes of the people of the earth, and deceives them so that they walk in vain through a pathless place:

**12:25** they will grope as though in darkness, and not in light, and He will make them wander like drunkards."

## Translator Notes

- **12:5** — A notoriously difficult verse in the Hebrew; the Vulgate construes it as Lampas contempta apud cogitationes divitum parata ad tempus statutum ("A lamp held in contempt in the thoughts of the rich is prepared for the appointed time").
- **12:6** — The Vulgate concludes cum ipse dederit omnia in manus eorum ("although He Himself has given all things into their hands"), whereas the Hebrew is commonly rendered "those who bring their god in their hand."
- **12:17** — The Vulgate reads in stultum finem ("to a foolish end") and in stuporem ("into stupor"), whereas the Hebrew speaks of counselors led away stripped or barefoot and judges made fools.
- **12:21** — The Vulgate reads eos qui oppressi fuerant relevans ("raising up those who had been oppressed"), whereas the Hebrew reads "loosens the belt of the strong."
- **12:23** — The Vulgate concludes with et subversas in integrum restituit ("and restores them completely after they have been overturned"), using the Latin legal idiom in integrum restituere ("to restore wholly"), where the Hebrew reads "enlarges nations and leads them away."
