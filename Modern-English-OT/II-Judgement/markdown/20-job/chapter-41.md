## Text

**41:1** "Not as one who is cruel will I rouse him; for who can withstand My face?

**41:2** Who gave to Me beforehand, so that I should repay him? All things that are beneath heaven are Mine.

**41:3** I will not spare him, nor powerful words, nor those composed for supplication.

**41:4** Who will uncover the face of his garment? And who will enter into the middle of his mouth?

**41:5** Who will open the gates of his face? All around his teeth is dread.

**41:6** His body is like cast shields, compacted with scales pressing against each other.

**41:7** One is united to another, and not even a breath passes through them.

**41:8** One will cling to the other, and holding each other, they will by no means be separated.

**41:9** His sneezing is the brightness of fire, and his eyes are like the eyelids of dawn.

**41:10** From his mouth torches go forth, like kindled brands of fire.

**41:11** From his nostrils smoke goes forth, like that of a kindled and boiling pot.

**41:12** His breath makes coals burn, and flame issues from his mouth.

**41:13** In his neck strength will dwell, and destitution precedes his face.

**41:14** The parts of his flesh cohere to one another; He will send thunderbolts against him, and they will not be carried to a different place.

**41:15** His heart will be hardened like a stone, and pressed firm like a blacksmith's anvil.

**41:16** When he is lifted up, the angels will be afraid, and terrified, they will be purified.

**41:17** When a sword lays hold of him, it will not be able to stand firm, nor a spear, nor a breastplate.

**41:18** For he will count iron like chaff, and bronze like decayed wood.

**41:19** An archer will not drive him into flight; stones of a sling have been turned into stubble for him.

**41:20** Like stubble he will consider a hammer, and he will deride the quivering spear.

**41:21** Beneath him will be the rays of the sun, and he will spread out gold for himself like mud.

**41:22** He will cause the deep sea to boil like a pot, and will render it as when ointments bubble.

**41:23** Behind him a path will shine; he will consider the abyss as growing old.

**41:24** On earth there is no power that can be compared to him, who was made so that he would fear no one.

**41:25** He sees every lofty thing; he himself is king over all the sons of pride."

## Translator Notes

- **41:1** — The Vulgate uses a first-person verb (Non quasi crudelis suscitabo eum, "Not as one who is cruel will I rouse him"), whereas the Hebrew has a third-person statement ("No one is so fierce that he dares to stir him up").
- **41:3** — Latin Non parcam ei, et verbis potentibus, et ad deprecandum compositis ("I will not spare him, nor powerful words, nor those composed for supplication") is a distinctive Vulgate reading where the Hebrew speaks of not keeping silence concerning Leviathan's limbs, strength, and frame.
- **41:14** — The second half (mittet contra eum fulmina, et ad locum alium non ferentur) is a distinctive Vulgate reading with God as the implied subject sending thunderbolts, whereas the Hebrew describes Leviathan's flesh as cast firm and immovable.
- **41:16** — Latin angeli ("the angels") and purgabuntur ("will be purified") are distinctive Vulgate renderings of Hebrew terms meaning "the mighty" and "they are beside themselves."
- **41:21** — Latin radii solis ("rays of the sun") and aurum ("gold") reflect the Vulgate's distinct rendering of Hebrew terms meaning "sharp potsherds" and "a threshing sledge."
