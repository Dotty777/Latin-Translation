## Text

**29:1** Job also continued, taking up his parable, and said:

**29:2** "Who would grant to me that I might be as in former months, according to the days in which God guarded me,

**29:3** when His lamp shone above my head, and by His light I walked in darkness,

**29:4** just as I was in the days of my youth, when God was privately in my tent,

**29:5** when the Almighty was with me, and my children were all around me,

**29:6** when I washed my feet in butter, and the rock poured out streams of oil for me,

**29:7** when I went out to the gate of the city, and in the public square they prepared a chair for me?

**29:8** The young men saw me and hid themselves, and the old men, rising up, stood.

**29:9** Princes stopped speaking and laid a finger over their mouths.

**29:10** Leaders held back their voice, and their tongue clung to their throat.

**29:11** The hearing ear called me blessed, and the seeing eye gave testimony for me,

**29:12** because I had rescued the poor man crying aloud, and the orphan for whom there was no helper.

**29:13** The blessing of the one about to perish came upon me, and I consoled the heart of the widow.

**29:14** I was clothed with righteousness, and I dressed myself with my judgment as with a garment and a diadem.

**29:15** I was an eye for the blind man, and a foot for the lame man.

**29:16** I was a father of the poor, and I most diligently searched out the case that I did not know.

**29:17** I crushed the jaws of the unjust man, and I carried off the prey from his teeth.

**29:18** And I used to say, 'In my little nest I will die, and like a palm tree I will multiply days.

**29:19** My root is open beside the waters, and the dew will linger on my harvest.

**29:20** My glory will continually be renewed, and my bow will be restored in my hand.'

**29:21** Those who listened to me awaited a verdict, and attentively kept silent for my counsel.

**29:22** They dared to add nothing to my words, and my speech distilled upon them.

**29:23** They awaited me like rain, and they opened their mouths as for the late shower.

**29:24** Whenever I smiled at them, they did not believe it, and the light of my face did not fall to the ground.

**29:25** If I had wished to go to them, I sat first; and while I sat like a king, with an army standing around, I was nevertheless a comforter of those who mourned.

## Translator Notes

- **29:4** — Latin in diebus adolescentiae meae ("in the days of my youth") renders Hebrew "in the days of my autumn/prime," and secreto ("privately/secretly") renders Hebrew sod ("intimate friendship/counsel").
- **29:7** — The Vulgate has third-person plural parabant ("they prepared a chair for me") where the Hebrew reads first-person singular ("I prepared my seat"); similarly in v. 9 Latin specifies digitum ("a finger") rather than "hand."
- **29:14** — The Vulgate makes Job the active reflexive subject of vestivi me ("I dressed myself with my judgment as with a garment and a diadem"), whereas the Hebrew has righteousness as the subject ("it clothed me") and reads "turban" instead of "diadem."
- **29:18** — Latin sicut palma ("like a palm tree") renders Hebrew ka-chol (often read as "sand" or, via ancient tradition linked to Greek phoinix, "phoenix" or "palm tree"); in v. 19 Latin messione ("harvest") reflects the alternate meaning of Hebrew qatsir ("harvest" or "branch").
