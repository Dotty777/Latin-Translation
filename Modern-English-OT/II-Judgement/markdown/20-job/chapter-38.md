## Text

**38:1** Then the Lord, answering Job from the whirlwind, said:

**38:2** "Who is this entangling judgments with ignorant words?

**38:3** Gird your loins like a man; I will question you, and answer Me.

**38:4** Where were you when I was laying the foundations of the earth? Declare to Me, if you have understanding.

**38:5** Who set its measurements, if you know? Or who stretched a line across it?

**38:6** On what were its bases made firm, or who lowered its cornerstone,

**38:7** when the morning stars praised Me together, and all the sons of God shouted with joy?

**38:8** Who shut in the sea with doors when it was bursting out, as though emerging from a womb,

**38:9** when I made a cloud its garment, and wrapped it in thick darkness as with the cloths of infancy?

**38:10** I surrounded it with My boundaries, and I set a bar and doors,

**38:11** and I said, 'This far you will come, and you will proceed no further, and here you will break your swelling waves.'

**38:12** Since your birth, have you commanded the daybreak and shown the dawn its place?

**38:13** And have you grasped the ends of the earth, shaking them, and shaken the wicked out of it?

**38:14** The seal will be restored like clay, and it will stand like a garment;

**38:15** from the wicked their light will be taken away, and the uplifted arm will be broken.

**38:16** Have you entered the depths of the sea, and walked about in the farthest recesses of the abyss?

**38:17** Have the gates of death been opened to you, and have you seen the dark doors?

**38:18** Have you considered the breadth of the earth? Declare everything to Me, if you know:

**38:19** in what way light dwells, and what the place of darkness is,

**38:20** so that you may lead each one to its boundaries, and understand the paths of its house.

**38:21** Did you know then that you were going to be born, and did you know the number of your days?

**38:22** Have you entered the treasuries of snow, or looked upon the treasuries of hail,

**38:23** which I have prepared for the time of the enemy, for the day of battle and war?

**38:24** By what way is light scattered, and heat distributed over the earth?

**38:25** Who gave a course to the fiercest downpour, and a way for the resounding thunder,

**38:26** so that it might rain on a land without a man, in the desert where no mortal dwells;

**38:27** so that it might fill the trackless and desolate land, and bring forth green plants?

**38:28** Who is the father of rain? Or who fathered the drops of dew?

**38:29** From whose womb did the ice come forth? And who gave birth to the frost from heaven?

**38:30** Like stone, the waters are hardened, and the surface of the abyss is bound fast.

**38:31** Will you be strong enough to join together the glittering stars of the Pleiades, or will you be able to break up the circuit of Arcturus?

**38:32** Do you bring forth the morning star in its time, and make the evening star rise over the sons of the earth?

**38:33** Do you know the order of heaven, and will you establish its design on the earth?

**38:34** Will you lift up your voice in the cloud, and will a surge of waters cover you?

**38:35** Will you send forth lightning bolts, and will they go, and returning, say to you, 'Here we are'?

**38:36** Who placed wisdom in the inward parts of man? Or who gave understanding to the rooster?

**38:37** Who will declare the design of the heavens? And who will make the harmony of heaven sleep?

**38:38** When was the dust poured out on the earth, and were the clods compacted together?

**38:39** Will you capture prey for the lioness, and fill the appetite of her cubs,

**38:40** when they crouch in dens, and lie in wait in caves?

**38:41** Who prepares for the raven its food, when its young cry out to God, wandering about because they have no nourishment?"

## Translator Notes

- **38:7** — The Vulgate uniquely adds "Me" and reads "praised Me" (me laudarent), whereas the Hebrew text reads "sang together."
- **38:14** — The Vulgate renders this difficult verse as "The seal will be restored like clay, and it will stand like a garment" (Restituetur ut lutum signaculum), differing from the Hebrew image of the earth changing like clay under a seal.
- **38:31** — Following Latin astronomical usage, the Vulgate names the Pleiades and Arcturus (where Hebrew has Orion), and in 38:32 similarly reads "the morning star" (luciferum) and "the evening star" (vesperum).
- **38:36** — The Vulgate specifies "in the inward parts of man" (in visceribus hominis) and renders the rare Hebrew term sekhwi as "the rooster" (gallo).
- **38:37** — The Vulgate reads "the harmony of heaven" (concentum cæli) and "make ... sleep" (dormire faciet), interpreting the Hebrew noun for "waterskins" through its homonym for a musical instrument.
