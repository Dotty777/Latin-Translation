## Text

**31:1** I have made a covenant with my eyes, so that I would not even think about a virgin.

**31:2** For what portion would God from above have in me, and what inheritance the Almighty from on high?

**31:3** Is there not destruction for the wicked man, and estrangement for those who work injustice?

**31:4** Does He Himself not consider my ways, and count all my steps?

**31:5** If I have walked in vanity, and my foot has hurried in deceit,

**31:6** let Him weigh me on a just scale, and let God know my integrity.

**31:7** If my step has turned aside from the way, and if my heart has followed my eyes, and if a stain has clung to my hands,

**31:8** let me sow, and let another eat, and let my offspring be uprooted.

**31:9** If my heart has been deceived over a woman, and if I have lain in wait at my friend's door,

**31:10** let my wife be another's prostitute, and let others bow down over her.

**31:11** For this is a heinous crime, and the greatest iniquity.

**31:12** It is a fire devouring even to destruction, and uprooting all produce.

**31:13** If I have scorned to submit to judgment with my male servant and my female servant, when they contended against me,

**31:14** for what will I do when God rises to judge? And when He inquires, what will I answer Him?

**31:15** Did not He who also fashioned him make me in the womb, and did not One and the same form me in the womb?

**31:16** If I have denied to the poor what they wanted, and made the eyes of the widow wait;

**31:17** if I have eaten my morsel alone, and the orphan has not eaten from it

**31:18** (for from my infancy compassion grew with me, and from my mother's womb it came out with me);

**31:19** if I have despised one who was perishing because he had no clothing, and a poor man without a covering;

**31:20** if his sides have not blessed me, nor has he been warmed by the fleeces of my sheep;

**31:21** if I have raised my hand against the orphan, even when I saw myself superior in the gate,

**31:22** let my shoulder fall away from its joint, and let my arm be shattered along with its bones.

**31:23** For always, like waves swelling over me, I feared God, and I could not bear His weight.

**31:24** If I have considered gold my strength, and said to fine gold, "My confidence";

**31:25** if I have rejoiced over my many riches, and because my hand has found very much;

**31:26** if I have seen the sun when it shone, and the moon moving brightly,

**31:27** and my heart has rejoiced in secret, and I have kissed my hand with my mouth,

**31:28** which is the greatest iniquity, and a denial against God Most High.

**31:29** If I have been glad at the ruin of the one who hated me, and exulted that evil had found him,

**31:30** for I have not given my throat to sin, so that by cursing I would demand his life.

**31:31** If the men of my tent have not said, "Who will give some of his flesh, so that we may be satisfied?"

**31:32** The stranger did not stay outside; my door stood open to the traveler.

**31:33** If I have hidden my sin like a man, and concealed my iniquity in my bosom;

**31:34** if I have been terrified at a very great multitude, and the contempt of relatives has frightened me, and I have not rather kept silent, nor gone out the door.

**31:35** Who will grant me a hearer, so that the Almighty may hear my desire, and that He Himself who judges may write a book,

**31:36** that I may carry it on my shoulder, and place it around myself like a crown?

**31:37** At each of my steps I will declare it, and as to a prince I will offer it.

**31:38** If my land cries out against me, and with it its furrows weep,

**31:39** if I have eaten its fruits without money, and I have afflicted the soul of its farmers,

**31:40** instead of wheat let the thistle spring up for me, and instead of barley the thorn. The words of Job are finished.

## Translator Notes

- **31:2** — Vulgate-distinct syntax "Quam enim partem haberet in me Deus" ("what portion would God ... have in me"), making God the subject who holds a portion in Job, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text asks what portion Job would receive from God above.
- **31:10** — Vulgate-distinct "scortum alterius" ("another's prostitute"), expressing directly what the Hebrew Masoretic Text states through the idiom of grinding for another.
- **31:18** — Vulgate-distinct "crevit mecum miseratio" ("compassion grew with me"), personifying compassion as accompanying Job from infancy and the womb, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks of raising the orphan and guiding the widow.
- **31:34** — The Vulgate's "et non magis tacui, nec egressus sum ostium" ("and I have not rather kept silent, nor gone out the door") introduces a negated clause that diverges from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("so that I kept silent and did not go out the door").
- **31:35** — Vulgate-distinct "desiderium meum" ("my desire") and "ipse qui judicat" ("He Himself who judges"), pointing to God as both hearer and author of the book, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to Job's signature and an indictment written by his adversary.
