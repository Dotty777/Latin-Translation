## Text

**16:1** Now the word of the Lord came to Jehu son of Hanani against Baasha, saying,

**16:2** "Because I exalted you from the dust and set you as leader over My people Israel, yet you walked in the way of Jeroboam and caused My people Israel to sin, so as to provoke Me by their sins,

**16:3** look, I will cut down the posterity of Baasha and the posterity of his house, and I will make your house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat.

**16:4** Anyone of Baasha who dies in the city, the dogs will eat him; and anyone of his who dies in the countryside, the birds of the sky will eat him."

**16:5** Now the rest of the matters of Baasha, and whatever he did, and his battles, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?

**16:6** So Baasha slept with his fathers and was buried in Tirzah, and Elah his son reigned in his place.

**16:7** Now when the word of the Lord had come by the hand of Jehu son of Hanani the prophet against Baasha and against his house, and against all the evil that he had done before the Lord, to provoke Him by the works of his hands, so that he would become like the house of Jeroboam, for this reason he killed him, that is, Jehu son of Hanani the prophet.

**16:8** In the twenty-sixth year of Asa king of Judah, Elah son of Baasha reigned over Israel in Tirzah for two years.

**16:9** And his servant Zimri, commander of half of the horsemen, rebelled against him; now Elah was in Tirzah, drinking and drunk in the house of Arza, the prefect of Tirzah.

**16:10** Rushing in, therefore, Zimri struck and killed him in the twenty-seventh year of Asa king of Judah, and reigned in his place.

**16:11** And when he had begun to reign and had sat upon his throne, he struck the entire house of Baasha, and did not leave from it one who urinates against a wall, nor his relatives or his friends.

**16:12** And Zimri destroyed the entire house of Baasha, according to the word of the Lord which He had spoken to Baasha by the hand of Jehu the prophet,

**16:13** because of all the sins of Baasha and the sins of Elah his son, who sinned and caused Israel to sin, provoking the Lord God of Israel by their vanities.

**16:14** Now the rest of the matters of Elah, and all that he did, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?

**16:15** In the twenty-seventh year of Asa king of Judah, Zimri reigned for seven days in Tirzah; meanwhile, the army was besieging Gibbethon, a city of the Philistines.

**16:16** And when they heard that Zimri had rebelled and had killed the king, all Israel made Omri, who was commander of the forces over Israel, king for themselves on that day in the camp.

**16:17** Therefore Omri, and all Israel with him, went up from Gibbethon, and they besieged Tirzah.

**16:18** Now Zimri, seeing that the city was about to be captured, entered the palace and set himself on fire along with the royal house; and he died

**16:19** in his sins which he had sinned, doing evil before the Lord and walking in the way of Jeroboam and in his sin by which he caused Israel to sin.

**16:20** Now the rest of the matters of Zimri, and of his conspiracy and tyranny, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?

**16:21** Then the people of Israel were divided into two parts: half of the people followed Tibni son of Ginath, to appoint him king, and half followed Omri.

**16:22** But the people who were with Omri prevailed over the people who followed Tibni son of Ginath; and Tibni died, and Omri reigned.

**16:23** In the thirty-first year of Asa king of Judah, Omri reigned over Israel for twelve years; in Tirzah he reigned for six years.

**16:24** And he bought the hill of Samaria from Shemer for two talents of silver, and built on it, and called the name of the city that he had constructed Samaria, after the name of Shemer, the owner of the hill.

**16:25** Now Omri did evil in the sight of the Lord, and acted wickedly more than all who were before him.

**16:26** And he walked in all the way of Jeroboam son of Nebat, and in his sins by which he had caused Israel to sin, so as to provoke the Lord God of Israel by his vanities.

**16:27** Now the rest of the matters of Omri, and his battles that he fought, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?

**16:28** And Omri slept with his fathers and was buried in Samaria; and Ahab his son reigned in his place. Moreover, he also removed from the land the remnant of the effeminate men who had remained in the days of Asa his father. And there was no king appointed in Edom at that time. Now King Jehoshaphat had made fleets on the sea to sail to Ophir for gold, yet they could not go, because they were shattered at Ezion-geber. Then Ahaziah son of Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, "Let my servants go with your servants in the ships." And Jehoshaphat was unwilling.

**16:29** Now Ahab son of Omri reigned over Israel in the thirty-eighth year of Asa king of Judah. And Ahab son of Omri reigned over Israel in Samaria for twenty-two years.

**16:30** And Ahab son of Omri did evil in the sight of the Lord more than all who were before him.

**16:31** Nor was it enough for him to walk in the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat; moreover, he took as wife Jezebel, daughter of Ethbaal king of the Sidonians. And he went and served Baal and worshiped him.

**16:32** And he set up an altar for Baal in the temple of Baal, which he had built in Samaria,

**16:33** and he planted a grove; and Ahab did yet more in his work, provoking the Lord God of Israel more than all the kings of Israel who were before him.

**16:34** In his days Hiel of Bethel built Jericho; in Abiram his firstborn he founded it, and in Segub his youngest he set its gates, according to the word of the Lord which He had spoken by the hand of Joshua son of Nun.

## Translator Notes

- **16:3** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew threat to sweep away Baasha's house with the agricultural metaphor 'cut down the posterity' (demetam posteriora).
- **16:7** — Striking Vulgate-distinct reading: whereas the Hebrew cites Baasha's slaughter of Jeroboam's house as a reason for the oracle, the Clementine Vulgate adds the gloss 'that is, Jehu son of Hanani the prophet' (hoc est, Jehu filium Hanani prophetam), stating that Baasha killed the prophet Jehu for delivering the message.
- **16:9** — The Vulgate reads 'horsemen' (equitum) rather than the Hebrew 'chariots', and condenses the Hebrew phrase 'who was over the house in Tirzah' into 'the prefect of Tirzah' (præfecti Thersa).
- **16:28** — Following the Septuagint (3 Kingdoms) placement of the Jehoshaphat summary, the material numbered 22:47-50 in the Saints Latin Critical Text stands here after Omri's death and Ahab's accession; 'Asa his father' signals the shift of subject to Jehoshaphat, whose name appears midway through.
- **16:34** — The Vulgate's literal preposition 'in' (in Abiram ... in Segub) reflects the Hebrew idiom of price ('at the cost of'), fulfilling Joshua's curse on the rebuilder of Jericho (Joshua 6:26).
