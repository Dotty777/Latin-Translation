## Text

**9:1** Now it happened, when Solomon had completed the building of the house of the Lord, and the building of the king, and everything that he had desired and wanted to do,

**9:2** that the Lord appeared to him a second time, just as He had appeared to him in Gibeon.

**9:3** And the Lord said to him, "I have heard your prayer and your plea, which you pleaded before Me; I have sanctified this house that you built, so that I might place My name there forever, and My eyes and My heart will be there for all days.

**9:4** You also, if you walk before Me just as your father walked, in simplicity of heart and in uprightness, and do everything that I have commanded you, and observe My statutes and My judgments,

**9:5** I will set the throne of your kingdom over Israel forever, just as I spoke to David your father, saying, 'A man of your lineage will not be removed from the throne of Israel.'

**9:6** But if you completely turn away, you and your sons, not following Me, nor keeping My commandments and My ceremonies that I have set before you, but go away and serve foreign gods and worship them,

**9:7** then I will remove Israel from the surface of the land that I gave them, and the temple that I sanctified for My name I will cast away from My sight; and Israel will become a proverb and a tale to all peoples.

**9:8** And this house will become an example; everyone who passes by it will be astonished and will hiss, and will say, 'Why has the Lord done so to this land and to this house?'

**9:9** And they will answer, 'Because they abandoned the Lord their God, who brought their fathers out of the land of Egypt, and followed foreign gods, and worshiped them, and served them; therefore the Lord has brought all this evil upon them.'" Now Pharaoh's daughter went up from the city of David into her own house, which Solomon had built for her; then he built Millo.

**9:10** Now when twenty years had been completed after Solomon had built the two houses, that is, the house of the Lord and the house of the king

**9:11** (with Hiram, king of Tyre, supplying Solomon with cedar and fir wood, and gold according to all that he had needed), then Solomon gave Hiram twenty towns in the land of Galilee.

**9:12** And Hiram went out from Tyre to see the towns that Solomon had given him, and they did not please him.

**9:13** And he said, "Are these the cities that you have given me, brother?" And he called them the land of Cabul, up to this day.

**9:14** Hiram also sent to King Solomon one hundred twenty talents of gold.

**9:26** King Solomon also built a fleet at Ezion-geber, which is near Elath on the shore of the Red Sea, in the land of Edom.

**9:27** And Hiram sent in that fleet his servants, men who were sailors and knowledgeable about the sea, with the servants of Solomon.

**9:28** And when they had come to Ophir, they took from there four hundred twenty talents of gold and brought it to King Solomon.

## Translator Notes

- **9:8** — The Vulgate reads "will become an example" (in exemplum), portraying the ruined house as a cautionary sign, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "will be high" (often understood or emended as "a heap of ruins").
- **9:9** — The notice of Pharaoh's daughter moving into her house and the building of Millo (Vulgate 9:24, so numbered in the Saints Latin Critical Text) stands here, where 3 Kingdoms places it.
