## Text

**6:1** And the king commanded that they take great stones, costly stones for the foundation of the temple, and square them. Therefore it came about in the four hundred and eightieth year from the departure of the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt, in the fourth year of the reign of Solomon over Israel, in the month of Ziv (that is the second month), that the house began to be built for the Lord. In the fourth year the house of the Lord was founded in the month of Ziv; and in the eleventh year, in the month of Bul (that is the eighth month), the house was completed in all its work and in all its utensils; and he built it in seven years.

**6:2** Now the house that King Solomon was building for the Lord had sixty cubits in length, and twenty cubits in width, and thirty cubits in height.

**6:3** And there was a portico before the temple, twenty cubits in length, according to the measure of the width of the temple; and it had ten cubits in width before the face of the temple.

**6:4** And he made oblique windows in the temple.

**6:5** And he built stories against the wall of the temple in a circuit, on the walls of the house around the temple and the oracle, and he made side chambers all around.

**6:6** The story that was underneath had five cubits in width, and the middle story was six cubits in width, and the third story had seven cubits in width. Moreover, he placed beams on the house all around on the outside, so that they would not attach to the walls of the temple.

**6:7** Now the house, as it was being built, was built of hewn and finished stones; and no hammer, nor axe, nor any iron tool was heard in the house as it was being built.

**6:8** The door of the middle side chamber was on the right side of the house; and by a winding staircase they went up into the middle upper chamber, and from the middle into the third.

**6:9** And he built the house and finished it; he also covered the house with paneled ceilings of cedar.

**6:10** And he built a story over the entire house, five cubits in height, and he overlaid the house with cedar wood.

**6:15** And he built the walls of the house on the inside with cedar paneling; from the floor of the house up to the top of the walls, and up to the paneled ceilings, he overlaid them with cedar wood on the inside; and he covered the floor of the house with fir planks.

**6:16** And he built cedar paneling of twenty cubits at the rear part of the temple, from the floor up to the upper parts; and he made the inner house of the oracle as the Holy of Holies.

**6:17** Moreover, the temple itself before the doors of the oracle was forty cubits.

**6:19** Now he had made the oracle in the middle of the house, in the interior part, so that he might place there the ark of the covenant of the Lord.

**6:20** Furthermore, the oracle had twenty cubits in length, and twenty cubits in width, and twenty cubits in height; and he overlaid and clad it with the purest gold; and he also clad the altar with cedar.

**6:21** He also overlaid the house before the oracle with the purest gold, and fastened the plates with golden nails.

**6:22** And there was nothing in the temple that was not covered with gold; and he also covered the entire altar of the oracle with gold.

**6:23** And he made in the oracle two cherubim of olive wood, ten cubits in height.

**6:24** One wing of the cherub was five cubits, and the other wing of the cherub was five cubits; that is, having ten cubits from the tip of one wing to the tip of the other wing.

**6:25** The second cherub was also ten cubits; the two cherubim were of equal measure and one workmanship,

**6:26** that is, one cherub had a height of ten cubits, and likewise the second cherub.

**6:27** And he placed the cherubim in the middle of the inner temple; and the cherubim stretched out their wings, and one wing touched the wall, and the wing of the second cherub touched the other wall; while the other wings touched one another in the middle part of the temple.

**6:28** He also covered the cherubim with gold.

**6:29** And he carved all the walls of the temple all around with varied engravings and turned work; and he made on them cherubim, and palm trees, and various figures, as if projecting from the wall and coming out.

**6:30** And he also covered the floor of the house with gold inside and outside.

**6:31** And at the entrance of the oracle he made small doors of olive wood, and doorposts of five angles.

**6:32** And two doors of olive wood; and he carved on them the figure of cherubim, and likenesses of palm trees, and greatly projecting reliefs; and he covered them with gold, and overlaid both the cherubim and the palm trees, and the rest, with gold.

**6:33** And he made at the entrance of the temple four-angled doorposts of olive wood,

**6:34** and two doors of fir wood on either side; and each door was double, and opened while holding together.

**6:35** And he carved cherubim, and palm trees, and very prominent engravings; and he overlaid all of them with golden plates in squared work according to the rule.

**6:36** And he built the inner court with three courses of polished stones, and one course of cedar timbers.

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The Clementine Vulgate spells the second month 'Zio' (a manuscript variant of 'Ziu' for Hebrew 'Ziv'), agrees with the Hebrew Masoretic Text in reading the 'four hundred and eightieth' year (Septuagint: 'four hundred and fortieth'), and renders 'in universis utensilibus suis' ('in all its utensils') where the Hebrew reads 'in all its specifications.'
- **6:4** — Latin 'fenestras obliquas' ('oblique windows') interprets the debated Hebrew architectural phrase as splayed or slanted embrasure windows cut through the thick temple wall.
- **6:5** — Throughout chapter 6, the Vulgate renders the inner sanctuary (Hebrew 'debir') as 'oraculum' ('oracle'), connecting it etymologically with the Hebrew root for speaking as the chamber of divine utterance.
- **6:6** — The Vulgate reads 'trabes' ('beams') placed around the outside of the house, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to stepped ledges or offsets cut into the outer temple wall.
- **6:21** — The Vulgate reads 'affixit laminas clavis aureis' ('fastened the plates with golden nails'), diverging from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which speaks of drawing golden chains across in front of the inner sanctuary.
