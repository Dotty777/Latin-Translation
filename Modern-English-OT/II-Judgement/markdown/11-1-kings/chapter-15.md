## Text

**15:1** So in the eighteenth year of the reign of Jeroboam son of Nebat, Abijam reigned over Judah.

**15:2** For three years he reigned in Jerusalem; the name of his mother was Maacah, daughter of Absalom.

**15:3** And he walked in all the sins of his father, which he had done before him; and his heart was not perfect with the Lord his God, like the heart of David his father.

**15:4** But for David's sake the Lord his God gave him a lamp in Jerusalem, so that He might raise up his son after him and establish Jerusalem,

**15:5** because David had done what was right in the eyes of the Lord, and had not turned aside from all that He had commanded him all the days of his life, except in the matter of Uriah the Hittite.

**15:7** Now the rest of the matters of Abijam, and all that he did, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Judah? And there was battle between Abijam and Jeroboam.

**15:8** And Abijam slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David; and Asa his son reigned in his place.

**15:9** Therefore in the twentieth year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Asa reigned as king of Judah,

**15:10** and for forty-one years he reigned in Jerusalem. The name of his mother was Maacah, daughter of Absalom.

**15:11** And Asa did what was right before the sight of the Lord, like David his father;

**15:12** and he removed the effeminate men from the land, and purged all the filth of the idols that his fathers had made.

**15:13** Moreover, he also removed Maacah his mother, so that she would not be leader in the sacred rites of Priapus and in his grove that she had consecrated; and he overthrew his cavern, and shattered the most shameful image, and burned it in the brook Kidron;

**15:14** yet he did not remove the high places. Nevertheless, the heart of Asa was perfect with the Lord all his days;

**15:15** and he brought the things that his father had sanctified and vowed into the house of the Lord: silver, and gold, and vessels.

**15:16** Now there was war between Asa and Baasha king of Israel all their days.

**15:17** Baasha king of Israel also went up against Judah and built Ramah, so that no one could go out or come in from the side of Asa king of Judah.

**15:18** Therefore Asa took all the silver and gold that had remained in the treasuries of the house of the Lord and in the treasuries of the royal house, and gave it into the hands of his servants; and he sent to Ben-hadad son of Tabrimmon, son of Hezion, king of Syria, who lived in Damascus, saying,

**15:19** "There is a treaty between me and you, and between my father and your father; therefore I have sent you gifts, silver and gold, and I ask that you come and make void the treaty that you have with Baasha king of Israel, so that he may withdraw from me."

**15:20** Consenting to King Asa, Ben-hadad sent the commanders of his army against the cities of Israel, and they struck Ijon, and Dan, and Abel-beth-maacah, and all Chinneroth, namely, all the land of Naphtali.

**15:21** When Baasha heard this, he stopped building Ramah and returned to Tirzah.

**15:22** Then King Asa sent a message into all Judah, saying, "Let no one be excused." And they carried away the stones from Ramah and its timber, with which Baasha had built, and out of them King Asa constructed Geba of Benjamin and Mizpah.

**15:23** Now the rest of all the matters of Asa, and all his mighty deeds, and everything that he did, and the cities that he constructed, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Judah? Nevertheless, in the time of his old age he suffered pain in his feet.

**15:24** And he slept with his fathers, and was buried with them in the city of David his father. And Jehoshaphat his son reigned in his place.

**15:25** Now Nadab son of Jeroboam reigned over Israel in the second year of Asa king of Judah; and he reigned over Israel for two years.

**15:26** And he did what is evil in the sight of the Lord, and walked in the ways of his father, and in his sins by which he caused Israel to sin.

**15:27** Now Baasha son of Ahijah, of the house of Issachar, conspired against him and struck him at Gibbethon, which is a city of the Philistines; for indeed Nadab and all Israel were besieging Gibbethon.

**15:28** Therefore Baasha killed him in the third year of Asa king of Judah, and reigned in his place.

**15:29** And when he had begun to reign, he struck the entire house of Jeroboam; he did not leave even a single soul of his seed until he destroyed him, according to the word of the Lord which He had spoken by the hand of His servant Ahijah the Shilonite,

**15:30** because of the sins of Jeroboam, which he had sinned and by which he had caused Israel to sin, and because of the offense by which he had provoked the Lord God of Israel.

**15:31** Now the rest of the matters of Nadab, and all that he performed, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?

**15:33** In the third year of Asa king of Judah, Baasha son of Ahijah reigned over all Israel in Tirzah for twenty-four years.

**15:34** And he did evil before the Lord, and walked in the way of Jeroboam, and in his sins by which he caused Israel to sin.

## Translator Notes

- **15:7** — Verse 6 is absent in this Clementine Vulgate text, with the battle notice between Abijam and Jeroboam appearing at the end of verse 7. The Vulgate's 'book of the words of the days' (in libro verborum dierum) literally reflects the Hebrew title for the royal annals or chronicles.
- **15:12** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew term for male cult prostitutes (qedeshim) as 'effeminate men' (effeminatos), a rendering repeated in 16:28.
- **15:13** — Distinctly Vulgate reading: Jerome interpreted the Hebrew 'horrid image for Asherah' in terms of the Greco-Roman fertility god Priapus ('in the sacred rites of Priapus', in sacris Priapi), rendering Asherah as 'grove' (luco) and adding 'his cavern' (specum ejus) and 'the most shameful image' (simulacrum turpissimum).
- **15:20** — The Vulgate half-translates the place name Abel-beth-maacah as Abeldomum Maacha ('Abel-house of Maacah') and inserts 'namely' (scilicet) to equate all Chinneroth with all the land of Naphtali.
- **15:31** — Verse 32 (which in the Hebrew repeats the war notice between Asa and Baasha from verse 16) is absent from this Clementine Vulgate text.
