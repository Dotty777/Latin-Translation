## Text

**2:1** Now the days drew near for David to die, and he commanded Solomon his son, saying:

**2:2** "I am entering the way of all the earth; be strong, and be a man.

**2:3** And observe the charges of the Lord your God, to walk in His ways, to keep His ceremonies, and His precepts, and judgments, and testimonies, as it is written in the law of Moses, so that you may understand all that you do, and wherever you turn,

**2:4** so that the Lord may confirm His words which He spoke concerning me, saying, 'If your sons keep their ways, and walk before Me in truth, with all their heart and with all their soul, no man will be removed from you from the throne of Israel.'

**2:5** You also know what Joab the son of Zeruiah did to me, what he did to the two commanders of the army of Israel, Abner the son of Ner and Amasa the son of Jether, whom he killed, and shed the blood of war in peace, and put the blood of battle on his belt that was around his waist and on his footwear that was on his feet.

**2:6** Therefore you will act according to your wisdom, and you will not bring his gray hair down peacefully to the underworld.

**2:7** But to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite also you will return kindness, and they will be eating at your table; for they came to meet me when I was fleeing from the face of Absalom your brother.

**2:8** You also have with you Shimei the son of Gera, the son of Jemini, from Bahurim, who cursed me with a very wicked curse when I was going to the camp; but because he came down to meet me when I was crossing the Jordan, and I swore to him by the Lord, saying, 'I will not put you to death with the sword,'

**2:9** you must not allow him to go unpunished. Now you are a wise man, so that you know what you will do to him, and you will bring his gray hairs down with blood to the underworld."

**2:10** So David slept with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David.

**2:11** Now the days in which David reigned over Israel are forty years: in Hebron he reigned seven years; in Jerusalem, thirty-three.

**2:12** And Solomon sat on the throne of David his father, and his kingdom was made exceedingly firm.

**2:13** And Adonijah the son of Haggith came in to Bathsheba the mother of Solomon. And she said to him, "Is your coming peaceful?" And he answered, "Peaceful."

**2:14** And he added, "I have a word for you." To him she said, "Speak." And he replied:

**2:15** "You," he said, "know that the kingdom was mine, and all Israel had set me over themselves as king; yet the kingdom has been transferred and has become my brother's, for it was appointed to him by the Lord.

**2:16** Now therefore I ask one request of you: do not put my face to shame." And she said to him, "Speak."

**2:17** And he said, "I beg that you speak to King Solomon (for he cannot deny you anything) that he give me Abishag the Shunammite as a wife."

**2:18** And Bathsheba said, "Very well; I will speak for you to the king."

**2:19** So Bathsheba came to King Solomon to speak to him for Adonijah; and the king rose to meet her, and bowed down to her, and sat on his throne; and a throne was set for the king's mother, who sat at his right hand.

**2:20** And she said to him, "One little request I ask of you: do not put my face to shame." And the king said to her, "Ask, my mother, for it is not right that I should turn away your face."

**2:21** And she said, "Let Abishag the Shunammite be given to Adonijah your brother as a wife."

**2:22** And King Solomon answered and said to his mother, "Why do you ask for Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? Ask for the kingdom for him as well, for he is my brother older than I, and he has Abiathar the priest and Joab the son of Zeruiah."

**2:23** Therefore King Solomon swore by the Lord, saying, "May God do these things to me, and add these things, for Adonijah has spoken this word against his own life.

**2:24** And now, as the Lord lives, who has established me and set me on the throne of David my father, and who has made a house for me as He spoke, today Adonijah will be killed."

**2:25** And King Solomon sent by the hand of Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, who put him to death, and he died.

**2:26** The king also said to Abiathar the priest, "Go to Anathoth, to your field; indeed you are a man of death, but today I will not put you to death, because you carried the ark of the Lord God before David my father, and you endured toil in all things in which my father toiled."

**2:27** So Solomon cast out Abiathar, that he might not be priest of the Lord, so that the word of the Lord might be fulfilled which He spoke concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh.

**2:28** Now a report came to Joab, for Joab had turned aside after Adonijah, and had not turned aside after Solomon; therefore Joab fled into the tabernacle of the Lord, and took hold of the horn of the altar.

**2:29** And it was reported to King Solomon that Joab had fled into the tabernacle of the Lord and was beside the altar; and Solomon sent Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, saying, "Go, put him to death."

**2:30** And Benaiah came to the tabernacle of the Lord and said to him, "This is what the king says: 'Come out.'" And he said, "I will not come out, but I will die here." Benaiah reported the word back to the king, saying, "This is what Joab spoke, and this is what he answered me."

**2:31** And the king said to him, "Do as he has spoken, and put him to death, and bury him; and you will remove the innocent blood that was shed by Joab from me and from the house of my father.

**2:32** And the Lord will return his blood upon his head, because he killed two righteous men, better than himself, and cut them down with the sword, while my father David was unaware, Abner the son of Ner, commander of the forces of Israel, and Amasa the son of Jether, commander of the army of Judah;

**2:33** and their blood will return upon the head of Joab and upon the head of his seed forever. But for David, and for his seed, and for his house, and for his throne, may there be peace to all eternity from the Lord."

**2:34** So Benaiah the son of Jehoiada went up, and attacking him, put him to death; and he was buried in his house in the wilderness.

**2:35** And the king appointed Benaiah the son of Jehoiada in his place over the army, and he set Zadok the priest in place of Abiathar.

**2:36** The king also sent and summoned Shimei, and said to him, "Build a house for yourself in Jerusalem, and live there; and you will not go out from there this way or that.

**2:37** Now on whatever day you go out, and cross the brook Kidron, know that you are to be put to death; your blood will be upon your head."

**2:38** And Shimei said to the king, "The word is good; just as my lord the king has spoken, so your servant will do." So Shimei lived in Jerusalem for many days.

**2:39** Now it happened after three years that servants of Shimei fled to Achish the son of Maacah, king of Gath; and it was reported to Shimei that his servants had gone to Gath.

**2:40** And Shimei rose, and saddled his donkey, and went to Achish in Gath to search for his servants, and brought them from Gath.

**2:41** Now it was reported to Solomon that Shimei had gone to Gath from Jerusalem and had returned.

**2:42** And he sent and summoned him, and said to him, "Did I not solemnly warn you by the Lord, and tell you beforehand, 'On whatever day you leave and go this way and that, know that you are going to die,' and you answered me, 'The word that I have heard is good'?

**2:43** Why then did you not keep the oath of the Lord, and the command that I had commanded you?"

**2:44** And the king said to Shimei, "You know all the evil of which your heart is conscious within you, which you did to David my father; the Lord has returned your wickedness upon your head;

**2:45** and King Solomon is blessed, and the throne of David will be steadfast before the Lord forever."

**2:46** So the king ordered Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, who went out and struck him, and he died.

## Translator Notes

- **2:3** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew verb for prospering or acting wisely as ut intelligas ("so that you may understand") and translates Hebrew "statutes" as caeremonias ("ceremonies").
- **2:6** — Latin ad inferos ("to the underworld," here and in 2:9) denotes the lower world or abode of the dead, corresponding to Hebrew Sheol.
- **2:8** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew ben-hayemini ("the Benjaminite") literally as a patronymic, "the son of Jemini," and translates the place name Mahanaim by its literal meaning, "the camp" (ad castra).
- **2:35** — Native Clementine Vulgate keeps 2:35 concise; the notices of the 550 chief officers and thrice-yearly sacrifices (placed in Saints.Bible 2:35 following 3 Kingdoms) stand at native 9:23 and 9:25.
- **2:46** — Native Clementine Vulgate keeps 2:46 concise; the summaries of Solomon's dominion (placed in Saints.Bible 2:46 following 3 Kingdoms) stand at native 4:20, 4:21, and 4:25.
