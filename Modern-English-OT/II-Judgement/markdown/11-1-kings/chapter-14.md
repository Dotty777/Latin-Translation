## Text

**14:1** At that time Abijah the son of Jeroboam fell ill.

**14:2** And Jeroboam said to his wife, "Rise and change your attire, so that you are not recognized as being the wife of Jeroboam, and go to Shiloh, where Ahijah the prophet is, who spoke to me that I would reign over this people.

**14:3** Take also in your hand ten loaves, and a cake, and a jar of honey, and go to him; for he himself will tell you what is going to happen to this boy."

**14:4** The wife of Jeroboam did as he had said; and rising up, she went away to Shiloh and came to the house of Ahijah; but he could not see, because his eyes had grown dim from old age.

**14:5** Now the Lord said to Ahijah, "Look, the wife of Jeroboam is coming in to consult you concerning her son, who is sick; you shall speak this and that to her." Therefore when she was entering and concealing who she was,

**14:6** Ahijah heard the sound of her feet entering through the doorway, and he said, "Come in, wife of Jeroboam! Why do you pretend to be another woman? Yet I have been sent to you as a harsh messenger.

**14:7** Go and say to Jeroboam: This is what the Lord God of Israel says: Because I lifted you up from the midst of the people and appointed you leader over My people Israel,

**14:8** and tore apart the kingdom of the house of David and gave it to you, and you have not been like My servant David, who kept My commandments and followed Me with his whole heart, doing what was pleasing in My sight:

**14:9** but you have done evil beyond all who were before you, and have made for yourself foreign gods and cast images to provoke Me to anger, and you have thrown Me behind your body:

**14:10** Therefore, look, I will bring evils upon the house of Jeroboam, and I will strike down from Jeroboam the one who urinates against a wall, both the confined and the last in Israel; and I will clean out the remnants of the house of Jeroboam, just as dung is usually cleaned out until it is clean.

**14:11** Those of Jeroboam who die in the city, dogs will eat; and those who die in the field, the birds of the sky will devour; for the Lord has spoken.

**14:12** You, therefore, rise and go to your house; and at the very entrance of your feet into the city, the boy will die,

**14:13** and all Israel will mourn him and bury him; for this one alone from Jeroboam will be carried into a tomb, because a good word from the Lord God of Israel has been found concerning him in the house of Jeroboam.

**14:14** Moreover, the Lord will appoint for Himself a king over Israel, who will strike the house of Jeroboam on this day and at this time:

**14:15** and the Lord God will strike Israel just as a reed is swayed in the water; and He will uproot Israel from this good land, which He gave to their fathers, and He will scatter them beyond the river, because they made sacred groves for themselves to anger the Lord.

**14:16** And the Lord will hand over Israel because of the sins of Jeroboam, who sinned and caused Israel to sin."

**14:17** Therefore the wife of Jeroboam rose, went away, and came to Tirzah; and as she was entering the threshold of the house, the boy died,

**14:18** and they buried him. And all Israel mourned him according to the word of the Lord, which He spoke by the hand of His servant Ahijah the prophet.

**14:19** Now the rest of the matters of Jeroboam, how he fought and how he reigned—look, they are written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel.

**14:20** Now the days in which Jeroboam reigned were twenty-two years; and he slept with his fathers, and Nadab his son reigned in his place.

**14:21** Now Rehoboam the son of Solomon reigned in Judah. Rehoboam was forty-one years old when he began to reign; he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city that the Lord chose out of all the tribes of Israel to place His name there. And his mother's name was Naamah the Ammonite.

**14:22** And Judah did evil before the Lord, and they provoked Him above all that their fathers had done, in their sins that they committed.

**14:23** For they also built for themselves altars, and statues, and groves upon every high hill and under every leafy tree.

**14:24** Moreover, there were also effeminate men in the land, and they did all the abominations of the nations that the Lord crushed before the face of the sons of Israel.

**14:25** Now in the fifth year of the reign of Rehoboam, Shishak king of Egypt went up against Jerusalem,

**14:26** and took the treasures of the house of the Lord and the royal treasures, and plundered everything, as well as the golden shields that Solomon had made;

**14:27** in place of which King Rehoboam made bronze shields, and delivered them into the hand of the commanders of the shield-bearers and of those who kept watch before the door of the king's house.

**14:28** And whenever the king entered the house of the Lord, those who had the duty of going ahead carried them, and afterward they brought them back to the armory of the shield-bearers.

**14:29** Now the rest of the words of Rehoboam, and all that he did, look, they are written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Judah.

**14:30** And there was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all the days.

**14:31** And Rehoboam slept with his fathers and was buried with them in the city of David; and his mother's name was Naamah the Ammonite; and Abijam his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — Native Clementine Vulgate 14:1-6, 10-13, 17-18 correspond to material placed at Saints.Bible 12:24.
- **14:9** — The Clementine Vulgate literally reads "behind your body" (post corpus tuum), corresponding to the Hebrew idiom "behind your back."
- **14:10** — The Vulgate distinctively reads "both the confined and the last" (et clausum, et novissimum).
- **14:13** — The Vulgate reads "a good word from the Lord God of Israel" (sermo bonus a Domino Deo Israël).
- **14:15** — The Clementine Vulgate renders the idolatrous Asherah poles of the Hebrew as "sacred groves" (lucos).
- **14:23** — The Vulgate renders the three Hebrew cultic terms (high places, sacred pillars, and Asherah poles) as "aras" ("altars"), "statuas" ("statues"), and "lucos" ("groves").
- **14:24** — Latin "effeminati" ("effeminate men") renders the Hebrew term for a male cult prostitute, and "attrivit" ("crushed") corresponds to Hebrew "drove out."
- **14:27** — Latin "scutariorum" ("shield-bearers," also in v. 28 with "those who had the duty of going ahead" and "armory") renders the Hebrew royal guard ("runners") and their guardroom.
- **14:29** — Latin "ecce scripta sunt" ("look, they are written") is declarative rather than the Hebrew rhetorical question ("are they not written?"), and "sermonum" and "libro sermonum dierum" literally reproduce the Hebrew idioms for royal acts and annals.
