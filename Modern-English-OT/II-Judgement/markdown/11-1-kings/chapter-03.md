## Text

**3:1** Therefore the kingdom was established in the hand of Solomon, and he was joined in marriage alliance to Pharaoh, king of Egypt; for he took his daughter and brought her into the city of David, until he finished building his own house, and the house of the Lord, and the wall of Jerusalem all around.

**3:2** However, the people were sacrificing on the high places, for a temple had not been built for the name of the Lord until that day.

**3:3** Now Solomon loved the Lord, walking in the precepts of David his father, except that he was sacrificing on the high places and burning incense.

**3:4** So he went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, for that was the greatest high place. Solomon offered a thousand sacrifices as a burnt offering on that altar in Gibeon.

**3:5** Now the Lord appeared to Solomon through a dream at night, saying, "Ask what you want Me to give you."

**3:6** And Solomon said, "You have shown great mercy to Your servant David my father, just as he walked in Your sight in truth and righteousness and with an upright heart toward You. You have kept Your great mercy for him, and You have given him a son sitting on his throne, as it is today.

**3:7** And now, Lord God, You have made Your servant reign in place of David my father, yet I am a little child and do not know my going out and my coming in.

**3:8** And Your servant is in the midst of the people whom You have chosen, a boundless people who cannot be numbered or reckoned because of their multitude.

**3:9** Therefore give Your servant a teachable heart, so that he may be able to judge Your people and discern between good and evil. For who will be able to judge this people, this numerous people of Yours?"

**3:10** Therefore the word was pleasing before the Lord, that Solomon had asked for such a thing.

**3:11** And the Lord said to Solomon, "Because you have asked for this matter, and have not sought for yourself many days, nor riches, nor the lives of your enemies, but have asked for yourself wisdom to discern justice,

**3:12** look, I have done for you according to your words, and I have given you a wise and understanding heart, to such an extent that no one like you has been before you, nor will arise after you.

**3:13** Moreover, I have also given you these things that you did not ask for: namely, riches and glory, so that no one among the kings has been like you in all past days.

**3:14** And if you walk in My ways, and keep My precepts and My commandments, just as your father walked, I will make your days long."

**3:15** Therefore Solomon awoke and realized that it was a dream. And when he had come to Jerusalem, he stood before the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and offered burnt offerings, and made peace offerings and a great banquet for all his servants.

**3:16** Then two prostitute women came to the king and stood before him.

**3:17** One of them said, "I implore you, my lord: I and this woman were living in one house, and I gave birth with her in the bedroom.

**3:18** And on the third day after I gave birth, this woman also gave birth; and we were together, and no one else was with us in the house except the two of us.

**3:19** Now the son of this woman died during the night, for while sleeping she crushed him.

**3:20** And rising up in the silence of the dead of night, she took my son from my side while your maidservant was sleeping, and placed him in her bosom; but her own son, who was dead, she laid in my bosom.

**3:21** And when I had risen in the morning to give milk to my son, he appeared dead. Looking at him more closely in the clear light, I discovered that he was not my own whom I had borne."

**3:22** And the other woman answered, "It is not as you say, but your son is dead, and mine is alive." On the contrary, the first kept saying, "You are lying! For my son is alive, and your son is dead." And in this manner they were arguing before the king.

**3:23** Then the king said, "This one says, 'My son is alive, and your son is dead,' and that one replied, 'No, rather your son is dead, and mine is alive.'"

**3:24** Therefore the king said, "Bring me a sword." And when they had brought a sword before the king,

**3:25** he said, "Divide the living infant into two parts, and give half to the one and half to the other."

**3:26** Now the woman whose son was alive said to the king (for her innermost being was moved over her son), "I implore you, lord, give her the living infant, and do not kill him." On the contrary, the other was saying, "Let him be neither mine nor yours, but let him be divided."

**3:27** The king answered and said, "Give this woman the living infant, and let him not be put to death, for she is his mother."

**3:28** So all Israel heard the judgment that the king had judged, and they feared the king, seeing that the wisdom of God was in him to do justice.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Native Clementine Vulgate opens chapter 3 with Solomon's marriage alliance (grouped with 4:34 and 9:16 at Saints.Bible 5:14 following 3 Kingdoms).
- **3:8** — Latin populi infiniti ("a boundless people") is a distinctive Vulgate rendering where the Hebrew reads "a great/numerous people."
- **3:9** — Latin cor docile literally means "a teachable heart," differing from the Hebrew idiom "a listening heart"; Dabis is literally future indicative ("You will give") used with the force of a petition.
- **3:13** — Latin cunctis retro diebus ("in all past days") is a distinctive Vulgate reading comparing Solomon to all earlier kings, whereas the Hebrew reads "all your days."
- **3:17** — Latin in cubiculo specifies "in the bedroom" (or chamber), whereas the Hebrew reads "in the house."
- **3:22** — Latin Mentiris ("You are lying!") is a distinctive Vulgate expansion where the Hebrew simply reads "No."
