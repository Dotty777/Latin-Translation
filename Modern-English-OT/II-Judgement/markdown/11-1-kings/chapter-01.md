## Text

**1:1** And King David had grown old and had very many days of age; and although he was covered with garments, he did not grow warm.

**1:2** Therefore his servants said to him, "Let us seek a young woman, a virgin, for our lord the king, and let her stand before the king and care for him, and let her sleep in his bosom and warm our lord the king."

**1:3** So they sought a lovely young woman throughout all the borders of Israel, and they found Abishag the Shunammite, and brought her to the king.

**1:4** Now the girl was exceedingly beautiful, and she slept with the king and served him; however, the king did not know her.

**1:5** Now Adonijah the son of Haggith was exalting himself, saying, "I will reign." And he prepared for himself chariots and horsemen, and fifty men to run before him.

**1:6** Nor did his father ever reprove him, saying, "Why have you done this?" Moreover, he himself was also very handsome, second by birth after Absalom.

**1:7** And he had discussion with Joab the son of Zeruiah and with Abiathar the priest, who were supporting the side of Adonijah.

**1:8** But Zadok the priest, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and Nathan the prophet, and Shimei and Rei, and the strength of David's army were not with Adonijah.

**1:9** Therefore Adonijah, having sacrificed rams and calves and all the fattened beasts beside the stone of Zoheleth, which was near the spring of Rogel, called all his brothers, the sons of the king, and all the men of Judah, the servants of the king.

**1:10** But Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah, and all the strong men, and Solomon his brother he did not call.

**1:11** So Nathan said to Bathsheba the mother of Solomon, "Have you heard that Adonijah the son of Haggith has begun to reign, and our lord David does not know this?

**1:12** Now therefore come, take counsel from me, and save your life and the life of your son Solomon.

**1:13** Go, and go in to King David, and say to him, 'Did you not, my lord the king, swear to me your maidservant, saying, "Solomon your son will reign after me, and he himself will sit on my throne"? Why then is Adonijah reigning?'

**1:14** And while you are still speaking there with the king, I will come after you and complete your words."

**1:15** So Bathsheba went in to the king in the bedchamber; now the king had grown exceedingly old, and Abishag the Shunammite was serving him.

**1:16** Bathsheba bowed down and paid homage to the king. To her the king said, "What do you want?"

**1:17** She answered and said, "My lord, you swore by the Lord your God to your maidservant, 'Solomon your son will reign after me, and he himself will sit on my throne.'

**1:18** And look, now Adonijah is reigning, while you, my lord the king, do not know it.

**1:19** He has slaughtered oxen, and all the fattened beasts, and very many rams, and has called all the sons of the king, Abiathar the priest also, and Joab the commander of the forces; but Solomon your servant he has not called.

**1:20** However, my lord the king, the eyes of all Israel are looking toward you, that you may declare to them who ought to sit on your throne, my lord the king, after you.

**1:21** And it will be that when my lord the king sleeps with his fathers, I and my son Solomon will be sinners."

**1:22** While she was still speaking with the king, Nathan the prophet came.

**1:23** And they announced to the king, saying, "Nathan the prophet is here." And when he had entered into the presence of the king and had paid homage to him, face down to the ground,

**1:24** Nathan said, "My lord the king, have you said, 'Adonijah shall reign after me, and he himself shall sit upon my throne'?

**1:25** For he has gone down today, and has sacrificed oxen, and fattened beasts, and very many rams, and has called all the sons of the king and the commanders of the army, and Abiathar the priest also; and while they are eating and drinking before him and saying, 'May King Adonijah live,'

**1:26** me your servant, and Zadok the priest, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and Solomon your servant he has not called.

**1:27** Has this word come out from my lord the king, and have you not declared to me, your servant, who was going to sit upon the throne of my lord the king after him?"

**1:28** And King David answered, saying, "Call Bathsheba to me." And when she had gone in before the king and had stood before him,

**1:29** the king swore and said, "As the Lord lives, who has delivered my life from every distress,

**1:30** surely, just as I swore to you by the Lord God of Israel, saying, 'Solomon your son will reign after me, and he himself will sit upon my throne in my place,' so I will do today."

**1:31** And Bathsheba, having lowered her face to the ground, paid homage to the king, saying, "May my lord David live forever."

**1:32** King David also said, "Call to me Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada." And when they had gone in before the king,

**1:33** he said to them, "Take with you the servants of your lord, and set my son Solomon upon my mule, and lead him to Gihon.

**1:34** And let Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anoint him there as king over Israel; and you will sound the trumpet and say, 'May King Solomon live!'

**1:35** And you will come up after him, and he will come and sit upon my throne, and he himself will reign in my place; and I will command him to be leader over Israel and over Judah."

**1:36** And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada answered the king, saying, "Amen! So may the Lord God of my lord the king speak.

**1:37** Just as the Lord was with my lord the king, so may He be with Solomon, and may He make his throne higher than the throne of my lord King David."

**1:38** Therefore Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites, and the Pelethites went down; and they set Solomon upon the mule of King David and brought him to Gihon.

**1:39** And Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the tabernacle and anointed Solomon; and they sounded the trumpet, and all the people said, "May King Solomon live!"

**1:40** And the entire multitude went up after him, and the people, playing flutes and rejoicing with great joy; and the earth resounded from their outcry.

**1:41** Now Adonijah and all who had been invited by him heard it, and the banquet was already finished; and Joab also, having heard the sound of the trumpet, said, "What does the outcry of the city in tumult mean?"

**1:42** While he was still speaking, Jonathan the son of Abiathar the priest came; to whom Adonijah said, "Come in, for you are a valiant man and bringing good news."

**1:43** And Jonathan answered Adonijah, "By no means! For our lord King David has appointed Solomon as king;

**1:44** and he sent with him Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites, and the Pelethites, and they set him upon the king's mule.

**1:45** And Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anointed him king in Gihon; and they came up from there rejoicing, and the city resounded; this is the sound that you heard.

**1:46** And moreover, Solomon is sitting upon the throne of the kingdom.

**1:47** And the servants of the king went in and blessed our lord King David, saying, 'May God enlarge the name of Solomon above your name, and make his throne great above your throne.' And the king worshiped on his bed,

**1:48** and spoke, 'Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who has given today one sitting on my throne, while my eyes see it.'"

**1:49** Therefore all who had been invited by Adonijah were terrified and rose up, and each one went on his way.

**1:50** Now Adonijah, fearing Solomon, rose up and went away, and took hold of the horn of the altar.

**1:51** And they announced to Solomon, saying, "Look, Adonijah, fearing King Solomon, has taken hold of the horn of the altar, saying, 'Let King Solomon swear to me today that he will not kill his servant with the sword.'"

**1:52** And Solomon said, "If he is a good man, not even a single hair of his will fall to the ground; but if evil is found in him, he will die."

**1:53** Therefore King Solomon sent and brought him out from the altar; and having come in, he paid homage to King Solomon; and Solomon said to him, "Go to your house."

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — Vulgate "and she slept with the king" (dormiebatque cum rege) is a distinctive Latin rendering where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "and she became an attendant to the king" and the Septuagint reads "she was warming him."
- **1:21** — Latin "peccatores" ("sinners") literally preserves the biblical idiom for being regarded as guilty offenders or traitors.
- **1:40** — Vulgate "insonuit terra" ("the earth resounded") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which has "the earth split open" from their sound.
- **1:47** — The Clementine Vulgate prints the active verb "magnificet" with the nominative "thronus ejus" (a blend of "magnificet thronum" and passive "magnificetur thronus"); here God is kept as the subject of both verbs.
- **1:50** — The Vulgate reads the singular "cornu altaris" ("the horn of the altar," also in 1:51), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the plural "horns of the altar."
