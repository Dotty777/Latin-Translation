## Text

**8:1** Then all the elders of Israel were gathered together with the chiefs of the tribes, and the leaders of the families of the sons of Israel, to King Solomon in Jerusalem, to bring the ark of the covenant of the Lord from the city of David, that is, from Zion.

**8:2** And all Israel assembled to King Solomon in the month of Ethanim, on the festival day; that is the seventh month.

**8:3** And all the elders from Israel came, and the priests took up the ark,

**8:4** and carried the ark of the Lord, and the tabernacle of the covenant, and all the vessels of the sanctuary that were in the tabernacle; and the priests and the Levites were carrying them.

**8:5** Now King Solomon, and all the multitude of Israel that had assembled to him, were walking with him before the ark, and they were sacrificing sheep and oxen without reckoning or number.

**8:6** And the priests brought the ark of the covenant of the Lord into its place, into the oracle of the temple, into the Holy of Holies, beneath the wings of the cherubim.

**8:7** For indeed the cherubim spread out their wings over the place of the ark, and covered the ark and its poles from above.

**8:8** And although the poles projected and their ends were visible outside the sanctuary before the oracle, they were not visible any further on the outside; and they have been there to the present day.

**8:9** Now in the ark there was nothing else except the two stone tablets that Moses had placed in it at Horeb, when the Lord made a covenant with the sons of Israel, as they were coming out of the land of Egypt.

**8:10** Now it happened, when the priests had come out of the sanctuary, that a cloud filled the house of the Lord,

**8:11** and the priests were unable to stand and minister because of the cloud, for the glory of the Lord had filled the house of the Lord.

**8:12** Then Solomon said, "The Lord said that He would dwell in a cloud.

**8:13** Building, I have built a house for Your dwelling, Your most firm throne forever."

**8:14** And the king turned his face and blessed the whole assembly of Israel; for the whole assembly of Israel was standing.

**8:15** And Solomon said, "Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who spoke with His mouth to my father David and fulfilled it with His hands, saying,

**8:16** 'From the day on which I brought My people Israel out of Egypt, I did not choose a city from all the tribes of Israel so that a house might be built and My name might be there; but I chose David to be over My people Israel.'

**8:17** And my father David desired to build a house for the name of the Lord God of Israel;

**8:18** and the Lord said to my father David, 'Because you thought in your heart to build a house for My name, you did well, considering this very thing in your mind.

**8:19** Nevertheless, you will not build a house for Me, but your son, who will come out from your loins, he himself will build a house for My name.'

**8:20** The Lord has confirmed His word that He spoke; and I stood in place of my father David and sat on the throne of Israel, just as the Lord spoke, and I built a house for the name of the Lord God of Israel.

**8:21** And I appointed there a place for the ark, in which is the covenant of the Lord that He made with our fathers when they came out of the land of Egypt."

**8:22** Now Solomon stood before the altar of the Lord in the sight of the assembly of Israel, and spread out his hands toward heaven,

**8:23** and said, "Lord God of Israel, there is no god like You in heaven above or on the earth below, You who keep covenant and mercy for Your servants who walk before You with all their heart;

**8:24** You who have kept for Your servant my father David what You spoke to him: You spoke with Your mouth, and with Your hands You have fulfilled it, as this day proves.

**8:25** Now therefore, Lord God of Israel, keep for Your servant my father David what You spoke to him, saying, 'A man will not be taken away from you before Me to sit on the throne of Israel, yet only if your sons keep their way, so that they walk before Me just as you walked in My sight.'

**8:26** And now, Lord God of Israel, let Your words be confirmed that You spoke to Your servant my father David.

**8:27** Is it then to be thought that God truly dwells on the earth? For if heaven and the heavens of heavens cannot contain You, how much less this house that I have built?

**8:28** Yet look upon the prayer of Your servant and upon his petitions, Lord my God; hear the hymn and the prayer that Your servant prays before You today,

**8:29** that Your eyes may be open over this house night and day, over the house of which You said, 'My name will be there,' so that You may hear the prayer that Your servant prays to You in this place;

**8:30** that You may hear the supplication of Your servant and of Your people Israel, whatever they pray in this place; and You will hear in the place of Your dwelling in heaven, and when You hear, You will be merciful.

**8:31** If a man sins against his neighbor, and has some oath by which he is held bound, and comes because of the oath before Your altar into Your house,

**8:32** You will hear in heaven, and act, and judge Your servants, condemning the wicked man and returning his way upon his head, and justifying the righteous man and rewarding him according to his righteousness.

**8:33** If Your people Israel flee from their enemies (because they will sin against You), and, repenting and confessing Your name, come and pray and make supplication to You in this house,

**8:34** hear in heaven, and forgive the sin of Your people Israel, and You will bring them back into the land that You gave to their fathers.

**8:35** If heaven is shut up, and it does not rain because of their sins, and praying in this place they repent to Your name, and turn back from their sins because of their affliction,

**8:36** hear them in heaven, and forgive the sins of Your servants and of Your people Israel; and show them the good way along which they should walk, and give rain upon Your land, which You gave to Your people as a possession.

**8:37** If famine arises in the land, or pestilence, or corrupted air, or mildew, or locust, or rust, and their enemy afflicts them, besieging the gates; every plague, every sickness,

**8:38** every curse and imprecation that happens to any man of Your people Israel: if anyone knows the plague of his own heart, and spreads out his hands in this house,

**8:39** You will hear in heaven, in the place of Your dwelling, and be merciful, and act so that You give to each one according to all his ways, just as You see his heart (for You alone know the heart of all the sons of men),

**8:40** so that they may fear You all the days that they live upon the face of the land that You gave to our fathers.

**8:41** Moreover also the foreigner, who is not of Your people Israel, when he comes from a distant land because of Your name (for Your great name will be heard of, and Your strong hand, and Your arm

**8:42** stretched out everywhere), when he comes therefore and prays in this place,

**8:43** You will hear in heaven, in the foundation of Your dwelling, and do everything for which the foreigner calls upon You, so that all the peoples of the lands may learn to fear Your name, just as Your people Israel does, and may verify that Your name has been invoked over this house that I have built.

**8:44** If Your people go out to war against their enemies by the way, wherever You send them, they will pray to You toward the way of the city that You have chosen, and toward the house that I have built for Your name,

**8:45** and You will hear in heaven their prayers and their petitions, and execute their judgment.

**8:46** Now if they sin against You (for there is no man who does not sin), and being angry You hand them over to their enemies, and they are led captive into the land of the enemies, far or near,

**8:47** and they repent in their heart in the place of captivity, and turning back they make supplication to You in their captivity, saying, 'We have sinned; we have acted iniquitously, we have done wickedly,'

**8:48** and they return to You with all their heart and all their soul in the land of their enemies to which they were led captive, and pray to You toward the way of their land, which You gave to their fathers, and of the city that You have chosen, and of the temple that I have built for Your name,

**8:49** You will hear in heaven, in the foundation of Your throne, their prayers and their petitions, and execute their judgment;

**8:50** and You will pardon Your people who have sinned against You, and all their iniquities by which they have transgressed against You; and You will grant mercy before those who hold them captive, so that they may have mercy on them.

**8:51** For they are Your people and Your inheritance, whom You brought out of the land of Egypt, from the midst of the iron furnace.

**8:52** May Your eyes be open to the supplication of Your servant and of Your people Israel, and may You hear them in all things for which they call upon You.

**8:53** For You separated them for Yourself as an inheritance from all the peoples of the earth, just as You spoke through Your servant Moses when You brought our fathers out of Egypt, Lord God."

**8:54** Now it happened, when Solomon had finished praying all this prayer and supplication to the Lord, that he rose from before the altar of the Lord; for he had fixed both knees on the ground and had spread out his hands toward heaven.

**8:55** Therefore he stood and blessed the whole assembly of Israel with a loud voice, saying,

**8:56** "Blessed be the Lord, who has given rest to His people Israel according to all that He spoke; not even one word has fallen from all the good things that He spoke through His servant Moses.

**8:57** May the Lord our God be with us, just as He was with our fathers, not abandoning nor casting us away.

**8:58** But may He incline our hearts to Himself, so that we may walk in all His ways and keep His commandments, and His ceremonies, and whatever judgments He commanded our fathers.

**8:59** And may these words of mine, with which I have made supplication before the Lord, be drawing near to the Lord our God day and night, so that He may execute judgment for His servant and for His people Israel day by day,

**8:60** so that all the peoples of the earth may know that the Lord Himself is God, and there is no other besides Him.

**8:61** May our heart also be perfect with the Lord our God, so that we may walk in His decrees and keep His commandments, just as also today."

**8:62** Therefore the king, and all Israel with him, were sacrificing victims before the Lord.

**8:63** And Solomon slaughtered peace offerings, which he sacrificed to the Lord: twenty-two thousand oxen and one hundred twenty thousand sheep; and the king and the sons of Israel dedicated the temple of the Lord.

**8:64** On that day the king sanctified the middle of the courtyard that was before the house of the Lord; for indeed he offered the burnt offering there, and the sacrifice, and the fat of the peace offerings, because the bronze altar that was before the Lord was too small and could not contain the burnt offering, and the sacrifice, and the fat of the peace offerings.

**8:65** Therefore Solomon held a celebrated festival at that time, and all Israel with him, a great multitude from the entrance of Hamath as far as the brook of Egypt, before the Lord our God, for seven days and seven days, that is, fourteen days.

**8:66** And on the eighth day he dismissed the peoples; and blessing the king, they departed to their tents rejoicing and with a cheerful heart over all the good things that the Lord had done for His servant David and for His people Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **8:6** — Latin oraculum ("oracle," also in 8:8) renders the inner sanctuary as the place of divine speech, reflecting Jerome's etymological translation of Hebrew debir.
- **8:8** — Latin foris sanctuarium literally reads "outside the sanctuary," differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("from the Holy Place").
- **8:12** — Native Clementine Vulgate places Solomon's declaration at 8:12-13 (located at 8:53 in Saints.Bible / 3 Kingdoms).
- **8:15** — Latin in manibus ejus ("in/with his hands") uses the non-reflexive ejus, which could grammatically point to David; the parallel in 8:24 (manibus perfecisti, "with Your hands You have fulfilled") indicates God's own hands.
- **8:28** — Latin hymnum ("hymn") is a distinctly Vulgate rendering where the Hebrew has "cry" or "ringing cry."
- **8:33** — Latin distinctively reads fugerit inimicos suos ("flee from their enemies"), the future periphrastic quia peccaturus est tibi ("because they will sin against You"), and agentes poenitentiam ("repenting"), whereas the Hebrew has "are defeated before an enemy because they have sinned against You, and turn back to You."
- **8:38** — Latin devotatio et imprecatio quae acciderit ("curse and imprecation that happens to") is a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Hebrew has "any prayer or plea" offered by anyone.
- **8:43** — Latin in firmamento habitaculi tui (and in firmamento solii tui in 8:49) uses firmamentum ("foundation/firm support"), with 8:49 distinctively specifying "Your throne" (solii tui) rather than "Your dwelling."
- **8:61** — Latin uses the first-person plural (cor nostrum, ambulemus, custodiamus -- "our heart ... we may walk ... keep"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text addresses the assembly in the second-person plural ("your heart").
