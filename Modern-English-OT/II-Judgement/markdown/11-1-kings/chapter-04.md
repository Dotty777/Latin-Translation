## Text

**4:1** Now King Solomon was reigning over all Israel.

**4:2** And these were the officials whom he had: Azariah the son of Zadok the priest;

**4:3** Elihoreph and Ahijah, the sons of Shisha, were scribes; Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was keeper of the records;

**4:4** Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the army; and Zadok and Abiathar were priests;

**4:5** Azariah the son of Nathan was over those who attended the king; Zabud the son of Nathan was priest, the friend of the king;

**4:6** and Ahishar was overseer of the household; and Adoniram the son of Abda was over the tributes.

**4:7** Now Solomon had twelve prefects over all Israel, who furnished provisions for the king and his household; for during each month of the year, each one supplied the necessities.

**4:8** And these were their names: Ben-hur, in Mount Ephraim.

**4:9** Ben-deker, in Makaz, and in Shaalbim, and in Beth-shemesh, and in Elon, and in Beth-hanan.

**4:10** Ben-hesed, in Arubboth; to him belonged Socoh and all the land of Hepher.

**4:11** Ben-abinadab, to whom belonged all Naphath-dor; he had Taphath, the daughter of Solomon, as his wife.

**4:12** Baana the son of Ahilud governed Taanach and Megiddo, and all Beth-shean, which is beside Zarethan below Jezreel, from Beth-shean as far as Abel-meholah, opposite Jokmeam.

**4:13** Ben-geber, in Ramoth-gilead; he had Havvoth-jair of the son of Manasseh in Gilead; he himself presided in all the region of Argob, which is in Bashan, over sixty great and walled cities that had bronze bars.

**4:14** Ahinadab the son of Iddo presided in Mahanaim.

**4:15** Ahimaaz, in Naphtali; and he also had Basemath, the daughter of Solomon, in marriage.

**4:16** Baana the son of Hushai, in Asher and in Bealoth.

**4:17** Jehoshaphat the son of Paruah, in Issachar.

**4:18** Shimei the son of Ela, in Benjamin.

**4:19** Geber the son of Uri, in the land of Gilead, in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites and of Og king of Bashan, over all things that were in that land.

**4:20** Judah and Israel were innumerable, like the sand of the sea in multitude, eating, and drinking, and rejoicing.

**4:21** Now Solomon was in his dominion, having all the kingdoms from the river of the land of the Philistines to the border of Egypt, who were offering him gifts and serving him all the days of his life.

**4:22** Now the food of Solomon for each day was thirty cors of fine flour, and sixty cors of meal,

**4:23** ten fattened oxen, and twenty pasture-fed oxen, and a hundred rams, besides the game of stags, roe deer, and wild oxen, and fattened fowl.

**4:24** For he himself held the entire region that was beyond the river, from Tiphsah as far as Gaza, and all the kings of those regions; and he had peace on every side all around.

**4:25** And Judah and Israel lived without any fear, each one under his vine and under his fig tree, from Dan to Beersheba, all the days of Solomon.

**4:26** And Solomon had forty thousand stalls for chariot horses and twelve thousand riding horses.

**4:27** And the above-mentioned prefects of the king provided food for them; moreover, they also supplied the necessities of the table of King Solomon with immense care in their due time.

**4:28** Barley also, and straw for the horses and beasts of burden, they brought to the place where the king was, according to what was appointed for them.

**4:29** God also gave wisdom to Solomon, and exceedingly great understanding, and breadth of heart like the sand that is on the shore of the sea.

**4:30** And the wisdom of Solomon surpassed the wisdom of all the people of the East and of the Egyptians,

**4:31** and he was wiser than all men: wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, and Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol; and he was renowned among all the nations all around.

**4:32** Solomon also spoke three thousand parables, and his songs were one thousand and five.

**4:33** And he discoursed concerning trees, from the cedar that is in Lebanon to the hyssop that comes out of the wall; and he discussed beasts, and birds, and creeping things, and fish.

**4:34** And they came from all the peoples to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and from all the kings of the earth who heard of his wisdom.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — Latin places sacerdotis ("the priest") in the genitive modifying Zadok ("the son of Zadok the priest"), whereas the Hebrew is commonly read as designating Azariah himself as the priest.
- **4:5** — Latin super eos qui assistebant regi ("over those who attended the king") interprets the office as royal attendants rather than the Hebrew "over the officers/deputies."
- **4:6** — Latin super tributa ("over the tributes") renders the office as overseeing taxation/tribute rather than the Hebrew "over the forced labor."
- **4:13** — Latin transliterates Hebrew Chavvot-Jair ("the tent-villages of Jair") as a single name Avothjair, followed by the genitive filii Manasse ("of the son of Manasseh").
- **4:17** — Native Vulgate 1 Kings 4:17-34 and 5:1-18 preserve the Hebrew/Vulgate chapter boundary (ch. 4 ends at 4:34; ch. 5 begins at Hiram's embassy), differing from Saints.Bible 3 Kingdoms numbering across ch. 2-16.
- **4:19** — The Clementine Vulgate orders 4:17-19 differently from the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Vulgate 4:17, 4:18, 4:19 correspond to Hebrew 4:18, 4:19, 4:17). Moreover, Latin super omnia quae erant in illa terra ("over all things that were in that land") is a distinctive Vulgate reading where the Hebrew reads "and he was the only officer in the land."
- **4:21** — The Latin reads a flumine terrae Philisthiim ("from the river of the land of the Philistines"), where the Hebrew reads "from the River to the land of the Philistines."
- **4:26** — In native Clementine Vulgate numbering, Solomon's horse stalls stand at 4:26 (placed at 10:26 in Saints.Bible / 3 Kingdoms).
