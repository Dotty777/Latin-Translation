## Text

**4:1** Now King Solomon was reigning over all Israel.

**4:2** And these were the officials whom he had: Azariah the son of Zadok the priest;

**4:3** Elihoreph and Ahijah, the sons of Shisha, were scribes; Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was keeper of the records;

**4:4** Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the army; and Zadok and Abiathar were priests;

**4:5** Azariah the son of Nathan was over those who attended the king; Zabud the son of Nathan was priest, the friend of the king;

**4:6** and Ahishar was overseer of the household; and Adoniram the son of Abda was over the tributes.

**4:7** Now Solomon had twelve prefects over all Israel, who furnished provisions for the king and his household; for during each month of the year, each one supplied the necessities.

**4:8** And these were their names: Ben-hur, in Mount Ephraim.

**4:9** Ben-deker, in Makaz, and in Shaalbim, and in Beth-shemesh, and in Elon, and in Beth-hanan.

**4:10** Ben-hesed, in Arubboth; to him belonged Socoh and all the land of Hepher.

**4:11** Ben-abinadab, to whom belonged all Naphath-dor; he had Taphath, the daughter of Solomon, as his wife.

**4:12** Baana the son of Ahilud governed Taanach and Megiddo, and all Beth-shean, which is beside Zarethan below Jezreel, from Beth-shean as far as Abel-meholah, opposite Jokmeam.

**4:13** Ben-geber, in Ramoth-gilead; he had Havvoth-jair of the son of Manasseh in Gilead; he himself presided in all the region of Argob, which is in Bashan, over sixty great and walled cities that had bronze bars.

**4:14** Ahinadab the son of Iddo presided in Mahanaim.

**4:15** Ahimaaz, in Naphtali; and he also had Basemath, the daughter of Solomon, in marriage.

**4:16** Baana the son of Hushai, in Asher and in Bealoth.

**4:17** Shimei the son of Ela, in Benjamin.

**4:18** Geber the son of Uri, in the land of Gilead, in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites and of Og king of Bashan, over all things that were in that land.

**4:19** Jehoshaphat the son of Paruah, in Issachar.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — Latin places sacerdotis ("the priest") in the genitive modifying Zadok ("the son of Zadok the priest"), whereas the Hebrew is commonly read as designating Azariah himself as the priest.
- **4:5** — Latin super eos qui assistebant regi ("over those who attended the king") interprets the office as royal attendants rather than the Hebrew "over the officers/deputies."
- **4:6** — Latin super tributa ("over the tributes") renders the office as overseeing taxation/tribute rather than the Hebrew "over the forced labor."
- **4:13** — Latin transliterates Hebrew Chavvot-Jair ("the tent-villages of Jair") as a single name Avothjair, followed by the genitive filii Manasse ("of the son of Manasseh").
- **4:18** — The Clementine Vulgate orders 4:17-19 differently from the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Vulgate 4:17, 4:18, 4:19 correspond to Hebrew 4:18, 4:19, 4:17). Moreover, Latin super omnia quae erant in illa terra ("over all things that were in that land") is a distinctive Vulgate reading where the Hebrew reads "and he was the only officer in the land."
