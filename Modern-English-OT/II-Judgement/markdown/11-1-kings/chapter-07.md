## Text

**7:1** Now Solomon built his own house in thirteen years, and brought it all the way to completion.

**7:2** He also built the house of the forest of Lebanon, one hundred cubits in length, fifty cubits in width, and thirty cubits in height, and four walkways between cedar pillars; for he had cut cedar timbers into pillars.

**7:3** And with cedar planks he covered the entire chamber, which was supported by forty-five pillars. Now one row had fifteen pillars

**7:4** set opposite one another,

**7:5** and facing one another directly, with equal space between the pillars, and above the pillars squared timbers equal in all respects.

**7:6** And he made a portico of pillars fifty cubits in length and thirty cubits in width, and another portico in front of the greater portico, and pillars, and architraves upon the pillars.

**7:7** He also made the portico of the throne, in which the tribunal is, and covered it with cedar timbers from the floor up to the top.

**7:8** And the small house, in which one sat to judge, was in the middle of the portico, of similar workmanship. He also made a house for Pharaoh's daughter (whom Solomon had taken as wife) of such workmanship as this portico.

**7:9** All were of costly stones, which had been sawed to a certain standard and measure both inside and outside, from the foundation up to the top of the walls, and on the outside as far as the greater courtyard.

**7:10** Now the foundations were of costly stones, large stones, of ten or eight cubits.

**7:11** And above, costly stones of equal measure had been cut, and likewise of cedar.

**7:12** And the greater courtyard was round, of three rows of cut stones and one row of dressed cedar, and also in the inner courtyard of the house of the Lord, and in the portico of the house.

**7:13** King Solomon also sent and brought Hiram from Tyre,

**7:14** the son of a widowed woman from the tribe of Naphtali, whose father was a Tyrian; he was a bronze craftsman, and full of wisdom, understanding, and knowledge for making every work of bronze. When he had come to King Solomon, he did all his work.

**7:15** And he fashioned two bronze pillars, one pillar eighteen cubits in height, and a line of twelve cubits encircled each pillar.

**7:16** He also made two capitals to be placed on the tops of the pillars, cast from bronze: one capital five cubits in height, and the other capital five cubits in height,

**7:17** and as it were in the manner of a net, and of chains interwoven with one another in marvelous workmanship. Each capital of the pillars was cast: seven networks in rows on the one capital, and seven networks on the other capital.

**7:18** And he finished the pillars, and two rows of pomegranates around each network to cover the capitals that were on the top; in the same way he did also for the second capital.

**7:19** Now the capitals that were on the tops of the pillars were crafted like lily work in the portico, of four cubits.

**7:20** And again there were other capitals on the top of the pillars above, according to the measure of the pillar, opposite the networks; and there were two hundred rows of pomegranates around the second capital.

**7:21** And he set up the two pillars in the portico of the temple; and when he had set up the right pillar, he called it by the name Jachin; likewise he erected the second pillar, and called its name Boaz.

**7:22** And on the tops of the pillars he placed work in the manner of a lily; and the work of the pillars was finished.

**7:23** He also made a cast sea ten cubits from rim to rim, round in circumference; its height was five cubits, and a cord of thirty cubits encircled it all around.

**7:24** And carving beneath the rim went around it, encompassing the sea for ten cubits; two rows of fluted carvings were cast.

**7:25** And it stood on twelve oxen, of which three faced toward the north, and three toward the west, and three toward the south, and three toward the east; and the sea was upon them above, and all their hindquarters were hidden inward.

**7:26** Now the thickness of the basin was three inches, and its rim was like the rim of a cup and the petal of a flared lily; it held two thousand baths.

**7:27** And he made ten bronze bases, each base four cubits in length, and four cubits in width, and three cubits in height.

**7:28** And the workmanship of the bases themselves was openwork, and there were carvings between the joints.

**7:29** And between the little crowns and the braids were lions, oxen, and cherubim, and on the joints likewise above; and beneath the lions and oxen, as it were straps of bronze hanging down.

**7:30** And there were four wheels for each base, and bronze axles; and on the four sides, as it were small shoulders cast beneath the basin, facing one another.

**7:31** The mouth of the basin also was on the inside at the summit of the head; and what appeared on the outside was one cubit, completely round, and altogether it had one cubit and a half; and at the corners of the pillars there were varied engravings, and the middle spaces between the pillars were square, not round.

**7:32** The four wheels also that were at the four corners of the base were joined to one another beneath the base; one wheel had a height of a cubit and a half.

**7:33** Now the wheels were such as are customarily made for a chariot; and their axles, spokes, rims, and hubs were all cast.

**7:34** For those four small shoulders also, at each corner of one base, were cast and joined from the base itself.

**7:35** Now at the top of the base there was a certain roundness of half a cubit, so crafted that a basin could be placed on top of it, having its own engravings and varied carvings out of itself.

**7:36** He also carved on those panels that were of bronze, and on the corners, cherubim, and lions, and palms, as if in the likeness of a standing man, so that they appeared not engraved, but set on all around.

**7:37** In this manner he made ten bases, of one casting, measure, and similar carving.

**7:38** He also made ten bronze basins: one basin held forty baths, and it was of four cubits; and he also placed each basin upon each one, that is, the ten bases.

**7:39** And he set the ten bases, five on the right side of the temple and five on the left; and the sea he placed on the right side of the temple, facing east toward the south.

**7:40** So Hiram made cauldrons, platters, and water vessels, and finished all the work of King Solomon in the temple of the Lord.

**7:41** Two pillars, and two cords of the capitals upon the capitals of the pillars, and two networks to cover the two cords that were on the tops of the pillars.

**7:42** And four hundred pomegranates on the two networks: two rows of pomegranates on each network, to cover the cords of the capitals that were on the tops of the pillars.

**7:43** And ten bases, and ten basins upon the bases.

**7:44** And one sea, and twelve oxen beneath the sea.

**7:45** And the cauldrons, and the platters, and the water vessels—all the vessels that Hiram made for King Solomon in the house of the Lord—were of brass.

**7:46** In the plain region of the Jordan the king cast them, in clay soil, between Succoth and Zarethan.

**7:47** And Solomon placed all the vessels; yet because of the exceedingly great multitude, there was no weight of the bronze.

**7:48** And Solomon made all the vessels in the house of the Lord: the golden altar, and the golden table on which the loaves of presentation were to be placed;

**7:49** and the golden lampstands, five on the right and five on the left in front of the oracle, of pure gold; and as it were lily blossoms, and golden lamps above, and golden tongs,

**7:50** and water jars, and small forks, and bowls, and small mortars, and censers, of purest gold; and the hinges of the doors of the inner house of the Holy of Holies, and of the doors of the house of the temple, were of gold.

**7:51** And Solomon finished all the work that he was doing in the house of the Lord, and brought in the things that his father David had consecrated, silver, gold, and vessels, and stored them in the treasuries of the house of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **7:13** — Native Vulgate 1 Kings 7:1-51 follow the Hebrew/Vulgate order (palace in 7:1-12, bronze furnishings with Hiram in 7:13-51), mapping to Saints.Bible 3 Kingdoms order (Hiram in 7:1-37, palace in 7:38-50). Restoring native 7:13 supplies the missing subject and verb leading into 7:14 ('the son of a widowed woman...').
- **7:20** — The Vulgate reads malogranatorum autem ducenti ordines ("two hundred rows of pomegranates") around the second capital, whereas the Hebrew describes two hundred pomegranates arranged in rows.
- **7:24** — The Vulgate renders the border ornament as fluted carvings (sculpturarum striatarum) encompassing the sea for ten cubits (decem cubitis), where the Hebrew speaks of gourds set ten to a cubit.
- **7:31** — This difficult architectural verse preserves the Vulgate's distinctive phrasing, including in angulis columnarum ("at the corners of the pillars") and media intercolumnia ("the middle spaces between the pillars").
- **7:36** — Native Vulgate 1 Kings 7:36 maps to Saints.Bible 7:22; Latin 'quasi in similitudinem hominis stantis, ut non cælata, sed apposita per circuitum viderentur' is a distinctive Vulgate architectural expansion.
- **7:41** — Latin funiculos capitellorum ("cords of the capitals") is a distinctive Vulgate rendering where the Hebrew refers to bowl-shaped globes on the capitals.
- **7:45** — Native Vulgate 1 Kings 7:45 maps to Saints.Bible 7:31; Latin 'de auricalco' renders the burnished bronze alloy specifically as 'brass/orichalcum.'
- **7:49** — Latin oraculum ("oracle") is the Vulgate's standard term for the inner sanctuary (the Holy of Holies) where the Lord spoke.
