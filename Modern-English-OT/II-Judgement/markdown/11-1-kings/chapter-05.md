## Text

**5:1** Hiram, king of Tyre, also sent his servants to Solomon; for he heard that they had anointed him king in place of his father, because Hiram had been a friend to David at all times.

**5:2** Now Solomon sent word to Hiram, saying:

**5:3** "You know the will of David my father, and that he could not build a house for the name of the Lord his God because of the wars threatening all around, until the Lord put them under the sole of his feet.

**5:4** But now the Lord my God has given me rest all around, and there is no adversary, nor evil encounter.

**5:5** For this reason I intend to build a temple for the name of the Lord my God, just as the Lord spoke to David my father, saying, 'Your son, whom I will set in your place upon your throne, he himself will build a house for My name.'

**5:6** Command therefore that your servants cut down cedars for me from Lebanon, and let my servants be with your servants; and I will give you the wages of your servants, whatever you ask; for you know how there is not a man among my people who knows how to cut timber like the Sidonians."

**5:7** Therefore, when Hiram heard the words of Solomon, he rejoiced greatly and said, "Blessed be the Lord God today, who has given David a most wise son over this very numerous people."

**5:8** And Hiram sent word to Solomon, saying, "I have heard whatever you have directed to me; I will carry out all your desire concerning cedar timber and fir timber.

**5:9** My servants will bring them down from Lebanon to the sea, and I will assemble them into rafts on the sea as far as the place that you indicate to me; and I will bring them ashore there, and you will take them; and you will supply the necessities for me, so that food may be given to my household."

**5:10** So Hiram kept giving Solomon cedar timber and fir timber, according to all his desire.

**5:11** And Solomon supplied Hiram twenty thousand cors of wheat as food for his household, and twenty cors of the purest oil; these things Solomon gave to Hiram each year.

**5:12** The Lord also gave wisdom to Solomon, just as He had spoken to him; and there was peace between Hiram and Solomon, and the two of them struck a covenant.

**5:13** And King Solomon selected laborers out of all Israel, and the levy was thirty thousand men.

**5:14** And he sent them to Lebanon, ten thousand each month in turns, so that for two months they were in their own homes; and Adoniram was over this kind of levy.

**5:15** And Solomon had seventy thousand of those who carried burdens, and eighty thousand stonecutters in the mountain,

**5:16** besides the overseers who were in charge of the individual works, three thousand three hundred in number, commanding the people and those who were doing the work.

**5:17** And the king commanded that they take great stones, costly stones for the foundation of the temple, and square them.

**5:18** These the masons of Solomon and the masons of Hiram hewed; moreover, the Gebalites prepared timber and stones for building the house.

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — The Vulgate reads 'voluntatem David patris mei' ('the will of David my father'), explicitly supplying 'will' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text simply reads 'You know David my father, that he could not build.'
- **5:4** — Latin retains the transliterated Hebrew common noun 'satan' here in its basic sense of 'adversary' or 'opponent,' paired with 'occursus malus' ('evil encounter').
- **5:9** — The Vulgate renders the verb as 'applicabo' ('I will bring ashore' or 'land'), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'I will break them apart' (that is, disassemble the rafts).
- **5:18** — Latin opens with the relative pronoun 'quos' ('these' or 'which'), referring to the great foundation stones whose quarrying and squaring stand in 5:17 (grouped at Saints.Bible 6:1).
