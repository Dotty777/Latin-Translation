## Text

**5:1** And the above-mentioned governors of the king provided food for them; moreover, they also supplied the necessities of the table of King Solomon with immense care in their due time. Barley also, and straw for the horses and beasts of burden, they brought to the place where the king was, according to what was appointed for them.

**5:2** Now the food of Solomon for each day was thirty cors of fine flour, and sixty cors of meal,

**5:3** ten fattened oxen, and twenty pasture-fed oxen, and a hundred rams, besides the game of stags, roe deer, and wild oxen, and fattened fowl.

**5:4** For he himself held the entire region that was beyond the river, from Tiphsah as far as Gaza, and all the kings of those regions; and he had peace on every side all around.

**5:9** God also gave wisdom to Solomon, and exceedingly great understanding, and breadth of heart like the sand that is on the shore of the sea.

**5:10** And the wisdom of Solomon surpassed the wisdom of all the people of the East and of the Egyptians,

**5:11** and he was wiser than all men: wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, and Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol; and he was renowned among all the nations all around.

**5:12** Solomon also spoke three thousand parables, and his songs were one thousand and five.

**5:13** And he discoursed concerning trees, from the cedar that is in Lebanon to the hyssop that comes out of the wall; and he discussed beasts, and birds, and creeping things, and fish.

**5:14** Therefore the kingdom was established in the hand of Solomon, and he was joined in marriage alliance to Pharaoh, king of Egypt; for he took his daughter and brought her into the city of David, until he finished building his own house, and the house of the Lord, and the wall of Jerusalem all around. And they came from all the peoples to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and from all the kings of the earth who heard of his wisdom. Pharaoh, king of Egypt, went up and captured Gezer, and burned it with fire, and killed the Canaanite who lived in the city; and he gave it as a dowry to his daughter, the wife of Solomon.

**5:15** Hiram, king of Tyre, also sent his servants to Solomon; for he heard that they had anointed him king in place of his father, because Hiram had been a friend to David at all times.

**5:16** Now Solomon sent word to Hiram, saying:

**5:17** "You know the will of David my father, and that he could not build a house for the name of the Lord his God because of the wars threatening all around, until the Lord put them under the sole of his feet.

**5:18** But now the Lord my God has given me rest all around, and there is no adversary, nor evil encounter.

**5:19** For this reason I intend to build a temple for the name of the Lord my God, just as the Lord spoke to David my father, saying, 'Your son, whom I will set in your place upon your throne, he himself will build a house for My name.'

**5:20** Command therefore that your servants cut down cedars for me from Lebanon, and let my servants be with your servants; and I will give you the wages of your servants, whatever you ask; for you know how there is not a man among my people who knows how to cut timber like the Sidonians."

**5:21** Therefore, when Hiram heard the words of Solomon, he rejoiced greatly and said, "Blessed be the Lord God today, who has given David a most wise son over this very numerous people."

**5:22** And Hiram sent word to Solomon, saying, "I have heard whatever you have directed to me; I will carry out all your desire concerning cedar timber and fir timber.

**5:23** My servants will bring them down from Lebanon to the sea, and I will assemble them into rafts on the sea as far as the place that you indicate to me; and I will bring them ashore there, and you will take them; and you will supply the necessities for me, so that food may be given to my household."

**5:24** So Hiram kept giving Solomon cedar timber and fir timber, according to all his desire.

**5:25** And Solomon supplied Hiram twenty thousand cors of wheat as food for his household, and twenty cors of the purest oil; these things Solomon gave to Hiram each year.

**5:26** And the Lord gave wisdom to Solomon, just as He had spoken to him; and there was peace between Hiram and Solomon, and the two of them struck a covenant.

**5:27** And King Solomon selected laborers out of all Israel, and the levy was thirty thousand men.

**5:28** And he sent them to Lebanon, ten thousand each month in turns, so that for two months they were in their own homes; and Adoniram was over this kind of levy.

**5:29** And Solomon had seventy thousand of those who carried burdens, and eighty thousand stonecutters in the mountain,

**5:30** besides the overseers who were in charge of the individual works, three thousand three hundred in number, commanding the people and those who were doing the work.

**5:32** These the masons of Solomon and the masons of Hiram hewed; moreover, the Gebalites prepared timber and stones for building the house.

## Translator Notes

- **5:17** — The Vulgate reads 'voluntatem David patris mei' ('the will of David my father'), explicitly supplying 'will' where the Hebrew Masoretic Text simply reads 'You know David my father, that he could not build.'
- **5:18** — Latin retains the transliterated Hebrew common noun 'satan' here in its basic sense of 'adversary' or 'opponent,' paired with 'occursus malus' ('evil encounter').
- **5:23** — The Vulgate renders the verb as 'applicabo' ('I will bring ashore' or 'land'), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'I will break them apart' (that is, disassemble the rafts).
- **5:32** — Latin opens with the relative pronoun 'quos' ('these' or 'which'), referring to the great foundation stones whose cutting appears at the beginning of 6:1 in this chapter arrangement.
