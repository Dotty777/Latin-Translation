## Text

**21:1** Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem; the name of his mother was Hephzibah.

**21:2** And he did evil in the sight of the Lord, according to the idols of the nations that the Lord destroyed from before the face of the sons of Israel.

**21:3** And he turned back and built the high places that Hezekiah his father had demolished; and he erected altars for Baal, and made groves just as Ahab king of Israel had done, and worshiped all the host of heaven and served it.

**21:4** And he constructed altars in the house of the Lord, concerning which the Lord said, "In Jerusalem I will place My name."

**21:5** And he constructed altars for the entire host of heaven in the two courts of the temple of the Lord.

**21:6** And he led his son through the fire, and practiced soothsaying, and observed omens, and appointed mediums, and multiplied diviners, so as to do evil before the Lord and provoke Him.

**21:7** He also placed the idol of the grove, which he had made, in the temple of the Lord, concerning which the Lord spoke to David and to Solomon his son: "In this temple and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen from all the tribes of Israel, I will place My name forever.

**21:8** And I will no longer cause the foot of Israel to be moved from the land that I gave to their fathers, if only they observe in practice all that I have commanded them, and the entire law that My servant Moses enjoined on them."

**21:9** Yet they did not listen, but were led astray by Manasseh to do evil beyond the nations that the Lord crushed from before the face of the sons of Israel.

**21:10** And the Lord spoke by the hand of His servants the prophets, saying,

**21:11** "Because Manasseh king of Judah has done these most wicked abominations, beyond all that the Amorites did before him, and has also made Judah sin in his impurities,

**21:12** therefore this is what the Lord God of Israel says: Look, I will bring evils upon Jerusalem and Judah, so that whoever hears, both of his ears will tingle.

**21:13** And I will stretch over Jerusalem the line of Samaria and the plummet of the house of Ahab; and I will erase Jerusalem just as tablets are usually erased, and while erasing I will turn it and draw the stylus repeatedly over its face.

**21:14** And I will abandon the remnant of My inheritance and deliver them into the hands of its enemies, and they will become a desolation and a plunder for all their adversaries,

**21:15** because they have done evil before Me and have persisted in provoking Me, from the day on which their fathers went out from Egypt up to this day."

**21:16** Moreover, Manasseh also shed exceedingly much innocent blood, until he filled Jerusalem up to the mouth, apart from his sins by which he made Judah sin, to do evil before the Lord.

**21:17** Now the rest of the words of Manasseh, such as all that he did, and his sin that he sinned, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Judah?

**21:18** And Manasseh slept with his fathers and was buried in the garden of his house, in the garden of Uzza; and Amon his son reigned in his place.

**21:19** Amon was twenty-two years old when he began to reign; he also reigned two years in Jerusalem; the name of his mother was Meshullemeth, daughter of Haruz of Jotbah.

**21:20** And he did evil in the sight of the Lord, just as Manasseh his father had done.

**21:21** And he walked in the entire way along which his father had walked, and he served the impurities that his father had served, and worshiped them;

**21:22** and he abandoned the Lord God of his fathers, and did not walk in the way of the Lord.

**21:23** And his servants laid an ambush for him, and killed the king in his house.

**21:24** However, the people of the land struck down all who had conspired against King Amon, and they appointed Josiah his son as king for themselves in his place.

**21:25** Now the rest of the words of Amon, what he did, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Judah?

**21:26** And they buried him in his tomb, in the garden of Uzza; and Josiah his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — The Vulgate reads idola ("idols") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "abominations."
- **21:3** — Latin lucos ("groves," and singular luci in 21:7) is the Vulgate's regular rendering of the Hebrew Asherah cult objects as sacred groves, here given in the plural rather than the Hebrew singular.
- **21:11** — Latin immunditiis ("impurities," also in 21:21) is the Vulgate's literal derogatory term for idols, corresponding to Hebrew gillulim.
- **21:13** — Whereas the Hebrew text compares Jerusalem to a dish wiped clean and turned upside down, the Vulgate renders the image as wax writing tablets (tabulae) erased by turning and drawing the stylus (stylum) repeatedly over their face.
- **21:17** — The Clementine Vulgate reads ut ("such as") before universa quae fecit where the Hebrew has "and"; sermonum ("words") here and in 21:25 reflects the Vulgate's literal rendering of Hebrew debarim ("words/matters"), including the chronicle title libro sermonum dierum ("book of the words of the days").
