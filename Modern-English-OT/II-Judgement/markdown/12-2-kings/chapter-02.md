## Text

**2:1** Now it happened, when the Lord willed to lift Elijah up by a whirlwind into heaven, that Elijah and Elisha were going from Gilgal.

**2:2** And Elijah said to Elisha, "Stay here, because the Lord has sent me as far as Bethel." And Elisha said to him, "As the Lord lives, and as your soul lives, I will not abandon you." And when they had gone down to Bethel,

**2:3** the sons of the prophets who were in Bethel came out to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today the Lord will take your master away from you?" And he answered, "I know it too; be silent."

**2:4** Then Elijah said to Elisha, "Stay here, because the Lord has sent me to Jericho." And he said, "As the Lord lives, and as your soul lives, I will not abandon you." And when they had come to Jericho,

**2:5** the sons of the prophets who were in Jericho approached Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that the Lord will take your master away from you today?" And he said, "I know it too; be silent."

**2:6** Then Elijah said to him, "Stay here, because the Lord has sent me as far as the Jordan." And he said, "As the Lord lives, and as your soul lives, I will not abandon you." So both of them went together,

**2:7** and fifty men from the sons of the prophets followed them, who also stood opposite at a distance, while those two stood by the Jordan.

**2:8** And Elijah took his cloak, rolled it up, and struck the waters, and they were divided to both sides, and both of them crossed over on dry ground.

**2:9** And when they had crossed over, Elijah said to Elisha, "Ask what you want me to do for you, before I am taken from you." And Elisha said, "I implore you, let your double spirit be in me."

**2:10** And he answered, "You have asked a difficult thing; nevertheless, if you see me when I am taken from you, what you have asked for will be yours; but if you do not see me, it will not be."

**2:11** And as they proceeded on and conversed while walking, look, a fiery chariot and fiery horses separated the two of them, and Elijah went up by a whirlwind into heaven.

**2:12** And Elisha was watching and crying out, "My father, my father, the chariot of Israel and its charioteer!" And he saw him no more; and he took hold of his own garments and tore them into two pieces.

**2:13** And he picked up the cloak of Elijah, which had fallen from him, and turning back, he stood on the bank of the Jordan,

**2:14** and with the cloak of Elijah, which had fallen from him, he struck the waters, and they were not divided; and he said, "Where is the God of Elijah even now?" And he struck the waters, and they were divided this way and that, and Elisha crossed over.

**2:15** Now when the sons of the prophets who were opposite in Jericho saw this, they said, "The spirit of Elijah has rested upon Elisha." And coming to meet him, they bowed down to him, prostrate to the ground,

**2:16** and said to him, "Look, with your servants are fifty strong men who can go and search for your master, in case perhaps the Spirit of the Lord has taken him away and cast him onto one of the mountains or into one of the valleys." And he said, "Do not send them."

**2:17** And they pressed him until he consented and said, "Send them." And they sent fifty men, and when they had searched for three days, they did not find him.

**2:18** And they returned to him, while he was staying in Jericho, and he said to them, "Did I not say to you, 'Do not send them'?"

**2:19** The men of the city also said to Elisha, "Look, the habitation of this city is very good, as you yourself, lord, clearly see; but the waters are very bad, and the land is barren."

**2:20** And he said, "Bring me a new vessel and put salt in it." And when they had brought it,

**2:21** he went out to the spring of the waters, threw salt into it, and said, "This is what the Lord says: I have healed these waters, and there will no longer be death or barrenness in them."

**2:22** Therefore the waters were healed to this day, according to the word of Elisha that he spoke.

**2:23** And he went up from there to Bethel; and as he was going up along the road, little boys came out of the city and were mocking him, saying, "Go up, bald man! Go up, bald man!"

**2:24** And when he had looked back, he saw them and cursed them in the name of the Lord; and two bears came out of the woods and tore apart forty-two boys from among them.

**2:25** And he departed from there to Mount Carmel, and from there he returned to Samaria.

## Translator Notes

- **2:3** — Here and in v. 5, the Vulgate reads simply "from you" (a te), omitting the Hebrew idiom "from over your head."
- **2:9** — Latin duplex spiritus tuus literally means "your double spirit," reflecting the Hebrew legal idiom for the firstborn son's double inheritance share.
- **2:12** — The Vulgate renders the title in the singular as "its charioteer" (auriga ejus), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads the plural "its horsemen."
- **2:14** — In a notable Vulgate reading shared with the Septuagint tradition, Elisha strikes the Jordan twice—first unsuccessfully ("and they were not divided") before calling out "Where is the God of Elijah even now?" (omitting "the Lord" of the Hebrew)—whereas the Hebrew text records a single strike.
- **2:24** — The Vulgate reads simply "two bears" (duo ursi), without specifying female bears as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
