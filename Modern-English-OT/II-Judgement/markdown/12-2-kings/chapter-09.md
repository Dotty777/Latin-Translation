## Text

**9:1** Now Elisha the prophet called one of the sons of the prophets and said to him, "Gird your loins, and take this flask of oil in your hand, and go to Ramoth-gilead.

**9:2** And when you arrive there, you will see Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi; and going in, you will make him rise from the midst of his brothers, and you will bring him into an inner room.

**9:3** And holding the flask of oil, you will pour it on his head and say, 'This is what the Lord says: I have anointed you king over Israel.' And you will open the door and flee, and you will not linger there."

**9:4** So the young man, the attendant of the prophet, went to Ramoth-gilead,

**9:5** and went in there; and look, the commanders of the army were sitting. And he said, "I have a word for you, commander." And Jehu said, "For which one of all of us?" And he said, "For you, commander."

**9:6** And he arose and entered the room; and the other poured the oil on his head and said, "This is what the Lord God of Israel says: 'I have anointed you king over the people of the Lord, Israel,

**9:7** and you will strike the house of Ahab your master, and I will avenge the blood of My servants the prophets, and the blood of all the servants of the Lord from the hand of Jezebel.

**9:8** And I will destroy the entire house of Ahab, and I will kill from Ahab anyone who urinates against a wall, both the confined and the last in Israel.

**9:9** And I will make the house of Ahab like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat, and like the house of Baasha son of Ahijah.

**9:10** The dogs will also eat Jezebel in the field of Jezreel, and there will be no one to bury her.'" And he opened the door and fled.

**9:11** Now Jehu went out to the servants of his master, and they said to him, "Is everything well? Why did this madman come to you?" And he said to them, "You know the man and what he spoke."

**9:12** But they answered, "It is false; rather, tell us." And he said to them, "He spoke this and that to me, and he said, 'This is what the Lord says: I have anointed you king over Israel.'"

**9:13** Therefore they hurried, and each one taking his cloak, they placed them under his feet in the likeness of a tribunal, and they blew the trumpet and said, "Jehu has become king!"

**9:14** So Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi, conspired against Joram. Now Joram had besieged Ramoth-gilead, he and all Israel, against Hazael king of Syria,

**9:15** and he had returned to be healed in Jezreel because of the wounds, because the Syrians had struck him while he was fighting against Hazael king of Syria. And Jehu said, "If it pleases you, let no one go out as a fugitive from the city, so that he does not go and report in Jezreel."

**9:16** And he mounted up and set out for Jezreel; for Joram was sick there, and Ahaziah king of Judah had gone down to visit Joram.

**9:17** Now the watchman who was standing on the tower of Jezreel saw the troop of Jehu coming, and said, "I see a troop." And Joram said, "Take a chariot and send it to meet them, and let him say as he goes, 'Is everything well?'"

**9:18** So the one who had mounted the chariot went to meet him and said, "This is what the king says: 'Is everything peaceful?'" And Jehu said, "What have you to do with peace? Pass over and follow me." The watchman also reported, saying, "The messenger came to them, and he is not returning."

**9:19** He also sent a second chariot of horses; and he came to them and said, "This is what the king says: 'Is it peace?'" And Jehu said, "What have you to do with peace? Pass over and follow me."

**9:20** And the watchman reported, saying, "He came right up to them, and he is not returning; and the pace is like the pace of Jehu son of Nimshi, for he advances headlong."

**9:21** And Joram said, "Harness the chariot." And they harnessed his chariot, and Joram king of Israel and Ahaziah king of Judah went out, each in his own chariot, and they went out to meet Jehu, and they found him in the field of Naboth the Jezreelite.

**9:22** And when Joram saw Jehu, he said, "Is it peace, Jehu?" And he answered, "What peace? The fornications of Jezebel your mother and her many sorceries still flourish."

**9:23** Then Joram turned his hand, and fleeing, he said to Ahaziah, "Treachery, Ahaziah!"

**9:24** Then Jehu drew a bow with his hand and struck Joram between the shoulder blades; and the arrow went out through his heart, and immediately he collapsed in his chariot.

**9:25** And Jehu said to Bidkar the captain, "Pick him up, throw him into the field of Naboth the Jezreelite; for I remember when you and I, sitting in a chariot, were following Ahab his father, that the Lord lifted up this burden against him, saying,

**9:26** 'Surely for the blood of Naboth and for the blood of his sons, which I saw yesterday,' says the Lord, 'I will repay you in this field,' says the Lord. Now therefore, pick him up and throw him into the field, according to the word of the Lord."

**9:27** Now Ahaziah king of Judah, seeing this, fled by the way of the garden house; and Jehu pursued him and said, "Strike him also in his chariot!" And they struck him at the ascent of Gaver, which is near Ibleam; and he fled to Megiddo and died there.

**9:28** And his servants placed him on his chariot and carried him to Jerusalem, and they buried him in a tomb with his fathers in the city of David.

**9:29** In the eleventh year of Joram son of Ahab, Ahaziah began to reign over Judah.

**9:30** And Jehu came to Jezreel. Now Jezebel, having heard of his arrival, painted her eyes with antimony, adorned her head, and looked out through the window

**9:31** at Jehu entering through the gate, and she said, "Can there be peace for Zimri, who killed his master?"

**9:32** And Jehu lifted his face toward the window and said, "Who is this woman?" And two or three eunuchs leaned down toward him.

**9:33** And he said to them, "Throw her down!" And they threw her down, and the wall was spattered with blood, and the hooves of the horses trampled her.

**9:34** And when he had gone in to eat and drink, he said, "Go and see to that cursed woman and bury her, for she is a king's daughter."

**9:35** And when they had gone to bury her, they found nothing except the skull, and the feet, and the ends of the hands.

**9:36** And returning, they reported to him. And Jehu said, "It is the word of the Lord, which He spoke through His servant Elijah the Tishbite, saying, 'In the field of Jezreel the dogs will eat the flesh of Jezebel,

**9:37** and the flesh of Jezebel will be like dung upon the face of the earth in the field of Jezreel, so that those passing by will say, "Is this that Jezebel?"'"

## Translator Notes

- **9:13** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew "on the bare steps" as "in the likeness of a tribunal" (in similitudinem tribunalis), depicting a raised seat of authority.
- **9:14** — The Vulgate reads "had besieged" (obsederat), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "had been guarding" (or defending); similarly, in 9:17-19 the Vulgate reads "chariot" (currum) where the Hebrew reads "horseman."
- **9:27** — The Vulgate reads "Gaver," a distinct place-name form corresponding to "Gur" in the Masoretic Hebrew text.
- **9:32** — The Vulgate reads "Who is this woman?" (Quae est ista?), differing from the Masoretic Hebrew "Who is on my side? Who?"
- **9:37** — The Vulgate renders the closing clause as a question asked by passersby ("so that those passing by will say, 'Is this that Jezebel?'"), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads negatively, "so that no one can say, 'This is Jezebel.'"
