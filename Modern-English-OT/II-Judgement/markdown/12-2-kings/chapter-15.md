## Text

**15:1** In the twenty-seventh year of Jeroboam, king of Israel, Azariah, son of Amaziah, king of Judah, began to reign.

**15:2** He was sixteen years old when he began to reign, and he reigned for fifty-two years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Jecoliah, from Jerusalem.

**15:3** And he did what was pleasing before the Lord, according to all that his father Amaziah had done.

**15:4** Yet he did not demolish the high places; the people were still sacrificing and burning incense on the high places.

**15:5** Now the Lord struck the king, and he was a leper until the day of his death, and he lived in a free house apart; while Jotham, the king's son, governed the palace and would judge the people of the land.

**15:6** Now the rest of the accounts of Azariah, and all that he did, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Judah?

**15:7** And Azariah slept with his fathers, and they buried him with his ancestors in the city of David, and his son Jotham reigned in his place.

**15:8** In the thirty-eighth year of Azariah, king of Judah, Zechariah, son of Jeroboam, reigned over Israel in Samaria for six months.

**15:9** And he did what is evil before the Lord, just as his fathers had done; he did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam, son of Nebat, who made Israel sin.

**15:10** Now Shallum, son of Jabesh, conspired against him, and struck him openly and killed him, and reigned in his place.

**15:11** Now the rest of the accounts of Zechariah, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?

**15:12** This is the word of the Lord which He spoke to Jehu, saying, "Your sons to the fourth generation will sit on the throne of Israel." And so it happened.

**15:13** Shallum, son of Jabesh, began to reign in the thirty-ninth year of Azariah, king of Judah, and he reigned for one month in Samaria.

**15:14** And Menahem, son of Gadi, went up from Tirzah and came to Samaria, and struck Shallum, son of Jabesh, in Samaria, and killed him, and reigned in his place.

**15:15** Now the rest of the accounts of Shallum, and his conspiracy by which he laid an ambush, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?

**15:16** Then Menahem struck Tiphsah and all who were in it, and its borders from Tirzah, for they had refused to open to him; and he killed all its pregnant women and ripped them open.

**15:17** In the thirty-ninth year of Azariah, king of Judah, Menahem, son of Gadi, reigned over Israel for ten years in Samaria.

**15:18** And he did what was evil before the Lord; he did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam, son of Nebat, who made Israel sin, all his days.

**15:19** Pul, king of the Assyrians, was coming into the land, and Menahem gave Pul a thousand talents of silver, so that he would be a help to him and strengthen his kingdom.

**15:20** And Menahem levied the silver upon Israel, upon all the powerful and wealthy, so that he might give to the king of the Assyrians fifty shekels of silver per person; and the king of the Assyrians turned back and did not stay in the land.

**15:21** Now the rest of the accounts of Menahem, and all that he did, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?

**15:22** And Menahem slept with his fathers, and his son Pekahiah reigned in his place.

**15:23** In the fiftieth year of Azariah, king of Judah, Pekahiah, son of Menahem, reigned over Israel in Samaria for two years.

**15:24** And he did what was evil before the Lord; he did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam, son of Nebat, who made Israel sin.

**15:25** Now Pekah, son of Remaliah, his commander, conspired against him and struck him in Samaria in the tower of the royal house, beside Argob and beside Arieh, and with him fifty men from the sons of the Gileadites; and he killed him and reigned in his place.

**15:26** Now the rest of the accounts of Pekahiah, and all that he did, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?

**15:27** In the fifty-second year of Azariah, king of Judah, Pekah, son of Remaliah, reigned over Israel in Samaria for twenty years.

**15:28** And he did what was evil before the Lord; he did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam, son of Nebat, who made Israel sin.

**15:29** In the days of Pekah, king of Israel, Tiglath-pileser, king of Assyria, came and captured Ijon, and Abel House of Maacah, and Janoah, and Kedesh, and Hazor, and Gilead, and Galilee, and all the land of Naphtali, and carried them away to the Assyrians.

**15:30** Now Hoshea, son of Elah, conspired and laid an ambush against Pekah, son of Remaliah, and struck him and killed him, and reigned in his place in the twentieth year of Jotham, son of Uzziah.

**15:31** Now the rest of the accounts of Pekah, and all that he did, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?

**15:32** In the second year of Pekah, son of Remaliah, king of Israel, Jotham, son of Uzziah, king of Judah, began to reign.

**15:33** He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned for sixteen years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Jerusha, daughter of Zadok.

**15:34** And he did what was pleasing before the Lord; he acted according to all that his father Uzziah had done.

**15:35** Yet he did not remove the high places; the people were still offering sacrifices and burning incense on the high places. He himself built the highest gate of the house of the Lord.

**15:36** Now the rest of the accounts of Jotham, and all that he did, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Judah?

**15:37** In those days the Lord began to send Rezin, king of Syria, and Pekah, son of Remaliah, against Judah.

**15:38** And Jotham slept with his fathers, and was buried with them in the city of David, his father; and his son Ahaz reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **15:5** — Latin "in domo libera seorsum" ("in a free house apart") renders the obscure Hebrew term for an isolated house. The Clementine Vulgate also reads the future tense "judicabit" ("will judge") rather than the imperfect "judicabat" ("was judging"), translated here as "would judge" to fit the past-tense narrative.
- **15:10** — Latin "palam" ("openly") translates the Hebrew phrase "before the people," which some Greek witnesses take as the place name Ibleam.
- **15:25** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew with "juxta" ("beside Argob and beside Arieh") and puts "quinquaginta viros" ("fifty men") in the accusative as additional victims struck down with Pekahiah, whereas the Hebrew is usually read as fifty Gileadites who accompanied Pekah in the conspiracy.
- **15:29** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew town name Abel-beth-maacah partly into Latin as "Abel Domum, Maacha" ("Abel House of Maacah," with a comma after "Domum" in the Clementine text), and renders the destination as "in Assyrios" ("to the Assyrians").
