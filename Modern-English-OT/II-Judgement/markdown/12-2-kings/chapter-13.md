## Text

**13:1** In the twenty-third year of Joash the son of Ahaziah, king of Judah, Jehoahaz the son of Jehu reigned over Israel in Samaria for seventeen years.

**13:2** And he did evil before the Lord, and followed the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who made Israel sin, and he did not turn aside from them.

**13:3** And the fury of the Lord was enraged against Israel, and He handed them over into the hand of Hazael king of Syria, and into the hand of Ben-hadad the son of Hazael, all the days.

**13:4** Now Jehoahaz implored the face of the Lord, and the Lord heard him; for He saw the distress of Israel, because the king of Syria had crushed them.

**13:5** And the Lord gave a savior to Israel, and Israel was delivered from the hand of the king of Syria; and the sons of Israel lived in their tents as yesterday and the day before yesterday.

**13:6** Nevertheless, they did not depart from the sins of the house of Jeroboam, who made Israel sin, but walked in them; indeed, even the sacred grove remained in Samaria.

**13:7** And none were left to Jehoahaz from the people except fifty horsemen, and ten chariots, and ten thousand foot soldiers; for the king of Syria had killed them and had reduced them like dust in the threshing of a threshing floor.

**13:8** Now the rest of the accounts of Jehoahaz, and all that he did, and his might, are these not written in the book of the accounts of the days of the kings of Israel?

**13:9** And Jehoahaz slept with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria; and Joash his son reigned in his place.

**13:10** In the thirty-seventh year of Joash king of Judah, Joash the son of Jehoahaz reigned over Israel in Samaria for sixteen years.

**13:11** And he did what is evil in the sight of the Lord; he did not turn aside from all the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who made Israel sin, but walked in them.

**13:12** Now the rest of the accounts of Joash, and all that he did, and his might, how he fought against Amaziah king of Judah, are these not written in the book of the accounts of the days of the kings of Israel?

**13:13** And Joash slept with his fathers, and Jeroboam sat upon his throne. Moreover, Joash was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel.

**13:14** Now Elisha was sick with the illness from which he also died; and Joash king of Israel went down to him, and wept before him, and said, "My father, my father, the chariot of Israel and its charioteer!"

**13:15** And Elisha said to him, "Bring a bow and arrows." And when he had brought a bow and arrows to him,

**13:16** he said to the king of Israel, "Place your hand upon the bow." And when he had placed his hand, Elisha placed his hands over the hands of the king,

**13:17** and said, "Open the eastern window." And when he had opened it, Elisha said, "Shoot an arrow." And he shot. And Elisha said, "The arrow of the Lord's salvation, and the arrow of salvation against Syria; and you will strike Syria in Aphek until you consume it."

**13:18** And he said, "Take the arrows." And when he had taken them, he said to him again, "Strike the ground with an arrow." And when he had struck three times and stopped,

**13:19** the man of God became angry with him and said, "If you had struck five times, or six times, or seven times, you would have struck Syria to complete destruction; but now you will strike it three times."

**13:20** So Elisha died, and they buried him. Now raiders from Moab came into the land in that very year.

**13:21** And some men who were burying a man saw the raiders, and threw the corpse into the tomb of Elisha. And when it touched the bones of Elisha, the man came back to life and stood upon his feet.

**13:22** Now Hazael king of Syria afflicted Israel all the days of Jehoahaz.

**13:23** And the Lord had mercy on them and turned back to them because of His covenant, which He had with Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob; and He was not willing to destroy them, nor to cast them away completely up to the present time.

**13:24** And Hazael king of Syria died, and Ben-hadad his son reigned in his place.

**13:25** Moreover, Joash the son of Jehoahaz took the cities from the hand of Ben-hadad the son of Hazael, which he had taken from the hand of Jehoahaz his father by right of battle; three times Joash struck him, and he restored the cities of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **13:6** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew "Asherah" (a cultic wooden pole or image) as lucus ("sacred grove"), its standard Latin rendering for the term.
- **13:14** — The Vulgate renders the final phrase in the singular as auriga ejus ("its charioteer," as in 2 Kings 2:12), whereas the Hebrew has the plural "its horsemen."
- **13:18** — The Vulgate adds jaculo ("with a dart/arrow"), specifying the instrument used to strike the ground, whereas the Hebrew simply reads "Strike the ground."
- **13:19** — The Vulgate reads quinquies, aut sexies, sive septies ("five times, or six times, or seven times"), adding "or seven times" where the Hebrew mentions only five or six times.
- **13:23** — The Vulgate reads neque projicere penitus ("nor to cast them away completely"), without the Hebrew's "from his presence," and concludes with usque in praesens tempus ("up to the present time").
