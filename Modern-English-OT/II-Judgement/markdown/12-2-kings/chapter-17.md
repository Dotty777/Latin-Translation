## Text

**17:1** In the twelfth year of Ahaz, king of Judah, Hoshea, son of Elah, reigned in Samaria over Israel for nine years.

**17:2** And he did evil before the Lord, yet not like the kings of Israel who had been before him.

**17:3** Against him Shalmaneser, king of the Assyrians, went up, and Hoshea became his servant and paid him tribute.

**17:4** And when the king of the Assyrians discovered that Hoshea, striving to rebel, had sent messengers to So, king of Egypt, so that he would not pay tribute to the king of the Assyrians as he had been accustomed to do each year, he besieged him and sent him bound into prison.

**17:5** And he traversed the whole land, and going up to Samaria, he besieged it for three years.

**17:6** Now in the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of the Assyrians captured Samaria and deported Israel to the Assyrians; and he placed them in Halah and in Habor by the river Gozan, in the cities of the Medes.

**17:7** For it happened that when the sons of Israel had sinned against the Lord their God, who had brought them out of the land of Egypt, from the hand of Pharaoh, king of Egypt, they worshiped foreign gods.

**17:8** And they walked according to the rite of the nations whom the Lord had consumed in the sight of the sons of Israel, and of the kings of Israel, because they had done likewise.

**17:9** And the sons of Israel offended the Lord their God with things that were not right; and they built for themselves high places in all their towns, from the tower of the watchmen to the fortified city.

**17:10** And they made for themselves statues and groves on every high hill and under every shady tree,

**17:11** and there they burned incense upon altars in the manner of the nations whom the Lord had removed from their presence; and they did most wicked things, provoking the Lord.

**17:12** And they worshiped impurities, concerning which the Lord commanded them not to do this thing.

**17:13** And the Lord testified against Israel and against Judah through the hand of all the prophets and seers, saying, "Turn back from your most wicked ways, and keep My precepts and ceremonies, according to all the law that I commanded your fathers, and as I sent to you by the hand of My servants the prophets."

**17:14** Yet they did not listen, but hardened their neck like the neck of their fathers, who refused to obey the Lord their God.

**17:15** And they rejected His ordinances, and the covenant that He made with their fathers, and the testimonies with which He bore witness to them; and they followed vanities and acted vainly, and they followed the nations that were all around them, concerning whom the Lord had commanded them that they should not do as those also did.

**17:16** And they abandoned all the precepts of the Lord their God, and made for themselves two cast calves and groves, and bowed down to all the host of heaven, and served Baal,

**17:17** and consecrated their sons and their daughters through the fire; and they devoted themselves to divinations and auguries, and gave themselves over to do evil before the Lord, so as to provoke Him.

**17:18** And the Lord was intensely angry with Israel, and removed them from His sight, and none remained except the tribe of Judah alone.

**17:19** Yet not even Judah itself kept the commandments of the Lord its God, but walked in the errors of Israel, which it had practiced.

**17:20** And the Lord cast off all the seed of Israel, and afflicted them, and delivered them into the hand of plunderers, until He cast them out from His presence,

**17:21** already from that time when Israel was torn away from the house of David, and they appointed Jeroboam, son of Nebat, as king for themselves; for Jeroboam separated Israel from the Lord and caused them to sin a great sin.

**17:22** And the sons of Israel walked in all the sins of Jeroboam that he had committed, and they did not depart from them,

**17:23** until the Lord removed Israel from His presence, just as He had spoken by the hand of all His servants the prophets; and Israel was deported from its own land to the Assyrians, to this day.

**17:24** Now the king of the Assyrians brought people from Babylon, and from Cuthah, and from Avva, and from Hamath, and from Sepharvaim, and settled them in the cities of Samaria in place of the sons of Israel; and they took possession of Samaria and lived in its towns.

**17:25** And when they began to live there, they did not fear the Lord; and the Lord sent lions among them, which were killing them.

**17:26** And it was reported to the king of the Assyrians, and it was said, "The nations that you deported and caused to live in the cities of Samaria do not know the ordinances of the God of the land; and the Lord has sent lions among them, and look, they are killing them, because they do not know the rite of the God of the land."

**17:27** Then the king of the Assyrians commanded, saying, "Lead to that place one of the priests whom you brought captive from there, and let him go and live with them, and let him teach them the ordinances of the God of the land."

**17:28** Therefore, when one of these priests who had been led away captive from Samaria arrived, he lived in Bethel and taught them how they should worship the Lord.

**17:29** And each nation crafted its own god, and they placed them in the high shrines that the Samaritans had made, nation by nation in its own towns in which it lived.

**17:30** For the Babylonian men made Succoth-benoth, and the Cuthite men made Nergal, and the men from Hamath made Ashima.

**17:31** Moreover, the Hivites made Nibhaz and Tartak. And those who were from Sepharvaim burned their sons with fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim,

**17:32** and nevertheless they worshiped the Lord. Moreover, they made for themselves priests of the high places from among the lowest, and placed them in the lofty shrines.

**17:33** And while they worshiped the Lord, they also served their own gods according to the custom of the nations from which they had been deported to Samaria.

**17:34** To the present day they follow the ancient custom: they do not fear the Lord, nor do they keep His ceremonies, judgments, and law, and the commandment that the Lord had commanded the sons of Jacob, whom He surnamed Israel;

**17:35** and He had made a covenant with them and had commanded them, saying, "Do not fear foreign gods, and do not bow down to them, nor worship them, and do not sacrifice to them;

**17:36** but the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt with great strength and with an outstretched arm: fear Him, and bow down to Him, and sacrifice to Him.

**17:37** Also observe the ceremonies, and the judgments, and the law, and the commandment that He wrote for you, so as to do them all the days; and do not fear foreign gods.

**17:38** And do not forget the covenant that He made with you, nor worship foreign gods,

**17:39** but fear the Lord your God, and He Himself will deliver you from the hand of all your enemies."

**17:40** Yet they did not listen, but continued to act according to their former custom.

**17:41** So these nations were indeed fearing the Lord, yet nevertheless also serving their own idols; for both their sons and their grandsons, just as their fathers did, so they do to the present day.

## Translator Notes

- **17:8** — The Vulgate reads 'consumed' (consumpserat) and concludes 'and of the kings of Israel, because they had done likewise' (et regum Israel, quia similiter fecerant), where the Hebrew refers to the customs that the kings of Israel had made.
- **17:10** — Following its consistent idiom, the Vulgate renders the Hebrew sacred pillars and Asherah poles as 'statues and groves' (statuas et lucos).
- **17:19** — The Vulgate reads 'errors' (erroribus) where the Hebrew has 'statutes' or 'customs.'
- **17:31** — The Vulgate reads 'the Hivites' (Hevaei), whereas the Hebrew text reads 'the Avvites' (the inhabitants of Avva, v. 24).
- **17:32** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew phrase for people of every rank as 'from among the lowest' (de novissimis), referring to priests appointed from the lowest class.
