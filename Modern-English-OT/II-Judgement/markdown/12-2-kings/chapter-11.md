## Text

**11:1** Now Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, seeing that her son was dead, arose and killed all the royal seed.

**11:2** But Jehosheba, the daughter of King Joram, sister of Ahaziah, taking Joash the son of Ahaziah, stole him from the midst of the king's sons who were being killed, and his nurse from the bedchamber, and she hid him from the face of Athaliah so that he would not be killed.

**11:3** And he was with her for six years secretly in the house of the Lord, while Athaliah reigned over the land.

**11:4** Now in the seventh year Jehoiada sent, and taking the centurions and the soldiers, he brought them in to himself into the temple of the Lord, and made a covenant with them; and putting them under oath in the house of the Lord, he showed them the king's son,

**11:5** and he commanded them, saying, "This is the word that you must carry out:

**11:6** let a third part of you come in on the Sabbath and keep the watch of the king's house. And let a third part be at the gate of Sur, and let a third part be at the gate that is behind the quarters of the shield-bearers; and you will keep the watch of the house of Messa.

**11:7** But let two parts of you, all who go out on the Sabbath, keep the watch of the house of the Lord around the king.

**11:8** And you will surround him, having weapons in your hands; and if anyone enters the enclosure of the temple, let him be killed; and you will be with the king as he comes in and goes out."

**11:9** And the centurions did according to all that Jehoiada the priest had commanded them; and each one taking his men who were coming in on the Sabbath, together with those who were going out on the Sabbath, they came to Jehoiada the priest.

**11:10** And he gave them the spears and weapons of King David, which were in the house of the Lord.

**11:11** And they stood, each one having weapons in his hand, from the right side of the temple to the left side of the altar and of the sanctuary, around the king.

**11:12** And he brought out the king's son, and placed on him the diadem and the testimony; and they made him king and anointed him; and clapping their hands, they said, "Long live the king!"

**11:13** Now Athaliah heard the sound of the people running, and going in to the crowds in the temple of the Lord,

**11:14** she saw the king standing on the platform according to custom, and the singers and the trumpets near him, and all the people of the land rejoicing and sounding trumpets. And she tore her garments and cried out, "Conspiracy! Conspiracy!"

**11:15** Then Jehoiada commanded the centurions who were over the army and said to them, "Lead her out beyond the enclosures of the temple, and whoever follows her, let him be struck with the sword." For the priest had said, "Let her not be killed in the temple of the Lord."

**11:16** And they laid hands on her and drove her by way of the entrance of the horses, beside the palace, and she was killed there.

**11:17** Therefore Jehoiada made a covenant between the Lord and the king and the people, that they would be the people of the Lord, and between the king and the people.

**11:18** And all the people of the land entered the temple of Baal and destroyed his altars, and thoroughly crushed the images; they also killed Mattan the priest of Baal before the altar. And the priest stationed guards in the house of the Lord.

**11:19** And he took the centurions, and the legions of the Cherethites and Pelethites, and all the people of the land, and they brought the king down from the house of the Lord, and came by way of the gate of the shield-bearers into the palace; and he sat on the throne of the kings.

**11:20** And all the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet; but Athaliah was killed with the sword in the king's house.

## Translator Notes

- **11:2** — Vulgate 'furata est eum ... et nutricem ejus de triclinio' ("stole him ... and his nurse from the bedchamber") treats the bedchamber as the place from which the nurse was taken, whereas the Hebrew states that Jehosheba hid Joash and his nurse in the bedchamber.
- **11:6** — Vulgate 'domus Messa' ("the house of Messa") treats the rare Hebrew term 'massah' ("barrier" or "defense") as a proper name, and renders the Hebrew "runners/guards" here and in 11:19 as 'scutarii' ("shield-bearers").
- **11:8** — Vulgate 'septum templi' ("the enclosure of the temple," also plural in 11:15) renders Hebrew 'sederot' ("ranks"), understanding the boundary as the temple precinct rather than the soldiers' lines.
- **11:14** — Vulgate reads 'super tribunal' ("on the platform") and 'cantores' ("the singers") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "by the pillar" and "the captains" (reflecting a different Hebrew vocalization).
- **11:19** — Vulgate 'Cerethi et Phelethi legiones' ("the legions of the Cherethites and Pelethites") identifies the royal guard with David's famed corps, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the Carites and the guards."
