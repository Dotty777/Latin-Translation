## Text

**10:1** Now Ahab had seventy sons in Samaria. Therefore Jehu wrote letters and sent them to Samaria, to the nobles of the city, and to the elders, and to the guardians of Ahab, saying,

**10:2** "As soon as you receive these letters, you who have the sons of your master, and chariots, and horses, and fortified cities, and weapons,

**10:3** choose the best one, and the one who pleases you from the sons of your master, and place him on the throne of his father, and fight for the house of your master."

**10:4** They were greatly afraid and said, "Look, two kings could not stand before him, and how will we be able to resist?"

**10:5** Therefore the overseers of the house, and the governors of the city, and the elders, and the guardians sent to Jehu, saying, "We are your servants; whatever you command we will do, nor will we appoint a king for ourselves; whatever pleases you, do it."

**10:6** Then he wrote letters back to them a second time, saying, "If you are mine and obey me, take the heads of the sons of your master and come to me at this same hour tomorrow in Jezreel." Now the king's sons, seventy men, were being brought up with the nobles of the city.

**10:7** And when the letters came to them, they took the king's sons and killed seventy men, and put their heads in baskets, and sent them to him in Jezreel.

**10:8** Then a messenger came and reported to him, saying, "They have brought the heads of the king's sons." And he replied, "Place them in two heaps beside the entrance of the gate until morning."

**10:9** And when daylight came, he went out, and standing, said to all the people, "You are righteous; if I conspired against my master and killed him, who struck down all these?

**10:10** See therefore now that nothing of the words of the Lord has fallen to the ground, which the Lord spoke concerning the house of Ahab; and the Lord has done what He spoke by the hand of His servant Elijah."

**10:11** Therefore Jehu struck down all who remained of the house of Ahab in Jezreel, and all his nobles, and acquaintances, and priests, until no remnant was left of him.

**10:12** And he arose and went to Samaria; and when he had come to the Chamber of the Shepherds on the way,

**10:13** he found the brothers of Ahaziah king of Judah, and he said to them, "Who are you?" And they answered, "We are the brothers of Ahaziah, and we have come down to greet the sons of the king and the sons of the queen."

**10:14** And he said, "Seize them alive." And when they had seized them alive, they slaughtered them in the cistern beside the Chamber, forty-two men; and he did not leave any of them.

**10:15** And when he had departed from there, he found Jonadab the son of Rechab coming to meet him, and he blessed him. And he said to him, "Is your heart right, just as my heart is with your heart?" And Jonadab said, "It is." "If it is," he said, "give me your hand." And he gave him his hand. And he lifted him up to himself into the chariot,

**10:16** and said to him, "Come with me, and see my zeal for the Lord." And having placed him in his chariot,

**10:17** he brought him into Samaria. And he struck down all who had remained of Ahab in Samaria, down to the last one, according to the word of the Lord which He spoke through Elijah.

**10:18** Therefore Jehu gathered all the people and said to them, "Ahab worshiped Baal a little, but I will worship him more.

**10:19** Now therefore, summon to me all the prophets of Baal, and all his servants, and all his priests; let there be no one who does not come, for I have a great sacrifice for Baal; whoever is missing will not live." Now Jehu was doing this deceitfully, so that he might destroy the worshipers of Baal.

**10:20** And he said, "Sanctify a solemn day for Baal." And he proclaimed it,

**10:21** and sent throughout all the borders of Israel, and all the servants of Baal came; not even one remained who did not come. And they entered the temple of Baal, and the house of Baal was filled from end to end.

**10:22** And he said to those who were over the garments, "Bring out vestments for all the servants of Baal." And they brought out garments for them.

**10:23** And Jehu and Jonadab the son of Rechab entered the temple of Baal, and he said to the worshipers of Baal, "Search carefully and see that none of the servants of the Lord happens to be with you, but only the servants of Baal."

**10:24** Therefore they went in to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings. Now Jehu had prepared for himself eighty men outside and had said to them, "Whoever escapes from these men whom I bring into your hands, his life will be for the life of that one."

**10:25** And it happened, when the burnt offering had been completed, that Jehu commanded his soldiers and captains, "Go in and strike them down; let no one escape." And they struck them down with the edge of the sword, and the soldiers and captains cast them out, and went into the city of the temple of Baal,

**10:26** and brought out the statue from the shrine of Baal and burned it,

**10:27** and shattered it. They also destroyed the sanctuary of Baal, and made latrines in its place until this day.

**10:28** Thus Jehu wiped out Baal from Israel;

**10:29** nevertheless, he did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who made Israel sin, nor did he abandon the golden calves that were in Bethel and in Dan.

**10:30** And the Lord said to Jehu, "Because you have diligently done what was right and what was pleasing in My eyes, and have done all things that were in My heart against the house of Ahab, your sons to the fourth generation will sit on the throne of Israel."

**10:31** Yet Jehu was not careful to walk in the law of the Lord God of Israel with all his heart, for he did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam, who had made Israel sin.

**10:32** In those days the Lord began to grow weary of Israel; and Hazael struck them in all the borders of Israel,

**10:33** from the Jordan toward the eastern region, all the land of Gilead, and Gad, and Reuben, and Manasseh, from Aroer, which is by the brook Arnon, and Gilead, and Bashan.

**10:34** Now the rest of the words of Jehu, and all that he did, and his might, are these not written in the book of the words of the days of the kings of Israel?

**10:35** And Jehu slept with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria; and Jehoahaz his son reigned in his place.

**10:36** Now the days that Jehu reigned over Israel are twenty-eight years in Samaria.

## Translator Notes

- **10:9** — Vulgate 'si ego conjuravi' ("if I conspired") frames Jehu's admission as a rhetorical conditional, whereas the Hebrew reads "look, I conspired."
- **10:12** — Vulgate 'Cameram pastorum' ("the Chamber of the Shepherds") renders the Hebrew place name Beth-eked of the Shepherds (literally "house of binding/shearing of the shepherds").
- **10:24** — Vulgate mirrors the compressed Hebrew idiom ('anima ejus ... pro anima illius'), meaning that the life of whichever guard lets a captive escape will answer for the escapee's life.
- **10:25** — Vulgate 'civitatem templi Baal' ("the city of the temple of Baal") literally renders the Hebrew expression for the inner citadel or sanctuary precinct of Baal's temple; in 10:26 the Vulgate also reads singular 'statuam' ("statue") where the Hebrew has plural pillars.
- **10:32** — Vulgate 'coepit Dominus taedere super Israel' ("the Lord began to grow weary of Israel") reflects a distinct reading of the Hebrew verb, where the Masoretic Text reads "began to cut off [territory] in Israel."
