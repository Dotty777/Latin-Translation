## Text

**4:1** Now a certain woman of the wives of the prophets cried out to Elisha, saying, "Your servant, my husband, is dead, and you know that your servant feared the Lord; and now the creditor has come to take my two sons to serve him."

**4:2** Elisha said to her, "What do you want me to do for you? Tell me, what do you have in your house?" And she answered, "I, your maidservant, have nothing in my house except a little oil with which to anoint myself."

**4:3** He said to her, "Go, borrow from all your neighbors empty vessels, not a few,

**4:4** and go inside and shut your door when you and your sons are within; and pour from it into all these vessels, and when they are full, you will take them away."

**4:5** So the woman went and shut the door on herself and on her sons; they kept bringing the vessels, and she kept pouring.

**4:6** And when the vessels were full, she said to her son, "Bring me yet another vessel." And he answered, "I do not have any." And the oil stopped.

**4:7** Then she came and reported to the man of God. And he said, "Go, sell the oil, and repay your creditor; and you and your sons, live on what remains."

**4:8** Now a certain day came, and Elisha was passing through Shunem; and there was a great woman there, who detained him to eat bread; and as he frequently passed by there, he would turn aside to her to eat bread.

**4:9** And she said to her husband, "I perceive that this man is a holy man of God, who passes by us frequently.

**4:10** Therefore let us make a small upper room for him, and let us place for him in it a bed, a table, a chair, and a lampstand, so that when he comes to us, he may stay there."

**4:11** So a certain day came, and coming by, he turned aside into the upper room and rested there.

**4:12** And he said to Gehazi his servant, "Call this Shunammite woman." And when he had called her, and she stood before him,

**4:13** he said to his servant, "Speak to her: 'Look, you have diligently served us in all things; what do you want me to do for you? Do you have a matter of business, and do you want me to speak to the king or to the commander of the army?'" And she answered, "I dwell in the midst of my people."

**4:14** And he said, "What then does she want me to do for her?" And Gehazi said, "Do not ask; for she has no son, and her husband is old."

**4:15** So he commanded him to call her; and when she had been called and stood before the door,

**4:16** he said to her, "At this time, and at this same hour, if life accompanies us, you will have a son in your womb." And she answered, "Do not, I beg you, my lord, man of God, do not lie to your maidservant."

**4:17** And the woman conceived and gave birth to a son at the time and at the same hour that Elisha had said.

**4:18** Now the boy grew; and when a certain day came, and he had gone out to his father, to the reapers,

**4:19** he said to his father, "My head hurts, my head hurts!" And he said to a servant, "Pick him up and bring him to his mother."

**4:20** And when he had picked him up and brought him to his mother, she placed him on her knees until noon, and he died.

**4:21** Then she went up and laid him on the bed of the man of God, and shut the door; and going out,

**4:22** she called her husband and said, "Please send with me one of the servants and a female donkey, so that I may run out to the man of God and return."

**4:23** He said to her, "For what reason are you going to him? Today is not the new moon, nor the Sabbath." And she answered, "I will go."

**4:24** And she saddled the female donkey and commanded the servant, "Drive on and hurry; do not delay me in going, and do this which I command you."

**4:25** Therefore she set out and came to the man of God to Mount Carmel; and when the man of God saw her across the way, he said to Gehazi his servant, "Look, there is that Shunammite woman.

**4:26** Go therefore to meet her and say to her, 'Is it going well with you, and with your husband, and with your son?'" And she answered, "It is well."

**4:27** And when she came to the man of God on the mountain, she grasped his feet; and Gehazi approached to move her away. And the man of God said, "Leave her alone, for her soul is in bitterness, and the Lord has hidden it from me and has not told me."

**4:28** And she said to him, "Did I ask for a son from my lord? Did I not say to you, 'Do not deceive me'?"

**4:29** And he said to Gehazi, "Bind up your loins, take my staff in your hand, and go. If a man meets you, do not greet him; and if anyone greets you, do not answer him; and you will place my staff upon the face of the boy."

**4:30** But the mother of the boy said, "As the Lord lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." Therefore he rose and followed her.

**4:31** Now Gehazi had gone on ahead of them and had placed the staff upon the face of the boy, and there was no voice and no sensation; and he returned to meet him and reported to him, saying, "The boy has not risen."

**4:32** Therefore Elisha entered the house, and look, the boy was lying dead on his bed;

**4:33** and having gone in, he shut the door on himself and on the boy, and prayed to the Lord.

**4:34** And he climbed up and lay upon the boy, and placed his mouth upon his mouth, and his eyes upon his eyes, and his hands upon his hands; and he bent himself over him, and the flesh of the boy grew warm.

**4:35** Then he turned back and walked about in the house, once back and forth; and he climbed up and lay upon him; and the boy yawned seven times and opened his eyes.

**4:36** Then he called Gehazi and said to him, "Call this Shunammite woman." Having been called, she entered to him. And he said, "Pick up your son."

**4:37** She came and fell at his feet, and bowed down upon the ground; and she picked up her son and went out.

**4:38** And Elisha returned to Gilgal. Now there was a famine in the land, and the sons of the prophets were dwelling before him. And he said to one of his servants, "Put on the large pot and cook stew for the sons of the prophets."

**4:39** And one went out into the field to gather wild herbs; and he found something like a wild vine, and gathered from it bitter gourds of the field, and filled his cloak, and returning, he chopped them into the pot of stew; for he did not know what it was.

**4:40** So they poured it out for their companions to eat; and when they tasted from the stew, they cried out, saying, "Death is in the pot, man of God!" And they could not eat.

**4:41** But he said, "Bring flour." And when they had brought it, he put it into the pot and said, "Pour it out for the crowd, so that they may eat." And there was no longer any bitterness in the pot.

**4:42** Now a certain man came from Baal-shalishah, bringing to the man of God loaves of the firstfruits, twenty barley loaves, and new grain in his bag. And he said, "Give it to the people, so that they may eat."

**4:43** And his attendant answered him, "How much is this, that I should set it before one hundred men?" Again he said, "Give it to the people, so that they may eat; for this is what the Lord says: 'They will eat, and there will be some left over.'"

**4:44** So he set it before them; and they ate, and there was some left over, according to the word of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — The Vulgate reads "with which to anoint myself" (quo ungar), interpreting the Hebrew term for an oil flask from the verbal root meaning "to anoint."
- **4:16** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew seasonal idiom as "if life accompanies us" (si vita comes fuerit) and reads "you will have a son in your womb" (habebis in utero filium) where the Hebrew has "you will embrace a son."
- **4:23** — The Vulgate reads "I will go" (Vadam) rather than the Hebrew greeting "Peace" (meaning "It is well").
- **4:35** — The Vulgate reads "yawned" (oscitavit) where the Hebrew text has "sneezed."
- **4:41** — The Vulgate specifies "bitterness" (amaritudinis), matching the bitter colocynth gourds of 4:39, where the Hebrew has "harmful thing."
