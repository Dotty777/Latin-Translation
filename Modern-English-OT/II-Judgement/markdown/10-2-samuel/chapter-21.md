## Text

**21:1** A famine also occurred in the days of David for three years continuously, and David consulted the oracle of the Lord. And the Lord said, "Because of Saul and his house of bloodshed, because he killed the Gibeonites."

**21:2** Therefore, having called the Gibeonites, the king spoke to them (now the Gibeonites were not of the sons of Israel, but the remnant of the Amorites; for the sons of Israel had sworn to them, and Saul wanted to strike them down in zeal, as if for the sons of Israel and Judah).

**21:3** David therefore said to the Gibeonites, "What should I do for you? And what will be the atonement for you, so that you may bless the inheritance of the Lord?"

**21:4** And the Gibeonites said to him, "We have no dispute over silver and gold, but against Saul and against his house; nor do we want any man of Israel to be killed." To them the king said, "What then do you want me to do for you?"

**21:5** They said to the king, "The man who crushed us and oppressed us unjustly, we must wipe out in such a way that not even one remains from his lineage in all the borders of Israel.

**21:6** Let seven men of his sons be given to us, so that we may crucify them to the Lord in Gibeah of Saul, once the chosen of the Lord." And the king said, "I will give them."

**21:7** And the king spared Mephibosheth the son of Jonathan, the son of Saul, because of the oath of the Lord that had been between David and Jonathan the son of Saul.

**21:8** So the king took the two sons of Rizpah the daughter of Aiah, whom she bore to Saul, Armoni and Mephibosheth, and the five sons of Michal the daughter of Saul, whom she had borne to Adriel the son of Barzillai, who was a Meholathite,

**21:9** and he gave them into the hands of the Gibeonites, who crucified them on the mountain before the Lord; and these seven fell killed together in the first days of the harvest, as the reaping of the barley was beginning.

**21:10** Then Rizpah the daughter of Aiah, taking sackcloth, spread it out for herself on the rock from the beginning of the harvest until water dripped upon them from the sky; and she did not allow the birds to tear them by day, nor the beasts by night.

**21:11** And it was reported to David what Rizpah the daughter of Aiah, the concubine of Saul, had done.

**21:12** And David went and took the bones of Saul and the bones of Jonathan his son from the men of Jabesh-gilead, who had stolen them from the square of Beth-shan, in which the Philistines had hanged them when they had killed Saul on Gilboa;

**21:13** and he carried away from there the bones of Saul and the bones of Jonathan his son; and gathering the bones of those who had been affixed,

**21:14** they buried them with the bones of Saul and of Jonathan his son in the land of Benjamin, on the side, in the tomb of Kish his father; and they did everything that the king had commanded, and God became favorable again to the land after these things.

**21:15** Now a battle of the Philistines occurred again against Israel, and David went down, and his servants with him, and they were fighting against the Philistines. And as David grew faint,

**21:16** Ishbi-benob, who was of the race of Arapha, the iron of whose spear weighed three hundred ounces, and who was girded with a new sword, attempted to strike down David.

**21:17** And Abishai the son of Zeruiah came to his defense, and struck the Philistine and killed him. Then the men of David swore, saying, "You will no longer go out with us to war, so that you do not extinguish the lamp of Israel."

**21:18** There was also a second war in Gob against the Philistines; then Sibbecai the Hushathite struck down Saph, of the lineage of Arapha, of the race of the giants.

**21:19** There was also a third war in Gob against the Philistines, in which Adeodatus the son of Saltus, an embroiderer, the Bethlehemite, struck down Goliath the Gittite, the shaft of whose spear was like a weavers' beam.

**21:20** There was a fourth war in Gath, in which there was a tall man who had six digits on each hand and foot, that is, twenty-four; and he was of the stock of Arapha.

**21:21** And he reviled Israel; but Jonathan the son of Shimea, the brother of David, struck him down.

**21:22** These four were born of Arapha in Gath, and they fell by the hand of David and of his servants.

## Translator Notes

- **21:1** — "the oracle of the Lord": the Vulgate renders the Hebrew idiom "the face of the Lord" as "oraculum Domini," referring to seeking a divine answer through the sanctuary oracle.
- **21:6** — "crucify" and "once the chosen of the Lord": the Vulgate specifically translates the Hebrew execution verb as crucifixion here ("crucifigamus"), in v. 9 ("crucifixerunt"), and in v. 13 ("affixi fuerant," "had been affixed"), and adds "once" ("quondam") to note Saul's former status as the Lord's chosen.
- **21:14** — "on the side": the Vulgate translates the Hebrew place name Zela (literally "side" or "rib") as a common noun phrase ("in latere").
- **21:16** — "Arapha" and "the iron of whose spear weighed three hundred ounces": the Vulgate fuses the Hebrew definite article with "raphah" into the proper name Arapha (vv. 16, 18, 20, 22; paired with "of the race of the giants" in v. 18), and specifies three hundred ounces of iron rather than three hundred shekels of bronze.
- **21:19** — "Adeodatus the son of Saltus, an embroiderer": the Vulgate translates the Hebrew name Elhanan as the Latin name Adeodatus ("Given by God"), Jaare as Saltus ("Forest"), and oregim as the trade "embroiderer" ("polymitarius"), while naming his opponent simply "Goliath the Gittite" as in the Masoretic Text (cf. 1 Chronicles 20:5).
