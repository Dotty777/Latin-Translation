## Text

**15:1** Now after these things, Absalom made for himself chariots and horsemen, and fifty men to go before him.

**15:2** And Absalom, rising early in the morning, would stand beside the entrance of the gate; and every man who had a case to come to the king's judgment, Absalom would call to himself and say, "From which city are you?" And answering, he would say, "I, your servant, am from one of the tribes of Israel."

**15:3** And Absalom would answer him, "Your words seem good and just to me, but there is no one appointed by the king to hear you." And Absalom would say,

**15:4** "Who will appoint me judge over the land, so that all who have a case may come to me, and I may judge justly?"

**15:5** Moreover, when a man approached him to greet him, he would stretch out his hand, and taking hold of him, would kiss him.

**15:6** And he would do this to all Israel coming to judgment to be heard by the king, and he enticed the hearts of the men of Israel.

**15:7** Now after forty years, Absalom said to King David, "Let me go and pay my vows that I vowed to the Lord in Hebron.

**15:8** For your servant solemnly vowed while he was in Geshur of Syria, saying, 'If the Lord brings me back to Jerusalem, I will sacrifice to the Lord.'"

**15:9** And King David said to him, "Go in peace." And he arose and went away to Hebron.

**15:10** Now Absalom sent spies throughout all the tribes of Israel, saying, "As soon as you hear the blast of the trumpet, say, 'Absalom has become king in Hebron.'"

**15:11** Moreover, two hundred invited men from Jerusalem went with Absalom, going with an innocent heart and completely ignorant of the matter.

**15:12** Absalom also summoned Ahithophel the Gilonite, David's counselor, from his city Giloh. And while he was offering sacrifices, a strong conspiracy was formed, and the people flocking together increased with Absalom.

**15:13** Then a messenger came to David, saying, "All Israel is following Absalom with its whole heart."

**15:14** And David said to his servants who were with him in Jerusalem, "Arise, let us flee, for there will be no escape for us from before Absalom; hurry to leave, or else he may come and overtake us, and bring ruin upon us, and strike the city with the edge of the sword."

**15:15** And the king's servants said to him, "Everything that our lord the king commands, we, your servants, will gladly carry out."

**15:16** Therefore the king and his entire household went out on foot; and the king left behind ten women who were concubines to guard the house.

**15:17** And the king and all Israel, having gone out on foot, stood at a distance from the house;

**15:18** and all his servants were walking beside him; and the legions of the Cherethites and the Pelethites, and all the Gittites, mighty warriors, six hundred men who had followed him on foot from Gath, were going before the king.

**15:19** Then the king said to Ittai the Gittite, "Why are you coming with us? Return and dwell with the king, because you are a foreigner and have departed from your own place.

**15:20** Yesterday you came, and today will you be compelled to go out with us? As for me, I will go where I am going; return, and take your brothers back with you, and the Lord will show mercy and truth toward you, because you have shown grace and faithfulness."

**15:21** And Ittai answered the king, saying, "As the Lord lives, and as my lord the king lives, in whatever place you may be, my lord the king, whether in death or in life, there your servant will be."

**15:22** And David said to Ittai, "Come, and pass over." And Ittai the Gittite passed over, and all the men who were with him, and the rest of the multitude.

**15:23** And everyone was weeping with a loud voice, and all the people were passing over; the king also was crossing the brook Kidron, and all the people were walking toward the road that faces the wilderness.

**15:24** Now Zadok the priest also came, and all the Levites with him, carrying the ark of the covenant of God; and they set down the ark of God. And Abiathar went up until the full number of all the people who had gone out of the city was complete.

**15:25** And the king said to Zadok, "Carry the ark of God back into the city; if I find favor in the eyes of the Lord, He will bring me back and show me it and His tabernacle.

**15:26** But if He says to me, 'You do not please Me,' here I am; let Him do what is good before Himself."

**15:27** And the king said to Zadok the priest, "Seer, return to the city in peace, and let Ahimaaz your son and Jonathan the son of Abiathar, your two sons, be with you.

**15:28** Look, I will hide myself in the plains of the wilderness, until word comes from you informing me."

**15:29** Therefore Zadok and Abiathar carried the ark of God back to Jerusalem, and they remained there.

**15:30** Moreover, David was going up the ascent of the Olives, climbing and weeping, walking barefoot and with his head covered; and all the people who were with him also went up with their heads covered, lamenting.

**15:31** Now it was reported to David that Ahithophel was also in the conspiracy with Absalom; and David said, "Make foolish, I pray, Lord, the counsel of Ahithophel."

**15:32** And as David was ascending the summit of the mountain where he was going to worship the Lord, look, Hushai the Archite met him with his garment torn and his head full of earth.

**15:33** And David said to him, "If you come with me, you will be a burden to me;

**15:34** but if you return to the city and say to Absalom, 'I am your servant, king; just as I was your father's servant, so I will be your servant,' you will thwart the counsel of Ahithophel.

**15:35** Moreover, you have with you Zadok and Abiathar the priests; and every word that you hear from the king's house, you will report to Zadok and Abiathar the priests.

**15:36** And their two sons are with them, Ahimaaz the son of Zadok and Jonathan the son of Abiathar; and through them you will send to me every word that you hear."

**15:37** So as Hushai, David's friend, was entering the city, Absalom also entered Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **15:7** — Both the Vulgate (Post quadraginta autem annos) and the Hebrew Masoretic Text read 'forty years,' whereas the Lucianic Greek and the Syriac read 'four years.'
- **15:18** — Vulgate describes the Cherethites and Pelethites as 'legions' (legiones) and adds 'mighty warriors' (pugnatores validi) and 'on foot' (pedites) in describing the six hundred Gittites.
- **15:20** — Vulgate expands the blessing to include 'and the Lord will show mercy and truth toward you, because you have shown grace and faithfulness' (et Dominus faciet tecum misericordiam et veritatem, quia ostendisti gratiam et fidem), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text ends simply with 'steadfast love and faithfulness be with you.'
- **15:27** — Vulgate renders the Hebrew expression as a direct vocative, 'O seer' (O videns), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text is often read as a question ('Are you not a seer?') or an imperative ('See!').
- **15:28** — Vulgate reads 'I will hide myself in the plains of the wilderness' (abscondar in campestribus deserti), compared to the Hebrew 'I will linger at the fords/plains of the wilderness.'
