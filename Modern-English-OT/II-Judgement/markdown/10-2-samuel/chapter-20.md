## Text

**20:1** It also happened that there was a man of Belial there, named Sheba, the son of Bichri, a Jeminite; and he blew the horn and said, "We have no part in David, nor inheritance in the son of Jesse; return to your tents, Israel!"

**20:2** So all Israel separated from David and followed Sheba the son of Bichri; but the men of Judah clung to their king from the Jordan to Jerusalem.

**20:3** And when the king came to his house in Jerusalem, he took the ten concubine women whom he had left behind to guard the house and placed them under guard, providing food for them; and he did not go in to them, but they were shut up until the day of their death, living in widowhood.

**20:4** Then the king said to Amasa, "Summon all the men of Judah to me by the third day, and be present yourself."

**20:5** So Amasa went out to summon Judah, but he delayed beyond the appointed time that the king had set for him.

**20:6** Then David said to Abishai, "Now Sheba the son of Bichri is going to afflict us more than Absalom; therefore, take your lord's servants and pursue him, or else he might find fortified cities and escape us."

**20:7** So Joab's men went out with him, along with the Cherethites and the Pelethites; and all the mighty men went out from Jerusalem to pursue Sheba the son of Bichri.

**20:8** And when they were beside the great stone that is in Gibeon, Amasa came and met them. Now Joab was dressed in a tunic fitted tightly to the measure of his frame, and over it he was belted with a sword hanging down to his hips in a sheath, which was so fashioned that at a slight movement it could come out and strike.

**20:9** So Joab said to Amasa, "Greetings, my brother." And with his right hand he took hold of Amasa's chin, as if to kiss him.

**20:10** Now Amasa did not notice the sword that Joab had; and he struck him in the side and poured out his intestines onto the ground, and did not add a second wound; and he died. Then Joab and his brother Abishai pursued Sheba the son of Bichri.

**20:11** Meanwhile, certain men from Joab's companions, standing beside Amasa's corpse, said, "Look at the one who wanted to be David's companion in place of Joab!"

**20:12** Now Amasa lay spattered with blood in the middle of the road. A certain man saw this, that all the people were stopping to look at him, and he removed Amasa from the road into a field and covered him with a garment, so that those passing by would not stop because of him.

**20:13** So once he had been removed from the road, every man passed on, following Joab to pursue Sheba the son of Bichri.

**20:14** Meanwhile, he had passed through all the tribes of Israel to Abel and Beth-maacah, and all the chosen men had gathered to him.

**20:15** So they came and attacked him in Abel and in Beth-maacah, and they surrounded the city with siegeworks, and the city was besieged; and all the crowd that was with Joab was working to destroy the walls.

**20:16** Then a wise woman cried out from the city, "Listen, listen! Tell Joab, 'Come near here, and I will speak with you.'"

**20:17** And when he had approached her, she said to him, "Are you Joab?" And he answered, "I am." And she spoke to him in this way: "Listen to the words of your maidservant." And he answered, "I am listening."

**20:18** And again she said, "A saying used to be spoken in an old proverb: 'Let those who inquire, inquire in Abel,' and so they brought matters to completion.

**20:19** Am I not the one who answers truth in Israel, and you seek to overthrow a city and destroy a mother in Israel? Why do you bring the inheritance of the Lord to ruin?"

**20:20** And Joab answered and said, "Far be it, far be this from me! I am not bringing ruin, nor am I demolishing.

**20:21** That is not how the matter stands, but a man from Mount Ephraim, Sheba the son of Bichri by surname, has lifted up his hand against King David. Hand him over alone, and we will withdraw from the city." And the woman said to Joab, "Look, his head will be thrown to you over the wall."

**20:22** So she went in to all the people and spoke to them wisely; and they cut off the head of Sheba the son of Bichri and threw it to Joab. And he blew the trumpet, and they withdrew from the city, each one to his tents; and Joab returned to Jerusalem to the king.

**20:23** Now Joab was over all the army of Israel, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites.

**20:24** Adoram was over the tribute, and Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was recorder.

**20:25** Sheva was scribe, and Zadok and Abiathar were priests.

**20:26** And Ira the Jairite was David's priest.

## Translator Notes

- **20:1** — Latin vir Belial retains the untranslated Hebrew phrase meaning "a worthless or wicked man," and Jemineus ("Jeminite") reflects the shortened gentilic for a Benjaminite (cf. 19:17).
- **20:8** — The Vulgate describes Joab's sword as deliberately fashioned to come out and strike at a slight movement, whereas the Masoretic Text states that it fell out as he walked.
- **20:11** — The Vulgate has plural soldiers mocking Amasa's corpse as "the one who wanted to be David's companion in place of Joab," whereas the Masoretic Text has a single soldier rallying troops to follow Joab.
- **20:19** — Vulgate "answers truth in Israel" (respondeo veritatem) differs from the Masoretic Text reading "peaceable and faithful in Israel."
- **20:24** — Vulgate super tributa ("over the tribute") translates the Hebrew term for "forced labor," and a commentariis ("recorder") uses a Roman administrative title for a keeper of official records.
