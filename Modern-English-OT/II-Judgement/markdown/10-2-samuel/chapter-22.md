## Text

**22:1** Now David spoke to the Lord the words of this song on the day when the Lord delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul.

**22:2** And he said, "The Lord is my rock, and my might, and my savior.

**22:3** My strong God, I will hope in Him; my shield and the horn of my salvation, the One who lifts me up and my refuge, my savior: You will deliver me from iniquity.

**22:4** I will call upon the Lord, who is praiseworthy, and I will be saved from my enemies.

**22:5** For the crushings of death surrounded me; the torrents of Belial terrified me.

**22:6** The cords of the underworld surrounded me; the snares of death confronted me.

**22:7** In my tribulation I will call upon the Lord, and to my God I will cry out; and from His temple He will hear my voice, and my cry will come to His ears.

**22:8** The earth heaved and trembled; the foundations of the mountains were shaken and convulsed, because He was angry with them.

**22:9** Smoke rose from His nostrils, and fire from His mouth will devour; coals were kindled by Him.

**22:10** He bent the heavens and descended, and thick darkness was beneath His feet.

**22:11** And He ascended upon the cherubim and flew, and glided upon the wings of the wind.

**22:12** He set darkness around Himself as a hiding place, sifting waters from the clouds of the heavens.

**22:13** From the brilliance in His presence, coals of fire were kindled.

**22:14** The Lord will thunder from heaven, and the Most High will give forth His voice.

**22:15** He sent arrows and dispersed them; lightning, and He consumed them.

**22:16** And the outpourings of the sea appeared, and the foundations of the world were uncovered at the rebuke of the Lord, at the blast of the breath of His fury.

**22:17** He sent from on high and took me up, and drew me out from many waters.

**22:18** He delivered me from my most powerful enemy, and from those who hated me, because they were stronger than I.

**22:19** He confronted me on the day of my affliction, and the Lord became my support.

**22:20** And He brought me out into a broad place; He delivered me, because I was pleasing to Him.

**22:21** The Lord will reward me according to my righteousness, and according to the cleanness of my hands He will repay me.

**22:22** For I have kept the ways of the Lord, and I have not acted impiously away from my God.

**22:23** For all His judgments are before me, and I have not put His precepts away from me.

**22:24** And I will be perfect with Him, and I will guard myself from my iniquity.

**22:25** And the Lord will repay me according to my righteousness, and according to the cleanness of my hands in the sight of His eyes.

**22:26** With the holy one You will be holy, and with the strong one You will be perfect.

**22:27** With the chosen one You will be chosen, and with the perverse one You will be perverted.

**22:28** And You will save the poor people, and with Your eyes You will humble the lofty.

**22:29** For You are my lamp, Lord, and You, Lord, will illuminate my darkness.

**22:30** For in You I will run girded; in my God I will spring over a wall.

**22:31** God: His way is spotless; the utterance of the Lord is tested by fire; He is a shield to all who hope in Him.

**22:32** Who is God besides the Lord, and who is strong besides our God?

**22:33** God is the One who girded me with strength and leveled my perfect way,

**22:34** making my feet equal to those of stags and setting me upon my high places;

**22:35** teaching my hands for battle and fashioning my arms like a bronze bow.

**22:36** You gave me the shield of Your salvation, and Your gentleness multiplied me.

**22:37** You will widen my steps beneath me, and my ankles will not falter.

**22:38** I will pursue my enemies and crush them, and I will not turn back until I consume them.

**22:39** I will consume them and shatter them, so that they will not rise up; they will fall beneath my feet.

**22:40** You girded me with strength for battle; You bowed down beneath me those who resist me.

**22:41** You made my enemies turn their backs to me, those who hate me, and I will destroy them.

**22:42** They will cry out, and there will be no one to save; to the Lord, and He will not hear them.

**22:43** I will wipe them out like the dust of the earth; like the mud of the streets I will pulverize them and shatter them.

**22:44** You will save me from the disputes of my people; You will preserve me as head of the nations; a people whom I do not know will serve me.

**22:45** The sons of foreigners will resist me; upon the hearing of the ear they will obey me.

**22:46** The sons of foreigners have faded away, and they will be constricted in their narrow confines.

**22:47** The Lord lives, and blessed is my God, and the strong God of my salvation will be exalted.

**22:48** God, You who give acts of vengeance to me and cast down peoples beneath me,

**22:49** You who bring me out from my enemies and lift me up from those who resist me, You will deliver me from the unjust man.

**22:50** Therefore I will give praise to You, Lord, among the nations, and I will sing to Your name.

**22:51** He magnifies the deliverances of His king and shows mercy to His anointed, David, and to his offspring forever."

## Translator Notes

- **22:3** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew "rock" (tsur) as fortis ("strong") here and in vv. 32 and 47, and renders the Hebrew "stronghold/high refuge" literally with the agent noun elevator ("the One who lifts me up").
- **22:12** — The Vulgate reads cribrans aquas ("sifting waters"), a distinctive rendering of the difficult Hebrew phrase usually understood as "a gathering of waters."
- **22:19** — The Vulgate has the singular verb praevenit ("he confronted me"), referring back to the singular "most powerful enemy" of v. 18 (though some commentators refer it to the Lord coming beforehand to meet David), whereas the Hebrew has the plural "they confronted me."
- **22:30** — The Vulgate reads curram accinctus ("I will run girded"), differing from the Hebrew "I can run against a troop."
- **22:45** — The Vulgate reads resistent mihi ("will resist me"), whereas the Hebrew verb expresses cringing or feigned submission.
