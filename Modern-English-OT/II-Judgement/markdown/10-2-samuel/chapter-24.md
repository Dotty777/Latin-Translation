## Text

**24:1** And the fury of the Lord raged again against Israel, and stirred up David against them to say, "Go, number Israel and Judah."

**24:2** And the king said to Joab, the commander of his army, "Go through all the tribes of Israel from Dan to Beersheba, and number the people, so that I may know their number."

**24:3** And Joab said to the king, "May the Lord your God add to your people, as many as they are now, and multiply them a hundredfold again in the sight of my lord the king; but what does my lord the king want for himself in a matter of this kind?"

**24:4** However, the word of the king prevailed over the words of Joab and of the commanders of the army; and Joab and the commander of the soldiers went out from the presence of the king to number the people of Israel.

**24:5** And when they had crossed over the Jordan, they came to Aroer, to the right of the city that is in the valley of Gad,

**24:6** and through Jazer they passed into Gilead and into the lower land of Hodshi, and they came to the woodlands of Dan. And going around near Sidon,

**24:7** they passed near the walls of Tyre and all the land of the Hivite and the Canaanite, and they came to the south of Judah, to Beersheba;

**24:8** and when the entire land had been surveyed, they arrived in Jerusalem after nine months and twenty days.

**24:9** Therefore Joab gave the number of the enrollment of the people to the king; and there were found of Israel eight hundred thousand valiant men who drew the sword, and of Judah five hundred thousand fighting men.

**24:10** But the heart of David struck him after the people were numbered; and David said to the Lord, "I have sinned greatly in this deed; but I pray, Lord, that You take away the iniquity of Your servant, because I have acted very foolishly."

**24:11** And so David rose in the morning, and the word of the Lord came to Gad the prophet and seer of David, saying,

**24:12** "Go, and speak to David: 'This is what the Lord says: The choice of three things is given to you; choose one of these that you wish, so that I may do it to you.'"

**24:13** And when Gad had come to David, he reported to him, saying, "Either famine will come upon you in your land for seven years, or for three months you will flee from your adversaries and they will pursue you, or else for three days there will be pestilence in your land. Now therefore consider, and see what word I should answer to Him who sent me."

**24:14** And David said to Gad, "I am in great distress; yet it is better that I fall into the hands of the Lord (for His mercies are many) than into the hands of men."

**24:15** And the Lord sent a pestilence upon Israel from the morning until the appointed time; and seventy thousand men of the people died, from Dan to Beersheba.

**24:16** And when the angel of the Lord had stretched out his hand over Jerusalem to destroy it, the Lord had compassion over the affliction and said to the angel who was striking the people, "It is enough; now hold back your hand." Now the angel of the Lord was beside the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.

**24:17** And David said to the Lord when he had seen the angel striking down the people, "It is I who have sinned; I have acted wickedly; these who are sheep, what have they done? Let Your hand, I plead, be turned against me and against the house of my father."

**24:18** And Gad came to David on that day and said to him, "Go up and set up an altar to the Lord on the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite."

**24:19** And David went up according to the word of Gad, which the Lord had commanded him.

**24:20** And Araunah looked out and noticed the king and his servants coming across to him;

**24:21** and going out, he paid homage to the king with his face bowed to the ground, and said, "What is the reason that my lord the king has come to his servant?" David said to him, "To buy the threshing floor from you and build an altar to the Lord, so that the slaughter that is raging among the people may cease."

**24:22** And Araunah said to David, "Let my lord the king take and offer up as it pleases him; you have oxen for a burnt offering, and the cart, and the yokes of the oxen for use as firewood."

**24:23** Araunah the king gave all things to the king; and Araunah said to the king, "May the Lord your God accept your vow."

**24:24** Answering him, the king said, "By no means as you wish, but I will buy it from you for a price, and I will not offer to the Lord my God burnt offerings that cost nothing." So David bought the threshing floor and the oxen for fifty shekels of silver.

**24:25** And David built an altar there to the Lord and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings; and the Lord became merciful to the land, and the plague was restrained from Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **24:1** — The Vulgate uses the accusative participle dicentem agreeing with David ("stirred up David ... to say"), making David rather than the Lord the immediate speaker of the command to number Israel and Judah.
- **24:4** — After referring to plural "commanders of the army" (principum exercitus) in the first clause, the Clementine Vulgate reads singular princeps militum ("the commander of the soldiers") in the second clause.
- **24:6** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew place names Tahtim-hodshi and Dan-jaan descriptively as "the lower land of Hodshi" (terram inferiorem Hodsi) and "the woodlands of Dan" (Dan silvestria).
- **24:23** — The Vulgate reads nominative Areuna rex regi ("Araunah the king gave ... to the king"), matching the Hebrew consonantal text where "the king" stands in apposition to Araunah rather than as a vocative, and reads "accept your vow" (suscipiat votum tuum) where the Hebrew has "accept you."
