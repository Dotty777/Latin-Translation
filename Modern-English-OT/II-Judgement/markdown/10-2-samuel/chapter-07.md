## Text

**7:1** Now it happened, when the king had settled in his house and the Lord had given him rest on every side from all his enemies,

**7:2** that he said to Nathan the prophet, "Do you see that I live in a cedar house, and the ark of God is placed in the midst of skins?"

**7:3** And Nathan said to the king, "Everything that is in your heart, go and do it, because the Lord is with you."

**7:4** Now it happened in that night, and look, the word of the Lord came to Nathan, saying,

**7:5** "Go and speak to My servant David: 'This is what the Lord says: Will you build a house for Me to live in?

**7:6** For I have not lived in a house from that day on which I led the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt up to this day, but I was walking about in a tabernacle and in a tent.

**7:7** Throughout all the places I passed through with all the sons of Israel, did I ever truly speak to any one of the tribes of Israel, whom I commanded to shepherd My people Israel, saying, "Why have you not built a cedar house for Me?"'

**7:8** And now you will say these things to My servant David: 'This is what the Lord of hosts says: I took you from the pastures, following the flocks, so that you would be a leader over My people Israel,

**7:9** and I have been with you in all things wherever you have walked, and I have killed all your enemies from before your face; and I have made a great name for you, like the name of the great ones who are on the earth.

**7:10** And I will appoint a place for My people Israel, and I will plant them, and they will live beneath it, and they will no longer be disturbed; nor will the sons of iniquity continue to afflict them as before,

**7:11** from the day on which I appointed judges over My people Israel. And I will give you rest from all your enemies; and the Lord foretells to you that the Lord will make a house for you.

**7:12** And when your days have been completed and you sleep with your fathers, I will raise up your offspring after you, who will come forth from your own body, and I will confirm his kingdom.

**7:13** He himself will build a house for My name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever.

**7:14** I will be a father to him, and he himself will be a son to Me. And if he acts iniquitously in anything, I will reprove him with the rod of men and with the blows of the sons of men.

**7:15** Yet I will not take away My mercy from him, as I took it away from Saul, whom I removed from before My face.

**7:16** And your house will be faithful, and your kingdom forever before your face, and your throne will be firm continually.'"

**7:17** According to all these words, and according to this entire vision, so Nathan spoke to David.

**7:18** Then King David went in and sat before the Lord, and said, "Who am I, Lord God, and what is my house, that You have brought me this far?

**7:19** Yet even this seemed too little in Your sight, Lord God, unless You spoke also about the house of Your servant for the distant future; for this is the law of Adam, Lord God.

**7:20** What, then, will David still be able to add to speak to You? For You know Your servant, Lord God.

**7:21** Because of Your word, and according to Your heart, You have done all these great things, so as to make it known to Your servant.

**7:22** Therefore You are magnified, Lord God, because there is no one like You, nor is there any god besides You, in everything that we have heard with our ears.

**7:23** And what nation on earth is like Your people Israel, for whose sake God went to redeem it for Himself as a people, and to set a name for Himself, and to do for them great and terrifying things upon the earth from before the face of Your people, whom You redeemed for Yourself out of Egypt, a nation and its god?

**7:24** For You have confirmed for Yourself Your people Israel as an everlasting people, and You, Lord God, have become God to them.

**7:25** Now therefore, Lord God, uphold forever the word that You have spoken concerning Your servant and concerning his house, and do as You have spoken,

**7:26** so that Your name may be magnified forever, and it may be said, 'The Lord of hosts is God over Israel.' And the house of Your servant David will be established before the Lord,

**7:27** because You, Lord of hosts, God of Israel, have uncovered the ear of Your servant, saying, 'I will build a house for you.' Therefore Your servant has found his heart to pray this prayer to You.

**7:28** Now therefore, Lord God, You are God, and Your words will be true; for You have spoken these good things to Your servant.

**7:29** Begin, therefore, and bless the house of Your servant, so that it may be forever before You; for You, Lord God, have spoken, and with Your blessing the house of Your servant will be blessed forever."

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — Vulgate "in medio pellium" ("in the midst of skins") specifies animal skins rather than the Masoretic Text's "curtains."
- **7:10** — Vulgate "sub eo" ("beneath it") literally renders the Hebrew idiom meaning "in its own place."
- **7:19** — Vulgate "lex Adam" ("the law of Adam") retains Hebrew "ha-adam" ("mankind" or "humanity") as the proper name Adam.
- **7:23** — Vulgate "gentem, et deum ejus" ("a nation and its god") uses singular nouns in an elliptical clause without an explicit verb such as "driving out."
- **7:26** — Vulgate "coram Domino" ("before the Lord") reads in the third person where the Masoretic Text reads "before You"; similarly in 7:29 Vulgate "Incipe" ("Begin") renders the Hebrew verb often translated "be pleased."
