## Text

**18:1** In those days there was no king in Israel, and the tribe of Dan was seeking a possession for itself so that it might live in it; for up to that day it had not received an allotment among the other tribes.

**18:2** Therefore the sons of Dan sent five very valiant men of their lineage and family from Zorah and Eshtaol to scout out the land and inspect it carefully, and they said to them, "Go and examine the land." When they went on and came to Mount Ephraim, and entered the house of Micah, they rested there.

**18:3** And recognizing the voice of the young Levite, and making use of his lodging, they said to him, "Who brings you here? What are you doing here? For what reason did you want to come here?"

**18:4** He answered them, "Micah has done such and such things for me, and he has hired me for wages so that I may be a priest to him."

**18:5** Then they asked him to consult the Lord, so that they might know whether they were proceeding on a prosperous journey and the matter would have success.

**18:6** He answered them, "Go in peace; the Lord looks upon your way, and the journey on which you are going."

**18:7** So the five men went and came to Laish, and they saw the people living in it without any fear, according to the custom of the Sidonians, secure and quiet, with no one resisting them at all, and of great wealth, and far from Sidon and separated from all men.

**18:8** And returning to their brothers in Zorah and Eshtaol, they answered those who asked what they had done,

**18:9** "Rise up, let us go up against them; for we have seen a very rich and fertile land. Do not be negligent, do not delay; let us go and possess it; there will be no toil.

**18:10** We will enter among a secure people, into a very broad region, and the Lord will deliver to us a place in which there is no lack of any of the things that are produced on the earth."

**18:11** So six hundred men set out from the clan of Dan, that is, from Zorah and Eshtaol, girded with battle weapons.

**18:12** And going up, they stayed in Kiriath-jearim of Judah; from that time that place received the name Camp of Dan, and it is behind Kiriath-jearim.

**18:13** From there they passed into Mount Ephraim. And when they came to the house of Micah,

**18:14** the five men who had previously been sent to examine the land of Laish said to the rest of their brothers, "Do you know that in these houses there is an ephod, and teraphim, and a carved image, and a cast image? See what pleases you."

**18:15** And when they had turned aside a little, they entered the house of the young Levite, who was in the house of Micah, and they greeted him with peaceful words.

**18:16** Meanwhile, the six hundred men, armed just as they were, stood before the door.

**18:17** But those who had entered the house of the young man were trying to take the carved image, and the ephod, and the teraphim, and the cast image, and the priest was standing before the door, with the six hundred very valiant men waiting not far off.

**18:18** Therefore those who had gone in took the carved image, the ephod, and the idols, and the cast image. The priest said to them, "What are you doing?"

**18:19** They answered him, "Be silent and put your finger over your mouth, and come with us so that we may have you as a father and a priest. What is better for you: to be a priest in the house of one man, or in one tribe and family in Israel?"

**18:20** When he heard this, he consented to their words, and he took the ephod, and the idols, and the carved image, and set out with them.

**18:21** As they went on, and had made the little ones and the livestock and everything that was valuable go ahead of them,

**18:22** and were already far from the house of Micah, the men who lived in the houses of Micah shouted out together and pursued them,

**18:23** and began to shout from behind. When they looked back, they said to Micah, "What do you want? Why are you shouting?"

**18:24** He answered, "You have taken my gods, which I made for myself, and the priest, and everything that I have, and you say, 'What is the matter with you?'"

**18:25** And the sons of Dan said to him, "Watch out that you do not speak to us any further, or men provoked in spirit will come upon you, and you yourself with your entire household will perish."

**18:26** And so they went on with the journey they had begun. And Micah, seeing that they were stronger than he was, returned to his own house.

**18:27** Now the six hundred men took the priest and the things we mentioned above, and they came to Laish, to a quiet and secure people, and struck them with the edge of the sword, and gave the city over to fire,

**18:28** with no one at all bringing aid, because they lived far from Sidon and had no fellowship or dealings of any kind with any man. Now the city was situated in the region of Rehob; and rebuilding it, they lived in it,

**18:29** calling the name of the city Dan, after the name of their father whom Israel had fathered; previously it was called Laish.

**18:30** And they set up the carved image for themselves, and appointed Jonathan son of Gershom, son of Moses, and his sons as priests in the tribe of Dan until the day of their captivity.

**18:31** And the idol of Micah remained with them all the time that the house of God was in Shiloh. In those days there was no king in Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **18:7** — Vulgate "of great wealth" (magnarumque opum) reflects an interpretation of a difficult Hebrew phrase for a magistrate or ruler as referring to abundant treasure.
- **18:12** — Vulgate translates the Hebrew place name Mahaneh-dan directly into Latin as Castrorum Dan ("Camp of Dan").
- **18:27** — Vulgate summarizes the objects taken from Micah as "the things we mentioned above" (quae supra diximus), where the Hebrew reads "what Micah had made."
- **18:30** — Vulgate reads "son of Moses" (filii Moysi) and "their captivity" (captivitatis suae), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "Manasseh" (with a suspended letter nun) and "the captivity of the land."
- **18:31** — The Clementine Vulgate places "In those days there was no king in Israel" at the end of 18:31 rather than at the opening of 19:1 as in the Hebrew text.
