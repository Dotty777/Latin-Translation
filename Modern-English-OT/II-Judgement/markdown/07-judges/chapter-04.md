## Text

**4:1** And the sons of Israel again did evil in the sight of the Lord after the death of Ehud,

**4:2** and the Lord handed them over into the hands of Jabin, king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor; and he had a commander of his army named Sisera, and he himself lived in Harosheth of the nations.

**4:3** And the sons of Israel cried out to the Lord, for he had nine hundred scythed chariots, and for twenty years he had severely oppressed them.

**4:4** Now there was Deborah, a prophetess, the wife of Lappidoth, who judged the people at that time.

**4:5** And she sat under the palm tree that was called by her name, between Ramah and Bethel in Mount Ephraim; and the sons of Israel went up to her for every judgment.

**4:6** And she sent and summoned Barak the son of Abinoam from Kedesh-naphtali, and she said to him, "The Lord God of Israel has commanded you: 'Go and lead an army to Mount Tabor, and you will take with you ten thousand fighters from the sons of Naphtali and from the sons of Zebulun;

**4:7** and I will bring to you, at the place of the torrent Kishon, Sisera, the chief of Jabin's army, and his chariots and all the multitude, and I will hand them over into your hand.'"

**4:8** And Barak said to her, "If you come with me, I will go; if you are unwilling to come with me, I will not go on."

**4:9** And she said to him, "I will indeed go with you, but on this occasion the victory will not be credited to you, because Sisera will be handed over into the hand of a woman." So Deborah rose and went on with Barak to Kedesh.

**4:10** And he, having summoned Zebulun and Naphtali, went up with ten thousand fighters, having Deborah in his company.

**4:11** Now Heber the Kenite had formerly withdrawn from the rest of the Kenites, his brothers, the sons of Hobab, the relative of Moses, and had pitched his tents as far as the valley that is called Sennim, and it was near Kedesh.

**4:12** And it was reported to Sisera that Barak the son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor;

**4:13** and he gathered nine hundred scythed chariots and all the army from Harosheth of the nations to the torrent Kishon.

**4:14** And Deborah said to Barak, "Rise, for this is the day on which the Lord has handed over Sisera into your hands; look, He Himself is your leader." So Barak went down from Mount Tabor, and ten thousand fighters with him.

**4:15** And the Lord terrified Sisera, and all his chariots, and the entire multitude by the edge of the sword at the sight of Barak, to such an extent that Sisera, leaping down from his chariot, fled on foot,

**4:16** and Barak pursued the fleeing chariots and the army as far as Harosheth of the nations, and the entire multitude of the enemies fell to utter destruction.

**4:17** Now Sisera, fleeing, reached the tent of Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite. For there was peace between Jabin, king of Hazor, and the house of Heber the Kenite.

**4:18** Therefore Jael went out to meet Sisera and said to him, "Come in to me, my lord; come in, do not be afraid." And having entered her tent and been covered by her with a cloak,

**4:19** he said to her, "Give me, I beg you, a little water, because I am very thirsty." And she opened a skin of milk and gave him a drink, and she covered him.

**4:20** And Sisera said to her, "Stand before the entrance of the tent; and when someone comes questioning you and saying, 'Is anyone here?' you will answer, 'There is no one.'"

**4:21** Therefore Jael, the wife of Heber, took a tent peg, taking along a hammer as well; and having gone in secretly and in silence, she placed the peg upon the temple of his head, and striking it with the hammer, she drove it into his brain all the way to the ground; and he, uniting deep sleep with death, fainted and died.

**4:22** And look, Barak was coming, following Sisera; and Jael went out to meet him and said to him, "Come, and I will show you the man whom you are seeking." And when he had gone in to her, he saw Sisera lying dead, and the peg driven into his temple.

**4:23** Therefore God humbled Jabin, king of Canaan, on that day before the sons of Israel,

**4:24** who grew daily and with a strong hand oppressed Jabin, king of Canaan, until they destroyed him.

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — Vulgate falcatos currus ('scythed chariots,' also in 4:13) interprets the Hebrew 'chariots of iron' as chariots fitted with blades or scythes.
- **4:6** — The Vulgate presents Deborah's opening words as a direct statement ('has commanded you') rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's rhetorical question ('Has not the LORD commanded?').
- **4:11** — Vulgate vallem, quae vocatur Sennim ('the valley that is called Sennim') differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'the oak in Zaanannim.'
- **4:21** — The Vulgate explicitly adds 'into his brain' (in cerebrum) and expresses Sisera's death as 'uniting deep sleep with death' (soporem morti consocians), where the Hebrew notes that he was fast asleep and exhausted.
