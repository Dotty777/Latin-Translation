## Text

**1:1** After the death of Joshua, the sons of Israel consulted the Lord, saying, "Who will go up before us against the Canaanites and be the leader of the war?"

**1:2** And the Lord said, "Judah will go up; look, I have handed the land over into his hands."

**1:3** And Judah said to his brother Simeon, "Go up with me into my lot and fight against the Canaanites, so that I also may go with you into your lot." And Simeon went with him.

**1:4** And Judah went up, and the Lord handed the Canaanites and the Perizzites over into their hands; and they struck down ten thousand men in Bezek.

**1:5** And they found Adoni-bezek in Bezek, and fought against him, and struck down the Canaanites and the Perizzites.

**1:6** But Adoni-bezek fled; and they pursued and captured him, cutting off the extremities of his hands and feet.

**1:7** And Adoni-bezek said, "Seventy kings, with the extremities of their hands and feet cut off, used to gather scraps of food under my table; just as I have done, so God has repaid me." And they brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.

**1:8** Therefore, attacking Jerusalem, the sons of Judah captured it and struck it by the edge of the sword, handing the entire city over to fire.

**1:9** And afterward, going down, they fought against the Canaanites who lived in the hill country, and toward the south, and in the plains.

**1:10** And Judah, advancing against the Canaanites who lived in Hebron (whose name in ancient times was Kiriath-arba), struck down Sheshai, and Ahiman, and Talmai;

**1:11** and setting out from there, he went to the inhabitants of Debir, whose old name was Kiriath-sepher, that is, the city of letters.

**1:12** And Caleb said, "Whoever strikes Kiriath-sepher and lays it waste, to him I will give my daughter Achsah as a wife."

**1:13** And when Othniel the son of Kenaz, the younger brother of Caleb, had captured it, he gave him his daughter Achsah as his wife.

**1:14** As she was going along the way, her husband advised her to ask her father for a field. And when she sighed while sitting on the donkey, Caleb said to her, "What is the matter with you?"

**1:15** But she answered, "Give me a blessing, because you have given me a dry land; give me also one watered with waters." Therefore Caleb gave her the upper watered ground and the lower watered ground.

**1:16** Now the sons of the Kenite, the relative of Moses, went up from the city of palms with the sons of Judah into the wilderness of his lot, which is south of Arad, and lived with him.

**1:17** And Judah went with his brother Simeon, and together they struck down the Canaanites who lived in Zephath and killed them. And the name of the city was called Hormah, that is, anathema.

**1:18** And Judah captured Gaza with its borders, and Ashkelon, and Ekron with its boundaries.

**1:19** And the Lord was with Judah, and he possessed the hill country; nor was he able to wipe out the inhabitants of the valley, because they abounded in scythed chariots.

**1:20** And just as Moses had said, they gave Hebron to Caleb, who wiped out the three sons of Anak from it.

**1:21** However, the sons of Benjamin did not wipe out the Jebusites, the inhabitants of Jerusalem; and the Jebusites have lived with the sons of Benjamin in Jerusalem to the present day.

**1:22** The house of Joseph also went up to Bethel, and the Lord was with them.

**1:23** For while they were besieging the city, which was formerly called Luz,

**1:24** they saw a man coming out of the city, and said to him, "Show us the entrance of the city, and we will show mercy to you."

**1:25** And when he had shown it to them, they struck the city by the edge of the sword; but they let that man and all his family go.

**1:26** And having been released, he went into the land of the Hittites, and built a city there, and called it Luz, which is so called to the present day.

**1:27** Manasseh also did not wipe out Beth-shean and Taanach with their villages, nor the inhabitants of Dor, and Ibleam, and Megiddo with their villages; and the Canaanites began to live with them.

**1:28** And after Israel grew strong, he made them tributaries, and was unwilling to wipe them out.

**1:29** Ephraim also did not kill the Canaanites who lived in Gezer, but lived with them.

**1:30** Zebulun did not wipe out the inhabitants of Kitron and Nahalol; instead, the Canaanites lived in his midst and became tributary to him.

**1:31** Asher also did not wipe out the inhabitants of Acco and of Sidon, Ahlab, and Achzib, and Helbah, and Aphek, and Rehob;

**1:32** and he lived in the midst of the Canaanites, the inhabitants of that land, nor did he kill them.

**1:33** Naphtali also did not wipe out the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath; and he lived among the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land, and the Beth-shemites and the Beth-anathites became tributary to him.

**1:34** And the Amorites confined the sons of Dan to the hill country, and did not give them room to come down to the flatter areas;

**1:35** and they lived on Mount Heres, which is translated as earthen, in Aijalon and Shaalbim. And the hand of the house of Joseph grew heavy, and they became tributary to it.

**1:36** Now the boundary of the Amorites was from the ascent of the Scorpion, the rock, and the higher places.

## Translator Notes

- **1:6** — Latin summitatibus manuum ejus ac pedum literally reads "the extremities of his hands and feet" (likewise in 1:7), whereas the Hebrew specifies the thumbs and big toes.
- **1:14** — Latin suspirasset sedens in asino ("sighed while sitting on the donkey") reflects Jerome's understanding of the rare Hebrew verb tsanach (apparently "got down" from the donkey); similarly, in 1:11, 1:17, and 1:35 the Vulgate adds etymological glosses (civitas litterarum, anathema, and testaceo, reading Heres as "earthen" rather than "sun").
- **1:19** — Latin falcatis curribus ("scythed chariots") renders Hebrew "chariots of iron"; throughout 1:19-33 (and 2:3, 21), the Vulgate also uses delere ("wipe out/destroy," and interficere, "kill," in 1:29, 32) where the Hebrew has horish ("drive out/dispossess").
- **1:23** — Latin obsiderent ("were besieging") differs from the Hebrew, which says the house of Joseph sent spies to scout out Bethel.
- **1:36** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew place names Akrabbim ("Scorpions") and Sela ("the rock") directly into Latin as ascensu Scorpionis ("ascent of the Scorpion," singular) and petra ("the rock").
