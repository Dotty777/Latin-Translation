## Text

**6:1** Now the sons of Israel did evil in the sight of the Lord, who handed them over into the hand of Midian for seven years,

**6:2** and they were greatly oppressed by them. And they made for themselves dens and caves in the mountains, and very strongly fortified places for resisting.

**6:3** And whenever Israel had sown, Midian and Amalek and the rest of the eastern nations would come up,

**6:4** and pitching their tents among them, they would lay waste everything just as it was in the green shoots, as far as the entrance of Gaza; and they would leave nothing at all pertaining to life in Israel, neither sheep, nor oxen, nor donkeys.

**6:5** For they themselves and all their flocks would come with their tents, and like locusts they would fill everything, a countless multitude of men and camels, devastating whatever they had touched.

**6:6** And Israel was greatly humbled in the sight of Midian.

**6:7** And they cried out to the Lord, asking for help against the Midianites.

**6:8** And He sent to them a man, a prophet, and he spoke: "This is what the Lord, the God of Israel, says: I caused you to go up from Egypt, and I led you out of the house of slavery,

**6:9** and I delivered you from the hand of the Egyptians and of all the enemies who were afflicting you; and I drove them out at your entrance and gave their land to you.

**6:10** And I said, 'I am the Lord your God; do not fear the gods of the Amorites, in whose land you dwell.' And you were unwilling to listen to My voice."

**6:11** Now the angel of the Lord came and sat under an oak that was in Ophrah and belonged to Joash, the father of the family of Ezri. And while his son Gideon was beating out and cleaning grain in a winepress in order to flee from Midian,

**6:12** the angel of the Lord appeared to him and said, "The Lord is with you, mightiest of men."

**6:13** And Gideon said to him, "Please, my lord, if the Lord is with us, why have all these things overtaken us? Where are His wonders, which our fathers recounted and said, 'The Lord led us out of Egypt'? But now the Lord has abandoned us and handed us over into the hand of Midian."

**6:14** And the Lord looked at him and said, "Go in this strength of yours, and you will deliver Israel from the hand of Midian; know that I have sent you."

**6:15** He answered and said, "Please, my lord, with what will I deliver Israel? Look, my family is the lowest in Manasseh, and I am the least in my father's house."

**6:16** And the Lord said to him, "I will be with you, and you will strike Midian as one man."

**6:17** And he said, "If I have found favor before You, give me a sign that it is You who are speaking to me,

**6:18** and do not depart from here until I return to You, bringing a sacrifice and offering it to You." And He answered, "I will wait for your coming."

**6:19** So Gideon went inside and cooked a young goat, and unleavened loaves from a measure of flour; and putting the meat in a basket and pouring the broth of the meat into a pot, he carried it all under the oak and offered it to Him.

**6:20** And the angel of the Lord said to him, "Take the meat and the unleavened loaves, and place them upon that rock, and pour the broth over them." And when he had done so,

**6:21** the angel of the Lord stretched out the tip of the staff that he held in his hand and touched the meat and the unleavened loaves; and fire went up from the rock and consumed the meat and the unleavened loaves; and the angel of the Lord vanished from his eyes.

**6:22** And Gideon, seeing that it was the angel of the Lord, said, "Alas, my Lord God! For I have seen the angel of the Lord face to face."

**6:23** And the Lord said to him, "Peace be with you; do not fear, you will not die."

**6:24** Therefore Gideon built an altar there to the Lord and called it "The Lord's Peace," until the present day. And while he was still in Ophrah, which belongs to the family of Ezri,

**6:25** on that night the Lord said to him, "Take your father's bull and a second bull, seven years old, and you will destroy the altar of Baal that belongs to your father, and cut down the grove that is around the altar.

**6:26** And you will build an altar to the Lord your God on the top of this rock, upon which you placed the sacrifice before; and you will take the second bull and offer a burnt offering upon the pile of wood that you have cut down from the grove."

**6:27** Therefore Gideon, having taken ten men from his servants, did just as the Lord had commanded him. However, fearing his father's house and the men of that city, he was unwilling to do it by day, but completed everything by night.

**6:28** And when the men of his town rose in the morning, they saw the altar of Baal destroyed, and the sacred grove cut down, and the second bull placed upon the altar that had then been built.

**6:29** And they said to one another, "Who did this?" And when they inquired about the author of the deed, it was said, "Gideon the son of Joash did all these things."

**6:30** And they said to Joash, "Bring your son out here, so that he may die, because he destroyed the altar of Baal and cut down the grove."

**6:31** And he answered them, "Are you the avengers of Baal, that you should fight for him? Whoever is his adversary, let him die before tomorrow's light comes. If he is a god, let him avenge himself on the one who undermined his altar."

**6:32** From that day Gideon was called Jerubbaal, because Joash had said, "Let Baal avenge himself on the one who undermined his altar."

**6:33** Therefore all Midian and Amalek and the eastern peoples were gathered together, and crossing the Jordan, they encamped in the valley of Jezreel.

**6:34** Now the Spirit of the Lord clothed Gideon, and sounding the trumpet, he summoned the house of Abiezer to follow him.

**6:35** And he sent messengers throughout all Manasseh, which itself also followed him, and other messengers into Asher and Zebulun and Naphtali, and they came to meet him.

**6:36** And Gideon said to God, "If You save Israel by my hand, just as You have spoken,

**6:37** I will place this fleece of wool on the threshing floor; if dew is on the fleece alone and dryness on all the ground, I will know that by my hand, just as You have spoken, You will deliver Israel."

**6:38** And it happened so. And rising in the night, having wrung out the fleece, he filled a bowl with dew.

**6:39** And he said again to God, "Do not let Your anger be aroused against me if I test one more time, seeking a sign in the fleece. I pray that the fleece alone may be dry, and all the ground wet with dew."

**6:40** And God did on that night as he had requested; and there was dryness on the fleece alone, and dew on all the ground.

## Translator Notes

- **6:11** — The Vulgate divides the Hebrew gentilic avi ha-ezri ("the Abiezrite") literally as patrem familiae Ezri ("the father of the family of Ezri"; cf. 6:24), whereas 6:34 uses the form Abiezer. At the end of the verse, the Latin reads ut fugeret Madian ("in order to flee from Midian") rather than hiding the grain from Midian.
- **6:24** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew altar name ("the Lord is peace") into Latin as Domini pax ("The Lord's Peace"), attaches "until the present day" to the naming, and joins "while he was still in Ophrah" to the Lord's nighttime command in 6:25.
- **6:25** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew ha-asherah (the Asherah pole) as nemus ("grove," also in 6:26, 30) and lucum ("sacred grove," 6:28), while distinguishing the Lord's altar (altare, 6:24, 26, 28) from Baal's altar (ara, 6:25, 28, 30-32).
- **6:26** — A distinctively Vulgate reading ("upon which you placed the sacrifice before"), whereas the Hebrew text directs that the altar be built "in proper order."
- **6:31** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew clause "whoever contends for him" as qui adversarius est ejus ("Whoever is his adversary"), preserving the literal Latin syntax of Joash's challenge.
