## Text

**10:1** After Abimelech, there arose a leader in Israel, Tola the son of Puah, the uncle of Abimelech, a man of Issachar, who lived in Shamir of Mount Ephraim;

**10:2** and he judged Israel for twenty-three years, and he died and was buried in Shamir.

**10:3** He was succeeded by Jair the Gileadite, who judged Israel for twenty-two years,

**10:4** having thirty sons who sat on thirty colts of female donkeys and were chiefs of thirty cities, which have been called Havvoth-jair after his name, that is, the towns of Jair, until the present day, in the land of Gilead.

**10:5** And Jair died and was buried in the place whose name is Kamon.

**10:6** Now the sons of Israel, adding new sins to the old, did evil in the sight of the Lord and served idols, the Baals and Ashtaroth, and the gods of Syria, and of Sidon, and of Moab, and of the sons of Ammon, and of the Philistines; and they abandoned the Lord and did not worship Him.

**10:7** Angry against them, the Lord gave them over into the hands of the Philistines and of the sons of Ammon.

**10:8** And they were afflicted and severely oppressed for eighteen years, all who lived across the Jordan in the land of the Amorite, who is in Gilead;

**10:9** to such an extent that the sons of Ammon, having crossed the Jordan, devastated Judah and Benjamin and Ephraim; and Israel was exceedingly afflicted.

**10:10** And crying out to the Lord, they said, "We have sinned against You, because we have abandoned the Lord our God and served the Baals."

**10:11** The Lord spoke to them, "Did not the Egyptians and the Amorites, and the sons of Ammon and the Philistines,

**10:12** the Sidonians also, and Amalek, and Canaan oppress you, and you cried out to Me, and I rescued you from their hand?

**10:13** And yet you have abandoned Me and worshiped foreign gods; therefore I will not continue to deliver you any longer:

**10:14** go and call upon the gods whom you have chosen; let them deliver you in the time of distress."

**10:15** And the sons of Israel said to the Lord, "We have sinned; render to us whatever pleases You, only deliver us now."

**10:16** Saying these things, they cast out all the idols of foreign gods from their borders and served the Lord God, who grieved over their miseries.

**10:17** Therefore the sons of Ammon, crying out together, pitched their tents in Gilead; and against them the sons of Israel gathered together and encamped in Mizpah.

**10:18** And the chiefs of Gilead said, each one to his neighbors, "Whoever among us first begins to fight against the sons of Ammon will be the leader of the people of Gilead."

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — The Vulgate reads 'dux in Israel' ('a leader in Israel') where the Hebrew has 'to save Israel,' and takes Hebrew 'ben-dodo' ('son of Dodo') as a common noun, rendering it 'patrui Abimelech' ('the uncle of Abimelech').
- **10:4** — The explanatory phrase 'id est, oppida Jair' ('that is, the towns of Jair') is a Vulgate gloss interpreting the Hebrew place-name Havvoth-jair.
- **10:12** — The Vulgate reads 'Chanaan' ('Canaan,' agreeing with the Septuagint) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'Maon' (the Maonites).
