## Text

**21:1** The sons of Israel also swore in Mizpah and said, "None of us will give any of his daughters as a wife to the sons of Benjamin."

**21:2** And they all came to the house of God in Shiloh, and sitting in His sight until evening, they lifted up their voice and with great wailing began to weep, saying,

**21:3** "Why, Lord God of Israel, has this evil happened among Your people, that today one tribe should be taken away from us?"

**21:4** Now on the next day, rising at daybreak, they built an altar and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings there, and said,

**21:5** "Who from all the tribes of Israel did not go up in the army of the Lord?" For they had bound themselves with a great oath when they were in Mizpah, that those who were absent should be put to death.

**21:6** And the sons of Israel, moved with remorse over their brother Benjamin, began to say, "One tribe has been taken away from Israel;

**21:7** from where will they take wives? For we have all sworn in common that we will not give them our daughters."

**21:8** Therefore they said, "Who is there from all the tribes of Israel who did not go up to the Lord in Mizpah?" And look, the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead were found not to have been in that army.

**21:9** (At that time also, when they were in Shiloh, none of them was found there.)

**21:10** Therefore they sent ten thousand very strong men and commanded them, "Go and strike the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead with the edge of the sword, both their wives and their little ones.

**21:11** And this will be what you must observe: kill every male and the women who have known men, but spare the virgins."

**21:12** And four hundred virgins were found from Jabesh-gilead who had not known a man's bed, and they brought them to the camp in Shiloh, in the land of Canaan.

**21:13** And they sent messengers to the sons of Benjamin who were at the rock of Rimmon, and commanded them to receive them in peace.

**21:14** And the sons of Benjamin came at that time, and wives from the daughters of Jabesh-gilead were given to them; but they did not find others whom they might hand over in a similar way.

**21:15** And all Israel grieved greatly and felt remorse over the slaughter of one tribe from Israel.

**21:16** And the elders said, "What shall we do for the rest, who have not received wives? All the women in Benjamin have fallen,

**21:17** and with great care and immense diligence we must see to it that one tribe is not blotted out from Israel.

**21:18** For we cannot give them our daughters, being bound by the oath and curse with which we said, 'Cursed is the one who gives any of his daughters as a wife to Benjamin.'"

**21:19** And they took counsel and said, "Look, there is an annual festival of the Lord in Shiloh, which is situated to the north of the city of Bethel, and on the eastern side of the road that leads from Bethel to Shechem, and to the south of the town of Lebonah."

**21:20** And they commanded the sons of Benjamin and said, "Go and hide in the vineyards.

**21:21** And when you see the daughters of Shiloh coming out according to custom to lead the dances, come out suddenly from the vineyards, and seize from among them each man a wife for himself, and go to the land of Benjamin.

**21:22** And when their fathers and brothers come and begin to complain and quarrel against you, we will say to them, 'Have pity on them, for they did not seize them by the right of warriors and victors; rather, when they asked to receive them, you did not give them, and the sin is on your part.'"

**21:23** And the sons of Benjamin did as they had been ordered, and according to their number they seized for themselves one wife each from those who were leading the dances; and they went away into their possession, building the cities and living in them.

**21:24** The sons of Israel also returned by tribes and families to their tents. In those days there was no king in Israel, but each one did what seemed right to himself.

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — As in 20:18, the Vulgate renders Hebrew Beth-el as domum Dei ("the house of God") and explicitly locates it in Silo ("in Shiloh"); compare also the mention of Shiloh in 21:9.
- **21:10** — The Vulgate reads decem millia ("ten thousand"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads twelve thousand.
- **21:22** — On a difficult Hebrew clause, the Vulgate gives a distinct sense: Benjamin did not seize the women by right of war, rather the families incurred sin by withholding them when asked.
- **21:24** — The Clementine Vulgate combines verses 24 and 25 of the Hebrew Masoretic Text into a single verse 21:24.
