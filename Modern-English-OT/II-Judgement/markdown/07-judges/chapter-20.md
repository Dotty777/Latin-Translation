## Text

**20:1** Therefore all the sons of Israel went out and were gathered together as one man, from Dan to Beersheba and the land of Gilead, to the Lord in Mizpah.

**20:2** And all the corners of the peoples, and all the tribes of Israel, came together into the assembly of the people of God, four hundred thousand fighting foot soldiers.

**20:3** (And it was not hidden from the sons of Benjamin that the sons of Israel had gone up to Mizpah.) And when the Levite, the husband of the murdered woman, was asked how so great a crime had been committed,

**20:4** he answered, "I came to Gibeah of Benjamin with my wife and turned in there,

**20:5** and look, the men of that city surrounded the house in which I was staying at night, wanting to kill me and abusing my wife with an unbelievable frenzy of lust; finally she died.

**20:6** I took hold of her, cut her into pieces, and sent the parts into all the borders of your possession, because never has so great a wickedness and so heinous a sin been done in Israel.

**20:7** You are present, all you sons of Israel; decide what you ought to do."

**20:8** And all the people stood and answered as with the speech of one man, "We will not withdraw to our tents, nor will anyone enter his own house;

**20:9** but let us do this in common against Gibeah.

**20:10** Let ten men be chosen out of a hundred from all the tribes of Israel, and a hundred out of a thousand, and a thousand out of ten thousand, so that they may bring provisions for the army, and we may be able to fight against Gibeah of Benjamin and repay it what it deserves for the crime."

**20:11** And all Israel came together to the city as one man, with the same mind and one counsel.

**20:12** And they sent messengers to the whole tribe of Benjamin to say, "Why has so great a wickedness been found among you?

**20:13** Hand over the men of Gibeah who committed this outrage, so that they may die and evil may be taken away from Israel." But they were unwilling to listen to the command of their brothers, the sons of Israel;

**20:14** instead, from all the cities that belonged to their lot, they came together to Gibeah to bring them help and to fight against the entire people of Israel.

**20:15** And twenty-five thousand from Benjamin who drew the sword were found, besides the inhabitants of Gibeah,

**20:16** who were seven hundred very valiant men, fighting with the left hand just as with the right, and hurling stones with slings so accurately that they could strike even a hair, and by no means would the strike of the stone be carried off to one side.

**20:17** Of the men of Israel also, apart from the sons of Benjamin, four hundred thousand were found who drew the sword and were prepared for battle.

**20:18** Rising up, they came to the house of God, that is, to Shiloh, and consulted God and said, "Who in our army will be the commander of the combat against the sons of Benjamin?" The Lord answered them, "Let Judah be your leader."

**20:19** And immediately the sons of Israel, rising in the morning, encamped near Gibeah;

**20:20** and advancing from there to battle against Benjamin, they began to assault the city.

**20:21** And the sons of Benjamin came out of Gibeah and killed twenty-two thousand men of the sons of Israel on that day.

**20:22** Again the sons of Israel, trusting both in their strength and in their number, drew up their battle line in the same place in which they had fought before,

**20:23** yet in such a way that first they went up and wept before the Lord until night, and consulted Him and said, "Should I advance further to fight against the sons of Benjamin, my brothers, or not?" He answered them, "Go up against them and enter into combat."

**20:24** And when on the second day the sons of Israel had advanced to battle against the sons of Benjamin,

**20:25** the sons of Benjamin burst out from the gates of Gibeah, and meeting them, raged against them with such great slaughter that they struck down eighteen thousand men who drew the sword.

**20:26** For this reason all the sons of Israel came to the house of God, and sitting down, they wept before the Lord; and they fasted on that day until evening, and offered to Him burnt offerings and peace sacrifices,

**20:27** and inquired concerning their situation. At that time the ark of the covenant of God was there,

**20:28** and Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron, was in charge of the house. Therefore they consulted the Lord and said, "Should we go out further to battle against the sons of Benjamin, our brothers, or cease?" The Lord said to them, "Go up, for tomorrow I will deliver them into your hands."

**20:29** And the sons of Israel set ambushes all around the city of Gibeah,

**20:30** and for the third time, just as the first and second times, they led out the army against Benjamin.

**20:31** And the sons of Benjamin also boldly burst out from the city and pursued their fleeing adversaries quite far, so that they wounded some of them just as on the first day and the second, and cut down those turning their backs along the two paths, one of which led to Bethel and the other to Gibeah, and struck down about thirty men;

**20:32** for they thought that they were retreating in their usual manner. But the others, artfully feigning flight, had formed a plan to draw them away from the city and, as though fleeing, lead them to the paths mentioned above.

**20:33** Therefore all the sons of Israel, rising from their positions, stretched out their battle line in the place that is called Baal-tamar. The ambushes also, which were around the city, began gradually to reveal themselves

**20:34** and to advance from the western side of the city. Moreover, another ten thousand men from all Israel were provoking the inhabitants of the city to combat. And the war grew heavy against the sons of Benjamin, and they did not realize that destruction was pressing upon them from every side.

**20:35** And the Lord struck them in the sight of the sons of Israel, and on that day they killed twenty-five thousand one hundred men of them, all warriors and men who drew the sword.

**20:36** Now when the sons of Benjamin saw that they were weaker, they began to flee. Seeing this, the sons of Israel gave them room to flee, so that they might come to the prepared ambushes that they had placed near the city.

**20:37** When these had suddenly risen from their hiding places, and Benjamin was turning its back to those cutting them down, they entered the city and struck it with the edge of the sword.

**20:38** Now the sons of Israel had given a signal to those whom they had stationed in ambush, that after they had captured the city they should kindle a fire, so that by the smoke rising high they might show that the city was captured.

**20:39** When the sons of Israel positioned in the very combat saw this (for the sons of Benjamin thought they were fleeing and were pursuing more urgently, thirty men of their army having been cut down),

**20:40** and saw something like a column of smoke rising up from the city, and when Benjamin also, looking back, saw the city captured and the flames borne upward,

**20:41** those who had previously feigned flight turned their faces and resisted more strongly. When the sons of Benjamin saw this, they turned in flight

**20:42** and began to go toward the road of the desert, with their adversaries pursuing them there as well; moreover, those who had set fire to the city also met them.

**20:43** And so it came about that they were cut down by their enemies on both sides, nor was there any respite for the dying. They fell and were struck down on the eastern side of the city of Gibeah.

**20:44** Now those who were killed in that same place were eighteen thousand men, all very strong fighters.

**20:45** When those who remained of Benjamin saw this, they fled into the wilderness and were making their way toward the rock whose name is Rimmon. In that flight also, they killed five thousand men who were straggling and heading in different directions. And when they pushed on further, they pursued them and killed another two thousand as well.

**20:46** And so it came about that all who had fallen of Benjamin in various places were twenty-five thousand fighters, most ready for wars.

**20:47** Therefore six hundred men remained out of the entire number of Benjamin who were able to escape and flee into the wilderness, and they stayed at the rock of Rimmon for four months.

**20:48** Now the sons of Israel returned and struck with the sword all the remnants of the city, from men even to livestock, and a devouring flame consumed all the cities and villages of Benjamin.

## Translator Notes

- **20:2** — Latin anguli populorum ("corners of the peoples") literally renders the Hebrew idiom pinnoth, referring to the chiefs or pillars of the tribes.
- **20:15** — The Vulgate reads viginti quinque millia ("twenty-five thousand"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads twenty-six thousand.
- **20:16** — The Vulgate describes these seven hundred men as ambidextrous (ita sinistra ut dextra praeliantes, "fighting with the left hand just as with the right"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text describes them as left-handed.
- **20:18** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew Beth-el as the appellative domum Dei ("the house of God") and adds the gloss hoc est, in Silo ("that is, to Shiloh"), a reading also reflected in 20:26 and 21:2.
- **20:36** — In the Vulgate, Benjamin begins to flee and Israel gives them room to flee toward the ambush, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text states that Israel gave ground to Benjamin because they relied on the ambush.
