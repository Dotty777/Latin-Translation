## Text

**15:1** And King Antiochus, the son of Demetrius, sent letters from the islands of the sea to Simon the priest and ruler of the nation of the Jews, and to the whole nation;

**15:2** and they contained this wording: "King Antiochus to Simon the high priest and to the nation of the Jews, greetings.

**15:3** Since indeed wicked men have taken possession of the kingdom of our fathers, I intend to reclaim the kingdom and restore it as it was before; and I have formed a chosen multitude of an army, and I have built warships.

**15:4** Now I intend to advance through the country so that I may take vengeance on those who have ruined our country and who have laid waste many cities in my kingdom.

**15:5** Now therefore I confirm to you all the offerings which all the kings before me remitted to you, and whatever other gifts they remitted to you;

**15:6** and I permit you to strike your own coinage in your country;

**15:7** and let Jerusalem be holy and free; and all the weapons that have been made, and the fortresses that you have built, which you hold, let them remain yours.

**15:8** And every debt owed to the king, and whatever will be owed to the king in the future, from this time and for all time, are remitted to you.

**15:9** And when we have gained possession of our kingdom, we will glorify you, and your nation, and the temple, with great glory, so that your glory may be made known in all the earth."

**15:10** In the one hundred and seventy-fourth year, Antiochus went out into the land of his fathers, and all the armies came together to him, so that few were left with Trypho.

**15:11** And King Antiochus pursued him, and he came to Dora, fleeing along the seacoast;

**15:12** for he knew that evils had gathered against him, and that the army had abandoned him.

**15:13** And Antiochus encamped against Dora with one hundred and twenty thousand fighting men and eight thousand horsemen;

**15:14** and he surrounded the city, and the ships approached from the sea; and they harassed the city by land and sea, and allowed no one to go in or go out.

**15:15** Now Numenius, and those who had been with him, came from the city of Rome, carrying letters written to the kings and countries, in which these things were contained:

**15:16** "Lucius, consul of the Romans, to King Ptolemy, greetings.

**15:17** Envoys of the Jews, our friends, have come to us, renewing the former friendship and alliance, sent by Simon the high priest and the people of the Jews.

**15:18** Moreover, they also brought a golden shield of one thousand minas.

**15:19** Therefore it has seemed good to us to write to the kings and countries, that they should not bring evils upon them, nor fight against them, their cities, or their countries, and that they should not give aid to those fighting against them.

**15:20** It has also seemed right to us to accept the shield from them.

**15:21** If therefore any wicked men have fled from their country to you, hand them over to Simon the high priest, so that he may punish them according to his law."

**15:22** These same things were written to King Demetrius, and to Attalus, and to Ariarathes, and to Arsaces,

**15:23** and to all the countries: to Lampsacus, and to the Spartans, and to Delos, and to Myndus, and to Sicyon, and to Caria, and to Samos, and to Pamphylia, and to Lycia, and to Halicarnassus, and to Cos, and to Side, and to Aradus, and to Rhodes, and to Phaselis, and to Gortyna, and Cnidus, and Cyprus, and Cyrene.

**15:24** And they wrote a copy of them to Simon the high priest and to the people of the Jews.

**15:25** Meanwhile King Antiochus pitched camp against Dora a second time, continually bringing forces against it and building siege engines; and he shut Trypho in so that he could not go out.

**15:26** And Simon sent to him two thousand chosen men for aid, and silver, and gold, and abundant equipment.

**15:27** And he refused to accept them, but broke everything that he had agreed to with him before, and alienated himself from him.

**15:28** And he sent to him Athenobius, one of his friends, to confer with him, saying, "You hold Joppa, and Gazara, and the citadel that is in Jerusalem, cities of my kingdom;

**15:29** you have laid waste their territories, and inflicted a great blow in the land, and exercised dominion throughout many places in my kingdom.

**15:30** Now therefore hand over the cities that you have seized, and the tributes of the places over which you have exercised dominion outside the borders of Judea;

**15:31** but if not, give for them five hundred talents of silver, and for the destruction that you have caused and for the tributes of the cities, another five hundred talents; otherwise, we will come and wage war against you."

**15:32** And Athenobius, the friend of the king, came to Jerusalem, and saw the glory of Simon, and the splendor in gold and silver, and the abundant array; and he was astounded, and reported to him the words of the king.

**15:33** And Simon answered him and said to him, "We have neither taken another's land nor do we hold what belongs to others, but the inheritance of our fathers, which was unjustly possessed by our enemies for a time.

**15:34** We, however, having the opportunity, are reclaiming the inheritance of our fathers.

**15:35** For as for Joppa and Gazara, which you demand, they themselves were inflicting a great blow among the people and in our country; for these we give one hundred talents." And Athenobius did not answer him a word.

**15:36** Returning in anger to the king, he reported to him these words, and the glory of Simon, and everything that he had seen; and the king became angry with great anger.

**15:37** Meanwhile Trypho fled by ship to Orthosia.

**15:38** And the king appointed Cendebeus commander of the seacoast, and gave him an army of foot soldiers and horsemen.

**15:39** And he commanded him to move camp facing Judea, and he commanded him to build Gedor, and to barricade the gates of the city, and to make war on the people. Meanwhile the king was pursuing Trypho.

**15:40** And Cendebeus arrived at Jamnia, and began to provoke the populace, and to trample Judea, and to take the people captive and kill them, and to build Gedor.

**15:41** And he stationed horsemen and an army there, so that going out they might traverse the road of Judea, just as the king had directed him.

## Translator Notes

- **15:3** — Vulgate reads "electam ... multitudinem exercitus" ("a chosen multitude of an army"), whereas the Greek refers to mercenary forces.
- **15:23** — Vulgate reads "Lampsaco" ("Lampsacus") where the Greek has Sampsames, and places Cos ahead of Side, Aradus, Rhodes, and Phaselis.
- **15:32** — Vulgate reads "claritatem in auro, et argento" ("the splendor in gold and silver"), where the Greek specifies a sideboard or cupboard of gold and silver vessels.
- **15:39** — Vulgate names the town "Gedorem" ("Gedor") here and in 15:40 (contrast "Cedronem" in 16:9, matching Greek Kedron in all three verses), and reads "obstruere portas civitatis" ("barricade the gates of the city") where the Greek speaks of fortifying the gates.
- **15:40** — Vulgate reads "conculcare Judaeam" ("trample Judea") where the Greek has "invade Judea," and includes "and to build Gedor" at the end of the verse.
