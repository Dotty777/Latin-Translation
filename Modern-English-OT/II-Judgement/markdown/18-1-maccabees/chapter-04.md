## Text

**4:1** And Gorgias took five thousand men and one thousand chosen horsemen, and they moved camp by night,

**4:2** so that they might draw near to the camp of the Jews and strike them suddenly; and the sons who were from the citadel were guides for them.

**4:3** And Judas heard, and he himself and the mighty men rose up to strike the force of the king's armies that were in Emmaus,

**4:4** for the army was still scattered away from the camp.

**4:5** And Gorgias came into the camp of Judas by night and found no one; and he was searching for them in the mountains, because he said, "These men are fleeing from us."

**4:6** And when day had come, Judas appeared in the plain with only three thousand men, who did not have armor and swords;

**4:7** and they saw the strong camp of the nations, and men wearing breastplates and cavalry around them, and these were trained for battle.

**4:8** And Judas said to the men who were with him, "Do not fear their multitude, and do not dread their onslaught.

**4:9** Remember how our fathers were saved in the Red Sea when Pharaoh was pursuing them with a large army.

**4:10** And now let us cry out to heaven, and the Lord will have mercy on us, and will be mindful of the covenant of our fathers, and will crush this army before our face today,

**4:11** and all the nations will know that there is One who redeems and frees Israel."

**4:12** And the foreigners lifted up their eyes and saw them coming from the opposite direction.

**4:13** And they went out from the camp into battle, and those who were with Judas blew the trumpet.

**4:14** And they clashed, and the nations were crushed and fled into the plain.

**4:15** But all those in the rear fell by the sword; and they pursued them as far as Gezer, and as far as the plains of Idumea, and of Azotus, and of Jamnia, and up to three thousand men fell from among them.

**4:16** And Judas returned, and his army was following him.

**4:17** And he said to the people, "Do not covet the plunder, because war is against us,

**4:18** and Gorgias and his army are near us on the mountain; but stand now against our enemies and fight against them, and afterward you will take the plunder securely."

**4:19** And while Judas was still speaking these things, look, a certain division appeared gazing out from the mountain.

**4:20** And Gorgias saw that his own men had been turned to flight and that they had set fire to the camp; for the smoke that was seen made clear what had been done.

**4:21** When they caught sight of these things, they were greatly afraid, seeing at the same time both Judas and the army in the plain ready for battle.

**4:22** And they all fled into the plain of the foreigners,

**4:23** and Judas returned to the plunder of the camp, and they took much gold, and silver, and blue, and sea purple, and great wealth.

**4:24** And turning back, they were singing a hymn and blessing God toward heaven, for He is good, for His mercy is forever.

**4:25** And a great deliverance came about in Israel on that day.

**4:26** Now whoever of the foreigners escaped came and reported to Lysias all the things that had happened.

**4:27** When he heard these things, dismayed in spirit, he was losing heart, because such things did not happen in Israel as he wished and as the king commanded.

**4:28** And in the following year, Lysias gathered sixty thousand chosen men and five thousand horsemen, so that he might conquer them.

**4:29** And they came into Judea and pitched camp at Beth-horon, and Judas met them with ten thousand men.

**4:30** And they saw the strong army, and he prayed and said, "Blessed are You, Savior of Israel, who crushed the onslaught of the mighty one by the hand of Your servant David, and handed over the camp of the foreigners into the hand of Jonathan the son of Saul and of his armor-bearer.

**4:31** Enclose this army in the hand of Your people Israel, and let them be put to shame in their army and horsemen.

**4:32** Give them terror, and melt away the boldness of their strength, and let them be shaken by their crushing.

**4:33** Cast them down by the sword of those who love You, and let all who know Your name praise You together in hymns."

**4:34** And they joined battle, and five thousand men fell from the army of Lysias.

**4:35** Now Lysias, seeing the flight of his own men and the boldness of the Jews, and that they were ready either to live or to die bravely, went away to Antioch and chose soldiers, so that, being multiplied, they might come again into Judea.

**4:36** Now Judas and his brothers said, "Look, our enemies are crushed; let us go up now to cleanse the holy places and renew them."

**4:37** And the whole army was gathered together, and they went up to Mount Zion.

**4:38** And they saw the sanctuary desolate, and the altar profaned, and the gates burned up, and in the courts shrubs grown up as in a forest or on the mountains, and the priests' chambers torn down.

**4:39** And they tore their garments, and lamented with a great lamentation, and put ashes on their heads,

**4:40** and fell on their faces on the ground, and sounded out with the signal trumpets, and cried out to heaven.

**4:41** Then Judas appointed men to fight against those who were in the citadel, until they cleansed the holy places.

**4:42** And he chose priests without blemish, having delight in the law of God;

**4:43** and they cleansed the holy places and carried the stones of defilement to an unclean place.

**4:44** And he deliberated concerning the altar of burnt offerings, which had been profaned, what he should do with it.

**4:45** And a good plan occurred to them, that they should tear it down, so that it might not perhaps be a reproach to them, because the nations had defiled it; and they demolished it.

**4:46** And they stored away the stones on the mountain of the house in a suitable place, until a prophet should come and answer concerning them.

**4:47** And they took whole stones according to the law, and built a new altar like the one that was before;

**4:48** and they built the holy places and the things that were within the interior of the house, and they sanctified both the shrine and the courts.

**4:49** And they made new holy vessels, and brought the lampstand, and the altar of incense, and the table into the temple.

**4:50** And they placed incense on the altar, and lit the lamps that were on the lampstand, and they shone in the temple.

**4:51** And they placed loaves on the table, and hung the curtains, and completed all the works that they had done.

**4:52** And they rose up before morning on the twenty-fifth day of the ninth month (this is the month of Chislev) of the one hundred and forty-eighth year,

**4:53** and offered sacrifice according to the law on the new altar of burnt offerings that they had made.

**4:54** At the time and on the day on which the nations had defiled it, on that very day it was renewed with songs, and harps, and lyres, and with cymbals.

**4:55** And all the people fell on their faces, and worshiped, and toward heaven blessed Him who had given them success.

**4:56** And they kept the dedication of the altar for eight days, and offered burnt offerings with gladness, and a sacrifice of salvation and of praise.

**4:57** And they adorned the front of the temple with golden crowns and small shields, and dedicated the gates and the priests' chambers, and set doors on them.

**4:58** And there was very great gladness among the people, and the reproach of the nations was turned away.

**4:59** And Judas, and his brothers, and the whole assembly of Israel decreed that the day of the dedication of the altar should be observed in its seasons from year to year for eight days from the twenty-fifth day of the month of Chislev, with gladness and joy.

**4:60** And at that time they built up Mount Zion, and high walls and strong towers all around, so that the nations might never come and trample it down as they had done before.

**4:61** And he stationed an army there to guard it, and he fortified it to watch over Beth-zur, so that the people might have a stronghold facing Idumea.

## Translator Notes

- **4:6** — The Vulgate adds "only" ("only three thousand men") and omits the Greek qualifier "as they wished," stating unconditionally that Judas's men lacked armor and swords.
- **4:10** — The Vulgate reads the confident statement "and the Lord will have mercy on us," whereas Greek manuscripts have a conditional clause ("if he favors us") without explicitly naming the Lord.
- **4:16** — The Vulgate reads "and his army was following him" (et exercitus ejus sequens eum), whereas Greek manuscripts say that Judas and his force returned "from pursuing after them."
- **4:29** — The Vulgate reads "Judea" and "Beth-horon," whereas Greek manuscripts read "Idumea" and "Beth-zur."
- **4:44** — The Vulgate uses singular verbs ("he deliberated ... what he should do"), referring to Judas, whereas Greek manuscripts use the plural ("they deliberated ... what they should do").
- **4:48** — The Vulgate reads "both the shrine and the courts" (et ædem, et atria), whereas Greek manuscripts mention sanctifying only the courts.
- **4:61** — The Vulgate reads "and he fortified it to watch over Beth-zur" (et munivit eum ad custodiendum Bethsuram), whereas Greek manuscripts say that they fortified Beth-zur itself to guard it.
