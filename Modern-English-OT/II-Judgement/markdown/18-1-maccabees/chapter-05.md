## Text

**5:1** And it happened that when the nations all around heard that the altar and the sanctuary had been built as before, they became very angry,

**5:2** and they planned to destroy the descendants of Jacob who were among them, and they began to kill some of the people and to persecute them.

**5:3** And Judas waged war against the sons of Esau in Idumea and those who were in Acrabattene, because they were besieging the Israelites; and he struck them with a great blow.

**5:4** And he remembered the wickedness of the sons of Baean, who were a snare and a stumbling block to the people, lying in ambush for them along the road.

**5:5** And they were shut up by him in the towers, and he encamped against them, and devoted them to destruction, and burned their towers with fire, together with all who were in them.

**5:6** And he crossed over to the sons of Ammon, and found a strong force and a numerous people, and Timothy their leader;

**5:7** and he engaged in many battles with them, and they were crushed in their sight, and he struck them down;

**5:8** and he captured the city of Gazer and its daughters, and returned to Judea.

**5:9** And the nations that were in Gilead gathered together against the Israelites who were within their borders to destroy them; and they fled into the fortress of Dathema.

**5:10** And they sent letters to Judas and his brothers, saying, "The nations all around have gathered together against us to do away with us,

**5:11** and they are preparing to come and seize the fortress into which we have fled, and Timothy is the leader of their army.

**5:12** Now therefore, come and rescue us from their hands, because a multitude of us have fallen.

**5:13** And all our brothers who were in the districts of Tubin have been killed; and they have led away captive their wives and children, and the spoils, and have put to death about one thousand men there."

**5:14** And the letters were still being read, and look, other messengers came from Galilee with their tunics torn, reporting according to these words,

**5:15** saying that people had assembled against them from Ptolemais, Tyre, and Sidon: "And all Galilee has been filled with foreigners to wipe us out."

**5:16** Now when Judas and the people heard these words, a great assembly came together to consider what they should do for their brothers who were in distress and were being attacked by them.

**5:17** And Judas said to Simon his brother, "Choose men for yourself, and go and deliver your brothers in Galilee; but I and my brother Jonathan will go into Gilead."

**5:18** And he left Joseph the son of Zechariah, and Azariah, leaders of the people, with the rest of the army in Judea to keep guard;

**5:19** and he commanded them, saying, "Take charge of this people, and do not engage in battle against the nations until we return."

**5:20** And three thousand men were allotted to Simon to go into Galilee, and to Judas, eight thousand for Gilead.

**5:21** And Simon went into Galilee and engaged in many battles with the nations; and the nations were crushed before him, and he pursued them as far as the gate

**5:22** of Ptolemais; and about three thousand men of the nations fell. And he took their spoils,

**5:23** and he took with him those who were in Galilee and in Arbatta, with their wives and children and all that belonged to them, and brought them into Judea with great joy.

**5:24** And Judas Maccabeus and Jonathan his brother crossed the Jordan and went a journey of three days through the desert.

**5:25** And the Nabateans met them, and received them peacefully, and recounted to them everything that had happened to their brothers in Gilead,

**5:26** and that many of them have been trapped in Barasa, Bosor, Alema, Casphor, Mageth, and Carnaim; all these are fortified and great cities.

**5:27** Moreover, in the remaining cities of Gilead as well, they are being held trapped, and for tomorrow they have arranged to move up the army against these cities, and to capture and destroy them in one day.

**5:28** And Judas and his army suddenly turned their way into the desert of Bosor, and seized the city, and killed every male by the edge of the sword, and took all their spoils, and set it ablaze with fire.

**5:29** And they rose up from there by night and went as far as the fortress.

**5:30** And it happened at dawn, when they lifted up their eyes, look: a great crowd of people, of whom there was no number, carrying ladders and siege engines to capture the fortress and attack them.

**5:31** And Judas saw that the battle had begun, and the cry of battle rose up to heaven like a trumpet, and a great cry from the city,

**5:32** and he said to his army, "Fight today for your brothers."

**5:33** And he came behind them in three divisions, and they sounded out with trumpets, and cried out in prayer.

**5:34** And the camp of Timothy recognized that it was Maccabeus, and they fled before him; and they struck them with a great blow. And about eight thousand men from among them fell on that day.

**5:35** And Judas turned aside to Mizpah, and attacked and captured it; and he killed every male in it, and took its spoils, and set it ablaze with fire.

**5:36** From there he proceeded and captured Casbon, Mageth, Bosor, and the remaining cities of Gilead.

**5:37** Now after these things Timothy gathered another army and pitched camp opposite Raphon across the stream.

**5:38** And Judas sent men to scout the army; and they reported back to him, saying, "All the nations that are around us have assembled to him, an exceedingly large army,

**5:39** and they have hired Arabs to help them, and they have pitched camp across the stream, ready to come against you in battle." And Judas went to meet them.

**5:40** And Timothy said to the commanders of his army, "When Judas and his army draw near to the stream of water, if he crosses over to us first, we will not be able to withstand him, because he will surely prevail against us;

**5:41** but if he is afraid to cross over and pitches camp beyond the river, we will cross over to them and prevail against him."

**5:42** Now when Judas drew near to the stream of water, he stationed the scribes of the people beside the stream and charged them, saying, "Leave no man behind, but let all come into battle."

**5:43** And he crossed over to them first, and all the people after him; and all the nations were crushed before them, and they threw away their weapons and fled to the temple that was in Carnaim.

**5:44** And he seized the city itself, and set the temple ablaze with fire, together with all who were in it; and Carnaim was overpowered and could not stand before the face of Judas.

**5:45** And Judas gathered together all the Israelites who were in Gilead, from the least to the greatest, and their wives and children, and a very great army, so that they might come into the land of Judah.

**5:46** And they came as far as Ephron; and this was a great city situated at the entrance, strongly fortified, and there was no way to turn aside from it to the right or to the left, but the way lay through the middle of it.

**5:47** And those who were in the city shut themselves in and blocked up the gates with stones; and Judas sent to them with peaceful words,

**5:48** saying, "Let us pass through your land so that we may go into our land; and no one will harm you; we will only pass through on foot." And they were unwilling to open to them.

**5:49** And Judas commanded it to be proclaimed in the camp that each one should encamp in the place where he was;

**5:50** and the men of valor encamped, and he assaulted that city all day and all night, and the city was delivered into his hand;

**5:51** and they put every male to death by the edge of the sword, and he razed it, and took its spoils, and passed through the whole city over those who had been killed.

**5:52** And they crossed the Jordan in the great plain, opposite Beth-shan.

**5:53** And Judas was gathering those at the rear and encouraging the people along the whole way, until they came into the land of Judah;

**5:54** and they went up to Mount Zion with gladness and joy, and offered burnt offerings, because not one of them had fallen until they returned in peace.

**5:55** And in the days when Judas and Jonathan were in the land of Gilead, and Simon his brother was in Galilee opposite Ptolemais,

**5:56** Joseph the son of Zechariah, and Azariah, commander of the force, heard of the deeds well accomplished and the battles that had taken place,

**5:57** and he said, "Let us also make a name for ourselves, and let us go to fight against the nations that are around us."

**5:58** And he commanded those who were in his army, and they went to Jamnia.

**5:59** And Gorgias and his men came out of the city to meet them in battle.

**5:60** And Joseph and Azariah were put to flight as far as the borders of Judea; and on that day about two thousand men of the people of Israel fell, and a great rout took place among the people,

**5:61** because they did not listen to Judas and his brothers, thinking that they would act bravely.

**5:62** However, they themselves were not of the offspring of those men through whom deliverance came about in Israel.

**5:63** And the men of Judah were greatly magnified in the sight of all Israel, and of all the nations where their name was heard.

**5:64** And people came together to them, shouting joyful acclamations.

**5:65** And Judas and his brothers went out and attacked the sons of Esau in the land that is to the south; and he struck Hebron and its daughters, and set its walls and towers all around ablaze with fire.

**5:66** And he moved camp to go into the land of the foreigners, and he passed through Samaria.

**5:67** On that day priests fell in war, while wishing to act bravely, while going out into battle without counsel.

**5:68** And Judas turned aside to Azotus, into the land of the foreigners, and tore down their altars, and set the carved images of their gods ablaze with fire; and he took the spoils of the cities, and returned to the land of Judah.

## Translator Notes

- **5:8** — The Vulgate reads "Gazer" (a name elsewhere associated with Gezer in Judea), whereas the Greek reads "Jazer" (a Transjordanian city in Ammonite territory). "Its daughters" is a biblical idiom for dependent surrounding villages and towns.
- **5:13** — The Vulgate reads "in the districts of Tubin" (in locis Tubin), corresponding to the territory of Tob (Greek Toubiou) east of the Jordan.
- **5:15** — The Vulgate renders this clause as a distinct passive statement, "all Galilee has been filled with foreigners" (repleta est omnis Galilaea alienigenis), whereas the Greek lists "all Galilee of the Gentiles" among the attacking regions. The Latin also shifts abruptly from indirect speech ("against them") to direct speech ("us").
- **5:26** — The Vulgate preserves the place-name forms "Barasa," "Casphor," and "Mageth," where the Greek text commonly reads "Bosora" (Bozrah), "Chaspho," and "Maked."
- **5:31** — The Vulgate reads "the cry of battle rose up to heaven like a trumpet, and a great cry from the city," whereas the Greek reads "the cry of the city went up to heaven with trumpets and a great shout."
- **5:36** — The Vulgate reads "Casbon" for the city called "Casphor" in 5:26, where the Greek reads "Chasphon" or "Chaspho."
- **5:42** — The Vulgate reads "Leave no man behind" (Neminem hominem reliqueritis), whereas the Greek reads "Do not allow any man to encamp."
- **5:45** — The Vulgate reads "and a very great army" (et exercitum magnum valde), omitting the Greek mention of their "baggage" and rendering the Greek appositional phrase ("a very great company") as a coordinated noun phrase.
- **5:63** — The Vulgate reads "the men of Judah" (viri Juda), whereas the Greek reads "the man Judas and his brothers."
- **5:66** — The Vulgate reads "Samaria" (reflecting a major Greek manuscript tradition), whereas some other Greek witnesses read "Marissa" (Mareshah), which lies directly between Hebron and Azotus.
