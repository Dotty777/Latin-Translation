## Text

**14:1** In the one hundred seventy-second year, King Demetrius gathered his army and departed into Media to assemble auxiliary forces for himself, so that he might defeat Trypho.

**14:2** And Arsaces, king of Persia and Media, heard that Demetrius had entered his borders, and he sent one of his commanders to capture him alive and bring him to himself.

**14:3** And he departed and struck the camp of Demetrius, and captured him, and led him to Arsaces, and placed him in custody.

**14:4** And the whole land of Judah was quiet all the days of Simon, and he sought good things for his nation, and his authority and his glory pleased them all the days.

**14:5** And with all his glory, he took Joppa as a harbor and made an entrance to the islands of the sea.

**14:6** And he extended the borders of his nation and gained control of the region.

**14:7** And he gathered many captives, and he ruled over Gazara, Beth-zur, and the citadel; and he removed the impurities from it, and there was no one who resisted him.

**14:8** And each one cultivated his own land in peace; and the land of Judah yielded its fruits, and the trees of the fields their fruit.

**14:9** All the elders sat in the public squares and discussed the good things of the land, and the young men clothed themselves with glory and robes of war.

**14:10** And he supplied provisions to the cities and established them to be instruments of fortification, until the name of his glory was named to the end of the earth.

**14:11** He made peace over the land, and Israel rejoiced with great joy.

**14:12** And each one sat under his own vine and under his own fig tree, and there was no one who frightened them.

**14:13** There ceased to be anyone fighting against them upon the land; kings were crushed in those days.

**14:14** And he strengthened all the humble of his people, and he sought out the law and removed every unjust and evil person.

**14:15** He glorified the sanctuary and multiplied the vessels of the sanctuary.

**14:16** And it was heard in Rome that Jonathan had died, and even as far as the Spartans, and they were deeply grieved.

**14:17** But when they heard that Simon his brother had become high priest in his place, and that he himself held control of the whole region and the cities in it,

**14:18** they wrote to him on bronze tablets to renew the friendship and the alliance which they had made with Judas and with Jonathan, his brothers.

**14:19** And they were read in the presence of the assembly in Jerusalem. And this is the copy of the letters which the Spartans sent:

**14:20** "The rulers and cities of the Spartans, to Simon the great priest, and to the elders, and to the priests, and to the rest of the people of the Jews, brothers: greetings.

**14:21** The envoys who were sent to our people reported to us about your glory, honor, and joy, and we rejoiced at their arrival.

**14:22** And we wrote down what had been spoken by them in the councils of the people, as follows: 'Numenius, son of Antiochus, and Antipater, son of Jason, envoys of the Jews, came to us, renewing the former friendship with us.

**14:23** And it pleased the people to receive the men gloriously and to place a copy of their words in the reserved books of the people, so that it may be for a memorial to the people of the Spartans.' And we have written a copy of these things to Simon the great priest."

**14:24** After these things, Simon sent Numenius to Rome, carrying a great golden shield weighing one thousand minas, to establish an alliance with them. Now when the Roman people heard

**14:25** these words, they said, "What thanksgiving shall we return to Simon and his sons?

**14:26** For he himself restored his brothers and fought off the enemies of Israel from them." And they established freedom for him, and inscribed it on bronze tablets, and placed them on pillars on Mount Zion.

**14:27** And this is the copy of the writing: "On the eighteenth day of the month of Elul, in the one hundred seventy-second year, in the third year under Simon the great priest in Asaramel,

**14:28** in a great assembly of the priests, and of the people, and of the rulers of the nation, and of the elders of the region, these things were made known: since battles frequently took place in our region,

**14:29** Simon, son of Mattathias, of the sons of Jarib, and his brothers gave themselves to danger and resisted the adversaries of their nation, so that their sanctuary and the law might stand, and with great glory they glorified their nation.

**14:30** And Jonathan gathered his nation, and became great priest for them, and was joined to his people.

**14:31** And their enemies wanted to trample and crush their region and to stretch out their hands against their sanctuary.

**14:32** Then Simon resisted and fought for his nation, and spent large sums of money, and armed the men of valor of his nation, and gave them wages;

**14:33** and he fortified the cities of Judea, and Beth-zur, which was on the borders of Judea, where the weapons of the enemies were previously, and he placed a garrison of Jewish men there.

**14:34** And he fortified Joppa, which was by the sea, and Gazara, which is on the borders of Azotus, in which enemies previously lived; and he settled Jews there, and whatever things were suitable for their correction he placed in them.

**14:35** And the people saw the conduct of Simon and the glory which he planned to do for his nation, and they appointed him their leader and chief priest, because he himself had done all these things, and for the justice and faithfulness which he preserved for his nation, and he sought in every way to exalt his people.

**14:36** And in his days it prospered in his hands, so that the Gentiles were removed from their region, and those who were in the city of David, in Jerusalem, in the citadel, from which they would come out and defile all the things that are around the sanctuary and inflict a great blow upon purity;

**14:37** and he stationed Jewish men in it for the protection of the region and of the city, and he heightened the walls of Jerusalem.

**14:38** And King Demetrius confirmed the high priesthood for him.

**14:39** In accordance with these things, he made him his friend and glorified him with great glory.

**14:40** For he heard that the Jews had been called friends, allies, and brothers by the Romans, and that they had received the envoys of Simon gloriously,

**14:41** and that the Jews and their priests had consented that he should be their leader and high priest forever, until a faithful prophet should arise;

**14:42** and that he should be leader over them, and that he should have care for the sanctuary, and that he should appoint overseers over their works, and over the region, and over the weapons, and over the garrisons;

**14:43** and that he should have care concerning the sanctuary; and that he should be obeyed by all, and that all documents in the region should be written in his name; and that he should be clothed in purple and gold;

**14:44** and that it should not be lawful for anyone of the people or of the priests to nullify any of these things, or to contradict the things spoken by him, or to convene an assembly in the region without him, or to dress in purple, or to wear a golden clasp.

**14:45** And whoever acts contrary to these things, or nullifies any of these things, will be guilty."

**14:46** And it pleased all the people to appoint Simon and to act according to these words.

**14:47** And Simon accepted, and it pleased him to exercise the high priesthood, and to be leader and ruler of the nation of the Jews and of the priests, and to preside over all.

**14:48** And they ordered this writing to be placed on bronze tablets, and for them to be placed in the enclosure of the sanctuary, in a prominent place;

**14:49** and a copy of them to be placed in the treasury, so that Simon and his sons might have it.

## Translator Notes

- **14:24** — The Clementine Vulgate explicitly reads populus Romanus ("the Roman people"), making the Romans the speakers in verses 25-26, whereas the Greek text has simply "the people," referring to the Jewish people.
- **14:26** — The Vulgate reads restituit enim ipse fratres suos ("for he himself restored his brothers") and statuerunt ei libertatem ("they established freedom for him"), whereas the Greek has Simon, his brothers, and his father's house standing firm and establishing freedom for Israel.
- **14:27** — Asaramel (Greek Saramel or Asaramel) is a transliterated term commonly understood as deriving from Hebrew hasar am El ("court of the people of God") or denoting an assembly place in Jerusalem.
- **14:29** — The Vulgate reads Jarib here for the priestly family name that appears as Joarib in the Greek text (cf. 1 Maccabees 2:1; 1 Chronicles 24:7).
- **14:34** — The Vulgate reads ad correptionem eorum ("for their correction"), a literal rendering of Greek epanorthosis, which in this context means "restoration," "upkeep," or "provision."
