## Text

**9:1** Meanwhile, when Demetrius heard that Nicanor and his army had fallen in battle, he proceeded to send Bacchides and Alcimus into Judea again, and the right wing with them.

**9:2** And they went by the road that leads to Gilgal, and pitched camp at Masaloth, which is in Arbela; and they captured it and destroyed many human lives.

**9:3** In the first month of the one hundred and fifty-second year, they brought the army up to Jerusalem.

**9:4** And they rose up and went to Berea, twenty thousand men and two thousand horsemen.

**9:5** And Judas had pitched camp at Laisa, and three thousand chosen men were with him.

**9:6** And they saw the multitude of the army, that they were many, and they were greatly afraid; and many withdrew from the camp, and none of them remained except eight hundred men.

**9:7** And Judas saw that his army had melted away and the battle was pressing hard upon him, and he was crushed in heart because he had no time to gather them, and he grew faint.

**9:8** And he said to those who remained, "Let us rise up and go against our adversaries, to see if we will be able to fight against them."

**9:9** And they tried to turn him back, saying, "We will not be able, but let us save our lives now and return to our brothers, and then we will fight against them; we, however, are few."

**9:10** And Judas said, "May it be far from us to do this thing, that we should flee from them; and if our time has drawn near, let us die in valor for the sake of our brothers, and let us not bring a reproach upon our glory."

**9:11** And the army moved out from the camp, and they stood to meet them; and the horsemen were divided into two divisions, and the slingers and archers went ahead of the army, and the foremost of the battle were all mighty men.

**9:12** Now Bacchides was on the right wing, and the legion drew near from both sides, and they sounded the trumpets.

**9:13** And those who were on the side of Judas also cried out themselves, and the earth was shaken by the sound of the armies; and the battle was fought from morning until evening.

**9:14** And Judas saw that the stronger part of the army of Bacchides was on the right, and all who were steadfast in heart came together with him.

**9:15** And the right part was crushed by them, and he pursued them as far as Mount Azotus.

**9:16** And those who were on the left wing saw that the right wing had been crushed, and they followed after Judas and those who were with him from the rear.

**9:17** And the battle grew heavy, and many fell wounded on this side and on that.

**9:18** And Judas fell, and the rest fled.

**9:19** And Jonathan and Simon took Judas their brother and buried him in the tomb of their fathers in the city of Modin.

**9:20** And all the people of Israel wept for him with great lamentation, and they mourned for many days,

**9:21** and said, "How the mighty one has fallen, who saved the people of Israel!"

**9:22** And the rest of the accounts of the wars of Judas, and of the deeds of valor that he performed, and of his greatness, have not been written down; for they were very many.

**9:23** And it happened that after the death of Judas, wicked men emerged within all the borders of Israel, and all who practiced wickedness sprang up.

**9:24** In those days there came a very great famine, and their whole region handed itself over to Bacchides along with them.

**9:25** And Bacchides chose ungodly men and appointed them masters of the region.

**9:26** And they sought out and searched thoroughly for the friends of Judas, and brought them to Bacchides, and he took vengeance on them and mocked them.

**9:27** And there came great tribulation in Israel, such as had not been since the day a prophet was no longer seen in Israel.

**9:28** And all the friends of Judas gathered together and said to Jonathan,

**9:29** "Since your brother Judas died, there is no man like him to go out against our enemies, Bacchides and those who are enemies of our nation.

**9:30** Now therefore, we have chosen you today to be in his place for us as ruler and leader, to fight our war."

**9:31** And Jonathan accepted the leadership at that time and rose up in place of Judas his brother.

**9:32** And Bacchides learned of it and sought to kill him.

**9:33** And Jonathan, and Simon his brother, and all who were with him learned of it; and they fled into the wilderness of Tekoa and camped by the water of the pool of Asphar.

**9:34** And Bacchides learned of it, and on the Sabbath day he himself and all his army came across the Jordan.

**9:35** And Jonathan sent his brother, the leader of the people, and asked the Nabateans, his friends, that they might lend them their equipment, which was abundant.

**9:36** And the sons of Jambri came out from Medeba, and seized John and all that he had, and went away with them in their possession.

**9:37** After these things, it was reported to Jonathan and to Simon his brother that the sons of Jambri were holding a great wedding and were bringing the bride from Medeba, the daughter of one of the great rulers of Canaan, with great pomp.

**9:38** And they remembered the blood of John their brother, and they went up and hid themselves under the cover of the mountain.

**9:39** And they lifted up their eyes and saw, and look, there was a commotion and much equipment; and the bridegroom came out, and his friends and his brothers, to meet them with tambourines and musical instruments and many weapons.

**9:40** And they rose up against them from ambush and killed them, and many fell wounded, and those who remained fled into the mountains; and they took all their spoils.

**9:41** And the wedding was turned into mourning, and the sound of their musical instruments into lament.

**9:42** And they took vengeance for the blood of their brother, and returned to the bank of the Jordan.

**9:43** And Bacchides heard of it, and came on the Sabbath day as far as the edge of the Jordan with a great force.

**9:44** And Jonathan said to his men, "Let us rise up and fight against our enemies, for today is not like yesterday and the day before;

**9:45** for look, the battle is in front of us, and the water of the Jordan is on this side and on that, and the banks, and the marshes, and the thickets; and there is no place to turn aside.

**9:46** Now therefore, cry out to heaven, that you may be delivered from the hand of your enemies." And the battle was joined.

**9:47** And Jonathan stretched out his hand to strike Bacchides, and he turned back away from him.

**9:48** And Jonathan and those who were with him leaped into the Jordan, and they swam across the Jordan to them.

**9:49** And one thousand men fell on the side of Bacchides on that day. And they returned to Jerusalem,

**9:50** and built fortified cities in Judea: the fortress that was in Jericho, and in Emmaus, and in Beth-horon, and in Bethel, and Timnath, and Phara, and Thopo, with high walls and gates and bars.

**9:51** And he placed a garrison in them, that they might carry on hostilities against Israel.

**9:52** And he fortified the city of Beth-zur, and Gazara, and the citadel, and placed troops in them and a supply of food.

**9:53** And he took the sons of the rulers of the region as hostages and placed them in the citadel in Jerusalem under guard.

**9:54** And in the one hundred and fifty-third year, in the second month, Alcimus commanded the walls of the inner holy house to be torn down, and the works of the prophets to be torn down; and he began to tear them down.

**9:55** At that time Alcimus was stricken, and his works were hindered, and his mouth was shut, and he was disabled by paralysis, and he could no longer speak a word or give instructions concerning his house.

**9:56** And Alcimus died at that time with great torment.

**9:57** And Bacchides saw that Alcimus had died, and he returned to the king. And the land was quiet for two years.

**9:58** And all the wicked men plotted, saying, "Look, Jonathan and those who are with him are living in quiet and confidence; now therefore, let us bring Bacchides, and he will seize them all in one night."

**9:59** And they went and gave him counsel.

**9:60** And he rose up to come with a large army, and secretly sent letters to his allies who were in Judea, that they should seize Jonathan and those who were with him; but they could not, because their plan became known to them.

**9:61** And from among the men of the region who were leaders of the wickedness, he seized fifty men and killed them.

**9:62** And Jonathan, and Simon, and those who were with him withdrew to Bethbessen, which is in the wilderness; and he rebuilt its ruins, and they fortified it.

**9:63** And Bacchides learned of it, and gathered together his entire multitude, and sent word to those who were from Judea.

**9:64** And he came and pitched camp above Bethbessen, and attacked it for many days, and made siege engines.

**9:65** And Jonathan left Simon his brother in the city, and went out into the region, and came with a number of men.

**9:66** And he struck Odaren and his brothers, and the sons of Phaseron in their tents; and he began to cut them down and to grow in forces.

**9:67** Now Simon and those who were with him went out from the city and set fire to the siege engines,

**9:68** and fought against Bacchides, and he was crushed by them; and they distressed him greatly, because his plan and his assault were in vain.

**9:69** And enraged against the wicked men who had given him counsel to come into their region, he killed many of them; and he himself planned to go away with the rest to his own region.

**9:70** And Jonathan learned of it, and he sent envoys to him to make peace with him and to return the captives to him.

**9:71** And he gladly accepted and did according to his words, and swore that he would do him no harm all the days of his life.

**9:72** And he returned to him the captives whom he had previously taken as plunder from the land of Judah; and he turned back and went away to his own land, and did not proceed to come into its borders any longer.

**9:73** And the sword ceased from Israel; and Jonathan lived in Michmash, and there Jonathan began to judge the people, and he wiped out the ungodly from Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **9:5** — The Clementine Vulgate reads Laisa, whereas most Greek manuscripts read Elasa.
- **9:24** — The Vulgate distinctly adds "to Bacchides" (Bacchidi) and "their whole" (omnis ... eorum), whereas the Greek reads simply that "the country defected with them."
- **9:35** — Latin commodarent means "lend"; the Greek has Jonathan's party asking to leave its abundant baggage with the Nabateans.
- **9:37** — The Vulgate reads "from Medeba" (ex Madaba), where the Greek reads "from Nadabath." Latin ambitione here carries its late-Latin sense of "pomp" or "stately display" (Greek: "a great escort").
- **9:45** — The Vulgate reads only "in front of us" (ex adverso, without the Greek "and behind us") and adds "the banks" (ripæ) before the marshes and thickets.
- **9:48** — The Vulgate compresses the longer Greek text ("and swam across to the other side, and the enemy did not cross the Jordan against them") into "and they swam across the Jordan to them."
- **9:50** — The Vulgate preserves the distinct place-name forms Phara (Greek: Pharathon) and Thopo (Greek: Tephon).
- **9:62** — The Vulgate reads Bethbessen (so also in 9:64), whereas most Greek manuscripts read Bethbasi.
- **9:66** — The Vulgate reads the name Odaren (Greek: Odomera) and "to grow in forces" (crescere in virtutibus), where the Greek has "to go up with forces."
