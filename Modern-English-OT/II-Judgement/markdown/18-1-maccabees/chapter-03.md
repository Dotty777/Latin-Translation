## Text

**3:1** And his son Judas, who was called Maccabeus, arose in his place;

**3:2** and all his brothers and all who had joined themselves to his father helped him, and they fought the battle of Israel with gladness.

**3:3** And he extended glory for his people, and put on a breastplate like a giant, and strapped on his weapons of war in battles, and protected the camp with his sword.

**3:4** He became like a lion in his deeds, and like a lion cub roaring in the hunt.

**3:5** And he pursued the wicked, searching them out, and those who troubled his people he burned with flames;

**3:6** and his enemies were driven back because of fear of him, and all the workers of wickedness were troubled, and deliverance was directed in his hand.

**3:7** And he embittered many kings, and gladdened Jacob in his deeds, and his memory is in blessing forever.

**3:8** And he passed through the cities of Judah, and destroyed the ungodly out of them, and turned away wrath from Israel.

**3:9** And he became renowned to the farthest end of the earth, and he gathered those who were perishing.

**3:10** And Apollonius gathered the nations, and from Samaria a numerous and great force, to wage war against Israel.

**3:11** And Judas learned of it and went out to meet him, and he struck and killed him; and many fell wounded, and the rest fled.

**3:12** And he took their spoils, and Judas took away the sword of Apollonius, and he fought with it all his days.

**3:13** And Seron, commander of the army of Syria, heard that Judas had gathered a congregation of the faithful and an assembly with him,

**3:14** and he said, "I will make a name for myself, and I will be glorified in the kingdom, and I will wage war against Judas and those who are with him, who despised the word of the king."

**3:15** And he prepared himself, and a force of the ungodly, strong helpers, went up with him to execute vengeance on the sons of Israel.

**3:16** And they drew near as far as Beth-horon, and Judas went out to meet him with a few men.

**3:17** But when they saw the army coming to meet them, they said to Judas, "How will we, being few, be able to fight against a multitude so great and so strong, when we are weary from fasting today?"

**3:18** And Judas said, "It is easy for many to be shut up into the hands of a few, and there is no difference in the sight of the God of heaven in delivering by many or by a few;

**3:19** for victory in war is not in the multitude of an army, but strength is from heaven.

**3:20** They come against us with an insolent multitude and pride, to destroy us and our wives and our children, and to plunder us;

**3:21** but we will fight for our lives and our laws;

**3:22** and the Lord Himself will crush them before our face; as for you, do not fear them."

**3:23** Now when he stopped speaking, he sprang upon them suddenly, and Seron and his army were crushed in his sight;

**3:24** and he pursued him along the descent of Beth-horon as far as the plain, and eight hundred men from among them fell, but the rest fled into the land of the Philistines.

**3:25** And the fear of Judas and of his brothers, and dread, fell upon all the nations around them;

**3:26** and his name reached the king, and all the nations were telling about the battles of Judas.

**3:27** Now when King Antiochus heard these words, he became angry in spirit, and he sent and gathered the army of his entire kingdom, a very strong force;

**3:28** and he opened his treasury and gave pay to the army for a year, and he commanded them to be ready for everything.

**3:29** And he saw that the money had failed from his treasuries, and that the tributes of the region were small because of the dissension and calamity that he had caused in the land in order to take away the ordinances which were from the first days;

**3:30** and he feared that he would not have enough, as he had once and twice, for the expenses and gifts that he had given before with a generous hand; and he had abounded beyond the kings who had been before him.

**3:31** And he was greatly dismayed in spirit, and he planned to go into Persia, and to collect the tributes of the regions, and to gather much silver.

**3:32** And he left Lysias, a noble man of royal lineage, over the royal affairs from the river Euphrates as far as the river of Egypt,

**3:33** and to raise his son Antiochus until he returned.

**3:34** And he handed over to him half the army and the elephants, and he commanded him concerning everything that he wanted, and concerning those inhabiting Judea and Jerusalem;

**3:35** and that he should send an army against them to crush and uproot the strength of Israel and the remnant of Jerusalem, and to take away their memory from the place;

**3:36** and that he should settle foreign sons as inhabitants in all their borders, and distribute their land by lot.

**3:37** And the king took part of the remaining army, and went out from Antioch, the city of his kingdom, in the one hundred and forty-seventh year; and he crossed the river Euphrates and passed through the upper regions.

**3:38** And Lysias chose Ptolemy the son of Dorymenes, and Nicanor, and Gorgias, mighty men from among the friends of the king;

**3:39** and he sent with them forty thousand men and seven thousand horsemen to come into the land of Judah and destroy it according to the word of the king.

**3:40** And they marched out with all their force, and they came and encamped at Emmaus in the plain country.

**3:41** And the merchants of the regions heard their name, and they took silver and very much gold and servants, and came into the camp to take the sons of Israel as slaves; and the armies of Syria and of the land of the foreigners were added to them.

**3:42** And Judas and his brothers saw that evils had multiplied and that armies were encamping at their borders, and they learned the words of the king, which he had commanded to be done to the people for destruction and utter end;

**3:43** and they said, each one to his neighbor, "Let us raise up the ruin of our people, and let us fight for our people and our holy places."

**3:44** And the assembly was gathered so that they might be ready for battle, and so that they might pray and ask for mercy and compassions.

**3:45** And Jerusalem was uninhabited, but was like a desert; there was no one of her children who went in and went out. And the holy place was trampled down, and the sons of foreigners were in the citadel; there was the dwelling place of the nations. And delight was taken away from Jacob, and the flute and harp ceased there.

**3:46** And they were gathered together and came to Mizpah, opposite Jerusalem, because there was a place of prayer in Mizpah formerly in Israel.

**3:47** And they fasted on that day, and put on sackcloth, and placed ashes on their heads, and tore their garments;

**3:48** and they spread out the books of the law, concerning which the nations searched the likeness of their idols;

**3:49** and they brought the priestly vestments, and the firstfruits, and the tithes, and they raised up the Nazirites who had completed the days,

**3:50** and they cried out with a loud voice to heaven, saying, "What will we do with these, and where will we lead them?

**3:51** And Your holy places have been trampled down and defiled, and Your priests have been brought into mourning and humiliation;

**3:52** and look, the nations have assembled against us to destroy us; You know what they are planning against us.

**3:53** How will we be able to stand before their face, unless You, God, help us?"

**3:54** And with trumpets they cried out with a loud voice.

**3:55** And after these things Judas appointed leaders of the people: tribunes, and centurions, and commanders of fifty, and decurions.

**3:56** And he said to those who were building houses, and betrothing wives, and planting vineyards, and to the fearful, that they should return, each one to his own house, according to the law.

**3:57** And they moved the camp and stationed it to the south of Emmaus.

**3:58** And Judas said, "Arm yourselves, and be mighty sons, and be ready in the morning to fight against these nations that have assembled against us to destroy us and our holy places;

**3:59** for it is better for us to die in war than to see the evils of our nation and of the holy places.

**3:60** But as the will in heaven may be, so let it be done."

## Translator Notes

- **3:3** — The Vulgate reads "in battles" (in præliis) rather than the Greek verb phrase ("he set battles in array"), and adds "his" to "sword" (gladio suo).
- **3:6** — Latin directa est (lit. "was directed" or "was guided straight") is an idiom expressing success or prosperity.
- **3:13** — The Vulgate attaches "of the faithful" to "congregation" (congregationem fidelium, et ecclesiam secum) and omits the closing clause found in the Greek ("and of those going out to war").
- **3:18** — The Vulgate explicitly reads "the God of heaven" (Dei cæli) here and "the Lord Himself" (ipse Dominus) in 3:22, where the standard Greek text uses only "Heaven" and "He" respectively.
- **3:41** — Vulgate "servants" (pueros, also meaning "boys") reflects a reading of Greek paidas ("servants/boys") rather than pedas ("fetters/chains") found in standard Greek manuscripts; likewise "of the foreigners" (alienigenarum) replaces the Greek "of the Philistines."
- **3:48** — This clause is grammatically difficult in both Latin and Greek, referring either to the matters about which the nations consulted the images of their idols (contrasting with Israel consulting the law) or to the nations searching the books of the law to paint idol images in them.
- **3:53** — The Vulgate explicitly adds "God" (Deus), whereas the standard Greek text reads simply "unless you help us."
- **3:55** — The Vulgate uses Roman military titles (tribunos, centuriones, decuriones) alongside the Greek loanword pentacontarchos ("commanders of fifty") for commanders of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens.
