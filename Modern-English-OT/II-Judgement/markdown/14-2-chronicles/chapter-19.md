## Text

**19:1** Now Jehoshaphat king of Judah returned to his own house peacefully in Jerusalem.

**19:2** Jehu the son of Hanani the seer met him and said to him, "You offer help to the wicked, and you are joined in friendship with those who hate the Lord, and therefore you indeed deserved the wrath of the Lord;

**19:3** but good works have been found in you, because you have removed the groves from the land of Judah and have prepared your heart to seek the Lord God of your fathers."

**19:4** So Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem, and he went out again to the people from Beersheba to Mount Ephraim, and he called them back to the Lord God of their fathers.

**19:5** And he appointed judges of the land in all the fortified cities of Judah, in each place,

**19:6** and charging the judges, he said, "See what you are doing, for you do not exercise the judgment of man, but of the Lord, and whatever you judge will come back upon you.

**19:7** Let the fear of the Lord be with you, and do all things with diligence; for with the Lord our God there is no injustice, nor partiality, nor desire for gifts."

**19:8** In Jerusalem also, Jehoshaphat appointed Levites, priests, and leaders of the families from Israel, to judge the judgment and the cause of the Lord for its inhabitants.

**19:9** And he commanded them, saying, "In this way you will act in the fear of the Lord, faithfully and with a perfect heart.

**19:10** Every case of your brothers who live in their cities that comes to you, between kindred and kindred, wherever there is a question concerning the law, concerning a commandment, concerning ceremonies, concerning ordinances, explain it to them, so that they may not sin against the Lord, and that wrath may not come upon you and upon your brothers; therefore, acting in this way, you will not sin.

**19:11** Now Amariah the priest and your high priest will preside over the matters that pertain to God; moreover, Zebadiah the son of Ishmael, who is the ruler in the house of Judah, will be over those affairs that pertain to the king's office; and you have the Levites as officers before you. Be strong and act diligently, and the Lord will be with you in good things."

## Translator Notes

- **19:2** — Vulgate states the rebuke declaratively ("You offer help... you indeed deserved the wrath of the Lord") rather than as a question as in the Hebrew, and in v. 3 expands Hebrew "God" to "the Lord God of your fathers."
- **19:6** — Vulgate "whatever you judge will come back upon you" (quodcumque judicaveritis, in vos redundabit) differs notably from the Hebrew "He is with you in the matter of judgment."
- **19:10** — Vulgate "between kindred and kindred" (inter cognationem et cognationem) interprets the Hebrew phrase "between blood and blood" (cases of bloodshed) in terms of blood kinship.
- **19:11** — Vulgate "in good things" (in bonis) renders the Hebrew "with the good," which can be understood either of upright persons or of good deeds.
