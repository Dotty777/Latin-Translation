## Text

**3:1** And Solomon began to build the house of the Lord in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, which had been shown to David his father, in the place that David had prepared on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

**3:2** Now he began to build in the second month, in the fourth year of his reign.

**3:3** And these are the foundations that Solomon laid to build the house of God: in length, sixty cubits by the first measure; in width, twenty cubits.

**3:4** And the portico before the front, which extended in length according to the measure of the width of the house, was twenty cubits; furthermore, the height was one hundred twenty cubits; and he gilded it on the inside with the purest gold.

**3:5** He also covered the greater house with planks of fir wood, and fastened plates of refined gold throughout; and he carved on it palm trees and what resembled small chains intertwining with one another.

**3:6** He also paved the floor of the temple with the most precious marble, with great beauty.

**3:7** Moreover, the gold was most thoroughly tested, with plates of which he covered the house, and its beams, and doorposts, and walls, and doors; and he engraved cherubim on the walls.

**3:8** He also made the house of the Holy of Holies: its length, according to the width of the house, was twenty cubits, and its width likewise was twenty cubits; and he covered it with gold plates of about six hundred talents.

**3:9** Moreover, he made gold nails such that each nail weighed fifty shekels; he also covered the upper rooms with gold.

**3:10** He also made two cherubim in the house of the Holy of Holies, of statuary work, and covered them with gold.

**3:11** The wings of the cherubim extended twenty cubits, so that one wing measured five cubits and touched the wall of the house, and the other, measuring five cubits, touched the wing of the other cherub.

**3:12** Similarly, a wing of the other cherub measured five cubits and touched the wall, and its other wing, of five cubits, met the wing of the other cherub.

**3:13** Therefore the wings of both cherubim were spread out and extended across twenty cubits; and they themselves stood upright on their feet, and their faces were turned toward the outer house.

**3:14** He also made the veil of blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen, and wove cherubim into it.

**3:15** Before the doors of the temple he also made two columns that measured thirty-five cubits in height; moreover, their capitals were five cubits.

**3:16** He also made what resembled small chains in the oracle, and set them on top of the capitals of the columns; and also one hundred pomegranates, which he placed between the small chains.

**3:17** He also set the columns themselves in the vestibule of the temple, one on the right and the other on the left; the one that was on the right he called Jachin, and the one on the left, Boaz.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Distinctly Vulgate reading: Latin qui demonstratus fuerat David patri ejus ('which had been shown to David his father') makes Mount Moriah the subject shown to David, whereas the Hebrew reads 'where the Lord had appeared to David his father.'
- **3:2** — Latin omits 'on the second day' found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, reading simply mense secundo, anno quarto regni sui ('in the second month, in the fourth year of his reign').
- **3:7** — Latin renders Hebrew 'gold of Parvaim' (at the end of 3:6 in the Hebrew) as aurum erat probatissimum ('the gold was most thoroughly tested') at the opening of 3:7.
- **3:9** — Latin uses distributive numerals (singuli clavi siclos quinquagenos), specifying that each individual nail weighed fifty shekels, whereas the Hebrew is commonly taken as fifty shekels for the weight of the nails.
- **3:16** — Latin in oraculo ('in the oracle') renders Hebrew debir, the inner sanctuary or Most Holy Place, understood by Jerome as the place of divine utterance.
