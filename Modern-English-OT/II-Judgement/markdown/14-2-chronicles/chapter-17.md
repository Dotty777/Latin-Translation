## Text

**17:1** Now Jehoshaphat his son reigned in his place, and he grew strong against Israel.

**17:2** And he stationed detachments of soldiers in all the cities of Judah that were surrounded with walls. And he positioned garrisons in the land of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that Asa his father had captured.

**17:3** And the Lord was with Jehoshaphat, because he walked in the first ways of David his father, and did not hope in the Baals,

**17:4** but in the God of his father, and walked on in His commandments, and not according to the sins of Israel.

**17:5** And the Lord established the kingdom in his hand, and all Judah gave gifts to Jehoshaphat, and boundless wealth and much glory became his.

**17:6** And when his heart had taken courage on account of the ways of the Lord, he also removed the high places and the groves from Judah.

**17:7** Now in the third year of his reign he sent some of his leaders, Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah, to teach in the cities of Judah,

**17:8** and with them the Levites Shemaiah, Nethaniah, and Zebadiah, also Asahel, Shemiramoth, and Jonathan, and Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-adonijah, Levites, and with them Elishama and Jehoram, the priests;

**17:9** and they taught the people in Judah, having the book of the law of the Lord, and they went around all the cities of Judah and instructed the people.

**17:10** Therefore the dread of the Lord came upon all the kingdoms of the lands that were all around Judah, and they did not dare to wage war against Jehoshaphat.

**17:11** Moreover, the Philistines also brought gifts to Jehoshaphat and a tribute of silver; the Arabs also brought flocks, seven thousand seven hundred rams and just as many male goats.

**17:12** So Jehoshaphat grew and was magnified to the heights, and he built in Judah houses like towers and walled cities.

**17:13** And he prepared many works in the cities of Judah; fighting and mighty men were also in Jerusalem,

**17:14** whose number by the houses and families of each was this: in Judah, the leaders of the army, Adnah the commander, and with him three hundred thousand very mighty men.

**17:15** After him, Johanan the leader, and with him two hundred eighty thousand.

**17:16** After him also, Amasiah the son of Zichri, consecrated to the Lord, and with him two hundred thousand valiant men.

**17:17** Eliada, mighty for battle, followed him, and with him two hundred thousand of those holding bow and shield.

**17:18** After him also, Jehozabad, and with him one hundred eighty thousand equipped soldiers.

**17:19** All these were at the hand of the king, apart from the others whom he had placed in the walled cities throughout all Judah.

## Translator Notes

- **17:3** — Vulgate "did not hope" (non speravit) renders the Hebrew verb meaning "sought"; similarly in v. 4 Vulgate has "sins" (peccata) where the Hebrew has "deeds" or "practices."
- **17:6** — Vulgate "groves" (lucos) regularly translates the Hebrew Asherim (sacred wooden poles or cultic groves associated with Asherah), as also in 19:3.
- **17:12** — Vulgate "houses like towers and walled cities" (domos ad instar turrium, urbesque muratas) differs from the Hebrew, which refers to fortresses and store cities.
- **17:17** — The Clementine Vulgate omits the tribal designation "of Benjamin" found in the Hebrew at the opening of this verse.
