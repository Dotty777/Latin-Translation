## Text

**36:1** Therefore the people of the land took Jehoahaz, the son of Josiah, and appointed him king in place of his father in Jerusalem.

**36:2** Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he began to reign, and he reigned in Jerusalem for three months.

**36:3** Now the king of Egypt removed him when he had come to Jerusalem, and fined the land one hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold.

**36:4** And he appointed Eliakim his brother king in his place over Judah and Jerusalem, and changed his name to Jehoiakim; but Jehoahaz himself he took with him and led away into Egypt.

**36:5** Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned in Jerusalem for eleven years; and he did evil before the Lord his God.

**36:6** Nebuchadnezzar, king of the Chaldeans, came up against him and led him, bound in chains, into Babylon.

**36:7** To it he also carried the vessels of the Lord and placed them in his temple.

**36:8** Now the rest of the deeds of Jehoiakim, and his abominations which he practiced, and what was found in him, are contained in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel. And Jehoiachin his son reigned in his place.

**36:9** Jehoiachin was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned in Jerusalem for three months and ten days; and he did evil in the sight of the Lord.

**36:10** And when the circuit of the year came around, King Nebuchadnezzar sent men who brought him into Babylon, with the most precious vessels of the house of the Lord carried off at the same time. And he appointed Zedekiah his uncle king over Judah and Jerusalem.

**36:11** Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned in Jerusalem for eleven years.

**36:12** And he did evil in the eyes of the Lord his God, and he was not ashamed before the face of Jeremiah the prophet, speaking to him from the mouth of the Lord.

**36:13** He also broke away from King Nebuchadnezzar, who had put him under oath by God; and he hardened his neck and heart so that he would not return to the Lord God of Israel.

**36:14** Moreover, all the leaders of the priests and the people transgressed wickedly according to all the abominations of the nations, and polluted the house of the Lord, which He had sanctified for Himself in Jerusalem.

**36:15** Now the Lord God of their fathers kept sending to them by the hand of His messengers, rising up in the night and warning them daily, because He was sparing His people and His dwelling place.

**36:16** But they mocked the messengers of God, and made light of His words, and scoffed at the prophets, until the fury of the Lord rose up against His people, and there was no healing.

**36:17** For He brought upon them the king of the Chaldeans, and killed their young men with the sword in the house of His sanctuary; He had no compassion on youth, or virgin, or old man, nor even on one who was decrepit, but He handed them all over into his hands.

**36:18** And all the vessels of the house of the Lord, both large and small, and the treasures of the temple, and of the king, and of the leaders, he carried into Babylon.

**36:19** The enemies set fire to the house of God and tore down the wall of Jerusalem; they burned down all the towers, and they demolished whatever had been precious.

**36:20** If anyone had escaped the sword, he was led into Babylon and served the king and his sons, until the king of the Persians ruled,

**36:21** and the word of the Lord from the mouth of Jeremiah was fulfilled, and the land celebrated its Sabbaths; for it kept Sabbath all the days of desolation, until seventy years were fulfilled.

**36:22** Now in the first year of Cyrus, king of the Persians, to fulfill the word of the Lord which He had spoken through the mouth of Jeremiah, the Lord stirred up the spirit of Cyrus, king of the Persians, who ordered it to be proclaimed throughout his entire kingdom, even in writing, saying:

**36:23** "This is what Cyrus, king of the Persians, says: The Lord God of heaven has given me all the kingdoms of the earth, and He Himself has commanded me to build a house for Him in Jerusalem, which is in Judea. Who of you is among all His people? May the Lord his God be with him, and let him go up."

## Translator Notes

- **36:6** — The Vulgate reads "king of the Chaldeans" (Latin rex Chaldaeorum) where the Hebrew has "king of Babylon," and uses the indicative "led him... into Babylon" (duxit in Babylonem) where the Hebrew expresses purpose ("to take him to Babylon").
- **36:10** — The Vulgate reads "his uncle" (Latin patruum ejus, paternal uncle, agreeing with 2 Kings 24:17) where the Hebrew text of 2 Chronicles has "his brother."
- **36:12** — The Vulgate reads "was not ashamed before the face of Jeremiah" (Latin nec erubuit faciem Jeremiae), whereas the Hebrew text reads "did not humble himself before Jeremiah."
- **36:15** — The Vulgate reads "rising up in the night and warning them daily" (Latin de nocte consurgens, et quotidie commonens), rendering the Hebrew idiom for persistent sending with the imagery of rising before dawn and admonishing every day.
- **36:17** — The Vulgate keeps a continuous third-person singular subject across the verse, reading "in the house of His sanctuary" (in domo sanctuarii sui, reflexive to the Lord) and connecting "had no compassion" directly to "handed them all over into his hands" with "but" (sed). The Hebrew instead reads "their sanctuary" and is commonly read with the Chaldean king as the subject of the killing.
