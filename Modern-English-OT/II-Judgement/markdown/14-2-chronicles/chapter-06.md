## Text

**6:1** Then Solomon said, "The Lord has promised that He would dwell in thick darkness;

**6:2** but I have built a house for His name, so that He might dwell there forever."

**6:3** And the king turned his face and blessed the entire multitude of Israel (for the whole crowd was standing attentively), and said,

**6:4** "Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who has fulfilled in deed what He spoke to my father David, saying,

**6:5** 'From the day on which I brought My people out of the land of Egypt, I did not choose a city from all the tribes of Israel so that a house might be built in it for My name, nor did I choose any other man to be leader among the people of Israel;

**6:6** but I chose Jerusalem so that My name may be in it, and I chose David so that I might appoint him over My people Israel.'

**6:7** Now when it had been the desire of my father David to build a house for the name of the Lord God of Israel,

**6:8** the Lord said to him, 'Because this was your desire, to build a house for My name, you indeed did well to have such a desire;

**6:9** yet you will not build the house; rather, your son who will come forth from your loins, he himself will build the house for My name.'

**6:10** Therefore the Lord has fulfilled His word that He had spoken, and I have risen in place of my father David and sat upon the throne of Israel, just as the Lord spoke, and I have built the house for the name of the Lord God of Israel.

**6:11** And in it I have placed the ark in which is the covenant of the Lord that He made with the sons of Israel."

**6:12** Then he stood before the altar of the Lord, opposite the entire multitude of Israel, and stretched out his hands.

**6:13** For indeed Solomon had made a bronze platform and had placed it in the middle of the basilica, measuring five cubits in length, five cubits in width, and three cubits in height; and he stood upon it, and then, with bent knees facing the entire multitude of Israel and with palms raised toward heaven,

**6:14** he said, "Lord God of Israel, there is no god like You in heaven and on earth, You who keep covenant and mercy with Your servants who walk before You with all their heart,

**6:15** who have performed for Your servant my father David all that You had spoken to him; and what You had promised with Your mouth, You have fulfilled in deed, just as the present time also proves.

**6:16** Now therefore, Lord God of Israel, fulfill for Your servant my father David all that You spoke, saying, 'A man from you will not be lacking before Me to sit upon the throne of Israel, provided only that your sons guard their ways and walk in My law, just as you also have walked before Me.'

**6:17** And now, Lord God of Israel, let Your word that You spoke to Your servant David be confirmed.

**6:18** Is it then believable that God should dwell with mankind on the earth? If heaven and the heavens of heavens do not contain You, how much more this house that I have built?

**6:19** Yet it has been made for this alone: that You may regard the prayer of Your servant and his supplication, Lord my God, and hear the petitions that Your servant pours out before You,

**6:20** that You may open Your eyes upon this house day and night, upon the place in which You promised that Your name would be invoked,

**6:21** and that You would hear the prayer that Your servant prays in it; and may You hear the petitions of Your servant and of Your people Israel. Whoever prays in this place, hear from Your dwelling, that is, from the heavens, and show mercy.

**6:22** If anyone sins against his neighbor, and comes prepared to swear against him, and binds himself with a curse before the altar in this house,

**6:23** You will hear from heaven and execute judgment on Your servants, so that You repay the wicked man his way upon his own head, and vindicate the righteous man, rewarding him according to his righteousness.

**6:24** If Your people Israel are defeated by enemies (for they will sin against You), and turning back they repent, and implore Your name, and plead in this place,

**6:25** You will hear from heaven; and pardon the sin of Your people Israel, and bring them back to the land that You gave to them and to their fathers.

**6:26** If, when heaven is shut, rain does not fall because of the sins of the people, and they plead with You in this place, and confess Your name, and turn back from their sins when You afflict them,

**6:27** hear from heaven, Lord, and forgive the sins of Your servants and of Your people Israel, and teach them the good way along which they should walk, and give rain to the land that You have given to Your people to possess.

**6:28** If famine arises in the land, and pestilence, blight and mildew, and locust and caterpillar, and enemies, having laid waste the regions, besiege the gates of the city, and every plague and sickness presses hard,

**6:29** if anyone of Your people Israel pleads, recognizing his own plague and sickness, and spreads out his hands in this house,

**6:30** You will hear from heaven, namely, from Your lofty dwelling; and show mercy, and repay each one according to his ways, which You know he has in his heart (for You alone know the hearts of the sons of men),

**6:31** so that they may fear You and walk in Your ways all the days that they live upon the face of the land that You gave to our fathers.

**6:32** And as for the foreigner also, who is not of Your people Israel, if he comes from a distant land because of Your great name, and because of Your strong hand and Your outstretched arm, and worships in this place,

**6:33** You will hear from heaven, Your most steadfast dwelling, and do everything for which that stranger calls upon You, so that all the peoples of the earth may know Your name, and fear You as Your people Israel does, and recognize that Your name has been invoked upon this house that I have built.

**6:34** If Your people go out to war against their adversaries by the way on which You send them, they will worship You facing the direction in which this city is, which You have chosen, and the house that I have built for Your name;

**6:35** You will hear from heaven their petitions and supplication, and may You execute vengeance.

**6:36** Now if they sin against You (for there is no man who does not sin), and You become angry with them and hand them over to enemies, and they lead them away captive to a distant land, or indeed one that is nearby,

**6:37** and turning back in their heart in the land to which they had been led captive, they repent and plead with You in the land of their captivity, saying, 'We have sinned; we have acted wickedly, we have dealt unjustly,'

**6:38** and return to You with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their captivity to which they were led, they will worship You facing the direction of their land that You gave to their fathers, and of the city that You have chosen, and of the house that I have built for Your name;

**6:39** You will hear from heaven, that is, from Your steadfast dwelling, their petitions, and may You execute judgment and forgive Your people, although sinful;

**6:40** for You are my God; please, let Your eyes be open, and let Your ears be attentive to the prayer that is made in this place.

**6:41** Now therefore arise, Lord God, into Your resting place, You and the ark of Your strength; let Your priests, Lord God, be clothed with salvation, and let Your holy ones rejoice in good things.

**6:42** Lord God, do not turn away the face of Your anointed one; remember the mercies of Your servant David."

## Translator Notes

- **6:2** — The Vulgate speaks of God in the third person ("for His name, so that He might dwell there"), whereas the Hebrew text addresses God directly in the second person.
- **6:13** — Latin basilicæ denotes a royal colonnaded hall or enclosed court, rendering the Hebrew term for the temple court.
- **6:18** — The Vulgate renders the a fortiori exclamation literally with quanto magis ("how much more [does this house fail to contain You]"), corresponding to the sense "how much less."
- **6:19** — The Vulgate adds the explanatory clause "Yet it has been made for this alone," which is not in the Hebrew text; similarly in 6:21, 6:30, and 6:39 it adds brief glosses ("that is," "namely") clarifying God's heavenly dwelling.
- **6:40** — The Vulgate reads "for You are my God" (tu es enim Deus meus), reflecting the Hebrew word for "you" rather than the homophonous word for "now."
