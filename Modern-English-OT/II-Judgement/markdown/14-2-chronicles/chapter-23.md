## Text

**23:1** Now in the seventh year, Jehoiada strengthened himself and took the centurions, namely Azariah son of Jeroham, and Ishmael son of Jehohanan, also Azariah son of Obed, and Maaseiah son of Adaiah, and Elishaphat son of Zichri; and he entered into a covenant with them.

**23:2** Going around Judah, they gathered the Levites from all the cities of Judah and the leaders of the families of Israel, and they came to Jerusalem.

**23:3** So the whole multitude entered into a pact in the house of God with the king, and Jehoiada said to them, "Look, the son of the king will reign, just as the Lord spoke concerning the sons of David.

**23:4** This, therefore, is the thing that you will do:

**23:5** a third part of you who come for the Sabbath, of the priests, and of the Levites, and of the gatekeepers, will be at the gates; and a third part at the house of the king; and a third at the gate that is called the Gate of the Foundation; but let all the rest of the common people be in the courts of the house of the Lord.

**23:6** And let no one else enter the house of the Lord, except the priests and those of the Levites who minister; let them alone enter, because they are consecrated, and let all the rest of the common people keep the watches of the Lord.

**23:7** Now let the Levites surround the king, each one having his weapons (and if anyone else enters the temple, let him be put to death), and let them be with the king both when he goes in and when he goes out."

**23:8** So the Levites and all Judah did according to all that Jehoiada the high priest had commanded; and each one took the men who were under him and were coming in the order of the Sabbath, along with those who had completed the Sabbath and were about to go out, since Jehoiada the high priest had not let the companies depart that were accustomed to succeed one another week by week.

**23:9** And Jehoiada the priest gave to the centurions the spears, shields, and bucklers of King David, which he had consecrated in the house of the Lord.

**23:10** And he stationed all the people, holding daggers, from the right side of the temple to the left side of the temple, before the altar and the temple, all around the king.

**23:11** And they brought out the king's son, and placed upon him the diadem and the testimony, and gave the law into his hand to hold, and made him king; Jehoiada the high priest and his sons also anointed him, and they invoked blessings upon him and said, "May the king live!"

**23:12** When Athaliah heard this, namely the sound of those running and praising the king, she went in to the people in the temple of the Lord.

**23:13** And when she saw the king standing upon the step at the entrance, and the leaders and the companies around him, and all the people of the land rejoicing and sounding trumpets and playing together on instruments of various kinds, and the sound of those praising, she tore her garments and said, "Conspiracy! Conspiracy!"

**23:14** Then Jehoiada the high priest went out to the centurions and the leaders of the army and said to them, "Bring her out beyond the precincts of the temple, and let her be put to death outside by the sword." And the priest commanded that she not be killed in the house of the Lord,

**23:15** and they laid hands on her neck; and when she had entered the Horse Gate of the house of the king, they put her to death there.

**23:16** Then Jehoiada made a covenant between himself, and all the people, and the king, that they would be the people of the Lord.

**23:17** And so all the people entered the house of Baal and destroyed it, and smashed its altars and images; they also put Mattan, the priest of Baal, to death before the altars.

**23:18** Now Jehoiada appointed overseers in the house of the Lord under the hands of the priests and the Levites, whom David had distributed in the house of the Lord, to offer burnt offerings to the Lord, as it is written in the law of Moses, with joy and songs, according to the arrangement of David.

**23:19** He also appointed gatekeepers at the gates of the house of the Lord, so that no one unclean in any matter should enter it.

**23:20** And he took the centurions, and the mightiest men, and the leaders of the people, and all the common people of the land, and they brought the king down from the house of the Lord and had him enter through the middle of the Upper Gate into the house of the king, and they seated him on the royal throne.

**23:21** And all the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet; but Athaliah was put to death by the sword.

## Translator Notes

- **23:5** — Unlike the Hebrew verse division (which places the first third in v. 4 as gatekeepers of the thresholds), the Vulgate moves all three divisions into v. 5 and coordinates 'of the gatekeepers' (et janitorum) with the priests and Levites stationed at the gates.
- **23:11** — Alongside placing the diadem and the testimony on the king, the Vulgate adds the explanatory clause 'and gave the law into his hand to hold' (dederuntque in manu ejus tenendam legem), as well as 'and they invoked blessings upon him' (imprecatique sunt ei) before the acclamation.
- **23:13** — Where the Hebrew describes the king standing by his pillar with the captains and trumpeters, the Vulgate reads 'upon the step' (super gradum) and 'the leaders and the companies around him' (et principes, turmasque circa eum).
- **23:14** — Whereas the Hebrew has Jehoiada bring out the commanders and order Athaliah led between the ranks (with any follower killed), the Vulgate has him go out to the centurions and army leaders and order her brought 'beyond the precincts of the temple' (extra septa templi) to be put to death outside.
