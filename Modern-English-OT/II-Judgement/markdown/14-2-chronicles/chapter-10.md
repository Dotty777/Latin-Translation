## Text

**10:1** Now Rehoboam set out for Shechem, for all Israel had gathered there to make him king.

**10:2** When Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who was in Egypt (for indeed he had fled there from before Solomon), had heard of this, he immediately returned.

**10:3** And they called him, and he came with all Israel; and they spoke to Rehoboam, saying,

**10:4** "Your father pressed us with a very harsh yoke; command lighter things than your father, who imposed a heavy servitude upon us, and lighten a little of the burden, so that we may serve you."

**10:5** And he said, "After three days, return to me." And when the people had departed,

**10:6** he took counsel with the elders who had stood before his father Solomon while he was still alive, saying, "What counsel do you give, that I may answer the people?"

**10:7** And they said to him, "If you please this people and soothe them with gentle words, they will serve you at all times."

**10:8** But he abandoned the counsel of the elders, and began to confer with the young men who had been brought up with him and were in his retinue.

**10:9** And he said to them, "What do you think? Or what should I answer this people, who said to me, 'Lighten the yoke that your father imposed upon us'?"

**10:10** But they answered as young men, and brought up with him in luxury, and said, "Thus you will speak to the people who said to you, 'Your father made our yoke heavy; you lighten it,' and thus you will answer them: 'My little finger is thicker than my father's loins.

**10:11** My father imposed a heavy yoke upon you, and I will add a greater weight; my father beat you with whips, but I will beat you with scorpions.'"

**10:12** Therefore Jeroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam on the third day, just as he had commanded them.

**10:13** And the king answered harsh things, having abandoned the counsel of the elders;

**10:14** and he spoke according to the will of the young men: "My father imposed a heavy yoke upon you, which I will make heavier; my father beat you with whips, but I will beat you with scorpions."

**10:15** And he did not yield to the pleas of the people; for it was the will of God that His word might be fulfilled, which He had spoken by the hand of Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam the son of Nebat.

**10:16** Now all the people, as the king spoke harsher things, spoke thus to him: "We have no portion in David, nor inheritance in the son of Jesse. Return to your tents, Israel; and you, shepherd your own house, David." And Israel went away to their tents.

**10:17** But over the sons of Israel who dwelt in the cities of Judah, Rehoboam reigned.

**10:18** And King Rehoboam sent Adoram, who was over the tributes, and the sons of Israel stoned him, and he died; however, King Rehoboam hurried to mount the chariot and fled to Jerusalem.

**10:19** And Israel withdrew from the house of David, to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **10:2** — Latin statim reversus est ("he immediately returned") agrees in sense with 1 Kings 12:2 rather than the Hebrew text of 2 Chronicles 10:2, which states that Jeroboam dwelt in Egypt.
- **10:10** — Latin ut juvenes, et nutriti cum eo in deliciis ("as young men, and brought up with him in luxury") is a distinctly Vulgate expansion characterizing Rehoboam's young advisers.
- **10:16** — Latin rege duriora dicente ("as the king spoke harsher things") diverges from the Hebrew ("saw that the king did not listen to them"), and pasce ("shepherd") takes the Hebrew verb in its pastoral sense rather than "look to."
- **10:18** — Latin Aduram reflects the name form Adoram (as in 1 Kings 12:18) rather than the Hebrew spelling Hadoram in 2 Chronicles 10:18.
