## Text

**11:1** Now Rehoboam came to Jerusalem and assembled the entire house of Judah and Benjamin, one hundred eighty thousand chosen and fighting men, to fight against Israel and turn his kingdom back to himself.

**11:2** And the word of the Lord came to Shemaiah the man of God, saying,

**11:3** "Speak to Rehoboam the son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all Israel who are in Judah and Benjamin:

**11:4** 'This is what the Lord says: You must not go up, nor fight against your brothers; let each one return to his own house, because this has been done by My will.'" When they heard the word of the Lord, they turned back and did not march against Jeroboam.

**11:5** Now Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem, and he built walled cities in Judah.

**11:6** He built up Bethlehem, Etam, and Tekoa,

**11:7** Beth-zur also, and Soco, and Adullam,

**11:8** as well as Gath, Mareshah, and Ziph,

**11:9** and also Adoraim, Lachish, and Azekah,

**11:10** Zorah also, Aijalon, and Hebron, which were in Judah and Benjamin, heavily fortified cities.

**11:11** And when he had enclosed them with walls, he placed commanders in them and storehouses of provisions, that is, of oil and wine.

**11:12** Moreover, in every single city he made an armory of shields and spears, and he strengthened them with the utmost care, and he ruled over Judah and Benjamin.

**11:13** Now the priests and the Levites who were in all Israel came to him from all their dwelling places,

**11:14** leaving their outlying lands and their possessions, and crossing over to Judah and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his descendants had cast them off so that they would not serve in the priesthood of the Lord.

**11:15** And he appointed for himself priests of the high places, and of the demons, and of the calves that he had made.

**11:16** And also from all the tribes of Israel, whoever had given their heart to seek the Lord God of Israel came to Jerusalem to sacrifice their offerings before the Lord God of their fathers.

**11:17** And they strengthened the kingdom of Judah and established Rehoboam the son of Solomon for three years, for they walked in the ways of David and Solomon for only three years.

**11:18** Now Rehoboam took as wife Mahalath the daughter of Jerimoth the son of David, and also Abihail the daughter of Eliab the son of Jesse,

**11:19** who bore him sons: Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham.

**11:20** After her he also took Maacah the daughter of Absalom, who bore him Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith.

**11:21** Now Rehoboam loved Maacah the daughter of Absalom above all his wives and concubines, for he had taken eighteen wives and sixty concubines, and he fathered twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters.

**11:22** And he appointed Abijah the son of Maacah at the head as leader over all his brothers, for he planned to make him king,

**11:23** because he was wiser and more powerful over all his sons, and in all the territories of Judah and Benjamin, and in all the walled cities; and he supplied them with abundant food, and sought many wives.

## Translator Notes

- **11:4** — The Vulgate reads "by My will" (mea ... voluntate), specifying divine agency where the Hebrew says simply "from Me."
- **11:15** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew term for goat-idols or satyrs (se'irim) as "demons" (daemoniorum).
- **11:18** — The Vulgate puts "Abihail the daughter of Eliab" in the accusative case (Abihail quoque filiam), presenting Abihail as an additional wife of Rehoboam rather than as the mother of Mahalath.
- **11:23** — A distinctly Vulgate reading: the Latin presents this verse as explaining why Rehoboam chose Abijah ("because he was wiser and more powerful over all his sons"), whereas the Hebrew describes Rehoboam acting wisely by dispersing his sons across the fortified cities of Judah and Benjamin.
