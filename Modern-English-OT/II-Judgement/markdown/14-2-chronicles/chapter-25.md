## Text

**25:1** Amaziah was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned for twenty-nine years in Jerusalem; his mother's name was Jehoaddan from Jerusalem.

**25:2** And he did good in the sight of the Lord, yet not with a complete heart.

**25:3** And when he saw that his rule had been strengthened for him, he executed the servants who had killed the king, his father.

**25:4** But he did not put their sons to death, just as it is written in the book of the law of Moses, where the Lord commanded, saying, "Fathers will not be killed for sons, nor sons for their fathers, but each one will die in his own sin."

**25:5** Therefore Amaziah assembled Judah and organized them by families, with tribunes and centurions throughout all Judah and Benjamin; and he mustered them from twenty years old and upward, and found three hundred thousand young men who could go out to battle and hold spear and shield.

**25:6** He also hired one hundred thousand strong men from Israel for one hundred talents of silver.

**25:7** Now a man of God came to him and said, "King, do not let the army of Israel go out with you, for the Lord is not with Israel and all the sons of Ephraim.

**25:8** But if you think that wars depend on the strength of an army, God will cause you to be overcome by enemies; for it belongs to God both to help and to put to flight."

**25:9** And Amaziah said to the man of God, "What then will become of the one hundred talents that I gave to the soldiers of Israel?" And the man of God answered him, "The Lord has the means from which He can give you much more than these."

**25:10** So Amaziah separated the army that had come to him from Ephraim, so that it would return to its own place; yet they, fiercely angry against Judah, returned to their own region.

**25:11** Then Amaziah confidently led out his people and went to the Valley of Salt, and struck down ten thousand of the sons of Seir.

**25:12** And the sons of Judah captured another ten thousand men and brought them to the cliff of a certain rock, and hurled them headlong from the summit; and all of them burst apart.

**25:13** But that army which Amaziah had sent back so that it would not go with him to battle spread out through the cities of Judah, from Samaria as far as Beth-horon, and after putting three thousand to death, seized great plunder.

**25:14** Now Amaziah, after the slaughter of the Edomites and after bringing the gods of the sons of Seir, set them up as gods for himself, and worshiped them, and burned incense to them.

**25:15** For this reason the Lord, angry against Amaziah, sent a prophet to him to say to him, "Why have you worshiped gods who did not deliver their own people from your hand?"

**25:16** And while he was speaking these things, he answered him, "Are you a counselor of the king? Stop, so that I do not put you to death." "I know," the prophet said as he departed, "that God has planned to kill you, because you have done this evil and, moreover, have not consented to my counsel."

**25:17** Therefore Amaziah king of Judah, having taken very bad counsel, sent to Joash son of Jehoahaz, son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying, "Come, let us see one another."

**25:18** But he sent back messengers, saying, "The thistle that is in Lebanon sent to the cedar of Lebanon, saying, 'Give your daughter to my son as a wife.' And look, the wild beasts that were in the forest of Lebanon passed by and trampled the thistle.

**25:19** You have said, 'I struck down Edom,' and therefore your heart is lifted up in pride. Stay in your house; why do you provoke disaster against yourself, so that both you and Judah with you fall?"

**25:20** Amaziah refused to listen, because it was the will of the Lord that he should be handed over into the hands of enemies because of the gods of Edom.

**25:21** Therefore Joash king of Israel went up, and they faced one another; now Amaziah king of Judah was in Beth-shemesh of Judah.

**25:22** And Judah fell before Israel and fled to their tents.

**25:23** Then Joash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, son of Joash, son of Jehoahaz, in Beth-shemesh and brought him to Jerusalem; and he tore down its wall from the Ephraim Gate to the Corner Gate, four hundred cubits.

**25:24** He also brought back to Samaria all the gold and silver, and all the vessels that he had found in the house of God and with Obed-edom, and also in the treasuries of the royal house, as well as the sons of the hostages.

**25:25** Now Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, lived fifteen years after Joash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel, died.

**25:26** Now the rest of the accounts of Amaziah, first and last, are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.

**25:27** And after he turned away from the Lord, they laid a plot against him in Jerusalem. And when he had fled to Lachish, they sent and put him to death there.

**25:28** And carrying him back on horses, they buried him with his fathers in the city of David.

## Translator Notes

- **25:8** — The Vulgate translates the difficult Hebrew clause as a clear conditional statement ("if you think that wars depend on the strength of an army"), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew is usually rendered "if you go, act, be strong for battle."
- **25:17** — The Vulgate explicitly adds the judgment "very bad" (pessimo) to Amaziah's counsel, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew simply states that he "took counsel."
- **25:24** — Latin filios obsidum ("sons of the hostages") is a literal rendering of the Hebrew idiom for hostages ("sons of pledges").
- **25:28** — The Vulgate reads "in the city of David" (agreeing with 2 Kings 14:20 and some ancient witnesses), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "in the city of Judah."
