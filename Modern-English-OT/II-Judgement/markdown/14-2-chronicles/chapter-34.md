## Text

**34:1** Josiah was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned in Jerusalem for thirty-one years.

**34:2** And he did what was right in the sight of the Lord and walked in the ways of David his father; he turned aside neither to the right nor to the left.

**34:3** Now in the eighth year of his reign, while he was still a boy, he began to seek the God of his father David; and in the twelfth year after he had begun to reign, he cleansed Judah and Jerusalem from the high places and the sacred groves, and the statues and the carved images.

**34:4** And they destroyed the altars of the Baals in his presence, and they demolished the statues that had been set upon them; he also cut down and crushed to pieces the sacred groves and the carved images, and scattered the fragments over the graves of those who had been accustomed to sacrifice to them.

**34:5** Moreover, he burned the bones of the priests on the altars of the idols, and cleansed Judah and Jerusalem.

**34:6** And also in the cities of Manasseh, Ephraim, and Simeon, as far as Naphtali, he overturned everything.

**34:7** And when he had torn down the altars, and crushed the sacred groves and the carved images into pieces, and demolished all the shrines from the entire land of Israel, he returned to Jerusalem.

**34:8** Therefore, in the eighteenth year of his reign, now that the land and the temple of the Lord had been cleansed, he sent Shaphan the son of Azaliah, and Maaseiah the governor of the city, and Joah the son of Joahaz, the keeper of records, to restore the house of the Lord his God.

**34:9** They came to Hilkiah the high priest; and having received from him the money that had been brought into the house of the Lord, and that the Levites and the gatekeepers had gathered from Manasseh and Ephraim and all the remnant of Israel, and also from all Judah and Benjamin and the inhabitants of Jerusalem,

**34:10** they delivered it into the hands of those who were over the workers in the house of the Lord, so that they might restore the temple and mend whatever was weak.

**34:11** And they gave it to the craftsmen and the masons to buy stones from the quarries, and timber for the joints of the building and for the beamwork of the houses that the kings of Judah had destroyed.

**34:12** They did everything faithfully. Now the overseers of the workers were Jahath and Obadiah of the sons of Merari, Zechariah and Meshullam of the sons of Kohath, who pressed the work forward: all Levites who knew how to play musical instruments.

**34:13** And over those who carried burdens for various uses, there were scribes and officers from the Levites, and gatekeepers.

**34:14** And while they were bringing out the money that had been brought into the temple of the Lord, Hilkiah the priest found the book of the law of the Lord by the hand of Moses.

**34:15** And he said to Shaphan the scribe, "I have found the book of the law in the house of the Lord." And he delivered it to him.

**34:16** And he brought the scroll to the king and reported to him, saying, "Look, everything that you placed in the hand of your servants is being carried out.

**34:17** They have melted down the silver that was found in the house of the Lord, and it has been given to the overseers of the craftsmen and of those who fashion the various works.

**34:18** Moreover, Hilkiah the priest delivered this book to me." And when he had read it aloud in the presence of the king,

**34:19** and the king had heard the words of the law, he tore his garments,

**34:20** and commanded Hilkiah, and Ahikam the son of Shaphan, and Abdon the son of Micah, and also Shaphan the scribe, and Asaiah the king's servant, saying,

**34:21** "Go and pray to the Lord for me, and for the remnant of Israel and Judah, concerning all the words of this book that has been found; for the great fury of the Lord has poured down upon us, because our fathers did not keep the words of the Lord, to do all that is written in this scroll."

**34:22** So Hilkiah and those who had been sent together by the king went to Huldah the prophetess, the wife of Shallum the son of Tokhath, the son of Hasrah, keeper of the garments, who lived in Jerusalem in the Second District; and they spoke to her the words that we have recounted above.

**34:23** And she answered them, "This is what the Lord God of Israel says: Tell the man who sent you to me,

**34:24** 'This is what the Lord says: Look, I will bring evils upon this place and upon its inhabitants, and all the curses that are written in this book which they read before the king of Judah.

**34:25** Because they have abandoned Me and have sacrificed to foreign gods, to provoke Me to anger in all the works of their hands, therefore My fury will pour down upon this place and will not be extinguished.'

**34:26** But to the king of Judah, who sent you to implore the Lord, speak in this way: 'This is what the Lord God of Israel says: Because you have heard the words of the scroll,

**34:27** and your heart was softened, and you humbled yourself in the sight of God concerning the things that were spoken against this place and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and revering My face you tore your garments and wept before Me, I also have heard you, says the Lord.

**34:28** For now I will gather you to your fathers, and you will be carried into your tomb in peace, and your eyes will not see all the evil that I am about to bring upon this place and upon its inhabitants.'" So they reported back to the king everything that she had said.

**34:29** Then he, having summoned all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem,

**34:30** went up into the house of the Lord, and together with him all the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the priests and the Levites, and all the people from the least to the greatest. While they listened in the house of the Lord, the king read all the words of the scroll.

**34:31** And standing on his platform, he made a covenant before the Lord to walk after Him and to keep His commandments, testimonies, and statutes with all his heart and with all his soul, and to do the things that are written in that scroll which he had read.

**34:32** He also placed under oath concerning this all who were found in Jerusalem and Benjamin; and the inhabitants of Jerusalem acted according to the covenant of the Lord God of their fathers.

**34:33** Therefore Josiah removed all the abominations from all the regions of the sons of Israel, and made all who remained in Israel serve the Lord their God. All his days they did not depart from the Lord God of their fathers.

## Translator Notes

- **34:3** — Latin "lucis" ("sacred groves") is the Vulgate's regular rendering of the Hebrew Asherah poles, and in 34:4 "simulacra quae superposita fuerant" ("statues that had been set upon them") corresponds to the incense altars of the Hebrew text.
- **34:9** — Vulgate "acceptamque ab eo pecuniam" ("having received from him the money") depicts the envoys receiving the money from Hilkiah to hand over to the overseers in 34:10, whereas the Hebrew text says they delivered the money to Hilkiah.
- **34:21** — Latin "orate Dominum" ("pray to the Lord") replaces the Hebrew "inquire of the Lord," and "stillavit" (also in 34:25) literally means "dripped" or "dropped down," picturing divine wrath pouring down from above.
- **34:22** — Latin "in Secunda" (literally "in the Second") refers to the Second District or Quarter of Jerusalem, and the closing clause "verba quae supra narravimus" ("the words that we have recounted above") is a distinctive Vulgate narrator's remark where the Hebrew simply has "to this effect."
- **34:31** — Latin "in tribunali suo" ("on his platform," literally "on his tribunal") renders the Hebrew "in his place" or "by his pillar," depicting a raised royal stand; in 34:32 "Adjuravit" ("placed under oath") renders the Hebrew "made to stand."
