## Text

**8:1** Now when twenty years had been completed after Solomon built the house of the Lord and his own house,

**8:2** he built the cities that Hiram had given to Solomon, and he made the sons of Israel dwell there.

**8:3** He also went to Hamath-zobah and took possession of it.

**8:4** And he built Palmyra in the desert, and he built other very strongly fortified cities in Hamath.

**8:5** And he constructed Upper Beth-horon and Lower Beth-horon, walled cities having gates and bars and bolts;

**8:6** also Baalath and all the very strong cities that belonged to Solomon, and all the cities for chariots and the cities for horsemen. Everything that Solomon desired and planned, he built in Jerusalem, and in Lebanon, and in the entire land of his dominion.

**8:7** All the people who had been left from the Hittites, and the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites, who were not of the lineage of Israel,

**8:8** from their sons and from their descendants, whom the sons of Israel had not killed, Solomon subjugated as tributaries, to this day.

**8:9** However, of the sons of Israel he appointed none to serve in the works of the king; for they themselves were fighting men, and chief leaders, and commanders of his chariots and horsemen.

**8:10** Now all the commanders of the army of King Solomon were two hundred fifty, who instructed the people.

**8:11** Now he moved the daughter of Pharaoh from the city of David into the house that he had built for her. For the king said, "My wife will not dwell in the house of David king of Israel, because it has been sanctified, since the ark of the Lord has entered into it."

**8:12** Then Solomon offered burnt offerings to the Lord upon the altar of the Lord, which he had constructed before the portico,

**8:13** so that offerings might be made on it each day according to the command of Moses, on the Sabbaths and on the first days of the month, and on the feast days, three times a year—that is, at the solemn feast of Unleavened Bread, and at the solemn feast of Weeks, and at the solemn feast of Tabernacles.

**8:14** And he appointed, according to the arrangement of his father David, the duties of the priests in their ministries, and the Levites in their order, to praise and minister before the priests according to the rite of each day, and the gatekeepers in their divisions, gate by gate; for so David, the man of God, had commanded.

**8:15** Nor did the priests or the Levites depart from the commands of the king in all that he had commanded, and in the guarding of the treasuries.

**8:16** Solomon had all the expenses prepared from the day on which he laid the foundation of the house of the Lord until the day on which he completed it.

**8:17** Then Solomon went to Ezion-geber and to Elath on the shore of the Red Sea, which is in the land of Edom.

**8:18** And Hiram sent him ships by the hands of his servants, and sailors skilled in the sea; and they went with the servants of Solomon to Ophir, and took from there four hundred fifty talents of gold, and brought it to King Solomon.

## Translator Notes

- **8:4** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew Tadmor by its classical Greco-Roman name, Palmyram ("Palmyra"), and translates the Hebrew store cities as civitates munitissimas ("very strongly fortified cities"; similarly urbes firmissimas in 8:6).
- **8:10** — Latin principes exercitus ("commanders of the army") and qui erudiebant populum ("who instructed the people") are distinctly Vulgate readings where the Hebrew refers to chief officers exercising oversight over the people.
- **8:13** — Latin in calendis literally refers to the Kalends, the first days of each month, corresponding here to the monthly new-moon festivals in the Hebrew text.
- **8:16** — Latin Omnes impensas praeparatas habuit Salomon ("Solomon had all the expenses prepared") differs from the Hebrew, which speaks of all Solomon's work being carried out from the founding to the completion of the house.
