## Text

**29:1** Therefore Hezekiah began to reign when he was twenty-five years old, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem; his mother's name was Abijah, daughter of Zechariah.

**29:2** And he did what was pleasing in the sight of the Lord, according to all that David his father had done.

**29:3** He, in the first year and month of his reign, opened the doors of the house of the Lord and repaired them.

**29:4** And he brought in the priests and the Levites, and gathered them into the eastern square.

**29:5** And he said to them, "Listen to me, Levites, and sanctify yourselves; cleanse the house of the Lord God of your fathers, and remove all uncleanness from the sanctuary.

**29:6** Our fathers have sinned and done evil in the sight of the Lord our God, abandoning Him; they have turned their faces away from the tabernacle of the Lord and turned their backs.

**29:7** They shut the doors that were in the portico, and extinguished the lamps, and did not burn incense, and did not offer burnt offerings in the sanctuary to the God of Israel.

**29:8** Therefore the wrath of the Lord was aroused upon Judah and Jerusalem, and He delivered them over to turmoil, and to destruction, and to hissing, just as you yourselves see with your eyes.

**29:9** Look, our fathers have fallen by the sword; our sons, our daughters, and our wives have been led away captive because of this crime.

**29:10** Now therefore it pleases me that we enter into a covenant with the Lord God of Israel, and He will turn away from us the wrath of His anger.

**29:11** My sons, do not be negligent; the Lord has chosen you to stand before Him, and serve Him, and worship Him, and burn incense to Him."

**29:12** Therefore the Levites arose: Mahath son of Amasai, and Joel son of Azariah, of the sons of Kohath; and of the sons of Merari, Kish son of Abdi, and Azariah son of Jehallelel. And of the sons of Gershom, Joah son of Zimmah, and Eden son of Joah.

**29:13** And of the sons of Elizaphan, Shimri and Jehiel. Also of the sons of Asaph, Zechariah and Mattaniah,

**29:14** as well as of the sons of Heman, Jehiel and Shimei; and of the sons of Jeduthun also, Shemaiah and Uzziel.

**29:15** And they gathered their brothers and sanctified themselves, and entered according to the command of the king and the decree of the Lord, to purify the house of God.

**29:16** The priests also entered the temple of the Lord to sanctify it, and brought out into the vestibule of the house of the Lord all the uncleanness that they had found inside; and the Levites took it and carried it outside to the Kidron brook.

**29:17** Now they began to cleanse on the first day of the first month, and on the eighth day of the same month they entered the portico of the temple of the Lord, and they purified the temple for eight days, and on the sixteenth day of the same month they completed what they had begun.

**29:18** They also went in to King Hezekiah and said to him, "We have sanctified the entire house of the Lord, and the altar of burnt offering and its vessels, as well as the table of showbread with all its vessels,

**29:19** and all the furnishings of the temple, which King Ahaz had defiled during his reign, after he transgressed; and look, all of them are set out before the altar of the Lord."

**29:20** And rising at dawn, King Hezekiah assembled all the leaders of the city and went up to the house of the Lord;

**29:21** and they presented together seven bulls, and seven rams, seven lambs, and seven male goats for a sin offering, for the kingdom, for the sanctuary, for Judah; and he told the priests, the sons of Aaron, to offer them on the altar of the Lord.

**29:22** Therefore they slaughtered the bulls, and the priests received the blood and poured it upon the altar; they also slaughtered the rams and poured their blood upon the altar, and they sacrificed the lambs and poured the blood upon the altar.

**29:23** They brought forward the male goats for the sin offering before the king and the entire multitude, and laid their hands on them;

**29:24** and the priests sacrificed them and sprinkled their blood before the altar for the atonement of all Israel; for the king had commanded that the burnt offering and the sin offering be made for all Israel.

**29:25** He also stationed the Levites in the house of the Lord with cymbals, harps, and lyres, according to the arrangement of King David, and of Gad the seer, and of Nathan the prophet; for indeed it was the command of the Lord through the hand of His prophets.

**29:26** And the Levites stood holding the instruments of David, and the priests the trumpets.

**29:27** And Hezekiah ordered that they offer the burnt offerings on the altar; and while the burnt offerings were being offered, they began to sing praises to the Lord, and to sound the trumpets, and to play loudly on the various instruments that David king of Israel had prepared.

**29:28** Now while the whole crowd was worshiping, the singers and those who held the trumpets were at their duty until the burnt offering was completed.

**29:29** And when the offering was finished, the king and all who were with him bowed down and worshiped.

**29:30** And Hezekiah and the leaders commanded the Levites to praise the Lord with the words of David and of Asaph the seer; and they praised Him with great joy, and bowing the knee, they worshiped.

**29:31** And Hezekiah added these words as well: "You have filled your hands for the Lord; come near, and offer victims and praises in the house of the Lord." Therefore the entire multitude offered sacrifices, and praises, and burnt offerings, with a devoted mind.

**29:32** Furthermore, the number of the burnt offerings that the multitude offered was this: seventy bulls, one hundred rams, and two hundred lambs.

**29:33** And they sanctified to the Lord six hundred oxen and three thousand sheep.

**29:34** However, the priests were few, and were not sufficient to strip off the hides of the burnt offerings; so the Levites their brothers also helped them until the work was completed and the priests were sanctified, for the Levites are sanctified by an easier rite than the priests.

**29:35** Therefore there were very many burnt offerings, the fat portions of the peace offerings, and the drink offerings of the burnt offerings; and the worship of the house of the Lord was completed.

**29:36** And Hezekiah and all the people rejoiced, because the service of the Lord had been fulfilled; for indeed it had seemed good for this to happen suddenly.

## Translator Notes

- **29:13** — Latin reads Jahiel ("Jehiel") in both vv. 13 and 14, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Jeuel" (or "Jeiel") in v. 13 and "Jehiel" in v. 14; similarly in v. 12 Latin Gersom ("Gershom") corresponds to Hebrew "Gershon."
- **29:19** — Latin polluerat ("had defiled") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "had cast aside" (or "rejected").
- **29:31** — "You have filled your hands" renders the literal Latin idiom Implestis manus vestras, denoting consecration to the service of the Lord.
- **29:34** — Latin faciliori ritu sanctificantur ("are sanctified by an easier rite") is a well-known Vulgate-distinct reading; the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "were more upright in heart to sanctify themselves."
- **29:36** — Latin eo quod ministerium Domini esset expletum: de repente quippe hoc fieri placuerat ("because the service of the Lord had been fulfilled; for indeed it had seemed good for this to happen suddenly") differs from the Hebrew "over what God had prepared for the people, because the thing came about suddenly."
