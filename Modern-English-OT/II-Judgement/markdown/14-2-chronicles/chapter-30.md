## Text

**30:1** Hezekiah also sent to all Israel and Judah, and wrote letters to Ephraim and Manasseh, that they should come to the house of the Lord in Jerusalem and keep the Passover to the Lord God of Israel.

**30:2** Therefore, after the king and the princes and the whole assembly of Jerusalem had taken counsel, they decreed to keep the Passover in the second month.

**30:3** For they had not been able to keep it at its proper time, because priests who could suffice had not been sanctified, and the people had not yet been gathered in Jerusalem.

**30:4** And the matter pleased the king and the whole multitude.

**30:5** And they decreed to send messengers throughout all Israel, from Beersheba to Dan, that they should come and keep the Passover to the Lord God of Israel in Jerusalem; for many had not kept it as was prescribed by the law.

**30:6** And the couriers went out with letters by the command of the king and his princes throughout all Israel and Judah, according to what the king had ordered, proclaiming, "Sons of Israel, return to the Lord God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, and He will return to the remnant that has escaped the hand of the king of the Assyrians.

**30:7** Do not become like your fathers and brothers, who departed from the Lord God of their fathers, who gave them over to destruction, as you yourselves see.

**30:8** Do not harden your necks, as your fathers did; yield your hands to the Lord, and come to His sanctuary, which He has sanctified forever; serve the Lord God of your fathers, and the wrath of His fury will be turned away from you.

**30:9** For if you return to the Lord, your brothers and sons will receive mercy before their masters who led them captive, and they will return to this land; for the Lord your God is compassionate and merciful, and He will not turn His face away from you if you return to Him."

**30:10** So the couriers traveled swiftly from city to city through the land of Ephraim and Manasseh as far as Zebulun, while those people laughed at and mocked them.

**30:11** Nevertheless, certain men from Asher, Manasseh, and Zebulun, consenting to the counsel, came to Jerusalem.

**30:12** In Judah, however, the hand of the Lord was present to give them one heart, so that they might carry out the word of the Lord according to the command of the king and the princes.

**30:13** And many people were gathered in Jerusalem to keep the feast of unleavened bread in the second month.

**30:14** And rising up, they destroyed the altars that were in Jerusalem, and overturning all the things on which incense was burned to idols, they threw them into the brook Kidron.

**30:15** Then they sacrificed the Passover on the fourteenth day of the second month. The priests also and the Levites, at last sanctified, offered burnt offerings in the house of the Lord.

**30:16** And they stood in their order according to the ordinance and the law of Moses, the man of God; and the priests received from the hands of the Levites the blood to be poured out,

**30:17** because a large crowd had not been sanctified, and therefore the Levites sacrificed the Passover for those who had not come in time to be sanctified to the Lord.

**30:18** Also a great part of the people from Ephraim, Manasseh, Issachar, and Zebulun, who had not been sanctified, ate the Passover not according to what is written; and Hezekiah prayed for them, saying, "The good Lord will be merciful

**30:19** to all who with their whole heart seek the Lord God of their fathers, and will not count it against them that they were insufficiently sanctified."

**30:20** And the Lord heard him, and was appeased toward the people.

**30:21** And the sons of Israel who were found in Jerusalem kept the feast of unleavened bread for seven days with great gladness, praising the Lord day by day, the Levites also and the priests with instruments that suited their office.

**30:22** And Hezekiah spoke to the heart of all the Levites who had good understanding concerning the Lord. And they ate for the seven days of the feast, sacrificing victims of peace offerings and praising the Lord God of their fathers.

**30:23** And it pleased the whole multitude to celebrate another seven days as well, which they indeed did with immense joy.

**30:24** For Hezekiah, king of Judah, had provided for the multitude a thousand bulls and seven thousand sheep, and the princes had given to the people a thousand bulls and ten thousand sheep; therefore a very great multitude of priests was sanctified.

**30:25** And the entire crowd of Judah was filled with cheerfulness, both the priests and the Levites, and the whole gathering that had come from Israel, as well as the proselytes from the land of Israel and those dwelling in Judah.

**30:26** And there was a great celebration in Jerusalem, such as had not been in that city since the days of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel.

**30:27** Then the priests and the Levites rose up, blessing the people; and their voice was heard, and the prayer reached the holy dwelling place of heaven.

## Translator Notes

- **30:11** — Vulgate 'consenting to the counsel' (acquiescentes consilio), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'humbled themselves'.
- **30:15** — Vulgate 'at last sanctified' (tandem sanctificati), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'were put to shame and sanctified themselves'.
- **30:19** — Vulgate explicitly states 'and will not count it against them that they were insufficiently sanctified' (et non imputabit eis quod minus sanctificati sunt), expanding the briefer Hebrew clause 'though not according to the purification of the sanctuary'.
- **30:20** — Vulgate 'was appeased toward the people' (placatus est populo), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'healed the people'.
