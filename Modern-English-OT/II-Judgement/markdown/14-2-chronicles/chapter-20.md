## Text

**20:1** After these things, the sons of Moab and the sons of Ammon, and with them some of the Ammonites, gathered together against Jehoshaphat to fight against him.

**20:2** And messengers came and reported to Jehoshaphat, saying, "A great multitude has come against you from those places that are beyond the sea, and from Syria; and look, they are stationed in Hazazon-tamar, which is En-gedi."

**20:3** Now Jehoshaphat, terrified with fear, devoted himself wholly to petitioning the Lord, and he proclaimed a fast for all Judah.

**20:4** And Judah gathered together to implore the Lord; indeed, everyone from their cities also came to plead with Him.

**20:5** And when Jehoshaphat had taken his stand in the midst of the assembly of Judah and Jerusalem, in the house of the Lord before the new court,

**20:6** he said, "Lord God of our fathers, You are God in heaven, and You rule over all the kingdoms of the nations; in Your hand are strength and power, and no one can resist You.

**20:7** Did You not, our God, put to death all the inhabitants of this land before Your people Israel, and give it to the seed of Abraham Your friend forever?

**20:8** And they lived in it, and built in it a sanctuary for Your name, saying,

**20:9** 'If evils rush upon us, the sword of judgment, pestilence, and famine, we will stand before this house in Your sight, in which Your name has been invoked, and we will cry out to You in our distresses, and You will hear and save us.'

**20:10** Now therefore, look, the sons of Ammon, and Moab, and Mount Seir, through whom You did not allow Israel to pass when they were coming out of Egypt, but they turned aside from them and did not kill them,

**20:11** act to the contrary, and strive to drive us out of the possession that You handed over to us.

**20:12** Our God, will You not then judge them? Indeed, in us there is not strength great enough that we can resist this multitude that rushes upon us. But since we do not know what we ought to do, we have this alone remaining, to direct our eyes to You."

**20:13** And all Judah was standing before the Lord with their little ones, their wives, and their children.

**20:14** Now there was Jahaziel son of Zechariah, son of Benaiah, son of Jeiel, son of Mattaniah, a Levite of the sons of Asaph, upon whom the Spirit of the Lord came in the midst of the crowd,

**20:15** and he said, "Pay attention, all Judah, and you who live in Jerusalem, and you, King Jehoshaphat: This is what the Lord says to you: 'Do not be afraid, nor be terrified of this multitude, for the battle is not yours, but God's.

**20:16** Tomorrow you will go down against them; for they are about to go up by the ascent named Ziz, and you will find them at the top of the torrent that is opposite the wilderness of Jeruel.

**20:17** It will not be you who fight, but only stand confidently, and you will see the help of the Lord over you, Judah and Jerusalem. Do not be afraid, nor be terrified; tomorrow you will go out against them, and the Lord will be with you.'"

**20:18** Therefore Jehoshaphat, and Judah, and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem fell facedown to the ground before the Lord and worshiped Him.

**20:19** Moreover, the Levites of the sons of Kohath and of the sons of Korah were praising the Lord God of Israel with a loud voice on high.

**20:20** And when they had risen in the morning, they went out through the desert of Tekoa; and as they set out, Jehoshaphat, standing in their midst, said, "Hear me, men of Judah and all inhabitants of Jerusalem: believe in the Lord your God, and you will be secure; believe His prophets, and all things will turn out prosperously."

**20:21** And he gave counsel to the people, and appointed singers of the Lord to praise Him in their companies and to go before the army, and with harmonious voice to say, "Give praise to the Lord, for His mercy is forever."

**20:22** And when they had begun to sing praises, the Lord turned their ambushes against themselves, namely, those of the sons of Ammon, and Moab, and Mount Seir, who had gone out to fight against Judah; and they were struck down.

**20:23** For the sons of Ammon and Moab rose up together against the inhabitants of Mount Seir to kill and destroy them; and when they had carried this out in deed, turning against themselves as well, they fell by mutual wounds.

**20:24** Now when Judah had come to the lookout that faces the wilderness, they saw from afar the whole region far and wide full of corpses, and that no one survived who had been able to escape slaughter.

**20:25** Therefore Jehoshaphat, and all the people with him, came to strip the spoils of the dead; and they found among the corpses various goods, garments as well, and very precious vessels, and they plundered so that they could not carry everything, nor carry away the spoils over three days because of the vastness of the plunder.

**20:26** On the fourth day, however, they gathered together in the Valley of Blessing; for indeed, because they had blessed the Lord there, they called that place Valley of Blessing up to the present day.

**20:27** And every man of Judah, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and Jehoshaphat before them, returned to Jerusalem with great gladness, because the Lord had given them joy over their enemies.

**20:28** And they entered into Jerusalem with harps, and lyres, and trumpets, into the house of the Lord.

**20:29** And the dread of the Lord fell upon all the kingdoms of the lands when they heard that the Lord had fought against the enemies of Israel.

**20:30** And the kingdom of Jehoshaphat was at rest, and God granted him peace all around.

**20:31** So Jehoshaphat reigned over Judah, and he was thirty-five years old when he began to reign; and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother was Azubah daughter of Shilhi.

**20:32** And he walked in the way of his father Asa, and did not turn aside from it, doing what was pleasing before the Lord.

**20:33** Nevertheless, he did not remove the high places, and the people had not yet directed their heart to the Lord God of their fathers.

**20:34** Now the rest of the deeds of Jehoshaphat, first and last, are written in the words of Jehu son of Hanani, which he arranged in the books of the kings of Israel.

**20:35** After these things, Jehoshaphat king of Judah entered into friendship with Ahaziah king of Israel, whose works were most wicked.

**20:36** And he became a partner in making ships that would go to Tarshish, and they made a fleet in Ezion-geber.

**20:37** Then Eliezer son of Dodavahu, from Mareshah, prophesied to Jehoshaphat, saying, "Because you have had a covenant with Ahaziah, the Lord has struck your works." And the ships were shattered, and they could not go to Tarshish.

## Translator Notes

- **20:1** — Vulgate reads "some of the Ammonites" (de Ammonitis), as does the Hebrew Masoretic Text, where the Septuagint reads "Meunites."
- **20:7** — Vulgate reads "put to death" (interfecisti) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "drove out."
- **20:21** — Vulgate reads "gave counsel to the people" (deditque consilium populo) and "in their companies" (in turmis suis), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "took counsel with the people" and "in holy splendor."
- **20:22** — Vulgate specifies that the Lord turned the enemies' own ambushes against themselves (vertit Dominus insidias eorum in semetipsos), whereas the Hebrew text speaks of the Lord setting ambushers against them.
- **20:26** — Vulgate translates the Hebrew place name Beracah literally into Latin as Vallis benedictionis ("Valley of Blessing").
