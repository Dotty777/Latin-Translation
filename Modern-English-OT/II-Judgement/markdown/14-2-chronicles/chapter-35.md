## Text

**35:1** Now Josiah kept a Passover to the Lord in Jerusalem, which was sacrificed on the fourteenth day of the first month.

**35:2** And he appointed the priests in their offices and encouraged them to minister in the house of the Lord.

**35:3** To the Levites also, by whose instruction all Israel was sanctified to the Lord, he said, "Put the ark in the sanctuary of the temple that Solomon the son of David, king of Israel, built, for you will no longer carry it at all; now, however, minister to the Lord your God and to His people Israel.

**35:4** And prepare yourselves according to your houses and clans, in the divisions of each one, just as David king of Israel commanded and Solomon his son wrote down.

**35:5** And minister in the sanctuary by families and Levitical companies,

**35:6** and being sanctified, sacrifice the Passover; and also prepare your brothers, so that they may be able to act according to the words that the Lord spoke by the hand of Moses."

**35:7** Moreover, Josiah gave to all the people who had been found there at the festival of the Passover lambs and young goats from the flocks and of the rest of the small livestock, thirty thousand, and also three thousand oxen; these were from all the king's possessions.

**35:8** His leaders also willingly offered what they had vowed, both to the people and to the priests and the Levites. Furthermore, Hilkiah, Zechariah, and Jehiel, the rulers of the house of the Lord, gave to the priests for keeping the Passover small livestock mixed together, two thousand six hundred, and three hundred oxen.

**35:9** And Conaniah, and Shemaiah and also Nethanel his brothers, as well as Hashabiah, Jehiel, and Jozabad, the rulers of the Levites, gave to the rest of the Levites for celebrating the Passover five thousand small livestock and five hundred oxen.

**35:10** And the ministry was prepared, and the priests stood in their office, and the Levites also in companies, according to the king's command.

**35:11** And the Passover was sacrificed; and the priests sprinkled the blood with their hand, and the Levites stripped off the skins of the burnt offerings.

**35:12** And they set them apart to give them by the houses and families of each one, so that they might be offered to the Lord, just as is written in the book of Moses; and they did likewise with the oxen also.

**35:13** And they roasted the Passover over the fire, according to what is written in the law; while the peace offerings they boiled in cauldrons, kettles, and pots, and hastily distributed them to the whole populace.

**35:14** For themselves and for the priests, however, they prepared afterward, for the priests were occupied until night in the offering of the burnt offerings and the fat; therefore the Levites prepared last of all for themselves and for the priests, the sons of Aaron.

**35:15** Furthermore, the singers, the sons of Asaph, stood in their order according to the command of David, and of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun, the prophets of the king; and the gatekeepers kept watch at each gate, so that they did not depart from their ministry even for a moment; for this reason their brothers the Levites also prepared food for them.

**35:16** Therefore all the worship of the Lord was duly completed on that day, to keep the Passover and to offer burnt offerings on the altar of the Lord, according to the command of King Josiah.

**35:17** And the sons of Israel who had been found there kept the Passover at that time, and the festival of unleavened bread for seven days.

**35:18** There was no Passover like this in Israel from the days of Samuel the prophet; nor did any of all the kings of Israel keep a Passover like Josiah did for the priests, and the Levites, and all Judah and Israel who had been found, and those living in Jerusalem.

**35:19** In the eighteenth year of the reign of Josiah this Passover was celebrated.

**35:20** After Josiah had restored the temple, Necho king of Egypt went up to fight at Carchemish by the Euphrates, and Josiah went out to meet him.

**35:21** But he, having sent messengers to him, said, "What have I to do with you, king of Judah? I am not coming against you today, but I am fighting against another house, to which God has commanded me to go in haste; cease acting against God, who is with me, so that He may not kill you."

**35:22** Josiah refused to turn back, but prepared war against him, and did not yield to the words of Necho from the mouth of God; instead, he proceeded to fight in the plain of Megiddo.

**35:23** And there, wounded by the archers, he said to his servants, "Take me out of the battle, for I am severely wounded."

**35:24** They transferred him from the chariot into another chariot, which followed him according to royal custom, and carried him away to Jerusalem; and he died and was buried in the mausoleum of his fathers, and all Judah and Jerusalem mourned for him.

**35:25** Jeremiah most of all: all the male and female singers repeat his lamentations over Josiah up to the present day, and it has prevailed like a law in Israel. Look, it is said to be written in the Lamentations.

**35:26** Now the rest of the words of Josiah, and of his mercies, which are commanded by the law of the Lord,

**35:27** and also his works, first and last, are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **35:3** — Latin "ad quorum eruditionem omnis Israel sanctificabatur Domino" ("by whose instruction all Israel was sanctified to the Lord") makes all Israel the passive subject of sanctification, whereas the Hebrew text describes the Levites themselves as teachers of Israel who were holy to the Lord.
- **35:15** — Latin "prophetarum regis" ("the prophets of the king") is plural in the Vulgate, applying to Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun together, whereas the Hebrew singular ("the king's seer") modifies Jeduthun alone.
- **35:18** — The Vulgate places "sacerdotibus ... omni Judae ... et habitantibus" in the dative case ("for the priests ... all Judah ... and those living in Jerusalem"), presenting Josiah as keeping the Passover on their behalf rather than coordinating them as co-subjects with Josiah as in the Hebrew.
- **35:22** — Vulgate "praeparavit contra eum bellum" ("prepared war against him") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which states that Josiah disguised himself to fight Necho.
- **35:25** — Latin "Jeremias maxime" ("Jeremiah most of all") continues the verb "mourned" from 35:24, and "Ecce scriptum fertur in lamentationibus" ("Look, it is said to be written in the Lamentations") uses "fertur" ("is reported" or "handed down") where the Hebrew simply states that they are written in the laments.
