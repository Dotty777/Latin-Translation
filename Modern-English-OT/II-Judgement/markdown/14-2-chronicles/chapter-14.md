## Text

**14:1** Now Asa did what was good and pleasing in the sight of his God, and he overturned the altars of foreign worship and the high places.

**14:2** And he shattered the statues and cut down the groves,

**14:3** and he commanded Judah to seek the Lord God of their fathers, and to carry out the law and all the commandments;

**14:4** and he removed from all the cities of Judah the altars and the shrines, and he reigned in peace.

**14:5** He also built fortified cities in Judah, because he was undisturbed, and no wars had arisen in his times, with the Lord granting peace.

**14:6** Now he said to Judah, "Let us build these cities, and surround them with walls, and strengthen them with towers, gates, and bars, while everything is quiet from wars, because we have sought the Lord God of our fathers, and He has given us peace all around." So they built, and there was no hindrance in building.

**14:7** Now Asa had in his army three hundred thousand from Judah who carried shields and spears, and from Benjamin two hundred eighty thousand shield-bearers and archers; all of these were very valiant men.

**14:8** Now Zerah the Ethiopian went out against them with his army of one million, and with three hundred chariots; and he came as far as Mareshah.

**14:9** Then Asa went forward to meet him and drew up the ranks for war in the valley of Zephathah, which is near Mareshah;

**14:10** and he called upon the Lord God and said, "Lord, there is no distinction at all with You whether You assist with few or with many. Help us, Lord our God, for having confidence in You and in Your name, we have come against this multitude. Lord, You are our God; may no man prevail against You."

**14:11** Therefore the Lord terrified the Ethiopians before Asa and Judah, and the Ethiopians fled.

**14:12** And Asa and the people who were with him pursued them as far as Gerar; and the Ethiopians fell to utter destruction, because they were crushed as the Lord struck them down and His army fought. So they carried off great spoils,

**14:13** and they struck all the cities around Gerar, for a great terror had seized them all; and they pillaged the cities and carried away much plunder.

**14:14** And destroying the sheepfolds as well, they carried off a countless multitude of livestock and camels, and returned to Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — In the Clementine Vulgate, chapter 14 begins at verse 2 of the Hebrew and modern English numbering (since Hebrew 14:1 stands at Vulgate 13:23). The Vulgate also reads 'his God' (Dei sui) and 'altars of foreign worship' (altaria peregrini cultus).
- **14:10** — The Vulgate reads 'whether You assist with few or with many' (utrum in paucis auxilieris, an in pluribus), whereas the Hebrew reads 'between the mighty and the one who has no strength.'
- **14:11** — The Vulgate reads 'terrified' (Exterruit, echoing the 'great terror' of 14:13, where Latin omits 'of the Lord'), whereas the Hebrew reads 'struck down' or 'defeated.'
