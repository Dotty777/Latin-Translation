## Text

**2:1** Now Solomon resolved to build a house for the name of the Lord, and a palace for himself.

**2:2** And he counted seventy thousand men carrying loads on their shoulders, and eighty thousand to cut stones in the mountains, and three thousand six hundred as their overseers.

**2:3** He also sent to Hiram, king of Tyre, saying, "Just as you dealt with David my father, and sent him cedar timber so that he might build a house for himself in which he also lived,

**2:4** so deal with me, that I may build a house for the name of the Lord my God, to consecrate it for burning incense before Him, and for sending up fragrant spices in smoke, and for the perpetual presentation of the loaves, and for burnt offerings morning and evening, on the Sabbaths also, and on the new moons, and on the solemn feasts of the Lord our God forever, which have been commanded to Israel.

**2:5** For the house that I desire to build is great; for our God is great above all gods.

**2:6** Who then will be able to prevail so as to build a worthy house for Him? If heaven and the heavens of heavens cannot contain Him, how great am I that I should be able to build a house for Him? But for this only, that incense may be burned before Him.

**2:7** Send me therefore a skilled man who knows how to work in gold and silver, bronze and iron, purple, crimson, and blue, and who knows how to carve engravings with these craftsmen whom I have with me in Judea and Jerusalem, whom David my father prepared.

**2:8** Send me also cedar, juniper, and pine timber from Lebanon; for I know that your servants know how to cut timber from Lebanon, and my servants will be with your servants,

**2:9** so that very much timber may be prepared for me. For the house that I desire to build is exceedingly great and renowned.

**2:10** Furthermore, to the workmen who are to cut the timber, your servants, I will give as provisions twenty thousand kors of wheat, and as many kors of barley, and twenty thousand metretes of wine, and also twenty thousand seahs of oil."

**2:11** Then Hiram, king of Tyre, said in a letter that he had sent to Solomon, "Because the Lord loved His people, for that reason He made you reign over them."

**2:12** And he added, saying, "Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who made heaven and earth, who has given King David a wise, learned, discerning, and prudent son to build a house for the Lord and a palace for himself.

**2:13** Therefore I have sent you a prudent and most knowledgeable man, Hiram my father,

**2:14** the son of a woman from the daughters of Dan, whose father was a Tyrian, who knows how to work in gold and silver, bronze and iron, marble and wood, in purple also, and blue, and fine linen, and crimson; and who knows how to engrave every carving and to devise prudently whatever is necessary in the work, with your craftsmen and with the craftsmen of my lord David, your father.

**2:15** Therefore send to your servants the wheat and barley, and the oil and wine that you promised, my lord.

**2:16** As for us, we will cut timber from Lebanon, as much as you need, and we will bring it in rafts by sea to Joppa; and it will be yours to transport it to Jerusalem."

**2:17** So Solomon counted all the proselyte men who were in the land of Israel, after the numbering that David his father had conducted, and there were found one hundred fifty thousand, and three thousand six hundred.

**2:18** And from them he assigned seventy thousand to carry burdens on their shoulders, and eighty thousand to cut stones in the mountains, and three thousand six hundred as overseers of the works of the people.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — In the Vulgate chapter division, 1:18 corresponds to 2:1 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text (shifting the verse numbers of chapter 2 by one); Latin also renders Hebrew 'house for his kingdom' as palatium ('palace'). Native Vulgate 2 Chronicles 2:1 maps to Saints.Bible 1:18 (2:2-18 to 2:1-17), and native 14:1 maps to Saints.Bible 13:23 (14:2-15 to 14:1-14).
- **2:8** — Latin renders the three kinds of Lebanon timber as cedrina (cedar), arceuthina (juniper), and pinea (pine), whereas the Hebrew lists cedar, cypress, and algum trees.
- **2:10** — Latin uses three distinct units of measure—coros (kors) for wheat and barley, metretas (metretes, an ancient liquid measure) for wine, and sata (seahs) for oil—where the Hebrew uses baths for both wine and oil.
- **2:13** — Latin renders Hebrew Huram-abi literally as Hiram patrem meum ('Hiram my father'), reflecting an honorific title for a master craftsman while adopting the spelling Hiram.
- **2:14** — Latin specifies marmore ('marble,' as also in 3:6) where the Hebrew reads 'stones.'
- **2:17** — Latin renders Hebrew 'resident aliens' with the Septuagint-influenced term proselytos ('proselytes').
