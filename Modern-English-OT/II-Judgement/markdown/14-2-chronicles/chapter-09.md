## Text

**9:1** The queen of Sheba also, when she had heard of the fame of Solomon, came to test him with riddles in Jerusalem, with great wealth and camels that carried spices, and a very great amount of gold, and precious gems. And when she had come to Solomon, she spoke to him of all that was in her heart.

**9:2** And Solomon explained to her all that she had put forward; and there was nothing that he did not make clear to her.

**9:3** After she saw these things—namely, the wisdom of Solomon, and the house that he had built,

**9:4** and also the food of his table, and the dwellings of the servants, and the duties of his attendants and their garments, the cupbearers also and their robes, and the sacrifices that he offered in the house of the Lord—there was no longer any spirit in her for astonishment.

**9:5** And she said to the king, "The report is true that I had heard in my land concerning your virtues and your wisdom.

**9:6** I did not believe those reporting it until I myself had come, and my eyes had seen, and I had verified that scarcely half of your wisdom had been told to me; you have surpassed the fame by your virtues.

**9:7** Blessed are your men, and blessed are your servants, who stand before you at all times and hear your wisdom.

**9:8** Blessed be the Lord your God, who willed to appoint you upon His throne, king of the Lord your God. Because God loves Israel and wills to preserve them forever, therefore He has placed you over them as king, so that you may execute judgments and justice."

**9:9** And she gave the king one hundred twenty talents of gold, and exceedingly many spices, and most precious gems; there were no spices such as these that the queen of Sheba gave to King Solomon.

**9:10** Moreover, the servants of Hiram with the servants of Solomon brought gold from Ophir, and thyine wood, and most precious gems,

**9:11** from which the king made—that is, from the thyine wood—steps in the house of the Lord and in the royal house, and also lyres and harps for the singers; such wood was never seen in the land of Judah.

**9:12** And King Solomon gave to the queen of Sheba all that she desired and asked for, and much more than she had brought to him; and she turned back and went to her own land with her servants.

**9:13** Now the weight of the gold that was brought to Solomon each year was six hundred sixty-six talents of gold,

**9:14** apart from that sum which the envoys of various nations and the merchants were accustomed to bring, and all the kings of Arabia and the satraps of the lands, who brought in gold and silver to Solomon.

**9:15** Therefore King Solomon made two hundred golden spears from a sum of six hundred gold pieces, which were spent on each spear;

**9:16** three hundred golden shields also, of three hundred gold pieces, with which each shield was covered; and the king placed them in the armory, which was planted with a grove.

**9:17** The king also made a great ivory throne, and clothed it with the purest gold;

**9:18** six steps also, by which one ascended to the throne, and a golden footstool, and two armrests on either side, and two lions standing beside the armrests,

**9:19** and also twelve other small lions standing upon the six steps on each side; there was no such throne in all the kingdoms.

**9:20** All the vessels of the king's banquet also were of gold, and the vessels of the house of the forest of Lebanon were of the purest gold. For in those days silver was regarded as nothing.

**9:21** For the ships of the king went to Tarshish with the servants of Hiram once in three years, and they brought from there gold, and silver, and ivory, and apes, and peacocks.

**9:22** Therefore Solomon was magnified above all the kings of the earth for riches and glory.

**9:23** And all the kings of the lands longed to see the face of Solomon, so that they might hear the wisdom that God had put in his heart;

**9:24** and they brought him gifts—vessels of silver and of gold, and garments, and weapons, and spices, horses, and mules—each year.

**9:25** Solomon also had forty thousand horses in stables, and twelve thousand chariots and horsemen; and he stationed them in the cities for chariots, and where the king was in Jerusalem.

**9:26** He also exercised dominion over all the kings from the river Euphrates to the land of the Philistines, and to the borders of Egypt.

**9:27** And he provided as great an abundance of silver in Jerusalem as of stones, and as great a multitude of cedars as of the sycamores that grow in the plains.

**9:28** And horses were brought to him from Egypt and from all the regions.

**9:29** Now the rest of the works of Solomon, the first and the last, are written in the words of Nathan the prophet, and in the books of Ahijah the Shilonite, and also in the vision of Iddo the seer against Jeroboam the son of Nebat.

**9:30** And Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel for forty years.

**9:31** And he slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David; and Rehoboam his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **9:10** — Latin ligna thyina ("thyine wood," an aromatic North African wood prized in antiquity) is the Vulgate rendering for Hebrew algum wood here and in 9:11.
- **9:15** — The Vulgate reads hastas aureas ("golden spears") and sexcentorum aureorum ("six hundred gold pieces") where the Hebrew describes large shields made with six hundred shekels of gold.
- **9:16** — Latin in armentario, quod erat consitum nemore ("in the armory, which was planted with a grove," with armentario as the Clementine form of armamentario) is Jerome's rendering of the House of the Forest of Lebanon, translated literally in 9:20 as domus saltus Libani.
- **9:22** — Latin gloria ("glory") is a Vulgate-distinct reading where the Hebrew text reads "wisdom."
- **9:25** — The Vulgate reads quadraginta millia ("forty thousand") horses in stables and twelve thousand chariots and horsemen, matching the higher figure of 1 Kings 4:26 rather than the four thousand stalls of the Hebrew text of 2 Chronicles.
