## Text

**27:1** Jotham was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned for sixteen years in Jerusalem; his mother's name was Jerushah, daughter of Zadok.

**27:2** And he did what was right before the Lord, according to all that Uzziah his father had done, except that he did not enter the temple of the Lord; and the people were still sinning.

**27:3** He himself built the high gate of the house of the Lord, and he constructed many things on the wall of Ophel.

**27:4** He also built cities in the mountains of Judah, and fortresses and towers in the forests.

**27:5** He himself fought against the king of the sons of Ammon and defeated them, and at that time the sons of Ammon gave him one hundred talents of silver, and ten thousand kors of wheat, and just as many kors of barley; the sons of Ammon supplied these things to him in the second and third year.

**27:6** And Jotham was strengthened, because he had directed his ways before the Lord his God.

**27:7** Now the rest of the accounts of Jotham, and all his battles and works, are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah.

**27:8** He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned for sixteen years in Jerusalem.

**27:9** And Jotham slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David; and Ahaz his son reigned in his place.

## Translator Notes

- **27:7** — The Vulgate reads "and works" (et opera), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "and his ways."
