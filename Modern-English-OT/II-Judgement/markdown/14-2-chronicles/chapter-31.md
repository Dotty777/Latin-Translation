## Text

**31:1** And when these things had been duly celebrated, all Israel who had been found in the cities of Judah went out, and they broke the images, and cut down the groves, demolished the high places, and destroyed the altars, not only from all Judah and Benjamin, but also from Ephraim and Manasseh, until they had completely overthrown them; and all the sons of Israel returned to their possessions and their cities.

**31:2** Now Hezekiah appointed the priestly and Levitical companies according to their divisions, each one in his own office, namely both of the priests and of the Levites, for burnt offerings and peace offerings, to minister and to give thanks and to sing in the gates of the camps of the Lord.

**31:3** And the king's portion was that from his own property the burnt offering should be offered, always in the morning and in the evening, on the Sabbaths also, and on the first days of the months, and on the other feasts, as it is written in the law of Moses.

**31:4** He also commanded the people dwelling in Jerusalem to give the portions to the priests and the Levites, so that they might devote themselves to the law of the Lord.

**31:5** And when this had spread abroad in the ears of the multitude, the sons of Israel offered abundant firstfruits of grain, wine, and oil; of honey also, and of all things that the ground produces, they offered tithes.

**31:6** Moreover, the sons of Israel and Judah who lived in the cities of Judah offered tithes of oxen and sheep, and tithes of the holy things that they had vowed to the Lord their God; and bringing them all, they made very many heaps.

**31:7** In the third month they began to lay the foundations of the heaps, and in the seventh month they completed them.

**31:8** And when Hezekiah and his princes had come in, they saw the heaps, and they blessed the Lord and the people of Israel.

**31:9** And Hezekiah asked the priests and the Levites why the heaps were lying like that.

**31:10** Azariah, the chief priest of the lineage of Zadok, answered him, saying, "Since the firstfruits began to be offered in the house of the Lord, we have eaten and been satisfied, and very much has remained, because the Lord has blessed His people; and this that you see is the abundance of what remains."

**31:11** Therefore Hezekiah commanded that they prepare storerooms in the house of the Lord. And when they had done this,

**31:12** they faithfully brought in both the firstfruits and the tithes, and whatever they had vowed. And Conaniah the Levite was overseer over them, and Shimei his brother was second,

**31:13** after whom Jehiel, Azariah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad also, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath, and Benaiah were supervisors under the hands of Conaniah and Shimei his brother, by the command of King Hezekiah and of Azariah, the high priest of the house of God, to whom all things pertained.

**31:14** Now Kore, the son of Imnah, a Levite and gatekeeper of the eastern gate, was in charge of the things that were freely offered to the Lord, and of the firstfruits and the things consecrated for the Holy of Holies.

**31:15** And under his care Eden, Benjamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah also, and Shecaniah were in the cities of the priests, to faithfully distribute portions to their brothers, both small and great:

**31:16** apart from the males from three years old and upward, all who entered the temple of the Lord, and whatever was needed day by day in the ministry and the observances according to their divisions,

**31:17** to the priests by their families, and to the Levites from the twentieth year and upward, by their orders and companies,

**31:18** and to the whole multitude, both their wives and their children of both sexes, food from the things that had been sanctified was faithfully provided.

**31:19** And also throughout the fields of the sons of Aaron and the outskirts of each city, men were stationed to distribute portions to every male among the priests and the Levites.

**31:20** Therefore Hezekiah did all the things that we have said throughout all Judah; and he performed what was good and right and true before the Lord his God,

**31:21** in all the worship of the ministry of the house of the Lord, according to the law and the ceremonies, desiring to seek his God with his whole heart; and he did so, and prospered.

## Translator Notes

- **31:1** — Vulgate 'groves' (lucos) is Jerome's standard Latin rendering of the Hebrew Asherah poles (asherim), and 'images' (simulacra) corresponds to the Hebrew sacred pillars.
- **31:3** — Vulgate 'first days of the months' renders Latin calendis (Kalends, corresponding to the new-moon observances), and reads 'in the law of Moses' (in lege Moysi) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'in the law of the Lord'.
- **31:13** — Vulgate reads 'Azariah' (Azarias) for the second supervisor, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'Azaziah'; the final clause 'to whom all things pertained' (ad quos omnia pertinebant) is a Vulgate explanatory addition.
- **31:15** — Vulgate reads 'Benjamin' (Benjamin), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'Miniamin'.
- **31:20** — Vulgate 'all the things that we have said' (universa quae diximus) introduces a first-person-plural narratorial phrase not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text ('thus Hezekiah did').
