## Text

**24:1** And Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shechem, and called the elders, and the leaders, and the judges, and the officers; and they stood in the presence of the Lord,

**24:2** and he spoke to the people in this way: "This is what the Lord God of Israel says: 'Beyond the river your fathers lived from the beginning, Terah, the father of Abraham and Nahor; and they served foreign gods.

**24:3** So I took your father Abraham from the borders of Mesopotamia, and brought him into the land of Canaan, and multiplied his offspring,

**24:4** and I gave him Isaac; and to him in turn I gave Jacob and Esau. Of these, to Esau I gave Mount Seir to possess; but Jacob and his sons went down into Egypt.

**24:5** And I sent Moses and Aaron, and I struck Egypt with many signs and wonders.

**24:6** And I brought you and your fathers out of Egypt, and you came to the sea; and the Egyptians pursued your fathers with chariots and cavalry, as far as the Red Sea.

**24:7** And the sons of Israel cried out to the Lord, who placed darkness between you and the Egyptians, and brought the sea upon them, and covered them. Your eyes saw everything that I did in Egypt, and you lived in the wilderness for a long time;

**24:8** and I brought you into the land of the Amorite, who lived beyond the Jordan. And when they fought against you, I handed them over into your hands, and you possessed their land, and killed them.

**24:9** Then Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, arose and fought against Israel. And he sent and called Balaam son of Beor, so that he might curse you;

**24:10** and I was unwilling to listen to him, but on the contrary, through him I blessed you, and I delivered you from his hand.

**24:11** And you crossed the Jordan, and came to Jericho. And the men of that city fought against you, the Amorite and the Perizzite, and the Canaanite, and the Hittite, and the Girgashite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite; and I handed them over into your hands.

**24:12** And I sent hornets before you, and I drove them out from their places, the two kings of the Amorites, not by your sword nor by your bow.

**24:13** And I gave you a land on which you did not labor, and cities that you did not build, so that you might live in them; vineyards and olive groves that you did not plant.'

**24:14** So now fear the Lord, and serve Him with a whole and most true heart; and remove the gods that your fathers served in Mesopotamia and in Egypt, and serve the Lord.

**24:15** If, however, serving the Lord seems evil to you, the choice is given to you: choose today what pleases you, whom above all you ought to serve, whether the gods that your fathers served in Mesopotamia, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land you live; but I and my house will serve the Lord."

**24:16** And the people answered and said, "Far be it from us to abandon the Lord and serve foreign gods.

**24:17** The Lord our God, He Himself brought us and our fathers out of the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery; and He did mighty signs while we watched, and guarded us along the whole way by which we walked, and among all the peoples through whom we passed.

**24:18** And He drove out all the nations, the Amorite, the inhabitant of the land that we entered. Therefore we will serve the Lord, because He Himself is our God."

**24:19** And Joshua said to the people, "You will not be able to serve the Lord, for He is a holy God, strong and jealous; nor will He forgive your wicked deeds and sins.

**24:20** If you abandon the Lord and serve foreign gods, He will turn and afflict you and overthrow you, after He has granted good things to you."

**24:21** And the people said to Joshua, "By no means will it be as you say, but we will serve the Lord."

**24:22** And Joshua said to the people, "You are witnesses that you yourselves have chosen the Lord for yourselves, to serve Him." And they answered, "Witnesses."

**24:23** "So now," he said, "remove the foreign gods from among you, and incline your hearts to the Lord God of Israel."

**24:24** And the people said to Joshua, "We will serve the Lord our God, and we will be obedient to His precepts."

**24:25** So Joshua made a covenant on that day, and set before the people precepts and ordinances in Shechem.

**24:26** He also wrote all these words in the scroll of the law of the Lord; and he took a very large stone, and placed it beneath the oak that was in the sanctuary of the Lord,

**24:27** and said to all the people, "Look, this stone will be a testimony to you, because it has heard all the words of the Lord that He spoke to you, so that afterward you may not wish to deny it and lie to the Lord your God."

**24:28** And he sent the people away, each to his own possession.

**24:29** And Israel served the Lord all the days of Joshua and of the elders who lived a long time after Joshua, and who knew all the works of the Lord that He had done in Israel.

**24:30** And after these things Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the Lord, died, being one hundred and ten years old;

**24:31** and they buried him within the borders of his possession in Timnath-serah, which is situated in Mount Ephraim, on the northern side of Mount Gaash.

**24:32** Also, the bones of Joseph, which the sons of Israel had brought from Egypt, they buried in Shechem, in the portion of the field that Jacob had bought from the sons of Hamor, the father of Shechem, for one hundred young sheep; and it became the possession of the sons of Joseph.

**24:33** Eleazar son of Aaron also died, and they buried him in Gibeah of Phinehas his son, which was given to him in Mount Ephraim.

## Translator Notes

- **24:3** — The Vulgate explicitly names Mesopotamia (de Mesopotamiae finibus here, and in Mesopotamia in vv. 14-15), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "beyond the River" (the Euphrates).
- **24:5** — The Vulgate reads "with many signs and wonders" (multis signis atque portentis), whereas the Hebrew reads "according to what I did in its midst, and afterward I brought you out."
- **24:15** — The Vulgate adds "the choice is given to you" (optio vobis datur) and expands the Hebrew "choose for yourselves today whom you will serve" to "choose today what pleases you, whom above all you ought to serve."
- **24:29** — Following the Septuagint order, the Clementine Vulgate places Israel's continued service to the Lord in v. 29 and Joshua's death and burial in vv. 30-31, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text places Joshua's death and burial in vv. 29-30 and Israel's service in v. 31.
- **24:32** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate renders the purchase price as "one hundred young sheep" (centum novellis ovibus), where the Hebrew has "one hundred qesitahs" (an ancient unit of weight or money); in v. 33 the Vulgate similarly treats Gabaath (Gibeah) as a proper place name rather than the common noun "hill."
