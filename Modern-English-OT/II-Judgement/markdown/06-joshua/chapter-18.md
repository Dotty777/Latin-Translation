## Text

**18:1** And all the sons of Israel were assembled at Shiloh, and there they set up the tabernacle of the testimony, and the land was subject to them.

**18:2** Now there had remained seven tribes of the sons of Israel that had not yet received their possessions.

**18:3** To them Joshua said, "How long will you languish in idleness and not enter to possess the land that the Lord God of your fathers has given you?

**18:4** Choose three men from each tribe, so that I may send them, and they may set out and go around the land, and describe it according to the number of each one's multitude, and bring back to me what they have described.

**18:5** Divide the land for yourselves into seven parts: let Judah be in its borders on the southern side, and the house of Joseph on the north.

**18:6** Describe the middle land between these in seven parts, and you will come here to me, so that I may cast the lot for you here before the Lord your God,

**18:7** because there is no portion for the Levites among you, but the priesthood of the Lord is their inheritance. And Gad and Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, had already received their possessions across the Jordan on the eastern side, which Moses the servant of the Lord gave them."

**18:8** And when the men had risen to set out to describe the land, Joshua commanded them, saying, "Go around the land and describe it, and return to me, so that here before the Lord, at Shiloh, I may cast the lot for you."

**18:9** Therefore they set out, and surveying it, they divided it into seven parts, writing in a scroll. And they returned to Joshua at the camp of Shiloh.

**18:10** And he cast lots before the Lord at Shiloh, and divided the land for the sons of Israel into seven parts.

**18:11** And the first lot of the sons of Benjamin came up by their families, so that they would possess the land between the sons of Judah and the sons of Joseph.

**18:12** And their border on the north was from the Jordan, proceeding along the flank of Jericho on the northern side, and from there going up toward the west to the hill country, and reaching the wilderness of Beth-aven,

**18:13** and passing along beside Luz to the south, that is, Bethel; and it goes down to Ataroth-addar, to the mountain that is to the south of Lower Beth-horon,

**18:14** and it bends, going around toward the sea to the south of the mountain that faces Beth-horon toward the southwest; and its extremities are at Kiriath-baal, which is also called Kiriath-jearim, a city of the sons of Judah. This is the side toward the sea, to the west.

**18:15** Now on the south, from the side of Kiriath-jearim, the border goes out toward the sea, and reaches as far as the spring of the waters of Nephtoah.

**18:16** And it goes down to the part of the mountain that faces the valley of the sons of Hinnom, and it is toward the northern side at the farthest part of the valley of Rephaim. And it goes down to Gehennom (that is, the valley of Hinnom) along the flank of the Jebusite to the south, and reaches the spring of Rogel,

**18:17** passing over to the north, and going out to En-shemesh, that is, the spring of the sun,

**18:18** and it passes along as far as the mounds that are opposite the ascent of Adummim; and it goes down to Eben-bohan, that is, the stone of Bohan the son of Reuben; and it passes along on the northern flank to the plains, and goes down into the plain,

**18:19** and passes by on the north of Beth-hoglah; and its extremities are opposite the tongue of the Saltiest Sea on the north, at the end of the Jordan on the southern side,

**18:20** which is its border on the east. This is the possession of the sons of Benjamin by their borders all around, and their families.

**18:21** And its cities were Jericho, and Beth-hoglah, and the valley of Keziz,

**18:22** Beth-arabah, and Zemaraim, and Bethel,

**18:23** and Avvim, and Parah, and Ophrah,

**18:24** the village of Ammonah, and Ophni, and Geba: twelve cities, and their villages.

**18:25** Gibeon, and Ramah, and Beeroth,

**18:26** and Mizpeh, and Chephirah, and Mozah,

**18:27** and Rekem, Irpeel, and Taralah,

**18:28** and Zela, Eleph, and Jebus, which is Jerusalem, Gibeath, and Kiriath: fourteen cities, and their villages. This is the possession of the sons of Benjamin according to their families.

## Translator Notes

- **18:1** — Latin tabernaculum testimonii ('the tabernacle of the testimony') renders Hebrew ohel mo'ed ('tent of meeting'), a frequent Vulgate translation.
- **18:4** — Latin reads juxta numerum uniuscujusque multitudinis ('according to the number of each one's multitude'), whereas Hebrew reads 'according to their inheritance'; similarly in 18:6 Latin specifies Mediam inter hos terram ('the middle land between these').
- **18:11** — Latin specifies sors prima ('the first lot') and uses a purpose clause (ut possiderent terram) where Hebrew has 'the lot of the tribe of the sons of Benjamin came up, and the border of their lot went out.'
- **18:16** — Latin has plural filiorum Ennom ('sons of Hinnom,' vs. Hebrew singular 'son of Hinnom') and includes an explanatory gloss (id est, vallem Ennom) after Geennom, just as it glosses Ensemes in 18:17 and Abenboen in 18:18.
- **18:18** — Latin translates Hebrew Gelilot as tumulos ('mounds') and ha-Aravah as campestria and planitiem ('plains' and 'plain'), and in 18:21 and 18:24 translates Hebrew Emek and Kefar as vallis ('valley') and villa ('village').
