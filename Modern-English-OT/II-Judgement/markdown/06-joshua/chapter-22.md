## Text

**22:1** At that same time Joshua summoned the Reubenites, and the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh,

**22:2** and said to them, "You have done all that Moses the servant of the Lord commanded you; you have also obeyed me in all things,

**22:3** nor have you abandoned your brothers for a long time, until the present day, keeping the command of the Lord your God.

**22:4** Therefore, because the Lord your God has given your brothers rest and peace, just as He promised, return and go to your tents, and to the land of possession that Moses the servant of the Lord delivered to you across the Jordan,

**22:5** provided only that you carefully keep and fulfill in deed the commandment and the law that Moses the servant of the Lord commanded you: to love the Lord your God, and to walk in all His ways, and to observe His commandments, and to cling to Him and serve Him with all your heart and with all your soul."

**22:6** And Joshua blessed them and sent them away. And they returned to their tents.

**22:7** Now Moses had given a possession in Bashan to half the tribe of Manasseh, and therefore to the half that remained Joshua gave an allotment among their other brothers across the Jordan on the western side. And as he was sending them away to their tents and had blessed them,

**22:8** he said to them, "Return to your dwellings with much property and riches, with silver and gold, bronze and iron, and abundant clothing; divide the plunder of the enemies with your brothers."

**22:9** And the sons of Reuben, and the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh returned and departed from the sons of Israel from Shiloh, which is situated in Canaan, to enter Gilead, the land of their possession, which they had obtained according to the command of the Lord by the hand of Moses.

**22:10** And when they had come to the mounds of the Jordan in the land of Canaan, they built beside the Jordan an altar of immense size.

**22:11** And when the sons of Israel heard this, and reliable messengers had reported to them that the sons of Reuben, and of Gad, and of the half-tribe of Manasseh had built an altar in the land of Canaan, upon the mounds of the Jordan, opposite the sons of Israel,

**22:12** they all gathered together at Shiloh to go up and fight against them.

**22:13** And meanwhile they sent to them into the land of Gilead Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest,

**22:14** and ten leaders with him, one from each tribe.

**22:15** They came to the sons of Reuben, and of Gad, and of the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead, and said to them:

**22:16** "All the people of the Lord send this charge: 'What is this transgression? Why have you abandoned the Lord, the God of Israel, building a sacrilegious altar and departing from His worship?

**22:17** Is it too little for you that you sinned at Baal-peor, and to the present day the stain of this crime remains on us, and many of the people fell?

**22:18** And today you have abandoned the Lord, and tomorrow His wrath will rage against all Israel.

**22:19** Now if you think the land of your possession is unclean, cross over to the land in which the tabernacle of the Lord is, and dwell among us; only do not depart from the Lord and from our fellowship by building an altar besides the altar of the Lord our God.

**22:20** Did not Achan the son of Zerah transgress the commandment of the Lord, and His wrath descended upon all the people of Israel? And he was one man, and if only he alone had perished in his crime!'"

**22:21** Then the sons of Reuben, and of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh answered the leaders of the delegation of Israel:

**22:22** "The Most Mighty God, the Lord, the Most Mighty God, the Lord, He Himself knows, and Israel also will understand: if we have constructed this altar with an intent of treachery, let Him not protect us, but let Him punish us at this present time;

**22:23** and if we did it with the intention that we might place burnt offerings, and sacrifice, and peace offerings upon it, let Him Himself inquire and judge;

**22:24** and not rather with this thought and deliberation, so that we said, 'Tomorrow your sons will say to our sons, "What do you have to do with the Lord, the God of Israel?

**22:25** The Lord has set the Jordan River as a boundary between us and you, sons of Reuben and sons of Gad, and therefore you have no portion in the Lord." And through this occasion your sons will turn our sons away from the fear of the Lord.' Therefore we thought it better,

**22:26** and said, 'Let us construct an altar for ourselves, not for burnt offerings, nor for offering sacrificial victims,

**22:27** but as a witness between us and you, and our offspring and your descendants, so that we may serve the Lord, and that it may be our right to offer burnt offerings, and sacrificial victims, and peace offerings; and so that tomorrow your sons may never say to our sons, "You have no portion in the Lord."'

**22:28** And if they should choose to say that, they will answer them, 'Look, the altar of the Lord, which our fathers made, not for burnt offerings, nor for sacrifice, but as a witness for us and for you.'

**22:29** Far be this crime from us, that we should depart from the Lord and abandon His footsteps by constructing an altar for offering burnt offerings, and sacrifices, and sacrificial victims, besides the altar of the Lord our God, which has been constructed before His tabernacle."

**22:30** Upon hearing these things, Phinehas the priest, and the leaders of the delegation of Israel who were with him, were appeased, and they most gladly accepted the words of the sons of Reuben, and of Gad, and of the half-tribe of Manasseh.

**22:31** And Phinehas the priest, the son of Eleazar, said to them, "Now we know that the Lord is with us, because you are free from this treachery, and you have delivered the sons of Israel from the hand of the Lord."

**22:32** And he returned with the leaders from the sons of Reuben and Gad from the land of Gilead, of the borders of Canaan, to the sons of Israel, and reported to them.

**22:33** And the report pleased all who heard it. And the sons of Israel praised God, and no longer spoke at all of going up against them and fighting, and destroying the land of their possession.

**22:34** And the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad called the altar that they had constructed, "Our Witness, that the Lord Himself is God."

## Translator Notes

- **22:10** — Vulgate "tumulos Jordanis" ("mounds of the Jordan") and "infinitae magnitudinis" ("of immense size") reflect Latin renderings distinct from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which speaks of the "districts" (gelilot) of the Jordan and an altar "imposing in appearance."
- **22:17** — Vulgate uses the proper name "Beelphegor" ("Baal-peor") and speaks of "the stain of this crime" remaining on Israel, whereas the Hebrew text reads simply "the iniquity of Peor" from which Israel has not cleansed itself.
- **22:20** — Vulgate reads "atque utinam solum periisset" ("and if only he alone had perished"), taking the underlying Hebrew particle as an optative wish rather than the negative statement ("that man did not perish alone") of the Masoretic Text.
- **22:32** — Vulgate has the genitive apposition "de terra Galaad, finium Chanaan" ("from the land of Gilead, of the borders of Canaan"), whereas the Hebrew text reads "from the land of Gilead to the land of Canaan."
- **22:34** — Vulgate gives the altar the explicit name "Testimonium nostrum, quod Dominus ipse sit Deus" ("Our Witness, that the Lord Himself is God"), whereas the Hebrew text leaves the name itself unstated before explaining that it is a witness between the tribes.
