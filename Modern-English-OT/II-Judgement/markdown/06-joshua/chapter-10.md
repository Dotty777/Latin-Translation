## Text

**10:1** When Adoni-zedek king of Jerusalem had heard these things, namely, that Joshua had captured Ai and overthrown it (for just as he had done to Jericho and its king, so he did to Ai and its king), and that the Gibeonites had defected to Israel and were their allies,

**10:2** he feared greatly. For Gibeon was a great city, and one of the royal cities, and larger than the town of Ai, and all its warriors were very mighty.

**10:3** Therefore Adoni-zedek king of Jerusalem sent to Hoham king of Hebron, and to Piram king of Jarmuth, also to Japhia king of Lachish, and to Debir king of Eglon, saying:

**10:4** "Come up to me and bring aid, so that we may conquer Gibeon, because it has defected to Joshua and to the sons of Israel."

**10:5** Therefore the five kings of the Amorites gathered and went up: the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, the king of Eglon, together with their armies; and they encamped around Gibeon, attacking it.

**10:6** Now the inhabitants of Gibeon, the besieged city, sent to Joshua, who was then staying in the camp at Gilgal, and said to him, "Do not withdraw your hands from helping your servants; come up quickly and rescue us, and bring aid, for all the kings of the Amorites who live in the hill country have come together against us."

**10:7** And Joshua went up from Gilgal, and the whole army of warriors with him, very mighty men.

**10:8** And the Lord said to Joshua, "Do not fear them, for I have handed them over into your hands; none of them will be able to resist you."

**10:9** Therefore Joshua rushed upon them suddenly, going up all night from Gilgal.

**10:10** And the Lord threw them into confusion before the face of Israel, and crushed them with a great slaughter in Gibeon, and pursued them along the road of the ascent of Beth-horon, and struck them as far as Azekah and Makkedah.

**10:11** And as they were fleeing from the sons of Israel and were on the descent of Beth-horon, the Lord cast large stones upon them from heaven as far as Azekah; and many more died from the hailstones than those whom the sons of Israel had struck with the sword.

**10:12** Then Joshua spoke to the Lord on the day when He handed over the Amorite in the sight of the sons of Israel, and he said before them, "Sun, do not move opposite Gibeon, and moon, opposite the valley of Aijalon."

**10:13** And the sun and the moon stood still until the nation avenged itself on its enemies. Is this not written in the book of the just? Therefore the sun stood still in the middle of heaven, and did not hasten to set for the space of one day.

**10:14** There was not before nor afterward so long a day, with the Lord obeying the voice of a man and fighting for Israel.

**10:15** And Joshua returned with all Israel to the camp at Gilgal.

**10:16** For the five kings had fled and had hidden themselves in the cave of the city of Makkedah.

**10:17** And it was reported to Joshua that the five kings had been found hiding in the cave of the city of Makkedah.

**10:18** He commanded his companions and said, "Roll huge stones to the mouth of the cave, and station diligent men to guard them shut inside;

**10:19** as for you, do not stand still, but pursue the enemies, and cut down all the rearmost of those fleeing; and do not allow them to enter the strongholds of their cities, whom the Lord God has handed over into your hands."

**10:20** Therefore, when the adversaries had been cut down with a great slaughter and almost consumed to utter destruction, those who were able to escape Israel entered the fortified cities.

**10:21** And the entire army returned to Joshua at Makkedah, where the camp then was, unharmed and in full number; and no one dared to mutter against the sons of Israel.

**10:22** And Joshua commanded, saying, "Open the mouth of the cave, and bring out to me the five kings who are hiding in it."

**10:23** And the attendants did as had been ordered them, and led out to him the five kings from the cave: the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, the king of Eglon.

**10:24** And when they had been led out to him, he called all the men of Israel and said to the commanders of the army who were with him, "Go and put your feet on the necks of these kings." When they had gone forward and were treading with their feet on the necks of those subdued,

**10:25** he said to them again, "Do not fear, nor be terrified; be strengthened and be resolute, for thus the Lord will do to all your enemies against whom you fight."

**10:26** And Joshua struck them and killed them, and hanged them on five stakes; and they were hanging until evening.

**10:27** And when the sun was setting, he commanded his companions to take them down from the gibbets. Having taken them down, they threw them into the cave in which they had hidden, and placed huge stones over its mouth, which remain until the present.

**10:28** On that same day also, Joshua captured Makkedah and struck it with the edge of the sword, and killed its king and all its inhabitants; he did not leave in it even a small remnant. And he did to the king of Makkedah just as he had done to the king of Jericho.

**10:29** Then he passed with all Israel from Makkedah to Libnah and fought against it:

**10:30** which the Lord handed over, along with its king, into the hands of Israel; and they struck the city with the edge of the sword, and all its inhabitants; they did not leave in it any remnant. And they did to the king of Libnah just as they had done to the king of Jericho.

**10:31** From Libnah he passed to Lachish with all Israel, and having deployed the army all around, he attacked it.

**10:32** And the Lord handed over Lachish into the hands of Israel, and he captured it on the second day and struck it with the edge of the sword, and every soul that had been in it, just as he had done to Libnah.

**10:33** At that time Horam king of Gezer came up to help Lachish; and Joshua struck him with all his people to utter destruction.

**10:34** And he passed from Lachish to Eglon and surrounded it,

**10:35** and conquered it on the same day; and he struck with the edge of the sword all the souls that were in it, according to all that he had done to Lachish.

**10:36** He also went up with all Israel from Eglon to Hebron and fought against it:

**10:37** he captured it and struck it with the edge of the sword, its king also, and all the towns of that region, and all the souls that had lived in it; he did not leave in it any remnant; just as he had done to Eglon, so he did also to Hebron, consuming with the sword everything that he found in it.

**10:38** Turning back from there to Debir,

**10:39** he captured it and laid it waste; its king also, and all the surrounding towns, he struck with the edge of the sword; he did not leave in it any remnant; just as he had done to Hebron and Libnah and their kings, so he did to Debir and its king.

**10:40** Therefore Joshua struck all the hill country and the south and the plains, and Ashdoth, with their kings; he did not leave in it any remnant, but he killed everything that could breathe, just as the Lord God of Israel had commanded him,

**10:41** from Kadesh-barnea as far as Gaza. All the land of Goshen as far as Gibeon,

**10:42** and all the kings and their regions he captured and laid waste in one assault, for the Lord God of Israel fought for him.

**10:43** And he returned with all Israel to the site of the camp in Gilgal.

## Translator Notes

- **10:12** — "Do not move opposite Gibeon" reflects Vulgate "contra Gabaon ne movearis," whereas the Hebrew reads "be still at Gibeon."
- **10:13** — "The book of the just" translates the Vulgate plural "in libro justorum," corresponding to the Hebrew "Book of Jashar" ("the Upright").
- **10:14** — "So long a day" preserves Vulgate "tam longa dies" (Hebrew: "a day like it").
- **10:40** — "Ashdoth" (Latin Asedoth) preserves the Vulgate treatment of the Hebrew geographic common noun "ashedot" ("the slopes" or "foothills") as a proper place name.
- **10:42** — "Fought for him" reflects Vulgate "pugnavit pro eo" (Hebrew: "fought for Israel").
