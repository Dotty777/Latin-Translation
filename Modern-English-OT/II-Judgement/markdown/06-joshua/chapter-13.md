## Text

**13:1** Joshua was old and advanced in age, and the Lord said to him, "You have grown old and are of great age, and a very extensive land has been left, which has not yet been divided by lot:

**13:2** namely, all Galilee of the Philistines, and all Geshur.

**13:3** From the muddy river that waters Egypt as far as the borders of Ekron toward the north, the land of Canaan, which is divided among the five rulers of the Philistines: the Gazites, the Ashdodites, the Ashkelonites, the Gittites, and the Ekronites.

**13:4** To the south, moreover, are the Hivites, all the land of Canaan, and Mearah of the Sidonians, as far as Aphek and the borders of the Amorites,

**13:5** and its borderlands. Also the region of Lebanon toward the east, from Baal-gad beneath Mount Hermon until you enter Hamath;

**13:6** of all who dwell in the hill country from Lebanon as far as the waters of Misrephoth, and all the Sidonians. It is I who will wipe them out from before the sons of Israel. Therefore let it come into the portion of the inheritance of Israel, just as I commanded you.

**13:7** And now divide the land as a possession for the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasseh."

**13:8** With it, Reuben and Gad possessed the land that Moses, the servant of the Lord, gave to them across the streams of the Jordan, toward the eastern region:

**13:9** from Aroer, which is situated on the bank of the stream of Arnon and in the middle of the valley, and all the plains of Medeba as far as Dibon,

**13:10** and all the cities of Sihon, king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon, as far as the borders of the sons of Ammon,

**13:11** and Gilead, and the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan as far as Salecah,

**13:12** all the kingdom of Og in Bashan, who reigned in Ashtaroth and Edrei; he himself was of the remnant of the Rephaim, and Moses struck them down and wiped them out.

**13:13** Yet the sons of Israel were unwilling to destroy the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and they have lived in the midst of Israel to the present day.

**13:14** To the tribe of Levi, however, he did not give a possession; rather, the sacrifices and offerings of the Lord God of Israel are themselves its inheritance, just as He spoke to it.

**13:15** Therefore Moses gave a possession to the tribe of the sons of Reuben according to their clans.

**13:16** And their territory was from Aroer, which is situated on the bank of the stream of Arnon and in the middle of the valley of that same stream: all the plain that leads to Medeba,

**13:17** and Heshbon, and all their small villages that are in the plains; also Dibon and Bamoth-baal, and the town of Baal-meon,

**13:18** and Jahaz, and Kedemoth, and Mephaath,

**13:19** and Kiriathaim, and Sibmah, and Zereth-shahar on the mountain of the valley;

**13:20** Beth-peor and Ashdoth, Pisgah and Beth-jeshimoth,

**13:21** and all the cities of the plain, and all the kingdoms of Sihon, king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon, whom Moses struck down along with the princes of Midian: Heveus, and Rekem, and Zur, and Hur, and Reba, leaders of Sihon, inhabitants of the land.

**13:22** And the sons of Israel killed Balaam the son of Beor, the diviner, with the sword, along with the others who were put to death.

**13:23** And the border of the sons of Reuben became the Jordan River. This is the possession of the Reubenites by their clans: cities and small villages.

**13:24** And Moses gave a possession to the tribe of Gad and to his sons by their clans, of which this is the division:

**13:25** the territory of Jazer, and all the cities of Gilead, and half of the land of the sons of Ammon, as far as Aroer, which is opposite Rabbah,

**13:26** and from Heshbon as far as Ramoth, Mizpeh, and Betonim; and from Mahanaim as far as the borders of Debir.

**13:27** Also in the valley: Beth-haram, and Beth-nimrah, and Succoth, and Zaphon, the remaining part of the kingdom of Sihon, king of Heshbon; its boundary also is the Jordan, as far as the farthest part of the Sea of Chinnereth across the Jordan, toward the eastern region.

**13:28** This is the possession of the sons of Gad by their families: the cities and their villages.

**13:29** He also gave a possession to the half-tribe of Manasseh and to his sons according to their clans,

**13:30** of which this is the beginning: from Mahanaim, all Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Og, king of Bashan, and all the villages of Jair that are in Bashan, sixty towns;

**13:31** and half of Gilead, and Ashtaroth and Edrei, cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan, for the sons of Machir, the son of Manasseh, for half of the sons of Machir according to their clans.

**13:32** Moses divided this possession in the plains of Moab across the Jordan, opposite Jericho, toward the eastern region.

**13:33** To the tribe of Levi, however, he did not give a possession, because the Lord God of Israel Himself is its possession, just as He spoke to it.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — Vulgate renders Hebrew gelilot ("districts" or "regions") as the proper noun Galilæa ("Galilee"); although the Clementine text places a comma after Galilæa, Philisthiim functions as a genitive ("of the Philistines"), as in 13:3.
- **13:3** — Vulgate translates the river name Shihor descriptively as fluvio turbido ("the muddy river") and reads qui irrigat Ægyptum ("that waters Egypt") where the Hebrew has "which is east of Egypt."
- **13:4** — Vulgate reads Hevæi ("the Hivites") where the Hebrew has "the Avvim."
- **13:5** — Vulgate reads ejusque confinia ("and its borderlands") where the Hebrew has "the land of the Gebalites," connecting the name with the Hebrew word for "border."
- **13:8** — Latin cum qua ("with it") refers back to the half-tribe of Manasseh in 13:7, specifically meaning the eastern half-tribe alongside which Reuben and Gad received Transjordan.
- **13:21** — Vulgate renders the first Midianite prince as Hevæum ("Heveus," the Latin form of "Hivite") where the Hebrew has the personal name "Evi" (cf. Numbers 31:8).
