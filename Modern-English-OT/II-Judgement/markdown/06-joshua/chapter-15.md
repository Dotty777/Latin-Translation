## Text

**15:1** Therefore the lot of the sons of Judah by their clans was this: from the border of Edom, the wilderness of Zin, toward the south, and as far as the farthest part of the southern region.

**15:2** Its beginning is from the end of the Saltiest Sea, and from its tongue that faces the south.

**15:3** And it goes out toward the ascent of the Scorpion, and passes on to Zin; and it goes up to Kadesh-barnea, and reaches Hezron, going up to Addar, and curving around Karka,

**15:4** and from there passing on to Azmon, and reaching the stream of Egypt; and its borders will be the Great Sea. This will be the boundary of the southern region.

**15:5** Now on the east the beginning will be the Saltiest Sea as far as the farthest end of the Jordan; and the parts that face toward the north are from the tongue of the sea as far as that same Jordan River.

**15:6** And the border goes up to Beth-hoglah, and passes on the north to Beth-arabah, going up to the stone of Bohan the son of Reuben,

**15:7** and stretching as far as the borders of Debir from the valley of Achor, toward the north facing Gilgal, which is opposite the ascent of Adummim, on the southern side of the stream; and it crosses the waters that are called the Spring of the Sun; and its outlets will be at the spring of Rogel.

**15:8** And it goes up through the valley of the son of Hinnom on the side of the Jebusite toward the south, that is, Jerusalem; and rising from there to the top of the mountain that is opposite Gehennom toward the west, at the end of the valley of Rephaim toward the north,

**15:9** and it passes on from the top of the mountain as far as the spring of the water of Nephtoah; and it reaches as far as the towns of Mount Ephron; and it turns toward Baalah, which is Kiriath-jearim, that is, the city of forests.

**15:10** And it curves around from Baalah toward the west, as far as Mount Seir; and it passes along the side of Mount Jearim toward the north to Chesalon; and it goes down to Beth-shemesh, and passes to Timnah.

**15:11** And it reaches toward the north of the region of Ekron on the side; and it turns to Shikkeron, and crosses Mount Baalah; and it reaches Jabneel, and ends at the boundary of the Great Sea toward the west.

**15:12** These are the borders of the sons of Judah all around in their clans.

**15:13** Now to Caleb the son of Jephunneh he gave a portion in the midst of the sons of Judah, just as the Lord had commanded him: Kiriath-arba of the father of Anak, which is Hebron.

**15:14** And from it Caleb wiped out the three sons of Anak, Sheshai and Ahiman and Talmai, of the lineage of Anak.

**15:15** And going up from there, he came to the inhabitants of Debir, which previously was called Kiriath-sepher, that is, the city of letters.

**15:16** And Caleb said, "Whoever strikes Kiriath-sepher and captures it, to him I will give my daughter Achsah as a wife."

**15:17** And Othniel the son of Kenaz, the younger brother of Caleb, captured it; and he gave him his daughter Achsah as a wife.

**15:18** And as they were going along together, she was persuaded by her husband to ask her father for a field. And she sighed as she sat on the donkey; and Caleb said to her, "What is the matter with you?"

**15:19** And she answered, "Give me a blessing: you have given me a southern and dry land; add also watered land." So Caleb gave her the upper and lower watered land.

**15:20** This is the possession of the tribe of the sons of Judah by their clans.

**15:22** and Kinah and Dimonah and Adadah,

**15:23** and Kedesh and Hazor and Ithnan,

**15:24** Ziph and Telem and Bealoth,

**15:25** New Hazor and Kerioth, Hezron, that is, Hazor;

**15:35** and Jarmuth and Adullam, Socoh and Azekah,

**15:37** Zenan and Hadashah and Migdal-gad,

**15:42** Libnah and Ether and Ashan,

**15:44** and Keilah and Achzib and Mareshah: nine cities, and their villages.

**15:45** Ekron, with its towns and small villages.

**15:46** From Ekron as far as the sea: all that lies toward Ashdod and its small towns.

**15:47** Ashdod, with its towns and small villages. Gaza, with its towns and small villages, as far as the stream of Egypt, and the Great Sea is its border.

**15:48** And in the hill country: Shamir and Jattir and Socoh,

**15:49** and Dannah and Kiriath-sannah, that is, Debir;

**15:50** Anab and Eshtemoh and Anim,

**15:52** Arab and Rumah and Eshan,

**15:53** and Janum and Beth-tappuah and Aphekah,

**15:54** Humtah, and Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron, and Zior: nine cities, and their villages.

**15:55** Maon and Carmel and Ziph and Juttah,

**15:56** Jezreel and Jokdeam and Zanoah,

**15:57** Kain, Gibeah, and Timnah: ten cities and their villages.

**15:58** Halhul, and Beth-zur, and Gedor,

**15:59** Maarath, and Beth-anoth, and Eltekon: six cities and their villages.

**15:60** Kiriath-baal, that is, Kiriath-jearim, the city of forests, and Rabbah: two cities, and their villages.

**15:61** In the wilderness: Beth-arabah, Middin, and Secacah,

**15:62** and Nibshan, and the City of Salt, and En-gedi: six cities, and their villages.

## Translator Notes

- **15:3** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew Akrabbim ("scorpions") as the singular Latin Scorpionis ("of the Scorpion"), and similarly renders other place names into Latin in this chapter, including fons solis ("the Spring of the Sun") in 15:7 and Asor nova ("New Hazor") in 15:25.
- **15:8** — Whereas the Hebrew reads "the valley of Hinnom," the Vulgate transliterates the phrase as a single proper name, Geennom (rendered "Gehennom"), without repeating the word for "valley."
- **15:9** — The explanatory gloss "that is, the city of forests" (id est, urbs silvarum) is distinctive to the Vulgate and absent from the Hebrew text, parallel to the Vulgate gloss "that is, the city of letters" (id est, civitas litterarum) in 15:15.
- **15:14** — The Vulgate uses delevit ("wiped out/destroyed") where the Hebrew has "drove out," and in 15:17 explicitly includes junior ("younger") in describing Caleb's brother.
- **15:18** — The Vulgate diverges notably from the Hebrew here: Achsah is passively "persuaded by her husband" (suasa est a viro suo) rather than urging him, and she "sighed as she sat on the donkey" (suspiravitque ut sedebat in asino) rather than dismounting from it.
- **15:52** — The Vulgate reads Ruma ("Rumah"), reflecting a Hebrew textual variant with resh rather than the Masoretic reading "Dumah."
- **15:60** — As in 15:9, the Vulgate adds the explanatory gloss urbs silvarum ("the city of forests") after Kiriath-jearim, which is absent from the Hebrew text.
- **15:63** — The Vulgate uses the collective singular Jebusæum habitatorem ("the Jebusite, the inhabitant") and the verb delere ("to wipe out/destroy"), whereas the Hebrew has plural "inhabitants" and "drive out."
