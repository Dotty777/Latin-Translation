## Text

**6:1** Now Jericho was shut and fortified out of fear of the sons of Israel, and no one dared to go out or come in.

**6:2** And the Lord said to Joshua, "Look, I have given Jericho into your hand, and its king, and all the mighty men.

**6:3** Circle the city, all you fighting men, once a day; thus you shall do for six days.

**6:4** But on the seventh day, let the priests take up seven trumpets that are used in the jubilee and go ahead of the ark of the covenant; you will circle the city seven times, and the priests will blow the trumpets.

**6:5** And when the sound of the trumpet resounds, longer and more broken, and rings out in your ears, all the people shall shout together with a very great outcry, and the walls of the city will collapse to their foundations, and each one shall enter through the place opposite which he stands."

**6:6** Therefore Joshua the son of Nun called the priests and said to them, "Take up the ark of the covenant, and let seven other priests take up seven jubilee horns and march before the ark of the Lord."

**6:7** To the people also he said, "Go and circle the city, armed, going ahead of the ark of the Lord."

**6:8** And when Joshua had finished his words, and the seven priests were sounding the seven horns before the ark of the covenant of the Lord,

**6:9** and the whole armed army was going ahead, the rest of the crowd followed the ark, and everything resounded with the horns.

**6:10** Now Joshua had commanded the people, saying, "You shall not shout, nor shall your voice be heard, nor shall any word go out from your mouth, until the day comes on which I say to you, 'Shout, and cry out.'"

**6:11** Therefore the ark of the Lord circled the city once during the day, and returned to the camp and remained there.

**6:12** Therefore, as Joshua rose during the night, the priests took up the ark of the Lord,

**6:13** and seven of them seven horns that are used in the jubilee; and they went ahead of the ark of the Lord, walking and sounding them, and the armed people went before them, while the rest of the crowd followed the ark and resounded with the horns.

**6:14** And they circled the city once on the second day and returned to the camp. Thus they did for six days.

**6:15** But on the seventh day, rising at dawn, they circled the city, as had been arranged, seven times.

**6:16** And when on the seventh circuit the priests sounded the horns, Joshua said to all Israel, "Cry out aloud, for the Lord has delivered the city to you.

**6:17** And let this city, and everything that is in it, be anathema to the Lord; only Rahab the prostitute shall live, with all who are with her in the house, for she hid the messengers whom we sent.

**6:18** But you, take care not to touch anything of these things that have been commanded, so that you may not be guilty of transgression, and the whole camp of Israel may not be under sin and be troubled.

**6:19** But whatever gold and silver there is, and bronze vessels and iron, shall be consecrated to the Lord, stored up in His treasuries."

**6:20** Therefore, as all the people cried out aloud and the trumpets sounded, after the voice and sound rang out in the ears of the multitude, the walls immediately collapsed, and each one went up through the place that was opposite him, and they captured the city,

**6:21** and they killed everything that was in it, from man to woman, from infant to old man. They also struck down oxen and sheep and donkeys with the edge of the sword.

**6:22** Now Joshua said to the two men who had been sent as spies, "Enter the house of the prostitute woman, and bring her out, and everything that belongs to her, just as you confirmed to her by an oath."

**6:23** And the young men went in and brought out Rahab, and her parents, her brothers also, and all her household goods and her relatives, and made them remain outside the camp of Israel.

**6:24** But the city, and everything that was in it, they set on fire, except for the gold and silver, and the bronze vessels and iron, which they consecrated into the treasury of the Lord.

**6:25** However, Joshua kept alive Rahab the prostitute, and her father's household, and everything that she had, and they lived in the midst of Israel until the present day, because she hid the messengers whom he had sent to spy out Jericho. At that time Joshua pronounced a curse, saying:

**6:26** "Cursed before the Lord be the man who raises up and builds the city of Jericho. At the cost of his firstborn may he lay its foundations, and at the cost of the youngest of his children may he set its gates."

**6:27** Therefore the Lord was with Joshua, and his name was spread throughout all the land.

## Translator Notes

- **6:5** — Latin longior atque concisior ("longer and more broken") describes a prolonged trumpet blast combined with sharp, broken tones; funditus ("to their foundations") intensifies the collapse of the plural muri ("walls").
- **6:6** — Latin jubilæorum buccinas ("jubilee horns," echoed in 6:13) interprets the Hebrew rams' horns in terms of the jubilee year, distinct from tuba ("trumpet") in 6:5 and 6:20.
- **6:17** — Latin anathema (from Greek anathema, rendering Hebrew cherem) denotes persons or property irrevocably devoted to the Lord under a sacred ban of destruction.
- **6:18** — Latin de his, quæ præcepta sunt ("of these things that have been commanded") and sub peccato ("under sin") are Vulgate-distinct phrasings where the Hebrew speaks directly of taking from the devoted things and bringing the ban upon the camp.
- **6:26** — Latin In primogenito suo ... in novissimo liberorum ("In his firstborn ... in the youngest of his children") uses the Hebraizing price idiom rendered "At the cost of"; Latin suscitaverit ("raises up") also gives a transitive sense where the Hebrew reads "rises up."
