## Text

**19:1** And the second lot of the sons of Simeon came out by their clans, and their inheritance was

**19:2** in the midst of the possession of the sons of Judah: Beersheba, and Sheba, and Moladah,

**19:3** and Hazar-shual, Balah, and Ezem,

**19:4** and Eltolad, Bethul, and Hormah,

**19:5** and Ziklag, and Beth-marcaboth, and Hazar-susah,

**19:6** and Beth-lebaoth, and Sharuhen: thirteen cities and their villages.

**19:7** Ain, and Rimmon, and Ether, and Ashan: four cities and their villages;

**19:8** all the hamlets around these cities as far as Baalath-beer, Ramath, toward the southern region. This is the inheritance of the sons of Simeon according to their clans,

**19:9** in the possession and measuring line of the sons of Judah, because it was too large; and therefore the sons of Simeon took possession in the midst of their inheritance.

**19:10** And the third lot of the sons of Zebulun fell by their clans; the border of their possession extended as far as Sarid.

**19:11** And it goes up from the sea and Maralah, and reaches to Dabbesheth, as far as the brook that is opposite Jokneam.

**19:12** And it turns back from Sarid toward the east to the borders of Chisloth-tabor, and goes out to Daberath, and goes up toward Japhia.

**19:13** And from there it passes over as far as the eastern side of Gath-hepher and Eth-kazin, and goes out to Rimmon, Amthar, and Neah.

**19:14** And it circles Hannathon to the north, and its endpoints are the valley of Iphtah-el,

**19:15** and Kattath, and Nahalal, and Shimron, and Jerala, and Bethlehem: twelve cities and their villages.

**19:16** This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Zebulun by their clans, the cities and their hamlets.

**19:17** For Issachar the fourth lot came out by its clans,

**19:18** and its inheritance was Jezreel, and Chesulloth, and Shunem,

**19:19** and Hapharaim, and Shion, and Anaharath,

**19:20** and Rabbith, and Kishion, Ebez,

**19:21** and Remeth, and En-gannim, and En-haddah, and Beth-pazzez.

**19:22** And its border reached as far as Tabor, and Shahazumah, and Beth-shemesh, and its endpoints will be the Jordan: sixteen cities and their villages.

**19:23** This is the possession of the sons of Issachar by their clans, the cities and their hamlets.

**19:24** And the fifth lot fell to the tribe of the sons of Asher by their clans,

**19:25** and their border was Helkath, and Hali, and Beten, and Achshaph,

**19:26** and Allammelech, and Amad, and Mishal; and it reaches as far as Carmel of the sea, and Shihor and Libnath,

**19:27** and turns back toward the east to Beth-dagon, and passes over to Zebulun and the valley of Iphtah-el toward the north, to Beth-emek and Neiel. And it goes out to the left, to Cabul,

**19:28** and Ebron, and Rehob, and Hammon, and Kanah, as far as great Sidon.

**19:29** And it turns back to Hormah as far as the strongly fortified city of Tyre, and as far as Hosah; and its endpoints will be at the sea from the measuring line of Achzib,

**19:30** and Ummah, and Aphek, and Rehob: twenty-two cities and their villages.

**19:31** This is the possession of the sons of Asher by their clans, both the cities and their hamlets.

**19:32** The sixth lot of the sons of Naphtali fell by their families,

**19:33** and the border began from Heleph and Elon in Zaanannim, and Adami, which is Nekeb, and Jabneel, as far as Lakkum; and their endpoints were as far as the Jordan;

**19:34** and the border turns back toward the west to Aznoth-tabor, and from there goes out to Hukkok, and passes over to Zebulun toward the south, and to Asher toward the west, and to Judah at the Jordan toward the rising of the sun;

**19:35** the strongly fortified cities: Ziddim, Zer, and Hammath, and Rakkath, and Chinnereth,

**19:36** and Adamah, and Ramah, Hazor,

**19:37** and Kedesh, and Edrei, En-hazor,

**19:38** and Iron, and Migdal-el, Horem, and Beth-anath, and Beth-shemesh: nineteen cities and their villages.

**19:39** This is the possession of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali by their clans, the cities and their hamlets.

**19:40** For the tribe of the sons of Dan by their families, the seventh lot came out,

**19:41** and the border of its possession was Zorah, and Eshtaol, and Ir-shemesh, that is, the city of the sun.

**19:42** Shaalabbin, and Aijalon, and Ithlah,

**19:43** Elon, and Timnah, and Ekron,

**19:44** Eltekeh, Gibbethon, and Baalath,

**19:45** and Jehud, and Bene, and Berak, and Gath-rimmon,

**19:46** and Me-jarkon, and Rakkon, with the border that faces Joppa.

**19:47** This is the possession of the tribe of the sons of Dan by their clans, the cities and their hamlets.

**19:48** And it is bounded by that very border. And the sons of Dan went up and fought against Leshem, and captured it, and struck it with the edge of the sword, and took possession and lived in it, calling its name Leshem Dan, after the name of Dan their father.

**19:49** And when he had finished dividing the land by lot to each one by their tribes, the sons of Israel gave a possession to Joshua the son of Nun in their midst,

**19:50** according to the command of the Lord, the city that he requested, Timnath-serah in the hill country of Ephraim; and he built the city and lived in it.

**19:51** These are the possessions that Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the leaders of the families and tribes of the sons of Israel divided by lot in Shiloh, before the Lord at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony; and they apportioned the land.

## Translator Notes

- **19:11** — Vulgate reads "from the sea" ("de mari"), whereas the Hebrew reads "westward" (lit. "toward the sea").
- **19:13** — Vulgate treats the Hebrew participle "ha-meto'ar" ("which bends/turns") as a distinct place name, "Amthar."
- **19:15** — Vulgate reads "Jerala" (reflecting an r/d consonant difference), whereas the Hebrew has "Idalah."
- **19:29** — Vulgate reads "Hormah" ("Horma") where the Hebrew has "Ramah," and renders Hebrew "chebel" ("region/cord") literally as "measuring line" ("funiculus," as also in v. 9).
- **19:48** — In the Clementine Vulgate, verses 47 and 48 are transposed relative to the Hebrew, so "And it is bounded by that very border" continues v. 46 (differing from Hebrew "the territory of the sons of Dan went out beyond them"). Vulgate also forms the compound name "Leshem Dan" where the Hebrew says they called Leshem "Dan."
- **19:51** — Vulgate renders Hebrew "tent of meeting" as "tabernacle of the testimony" ("tabernaculi testimonii"), and omits "finished" before "apportioned the land."
