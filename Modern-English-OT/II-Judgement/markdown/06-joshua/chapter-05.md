## Text

**5:1** Therefore, after all the kings of the Amorites who lived beyond the Jordan on the western side, and all the kings of Canaan who possessed the places near the great sea, heard that the Lord had dried up the streams of the Jordan before the sons of Israel until they crossed over, their heart melted, and no spirit remained in them, as they feared the entrance of the sons of Israel.

**5:2** At that time the Lord said to Joshua, "Make for yourself stone knives, and circumcise the sons of Israel a second time."

**5:3** He did what the Lord had commanded, and circumcised the sons of Israel on the hill of the foreskins.

**5:4** Now this is the reason for the second circumcision: all the people of the male sex who came out of Egypt, all the fighting men, died in the desert along the very long circuits of the way,

**5:5** all of whom were circumcised. But the people who were born in the desert

**5:6** were uncircumcised during the forty years of the journey through the very vast wilderness, until those were consumed who had not listened to the voice of the Lord, and to whom He had previously sworn that He would not show them a land flowing with milk and honey.

**5:7** Their sons succeeded to the place of their fathers, and were circumcised by Joshua, because, just as they had been born, they still had their foreskin, and no one had circumcised them on the way.

**5:8** Now after they were all circumcised, they remained in the same place of the camp until they were healed.

**5:9** And the Lord said to Joshua, "Today I have taken away the reproach of Egypt from you." And the name of that place was called Gilgal, until the present day.

**5:10** And the sons of Israel remained at Gilgal, and kept the Passover on the fourteenth day of the month toward evening in the plains of Jericho;

**5:11** and they ate of the produce of the land on the next day, unleavened loaves and parched grain of the same year.

**5:12** And the manna ceased after they ate of the produce of the land, nor did the sons of Israel use that food any longer, but they ate of the produce of the present year of the land of Canaan.

**5:13** Now when Joshua was in the field of the city of Jericho, he lifted up his eyes and saw a man standing opposite him, holding a drawn sword; and he went over to him and said, "Are you ours, or of our adversaries?"

**5:14** He answered, "Not at all, but I am the commander of the army of the Lord, and now I come."

**5:15** Joshua fell face down to the ground, and bowing in homage, he said, "What does my lord speak to his servant?"

**5:16** "Loosen your sandal from your feet," he said, "for the place on which you are standing is holy." And Joshua did as he had been ordered.

## Translator Notes

- **5:4** — Latin per longissimos viæ circuitus ("along the very long circuits of the way") expands the Hebrew phrase "on the way" to highlight the prolonged desert wanderings.
- **5:11** — Latin ejusdem anni ("of the same year")—paralleled by præsentis anni ("of the present year") in 5:12—is a Vulgate-distinct reading where the Hebrew reads "on that very day."
- **5:13** — Latin in agro urbis Jericho ("in the field of the city of Jericho") specifies the open countryside outside the city, where the Hebrew reads simply "by Jericho."
- **5:15** — Native Vulgate Joshua 5:15 (Joshua's prostration) maps to Saints.Bible 5:14b, and native Vulgate 5:16 ('Solve, inquit...') maps to Saints.Bible 5:15.
- **5:16** — Native Vulgate numbers the commander's sandal command as 5:16 (mapping to Saints.Bible 5:15), following Joshua's prostration in native 5:15.
