## Text

**12:1** These are the kings whom the sons of Israel struck down, and whose land they possessed beyond the Jordan toward the rising of the sun, from the torrent of Arnon as far as Mount Hermon, and all the eastern region that faces the wilderness.

**12:2** Sihon, king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, ruled from Aroer, which is situated on the bank of the torrent of Arnon, and over the middle part in the valley, and over half of Gilead, as far as the torrent of Jabbok, which is the border of the sons of Ammon;

**12:3** and from the wilderness as far as the Sea of Chinneroth toward the east, and as far as the sea of the desert, which is the very salty sea, toward the eastern region by the road that leads to Beth-jeshimoth, and on the southern side, which lies beneath Ashdoth-pisgah.

**12:4** The territory of Og, king of Bashan, of the remnant of the Rephaim, who lived in Ashtaroth and in Edrei, and ruled in Mount Hermon, and in Salecah, and in all Bashan, as far as the borders

**12:5** of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and of half of Gilead, the borders of Sihon, king of Heshbon.

**12:6** Moses the servant of the Lord and the sons of Israel struck them down, and Moses gave their land as a possession to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.

**12:7** These are the kings of the land whom Joshua and the sons of Israel struck down beyond the Jordan toward the western region, from Baal-gad in the plain of Lebanon as far as the mountain a part of which ascends into Seir; and Joshua gave it as a possession to the tribes of Israel, to each their portions,

**12:8** both in the hill country and in the plains and the lowlands. In Ashdoth, and in the wilderness, and in the south, were the Hittite and the Amorite, the Canaanite and the Perizzite, the Hivite and the Jebusite.

**12:9** The king of Jericho, one; the king of Ai, which is beside Bethel, one;

**12:10** the king of Jerusalem, one; the king of Hebron, one;

**12:11** the king of Jarmuth, one; the king of Lachish, one;

**12:12** the king of Eglon, one; the king of Gezer, one;

**12:13** the king of Debir, one; the king of Geder, one;

**12:14** the king of Hormah, one; the king of Arad, one;

**12:15** the king of Libnah, one; the king of Adullam, one;

**12:16** the king of Makkedah, one; the king of Bethel, one;

**12:17** the king of Tappuah, one; the king of Hepher, one;

**12:18** the king of Aphek, one; the king of Sharon, one;

**12:19** the king of Madon, one; the king of Hazor, one;

**12:20** the king of Shimron, one; the king of Achshaph, one;

**12:21** the king of Taanach, one; the king of Megiddo, one;

**12:22** the king of Kedesh, one; the king of Jachanan of Carmel, one;

**12:23** the king of Dor and of the province of Dor, one; the king of the nations of Gilgal, one;

**12:24** the king of Tirzah, one: thirty-one kings in all.

## Translator Notes

- **12:3** — Latin "mare salsissimum" ("the very salty sea") uses a superlative for Hebrew "yam hammelach" ("the Salt Sea"). Both here ("Asedoth, Phasga") and in 12:8 ("In Asedoth"), the Vulgate retains Hebrew "ashedoth" ("slopes") as a proper name, Ashdoth.
- **12:8** — The Clementine Vulgate attaches the first three geographical regions to the preceding sentence (v. 7) and begins a new sentence with the remaining three ("In Ashdoth, and in the wilderness, and in the south") linked to the nations by the verb "fuit" ("were"), whereas the Hebrew lists all six regions together.
- **12:18** — The Vulgate reads "Saron" ("Sharon"), treating the prefix of Hebrew "la-Sharon" as a preposition rather than part of the place name ("Lasharon").
- **12:20** — The Vulgate reads simply "Semeron" ("Shimron," as in 11:1), omitting the second element of Hebrew "Shimron-meron."
- **12:22** — The Clementine Vulgate distinctly reads "Jachanan Carmeli" ("Jachanan of Carmel"), where the Hebrew text has "Jokneam in Carmel" (rendered "Jecnam" in the Vulgate of 19:11 and 21:34). In 12:23, the Vulgate also translates Hebrew "Goiim" literally as "gentium" ("the nations of Gilgal").
