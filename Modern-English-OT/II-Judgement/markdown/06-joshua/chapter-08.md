## Text

**8:1** Then the Lord said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid, and do not be terrified; take with you the entire multitude of fighting men, and rising up, go up against the town of Ai. Look, I have handed over into your hand its king and people, and the city and the land.

**8:2** And you shall do to the city of Ai and to its king just as you did to Jericho and to its king; however, you shall seize the plunder and all the animals for yourselves. Set an ambush for the city behind it."

**8:3** And Joshua rose up, and the entire army of warriors with him, to go up against Ai; and he sent thirty thousand chosen valiant men by night,

**8:4** and commanded them, saying, "Set an ambush behind the city, and do not withdraw too far; and you shall all be ready.

**8:5** But I and the rest of the multitude that is with me will advance from the opposite side against the city. And when they come out against us, just as we did before, we will flee and turn our backs,

**8:6** until in pursuing us they are drawn far away from the city; for they will think that we are fleeing just as before.

**8:7** Therefore, while we flee and they pursue, you shall rise up from the ambush and lay waste to the city; and the Lord your God will hand it over into your hands.

**8:8** And when you have captured it, set fire to it, and so you shall do everything as I have ordered."

**8:9** And he sent them away, and they went to the place of the ambush and stayed between Bethel and Ai, on the western side of the city of Ai; but Joshua remained that night in the midst of the people,

**8:10** and rising at daybreak, he mustered his companions and went up with the elders at the front of the army, surrounded by the support of fighting men.

**8:11** And when they had come and gone up opposite the city, they took their stand on the northern side of the city, with an intervening valley between it and them.

**8:12** Now he had chosen five thousand men and placed them in ambush between Bethel and Ai on the western side of that same city; but all the rest of the army drew up its battle line to the north, so that the rearmost of that multitude reached the western side of the city. Therefore Joshua went away that night and stood in the middle of the valley.

**8:14** When the king of Ai saw this, he hurried in the morning and went out with the entire army of the city, and drew up his battle line facing the desert, not knowing that an ambush lay hidden behind his back.

**8:15** Then Joshua and all Israel gave ground, feigning fear and fleeing along the way of the wilderness.

**8:16** And they, shouting out together and urging one another on, pursued them. And when they had withdrawn from the city,

**8:17** and not even one had remained in the city of Ai and Bethel who was not pursuing Israel (just as they had burst out, leaving the towns open),

**8:18** the Lord said to Joshua, "Raise the shield that is in your hand toward the city of Ai, for I will hand it over to you."

**8:19** And when he had raised the shield opposite the city, the men in ambush, who lay hidden, rose up immediately, and going to the city, they captured and set fire to it.

**8:20** Now the men of the city who were pursuing Joshua, looking back and seeing the smoke of the city rising all the way to the sky, were no longer able to flee this way or that, especially since those who had feigned flight and were heading toward the wilderness had resisted the pursuers most strongly.

**8:21** And Joshua and all Israel, seeing that the city had been captured and that the smoke of the city was rising, turned back and struck down the men of Ai.

**8:22** Indeed, those also who had captured and set fire to the city, coming out of the city toward their own men, began to strike the enemy in the middle. Therefore, while the adversaries were being cut down from both sides, so that no one from so great a multitude was saved,

**8:23** they also seized the king of the city of Ai alive and presented him to Joshua.

**8:24** Therefore, when all those who had pursued Israel as it headed toward the desert had been killed and had fallen by the sword in that same place, the sons of Israel turned back and struck down the city.

**8:25** Now those who had fallen on that same day, from man to woman, were twelve thousand people, all of the city of Ai.

**8:27** However, the sons of Israel divided the livestock and the plunder of the city among themselves, just as the Lord had commanded Joshua.

**8:28** And he set fire to the city and made it an everlasting mound.

**8:29** He also hanged its king on a gibbet until evening and the setting of the sun. And Joshua gave the command, and they took his corpse down from the cross and threw it down at the very entrance of the city, piling over him a great heap of stones, which remains until the present day.

## Translator Notes

- **8:10** — The Vulgate describes Joshua as vallatus auxilio pugnatorum ("surrounded by the support of fighting men"), using Roman military phrasing that incorporates the fighting force mentioned at the start of verse 11 in the Hebrew.
- **8:12** — In this Clementine Vulgate text, the material numbered as verse 13 in other editions is included within verse 12, and Joshua is said to have "stood" (stetit) in the middle of the valley rather than "spent the night."
- **8:18** — The Vulgate reads clypeum ("shield") here and in 8:19, whereas the Hebrew has kidon ("javelin" or "short spear").
- **8:29** — The Vulgate employs Roman execution terms, referring to the wooden structure first as patibulo ("gibbet") and then as cruce ("cross"), where the Hebrew uses the general word for "tree" or "wood" in both places.
