## Text

**11:1** When Jabin, king of Hazor, had heard these things, he sent to Jobab, king of Madon, and to the king of Shimron, and to the king of Achshaph,

**11:2** and also to the kings of the north, who lived in the hill country and in the plain south of Chinneroth, in the lowlands also and in the regions of Dor beside the sea,

**11:3** and also to the Canaanite on the east and the west, and the Amorite, the Hittite, the Perizzite, and the Jebusite in the hill country, and the Hivite also, who lived at the foot of Hermon in the land of Mizpah.

**11:4** And they all went out with their troops, an exceedingly numerous people, like the sand that is on the shore of the sea, and horses and chariots also, in an immense multitude.

**11:5** And all these kings came together as one at the waters of Merom to fight against Israel.

**11:6** And the Lord said to Joshua, "Do not fear them, for tomorrow at this same hour I will deliver all of these to be wounded in the sight of Israel; you shall hamstring their horses and burn the chariots with fire."

**11:7** And Joshua, and the whole army with him, came suddenly against them at the waters of Merom, and they rushed upon them,

**11:8** and the Lord delivered them into the hands of Israel. And they struck them down and pursued them as far as Great Sidon, and the waters of Misrephoth, and the plain of Mizpah, which is on its eastern side. He struck all of them down so that he left no remnant of them.

**11:9** And he did just as the Lord had commanded him: he hamstrung their horses and burned the chariots with fire.

**11:10** And turning back at once, he captured Hazor and struck down its king with the sword. For in former times Hazor held the primacy among all these kingdoms.

**11:11** And he struck down all the souls who lived there; he left no remnant in it, but devastated everything to utter destruction, and destroyed the city itself by fire.

**11:12** And he captured, struck down, and destroyed all the surrounding cities and their kings, just as Moses the servant of the Lord had commanded him.

**11:13** Apart from the cities that were situated on hills and on mounds, Israel burned the rest; flame consumed one alone, Hazor, the most strongly fortified.

**11:14** And the sons of Israel divided for themselves all the plunder of these cities and the livestock, after all the people had been killed.

**11:15** Just as the Lord had commanded Moses His servant, so Moses commanded Joshua, and he fulfilled everything; he did not omit, from all the commands, even a single word that the Lord had ordered Moses.

**11:16** So Joshua captured all the hill country and the southern land, and the land of Goshen, and the plain, and the western region, and the mountain of Israel and its lowlands,

**11:17** and the part of the mountain that ascends to Seir, as far as Baal-gad, through the plain of Lebanon beneath Mount Hermon; all their kings he captured, struck down, and put to death.

**11:18** For a long time Joshua fought against these kings.

**11:19** There was not a city that surrendered itself to the sons of Israel, except the Hivite who lived in Gibeon; for he captured them all by waging war.

**11:20** For it had been the decree of the Lord that their hearts should be hardened, and that they should fight against Israel, and fall, and not deserve any mercy, and perish, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**11:21** At that time Joshua came and killed the Anakim from the hill country, from Hebron, Debir, and Anab, and from all the mountain of Judah and Israel, and destroyed their cities.

**11:22** He left no one of the lineage of the Anakim in the land of the sons of Israel, apart from the cities of Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod, in which alone they were left.

**11:23** Therefore Joshua captured the whole land, just as the Lord spoke to Moses, and gave it as a possession to the sons of Israel according to their portions and tribes; and the land rested from battles.

## Translator Notes

- **11:6** — Latin "vulnerandos" ("to be wounded") renders Hebrew "chalalim" ("pierced/killed"), where the Septuagint reads "put to flight."
- **11:8** — The Vulgate translates the second element of Hebrew "Misrephoth-maim" into Latin as "aquas Maserephoth" ("the waters of Misrephoth"), and shifts to the singular ("percussit ... dimitteret," referring to Joshua) where the Hebrew has the plural ("they struck").
- **11:13** — A distinctly Vulgate reading: Israel burned the other cities ("ceteras succendit Israël") apart from those on hills and mounds, and flame consumed Hazor alone, "the most strongly fortified" ("munitissimam," a Vulgate addition; "flamma" may be read as nominative "flame" or ablative "with flame"). By contrast, the Hebrew text states that Israel burned none of the cities standing on mounds except Hazor alone, which Joshua burned.
- **11:17** — Latin "partem montis" ("the part of the mountain," and similarly "cujus pars" in 12:7) interprets Hebrew "hahar hechalaq" ("Mount Halak" or "the bare mountain") from the root meaning "portion/part." In 11:16, the Vulgate likewise renders Hebrew "ha'arabah" ("the Arabah") as "occidentalem plagam" ("the western region").
- **11:20** — The Vulgate uses distinctive judicial phrasing—"sententia" ("decree"), passive "indurarentur" ("should be hardened"), and "non mererentur ullam clementiam" ("not deserve any mercy")—where the Hebrew reads more briefly "it was from the Lord to harden their hearts ... so that they might receive no mercy."
