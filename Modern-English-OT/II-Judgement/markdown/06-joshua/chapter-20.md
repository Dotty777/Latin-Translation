## Text

**20:1** And the Lord spoke to Joshua, saying, "Speak to the sons of Israel, and say to them,

**20:2** 'Set apart the cities of fugitives, about which I spoke to you through the hand of Moses,

**20:3** so that whoever has struck a person unknowingly may flee to them, and may be able to escape the wrath of the kinsman who is the avenger of blood.'"

**20:7** And they designated Kedesh in Galilee of the hill country of Naphtali, and Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, and Kiriath-arba, which is Hebron, in the hill country of Judah.

**20:8** And beyond the Jordan toward the eastern side of Jericho, they appointed Bezer, which is situated in the wilderness plain, from the tribe of Reuben, and Ramoth in Gilead, from the tribe of Gad, and Golan in Bashan, from the tribe of Manasseh.

**20:9** These cities were established for all the sons of Israel, and for the foreigners who lived among them, so that the one who had struck a person unknowingly might flee to them, and might not die by the hand of the kinsman desiring to avenge the shed blood, until he stood before the people to present his case.

## Translator Notes

- **20:2** — Vulgate reads "cities of fugitives" ("urbes fugitivorum") rather than the Hebrew "cities of refuge."
- **20:3** — Vulgate explicitly adds "the wrath of the kinsman" ("iram proximi"), paraphrasing the Hebrew "avenger of blood" as "the kinsman who is the avenger of blood," and condenses Hebrew "unintentionally, without knowledge" into "unknowingly" ("nescius").
- **20:9** — Vulgate expands "avenger of blood" into "the kinsman desiring to avenge the shed blood," and reads "before the people to present his case" where the Hebrew has "before the congregation for judgment."
