## Text

**9:1** When these things were heard, all the kings beyond the Jordan, who lived in the hill country and the plains, in the coastal regions and on the shore of the great sea, and those also who lived near Lebanon, the Hittite and the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite,

**9:2** gathered together as one to fight against Joshua and Israel with one mind and the same resolve.

**9:3** But those who lived in Gibeon, hearing all that Joshua had done to Jericho and Ai,

**9:4** and planning craftily, took provisions for themselves, putting old sacks on donkeys, and wineskins that were torn and sewn up,

**9:5** and very old sandals that had been sewn with patches as a sign of age, clothed in old garments; the loaves also, which they carried for travel provisions, were hard and crumbled into pieces:

**9:6** and they went to Joshua, who was then staying in the camp of Gilgal, and said to him, and at the same time to all Israel, "We have come from a distant land, desiring to make peace with you." And the men of Israel answered them and said:

**9:7** "Perhaps you live in the land that is owed to us by lot, and we cannot enter into a covenant with you."

**9:8** But they said to Joshua, "We are your servants." Joshua said to them, "Who are you? And where have you come from?"

**9:9** They answered, "From a very distant land your servants have come in the name of the Lord your God. For we have heard the report of His power, everything that He did in Egypt,

**9:10** and to the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, Sihon king of Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who was in Ashtaroth:

**9:11** and the elders and all the inhabitants of our land said to us, 'Take provisions in your hands for the very long journey, and go to meet them, and say, "We are your servants; enter into a covenant with us."'

**9:12** Look at these loaves: we took them warm when we went out from our houses to come to you; now they have become dry and crumbled from excessive age.

**9:13** We filled new wineskins; now they have burst and come apart. The garments and sandals in which we are clothed and which we have on our feet are worn out and almost consumed because of the length of the very long journey."

**9:14** Therefore they took some of their provisions, and did not inquire of the mouth of the Lord.

**9:15** And Joshua made peace with them, and having entered into a covenant, he promised that they would not be killed; the leaders of the multitude also swore to them.

**9:16** Now three days after the covenant had been entered into, they heard that they lived nearby and would be among them.

**9:17** And the sons of Israel moved camp and came to their cities on the third day, whose names are these: Gibeon, and Chephirah, and Beeroth, and Kiriath-jearim.

**9:18** And they did not strike them, because the leaders of the multitude had sworn to them in the name of the Lord God of Israel. Therefore the entire populace grumbled against the leaders.

**9:19** They answered them, "We have sworn to them in the name of the Lord God of Israel, and for that reason we cannot touch them.

**9:20** But this we will do to them: let them indeed be spared to live, so that the wrath of the Lord may not be stirred up against us if we perjure ourselves;

**9:21** rather, let them live in this way, so that they cut wood and carry water for the use of the whole multitude." While they were saying these things,

**9:22** Joshua called the Gibeonites and said to them, "Why did you want to deceive us by fraud, so as to say, 'We live very far from you,' when you are in our midst?

**9:23** Therefore you will be under a curse, and from your lineage there will not fail one cutting wood and carrying water into the house of my God."

**9:24** They answered, "It was reported to us, your servants, that the Lord your God had promised Moses His servant that He would hand over to you the entire land and destroy all its inhabitants. Therefore we were greatly afraid, and we provided for our lives, compelled by terror of you, and we entered into this plan.

**9:25** Now, however, we are in your hand: what seems good and right to you, do to us."

**9:26** So Joshua did as he had said, and rescued them from the hand of the sons of Israel, so that they would not be killed.

**9:27** And he decreed on that day that they should be in the service of all the people and of the altar of the Lord, cutting wood and carrying water, until the present time, in the place that the Lord would choose.

## Translator Notes

- **9:5** — "As a sign of age" translates the Vulgate explanatory phrase "ad indicium vetustatis," which has no equivalent in the Hebrew; similarly, the Vulgate describes the loaves as "hard and crumbled into pieces" where the Hebrew reads "dry and crumbly."
- **9:7** — "In the land that is owed to us by lot" follows the distinctly Vulgate clause "in terra, quæ nobis sorte debetur," whereas the Hebrew reads, "Perhaps you live among us."
- **9:16** — "Would be among them" preserves the Vulgate future periphrastic ("inter eos futuri essent"), whereas the Hebrew states simply that they were living in their midst.
