## Text

**2:1** Therefore Joshua, the son of Nun, sent two men from Shittim as spies in secret, and said to them, "Go and inspect the land, and the city of Jericho." Going on, they entered the house of a woman who was a prostitute, named Rahab, and rested at her house.

**2:2** And it was reported to the king of Jericho, and said, "Look, men from the sons of Israel have come in here by night to spy out the land."

**2:3** And the king of Jericho sent to Rahab, saying, "Bring out the men who came to you and entered your house, for indeed they are spies, and they have come to inspect all the land."

**2:4** And taking the men, the woman hid them and said, "I admit it, they came to me, but I did not know where they were from;

**2:5** and when the gate was being closed in the darkness, they also went out at the same time; I do not know where they went. Pursue them quickly, and you will catch them."

**2:6** But she herself had made the men go up onto the rooftop of her house, and had covered them with the stalks of flax that were there.

**2:7** Now those who had been sent followed them along the road that leads to the ford of the Jordan; and when they had gone out, the gate was immediately closed.

**2:8** Those who were hiding had not yet fallen asleep, and look, the woman went up to them and said:

**2:9** "I know that the Lord has delivered the land to you, for indeed terror of you has fallen upon us, and all the inhabitants of the land have grown faint.

**2:10** We have heard that the Lord dried up the waters of the Red Sea at your entry, when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, Sihon and Og, whom you killed.

**2:11** And hearing these things, we were greatly afraid, and our heart grew faint, nor did any spirit remain in us at your entry; for the Lord your God, He Himself is God in heaven above and on earth below.

**2:12** Now therefore, swear to me by the Lord that just as I have acted with mercy toward you, so you also will act toward the house of my father, and give me a true sign,

**2:13** that you will save my father and mother, and my brothers and sisters, and everything that belongs to them, and rescue our lives from death."

**2:14** They answered her, "May our life be for you to death, provided only that you do not betray us; and when the Lord delivers the land to us, we will act with mercy and truth toward you."

**2:15** So she let them down by a rope from the window, for her house was attached to the wall.

**2:16** And she said to them, "Climb up to the hill country, so that those returning may not happen to encounter you, and stay hidden there for three days until they return; and then you will go on your way."

**2:17** They said to her, "We will be guiltless of this oath by which you made us swear,

**2:18** if, as we enter the land, this scarlet cord is a sign, and you tie it in the window through which you let us down, and you gather your father and mother, and your brothers and all your relatives, into your house.

**2:19** Whoever goes out the door of your house, his own blood will be on his head, and we will be clear. But the blood of all who are with you in the house will fall back on our head if anyone touches them.

**2:20** However, if you choose to betray us and bring this matter out into the open, we will be free from this oath by which you made us swear."

**2:21** And she answered, "Just as you have spoken, so let it be." And sending them away so that they might go on, she hung the scarlet cord in the window.

**2:22** Now walking on, they reached the hill country and remained there three days, until those who had pursued them returned; for searching all along the road, they did not find them.

**2:23** When those men had entered the city, the spies turned back and came down from the mountain; and having crossed the Jordan, they came to Joshua, the son of Nun, and recounted to him everything that had happened to them,

**2:24** and said, "The Lord has delivered all this land into our hands, and all its inhabitants are laid low with fear."

## Translator Notes

- **2:10** — Here and in 2:11 the Vulgate reads 'ad vestrum introitum' / 'ad introitum vestrum' ('at your entry'), rendering the Hebrew preposition 'before you' or 'because of you' with a noun denoting Israel's arrival or approach.
- **2:16** — The Vulgate reads 'revertentes' ('those returning') rather than the Hebrew 'the pursuers.'
- **2:18** — The Vulgate connects 2:17 to 2:18 with 'si' ('if')—likely reading the Hebrew particle 'hinneh' ('look') as 'hen' ('if')—and adds 'signum' ('a sign'), stating the spies' discharge from the oath conditionally upon Rahab's compliance.
- **2:23** — The Vulgate opens with 'Quibus urbem ingressis' ('When those men had entered the city'), an explanatory transition absent from the Hebrew text.
