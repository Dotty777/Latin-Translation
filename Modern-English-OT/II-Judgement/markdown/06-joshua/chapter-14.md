## Text

**14:1** This is what the sons of Israel possessed in the land of Canaan, which Eleazar the priest, Joshua the son of Nun, and the leaders of the families throughout the tribes of Israel gave to them,

**14:2** dividing everything by lot, just as the Lord had commanded by the hand of Moses, for the nine tribes and the half-tribe.

**14:3** For to the two tribes and a half Moses had given a possession across the Jordan, apart from the Levites, who received no land among their brothers;

**14:4** but the sons of Joseph, divided into two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim, succeeded to their place; nor did the Levites receive any other portion in the land except cities to live in, and their surrounding lands for feeding their beasts of burden and their livestock.

**14:5** Just as the Lord had commanded Moses, so the sons of Israel did, and they divided the land.

**14:6** Therefore the sons of Judah approached Joshua in Gilgal, and Caleb the son of Jephunneh, the Kenizzite, spoke to him, "You know what the Lord spoke to Moses, the man of God, concerning me and you in Kadesh-barnea.

**14:7** I was forty years old when Moses, the servant of the Lord, sent me from Kadesh-barnea to examine the land, and I reported to him what seemed true to me.

**14:8** My brothers, however, who had gone up with me, melted the heart of the people; and nevertheless I followed the Lord my God.

**14:9** And Moses swore on that day, saying, 'The land that your foot stepped on will be your possession and that of your sons forever, because you have followed the Lord my God.'

**14:10** Therefore the Lord has granted me life, just as He promised, to the present day. It has been forty-five years since the Lord spoke this word to Moses, while Israel was walking through the wilderness; today I am eighty-five years old,

**14:11** just as strong as I was at that time when I was sent to explore; the strength of that time endures in me to this day, both for waging war and for marching.

**14:12** Therefore give me this hill country that the Lord promised while you also were listening, in which the Anakim are, and great and fortified cities, if perhaps the Lord will be with me and I will be able to wipe them out, just as He promised me."

**14:13** And Joshua blessed him, and gave him Hebron as a possession;

**14:14** and from then on Hebron belonged to Caleb the son of Jephunneh, the Kenizzite, to the present day, because he followed the Lord God of Israel.

**14:15** The name of Hebron was previously called Kiriath-arba; Adam, the greatest among the Anakim, lies buried there; and the land ceased from battles.

## Translator Notes

- **14:4** — Vulgate explicitly writes in eorum successerunt locum ("succeeded to their place") to explain how Joseph's two tribes took Levi's territorial place, and renders the Hebrew for "their livestock and possessions" as jumenta et pecora sua ("their beasts of burden and their livestock").
- **14:7** — Vulgate renders the Hebrew idiom "as it was in my heart" as quod mihi verum videbatur ("what seemed true to me").
- **14:8** — Here and in 14:9 and 14:14, Vulgate simply reads "followed the Lord" (secutus sum, secutus es, secutus est) without the Hebrew emphasis "wholly" or "fully."
- **14:15** — Reflecting an ancient tradition that Adam was buried at Hebron, Vulgate reads Hebrew ha-adam ("the man") as the proper name Adam and adds ibi ... situs est ("lies buried there").
