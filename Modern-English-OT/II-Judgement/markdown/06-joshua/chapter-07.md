## Text

**7:1** But the sons of Israel violated the command and appropriated some of what was devoted to destruction. For Achan, the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took something from what was devoted to destruction, and the Lord became angry against the sons of Israel.

**7:2** And when Joshua sent men from Jericho against Ai, which is beside Beth-aven on the eastern side of the town of Bethel, he said to them, "Go up and scout the land." Carrying out the commands, they scouted Ai.

**7:3** And when they returned, they said to him, "Do not let all the people go up, but let two or three thousand men go and wipe out the city. Why should all the people be worn out in vain against so very few enemies?"

**7:4** So three thousand fighting men went up. Immediately turning their backs,

**7:5** they were struck down by the men of the city of Ai, and thirty-six men among them fell. And the adversaries pursued them from the gate as far as Shebarim, and they fell while fleeing down the slopes; and the heart of the people grew terrified and melted like water.

**7:6** Then Joshua tore his garments and fell face down to the ground before the ark of the Lord until evening, both he and all the elders of Israel, and they threw dust on their heads.

**7:7** And Joshua said, "Ah, Lord God, why did You wish to lead this people across the Jordan River, to hand us over into the hands of the Amorite and destroy us? If only we had remained across the Jordan, just as we began!

**7:8** My Lord God, what shall I say, seeing Israel turning its back to its enemies?

**7:9** The Canaanites and all the inhabitants of the land will hear, and massing together as one, they will surround us and wipe out our name from the earth. And what will You do for Your great name?"

**7:10** And the Lord said to Joshua, "Get up! Why are you lying face down on the ground?

**7:11** Israel has sinned and has violated My covenant; they have taken from what was devoted to destruction, and have stolen and lied, and have hidden it among their belongings.

**7:12** And Israel will not be able to stand before its enemies, and it will flee from them, because it has been defiled by what was devoted to destruction. I will not be with you any longer until you crush the one who is guilty of this crime.

**7:13** Get up, sanctify the people, and say to them, 'Sanctify yourselves for tomorrow, for this is what the Lord God of Israel says: "What is devoted to destruction is in your midst, Israel; you will not be able to stand before your enemies until the one who is contaminated by this crime is wiped out from among you."

**7:14** And in the morning you shall come forward one by one by your tribes; and whichever tribe the lot finds shall come forward by its clans, and the clan by households, and the household by men.

**7:15** And whoever he may be who is caught in this misdeed shall be burned with fire along with all his property, because he has violated the covenant of the Lord and has committed an outrage in Israel.'"

**7:16** So Joshua rose in the morning and brought Israel forward by its tribes, and the tribe of Judah was found.

**7:17** When it had been presented according to its families, the family of Zerah was found. Presenting that family also by households, he found Zabdi.

**7:18** And dividing his household into individual men, he found Achan, the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah.

**7:19** And Joshua said to Achan, "My son, give glory to the Lord God of Israel and confess, and tell me what you have done; do not hide it."

**7:20** And Achan answered Joshua and said to him, "Truly I have sinned against the Lord God of Israel, and in this manner and that I have done.

**7:21** For I saw among the spoils a very fine scarlet cloak, and two hundred shekels of silver, and a golden bar of fifty shekels; and coveting them, I carried them off and hid them in the ground at the middle of my tent, and I covered the silver with dug-up earth."

**7:22** So Joshua sent attendants, who ran to his tent and discovered everything hidden in that same place, together with the silver.

**7:23** Removing them from the tent, they brought them to Joshua and to all the sons of Israel, and threw them down before the Lord.

**7:24** Therefore Joshua took Achan the son of Zerah, and the silver, the cloak, and the golden bar, his sons also and his daughters, the oxen, donkeys, and sheep, the tent itself, and all the household goods (and all Israel was with him), and they led them to the Valley of Achor,

**7:25** where Joshua said, "Because you have troubled us, may the Lord utterly trouble you on this day." And all Israel stoned him, and everything that belonged to him was consumed by fire.

**7:26** And they gathered over him a great heap of stones, which remains until the present day. And the fury of the Lord turned away from them. And the name of that place has been called the Valley of Achor until today.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — Latin anathema denotes spoils solemnly placed under the sacred ban and devoted to God for destruction; it is rendered consistently as "what was devoted to destruction" throughout the chapter. The Vulgate also specifies praevaricati sunt mandatum ("violated the command").
- **7:7** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew verb as ut coepimus ("just as we began"), taking the root in the sense of "to begin" rather than "to be content."
- **7:12** — The Vulgate reads pollutus est anathemate ("has been defiled by what was devoted to destruction") and directs the judgment personally against "the one who is guilty of this crime" (eum qui hujus sceleris reus est; similarly in 7:13), where the Hebrew refers to the devoted thing itself.
- **7:21** — The Vulgate reads pallium coccineum ("a scarlet cloak") rather than the Hebrew "mantle from Shinar," and renders the gold object as regulam auream ("a golden bar").
- **7:25** — The Latin pairs turbasti ("you have troubled") with the intensive exturbet ("utterly trouble"), echoing the wordplay connected with the name of the Valley of Achor ("trouble").
