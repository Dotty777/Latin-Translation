## Text

**3:1** Therefore Joshua, rising in the night, moved the camp; and going out from Shittim, he himself and all the sons of Israel came to the Jordan, and they stayed there three days.

**3:2** When these had elapsed, the heralds passed through the middle of the camp,

**3:3** and began to call out, "When you see the ark of the covenant of the Lord your God, and the priests of the Levitical lineage carrying it, you also rise up and follow those going ahead;

**3:4** and let there be between you and the ark a space of two thousand cubits, so that you may be able to see from a distance and know by which road you should go, because you have not walked along it before; and take care that you do not approach the ark."

**3:5** And Joshua said to the people, "Sanctify yourselves, for tomorrow the Lord will do wonders among you."

**3:6** And he said to the priests, "Take up the ark of the covenant, and go ahead of the people." Fulfilling the orders, they took it up and walked ahead of them.

**3:7** And the Lord said to Joshua, "Today I will begin to exalt you before all Israel, so that they may know that just as I was with Moses, so I am also with you.

**3:8** As for you, command the priests who carry the ark of the covenant, and say to them, 'When you have entered part of the water of the Jordan, stand in it.'"

**3:9** And Joshua said to the sons of Israel, "Come near here, and hear the word of the Lord your God."

**3:10** And again, "By this," he said, "you will know that the Lord, the living God, is in your midst, and He will destroy in your sight the Canaanite and the Hittite, the Hivite and the Perizzite, the Girgashite also and the Jebusite, and the Amorite.

**3:11** Look, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth will go ahead of you through the Jordan.

**3:12** Prepare twelve men from the tribes of Israel, one for each tribe.

**3:13** And when the priests who carry the ark of the Lord God of the whole earth place the soles of their feet in the waters of the Jordan, the waters that are lower will flow down and fail, while those that come from above will stand still in one mass."

**3:14** Therefore the people went out from their tents to cross the Jordan, and the priests who were carrying the ark of the covenant went on ahead of them.

**3:15** And when they had entered the Jordan, and their feet had been dipped in part of the water (for the Jordan had filled the banks of its channel at the time of harvest),

**3:16** the descending waters stood still in one place, and swelling up like a mountain, they appeared from a distance, from the city that is called Adam as far as the place Zarethan; while those that were lower went down into the Sea of the Wilderness (which is now called the Dead Sea), until they failed completely.

**3:17** Meanwhile the people marched opposite Jericho, and the priests who were carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord stood ready on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan, and all the people were crossing through the dried-up channel.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Vulgate reads 'de nocte' ('in the night') instead of the Hebrew 'in the morning,' and ends with 'et morati sunt ibi tres dies' ('and they stayed there three days') rather than 'and lodged there before they crossed.'
- **3:4** — The Vulgate adds 'ut procul videre possitis' ('so that you may be able to see from a distance') and places the warning against approaching the ark at the end of the verse.
- **3:16** — The Vulgate depicts the waters as 'ad instar montis intumescentes' ('swelling up like a mountain'), reads 'from the city that is called Adam [Latin Adom] as far as the place Zarethan [Latin Sarthan]' (Hebrew: 'at Adam, the city beside Zarethan'), and identifies the lower sea as 'the Sea of the Wilderness (which is now called the Dead Sea)' rather than 'the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea.'
- **3:17** — The Vulgate describes the priests as 'accincti' (literally 'girded' or 'standing ready'), corresponding to the Hebrew 'firmly' or 'steadfastly.'
