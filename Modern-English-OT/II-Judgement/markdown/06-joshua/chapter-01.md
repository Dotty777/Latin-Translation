## Text

**1:1** And it happened after the death of Moses, the servant of the Lord, that the Lord spoke to Joshua, the son of Nun, the attendant of Moses, and said to him:

**1:2** "Moses My servant is dead. Rise, and cross this Jordan, you and all the people with you, into the land that I will give to the sons of Israel.

**1:3** Every place that the sole of your foot treads on, I will deliver to you, just as I spoke to Moses.

**1:4** From the desert and Lebanon as far as the great river Euphrates, all the land of the Hittites as far as the great sea toward the setting of the sun will be your boundary.

**1:5** No one will be able to resist you all the days of your life; just as I was with Moses, so I will be with you. I will not let you go, nor will I abandon you.

**1:6** Be strong and resolute, for you will divide by lot to this people the land for which I swore to their fathers that I would deliver it to them.

**1:7** Be strong, therefore, and very resolute, so that you may keep and carry out all the law that Moses My servant commanded you. Do not turn aside from it to the right or to the left, so that you may understand all that you do.

**1:8** Let the scroll of this law not depart from your mouth, but you will meditate on it day and night, so that you may keep and carry out everything that is written in it; then you will direct your way, and you will understand it.

**1:9** Look, I command you: be strong and resolute. Do not dread, and do not fear, because the Lord your God is with you in all things, wherever you may go."

**1:10** And Joshua commanded the leaders of the people, saying, "Pass through the middle of the camp, and order the people, and say:

**1:11** 'Prepare provisions for yourselves, because after the third day you will cross the Jordan, and you will enter to possess the land that the Lord your God is about to give to you.'"

**1:12** To the Reubenites also, and to the Gadites, and to the half-tribe of Manasseh, he said:

**1:13** "Remember the word that Moses, the servant of the Lord, commanded you, saying, 'The Lord your God has given you rest, and all the land.'

**1:14** Your wives, and children, and livestock will remain in the land that Moses delivered to you beyond the Jordan; but you, cross over armed ahead of your brothers, all who are strong of hand, and fight for them,

**1:15** until the Lord gives rest to your brothers, just as He also gave to you, and they themselves also possess the land that the Lord your God is about to give to them; and so return to the land of your possession, and you will live in it, the land that Moses, the servant of the Lord, gave to you beyond the Jordan toward the rising of the sun."

**1:16** And they answered Joshua and said, "Everything that you have commanded us we will do, and wherever you send us we will go.

**1:17** Just as we obeyed Moses in everything, so we will also obey you; only may the Lord your God be with you, just as He was with Moses.

**1:18** Whoever contradicts your mouth and does not obey all the words that you command him, let him die. Only be strong yourself, and act courageously."

## Translator Notes

- **1:6** — The Vulgate reads 'sorte divides' ('divide by lot'), making the lottery allotment of the land explicit where the Hebrew reads simply 'cause to inherit.'
- **1:7** — The Vulgate renders this clause 'ut intelligas cuncta quæ agis' ('so that you may understand all that you do'), and similarly in 1:8 'diriges viam tuam, et intelliges eam' ('you will direct your way, and you will understand it'), emphasizing practical understanding where the Hebrew is commonly rendered 'succeed' or 'prosper.'
- **1:9** — The Vulgate opens with an emphatic command, 'Ecce præcipio tibi' ('Look, I command you'), whereas the Hebrew poses a rhetorical question ('Have I not commanded you?').
- **1:11** — The Vulgate specifies 'post diem tertium' ('after the third day'), matching the three-day stay in 3:1-2, whereas the Hebrew reads 'within three days.'
