## Text

**23:1** Now after much time had passed, after the Lord had given peace to Israel, with all the surrounding nations subdued, and Joshua was now advanced in years and of extreme old age,

**23:2** Joshua summoned all Israel, and the elders, and the leaders and commanders, and the officers, and said to them, "I have grown old and am well along in years;

**23:3** and you see everything that the Lord your God has done to all the nations all around, how He Himself has fought for you.

**23:4** And now, because He has divided the whole land for you by lot, from the eastern side of the Jordan to the Great Sea, and many nations still remain,

**23:5** the Lord your God will destroy them and remove them from before your face, and you will possess the land, just as He promised you.

**23:6** Only be strong and be careful to keep everything that is written in the scroll of the law of Moses, and do not turn aside from them either to the right or to the left,

**23:7** so that after you have gone in to the nations who will be among you, you do not swear by the name of their gods, nor serve them, nor worship them,

**23:8** but cling to the Lord your God, which you have done until this day.

**23:9** And then the Lord God will remove in your sight great and very strong nations, and no one will be able to resist you.

**23:10** One of you will pursue a thousand men of the enemies, because the Lord your God Himself will fight for you, just as He promised.

**23:11** Take care most diligently for this alone, that you love the Lord your God.

**23:12** But if you choose to cling to the errors of these nations who dwell among you, and to join in marriage with them, and to form friendships,

**23:13** know right now that the Lord your God will not wipe them out before your face, but they will be a pit and a snare to you, and a stumbling block at your side, and stakes in your eyes, until He removes and destroys you from this excellent land, which He delivered to you.

**23:14** Look, today I am entering the way of all the earth, and you will know with your whole mind that of all the words that the Lord promised He would fulfill for you, not one has passed in vain.

**23:15** Therefore, just as He has fulfilled in deed what He promised, and all prosperous things have come, so He will bring upon you whatever evils He has threatened, until He removes and destroys you from this excellent land, which He delivered to you,

**23:16** because you have transgressed the covenant of the Lord your God, which He made with you, and have served foreign gods and worshiped them: quickly and swiftly the fury of the Lord will rise up against you, and you will be removed from this excellent land, which He delivered to you."

## Translator Notes

- **23:4** — Vulgate reads third-person "divisit" ("He has divided") with the Lord as subject and specifies "from the eastern side of the Jordan," whereas the Hebrew text has Joshua speaking in the first person ("I have allotted") without "eastern side."
- **23:9** — Vulgate renders the verbs in the future tense ("auferet ... poterit", "will remove ... will be able to resist") introduced by "Et tunc" ("And then"), turning the Hebrew past-tense summary of victories into a conditional future promise.
- **23:12** — Vulgate reads "erroribus adhaerere" ("to cling to the errors") of these peoples, whereas the Hebrew text speaks of clinging to the "remnant" of these nations.
- **23:13** — Vulgate renders the images of judgment as "a stumbling block at your side, and stakes in your eyes" ("offendiculum ... sudes"), where the Hebrew text speaks of a "scourge" on the sides and "thorns" in the eyes.
