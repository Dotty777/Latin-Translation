## Text

**17:1** Now the lot fell to the tribe of Manasseh (for he is the firstborn of Joseph): to Machir, the firstborn of Manasseh, the father of Gilead, who was a warrior and had the possession of Gilead and Bashan,

**17:2** and to the rest of the sons of Manasseh according to their families: to the sons of Abiezer, and to the sons of Helek, and to the sons of Asriel, and to the sons of Shechem, and to the sons of Hepher, and to the sons of Shemida. These are the sons of Manasseh, the son of Joseph, the males, by their clans.

**17:3** Now Zelophehad, the son of Hepher, the son of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh, had no sons, but only daughters, whose names are these: Mahlah, and Noah, and Hoglah, and Milcah, and Tirzah.

**17:4** And they came into the presence of Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the leaders, saying, "The Lord commanded through the hand of Moses that a possession be given to us in the midst of our brothers." And he gave them, according to the command of the Lord, a possession in the midst of their father's brothers.

**17:5** And ten measuring lines fell to Manasseh, besides the land of Gilead and Bashan across the Jordan.

**17:6** For the daughters of Manasseh possessed an inheritance in the midst of his sons. And the land of Gilead fell to the lot of the sons of Manasseh who remained.

**17:7** And the border of Manasseh was from Asher to Michmethath, which faces Shechem; and it goes out to the right beside the inhabitants of the spring of Tappuah.

**17:8** For the land of Tappuah had fallen in the lot of Manasseh, which, beside the borders of Manasseh, belongs to the sons of Ephraim.

**17:9** And the border of the valley of the reed bed goes down to the south of the stream of the cities of Ephraim, which are in the midst of the cities of Manasseh; the border of Manasseh is on the north of the stream, and its extremity goes to the sea,

**17:10** so that the possession of Ephraim is on the south, and on the north that of Manasseh, and the sea encloses both, and they are joined to one another in the tribe of Asher on the north, and in the tribe of Issachar on the east.

**17:11** And the inheritance of Manasseh in Issachar and in Asher was Beth-shean and its villages, and Ibleam with its villages, and the inhabitants of Dor with its towns, the inhabitants of En-dor also with its villages, and likewise the inhabitants of Taanach with its villages, and the inhabitants of Megiddo with its villages, and a third part of the city of Naphoth.

**17:12** And the sons of Manasseh could not overthrow these cities, but the Canaanite began to live in his land.

**17:13** Now after the sons of Israel grew strong, they subdued the Canaanites and made them tributaries to themselves, and did not kill them.

**17:14** And the sons of Joseph spoke to Joshua and said, "Why have you given me the possession of one lot and measuring line, since I am so great a multitude, and the Lord has blessed me?"

**17:15** To them Joshua said, "If you are a numerous people, go up into the forest and cut out spaces for yourself in the land of the Perizzite and of the Rephaim, because the possession of Mount Ephraim is too narrow for you."

**17:16** The sons of Joseph answered him, "We will not be able to go up to the hill country, since the Canaanites use iron chariots, those who live in the level land in which are situated Beth-shean with its villages, and Jezreel, possessing the middle of the valley."

**17:17** And Joshua said to the house of Joseph, Ephraim and Manasseh, "You are a numerous people, and of great strength; you will not have one lot,

**17:18** but you will pass over to the mountain, and you will cut down for yourself and clear spaces to live in; and you will be able to proceed further when you have overthrown the Canaanite, who, you say, has iron chariots and is very strong."

## Translator Notes

- **17:8** — Latin quae est juxta terminos Manasse filiorum Ephraim attaches the relative pronoun to the town of Tappuah (Taphuae), using a genitive of possession (est ... filiorum Ephraim, 'belongs to the sons of Ephraim') to distinguish the town from its surrounding territory.
- **17:9** — Latin construes vallis arundineti ('of the valley of the reed bed') and civitatum Ephraim ('of the cities of Ephraim') as genitives modifying terminus and torrentis, whereas Hebrew reads 'to the brook of Kanah, south of the brook; these cities belong to Ephraim.'
- **17:11** — Latin et tertia pars urbis Nopheth ('and a third part of the city of Naphoth') renders Hebrew sheloshet han-nafet ('the three heights' or 'the third is Naphath') as a fraction of a city named Nopheth/Naphoth.
- **17:12** — Latin reads subvertere ('overthrow,' vs. Hebrew 'dispossess'), coepit ... habitare ('began to live,' vs. Hebrew 'persisted in living'), and in terra sua ('in his land,' vs. Hebrew 'in this land').
- **17:16** — Latin Non poterimus ad montana conscendere ('We will not be able to go up to the hill country') differs from Hebrew ('The hill country is not enough for us'); similarly in 17:18 Latin adds transibis ad montem and quem dicis ('who, you say').
