## Text

**21:1** And the heads of the families of Levi approached Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the leaders of the clans across each of the tribes of the sons of Israel.

**21:2** And they spoke to them in Shiloh of the land of Canaan and said, "The Lord commanded by the hand of Moses that cities be given to us to live in, and their outlying lands for feeding livestock."

**21:3** And the sons of Israel gave from their possessions, according to the command of the Lord, cities and their outlying lands.

**21:4** And the lot came out for the family of Kohath, of the sons of Aaron the priest: from the tribes of Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin, thirteen cities;

**21:5** and for the rest of the sons of Kohath, that is, the Levites who remained, from the tribes of Ephraim, Dan, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, ten cities.

**21:6** Furthermore, as for the sons of Gershon, the lot came out that they should receive from the tribes of Issachar, Asher, and Naphtali, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in Bashan, thirteen cities in number.

**21:7** And for the sons of Merari by their clans, from the tribes of Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun, twelve cities.

**21:8** And the sons of Israel gave to the Levites cities and their outlying lands, just as the Lord commanded by the hand of Moses, assigning to each one by lot.

**21:9** From the tribes of the sons of Judah and Simeon, Joshua gave cities, whose names are these,

**21:10** to the sons of Aaron by the families of Kohath of Levitical descent (for the first lot came out for them):

**21:11** Kiriath-arba of the father of Anak, which is called Hebron, in the hill country of Judah, and its outlying lands all around.

**21:12** Its fields and its villages, however, he had given to Caleb the son of Jephunneh to possess.

**21:13** Therefore he gave to the sons of Aaron the priest Hebron, a city of refuge, and its outlying lands, and Libnah with its outlying lands,

**21:14** and Jattir, and Eshtemoa,

**21:15** and Holon, and Debir,

**21:16** and Ain, and Juttah, and Beth-shemesh, with their outlying lands: nine cities from the two tribes, as has been said.

**21:17** And from the tribe of the sons of Benjamin, Gibeon and Geba,

**21:18** and Anathoth and Almon, with their outlying lands: four cities.

**21:19** All the cities together of the sons of Aaron the priest: thirteen, with their outlying lands.

**21:20** Now to the rest, by the families of the sons of Kohath of Levitical descent, this possession was given.

**21:21** From the tribe of Ephraim, cities of refuge: Shechem with its outlying lands in the hill country of Ephraim, and Gezer,

**21:22** and Kibzaim and Beth-horon, with their outlying lands, four cities.

**21:23** Also from the tribe of Dan, Elteke and Gibbethon,

**21:24** and Aijalon and Gath-rimmon, with their outlying lands, four cities.

**21:25** Furthermore, from the half-tribe of Manasseh, Taanach and Gath-rimmon, with their outlying lands, two cities.

**21:26** All ten cities and their outlying lands were given to the sons of Kohath of the lower rank.

**21:27** Also to the sons of Gershon of Levitical descent he gave from the half-tribe of Manasseh cities of refuge: Golan in Bashan and Bosra, with their outlying lands, two cities.

**21:28** Furthermore, from the tribe of Issachar, Kishion and Daberath,

**21:29** and Jarmuth and En-gannim, with their outlying lands, four cities.

**21:30** And from the tribe of Asher, Mishal and Abdon,

**21:31** and Helkath and Rehob, with their outlying lands, four cities.

**21:32** Also from the tribe of Naphtali, cities of refuge: Kedesh in Galilee, and Hammoth-dor, and Kartan, with their outlying lands, three cities.

**21:33** All the cities of the families of Gershon: thirteen, with their outlying lands.

**21:34** Now to the sons of Merari, the Levites of the lower rank, by their families, from the tribe of Zebulun were given Jokneam and Kartah,

**21:35** and Dimnah and Nahalal, four cities with their outlying lands.

**21:36** From the tribe of Reuben beyond the Jordan opposite Jericho, cities of refuge: Bezer in the wilderness, Misor, and Jaser, and Jethson, and Mephaath, four cities with their outlying lands.

**21:38** From the tribe of Gad, cities of refuge: Ramoth in Gilead, and Mahanaim, and Heshbon, and Jazer, four cities with their outlying lands.

**21:40** All the cities of the sons of Merari by their families and clans: twelve.

**21:41** Thus all the cities of the Levites in the midst of the possession of the sons of Israel were forty-eight,

**21:42** with their outlying lands, each one distributed by families.

**21:43** And the Lord God gave to Israel all the land that He had sworn He would deliver to their fathers, and they took possession of it and lived in it.

**21:44** And peace was given by Him toward all the nations all around; and none of the enemies dared to resist them, but all were brought under their dominion.

**21:45** Not even one word that He had promised He would perform for them proved void, but all things were fulfilled in deeds.

## Translator Notes

- **21:9** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which uses an unspecified plural ("they gave"), the Vulgate names Joshua as the singular subject ("Joshua gave") and maintains singular verbs in vv. 12, 13, and 27.
- **21:21** — While v. 13 uses the singular for Hebron, the Vulgate renders "cities of refuge" in the plural here and in vv. 27, 32, 36, and 38, omitting the Hebrew qualifier "for the manslayer" that singles out only the first-named city.
- **21:26** — Here and in v. 34, the Vulgate renders the remaining non-priestly Levites as those "of the lower rank" (inferioris gradus), distinguishing them from the priestly sons of Aaron (cf. v. 5, "that is, the Levites who remained").
- **21:27** — The Vulgate reads Bosram ("Bosra," matching the Septuagint Bosoran) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Be-eshterah.
- **21:36** — In the Clementine Vulgate, v. 36 encompasses modern vv. 36-37 (and v. 38 encompasses vv. 38-39), includes "beyond the Jordan opposite Jericho," and lists Misor (from Hebrew mishor, "tableland/plain"), Jaser, and Jethson alongside Bezer and Mephaath where the Hebrew tradition lists Bezer, Jahaz, Kedemoth, and Mephaath.
