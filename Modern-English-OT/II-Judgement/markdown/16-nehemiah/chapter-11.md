## Text

**11:1** Now the leaders of the people lived in Jerusalem, while the rest of the people cast lots to take one part out of ten who were to live in Jerusalem, the holy city, and nine parts in the cities.

**11:2** And the people blessed all the men who had offered themselves of their own accord to live in Jerusalem.

**11:3** Therefore these are the leaders of the province who lived in Jerusalem and in the cities of Judah. And each one lived in his own possession, in their cities: Israel, the priests, the Levites, the Nethinim, and the sons of the servants of Solomon.

**11:4** And in Jerusalem lived some of the sons of Judah and some of the sons of Benjamin: of the sons of Judah, Athaiah son of Aziam, son of Zechariah, son of Amariah, son of Shephatiah, son of Mahalalel; of the sons of Perez,

**11:5** Maaseiah son of Baruch, son of Col-hozeh, son of Hazaiah, son of Adaiah, son of Joiarib, son of Zechariah, son of the Shilonite:

**11:6** all these sons of Perez who lived in Jerusalem were four hundred sixty-eight valiant men.

**11:7** Now these are the sons of Benjamin: Shallum son of Meshullam, son of Joed, son of Pedaiah, son of Kolaiah, son of Maaseiah, son of Ithiel, son of Jeshaiah,

**11:8** and after him, Gabbai, Sallai: nine hundred twenty-eight,

**11:9** and Joel son of Zichri was their chief officer, and Judah son of Senuah was second over the city.

**11:10** And of the priests: Jedaiah son of Joiarib, Jachin,

**11:11** Seraiah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub, leader of the house of God,

**11:12** and their brothers who did the work of the temple: eight hundred twenty-two. And Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pelaliah, son of Amzi, son of Zechariah, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah,

**11:13** and his brothers, leaders of the fathers: two hundred forty-two. And Amashsai son of Azarel, son of Ahzai, son of Meshillemoth, son of Immer,

**11:14** and their brothers, very mighty men: one hundred twenty-eight, and their chief officer was Zabdiel, son of the mighty men.

**11:15** And of the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, son of Bunni,

**11:17** And Mattaniah son of Mica, son of Zabdi, son of Asaph, the leader for praising and for giving thanks in prayer, and Bakbukiah, second among his brothers, and Abda son of Shammua, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun:

**11:18** all the Levites in the holy city were two hundred eighty-four.

**11:19** And the gatekeepers: Akkub, Talmon, and their brothers who guarded the doors, one hundred seventy-two.

**11:22** And the overseer of the Levites in Jerusalem was Uzzi son of Bani, son of Hashabiah, son of Mattaniah, son of Mica. Of the sons of Asaph were the singers in the ministry of the house of God.

**11:23** For an ordinance of the king was over them, and an order among the singers for each day,

**11:24** and Pethahiah son of Meshezabel, of the sons of Zerah, son of Judah, was at the king's hand concerning every matter of the people,

**11:25** and in the houses throughout all their regions. Some of the sons of Judah lived in Kiriath-arba and in its daughters, and in Dibon and in its daughters, and in Kabzeel and in its small villages,

**11:26** and in Jeshua, and in Moladah, and in Beth-pelet,

**11:27** and in Hazar-shual, and in Beersheba and in its daughters,

**11:30** Zanoah, Adullam, and in their villages, Lachish and its regions, and Azekah and its daughters. And they remained in Beersheba as far as the Valley of Hinnom.

**11:31** Now the sons of Benjamin, from Geba: Michmash, and Ai, and Bethel and its daughters,

**11:36** And of the Levites were portions of Judah and Benjamin.

## Translator Notes

- **11:4** — The Vulgate reads Aziam here, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has Uzziah; the Clementine punctuation also joins 'of the sons of Perez' to the following verse rather than exclusively to Athaiah.
- **11:7** — The Vulgate reads Sellum (Shallum), where the Hebrew text reads Sallu.
- **11:14** — Latin filius potentium ('son of the mighty men') translates Hebrew ben-haggedolim as a descriptive title rather than a proper name ('son of Haggedolim').
- **11:17** — The Vulgate reads 'for praising and for giving thanks' (ad laudandum et ad confitendum), reflecting tehillah ('praise') where the Masoretic Text has techillah ('beginning').
- **11:25** — Following the Clementine punctuation, 'and in the houses throughout all their regions' concludes the sentence from v. 24. Throughout vv. 25-31, 'daughters' (filiabus) preserves the literal Hebraism for dependent surrounding towns, and Cabseel (Kabzeel) corresponds to Hebrew Jekabzeel (cf. Josh 15:21).
- **11:21 *(additional)*** — The Nethinim were a class of temple servants.
