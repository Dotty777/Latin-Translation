## Text

**12:1** Now these are the priests and the Levites who went up with Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, and Joshua: Seraiah, Jeremiah, Ezra,

**12:2** Amariah, Malluch, Hattush,

**12:3** Shebaniah, Rehum, Meremoth,

**12:7** Jedaiah. These were the leaders of the priests and their brothers in the days of Joshua.

**12:8** Moreover, the Levites were Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel, Sherebiah, Judah, and Mattaniah, who were over the hymns, they and their brothers;

**12:9** and Bakbukiah and Unni, and their brothers, each one in his own duty.

**12:10** Now Joshua fathered Joiakim, and Joiakim fathered Eliashib, and Eliashib fathered Joiada,

**12:11** and Joiada fathered Jonathan, and Jonathan fathered Jaddua.

**12:12** Now in the days of Joiakim, there were priests and leaders of the families: of Seraiah, Meraiah; of Jeremiah, Hananiah;

**12:13** of Ezra, Meshullam; of Amariah, Johanan;

**12:14** of Malluchi, Jonathan; of Shebaniah, Joseph;

**12:15** of Harim, Adna; of Meraioth, Helkai;

**12:16** of Adaiah, Zechariah; of Ginnethon, Meshullam;

**12:17** of Abijah, Zichri; of Mijamin and Moadiah, Piltai;

**12:18** of Bilgah, Shammua; of Shemaiah, Jonathan;

**12:19** of Joiarib, Mattenai; of Jedaiah, Uzzi;

**12:20** of Sallai, Kallai; of Amok, Eber;

**12:21** of Hilkiah, Hashabiah; of Jedaiah, Nethanel.

**12:22** The Levites in the days of Eliashib, Joiada, Johanan, and Jaddua were recorded as leaders of the families, and the priests in the reign of Darius the Persian.

**12:23** The sons of Levi, the leaders of the families, were recorded in the book of the words of the days, even up to the days of Jonathan the son of Eliashib.

**12:24** And the leaders of the Levites were Hashabiah, Sherebiah, and Joshua the son of Kadmiel, and their brothers in their turns, to praise and give thanks according to the command of David, the man of God, and to keep watch equally in order.

**12:25** Mattaniah, Bakbukiah, Obadiah, Meshullam, Talmon, and Akkub were guards of the gates and of the vestibules before the gates.

**12:26** These were in the days of Joiakim the son of Joshua, the son of Jozadak, and in the days of Nehemiah the governor, and of Ezra the priest and scribe.

**12:27** Now at the dedication of the wall of Jerusalem, they sought out the Levites from all their places to bring them to Jerusalem and celebrate the dedication and rejoicing with thanksgiving and song, and with cymbals, harps, and lyres.

**12:28** And the sons of the singers were gathered together from the plains around Jerusalem, and from the villages of the Netophathites,

**12:29** and from the house of Gilgal, and from the regions of Geba and Azmaveth; for the singers had built villages for themselves all around Jerusalem.

**12:30** And the priests and the Levites were cleansed, and they cleansed the people, the gates, and the wall.

**12:31** Then I had the leaders of Judah go up onto the wall, and I stationed two large choirs of those giving praise. And they went to the right on the wall toward the Dung Gate.

**12:32** And Hoshaiah went after them, and half of the leaders of Judah,

**12:33** and Azariah, Ezra, Meshullam,

**12:34** Judah, Benjamin, Shemaiah, and Jeremiah.

**12:35** And from the sons of the priests with trumpets: Zechariah the son of Jonathan, the son of Shemaiah, the son of Mattaniah, the son of Micaiah, the son of Zaccur, the son of Asaph,

**12:36** and his brothers Shemaiah, Azarel, Milalai, Gilalai, Maai, Nethanel, Judah, and Hanani, with the instruments of song of David, the man of God; and Ezra the scribe was before them at the Fountain Gate.

**12:37** And opposite them they went up by the stairs of the city of David, at the ascent of the wall above the house of David, and as far as the Water Gate to the east.

**12:38** And the second choir of those giving thanks went on the opposite side, and I after it, and half of the people on the wall, and above the Tower of the Ovens, and as far as the very broad wall,

**12:39** and above the Gate of Ephraim, and above the Old Gate, and above the Fish Gate, and the Tower of Hananel, and the Tower of Emath, and as far as the Gate of the Flock; and they stood at the Gate of the Guard,

**12:40** and the two choirs of those giving praise stood in the house of God, and I, and half of the magistrates with me.

**12:41** And the priests, Eliakim, Maaseiah, Mijamin, Micaiah, Elioenai, Zechariah, and Hananiah, with trumpets,

**12:42** and Maaseiah, Shemaiah, Eleazar, Uzzi, Johanan, Malchijah, Elam, and Ezer. And the singers sang out clearly, and Jezrahiah was the overseer;

**12:43** and they offered great sacrifices on that day and rejoiced, for God had made them joyful with great joy; and their wives and children also were glad, and the joy of Jerusalem was heard far away.

**12:44** They also assigned men on that day over the storerooms of the treasury for the drink offerings, the firstfruits, and the tithes, so that the leaders of the city might bring in the priests and the Levites through them in the beauty of thanksgiving; for Judah was made joyful in the priests and the Levites who stood in attendance.

**12:45** And they kept the charge of their God and the charge of expiation, and so did the singers and the gatekeepers, according to the command of David and of Solomon his son,

**12:46** for in the days of David and Asaph from the beginning, there were appointed leaders of the singers praising and giving thanks to God in song.

**12:47** And all Israel in the days of Zerubbabel and in the days of Nehemiah gave the portions to the singers and to the gatekeepers day by day, and they consecrated the Levites, and the Levites consecrated the sons of Aaron.

## Translator Notes

- **12:17** — The Vulgate links Mijamin (Hebrew Miniamin) and Moadiah with et ('and') under a single leader, Piltai, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text leaves a gap after Miniamin; similarly, the Vulgate reads Shebaniah in v. 3 (Hebrew Shecaniah) and Adaiah in v. 16 (Hebrew Iddo).
- **12:23** — The Latin in libro verborum dierum ('in the book of the words of the days') renders the Hebrew idiom for 'chronicles' literally; the Vulgate also reads Jonathan here where the Hebrew has Johanan.
- **12:39** — The Vulgate reads turrim Emath ('the Tower of Emath') where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the Tower of the Hundred (Hammeah), and portam gregis ('the Gate of the Flock') for the Sheep Gate.
- **12:44** — This verse preserves several distinctive Vulgate readings: principes civitatis ('leaders of the city') for Hebrew 'fields of the cities', in decore gratiarum actionis ('in the beauty of thanksgiving') for Hebrew 'portions of the law', and direct accusatives sacerdotes et Levitas ('the priests and the Levites') rather than 'for the priests and the Levites'.
- **12:47** — The Vulgate construes Levitas and filios Aaron as direct objects of sanctificabant ('consecrated the Levites ... consecrated the sons of Aaron'), whereas the Hebrew uses indirect objects ('set apart [holy portions] for the Levites ... for the sons of Aaron').
