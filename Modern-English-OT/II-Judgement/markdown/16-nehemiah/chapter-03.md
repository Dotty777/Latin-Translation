## Text

**3:1** And Eliashib the high priest rose up, and his brothers the priests, and they built the Flock Gate; they consecrated it and set up its doors, and as far as the tower of a hundred cubits they consecrated it, as far as the Tower of Hananel.

**3:2** And next to him the men of Jericho built, and next to him Zaccur son of Imri built.

**3:3** Now the sons of Hassenaah built the Fish Gate; they covered it, and set up its doors, and bolts, and bars. And next to them Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, built.

**3:4** And next to him Meshullam son of Berechiah, son of Meshezabel, built; and next to them Zadok son of Baana built.

**3:5** And next to them the Tekoites built; however, their nobles did not submit their necks in the work of their Lord.

**3:6** And Joiada son of Paseah and Meshullam son of Besodeiah built the Old Gate; they covered it, and set up its doors, and bolts, and bars.

**3:8** And next to him Uzziel son of Harhaiah, a goldsmith, built; and next to him Hananiah, the son of a perfumer, built; and they left Jerusalem as far as the wall of the broader street.

**3:9** And next to him Rephaiah son of Hur, ruler of the district of Jerusalem, built.

**3:10** And next to him Jedaiah son of Harumaph built opposite his own house; and next to him Hattush son of Hashabneiah built.

**3:11** Malchijah son of Harim and Hasshub son of Pahath-moab built half of the district, and the Tower of the Ovens.

**3:12** And next to him Shallum son of Hallohesh, ruler of half of the district of Jerusalem, built, he and his daughters.

**3:13** And Hanun and the inhabitants of Zanoah built the Valley Gate; they built it, and set up its doors, and bolts, and bars, and a thousand cubits in the wall as far as the Dung Gate.

**3:14** And Malchijah son of Rechab, ruler of the district of Beth-haccherem, built the Dung Gate; he built it, and set up its doors, and bolts, and bars.

**3:15** And Shallum son of Col-hozeh, ruler of the territory of Mizpah, built the Fountain Gate; he built it, and covered it, and set up its doors, and bolts, and bars, and the walls of the Pool of Siloam toward the king's garden, and as far as the steps that go down from the City of David.

**3:16** After him Nehemiah son of Azbuk, ruler of half of the district of Beth-zur, built as far as opposite the tomb of David, and as far as the pool that was constructed with great labor, and as far as the house of the mighty men.

**3:17** After him the Levites built: Rehum son of Bani; after him Hashabiah, ruler of half of the district of Keilah, built in his own district.

**3:18** After him their brothers built: Bavvai son of Henadad, ruler of half of Keilah;

**3:19** and next to him Ezer son of Joshua, ruler of Mizpah, built a second measure, opposite the ascent of the strongest corner.

**3:20** After him, on the mountain, Baruch son of Zaccai built a second measure, from the corner as far as the gate of the house of Eliashib the high priest.

**3:21** After him Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, built a second measure, from the gate of the house of Eliashib as far as the house of Eliashib extended.

**3:22** And after him the priests built, men from the plains of the Jordan.

**3:23** After him Benjamin and Hasshub built opposite their own house; and after him Azariah son of Maaseiah, son of Ananiah, built opposite his own house.

**3:24** After him Binnui son of Henadad built a second measure, from the house of Azariah as far as the bend, and as far as the corner.

**3:25** Palal son of Uzai, opposite the bend and the tower that projects from the upper house of the king, that is, in the court of the prison; after him, Pedaiah son of Parosh.

**3:26** Now the Nethinim lived in Ophel, as far as opposite the Water Gate toward the east and the tower that jutted out.

**3:27** After him the Tekoites built a second measure opposite, from the great projecting tower as far as the wall of the temple.

**3:28** Now above the Horse Gate the priests built, each one opposite his own house.

**3:29** After them Zadok son of Immer built opposite his own house. And after him Shemaiah son of Shecaniah, keeper of the East Gate, built.

**3:30** After him Hananiah son of Shelemiah and Hanun, the sixth son of Zalaph, built a second measure; after him Meshullam son of Berechiah built opposite his own treasury. After him Malchijah, the son of a goldsmith, built as far as the house of the Nethinim and of those selling small wares, opposite the Judicial Gate, and as far as the upper room of the corner.

**3:32** And between the upper room of the corner and the Flock Gate, the goldsmiths and the merchants built.

**3:33** Now it happened, when Sanballat had heard that we were building the wall, that he became very angry; and greatly agitated, he mocked the Jews,

**3:34** and he said before his brothers and the throng of the Samaritans, "What are the feeble Jews doing? Will the nations let them alone? Will they sacrifice, and finish in one day? Will they be able to build the stones from the heaps of dust, which have been burned?"

**3:35** Moreover, Tobiah the Ammonite, who was next to him, said, "Let them build! If a fox climbs up, it will leap over their stone wall."

**3:36** Hear, our God, for we have become an object of contempt; turn back the reproach upon their head, and give them over to contempt in a land of captivity.

**3:37** Do not cover their iniquity, and let not their sin be blotted out before Your face, because they have derided the builders.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Throughout chapter 3, the Vulgate uniformly uses aedifico ("build") where the Hebrew distinguishes between "build" and "repair/strengthen." Here it also reads "Flock Gate" (portam gregis, Hebrew "Sheep Gate") and renders the Hebrew Tower of the Hundred (Meah) as "the tower of a hundred cubits" (turrim centum cubitorum).
- **3:5** — "Of their Lord" translates the Vulgate's singular Domini sui (referring to God), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has plural "their lords" (understood either as their supervisors or as an honorific plural for God).
- **3:8** — "They left Jerusalem as far as the wall of the broader street" translates the Vulgate's dimiserunt ... murum plateae latioris, whereas the Hebrew is often understood as "they restored Jerusalem as far as the Broad Wall."
- **3:15** — "Pool of Siloam" translates the Vulgate's piscinae Siloe, identifying the Hebrew Pool of Shelah with the Siloam pool of John 9:7. Other notable Vulgate readings in this section include "half of the district" in 3:11 (Hebrew "a second section"), "the strongest corner" in 3:19 (Hebrew "the armory at the angle"), "on the mountain" in 3:20 (Hebrew "zealously"), "of the Jordan" in 3:22, and "the wall of the temple" in 3:27 (Hebrew "the wall of Ophel").
- **3:34** — In 3:34-36 the Vulgate preserves several distinctive readings: "Will the nations let them alone?" (Hebrew "Will they restore it for themselves?"), "build the stones" (Hebrew "revive the stones"), "it will leap over" (transiliet in 3:35, Hebrew "break down"), and "to contempt" (in despectionem in 3:36, Hebrew "to plunder").
