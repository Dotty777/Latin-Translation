## Text

**10:1** Therefore, because of all these things, we ourselves make a covenant and write it down, and our leaders, our Levites, and our priests seal it.

**10:2** Now those who sealed it were Nehemiah the Tirshatha, son of Hacaliah, and Zedekiah,

**10:3** Seraiah, Azariah, Jeremiah,

**10:4** Pashhur, Amariah, Malchijah,

**10:5** Hattush, Shebaniah, Malluch,

**10:6** Harim, Meremoth, Obadiah,

**10:7** Daniel, Ginnethon, Baruch,

**10:8** Meshullam, Abijah, Mijamin,

**10:9** Maaziah, Bilgai, Shemaiah: these were the priests.

**10:10** Next, the Levites were Jeshua son of Azaniah, Binnui of the sons of Henadad, Kadmiel,

**10:11** and their brothers: Shebaniah, Hodiah, Kelita, Pelaiah, Hanan,

**10:12** Mica, Rehob, Hashabiah,

**10:13** Zaccur, Sherebiah, Shebaniah,

**10:14** Hodiah, Bani, Beninu.

**10:15** The heads of the people were Parosh, Pahath-moab, Elam, Zattu, Bani,

**10:16** Bunni, Azgad, Bebai,

**10:17** Adonijah, Bigvai, Adin,

**10:18** Ater, Hezekiah, Azzur,

**10:19** Hodiah, Hashum, Bezai,

**10:20** Hariph, Anathoth, Nebai,

**10:21** Magpiash, Meshullam, Hezir,

**10:22** Meshezabel, Zadok, Jaddua,

**10:23** Pelatiah, Hanan, Anaiah,

**10:24** Hoshea, Hananiah, Hasshub,

**10:25** Hallohesh, Pilha, Shobek,

**10:26** Rehum, Hashabnah, Maaseiah,

**10:27** Ahijah, Hanan, Anan,

**10:28** Malluch, Haran, Baanah.

**10:29** And the rest of the people, the priests, the Levites, the gatekeepers, and the singers, the Nethinim, and all who separated themselves from the peoples of the lands to the law of God, their wives, their sons, and their daughters,

**10:30** all who were able to understand, pledged on behalf of their brothers, their nobles, and came to promise and to swear that they would walk in the law of God, which He had given by the hand of Moses the servant of God, that they would do and keep all the commandments of the Lord our God, and His judgments and His ceremonies,

**10:31** and that we would not give our daughters to the people of the land, and would not take their daughters for our sons.

**10:32** Also, as for the peoples of the land who bring wares and all things for use on the Sabbath day to sell, we will not buy from them on the Sabbath or on a consecrated day. And we will forgo the seventh year and the exaction of every hand.

**10:33** And we will establish ordinances upon ourselves to give a third part of a shekel each year for the work of the house of our God,

**10:34** for the loaves of presentation, and for the perpetual sacrifice, and for the perpetual burnt offering on the Sabbaths, on the new moons, on the solemn feasts, and for the consecrated offerings, and for the sin offering so that propitiation may be made for Israel, and for every use of the house of our God.

**10:35** Therefore we cast lots concerning the offering of wood among the priests, the Levites, and the people, that it should be brought into the house of our God according to the houses of our fathers, at set times, from the times of one year to the next, to burn on the altar of the Lord our God, just as it is written in the law of Moses,

**10:36** and that we would bring the first produce of our land, and the firstfruits of all the fruit of every tree, from year to year, to the house of the Lord,

**10:37** and the firstborn of our sons and of our livestock, just as it is written in the law, and the firstborn of our oxen and of our sheep, that they should be offered in the house of our God to the priests who minister in the house of our God.

**10:38** And we will bring the firstfruits of our food and of our drink offerings, and the fruit of every tree, also of the vintage and of the oil, to the priests, to the treasury of our God, and the tenth part of our land to the Levites. The Levites themselves will receive the tithes from all the cities of our labors.

**10:39** And the priest, the son of Aaron, will be with the Levites in the tithes of the Levites, and the Levites will offer the tenth part of their tithe in the house of our God, to the treasury, into the treasure house.

**10:40** For to the treasury the sons of Israel and the sons of Levi will bring the firstfruits of grain, wine, and oil; and there will be the consecrated vessels, and the priests, and the singers, and the gatekeepers, and the ministers; and we will not abandon the house of our God.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — In the Vulgate, chapter 10 begins one verse earlier than in the Hebrew Masoretic Text (where this verse is 9:38), shifting all subsequent verse numbers in the chapter by one. Latin foedus renders Hebrew amanah ('binding agreement') with the standard covenant term.
- **10:2** — Latin Athersatha ("the Tirshatha") transliterates the Persian title hattirshata ('the governor'), borne by Nehemiah (cf. Neh 8:9).
- **10:28** — The Vulgate reads Haran here, whereas the Hebrew text reads Harim (cf. Harem in v. 6).
- **10:30** — The Vulgate renders this clause distinctively as 'pledging on behalf of their brothers' (spondentes pro fratribus suis) and 'to promise and to swear' (ad pollicendum et jurandum), where the Hebrew reads 'joining with their brothers' and 'entering into a curse and an oath.'
- **10:40** — The Vulgate lists four distinct groups in the order 'priests, singers, gatekeepers, and ministers' (et ministri), separating the Hebrew participle ha-mesharetim ('who minister') from the priests.
