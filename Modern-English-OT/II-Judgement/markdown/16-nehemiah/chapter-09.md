## Text

**9:1** Now on the twenty-fourth day of this month, the sons of Israel assembled in fasting and in sackcloth, with earth upon them.

**9:2** And the seed of the sons of Israel was separated from every son of a foreigner; and they stood and were confessing their sins and the iniquities of their fathers.

**9:3** And they rose up to stand, and they read in the scroll of the law of the Lord their God four times in the day, and four times they were confessing and worshiping the Lord their God.

**9:4** And Jeshua, and Bani, and Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, and Chenani rose up upon the platform of the Levites, and they cried out with a loud voice to the Lord their God.

**9:5** And the Levites Jeshua, and Kadmiel, Bunni, Hashabniah, Sherebiah, Hodiah, Shebaniah, Pethahiah said, "Arise, bless the Lord your God from eternity to eternity; and let them bless the name of Your glory, exalted in all blessing and praise.

**9:6** You Yourself, Lord, alone, You made heaven, and the heaven of heavens, and all their host; the earth, and all things that are in it; the seas, and everything that is in them; and You give life to all these things, and the host of heaven worships You.

**9:7** It is You Yourself, Lord God, who chose Abram, and brought him out from the fire of the Chaldeans, and gave him the name Abraham;

**9:8** and You found his heart faithful before You, and You struck a covenant with him to give him the land of the Canaanite, the Hittite, the Amorite, the Perizzite, the Jebusite, and the Girgashite, to give it to his seed; and You fulfilled Your words, because You are righteous.

**9:9** And You saw the affliction of our fathers in Egypt, and You heard their cry by the Red Sea.

**9:10** And You gave signs and wonders upon Pharaoh, and upon all his servants, and upon all the people of that land; for You knew that they had acted arrogantly against them; and You made a name for Yourself, even as on this day.

**9:11** And You divided the sea before them, and they crossed through the middle of the sea on dry ground; but their pursuers You cast into the deep, like a stone into mighty waters.

**9:12** And in a pillar of cloud You were their guide by day, and in a pillar of fire by night, so that the way along which they were going might appear to them.

**9:13** You also came down to Mount Sinai, and spoke with them from heaven, and gave them right judgments and a law of truth, ceremonies, and good precepts;

**9:14** and You showed them Your consecrated Sabbath; and commandments, and ceremonies, and the law You commanded them by the hand of Moses Your servant.

**9:15** You also gave them bread from heaven in their hunger, and You brought out water from the rock for them when they were thirsty, and You told them to enter and possess the land over which You lifted up Your hand to deliver it to them.

**9:16** But they and our fathers acted arrogantly, and hardened their necks, and did not listen to Your commandments.

**9:17** And they refused to listen, and did not remember Your wonders that You had done for them. And they hardened their necks, and appointed a leader so that they might return to their servitude, as if through contention. But You, a forgiving God, gracious and merciful, long-suffering and of great compassion, did not abandon them,

**9:18** even when they had made for themselves a molten calf, and had said, 'This is your god, who brought you out of Egypt,' and committed great blasphemies;

**9:19** yet You, in Your many mercies, did not abandon them in the desert: the pillar of cloud did not depart from them by day to lead them on the way, and the pillar of fire by night to show them the path along which they should go.

**9:20** And You gave Your good Spirit to teach them; and Your manna You did not withhold from their mouth, and You gave them water in thirst.

**9:21** For forty years You fed them in the desert, and nothing was lacking to them: their garments did not grow old, and their feet were not worn down.

**9:22** And You gave them kingdoms and peoples, and You apportioned lots to them; and they possessed the land of Sihon, and the land of the king of Heshbon, and the land of Og king of Bashan.

**9:23** And You multiplied their sons like the stars of heaven, and You brought them to the land about which You had spoken to their fathers, that they should enter and possess it.

**9:24** And the sons came and possessed the land, and You humbled before them the inhabitants of the land, the Canaanites, and You gave them into their hand, and their kings, and the peoples of the land, so that they might do to them as pleased them.

**9:25** So they captured fortified cities and rich soil, and possessed houses full of all good things: cisterns built by others, vineyards, and olive groves, and many fruit-bearing trees; and they ate, and were filled, and grew fat, and abounded in delights in Your great goodness.

**9:26** But they provoked You to wrath, and departed from You, and cast Your law behind their backs; and they killed Your prophets, who were admonishing them to return to You; and they committed great blasphemies.

**9:27** And You gave them into the hand of their adversaries, and they afflicted them. And in the time of their distress they cried out to You, and You heard from heaven, and according to Your many compassions You gave them saviors, who would save them from the hand of their adversaries.

**9:28** And when they had rested, they returned to doing evil in Your sight, and You abandoned them in the hand of their enemies, and they possessed them. And they turned back and cried out to You; but You heard from heaven, and You rescued them in Your mercies, many times.

**9:29** And You admonished them to return to Your law. But they acted arrogantly, and did not listen to Your commandments, and sinned against Your judgments, which a man will do and live in them; and they gave a withdrawing shoulder, and hardened their neck, and did not listen.

**9:30** And You prolonged many years over them, and admonished them in Your Spirit through the hand of Your prophets; and they did not listen, and You delivered them into the hand of the peoples of the lands.

**9:31** Yet in Your most abundant mercies You did not bring them to complete destruction, nor did You abandon them; for You are a God of compassions, and gracious.

**9:32** Now therefore, our God, great, mighty, and awesome, who keeps covenant and mercy, do not turn away from Your face all the hardship that has found us, our kings, and our leaders, and our priests, and our prophets, and our fathers, and all Your people, from the days of the king of Assyria even to this day.

**9:33** And You are righteous in all things that have come upon us; for You have acted in truth, while we have acted wickedly.

**9:34** Our kings, our leaders, our priests, and our fathers did not carry out Your law, and did not heed Your commandments and Your testimonies that You testified among them.

**9:35** And they themselves, in their kingdoms, and in Your great goodness that You had given them, and in the very broad and fertile land that You had delivered in their sight, did not serve You, nor did they turn back from their most wicked pursuits.

**9:36** Look, we ourselves are slaves today; and as for the land that You gave to our fathers so that they might eat its bread and what is good in it, we ourselves are slaves in it.

**9:37** And its crops are multiplied for the kings whom You have set over us because of our sins; and they rule over our bodies and over our livestock according to their will; and we are in great distress."

**9:38** Therefore, because of all these things, we ourselves make a covenant and write it down, and our leaders, our Levites, and our priests seal it.

## Translator Notes

- **9:3** — The Vulgate reads 'quater in die, et quater' ("four times in the day, and four times"), whereas the Hebrew specifies a fourth part of the day for reading and another fourth part for confession and worship.
- **9:7** — The Vulgate translates 'Ur of the Chaldeans' etymologically as 'de igne Chaldæorum' ("from the fire of the Chaldeans"), reflecting an ancient interpretive tradition linking the place-name Ur to the Hebrew word for fire.
- **9:17** — 'Dederunt caput' (literally "they gave a head") renders the idiom for appointing a leader, and 'quasi per contentionem' ("as if through contention") represents the Vulgate's rendering of the Hebrew "in their rebellion."
- **9:32** — The Vulgate reads 'ne avertas a facie tua' ("do not turn away from Your face"; Hebrew: "let it not seem little before You") and singular 'regis Assur' ("the king of Assyria"; Hebrew plural: "the kings of Assyria").
- **9:36** — The Vulgate reads 'panem ejus' ("its bread") where the Hebrew has "its fruit."
- **9:38** — As in the Hebrew, chapter 10 begins with this verse. Latin foedus renders Hebrew amanah ('binding agreement') with the standard covenant term. Native Vulgate Nehemiah 9:38 maps to Saints.Bible 10:1 (10:1-39 to 10:2-40), and native 12:33 covers Saints 12:33-34 (shifting native 12:34-46 to Saints 12:35-47).
