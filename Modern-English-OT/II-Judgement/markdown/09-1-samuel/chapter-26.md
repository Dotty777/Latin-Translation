## Text

**26:1** And the Ziphites came to Saul at Gibeah, saying, "Look, David is hidden on the hill of Hachilah, which is opposite the wasteland."

**26:2** And Saul rose and went down into the wilderness of Ziph, and with him three thousand men from the chosen of Israel, to search for David in the wilderness of Ziph.

**26:3** And Saul encamped at Gibeah of Hachilah, which was opposite the wasteland on the road, while David was dwelling in the wilderness. And seeing that Saul had come after him into the wilderness,

**26:4** he sent scouts and learned that he had most certainly come there.

**26:5** And David rose secretly and came to the place where Saul was; and when he had seen the place in which Saul was sleeping, and Abner the son of Ner, the commander of his army, and Saul sleeping in the tent, and the rest of the crowd around him,

**26:6** David spoke to Ahimelech the Hittite and Abishai the son of Zeruiah, the brother of Joab, saying, "Who will go down with me to Saul into the camp?" And Abishai said, "I will go down with you."

**26:7** So David and Abishai came to the people by night, and they found Saul lying and sleeping in the tent, with a spear fixed in the ground at his head, while Abner and the people were sleeping around him.

**26:8** And Abishai said to David, "God has shut your enemy into your hands today; now therefore let me pierce him to the ground with the lance once, and there will be no need a second time."

**26:9** And David said to Abishai, "Do not kill him, for who will stretch out his hand against the anointed of the Lord and be innocent?"

**26:10** And David said, "As the Lord lives, unless the Lord strikes him, or his day comes to die, or going down into battle he perishes,

**26:11** may the Lord be merciful to me so that I do not stretch out my hand against the anointed of the Lord. Now therefore take the spear that is at his head and the cup of water, and let us go."

**26:12** So David took the spear and the cup of water that was at Saul's head, and they went away; and there was no one who saw, or perceived, or woke up, but all of them were sleeping, because a deep sleep from the Lord had fallen upon them.

**26:13** And when David had crossed over to the opposite side, and stood on the top of the mountain from afar, and there was a great space between them,

**26:14** David called out to the people and to Abner the son of Ner, saying, "Will you not answer, Abner?" And Abner answered and said, "Who are you, who call out and disturb the king?"

**26:15** And David said to Abner, "Are you not a man? And who else is like you in Israel? Why then have you not guarded your lord the king? For one of the crowd came in to kill the king your lord.

**26:16** This is not good, what you have done. As the Lord lives, you are sons of death, you who have not guarded your lord, the anointed of the Lord. Now therefore see where the king's spear is, and where the cup of water is that was at his head."

**26:17** And Saul recognized David's voice and said, "Is this your voice, my son David?" And David said, "It is my voice, my lord the king."

**26:18** And he said, "For what reason does my lord pursue his servant? What have I done? Or what evil is in my hand?

**26:19** Now therefore hear, I ask you, my lord the king, the words of your servant: if the Lord incites you against me, let Him smell a sacrifice; but if the sons of men, cursed are they in the sight of the Lord, who have driven me out today so that I should not dwell in the inheritance of the Lord, saying, 'Go, serve foreign gods.'

**26:20** And now let not my blood be poured out on the ground before the Lord, for the king of Israel has come out to search for a single flea, just as a partridge is pursued in the mountains."

**26:21** And Saul said, "I have sinned. Return, my son David, for I will never again do you harm, because my life has been precious in your eyes today; for it is clear that I have acted foolishly and have been ignorant of very many things."

**26:22** And David answered and said, "Here is the king's spear; let one of the king's young men cross over and take it.

**26:23** And the Lord will repay each one according to his righteousness and faithfulness, for the Lord delivered you today into my hand, and I was unwilling to stretch out my hand against the anointed of the Lord.

**26:24** And just as your life has been magnified today in my eyes, so may my life be magnified in the eyes of the Lord, and may He deliver me from all distress."

**26:25** Therefore Saul said to David, "Blessed are you, my son David; and indeed you will surely act, and you will surely prevail." And David went on his way, and Saul returned to his place.

## Translator Notes

- **26:3** — The Vulgate here transliterates Hebrew gib'ah ("hill") as the place name Gabaa ("Gibeah"), contrasting with in colle Hachila ("on the hill of Hachilah") in 26:1.
- **26:5** — The Vulgate adds clam ("secretly") and renders Hebrew bamma'gal ("encampment" or "barricade") as in tentorio ("in the tent," also in 26:7).
- **26:10** — Translating Hebrew ki im ("surely") literally as quia nisi ("unless"), the Vulgate links verse 10 grammatically to verse 11, where propitius sit mihi Dominus (literally "may the Lord be merciful to me") is the Vulgate idiom for "far be it from me."
- **26:14** — Distinct from the Hebrew ("who call to the king"), the Vulgate adds et inquietas ("and disturb"), using the same verb that Samuel speaks in 28:15.
- **26:20** — The Vulgate reads coram Domino ("before the Lord"), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "away from the presence of the Lord."
