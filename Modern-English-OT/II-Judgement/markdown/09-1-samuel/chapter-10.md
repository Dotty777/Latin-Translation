## Text

**10:1** Now Samuel took a flask of oil and poured it upon his head, and kissed him and said, "Look, the Lord has anointed you over His inheritance as prince, and you will deliver His people from the hands of their enemies who are all around them. And this shall be a sign to you that God has anointed you as prince.

**10:2** When you depart from me today, you will find two men beside the tomb of Rachel in the territory of Benjamin, in the south; and they will say to you, 'The female donkeys that you had gone to search for have been found; and your father, having put aside the female donkeys, is anxious about you and says, "What shall I do about my son?"'

**10:3** And when you depart from there, and pass farther on, and come to the oak of Tabor, three men going up to God at Bethel will find you there, one carrying three young goats, and another three loaves of bread, and another carrying a jar of wine.

**10:4** And when they have greeted you, they will give you two loaves of bread, and you shall accept them from their hand.

**10:5** After these things you will come to the hill of God, where the garrison of the Philistines is; and when you have entered the city there, you will meet a band of prophets coming down from the high place, and before them a harp, a tambourine, a flute, and a lyre, and they themselves prophesying.

**10:6** And the Spirit of the Lord will leap upon you, and you will prophesy with them, and you will be changed into another man.

**10:7** When therefore all these signs have come about for you, do whatever your hand finds, because the Lord is with you.

**10:8** And you shall go down ahead of me to Gilgal (for I indeed will come down to you), so that you may offer an offering and sacrifice peace offerings; for seven days you shall wait until I come to you and show you what you are to do."

**10:9** And so, when he had turned his shoulder to depart from Samuel, God changed his heart into another, and all these signs came about on that day.

**10:10** And they came to the aforementioned hill, and look, a company of prophets met him; and the Spirit of the Lord leaped upon him, and he prophesied in the midst of them.

**10:11** Now all who had known him yesterday and the day before, seeing that he was with the prophets and was prophesying, said to one another, "What thing has happened to the son of Kish? Is Saul also among the prophets?"

**10:12** And one answered another, saying, "And who is their father?" Therefore it turned into a proverb: "Is Saul also among the prophets?"

**10:13** Now he stopped prophesying and came to the high place.

**10:14** And Saul's uncle said to him and to his servant, "Where did you go?" And they answered, "To search for the female donkeys; and when we had not found them, we came to Samuel."

**10:15** And his uncle said to him, "Tell me what Samuel said to you."

**10:16** And Saul said to his uncle, "He told us that the female donkeys had been found." But concerning the word of the kingdom that Samuel had spoken to him, he did not tell him.

**10:17** And Samuel summoned the people to the Lord at Mizpah,

**10:18** and he said to the sons of Israel, "This is what the Lord God of Israel says: 'I brought Israel out of Egypt, and rescued you from the hand of the Egyptians and from the hand of all the kings who were afflicting you.'

**10:19** But today you have rejected your God, who alone has saved you from all your evils and tribulations; and you have said, 'By no means, but set a king over us.' Now therefore stand before the Lord by your tribes and by your families."

**10:20** And Samuel brought forward all the tribes of Israel, and the lot of the tribe of Benjamin fell.

**10:21** And he brought forward the tribe of Benjamin and its clans, and the clan of Matri fell; and it came down to Saul the son of Kish. Therefore they searched for him, and he was not found.

**10:22** And after these things they consulted the Lord as to whether he would come there. And the Lord answered, "Look, he is hidden at home."

**10:23** Therefore they ran and brought him from there; and he stood in the midst of the people, and he was taller than all the people from the shoulder and upward.

**10:24** And Samuel said to all the people, "Surely you see the one whom the Lord has chosen, that there is no one like him among all the people." And all the people shouted and said, "May the king live!"

**10:25** Now Samuel spoke to the people the law of the kingdom, and wrote it in a book, and deposited it before the Lord; and Samuel sent all the people away, each one to his own house.

**10:26** And Saul also went away to his house in Gibeah; and there went with him a part of the army, whose hearts God had touched.

**10:27** But the sons of Belial said, "Will this one be able to save us?" And they despised him and did not bring him gifts; but he pretended not to hear.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — The Vulgate (agreeing with the Septuagint and 4QSam-a) includes the longer clause "and you will deliver His people... that God has anointed you as prince", which is absent from the shorter Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **10:2** — Latin reads "in meridie" ("in the south", or possibly "at midday"), interpreting the Hebrew place name "Zelzah".
- **10:8** — Latin uses second-person singular verbs ("offeras... immoles", "you may offer... sacrifice", addressed to Saul), whereas Hebrew uses infinitives usually understood of Samuel.
- **10:22** — Latin reads "domi" ("at home"), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "among the baggage".
- **10:27** — "Sons of Belial" literally translates Latin "filii Belial", a biblical Hebrew idiom meaning "worthless men".
