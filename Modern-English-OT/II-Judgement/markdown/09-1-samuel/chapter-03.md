## Text

**3:1** Now the boy Samuel was ministering to the Lord before Eli, and the word of the Lord was precious in those days; there was no clear vision.

**3:2** So it happened on a certain day that Eli was lying in his place, and his eyes had grown dim, nor could he see.

**3:3** Before the lamp of God was extinguished, Samuel was sleeping in the temple of the Lord, where the ark of God was.

**3:4** And the Lord called Samuel. Answering, he said, "Here I am."

**3:5** And he ran to Eli and said, "Here I am, for you called me." And he said, "I did not call; return and sleep." And he went away and slept.

**3:6** And the Lord proceeded again to call Samuel. And rising up, Samuel went to Eli and said, "Here I am, because you called me." And he answered, "I did not call you, my son; return and sleep."

**3:7** Moreover, Samuel did not yet know the Lord, nor had the word of the Lord been revealed to him.

**3:8** And the Lord continued and called Samuel yet a third time. And rising up, he went to Eli,

**3:9** and said, "Here I am, because you called me." Then Eli understood that the Lord was calling the boy, and he said to Samuel, "Go and sleep; and if He calls you after this, you will say, 'Speak, Lord, because Your servant is listening.'" So Samuel went away and slept in his place.

**3:10** And the Lord came and stood, and called, just as He had called the second time, "Samuel, Samuel." And Samuel said, "Speak, Lord, because Your servant is listening."

**3:11** And the Lord said to Samuel, "Look, I am doing a thing in Israel; for whoever hears it, both of his ears will tingle.

**3:12** On that day I will raise up against Eli all that I have spoken concerning his house; I will begin, and I will finish.

**3:13** For I foretold to him that I would judge his house forever because of the iniquity, since he knew that his sons were acting unworthily, and he did not rebuke them.

**3:14** Therefore I have sworn to the house of Eli that the iniquity of his house will not be expiated by sacrifices and offerings for all eternity."

**3:15** Now Samuel slept until morning, and he opened the doors of the house of the Lord. And Samuel was afraid to report the vision to Eli.

**3:16** Then Eli called Samuel and said, "Samuel, my son?" Answering, he said, "I am here."

**3:17** And he asked him, "What is the word that the Lord spoke to you? I beg you, do not hide it from me; may God do these things to you, and add these as well, if you conceal from me a word from all the words that were spoken to you."

**3:18** Therefore Samuel reported all the words to him, and did not conceal them from him. And he answered, "He is the Lord; let Him do what is good in His eyes."

**3:19** Now Samuel grew, and the Lord was with him, and none of all his words fell to the ground.

**3:20** And all Israel, from Dan as far as Beersheba, knew that Samuel was a faithful prophet of the Lord.

**3:21** And the Lord continued to appear in Shiloh, since the Lord had been revealed to Samuel in Shiloh according to the word of the Lord. And the word of Samuel came to all Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew terms for "rare" and "widespread" as "pretiosus" ("precious," i.e., rare and valued) and "manifesta" ("clear" or "open").
- **3:10** — The Vulgate reads "sicut vocaverat secundo" ("just as He had called the second time"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "as at the other times."
- **3:13** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks of Eli's sons cursing themselves (or God, in the Septuagint) and Eli not restraining them, the Vulgate reads "indigne agere" ("acting unworthily") and "non corripuerit" ("did not rebuke").
- **3:21** — In the Vulgate and Septuagint, "And the word of Samuel came to all Israel" concludes chapter 3, whereas in the Hebrew Masoretic Text it opens 4:1.
