## Text

**14:1** And it happened on a certain day that Jonathan the son of Saul said to the young man, his armor-bearer, "Come, and let us cross over to the outpost of the Philistines, which is on the other side of that place." But he did not tell his father this.

**14:2** Now Saul was staying in the outermost part of Gibeah under the pomegranate tree that was in Migron, and the people with him were about six hundred men.

**14:3** And Ahijah the son of Ahitub, brother of Ichabod, the son of Phinehas, who was descended from Eli, the priest of the Lord in Shiloh, was carrying an ephod. Moreover, the people did not know where Jonathan had gone.

**14:4** Now between the ascents by which Jonathan was striving to cross over to the outpost of the Philistines, there were jutting rocks on both sides, and sheer crags, as it were in the shape of teeth, on this side and on that; the name of the one was Bozez, and the name of the other was Seneh:

**14:5** one crag projecting toward the north opposite Michmash, and the other toward the south facing Gibeah.

**14:6** Then Jonathan said to the young man, his armor-bearer, "Come, let us cross over to the outpost of these uncircumcised men; perhaps the Lord will act for us, for it is not difficult for the Lord to save, whether by many or by few."

**14:7** And his armor-bearer said to him, "Do all that pleases your heart; go on where you desire, and I will be with you wherever you wish."

**14:8** And Jonathan said, "Look, we are crossing over to those men. And when we appear to them,

**14:9** if they speak to us in this way, 'Stay until we come to you,' then let us stand in our place and not go up to them.

**14:10** But if they say, 'Come up to us,' let us go up, for the Lord has handed them over into our hands; this will be a sign for us."

**14:11** Therefore both of them appeared to the outpost of the Philistines, and the Philistines said, "Look, the Hebrews are coming out of the caves in which they had been hidden."

**14:12** And the men from the outpost spoke to Jonathan and to his armor-bearer, and said, "Come up to us, and we will show you something." And Jonathan said to his armor-bearer, "Let us go up; follow me, for the Lord has handed them over into the hands of Israel."

**14:13** Then Jonathan climbed up, crawling on hands and feet, and his armor-bearer after him. And so some fell before Jonathan, and his armor-bearer killed others as he followed him.

**14:14** And the first slaughter, with which Jonathan and his armor-bearer struck, was of about twenty men, within half an acre that a pair of oxen is accustomed to plow in a day.

**14:15** And a miracle occurred in the camp throughout the fields; moreover, all the people of the outpost of those who had gone to plunder were astounded, and the earth was thrown into turmoil, and it happened like a miracle from God.

**14:16** And the watchmen of Saul who were in Gibeah of Benjamin looked out, and look, the multitude was laid low and fleeing this way and that.

**14:17** And Saul said to the people who were with him, "Inquire and see who has gone away from us." And when they had inquired, it was found that Jonathan and his armor-bearer were not present.

**14:18** And Saul said to Ahijah, "Bring near the ark of God." (For the ark of God was there on that day with the sons of Israel.)

**14:19** And while Saul was speaking to the priest, a great uproar arose in the camp of the Philistines, and it was growing little by little and resounding more loudly. And Saul said to the priest, "Withdraw your hand."

**14:20** Therefore Saul and all the people who were with him shouted together, and they came all the way to the place of combat; and look, each man's sword had been turned against his neighbor, and there was exceedingly great carnage.

**14:21** Moreover, the Hebrews who had been with the Philistines yesterday and the day before, and had gone up with them into the camp, turned back to be with Israel, who were with Saul and Jonathan.

**14:22** All the Israelites also who had hidden themselves in Mount Ephraim, hearing that the Philistines had fled, joined themselves with their own men in the battle. And there were with Saul about ten thousand men.

**14:23** And the Lord saved Israel on that day, and the fight reached as far as Beth-aven.

**14:24** And the men of Israel were joined together on that day; and Saul placed the people under an oath, saying, "Cursed is the man who eats bread until evening, until I take vengeance on my enemies." And the entire people ate no bread,

**14:25** and all the populace of the land came into a forest, in which there was honey on the surface of the field.

**14:26** Therefore the people entered the forest, and flowing honey appeared, and no one brought his hand to his mouth, for the people feared the oath.

**14:27** Now Jonathan had not heard when his father placed the people under the oath; and he stretched out the tip of the rod that he had in his hand, and dipped it into a honeycomb, and brought his hand back to his mouth, and his eyes were brightened.

**14:28** And one of the people answered and said, "Your father bound the people with an oath, saying, 'Cursed is the man who eats bread today.'" (Now the people had grown faint.)

**14:29** And Jonathan said, "My father has troubled the land; you yourselves have seen that my eyes were brightened because I tasted a little of this honey;

**14:30** how much more if the people had eaten from the plunder of their enemies that they found? Would not a greater slaughter have taken place among the Philistines?"

**14:31** Therefore on that day they struck the Philistines from Michmash as far as Aijalon. But the people were exceedingly exhausted;

**14:32** and turning to the plunder, they took sheep, and oxen, and calves, and slaughtered them on the ground; and the people ate with the blood.

**14:33** Then they reported to Saul, saying that the people had sinned against the Lord, eating with the blood. And he said, "You have transgressed; roll a large stone to me right now."

**14:34** And Saul said, "Scatter among the populace and tell them that each man bring to me his ox and ram, and kill them on this, and eat, and you will not sin against the Lord by eating with the blood." Therefore all the people brought, each man an ox in his hand, until night, and they killed them there.

**14:35** And Saul built an altar to the Lord, and then for the first time he began to build an altar to the Lord.

**14:36** And Saul said, "Let us rush upon the Philistines by night, and ravage them until morning dawns, and let us not leave a man of them." And the people said, "Do everything that seems good in your eyes." And the priest said, "Let us draw near here to God."

**14:37** And Saul consulted the Lord: "Shall I pursue the Philistines? Will You hand them over into the hands of Israel?" And He did not answer him on that day.

**14:38** And Saul said, "Bring here all the corners of the people, and know and see through whom this sin has happened today.

**14:39** As the Lord, the Savior of Israel, lives, even if it has been done through Jonathan my son, he will die without retraction." To this no one of all the people contradicted him.

**14:40** And he said to all Israel, "Separate yourselves to one side, and I with Jonathan my son will be on the other side." And the people answered Saul, "Do what seems good in your eyes."

**14:41** And Saul said to the Lord God of Israel, "Lord God of Israel, give an indication: why is it that You have not answered Your servant today? If this iniquity is in me or in Jonathan my son, give a manifestation; or if this iniquity is in Your people, give holiness." And Jonathan and Saul were caught, but the people went out.

**14:42** And Saul said, "Cast the lot between me and Jonathan my son." And Jonathan was taken.

**14:43** Then Saul said to Jonathan, "Tell me what you have done." And Jonathan told him and said, "I indeed tasted a little honey on the tip of the rod that was in my hand, and look, I die."

**14:44** And Saul said, "May God do these things to me and add these things as well, for you will surely die, Jonathan."

**14:45** And the people said to Saul, "Will Jonathan then die, who has accomplished this great salvation in Israel? This is an outrage! As the Lord lives, not a hair from his head will fall to the ground, for he has worked with God today." Therefore the people rescued Jonathan, so that he did not die.

**14:46** And Saul withdrew and did not pursue the Philistines; and the Philistines went away to their own places.

**14:47** And Saul, when the kingdom over Israel was established, fought all around against all his enemies, against Moab, and the sons of Ammon, and Edom, and the kings of Zobah, and the Philistines; and wherever he turned, he prevailed.

**14:48** And having gathered an army, he struck Amalek and delivered Israel from the hand of its ravagers.

**14:49** Now the sons of Saul were Jonathan, and Ishvi, and Malchishua; and the names of his two daughters: the name of the firstborn was Merab, and the name of the younger was Michal.

**14:50** And the name of Saul's wife was Ahinoam, the daughter of Ahimaaz; and the name of the commander of his army was Abner, the son of Ner, Saul's cousin.

**14:51** Now Kish was the father of Saul, and Ner, the father of Abner, was the son of Abiel.

**14:52** Now there was fierce war against the Philistines all the days of Saul. For whenever Saul saw any man who was strong and fit for battle, he joined him to himself.

## Translator Notes

- **14:15** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew term for "trembling" or "panic" twice as miraculum ("miracle").
- **14:22** — The closing sentence ("And there were with Saul about ten thousand men") is a Vulgate reading shared with the Septuagint that does not appear in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **14:24** — The Vulgate reads sociati sunt sibi ("were joined together"), reflecting a different vocalization or reading of the Hebrew verb that the Masoretic Text renders "were hard-pressed."
- **14:38** — Latin angulos ("corners") literally reproduces the Hebrew idiom for the chiefs or leaders of the people, viewed as their cornerstones.
- **14:41** — Following the longer text also attested in the Septuagint (against the shorter Masoretic Text), the Vulgate includes Saul's full appeal and renders the sacred lots Urim and Thummim as ostensionem ("manifestation") and sanctitatem ("holiness").
