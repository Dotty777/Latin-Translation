## Text

**28:1** And it happened in those days that the Philistines gathered their armies to prepare for war against Israel; and Achish said to David, "Now know for certain that you will go out with me in the camp, you and your men."

**28:2** And David said to Achish, "Now you will know what your servant is about to do." And Achish said to David, "And I will appoint you guardian of my head for all days."

**28:3** Now Samuel was dead, and all Israel had mourned him and buried him in Ramah, his city. And Saul had removed the magicians and soothsayers from the land.

**28:4** And the Philistines assembled, and came, and encamped at Shunem; and Saul also gathered all Israel, and came to Gilboa.

**28:5** And Saul saw the camp of the Philistines, and he was afraid, and his heart was exceedingly terrified.

**28:6** And he consulted the Lord, and He did not answer him, neither through dreams, nor through priests, nor through prophets.

**28:7** And Saul said to his servants, "Search for a woman for me who has a divining spirit, and I will go to her and inquire through her." And his servants said to him, "There is a woman who has a divining spirit at Endor."

**28:8** So he changed his appearance and put on other garments, and he went, he and two men with him; and they came to the woman by night, and he said to her, "Divine for me by the divining spirit, and raise up for me the one whom I tell you."

**28:9** And the woman said to him, "Look, you know how much Saul has done, and how he has wiped out the magicians and soothsayers from the land; why then do you lay a snare for my life, so that I may be killed?"

**28:10** And Saul swore to her by the Lord, saying, "As the Lord lives, no evil at all will happen to you because of this matter."

**28:11** And the woman said to him, "Whom shall I raise up for you?" And he said, "Raise up Samuel for me."

**28:12** And when the woman saw Samuel, she cried out with a loud voice and said to Saul, "Why have you deceived me? For you are Saul."

**28:13** And the king said to her, "Do not be afraid. What did you see?" And the woman said to Saul, "I saw gods ascending out of the earth."

**28:14** And he said to her, "What is his appearance?" And she said, "An old man is ascending, and he is wrapped in a cloak." And Saul understood that it was Samuel, and he bowed himself upon his face to the ground and paid homage.

**28:15** And Samuel said to Saul, "Why have you disturbed me, so that I should be raised up?" And Saul said, "I am exceedingly distressed, for the Philistines are fighting against me, and God has departed from me and has been unwilling to answer me, neither by the hand of prophets nor through dreams; therefore I have called you, so that you may show me what I should do."

**28:16** And Samuel said, "Why do you question me, since the Lord has departed from you and has passed over to your rival?

**28:17** For the Lord will do to you just as He spoke by my hand, and He will tear your kingdom from your hand and will give it to your neighbor David,

**28:18** because you did not obey the voice of the Lord, nor did you carry out the wrath of His fury against Amalek; therefore what you are suffering, the Lord has done to you today.

**28:19** And the Lord will also give Israel with you into the hands of the Philistines, and tomorrow you and your sons will be with me; and moreover the Lord will deliver the camp of Israel into the hands of the Philistines."

**28:20** And immediately Saul fell stretched out upon the ground, for he was terrified by the words of Samuel, and there was no strength in him, because he had not eaten bread all that day.

**28:21** Then that woman came in to Saul (for he was greatly troubled) and said to him, "Look, your maidservant has obeyed your voice, and I have placed my life in my hand, and I have listened to your words that you spoke to me.

**28:22** Now therefore, you also listen to the voice of your maidservant, and I will set before you a morsel of bread, so that by eating you may regain strength and be able to travel."

**28:23** But he refused and said, "I will not eat." However, his servants and the woman pressed him, and at last, having listened to their voice, he rose from the ground and sat on the bed.

**28:24** Now that woman had a pasture-fed calf in the house, and she hurried and killed it; and taking flour, she mixed it and baked unleavened bread,

**28:25** and set it before Saul and before his servants. And when they had eaten, they rose and walked throughout that whole night.

## Translator Notes

- **28:6** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew ba'urim ("by Urim") as per sacerdotes ("through priests"), reflecting the priestly role in consulting the sacred lot.
- **28:7** — The Vulgate uses the Greek loanword python (an oracular or divining spirit, originally linked to the Pythian oracle) for Hebrew ov ("ghost" or "familiar spirit"), and similarly in pythone in 28:8.
- **28:13** — The Vulgate preserves the plural noun and participle (Deos ... ascendentes) of Hebrew elohim olim, referring to supernatural or spirit beings rising from the earth, even though Saul asks about a single figure in 28:14.
- **28:16** — The Vulgate reads that the Lord has passed over to Saul's rival (David), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "and has become your adversary." In 28:17, the Vulgate also uses future verbs and tibi ("to you") where the Hebrew uses past tense and "for himself."
- **28:20** — The Vulgate reads tota die illa ("all that day"), omitting "and all night" found in the Masoretic Hebrew. In 28:24, the Vulgate similarly renders the Hebrew "fattened calf" as vitulum pascualem ("pasture-fed calf").
