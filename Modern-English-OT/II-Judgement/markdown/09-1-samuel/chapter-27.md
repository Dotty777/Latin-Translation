## Text

**27:1** And David said in his heart, "Sometime, one day, I will fall into the hands of Saul. Is it not better that I flee and be saved in the land of the Philistines, so that Saul will despair and stop searching for me within all the borders of Israel? Therefore I will escape his hands."

**27:2** And David rose and went, he and six hundred men with him, to Achish the son of Maoch, king of Gath.

**27:3** And David dwelt with Achish in Gath, he and his men, each man and his household, and David and his two wives, Ahinoam the Jezreelite and Abigail the wife of Nabal of Carmel.

**27:4** And it was reported to Saul that David had fled to Gath, and he did not continue searching for him any longer.

**27:5** And David said to Achish, "If I have found favor in your eyes, let a place be given to me in one of the towns of this region, so that I may dwell there; for why does your servant remain in the city of the king with you?"

**27:6** Therefore Achish gave him Ziklag on that day; for this reason Ziklag came to belong to the kings of Judah until this day.

**27:7** And the number of the days during which David dwelt in the region of the Philistines was four months.

**27:8** And David and his men went up and carried off plunder from the Geshurites, and from the Girzites, and from the Amalekites; for these districts were inhabited in the land from ancient times, as one goes to Shur, as far as the land of Egypt.

**27:9** And David would strike the whole land, and would not leave man or woman alive; and taking sheep, and oxen, and donkeys, and camels, and garments, he would return and come to Achish.

**27:10** And Achish would say to him, "Against whom have you raided today?" David would answer, "Against the south of Judah, and against the south of Jerahmeel, and against the south of the Kenites."

**27:11** Neither man nor woman would David keep alive, nor bring to Gath, saying, "Otherwise they might speak against us, 'David has done these things.'" And this was his decree all the days during which he dwelt in the region of the Philistines.

**27:12** Therefore Achish believed David, saying, "He has worked many evils against his people Israel; therefore he will be my perpetual servant."

## Translator Notes

- **27:7** — A notable Vulgate reading: Latin quatuor mensium gives only "four months," whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "a year [lit. days] and four months."
- **27:8** — The Vulgate renders this clause as referring to rural districts or villages (pagi) inhabited from ancient times, whereas the Hebrew refers directly to the named peoples as ancient inhabitants of the land.
- **27:12** — The Vulgate translates literally as working many evils (Multa mala operatus est) against Israel, whereas the Hebrew uses an idiom meaning "he has made himself utterly odious" to his people.
