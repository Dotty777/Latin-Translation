## Text

**6:1** So the ark of the Lord was in the region of the Philistines for seven months.

**6:2** And the Philistines called the priests and diviners, saying, "What will we do with the ark of the Lord? Show us how we should send it back to its own place." And they said,

**6:3** "If you send back the ark of the God of Israel, do not send it away empty, but render to Him what you owe for sin; and then you will be healed, and you will know why His hand does not withdraw from you."

**6:4** And they said, "What is it that we ought to render to Him for the trespass?" And they answered,

**6:5** "According to the number of the provinces of the Philistines, you will make five golden anuses and five golden mice, because one plague was upon all of you and upon your satraps. And you will make likenesses of your anuses and likenesses of the mice that have devastated the land, and you will give glory to the God of Israel, if perhaps He may lift His hand from you, and from your gods, and from your land.

**6:6** Why do you harden your hearts, just as Egypt and Pharaoh hardened their heart? After he was struck, did he not then let them go, and they departed?

**6:7** Now therefore, take and make one new cart, and hitch to the cart two cows with calves on which no yoke has been placed, and shut up their calves at home.

**6:8** And you will take the ark of the Lord and place it on the cart, and the golden articles that you have paid to Him for the trespass you will place in a small box at its side, and send it away so that it may go.

**6:9** And you will watch, and if indeed it goes up by the way of its own borders toward Beth-shemesh, He Himself has done this great evil to us; but if not, then not at all: we will know that His hand by no means touched us, but it happened by chance."

**6:10** So they did in this manner, and taking two cows that were nursing calves, they hitched them to the cart, and shut up their calves at home.

**6:11** And they placed the ark of God upon the cart, and the small box that held the golden mice and the likenesses of the anuses.

**6:12** Now the cows went straight along the way that leads to Beth-shemesh, and they walked along one path, proceeding and lowing, and they did not turn aside either to the right or to the left; and indeed the satraps of the Philistines were following as far as the borders of Beth-shemesh.

**6:13** Now the Beth-shemites were harvesting wheat in the valley, and lifting up their eyes, they saw the ark and rejoiced when they saw it.

**6:14** And the cart came into the field of Joshua the Beth-shemite and stood still there. Now a large stone was there; and they chopped up the timbers of the cart and placed the cows upon them as a burnt offering to the Lord.

**6:15** And the Levites took down the ark of God and the small box that was beside it, in which were the golden articles, and placed them upon the great stone. And the men of Beth-shemesh offered burnt offerings and sacrificed victims to the Lord on that day.

**6:16** And the five satraps of the Philistines saw it, and returned to Ekron on that day.

**6:17** Now these are the golden anuses that the Philistines rendered to the Lord for the trespass: Ashdod one, Gaza one, Ashkelon one, Gath one, Ekron one;

**6:18** and the golden mice according to the number of the cities of the Philistines of the five provinces, from the walled city to the village that was without a wall, and as far as Great Abel, upon which they placed the ark of the Lord, which was until that day in the field of Joshua the Beth-shemite.

**6:19** And He struck some of the men of Beth-shemesh because they had seen the ark of the Lord; and He struck from the people seventy men and fifty thousand of the common people. And the people mourned because the Lord had struck the common people with a great plague.

**6:20** And the men of Beth-shemesh said, "Who will be able to stand in the sight of this holy Lord God? And to whom will He go up from us?"

**6:21** And they sent messengers to the inhabitants of Kiriath-jearim, saying, "The Philistines have brought back the ark of the Lord; come down and bring it back to yourselves."

## Translator Notes

- **6:5** — Vulgate anos aureos literally means "golden anuses" (matching the Septuagint hedras, "seats/posteriors"), depicting the afflicted bodily part as a votive offering, whereas the Hebrew has a word for "tumors" or "hemorrhoids." Latin satrapis ("satraps") also uses a Persian-Greek imperial title for the Philistine rulers.
- **6:6** — Vulgate postquam percussus est, tunc dimisit eos ("after he was struck, did he not then let them go") uses singular verbs referring to Pharaoh, whereas the Hebrew reads "when He dealt severely with them, did they not send them away."
- **6:18** — Vulgate Abelmagnum ("Great Abel") preserves Hebrew Abel haggedolah as a proper name rather than the variant reading "great stone," and reads usque in illum diem ("until that day") instead of "to this day." The feminine relative quae agrees grammatically with arcam ("the ark"), though the corresponding Hebrew clause refers to the landmark in Joshua's field.
- **6:19** — Vulgate reads septuaginta viros, et quinquaginta millia plebis ("seventy men and fifty thousand of the common people"), inserting "and" and distinguishing viros from plebis at a well-known numerical crux in the Hebrew text.
