## Text

**8:1** Now it happened, when Samuel had grown old, that he appointed his sons as judges of Israel.

**8:2** And the name of his firstborn son was Joel, and the name of the second was Abijah, judges in Beersheba.

**8:3** And his sons did not walk in his ways, but turned aside after greed, and accepted bribes, and perverted justice.

**8:4** Therefore all the elders of Israel gathered together and came to Samuel at Ramah.

**8:5** And they said to him, "Look, you have grown old, and your sons do not walk in your ways; appoint a king for us to judge us, just as all the nations also have."

**8:6** The word was displeasing in the eyes of Samuel because they had said, "Give us a king to judge us." And Samuel prayed to the Lord.

**8:7** And the Lord said to Samuel, "Listen to the voice of the people in everything that they say to you, for they have not rejected you, but Me, so that I should not reign over them.

**8:8** According to all their deeds that they have done from the day on which I brought them out of Egypt until this day: just as they have abandoned Me and served foreign gods, so they are also doing to you.

**8:9** Now therefore, listen to their voice; yet solemnly warn them and foretell to them the right of the king who is going to reign over them."

**8:10** And so Samuel spoke all the words of the Lord to the people who had asked a king from him.

**8:11** And he said, "This will be the right of the king who is going to rule over you: he will take your sons and place them in his chariots, and he will make them horsemen for himself and forerunners of his four-horse chariots,

**8:12** and he will appoint for himself tribunes and centurions, and plowmen of his fields, and reapers of the crops, and craftsmen of his weapons and of his chariots.

**8:13** He will also make your daughters perfumers, cooks, and bakers for himself.

**8:14** He will also take your fields, and vineyards, and best olive groves, and give them to his servants.

**8:15** Moreover, he will take a tenth of your crops and of the yield of your vineyards, so that he may give it to his eunuchs and his attendants.

**8:16** He will also take away your male servants and female servants, and your best young men, and your donkeys, and put them to his own work.

**8:17** He will also take a tenth of your flocks, and you yourselves will be servants to him.

**8:18** And you will cry out on that day because of your king whom you have chosen for yourselves, and the Lord will not hear you on that day, because you have asked a king for yourselves."

**8:19** However, the people refused to listen to the voice of Samuel, but they said, "By no means, for there will be a king over us,

**8:20** and we also will be like all the nations, and our king will judge us and go out before us and fight our battles for us."

**8:21** And Samuel heard all the words of the people, and he spoke them in the ears of the Lord.

**8:22** And the Lord said to Samuel, "Listen to their voice, and appoint a king over them." And Samuel said to the men of Israel, "Let each one go to his own city."

## Translator Notes

- **8:9** — Latin jus regis ("the right of the king," repeated in 8:11) denotes the legal prerogative or custom a monarch will claim, rendering Hebrew mishpat hammelek.
- **8:12** — Vulgate tribunos, et centuriones ("tribunes and centurions") casts the military ranks in Roman terminology, substituting centurions (commanders of hundreds) where the Hebrew has commanders of fifties.
- **8:15** — Vulgate translates Hebrew sarisim literally as eunuchis ("eunuchs") rather than the broader sense "court officials."
- **8:18** — The closing clause quia petistis vobis regem ("because you have asked a king for yourselves") is not found in the Masoretic Hebrew text; the Septuagint has a similar clause.
