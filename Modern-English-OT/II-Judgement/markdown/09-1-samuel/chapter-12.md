## Text

**12:1** Now Samuel said to all Israel, "Look, I have listened to your voice in accordance with everything that you have spoken to me, and I have appointed a king over you.

**12:2** And now the king walks before you, while I have grown old and turned gray; moreover, my sons are with you. And so, having lived before you from my youth until this day, look, I stand ready.

**12:3** Speak concerning me before the Lord and before His anointed, whether I have taken anyone's ox or donkey, if I have falsely accused anyone, if I have oppressed anyone, if I have accepted a gift from anyone's hand; and I will despise it today, and restore it to you."

**12:4** And they said, "You have not falsely accused us, nor oppressed us, nor taken anything from anyone's hand."

**12:5** And he said to them, "The Lord is a witness against you, and His anointed is a witness on this day, that you have not found anything in my hand." And they said, "He is a witness."

**12:6** And Samuel said to the people, "It is the Lord, who made Moses and Aaron, and brought our fathers out of the land of Egypt.

**12:7** Now therefore, stand, so that I may contend in judgment against you before the Lord concerning all the mercies of the Lord that He performed for you and for your fathers:

**12:8** how Jacob entered Egypt, and your fathers cried out to the Lord, and the Lord sent Moses and Aaron, and brought your fathers out of Egypt, and settled them in this place.

**12:9** Yet they forgot the Lord their God, and He handed them over into the hand of Sisera, commander of the army of Hazor, and into the hand of the Philistines, and into the hand of the king of Moab; and they fought against them.

**12:10** Afterward, however, they cried out to the Lord and said, 'We have sinned, because we have abandoned the Lord and served the Baals and the Ashtaroth; now therefore, rescue us from the hand of our enemies, and we will serve You.'

**12:11** And the Lord sent Jerubbaal, and Bedan, and Jephthah, and Samuel, and rescued you from the hand of your enemies all around, and you lived securely.

**12:12** But seeing that Nahash, king of the sons of Ammon, had come against you, you said to me, 'By no means; rather, a king will rule over us,' when the Lord your God was reigning over you.

**12:13** Now therefore, here stands your king, whom you have chosen and asked for; look, the Lord has given you a king.

**12:14** If you fear the Lord, and serve Him, and listen to His voice, and do not provoke the mouth of the Lord, both you and the king who rules over you will be following the Lord your God.

**12:15** However, if you do not listen to the voice of the Lord, but provoke His words, the hand of the Lord will be upon you and upon your fathers.

**12:16** Now also, stand and see this great thing that the Lord is about to do in your sight.

**12:17** Is it not the wheat harvest today? I will call upon the Lord, and He will give thunder and rain; and you will know and see that you have done a great evil to yourselves in the sight of the Lord by asking for a king over you."

**12:18** And Samuel cried out to the Lord, and the Lord gave thunder and rain on that day.

**12:19** And all the people feared the Lord and Samuel greatly, and the entire people said to Samuel, "Pray for your servants to the Lord your God, so that we may not die; for we have added an evil to all our sins by asking for a king for ourselves."

**12:20** Then Samuel said to the people, "Do not be afraid. You yourselves have done all this evil; nevertheless, do not turn away from behind the Lord, but serve the Lord with all your heart.

**12:21** And do not turn aside after empty things, which will not benefit you nor rescue you, because they are empty.

**12:22** And the Lord will not abandon His people for the sake of His great name, because the Lord has sworn to make you a people for Himself.

**12:23** Moreover, far be this sin against the Lord from me, that I should cease praying for you; and I will teach you the good and right way.

**12:24** Therefore fear the Lord, and serve Him in truth and with your whole heart; for you have seen the magnificent things that He has done among you.

**12:25** But if you persist in wickedness, both you and your king will perish together."

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**13:1** *(after `12:25`)* Saul was a son of one year when he began to reign, and he reigned over Israel for two years.

## Translator Notes

- **12:3** — The Vulgate reads 'et contemnam illud hodie' ('and I will despise it today'), differing from the Hebrew ('to blind my eyes with it') and the Septuagint ('even a sandal'); 'munus' ('gift') carries the judicial sense of a bribe.
- **12:7** — The Vulgate reads 'misericordiis' ('mercies'), whereas the Hebrew text has 'righteous deeds.'
- **12:11** — The Vulgate 'Badan' ('Bedan') follows the Hebrew Masoretic Text; this deliverer is otherwise unknown by that name, while the Septuagint and Syriac read 'Barak.'
- **12:17** — Latin 'voces et pluvias' literally means 'voices and rains,' a Hebraic idiom for peals of thunder and rainstorms (likewise in 12:18).
- **12:22** — The Vulgate reads 'juravit' ('has sworn'), whereas the Hebrew has 'was pleased' or 'resolved.'
- **13:1 *(additional)*** — The Vulgate renders a difficult Hebrew idiom literally as "a son of one year," often explained as a missing numeral or the start of Saul's kingship.
