## Text

**29:1** So all the divisions of the Philistines were gathered together in Aphek, and Israel also encamped by the spring that was in Jezreel.

**29:2** And the satraps of the Philistines were marching in hundreds and in thousands, while David and his men were in the rear division with Achish.

**29:3** And the leaders of the Philistines said to Achish, "What do these Hebrews want?" And Achish said to the leaders of the Philistines, "Are you unaware of David, who was the servant of Saul, king of Israel, and has been with me for many days, or years, and I have not found anything in him from the day on which he defected to me until this day?"

**29:4** But the leaders of the Philistines became angry with him and said to him, "Let this man return and stay in his place where you appointed him, and let him not go down with us into battle, so that he does not become an adversary to us when we begin to fight; for how else will he be able to appease his master, except with our heads?

**29:5** Is this not David, for whom they sang in dances, saying, 'Saul has struck in his thousands, and David in his ten thousands'?"

**29:6** So Achish called David and said to him, "As the Lord lives, you are upright and good in my sight, and your going out and your coming in are with me in the camp; and I have not found anything evil in you from the day on which you came to me until this day; but you do not please the satraps.

**29:7** Therefore return, and go in peace, and do not offend the eyes of the satraps of the Philistines."

**29:8** And David said to Achish, "For what have I done, and what have you found in me, your servant, from the day on which I have been in your sight until this day, that I should not come and fight against the enemies of my lord the king?"

**29:9** Then Achish answered and spoke to David, "I know that you are good in my eyes, like an angel of God; but the leaders of the Philistines have said, 'He will not go up with us into battle.'

**29:10** Therefore rise up in the morning, you and the servants of your master who came with you; and when you have risen while it is still night and it begins to grow light, depart."

**29:11** So David rose while it was still night, he and his men, to set out in the morning and return to the land of the Philistines; but the Philistines went up to Jezreel.

## Translator Notes

- **29:2** — Latin satrapae ("satraps") reflects the distinct Philistine title (Hebrew seranim), whereas the Vulgate uses principes ("leaders") in verses 3, 4, and 9 for the Philistine commanders.
- **29:5** — Latin in millibus suis ... in decem millibus suis ("in his thousands ... in his ten thousands") preserves the Vulgate's literal rendering of the Hebrew idiom for Saul's thousands and David's ten thousands.
- **29:6** — The Vulgate joins et bonus in conspectu meo ("and good in my sight") to rectus es tu ("you are upright") and makes David's going out and coming in a separate clause, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text makes his going out and coming in the subject of "good in my eyes."
