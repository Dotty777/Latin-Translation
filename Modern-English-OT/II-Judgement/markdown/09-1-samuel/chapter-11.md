## Text

**11:1** And it happened about a month later that Nahash the Ammonite went up and began to fight against Jabesh-gilead. And all the men of Jabesh said to Nahash, "Have us as allies, and we will serve you."

**11:2** And Nahash the Ammonite answered them, "On this condition I will make a treaty with you: that I gouge out the right eyes of all of you, and make you a reproach in all Israel."

**11:3** And the elders of Jabesh said to him, "Grant us seven days, so that we may send messengers to all the territories of Israel; and if there is no one to defend us, we will come out to you."

**11:4** Therefore the messengers came to Gibeah of Saul and spoke these words in the hearing of the people; and all the people lifted up their voice and wept.

**11:5** And look, Saul was coming, following the oxen from the field, and he said, "What is wrong with the people, that they are weeping?" And they recounted to him the words of the men of Jabesh.

**11:6** And the Spirit of the Lord leaped upon Saul when he had heard these words, and his anger grew exceedingly fierce.

**11:7** And taking both oxen, he cut them into pieces and sent them into all the territories of Israel by the hand of messengers, saying, "Whoever does not come out and follow Saul and Samuel, so shall it be done to his oxen." Therefore the fear of the Lord seized the people, and they went out as one man.

**11:8** And he mustered them at Bezek; and of the sons of Israel there were three hundred thousand, and of the men of Judah thirty thousand.

**11:9** And they said to the messengers who had come, "Thus you shall say to the men who are in Jabesh-gilead: 'Tomorrow there will be deliverance for you, when the sun grows hot.'" Therefore the messengers came and reported to the men of Jabesh, who rejoiced.

**11:10** And they said, "In the morning we will come out to you, and you may do to us everything that pleases you."

**11:11** And it happened, when the next day had come, that Saul arranged the people into three divisions; and he entered the middle of the camp in the morning watch and struck Ammon until the day grew hot; and the rest were scattered, so that not two among them were left together.

**11:12** And the people said to Samuel, "Who is this one who said, 'Shall Saul reign over us?' Hand over the men, and we will put them to death."

**11:13** And Saul said, "No one shall be put to death on this day, because today the Lord has accomplished deliverance in Israel."

**11:14** Now Samuel said to the people, "Come, and let us go to Gilgal, and let us renew the kingdom there."

**11:15** And all the people went to Gilgal, and there they made Saul king before the Lord in Gilgal, and there they sacrificed peace offerings before the Lord. And there Saul and all the men of Israel rejoiced exceedingly.

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — The opening phrase "about a month later" (Latin "quasi post mensem", shared with the Septuagint and 4QSam-a) does not appear in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which instead reads the related consonants at the end of 10:27 as "like one keeping silent" (also reflected in Vulgate 10:27).
- **11:6** — Latin reads "spiritus Domini" ("the Spirit of the Lord", as also in 10:10), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the Spirit of God".
- **11:10** — Latin reads "Mane" ("In the morning"), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Tomorrow".
