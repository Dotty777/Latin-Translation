## Text

**17:1** Now the Philistines, gathering their forces for battle, came together at Socoh of Judah, and they encamped between Socoh and Azekah in the borders of Dommim.

**17:2** Meanwhile, Saul and the sons of Israel, having gathered together, came into the Valley of the Terebinth, and they drew up the battle line to fight against the Philistines.

**17:3** And the Philistines were standing on the mountain on this side, and Israel was standing on the mountain on the other side, and a valley was between them.

**17:4** And an illegitimate man came out from the camp of the Philistines, named Goliath, from Gath, six cubits and a palm in height.

**17:5** And a bronze helmet was on his head, and he was clothed in a scaled coat of armor. Moreover, the weight of his coat of armor was five thousand shekels of bronze.

**17:6** And he had bronze greaves on his legs, and a bronze shield covered his shoulders.

**17:7** Now the shaft of his spear was like a weavers' beam, and the very iron of his spear had six hundred shekels of iron, and his armor-bearer went ahead of him.

**17:8** And standing, he shouted against the battle ranks of Israel and said to them, "Why have you come prepared for battle? Am I not a Philistine, and you the servants of Saul? Choose a man from among yourselves, and let him come down to single combat.

**17:9** If he is able to fight with me and strikes me down, we will be servants to you; but if I prevail and strike him down, you will be servants, and you will serve us."

**17:10** And the Philistine said, "I have taunted the forces of Israel today; give me a man, and let him enter into single combat with me."

**17:11** Now when Saul and all the Israelites heard such words of the Philistine, they were stunned and were exceedingly afraid.

**17:12** Now David was the son of the Ephrathite man who was mentioned above, from Bethlehem of Judah, whose name was Jesse, who had eight sons; and in the days of Saul the man was old and advanced in years among men.

**17:13** Now his three oldest sons went after Saul into battle; and the names of his three sons who marched out to war were Eliab the firstborn, the second Abinadab, and the third Shammah.

**17:14** David, however, was the youngest. So when the three oldest had followed Saul,

**17:15** David went away and returned from Saul to pasture his father's flock in Bethlehem.

**17:16** Meanwhile the Philistine came forward morning and evening, and took his stand for forty days.

**17:17** Now Jesse said to his son David, "Take for your brothers an ephah of roasted grain and these ten loaves of bread, and run into the camp to your brothers,

**17:18** and bring these ten rounds of cheese to the tribune; visit your brothers to see if they are doing well, and learn with whom they are stationed."

**17:19** Now Saul, and they, and all the sons of Israel were fighting against the Philistines in the Valley of the Terebinth.

**17:20** So David rose early in the morning, entrusted the flock to a keeper, and set out carrying his load, just as Jesse had commanded him. And he came to the place Magala and to the army, which had gone out to battle and had shouted in the combat.

**17:21** For Israel had drawn up its battle line, and the Philistines too had been prepared on the opposite side.

**17:22** So leaving the supplies he had brought under the charge of the keeper at the baggage, David ran to the place of combat and asked whether everything was going well with his brothers.

**17:23** And while he was still speaking with them, that man of illegitimate birth appeared coming up—Goliath by name, a Philistine from Gath—from the camp of the Philistines; and as he spoke these same words, David heard them.

**17:24** Now all the Israelites, when they saw the man, fled from before him, fearing him greatly.

**17:25** And someone from Israel said, "Have you seen this man who has come up? For he has come up to taunt Israel. Therefore the king will enrich with great riches the man who strikes him down, and will give him his daughter, and will make his father's house exempt from tribute in Israel."

**17:26** And David spoke to the men who were standing with him, saying, "What will be given to the man who strikes down this Philistine and takes away the disgrace from Israel? For who is this uncircumcised Philistine, who has taunted the battle lines of the living God?"

**17:27** And the people repeated the same report to him, saying, "These things will be given to the man who strikes him down."

**17:28** When his oldest brother Eliab heard this while he was speaking with others, he grew angry with David and said, "Why have you come, and why have you left those few little sheep behind in the wilderness? I know your pride and the wickedness of your heart, for you have come down to see the battle."

**17:29** And David said, "What have I done? Is it not a word?"

**17:30** And he turned slightly away from him toward another and spoke the same words; and the people gave him an answer just as before.

**17:31** Now the words that David spoke were heard, and were reported in the presence of Saul.

**17:32** When he had been brought to him, he spoke to him, "Let no one's heart fail because of him; I, your servant, will go and fight against the Philistine."

**17:33** And Saul said to David, "You are not able to withstand this Philistine, nor to fight against him, because you are a boy, whereas this man has been a warrior from his youth."

**17:34** And David said to Saul, "Your servant was tending his father's flock, and a lion or a bear would come and carry off a ram from the middle of the flock,

**17:35** and I would pursue them and strike them and rescue it from their mouth; and they would rise up against me, and I would seize their chin, and strangle and kill them.

**17:36** For I, your servant, have killed both a lion and a bear; therefore this uncircumcised Philistine also will be like one of them. Now I will go and take away the disgrace of the people; for who is this uncircumcised Philistine, who has dared to curse the army of the living God?"

**17:37** And David said, "The Lord, who rescued me from the hand of the lion and from the hand of the bear, He Himself will deliver me from the hand of this Philistine." And Saul said to David, "Go, and may the Lord be with you."

**17:38** And Saul dressed David in his own garments, and placed a bronze helmet on his head, and clothed him in a coat of armor.

**17:39** So David, having strapped on his sword over his garment, began to test whether he could walk while armed, for he was not accustomed to it. And David said to Saul, "I cannot walk like this, because I have no practice." And he took them off,

**17:40** and took his staff, which he always had in his hands, and chose for himself five very clean stones from the brook, and put them into the shepherd's pouch that he had with him, and took a sling in his hand; and he advanced against the Philistine.

**17:41** Now the Philistine walked on, advancing and drawing near against David, with his armor-bearer before him.

**17:42** And when the Philistine looked closely and saw David, he despised him. For he was a youth, ruddy, and handsome in appearance.

**17:43** And the Philistine said to David, "Am I a dog, that you come to me with a staff?" And the Philistine cursed David by his gods.

**17:44** And he said to David, "Come to me, and I will give your flesh to the birds of the sky and the beasts of the earth."

**17:45** But David said to the Philistine, "You come to me with a sword, and a spear, and a shield; but I come to you in the name of the Lord of hosts, the God of the forces of Israel, whom you have taunted

**17:46** today, and the Lord will give you into my hand, and I will strike you down and remove your head from you; and I will give the corpses of the camp of the Philistines today to the birds of the sky and the beasts of the earth, so that all the earth may know that there is a God in Israel,

**17:47** and this entire assembly may know that the Lord does not save with sword or with spear; for the war is His, and He will give you over into our hands."

**17:48** Therefore, when the Philistine had risen and was coming and drawing near against David, David hurried and ran to the fight facing the Philistine.

**17:49** And he put his hand into the pouch and took one stone, and hurled it with the sling, and swinging around, struck the Philistine on the forehead; and the stone was embedded in his forehead, and he fell on his face to the ground.

**17:50** And David prevailed against the Philistine with a sling and a stone, and struck the Philistine and killed him. And since David had no sword in his hand,

**17:51** He ran and stood over the Philistine, and took his sword and drew it out of its sheath; and he killed him and cut off his head. Now when the Philistines saw that their mightiest one was dead, they fled.

**17:52** And rising up, the men of Israel and Judah shouted aloud and pursued the Philistines until they came into the valley and as far as the gates of Ekron; and the wounded of the Philistines fell on the road of Shaaraim, and as far as Gath, and as far as Ekron.

**17:53** And the sons of Israel, returning after they had pursued the Philistines, raided their camp.

**17:54** Now David, taking the head of the Philistine, brought it to Jerusalem; but he placed his weapons in his own tent.

**17:55** Now at the time when Saul had seen David going out against the Philistine, he said to Abner, the commander of the army, "From what lineage does this young man descend, Abner?" And Abner said, "As your soul lives, king, I do not know."

**17:56** And the king said, "You inquire whose son this youth is."

**17:57** And when David had returned after striking down the Philistine, Abner took him and brought him into Saul's presence, while David held the Philistine's head in his hand.

**17:58** And Saul said to him, "From what family are you, young man?" And David said, "I am the son of your servant Jesse the Bethlehemite."

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — Vulgate "in the borders of Dommim" (in finibus Dommim) translates the first element of the Hebrew place name Ephes-dammim as "in the borders of" and retains Dommim as the proper name. Similarly, in 17:2 Vulgate renders the Hebrew Valley of Elah literally as "the Valley of the Terebinth" (Vallem terebinthi).
- **17:4** — Vulgate vir spurius ("an illegitimate man") reflects Jerome's interpretation of the Hebrew phrase ish ha-benayim ("man of the between-space," that is, "champion") as denoting mixed or illegitimate birth. Vulgate also gives his height as six cubits and a palm (palmi, corresponding to Hebrew "span").
- **17:6** — Vulgate renders Hebrew kidon ("javelin" or "scimitar") as clypeus ("shield") here and in 17:45.
- **17:20** — The Vulgate treats the Hebrew word for "encampment" or "entrenchment" as a place name, Magala.
- **17:23** — Latin spurius ("illegitimate") reflects Jerome's rendering of the Hebrew term for a single-combat champion ("man of the in-between").
- **17:29** — Latin verbum ("word") reflects Hebrew davar, which can mean either a spoken word (a mere question) or a matter and reason.
- **17:35** — Vulgate includes "and strangle" (et suffocabam), a detail not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text; in 17:34 Vulgate also specifies "a ram" (arietem) where the Hebrew has a general term for a sheep or lamb, and in 17:36 includes "Now I will go and take away the disgrace of the people," matching the Septuagint.
- **17:47** — Vulgate uses ecclesia ("assembly") for the gathered Israelites, and in 17:52 translates the Hebrew place name Gai as "into the valley" (in vallem).
- **17:55** — Latin literally reads "Your soul lives, king, if I know," a Hebrew oath idiom expressing an emphatic denial.
