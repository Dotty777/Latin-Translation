## Text

**22:1** David therefore departed from there and fled into the cave of Adullam. When his brothers and all his father's house heard of this, they went down to him there.

**22:2** And all who were caught in distress, and oppressed by debt, and bitter in spirit gathered to him; and he became their leader, and about four hundred men were with him.

**22:3** And David set out from there to Mizpah, which belongs to Moab, and said to the king of Moab, "Please let my father and my mother stay with you, until I know what God will do for me."

**22:4** And he left them in the presence of the king of Moab, and they stayed with him all the days that David was in the stronghold.

**22:5** And Gad the prophet said to David, "Do not stay in the stronghold; set out and go into the land of Judah." And David set out and came into the forest of Hereth.

**22:6** And Saul heard that David and the men who were with him had appeared. Now while Saul was staying in Gibeah and was in the grove that is in Ramah, holding a spear in his hand, and all his servants were standing around him,

**22:7** he said to his servants who were standing by him, "Listen now, sons of Jemini: will the son of Jesse give fields and vineyards to all of you, and make all of you tribunes and centurions?

**22:8** For all of you have conspired against me, and there is no one who reports to me, especially since my son also has entered into a covenant with the son of Jesse. There is no one among you who grieves over my plight, nor anyone who informs me, because my son has stirred up my servant against me, lying in wait for me to this day."

**22:9** Then Doeg the Edomite, who was standing by and was foremost among the servants of Saul, answered and said, "I saw the son of Jesse in Nob, with Ahimelech the son of Ahitub, the priest.

**22:10** He consulted the Lord for him and gave him provisions, and he also gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine."

**22:11** The king therefore sent to summon Ahimelech the priest, the son of Ahitub, and all his father's house, the priests who were in Nob; and they all came to the king.

**22:12** And Saul said to Ahimelech, "Listen, son of Ahitub." And he answered, "I am ready, my lord."

**22:13** And Saul said to him, "Why have you conspired against me, you and the son of Jesse, and given him loaves of bread and a sword, and consulted God for him, so that he might rise up against me, remaining one who lies in wait to this day?"

**22:14** And Ahimelech answered the king and said, "And who among all your servants is like David—faithful, and the king's son-in-law, and going at your command, and honored in your house?

**22:15** Did I begin today to consult God for him? Far be this from me! Let the king not suspect a thing of this kind against his servant, in all my father's house; for your servant knew nothing about this affair, either small or great."

**22:16** And the king said, "You will certainly die, Ahimelech, you and all your father's house."

**22:17** And the king said to the emissaries who stood around him, "Turn and kill the priests of the Lord, for their hand is with David; knowing that he had fled, yet they did not inform me." But the servants of the king were unwilling to stretch out their hands against the priests of the Lord.

**22:18** And the king said to Doeg, "You turn and rush upon the priests." And Doeg the Edomite turned and rushed upon the priests, and slaughtered on that day eighty-five men clothed in a linen ephod.

**22:19** And Nob, the city of the priests, he struck with the edge of the sword: men and women, and little children and nursing infants, and ox, donkey, and sheep, with the edge of the sword.

**22:20** Now one son of Ahimelech the son of Ahitub, whose name was Abiathar, escaped and fled to David,

**22:21** and reported to him that Saul had killed the priests of the Lord.

**22:22** And David said to Abiathar, "I knew on that day, since Doeg the Edomite was there, that he would without doubt report it to Saul; I am guilty of all the souls of your father.

**22:23** Stay with me; do not be afraid: if anyone seeks my life, he will seek your life also, and with me you will be kept safe."

## Translator Notes

- **22:6** — The Vulgate reads "in the grove that is in Ramah" (in nemore quod est in Rama), treating Rama as a proper place name, whereas the Hebrew refers to a tamarisk tree "on the height" in Gibeah.
- **22:7** — "sons of Jemini" renders the Vulgate filii Jemini literally, reflecting the shorter Hebrew gentilic form for Benjaminites; "tribunes and centurions" preserves the Vulgate's Roman military titles for commanders of thousands and hundreds.
- **22:14** — The Vulgate reads "going at your command" (pergens ad imperium tuum), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text is commonly rendered "captain of your bodyguard."
- **22:19** — "with the edge of the sword" translates the Latin idiom in ore gladii (literally "in the mouth of the sword"), mirroring the corresponding Hebrew expression.
- **22:22** — The Vulgate reads "of all the souls of your father" (omnium animarum patris tui), without the word "house" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
