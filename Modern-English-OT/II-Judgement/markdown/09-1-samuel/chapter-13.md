## Text

**13:2** And Saul chose for himself three thousand from Israel; and two thousand were with Saul in Michmash and in the mountain of Bethel, while one thousand were with Jonathan in Gibeah of Benjamin; and as for the rest of the people, he sent them away, each one to his tents.

**13:3** And Jonathan struck the garrison of the Philistines that was in Gibeah. And when the Philistines heard of this, Saul sounded the trumpet throughout all the land, saying, "Let the Hebrews hear."

**13:4** And all Israel heard a report of this kind: "Saul has struck the garrison of the Philistines, and Israel has risen up against the Philistines." Therefore the people cried out after Saul in Gilgal.

**13:5** And the Philistines assembled to do battle against Israel, thirty thousand chariots and six thousand horsemen, and the rest of the multitude as numerous as the sand that is on the seashore. And going up, they encamped in Michmash to the east of Beth-aven.

**13:6** When the men of Israel saw that they were placed in tight straits (for the people were distressed), they hid themselves in caves, and in hidden places, in rocks as well, and in caverns, and in cisterns.

**13:7** Now the Hebrews crossed the Jordan into the land of Gad and Gilead. And while Saul was still in Gilgal, all the people who were following him were terrified.

**13:8** And he waited seven days according to the appointed time of Samuel, yet Samuel did not come to Gilgal, and the people slipped away from him.

**13:9** Therefore Saul said, "Bring me the burnt offering and the peace offerings." And he offered the burnt offering.

**13:10** And when he had finished offering the burnt offering, look, Samuel was coming; and Saul went out to meet him in order to greet him.

**13:11** And Samuel spoke to him, "What have you done?" Saul answered, "Because I saw that the people were slipping away from me, and you had not come according to the appointed days, and moreover the Philistines had assembled in Michmash,

**13:12** I said, 'Now the Philistines will come down to me in Gilgal, and I have not appeased the face of the Lord.' Compelled by necessity, I offered the burnt offering."

**13:13** And Samuel said to Saul, "You have acted foolishly, and you have not kept the commands of the Lord your God that He commanded you. If you had not done this, even now the Lord would have established your kingdom over Israel forever;

**13:14** but your kingdom will by no means rise any further. The Lord has sought out for Himself a man according to His own heart, and the Lord has commanded him to be leader over His people, because you have not observed what the Lord commanded."

**13:15** Then Samuel rose up and went up from Gilgal to Gibeah of Benjamin. And the rest of the people went up after Saul to meet the people who were attacking them, coming from Gilgal to Gibeah, on the hill of Benjamin. And Saul counted the people who had been found with him, about six hundred men.

**13:16** And Saul, and Jonathan his son, and the people who had been found with them, were in Gibeah of Benjamin, while the Philistines had taken up position in Michmash.

**13:17** And three companies went out from the camp of the Philistines to plunder. One company proceeded toward the road to Ophrah, to the land of Shual.

**13:18** Moreover, another went along the road to Beth-horon, while the third had turned toward the path of the border overlooking the valley of Zeboim, toward the wilderness.

**13:19** Now no blacksmith was found in all the land of Israel, for the Philistines had taken precautions so that the Hebrews would not make a sword or a spear.

**13:20** Therefore all Israel used to go down to the Philistines, so that each one might sharpen his plowshare, and his mattock, and his axe, and his hoe.

**13:21** And so the edges of the plowshares, and of the mattocks, and of the three-pronged forks, and of the axes were blunted, even to the straightening of a goad.

**13:22** And when the day of battle came, neither sword nor spear was found in the hand of all the people who were with Saul and Jonathan, except for Saul and Jonathan his son.

**13:23** And the garrison of the Philistines went out to cross over into Michmash.

## Translator Notes

- **13:3** — The Vulgate uses 'Gabaa' consistently throughout this chapter (13:2, 3, 15, 16), whereas the Hebrew text alternates between Gibeah (13:2, 15) and Geba (13:3, 16).
- **13:4** — The Vulgate reads 'erexit se Israel adversus Philisthiim' ('Israel has risen up against the Philistines') and active 'Clamavit' ('cried out'), whereas the Hebrew has 'Israel had become odious to the Philistines' and 'the people were called out.'
- **13:15** — The middle sentence ('And the rest of the people went up after Saul... on the hill of Benjamin') is a distinctly Vulgate reading shared with the Septuagint tradition but absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **13:21** — The Vulgate 'Retusae itaque erant acies' ('And so the edges ... were blunted') reflects an ancient interpretation of an obscure Hebrew phrase that modern scholarship identifies as a sharpening fee of one pim (about two-thirds of a shekel).
- **13:23** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew noun phrase 'to the pass of Michmash' as a purpose clause, 'ut transcenderet in Machmas' ('to cross over into Michmash').
