## Text

**4:1** And it happened in those days that the Philistines assembled for combat; and Israel went out to meet the Philistines for battle, and encamped beside the Stone of Help. Meanwhile, the Philistines came to Aphek,

**4:2** and drew up the battle line against Israel. Now when the engagement had begun, Israel turned its back to the Philistines; and about four thousand men were cut down in that engagement here and there throughout the fields.

**4:3** And the people returned to the camp, and the elders of Israel said, "Why has the Lord struck us today before the Philistines? Let us bring the ark of the covenant of the Lord to us from Shiloh, and let it come into our midst, so that it may save us from the hand of our enemies."

**4:4** Therefore the people sent to Shiloh, and from there they brought the ark of the covenant of the Lord of hosts, who sits upon the cherubim; and the two sons of Eli were with the ark of the covenant of God, Hophni and Phinehas.

**4:5** And when the ark of the covenant of the Lord had come into the camp, all Israel shouted with a great shout, and the earth resounded.

**4:6** And the Philistines heard the sound of the shout, and they said, "What is this sound of a great shout in the camp of the Hebrews?" And they learned that the ark of the Lord had come into the camp.

**4:7** And the Philistines were afraid, saying, "God has come into the camp." And they groaned, saying,

**4:8** "Woe to us! For there was not such great exultation yesterday and the day before; woe to us! Who will save us from the hand of these exalted gods? These are the gods who struck Egypt with every plague in the desert.

**4:9** Be strong and be men, Philistines, so that you do not serve the Hebrews, just as they also served you; be strong and fight."

**4:10** So the Philistines fought, and Israel was cut down, and each one fled to his tent; and an exceedingly great slaughter took place, and thirty thousand foot soldiers fell from Israel.

**4:11** And the ark of God was captured; the two sons of Eli also died, Hophni and Phinehas.

**4:12** Now a man of Benjamin, running from the battle line, came to Shiloh on that day, with his garment torn and his head sprinkled with dust.

**4:13** And when he had arrived, Eli was sitting upon a seat, looking toward the road. For his heart was trembling for the ark of God. Now after that man had entered, he reported it to the city, and the entire city wailed.

**4:14** And Eli heard the sound of the outcry, and he said, "What is this sound of this tumult?" And that man hurried, and came, and reported to Eli.

**4:15** Now Eli was ninety-eight years old, and his eyes had grown dim, and he could not see.

**4:16** And he said to Eli, "I am the one who came from the battle, and I am the one who fled from the battle line today." And he said to him, "What has happened, my son?"

**4:17** Now the one who was reporting answered, "Israel has fled before the Philistines, and a great ruin has occurred among the people; furthermore, your two sons also have died, Hophni and Phinehas, and the ark of God has been captured."

**4:18** And when he had mentioned the ark of God, he fell backward from the seat beside the door, and with his neck broken, he died. For the man was old and advanced in age; and he himself judged Israel for forty years.

**4:19** Now his daughter-in-law, the wife of Phinehas, was pregnant and near to childbirth; and upon hearing the report that the ark of God had been captured, and that her father-in-law and her husband had died, she bowed herself down and gave birth, for sudden pains had rushed upon her.

**4:20** Now at the very moment of her death, the women who were standing around her said to her, "Do not be afraid, because you have given birth to a son." But she did not answer them, nor did she pay attention.

**4:21** And she called the boy Ichabod, saying, "The glory has been carried away from Israel," because the ark of God was captured, and on account of her father-in-law and her husband;

**4:22** and she said, "The glory has been carried away from Israel, because the ark of God was captured."

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — The Vulgate (with the Septuagint) opens the chapter with the Philistines assembling for combat, a clause absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text; it also translates the place name Ebenezer literally as "lapis Adjutorii" ("the Stone of Help," as again in 5:1).
- **4:8** — The Vulgate reads "tanta exultatio" ("such great exultation"), referring back to Israel's shout in 4:5, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "nothing like this has happened before."
- **4:18** — The Vulgate describes Eli as "grandaevus" ("advanced in age"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text describes him as "heavy."
- **4:21** — The Clementine Vulgate prints "vocabit," a well-known orthographic b/v variant for "vocavit" ("she called"), translated here in the past tense in keeping with the narrative context and "ait" ("she said") in 4:22.
