## Text

**2:1** "My heart has exulted in the Lord, and my horn has been exalted in my God; my mouth has opened wide over my enemies, because I have rejoiced in Your salvation.

**2:2** There is no one holy as the Lord is, for indeed there is no other besides You, and there is no strong one like our God.

**2:3** Do not multiply speaking lofty things while boasting; let old things depart from your mouth, for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and for Him thoughts are prepared.

**2:4** The bow of the mighty has been overcome, and the weak have been girded with strength.

**2:5** Those who were full before have hired themselves out for bread, and the hungry have been satisfied, until the barren woman has borne very many, and she who had many children has been weakened.

**2:6** The Lord puts to death and makes alive; He brings down to the underworld and brings back up.

**2:7** The Lord makes poor and enriches; He humbles and lifts up.

**2:8** He raises up the needy from the dust and lifts up the poor from the dung, so that he may sit with princes and hold a throne of glory. For the pivots of the earth belong to the Lord, and He has set the world upon them.

**2:9** He will guard the feet of His holy ones, and the wicked will fall silent in darkness, for not by his own strength will a man be made strong.

**2:10** His adversaries will dread the Lord, and over them He will thunder in the heavens. The Lord will judge the ends of the earth, and He will give dominion to His king, and He will lift high the horn of His anointed one."

**2:11** And Elkanah went away to Ramah, to his house; but the boy was a minister in the sight of the Lord before the face of Eli the priest.

**2:12** Now the sons of Eli were sons of Belial, not knowing the Lord,

**2:13** nor the duty of the priests toward the people; rather, whenever anyone had offered a sacrifice, the priest's servant would come while the meat was being cooked, and he had a three-pronged fork in his hand,

**2:14** and he would thrust it into the kettle, or into the cauldron, or into the pot, or into the pan; and everything that the fork lifted up, the priest took for himself. Thus they did to all Israel who came to Shiloh.

**2:15** Even before they burned the fat, the priest's servant would come and say to the one offering sacrifice, "Give me meat to cook for the priest, for I will not accept cooked meat from you, but raw."

**2:16** And the one offering sacrifice would say to him, "Let the fat be burned first today according to custom, and take for yourself as much as your soul desires." And answering, he would say to him, "By no means; for you will give it now, otherwise I will take it by force."

**2:17** Therefore the sin of the young men was exceedingly great before the Lord, because they were holding people back from the sacrifice of the Lord.

**2:18** Now Samuel was ministering before the face of the Lord, a boy girded with a linen ephod.

**2:19** And his mother used to make a small tunic for him, which she would bring on the appointed days when going up with her husband to offer the solemn sacrifice.

**2:20** And Eli blessed Elkanah and his wife, and he said to him, "May the Lord repay you with seed from this woman for the loan that you have lent to the Lord." And they went away to their own place.

**2:21** Therefore the Lord visited Hannah, and she conceived and bore three sons and two daughters. And the boy Samuel was magnified before the Lord.

**2:22** Now Eli was very old, and he heard everything that his sons were doing to all Israel, and how they were sleeping with the women who kept watch at the doorway of the tabernacle;

**2:23** and he said to them, "Why do you do things of this kind, most wicked things, which I hear from all the people?

**2:24** No, my sons, for it is not a good report that I hear, that you are making the people of the Lord transgress.

**2:25** If a man sins against a man, God can be appeased for him; but if a man sins against the Lord, who will pray for him?" And they did not listen to the voice of their father, because the Lord willed to kill them.

**2:26** Now the boy Samuel was advancing and growing, and he was pleasing both to the Lord and to people.

**2:27** Now a man of God came to Eli and said to him, "This is what the Lord says: Was I not openly revealed to the house of your father, when they were in Egypt in the house of Pharaoh?

**2:28** And I chose him out of all the tribes of Israel for Myself as a priest, so that he might go up to My altar, and burn incense to Me, and bear an ephod before Me; and I gave to the house of your father everything from the sacrifices of the sons of Israel.

**2:29** Why have you kicked away My sacrifice and My offerings that I commanded to be offered in the temple, and honored your sons more than Me, so that you might eat the firstfruits of every sacrifice of Israel, My people?

**2:30** Therefore the Lord God of Israel says: I certainly spoke that your house and the house of your father should minister in My sight forever. But now the Lord says: Far be this from Me; rather, whoever glorifies Me, I will glorify him, but those who despise Me will be without honor.

**2:31** Look, the days are coming, and I will cut off your arm and the arm of the house of your father, so that there will not be an old man in your house.

**2:32** And you will see your rival in the temple, in all the prosperity of Israel; and there will not be an old man in your house for all days.

**2:33** Nevertheless, I will not completely take away a man of yours from My altar, but so that your eyes may fail and your soul may waste away; and a great part of your house will die when they reach manhood.

**2:34** And this will be a sign to you, what is about to come upon your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas: on one day both of them will die.

**2:35** And I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest, who will act according to My heart and My soul; and I will build for him a faithful house, and he will walk before My anointed one for all days.

**2:36** And it will come about that whoever remains in your house will come so that prayer may be made for him, and will offer a silver coin and a loaf of bread, and will say, 'Admit me, I beg you, to one priestly portion, so that I may eat a morsel of bread.'"

## Translator Notes

- **2:3** — The Vulgate reads 'vetera' ("old things", connecting Hebrew 'ataq' with the root "ancient" rather than "arrogant") and 'ipsi præparantur cogitationes' ("for Him thoughts are prepared"), where the Hebrew has "by Him actions are weighed."
- **2:5** — The Vulgate renders 'plurimos' ("very many") where the Hebrew specifies "seven," and 'saturati sunt' ("have been satisfied") where the Hebrew reads "have ceased [to hunger]."
- **2:10** — Latin 'christi sui' (like 'christo meo' in 2:35) translates Hebrew 'mashiach' ("anointed one"); the Vulgate also reads 'imperium' ("dominion") where the Hebrew has "strength."
- **2:32** — The Vulgate reads 'æmulum tuum in templo' ("your rival in the temple"), understanding Hebrew 'tsar ma'on' as a rival in the sanctuary rather than "distress in the dwelling."
- **2:36** — The Vulgate reads 'ut oretur pro eo, et offerat nummum argenteum, et tortam panis' ("so that prayer may be made for him, and will offer a silver coin and a loaf of bread"), whereas the Hebrew depicts Eli's descendant bowing down to beg for silver and bread.
