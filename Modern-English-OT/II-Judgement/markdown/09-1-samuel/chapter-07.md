## Text

**7:1** So the men of Kiriath-jearim came and brought back the ark of the Lord, and brought it into the house of Abinadab in Gibeah; and they sanctified Eleazar his son to guard the ark of the Lord.

**7:2** And it happened that from the day on which the ark of the Lord remained in Kiriath-jearim, the days were multiplied (for it was already the twentieth year), and all the house of Israel rested after the Lord.

**7:3** And Samuel spoke to the entire house of Israel, saying, "If you are returning to the Lord with your whole heart, remove the foreign gods from your midst, the Baals and the Ashtaroth, and prepare your hearts for the Lord, and serve Him alone, and He will rescue you from the hand of the Philistines."

**7:4** So the sons of Israel removed the Baals and the Ashtaroth, and served the Lord alone.

**7:5** And Samuel said, "Gather all Israel to Mizpah, so that I may pray to the Lord for you."

**7:6** And they assembled at Mizpah, and drew water and poured it out in the sight of the Lord; and they fasted on that day and said there, "We have sinned against the Lord." And Samuel judged the sons of Israel at Mizpah.

**7:7** And the Philistines heard that the sons of Israel had been gathered together at Mizpah, and the satraps of the Philistines went up against Israel. When the sons of Israel heard this, they were afraid before the Philistines.

**7:8** And they said to Samuel, "Do not cease crying out for us to the Lord our God, so that He may save us from the hand of the Philistines."

**7:9** And Samuel took one nursing lamb and offered it as a whole burnt offering to the Lord; and Samuel cried out to the Lord for Israel, and the Lord heard him.

**7:10** Now it happened, while Samuel was offering the burnt offering, that the Philistines entered into battle against Israel; but the Lord thundered with a great crash on that day over the Philistines and terrified them, and they were struck down before Israel.

**7:11** And the men of Israel went out from Mizpah, pursued the Philistines, and struck them as far as the place that was below Beth-car.

**7:12** Then Samuel took one stone and set it between Mizpah and Shen, and he called the name of that place Stone of Help. And he said, "Up to this point the Lord has helped us."

**7:13** And the Philistines were humbled, and they did not come again into the borders of Israel. And so the hand of the Lord was against the Philistines all the days of Samuel.

**7:14** And the cities that the Philistines had taken from Israel were restored to Israel, from Ekron to Gath, and their borders; and He delivered Israel from the hand of the Philistines, and there was peace between Israel and the Amorites.

**7:15** Samuel also judged Israel all the days of his life;

**7:16** and he went year by year making a circuit of Bethel and Gilgal and Mizpah, and he judged Israel in the aforementioned places.

**7:17** And he would return to Ramah, for his house was there, and there he judged Israel; he also built an altar there to the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — Vulgate in Gabaa ("in Gibeah") takes Hebrew baggibah as a proper place name rather than the common noun "on the hill."
- **7:2** — Vulgate requievit ("rested") derives the underlying Hebrew verb from nuah ("to rest") rather than nahah ("to lament/mourn"), producing a distinctly Vulgate reading.
- **7:12** — Vulgate translates the Hebrew name Ebenezer directly into Latin as Lapis adjutorii ("Stone of Help") and explicitly specifies nomen loci illius ("the name of that place") rather than the stone itself.
- **7:14** — Vulgate attaches et terminos suos ("and their borders") to the restored cities and begins a new clause with liberavitque ("and He delivered"), treating Israel as the object delivered from the Philistines rather than the subject rescuing its territory as in the Hebrew.
