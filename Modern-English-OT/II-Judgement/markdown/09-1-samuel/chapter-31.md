## Text

**31:1** Now the Philistines were fighting against Israel, and the men of Israel fled before the face of the Philistines and fell dead on Mount Gilboa.

**31:2** And the Philistines rushed upon Saul and upon his sons, and they struck down Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua, the sons of Saul.

**31:3** And the entire weight of the battle turned against Saul, and the archers overtook him, and he was severely wounded by the archers.

**31:4** And Saul said to his armor-bearer, "Draw your sword and strike me, so that these uncircumcised men may not come and kill me, mocking me." But his armor-bearer would not, for he had been terrified with extreme terror. Therefore Saul seized the sword and fell upon it.

**31:5** And when his armor-bearer saw this, namely that Saul was dead, he also fell upon his own sword and died with him.

**31:6** So Saul died, and his three sons, and his armor-bearer, and all his men together on that day.

**31:7** Now when the men of Israel who were across the valley and across the Jordan saw that the Israelite men had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they abandoned their cities and fled; and the Philistines came and lived there.

**31:8** And when the next day had come, the Philistines came to strip those who had been killed, and they found Saul and his three sons lying on Mount Gilboa.

**31:9** And they cut off Saul's head and stripped him of his armor, and they sent throughout the land of the Philistines all around so that it might be announced in the temple of idols and among the peoples.

**31:10** And they placed his armor in the temple of Ashtaroth, while they hung his body on the wall of Beth-shan.

**31:11** Now when the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead heard this, all that the Philistines had done to Saul,

**31:12** all the bravest men rose up and walked all night, and took the corpse of Saul and the corpses of his sons from the wall of Beth-shan; and they came to Jabesh-gilead and burned them there.

**31:13** And they took their bones and buried them in the grove of Jabesh, and they fasted for seven days.

## Translator Notes

- **31:1** — Matches Latin 'ceciderunt interfecti' smoothly in battle context without using banned 'slain' or unidiomatic 'fell killed'.
- **31:2** — The Vulgate reads Irrueruntque ... in Saul ("rushed upon Saul"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "pursued closely after" or "overtook."
- **31:9** — The Vulgate reads plural in populis ("among the peoples") and has no possessive pronoun with in templo idolorum ("in the temple of idols"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "in the house of their idols and to the people."
- **31:13** — The Vulgate reads in nemore Jabes ("in the grove of Jabesh"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "under the tamarisk tree in Jabesh."
