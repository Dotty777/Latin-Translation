## Text

**23:1** And they told David, saying, "Look, the Philistines are attacking Keilah and plundering the threshing floors."

**23:2** David therefore consulted the Lord, saying, "Should I go and strike these Philistines?" And the Lord said to David, "Go, and you will strike the Philistines and save Keilah."

**23:3** And the men who were with David said to him, "Look, we are afraid while staying here in Judea; how much more if we go to Keilah against the ranks of the Philistines?"

**23:4** David therefore consulted the Lord again. And answering, He said to him, "Rise and go to Keilah, for I will deliver the Philistines into your hand."

**23:5** David and his men therefore went to Keilah, and he fought against the Philistines, and drove off their livestock, and struck them with a great blow; and David saved the inhabitants of Keilah.

**23:6** Now at the time when Abiathar the son of Ahimelech was fleeing to David in Keilah, he had come down having an ephod with him.

**23:7** Now it was reported to Saul that David had come to Keilah, and Saul said, "God has delivered him into my hands, and he is shut in, having entered a city in which there are gates and bars."

**23:8** And Saul commanded all the people to go down to Keilah for battle and to besiege David and his men.

**23:9** When David learned that Saul was secretly preparing evil against him, he said to Abiathar the priest, "Bring the ephod near."

**23:10** And David said, "Lord God of Israel, Your servant has heard a report that Saul intends to come to Keilah to destroy the city on account of me:

**23:11** will the men of Keilah deliver me into his hands? And will Saul come down, just as Your servant has heard? Lord God of Israel, tell Your servant." And the Lord said, "He will come down."

**23:13** David and his men, about six hundred, therefore rose, and having gone out from Keilah, they wandered here and there uncertainly; and it was reported to Saul that David had fled from Keilah and had escaped; for this reason he refrained from going out.

**23:14** Now David stayed in the desert in the strongest places, and remained on the mountain of the wilderness of Ziph, on a shaded mountain; yet Saul sought him every day, and God did not deliver him into his hands.

**23:15** And David saw that Saul had gone out to seek his life. Now David was in the desert of Ziph, in a forest.

**23:16** And Jonathan the son of Saul rose and went to David into the forest, and strengthened his hands in God, and said to him,

**23:17** "Do not be afraid, for the hand of my father Saul will not find you; and you will reign over Israel, and I will be second to you; and my father Saul also knows this."

**23:18** Both of them therefore made a covenant before the Lord; and David remained in the forest, while Jonathan returned to his house.

**23:19** Now the Ziphites went up to Saul at Gibeah, saying, "Look, is David not hiding among us in the safest places of the forest, on the hill of Hachilah, which is to the right of the desert?

**23:20** Now therefore, just as your soul has desired to come down, come down; and it will be our part to deliver him into the hands of the king."

**23:21** And Saul said, "May you be blessed by the Lord, because you have grieved over my plight.

**23:22** Go therefore, please, and prepare more diligently, and inquire more closely, and observe the place where his foot is, or who has seen him there; for he thinks about me that I craftily lie in wait for him.

**23:23** Observe and see all his hiding places in which he hides himself, and return to me with certainty, so that I may go with you. And if he even hides himself in the earth, I will search him out among all the thousands of Judah."

**23:24** So rising, they went to Ziph ahead of Saul; now David and his men were in the desert of Maon, in the plains to the right of Jeshimon.

**23:25** Saul and his companions therefore went to seek him. And it was reported to David, and immediately he went down to the rock and stayed in the desert of Maon; and when Saul heard of this, he pursued David in the desert of Maon.

**23:26** And Saul was going along the side of the mountain on one side, while David and his men were on the side of the mountain on the other side. Moreover, David was despairing of being able to escape from the presence of Saul; and so Saul and his men were encircling David and his men in the form of a ring, in order to capture them.

**23:27** And a messenger came to Saul, saying, "Hurry and come, for the Philistines have poured themselves over the land."

**23:28** Saul therefore turned back, ceasing to pursue David, and proceeded to meet the Philistines; on account of this they called that place the Dividing Rock.

## Translator Notes

- **23:14** — The Vulgate adds "on a shaded mountain" (in monte opaco), a descriptive phrase not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **23:15** — Here and in verses 16, 18, and 19, the Vulgate translates Hebrew horeshah as the common noun "forest" (silva) rather than as the proper place name "Horesh."
- **23:22** — The Vulgate reads "for he thinks about me that I craftily lie in wait for him" (recogitat enim de me, quod callide insidier ei), making David the subject of the reflection, whereas the Hebrew reads "for it is told to me that he is very cunning."
- **23:26** — The Vulgate renders David's reaction as "despairing of being able to escape" (desperabat se posse evadere), whereas the Hebrew describes David hurrying to get away.
- **23:28** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew place name Sela-hammahlekoth as "the Dividing Rock" (Petram dividentem).
