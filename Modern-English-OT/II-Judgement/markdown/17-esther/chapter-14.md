## Text

**14:1** Queen Esther also fled for refuge to the Lord, dreading the danger that was impending.

**14:2** And when she had laid aside her royal garments, she put on clothing suited for weeping and mourning; and instead of various perfumes, she filled her head with ashes and dung, and humbled her body with fastings; and all the places in which she had previously been accustomed to rejoice, she filled with the tearing of her hair.

**14:3** And she implored the Lord God of Israel, saying, "My Lord, who alone are our King, help me who am alone, and who have no other helper besides You.

**14:4** My peril is in my hands.

**14:5** I have heard from my father that You, Lord, took Israel from all the nations, and our fathers from all their ancestors before them, so that You might possess an everlasting inheritance, and You did for them just as You spoke.

**14:6** We have sinned in Your sight, and for that reason You have handed us over into the hands of our enemies,

**14:7** for we worshiped their gods. You are righteous, Lord;

**14:8** and now it is not enough for them that they oppress us with the harshest servitude, but ascribing the strength of their hands to the power of idols,

**14:9** they wish to alter Your promises, and to blot out Your inheritance, and to close the mouths of those who praise You, and to extinguish the glory of Your temple and altar,

**14:10** so that they may open the mouths of the nations, and praise the might of idols, and proclaim a fleshly king forever.

**14:11** Do not hand over Your scepter, Lord, to those who are not, so that they do not laugh at our ruin; but turn their counsel back upon them, and destroy the one who has begun to rage against us.

**14:12** Remember, Lord, and show Yourself to us in the time of our tribulation, and give me confidence, Lord, King of gods and of all power;

**14:13** put well-ordered speech in my mouth in the sight of the lion, and turn his heart to hatred of our enemy, so that both he himself and the others who agree with him may perish.

**14:14** But deliver us by Your hand, and help me, who have no other help except You, Lord, who have knowledge of all things,

**14:15** and You know that I hate the glory of the wicked, and detest the bed of the uncircumcised and of every foreigner.

**14:16** You know my necessity, that I abhor the sign of my pride and glory, which is upon my head in the days of my public display, and detest it like a menstruous cloth, and do not wear it in the days of my seclusion,

**14:17** and that I have not eaten at Haman's table, nor has the king's banquet pleased me, and I have not drunk the wine of drink offerings;

**14:18** and Your maidservant has never rejoiced from the time I was brought here until the present day, except in You, Lord God of Abraham.

**14:19** God, mighty above all, hear the voice of those who have no other hope, and deliver us from the hand of the wicked, and rescue me from my fear."

## Translator Notes

- **14:16** — Latin in diebus silentii mei literally means 'in the days of my silence' (i.e., private seclusion).
