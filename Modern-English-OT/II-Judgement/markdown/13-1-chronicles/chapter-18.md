## Text

**18:1** Now it happened after these things that David struck the Philistines and humbled them, and took Gath and its daughters from the hand of the Philistines,

**18:2** and he struck Moab, and the Moabites became servants of David, offering him gifts.

**18:3** At that time David also struck Hadarezer king of Zobah of the region of Hamath, when he went to extend his dominion as far as the river Euphrates.

**18:4** Therefore David captured a thousand of his chariots, and seven thousand horsemen, and twenty thousand men on foot; and he hamstrung all the chariot horses, except for a hundred chariots, which he reserved for himself.

**18:5** Now the Syrians of Damascus also came up to provide help to Hadarezer king of Zobah, yet of them also David struck twenty-two thousand men.

**18:6** And he placed soldiers in Damascus, so that Syria also served him and offered gifts. And the Lord helped him in everything to which he had gone.

**18:7** David also took the golden quivers that the servants of Hadarezer had possessed, and he brought them to Jerusalem;

**18:8** and also from Tibhath and Cun, cities of Hadarezer, very much bronze, from which Solomon made the bronze sea, and the pillars, and the bronze vessels.

**18:9** When Tou king of Hamath had heard this, namely, that David had struck the entire army of Hadarezer king of Zobah,

**18:10** he sent Hadoram his son to King David to ask him for peace and to congratulate him because he had struck and conquered Hadarezer; for Hadarezer was an adversary of Tou.

**18:11** And King David also consecrated all the golden, silver, and bronze vessels to the Lord, along with the silver and gold that he had taken from all the nations, both from Idumea, and Moab, and the sons of Ammon, and from the Philistines and Amalek.

**18:12** Now Abishai son of Zeruiah struck Edom in the Valley of Salt Pits, eighteen thousand;

**18:13** and he stationed a garrison in Edom, so that Idumea served David; and the Lord saved David in everything to which he had gone.

**18:14** Therefore David reigned over all Israel, and he administered judgment and justice for all his people.

**18:15** Now Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army, and Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was in charge of the records;

**18:16** and Zadok son of Ahitub, and Ahimelech son of Abiathar, were priests; and Shavsha was scribe;

**18:17** and Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the legions of the Cherethites and the Pelethites; and the sons of David were first at the hand of the king.

## Translator Notes

- **18:1** — "Its daughters" (Latin filias ejus) is a literal biblical idiom for the smaller dependent towns and villages surrounding Gath.
- **18:3** — The Vulgate reads Adarezer (Hadarezer), preserving a textual variant with r rather than d (Hadadezer in 2 Samuel 8 and many Hebrew manuscripts), and explicitly adds "of the region" (regionis) before Hamath.
- **18:7** — The Vulgate reads pharetras aureas ("golden quivers"), whereas the Hebrew term is usually translated "golden shields."
- **18:12** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew "Valley of Salt" as valle Salinarum ("Valley of Salt Pits"), and uses the Greco-Latin name Idumaea (Idumea) for Edom in vv. 11 and 13.
- **18:16** — The Vulgate reads Ahimelech (matching the Septuagint and 2 Samuel 8:17) where the Masoretic Hebrew has Abimelech, and transliterates Shavsha as Susa.
