## Text

**27:1** Now the sons of Israel according to their number, the leaders of families, tribunes, and centurions, and officers, who served the king according to their contingents, coming in and going out month by month in the year, were each in charge of twenty-four thousand.

**27:2** Over the first contingent in the first month, Jashobeam the son of Zabdiel was in charge, and under him were twenty-four thousand.

**27:3** He was from the sons of Perez, the leader of all the leaders in the army in the first month.

**27:4** Dodai the Ahohite had the contingent of the second month, and after him another named Mikloth, who commanded a part of the army of twenty-four thousand.

**27:5** Also, the commander of the third contingent in the third month was Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, the priest; and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**27:6** This is Benaiah, the mightiest among the thirty and over the thirty; and his son Ammizabad was in charge of his contingent.

**27:7** The fourth, in the fourth month, was Asahel the brother of Joab, and Zebadiah his son after him; and in his contingent were twenty-four thousand.

**27:8** The fifth, in the fifth month, was the leader Shamhuth the Izrahite; and in his contingent were twenty-four thousand.

**27:9** The sixth, in the sixth month, was Ira the son of Ikkesh the Tekoite; and in his contingent were twenty-four thousand.

**27:10** The seventh, in the seventh month, was Helez the Pelonite, from the sons of Ephraim; and in his contingent were twenty-four thousand.

**27:11** The eighth, in the eighth month, was Sibbecai the Hushathite, from the lineage of Zerah; and in his contingent were twenty-four thousand.

**27:12** The ninth, in the ninth month, was Abiezer the Anathothite, from the sons of Jemini; and in his contingent were twenty-four thousand.

**27:13** The tenth, in the tenth month, was Maharai, himself also a Netophathite, from the lineage of Zerah; and in his contingent were twenty-four thousand.

**27:14** The eleventh, in the eleventh month, was Benaiah the Pirathonite, from the sons of Ephraim; and in his contingent were twenty-four thousand.

**27:15** The twelfth, in the twelfth month, was Heldai the Netophathite, from the lineage of Othniel; and in his contingent were twenty-four thousand.

**27:16** Furthermore, those in charge of the tribes of Israel were: over the Reubenites, the chief Eliezer the son of Zichri; over the Simeonites, the chief Shephatiah the son of Maacah;

**27:17** over the Levites, Hashabiah the son of Kemuel; over the Aaronites, Zadok;

**27:18** over Judah, Elihu the brother of David; over Issachar, Omri the son of Michael;

**27:19** over the Zebulunites, Ishmaiah the son of Obadiah; over the Naphtalites, Jerimoth the son of Azriel;

**27:20** over the sons of Ephraim, Hoshea the son of Azaziah; over the half-tribe of Manasseh, Joel the son of Pedaiah;

**27:21** and over the half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead, Iddo the son of Zechariah; and over Benjamin, Jaasiel the son of Abner;

**27:22** and over Dan, Azarel the son of Jeroham. These were the leaders of the sons of Israel.

**27:23** But David was unwilling to count those from twenty years old and under, because the Lord had said that He would multiply Israel like the stars of heaven.

**27:24** Joab the son of Zeruiah had begun to count, and did not finish, because over this wrath had burst upon Israel; and therefore the number of those who had been registered was not entered into the annals of King David.

**27:25** Now over the treasuries of the king was Azmaveth the son of Adiel; and over those treasuries that were in the cities, and in the villages, and in the towers, Jonathan the son of Uzziah presided.

**27:26** And over the farm work and the farmers who tilled the land, Ezri the son of Chelub was in charge;

**27:27** and over the cultivators of the vineyards, Shemaiah the Ramathite; and over the wine cellars, Zabdi the Aphonite.

**27:28** Moreover, over the olive groves and fig groves that were in the plains was Baal-hanan the Gederite; and over the storehouses of oil was Joash.

**27:29** Furthermore, over the herds that grazed in Sharon, Shitrai the Sharonite was appointed; and over the cattle in the valleys, Shaphat the son of Adlai;

**27:30** and over the camels, Obil the Ishmaelite; and over the donkeys, Jehdeiah the Meronothite;

**27:31** and over the sheep also, Jaziz the Hagrite. All these were the officials of King David's property.

**27:32** Now Jonathan, David's uncle, was a counselor, a prudent and learned man; he and Jehiel the son of Hachmoni were with the king's sons.

**27:33** Ahithophel was also a counselor of the king, and Hushai the Archite was the king's friend.

**27:34** After Ahithophel were Jehoiada the son of Benaiah and Abiathar. And the commander of the king's army was Joab.

## Translator Notes

- **27:4** — The Vulgate distinctively describes Mikloth as "after him another named Mikloth, who commanded a part of the army of twenty-four thousand" (Latin et post se alter nomine Macelloth, qui regebat partem exercitus viginti quatuor millium), whereas the Hebrew reads "of his division Mikloth was the chief officer."
- **27:5** — Latin sacerdos ("the priest") is nominative, modifying Benaiah rather than his father Jehoiada, and the Vulgate does not include the Hebrew title "chief" (rosh).
- **27:12** — Latin de filiis Jemini ("from the sons of Jemini") reflects a literal division of Hebrew Ben-yemini ("Benjaminites") into "sons of Jemini."
- **27:27** — The Vulgate reads Aphonites ("the Aphonite"), a distinct gentilic where the Hebrew has "the Shiphmite" (ha-Shifmi).
- **27:28** — Latin ficeta means "fig groves," whereas the Hebrew specifies "sycamore trees."
