## Text

**10:1** Now the Philistines were fighting against Israel, and the men of Israel fled from the Philistines and fell wounded on Mount Gilboa.

**10:2** And when the Philistines had drawn near, pursuing Saul and his sons, they struck down Jonathan, and Abinadab, and Malchishua, the sons of Saul.

**10:3** And the battle grew heavy against Saul, and the archers found him and wounded him with arrows.

**10:4** And Saul said to his armor-bearer, "Unsheathe your sword and kill me, so that these uncircumcised men may not come and mock me." But his armor-bearer was unwilling to do this, terrified with fear; therefore Saul seized the sword and threw himself upon it.

**10:5** And when his armor-bearer saw this, namely, that Saul was dead, he also threw himself upon his sword and died.

**10:6** Therefore Saul perished, and his three sons, and all his house fell together.

**10:7** And when the men of Israel who lived in the plains saw this, they fled; and with Saul and his sons dead, they abandoned their cities and were scattered here and there; and the Philistines came and lived in them.

**10:8** On the next day, therefore, while the Philistines were stripping the spoils of those killed, they found Saul and his sons lying on Mount Gilboa.

**10:9** And when they had despoiled him, and cut off his head, and stripped him of his armor, they sent it into their land so that it might be carried around and shown to the temples of the idols and to the peoples;

**10:10** and they consecrated his armor in the shrine of their god, and fastened his head in the temple of Dagon.

**10:11** When the men of Jabesh-gilead heard this, namely, all that the Philistines had done to Saul,

**10:12** every one of the valiant men rose up and took the corpses of Saul and of his sons, and brought them to Jabesh, and buried their bones under the oak that was in Jabesh, and fasted seven days.

**10:13** Therefore Saul died because of his iniquities, because he transgressed the command of the Lord which He had commanded and did not keep it, and moreover even consulted a female medium,

**10:14** and did not hope in the Lord; because of which He killed him and transferred his kingdom to David son of Jesse.

## Translator Notes

- **10:9** — The Vulgate reads "so that it might be carried around and shown to the temples of the idols and to the peoples," whereas the Hebrew reads "to carry the good news to their idols and to the people."
- **10:13** — The Vulgate uses the explicitly feminine term pythonissam ("a female medium"), identifying the medium of Endor (1 Samuel 28), where the Hebrew term is not grammatically feminine.
- **10:14** — The Vulgate reads "and did not hope in the Lord" (nec speraverit in Domino), whereas the Hebrew reads "and did not inquire of the Lord."
