## Text

**1:1** Adam, Seth, Enosh,

**1:2** Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared,

**1:3** Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech,

**1:4** Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

**1:5** The sons of Japheth: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.

**1:6** Now the sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.

**1:7** And the sons of Javan: Elishah and Tarshish, Kittim and Dodanim.

**1:8** The sons of Ham: Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.

**1:9** And the sons of Cush: Seba, Havilah, Sabta, Raamah, and Sabteca. Now the sons of Raamah: Sheba and Dedan.

**1:10** And Cush fathered Nimrod; he began to be mighty on the earth.

**1:17** The sons of Shem: Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, Aram, Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech.

**1:24** Shem, Arpachshad, Shelah,

**1:25** Eber, Peleg, Reu,

**1:26** Serug, Nahor, Terah,

**1:27** Abram: he is Abraham.

**1:28** And the sons of Abraham were Isaac and Ishmael.

**1:29** And these are their generations. The firstborn of Ishmael, Nebaioth; and Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,

**1:30** Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema,

**1:31** Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah: these are the sons of Ishmael.

**1:32** And the sons of Keturah, Abraham's concubine, whom she bore: Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. Now the sons of Jokshan: Sheba and Dedan. And the sons of Dedan: Asshurim, Letushim, and Leummim.

**1:33** And the sons of Midian: Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these were sons of Keturah.

**1:34** And Abraham fathered Isaac, whose sons were Esau and Israel.

**1:35** The sons of Esau: Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.

**1:36** The sons of Eliphaz: Teman, Omar, Zephi, Gatam, Kenaz, Timna, and Amalek.

**1:37** The sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

**1:38** The sons of Seir: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan.

**1:39** The sons of Lotan: Hori and Homam. And Lotan's sister was Timna.

**1:40** The sons of Shobal: Alian, Manahath, Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. The sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Anah. The sons of Anah: Dishon.

**1:41** The sons of Dishon: Hamran, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.

**1:42** The sons of Ezer: Bilhan, Zaavan, and Jaakan. The sons of Dishan: Uz and Aran.

**1:43** These are the kings who ruled in the land of Edom before there was a king over the sons of Israel: Bela son of Beor, and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

**1:44** Then Bela died, and Jobab son of Zerah from Bozrah reigned in his place.

**1:45** And when Jobab had also died, Husham from the land of the Temanites reigned in his place.

**1:46** Husham also died, and in his place reigned Hadad son of Bedad, who struck down Midian in the land of Moab; and the name of his city was Avith.

**1:47** And when Hadad had also died, Samlah from Masrekah reigned in his place.

**1:48** And Samlah also died, and in his place reigned Shaul from Rehoboth, which is situated beside the river.

**1:49** When Shaul also died, Baal-hanan son of Achbor reigned in his place.

**1:50** And he also died, and Hadad reigned in his place; the name of his city was Pau, and his wife was called Mehetabel, daughter of Matred, daughter of Mezahab.

**1:51** And when Hadad died, there began to be chiefs in place of kings in Edom: Chief Timna, Chief Alvah, Chief Jetheth,

**1:52** Chief Oholibamah, Chief Elah, Chief Pinon,

**1:53** Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mibzar,

**1:54** Chief Magdiel, and Chief Iram. These were the chiefs of Edom.

## Translator Notes

- **1:6** — The Vulgate reads Riphath (agreeing with Gen 10:3, the Septuagint, and several Hebrew manuscripts), whereas the standard Masoretic Text of 1 Chronicles 1:6 reads Diphath.
- **1:7** — The Vulgate reads Dodanim (agreeing with Gen 10:4 and several Hebrew manuscripts), whereas the standard Masoretic Text of 1 Chronicles 1:7 reads Rodanim.
- **1:32** — The closing sentence listing the sons of Dedan (Asshurim, Letushim, and Leummim) appears in the Clementine Vulgate (parallel to Gen 25:3 and some Septuagint witnesses) but is absent from the Masoretic Text of 1 Chronicles 1:32.
- **1:50** — The Vulgate reads Phau (Pau, agreeing with Gen 36:39 and the Septuagint), whereas the Masoretic Text of 1 Chronicles 1:50 reads Pai.
- **1:51** — The Vulgate explicitly states that chiefs "began to be in place of kings" (duces pro regibus ... esse coeperunt), clarifying the shift in Edomite governance where the Masoretic Text simply notes Hadad's death before listing the chiefs.
