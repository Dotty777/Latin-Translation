## Text

**11:1** Therefore all Israel gathered to David in Hebron, saying, "We are your bone and your flesh.

**11:2** In former days also, while Saul was still reigning, you were the one who led out and brought in Israel; for the Lord your God said to you, 'You will shepherd My people Israel, and you will be leader over them.'"

**11:3** Therefore all the elders of Israel came to the king in Hebron, and David entered into a covenant with them before the Lord; and they anointed him king over Israel, according to the word of the Lord that He spoke by the hand of Samuel.

**11:4** David and all Israel also went to Jerusalem; this is Jebus, where the Jebusites were, the inhabitants of the land.

**11:5** And those who inhabited Jebus said to David, "You will not enter here." Yet David captured the citadel of Zion, which is the city of David.

**11:6** And he said, "Whoever strikes a Jebusite first will be leader and commander." Therefore Joab the son of Zeruiah went up first, and he became leader.

**11:7** Now David lived in the citadel, and for that reason it was called the city of David.

**11:8** And he built the city all around, from Millo as far as the surrounding circuit; Joab, however, built up the rest of the city.

**11:9** And David kept advancing, going on and growing, and the Lord of hosts was with him.

**11:10** These are the leaders of David's mighty men, who helped him so that he would become king over all Israel, according to the word of the Lord that He spoke to Israel.

**11:11** And this is the number of David's strong men: Jashobeam the son of Hachmoni, leader among the thirty; he lifted his spear over three hundred wounded men at one time.

**11:12** And after him was Eleazar the son of his uncle, the Ahohite, who was among the three powerful men.

**11:13** He was with David at Pas-dammim when the Philistines gathered to that place for battle; and a field of that region was full of barley, and the people had fled from before the Philistines.

**11:14** They stood in the middle of the field and defended it; and when they had struck down the Philistines, the Lord gave a great deliverance to His people.

**11:15** Now three of the thirty leaders went down to the rock where David was, to the cave of Adullam, when the Philistines had encamped in the valley of Rephaim.

**11:16** Meanwhile David was in the stronghold, and the garrison of the Philistines was in Bethlehem.

**11:17** Then David had a longing and said, "Oh, if only someone would give me water from the cistern of Bethlehem that is at the gate!"

**11:18** So those three went through the middle of the camp of the Philistines and drew water from the cistern of Bethlehem that was at the gate, and brought it to David so that he might drink. But he was unwilling, and instead poured it out as a drink offering to the Lord,

**11:19** saying, "Far be it from me to do this in the sight of my God and drink the blood of these men, for at the risk of their lives they brought me water." And for this reason he was unwilling to drink. The three strongest men did these things.

**11:20** Abishai also, the brother of Joab, was himself leader of the three; and he lifted his spear against three hundred wounded men, and he was most renowned among the three,

**11:21** and distinguished among the second three, and their leader; yet he had not attained to the first three.

**11:22** Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, a very strong man who had accomplished many deeds, from Kabzeel: he struck down two Ariels of Moab, and he went down and killed a lion in the middle of a cistern in a time of snow.

**11:23** And he struck down an Egyptian man whose stature was five cubits, and he had a lance like a weavers' beam; so he went down to him with a staff, and snatched the spear that he was holding in his hand, and killed him with his own spear.

**11:24** Benaiah the son of Jehoiada did these things; he was most renowned among the three strong men,

**11:25** first among the thirty, yet he had not attained to the three. And David set him at his ear.

**11:26** Now the mightiest men in the army were Asahel the brother of Joab, and Elhanan the son of his uncle, from Bethlehem,

**11:27** Shammoth the Harorite, Helez the Pharonite,

**11:28** Ira the son of Ikkesh the Tekoite, Abiezer the Anathothite,

**11:29** Sibbecai the Hushathite, Ilai the Ahohite,

**11:30** Maharai the Netophathite, Heled the son of Baanah the Netophathite,

**11:31** Ithai the son of Ribai from Gibeah of the sons of Benjamin, Benaiah the Pirathonite,

**11:32** Hurai from the brook of Gaash, Abiel the Arbathite, Azmaveth the Baharumite, Eliahba the Shaalbonite.

**11:34** The sons of Hashem the Gizonite, Jonathan the son of Shagee the Hararite,

**11:35** Ahiam the son of Sacar the Hararite,

**11:36** Hepher the Mecherathite, Ahijah the Pelonite,

**11:37** Hezro the Carmelite, Naarai the son of Ezbai,

**11:38** Joel the brother of Nathan, Mibhar the son of Hagri,

**11:39** Zelek the Ammonite, Naharai the Berothite, the armor-bearer of Joab the son of Zeruiah,

**11:40** Ira the Ithrite, Gareb the Ithrite,

**11:41** Uriah the Hittite, Zabad the son of Ahlai,

**11:42** Adina the son of Shiza the Reubenite, leader of the Reubenites, and with him thirty;

**11:43** Hanan the son of Maacah, and Joshaphat the Mithnite,

**11:44** Uzzia the Ashterathite, Shama and Jeiel the sons of Hotham the Aroerite,

**11:45** Jediael the son of Shimri, and Joha his brother, the Thosaite,

**11:46** Eliel the Mahumite, and Jeribai and Josaia the sons of Elnaam, and Ithmah the Moabite, Eliel, and Obed, and Jaasiel from Masobia.

## Translator Notes

- **11:12** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew ben-Dodo ("son of Dodo") literally as a common noun, "the son of his uncle" (filius patrui ejus), both here and in 11:26.
- **11:21** — The Vulgate resolves a difficult Hebrew phrase by explicitly contrasting "the second three" (tres secundos) with "the first three" (tres primos).
- **11:22** — The Vulgate leaves the obscure Hebrew term ariel untranslated (duos ariel Moab); it is variously understood as "lion-like warriors" or "sons of Ariel."
- **11:25** — Literally "at his ear" (ad auriculam suam), rendering Hebrew mishma'to ("bodyguard" or "listening council") to denote David's trusted personal counselor and guard.
- **11:35** — This Latin text lacks "Eliphal the son of Ur," present here in the Hebrew Masoretic Text; in this warrior roster it also has distinct proper-name readings including Pharonite (11:27), Thosaite (11:45), and Mahumite, Josaia, and Masobia (11:46).
