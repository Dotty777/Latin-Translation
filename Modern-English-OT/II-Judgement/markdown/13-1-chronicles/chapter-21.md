## Text

**21:1** Now Satan rose up against Israel and incited David to number Israel.

**21:2** And David said to Joab and to the leaders of the people, "Go and number Israel from Beersheba to Dan, and bring me the number so that I may know."

**21:3** And Joab answered, "May the Lord increase His people a hundred times more than they are. Are they not all, my lord the king, your servants? Why does my lord seek this thing, which would be reckoned as a sin to Israel?"

**21:4** But the word of the king prevailed the more; and Joab went out and went around all Israel, and returned to Jerusalem,

**21:5** and gave David the number of those whom he had gone around to; and the whole number of Israel was found to be one million one hundred thousand men drawing the sword, and of Judah four hundred seventy thousand warriors.

**21:6** For he did not number Levi and Benjamin, because Joab was carrying out the king's command unwillingly.

**21:7** Now what had been commanded displeased God, and He struck Israel.

**21:8** And David said to God, "I have sinned greatly in doing this; I implore You, take away the iniquity of Your servant, for I have acted foolishly."

**21:9** And the Lord spoke to Gad, David's seer, saying,

**21:10** "Go and speak to David, and say to him, 'This is what the Lord says: I give you a choice of three things; choose one, whichever you wish, and I will do it to you.'"

**21:11** And when Gad had come to David, he said to him, "This is what the Lord says: 'Choose what you wish:

**21:12** either three years of famine; or three months in which you flee your enemies and are unable to escape their sword; or three days of the sword of the Lord, and pestilence moving about in the land, and the angel of the Lord killing in all the borders of Israel.' Now therefore see what I should answer Him who sent me."

**21:13** And David said to Gad, "Distresses press upon me from every side; but it is better for me that I fall into the hands of the Lord, for His mercies are many, than into the hands of men."

**21:14** Therefore the Lord sent pestilence upon Israel, and seventy thousand men of Israel fell.

**21:15** He also sent an angel to Jerusalem to strike it; and while it was being struck, the Lord saw and took pity over the greatness of the calamity, and commanded the angel who was striking, "It is enough; now let your hand cease." Now the angel of the Lord was standing beside the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

**21:16** And David, lifting up his eyes, saw the angel of the Lord standing between heaven and earth, and a drawn sword in his hand, turned against Jerusalem; and both he himself and the elders, clothed in sackcloth, fell prostrate to the ground.

**21:17** And David said to God, "Is it not I who commanded that the people be numbered? I who have sinned? I who have done evil? This flock, what has it deserved? Lord my God, let Your hand, I implore You, be turned against me and against the house of my father; but let Your people not be struck."

**21:18** Now the angel of the Lord commanded Gad to tell David to go up and erect an altar to the Lord God on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

**21:19** Therefore David went up according to the word of Gad, which he had spoken to him in the name of the Lord.

**21:20** Now when Ornan looked up and saw the angel, he and his four sons with him hid themselves; for at that time he was threshing wheat on the threshing floor.

**21:21** Therefore as David was coming to Ornan, Ornan caught sight of him, and went out from the threshing floor to meet him, and paid homage to him, prostrate to the ground.

**21:22** And David said to him, "Give me the site of your threshing floor to build an altar to the Lord on it, so that you may receive as much silver as it is worth, and the plague may cease from the people."

**21:23** And Ornan said to David, "Take it, and let my lord the king do whatever pleases him; indeed, I also give the oxen for a burnt offering, and the threshing sledges for wood, and the wheat for a sacrifice; I will gladly provide everything."

**21:24** And King David said to him, "By no means will it be so, but I will give as much silver as it is worth; for I ought not to take from you and thus offer to the Lord burnt offerings without cost."

**21:25** Therefore David gave Ornan six hundred shekels of gold of the most exact weight for the site.

**21:26** And he built an altar there to the Lord, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings, and called upon the Lord; and He answered him with fire from heaven upon the altar of burnt offering.

**21:27** And the Lord commanded the angel, and he returned his sword into the sheath.

**21:28** Immediately therefore David, seeing that the Lord had answered him on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite, sacrificed victims there.

**21:29** Now the tabernacle of the Lord, which Moses had made in the wilderness, and the altar of burnt offerings were at that time on the high place of Gibeon.

**21:30** And David could not go to the altar to implore God there, for he had been terrified with excessive fear, seeing the sword of the angel of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **21:15** — The Vulgate reads "took pity" (misertus est) where the Hebrew verb denotes relenting or grieving.
- **21:20** — The Vulgate reads "looked up" (suspexisset), aligning with the angel standing between heaven and earth in verse 16, whereas the Hebrew text reads "turned back."
- **21:28** — The Vulgate reads "Immediately" (Protinus) where the Hebrew text reads "At that time."
- **21:30** — The Vulgate specifies "to the altar" (ad altare) where the Hebrew text reads "before it."
