## Text

**24:1** Now these were the divisions for the sons of Aaron. The sons of Aaron: Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

**24:2** But Nadab and Abihu died before their father without children, and Eleazar and Ithamar served in the priesthood.

**24:3** And David divided them, that is, Zadok of the sons of Eleazar, and Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, according to their divisions and service.

**24:4** And many more sons of Eleazar were found among the chief men than sons of Ithamar. And he divided for them, that is, for the sons of Eleazar, sixteen chiefs by families, and for the sons of Ithamar, eight by their families and houses.

**24:5** Moreover, he divided both families among themselves by lots; for there were leaders of the sanctuary and leaders of God both from the sons of Eleazar and from the sons of Ithamar.

**24:6** And Shemaiah the son of Nethanel, the scribe, a Levite, recorded them before the king and the leaders, and Zadok the priest, and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, and also the chiefs of the priestly and Levitical families: one house, which presided over the rest, of Eleazar, and the other house, which had the rest under it, of Ithamar.

**24:7** Now the first lot came out for Jehoiarib, the second for Jedaiah,

**24:8** the third for Harim, the fourth for Seorim,

**24:9** the fifth for Malchijah, the sixth for Mijamin,

**24:10** the seventh for Hakkoz, the eighth for Abijah,

**24:11** the ninth for Jeshua, the tenth for Shecaniah,

**24:12** the eleventh for Eliashib, the twelfth for Jakim,

**24:13** the thirteenth for Huppah, the fourteenth for Jeshebeab,

**24:14** the fifteenth for Bilgah, the sixteenth for Immer,

**24:15** the seventeenth for Hezir, the eighteenth for Happizzez,

**24:16** the nineteenth for Pethahiah, the twentieth for Jehezkel,

**24:17** the twenty-first for Jachin, the twenty-second for Gamul,

**24:18** the twenty-third for Delaiah, the twenty-fourth for Maaziah.

**24:19** These were their divisions according to their services, to enter the house of the Lord, and according to their rite under the hand of Aaron their father, just as the Lord God of Israel had commanded.

**24:20** Now of the sons of Levi who remained: from the sons of Amram was Shubael, and from the sons of Shubael, Jehdeiah.

**24:21** Also from the sons of Rehabiah, Isshiah the chief.

**24:22** And the son of Izhar was Shelomoth, and the son of Shelomoth was Jahath;

**24:23** and his son: Jeriah the first, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, Jekameam the fourth.

**24:24** The son of Uzziel was Micah; the son of Micah, Shamir.

**24:25** The brother of Micah was Isshiah; and the son of Isshiah, Zechariah.

**24:26** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The son of Jaaziah: Beno.

**24:27** Also the son of Merari: Jaaziah, Shoham, Zaccur, and Ibri.

**24:28** Moreover, the son of Mahli was Eleazar, who had no children.

**24:29** And the son of Kish was Jerahmeel.

**24:30** The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth; these were the sons of Levi according to the houses of their families.

**24:31** And they also cast lots opposite their brothers, the sons of Aaron, before King David, and Zadok, and Ahimelech, and the chiefs of the priestly and Levitical families, both the greater and the lesser; the lot divided all equally.

## Translator Notes

- **24:3** — The Vulgate renders the clause with id est ("that is"), placing Zadok and Ahimelech in apposition to the priests being divided, whereas the Hebrew reads "with Zadok ... and Ahimelech" as David's assistants.
- **24:6** — Vulgate-distinct rendering of the closing clause ("one house, which presided over the rest, of Eleazar, and the other house, which had the rest under it, of Ithamar"), where the Hebrew describes drawing one father's house for Eleazar and one for Ithamar.
- **24:23** — The Vulgate reads filiusque ejus ("and his son"), linking these four men to Jahath (v. 22) and supplying "the first" (primus), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew omits the father's name here (cf. 23:19, where they are listed as sons of Hebron).
- **24:27** — The Vulgate uses singular Filius ("son"), spells Jaaziah as Oziau (here and in v. 26), and lists him directly among the sons of Merari without repeating Beno, whereas the Hebrew reads "the sons of Merari by Jaaziah: Beno, Shoham, Zaccur, and Ibri."
- **24:31** — The Vulgate summarizes the closing clause as "both the greater and the lesser; the lot divided all equally," whereas the Hebrew reads "the head of the father's house just like his younger brother."
