## Text

**8:1** Now Benjamin fathered Bela his firstborn, Ashbel the second, Aharah the third,

**8:2** Nohah the fourth, and Rapha the fifth.

**8:3** And the sons of Bela were Addar, Gera, and Abihud;

**8:4** also Abishua, Naaman, and Ahoah;

**8:5** as well as Gera, Shephuphan, and Huram.

**8:6** These are the sons of Ehud, leaders of the clans living in Geba, who were carried away to Manahath.

**8:7** Now Naaman, Ahijah, and Gera—he himself carried them away, and he fathered Uzza and Ahihud.

**8:8** Now Shaharaim became a father in the region of Moab, after he sent away Hushim and Baara, his wives.

**8:9** And by Hodesh his wife he fathered Jobab, Zibia, Mosa, and Malcam,

**8:10** also Jeuz, Sachia, and Mirmah: these are his sons, leaders in their families.

**8:11** And Mehusim fathered Abitub and Elpaal.

**8:12** Now the sons of Elpaal were Eber, Misham, and Shemed; he built Ono and Lod, and its daughters.

**8:13** And Beriah and Shema were leaders of the clans living in Aijalon; these put to flight the inhabitants of Gath.

**8:14** And Ahio, Shashak, Jeremoth,

**8:15** Zebadiah, Arad, Eder,

**8:16** also Michael, Ishpah, and Joha were the sons of Beriah.

**8:17** And Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, Heber,

**8:18** Ishmerai, Izliah, and Jobab were the sons of Elpaal;

**8:19** and Jakim, Zichri, Zabdi,

**8:20** Elioenai, Zillethai, Eliel,

**8:21** Adaiah, Beraiah, and Shimrath were the sons of Shimei.

**8:22** And Ishpan, Eber, Eliel,

**8:23** Abdon, Zichri, Hanan,

**8:24** Hananiah, Elam, Anthothijah,

**8:25** Iphdeiah, and Penuel were the sons of Shashak.

**8:26** And Shamsherai, Shehariah, Athaliah,

**8:27** Jaareshiah, Elijah, and Zichri were the sons of Jeroham.

**8:28** These were the patriarchs and leaders of the clans, who lived in Jerusalem.

**8:29** Now in Gibeon lived Abigabaon, and the name of his wife was Maacah;

**8:30** and his firstborn son was Abdon, and Zur, Kish, Baal, and Nadab;

**8:31** also Gedor, Ahio, Zecher, and Mikloth.

**8:32** And Mikloth fathered Shimeah; and they lived opposite their brothers in Jerusalem with their brothers.

**8:33** Now Ner fathered Kish, and Kish fathered Saul. And Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal.

**8:34** And the son of Jonathan was Merib-baal; and Merib-baal fathered Micah.

**8:35** The sons of Micah were Pithon, Melech, Tarea, and Ahaz.

**8:36** And Ahaz fathered Jehoaddah, and Jehoaddah fathered Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri; and Zimri fathered Moza,

**8:37** and Moza fathered Binea, whose son was Raphah, from whom Eleasah was born, who fathered Azel.

**8:38** Now Azel had six sons, with these names: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. All these were the sons of Azel.

**8:39** And the sons of Eshek his brother were Ulam the firstborn, Jeush the second, and Eliphelet the third.

**8:40** And the sons of Ulam were very strong men, drawing the bow with great strength, and having many sons and grandsons, up to one hundred fifty. All these were the sons of Benjamin.

## Translator Notes

- **8:7** — The abrupt transition follows the Latin and Hebrew closely, with the singular pronoun "he himself" (ipse) singling out Gera as the one who carried them away and fathered Uzza and Ahihud.
- **8:9** — The Vulgate reads Mosa here, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Mesha.
- **8:11** — The Vulgate reads Mehusim as a distinct proper name and subject of "fathered," whereas the Hebrew reads me-Chushim ("by Hushim"), referring back to Shaharaim's wife Hushim in v. 8.
- **8:29** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew phrase "the father of Gibeon" (named Jeiel in 1 Chronicles 9:35) as a personal name, Abigabaon, while preserving the plural verb habitaverunt ("lived") from the Hebrew.
- **8:31** — The Vulgate includes "and Mikloth" (et Macelloth) at the end of the verse, agreeing with the Septuagint and 1 Chronicles 9:37, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text omits Mikloth in v. 31 before introducing him in v. 32.
