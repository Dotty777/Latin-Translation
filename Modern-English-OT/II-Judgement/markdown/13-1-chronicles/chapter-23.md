## Text

**23:1** Therefore David, old and full of days, appointed his son Solomon king over Israel.

**23:2** And he assembled all the leaders of Israel, and the priests and the Levites.

**23:3** And the Levites were counted from thirty years of age and upward, and thirty-eight thousand men were found.

**23:4** From these, twenty-four thousand were chosen and assigned to the service of the house of the Lord, and of overseers and judges, six thousand.

**23:5** Moreover, four thousand were gatekeepers, and as many were singers, singing to the Lord with the instruments that he had made for singing.

**23:6** And David distributed them according to the divisions of the sons of Levi: namely, Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

**23:7** The sons of Gershon: Ladan and Shimei.

**23:8** The sons of Ladan: Jehiel the chief, Zetham, and Joel, three.

**23:9** The sons of Shimei: Shelomith, Haziel, and Haran, three; these were the chiefs of the families of Ladan.

**23:10** Furthermore, the sons of Shimei: Leheth, Ziza, Jeush, and Beriah; these were the sons of Shimei, four.

**23:11** Now Leheth was the first, Ziza the second; but Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons, and therefore they were counted as one family and one house.

**23:12** The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel, four.

**23:13** The sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses. And Aaron was set apart to minister in the Holy of Holies, he and his sons forever, and to burn incense to the Lord according to his rite, and to bless His name for all time.

**23:14** The sons of Moses, the man of God, were also counted in the tribe of Levi.

**23:15** The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer.

**23:16** The sons of Gershom: Shebuel the first.

**23:17** Now the sons of Eliezer were Rehabiah the first, and Eliezer had no other sons. However, the sons of Rehabiah multiplied exceedingly.

**23:18** The sons of Izhar: Shelomith the first.

**23:19** The sons of Hebron: Jeriah the first, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, Jekameam the fourth.

**23:20** The sons of Uzziel: Micah the first, Isshiah the second.

**23:21** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish.

**23:22** Now Eleazar died and had no sons, but daughters; and the sons of Kish, their brothers, took them.

**23:23** The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth, three.

**23:24** These were the sons of Levi in their clans and families, chiefs according to divisions and the number of individual heads, who did the works of the service of the house of the Lord, from twenty years of age and upward.

**23:25** For David said, "The Lord God of Israel has given rest to His people, and a dwelling in Jerusalem forever.

**23:26** Nor will it any longer be the duty of the Levites to carry the tabernacle and all its vessels for ministering."

**23:27** Also, according to the last commands of David, the number of the sons of Levi will be reckoned from twenty years of age and upward.

**23:28** And they will be under the hand of the sons of Aaron for the worship of the house of the Lord, in the forecourts, and in the chambers, and in the place of purification, and in the sanctuary, and in all the works of the service of the temple of the Lord.

**23:29** But the priests will be over the loaves of presentation, and for the sacrifice of fine flour, and for the unleavened wafers, and the griddle, and for roasting, and over every weight and measure.

**23:30** And the Levites are to stand in the morning to give thanks and sing to the Lord, and likewise in the evening,

**23:31** both at the offering of the burnt offerings of the Lord and on the Sabbaths, the new moons, and the other solemn feasts, according to the number and ceremonies of each thing, continually before the Lord.

**23:32** And let them keep the observances of the tabernacle of the covenant, and the rite of the sanctuary, and the observance of the sons of Aaron their brothers, to minister in the house of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **23:5** — The Vulgate renders verses 4-5 as third-person narration ("that he had made for singing"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text presents them as direct speech of David ("which I made for praising").
- **23:10** — The Clementine Vulgate reads Leheth (here and in v. 11), corresponding to Hebrew Jahath (cf. 24:22), and Ziza in both verses 10 and 11, where the Hebrew reads Zina in v. 10.
- **23:13** — Vulgate-distinct readings: "to minister in the Holy of Holies" (Hebrew: "to consecrate the most holy things"), "according to his rite" (Hebrew: "to minister to Him"), and "to bless His name" (dative nomini ejus; Hebrew: "to bless in His name").
- **23:25** — In the Vulgate, habitationem ("a dwelling") is a second direct object of dedit ("has given"), whereas the Hebrew reads "and He dwells in Jerusalem forever."
- **23:29** — The Vulgate explicitly introduces "But the priests" (Sacerdotes autem), assigning the duties of this verse to the priests before turning to the Levites in v. 30; the Hebrew omits "the priests" here and continues the Levitical duties.
