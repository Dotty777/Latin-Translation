## Text

**4:1** The sons of Judah: Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal.

**4:2** Now Reaiah the son of Shobal fathered Jahath, from whom were born Ahumai and Lahad; these are the clans of the Zorathites.

**4:3** This also is the lineage of Etam: Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash. And the name of their sister was Hazzelelponi.

**4:4** And Penuel was the father of Gedor, and Ezer was the father of Hushah. These are the sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah, the father of Bethlehem.

**4:5** Now Ashhur, the father of Tekoa, had two wives, Helah and Naarah.

**4:6** And Naarah bore him Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari. These are the sons of Naarah.

**4:7** Moreover, the sons of Helah were Zereth, Izhar, and Ethnan.

**4:8** And Koz fathered Anub, Zobebah, and the clan of Aharhel the son of Harum.

**4:9** Now Jabez was renowned above his brothers, and his mother called his name Jabez, saying, "Because I bore him in pain."

**4:10** And Jabez called upon the God of Israel, saying, "If in blessing You bless me, and expand my borders, and Your hand is with me, and You cause me not to be oppressed by evil." And God granted what he prayed for.

**4:11** Now Caleb the brother of Shuhah fathered Mehir, who was the father of Eshton.

**4:12** Moreover, Eshton fathered Beth-rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah, the father of the city of Nahash; these are the men of Recah.

**4:13** Now the sons of Kenaz were Othniel and Seraiah. Moreover, the sons of Othniel were Hathath and Meonothai.

**4:14** Meonothai fathered Ophrah, and Seraiah fathered Joab, the father of the Valley of Craftsmen, for craftsmen were there.

**4:15** Now the sons of Caleb the son of Jephunneh were Ir, Elah, and Naam. And the sons of Elah: Kenaz.

**4:16** And the sons of Jehallelel: Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asarel.

**4:17** And the sons of Ezrah were Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon; and he fathered Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah, the father of Eshtemoa.

**4:18** And his wife Judaia bore Jered, the father of Gedor, and Heber, the father of Soco, and Jekuthiel, the father of Zanoah. Now these were the sons of Bithiah, the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Mered took.

**4:19** And the sons of the wife of Hodiah, the sister of Naham the father of Keilah, were Garmi and Eshtemoa, who was from Maacath.

**4:20** And the sons of Shimon were Amnon, and Rinnah the son of Hanan, and Tilon. And the sons of Ishi were Zoheth and Ben-zoheth.

**4:21** The sons of Shelah the son of Judah: Er, the father of Lecah, and Laadah, the father of Mareshah, and the clans of the house of those who work fine linen in the house of the oath.

**4:22** And he who made the sun stand still, and the men of Falsehood, and Secure, and Burning, who were princes in Moab, and who returned to Lahem; now these are ancient words.

**4:23** These are the potters living in plantations and in hedges, with the king in his works; and they remained there.

**4:24** The sons of Simeon: Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul;

**4:25** Shallum his son, Mibsam his son, Mishma his son.

**4:26** The sons of Mishma: Hammuel his son, Zaccur his son, Shimei his son.

**4:27** The sons of Shimei were sixteen, and his daughters six; but his brothers did not have many sons, and the entire clan could not equal the total of the sons of Judah.

**4:28** Now they lived in Beersheba, Moladah, and Hazar-shual,

**4:29** and in Bilhah, and in Ezem, and in Tolad,

**4:30** and in Bethuel, and in Hormah, and in Ziklag,

**4:31** and in Beth-marcaboth, and in Hazar-susim, and in Beth-biri, and in Shaaraim; these were their cities until King David.

**4:32** Their villages also were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen, and Ashan, five cities.

**4:33** And all their hamlets around these cities as far as Baal; this is their dwelling place and the distribution of their settlements.

**4:34** Meshobab also, and Jamlech, and Joshah the son of Amaziah,

**4:35** and Joel, and Jehu the son of Joshibiah, the son of Seraiah, the son of Asiel,

**4:36** and Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, and Benaiah,

**4:37** and Ziza also, the son of Shiphi, the son of Allon, the son of Jedaiah, the son of Shimri, the son of Shemaiah.

**4:38** These are those named as leaders in their clans, and in the house of their kindred they multiplied greatly.

**4:39** And they set out to enter Gedor, as far as the east side of the valley, and to seek pastures for their flocks.

**4:40** And they found rich and very good pastures, and a very broad, quiet, and fertile land, in which some from the lineage of Ham had lived before.

**4:41** These, therefore, whom we recorded above by name, came in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah, and struck their tents and the inhabitants who had been found there, and destroyed them until the present day; and they lived in their place, because they found very rich pastures in that very place.

**4:42** From the sons of Simeon also, five hundred men departed to Mount Seir, having as leaders Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, the sons of Ishi;

**4:43** and they struck the remnant of the Amalekites who had been able to escape, and lived there in their place until this day.

## Translator Notes

- **4:10** — Jabez's prayer preserves the unfinished conditional wish construction ('If in blessing You bless me...'); Vulgate renders the final petition literally as 'cause me not to be oppressed by evil' (a malitia non opprimi).
- **4:11** — Vulgate reads 'Caleb' (agreeing with the Septuagint), whereas the Hebrew text reads 'Chelub.'
- **4:18** — Vulgate treats the Hebrew 'his Judean wife' as a proper name, Judaia; similarly, in 4:19 it takes 'Garmi' as a son's name rather than 'the Garmite,' and in 4:20 renders 'Ben-hanan' as 'the son of Hanan.'
- **4:22** — In 4:21-23, the Vulgate translates several Hebrew proper names by etymology and tradition: 'house of the oath' (Beth-ashbea), 'he who made the sun stand still,' 'men of Falsehood,' 'Secure,' 'Burning,' and 'who returned to Lahem' (Jokim, Cozeba, Joash, Saraph, Jashubi-lehem), and 'plantations' and 'hedges' (Netaim and Gederah).
- **4:41** — Vulgate reads 'the inhabitants' (habitatores) where the Hebrew text names 'the Meunites,' and uses first-person plural ('whom we recorded above by name') rather than a passive participle.
