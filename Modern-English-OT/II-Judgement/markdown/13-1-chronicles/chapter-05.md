## Text

**5:1** Also, the sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel. (For he himself was his firstborn, but when he had violated his father's bed, his birthright was given to the sons of Joseph, the son of Israel, and he was not reckoned as the firstborn.

**5:2** Moreover, as for Judah, who was the strongest among his brothers, from his stock leaders sprang forth, yet the birthright was reckoned to Joseph.)

**5:3** Therefore, the sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel: Hanoch and Pallu, Hezron and Carmi.

**5:4** The sons of Joel: Shemaiah his son, Gog his son, Shimei his son,

**5:5** Micah his son, Reaiah his son, Baal his son,

**5:6** Beerah his son, whom Tilgath-pilneser, king of the Assyrians, led captive; and he was a leader in the tribe of Reuben.

**5:7** And his brothers and his entire clan, when they were numbered by their families, had Jeiel and Zechariah as leaders.

**5:8** Now Bela the son of Azaz, the son of Shema, the son of Joel, he himself lived in Aroer as far as Nebo and Baal-meon.

**5:9** Toward the eastern region also he lived as far as the entrance of the wilderness and the river Euphrates. For they possessed a great number of livestock in the land of Gilead.

**5:10** Now in the days of Saul they fought against the Hagrites and killed them, and lived in their place in their tents, in the entire region that looks toward the east of Gilead.

**5:11** And the sons of Gad lived opposite them in the land of Bashan as far as Salecah:

**5:12** Joel at the head, and Shapham second; and Janai and Shaphat in Bashan.

**5:13** And their brothers, according to the houses of their clans: Michael, Meshullam, Sheba, Jorai, Jacan, Zia, and Eber, seven.

**5:14** These are the sons of Abihail, the son of Huri, the son of Jaroah, the son of Gilead, the son of Michael, the son of Jeshishai, the son of Jahdo, the son of Buz.

**5:15** Also the brothers, the sons of Abdiel, the son of Guni, leader of the house in their families.

**5:16** And they lived in Gilead, and in Bashan and in its villages, and in all the outskirts of Sharon, as far as the borders.

**5:17** All these were numbered in the days of Jotham, king of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam, king of Israel.

**5:18** The sons of Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, fighting men, bearing shields and swords, drawing the bow, and trained for battles, were forty-four thousand seven hundred sixty, going out to combat.

**5:19** They fought against the Hagrites; now the Itureans, Naphish, and Nodab

**5:20** gave them aid. And the Hagrites were handed over into their hands, and all who had been with them, because they called upon God while they were fighting; and He heard them, because they had believed in Him.

**5:21** And they captured all that they had possessed: fifty thousand camels, and two hundred fifty thousand sheep, and two thousand donkeys, and one hundred thousand human souls.

**5:22** And many fell wounded, for it was the Lord's war. And they lived in their place until the exile.

**5:23** Also, the sons of the half-tribe of Manasseh possessed the land from the borders of Bashan as far as Baal, Hermon, and Senir, and Mount Hermon; for the number was immense.

**5:24** And these were the leaders of the house of their clan: Epher, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah, and Jahdiel, very strong and powerful men, and renowned chiefs in their families.

**5:25** But they abandoned the God of their fathers and committed fornication after the gods of the peoples of the land, whom God removed before them.

**5:26** And the God of Israel stirred up the spirit of Pul, king of the Assyrians, and the spirit of Tilgath-pilneser, king of Assyria; and he carried away Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, and brought them to Lahela, and to Habor, Hara, and the river Gozan, to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — The Vulgate reads plural principes germinati sunt ("leaders sprang forth") from Judah's stock, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads singular ("a ruler came from him").
- **5:15** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew personal name Ahi as the common noun Fratres ("brothers") and makes filii ("sons") plural, while keeping princeps ("leader") singular.
- **5:19** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew Jetur as the plural gentilic Ituraei ("Itureans") and makes the Itureans, Naphish, and Nodab the subjects of praebuerunt eis auxilium ("gave them aid") in 5:20, whereas the Hebrew lists them as opponents alongside the Hagrites and uses a passive verb ("they were helped against them").
- **5:26** — The Vulgate keeps the place name Lahela, fusing the Hebrew preposition la- ("to") with the place name Halah while also supplying the Latin preposition in ("to") before it.
