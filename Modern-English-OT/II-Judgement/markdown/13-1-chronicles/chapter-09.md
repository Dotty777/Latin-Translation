## Text

**9:1** Therefore all Israel was counted, and their total was written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah; and they were carried away to Babylon because of their transgression.

**9:2** Now the first ones who lived in their possessions and in their cities were Israel, the priests, the Levites, and the Nethinim.

**9:3** In Jerusalem resided some of the sons of Judah, and of the sons of Benjamin, and also of the sons of Ephraim and Manasseh.

**9:4** Uthai son of Ammihud, son of Omri, son of Imri, son of Bani, of the sons of Perez son of Judah.

**9:5** And of the Shilonites: Asaiah the firstborn and his sons.

**9:6** And of the sons of Zerah: Jeuel and their brothers, six hundred ninety.

**9:7** Furthermore, of the sons of Benjamin: Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Hodaviah, son of Hassenuah,

**9:8** and Ibneiah son of Jeroham, and Elah son of Uzzi, son of Michri, and Meshullam son of Shephatiah, son of Reuel, son of Ibnijah,

**9:9** and their brothers according to their families, nine hundred fifty-six. All these were chiefs of clans according to their fathers' houses.

**9:10** Now of the priests: Jedaiah, Joiarib, and Jachin;

**9:11** also Azariah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub, the chief priest of the house of God.

**9:12** Furthermore, Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah; and Maasai son of Adiel, son of Jahzerah, son of Meshullam, son of Meshillemith, son of Immer.

**9:13** Also their brothers, chiefs according to their families, one thousand seven hundred sixty, very mighty in strength for doing the work of the service in the house of God.

**9:14** Now of the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, of the sons of Merari.

**9:15** Also Bakbakkar the carpenter, and Galal, and Mattaniah son of Micah, son of Zichri, son of Asaph;

**9:16** and Obadiah son of Shemaiah, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun; and Berechiah son of Asa, son of Elkanah, who lived in the courts of the Netophathites.

**9:17** Now the gatekeepers were Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, and Ahiman, and their brother Shallum was the chief;

**9:18** until that time, at the king's gate on the east, they of the sons of Levi kept watch in their turns.

**9:19** And Shallum son of Kore, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah, with his brothers and his father's house: these are the Korahites over the works of the service, keepers of the vestibules of the tabernacle; and their families, in the turns of the camp of the Lord, were guarding the entrance.

**9:20** Now Phinehas son of Eleazar was their leader before the Lord.

**9:21** Furthermore, Zechariah son of Meshelemiah was gatekeeper of the gate of the tabernacle of the testimony.

**9:22** All these chosen as doorkeepers at the gates were two hundred twelve, and they were registered in their own villages, whom David and Samuel the seer appointed in their faithfulness,

**9:23** both them and their sons, at the doors of the house of the Lord and in the tabernacle, in their turns.

**9:24** Along the four winds were the doorkeepers: that is, to the east, and to the west, and to the north, and to the south.

**9:25** Now their brothers stayed in small villages, and they came on their sabbaths from time to time.

**9:26** To these four Levites was entrusted the entire number of the gatekeepers, and they were over the chambers and treasuries of the house of the Lord.

**9:27** Also around the temple of the Lord they stayed in their watches, so that when the time came, they themselves would open the doors in the morning.

**9:28** Some of their lineage were also over the vessels of the service, for by count the vessels were both brought in and carried out.

**9:29** Some of them also, who had the utensils of the sanctuary entrusted to them, were in charge of the fine flour, and the wine, and the oil, and the frankincense, and the spices.

**9:30** Now the sons of the priests prepared the ointments from the spices.

**9:31** And Mattithiah the Levite, the firstborn of Shallum the Korahite, was set over the things that were fried in the pan.

**9:32** Furthermore, some of the sons of Kohath, their brothers, were over the loaves of presentation, so that they would always prepare new ones on each sabbath.

**9:33** These are the chiefs of the singers according to the families of the Levites, who stayed in the chambers so that day and night they might continually attend to their service.

**9:34** The heads of the Levites, chiefs according to their families, remained in Jerusalem.

**9:35** Now in Gibeon resided the father of Gibeon, Jeiel, and the name of his wife was Maacah.

**9:36** His firstborn son was Abdon, and Zur, and Kish, and Baal, and Ner, and Nadab,

**9:37** also Gedor, and Ahio, and Zechariah, and Mikloth.

**9:38** Furthermore, Mikloth fathered Shimeam; these lived opposite their brothers in Jerusalem with their brothers.

**9:39** Now Ner fathered Kish, and Kish fathered Saul, and Saul fathered Jonathan, and Malchishua, and Abinadab, and Eshbaal.

**9:40** Now the son of Jonathan was Merib-baal, and Merib-baal fathered Micah.

**9:41** Furthermore, the sons of Micah were Pithon, and Melech, and Tahrea, and Ahaz.

**9:42** Now Ahaz fathered Jarah, and Jarah fathered Alemeth, and Azmaveth, and Zimri. And Zimri fathered Moza.

**9:43** And Moza fathered Binea, whose son Rephaiah fathered Eleasah, from whom Azel was born.

**9:44** Furthermore, Azel had six sons, by these names: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, Hanan; these are the sons of Azel.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — The Vulgate joins "and Judah" to the book title ("the book of the kings of Israel and Judah") and reads "and they were carried away," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text begins the following clause with "and Judah was carried away into exile."
- **9:15** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew personal name Heresh as the occupation "the carpenter" (carpentarius), taking it from the Hebrew noun for a craftsman.
- **9:20** — The Vulgate reads "before the Lord" (coram Domino), combining elements that the Hebrew Masoretic Text divides as "in time past; the LORD was with him."
- **9:25** — The Vulgate renders the weekly rotation as "on their sabbaths" (in sabbatis suis), whereas the Hebrew reads "every seven days."
- **9:41** — In agreement with the Septuagint and the parallel genealogy in 1 Chronicles 8:35, the Vulgate includes "and Ahaz" among the sons of Micah, which is absent in the Hebrew Masoretic Text of this verse.
