## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — This text numbers these verses 5:27-41, as the Hebrew does; the printed Sixto-Clementine edition numbers them 6:1-15. Native Vulgate numbers these verses 6:1-15 (mapping to Saints.Bible 5:27-41), and continues the chapter through 6:81 (mapping to Saints.Bible 6:1-66).
- **6:16** — In native Clementine Vulgate numbering, these verses stand at 6:16-81 (mapped to Saints.Bible 6:1-66, while native 6:1-15 map to Saints 5:27-41). Native Vulgate numbers these verses 6:16-81 (mapping to Saints.Bible 6:1-66).
- **6:28** — The Vulgate reads 'primogenitus Vasseni, et Abia' ('the firstborn Vashni, and Abijah'), following the Hebrew consonantal text where 'Vashni' is taken as the firstborn's name rather than 'Joel' with 'and the second, Abijah' (compare Joel son of Samuel in v. 18 and 1 Sam 8:2).
- **6:31** — The Vulgate reads 'super cantores' ('over the singers') and 'ex quo collocata est arca' ('from the time the ark was set in place') where the Hebrew has 'over the service of song' and 'after the ark had rest'; likewise, v. 17 has 'tabernaculo testimonii' ('the tabernacle of the testimony') for Hebrew 'the tabernacle of the tent of meeting.'
- **6:34** — The Vulgate has several distinct proper names here and in vv. 29-31: 'Thohu' ('Tohu', matching 1 Sam 1:1) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'Toah'; in v. 29 'Cusi' ('Cushi', related to 'Kushaiah' in 1 Chr 15:17) for Hebrew 'Kishi'; and in v. 31 'Amasai' for Hebrew 'Amzi.'
- **6:48** — The Vulgate reads 'domus Domini' ('the house of the Lord') where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'the house of God,' and links the Levites by the relative clause 'qui ordinati sunt' ('who were assigned').
- **6:49** — The Vulgate reads 'adolebant incensum' ('burned incense') for both altars and 'ut precarentur pro Israel' ('to pray for Israel') where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'made offerings' and 'to make atonement for Israel.'
- **6:55** — Here and throughout vv. 40-66, the Vulgate renders the open lands surrounding Levitical cities as 'suburbana' ('outskirts') where the Hebrew has 'pasturelands.'
- **6:58** — The Vulgate reads 'Helon' in both native 6:58 (Saints.Bible 6:43, where Hebrew reads Hilen and current English reads 'Hilen') and native 6:69 (Saints.Bible 6:54, where Hebrew reads Aijalon and current English reads 'Helon').
- **6:61** — The Vulgate reads 'de cognatione sua dederunt ... in possessionem' ('from their clan, they gave ... as a possession') where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'from the family of the tribe ... by lot.'
- **6:65** — The Vulgate reads 'quas vocaverunt nominibus suis' ('which they called by their names') where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'which are mentioned by name.'
