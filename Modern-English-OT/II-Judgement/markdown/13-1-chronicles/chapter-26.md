## Text

**26:1** Now the divisions of the gatekeepers: of the Korahites, Meshelemiah, the son of Kore, of the sons of Asaph.

**26:2** The sons of Meshelemiah: Zechariah the firstborn, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, Jathniel the fourth,

**26:3** Elam the fifth, Jehohanan the sixth, Elioenai the seventh.

**26:4** And the sons of Obed-edom: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, Sacar the fourth, Nethanel the fifth,

**26:5** Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, Peullethai the eighth; because the Lord blessed him.

**26:6** Also to his son Shemaiah sons were born, rulers of their families, for they were very strong men.

**26:7** Therefore the sons of Shemaiah: Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad; his brothers, very strong men: Elihu and Semachiah also.

**26:8** All these were of the sons of Obed-edom: they and their sons and their brothers, very strong for service, sixty-two of Obed-edom.

**26:9** And the sons of Meshelemiah and their brothers, very vigorous men, were eighteen.

**26:10** Now of Hosah, that is, of the sons of Merari: Shimri was chief (for he had not had a firstborn, and therefore his father had appointed him as chief),

**26:11** Hilkiah the second, Tebaliah the third, Zechariah the fourth; all these sons and brothers of Hosah were thirteen.

**26:12** These were divided as gatekeepers, so that the chiefs of the watches, just as their brothers also, might always serve in the house of the Lord.

**26:13** Therefore lots were cast equally, for both the small and the great, according to their families, for each of the gates.

**26:14** So the eastern lot fell to Shelemiah. And to his son Zechariah, a very prudent and learned man, the northern quarter fell by lot.

**26:15** And to Obed-edom and his sons, to the south, in which part of the house was the council of the elders.

**26:16** To Shuppim and Hosah, to the west, beside the gate that leads to the road of ascent, watch opposite watch.

**26:17** To the east were six Levites; to the north, four each day; to the south likewise, four a day; and where the council was, two and two.

**26:18** Also in the chambers of the gatekeepers to the west, four on the road, and two at the chambers.

**26:19** These are the divisions of the gatekeepers of the sons of Korah and Merari.

**26:20** Now Ahijah was over the treasuries of the house of God and the vessels of the holy things.

**26:21** The sons of Ladan, the sons of the Gershonite: of Ladan, the chiefs of the families of Ladan and of the Gershonite: Jehieli.

**26:22** The sons of Jehieli: Zetham and Joel his brothers, over the treasuries of the house of the Lord.

**26:23** For the Amramites, the Izharites, the Hebronites, and the Uzzielites.

**26:24** Now Shubael, the son of Gershom, the son of Moses, was set over the treasuries.

**26:25** Also his brothers: Eliezer, whose son was Rehabiah, and his son Jeshaiah, and his son Joram, and his son also Zichri, and his son Shelomith.

**26:26** This Shelomith and his brothers were over the treasuries of the holy things that King David, the chiefs of the families, the tribunes, the centurions, and the commanders of the army sanctified,

**26:27** from the wars and the spoils of the battles, which they had consecrated for the restoration and furnishing of the temple of the Lord.

**26:28** And all these things Samuel the seer, Saul the son of Kish, Abner the son of Ner, and Joab the son of Zeruiah sanctified: all who had sanctified them by the hand of Shelomith and his brothers.

**26:29** Now over the Izharites were Chenaniah and his sons, for the outside works over Israel, to teach and to judge them.

**26:30** And of the Hebronites, Hashabiah and his brothers, very strong men, one thousand seven hundred, were over Israel beyond the Jordan toward the west, in all the works of the Lord and for the service of the king.

**26:31** Now Jeriah was chief of the Hebronites according to their families and clans. In the fortieth year of the reign of David they were numbered, and very strong men were found in Jazer of Gilead,

**26:32** and his brothers of vigorous age, two thousand seven hundred chiefs of families. And King David set them over the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, for every service of God and of the king.

## Translator Notes

- **26:6** — The Clementine Vulgate spells the name 'Semei' (Shimei) here against 'Semeias' (Shemaiah) in 26:4 and 'Semeiæ' in 26:7.
- **26:10** — Distinctively Vulgate: Latin 'non enim habuerat primogenitum' reads 'for he had not had a firstborn' (referring to Hosah), whereas the Hebrew states of Shimri that 'he was not the firstborn.'
- **26:15** — Distinctively Vulgate: Latin 'seniorum concilium' ('the council of the elders'; likewise 'concilium' in 26:17) interprets the Hebrew 'beit ha-asuppim' (usually understood as a storehouse) as a meeting place for the elders.
- **26:16** — The Vulgate omits the Hebrew proper name of the gate (Shallecheth), rendering the clause simply as 'beside the gate that leads to the road of ascent.' Similarly, in 26:18, Latin 'cellulis janitorum' ('chambers of the gatekeepers') translates the obscure Hebrew architectural term 'Parbar.'
- **26:20** — The Vulgate retains the personal name Ahijah ('Achias', agreeing with the Hebrew against the Septuagint's 'their brothers') and distinctively reads 'vasa sanctorum' ('vessels of the holy things') where the Hebrew has 'treasuries of the dedicated things.'
- **26:29** — Distinctively Vulgate: Latin 'Isaaritis ... praeerat' places Chenaniah and his sons 'over the Izharites' (rather than merely 'of the Izharites'), and 'ad docendum et judicandum eos' ('to teach and to judge them') translates the Hebrew nouns 'as officers and judges.'
