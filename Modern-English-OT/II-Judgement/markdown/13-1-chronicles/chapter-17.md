## Text

**17:1** Now when David was living in his house, he said to Nathan the prophet, "Look, I live in a cedar house, while the ark of the covenant of the Lord is under skins."

**17:2** And Nathan said to David, "Do everything that is in your heart, for God is with you."

**17:3** So on that night the word of God came to Nathan, saying,

**17:4** "Go and speak to David My servant: 'This is what the Lord says: You will not build a house for Me to live in.

**17:5** For I have not remained in a house from the time when I brought Israel out until this day, but I have always been changing the places of the tabernacle, and in a tent

**17:6** remaining with all Israel. Have I spoken to even one of the judges of Israel, whom I had commanded to shepherd My people, and said, "Why have you not built a cedar house for Me?"'

**17:7** Now therefore, you will speak in this way to My servant David: 'This is what the Lord of hosts says: I took you when you were following the flock in the pastures, so that you might be leader of My people Israel,

**17:8** and I have been with you wherever you went, and I have killed all your enemies before you, and I have made for you a name like that of one of the great ones who are celebrated on the earth.

**17:9** And I have given a place to My people Israel: it will be planted and will live in it, and will no longer be moved; nor will the sons of iniquity wear them down, as from the beginning,

**17:10** from the days in which I gave judges to My people Israel; and I have humbled all your enemies. Therefore I announce to you that the Lord is going to build a house for you.

**17:11** And when you have fulfilled your days to go to your fathers, I will raise up your seed after you, who will be from your sons, and I will establish his kingdom.

**17:12** He will build a house for Me, and I will make his throne firm forever.

**17:13** I will be a father to him, and he will be a son to Me; and I will not take away My mercy from him, as I took it away from the one who was before you.

**17:14** And I will set him in My house and in My kingdom forever, and his throne will be most firm for all time.'"

**17:15** According to all these words and according to this entire vision, so Nathan spoke to David.

**17:16** And when King David had come and sat before the Lord, he said, "Who am I, Lord God, and what is my house, that You would grant me such things?

**17:17** Yet even this seemed small in Your sight, and therefore You have spoken concerning the house of Your servant even for the future; and You have made me remarkable above all men, Lord God.

**17:18** What more can David add, since You have so glorified Your servant and known him?

**17:19** Lord, for the sake of Your servant, according to Your heart, You have done all this greatness, and You have willed all great deeds to be known.

**17:20** Lord, there is no one like You, and there is no other god besides You, out of all whom we have heard of with our ears.

**17:21** For what other is there like Your people Israel, one nation on the earth, to which God went to free and make a people for Himself, and by His greatness and terrors to drive out nations from before the one whom He had freed from Egypt?

**17:22** And You have set Your people Israel as a people for Yourself forever, and You, Lord, have become its God.

**17:23** Now therefore, Lord, let the word that You have spoken to Your servant and concerning his house be confirmed forever, and do as You have spoken.

**17:24** And let Your name remain and be magnified forever, and let it be said, 'The Lord of hosts is the God of Israel, and the house of David His servant remains before Him.'

**17:25** For You, Lord my God, have uncovered the ear of Your servant that You would build a house for him, and for that reason Your servant has found confidence to pray before You.

**17:26** Now therefore, Lord, You are God, and You have spoken such great benefits to Your servant.

**17:27** And You have begun to bless the house of Your servant, so that it may always be before You; for with You blessing it, Lord, it will be blessed forever."

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — The Vulgate reads sub pellibus ("under skins"), referring to the animal-skin coverings of the tabernacle, where the Hebrew has "under curtains."
- **17:5** — The Vulgate renders this sentence distinctively as "changing the places of the tabernacle, and in a tent remaining with all Israel" (spanning vv. 5-6), whereas the Hebrew speaks of moving "from tent to tent and from dwelling to dwelling."
- **17:16** — The Vulgate reads ut praestares mihi talia ("that You would grant me such things"), whereas the Hebrew has "that You have brought me this far."
- **17:17** — The Vulgate renders a difficult Hebrew clause ("according to the rank of a man of high degree") as fecisti me spectabilem super omnes homines ("You have made me remarkable above all men").
- **17:27** — The Vulgate reads coepisti ("You have begun"), whereas the Hebrew verb means "You have been pleased."
