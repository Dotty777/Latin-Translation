## Text

**16:1** So they brought the ark of God and set it in the middle of the tent that David had pitched for it, and they offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before God.

**16:2** And when David had finished offering the burnt offerings and peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the Lord.

**16:3** And he distributed to all individually, from man to woman, a loaf of bread, a portion of roasted beef, and fine flour fried in oil.

**16:4** And he appointed some of the Levites before the ark of the Lord to minister, and to remember His works, and to glorify and praise the Lord God of Israel:

**16:5** Asaph as chief, and second to him Zechariah; furthermore, Jahiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, and Obed-edom; Jehiel over the instruments of the psaltery and lyres; and Asaph to sound aloud with cymbals;

**16:6** and Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests to blow the trumpet continually before the ark of the covenant of the Lord.

**16:7** On that day David made Asaph and his brothers chief to give thanks to the Lord:

**16:8** "Give thanks to the Lord and call upon His name; make His deeds known among the peoples.

**16:9** Sing to Him and sing psalms to Him, and recount all His wonders.

**16:10** Praise His holy name; let the heart of those who seek the Lord rejoice.

**16:11** Seek the Lord and His power; seek His face always.

**16:12** Remember His wonders that He has done, His signs, and the judgments of His mouth,

**16:13** offspring of Israel, His servant, sons of Jacob, His chosen ones.

**16:14** He is the Lord our God; His judgments are in all the earth.

**16:15** Remember His covenant forever, the word that He commanded for a thousand generations,

**16:16** which He made with Abraham, and His oath with Isaac.

**16:17** And He established it for Jacob as a statute, and for Israel as an everlasting covenant,

**16:18** saying, 'To you I will give the land of Canaan, the cord of your inheritance,'

**16:19** when they were few in number, small and settlers in it.

**16:20** And they passed from nation to nation, and from a kingdom to another people.

**16:21** He did not allow anyone to oppress them, but He rebuked kings for their sake:

**16:22** 'Do not touch My anointed ones, and do no evil against My prophets.'

**16:23** Sing to the Lord, all the earth; proclaim His salvation from day to day.

**16:25** For the Lord is great and exceedingly worthy of praise, and fearsome above all gods.

**16:26** For all the gods of the peoples are idols, but the Lord made the heavens.

**16:27** Thanksgiving and magnificence are before Him; strength and joy are in His place.

**16:28** Bring to the Lord, families of the peoples; bring to the Lord glory and dominion.

**16:29** Give glory to the Lord; lift up a sacrifice to His name, and come into His presence, and worship the Lord in holy beauty.

**16:30** Let all the earth be shaken before His face, for He Himself founded the world immovable.

**16:31** Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth exult, and let them say among the nations, 'The Lord has reigned.'

**16:32** Let the sea thunder and its fullness; let the fields exult and all things that are in them.

**16:33** Then the trees of the forest will give praise before the Lord, for He is coming to judge the earth.

**16:34** Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy is forever.

**16:35** And say, 'Save us, God our Savior, and gather us and rescue us from the nations, so that we may give thanks to Your holy name and exult in Your songs.'

**16:36** Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, from eternity to eternity. And let all the people say, 'Amen,' and a hymn to the Lord."

**16:37** So he left Asaph and his brothers there before the ark of the covenant of the Lord to minister in the presence of the ark continually, each day and in their turns.

**16:38** Moreover, he appointed Obed-edom and his sixty-eight brothers, along with Obed-edom the son of Jeduthun and Hosah, as doorkeepers;

**16:39** and Zadok the priest and his brothers the priests before the tabernacle of the Lord at the high place that was in Gibeon,

**16:40** to offer burnt offerings to the Lord on the altar of burnt offering continually, morning and evening, according to all that is written in the law of the Lord, which He commanded Israel.

**16:41** And after him, Heman and Jeduthun, and the rest of the chosen ones, each one by his name, to give thanks to the Lord, for His mercy is forever.

**16:42** Heman and Jeduthun also were blowing the trumpet, and striking cymbals and all musical instruments to sing to God; and he made the sons of Jeduthun gatekeepers.

**16:43** And all the people returned to their own home, and David, so that he might bless his own household as well.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**16:24** *(after `16:23`)* Declare His glory among the nations, His wonders among all peoples.

## Translator Notes

- **16:3** — Vulgate partem assae carnis bubalae, et frixam oleo similam ("a portion of roasted beef, and fine flour fried in oil") translates two rare Hebrew food terms probably meaning "a portion of meat" (or "date cake") and "a raisin cake."
- **16:27** — Vulgate confessio ("thanksgiving," reflecting Septuagint exomologesis) denotes public praise and thanksgiving before God rather than admission of guilt, where the Hebrew text reads "splendor."
- **16:29** — Unlike the Hebrew text ("Give to the Lord the glory due His name; bring an offering"), the Clementine Vulgate construes dative nomini ejus ("to His name") with the following clause, nomini ejus levate sacrificium ("lift up a sacrifice to His name").
- **16:36** — Vulgate Et dicat omnis populo: Amen, et hymnum Domino ("And let all the people say, 'Amen,' and a hymn to the Lord") uses a jussive subjunctive (dicat) rather than the Hebrew past narrative ("and all the people said... and praised the Lord"), and the text here reads omnis populo where omnis populus would be expected.
