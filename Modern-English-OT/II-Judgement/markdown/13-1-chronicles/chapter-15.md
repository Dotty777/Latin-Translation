## Text

**15:1** He also made houses for himself in the city of David; and he built a place for the ark of God, and pitched a tent for it.

**15:2** Then David said, "It is unlawful for the ark of God to be carried by anyone except the Levites, whom the Lord chose to carry it and to minister to Him forever."

**15:3** And he gathered all Israel to Jerusalem so that the ark of God might be brought into its place, which he had prepared for it;

**15:4** and likewise the sons of Aaron and the Levites.

**15:5** Of the sons of Kohath, Uriel was the chief, and his brothers, one hundred twenty.

**15:6** Of the sons of Merari, Asaiah the chief, and his brothers, two hundred twenty.

**15:7** Of the sons of Gershom, Joel the chief, and his brothers, one hundred thirty.

**15:8** Of the sons of Elizaphan, Shemaiah the chief, and his brothers, two hundred.

**15:9** Of the sons of Hebron, Eliel the chief, and his brothers, eighty.

**15:10** Of the sons of Uzziel, Amminadab the chief, and his brothers, one hundred twelve.

**15:11** And David called Zadok and Abiathar the priests, and the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab,

**15:12** and said to them, "You who are the chiefs of the Levitical families, sanctify yourselves with your brothers and bring the ark of the Lord God of Israel to the place that has been prepared for it,

**15:13** so that, just as at the beginning the Lord struck us because you were not present, it may not happen the same way now as well by our doing something unlawful."

**15:14** Therefore the priests and the Levites sanctified themselves to carry the ark of the Lord God of Israel.

**15:15** And the sons of Levi carried the ark of God, just as Moses had commanded according to the word of the Lord, on their shoulders with poles.

**15:16** And David told the chiefs of the Levites to appoint singers from their brothers with musical instruments, namely harps, lyres, and cymbals, so that the sound of joy might resound on high.

**15:17** And they appointed the Levites: Heman the son of Joel, and of his brothers, Asaph the son of Berechiah; and of the sons of Merari, their brothers, Ethan the son of Kushaiah.

**15:18** And with them their brothers in the second rank: Zechariah, Ben, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jahiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, and Jehiel, the doorkeepers.

**15:19** Now the singers Heman, Asaph, and Ethan were sounding loudly on bronze cymbals.

**15:20** And Zechariah, Uzziel, Shemiramoth, Jahiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah were singing mysteries on harps.

**15:21** Moreover, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, Jehiel, and Azaziah were singing a song of victory on lyres for the octave.

**15:22** And Chenaniah, chief of the Levites, was in charge of prophecy, to lead the melody, for he was very wise.

**15:23** And Berechiah and Elkanah were doorkeepers of the ark.

**15:24** Moreover, Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer, the priests, were sounding trumpets before the ark of God; and Obed-edom and Jehiah were doorkeepers of the ark.

**15:25** So David, and all the elders of Israel, and the tribunes went to bring the ark of the covenant of the Lord from the house of Obed-edom with joy.

**15:26** And when God had helped the Levites who were carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord, seven bulls and seven rams were sacrificed.

**15:27** Now David was clothed in a robe of fine linen, as were all the Levites who were carrying the ark, and the singers, and Chenaniah, chief of prophecy among the singers; and David was also clothed in a linen ephod.

**15:28** And all Israel was escorting the ark of the covenant of the Lord with shouting and the sound of the horn, sounding loudly with trumpets, cymbals, harps, and lyres.

**15:29** And when the ark of the covenant of the Lord had reached the city of David, Michal the daughter of Saul, looking out through a window, saw King David dancing and playing, and she despised him in her heart.

## Translator Notes

- **15:13** — Vulgate illicitum quid nobis agentibus ("by our doing something unlawful") echoes illicitum est in 15:2 and renders a Hebrew clause meaning "because we did not seek Him according to the ordinance."
- **15:16** — Vulgate in excelsis ("on high") renders the Hebrew infinitive for "raising [the voice]" as a locative expression meaning "in the heights."
- **15:20** — Vulgate arcana ("mysteries") translates the Hebrew musical direction al-alamot ("according to Alamoth") from the root meaning "to be hidden."
- **15:21** — Vulgate pro octava ("for the octave," literally "for the eighth") and the Greek loanword epinicion ("a song of victory") translate the Hebrew musical terms al-hasheminit ("on the Sheminith") and lenatseah ("to lead").
- **15:22** — Vulgate prophetiae ("prophecy," also in 15:27) renders Hebrew massa ("lifting up" or "musical delivery"), which elsewhere denotes a prophetic oracle.
