## Text

**7:1** Now the sons of Issachar: Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron, four.

**7:2** The sons of Tola: Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Ibsam, and Samuel, chiefs by the houses of their clans. From the lineage of Tola, very valiant men were numbered in the days of David: twenty-two thousand six hundred.

**7:3** The sons of Uzzi: Izrahiah, from whom were born Michael, Obadiah, Joel, and Isshiah, five, all of them chiefs.

**7:4** And with them, by their families and peoples, equipped for battle, were very valiant men, thirty-six thousand; for they had many wives and sons.

**7:5** Their brothers also, throughout the entire clan of Issachar, very strong for fighting, were numbered: eighty-seven thousand.

**7:6** The sons of Benjamin: Bela, Becher, and Jediael, three.

**7:7** The sons of Bela: Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, and Iri, five chiefs of families and very strong for fighting; and their number was twenty-two thousand and thirty-four.

**7:8** Now the sons of Becher: Zemirah, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jerimoth, Abijah, Anathoth, and Alemeth; all these were the sons of Becher.

**7:9** And they were numbered by their families, chiefs of their clans, very valiant for wars: twenty thousand and two hundred.

**7:10** Now the sons of Jediael: Bilhan. And the sons of Bilhan: Jeush, Benjamin, Ehud, Chenaanah, Zethan, Tarshish, and Ahishahar.

**7:11** All these were the sons of Jediael, chiefs of their clans, very valiant men, seventeen thousand and two hundred going out to battle.

**7:12** Shuppim also and Huppim were the sons of Ir; and Hushim, the sons of Aher.

**7:13** And the sons of Naphtali: Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shallum, the sons of Bilhah.

**7:14** Now the son of Manasseh was Asriel; and his Syrian concubine bore Machir, the father of Gilead.

**7:15** And Machir took wives for his sons Huppim and Shuppim, and he had a sister named Maacah; and the name of the second was Zelophehad, and daughters were born to Zelophehad.

**7:16** And Maacah, the wife of Machir, bore a son, and she called his name Peresh; moreover, the name of his brother was Sheresh, and his sons were Ulam and Rakem.

**7:17** And the son of Ulam was Bedan. These are the sons of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh.

**7:18** And his sister Queen bore Handsome Man, Abiezer, and Mahlah.

**7:19** And the sons of Shemida were Ahian, Shechem, Likhi, and Aniam.

**7:20** And the sons of Ephraim: Shuthelah, Bered his son, Tahath his son, Eleadah his son, Tahath his son, his son Zabad,

**7:21** and his son Shuthelah, and his son Ezer and Elead; and the native men of Gath killed them, because they had gone down to seize their possessions.

**7:22** Therefore Ephraim their father mourned for many days, and his brothers came to comfort him.

**7:23** And he went in to his wife, and she conceived and bore a son, and he called his name Beriah, because he had been born in the misfortunes of his house.

**7:24** And his daughter was Sheerah, who built Lower and Upper Beth-horon, and Uzzen-sheerah.

**7:25** Now his son was Rephah, and Resheph, and Telah, from whom Tahan was born,

**7:26** who fathered Ladan; his son also was Ammihud, who fathered Elishama,

**7:27** from whom Nun was born, who had a son, Joshua.

**7:28** And their possession and dwelling were Bethel with its daughters, and toward the east Naaran, toward the western region Gezer and its daughters, Shechem also with its daughters, as far as Aza with its daughters.

**7:29** And alongside the sons of Manasseh were Beth-shean and its daughters, Taanach and its daughters, Megiddo and its daughters, and Dor and its daughters; in these the sons of Joseph, the son of Israel, lived.

**7:30** The sons of Asher: Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriah, and Serah their sister.

**7:31** And the sons of Beriah: Heber and Malchiel; he is the father of Birzaith.

**7:32** And Heber fathered Japhlet, Shomer, Hotham, and Shua their sister.

**7:33** The sons of Japhlet: Pasach, Chamaal, and Ashvath; these were the sons of Japhlet.

**7:34** Now the sons of Shomer: Ahi, Rohgah, Hubbah, and Aram.

**7:35** And the sons of Helem his brother: Zophah, Imna, Shelesh, and Amal.

**7:36** The sons of Zophah: Suah, Harnepher, Shual, Beri, Imrah,

**7:37** Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran, and Beera.

**7:38** The sons of Jether: Jephunneh, Pispah, and Ara.

**7:39** And the sons of Ulla: Arah, Hanniel, and Rizia.

**7:40** All these were the sons of Asher, chiefs of the clans, chosen and very valiant commanders of commanders; and the number of those of an age that was fit for war was twenty-six thousand.

## Translator Notes

- **7:15** — The Vulgate explicitly reads 'wives for his sons' (uxores filiis suis), identifying Huppim and Shuppim as sons of Machir, whereas the Hebrew reads 'a wife for Huppim and for Shuppim' without 'his sons.'
- **7:18** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew personal names Hammolecheth ('the queen') and Ishhod ('man of splendor') directly into Latin as Regina ('Queen') and Virum decorum ('Handsome Man').
- **7:21** — The Vulgate adds 'and his son' before Ezer and Elead and renders the Hebrew 'their livestock' as 'their possessions' (possessiones eorum).
- **7:28** — 'Daughters' is a literal rendering of the biblical idiom for dependent outlying villages surrounding a main town. The Vulgate reads 'Aza' (reflecting a Hebrew variant for Azzah/Gaza), whereas the standard Masoretic Hebrew text reads 'Ayyah.'
- **7:33** — The Vulgate reads 'Chamaal' (reflecting an initial Hebrew kaf), whereas the standard Hebrew text reads 'Bimhal' (initial bet).
