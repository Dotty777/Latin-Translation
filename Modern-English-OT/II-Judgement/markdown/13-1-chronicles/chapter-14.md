## Text

**14:1** Hiram king of Tyre also sent messengers to David, and cedar timbers, and craftsmen of walls and of wood, to build him a house.

**14:2** And David knew that the Lord had confirmed him as king over Israel, and that his kingdom had been lifted up over His people Israel.

**14:3** David also took other wives in Jerusalem, and fathered sons and daughters.

**14:4** And these are the names of those who were born to him in Jerusalem: Shammua, and Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon,

**14:5** Ibhar, and Elishua, and Eliphelet,

**14:6** Nogah also, and Nepheg, and Japhia,

**14:7** Elishama, and Beeliada, and Eliphelet.

**14:8** Now the Philistines, hearing that David had been anointed king over all Israel, all went up to seek him; and when David had heard this, he went out to meet them.

**14:9** Moreover, the Philistines came and spread out in the Valley of Rephaim.

**14:10** And David consulted the Lord, saying, "Shall I go up against the Philistines, and will You deliver them into my hand?" And the Lord said to him, "Go up, and I will deliver them into your hand."

**14:11** And when they had gone up to Baal-perazim, David struck them there and said, "God has divided my enemies by my hand, just as waters are divided." And for that reason the name of that place was called Baal-perazim.

**14:12** And they abandoned their gods there, which David ordered to be burned up.

**14:13** On another occasion also, the Philistines rushed in and spread out in the valley.

**14:14** And David consulted God again, and God said to him, "Do not go up after them; withdraw from them, and you will come against them opposite the pear trees.

**14:15** And when you hear the sound of one marching in the tops of the pear trees, then you will go out to war, for God has gone out before you to strike the camp of the Philistines."

**14:16** Therefore David did just as God had commanded him, and he struck the camp of the Philistines from Gibeon as far as Gezer.

**14:17** And the name of David was spread abroad in all the regions, and the Lord put the dread of him upon all the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **14:2** — The Vulgate reads 'over His people Israel' (super populum ejus Israel), whereas the Hebrew reads 'for the sake of His people Israel.'
- **14:5** — The Vulgate spells both 14:5 and 14:7 identically as Eliphalet (Eliphelet), whereas the Hebrew distinguishes Elpelet in verse 5 from Eliphelet in verse 7.
- **14:14** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate renders the Hebrew baca-trees (often translated balsam or mulberry trees) as 'pear trees' (pyrorum) here and in verse 15.
