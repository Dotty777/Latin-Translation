## Text

**3:1** Now David had these sons, who were born to him in Hebron: the firstborn, Amnon, by Ahinoam the Jezreelitess; the second, Daniel, by Abigail the Carmelitess;

**3:2** the third, Absalom, the son of Maacah the daughter of Talmai king of Geshur; the fourth, Adonijah, the son of Haggith;

**3:3** the fifth, Shephatiah, by Abital; the sixth, Ithream, by Eglah his wife.

**3:4** Six, therefore, were born to him in Hebron, where he reigned for seven years and six months. And he reigned for thirty-three years in Jerusalem.

**3:5** Moreover, in Jerusalem sons were born to him: Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon, four by Bathsheba the daughter of Ammiel;

**3:6** Ibhar also, and Elishama,

**3:7** and Eliphelet, Nogah, Nepheg, and Japhia,

**3:8** as well as Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet, nine.

**3:9** All these were sons of David, besides the sons of the concubines; and they had a sister, Tamar.

**3:10** Now the son of Solomon was Rehoboam, whose son Abijah fathered Asa. From him also was born Jehoshaphat,

**3:11** the father of Joram; this Joram fathered Ahaziah, from whom Joash sprang;

**3:12** and his son Amaziah fathered Azariah. Moreover, Azariah's son Jotham

**3:13** fathered Ahaz, the father of Hezekiah, from whom Manasseh was born.

**3:14** And Manasseh also fathered Amon, the father of Josiah.

**3:15** Now the sons of Josiah were: the firstborn, Johanan; the second, Jehoiakim; the third, Zedekiah; the fourth, Shallum.

**3:16** From Jehoiakim were born Jeconiah and Zedekiah.

**3:17** The sons of Jeconiah were Assir, Shealtiel,

**3:18** Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Sama, and Nedabiah.

**3:19** From Pedaiah sprang Zerubbabel and Shimei. Zerubbabel fathered Meshullam, Hananiah, and Shelomith their sister;

**3:20** also Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Jushab-hesed, five.

**3:21** Now the son of Hananiah was Pelatiah, the father of Jeshaiah, whose son was Rephaiah; his son also was Arnan, from whom Obadiah was born, whose son was Shecaniah.

**3:22** The son of Shecaniah was Shemaiah, whose sons were Hattush, Igal, Bariah, Neariah, and Shaphat, six in number.

**3:23** The son of Neariah: Elioenai, Hezekiah, and Azrikam, three.

**3:24** The sons of Elioenai: Hodaviah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Delaiah, and Anani, seven.

## Translator Notes

- **3:5** — Vulgate reads 'Bathsheba' (Latin Bethsabee, as in 2 Samuel 11:3), whereas the Hebrew text of 1 Chronicles 3:5 reads 'Bath-shua.'
- **3:17** — Vulgate treats Asir ('Assir') as the proper name of Jeconiah's first son, whereas many translations of the Hebrew take it as an epithet ('Jeconiah the captive').
- **3:18** — Vulgate reads 'Sama,' corresponding to 'Hoshama' in the Hebrew text.
- **3:21** — Vulgate presents the names after Pelatiah as a continuous father-to-son lineage, whereas the Hebrew text lists separate collateral families ('the sons of Rephaiah, the sons of Arnan...').
- **3:23** — Vulgate uses the singular 'son' (Filius) even though three names are listed and counted.
