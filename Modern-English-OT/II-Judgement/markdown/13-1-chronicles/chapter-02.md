## Text

**2:1** Now the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun,

**2:2** Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.

**2:3** The sons of Judah: Er, Onan, and Shelah; these three were born to him by the daughter of Shua, a Canaanite woman. Now Er, the firstborn of Judah, was evil before the Lord, and He put him to death.

**2:4** And Tamar, his daughter-in-law, bore him Perez and Zerah. So all the sons of Judah were five.

**2:5** Now the sons of Perez: Hezron and Hamul.

**2:6** The sons of Zerah also: Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calcol as well, and Dara, five in all.

**2:7** The sons of Carmi: Achar, who troubled Israel and sinned in the theft of the devoted thing.

**2:8** The sons of Ethan: Azariah.

**2:9** Now the sons of Hezron who were born to him: Jerahmeel, Ram, and Chelubai.

**2:10** And Ram fathered Amminadab. Now Amminadab fathered Nahshon, leader of the sons of Judah.

**2:11** Nahshon also fathered Salma, from whom Boaz was born.

**2:12** And Boaz fathered Obed, who himself also fathered Jesse.

**2:13** Now Jesse fathered the firstborn Eliab, the second Abinadab, the third Shimea,

**2:14** the fourth Nethanel, the fifth Raddai,

**2:15** the sixth Ozem, the seventh David.

**2:16** Their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. The sons of Zeruiah: Abishai, Joab, and Asahel, three.

**2:17** And Abigail bore Amasa, whose father was Jether the Ishmaelite.

**2:18** Now Caleb, the son of Hezron, took a wife named Azubah, by whom he fathered Jerioth; and her sons were Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon.

**2:19** And when Azubah had died, Caleb took Ephrathah as a wife, who bore him Hur.

**2:20** And Hur fathered Uri, and Uri fathered Bezalel.

**2:21** After these things Hezron went in to the daughter of Machir, the father of Gilead, and he took her when he was sixty years old; and she bore him Segub.

**2:22** And Segub also fathered Jair, and he possessed twenty-three cities in the land of Gilead.

**2:23** And Geshur and Aram took the towns of Jair, and Kenath and its villages, sixty cities; all these were the sons of Machir, the father of Gilead.

**2:24** Now when Hezron had died, Caleb went in to Ephrathah. Hezron also had a wife Abijah, who bore him Ashhur, the father of Tekoa.

**2:25** Now the sons born to Jerahmeel, the firstborn of Hezron, were Ram his firstborn, Bunah, Aram, Ozem, and Ahijah.

**2:26** Jerahmeel also married another wife, named Atarah, who was the mother of Onam.

**2:27** Moreover, the sons of Ram, the firstborn of Jerahmeel, were Maaz, Jamin, and Eker.

**2:28** Now Onam had sons, Shammai and Jada. And the sons of Shammai: Nadab and Abishur.

**2:29** And the name of Abishur's wife was Abihail, who bore him Ahban and Molid.

**2:30** And the sons of Nadab were Seled and Appaim. But Seled died without children.

**2:31** And the son of Appaim was Ishi, and this Ishi fathered Sheshan. And Sheshan fathered Ahlai.

**2:32** And the sons of Jada, the brother of Shammai: Jether and Jonathan. And Jether also died without children.

**2:33** And Jonathan fathered Peleth and Zaza. These were the sons of Jerahmeel.

**2:34** Now Sheshan had no sons, but daughters; and he had an Egyptian servant named Jarha.

**2:35** And he gave him his daughter as a wife, and she bore him Attai.

**2:36** And Attai fathered Nathan, and Nathan fathered Zabad.

**2:37** Zabad also fathered Ephlal, and Ephlal fathered Obed.

**2:38** Obed fathered Jehu, Jehu fathered Azariah,

**2:39** Azariah fathered Helez, and Helez fathered Eleasah.

**2:40** Eleasah fathered Sismai, Sismai fathered Shallum,

**2:41** Shallum fathered Jekamiah, and Jekamiah fathered Elishama.

**2:42** Now the sons of Caleb, the brother of Jerahmeel: Mesha his firstborn; he is the father of Ziph; and the sons of Mareshah, the father of Hebron.

**2:43** And the sons of Hebron: Korah, Tappuah, Rekem, and Shema.

**2:44** And Shema fathered Raham, the father of Jorkeam, and Rekem fathered Shammai.

**2:45** The son of Shammai was Maon, and Maon was the father of Beth-zur.

**2:46** Now Ephah, Caleb's concubine, bore Haran, Moza, and Gazez. And Haran fathered Gazez.

**2:47** And the sons of Jahdai: Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah, and Shaaph.

**2:48** Caleb's concubine Maacah bore Sheber and Tirhanah.

**2:49** And Shaaph, the father of Madmannah, fathered Sheva, the father of Machbena and the father of Gibea. And the daughter of Caleb was Achsah.

**2:50** These were the sons of Caleb, the son of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah: Shobal, the father of Kiriath-jearim;

**2:51** Salma, the father of Bethlehem; Hareph, the father of Beth-gader.

**2:52** Now there were sons of Shobal, the father of Kiriath-jearim, who saw half of the resting places.

**2:53** And from the family of Kiriath-jearim: the Ithrites, the Puthites, the Shumathites, and the Mishraites. From these came out the Zorathites and the Eshtaolites.

**2:54** The sons of Salma: Bethlehem, the Netophathites, the crowns of the house of Joab, and half of the resting place of the Zorites;

**2:55** also the families of the scribes living in Jabez, singing and resounding, and dwelling in tents. These are the Kenites, who came from the heat of the father of the house of Rechab.

## Translator Notes

- **2:18** — Vulgate reads "took a wife named Azubah, by whom he fathered Jerioth," presenting Jerioth as a child of Caleb and Azubah rather than a second wife as in the Hebrew text.
- **2:24** — Vulgate reads "when Hezron had died, Caleb went in to Ephrathah," taking Caleb-ephrathah (a place name in the Hebrew text) as Caleb taking Ephrathah as his wife.
- **2:25** — Vulgate preserves the proper name Aram, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads Oren.
- **2:49** — Vulgate makes Shaaph, the father of Madmannah, the subject who fathered Sheva, whereas the Hebrew text lists both Shaaph and Sheva as sons borne by Maacah.
- **2:52** — Vulgate translates the Hebrew names Haroeh and half of the Menuhoth literally as "who saw half of the resting places" (Latin qui videbat dimidium requietionum).
- **2:54** — Vulgate renders the Hebrew names Atroth-beth-joab and half of the Manahathites, the Zorites as "the crowns of the house of Joab, and half of the resting place of the Zorites" (Latin coronae domus Joab, et dimidium requietionis Sarai).
- **2:55** — Vulgate translates the Hebrew clan names Tirathites, Shimeathites, and Sucathites etymologically as "singing and resounding, and dwelling in tents," and renders Hebrew "from Hammath, the father of the house of Rechab" as "from the heat of the father of the house of Rechab" (Latin de Calore patris domus Rechab, translating Hammath as "heat" with genitive patris).
