## Text

**28:1** David therefore summoned all the leaders of Israel, the chiefs of the tribes, and the overseers of the contingents who served the king, also the tribunes and centurions, and those who were in charge of the property and possessions of the king, and his sons with the eunuchs, and the mighty men and all the strongest in the army of Jerusalem.

**28:2** And when the king had risen and stood up, he said, "Listen to me, my brothers and my people: I planned to build a house in which the ark of the covenant of the Lord and the footstool of our God might rest, and I prepared everything for building.

**28:3** But God said to me, 'You will not build a house for My name, since you are a fighting man and have shed blood.'

**28:4** Yet the Lord God of Israel chose me from the entire house of my father to be king over Israel forever; for from Judah He chose the leaders, and from the house of Judah, the house of my father, and among the sons of my father it pleased Him to choose me as king over all Israel.

**28:5** And also from my sons (for the Lord has given me many sons) He chose my son Solomon to sit upon the throne of the kingdom of the Lord over Israel,

**28:6** and He said to me, 'Your son Solomon will build My house and My courts; for I have chosen him for Myself as a son, and I will be a father to him.

**28:7** And I will establish his kingdom forever, if he perseveres in doing My precepts and judgments, just as today.'

**28:8** Now therefore, before the entire assembly of Israel, with our God listening, keep and seek out all the commandments of the Lord our God, so that you may possess the good land and leave it to your sons after you forever.

**28:9** And you, my son Solomon, know the God of your father and serve Him with a complete heart and a willing spirit; for the Lord examines all hearts and understands all the thoughts of minds. If you seek Him, you will find Him; but if you abandon Him, He will cast you away forever.

**28:10** Now therefore, since the Lord has chosen you to build the house of the sanctuary, be strong and accomplish it."

**28:11** Then David gave to his son Solomon the plan of the portico, and of the temple, and of the storerooms, and of the upper room, and of the chambers in the inner recesses, and of the house of propitiation,

**28:12** and also of all that he had planned, of the courts and of the side chambers all around, for the treasuries of the house of the Lord and for the treasuries of the holy things,

**28:13** and of the priestly and Levitical divisions, for all the works of the house of the Lord and for all the vessels of the service of the temple of the Lord.

**28:14** Gold by weight for each vessel of service. Also the weight of silver according to the variety of the vessels and works.

**28:15** And also for the golden lampstands and for their lamps, gold according to the measure of each lampstand and the lamps. Likewise also for the silver lampstands and for their lamps, he delivered the weight of silver according to the difference in measure.

**28:16** He also gave gold for the tables of presentation according to the variety of the tables; likewise also silver for the other silver tables.

**28:17** Also for the forks, and the bowls, and the censers of purest gold, and for the little golden lions, he apportioned the weight according to the kind of measure for each little lion. Likewise also for the silver lions he set aside a different weight of silver.

**28:18** And for the altar on which incense is burned, he gave purest gold, so that from it might be made the likeness of the chariot of the cherubim stretching out their wings and veiling the ark of the covenant of the Lord.

**28:19** "All things," he said, "came to me written by the hand of the Lord, so that I might understand all the works of the pattern."

**28:20** David also said to his son Solomon, "Act courageously, and be strong, and do it; do not fear and do not be terrified, for the Lord my God will be with you, and He will not leave you nor abandon you until you accomplish all the work of the service of the house of the Lord.

**28:21** Here are the divisions of the priests and Levites: for every service of the house of the Lord they stand by you and are ready, and both the leaders and the people know how to carry out all your commands."

## Translator Notes

- **28:1** — Unlike the Hebrew, which places "to Jerusalem" at the beginning of the verse and links the king's sons to the royal estates, the Vulgate makes "his sons with the eunuchs" direct objects of David's summons and places "in the army of Jerusalem" at the end.
- **28:9** — Where the Hebrew has "every intent of the thoughts" and passive "He will be found by you," the Vulgate reads "all the thoughts of minds" (universas mentium cogitationes) and active "you will find Him" (invenies).
- **28:12** — The Vulgate reads simply "all that he had planned" (omnium quae cogitaverat), omitting the Hebrew phrase "by the Spirit."
- **28:17** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew keforim ("bowls") as kefirim ("young lions"), yielding "little golden lions" (leunculos aureos) and "silver lions" (leones argenteos), and translates Hebrew qasoth ("pitchers") as "censers" (thuribula).
- **28:21** — The Vulgate reads "house of the Lord" (Hebrew "house of God") and condenses the closing clauses to "they stand by you and are ready, and both the leaders and the people know how to carry out all your commands."
