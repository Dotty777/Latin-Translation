## Text

**18:1** The priests and the Levites, and all who are of the same tribe, shall have no portion and inheritance with the rest of Israel, because they shall eat the sacrifices of the Lord and His offerings,

**18:2** and they shall receive nothing else from the possession of their brothers; for the Lord Himself is their inheritance, just as He spoke to them.

**18:3** This shall be the right of the priests from the people, and from those who offer sacrificial victims: whether they sacrifice an ox or a sheep, they shall give to the priest the shoulder and the stomach;

**18:4** the firstfruits of grain, wine, and oil, and a portion of the wool from the shearing of the sheep.

**18:5** For the Lord your God has chosen him out of all your tribes to stand and minister to the name of the Lord, he and his sons forever.

**18:6** If a Levite goes out from one of your cities out of all Israel in which he dwells, and wishes to come, longing for the place that the Lord will choose,

**18:7** he shall minister in the name of the Lord his God, just like all his brothers the Levites who will stand at that time before the Lord.

**18:8** He shall receive the same portion of food as the others, except for what is owed to him in his own city by paternal succession.

**18:9** When you have entered the land that the Lord your God will give you, be careful that you do not wish to imitate the abominations of those nations.

**18:10** Let there not be found among you anyone who purifies his son or daughter, leading them through fire, or who consults soothsayers and observes dreams and auguries, nor let there be a sorcerer,

**18:11** nor an enchanter, nor anyone who consults mediums or diviners, or seeks the truth from the dead.

**18:12** For the Lord abhors all these things, and because of crimes of this sort He will destroy them at your entrance.

**18:13** You shall be perfect and without blemish with the Lord your God.

**18:14** These nations, whose land you will possess, listen to augurs and diviners; but you have been instructed otherwise by the Lord your God.

**18:15** A prophet from your nation and from your brothers, like me, the Lord your God will raise up for you; to him you shall listen,

**18:16** just as you asked of the Lord your God at Horeb, when the assembly was gathered together, and you said, "I will no longer hear the voice of the Lord my God, and I will no longer see this exceedingly great fire, so that I do not die."

**18:17** And the Lord said to me, "They have spoken all things well.

**18:18** I will raise up for them a prophet from the midst of their brothers, like you, and I will put My words in his mouth, and he will speak to them everything that I command him.

**18:19** And as for the one who is unwilling to listen to his words that he will speak in My name, I Myself will be the avenger.

**18:20** But the prophet who, corrupted by arrogance, chooses to speak in My name things that I did not command him to say, or by the name of foreign gods, shall be put to death."

**18:21** And if in silent thought you reply, "How can I discern the word that the Lord has not spoken?"

**18:22** you shall have this sign: whatever that prophet has foretold in the name of the Lord and it does not come about, this the Lord has not spoken, but the prophet has fabricated it through the swelling of his mind, and therefore you shall not fear him.

## Translator Notes

- **18:3** — The Vulgate names only two sacrificial portions (armum ac ventriculum, "the shoulder and the stomach"), omitting the cheeks listed in the Hebrew text.
- **18:8** — Latin excepto eo, quod in urbe sua ex paterna ei successione debetur ("except for what is owed to him in his own city by paternal succession") clarifies an obscure Hebrew phrase commonly taken as proceeds from the sale of ancestral property.
- **18:10** — Latin lustret ... ducens per ignem ("purifies ... leading them through fire") frames the practice as pagan ritual purification, and observet somnia ("observes dreams") renders a debated Hebrew divination term.
- **18:11** — Latin pythones (translated "mediums") literally designates oracular spirits or persons possessed by a spirit of divination (cf. Acts 16:16).
- **18:19** — Latin ego ultor existam ("I Myself will be the avenger") is a vivid Vulgate rendering of the Hebrew idiom "I will require it of him."
