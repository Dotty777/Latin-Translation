## Text

**31:1** Therefore Moses went and spoke all these words to the whole of Israel,

**31:2** and he said to them, "I am one hundred twenty years old today; I am no longer able to go out and come in, especially since the Lord has also said to me, 'You will not cross this Jordan.'

**31:3** Therefore the Lord your God will cross over ahead of you; He Himself will destroy all these nations in your sight, and you will possess them; and this Joshua will cross over ahead of you, just as the Lord has spoken.

**31:4** And the Lord will do to them just as He did to Sihon and Og, kings of the Amorites, and to their land, and He will destroy them.

**31:5** Therefore, when He has handed these over to you as well, you will do to them in the same way, just as I have commanded you.

**31:6** Act courageously and be strengthened; do not be afraid, nor be terrified at the sight of them, for the Lord your God Himself is your leader, and He will neither leave nor abandon you."

**31:7** And Moses called Joshua and said to him in the presence of all Israel, "Be strengthened and be resolute, for you will bring this people into the land that the Lord swore He would give to their fathers, and you will divide it by lot.

**31:8** And the Lord, who is your leader, will Himself be with you; He will neither leave nor abandon you; do not be afraid, nor be terrified."

**31:9** So Moses wrote this law and handed it over to the priests, the sons of Levi, who carried the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and to all the elders of Israel.

**31:10** And he commanded them, saying, "After seven years, in the year of remission, at the solemn feast of Tabernacles,

**31:11** when all those from Israel assemble to appear in the sight of the Lord your God in the place that the Lord will choose, you will read the words of this law in the presence of all Israel, in their hearing,

**31:12** and with all the people gathered together as one, both men and women, little ones, and foreigners who are within your gates, so that as they listen they may learn and fear the Lord your God, and keep and fulfill all the words of this law;

**31:13** so that their children also, who now do not know, may be able to hear and may fear the Lord their God all the days in which they live in the land that you are going to obtain after crossing the Jordan."

**31:14** And the Lord said to Moses, "Look, the days of your death are near; call Joshua, and stand in the tabernacle of the testimony, so that I may command him." So Moses and Joshua went and stood in the tabernacle of the testimony.

**31:15** And the Lord appeared there in a pillar of cloud, which stood at the entrance of the tabernacle.

**31:16** And the Lord said to Moses, "Look, you will sleep with your fathers, and this people will rise up and prostitute themselves after foreign gods in the land into which they are entering so that they may dwell in it; there they will abandon Me and nullify the covenant that I made with them.

**31:17** And My fury will rage against them on that day, and I will abandon them and hide My face from them, and they will be for devouring; all evils and afflictions will find them, so that they will say on that day, 'Truly, because God is not with me, these evils have found me.'

**31:18** But I will hide and conceal My face on that day on account of all the evils that they have done, because they have followed foreign gods.

**31:19** Now therefore, write this song for yourselves and teach it to the sons of Israel, so that they may keep it in memory and sing it with their mouths, and this song may be a witness for Me among the sons of Israel.

**31:20** For I will bring them into the land for which I swore to their fathers, flowing with milk and honey. And when they have eaten and been satisfied and grown fat, they will turn aside to foreign gods and serve them, and they will revile Me and nullify My covenant.

**31:21** After many evils and afflictions have found them, this song, which no forgetfulness will erase from the mouth of their seed, will answer them as a witness. For I know their thoughts, what they are about to do today, before I bring them into the land that I have promised them."

**31:22** Therefore Moses wrote the song and taught it to the sons of Israel.

**31:23** And the Lord commanded Joshua son of Nun and said, "Be strengthened and be resolute, for you will bring the sons of Israel into the land that I have promised, and I will be with you."

**31:24** Therefore, after Moses had written the words of this law in a scroll and completed them,

**31:25** he commanded the Levites who carried the ark of the covenant of the Lord, saying,

**31:26** "Take this book and place it at the side of the ark of the covenant of the Lord your God, so that it may be there as a witness against you.

**31:27** For I know your contentiousness and your most stubborn neck. While I am still alive and walking with you, you have always acted contentiously against the Lord; how much more when I am dead?

**31:28** Assemble to me all the elders throughout your tribes, and the teachers, and I will speak these words in their hearing, and I will call upon heaven and earth against them.

**31:29** For I know that after my death you will act wickedly and quickly turn aside from the way that I have commanded you; and evils will meet you in the final time, when you do evil in the sight of the Lord, so as to provoke Him through the works of your hands."

**31:30** Therefore Moses spoke, in the hearing of the entire assembly of Israel, the words of this song, and completed them to the very end.

## Translator Notes

- **31:6** — The Vulgate uses the title ductor ("leader") here and in verse 8, rendering distinct Hebrew phrases ("goes with you" in v. 6 and "goes before you" in v. 8) with one consistent term.
- **31:7** — Latin reads "you will bring this people in" (introduces, agreeing with the Septuagint against the Hebrew "go with") and "divide it by lot" (sorte divides), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "cause them to inherit it."
- **31:12** — Whereas the Hebrew begins verse 12 with a new imperative ("Assemble the people"), the Vulgate continues the sentence from verse 11 with a coordinated ablative absolute ("and with all the people gathered together as one").
- **31:17** — The Vulgate turns the Hebrew rhetorical question ("Is it not because my God is not in my midst...?") into a direct affirmative statement ("Truly, because God is not with me..."), and reads "all evils" (omnia mala) rather than "many evils" (contrast v. 21).
- **31:28** — Latin renders the Hebrew term for tribal officers/officials (shoterim) as doctores ("teachers"), reflecting a traditional view of these leaders as instructors in the law; the Vulgate also omits "as witnesses" with "call upon heaven and earth."
