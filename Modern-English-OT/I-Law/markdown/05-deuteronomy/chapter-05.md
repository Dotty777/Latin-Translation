## Text

**5:1** And Moses called all Israel and said to them, "Hear, Israel, the ceremonies and judgments that I speak in your ears today; learn them and fulfill them in deed.

**5:2** The Lord our God established a covenant with us at Horeb.

**5:3** Not with our fathers did He enter into the pact, but with us who are here at the present time and live.

**5:4** Face to face He spoke to us on the mountain from the midst of the fire.

**5:5** I was the mediator and intermediary between the Lord and you at that time, to declare His words to you, for you feared the fire and did not go up onto the mountain. And He said:

**5:6** 'I am the Lord your God, who led you out from the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery.

**5:7** You shall not have foreign gods in My sight.

**5:8** You shall not make for yourself a sculpted image, nor a likeness of any of the things that are in heaven above, and that are on the earth below, and that move about in the waters beneath the earth.

**5:9** You shall not worship them, and you shall not revere them. For I am the Lord your God, a jealous God, repaying the iniquity of the fathers upon the children to the third and fourth generation, to those who hate Me,

**5:10** and showing mercy to many thousands, to those who love Me and keep My precepts.

**5:11** You shall not use the name of the Lord your God in vain, for the one who takes up His name over an empty matter will not go unpunished.

**5:12** Observe the Sabbath day so that you may sanctify it, just as the Lord your God commanded you.

**5:13** For six days you shall work and do all your works.

**5:14** The seventh day is the Sabbath, that is, the rest of the Lord your God. On it you shall not do any work: neither you, nor your son, nor daughter, nor male servant, nor female servant, nor ox, nor donkey, nor any beast of burden of yours, nor the foreigner who is within your gates, so that your male servant and your female servant may rest, just as you also do.

**5:15** Remember that you yourself also were a slave in Egypt, and the Lord your God led you out from there with a strong hand and an outstretched arm. Therefore He commanded you to observe the Sabbath day.

**5:16** Honor your father and mother, just as the Lord your God commanded you, so that you may live for a long time and it may go well with you in the land that the Lord your God is about to give to you.

**5:17** And you shall not commit adultery.

**5:18** You shall not kill,

**5:19** and you shall not commit theft;

**5:20** nor shall you speak false testimony against your neighbor.

**5:21** You shall not covet the wife of your neighbor: neither house, nor field, nor male servant, nor female servant, nor ox, nor donkey, nor anything at all that belongs to him.'

**5:22** These words the Lord spoke to your whole multitude on the mountain from the midst of the fire, the cloud, and the thick gloom, with a loud voice, adding nothing further; and He wrote them on two stone tablets, which He delivered to me.

**5:23** Then after you heard the voice from the midst of the darkness and saw the mountain burning, you approached me, all the leaders of the tribes and the elders, and said:

**5:24** 'Look, the Lord our God has shown us His majesty and greatness; we have heard His voice from the midst of the fire, and we have proved today that when God speaks with man, man has lived.

**5:25** Why then will we die, and will this enormous fire devour us? For if we hear the voice of the Lord our God any further, we will die.

**5:26** What is all flesh, that it should hear the voice of the living God, who speaks from the midst of the fire just as we have heard, and be able to live?

**5:27** Rather, you draw near and hear all the things that the Lord our God will say to you; and you will speak to us, and listening, we will do them.'

**5:28** When the Lord had heard this, He said to me, 'I have heard the voice of the words of this people that they have spoken to you; they have spoken all things well.

**5:29** Who would grant that they might have such a mind, to fear Me and keep all My commandments at all times, so that it might go well with them and their children forever?

**5:30** Go and say to them, "Return to your tents."

**5:31** But you, stand here with Me, and I will speak to you all My commandments, and ceremonies, and judgments, which you will teach them, so that they may do them in the land that I will give to them as a possession.'

**5:32** Therefore keep and do the things that the Lord God has commanded you; you shall not turn aside either to the right or to the left,

**5:33** but you shall walk along the way that the Lord your God has commanded, so that you may live, and it may go well with you, and days may be lengthened in the land of your possession."

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Latin 'caeremonias' ("ceremonies," used consistently throughout chs. 5-6) is the Vulgate's characteristic rendering of the Hebrew term usually translated "statutes."
- **5:4** — The Vulgate reads 'nobis' ("to us"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "to you" (plural).
- **5:5** — Latin 'sequester et medius' ("mediator and intermediary") expands the Hebrew "standing [between]" into a double mediatorial expression.
- **5:14** — The Vulgate inserts the explanatory gloss 'id est, requies' ("that is, the rest") to explain the loanword 'sabbati' for Latin readers.
- **5:17** — In this Latin source text, 'neque moechaberis' ("And you shall not commit adultery") stands at v. 17 and 'Non occides' ("You shall not kill") at v. 18, reversing the order of the Hebrew text and standard Vulgate editions (note the connective 'neque' in v. 17 and capitalized 'Non' in v. 18).
