## Text

**3:1** And so, turning, we went up by the road of Bashan; and Og king of Bashan came out to meet us with his people to wage war at Edrei.

**3:2** And the Lord said to me, "Do not fear him, because he has been handed over into your hand with all his people and his land; and you will do to him just as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon."

**3:3** Therefore the Lord our God handed over into our hands Og king of Bashan also, and all his people; and we struck them down to utter destruction,

**3:4** devastating all his cities at one time. There was not a town that escaped us: sixty cities, the entire region of Argob of the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

**3:5** All the cities were fortified with very high walls, gates, and bars, apart from countless towns that had no walls.

**3:6** And we destroyed them, just as we had done to Sihon king of Heshbon, wiping out every city, men and women and little ones;

**3:7** but we plundered the livestock and the spoils of the cities.

**3:8** And at that time we took the land from the hand of the two kings of the Amorites who were across the Jordan, from the stream of Arnon as far as Mount Hermon,

**3:9** which the Sidonians call Sirion, and the Amorites Senir:

**3:10** all the cities that are situated on the plain, and the entire land of Gilead and Bashan as far as Salecah and Edrei, cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

**3:11** For Og king of Bashan alone had remained from the lineage of the giants. His iron bed is shown, which is in Rabbah of the sons of Ammon, having nine cubits in length and four in width according to the measure of the cubit of a man's hand.

**3:12** And we took possession of the land at that time from Aroer, which is on the bank of the stream of Arnon, as far as half of Mount Gilead; and I gave its cities to Reuben and Gad.

**3:13** Now the remaining part of Gilead, and all Bashan of the kingdom of Og, I handed over to the half-tribe of Manasseh, the entire region of Argob; and all Bashan is called the Land of Giants.

**3:14** Jair the son of Manasseh took possession of the entire region of Argob as far as the borders of the Geshurites and the Maacathites. And he called Bashan after his own name Havvoth-jair, that is, the Villages of Jair, to the present day.

**3:15** To Machir also I gave Gilead.

**3:16** And to the tribes of Reuben and Gad I gave from the land of Gilead as far as the stream of Arnon, the middle of the stream, and the boundary as far as the stream of Jabbok, which is the border of the sons of Ammon,

**3:17** and the plain of the wilderness, and the Jordan, and the borders of Chinnereth as far as the sea of the desert, which is the saltiest, at the foot of Mount Pisgah toward the east.

**3:18** And I commanded you at that time, saying, "The Lord your God is giving you this land as an inheritance; go armed ahead of your brothers, the sons of Israel, all you strong men,

**3:19** apart from your wives, little ones, and livestock. For I know that you have many herds, and they must remain in the cities that I handed over to you,

**3:20** until the Lord grants rest to your brothers, just as He granted to you, and they themselves also possess the land that He is about to give them across the Jordan; then each one will return to his own possession, which I gave to you."

**3:21** I also commanded Joshua at that time, saying, "Your eyes have seen what the Lord your God did to these two kings; so He will do to all the kingdoms to which you are about to cross over.

**3:22** Do not fear them, for the Lord your God will fight for you."

**3:23** And I prayed to the Lord at that time, saying:

**3:24** "Lord God, You have begun to show Your servant Your greatness and Your very strong hand; for there is no other god either in heaven or on earth who can do Your works and be compared to Your strength.

**3:25** Therefore I will cross over and see this finest land across the Jordan, and this splendid mountain, and Lebanon."

**3:26** And the Lord was angry with me because of you, and did not listen to me, but said to me, "It is enough for you; never speak to Me again about this matter.

**3:27** Go up to the summit of Pisgah, and turn your eyes around to the west, and to the north, and south, and east, and look; for you will not cross this Jordan.

**3:28** Command Joshua, and strengthen him and encourage him, because he himself will go ahead of this people, and he will divide for them the land that you are about to see."

**3:29** And we stayed in the valley opposite the shrine of Peor.

## Translator Notes

- **3:11** — Where the Hebrew asks rhetorically whether Og's bed is not in Rabbah and specifies "by the cubit of a man," the Vulgate states Monstratur lectus ejus ferreus ("His iron bed is shown") and clarifies the measure as ad mensuram cubiti virilis manus ("according to the measure of the cubit of a man's hand").
- **3:14** — The explanatory gloss id est, Villas Jair ("that is, the Villages of Jair") is a Vulgate addition translating the Hebrew name Havvoth-jair for Latin readers.
- **3:17** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew "Salt Sea" with a superlative clause, quod est salsissimum ("which is the saltiest"), and reads terminos Cenereth ("the borders of Chinnereth") rather than "the border, from Chinnereth."
- **3:29** — Rather than transliterating the Hebrew place name Beth-peor, the Vulgate translates Beth ("house") as fanum ("shrine"), rendering the site as fanum Phogor ("the shrine of Peor").
