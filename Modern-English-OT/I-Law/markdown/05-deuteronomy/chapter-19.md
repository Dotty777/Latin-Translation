## Text

**19:1** When the Lord your God has destroyed the nations whose land He is about to hand over to you, and you have possessed it and lived in its cities and in its houses,

**19:2** you shall set apart three cities for yourself in the midst of the land that the Lord your God will give you as a possession,

**19:3** carefully preparing the road; and you shall divide the entire territory of your land equally into three parts, so that the one who is a fugitive on account of homicide may have nearby a place to which he can escape.

**19:4** This shall be the law of the fleeing killer whose life is to be preserved: the one who strikes his neighbor unknowingly, and who is proved to have held no hatred against him yesterday and the day before yesterday,

**19:5** but to have gone with him simply into the forest to cut wood, and in the cutting down of the wood the axe flies from his hand, and the iron, slipping off the handle, strikes his friend and kills him: this man shall flee to one of the aforementioned cities and live,

**19:6** so that the near relative of the one whose blood was shed may not perhaps, goaded by grief, pursue and seize him if the road is too long, and strike down his life, though he is not liable to death, because he is shown to have held no prior hatred against the one who was killed.

**19:7** Therefore I command you to divide off three cities at equal distance from one another.

**19:8** Now when the Lord your God has enlarged your borders, just as He swore to your fathers, and has given you all the land that He promised them

**19:9** (if indeed you keep His commandments and do what I command you today, to love the Lord your God and to walk in His ways at all times), you shall add for yourself three other cities, and you shall double the number of the three aforementioned cities,

**19:10** so that innocent blood may not be shed in the midst of the land that the Lord your God will give you to possess, and so that you may not be guilty of blood.

**19:11** But if anyone, hating his neighbor, lies in wait for his life, and rises up and strikes him so that he dies, and flees to one of the aforementioned cities,

**19:12** the elders of that city shall send and seize him from the place of refuge, and hand him over into the hand of the near relative of the one whose blood was shed, and he shall die.

**19:13** You shall show him no pity, and you shall remove innocent blood from Israel, so that it may go well with you.

**19:14** You shall not take up and move the boundary markers of your neighbor, which predecessors fixed in your possession, which the Lord your God will give you in the land that you receive to possess.

**19:15** A single witness shall not stand against anyone, whatever that sin and crime may be; rather, every word shall stand by the mouth of two or three witnesses.

**19:16** If a lying witness stands against a man, accusing him of transgression,

**19:17** both of those whose case it is shall stand before the Lord in the presence of the priests and the judges who shall be in those days.

**19:18** And when, investigating most diligently, they find that the false witness has spoken a lie against his brother,

**19:19** they shall repay him just as he planned to do to his brother, and you shall remove the evil from your midst,

**19:20** so that the rest, hearing of it, may have fear, and by no means dare to do such things.

**19:21** You shall show him no pity, but you shall exact life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.

## Translator Notes

- **19:4** — Vulgate adds legal evidentiary verbs in v. 4 (comprobatur, "is proved") and v. 6 (monstratur, "is shown"), and in v. 5 adds simpliciter ("simply," i.e., without guile), clarifying judicial proof of unintentional killing.
- **19:6** — Latin proximus ejus, cujus effusus est sanguis ("near relative of the one whose blood was shed"; similarly v. 12) renders Hebrew go'el haddam ("avenger of blood"); Vulgate also renders Hebrew "while his heart is hot" with dolore stimulatus ("goaded by grief").
- **19:7** — Vulgate specifies æqualis inter se spatii ("at equal distance from one another"), an explanatory detail not present in the Hebrew text (cf. also numerum duplicabis, "you shall double the number," in v. 9).
- **19:21** — Vulgate supplies the explicit verb exiges ("you shall exact"), whereas the Hebrew lists the five retaliatory phrases without a finite verb.
