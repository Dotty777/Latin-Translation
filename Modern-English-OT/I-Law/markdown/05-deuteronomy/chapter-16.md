## Text

**16:1** Observe the month of new produce, and the first of the spring season, so that you may keep the Passover to the Lord your God, because in that month the Lord your God brought you out of Egypt by night.

**16:2** And you shall sacrifice the Passover to the Lord your God from the sheep and from the cattle, in the place that the Lord your God will choose, so that His name may dwell there.

**16:3** You shall not eat leavened bread with it; for seven days you shall eat bread of affliction without leaven, because you came out of Egypt in fear, so that you may remember the day of your departure from Egypt all the days of your life.

**16:4** No leaven shall appear within all your borders for seven days, and nothing of the flesh of what was sacrificed in the evening on the first day shall remain until morning.

**16:5** You may not sacrifice the Passover in just any of your cities that the Lord your God is about to give you,

**16:6** but in the place that the Lord your God will choose, so that His name may dwell there. You shall sacrifice the Passover in the evening at the setting of the sun, when you came out of Egypt.

**16:7** And you shall cook and eat it in the place that the Lord your God will choose, and rising in the morning, you shall go to your tents.

**16:8** For six days you shall eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day, because it is an assembly of the Lord your God, you shall do no work.

**16:9** You shall count seven weeks for yourself from the day on which you put the sickle to the standing grain.

**16:10** And you shall celebrate the feast day of Weeks to the Lord your God, with a voluntary offering of your hand, which you shall offer according to the blessing of the Lord your God,

**16:11** and you shall feast before the Lord your God, you, your son and your daughter, your male servant and your female servant, and the Levite who is within your gates, the foreigner and the orphan and the widow who dwell with you, in the place that the Lord your God will choose, so that His name may dwell there.

**16:12** And you shall remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and you shall keep and do the things that have been commanded.

**16:13** You shall also celebrate the solemn feast of Tabernacles for seven days, when you have gathered your produce from the threshing floor and the winepress,

**16:14** and you shall feast in your festival, you, your son and daughter, your male servant and female servant, also the Levite and the foreigner, the orphan and the widow who are within your gates.

**16:15** For seven days you shall celebrate feasts to the Lord your God in the place that the Lord will choose; and the Lord your God will bless you in all your produce and in every work of your hands, and you will be in gladness.

**16:16** Three times a year every male of yours shall appear in the sight of the Lord your God in the place that He will choose: at the solemn feast of Unleavened Bread, at the solemn feast of Weeks, and at the solemn feast of Tabernacles. He shall not appear before the Lord empty,

**16:17** but each one shall offer in accordance with what he has, according to the blessing of the Lord his God that He has given him.

**16:18** You shall appoint judges and officials in all your gates that the Lord your God will give you, throughout each of your tribes, so that they may judge the people with just judgment,

**16:19** and not turn aside to either side. You shall not show partiality or accept bribes, because bribes blind the eyes of the wise and change the words of the righteous.

**16:20** You shall justly pursue what is just, so that you may live and possess the land that the Lord your God will give you.

**16:21** You shall not plant a grove or any tree beside the altar of the Lord your God.

**16:22** Neither shall you make for yourself nor set up a statue: things that the Lord your God hates.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew month name Abib ("ears of grain") with the descriptive doublet novarum frugum, et verni primum temporis ("of new produce, and the first of the spring season").
- **16:3** — Latin in pavore ("in fear") is a distinctive Vulgate reading where the Hebrew text reads "in haste."
- **16:11** — Latin epulaberis ("you shall feast," also in v. 14) concretizes the Hebrew verb for rejoicing into a festive meal.
- **16:20** — Latin Juste quod justum est persequeris ("You shall justly pursue what is just") renders the emphatic Hebrew repetition ("Justice, justice") with an adverb and relative clause.
- **16:21** — Latin lucum ("grove") is the Vulgate's regular rendering of Hebrew Asherah; likewise statuam ("statue") in v. 22 renders the Hebrew sacred pillar.
