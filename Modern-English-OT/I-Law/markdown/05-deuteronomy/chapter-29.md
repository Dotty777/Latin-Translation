## Text

**29:1** And Moses called all Israel and said to them, "You have seen everything that the Lord did before you in the land of Egypt to Pharaoh, and to all his servants, and to his entire land,

**29:2** the great trials which your eyes saw, those signs and mighty wonders.

**29:3** Yet the Lord has not given you an understanding heart, and eyes that see, and ears that can hear, up to the present day.

**29:4** He led you for forty years through the wilderness; your garments did not wear out, nor were the sandals of your feet consumed by age.

**29:5** You did not eat bread; you did not drink wine or strong drink, so that you might know that I am the Lord your God.

**29:6** And you came to this place, and Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came out, meeting us for battle. And we struck them down,

**29:7** and we took their land, and handed it over to Reuben and Gad and to the half-tribe of Manasseh to possess.

**29:8** Keep, therefore, the words of this covenant, and fulfill them, so that you may understand everything that you do.

**29:9** All of you stand today before the Lord your God: your leaders and tribes, and elders, and teachers, all the people of Israel,

**29:10** your children and wives, and the stranger who stays with you in the camp, except the cutters of wood and those who carry water,

**29:11** so that you may pass into the covenant of the Lord your God, and into the oath which the Lord your God makes with you today,

**29:12** so that He may raise you up for Himself as a people, and He Himself may be your God, just as He spoke to you, and just as He swore to your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

**29:13** And not with you alone do I make this covenant and confirm these oaths,

**29:14** but with all who are present and absent.

**29:15** For you know how we lived in the land of Egypt, and how we passed through the midst of the nations, passing through which

**29:16** you saw the abominations and filth, that is, their idols, wood and stone, silver and gold, which they worshiped.

**29:17** Let there not by chance be among you a man or woman, family or tribe, whose heart is turned away today from the Lord our God, to go and serve the gods of those nations, nor let there be among you a root sprouting gall and bitterness.

**29:18** And when he hears the words of this oath, he may bless himself in his heart, saying, 'Peace will be mine, and I will walk in the depravity of my heart,' and the drunken one may consume the thirsty one;

**29:19** yet the Lord will not forgive him, but then above all His fury and jealousy will smoke against that man, and all the curses that are written in this scroll will settle upon him, and the Lord will blot out his name under heaven,

**29:20** and consume him to destruction out of all the tribes of Israel, according to the curses that are contained in the book of this law and covenant.

**29:21** And the following generation will say, and the children who will be born afterward, and the foreigners who come from far away, seeing the plagues of that land and the sicknesses with which the Lord has afflicted it,

**29:22** burning it with sulfur and the heat of salt, so that it is no longer sown, nor does anything green sprout, as an example of the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, which the Lord overthrew in His anger and fury.

**29:23** And all the nations will say, 'Why has the Lord done so to this land? What is this immense anger of His fury?'

**29:24** And they will answer, 'Because they abandoned the covenant of the Lord, which He made with their fathers, when He brought them out of the land of Egypt,

**29:25** and they served foreign gods, and worshiped those whom they did not know, and to whom they had not been assigned;

**29:26** therefore the fury of the Lord grew angry against this land, so as to bring upon it all the curses that are written in this scroll;

**29:27** and He drove them out of their land in anger and in fury, and in the greatest indignation, and cast them into a foreign land, as is proven today.'

**29:28** The hidden things belong to the Lord our God; the things that are revealed belong to us and to our children forever, so that we may do all the words of this law."

## Translator Notes

- **29:4** — The Vulgate reads third-person Adduxit ('He led,' referring back to the Lord in 29:3) before shifting to first-person 'I am the Lord your God' in 29:5, whereas the Hebrew has first-person ('I led') in both verses.
- **29:10** — The Vulgate reads exceptis lignorum caesoribus, et his qui comportant aquas ('except the cutters of wood and those who carry water'), taking the Hebrew preposition exclusively rather than inclusively ('from your woodcutter to your water-drawer').
- **29:14** — The Vulgate compresses the longer Hebrew wording ('both with him who stands here with us today before the Lord our God and with him who is not here with us today') into the concise phrase sed cunctis praesentibus et absentibus ('but with all who are present and absent').
- **29:18** — The proverbial Vulgate clause et absumat ebria sitientem ('and the drunken one may consume the thirsty one') literally preserves the feminine adjectives of a notoriously difficult Hebrew expression, generally taken to signify sweeping away saturated and dry alike in collective ruin.
- **29:25** — The Vulgate uses a passive construction, et quibus non fuerant attributi ('and to whom they had not been assigned,' i.e., Israel to those gods), reversing the direction of the Hebrew ('whom He had not allotted to them').
