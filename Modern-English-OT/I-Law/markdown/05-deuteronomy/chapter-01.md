## Text

**1:1** These are the words that Moses spoke to all Israel across the Jordan in the wilderness plain, opposite the Red Sea, between Paran and Tophel and Laban and Hazeroth, where gold is very plentiful.

**1:2** It is eleven days from Horeb by the way of Mount Seir as far as Kadesh-barnea.

**1:3** In the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, Moses spoke to the sons of Israel all that the Lord had commanded him to say to them,

**1:4** after he struck down Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who stayed in Ashtaroth and in Edrei,

**1:5** across the Jordan in the land of Moab. And Moses began to explain the law and to say:

**1:6** "The Lord our God spoke to us in Horeb, saying, 'It is enough for you that you have stayed on this mountain:

**1:7** turn back and come to the mountain of the Amorites, and to the other regions that are nearest to it, the plains and the mountains and the lower places toward the south, and beside the shore of the sea, the land of the Canaanites and of Lebanon, as far as the great river Euphrates.

**1:8** Look,' He said, 'I have delivered it to you: go in and possess it, concerning which the Lord swore to your fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, that He would give it to them and to their seed after them.'

**1:9** And I said to you at that time:

**1:10** 'I cannot bear you alone, because the Lord your God has multiplied you, and today you are like the stars of heaven, very numerous.

**1:11** (May the Lord God of your fathers add many thousands to this number, and bless you just as He has spoken.)

**1:12** I am not able by myself to bear your affairs, and the burden and disputes.

**1:13** Give from among you men who are wise and knowledgeable, and whose conduct is proven in your tribes, so that I may set them as leaders for you.'

**1:14** Then you answered me, 'It is a good thing that you want to do.'

**1:15** And I took from your tribes wise and noble men, and I appointed them as leaders: tribunes, and centurions, and officers of fifty and officers of ten, who would teach you each individual matter.

**1:16** And I commanded them, saying, 'Hear them, and judge what is just, whether he is a citizen or a foreigner.

**1:17** There shall be no distinction of persons: you shall hear the small just as the great, nor shall you favor the person of anyone, because judgment belongs to God. And if anything seems difficult to you, refer it to me, and I will hear it.'

**1:18** And I commanded everything that you should do.

**1:19** Now setting out from Horeb, we passed through a terrible and very great desert, which you saw, by the way of the mountain of the Amorite, just as the Lord our God had commanded us. And when we had come to Kadesh-barnea,

**1:20** I said to you, 'You have come to the mountain of the Amorite, which the Lord our God is about to give to us:

**1:21** see the land that the Lord your God is giving to you: go up and possess it, just as the Lord our God spoke to your fathers: do not fear, nor dread anything.'

**1:22** And all of you approached me and said, 'Let us send men who will examine the land and report back by which route we should go up and to which cities we should proceed.'

**1:23** And when the word pleased me, I sent twelve men from among you, one from each of their tribes.

**1:24** And when they had proceeded and gone up into the mountains, they came as far as the Valley of the Cluster; and having examined the land,

**1:25** taking some of its fruits so that they might show its fertility, they brought them to us and said, 'The land that the Lord our God is about to give to us is good.'

**1:26** And you were unwilling to go up, but being unbelieving toward the word of the Lord our God,

**1:27** you grumbled in your tents and said, 'The Lord hates us, and for that reason He brought us out of the land of Egypt, to deliver us into the hand of the Amorite and destroy us.

**1:28** Where shall we go up? The messengers have terrified our heart, saying, "The multitude is very great, and taller in stature than we are; the cities are great and fortified up to heaven; we saw the sons of the Anakim there."'

**1:29** And I said to you, 'Do not be frightened, nor fear them:

**1:30** the Lord God, who is your leader, He Himself will fight for you, just as He did in Egypt while all were watching.

**1:31** And in the wilderness (you yourself saw it) the Lord your God carried you, as a man is accustomed to carry his little son, on all the way along which you walked, until you came to this place.'

**1:32** And not even so did you believe the Lord your God,

**1:33** who went ahead of you on the way, and marked out the place in which you should pitch tents, showing you the route by night through fire, and by day through a pillar of cloud.

**1:34** And when the Lord heard the voice of your words, being angry, He swore and said:

**1:35** 'Not one of the men of this most wicked generation will see the good land that I promised under oath to your fathers,

**1:36** except Caleb son of Jephunneh: for he himself will see it, and to him I will give the land that he trod upon, and to his sons, because he followed the Lord.'

**1:37** Nor is the indignation against the people surprising, since the Lord, being angry with me also on account of you, said, 'Neither will you go in there;

**1:38** but Joshua son of Nun, your attendant, he himself will enter in your place. Encourage and strengthen him, and he himself will divide the land by lot for Israel.

**1:39** Your little ones, about whom you said that they would be led away captive, and the children who today do not know the distinction between good and evil, they themselves will go in; and to them I will give the land, and they will possess it.

**1:40** But as for you, turn back and depart into the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea.'

**1:41** And you answered me, 'We have sinned against the Lord: we will go up and fight, just as the Lord our God has commanded.' And when, equipped with weapons, you were proceeding into the mountain,

**1:42** the Lord said to me, 'Say to them, "Do not go up, nor fight, for I am not with you, so that you do not fall before your enemies."'

**1:43** I spoke, and you did not listen; but opposing the command of the Lord, and swelling with pride, you went up into the mountain.

**1:44** Therefore the Amorite who lived in the mountains came out, and coming to meet you, pursued you, just as bees are accustomed to pursue, and struck you down from Seir as far as Hormah.

**1:45** And when you returned and wept before the Lord, He did not listen to you, nor was He willing to yield to your voice.

**1:46** Therefore you remained in Kadesh-barnea for a long time."

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew place name Suph as "the Red Sea" (mare Rubrum) and translates the place name Di-zahab literally as a descriptive clause, "where gold is very plentiful" (ubi auri est plurimum).
- **1:15** — The Vulgate adapts the Israelite ranks into Roman military titles (tribunos, centuriones, quinquagenarios, decanos) and closes with "who would teach you each individual matter" (qui docerent vos singula), where the Hebrew reads "and officers for your tribes."
- **1:24** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew place name Nahal Eshcol literally as "the Valley of the Cluster" (Vallem botri).
- **1:25** — The explanatory clause "so that they might show its fertility" (ut ostenderent ubertatem) is a Vulgate-distinct addition not present in the Hebrew text.
- **1:37** — The opening clause "Nor is the indignation against the people surprising" (Nec miranda indignatio in populum) is a distinctive Vulgate expansion not found in the Hebrew text.
