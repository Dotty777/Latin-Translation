## Text

**22:1** You shall not see your brother's ox or sheep straying and pass by, but you shall lead it back to your brother,

**22:2** even if your brother is not near and you do not know him; you shall lead them into your house, and they shall remain with you until your brother searches for them and receives them back.

**22:3** You shall do likewise with a donkey, and with a garment, and with anything of your brother's that has been lost; if you find it, do not neglect it as though it belonged to another.

**22:4** If you see your brother's donkey or ox fallen on the road, you shall not disregard it, but you shall lift it up with him.

**22:5** A woman shall not put on a man's clothing, nor shall a man use a woman's clothing, for the one who does these things is abominable before God.

**22:6** If, while walking along the road, you find a bird's nest in a tree or on the ground, with the mother brooding above the chicks or the eggs, you shall not keep her with the young,

**22:7** but you shall allow her to go away, keeping the captured young, so that it may go well for you and you may live a long time.

**22:8** When you build a new house, you shall make a wall all around the roof, so that blood may not be shed in your house and you may not be guilty if someone slips and plunges headlong.

**22:9** You shall not sow your vineyard with another seed, so that both the crop that you have sown and what grows from the vineyard may not be sanctified together.

**22:10** You shall not plow with an ox and a donkey together.

**22:11** You shall not put on a garment that is woven together of wool and linen.

**22:12** You shall make cords on the fringes at the four corners of your cloak, with which you cover yourself.

**22:13** If a man takes a wife and afterward hates her,

**22:14** and looks for pretexts by which he may send her away, bringing a very bad name against her, and says, "I took this woman as a wife, and having gone in to her, I did not find her a virgin,"

**22:15** then her father and her mother shall take her and bring with them the signs of her virginity to the elders of the city who are at the gate,

**22:16** and the father shall say, "I gave my daughter to this man as a wife, and because he hates her,

**22:17** he puts a very bad name upon her, so as to say, 'I did not find your daughter a virgin.' And look, these are the signs of my daughter's virginity." They shall spread out the garment before the elders of the city,

**22:18** and the elders of that city shall seize the man and flog him,

**22:19** condemning him besides to one hundred shekels of silver, which he shall give to the father of the young woman, because he spread a very bad name over a virgin of Israel; and he shall have her as his wife, and he will not be able to send her away all the days of his life.

**22:20** But if what he brings against her is true, and virginity is not found in the young woman,

**22:21** they shall cast her out beyond the doors of her father's house, and the men of that city shall overwhelm her with stones, and she shall die, because she committed an outrage in Israel by committing fornication in her father's house; and you shall remove the evil from your midst.

**22:22** If a man sleeps with another's wife, both of them shall die, that is, the adulterer and the adulteress; and you shall remove the evil from Israel.

**22:23** If a man has betrothed a young woman who is a virgin, and someone finds her in the city and lies with her,

**22:24** you shall bring both of them out to the gate of that city, and they shall be overwhelmed with stones: the young woman, because she did not cry out while she was in the city; the man, because he humiliated his neighbor's wife; and you shall remove the evil from your midst.

**22:25** But if in the field a man finds a young woman who is betrothed, and seizing her, lies with her, he alone shall die;

**22:26** the young woman shall suffer nothing, nor is she guilty of death, for just as a robber rises up against his brother and takes his life, so also has the young woman suffered.

**22:27** She was alone in the field; she cried out, and no one was present to rescue her.

**22:28** If a man finds a young woman who is a virgin, who has no betrothed, and seizing her, lies with her, and the matter comes to judgment,

**22:29** the one who slept with her shall give fifty shekels of silver to the father of the young woman, and he shall have her as his wife, because he humiliated her; he will not be able to send her away all the days of his life.

**22:30** A man shall not take his father's wife, nor shall he uncover his covering.

## Translator Notes

- **22:9** — Latin sanctificentur ('be sanctified') literally renders the Hebrew idiom for produce forfeited or consecrated to the sanctuary because of unlawful mixing.
- **22:22** — The Vulgate supplies the explanatory phrase id est, adulter et adultera ('that is, the adulterer and the adulteress'), whereas the Hebrew reads 'the man who lay with the woman and the woman.'
- **22:26** — Distinctively in the Vulgate, the attacker in the comparison is called a latro ('robber') rising against fratrem suum ('his brother'), where the Hebrew has 'a man' rising against 'his neighbor.'
- **22:28** — The Vulgate specifies et res ad judicium venerit ('and the matter comes to judgment'), where the Hebrew reads simply 'and they are found.'
- **22:30** — In the Latin, as in the Hebrew text, chapter 23 begins with this verse. Native Vulgate Deuteronomy 22:30 maps to Saints.Bible 23:1; native 23:1-23 map to Saints.Bible 23:2-24, while native 23:24 (vineyard) and 23:25 (standing grain) map to Saints.Bible 23:26 and 23:25 respectively.
