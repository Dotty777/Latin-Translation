## Text

**33:1** This is the blessing with which Moses, the man of God, blessed the sons of Israel before his death.

**33:2** And he said: "The Lord came from Sinai, and from Seir He rose upon us; He appeared from Mount Paran, and with Him were thousands of holy ones. In His right hand was a fiery law.

**33:3** He loved the peoples; all the holy ones are in His hand, and those who draw near to His feet will receive from His teaching.

**33:4** Moses commanded a law for us, an inheritance of the multitude of Jacob.

**33:5** There will be a king with the most upright one, when the leaders of the people are gathered together with the tribes of Israel.

**33:6** Let Reuben live and not die, and let him be small in number."

**33:7** This is the blessing of Judah: "Hear, Lord, the voice of Judah, and bring him in to his people; his hands will fight for him, and He will be his helper against his adversaries."

**33:8** Of Levi he also said: "Your perfection and Your teaching belong to Your holy man, whom You proved in testing and judged at the waters of contradiction.

**33:9** He said to his father and to his mother, 'I do not know you,' and to his brothers, 'I do not recognize you,' and they did not know their sons. These kept Your word and observed Your covenant,

**33:10** Your judgments, Jacob, and Your law, Israel. They will place incense in Your wrath and a burnt offering upon Your altar.

**33:11** Bless, Lord, his strength, and accept the works of his hands. Strike the backs of his enemies, and let those who hate him not rise up."

**33:12** And of Benjamin he said: "The most beloved of the Lord will dwell confidently in Him; as in a bridal chamber he will remain all day long, and between His shoulders he will rest."

**33:13** Of Joseph he also said: "From the blessing of the Lord is his land, from the fruits of heaven and the dew, and from the deep lying beneath,

**33:14** from the fruits of the yields of the sun and moon,

**33:15** from the summit of the ancient mountains, from the fruits of the eternal hills,

**33:16** and from the crops of the earth and from its fullness. May the blessing of Him who appeared in the bush come upon the head of Joseph, and upon the crown of the Nazirite among his brothers.

**33:17** His beauty is like that of a firstborn bull; his horns are the horns of a rhinoceros. With them he will scatter the nations to the ends of the earth. These are the multitudes of Ephraim, and these are the thousands of Manasseh."

**33:18** And of Zebulun he said: "Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out, and Issachar, in your tents.

**33:19** They will call peoples to the mountain; there they will offer sacrifices of righteousness. They will suck the overflowing of the sea like milk, and the hidden treasures of the sands."

**33:20** And of Gad he said: "Blessed is Gad in breadth; like a lion he rested, and he seized the arm and the crown of the head.

**33:21** And he saw his preeminence, that in his portion the teacher was laid up, who was with the leaders of the people, and carried out the righteous deeds of the Lord and His judgment with Israel."

**33:22** Of Dan he also said: "Dan is a lion's cub; he will flow abundantly from Bashan."

**33:23** And of Naphtali he said: "Naphtali will fully enjoy abundance and will be full of the blessings of the Lord; he will possess the sea and the south."

**33:24** Of Asher he also said: "Blessed is Asher among sons; let him be pleasing to his brothers, and let him dip his foot in oil.

**33:25** Iron and bronze are his footwear. As the days of your youth, so also will your old age be.

**33:26** There is no other god like the God of the most upright one, the rider of heaven, your helper. By His majesty the clouds run to and fro,

**33:27** His dwelling place is above, and underneath are the everlasting arms; He will cast out the enemy from before your face, and He will say, 'Be crushed!'

**33:28** Israel will dwell confidently, and alone. The eye of Jacob is in a land of grain and wine, and the heavens will grow dark with dew.

**33:29** Blessed are you, Israel! Who is like you, a people who are saved in the Lord? He is the shield of your help and the sword of your glory; your enemies will deny you, and you will tread upon their necks."

## Translator Notes

- **33:5** — Latin rectissimum ("the most upright one"), repeated in 33:26, translates the poetic Hebrew title Jeshurun as a superlative adjective rather than a proper name; unlike the Hebrew past tense, the Vulgate also uses the future Erit ("There will be").
- **33:10** — Unlike the Hebrew ("They will teach Your ordinances to Jacob"), the Vulgate has no verb "teach," linking "Your judgments" and "Your law" to "observed" in v. 9 with vocatives to Jacob and Israel, and renders the Hebrew for "before You" / "to Your nostrils" literally as in furore tuo ("in Your wrath").
- **33:17** — Latin cornua rhinocerotis ("horns of a rhinoceros") is the Vulgate's distinctive rendering of the Hebrew re'em ("wild ox"), and ventilabit ("will scatter") literally means to winnow or toss into the air.
- **33:21** — Latin in parte sua doctor esset repositus ("in his portion the teacher was laid up") is the Vulgate's rendering of the difficult Hebrew clause about a commander's or lawgiver's portion, traditionally understood of Moses's burial in Gad's territory.
- **33:28** — Latin Oculus Jacob ("The eye of Jacob") translates Hebrew ayin literally as "eye" rather than "fountain," and caligabunt rore ("will grow dark with dew") reflects the Vulgate's reading of the Hebrew verb as clouding over or growing misty.
