## Text

**11:1** Love the Lord your God, therefore, and observe His precepts and ceremonies, judgments and commandments, at all times.

**11:2** Know today what your children do not know, who have not seen the discipline of the Lord your God, His mighty deeds and strong hand and outstretched arm,

**11:3** the signs and works that He did in the midst of Egypt to Pharaoh the king and to all his land,

**11:4** and to the entire army of the Egyptians, and to the horses and chariots: how the waters of the Red Sea covered them while they were pursuing you, and the Lord destroyed them up to the present day;

**11:5** and what He did for you in the wilderness until you came to this place,

**11:6** and to Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, who was the son of Reuben, whom the earth swallowed up when it opened its mouth, together with their households and tents and all their possessions that they had in the midst of Israel.

**11:7** Your eyes have seen the great works of the Lord that He did,

**11:8** so that you may keep all His commandments that I command you today, and may be able to enter and possess the land into which you are going,

**11:9** and may live for a long time in it, the land that the Lord promised under oath to your fathers and to their offspring, flowing with milk and honey.

**11:10** For the land that you are entering to possess is not like the land of Egypt, from which you came out, where, once the seed has been sown, irrigating waters are channeled in the manner of gardens;

**11:11** but it is mountainous and has plains, awaiting rains from heaven,

**11:12** which the Lord your God always looks after, and His eyes are upon it from the beginning of the year to its end.

**11:13** If therefore you obey my commandments that I command you today, to love the Lord your God and to serve Him with all your heart and with all your soul,

**11:14** He will give rain to your land, early and late, so that you may gather grain, wine, and oil,

**11:15** and hay from the fields to feed the livestock, and so that you yourselves may eat and be satisfied.

**11:16** Be careful, or your heart may somehow be deceived, and you may turn away from the Lord, and serve foreign gods and worship them,

**11:17** and the Lord, growing angry, may shut heaven, and the rains may not come down, nor may the earth yield its produce, and you may perish quickly from the excellent land that the Lord is about to give you.

**11:18** Place these words of mine in your hearts and in your souls, and hang them as a sign on your hands, and set them between your eyes.

**11:19** Teach your children to meditate on them, when you sit in your house, and walk along the way, and lie down and rise up.

**11:20** You shall write them on the doorposts and doors of your house,

**11:21** so that your days and those of your children may be multiplied in the land that the Lord swore to your fathers to give them, as long as heaven hangs over the earth.

**11:22** For if you keep the commandments that I command you, and do them, to love the Lord your God and to walk in all His ways, clinging to Him,

**11:23** the Lord will destroy all these nations before your face, and you will possess them, nations that are greater and stronger than you.

**11:24** Every place on which your foot treads will be yours. From the desert and from Lebanon, from the great river Euphrates to the western sea will be your borders.

**11:25** No one will stand against you; the Lord your God will put the terror and dread of you upon all the land that you are about to tread on, just as He spoke to you.

**11:26** Look, I set in your sight today a blessing and a curse:

**11:27** a blessing, if you obey the commandments of the Lord your God that I command you today;

**11:28** a curse, if you do not obey the commandments of the Lord your God, but turn away from the way that I now show you, and walk after foreign gods that you do not know.

**11:29** Now when the Lord your God brings you into the land that you are going to inhabit, you shall place the blessing on Mount Gerizim, the curse on Mount Ebal,

**11:30** which are across the Jordan, beyond the road that leads toward the setting of the sun, in the land of the Canaanite who lives in the plains opposite Gilgal, which is beside the valley that stretches out and goes far inward.

**11:31** For you will cross the Jordan so that you may possess the land that the Lord your God is about to give you, so that you may have and possess it.

**11:32** See to it, therefore, that you fulfill the ceremonies and judgments that I will set in your sight today.

## Translator Notes

- **11:7** — The Vulgate connects vv. 7-8 into a single sentence with the purpose clause "ut custodiatis" ("so that you may keep"), whereas the Hebrew begins a new independent command at v. 8.
- **11:10** — Where the Hebrew refers to watering Egypt "with your foot," the Vulgate renders the clause passively as "aquæ ducuntur irriguæ" ("irrigating waters are channeled").
- **11:14** — The Vulgate uses the third person "dabit" ("He will give") rather than the Hebrew first person ("I will give"), and in v. 15 construes "fœnumque ex agris" ("and hay from the fields") as an object of "colligatis" ("gather") rather than a separate clause.
- **11:30** — Instead of transliterating the Hebrew place name "elonei Moreh" ("oaks/terebinths of Moreh"), the Vulgate renders it descriptively as "vallem tendentem et intrantem procul" ("the valley that stretches out and goes far inward").
