## Text

**6:1** These are the precepts, and ceremonies, and judgments that the Lord your God commanded that I teach you, and that you do them in the land that you are crossing over to possess,

**6:2** so that you may fear the Lord your God and keep all His commandments and precepts, which I command you and your children and your grandchildren, all the days of your life, so that your days may be prolonged.

**6:3** Hear, Israel, and take care that you do what the Lord has commanded you, so that it may go well with you and you may be multiplied even more, just as the Lord, the God of your fathers, promised you a land flowing with milk and honey.

**6:4** Hear, Israel: the Lord our God is one Lord.

**6:5** You shall love the Lord your God from your whole heart, and from your whole soul, and from your whole strength.

**6:6** And these words, which I command you today, shall be in your heart;

**6:7** and you shall recount them to your children, and meditate on them sitting in your house and walking on the road, sleeping and rising up.

**6:8** And you shall bind them like a sign on your hand, and they shall be and move between your eyes,

**6:9** and you shall write them on the threshold and the doors of your house.

**6:10** And when the Lord your God has brought you into the land regarding which He swore to your fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and has given you great and most excellent cities that you did not build,

**6:11** houses full of all riches, which you did not construct, cisterns that you did not dig, vineyards and olive groves that you did not plant, and you have eaten and been satisfied, be very careful not to forget the Lord, who led you out from the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery. You shall fear the Lord your God, and Him alone you shall serve, and by His name you shall swear.

**6:14** You shall not go after the foreign gods of all the nations that are around you,

**6:15** because the Lord your God in your midst is a jealous God; otherwise the fury of the Lord your God might at some time be provoked against you and remove you from the surface of the earth.

**6:16** You shall not test the Lord your God, just as you tested Him at the place of testing.

**6:17** Keep the precepts of the Lord your God, and the testimonies and ceremonies that He commanded you,

**6:18** and do what is pleasing and good in the sight of the Lord, so that it may go well with you, and entering in you may possess the most excellent land about which the Lord swore to your fathers,

**6:19** that He would wipe out all your enemies before you, just as He has spoken.

**6:20** And when your son asks you tomorrow, saying, "What do these testimonies, and ceremonies, and judgments mean, which the Lord our God commanded us?"

**6:21** you shall say to him, "We were slaves of Pharaoh in Egypt, and the Lord led us out from Egypt with a strong hand,

**6:22** and He performed signs and wonders, great and most grievous, in Egypt against Pharaoh and his whole house in our sight,

**6:23** and He led us out from there, so that, having brought us in, He might give us the land concerning which He swore to our fathers.

**6:24** And the Lord commanded us to do all these ordinances and to fear the Lord our God, so that it may go well with us all the days of our life, just as it is today.

**6:25** And He will be merciful to us, if we keep and do all His precepts before the Lord our God, just as He commanded us."

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — Latin 'Dominus Deus noster, Dominus unus est' uses a single verb 'est' and can be read either as "the Lord our God is one Lord" (taking 'Dominus unus' as predicate) or "the Lord our God, the Lord is one."
- **6:8** — Latin 'eruntque et movebuntur' ("and they shall be and move") stands where the Hebrew has a rare noun for bands or frontlets; likewise, v. 9 reads 'in limine, et ostiis' ("on the threshold and the doors") rather than Hebrew "doorposts" and "gates."
- **6:11** — In this Latin text, the material corresponding to standard verses 11-13 is combined under v. 11; note also the Vulgate-distinct readings 'quas non exstruxisti' ("which you did not construct," vs. Hebrew "fill") and 'illi soli' ("Him alone," matching the Septuagint and Matt 4:10).
- **6:16** — Latin 'in loco tentationis' ("at the place of testing") translates the Hebrew place name Massah according to its meaning.
- **6:25** — Latin 'Eritque nostri misericors' ("And He will be merciful to us") aligns with the Septuagint in reading "mercy" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "righteousness" ("it will be righteousness for us").
