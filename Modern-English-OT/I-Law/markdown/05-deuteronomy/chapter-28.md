## Text

**28:1** Now if you listen to the voice of the Lord your God, to do and keep all His commandments which I command you today, the Lord your God will make you higher than all the nations that dwell on the earth.

**28:2** And all these blessings will come upon you and take hold of you, if only you listen to His precepts.

**28:3** Blessed are you in the city, and blessed in the field.

**28:4** Blessed is the fruit of your womb, and the fruit of your land, and the fruit of your livestock, the herds of your cattle, and the folds of your sheep.

**28:5** Blessed are your granaries, and blessed are your remnants.

**28:6** Blessed will you be coming in and going out.

**28:7** The Lord will cause your enemies who rise up against you to fall in your sight; by one way they will come against you, and by seven they will flee from your face.

**28:8** The Lord will send forth a blessing upon your storerooms and upon all the works of your hands, and He will bless you in the land that you will receive.

**28:9** The Lord will raise you up for Himself as a holy people, just as He swore to you, if you keep the commandments of the Lord your God and walk in His ways.

**28:10** And all the peoples of the lands will see that the name of the Lord has been invoked upon you, and they will fear you.

**28:11** The Lord will make you abound in all good things: in the fruit of your womb, and in the fruit of your livestock, in the fruit of your land, which the Lord swore to your fathers that He would give you.

**28:12** The Lord will open His best treasury, the sky, to grant rain to your land in its season, and He will bless all the works of your hands. And you will lend to many nations, and you yourself will take a loan from no one.

**28:13** The Lord will establish you as the head and not as the tail, and you will always be above and not beneath, if only you listen to the commandments of the Lord your God which I command you today, and keep and do them,

**28:14** and do not turn aside from them to the right or to the left, nor follow foreign gods, nor worship them.

**28:15** But if you are unwilling to listen to the voice of the Lord your God, to keep and do all His commandments and ceremonies which I command you today, all these curses will come upon you and take hold of you.

**28:16** Cursed will you be in the city, cursed in the field.

**28:17** Cursed is your granary, and cursed are your remnants.

**28:18** Cursed is the fruit of your womb, and the fruit of your land, the herds of your oxen, and the flocks of your sheep.

**28:19** Cursed will you be coming in, and cursed going out.

**28:20** The Lord will send upon you famine and hunger, and rebuke in all your works that you will do, until He crushes you and destroys you swiftly, because of your most wicked devices in which you have abandoned Me.

**28:21** May the Lord attach pestilence to you, until He consumes you from the land that you will enter to possess.

**28:22** May the Lord strike you with destitution, fever and cold, burning and heat, and corrupted air and mildew, and may He pursue you until you perish.

**28:23** May the sky that is above you be bronze, and the earth that you tread upon be iron.

**28:24** May the Lord give dust as the rain of your land, and from the sky may ash descend upon you, until you are crushed.

**28:25** May the Lord hand you over to fall before your enemies; by one way may you go out against them, and by seven flee, and be scattered through all the kingdoms of the earth,

**28:26** and may your corpse be food for all the birds of the sky and beasts of the earth, and may there be no one to drive them away.

**28:27** May the Lord strike you with the ulcer of Egypt, and strike the part of the body through which excrement is discharged, with scab and itching as well, so that you cannot be cured.

**28:28** May the Lord strike you with madness and blindness and frenzy of mind,

**28:29** and may you grope at midday just as a blind man is accustomed to grope in darkness, and not direct your ways. And at all times may you endure false accusation and be oppressed by violence, and have no one to rescue you.

**28:30** May you take a wife, and another man sleep with her. May you build a house, and not live in it. May you plant a vineyard, and not harvest its grapes.

**28:31** May your ox be slaughtered before you, and you not eat from it. May your donkey be seized in your sight, and not be returned to you. May your sheep be given to your enemies, and may there be no one to help you.

**28:32** May your sons and your daughters be handed over to another people, while your eyes look on and fail at the sight of them all day long, and may there be no strength in your hand.

**28:33** May a people whom you do not know eat the fruits of your land and all your labors, and may you always be enduring false accusation and oppressed every day,

**28:34** and stunned at the terror of the things that your eyes will see.

**28:35** May the Lord strike you with a most severe ulcer on the knees and on the calves, and may you not be able to be healed from the sole of the foot all the way to the crown of your head.

**28:36** The Lord will lead you, and your king whom you set over yourself, into a nation that you and your fathers do not know; and there you will serve foreign gods, wood and stone.

**28:37** And you will be ruined, a proverb and a tale to all the peoples to whom the Lord will bring you.

**28:38** You will cast much seed into the ground, and you will gather little, because the locusts will devour everything.

**28:39** You will plant a vineyard and dig it, and you will not drink wine nor gather anything from it, because it will be laid waste by worms.

**28:40** You will have olive trees in all your borders, and you will not anoint yourself with oil, because they will drop off and perish.

**28:41** You will father sons and daughters, and you will not enjoy them, because they will be led into captivity.

**28:42** Mildew will consume all your trees and the crops of your land.

**28:43** The stranger who dwells with you in the land will rise above you and be higher, while you will go down and be lower.

**28:44** He will lend to you, and you will not lend to him. He will be the head, and you will be the tail.

**28:45** And all these curses will come upon you, and pursuing you they will take hold of you until you perish, because you did not listen to the voice of the Lord your God, nor keep His commandments and ceremonies which He commanded you.

**28:46** And they will be signs and wonders upon you, and upon your offspring forever,

**28:47** because you did not serve the Lord your God in joy and gladness of heart, on account of the abundance of all things.

**28:48** You will serve your enemy, whom the Lord will send against you, in hunger and thirst and nakedness and all scarcity; and he will place an iron yoke upon your neck until he crushes you.

**28:49** The Lord will bring upon you a nation from afar, and from the farthest ends of the earth, in the likeness of an eagle flying with a rush, whose language you cannot understand,

**28:50** a most brazen nation, which will show no deference to an old man nor have mercy on a little child,

**28:51** and will devour the fruit of your livestock and the crops of your land until you perish, and will not leave you wheat, wine, and oil, herds of oxen and flocks of sheep, until it destroys you,

**28:52** and crushes you in all your cities, and your strong and lofty walls, in which you had trust in all your land, are torn down. You will be besieged within your gates in all your land, which the Lord your God will give you,

**28:53** and you will eat the fruit of your womb and the flesh of your sons and your daughters, whom the Lord your God has given you, in the distress and devastation with which your enemy will oppress you.

**28:54** The delicate and very self-indulgent man among you will begrudge his brother and the wife who lies in his bosom,

**28:55** so as not to give them any of the flesh of his sons that he will eat, because he has nothing else in the siege and scarcity with which your enemies will devastate you within all your gates.

**28:56** The tender and delicate woman, who was not able to walk upon the earth nor set the sole of her foot because of her excessive softness and tenderness, will begrudge her husband, who lies in her bosom, the flesh of her son and daughter,

**28:57** and the discharge of the afterbirth that comes out from between her thighs, and the children who were born in that very hour. For they will eat them secretly because of the scarcity of all things in the siege and devastation with which your enemy will oppress you within your gates.

**28:58** If you do not keep and do all the words of this law which are written in this scroll, and fear His glorious and fearsome name, that is, the Lord your God,

**28:59** the Lord will increase your plagues and the plagues of your offspring, great and persistent plagues, most severe and perpetual sicknesses,

**28:60** and He will turn upon you all the afflictions of Egypt, which you feared, and they will cling to you.

**28:61** Furthermore, the Lord will bring upon you all ailments and plagues that are not written in the scroll of this law, until He crushes you,

**28:62** and you will remain few in number, who previously were like the stars of the sky for multitude, because you did not listen to the voice of the Lord your God.

**28:63** And just as the Lord previously rejoiced over you, doing good to you and multiplying you, so He will rejoice in destroying you and overthrowing you, so that you will be taken away from the land that you will enter to possess.

**28:64** The Lord will scatter you among all peoples, from the extremity of the earth all the way to its borders; and there you will serve foreign gods, whom neither you nor your fathers know, wood and stones.

**28:65** Among those nations also you will not rest, nor will there be rest for the sole of your foot. For there the Lord will give you a fearful heart, and failing eyes, and a soul consumed with sorrow,

**28:66** and your life will be as though hanging before you. You will be afraid night and day, and you will not trust in your life.

**28:67** In the morning you will say, "Who would give me evening?" and in the evening, "Who would give me morning?" because of the dread of your heart with which you will be terrified, and because of the things that you will see with your eyes.

**28:68** The Lord will bring you back in ships to Egypt by the way of which He told you that you should see it no more. There you will be sold to your enemies as male and female slaves, and there will be no one to buy.

**28:69** These are the words of the covenant which the Lord commanded Moses to make with the sons of Israel in the land of Moab, besides that covenant which He made with them at Horeb.

## Translator Notes

- **28:5** — The Vulgate reads horrea tua ('your granaries', singular horreum in v. 17) and reliquiæ tuæ ('your remnants'), where the Hebrew has 'your basket' and 'your kneading trough' (taken by both the Septuagint and Vulgate from the related root meaning 'to remain').
- **28:20** — The Vulgate reads famem et esuriem ('famine and hunger') where the Hebrew has 'curse and confusion/panic'; the closing words reliquisti me ('you have forsaken Me') shift abruptly into the divine first person.
- **28:21** — From v. 21 through v. 35 the Vulgate shifts from the future indicative of vv. 1-20 to the optative/jussive present subjunctive (Adjungat, Percutiat, Sit, etc.), translated 'May...' to preserve the imprecatory curse mood of the Latin.
- **28:22** — The Vulgate renders these afflictions distinctively as egestate ('destitution'), frigore ('cold'), and aëre corrupto ('corrupted air') where the Hebrew has 'consumption', 'inflammation', and 'blight', and uses singular persequatur ('may He pursue you') with the Lord as subject.
- **28:27** — The Vulgate gives an explicit anatomical periphrasis, partem corporis, per quam stercora egeruntur ('the part of the body through which excrement is discharged'), for the Hebrew term usually translated 'tumors' or 'hemorrhoids'.
- **28:42** — The Vulgate reads rubigo ('mildew', matching rubigine in v. 22), whereas the Hebrew term denotes a species of locust or whirring insect.
- **28:54** — The Vulgate mentions only the man's brother and wife, omitting the third phrase found in the Hebrew ('and the rest of his children whom he has left').
- **28:57** — The Clementine Vulgate has the plural verb Comedent ('they will eat') rather than the Hebrew singular ('she will eat').
- **28:68** — The Vulgate uses third-person dixit ('He told', referring to the Lord) and passive venderis ('you will be sold'), where the Hebrew has first-person 'I said to you' and reflexive 'you will offer yourselves for sale'.
- **28:69** — As in the Hebrew text, this summary verse concludes chapter 28 in the Latin (28:69).
