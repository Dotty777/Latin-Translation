## Text

**9:1** Hear, Israel: you will cross the Jordan today to possess very great nations, stronger than you, huge cities, walled all the way to heaven,

**9:2** a great and tall people, the sons of the Anakim, whom you yourself have seen and heard, whom no one can resist face to face.

**9:3** Therefore you will know today that the Lord your God Himself will cross over before you, a devouring and consuming fire, who will crush them, and wipe them out and destroy them quickly before your face, just as He spoke to you.

**9:4** Do not say in your heart, when the Lord your God has wiped them out in your sight, "Because of my righteousness the Lord has brought me in to possess this land," whereas because of their own wicked deeds those nations have been wiped out.

**9:5** For it is not because of your righteous deeds and the uprightness of your heart that you will enter to possess their lands, but because they acted wickedly, at your entering they have been wiped out; and so that the Lord might fulfill His word, which He promised under oath to your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

**9:6** Know, therefore, that it is not because of your righteous deeds that the Lord your God has given you this excellent land as a possession, since you are a very stiff-necked people.

**9:7** Remember, and do not forget, how you provoked the Lord your God to anger in the wilderness. From the day on which you came out of Egypt up to this place, you have always contended against the Lord.

**9:8** For even at Horeb you provoked Him, and being angry, He wanted to wipe you out,

**9:9** when I went up onto the mountain to receive the stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant that the Lord made with you; and I remained on the mountain for forty days and nights, not eating bread and not drinking water.

**9:10** And the Lord gave me two stone tablets written by the finger of God, and containing all the words that He spoke to you on the mountain from the midst of the fire, when the assembly of the people was gathered together.

**9:11** And when forty days and as many nights had passed, the Lord gave me the two stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant,

**9:12** and said to me, "Rise, and go down from here quickly, because your people, whom you brought out of Egypt, have quickly abandoned the way that you showed them, and they have made a molten image for themselves."

**9:13** And again the Lord said to me, "I see that this people is stiff-necked;

**9:14** leave Me alone, so that I may crush them and wipe out their name from under heaven, and appoint you over a nation that is greater and stronger than this one."

**9:15** And as I was coming down from the burning mountain and was holding the two tablets of the covenant in both hands,

**9:16** and saw that you had sinned against the Lord your God, and had made a molten calf for yourselves, and had quickly abandoned His way, which He had shown you,

**9:17** I threw the tablets from my hands and shattered them in your sight.

**9:18** And I fell down before the Lord as at first, for forty days and nights not eating bread and not drinking water, because of all your sins that you committed against the Lord; and you provoked Him to anger.

**9:19** For I feared His indignation and wrath, with which, stirred up against you, He wanted to wipe you out. And the Lord listened to me this time as well.

**9:20** Fiercely angry against Aaron as well, He wanted to crush him, and I pleaded for him in the same way.

**9:21** And seizing your sin that you had made, that is, the calf, I burned it with fire, and crushing it into pieces and reducing it completely to dust, I threw it into the stream that comes down from the mountain.

**9:22** At the Burning also, and at the Testing, and at the Graves of Craving, you provoked the Lord;

**9:23** and when He sent you from Kadesh-barnea, saying, "Go up, and possess the land that I have given you," you despised the command of the Lord your God, and did not believe Him, nor were you willing to listen to His voice;

**9:24** but you have always been rebellious from the day that I began to know you.

**9:25** And I lay before the Lord for forty days and nights, during which I humbly pleaded with Him that He would not wipe you out, as He had threatened;

**9:26** and praying, I said, "Lord God, do not destroy Your people and Your inheritance, whom You redeemed in Your greatness, whom You brought out of Egypt with a strong hand.

**9:27** Remember Your servants Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; do not look upon the hardness of this people, nor its wickedness and sin,

**9:28** so that the inhabitants of the land from which You brought us out may not perhaps say, 'The Lord was not able to bring them into the land that He promised them, and He hated them; therefore He brought them out to kill them in the wilderness.'

**9:29** They are Your people and Your inheritance, whom You brought out in Your great strength and with Your outstretched arm."

## Translator Notes

- **9:2** — The Vulgate reads "whom you yourself have seen and heard, whom no one can resist face to face" (quos ipse vidisti et audisti, quibus nullus potest ex adverso resistere), whereas the Hebrew text reads "whom you know and of whom you have heard it said, 'Who can stand before the sons of Anak?'"
- **9:12** — The Vulgate reads "the way that you showed them" (viam, quam demonstrasti eis), referring to Moses in the second person, where the Hebrew has "the way that I commanded them" (compare 9:16, "which He had shown you").
- **9:14** — The Vulgate reads "appoint you over a nation" (constituam te super gentem) rather than the Hebrew "make you into a nation."
- **9:22** — The Vulgate translates the three Hebrew wilderness place names (Taberah, Massah, and Kibroth-hattaavah) etymologically into Latin as "the Burning" (incendium), "the Testing" (tentatio), and "the Graves of Craving" (Sepulchra concupiscentiae).
