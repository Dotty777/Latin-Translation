## Text

**7:1** When the Lord your God brings you into the land that you will enter to possess, and wipes out many nations before you, the Hittites, the Girgashites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, seven nations far greater in number than you are and stronger than you,

**7:2** and the Lord your God hands them over to you, you shall strike them down to utter destruction. You shall not enter into a covenant with them, nor show mercy to them,

**7:3** nor shall you form marriages with them. You shall not give your daughter to his son, nor shall you take his daughter for your son,

**7:4** for she will lead your son astray, so that he does not follow Me and instead serves foreign gods; and the fury of the Lord will be enraged, and He will wipe you out quickly.

**7:5** Rather, you shall do these things to them: overturn their altars, and shatter the statues, and cut down the groves, and burn up the carved images.

**7:6** For you are a holy people to the Lord your God. The Lord your God has chosen you so that you may be a special people to Him out of all the peoples who are on the earth.

**7:7** Not because you surpassed all the nations in number did the Lord join Himself to you and choose you, since you are fewer than all the peoples,

**7:8** but because the Lord loved you and kept the oath that He swore to your fathers; and He brought you out with a strong hand and redeemed you from the house of slavery, from the hand of Pharaoh, king of Egypt.

**7:9** And you will know that the Lord your God, He Himself is the mighty and faithful God, keeping covenant and mercy for those who love Him and for those who keep His precepts, to a thousand generations,

**7:10** and repaying those who hate Him immediately, so as to destroy them and delay no longer, at once paying them back what they deserve.

**7:11** Keep, therefore, the precepts and ceremonies and judgments that I command you today to do.

**7:12** If, after you have heard these judgments, you keep them and do them, the Lord your God will also keep for you the covenant and the mercy that He swore to your fathers,

**7:13** and He will love you and multiply you, and He will bless the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your land, your grain, vintage, oil, herds, and the flocks of your sheep, on the land about which He swore to your fathers that He would give it to you.

**7:14** You will be blessed among all peoples. There will not be among you any barren one of either sex, both among people and among your flocks.

**7:15** The Lord will take away every sickness from you; and the worst diseases of Egypt, which you know, He will not bring upon you, but upon all your enemies.

**7:16** You will devour all the peoples whom the Lord your God is about to give to you. Your eye shall not spare them, nor shall you serve their gods, so that they may not become your ruin.

**7:17** If you say in your heart, "These nations are more numerous than I; how will I be able to wipe them out?"

**7:18** do not be afraid, but remember what the Lord your God did to Pharaoh and to all the Egyptians,

**7:19** the very great plagues that your eyes saw, and the signs and wonders, and the strong hand and outstretched arm, so that the Lord your God might bring you out; so He will do to all the peoples whom you dread.

**7:20** Moreover, the Lord your God will also send hornets among them, until He wipes out and destroys all who have fled from you and have been able to hide.

**7:21** You shall not fear them, for the Lord your God is in your midst, a great and terrifying God.

**7:22** He Himself will consume these nations in your sight little by little and in parts. You will not be able to wipe them out all at once, so that the beasts of the land might not multiply against you.

**7:23** And the Lord your God will give them over in your sight, and He will put them to death until they are completely wiped out.

**7:24** And He will hand their kings over into your hands, and you will destroy their names under heaven; no one will be able to resist you until you crush them.

**7:25** You shall burn up their carved images with fire; you shall not covet the silver and gold from which they are made, nor shall you take anything from them for yourself, so that you do not stumble, because it is an abomination to your Lord;

**7:26** nor shall you bring anything from an idol into your house, so that you do not become anathema, just as it also is. You shall detest it like filth, and you shall hold it in abomination like defilement and grime, because it is anathema.

## Translator Notes

- **7:5** — The Vulgate renders the Canaanite cult objects as statuas ("statues") and lucos ("groves"), whereas the Hebrew text refers to sacred pillars and Asherah poles.
- **7:10** — The Vulgate expands the Hebrew idiom "to his face" into the explicit temporal rendering statim ... protinus eis restituens quod merentur ("immediately ... at once paying them back what they deserve").
- **7:23** — The Vulgate reads et interficiet illos ("and He will put them to death"), whereas the Hebrew text reads "and throw them into great confusion."
- **7:25** — This Latin text reads Domini tui ("your Lord") without Dei ("God"), and reads de quibus facta sunt ("from which they are made") rather than the Hebrew "which is on them."
- **7:26** — The Vulgate specifies ex idolo ("from an idol", vs. Hebrew "an abomination") and retains the loanword anathema, denoting something placed under a divine ban and devoted to destruction.
