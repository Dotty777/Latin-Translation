## Text

**27:1** And Moses and the elders of Israel commanded the people, saying, "Observe every commandment that I command you today.

**27:2** And when you have crossed the Jordan into the land that the Lord your God will give you, you will set up huge stones and smooth them over with lime,

**27:3** so that you may be able to write on them all the words of this law, once the Jordan has been crossed, so that you may enter the land that the Lord your God will give you, a land flowing with milk and honey, just as He swore to your fathers.

**27:4** Therefore, when you have crossed the Jordan, set up on Mount Ebal the stones that I command you today, and you will smooth them over with lime;

**27:5** and you will build an altar there to the Lord your God of stones that iron has not touched,

**27:6** and of unshaped and unpolished rocks; and you will offer on it burnt offerings to the Lord your God,

**27:7** and you will sacrifice peace offerings, and you will eat there and feast before the Lord your God.

**27:8** And you will write on the stones all the words of this law plainly and clearly."

**27:9** And Moses and the priests of Levitical lineage said to all Israel, "Pay attention and listen, Israel: today you have become the people of the Lord your God;

**27:10** you will listen to His voice, and you will carry out the commandments and righteous decrees that I command you."

**27:11** And Moses commanded the people on that day, saying,

**27:12** "These will stand to bless the people on Mount Gerizim, once the Jordan has been crossed: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin.

**27:13** And on the opposite side, these will stand to curse on Mount Ebal: Reuben, Gad, and Asher, and Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali.

**27:14** And the Levites will declare and say to all the men of Israel with a loud voice:

**27:15** 'Cursed is the man who makes a carved and cast image, an abomination to the Lord, the work of the hands of craftsmen, and will set it in a hidden place.' And all the people will answer and say, 'Amen.'

**27:16** 'Cursed is the one who does not honor his father and mother.' And all the people will say, 'Amen.'

**27:17** 'Cursed is the one who moves the boundaries of his neighbor.' And all the people will say, 'Amen.'

**27:18** 'Cursed is the one who causes a blind man to go astray on the road.' And all the people will say, 'Amen.'

**27:19** 'Cursed is the one who perverts the justice of the stranger, the orphan, and the widow.' And all the people will say, 'Amen.'

**27:20** 'Cursed is the one who sleeps with the wife of his father and uncovers the covering of his bed.' And all the people will say, 'Amen.'

**27:21** 'Cursed is the one who sleeps with any animal.' And all the people will say, 'Amen.'

**27:22** 'Cursed is the one who sleeps with his sister, the daughter of his father or of his mother.' And all the people will say, 'Amen.'

**27:23** 'Cursed is the one who sleeps with his mother-in-law.' And all the people will say, 'Amen.'

**27:24** 'Cursed is the one who secretly strikes down his neighbor.' And all the people will say, 'Amen.'

**27:25** 'Cursed is the one who accepts bribes to strike down a life of innocent blood.' And all the people will say, 'Amen.'

**27:26** 'Cursed is the one who does not remain in the words of this law, and does not fulfill them in deed.' And all the people will say, 'Amen.'"

## Translator Notes

- **27:3** — The Vulgate reads 'sicut juravit patribus tuis' ("just as He swore to your fathers"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "just as the Lord, the God of your fathers, promised you."
- **27:6** — The Vulgate attaches 'et de saxis informibus et impolitis' ("and of unshaped and unpolished rocks") directly to the altar-building clause of verse 5 (which reads "that iron has not touched"), rather than repeating "you shall build the altar of the Lord your God" as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **27:7** — The Vulgate reads 'et epulaberis' ("and you will feast"), highlighting the sacred banquet, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "and you shall rejoice."
- **27:20** — The Vulgate reads 'et revelat operimentum lectuli ejus' ("and uncovers the covering of his bed"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "because he has uncovered the skirt of his father."
- **27:26** — The Vulgate reads 'non permanet in sermonibus legis hujus, nec eos opere perficit' ("does not remain in the words of this law, and does not fulfill them in deed"), aligning with the Septuagint and Galatians 3:10 rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "does not uphold the words of this law to do them."
