## Text

**4:1** And now, Israel, listen to the precepts and judgments that I teach you, so that, doing them, you may live, and entering in, you may possess the land that the Lord God of your fathers is about to give to you.

**4:2** You must not add to the word that I am speaking to you, nor subtract from it; keep the commandments of the Lord your God that I command you.

**4:3** Your eyes have seen all that the Lord did against Baal-peor, how He crushed all his worshipers from your midst.

**4:4** But you who hold fast to the Lord your God are all alive to the present day.

**4:5** You know that I have taught you precepts and just decrees, just as the Lord my God commanded me; so you will do them in the land that you are about to possess,

**4:6** and you will observe and fulfill them in deed. For this is your wisdom and understanding before the peoples, so that, hearing all these precepts, they may say, "Look, a wise and understanding people, a great nation."

**4:7** Nor is there another nation so great that has gods drawing near to it, as our God is present at all our pleas.

**4:8** For what other nation is so renowned that it has ceremonies, just judgments, and the entire law that I will set before your eyes today?

**4:9** Guard yourself, therefore, and your soul carefully. Do not forget the words that your eyes have seen, and do not let them slip from your heart all the days of your life. You will teach them to your sons and grandsons,

**4:10** from the day on which you stood before the Lord your God at Horeb, when the Lord spoke to me, saying, "Gather the people to Me, so that they may hear My words, and learn to fear Me all the time that they live on the earth, and teach their sons."

**4:11** And you came near to the foot of the mountain, which was burning up to heaven; and on it were darkness, cloud, and dense gloom.

**4:12** And the Lord spoke to you from the midst of the fire. You heard the sound of His words, and you saw no form at all.

**4:13** And He showed you His covenant, which He commanded you to do, and the ten words, which He wrote on two stone tablets.

**4:14** And at that time He commanded me to teach you the ceremonies and judgments that you ought to do in the land that you are about to possess.

**4:15** Guard your souls carefully, therefore. You did not see any likeness on the day when the Lord spoke to you at Horeb from the midst of the fire,

**4:16** so that you might not be deceived and make for yourselves a carved likeness, or an image of male or female,

**4:17** a likeness of all beasts that are on the earth, or of birds flying under heaven,

**4:18** and of crawling creatures that move on the earth, or of fish that dwell beneath the earth in the waters;

**4:19** so that, lifting your eyes to heaven, you might not see the sun and the moon and all the stars of heaven, and, deceived by error, worship them and serve what the Lord your God created for the service of all the nations that are under heaven.

**4:20** You, however, the Lord took and led out of the iron furnace of Egypt, so that He might have a people of inheritance, as it is on the present day.

**4:21** And the Lord became angry against me because of your words, and swore that I would not cross the Jordan, nor enter the finest land that He is about to give to you.

**4:22** Look, I am dying on this soil; I will not cross the Jordan; you will cross and possess the excellent land.

**4:23** Be careful that you never forget the covenant of the Lord your God, which He made with you, nor make for yourself a carved likeness of those things that the Lord has forbidden to be made;

**4:24** because the Lord your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God.

**4:25** If you have fathered sons and grandsons, and have remained in the land, and, being deceived, have made for yourselves any likeness, committing evil before the Lord your God so as to provoke Him to anger,

**4:26** I call upon heaven and earth today as witnesses that you will swiftly perish from the land which, after crossing the Jordan, you are about to possess; you will not live in it for a long time, but the Lord will destroy you,

**4:27** and scatter you among all the peoples, and you will remain few among the nations to which the Lord is about to lead you.

**4:28** And there you will serve gods that have been crafted by the hand of men, wood and stone that do not see, nor hear, nor eat, nor smell.

**4:29** And when you seek the Lord your God there, you will find Him, provided that you seek Him with your whole heart and with all the affliction of your soul.

**4:30** After all the things that were foretold have found you, in the last time you will return to the Lord your God and listen to His voice.

**4:31** For the Lord your God is a merciful God; He will not abandon you, nor utterly destroy you, nor will He forget the covenant in which He swore to your fathers.

**4:32** Inquire about the ancient days that were before you, from the day on which God created man upon the earth, from one end of heaven to the other, whether a thing of this kind has ever happened, or has ever been known,

**4:33** that a people should hear the voice of God speaking from the midst of the fire, just as you heard and lived;

**4:34** whether God has acted so as to go in and take a nation for Himself from the midst of the nations, through trials, signs, and wonders, through battle and a strong hand and an outstretched arm, and terrifying visions, according to all that the Lord your God did for you in Egypt while your eyes looked on;

**4:35** so that you might know that the Lord Himself is God, and there is no other besides Him.

**4:36** From heaven He caused you to hear His voice, so that He might teach you, and on earth He showed you His very great fire, and you heard His words from the midst of the fire,

**4:37** because He loved your fathers and chose their seed after them. And going ahead in His great power, He led you out of Egypt,

**4:38** so that He might destroy nations very great and stronger than you at your entry, and bring you in, and give you their land as a possession, as you see on the present day.

**4:39** Know, therefore, today, and reflect in your heart that the Lord Himself is God in heaven above and on earth below, and there is no other.

**4:40** Keep His precepts and commandments, which I command you, so that it may go well for you and for your sons after you, and that you may remain for a long time upon the land that the Lord your God is about to give to you.

**4:41** Then Moses set apart three cities beyond the Jordan toward the eastern region,

**4:42** so that one who has unintentionally killed his neighbor, and was not his enemy a day or two before, might flee to them and be able to escape to one of these cities:

**4:43** Bezer in the wilderness, which is situated in the level land of the tribe of Reuben; and Ramoth in Gilead, which is in the tribe of Gad; and Golan in Bashan, which is in the tribe of Manasseh.

**4:44** This is the law that Moses set before the sons of Israel,

**4:45** and these are the testimonies and ceremonies and judgments that he spoke to the sons of Israel when they came out of Egypt,

**4:46** beyond the Jordan in the valley opposite the shrine of Peor, in the land of Sihon, the Amorite king, who lived in Heshbon, whom Moses struck down. The sons of Israel also, having come out of Egypt,

**4:47** took possession of his land and the land of Og, king of Bashan, the two kings of the Amorites, who were beyond the Jordan toward the rising of the sun,

**4:48** from Aroer, which is situated on the bank of the stream of Arnon, as far as Mount Sion, which is also Hermon,

**4:49** all the plain beyond the Jordan toward the eastern region, as far as the sea of the wilderness, and as far as the foot of Mount Pisgah.

## Translator Notes

- **4:5** — The Vulgate reads Scitis ("You know"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "See/Look."
- **4:19** — The Vulgate reads quæ creavit ... in ministerium ("what the Lord your God created for the service"), whereas the Hebrew reads "which the Lord your God allotted" to all the peoples.
- **4:29** — The Vulgate reads tota tribulatione animæ tuæ ("with all the affliction of your soul") where the Hebrew has simply "with all your soul."
- **4:46** — The Vulgate renders Beth-peor as fanum Phogor ("the shrine of Peor") and ends the sentence after Moyses., making "The sons of Israel also, having come out of Egypt" the subject of "took possession" in verse 47 rather than pairing them with Moses in striking down Sihon.
- **4:48** — Latin 'montem Sion' renders Mount Sion, an alternative name for Mount Hermon in Transjordan (cf. Deut 3:9) — distinct from Mount Zion in Jerusalem.
