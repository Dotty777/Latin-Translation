## Text

**2:1** And setting out from there, we came into the wilderness that leads to the Red Sea, just as the Lord had said to me; and we went around Mount Seir for a long time.

**2:2** And the Lord said to me:

**2:3** "It is enough for you to go around this mountain; go toward the north,

**2:4** and command the people, saying, 'You will pass through the territory of your brothers, the sons of Esau, who live in Seir, and they will fear you.

**2:5** Watch carefully, therefore, that you do not move against them. For I will not give you as much of their land as the sole of one foot can tread on, because I have given Mount Seir to Esau as a possession.

**2:6** You will buy food from them with money and eat; you will draw water that you have bought and drink.'"

**2:7** The Lord your God has blessed you in every work of your hands; He knows your journey, how you passed through this great wilderness, the Lord your God living with you for forty years, and nothing was lacking to you.

**2:8** And when we had passed by our brothers, the sons of Esau, who lived in Seir, by the way of the plain from Elath and from Ezion-geber, we came to the road that leads into the desert of Moab.

**2:9** And the Lord said to me, "Do not fight against the Moabites, nor enter into battle against them; for I will not give you anything from their land, because I have handed Ar over to the sons of Lot as a possession."

**2:10** The Emim were its first inhabitants, a great and strong people, and so tall that, as of the lineage of the Anakim,

**2:11** they were thought to be like giants and were like the sons of the Anakim. Indeed, the Moabites call them Emim.

**2:12** Now the Horites previously lived in Seir; after they were driven out and destroyed, the sons of Esau lived there, just as Israel did in the land of its possession, which the Lord gave to it.

**2:13** Rising, therefore, to cross the stream of Zered, we came to it.

**2:14** Now the time during which we walked from Kadesh-barnea until the crossing of the stream of Zered was thirty-eight years, until the entire generation of the men of war was consumed from the camp, just as the Lord had sworn,

**2:15** whose hand was against them, so that they would perish from the middle of the camp.

**2:16** Now after all the warriors had fallen,

**2:17** the Lord spoke to me, saying:

**2:18** "Today you will cross the borders of Moab, the city named Ar;

**2:19** and approaching the vicinity of the sons of Ammon, be careful not to fight against them, nor move to battle; for I will not give you any of the land of the sons of Ammon, because I have given it to the sons of Lot as a possession."

**2:20** It was considered a land of giants; and giants formerly lived in it, whom the Ammonites call Zamzummim,

**2:21** a great and numerous people, and of tall stature, like the Anakim, whom the Lord destroyed from before their face; and He made them live in their place,

**2:22** just as He had done for the sons of Esau, who live in Seir, destroying the Horites and handing their land over to them, which they possess to the present.

**2:23** The Cappadocians also drove out the Hivites, who lived in Hazerim as far as Gaza; coming out of Cappadocia, they destroyed them and lived in their place.

**2:24** "Rise and cross the stream of Arnon; look, I have handed over into your hand Sihon king of Heshbon, the Amorite, and begin to possess his land, and engage in battle against him.

**2:25** Today I will begin to send the terror and dread of you upon the peoples who live under the whole heaven, so that when your name is heard, they will be terrified, and tremble like women in labor, and be gripped by pain."

**2:26** Therefore I sent messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon with peaceful words, saying:

**2:27** "We will pass through your land; we will walk along the public road; we will not turn aside either to the right or to the left.

**2:28** Sell us provisions for a price so that we may eat; supply water for money, and so we will drink. Only this, that you grant us passage,

**2:29** just as the sons of Esau, who live in Seir, and the Moabites, who reside in Ar, did, until we come to the Jordan and cross over to the land that the Lord our God is about to give us."

**2:30** And Sihon king of Heshbon was unwilling to give us passage, because the Lord your God had hardened his spirit and made his heart obstinate, so that he would be handed over into your hands, just as you see now.

**2:31** And the Lord said to me, "Look, I have begun to hand Sihon and his land over to you; begin to possess it."

**2:32** And Sihon came out to meet us with all his people for battle at Jahaz.

**2:33** And the Lord our God handed him over to us; and we struck him down with his sons and all his people.

**2:34** And we captured all the cities at that time, having killed their inhabitants, men and women and little ones; we left nothing in them,

**2:35** apart from the livestock, which came as the share of the plunderers, and the spoils of the cities that we captured

**2:36** from Aroer, which is on the bank of the stream of Arnon, the town that is situated in the valley, as far as Gilead. There was not a village or city that escaped our hands; the Lord our God handed all of them over to us,

**2:37** apart from the land of the sons of Ammon, which we did not approach, and all that adjoins the stream of Jabbok, and the mountain cities, and all the places from which the Lord our God barred us.

## Translator Notes

- **2:11** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew Rephaim throughout chapters 2-3 (2:11, 2:20; 3:11, 3:13) as gigantes ("giants"), and here explicitly links the Emim's stature to the Anakim with the result clause "so tall that, as of the lineage of the Anakim, they were thought to be like giants."
- **2:13** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, where God commands Israel to rise and cross the brook Zered before Moses reports that they crossed it, the Vulgate presents the entire verse as Moses' narrative: "Rising, therefore, to cross the stream of Zered, we came to it."
- **2:23** — Following the Septuagint tradition, the Vulgate preserves three distinctive proper names here: Hevaeos ("Hivites", where the Hebrew has "Avvim"), Haserim treated as a place name ("Hazerim", Hebrew "villages"), and Cappadoces / Cappadocia ("Cappadocians" / "Cappadocia", where the Hebrew has "Caphtorim" / "Caphtor").
- **2:25** — The Vulgate expands the Hebrew verb for writhing in anguish into an explicit simile, in morem parturientium ("like women in labor").
