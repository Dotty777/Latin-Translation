## Text

**32:1** Hear, heavens, what I speak; let the earth hear the words of my mouth.

**32:2** May my teaching condense like rain, may my speech flow like dew, like a shower upon vegetation, and like drops upon the grass.

**32:3** For I will call upon the name of the Lord; give magnificence to our God.

**32:4** The works of God are perfect, and all His ways are judgments; God is faithful and without any iniquity, just and upright.

**32:5** They have sinned against Him, and are not His children in filth: a crooked and perverse generation.

**32:6** Do you repay these things to the Lord, you foolish and unwise people? Is He not Himself your Father, who possessed you, and made and created you?

**32:7** Remember the ancient days, consider each generation; ask your father, and he will declare it to you; your elders, and they will tell you.

**32:8** When the Most High was dividing the nations, when He was separating the sons of Adam, He established the boundaries of the peoples according to the number of the sons of Israel.

**32:9** But the portion of the Lord is His people; Jacob is the cord of His inheritance.

**32:10** He found him in a desert land, in a place of horror and vast desolation; He led him around and taught him, and guarded him like the pupil of His eye.

**32:11** Like an eagle urging its young to fly and hovering over them, He spread out His wings and took him up, and carried him on His shoulders.

**32:12** The Lord alone was his guide, and there was no foreign god with him.

**32:13** He set him upon a high land, so that he might eat the fruits of the fields, so that he might suck honey from the rock and oil from the hardest stone;

**32:14** butter from the herd and milk from the sheep, with the fat of lambs and of rams, the sons of Bashan, and male goats with the marrow of wheat, and might drink the purest blood of the grape.

**32:15** The beloved grew fat and kicked; grown fat, grown plump, grown broad, he abandoned God his Maker, and departed from God his Savior.

**32:16** They provoked Him with foreign gods, and with abominations they stirred Him up to anger.

**32:17** They sacrificed to demons and not to God, to gods whom they did not know; new and recent ones came, whom their fathers did not worship.

**32:18** You have abandoned the God who fathered you, and have forgotten the Lord your Creator.

**32:19** The Lord saw, and was stirred up to anger, because His sons and daughters provoked Him.

**32:20** And He said, "I will hide My face from them, and consider their final end; for it is a perverse generation, and unfaithful children.

**32:21** They themselves provoked Me with what was not God, and angered Me with their vanities; and I will provoke them with what is not a people, and with a foolish nation I will anger them.

**32:22** A fire has been kindled in My fury, and it will burn all the way to the lowest depths of the underworld; and it will devour the earth with its growth, and burn up the foundations of the mountains.

**32:23** I will gather evils upon them, and I will use up My arrows on them.

**32:24** They will be consumed by famine, and birds will devour them with a most bitter bite; I will send the teeth of beasts against them, with the fury of things crawling upon the earth, and of serpents.

**32:25** Outside, the sword will lay them waste, and inside, terror: the young man together with the virgin, the nursing child with the aged man.

**32:26** I said, 'Where are they? I will make the remembrance of them cease from among mankind.'

**32:27** But because of the wrath of the enemies I delayed, so that their foes might not perhaps become arrogant and say, 'Our exalted hand, and not the Lord, has done all these things.'"

**32:28** It is a nation without counsel, and without prudence.

**32:29** If only they were wise, and understood, and foresaw the final end!

**32:30** How could one pursue a thousand, and two put ten thousand to flight? Is it not for this reason, because their God sold them, and the Lord shut them up?

**32:31** For our God is not like their gods, and our enemies are judges.

**32:32** From the vineyard of Sodom is their vineyard, and from the outskirts of Gomorrah; their grape is a grape of gall, and their clusters are most bitter.

**32:33** Their wine is the gall of dragons, and the incurable venom of asps.

**32:34** "Are not these things stored up with Me, and sealed in My treasuries?

**32:35** Vengeance is Mine, and I will repay in time, so that their foot may slip; the day of destruction is near, and the times are hurrying to arrive."

**32:36** The Lord will judge His people, and will have compassion on His servants; He will see that the hand has been weakened, and those shut up have also failed, and those remaining have been consumed.

**32:37** And He will say, "Where are their gods, in whom they had confidence,

**32:38** from whose sacrifices they ate the fat and drank the wine of drink offerings? Let them rise up and aid you, and protect you in distress.

**32:39** See that I alone am, and there is no other god besides Me: I will kill, and I will make alive; I will strike, and I will heal, and there is no one who can rescue from My hand.

**32:40** I will lift up My hand to heaven and say, 'I live forever.'

**32:41** If I sharpen My sword like lightning, and My hand takes hold of judgment, I will render vengeance to My foes, and I will repay those who hate Me.

**32:42** I will make My arrows drunk with blood, and My sword will devour flesh, from the gore of the killed and from the captivity, of the bared head of the enemies."

**32:43** Praise His people, nations, because He will avenge the blood of His servants, and He will repay vengeance upon their foes, and He will be merciful to the land of His people.

**32:44** So Moses came and spoke all the words of this song in the ears of the people, he himself and Joshua the son of Nun.

**32:45** And he completed all these sayings, speaking to all Israel,

**32:46** and he said to them, "Set your hearts on all the words that I testify to you today, so that you may command your children to keep and do them, and to fulfill all things of this law that are written;

**32:47** for they have not been commanded to you in vain, but so that each one might live in them; and doing these things, you may continue for a long time in the land that, once the Jordan has been crossed, you are entering to possess."

**32:48** And the Lord spoke to Moses on that same day, saying,

**32:49** "Go up onto this mountain Abarim, that is, the Crossing, onto Mount Nebo, which is in the land of Moab opposite Jericho, and see the land of Canaan, which I will hand over to the sons of Israel to obtain, and die on the mountain.

**32:50** Ascending it, you will be joined to your people, just as Aaron your brother died on Mount Hor and was united with his people;

**32:51** because you transgressed against Me in the midst of the sons of Israel at the waters of Contradiction in Kadesh of the wilderness of Zin, and you did not sanctify Me among the sons of Israel.

**32:52** From across, you will see the land that I will give to the sons of Israel, and you will not enter into it."

## Translator Notes

- **32:4** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew divine title "the Rock" as "God" (Dei, and similarly in vv. 15, 18, 30, 31, and 37), and reads plural "works" (opera) and "judgments" (judicia).
- **32:15** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew poetic name for Israel, Jeshurun, as "the beloved" (dilectus), following the Septuagint tradition.
- **32:24** — Where the Hebrew text refers to burning plague or pestilence, the Vulgate renders "birds" (aves), picturing birds of prey devouring the people with a most bitter bite.
- **32:35** — The Vulgate reads "I will repay in time, so that their foot may slip" (et ego retribuam in tempore, ut labatur pes eorum) and plural "times" (tempora), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "vengeance is Mine and recompense, for the time when their foot slips."
- **32:49** — The Vulgate incorporates Jerome's explanatory gloss "that is, the Crossing" (id est, transitum) for the Hebrew name Abarim; similarly in v. 51 the Latin translates Meribah as "the waters of Contradiction" (aquas contradictionis).
