## Text

**12:1** These are the precepts and judgments that you must do in the land that the Lord, the God of your fathers, is about to give you, so that you may possess it all the days that you walk upon the ground.

**12:2** Overthrow all the places in which the nations that you are about to possess worshiped their gods, on high mountains and hills, and under every leafy tree.

**12:3** Tear down their altars and shatter the statues; burn the groves with fire and crush the idols to pieces; destroy their names from those places.

**12:4** You shall not do so to the Lord your God;

**12:5** but you shall come to the place that the Lord your God will choose from all your tribes, to put His name there and to dwell in it,

**12:6** and in that place you shall offer your burnt offerings and sacrifices, tithes and the firstfruits of your hands, and vows and gifts, the firstborn of oxen and sheep.

**12:7** And you shall eat there in the sight of the Lord your God, and you and your households shall rejoice in all the things to which you put your hand, in which the Lord your God has blessed you.

**12:8** You shall not do there the things that we are doing here today, each one what seems right to himself;

**12:9** for up to the present time you have not come to the rest and possession that the Lord your God is about to give you.

**12:10** You will cross the Jordan and live in the land that the Lord your God is about to give you, so that you may rest from all enemies on every side, and may live without any fear

**12:11** in the place that the Lord your God will choose, so that His name may be in it; to that place you shall bring everything that I command, burnt offerings and sacrifices, and tithes and the firstfruits of your hands, and whatever is choicest among the gifts that you will vow to the Lord.

**12:12** There you shall feast before the Lord your God, you and your sons and daughters, male and female servants, and the Levite who resides in your cities; for he has no other share and possession among you.

**12:13** Be careful not to offer your burnt offerings in every place that you see,

**12:14** but in the place that the Lord will choose in one of your tribes, you shall offer sacrifices, and you shall do whatever I command you.

**12:15** But if you wish to eat, and eating meat delights you, slaughter and eat according to the blessing of the Lord your God that He has given you in your cities; whether it is unclean, that is, blemished and weak, or clean, that is, whole and without blemish, which is lawful to offer, you shall eat it just like a roe deer and a stag,

**12:16** only without eating the blood, which you shall pour out upon the ground like water.

**12:17** You may not eat in your towns the tithe of your grain, wine, and oil, the firstborn of the herds and flocks, and all the things that you vow and wish to offer voluntarily, and the firstfruits of your hands;

**12:18** but you shall eat them before the Lord your God in the place that the Lord your God will choose, you and your son and your daughter, and your male servant and female servant, and the Levite who remains in your cities; and you shall rejoice and be refreshed before the Lord your God in all things to which you stretch out your hand.

**12:19** Be careful not to abandon the Levite during all the time that you live in the land.

**12:20** When the Lord your God enlarges your borders, just as He spoke to you, and you wish to eat meat that your soul desires,

**12:21** and if the place that the Lord your God will choose so that His name may be there is far away, you shall slaughter from the herds and flocks that you have, just as I have commanded you, and you shall eat in your towns, as it pleases you.

**12:22** Just as a roe deer and a stag are eaten, so you shall eat them; both the clean and the unclean shall eat together.

**12:23** Be careful of this alone, that you do not eat the blood; for their blood is in place of the life, and therefore you must not eat the life with the meat,

**12:24** but you shall pour it on the ground like water,

**12:25** so that it may go well with you and with your children after you, when you do what is pleasing in the sight of the Lord.

**12:26** But the things that you consecrate and vow to the Lord you shall take, and you shall come to the place that the Lord will choose,

**12:27** and you shall offer your offerings, the meat and the blood, upon the altar of the Lord your God; the blood of the sacrifices you shall pour on the altar, but you yourself shall eat the meat.

**12:28** Observe and hear everything that I command you, so that it may go well with you and with your children after you forever, when you do what is good and pleasing in the sight of the Lord your God.

**12:29** When the Lord your God destroys before your face the nations that you will enter to possess, and you possess them and live in their land,

**12:30** be careful not to imitate them after they have been overthrown as you enter, and not to inquire into their ceremonies, saying, "Just as these nations worshiped their gods, so I also will worship."

**12:31** You shall not do likewise to the Lord your God. For all the abominations that the Lord detests they have done for their gods, offering their sons and daughters and burning them with fire.

## Translator Notes

- **12:3** — The Vulgate renders the Canaanite cult objects as "statuas" ("statues," for Hebrew pillars), "lucos" ("groves," for Hebrew Asherim), and "idola" ("idols"), and uses the plural for both "nomina" ("names") and "locis illis" ("those places").
- **12:10** — The Vulgate joins the end of v. 10 ("habitetis") directly to the ablative phrase beginning v. 11 ("in loco, quem elegerit..."), whereas the Hebrew concludes the sentence at v. 10 and opens a new clause at v. 11.
- **12:15** — The Vulgate treats "immundum" and "mundum" as neuter adjectives referring to the animal and inserts explanatory glosses ("that is, blemished and weak" and "that is, whole and without blemish, which is lawful to offer"), whereas the Hebrew refers to ceremonially unclean and clean persons eating the meat (as the Vulgate itself does with masculine forms in v. 22).
- **12:23** — Latin "pro anima" ("in place of the life," where "anima" can mean "life" or "soul") softens the Hebrew's direct identification ("the blood is the life").
- **12:30** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew "be ensnared after them" as "imiteris eas" ("imitate them") and translates "before you" with the ablative absolute "te ... introëunte" ("as you enter").
