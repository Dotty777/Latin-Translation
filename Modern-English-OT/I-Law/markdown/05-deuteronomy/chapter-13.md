## Text

**13:1** What I command you, do this alone for the Lord; neither add anything nor diminish it.

**13:2** If a prophet rises up in your midst, or someone who says that he has seen a dream, and he foretells a sign and a portent,

**13:3** and what he spoke comes about, and he says to you, "Let us go and follow foreign gods whom you do not know, and let us serve them,"

**13:4** you shall not listen to the words of that prophet or dreamer, because the Lord your God is testing you, so that it may become evident whether you love Him or not, with all your heart and with all your soul.

**13:5** Follow the Lord your God, and fear Him, and keep His commandments, and listen to His voice; Him you shall serve, and to Him you shall cling.

**13:6** But that prophet or fabricator of dreams shall be put to death, because he spoke to turn you away from the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt and redeemed you from the house of slavery, in order to make you stray from the way that the Lord your God commanded you; and you shall remove the evil from your midst.

**13:7** If your brother, the son of your mother, or your son or daughter, or the wife who is in your embrace, or a friend whom you love as your own soul, wants to persuade you, saying secretly, "Let us go and serve foreign gods," whom you and your fathers do not know,

**13:8** of all the nations all around, which are near or far, from the beginning to the end of the earth,

**13:9** do not consent to him or listen to him, nor let your eye spare him so that you take pity and conceal him,

**13:10** but you shall put him to death immediately; let your hand be upon him first, and afterward let all the people stretch out their hand.

**13:11** Overwhelmed with stones, he shall be put to death, because he sought to draw you away from the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery,

**13:12** so that all Israel, hearing of it, may fear, and never again do anything like this.

**13:13** If you hear in one of your cities, which the Lord your God will give you to dwell in, some people saying,

**13:14** "Sons of Belial have gone out from your midst and have turned away the inhabitants of their city, and have said, 'Let us go and serve foreign gods whom you do not know,'"

**13:15** inquire carefully and diligently; and once the truth of the matter has been thoroughly examined, if you find that what is said is certain and that this abomination has been committed in deed,

**13:16** you shall immediately strike the inhabitants of that city with the edge of the sword, and you shall destroy it and everything that is in it, down to the livestock.

**13:17** Also, whatever household goods there may be, you shall gather into the middle of its streets and burn them together with the city itself, so that you consume everything for the Lord your God, and it may be an everlasting mound. It shall not be built again,

**13:18** and nothing from that accursed thing shall cling to your hand, so that the Lord may turn away from the anger of His fury, and have mercy on you, and multiply you just as He swore to your fathers,

**13:19** when you listen to the voice of the Lord your God, keeping all His precepts that I command you today, so that you do what is pleasing in the sight of the Lord your God.

## Translator Notes

- **13:1** — In the Vulgate, chapter 13 begins with this verse (numbered 12:32 in Hebrew and most modern English Bibles), shifting the rest of the chapter's numbering by one. Latin "do this alone for the Lord" (hoc tantum facito Domino) adds "for the Lord" and "alone" where the Hebrew reads "observe to do all the word."
- **13:6** — Latin "fictor somniorum" ("fabricator of dreams") replaces the neutral "somniator" ("dreamer") of 13:4, emphasizing deliberate deceit.
- **13:14** — Latin "filii Belial" ("sons of Belial") renders the Hebrew idiom for worthless or wicked people using the personal-name form Belial.
- **13:18** — Latin "anathemate" ("accursed thing") translates Hebrew herem, referring to anything irrevocably devoted to God for total destruction under the ban.
