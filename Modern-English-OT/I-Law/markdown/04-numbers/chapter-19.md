## Text

**19:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**19:2** "This is the rite of the sacrifice that the Lord has established. Command the sons of Israel to bring to you a red cow of full age, in which there is no blemish and that has not borne a yoke;

**19:3** and you shall hand her over to Eleazar the priest, who, after she has been led outside the camp, shall sacrifice her in the sight of all;

**19:4** and dipping his finger in her blood, he shall sprinkle toward the doors of the tabernacle seven times,

**19:5** and he shall burn her while all look on, with both her hide and her flesh as well as her blood and dung committed to the flame.

**19:6** The priest shall also cast cedar wood, and hyssop, and twice-dyed scarlet into the flame that devours the cow.

**19:7** And only then, having washed his clothes and his body, shall he enter the camp, and he will be tainted until evening.

**19:8** Moreover, the one who has burned her shall wash his clothes and body, and he will be unclean until evening.

**19:9** Now a clean man shall gather the ashes of the cow and pour them out outside the camp in a most pure place, so that they may be for safekeeping and for water of sprinkling for the multitude of the sons of Israel, because the cow was burned for sin.

**19:10** And when the one who had carried the ashes of the cow has washed his clothes, he will be unclean until evening. The sons of Israel, and the foreigners who dwell among them, shall hold this sacred by a perpetual law.

**19:11** Whoever touches the corpse of a human being, and because of this is unclean for seven days,

**19:12** shall be sprinkled with this water on the third and the seventh day, and so will be made clean. If he has not been sprinkled on the third day, on the seventh he will not be able to be made clean.

**19:13** Everyone who touches the dead body of a human soul and has not been sprinkled with this mixture will defile the tabernacle of the Lord and will perish from Israel; because he has not been sprinkled with the water of expiation, he will be unclean, and his filth will remain upon him.

**19:14** This is the law of a man who dies in a tent: all who enter his tent, and all the vessels that are there, will be defiled for seven days.

**19:15** A vessel that has neither a cover nor a fastening over it will be unclean.

**19:16** If anyone in the field touches the corpse of a man who was killed or who died naturally, or a bone of his, or a grave, he will be unclean for seven days.

**19:17** And they shall take some of the ashes of the burning and of sin, and pour living waters over them into a vessel;

**19:18** and when a clean man has dipped hyssop into them, he shall sprinkle the entire tent with it, and all the furnishings, and the people defiled by a contagion of this kind;

**19:19** and in this way the clean man shall purify the unclean man on the third and the seventh day; and once cleansed on the seventh day, he shall wash both himself and his clothes, and he will be unclean until evening.

**19:20** If anyone has not been cleansed by this rite, his soul will perish from the midst of the assembly, because he has defiled the sanctuary of the Lord and has not been sprinkled with the water of purification.

**19:21** This shall be an everlasting lawful ordinance. He also who sprinkles the waters shall wash his clothes. Everyone who touches the waters of expiation will be unclean until evening.

**19:22** Whatever an unclean person touches, he will make unclean; and the soul that touches any of these things will be unclean until evening."

## Translator Notes

- **19:2** — Latin religio victimae ("rite of the sacrifice") corresponds to Hebrew "statute of the law," and aetatis integrae ("of full age," literally "of unimpaired age") renders Hebrew temimah ("unblemished") alongside in qua nulla sit macula ("in which there is no blemish").
- **19:3** — The Vulgate has Eleazar himself sacrifice the cow in conspectu omnium ("in the sight of all") and burn her cunctis videntibus ("while all look on," v. 5), whereas the Hebrew text has another person slaughter and burn her in Eleazar's presence.
- **19:18** — The Vulgate summarizes the Hebrew list of corpse-defilement sources with the concise phrase homines hujuscemodi contagione pollutos ("the people defiled by a contagion of this kind").
- **19:19** — The Vulgate reads immundus erit usque ad vesperum ("he will be unclean until evening"), as in verses 8, 10, 21, and 22, whereas the Hebrew states that he will be "clean at evening."
