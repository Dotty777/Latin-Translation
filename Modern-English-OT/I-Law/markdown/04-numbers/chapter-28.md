## Text

**28:1** The Lord also said to Moses:

**28:2** "Command the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them: 'Offer My offering, and bread, and a fire offering of a very sweet fragrance at their proper times.

**28:3** These are the sacrifices that you must offer: two unblemished year-old lambs daily as a perpetual burnt offering:

**28:4** one you shall offer in the morning, and the other toward evening,

**28:5** a tenth part of an ephah of fine flour that is mixed with the purest oil and has a fourth part of a hin.

**28:6** It is the continual burnt offering that you offered on Mount Sinai, for a very sweet fragrance of a fire offering of the Lord.

**28:7** And you shall pour out as a drink offering a fourth part of a hin of wine for each lamb in the sanctuary of the Lord.

**28:8** And the other lamb you shall offer likewise toward evening, according to the entire rite of the morning sacrifice and of its drink offerings, an offering of a very sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**28:9** And on the Sabbath day you shall offer two unblemished year-old lambs, and two-tenths of fine flour mixed with oil as a sacrifice, and the drink offerings,

**28:10** which are duly poured out on each Sabbath as a perpetual burnt offering.

**28:11** Now on the first days of the months you shall offer a burnt offering to the Lord: two young bulls from the herd, one ram, seven unblemished year-old lambs,

**28:12** and three-tenths of fine flour mixed with oil as a sacrifice for each young bull, and two-tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for each ram,

**28:13** and a tenth of a tenth of fine flour with oil as a sacrifice for each lamb; it is a burnt offering of a very sweet fragrance and of a fire offering to the Lord.

**28:14** And the drink offerings of wine, which are to be poured out for each victim, shall be these: half a hin for each young bull, a third for a ram, a fourth for a lamb. This shall be the burnt offering through all the months that succeed one another as the year turns.

**28:15** A male goat shall also be offered to the Lord for sins, as a perpetual burnt offering with its drink offerings.

**28:16** And in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, there shall be the Passover of the Lord,

**28:17** and on the fifteenth day a solemn feast; for seven days they shall eat unleavened bread.

**28:18** The first day of these shall be venerable and holy; you shall not do any servile work on it.

**28:19** And you shall offer a fire offering, a burnt offering, to the Lord: two young bulls from the herd, one ram, seven unblemished year-old lambs,

**28:20** and the sacrifices of each one from fine flour that is mixed with oil: three-tenths for each young bull, and two-tenths for a ram,

**28:21** and a tenth of a tenth for each lamb, that is, for the seven lambs;

**28:22** and one male goat for sin, so that atonement may be made for you,

**28:23** besides the morning burnt offering, which you shall always offer.

**28:24** So you shall do on each day of the seven days as fuel for the fire, and for a very sweet fragrance to the Lord, which will rise from the burnt offering and from the libations of each one.

**28:25** The seventh day also shall be most celebrated and holy for you; you shall not do any servile work on it.

**28:26** The day of the firstfruits also, when you offer new crops to the Lord, after the weeks have been completed, shall be venerable and holy; you shall not do any servile work on it.

**28:27** And you shall offer a burnt offering for a very sweet fragrance to the Lord: two young bulls from the herd, one ram, and seven unblemished year-old lambs,

**28:28** and in their sacrifices, three-tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for each young bull, two for each ram,

**28:29** a tenth of a tenth for each of the lambs, which together are seven lambs; also a male goat, which is slaughtered for atonement, besides the perpetual burnt offering and its drink offerings. You shall offer them all unblemished, with their libations.'"

## Translator Notes

- **28:2** — Latin "incensum" (literally "that which is burned/kindled," often "incense") is used throughout this chapter (vv. 2, 6, 13, 19) for the sacrificial fire offering.
- **28:6** — Vulgate "quod obtulistis" ("that you offered") is active second-person plural, differing from the Hebrew passive participle ("which was ordained"); similarly in v. 7, Vulgate "vini" ("wine") renders Hebrew "strong drink."
- **28:10** — The Vulgate links this verse directly to the drink offerings ("which are duly poured out... as a perpetual burnt offering," and similarly v. 15), whereas the Hebrew places these offerings "besides" the continual burnt offering.
- **28:13** — Latin "decimam decimae" (literally "a tenth of a tenth," also in vv. 21 and 29) renders the Hebrew distributive repetition "a tenth [of an ephah] each" as a nested fraction.
- **28:24** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew "food of the fire offering" as "fuel for the fire" ("in fomitem ignis") and explicitly describes the sweet fragrance as rising from the burnt offering and each offering's libations.
- **28:29** — In this Clementine Vulgate versification, verse 29 combines the text commonly divided across verses 29-31, and renders Hebrew "its grain offering" as "its drink offerings" ("liba ejus").
