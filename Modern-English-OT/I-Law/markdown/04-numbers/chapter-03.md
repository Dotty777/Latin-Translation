## Text

**3:1** These are the generations of Aaron and Moses on the day when the Lord spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai.

**3:2** And these are the names of the sons of Aaron: his firstborn Nadab, then Abihu, and Eleazar, and Ithamar.

**3:3** These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the priests who were anointed and whose hands were filled and consecrated so that they might exercise the priesthood.

**3:4** For Nadab and Abihu died when they offered foreign fire in the sight of the Lord in the wilderness of Sinai, without children; and Eleazar and Ithamar exercised the priesthood before Aaron their father.

**3:5** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**3:6** "Bring near the tribe of Levi, and make them stand in the sight of Aaron the priest so that they may minister to him and keep watch,

**3:7** and observe whatever pertains to the service of the multitude before the tabernacle of the testimony,

**3:8** and guard the vessels of the tabernacle, serving in its ministry.

**3:9** And you will give the Levites as a gift to Aaron and his sons, to whom they have been handed over by the sons of Israel.

**3:10** But you will appoint Aaron and his sons over the service of the priesthood. An outsider who approaches to minister will die."

**3:11** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**3:12** "I have taken the Levites from the sons of Israel in place of every firstborn who opens the womb among the sons of Israel, and the Levites will be Mine.

**3:13** For every firstborn is Mine: from the time I struck the firstborn in the land of Egypt, I consecrated to Myself whatever is born first in Israel; from man even to livestock, they are Mine. I am the Lord."

**3:14** And the Lord spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying:

**3:15** "Count the sons of Levi by the houses of their fathers and families, every male from one month and upward."

**3:16** Moses counted them, as the Lord had commanded,

**3:17** and the sons of Levi were found by their names: Gershon, and Kohath, and Merari.

**3:18** The sons of Gershon: Libni and Shimei.

**3:19** The sons of Kohath: Amram and Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.

**3:20** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi.

**3:21** From Gershon there were two families, the Libnite and the Shimeite,

**3:22** whose people of the male sex were counted from one month and upward, seven thousand five hundred.

**3:23** These will pitch camp behind the tabernacle toward the west,

**3:24** under the leader Eliasaph, son of Lael.

**3:25** And they will have the watch in the tabernacle of the covenant:

**3:26** the tabernacle itself and its covering, the hanging that is drawn before the doors of the shelter of the covenant, and the curtains of the courtyard; also the hanging that is suspended at the entrance of the courtyard of the tabernacle, and whatever pertains to the rite of the altar, the cords of the tabernacle, and all its utensils.

**3:27** The clan of Kohath will have the Amramite, Izharite, Hebronite, and Uzzielite peoples. These are the families of the Kohathites, enrolled by their names.

**3:28** All of the male sex from one month and upward, eight thousand six hundred, will have the watch of the sanctuary,

**3:29** and will encamp on the southern side.

**3:30** And their leader will be Elizaphan, son of Uzziel,

**3:31** and they will guard the ark, and the table and the lampstand, the altars and the vessels of the sanctuary with which ministry is performed, and the veil, and all furnishings of this kind.

**3:32** Now the leader of the leaders of the Levites, Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, will be over the watchmen of the custody of the sanctuary.

**3:33** Now from Merari there will be the Mahlite and Mushite peoples, enrolled by their names:

**3:34** all of the male sex from one month and upward, six thousand two hundred.

**3:35** Their leader is Zuriel, son of Abihail; they will encamp on the northern side.

**3:36** Under their custody will be the boards of the tabernacle and the bars, and the pillars and their bases, and all things that pertain to service of this kind,

**3:37** and the pillars of the courtyard all around with their bases, and the pegs with the cords.

**3:38** Moses, and Aaron with his sons, will encamp before the tabernacle of the covenant, that is, on the eastern side, having custody of the sanctuary in the midst of the sons of Israel. Any stranger who approaches will die.

**3:39** All the Levites whom Moses and Aaron counted according to the command of the Lord by their families, of the male sex from one month and upward, were twenty-two thousand.

**3:40** And the Lord said to Moses, "Count the firstborn of the male sex from the sons of Israel from one month and upward, and you will have their sum.

**3:41** And you will take the Levites for Me in place of every firstborn of the sons of Israel; I am the Lord; and their livestock in place of all the firstborn of the livestock of the sons of Israel."

**3:42** Moses enrolled the firstborn of the sons of Israel, just as the Lord had commanded,

**3:43** and the males by their names, from one month and upward, were twenty-two thousand two hundred seventy-three.

**3:44** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**3:45** "Take the Levites in place of the firstborn of the sons of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites in place of their livestock, and the Levites will be Mine. I am the Lord.

**3:46** Now as the price of the two hundred seventy-three who exceed the number of the Levites from the firstborn of the sons of Israel,

**3:47** you will receive five shekels for each head according to the measure of the sanctuary (a shekel has twenty obols),

**3:48** and you will give the money to Aaron and his sons, the price of those who are in excess."

**3:49** Therefore Moses took the money of those who had been in excess, and whom they had redeemed from the Levites,

**3:50** for the firstborn of the sons of Israel, one thousand three hundred sixty-five shekels according to the weight of the sanctuary,

**3:51** and he gave it to Aaron and his sons according to the word that the Lord had commanded him.

## Translator Notes

- **3:10** — The Vulgate specifies that the outsider approaches "to minister" (ad ministrandum), a detail not explicit in the Hebrew text.
- **3:25** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew ohel mo'ed ("tent of meeting") here and in 3:38 as tabernaculum foederis ("tabernacle of the covenant")—and in 3:26 as tectum foederis ("shelter of the covenant")—whereas in 3:7 it renders the same Hebrew phrase as tabernaculum testimonii ("tabernacle of testimony").
- **3:38** — The Vulgate uniquely reads in medio filiorum Israel ("in the midst of the sons of Israel"), whereas the Hebrew text reads "for the charge/obligation of the sons of Israel."
- **3:47** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate renders the Hebrew weight unit gerah with the Greek-derived term obolos ("obols"), and refers to the sanctuary standard as mensuram sanctuarii ("measure of the sanctuary") here and pondus sanctuarii ("weight of the sanctuary") in 3:50.
- **3:49** — The Vulgate reads et quos redemerant a Levitis ("and whom they had redeemed from the Levites"), whereas the Hebrew text speaks of those over and above "those redeemed by the Levites."
