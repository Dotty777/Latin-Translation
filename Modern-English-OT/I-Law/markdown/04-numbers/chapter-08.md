## Text

**8:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:2** "Speak to Aaron, and you shall say to him, 'When you place the seven lamps, let the lampstand be set up on the south side.' Therefore command this, that the lamps face toward the north, directly opposite the table of the loaves of presentation; toward that side which the lampstand faces, they must shine."

**8:3** And Aaron did so, and placed the lamps upon the lampstand, as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**8:4** Now this was the workmanship of the lampstand, of hammered gold: both the central shaft and all that sprang forth from both sides of the branches. According to the pattern that the Lord showed Moses, so he crafted the lampstand.

**8:5** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:6** "Take the Levites from the midst of the sons of Israel, and you shall purify them

**8:7** according to this rite: let them be sprinkled with the water of purification, and let them shave all the hair of their flesh. And when they have washed their garments and have been cleansed,

**8:8** they shall take an ox from the herds, and its offering, fine flour mixed with oil; but another ox from the herd you shall take for sin;

**8:9** and you shall bring the Levites before the tabernacle of the covenant, with the whole multitude of the sons of Israel called together.

**8:10** And when the Levites are before the Lord, the sons of Israel shall lay their hands upon them.

**8:11** And Aaron shall offer the Levites as a gift in the sight of the Lord from the sons of Israel, so that they may serve in His ministry.

**8:12** The Levites also shall lay their hands upon the heads of the oxen, one of which you shall offer for sin, and the other as a burnt offering to the Lord, so that you may intercede for them.

**8:13** And you shall station the Levites in the sight of Aaron and his sons, and you shall consecrate them, offered to the Lord,

**8:14** and you shall separate them from the midst of the sons of Israel, so that they may be Mine.

**8:15** And afterward they shall enter the tabernacle of the covenant, so that they may serve Me. And thus you shall purify and consecrate them as an offering to the Lord, for they have been given as a gift to Me by the sons of Israel.

**8:16** In place of the firstborn that open every womb in Israel, I have taken them.

**8:17** For all the firstborn of the sons of Israel are Mine, both of humans and of beasts. From the day on which I struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I sanctified them to Myself,

**8:18** and I have taken the Levites in place of all the firstborn of the sons of Israel,

**8:19** and I have given them as a gift to Aaron and his sons from the midst of the people, so that they may serve Me on behalf of Israel in the tabernacle of the covenant, and pray for them, so that there may not be a plague among the people if they should dare to approach the sanctuary."

**8:20** And Moses and Aaron and the whole multitude of the sons of Israel did to the Levites what the Lord had commanded Moses;

**8:21** and they were purified, and washed their garments. And Aaron lifted them up in the sight of the Lord, and prayed for them,

**8:22** so that, purified, they might enter upon their duties in the tabernacle of the covenant before Aaron and his sons. Just as the Lord had commanded Moses concerning the Levites, so it was done.

**8:23** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:24** "This is the law of the Levites: from twenty-five years and upward, they shall enter to minister in the tabernacle of the covenant.

**8:25** And when they have completed the fiftieth year of age, they shall cease to serve,

**8:26** and they shall be attendants of their brothers in the tabernacle of the covenant, so that they may keep watch over what was entrusted to them; but the works themselves they shall not do. Thus you shall make arrangements for the Levites in their charges."

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — The Vulgate expands the brief Hebrew directive by explicitly specifying the lampstand's placement on the south side and its north-facing orientation toward the table of the loaves of presentation (panum propositionis), in line with Exodus 26:35.
- **8:4** — The Vulgate describes the lampstand as "both the central shaft and all that sprang forth from both sides of the branches" (tam medius stipes, quam cuncta quæ ex utroque calamorum latere nascebantur), where the Hebrew reads "from its base to its flowers."
- **8:8** — Latin libamentum (literally "libation" or "offering") is used here by the Vulgate for the accompanying grain offering, defined in apposition as fine flour mixed with oil.
- **8:9** — Throughout this chapter, the Vulgate renders Hebrew ohel mo'ed ("tent of meeting") as tabernaculum fœderis, "tabernacle of the covenant."
- **8:12** — Here (ut depreceris pro eis) and in verses 19 and 21 (orent / oravit), the Vulgate uses verbs of intercession and prayer where the Hebrew specifies making atonement; likewise, in verse 21 elevavit ("lifted up") renders the Hebrew wave-offering ritual.
