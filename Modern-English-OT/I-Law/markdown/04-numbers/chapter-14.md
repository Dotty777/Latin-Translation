## Text

**14:1** Therefore, crying out loudly, the whole crowd wept that night,

**14:2** and all the sons of Israel grumbled against Moses and Aaron, saying,

**14:3** "If only we had died in Egypt! And in this vast wilderness, if only we might perish, and may the Lord not lead us into this land, so that we do not fall by the sword and our wives and children are not led away captive. Is it not better to return to Egypt?"

**14:4** And they said to one another, "Let us appoint a leader for ourselves, and let us return to Egypt."

**14:5** Upon hearing this, Moses and Aaron fell face down to the ground before the whole multitude of the sons of Israel.

**14:6** But Joshua the son of Nun and Caleb the son of Jephunneh, who had themselves also surveyed the land, tore their garments,

**14:7** and spoke to the whole multitude of the sons of Israel: "The land that we traversed is exceedingly good.

**14:8** If the Lord is gracious, He will lead us into it, and He will hand over ground flowing with milk and honey.

**14:9** Do not be rebellious against the Lord, and do not fear the people of this land, for just like bread, we can devour them. All protection has departed from them. The Lord is with us; do not be afraid."

**14:10** And while the whole multitude was shouting and wanting to crush them with stones, the glory of the Lord appeared above the roof of the covenant to all the sons of Israel.

**14:11** And the Lord said to Moses, "How long will this people disparage Me? How long will they not believe Me, amid all the signs that I have done before them?

**14:12** Therefore I will strike them with pestilence and consume them; but I will make you a ruler over a nation great and stronger than this one is."

**14:13** And Moses said to the Lord, "So that the Egyptians, from whose midst You brought out this people,

**14:14** and the inhabitants of this land, who have heard that You, Lord, are among this people, and are seen face to face, and Your cloud protects them, and in a pillar of cloud You go before them by day, and in a pillar of fire by night,

**14:15** may hear that You have killed so great a multitude like one man, and may say,

**14:16** 'He was not able to bring the people into the land concerning which He had sworn; therefore He killed them in the wilderness'?

**14:17** Therefore let the strength of the Lord be magnified, just as You swore, saying,

**14:18** 'The Lord, patient and of great mercy, taking away iniquity and wicked deeds, and leaving no one guiltless, You who visit the sins of the fathers upon the sons to the third and fourth generation.'

**14:19** Forgive, I implore You, the sin of this people according to the greatness of Your mercy, just as You have been gracious to those coming out of Egypt up to this place."

**14:20** And the Lord said, "I have forgiven according to your word.

**14:21** As I live, the whole earth will be filled with the glory of the Lord.

**14:22** Yet all the men who have seen My majesty and the signs that I did in Egypt and in the wilderness, and have now tested Me ten times and have not obeyed My voice,

**14:23** will not see the land concerning which I swore to their fathers, nor will anyone among those who disparaged Me look upon it.

**14:24** My servant Caleb, who is full of another spirit and has followed Me, I will lead into this land that he traversed; and his offspring will possess it.

**14:25** Since the Amalekite and the Canaanite dwell in the valleys, tomorrow move camp and return into the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea."

**14:26** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**14:27** "How long does this most wicked multitude grumble against Me? I have heard the complaints of the sons of Israel.

**14:28** Therefore say to them, 'As I live, says the Lord, just as you have spoken in My hearing, so I will do to you.

**14:29** In this wilderness your corpses will lie. All of you who were numbered from twenty years old and upward, and grumbled against Me,

**14:30** will not enter the land over which I raised My hand to make you dwell in it, except Caleb the son of Jephunneh and Joshua the son of Nun.

**14:31** But your little ones, of whom you said that they would be prey for enemies, I will bring in, so that they may see the land that displeased you.

**14:32** Your corpses will lie in the wilderness.

**14:33** Your sons will be wanderers in the desert for forty years, and they will bear your fornication, until the corpses of the fathers are consumed in the desert,

**14:34** according to the number of the forty days in which you examined the land: a year will be reckoned for a day. And for forty years you will receive your iniquities, and you will know My vengeance:

**14:35** for just as I have spoken, so I will do to all this most wicked multitude that has risen up against Me: in this wilderness it will come to an end and die.'"

**14:36** Therefore all the men whom Moses had sent to observe the land, and who, having returned, had made the whole multitude grumble against him, disparaging the land as being bad,

**14:37** died and were struck down in the sight of the Lord.

**14:38** But Joshua the son of Nun and Caleb the son of Jephunneh lived, out of all those who had gone to examine the land.

**14:39** And Moses spoke all these words to all the sons of Israel, and the people mourned greatly.

**14:40** And look, rising very early in the morning, they went up to the summit of the mountain and said, "We are ready to go up to the place of which the Lord has spoken, for we have sinned."

**14:41** To them Moses said, "Why do you transgress the word of the Lord, which will not turn out favorably for you?

**14:42** Do not go up, for the Lord is not with you, so that you do not fall before your enemies.

**14:43** The Amalekite and the Canaanite are before you, by whose sword you will fall, because you were unwilling to submit to the Lord; nor will the Lord be with you."

**14:44** But they, darkened, went up to the summit of the mountain. Yet the ark of the testament of the Lord and Moses did not depart from the camp.

**14:45** And the Amalekite and the Canaanite, who dwelt on the mountain, came down, and striking them and cutting them down, pursued them as far as Hormah.

## Translator Notes

- **14:3** — The Vulgate adds "vast" (vasta) to the wilderness, shifts to a present-tense wish ("if only we might perish," utinam pereamus), and continues with a negative wish ("may the Lord not lead us") rather than the Hebrew question ("Why is the Lord bringing us?").
- **14:10** — Latin super tectum foederis literally reads "above the roof of the covenant," a distinctive Vulgate wording for the covering of the tent of meeting.
- **14:12** — Latin faciam principem super gentem magnam specifies making Moses a ruler over a greater nation, whereas the Hebrew reads making him into a greater and mightier nation.
- **14:18** — Latin nullumque innoxium derelinquens literally means "leaving no one guiltless" (or "unpunished"), rendering the objectless Hebrew idiom for not acquitting guilt. The verb visitas ("You who visit") shifts into second-person address within the quoted proclamation.
- **14:44** — Latin contenebrati ("darkened," i.e., blinded in mind) is a distinctive Vulgate rendering reflecting a connection between the Hebrew verb for acting presumptuously and the Hebrew root for darkness.
