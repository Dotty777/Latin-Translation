## Text

**5:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**5:2** "Command the sons of Israel to expel from the camp every leper, and whoever has a discharge of seed, and whoever is defiled on account of a dead person;

**5:3** expel both male and female from the camp, so that they do not contaminate it while they dwell with you."

**5:4** And the sons of Israel did so, and expelled them outside the camp, just as the Lord had spoken to Moses.

**5:5** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**5:6** "Speak to the sons of Israel: A man or a woman, when they commit any of all the sins that commonly happen to people, and through negligence transgress the command of the Lord and commit an offense,

**5:7** they shall confess their sin, and they shall repay the principal amount itself, and a fifth part in addition, to the one against whom they have sinned.

**5:8** But if there is no one to receive it, they shall give it to the Lord, and it shall belong to the priest, apart from the ram that is offered for atonement, so that it may be an appeasing sacrifice.

**5:9** Also all the firstfruits that the sons of Israel offer belong to the priest;

**5:10** and whatever is offered into the sanctuary by each person, and is delivered into the hands of the priest, shall be his."

**5:11** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**5:12** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them: A man whose wife has gone astray, and despising her husband

**5:13** has slept with another man, and the husband has not been able to detect this, but the adultery lies hidden and cannot be proved by witnesses, because she was not found in illicit intercourse:

**5:14** if a spirit of jealousy has stirred up the man against his wife, who either has been defiled or is assailed by a false suspicion:

**5:15** he shall bring her to the priest, and shall present an offering for her, a tenth part of a seah of barley flour; he shall not pour oil over it, nor place frankincense on it, because it is a sacrifice of jealousy, and an offering investigating adultery.

**5:16** Therefore the priest shall bring her forward and set her before the Lord,

**5:17** and he shall take holy water in an earthen vessel, and he shall put a small amount of earth from the floor of the tabernacle into it.

**5:18** And when the woman stands in the sight of the Lord, he shall uncover her head, and place upon her hands the sacrifice of remembrance and the offering of jealousy; he himself, however, shall hold the most bitter waters, in which he has gathered curses with imprecation.

**5:19** And he shall put her under oath and say, 'If no strange man has slept with you, and if you have not been defiled by abandoning the marriage bed of your husband, these most bitter waters, into which I have gathered curses, will not harm you.

**5:20** But if you have turned aside from your husband, and have been defiled, and have lain with another man,

**5:21** you will be subject to these curses: may the Lord make you a curse and an example to all among His people; may He make your thigh rot, and may your swelling womb burst apart.

**5:22** May the cursed waters enter into your belly, and as your womb swells, may your thigh rot.' And the woman shall answer, 'Amen, amen.'

**5:23** And the priest shall write these curses on a scroll, and shall blot them out with the most bitter waters into which he has gathered curses,

**5:24** and shall give them to her to drink.

**5:25** When she has drained them, the priest shall take from her hand the sacrifice of jealousy, and shall lift it up before the Lord, and shall place it upon the altar,

**5:26** provided, however, that first he take a handful of the sacrifice from what is offered and burn it upon the altar, and thus give the woman the most bitter waters to drink.

**5:27** When she has drunk them, if she has been defiled and, having despised her husband, is guilty of adultery, the waters of the curse will pass through her, and with her belly swollen, her thigh will rot away; and the woman will become a curse and an example to all the people.

**5:28** But if she has not been defiled, she will be innocent, and she will bear children.

**5:29** This is the law of jealousy. If a woman has turned aside from her husband, and if she has been defiled,

**5:30** and the husband, stirred up by a spirit of jealousy, has brought her into the sight of the Lord, and the priest has done to her according to all that is written:

**5:31** the husband will be without guilt, and she will receive her iniquity."

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — The Vulgate reads cum habitaverint vobiscum ("while they dwell with you"), referring to those expelled, whereas the Hebrew text reads "in the midst of which I dwell."
- **5:15** — The Vulgate specifies decimam partem sati ("a tenth part of a seah," a dry measure equal to one-third of an ephah), whereas the Hebrew text specifies a tenth of an ephah.
- **5:21** — The Vulgate reads in populo suo ("among His people") and vividly renders the curse as the thigh rotting and the swelling womb bursting apart, differing from the Hebrew ("among your people"; thigh wasting away and belly swelling).
- **5:26** — The Vulgate qualifying clause ita dumtaxat ut prius ("provided, however, that first") clarifies that the handful of the sacrifice is burned before the woman drinks the bitter waters.
