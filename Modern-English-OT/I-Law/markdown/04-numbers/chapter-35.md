## Text

**35:1** The Lord also spoke these things to Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan, opposite Jericho:

**35:2** "Command the sons of Israel to give to the Levites from their possessions

**35:3** cities to dwell in, and their outlying lands all around, so that they themselves may remain in the towns, and the outlying lands may be for flocks and beasts of burden,

**35:4** which will extend outward from the walls of the cities, all around, for a distance of a thousand paces.

**35:5** Toward the east there will be two thousand cubits, and toward the south similarly there will be two thousand; toward the sea also, which faces toward the west, there will be the same measurement, and the northern side will end with an equal boundary; and the cities will be in the middle, and the outlying lands outside.

**35:6** Now from these very towns that you will give to the Levites, six will be set apart for the aid of fugitives, so that the one who has shed blood may flee to them; and apart from these, forty-two other towns,

**35:7** that is, forty-eight altogether, with their outlying lands.

**35:8** And as for the cities themselves that will be given from the possessions of the sons of Israel: from those who have more, more will be taken, and from those who have less, fewer; each one will give towns to the Levites according to the measure of his inheritance."

**35:9** The Lord said to Moses,

**35:10** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you will say to them: 'When you have crossed the Jordan into the land of Canaan,

**35:11** designate which cities ought to be for the protection of fugitives who have shed blood unintentionally,

**35:12** in which, when the fugitive is there, the kinsman of the one killed will not be able to kill him until he stands in the sight of the multitude and his case is judged.

**35:15** Now of those very cities that are set apart for the refuge of fugitives, three will be across the Jordan and three in the land of Canaan, both for the sons of Israel and for strangers and sojourners, so that the one who has shed blood unintentionally may flee to them.

**35:16** If anyone strikes with iron, and the one who was struck dies, he will be guilty of murder, and he himself will die.

**35:17** If he throws a stone, and the one struck falls dead, he will be punished in the same way.

**35:18** If one struck with wood perishes, he will be avenged by the blood of the striker.

**35:19** The close relative of the one killed will put the murderer to death; as soon as he seizes him, he will put him to death.

**35:20** If anyone pushes a person out of hatred, or throws anything at him from ambush,

**35:21** or, being an enemy, strikes him with his hand, and that one dies, the striker will be guilty of murder; the kinsman of the one killed, as soon as he finds him, will execute him.

**35:25** But if by chance, and without hatred and hostilities, he has done any of these things, and this has been proven in the hearing of the people, and the inquiry has been examined between the striker and the close relative of the blood, the innocent one will be freed from the hand of the avenger, and he will be brought back by verdict into the city to which he had fled, and he will remain there until the high priest, who was anointed with the holy oil, dies.

**35:26** If the killer outside the borders of the cities that have been assigned to exiles

**35:27** is found and struck by the one who is the avenger of blood, the one who kills him will be without guilt.

**35:28** For the fugitive ought to have stayed in the city until the death of the high priest. But after he has died, the killer will return to his own land.

**35:29** These things will be perpetual and established by law in all your dwellings.

**35:30** A murderer will be punished under witnesses; on the testimony of one person, no one will be condemned.

**35:31** You will not accept a payment from one who is guilty of blood; he himself also will die immediately.

**35:32** Exiles and fugitives will by no means be able to return to their own cities before the death of the high priest,

**35:33** so that you do not pollute the land of your dwelling, which is stained by the blood of the innocent; nor can it be expiated in any other way except through the blood of the one who shed the blood of another.

**35:34** And thus your possession will be cleansed while I dwell with you. For I am the Lord, who dwell among the sons of Israel.'"

## Translator Notes

- **35:4** — The Vulgate gives mille passuum spatio ("a distance of a thousand paces," a Roman mile) where the Hebrew has "a thousand cubits," though the Vulgate retains "two thousand cubits" in v. 5.
- **35:15** — The Clementine Vulgate combines the material of Hebrew vv. 13-15 into this single verse, so vv. 13-14 are not numbered separately.
- **35:25** — The Clementine Vulgate condenses Hebrew vv. 22-25 into one verse, omitting the specific examples of accidental killing in vv. 22-23; propinquum sanguinis ("close relative of the blood") renders Hebrew go'el ha-dam ("avenger of blood," translated ultor sanguinis in v. 27).
- **35:32** — Unlike the Hebrew, which forbids accepting a ransom to let a fugitive return early, the Vulgate states directly that exiles and fugitives may by no means return before the high priest's death.
- **35:33** — The Vulgate specifies insontium cruore ("the blood of the innocent") where the Hebrew says simply "blood," and in v. 34 turns the prohibition against defiling the land into the promise emundabitur vestra possessio ("your possession will be cleansed").
