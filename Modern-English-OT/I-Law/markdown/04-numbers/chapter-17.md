## Text

**17:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel and receive from them one rod each according to their clans, from all the leaders of the tribes, twelve rods; and you shall inscribe the name of each one upon his rod.

**17:3** Now the name of Aaron shall be in the tribe of Levi, and one rod shall include all the families separately;

**17:4** and you shall place them in the tabernacle of the covenant before the testimony, where I will speak to you.

**17:5** He whom I choose from among these, his rod will sprout; and I will restrain from Me the complaints of the sons of Israel, with which they grumble against you."

**17:6** And Moses spoke to the sons of Israel, and all the leaders gave him rods, one for each tribe; and there were twelve rods, apart from the rod of Aaron.

**17:7** When Moses had placed them before the Lord in the tabernacle of the testimony,

**17:8** returning on the following day, he found that the rod of Aaron in the house of Levi had sprouted; and with the buds swelling, blossoms had burst forth, which, as the leaves spread open, were formed into almonds.

**17:9** Therefore Moses brought out all the rods from the presence of the Lord to all the sons of Israel; and they looked, and each one received back his own rod.

**17:10** And the Lord said to Moses, "Bring back the rod of Aaron into the tabernacle of the testimony, so that it may be kept there as a sign for the rebellious sons of Israel, and that their complaints may subside from Me, so that they may not die."

**17:11** And Moses did just as the Lord had commanded.

**17:12** Then the sons of Israel said to Moses, "Look, we are consumed! We have all perished!

**17:13** Whoever approaches the tabernacle of the Lord dies. Are we all to be destroyed to utter annihilation?"

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — Native Vulgate Numbers 16:36-50 contain the censers and plague (Saints.Bible 17:1-15), and native Numbers 17:1-13 contain Aaron's budding rod (Saints.Bible 17:16-28).
- **17:6** — The Vulgate specifies "apart from the rod of Aaron" (Latin "absque virga Aaron"), indicating twelve tribal rods in addition to Aaron's, whereas the Hebrew places Aaron's staff "among their staffs."
