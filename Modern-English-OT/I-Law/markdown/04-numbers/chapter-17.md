## Text

**17:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:2** "Command Eleazar the priest, the son of Aaron, to take up the censers that lie in the blaze and scatter the fire here and there, since they have been sanctified

**17:3** in the deaths of the sinners; and let him beat them out into plates and fasten them to the altar, because incense was offered in them to the Lord and they have been sanctified, so that the sons of Israel may see them as a sign and a reminder."

**17:4** Therefore Eleazar the priest took the bronze censers in which those whom the blaze devoured had offered, and beat them out into plates, fastening them to the altar,

**17:5** so that afterward the sons of Israel might have things by which to be reminded that no outsider, and no one who is not of the offspring of Aaron, may approach to offer incense to the Lord, so that he may not suffer as Korah and all his assembly suffered, as the Lord spoke to Moses.

**17:6** Now the whole multitude of the sons of Israel grumbled on the following day against Moses and Aaron, saying, "You have killed the people of the Lord."

**17:7** And when a rebellion arose and the uproar grew, Moses and Aaron fled to the tabernacle of the covenant. After they entered it, the cloud covered it, and the glory of the Lord appeared.

**17:9** And the Lord said to Moses,

**17:10** "Withdraw from the midst of this multitude; even now I will destroy them." And while they were lying on the ground,

**17:11** Moses said to Aaron, "Take the censer, and having drawn fire from the altar, put incense on top, going quickly to the people so that you may pray for them; for wrath has already gone out from the Lord, and the plague is raging."

**17:12** When Aaron had done this and had run into the midst of the multitude, which the blaze was already devastating, he offered the incense,

**17:13** and standing between the dead and the living, he pleaded for the people, and the plague ceased.

**17:14** Now those who were struck down were fourteen thousand seven hundred people, apart from those who had perished in the rebellion of Korah.

**17:15** And Aaron returned to Moses at the entrance of the tabernacle of the covenant after the destruction had subsided.

**17:16** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:17** "Speak to the sons of Israel and receive from them one rod each according to their clans, from all the leaders of the tribes, twelve rods; and you shall inscribe the name of each one upon his rod.

**17:18** Now the name of Aaron shall be in the tribe of Levi, and one rod shall include all the families separately;

**17:19** and you shall place them in the tabernacle of the covenant before the testimony, where I will speak to you.

**17:20** He whom I choose from among these, his rod will sprout; and I will restrain from Me the complaints of the sons of Israel, with which they grumble against you."

**17:21** And Moses spoke to the sons of Israel, and all the leaders gave him rods, one for each tribe; and there were twelve rods, apart from the rod of Aaron.

**17:22** When Moses had placed them before the Lord in the tabernacle of the testimony,

**17:23** returning on the following day, he found that the rod of Aaron in the house of Levi had sprouted; and with the buds swelling, blossoms had burst forth, which, as the leaves spread open, were formed into almonds.

**17:24** Therefore Moses brought out all the rods from the presence of the Lord to all the sons of Israel; and they looked, and each one received back his own rod.

**17:25** And the Lord said to Moses, "Bring back the rod of Aaron into the tabernacle of the testimony, so that it may be kept there as a sign for the rebellious sons of Israel, and that their complaints may subside from Me, so that they may not die."

**17:26** And Moses did just as the Lord had commanded.

**17:27** Then the sons of Israel said to Moses, "Look, we are consumed! We have all perished!

**17:28** Whoever approaches the tabernacle of the Lord dies. Are we all to be destroyed to utter annihilation?"

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — In the Clementine Vulgate, Numbers 17:1-15 corresponds to 16:36-50 in the Hebrew numbering, while 17:16-28 corresponds to 17:1-13; verse number 17:8 is omitted because its substance is merged into 17:7.
- **17:3** — The Vulgate reads "in the deaths of the sinners" (Latin "in mortibus peccatorum"), whereas the Hebrew reads "at the cost of their lives."
- **17:7** — "Tabernacle of the covenant" translates Latin "tabernaculum foederis" (used alongside "tabernaculum testimonii," "tabernacle of the testimony," in 17:22 and 17:25), where the Hebrew has "tent of meeting." The Vulgate also states that Moses and Aaron "fled" ("fugerunt") to it.
- **17:12** — The Vulgate uses "incendium" ("blaze" or "fire") for what was devastating the multitude, matching 17:2 and 17:4, whereas the Hebrew refers here to "the plague."
- **17:21** — The Vulgate specifies "apart from the rod of Aaron" (Latin "absque virga Aaron"), indicating twelve tribal rods in addition to Aaron's, whereas the Hebrew places Aaron's staff "among their staffs."
