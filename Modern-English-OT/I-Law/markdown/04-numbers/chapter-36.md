## Text

**36:1** Now the chiefs of the families of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, from the lineage of the sons of Joseph, also came forward; and they spoke to Moses before the chiefs of Israel and said,

**36:2** "The Lord commanded you, our lord, to divide the land by lot to the sons of Israel, and to give to the daughters of Zelophehad our brother the possession due to their father;

**36:3** and if men of another tribe take them as wives, their possession will follow, and, transferred to another tribe, it will be deducted from our inheritance.

**36:4** And so it will happen that when the Jubilee, that is, the fiftieth year of release, arrives, the distribution of the lots will be confused, and the possession of some will pass over to others."

**36:5** Moses answered the sons of Israel, and at the Lord's command he said, "The tribe of the sons of Joseph has spoken rightly.

**36:6** And this law concerning the daughters of Zelophehad has been proclaimed by the Lord: let them marry whomever they wish, provided they marry only men of their own tribe,

**36:7** so that the possession of the sons of Israel may not be mingled from tribe to tribe.

**36:8** For all men will take wives from their own tribe and clan, and all women will take husbands from the same tribe, so that the inheritance may remain in the families,

**36:9** and the tribes may not be mingled with one another, but may remain just as they were separated by the Lord."

**36:10** And the daughters of Zelophehad did as had been commanded,

**36:11** and Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah married the sons of their father's brother

**36:12** from the family of Manasseh, who was the son of Joseph; and the possession that had been assigned to them remained in the tribe and family of their father.

**36:13** These are the commands and judgments that the Lord commanded through the hand of Moses to the sons of Israel, in the plains of Moab by the Jordan, opposite Jericho.

## Translator Notes

- **36:4** — The Vulgate adds the explanatory gloss id est, quinquagesimus annus remissionis ("that is, the fiftieth year of release") and summarizes the consequence as confusion of the lots and transfer of possessions among tribes.
- **36:8** — The Vulgate generalizes the marriage requirement to all men and all women rather than only to inheriting daughters as in the Hebrew.
