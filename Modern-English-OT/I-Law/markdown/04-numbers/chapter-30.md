## Text

**30:1** And Moses told the sons of Israel everything that the Lord had commanded him.

**30:2** And he spoke to the leaders of the tribes of the sons of Israel: "This is the word that the Lord has commanded:

**30:3** If any man vows a vow to the Lord, or binds himself by an oath, he shall not make his word void, but shall fulfill everything that he has promised.

**30:4** If a woman who is in her father's house and still in her girlhood vows anything and binds herself by an oath, if the father learns of the vow that she has promised, and the oath by which she has obligated her soul, and keeps silent, she will be bound by the vow;

**30:5** whatever she has promised and sworn, she shall carry out in deed.

**30:6** But if, as soon as he hears, the father objects, both her vows and her oaths will be void, nor will she be held liable to the pledge, because the father objected.

**30:7** If she has a husband, and vows something, and a word once going forth from her mouth obligates her soul by an oath,

**30:8** on the day the husband hears and does not object, she will be bound by the vow, and shall pay whatever she had promised.

**30:9** But if, upon hearing, he immediately objects, and makes her promises void, and the words by which she had bound her soul, the Lord will be merciful to her.

**30:10** A widow and a divorced woman shall pay whatever they vow.

**30:11** When a wife in her husband's house binds herself by a vow and an oath,

**30:12** if the husband hears and keeps silent, and does not object to the pledge, she shall pay whatever she had promised.

**30:13** But if he objects at once, she will not be held bound by the promise, because the husband objected, and the Lord will be merciful to her.

**30:14** If she vows, and binds herself by an oath to afflict her soul through fasting or abstinence from other things, it will be in the husband's discretion whether she does it or does not do it.

**30:15** Now if the husband, upon hearing, keeps silent, and defers his decision to another day, she shall pay whatever she had vowed and promised, because as soon as he heard, he kept silent.

**30:16** But if he objects after he has learned of it, he himself will bear her iniquity."

**30:17** These are the laws that the Lord established for Moses between a husband and a wife, between a father and a daughter who is still in her girlhood, or who remains in her parent's house.

## Translator Notes

- **30:1** — In the Clementine Vulgate, chapter 30 begins with this verse (corresponding to Numbers 29:40 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text), so Vulgate 30:2-17 correspond to Hebrew 30:1-16.
- **30:4** — The classical Latin legal idiom 'voti rea' (also in v. 8; compare 'promissionis rea' in v. 13) means bound by or liable for a vow, rather than morally guilty.
- **30:7** — Vulgate 'semel de ore ejus verbum egrediens' ("a word once going forth from her mouth") corresponds to Hebrew mivta sefateiha ("the rash utterance of her lips").
- **30:14** — Vulgate adds the explanatory phrase 'per jejunium, vel ceterarum rerum abstinentiam' ("through fasting or abstinence from other things"), which is absent from the Hebrew text, and renders the husband's authority to confirm or annul the vow as 'in arbitrio viri erit ut faciat, sive non faciat'.
- **30:17** — Vulgate 'vel quae manet in parentis domo' ("or who remains in her parent's house") introduces an alternative clause ('vel') and uses gender-neutral 'parentis' ("parent's"), where the Hebrew has a single phrase ("in her youth in her father's house").
