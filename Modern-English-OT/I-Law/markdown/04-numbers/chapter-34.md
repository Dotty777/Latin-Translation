## Text

**34:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**34:2** "Command the sons of Israel, and you will say to them: 'When you have entered the land of Canaan, and it has fallen to you by lot as a possession, it will be bounded by these borders.

**34:3** The southern part will begin from the wilderness of Zin, which is beside Edom, and it will have as its boundaries toward the east the saltiest sea.

**34:4** These will go around the southern side by way of the Ascent of the Scorpion, so that they cross over into Senna and reach on the south as far as Kadesh-barnea, from where the borders will go out to the village named Addar and will extend as far as Azmon.

**34:5** And the boundary will go in a curve from Azmon as far as the torrent of Egypt, and it will end at the shore of the Great Sea.

**34:6** Now the western side will begin from the Great Sea, and it will be closed by that very border.

**34:7** Furthermore, for the northern side, the boundaries will begin from the Great Sea, reaching as far as the highest mountain,

**34:8** from which they will come into Hamath as far as the boundaries of Zedad;

**34:9** and the borders will go as far as Ziphron and the village of Enan. These will be the boundaries in the northern part.

**34:10** From there the borders will be marked out toward the eastern side, from the village of Enan as far as Shepham,

**34:11** and from Shepham the boundaries will go down to Riblah, opposite the spring Daphnis; from there they will reach toward the east to the Sea of Chinnereth,

**34:12** and they will extend as far as the Jordan, and at the last they will be closed by the saltiest sea. You will have this land according to its borders all around.'"

**34:13** And Moses commanded the sons of Israel, saying, "This will be the land that you will possess by lot, and that the Lord ordered to be given to the nine tribes and to the half-tribe.

**34:14** For the tribe of the sons of Reuben according to their families, and the tribe of the sons of Gad according to the number of their clans, and also the half-tribe of Manasseh,

**34:15** that is, two and a half tribes, have received their portion across the Jordan opposite Jericho, toward the eastern side."

**34:16** And the Lord said to Moses,

**34:17** "These are the names of the men who will divide the land for you: Eleazar the priest, and Joshua son of Nun,

**34:18** and one chief from each tribe,

**34:19** whose names are these. From the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh.

**34:20** From the tribe of Simeon, Shemuel son of Ammihud.

**34:21** From the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon.

**34:22** From the tribe of the sons of Dan, Bukki son of Jogli.

**34:23** Of the sons of Joseph, from the tribe of Manasseh, Hanniel son of Ephod.

**34:24** From the tribe of Ephraim, Kemuel son of Shiphtan.

**34:25** From the tribe of Zebulun, Elizaphan son of Parnach.

**34:26** From the tribe of Issachar, the leader Paltiel son of Azzan.

**34:27** From the tribe of Asher, Ahihud son of Shelomi.

**34:28** From the tribe of Naphtali, Pedahel son of Ammihud."

**34:29** These are the ones whom the Lord commanded to divide the land of Canaan for the sons of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **34:4** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew Akrabbim as singular Latin Scorpionis ("of the Scorpion"), renders Hebrew Tsinah as Senna (distinct from Sin/Zin in v. 3, following the Septuagint), and translates the first element of Hazar-addar as villam nomine Adar ("the village named Addar").
- **34:7** — Where the Hebrew reads "Mount Hor" (Hor ha-har), the Vulgate translates descriptively as montem altissimum ("the highest mountain").
- **34:11** — The Vulgate reads contra fontem Daphnim ("opposite the spring Daphnis"), naming the spring where the Hebrew has only Ain ("spring").
- **34:26** — The Vulgate includes the title dux ("leader") only for Issachar's representative in this list, whereas the Hebrew applies the title nasi to each of the last seven tribal chiefs.
