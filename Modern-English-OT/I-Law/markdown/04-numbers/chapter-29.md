## Text

**29:1** The first day of the seventh month also will be venerable and holy to you. You shall not do any servile work on it, because it is a day of loud blast and of trumpets.

**29:2** And you shall offer a burnt offering for a most pleasing aroma to the Lord: one calf from the herd, one ram, and seven unblemished year-old lambs;

**29:3** and in their sacrifices, three tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for each calf, two tenths for the ram,

**29:4** one tenth per lamb, which together are seven lambs;

**29:5** and a male goat for sin, which is offered for the expiation of the people,

**29:6** besides the burnt offering of the first of the month with its sacrifices, and the perpetual burnt offering with the customary libations; with the same ceremonies you shall offer a fire offering for a most pleasing aroma to the Lord.

**29:7** The tenth day of this seventh month also will be holy and venerable to you, and you shall afflict your souls; you shall not do any servile work on it.

**29:8** And you shall offer a burnt offering to the Lord for a most pleasing aroma: one calf from the herd, one ram, seven unblemished year-old lambs;

**29:9** and in their sacrifices, three tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for each calf, two tenths for the ram,

**29:10** a tenth of a tenth for each lamb, which together are seven lambs;

**29:11** and a male goat for sin, apart from those that are customarily offered for trespass as expiation, and the perpetual burnt offering, with their sacrifice and libations.

**29:12** Now on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, which will be holy and venerable to you, you shall not do any servile work on it, but you shall celebrate a solemn feast to the Lord for seven days.

**29:13** And you shall offer a burnt offering for a most pleasing aroma to the Lord: thirteen calves from the herd, two rams, fourteen unblemished year-old lambs;

**29:14** and in their libations, three tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for each calf, which together are thirteen calves, and two tenths for one ram, that is, for the two rams together,

**29:15** and a tenth of a tenth for each lamb, which together are fourteen lambs;

**29:16** and a male goat for sin, apart from the perpetual burnt offering, and its sacrifice, and its libation.

**29:17** On the second day you shall offer twelve calves from the herd, two rams, fourteen unblemished year-old lambs;

**29:18** and you shall duly celebrate the sacrifices and libations of each one, for the calves, and the rams, and the lambs;

**29:19** and a male goat for sin, apart from the perpetual burnt offering, and its sacrifice and libation.

**29:20** On the third day you shall offer eleven calves, two rams, fourteen unblemished year-old lambs;

**29:21** and you shall duly celebrate the sacrifices and libations of each one, for the calves, and the rams, and the lambs;

**29:22** and a male goat for sin, apart from the perpetual burnt offering, and its sacrifice and libation.

**29:23** On the fourth day you shall offer ten calves, two rams, fourteen unblemished year-old lambs;

**29:24** and you shall duly celebrate the sacrifices and libations of each one, for the calves, and the rams, and the lambs;

**29:25** and a male goat for sin, apart from the perpetual burnt offering, and its sacrifice and libation.

**29:26** On the fifth day you shall offer nine calves, two rams, fourteen unblemished year-old lambs;

**29:27** and you shall duly celebrate the sacrifices and libations of each one, for the calves, and the rams, and the lambs;

**29:28** and a male goat for sin, apart from the perpetual burnt offering, and its sacrifice and libation.

**29:29** On the sixth day you shall offer eight calves, two rams, fourteen unblemished year-old lambs;

**29:30** and you shall duly celebrate the sacrifices and libations of each one, for the calves, and the rams, and the lambs;

**29:31** and a male goat for sin, apart from the perpetual burnt offering, and its sacrifice and libation.

**29:32** On the seventh day you shall offer seven calves, and two rams, fourteen unblemished year-old lambs;

**29:33** and you shall duly celebrate the sacrifices and libations of each one, for the calves, and the rams, and the lambs;

**29:34** and a male goat for sin, apart from the perpetual burnt offering, and its sacrifice and libation.

**29:35** On the eighth day, which is most celebrated, you shall not do any servile work,

**29:36** offering a burnt offering for a most pleasing aroma to the Lord: one calf, one ram, seven unblemished year-old lambs;

**29:37** and you shall duly celebrate the sacrifices and libations of each one, for the calves, and the rams, and the lambs;

**29:38** and a male goat for sin, apart from the perpetual burnt offering, and its sacrifice and libation.

**29:39** These things you shall offer to the Lord at your solemn feasts, besides vows and voluntary offerings, in burnt offering, in sacrifice, in libation, and in peace offerings.

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — Vulgate 'venerabilis et sancta' ("venerable and holy"; also in vv. 7, 12) translates Hebrew miqra qodesh ("holy convocation").
- **29:6** — Vulgate 'calendarum' uses the Roman term for the first day of the month, corresponding to Hebrew hachodesh ("the new moon"); 'incensum' literally denotes a burned or fire offering (Hebrew isheh).
- **29:10** — Vulgate 'decimam decimae' ("a tenth of a tenth"; also in v. 15) renders the Hebrew distributive idiom issaron issaron ("a tenth each") as a genitive phrase; contrast 'unam decimam' ("one tenth") in v. 4.
- **29:14** — Vulgate reads 'in libamentis eorum' ("in their libations") here instead of 'in sacrificiis eorum' ("in their sacrifices") as in vv. 3 and 9, and adds the explanatory gloss 'id est, simul arietibus duobus' ("that is, for the two rams together").
- **29:35** — Vulgate 'qui est celeberrimus' ("which is most celebrated") translates Hebrew atseret ("a solemn assembly").
