## Text

**25:1** Now at that time Israel was staying in Shittim, and the people committed sexual immorality with the daughters of Moab,

**25:2** who invited them to their sacrifices. And they ate and worshiped their gods.

**25:3** And Israel was initiated into Baal-peor; and the Lord, becoming angry,

**25:4** said to Moses, "Take all the chiefs of the people and hang them facing the sun on gallows, so that My fury may be turned away from Israel."

**25:5** And Moses said to the judges of Israel, "Let each one kill his neighbors who have been initiated into Baal-peor."

**25:6** And look, one of the sons of Israel went in before his brothers to a Midianite prostitute, in the sight of Moses and the entire crowd of the sons of Israel, who were weeping before the doors of the tabernacle.

**25:7** When Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, saw this, he rose up from the midst of the multitude, and seizing a dagger,

**25:8** he went in after the Israelite man into the brothel and pierced both of them at once, namely, the man and the woman, in the genital parts. And the plague ceased from the sons of Israel;

**25:9** and twenty-four thousand people were killed.

**25:10** And the Lord said to Moses,

**25:11** "Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, has turned away My anger from the sons of Israel, because he was moved with My zeal against them, so that I Myself would not destroy the sons of Israel in My zeal.

**25:12** Therefore speak to him: 'Look, I give him the peace of My covenant,

**25:13** and there will be both for him and for his offspring an everlasting pact of the priesthood, because he was zealous for his God and atoned for the crime of the sons of Israel.'"

**25:14** Now the name of the Israelite man who was killed with the Midianite woman was Zimri son of Salu, a leader from the clan and tribe of Simeon.

**25:15** Moreover, the Midianite woman who was put to death at the same time was called Cozbi, daughter of Zur, a most noble chief of the Midianites.

**25:16** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**25:17** "Let the Midianites experience you as enemies, and strike them,

**25:18** because they themselves also acted with hostility against you and deceived you with plots through the idol of Peor and Cozbi, daughter of a leader of Midian, their sister, who was struck on the day of the plague for the sacrilege of Peor."

## Translator Notes

- **25:3** — Vulgate Initiatusque est ("was initiated into," also in 25:5) follows the Septuagint in describing Israel's attachment to Baal-peor in terms of cultic initiation (Hebrew: "yoked/joined itself to").
- **25:4** — Vulgate in patibulis ("on gallows") specifies the instrument of public execution and does not include the Hebrew phrase "to the Lord."
- **25:8** — The Vulgate distinctively reads scortum ("prostitute") in 25:6, pugione ("dagger") in 25:7, and lupanar ("brothel") with in locis genitalibus ("in the genital parts") here, where the Hebrew refers to a Midianite woman, a spear, an inner chamber, and her belly.
- **25:12** — Vulgate pacem foederis mei ("the peace of My covenant") inverts the Hebrew genitive construction ("My covenant of peace").
