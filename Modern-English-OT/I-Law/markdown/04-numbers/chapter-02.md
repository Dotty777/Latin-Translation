## Text

**2:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**2:2** "The sons of Israel will camp, each by companies, standards, and banners, and by the houses of their kindred, all around the tabernacle of the covenant.

**2:3** Toward the east, Judah will pitch tents by the companies of his army, and the leader of his sons will be Nahshon son of Amminadab.

**2:4** And the entire total of warriors from his lineage was seventy-four thousand six hundred.

**2:5** Next to him camped those of the tribe of Issachar, whose leader was Nethanel son of Zuar.

**2:6** And the entire number of his warriors was fifty-four thousand four hundred.

**2:7** In the tribe of Zebulun, the leader was Eliab son of Helon.

**2:8** The entire army of warriors from his lineage was fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

**2:9** All who were enumerated in the camp of Judah were one hundred eighty-six thousand four hundred; and by their companies they will go out first.

**2:10** In the camp of the sons of Reuben toward the southern region, the leader will be Elizur son of Shedeur.

**2:11** And the whole army of his warriors who were counted was forty-six thousand five hundred.

**2:12** Next to him camped those of the tribe of Simeon, whose leader was Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai.

**2:13** And the whole army of his warriors who were counted was fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

**2:14** In the tribe of Gad, the leader was Eliasaph son of Deuel.

**2:15** And the whole army of his warriors who were counted was forty-five thousand six hundred fifty.

**2:16** All who were mustered in the camp of Reuben were one hundred fifty-one thousand four hundred fifty by their companies; they will set out in the second place.

**2:17** Then the tabernacle of the testimony will be lifted up through the services of the Levites and their companies; just as it will be erected, so also it will be taken down. Each will set out by their places and ranks.

**2:18** Toward the western region will be the camp of the sons of Ephraim, whose leader was Elishama son of Ammihud.

**2:19** The whole army of his warriors who were counted was forty thousand five hundred.

**2:20** And with them will be the tribe of the sons of Manasseh, whose leader was Gamaliel son of Pedahzur.

**2:21** And the whole army of his warriors who were counted was thirty-two thousand two hundred.

**2:22** In the tribe of the sons of Benjamin, the leader was Abidan son of Gideon.

**2:23** And the whole army of his warriors who were mustered was thirty-five thousand four hundred.

**2:24** All who were counted in the camp of Ephraim were one hundred eight thousand one hundred by their companies; they will set out third.

**2:25** Toward the northern side camped the sons of Dan, whose leader was Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.

**2:26** The whole army of his warriors who were counted was sixty-two thousand seven hundred.

**2:27** Next to him, those of the tribe of Asher pitched tents; their leader was Pagiel son of Ochran.

**2:28** The whole army of his warriors who were counted was forty-one thousand five hundred.

**2:29** Of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali, the leader was Ahira son of Enan.

**2:30** The whole army of his warriors was fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**2:31** All who were counted in the camp of Dan were one hundred fifty-seven thousand six hundred; and they will set out last."

**2:32** This is the number of the sons of Israel, by the houses of their kindred and the companies of the divided army: six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty.

**2:33** The Levites, however, were not counted among the sons of Israel, for so the Lord had directed Moses.

**2:34** And the sons of Israel did according to all that the Lord had commanded. They camped by their companies and set out by families and the houses of their fathers.

## Translator Notes

- **2:2** — The Vulgate calls the sanctuary "tabernaculum foederis" (tabernacle of the covenant) here and "tabernaculum testimonii" (tabernacle of the testimony) in 2:17, where the Hebrew has "tent of meeting" in both verses.
- **2:5** — Within the Lord's instructions (2:2-31), the Vulgate alternates between future tense for the camp layout and order of march (2:2-3, 9-10, 16-18, 24, 31) and past tense for several tribal encampments and leaders (2:5, 7, 12, 14, 18, 25, 27), reflecting the already completed organization and census of chapter 1.
- **2:14** — The Vulgate reads "Duel" (Deuel), matching Numbers 1:14, 7:42, and 10:20, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Reuel" here.
- **2:17** — Distinctively, the Vulgate describes the tabernacle of the testimony being lifted up through the services of the Levites and taken down just as it will be erected, whereas the Hebrew describes the tent of meeting setting out with the camp of the Levites in the midst of the camps, setting out just as they camp.
- **2:22** — The Vulgate reads "Gedeonis" (genitive of "Gedeon", i.e., Gideon), whereas the Hebrew text names Abidan's father "Gideoni".
