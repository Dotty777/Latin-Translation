## Text

**18:1** And the Lord said to Aaron, "You and your sons and the house of your father with you shall bear the iniquity of the sanctuary, and you and your sons together shall bear the sins of your priesthood.

**18:2** Also take with you your brothers from the tribe of Levi and the scepter of your father, and let them be on hand and minister to you; but you and your sons shall minister in the tabernacle of the testimony.

**18:3** And the Levites shall keep watch at your commands and over all the works of the tabernacle, provided only that they do not approach the vessels of the sanctuary and the altar, so that neither they die nor you perish together.

**18:4** Let them be with you and keep watch in the guard duties of the tabernacle and in all its ceremonies. No outsider shall mingle with you.

**18:5** Keep watch in the custody of the sanctuary and in the ministry of the altar, so that indignation may not arise upon the sons of Israel.

**18:6** I Myself have given you your brothers the Levites from the midst of the sons of Israel, and have handed them over as a gift to the Lord, so that they may serve in the ministries of His tabernacle.

**18:7** But you and your sons, guard your priesthood; and all things that pertain to the service of the altar and are within the veil shall be administered by the priests. If any outsider approaches, he shall be put to death."

**18:8** And the Lord spoke to Aaron, "Look, I have given you the custody of My firstfruits. All the things that are sanctified by the sons of Israel I have handed over to you and to your sons for the priestly office as everlasting ordinances.

**18:9** These things, therefore, you shall receive from those that are sanctified and have been offered to the Lord. Every offering, and sacrifice, and whatever is rendered to Me for sin and for guilt, and passes into the most holy things, shall be yours and your sons'.

**18:10** You shall eat it in the sanctuary; only males shall eat of it, because it has been consecrated to you.

**18:11** And the firstfruits that the sons of Israel vow and offer, I have given to you, and to your sons, and to your daughters, by a perpetual right; whoever is clean in your house shall eat them.

**18:12** All the marrow of the oil, and of the wine, and of the grain, whatever firstfruits they offer to the Lord, I have given to you.

**18:13** All the first produce of the crops that the ground yields and that are brought to the Lord shall go to your use; whoever is clean in your house shall eat them.

**18:14** Everything that the sons of Israel render by a vow shall be yours.

**18:15** Whatever first breaks forth from the womb of all flesh that they offer to the Lord, whether it is of human beings or of livestock, shall be yours by right, provided only that you accept a price for the firstborn of a human being and have every animal that is unclean redeemed,

**18:16** whose redemption shall be after one month, for five shekels of silver, by the weight of the sanctuary. A shekel has twenty obols.

**18:17** However, the firstborn of an ox, and of a sheep, and of a goat, you shall not have redeemed, because they have been sanctified to the Lord. Their blood alone you shall pour out upon the altar, and their fat you shall burn as a most sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**18:18** But the flesh shall go to your use, just like the consecrated breast and the right shoulder; they shall be yours.

**18:19** All the firstfruits of the sanctuary that the sons of Israel offer to the Lord I have given to you, and to your sons, and to your daughters, by a perpetual right. It is an everlasting covenant of salt before the Lord for you and for your sons."

**18:20** And the Lord said to Aaron, "In their land you shall possess nothing, nor shall you have a portion among them; I am your portion and your inheritance in the midst of the sons of Israel.

**18:21** And to the sons of Levi I have given all the tithes of Israel as a possession, in return for the ministry by which they serve Me in the tabernacle of the covenant,

**18:22** so that the sons of Israel may no longer approach the tabernacle, nor commit a deadly sin,

**18:23** with only the sons of Levi serving Me in the tabernacle and bearing the sins of the people. It shall be an everlasting ordinance throughout your generations. They shall possess nothing else,

**18:24** being content with the offering of the tithes, which I have set apart for their uses and necessities."

**18:25** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**18:26** "Command the Levites and declare to them, 'When you receive from the sons of Israel the tithes that I have given to you, offer the firstfruits of them to the Lord, that is, a tenth part of the tithe,

**18:27** so that it may be counted to you as an offering of firstfruits, both from the threshing floors and from the winepresses;

**18:28** and from all things whose firstfruits you receive, offer them to the Lord and give them to Aaron the priest.

**18:29** All the things that you offer from the tithes and set apart for the gifts of the Lord shall be the best and choicest.'

**18:30** And you shall say to them, 'If you offer all the finest and superior portions from the tithes, it shall be counted to you as though you had given firstfruits from the threshing floor and the winepress;

**18:31** and you shall eat them in all your places, both you and your families, because it is payment for the ministry by which you serve in the tabernacle of the testimony.

**18:32** And you shall not sin in this matter by reserving the choice and fat things for yourselves, so that you may not defile the offerings of the sons of Israel and die.'"

## Translator Notes

- **18:2** — "Scepter of your father" translates Latin "sceptrum patris tui," rendering Hebrew "shevet" in its sense of "rod" or "scepter" rather than "tribe."
- **18:8** — Throughout Chapter 18 (18:8, 11, 19, 26, 28, 30), the Vulgate translates the Hebrew term for "contribution" or "heave offering" ("terumah") as "primitiae" ("firstfruits").
- **18:12** — "Marrow" translates Latin "medullam," a literal Vulgate idiom for the richest or choicest part of the oil, wine, and grain (corresponding to Hebrew "fat").
- **18:16** — "Obols" translates Latin "obolos," a Greco-Roman unit of weight used in the Vulgate (following the Septuagint) in place of the Hebrew "gerah."
- **18:32** — Unlike the Hebrew (which states that the Levites will incur no sin once they contribute the best part), the Vulgate frames this verse as a prohibition against reserving the choice and fat portions for themselves ("egregia vobis et pinguia reservantes").
