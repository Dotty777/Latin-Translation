**7:4** Then the Lord said to Moses,

**7:5** "Accept them from them so that they may serve in the service of the tabernacle, and you will hand them over to the Levites according to the order of their service."

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — Vulgate complevit ... et erexit ("completed ... and erected") uses two coordinate finite verbs where the Hebrew reads "finished setting up."
- **7:13** — Throughout this chapter, the Vulgate renders the Hebrew grain offering (minchah) simply as sacrificium ("sacrifice") and the standard weight as pondus sanctuarii ("weight of the sanctuary") rather than "shekel of the sanctuary."
- **7:14** — Vulgate mortariolum ("small mortar") translates Hebrew kaph ("pan" or "ladle"); here it is described as ex decem siclis aureis ("made of ten gold shekels"), while later verses shift to habens ("of ten shekels," v. 20) and appendens ("weighing ten shekels," vv. 26, 32, 38, 44).
- **7:60** — Latin Gedeonis is the genitive of Gedeon ("Gideon"), whereas the Hebrew reads Gid'oni ("Gideoni"); the same form recurs in 7:65.
- **7:87** — Vulgate libamenta ("drink offerings" or "libations") differs from the Hebrew, which reads minchah ("grain offering").
- **7:89** — Vulgate tabernaculum foederis ("tabernacle of the covenant") renders Hebrew "tent of meeting," and ut consuleret oraculum ("to consult the oracle") stands where the Hebrew reads "to speak with Him."
