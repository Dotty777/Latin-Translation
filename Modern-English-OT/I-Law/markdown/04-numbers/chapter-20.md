## Text

**20:1** And the sons of Israel and the whole multitude came into the desert of Zin in the first month, and the people stayed at Kadesh. And Miriam died there and was buried in the same place.

**20:2** And when the people lacked water, they assembled against Moses and Aaron,

**20:3** and turning to sedition, they said, "If only we had perished among our brothers before the Lord!

**20:4** Why have you brought the assembly of the Lord out into the wilderness, so that both we and our livestock should die?

**20:5** Why have you made us come up out of Egypt and brought us into this worst of places, which cannot be sown, which produces neither fig nor vines nor pomegranates, and moreover has no water to drink?"

**20:6** And Moses and Aaron, having left the multitude, entered the tabernacle of the covenant, fell face down to the ground, and cried out to the Lord and said, "Lord God, hear the cry of this people, and open to them Your treasure, a spring of living water, so that, once they are satisfied, their murmuring may cease." And the glory of the Lord appeared over them.

**20:7** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**20:8** "Take the staff, and assemble the people, you and Aaron your brother, and speak to the rock before them, and it will give waters. And when you have brought water out of the rock, the whole multitude and its livestock will drink."

**20:9** So Moses took the staff that was in the sight of the Lord, just as He had commanded him;

**20:10** and when the multitude had been assembled before the rock, he said to them, "Listen, rebels and unbelievers: can we really bring water out of this rock for you?"

**20:11** And when Moses had raised his hand, striking the flint twice with the staff, most abundant waters came forth, so that the people and the livestock drank.

**20:12** And the Lord said to Moses and Aaron, "Because you did not believe Me, so as to sanctify Me before the sons of Israel, you will not bring these peoples into the land that I will give them."

**20:13** This is the water of contradiction, where the sons of Israel contended against the Lord, and He was sanctified among them.

**20:14** Meanwhile Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom to say, "Your brother Israel sends this message: 'You know all the hardship that has overtaken us,

**20:15** how our fathers went down into Egypt, and we dwelt there for a long time, and the Egyptians afflicted us and our fathers;

**20:16** and how we cried out to the Lord, and He heard us and sent an angel who brought us out of Egypt. Look, situated in the city of Kadesh, which is at the outermost edge of your borders,

**20:17** we implore that we be permitted to pass through your land. We will not go through fields or through vineyards; we will not drink waters from your wells; but we will walk along the public road, turning aside neither to the right nor to the left, until we cross your boundaries.'"

**20:18** Edom answered him, "You will not pass through me; otherwise I will come out armed to meet you."

**20:19** And the sons of Israel said, "We will walk along the beaten road; and if we drink your waters, we and our herds, we will give what is fair; there will be no difficulty over the price; only let us pass through quickly."

**20:20** But he answered, "You will not pass through." And immediately he went out to meet them with a countless multitude and a strong hand,

**20:21** and he was unwilling to yield to the one pleading with him, so as to grant passage through his borders. For this reason Israel turned away from him.

**20:22** And when they had moved camp from Kadesh, they came to Mount Hor, which is at the borders of the land of Edom,

**20:23** where the Lord spoke to Moses.

**20:24** "Let Aaron go," He said, "to his peoples; for he will not enter the land that I have given to the sons of Israel, because he was unbelieving toward My mouth at the waters of contradiction.

**20:25** Take Aaron and his son with him, and you shall lead them onto Mount Hor.

**20:26** And when you have stripped the father of his garment, you shall clothe Eleazar his son with it; Aaron will be gathered, and he will die there."

**20:27** Moses did as the Lord had commanded, and they went up onto Mount Hor before the whole multitude.

**20:28** And when he had stripped Aaron of his garments, he clothed Eleazar his son with them.

**20:29** When Aaron died on the brow of the mountain, Moses came down with Eleazar.

**20:30** Now the whole multitude, seeing that Aaron had died, wept over him for thirty days throughout all its families.

## Translator Notes

- **20:6** — The prayer of Moses and Aaron ("and they cried out to the Lord and said ... their murmuring may cease") is a notable Vulgate-distinct addition not present in the Hebrew Masoretic Text or the Septuagint.
- **20:13** — Latin aqua contradictionis ("water of contradiction," and plural aquas contradictionis in v. 24) translates the Hebrew place-name Meribah ("contention/strife") rather than transliterating it.
- **20:17** — Latin via publica ("the public road") renders Hebrew "the king's highway" in Roman idiom; likewise in v. 19 the Vulgate renders Hebrew's brief idiom ("only let me pass on foot") as "there will be no difficulty over the price; only let us pass through quickly."
- **20:24** — Latin eo quod incredulus fuerit ori meo ("because he was unbelieving toward My mouth," preserving the Hebraism "mouth" for spoken command) uses third-person singular for Aaron where Hebrew has second-person plural ("because you rebelled against My word"), echoing rebelles et increduli ("rebels and unbelievers") in v. 10.
- **20:28** — Native Vulgate Numbers 20 divides Aaron's death and mourning across 20:28-30 (mapping to Saints.Bible 20:28-29), ending with per cunctas familias suas ("throughout all its families") where the Hebrew has "all the house of Israel."
- **20:30** — Native Clementine Vulgate divides Aaron's death across 20:28-30 (mapped to Saints.Bible 20:28-29), ending with per cunctas familias suas ("throughout all its families") where the Hebrew has "all the house of Israel."
