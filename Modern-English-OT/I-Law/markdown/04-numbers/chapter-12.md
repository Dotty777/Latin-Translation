## Text

**12:1** And Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses on account of his Ethiopian wife,

**12:2** and they said, "Has the Lord spoken through Moses alone? Has He not also spoken to us in the same way?" When the Lord had heard this

**12:3** (for Moses was a most gentle man, above all people who lived on the earth),

**12:4** He spoke at once to him, and to Aaron and Miriam, "Go out, the three of you alone, to the tabernacle of the covenant." And when they had gone out,

**12:5** the Lord came down in a pillar of cloud and stood at the entrance of the tabernacle, calling Aaron and Miriam. When they had come,

**12:6** He said to them, "Hear My words: if anyone among you is a prophet of the Lord, I will appear to him in a vision, or I will speak to him through a dream.

**12:7** But My servant Moses, who is most faithful in all My house, is not like that:

**12:8** for I speak to him mouth to mouth; and openly, and not through riddles and figures, he sees the Lord. Why, then, were you not afraid to speak ill of My servant Moses?"

**12:9** And angry against them, He departed.

**12:10** The cloud that was over the tabernacle also withdrew, and look, Miriam appeared bright white with leprosy, like snow. And when Aaron had turned to look at her and seen her covered all over with leprosy,

**12:11** he said to Moses, "I implore you, my lord, do not lay upon us this sin that we have foolishly committed;

**12:12** do not let her become like one who is dead, and like a stillborn child that is cast out from its mother's womb; look, already half of her flesh has been devoured by leprosy."

**12:13** And Moses cried out to the Lord, saying, "God, I implore You, heal her."

**12:14** The Lord answered him, "If her father had spit in her face, should she not have been flushed with shame for at least seven days? Let her be separated outside the camp for seven days, and afterward she will be called back."

**12:15** Therefore Miriam was shut outside the camp for seven days, and the people did not move from that place until Miriam was called back.

**12:16** And the people set out from Hazeroth, pitching their tents in the wilderness of Paran.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — The Vulgate reads 'Aethiopissam' ("Ethiopian woman"), matching the Septuagint, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "Cushite woman."
- **12:4** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew "tent of meeting" as 'tabernaculum foederis' ("tabernacle of the covenant") and adds 'tantum' ("alone") to "the three of you."
- **12:6** — The Vulgate explicitly reads 'propheta Domini' ("a prophet of the Lord"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "your prophet" (or "a prophet among you") without "of the Lord" in this clause.
- **12:8** — In a Vulgate-distinct construction, 'et figuras' ("and figures") is governed by 'non per' ("not through") while 'Dominum' ("the Lord") is the direct object of 'videt' ("he sees"), whereas the Hebrew reads that Moses beholds "the form of the Lord."
