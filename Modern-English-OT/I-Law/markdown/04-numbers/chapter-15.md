## Text

**15:1** The Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**15:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'When you have entered the land of your dwelling, which I will give to you,

**15:3** and have made an offering to the Lord as a burnt offering or a sacrifice, paying vows, or willingly offering gifts, or in your solemn feasts burning a fragrance of sweetness to the Lord, from the oxen or from the sheep,

**15:4** whoever slaughters a sacrificial victim shall offer a sacrifice of fine flour, a tenth part of an ephah, mixed with oil that shall have the measure of a fourth part of a hin; and he shall give wine for pouring drink offerings of the same measure for a burnt offering or for a sacrifice. For individual lambs and rams there shall be a sacrifice of two tenths of fine flour, which shall be mixed with oil of a third part of a hin; and he shall offer wine for a drink offering of a third part of the same measure for a fragrance of sweetness to the Lord.

**15:8** But when you make a burnt offering or a sacrifice from the oxen, to fulfill a vow or peace offerings,

**15:9** you shall give for each ox three tenths of fine flour mixed with oil that shall have half the measure of a hin,

**15:10** and wine for pouring drink offerings of the same measure, for an offering of the sweetest fragrance to the Lord.

**15:11** So you shall do for each ox and ram and lamb and young goat. Both the native-born and foreigners shall offer sacrifices by the same rite. There shall be one precept and judgment both for you and for the strangers of the land.'" The Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'When you come into the land that I will give to you,

**15:19** and eat from the breads of that region, you shall set apart firstfruits to the Lord

**15:20** from your foods. Just as you set apart firstfruits from the threshing floors,

**15:21** so also from the cooked dishes you shall give firstfruits to the Lord.

**15:22** But if through ignorance you omit any of these things that the Lord has spoken to Moses,

**15:23** and has commanded to you through him, from the day on which He began to command and onward,

**15:24** and the multitude has forgotten to do so, it shall offer a calf from the herd as a burnt offering for a most sweet fragrance to the Lord, and its sacrifice and drink offerings, as the ceremonies require, and a male goat for sin;

**15:25** and the priest shall pray for the whole multitude of the sons of Israel, and it shall be forgiven them, because they did not sin willingly, nevertheless offering incense to the Lord for themselves and for their sin and error;

**15:26** and it shall be forgiven to the entire populace of the sons of Israel and to the strangers who sojourn among them, because it is a fault of all the people through ignorance.

**15:27** But if one soul sins unknowingly, he shall offer a year-old female goat for his sin;

**15:28** and the priest shall intercede for him, because he sinned unknowingly before the Lord; and he shall obtain pardon for him, and it shall be forgiven him.

**15:29** Both for the native-born and for the strangers, there shall be one law for all who sin in ignorance.

**15:30** But the soul who commits anything through pride, whether he is a citizen or a foreigner, because he has been rebellious against the Lord, shall perish from his people;

**15:31** for he has despised the word of the Lord and has made His precept void; therefore he shall be wiped out, and he shall bear his iniquity.'"

**15:32** Now it happened that when the sons of Israel were in the wilderness and had found a man gathering wood on the Sabbath day,

**15:33** they brought him to Moses and Aaron and to the entire multitude.

**15:34** And they confined him in prison, not knowing what they ought to do concerning him.

**15:35** And the Lord said to Moses, "Let this man surely die; let the whole crowd overwhelm him with stones outside the camp."

**15:36** And when they had led him outside, they overwhelmed him with stones, and he died, just as the Lord had commanded.

**15:37** The Lord also said to Moses,

**15:38** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them to make for themselves fringes along the corners of their cloaks, placing blue-violet ribbons in them,

**15:39** so that when they see them, they may remember all the commandments of the Lord, and may not follow their own thoughts and eyes, prostituting themselves among various things,

**15:40** but rather, mindful of the precepts of the Lord, may do them, and may be holy to their God.

**15:41** I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, so that I might be your God."

## Translator Notes

- **15:4** — The Clementine Vulgate attaches "For individual lambs" (Per agnos singulos) to "and rams" (et arietes) under the two-tenths flour and third-of-a-hin measure, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text closes the preceding one-tenth/fourth-of-a-hin measure with "for each lamb."
- **15:20** — Latin de cibis vestris ("from your foods") here and de pulmentis ("from the cooked dishes") in 15:21 are distinctly Vulgate renderings of the rare Hebrew term usually understood as coarse meal or dough.
- **15:25** — Latin incensum ("incense") here renders the Hebrew term for a fire-offering, and the Vulgate adds pro se ("for themselves") alongside "for their sin and error."
- **15:30** — Latin per superbiam ("through pride") renders the Hebrew idiom "with a high hand" (meaning defiant, deliberate sin), and rebellis fuit ("has been rebellious") stands where the Hebrew reads "blasphemes."
