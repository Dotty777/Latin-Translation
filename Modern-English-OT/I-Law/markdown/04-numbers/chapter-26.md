## Text

**26:1** After the blood of the guilty had been poured out, the Lord spoke to Moses and Eleazar the priest, the son of Aaron:

**26:2** "Count the entire total of the sons of Israel from twenty years old and above, by their houses and clans, all who are able to go out to war."

**26:3** So Moses and Eleazar the priest spoke on the plains of Moab by the Jordan opposite Jericho to those who were

**26:4** from twenty years old and above, just as the Lord had commanded; and this is their number.

**26:5** Reuben, the firstborn of Israel: his son, Hanoch, from whom came the family of the Hanochites; and Pallu, from whom came the family of the Palluites;

**26:6** and Hezron, from whom came the family of the Hezronites; and Carmi, from whom came the family of the Carmites.

**26:7** These are the families from the lineage of Reuben, whose number was found to be forty-three thousand seven hundred thirty.

**26:8** The son of Pallu was Eliab.

**26:9** His sons were Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram. These are the Dathan and Abiram, leaders of the people, who rose up against Moses and Aaron in the uprising of Korah, when they rebelled against the Lord,

**26:10** and the earth, opening its mouth, devoured Korah while very many died, when fire burned up two hundred fifty men. And a great miracle took place,

**26:11** that while Korah perished, his sons did not perish.

**26:12** The sons of Simeon by their clans: Nemuel, from him came the family of the Nemuelites; Jamin, from him came the family of the Jaminites; Jachin, from him came the family of the Jachinites;

**26:13** Zerah, from him came the family of the Zerahites; Shaul, from him came the family of the Shaulites.

**26:14** These are the families from the lineage of Simeon, whose entire number was twenty-two thousand two hundred.

**26:15** The sons of Gad by their clans: Zephon, from him came the family of the Zephonites; Haggi, from him came the family of the Haggites; Shuni, from him came the family of the Shunites;

**26:16** Ozni, from him came the family of the Oznites; Er, from him came the family of the Erites;

**26:17** Arod, from him came the family of the Arodites; Ariel, from him came the family of the Arielites.

**26:18** These are the families of Gad, whose entire number was forty thousand five hundred.

**26:19** The sons of Judah were Er and Onan, both of whom died in the land of Canaan.

**26:20** And the sons of Judah by their clans were: Shelah, from whom came the family of the Shelahites; Perez, from whom came the family of the Perezites; Zerah, from whom came the family of the Zerahites.

**26:21** Furthermore, the sons of Perez: Hezron, from whom came the family of the Hezronites; and Hamul, from whom came the family of the Hamulites.

**26:22** These are the families of Judah, whose entire number was seventy-six thousand five hundred.

**26:23** The sons of Issachar by their clans: Tola, from whom came the family of the Tolaites; Puah, from whom came the family of the Puahites;

**26:24** Jashub, from whom came the family of the Jashubites; Shimron, from whom came the family of the Shimronites.

**26:25** These are the clans of Issachar, whose number was sixty-four thousand three hundred.

**26:26** The sons of Zebulun by their clans: Sered, from whom came the family of the Seredites; Elon, from whom came the family of the Elonites; Jahleel, from whom came the family of the Jahleelites.

**26:27** These are the clans of Zebulun, whose number was sixty thousand five hundred.

**26:28** The sons of Joseph by their clans were Manasseh and Ephraim.

**26:29** From Manasseh was born Machir, from whom came the family of the Machirites. Machir fathered Gilead, from whom came the family of the Gileadites.

**26:30** Gilead had sons: Iezer, from whom came the family of the Iezerites; and Helek, from whom came the family of the Helekites;

**26:31** and Asriel, from whom came the family of the Asrielites; and Shechem, from whom came the family of the Shechemites;

**26:32** and Shemida, from whom came the family of the Shemidaites; and Hepher, from whom came the family of the Hepherites.

**26:33** Now Hepher was the father of Zelophehad, who did not have sons, but only daughters; and these are their names: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.

**26:34** These are the families of Manasseh, and their number was fifty-two thousand seven hundred.

**26:35** Now the sons of Ephraim by their clans were these: Shuthelah, from whom came the family of the Shuthelahites; Becher, from whom came the family of the Becherites; Tahan, from whom came the family of the Tahanites.

**26:36** Furthermore, the son of Shuthelah was Eran, from whom came the family of the Eranites.

**26:37** These are the clans of the sons of Ephraim, whose number was thirty-two thousand five hundred.

**26:38** These are the sons of Joseph by their families. The sons of Benjamin in their clans: Bela, from whom came the family of the Belaites; Ashbel, from whom came the family of the Ashbelites; Ahiram, from whom came the family of the Ahiramites;

**26:39** Shupham, from whom came the family of the Shuphamites; Hupham, from whom came the family of the Huphamites.

**26:40** The sons of Bela were Ard and Naaman. From Ard came the family of the Ardites; from Naaman came the family of the Naamanites.

**26:41** These are the sons of Benjamin by their clans, whose number was forty-five thousand six hundred.

**26:42** The sons of Dan by their clans: Shuham, from whom came the family of the Shuhamites. These are the clans of Dan by their families.

**26:43** All were Shuhamites, whose number was sixty-four thousand four hundred.

**26:44** The sons of Asher by their clans: Imnah, from whom came the family of the Imnites; Ishvi, from whom came the family of the Ishvites; Beriah, from whom came the family of the Beriites.

**26:45** The sons of Beriah: Heber, from whom came the family of the Heberites; and Malchiel, from whom came the family of the Malchielites.

**26:46** Now the name of the daughter of Asher was Serah.

**26:47** These are the clans of the sons of Asher, and their number was fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**26:48** The sons of Naphtali by their clans: Jahzeel, from whom came the family of the Jahzeelites; Guni, from whom came the family of the Gunites;

**26:49** Jezer, from whom came the family of the Jezerites; Shillem, from whom came the family of the Shillemites.

**26:50** These are the clans of the sons of Naphtali by their families, whose number was forty-five thousand four hundred.

**26:51** This is the total of the sons of Israel who were registered: six hundred one thousand seven hundred thirty.

**26:52** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**26:53** "To these the land shall be divided according to the number of names as their possessions.

**26:54** To the more numerous you shall give a larger share, and to the fewer a smaller one; to each, just as they have now been registered, a possession shall be handed over,

**26:55** provided only that the lot divides the land among the tribes and families.

**26:56** Whatever falls by lot, either the more numerous or the fewer shall receive it."

**26:57** This also is the number of the sons of Levi by their families: Gershon, from whom came the family of the Gershonites; Kohath, from whom came the family of the Kohathites; Merari, from whom came the family of the Merarites.

**26:58** These are the families of Levi: the family of Libni, the family of Hebron, the family of Mahli, the family of Mushi, the family of Korah. Now Kohath fathered Amram,

**26:59** who had Jochebed, the daughter of Levi, as his wife, who was born to him in Egypt. She bore sons for her husband Amram: Aaron and Moses, and their sister Miriam.

**26:60** From Aaron were born Nadab and Abihu, and Eleazar and Ithamar,

**26:61** of whom Nadab and Abihu died when they had offered foreign fire in the presence of the Lord.

**26:62** And all who were counted were twenty-three thousand of the male sex from one month old and above, because they were not registered among the sons of Israel, nor were they given a possession with the others.

**26:63** This is the number of the sons of Israel who were recorded by Moses and Eleazar the priest on the plains of Moab by the Jordan opposite Jericho,

**26:64** among whom there was not one of those who had previously been counted by Moses and Aaron in the desert of Sinai,

**26:65** for the Lord had foretold that all of them would die in the wilderness. And not one of them remained except Caleb the son of Jephunneh and Joshua the son of Nun.

## Translator Notes

- **26:1** — The Vulgate reads 'After the blood of the guilty had been poured out' (Postquam noxiorum sanguis effusus est), referring back to the executions in chapter 25, whereas the Masoretic Text reads 'After the plague.'
- **26:3** — In verses 3-4, the Vulgate has Moses and Eleazar speak directly to those twenty years old and above without quoting a census command or mentioning the departure from Egypt, transitioning straight to 'and this is their number.'
- **26:10** — The Vulgate reads 'devoured Korah while very many died' and links verses 10-11 into a single statement ('And a great miracle took place, that while Korah perished, his sons did not perish'), whereas the Masoretic Text says the earth swallowed them along with Korah so they became a warning sign, before separately stating that Korah's sons did not die.
- **26:15** — Native Vulgate Numbers 26:15-47 follow the Hebrew/Vulgate tribal sequence (Gad 26:15-18, Judah 26:19-22, Issachar 26:23-25, Zebulun 26:26-27, Manasseh 26:28-34, Ephraim 26:35-37, Benjamin 26:38-41, Dan 26:42-43, Asher 26:44-47), differing from Saints.Bible Septuagint-aligned order.
- **26:17** — The Vulgate names Gad's seventh son 'Ariel' (with the 'Arielites') and his fifth son 'Her' ('Er' in v. 16, matching Judah's son in v. 19), whereas the Masoretic Text has 'Areli' ('Arelites') and 'Eri'; likewise, Latin 'Selaitarum' (v. 20, 'Shelahites') and 'Phuaitarum' (v. 23, 'Puahites') lack the '-n-' of Hebrew 'Shelanites' and 'Punites.'
- **26:19** — In native Clementine Vulgate Numbers 26, Judah stands at vv. 19-22 after Gad (vv. 15-18), followed by Issachar (vv. 23-25) and Zebulun (vv. 26-27), matching the Hebrew tribal sequence (in Saints.Bible Septuagint-aligned order, Judah begins at 26:15 and Gad stands at 26:24-27).
- **26:39** — The Vulgate reads 'Supham' ('Shupham') rather than the Masoretic Text's longer form 'Shephupham,' and in v. 40 reads 'Noëmanitarum' ('Naamanites'), retaining the '-n-' where the Masoretic Text has 'Naamites.'
- **26:43** — The Vulgate reads 'All were Shuhamites' (Omnes fuere Suhamitae), whereas the Masoretic Text reads 'All the families of the Shuhamites according to those numbered of them.'
- **26:55** — In verses 55-56, the Vulgate specifies that the lot divides the land among the tribes and families and directs that both the more numerous and the fewer must accept whatever falls by lot, differing in structure from the Masoretic Text's statement that the inheritance is divided by lot between the larger and the smaller.
- **26:58** — Unlike the '-itarum' clan endings used in verse 57, the Vulgate lists these five Levite divisions as 'familia Lobni, familia Hebroni, familia Moholi, familia Musi, familia Core,' rendered here with the ancestor names Libni, Hebron, Mahli, Mushi, and Korah.
