## Text

**16:1** Now look, Korah the son of Izhar, the son of Kohath, the son of Levi, and Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, and also On the son of Peleth, of the sons of Reuben,

**16:2** rose up against Moses, together with two hundred fifty other men of the sons of Israel, leaders of the assembly, and those who were called by name at the time of council.

**16:3** And when they had stood against Moses and Aaron, they said, "Let it be enough for you, since the whole multitude consists of holy ones, and the Lord is among them; why do you exalt yourselves above the people of the Lord?"

**16:4** When Moses heard this, he fell prostrate on his face;

**16:5** and speaking to Korah and to the whole multitude, "In the morning," he said, "the Lord will make known who belong to Him, and He will draw the holy ones to Himself; and those whom He chooses will draw near to Him.

**16:6** Therefore do this: let everyone take their censers, you, Korah, and all your council;

**16:7** and tomorrow, having drawn fire, put incense on top before the Lord; and whomever He chooses, he shall be holy. You raise yourselves up too much, sons of Levi."

**16:8** And he said again to Korah, "Listen, sons of Levi:

**16:9** is it too little for you that the God of Israel has separated you from all the people and joined you to Himself, so that you might serve Him in the worship of the tabernacle, and stand before the throng of the people, and minister to Him?

**16:10** Did He therefore cause you and all your brothers, the sons of Levi, to draw near to Himself, so that you might also claim the priesthood for yourselves,

**16:11** and that your entire band might stand against the Lord? For what is Aaron, that you murmur against him?"

**16:12** Therefore Moses sent to summon Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab. And they answered, "We are not coming.

**16:13** Is it too little for you that you brought us out of a land that flowed with milk and honey, to kill us in the desert, unless you also rule over us?

**16:14** Indeed, you have brought us into a land that flows with streams of milk and honey, and have given us possessions of fields and vineyards; or do you also want to gouge out our eyes? We are not coming."

**16:15** And Moses, becoming very angry, said to the Lord, "Do not regard their sacrifices; You know that I have never taken even a little donkey from them, nor have I afflicted any one of them."

**16:16** And he said to Korah, "You and all your congregation, stand apart before the Lord, and Aaron separately tomorrow.

**16:17** Take each of you your censers, and put incense upon them, offering to the Lord two hundred fifty censers; let Aaron also hold his censer."

**16:18** When they had done this, while Moses and Aaron were standing,

**16:19** and had amassed the whole multitude against them at the entrance of the tabernacle, the glory of the Lord appeared to all.

**16:20** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

**16:21** "Separate yourselves from the midst of this congregation, so that I may suddenly destroy them."

**16:22** And they fell prostrate on their faces and said, "Most mighty God of the spirits of all flesh, when one sins, will Your wrath rage against all?"

**16:23** And the Lord said to Moses,

**16:24** "Command the entire people to separate themselves from the tents of Korah and Dathan and Abiram."

**16:25** And Moses rose up and went to Dathan and Abiram; and with the elders of Israel following him,

**16:26** he said to the crowd, "Depart from the tents of the wicked men, and do not touch what belongs to them, so that you may not be entangled in their sins."

**16:27** And when they had departed from their tents all around, Dathan and Abiram came out and stood at the entrance of their pavilions with their wives and children and all the throng.

**16:28** And Moses said, "By this you will know that the Lord has sent me to do all the things that you see, and that I have not brought them forth from my own heart:

**16:29** if they perish by the ordinary death of men, and a stroke visits them with which the rest also are accustomed to be visited, the Lord has not sent me;

**16:30** but if the Lord does a new thing, so that the earth, opening its mouth, swallows them up and all things that belong to them, and they go down alive into the underworld, you will know that they have blasphemed the Lord."

**16:31** Immediately, therefore, as he ceased speaking, the earth split apart beneath their feet,

**16:32** and opening its mouth, it devoured them with their tents and all their possessions;

**16:33** and they went down alive into the underworld, covered with earth, and perished from the midst of the multitude.

**16:34** But all Israel, who was standing all around, fled at the cry of those who were perishing, saying, "The earth might swallow us up too!"

**16:35** Moreover, fire coming out from the Lord killed the two hundred fifty men who were offering incense.

**16:36** Then the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**16:37** "Command Eleazar the priest, the son of Aaron, to take up the censers that lie in the blaze and scatter the fire here and there, since they have been sanctified

**16:38** in the deaths of the sinners; and let him beat them out into plates and fasten them to the altar, because incense was offered in them to the Lord and they have been sanctified, so that the sons of Israel may see them as a sign and a reminder."

**16:39** Therefore Eleazar the priest took the bronze censers in which those whom the blaze devoured had offered, and beat them out into plates, fastening them to the altar,

**16:40** so that afterward the sons of Israel might have things by which to be reminded that no outsider, and no one who is not of the seed of Aaron, may approach to offer incense to the Lord, so that he may not suffer as Korah and all his congregation suffered, as the Lord spoke to Moses.

**16:41** Now the whole multitude of the sons of Israel grumbled on the following day against Moses and Aaron, saying, "You have killed the people of the Lord."

**16:42** And when a rebellion arose and the uproar grew,

**16:43** Moses and Aaron fled to the tabernacle of the covenant. After they entered it, the cloud covered it, and the glory of the Lord appeared.

**16:44** Then the Lord said to Moses:

**16:45** "Withdraw from the midst of this multitude; even now I will destroy them." And while they were lying on the ground,

**16:46** Moses said to Aaron, "Take the censer, and having drawn fire from the altar, put incense on top, going quickly to the people so that you may pray for them; for wrath has already gone out from the Lord, and the plague is raging."

**16:47** When Aaron had done this and had run into the midst of the multitude, which the blaze was already devastating, he offered the incense,

**16:48** and standing between the dead and the living, he pleaded for the people, and the plague ceased.

**16:49** Now those who were struck down were fourteen thousand seven hundred people, apart from those who had perished in the rebellion of Korah.

**16:50** And Aaron returned to Moses at the entrance of the tabernacle of the covenant after the destruction had subsided.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — The Vulgate introduces the rebellion with Ecce autem ("Now look") and joins Korah, Dathan, Abiram, and On directly to "rose up" (surrexerunt) in 16:2, smoothing a difficult Hebrew verb ("took") that lacks an explicit object.
- **16:9** — Latin singular ei ("to Him") is grammatically ambiguous: it naturally parallels the preceding serviretis ei ("serve Him," referring to God), though it can also refer to frequentia ("the throng," corresponding to Hebrew plural "them").
- **16:14** — Unlike the Hebrew negative ("you have not brought us"), the Vulgate has the affirmative Revera induxisti nos ("Indeed, you have brought us") and reads "our eyes" (oculos nostros) rather than "the eyes of these men."
- **16:30** — Latin in infernum ("into the underworld") is the Vulgate's standard term for the realm of the dead (Hebrew Sheol), and blasphemaverint ("have blasphemed") renders Hebrew "have despised/spurned."
- **16:35** — In this versification, as in the Hebrew, Numbers 16 ends at 16:35, and the subsequent account of the bronze censers begins Numbers 17.
- **16:38** — The Vulgate reads "in the deaths of the sinners" (Latin "in mortibus peccatorum"), whereas the Hebrew reads "at the cost of their lives."
- **16:42** — In native Clementine Vulgate numbering, 16:36-50 conclude chapter 16 (mapped to Saints.Bible 17:1-15).
- **16:47** — The Vulgate uses "incendium" ("blaze" or "fire") for what was devastating the multitude, matching 17:2 and 17:4, whereas the Hebrew refers here to "the plague."
