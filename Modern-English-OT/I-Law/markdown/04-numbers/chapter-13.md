## Text

**13:1** And there the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**13:2** "Send men to examine the land of Canaan, which I am about to give to the sons of Israel, one from each tribe, from among the leaders."

**13:3** Moses did what the Lord had commanded, sending leading men from the wilderness of Paran, whose names are these:

**13:4** From the tribe of Reuben, Shammua son of Zaccur.

**13:5** From the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat son of Hori.

**13:6** From the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh.

**13:7** From the tribe of Issachar, Igal son of Joseph.

**13:8** From the tribe of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Nun.

**13:9** From the tribe of Benjamin, Palti son of Raphu.

**13:10** From the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel son of Sodi.

**13:11** From the tribe of Joseph, of the scepter of Manasseh, Gaddi son of Susi.

**13:12** From the tribe of Dan, Ammiel son of Gemalli.

**13:13** From the tribe of Asher, Sethur son of Michael.

**13:14** From the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi son of Vophsi.

**13:15** From the tribe of Gad, Geuel son of Machi.

**13:16** These are the names of the men whom Moses sent to examine the land; and he called Hoshea, son of Nun, Joshua.

**13:17** So Moses sent them to examine the land of Canaan, and said to them, "Go up through the southern region. And when you come to the mountains,

**13:18** examine the land, what kind it is, and the people who are its inhabitants, whether they are strong or weak, whether few in number or many;

**13:19** the land itself, good or bad; what the cities are like, walled or without walls;

**13:20** the soil, rich or barren, wooded or without trees. Take courage, and bring us some of the fruits of the land." Now it was the season when the early grapes can already be eaten.

**13:21** And when they had gone up, they scouted the land from the wilderness of Zin as far as Rehob, as one enters Hamath.

**13:22** And they went up to the south and came to Hebron, where Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, the sons of Anak, were; for Hebron was founded seven years before Tanis, a city of Egypt.

**13:23** And going on as far as the Stream of the Cluster, they cut off a vine branch with its bunch of grapes, which two men carried on a pole. They also took some of the pomegranates and the figs of that place,

**13:24** which was called Nehelescol, that is, the Stream of the Cluster, because the sons of Israel had carried a cluster from there.

**13:25** And the scouts of the land, returning after forty days, having traveled around the entire region,

**13:26** came to Moses and Aaron and to the whole assembly of the sons of Israel in the wilderness of Paran, which is in Kadesh. And speaking to them and to the whole multitude, they showed the fruits of the land,

**13:27** and they reported, saying, "We came into the land to which you sent us, which truly flows with milk and honey, as can be known from these fruits;

**13:28** but it has very strong inhabitants, and large and walled cities. We saw the offspring of Anak there.

**13:29** Amalek lives in the south; the Hittites, the Jebusites, and the Amorites in the hill country; while the Canaanites dwell beside the sea and along the streams of the Jordan."

**13:30** Meanwhile, Caleb, quieting the murmuring of the people, who were rising up against Moses, said, "Let us go up and possess the land, for we will be able to obtain it."

**13:31** But the others, who had been with him, were saying, "By no means are we able to go up against this people, because they are stronger than we are."

**13:32** And before the sons of Israel they spoke ill of the land that they had inspected, saying, "The land that we traversed devours its inhabitants; the people whom we looked upon are of tall stature.

**13:33** There we saw certain monsters of the sons of Anak, of the giant race, compared to whom we seemed like locusts."

## Translator Notes

- **13:11** — Where the Hebrew repeats 'matteh' ("staff/tribe"), the Vulgate renders the second occurrence literally as 'sceptri' ("of the scepter") alongside 'tribu' ("tribe").
- **13:22** — The Vulgate uses the Greco-Latin name 'Tanim' (Tanis), matching the Septuagint, for the Egyptian city called Zoan in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **13:24** — The Vulgate transliterates the Hebrew place name 'Nahal Eshcol' as a single name 'Nehelescol' and supplies the explanatory gloss 'id est Torrens botri' ("that is, the Stream of the Cluster").
- **13:30** — The Vulgate expands the Hebrew ("Caleb quieted the people before Moses") by explicitly naming 'murmur populi, qui oriebatur contra Moysen' ("the murmuring of the people, who were rising up against Moses").
- **13:33** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew 'Nephilim' as 'monstra quaedam ... de genere giganteo' ("certain monsters ... of the giant race") and omits the Masoretic Text's final clause ("in our own sight, and so we were in their sight"), ending with 'quasi locustae videbamur' ("we seemed like locusts").
