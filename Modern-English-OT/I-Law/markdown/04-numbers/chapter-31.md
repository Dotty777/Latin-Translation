## Text

**31:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:2** "First avenge the sons of Israel on the Midianites, and so you will be gathered to your people."

**31:3** And immediately Moses said, "Arm men from among you for battle, who can exact the vengeance of the Lord on the Midianites.

**31:4** Let a thousand men from each tribe be chosen out of Israel to be sent to war."

**31:5** And they gave a thousand from each tribe, that is, twelve thousand equipped for battle,

**31:6** whom Moses sent with Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest; he also delivered to him the holy vessels and the trumpets for sounding a blast.

**31:7** And when they had fought against the Midianites and were victorious, they killed all the males,

**31:8** and their kings, Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, five leaders of the nation; they also put Balaam the son of Beor to death with the sword.

**31:9** And they captured their women and little ones, and all the livestock, and all the household goods; whatever they had been able to possess, they pillaged.

**31:10** Flame consumed cities as well as villages and strongholds.

**31:11** And they took the plunder and everything they had captured, of people as well as of beasts,

**31:12** and brought them to Moses and Eleazar the priest, and to the whole multitude of the sons of Israel; but the remaining articles they carried to the camp on the plains of Moab by the Jordan, opposite Jericho.

**31:13** Now Moses and Eleazar the priest, and all the leaders of the assembly, went out to meet them outside the camp.

**31:14** And Moses, angry with the leaders of the army, the tribunes, and the centurions who had come from the war,

**31:15** said, "Why have you spared the women?

**31:16** Are these not the ones who deceived the sons of Israel at the suggestion of Balaam, and made you transgress against the Lord over the sin of Peor, because of which the people were also struck?

**31:17** Therefore, put to death all who are of the male sex, even among the little ones, and kill the women who have known men in intercourse;

**31:18** but spare for yourselves the girls and all the virgin women,

**31:19** and remain outside the camp for seven days. Anyone who has killed a person or touched someone who was killed shall be cleansed on the third and seventh day.

**31:20** And of all the plunder, whether it is a garment, or a vessel, or anything prepared as an article of use from goatskins, hair, and wood, it shall be purified."

**31:21** Eleazar the priest also spoke in this way to the men of the army who had fought: "This is the precept of the law that the Lord commanded Moses:

**31:22** gold, silver, bronze, iron, lead, and tin,

**31:23** and everything that can pass through the flames shall be purged by fire; but whatever cannot endure the fire shall be sanctified by the water of expiation;

**31:24** and you shall wash your garments on the seventh day, and once purified, you shall enter the camp afterward."

**31:25** The Lord also said to Moses,

**31:26** "Take the sum of what has been captured, from person to livestock, you and Eleazar the priest and the leaders of the people;

**31:27** and you shall divide the plunder equally between those who fought and went out to war and all the remaining multitude.

**31:28** And you shall set apart a portion for the Lord from those who fought and were in the war, one soul out of five hundred, from people as well as from oxen, donkeys, and sheep,

**31:29** and you shall give it to Eleazar the priest, because it is the firstfruits of the Lord.

**31:30** Also from the half portion of the sons of Israel, you shall take every fiftieth head of the people, oxen, donkeys, and sheep, of all living creatures, and you shall give them to the Levites who stand guard in the watches of the tabernacle of the Lord."

**31:31** And Moses and Eleazar did just as the Lord had commanded.

**31:32** Now the plunder that the army had captured was: of sheep, six hundred seventy-five thousand;

**31:33** of oxen, seventy-two thousand;

**31:34** of donkeys, sixty-one thousand;

**31:35** human souls of the female sex who had not known men, thirty-two thousand.

**31:36** And the half portion was given to those who had been in the battle: of sheep, three hundred thirty-seven thousand five hundred;

**31:37** from which six hundred seventy-five sheep were reckoned for the Lord's portion;

**31:38** and from thirty-six thousand oxen, seventy-two oxen;

**31:39** from thirty thousand five hundred donkeys, sixty-one donkeys;

**31:40** from sixteen thousand human souls, thirty-two souls fell to the Lord's portion.

**31:41** And Moses delivered the number of the firstfruits of the Lord to Eleazar the priest, just as he had been commanded,

**31:42** from the half portion of the sons of Israel that he had set apart for those who had been in the battle.

**31:43** But from the half portion that had fallen to the remaining multitude, that is, from three hundred thirty-seven thousand five hundred sheep,

**31:44** and from thirty-six thousand oxen,

**31:45** and from thirty thousand five hundred donkeys,

**31:46** and from sixteen thousand people,

**31:47** Moses took every fiftieth head and gave them to the Levites who stood guard in the tabernacle of the Lord, just as the Lord had commanded.

**31:48** And when the leaders of the army, and the tribunes and centurions, had approached Moses, they said,

**31:49** "We, your servants, have counted the number of the fighting men whom we had under our hand, and not even one was missing.

**31:50** For this reason we offer among the gifts of the Lord, each of us, what gold we were able to find in the plunder—anklets and armlets, rings and bracelets, and chain necklaces—so that you may pray to the Lord for us."

**31:51** And Moses and Eleazar the priest received all the gold in various forms,

**31:52** sixteen thousand seven hundred fifty shekels by weight, from the tribunes and centurions.

**31:53** For what each one had seized in the plunder was his own.

**31:54** And they brought what they had received into the tabernacle of the testimony, as a memorial of the sons of Israel before the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **31:7** — The Vulgate adds atque vicissent ("and were victorious") and omits the Hebrew clause "as the Lord commanded Moses."
- **31:14** — The Vulgate renders the Israelite officers of thousands and hundreds with the Roman military titles tribunis ("tribunes") and centurionibus ("centurions"), which also recur in vv. 48 and 52.
- **31:29** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew tribute/contribution (terumah) as primitiae ("firstfruits"), a distinctive choice repeated in v. 41.
- **31:42** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which begins the congregation's half here, the Vulgate joins v. 42 to v. 41 with the dative his ("for those who had been in the battle"), then contrasts it with the remaining multitude's half in v. 43 (De media vero parte).
- **31:50** — The Vulgate translates the final two golden ornaments as dextralia ("bracelets" for the right arm or wrist) and muraenulas ("chain necklaces"), and renders the closing purpose clause as a request for Moses's intercession (ut depreceris pro nobis Dominum, "so that you may pray to the Lord for us") rather than Hebrew "to make atonement for our souls before the Lord."
