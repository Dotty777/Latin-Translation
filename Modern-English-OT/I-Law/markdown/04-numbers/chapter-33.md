## Text

**33:1** These are the stopping places of the sons of Israel, who departed from Egypt by their companies under the hand of Moses and Aaron,

**33:2** which Moses wrote down according to the sites of the camps, which they changed at the command of the Lord.

**33:3** Therefore, setting out from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month, the day after the Passover, the sons of Israel, with an uplifted hand, while all the Egyptians were watching

**33:4** and burying the firstborn whom the Lord had struck (for He had also executed vengeance upon their gods),

**33:5** camped at Succoth.

**33:6** And from Succoth they came to Etham, which is at the outermost borders of the wilderness.

**33:7** Departing from there, they came opposite Pi-hahiroth, which faces Baal-zephon, and they camped in front of Migdol.

**33:8** And setting out from Pi-hahiroth, they passed through the middle of the sea into the wilderness; and walking for three days through the desert of Etham, they camped at Marah.

**33:9** And setting out from Marah, they came to Elim, where there were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees; and there they camped.

**33:10** Moreover, departing from there, they pitched tents by the Red Sea. And setting out from the Red Sea,

**33:11** they camped in the desert of Sin.

**33:12** Departing from there, they came to Dophkah.

**33:13** And setting out from Dophkah, they camped at Alush.

**33:14** And departing from Alush, they pitched tents at Rephidim, where the people lacked water to drink.

**33:15** And setting out from Rephidim, they camped in the desert of Sinai.

**33:16** Moreover, departing from the wilderness of Sinai, they came to the Graves of Craving.

**33:17** And setting out from the Graves of Craving, they camped at Hazeroth.

**33:18** And from Hazeroth they came to Rithmah.

**33:19** And setting out from Rithmah, they camped at Rimmon-perez.

**33:20** Departing from there, they came to Libnah.

**33:21** From Libnah they camped at Rissah.

**33:22** And departing from Rissah, they came to Kehelathah.

**33:23** Setting out from there, they camped at Mount Shepher.

**33:24** Departing from Mount Shepher, they came to Haradah.

**33:25** Setting out from there, they camped at Makheloth.

**33:26** And setting out from Makheloth, they came to Tahath.

**33:27** From Tahath they camped at Terah.

**33:28** Departing from there, they pitched tents at Mithkah.

**33:29** And from Mithkah they camped at Hashmonah.

**33:30** And setting out from Hashmonah, they came to Moseroth.

**33:31** And from Moseroth they camped at Bene-jaakan.

**33:32** And setting out from Bene-jaakan, they came to Mount Gadgad.

**33:33** Setting out from there, they camped at Jotbathah.

**33:34** And from Jotbathah they came to Abronah.

**33:35** And departing from Abronah, they camped at Ezion-geber.

**33:36** Setting out from there, they came to the desert of Zin, that is, Kadesh.

**33:37** And departing from Kadesh, they camped at Mount Hor, at the outermost borders of the land of Edom.

**33:38** And Aaron the priest went up onto Mount Hor as the Lord commanded; and there he died in the fortieth year of the departure of the sons of Israel from Egypt, in the fifth month, on the first day of the month,

**33:39** when he was one hundred twenty-three years old.

**33:40** And the Canaanite, king of Arad, who lived in the south, heard that the sons of Israel had come into the land of Canaan.

**33:41** And setting out from Mount Hor, they camped at Zalmonah.

**33:42** Departing from there, they came to Punon.

**33:43** And setting out from Punon, they camped at Oboth.

**33:44** And from Oboth they came to Iye-abarim, which is on the borders of the Moabites.

**33:45** And setting out from Iye-abarim, they pitched tents at Dibon-gad.

**33:46** Departing from there, they camped at Almon-diblathaim.

**33:47** And departing from Almon-diblathaim, they came to the mountains of Abarim opposite Nebo.

**33:48** And setting out from the mountains of Abarim, they passed over to the plains of Moab, by the Jordan, opposite Jericho.

**33:49** And there they camped from Beth-jeshimoth as far as Abel-shittim in the more level places of the Moabites.

**33:50** There the Lord spoke to Moses:

**33:51** "Command the sons of Israel and say to them, 'When you cross the Jordan, entering the land of Canaan,

**33:52** destroy all the inhabitants of that land; shatter the monuments, and crush the statues, and lay waste all the high places,

**33:53** cleansing the land and living in it. For I have given it to you as a possession,

**33:54** which you will divide among yourselves by lot. To the more numerous you will give a broader portion, and to the few a narrower one. To each, as the lot falls, so the inheritance will be assigned. By tribes and families the possession will be divided.

**33:55** But if you are unwilling to kill the inhabitants of the land, those who remain will be to you like nails in your eyes and spears in your sides, and they will oppose you in the land of your dwelling;

**33:56** and whatever I had planned to do to them, I will do to you.'"

## Translator Notes

- **33:2** — The Vulgate omits the Hebrew's repeated second clause and links "at the command of the Lord" directly to the Israelites' changing of their camp sites (quae Domini jussione mutabant).
- **33:16** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew place name Kibroth-hattaavah into Latin as sepulchra concupiscentiae ("the Graves of Craving"; cf. Numbers 11:34).
- **33:32** — The Vulgate reads montem Gadgad ("Mount Gadgad"), interpreting the first element of the Hebrew place name Hor-haggidgad as "mount" (hor).
- **33:40** — With the accusative in terram Chanaan ("into the land of Canaan"), the Vulgate construes the phrase as the destination of Israel's arrival rather than as part of the description of where the king of Arad lived (as in the Hebrew).
- **33:52** — In verses 52-55, the Vulgate intensifies the Hebrew commands to "drive out" and "dispossess" into disperdite ("destroy"), mundantes terram ("cleansing the land," v. 53), and interficere ("kill," v. 55), and renders the Hebrew "figured stones" as titulos ("monuments").
