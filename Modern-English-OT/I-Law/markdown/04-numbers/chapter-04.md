## Text

**4:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:

**4:2** "Take the total of the sons of Kohath from the midst of the Levites, by their houses and families,

**4:3** from the thirtieth year and upward, up to the fiftieth year, of all who enter to stand and minister in the tabernacle of the covenant.

**4:4** This is the service of the sons of Kohath: into the tabernacle of the covenant and the Holy of Holies

**4:5** Aaron and his sons shall enter when the camp is to be moved, and they shall take down the veil that hangs before the doors, and wrap the ark of the testimony with it,

**4:6** and they shall cover it again with a covering of violet skins, and spread an entirely blue cloth over it, and insert the poles.

**4:7** They shall also wrap the table of presentation in a blue cloth, and place with it the censers and small mortars, the cups and bowls for pouring libations; the loaves shall always be on it;

**4:8** and they shall spread a scarlet cloth over it, which they shall cover again with a covering of violet skins, and insert the poles.

**4:9** They shall also take a blue cloth, with which they shall cover the lampstand with its lamps and tongs and snuffers, and all the oil vessels that are needed for preparing the lamps;

**4:10** and over everything they shall place a covering of violet skins, and insert the poles.

**4:11** Likewise, they shall wrap the golden altar in a blue cloth, and spread a covering of violet skins over it, and insert the poles.

**4:12** All the vessels with which ministry is performed in the sanctuary they shall wrap in a blue cloth, and spread a covering of violet skins over them, and insert the poles.

**4:13** Moreover, they shall clean the altar of ashes, and wrap it in a purple cloth,

**4:14** and place with it all the vessels that they use in its ministry, that is, the fire receptacles, the small forks and three-pronged forks, the hooks, and the shovels. All the vessels of the altar they shall cover together with a covering of violet skins, and insert the poles.

**4:15** And when Aaron and his sons have wrapped the sanctuary and all its vessels at the moving of the camp, then the sons of Kohath shall enter to carry the wrapped things; and they shall not touch the vessels of the sanctuary, so that they do not die. These are the burdens of the sons of Kohath in the tabernacle of the covenant:

**4:16** over whom shall be Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, to whose care belong the oil for preparing the lamps, and the compounded incense, and the sacrifice that is always offered, and the anointing oil, and whatever pertains to the service of the tabernacle and of all the vessels that are in the sanctuary."

**4:17** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:

**4:18** "Do not destroy the people of Kohath from the midst of the Levites,

**4:19** but do this for them, so that they may live and not die if they touch the most holy things. Aaron and his sons shall enter, and they themselves shall arrange the tasks of each one and apportion what each one is to carry.

**4:20** Others shall not look with any curiosity at the things that are in the sanctuary before they are wrapped; otherwise they will die."

**4:21** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**4:22** "Take the total of the sons of Gershon also, by their houses and families and clans,

**4:23** from thirty years and upward, up to fifty years. Count all who enter and minister in the tabernacle of the covenant.

**4:24** This is the duty of the family of the Gershonites,

**4:25** to carry the curtains of the tabernacle and the roof of the covenant, the other covering, and over all the violet covering, and the tent-curtain that hangs at the entrance of the tabernacle of the covenant,

**4:26** the curtains of the courtyard, and the veil at the entrance that is before the tabernacle. All things that pertain to the altar, the cords, and the vessels of ministry,

**4:27** at the command of Aaron and his sons, the sons of Gershon shall carry; and each one shall know to what burden he must be assigned.

**4:28** This is the service of the family of the Gershonites in the tabernacle of the covenant, and they shall be under the hand of Ithamar, the son of Aaron the priest.

**4:29** You shall also register the sons of Merari by families and the houses of their fathers,

**4:30** from thirty years and upward, up to fifty years, all who enter for the duty of their ministry and the service of the covenant of the testimony.

**4:31** These are their burdens: they shall carry the boards of the tabernacle and its bars, the pillars and their bases,

**4:32** also the pillars of the courtyard all around with their bases and pegs and ropes. They shall receive all the vessels and equipment by number, and so they shall carry them.

**4:33** This is the duty of the family of the Merarites and the ministry in the tabernacle of the covenant, and they shall be under the hand of Ithamar, the son of Aaron the priest."

**4:34** Therefore Moses and Aaron and the leaders of the assembly registered the sons of Kohath by clans and the houses of their fathers,

**4:35** from thirty years and upward, up to the fiftieth year, all who enter for the ministry of the tabernacle of the covenant;

**4:36** and there were found two thousand seven hundred fifty.

**4:37** This is the number of the people of Kohath who enter the tabernacle of the covenant; these Moses and Aaron counted according to the word of the Lord by the hand of Moses.

**4:38** The sons of Gershon were also counted by clans and the houses of their fathers,

**4:39** from thirty years and upward, up to the fiftieth year, all who enter to minister in the tabernacle of the covenant;

**4:40** and there were found two thousand six hundred thirty.

**4:41** This is the people of the Gershonites, whom Moses and Aaron counted according to the word of the Lord.

**4:42** The sons of Merari were also counted by clans and the houses of their fathers,

**4:43** from thirty years and upward, up to the fiftieth year, all who enter to fulfill the rites of the tabernacle of the covenant;

**4:44** and there were found three thousand two hundred.

**4:45** This is the number of the sons of Merari, whom Moses and Aaron registered according to the command of the Lord by the hand of Moses.

**4:46** All who were registered from the Levites, and whom Moses and Aaron and the leaders of Israel caused to be registered by name, by clans and the houses of their fathers,

**4:47** from thirty years and upward, up to the fiftieth year, entering for the ministry of the tabernacle and for carrying the burdens,

**4:48** were altogether eight thousand five hundred eighty.

**4:49** According to the word of the Lord, Moses registered them, each one according to his duty and burdens, just as the Lord had commanded him.

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — The Clementine Vulgate links verses 4 and 5 syntactically, making "the tabernacle of the covenant and the Holy of Holies" (tabernaculum foederis, et Sanctum sanctorum) the direct object of "shall enter" (ingredientur), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text ends verse 4 with "the most holy things."
- **4:6** — The Vulgate renders the obscure Hebrew hide term (tachash) by color as "violet skins" (janthinarum pellium; also in vv. 8, 10, 11, 12, 14, and 25), keeping it distinct from hyacinthinus ("blue") and purpureus ("purple").
- **4:14** — The Vulgate lists five altar implements (fire receptacles, small forks, three-pronged forks, hooks, and shovels) and specifies "All the vessels of the altar" (Cuncta vasa altaris), whereas the Hebrew text lists four implements and reads simply "upon it."
- **4:20** — "Others shall not look with any curiosity" translates the distinctly Vulgate phrasing Alii nulla curiositate videant; the Hebrew Masoretic Text forbids going in to see the holy things even for a moment (or as they are covered).
- **4:32** — The Vulgate reads "by number" (ad numerum) for receiving the equipment, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "by name."
