## Text

**27:1** Now the daughters of Zelophehad, son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, who was the son of Joseph, approached; their names are Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.

**27:2** And they stood before Moses and Eleazar the priest and all the leaders of the people at the entrance of the tabernacle of the covenant, and said:

**27:3** "Our father died in the wilderness, and he was not in the rebellion that was stirred up against the Lord under Korah, but he died in his own sin; he had no male children. Why is his name taken away from his family because he had no son? Give us a possession among the relatives of our father."

**27:4** And Moses brought their case to the judgment of the Lord.

**27:5** He said to him,

**27:6** "The daughters of Zelophehad ask for what is just: give them a possession among the relatives of their father, and let them succeed him in the inheritance.

**27:7** And to the sons of Israel you shall speak these words:

**27:8** 'When a man dies without a son, the inheritance shall pass to his daughter.

**27:9** If he has no daughter, he shall have his brothers as successors.

**27:10** And if there are no brothers either, you shall give the inheritance to the brothers of his father.

**27:11** But if he has no paternal uncles either, the inheritance shall be given to those who are nearest to him.' And this shall be sacred for the sons of Israel by a perpetual law," just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**27:12** The Lord also said to Moses, "Go up onto this mountain of Abarim, and view from there the land that I am about to give to the sons of Israel.

**27:13** And when you have seen it, you too will go to your people, just as your brother Aaron went,

**27:14** because you offended Me in the wilderness of Zin during the contention of the multitude, and you were not willing to sanctify Me before it over the waters." These are the waters of Contention in Kadesh of the wilderness of Zin.

**27:15** Moses answered Him:

**27:16** "Let the Lord God of the spirits of all flesh provide a man who will be over this multitude,

**27:17** and who can go out and come in before them, and lead them out or bring them in, so that the people of the Lord will not be like sheep without a shepherd."

**27:18** And the Lord said to him, "Take Joshua the son of Nun, a man in whom the spirit is, and place your hand on him.

**27:19** He shall stand before Eleazar the priest and all the multitude,

**27:20** and you shall give him instructions while all are watching, and a share of your glory, so that the whole assembly of the sons of Israel may listen to him.

**27:21** For him, if anything is to be done, Eleazar the priest will consult the Lord. At his word he himself will go out and come in, and all the sons of Israel with him, and the rest of the multitude."

**27:22** Moses did as the Lord had commanded; and when he had taken Joshua, he set him before Eleazar the priest and the whole gathering of the people.

**27:23** And after laying his hands on his head, he repeated everything that the Lord had commanded.

## Translator Notes

- **27:2** — Latin "tabernaculi foederis" ("tabernacle of the covenant") is the Vulgate's characteristic rendering of the Hebrew "tent of meeting."
- **27:3** — Native Clementine Vulgate divides Zelophehad's daughters' petition and the Lord's ruling across 27:3-8 (mapped to Saints.Bible 27:3); distinctive Vulgate readings include "in the rebellion that was stirred up... under Korah," "the relatives of our father," and "to the judgment of the Lord" (v. 4).
- **27:14** — Latin "Sin" renders Hebrew Zin (just as "Sion" renders Zion), and "aquae contradictionis" ("waters of Contention," literally "contradiction") translates the Hebrew place name Meribah.
- **27:21** — The Vulgate states simply that Eleazar will consult the Lord "if anything is to be done," omitting the Hebrew reference to the judgment of the Urim.
- **27:23** — Latin "capiti ejus" ("on his head") specifies where Moses laid his hands, and "replicavit" ("repeated") differs from the Hebrew "commissioned him."
