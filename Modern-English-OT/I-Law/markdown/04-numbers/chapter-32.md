## Text

**32:1** Now the sons of Reuben and Gad had many flocks, and they had boundless wealth in cattle. And when they saw that Jazer and Gilead were lands suited for raising animals,

**32:2** they came to Moses, and to Eleazar the priest, and to the leaders of the multitude, and said,

**32:3** "Ataroth, and Dibon, and Jazer, and Nimrah, Heshbon, and Elealeh, and Sebam, and Nebo, and Beon,

**32:4** the land that the Lord struck down in the sight of the sons of Israel is a very fertile region for pasturing animals; and we, your servants, have very many cattle,

**32:5** and we ask, if we have found favor before you, that you give it to us, your servants, as a possession, and not make us cross the Jordan."

**32:6** Moses answered them, "Will your brothers go to battle, and will you sit here?

**32:7** Why do you undermine the minds of the sons of Israel, so that they do not dare to cross into the place that the Lord is about to give them?

**32:8** Did not your fathers act in this way, when I sent them from Kadesh-barnea to explore the land?

**32:9** And when they had come as far as the Valley of the Cluster, after surveying the entire region, they undermined the heart of the sons of Israel, so that they did not enter the territory that the Lord gave them.

**32:10** And He, being angry, swore, saying,

**32:11** 'Surely these men, who came up out of Egypt from twenty years old and above, will not see the land that I promised under oath to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and they refused to follow Me,

**32:12** except Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite, and Joshua the son of Nun; these have fulfilled My will.'

**32:13** And the Lord, being angry against Israel, led them around through the desert for forty years, until the entire generation that had done evil in His sight was consumed.

**32:14** And look," he said, "you have risen up in place of your fathers, offspring and nurslings of sinful men, to increase the fury of the Lord against Israel.

**32:15** And if you refuse to follow Him, He will abandon the people in the wilderness, and you will be the cause of the death of them all."

**32:16** But they, coming near, said, "We will build folds for sheep and stalls for cattle, and also fortified cities for our little ones;

**32:17** but we ourselves, armed and ready, will march to battle ahead of the sons of Israel, until we bring them into their places. Our little ones, and whatever we may have, will be in walled cities, because of the ambushes of the inhabitants.

**32:18** We will not return to our houses until the sons of Israel possess their inheritance;

**32:19** nor will we seek anything across the Jordan, because we already have our possession on its eastern side."

**32:20** Moses said to them, "If you do what you promise, march equipped before the Lord to battle;

**32:21** and let every armed fighting man cross the Jordan, until the Lord overthrows His enemies,

**32:22** and the entire land is subjected to Him; then you will be blameless before the Lord and before Israel, and you will obtain the regions that you desire before the Lord.

**32:23** But if you do not do what you say, no one has any doubt that you sin against God; and know that your sin will seize you.

**32:24** Therefore build cities for your little ones, and folds and stalls for sheep and cattle; and fulfill what you have promised."

**32:25** And the sons of Gad and Reuben said to Moses, "We are your servants; we will do what our lord commands.

**32:26** We will leave our little ones, our wives, and our flocks and cattle in the cities of Gilead;

**32:27** but we, your servants, will all march equipped to war, just as you speak, lord."

**32:28** Therefore Moses commanded Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the leaders of the families throughout the tribes of Israel, and said to them,

**32:29** "If the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben cross the Jordan with you, all armed for war before the Lord, and the land is subjected to you, give them Gilead as a possession.

**32:30** But if they refuse to cross armed with you into the land of Canaan, let them receive places to live among you."

**32:31** And the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben answered, "Just as the Lord has spoken to His servants, so we will do;

**32:32** we ourselves will march armed before the Lord into the land of Canaan, and we acknowledge that we have already received a possession across the Jordan."

**32:33** And so Moses gave to the sons of Gad and Reuben, and to the half-tribe of Manasseh the son of Joseph, the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, and their land with its cities all around.

**32:34** So the sons of Gad constructed Dibon, and Ataroth, and Aroer,

**32:35** and Atroth, and Shophan, and Jazer, and Jogbehah,

**32:36** and Beth-nimrah, and Beth-haran, fortified cities, and folds for their flocks.

**32:37** And the sons of Reuben built Heshbon, and Elealeh, and Kiriathaim,

**32:38** and Nebo, and Baal-meon, with their names changed, and Sibmah as well, giving names to the cities that they constructed.

**32:39** Now the sons of Machir the son of Manasseh marched into Gilead and laid it waste, after killing the Amorites who inhabited it.

**32:40** Therefore Moses gave the land of Gilead to Machir the son of Manasseh, who lived in it.

**32:41** Now Jair the son of Manasseh went and seized its villages, which he called Havvoth-jair, that is, the Villages of Jair.

**32:42** Nobah also marched out and captured Kenath with its small villages; and he called it Nobah, after his own name.

## Translator Notes

- **32:9** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew place name Eshcol ("cluster") literally into Latin as Vallem botri ("the Valley of the Cluster").
- **32:11** — Latin Si videbunt (literally "If they will see") preserves the Hebraic negative oath idiom meaning "Surely they will not see."
- **32:14** — The Vulgate inserts inquit ("he said") to mark Moses resuming direct address after recounting the Lord's oath, and renders Hebrew "brood" with the doubled phrase incrementa et alumni ("offspring and nurslings").
- **32:35** — The Vulgate (et Etroth, et Sophan) divides the single Hebrew compound place name Atroth-shophan into two separate towns.
- **32:41** — The Vulgate adds the explanatory gloss id est, Villas Jair ("that is, the Villages of Jair") to translate the Hebrew place name Havvoth-jair.
