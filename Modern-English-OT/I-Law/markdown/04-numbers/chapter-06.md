## Text

**6:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**6:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them: A man or a woman, when they make a vow to be sanctified and wish to consecrate themselves to the Lord,

**6:3** they shall abstain from wine and from everything that can intoxicate. Vinegar from wine and from any other drink, and whatever is pressed from the grape, they shall not drink; fresh and dried grapes they shall not eat

**6:4** all the days during which they are consecrated to the Lord by a vow; whatever can come from the vine, from the raisin even to the seed, they shall not eat.

**6:5** Throughout the entire time of his separation, no razor shall pass over his head until the day is completed for which he is consecrated to the Lord. He shall be holy, as the locks of his head grow.

**6:6** Throughout the entire time of his consecration, he shall not go in to a dead person,

**6:7** not even over the dead body of his father, or his mother, or his brother, or his sister shall he be contaminated, because the consecration of his God is upon his head.

**6:8** All the days of his separation he shall be holy to the Lord.

**6:9** But if anyone dies suddenly before him, the head of his consecration will be defiled; and he shall shave it immediately on that same day of his purification, and again on the seventh.

**6:10** Then on the eighth day he shall offer two turtledoves or two young doves to the priest at the entrance of the covenant of testimony.

**6:11** And the priest shall prepare one for sin and the other as a burnt offering, and he shall intercede for him, because he sinned on account of a dead person; and he shall sanctify his head on that day,

**6:12** and he shall consecrate to the Lord the days of his separation, offering a year-old lamb for sin, yet in such a way that the previous days become void, since his sanctification has been defiled.

**6:13** This is the law of consecration. When the days that he had determined by a vow are completed, he shall bring him to the doorway of the tabernacle of the covenant,

**6:14** and shall present his offerings to the Lord: an unblemished year-old lamb as a burnt offering, and an unblemished year-old ewe for sin, and an unblemished ram as a peace offering,

**6:15** also a basket of unleavened loaves that are mixed with oil, and wafers without leaven anointed with oil, and the drink offerings of each one,

**6:16** which the priest shall offer before the Lord, and he shall prepare both the offering for sin and the burnt offering.

**6:17** But the ram he shall sacrifice as a peace offering to the Lord, offering at the same time the basket of unleavened loaves and the drink offerings that are due by custom.

**6:18** Then the Nazirite shall be shaved of the locks of his consecration before the doorway of the tabernacle of the covenant; and he shall take his hair and place it upon the fire that is set beneath the sacrifice of the peace offerings;

**6:19** and he shall deliver the cooked shoulder of the ram, and one cake without leaven from the basket, and one unleavened wafer into the hands of the Nazirite, after his head has been shaved.

**6:20** And having received them back from him, he shall lift them up in the sight of the Lord; and once sanctified, they shall belong to the priest, just like the breast that was commanded to be separated, and the thigh. After these things, the Nazirite may drink wine.

**6:21** This is the law of the Nazirite, when he has vowed his offering to the Lord during the time of his consecration, apart from those things that his hand has found; according to what he had vowed in his mind, so he shall do for the completion of his sanctification."

**6:22** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**6:23** "Speak to Aaron and his sons: In this way you shall bless the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them: And they shall invoke My name upon the sons of Israel, and I will bless them.

**6:24** 'May the Lord bless you and guard you.

**6:25** May the Lord show His face to you and have mercy on you.

**6:26** May the Lord turn His countenance toward you and give you peace.'"

## Translator Notes

- **6:4** — The Vulgate reads ab uva passa usque ad acinum ("from the raisin even to the seed"), whereas the Hebrew text reads "from the seeds even to the skin."
- **6:9** — The Vulgate prescribes shaving immediately on that day of purification and again on the seventh (illico ... et rursum septima), whereas the Hebrew text describes a single shaving on the seventh day.
- **6:10** — The Vulgate uniquely reads in introitu foederis testimonii ("at the entrance of the covenant of testimony") where the Hebrew text has "at the entrance of the tent of meeting."
- **6:23** — In the Clementine Vulgate, the promise corresponding to Hebrew verse 27 ("And they shall invoke My name...") is placed inside verse 23 before the blessing in verses 24-26, and renders Hebrew "put My name" as "invoke My name."
- **6:25** — In verses 25-26, the Vulgate distinctively renders the blessing with Ostendat ("show") and misereatur ("have mercy on") in verse 25, and Convertat ("turn") in verse 26, where the Hebrew reads "make shine," "be gracious to," and "lift up."
