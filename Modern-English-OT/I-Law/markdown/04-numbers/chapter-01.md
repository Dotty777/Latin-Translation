## Text

**1:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses in the desert of Sinai, in the tabernacle of the covenant, on the first day of the second month, in the second year of their departure from Egypt, saying,

**1:2** "Take the total of the entire congregation of the sons of Israel by their clans and houses, and the names of each one, everyone of the male sex

**1:3** from twenty years old and above, of all the strong men from Israel, and you shall count them by their companies, you and Aaron.

**1:4** And there shall be with you the leaders of the tribes and of the houses in their clans,

**1:5** whose names are these: from Reuben, Elizur, son of Shedeur;

**1:6** from Simeon, Shelumiel, son of Zurishaddai;

**1:7** from Judah, Nahshon, son of Amminadab;

**1:8** from Issachar, Nethanel, son of Zuar;

**1:9** from Zebulun, Eliab, son of Helon;

**1:10** and of the sons of Joseph, from Ephraim, Elishama, son of Ammihud; from Manasseh, Gamaliel, son of Pedahzur;

**1:11** from Benjamin, Abidan, son of Gideoni;

**1:12** from Dan, Ahiezer, son of Ammishaddai;

**1:13** from Asher, Pagiel, son of Ochran;

**1:14** from Gad, Eliasaph, son of Deuel;

**1:15** from Naphtali, Ahira, son of Enan."

**1:16** These were the most noble leaders of the multitude by their tribes and clans, and the heads of the army of Israel,

**1:17** whom Moses and Aaron took with the entire multitude of the common people,

**1:18** and they assembled them on the first day of the second month, registering them by clans, houses, families, heads, and the names of each one from twenty years old and above,

**1:19** just as the Lord had commanded Moses. And they were counted in the desert of Sinai.

**1:20** From Reuben, the firstborn of Israel, by their generations and families and houses, and the names of the individual heads, everyone of the male sex from twenty years old and above, going out to war:

**1:21** forty-six thousand five hundred.

**1:22** From the sons of Simeon, by their generations and families and the houses of their clans, there were registered by the names and heads of each one, everyone of the male sex from twenty years old and above, going out to war:

**1:23** fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

**1:24** From the sons of Judah, by their generations and families and the houses of their clans, by the names of each one from twenty years old and above, all who were able to go out to war,

**1:25** there were registered seventy-four thousand six hundred.

**1:26** From the sons of Issachar, by their generations and families and the houses of their clans, by the names of each one from twenty years old and above, all who would go out to war,

**1:27** there were registered fifty-four thousand four hundred.

**1:28** From the sons of Zebulun, by their generations and families and the houses of their clans, there were registered by the names of each one from twenty years old and above, all who were able to go out to war:

**1:29** fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

**1:30** From the sons of Joseph, of the sons of Ephraim, by their generations and families and the houses of their clans, there were registered by the names of each one from twenty years old and above, all who were able to go out to war:

**1:31** forty thousand five hundred.

**1:32** Now of the sons of Manasseh, by their generations and families and the houses of their clans, there were registered by the names of each one from twenty years old and above, all who were able to go out to war:

**1:33** thirty-two thousand two hundred.

**1:34** From the sons of Benjamin, by their generations and families and the houses of their clans, there were registered by the names of each one from twenty years old and above, all who were able to go out to war:

**1:35** thirty-five thousand four hundred.

**1:36** From the sons of Gad, by their generations and families and the houses of their clans, there were registered by the names of each one from twenty years old and above, all who would go out to war:

**1:37** forty-five thousand six hundred fifty.

**1:38** From the sons of Dan, by their generations and families and the houses of their clans, there were registered by the names of each one from twenty years old and above, all who were able to go out to war:

**1:39** sixty-two thousand seven hundred.

**1:40** From the sons of Asher, by their generations and families and the houses of their clans, there were registered by the names of each one from twenty years old and above, all who were able to go out to war:

**1:41** forty-one thousand five hundred.

**1:42** From the sons of Naphtali, by their generations and families and the houses of their clans, there were registered by the names of each one from twenty years old and above, all who were able to go out to war:

**1:43** fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**1:44** These are the ones whom Moses and Aaron and the twelve leaders of Israel counted, each one by the houses of their clans.

**1:45** And the entire number of the sons of Israel by their houses and families, from twenty years old and above, who were able to go out to war, was

**1:46** six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty men.

**1:47** The Levites, however, were not counted with them in the tribe of their families.

**1:48** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**1:49** "Do not count the tribe of Levi, nor shall you place their total with the sons of Israel,

**1:50** but appoint them over the tabernacle of the testimony and all its vessels, and whatever pertains to the ceremonies. They themselves shall carry the tabernacle and all its utensils, and they shall be in service, and they shall camp around the tabernacle.

**1:51** When setting out, the Levites shall take down the tabernacle; when pitching camp, they shall set it up. Any outsider who comes near shall be put to death.

**1:52** Now the sons of Israel shall pitch camp, each one by his companies and formations and army.

**1:53** But the Levites shall pitch tents around the tabernacle, so that indignation may not come upon the multitude of the sons of Israel, and they shall keep watch in the guard duties of the tabernacle of the testimony."

**1:54** Therefore the sons of Israel did according to everything that the Lord had commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Vulgate reads tabernaculo foederis ("tabernacle of the covenant"), whereas the Hebrew text has "tent of meeting."
- **1:3** — The Vulgate reads virorum fortium ("strong men"), where the Hebrew text reads "everyone able to go out to war."
- **1:11** — The Vulgate reads Gedeonis ("Gideon"), declining the patronymic as a form of Gideon, whereas the Hebrew text has "Gideoni."
- **1:50** — The Vulgate specifies quidquid ad caeremonias pertinet ("whatever pertains to the ceremonies"), whereas the Hebrew text reads more generally "all that belongs to it."
