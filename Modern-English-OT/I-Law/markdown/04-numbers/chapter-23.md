## Text

**23:1** And Balaam said to Balak, "Build for me here seven altars, and prepare as many calves, and rams of the same number."

**23:2** And when he had done according to the word of Balaam, they placed a calf and a ram together upon the altar.

**23:3** And Balaam said to Balak, "Stand for a little while beside your burnt offering while I go, if perhaps the Lord will meet me; and whatever He commands, I will speak to you."

**23:4** And when he had gone away quickly, God met him. And speaking to Him, Balaam said, "I have raised seven altars, and I have placed a calf and a ram on top."

**23:5** Now the Lord put a word in his mouth and said, "Return to Balak, and you will speak these things."

**23:6** Returning, he found Balak standing beside his burnt offering, and all the princes of the Moabites;

**23:7** and taking up his parable, he said, "From Aram Balak king of the Moabites brought me, from the mountains of the east: 'Come,' he said, 'and curse Jacob; hurry, and denounce Israel.'

**23:8** How can I curse the one whom God has not cursed? By what reason can I denounce the one whom the Lord does not denounce?

**23:9** From the highest rocks I will see him, and from the hills I will observe him. The people will dwell alone, and will not be counted among the nations.

**23:10** Who could count the dust of Jacob, and know the number of the lineage of Israel? May my soul die the death of the righteous, and may my last end become like theirs."

**23:11** And Balak said to Balaam, "What is this that you are doing? I called you so that you might curse my enemies, and you, on the contrary, are blessing them."

**23:12** To him he answered, "Can I speak anything other than what the Lord commands?"

**23:13** Therefore Balak said, "Come with me to another place from which you may see a part of Israel, and may not be able to see the whole; from there, curse him."

**23:14** And when he had led him to a high place, upon the summit of Mount Pisgah, Balaam built seven altars, and with a calf and a ram placed on top,

**23:15** he said to Balak, "Stand here beside your burnt offering while I go forward to meet Him."

**23:16** And when the Lord had met him and had put a word in his mouth, He said, "Return to Balak, and you will speak these things to him."

**23:17** Returning, he found him standing beside his burnt offering, and the princes of the Moabites with him. To him Balak said, "What has the Lord spoken?"

**23:18** And he, taking up his parable, said, "Stand, Balak, and listen; hear, son of Zippor:

**23:19** God is not like a man, that He should lie, nor like a son of man, that He should be changed. Has He said, then, and will He not do it? Has He spoken, and will He not fulfill it?

**23:20** I have been brought to bless; I am unable to prevent the blessing.

**23:21** There is no idol in Jacob, nor is an image seen in Israel. The Lord his God is with him, and the shout of the king's victory is in him.

**23:22** God brought him out of Egypt, whose strength is like that of a rhinoceros.

**23:23** There is no augury in Jacob, nor divination in Israel; in their own times it will be told to Jacob and to Israel what God has worked.

**23:24** Look, the people will rise up like a lioness, and like a lion it will lift itself up; it will not lie down until it devours the prey, and drinks the blood of those killed."

**23:25** And Balak said to Balaam, "Neither curse him nor bless him."

**23:26** And he said, "Did I not tell you that whatever God commanded me, this I would do?"

**23:27** And Balak said to him, "Come, and I will lead you to another place, if perhaps it may please God that from there you curse them."

**23:28** And when he had led him onto the summit of Mount Peor, which looks out toward the wilderness,

**23:29** Balaam said to him, "Build for me here seven altars, and prepare as many calves, and rams of the same number."

**23:30** Balak did as Balaam had said, and he placed calves and rams upon each altar.

## Translator Notes

- **23:4** — The Vulgate reads "quickly" (velociter) where the Hebrew text has "to a bare height" (shephi).
- **23:7** — Latin "parable" (parabola, also in 23:18) reflects the Septuagint rendering of Hebrew mashal, here referring to a poetic prophetic oracle or discourse.
- **23:14** — The Vulgate reads "a high place" (locum sublimem) instead of the Hebrew "field of Zophim," and explicitly names Balaam as the builder of the seven altars rather than leaving the subject as Balak.
- **23:21** — The Vulgate distinctively reads "idol" (idolum) and "image" (simulacrum) where the Hebrew has "iniquity" and "trouble," and adds "of victory" (victoriae) to "the king."
- **23:22** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew re'em ("wild ox") as "rhinoceros" (rhinocerotis), a distinctive Latin reading also found in Numbers 24:8 and Job 39:9.
