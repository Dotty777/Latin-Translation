## Text

**10:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**10:2** "Make for yourself two hammered silver trumpets, with which you may summon the multitude when the camp is to be moved.

**10:3** And when you sound the trumpets, the whole crowd will gather to you at the entrance of the tabernacle of the covenant.

**10:4** If you blow once, the leaders and the heads of the multitude of Israel will come to you.

**10:5** But if a longer and broken blast sounds, those who are on the eastern side shall move camp first.

**10:6** And at the second sound and a similar alarm blast of the trumpet, those who dwell toward the south shall take up their tents; and the rest shall do in this manner, as the trumpets sound an alarm for departure.

**10:7** But when the people are to be gathered, there shall be a simple blast of the trumpets, and they shall not sound a broken alarm.

**10:8** And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall blow the trumpets; and this shall be a perpetual ordinance throughout your generations.

**10:9** If you go out to war from your land against adversaries who fight against you, you shall blow with trumpets sounding an alarm, and there will be a remembrance of you before the Lord your God, so that you may be rescued from the hands of your enemies.

**10:10** Whenever you have a banquet, and festival days, and the first days of the month, you shall sound the trumpets over the burnt offerings and the peace sacrifices, so that they may be for you a remembrance of your God. I am the Lord your God."

**10:11** In the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth day of the month, the cloud was lifted up from the tabernacle of the covenant,

**10:12** and the sons of Israel set out by their companies from the desert of Sinai, and the cloud settled in the wilderness of Paran.

**10:13** And they moved camp first according to the command of the Lord by the hand of Moses:

**10:14** the sons of Judah by their companies, whose leader was Nahshon the son of Amminadab.

**10:15** In the tribe of the sons of Issachar, the leader was Nethanel the son of Zuar.

**10:16** In the tribe of Zebulun, the leader was Eliab the son of Helon.

**10:17** And the tabernacle was taken down, and the sons of Gershon and Merari went out carrying it.

**10:18** And the sons of Reuben also set out by their companies and their order, whose leader was Elizur the son of Shedeur.

**10:19** And in the tribe of the sons of Simeon, the leader was Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai.

**10:20** Furthermore, in the tribe of Gad, the leader was Eliasaph the son of Deuel.

**10:21** And the Kohathites also set out, carrying the sanctuary. The tabernacle was carried all the while until they came to the place of setting it up.

**10:22** The sons of Ephraim also moved camp by their companies, in whose army the leader was Elishama the son of Ammihud.

**10:23** And in the tribe of the sons of Manasseh, the leader was Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur.

**10:24** And in the tribe of Benjamin, the commander was Abidan the son of Gideon.

**10:25** Last of all the camps, the sons of Dan set out by their companies, in whose army the leader was Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai.

**10:26** And in the tribe of the sons of Asher, the leader was Pagiel the son of Ochran.

**10:27** And in the tribe of the sons of Naphtali, the leader was Ahira the son of Enan.

**10:28** These are the camps and departures of the sons of Israel by their companies when they went out.

**10:29** And Moses said to Hobab the son of Reuel the Midianite, his relative: "We are setting out for the place that the Lord is going to give us; come with us, so that we may do good to you, for the Lord has promised good things to Israel."

**10:30** He answered him, "I will not go with you, but I will return to my own land, in which I was born."

**10:31** And he said, "Do not leave us, for you know in which places through the desert we ought to pitch camp, and you will be our guide.

**10:32** And when you come with us, whatever is best from the riches that the Lord is going to deliver to us, we will give to you."

**10:33** Therefore they set out from the mountain of the Lord a journey of three days, and the ark of the covenant of the Lord went ahead of them, for three days providing a place for the camp.

**10:34** The cloud of the Lord was also over them by day when they marched.

**10:35** And when the ark was lifted up, Moses would say, "Rise up, Lord, and let Your enemies be scattered, and let those who hate You flee from Your face."

**10:36** And when it was set down, he would say, "Return, Lord, to the multitude of the army of Israel."

## Translator Notes

- **10:3** — Latin tabernaculi foederis ("tabernacle of the covenant") is the Vulgate's consistent rendering here, in 10:11, and in 11:16, where the Hebrew has "tent of meeting" (10:3, 11:16) or "tabernacle of the testimony" (10:11).
- **10:4** — Vulgate Si semel clangueris ("If you blow once") renders the Hebrew expression adverbially as a single blast rather than as blowing only one of the two trumpets.
- **10:6** — Latin ululatus and ululare (literally "wailing" or "ululating," also in 10:7 and 10:9) render the broken, ringing alarm blast of the trumpets (Hebrew teruah).
- **10:21** — The Vulgate states that the tabernacle continued to be carried until they reached the place of setting it up, whereas the Hebrew states that the tabernacle was erected before the Kohathites arrived.
- **10:34** — Native Vulgate Numbers 10:34-36 place the cloud notice first (10:34), followed by the lifting up (10:35) and setting down (10:36) of the ark, matching the Hebrew order (mapping to Saints.Bible Septuagint-aligned order 10:36, 10:34, 10:35).
- **10:35** — In the Clementine Vulgate, verses 10:34-36 stand in a different order from the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Vulgate 10:34-35 correspond to MT 10:35-36, and Vulgate 10:36 corresponds to MT 10:34); in 10:35 the Vulgate also reads "multitude of the army of Israel" where the Hebrew has "ten thousands of the thousands of Israel."
