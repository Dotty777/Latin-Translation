## Text

**20:1** Then the Lord spoke all these words:

**20:2** "I am the Lord your God, who led you out from the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery.

**20:3** You must not have foreign gods before Me.

**20:4** You must not make for yourself a carved image, nor any likeness of what is in heaven above, or of what is on the earth below, nor of those things that are in the waters beneath the earth.

**20:5** You must not worship them or revere them: I am the Lord your God, mighty and jealous, visiting the iniquity of fathers on children, into the third and fourth generation of those who hate Me,

**20:6** and showing mercy to thousands, to those who love Me and keep My precepts.

**20:7** You must not take up the name of the Lord your God in vain, for the Lord will not regard as guiltless the one who takes up the name of the Lord his God for nothing.

**20:8** Remember to sanctify the Sabbath day.

**20:9** For six days you will labor, and you will do all your work.

**20:10** But on the seventh day is a Sabbath of the Lord your God: you must not do any work on it, you, and your son and your daughter, your male slave and your female slave, your livestock, and the resident foreigner who is within your gates.

**20:11** For in six days the Lord made heaven and earth, and the sea, and everything that is in them, and He rested on the seventh day; for this reason the Lord blessed the Sabbath day and sanctified it.

**20:12** Honor your father and your mother, so that you may be long-lived on the land that the Lord your God will give to you.

**20:13** You must not kill.

**20:14** You must not commit adultery.

**20:15** You must not commit theft.

**20:16** You must not speak false testimony against your neighbor.

**20:17** You must not covet your neighbor's house, nor desire his wife, not his male slave, not his female slave, not his ox, not his donkey, nor anything that belongs to him."

**20:18** Now all the people saw the voices and the torches, and the sound of the trumpet, and the smoking mountain; and terrified and shaken with dread, they stood far off,

**20:19** saying to Moses, "You speak to us, and we will listen; let the Lord not speak to us, or else we might die."

**20:20** And Moses said to the people, "Do not be afraid, for God has come to test you, and so that terror of Him might be in you, and you would not sin."

**20:21** And the people stood at a distance. But Moses approached the dense darkness in which God was.

**20:22** Furthermore, the Lord said to Moses, "This is what you will say to the sons of Israel: 'You yourselves have seen that I have spoken to you from heaven.

**20:23** You must not make silver gods, nor make gold gods for yourselves.

**20:24** You will make an altar of earth for Me, and you will offer on it your burnt offerings and peace offerings, your sheep and oxen, in every place where there is a remembrance of My name: I will come to you and bless you.

**20:25** And if you make a stone altar for Me, you must not build it from cut stones; for if you lift a knife over it, it will be defiled.

**20:26** You must not go up by steps to My altar, so that your nakedness is not uncovered.'"

## Translator Notes

- **20:1** — The Vulgate reads Dominus ("the Lord") rather than Hebrew Elohim ("God") and omits Hebrew "saying"; similarly, v. 19 reads "the Lord" for Hebrew "God."
- **20:5** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew El qanna ("a jealous God") as fortis, zelotes ("mighty, jealous"), translating El according to its sense of "mighty."
- **20:13** — Native Vulgate Exodus 20:13-15 order the prohibitions as killing (20:13), adultery (20:14), and theft (20:15); Saints.Bible parallel display maps to 20:15, 20:13, 20:14 respectively.
- **20:18** — Literally "saw the voices and the torches" (videbat voces et lampades), preserving the Vulgate's vivid portrayal of thunder as voices and lightning as fiery torches.
- **20:25** — Latin cultrum literally means "knife," rendering Hebrew cherev ("sword/knife"), here referring to an iron stone-cutting blade or tool.
