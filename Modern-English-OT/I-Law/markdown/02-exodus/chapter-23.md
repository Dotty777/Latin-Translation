## Text

**23:1** You shall not accept a report of falsehood, nor join your hand to give false testimony on behalf of a wicked person.

**23:2** You shall not follow a crowd to do evil, nor in judgment yield to the opinion of the majority so as to turn aside from the truth.

**23:3** You shall not show pity to a poor person in judgment either.

**23:4** If you come upon your enemy's ox or donkey straying, lead it back to him.

**23:5** If you see the donkey of one who hates you lying under its load, you shall not pass by, but you shall lift it up with him.

**23:6** You shall not turn aside in the judgment of a poor person.

**23:7** You shall flee from falsehood. You shall not put the innocent and the righteous to death, for I abhor the wicked.

**23:8** Nor shall you accept gifts, which blind even the prudent and overturn the words of the righteous.

**23:9** You shall not trouble a foreigner. For you know the souls of strangers, because you yourselves were also foreigners in the land of Egypt.

**23:10** For six years you shall sow your land and gather its crops.

**23:11** But in the seventh year you shall leave it alone and let it rest, so that the poor of your people may eat, and whatever remains, let the wild animals of the field eat. You shall do the same with your vineyard and your olive grove.

**23:12** For six days you shall work; on the seventh day you shall cease, so that your ox and your donkey may rest, and the son of your female servant and the stranger may be refreshed.

**23:13** Keep all the things that I have said to you. And you shall not swear by the name of foreign gods, nor shall it be heard from your mouth.

**23:14** Three times each year you shall celebrate feasts to Me.

**23:15** You shall keep the solemn feast of unleavened bread. For seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, just as I commanded you, at the time of the month of new crops, when you came out of Egypt. You shall not appear in My sight empty-handed.

**23:16** And the solemn feast of the harvest of the first produce of your labor, whatever you have sown in the field; and also the solemn feast at the end of the year, when you have gathered all your crops from the field.

**23:17** Three times in the year every male of yours shall appear before the Lord your God.

**23:18** You shall not sacrifice the blood of My offering over leaven, nor shall the fat of My solemn feast remain until morning.

**23:19** You shall bring the firstfruits of the crops of your land into the house of the Lord your God. You shall not cook a young goat in the milk of its mother.

**23:20** Look, I will send My angel to go ahead of you, to guard you on the way, and to bring you into the place that I have prepared.

**23:21** Pay careful attention to him and listen to his voice, and do not think he is to be disregarded, because he will not forgive when you sin, and My name is in him.

**23:22** But if you listen to his voice and do everything that I speak, I will be an enemy to your enemies and I will afflict those who afflict you.

**23:23** And My angel will go ahead of you and bring you to the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Canaanites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, whom I will crush.

**23:24** You shall not worship their gods, nor serve them; you shall not do their deeds, but you shall destroy them and shatter their statues.

**23:25** And you shall serve the Lord your God, so that I may bless your bread and waters, and remove sickness from your midst.

**23:26** There will be neither an unfruitful nor a barren woman in your land; I will fulfill the number of your days.

**23:27** I will send My terror as your forerunner, and I will kill every people among whom you enter, and I will turn the backs of all your enemies before you,

**23:28** sending out hornets beforehand, which will put to flight the Hivites, the Canaanites, and the Hittites before you enter.

**23:29** I will not drive them out from before your face in one year, so that the land may not be reduced to a desolation and the wild animals increase against you.

**23:30** Little by little I will expel them from your sight, until you increase and possess the land.

**23:31** And I will set your boundaries from the Red Sea as far as the sea of the Philistines, and from the desert as far as the river; I will deliver the inhabitants of the land into your hands, and I will drive them out from your sight.

**23:32** You shall not enter into a covenant with them, nor with their gods.

**23:33** Let them not live in your land, so that they might not cause you to sin against Me if you serve their gods, which will certainly be a stumbling block to you.

## Translator Notes

- **23:7** — The Vulgate reads 'for I abhor the wicked' (quia aversor impium), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'for I will not acquit the wicked.'
- **23:15** — The Latin 'month of new crops' (mensis novorum) translates Hebrew chodesh ha-aviv ('month of Abib'), referring to the spring month of fresh ears of grain.
- **23:25** — The Vulgate uses first-person verbs ('so that I may bless... and remove', ut benedicam... et auferam), matching the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text shifts to the third person ('and he will bless') before returning to the first person.
- **23:27** — The Vulgate reads 'I will kill' (occidam), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'I will throw into confusion.'
- **23:31** — The Latin 'sea of the Philistines' (mare Palaestinorum) designates the Mediterranean Sea. In the final clause, the Vulgate reads in the first person ('and I will drive them out', et ejiciam eos), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads in the second person ('and you shall drive them out').
