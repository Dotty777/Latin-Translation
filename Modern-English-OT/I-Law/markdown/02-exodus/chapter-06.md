## Text

**6:1** And the Lord said to Moses, "Now you will see what I am about to do to Pharaoh; for by a strong hand he will let them go, and with a mighty hand he will drive them out of his land."

**6:2** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "I am the Lord,

**6:3** who appeared to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as God Almighty, and I did not make My name Adonai known to them.

**6:4** And I established a covenant with them to give them the land of Canaan, the land of their sojourn, in which they were foreigners.

**6:5** I have heard the groaning of the sons of Israel, with which the Egyptians have oppressed them, and I have remembered My covenant.

**6:6** Therefore say to the sons of Israel, 'I am the Lord, who will bring you out from the slave prison of the Egyptians, and rescue you from slavery, and redeem you with an uplifted arm and with great judgments.

**6:7** And I will take you to Myself as a people, and I will be your God; and you will know that I am the Lord your God, who has brought you out from the slave prison of the Egyptians,

**6:8** and has brought you into the land over which I raised My hand to give it to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; and I will give it to you to possess. I am the Lord.'"

**6:9** So Moses told everything to the sons of Israel, who did not heed him because of anguish of spirit and the harshest labor.

**6:10** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**6:11** "Go in and speak to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, that he should let the sons of Israel go from his land."

**6:12** Moses answered before the Lord, "Look, the sons of Israel do not listen to me; and how will Pharaoh listen, especially since I am uncircumcised in lips?"

**6:13** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, and gave a command for the sons of Israel and for Pharaoh, king of Egypt, that they should bring the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt.

**6:14** These are the heads of the houses by their families. The sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel: Hanoch and Pallu, Hezron and Carmi;

**6:15** these are the clans of Reuben. The sons of Simeon: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, the son of a Canaanite woman; these are the descendants of Simeon.

**6:16** And these are the names of the sons of Levi by their clans: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. Now the years of the life of Levi were one hundred thirty-seven.

**6:17** The sons of Gershon: Libni and Shimei, by their clans.

**6:18** The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel; and the years of the life of Kohath were one hundred thirty-three.

**6:19** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These are the clans of Levi by their families.

**6:20** Now Amram took as his wife Jochebed, his paternal cousin, who bore him Aaron and Moses. And the years of the life of Amram were one hundred thirty-seven.

**6:21** And the sons of Izhar: Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri.

**6:22** And the sons of Uzziel: Mishael, Elizaphan, and Sithri.

**6:23** Now Aaron took as his wife Elisheba, the daughter of Amminadab, the sister of Nahshon, who bore him Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

**6:24** And the sons of Korah: Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph. These are the clans of the Korahites.

**6:25** Moreover, Eleazar, the son of Aaron, took a wife from the daughters of Putiel, who bore him Phinehas. These are the heads of the Levitical families by their clans.

**6:26** This is Aaron and Moses, whom the Lord commanded to bring the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt by their companies.

**6:27** These are the ones who speak to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, to bring the sons of Israel out of Egypt; this is Moses and Aaron,

**6:28** on the day when the Lord spoke to Moses in the land of Egypt.

**6:29** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "I am the Lord; speak to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, everything that I speak to you."

**6:30** And Moses said before the Lord, "Look, I am uncircumcised in lips; how will Pharaoh listen to me?"

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — Latin literally reads "in God Almighty" (in Deo omnipotente), reflecting the Hebrew idiom for appearing in that character. For the divine name, the Vulgate uses Adonai here rather than a form of the Tetragrammaton.
- **6:6** — Latin ergastulo (also in v. 7) refers to a slave prison or forced-labor workhouse, a distinctive Vulgate rendering where the Hebrew has "burdens." Likewise, "an uplifted arm" translates Vulgate brachio excelso (literally "high" or "lofty arm") rather than the Hebrew "outstretched arm."
- **6:12** — The literal idiom "uncircumcised in lips" (also in v. 30) signifies faltering or unready speech; the Vulgate also adds praesertim ("especially") here.
- **6:20** — The Vulgate identifies Jochebed as patruelem suam ("his paternal cousin," agreeing with the Septuagint), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text identifies her as his father's sister (his aunt).
