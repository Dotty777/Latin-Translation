## Text

**31:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:2** "See, I have called by name Bezalel, the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah,

**31:3** and I have filled him with the Spirit of God, with wisdom, and understanding, and knowledge in all work,

**31:4** to devise whatever can be crafted from gold, and silver, and bronze,

**31:5** marble, and gems, and a variety of woods.

**31:6** And I have given him as a companion Oholiab, the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan. And in the heart of every skilled person I have placed wisdom, so that they may make all things that I have commanded you:

**31:7** the tabernacle of the covenant, and the ark of the testimony, and the mercy seat that is over it, and all the vessels of the tabernacle,

**31:8** and the table and its vessels, the purest lampstand with its vessels, and the altar of incense,

**31:9** and that of burnt offering, and all their vessels, the basin with its base,

**31:10** the holy garments for the ministry, for Aaron the priest and his sons, so that they may perform their office in sacred things:

**31:11** the anointing oil, and the incense of spices in the sanctuary; they shall make all things that I have commanded you."

**31:12** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**31:13** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'See that you keep My Sabbath, for it is a sign between Me and you in your generations, so that you may know that I am the Lord who sanctifies you.

**31:14** Keep My Sabbath, for it is holy to you; whoever defiles it shall surely die; whoever does work on it, his soul shall perish from the midst of his people.

**31:15** Six days you shall do work; on the seventh day is a Sabbath, a holy rest to the Lord; everyone who does work on this day shall die.

**31:16** Let the sons of Israel keep the Sabbath, and celebrate it in their generations. It is an everlasting covenant

**31:17** between Me and the sons of Israel, and a perpetual sign; for in six days the Lord made heaven and earth, and on the seventh He ceased from work.'"

**31:18** And the Lord gave to Moses, when words of this sort had been completed on Mount Sinai, two stone tablets of the testimony, written by the finger of God.

## Translator Notes

- **31:5** — The Vulgate summarizes the stone- and wood-working crafts as "marble, and gems, and a variety of woods" (marmore, et gemmis, et diversitate lignorum), whereas the Hebrew specifies cutting stones for setting and carving wood.
- **31:8** — The Clementine Vulgate prints the genitive altaris ("of the altar"), a textual variant attracted to thymiamatis for the accusative altare ("the altar"), rendered here as the direct object "the altar of incense."
- **31:14** — The Vulgate reads "My Sabbath" (sabbatum meum) rather than "the Sabbath," and uses active morietur ("shall surely die," and "shall die" in 31:15) rather than the passive "shall be put to death" of the Hebrew.
- **31:17** — The Vulgate ends with "He ceased from work" (ab opere cessavit), omitting the additional Hebrew clause "and was refreshed."
