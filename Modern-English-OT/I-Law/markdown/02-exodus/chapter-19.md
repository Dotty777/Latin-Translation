## Text

**19:1** In the third month of the departure of Israel from the land of Egypt, on this day they came into the wilderness of Sinai.

**19:2** For having set out from Rephidim and arriving as far as the desert of Sinai, they encamped in that same place, and there Israel pitched tents opposite the mountain.

**19:3** Now Moses went up to God; and the Lord called to him from the mountain and said, "These things you will say to the house of Jacob, and announce to the sons of Israel:

**19:4** 'You yourselves have seen what I did to the Egyptians, how I carried you on the wings of eagles and took you to Myself.

**19:5** If therefore you listen to My voice and keep My covenant, you will be to Me a special possession out of all peoples, for all the earth is Mine;

**19:6** and you will be to Me a priestly kingdom and a holy nation.' These are the words that you will speak to the sons of Israel."

**19:7** Moses came, and having summoned the elders of the people, he set forth all the words that the Lord had commanded.

**19:8** And all the people answered together, "Everything that the Lord has spoken, we will do." And when Moses had reported the words of the people to the Lord,

**19:9** the Lord said to him, "Even now I will come to you in the darkness of a cloud, so that the people may hear Me speaking to you and believe you forever." Therefore Moses declared the words of the people to the Lord.

**19:10** And He said to him, "Go to the people and consecrate them today and tomorrow, and let them wash their garments.

**19:11** And let them be ready for the third day; for on the third day the Lord will come down before all the people upon Mount Sinai.

**19:12** And you will set boundaries for the people all around, and you will say to them, 'Be careful that you do not go up onto the mountain, nor touch its borders.' Everyone who touches the mountain will surely die.

**19:13** No hand will touch him, but he will be crushed with stones or pierced with javelins; whether it is a beast or a human, it will not live; when the trumpet begins to sound, then let them go up onto the mountain."

**19:14** And Moses came down from the mountain to the people and consecrated them. And when they had washed their garments,

**19:15** he said to them, "Be ready for the third day, and do not draw near to your wives."

**19:16** And now the third day had arrived, and morning had brightened; and look, thunder began to be heard, and lightning to flash, and a very dense cloud to cover the mountain, and the blast of the trumpet rang out very loudly; and the people who were in the camp were afraid.

**19:17** And when Moses had brought them out from the place of the camp to meet God, they stood at the foot of the mountain.

**19:18** Now all of Mount Sinai was smoking, because the Lord had come down upon it in fire, and smoke rose from it as from a furnace; and the entire mountain was terrifying.

**19:19** And the sound of the trumpet gradually grew louder and was drawn out longer; Moses spoke, and God answered him.

**19:20** And the Lord came down upon Mount Sinai, on the very summit of the mountain, and called Moses to its peak. And when he had gone up there,

**19:21** He said to him, "Go down and solemnly warn the people, so that they do not wish to cross the boundaries to see the Lord, and a very great multitude of them perish.

**19:22** Let the priests also who approach the Lord be consecrated, so that He does not strike them."

**19:23** And Moses said to the Lord, "The common people will not be able to go up onto Mount Sinai, for You solemnly warned and commanded, saying, 'Set boundaries around the mountain, and consecrate it.'"

**19:24** The Lord said to him, "Go, descend, and you will come up, and Aaron with you; but the priests and the people must not cross the boundaries nor go up to the Lord, so that He may not kill them."

**19:25** And Moses came down to the people and recounted everything to them.

## Translator Notes

- **19:6** — Latin regnum sacerdotale ("a priestly kingdom") translates the Hebrew "kingdom of priests" with an adjective modifying "kingdom."
- **19:18** — Latin eratque omnis mons terribilis ("and the entire mountain was terrifying") is a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text states that the whole mountain trembled violently.
- **19:19** — Latin et Deus respondebat ei ("and God answered him") omits the Hebrew phrase "in thunder" (or "with a voice"), while expanding the trumpet description with et prolixius tendebatur ("and was drawn out longer").
