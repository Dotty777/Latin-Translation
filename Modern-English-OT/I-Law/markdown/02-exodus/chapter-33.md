## Text

**33:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Go, go up from this place, you and your people whom you led out of the land of Egypt, into the land that I swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying, 'To your offspring I will give it';

**33:2** and I will send an angel as your forerunner, so that I may drive out the Canaanite, and the Amorite, and the Hittite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite,

**33:3** and you may enter a land flowing with milk and honey. For I will not go up with you, because you are a hard-necked people; otherwise I might destroy you on the way."

**33:4** And when the people heard this terrible word, they mourned, and no one dressed in his finery according to custom.

**33:5** And the Lord said to Moses, "Speak to the sons of Israel: 'You are a hard-necked people; once I will go up in your midst and blot you out. Right now lay aside your adornment, so that I may know what I should do to you.'"

**33:6** Therefore the sons of Israel laid aside their adornment from Mount Horeb.

**33:7** Moses also, taking the tabernacle, pitched it far outside the camp, and called its name the Tabernacle of the Covenant. And everyone of the people who had any question would go out to the Tabernacle of the Covenant, outside the camp.

**33:8** And whenever Moses went out to the tabernacle, the entire populace would rise, and each one would stand at the entrance of his pavilion, and they would watch the back of Moses until he entered the tent.

**33:9** Now when he had entered the Tabernacle of the Covenant, the pillar of cloud would come down and stand at the entrance, and speak with Moses,

**33:10** while all saw that the pillar of cloud was standing at the entrance of the tabernacle. And they themselves would stand and worship at the doorways of their tents.

**33:11** Now the Lord would speak to Moses face to face, just as a man usually speaks to his friend. And whenever he returned to the camp, his attendant Joshua son of Nun, a young man, would not depart from the tabernacle.

**33:12** Now Moses said to the Lord, "You command that I lead this people out, and You do not make known to me whom You are going to send with me, especially since You have said, 'I know you by name, and you have found favor before Me.'

**33:13** Therefore, if I have found favor in Your sight, show me Your face, so that I may know You and find favor before Your eyes; look upon Your people, this nation."

**33:14** And the Lord said, "My face will go ahead of you, and I will give you rest."

**33:15** And Moses said, "If You Yourself do not go ahead, do not lead us out of this place.

**33:16** For by what will I and Your people be able to know that we have found favor in Your sight, unless You walk with us, so that we may be glorified by all the peoples who dwell on the earth?"

**33:17** And the Lord said to Moses, "I will also do this word that you have spoken; for you have found favor before Me, and I know you yourself by name."

**33:18** He said, "Show me Your glory."

**33:19** He answered, "I Myself will show all good to you, and I will call out in the name of the Lord before you; and I will have mercy on whom I wish, and I will be compassionate toward whom it pleases Me."

**33:20** And again He said, "You will not be able to see My face; for a man will not see Me and live."

**33:21** And once more, "Look," He said, "there is a place beside Me, and you will stand upon the rock.

**33:22** And when My glory passes by, I will place you in a crevice of the rock, and I will shield you with My right hand until I pass by;

**33:23** and I will remove My hand, and you will see My back; but My face you will not be able to see."

## Translator Notes

- **33:5** — The Vulgate expresses the threat as a direct future ("once I will go up in your midst and blot you out"), whereas the Hebrew is usually understood conditionally ("if for a single moment I should go up in your midst, I would consume you").
- **33:7** — The Vulgate calls the tent "the Tabernacle of the Covenant" (Tabernaculum foederis, rather than "tent of meeting") and reads "who had any question" (aliquam quaestionem) where the Hebrew reads "who sought the Lord."
- **33:13** — The Vulgate reads "show me Your face" (ostende mihi faciem tuam), linking directly to the Lord's replies in 33:14 and 33:20, whereas the Hebrew reads "make known to me Your ways."
- **33:16** — The Vulgate reads "so that we may be glorified by all the peoples" (ut glorificemur ab omnibus populis; aligning with the Septuagint, and also construable as "above all the peoples"), whereas the Hebrew reads "that we may be distinguished from all the peoples."
- **33:22** — The Vulgate specifies "My right hand" (dextera mea), whereas the Hebrew simply has "My palm" or "My hand."
