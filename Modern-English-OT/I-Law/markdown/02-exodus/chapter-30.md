## Text

**30:1** "You shall also make an altar for burning incense, of acacia wood,

**30:2** having a cubit in length and another in width, that is, square, and two cubits in height. The horns shall proceed from it.

**30:3** And you shall overlay it with purest gold, both its grate and the walls all around, and the horns. And you shall make for it a small golden crown around it,

**30:4** and two golden rings under the crown on each side, so that poles may be inserted into them and the altar may be carried.

**30:5** You shall also make the poles themselves of acacia wood, and you shall overlay them with gold.

**30:6** And you shall place the altar opposite the veil that hangs before the ark of the testimony, in front of the mercy seat by which the testimony is covered, where I will speak to you.

**30:7** And Aaron shall burn sweetly fragrant incense on it in the morning. When he sets the lamps in order, he shall kindle it;

**30:8** and when he sets them in place at evening, he shall burn perpetual incense before the Lord throughout your generations.

**30:9** You shall not offer on it incense of another composition, nor an offering and a sacrifice, nor shall you pour out libations.

**30:10** And Aaron shall make intercession over its horns once a year with the blood that was offered for sin, and he shall make atonement upon it in your generations. It shall be most holy to the Lord."

**30:11** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**30:12** "When you take the sum of the sons of Israel by number, they shall each give a price for their souls to the Lord, and there shall be no plague among them when they have been counted.

**30:13** Now everyone who passes by to be named shall give this: half a shekel according to the measure of the temple (a shekel has twenty obols); half of a shekel shall be offered to the Lord.

**30:14** Anyone who is included in the number, from twenty years old and above, shall give the price.

**30:15** The rich man shall not add to the half shekel, and the poor man shall subtract nothing.

**30:16** And the money received, which has been contributed by the sons of Israel, you shall hand over for the uses of the tabernacle of the testimony, so that it may be a memorial of them before the Lord, and He may be merciful to their souls."

**30:17** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**30:18** "You shall also make a bronze basin with its base for washing; and you shall place it between the tabernacle of the testimony and the altar. And when water has been put in,

**30:19** Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and feet in it,

**30:20** when they are about to enter the tabernacle of the testimony, and when they are about to approach the altar to offer incense on it to the Lord,

**30:21** so that they may not die; it shall be an everlasting statute for him and for his offspring through successive generations."

**30:22** And the Lord spoke to Moses,

**30:23** saying, "Take for yourself spices: five hundred shekels of prime and choice myrrh, and half as much of cinnamon, that is, two hundred fifty shekels, likewise two hundred fifty of calamus,

**30:24** and five hundred shekels of cassia, by the weight of the sanctuary, a hin measure of oil from olive groves;

**30:25** and you shall make holy anointing oil, an ointment compounded by the work of a perfumer,

**30:26** and with it you shall anoint the tabernacle of the testimony, and the ark of the testament,

**30:27** and the table with its vessels, and the lampstand and its utensils, the altars of incense

**30:28** and of burnt offering, and all the furnishings that pertain to their service.

**30:29** And you shall sanctify all of them, and they shall be most holy; whoever touches them shall be sanctified.

**30:30** You shall anoint Aaron and his sons, and you shall sanctify them, so that they may exercise the priesthood for Me.

**30:31** You shall also say to the sons of Israel, 'This anointing oil shall be holy to Me throughout your generations.

**30:32** The flesh of man shall not be anointed with it, and you shall not make another according to its composition, for it has been sanctified, and it shall be holy to you.

**30:33** Any person who compounds anything like it and gives any of it to an outsider shall be cut off from his people.'"

**30:34** And the Lord said to Moses, "Take for yourself spices: stacte and onycha, galbanum of pleasant fragrance, and clearest frankincense; all of them shall be of equal weight;

**30:35** and you shall make incense compounded by the work of a perfumer, carefully mixed, and pure, and most worthy of sanctification.

**30:36** And when you have pounded all of it into the finest powder, you shall place some of it before the tabernacle of the testimony, in the place where I will appear to you. The incense shall be most holy to you.

**30:37** You shall not make such a composition for your own uses, for it is holy to the Lord.

**30:38** Any person who makes anything like it in order to enjoy its fragrance shall perish from his peoples."

## Translator Notes

- **30:3** — The Vulgate reads "its grate" (craticulam ejus), whereas the Hebrew text reads "its top" (or "roof").
- **30:6** — The Clementine Vulgate prints arcum (normally "arch" or "bow"), a known printing variant for arcam ("ark," as in 30:26 and 31:7), translated here as "ark" according to the intended sense.
- **30:13** — The Vulgate uses Roman and Greek reckoning terms: "passes by to be named" (transit ad nomen, a roll-call idiom), "measure of the temple" (mensuram templi, where the Hebrew has "shekel of the sanctuary"), and "obols" (obolos, for Hebrew gerahs).
- **30:28** — The Vulgate reads "and all the furnishings that pertain to their service" (et universam supellectilem quae ad cultum eorum pertinet) rather than naming the basin and its base here as in the Hebrew text.
- **30:35** — The Vulgate reads "carefully mixed" (mistum diligenter), whereas the Hebrew text reads "salted" (or "seasoned with salt").
