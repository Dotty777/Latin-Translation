## Text

**39:1** Now of blue and purple, crimson and fine linen, he made the garments in which Aaron was to be clothed when he ministered in the holy places, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**39:2** Therefore he made the shoulder vestment of gold, blue, and purple, and twice-dyed scarlet, and twisted fine linen,

**39:3** with patterned weaving; and he cut thin gold sheets and drew them out into threads, so that they could be twisted with the weft of the preceding colors,

**39:4** and two borders joined to one another on both sides of the upper edges,

**39:5** and a belt of the same colors, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**39:6** He also prepared two onyx stones, fastened and enclosed in gold, and engraved by the craft of a gem-engraver with the names of the sons of Israel;

**39:7** and he placed them on the sides of the shoulder vestment as a memorial of the sons of Israel, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**39:8** He also made the breastpiece with patterned weaving, like the work of the shoulder vestment, of gold, blue, purple, twice-dyed scarlet, and twisted fine linen,

**39:9** four-cornered, doubled, of a palm's measure.

**39:10** And he set in it four rows of gems. In the first row were a sardius, a topaz, and an emerald.

**39:11** In the second, a carbuncle, a sapphire, and a jasper.

**39:12** In the third, a ligure, an agate, and an amethyst.

**39:13** In the fourth, a chrysolite, an onyx, and a beryl, surrounded and enclosed in gold in their rows.

**39:14** And those twelve stones were engraved with the names of the twelve tribes of Israel, each one according to each individual name.

**39:15** They also made on the breastpiece small chains linked to one another, of purest gold,

**39:16** and two hooks, and the same number of gold rings. Moreover, they placed the rings on both sides of the breastpiece,

**39:17** from which two gold chains were to hang, which they inserted into the hooks that projected at the corners of the shoulder vestment.

**39:18** These fit together with one another both in front and behind in such a way that the shoulder vestment and the breastpiece were bound to each other,

**39:19** drawn tight to the belt and more firmly joined by the rings, which a blue cord connected, so that they would not hang loosely and shift away from one another, just as the Lord commanded Moses.

**39:20** And they also made the tunic of the shoulder vestment entirely violet-blue,

**39:21** and an opening for the head in the upper part at the middle, and a woven border all around the opening;

**39:22** and below, at the feet, pomegranates of blue, purple, crimson, and twisted fine linen;

**39:23** and bells of purest gold, which they placed between the pomegranates at the bottom edge of the tunic all around:

**39:24** a gold bell and a pomegranate, with which the high priest walked adorned when he performed his ministry, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**39:25** They also made tunics of fine linen with woven work for Aaron and his sons;

**39:26** and turbans with their little crowns, of fine linen;

**39:27** also linen undergarments, of fine linen;

**39:28** and a sash of twisted fine linen, blue, purple, and twice-dyed crimson, with the craft of embroidery, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**39:29** They also made the plate of sacred veneration of purest gold, and they wrote on it with the work of a gem-engraver, "Holiness of the Lord,"

**39:30** and they bound it to the turban with a blue cord, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**39:31** Therefore all the work of the tabernacle and of the tent of the testimony was completed, and the sons of Israel did all that the Lord had commanded Moses.

**39:32** And they presented the tabernacle and the tent and all the furnishings: the rings, the boards, the bars, the pillars, and the bases,

**39:33** the covering of rams' skins dyed red, and another covering of violet-colored skins,

**39:34** the veil; the ark, the poles, the mercy seat,

**39:35** the table with its vessels and the loaves of presentation;

**39:36** the lampstand, the lamps, and their utensils with the oil;

**39:37** the golden altar, and the ointment, and the incense of spices,

**39:38** and the hanging at the entrance of the tabernacle;

**39:39** the bronze altar, the grating, the poles, and all its vessels; the basin with its base; the hangings of the courtyard, and the pillars with their bases;

**39:40** the hanging at the entrance of the courtyard, and its cords and pegs. Nothing was lacking of the vessels that were ordered to be made for the service of the tabernacle and for the tent of the covenant.

**39:41** The garments also that the priests use in the sanctuary, namely, Aaron and his sons,

**39:42** the sons of Israel presented, just as the Lord had commanded.

**39:43** After Moses saw that all these things were completed, he blessed them.

## Translator Notes

- **39:1** — Native Clementine Vulgate Exodus 39:1-31 narrates the priestly garments (mapped to Saints.Bible 36:8-38). The Latin uses singular 'fecit' ("he made") through v. 14 before shifting to plural 'fecerunt' ("they made") in v. 15.
- **39:2** — Latin 'superhumerale' (literally "over-shoulder garment") renders the Hebrew 'ephod', and 'cocco bis tincto' ("twice-dyed scarlet," cf. v. 8) reflects an ancient reading of Hebrew 'shani' as "double."
- **39:8** — Latin 'rationale' (reflecting Greek 'logeion', "oracle-piece" or "piece of reason") denotes the high priest's breastpiece. In vv. 10-13, the Vulgate's names for the gems follow the Greek tradition rather than the Hebrew (for example, 'jaspis' in row two and 'beryllus' in row four, where the Hebrew has jasper last).
- **39:24** — The clause "with which the high priest walked adorned when he performed his ministry" ('quibus ornatus incedebat pontifex quando ministerio fungebatur') is a distinctive Vulgate expansion of the briefer Hebrew phrase ("for ministering").
- **39:29** — Latin 'laminam sacrae venerationis' ("the plate of sacred veneration") and the genitive inscription 'Sanctum Domini' ("Holiness of the Lord," matching Septuagint 'hagiasma Kyriou') differ slightly from the Hebrew ("plate of the holy diadem" inscribed "Holy to the Lord," cf. Vulgate Exod 28:36 'Sanctum Domino').
- **39:33** — Latin 'de janthinis pellibus' ('of violet-colored skins,' from Greek 'ianthinos') preserves a distinctive Vulgate and Septuagint color rendering of Hebrew 'tahash', a term of uncertain meaning.
- **39:35** — Latin 'propositionis panibus' literally means 'loaves of setting forth' or 'presentation' (following the Septuagint), corresponding to the 'bread of the Presence' in the Hebrew.
