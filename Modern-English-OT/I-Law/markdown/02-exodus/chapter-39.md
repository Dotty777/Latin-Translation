## Text

**39:1** All the gold that was spent in the work of the sanctuary, and that was offered in gifts, was twenty-nine talents and seven hundred and thirty shekels, according to the measure of the sanctuary.

**39:3** Now it was offered by those who passed to the numbering, from twenty years old and upward, from six hundred three thousand, five hundred and fifty armed men.

**39:4** Moreover, there were one hundred talents of silver from which were cast the bases of the sanctuary, and of the entrance where the veil hangs. One hundred bases were made from one hundred talents, with one talent reckoned for each base.

**39:5** Now from the one thousand seven hundred and seventy-five, he made the capitals of the pillars, and the pillars themselves he also overlaid with silver.

**39:6** Of bronze also there were offered seventy-two thousand talents, and four hundred shekels in addition,

**39:8** and the bases of the courtyard both around it and at its entrance, and the pegs of the tabernacle and of the courtyard all around.

**39:9** from which were cast the bases at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony, and the bronze altar with its grating, and all the vessels that belong to its use,

**39:10** Therefore all the work of the tabernacle and of the tent of the testimony was completed, and the sons of Israel did all that the Lord had commanded Moses.

**39:13** And they presented the tabernacle and the tent and all the furnishings: the rings, the boards, the bars, the pillars, and the bases,

**39:14** the veil; the ark, the poles, the mercy seat,

**39:15** the golden altar, and the ointment, and the incense of spices,

**39:16** the lampstand, the lamps, and their utensils with the oil;

**39:17** the table with its vessels and the loaves of presentation;

**39:18** the garments also that the priests use in the sanctuary, namely, Aaron and his sons,

**39:19** and the hanging at the entrance of the tabernacle; the hanging at the entrance of the courtyard, and its cords and pegs. Nothing was lacking of the vessels that were ordered to be made for the service of the tabernacle and for the tent of the covenant.

**39:20** the covering of rams' skins dyed red, and another covering of violet-colored skins,

**39:22** the sons of Israel presented, just as the Lord had commanded.

**39:23** After Moses saw that all these things were completed, he blessed them.

## Translator Notes

- **39:3** — The Vulgate explicitly specifies 'armatorum' ('of armed men'), a clarifying detail not present in the Hebrew text.
- **39:6** — Following the Clementine Vulgate punctuation ('talenta septuaginta duo millia, et quadringenti supra sicli'), the Latin reads seventy-two thousand talents and four hundred shekels, whereas the Hebrew and Septuagint read seventy talents and two thousand four hundred shekels.
- **39:17** — Latin 'propositionis panibus' literally means 'loaves of setting forth' or 'presentation' (following the Septuagint), corresponding to the 'bread of the Presence' in the Hebrew.
- **39:20** — Latin 'de janthinis pellibus' ('of violet-colored skins,' from Greek 'ianthinos') preserves a distinctive Vulgate and Septuagint color rendering of Hebrew 'tahash', often translated as fine leather or sea-mammal skins.
