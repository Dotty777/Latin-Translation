## Text

**29:1** But you will also do this, so that they may be consecrated to Me in the priesthood. Take a calf from the herd, and two unblemished rams,

**29:2** and unleavened loaves, and a cake without leaven that is mixed with oil, and also unleavened wafers coated with oil; you will make all of them from fine wheat flour.

**29:3** And you will offer them placed in a basket, and also the calf and the two rams.

**29:4** And you will bring Aaron and his sons forward to the doorway of the tabernacle of the testimony. And when you have washed the father along with his sons with water,

**29:5** you will clothe Aaron with his garments, that is, with the linen garment and the tunic, and with the shoulder vestment and the breastpiece, which you will fasten tightly with a sash.

**29:6** And you will place the turban on his head, and the holy plate upon the turban,

**29:7** and you will pour the anointing oil upon his head; and by this rite he will be consecrated.

**29:8** You will also bring his sons forward, and clothe them with linen tunics, and bind them with a sash,

**29:9** namely Aaron and his children, and you will place headwraps on them; and they will be priests to Me by a perpetual religious observance. After you have initiated their hands,

**29:10** you will also bring the calf forward before the tabernacle of the testimony. And Aaron and his sons will place their hands upon its head,

**29:11** and you will slaughter it in the sight of the Lord, beside the doorway of the tabernacle of the testimony.

**29:12** And taking some of the blood of the calf, you will put it upon the horns of the altar with your finger, but you will pour out the remaining blood beside its base.

**29:13** You will also take all the fat that covers the intestines, and the membrane of the liver, and the two kidneys, and the fat that is upon them, and you will offer them to be burned upon the altar;

**29:14** but the flesh of the calf, and the hide, and the dung, you will burn up outside, beyond the camp, because it is for sin.

**29:15** You will also take one ram, upon whose head Aaron and his sons will place their hands.

**29:16** And when you have slaughtered it, you will take some of its blood, and pour it around the altar.

**29:17** And the ram itself you will cut into pieces; and having washed its intestines and feet, you will place them upon the cut flesh, and upon its head.

**29:18** And you will offer the whole ram to be burned upon the altar; it is an offering to the Lord, a most pleasing aroma of the sacrifice of the Lord.

**29:19** You will also take the other ram, upon whose head Aaron and his sons will place their hands.

**29:20** And when you have sacrificed it, you will take some of its blood, and put it upon the tip of the right ear of Aaron and of his sons, and upon the thumbs of their right hands and the big toes of their right feet, and you will pour the blood upon the altar all around.

**29:21** And when you have taken some of the blood that is upon the altar, and some of the anointing oil, you will sprinkle Aaron and his vestments, and his sons and their garments. And once they themselves and their vestments have been consecrated,

**29:22** you will take the fat from the ram, and the tail, and the suet that covers the vital organs, and the membrane of the liver, and the two kidneys, and the fat that is upon them, and the right shoulder, because it is the ram of consecration;

**29:23** and one round loaf of bread, a cake mixed with oil, and a wafer from the basket of unleavened bread that has been placed in the sight of the Lord;

**29:24** and you will place them all upon the hands of Aaron and of his sons, and you will sanctify them, lifting them up before the Lord.

**29:25** And you will receive all of them from their hands, and burn them upon the altar as a whole burnt offering, a most pleasing aroma in the sight of the Lord, because it is His offering.

**29:26** You will also take the breast from the ram with which Aaron was initiated, and you will sanctify it, lifted up before the Lord, and it will fall to your share.

**29:27** And you will also sanctify the consecrated breast, and the shoulder that you separated from the ram,

**29:28** with which Aaron and his sons were initiated, and they will fall to the share of Aaron and his sons by a perpetual right from the sons of Israel; because they are firstfruits and first portions from their peace sacrifices that they offer to the Lord.

**29:29** And the holy vestment that Aaron will wear, his sons will have after him, so that they may be anointed in it, and their hands may be consecrated.

**29:30** The one from among his sons who has been appointed high priest in his place, and who will enter the tabernacle of the testimony to minister in the sanctuary, will wear it for seven days.

**29:31** And you will take the ram of consecration, and cook its flesh in a holy place;

**29:32** and Aaron and his sons will eat it. They will also eat the loaves that are in the basket in the vestibule of the tabernacle of the testimony,

**29:33** so that it may be an appeasing sacrifice, and the hands of those offering may be sanctified. A foreigner will not eat from them, because they are holy.

**29:34** But if anything remains of the consecrated flesh or of the loaves until morning, you will burn up the remainder with fire; they will not be eaten, because they have been sanctified.

**29:35** You will do all the things that I have commanded you concerning Aaron and his sons. For seven days you will consecrate their hands,

**29:36** and you will offer a calf for sin each day to make atonement. And you will cleanse the altar when you have sacrificed the offering of atonement, and you will anoint it for sanctification.

**29:37** For seven days you will make atonement for the altar and sanctify it, and it will be the Holy of Holies; everyone who touches it will be sanctified.

**29:38** This is what you will do upon the altar: two one-year-old lambs each day continually,

**29:39** one lamb in the morning, and the other in the evening,

**29:40** a tenth part of fine flour mixed with beaten oil, which has the measure of a fourth part of a hin, and wine for a drink offering of the same measure for one lamb.

**29:41** And you will offer the other lamb toward evening according to the rite of the morning offering, and according to the things that We have spoken, for a pleasing aroma;

**29:42** it is a sacrifice to the Lord, by a perpetual offering throughout your generations, at the doorway of the tabernacle of the testimony before the Lord, where I will arrange to speak to you.

**29:43** And there I will command the sons of Israel, and the altar will be sanctified in My glory.

**29:44** I will also sanctify the tabernacle of the testimony with the altar, and Aaron with his sons, so that they may exercise the priesthood for Me.

**29:45** And I will dwell in the midst of the sons of Israel, and I will be God to them,

**29:46** and they will know that I am the Lord their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt, so that I might remain among them; I am the Lord their God.

## Translator Notes

- **29:5** — Vulgate superhumerali ("shoulder vestment") and rationali ("breastpiece," literally "oracle-piece" reflecting Greek logeion) render the Hebrew ephod and breastpiece, and quod constringes balteo ("which you will fasten tightly with a sash") binds the breastpiece with a sash rather than girding Aaron with the woven band of the ephod as in the Hebrew.
- **29:22** — Vulgate armumque dextrum literally specifies the "right shoulder" (and similarly armum in 29:27), whereas the Hebrew text refers to the right thigh.
- **29:33** — Vulgate ut sit placabile sacrificium ("so that it may be an appeasing sacrifice") expresses the priests' meal as a purpose clause, whereas the Hebrew refers back to the consecrated food with which atonement was made.
- **29:41** — Vulgate et juxta ea quæ diximus ("and according to the things that We have spoken") uses a divine first-person plural to summarize the accompanying offerings, where the Hebrew explicitly mentions the drink offering.
- **29:43** — Vulgate reads præcipiam ("I will command") where the Hebrew has "I will meet," and explicitly specifies altare ("the altar") as what will be sanctified in God's glory.
