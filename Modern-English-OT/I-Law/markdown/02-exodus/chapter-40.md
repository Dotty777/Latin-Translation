## Text

**40:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**40:2** "In the first month, on the first day of the month, you shall erect the tabernacle of the testimony,

**40:3** and you shall place the ark in it, and let down the veil before it;

**40:4** and when the table has been brought in, you shall place upon it the things that have been duly commanded. The lampstand shall stand with its lamps,

**40:5** and the golden altar, on which incense is burned, before the ark of the testimony. You shall place the hanging at the entrance of the tabernacle,

**40:6** and before it the altar of burnt offering:

**40:8** And you shall surround the courtyard with hangings, and its entrance.

**40:9** And having taken the oil of anointing, you shall anoint the tabernacle with its vessels, so that they may be sanctified:

**40:10** the altar of burnt offering and all its vessels,

**40:12** And you shall bring Aaron and his sons to the doors of the tabernacle of the testimony, and when they have been washed with water,

**40:13** you shall clothe them with holy garments, so that they may minister to Me, and their anointing may serve for an everlasting priesthood."

**40:16** And Moses did all that the Lord had commanded.

**40:17** Therefore, in the first month of the second year, on the first day of the month, the tabernacle was set in place.

**40:18** And Moses erected it, and placed the boards and bases and bars, and set up the pillars,

**40:19** and spread the tent over the tabernacle, with the covering placed on top, just as the Lord had ordered.

**40:20** He also placed the testimony in the ark, with the poles placed underneath, and the oracle on top.

**40:21** And when he had brought the ark into the tabernacle, he hung the veil before it so that he might fulfill the order of the Lord.

**40:22** He also placed the table in the tabernacle of the testimony on the north side outside the veil,

**40:23** with the loaves of presentation arranged in order in front, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

**40:24** He also placed the lampstand in the tabernacle of the testimony opposite the table on the south side,

**40:25** with the lamps set in order, according to the command of the Lord.

**40:26** He also placed the golden altar under the tent of the testimony facing the veil,

**40:27** and burned incense of spices upon it, just as the Lord had ordered Moses.

**40:29** and the altar of burnt offering in the entryway of the testimony, offering on it the burnt offering and the sacrifices, as the Lord had ordered.

**40:33** He also erected the courtyard all around the tabernacle and the altar, with the hanging drawn across its entrance. After all things were completed,

**40:34** a cloud covered the tabernacle of the testimony, and the glory of the Lord filled it.

**40:35** Nor was Moses able to enter the tent of the covenant, with the cloud covering everything and the majesty of the Lord flashing forth, because the cloud had covered all things.

**40:36** Whenever the cloud left the tabernacle, the sons of Israel set out by their companies:

**40:37** if it hung above, they stayed in the same place.

**40:38** For the cloud of the Lord rested upon the tabernacle by day, and fire in the night, with all the peoples of Israel watching throughout all their encampments.

## Translator Notes

- **40:13** — The Vulgate condenses the Hebrew instructions for anointing Aaron and his sons (vv. 13-15) into the single clause 'et unctio eorum in sacerdotium sempiternum proficiat' ('and their anointing may serve for an everlasting priesthood').
- **40:20** — The Vulgate here calls the mercy seat simply 'oraculum' ('the oracle'), echoing its explanatory gloss in 38:5.
- **40:35** — The Vulgate distinctively renders the divine presence as 'majestate Domini coruscante' ('the majesty of the Lord flashing forth') and states twice that the cloud covered everything ('nube operiente omnia ... quia cuncta nubes operuerat').
- **40:36** — Latin 'per turmas suas' ('by their companies') is a distinctive Vulgate rendering where the Hebrew reads 'throughout all their journeys'; similarly, 40:38 reads plural 'populis Israel' ('peoples of Israel') and 'mansiones' ('encampments') where the Hebrew reads 'house of Israel' and 'journeys.'
- **40:11 *(additional)*** — Latin literally reads "Holy of holies" (Sancta sanctorum), expressing the superlative "most holy."
