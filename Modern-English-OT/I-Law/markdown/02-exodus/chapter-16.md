## Text

**16:1** And they set out from Elim, and the whole multitude of the sons of Israel came into the desert of Sin, which is between Elim and Sinai, on the fifteenth day of the second month after they had gone out from the land of Egypt.

**16:2** And the whole congregation of the sons of Israel grumbled against Moses and Aaron in the wilderness.

**16:3** And the sons of Israel said to them, "If only we had died by the hand of the Lord in the land of Egypt, when we sat over the pots of meat and ate bread to the full! Why have you brought us out into this desert, to kill the whole multitude with hunger?"

**16:4** Then the Lord said to Moses, "Look, I will rain bread for you from heaven; let the people go out and gather what is enough for each day, so that I may test them, whether they walk in My law or not.

**16:5** And on the sixth day let them prepare what they bring in, and let it be double what they were accustomed to gather each day."

**16:6** And Moses and Aaron said to all the sons of Israel, "In the evening you will know that the Lord brought you out from the land of Egypt,

**16:7** and in the morning you will see the glory of the Lord, for He has heard your grumbling against the Lord. Indeed, what are we, that you have muttered against us?"

**16:8** And Moses said, "The Lord will give you meat to eat in the evening, and bread to the full in the morning, because He has heard your grumblings with which you have grumbled against Him. For what are we? Your grumbling is not against us, but against the Lord."

**16:9** Moses also said to Aaron, "Say to the entire congregation of the sons of Israel, 'Come near before the Lord, for He has heard your grumbling.'"

**16:10** And while Aaron was speaking to the whole assembly of the sons of Israel, they looked toward the wilderness, and look, the glory of the Lord appeared in the cloud.

**16:11** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**16:12** "I have heard the grumblings of the sons of Israel. Speak to them: 'In the evening you will eat meat, and in the morning you will be filled with bread, and you will know that I am the Lord your God.'"

**16:13** So it came about in the evening that quail came up and covered the camp; in the morning also, dew lay all around the camp.

**16:14** And when it had covered the surface of the earth, there appeared in the wilderness something fine, and as if pounded with a pestle, in the likeness of frost upon the earth.

**16:15** And when the sons of Israel saw it, they said to one another, "Manhu?" which means, "What is this?" for they did not know what it was. To them Moses said, "This is the bread that the Lord has given you to eat.

**16:16** This is the word that the Lord has commanded: 'Let each one gather from it as much as is enough to eat: an omer per head, according to the number of your souls who live in the tent; so you will take it.'"

**16:17** And the sons of Israel did so; and they gathered, some more, some less.

**16:18** And they measured by the measure of an omer; and the one who had gathered more had nothing extra, nor did the one who had prepared less find less; rather, they each collected according to what they were able to eat.

**16:19** And Moses said to them, "Let no one leave any of it until morning."

**16:20** Yet they did not listen to him, but some of them left it until morning, and it began to swarm with worms and rotted; and Moses was angry with them.

**16:21** Now they gathered in the morning, each one as much as could be enough to eat; and when the sun had grown hot, it melted.

**16:22** On the sixth day, however, they gathered double food, that is, two omers for each person; and all the leaders of the multitude came and reported to Moses.

**16:23** He said to them, "This is what the Lord has spoken: 'The rest of the Sabbath is sanctified to the Lord tomorrow; whatever is to be made, do it, and what is to be cooked, cook; whatever is left over, however, set aside until morning.'"

**16:24** And they did just as Moses had commanded, and it did not rot, nor was a worm found in it.

**16:25** And Moses said, "Eat it today, because it is the Sabbath of the Lord; today it will not be found in the field.

**16:26** For six days gather it; on the seventh day, however, is the Sabbath of the Lord; therefore it will not be found."

**16:27** And the seventh day came; and some of the people went out to gather, and they did not find any.

**16:28** Then the Lord said to Moses, "How long do you refuse to keep My commandments and My law?

**16:29** See that the Lord has given you the Sabbath, and because of this on the sixth day He gives you double food; let each one remain where he is; let no one go out from his place on the seventh day."

**16:30** And the people kept the Sabbath on the seventh day.

**16:31** And the house of Israel called its name Manna; it was white like coriander seed, and its taste was like fine flour with honey.

**16:32** Then Moses said, "This is the word that the Lord has commanded: 'Fill an omer from it, and let it be kept for future generations to come, so that they may know the bread with which I nourished you in the wilderness, when you were brought out from the land of Egypt.'"

**16:33** And Moses said to Aaron, "Take one vessel, and put manna in it, as much as an omer can hold, and place it before the Lord to be preserved for your generations,"

**16:34** just as the Lord commanded Moses. And Aaron placed it in the tabernacle to be preserved.

**16:35** Now the sons of Israel ate manna for forty years, until they came to a habitable land; with this food they were nourished until they touched the borders of the land of Canaan.

**16:36** Now an omer is the tenth part of an ephah.

## Translator Notes

- **16:14** — The Vulgate reads 'Cumque operuisset superficiem terrae' ("when it had covered the surface of the earth," rather than when the dew lifted) and renders the rare Hebrew descriptor with 'quasi pilo tusum' ("as if pounded with a pestle").
- **16:15** — Latin 'Manhu' transliterates the Hebrew exclamation 'man hu' ("What is it?"), which the Vulgate immediately glosses as 'Quid est hoc?' ("What is this?").
- **16:16** — The Latin dry measure 'gomor' (like 'ephi' in 16:36) is rendered using the standard modern English spelling "omer" (and "ephah").
- **16:31** — The Vulgate transliterates the name as 'Man' (also in 16:33 and 16:35, echoing 'Manhu' in 16:15), rendered "Manna" / "manna" for clarity in English, and reads 'similae cum melle' ("fine flour with honey") where the Hebrew has "wafers with honey."
- **16:34** — The Vulgate distinctly reads 'in tabernaculo' ("in the tabernacle"), where the Hebrew reads "before the Testimony."
