## Text

**8:1** The Lord also said to Moses, "Go in to Pharaoh, and you will say to him, 'This is what the Lord says: "Let My people go, so that they may sacrifice to Me;

**8:2** but if you refuse to let them go, look, I will strike all your borders with frogs,

**8:3** and the river will swarm with frogs, which will come up and enter your house, and the chamber of your bed, and upon your couch, and into the houses of your servants, and among your people, and into your ovens, and into the remains of your food;

**8:4** and the frogs will come in to you, and to your people, and to all your servants."'"

**8:5** And the Lord said to Moses, "Say to Aaron, 'Stretch out your hand over the rivers and over the streams and marshes, and bring forth frogs over the land of Egypt.'"

**8:6** And Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt, and the frogs came up and covered the land of Egypt.

**8:7** But the sorcerers also did likewise through their incantations, and brought forth frogs over the land of Egypt.

**8:8** Then Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron and said to them, "Pray to the Lord that He may take away the frogs from me and from my people, and I will let the people go so that they may sacrifice to the Lord."

**8:9** And Moses said to Pharaoh, "Appoint for me when I should plead for you, and for your servants, and for your people, so that the frogs may be driven away from you, and from your house, and from your servants, and from your people, and remain only in the river."

**8:10** He answered, "Tomorrow." And he said, "I will do according to your word, so that you may know that there is no one like the Lord our God.

**8:11** And the frogs will depart from you, and from your house, and from your servants, and from your people, and they will remain only in the river."

**8:12** And Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh, and Moses cried out to the Lord regarding the promise about the frogs that he had agreed upon with Pharaoh.

**8:13** And the Lord did according to the word of Moses, and the frogs died out of the houses, and out of the farmsteads, and out of the fields.

**8:14** And they gathered them into immense heaps, and the land became putrid.

**8:15** But Pharaoh, seeing that respite had been given, made his heart heavy and did not listen to them, just as the Lord had commanded.

**8:16** And the Lord said to Moses, "Speak to Aaron, 'Stretch out your staff and strike the dust of the earth, and let there be gnats in the entire land of Egypt.'"

**8:17** And they did so. And Aaron stretched out his hand, holding the staff, and struck the dust of the earth, and gnats came to be on people and on livestock; all the dust of the earth was turned into gnats throughout the whole land of Egypt.

**8:18** And the sorcerers did likewise with their incantations, to bring forth gnats, and they could not; and the gnats were both on people and on livestock.

**8:19** And the sorcerers said to Pharaoh, "This is the finger of God." And Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he did not listen to them, just as the Lord had commanded.

**8:20** The Lord also said to Moses, "Rise at daybreak and stand before Pharaoh, for he will go out to the waters; and you will say to him, 'This is what the Lord says: Let My people go so that they may sacrifice to Me.

**8:21** But if you do not let them go, look, I will send upon you, and upon your servants, and upon your people, and into your houses, every kind of fly; and the houses of the Egyptians will be filled with flies of various kinds, and the entire land in which they are.

**8:22** And on that day I will make marvelous the land of Goshen, in which My people are, so that there will be no flies there, and you may know that I am the Lord in the midst of the earth.

**8:23** And I will place a division between My people and your people; tomorrow this sign will take place.'"

**8:24** And the Lord did so. And most oppressive flies came into the houses of Pharaoh and of his servants, and into all the land of Egypt; and the land was ruined by flies of this sort.

**8:25** And Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron and said to them, "Go and sacrifice to your God in this land."

**8:26** And Moses said, "It cannot be done this way; for we will offer in sacrifice the abominations of the Egyptians to the Lord our God. And if we slaughter before them the things that the Egyptians worship, they will overwhelm us with stones.

**8:27** We will go a journey of three days into the wilderness, and we will sacrifice to the Lord our God, just as He has commanded us."

**8:28** And Pharaoh said, "I will let you go so that you may sacrifice to the Lord your God in the desert; only do not go very far away; pray for me."

**8:29** And Moses said, "After going out from you, I will pray to the Lord, and the flies will depart from Pharaoh, and from his servants, and from his people tomorrow; only do not deceive anymore by not letting the people go to sacrifice to the Lord."

**8:30** And Moses went out from Pharaoh and prayed to the Lord.

**8:31** And He did according to his word, and took away the flies from Pharaoh, and from his servants, and from his people; not even one remained.

**8:32** And Pharaoh's heart was made heavy, so that not even this time did he let the people go.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — Native Vulgate Exodus 8:1-4 (the warning of the plague of frogs) map to Saints.Bible 7:26-29, and native Vulgate 8:5-32 map to Saints.Bible 8:1-28.
- **8:3** — The Vulgate translates "into the remains of your food" (in reliquias ciborum tuorum) where the Hebrew has "into your kneading bowls." Native Vulgate Exodus 8:1-4 map to Saints.Bible 7:26-29.
- **8:5** — As in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, chapter 8 of this Latin text begins at the command to stretch out Aaron's hand, because the preceding warning about frogs appears at 7:26-29; thus chapter 8 has 28 verses rather than the 32 of the alternative numbering, in which this verse is 8:5.
- **8:12** — The Vulgate reads pro sponsione ranarum quam condixerat Pharaoni ("regarding the promise about the frogs that he had agreed upon with Pharaoh," reflecting the Septuagint), whereas the Hebrew text reads "because of the frogs that He had brought upon Pharaoh."
- **8:15** — The Vulgate renders "just as the Lord had commanded" (praeceperat, also in 8:15 and 9:35), whereas the Hebrew reads "just as the Lord had spoken." It also distinguishes ingravare ("make heavy," 8:11, 8:28, 9:7, 9:35) from indurare ("harden," 8:15, 9:12, 9:35).
- **8:21** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew term for a swarm or mixture of insects as "every kind of fly" (omne genus muscarum) and "flies of various kinds" (muscis diversi generis), and uses collective singular musca in 8:20 and 8:25.
- **8:26** — The Vulgate clarifies the second clause with ea quae colunt Aegyptii ("the things that the Egyptians worship") and casts the conclusion as a statement ("they will overwhelm us with stones") rather than the Hebrew rhetorical question ("will they not stone us?").
