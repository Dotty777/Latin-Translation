## Text

**22:1** If a thief is found breaking into a house or digging under it, and dies after receiving a wound, the one who struck him shall not be guilty of blood.

**22:2** But if he does this after the sun has risen, he has committed homicide, and he himself shall die. If he does not have anything to pay back for the theft, he himself shall be sold.

**22:3** If what he stole is found alive in his possession, whether an ox, a donkey, or a sheep, he shall restore double.

**22:4** If anyone damages a field or a vineyard and lets his livestock loose so that it grazes on what belongs to another, he shall restore the best of whatever he has in his own field or in his vineyard, according to the valuation of the damage.

**22:5** If fire breaks out and reaches thorns, and takes hold of stacks of crops or standing grain in the fields, the one who kindled the fire shall repay the damage.

**22:6** If someone entrusts money or a vessel to a friend for safekeeping, and they are taken away by theft from the one who had received them, if the thief is found, he shall pay back double.

**22:7** If the thief remains hidden, the owner of the house shall be brought before the gods and shall swear that he did not stretch out his hand against his neighbor's property,

**22:8** to commit fraud, whether concerning an ox or a donkey, or a sheep or a garment, or anything that can cause loss; the case of both parties shall come before the gods, and if they pass judgment, he shall restore double to his neighbor.

**22:9** If someone entrusts a donkey, an ox, a sheep, or any livestock to his neighbor for safekeeping, and it dies, or is crippled, or is captured by enemies, and no one has seen it,

**22:10** an oath shall be between them that he did not stretch out his hand to his neighbor's property; and the owner shall accept the oath, and the other shall not be compelled to repay.

**22:11** But if it was taken away by theft, he shall restore the loss to the owner.

**22:12** If it was eaten by a wild animal, let him bring to him what was killed, and he shall not make restitution.

**22:13** Whoever borrows any of these from his neighbor, and it is crippled or dies while the owner is not present, shall be compelled to repay.

**22:14** But if the owner was present, he shall not make restitution, especially if it had come hired for the wages of its work.

**22:15** If someone seduces a virgin not yet betrothed and sleeps with her, he shall provide a dowry for her and have her as his wife.

**22:16** If the virgin's father refuses to give her, he shall pay money according to the measure of the dowry that virgins are accustomed to receive.

**22:17** You shall not permit sorcerers to live.

**22:18** Whoever has sexual relations with an animal must surely die.

**22:19** Whoever sacrifices to gods, except to the Lord alone, shall be put to death.

**22:20** You shall not grieve a stranger, nor afflict him, for you yourselves were also strangers in the land of Egypt.

**22:21** You shall not harm a widow or an orphan.

**22:22** If you injure them, they will cry out to Me, and I will hear their outcry.

**22:23** And My fury will be aroused, and I will strike you with the sword, and your wives will become widows and your children orphans.

**22:24** If you lend money to a poor person of My people who lives with you, you shall not press him like a creditor, nor oppress him with interest.

**22:25** If you take a garment from your neighbor as a pledge, you shall return it to him before sunset.

**22:26** For that is the only thing with which he is covered, the clothing of his flesh, and he has nothing else in which to sleep. If he cries out to Me, I will hear him, because I am merciful.

**22:27** You shall not revile the gods, and you shall not curse the ruler of your people.

**22:28** You shall not delay to render your tithes and your firstfruits; the firstborn of your sons you shall give to Me.

**22:29** With oxen and sheep also you shall do the same: for seven days let it stay with its mother; on the eighth day you shall render it to Me.

**22:30** You shall be holy men to Me: you shall not eat meat that has been tasted beforehand by wild animals, but you shall throw it to the dogs.

## Translator Notes

- **22:2** — The Vulgate explicitly states 'he has committed homicide, and he himself shall die' (homicidium perpetravit, et ipse morietur), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text states more briefly that there is bloodguilt for him before continuing with the thief's restitution.
- **22:7** — The Latin literally reads 'before the gods' (ad deos, echoed in 22:8 with plural 'if they pass judgment' and in 22:27 'revile the gods'), rendering Hebrew elohim in the plural; traditional Latin exegesis understood this to refer to judges acting under divine authority.
- **22:17** — The Vulgate uses the masculine plural 'sorcerers' (maleficos, practitioners of harmful magic), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses the feminine singular ('a sorceress').
- **22:28** — The Vulgate reads 'your tithes and your firstfruits' (decimas tuas et primitias tuas), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to the fullness of the harvest and the outflow of the presses.
- **22:30** — The Vulgate reads meat 'tasted beforehand by wild animals' (a bestiis fuerit praegustata), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks of flesh torn by beasts in the field.
