## Text

**10:1** And the Lord said to Moses, "Go in to Pharaoh, for I have hardened his heart and that of his servants, so that I may perform these signs of Mine in him,

**10:2** and that you may recount in the ears of your son and of your grandsons how many times I have crushed the Egyptians and performed My signs among them, and that you may know that I am the Lord."

**10:3** So Moses and Aaron went in to Pharaoh and said to him, "This is what the Lord God of the Hebrews says: 'How long do you refuse to be subject to Me? Let My people go, so that they may sacrifice to Me.

**10:4** But if you resist and refuse to let them go, look, tomorrow I will bring locusts into your borders,

**10:5** which will cover the surface of the land, so that nothing of it may be visible, but what was left over from the hail will be eaten; for they will eat away all the trees that sprout in the fields.

**10:6** And they will fill your houses, and those of your servants, and of all the Egyptians, such as your fathers and grandfathers have not seen since the time they came into being upon the earth until the present day.'" And he turned away and went out from Pharaoh.

**10:7** Then Pharaoh's servants said to him, "How long will we endure this stumbling block? Let the men go, so that they may sacrifice to the Lord their God; do you not see that Egypt has perished?"

**10:8** And they called Moses and Aaron back to Pharaoh, who said to them, "Go, sacrifice to the Lord your God; who exactly are the ones who will go?"

**10:9** Moses said, "With our little ones and elders we will go, with our sons and daughters, with our sheep and herds; for it is a solemn feast of the Lord our God."

**10:10** And Pharaoh answered, "So may the Lord be with you, just as I will let you and your little ones go! Who doubts that you are plotting most wickedly?

**10:11** It will not be so; instead, go, you men only, and sacrifice to the Lord, for this is what you yourselves asked for." And immediately they were driven out from Pharaoh's presence.

**10:12** Then the Lord said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the land of Egypt for the locusts, so that they may come up over it and devour all the vegetation that was left over from the hail."

**10:13** And Moses stretched out the staff over the land of Egypt, and the Lord brought in a scorching wind all that day and night; and when morning came, the scorching wind lifted up the locusts.

**10:14** And they came up over the entire land of Egypt and settled, innumerable, within all the borders of the Egyptians, such as had not been before that time, nor will be afterward.

**10:15** And they covered the entire surface of the land, devastating everything. Therefore the vegetation of the land was devoured, and whatever fruit was on the trees that the hail had left behind; and nothing green at all was left on the trees or on the plants of the land, in all Egypt.

**10:16** For this reason Pharaoh hurriedly called Moses and Aaron and said to them, "I have sinned against the Lord your God and against you.

**10:17** But now forgive me my sin even this once, and plead with the Lord your God that He may take away this death from me."

**10:18** And Moses went out from Pharaoh's presence and prayed to the Lord.

**10:19** And He caused a very strong wind to blow from the west, and seizing the locusts, He cast them into the Red Sea; not even a single one remained within all the borders of Egypt.

**10:20** And the Lord hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he did not let the sons of Israel go.

**10:21** Then the Lord said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand toward the sky, and let there be darkness over the land of Egypt, so dense that it can be felt."

**10:22** And Moses stretched out his hand toward the sky, and there was dreadful darkness in the entire land of Egypt for three days.

**10:23** No one saw his brother, nor moved from the place where he was; but wherever the sons of Israel lived, there was light.

**10:24** And Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron and said to them, "Go, sacrifice to the Lord. Only let your sheep and herds remain behind; your little ones may go with you."

**10:25** Moses said, "You will also give us sacrifices and burnt offerings that we may offer to the Lord our God.

**10:26** All the flocks will go with us; not a hoof of them will remain behind, for they are necessary for the worship of the Lord our God, especially since we do not know what must be offered in sacrifice until we reach the place itself."

**10:27** But the Lord hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he was unwilling to let them go.

**10:28** And Pharaoh said to Moses, "Go away from me, and be careful not to see my face again; on whatever day you appear before me, you will die."

**10:29** Moses answered, "So it will be, as you have spoken; I will not see your face again."

## Translator Notes

- **10:2** — Latin "quoties contriverim Ægyptios" ("how many times I have crushed the Egyptians") is a distinct Vulgate rendering; the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "how I have dealt harshly with / made sport of the Egyptians," and also has a singular "son's son" where the Vulgate has plural "nepotum" ("grandsons").
- **10:7** — Latin "patiemur hoc scandalum" ("will we endure this stumbling block") differs in subject and phrasing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which asks "how long will this [man] be a snare to us?"
- **10:10** — Latin "cui dubium est quod pessime cogitetis?" ("Who doubts that you are plotting most wickedly?") is a Vulgate-distinct rendering of the Hebrew idiom "see, evil is before your face."
- **10:13** — Latin "ventum urentem" ("a scorching wind") characterizes the Hebrew "east wind" (the hot desert sirocco) by its burning effect rather than its compass direction.
- **10:15** — Latin "vastantes omnia" ("devastating everything") diverges from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "and the land was darkened."
