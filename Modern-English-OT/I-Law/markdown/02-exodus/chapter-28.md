## Text

**28:1** Bring near to yourself also Aaron your brother with his sons from the midst of the sons of Israel, so that they may exercise the priesthood for Me: Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**28:2** And you will make a holy garment for Aaron your brother, for glory and beauty.

**28:3** And you will speak to all the wise in heart, whom I have filled with the spirit of prudence, so that they may make the garments of Aaron, in which, having been sanctified, he may minister to Me.

**28:4** Now these will be the vestments that he will make: a breastpiece and a shoulder vestment, a tunic and a close-fitting linen garment, a turban and a sash. They will make holy vestments for your brother Aaron and his sons, so that they may exercise the priesthood for Me.

**28:5** And they will receive gold, violet-blue, purple, twice-dyed scarlet, and fine linen.

**28:6** Now they will make the shoulder vestment of gold, violet-blue, purple, twice-dyed scarlet, and twisted fine linen, in variegated woven work.

**28:7** It will have two joined borders on each side of the upper ends, so that they may come together into one.

**28:8** The weaving itself also, and all the variety of the work, will be of gold, violet-blue, purple, twice-dyed scarlet, and twisted fine linen.

**28:9** And you will take two onyx stones, and you will engrave on them the names of the sons of Israel:

**28:10** six names on one stone, and the remaining six on the other, according to the order of their birth.

**28:11** With the work of a carver and the engraving of a gem-cutter, you will engrave them with the names of the sons of Israel, enclosed and encircled in gold:

**28:12** and you will place them on each side of the shoulder vestment, as a memorial for the sons of Israel. And Aaron will bear their names before the Lord on both shoulders, for remembrance.

**28:13** You will also make hooks of gold,

**28:14** and two small chains of the purest gold, linked to one another, which you will insert into the hooks.

**28:15** You will also make the breastpiece of judgment in variegated woven work, according to the weaving of the shoulder vestment, of gold, violet-blue, purple, twice-dyed scarlet, and twisted fine linen.

**28:16** It will be four-cornered and doubled: it will have the measure of a palm both in length and in width.

**28:17** And you will place in it four rows of stones: in the first row there will be a sardius stone, a topaz, and an emerald;

**28:18** in the second, a carbuncle, a sapphire, and a jasper;

**28:19** in the third, a ligure, an agate, and an amethyst;

**28:20** in the fourth, a chrysolite, an onyx, and a beryl. They will be enclosed in gold in their rows.

**28:21** And they will have the names of the sons of Israel: they will be engraved with twelve names, each stone with the name of each one, for the twelve tribes.

**28:22** You will make on the breastpiece chains linked to one another of the purest gold,

**28:23** and two gold rings, which you will place at both ends of the breastpiece:

**28:24** and you will join the gold chains to the rings that are on its edges,

**28:25** and you will fasten the ends of the chains themselves to two hooks on each side of the shoulder vestment that faces the breastpiece.

**28:26** You will also make two gold rings, which you will place at the ends of the breastpiece, on the edges that are opposite the shoulder vestment and face its back side.

**28:27** And also two other gold rings, which are to be placed below on each side of the shoulder vestment that faces the front of the lower joining, so that it may be fitted together with the shoulder vestment,

**28:28** and the breastpiece may be bound tightly by its rings to the rings of the shoulder vestment with a blue cord, so that the finely crafted joining remains intact, and the breastpiece and the shoulder vestment cannot be separated from one another.

**28:29** And Aaron will bear the names of the sons of Israel in the breastpiece of judgment over his chest, when he enters the sanctuary, as a memorial before the Lord forever.

**28:30** Moreover, you will place in the breastpiece of judgment Doctrine and Truth, which will be on Aaron's chest when he enters before the Lord; and he will carry the judgment of the sons of Israel on his chest, in the sight of the Lord always.

**28:31** You will also make the tunic of the shoulder vestment entirely violet-blue,

**28:32** in the middle of which at the top there will be an opening for the head, and a woven border all around it, just as is customarily made at the outermost parts of garments, so that it may not easily be torn.

**28:33** Below, however, at the foot of that same tunic, all around, you will make, as it were, pomegranates, of violet-blue, purple, and twice-dyed scarlet, with bells mingled in between,

**28:34** so that there is a gold bell and a pomegranate, and again another gold bell and a pomegranate.

**28:35** And Aaron will be clothed with it in the office of ministry, so that the sound may be heard when he enters and leaves the sanctuary in the sight of the Lord, and he may not die.

**28:36** You will also make a plate of the purest gold, on which you will engrave with the work of an engraver: "Holy to the Lord."

**28:37** And you will bind it with a violet-blue ribbon, and it will be upon the headdress,

**28:38** overhanging the forehead of the high priest. And Aaron will bear the iniquities of those things which the sons of Israel have offered and sanctified in all their gifts and offerings. Now the plate will always be on his forehead, so that the Lord may be appeased toward them.

**28:39** And you will fit the tunic closely with fine linen, and you will make a headdress of fine linen, and a sash with the work of an embroiderer.

**28:40** Furthermore, for the sons of Aaron you will prepare linen tunics, and sashes and headdresses, for glory and beauty:

**28:41** and with all these you will clothe Aaron your brother and his sons with him. And you will consecrate the hands of all of them, and you will sanctify them, so that they may exercise the priesthood for Me.

**28:42** You will also make linen undergarments, so that they may cover the flesh of their shame, from the loins to the thighs:

**28:43** and Aaron and his sons will use them when they enter the tabernacle of the testimony, or when they approach the altar to minister in the sanctuary, so that they may not die guilty of iniquity. It will be an everlasting statute for Aaron, and for his seed after him.

## Translator Notes

- **28:4** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew breastpiece as rationale (literally "rationale" or "piece of reason," echoing the Septuagint logeion) and the ephod descriptively as superhumerale ("shoulder vestment"). The Clementine Vulgate also reads the singular verb faciet ("he will make") here before shifting to plural Facient ("They will make") in the next sentence.
- **28:13** — Here and in 28:14 and 28:25, the Vulgate reads uncinos ("hooks"), whereas the Hebrew text describes gold filigree settings.
- **28:30** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew Urim and Thummim interpretatively as Doctrinam et Veritatem ("Doctrine and Truth"), following the Septuagint's delosis kai aletheia.
- **28:32** — The Vulgate compares the woven neck-border to what is customarily made "at the outermost parts of garments" (in extremis vestium partibus), where the Hebrew text compares it to the opening of a coat of mail.
- **28:43** — The Vulgate reads tabernaculum testimonii ("tabernacle of the testimony"), following the Septuagint skene tou martyriou rather than the Hebrew "tent of meeting."
