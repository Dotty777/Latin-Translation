## Text

**17:1** Therefore the entire multitude of the sons of Israel, having set out from the desert of Sin by their stages according to the word of the Lord, encamped at Rephidim, where there was no water for the people to drink.

**17:2** And they disputed with Moses and said, "Give us water, so that we may drink." To them Moses replied, "Why do you dispute with me? Why do you test the Lord?"

**17:3** So the people thirsted there because of the lack of water, and grumbled against Moses, saying, "Why did you make us come out of Egypt to kill us, our children, and livestock with thirst?"

**17:4** Then Moses cried out to the Lord, saying, "What should I do with this people? A little longer, and they will stone me."

**17:5** And the Lord said to Moses, "Go ahead of the people, and take with you some of the elders of Israel; and take in your hand the staff with which you struck the river, and go.

**17:6** Look, I will stand there before you on the rock of Horeb; and you will strike the rock, and water will come out of it, so that the people may drink." Moses did so before the elders of Israel,

**17:7** and he called the name of that place Testing, because of the dispute of the sons of Israel, and because they tested the Lord, saying, "Is the Lord among us, or not?"

**17:8** Then Amalek came and fought against Israel at Rephidim.

**17:9** And Moses said to Joshua, "Choose men, and go out and fight against Amalek; tomorrow I will stand on the summit of the hill, holding the staff of God in my hand."

**17:10** Joshua did as Moses had spoken and fought against Amalek, while Moses, Aaron, and Hur went up to the summit of the hill.

**17:11** And whenever Moses raised his hands, Israel was winning; but if he lowered them a little, Amalek prevailed.

**17:12** But the hands of Moses were heavy; therefore, taking a stone, they placed it beneath him, on which he sat, and Aaron and Hur supported his hands on either side. And it came about that his hands did not grow weary until the setting of the sun.

**17:13** And Joshua put Amalek and his people to flight with the edge of the sword.

**17:14** Then the Lord said to Moses, "Write this as a memorial in a book, and deliver it to the ears of Joshua; for I will blot out the memory of Amalek under heaven."

**17:15** And Moses built an altar and called its name "The Lord Is My Exaltation," saying,

**17:16** "Because the hand of the throne of the Lord, and the war of the Lord, will be against Amalek from generation to generation."

## Translator Notes

- **17:7** — Latin Tentatio ("Testing" or "Temptation") replaces the Hebrew dual place-name Massah and Meribah with a single translated name, absorbing the sense of Meribah into the explanatory phrase propter jurgium ("because of the dispute").
- **17:12** — Latin non lassarentur ("did not grow weary") is a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Hebrew states that the hands of Moses remained steady.
- **17:15** — Latin Dominus exaltatio mea ("The Lord Is My Exaltation") interprets the altar's name distinctively, where the Hebrew reads YHWH-Nissi ("the Lord is my banner").
- **17:16** — Latin manus solii Domini ("the hand of the throne of the Lord") renders a difficult Hebrew clause without a preposition before "throne," joining it with "the war of the Lord" as the subject of "will be against Amalek."
