## Text

**14:1** Now the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**14:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel: let them turn back and encamp facing Pi-hahiroth, which is between Migdol and the sea, opposite Baal-zephon; in sight of it you will pitch camp by the sea.

**14:3** And Pharaoh will say concerning the sons of Israel, 'They are hemmed in within the land; the desert has shut them in.'

**14:4** And I will harden his heart, and he will pursue you; and I will be glorified in Pharaoh and in all his army, and the Egyptians will know that I am the Lord." And they did so.

**14:5** And it was reported to the king of the Egyptians that the people had fled; and the heart of Pharaoh and of his servants was changed regarding the people, and they said, "What did we mean to do, that we let Israel go so that they would not serve us?"

**14:6** Therefore he harnessed the chariot, and took all his people with him.

**14:7** And he took six hundred chosen chariots, and whatever chariots were in Egypt, and the commanders of the whole army.

**14:8** And the Lord hardened the heart of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and he pursued the sons of Israel; yet they went out with an uplifted hand.

**14:9** And as the Egyptians were pursuing the tracks of those going ahead, they found them in camp by the sea; all the cavalry and chariots of Pharaoh, and the entire army, were at Pi-hahiroth, opposite Baal-zephon.

**14:10** And when Pharaoh had drawn near, the sons of Israel, lifting up their eyes, saw the Egyptians behind them, and they feared greatly, and cried out to the Lord,

**14:11** and said to Moses, "Perhaps there were no graves in Egypt; therefore you took us so that we would die in the wilderness. Why did you want to do this, to bring us out of Egypt?

**14:12** Is this not the word that we were speaking to you in Egypt, saying, 'Withdraw from us, so that we may serve the Egyptians'? For it was much better to serve them than to die in the wilderness."

**14:13** And Moses said to the people, "Do not fear; stand, and see the great works of the Lord that He is about to do today; for the Egyptians whom you see now, you will by no means see again, forever.

**14:14** The Lord will fight for you, and you will be silent."

**14:15** And the Lord said to Moses, "Why are you crying out to Me? Speak to the sons of Israel so that they may set out.

**14:16** And you, lift up your staff, and stretch out your hand over the sea, and divide it, so that the sons of Israel may walk in the middle of the sea across dry ground.

**14:17** And I will harden the heart of the Egyptians, so that they may pursue you; and I will be glorified in Pharaoh, and in all his army, and in his chariots and in his horsemen.

**14:18** And the Egyptians will know that I am the Lord, when I have been glorified in Pharaoh, and in his chariots and in his horsemen."

**14:19** And the angel of God, who was going ahead of the camp of Israel, lifted himself up and went behind them; and together with him the pillar of cloud, leaving the front, in the rear

**14:20** stood between the camp of the Egyptians and the camp of Israel; and the cloud was dark, and lighting up the night, so that they were unable to approach one another throughout the whole time of the night.

**14:21** And when Moses had stretched out his hand over the sea, the Lord removed it with a strong and burning wind blowing all night, and turned it into dry ground; and the water was divided.

**14:22** And the sons of Israel went in through the middle of the dry sea, for the water was like a wall on their right and on their left.

**14:23** And the Egyptians, pursuing, went in after them, and all the cavalry of Pharaoh, his chariots and horsemen, through the middle of the sea.

**14:24** And already the morning watch had arrived, and look, the Lord, looking down upon the camp of the Egyptians through the pillar of fire and of cloud, killed their army,

**14:25** and overturned the wheels of the chariots, and they were carried into the deep. Therefore the Egyptians said, "Let us flee from Israel, for the Lord is fighting for them against us."

**14:26** And the Lord said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the sea, so that the waters may return to the Egyptians, over their chariots and horsemen."

**14:27** And when Moses had stretched out his hand toward the sea, it returned at first dawn to its former place; and the waters met the fleeing Egyptians, and the Lord enveloped them in the middle of the waves.

**14:28** And the waters returned and covered the chariots and horsemen of all the army of Pharaoh, who, following, had gone into the sea; not even one of them survived.

**14:29** But the sons of Israel proceeded through the middle of the dry sea, and the waters were to them as if a wall on the right and on the left;

**14:30** and the Lord delivered Israel on that day from the hand of the Egyptians.

**14:31** And they saw the Egyptians dead on the shore of the sea, and the great hand that the Lord had exercised against them; and the people feared the Lord, and they believed the Lord, and Moses His servant.

## Translator Notes

- **14:3** — Latin coarctati sunt ("they are hemmed in") is a Vulgate-distinct rendering where the Hebrew means "they are wandering in confusion."
- **14:9** — The Vulgate adds vestigia praecedentium ("the tracks of those going ahead") and renders Hebrew "overtook" as repererunt ("they found").
- **14:13** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew "salvation of the Lord" as magnalia Domini ("the great works of the Lord").
- **14:21** — Latin vento vehementi et urente ("a strong and burning wind") interprets the Hebrew "strong east wind" as a scorching wind, and abstulit ("removed") stands in place of "drove back."
- **14:24** — The Vulgate reads interfecit exercitum eorum ("killed their army") and in 14:25 subvertit rotas ... ferebanturque in profundum ("overturned the wheels ... and they were carried into the deep"), differing from the Hebrew "threw the army into panic" and "removed/bound their wheels so that they drove heavily."
