## Text

**35:1** Therefore, when the whole crowd of the sons of Israel had been gathered together, he said to them, "These are the things that the Lord has commanded to be done.

**35:2** For six days you shall do work; the seventh day shall be holy to you, a Sabbath and a rest of the Lord: whoever does work on it shall be put to death.

**35:3** You shall not kindle a fire in any of your dwellings throughout the Sabbath day."

**35:4** And Moses said to the entire company of the sons of Israel, "This is the word that the Lord has commanded, saying:

**35:5** 'Set apart among yourselves firstfruits for the Lord. Let everyone who is willing and of a ready spirit offer them to the Lord: gold, and silver, and bronze,

**35:6** blue and purple, and twice-dyed scarlet, and fine linen, goat hair,

**35:7** and ram skins dyed red, and violet-colored skins, acacia wood,

**35:9** onyx stones, and gems for the adornment of the shoulder-vestment and the breastpiece.

**35:10** Let everyone among you who is wise come and make what the Lord has commanded:

**35:11** namely, the tabernacle, and its roof and covering, the rings, and the boards with the bars, the pegs, and the bases;

**35:12** the ark and the poles, the mercy seat, and the veil that is stretched out in front of it;

**35:13** the table with the poles and utensils, and the loaves of presentation;

**35:14** the lampstand for supporting the lights, its utensils and lamps, and the oil for fueling the fires;

**35:16** the altar of burnt offering, and its bronze grate with its poles and utensils; the basin and its base;

**35:19** the garments whose use is in the ministry of the sanctuary, the vestments of Aaron the high priest and of his sons, so that they may serve in the priesthood for Me.'"

**35:20** And the entire multitude of the sons of Israel, having gone out from the presence of Moses,

**35:21** offered firstfruits to the Lord with a most ready and devoted mind for doing the work of the tabernacle of the testimony. Whatever was necessary for the worship and for the holy garments,

**35:22** men along with women provided: bracelets and earrings, rings and armlets; every gold vessel was set apart for the offerings of the Lord.

**35:23** If anyone had blue, and purple, and twice-dyed scarlet, fine linen and goat hair, ram skins dyed red, and violet-colored skins,

**35:24** metals of silver and of bronze, they offered them to the Lord, and acacia wood for various uses.

**35:25** And skilled women also, who had spun, gave blue, purple, and crimson, and fine linen,

**35:26** and goat hair, giving everything of their own accord.

**35:27** Now the leaders offered onyx stones, and gems for the shoulder-vestment and the breastpiece,

**35:28** and spices and oil for tending the lights, and for preparing the ointment, and for compounding the incense of sweetest fragrance.

**35:29** All the men and women with a devoted mind presented offerings, so that the works might be made which the Lord had commanded by the hand of Moses. All the sons of Israel dedicated voluntary offerings to the Lord.

**35:30** And Moses said to the sons of Israel, "Look, the Lord has called by name Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah,

**35:31** and has filled him with the Spirit of God, with wisdom and understanding, and knowledge and all learning,

**35:32** to devise and to do work in gold, and silver, and bronze,

**35:33** and in carving stones, and in carpentry work; whatever can be skillfully devised,

**35:34** He has put in his heart; Oholiab also, son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan:

**35:35** both of them He has instructed with wisdom, so that they may do the works of a woodworker, a tapestry-weaver, and an embroiderer, in blue and purple, and twice-dyed scarlet, and fine linen, and weave all things, and invent all kinds of new things."

## Translator Notes

- **35:5** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew term for a sanctuary contribution (terumah) as primitias ('firstfruits'), here and in v. 21.
- **35:7** — The Vulgate renders the obscure Hebrew skin term tachash as janthinas ('violet-colored', also in v. 23).
- **35:9** — The Vulgate calls the high priest's ephod the superhumerale ('shoulder-vestment') and the breastpiece the rationale (literally 'piece of reason/judgment', calquing Greek logeion), here and in v. 27.
- **35:35** — The Vulgate specifies the artisans' crafts with the Latin triad abietarii, polymitarii, ac plumarii ('woodworker, tapestry-weaver, and embroiderer') and concludes with ac nova quæque reperiant ('and invent all kinds of new things').
