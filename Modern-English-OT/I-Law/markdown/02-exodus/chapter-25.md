## Text

**25:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**25:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, that they take up firstfruits for Me; from every person who offers willingly, you shall receive them.

**25:3** And these are the things that you are to receive: gold, and silver, and bronze,

**25:4** violet-blue and purple, and twice-dyed scarlet, and fine linen, goat hair,

**25:5** and ram skins dyed red, and violet skins, and acacia wood;

**25:6** oil for preparing the lamps; spices for the ointment, and incense of pleasant fragrance;

**25:7** onyx stones, and gems for adorning the ephod and the breastpiece.

**25:8** And they shall make a sanctuary for Me, and I will dwell in their midst,

**25:9** according to the entire likeness of the tabernacle that I will show you, and of all the vessels for its service. And in this way you shall make it:

**25:10** construct an ark of acacia wood, whose length shall be two and a half cubits, its width a cubit and a half, and its height likewise a cubit and a half.

**25:11** And you shall overlay it with purest gold, inside and outside; and you shall make on top a golden crown all around,

**25:12** and four golden rings, which you shall place at the four corners of the ark: two rings shall be on one side, and two on the other.

**25:13** You shall also make poles of acacia wood and cover them with gold.

**25:14** And you shall insert them through the rings that are on the sides of the ark, so that it may be carried by them;

**25:15** they shall always remain in the rings and shall never be drawn out from them.

**25:16** And you shall place in the ark the testimony that I will give you.

**25:17** You shall also make a mercy seat of purest gold: its length shall measure two and a half cubits, and its width a cubit and a half.

**25:18** You shall also make two golden cherubim of hammered work on each side of the oracle.

**25:19** One cherub shall be on one side, and the other on the other.

**25:20** They shall shield both sides of the mercy seat, spreading out their wings and covering the oracle, and they shall look at one another with their faces turned toward the mercy seat with which the ark is to be covered,

**25:21** in which you shall place the testimony that I will give you.

**25:22** From there I will give commands, and I will speak to you above the mercy seat and from the midst of the two cherubim that will be upon the ark of the testimony, all that I will command the sons of Israel through you.

**25:23** You shall also make a table of acacia wood, having a length of two cubits, and in width a cubit, and in height a cubit and a half.

**25:24** And you shall overlay it with purest gold, and you shall make for it a golden rim all around,

**25:25** and for the rim itself a carved crown four fingers high, and above it another small golden crown.

**25:26** You shall also prepare four golden rings and place them at the four corners of the same table, at each foot.

**25:27** Beneath the crown shall be the golden rings, so that the poles may be put through them and the table may be carried.

**25:28** You shall also make the poles themselves of acacia wood and encase them with gold for carrying the table.

**25:29** You shall also prepare, of purest gold, dishes and bowls, censers and cups, in which libations are to be offered.

**25:30** And you shall place upon the table the loaves of presentation in My sight always.

**25:31** You shall also make a hammered lampstand of purest gold, its shaft and branches, cups, small globes, and lilies proceeding from it.

**25:32** Six branches shall go out from its sides, three from one side and three from the other.

**25:33** Three cups as if in the shape of a nut on each branch, together with a small globe and a lily; and likewise three cups in the likeness of a nut on the other branch, together with a small globe and a lily. This shall be the workmanship of the six branches that are to be brought out from the shaft;

**25:34** and on the lampstand itself there shall be four cups in the shape of a nut, and small globes on each one, and lilies.

**25:35** There shall be small globes under two branches in three places, which together make six proceeding from one shaft.

**25:36** Therefore both the small globes and the branches shall be from it, all of hammered work of purest gold.

**25:37** You shall also make seven lamps, and you shall set them upon the lampstand so that they may give light opposite it.

**25:38** The snuffers also, and the receptacles where what has been snuffed is extinguished, shall be made of purest gold.

**25:39** The entire weight of the lampstand with all its vessels shall be a talent of purest gold.

**25:40** Look carefully, and make it according to the model that was shown to you on the mountain."

## Translator Notes

- **25:5** — Vulgate "pelles janthinas" ("violet skins," following the Septuagint) differs from the uncertain Hebrew term "tachash" (variously "fine leather" or "porpoise skins"); "ligna setim" is Jerome's transliteration of Hebrew "shittim," meaning acacia wood.
- **25:7** — Latin "rationale" (lit., "rational" or "oracle-piece," translating Septuagint "logeion") denotes the high priest's breastpiece.
- **25:18** — Here and in v. 20 the Vulgate uses "oraculum" ("oracle") alongside "propitiatorium" ("mercy seat," vv. 17, 20, 22), whereas the Hebrew uses a single term ("kapporet") throughout.
- **25:22** — Vulgate "Inde praecipiam" ("From there I will give commands") differs from the Hebrew reading "there I will meet with you."
- **25:29** — Vulgate "thuribula" ("censers") is a distinctive Latin rendering for one of the golden table vessels.
- **25:33** — Latin "in nucis modum" and "instar nucis" ("in the shape/likeness of a nut") and "lilia" ("lilies," also vv. 31, 34) are Vulgate renderings where the Hebrew specifies almond-shaped cups and blossoms.
