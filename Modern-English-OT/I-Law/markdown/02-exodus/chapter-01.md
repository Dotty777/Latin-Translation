## Text

**1:1** These are the names of the sons of Israel who entered Egypt with Jacob; each one went in with his household:

**1:2** Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah,

**1:3** Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin,

**1:4** Dan and Naphtali, Gad and Asher.

**1:5** Therefore all the souls of those who came out from the thigh of Jacob were seventy; Joseph, however, was in Egypt.

**1:6** When he had died, and all his brothers, and all that kindred,

**1:7** the sons of Israel grew and, as though sprouting forth, they multiplied; and strengthened exceedingly, they filled the land.

**1:8** Meanwhile a new king arose over Egypt, who did not know Joseph.

**1:9** And he said to his people, "Look, the people of the sons of Israel are numerous and stronger than we are.

**1:10** Come, let us wisely oppress them, so that they may not perhaps multiply and, if war breaks out against us, be added to our enemies and, once we are conquered, go out from the land."

**1:11** Therefore he set masters of the labors over them, so that they might afflict them with burdens; and they built cities of tabernacles for Pharaoh, Pithom and Rameses.

**1:12** And the more they oppressed them, so much the more they multiplied and grew.

**1:13** And the Egyptians hated the sons of Israel and afflicted them, mocking them,

**1:14** and they brought their life to bitterness with hard labors of clay and brick, and with all servitude by which they were pressed hard in the labors of the earth.

**1:15** Now the king of Egypt spoke to the midwives of the Hebrews, of whom one was called Shiphrah, and the other Puah,

**1:16** commanding them, "When you act as midwives to the Hebrew women and the time of birth has come, if it is a male, kill him; if a female, spare her."

**1:17** But the midwives feared God and did not do according to the command of the king of Egypt, but preserved the males.

**1:18** Having summoned them to himself, the king said, "What is this that you wanted to do, that you spared the boys?"

**1:19** They answered, "The Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women; for they themselves have the knowledge of midwifery, and before we come to them, they give birth."

**1:20** Therefore God dealt well with the midwives; and the people grew and was strengthened exceedingly.

**1:21** And because the midwives feared God, He built houses for them.

**1:22** Therefore Pharaoh commanded all his people, saying, "Whatever is born of the male sex, throw into the river; whatever is female, spare."

## Translator Notes

- **1:7** — Latin "quasi germinantes" ("as though sprouting forth") renders the Hebrew verb for swarming or teeming with a botanical image of rapid growth.
- **1:11** — Latin "urbes tabernaculorum" ("cities of tabernacles") is a distinctly Vulgate rendering, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "store cities" and the Septuagint reads "fortified cities."
- **1:16** — Latin "et partus tempus advenerit" ("and the time of birth has come") renders functionally the difficult Hebrew phrase usually translated "upon the birthstool."
- **1:19** — Latin "ipsae enim obstetricandi habent scientiam" ("for they themselves have the knowledge of midwifery") is a notable Vulgate reading following ancient interpretive traditions, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text is commonly rendered "for they are vigorous."
