## Text

**7:1** And the Lord said to Moses, "Look, I have appointed you as God of Pharaoh, and Aaron your brother will be your prophet.

**7:2** You will speak to him everything that I command you, and he will speak to Pharaoh, that he should let the sons of Israel go from his land.

**7:3** But I will harden his heart, and I will multiply My signs and portents in the land of Egypt,

**7:4** and he will not listen to you; and I will lay My hand upon Egypt, and I will bring out My army and My people, the sons of Israel, from the land of Egypt by very great judgments.

**7:5** And the Egyptians will know that I am the Lord, who has stretched out My hand over Egypt and has brought out the sons of Israel from their midst."

**7:6** Therefore Moses and Aaron did just as the Lord had commanded; thus they acted.

**7:7** Now Moses was eighty years old, and Aaron was eighty-three, when they spoke to Pharaoh.

**7:8** And the Lord said to Moses and Aaron,

**7:9** "When Pharaoh says to you, 'Show signs,' you will say to Aaron, 'Take your staff and throw it down before Pharaoh, and it will be turned into a serpent.'"

**7:10** So Moses and Aaron went in to Pharaoh and did just as the Lord had commanded; and Aaron took the staff before Pharaoh and his servants, and it was turned into a serpent.

**7:11** Now Pharaoh summoned the wise men and the sorcerers, and they themselves also did likewise through Egyptian incantations and certain secrets.

**7:12** And they each threw down their staffs, which were turned into dragons; but Aaron's staff devoured their staffs.

**7:13** And Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he did not listen to them, just as the Lord had commanded.

**7:14** Now the Lord said to Moses, "Pharaoh's heart is weighed down; he refuses to let the people go.

**7:15** Go to him in the morning; look, he will go out to the waters, and you will stand to meet him on the bank of the river, and you will take in your hand the staff that was turned into a dragon.

**7:16** And you will say to him, 'The Lord, the God of the Hebrews, has sent me to you, saying, "Let My people go, so that they may sacrifice to Me in the wilderness," and up to the present you have refused to listen.

**7:17** Therefore this is what the Lord says: "By this you will know that I am the Lord: look, I will strike the water of the river with the staff that is in My hand, and it will be turned into blood.

**7:18** The fish also that are in the river will die, and the waters will grow putrid, and the Egyptians will be afflicted drinking the water of the river."'"

**7:19** The Lord also said to Moses, "Say to Aaron, 'Take your staff and stretch out your hand over the waters of Egypt, and over their rivers, and streams, and marshes, and all pools of water, so that they may be turned into blood; and let there be blood in all the land of Egypt, both in wooden vessels and in stone ones.'"

**7:20** And Moses and Aaron did just as the Lord had commanded; and lifting up the staff, he struck the water of the river before Pharaoh and his servants, and it was turned into blood.

**7:21** And the fish that were in the river died; and the river grew putrid, and the Egyptians could not drink the water of the river, and there was blood in the whole land of Egypt.

**7:22** And the sorcerers of the Egyptians did likewise with their incantations; and Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he did not listen to them, just as the Lord had commanded.

**7:23** And he turned away and entered his house, nor did he take it to heart even this time.

**7:24** Now all the Egyptians dug all around the river for water to drink, for they could not drink from the water of the river.

**7:25** And seven days were completed after the Lord struck the river.

**7:26** The Lord also said to Moses, "Go in to Pharaoh, and you will say to him, 'This is what the Lord says: "Let My people go, so that they may sacrifice to Me;

**7:27** but if you refuse to let them go, look, I will strike all your borders with frogs,

**7:28** and the river will swarm with frogs, which will come up and enter your house, and the chamber of your bed, and upon your couch, and into the houses of your servants, and among your people, and into your ovens, and into the remains of your food;

**7:29** and the frogs will come in to you, and to your people, and to all your servants."'"

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — Latin Deum Pharaonis (literally "God of Pharaoh," where the Hebrew has "God to Pharaoh") signifies that Moses will stand in God's place with authority over Pharaoh while Aaron speaks as his prophet.
- **7:10** — The Vulgate reads tulit ("took"), whereas the Hebrew says Aaron cast down his staff.
- **7:12** — The Vulgate shifts from colubrum ("serpent," vv. 9-10) to dracones/draconem ("dragons"/"dragon," vv. 12, 15), where the Hebrew uses a single term throughout.
- **7:13** — The Vulgate reads sicut praeceperat Dominus ("just as the Lord had commanded," also in v. 22), whereas the Hebrew has "as the Lord had spoken."
- **7:28** — The Vulgate translates "into the remains of your food" (in reliquias ciborum tuorum) where the Hebrew has "into your kneading bowls." Following the Hebrew chapter division, vv. 26-29 conclude chapter 7 in this Latin text (8:1-4 in the alternative numbering).
