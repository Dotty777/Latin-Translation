## Text

**4:1** Answering, Moses said, "They will not believe me, nor will they listen to my voice, but they will say, 'The Lord has not appeared to you.'"

**4:2** Therefore He said to him, "What is it that you are holding in your hand?" He answered, "A staff."

**4:3** And the Lord said, "Throw it to the ground." He threw it, and it turned into a snake, so that Moses fled.

**4:4** And the Lord said, "Reach out your hand and grasp its tail." He reached out and held it, and it turned into a staff.

**4:5** "So that they may believe," He said, "that the Lord God of their fathers has appeared to you, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob."

**4:6** And the Lord said again, "Put your hand into your bosom." When he had put it into his bosom, he brought it out leprous, like snow.

**4:7** "Draw your hand back," He said, "into your bosom." He drew it back and brought it out again, and it was like the rest of his flesh.

**4:8** "If they do not believe you," He said, "nor listen to the message of the first sign, they will believe the word of the following sign.

**4:9** And if they do not believe even these two signs, nor listen to your voice, take water from the river and pour it out on the dry ground, and whatever you draw from the river will turn into blood."

**4:10** Moses said, "I beg You, Lord, I am not eloquent from yesterday and the day before, and since You have spoken to Your servant, I have a more hindered and slower tongue."

**4:11** The Lord said to him, "Who made man's mouth? Or who fashioned the mute and the deaf, the seeing and the blind? Is it not I?

**4:12** Go, therefore, and I will be in your mouth, and I will teach you what you are to speak."

**4:13** But he said, "I beg You, Lord, send the one whom You are going to send."

**4:14** The Lord, angry with Moses, said, "Aaron your brother, the Levite: I know that he is eloquent; look, he himself is coming out to meet you, and seeing you, he will rejoice in his heart.

**4:15** Speak to him and put My words in his mouth; and I will be in your mouth and in his mouth, and I will show you what you must do.

**4:16** He himself will speak for you to the people, and he will be your mouth; but you will be to him in those things that pertain to God.

**4:17** Also take this staff in your hand, with which you are going to perform signs."

**4:18** Moses went away and returned to Jethro his father-in-law, and said to him, "I will go and return to my brothers in Egypt, so that I may see if they are still alive." Jethro said to him, "Go in peace."

**4:19** Therefore the Lord said to Moses in Midian, "Go and return to Egypt, for all those who were seeking your life are dead."

**4:20** Therefore Moses took his wife and his sons and set them on a donkey, and he returned to Egypt, carrying the staff of God in his hand.

**4:21** And the Lord said to him as he was returning to Egypt, "See that you perform before Pharaoh all the wonders that I have put in your hand; I will harden his heart, and he will not let the people go.

**4:22** And you will say to him, 'This is what the Lord says: Israel is My firstborn son.

**4:23** I have said to you, "Let My son go so that he may serve Me," and you refused to let him go; look, I will kill your firstborn son.'"

**4:24** And when he was on the way, at a lodging place the Lord met him and wanted to kill him.

**4:25** Therefore Zipporah took a very sharp stone and circumcised the foreskin of her son, and touched his feet, and said, "You are a bridegroom of blood to me."

**4:26** And He let him go after she had said, "A bridegroom of blood because of the circumcision."

**4:27** Now the Lord said to Aaron, "Go into the desert to meet Moses." He went to the mountain of God to meet him, and kissed him.

**4:28** And Moses told Aaron all the words of the Lord with which He had sent him, and the signs that He had commanded.

**4:29** And together they came and gathered all the elders of the sons of Israel.

**4:30** And Aaron spoke all the words that the Lord had said to Moses, and he performed the signs before the people,

**4:31** and the people believed. And they heard that the Lord had visited the sons of Israel and had looked upon their affliction, and bowing face down, they worshiped.

## Translator Notes

- **4:12** — Latin in ore tuo ("in your mouth"; likewise in 4:15) is a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Hebrew reads "with your mouth."
- **4:13** — Latin mitte quem missurus es ("send the one whom You are going to send") uses the future active participle; the Hebrew reads "send by the hand of whom You will send."
- **4:16** — Latin in his quae ad Deum pertinent ("in those things that pertain to God") is a Vulgate-distinct rendering where the Hebrew literally reads "as God."
- **4:25** — Latin acutissimam petram ("a very sharp stone") renders the Hebrew "flint," and Sponsus sanguinum ("a bridegroom of blood," literally plural "of bloods") preserves a famously difficult idiom whose precise referent remains debated.
