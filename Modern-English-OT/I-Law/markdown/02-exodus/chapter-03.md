## Text

**3:1** Now Moses was tending the sheep of Jethro his father-in-law, the priest of Midian; and when he had driven the flock to the interior of the desert, he came to the mountain of God, Horeb.

**3:2** And the Lord appeared to him in a flame of fire from the midst of a bush; and he saw that the bush was burning, and was not being consumed.

**3:3** Therefore Moses said, "I will go and see this great sight, why the bush is not consumed."

**3:4** Now when the Lord perceived that he was going forward to see, He called him from the midst of the bush and said, "Moses, Moses." And he answered, "I am here."

**3:5** But He said, "Do not come near here; untie the footwear from your feet, for the place on which you are standing is holy ground."

**3:6** And He said, "I am the God of your father, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob." Moses hid his face, for he did not dare to look toward God.

**3:7** The Lord said to him, "I have seen the affliction of My people in Egypt, and I have heard their cry because of the harshness of those who are over the labors;

**3:8** and knowing their sorrow, I have come down to deliver them from the hands of the Egyptians, and to bring them out from that land into a good and spacious land, into a land that flows with milk and honey, to the places of the Canaanite and the Hittite, and the Amorite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite.

**3:9** Therefore the cry of the sons of Israel has come to Me, and I have seen their affliction, with which they are oppressed by the Egyptians.

**3:10** But come, and I will send you to Pharaoh, so that you may bring out My people, the sons of Israel, from Egypt."

**3:11** And Moses said to God, "Who am I that I should go to Pharaoh and bring out the sons of Israel from Egypt?"

**3:12** He said to him, "I will be with you; and you will have this as a sign that I have sent you: when you have brought out My people from Egypt, you will sacrifice to God upon this mountain."

**3:13** Moses said to God, "Look, I will go to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'The God of your fathers has sent me to you.' If they say to me, 'What is His name?' what shall I say to them?"

**3:14** God said to Moses, "I am who I am." He said, "In this way you shall say to the sons of Israel: 'He Who Is has sent me to you.'"

**3:15** And God said again to Moses, "These things you shall say to the sons of Israel: 'The Lord God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has sent me to you.' This is My name forever, and this is My memorial from generation to generation.

**3:16** Go and gather the elders of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'The Lord God of your fathers has appeared to me, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, saying, "Visiting, I have visited you, and I have seen all the things that have happened to you in Egypt.

**3:17** And I have said that I will bring you out from the affliction of Egypt into the land of the Canaanite, and the Hittite, and the Amorite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite, to a land flowing with milk and honey."'

**3:18** And they will listen to your voice; and you and the elders of Israel shall go in to the king of Egypt, and you shall say to him, 'The Lord God of the Hebrews has called us; we will go a journey of three days into the wilderness, so that we may sacrifice to the Lord our God.'

**3:19** But I know that the king of Egypt will not release you so that you may go, except by a strong hand.

**3:20** For I will stretch out My hand and strike Egypt with all My wonders that I am about to do in the midst of them; after these things he will release you.

**3:21** And I will give favor to this people before the Egyptians; and when you go out, you will not depart empty;

**3:22** but each woman will ask from her neighbor and from her houseguest for silver and gold vessels and garments; and you will put them upon your sons and your daughters, and you will plunder Egypt."

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — Latin "Dominus" ("the Lord") appears directly without "angel of," whereas both the Hebrew Masoretic Text and the Septuagint read "the angel of the Lord."
- **3:12** — Latin "immolabis" ("you will sacrifice") uses a second-person singular verb specifically denoting sacrifice, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the second-person plural "you shall serve."
- **3:14** — Latin "Qui est" ("He Who Is") is a third-person formulation shared with the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text repeats the first-person form ("I am").
- **3:18** — Latin "vocavit nos" ("has called us") agrees with the Septuagint against the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "has met with us."
- **3:22** — Latin "hospita" can denote either a female houseguest/lodger staying in the home (matching the Hebrew expression for a woman sojourning in the house) or a hostess.
