## Text

**24:1** He also said to Moses, "Come up to the Lord, you and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy elders from Israel, and you shall worship from a distance.

**24:2** And Moses alone shall come up to the Lord, and they shall not come near, nor shall the people come up with him."

**24:3** So Moses came and recounted to the people all the words of the Lord and the judgments; and all the people answered with one voice, "All the words of the Lord that He has spoken, we will do."

**24:4** And Moses wrote down all the words of the Lord; and rising early in the morning, he built an altar at the foot of the mountain, and twelve monuments according to the twelve tribes of Israel.

**24:5** And he sent young men from among the sons of Israel, and they offered burnt offerings, and sacrificed peace offerings to the Lord, calves.

**24:6** So Moses took half of the blood and put it into bowls, and the remaining part he poured on the altar.

**24:7** And taking the scroll of the covenant, he read it in the hearing of the people; and they said, "All that the Lord has spoken we will do, and we will be obedient."

**24:8** Then he took the blood and sprinkled it on the people, and said, "This is the blood of the covenant that the Lord has made with you concerning all these words."

**24:9** And Moses and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel went up,

**24:10** and they saw the God of Israel; and under His feet was something like a work of sapphire stone, and like the sky when it is clear.

**24:11** And He did not lay His hand upon those of the sons of Israel who had withdrawn to a distance; and they saw God, and ate and drank.

**24:12** And the Lord said to Moses, "Come up to Me on the mountain and remain there; and I will give you tablets of stone, and the law and the commandments that I have written, so that you may teach them."

**24:13** Moses and his attendant Joshua rose up; and as Moses went up onto the mountain of God,

**24:14** he said to the elders, "Wait here until we return to you. You have Aaron and Hur with you; if any question arises, you shall refer it to them."

**24:15** And when Moses had gone up, a cloud covered the mountain,

**24:16** and the glory of the Lord dwelt on Sinai, covering it with the cloud for six days; and on the seventh day He called him from the midst of the thick darkness.

**24:17** Now the appearance of the glory of the Lord was like a burning fire on the summit of the mountain in the sight of the sons of Israel.

**24:18** And Moses entered the midst of the mist and went up onto the mountain, and he was there for forty days and forty nights.

## Translator Notes

- **24:11** — Vulgate "qui procul recesserant" ("who had withdrawn to a distance") reflects Jerome's rendering of Hebrew "atsilei" (usually "nobles") from a root meaning "to set apart" or "withdraw."
- **24:16** — In the Vulgate, "gloria Domini" ("the glory of the Lord") is the subject of the participle "tegens" ("covering it with the cloud"), whereas the Hebrew makes the cloud the subject; "caliginis" ("thick darkness") and "nebulae" ("mist," v. 18) vary the repeated Hebrew word for "cloud."
- **24:17** — Latin "ignis ardens" literally means "burning fire," whereas the Hebrew reads "consuming/devouring fire."
