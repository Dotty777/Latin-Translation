## Text

**20:1** Abraham set out from there into the southern land, lived between Kadesh and Shur, and sojourned in Gerar.

**20:2** And he said of Sarah his wife, "She is my sister." So Abimelech, king of the land, sent and took her.

**20:3** But God came to Abimelech through a dream in the night and said to him, "Look, you will die because of the woman whom you have taken, for she has a husband."

**20:4** Now Abimelech had not touched her, and he said, "Lord, will You kill an unknowing and righteous nation?

**20:5** Did he not himself say to me, 'She is my sister,' and she herself said, 'He is my brother'? In the sincerity of my heart and the cleanness of my hands I have done this."

**20:6** And God said to him, "I also know that you have done this with a sincere heart, and therefore I kept you from sinning against Me, and I did not allow you to touch her.

**20:7** Now therefore, return the wife to her husband, because he is a prophet, and he will pray for you, and you will live; but if you are unwilling to return her, know that you will certainly die, you and all that is yours."

**20:8** And immediately rising in the night, Abimelech called all his servants and spoke all these words in their ears, and all the men were greatly afraid.

**20:9** Then Abimelech called Abraham also and said to him, "What have you done to us? How have we sinned against you, that you have brought a great sin upon me and upon my kingdom? You have done to us things you should not have done."

**20:10** And protesting again, he said, "What did you see, that you did this?"

**20:11** Abraham answered, "I thought to myself, saying, 'Perhaps there is no fear of God in this place, and they will kill me because of my wife.'

**20:12** Besides, she is truly my sister as well, the daughter of my father and not the daughter of my mother, and I took her as my wife.

**20:13** Now after God brought me out from my father's house, I said to her, 'You will show this mercy to me: in every place that we enter, you will say that I am your brother.'"

**20:14** So Abimelech took sheep and oxen, and male servants and female servants, and gave them to Abraham, and returned Sarah his wife to him,

**20:15** and said, "The land is before you; live wherever it pleases you."

**20:16** And to Sarah he said, "Look, I have given a thousand pieces of silver to your brother; this will be for you a covering of the eyes before all who are with you and wherever you go; and remember that you were caught."

**20:17** Then as Abraham prayed, God healed Abimelech and his wife and his female servants, and they gave birth;

**20:18** for God had closed up every womb of the house of Abimelech because of Sarah, the wife of Abraham.

## Translator Notes

- **20:2** — The Latin text here reads "Abimelech rex Terrae" ("Abimelech, king of the land"), rather than "king of Gerar" ("rex Gerarae") found in other Vulgate editions and the Hebrew.
- **20:4** — The Vulgate follows the Septuagint in reading "gentem ignorantem et justam" ("an unknowing and righteous nation"), whereas the Hebrew reads "even a righteous nation."
- **20:8** — The Vulgate specifies "Statimque de nocte" ("And immediately in the night"), whereas the Hebrew reads "early in the morning."
- **20:16** — Distinctively in the Vulgate, Abimelech's closing words read "et quocumque perrexeris: mementoque te deprehensam" ("and wherever you go; and remember that you were caught"), whereas the Hebrew reads "and before all you are vindicated."
