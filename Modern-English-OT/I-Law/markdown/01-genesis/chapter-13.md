## Text

**13:1** So Abram went up from Egypt, he and his wife, and all that he had, and Lot with him, toward the southern region.

**13:2** Now he was very rich in the possession of silver and gold.

**13:3** And he returned by the way by which he had come, from the south to Bethel, as far as the place where he had previously pitched the tent between Bethel and Ai,

**13:4** at the place of the altar that he had made previously; and there he called upon the name of the Lord.

**13:5** And Lot also, who was with Abram, had flocks of sheep, and herds, and tents.

**13:6** Nor could the land hold them so that they might live together, for their wealth was great, and they were not able to live in common.

**13:7** Because of this, strife also arose between the herdsmen of the flocks of Abram and Lot. Now at that time the Canaanite and the Perizzite were living in that land.

**13:8** So Abram said to Lot, "Please, let there be no dispute between me and you, and between my herdsmen and your herdsmen, for we are brothers.

**13:9** Look, the entire land is before you: withdraw from me, I beg you; if you go to the left, I will hold to the right; if you choose the right, I will proceed to the left."

**13:10** So when Lot lifted up his eyes, he saw all the region around the Jordan, which was entirely watered before the Lord overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah, like the paradise of the Lord, and like Egypt for those coming to Zoar.

**13:11** And Lot chose for himself the region around the Jordan and withdrew from the east; and they were separated each from his brother.

**13:12** Abram lived in the land of Canaan; Lot stayed in the towns that were around the Jordan and lived in Sodom.

**13:13** Now the men of Sodom were very wicked, and very great sinners before the Lord.

**13:14** And the Lord said to Abram, after Lot was separated from him, "Lift up your eyes and look from the place where you now are, to the north and to the south, to the east and to the west.

**13:15** All the land that you see I will give to you and to your seed forever.

**13:16** And I will make your seed like the dust of the earth: if any man can count the dust, he will also be able to count your seed.

**13:17** Arise and walk through the land in its length and in its breadth, for I am going to give it to you."

**13:18** So Abram, moving his tent, came and lived beside the valley of Mamre, which is in Hebron, and built there an altar to the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew miqneh ("livestock/possessions") as in possessione ("in the possession of"), yielding "in the possession of silver and gold" without separately listing livestock.
- **13:11** — Latin ab oriente ("from the east") renders the Hebrew miqqedem, which can also mean "eastward."
- **13:12** — Latin habitavit in Sodomis ("lived in Sodom") is distinctly Vulgate; the Hebrew reads "moved his tent as far as Sodom."
- **13:18** — Latin convallem Mambre ("the valley of Mamre") renders the Hebrew elone Mamre ("the oaks/terebinths of Mamre"), as in 12:6.
