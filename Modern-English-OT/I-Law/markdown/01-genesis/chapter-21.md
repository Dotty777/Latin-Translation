## Text

**21:1** Now the Lord visited Sarah, just as He had promised, and He fulfilled the things He had spoken.

**21:2** And she conceived and gave birth to a son in her old age, at the time God had foretold to her.

**21:3** And Abraham called the name of his son, whom Sarah had borne to him, Isaac;

**21:4** and he circumcised him on the eighth day, just as God had commanded him,

**21:5** when he was one hundred years old; for at this age of his father, Isaac was born.

**21:6** And Sarah said, "God has made laughter for me; whoever hears will laugh with me."

**21:7** And again she said, "Who, upon hearing it, would have believed Abraham that Sarah was nursing a son, whom she bore to him when he was already old?"

**21:8** So the boy grew and was weaned; and Abraham made a great banquet on the day of his weaning.

**21:9** And when Sarah saw the son of Hagar the Egyptian playing with Isaac, her son, she said to Abraham,

**21:10** "Drive out this servant woman and her son, for the son of the servant woman will not be an heir with my son Isaac."

**21:11** Abraham took this hard on account of his son.

**21:12** God said to him, "Do not let it seem harsh to you concerning the boy and concerning your servant woman; in all that Sarah says to you, listen to her voice, because in Isaac your seed will be called.

**21:13** But I will also make the son of the servant woman into a great nation, because he is your seed."

**21:14** So Abraham rose early in the morning, and taking bread and a skin of water, he placed them on her shoulder, handed over the boy, and sent her away. When she had departed, she wandered in the wilderness of Beersheba.

**21:15** And when the water in the skin was used up, she cast the boy down beneath one of the trees that were there.

**21:16** And she went away and sat down opposite, as far off as a bow can shoot; for she said, "I will not watch the boy dying." And sitting opposite, she lifted up her voice and wept.

**21:17** Now God heard the voice of the boy; and the angel of God called to Hagar from heaven, saying, "What are you doing, Hagar? Do not be afraid, for God has heard the voice of the boy from the place where he is.

**21:18** Rise, take up the boy, and hold his hand, because I will make him into a great nation."

**21:19** And God opened her eyes; and seeing a well of water, she went and filled the skin, and gave the boy a drink.

**21:20** And He was with him; and he grew, and stayed in the wilderness, and became a young archer.

**21:21** And he lived in the desert of Paran, and his mother took a wife for him from the land of Egypt.

**21:22** At that same time Abimelech and Phicol, the commander of his army, said to Abraham, "God is with you in everything that you do.

**21:23** Swear therefore by God that you will not harm me, my descendants, or my lineage; rather, according to the mercy that I have done for you, you will do for me and for the land in which you have lived as a stranger."

**21:24** And Abraham said, "I will swear."

**21:25** And he rebuked Abimelech because of a well of water that his servants had taken away by force.

**21:26** Abimelech answered, "I did not know who did this thing; besides, you did not inform me, and I have not heard of it except today."

**21:27** So Abraham took sheep and oxen and gave them to Abimelech; and both of them struck a covenant.

**21:28** And Abraham set seven ewe lambs of the flock apart.

**21:29** Abimelech said to him, "What do these seven ewe lambs mean, which you have made stand apart?"

**21:30** But he said, "You will accept seven ewe lambs from my hand, so that they may be a witness for me that I dug this well."

**21:31** Therefore that place was called Beersheba, because there both of them swore.

**21:32** And they entered into a covenant for the well of the oath.

**21:33** Now Abimelech and Phicol, the commander of his forces, rose and returned to the land of the Philistines. But Abraham planted a grove in Beersheba, and there he called on the name of the Lord, the eternal God.

**21:34** And he was a resident of the land of the Philistines for many days.

## Translator Notes

- **21:2** — Latin in senectute sua ("in her old age") refers reflexively to Sarah, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "to Abraham in his old age."
- **21:7** — The Clementine Vulgate reads nominative 'Quis auditurus crederet Abraham' (literally 'Who, about to hear / upon hearing, would believe Abraham...'; Codex Amiatinus reads accusative 'auditurum', 'that Abraham would hear'), whereas the Hebrew reads 'Who would have said to Abraham... children.'
- **21:9** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate explicitly adds cum Isaac filio suo ("playing with Isaac, her son"), expanding the shorter Hebrew participle meaning "laughing" or "mocking."
- **21:32** — Here the Vulgate translates Beersheba as pro puteo juramenti ("for the well of the oath") and moves Abimelech and Phicol's departure to the start of verse 33; verse 33 also renders the Hebrew tamarisk tree as nemus ("a grove"), and verse 34 uses Palæstinorum as a Latin variant for the Philistines (cf. Philistinorum in verse 33).
