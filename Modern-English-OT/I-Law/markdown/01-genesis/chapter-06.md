## Text

**6:1** And when men had begun to multiply on the earth and had brought forth daughters,

**6:2** the sons of God, seeing the daughters of men, that they were beautiful, took wives for themselves from all whom they had chosen.

**6:3** And God said, "My Spirit will not remain in man forever, because he is flesh; and his days will be one hundred twenty years."

**6:4** Now giants were on the earth in those days; for after the sons of God went in to the daughters of men and they gave birth, these are the mighty ones from ancient times, famous men.

**6:5** Now when God saw that the wickedness of men was great on the earth, and that every thought of the heart was intent on evil at all times,

**6:6** He regretted that He had made man on the earth. And touched inwardly with sorrow of heart,

**6:7** He said, "I will wipe out man, whom I have created, from the face of the earth, from man to animals, from the crawling creature to the birds of the sky; for I regret having made them."

**6:8** Noah, however, found grace before the Lord.

**6:9** These are the generations of Noah: Noah was a righteous and perfect man among his generations; he walked with God.

**6:10** And he fathered three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

**6:11** Now the earth was corrupt before God, and it was filled with iniquity.

**6:12** And when God had seen that the earth was corrupt (for all flesh had corrupted its way on the earth),

**6:13** He said to Noah, "The end of all flesh has come before Me; the earth is filled with iniquity because of them, and I will destroy them together with the earth.

**6:14** Make for yourself an ark of planed wood; you shall make small rooms in the ark, and you shall coat it with bitumen inside and outside.

**6:15** And this is how you shall make it: the length of the ark will be three hundred cubits, the width fifty cubits, and its height thirty cubits.

**6:16** You shall make a window in the ark, and you shall finish the top to a cubit; and you shall place the door of the ark on the side; you shall make lower rooms, second-story chambers, and third stories in it.

**6:17** Look, I Myself will bring the waters of the flood upon the earth to kill all flesh in which is the spirit of life under heaven; everything that is on the earth will be consumed.

**6:18** And I will establish My covenant with you; and you shall enter the ark, you and your sons, your wife, and the wives of your sons with you.

**6:19** And of all living creatures of all flesh, you shall bring two of each into the ark, so that they may live with you: of the male sex and of the female.

**6:20** Of birds according to their kind, and of livestock in their kind, and of every crawling creature of the earth according to its kind: two of each from all of them will enter with you, so that they may be able to live.

**6:21** You shall therefore take with you of all foods that can be eaten, and you shall gather them to yourself; and they will be food both for you and for them."

**6:22** Therefore Noah did everything that the Lord had commanded him.

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — Vulgate reads "God" (Deus) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has YHWH, and renders the debated Hebrew verb yadon as permanebit ("will remain"), agreeing with the Septuagint.
- **6:4** — Vulgate translates Hebrew nephilim as gigantes ("giants"), following the Septuagint, and links the clause with postquam enim ("for after") rather than "and also afterward."
- **6:11** — Vulgate renders Hebrew chamas ("violence") here and in 6:13 as iniquitate ("iniquity").
- **6:14** — Vulgate translates the rare Hebrew phrase for gopher wood (atsei gofer) as de lignis laevigatis ("of planed wood" or "smoothed timber").
- **6:16** — Vulgate renders Hebrew tzohar as fenestram ("window"), and specifies the three levels of the ark as deorsum, coenacula et tristega ("lower rooms, second-story chambers, and third stories").
