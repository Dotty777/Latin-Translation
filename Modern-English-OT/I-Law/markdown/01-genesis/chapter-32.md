## Text

**32:1** Now Laban, rising up in the night, kissed his sons and his daughters and blessed them, returning to his own place.

**32:2** Jacob also went on along the journey he had begun, and angels of God met him.

**32:3** When he saw them, he said, "This is the camp of God," and he called the name of that place Mahanaim, that is, Camp.

**32:4** He also sent messengers ahead of him to his brother Esau in the land of Seir, in the regions of Edom,

**32:5** and he commanded them, saying, "Speak in this way to my lord Esau: 'This is what your brother Jacob says: I have lived as a foreigner with Laban and have remained until the present day.

**32:6** I have cattle and donkeys, sheep, and male servants and female servants, and now I am sending a delegation to my lord so that I may find favor in your sight.'"

**32:7** The messengers returned to Jacob, saying, "We came to your brother Esau, and look, he is hurrying to meet you with four hundred men."

**32:8** Jacob was greatly afraid, and, terrified, he divided the people who were with him, as well as the herds, and the sheep, and the cattle, and the camels, into two companies,

**32:9** saying, "If Esau comes to one company and strikes it, the other company that remains will be saved."

**32:10** And Jacob said, "God of my father Abraham and God of my father Isaac, Lord who said to me, 'Return to your land and to the place of your birth, and I will do good to you,'

**32:11** I am mindful of all the mercies and the truth that You have fulfilled for Your servant. With my staff I crossed this Jordan, and now I am returning with two companies.

**32:12** Rescue me from the hand of my brother, from the hand of Esau, because I am greatly afraid of him, so that he might not come and strike the mother with the children.

**32:13** You said that You would do good to me and would expand my seed like the sand of the sea, which cannot be counted because of its multitude."

**32:14** And when he had slept there that night, he set apart from the things that he had gifts for his brother Esau:

**32:15** two hundred female goats, twenty male goats, two hundred ewes, twenty rams,

**32:16** thirty nursing female camels with their young, forty cows and twenty bulls, twenty female donkeys and ten of their young.

**32:17** And he sent each herd separately by the hands of his servants, and he said to his attendants, "Go ahead of me, and let there be a space between herd and herd."

**32:18** And he commanded the first, saying, "If you meet my brother Esau and he asks you, 'Whose are you?' and, 'Where are you going?' and, 'Whose are these that you are following?'

**32:19** you will answer, 'Your servant Jacob's; he has sent gifts to my lord Esau, and he himself is also following behind us.'"

**32:20** In the same way he gave orders to the second and the third, and to all who were following the herds, saying, "Speak with the same words to Esau when you find him.

**32:21** And you will add, 'Your servant Jacob himself is also following our path.'" For he said, "I will appease him with the gifts that go ahead, and afterward I will see; perhaps he will be favorable to me."

**32:22** So the gifts went on ahead of him, but he himself stayed that night in the camp.

**32:23** And when he had risen early, he took his two wives and the same number of female servants, with eleven sons, and crossed the ford of Jacob.

**32:24** And after everything that belonged to him had been brought across,

**32:25** he remained alone; and look, a man was wrestling with him until morning.

**32:26** When he saw that he could not overpower him, he touched the sinew of his thigh, and immediately it withered.

**32:27** And he said to him, "Let me go, for dawn is already rising." He answered, "I will not let you go unless you bless me."

**32:28** So he said, "What is your name?" He answered, "Jacob."

**32:29** But he said, "By no means will your name be called Jacob, but Israel; for if you have been strong against God, how much more will you prevail against men?"

**32:30** Jacob asked him, "Tell me, by what name are you called?" He answered, "Why do you ask my name?" And he blessed him in that same place.

**32:31** And Jacob called the name of that place Penuel, saying, "I have seen God face to face, and my life has been saved."

**32:32** And immediately the sun rose upon him after he passed beyond Penuel, but he himself was limping on his foot.

**32:33** For this reason the sons of Israel do not eat the sinew that withered in Jacob's thigh until the present day, because he touched the sinew of his thigh and it grew numb.

## Translator Notes

- **32:3** — The explanatory gloss "that is, Camp" (id est, Castra) is added in the Vulgate and does not appear in the Hebrew text; Latin castra is grammatically plural, echoing the dual Hebrew name Mahanaim ("Two Camps").
- **32:11** — The Latin text here reads memor sum ("I am mindful of"), whereas other Vulgate editions read minor sum ("I am less than" or "unworthy of"), corresponding to the Hebrew text.
- **32:23** — The Latin text here reads vadum Jacob ("the ford of Jacob") where the Hebrew text has the stream name Jabbok.
- **32:26** — The Vulgate reads emarcuit ("withered") here and in 32:33 (adding et obstipuerit, "and it grew numb"), whereas the Hebrew indicates that Jacob's hip socket was dislocated.
- **32:29** — Distinctively, the Vulgate expresses the explanation of the name Israel as an a fortiori question ("if you have been strong against God, how much more will you prevail against men?"), whereas the Hebrew has a declarative statement ("you have striven with God and with men, and have prevailed").
