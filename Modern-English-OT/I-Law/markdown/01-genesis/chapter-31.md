## Text

**31:1** Now after he heard the words of Laban's sons saying, "Jacob has taken all that belonged to our father, and having grown rich from his property, he has become renowned,"

**31:2** he also noticed Laban's face, that it was not toward him as yesterday and the day before yesterday,

**31:3** especially with the Lord saying to him, "Return to the land of your fathers and to your relatives, and I will be with you."

**31:4** He sent and called Rachel and Leah into the field where he was pasturing the flocks,

**31:5** and said to them, "I see your father's face, that it is not toward me as yesterday and the day before yesterday; but the God of my father has been with me.

**31:6** And you yourselves know that I have served your father with all my strength.

**31:7** But your father deceived me and changed my wages ten times; and yet God did not allow him to harm me.

**31:8** Whenever he said, 'Your wages will be speckled,' all the sheep bore speckled young; but when, on the contrary, he said, 'All the white ones you will receive as wages,' all the flocks bore white ones.

**31:9** And God took away your father's possessions and gave them to me.

**31:10** For after the time of the sheep's conception had arrived, I lifted up my eyes and saw in a dream the males mounting the females, speckled and spotted and of different colors.

**31:11** And the angel of God said to me in a dream, 'Jacob?' And I answered, 'Here I am.'

**31:12** He said, 'Lift up your eyes and see all the males mounting the females, speckled, flecked, and spotted. For I have seen all that Laban has done to you.

**31:13** I am the God of Bethel, where you anointed the stone and vowed a vow to Me. Now therefore, rise and go out from this land, returning to the land of your birth.'"

**31:14** Rachel and Leah answered, "Do we have anything remaining in the property and inheritance of our father's house?

**31:15** Has he not regarded us as foreigners, and sold us, and consumed our price?

**31:16** But God has taken away our father's wealth and handed it over to us and to our children; therefore do all that God has commanded you."

**31:17** So Jacob rose up, and having placed his children and his wives on camels, departed.

**31:18** And he took all his possessions and flocks, and whatever he had acquired in Mesopotamia, going to his father Isaac in the land of Canaan.

**31:19** At that time Laban had gone to shear the sheep, and Rachel stole her father's idols.

**31:20** And Jacob was unwilling to disclose to his father-in-law that he was fleeing.

**31:21** And when both he himself and all that belonged to him had departed, and after crossing the river he was heading toward Mount Gilead,

**31:22** it was reported to Laban on the third day that Jacob was fleeing.

**31:23** Taking his brothers with him, he pursued him for seven days and overtook him on Mount Gilead.

**31:24** And in a dream he saw God saying to him, "Be careful not to speak anything harshly against Jacob."

**31:25** Now Jacob had already pitched his tent on the mountain, when he caught up with him along with his brothers and set up his tent on the same Mount Gilead.

**31:26** And he said to Jacob, "Why have you acted like this, that without my knowledge you drove away my daughters like captives of the sword?

**31:27** Why did you want to flee while I was unaware, and not inform me, so that I might send you off with joy, and songs, and tambourines, and lyres?

**31:28** You did not allow me to kiss my sons and daughters; you have acted foolishly; and now

**31:29** my hand indeed has the power to repay you with harm; but the God of your father said to me yesterday, 'Be careful not to speak anything too harsh against Jacob.'

**31:30** Granted, you desired to go to your own people, and you had a longing for your father's house; why did you steal my gods?"

**31:31** Jacob answered, "As for setting out without your knowledge, I was afraid that you would take away your daughters by force.

**31:32** But as for your accusing me of theft, with whomever you find your gods, let him be put to death before our brothers; search out whatever of yours you find with me, and take it away." Saying these things, he did not know that Rachel had stolen the idols.

**31:33** So Laban entered the tent of Jacob, and of Leah, and of both maidservants; he did not find them. And when he had entered Rachel's tent,

**31:34** hurrying, she hid the idols under the camel's bedding and sat on top; and as he searched the whole tent and found nothing,

**31:35** she said, "Let my lord not be angry that I cannot rise before you, for it has now happened to me according to the custom of women." Thus the diligence of the one searching was foiled.

**31:36** And Jacob, swelling with anger, said in contention, "For what fault of mine, and for what sin of mine, have you burned so hotly after me,

**31:37** and searched all my household goods? What have you found of all the possessions of your house? Set it here before my brothers and your brothers, and let them judge between me and you.

**31:38** Was it for this that I have been with you twenty years? Your sheep and goats were not barren; the rams of your flock I did not eat;

**31:39** nor did I show you what was seized by a beast; I myself repaid every loss; whatever was lost by theft, you demanded from me;

**31:40** day and night I was burned by heat and by frost; sleep fled from my eyes.

**31:41** Thus for twenty years in your house I served you, fourteen for your daughters and six for your flocks; you also changed my wages ten times.

**31:42** Unless the God of my father Abraham and the fear of Isaac had been with me, perhaps now you would have sent me away naked; God has looked upon my affliction and the labor of my hands, and rebuked you yesterday."

**31:43** Laban answered him, "My daughters and the children, and your flocks, and all that you see, are mine; what can I do to my children and my grandchildren?

**31:44** Come therefore, and let us enter into a covenant, so that it may be a testimony between me and you."

**31:45** So Jacob took a stone and set it up as a monument,

**31:46** and said to his brothers, "Bring stones." Gathering them, they made a mound and ate on it;

**31:47** which Laban called Mound of Witness, and Jacob, Heap of Testimony, each according to the idiom of his own language.

**31:48** And Laban said, "This mound will be a witness between me and you today," and for this reason its name was called Gilead, that is, Mound of Witness.

**31:49** "May the Lord watch and judge between us when we have departed from one another,

**31:50** if you afflict my daughters, and if you bring in other wives besides them; there is no witness to our word except God, who is present and looks on."

**31:51** And he said again to Jacob, "Look, this mound and the stone which I have set up between me and you,

**31:52** will be a witness: let this mound, I say, and the stone be for a testimony, if either I pass beyond it going to you, or you pass by, devising harm against me.

**31:53** May the God of Abraham and the God of Nahor judge between us, the God of their father." Jacob swore by the fear of his father Isaac,

**31:54** and having offered sacrifices on the mountain, he called his brothers to eat bread. And when they had eaten, they remained there.

**31:55** Now Laban, rising up in the night, kissed his sons and his daughters and blessed them, returning to his own place.

## Translator Notes

- **31:8** — In the Vulgate, Laban's second condition specifies alba ("white"), whereas the Hebrew has "striped" (or "streaked").
- **31:24** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew idiom "from good to bad" here as aspere ("harshly"), and similarly as durius ("too harsh") in 31:29.
- **31:42** — The Vulgate reads nudum ("naked"), whereas the Hebrew has "empty-handed."
- **31:47** — Rather than transliterating the Aramaic and Hebrew place names (Jegar-sahadutha and Galeed), the Vulgate translates both directly into Latin (Tumulus testis and Acervum testimonii) and adds "each according to the idiom of his own language."
