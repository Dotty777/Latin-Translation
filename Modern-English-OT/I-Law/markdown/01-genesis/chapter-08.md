## Text

**8:1** Now God remembered Noah, and all the living creatures, and all the livestock that were with him in the ark; He brought a wind over the earth, and the waters diminished.

**8:2** And the springs of the deep and the floodgates of heaven were closed, and the rains from heaven were restrained.

**8:3** And the waters turned back from the earth, going and returning; and they began to diminish after one hundred fifty days.

**8:4** And the ark rested in the seventh month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, on the mountains of Armenia.

**8:5** Meanwhile, the waters went on and decreased until the tenth month; for in the tenth month, on the first day of the month, the peaks of the mountains appeared.

**8:6** And when forty days had passed, Noah, opening the window of the ark that he had made, released a raven,

**8:7** which went out and did not return until the waters were dried up on the earth.

**8:8** He also sent out a dove after it, to see whether the waters had already ceased on the face of the earth.

**8:9** And when it had not found where its foot might rest, it returned to him into the ark, for the waters were over the whole earth; and he stretched out his hand, took hold of it, and brought it into the ark.

**8:10** Then, having waited yet seven other days, he again released the dove from the ark.

**8:11** And it came to him toward evening, carrying an olive branch with green leaves in its mouth; therefore Noah understood that the waters had ceased on the earth.

**8:12** And nevertheless he waited seven other days, and sent out the dove, which did not return to him anymore.

**8:13** Therefore in the six hundred first year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the waters diminished on the earth; and Noah, opening the roof of the ark, looked out and saw that the surface of the earth had dried up.

**8:14** In the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dry.

**8:15** Then God spoke to Noah, saying,

**8:16** "Go out of the ark, you and your wife, your sons and the wives of your sons with you.

**8:17** Bring out with you all the living creatures that are with you, of all flesh, both among flying creatures and among beasts and among all crawling creatures that crawl on the earth, and enter upon the earth: increase and multiply upon it."

**8:18** So Noah went out, and his sons, his wife, and the wives of his sons with him.

**8:19** And also all the living creatures, livestock, and crawling creatures that crawl on the earth went out of the ark according to their kind.

**8:20** Then Noah built an altar to the Lord; and taking from all the clean livestock and birds, he offered burnt offerings on the altar.

**8:21** And the Lord smelled a sweet aroma, and said, "By no means will I curse the earth again because of men, for the perception and thought of the human heart are prone to evil from its youth; therefore I will not again strike every living soul as I have done.

**8:22** For all the days of the earth, sowing and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, night and day will not rest."

## Translator Notes

- **8:4** — Vulgate reads "the twenty-seventh day" (vigesimo septimo die, agreeing with the Septuagint against the Hebrew "seventeenth") and translates Hebrew Ararat as Armeniae ("Armenia").
- **8:7** — Vulgate agrees with the Septuagint in reading et non revertebatur ("and did not return"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "going and returning" without a negative.
- **8:11** — Vulgate describes what the dove carried as ramum olivae virentibus foliis ("an olive branch with green leaves"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks of a freshly plucked olive leaf.
- **8:17** — Vulgate reads ingredimini super terram ("enter upon the earth"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "let them swarm on the earth."
