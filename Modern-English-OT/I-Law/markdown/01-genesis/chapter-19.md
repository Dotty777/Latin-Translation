## Text

**19:1** And the two angels came to Sodom in the evening, while Lot was sitting at the gates of the city. When he saw them, he got up and went to meet them, and bowed down prostrate on the ground,

**19:2** and said, "I beg you, my lords, turn aside into the house of your servant and stay there; wash your feet, and in the morning set out on your way." They said, "No; instead, we will stay in the square."

**19:3** He pressed them very strongly to turn in with him; and when they had entered his house, he prepared a feast, baked unleavened bread, and they ate.

**19:4** But before they went to lie down, the men of the city surrounded the house, from boy to old man, all the people together.

**19:5** And they called to Lot and said to him, "Where are the men who came in to you in the night? Bring them out here, so that we may know them."

**19:6** Lot went out to them, shutting the door behind him, and said,

**19:7** "Please, my brothers, do not, do not do this evil.

**19:8** I have two daughters who have not yet known a man; I will bring them out to you, and abuse them as it pleases you, provided only that you do no evil to these men, because they have come under the shadow of my roof."

**19:9** But they said, "Step back over there." And again, "You came in," they said, "as a foreigner; surely not to judge? Therefore we will afflict you yourself more than these men." And they pressed against Lot most violently, and were already close to breaking down the doors.

**19:10** And look, the men reached out their hands and brought Lot inside to themselves, and shut the door,

**19:11** and they struck those who were outside with blindness, from the smallest to the greatest, so that they could not find the door.

**19:12** Now they said to Lot, "Do you have anyone of your own here? Son-in-law, or sons, or daughters, all who belong to you, bring them out of this city;

**19:13** for we will wipe out this place, because their outcry has grown great before the Lord, who sent us to destroy them."

**19:14** So Lot went out and spoke to his sons-in-law, who were about to marry his daughters, and said, "Get up, go out from this place, because the Lord will wipe out this city." And he seemed to them to be speaking as if joking.

**19:15** And when it was morning, the angels pressed him, saying, "Get up, take your wife and the two daughters whom you have, so that you do not likewise perish in the wickedness of the city."

**19:16** While he hesitated, they took hold of his hand, and the hand of his wife, and of his two daughters, because the Lord was sparing him.

**19:17** And they brought him out and set him outside the city. And there they spoke to him, saying, "Save your life; do not look behind you, nor stop in all the surrounding region, but save yourself on the mountain, so that you also do not perish at the same time."

**19:18** And Lot said to them, "Please, my lord,

**19:19** since your servant has found favor before you, and you have magnified your mercy which you have shown to me to save my life, yet I cannot be saved on the mountain, for fear that disaster may overtake me and I die;

**19:20** there is this city nearby to which I can flee, a small one, and I will be saved in it; is it not a little one, and my soul will live?"

**19:21** And he said to him, "Look, even in this I have accepted your prayers, so that I will not overthrow the city for which you have spoken.

**19:22** Hurry, and be saved there, because I will not be able to do anything until you enter there." For that reason, the name of that city was called Zoar.

**19:23** The sun rose over the earth, and Lot entered Zoar.

**19:24** Then the Lord rained upon Sodom and Gomorrah sulfur and fire from the Lord out of heaven,

**19:25** and overthrew these cities and all the surrounding region, all the inhabitants of the cities, and all the green things of the earth.

**19:26** And as his wife looked back behind her, she was turned into a statue of salt.

**19:27** Now Abraham, rising in the morning where he had previously stood with the Lord,

**19:28** gazed out over Sodom and Gomorrah and the entire land of that region, and he saw ashes rising from the earth like the smoke of a furnace.

**19:29** For when God overthrew the cities of that region, remembering Abraham, He delivered Lot from the overthrow of the cities in which he had lived.

**19:30** And Lot went up from Zoar and stayed on the mountain, and his two daughters also with him (for he had been afraid to stay in Zoar), and he stayed in a cave, he and his two daughters with him.

**19:31** And the older said to the younger, "Our father is old, and none of the men has remained on the earth who could come in to us according to the custom of the whole earth.

**19:32** Come, let us make him drunk with wine, and let us sleep with him, so that we may preserve seed from our father."

**19:33** So they gave their father wine to drink that night. And the older went in and slept with her father; but he did not notice either when his daughter lay down or when she got up.

**19:34** On the next day also, the older said to the younger, "Look, yesterday I slept with my father; let us give him wine to drink this night also, and you will sleep with him, so that we may save seed from our father."

**19:35** On that night as well they gave their father wine to drink, and the younger daughter went in and slept with him; and not even then did he notice when she lay with him or when she got up.

**19:36** So the two daughters of Lot conceived by their father.

**19:37** And the older gave birth to a son and called his name Moab; he is the father of the Moabites to the present day.

**19:38** The younger also gave birth to a son and called his name Ammon, that is, "Son of my people"; he is the father of the Ammonites to this day.

## Translator Notes

- **19:17** — In v. 18 Lot speaks to "them" ("ad eos") while addressing a single spokesperson as lowercase "domine mi" ("my lord").
- **19:22** — The Latin spells Zoar as "Segor" (following the Septuagint transliteration of Hebrew Tsoar, linked to "small" in v. 20).
- **19:26** — The Vulgate reads "statuam salis" ("a statue of salt"), rather than the Hebrew "pillar of salt."
- **19:29** — The Vulgate reads "civitates regionis illius" ("the cities of that region") where the Hebrew has "the cities of the valley."
- **19:38** — Whereas the Hebrew gives the son's name as Ben-ammi ("son of my people"), the Vulgate writes "Ammon" directly and supplies the explanatory gloss "id est, Filius populi mei" ("that is, 'Son of my people'").
