## Text

**5:1** This is the book of the generation of Adam. On the day that God created man, He made him in the likeness of God.

**5:2** Male and female He created them, and blessed them, and called their name Adam on the day that they were created.

**5:3** Now Adam lived one hundred thirty years, and fathered one in his own likeness and image, and called his name Seth.

**5:4** And the days of Adam, after he fathered Seth, came to eight hundred years; and he fathered sons and daughters.

**5:5** And all the time that Adam lived came to nine hundred thirty years, and he died.

**5:6** Seth also lived one hundred five years, and fathered Enosh.

**5:7** And Seth lived eight hundred seven years after he fathered Enosh, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:8** And all the days of Seth came to nine hundred twelve years, and he died.

**5:9** Now Enosh lived ninety years, and fathered Kenan.

**5:10** After his birth he lived eight hundred fifteen years, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:11** And all the days of Enosh came to nine hundred five years, and he died.

**5:12** Kenan also lived seventy years, and fathered Mahalalel.

**5:13** And Kenan lived eight hundred forty years after he fathered Mahalalel, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:14** And all the days of Kenan came to nine hundred ten years, and he died.

**5:15** Now Mahalalel lived sixty-five years, and fathered Jared.

**5:16** And Mahalalel lived eight hundred thirty years after he fathered Jared, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:17** And all the days of Mahalalel came to eight hundred ninety-five years, and he died.

**5:18** And Jared lived one hundred sixty-two years, and fathered Enoch.

**5:19** And Jared lived eight hundred years after he fathered Enoch, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:20** And all the days of Jared came to nine hundred sixty-two years, and he died.

**5:21** Moreover, Enoch lived sixty-five years, and fathered Methuselah.

**5:22** And Enoch walked with God for three hundred years after he fathered Methuselah, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:23** And all the days of Enoch came to three hundred sixty-five years.

**5:24** And he walked with God, and he was not seen, because God took him.

**5:25** Methuselah also lived one hundred eighty-seven years, and fathered Lamech.

**5:26** And Methuselah lived seven hundred eighty-two years after he fathered Lamech, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:27** And all the days of Methuselah came to nine hundred sixty-nine years, and he died.

**5:28** Now Lamech lived one hundred eighty-two years and fathered a son.

**5:29** And he called his name Noah, saying, "This one will comfort us from the works and toils of our hands on the earth which the Lord has cursed."

**5:30** And Lamech lived five hundred ninety-five years after he fathered Noah, and fathered sons and daughters.

**5:31** And all the days of Lamech came to seven hundred seventy-seven years, and he died. Now Noah, when he was five hundred years old, fathered Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

## Translator Notes

- **5:2** — The Vulgate retains "Adam" as a proper name for the pair ("called their name Adam") rather than translating it as a common noun ("Man" or "Humankind").
- **5:3** — Like the Hebrew, the Latin omits an explicit direct object after "genuit" ("fathered"), translated literally here as "fathered one."
- **5:24** — The Vulgate reads "non apparuit" ("he was not seen" or "he did not appear"), corresponding to the Hebrew "he was not" and the Septuagint "he was not found."
- **5:31** — In the Vulgate verse numbering, Genesis 5 ends at verse 31, incorporating the notice of Noah's age and his three sons that Hebrew and most modern English Bibles place in a separate verse 32.
