## Text

**15:1** So after these things had taken place, the word of the Lord came to Abram through a vision, saying, "Do not be afraid, Abram; I am your protector, and your reward is exceedingly great."

**15:2** And Abram said, "Lord God, what will You give me? I will go without children, and the son of the steward of my house is this Damascus Eliezer."

**15:3** And Abram added, "Yet to me You have not given offspring, and look, my household-born servant will be my heir."

**15:4** And immediately the word of the Lord came to him, saying, "This one will not be your heir, but he who will come out from your body, him you will have as heir."

**15:5** And He led him outside and said to him, "Look up at heaven and count the stars, if you can." And He said to him, "So will your offspring be."

**15:6** He believed the Lord, and it was credited to him as righteousness.

**15:7** And He said to him, "I am the Lord who led you out of Ur of the Chaldeans, so that I might give you this land and you might possess it."

**15:8** But he said, "Lord God, how can I know that I will possess it?"

**15:9** The Lord answered and said, "Take for Me a three-year-old cow, a three-year-old female goat, and a three-year-old ram, a turtledove also, and a pigeon."

**15:10** Taking all these, he divided them through the middle and placed each half opposite the other on either side; but he did not divide the birds.

**15:11** And birds descended upon the carcasses, and Abram kept driving them away.

**15:12** And as the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell upon Abram, and a great and dark dread seized him.

**15:13** And it was said to him, "Know beforehand for certain that your offspring will be a stranger in a land not its own, and they will subject them to servitude and afflict them for four hundred years.

**15:14** Yet the nation whom they will serve I will judge, and after these things they will come out with great possessions.

**15:15** As for you, you will go to your fathers in peace, buried in a good old age.

**15:16** And in the fourth generation they will return here, for the iniquities of the Amorites are not yet completed up to the present time."

**15:17** So when the sun had gone down, a dark gloom came about, and a smoking furnace appeared, and a torch of fire passing between those divided parts.

**15:18** On that day the Lord made a covenant with Abram, saying, "To your offspring I will give this land, from the river of Egypt as far as the great river, the river Euphrates,

**15:19** the Kenites, and the Kenizzites, and the Kadmonites,

**15:20** and the Hittites, and the Perizzites, the Rephaim also,

**15:21** and the Amorites, and the Canaanites, and the Girgashites, and the Jebusites."

## Translator Notes

- **15:1** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew metaphor "shield" as "protector" (Latin protector), echoing "under whose protection" in 14:20.
- **15:2** — The Vulgate resolves a difficult Hebrew phrase as "the son of the steward of my house is this Damascus Eliezer" (Latin filius procuratoris domus meae iste Damascus Eliezer).
- **15:4** — Latin de utero tuo literally means "from your belly" or "from your womb," used here in its broader anatomical sense for Abram's own body as the source of his natural heir.
- **15:6** — Following the passive construction also found in the Septuagint and Romans 4:3, the Latin reads passive "it was credited to him" (deputatum est ei) rather than the Hebrew active "He reckoned it to him."
