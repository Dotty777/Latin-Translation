## Text

**34:1** Now Dinah, the daughter of Leah, went out to see the women of that region.

**34:2** When Shechem, the son of Hamor the Hivite, prince of that land, saw her, he fell in love with her and seized her, and he slept with her, overpowering the virgin by force.

**34:3** And his soul was closely bound to her, and in her sorrow he soothed her with gentle words.

**34:4** And going to his father Hamor, he said, "Take this girl for me as a wife."

**34:5** When Jacob heard this, while his sons were absent and occupied in pasturing the livestock, he kept silent until they returned.

**34:6** Now when Hamor, the father of Shechem, had gone out to speak to Jacob,

**34:7** look, his sons were coming from the field; and when they heard what had happened, they were very angry, because he had done a disgraceful thing in Israel and, having violated the daughter of Jacob, had committed an unlawful deed.

**34:8** So Hamor spoke to them: "The soul of my son Shechem has clung to your daughter; give her to him as a wife,

**34:9** and let us join in marriage with one another; give your daughters to us, and take our daughters,

**34:10** and live with us; the land is in your power: cultivate it, trade, and possess it."

**34:11** Shechem also said to her father and to her brothers, "Let me find favor before you, and whatever you determine, I will give;

**34:12** increase the dowry, demand gifts; I will gladly grant what you ask; only give me this girl as a wife."

**34:13** The sons of Jacob answered Shechem and his father with deceit, raging because of the defilement of their sister:

**34:14** "We cannot do what you ask, nor give our sister to an uncircumcised man, which is unlawful and abominable among us.

**34:15** But on this condition we will be able to enter into an alliance: if you are willing to be like us, and everyone of the male sex among you is circumcised;

**34:16** then we will mutually give and take our daughters and yours, and we will live with you, and we will be one people.

**34:17** But if you are unwilling to be circumcised, we will take our daughter and depart."

**34:18** Their proposal pleased Hamor and his son Shechem,

**34:19** and the young man did not delay in immediately fulfilling what was asked, for he loved the girl very much, and he himself was renowned in all the house of his father.

**34:20** And having entered the gate of the city, they spoke to the people:

**34:21** "These men are peaceful and wish to live with us; let them trade in the land and cultivate it, for being spacious and wide it needs cultivators; we will take their daughters as wives, and we will give ours to them.

**34:22** There is one thing by which so great a good is delayed: if we circumcise our males, imitating the rite of the nation.

**34:23** And their property, and their livestock, and all that they possess will be ours; only let us consent to this, and living together, we will form one people."

**34:24** All agreed, and all the males were circumcised.

**34:25** And look, on the third day, when the pain of the wounds is most severe, two sons of Jacob, Simeon and Levi, the brothers of Dinah, having seized their swords, entered the city boldly; and having killed all the males,

**34:26** they put Hamor and Shechem to death together, taking Dinah their sister from the house of Shechem.

**34:27** When they had gone out, the rest of the sons of Jacob rushed upon those who had been killed and pillaged the city in vengeance for the defilement.

**34:28** Plundering their sheep, and herds, and donkeys, and everything that was in the houses and in the fields,

**34:29** they also led away their little ones and their wives captive.

**34:30** When these things had been audaciously carried out, Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, "You have troubled me and made me hateful to the Canaanites and the Perizzites, the inhabitants of this land; we are few; they will gather together and strike me, and I will be destroyed, I and my house."

**34:31** They answered, "Should they have abused our sister like a prostitute?"

## Translator Notes

- **34:2** — The Vulgate adds 'fell in love with her' (adamavit) and renders the final clause as 'overpowering the virgin by force' (vi opprimens virginem), where the Hebrew simply has 'took her, lay with her, and humbled her.'
- **34:21** — The Vulgate interprets the Hebrew idiom 'wide of hands' as 'being spacious and wide it needs cultivators' (quae spatiosa et lata cultoribus indiget), expanding on the theme of open land.
- **34:22** — Latin 'Unum est quod differtur tantum bonum' ('There is one thing by which so great a good is delayed') is a distinctive Vulgate rendering of the Hebrew condition ('Only on this condition will the men consent to live with us').
- **34:25** — Latin 'confidenter' ('boldly') applies the Hebrew adverb (often understood as 'unsuspectingly' of the city) to Simeon and Levi's confident entry; the Vulgate also specifies 'when the pain of the wounds is most severe' (quando gravissimus vulnerum dolor est).
