## Text

**28:1** Therefore Isaac called Jacob, and blessed him and commanded him, saying, "Do not take a wife from the lineage of Canaan;

**28:2** but go and set out for Mesopotamia of Syria, to the house of Bethuel, your mother's father, and take for yourself a wife from there, from the daughters of Laban, your uncle.

**28:3** And may Almighty God bless you, and make you grow and multiply you, so that you may become multitudes of peoples.

**28:4** And may He give the blessings of Abraham to you and to your seed after you, so that you may possess the land of your sojourning, which He promised to your grandfather."

**28:5** And when Isaac had sent him away, he set out and came to Mesopotamia of Syria, to Laban, son of Bethuel the Syrian, the brother of his mother Rebekah.

**28:6** Now Esau, seeing that his father had blessed Jacob and had sent him to Mesopotamia of Syria to take a wife from there, and that after the blessing he had commanded him, saying, "You will not take a wife from the daughters of Canaan,"

**28:7** and that Jacob, obeying his parents, had gone to Syria,

**28:8** realizing also that his father did not look gladly upon the daughters of Canaan,

**28:9** went to Ishmael, and took as a wife, besides those whom he previously had, Maelech, the daughter of Ishmael, son of Abraham, the sister of Nebaioth.

**28:10** Therefore Jacob, departing from Beersheba, was journeying toward Haran.

**28:11** And when he had come to a certain place, and wanted to rest in it after the setting of the sun, he took from the stones that were lying there, and placing one under his head, he slept in that same place.

**28:12** And he saw in a dream a ladder standing on the earth, with its summit touching heaven, and angels of God also ascending and descending along it,

**28:13** and the Lord leaning upon the ladder, saying to him, "I am the Lord God of your father Abraham, and the God of Isaac. The land on which you sleep, I will give to you and to your seed.

**28:14** And your offspring will be like the dust of the earth; you will spread out to the west and the east, the north and the south; and in you and in your seed all the tribes of the earth will be blessed.

**28:15** And I will be your guardian wherever you go, and I will bring you back to this land; nor will I leave you until I have fulfilled everything that I have spoken."

**28:16** And when Jacob awoke from sleep, he said, "Truly the Lord is in this place, and I did not know."

**28:17** And trembling with fear, he said, "How fearful is this place! There is nothing else here except the house of God, and the gates of heaven."

**28:18** Rising in the morning, therefore, he took the stone that he had placed under his head, and set it up as a monument, pouring oil on top of it.

**28:19** And he called the name of the city Bethel, which was previously called Luz.

**28:20** He also vowed a vow, saying, "If God is with me, and guards me on the way along which I walk, and gives me bread to eat and clothing to put on,

**28:21** and I return prosperously to my father's house, the Lord will be God to me,

**28:22** and this stone, which I have set up as a monument, will be called the House of God; and of all things that You give me, I will offer tithes to You."

## Translator Notes

- **28:2** — Vulgate renders Hebrew Paddan-aram as 'Mesopotamia of Syria' (here and in vv. 5-6), and correspondingly uses 'the Syrian' and 'Syria' in vv. 5 and 7.
- **28:4** — Vulgate reads plural 'blessings' and 'which He promised to your grandfather' (quam pollicitus est avo tuo), where the Hebrew has singular 'blessing' and 'which God gave to Abraham.'
- **28:9** — Clementine Vulgate reads the proper name Maelech, corresponding to Mahalath in the Hebrew text.
- **28:13** — Vulgate renders 'leaning upon the ladder' (innixum scalae), giving a concrete sense to the Hebrew phrase that can mean 'standing above it' or 'standing beside him.'
- **28:17** — The Latin text reads plural portae caeli ('the gates of heaven'), whereas the Hebrew text and other Vulgate witnesses read the singular 'gate.'
