## Text

**38:1** At that time, Judah, going down from his brothers, turned aside to an Adullamite man named Hirah.

**38:2** And there he saw the daughter of a Canaanite man called Shua; and having taken her as wife, he went in to her.

**38:3** She conceived and bore a son, and called his name Er.

**38:4** Having conceived a child again, she named the son who was born Onan.

**38:5** She also bore a third, whom she called Shelah; and after he was born, she ceased to give birth any further.

**38:6** Now Judah gave a wife to his firstborn Er, named Tamar.

**38:7** And Er, Judah's firstborn, was wicked in the sight of the Lord, and he was killed by Him.

**38:8** Therefore Judah said to Onan his son, "Go in to your brother's wife, and be united to her, so that you may raise up seed for your brother."

**38:9** He, knowing that the sons would not be born for himself, when going in to his brother's wife, poured out the seed on the ground, so that children would not be born in his brother's name.

**38:10** And for this reason the Lord struck him down, because he was doing a detestable thing.

**38:11** For this reason Judah said to Tamar his daughter-in-law, "Be a widow in your father's house until my son Shelah grows up." For he was afraid that he too might die, like his brothers. And she went away and lived in her father's house.

**38:12** Now after many days had passed, the daughter of Shua, Judah's wife, died; and he, having received consolation after mourning, was going up to the shearers of his sheep, he and Hirah the Adullamite, shepherd of the flock, to Timnah.

**38:13** And it was reported to Tamar that her father-in-law was going up to Timnah to shear the sheep.

**38:14** Having laid aside the garments of widowhood, she put on a veil; and having changed her attire, she sat at the fork of the road that leads to Timnah, because Shelah had grown up and she had not received him as a husband.

**38:15** When Judah saw her, he suspected she was a prostitute, for she had covered her face so that she would not be recognized.

**38:16** And approaching her, he said, "Allow me to lie with you," for he did not know that she was his daughter-in-law. When she answered, "What will you give me so that you may enjoy lying with me?"

**38:17** he said, "I will send you a young goat from the flocks." And when she said again, "I will permit what you want, if you give me a pledge until you send what you promise,"

**38:18** Judah said, "What do you want to be given to you as a pledge?" She answered, "Your ring and bracelet, and the staff that you hold in your hand." Therefore at a single union the woman conceived,

**38:19** and rising up, she went away; and having laid aside the attire that she had put on, she put on the garments of widowhood.

**38:20** Now Judah sent the young goat by his Adullamite shepherd in order to recover the pledge that he had given to the woman; and when he did not find her,

**38:21** he asked the men of that place, "Where is the woman who was sitting at the fork in the road?" And all answered, "There has been no prostitute in this place."

**38:22** He returned to Judah and said to him, "I have not found her; and besides, the men of that place told me that a prostitute had never sat there."

**38:23** Judah said, "Let her keep it for herself; certainly she will not be able to accuse us of a lie. I sent the young goat that I had promised, and you did not find her."

**38:24** Now, after three months, they reported to Judah, saying, "Tamar your daughter-in-law has committed fornication, and her womb appears to be swelling." Judah said, "Bring her out so that she may be burned."

**38:25** When she was being led to punishment, she sent word to her father-in-law, saying, "By the man to whom these belong, I have conceived; recognize to whom the ring, bracelet, and staff belong."

**38:26** And he, having recognized the gifts, said, "She is more righteous than I, because I did not give her to my son Shelah." Yet he did not know her any further.

**38:27** Now when birth was at hand, twins appeared in the womb; and in the very delivery of the infants, one put out a hand, on which the midwife tied a scarlet thread, saying,

**38:28** "This one is coming out first."

**38:29** But as he drew back his hand, the other came out; and the woman said, "Why has the wall been divided because of you?" And for this reason she called his name Perez.

**38:30** Afterward the brother came out, on whose hand was the scarlet thread, whom she called Zerah.

## Translator Notes

- **38:5** — Reading the Hebrew place name Chezib as a verb meaning "to cease," the Vulgate renders quo nato, parere ultra cessavit ("after he was born, she ceased to give birth any further").
- **38:12** — The Vulgate identifies Hirah as opilio gregis ("shepherd of the flock") and in 38:20 as pastorem suum ("his shepherd"), reading the underlying Hebrew consonants as "shepherd" (with the Septuagint) rather than Masoretic "his friend."
- **38:14** — Latin in bivio itineris ("at the fork of the road," and in bivio in 38:21) treats the Hebrew phrase rendered in the Masoretic Text as the place name Enaim as a common noun denoting a fork in the road.
- **38:18** — Latin armillam ("bracelet," also in 38:25) renders Hebrew petil ("cord"), and Ad unum igitur coitum ("at a single union") is a distinctly concise Vulgate wording of the encounter.
- **38:29** — Latin Quare divisa est propter te maceria? ("Why has the wall been divided because of you?") renders the Hebrew wordplay on "breach" (explaining the name Perez, Latin Phares) as a divided enclosure wall (maceria).
