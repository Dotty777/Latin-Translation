## Text

**24:1** Now Abraham was old and of many days, and the Lord had blessed him in everything.

**24:2** And he said to the eldest servant of his house, who was over all that he had, "Place your hand under my thigh,

**24:3** so that I may make you swear by the Lord God of heaven and earth that you will not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites, among whom I live,

**24:4** but will journey to my land and to my relatives, and from there take a wife for my son Isaac."

**24:5** The servant answered, "If the woman is not willing to come with me into this land, must I bring your son back to the place from which you came out?"

**24:6** Abraham said, "Be careful that you never bring my son back there.

**24:7** The Lord God of heaven, who took me from the house of my father and from the land of my birth, who spoke to me and swore, saying, 'I will give this land to your offspring,' He Himself will send His angel before you, and from there you will take a wife for my son.

**24:8** But if the woman is not willing to follow you, you will not be bound by the oath; only do not bring my son back there."

**24:9** Therefore the servant put his hand under the thigh of his master Abraham and swore to him about this matter.

**24:10** And he took ten camels from his master's herd and departed, carrying with him some of all his goods; and setting out, he proceeded to Mesopotamia, to the city of Nahor.

**24:11** And when he had made the camels lie down outside the town beside a well of water that evening, at the time when women usually go out to draw water, he said,

**24:12** "Lord God of my master Abraham, meet me today, I beg You, and show mercy to my master Abraham.

**24:13** Look, I am standing beside the spring of water, and the daughters of the inhabitants of this city will come out to draw water.

**24:14** Therefore the young woman to whom I say, 'Lower your jar so that I may drink,' and she answers, 'Drink, and I will even give your camels a drink,' she is the one whom You have prepared for Your servant Isaac; and by this I will understand that You have shown mercy to my master."

**24:15** He had not yet finished the words within himself, and look, Rebekah was coming out, the daughter of Bethuel, the son of Milcah, the wife of Nahor, Abraham's brother, with a jar on her shoulder,

**24:16** a very lovely young woman, and a most beautiful virgin, and unknown to any man. Now she had gone down to the spring, and had filled the jar, and was returning.

**24:17** And the servant ran to meet her and said, "Give me a tiny bit of water to sip from your jar."

**24:18** She answered, "Drink, my lord." And she quickly lowered the jar onto her forearm and gave him a drink.

**24:19** And when he had drunk, she added, "Indeed, I will draw water for your camels as well, until all of them drink."

**24:20** And pouring out the jar into the troughs, she ran back to the well to draw water; and when she had drawn it, she gave it to all the camels.

**24:21** Now he was gazing at her silently, wanting to know whether the Lord had made his journey prosperous or not.

**24:22** Now after the camels had drunk, the man brought out golden earrings weighing two shekels, and just as many bracelets weighing ten shekels.

**24:23** And he said to her, "Whose daughter are you? Tell me, is there a place in your father's house to stay?"

**24:24** And she replied, "I am the daughter of Bethuel, son of Milcah, whom she bore to Nahor."

**24:25** And she added, saying, "There is also plenty of straw and hay with us, and a spacious place to stay."

**24:26** The man bowed down and worshiped the Lord,

**24:27** saying, "Blessed be the Lord God of my master Abraham, who has not withdrawn His mercy and truth from my master, and has guided me by a straight path into the house of my master's brother."

**24:28** So the young woman ran and reported to her mother's house everything that she had heard.

**24:29** Now Rebekah had a brother named Laban, who went out hurriedly to the man, where the spring was.

**24:30** And when he had seen the earrings and the bracelets on the hands of his sister, and had heard all the words she reported, "The man spoke these things to me," he came to the man who was standing beside the camels, near the spring of water,

**24:31** and said to him, "Enter, blessed of the Lord! Why are you standing outside? I have prepared the house and a place for the camels."

**24:32** And he brought him into the lodging, and unsaddled the camels, and gave straw and hay, and water to wash the feet of the camels and of the men who had come with him.

**24:33** And bread was set before him. But he said, "I will not eat until I speak my words." He answered him, "Speak."

**24:34** And he said, "I am the servant of Abraham.

**24:35** And the Lord has greatly blessed my master, and he has been made great; and He has given him sheep and oxen, silver and gold, male servants and female servants, camels and donkeys.

**24:36** And Sarah, the wife of my master, bore a son to my master in her old age, and he gave him all that he possessed.

**24:37** And my master made me swear, saying, 'You will not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites, in whose land I live,

**24:38** but you will go to the house of my father, and from my relatives you will take a wife for my son.'

**24:39** But I answered my master, 'What if the woman is not willing to come with me?'

**24:40** He said, 'The Lord, in whose sight I walk, will send His angel with you and will direct your way; and you will take a wife for my son from my relatives and from the house of my father.

**24:41** You will be innocent of my curse when you come to my kin and they do not give her to you.'

**24:42** Therefore I came today to the spring and said, 'Lord God of my master Abraham, if You have directed my way on which I now walk,

**24:43** look, I am standing beside the spring of water, and the virgin who will come out to draw water hears from me, "Give me a tiny bit of water to drink from your jar,"

**24:44** and she says to me, "You drink, and I will draw for your camels as well," she is the woman whom the Lord has prepared for my master's son.'

**24:45** While I was silently turning these things over within myself, Rebekah appeared, coming with a jar that she was carrying on her shoulder; and she went down to the spring and drew water. And I said to her, 'Give me a little to drink.'

**24:46** And she hurriedly lowered the jar from her shoulder and said to me, 'You drink, and I will give your camels a drink as well.' I drank, and she watered the camels.

**24:47** And I asked her and said, 'Whose daughter are you?' She answered, 'I am the daughter of Bethuel, the son of Nahor, whom Milcah bore to him.' Therefore I hung the earrings to adorn her face, and I placed the bracelets on her hands.

**24:48** And bowing low, I worshiped the Lord, blessing the Lord God of my master Abraham, who had guided me by a straight path so that I might take the daughter of my master's brother for his son.

**24:49** For this reason, if you show mercy and truth to my master, tell me; but if something else pleases you, say this as well, so that I may go to the right or to the left."

**24:50** Laban and Bethuel answered, "The word has come out from the Lord; we cannot speak anything else with you beyond His good pleasure.

**24:51** Look, Rebekah is before you: take her and set out, and may she be the wife of the son of your master, just as the Lord has spoken."

**24:52** When Abraham's servant had heard this, he worshiped the Lord on the ground.

**24:53** And having brought out vessels of silver and of gold, and garments, he gave them to Rebekah as a gift; he also offered gifts to her brothers and to her mother.

**24:54** And when a feast had begun, eating together and drinking, they stayed there. Now rising in the morning, the servant said, "Send me away, so that I may go to my master."

**24:55** Her brothers and mother answered, "Let the young woman stay with us at least ten days, and afterward she will set out."

**24:56** "Do not detain me," he said, "because the Lord has directed my way; send me away so that I may go to my master."

**24:57** They said, "Let us call the young woman and ask her own will."

**24:58** And when she had come after being called, they inquired, "Do you want to go with this man?" She said, "I will go."

**24:59** Therefore they sent her away, and her nurse, and Abraham's servant, and his companions,

**24:60** invoking prosperity upon their sister and saying, "You are our sister; may you grow into a thousand thousands, and may your offspring possess the gates of their enemies."

**24:61** Therefore Rebekah and her young women, having mounted the camels, followed the man, who was hurriedly returning to his master.

**24:62** At that time Isaac was walking along the way that leads to the well whose name is Of the Living and Seeing One, for he lived in the southern land;

**24:63** and he had gone out to meditate in the field as the day was already declining; and when he had lifted up his eyes, he saw camels coming in the distance.

**24:64** Rebekah also, having caught sight of Isaac, dismounted from the camel,

**24:65** and said to the servant, "Who is that man who is coming through the field to meet us?" He said to her, "That is my master." Then she, quickly taking her cloak, covered herself.

**24:66** Now the servant recounted to Isaac everything that he had done.

**24:67** And he brought her into the tent of Sarah his mother and took her as his wife; and he loved her so greatly that he eased the grief that had arisen from the death of his mother.

## Translator Notes

- **24:22** — The Vulgate explicitly reads plural golden earrings weighing two shekels ("inaures aureas, appendentes siclos duos"; cf. v. 30), whereas the Hebrew and Septuagint describe a single gold ring weighing half a shekel.
- **24:27** — The Vulgate specifies that the Lord led the servant "by a straight path" ("recto itinere") into the house of his master's singular "brother" ("fratris"), where the Hebrew reads "in the way" and plural "brothers."
- **24:32** — The Clementine Vulgate distinctly reads "to wash the feet of the camels and of the men" ("ad lavandos pedes camelorum, et virorum"), whereas the Hebrew and Septuagint read "to wash his feet and the feet of the men."
- **24:47** — The Vulgate renders this clause as "I hung the earrings to adorn her face" ("Suspendi itaque inaures ad ornandam faciem ejus"), reconciling plural "earrings" with the Hebrew wording "I put the ring on her nose."
- **24:53** — Here and in v. 55 the Vulgate reads plural "brothers" ("fratribus" / "fratres"), whereas the Hebrew reads singular "her brother."
- **24:62** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew place names Beer-lahai-roi ("the well whose name is Of the Living and Seeing One," "Viventis et videntis") and the Negev ("the southern land," "in terra australi") rather than transliterating them.
