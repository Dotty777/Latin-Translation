## Text

**47:1** So Joseph went in and reported to Pharaoh, saying, "My father and brothers, their sheep and herds, and all that they possess have come from the land of Canaan; and look, they are staying in the land of Goshen."

**47:2** He also set five men, the last of his brothers, before the king,

**47:3** whom he asked, "What occupation do you have?" They answered, "Your servants are shepherds of sheep, both we and our fathers.

**47:4** We have come to sojourn in your land, since there is no grass for the flocks of your servants, as the famine grows severe in the land of Canaan; and we ask that you command us, your servants, to be in the land of Goshen."

**47:5** Therefore the king said to Joseph, "Your father and your brothers have come to you.

**47:6** The land of Egypt is before you; have them dwell in the best place, and give them the land of Goshen. And if you know that there are industrious men among them, appoint them masters of my livestock."

**47:7** After these things, Joseph brought his father in to the king and set him before him; and after blessing him,

**47:8** and being asked by him, "How many are the days of the years of your life?"

**47:9** he answered, "The days of my sojourning are one hundred and thirty years, few and evil, and they have not reached the days of my fathers in which they sojourned."

**47:10** And after blessing the king, he went outside.

**47:11** Now Joseph gave his father and his brothers a possession in Egypt in the best place of the land, Rameses, as Pharaoh had commanded.

**47:12** And he nourished them and all the house of his father, supplying provisions to each one.

**47:13** For bread was lacking in the whole world, and famine had oppressed the land, especially that of Egypt and Canaan.

**47:14** From them he gathered all the money for the sale of grain, and brought it into the king's treasury.

**47:15** And when the buyers' money to pay had run out, all Egypt came to Joseph, saying, "Give us bread; why are we dying before you now that money has run out?"

**47:16** To them he answered, "Bring your livestock, and I will give you food for them, if you do not have the price."

**47:17** When they had brought them, he gave them nourishment in exchange for horses, sheep, oxen, and donkeys; and he sustained them that year through the exchange of livestock.

**47:18** And they came in the second year and said to him, "We will not hide from our lord that now that money has run out, the livestock have also run out; nor is it hidden from you that apart from our bodies and our land we have nothing.

**47:19** Why then shall we die while you look on? Both we and our land will be yours; buy us into royal servitude and supply seed, so that the land may not be reduced to desolation as the tiller perishes."

**47:20** Therefore Joseph bought all the land of Egypt, as each one sold his possessions because of the severity of the famine. And he subjected it to Pharaoh,

**47:21** and all its peoples, from the farthest borders of Egypt to its outermost boundaries,

**47:22** except the land of the priests, which had been given to them by the king, to whom fixed provisions were also supplied from the public granaries; and for that reason they were not forced to sell their possessions.

**47:23** Therefore Joseph said to the peoples, "Look, as you see, Pharaoh possesses both you and your land; take seed and sow the fields,

**47:24** so that you may have crops. You will give a fifth part to the king; the remaining four I leave to you for sowing, and for food for your households and your children."

**47:25** And they answered, "Our preservation is in your hand; only let our lord regard us, and we will gladly serve the king."

**47:26** From that time up to the present day, throughout the entire land of Egypt a fifth part is paid to the kings, and it became as it were a law, apart from the priestly land, which was free from this condition.

**47:27** So Israel dwelt in Egypt, that is, in the land of Goshen, and he possessed it; and he increased and multiplied exceedingly.

**47:28** And he lived in it seventeen years; and all the days of his life came to one hundred and forty-seven years.

**47:29** And when he saw that the day of his death was approaching, he called his son Joseph and said to him, "If I have found favor in your sight, place your hand under my thigh, and you will show mercy and truth to me, that you not bury me in Egypt,

**47:30** but that I sleep with my fathers, and that you carry me away from this land and lay me in the tomb of my ancestors." To him Joseph answered, "I will do what you have commanded."

**47:31** And he said, "Swear to me, then." As he swore, Israel worshiped God, having turned toward the head of the bed.

## Translator Notes

- **47:2** — Latin extremos ("the last" or "outermost") renders Hebrew mi-qetzeh ("from among" or "from the whole number") literally.
- **47:13** — Latin In toto enim orbe ("in the whole world") broadens Hebrew "in all the land."
- **47:21** — Latin Subjecitque eam Pharaoni, et cunctos populos ejus ("he subjected it to Pharaoh, and all its peoples") agrees with the Septuagint and Samaritan Pentateuch ("reduced to servitude") rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("removed them to the cities").
- **47:31** — Latin adds Deum ("God") and conversus ("having turned"), and reads lectuli caput ("the head of the bed") with the Hebrew vocalization rather than "the top of his staff" as in the Septuagint and Hebrews 11:21.
