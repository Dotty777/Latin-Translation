## Text

**36:1** Now these are the generations of Esau; he is Edom.

**36:2** Esau took wives from the daughters of Canaan: Adah, the daughter of Elon the Hittite, and Oholibamah, the daughter of Anah, the daughter of Zibeon the Hivite;

**36:3** also Basemath, the daughter of Ishmael, the sister of Nebaioth.

**36:4** Now Adah bore Eliphaz; Basemath gave birth to Reuel;

**36:5** Oholibamah brought forth Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. These are the sons of Esau who were born to him in the land of Canaan.

**36:6** Then Esau took his wives, his sons, and his daughters, and every soul of his household, and his property, and his livestock, and everything that he was able to possess in the land of Canaan, and he went away into another region and withdrew from his brother Jacob.

**36:7** For they were very rich, and they could not dwell together; nor did the land of their sojourning sustain them because of the multitude of their flocks.

**36:8** And Esau dwelt in Mount Seir; he is Edom.

**36:9** These are the generations of Esau, the father of Edom, in Mount Seir,

**36:10** and these are the names of his sons: Eliphaz, the son of Adah, the wife of Esau; also Reuel, the son of Basemath, his wife.

**36:11** And the sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz.

**36:12** Now Timna was a concubine of Eliphaz, the son of Esau, and she bore Amalek to him. These are the sons of Adah, the wife of Esau.

**36:13** Now the sons of Reuel were Nahath and Zerah, Shammah and Mizzah; these are the sons of Basemath, the wife of Esau.

**36:14** These also were the sons of Oholibamah, the daughter of Anah, the daughter of Zibeon, the wife of Esau, whom she bore to him: Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.

**36:15** These are the chiefs of the sons of Esau. The sons of Eliphaz, the firstborn of Esau: Chief Teman, Chief Omar, Chief Zepho, Chief Kenaz,

**36:16** Chief Korah, Chief Gatam, Chief Amalek. These are the sons of Eliphaz in the land of Edom, and these are the sons of Adah.

**36:17** These also are the sons of Reuel, the son of Esau: Chief Nahath, Chief Zerah, Chief Shammah, Chief Mizzah. These are the chiefs of Reuel in the land of Edom; these are the sons of Basemath, the wife of Esau.

**36:18** Now these are the sons of Oholibamah, the wife of Esau: Chief Jeush, Chief Jalam, Chief Korah. These are the chiefs of Oholibamah, the daughter of Anah, the wife of Esau.

**36:19** These are the sons of Esau, and these are their chiefs; he is Edom.

**36:20** These are the sons of Seir the Horite, the inhabitants of the land: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah,

**36:21** Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. These are the Horite chiefs, the sons of Seir in the land of Edom.

**36:22** Now the sons of Lotan were Hori and Heman; and the sister of Lotan was Timna.

**36:23** And these are the sons of Shobal: Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam.

**36:24** And these are the sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Anah. This is Anah, who found warm waters in the wilderness while pasturing the donkeys of Zibeon his father;

**36:25** and he had a son, Dishon, and a daughter, Oholibamah.

**36:26** And these are the sons of Dishon: Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.

**36:27** These also are the sons of Ezer: Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan.

**36:28** Now Dishan had sons: Uz and Aram.

**36:29** These are the chiefs of the Horites: Chief Lotan, Chief Shobal, Chief Zibeon, Chief Anah,

**36:30** Chief Dishon, Chief Ezer, Chief Dishan. These are the chiefs of the Horites who ruled in the land of Seir.

**36:31** Now the kings who reigned in the land of Edom before the sons of Israel had a king were these:

**36:32** Bela the son of Beor, and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

**36:33** Then Bela died, and Jobab the son of Zerah, from Bozrah, reigned in his place.

**36:34** And when Jobab had died, Husham from the land of the Temanites reigned in his place.

**36:35** When he also had died, Hadad the son of Bedad, who struck down Midian in the region of Moab, reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Avith.

**36:36** And when Hadad had died, Samlah from Masrekah reigned in his place.

**36:37** When he also had died, Shaul from the river Rehoboth reigned in his place.

**36:38** And when he also had passed away, Baal-hanan the son of Achbor succeeded to the kingdom.

**36:39** When he also had died, Hadar reigned in his place, and the name of his city was Pau; and his wife was called Mehetabel, the daughter of Matred, the daughter of Mezahab.

**36:40** Therefore these are the names of the chiefs of Esau in their clans, places, and designations: Chief Timna, Chief Alvah, Chief Jetheth,

**36:41** Chief Oholibamah, Chief Elah, Chief Pinon,

**36:42** Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mibzar,

**36:43** Chief Magdiel, Chief Iram. These are the chiefs of Edom, dwelling in the land of their dominion; he is Esau, the father of the Edomites.

## Translator Notes

- **36:2** — The Latin uses the feminine genitive "filiae" ("daughter") in apposition to Anah here and in 36:14 (and similarly with Matred in 36:39), even though 36:24-25 identifies Anah as a son of Zibeon.
- **36:24** — The Vulgate renders the rare Hebrew term "ha-yemim" as "aquas calidas" ("warm waters", i.e., thermal hot springs), and continues in 36:25 with "and he had a son, Dishon, and a daughter, Oholibamah".
- **36:37** — The Vulgate reads "de fluvio Rohoboth" ("from the river Rehoboth"), whereas the Hebrew has "from Rehoboth by the River"; similarly in 36:39 the Vulgate reads "Adad" (Hadad, matching 1 Chronicles 1:50) rather than the Hebrew Genesis "Hadar".
- **36:39** — The Clementine Vulgate reads 'Adar' ('Hadar', matching 25:15 and some Hebrew manuscripts) where other traditions read 'Hadad.'
- **36:40** — In this section, 36:16 reads "sons of Eliphaz" where the Hebrew has "chiefs of Eliphaz", and 36:43 reads "imperii sui" ("of their dominion") rather than "of their possession".
