## Text

**18:1** Now the Lord appeared to him in the valley of Mamre as he was sitting at the doorway of his tent in the very heat of the day.

**18:2** And when he had lifted up his eyes, three men appeared to him standing near him; and when he saw them, he ran to meet them from the doorway of the tent and bowed down on the ground.

**18:3** And he said, "Lord, if I have found favor in your eyes, do not pass by your servant;

**18:4** but I will bring a little water; and wash your feet, and rest under the tree.

**18:5** I will set out a morsel of bread, and strengthen your heart; afterward you will pass on, for this is why you have turned aside to your servant." And they said, "Do as you have spoken."

**18:6** Abraham hurried into the tent to Sarah and said to her, "Be quick, mix three measures of fine flour, and make loaves baked under the ashes."

**18:7** And he himself ran to the herd, and took from there a very tender and choice calf, and gave it to a young servant, who hurried and cooked it.

**18:8** He also took butter and milk, and the calf that he had cooked, and set them before them; and he himself stood beside them under the tree.

**18:9** And when they had eaten, they said to him, "Where is Sarah your wife?" He answered, "Look, she is in the tent."

**18:10** And He said to him, "Returning, I will come to you at this time, with life as companion, and Sarah your wife will have a son." Upon hearing this, Sarah laughed behind the doorway of the tent.

**18:11** Now both were old, and of advanced age, and the way of women had ceased to occur for Sarah.

**18:12** And she laughed secretly, saying, "After I have grown old, and my lord is an old man, shall I give myself to pleasure?"

**18:13** Now the Lord said to Abraham, "Why did Sarah laugh, saying, 'Am I really going to give birth as an old woman?'

**18:14** Is anything difficult for God? According to what was appointed, I will return to you at this same time, with life as companion, and Sarah will have a son."

**18:15** Sarah denied it, saying, "I did not laugh," terrified with fear. But the Lord said, "It is not so; rather, you did laugh."

**18:16** Then when the men had risen from there, they directed their eyes toward Sodom; and Abraham walked along with them, escorting them.

**18:17** And the Lord said, "Shall I be able to hide from Abraham the things that I am about to do,

**18:18** since he is to become a great and most mighty nation, and all the nations of the earth are to be blessed in him?

**18:19** For I know that he will command his sons and his household after him to keep the way of the Lord and to do justice and righteousness, so that the Lord may bring about for Abraham's sake all the things that He has spoken to him."

**18:20** Therefore the Lord said, "The outcry of Sodom and Gomorrah has been multiplied, and their sin has become exceedingly grave.

**18:21** I will go down and see whether they have fulfilled in deed the outcry that has come to Me, or whether it is not so, that I may know."

**18:22** And they turned from there and went away to Sodom; but Abraham was still standing before the Lord.

**18:23** And drawing near, he said, "Will You really destroy the righteous with the wicked?

**18:24** If there are fifty righteous people in the city, will they perish together? And will You not spare that place for the sake of the fifty righteous people, if they are in it?

**18:25** Far be it from You to do this thing, and kill the righteous with the wicked, so that the righteous becomes like the wicked; this is not like You: You who judge all the earth will by no means execute this judgment."

**18:26** And the Lord said to him, "If I find in Sodom fifty righteous people in the midst of the city, I will pardon the whole place for their sake."

**18:27** Answering, Abraham said, "Since I have once begun, I will speak to my Lord, though I am dust and ashes.

**18:28** What if there are five fewer than fifty righteous people? Will You wipe out the entire city because of forty-five?" And He said, "I will not wipe it out if I find forty-five there."

**18:29** And again he spoke to Him, "But if forty are found there, what will You do?" He said, "I will not strike it for the sake of forty."

**18:30** "Please do not be indignant, Lord," he said, "if I speak: what if thirty are found there?" He answered, "I will not do it if I find thirty there."

**18:31** "Since I have once begun," he said, "I will speak to my Lord: what if twenty are found there?" He said, "I will not kill them for the sake of twenty."

**18:32** "I implore You," he said, "do not be angry, Lord, if I speak just once more: what if ten are found there?" He said, "I will not wipe it out for the sake of ten."

**18:33** The Lord departed after He ceased speaking to Abraham, and he returned to his place.

## Translator Notes

- **18:1** — The Vulgate reads "in convalle Mambre" ("in the valley of Mamre"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to the oaks or terebinths of Mamre.
- **18:10** — Latin "vita comite" (literally "with life as companion," repeated in 18:14) is a classical Latin ablative absolute rendering the Hebrew idiom for the return of the season next year, meaning if life continues.
- **18:19** — Latin "propter Abraham" ("for Abraham's sake") differs slightly from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("upon Abraham").
- **18:25** — The Vulgate renders the closing clause as a negative statement with "nequaquam" ("You who judge all the earth will by no means execute this judgment"), unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text's rhetorical question ("Shall not the Judge of all the earth do justice?").
- **18:28** — The Sixto-Clementine Vulgate distinctively reads 'propter quadraginta quinque' ('because of forty-five') where the Hebrew text and Codex Amiatinus read 'because of five.'
