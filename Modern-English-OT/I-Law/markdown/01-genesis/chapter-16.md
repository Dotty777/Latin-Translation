## Text

**16:1** Now Sarai, the wife of Abram, had not borne children; but having an Egyptian maidservant named Hagar,

**16:2** she said to her husband, "Look, the Lord has closed me off so that I should not give birth. Go in to my maidservant; perhaps at least from her I may obtain children." And when he agreed to her pleading,

**16:3** she took Hagar the Egyptian, her maidservant, ten years after they had begun to live in the land of Canaan, and gave her to her husband as a wife.

**16:4** And he went in to her. But she, seeing that she had conceived, despised her mistress.

**16:5** And Sarai said to Abram, "You are acting unjustly against me! I gave my maidservant into your embrace, and she, seeing that she has conceived, holds me in contempt; may the Lord judge between me and you."

**16:6** Abram answered her and said, "Look, your maidservant is in your hand; treat her as you please." So when Sarai afflicted her, she took flight.

**16:7** And when the angel of the Lord found her beside a spring of water in the wilderness, which is on the way to Shur in the desert,

**16:8** he said to her, "Hagar, maidservant of Sarai, where are you coming from? And where are you going?" She answered, "I am fleeing from the presence of Sarai my mistress."

**16:9** And the angel of the Lord said to her, "Return to your mistress, and humble yourself under her hand."

**16:10** And again he said, "Multiplying, I will multiply your seed, and it will not be counted because of the multitude."

**16:11** And further he said, "Look, you have conceived and will bear a son, and you will call his name Ishmael, because the Lord has heard your affliction.

**16:12** This one will be a wild man; his hand will be against all, and the hand of all against him, and opposite all his brothers he will pitch tents."

**16:13** Then she called the name of the Lord who was speaking to her, "You are the God who saw me." For she said, "Truly here I have seen the back of Him who sees me."

**16:14** Therefore she called that well the Well of Him who lives and sees me. It is between Kadesh and Bered.

**16:15** And Hagar bore Abram a son, and he called his name Ishmael.

**16:16** Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore Ishmael to him.

## Translator Notes

- **16:2** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew idiom "be built up from her" as "obtain children from her" (Latin ex illa suscipiam filios).
- **16:7** — The Clementine Vulgate combines both 'on the way to Shur' and 'in the desert' (in via Sur in deserto).
- **16:12** — The Vulgate simplifies Hebrew "a wild donkey of a man" to "a wild man" (Latin ferus homo), and renders Hebrew "he will dwell" concretely as "he will pitch tents" (Latin figet tabernacula).
- **16:13** — Where the Hebrew phrasing is notoriously elliptical and often taken as a question, the Vulgate gives a declarative exclamation echoing Exodus 33:23: "Truly here I have seen the back of Him who sees me" (Latin Profecto hic vidi posteriora videntis me).
