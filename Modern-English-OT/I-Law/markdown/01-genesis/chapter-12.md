## Text

**12:1** Now the Lord said to Abram, "Go out from your land, and from your relatives, and from your father's house, into the land that I will show you.

**12:2** And I will make you into a great nation, and I will bless you, and I will magnify your name, and you will be blessed.

**12:3** I will bless those who bless you, and I will curse those who curse you, and in you all the families of the earth will be blessed."

**12:4** So Abram went out just as the Lord had commanded him, and Lot went with him. Abram was seventy-five years old when he went out from Haran.

**12:5** And he took Sarai his wife, and Lot his brother's son, and all the wealth that they possessed, and the souls whom they had made in Haran; and they went out to go into the land of Canaan. And when they had come into it,

**12:6** Abram passed through the land as far as the place of Shechem, as far as the illustrious valley. Now the Canaanite was then in the land.

**12:7** And the Lord appeared to Abram and said to him, "To your seed I will give this land." And he built there an altar to the Lord, who had appeared to him.

**12:8** And moving on from there to the mountain that was on the east of Bethel, he pitched his tent there, having Bethel on the west and Ai on the east; he also built there an altar to the Lord and called upon His name.

**12:9** And Abram went on, traveling and advancing further toward the south.

**12:10** Now a famine arose in the land, and Abram went down into Egypt to sojourn there, for the famine had prevailed in the land.

**12:11** And when he was near to entering Egypt, he said to Sarai his wife, "I know that you are a beautiful woman,

**12:12** and that when the Egyptians see you, they will say, 'She is his wife,' and they will kill me, and spare you.

**12:13** Say therefore, I beg you, that you are my sister, so that it may go well with me because of you, and that my soul may live for your sake."

**12:14** So when Abram had entered Egypt, the Egyptians saw the woman, that she was exceedingly beautiful.

**12:15** And the princes reported to Pharaoh and praised her before him, and the woman was taken into Pharaoh's house.

**12:16** And they treated Abram well because of her, and he had sheep and oxen and male donkeys, and male servants and female servants, and female donkeys and camels.

**12:17** But the Lord scourged Pharaoh and his house with very great plagues because of Sarai, Abram's wife.

**12:18** And Pharaoh called Abram and said to him, "What is it that you have done to me? Why did you not disclose that she was your wife?

**12:19** For what reason did you say she was your sister, so that I might take her for myself as a wife? Now therefore, here is your wife; take her and go."

**12:20** And Pharaoh commanded men concerning Abram, and they escorted him, and his wife, and all that he had.

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — Latin erisque benedictus ("and you will be blessed") uses a future passive construction, differing from the Hebrew imperative "and be a blessing."
- **12:5** — Latin animas quas fecerant ("the souls whom they had made") literally preserves a Hebrew idiom often understood as either servants acquired or converts won in Haran.
- **12:6** — Latin convallem illustrem ("the illustrious valley") is a distinctly Vulgate rendering of the Hebrew elon Moreh ("the oak/terebinth of Moreh").
- **12:16** — Latin uses the plural verb bene usi sunt ("they treated ... well"), whereas the Hebrew has a singular verb referring specifically to Pharaoh.
