## Text

**7:1** And the Lord said to him, "Enter the ark, you and all your household; for I have seen you to be righteous before Me in this generation.

**7:2** From all clean animals take seven and seven, male and female; but from the animals that are not clean, two and two, male and female.

**7:3** And also from the flying creatures of the sky, seven and seven, male and female, so that seed may be preserved on the face of the whole earth.

**7:4** For yet after seven days I will send rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights; and I will wipe out every substance that I have made from the surface of the earth."

**7:5** Therefore Noah did everything that the Lord had commanded him.

**7:6** And he was six hundred years old when the waters of the flood overflowed upon the earth.

**7:7** And Noah and his sons, his wife, and the wives of his sons entered the ark with him because of the waters of the flood.

**7:8** Also of the clean and unclean animals, and of the birds, and of everything that moves on the earth,

**7:9** two and two entered to Noah into the ark, male and female, just as the Lord had commanded Noah.

**7:10** And when the seven days had passed, the waters of the flood overflowed upon the earth.

**7:11** In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, all the springs of the great deep burst open, and the floodgates of heaven were opened;

**7:12** and rain came upon the earth for forty days and forty nights.

**7:13** On that very day Noah, and Shem, Ham, and Japheth his sons, his wife, and the three wives of his sons with them entered into the ark:

**7:14** they themselves, and every animal according to its kind, and all livestock in their kind, and everything that moves on the earth in its kind, and every flying creature according to its kind, all birds, and all winged creatures,

**7:15** entered to Noah into the ark, two by two of all flesh in which there was the spirit of life.

**7:16** And those that entered went in male and female of all flesh, just as God had commanded him; and the Lord shut him in from the outside.

**7:17** And the flood continued for forty days on the earth; and the waters multiplied, and they lifted the ark high above the earth.

**7:18** They overflowed violently and filled everything on the surface of the earth; meanwhile, the ark was carried on the waters.

**7:19** And the waters prevailed exceedingly on the earth, and all the high mountains under the whole heaven were covered.

**7:20** The water was fifteen cubits higher above the mountains that it had covered.

**7:21** And all flesh that moved on the earth was consumed: of birds, of animals, of beasts, and of all crawling creatures that crawl on the earth; all men,

**7:22** and all things in which there is the breath of life on the earth, died.

**7:23** And He wiped out every substance that was on the earth, from man to livestock, both the crawling creature and the birds of the sky; and they were wiped out from the earth. But Noah alone remained, and those who were with him in the ark.

**7:24** And the waters prevailed over the lands for one hundred fifty days.

## Translator Notes

- **7:4** — Latin omnem substantiam ("every substance") renders Hebrew kol ha-yekum ("every existing thing"), as also in 7:23.
- **7:9** — Vulgate reads "the Lord" (Dominus) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "God" (Elohim), as also in 6:22.
- **7:22** — Vulgate reads in terra ("on the earth") and omits "in its nostrils" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text specifies "in whose nostrils was the breath of the spirit of life, of all that was on dry land."
