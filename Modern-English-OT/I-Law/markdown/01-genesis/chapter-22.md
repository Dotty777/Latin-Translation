## Text

**22:1** After these things had taken place, God tested Abraham and said to him, "Abraham." He answered, "Here I am."

**22:2** He said to him, "Take your only-begotten son, whom you love, Isaac, and go into the land of vision, and offer him there as a burnt offering on one of the mountains that I will show you."

**22:3** So Abraham, rising in the night, saddled his donkey, taking with him two young men and Isaac his son; and when he had cut wood for the burnt offering, he went to the place that God had commanded him.

**22:4** Now on the third day, lifting up his eyes, he saw the place far off,

**22:5** and he said to his young men, "Wait here with the donkey; I and the boy will hurry all the way over there, and after we have worshiped, we will return to you."

**22:6** He also took the wood of the burnt offering and placed it on Isaac his son, while he himself carried the fire and the sword in his hands. And as the two went on together,

**22:7** Isaac said to his father, "My father." And he answered, "What do you want, son?" "Look," he said, "the fire and the wood; where is the victim of the burnt offering?"

**22:8** Abraham said, "God will provide for Himself the victim of the burnt offering, my son." So they went on together.

**22:9** And they came to the place that God had shown him, where he built an altar and arranged the wood on top; and when he had bound Isaac his son, he laid him on the altar upon the pile of wood.

**22:10** And he stretched out his hand and seized the sword to sacrifice his son.

**22:11** And look, the angel of the Lord called out from heaven, saying, "Abraham, Abraham!" And he answered, "Here I am."

**22:12** And he said to him, "Do not stretch out your hand against the boy, nor do anything to him; now I know that you fear the Lord and have not spared your only-begotten son because of Me."

**22:13** Abraham lifted up his eyes and saw behind his back a ram caught by its horns among the briers; and taking it, he offered it as a burnt offering in place of his son.

**22:14** And he called the name of that place "The Lord sees." For this reason it is said to this day, "On the mountain the Lord will see."

**22:15** Now the angel of the Lord called to Abraham a second time from heaven, saying,

**22:16** "By Myself I have sworn, says the Lord: because you have done this thing and have not spared your only-begotten son,

**22:17** I will bless you and multiply your offspring like the stars of heaven and like the sand that is on the seashore; your offspring will possess the gates of their enemies,

**22:18** and in your offspring all the nations of the earth will be blessed, because you have obeyed My voice."

**22:19** Abraham returned to his young men, and they went to Beersheba together, and he lived there.

**22:20** So after these things had taken place, it was reported to Abraham that Milcah also had borne sons to Nahor his brother:

**22:21** Uz the firstborn, and Buz his brother, Kemuel the father of the Syrians,

**22:22** and Chesed and Hazo, also Pildash and Jidlaph,

**22:23** and Bethuel, from whom Rebekah was born. Milcah bore these eight to Nahor, Abraham's brother.

**22:24** And his concubine, named Reumah, gave birth to Tebah, and Gaham, and Tahash, and Maacah.

## Translator Notes

- **22:2** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew "land of Moriah" interpretively as terram visionis ("the land of vision"), connecting it to "The Lord sees" in verse 14; it also uses unigenitum ("only-begotten," repeated in verses 12 and 16).
- **22:6** — Here and in verse 10 the Vulgate translates the Hebrew slaughter-knife as gladium ("sword"); in verses 7 and 8 it similarly renders Hebrew "lamb" as victima ("victim").
- **22:13** — Latin post tergum ("behind his back") reflects reading the Hebrew text as "behind" rather than "one/a," and vepres renders the thicket specifically as "briers."
- **22:14** — Latin Dominus videt ("The Lord sees") and In monte Dominus videbit ("On the mountain the Lord will see") render the place-name and proverb literally with "see," echoing "the land of vision" in verse 2 rather than "provide."
- **22:21** — Latin patrem Syrorum ("the father of the Syrians") renders the Hebrew name Aram with the classical ethnic designation "Syrians," following the Septuagint.
