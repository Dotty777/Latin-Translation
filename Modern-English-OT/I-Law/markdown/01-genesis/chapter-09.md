## Text

**9:1** Then God blessed Noah and his sons. And He said to them, "Increase and multiply, and fill the earth.

**9:2** And let terror and dread of you be upon all the animals of the earth and upon all the birds of the sky, together with everything that moves upon the earth; all the fish of the sea have been delivered into your hand.

**9:3** And everything that moves and lives will be food for you; just like green plants, I have delivered all things to you.

**9:4** Except that you must not eat flesh with blood.

**9:5** For I will require the blood of your lives from the hand of all beasts; and from the hand of man, from the hand of a man and his brother, I will require the life of man.

**9:6** Whoever sheds human blood, his blood will be shed, for in the image of God man was made.

**9:7** As for you, increase and multiply, and go forth upon the earth, and fill it."

**9:8** God also said these things to Noah and to his sons with him:

**9:9** "Look, I will establish My covenant with you, and with your offspring after you,

**9:10** and to every living soul that is with you, both among the birds and among the livestock and all the cattle of the earth that came out of the ark, and all the wild beasts of the earth.

**9:11** I will establish My covenant with you, and never again will all flesh be killed by the waters of a flood, nor will there be from now on a flood devastating the earth."

**9:12** And God said, "This is the sign of the covenant that I am giving between Me and you, and to every living soul that is with you, for everlasting generations:

**9:13** I will place My bow in the clouds, and it will be the sign of the covenant between Me and the earth.

**9:14** And when I cover the sky with clouds, My bow will appear in the clouds,

**9:15** and I will remember My covenant with you, and with every living soul that animates flesh; and there will no longer be waters of a flood to wipe out all flesh.

**9:16** And the bow will be in the clouds, and I will see it, and I will remember the everlasting covenant that has been made between God and every living soul of all flesh that is upon the earth."

**9:17** And God said to Noah, "This will be the sign of the covenant that I have established between Me and all flesh upon the earth."

**9:18** So the sons of Noah who came out of the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth; and Ham himself is the father of Canaan.

**9:19** These three are the sons of Noah, and from them the entire human race was dispersed over all the earth.

**9:20** And Noah, a farmer, began to cultivate the ground, and he planted a vineyard.

**9:21** And drinking wine, he became drunk, and was uncovered in his tent.

**9:22** When Ham, the father of Canaan, saw this, namely that his father's private parts were uncovered, he reported it to his two brothers outside.

**9:23** But Shem and Japheth placed a cloak over their shoulders, and walking backward, they covered their father's nakedness; and their faces were turned away, and they did not see their father's private parts.

**9:24** Now waking from the wine, when Noah learned what his younger son had done to him,

**9:25** he said, "Cursed be Canaan; he will be a servant of servants to his brothers."

**9:26** And he said, "Blessed be the Lord God of Shem; may Canaan be his servant.

**9:27** May God expand Japheth, and may he dwell in the tents of Shem, and may Canaan be his servant."

**9:28** Now Noah lived three hundred fifty years after the flood.

**9:29** And all his days were completed, nine hundred fifty years, and he died.

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — The Vulgate omits the Hebrew reference to the flesh's 'life' (or 'soul'), stating simply that flesh with blood must not be eaten.
- **9:6** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, the Vulgate omits 'by man' before 'his blood will be shed' and renders the final clause passively ('man was made in the image of God').
- **9:7** — Latin 'ingredimini super terram' ('go forth upon the earth') differs from the Hebrew, which reads 'swarm (or teem) upon the earth.'
- **9:15** — Latin 'quae carnem vegetat' ('that animates flesh') is a distinctive Vulgate rendering for the Hebrew phrase 'of all flesh.'
- **9:22** — In verses 22-23, the Vulgate renders the single Hebrew word for 'nakedness' with three explicit Latin modesty terms ('verenda,' 'verecunda,' and 'virilia').
