## Text

**17:1** Now after he had begun to be ninety-nine years old, the Lord appeared to him and said to him, "I am God Almighty; walk before Me, and be perfect.

**17:2** And I will set My covenant between Me and you, and I will multiply you very greatly."

**17:3** Abram fell prostrate on his face.

**17:4** And God said to him, "I am, and My covenant is with you, and you will be the father of many nations.

**17:5** And your name will no longer be called Abram, but you will be named Abraham, because I have appointed you the father of many nations.

**17:6** And I will make you grow exceedingly, and I will set you among the nations, and kings will come forth from you.

**17:7** And I will establish My covenant between Me and you, and between your offspring after you throughout their generations, by an everlasting covenant, so that I may be your God and the God of your offspring after you.

**17:8** And I will give to you and to your offspring the land of your sojourning, all the land of Canaan, for an eternal possession, and I will be their God."

**17:9** God said again to Abraham, "And you therefore shall keep My covenant, and your offspring after you throughout their generations.

**17:10** This is the covenant that you shall observe between Me and you, and your offspring after you: every male among you shall be circumcised,

**17:11** and you shall circumcise the flesh of your foreskin, so that it may be a sign of the covenant between Me and you.

**17:12** An infant eight days old shall be circumcised among you, every male throughout your generations; both the homeborn and the purchased shall be circumcised, and whoever is not of your lineage,

**17:13** and My covenant shall be in your flesh for an eternal covenant.

**17:14** The male, the flesh of whose foreskin has not been circumcised, that soul shall be wiped out from his people, because he has made My covenant void."

**17:15** God also said to Abraham, "As for your wife Sarai, you shall not call her Sarai, but Sarah.

**17:16** And I will bless her, and from her I will give you a son whom I am going to bless; and he will become nations, and kings of peoples will arise from him."

**17:17** Abraham fell on his face and laughed, saying in his heart, "Do you think a son will be born to a man one hundred years old? And will Sarah, at ninety years old, give birth?"

**17:18** And he said to God, "If only Ishmael might live before You!"

**17:19** And God said to Abraham, "Sarah your wife will bear you a son, and you shall call his name Isaac; and I will establish My covenant for him as an everlasting covenant, and for his offspring after him.

**17:20** Regarding Ishmael also I have heard you: see, I will bless him, and increase him, and multiply him greatly; he will father twelve leaders, and I will make him into a great nation.

**17:21** But My covenant I will establish with Isaac, whom Sarah will bear to you at this time in the next year."

**17:22** And when the speech of Him who was speaking with him had ended, God ascended from Abraham.

**17:23** Then Abraham took his son Ishmael, and all the homeborn of his household, and all whom he had purchased, all the males among all the men of his household, and circumcised the flesh of their foreskin immediately on that very day, just as God had commanded him.

**17:24** He was ninety-nine years old when he circumcised the flesh of his foreskin.

**17:25** And his son Ishmael had completed thirteen years at the time of his circumcision.

**17:26** On that same day Abraham was circumcised, and his son Ishmael;

**17:27** and all the men of his household, both the homeborn and the purchased and the foreigners, were circumcised together.

## Translator Notes

- **17:4** — Latin "Ego sum" ("I am") renders the emphatic Hebrew pronoun phrase ("As for Me, look").
- **17:6** — Latin "in gentibus" (ablative, "among the nations") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("into nations").
- **17:16** — The Vulgate uses masculine pronouns ("cui ... eritque ... ex eo"), applying the second blessing and the descent of nations and kings to the promised son rather than to Sarah as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **17:24** — The Vulgate uses the active verb "circumcidit" ("when he circumcised the flesh of his foreskin"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses the passive ("when he was circumcised").
