## Text

**10:1** These are the generations of the sons of Noah: Shem, Ham, and Japheth; and sons were born to them after the flood.

**10:2** The sons of Japheth: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.

**10:3** And the sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.

**10:4** Now the sons of Javan: Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.

**10:5** From these the islands of the nations were divided in their regions, each one according to his own language and his families in their nations.

**10:6** And the sons of Ham: Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.

**10:7** The sons of Cush: Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca. The sons of Raamah: Sheba and Dedan.

**10:8** Now Cush fathered Nimrod; he himself began to be powerful on the earth,

**10:9** and he was a mighty hunter before the Lord. Because of this came the proverb, "Like Nimrod, a mighty hunter before the Lord."

**10:10** Now the beginning of his kingdom was Babylon, Erech, Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar.

**10:11** From that land Asshur went out, and he built Nineveh, and the streets of the city, and Calah,

**10:12** and also Resen, between Nineveh and Calah; this is the great city.

**10:13** Now Mizraim fathered Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, Naphtuhim,

**10:14** Pathrusim, and Casluhim, from whom the Philistines and Caphtorim came out.

**10:15** Now Canaan fathered Sidon, his firstborn, and the Hittite,

**10:16** the Jebusite, the Amorite, the Girgashite,

**10:17** the Hivite, the Arkite, the Sinite,

**10:18** the Arvadite, the Zemarite, and the Hamathite; and after this the peoples of the Canaanites were dispersed.

**10:19** And the borders of Canaan were set for those coming from Sidon toward Gerar as far as Gaza, until you enter Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, as far as Lasha.

**10:20** These are the sons of Ham, in their clans, languages, generations, lands, and nations.

**10:21** From Shem also children were born, the father of all the sons of Eber, the older brother of Japheth.

**10:22** The sons of Shem: Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, and Aram.

**10:23** The sons of Aram: Uz, Hul, Gether, and Mash.

**10:24** Now Arpachshad fathered Shelah, from whom Eber was born.

**10:25** And to Eber two sons were born: the name of the one was Peleg, because in his days the earth was divided, and the name of his brother was Joktan.

**10:26** Now Joktan fathered Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah,

**10:27** Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah,

**10:28** Ebal, Abimael, Sheba,

**10:29** Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab; all these were the sons of Joktan.

**10:30** And their dwelling place was from Mesha, for those going as far as Sephar, the eastern mountain.

**10:31** These are the sons of Shem, according to their clans, languages, and regions in their nations.

**10:32** These are the families of Noah according to their peoples and nations. From these the nations were divided on the earth after the flood.

## Translator Notes

- **10:9** — Where the Hebrew says 'therefore it is said,' the Vulgate explicitly calls the saying a proverb ('Ob hoc exivit proverbium').
- **10:11** — The Vulgate makes Asshur the subject who went out from Shinar (rather than Nimrod going into Assyria) and translates the Hebrew city name Rehoboth-Ir as a common noun phrase, 'the streets of the city' ('plateas civitatis').
- **10:15** — Where the Hebrew has the personal name Heth, the Vulgate uses the gentilic 'Hethaeum' ('the Hittite'), matching the tribal names that follow.
- **10:21** — The Vulgate's ablative phrasing ('fratre Japheth majore') resolves the ambiguity of the Hebrew syntax by identifying Shem, rather than Japheth, as the older brother.
- **10:28** — The Vulgate reads 'Ebal' (matching 1 Chronicles 1:22 and the Samaritan Pentateuch) where the Hebrew Masoretic Text of Genesis reads 'Obal.'
