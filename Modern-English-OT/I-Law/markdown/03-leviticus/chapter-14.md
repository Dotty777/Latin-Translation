## Text

**14:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**14:2** "This is the rite of a leper when he is to be cleansed. He will be brought to the priest,

**14:3** who, having gone out from the camp, when he finds that the leprosy has been cleansed,

**14:4** will command the one who is being purified to offer for himself two living sparrows that it is lawful to eat, and cedar wood, and scarlet, and hyssop.

**14:5** And he will order one of the sparrows to be sacrificed in a clay vessel over living waters;

**14:6** but he will dip the other living one, with the cedar wood, the scarlet, and the hyssop, in the blood of the sacrificed sparrow,

**14:7** with which he will sprinkle the one who is to be cleansed seven times, so that he may be lawfully purged; and he will release the living sparrow so that it may fly away into the field.

**14:8** And when the man has washed his garments, he will shave all the hair of his body and will wash himself with water; and having been purified, he will enter the camp, provided only that he remains outside his tent for seven days,

**14:9** and on the seventh day he will shave the hair of his head, and his beard and eyebrows, and the hair of his whole body. And when his garments and body have been washed again,

**14:10** on the eighth day he will take two unblemished lambs, and a year-old ewe without blemish, and three tenths of fine flour, which is mixed with oil, for a sacrifice, and separately a sextarius of oil.

**14:11** And when the priest purifying the man has set him and all these things before the Lord at the doorway of the tabernacle of testimony,

**14:12** he will take a lamb and offer it for trespass, and the sextarius of oil; and when all of them have been offered before the Lord,

**14:13** he will sacrifice the lamb where the victim for sin and the burnt offering are accustomed to be sacrificed, that is, in the holy place. For just as for sin, so also for trespass the victim belongs to the priest; it is most holy.

**14:14** And the priest, taking some of the blood of the victim that was sacrificed for trespass, will put it on the tip of the right ear of the one who is being cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand and the big toe of his right foot;

**14:15** and from the sextarius of oil he will pour some into his left hand,

**14:16** and he will dip his right finger in it and sprinkle before the Lord seven times.

**14:17** And what remains of the oil in his left hand he will pour on the tip of the right ear of the one who is being cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand and the big toe of his right foot, and on the blood that was poured out for trespass,

**14:18** and on his head.

**14:19** And he will pray for him before the Lord and will make the sacrifice for sin; then he will sacrifice the burnt offering,

**14:20** and will place it on the altar with its libations, and the man will be duly cleansed.

**14:21** But if he is poor and his hand cannot find the things that were spoken for trespass, he will take a lamb for an offering, so that the priest may pray for him, and a tenth part of fine flour mixed with oil for a sacrifice, and a sextarius of oil,

**14:22** and two turtledoves or two young pigeons, one of which is to be for sin, and the other for a burnt offering;

**14:23** and he will offer them on the eighth day of his purification to the priest, at the doorway of the tabernacle of testimony before the Lord.

**14:24** Receiving the lamb for trespass and the sextarius of oil, he will lift them up together;

**14:25** and when the lamb has been sacrificed, he will put some of its blood on the tip of the right ear of the one who is being cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand and the big toe of his right foot;

**14:26** and he will pour part of the oil into his left hand,

**14:27** and dipping a finger of his right hand in it, he will sprinkle seven times before the Lord;

**14:28** and he will touch the tip of the right ear of the one who is being cleansed, and the thumb of his right hand and the big toe of his right foot, in the place of the blood that was poured out for trespass;

**14:29** and the remaining part of the oil, which is in his left hand, he will pour on the head of the one who has been purified, so that he may appease the Lord for him;

**14:30** and he will offer a turtledove or a young pigeon,

**14:31** one for trespass, and the other for a burnt offering with its libations.

**14:32** This is the sacrifice of a leper who cannot have everything for his cleansing."

**14:33** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:

**14:34** "When you have entered the land of Canaan, which I will give to you as a possession, if there is a plague of leprosy in a house,

**14:35** the one whose house it is will go, reporting to the priest, and will say, 'It seems to me that something like a plague of leprosy is in my house.'

**14:36** Then he will command that they carry everything out of the house before he enters it and sees whether it is leprous, so that all the things that are in the house may not become unclean. And afterward he will enter to examine the leprosy of the house;

**14:37** and when he sees on its walls something like little hollows disfigured with paleness or redness, and lower than the rest of the surface,

**14:38** he will go out of the doorway of the house and will immediately close it for seven days.

**14:39** And having returned on the seventh day, he will examine it; if he finds that the leprosy has grown,

**14:40** he will order that the stones in which the leprosy is be torn out, and that they be thrown outside the city into an unclean place;

**14:41** and that the house itself be scraped on the inside all around, and that the dust of the scraping be scattered outside the city into an unclean place,

**14:42** and that other stones be put back in place of those that were taken away, and that the house be plastered with other clay.

**14:43** But if, after the stones have been torn out, and the dust has been scraped off, and other earth has been plastered on,

**14:44** the priest, having entered, sees that the leprosy has returned and that the walls are speckled with spots, it is a persistent leprosy, and the house is unclean;

**14:45** and they will immediately tear it down and throw its stones and timbers, and all the dust, outside the town into an unclean place.

**14:46** Whoever enters the house when it is closed will be unclean until evening;

**14:47** and whoever sleeps in it and eats anything will wash his garments.

**14:48** But if the priest, entering, sees that the leprosy has not grown in the house after it has been plastered anew, he will purify it, since health has been restored;

**14:49** and for its purification he will take two sparrows, and cedar wood, and scarlet, and hyssop;

**14:50** and when one sparrow has been sacrificed in a clay vessel over living waters,

**14:51** he will take the cedar wood, and the hyssop, and the scarlet, and the living sparrow, and he will dip them all in the blood of the sacrificed sparrow and in the living waters, and he will sprinkle the house seven times,

**14:52** and he will purify it both with the blood of the sparrow and with the living waters, and with the living sparrow, and the cedar wood, and the hyssop, and the scarlet.

**14:53** And when he has released the sparrow to fly away freely into the field, he will pray for the house, and it will be lawfully cleansed.

**14:54** This is the law of every leprosy and affliction,

**14:55** of leprosy of garments and of houses,

**14:56** of a scar and of erupting pustules, of a shining spot, and when the colors have been changed into various appearances,

**14:57** so that it can be known at what time anything is clean or unclean."

## Translator Notes

- **14:4** — Throughout this chapter (vv. 4-7, 49-53), the Vulgate renders the generic Hebrew term for birds specifically as 'passeres' ("sparrows"), and here translates "clean" as 'quibus vesci licitum est' ("that it is lawful to eat").
- **14:10** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew liquid measure 'log' with the Roman unit 'sextarius' (roughly a pint; also in vv. 12, 15, 21, 24) and omits "of an ephah" after "three tenths of fine flour."
- **14:20** — Here and in v. 31, the Vulgate has 'libamentis' ("libations" or drink offerings) where the Hebrew text refers to the grain offering ('minchah').
- **14:31** — Unlike v. 22 ('pro peccato', "for sin") and the Hebrew text of v. 31 ("sin offering"), the Clementine Vulgate reads 'pro delicto' ("for trespass") and omits the closing atonement clause; elsewhere in the chapter (vv. 19, 21, 29, 53), it translates Hebrew "make atonement" variously as 'rogare' ("pray"), 'placare' ("appease"), and 'orare' ("pray").
- **14:56** — The phrase 'et in varias species, coloribus immutatis' ("and when the colors have been changed into various appearances") is a Vulgate-distinct addition not found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, while v. 57 omits the Hebrew's concluding line ("This is the law of leprosy").
