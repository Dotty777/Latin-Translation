## Text

**22:1** The Lord also spoke to Moses, saying:

**22:2** "Speak to Aaron and to his sons, that they beware of the things of the sons of Israel that have been consecrated, and do not defile the name of the things sanctified to Me, which they themselves offer. I am the Lord.

**22:3** Say to them, and to their descendants: 'Every man from your lineage in whom there is uncleanness, who approaches those things that have been consecrated and that the sons of Israel have offered to the Lord, shall perish before the Lord. I am the Lord.

**22:4** A man from the seed of Aaron who is leprous, or suffering a flow of seed, shall not eat of the things that have been sanctified to Me, until he is healed. He who touches one unclean because of a dead person, or he from whom seed goes out as in intercourse,

**22:5** or he who touches a creeping thing, or any unclean thing whose touch is foul,

**22:6** shall be unclean until evening, and shall not eat of the things that have been sanctified; but when he has washed his flesh with water,

**22:7** and the sun has set, then, having been cleansed, he shall eat of the sanctified things, because it is his food.

**22:8** They shall not eat what has died of itself or what has been caught by a beast, nor shall they be polluted by them. I am the Lord.

**22:9** Let them keep My precepts, so that they do not become subject to sin and die in the sanctuary when they have polluted it. I am the Lord who sanctifies them.

**22:10** No foreigner shall eat of the sanctified things; a tenant of a priest or a hired worker shall not eat of them.

**22:11** However, the one whom a priest has bought, and the one who is born in his house, these shall eat of them.

**22:12** If the daughter of a priest is married to anyone from the people, she shall not eat of the things that have been sanctified, nor of the firstfruits.

**22:13** But if, being a widow or divorced and without children, she has returned to the house of her father, just as she had been accustomed as a young woman, she shall be fed with the food of her father. No foreigner has the authority to eat of them.

**22:14** Whoever eats of the sanctified things through ignorance shall add a fifth part together with what he ate, and shall give it to the priest into the sanctuary.

**22:15** Nor shall they defile the sanctified things of the sons of Israel, which they offer to the Lord,

**22:16** so that they may not bear the iniquity of their offense when they eat the sanctified things. I am the Lord who sanctifies them.'"

**22:17** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**22:18** "Speak to Aaron and his sons, and to all the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them: 'Any man from the house of Israel, or from the sojourners who dwell among you, who offers his offering, whether fulfilling vows or offering freely, whatever he offers as a burnt offering to the Lord,

**22:19** so that it may be offered through you, it shall be an unblemished male from the cattle, or from the sheep, or from the goats;

**22:20** if it has a blemish, you shall not offer it, nor shall it be acceptable.

**22:21** A man who offers a sacrifice of peace offerings to the Lord, whether fulfilling vows or offering freely, whether from the cattle or from the sheep, shall offer an unblemished one so that it may be acceptable; no blemish shall be in it.

**22:22** If it is blind, if broken, if having a scar, if pustules, or scabies, or impetigo, you shall not offer them to the Lord, nor shall you burn any of them upon the altar of the Lord.

**22:23** An ox or a sheep with ear and tail cut off you may offer voluntarily, but a vow cannot be fulfilled from them.

**22:24** You shall not offer to the Lord any animal whose testicles are either crushed, or bruised, or cut and removed, and in your land do not do this at all.

**22:25** From the hand of a foreigner you shall not offer loaves to your God, nor anything else that he may wish to give, because they are all corrupted and blemished; you shall not accept them.'"

**22:26** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**22:27** "An ox, a sheep, and a goat, when they are born, shall be for seven days under the udder of their mother; but on the eighth day, and from then on, they may be offered to the Lord.

**22:28** Whether it is a cow or a sheep, they shall not be sacrificed on the same day with their young.

**22:29** If you sacrifice an offering for thanksgiving to the Lord, so that it may be pleasing,

**22:30** you shall eat it on the same day; nothing shall remain until the morning of the next day. I am the Lord.

**22:31** Keep My commandments, and do them. I am the Lord.

**22:32** Do not pollute My holy name, so that I may be sanctified in the midst of the sons of Israel. I am the Lord who sanctifies you,

**22:33** and brought you out of the land of Egypt, so that I might be God to you. I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **22:2** — The Vulgate's "nomen sanctificatorum mihi" ("the name of the things sanctified to Me") attaches "name" directly to the consecrated offerings, whereas the Hebrew warns against profaning "My holy name" regarding what the Israelites consecrate.
- **22:9** — Latin "in sanctuario" ("in the sanctuary") explicitly names the sanctuary where the Hebrew has the pronoun "in it" (or "because of it"), referring to the priestly charge or sacred offerings.
- **22:10** — Latin "alienigena" (literally "foreigner" or "one of another stock") is used here and in v. 13 for any layperson outside the priestly household, the same Latin word used in v. 25 for a non-Israelite foreigner.
- **22:19** — The Vulgate's "ut offeratur per vos" ("so that it may be offered through you") is a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Hebrew means "for your acceptance" (that it may be accepted on your behalf).
- **22:23** — Latin "aure et cauda amputatis" ("with ear and tail cut off") is a distinctive Vulgate rendering of two Hebrew terms generally understood to describe an animal with an elongated or stunted limb.
