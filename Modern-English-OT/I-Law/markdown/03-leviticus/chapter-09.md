## Text

**9:1** Now when the eighth day had come, Moses called Aaron, and his sons, and the elders of Israel, and said to Aaron,

**9:2** "Take from the herd a calf for sin, and a ram for a burnt offering, both unblemished, and offer them before the Lord.

**9:3** And to the sons of Israel you shall speak, 'Take a male goat for sin, and a calf and a lamb, a year old and without blemish, for a burnt offering,

**9:4** an ox and a ram for peace offerings; and sacrifice them before the Lord, offering fine flour mixed with oil in the sacrifice of each one; for today the Lord will appear to you.'"

**9:5** Therefore they brought all the things that Moses had ordered to the entrance of the tabernacle, where, while the whole multitude stood by,

**9:6** Moses said, "This is the word that the Lord has commanded: do it, and His glory will appear to you."

**9:7** And he said to Aaron, "Approach the altar and sacrifice for your sin; offer the burnt offering, and intercede for yourself and for the people; and when you have slaughtered the sacrifice of the people, pray for them, just as the Lord has commanded."

**9:8** And immediately Aaron, approaching the altar, sacrificed the calf for his sin,

**9:9** whose blood his sons brought to him; dipping his finger in it, he touched the horns of the altar and poured out the remainder at its base.

**9:10** And the fat, and the kidneys, and the membrane of the liver, which are for sin, he burned on the altar, just as the Lord had commanded Moses;

**9:11** but its flesh and its hide he burned up with fire outside the camp.

**9:12** He also sacrificed the victim of the burnt offering; and his sons brought its blood to him, which he poured out all around the altar.

**9:13** They also brought the sacrifice itself, cut into pieces, with the head and each of the limbs, all of which he burned with fire on the altar,

**9:14** after first washing the intestines and the feet with water.

**9:15** And offering for the sin of the people, he slaughtered the male goat; and when the altar had been purified,

**9:16** he made the burnt offering,

**9:17** adding in the sacrifice the libations that are offered along with it, and burning them on the altar, apart from the ceremonies of the morning burnt offering.

**9:18** He also sacrificed the ox and the ram, the peace sacrifices of the people; and his sons brought the blood to him, which he poured out on the altar all around.

**9:19** And the fat of the ox, and the tail of the ram, and the kidneys with their fat, and the membrane of the liver,

**9:20** they placed on the breasts; and when the fat pieces had been burned on the altar,

**9:21** Aaron separated their breasts and right shoulders, lifting them up before the Lord, just as Moses had commanded.

**9:22** And stretching out his hands toward the people, he blessed them. And thus, when the sacrifices for sin, and the burnt offerings, and the peace offerings had been completed, he came down.

**9:23** And Moses and Aaron entered the tabernacle of the testimony, and afterward came out and blessed the people. And the glory of the Lord appeared to the whole multitude;

**9:24** and look, fire came out from the Lord and devoured the burnt offering and the fat pieces that were on the altar. And when the crowds saw this, they praised the Lord, falling on their faces.

## Translator Notes

- **9:7** — Where the Hebrew twice uses the verb for "make atonement," the Vulgate renders the first instance as deprecare ("intercede") and the second as ora ("pray").
- **9:16** — The Vulgate condenses the end of 9:15 and all of 9:16 into "when the altar had been purified, he made the burnt offering," omitting the Hebrew references to offering the sin offering like the first and performing the burnt offering according to the ordinance.
- **9:17** — Latin libamenta ("libations") stands where the Hebrew specifies a grain offering taken by the handful and burned on the altar.
- **9:23** — Latin tabernaculum testimonii ("the tabernacle of the testimony") follows the Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew "tent of meeting." In 9:24, the Vulgate also reads "they praised the Lord" where the Hebrew reads "they shouted."
