## Text

**23:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**23:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you will say to them: 'These are the feast days of the Lord, which you will proclaim holy.

**23:3** For six days you will do work; the seventh day, because it is the rest of the sabbath, will be proclaimed holy; you will not do any work on it; it is a sabbath of the Lord in all your dwellings.

**23:4** These, therefore, are the holy feast days of the Lord, which you must celebrate in their seasons.

**23:5** In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month at evening, is the Passover of the Lord;

**23:6** and on the fifteenth day of this month is the solemnity of unleavened bread of the Lord. For seven days you will eat unleavened bread.

**23:7** The first day will be most celebrated and holy for you; you will not do any servile work on it,

**23:8** but you will offer a sacrifice by fire to the Lord for seven days. The seventh day, however, will be more celebrated and holier; and you will do no servile work on it.'"

**23:9** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**23:10** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you will say to them: 'When you have entered the land which I will give to you, and have reaped the crop, you will bring sheaves of ears of grain, the firstfruits of your harvest, to the priest,

**23:11** who will lift up the bundle before the Lord, so that it may be acceptable for you, on the day after the sabbath, and he will sanctify it.

**23:12** And on the same day on which the sheaf is consecrated, an unblemished year-old lamb will be slaughtered as a burnt offering to the Lord.

**23:13** And libations will be offered with it, two-tenths of fine flour moistened with oil as a fire offering to the Lord and a most sweet aroma; offerings of wine also, a fourth part of a hin.

**23:14** You will not eat bread, parched grain, or porridge from the crop until the day on which you offer from it to your God. It is an everlasting precept in your generations and in all your dwelling places.

**23:15** Therefore you will count from the day after the sabbath, on which you offered the sheaf of the firstfruits, seven full weeks,

**23:16** until the day after the completion of the seventh week, that is, fifty days; and so you will offer a new sacrifice to the Lord

**23:17** from all your dwelling places, two loaves of firstfruits from two-tenths of leavened fine flour, which you will bake as firstfruits to the Lord.

**23:18** And you will offer with the loaves seven unblemished year-old lambs, and one calf from the herd, and two rams, and they will be for a burnt offering with their libations, for a most sweet aroma to the Lord.

**23:19** You will also prepare a male goat for sin, and two year-old lambs as victims of peace offerings.

**23:20** And when the priest has lifted them up with the loaves of firstfruits before the Lord, they will pass to his use.

**23:21** And you will proclaim this day most celebrated and most holy; you will not do any servile work on it. It will be an everlasting statute in all your dwelling places and your generations.

**23:22** Now after you have reaped the crop of your land, you will neither cut it down to the ground nor gather the remaining ears of grain; but you will leave them for the poor and the foreigners. I am the Lord your God.'"

**23:23** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**23:24** "Speak to the sons of Israel: 'In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, there will be for you a sabbath, a memorial, with trumpets sounding, and it will be proclaimed holy;

**23:25** you will not do any servile work on it, and you will offer a burnt offering to the Lord.'"

**23:26** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**23:27** "On the tenth day of this seventh month, the day of atonements will be most celebrated, and it will be proclaimed holy; and you will afflict your souls on it, and you will offer a burnt offering to the Lord.

**23:28** You will not do any servile work during the time of this day, because it is a day of propitiation, so that the Lord your God may be propitious to you.

**23:29** Every soul that has not been afflicted on this day will perish from its peoples;

**23:30** and the one that has done any work at all, I will blot it out from its people.

**23:31** Therefore you will do no work at all on it; it will be an everlasting statute for you in all your generations and your dwellings.

**23:32** It is a sabbath of rest, and you will afflict your souls on the ninth day of the month; from evening until evening you will celebrate your sabbaths."

**23:33** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**23:34** "Speak to the sons of Israel: 'From the fifteenth day of this seventh month, there will be the feast days of tabernacles for seven days to the Lord.

**23:35** The first day will be proclaimed most celebrated and most holy; you will not do any servile work on it.

**23:36** And for seven days you will offer burnt offerings to the Lord. The eighth day also will be most celebrated and most holy, and you will offer a burnt offering to the Lord, for it is a day of assembly and gathering; you will not do any servile work on it.

**23:37** These are the feast days of the Lord, which you will proclaim most celebrated and most holy, and you will offer on them oblations to the Lord, burnt offerings and libations according to the rite of each day,

**23:38** besides the sabbaths of the Lord, and your gifts, and what you will offer by a vow, or what you will give voluntarily to the Lord.

**23:39** From the fifteenth day of the seventh month, therefore, when you have gathered all the fruits of your land, you will celebrate the feast days of the Lord for seven days; on the first day and on the eighth day there will be a sabbath, that is, a rest.

**23:40** And you will take for yourselves on the first day fruits of the most beautiful tree, and fronds of palm trees, and boughs of a tree of dense foliage, and willows from the brook, and you will rejoice before the Lord your God.

**23:41** And you will celebrate His solemnity for seven days each year; it will be an everlasting statute in your generations. In the seventh month you will celebrate the festivals,

**23:42** and you will dwell in shaded shelters for seven days; everyone who is of the lineage of Israel will remain in tabernacles,

**23:43** so that your descendants may learn that I made the sons of Israel dwell in tabernacles when I brought them out from the land of Egypt. I am the Lord your God.'"

**23:44** And Moses spoke concerning the solemnities of the Lord to the sons of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **23:8** — The Vulgate uses comparative adjectives (celebrior et sanctior, "more celebrated and holier") for the seventh day, contrasting with the first day's "most celebrated and holy" (23:7), where the Hebrew uses the same phrase ("holy convocation") for both days.
- **23:11** — The Vulgate adds "and he will sanctify it" (et sanctificabit illum), a clause not found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **23:13** — The Vulgate uses libamenta ("libations") for the accompanying offering of fine flour moistened with oil (Hebrew minchah, grain offering), and liba vini ("offerings of wine") for the drink offering.
- **23:22** — The Vulgate reads "you will neither cut it down to the ground" (nec secabitis eam usque ad solum), whereas the Hebrew prohibits harvesting the edge or corner of the field.
- **23:36** — The Vulgate renders the closing assembly with a doubled genitive phrase, est enim cœtus atque collectæ ("for it is a day of assembly and gathering"), where the Hebrew has a single term for a solemn assembly.
