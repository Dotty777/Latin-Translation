## Text

**2:1** "When a soul offers an offering of a sacrifice to the Lord, his offering shall be fine flour; and he shall pour oil over it and place frankincense on it,

**2:2** and he shall bring it to the sons of Aaron, the priests, one of whom shall take a full handful of the fine flour and of the oil, and all the frankincense, and shall place it as a memorial on the altar, as a very sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**2:3** Now what remains from the sacrifice shall belong to Aaron and his sons, most holy from the offerings of the Lord.

**2:4** Now when you offer a sacrifice baked in an oven, it shall be of fine flour, namely, loaves without leaven, mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers spread with oil.

**2:5** If your offering is from a frying pan, of fine flour mixed with oil and without leaven,

**2:6** you shall divide it into small pieces, and you shall pour oil over it.

**2:7** But if the sacrifice is from a grill, likewise the fine flour shall be mixed with oil;

**2:8** and offering it to the Lord, you shall deliver it into the hands of the priest.

**2:9** And when he has offered it, he shall take a memorial from the sacrifice and burn it on the altar as a fragrance of sweetness to the Lord;

**2:10** however, whatever remains shall belong to Aaron and his sons, most holy from the offerings of the Lord.

**2:11** Every offering that is offered to the Lord shall be made without leaven, nor shall anything of leaven or honey be burned in a sacrifice to the Lord.

**2:12** You shall offer only firstfruits of them and gifts; however, they shall not be placed on the altar as a fragrance of sweetness.

**2:13** Whatever sacrifice you offer, you shall season with salt, nor shall you remove the salt of the covenant of your God from your sacrifice; in every offering of yours you shall offer salt.

**2:14** Now if you offer a gift of your first produce to the Lord from ears of grain still green, you shall roast them with fire and crush them in the manner of coarse grain, and so you shall offer your firstfruits to the Lord,

**2:15** pouring oil on top and placing frankincense upon it, because it is an offering of the Lord,

**2:16** from which the priest shall burn as a memorial of the gift a part of the crushed grain and of the oil, and all the frankincense."

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Vulgate preserves the literal "Anima" ("a soul," rendering Hebrew "nefesh") for the offerer and translates Hebrew "minchah" ("grain offering") simply as "sacrificium" ("sacrifice") throughout chapter 2.
- **2:2** — Vulgate reads "ponet" ("shall place") for the memorial on the altar, whereas the Hebrew specifies burning it in smoke.
- **2:5** — Vulgate renders the two cooking utensils of verses 5 and 7 as "sartago" ("frying pan") and "craticula" ("grill" or "gridiron"), whereas the Hebrew refers to a flat griddle and a deep pan.
- **2:14** — Latin "far" (here and in 2:16 "farris fracti") denotes coarse, husked grain or groats, contrasting with "simila" ("fine flour") in verses 1-7.
