## Text

**19:1** The Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**19:2** "Speak to the entire assembly of the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'Be holy, because I am holy, the Lord your God.

**19:3** Each one shall fear his father and his mother. Keep My Sabbaths. I am the Lord your God.

**19:4** Do not turn to idols, nor make for yourselves molten gods. I am the Lord your God.

**19:5** If you offer a sacrifice of peace offerings to the Lord, so that it may be acceptable,

**19:6** on the day on which it is offered, you shall eat it, and on the next day; but whatever remains until the third day, you shall burn up with fire.

**19:7** If anyone eats from it after two days, he will be profane and guilty of impiety,

**19:8** and he will bear his iniquity, because he has defiled the holy thing of the Lord, and that soul will perish from its people.

**19:9** And when you harvest the crops of your land, you shall not shear the surface of the land all the way to the ground, nor shall you gather the remaining ears of grain,

**19:10** nor shall you collect the clusters and fallen grapes in your vineyard, but you shall leave them for the poor and the foreigners to pick. I am the Lord your God.

**19:11** You shall not commit theft. You shall not lie, nor shall anyone deceive his neighbor.

**19:12** You shall not swear falsely in My name, nor shall you defile the name of your God. I am the Lord.

**19:13** You shall not falsely accuse your neighbor, nor oppress him by force. The wages of your hired worker shall not remain with you until morning.

**19:14** You shall not curse a deaf person, nor place a stumbling block before a blind person, but you shall fear the Lord your God, because I am the Lord.

**19:15** You shall not do what is unjust, nor judge unjustly. Do not regard the person of the poor, nor honor the face of the powerful. Judge your neighbor justly.

**19:16** You shall not be a slanderer nor a whisperer among the people. You shall not stand against the blood of your neighbor. I am the Lord.

**19:17** You shall not hate your brother in your heart, but openly reprove him, so that you do not have sin on account of him.

**19:18** Do not seek vengeance, nor remember an injury from your fellow citizens. You shall love your friend as yourself. I am the Lord.

**19:19** Keep My laws. You shall not make your beast mate with animals of another kind. You shall not sow your field with diverse seed. You shall not be clothed in a garment that is woven from two kinds.

**19:20** If a man sleeps with a woman in an intercourse of seed, who is a servant woman even of marriageable age, and yet not redeemed by a price nor granted freedom, both shall be beaten, and they shall not die, because she was not free.

**19:21** Now for his offense, he shall offer a ram to the Lord at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony,

**19:22** and the priest shall pray for him and for his sin before the Lord, and He will be gracious to him again, and the sin will be forgiven.

**19:23** When you have entered the land and planted fruit-bearing trees in it, you shall remove their foreskins; the fruits that sprout shall be unclean to you, and you shall not eat from them.

**19:24** But in the fourth year, all their produce shall be sanctified, praiseworthy to the Lord.

**19:25** And in the fifth year, you shall eat the produce, gathering the fruits that they bring forth. I am the Lord your God.

**19:26** You shall not eat with blood. You shall not practice augury, nor observe dreams.

**19:27** You shall neither trim your hair in a circle nor shave your beard.

**19:28** And over a dead person you shall not cut your flesh, nor make any figures or marks on yourselves. I am the Lord.

**19:29** Do not prostitute your daughter, so that the land is not contaminated and filled with wickedness.

**19:30** Keep My Sabbaths, and revere My sanctuary. I am the Lord.

**19:31** Do not turn aside to magicians, nor inquire anything from soothsayers, so as to be defiled through them. I am the Lord your God.

**19:32** Rise up before a gray head, and honor the person of an elderly man, and fear the Lord your God. I am the Lord.

**19:33** If a stranger lives in your land and stays among you, do not reproach him,

**19:34** but let him be among you as a native, and you shall love him as yourselves, for you also were strangers in the land of Egypt. I am the Lord your God.

**19:35** Do not do anything unjust in judgment, in measuring rule, in weight, in measure.

**19:36** Let the balance be just and the weights equal, the modius just and the sextarius equal. I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt.

**19:37** Keep all My precepts and all the judgments, and do them. I am the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **19:9** — Vulgate reads 'non tondebis usque ad solum superficiem terræ' (not shearing the surface of the land all the way to the ground), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text forbids reaping to the edges or corners of the field.
- **19:18** — Vulgate uniquely uses 'amicum tuum' ('your friend') here rather than 'proximum tuum' ('your neighbor', used in vv. 11, 13, 15, and 16) to render the same Hebrew noun.
- **19:20** — Vulgate describes the servant woman as 'etiam nubilis' ('even of marriageable age') and prescribes 'vapulabunt ambo' ('both shall be beaten'), whereas the Hebrew text refers to a slave woman designated for a man and states that there shall be an inquiry or punishment.
- **19:23** — Vulgate renders the idiom literally as 'auferetis præputia eorum' ('you shall remove their foreskins') and omits the phrase 'for three years' found in the Hebrew text, though 'the fourth year' follows in v. 24.
- **19:36** — Vulgate uses the Roman units 'modius' (a dry measure) and 'sextarius' (a liquid or dry measure) to render the Hebrew 'ephah' and 'hin'.
