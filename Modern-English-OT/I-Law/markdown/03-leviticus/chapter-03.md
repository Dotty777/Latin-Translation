## Text

**3:1** "Now if his offering is a sacrifice of peace offerings, and he wishes to offer from the cattle, whether male or female, he shall offer it unblemished before the Lord.

**3:2** And he shall place his hand on the head of his victim, which shall be slaughtered at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony, and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pour the blood all around the altar.

**3:3** And they shall offer from the sacrifice of peace offerings as an offering to the Lord the fat that covers the internal organs, and whatever fatness is inside:

**3:4** the two kidneys with the fat by which the flanks are covered, and the membrane of the liver with the kidneys.

**3:5** And they shall burn them on the altar as a burnt offering, on the wood with fire placed underneath, as an offering of a very sweet fragrance to the Lord.

**3:6** Now if his offering and sacrifice of peace offerings is from the sheep, whether he offers a male or a female, they shall be unblemished.

**3:7** If he offers a lamb before the Lord,

**3:8** he shall place his hand on the head of his victim, which shall be slaughtered in the vestibule of the tabernacle of the testimony; and the sons of Aaron shall pour its blood all around the altar.

**3:9** And they shall offer from the sacrifice of peace offerings a sacrifice to the Lord: the fat and the entire tail

**3:10** with the kidneys, and the fatness that covers the belly and all the internal organs, and both kidneys with the fat that is beside the flanks, and the membrane of the liver with the kidneys.

**3:11** And the priest shall burn them on the altar as food of the fire and of the offering of the Lord.

**3:12** If his offering is a goat, and he offers it to the Lord,

**3:13** he shall place his hand on its head, and he shall slaughter it at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony, and the sons of Aaron shall pour its blood all around the altar.

**3:14** And they shall take from it as sustenance for the Lord's fire the fat that covers the belly and that covers all the internal organs:

**3:15** the two kidneys with the membrane that is over them beside the flanks, and the fat of the liver with the kidneys;

**3:16** and the priest shall burn them on the altar as nourishment for the fire, and of a very sweet fragrance. All fat shall belong to the Lord

**3:17** by a perpetual statute throughout your generations and in all your dwellings; you shall not eat either blood or fat at all."

## Translator Notes

- **3:5** — Vulgate reads "in holocaustum" ("as a burnt offering"), whereas the Hebrew reads "upon the burnt offering" already on the altar.
- **3:6** — Vulgate renders the Hebrew term for a mixed flock of sheep and goats simply as "de ovibus" ("from the sheep"), even though verse 12 goes on to prescribe the offering of a goat.
- **3:10** — Vulgate connects "caudam totam" (3:9) with "cum renibus" ("the entire tail with the kidneys"), whereas the Hebrew states that the fat tail is removed "close to the backbone."
- **3:15** — Compared with 3:4 and 3:10, the Vulgate here exchanges the anatomical terms, placing "reticulum" ("membrane") over the kidneys beside the flanks and "arvina" ("fat") on the liver.
