## Text

**20:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**20:2** "You shall speak these things to the sons of Israel: 'If any man from the sons of Israel, and from the strangers who live in Israel, gives from his seed to the idol Molech, he shall surely die; the people of the land shall stone him.

**20:3** And I will set My face against him and will cut him off from the midst of his people, because he has given from his seed to Molech, and has contaminated My sanctuary, and has defiled My holy name.

**20:4** And if the people of the land, being negligent and as if thinking little of My command, lets go the man who gave from his seed to Molech, and refuses to kill him,

**20:5** I will set My face upon that man and upon his relatives, and I will cut off both him and all who consented with him to commit fornication with Molech from the midst of their people.

**20:6** As for the soul that turns aside to magicians and soothsayers and commits fornication with them, I will set My face against it and will destroy it from the midst of its people.

**20:7** Sanctify yourselves and be holy, because I am the Lord your God.

**20:8** Keep My precepts and do them; I am the Lord who sanctifies you.

**20:9** Whoever curses his father or mother shall surely die; he has cursed his father and mother; let his blood be upon him.

**20:10** If anyone commits adultery with the wife of another, and perpetrates adultery with the spouse of his neighbor, both the adulterer and the adulteress shall surely die.

**20:11** Whoever sleeps with his stepmother and uncovers his father's disgrace, both of them shall surely die; let their blood be upon them.

**20:12** If anyone sleeps with his daughter-in-law, both of them shall die, because they have committed wickedness; let their blood be upon them.

**20:13** Whoever sleeps with a male in the intercourse of a woman, both of them have committed an abomination; they shall surely die; let their blood be upon them.

**20:14** Whoever, in addition to a daughter as his wife, marries her mother has committed wickedness; he shall burn alive with them, and so great an abomination shall not remain in your midst.

**20:15** Whoever has intercourse with a beast and a herd animal shall surely die; kill the herd animal as well.

**20:16** A woman who lies beneath any beast shall be put to death together with it; let their blood be upon them.

**20:17** Whoever takes his sister, his father's daughter or his mother's daughter, and sees her shame, and she looks upon her brother's disgrace, they have committed an abominable thing; they shall be killed in the sight of their people, because they have mutually uncovered their shame, and they shall bear their iniquity.

**20:18** Whoever has intercourse with a woman during her menstrual flow and uncovers her shame, and she herself opens the fountain of her blood, both of them shall be destroyed from the midst of their people.

**20:19** You shall not uncover the shame of your mother's sister and your father's sister; whoever does this has laid bare the disgrace of his own flesh; both of them shall bear their iniquity.

**20:20** Whoever has intercourse with the wife of his paternal uncle or his maternal uncle and uncovers the disgrace of his relatives, both of them shall bear their sin; they shall die without children.

**20:21** Whoever marries his brother's wife does an unlawful thing; he has uncovered his brother's shame; they shall be without children.

**20:22** Keep My laws and judgments, and do them, so that the land which you are about to enter and inhabit does not vomit you out as well.

**20:23** Do not walk in the statutes of the nations that I am about to drive out before you. For they did all these things, and I abhorred them.

**20:24** But to you I speak. Possess their land, which I will give to you as an inheritance, a land flowing with milk and honey. I am the Lord your God, who has separated you from the other peoples.

**20:25** Therefore you also separate the clean beast from the unclean, and the clean bird from the unclean, so that you do not defile your souls by herd animals, and birds, and all things that move on the earth, and which I have shown to you to be defiled.

**20:26** You shall be holy to Me, because I, the Lord, am holy, and I have separated you from the other peoples, so that you would be Mine.

**20:27** A man or a woman in whom there is a pythonic spirit or a spirit of divination shall surely die; they shall overwhelm them with stones; let their blood be upon them.'"

## Translator Notes

- **20:11** — Vulgate uses 'noverca' ('stepmother') here for Hebrew 'father's wife', and throughout vv. 11-21 renders the Hebrew idiom 'nakedness' with the moral terms 'turpitudo' ('shame') and 'ignominia' ('disgrace').
- **20:14** — Vulgate reads 'vivus ardebit cum eis' ('he shall burn alive with them'), explicitly specifying burning alive, whereas the Hebrew text states that they shall burn him and them with fire.
- **20:20** — Vulgate expands Hebrew 'his uncle' to 'patrui vel avunculi sui' ('his paternal uncle or his maternal uncle') and reads 'cognationis suæ' ('of his relatives') where Hebrew reads 'of his uncle'.
- **20:24** — Vulgate opens with a present-tense statement and imperative ('Vobis autem loquor. Possidete'), whereas the Hebrew text uses past narration followed by a future verb ('I said to you, You shall inherit').
- **20:27** — Vulgate 'pythonicus spiritus' ('a pythonic spirit') is a Greco-Roman term derived from the Pythian oracle at Delphi, used here for a spirit of mediumship or necromancy.
