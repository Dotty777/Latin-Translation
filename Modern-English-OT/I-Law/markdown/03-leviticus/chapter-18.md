## Text

**18:1** The Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**18:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'I am the Lord your God.

**18:3** You shall not do according to the custom of the land of Egypt, in which you lived, and you shall not act according to the practice of the region of Canaan, to which I am about to bring you, nor shall you walk in their statutes.

**18:4** You shall do My judgments, and observe My precepts, and walk in them. I am the Lord your God.

**18:5** Keep My laws and judgments, by doing which a man shall live in them. I am the Lord.

**18:6** No man shall approach a close relative of his blood to expose her shame. I am the Lord.

**18:7** You shall not uncover the shame of your father and the shame of your mother; she is your mother; you shall not expose her shame.

**18:8** You shall not uncover the shame of your father's wife, for it is your father's shame.

**18:9** You shall not expose the shame of your sister, whether by your father or by your mother, who was born at home or outside.

**18:10** You shall not expose the shame of your son's daughter or of your granddaughter by your daughter, because it is your own shame.

**18:11** You shall not expose the shame of the daughter of your father's wife, whom she bore to your father, and who is your sister.

**18:12** You shall not uncover the shame of your father's sister, because she is your father's flesh.

**18:13** You shall not expose the shame of your mother's sister, since she is your mother's flesh.

**18:14** You shall not expose the shame of your paternal uncle, nor shall you approach his wife, who is joined to you by marriage.

**18:15** You shall not expose the shame of your daughter-in-law, because she is your son's wife, nor shall you uncover her disgrace.

**18:16** You shall not expose the shame of your brother's wife, because it is your brother's shame.

**18:17** You shall not expose the shame of your wife and of her daughter. You shall not take her son's daughter or her daughter's daughter to expose her disgrace, because they are her flesh, and such intercourse is incest.

**18:18** You shall not take your wife's sister as a rival concubine to her, nor shall you expose her shame while she is still living.

**18:19** You shall not approach a woman who is undergoing her menstrual flow, nor shall you expose her foulness.

**18:20** You shall not have intercourse with your neighbor's wife, nor shall you be defiled by the mingling of seed.

**18:21** You shall not give any of your seed to be consecrated to the idol Molech, nor shall you pollute the name of your God. I am the Lord.

**18:22** You shall not unite with a male in intercourse as with a woman, because it is an abomination.

**18:23** You shall not have intercourse with any animal, nor shall you be defiled with it. A woman shall not submit herself to a beast, nor unite with it, because it is a wicked deed.

**18:24** And do not pollute yourselves in any of these things by which all the nations have been contaminated, whom I will cast out before your sight,

**18:25** and by which the land has been polluted, whose wicked deeds I will punish, so that it may vomit out its inhabitants.

**18:26** Keep My statutes and judgments, and do not do any of all these abominations, whether the native-born or the resident alien who sojourns among you.

**18:27** For the residents of the land who were before you did all these detestable things, and they polluted it.

**18:28** Take care, therefore, that it does not similarly vomit you out too when you do like things, just as it vomited out the nation that was before you.

**18:29** Every person who does any of these abominations shall perish from the midst of his people.

**18:30** Keep My commandments. Do not do the things that those who were before you did, and do not pollute yourselves in them. I am the Lord your God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **18:6** — Throughout verses 6-19, the Vulgate avoids anatomical 'nuditas' ('nakedness') for Hebrew 'ervah,' translating it with 'turpitudo' ('shame/indecency') and varying it with 'ignominia' ('disgrace,' vv. 15, 17) and 'fœditas' ('foulness,' v. 19). Latin 'proximam sanguinis sui' explicitly specifies a close female blood relative.
- **18:14** — Where the Hebrew states 'she is your aunt,' the Vulgate renders 'quæ tibi affinitate conjungitur' ('who is joined to you by marriage,' using the Roman legal term 'affinitas' for relationship by marriage).
- **18:17** — The Vulgate specifies 'uxoris tuæ' ('your wife') rather than Hebrew 'a woman,' and renders Hebrew 'depravity/wickedness' explicitly as 'et talis coitus incestus est' ('and such intercourse is incest').
- **18:21** — Where the Hebrew speaks of causing offspring to 'pass over' (commonly understood as passing through fire) to Molech, the Vulgate reads 'ut consecretur idolo Moloch' ('to be consecrated to the idol Molech').
- **18:25** — The Vulgate expresses 'ut evomat' as a purpose or result clause ('so that it may vomit out its inhabitants') rather than the Hebrew statement that the land already vomited them out; similarly, in verse 28 the Vulgate reads 'cum paria feceritis' ('when you do like things') where the Hebrew has 'when you defile it.'
