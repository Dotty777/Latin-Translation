## Text

**17:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**17:2** "Speak to Aaron and to his sons, and to all the sons of Israel, saying to them, 'This is the word that the Lord has commanded, saying,

**17:3** "Any man of the house of Israel, if he kills an ox or a sheep or a goat in the camp or outside the camp,

**17:4** and does not bring an offering to the Lord at the entrance of the tabernacle, he shall be guilty of blood; as if he had shed blood, so he shall perish from the midst of his people.

**17:5** Therefore the sons of Israel must bring to the priest their sacrifices that they kill in the field, so that they may be sanctified to the Lord before the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony, and they may sacrifice them as peace offerings to the Lord.

**17:6** And the priest shall pour out the blood upon the altar of the Lord at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony, and he shall burn the fat for a sweet aroma to the Lord;

**17:7** and they shall never again offer their sacrifices to demons, with whom they have committed fornication. It shall be an everlasting statute for them and for their descendants."'

**17:8** And to them you shall say, 'Any man of the house of Israel and of the foreigners who sojourn among you, who offers a burnt offering or a sacrifice,

**17:9** and does not bring it to the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony so that it may be offered to the Lord, he shall perish from his people.

**17:10** Any man of the house of Israel and of the foreigners who sojourn among them, if he eats blood, I will set My face firmly against his soul, and I will destroy it from his people,

**17:11** because the life of the flesh is in the blood; and I have given it to you so that upon the altar you may make expiation by it for your souls, and that the blood may be for the expiation of the soul.

**17:12** For this reason I have said to the sons of Israel, "No person among you shall eat blood, nor any of the foreigners who sojourn among you."

**17:13** Any man of the sons of Israel and of the foreigners who sojourn among you, if by hunting and bird-catching he catches a wild animal or a bird which it is lawful to eat, let him pour out its blood and cover it with earth.

**17:14** For the life of all flesh is in the blood; therefore I have said to the sons of Israel, "You shall not eat the blood of any flesh, because the life of the flesh is in the blood; and whoever eats it shall perish."

**17:15** Any person who eats what died of itself or what was seized by a wild beast, whether among the native-born or among the foreigners, shall wash his garments and himself with water, and he shall be contaminated until evening; and in this manner he shall become clean.

**17:16** But if he does not wash his garments and his body, he shall bear his iniquity.'"

## Translator Notes

- **17:4** — The Vulgate softens the Hebrew declaration 'he has shed blood' into a comparison, 'quasi si sanguinem fuderit' ('as if he had shed blood'), and uses the active 'peribit' ('he shall perish') where the Hebrew has passive 'shall be cut off.'
- **17:5** — The Vulgate reads 'ut sanctificentur Domino' ('so that they may be sanctified to the Lord') rather than the Hebrew 'that they may bring them to the Lord,' and follows the Septuagint in rendering 'tent of meeting' as 'tabernaculum testimonii' ('tabernacle of the testimony').
- **17:7** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew goat-demons or satyrs ('se'irim') simply as 'dæmonibus' ('demons') and 'throughout their generations' as 'et posteris eorum' ('and for their descendants').
- **17:11** — Latin 'anima' encompasses both 'life' and 'soul' across this verse. The closing clause 'et sanguis pro animæ piaculo sit' ('and that the blood may be for the expiation of the soul') is a Vulgate-distinct rendering compared with the Hebrew 'for it is the blood that makes atonement by the life.'
- **17:14** — The Vulgate reads 'in sanguine est' ('is in the blood') twice here, echoing verse 11, where the Hebrew states that the life of all flesh 'is its blood.'
