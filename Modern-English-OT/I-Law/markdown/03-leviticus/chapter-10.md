## Text

**10:1** And Nadab and Abihu, the sons of Aaron, seizing censers, placed fire in them and incense on top, offering foreign fire before the Lord, which had not been commanded to them.

**10:2** And fire went out from the Lord and devoured them, and they died before the Lord.

**10:3** And Moses said to Aaron, "This is what the Lord spoke: 'I will be sanctified in those who draw near to Me, and in the sight of all the people I will be glorified.'" Hearing this, Aaron kept silent.

**10:4** Now Moses, having summoned Mishael and Elzaphan, the sons of Uzziel, Aaron's uncle, said to them, "Go and take up your brothers from the sight of the sanctuary, and carry them outside the camp."

**10:5** And going immediately, they took them up just as they lay, clothed in linen tunics, and cast them outside, as had been commanded to them.

**10:6** And Moses spoke to Aaron, and to Eleazar and Ithamar, his sons, "Do not uncover your heads, and do not tear your garments, so that you might not die and indignation arise over the entire assembly. Let your brothers, and the entire house of Israel, mourn the burning that the Lord has kindled;

**10:7** but you shall not go out the doors of the tabernacle, otherwise you will perish; for the oil of holy anointing is upon you." And they did all things according to the command of Moses.

**10:8** The Lord also said to Aaron,

**10:9** "Wine, and anything that can intoxicate, you and your sons shall not drink when you enter the tabernacle of the testimony, so that you may not die; for it is an everlasting command throughout your generations,

**10:10** and so that you may have the knowledge to distinguish between the holy and the profane, between the defiled and the clean,

**10:11** and may teach the sons of Israel all My statutes that the Lord has spoken to them by the hand of Moses."

**10:12** And Moses spoke to Aaron, and to Eleazar and Ithamar, his sons who were left, "Take the sacrifice that remained from the offering of the Lord, and eat it without leaven beside the altar, for it is most holy.

**10:13** And you shall eat in a holy place what has been given to you and to your sons from the offerings of the Lord, as was commanded to me.

**10:14** Also, the breast that was offered and the shoulder that was set apart, you shall eat in a most clean place, you and your sons, and your daughters with you; for they have been reserved for you and your children from the sacrifices of salvation of the sons of Israel,

**10:15** because they have lifted up the shoulder and the breast, and the fat portions that are burned on the altar, before the Lord, and they belong to you and to your sons by a perpetual law, just as the Lord has commanded."

**10:16** Meanwhile, when Moses searched for the male goat that had been offered for sin, he found it burned up; and being angry with Eleazar and Ithamar, the sons of Aaron who had remained, he said,

**10:17** "Why did you not eat the sacrifice for sin in a holy place, which is most holy, and was given to you so that you might bear the iniquity of the multitude and pray for it in the sight of the Lord,

**10:18** especially since none of its blood was brought inside the holy places, and you ought to have eaten it in the sanctuary, as was commanded to me?"

**10:19** Aaron answered, "Today the victim for sin and the burnt offering have been offered before the Lord, yet what you see has happened to me; how could I eat it, or please the Lord in the ceremonies with a mournful mind?"

**10:20** When Moses had heard this, he accepted satisfaction.

## Translator Notes

- **10:5** — The Vulgate explicitly reads "just as they lay, clothed in linen tunics" (sicut jacebant, vestitos lineis tunicis) and "cast them outside" (ejecerunt foras), whereas the Hebrew simply says they carried them out "by their tunics."
- **10:9** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew "strong drink" descriptively as "anything that can intoxicate" (omne quod inebriare potest), and renders the "tent of meeting" as "tabernacle of the testimony" (tabernaculum testimonii), following the Septuagint.
- **10:14** — The Vulgate reads "shoulder" (armum) for the Hebrew thigh/leg, "in a most clean place" (in loco mundissimo, superlative), and "sacrifices of salvation" (hostiis salutaribus), echoing the Septuagint's rendering of peace/fellowship offerings.
- **10:18** — The Vulgate reads "as was commanded to me" (sicut praeceptum est mihi), agreeing with the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "as I commanded."
- **10:19** — The Vulgate renders Aaron's closing question distinctively as "or please the Lord in the ceremonies with a mournful mind?" (mente lugubri), whereas the Hebrew asks whether eating the sin offering would have pleased the Lord; in 10:20, recepit satisfactionem literally means "accepted satisfaction," i.e., accepted Aaron's justification.
