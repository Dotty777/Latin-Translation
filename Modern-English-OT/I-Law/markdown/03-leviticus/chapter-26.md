## Text

**26:1** "I am the Lord your God: you must not make for yourselves an idol or a carved image, nor erect memorial pillars, nor place a marked stone in your land so that you may worship it. For I am the Lord your God.

**26:2** Keep My Sabbaths, and stand in awe before My sanctuary. I am the Lord.

**26:3** If you walk in My precepts, and keep My commandments and do them, I will give you rains in their seasons,

**26:4** and the land will bring forth its produce, and the trees will be filled with fruit.

**26:5** The threshing of the harvests will overtake the grape harvest, and the grape harvest will reach the sowing; and you will eat your bread to satisfaction, and you will dwell without dread in your land.

**26:6** I will give peace within your borders; you will sleep, and there will be no one to terrify you. I will remove evil beasts, and the sword will not cross your boundaries.

**26:7** You will pursue your enemies, and they will collapse before you.

**26:8** Five of your own will pursue a hundred foreigners, and a hundred of you ten thousand; your enemies will fall by the sword in your sight.

**26:9** I will look upon you and make you grow; you will be multiplied, and I will confirm My covenant with you.

**26:10** You will eat the oldest of the old, and as the new arrives you will throw out the old.

**26:11** I will set My tabernacle in your midst, and My soul will not reject you.

**26:12** I will walk among you, and I will be your God, and you will be My people.

**26:13** I am the Lord your God, who brought you out from the land of the Egyptians so that you would not serve them, and who shattered the chains on your necks so that you might walk upright.

**26:14** But if you do not listen to Me, nor do all My commandments,

**26:15** if you spurn My laws and hold My judgments in contempt, so that you do not do the things that have been established by Me, and bring My covenant to nothing:

**26:16** I also will do these things to you: I will visit you swiftly with want, and with burning fever that will wear out your eyes and consume your souls. In vain you will sow seed, which will be devoured by foes.

**26:17** I will set My face against you, and you will collapse before your foes, and you will be subjected to those who hate you; you will flee with no one pursuing.

**26:18** But if even so you do not obey Me, I will increase your chastisements sevenfold because of your sins,

**26:19** and I will crush the pride of your hardness. And I will give you a sky above like iron, and a bronze earth.

**26:20** Your labor will be consumed in vain; the land will not bring forth produce, nor will the trees yield fruit.

**26:21** If you walk in opposition to Me and are unwilling to listen to Me, I will increase your plagues sevenfold because of your sins;

**26:22** and I will send the beasts of the field among you, which will consume you and your livestock and reduce all things to a few, and your roads will become deserted.

**26:23** And if even so you are unwilling to accept discipline, but walk in opposition to Me,

**26:24** I also will advance against you in opposition, and I will strike you seven times because of your sins,

**26:25** and I will bring upon you a sword avenging My covenant. And when you take refuge in the cities, I will send pestilence into your midst, and you will be handed over into the hands of foes,

**26:26** after I have broken the staff of your bread, so that ten women bake loaves in one oven and hand them back by weight; and you will eat, and will not be satisfied.

**26:27** But if even through these things you do not listen to Me, but walk against Me,

**26:28** then I too will advance against you in opposing fury, and I will chastise you with seven plagues because of your sins,

**26:29** so that you eat the flesh of your sons and of your daughters.

**26:30** I will destroy your high places, and shatter the images. You will fall among the ruins of your idols, and My soul will abhor you,

**26:31** to such an extent that I will reduce your cities to desolation, and make your sanctuaries deserted, and no longer accept the sweetest aroma.

**26:32** And I will lay waste your land, and your enemies will be astonished over it when they become its inhabitants.

**26:33** You, however, I will scatter among the nations, and I will unsheathe the sword after you, and your land will be deserted, and your cities demolished.

**26:34** Then its Sabbaths will please the land all the days of its desolation; when you are

**26:35** in a hostile land, it will keep Sabbath and rest during the Sabbaths of its desolation, because it did not rest during your Sabbaths when you were dwelling in it.

**26:36** And as for those of you who remain, I will put dread into their hearts in the regions of foes; the sound of a flying leaf will terrify them, and they will flee as though from a sword; they will fall with no one pursuing,

**26:37** and each will collapse upon his brothers, as though fleeing battles; none of you will dare to resist enemies.

**26:38** You will perish among the nations, and a hostile land will consume you.

**26:39** And if some of these also remain, they will waste away in their iniquities in the land of their enemies, and because of the sins of their fathers and their own they will be afflicted,

**26:40** until they confess their iniquities and those of their ancestors, by which they transgressed against Me and walked in opposition to Me.

**26:41** Therefore I also will walk against them, and I will bring them into a hostile land, until their uncircumcised mind is ashamed; then they will pray for their impieties.

**26:42** And I will remember My covenant which I made with Jacob, and Isaac, and Abraham. I will also be mindful of the land,

**26:43** which, when it has been abandoned by them, will delight itself in its Sabbaths, enduring desolation because of them. But they themselves will plead for their sins, because they rejected My judgments and despised My laws.

**26:44** And yet even when they were in a hostile land, I did not utterly reject them, nor did I so despise them that they would be consumed and make My covenant with them void. For I am the Lord their God,

**26:45** and I will remember My former covenant, when I brought them out from the land of Egypt in the sight of the nations, so that I might be their God. I am the Lord." These are the judgments and precepts and laws which the Lord gave between Himself and the sons of Israel on Mount Sinai by the hand of Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **26:1** — The Clementine Vulgate opens this verse with "I am the Lord your God" (Ego Dominus Deus vester), framing the verse with a double divine self-declaration where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has this formula only at the end; it also uses the plural titulos ("memorial pillars") and insignem lapidem ("marked stone").
- **26:16** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text lists sudden terror, wasting disease, and fever, the Vulgate renders the first term adverbially as velociter ("swiftly") and translates the affliction as egestate, et ardore ("want, and ... burning fever"), with the masculine relative qui referring specifically to ardore.
- **26:41** — The Vulgate distinctively reads donec erubescat incircumcisa mens eorum ("until their uncircumcised mind is ashamed") and tunc orabunt pro impietatibus suis ("then they will pray for their impieties"), differing from the Hebrew "if their uncircumcised heart is humbled and they make amends for their iniquity" (compare rogabunt in v. 43).
- **26:44** — The Vulgate uses past tenses throughout this verse and a third-person plural verb facerent ("and make My covenant with them void"), making Israel rather than God the grammatical subject of voiding the covenant.
- **26:45** — The Clementine Vulgate includes the concluding colophon ("These are the judgments...") within verse 45, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text and most modern English Bibles number it separately as verse 46.
