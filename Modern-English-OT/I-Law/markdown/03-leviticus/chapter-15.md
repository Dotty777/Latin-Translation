## Text

**15:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:

**15:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and say to them, 'A man who suffers a flow of seed will be unclean.

**15:3** And then he will be judged to be subject to this ailment, when moment by moment a foul fluid clings to his flesh and congeals.

**15:4** All bedding on which he sleeps will be unclean, and wherever he sits.

**15:5** If any person touches his bed, he will wash his garments, and having washed himself with water, he will be unclean until evening.

**15:6** If someone sits where that man had sat, he also will wash his garments, and having washed with water, he will be unclean until evening.

**15:7** The one who touches his flesh will wash his garments, and having washed himself with water, he will be unclean until evening.

**15:8** If a man of this kind casts saliva on one who is clean, he will wash his garments, and having washed with water, he will be unclean until evening.

**15:9** The saddle on which he sits will be unclean,

**15:10** and whatever has been beneath him who suffers a flow of seed will be defiled until evening. The one who carries any of these things will wash his garments, and having washed himself with water, he will be unclean until evening.

**15:11** Everyone whom such a man touches without first washing his hands will wash his garments, and having washed with water, he will be unclean until evening.

**15:12** An earthenware vessel that he touches will be shattered, but a wooden vessel will be washed with water.

**15:13** If the one who endures an affliction of this kind is healed, he will count seven days after his cleansing, and when his garments and his whole body have been washed in living waters, he will be clean.

**15:14** And on the eighth day he will take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, and come into the presence of the Lord to the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony, and give them to the priest,

**15:15** who will prepare one for sin and the other as a burnt offering, and will pray for him before the Lord, so that he may be cleansed from the flow of his seed.

**15:16** A man from whom seed of intercourse goes out will wash his whole body with water, and he will be unclean until evening.

**15:17** He will wash with water the garment and leather that he has, and it will be unclean until evening.

**15:18** The woman with whom he has intercourse will wash herself with water, and she will be unclean until evening.

**15:19** A woman who suffers a flow of blood at the return of the month will be separated for seven days.

**15:20** Everyone who touches her will be unclean until evening,

**15:21** and whatever she sleeps or sits on during the days of her separation will be defiled.

**15:22** The one who touches her bed will wash his garments, and having washed himself with water, he will be unclean until evening.

**15:23** Whoever touches any article on which she sits will wash his garments, and having washed himself with water, he will be defiled until evening.

**15:24** If a man has intercourse with her at the time of menstrual blood, he will be unclean for seven days, and all bedding on which he sleeps will be defiled.

**15:25** A woman who suffers a flow of blood for many days not at the menstrual time, or who does not cease to flow after menstrual blood, will be unclean as long as she is subject to this affliction, as if she were in the menstrual time.

**15:26** All bedding on which she sleeps, and any article on which she sits, will be defiled.

**15:27** Whoever touches them will wash his garments, and having washed himself with water, he will be unclean until evening.

**15:28** If the blood stops and ceases to flow, she will count seven days of her purification,

**15:29** and on the eighth day she will offer for herself to the priest two turtledoves or two young pigeons, at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony,

**15:30** who will prepare one for sin and the other as a burnt offering, and will pray for her before the Lord, and for the flow of her uncleanness.'

**15:31** Therefore you will teach the sons of Israel to guard against uncleanness, and not die in their filth when they defile My tabernacle that is among them.

**15:32** This is the law of the one who suffers a flow of seed, and who is defiled by intercourse,

**15:33** and of her who is separated at menstrual times, or who flows with continual blood, and of the man who sleeps with her."

## Translator Notes

- **15:2** — Vulgate "fluxum seminis" ("a flow of seed") specifies a seminal discharge, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads more generally "a discharge from his flesh."
- **15:3** — Vulgate "when moment by moment a foul fluid clings to his flesh and congeals" is a distinctly clinical Latin rendering of the Hebrew clause describing a discharge that either runs or is stopped up.
- **15:10** — Vulgate omits the verb "touches" found in the Hebrew and Greek texts here, stating directly that whatever has been beneath the man is defiled until evening.
- **15:19** — Native Clementine Vulgate Leviticus 15:19-20 map to Saints.Bible 15:19 and 15:21-23 map to Saints.Bible 15:20-22 (Saints.Bible 15:23 has no counterpart in the Clementine Vulgate). Latin "vas" (v. 22) denotes any household article or furnishing.
- **15:23** — Latin "vas" (literally "vessel" or "utensil") is used broadly in the Vulgate for any household article or furnishing. The Vulgate divides Hebrew 15:19 into two verses, so its 15:21-23 correspond to Hebrew 15:20-22; it has no counterpart to Hebrew 15:23.
