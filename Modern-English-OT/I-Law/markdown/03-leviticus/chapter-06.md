## Text

**6:1** The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**6:2** "A soul who sins and, having despised the Lord, denies to his neighbor a deposit that had been committed to his trust, or extorts something by force, or makes a false accusation,

**6:3** or finds a lost item and, denying it, moreover swears falsely, and does any other of the many things in which people customarily sin,

**6:4** when convicted of the offense,

**6:5** he shall return in full everything that he wanted to obtain through fraud, and a fifth part in addition to the owner to whom he had caused the loss.

**6:6** But for his sin he shall offer an unblemished ram from the flock, and give it to the priest, according to the valuation and measure of the offense;

**6:7** who shall pray for him before the Lord, and it will be forgiven him for each of the things that he sinned by doing."

**6:8** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**6:9** "Command Aaron and his sons, 'This is the law of the burnt offering: it will be burned on the altar all night until morning; the fire will be from the same altar.

**6:10** The priest will clothe himself in a tunic and linen undergarments, and he will take up the ashes that the devouring fire has burned, and setting them beside the altar,

**6:11** he will take off the former garments, and clothed in others, he will carry them outside the camp, and in a very clean place he will cause them to be consumed down to fine ash.

**6:12** Now the fire on the altar will always burn, which the priest will feed, placing wood underneath in the morning each day; and with the burnt offering placed upon it, he will burn the fats of the peace offerings on top.

**6:13** This is the perpetual fire, which will never fail on the altar.

**6:14** This is the law of the sacrifice and of the libations, which the sons of Aaron will offer before the Lord and before the altar.

**6:15** The priest will take a handful of the fine flour that has been moistened with oil, and all the frankincense that has been placed on the fine flour, and he will burn it on the altar as a memorial of a very sweet fragrance to the Lord;

**6:16** but Aaron will eat the remaining part of the fine flour with his sons, without leaven, and he will eat it in the holy place of the courtyard of the tabernacle.

**6:17** Now it will not be leavened for this reason, because part of it is offered as a fire offering of the Lord. It will be most holy, just like that for sin and for trespass.

**6:18** Only the males of the lineage of Aaron will eat it. It will be a lawful and everlasting ordinance in your generations from the sacrifices of the Lord: everyone who touches them will be made holy.'"

**6:19** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**6:20** "This is the offering of Aaron and of his sons, which they ought to offer to the Lord on the day of their anointing. They will offer a tenth part of an ephah of fine flour as an everlasting sacrifice, half of it in the morning, and half of it in the evening,

**6:21** which, moistened with oil, will be fried in a frying pan. And it will be offered hot for a very sweet fragrance to the Lord

**6:22** by the priest who succeeds his father by right, and all of it will be burned on the altar.

**6:23** For every sacrifice of the priests will be consumed by fire, and no one will eat from it."

**6:24** Now the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

**6:25** "Speak to Aaron and his sons, 'This is the law of the sacrifice for sin: in the place where the burnt offering is offered, it will be sacrificed before the Lord. It is most holy.

**6:26** The priest who offers it will eat it in a holy place, in the courtyard of the tabernacle.

**6:27** Whatever touches its flesh will be made holy. If a garment is sprinkled with some of its blood, it will be washed in a holy place.

**6:28** Now a clay vessel in which it was cooked will be broken; but if it is a bronze vessel, it will be scoured and washed with water.

**6:29** Every male of the priestly family will eat from its flesh, because it is most holy.

**6:30** For a sacrifice that is killed for sin, whose blood is brought into the tabernacle of the testimony to make expiation in the sanctuary, will not be eaten, but will be burned up with fire.'"

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — In this versification (matching the Hebrew Masoretic Text), Leviticus 5 continues through 5:26; other versifications number 5:20-26 as 6:1-7. Native Vulgate Leviticus 6:1-7 map to Saints.Bible 5:20-26, and native 6:8-30 map to Saints.Bible 6:1-23.
- **6:8** — In native Clementine Vulgate numbering, this verse is 6:8 (mapped to Saints.Bible 6:1, while native 6:1-7 map to Saints 5:20-26).
- **6:11** — Vulgate distinctly adds "usque ad favillam consumi faciet" ("will cause them to be consumed down to fine ash"), distinguishing coarse charred ashes ("cineres", v. 3) from fine ash ("favillam"). Clementine "efferret" is an orthographic variant of future indicative "efferet".
- **6:17** — Vulgate "quia pars ejus in Domini offertur incensum" ("because part of it is offered as a fire offering of the Lord") differs from MT ("I have given it as their portion from My fire offerings"). Here and in 7:5, 25, Latin "incensum" (literally "what is kindled/burned") denotes a fire offering on the altar, distinct from "thus" ("frankincense", v. 8), and "Sanctum sanctorum" is literally "holy of holies" (a superlative idiom).
- **6:19** — In this versification (matching the Hebrew Masoretic Text), chapter 6 begins here; other versifications number this verse 6:8.
- **6:21** — Vulgate "calidam" ("hot") reflects a distinct reading from MT ("well-mixed" / "baked pieces"), and v. 15 "qui jure patri successerit" ("who succeeds his father by right") renders MT's "anointed priest in his place."
