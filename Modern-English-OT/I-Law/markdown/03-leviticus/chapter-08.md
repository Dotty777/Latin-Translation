## Text

**8:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,

**8:2** "Take Aaron with his sons, their garments and the anointing oil, a calf for sin, two rams, and a basket with unleavened bread;

**8:3** and you shall gather the whole assembly at the entrance of the tabernacle."

**8:4** Moses did as the Lord had commanded. And when the whole crowd had been gathered before the doors of the tabernacle,

**8:5** he said, "This is the word that the Lord has ordered to be done."

**8:6** And immediately he brought forward Aaron and his sons. And when he had washed them,

**8:7** he clothed the high priest in the linen under-tunic, fastening the sash around him and dressing him in the violet-blue tunic, and over it he placed the shoulder-vestment,

**8:8** which he bound tightly with the waistband and fitted to the breastpiece, in which was Doctrine and Truth.

**8:9** He also covered his head with the turban, and upon it, in front of his forehead, he placed the golden plate consecrated in sanctification, just as the Lord had commanded him.

**8:10** He also took the anointing oil, with which he anointed the tabernacle with all its furnishings.

**8:11** And when, sanctifying it, he had sprinkled the altar seven times, he anointed it and all its vessels, and the basin along with its base he sanctified with oil.

**8:12** Pouring this on Aaron's head, he anointed him and consecrated him.

**8:13** Having brought forward his sons as well, he clothed them in linen tunics, fastened sashes around them, and placed headdresses on them, as the Lord had ordered.

**8:14** He also brought forward the calf for sin; and when Aaron and his sons had placed their hands on its head,

**8:15** he sacrificed it, drawing the blood, and dipping his finger, he touched the horns of the altar all around; and when it had been purified and sanctified, he poured out the remaining blood at its foundations.

**8:16** But the fat that was on the internal organs, and the membrane of the liver, and the two kidneys with their suet, he burned on the altar,

**8:17** burning up the calf with its hide, and flesh, and dung outside the camp, just as the Lord had commanded.

**8:18** He also brought forward the ram for a burnt offering; and when Aaron and his sons had laid their hands on its head,

**8:19** he sacrificed it and poured out its blood all around the altar.

**8:20** And cutting the ram itself into pieces, he burned its head, its limbs, and its fat with fire,

**8:21** after first washing the intestines and the feet; and he burned the entire ram together on the altar, because it was a burnt offering of a most sweet fragrance to the Lord, just as He had commanded him.

**8:22** He also brought forward the second ram for the consecration of the priests, and Aaron and his sons placed their hands on its head.

**8:23** When Moses had sacrificed it, taking some of its blood, he touched the tip of Aaron's right ear, and the thumb of his right hand, and likewise the big toe of his foot.

**8:24** He also brought forward the sons of Aaron; and when with some of the blood of the sacrificed ram he had touched the tip of each one's right ear, and the thumbs of their right hands and the big toes of their right feet, he poured out the rest on the altar all around.

**8:25** But he separated the fat, and the tail, and all the fatness that covers the intestines, and the membrane of the liver, and the two kidneys with their fat and the right shoulder.

**8:26** And taking from the basket of unleavened bread that was before the Lord a loaf without leaven, and a cake mixed with oil, and a wafer, he placed them on the fat pieces and the right shoulder,

**8:27** delivering them all together to Aaron and his sons. And after they had lifted them up before the Lord,

**8:28** taking them back again from their hands, he burned them on the altar of burnt offering, because it was an offering of consecration, for a fragrance of sweetness, of a sacrifice to the Lord.

**8:29** And he took the breast, lifting it up before the Lord, from the ram of consecration as his portion, just as the Lord had commanded him.

**8:30** And taking the ointment and the blood that was on the altar, he sprinkled them on Aaron and his vestments, and on his sons and their garments.

**8:31** And when he had sanctified them in their attire, he commanded them, saying, "Cook the flesh before the doors of the tabernacle, and eat it there; eat also the loaves of consecration that have been placed in the basket, just as the Lord commanded me, saying, 'Aaron and his sons shall eat them.'

**8:32** But whatever remains of the flesh and the loaves, fire will consume.

**8:33** Also, you shall not go out from the entrance of the tabernacle for seven days, until the day on which the time of your consecration will be completed; for in seven days the consecration is finished,

**8:34** just as has also been done at the present time, so that the rite of the sacrifice might be completed.

**8:35** Day and night you shall remain in the tabernacle, keeping the watches of the Lord, so that you do not die; for so I have been commanded."

**8:36** And Aaron and his sons did everything that the Lord had spoken through the hand of Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **8:8** — The Vulgate uses humerale ("shoulder-vestment", v. 7) for the Hebrew ephod, rationale ("breastpiece", from Latin ratio, reflecting Greek logeion) for the breastpiece of judgment, and Doctrina et Veritas ("Doctrine and Truth") for the Hebrew Urim and Thummim. Syntactically, the Vulgate fits the shoulder-vestment (quod) to the breastpiece (rationali).
- **8:16** — Latin reticulum jecoris literally denotes the net-like membrane (caul or omentum) of the liver, where the Hebrew refers to the appendage or lobe of the liver.
- **8:25** — Latin armo dextro ("right shoulder", as also in 8:26 and 9:21) follows the Septuagint against the Hebrew "right thigh." Similarly, in 8:27, 8:29, and 9:21, the Vulgate uses verbs of lifting up (levaverunt / elevans) where the Hebrew specifies a wave offering.
- **8:34** — The Vulgate reads "just as has also been done at the present time, so that the rite of the sacrifice might be completed," where the Hebrew reads "As has been done this day, the Lord has commanded to do, to make atonement for you."
