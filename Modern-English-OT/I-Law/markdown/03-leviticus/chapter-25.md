## Text

**25:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai, saying,

**25:2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, 'When you have entered the land that I will give to you, you shall keep a sabbath to the Lord.

**25:3** For six years you shall sow your field, and for six years you shall prune your vineyard, and you shall gather its fruits;

**25:4** but in the seventh year there shall be a sabbath for the land, of the rest of the Lord: you shall not sow the field, and you shall not prune the vineyard.

**25:5** What the ground produces of its own accord you shall not reap, and the grapes of your firstfruits you shall not gather as a vintage, for it is a year of rest for the land;

**25:6** but they shall be for food to you: for you and your male servant, your female servant and your hired worker, and the strangers who sojourn with you;

**25:7** to your beasts of burden and livestock, everything that grows shall provide food.

**25:8** You shall also count for yourself seven weeks of years, that is, seven times seven, which together make forty-nine years;

**25:9** and you shall sound the trumpet in the seventh month, on the tenth day of the month, at the time of propitiation, throughout all your land.

**25:10** And you shall sanctify the fiftieth year, and proclaim release to all the inhabitants of your land, for it is the jubilee. A man shall return to his possession, and each one shall go back to his former family,

**25:11** because it is the jubilee and the fiftieth year. You shall not sow, nor shall you reap what grows of its own accord in the field, and you shall not gather the firstfruits of the vintage,

**25:12** on account of the sanctification of the jubilee, but you shall immediately eat what is presented.

**25:13** In the year of jubilee, all shall return to their possessions.

**25:14** When you sell anything to your fellow citizen, or buy from him, do not grieve your brother, but according to the number of years of the jubilee you shall buy from him,

**25:15** and according to the reckoning of the crops he shall sell to you.

**25:16** The more years that remain after the jubilee, the more the price shall also increase; and the less time you count, the less the purchase shall also cost, for he shall sell to you the time of the crops.

**25:17** Do not afflict your fellow tribesmen, but let each one fear his God, because I am the Lord your God.

**25:18** Do My precepts, and keep the judgments, and fulfill them, so that you may dwell in the land without any dread,

**25:19** and the ground may produce for you its fruits, which you may eat to fullness, fearing the attack of no one.

**25:20** And if you say, "What shall we eat in the seventh year, if we have not sown nor gathered our crops?"

**25:21** I will give My blessing to you in the sixth year, and it shall produce the fruits of three years;

**25:22** and you shall sow in the eighth year, and you shall eat the old crops until the ninth year; until the new grow, you shall eat the old.

**25:23** The land also shall not be sold in perpetuity, because it is Mine, and you are strangers and tenants of Mine;

**25:24** therefore the entire region of your possession shall be sold under the condition of redemption.

**25:25** If your impoverished brother sells his small possession, and his near relative is willing, he may redeem what the other had sold.

**25:26** But if he does not have a next of kin, and he himself is able to find the price to redeem it,

**25:27** the fruits shall be calculated from the time when he sold it, and what remains he shall pay back to the buyer, and thus he shall recover his possession.

**25:28** But if his hand does not find enough to pay back the price, the buyer shall hold what he had bought until the jubilee year. For in that year every sale shall return to the owner and to the former possessor.

**25:29** Whoever sells a house within the walls of a city shall have the right to redeem it until one year is completed.

**25:30** If he does not redeem it, and the cycle of the year has elapsed, the buyer shall possess it, and his descendants in perpetuity, and it will not be able to be redeemed, even in the jubilee.

**25:31** But if a house is in a village that has no walls, it shall be sold by the law of fields; if it has not been redeemed beforehand, in the jubilee it shall return to the owner.

**25:32** The houses of the Levites that are in the cities may always be redeemed;

**25:33** if they have not been redeemed, in the jubilee they shall return to the owners, because the houses of the cities of the Levites are in place of possessions among the sons of Israel.

**25:34** But their outlying lands shall not be sold, because it is a perpetual possession.

**25:35** If your brother becomes impoverished and weak in hand, and you receive him as a stranger and a sojourner, and he lives with you,

**25:36** do not take interest from him, nor more than you gave; fear your God, so that your brother may live with you.

**25:37** You shall not give your money to him at interest, and you shall not demand a surplus of crops.

**25:38** I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, so that I might give you the land of Canaan and be your God.

**25:39** If, compelled by poverty, your brother sells himself to you, you shall not oppress him with the servitude of slaves,

**25:40** but he shall be like a hired worker and a tenant; until the jubilee year he shall work with you,

**25:41** and afterward he shall go out with his children, and return to his kindred, to the possession of his fathers.

**25:42** For they are My servants, and I brought them out of the land of Egypt; they shall not be sold under the condition of slaves;

**25:43** do not afflict him by force, but fear your God.

**25:44** Let your male slave and female slave be from the nations that are around you;

**25:45** and from the strangers who sojourn among you, or those who have been born from them in your land, these you shall have as slaves;

**25:46** and by hereditary right you shall hand them down to your descendants and possess them forever; but do not oppress your brothers, the sons of Israel, by force.

**25:47** If the hand of a stranger and sojourner grows strong among you, and your impoverished brother sells himself to him, or to anyone of his lineage,

**25:48** after the sale he may be redeemed. Whoever wishes from among his brothers shall redeem him,

**25:49** and a paternal uncle, and a paternal cousin, and a blood relative, and a relative by marriage. But if he himself is also able, he shall redeem himself,

**25:50** provided only that the years are reckoned from the time of his sale until the jubilee year, and the money for which he had been sold is reckoned according to the number of years and the rate of a hired worker.

**25:51** If there are more years that remain until the jubilee, according to these he shall also pay back the price;

**25:52** if few, he shall make a reckoning with him according to the number of years, and he shall pay back to the buyer what remains of the years,

**25:53** with the wages for which he previously served being credited; he shall not afflict him violently in your sight.

**25:54** But if he cannot be redeemed by these means, in the jubilee year he shall go out with his children.

**25:55** For the sons of Israel are My servants, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt.'"

## Translator Notes

- **25:2** — The Latin text reads second-person singular sabbatizes ("you shall keep a sabbath"), anticipating the singular address of verse 3, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text (and some Latin witnesses reading sabbatizet) makes the land the subject ("the land shall keep a sabbath").
- **25:5** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew term for unpruned or consecrated vines (nazir) as uvas primitiarum tuarum ("the grapes of your firstfruits"; compare primitias vindemiae, "the firstfruits of the vintage," in verse 11).
- **25:12** — The Latin statim oblata comedetis ("you shall immediately eat what is presented") refers to eating directly what the land yields without harvesting and storing it, rendering the Hebrew "you shall eat its produce from the field."
- **25:35** — In the Vulgate syntax, "and you receive him ... and he lives with you" remains part of the conditional clause leading into the prohibition of verse 36, whereas the Hebrew makes "you shall uphold him" the main command of verse 35.
- **25:53** — The Vulgate renders the opening clause as quibus ante servivit mercedibus imputatis ("with the wages for which he previously served being credited"), differing from the Hebrew "like a hired worker year by year he shall be with him."
