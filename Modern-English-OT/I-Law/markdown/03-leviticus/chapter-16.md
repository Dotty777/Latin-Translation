## Text

**16:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses after the death of the two sons of Aaron, when they were killed while offering strange fire,

**16:2** and commanded him, saying: "Speak to Aaron your brother, that he not enter at all times into the sanctuary that is inside the veil before the mercy seat by which the ark is covered, so that he may not die (for I will appear in a cloud over the oracle),

**16:3** unless he has done these things beforehand: he will offer a calf for sin, and a ram as a burnt offering.

**16:4** He will be clothed with a linen tunic, he will conceal his private parts with linen undergarments; he will be girded with a linen sash, he will place a linen turban on his head; for these are holy garments, in all of which he will dress himself when he has washed.

**16:5** And he will receive from the entire multitude of the sons of Israel two male goats for sin, and one ram as a burnt offering.

**16:6** And when he has offered the calf, and has prayed for himself and for his household,

**16:7** he will make the two male goats stand before the Lord at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony,

**16:8** and casting a lot over each of the two, one for the Lord, the other for the emissary goat.

**16:9** The one whose lot comes out for the Lord, he will offer it for sin;

**16:10** but the one whose lot is for the emissary goat, he will set it alive before the Lord, so that he may pour out prayers over it and send it out into the wilderness.

**16:11** When these things have been duly performed, he will offer the calf, and praying for himself and for his household, he will sacrifice it,

**16:12** and taking the censer, which he has filled from the live coals of the altar, and scooping up with his hand the compounded perfume for incense, he will enter beyond the veil into the holy place,

**16:13** so that, when the aromatics are placed on the fire, their cloud and vapor may cover the oracle that is above the testimony, and he may not die.

**16:14** He will also take some of the blood of the calf, and sprinkle with his finger seven times facing the mercy seat toward the east.

**16:15** And when he has slaughtered the male goat for the sin of the people, he will bring its blood inside the veil, just as was commanded concerning the blood of the calf, so that he may sprinkle it opposite the oracle,

**16:16** and expiate the sanctuary from the uncleannesses of the sons of Israel, and from their transgressions and all their sins. According to this rite he will do for the tabernacle of the testimony, which is pitched among them in the midst of the filth of their dwelling.

**16:17** Let no person be in the tabernacle when the high priest enters the sanctuary to pray for himself, and for his household, and for the entire assembly of Israel, until he comes out.

**16:18** And when he has gone out to the altar that is before the Lord, let him pray for himself, and having taken the blood of the calf and of the male goat, let him pour it on its horns all around;

**16:19** and sprinkling with his finger seven times, let him expiate and sanctify it from the uncleannesses of the sons of Israel.

**16:20** After he has cleansed the sanctuary, and the tabernacle, and the altar, then let him offer the living male goat;

**16:21** and having placed both hands on its head, let him confess all the iniquities of the sons of Israel, and all their offenses and sins; invoking these upon its head, he will send it out by a prepared man into the desert.

**16:22** And when the male goat has carried all their iniquities into a solitary land, and has been released in the desert,

**16:23** Aaron will return into the tabernacle of the testimony, and having taken off the garments with which he had previously been clothed when he entered the sanctuary, and having left them there,

**16:24** he will wash his flesh in a holy place, and will dress himself in his own garments. And after he has gone out and offered his own burnt offering and that of the people, he will pray both for himself and for the people,

**16:25** and he will burn on the altar the fat that was offered for sins.

**16:26** And the one who has released the emissary goat will wash his garments and his body with water, and thus he will enter into the camp.

**16:27** And they will carry outside the camp the calf and the male goat that had been sacrificed for sin, and whose blood was brought into the sanctuary so that the expiation might be completed, and they will burn up with fire both their hides and their flesh, and their dung;

**16:28** and whoever has burned them up will wash his garments and his flesh with water, and thus he will enter into the camp.

**16:29** And this will be an everlasting statute for you: in the seventh month, on the tenth day of the month, you will afflict your souls and do no work, whether a native or a foreigner who sojourns among you.

**16:30** On this day there will be expiation for you, and cleansing from all your sins; before the Lord you will be cleansed.

**16:31** For it is a Sabbath of rest, and you will afflict your souls by a perpetual observance.

**16:32** And the priest who has been anointed, and whose hands have been consecrated to perform the priesthood in place of his father, will make expiation; and he will be clothed with a linen robe and holy garments,

**16:33** and he will expiate the sanctuary, and the tabernacle of the testimony, and the altar, the priests also, and the entire people.

**16:34** And this will be an everlasting statute for you, that you pray for the sons of Israel, and for all their sins, once a year." Therefore he did just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — Vulgate "when they were killed while offering strange fire" explicitly recalls the incident of Leviticus 10:1, whereas the Hebrew reads "when they drew near before the Lord and died."
- **16:2** — Throughout this chapter the Vulgate alternates between "propitiatorium" ("mercy seat", vv. 2, 14) and "oraculum" ("oracle", vv. 2, 13, 15) to render the single Hebrew term for the cover of the ark.
- **16:8** — Vulgate "capro emissario" ("for the emissary goat") interprets Hebrew "la'azazel" ("for Azazel") as a goat designated to be sent away; see also verses 10 and 26.
- **16:10** — Vulgate "ut fundat preces super eo" ("so that he may pour out prayers over it") renders the Hebrew verb for making atonement in terms of intercessory prayer, a distinctive Vulgate pattern also reflected in verses 6, 11, 17, 18, 24, and 34.
- **16:18** — Vulgate "oret pro se" ("let him pray for himself") differs from the Hebrew, which speaks of making atonement for the altar itself.
