## Text

**9:1** "God of my fathers, and Lord of mercy, who made all things by Your word,

**9:2** and by Your wisdom established man, so that he might rule over the creation that was made by You,

**9:3** that he might govern the world in equity and justice, and in uprightness of heart render judgment:

**9:4** give me wisdom, who sits beside Your thrones, and do not reject me from among Your children;

**9:5** for I am Your servant and the son of Your maidservant, a weak man and of short time, and too little for the understanding of judgment and laws.

**9:6** For even if anyone is perfected among the sons of men, if Your wisdom is absent from him, he will be counted as nothing.

**9:7** You chose me as king for Your people, and as judge of Your sons and daughters;

**9:8** and You told me to build a temple on Your holy mountain, and an altar in the city of Your dwelling, a likeness of Your holy tabernacle, which You prepared from the beginning.

**9:9** And with You is Your wisdom, who knows Your works, and who was also present then when You made the world, and knew what was pleasing to Your eyes and what was upright in Your commandments.

**9:10** Send her from Your holy heavens, and from the throne of Your greatness, so that she may be with me and labor with me, so that I may know what is acceptable before You;

**9:11** for she knows all things and understands, and she will guide me soberly in my works, and will guard me in her power.

**9:12** And my works will be acceptable, and I will govern Your people justly, and I will be worthy of the thrones of my father.

**9:13** For who among men will be able to know the counsel of God? Or who will be able to conceive what God wills?

**9:14** For the thoughts of mortals are timid, and our foresights are uncertain;

**9:15** for the body that decays burdens the soul, and the earthly dwelling weighs down the mind that ponders many things.

**9:16** And with difficulty we estimate the things that are on earth, and with labor we find the things that are in view; but who will search out the things that are in the heavens?

**9:17** And who will know Your mind, unless You give wisdom and send Your Holy Spirit from the highest places,

**9:18** and thus the paths of those who are on earth are set straight, and men learn the things that please You?

**9:19** For through wisdom all who pleased You, Lord, from the beginning were healed."

## Translator Notes

- **9:4** — Both here (sedium tuarum) and in 9:12 (sedium patris mei), the Vulgate preserves the plural "thrones" (a majestic plural matching Greek thronon), whereas 9:10 uses the singular sede.
- **9:10** — Latin magnitudinis tuae ("of Your greatness") is a Vulgate-distinct reading where the Greek Septuagint has doxes sou ("of Your glory"); similarly, 9:11 reads potentia ("power") where Greek has doxe ("glory").
- **9:16** — Latin in prospectu ("in view") is a Vulgate-distinct reading where the Greek Septuagint has en chersin ("in our hands" or "close at hand").
- **9:18** — Native Vulgate splits Saints/Greek 9:18 across native 9:18 and 9:19 (closing Solomon's prayer opened in 9:1 at the end of 9:19).
- **9:19** — Native Vulgate 9:19 corresponds to the closing clause of Saints/Greek 9:18. Latin sanati sunt ("were healed") and quicumque placuerunt tibi, Domine, a principio ("all who pleased You, Lord, from the beginning") are Vulgate-distinct readings compared with the shorter Greek Septuagint text ("by wisdom they were saved").
