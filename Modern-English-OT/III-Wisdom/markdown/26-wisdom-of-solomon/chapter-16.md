## Text

**16:1** Because of these things, they also deservedly suffered torments through things similar to these, and through a multitude of beasts they were destroyed.

**16:2** Instead of these torments, You treated Your people well, to whom You gave the desire of their delight, a new taste, preparing quail for them as food,

**16:3** so that those indeed, desiring food, because of the things that were shown and sent to them might be turned away even from necessary desire. These, however, having become needy for a short time, tasted a new food.

**16:4** For it was necessary indeed for destruction without excuse to come upon those exercising tyranny, yet only to show to these how their enemies were being destroyed.

**16:5** For indeed, when the fierce wrath of beasts came upon them, they were being destroyed by the bites of crooked serpents.

**16:6** But Your wrath did not remain forever; rather, for correction they were troubled for a short time, having a sign of salvation as a reminder of the commandment of Your law.

**16:7** For the one who turned was not healed through what he saw, but through You, the Savior of all.

**16:8** And in this You showed our enemies that it is You who deliver from every evil.

**16:9** For the bites of locusts and flies killed them, and no healing was found for their soul, because they were worthy to be destroyed by such things.

**16:10** Yet not even the teeth of venomous dragons overcame Your children, for Your mercy came and healed them.

**16:11** For in the remembrance of Your words they were examined and quickly saved, so that, falling into deep forgetfulness, they might not be unable to make use of Your help.

**16:12** For indeed neither herb nor poultice healed them, but Your word, Lord, which heals all things.

**16:13** For it is You, Lord, who have authority over life and death, and lead down to the gates of death, and bring back.

**16:14** A man, however, indeed kills through malice; and when the spirit has gone out, it will not return, nor will he call back the soul that has been received.

**16:15** But it is impossible to escape Your hand.

**16:16** For the ungodly, denying that they knew You, were scourged by the strength of Your arm, suffering persecution by unusual waters and hailstorms and rains, and consumed by fire.

**16:17** For, what was marvelous, in water, which extinguishes all things, fire prevailed even more; for the world is the defender of the righteous.

**16:18** For at one time the fire was tamed, so that the animals that had been sent to the ungodly would not be burned up, but so that they themselves, upon seeing, might know that they are suffering persecution by the judgment of God.

**16:19** And at another time, in water, fire blazed up on every side beyond its power, so that it might destroy the nation of a wicked land.

**16:20** Instead of these things, You nourished Your people with the food of angels, and provided for them prepared bread from heaven without labor, having every delight in itself and the pleasantness of every taste.

**16:21** For Your substance showed Your sweetness, which You have toward children; and serving the will of each one, it was turned into whatever each one wished.

**16:22** Snow and ice, however, endured the force of fire and did not melt, so that they might know that fire, burning in hail and flashing in rain, was destroying the fruits of the enemies;

**16:23** yet this fire again, so that the righteous might be nourished, forgot even its own power.

**16:24** For creation, serving You, the Maker, blazes up into torment against the unrighteous, and becomes gentler to do good on behalf of those who trust in You.

**16:25** Because of this, at that time also, transformed into all things, it served Your grace, the nourisher of all, according to the will of those who asked of You,

**16:26** so that Your children whom You loved, Lord, might know that it is not the fruits of growth that feed people, but Your word preserves those who believe in You.

**16:27** For what could not be destroyed by fire immediately melted when warmed by a slight ray of the sun,

**16:28** so that it might be known to all that it is necessary to anticipate the sun to bless You, and to worship You at the rising of the light.

**16:29** For the hope of the ungrateful will melt away like winter ice, and will perish like useless water.

## Translator Notes

- **16:3** — Vulgate 'propter ea quae illis ostensa et missa sunt' ("because of the things that were shown and sent to them") is a distinct Latin reading where the Greek has "because of the loathsomeness of the creatures sent upon them."
- **16:11** — Vulgate 'examinabantur' ("they were examined/tested") differs from the Greek reading, which means "they were stung/pricked."
- **16:13** — The Vulgate reads 'portas mortis' ("the gates of death") where the Greek has "the gates of Hades."
- **16:19** — Vulgate 'iniquae terrae nationem' ("the nation of a wicked land") renders Greek 'adikou ges genemata' ("the produce/crops of an unrighteous land"), taking 'genema' in the sense of offspring or nation.
- **16:21** — Latin 'Substantia' (rendering Greek 'hypostasis') literally means "substance" and can also carry the sense "sustenance," referring here to the manna.
