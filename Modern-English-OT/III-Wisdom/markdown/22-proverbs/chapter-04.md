## Text

**4:1** Listen, sons, to a father's discipline, and pay attention so that you may know discernment.

**4:2** I will bestow a good gift on you; do not abandon my law.

**4:3** For I too was a son of my father, tender and an only child before my mother.

**4:4** And he taught me and said, "Let your heart accept my words; guard my precepts, and you will live.

**4:5** Possess wisdom, possess discernment; do not forget, nor turn aside from the words of my mouth.

**4:6** Do not let her go, and she will guard you; love her, and she will preserve you.

**4:8** Seize her, and she will exalt you; you will be glorified by her when you have embraced her.

**4:9** She will give an increase of graces to your head, and she will shield you with a glorious crown."

**4:10** Listen, my son, and accept my words, so that years of life may be multiplied for you.

**4:11** I will show you the way of wisdom; I will lead you through paths of equity.

**4:12** When you have entered them, your steps will not be hemmed in, and as you run you will have no stumbling block.

**4:13** Hold fast to discipline, do not let it go; guard it, for it is your life.

**4:14** Do not delight in the paths of the wicked, nor let the way of evil people please you.

**4:15** Flee from it, and do not pass through it; turn aside, and desert it.

**4:16** For they do not sleep unless they have done evil, and sleep is snatched away from them unless they have tripped someone up.

**4:17** They eat the bread of wickedness, and they drink the wine of injustice.

**4:18** But the path of the righteous goes forward like a radiant light, and grows until the perfect day.

**4:19** The way of the wicked is dark; they do not know where they may fall.

**4:20** My son, listen closely to my words, and bend your ear to my utterances.

**4:21** Do not let them depart from your eyes; guard them in the center of your heart,

**4:22** because they are life for those who find them, and health for all flesh.

**4:23** With every safeguard preserve your heart, because from it life proceeds.

**4:24** Remove a crooked mouth from yourself, and let slandering lips be far from you.

**4:25** Let your eyes see what is straight, and let your eyelids go ahead of your steps.

**4:26** Direct the path for your feet, and all your ways will be made firm.

**4:27** Do not turn aside to the right or to the left; turn your foot away from evil. For the Lord knows the ways that are on the right, but those that are on the left are perverse. He Himself, however, will make your courses straight, and He will lead your journeys forward in peace.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**4:7** *(after `4:6`)* The beginning of wisdom: gain wisdom, and with all you possess acquire prudence.

## Translator Notes

- **4:2** — Vulgate 'Donum bonum' ("a good gift") follows the Septuagint rather than the Hebrew ("good teaching").
- **4:9** — Vulgate 'augmenta gratiarum' ("an increase of graces") reflects deriving the Hebrew term for "garland" from a root meaning "to add/join," and 'corona inclyta proteget te' uses an ablative of means ("shield you with a glorious crown") matching the Septuagint.
- **4:27** — The second half of the verse ("For the Lord knows the ways that are on the right ... lead your journeys forward in peace") is a major Vulgate expansion derived from the Septuagint and absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
