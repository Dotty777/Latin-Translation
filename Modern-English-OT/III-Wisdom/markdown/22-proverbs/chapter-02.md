## Text

**2:1** My son, if you receive my words, and hide my commandments within yourself,

**2:2** so that your ear may hear wisdom, incline your heart to come to know prudence.

**2:3** For if you call upon wisdom, and incline your heart to prudence;

**2:4** if you seek her like money, and dig her up like treasures,

**2:5** then you will understand the fear of the Lord, and you will find the knowledge of God,

**2:6** because the Lord gives wisdom, and from His mouth come prudence and knowledge.

**2:7** He will guard the salvation of the upright, and protect those who walk guilelessly,

**2:8** preserving the paths of righteousness, and guarding the ways of the saints.

**2:9** Then you will understand righteousness, judgment, equity, and every good path.

**2:10** If wisdom enters your heart, and knowledge pleases your soul,

**2:11** counsel will guard you, and prudence will preserve you,

**2:12** so that you may be rescued from the evil way, and from the man who speaks perverse things;

**2:13** who abandon the straight road, and walk through dark ways;

**2:14** who rejoice when they have done evil, and exult in the worst things;

**2:15** whose ways are perverse, and their steps are disreputable.

**2:16** So that you may be rescued from a strange woman, and from an outsider who softens her words,

**2:17** and abandons the guide of her youth,

**2:18** and has forgotten the covenant of her God. For her house leans toward death, and her paths toward the underworld.

**2:19** None who go in to her will return, nor will they lay hold of the paths of life.

**2:20** So that you may walk in the good way, and keep the paths of the righteous:

**2:21** for those who are upright will dwell in the land, and the guileless will remain in it;

**2:22** but the ungodly will be destroyed from the land, and those who act unjustly will be taken away from it.

## Translator Notes

- **2:3** — The Vulgate reads sapientiam ("wisdom") and inclinaveris cor tuum prudentiae ("incline your heart to prudence"), whereas the Hebrew has "call out for discernment" and "raise your voice for understanding."
- **2:7** — Vulgate salutem ("salvation" or "safety") corresponds to Hebrew "sound wisdom," and simpliciter (like simplices in 2:21) means "guilelessly" or with unmixed integrity, distinct from the naive parvuli ("little ones") of chapter 1.
- **2:16** — Latin mulier aliena ("strange woman") and extranea ("outsider") refer to a woman outside one's lawful marriage bond, traditionally understood as an adulteress.
- **2:18** — The Clementine Vulgate places "and has forgotten the covenant of her God" at the beginning of 2:18 rather than at the end of 2:17 as in Hebrew versification; inferos literally denotes "those below" or the underworld.
