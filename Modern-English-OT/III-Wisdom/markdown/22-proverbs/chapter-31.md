## Text

**31:1** The words of King Lemuel. The vision by which his mother instructed him.

**31:2** What, my beloved? What, beloved of my womb? What, beloved of my vows?

**31:3** Do not give your substance to women, or your wealth for destroying kings.

**31:4** Do not give wine to kings, Lemuel, do not give wine to kings, because there is no secret where drunkenness reigns;

**31:5** and so that they might not drink and forget judgments, and alter the cause of the children of the poor.

**31:6** Give strong drink to those who mourn, and wine to those who are bitter in spirit.

**31:7** Let them drink and forget their poverty, and no longer remember their grief.

**31:8** Open your mouth for the mute, and for the causes of all the children who pass through.

**31:9** Open your mouth, decide what is just, and judge the needy and the poor.

**31:10** Who will find a valiant woman? Far off and from the farthest borders is her worth.

**31:11** Her husband's heart trusts in her, and he will not lack spoils.

**31:12** She will return good to him, and not evil, all the days of her life.

**31:13** She sought wool and flax, and carried out the plans of her hands.

**31:14** She became like a merchant's ship, carrying her bread from afar.

**31:15** And she rose in the night, and gave prey to those of her household, and food to her maidservants.

**31:16** She examined a field and bought it; from the fruit of her hands she planted a vineyard.

**31:17** She girded her loins with strength, and made her arm strong.

**31:18** She tasted and saw that her business is good; her lamp will not be extinguished in the night.

**31:19** She put her hand to strong things, and her fingers grasped the spindle.

**31:20** She opened her hand to the needy, and extended her palms to the poor.

**31:21** She will not fear for her house because of the cold of snow, for all those of her household are dressed in double garments.

**31:22** She made a tapestry garment for herself; fine linen and purple are her clothing.

**31:23** Her husband is noble in the gates, when he sits with the senators of the land.

**31:24** She made linen cloth and sold it, and delivered a belt to the Canaanite.

**31:25** Strength and splendor are her clothing, and she will laugh on the last day.

**31:26** She opened her mouth to wisdom, and the law of mercy is on her tongue.

**31:27** She examined the paths of her house, and did not eat bread in idleness.

**31:28** Her children rose up and proclaimed her most blessed; her husband also, and he praised her.

**31:29** "Many daughters have gathered wealth; you have surpassed all of them."

**31:30** Charm is deceptive, and beauty is empty: a woman who fears the Lord, she herself will be praised.

**31:31** Give to her from the fruit of her hands, and let her deeds praise her in the gates.

## Translator Notes

- **31:4** — Latin quia nullum secretum est ubi regnat ebrietas ("because there is no secret where drunkenness reigns") is a Vulgate-distinct reading; the Hebrew Masoretic Text instead speaks of rulers desiring strong drink.
- **31:10** — Latin procul et de ultimis finibus ("far off and from the farthest borders") is a Vulgate-distinct rendering where the Hebrew text reads "far above jewels/corals"; Mulierem fortem literally means "a strong/valiant woman."
- **31:19** — Latin ad fortia ("to strong things") preserves a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to the distaff.
- **31:21** — Latin duplicibus ("in double garments") reflects reading the Hebrew consonantal text as "double" rather than "scarlet," a well-known reading shared by the Vulgate and the Septuagint.
- **31:24** — Latin Chananæo ("to the Canaanite") renders the Hebrew kena'ani literally by the proper name "Canaanite," a term also used idiomatically for Phoenician traders and merchants.
- **31:25** — Native Vulgate 31:25 ('Fortitudo et decor...') matches Hebrew 31:25 (mapped to Saints 31:26 / Greek 31:25); Stage 1 corrected the prior CT swap of 31:25 and 31:26.
- **31:26** — Native Vulgate 31:26 ('Os suum aperuit sapientiæ...') matches Hebrew 31:26; Saints.Bible parallel display joins it to 31:28.
- **31:28** — Native Vulgate 31:28 contains only 'Surrexerunt filii ejus...'; 'She opened her mouth to wisdom...' stands at its native Vulgate slot 31:26 (and 'Strength and splendor...' at native 31:25), correcting the earlier critical-text swap. Parallel Saints.Bible display maps native 31:26 into Saints 31:28.
