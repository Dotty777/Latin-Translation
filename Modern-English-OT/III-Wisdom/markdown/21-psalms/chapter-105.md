## Text

**105:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord, because He is good, because His mercy lasts forever.

**105:2** Who will speak of the mighty acts of the Lord; who will make all His praises heard?

**105:3** Blessed are those who guard justice and do righteousness at all times.

**105:4** Remember us, Lord, in the favor toward Your people; visit us with Your salvation,

**105:5** to look upon the goodness of Your chosen ones, to rejoice in the joy of Your nation, so that You may be praised with Your inheritance.

**105:6** We have sinned with our fathers; we have acted unjustly; we have committed iniquity.

**105:7** Our fathers in Egypt did not understand Your wonders; they were not mindful of the abundance of Your mercy. And they provoked Him as they went up into the sea, the Red Sea;

**105:8** and He saved them because of His name, to make His might known.

**105:9** And He rebuked the Red Sea, and it dried up; and He led them in the depths as in a desert.

**105:10** And He saved them out of the hand of those who hated them, and He redeemed them out of the hand of the enemy.

**105:11** And the water covered those who oppressed them; not one of them remained.

**105:12** And they believed His words, and they praised His praise.

**105:13** They acted quickly; they forgot His works, and they did not wait for His counsel.

**105:14** And they craved a craving in the desert, and they tested God in a waterless place.

**105:15** And He gave them their petition, and He sent satiety into their souls.

**105:16** And they provoked Moses in the camp, and Aaron, the holy one of the Lord.

**105:17** The earth opened up and swallowed Dathan, and it covered over the congregation of Abiram.

**105:18** And a fire blazed up in their assembly; a flame burned up the sinners.

**105:19** And they made a calf at Horeb, and they worshiped a carved image.

**105:20** And they exchanged their glory for the likeness of a calf eating grass.

**105:21** They forgot God, who saved them, who performed great deeds in Egypt,

**105:22** wonders in the land of Ham, fearsome deeds at the Red Sea.

**105:23** And He said that He would destroy them, had not Moses, His chosen one, stood in the breach in His sight to turn away His wrath, so that He would not destroy them.

**105:24** And they regarded the desirable land as nothing; they did not believe His word.

**105:25** And they murmured in their tents; they did not heed the voice of the Lord.

**105:26** And He raised His hand over them to strike them down in the desert,

**105:27** and to cast down their seed among the nations, and to scatter them in the regions.

**105:28** And they were initiated to Baal-peor, and they ate the sacrifices of the dead.

**105:29** And they provoked Him with their contrivances, and ruin was multiplied among them.

**105:30** And Phinehas stood up and appeased Him, and the scourge ceased.

**105:31** And it was credited to him as righteousness, to generation and generation, forever.

**105:32** And they provoked Him at the waters of contradiction, and Moses was distressed because of them,

**105:33** because they embittered his spirit, and he made a distinction with his lips.

**105:34** They did not destroy the nations about which the Lord spoke to them,

**105:35** and they were mingled among the nations, and they learned their works;

**105:36** and they served their carved images, and it became a stumbling block to them.

**105:37** And they offered their sons and their daughters in sacrifice to demons.

**105:38** And they poured out innocent blood, the blood of their sons and their daughters, whom they sacrificed to the carved images of Canaan. And the land was stained with bloodshed,

**105:39** and it was defiled by their works, and they committed fornication in their contrivances.

**105:40** And the Lord was angry with fury against His people, and He detested His inheritance.

**105:41** And He handed them over into the hands of the nations, and those who hated them held dominion over them.

**105:42** And their enemies oppressed them, and they were humbled beneath their hands;

**105:43** often He delivered them. Yet they themselves embittered Him in their counsel, and they were humbled in their iniquities.

**105:44** And He saw when they were oppressed, and He heard their prayer.

**105:45** And He was mindful of His covenant, and He repented according to the abundance of His mercy,

**105:46** and He gave them over to mercies in the sight of all who had captured them.

**105:47** Save us, Lord our God, and gather us out of the nations, so that we may give thanks to Your holy name and boast in Your praise.

**105:48** Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, from eternity even to eternity; and all the people will say, "So be it, so be it."

## Translator Notes

- **105:5** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate has plural "us" in v. 4 and the second-person passive "ut lauderis" ("so that You may be praised") here, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Ps 106:4-5) uses first-person singular ("Remember me... that I may glory").
- **105:7** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads "ascendentes in mare" ("as they went up into the sea"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "by the sea."
- **105:15** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads "saturitatem" ("satiety" or "fullness," implying surfeit as in Num 11:20), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "leanness" or "a wasting disease."
- **105:33** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads "distinxit in labiis suis" ("made a distinction with his lips"), traditionally taken to mean speaking hesitantly or waveringly, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "spoke rashly."
- **105:48** — The Vulgate uses the future indicative "dicet" ("will say") and, following the Septuagint, reads "Fiat, fiat" ("So be it, so be it") where the Hebrew has "Amen. Hallelujah."
