## Text

**43:1** To the end. For the sons of Korah, for understanding.

**43:2** God, with our ears we have heard; our fathers have declared to us the work that You worked in their days and in ancient days.

**43:3** Your hand destroyed the nations, and You planted them; You afflicted the peoples and drove them out.

**43:4** For they did not possess the land by their sword, and their arm did not save them; but Your right hand and Your arm, and the illumination of Your face, because You were well pleased with them.

**43:5** You Yourself are my King and my God, who command the deliverances of Jacob.

**43:6** In You we will toss our enemies with the horn, and in Your name we will spurn those who rise up against us.

**43:7** For I will not hope in my bow, and my sword will not save me;

**43:8** for You have saved us from those who afflict us, and You have put to shame those who hate us.

**43:9** In God we will be praised all day long, and in Your name we will give thanks forever.

**43:10** But now You have rejected us and put us to shame, and You will not go out, God, with our armies.

**43:11** You have turned us backward behind our enemies, and those who hate us were plundering for themselves.

**43:12** You have given us like sheep for food, and You have scattered us among the nations.

**43:13** You have sold Your people without a price, and there was no abundance in their exchanges.

**43:14** You have made us a reproach to our neighbors, a mockery and a derision to those who are all around us.

**43:15** You have made us a comparison to the nations, a shaking of the head among the peoples.

**43:16** All day long my disgrace is before me, and the shame of my face has covered me,

**43:17** from the voice of one who reproaches and reviles, from the face of the enemy and persecutor.

**43:18** All these things have come upon us, and we have not forgotten You, and we have not acted unjustly in Your covenant.

**43:19** And our heart has not drawn back, and You have turned aside our paths from Your way,

**43:20** because You have humbled us in the place of affliction, and the shadow of death has covered us.

**43:21** If we have forgotten the name of our God, and if we have stretched out our hands to a foreign god,

**43:23** will God not search out these things? For He Himself knows the hidden things of the heart. Because for Your sake we are put to death all day long; we have been reckoned like sheep for slaughter.

**43:24** Arise; why do You sleep, Lord? Arise, and do not reject us forever.

**43:25** Why do You turn Your face away? Do You forget our poverty and our distress?

**43:26** For our soul has been humbled in the dust; our belly has clung fast to the earth.

**43:27** Arise, Lord, help us, and redeem us for the sake of Your name.

## Translator Notes

- **43:9** — Latin laudabimur is passive ("we will be praised," rendering Greek epainesometha), whereas the Hebrew uses an active verb ("we have boasted").
- **43:13** — "There was no abundance in their exchanges" (non fuit multitudo in commutationibus eorum) follows a Septuagint reading where the Hebrew means "You did not increase the price in their sale."
- **43:19** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads the second-person verb "You have turned aside" (declinasti), whereas the Hebrew reads third-person "our steps have not turned aside."
- **43:20** — "In the place of affliction" (in loco afflictionis) follows the Septuagint, contrasting with the Hebrew "in the place of jackals."
- **43:23** — The Clementine Vulgate verse numbering skips 43:22 and combines both sentences under 43:23.
- **43:27** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads "for the sake of Your name" (propter nomen tuum) rather than Hebrew "for the sake of Your steadfast love."
