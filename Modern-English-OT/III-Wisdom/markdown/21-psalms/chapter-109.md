## Text

**109:1** A psalm of David. The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at My right hand until I set Your enemies as a footstool for Your feet."

**109:2** The Lord will send out the rod of Your power from Zion; rule in the midst of Your enemies.

**109:3** With You is sovereignty in the day of Your power, in the splendors of the holy ones; from the womb, before the morning star, I have begotten You.

**109:4** The Lord has sworn, and He will not repent: "You are a priest for eternity according to the order of Melchizedek."

**109:5** The Lord is at Your right hand; He has shattered kings in the day of His wrath.

**109:6** He will judge among the nations; He will fill up ruins; He will crush heads in the land of many.

**109:7** From the stream along the way He will drink; therefore He will lift His head high.

## Translator Notes

- **109:3** — Following the LXX, the Vulgate reads 'principium' ('sovereignty' or 'beginning'), 'in splendoribus sanctorum' ('in the splendors of the holy ones,' or 'of the saints'), and 'ex utero, ante luciferum, genui te' ('from the womb, before the morning star, I have begotten You'), diverging sharply from the Hebrew MT.
- **109:5** — Vulgate 'confregit' uses the past/perfect tense ('has shattered,' following the LXX aorist), as does the Hebrew, though the Hebrew perfect here may carry a future sense.
- **109:6** — Vulgate 'implebit ruinas' ('will fill up ruins,' from Greek ptomata, 'falls' or 'corpses') and plural 'capita in terra multorum' ('heads in the land of many') follow the LXX against the Hebrew ('fill with corpses; crush the head over a wide earth').
