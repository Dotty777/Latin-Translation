## Text

**144:1** Praise of David himself. I will exalt You, my God, the King, and I will bless Your name forever, and forever and ever.

**144:2** Every single day I will bless You, and I will praise Your name forever, and forever and ever.

**144:3** Great is the Lord, and exceedingly praiseworthy, and there is no end to His greatness.

**144:4** Generation after generation will praise Your works, and they will proclaim Your power.

**144:5** They will speak of the magnificence of the glory of Your holiness, and they will recount Your wonders.

**144:6** And they will tell of the might of Your fearsome deeds, and they will recount Your greatness.

**144:7** They will pour out the memory of the abundance of Your sweetness, and they will exult in Your righteousness.

**144:8** Compassionate and merciful is the Lord: patient, and greatly merciful.

**144:9** Sweet is the Lord to all, and His compassions are over all His works.

**144:10** May all Your works give thanks to You, Lord, and may Your holy ones bless You.

**144:11** They will tell of the glory of Your kingdom, and they will speak of Your power,

**144:12** so that they may make known to the sons of men Your power, and the glory of the magnificence of Your kingdom.

**144:13** Your kingdom is a kingdom of all ages, and Your dominion is throughout every generation and generation. Faithful is the Lord in all His words, and holy in all His works.

**144:14** The Lord lifts up all who are falling, and raises up all who are struck down.

**144:15** The eyes of all hope in You, Lord, and You give them their food at the proper time.

**144:16** You open Your hand and fill every living creature with blessing.

**144:17** Righteous is the Lord in all His ways, and holy in all His works.

**144:18** Near is the Lord to all who call on Him, to all who call on Him in truth.

**144:19** He will do the will of those who fear Him, and He will heed their supplication, and He will save them.

**144:20** The Lord guards all who love Him, and He will destroy all sinners.

**144:21** My mouth will speak the praise of the Lord, and may all flesh bless His holy name forever, and forever and ever.

## Translator Notes

- **144:5** — Here and in v. 6, the Vulgate (following the Septuagint) uses third-person plural verbs ("they will speak... recount") and reads "of Your holiness," where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has first-person singular verbs and "Your majesty."
- **144:13** — The second sentence ("Faithful is the Lord in all His words, and holy in all His works") preserves the "nun" stanza of this Hebrew alphabetical acrostic, attested in the Vulgate, Septuagint, Syriac, and Dead Sea Scrolls (11QPs-a) though absent from the Masoretic Text.
- **144:14** — Vulgate "elisos" ("those who are struck down," following the Septuagint; also in 145:8) contrasts with the Hebrew reading "those who are bowed down."
- **144:16** — Vulgate "benedictione" ("with blessing") reflects a distinctive reading compared with the Hebrew "with desire" and Septuagint "with good will/favor."
