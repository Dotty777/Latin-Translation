## Text

**75:1** To the end, in praises. A psalm of Asaph, a song concerning the Assyrians.

**75:2** God is known in Judea; in Israel His name is great.

**75:3** And His place has been made in peace, and His dwelling in Zion.

**75:4** There He shattered the powers of the bows, shield, sword, and war.

**75:5** You give light wondrously from the eternal mountains;

**75:6** all the foolish in heart were troubled. They slept their sleep, and all the men of riches found nothing in their hands.

**75:7** At Your rebuke, God of Jacob, those who mounted horses slumbered.

**75:8** You are fearsome; and who will resist You? From then is Your wrath.

**75:9** From heaven You made judgment heard; the earth trembled and became still

**75:10** when God rose up for judgment, to save all the meek of the earth.

**75:11** For the thought of man will give thanks to You, and the remnant of thought will keep a feast day to You.

**75:12** Vow and pay to the Lord your God, all you around Him who bring gifts to the Fearsome One,

**75:13** and to Him who takes away the spirit of princes, fearsome among the kings of the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **75:1** — Vulgate "in praises" and "concerning the Assyrians" follow the Septuagint superscription; the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "with stringed instruments" and has no mention of the Assyrians.
- **75:3** — Vulgate "in peace" translates the place-name "Salem" (Hebrew "Shalem") as a common noun, following the Septuagint.
- **75:5** — Vulgate "from the eternal mountains" follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "from the mountains of prey."
- **75:6** — Vulgate "the foolish in heart" and "men of riches" follow the Septuagint, contrasting with the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "stouthearted" and "men of might."
- **75:11** — Vulgate "the thought of man will give thanks to You, and the remnant of thought will keep a feast day to You" follows the Septuagint; the Hebrew Masoretic Text instead speaks of human wrath praising God and God girding Himself with the remainder of wrath.
