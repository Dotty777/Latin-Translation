## Text

**53:1** To the end, with songs. Understanding of David,

**53:2** when the Ziphites came and said to Saul, "Is David not hidden with us?"

**53:3** God, save me in Your name, and judge me in Your power.

**53:4** God, hear my prayer; receive with Your ears the words of my mouth.

**53:5** Because strangers have risen up against me, and strong men have sought my soul, and they have not placed God before their sight.

**53:6** For look, God helps me, and the Lord is the upholder of my soul.

**53:7** Turn back evils upon my enemies, and in Your truth destroy them.

**53:8** I will sacrifice to You willingly, and I will offer praise to Your name, Lord, because it is good.

**53:9** For You have rescued me out of every affliction, and my eye has looked down on my enemies.

## Translator Notes

- **53:1** — Psalm 53 in the Vulgate corresponds to Psalm 54 in Hebrew numbering. The superscription "To the end, with songs. Understanding of David" follows the Septuagint, where the Hebrew reads "To the choirmaster, with stringed instruments, a maskil of David."
- **53:5** — Latin "fortes" ("strong men") follows the Septuagint ("krataioi"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "ruthless" or "violent men."
- **53:7** — Vulgate "Averte" ("Turn back") is an imperative following the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads an indicative ("He will return").
