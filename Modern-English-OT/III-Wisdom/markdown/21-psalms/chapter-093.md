## Text

**93:1** A psalm for David himself, on the fourth day of the week. The Lord is the God of vengeances; the God of vengeances has acted freely.

**93:2** Be exalted, You who judge the earth; render retribution to the proud.

**93:3** How long will sinners, Lord, how long will sinners boast;

**93:4** will they utter and speak iniquity; will all who work injustice speak?

**93:5** They have humbled Your people, Lord, and they have afflicted Your inheritance.

**93:6** They have killed the widow and the sojourner, and they have murdered the orphans.

**93:7** And they have said, "The Lord will not see, nor will the God of Jacob understand."

**93:8** Understand, you unwise ones among the people; and you fools, finally be wise.

**93:9** Will He who planted the ear not hear? Or does He who fashioned the eye not observe?

**93:10** Will He who disciplines the nations not reprove, He who teaches man knowledge?

**93:11** The Lord knows the thoughts of men, that they are futile.

**93:12** Blessed is the man whom You instruct, Lord, and whom You teach from Your law,

**93:13** so that You may relieve him from evil days, until a pit is dug for the sinner.

**93:14** For the Lord will not reject His people, and He will not abandon His inheritance,

**93:15** until righteousness is turned into judgment, and those who are close to it are all who are upright in heart.

**93:16** Who will rise up for me against those who do evil? Or who will stand with me against those who work iniquity?

**93:17** If the Lord had not helped me, my soul would nearly have dwelt in the underworld.

**93:18** If I said, "My foot has been moved," Your mercy, Lord, kept helping me.

**93:19** According to the multitude of my sorrows in my heart, Your consolations have gladdened my soul.

**93:20** Does a throne of iniquity cling to You, who fashion toil in a precept?

**93:21** They will hunt for the soul of the righteous one, and they will condemn innocent blood.

**93:22** And the Lord has become a refuge for me, and my God a help of my hope.

**93:23** And He will repay them their own iniquity, and in their wickedness He will utterly destroy them; the Lord our God will utterly destroy them.

## Translator Notes

- **93:1** — The superscription 'quarta sabbati' (lit. 'fourth of the Sabbath,' i.e., Wednesday, the fourth day of the week) comes from the Septuagint and is absent in the Hebrew; 'libere egit' ('has acted freely') follows the Septuagint past indicative rather than the Hebrew imperative ('shine forth').
- **93:15** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate connects this verse to verse 14 with 'quoadusque' ('until'), whereas the Hebrew starts a new clause ('for judgment will return to righteousness').
- **93:17** — Latin 'in inferno' ('in the underworld,' traditionally 'hell') translates the Septuagint's 'Hades,' whereas the Hebrew reads 'silence.'
- **93:20** — The Vulgate's second-person verb 'qui fingis' ('who fashion') attaches grammatically to 'tibi' ('You,' God, understood by Augustine as God appointing toil in His commandment), whereas the Hebrew uses a third-person participle describing the wicked throne framing mischief by statute.
- **93:22** — Latin 'in adjutorium spei meae' ('a help of my hope') follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads 'the rock of my refuge.'
