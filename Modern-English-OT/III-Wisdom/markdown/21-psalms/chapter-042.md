## Text

**42:1** A psalm of David. Judge me, God, and separate my case from an unholy nation; rescue me from an unjust and deceitful man.

**42:2** For You, God, are my strength; why have You rejected me? And why do I walk about sorrowful, while the enemy afflicts me?

**42:3** Send out Your light and Your truth; they themselves have led me and brought me to Your holy mountain and into Your tabernacles.

**42:4** And I will go in to the altar of God, to God, who gladdens my youth. I will give thanks to You on the lyre, God, my God.

**42:5** Why are you sorrowful, my soul? And why do you trouble me? Hope in God, for I will still give thanks to Him, the salvation of my face, and my God.

## Translator Notes

- **42:1** — The superscription "A psalm of David" (Psalmus David) follows the Septuagint; the Hebrew Masoretic Text has no heading for this psalm (MT Psalm 43).
- **42:3** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate uses perfect indicative verbs ("have led ... and brought," deduxerunt, et adduxerunt) rather than the Hebrew jussive ("let them lead ... and bring").
- **42:4** — "Who gladdens my youth" (qui laetificat juventutem meam) follows the Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew phrase usually translated "my exceeding joy."
