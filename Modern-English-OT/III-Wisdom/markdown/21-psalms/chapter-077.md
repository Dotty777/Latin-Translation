## Text

**77:1** Understanding of Asaph. Pay attention, my people, to my law; incline your ear to the words of my mouth.

**77:2** I will open my mouth in parables; I will speak riddles from the beginning.

**77:3** All the things that we have heard and known, and that our fathers have recounted to us,

**77:4** have not been hidden from their children in another generation, recounting the praises of the Lord and His mighty deeds, and His wonders that He performed.

**77:5** And He raised up a testimony in Jacob, and set a law in Israel, all the things that He commanded our fathers to make known to their children,

**77:6** so that another generation might know: the children who will be born and will rise up, and they will recount them to their children,

**77:7** so that they might set their hope in God, and not forget the works of God, and seek out His commands,

**77:8** so that they might not become, like their fathers, a crooked and provoking generation, a generation that did not direct its heart; and its spirit was not faithful with God.

**77:9** The sons of Ephraim, drawing and shooting the bow, turned back on the day of battle.

**77:10** They did not keep the covenant of God, and they were unwilling to walk in His law.

**77:11** And they forgot His good deeds, and His wonders that He showed them.

**77:12** Before their fathers He performed wonders in the land of Egypt, in the plain of Tanis.

**77:13** He split open the sea and led them through, and made the waters stand as in a wineskin.

**77:14** And He led them in a cloud by day, and the whole night in the light of fire.

**77:15** He split open the rock in the wilderness, and gave them drink as in a great deep.

**77:16** And He brought water out of the rock, and brought down waters like rivers.

**77:17** And they continued still to sin against Him; they provoked the Most High to wrath in a waterless place.

**77:18** And they tested God in their hearts to ask for food for their souls.

**77:19** And they spoke evil of God; they said, "Will God really be able to prepare a table in the desert?

**77:20** Since He struck the rock, and waters flowed, and torrents overflowed, will He also be able to give bread, or prepare a table for His people?"

**77:21** Therefore the Lord heard and delayed; and a fire was kindled in Jacob, and wrath rose up against Israel,

**77:22** because they did not believe in God, nor did they hope in His salvation.

**77:23** And He commanded the clouds from above, and opened the doors of heaven.

**77:24** And He rained down manna for them to eat, and gave them the bread of heaven.

**77:25** The bread of angels man ate; He sent provisions to them in abundance.

**77:26** He moved the south wind from heaven, and brought in the southwest wind by His might.

**77:27** And He rained down upon them meat like dust, and winged birds like the sand of the sea.

**77:28** And they fell in the midst of their camp, around their tents.

**77:29** And they ate and were greatly satisfied, and He brought their desire to them;

**77:30** they were not deprived of their desire. Their food was still in their mouth,

**77:31** and the wrath of God rose up over them; and He killed their fat ones, and entangled the chosen ones of Israel.

**77:32** In all these things they still sinned, and did not believe in His wonders.

**77:33** And their days ended in emptiness, and their years with haste.

**77:34** When He killed them, they sought Him and returned, and at dawn they came to Him.

**77:35** And they remembered that God is their helper, and the Most High God is their redeemer.

**77:36** And they loved Him with their mouth, and with their tongue they lied to Him.

**77:37** But their heart was not right with Him, nor were they counted faithful in His covenant.

**77:38** But He is merciful, and He will be forgiving toward their sins, and will not destroy them. And He abounded in turning away His wrath, and did not kindle all His wrath.

**77:39** And He remembered that they are flesh, a wind going and not returning.

**77:40** How often did they provoke Him in the desert, rouse Him to wrath in a waterless place?

**77:41** And they turned back and tested God, and provoked the Holy One of Israel.

**77:42** They did not remember His hand, on the day when He redeemed them from the hand of the oppressor,

**77:43** how He set His signs in Egypt, and His portents in the plain of Tanis;

**77:44** and He turned their rivers into blood, and their rains, so that they could not drink.

**77:45** He sent among them the dog-fly, and it devoured them, and the frog, and it destroyed them;

**77:46** and He gave their fruits to the blight, and their labors to the locust;

**77:47** and He killed their vineyards with hail, and their mulberry trees with frost;

**77:48** and He handed over their livestock to the hail, and their property to fire.

**77:49** He sent upon them the wrath of His indignation, indignation and wrath and tribulation, visitations through evil angels.

**77:50** He made a way for the path of His wrath; He did not spare their souls from death, and He shut up their livestock in death.

**77:51** And He struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, the firstfruits of all their labor in the tents of Ham.

**77:52** And He led away His people like sheep, and led them through like a flock in the desert.

**77:53** And He led them in hope, and they did not fear, and the sea covered their enemies.

**77:54** And He brought them into the mountain of His sanctification, the mountain that His right hand acquired; and He cast out the nations from before their face, and He divided the land for them by lot with a line of distribution,

**77:55** and He made the tribes of Israel dwell in their tents.

**77:56** And they tested and provoked the Most High God, and did not keep His testimonies.

**77:57** And they turned away and did not observe the pact; just like their fathers, they were turned into a crooked bow.

**77:58** They roused Him to wrath on their hills, and with their carved images they provoked Him to jealousy.

**77:59** God heard, and despised and utterly reduced Israel to nothing.

**77:60** And He rejected the tent of Shiloh, His own tent, where He dwelt among people.

**77:61** And He handed over their might into captivity, and their beauty into the hands of the enemy.

**77:62** And He shut up His people under the sword, and despised His inheritance.

**77:63** Fire devoured their young men, and their virgins were not lamented.

**77:64** Their priests fell by the sword, and their widows were not wept for.

**77:65** And the Lord was awakened like one sleeping, like a mighty man intoxicated from wine.

**77:66** And He struck His enemies in the rear; He gave them everlasting reproach.

**77:67** And He rejected the tent of Joseph, and He did not choose the tribe of Ephraim;

**77:68** but He chose the tribe of Judah, Mount Zion, which He loved.

**77:69** And He built His sanctuary like that of unicorns, on the earth that He founded forever.

**77:70** And He chose David His servant, and took him up from the flocks of sheep; He took him from behind the ewes with young,

**77:71** to shepherd Jacob His servant, and Israel His inheritance.

**77:72** And he shepherded them in the innocence of his heart, and with the understanding of his hands he led them.

## Translator Notes

- **77:12** — Latin "in campo Taneos" ("in the plain of Tanis") uses the Greek and Latin name Tanis for the Egyptian city called Zoan in Hebrew.
- **77:13** — Latin "quasi in utre" ("as in a wineskin") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "like a heap."
- **77:20** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads "quoniam" ("Since") and repeats "parare mensam" ("prepare a table") from verse 19, where the Hebrew reads "Look" and "provide meat."
- **77:21** — Latin "distulit" ("delayed") renders Septuagint "anebaleto" ("put off"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "was full of wrath."
- **77:36** — Latin "dilexerunt" ("loved") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "flattered" or "deceived."
- **77:44** — Following the Septuagint, Latin "imbres eorum" ("their rains") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "their streams."
- **77:45** — Latin "cœnomyiam" ("the dog-fly") transliterates the Septuagint singular collective "kynomyian," whereas the Hebrew reads "swarms of flies."
- **77:46** — Latin "ærugini" ("to the blight" or "rust") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "to the caterpillar" (or stripping locust).
- **77:63** — Latin "non sunt lamentatæ" ("were not lamented") here and "non plorabantur" ("were not wept for") in verse 64 follow the Septuagint passives, whereas the Hebrew reads "had no wedding songs" and "did not weep."
- **77:69** — Latin "sicut unicornium" ("like that of unicorns") follows the Septuagint ("hos monokeroton"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "like the heights."
