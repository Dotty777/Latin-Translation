## Text

**133:1** A song of steps. Look now, bless the Lord, all servants of the Lord, you who stand in the house of the Lord, in the courts of the house of our God.

**133:2** During the nights, raise your hands toward the sanctuary, and bless the Lord.

**133:3** May the Lord, who made heaven and earth, bless you from Zion.

## Translator Notes

- **133:1** — The closing phrase in atriis domus Dei nostri ("in the courts of the house of our God") follows the Septuagint and does not appear in the Hebrew Masoretic Text of this verse.
- **133:2** — The Vulgate opens v. 2 with In noctibus ("During the nights"), whereas the Hebrew places "by night" at the end of v. 1; in sancta is literally plural ("toward the holy places"), referring to the temple sanctuary.
