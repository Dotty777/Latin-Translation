## Text

**79:1** To the end, for those who will be changed. A testimony of Asaph, a psalm.

**79:2** You who rule Israel, pay attention; You who lead Joseph like a sheep. You who sit above the cherubim, appear

**79:3** before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh. Stir up Your power, and come so that You may save us.

**79:4** God, turn us back, and show Your face, and we will be saved.

**79:5** Lord God of hosts, how long will You be angry against the prayer of Your servant?

**79:6** Will You feed us with the bread of tears and give us tears to drink by measure?

**79:7** You have made us a cause of contention to our neighbors, and our enemies have mocked us.

**79:8** God of hosts, turn us back, and show Your face, and we will be saved.

**79:9** You transplanted a vine from Egypt; You drove out the nations and planted it.

**79:10** You were the leader of the journey before it; You planted its roots, and it filled the land.

**79:11** Its shadow covered the mountains, and its boughs the cedars of God.

**79:12** It stretched out its branches as far as the sea, and its offshoots as far as the river.

**79:13** Why have You torn down its stone wall, so that all who pass along the way harvest its grapes?

**79:14** The boar from the forest has ravaged it, and the solitary wild beast has fed upon it.

**79:15** God of hosts, turn back; look down from heaven and see, and visit this vine,

**79:16** and perfect the one that Your right hand planted, and upon the son of man whom You have strengthened for Yourself.

**79:17** It is burned with fire and dug up; at the rebuke of Your countenance they will perish.

**79:18** Let Your hand be upon the man of Your right hand, and upon the son of man whom You have strengthened for Yourself.

**79:19** And we do not depart from You; You will give us life, and we will call upon Your name.

**79:20** Lord God of hosts, turn us back, and show Your face, and we will be saved.

## Translator Notes

- **79:1** — The superscription "In finem, pro iis qui commutabuntur" ("To the end, for those who will be changed") translates the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads "To the choirmaster: according to Lilies."
- **79:5** — The Vulgate reads singular "servi tui" ("of Your servant") with the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads "of Your people."
- **79:10** — The Latin "Dux itineris fuisti ... plantasti radices ejus" ("You were the leader of the journey ... You planted its roots") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads "You cleared the ground before it; it took deep root."
- **79:14** — The Vulgate "singularis ferus" ("the solitary wild beast") renders the Septuagint "monios agrios," whereas the Hebrew reads "all that moves in the field."
- **79:16** — The Latin "perfice eam" ("perfect the one") treats the Hebrew noun ("stock/root") as an imperative verb following the Septuagint, and the following "et super filium hominis" ("and upon the son of man") preserves the abrupt ellipsis of the source text.
