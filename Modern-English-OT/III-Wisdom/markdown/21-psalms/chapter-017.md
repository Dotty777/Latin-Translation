## Text

**17:1** To the end. For the Lord's servant David, who spoke to the Lord the words of this song on the day when the Lord rescued him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul, and he said:

**17:2** I will love You, Lord, my strength.

**17:3** The Lord is my stronghold, and my refuge, and my deliverer. My God is my helper, and I will hope in Him; my protector, and the horn of my salvation, and my upholder.

**17:4** Praising, I will call upon the Lord, and from my enemies I will be saved.

**17:5** The pains of death surrounded me, and the torrents of iniquity threw me into turmoil.

**17:6** The pains of the underworld surrounded me; the snares of death overtook me.

**17:7** In my tribulation I called upon the Lord, and to my God I cried out: and He heard my voice from His holy temple; and my cry in His sight entered into His ears.

**17:8** The earth was shaken and trembled; the foundations of the mountains were thrown into turmoil and were shaken, because He was angry with them.

**17:9** Smoke rose up in His anger, and fire blazed forth from His face; coals were kindled by Him.

**17:10** He inclined the heavens and descended, and thick darkness was under His feet.

**17:11** And He mounted upon the cherubim and flew; He flew upon the wings of the winds.

**17:12** And He made darkness His hiding place; around Him was His tent, dark water in the clouds of the air.

**17:13** Because of the brightness in His sight the clouds passed by; hail and coals of fire.

**17:14** And the Lord thundered from heaven, and the Most High gave forth His voice: hail and coals of fire.

**17:15** And He sent out His arrows and scattered them; He multiplied lightning bolts and threw them into turmoil.

**17:16** And the springs of the waters appeared, and the foundations of the world were uncovered, at Your rebuke, Lord, at the blast of the breath of Your anger.

**17:17** He sent from on high and took me; and He took me up out of many waters.

**17:18** He rescued me from my strongest enemies, and from those who hate me. For they grew stronger than me;

**17:19** they confronted me on the day of my affliction, and the Lord became my protector.

**17:20** And He brought me out into a wide place; He saved me, because He desired me,

**17:21** and the Lord will repay me according to my righteousness, and according to the purity of my hands He will repay me:

**17:22** because I have kept the ways of the Lord, and have not acted wickedly away from my God;

**17:23** for all His judgments are in my sight, and His righteous decrees I have not cast away from me.

**17:24** And I will be blameless with Him, and I will guard myself from my iniquity.

**17:25** And the Lord will repay me according to my righteousness, and according to the purity of my hands in the sight of His eyes.

**17:26** With the holy one You will be holy, and with an innocent man You will be innocent,

**17:27** and with the chosen one You will be chosen, and with the perverse one You will act perversely.

**17:28** For You will save a humble people, and You will humble the eyes of the proud.

**17:29** For You light up my lamp, Lord; my God, light up my darkness.

**17:30** For in You I will be rescued from temptation; and in my God I will cross over a wall.

**17:31** My God, His way is undefiled; the utterances of the Lord are examined by fire: He is the protector of all who hope in Him.

**17:32** For who is a god besides the Lord? Or who is a god besides our God?

**17:33** God, who has equipped me with strength and made my way blameless;

**17:34** who has perfected my feet like those of deer, and sets me upon high places;

**17:35** who teaches my hands for battle. And You have made my arms like a bronze bow,

**17:36** and You have given me the protection of Your salvation: and Your right hand has upheld me, and Your discipline has corrected me to the end, and Your discipline itself will teach me.

**17:37** You have widened my steps beneath me, and my footsteps have not grown weak.

**17:38** I will pursue my enemies and overtake them; and I will not turn back until they fail.

**17:39** I will shatter them, and they will not be able to stand; they will fall beneath my feet.

**17:40** And You have equipped me with strength for war, and You have overthrown beneath me those who rise up against me.

**17:41** And You have made my enemies turn their backs to me, and those who hate me You have destroyed.

**17:42** They cried out, and there was no one to save them; to the Lord, and He did not hear them.

**17:43** And I will grind them fine like dust before the face of the wind; like the mud of the streets I will wipe them out.

**17:44** You will rescue me from the disputes of the people; You will appoint me as head of the nations.

**17:45** A people whom I did not know served me; at the hearing of the ear they obeyed me.

**17:46** Foreign sons have lied to me, foreign sons have grown old, and they have limped from their paths.

**17:47** The Lord lives, and blessed is my God, and may the God of my salvation be exalted.

**17:48** God, You who give acts of vengeance to me and subdue peoples under me; my deliverer from my wrathful enemies.

**17:49** And above those who rise up against me You will exalt me; from the unjust man You will rescue me.

**17:50** Therefore I will give thanks to You among the nations, Lord, and to Your name I will sing a psalm;

**17:51** magnifying the acts of salvation of His king, and showing mercy to His anointed, David, and to his seed forever.

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — The Vulgate superscription "To the end" (In finem) renders the Septuagint eis to telos, corresponding to the Hebrew "for the choir director."
- **17:9** — The Vulgate reads "in His anger" (in ira ejus) and "from His face" (a facie ejus), following the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads "from His nostrils" and "from His mouth."
- **17:27** — The Vulgate "chosen" (electo/electus) follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads "pure"; the verb perverteris literally means "You will be turned crooked," expressing that God opposes the perverse in kind.
- **17:30** — The Vulgate "I will be rescued from temptation" (eripiar a tentatione) follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "I can run against a troop."
- **17:36** — The Vulgate doublet "and Your discipline has corrected me to the end, and Your discipline itself will teach me" follows the Septuagint tradition, whereas the Hebrew reads simply "and Your gentleness has made me great."
