## Text

**14:1** A psalm of David. Lord, who will dwell in Your tabernacle? Or who will rest on Your holy mountain?

**14:2** The one who walks without blemish and practices righteousness;

**14:3** who speaks truth in his heart; who has not committed deceit with his tongue, nor done evil to his neighbor, and has not accepted a reproach against his neighbors.

**14:4** The wicked one is reduced to nothing in his sight, but he honors those who fear the Lord. The one who swears to his neighbor and does not deceive;

**14:5** who has not given his money at interest and has not accepted bribes against an innocent person: the one who does these things will never be moved.

## Translator Notes

- **14:2** — Latin "sine macula" ("without blemish," following the Septuagint) corresponds to "blamelessly" or "with integrity" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **14:4** — Latin "Qui jurat proximo suo, et non decipit" ("The one who swears to his neighbor and does not deceive," following the Septuagint) reflects a different vocalization of the Hebrew consonants from the Masoretic Text, which reads "who swears to his own hurt and does not change."
