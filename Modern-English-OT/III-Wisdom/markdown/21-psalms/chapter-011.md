## Text

**11:1** To the end, for the eighth. A psalm of David.

**11:2** Save me, Lord, for the holy one has vanished, for truths have been diminished from among the sons of men.

**11:3** They have spoken empty things, each one to his neighbor; with deceitful lips, in heart and heart they have spoken.

**11:4** May the Lord utterly destroy all deceitful lips and the boastful tongue,

**11:5** those who have said, "We will magnify our tongue; our lips are from us. Who is our master?"

**11:6** "Because of the misery of the needy and the groaning of the poor, now I will rise up," says the Lord. "I will place them in salvation; I will act confidently in it."

**11:7** The utterances of the Lord are pure utterances; silver tested by fire, proven to the earth, purified sevenfold.

**11:8** You, Lord, will preserve us, and You will guard us from this generation forever.

**11:9** The ungodly walk all around; according to Your height, You have multiplied the sons of men.

## Translator Notes

- **11:1** — Vulgate Psalm 11 corresponds to Psalm 12 in Hebrew numbering. "for the eighth" translates Latin "pro octava" (Septuagint "hyper tes ogdoes"), corresponding to the Hebrew musical term "sheminith."
- **11:3** — "in heart and heart" preserves the literal Vulgate Hebraism ("in corde et corde"), meaning with a double or divided heart.
- **11:6** — "I will place them in salvation; I will act confidently in it" follows the Vulgate ("Ponam in salutari; fiducialiter agam in eo") and Septuagint, where the object of "place" is unexpressed and "in eo" can refer either to salvation ("in it") or to the needy person ("in him").
- **11:7** — "proven to the earth" follows the Vulgate ("probatum terrae") and Septuagint, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "in a furnace on the earth."
- **11:9** — "according to Your height, You have multiplied the sons of men" follows the Vulgate ("secundum altitudinem tuam multiplicasti filios hominum") and Septuagint, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "when vileness is exalted among the sons of men."
