## Text

**54:1** To the end, with songs. Understanding of David.

**54:2** Hear my prayer, God, and do not disregard my supplication;

**54:3** pay attention to me and hear me. I have been saddened in my exercise, and I have been disturbed

**54:4** by the voice of the enemy and by the affliction of the sinner. For they have turned iniquities upon me, and in anger they were grievous to me.

**54:5** My heart is disturbed within me, and the dread of death has fallen on me.

**54:6** Fear and trembling have come upon me, and darkness has covered me over.

**54:7** And I said, "Who will give me wings like those of a dove, and I will fly, and I will rest?"

**54:8** Look, I went far away, fleeing, and remained in the wilderness.

**54:9** I was waiting for Him who saved me from faintheartedness of spirit and storm.

**54:10** Cast them down, Lord; divide their tongues, for I have seen iniquity and dispute in the city.

**54:11** Day and night iniquity will surround it on its walls, and toil is in its midst,

**54:12** and injustice; and usury and deceit have not ceased from its squares.

**54:13** For if my enemy had cursed me, I would certainly have endured it. And if the one who hated me had spoken great things over me, perhaps I would have hidden myself from him.

**54:14** But you, a like-minded man, my guide and my acquaintance,

**54:15** who together with me used to take sweet foods; in the house of God we walked in agreement.

**54:16** Let death come upon them, and let them descend into hell alive, for wicked deeds are in their dwellings, in their midst.

**54:17** As for me, I cried out to God, and the Lord will save me.

**54:18** At evening, and in the morning, and at noon, I will recount and declare, and He will hear my voice.

**54:19** He will redeem my soul in peace from those who draw near to me, for among many they were with me.

**54:20** God will hear and humble them, He who is before the ages. For there is no change for them, and they have not feared God.

**54:21** He has stretched out His hand in repaying; they have defiled His covenant;

**54:22** they were divided by the anger of His face, and that one's heart drew near; his words were made softer than oil, yet they themselves are darts.

**54:23** Cast your concern on the Lord, and He Himself will nourish you; He will never give wavering to the righteous one.

**54:24** But You, God, will bring them down into the pit of destruction. Men of blood and deceitful men will not reach half their days; but I will hope in You, Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **54:3** — Psalm 54 in the Vulgate corresponds to Psalm 55 in Hebrew numbering. Latin "in exercitatione mea" ("in my exercise") denotes mental or spiritual exertion, preoccupation, or trial, rendering the Septuagint ("adoleschia") where the Hebrew reads "in my complaint."
- **54:9** — Latin "a pusillanimitate spiritus, et tempestate" ("from faintheartedness of spirit and storm") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "from the rushing wind and tempest."
- **54:15** — Latin "dulces capiebas cibos" ("used to take sweet foods") follows the Septuagint ("edesmata," foods/meals), whereas the Hebrew reads "we took sweet counsel together." Similarly, in verse 12, "usura" ("usury") reflects the Septuagint rendering of Hebrew "oppression."
- **54:19** — Vulgate "ab his qui appropinquant mihi: quoniam inter multos erant mecum" ("from those who draw near to me, for among many they were with me") closely follows the Septuagint; the Hebrew reads "from the battle waged against me, for many are against me."
- **54:21** — In verses 21-22, the Vulgate follows the Septuagint in speaking first of God's hand in repaying, His defiled covenant, and the anger of His face, before shifting via Latin "illius" ("that one's") to the treacherous enemy whose words are softer than oil yet are darts; the Hebrew refers the whole passage to the traitor.
