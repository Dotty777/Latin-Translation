## Text

**139:1** To the end. A psalm of David.

**139:2** Rescue me, Lord, from the evil man; from the wicked man rescue me.

**139:3** Those who have plotted iniquities in their heart were drawing up battles all day long.

**139:4** They have sharpened their tongues like a serpent's; the poison of asps is under their lips.

**139:5** Guard me, Lord, from the hand of the sinner, and rescue me from wicked men, who have plotted to trip up my steps:

**139:6** the proud have hidden a snare for me. And they have stretched out ropes as a snare; beside the way they have set a stumbling block for me.

**139:7** I said to the Lord, "You are my God; hear, Lord, the voice of my supplication."

**139:8** Lord, Lord, strength of my salvation, You have overshadowed my head on the day of war.

**139:9** Do not hand me over, Lord, from my desire to the sinner: they have plotted against me; do not abandon me, so that they may not be exalted.

**139:10** The head of their encircling: the labor of their own lips will cover them.

**139:11** Coals will fall upon them; into the fire You will cast them down: in miseries they will not endure.

**139:12** A talkative man will not be directed on the earth; evils will seize the unjust man in destruction.

**139:13** I know that the Lord will execute the judgment of the needy, and the vindication of the poor.

**139:14** Yet the righteous will give thanks to Your name, and the upright will dwell with Your countenance.

## Translator Notes

- **139:9** — Vulgate Ne tradas me, Domine, a desiderio meo peccatori ("Do not hand me over, Lord, from my desire to the sinner") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads "Do not grant, Lord, the desires of the wicked."
- **139:10** — Vulgate Caput circuitus eorum ("The head of their encircling") follows the Septuagint, where the Hebrew has "the head of those who surround me."
- **139:11** — Vulgate in miseriis non subsistent ("in miseries they will not endure") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads "into deep pits, that they may not rise."
