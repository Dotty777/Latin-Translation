## Text

**117:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord, because He is good, because His mercy is forever.

**117:2** Let Israel say now that He is good, that His mercy is forever.

**117:3** Let the house of Aaron say now that His mercy is forever.

**117:4** Let those who fear the Lord say now that His mercy is forever.

**117:5** Out of distress I called on the Lord, and the Lord heard me in a broad space.

**117:6** The Lord is my helper; I will not fear what man may do to me.

**117:7** The Lord is my helper, and I will look down on my enemies.

**117:8** It is good to trust in the Lord rather than to trust in man.

**117:9** It is good to hope in the Lord rather than to hope in princes.

**117:10** All the nations encircled me, and in the name of the Lord, indeed I took vengeance on them.

**117:11** Surrounding me, they surrounded me, and in the name of the Lord, indeed I took vengeance on them.

**117:12** They surrounded me like bees, and they blazed up like fire among thorns; and in the name of the Lord, indeed I took vengeance on them.

**117:13** Having been pushed, I was overturned so that I would fall, and the Lord upheld me.

**117:14** My strength and my praise is the Lord, and He has become salvation for me.

**117:15** The sound of exultation and salvation is in the tents of the righteous.

**117:16** The right hand of the Lord has acted with power; the right hand of the Lord has exalted me; the right hand of the Lord has acted with power.

**117:17** I will not die, but I will live, and I will recount the works of the Lord.

**117:18** Chastising me, the Lord has chastised me, and He has not handed me over to death.

**117:19** Open for me the gates of righteousness; having entered into them, I will give thanks to the Lord.

**117:20** This is the gate of the Lord; the righteous will enter into it.

**117:21** I will give thanks to You, because You have heard me and have become salvation for me.

**117:22** The stone that the builders rejected, this one has become the head of the corner.

**117:23** By the Lord this has been done, and it is wonderful in our eyes.

**117:24** This is the day that the Lord has made; let us exult and be glad in it.

**117:25** Lord, save me; Lord, grant good prosperity.

**117:26** Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord; we have blessed you from the house of the Lord.

**117:27** The Lord is God, and He has shone on us. Establish a solemn day with dense boughs, up to the horn of the altar.

**117:28** You are my God, and I will give thanks to You; You are my God, and I will exalt You. I will give thanks to You, because You have heard me and have become salvation for me.

**117:29** Give thanks to the Lord, because He is good, because His mercy is forever.

## Translator Notes

- **117:1** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate opens this psalm with Alleluja (Hallelujah), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text places it at the end of the preceding psalm. In v. 2, the Vulgate likewise includes quoniam bonus ("that He is good"), which is absent from the Masoretic Text.
- **117:10** — Latin et in nomine Domini, quia ultus sum in eos retains the emphatic conjunction quia ("indeed") and uses the past tense "I took vengeance on them" (following the Septuagint; repeated in vv. 11-12), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads a future "I will cut them off."
- **117:12** — Latin exarserunt ("blazed up," following the Septuagint) contrasts with the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "they were extinguished."
- **117:27** — Latin Constituite diem solemnem in condensis, usque ad cornu altaris ("Establish a solemn day with dense boughs, up to the horn of the altar") follows the Septuagint in referring to a festival with thick foliage and uses the singular cornu ("horn"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks of binding the festal sacrifice with cords up to the horns of the altar.
- **117:28** — The Clementine Vulgate repeats the closing sentence of v. 21 (Confitebor tibi quoniam exaudisti me, et factus es mihi in salutem), an addition absent from v. 28 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
