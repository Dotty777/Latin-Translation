## Text

**46:1** To the end, for the sons of Korah. A psalm.

**46:2** All nations, clap your hands; shout joyfully to God with a voice of exultation,

**46:3** for the Lord is high, fearsome, a great king over all the earth.

**46:4** He has subjected peoples to us, and nations beneath our feet.

**46:5** He has chosen His inheritance for us, the splendor of Jacob, which He loved.

**46:6** God has ascended with a joyful shout, and the Lord with the voice of a trumpet.

**46:7** Sing psalms to our God, sing psalms; sing psalms to our king, sing psalms,

**46:8** for God is king of all the earth; sing psalms wisely.

**46:9** God will reign over the nations; God sits upon His holy throne.

**46:10** The princes of the peoples have been gathered together with the God of Abraham, for the mighty gods of the earth have been greatly lifted up.

## Translator Notes

- **46:5** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads haereditatem suam ("His inheritance") rather than the Hebrew "our inheritance."
- **46:8** — Latin sapienter ("wisely," following the Septuagint) renders the Hebrew musical term maskil ("a skillful psalm") as an adverb.
- **46:9** — The Clementine Vulgate uses the future tense Regnabit ("God will reign"), whereas the Hebrew has a perfect and the Septuagint an aorist.
- **46:10** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads cum Deo Abraham ("with the God of Abraham," vs. Hebrew "the people of the God of Abraham"), and distinctively renders the final clause as dii fortes terrae ("the mighty gods of the earth," vs. Hebrew "to God belong the shields of the earth").
