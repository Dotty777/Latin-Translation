## Text

**71:1** A psalm for Solomon.

**71:2** God, give Your judgment to the king, and Your righteousness to the son of the king, to judge Your people in righteousness, and Your poor in judgment.

**71:3** May the mountains receive peace for the people, and the hills righteousness.

**71:4** He will judge the poor of the people, and he will save the children of the poor, and he will bring low the false accuser.

**71:5** And he will endure with the sun, and before the moon, through generation and generation.

**71:6** He will come down like rain upon a fleece, and like droplets dripping upon the earth.

**71:7** In his days righteousness will arise, and an abundance of peace, until the moon is taken away.

**71:8** And he will rule from sea to sea, and from the river to the boundaries of the world.

**71:9** Before him the Ethiopians will fall down, and his enemies will lick the earth.

**71:10** The kings of Tarshish and the islands will offer gifts; the kings of the Arabs and of Sheba will bring presents;

**71:11** and all the kings of the earth will adore him; all nations will serve him.

**71:12** For he will deliver the poor man from the mighty, and the poor man who had no helper.

**71:13** He will spare the poor and the needy, and he will save the souls of the poor.

**71:14** From usury and iniquity he will redeem their souls, and their name is honorable before him.

**71:15** And he will live, and some of the gold of Arabia will be given to him; and they will pray concerning him always, all day long they will bless him.

**71:16** And there will be a support in the earth on the tops of the mountains; its fruit will be exalted above Lebanon, and they will flourish from the city like the grass of the earth.

**71:17** May his name be blessed forever; before the sun his name endures. And in him all the tribes of the earth will be blessed; all nations will magnify him.

**71:18** Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who alone does marvelous things.

**71:19** And blessed be the name of His majesty forever, and all the earth will be filled with His majesty. So be it, so be it.

**71:20** The praises of David, the son of Jesse, have come to an end.

## Translator Notes

- **71:6** — Latin in vellus ('upon a fleece') follows the Septuagint reading of a Hebrew term often rendered 'mown grass.'
- **71:9** — Latin Aethiopes ('Ethiopians') follows the Septuagint where the Hebrew has 'desert-dwellers'; terram ('earth') stands where both the Septuagint and the Hebrew have 'dust.'
- **71:14** — Latin ex usuris ('from usury') and nomen eorum ('their name') follow the Septuagint against the Hebrew 'from oppression' and 'their blood'; similarly in v. 12 Latin a potente ('from the mighty') differs from the Hebrew 'who cries out.'
- **71:15** — Latin adorabunt de ipso renders Greek proseuxontai peri autou ('they will pray concerning him'), using adorare in its sense of praying or petitioning.
- **71:16** — Latin firmamentum ('a support' or 'foundation') follows the Septuagint sterigma where the Hebrew has 'abundance of grain.'
