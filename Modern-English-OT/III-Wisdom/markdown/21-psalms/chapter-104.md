## Text

**104:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord, and call on His name; proclaim His works among the nations.

**104:2** Sing to Him, and sing psalms to Him; recount all His marvels.

**104:3** Glory in His holy name; let the heart of those seeking the Lord rejoice.

**104:4** Seek the Lord, and be strengthened; seek His face always.

**104:5** Remember His marvels that He has done, His wonders, and the judgments of His mouth,

**104:6** offspring of Abraham His servant, sons of Jacob, His chosen ones.

**104:7** He Himself is the Lord our God; His judgments are throughout the whole earth.

**104:8** He has been mindful of His covenant forever, of the word that He commanded for a thousand generations,

**104:9** which He made with Abraham, and of His oath to Isaac,

**104:10** and He established it for Jacob as a decree, and for Israel as an eternal covenant,

**104:11** saying, "To you I will give the land of Canaan, the measuring line of your inheritance,"

**104:12** when they were small in number, very few, and sojourners in it.

**104:13** And they traveled from nation to nation, and from one kingdom to a different people.

**104:14** He allowed no man to harm them, and He rebuked kings on their behalf.

**104:15** "Do not touch My anointed ones, and do not act maliciously against My prophets."

**104:16** And He called a famine on the land, and shattered every support of bread.

**104:17** He sent a man ahead of them: Joseph was sold as a slave.

**104:18** They humbled his feet in shackles; iron passed through his soul,

**104:19** until his word came. The utterance of the Lord inflamed him.

**104:20** The king sent and unbound him; the ruler of peoples, and let him go.

**104:21** He appointed him lord of his house and ruler of all his property,

**104:22** so that he might instruct his princes like himself, and teach his elders prudence.

**104:23** And Israel entered Egypt, and Jacob was a sojourner in the land of Ham.

**104:24** And He greatly increased His people, and strengthened them above their enemies.

**104:25** He turned their heart to hate His people, and to practice deceit against His servants.

**104:26** He sent Moses His servant; Aaron, the very one whom He chose.

**104:27** He placed in them the words of His signs and wonders in the land of Ham.

**104:28** He sent darkness, and made it dark; and He did not embitter His words.

**104:29** He turned their waters into blood, and killed their fish.

**104:30** Their land brought forth frogs in the innermost chambers of their kings.

**104:31** He spoke, and dog-flies and gnats came in all their borders.

**104:32** He made their rains hail, a consuming fire in their land.

**104:33** And He struck their vines and their fig trees, and shattered the trees of their borders.

**104:34** He spoke, and the locust came, and the wingless locust without number,

**104:35** and it devoured all the grass in their land, and devoured all the fruit of their land.

**104:36** And He struck every firstborn in their land, the firstfruits of all their labor.

**104:37** And He brought them out with silver and gold, and there was no one weak in their tribes.

**104:38** Egypt rejoiced at their departure, because the fear of them fell upon them.

**104:39** He spread out a cloud for their protection, and fire to shine for them through the night.

**104:40** They asked, and quail came, and He satisfied them with the bread of heaven.

**104:41** He split open the rock, and waters flowed; rivers ran in dry ground.

**104:42** For He was mindful of His holy word, which He had with Abraham His servant.

**104:43** And He brought out His people with exultation, and His chosen ones with gladness.

**104:44** And He gave them the regions of the nations, and they took possession of the labors of the peoples,

**104:45** so that they may keep His statutes and seek out His law.

## Translator Notes

- **104:4** — Latin et confirmamini ("and be strengthened"), following the Septuagint, differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "and His strength."
- **104:18** — Latin ferrum pertransiit animam ejus makes "iron" the grammatical subject ("iron passed through his soul"), a distinctive Vulgate reading compared with the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("his neck was put in iron").
- **104:19** — Latin inflammavit ("inflamed") renders Greek epyrosen ("tried or refined by fire," corresponding to Hebrew "tested"), carrying the metaphor of purifying metal in fire as well as kindling ardor.
- **104:22** — Latin ut erudiret principes ejus sicut semetipsum ("so that he might instruct his princes like himself") follows the Septuagint rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("to bind his princes at his pleasure").
- **104:28** — Latin et non exacerbavit sermones suos ("and He did not embitter His words") uses a singular verb with a reflexive pronoun referring to God, a distinctive Vulgate reading differing from both the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("and they did not rebel against His words") and most Septuagint witnesses ("and they provoked His words").
