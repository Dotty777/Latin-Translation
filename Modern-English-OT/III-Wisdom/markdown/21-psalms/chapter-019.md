## Text

**19:1** To the end. A psalm of David.

**19:2** May the Lord hear you in the day of distress; may the name of the God of Jacob shield you.

**19:3** May He send you help from the sanctuary, and guard you from Zion.

**19:4** May He be mindful of every sacrifice of yours, and may your burnt offering become fat.

**19:5** May He grant you according to your heart, and confirm every plan of yours.

**19:6** We will rejoice in your salvation, and in the name of our God we will be magnified.

**19:7** May the Lord fulfill all your requests; now I know that the Lord has saved His anointed one. He will hear him from His holy heaven; in mighty deeds is the salvation of His right hand.

**19:8** Some in chariots, and some in horses; but we will call out in the name of the Lord our God.

**19:9** They themselves were bound and fell; but we rose and were raised upright.

**19:10** Lord, save the king, and hear us on the day when we call upon You.

## Translator Notes

- **19:4** — Latin "pingue fiat" (literally "become fat") is a sacrificial idiom for an offering being rich and acceptable to God.
- **19:6** — Latin "magnificabimur" ("we will be magnified") follows the Septuagint ("megalynthesometha"), whereas the Hebrew reads "we will raise our banners."
- **19:8** — Following the Hebrew and Septuagint, the first clause omits a verb; Latin "invocabimus" ("we will call out") follows the Septuagint tradition where the Hebrew reads "we will remember/boast."
- **19:9** — Latin "obligati sunt" ("were bound") follows the Septuagint ("synepodisthesan," "were shackled"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "bowed down/collapsed."
