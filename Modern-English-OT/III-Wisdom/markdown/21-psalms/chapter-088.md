## Text

**88:1** Understanding of Ethan the Ezrahite.

**88:2** I will sing of the mercies of the Lord forever; to generation after generation I will proclaim Your truth with my mouth.

**88:3** For You have said, "Mercy will be built up forever in the heavens; Your truth will be prepared in them.

**88:4** I have ordained a covenant for My chosen ones; I have sworn to David My servant:

**88:5** 'Forevermore I will prepare your offspring, and I will build up your throne to generation after generation.'"

**88:6** The heavens will praise Your wonders, Lord, and indeed Your truth in the assembly of the holy ones.

**88:7** For who in the clouds will be equal to the Lord, or be like God among the sons of God?

**88:8** God, who is glorified in the council of the holy ones, is great and fearsome above all who are around Him.

**88:9** Lord God of hosts, who is like You? You are mighty, Lord, and Your truth is around You.

**88:10** You rule over the power of the sea, and You calm the motion of its waves.

**88:11** You have humbled the proud one like a wounded man; with the arm of Your strength You have scattered Your enemies.

**88:12** Yours are the heavens, and Yours is the earth; the world and its fullness You have founded;

**88:13** the north and the sea You have created. Tabor and Hermon will exult in Your name.

**88:14** Your arm is with power. Let Your hand be made firm, and let Your right hand be exalted.

**88:15** Righteousness and judgment are the preparation of Your throne; mercy and truth will go before Your face.

**88:16** Blessed is the people that knows jubilation; Lord, they will walk in the light of Your countenance,

**88:17** and in Your name they will exult all day long, and in Your righteousness they will be exalted.

**88:18** For You are the glory of their strength, and in Your good pleasure our horn will be exalted.

**88:19** For our support belongs to the Lord, and to the Holy One of Israel, our King.

**88:20** Then You spoke in a vision to Your holy ones, and said, "I have placed help upon a mighty one, and I have exalted a chosen one from My people.

**88:21** I have found David, My servant; with My holy oil I have anointed him.

**88:22** For My hand will aid him, and My arm will strengthen him.

**88:23** The enemy will gain nothing against him, and the son of iniquity will not proceed to harm him.

**88:24** And I will cut down his enemies from before his face, and those who hate him I will turn to flight.

**88:25** And My truth and My mercy will be with him, and in My name his horn will be exalted.

**88:26** And I will place his hand upon the sea, and his right hand upon the rivers.

**88:27** He will call upon Me, 'You are my Father, my God, and the upholder of my salvation.'

**88:28** And I will make him the firstborn, high above the kings of the earth.

**88:29** Forever I will keep My mercy for him, and My covenant will be faithful to him.

**88:30** And I will set his offspring forever and ever, and his throne like the days of heaven.

**88:31** But if his sons abandon My law, and do not walk in My judgments;

**88:32** if they profane My ordinances, and do not keep My commandments:

**88:33** I will visit their iniquities with a rod, and their sins with lashes;

**88:34** yet I will not scatter My mercy from him, nor will I do harm in My truth,

**88:35** nor will I profane My covenant, and what proceeds from My lips I will not make void.

**88:36** Once I have sworn by My holiness, I will not lie to David:

**88:37** his offspring will remain forever. And his throne will be like the sun in My sight,

**88:38** and like the moon perfected forever, and a faithful witness in heaven."

**88:39** Yet You have rejected and despised; You have put off Your anointed one.

**88:40** You have overturned the covenant of Your servant; You have profaned his sanctuary on the ground.

**88:41** You have destroyed all his hedges; You have made his stronghold a terror.

**88:42** All who pass along the way have plundered him; he has become a reproach to his neighbors.

**88:43** You have exalted the right hand of those who oppress him; You have made all his enemies glad.

**88:44** You have turned away the help of his sword, and You have not aided him in war.

**88:45** You have destroyed him from purification, and You have dashed his throne to the ground.

**88:46** You have shortened the days of his time; You have poured shame over him.

**88:47** How long, Lord, do You turn away to the end? Will Your wrath blaze up like fire?

**88:48** Remember what my substance is; for have You established all the sons of men in vain?

**88:49** Who is the man who will live and not see death? Will he deliver his soul from the hand of the underworld?

**88:50** Where are Your ancient mercies, Lord, as You swore to David in Your truth?

**88:51** Be mindful, Lord, of the reproach of Your servants, which I have held in my bosom, of many nations,

**88:52** with which Your enemies have cast reproach, Lord; with which they have reproached the exchange of Your anointed one.

**88:53** Blessed be the Lord forever. So be it, so be it.

## Translator Notes

- **88:3** — Vulgate reads second-person 'dixisti' ('You have said', following the Septuagint) rather than the Hebrew first-person 'I said', while retaining 'veritas tua' ('Your truth') in the following clause.
- **88:11** — Vulgate 'superbum' ('the proud one') and 'vulneratum' ('a wounded man') follow the Septuagint where the Hebrew text reads 'Rahab' and 'one who is slain'; similarly, 88:13 has 'mare' ('the sea') where Hebrew has 'south'.
- **88:36** — The Latin literally uses the biblical oath formula 'si David mentiar' ('if I should lie to David'), an idiomatic emphatic denial meaning 'I will not lie to David'.
- **88:45** — Vulgate 'Destruxisti eum ab emundatione' ('You have destroyed him from purification') follows the Septuagint where the Hebrew text is commonly rendered 'You have made his splendor cease'.
- **88:52** — Vulgate 'commutationem' ('exchange', following the Septuagint) stands where the Hebrew text reads 'footsteps'; in 88:53 Latin 'Fiat, fiat' ('So be it, so be it') translates Hebrew 'Amen, amen'.
