## Text

**132:1** A song of the steps, of David. Look, how good and how delightful it is for brothers to dwell as one!

**132:2** Like ointment on the head, which descends onto the beard, the beard of Aaron, which descends onto the border of his garment;

**132:3** like the dew of Hermon, which descends onto Mount Zion. For there the Lord commanded blessing, and life forever.

## Translator Notes

- **132:2** — The Vulgate reads simply unguentum ("ointment") without the Hebrew adjective "precious," and renders oram vestimenti ejus ("the border of his garment") in the singular following the Septuagint rather than the Hebrew plural "his robes."
- **132:3** — The Vulgate reads singular montem Sion ("Mount Zion"), whereas the Hebrew and Septuagint have plural "mountains of Zion," and includes et ("and life forever") following the Septuagint.
