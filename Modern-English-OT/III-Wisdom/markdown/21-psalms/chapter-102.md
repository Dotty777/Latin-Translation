## Text

**102:1** For David himself. Bless the Lord, my soul, and everything that is within me, His holy name.

**102:2** Bless the Lord, my soul, and do not forget all His recompenses.

**102:3** It is He who pardons all your iniquities, who heals all your infirmities;

**102:4** who redeems your life from destruction, who crowns you with mercy and compassion;

**102:5** who fills your desire with good things; your youth will be renewed like an eagle's.

**102:6** The Lord performs acts of mercy, and judgment for all who suffer wrong.

**102:7** He made His ways known to Moses; to the sons of Israel, His will.

**102:8** Compassionate and merciful is the Lord: patient, and greatly merciful.

**102:9** He will not be angry perpetually, nor will He threaten forever.

**102:10** He has not done to us according to our sins, nor has He repaid us according to our iniquities.

**102:11** For according to the height of heaven above the earth, He has strengthened His mercy over those who fear Him;

**102:12** as far as the east is distant from the west, He has put our iniquities far from us.

**102:13** Just as a father has compassion on his children, the Lord has had compassion on those who fear Him.

**102:14** For He Himself knows our formation; He has remembered that we are dust.

**102:15** Man: his days are like grass; like a flower of the field, so he will blossom:

**102:16** for a wind will pass through him, and he will not endure, and he will no longer know his place.

**102:17** But the mercy of the Lord is from eternity and even to eternity upon those who fear Him. And His righteousness is to children's children,

**102:18** to those who keep His covenant and are mindful of His commandments to do them.

**102:19** The Lord has prepared His throne in heaven, and His kingdom will rule over all.

**102:20** Bless the Lord, all His angels, mighty in strength, doing His word, to hear the voice of His words.

**102:21** Bless the Lord, all His powers; His ministers, who do His will.

**102:22** Bless the Lord, all His works: in every place of His dominion, bless the Lord, my soul.

## Translator Notes

- **102:5** — Latin desiderium tuum ("your desire") follows the Septuagint (epithymian), where the Hebrew has a rare noun of uncertain meaning (perhaps "your years" or "your prime").
- **102:6** — Latin misericordias ("acts of mercy") follows the Septuagint (eleemosynas), whereas the Hebrew reads "righteous deeds."
- **102:7** — Latin voluntates suas is literally plural ("His wills," following the Septuagint thelemata), whereas the Hebrew reads "His deeds."
- **102:9** — Latin comminabitur literally means "will threaten," whereas the Hebrew means "will keep His anger."
- **102:16** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate makes locum suum ("his place") the direct object ("he will no longer know his place"), whereas the Hebrew makes "its place" the subject ("its place knows it no more").
