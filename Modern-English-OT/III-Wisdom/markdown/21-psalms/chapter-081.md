## Text

**81:1** A psalm of Asaph. God has stood in the assembly of the gods, and in the midst He passes judgment on the gods.

**81:2** "How long do you judge unjustly and show partiality to sinners?

**81:3** Judge for the needy and the orphan; vindicate the humble and the poor.

**81:4** Rescue the poor, and free the needy from the hand of the sinner.

**81:5** They have neither known nor understood; they walk in darkness; all the foundations of the earth will be shaken.

**81:6** I said, 'You are gods, and you are all sons of the Most High.'

**81:7** Yet you will die like men, and like one of the princes you will fall."

**81:8** Arise, God, judge the earth, for You will inherit among all the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **81:1** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads "in the assembly of the gods" (in synagoga deorum) and takes "the gods" (deos) as the direct object of "passes judgment on" (dijudicat), whereas the Hebrew reads "in the divine assembly; in the midst of the gods He judges."
- **81:2** — Literally "judge iniquity and accept the faces of sinners," a biblical idiom for rendering unjust verdicts and showing partiality; the Vulgate uses the present tense and "sinners" (peccatorum) rather than "the wicked."
- **81:8** — The Vulgate preserves the literal prepositional phrase "among [lit. in] all the nations" (in omnibus gentibus), reflecting the Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew idiom for possessing all the nations.
