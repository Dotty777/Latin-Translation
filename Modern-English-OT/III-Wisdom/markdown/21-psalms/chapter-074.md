## Text

**74:1** To the end, do not destroy. A psalm of a song of Asaph.

**74:2** We will give thanks to You, God, we will give thanks, and we will call upon Your name; we will recount Your wonders.

**74:3** When I have received the time, I will judge with righteousness.

**74:4** The earth has melted, and all who dwell in it; I have made firm its pillars.

**74:5** I said to the wicked, "Do not act wickedly," and to those who transgress, "Do not lift up the horn;

**74:6** do not raise your horn on high; do not speak wickedness against God."

**74:7** For neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the desert mountains;

**74:8** because God is judge. One He humbles, and another He lifts up;

**74:9** for in the hand of the Lord is a cup of pure wine, full of mixture. And He has poured from this into that; yet its dregs have not been emptied: all the sinners of the earth will drink.

**74:10** But I will declare forever; I will sing to the God of Jacob,

**74:11** and I will shatter all the horns of sinners, and the horns of the righteous one will be lifted up.

## Translator Notes

- **74:2** — Vulgate "and we will call upon Your name" (following the Septuagint) differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "for Your name is near."
- **74:6** — Vulgate "wickedness against God" (following the Septuagint) differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "with an insolent neck."
- **74:7** — Vulgate "nor from the desert mountains" follows the Septuagint in reading the Hebrew plural noun as "mountains" rather than "lifting up," and preserves the elliptical lack of a main verb.
- **74:9** — Latin "vini meri, plenus misto" ("of pure wine, full of mixture") pairs undiluted, strong wine with mixed spices or additives.
