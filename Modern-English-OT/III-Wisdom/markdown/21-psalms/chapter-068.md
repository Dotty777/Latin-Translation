## Text

**68:1** To the end, for those who will be changed. Of David.

**68:2** Save me, God, for the waters have entered even to my soul.

**68:3** I am stuck fast in the mire of the deep, and there is no standing ground. I have come into the depth of the sea, and a storm has submerged me.

**68:4** I have grown weary crying out, my throat has become hoarse; my eyes have failed while I hope in my God.

**68:5** Those who hate me without cause have multiplied beyond the hairs of my head. My enemies who have persecuted me unjustly have grown strong; what I did not seize, then I was paying back.

**68:6** God, You know my foolishness, and my offenses are not hidden from You.

**68:7** May those who wait for You not be ashamed because of me, Lord, Lord of hosts; may those who seek You not be put to shame over me, God of Israel.

**68:8** For on account of You I have endured reproach; shame has covered my face.

**68:9** I have become an outsider to my brothers, and a foreigner to the sons of my mother.

**68:10** Because zeal for Your house has devoured me, and the reproaches of those who cast reproach on You have fallen upon me.

**68:11** And I covered my soul in fasting, and it became a reproach to me.

**68:12** And I made my garment sackcloth, and I became a proverb to them.

**68:13** Those who sat in the gate were speaking against me, and those who drank wine were singing songs against me.

**68:14** But as for me, my prayer is to You, Lord; a time of good pleasure, God. In the multitude of Your mercy, hear me in the truth of Your salvation.

**68:15** Rescue me from the mud, so that I may not be stuck fast; deliver me from those who hate me, and from the depths of the waters.

**68:16** May the storm of water not submerge me, nor may the deep swallow me up, nor may the pit press its mouth over me.

**68:17** Hear me, Lord, for Your mercy is kind; according to the multitude of Your compassions, look upon me.

**68:18** And do not turn Your face away from Your servant; because I am afflicted, hear me quickly.

**68:19** Attend to my soul and deliver it; because of my enemies, rescue me.

**68:20** You know my reproach, and my shame, and my humiliation;

**68:21** in Your sight are all who afflict me. My heart expected reproach and misery; and I waited for someone who would grieve together with me, and there was no one, and for someone who would comfort me, and I found no one.

**68:22** And they gave gall as my food, and in my thirst they made me drink vinegar.

**68:23** May their table before them turn into a snare, and into retributions, and into a stumbling block.

**68:24** May their eyes be darkened so that they may not see, and bend their back down always.

**68:25** Pour out Your wrath upon them, and may the fury of Your wrath seize them.

**68:26** May their dwelling become desolate, and in their tents may there be no one who dwells.

**68:27** For they have persecuted the one whom You struck, and they have added to the pain of my wounds.

**68:28** Add iniquity upon their iniquity, and may they not enter into Your righteousness.

**68:29** May they be erased from the book of the living, and may they not be written down with the righteous.

**68:30** I am poor and in pain; Your salvation, God, has upheld me.

**68:31** I will praise the name of God with a song, and I will magnify Him in praise;

**68:32** and it will please God more than a young bull bringing forth horns and hooves.

**68:33** May the poor see and be glad; seek God, and your soul will live;

**68:34** for the Lord has heard the poor and has not despised His prisoners.

**68:35** May the heavens and the earth praise Him, the sea, and all creeping creatures in them.

**68:36** For God will save Zion, and the cities of Judah will be built, and they will dwell there and acquire it by inheritance.

**68:37** And the seed of His servants will possess it, and those who love His name will dwell in it.

## Translator Notes

- **68:1** — The Vulgate superscription "To the end, for those who will be changed" (In finem, pro iis qui commutabuntur) follows the Septuagint; the Hebrew heading reads "For the choir director, according to Lilies."
- **68:11** — Vulgate operui ("I covered") is a distinctly Vulgate reading, whereas the Septuagint reads "I humbled/bowed down" and the Hebrew reads "I wept."
- **68:21** — Vulgate exspectavit ("expected") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads "reproach has broken my heart." In v. 20, Latin reverentiam ("humiliation") renders the Greek noun for shame or disgrace (entrope).
- **68:24** — Vulgate dorsum eorum semper incurva ("bend their back down always") follows the Septuagint (cf. Rom 11:10), whereas the Hebrew reads "make their loins continually shake."
- **68:27** — Vulgate super dolorem vulnerum meorum addiderunt ("they have added to the pain of my wounds") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads "they recount the pain of those whom You have wounded."
