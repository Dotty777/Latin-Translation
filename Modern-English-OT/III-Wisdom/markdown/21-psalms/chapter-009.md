## Text

**9:1** To the end, for the hidden things of the son. A psalm of David.

**9:2** I will give thanks to You, Lord, with my whole heart; I will recount all Your wonders.

**9:3** I will be glad and exult in You; I will sing psalms to Your name, Most High.

**9:4** When my enemy is turned backward, they will grow weak and perish from before Your face.

**9:5** For You have carried out my judgment and my cause; You have sat upon the throne, You who judge righteousness.

**9:6** You have rebuked the nations, and the ungodly one has perished; You have blotted out their name forever, and for age upon age.

**9:7** The swords of the enemy have failed to the end, and You have destroyed their cities. Their memory has perished with a sound,

**9:8** and the Lord remains forever. He has prepared His throne in judgment,

**9:9** and He Himself will judge the world in equity; He will judge the peoples in righteousness.

**9:10** And the Lord has become a refuge for the poor man, a helper in times of need, in tribulation.

**9:11** And let those who know Your name hope in You, for You have not abandoned those who seek You, Lord.

**9:12** Sing psalms to the Lord, who dwells in Zion; proclaim among the nations His endeavors,

**9:13** for He who requires their blood has remembered; He has not forgotten the cry of the poor.

**9:14** Have mercy on me, Lord; see my humiliation from my enemies,

**9:15** You who lift me up from the gates of death, so that I may proclaim all Your praises in the gates of the daughter of Zion;

**9:16** I will exult in Your salvation. The nations have sunk fast in the destruction that they made; in this snare that they hid, their foot has been caught.

**9:17** The Lord will be known executing judgments; in the works of his own hands the sinner has been caught.

**9:18** Let sinners be turned into the underworld, all the nations that forget God.

**9:19** For there will not be forgetfulness of the poor man to the end; the endurance of the poor will not perish to the end.

**9:20** Rise up, Lord; let man not grow strong; let the nations be judged in Your sight.

**9:21** Appoint, Lord, a lawgiver over them, so that the nations may know that they are men.

**9:22** Why, Lord, have You departed far off; do You look away in times of need, in tribulation?

**9:23** While the ungodly one grows proud, the poor man is set on fire; they are caught in the schemes that they devise.

**9:24** For the sinner is praised in the desires of his soul, and the unjust man is blessed.

**9:25** The sinner has provoked the Lord; according to the multitude of his anger, he will not seek.

**9:26** God is not in his sight; his ways are defiled at all times. Your judgments are removed from before his face; he will have dominion over all his enemies.

**9:27** For he has said in his heart, "I will not be moved from generation to generation, without evil."

**9:28** His mouth is full of cursing, and bitterness, and deceit; under his tongue are toil and pain.

**9:29** He sits in ambush with the rich in hidden places, so that he may kill the innocent.

**9:30** His eyes look upon the poor man; he lies in ambush in a concealed place, like a lion in his den. He lies in ambush to seize the poor man; to seize the poor man while he draws him in.

**9:31** In his snare he will bring him low; he will bow himself down and fall when he has gained dominion over the poor.

**9:32** For he has said in his heart, "God has forgotten; He has turned away His face so that He will not see to the end."

**9:33** Rise up, Lord God; let Your hand be lifted up; do not forget the poor.

**9:34** For what reason has the ungodly one provoked God? For he has said in his heart, "He will not inquire."

**9:35** You see, for You consider toil and pain, so that You may deliver them into Your hands. To You the poor man has been left; to the orphan You will be a helper.

**9:36** Crush the arm of the sinner and the evildoer; his sin will be sought, and it will not be found.

**9:37** The Lord will reign forever, and for age upon age; you will perish, nations, from His land.

**9:38** The Lord has heard the desire of the poor; Your ear has heard the preparation of their heart,

**9:39** to judge for the fatherless and the humble, so that man may no longer continue to magnify himself upon the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — Following the Septuagint, Vulgate Psalm 9 combines Hebrew Psalms 9 and 10 into a single psalm (Hebrew Psalm 10 begins at 9:22); the heading "for the hidden things of the son" reflects the Septuagint reading where the Hebrew has "according to Muth-labben."
- **9:7** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads "swords" (frameae) and "with a sound" (cum sonitu), whereas the Hebrew reads "ruins" and "they themselves."
- **9:21** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads "a lawgiver" (legislatorem), whereas the Hebrew reads "terror" (or "fear").
- **9:25** — The Vulgate describes the sinner refusing to seek God because of his own abundant anger ("according to the multitude of his anger, he will not seek"), whereas the Hebrew depicts the wicked in his pride claiming that God will not call him to account.
- **9:29** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads "with the rich" (cum divitibus), whereas the Hebrew reads "in the villages."
