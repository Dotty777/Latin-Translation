## Text

**72:1** A psalm of Asaph. How good God is to Israel, to those who are upright in heart!

**72:2** But my feet were nearly moved, my steps were nearly poured out,

**72:3** because I was envious of the unrighteous, seeing the peace of sinners.

**72:4** For there is no regard for their death, and there is firmness in their affliction.

**72:5** They are not in the toil of men, and with men they will not be scourged.

**72:6** Therefore pride has taken hold of them; they are covered with their iniquity and ungodliness.

**72:7** Their iniquity has come forth as if from fatness; they have gone over into the desire of the heart.

**72:8** They have thought and spoken wickedness; they have spoken iniquity on high.

**72:9** They have set their mouth against heaven, and their tongue has passed over the earth.

**72:10** Therefore my people will turn here, and full days will be found in them.

**72:11** And they said, "How does God know, and is there knowledge in the Most High?"

**72:12** Look, these are sinners, and abounding in the world, they have obtained riches.

**72:13** And I said, "Therefore without cause I have justified my heart, and washed my hands among the innocent,

**72:14** and I have been scourged all day long, and my chastisement has been in the mornings."

**72:15** If I said, "I will speak like this," look, I condemned the race of Your children.

**72:16** I pondered in order to know this; it is a toil before me,

**72:17** until I enter into the sanctuary of God, and understand their final end.

**72:18** Nevertheless, because of deceits You have set it for them; You cast them down while they were being lifted up.

**72:19** How have they been made into a desolation? Suddenly they have failed; they have perished because of their iniquity.

**72:20** Like the dream of those who rise, Lord, in Your city You will bring their image to nothing.

**72:21** For my heart was inflamed, and my kidneys were changed;

**72:22** and I was brought to nothing, and I did not know;

**72:23** I became like a beast before You, and I am always with You.

**72:24** You have held my right hand, and in Your will You have led me, and with glory You have received me.

**72:25** For what do I have in heaven? And apart from You, what have I desired upon the earth?

**72:26** My flesh and my heart have failed; God of my heart, and my portion, God forever.

**72:27** For look, those who distance themselves from You will perish; You have destroyed all who commit fornication apart from You.

**72:28** But for me it is good to cling to God, to place my hope in the Lord God, so that I may declare all Your praises in the gates of the daughter of Zion.

## Translator Notes

- **72:4** — Latin respectus morti eorum ('regard for their death') and firmamentum in plaga eorum ('firmness in their affliction') follow the Septuagint against the Hebrew 'pangs in their death' and 'sound is their body.'
- **72:10** — Latin dies pleni ('full days') follows the Septuagint where the Hebrew reads 'waters of fullness'; similarly in v. 7 Latin iniquitas eorum ('their iniquity') differs from the Hebrew 'their eyes.'
- **72:18** — Latin propter dolos posuisti eis ('because of deceits You have set it for them') follows the Septuagint's elliptical phrasing where the Hebrew has 'in slippery places You set them.'
- **72:20** — Latin in civitate tua ('in Your city') follows the Septuagint reading of a Hebrew form that otherwise means 'on awakening.'
- **72:28** — The closing phrase in portis filiae Sion ('in the gates of the daughter of Zion') follows the Septuagint and is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
