## Text

**1:1** Blessed is the man who has not gone in the counsel of the ungodly, and has not stood in the way of sinners, and has not sat in the seat of pestilence.

**1:2** But in the law of the Lord is his will, and on His law he will meditate day and night.

**1:3** And he will be like a tree that has been planted beside channels of water, which will give its fruit in its season; and its leaf will not fall away, and everything that he will do will prosper.

**1:4** Not so the ungodly, not so, but like dust that the wind drives away from the face of the earth.

**1:5** Therefore the ungodly will not rise again in judgment, nor sinners in the council of the righteous.

**1:6** For the Lord knows the way of the righteous, and the path of the ungodly will perish.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Vulgate "cathedra pestilentiae" ("seat of pestilence") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "seat of scoffers."
- **1:3** — Vulgate "non defluet" ("will not fall away") follows the Septuagint ("will not drop off"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "will not wither."
- **1:4** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate repeats "non sic" ("Not so ... not so"), renders "pulvis" ("dust") rather than Hebrew "chaff," and includes "a facie terrae" ("from the face of the earth").
- **1:5** — Vulgate "non resurgent" ("will not rise again") follows the Septuagint ("will not rise up"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "will not stand."
