## Text

**113:1** Hallelujah. At the departure of Israel from Egypt, of the house of Jacob from a foreign people,

**113:2** Judea became His sanctuary; Israel, His dominion.

**113:3** The sea saw and fled; the Jordan was turned backward.

**113:4** The mountains leaped like rams, and the hills like lambs of the sheep.

**113:5** What is the matter with you, sea, that you fled? And you, Jordan, that you were turned backward?

**113:6** Mountains, did you leap like rams? And hills, like lambs of the sheep?

**113:7** Before the face of the Lord the earth was shaken, before the face of the God of Jacob,

**113:8** who turned the rock into pools of water, and the cliff into springs of water.

**113:9** Not to us, Lord, not to us, but to Your name give glory,

**113:10** because of Your mercy and Your truth, so that the nations may never say, "Where is their God?"

**113:11** But our God is in heaven; all that He willed, He has done.

**113:12** The idols of the nations are silver and gold, the works of human hands.

**113:13** They have a mouth, and they will not speak; they have eyes, and they will not see.

**113:14** They have ears, and they will not hear; they have nostrils, and they will not smell.

**113:15** They have hands, and they will not feel; they have feet, and they will not walk; they will not cry out with their throat.

**113:16** May those who make them become like them, and all who trust in them.

**113:17** The house of Israel hoped in the Lord; He is their helper and their protector.

**113:18** The house of Aaron hoped in the Lord; He is their helper and their protector.

**113:19** Those who fear the Lord hoped in the Lord; He is their helper and their protector.

**113:20** The Lord was mindful of us and blessed us. He blessed the house of Israel; He blessed the house of Aaron.

**113:21** He blessed all who fear the Lord, the small along with the great.

**113:22** May the Lord add to you, to you and to your children.

**113:23** Blessed are you by the Lord, who made heaven and earth.

**113:24** The heaven of heaven belongs to the Lord, but the earth He gave to the children of men.

**113:25** The dead will not praise You, Lord, nor all who descend into the underworld;

**113:26** but we who live bless the Lord from this time on and forever.

## Translator Notes

- **113:1** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate combines Hebrew Psalms 114 (vv. 1-8) and 115 (vv. 9-26) into a single psalm and places "Hallelujah" at its opening. Latin "populo barbaro" refers to a people of foreign speech.
- **113:2** — Vulgate "Judæa" ("Judea") follows the Septuagint where the Hebrew reads "Judah," and "sanctificatio" literally means "sanctification" or "sanctuary."
- **113:7** — Vulgate "mota est" ("was shaken") is a past passive indicative following the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew has an imperative ("tremble").
- **113:17** — Following the Septuagint, verses 17-19 use past indicative verbs ("hoped") rather than the Hebrew imperatives ("trust"), read "house of Israel" in verse 17, and render "protector" where the Hebrew has "shield"; verses 20-21 likewise use past tense ("blessed") where the Hebrew has future.
- **113:25** — Vulgate "in infernum" ("into the underworld," literally "the lower region/hell") renders Septuagint "Hades" where the Hebrew reads "into silence," and the Vulgate adds the vocative "Domine" ("Lord").
