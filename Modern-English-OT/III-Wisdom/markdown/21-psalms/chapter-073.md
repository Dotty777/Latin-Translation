## Text

**73:1** Understanding of Asaph. Why, God, have You cast us off to the end; has Your fury grown angry against the sheep of Your pasture?

**73:2** Remember Your congregation, which You have possessed from the beginning. You redeemed the rod of Your inheritance, Mount Zion, in which You dwelt.

**73:3** Lift up Your hands against their acts of pride to the end; how much evil the enemy has done in the holy place!

**73:4** And those who hate You have boasted in the midst of Your solemn feast; they have set up their signs as signs,

**73:5** and they did not know, as in the exit above the highest point. As in a forest of trees, with axes

**73:6** they cut down its doors all at once; with axe and hatchet they cast it down.

**73:7** They have burned Your sanctuary with fire; on the ground they have defiled the tabernacle of Your name.

**73:8** Their kindred said together in their heart, "Let us make all the feast days of God cease from the earth."

**73:9** We have not seen our signs; there is no longer a prophet, and he will know us no more.

**73:10** How long, God, will the enemy reproach? Does the adversary provoke Your name to the end?

**73:11** Why do You turn away Your hand, and Your right hand from the midst of Your bosom to the end?

**73:12** Yet God is our King before the ages; He has worked salvation in the midst of the earth.

**73:13** You strengthened the sea in Your power; You crushed the heads of the dragons in the waters.

**73:14** You shattered the heads of the dragon; You gave him as food to the peoples of the Ethiopians.

**73:15** You broke open springs and torrents; You dried up the rivers of Ethan.

**73:16** Yours is the day, and Yours is the night; You fashioned the dawn and the sun.

**73:17** You made all the boundaries of the earth; summer and spring, You formed them.

**73:18** Remember this: the enemy has reproached the Lord, and a foolish people has provoked Your name.

**73:19** Do not hand over to the beasts the souls confessing to You, and do not forget the souls of Your poor to the end.

**73:20** Look upon Your covenant, for those of the earth who have been darkened have been filled with houses of iniquities.

**73:21** May the humble not be turned away, brought to shame; the poor and the needy will praise Your name.

**73:22** Rise up, God, judge Your cause; remember Your reproaches, those that come from the fool all day long.

**73:23** Do not forget the voices of Your enemies; the pride of those who hate You ascends always.

## Translator Notes

- **73:5** — Latin sicut in exitu super summum ('as in the exit above the highest point') literally reflects the Septuagint's obscure rendering of this difficult verse; in v. 8, 'make all the feast days cease' likewise follows the Septuagint against the Hebrew 'burned all the meeting places.'
- **73:9** — Latin et nos non cognoscet amplius ('and he will know us no more') follows the Septuagint against the Hebrew 'none among us knows how long'; the unnamed singular subject may refer either to the prophet or to God.
- **73:15** — The Vulgate, following the Septuagint, treats the Hebrew adjective for 'perennial' or 'ever-flowing' as a proper name, Ethan ('the rivers of Ethan'); likewise v. 13 has 'strengthened' for Hebrew 'divided,' v. 14 has 'peoples of the Ethiopians' for 'desert-dwellers,' and v. 17 has 'spring' for 'winter.'
- **73:19** — Latin animas confitentes tibi ('the souls confessing to You') follows the Septuagint where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'the soul of Your turtledove.'
- **73:20** — Latin qui obscurati sunt terrae ('those of the earth who have been darkened') follows the Septuagint's personal rendering of the Hebrew phrase 'the dark places of the earth.'
