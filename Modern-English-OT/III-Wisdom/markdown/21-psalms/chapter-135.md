## Text

**135:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy is forever.

**135:2** Give thanks to the God of gods, for His mercy is forever.

**135:3** Give thanks to the Lord of lords, for His mercy is forever.

**135:4** He who alone does great wonders, for His mercy is forever.

**135:5** He who made the heavens with understanding, for His mercy is forever.

**135:6** He who established the earth above the waters, for His mercy is forever.

**135:7** He who made great lights, for His mercy is forever:

**135:8** the sun for dominion over the day, for His mercy is forever;

**135:9** the moon and the stars for dominion over the night, for His mercy is forever.

**135:10** He who struck Egypt with their firstborn, for His mercy is forever.

**135:11** He who led Israel out from their midst, for His mercy is forever,

**135:12** with a powerful hand and an uplifted arm, for His mercy is forever.

**135:13** He who divided the Red Sea into divisions, for His mercy is forever;

**135:14** and led Israel out through the middle of it, for His mercy is forever;

**135:15** and shook off Pharaoh and his force in the Red Sea, for His mercy is forever.

**135:16** He who led His people through the desert, for His mercy is forever.

**135:17** He who struck great kings, for His mercy is forever;

**135:18** and killed strong kings, for His mercy is forever:

**135:19** Sihon, king of the Amorites, for His mercy is forever;

**135:20** and Og, king of Bashan, for His mercy is forever;

**135:21** and gave their land as an inheritance, for His mercy is forever;

**135:22** an inheritance to Israel, His servant, for His mercy is forever.

**135:23** Because in our lowliness He was mindful of us, for His mercy is forever;

**135:24** and redeemed us from our enemies, for His mercy is forever.

**135:25** He who gives food to all flesh, for His mercy is forever.

**135:26** Give thanks to the God of heaven, for His mercy is forever. Give thanks to the Lord of lords, for His mercy is forever.

## Translator Notes

- **135:6** — Latin "firmavit" ("established / made firm") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads "spread out."
- **135:10** — Latin "cum primogenitis eorum" ("with their firstborn") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew has "in their firstborn."
- **135:12** — Latin "brachio excelso" ("an uplifted arm") follows the Septuagint ("a high arm"), whereas the Hebrew has "an outstretched arm."
- **135:23** — Latin "Quia" ("Because") reflects the causal Septuagint reading, whereas the Hebrew is normally read as a relative clause ("who remembered us").
- **135:26** — The Clementine Vulgate includes a closing repetition of verse 3 ("Give thanks to the Lord of lords, for His mercy is forever") that is absent from the Hebrew text.
