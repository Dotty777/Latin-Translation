## Text

**145:1** Hallelujah, of Haggai and Zechariah.

**145:2** Praise the Lord, my soul. I will praise the Lord in my life; I will sing psalms to my God as long as I exist. Do not trust in princes,

**145:3** in the sons of men, in whom there is no salvation.

**145:4** His spirit will go out, and he will return to his earth; on that day all their thoughts will perish.

**145:5** Blessed is the one whose helper is the God of Jacob; his hope is in the Lord his God,

**145:6** who made heaven and earth, the sea, and everything that is in them;

**145:7** who guards truth forever, renders justice for those suffering wrong, gives food to the hungry. The Lord releases those in shackles;

**145:8** the Lord gives light to the blind. The Lord raises up those who are struck down; the Lord loves the righteous.

**145:9** The Lord guards sojourners; He will uphold the orphan and the widow, and He will destroy the ways of sinners.

**145:10** The Lord will reign for all ages; your God, Zion, from generation to generation.

## Translator Notes

- **145:1** — The attribution to Haggai and Zechariah follows the Septuagint and is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **145:4** — Vulgate "cogitationes eorum" ("their thoughts," following the Septuagint) shifts to the plural pronoun referring back to princes and sons of men, whereas the Hebrew uses the singular ("his plans").
- **145:8** — Vulgate "illuminat caecos" ("gives light to the blind") differs in wording from both the Hebrew ("opens the eyes of the blind") and the Greek Septuagint ("makes the blind wise").
- **145:9** — Vulgate "vias peccatorum disperdet" ("will destroy the ways of sinners") has plural "ways" and "will destroy" (following the Septuagint), whereas the Hebrew has singular "way of the wicked" and "makes crooked/brings to ruin."
