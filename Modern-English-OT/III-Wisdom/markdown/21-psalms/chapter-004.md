## Text

**4:1** To the end, in songs. A psalm of David.

**4:2** When I called out, the God of my righteousness heard me; in distress You made room for me. Have mercy on me, and hear my prayer.

**4:3** Sons of men, how long will you be heavy of heart? Why do you love emptiness and seek falsehood?

**4:4** And know that the Lord has made His holy one wondrous; the Lord will hear me when I cry out to Him.

**4:5** Be angry, and do not sin; over what you say in your hearts, feel remorse on your beds.

**4:6** Offer a sacrifice of righteousness, and hope in the Lord. Many say, "Who shows us good things?"

**4:7** The light of Your face has been sealed upon us, Lord; You have given gladness in my heart.

**4:8** From the fruit of their grain, wine, and oil, they have been multiplied.

**4:9** In peace, at once, I will sleep and rest;

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**4:10** *(after `4:9`)* for You, Lord, have uniquely established me in hope.

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — Latin In finem, in carminibus ("To the end, in songs") follows the Septuagint superscription, whereas the Hebrew reads "For the choirmaster, with stringed instruments."
- **4:3** — Latin gravi corde ("heavy of heart") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "how long will my glory become shame."
- **4:5** — Latin Irascimini ("Be angry") and compungimini ("feel remorse") follow the Septuagint (cf. Eph 4:26), where the Hebrew reads "Tremble" and "be silent."
- **4:7** — Latin Signatum est ("has been sealed") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads an imperative, "Lift up."
- **4:8** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate forms an independent sentence here and includes "oil," whereas the Hebrew compares the psalmist's gladness to the abundance of their grain and new wine.
- **4:9** — Latin in idipsum (literally "in the very same") renders Greek epi to auto ("together / at once").
- **4:10 *(additional)*** — Latin singulariter in spe ("uniquely in hope") follows the Septuagint, where the Hebrew reads "make me dwell alone in safety."
