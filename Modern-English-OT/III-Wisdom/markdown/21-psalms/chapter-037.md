## Text

**37:1** A psalm of David, for remembrance concerning the Sabbath.

**37:2** Lord, do not rebuke me in Your fury, nor discipline me in Your anger,

**37:3** for Your arrows have been driven into me, and You have pressed Your hand firmly upon me.

**37:4** There is no health in my flesh because of Your anger; there is no peace for my bones because of my sins,

**37:5** for my iniquities have passed over my head, and like a heavy burden they have weighed heavily upon me.

**37:6** My scars have festered and decayed because of my foolishness.

**37:7** I have become wretched and bowed down even to the end; all day long I went about saddened.

**37:8** For my loins are filled with mockeries, and there is no health in my flesh.

**37:9** I have been afflicted and exceedingly humbled; I roared from the groaning of my heart.

**37:10** Lord, before You is all my desire, and my groaning is not hidden from You.

**37:11** My heart is troubled; my strength has abandoned me, and the light of my eyes, even that is not with me.

**37:12** My friends and my neighbors drew near against me and stood, and those who were near me stood far off; and those who were seeking my life were using violence.

**37:13** And those who sought out evils for me spoke vanities, and they meditated on deceits all day long.

**37:14** But I, like a deaf man, did not hear, and like a mute man not opening his mouth.

**37:15** And I became like a man not hearing, and having no rebuttals in his mouth.

**37:16** For in You, Lord, I have hoped; You will hear me, Lord my God.

**37:17** For I said, "May my enemies never rejoice over me"; and while my feet are shaken, they have spoken great things against me.

**37:18** For I am ready for scourges, and my pain is in my sight always.

**37:19** For I will declare my iniquity, and I will take thought for my sin.

**37:20** But my enemies live and have grown strong over me, and those who hate me unjustly have been multiplied.

**37:21** Those who repay evil for good were slandering me, because I was pursuing goodness.

**37:22** Do not abandon me, Lord my God; do not depart from me.

**37:23** Attend to my help, Lord God of my salvation.

## Translator Notes

- **37:1** — Vulgate adds "de sabbato" ("concerning the Sabbath," following the Septuagint) to the superscription, a phrase absent from the Hebrew MT.
- **37:4** — Latin "a facie" (literally "from the face of," here and in v. 6) is a literal rendering of a biblical idiom meaning "because of" or "on account of."
- **37:8** — Vulgate "illusionibus" ("mockeries," or in late Latin "illusions/delusions," translating Septuagint "empaigmon") differs from the Hebrew MT, which speaks of "burning" or "inflammation" in the loins.
- **37:12** — Vulgate "adversum me appropinquaverunt" ("drew near against me") and "vim faciebant" ("were using violence") follow the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew MT reads "stand aloof from my plague" and "lay snares."
- **37:18** — Vulgate "in flagella" ("for scourges," following the Septuagint) differs from the Hebrew MT, which reads "ready to stumble" or "ready to fall."
