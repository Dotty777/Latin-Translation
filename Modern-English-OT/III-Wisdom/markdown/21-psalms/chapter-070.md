## Text

**70:1** A psalm of David, of the sons of Jonadab, and of the first captives. In You, Lord, I have hoped; may I not be put to shame forever.

**70:2** In Your righteousness deliver me and rescue me; incline Your ear to me and save me.

**70:3** Be to me a protecting God and a fortified place, so that You may save me; for You are my stronghold and my refuge.

**70:4** My God, rescue me from the hand of the sinner, and from the hand of the one acting against the law and of the unjust;

**70:5** for You are my patience, Lord; Lord, my hope from my youth.

**70:6** In You I have been strengthened from the womb; from the belly of my mother You are my protector; in You is my song always.

**70:7** I have become like a portent to many, and You are a strong helper.

**70:8** May my mouth be filled with praise, so that I may sing Your glory, all day long Your greatness.

**70:9** Do not cast me away in the time of old age; when my strength fails, do not abandon me.

**70:10** For my enemies have spoken about me, and those who were watching my soul took counsel together,

**70:11** saying, "God has abandoned him; pursue and seize him, because there is no one to rescue him."

**70:12** God, do not keep far from me; my God, look to my help.

**70:13** May those who slander my soul be put to shame and fail; may those who seek evil for me be covered with shame and dishonor.

**70:14** But I will always hope, and I will add to all Your praise.

**70:15** My mouth will declare Your righteousness, all day long Your salvation. Since I have not known learning,

**70:16** I will enter into the powers of the Lord; Lord, I will remember Your righteousness alone.

**70:17** God, You have taught me from my youth, and up to now I will proclaim Your wonders.

**70:18** And even into old age and advanced age, God, do not abandon me, until I declare Your arm to every generation that is to come, Your power,

**70:19** and Your righteousness, God, even to the highest heights; the great things that You have done, God: who is like You?

**70:20** What great afflictions, many and evil, You have shown me! And turning back, You have given me life, and from the abysses of the earth You have brought me back again.

**70:21** You have multiplied Your magnificence, and turning back, You have comforted me.

**70:22** For I also will acknowledge Your truth to You with instruments of the psalm, God; I will sing praise to You on the lyre, Holy One of Israel.

**70:23** My lips will exult when I sing to You, and my soul, which You have redeemed.

**70:24** Moreover, my tongue also will meditate on Your righteousness all day long, when those who seek evil for me have been put to shame and humiliated.

## Translator Notes

- **70:1** — The superscription "A psalm of David, of the sons of Jonadab, and of the first captives" follows the Septuagint; the Hebrew text of this psalm (Hebrew Ps 71) has no heading.
- **70:5** — Vulgate patientia mea ("my patience") follows the Septuagint hypomone ("endurance/patience"), whereas the Hebrew reads "my hope."
- **70:15** — Vulgate Quoniam non cognovi litteraturam ("Since I have not known learning") follows the Septuagint ("writings/learning"), whereas the Hebrew reads "for I do not know their numbers."
- **70:20** — The Vulgate past-tense verbs here and in v. 21 (vivificasti, reduxisti, multiplicasti, consolatus es) follow the Septuagint aorists, whereas the Hebrew uses future forms.
