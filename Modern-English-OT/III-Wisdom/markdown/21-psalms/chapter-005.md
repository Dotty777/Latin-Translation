## Text

**5:1** To the end, for her who attains the inheritance. A psalm of David.

**5:2** Receive my words with Your ears, Lord; understand my cry.

**5:3** Attend to the voice of my prayer, my King and my God.

**5:4** For I will pray to You, Lord; in the morning You will hear my voice.

**5:5** In the morning I will stand before You, and I will see that You are not a God who wills iniquity.

**5:6** Neither will a wicked person dwell beside You, nor will the unrighteous remain before Your eyes.

**5:7** You hate all who practice iniquity; You will destroy all who speak falsehood. The Lord will abhor a deceitful man of bloodshed.

**5:8** But I, in the multitude of Your mercy, will enter Your house; I will worship toward Your holy temple in fear of You.

**5:9** Lord, lead me in Your righteousness; because of my enemies, make my way straight in Your sight.

**5:10** For there is no truth in their mouth; their heart is empty.

**5:11** Their throat is an open tomb; with their tongues they acted deceitfully; judge them, God. Let them fall from their designs; according to the multitude of their impieties drive them out, for they have provoked You, Lord.

**5:12** And let all who hope in You be glad; they will exult forever, and You will dwell in them. And all who love Your name will glory in You,

**5:13** for You will bless the righteous one. Lord, as with a shield of Your good will, You have crowned us.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Latin pro ea quæ hæreditatem consequitur ("for her who attains the inheritance") follows the Septuagint, which derived the Hebrew term from the root "inherit" rather than "flutes."
- **5:5** — Latin astabo tibi ("I will stand before You") follows the Septuagint (Hebrew: "I will arrange [my prayer] for You"); videbo quoniam may be read either as "I will see that" (following the Latin punctuation) or "I will watch, for."
- **5:9** — Latin viam meam ("my way") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "Your way."
- **5:12** — Latin habitabis in eis ("You will dwell in them") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads "You will shelter/protect them."
- **5:13** — Latin coronasti nos ("You have crowned us") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "You surround him."
