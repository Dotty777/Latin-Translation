## Text

**30:1** To the end. A psalm of David, for ecstasy.

**30:2** In You, Lord, I have hoped; may I never be put to shame; deliver me in Your righteousness.

**30:3** Incline Your ear to me; hurry to rescue me. Be for me a protecting God and a house of refuge, so that You may save me,

**30:4** for You are my might and my refuge; and for the sake of Your name You will lead me and nourish me.

**30:5** You will bring me out of this snare that they have hidden for me, for You are my protector.

**30:6** Into Your hands I entrust my spirit; You have redeemed me, Lord God of truth.

**30:7** You hate those who observe vanities uselessly; but I have hoped in the Lord.

**30:8** I will exult and be glad in Your mercy, because You have looked upon my lowliness; You have saved my soul from distresses.

**30:9** Nor have You shut me up in the hands of the enemy; You have placed my feet in a broad place.

**30:10** Have mercy on me, Lord, for I am in distress; my eye is troubled in anger, my soul, and my belly.

**30:11** For my life has failed in sorrow, and my years in groans. My strength has been weakened in poverty, and my bones are troubled.

**30:12** Above all my enemies I have become a reproach, and exceedingly so to my neighbors, and a dread to my acquaintances; those who saw me outside fled from me.

**30:13** I have been given to oblivion like a dead man from the heart; I have become like a ruined vessel,

**30:14** for I heard the reviling of many dwelling all around. While they gathered together against me, they took counsel to take my soul.

**30:15** But I have hoped in You, Lord; I said, "You are my God."

**30:16** In Your hands are my lots; rescue me from the hand of my enemies and from those who pursue me.

**30:17** Cause Your face to shine upon Your servant; save me in Your mercy.

**30:18** Lord, may I not be put to shame, for I have called upon You. Let the ungodly be ashamed, and let them be led down into the underworld;

**30:19** let deceitful lips become mute, which speak iniquity against the righteous in pride and in scorn.

**30:20** How great is the abundance of Your sweetness, Lord, which You have hidden for those who fear You; You have accomplished it for those who hope in You in the sight of the sons of men!

**30:21** You will hide them in the hidden place of Your face from the turmoil of men; You will shield them in Your tabernacle from the contradiction of tongues.

**30:22** Blessed be the Lord, for He has made His mercy wondrous to me in a fortified city.

**30:23** But I said in the consternation of my mind, "I have been cast out from the sight of Your eyes." Therefore You heard the voice of my prayer while I cried out to You.

**30:24** Love the Lord, all His holy ones, for the Lord will seek out truth, and He will abundantly repay those who practice pride.

**30:25** Act courageously, and let your heart be strengthened, all you who hope in the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **30:1** — In finem ("To the end") is the standard Vulgate and Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew musical heading ("for the choirmaster"), and pro extasi ("for ecstasy") is a Septuagint/Vulgate addition connected with v. 23.
- **30:7** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads second-person Odisti ("You hate"), whereas the Hebrew has first-person "I hate."
- **30:11** — Vulgate in paupertate ("in poverty") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads "because of my iniquity."
- **30:16** — Vulgate sortes meae ("my lots") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads "my times."
- **30:23** — Vulgate in excessu mentis meae (literally "in the going-out/transport of my mind," rendering Greek ekstasis) conveys sudden dismay or being beside oneself, and ideo ("Therefore") follows the Septuagint where the Hebrew reads "yet" or "nevertheless."
