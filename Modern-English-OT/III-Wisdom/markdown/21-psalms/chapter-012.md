## Text

**12:1** To the end. A psalm of David. How long, Lord, will You forget me to the end? How long do You turn Your face away from me?

**12:2** How long will I place counsels in my soul, sorrow in my heart throughout the day?

**12:3** How long will my enemy be exalted over me?

**12:4** Look, and hear me, Lord my God. Enlighten my eyes, so that I may never fall asleep in death;

**12:5** so that my enemy may never say, "I have prevailed against him." Those who afflict me will exult if I am moved;

**12:6** but I have hoped in Your mercy. My heart will exult in Your salvation. I will sing to the Lord, who has granted good things to me; and I will sing psalms to the name of the Lord Most High.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — Vulgate Psalm 12 corresponds to Psalm 13 in Hebrew numbering. Native Vulgate 12:1 combines the superscription (Saints 12:1) and the opening questions (Saints 12:2). Here "to the end" translates Latin "in finem" (Septuagint "eis telos"), meaning completely or forever, echoing the superscription.
- **12:2** — Native Vulgate 12:2 corresponds to the first half of Saints 12:3; the following question ("How long will my enemy be exalted over me?") now stands in restored native Vulgate 12:3 (Saints 12:3b).
- **12:3** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 12:3 (parallel to the second half of Saints 12:3 / Hebrew 13:2b).
- **12:6** — "and I will sing psalms to the name of the Lord Most High" follows the Vulgate ("et psallam nomini Domini altissimi") and Septuagint; this final clause is absent from the Masoretic Hebrew.
