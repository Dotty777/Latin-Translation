## Text

**134:1** Hallelujah. Praise the name of the Lord; servants, praise the Lord,

**134:2** you who stand in the house of the Lord, in the courtyards of the house of our God.

**134:3** Praise the Lord, because the Lord is good; sing psalms to His name, for it is pleasant.

**134:4** For the Lord chose Jacob for Himself, Israel as a possession for Himself.

**134:5** For I myself know that the Lord is great, and that our God is above all gods.

**134:6** Everything that the Lord willed, He did, in heaven, on earth, in the sea, and in all the depths.

**134:7** Bringing up clouds from the end of the earth, He made flashes of lightning for the rain, He who brings forth the winds from His treasuries.

**134:8** He who struck the firstborn of Egypt, from man even to livestock.

**134:9** And He sent signs and wonders in the midst of you, Egypt, against Pharaoh and against all his servants.

**134:10** He who struck many nations and killed strong kings:

**134:11** Sihon, king of the Amorites, and Og, king of Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Canaan;

**134:12** and gave their land as an inheritance, an inheritance to Israel, His people.

**134:13** Lord, Your name is forever; Lord, Your memorial is through generation and generation.

**134:14** For the Lord will judge His people, and toward His servants He will be moved by prayer.

**134:15** The images of the nations are silver and gold, the works of human hands.

**134:16** They have a mouth, and they will not speak; they have eyes, and they will not see.

**134:17** They have ears, and they will not hear; for indeed there is no breath in their mouth.

**134:18** May those who make them become like them, and all who trust in them.

**134:19** House of Israel, bless the Lord; house of Aaron, bless the Lord.

**134:20** House of Levi, bless the Lord; you who fear the Lord, bless the Lord.

**134:21** Blessed is the Lord out of Zion, He who dwells in Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **134:1** — Latin "laudate, servi, Dominum" treats "servants" as a standalone vocative and "the Lord" as the direct object, following the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew reads "praise, servants of the Lord."
- **134:5** — Latin "Deus noster" ("our God") follows the Septuagint, where the Hebrew has "our Lord."
- **134:14** — Latin "in servis suis deprecabitur" uses "deprecor" passively ("will be moved by prayer / pleaded with"), following the Septuagint ("will be consoled / appealed to"), whereas the Hebrew has "will have compassion on His servants."
- **134:21** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate omits the closing "Hallelujah" found at the end of Hebrew Psalm 135:21, placing it instead at the opening of Vulgate Psalm 135:1.
