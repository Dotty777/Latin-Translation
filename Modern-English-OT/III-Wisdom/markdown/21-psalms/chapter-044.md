## Text

**44:1** To the end, for those who will be changed. For the sons of Korah, for understanding. A song for the beloved.

**44:2** My heart has poured forth a good word; I myself speak my works to the king. My tongue is the reed pen of a scribe writing swiftly.

**44:3** You are handsome in form above the sons of men; grace has been poured out on Your lips; therefore God has blessed You forever.

**44:4** Gird Yourself with Your sword upon Your thigh, most mighty One.

**44:5** In Your splendor and Your beauty, press on, go forth prosperously, and reign, for the sake of truth, gentleness, and righteousness; and Your right hand will lead You wonderfully.

**44:6** Your arrows are sharp; peoples will fall beneath You, into the hearts of the king's enemies.

**44:7** Your throne, God, is forever and ever; a scepter of rectitude is the scepter of Your kingdom.

**44:8** You have loved righteousness and hated iniquity; therefore God, Your God, has anointed You with oil of gladness above Your companions.

**44:9** Myrrh, stacte, and cassia come from Your garments, from ivory houses, out of which they have delighted You,

**44:10** daughters of kings, in Your honor. The queen has taken her stand at Your right hand in gilded attire, wrapped around in varied color.

**44:11** Listen, daughter, and see, and incline your ear; and forget your people and the house of your father.

**44:12** And the king will desire your beauty, for He Himself is the Lord your God, and they will worship Him.

**44:13** And with gifts the daughters of Tyre will implore your face, all the wealthy of the people.

**44:14** All the glory of her, the king's daughter, is from within, in golden fringes,

**44:15** clothed around in varied colors. Virgins will be led to the king after her; her close companions will be brought to You.

**44:16** They will be brought with gladness and exultation; they will be led into the temple of the king.

**44:17** In place of Your fathers, sons have been born to You; You will appoint them princes over all the earth.

**44:18** They will be mindful of Your name in every generation and generation; therefore the peoples will give praise to You forever, and forever and ever.

## Translator Notes

- **44:1** — "To the end, for those who will be changed" reflects the Vulgate and Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew superscription ("for the choir director, according to lilies"), interpreting the Hebrew terms through roots for "end" and "change."
- **44:9** — Latin gutta (literally "drop") denotes stacte, a fragrant myrrh-resin, where the Hebrew has "aloes." Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate links ex quibus delectaverunt te across the verse boundary to "daughters of kings" in 44:10, whereas the Hebrew refers to stringed instruments.
- **44:12** — The Vulgate explicitly identifies the king as Dominus Deus tuus ("the Lord your God") and uses third-person plural adorabunt eum ("they will worship Him"), whereas the Hebrew reads "your lord; bow down to him."
- **44:14** — The Vulgate retains the double genitive ejus filiae regis ("of her, the king's daughter") and reads ab intus ("from within," following the Septuagint, where the Hebrew means "inside the palace").
- **44:18** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads third-person plural Memores erunt ("They will be mindful"), whereas the Hebrew has first-person singular ("I will cause your name to be remembered").
