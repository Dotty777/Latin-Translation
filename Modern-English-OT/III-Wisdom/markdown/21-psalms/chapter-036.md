## Text

**36:1** A psalm for David himself. Do not be envious of those who do evil, nor be jealous of those who commit iniquity,

**36:2** for like grass they will quickly dry up, and just like green herbs they will swiftly fall away.

**36:3** Hope in the Lord and do goodness, and dwell in the land, and you will be fed on its riches.

**36:4** Take delight in the Lord, and He will give you the petitions of your heart.

**36:5** Reveal your way to the Lord and hope in Him, and He Himself will act.

**36:6** And He will bring out your righteousness like light, and your judgment like midday.

**36:7** Be subject to the Lord and pray to Him. Do not be envious of the one who prospers in his way, of the man who commits injustices.

**36:8** Cease from anger and abandon fury; do not be envious so as to do evil.

**36:9** For those who do evil will be wiped out, but those who wait patiently for the Lord, they themselves will inherit the land.

**36:10** And still a little while, and the sinner will be no more; and you will seek his place, and you will not find it.

**36:11** But the gentle will inherit the land, and they will take delight in an abundance of peace.

**36:12** The sinner will watch the righteous one closely and will gnash his teeth at him.

**36:13** But the Lord will laugh at him, for He foresees that his day will come.

**36:14** Sinners have unsheathed the sword; they have bent their bow to bring down the poor and the needy, to slaughter the upright in heart.

**36:15** May their sword enter into their own hearts, and may their bow be shattered.

**36:16** Better is a little for the righteous one than the abundant riches of sinners,

**36:17** for the arms of sinners will be crushed, yet the Lord strengthens the righteous.

**36:18** The Lord knows the days of the spotless, and their inheritance will be forever.

**36:19** They will not be put to shame in an evil time, and in days of famine they will be satisfied,

**36:20** because sinners will perish. Indeed, the enemies of the Lord, as soon as they have been honored and exalted, vanishing just like smoke, they will vanish.

**36:21** The sinner will borrow and will not repay, but the righteous one shows mercy and will give,

**36:22** for those who bless Him will inherit the land, while those who curse Him will be destroyed.

**36:23** With the Lord the steps of a man will be directed, and He will desire his way.

**36:24** When he falls, he will not be dashed down, because the Lord places His hand underneath.

**36:25** I was young, and indeed I have grown old, and I have not seen the righteous one abandoned, nor his offspring seeking bread.

**36:26** All day long he shows mercy and lends, and his offspring will be in blessing.

**36:27** Turn away from evil and do good, and dwell forever and ever,

**36:28** for the Lord loves judgment and will not abandon His holy ones; they will be preserved forever. The unjust will be punished, and the offspring of the ungodly will perish.

**36:29** But the righteous will inherit the land, and they will dwell upon it forever and ever.

**36:30** The mouth of the righteous one will meditate on wisdom, and his tongue will speak judgment.

**36:31** The law of his God is in his own heart, and his steps will not be tripped up.

**36:32** The sinner observes the righteous one and seeks to put him to death.

**36:33** But the Lord will not abandon him in his hands, nor will He condemn him when judgment is passed on him.

**36:34** Wait for the Lord and keep His way, and He will exalt you so that you may take the land as an inheritance; when sinners perish, you will see it.

**36:35** I saw the ungodly one highly exalted and lifted up like the cedars of Lebanon;

**36:36** and I passed by, and look, he was no more; and I sought him, and his place was not found.

**36:37** Keep innocence and look upon equity, for there is a remnant for a peaceable man.

**36:38** But the unjust will be destroyed together; the remnant of the ungodly will perish.

**36:39** But the salvation of the righteous is from the Lord, and He is their protector in a time of tribulation.

**36:40** And the Lord will help them and deliver them; and He will rescue them from sinners and save them, because they have hoped in Him.

## Translator Notes

- **36:3** — Vulgate "pasceris in divitiis ejus" ("you will be fed on its riches," following the Septuagint) contrasts with the Hebrew MT, which reads "feed on faithfulness" or "cultivate faithfulness."
- **36:5** — Vulgate "Revela" ("Reveal," following the Septuagint) reflects a reading of the Hebrew root as "uncover/reveal" rather than the MT reading "roll/commit."
- **36:20** — Vulgate "mox ut honorificati fuerint et exaltati" ("as soon as they have been honored and exalted," following the Septuagint) differs from the Hebrew MT, which compares the enemies of the Lord to "the splendor of the pastures."
- **36:22** — Vulgate "benedicentes ei" and "maledicentes ... ei" use active participles ("those who bless Him ... those who curse Him," following the Septuagint), whereas the Hebrew MT has passives ("those blessed/cursed by Him"); in the Latin syntax, "ei" may refer either to the Lord or to the righteous one of v. 21.
- **36:35** — Vulgate "sicut cedros Libani" ("like the cedars of Lebanon," following the Septuagint) contrasts with the Hebrew MT, which reads "like a luxuriant native tree."
