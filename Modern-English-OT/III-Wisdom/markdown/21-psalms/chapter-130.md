## Text

**130:1** A song of steps, of David. Lord, my heart has not been exalted, nor have my eyes been lifted up; nor have I walked in great things, nor in marvelous things above me.

**130:2** If I did not think humbly, but have exalted my soul: just as a weaned child is upon his mother, so is retribution in my soul.

**130:3** Let Israel hope in the Lord, from now on and forever.

## Translator Notes

- **130:2** — Vulgate Si non humiliter sentiebam, sed exaltavi animam meam ("If I did not think humbly, but have exalted my soul") follows the Septuagint's conditional oath form against the Hebrew affirmation ("Surely I have calmed and quieted my soul"). Likewise, Vulgate retributio ("retribution") reflects reading the second Hebrew gamul ("weaned child") as gemul ("recompense").
- **130:3** — Latin ex hoc nunc et usque in saeculum, literally "from this now and even into the age," is the standard Vulgate idiom for "from now on and forever."
