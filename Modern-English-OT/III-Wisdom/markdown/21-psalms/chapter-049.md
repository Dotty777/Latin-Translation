## Text

**49:1** A psalm of Asaph. The God of gods, the Lord, has spoken and called the earth from the rising of the sun even to its setting.

**49:2** Out of Zion is the splendor of His beauty:

**49:3** God will come visibly, our God, and He will not be silent. A fire will blaze up in His sight, and all around Him is a mighty storm.

**49:4** He will summon heaven from above, and the earth, to judge His people.

**49:5** Gather to Him His holy ones, who establish His covenant over sacrifices.

**49:6** And the heavens will declare His righteousness, for God is judge.

**49:7** "Hear, My people, and I will speak; Israel, and I will testify to you: I am God, your God.

**49:8** I will not reprove you for your sacrifices, and your burnt offerings are always in My sight.

**49:9** I will not take calves from your house, nor male goats from your flocks:

**49:10** for all the wild animals of the forests are Mine, the beasts on the mountains, and the oxen.

**49:11** I know all the birds of the sky, and the beauty of the field is with Me.

**49:12** If I grow hungry, I will not tell you: for Mine is the world and its fullness.

**49:13** Will I eat the flesh of bulls, or will I drink the blood of male goats?

**49:14** Offer to God a sacrifice of praise, and pay your vows to the Most High.

**49:15** And call upon Me in the day of distress: I will deliver you, and you will honor Me."

**49:16** But to the sinner God said, "Why do you recount My righteous decrees, and take up My covenant through your mouth?

**49:17** Yet you have hated discipline and thrown My words behind you.

**49:18** If you saw a thief, you ran with him, and you placed your portion with adulterers.

**49:19** Your mouth abounded with malice, and your tongue crafted deceits.

**49:20** Sitting, you spoke against your brother, and against the son of your mother you placed a stumbling block.

**49:21** These things you have done, and I kept silent. You have wickedly supposed that I will be like you: I will reprove you, and I will set them before your face.

**49:22** Understand these things, you who forget God; otherwise He may snatch you away at any time, and there will be no one to rescue.

**49:23** A sacrifice of praise will honor Me, and there is the way by which I will show him the salvation of God."

## Translator Notes

- **49:3** — Latin manifeste veniet ("will come visibly") follows the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew places the verb "shines forth" at the end of the preceding verse.
- **49:5** — Latin illi ... ejus ... ejus ("to Him ... His ... His") follows the Septuagint in using third-person pronouns rather than the Hebrew first-person ("to Me ... My ... My").
- **49:10** — Latin jumenta in montibus, et boves ("the beasts on the mountains, and the oxen") follows the Septuagint in reading the Hebrew phrase for "on a thousand hills" as "on the mountains, and oxen"; similarly, 49:11 reads "the beauty of the field" for Hebrew "all that moves in the field."
- **49:18** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads currebas cum eo ("you ran with him") in 49:18 and ponebas scandalum ("you placed a stumbling block") in 49:20, where the Hebrew has "you were pleased with him" and "you cast slander."
- **49:23** — Latin et illic iter quo ostendam illi salutare Dei ("and there is the way by which I will show him the salvation of God") follows the Septuagint in reading the Hebrew consonants as "and there" rather than "and to the one who orders [his way]."
