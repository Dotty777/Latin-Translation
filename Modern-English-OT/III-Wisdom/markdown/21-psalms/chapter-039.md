## Text

**39:1** For the end. A psalm for David himself.

**39:2** Waiting, I waited for the Lord, and He attended to me.

**39:3** And He heard my prayers, and led me out of the pit of misery and out of the mud of sediment. And He set my feet on a rock, and directed my steps.

**39:4** And He put a new song into my mouth, a hymn to our God. Many will see and will fear, and will hope in the Lord.

**39:5** Blessed is the man whose hope is the name of the Lord, and who has not looked toward vanities and false follies.

**39:6** Many are Your wonders that You have done, Lord my God, and in Your thoughts there is no one who is like You. I have proclaimed and spoken: they have been multiplied beyond number.

**39:7** Sacrifice and offering You did not desire, but You perfected ears for me. A burnt offering and a sin offering You did not demand;

**39:8** then I said, "Look, I come. At the head of the book it is written about me,

**39:9** that I should do Your will. My God, I have desired it, and Your law is in the midst of my heart."

**39:10** I have proclaimed Your righteousness in the great assembly; look, I will not restrain my lips: Lord, You know.

**39:11** I have not hidden Your righteousness in my heart; I have spoken of Your truth and Your salvation; I have not hidden Your mercy and Your truth from the vast council.

**39:12** But You, Lord, do not keep Your compassions far from me; Your mercy and Your truth have always upheld me.

**39:13** For evils that have no number have surrounded me; my iniquities have seized me, and I was not able to see. They have been multiplied beyond the hairs of my head, and my heart has abandoned me.

**39:14** May it please You, Lord, to rescue me; Lord, look to help me.

**39:15** May they be put to shame and humiliated together, those who seek my soul to take it away; may they be turned backward and humiliated, those who wish evil for me.

**39:16** May they immediately bear their shame, those who say to me, "Aha, aha!"

**39:17** May all who seek You exult and be glad in You; and may those who love Your salvation always say, "May the Lord be magnified."

**39:18** But I am a beggar and poor; the Lord is concerned for me. You are my helper and my protector; my God, do not delay.

## Translator Notes

- **39:5** — "The name of the Lord" (nomen Domini) follows the Septuagint ("to onoma kyriou"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads simply "the Lord."
- **39:7** — "You perfected ears for me" (aures autem perfecisti mihi) is a distinctive Gallican Vulgate reading combining "ears" (cf. Hebrew "ears You have dug/opened for me") with "perfecisti" from Greek "katertiso," where most Septuagint manuscripts and Hebrews 10:5 read "a body You have prepared for me."
- **39:8** — "At the head of the book" (In capite libri) translates Greek "en kephalidi bibliou" (the roll or heading of the scroll). The Clementine Vulgate attaches "ut facerem voluntatem tuam" ("that I should do Your will") in 39:9 directly to 39:8 rather than to "volui" ("I have desired it").
- **39:12** — "Have always upheld me" (semper susceperunt me) is a past/perfect indicative statement in the Vulgate and Septuagint, contrasting with the jussive wish in the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("may Your steadfast love and faithfulness continually preserve me").
- **39:14** — "Look" (respice) in 39:14, "immediately" (confestim) in 39:16, and "protector" (protector) in 39:18 follow the Septuagint, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "hasten," "because of," and "my deliverer" respectively.
