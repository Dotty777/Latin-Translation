## Text

**33:1** For David, when he altered his face before Ahimelech, and he dismissed him, and he departed.

**33:2** I will bless the Lord at every time; His praise will always be in my mouth.

**33:3** In the Lord my soul will be praised; let the meek hear and be glad.

**33:4** Magnify the Lord with me, and let us exalt His name as one.

**33:5** I sought out the Lord, and He heard me, and out of all my afflictions He rescued me.

**33:6** Draw near to Him and be enlightened, and your faces will not be put to shame.

**33:7** This poor man cried out, and the Lord heard him, and from all his afflictions He saved him.

**33:8** The angel of the Lord will encamp all around those who fear Him and will rescue them.

**33:9** Taste and see that the Lord is sweet; blessed is the man who hopes in Him.

**33:10** Fear the Lord, all His holy ones, because there is no lack for those who fear Him.

**33:11** The rich have been in need and have grown hungry, but those who seek the Lord will not fall short of any good thing.

**33:12** Come, children, hear me; I will teach you the fear of the Lord.

**33:13** Who is the man who desires life, who loves to see good days?

**33:14** Restrain your tongue from evil, and your lips from speaking deceit.

**33:15** Turn aside from evil and do good; seek out peace and pursue it.

**33:16** The eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous, and His ears are toward their prayers.

**33:17** But the face of the Lord is against those who do evil, so that He may destroy their memory from the earth.

**33:18** The righteous cried out, and the Lord heard them, and out of all their afflictions He delivered them.

**33:19** The Lord is near to those who are afflicted in heart, and He will save the humble in spirit.

**33:20** Many are the afflictions of the righteous, and from all these the Lord will deliver them.

**33:21** The Lord guards all their bones; not one of these will be broken.

**33:22** The death of sinners is most evil, and those who hate the righteous man will incur guilt.

**33:23** The Lord will redeem the souls of His servants, and none of those who hope in Him will incur guilt.

## Translator Notes

- **33:1** — The Vulgate reads Achimelech (Ahimelech) and vultum suum ("his face"), whereas the Hebrew reads "Abimelech" and "his behavior/sanity" (cf. 1 Sam 21); Vulgate Psalm 33 corresponds to Psalm 34 in Hebrew numbering.
- **33:3** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate uses the passive laudabitur ("will be praised"), whereas the Hebrew uses a reflexive verb ("will boast").
- **33:6** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate uses second-person plural imperatives (Accedite ... illuminamini) and "your faces," whereas the Hebrew uses third-person plural ("They looked to him and were radiant, and their faces shall not be ashamed").
- **33:11** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads Divites ("The rich"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "The young lions."
- **33:22** — The Vulgate reads Mors peccatorum pessima ("The death of sinners is most evil," intensifying the Septuagint "is evil"), where the Hebrew reads "Evil will slay the wicked"; here and in verse 23, delinquent (literally "will offend/be at fault") carries the judicial sense "will incur guilt."
