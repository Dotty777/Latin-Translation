## Text

**106:1** Hallelujah. Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy is forever.

**106:2** Let those who were redeemed by the Lord say so, whom He redeemed from the hand of the enemy, and He gathered them from the regions,

**106:3** from the rising of the sun and its setting, from the north and the sea.

**106:4** They wandered in the wilderness, in a waterless place; they did not find the way to a city of habitation.

**106:5** Hungering and thirsting, their soul failed within them.

**106:6** And they cried out to the Lord when they were afflicted, and He rescued them from their distresses;

**106:7** and He led them onto a straight way, so that they might go into a city of habitation.

**106:8** Let His mercies give thanks to the Lord, and His wonders to the sons of men.

**106:9** For He has satisfied the empty soul, and the hungry soul He has satisfied with good things.

**106:10** Those sitting in darkness and the shadow of death, bound in destitution and iron.

**106:11** Because they provoked the utterances of God, and they exasperated the counsel of the Most High.

**106:12** And their heart was humbled in labors; they grew weak, and there was no one to help.

**106:13** And they cried out to the Lord when they were afflicted, and He freed them from their distresses.

**106:14** And He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death, and He tore apart their bonds.

**106:15** Let His mercies give thanks to the Lord, and His wonders to the sons of men.

**106:16** For He has crushed bronze gates, and He has broken iron bars.

**106:17** He took them up from the way of their iniquity, for because of their injustices they were humbled.

**106:18** Their soul loathed all food, and they came near, even to the gates of death.

**106:19** And they cried out to the Lord when they were afflicted, and He freed them from their distresses.

**106:20** He sent His word and healed them, and He rescued them from their destructions.

**106:21** Let His mercies give thanks to the Lord, and His wonders to the sons of men.

**106:22** And let them offer a sacrifice of praise, and proclaim His works in exultation.

**106:23** Those who go down to the sea in ships, doing work in many waters:

**106:24** they themselves saw the works of the Lord, and His wonders in the deep.

**106:25** He spoke, and a storm wind arose, and its waves were lifted high.

**106:26** They ascend all the way to the heavens, and they descend all the way to the abysses; their soul was melting away in evils.

**106:27** They were thrown into turmoil, and they were shaken like a drunkard, and all their wisdom was devoured.

**106:28** And they cried out to the Lord when they were afflicted, and He brought them out of their distresses.

**106:29** And He stilled its storm into a breeze, and its waves fell silent.

**106:30** And they rejoiced because they fell silent, and He led them into the harbor of their desire.

**106:31** Let His mercies give thanks to the Lord, and His wonders to the sons of men.

**106:32** And let them exalt Him in the assembly of the people, and let them praise Him in the seat of the elders.

**106:33** He turned rivers into a desert, and outlets of water into thirst;

**106:34** a fruitful land into saltiness, because of the wickedness of those dwelling in it.

**106:35** He turned a desert into pools of water, and a land without water into outlets of water.

**106:36** And He settled the hungry there, and they established a city of habitation:

**106:37** and they sowed fields and planted vineyards, and they produced fruit of birth.

**106:38** And He blessed them, and they were greatly multiplied, and He did not diminish their livestock.

**106:39** And they became few and were harassed by the tribulation of evils and sorrow.

**106:40** Contempt was poured out upon princes, and He made them wander in a trackless place, and not on a way.

**106:41** And He helped the poor man out of poverty, and He made families like sheep.

**106:42** The upright will see and rejoice, and all iniquity will close its mouth.

**106:43** Who is wise, and will observe these things, and understand the mercies of the Lord?

## Translator Notes

- **106:8** — In the Vulgate (and vv. 15, 21, 31), 'His mercies' (misericordiae ejus) is nominative plural and serves as the grammatical subject of 'give thanks' (confiteantur), reflecting the ambiguous neuter plural of the Septuagint; the Hebrew instead makes the redeemed the subject giving thanks to the Lord for His mercies.
- **106:10** — The Vulgate uses accusative participles (sedentes ... vinctos) with no finite verb and reads 'in destitution' (in mendicitate, following the Septuagint's 'poverty'), whereas the Hebrew has 'in affliction.'
- **106:17** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate reads 'He took them up' (Suscepit eos), whereas the Hebrew text begins the verse with 'Fools.'
- **106:37** — The Vulgate's 'fruit of birth' (fructum nativitatis) reflects the Septuagint Greek noun for 'yield' or 'produce' (genematos), rendered in Latin according to its cognate sense of 'birth' or 'offspring.'
