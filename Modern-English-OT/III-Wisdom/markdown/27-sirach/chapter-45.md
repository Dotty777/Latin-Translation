## Text

**45:1** And He preserved for him men of mercy, finding favor in the eyes of all flesh.

**45:2** He made him equal in the glory of the holy ones, and He magnified him in the fear of enemies, and by his words he calmed portents.

**45:3** He glorified him in the sight of kings, and commanded him before His people, and showed him His glory.

**45:4** In his faithfulness and gentleness He made him holy, and chose him out of all flesh.

**45:5** For He heard him and his voice, and brought him into the cloud.

**45:6** He made his brother Aaron exalted, and like him, from the tribe of Levi.

**45:7** He established for him an eternal covenant, and gave him the priesthood of the nation, and blessed him in glory;

**45:8** and He encircled him with a sash of glory, and clothed him in a robe of glory, and crowned him with instruments of strength.

**45:9** He placed on him a foot-length robe, and undergarments, and a shoulder garment, and surrounded him with very many golden bells all around.

**45:10** A holy robe of gold, and blue, and purple, the woven work of a wise man endowed with judgment and truth;

**45:11** with twisted scarlet, the work of a craftsman, with precious stones fashioned in a setting of gold and engraved by the work of a stonecutter, for a memorial according to the number of the tribes of Israel.

**45:12** A golden crown upon his turban, stamped with the sign of holiness and glory of honor: a work of strength, and adorned desires of the eyes.

**45:13** There were no such things so beautiful before him, all the way to the beginning.

**45:14** His sacrifices were consumed by fire daily.

**45:15** Moses filled his hands, and anointed him with holy oil.

**45:16** He chose him from all the living to offer sacrifice to God, incense and a pleasing aroma, for a memorial, to make atonement for His people;

**45:17** and He gave him authority in His precepts, in the covenants of judgments, to teach Jacob the testimonies and in His law to give light to Israel.

**45:18** For outsiders stood against him, and because of envy men surrounded him in the wilderness, those who were with Dathan and Abiram, and the congregation of Korah in wrath.

**45:19** The Lord God saw, and it did not please Him, and they were consumed in the fury of wrath.

**45:20** And He added glory to Aaron, and gave him an inheritance, and He allotted to him the firstfruits of the produce of the land.

**45:21** First of all, He prepared bread for them to the full; for they will also eat the sacrifices of the Lord, which He gave to him and to his offspring.

**45:22** However, in the land he will not inherit nations, and there is no portion for him in the nation; for He Himself is his portion and inheritance.

**45:23** Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, is third in glory, by imitating him in the fear of the Lord and standing in the reverence of the nation; in the goodness and eagerness of his soul he pleased God for Israel.

**45:24** Therefore He established for him a covenant of peace, as ruler of the holy things and of his nation, so that the dignity of the priesthood might belong to him and to his offspring forever.

**45:26** And a covenant for King David, the son of Jesse, from the tribe of Judah, an inheritance for him and for his offspring, so that He might give wisdom into our heart, to judge His nation in righteousness, so that their good things would not be wiped out; and He made their glory eternal for their nation.

## Translator Notes

- **45:5** — Vulgate 'Audivit enim eum, et vocem ipsius' ('For He heard him and his voice') differs from the Greek ('He caused him to hear His voice'), portraying God as hearing Moses rather than making Moses hear God.
- **45:8** — Vulgate 'coronavit eum in vasis virtutis' ('crowned him with instruments of strength') reads 'crowned' where the Greek has 'established/strengthened him with articles of strength.'
- **45:10** — Vulgate 'viri sapientis, judicio et veritate praediti' ('of a wise man endowed with judgment and truth') describes the craftsman rather than naming the high priest's breastpiece of judgment and Urim of truth as in the Greek.
- **45:22** — The Clementine Vulgate reads accusative plural 'gentes' ('will not inherit nations') where the Greek and some Latin witnesses have genitive singular ('in the land of the people he will not inherit').
- **45:23** — Vulgate 'imitando eum' ('imitating him'), 'in reverentia' ('in reverence'), and 'placuit Deo' ('pleased God') differ from the Greek, which speaks of Phinehas being zealous, standing firm in the people's turning away, and making atonement.
