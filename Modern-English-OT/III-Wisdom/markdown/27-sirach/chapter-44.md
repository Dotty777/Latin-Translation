## Text

**44:1** Let us praise glorious men, and our ancestors in their generation.

**44:2** The Lord has made great glory; His magnificence is from eternity.

**44:3** Ruling in their dominions, men great in strength and endowed with their prudence, proclaiming among the prophets the dignity of the prophets,

**44:4** and governing over the present people, and by the strength of prudence speaking most holy words to the peoples,

**44:5** in their skill seeking out musical melodies, and recounting the songs of the Scriptures,

**44:6** men rich in strength, having a zeal for beauty, keeping peace in their houses.

**44:7** All these obtained glory in the generations of their nation, and in their days they are held in praise.

**44:8** Those who were born of them left a name for recounting their praises.

**44:9** And there are some of whom there is no remembrance; they perished like those who had never been, and they were born as though not born, and their children with them.

**44:10** But those are men of mercy, whose acts of devotion have not failed.

**44:11** With their seed good things endure;

**44:12** their descendants are a holy inheritance, and in the covenants their seed stood;

**44:13** and for their sake their children remain forever; their seed and their glory will not be abandoned.

**44:14** Their bodies were buried in peace, and their name lives from generation to generation.

**44:15** Let the peoples recount their wisdom, and let the assembly proclaim their praise.

**44:16** Enoch pleased God, and was carried into paradise, so that he may give repentance to the nations.

**44:17** Noah was found perfect, righteous, and in the time of wrath he became a reconciliation.

**44:18** Therefore a remnant was left to the earth when the flood occurred.

**44:19** Covenants of eternity were placed with him, so that all flesh could not be destroyed by a flood.

**44:20** Abraham was a great father of a multitude of nations, and no one was found like him in glory; he kept the law of the Most High and was in covenant with Him.

**44:21** In his flesh he made the covenant stand, and in testing he was found faithful.

**44:22** Therefore with an oath He gave him glory in his nation, that he would increase like a heap of earth.

**44:23** and to exalt his seed like the stars, and to give them an inheritance from sea to sea, and from the river to the ends of the earth.

**44:24** And in Isaac He acted in the same manner, for the sake of Abraham his father.

**44:25** The Lord gave him the blessing of all nations, and confirmed the covenant upon the head of Jacob.

**44:26** He acknowledged him in His blessings, and gave him an inheritance, and divided for him a portion among twelve tribes.

**44:27** And He preserved for him men of mercy, finding favor in the eyes of all flesh.

## Translator Notes

- **44:3** — Vulgate 'nuntiantes in prophetis dignitatem prophetarum' ('proclaiming among the prophets the dignity of the prophets') expands upon the shorter Greek reading 'declaring in prophecies'.
- **44:8** — Vulgate 'Qui de illis nati sunt' ('Those who were born of them') refers specifically to their descendants, whereas the Greek reads 'There are some of them who left a name'.
- **44:16** — Vulgate adds 'in paradisum' ('into paradise') and reads 'ut det gentibus poenitentiam' ('so that he may give repentance to the nations'), whereas the Greek describes Enoch as 'an example of repentance to the generations'.
- **44:18** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 44:18 (parallel to Saints/Greek 44:17b).
- **44:21** — The subject of 'stare fecit' ('made ... stand') is translated here as Abraham, continuing the verbs of native 44:20-21 (Saints 44:19-20); however, the non-reflexive Latin pronoun 'ejus' ('his') also permits reading God as the subject.
- **44:22** — Vulgate 'dedit illi gloriam in gente sua' ('He gave him glory in his nation') and 'quasi terrae cumulum' ('like a heap of earth') differ from the Greek ('that the nations would be blessed in his offspring' and 'like the dust of the earth'); the concluding clauses of Greek 44:21 now stand in restored native Vulgate 44:23.
- **44:23** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 44:23 (continuing 44:22 = Saints 44:21b; previously claimed omitted in the Vulgate by the note on old 44:21). Latin 'hæreditare illos' uses transitive Late Latin 'hæreditare' ("cause them to inherit / give them an inheritance").
- **44:27** — Native Vulgate places 'Et conservavit illi homines misericordiæ...' at the close of chapter 44 (44:27 = Saints 45:1a), opening chapter 45 with restored native 45:1 ('Dilectus Deo et hominibus Moyses...').
