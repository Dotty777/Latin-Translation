## Text

**15:1** He who fears God will do what is good, and he who holds fast to righteousness will take hold of her,

**15:2** and she will meet him like an honored mother, and like a wife from her virginity she will receive him.

**15:3** She will feed him with the bread of life and understanding, and she will give him the water of saving wisdom to drink. And she will be made firm in him, and he will not be bent;

**15:4** and she will hold him fast, and he will not be put to shame; and she will exalt him among his neighbors,

**15:5** and in the midst of the assembly she will open his mouth, and she will fill him with the spirit of wisdom and understanding, and she will clothe him with a robe of glory.

**15:6** She will store up joy and exultation as treasure upon him, and she will give him an eternal name as an inheritance.

**15:7** Foolish people will not take hold of her, and sensible people will meet her. Foolish people will not see her, for she is far away from pride and deceit.

**15:8** Lying men will not be mindful of her, and truthful men will be found in her, and they will have success until the inspection of God.

**15:9** Praise is not becoming in the mouth of a sinner,

**15:10** because wisdom has come forth from God. For praise will stand beside the wisdom of God, and in a faithful mouth it will abound, and the Sovereign will give it to him.

**15:11** Do not say, "Through God she is absent," for what He hates, do not do.

**15:12** Do not say, "He has led me astray," for ungodly people are not needed by Him.

**15:13** The Lord hates every abomination of error, and it will not be lovable to those who fear Him.

**15:14** From the beginning God established man, and left him in the hand of his own deliberation.

**15:15** If you wish to keep the commandments and to practice pleasing faithfulness forever, they will preserve you.

**15:16** He has placed water and fire before you; stretch out your hand to whichever you wish.

**15:17** Before man are life and death, good and evil; whatever pleases him will be given to him,

**15:18** for the wisdom of God is great, and He is strong in power, seeing everyone without ceasing.

**15:19** The eyes of the Lord are toward those who fear Him, and He Himself recognizes every work of man.

**15:20** He has commanded no one to act impiously, and He has given no one room to sin.

## Translator Notes

- **15:1** — Vulgate justitiæ ("righteousness") differs from the Greek Septuagint, which reads "the law."
- **15:3** — In the Vulgate, Wisdom is the subject of firmabitur in illo ("she will be made firm in him") and continebit illum in v. 4 ("she will hold him fast"), whereas the Greek has the God-fearing person leaning and relying upon her; non flectetur ("will not be bent") may take either the man or Wisdom as subject.
- **15:8** — The clause from "and truthful men" through "the inspection of God" is a Vulgate-distinct expansion absent from the main Greek text; inspectio Dei refers to God's final scrutiny or visitation.
- **15:11** — Vulgate Per Deum abest ("Through God she is absent," or "it is absent") differs from the Greek "Because of the Lord I fell away," echoing Wisdom's absence in v. 7.
- **15:15** — In the Latin word order, conservabunt te ("they will preserve you") stands between the two coordinated infinitives (mandata servare and in perpetuum fidem placitam facere); the Vulgate also reads "they will preserve you" where the Greek has "you will keep them."
