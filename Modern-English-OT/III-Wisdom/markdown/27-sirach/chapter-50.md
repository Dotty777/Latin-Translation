## Text

**50:1** Simon, son of Onias, the high priest, who in his life shored up the house, and in his days strengthened the temple.

**50:2** The height of the temple also was founded by him, the double structure and the lofty walls of the temple.

**50:3** In his days the wells of water flowed forth, and like the sea they were filled beyond measure.

**50:4** He cared for his nation and delivered it from destruction;

**50:5** he prevailed in enlarging the city, attained glory in the company of the nation, and enlarged the entrance of the house and of the court.

**50:6** Like the morning star in the midst of a cloud, and like the full moon, he shines in his days;

**50:7** and like the radiant sun, so he shone forth in the temple of God.

**50:8** Like a radiant rainbow among clouds of glory, and like the bloom of roses in the days of spring, and like lilies that are by a passage of water, and like fragrant frankincense in the days of summer;

**50:9** like fire shining forth, and frankincense burning in the fire;

**50:10** like a vessel of solid gold, adorned with every precious stone;

**50:11** like a sprouting olive tree and a cypress lifting itself on high, when he received the robe of glory and was clothed in the perfection of strength.

**50:12** At the ascent of the holy altar, he gave glory to the vestment of holiness.

**50:13** And when he received the portions from the hand of the priests, while he himself stood beside the altar and around him was a crown of brothers, like a planting of cedar on Mount Lebanon,

**50:14** so they stood around him like branches of a palm tree, and all the sons of Aaron in their glory.

**50:15** And the offering of the Lord was in their hands before the whole assembly of Israel; and performing the completion at the altar, to magnify the offering of the Most High King,

**50:16** he stretched out his hand in the libation and poured out from the blood of the grape.

**50:17** He poured out at the foundation of the altar a divine fragrance to the Prince on high.

**50:18** Then the sons of Aaron shouted aloud; they sounded on hammered trumpets and made a loud voice heard as a remembrance before God.

**50:19** Then all the people hastened together and fell on their faces upon the ground, to worship the Lord their God and to offer prayers to Almighty God Most High.

**50:20** And the singers swelled their voices, and in the great house a sound full of sweetness was increased.

**50:21** And the people petitioned the Lord Most High in prayer, until the honor of the Lord was completed and they completed their service.

**50:22** Then descending, he raised his hands over the whole congregation of the sons of Israel, to give glory to God from his lips and to boast in His name;

**50:23** and he repeated his prayer, wishing to show the power of God.

**50:24** And now pray to the God of all, who accomplished great things throughout the whole earth, who increased our days from the womb of our mother, and dealt with us according to His mercy:

**50:25** may He grant us gladness of heart, and that there be peace in our days in Israel throughout everlasting days;

**50:26** that Israel may trust that the mercy of God is with us, so that He may deliver us in His days.

**50:27** Two nations my soul hates, and the third, which I hate, is not a nation:

**50:28** those who dwell on Mount Seir, and the Philistines, and the foolish people who live in Shechem.

**50:29** Jesus, son of Sirach, of Jerusalem, who renewed wisdom from his heart, wrote in this book the instruction of wisdom and discipline.

**50:30** Blessed is he who occupies himself with these good things; he who sets them in his heart will always be wise.

**50:31** For if he does these things, he will be strong for all things, because the light of God is his path.

## Translator Notes

- **50:3** — Vulgate reads 'the wells of water flowed forth, and like the sea they were filled beyond measure,' where the Greek describes quarrying a bronze reservoir like the circumference of the sea.
- **50:5** — Vulgate renders the Greek phrase describing Simon's emergence from the sanctuary veil ('as he came out of the house of the veil') as 'and enlarged the entrance of the house and of the court.'
- **50:10** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 50:10 (parallel to Saints/Greek 50:9b).
- **50:12** — Vulgate translates Greek peribolen ('enclosure/court') in its alternative sense of 'garment' as amictum ('vestment'), hence 'he gave glory to the vestment of holiness.'
- **50:17** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 50:17 (parallel to Saints/Greek 50:15b).
- **50:23** — Vulgate keeps Simon as singular subject ('he repeated his prayer, wishing to show the power of God'), whereas the Greek has the people bowing down a second time to receive the blessing.
- **50:26** — Vulgate renders the verse with an infinitive clause ('that Israel may trust...') and reflexive in diebus suis, which grammatically refers to God ('His days'), though the Greek autou may refer to Simon's lifetime.
