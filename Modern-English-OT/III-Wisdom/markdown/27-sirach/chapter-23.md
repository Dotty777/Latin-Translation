## Text

**23:1** Lord, Father and Ruler of my life, do not abandon me in their counsel, and do not allow me to fall in them.

**23:2** Who will place whips upon my thought, and the instruction of wisdom upon my heart, so that they may not spare me for their acts of ignorance, and their offenses may not appear,

**23:3** and so that my acts of ignorance may not grow greater, nor my offenses be multiplied, nor my sins abound, and so that I may not fall in the sight of my adversaries, and my enemy may not rejoice over me?

**23:4** Lord, Father and God of my life, do not abandon me in their thought.

**23:5** Do not give me haughtiness of my eyes, and turn every desire away from me.

**23:6** Take away from me the cravings of the belly, and do not let the cravings of sexual union seize me, and do not hand me over to an irreverent and senseless soul.

**23:7** Listen to the instruction of the mouth, children, and the one who keeps it will not perish by his lips, nor stumble in the most wicked deeds.

**23:8** In his vanity the sinner is caught, and the proud and the reviler will stumble in them.

**23:9** Do not let your mouth become accustomed to swearing, for there are many falls in it.

**23:10** And let the naming of God not be continual in your mouth, and do not mingle with the names of the holy ones, because one will not be free from guilt regarding them.

**23:11** For just as a slave continually interrogated is not lacking in bruising, so everyone who swears and invokes in everything will not be cleansed from sin.

**23:12** A man who swears much will be filled with iniquity, and a scourge will not depart from his house.

**23:13** And if he fails to fulfill it, his offense will be upon himself; and if he pretends not to notice, he offends doubly;

**23:14** and if he has sworn in vain, he will not be justified, for his house will be filled with retribution.

**23:15** There is also another speech contrary to death; let it not be found in the inheritance of Jacob.

**23:16** For indeed, all these things will be taken away from the merciful, and they will not wallow in offenses.

**23:17** Do not let your mouth become accustomed to undisciplined speech, for there is a word of sin in it.

**23:18** Remember your father and your mother, for you stand in the midst of great men.

**23:19** Or else God might forget you in their sight, and, made foolish by your habit, you might suffer reproach, and you would have preferred not to be born, and you would curse the day of your birth.

**23:20** A man accustomed to words of reproach will not be instructed in all his days.

**23:21** Two kinds abound in sins, and a third brings wrath and destruction.

**23:22** A hot soul, like a burning fire, will not be extinguished until it swallows something.

**23:23** And a wicked man in the mouth of his flesh will not cease until he kindles a fire.

**23:24** To a fornicating man all bread is sweet; he will not grow tired of transgressing until the end.

**23:25** Every man who transgresses his own bed, despising his own soul and saying, "Who sees me?

**23:26** Darkness surrounds me, and walls cover me, and no one looks around at me; whom do I fear? The Most High will not be mindful of my offenses."

**23:27** And he does not understand that His eye sees all things, because the fear of such a man and the eyes of men fearing him drive the fear of God away from him;

**23:28** and he has not known that the eyes of the Lord are much more radiant than the sun, surveying all the ways of men, and the depth of the abyss, and the hearts of men, looking into the hidden places.

**23:29** For all things were known to the Lord God before they were created; so also after completion He looks upon all things.

**23:30** This man will be punished in the streets of the city, and like a horse's colt he will be put to flight, and where he did not expect it he will be caught.

**23:31** And he will be a disgrace to everyone, because he has not understood the fear of the Lord.

**23:32** So also every woman who leaves her husband and establishes an inheritance from another marriage:

**23:33** for first, she was unfaithful in the law of the Most High; second, she offended against her husband; third, she committed fornication in adultery, and established children for herself by another man.

**23:34** This woman will be brought into the assembly, and her children will be scrutinized:

**23:35** her children will not put down roots, and her branches will not bear fruit;

**23:36** she will leave her memory for a curse, and her disgrace will not be blotted out.

**23:37** And those who are left behind will recognize that nothing is better than the fear of God, and nothing is sweeter than to look to the commandments of the Lord.

**23:38** To follow the Lord is a great glory, for length of days will be received from Him.

## Translator Notes

- **23:2** — The Vulgate uniquely reads ignorationibus eorum ("their acts of ignorance") alongside mihi ("me") and non appareant ("may not appear"), whereas the Greek reads "my errors" and "may not pass over their sins."
- **23:10** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 23:10 (parallel to Saints/Greek 23:9b); Latin third-person 'erit' shifts from second-person 'tuo/admiscearis' as in Clem1598-USFX.
- **23:13** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 23:13 (continuing 23:12 = Saints 23:11).
- **23:14** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 23:14 (continuing 23:13 = Saints 23:11c).
- **23:15** — Latin contraria morti ("contrary to death" or "opposite to death") is a literal Vulgate rendering of Greek antiparakeimene thanato ("standing over against / comparable to death"), referring to speech such as blasphemy that merits death.
- **23:16** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 23:16 (continuing 23:15 = Saints 23:12b).
- **23:19** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 23:19 (continuing 23:18 = Saints 23:14b).
- **23:23** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 23:23 (continuing 23:22 = Saints 23:16b); Latin 'in ore carnis suæ' literally reads "in the mouth of his flesh" (Vulgate reading where Greek reads 'in the body of his flesh').
- **23:24** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 23:24 (parallel to Saints/Greek 23:17).
- **23:25** — Direct speech continues directly into restored native Vulgate 23:26, where the closing quotation mark now stands.
- **23:26** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 23:26 (completing the direct speech opened in 23:25 = Saints 23:18).
- **23:27** — The Vulgate uniquely presents hujusmodi hominis timor ("the fear of such a man") and oculi hominum timentes illum ("the eyes of men fearing him") as driving away the fear of God, whereas the Greek states simply that the eyes of men are his fear.
- **23:30** — The clause et quasi pullus equinus fugabitur ("and like a horse's colt he will be put to flight") is a Vulgate-distinct expansion absent from the standard Greek text.
