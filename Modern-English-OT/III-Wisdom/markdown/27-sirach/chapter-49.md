## Text

**49:1** The memory of Josiah is made into a blend of fragrance, the work of a perfumer.

**49:2** In every mouth his memory will be sweetened like honey, and like music at a banquet of wine.

**49:3** He himself was divinely directed to the repentance of the nation, and he took away the abominations of impiety.

**49:4** And he guided his heart to the Lord, and in the days of sinners he strengthened piety.

**49:5** Except for David and Hezekiah and Josiah, all committed sin.

**49:6** For the kings of Judah abandoned the law of the Most High and despised the fear of God.

**49:7** For they gave their own kingdom to others, and their own glory to a foreign nation.

**49:8** They burned the chosen city of holiness and made its streets desolate, by the hand of Jeremiah.

**49:9** For they mistreated him who from his mother's womb was consecrated a prophet, to overthrow, and to uproot, and to destroy, and again to build and to renew.

**49:10** Ezekiel, who saw the vision of glory that He showed him on the chariot of the cherubim.

**49:11** For he made mention of the enemies in the rain, to do good to those who showed the right ways.

**49:12** And may the bones of the twelve prophets sprout from their place; for they strengthened Jacob, and they redeemed themselves in the faith of strength.

**49:13** How shall we magnify Zerubbabel? For he also is like a signet on the right hand,

**49:14** so also Jeshua, son of Jozadak, who in their days built the house and raised up a holy temple to the Lord, prepared for everlasting glory.

**49:15** And Nehemiah is in memory for a long time, who raised up for us the overthrown walls, and made the gates and bars stand, who raised up our houses.

**49:16** No one has been born on earth such as Enoch, for he himself was also received up from the earth;

**49:17** nor like Joseph, who was born a man, prince of his brothers, support of the nation, ruler of his brothers, mainstay of the people;

**49:18** and his bones were visited, and after death they prophesied.

**49:19** Seth and Shem attained glory among men, and above every soul in origin is Adam.

## Translator Notes

- **49:1** — Native Vulgate ends 49:1 after 'the work of a perfumer' and places the lines comparing Josiah's memory to honey and banquet music in restored native Vulgate 49:2 (together corresponding to Saints/Greek 49:1).
- **49:2** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 49:2 (parallel to Saints/Greek 49:1b; previously claimed omitted in the note on 49:1).
- **49:6** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 49:6 (continuing 49:5 = Saints 49:4b).
- **49:11** — Following the Greek (which misread Hebrew 'Job' as 'enemies'), Vulgate reads 'the enemies in the rain' and renders the closing clause 'who showed the right ways' (Greek: 'who kept straight ways').
- **49:12** — Vulgate reads reflexive 'redeemed themselves in the faith of strength' (redemerunt se in fide virtutis), whereas the Greek has 'delivered them by confident hope.'
- **49:17** — Vulgate doubles the Greek epithets for Joseph ('prince of his brothers, support of the nation, ruler of his brothers, mainstay of the people').
- **49:18** — Vulgate adds 'and after death they prophesied,' absent from the standard Greek.
- **49:19** — Vulgate reverses the Greek order ('Shem and Seth') to 'Seth and Shem' and renders Greek 'in creation' as in origine ('in origin').
