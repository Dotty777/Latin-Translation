## Text

**46:1** Mighty in war was Joshua son of Nave, successor of Moses among the prophets, who was great according to his name,

**46:2** greatest for the salvation of the chosen of God, to conquer the enemies rising up, so that he might obtain the inheritance of Israel.

**46:3** What glory he attained in raising his hands and brandishing swords against the cities!

**46:4** Who before him stood so firm? For the Lord Himself brought forth the enemies.

**46:5** Was not the sun held back in his wrath, and one day made like two?

**46:6** He called upon the Most High, the Mighty One, in fighting against enemies on every side; and the great and holy God heard him with hailstones of exceedingly strong power.

**46:7** He made an assault against the hostile nation, and on the descent He destroyed the adversaries,

**46:8** so that the nations might know His power, because it is not easy to fight against God. And he followed behind the Mighty One.

**46:9** And in the days of Moses he showed mercy, he and Caleb the son of Jephunneh, to stand against the enemy, and to restrain the nation from sins, and to break the grumbling of malice.

**46:10** And those two who were appointed were delivered from danger out of the number of six hundred thousand foot soldiers, to bring them into the inheritance, into a land that flows with milk and honey.

**46:11** And the Lord gave might to Caleb himself, and even into old age strength remained with him, so that he ascended to the high place of the land, and his seed obtained the inheritance,

**46:12** so that all the sons of Israel might see that it is good to obey the holy God.

**46:13** And the judges, each by his own name, whose heart was not corrupted, who did not turn away from the Lord,

**46:14** may their memory be in blessing, and may their bones sprout from their place.

**46:15** And may their name remain forever, remaining to their children, the glory of holy men.

**46:16** Beloved by the Lord his God, Samuel, the prophet of the Lord, renewed the kingdom, and anointed rulers in his nation.

**46:17** In the law of the Lord he judged the congregation, and God saw Jacob; and in his faithfulness he was proven a prophet,

**46:18** and in his words he was known to be faithful, because he saw the God of light.

**46:19** And he called upon the Lord Almighty in fighting against enemies standing around on every side, in the offering of an unblemished lamb.

**46:20** And the Lord thundered from heaven, and with a great sound He made His voice heard,

**46:21** and He crushed the rulers of the Tyrians and all the leaders of the Philistines.

**46:22** And before the time of the end of his life and of the age, he bore testimony in the sight of the Lord and of the anointed one: he did not accept money, even down to sandals, from any flesh, and no man accused him.

**46:23** And after this he fell asleep; and he made known to the king and showed him the end of his life; and he lifted up his voice from the earth in prophecy, to blot out the ungodliness of the nation.

## Translator Notes

- **46:1** — Latin 'Jesus Nave' preserves the Septuagint form (Iesous Naue) for Joshua son of Nun, keeping the wordplay on Joshua's name ('the Lord saves') in 'great according to his name.' Latin 'in prophetis' ('among the prophets') may also reflect contracted 'prophetiīs' ('in prophecies'), matching the Greek.
- **46:2** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 46:2 (continuing 46:1 = Saints 46:1b).
- **46:4** — Vulgate 'hostes ipse Dominus perduxit' ('the Lord Himself brought forth the enemies') is a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Greek has 'for he waged the wars of the Lord.'
- **46:5** — Vulgate 'in iracundia ejus' ('in his wrath') differs from the Greek, which reads 'by his hand.'
- **46:8** — Vulgate 'quia contra Deum pugnare non est facile' ('because it is not easy to fight against God') differs from the Greek ('that his battle was in the sight of the Lord'). In the final clause, 'potentis' ('the Mighty One') refers to God.
- **46:9** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 46:9 (parallel to Saints/Greek 46:7).
- **46:15** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 46:15 (continuing 46:14 = Saints 46:12b).
- **46:18** — Vulgate 'quia vidit Deum lucis' ('because he saw the God of light') is a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Greek describes Samuel as 'trustworthy in vision.'
