## Text

**2:1** Son, approaching the service of God, stand in righteousness and fear, and prepare your soul for temptation.

**2:2** Humble your heart, and endure; incline your ear, and receive words of understanding; and do not hasten in the time of gloom.

**2:3** Endure the delays of God; be joined to God, and endure, so that your life may increase at the last.

**2:4** Accept everything that is laid upon you, and in sorrow endure, and in your lowliness have patience,

**2:5** for in fire gold and silver are tested, and acceptable men in the furnace of humiliation.

**2:6** Believe God, and He will restore you; and direct your way, and hope in Him; keep His fear, and grow old in it.

**2:7** You who fear the Lord, wait for His mercy, and do not turn aside from Him, so that you do not fall.

**2:8** You who fear the Lord, believe Him, and your reward will not be made void.

**2:9** You who fear the Lord, hope in Him, and mercy will come to you for delight.

**2:10** Look, sons, at the nations of men, and know that no one has hoped in the Lord and been put to shame.

**2:11** For God is compassionate and merciful, and will forgive sins in the day of tribulation, and is a protector to all who seek Him in truth.

**2:12** Woe to the double-hearted, and to wicked lips, and to hands that do evil, and to the sinner who walks the earth by two ways!

**2:13** Woe to those faint in heart, who do not believe God, and therefore will not be protected by Him!

**2:14** Woe to those who have lost endurance, and who have abandoned straight ways, and turned aside into crooked ways!

**2:15** Those who fear the Lord will not be distrustful of His word, and those who love Him will keep His way.

**2:16** Those who fear the Lord will seek out the things that are well-pleasing to Him, and those who love Him will be filled with His law.

**2:17** Those who fear the Lord will prepare their hearts, and in His sight will sanctify their souls.

**2:18** saying, "If we do not repent, we will fall into the hands of the Lord, and not into the hands of men. For according to His greatness, so also is His mercy with Him."

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — The clause "stand in righteousness and fear" ("sta in justitia et timore") is a Vulgate addition absent from the Greek; Latin "tentationem" ("temptation") carries the broad sense of both trial/testing and temptation.
- **2:2** — The clause "incline your ear, and receive words of understanding" is a Vulgate addition; "obductionis" ("gloom," literally "covering over" or "clouding over") is an Old Latin calque of Greek "epagōgē" ("calamity/affliction").
- **2:3** — The opening command "Endure the delays of God" ("Sustine sustentationes Dei," literally "endure the deferrals/sustainings of God") is a Vulgate addition not present in the Greek.
- **2:10** — Vulgate "nationes hominum" ("the nations of men") reflects an Old Latin rendering of Greek "archaias geneas" ("ancient generations") and adds "sons" ("filii"), while omitting two rhetorical questions that follow in the Greek.
- **2:18** — The Vulgate adds "saying, 'If we do not repent'" ("dicentes: Si pœnitentiam non egerimus," literally "do not do penance"), absent from the Greek, framing the verse as a conditional statement, and adds "with Him" ("cum ipso est") at the end.
