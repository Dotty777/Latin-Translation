## Text

**6:1** Do not become an enemy to your neighbor instead of a friend; for an evil person will inherit reproach and dishonor, as will every envious and double-tongued sinner.

**6:2** Do not exalt yourself in the thought of your soul like a bull, or else your strength may be crushed through folly,

**6:3** and it may devour your leaves and destroy your fruits, and you may be left like a dry tree in the wilderness.

**6:4** For a wicked soul will destroy the one who possesses it, and gives him as a joy to enemies, and will lead him down into the lot of the ungodly.

**6:5** A sweet word multiplies friends and appeases enemies, and a gracious tongue abounds in a good person.

**6:6** Let many be at peace with you, and let your counselor be one out of a thousand.

**6:7** If you acquire a friend, acquire him in testing, and do not easily trust him.

**6:8** For there is a friend according to his own time, and he will not remain in the day of tribulation.

**6:9** And there is a friend who turns to enmity, and there is a friend who will expose hatred and strife and insults.

**6:10** And there is a friend who is a companion of the table, and he will not remain in the day of need.

**6:11** If a friend remains steadfast, he will be to you like an equal, and among those of your household he will act confidently.

**6:12** If he humbles himself before you and hides himself from your face, you will have a good friendship of one mind.

**6:13** Separate yourself from your enemies, and beware of your friends.

**6:14** A faithful friend is a strong protection; and the one who finds him has found a treasure.

**6:15** There is no comparison to a faithful friend, and no weight of gold and silver is worthy compared with the goodness of his faithfulness.

**6:16** A faithful friend is a medicine of life and immortality; and those who fear the Lord will find him.

**6:17** The one who fears God will likewise have a good friendship, because his friend will be like him.

**6:18** Son, from your youth receive instruction, and even to gray hairs you will find wisdom.

**6:19** Like one who plows and sows, draw near to her, and wait for her good fruits.

**6:20** For in her work you will labor a little, and swiftly you will eat from her produce.

**6:21** How exceedingly harsh wisdom is to uninstructed people! And a senseless person will not remain in her.

**6:22** Like the strength of a stone, the testing will be upon them, and they will not delay to cast her off.

**6:23** For the wisdom of instruction is according to her name, and she is not made manifest to many; but to those by whom she is known, she remains even to the presence of God.

**6:24** Listen, son, and accept the counsel of understanding, and do not reject my counsel.

**6:25** Put your foot into her fetters, and your neck into her collar.

**6:26** Bow your shoulder and carry her, and do not grow weary of her bonds.

**6:27** With all your mind draw near to her, and with all your strength keep her ways.

**6:28** Search her out, and she will be made manifest to you; and once you have taken hold of her, do not abandon her;

**6:29** for in the end you will find rest in her, and she will be turned into delight for you.

**6:30** And her fetters will become for you a protection of might and foundations of strength, and her collar a robe of glory;

**6:31** for the beauty of life is in her, and her bonds are a saving binding.

**6:32** You will put her on as a robe of glory, and you will place a crown of rejoicing upon yourself.

**6:33** Son, if you pay attention to me, you will learn; and if you apply your mind, you will be wise.

**6:34** If you incline your ear, you will receive instruction; and if you love to listen, you will be wise.

**6:35** Stand in the multitude of prudent elders, and join yourself from the heart to their wisdom, so that you may be able to hear every account of God, and proverbs of praise may not escape from you.

**6:36** And if you see a discerning person, rise early to go to him, and let your foot wear away the steps of his doors.

**6:37** Keep your thought on the precepts of God, and in His commandments be especially diligent; and He Himself will give you a heart, and the desire for wisdom will be given to you.

## Translator Notes

- **6:12** — This verse is a notable Vulgate-distinct reading: the Latin turns it into a positive promise about a humble, deferential friend ("If he humbles himself before you [contra te] ... you will have a good friendship of one mind"), whereas the Greek warns of a false friend turning against one who is brought low.
- **6:16** — Vulgate "and immortality" (et immortalitatis) is a distinct Latin expansion where the Greek reads only "a medicine of life."
- **6:20** — Restored native Clementine Vulgate verse 6:20 (parallel to Saints/Greek 6:19b); Latin 'de generationibus illius' (lit. "from her yields/generations") follows the established rendering "produce" (cf. 1:21 / Saints 1:17).
- **6:22** — Latin "Like the strength of a stone, the testing will be upon them" (Quasi lapidis virtus probatio erit in illis) literally renders the Greek image of a heavy lifting-stone used as a test of strength.
- **6:23** — Latin adds "of instruction" (doctrinae) to "wisdom" and concludes with the Vulgate-distinct promise "she remains even to the presence of God" (permanet usque ad conspectum Dei).
- **6:31** — Vulgate "the beauty of life" (decor vitae) and "a saving binding" (alligatura salutaris, conveying both healing and salvation) are distinct Latin readings where the Greek describes "a golden ornament" and "a blue cord."
