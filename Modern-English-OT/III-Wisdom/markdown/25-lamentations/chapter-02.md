## Text

**2:1** Aleph: How the Lord has covered the daughter of Zion with darkness in His fury! He has cast down from heaven to earth the glorious one of Israel, and has not remembered the footstool of His feet on the day of His fury!

**2:2** Beth: The Lord has overthrown, and has not spared, all the lovely things of Jacob; in His fury He has destroyed the strongholds of the virgin of Judah and thrown them down to the ground; He has defiled the kingdom and its princes.

**2:3** Gimel: In the wrath of His fury He has shattered every horn of Israel; He has turned His right hand backward from the face of the enemy, and He has kindled in Jacob, as it were, a fire of a devouring flame all around.

**2:4** Daleth: He has bent His bow like an enemy, He has steadied His right hand like a foe, and He has killed everything that was beautiful to look at in the tabernacle of the daughter of Zion; He has poured out His indignation like fire.

**2:5** He: The Lord has become like an enemy; He has overthrown Israel. He has overthrown all its ramparts, He has laid waste its strongholds, and He has filled the daughter of Judah with the humiliated man and the humiliated woman.

**2:6** Vav: And He has laid waste His tent like a garden; He has demolished His tabernacle. The Lord has handed over to oblivion in Zion festival and sabbath, and to reproach and to the indignation of His fury, king and priest.

**2:7** Zayin: The Lord has rejected His altar; He has cursed His holy place; He has handed over into the hand of the enemy the walls of her towers. They have raised their voice in the house of the Lord as on a festival day.

**2:8** Heth: The Lord has planned to lay waste the wall of the daughter of Zion; He has stretched out His line and has not turned His hand away from destruction; and the outer wall has mourned, and the wall has been laid waste together with it.

**2:9** Teth: Her gates have sunk into the ground; He has ruined and crushed her bars; her king and her princes are among the nations. There is no law, and her prophets have not found a vision from the Lord.

**2:10** Yod: The elders of the daughter of Zion have sat on the ground, they have kept silent; they have sprinkled their heads with ashes, they have bound themselves with sackcloth; the virgins of Jerusalem have cast their heads down to the ground.

**2:11** Kaph: My eyes have failed from tears, my inward parts are in turmoil; my liver has been poured out on the ground over the shattering of the daughter of my people, while the little one and the nursing infant fainted in the streets of the town.

**2:12** Lamed: They said to their mothers, "Where is wheat and wine?" as they fainted like the wounded in the streets of the city, as they breathed out their souls in the bosom of their mothers.

**2:13** Mem: To what shall I compare you, or to what shall I liken you, daughter of Jerusalem? To what shall I equate you and comfort you, virgin, daughter of Zion? For your shattering is as great as the sea; who will heal you?

**2:14** Nun: Your prophets have seen false and foolish things for you, and they did not expose your iniquity so as to provoke you to repentance; instead, they have seen for you false oracles and expulsions.

**2:15** Samekh: All who pass along the road have clapped their hands over you; they have hissed and shaken their head over the daughter of Jerusalem, saying, "Is this the city of perfect beauty, the joy of the whole earth?"

**2:16** Pe: All your enemies have opened their mouth against you; they have hissed and gnashed their teeth, and said, "We will devour her! Look, this is the day that we were waiting for; we have found it, we have seen it."

**2:17** Ayin: The Lord has done what He planned; He has fulfilled His word, which He had commanded from ancient days; He has destroyed and has not spared, and He has made the enemy rejoice over you, and He has exalted the horn of your foes.

**2:18** Tsade: Their heart cried out to the Lord upon the walls of the daughter of Zion. Pour down tears like a torrent through day and night; do not give yourself rest, and let the pupil of your eye not keep silent.

**2:19** Qoph: Rise up, give praise in the night, at the beginning of the watches; pour out your heart like water before the presence of the Lord; lift up your hands to Him for the soul of your little ones, who have fainted in hunger at the head of all the crossroads.

**2:20** Resh: See, Lord, and consider whom You have harvested in this way. Will women really eat their fruit, little ones the measure of a palm? Should priest and prophet be killed in the sanctuary of the Lord?

**2:21** Shin: The boy and the old man have lain on the ground outside; my virgins and my young men have fallen by the sword. You have killed on the day of Your fury; You have struck down and have not shown mercy.

**2:22** Tav: You have called, as to a festival day, those who would terrify me from all around; and on the day of the fury of the Lord there was no one who escaped or was left behind. Those whom I brought up and nourished, my enemy has consumed them.

## Translator Notes

- **2:5** — Vulgate "humiliatum et humiliatam" ("the humiliated man and the humiliated woman") mirrors a Hebrew rhyming pair ("mourning and lamentation") by rendering it from the related Hebrew root for humiliation.
- **2:14** — Vulgate "ut te ad poenitentiam provocarent" ("so as to provoke you to repentance") interprets the Hebrew phrase for restoring fortunes in a spiritual sense; "assumptiones ... et ejectiones" ("oracles and expulsions") reflects Jerome's understanding of the Hebrew terms for prophetic burdens and banishments.
- **2:16** — In chapter 2, the Vulgate preserves the Hebrew acrostic's reversal of Pe (2:16) and Ayin (2:17) compared with chapter 1; Vulgate future "Devorabimus" ("We will devour") contrasts with the Hebrew perfect ("We have swallowed her up").
- **2:18** — Vulgate "super muros filiae Sion" ("upon the walls of the daughter of Zion") takes the phrase adverbially rather than as the Masoretic Text's vocative ("O wall of the daughter of Zion"); in 2:19 Vulgate "lauda" ("give praise") renders a Hebrew imperative meaning either "cry out" or "praise".
- **2:20** — Vulgate "parvulos ad mensuram palmae" ("little ones the measure of a palm") describes tiny infants only a handbreadth long, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to children tenderly cared for.
