## Text

**1:1** And it happened, after Israel was brought into captivity and Jerusalem was deserted, that Jeremiah the prophet sat weeping, and lamented with this lamentation over Jerusalem; and sighing and wailing with a bitter spirit, he said, Aleph: "How alone the city sits, full of people! The mistress of the nations has become like a widow; the princess of the provinces has come under tribute.

**1:2** Beth: Weeping, she wept in the night, and her tears are on her cheeks; from among all her dear ones there is no one to comfort her; all her friends have spurned her and have become enemies to her.

**1:3** Gimel: Judah has departed because of affliction and the abundance of servitude; she has lived among the nations and has not found rest; all her pursuers have seized her in the midst of straits.

**1:4** Daleth: The roads of Zion mourn because there are none who come to the festival; all her gates are destroyed, her priests are groaning, her virgins are unkempt, and she herself is oppressed with bitterness.

**1:5** He: Her foes have been placed at the head; her enemies have grown wealthy, for the Lord has spoken against her because of the multitude of her iniquities. Her little ones have been led into captivity before the face of the oppressor.

**1:6** Vav: And all her beauty has gone out from the daughter of Zion; her princes have become like rams finding no pasture, and they have gone away without strength before the face of the pursuer.

**1:7** Zayin: Jerusalem has remembered the days of her affliction and transgression, all her treasured things that she had possessed from ancient days, when her people fell into a hostile hand and there was no helper; the foes saw her and mocked her sabbaths.

**1:8** Heth: Jerusalem has sinned a sin; therefore she has become unstable. All who glorified her have spurned her, because they have seen her disgrace; she herself, however, groaning, has turned backward.

**1:9** Teth: Her filth is on her feet, and she has not remembered her end; she has been brought down severely, having no comforter. See, Lord, my affliction, for the enemy has lifted himself up.

**1:10** Yod: The foe has stretched out his hand to all her treasured things, for she has seen the nations enter her sanctuary, concerning whom You had commanded that they should not enter Your assembly.

**1:11** Kaph: All her people are groaning and seeking bread; they have given every precious thing for food to revive the soul. See, Lord, and consider that I have become worthless!

**1:12** Lamed: All you who pass along the road, pay attention and see if there is sorrow like my sorrow! For He has harvested me, as the Lord has spoken, on the day of the wrath of His fury.

**1:13** Mem: From on high He has sent fire into my bones and has instructed me; He has spread a net for my feet, He has turned me backward; He has made me desolate, worn out with grief all day long.

**1:14** Nun: The yoke of my iniquities has kept watch; in His hand they have been twisted together and placed upon my neck. My strength has been weakened; the Lord has given me into a hand from which I will not be able to rise.

**1:15** Samekh: The Lord has taken away all my noble ones from my midst; He has summoned a time against me to crush my chosen ones. The Lord has trodden the winepress for the virgin daughter of Judah.

**1:16** Ayin: For this reason I am weeping, and my eye is pouring down waters, because a comforter who restores my soul has been removed far from me. My children have been ruined, because the enemy has prevailed.

**1:17** Pe: Zion has spread out her hands; there is no one to comfort her. The Lord has commanded against Jacob his foes around him; Jerusalem has become like one defiled by menstruation among them.

**1:18** Tsade: The Lord is righteous, for I have provoked His mouth to anger. Hear, I implore you, all peoples, and see my sorrow: my virgins and my young men have gone away into captivity.

**1:19** Qoph: I called my friends, and they themselves deceived me; my priests and my elders have been consumed in the city, because they sought food for themselves to revive their soul.

**1:20** Resh: See, Lord, that I am in distress: my belly is in turmoil, my heart is overturned within myself, for I am full of bitterness. Outside the sword has killed, and at home death is like it.

**1:21** Shin: They have heard that I groan and that there is no one to comfort me; all my enemies have heard of my calamity and have rejoiced because You have done it. You have brought the day of consolation, and they will become like me.

**1:22** Tav: Let all their wickedness come before You, and harvest them just as You have harvested me because of all my iniquities; for my groans are many, and my heart is grieving."

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Following the Septuagint, the Vulgate includes the opening historical preface attributing the lament to Jeremiah; this preface is absent from the Hebrew Masoretic Text. The Hebrew alphabet letter names (Aleph through Tav) at the head of each verse reflect the acrostic structure preserved in the Latin text.
- **1:5** — Vulgate "locutus est super eam" ("has spoken against her"; so also "locutus est" in 1:12) reflects a reading of the Hebrew verb as speaking rather than the Masoretic Text's "has afflicted her".
- **1:7** — Vulgate "praevaricationis" ("transgression") and "sabbata ejus" ("her sabbaths") are distinctive Latin renderings where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "wandering" and "her downfall"; similarly in 1:6 Vulgate "arietes" ("rams") contrasts with Hebrew "stags", and in 1:9 "in pedibus ejus" ("on her feet") contrasts with Hebrew "in her skirts".
- **1:12** — Vulgate "vindemiavit" ("has harvested"; also in 1:22 and 2:20) literally denotes harvesting grapes at the vintage, rendering the Hebrew verb that can mean either "to glean a vineyard" or "to deal severely with".
- **1:21** — Vulgate "adduxisti diem consolationis" ("You have brought the day of consolation") is a notable Vulgate-distinct reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Bring the day You have announced"; see also "Vigilavit" ("has kept watch") in 1:14 and "amaritudine plena sum" ("I am full of bitterness") in 1:20.
