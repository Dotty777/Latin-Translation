## Text

**4:1** "And you, son of man, take for yourself a brick, and you will place it before you and draw on it the city of Jerusalem.

**4:2** And you will set a siege against it, and build fortifications, and heap up a mound, and set camps against it, and place battering rams all around.

**4:3** And you, take for yourself an iron pan, and you will place it as an iron wall between you and the city; and you will set your face firmly toward it, and it will be under siege, and you will surround it: it is a sign to the house of Israel.

**4:4** And you will sleep on your left side, and you will place the iniquities of the house of Israel upon it, according to the number of days during which you will sleep on it; and you will take up their iniquity.

**4:5** Now I have given you the years of their iniquity, by the number of days, three hundred and ninety days; and you will bear the iniquity of the house of Israel.

**4:6** And when you have completed these things, you will sleep on your right side a second time, and you will take up the iniquity of the house of Judah for forty days: a day for a year, a day, I say, for a year, I have given you.

**4:7** And you will turn your face toward the siege of Jerusalem, and your arm will be outstretched, and you will prophesy against it.

**4:8** Look, I have surrounded you with bonds, and you will not turn yourself from your side to another side until you complete the days of your siege.

**4:9** And you, take for yourself wheat, and barley, and beans, and lentils, and millet, and vetch, and you will put them into one vessel and make for yourself loaves according to the number of days during which you will sleep on your side: for three hundred and ninety days you will eat it.

**4:10** Now your food that you will eat will be by weight twenty staters a day; from time to time you will eat it.

**4:11** And you will drink water by measure, the sixth part of a hin; from time to time you will drink it.

**4:12** And you will eat it like a barley cake baked under the ashes, and with the excrement that comes out from a man you will cover it before their eyes."

**4:13** And the Lord said, "In this way the sons of Israel will eat their defiled bread among the nations to which I will cast them out." And I said,

**4:14** "Ah, ah, ah, Lord God, look, my soul has not been defiled, and I have not eaten what died of itself or what was torn by beasts from my infancy until now, and no unclean flesh has entered into my mouth."

**4:15** And He said to me, "Look, I have given you cattle dung instead of human excrement, and you will make your bread on it."

**4:16** And He said to me, "Son of man, look, I will break the staff of bread in Jerusalem, and they will eat bread by weight and in anxiety, and they will drink water by measure and in distress,

**4:17** so that, as bread and water fail, each one may collapse toward his brother, and they may waste away in their iniquities."

## Translator Notes

- **4:4** — Vulgate uses dormies ("sleep") here and in 4:6 and 4:9, whereas the Hebrew reads "lie down".
- **4:7** — Vulgate reads extentum ("outstretched"), whereas the Hebrew reads "bared" or "uncovered".
- **4:9** — Vulgate names viciam ("vetch") as the sixth ingredient where the Hebrew has "spelt" or "emmer"; similarly in 4:10, Vulgate uses the Greco-Roman weight stateres ("staters") for Hebrew "shekels".
- **4:12** — Vulgate reads operies illud ("you will cover it") rather than the Hebrew "you will bake it", picturing the ash-cake covered with dung fuel to bake.
- **4:17** — Vulgate reads corruat ("collapse/fall down"), whereas the Hebrew reads "be appalled" or "be dismayed".
