## Text

**11:1** And the Spirit lifted me up and brought me into the east gate of the house of the Lord, which faces toward the rising of the sun; and look, at the entrance of the gate were twenty-five men, and I saw in the midst of them Jaazaniah son of Azzur, and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, leaders of the people.

**11:2** And He said to me, "Son of man, these are the men who devise iniquity and plot the worst counsel in this city,

**11:3** saying, 'Were not the houses built recently? This is the cauldron, and we are the meat.'

**11:4** Therefore prophesy about them; prophesy, son of man."

**11:5** And the Spirit of the Lord rushed upon me and said to me, "Speak: This is what the Lord says, 'So you have spoken, house of Israel, and I know the thoughts of your heart.

**11:6** You have killed very many in this city, and you have filled its streets with those who were killed.'

**11:7** Therefore this is what the Lord God says, 'Those killed by you, whom you have placed in the midst of it, these are the meat, and this is the cauldron; and I will bring you out from the midst of it.

**11:8** You have feared the sword, and I will bring the sword upon you,' says the Lord God.

**11:9** 'And I will cast you out from the midst of it, and I will give you into the hand of enemies, and I will execute judgments upon you.

**11:10** By the sword you will fall; at the borders of Israel I will judge you, and you will know that I am the Lord.

**11:11** This will not be a cauldron for you, and you will not be meat in the midst of it; at the borders of Israel I will judge you,

**11:12** and you will know that I am the Lord, because you have not walked in My precepts and have not carried out My judgments, but have acted according to the judgments of the nations that are all around you.'"

**11:13** And it happened, while I was prophesying, that Pelatiah son of Benaiah died; and I fell upon my face, crying out with a loud voice, and said, "Alas, alas, alas, Lord God! Are You making a complete end of the remnant of Israel?"

**11:14** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**11:15** "Son of man, your brothers, your brothers, the men who are your kinsmen, and all the house of Israel, all of them, are those to whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem have said, 'Depart far from the Lord; to us the land has been given as a possession.'

**11:16** Therefore this is what the Lord God says, 'Because I have removed them far off among the nations, and because I have scattered them among the lands, I will be to them a little sanctification in the lands to which they have come.'

**11:17** Therefore speak: This is what the Lord God says, 'I will gather you from the peoples, and unite you from the lands in which you have been scattered, and I will give you the soil of Israel.

**11:18** And they will enter there, and they will remove all the offenses and all its abominations from it.

**11:19** And I will give them one heart, and I will bestow a new spirit in their inward parts; and I will remove the stony heart from their flesh and give them a heart of flesh,

**11:20** so that they may walk in My precepts, and keep My judgments and carry them out, and be a people to Me, and I may be God to them.

**11:21** As for those whose heart walks after their stumbling blocks and abominations, I will place their way upon their own head,' says the Lord God."

**11:22** And the cherubim lifted their wings, and the wheels were with them, and the glory of the God of Israel was above them.

**11:23** And the glory of the Lord ascended from the midst of the city and stood upon the mountain that is to the east of the city.

**11:24** And the Spirit lifted me up and brought me into Chaldea to the exiles, in a vision, in the Spirit of God; and the vision that I had seen was lifted away from me.

**11:25** And I spoke to the exiles all the words of the Lord that He had shown me.

## Translator Notes

- **11:3** — Latin "Nonne dudum aedificatae sunt domus?" ("Were not the houses built recently?") frames the leaders' boast as a confident question, whereas the Hebrew is commonly rendered "The time is not near to build houses."
- **11:13** — The Vulgate intensifies Ezekiel's cry with a threefold "Heu! heu! heu!" ("Alas, alas, alas"), where the Hebrew has a single exclamation.
- **11:16** — Latin "in sanctificationem modicam" literally means "a little sanctification" (or "a modest sanctification"), rendering the Hebrew phrase often understood as "a little sanctuary" or "a sanctuary for a little while."
- **11:24** — Latin "transmigratio" (literally "migration" or "deportation") is used collectively here and in 11:25 for the community of exiles in Chaldea.
