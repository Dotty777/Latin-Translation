## Text

**12:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**12:2** "Son of man, you live in the midst of a provoking house, who have eyes to see and do not see, and ears to hear and do not hear, because it is a provoking house.

**12:3** You therefore, son of man, make for yourself baggage for exile, and you will go into exile by day before them. And you will go into exile from your place to another place in their sight, if perhaps they will take notice, because it is a provoking house.

**12:4** And you will bring your baggage outside like the baggage of one going into exile by day in their sight; but you yourself will go out in the evening before them, just as one departing goes out.

**12:5** Before their eyes dig through the wall for yourself, and you will go out through it.

**12:6** In their sight you will be carried on shoulders; in darkness you will be brought out. You will veil your face and will not see the land, because I have given you as a portent to the house of Israel."

**12:7** Therefore I did just as the Lord had commanded me: I brought out my baggage like the baggage of one going into exile by day, and in the evening I dug through the wall for myself by hand; and in darkness I went out, carried on shoulders in their sight.

**12:8** And the word of the Lord came to me in the morning, saying:

**12:9** "Son of man, has not the house of Israel, the provoking house, said to you, 'What are you doing?'

**12:10** Say to them, 'This is what the Lord God says: This burden is upon the leader who is in Jerusalem, and upon all the house of Israel that is in their midst.'

**12:11** Say, 'I am your portent; just as I have done, so it will be done to them: they will go into exile and into captivity.'

**12:12** And the leader who is in their midst will be carried on shoulders; in darkness he will go out. They will dig through the wall to bring him out; his face will be covered, so that he may not see the land with his eye.

**12:13** And I will spread My net over him, and he will be caught in My dragnet; and I will bring him to Babylon, to the land of the Chaldeans, yet he will not see it, and there he will die.

**12:14** And all who are around him, his guard and his troops, I will scatter to every wind, and I will unsheathe a sword after them.

**12:15** And they will know that I am the Lord, when I have scattered them among the nations and dispersed them through the lands.

**12:16** And I will leave a few men of them from the sword, and famine, and pestilence, so that they may recount all their wicked deeds among the nations that they will enter, and they will know that I am the Lord."

**12:17** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**12:18** "Son of man, eat your bread in agitation, and also drink your water in haste and sorrow.

**12:19** And you will say to the people of the land, 'This is what the Lord God says to those who live in Jerusalem, in the land of Israel: They will eat their bread in anxiety, and they will drink their water in desolation, so that the land may be made desolate of its multitude, because of the iniquity of all who live in it.

**12:20** And the cities that are now inhabited will be desolate, and the land deserted; and you will know that I am the Lord.'"

**12:21** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**12:22** "Son of man, what is this proverb that you have in the land of Israel, saying, 'The days will be put off for a long time, and every vision will perish'?

**12:23** Therefore say to them, 'This is what the Lord God says: I will make this proverb cease, and it will no longer be commonly spoken in Israel.' And tell them that the days have drawn near, and the word of every vision.

**12:24** For there will no longer be any futile vision nor ambiguous divination in the midst of the sons of Israel;

**12:25** because I, the Lord, will speak, and whatever word I speak will come to pass, and it will not be delayed any more; but in your days, provoking house, I will speak a word and will do it, says the Lord God."

**12:26** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**12:27** "Son of man, look, the house of Israel is saying, 'The vision that this man sees is for many days, and he prophesies for distant times.'

**12:28** Therefore say to them, 'This is what the Lord God says: Not one word of Mine will be delayed any longer; the word that I speak will be fulfilled, says the Lord God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew "rebellious house" with Latin "domus exasperans" ("provoking house"), a phrase repeated in 12:3, 9, and 25.
- **12:6** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which uses active verbs for lifting and carrying out baggage, the Vulgate uses passive verbs ("portaberis," "effereris"; cf. 12:7, 12), portraying Ezekiel and the leader himself as being carried out.
- **12:18** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "with trembling and anxiety," the Vulgate translates "in festinatione et mœrore" ("in haste and sorrow").
- **12:24** — Whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "flattering divination," the Vulgate reads "divinatio ambigua" ("ambiguous divination").
