## Text

**13:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**13:2** "Son of man, prophesy to the prophets of Israel who prophesy, and you will say to those who prophesy from their own heart, 'Hear the word of the Lord.

**13:3** This is what the Lord God says: Woe to the foolish prophets, who follow their own spirit and see nothing!

**13:4** Like foxes in the deserts were your prophets, Israel.

**13:5** You have not gone up in opposition, nor have you set up a wall for the house of Israel, so that you might stand in battle on the day of the Lord.

**13:6** They see empty things and divine falsehood, saying, "The Lord says," when the Lord has not sent them; and they have persisted in confirming the word.

**13:7** Have you not seen a futile vision and spoken a lying divination, and you say, "The Lord says," when I have not spoken?

**13:8** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: Because you have spoken empty things and have seen falsehood, therefore, look, I am against you, says the Lord God.

**13:9** And My hand will be upon the prophets who see empty things and divine falsehood; they will not be in the council of My people, and they will not be written in the register of the house of Israel, nor will they enter into the land of Israel; and you will know that I am the Lord God,

**13:10** because they have deceived My people, saying, "Peace," and there is no peace; and the people itself was building a wall, while they were coating it with clay without straw.'

**13:11** Say to those who coat without tempering that it is going to fall; for there will be a flooding rain, and I will send huge stones rushing down from above, and a shattering storm wind.

**13:12** Indeed, look, the wall has fallen; will it not be said to you, 'Where is the coating that you applied?'

**13:13** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: And I will make a wind of tempests break forth in My indignation, and there will be a flooding rain in My fury, and great stones in wrath for destruction.

**13:14** And I will tear down the wall that you coated without tempering, and I will level it to the ground, and its foundation will be uncovered; and it will fall, and will be consumed in its midst, and you will know that I am the Lord.

**13:15** And I will complete My indignation upon the wall and upon those who coat it without tempering; and I will say to you, 'The wall is no more, and those who coat it are no more:

**13:16** the prophets of Israel, who prophesy to Jerusalem and see for it a vision of peace, and there is no peace,' says the Lord God.

**13:17** And you, son of man, set your face against the daughters of your people who prophesy from their own heart, and prophesy over them,

**13:18** and say, 'This is what the Lord God says: Woe to those who sew cushions under every elbow of the hand and make pillows under the head of every age to capture souls; and when they captured the souls of My people, they kept their souls alive!

**13:19** And they profaned Me among My people for a handful of barley and a piece of bread, to put to death souls that do not die and to keep alive souls that do not live, lying to My people who believe lies.

**13:20** Because of this, this is what the Lord God says: Look, I am against your cushions with which you capture flying souls; and I will tear them from your arms, and I will release the souls that you capture, souls to fly.

**13:21** And I will tear apart your pillows, and I will free My people from your hand, and they will no longer be in your hands to be preyed upon; and you will know that I am the Lord.

**13:22** Because you have falsely made the heart of the righteous grieve, whom I did not sadden, and you have strengthened the hands of the wicked, so that he would not turn back from his evil way and live,

**13:23** therefore you will not see empty things, and you will no longer divine divinations, and I will rescue My people from your hand; and you will know that I am the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **13:5** — Where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "into the breaches," the Vulgate translates "ex adverso" ("in opposition" or "facing opposite").
- **13:6** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("they wait/hope for the confirmation of the word"), the Vulgate reads "perseveraverunt confirmare sermonem" ("they have persisted in confirming the word").
- **13:10** — Latin "ipse" ("he/it itself") refers back to the singular "populum meum" ("My people"). Whereas the Hebrew uses one term for whitewash or untempered mortar throughout vv. 10-15, the Vulgate renders it here as "luto absque paleis" ("with clay without straw") and in vv. 11, 14, and 15 as "without tempering."
- **13:14** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text's second-person plural ("you will be consumed in its midst"), the Vulgate uses the third-person singular passive "consumetur" ("will be consumed").
- **13:18** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew "bands on all wrists" and "veils for heads of every height" as "cushions under every elbow of the hand" and "pillows under the head of every age," and renders the closing clause as a third-person statement rather than a second-person question.
