## Text

**1:1** And it happened in the thirtieth year, in the fourth, on the fifth of the month, while I was in the midst of the captives beside the river Chebar, that the heavens were opened, and I saw visions of God.

**1:2** On the fifth of the month, that is, the fifth year of the exile of King Jehoiachin,

**1:3** the word of the Lord came to Ezekiel the priest, son of Buzi, in the land of the Chaldeans, alongside the river Chebar; and the hand of the Lord came upon him there.

**1:4** And I saw, and look, a whirling wind was coming from the north, and a great cloud, and an enfolding fire, and radiance all around it; and from its midst, something like the look of electrum, that is, from the midst of the fire;

**1:5** and in its midst was the likeness of four living creatures. And this was their appearance: the likeness of a man was in them.

**1:6** Each one had four faces, and each one had four wings.

**1:7** Their feet were straight feet, and the sole of their foot was like the sole of a calf's foot; and there were sparks like the appearance of glowing bronze.

**1:8** And the hands of a man were under their wings on four sides, and they had faces and wings across the four sides.

**1:9** And their wings were joined one to another; they did not turn around when they advanced, but each one proceeded before its own face.

**1:10** Now as for the likeness of their faces: the face of a man and the face of a lion were on the right of the four of them, and the face of an ox on the left of the four of them, and the face of an eagle above the four of them.

**1:11** Their faces and their wings were extended above; two wings of each one were joined together, and two covered their bodies.

**1:12** And each one of them walked before its own face; wherever the impulse of the spirit was, there they proceeded, and they did not turn around when they walked.

**1:13** And as for the likeness of the living creatures, their appearance was like burning coals of fire and like the appearance of torches; this was a vision moving back and forth in the midst of the living creatures, a radiance of fire, and lightning coming forth from the fire.

**1:15** And as I gazed at the living creatures, one wheel appeared on the earth beside the living creatures, having four faces.

**1:16** And the appearance of the wheels and their work was like the sight of the sea; and the four of them had one likeness; and their appearance and their works were as if a wheel were in the middle of a wheel.

**1:17** As they went along their four sides, they kept going, and they did not turn around when they walked.

**1:18** The wheels also had stature, and height, and a fearsome appearance; and the entire body was full of eyes all around the four of them.

**1:19** And when the living creatures walked, the wheels also walked together beside them; and when the living creatures were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were also lifted up at the same time.

**1:20** Wherever the spirit went, there, as the spirit went, the wheels also were lifted up together following it; for the spirit of life was in the wheels.

**1:21** When those went, they went, and when those stood, they stood; and when those were lifted up from the earth, the wheels also were lifted up together following them, because the spirit of life was in the wheels.

**1:22** And over the heads of the living creatures was the likeness of an expanse, like the appearance of fearsome crystal, and stretched out over their heads above.

**1:23** And under the expanse their wings were straight, one toward another; each one veiled its own body with two wings, and the other was veiled in the same way.

**1:24** And I heard the sound of wings, like the sound of many waters, like the sound of the exalted God; when they walked, it was like the sound of a multitude, like the sound of an encampment; and when they stood, their wings were lowered.

**1:25** For when a voice came above the expanse that was over their head, they stood and lowered their wings.

**1:26** And above the expanse that was overhanging their head, like the appearance of a sapphire stone was the likeness of a throne; and upon the likeness of the throne was a likeness like the appearance of a man above.

**1:27** And I saw something like the look of electrum, like the appearance of fire inside it all around; from His loins and upward, and from His loins all the way downward, I saw something like the look of radiant fire all around,

**1:28** like the appearance of a bow when it is in a cloud on a day of rain. This was the appearance of the radiance in a circle all around.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**1:14** *(after `1:13`)* And the living creatures went and returned in the likeness of flashing lightning.

## Translator Notes

- **1:4** — Latin 'electri' ("electrum") can denote either the gleaming gold-silver alloy or amber. The explanatory phrase 'id est, de medio ignis' ("that is, from the midst of the fire") is a Vulgate-distinct gloss.
- **1:10** — The Vulgate specifies that the eagle's face is 'desuper' ("above") the four living creatures, a spatial detail not explicitly stated in the Hebrew text.
- **1:13** — Verse 1:14 (found in the Hebrew text) is absent from the Clementine Vulgate, and Latin 'visio' ("vision") in 1:13 reflects a Vulgate-distinct reading for the movement of the fire among the living creatures.
- **1:16** — Latin 'quasi visio maris' ("like the sight of the sea") reflects Jerome's rendering of Hebrew 'tarshish' as "the sea" rather than a gemstone such as beryl or chrysolite.
- **1:20** — Latin 'spiritus vitæ' ("the spirit of life," repeated in 1:21) is a Vulgate-distinct reading where the Hebrew phrase is commonly rendered "the spirit of the living creature."
