## Text

**26:1** And it happened in the eleventh year, on the first of the month, that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**26:2** "Son of man, because Tyre has said concerning Jerusalem, 'Aha! The gates of the peoples are shattered; she has turned to me; I will be filled; she is deserted,'

**26:3** therefore this is what the Lord God says: Look, I am against you, Tyre, and I will cause many nations to rise up against you, just as the surging sea rises up.

**26:4** And they will demolish the walls of Tyre, and they will destroy her towers; and I will scrape her dust from her, and I will make her a most gleaming rock.

**26:5** She will be a drying place for dragnets in the midst of the sea, for I have spoken, says the Lord God; and she will become plunder for the nations.

**26:6** Also her daughters who are in the field will be killed by the sword; and they will know that I am the Lord.

**26:7** For this is what the Lord God says: Look, I will bring against Tyre Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon from the north, king of kings, with horses, and chariots, and horsemen, and a company, and a great people.

**26:8** Your daughters who are in the field he will kill by the sword, and he will surround you with fortifications, and he will heap up a rampart all around, and he will raise a shield against you;

**26:9** and he will direct siege sheds and battering rams against your walls, and he will destroy your towers with his weaponry.

**26:10** By the flood of his horses, their dust will cover you; at the sound of the horsemen, and of the wheels, and of the chariots, your walls will shake, when he enters your gates as through the entrance of a demolished city.

**26:11** With the hooves of his horses he will trample all your streets; he will cut down your people with the sword, and your noble statues will collapse to the ground.

**26:12** They will ravage your wealth; they will plunder your merchandise, and they will destroy your walls, and they will overturn your splendid houses; and they will place your stones, and your timbers, and your dust in the midst of the waters.

**26:13** And I will make the multitude of your songs cease, and the sound of your lyres will be heard no more.

**26:14** And I will make you a most gleaming rock; you will be a drying place for dragnets, nor will you be built again, for I have spoken, says the Lord God.

**26:15** This is what the Lord God says to Tyre: Will not the islands be shaken at the sound of your downfall, and at the groaning of those killed among you, when they are put to death in your midst?

**26:16** And all the princes of the sea will come down from their thrones, and they will take off their robes, and they will cast off their multicolored garments, and they will be clothed with astonishment; they will sit on the ground, and, stunned, they will marvel at your sudden fall;

**26:17** and taking up a lament over you, they will say to you, 'How have you perished, you who dwell in the sea, renowned city, who were strong in the sea with your inhabitants, whom all dreaded?

**26:18** Now the ships will be astounded on the day of your terror, and the islands in the sea will be troubled, because no one goes out from you.'

**26:19** For this is what the Lord God says: When I make you a desolate city, like the cities that are not inhabited, and bring the abyss over you, and many waters cover you,

**26:20** and I drag you down with those who go down into the pit, to the everlasting people, and I place you in the lowest land, like ancient wastelands, with those who are led down into the pit, so that you will not be inhabited; moreover, when I give glory in the land of the living,

**26:21** I will reduce you to nothing, and you will not be; and though sought, you will not be found again forever, says the Lord God."

## Translator Notes

- **26:9** — Latin vineas et arietes ("siege sheds and battering rams") adapts Roman military terminology—vineae being movable protective shelters—to render Hebrew "the blow of his battering ram," while in armatura sua ("with his weaponry") renders Hebrew "with his axes/swords."
- **26:16** — Vulgate super repentino casu tuo ("at your sudden fall") takes the Hebrew phrase usually rendered "every moment" (lirga'im) in the sense of suddenness.
- **26:18** — Vulgate naves ("ships") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "coastlands/islands" (ha-iyim), and eo quod nullus egrediatur ex te ("because no one goes out from you") interprets Hebrew mi-tzetekh ("at your departure").
- **26:20** — Vulgate populum sempiternum ("the everlasting people") renders Hebrew am olam ("people of old"), and porro cum dedero gloriam ("moreover, when I give glory") construes the clause temporally as leading into v. 21's main verb, in nihilum redigam te ("I will reduce you to nothing," where Hebrew reads "a terror").
