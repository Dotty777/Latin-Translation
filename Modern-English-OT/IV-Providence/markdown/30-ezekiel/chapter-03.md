## Text

**3:1** And He said to me, "Son of man, whatever you find, eat; eat this scroll, and go, speak to the sons of Israel."

**3:2** And I opened my mouth, and He fed me with that scroll,

**3:3** and He said to me, "Son of man, your belly will eat, and your inner parts will be filled with this scroll that I give to you." And I ate it, and it became in my mouth like sweet honey.

**3:4** And He said to me, "Son of man, go to the house of Israel, and you shall speak My words to them.

**3:5** For not to a people of deep speech and of an unknown language are you being sent—to the house of Israel;

**3:6** nor to many peoples of deep speech and of an unknown language, whose words you cannot understand; and if you were sent to them, they themselves would listen to you;

**3:7** but the house of Israel are unwilling to listen to you, because they are unwilling to listen to Me; for the entire house of Israel has a brazen forehead and a hard heart.

**3:8** Look, I have made your face stronger than their faces, and your forehead harder than their foreheads;

**3:9** like adamant and like flint I have made your face; do not fear them, and do not be in dread before their face, because it is a provoking house."

**3:10** And He said to me, "Son of man, all My words that I speak to you, receive in your heart and hear with your ears;

**3:11** and go, go in to the exiles, to the sons of your people, and you shall speak to them and say to them, 'This is what the Lord God says,' if perhaps they will listen and desist."

**3:12** And the Spirit took me up, and I heard behind me the sound of a great commotion: "Blessed be the glory of the Lord from His place,"

**3:13** and the sound of the wings of the living creatures striking one against another, and the sound of the wheels following the living creatures, and the sound of a great commotion.

**3:14** The Spirit also lifted me up and took me away; and I went away bitter in the indignation of my spirit, for the hand of the Lord was with me, strengthening me.

**3:15** And I came to the exiles, to the heap of new crops, to those who lived beside the river Chebar; and I sat where they were sitting, and I remained there seven days mourning in their midst.

**3:16** Now when seven days had passed, the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**3:17** "Son of man, I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel, and you shall hear a word from My mouth and declare it to them from Me.

**3:18** If, when I say to the wicked man, 'You will certainly die,' you do not declare it to him, nor speak so that he may turn away from his wicked way and live, that wicked man himself will die in his iniquity, but I will require his blood from your hand.

**3:19** But if you declare it to the wicked man, and he does not turn from his wickedness and from his wicked way, he himself indeed will die in his iniquity, but you have delivered your own soul.

**3:20** But also if a righteous man turns away from his righteousness and commits iniquity, I will place a stumbling block before him; he himself will die because you did not declare it to him. He will die in his sin, and his righteous deeds that he did will not be held in remembrance, yet I will require his blood from your hand.

**3:21** But if you declare to the righteous man that the righteous man should not sin, and he does not sin, he will certainly live, because you declared it to him, and you have delivered your own soul."

**3:22** And the hand of the Lord came upon me, and He said to me, "Rise up and go out into the plain, and there I will speak with you."

**3:23** And rising up, I went out into the plain; and look, the glory of the Lord was standing there, like the glory that I saw beside the river Chebar; and I fell on my face.

**3:24** And the Spirit entered into me and set me on my feet, and He spoke to me and said to me, "Go in and shut yourself in the midst of your house.

**3:25** And you, son of man, look, bonds have been placed upon you, and they will bind you with them, and you will not go out from their midst.

**3:26** And I will make your tongue cling to your palate, and you will be mute, and not like a man who rebukes, because it is a provoking house.

**3:27** But when I speak to you, I will open your mouth, and you shall say to them, 'This is what the Lord God says: Whoever listens, let him listen, and whoever desists, let him desist,' because it is a provoking house."

## Translator Notes

- **3:5** — The Vulgate preserves the elliptical Hebrew syntax with no expressed "but" before 'ad domum Israël' ("to the house of Israel"). In 3:6, Latin 'audire' ("hear") carries the sense "understand."
- **3:12** — Latin reads bare spiritus (without Dei or Domini), which can be rendered 'a spirit', 'the spirit', or 'wind' (compare lowercase 'spirit' in 1:12, 1:20, and 10:17, versus 'Spirit of the Lord' in 11:5, 37:1); capitalization as 'the Spirit' is subject to edition-wide editorial policy.
- **3:14** — Latin 'erat mecum, confortans me' ("was with me, strengthening me") is a Vulgate-distinct rendering where the Hebrew text reads "was strong upon me." Latin reads bare spiritus (without Dei or Domini), which can be rendered 'a spirit', 'the spirit', or 'wind' (compare lowercase 'spirit' in 1:12, 1:20, and 10:17, versus 'Spirit of the Lord' in 11:5, 37:1); capitalization as 'the Spirit' is subject to edition-wide editorial policy.
- **3:15** — Latin 'ad acervum novarum frugum' ("to the heap of new crops") translates the Hebrew place name Tel-abib etymologically, and 'mœrens' ("mourning") reflects a Vulgate-distinct reading for Hebrew "overwhelmed" or "appalled."
- **3:17** — Latin 'annuntiare' ("declare," repeated through 3:21) renders the watchman's duty of giving formal notice or warning of God's word.
- **3:24** — Latin reads bare spiritus (without Dei or Domini), which can be rendered 'a spirit', 'the spirit', or 'wind' (compare lowercase 'spirit' in 1:12, 1:20, and 10:17, versus 'Spirit of the Lord' in 11:5, 37:1); capitalization as 'the Spirit' is subject to edition-wide editorial policy.
