## Text

**43:1** And he led me to the gate that faced toward the eastern way.

**43:2** And look, the glory of the God of Israel was entering by the eastern way, and His voice was like the voice of many waters, and the earth was shining from His majesty.

**43:3** And I saw a vision according to the appearance that I had seen when He came to destroy the city, and the appearance was according to the sight that I had seen beside the river Chebar; and I fell on my face.

**43:4** And the majesty of the Lord entered the temple by the way of the gate that faced toward the east.

**43:5** And the Spirit lifted me up and brought me into the inner court; and look, the house was filled with the glory of the Lord.

**43:6** And I heard One speaking to me from the house, and the man who was standing beside me

**43:7** said to me, "Son of man, this is the place of My throne and the place of the soles of My feet, where I dwell in the midst of the sons of Israel forever; and the house of Israel will no longer defile My holy name, they and their kings, in their fornications, and in the ruins of their kings, and in the high places.

**43:8** They built their threshold beside My threshold and their doorposts beside My doorposts, and there was a wall between Me and them; and they defiled My holy name in the abominations that they committed; therefore I consumed them in My anger.

**43:9** Now then, let them cast their fornication and the ruins of their kings far away from Me, and I will dwell in their midst always.

**43:10** As for you, son of man, show the temple to the house of Israel, and let them be put to shame by their iniquities, and let them measure the structure,

**43:11** and let them be ashamed of all that they have done. Show them the form of the house and of its structure, the exits and entrances, and its entire plan, and all its precepts, and all its order, and all its laws, and you shall write them in their sight, so that they may keep all its plans and its precepts, and do them.

**43:12** This is the law of the house on the summit of the mountain: its entire boundary all around is Holy of Holies. This, then, is the law of the house."

**43:13** Now these are the measurements of the altar in the truest cubit, which had a cubit and a handbreadth: in its hollow was a cubit, and a cubit in width; and its border up to its lip and all around was one handbreadth; this also was the trench of the altar.

**43:14** And from the hollow of the ground to the lowest ledge were two cubits, and a width of one cubit; and from the smaller ledge to the greater ledge were four cubits, and a width of one cubit.

**43:15** And the ariel itself was four cubits, and from the ariel upward were four horns.

**43:16** And the ariel was twelve cubits in length by twelve cubits in width, square, with equal sides.

**43:17** And the ledge was fourteen cubits in length by fourteen cubits in width on its four corners; and the rim all around it was half a cubit, and its hollow was one cubit all around; and its steps were turned toward the east.

**43:18** And he said to me, "Son of man, this is what the Lord God says: 'These are the rites of the altar, on whatever day it is built, so that a burnt offering may be offered upon it and blood may be poured out.

**43:19** And you shall grant to the priests and the Levites who are of the seed of Zadok, who approach Me,' says the Lord God, 'to offer to Me a young bull from the herd for sin.

**43:20** And taking some of its blood, you shall put it on its four horns, and on the four corners of the ledge, and on the rim all around; and you shall cleanse it and atone for it.

**43:21** And you shall take the young bull that has been offered for sin, and you shall burn it in a separate place of the house, outside the sanctuary.

**43:22** And on the second day you shall offer an unblemished male of the goats for sin; and they shall atone for the altar just as they atoned with the young bull.

**43:23** And when you have finished atoning for it, you shall offer an unblemished young bull from the herd and an unblemished ram from the flock.

**43:24** And you shall offer them in the presence of the Lord; and the priests shall cast salt upon them, and they shall offer them as a burnt offering to the Lord.

**43:25** For seven days you shall prepare a male goat for sin daily; and they shall offer a young bull from the herd and a ram from the flocks, unblemished.

**43:26** For seven days they shall atone for the altar and cleanse it, and they shall fill its hand.

**43:27** And when the days are completed, on the eighth day and onward, the priests shall prepare your burnt offerings on the altar, and what they offer for peace; and I will be appeased toward you,' says the Lord God."

## Translator Notes

- **43:5** — Latin reads bare spiritus (without Dei or Domini), which can be rendered 'a spirit', 'the spirit', or 'wind' (compare lowercase 'spirit' in 1:12, 1:20, and 10:17, versus 'Spirit of the Lord' in 11:5, 37:1); capitalization as 'the Spirit' is subject to edition-wide editorial policy.
- **43:7** — The Vulgate links 'the man who was standing beside me' (v. 6) grammatically as the subject of 'said to me' (v. 7) even though the speech is in God's first person. It also reads 'in the ruins of their kings' (Latin in ruinis regum suorum, repeated in v. 9), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'the corpses [or monuments] of their kings.'
- **43:11** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which begins with a conditional clause ('And if they are ashamed...'), the Vulgate continues the jussive commands from v. 10 ('and let them be ashamed of all that they have done').
- **43:13** — The Vulgate specifies 'the truest cubit' (Latin in cubito verissimo) and 'the trench of the altar' (Latin fossa altaris), distinct from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's 'cubits' and 'base/height.'
- **43:15** — The Vulgate retains the untranslated Hebrew technical term ariel (also in v. 16), denoting the upper hearth of the altar.
- **43:26** — 'Fill its hand' translates the Latin idiom implebunt manum ejus literally, a sacrificial expression for consecrating or dedicating the altar.
