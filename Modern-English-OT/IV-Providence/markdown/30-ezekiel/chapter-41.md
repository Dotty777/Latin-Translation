## Text

**41:1** And he brought me into the temple, and he measured the fronts: six cubits in width on this side, and six cubits in width on that side, the width of the tabernacle.

**41:2** And the width of the gate was ten cubits, and the sides of the gate were five cubits on this side and five cubits on that side; and he measured its length, forty cubits, and the width, twenty cubits.

**41:3** And having entered inside, he measured on the front of the gate two cubits, and the gate, six cubits, and the width of the gate, seven cubits.

**41:4** And he measured its length, twenty cubits, and its width, twenty cubits, before the face of the temple. And he said to me, "This is the Holy of Holies."

**41:5** And he measured the wall of the house, six cubits, and the width of the side, four cubits everywhere all around the house.

**41:6** Now the sides, side to side, were twice thirty-three; and there were projections that entered through the wall of the house, in the sides all around, so that they might hold fast and not touch the wall of the temple.

**41:7** And there was a broad passage in a circle, ascending upward by a spiral staircase, and it led into the upper room of the temple in a circuit; therefore the temple was wider in the upper parts, and thus from the lower parts one ascended to the upper parts into the middle.

**41:8** And I saw in the house a height all around, the sides founded to the measure of a reed, a space of six cubits,

**41:9** and the width along the wall of the side on the outside, five cubits; and there was an inner house in the sides of the house.

**41:10** And between the treasury chambers was a width of twenty cubits all around the house on every side,

**41:11** and the door of the side was for prayer: one door toward the way of the north, and one door toward the southern way; and the width of the place for prayer was five cubits all around.

**41:12** And the building that was separated and turned toward the way facing the sea was seventy cubits in width; now the wall of the building was five cubits in width all around, and its length was ninety cubits.

**41:13** And he measured the length of the house, one hundred cubits; and the building that was separated, and its walls, one hundred cubits in length.

**41:14** Now the width before the face of the house, and of that which was separated facing the east, was one hundred cubits.

**41:15** And he measured the length of the building opposite the face of that which was separated at the back, the galleries on each side, one hundred cubits, and the inner temple, and the vestibules of the court.

**41:16** The thresholds, and the slanted windows, and the galleries all around in three parts, opposite the threshold of each one, and overlaid with wood in a circuit all around; and from the ground up to the windows, and the windows were closed above the doors.

**41:17** And up to the inner house, and on the outside along the entire wall all around, inside and outside, by measure.

**41:18** And cherubim and palm trees were crafted, and a palm tree was between cherub and cherub, and the cherub had two faces.

**41:19** The face of a man was beside the palm tree on this side, and the face of a lion beside the palm tree on the other side, fashioned throughout the whole house all around.

**41:20** From the ground up to the upper parts of the gate, cherubim and palm trees were engraved on the wall of the temple.

**41:21** The threshold was four-cornered, and the face of the sanctuary, appearance opposite appearance.

**41:22** The height of the wooden altar was three cubits, and its length was two cubits; and its corners, and its length, and its walls were wooden. And he spoke to me, "This is the table before the Lord."

**41:23** And there were two doors in the temple and in the sanctuary.

**41:24** And in the two doors on each side there were two small doors that folded upon one another; for there were two doors on each side of the doors.

**41:25** And cherubim were engraved on the doors of the temple themselves, and carvings of palm trees, just as they were also fashioned on the walls; for this reason the timbers were also thicker on the front of the vestibule on the outside.

**41:26** Above these were slanted windows, and the likeness of palm trees on this side and on that, on the small shoulders of the vestibule, according to the sides of the house and the width of the walls.

## Translator Notes

- **41:6** — Latin latera ('sides') renders Hebrew tsela'ot ('side chambers'). Distinctively, the Vulgate reads bis triginta tria ('twice thirty-three'), interpreting the Hebrew expression for thirty chambers in three tiers with a dual multiplier.
- **41:7** — The Vulgate renders this difficult architectural verse with a circular broad passage (platea) ascending by a spiral staircase (per cochleam) into the upper room of the temple, accounting for the greater width of the upper levels.
- **41:11** — Distinctively, the Vulgate translates the Hebrew term for the open space beside the chambers (munnach) as ad orationem ('for prayer') and loci ad orationem ('of the place for prayer').
- **41:15** — Latin ethecas (also in v. 16) is Jerome's transliteration of the rare Hebrew architectural term 'attiqim, referring to projecting galleries or corridors.
- **41:25** — Where the Hebrew mentions a thick wooden canopy at the front of the vestibule, the Vulgate adds a causal connection (quam ob rem et grossiora erant ligna), explaining that the exterior timbers were thicker because of the carvings.
