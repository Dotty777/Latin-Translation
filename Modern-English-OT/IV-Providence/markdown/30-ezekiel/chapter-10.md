## Text

**10:1** And I saw, and look, in the expanse that was above the head of the cherubim, something like a sapphire stone, like the appearance of the likeness of a throne, appeared above them.

**10:2** And He spoke to the man who was clothed in linen and said, "Enter into the midst of the wheels that are beneath the cherubim, and fill your hand with coals of fire that are among the cherubim, and pour them out over the city." And he entered in my sight.

**10:3** Now the cherubim were standing on the right side of the house when the man was entering, and a cloud filled the inner court.

**10:4** And the glory of the Lord was lifted up from above the cherub to the threshold of the house; and the house was filled with the cloud, and the court was filled with the splendor of the glory of the Lord.

**10:5** And the sound of the wings of the cherubim was heard as far as the outer court, like the voice of Almighty God speaking.

**10:6** And when He had commanded the man who was clothed in linen, saying, "Take fire from the midst of the wheels that are among the cherubim," he entered and stood beside a wheel.

**10:7** And a cherub stretched out his hand from the midst of the cherubim to the fire that was among the cherubim, and took some, and put it into the hands of the one who was clothed in linen, who received it and went out.

**10:8** And there appeared on the cherubim the likeness of a man's hand beneath their wings.

**10:9** And I saw, and look, four wheels beside the cherubim: one wheel beside one cherub, and another wheel beside another cherub; and the appearance of the wheels was like the sight of a chrysolite stone.

**10:10** And their aspect was one likeness for the four, as if a wheel were in the midst of a wheel.

**10:11** And when they went, they proceeded in four directions, and they did not turn back as they went; rather, to the place toward which the one that was first inclined to go, the others also followed, and they did not turn around.

**10:12** And their whole body, and necks, and hands, and wings, and circles were full of eyes all around the four wheels.

**10:13** And He called these wheels "Whirling" in my hearing.

**10:14** Now each one had four faces: one face was the face of a cherub, and the second face was the face of a man; and in the third was the face of a lion, and in the fourth was the face of an eagle.

**10:15** And the cherubim were lifted up; this is the very living creature that I had seen beside the river Chebar.

**10:16** And when the cherubim went, the wheels also went alongside them; and when the cherubim lifted their wings to be raised up from the earth, the wheels did not stay behind, but they themselves also were beside them.

**10:17** When those stood, they stood, and together with those lifted up, they were lifted up; for the spirit of life was in them.

**10:18** And the glory of the Lord went out from the threshold of the temple and stood above the cherubim.

**10:19** And the cherubim, lifting their wings, were raised up from the earth before me; and as they went out, the wheels also followed after; and it stood at the entrance of the east gate of the house of the Lord, and the glory of the God of Israel was above them.

**10:20** This is the very living creature that I saw beneath the God of Israel beside the river Chebar, and I understood that they were cherubim.

**10:21** Each one had four faces, and each one had four wings, and the likeness of a man's hand was under their wings.

**10:22** And the likeness of their faces was the very faces that I had seen beside the river Chebar, and their look; and the impulse of each one was to proceed before its face.

## Translator Notes

- **10:11** — Latin "quae prima erat" ("the one that was first," feminine singular) refers grammatically to the foremost wheel, whereas the Hebrew reads "the head."
- **10:12** — The Vulgate reads "colla" ("necks") and "circuli" ("circles"), where the Hebrew has "their backs" and "the wheels."
- **10:13** — Latin "volubiles" ("Whirling" or "revolving") renders the Hebrew term for the wheelwork ("Galgal").
- **10:14** — The phrasing "in the third" and "in the fourth" literally reflects the Vulgate shift from nominative to prepositional phrases.
- **10:17** — The Vulgate reads "spiritus vitae" ("the spirit of life"), whereas the Hebrew reads "the spirit of the living creature."
- **10:19** — Latin "stetit" ("it stood") is third-person singular, referring to the cherubim assembly as a single living creature (compare 10:15, 20).
