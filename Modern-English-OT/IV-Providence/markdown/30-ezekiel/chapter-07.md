## Text

**7:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**7:2** "And you, son of man, this is what the Lord God says to the land of Israel: 'The end has come; the end has come upon the four quarters of the land.

**7:3** Now the end is upon you, and I will send My fury against you; and I will judge you according to your ways, and I will set all your abominations against you.

**7:4** And My eye will not spare you, and I will not have pity; but I will set your ways upon you, and your abominations will be in your midst, and you will know that I am the Lord.'

**7:5** This is what the Lord God says: 'One affliction, an affliction, look, it is coming!

**7:6** The end has come, the end has come; it has awakened against you; look, it is coming.

**7:7** Ruin has come upon you, who dwell in the land; the time has come, the day of slaughter is near, and not of the glory of the mountains.

**7:8** Now shortly I will pour out My wrath upon you, and I will complete My fury against you; and I will judge you according to your ways, and I will lay all your crimes upon you,

**7:9** and My eye will not spare, nor will I have pity; but I will lay your ways upon you, and your abominations will be in your midst, and you will know that I am the Lord who strikes.

**7:10** Look, the day! Look, it is coming! Ruin has gone out, the rod has blossomed, pride has sprouted,

**7:11** iniquity has risen up in a rod of wickedness. None of them, and none of the people, nor of their sound; and there will be no rest among them.

**7:12** The time has come, the day has drawn near: let the one who buys not rejoice, and let the one who sells not mourn, for wrath is upon all its people.

**7:13** For the one who sells will not return to what he sold, and their life is still among the living; for the vision concerning all its multitude will not turn back, and a man in the iniquity of his life will not be strengthened.

**7:14** Blow the trumpet, let all be prepared; yet there is no one who goes to battle, for My wrath is upon its entire people.

**7:15** The sword is outside, and plague and famine are inside; the one who is in the field will die by the sword, and those who are in the city will be devoured by pestilence and famine.

**7:16** And those of them who flee will be saved, and they will be on the mountains like doves of the valleys, all trembling, each one in his iniquity.

**7:17** All hands will go limp, and all knees will flow with water.

**7:18** And they will bind sackcloth around themselves, and dread will cover them; and shame will be on every face, and baldness on all their heads.

**7:19** Their silver will be thrown outside, and their gold will become a dung heap; their silver and their gold will not be able to deliver them on the day of the fury of the Lord. They will not satisfy their soul, and their bellies will not be filled, because it has become the stumbling block of their iniquity.

**7:20** And they set the ornament of their jewels for pride, and from it they made images of their abominations and idols; because of this I have given it to them as uncleanness.

**7:21** And I will give it into the hands of foreigners for plundering, and to the wicked of the earth as spoil, and they will defile it.

**7:22** And I will turn My face away from them, and they will violate My secret place; and emissaries will enter into it, and they will defile it.

**7:23** Make a confinement, because the land is full of the judgment of bloodshed, and the city is full of iniquity.

**7:24** And I will bring the worst of the nations, and they will possess their houses; and I will make the pride of the mighty cease, and they will possess their sanctuaries.

**7:25** When distress comes upon them, they will seek peace, and there will be none.

**7:26** Trouble upon trouble will come, and report upon report; and they will seek a vision from the prophet, and the law will perish from the priest, and counsel from the elders.

**7:27** The king will mourn, and the prince will be clothed with grief, and the hands of the people of the land will be troubled; according to their way I will do to them, and according to their judgments I will judge them, and they will know that I am the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **7:3** — In native Clementine Vulgate numbering, 7:3-9 follow the Masoretic sequence (mapping to Saints.Bible 7:7, 7:8, 7:9, 7:3, 7:4, 7:5, 7:6 respectively); 7:10 aligns 1-to-1.
- **7:7** — Latin "the day of slaughter is near, and not of the glory of the mountains" (dies occisionis, et non gloriæ montium) is a distinctive Vulgate rendering where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads a day of tumult rather than joyful shouting on the mountains.
- **7:11** — The abrupt verbless clause ("None of them, and none of the people, nor of their sound") reflects an obscure Hebrew line, and the Vulgate reads "no rest" (non erit requies) where the Hebrew is variously understood as eminence or wailing.
- **7:23** — Latin "Make a confinement" (Fac conclusionem, i.e., a shutting-in or blockade) renders a rare Hebrew noun usually understood as "the chain"; similarly in verse 22 emissarii ("emissaries") corresponds to Hebrew "violent ones," and in verse 24 possidebunt ("they will possess") corresponds to Hebrew "they will be profaned."
