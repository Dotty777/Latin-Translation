## Text

**33:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**33:2** "Son of man, speak to the children of your people, and you will say to them: 'A land, when I bring a sword upon it, and the people of the land take one man from the outermost among them and appoint him over themselves as a watchman,

**33:3** and he sees the sword coming upon the land, and blows the trumpet, and announces it to the people:

**33:4** then whoever he may be who hears the sound of the trumpet and does not take heed for himself, and the sword comes and takes him away, his blood will be upon his own head.

**33:5** He heard the sound of the trumpet and did not take heed for himself; his blood will be upon himself. But if he guards himself, he will save his life.

**33:6** But if the watchman sees the sword coming and does not sound the trumpet, and the people do not guard themselves, and the sword comes and takes a life from among them, that one indeed has been seized in his iniquity, but I will require his blood from the hand of the watchman.'

**33:7** And you, son of man, I have appointed you as a watchman for the house of Israel; therefore, hearing a word from My mouth, you will announce it to them from Me.

**33:8** If, when I say to the wicked man, 'Wicked man, you will surely die,' you do not speak so that the wicked man may guard himself from his way, that wicked man himself will die in his iniquity, but I will require his blood from your hand.

**33:9** But if, when you announce to the wicked man that he should turn back from his ways, he does not turn back from his way, he himself will die in his iniquity, whereas you have delivered your own life.

**33:10** Therefore you, son of man, say to the house of Israel: 'You have spoken thus, saying, "Our iniquities and our sins are upon us, and in them we are wasting away; how then will we be able to live?"'

**33:11** Say to them: 'As I live, says the Lord God, I do not desire the death of the wicked man, but that the wicked man turn back from his way and live. Turn back, turn back from your most evil ways; and why will you die, house of Israel?'

**33:12** And so, son of man, say to the children of your people: 'The righteousness of the righteous man will not deliver him on whatever day he sins, and the wickedness of the wicked man will not harm him on whatever day he turns back from his wickedness; and the righteous man will not be able to live in his righteousness on whatever day he sins.

**33:13** Even if I say to the righteous man that he will surely live, and trusting in his righteousness he commits iniquity, all his righteous deeds will be given over to oblivion, and in his iniquity which he has practiced, in it he will die.

**33:14** But if I say to the wicked man, "You will surely die," and he repents from his sin and practices justice and righteousness,

**33:15** and that wicked man restores the pledge, and gives back what was taken by robbery, walks in the commandments of life, and does not do anything unjust, he will surely live and will not die.

**33:16** None of his sins that he has committed will be imputed to him; he has practiced justice and righteousness; he will surely live.'

**33:17** And the children of your people have said, 'The way of the Lord is not of equal weight,' yet their own way is unjust.

**33:18** For when the righteous man departs from his righteousness and commits iniquities, he will die in them.

**33:19** And when the wicked man departs from his wickedness and practices justice and righteousness, he will live in them.

**33:20** Yet you say, 'The way of the Lord is not right.' I will judge each one of you according to his ways, house of Israel."

**33:21** And it happened in the twelfth year, in the tenth month, on the fifth of the month of our exile, one who had fled from Jerusalem came to me, saying, "The city has been laid waste."

**33:22** Now the hand of the Lord had come upon me in the evening, before the one who had fled arrived, and He opened my mouth until he came to me in the morning; and with my mouth opened, I was silent no longer.

**33:23** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**33:24** "Son of man, those who dwell in these ruins upon the soil of Israel speak, saying, 'Abraham was one, and he possessed the land by inheritance; but we are many; the land has been given to us as a possession.'

**33:25** Therefore you will say to them: 'This is what the Lord God says: "You who eat with blood, and lift up your eyes to your uncleannesses, and shed blood, will you indeed possess the land by inheritance?"'

**33:27** These things you will say to them: 'This is what the Lord God says: "As I live, surely those who dwell in the ruins will fall by the sword, and the one who is in the field will be handed over to the beasts to be devoured, while those who are in strongholds and caves will die by pestilence.

**33:28** And I will make the land a wasteland and a desert, and its proud strength will fail; and the mountains of Israel will be made desolate, because there will be no one who passes through them;

**33:29** and they will know that I am the Lord, when I make their land desolate and deserted, because of all their abominations which they have practiced."'

**33:30** And you, son of man, the children of your people, who talk about you beside the walls and in the doorways of the houses, also say one to another, each man to his neighbor, speaking, 'Come, and let us hear what word is going forth from the Lord.'

**33:31** And they come to you as if a people were entering, and they sit before you, My people; and they hear your words, yet they do not do them, for they turn them into a song of their mouth, and their heart follows their greed.

**33:32** And you are to them like a musical song that is sung with a pleasant and sweet sound; and they hear your words, and they do not do them.

**33:33** And when what has been foretold comes (for look, it is coming), then they will know that a prophet has been among them."

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**33:26** *(after `33:25`)* You have stood on your swords, you have committed abominations, and each one has defiled the wife of his neighbor; and will you possess the land by inheritance?

## Translator Notes

- **33:2** — Vulgate 'de novissimis suis' ('from the outermost among them') literally renders the Hebrew idiom for 'from their extremities/whole number,' and in Latin can also carry the sense 'from the lowest among them.'
- **33:14** — Vulgate 'egerit poenitentiam' ('repents') renders a Hebrew verb meaning 'turns back'; similarly in v. 16, 'non imputabuntur' ('will not be imputed') corresponds to 'will not be remembered' in the Masoretic Text.
- **33:31** — Vulgate 'in canticum oris sui vertunt illos' ('they turn them into a song of their mouth') diverges from the Masoretic Text, which speaks of expressing lustful or affectionate desires with their mouths; likewise v. 32 renders the Hebrew 'love song' as 'carmen musicum' ('a musical song').
- **33:26 *(additional)*** — The expression "stood on your swords" literally renders the Latin idiom meaning to rely on armed force.
