## Text

**34:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**34:2** "Son of man, prophesy concerning the shepherds of Israel; prophesy, and you will say to the shepherds, 'This is what the Lord God says: Woe to the shepherds of Israel who were feeding themselves! Are not the flocks fed by the shepherds?

**34:3** You were eating the milk, and clothing yourselves with the wool, and killing what was fat, yet you were not feeding My flock.

**34:4** What was weak you did not strengthen, and what was sick you did not heal; what was broken you did not bind up, and what was cast away you did not bring back, and what had been lost you did not seek; but with harshness you were ruling over them, and with force.

**34:5** And My sheep were scattered because there was no shepherd; and they came to be devoured by all the beasts of the field, and they were scattered.

**34:6** My flocks wandered on all the mountains and on every high hill; and My flocks were scattered over the entire face of the earth, and there was no one who searched; there was no one, I say, who searched.

**34:7** Therefore, shepherds, hear the word of the Lord.

**34:8** As I live, says the Lord God, surely, since My flocks have become prey, and My sheep have come to be devoured by all the beasts of the field because there was no shepherd, nor indeed did My shepherds seek My flock, but the shepherds were feeding themselves and were not feeding My flocks,

**34:9** therefore, shepherds, hear the word of the Lord.

**34:10** This is what the Lord God says: Look, I Myself am against the shepherds; I will demand My flock from their hand, and I will make them cease so that they no longer feed the flock, nor will the shepherds feed themselves anymore; and I will rescue My flock from their mouth, and it will no longer be food for them.

**34:11** For this is what the Lord God says: Look, I Myself will search for My sheep and look after them.

**34:12** Just as a shepherd looks after his flock on the day when he is in the midst of his dispersed sheep, so I will look after My sheep, and I will rescue them from all the places in which they had been scattered on the day of cloud and thick darkness.

**34:13** And I will bring them out from the peoples, and gather them from the lands, and lead them into their own land; and I will feed them on the mountains of Israel, by the streams, and in all the dwelling places of the land.

**34:14** In the most fertile pastures I will feed them, and their pastures will be on the high mountains of Israel; there they will rest in green grass, and in rich pastures they will graze upon the mountains of Israel.

**34:15** I will feed My sheep, and I will make them lie down, says the Lord God.

**34:16** What had been lost I will search for, and what was cast away I will bring back, and what had been broken I will bind up, and what had been weak I will strengthen, and what is fat and strong I will guard; and I will feed them in justice.

**34:17** As for you, My flocks, this is what the Lord God says: Look, I am judging between animal and animal, of rams and male goats.

**34:18** Was it not enough for you to graze on the good pastures? Moreover, you also trampled the rest of your pastures with your feet; and while you were drinking the purest water, you were muddying the rest with your feet;

**34:19** and My sheep were feeding on what had been trampled by your feet, and what your feet had muddied, that they were drinking.

**34:20** Therefore, this is what the Lord God says to you: Look, I Myself am judging between the fat animal and the lean:

**34:21** because you were pushing with flanks and shoulders, and with your horns you were thrusting all the weak animals until they were scattered abroad,

**34:22** I will save My flock, and it will no longer be prey, and I will judge between animal and animal.

**34:23** And I will raise up over them one shepherd who will feed them, My servant David; he himself will feed them, and he himself will be their shepherd.

**34:24** And I, the Lord, will be their God, and My servant David will be prince in their midst; I, the Lord, have spoken.

**34:25** And I will make a covenant of peace with them, and I will make the worst beasts cease from the land; and those who dwell in the wilderness will sleep securely in the woodlands.

**34:26** And I will set them around My hill as a blessing, and I will bring down the rain in its season; there will be rains of blessing.

**34:27** And the tree of the field will yield its fruit, and the land will yield its produce, and they will be in their own land without fear; and they will know that I am the Lord, when I have broken the chains of their yoke and delivered them from the hand of those who rule over them.

**34:28** And they will no longer be prey among the nations, nor will the beasts of the earth devour them; instead, they will dwell confidently, without any terror.

**34:29** And I will raise up for them a renowned shoot, and they will no longer be diminished by famine in the land, nor will they bear the reproach of the nations any longer.

**34:30** And they will know that I, the Lord their God, am with them, and that they themselves are My people, the house of Israel, declares the Lord God.

**34:31** As for you, My flocks, the flocks of My pasture, you are human beings, and I am the Lord your God, says the Lord God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **34:3** — Latin Lac ("milk") follows the Septuagint in reading the Hebrew consonants as halab ("milk"), whereas the Masoretic Text vocalizes them as heleb ("fat").
- **34:6** — Latin non erat, inquam, qui requireret ("there was no one, I say, who searched") repeats the same verb with an emphatic parenthetical "I say," whereas the Masoretic Text uses two different verbs without "I say."
- **34:16** — Latin custodiam ("I will guard") agrees with the Septuagint against the Masoretic Text's "I will destroy," reflecting a consonant variant in the underlying Hebrew.
- **34:25** — Latin qui habitant in deserto, securi dormient in saltibus makes "those who dwell in the wilderness" the subject of "will sleep securely in the woodlands," whereas the Masoretic Text coordinates two future verbs ("they will dwell securely in the wilderness and sleep in the woods").
- **34:29** — Latin germen nominatum ("a renowned shoot") renders the Hebrew "planting of renown" with germen ("shoot/sprout"), echoing the messianic title used in the Vulgate of Jeremiah 23:5 and 33:15.
