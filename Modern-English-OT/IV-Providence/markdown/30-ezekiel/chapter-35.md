## Text

**35:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**35:2** "Son of man, set your face against Mount Seir, and you will prophesy concerning it, and you will say to it,

**35:3** 'This is what the Lord God says: Look, I am against you, Mount Seir; and I will stretch out My hand over you, and I will make you desolate and deserted.

**35:4** I will demolish your cities, and you will be deserted; and you will know that I am the Lord.

**35:5** Because you have been a perpetual enemy, and have delivered the sons of Israel into the hands of the sword in the time of their affliction, in the time of final iniquity,

**35:6** therefore, as I live, says the Lord God, surely I will hand you over to blood, and blood will pursue you; and since you hate blood, blood will pursue you.

**35:7** And I will make Mount Seir desolate and deserted, and I will remove from it the one who goes and the one who returns.

**35:8** And I will fill its mountains with its slaughtered; on your hills, and in your valleys and in the streambeds, those killed by the sword will fall.

**35:9** I will hand you over to perpetual wastelands, and your cities will not be inhabited; and you will know that I am the Lord God.

**35:10** Because you have said, "The two nations and the two lands will be mine, and I will possess them by inheritance," although the Lord was there,

**35:11** therefore, as I live, says the Lord God, surely I will act according to your anger and according to your envy, which you carried out while hating them; and I will be made known through them when I have judged you.

**35:12** And you will know that I, the Lord, have heard all your reproaches which you spoke concerning the mountains of Israel, saying, "Deserted, they have been given to us to be devoured."

**35:13** And you have risen up against Me with your mouth, and you have spoken your disparaging words against Me; I have heard.

**35:14** This is what the Lord God says: While the whole earth rejoices, I will reduce you to a wasteland;

**35:15** just as you rejoiced over the inheritance of the house of Israel because it was laid waste, so I will do to you: you will be laid waste, Mount Seir, and all Idumea; and they will know that I am the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **35:6** — Latin cum sanguinem oderis ("since you hate blood," or concessively "although you hate blood") lacks the negation found in the Masoretic Text ("since you did not hate bloodshed").
- **35:11** — Latin notus efficiar per eos ("I will be made known through them") uses per ("through") where the Masoretic Text reads "among them."
- **35:13** — Latin derogastis adversum me verba vestra ("you have spoken your disparaging words against Me") interprets the verb as disparagement rather than the Masoretic Text's "multiplied your words against Me."
- **35:15** — Latin Idumæa ("Idumea") is the Greco-Latin name used by the Vulgate for Hebrew Edom.
