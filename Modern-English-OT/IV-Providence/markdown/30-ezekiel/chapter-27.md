## Text

**27:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**27:2** "You, therefore, son of man, take up a lament over Tyre,

**27:3** and you will say to Tyre, who dwells at the entrance of the sea, the commerce of the peoples to many islands, 'This is what the Lord God says: Tyre, you have said, "I am of perfect splendor,

**27:4** and situated in the heart of the sea." Your neighbors who built you have fulfilled your splendor.

**27:5** With fir trees from Senir they constructed you, with all the planks of the sea; they took a cedar from Lebanon to make a mast for you.

**27:6** Oaks from Bashan they hewed into your oars, and they made your rowing benches for you from Indian ivory, and little cabins from the islands of Italy.

**27:7** Multicolored fine linen from Egypt was woven for you into a sail, to be set on the mast; blue and purple from the islands of Elishah became your covering.

**27:8** The inhabitants of Sidon and Arvad were your rowers; your wise men, Tyre, became your pilots.

**27:9** The elders of Gebal and its skilled men had sailors for the service of your varied gear; all the ships of the sea and their sailors were among the people of your trade.

**27:10** Persians, and Lydians, and Libyans were in your army as your men of war; they hung shield and helmet in you for your adornment.

**27:11** The sons of Arvad with your army were upon your walls all around; and the Pygmies also, who were in your towers, hung their quivers on your walls all the way around; they completed your beauty.

**27:12** The Carthaginians were your merchants because of the abundance of all riches; with silver, iron, tin, and lead they filled your fairs.

**27:13** Greece, Tubal, and Meshech, they were your traders; they brought slaves and bronze vessels to your people.

**27:14** From the house of Togarmah they brought horses, and horsemen, and mules to your marketplace.

**27:15** The sons of Dedan were your merchants; many islands were the trade of your hand; they exchanged tusks of ivory and ebony as your payment.

**27:16** Syria was your merchant because of the abundance of your works; they displayed gems, and purple, and checkered cloth, and fine linen, and silk, and chodchod in your market.

**27:17** Judah and the land of Israel, they were your traders in the finest wheat; they displayed balsam, and honey, and oil, and resin in your fairs.

**27:18** Damascus was your merchant in the abundance of your works, in the abundance of diverse wealth, in rich wine, in wool of the finest color.

**27:19** Dan, and Greece, and Mosel displayed wrought iron in your fairs; stacte and calamus were in your trade.

**27:20** Dedan were your traders in carpets for sitting.

**27:21** Arabia and all the princes of Kedar, they were the merchants of your hand; with lambs, and rams, and young goats, they came to you as your merchants.

**27:22** The sellers of Sheba and Raamah, they were your merchants; with all the finest spices, and precious stone, and gold, which they displayed in your market.

**27:23** Haran, and Canneh, and Eden were your merchants; Sheba, Asshur, and Chilmad were your sellers.

**27:24** They were your merchants in many ways, with wrappings of blue, and of multicolored fabrics, and of precious treasures that were wrapped up and bound tight with cords; they also had cedars in your trade dealings.

**27:25** The ships of the sea were your princes in your trade; and you were filled, and exceedingly glorified in the heart of the sea.

**27:26** Into many waters your rowers have brought you; the south wind has shattered you in the heart of the sea.

**27:27** Your riches, and your treasures, and your manifold equipment, your sailors and your pilots, who handled your gear and were in charge of your people, your men of war also, who were in you, with all your multitude that is in your midst, will fall in the heart of the sea on the day of your ruin.

**27:28** At the sound of the outcry of your pilots, the fleets will be thrown into turmoil.

**27:29** And all who held the oar will come down from their ships; the sailors and all the pilots of the sea will stand on the land.

**27:30** And they will wail over you with a loud voice, and cry out bitterly, and cast dust upon their heads, and be sprinkled with ashes.

**27:32** And they will take up a mournful song over you, and lament over you: "Who is like Tyre, who has fallen silent in the midst of the sea?

**27:33** You who, at the going forth of your wares from the sea, filled many peoples; with the abundance of your riches and of your peoples, you enriched the kings of the earth.

**27:34** Now you are shattered by the sea; in the depths of the waters your wealth and all your multitude that was in your midst have fallen.

**27:35** All the inhabitants of the islands have been appalled over you, and all their kings, struck by the tempest, have altered their faces.

**27:36** The merchants of the peoples have hissed over you; you have been brought to nothing, and you will not be forever."'"

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**27:31** *(after `27:30`)* And they will shave themselves bald over you, and wrap themselves in sackcloth; and they will weep for you in bitterness of soul, with the most bitter weeping.

## Translator Notes

- **27:6** — Vulgate "from Indian ivory, and little cabins from the islands of Italy" (ex ebore indico, et prætoriola de insulis Italiæ) is a distinctly Latin rendering where the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to boxwood or cypress decks from the coastlands of Kittim (Cyprus).
- **27:11** — Vulgate "Pygmies" (Pigmæi) translates Hebrew Gammadim by linking it to a root meaning "cubit," and "quivers" (pharetras) corresponds to Hebrew "shields."
- **27:12** — Vulgate identifies Hebrew Tarshish as "the Carthaginians" (Carthaginenses), just as in 27:10 it renders Lud and Put as "Lydians" and "Libyans."
- **27:16** — Vulgate retains "chodchod" directly from the obscure Hebrew term kadkod, commonly understood as a precious gem such as agate or ruby.
- **27:19** — Vulgate "Mosel" treats the Hebrew preposition and place name me-Uzal ("from Uzal") as a single proper name; similarly, 27:26 has "south wind" (ventus auster) for Hebrew "east wind," and 27:28 reads "fleets" (classes) for Hebrew "coastlands/pasturelands."
