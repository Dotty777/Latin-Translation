## Text

**38:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**38:2** "Son of man, set your face against Gog, the land of Magog, the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal, and prophesy concerning him.

**38:3** And you will say to him, 'This is what the Lord God says: Look, I am against you, Gog, the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal.

**38:4** And I will turn you around, and I will put a bridle in your jaws; and I will lead you out, and all your army, horses and horsemen all clothed in coats of mail, a great multitude of those grasping spear and shield and sword.

**38:5** Persians, Ethiopians, and Libyans with them, all bearing shields and helmets.

**38:6** Gomer and all its troops, the house of Togarmah, the sides of the north, and all its strength, and many peoples with you.

**38:7** Prepare and equip yourself, and all your multitude that has been gathered together to you, and be a command to them.

**38:8** After many days you will be visited; in the last of the years you will come to the land that has returned from the sword and has been gathered from many peoples to the mountains of Israel, which were continually desolate; it has been led out from the peoples, and all of them will live in it confidently.

**38:9** And ascending like a storm you will come, and like a cloud to cover the land, you and all your troops, and many peoples with you.

**38:10** This is what the Lord God says: On that day, words will rise up upon your heart, and you will think a most wicked thought,

**38:11** and you will say, "I will go up against a land without a wall; I will come to those who are at rest and living securely; all of these live without a wall; they have no bars and gates,"

**38:12** so that you may plunder spoils and seize booty, so that you may bring your hand upon those who had been deserted and afterward restored, and upon a people that has been gathered from the nations, that has begun to take possession and to be an inhabitant of the navel of the earth.

**38:13** Sheba, and Dedan, and the merchants of Tarshish, and all its lions will say to you, "Are you coming to take spoils? Look, you have gathered your multitude to plunder booty, to carry off silver and gold, and to take away furnishings and property, and to plunder boundless war spoils."'

**38:14** Therefore prophesy, son of man, and you will say to Gog, 'This is what the Lord God says: On that day, when My people Israel live confidently, will you not know?

**38:15** And you will come from your place, from the sides of the north, you and many peoples with you, all of them riders of horses, a great assembly and a mighty army.

**38:16** And you will go up against My people Israel like a cloud to cover the land. In the last days you will be there, and I will bring you upon My land, so that the nations may know Me when I have been sanctified in you in their eyes, Gog!

**38:17** This is what the Lord God says: You, then, are the one of whom I spoke in ancient days by the hand of My servants the prophets of Israel, who prophesied in the days of those times that I would bring you upon them.

**38:18** And it will be on that day, on the day of the coming of Gog upon the land of Israel, says the Lord God: My indignation will rise up in My fury.

**38:19** And in My zeal, in the fire of My wrath, I have spoken that on that day there will be a great shaking upon the land of Israel;

**38:20** and before My face the fish of the sea will be shaken, and the birds of the sky, and the beasts of the field, and every creeping thing that moves upon the ground, and all people who are upon the face of the earth; and the mountains will be overturned, and the fences will fall, and every wall will collapse to the ground.

**38:21** And I will summon a sword against him on all My mountains, says the Lord God; the sword of each one will be directed against his brother.

**38:22** And I will judge him with pestilence and blood, and with violent rain and immense stones; I will rain fire and sulfur upon him, and upon his army, and upon the many peoples who are with him.

**38:23** And I will be magnified, and I will be sanctified, and I will be known in the eyes of many nations; and they will know that I am the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **38:2** — Latin 'principem capitis' literally reads "prince of the head," rendering Hebrew 'rosh' as a common noun ("chief") rather than the proper name "Rosh" found in the Septuagint; 'terram Magog' ("the land of Magog") stands in direct apposition to Gog.
- **38:4** — Vulgate 'frenum' ("a bridle") and 'hastam' ("spear") differ from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which has "hooks" and "buckler" (large shield); similarly in 38:5 the Vulgate uses the ethnonyms 'Persae, Aethiopes, et Libyes' ("Persians, Ethiopians, and Libyans") for Hebrew "Persia, Cush, and Put."
- **38:7** — Vulgate 'esto eis in praeceptum' literally reads "be to them for a command," rendering Hebrew 'lemishmar' ("for a guard/watch") in the sense of giving orders.
- **38:16** — Vulgate 'eris' ("you will be") takes the ambiguous Hebrew form 'tihyeh' as second-person singular addressed to Gog rather than third-person feminine ("it will come to pass").
- **38:17** — Unlike the Hebrew text, which frames this as a question ("Are you the one...?"), the Vulgate reads a declarative statement ('Tu ergo ille es', "You, then, are the one...") without an interrogative particle.
