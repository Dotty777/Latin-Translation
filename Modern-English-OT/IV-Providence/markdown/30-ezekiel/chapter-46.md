## Text

**46:1** This is what the Lord God says: "The gate of the inner court that faces toward the east shall be closed on the six days on which work is done; but on the Sabbath day it shall be opened, and also on the day of the new moon it shall be opened.

**46:2** And the prince shall enter by way of the vestibule of the gate from the outside, and shall stand at the threshold of the gate; and the priests shall prepare his burnt offering and his peace offerings; and he shall worship upon the threshold of the gate, and go out; but the gate shall not be closed until evening.

**46:3** And the people of the land shall worship at the entrance of that gate on the Sabbaths and at the new moons before the Lord.

**46:4** And this is the burnt offering that the prince shall offer to the Lord: on the Sabbath day, six unblemished lambs and an unblemished ram,

**46:5** and a sacrifice of an ephah for the ram, and for the lambs a sacrifice of what his hand gives, and a hin of oil for each ephah.

**46:6** And on the day of the new moon, an unblemished young bull from the herd, and six lambs and rams shall be without blemish.

**46:7** And he shall prepare a sacrifice of an ephah for the young bull, and also an ephah for the ram; and for the lambs, just as his hand finds, and a hin of oil for each ephah.

**46:8** And when the prince is about to enter, let him enter by way of the vestibule of the gate, and by the same way let him go out.

**46:9** And when the people of the land enter in the sight of the Lord at the feasts, the one who enters through the north gate to worship, let him go out by way of the south gate; and the one who enters by way of the south gate, let him go out by way of the north gate. He shall not return by way of the gate through which he entered, but he shall go out directly opposite it.

**46:10** And the prince in the midst of them shall enter with those entering, and shall go out with those going out.

**46:11** And at the fairs and at the feasts, the sacrifice shall be an ephah for each young bull, and an ephah for each ram; and for the lambs the sacrifice shall be just as his hand finds, and a hin of oil for each ephah.

**46:12** And when the prince prepares a freewill burnt offering, or voluntary peace offerings to the Lord, the gate that faces toward the east shall be opened for him, and he shall prepare his burnt offering and his peace offerings, just as is customarily done on the Sabbath day; and he shall go out, and the gate shall be closed after he has gone out.

**46:13** And an unblemished lamb of the same year he shall prepare as a burnt offering daily to the Lord; always in the morning he shall prepare it.

**46:14** And he shall prepare a sacrifice upon it morning by morning, a sixth part of an ephah, and of oil a third part of a hin, to be mixed with the fine flour: a lawful sacrifice to the Lord, continual and perpetual.

**46:15** He shall prepare the lamb, and the sacrifice, and the oil morning by morning, an everlasting burnt offering."

**46:16** This is what the Lord God says: "If the prince gives a gift to any one of his sons, his inheritance shall belong to his sons; they shall possess it by inheritance.

**46:17** But if he gives a bequest from his inheritance to one of his servants, it shall be his until the year of release, and it shall return to the prince; but his inheritance shall belong to his sons.

**46:18** And the prince shall not take from the inheritance of the people by violence, nor from their possession; but from his own possession he shall give an inheritance to his sons, so that My people may not be scattered, each one from his own possession."

**46:19** And he brought me in through the entrance that was at the side of the gate, into the treasury chambers of the sanctuary for the priests, which faced toward the north; and there was a place there lying toward the west.

**46:20** And he said to me, "This is the place where the priests shall cook what is for sin and for guilt, where they shall cook the sacrifice, so that they may not carry it out into the outer court, and the people be sanctified."

**46:21** And he led me out into the outer court, and led me around by the four corners of the court; and look, there was a small court in a corner of the court, one small court in each of the corners of the court.

**46:22** In the four corners of the court were small courts laid out, forty cubits in length and thirty in width; the four were of one measure.

**46:23** And there was a wall all around enclosing the four small courts; and kitchens were constructed beneath the porticoes around the perimeter.

**46:24** And he said to me, "This is the house of the kitchens, in which the ministers of the house of the Lord shall cook the sacrifices of the people."

## Translator Notes

- **46:6** — Clementine Vulgate reads plural arietes ("rams"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the singular ("a ram"), which the Vulgate itself reflects in v. 7 (per arietem, "for the ram").
- **46:11** — Vulgate translates the Hebrew pilgrim festivals with Latin nundinis ("fairs" or public market-festivals), distinguishing them from the adjacent solemnitatibus ("feasts").
- **46:14** — Vulgate uses the unusual Graeco-Latin distributive idiom cata mane mane ("morning by morning") here and in v. 15, borrowing Greek kata to mirror the Hebrew repetition; vv. 13-15 also use the third-person singular ("he shall prepare") rather than the Hebrew's second-person singular (vv. 13-14) and third-person plural (v. 15).
- **46:20** — Vulgate uses coquent ("cook") for both verbs, where the Hebrew distinguishes boiling the sin and guilt offerings from baking the grain sacrifice.
