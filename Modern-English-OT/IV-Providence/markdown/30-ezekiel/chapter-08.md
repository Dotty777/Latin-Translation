## Text

**8:1** And it happened in the sixth year, in the sixth month, on the fifth of the month, I was sitting in my house, and the elders of Judah were sitting before me, and the hand of the Lord God fell upon me there.

**8:2** And I saw, and look, a likeness like the appearance of fire: from the appearance of His waist downward, fire; and from His waist upward, like the appearance of brightness, as the sight of electrum.

**8:3** And the likeness of a hand was stretched out and seized me by a lock of my head, and the Spirit lifted me up between earth and heaven and brought me into Jerusalem, in a vision of God, beside the inner entrance that faced toward the north, where the idol of jealousy was set up to provoke jealousy.

**8:4** And look, there was the glory of the God of Israel, according to the vision that I had seen in the plain.

**8:5** And He said to me, "Son of man, lift up your eyes toward the way of the north." And I lifted up my eyes toward the way of the north, and look, to the north of the gate of the altar was the idol of jealousy in the very entryway.

**8:6** And He said to me, "Son of man, do you think you see what these people are doing, the great abominations that the house of Israel is doing here, so that I should withdraw far from My sanctuary? And turning yet again, you will see greater abominations."

**8:7** And He brought me in to the entrance of the court, and I saw, and look, one hole in the wall.

**8:8** And He said to me, "Son of man, dig through the wall." And when I had dug through the wall, one doorway appeared.

**8:9** And He said to me, "Go in, and see the worst abominations that these people are doing here."

**8:10** And having gone in, I saw, and look, every likeness of creeping things and animals, an abomination, and all the idols of the house of Israel were painted on the wall all around throughout;

**8:11** and seventy men of the elders of the house of Israel; and Jaazaniah son of Shaphan was standing in the midst of them as they stood before the paintings; and each one had a censer in his hand, and a vapor of mist was rising from the incense.

**8:12** And He said to me, "Surely you see, son of man, what the elders of the house of Israel are doing in the dark, each one in the hidden place of his chamber; for they say, 'The Lord does not see us; the Lord has abandoned the land.'"

**8:13** And He said to me, "Turning yet again, you will see greater abominations that these people are doing."

**8:14** And He brought me in through the entrance of the gate of the house of the Lord that faced toward the north, and look, there women were sitting, mourning Adonis.

**8:15** And He said to me, "Surely you have seen, son of man; turning yet again, you will see greater abominations than these."

**8:16** And He brought me into the inner court of the house of the Lord, and look, at the entrance of the temple of the Lord, between the vestibule and the altar, were about twenty-five men, having their backs toward the temple of the Lord and their faces toward the east; and they were worshiping toward the rising of the sun.

**8:17** And He said to me, "Surely you have seen, son of man. Is this a light thing to the house of Judah, that they should do these abominations which they have done here, because, filling the land with iniquity, they have turned again to provoke Me? And look, they hold the branch to their noses.

**8:18** Therefore I also will act in fury: My eye will not spare, nor will I have pity; and when they cry out to My ears with a loud voice, I will not listen to them."

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — Latin aspectus ignis ("appearance of fire") follows the Hebrew consonantal reading "fire" rather than "man" (as in the Septuagint); electri ("electrum") denotes a lustrous gold-silver alloy (or amber), rendering Hebrew chashmal.
- **8:10** — Latin depicta erant ("were painted," echoed by picturas "paintings" in verse 11) renders a Hebrew participle meaning "carved" or "engraved," and abominatio stands as a singular noun in apposition rather than an adjective modifying "animals."
- **8:12** — Latin in abscondito cubiculi sui ("in the hidden place of his chamber") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "in the rooms of his imagery," and here (as in verses 15 and 17) the Vulgate renders the opening clause as an emphatic statement (Certe, "Surely") rather than a question.
- **8:14** — Latin Adonidem ("Adonis") is a distinctive Vulgate rendering in which Jerome identifies the Mesopotamian fertility deity Tammuz with his classical Greek counterpart Adonis.
