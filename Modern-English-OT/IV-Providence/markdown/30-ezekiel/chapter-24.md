## Text

**24:1** And the word of the Lord came to me in the ninth year, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, saying,

**24:2** "Son of man, write for yourself the name of this day, on which the king of Babylon has firmly established himself against Jerusalem today.

**24:3** And you shall speak a parable through a proverb to the provoking house, and you shall say to them, 'This is what the Lord God says: Set on the pot; set it on, I say, and pour water into it.

**24:4** Gather its pieces into it, every good part, the thigh and the shoulder, choice and full of bones.

**24:5** Take the fattest of the flock, and also stack piles of bones beneath it: its boiling has boiled up, and its bones have been boiled thoroughly in its midst.

**24:6** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: Woe to the city of blood, to the pot whose rust is in it, and its rust has not gone out of it! By parts and by its own parts, cast it out; no lot has fallen upon it.

**24:7** For her blood is in her midst; she poured it out upon the most gleaming rock; she did not pour it out upon the ground, so that it could be covered with dust.

**24:8** So that I might bring up My indignation and exact retribution with vengeance, I set her blood upon the most gleaming rock, so that it would not be covered.

**24:9** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: Woe to the city of blood, whose pyre I will make great!

**24:10** Heap up the bones, which I will kindle with fire; the flesh will be consumed, and the entire mixture will be cooked, and the bones will waste away.

**24:11** Also set it empty upon the burning coals, so that it may grow hot and its bronze may melt, and its defilement may be melted down in its midst, and its rust may be consumed.

**24:12** With much labor there has been sweating, and its excessive rust has not gone out of it, not even by fire.

**24:13** Your uncleanness is detestable, because I wanted to cleanse you, and you were not cleansed from your filth; nor will you be cleansed beforehand, until I make My indignation rest upon you.

**24:14** I, the Lord, have spoken: it will come, and I will act; I will not pass by, nor will I spare, nor will I be appeased; according to your ways and according to your devices I will judge you, says the Lord.'"

**24:15** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**24:16** "Son of man, look, I am taking from you the delight of your eyes by a stroke; and you shall not mourn, nor shall you weep, nor shall your tears flow.

**24:17** Groan in silence; you shall make no mourning for the dead; let your crown be bound around you, and your sandals shall be on your feet; you shall not veil your face with a covering, nor shall you eat the food of mourners."

**24:18** So I spoke to the people in the morning, and my wife died in the evening; and in the morning I did just as He had commanded me.

**24:19** And the people said to me, "Why do you not tell us what these things mean that you are doing?"

**24:20** And I said to them, "The word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**24:21** 'Speak to the house of Israel, "This is what the Lord God says: Look, I will defile My sanctuary, the pride of your dominion, and the delight of your eyes, and that over which your soul is in dread; your sons and your daughters whom you left behind will fall by the sword.

**24:22** And you will do just as I have done: you will not veil your faces with a covering, and you will not eat the food of mourners;

**24:23** you will have crowns on your heads and sandals on your feet; you will not mourn nor weep, but you will waste away in your iniquities, and each one will groan to his brother.

**24:24** And Ezekiel will be a portent to you; according to all that he has done, you will do when this comes; and you will know that I am the Lord God."

**24:25** And you, son of man, look, on the day when I take from them their strength, and the joy of dignity, and the desire of their eyes, upon which their souls rest, their sons and their daughters,

**24:26** on that day, when one who flees comes to you to report to you,

**24:27** on that day, I say, your mouth will be opened with the one who has fled, and you will speak, and you will be silent no longer; and you will be a portent to them, and you will know that I am the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **24:5** — Latin strues ossium ("piles of bones", echoed by ossa in v. 10) follows the Hebrew "bones" literally, as bones used for fuel. The Vulgate also renders the second half of the verse with perfect indicatives ("has boiled up... have been boiled thoroughly") rather than imperatives.
- **24:7** — Latin limpidissimam petram (lit. "most clear/gleaming rock", recurring in 24:8 and 26:4, 14) renders Hebrew tzechich sela ("bare, sun-bleached rock"), emphasizing a smooth surface completely stripped of dust.
- **24:17** — Latin corona ("crown", also in v. 23) translates Hebrew pe'er ("turban/headdress"), and cibos lugentium ("the food of mourners", also in v. 22) explicitly interprets Hebrew "bread of men."
- **24:21** — Vulgate super quo pavet anima vestra ("that over which your soul is in dread") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "the compassion/longing of your soul"; contrast v. 25, where the Vulgate has requiescunt ("rest").
- **24:27** — Vulgate et scietis is second-person plural ("and you [pl.] will know"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads third-person plural ("and they will know").
