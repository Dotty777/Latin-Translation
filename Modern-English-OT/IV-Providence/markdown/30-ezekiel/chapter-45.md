## Text

**45:1** "And when you begin to divide the land by lot, set apart firstfruits to the Lord, a sanctified portion of the land, twenty-five thousand in length and ten thousand in width; it shall be sanctified throughout all its border all around.

**45:2** And on every side there shall be a sanctified portion of five hundred by five hundred, square all around, and fifty cubits for its outskirts around its perimeter.

**45:3** And from this measurement you shall measure a length of twenty-five thousand and a width of ten thousand; and in it shall be the temple, and the Holy of Holies.

**45:4** The sanctified portion of the land shall be for the priests, the ministers of the sanctuary, who draw near to the ministry of the Lord; and it shall be a place for them for houses, and for a sanctuary of holiness.

**45:5** And twenty-five thousand in length and ten thousand in width shall be for the Levites who minister to the house; they themselves shall possess twenty treasury chambers.

**45:6** And you shall give as the possession of the city five thousand in width and twenty-five thousand in length, corresponding to the separated portion of the sanctuary, for the whole house of Israel.

**45:7** And for the prince also, on this side and on that side, alongside the separated portion of the sanctuary and alongside the possession of the city, opposite the face of the separated portion of the sanctuary and opposite the face of the possession of the city, from the sea side to the sea, and from the east side to the east; and in length corresponding to each portion, from the western border to the eastern border.

**45:8** From the land there shall be a possession for him in Israel, and the princes shall no longer ravage My people; but they shall give the land to the house of Israel according to their tribes."

**45:9** This is what the Lord God says: "Let it be enough for you, princes of Israel! Cease iniquity and robbery, and do judgment and justice; separate your borders from My people," says the Lord God.

**45:10** "You shall have a just balance, and a just ephah, and a just bath.

**45:11** The ephah and the bath shall be equal and of one measure, so that the bath holds a tenth part of a cor, and the ephah a tenth part of a cor; according to the measure of the cor shall be their equal standard.

**45:12** And the shekel has twenty obols; moreover, twenty shekels, and twenty-five shekels, and fifteen shekels make a mina.

**45:13** And these are the firstfruits that you shall take: a sixth part of an ephah from a cor of wheat, and a sixth part of an ephah from a cor of barley.

**45:14** And the measure of oil, the bath of oil, is a tenth part of a cor; and ten baths make a cor, because ten baths fill a cor.

**45:15** And one ram from a flock of two hundred, from the things that nourish Israel, for a sacrifice, and for a burnt offering, and for peace offerings, to make atonement for them," says the Lord God.

**45:16** "All the people of the land shall be obligated for these firstfruits to the prince in Israel.

**45:17** And upon the prince shall be the burnt offerings, and the sacrifice, and the drink offerings, at the feasts, and at the new moons, and on the Sabbaths, and at all the feasts of the house of Israel; he himself shall prepare the sacrifice for sin, and the burnt offering, and the peace offerings, to make atonement for the house of Israel."

**45:18** This is what the Lord God says: "In the first month, on the first day of the month, you shall take an unblemished young bull from the herd, and you shall make atonement for the sanctuary.

**45:19** And the priest shall take some of the blood that is for sin, and put it on the posts of the house, and on the four corners of the ledge of the altar, and on the posts of the gate of the inner court.

**45:20** And so you shall do on the seventh day of the month, for everyone who has been ignorant and has been deceived by error; and you shall make atonement for the house.

**45:21** In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, you shall have the feast of the Passover; for seven days unleavened bread shall be eaten.

**45:22** And on that day the prince shall prepare, for himself and for all the people of the land, a young bull for sin.

**45:23** And during the feast of seven days he shall prepare a burnt offering to the Lord, seven young bulls and seven rams without blemish, daily for seven days; and for sin, a male of the goats daily.

**45:24** And he shall prepare a sacrifice of an ephah for each young bull, and an ephah for each ram, and a hin of oil for each ephah.

**45:25** In the seventh month, on the fifteenth day of the month, at the feast, he shall do just as has been stated above for seven days, both for sin and for the burnt offering, and in the sacrifice, and in the oil."

## Translator Notes

- **45:1** — Vulgate renders Hebrew terumah ("offering" or "allotment") as primitias ("firstfruits") here and in vv. 13 and 16, and follows the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "ten thousand" (without specifying cubits or reeds), whereas the Septuagint reads "twenty thousand."
- **45:5** — Vulgate viginti gazophylacia ("twenty treasury chambers") reflects the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("twenty chambers"), whereas the Septuagint reads "cities to dwell in."
- **45:9** — Vulgate separate confinia vestra a populo meo ("separate your borders from My people") translates the Hebrew noun as territorial boundaries rather than "evictions" or "exactions."
- **45:11** — Vulgate uses the measure cor (corus) here and in vv. 13-14 where the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses homer (both equal ten baths or ten ephahs); in v. 12, Vulgate similarly renders Hebrew gerahs with the Greek-derived weight obols (obolos).
- **45:15** — Vulgate de his quæ nutriunt Israël ("from the things that nourish Israel") translates the Hebrew expression for the "well-watered pastures of Israel" as what sustains the nation, and specifies a "ram" (arietem) rather than a generic sheep.
