## Text

**14:1** And men from the elders of Israel came to me and sat down before me.

**14:2** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**14:3** "Son of man, these men have placed their impurities in their hearts and have set up the stumbling block of their iniquity in front of their face. When inquired of, will I really answer them?

**14:4** For this reason, speak to them and you will say to them, 'This is what the Lord God says: Each and every man from the house of Israel who places his impurities in his heart, and sets up the stumbling block of his iniquity in front of his face, and comes to a prophet inquiring of Me through him—I, the Lord, will answer him in the multitude of his impurities,

**14:5** so that the house of Israel may be captured in its heart, by which they have departed from Me in all their idols.'

**14:6** Therefore say to the house of Israel, 'This is what the Lord God says: Turn back and depart from your idols, and turn your faces away from all your defilements.

**14:7** For each and every man from the house of Israel, and from the proselytes, whoever is a resident alien in Israel, if he becomes estranged from Me, and places his idols in his heart, and sets up the stumbling block of his iniquity in front of his face, and comes to a prophet to inquire of Me through him—I, the Lord, will answer him by Myself;

**14:8** and I will set My face against that man, and I will make him into an example and a proverb, and I will destroy him from the midst of My people; and you will know that I am the Lord.

**14:9** And when a prophet goes astray and speaks a word, I, the Lord, have deceived that prophet, and I will stretch out My hand against him and blot him out from the midst of My people Israel.

**14:10** And they will bear their iniquity; according to the iniquity of the inquirer, so will the iniquity of the prophet be,

**14:11** so that the house of Israel may no longer go astray from Me, nor be polluted in all its transgressions, but that they may be a people for Me, and I may be God for them, says the Lord of hosts.'"

**14:12** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**14:13** "Son of man, when a land sins against Me so that it truly transgresses, I will stretch out My hand against it, and I will break its staff of bread, and I will send famine into it, and I will kill man and beast from it.

**14:14** And if these three men are in its midst—Noah, Daniel, and Job—they themselves by their righteousness will deliver their own lives, says the Lord of hosts.

**14:15** Now if I also bring the worst beasts upon the land to lay it waste, and it becomes impassable because there is no one passing through on account of the beasts,

**14:16** if these three men are in it, as I live, says the Lord God, indeed they will deliver neither sons nor daughters, but they alone will be delivered, while the land will be made desolate.

**14:17** Or if I bring a sword upon that land, and say to the sword, 'Pass through the land,' and I kill man and beast from it,

**14:18** and these three men are in its midst: as I live, says the Lord God, they will not deliver sons or daughters, but they alone will be delivered.

**14:19** But if I also send pestilence upon that land, and pour out My indignation upon it in blood, to remove man and beast from it,

**14:20** and Noah, and Daniel, and Job are in its midst: as I live, says the Lord God, indeed they will not deliver a son or a daughter, but they themselves by their righteousness will deliver their own lives.

**14:21** For this is what the Lord God says: Even if I send My four worst judgments—sword, and famine, and evil beasts, and pestilence—into Jerusalem to kill man and livestock from it,

**14:22** yet there will be left in it a deliverance of those leading out sons and daughters; look, they themselves will come in to you, and you will see their way and their contrivances, and you will be consoled over the calamity that I have brought upon Jerusalem, in all things that I have brought in upon it.

**14:23** And they will console you when you see their way and their contrivances; and you will recognize that I have not done without reason everything that I have done in it, says the Lord God."

## Translator Notes

- **14:4** — Latin "Homo, homo" (literally "Man, man") reproduces a Hebrew distributive idiom meaning "each and every person." Here and in 14:3 the Vulgate renders the Hebrew term for idols as "immunditias" ("impurities") before switching to "idola" ("idols") in 14:5-7, and reads "in multitudine" ("in the multitude") with the Hebrew Ketib.
- **14:11** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "Dominus exercituum" ("the Lord of hosts") here and in 14:14, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the Lord God."
- **14:13** — Latin "ut prævaricetur prævaricans" (literally "so that, transgressing, it transgresses") uses a cognate participle to reflect a Hebrew emphatic construction.
- **14:21** — Unlike the Hebrew ("How much more when..."), the Vulgate frames 14:21-22 as a concessive sentence: "Quod etsi ... tamen" ("Even if ... yet").
- **14:22** — The Vulgate uses the abstract noun "salvatio" ("deliverance/salvation") with an active participle "educentium" ("of those leading out"), whereas the Hebrew describes surviving sons and daughters being brought out; it likewise renders "ingredientur" ("will come in") for their arrival among the exiles.
