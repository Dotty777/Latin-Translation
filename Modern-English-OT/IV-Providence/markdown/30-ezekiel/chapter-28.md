## Text

**28:1** And the message of the Lord came to me, saying:

**28:2** "Son of man, say to the prince of Tyre, 'This is what the Lord God says: Because your heart has been lifted up, and you have said, "I am God, and I have sat in the seat of God in the heart of the sea," although you are a man and not God, and you have set your heart like the heart of God;

**28:3** look, you are wiser than Daniel; no secret is hidden from you;

**28:4** by your wisdom and understanding you have made strength for yourself, and you have acquired gold and silver in your treasuries;

**28:5** by the abundance of your wisdom and by your trade you have multiplied strength for yourself, and your heart has been lifted up in your might.

**28:6** Therefore, this is what the Lord God says: Because your heart has been lifted up like the heart of God,

**28:7** for this reason, look, I will bring foreigners upon you, the mightiest of the nations; and they will bare their swords against the beauty of your wisdom, and they will defile your splendor.

**28:8** They will kill and drag you down, and you will die in the death of those struck down in the heart of the sea.

**28:9** Will you really speak, saying, "I am God," before those who kill you, although you are a man and not God, in the hand of those who strike you down?

**28:10** You will die the death of the uncircumcised by the hand of foreigners, for I have spoken, says the Lord God.'"

**28:11** And the message of the Lord came to me, saying: "Son of man, raise a lamentation over the king of Tyre,

**28:12** and you will say to him, 'This is what the Lord God says: You were the seal of likeness, full of wisdom, and perfect in splendor.

**28:13** You were in the delights of the paradise of God; every precious stone was your covering: sardius, topaz, and jasper; chrysolite, and onyx, and beryl; sapphire, and carbuncle, and emerald; gold was the workmanship of your splendor, and your openings were prepared on the day on which you were created.

**28:14** You were an outstretched and protecting cherub, and I placed you on the holy mountain of God; you walked in the midst of fiery stones,

**28:15** perfect in your ways from the day of your creation, until iniquity was found in you.

**28:16** By the abundance of your trade your inner parts were filled with iniquity, and you sinned; and I cast you out from the mountain of God, and I destroyed you, protecting cherub, from the midst of the fiery stones.

**28:17** And your heart was lifted up in your splendor; you destroyed your wisdom in your splendor; I cast you down to the earth; I set you before the face of kings so that they might gaze upon you.

**28:18** By the abundance of your iniquities and by the iniquity of your trade, you defiled your sanctification; therefore, I will bring forth fire from your midst to consume you, and I will make you into ashes upon the earth in the sight of all who see you.

**28:19** All who see you among the nations will be appalled over you; you have become nothing, and you will not be forever.'"

**28:20** And the message of the Lord came to me, saying:

**28:21** "Son of man, set your face against Sidon, and you will prophesy concerning her,

**28:22** and you will say, 'This is what the Lord God says: Look, I am against you, Sidon, and I will be glorified in your midst; and they will know that I am the Lord when I have executed judgments in her and have been sanctified in her.

**28:23** And I will send pestilence upon her, and blood in her streets; and those who are killed will fall in her midst by the sword all around, and they will know that I am the Lord.

**28:24** And there will no longer be for the house of Israel a stumbling block of bitterness and a thorn bringing pain on every side all around, from those who oppose them; and they will know that I am the Lord God.

**28:25** This is what the Lord God says: When I have gathered the house of Israel from the peoples among whom they have been scattered, I will be sanctified in them before the nations; and they will dwell in their own land, which I gave to My servant Jacob;

**28:26** and they will dwell in it securely, and they will build houses and plant vineyards, and they will dwell confidently, when I have executed judgments upon all who oppose them all around; and they will know that I am the Lord their God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **28:8** — Vulgate "They will kill and drag you down" (Interficient, et detrahent te) renders Hebrew shahat ("the pit") verbally as "they will kill."
- **28:12** — Vulgate "seal of likeness" (signaculum similitudinis) differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("sealer of perfection/proportion"), reflecting a reading connected to "likeness" or "pattern."
- **28:13** — Vulgate renders Hebrew "Eden, the garden of God" as "the delights of the paradise of God" (in deliciis paradisi Dei). "Openings" translates Latin foramina (literally "holes/perforations/sockets"), reflecting the root of Hebrew neqabeka (often taken as pipes or gem settings).
- **28:14** — Vulgate "outstretched" (extentus) derives Hebrew mimshah ("anointed") from a root meaning "to stretch out" or "measure."
- **28:18** — Vulgate has singular "your sanctification" (sanctificationem tuam, the Vulgate's standard rendering of Hebrew miqdash, "sanctuary"), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has plural "your sanctuaries."
