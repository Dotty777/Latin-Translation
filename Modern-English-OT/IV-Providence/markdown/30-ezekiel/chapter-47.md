## Text

**47:1** And he brought me back to the gate of the house, and look, waters were coming out from under the threshold of the house toward the east; for the front of the house faced toward the east, and the waters were descending on the right side of the temple, to the south of the altar.

**47:2** And he led me out by way of the north gate, and brought me around to the way outside the outer gate, the way that faced toward the east; and look, waters were overflowing from the right side.

**47:3** When the man who had a line in his hand went out toward the east, he measured a thousand cubits, and led me through the water up to the ankles.

**47:4** And again he measured a thousand, and led me through the water up to the knees.

**47:5** And he measured a thousand, and led me through the water up to the loins. And he measured a thousand, a torrent that I could not cross, for the waters of the deep torrent had swelled, which cannot be forded.

**47:6** And he said to me, "Surely you have seen, son of man." And he led me out, and brought me back to the bank of the torrent.

**47:7** And when I had turned back, look, on the bank of the torrent were very many trees on both sides.

**47:8** And he said to me, "These waters that go out toward the mounds of eastern sand, and descend to the plains of the desert, shall enter the sea and go out; and the waters shall be healed.

**47:9** And every living creature that creeps, wherever the torrent comes, shall live; and there shall be very many fish, after these waters have come there; and they shall be healed, and all things shall live to which the torrent comes.

**47:10** And fishermen shall stand beside them; from En-gedi to En-eglaim there shall be a drying of dragnets; there shall be very many kinds of its fish, like the fish of the Great Sea, of an exceedingly great multitude.

**47:11** But on its shores and in its marshes, they shall not be healed, because they shall be given over to salt pits.

**47:12** And along the torrent there shall spring up on its banks, on both sides, every fruit-bearing tree; no leaf shall fall from it, and its fruit shall not fail; each month it shall bear firstfruits, because its waters shall come out from the sanctuary; and its fruits shall be for food, and its leaves for medicine."

**47:13** This is what the Lord God says: "This is the border within which you shall possess the land among the twelve tribes of Israel: for Joseph has a double portion.

**47:14** And you shall possess it, each one equally as his brother, concerning which I raised My hand to give it to your fathers; and this land shall fall to you as a possession.

**47:15** And this is the border of the land: on the northern side, from the Great Sea by the way of Hethlon, as one comes to Zedad,

**47:16** Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim, which is between the border of Damascus and the boundary of Hamath, the house of Tichon, which is beside the border of Hauran.

**47:17** And the border shall be from the sea to the court of Enon, the border of Damascus; and from the north to the north, the border of Hamath: the northern side.

**47:18** Moreover, the eastern side is from the midst of Hauran, and from the midst of Damascus, and from the midst of Gilead, and from the midst of the land of Israel, with the Jordan marking the boundary to the eastern sea. You shall also measure the eastern side.

**47:19** And the southern side southward is from Tamar to the waters of contradiction of Kadesh, and the torrent to the Great Sea; and this is the southern side toward the south.

**47:20** And the sea side is the Great Sea, from the boundary straight on, until you come to Hamath; this is the sea side.

**47:21** And you shall divide this land for yourselves by the tribes of Israel;

**47:22** and you shall allot it as an inheritance for yourselves, and for the sojourners who have come to you, who have fathered sons in the midst of you; and they shall be to you like the native-born among the sons of Israel; with you they shall divide a possession in the midst of the tribes of Israel.

**47:23** And in whatever tribe the sojourner is, there you shall give him a possession," says the Lord God.

## Translator Notes

- **47:6** — Vulgate renders the Hebrew question ("Have you seen, son of man?") as an emphatic affirmation, Certe vidisti, fili hominis ("Surely you have seen, son of man.").
- **47:8** — Vulgate translates Hebrew ha-gelilah ha-qadmonah ("the eastern region") as tumulos sabuli orientalis ("mounds of eastern sand"), and renders the phrase after "the sea" as a future verb, et exibunt ("and go out").
- **47:13** — Literally "a double cord/line" (duplicem funiculum), a biblical idiom for a measured allotment; Vulgate specifies "double" where the Hebrew reads simply "portions."
- **47:16** — Vulgate translates the first element of the Hebrew place names Hazer-hatticon here and Hazar-enan in v. 17 as common nouns: domus Tichon ("the house of Tichon") and atrium Enon ("the court of Enon").
- **47:18** — Vulgate Metiemini etiam plagam orientalem ("You shall also measure the eastern side") reflects a verbal reading of the Hebrew consonants normally rendered "this is the eastern side"; in v. 19, Vulgate also translates Hebrew Meriboth literally as aquas contradictionis ("the waters of contradiction").
