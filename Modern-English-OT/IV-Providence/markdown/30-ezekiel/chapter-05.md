## Text

**5:1** "And you, son of man, take for yourself a sharp sword that shaves hairs, and you will take it and draw it over your head and over your beard, and you will take for yourself scales for weighing and divide them.

**5:2** You will burn a third part with fire in the midst of the city at the completion of the days of the siege, and you will take a third part and cut it up with the sword all around it; and another third you will scatter to the wind, and I will bare the sword after them.

**5:3** And you will take from there a small number and bind them in the edge of your cloak;

**5:4** and from them you will take again, and cast them into the midst of the fire, and burn them with fire; and from it fire will go out into all the house of Israel."

**5:5** This is what the Lord God says: "This is Jerusalem: I have placed her in the midst of the nations, and lands all around her.

**5:6** And she has despised My judgments so as to be more wicked than the nations, and My precepts more than the lands that are around her; for they have cast away My judgments, and they have not walked in My precepts."

**5:7** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: "Because you have surpassed the nations that are around you, and have not walked in My precepts, and have not carried out My judgments, and have not acted according to the judgments of the nations that are around you,"

**5:8** therefore this is what the Lord God says: "Look, I am against you, and I Myself will execute judgments in your midst before the eyes of the nations,

**5:9** and I will do in you what I have not done, and the like of which I will not do again, because of all your abominations.

**5:10** Therefore fathers will eat sons in your midst, and sons will eat their fathers; and I will execute judgments in you, and I will winnow all your remnants to every wind.

**5:11** Therefore, as I live," says the Lord God, "surely, because you have violated My sanctuary with all your offenses and with all your abominations, I also will break in pieces, and My eye will not spare, and I will not have mercy.

**5:12** A third part of you will die by plague and be consumed by famine in your midst, and a third part of you will fall by the sword all around you; and your third part I will scatter to every wind, and I will unsheathe the sword after them.

**5:13** And I will complete My fury, and make My indignation rest upon them, and I will be consoled; and they will know that I, the Lord, have spoken in My zeal, when I have fulfilled My indignation upon them.

**5:14** And I will make you a wasteland and a reproach to the nations that are around you, in the sight of everyone passing by;

**5:15** and you will be a reproach and a reviling, an example and an astonishment among the nations that are around you, when I execute judgments in you in fury, and in indignation, and in rebukes of wrath.

**5:16** I, the Lord, have spoken: when I send the worst arrows of famine against them, which will be deadly and which I will send to destroy you, I will also gather famine upon you and break the staff of bread among you;

**5:17** and I will send against you famine and the worst beasts, even to utter destruction; and pestilence and blood will pass through you, and I will bring the sword upon you. I, the Lord, have spoken."

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Vulgate literally reads gladium acutum, radentem pilos ("a sharp sword that shaves hairs"), where the Hebrew has "a sharp sword; as a barber's razor".
- **5:7** — Vulgate reads superastis ("you have surpassed"), interpreting the difficult Hebrew term for "your raging/turbulence" as exceeding the surrounding nations in wickedness.
- **5:11** — Vulgate renders the affirmative Hebrew oath particle literally as nisi ("unless", i.e. "surely") and reads confringam ("break in pieces"), matching a textual variant rather than the Masoretic "withdraw/diminish".
- **5:16** — Vulgate reads congregabo ("gather"), where the Hebrew has "increase/add".
- **5:17** — Vulgate reads usque ad internecionem ("even to utter destruction"), whereas the Hebrew reads "and they will bereave you [of children]".
