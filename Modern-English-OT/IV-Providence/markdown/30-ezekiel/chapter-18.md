## Text

**18:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**18:2** "What is it that among yourselves you turn a parable into this proverb in the land of Israel, saying, 'The fathers ate a sour grape, and the teeth of the children grow numb'?

**18:3** As I live, says the Lord God, this parable will no longer be a proverb for you in Israel.

**18:4** Look, all souls are Mine: as the soul of the father, so also the soul of the son is Mine; the soul that sins, that very one will die.

**18:5** And if a man is righteous, and does judgment and righteousness,

**18:6** does not eat on the mountains, and does not lift up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, and does not violate his neighbor's wife, and does not approach a menstruating woman,

**18:7** and does not distress a person, returns the pledge to the debtor, seizes nothing by violence, gives his bread to the hungry, and covers the naked with a garment,

**18:8** does not lend at interest and does not take more, turns his hand away from iniquity, and renders true judgment between man and man,

**18:9** walks in My precepts, and keeps My judgments so as to practice truth: this man is righteous; he will certainly live, says the Lord God.

**18:10** But if he fathers a son who is a robber, shedding blood, and who does one of these things,

**18:11** and indeed not doing all these things, but eating on the mountains, and defiling his neighbor's wife,

**18:12** distressing the needy and the poor, seizing plunder, not returning a pledge, and lifting up his eyes to idols, committing abomination,

**18:13** giving at interest and taking more: will he live? He will not live. Since he has done all these detestable things, he will certainly die; his blood will be upon himself.

**18:14** Now if he fathers a son who, seeing all the sins of his father that he committed, fears and does not do anything like them,

**18:15** does not eat upon the mountains, and does not lift up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, and does not violate his neighbor's wife,

**18:16** and does not distress a man, does not withhold a pledge, and does not seize plunder, gives his bread to the hungry, and covers the naked with a garment,

**18:17** turns his hand away from injury to the poor, does not take interest and excess, executes My judgments, walks in My precepts: this man will not die in the iniquity of his father, but will certainly live.

**18:18** As for his father, because he committed extortion, and did violence to his brother, and practiced evil in the midst of his people, look, he has died in his iniquity.

**18:19** And you say, 'Why has the son not borne the iniquity of the father?' Clearly because the son has practiced judgment and righteousness, has kept all My precepts, and has done them, he will certainly live.

**18:20** The soul that sins, that very one will die: the son will not bear the iniquity of the father, and the father will not bear the iniquity of the son; the righteousness of the righteous man will be upon him, and the wickedness of the wicked man will be upon him.

**18:21** But if the wicked man repents from all his sins that he has practiced, and keeps all My precepts, and does judgment and righteousness, he will certainly live, and he will not die.

**18:22** None of all his iniquities that he has practiced will I remember; in his righteousness that he has practiced, he will live.

**18:23** Is the death of the wicked man My will, says the Lord God, and not that he should turn back from his ways and live?

**18:24** But if the righteous man turns away from his righteousness, and commits iniquity according to all the abominations that the wicked man is accustomed to practice, will he live? None of all his righteous deeds that he had done will be remembered; in the transgression by which he has transgressed, and in his sin that he has sinned, in them he will die.

**18:25** And you have said, 'The way of the Lord is not fair!' Hear therefore, house of Israel: is My way not fair, and are your ways not rather crooked?

**18:26** For when a righteous man turns away from his righteousness and commits iniquity, he will die in them; in the unrighteousness that he has practiced, he will die.

**18:27** And when a wicked man turns away from his wickedness that he has practiced, and does judgment and righteousness, he himself will keep his soul alive;

**18:28** for, considering and turning away from all his iniquities that he has practiced, he will certainly live, and he will not die.

**18:29** And the sons of Israel say, 'The way of the Lord is not fair!' Are My ways not fair, house of Israel, and are your ways not rather crooked?

**18:30** Therefore I will judge each one according to his ways, house of Israel, says the Lord God. Turn back and repent from all your iniquities, and iniquity will not become a ruin for you.

**18:31** Cast away from yourselves all your transgressions in which you have transgressed, and make for yourselves a new heart and a new spirit; and why will you die, house of Israel?

**18:32** For I do not desire the death of the one who dies, says the Lord God: return and live."

## Translator Notes

- **18:2** — Vulgate "parabolam vertitis in proverbium istud" ("you turn a parable into this proverb") distinguishes two nouns where the Hebrew uses a cognate phrase ("using this proverb").
- **18:3** — Vulgate "si erit ultra" preserves the Semitic oath construction in which "if" introduces an emphatic negation ("will no longer be").
- **18:14** — Vulgate "timuerit" ("fears") reflects a Hebrew reading "and fears" rather than the Masoretic Text vocalization "and sees."
- **18:21** — Vulgate "egerit poenitentiam" (and "agite poenitentiam" in 18:30) renders the Hebrew verb "turn" explicitly with the Latin idiom for repentance ("repent").
- **18:22** — Vulgate "non recordabor" ("I will not remember") is first-person active, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has a passive ("will not be remembered," as in Vulgate 18:24).
