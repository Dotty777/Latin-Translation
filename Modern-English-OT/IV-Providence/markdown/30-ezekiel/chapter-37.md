## Text

**37:1** The hand of the Lord came upon me, and He brought me out in the Spirit of the Lord, and He set me down in the middle of a plain that was full of bones.

**37:2** And He led me around through them in a circle; and they were very many on the surface of the plain, and exceedingly dry.

**37:3** And He said to me, "Son of man, do you think these bones will live?" And I said, "Lord God, You know."

**37:4** And He said to me, "Prophesy concerning these bones, and you will say to them, 'Dry bones, hear the word of the Lord.

**37:5** This is what the Lord God says to these bones: Look, I will send spirit into you, and you will live.

**37:6** And I will put sinews upon you, and I will cause flesh to grow up upon you, and I will stretch skin over you, and I will give you spirit, and you will live; and you will know that I am the Lord.'"

**37:7** And I prophesied just as He had commanded me; and there was a sound as I was prophesying, and look, a shaking; and bones drew near to bones, each one to its own joint.

**37:8** And I saw, and look, sinews and flesh rose up upon them, and skin was stretched over them from above; and they had no spirit.

**37:9** And He said to me, "Prophesy to the spirit; prophesy, son of man, and you will say to the spirit, 'This is what the Lord God says: Come from the four winds, spirit, and breathe upon these who were killed, and let them live again.'"

**37:10** And I prophesied just as He had commanded me; and the spirit entered into them, and they lived, and they stood upon their feet, a very, exceedingly great army.

**37:11** And He said to me, "Son of man, all these bones are the house of Israel. They themselves say, 'Our bones have dried up, and our hope has perished, and we have been cut off.'

**37:12** Therefore prophesy, and you will say to them, 'This is what the Lord God says: Look, I will open your graves and bring you out of your tombs, My people, and I will bring you into the land of Israel.

**37:13** And you will know that I am the Lord, when I open your tombs and bring you out of your graves, My people,

**37:14** and put My Spirit in you, and you live; and I will cause you to rest on your own soil, and you will know that I, the Lord, have spoken and have done it, says the Lord God.'"

**37:15** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**37:16** "And you, son of man, take for yourself one piece of wood, and write on it, 'Of Judah, and of the sons of Israel, his companions'; and take another piece of wood, and write on it, 'For Joseph, the wood of Ephraim, and for all the house of Israel and of his companions.'

**37:17** And join them one to the other for yourself into one piece of wood, and they will be in union in your hand.

**37:18** And when the sons of your people speak to you, saying, 'Will you not tell us what you mean by these?'

**37:19** you will speak to them, 'This is what the Lord God says: Look, I will take the wood of Joseph, which is in the hand of Ephraim, and the tribes of Israel that are joined to him, and I will put them together with the wood of Judah, and I will make them into one piece of wood, and they will be one in his hand.'

**37:20** And the pieces of wood on which you have written will be in your hand before their eyes.

**37:21** And you will say to them, 'This is what the Lord God says: Look, I will take the sons of Israel from the midst of the nations to which they went away, and I will gather them from every side, and I will bring them to their own soil.

**37:22** And I will make them into one nation in the land, on the mountains of Israel, and one king will be ruling over them all; and they will no longer be two nations, nor will they be divided any longer into two kingdoms,

**37:23** nor will they be defiled any longer with their idols, and their abominations, and all their iniquities; and I will save them from all the dwelling places in which they have sinned, and I will cleanse them; and they will be My people, and I will be their God.

**37:24** And My servant David will be king over them, and there will be one shepherd of them all. They will walk in My judgments, and they will keep My commands and do them;

**37:25** and they will live on the land that I gave to My servant Jacob, in which your fathers lived; and they will live on it, they themselves, and their sons, and the sons of their sons, for all eternity; and David, My servant, will be their prince forever.

**37:26** And I will strike a covenant of peace with them; it will be an everlasting covenant for them. And I will establish them and multiply them, and I will place My sanctification in their midst forever.

**37:27** And My tabernacle will be among them; and I will be their God, and they themselves will be My people.

**37:28** And the nations will know that I am the Lord, the Sanctifier of Israel, when My sanctification is in their midst forever.'"

## Translator Notes

- **37:1** — Latin campi ("plain") renders Hebrew biq'ah (a broad valley or plain) as an open plain (also in v. 2).
- **37:5** — Latin spiritus conveys both "breath" and "spirit" throughout vv. 5-10, directly linking the animating breath of the bones with spiritum meum ("My Spirit") in v. 14, while Latin ventis ("winds") is used separately in v. 9.
- **37:16** — Latin lignum ("piece of wood" or "wood") is used throughout vv. 16-20 for Hebrew etz ("wood" or "stick"). The Vulgate inscriptions shift from genitive ("Of Judah, and of the sons of Israel") on the first wood to dative ("For Joseph, the wood of Ephraim, and for all the house of Israel") on the second.
- **37:19** — The Vulgate reads in manu ejus ("in his hand," referring to Judah or the prophet), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "in My hand."
- **37:26** — Latin sanctificationem meam ("My sanctification," repeated in v. 28) translates Hebrew miqdash ("sanctuary") and pairs directly with sanctificator Israël ("the Sanctifier of Israel") in v. 28.
