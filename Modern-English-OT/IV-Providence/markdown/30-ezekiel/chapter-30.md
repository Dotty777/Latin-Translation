## Text

**30:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**30:2** "Son of man, prophesy, and say, 'This is what the Lord God says: Wail, "Woe, woe for the day!"

**30:3** For the day is near, and the day of the Lord is approaching, a day of cloud; it will be the time of the nations.

**30:4** And a sword will come into Egypt, and dread will be in Ethiopia, when the wounded fall in Egypt, and its multitude is carried away, and its foundations are destroyed.

**30:5** Ethiopia, and Libya, and the Lydians, and all the rest of the common people, and Chub, and the sons of the land of the covenant, will fall with them by the sword.

**30:6** This is what the Lord God says: And those who uphold Egypt will collapse, and the pride of its dominion will be destroyed; from the tower of Syene they will fall by the sword in it, says the Lord God of hosts.

**30:7** And they will be devastated in the midst of desolate lands, and its cities will be in the midst of deserted cities;

**30:8** and they will know that I am the Lord, when I put fire in Egypt, and all its helpers are crushed.

**30:9** In that day messengers will go out from My presence in triremes to crush the confidence of Ethiopia; and dread will be in them in the day of Egypt, for without a doubt it will come.

**30:10** This is what the Lord God says: I will cause the multitude of Egypt to cease by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.

**30:11** He and his people with him, the mightiest of the nations, will be brought to destroy the land; and they will unsheathe their swords against Egypt, and they will fill the land with those killed.

**30:12** And I will make the channels of the rivers dry, and I will hand over the land into the hands of the most wicked; and I will devastate the land and its fullness by the hand of foreigners: I, the Lord, have spoken.

**30:13** This is what the Lord God says: And I will destroy the images, and I will cause the idols to cease from Memphis; and there will no longer be a leader from the land of Egypt, and I will put terror in the land of Egypt.

**30:14** And I will destroy the land of Pathros, and I will put fire in Tahpanhes, and I will execute judgments in Alexandria.

**30:15** And I will pour out My indignation upon Pelusium, the strength of Egypt, and I will kill the multitude of Alexandria.

**30:16** And I will put fire in Egypt; Pelusium will suffer pain like a woman in labor, and Alexandria will be devastated, and in Memphis there will be daily distresses.

**30:17** The young men of Heliopolis and Bubastis will fall by the sword, and they themselves will be led away captive.

**30:18** And in Tahpanhes the day will grow dark, when I crush the scepters of Egypt there, and the pride of its power comes to an end in it; a cloud will cover it, and its daughters will be led into captivity.

**30:19** And I will execute judgments in Egypt, and they will know that I am the Lord.'"

**30:20** And it happened in the eleventh year, in the first month, on the seventh day of the month, that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**30:21** "Son of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh king of Egypt; and look, it has not been wrapped up so that health might be restored to it, to be bound with cloths and bandaged with linen strips, so that, having regained strength, it might be able to hold the sword.

**30:22** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: Look, I am against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and I will crush his arm, strong yet broken, and I will cast the sword down from his hand,

**30:23** and I will scatter Egypt among the nations, and I will winnow them in the lands.

**30:24** And I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, and I will put My sword in his hand, and I will break the arms of Pharaoh, and they will groan with the groans of one killed before his face.

**30:25** And I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, and the arms of Pharaoh will fall down; and they will know that I am the Lord, when I put My sword in the hand of the king of Babylon, and he stretches it out over the land of Egypt.

**30:26** And I will scatter Egypt among the nations, and I will winnow them into the lands; and they will know that I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **30:5** — Vulgate uses Greco-Roman names ("Ethiopia" for Hebrew Cush, "Libya" for Put, "the Lydians" for Lud) and renders Hebrew kol ha'erev ("all the mixed people") as "all the rest of the common people" (omne reliquum vulgus).
- **30:9** — Vulgate specifies "in triremes" (in trieribus) where the Hebrew simply reads "in ships," and "without a doubt" (absque dubio) where the Hebrew reads "behold."
- **30:14** — Across vv. 13-17, the Vulgate substitutes Greco-Roman city names for the Hebrew names: Memphis (for Noph), Alexandria (for No/Thebes), Pelusium (for Sin), Heliopolis (for Aven/On), and Bubastis (for Pi-beseth). In v. 17, ipsae captivae is feminine plural, referring to the cities themselves (or their female inhabitants).
- **30:16** — Vulgate reads "daily distresses" (angustiae quotidianae), whereas the Hebrew reads "adversaries by day."
- **30:22** — Vulgate reads singular "his arm, strong yet broken" (brachium ejus forte, sed confractum), whereas the Hebrew reads plural "his arms, both the strong one and the broken one." In v. 24, the Clementine Vulgate also reads plural gement ("they will groan") where the Hebrew has singular "he will groan."
