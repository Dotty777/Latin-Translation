## Text

**36:1** "Now you, son of man, prophesy over the mountains of Israel, and you will say, 'Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord.

**36:2** This is what the Lord God says: Because the enemy has said about you, "Aha! The everlasting heights have been given to us as an inheritance,"'

**36:3** therefore prophesy and say, 'This is what the Lord God says: Because you have been made desolate and trampled all around, and have become an inheritance for the remaining nations, and you have risen onto the lip of the tongue and the reproach of the people,

**36:4** therefore, mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord God. This is what the Lord God says to the mountains and hills, to the torrents and valleys and desolate places, to the ruined walls and abandoned cities, which have been plundered and mocked by the remaining nations all around.

**36:5** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: Since in the fire of My zeal I have spoken concerning the remaining nations and concerning all Idumea, who gave My land to themselves as an inheritance with joy, with a whole heart and from the soul, and cast it out to lay it waste,'

**36:6** therefore prophesy over the soil of Israel, and you will say to the mountains and hills, to the ridges and valleys, 'This is what the Lord God says: Look, I have spoken in My zeal and in My fury, because you have endured the shame of the nations.

**36:7** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: I have lifted up My hand, that the nations that are around you will themselves bear their own shame.

**36:8** But you, mountains of Israel, sprout your branches and yield your fruit for My people Israel, for their coming is near.

**36:9** For look, I am for you, and I will turn to you; and you will be plowed, and you will receive seed.

**36:10** And I will multiply men upon you, and all the house of Israel; and the cities will be inhabited, and the ruined places will be restored.

**36:11** And I will fill you with men and livestock, and they will multiply and increase; and I will cause you to be inhabited as from the beginning, and I will endow you with greater good things than you had from the start; and you will know that I am the Lord.

**36:12** And I will bring men upon you, My people Israel, and they will possess you by inheritance; and you will be an inheritance for them, and you will never again be without them.

**36:13** This is what the Lord God says: Because they say about you, "You are a devourer of men and one who chokes your nation,"

**36:14** therefore you will no longer eat men, and you will no longer kill your nation, says the Lord God.

**36:15** Nor will I cause the shame of the nations to be heard in you any longer, and you will by no means bear the reproach of the peoples, and you will no longer lose your nation, says the Lord God.'"

**36:16** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**36:17** "Son of man, the house of Israel lived on their own soil, and they defiled it by their ways and by their practices; their way became like the uncleanness of a menstruating woman before Me.

**36:18** And I poured out My indignation upon them for the blood that they poured out upon the land, and with their idols they defiled it.

**36:19** And I scattered them among the nations, and they were winnowed into the lands; according to their ways and their devices I judged them.

**36:20** And they entered among the nations into which they went, and they defiled My holy name, when it was said about them, 'This is the people of the Lord, and from His land they have gone out.'

**36:21** And I spared My holy name, which the house of Israel had defiled among the nations into which they entered.

**36:22** Therefore you will say to the house of Israel, 'This is what the Lord God says: It is not for your sake that I will act, house of Israel, but for My holy name, which you defiled among the nations into which you entered.

**36:23** And I will sanctify My great name, which has been defiled among the nations, which you defiled in their midst, so that the nations may know that I am the Lord, says the Lord of hosts, when I have been sanctified in you before them.

**36:24** For indeed I will take you from the nations, and I will gather you from all the lands, and I will bring you into your own land.

**36:25** And I will pour out clean water upon you, and you will be cleansed from all your impurities, and from all your idols I will cleanse you.

**36:26** And I will give you a new heart, and I will place a new spirit in your midst; and I will take away the stony heart from your flesh, and I will give you a heart of flesh.

**36:27** And I will place My Spirit in your midst, and I will cause you to walk in My precepts, and to keep My judgments and perform them.

**36:28** And you will live in the land that I gave to your fathers; and you will be a people for Me, and I will be God for you.

**36:29** And I will save you from all your impurities; and I will summon the grain and multiply it, and I will not impose famine upon you.

**36:30** And I will multiply the fruit of the tree and the produce of the field, so that you will no longer bear the reproach of famine among the nations.

**36:31** And you will remember your most wicked ways and your practices that were not good, and your iniquities and your crimes will displease you.

**36:32** It is not for your sake that I will act, says the Lord God; let it be known to you. Be ashamed and blush over your ways, house of Israel.

**36:33** This is what the Lord God says: On the day when I cleanse you from all your iniquities, and cause the cities to be inhabited, and restore the ruined places,

**36:34** and the deserted land is cultivated, which formerly was desolate in the eyes of every traveler,

**36:35** they will say, "That uncultivated land has become like a garden of delight, and the deserted, destitute, and undermined cities sit fortified."

**36:36** And whatever nations are left around you will know that I, the Lord, have built up what was demolished and planted what was uncultivated; I, the Lord, have spoken, and I have done it.

**36:37** This is what the Lord God says: Yet in this the house of Israel will find Me, so that I may act for them: I will multiply them like a flock of men,

**36:38** like a holy flock, like the flock of Jerusalem at her solemn feasts; so the deserted cities will be full of flocks of men, and they will know that I am the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **36:5** — Latin Idumæa ("Idumea") represents the territory of Edom. The Vulgate's "from the soul, and cast it out to lay it waste" (et ex animo, et ejecerunt eam ut vastarent) differs from the Masoretic Hebrew, which speaks of acting with utter scorn so that the land's pasture might be for plunder.
- **36:13** — Latin suffocans ("choking"), followed by necabis ("kill") in v. 14 and amittes ("lose") in v. 15, reflects Vulgate-distinct renderings where the Masoretic Hebrew uses verbs for bereaving (v. 13) and causing to stumble (vv. 14-15).
- **36:23** — The Vulgate reads ait Dominus exercituum ("says the Lord of hosts"), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads "declares the Lord God."
- **36:25** — Latin effundam ("I will pour out") echoes v. 18 (effudi, "I poured out") rather than the Hebrew verb for sprinkling.
- **36:35** — Latin hortus voluptatis ("a garden of delight") translates Hebrew "garden of Eden" by meaning, consistent with the Vulgate's rendering of Eden in Genesis.
