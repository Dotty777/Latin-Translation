## Text

**48:1** And these are the names of the tribes from the boundaries of the north, beside the way of Hethlon, for those going to Hamath, the court of Enan, the border of Damascus to the north, beside the way of Hamath; and there will be for it the eastern side, the sea: Dan, one.

**48:2** And along the border of Dan, from the eastern side as far as the side of the sea: Asher, one.

**48:3** And along the border of Asher, from the eastern side as far as the side of the sea: Naphtali, one.

**48:4** And along the border of Naphtali, from the eastern side as far as the side of the sea: Manasseh, one.

**48:5** And along the border of Manasseh, from the eastern side as far as the side of the sea: Ephraim, one.

**48:6** And along the border of Ephraim, from the eastern side as far as the side of the sea: Reuben, one.

**48:7** And along the border of Reuben, from the eastern side as far as the side of the sea: Judah, one.

**48:8** And along the border of Judah, from the eastern side as far as the side of the sea, will be the firstfruits that you will set apart, twenty-five thousand in width, and in length like the individual portions from the eastern side as far as the side of the sea; and the sanctuary will be in the middle of it.

**48:9** The firstfruits that you will set apart for the Lord: a length of twenty-five thousand, and a width of ten thousand.

**48:10** Now these will be the firstfruits of the sanctuary of the priests: toward the north, twenty-five thousand in length, and toward the sea, ten thousand in width, and also toward the east, ten thousand in width, and toward the south, twenty-five thousand in length; and the sanctuary of the Lord will be in the middle of it.

**48:11** The sanctuary will be for the priests from the sons of Zadok, who kept My ceremonies and did not go astray when the sons of Israel went astray, just as the Levites also went astray.

**48:12** And there will be for them firstfruits from the firstfruits of the land, a Holy of Holies, beside the border of the Levites.

**48:13** And also for the Levites likewise, beside the boundaries of the priests: twenty-five thousand in length, and ten thousand in width. The entire length is twenty-five thousand, and the width ten thousand.

**48:14** And they will not sell any of it, nor exchange it, nor will the firstfruits of the land be transferred, because they are consecrated to the Lord.

**48:15** Now the five thousand that remain in width by twenty-five thousand will be common land of the city, for a dwelling place and for outskirts; and the city will be in the middle of it.

**48:16** And these are its measurements: on the northern side, four thousand five hundred; and on the southern side, four thousand five hundred; and on the eastern side, four thousand five hundred; and on the western side, four thousand five hundred.

**48:17** Now the outskirts of the city will be: toward the north, two hundred fifty; and toward the south, two hundred fifty; and toward the east, two hundred fifty; and toward the sea, two hundred fifty.

**48:18** And what remains in length alongside the firstfruits of the sanctuary, ten thousand to the east and ten thousand to the west, will be like the firstfruits of the sanctuary; and its produce will be as bread for those who serve the city.

**48:19** And those who serve the city will work from all the tribes of Israel.

**48:20** All the firstfruits of twenty-five thousand by twenty-five thousand, in a square, will be set apart for the firstfruits of the sanctuary and for the possession of the city.

**48:21** And what remains will belong to the prince, on every side of the firstfruits of the sanctuary and of the possession of the city, opposite the twenty-five thousand of the firstfruits as far as the eastern border; and also toward the sea, opposite the twenty-five thousand, as far as the border of the sea, it will likewise be among the portions of the prince; and the firstfruits of the sanctuary and the sanctuary of the temple will be in the middle of it.

**48:22** And from the possession of the Levites and from the possession of the city, in the middle of the portions of the prince, it will be between the border of Judah and the border of Benjamin, and it will belong to the prince.

**48:23** And for the remaining tribes, from the eastern side as far as the western side: Benjamin, one.

**48:24** And facing the border of Benjamin, from the eastern side as far as the western side: Simeon, one.

**48:25** And along the border of Simeon, from the eastern side as far as the western side: Issachar, one.

**48:26** And along the border of Issachar, from the eastern side as far as the western side: Zebulun, one.

**48:27** And along the border of Zebulun, from the eastern side as far as the side of the sea: Gad, one.

**48:28** And along the border of Gad, on the southern side, southward, the boundary will be from Tamar as far as the waters of contradiction of Kadesh, an inheritance facing the Great Sea.

**48:29** This is the land that you will cast by lot for the tribes of Israel, and these are their divisions, says the Lord God.

**48:30** And these are the exits of the city: on the northern side, you will measure four thousand five hundred.

**48:31** And the gates of the city are according to the names of the tribes of Israel; three gates on the north: the gate of Reuben, one; the gate of Judah, one; the gate of Levi, one.

**48:32** And on the eastern side, four thousand five hundred, and three gates: the gate of Joseph, one; the gate of Benjamin, one; the gate of Dan, one.

**48:33** And on the southern side, you will measure four thousand five hundred, and three gates: the gate of Simeon, one; the gate of Issachar, one; the gate of Zebulun, one.

**48:34** And on the western side, four thousand five hundred, and their three gates: the gate of Gad, one; the gate of Asher, one; the gate of Naphtali, one.

**48:35** All around, eighteen thousand; and the name of the city from that day will be, "The Lord Is There."

## Translator Notes

- **48:1** — Vulgate partially translates the place name Hazar-enan as atrium Enan ("the court of Enan") and keeps the terse clause et erit ei plaga orientalis mare ("and there will be for it the eastern side, the sea"), indicating a strip extending from the eastern side to the sea as in verses 2-7.
- **48:8** — Throughout this chapter, Vulgate renders the sacred territorial allotment (Hebrew terumah) as primitiae ("firstfruits"). Likewise, Latin mare ("the sea") is kept literally where Vulgate refers to the western boundary by the Mediterranean, distinct from occidens/occidentalis ("west/western") elsewhere in the chapter.
- **48:11** — Vulgate reads sanctuarium ("the sanctuary"), taking the underlying Hebrew form as the noun "sanctuary" rather than as the participle "consecrated" applied to the priests.
- **48:28** — Vulgate translates Meribath-kadesh literally as aquas contradictionis Cades ("the waters of contradiction of Kadesh") and renders the following term as haereditas ("an inheritance"), reading the unpointed Hebrew consonants as "inheritance" rather than "to the brook" (the Brook of Egypt).
- **48:35** — Latin Dominus ibidem (literally "the Lord in that very place") renders the Hebrew name meaning "the Lord is there."
