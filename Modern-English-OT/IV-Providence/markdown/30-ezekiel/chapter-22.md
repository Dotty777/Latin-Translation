## Text

**22:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**22:2** "And you, son of man, do you not judge, do you not judge the city of bloodshed?

**22:3** And you will show her all her abominations, and you will say, 'This is what the Lord God says: "A city shedding blood in her midst, so that her time may come, and that has made idols against herself, so that she might be defiled.

**22:4** By your blood, which was shed by you, you have become guilty, and by your idols, which you have made, you have been defiled. And you have made your days draw near, and you have brought on the time of your years; therefore I have made you a reproach to the nations, and a mockery to all lands.

**22:5** Those that are near and those that are far from you will triumph over you, you filthy, renowned one, great in destruction.

**22:6** Look, the princes of Israel, each with his own arm, have been in you to shed blood.

**22:7** They have treated father and mother with insults; in you they have extorted from the foreigner in your midst; they have grieved the orphan and the widow within you.

**22:8** You have spurned My sanctuaries, and you have defiled My Sabbaths.

**22:9** Slanderous men have been in you to shed blood, and upon the mountains they have eaten in you; they have committed wickedness in your midst.

**22:10** They have uncovered the nakedness of a father in you; they have humbled the uncleanness of a menstruating woman in you.

**22:11** And each man has committed an abomination with his neighbor's wife, and a father-in-law has wickedly defiled his daughter-in-law; a brother has violated his sister, the daughter of his father, in you.

**22:12** They have taken bribes within you to shed blood; you have taken interest and excess gain, and you greedily extorted from your neighbors, and you have forgotten Me, says the Lord God.

**22:13** Look, I have struck My hands together over your greed that you have practiced, and over the blood that has been shed in your midst.

**22:14** Will your heart endure, or will your hands prevail, in the days that I will make for you? I, the Lord, have spoken, and I will do it.

**22:15** And I will scatter you among the nations, and I will winnow you into the lands, and I will make your uncleanness cease from you.

**22:16** And I will possess you in the sight of the nations, and you will know that I am the Lord."'"

**22:17** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**22:18** "Son of man, the house of Israel has been turned into dross for Me; all of these are bronze, and tin, and iron, and lead in the midst of the furnace; they have become the dross of silver.

**22:19** Therefore, this is what the Lord God says: 'Because all of you have been turned into dross, therefore, look, I will gather you in the midst of Jerusalem,

**22:20** with a gathering of silver, and bronze, and tin, and iron, and lead in the midst of a furnace, to kindle a fire in it for melting. So I will gather you in My fury and in My wrath, and I will rest, and I will melt you.

**22:21** And I will gather you, and I will kindle you in the fire of My fury, and you will be melted in the midst of it.

**22:22** As silver is melted in the midst of a furnace, so you will be in the midst of it; and you will know that I am the Lord, when I have poured out My indignation upon you.'"

**22:23** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**22:24** "Son of man, say to her, 'You are an unclean land, and not rained upon in the day of fury.'

**22:25** There is a conspiracy of prophets in her midst; like a roaring lion seizing prey, they have devoured lives; they have taken wealth and valuables; they have multiplied her widows in the midst of her.

**22:26** Her priests have despised My law, and they have defiled My sanctuaries; they have kept no distinction between the holy and the profane, and they have not discerned between the defiled and the clean; and they have turned their eyes away from My Sabbaths, and I was profaned in their midst.

**22:27** Her princes in the midst of her are like wolves seizing prey, to shed blood, and to destroy lives, and to pursue gain greedily.

**22:28** And her prophets were plastering them without tempering, seeing vain things, and divining falsehood for them, saying, 'This is what the Lord God says,' when the Lord has not spoken.

**22:29** The peoples of the land practiced extortion and robbed violently; they afflicted the needy and the poor, and they oppressed the foreigner with extortion without justice.

**22:30** And I sought from among them a man who would put up a hedge and stand in opposition against Me for the land, so that I would not destroy it; and I found no one.

**22:31** And I have poured out My indignation upon them; in the fire of My wrath I have consumed them. I have returned their way upon their head, says the Lord God."

## Translator Notes

- **22:16** — Vulgate-distinct reading: Latin "possidebo te" ("I will possess you") reflects a first-person verb of possessing or inheriting, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "you will be profaned in yourself."
- **22:20** — Vulgate-distinct reading: Latin "requiescam" ("I will rest") interprets the Hebrew verb as "rest" rather than "leave/deposit," and in 22:21 "succendam vos" reads "I will kindle you" where the Masoretic Text has "I will blow upon you."
- **22:28** — Latin "liniebant eos absque temperamento" literally describes plastering or daubing without proper tempering (an allusion to flimsy whitewash or untempered mortar, as in Ezekiel 13:10-15).
- **22:30** — Vulgate-distinct reading: Latin "qui interponeret sepem, et staret oppositus contra me" ("who would put up a hedge and stand in opposition against Me") corresponds to the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "build up the wall and stand in the breach before Me."
