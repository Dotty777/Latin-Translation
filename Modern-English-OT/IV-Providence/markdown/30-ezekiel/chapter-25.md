## Text

**25:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**25:2** "Son of man, set your face against the sons of Ammon, and you shall prophesy concerning them.

**25:3** And you shall say to the sons of Ammon, 'Hear the word of the Lord God. This is what the Lord God says: Because you said, "Aha, aha!" over My sanctuary, because it was defiled, and over the land of Israel, because it was made desolate, and over the house of Judah, because they were led into captivity,

**25:4** therefore I will hand you over to the sons of the east as an inheritance; and they will set up their sheepfolds in you, and they will place their tents in you; they themselves will eat your produce, and they themselves will drink your milk.

**25:5** And I will make Rabbah a dwelling place for camels, and the sons of Ammon a resting place for flocks; and you will know that I am the Lord.

**25:6** For this is what the Lord God says: Because you clapped with your hand and stamped with your foot, and rejoiced with all your passion over the land of Israel,

**25:7** therefore, look, I will stretch out My hand over you, and I will hand you over as plunder for the nations, and I will kill you off from the peoples, and destroy you from the lands, and crush you; and you will know that I am the Lord.'

**25:8** This is what the Lord God says: Because Moab and Seir have said, 'Look, the house of Judah is like all the nations,'

**25:9** therefore, look, I will open the shoulder of Moab from the cities, from its cities, I say, and from its borders, the renowned ones of the land, Beth-jeshimoth, and Baal-meon, and Kiriathaim,

**25:10** to the sons of the east along with the sons of Ammon, and I will give it as an inheritance, so that there may no longer be any memory of the sons of Ammon among the nations.

**25:11** And on Moab I will execute judgments, and they will know that I am the Lord.

**25:12** This is what the Lord God says: Because Idumea has carried out retribution so as to avenge itself on the sons of Judah, and has sinned in offending, and has sought vengeance against them,

**25:13** therefore this is what the Lord God says: I will stretch out My hand over Idumea, and I will remove from it man and beast, and I will make it deserted from the south; and those who are in Dedan will fall by the sword.

**25:14** And I will lay My retribution upon Idumea by the hand of My people Israel; and they will act in Edom according to My anger and My fury, and they will know My vengeance, says the Lord God.

**25:15** This is what the Lord God says: Because the Philistines have carried out vengeance, and have exacted retribution with all their spirit, killing and fulfilling ancient enmities,

**25:16** therefore this is what the Lord God says: Look, I will stretch out My hand over the Philistines, and I will kill the killers, and I will destroy the remnant of the coastal region,

**25:17** and I will execute great acts of retribution upon them, rebuking them in fury; and they will know that I am the Lord, when I lay My vengeance upon them."

## Translator Notes

- **25:4** — Latin caulas suas ("their sheepfolds") renders Hebrew tirotehem ("their encampments"), reflecting a pastoral reading of the eastern invaders' settlements.
- **25:6** — Vulgate ex toto affectu ("with all your passion/feeling") renders a rare Hebrew phrase meaning "with all scorn of soul" in a more general emotional sense (compare toto animo in v. 15).
- **25:13** — Vulgate ab austro ("from the south") translates the Hebrew place name Teman according to its literal geographical meaning.
- **25:14** — In vv. 12-14a the Vulgate uses the Greco-Latin regional name Idumaea ("Idumea") for Edom before switching directly to Edom in the second half of v. 14.
- **25:16** — Vulgate interficiam interfectores ("I will kill the killers") translates the Hebrew gentilic Keretim ("Cherethites") etymologically as a cognate noun of destruction; in vv. 15-16 Latin Palaestini is Jerome's standard rendering for the Philistines.
