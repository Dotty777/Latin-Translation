## Text

**21:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**21:2** "Son of man, set your face toward the way of the south, and let words drop toward the southwest, and prophesy to the forest of the southern field.

**21:3** And you will say to the southern forest, 'Hear the word of the Lord. This is what the Lord God says: "Look, I will kindle a fire in you, and I will burn up in you every green tree and every dry tree. The flame of the burning will not be extinguished, and every face from the south to the north will be burned up in it,

**21:4** and all flesh will see that I, the Lord, have kindled it, and it will not be extinguished."'"

**21:5** And I said, "Ah, ah, ah, Lord God! They themselves say of me, 'Does this man not speak in parables?'"

**21:6** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**21:7** "Son of man, set your face toward Jerusalem, and let words drop toward the sanctuaries, and prophesy against the ground of Israel.

**21:8** And you will say to the land of Israel, 'This is what the Lord God says: "Look, I am against you, and I will draw My sword out of its sheath, and I will kill in you the righteous and the wicked.

**21:9** Now because I have killed in you the righteous and the wicked, therefore My sword will go out from its sheath against all flesh, from the south to the north,

**21:10** so that all flesh may know that I, the Lord, have drawn My sword out of its sheath beyond recall."'

**21:11** And you, son of man, groan with a breaking of the loins, and groan in bitterness before them.

**21:12** And when they say to you, 'Why are you groaning?' you will say, 'Because of the report, for it is coming, and every heart will melt, and all hands will go limp, and every spirit will grow faint, and waters will flow down all knees. Look, it is coming, and it will happen, says the Lord God.'"

**21:13** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**21:14** "Son of man, prophesy, and you will say, 'This is what the Lord God says: Speak, "A sword, a sword is sharpened and polished.

**21:15** It is sharpened to slaughter victims; it is polished to shine. You who move the scepter of My son, you have cut down every tree.

**21:16** And I have given it to be burnished, so that it may be held by the hand; this sword is sharpened, and this one is polished, so that it may be in the hand of the killer."'

**21:17** Cry out and wail, son of man, for it has come upon My people, it is upon all the leaders of Israel who had fled; they have been handed over to the sword with My people. Therefore strike your thigh,

**21:18** for it has been tested; and this too, when it overthrows the scepter, and it will be no more, says the Lord God.

**21:19** You therefore, son of man, prophesy, and strike hand against hand; and let the sword be doubled, and let the sword of those killed be tripled. This is the sword of great slaughter, which causes them to be stunned

**21:20** and to melt in heart, and multiplies ruins. At all their gates I have placed the turmoil of the sword that is sharp, and polished to flash, wrapped for slaughter.

**21:21** Sharpen yourself, go to the right or to the left, wherever the desire of your face is.

**21:22** Indeed, I also will clap hand to hand, and I will fulfill My indignation; I, the Lord, have spoken."

**21:23** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**21:24** "And you, son of man, lay out for yourself two roads, so that the sword of the king of Babylon may come. Both of them will go out from one land; and with his hand he will take a conjecture; at the head of the road to the city he will conjecture.

**21:25** You will lay out a road so that the sword may come to Rabbah of the sons of Ammon, and to Judah, into strongly fortified Jerusalem.

**21:26** For the king of Babylon stood at the fork in the road, at the head of the two roads, seeking divination, mixing arrows; he questioned the idols, he consulted the entrails.

**21:27** At his right hand came the divination against Jerusalem, to set battering rams, to open his mouth in slaughter, to lift up his voice in howling, to set battering rams against the gates, to heap up a mound, to build fortifications.

**21:28** And he will be like one consulting an oracle in vain in their eyes, and imitating the rest of the Sabbaths; yet he himself will remember iniquity in order to capture them.

**21:29** Therefore, this is what the Lord God says: 'Because you have remembered your iniquity, and have uncovered your transgressions, and your sins have appeared in all your thoughts, because, I say, you have remembered, you will be captured by hand.'

**21:30** And you, profane, wicked leader of Israel, whose day has come, appointed in the time of iniquity,

**21:31** this is what the Lord God says: 'Remove the turban, take off the crown. Is this not the one that raised up the humble and humbled the lofty?

**21:32** Iniquity, iniquity, iniquity I will make it; and this has not happened until he should come to whom judgment belongs, and I will hand it over to him.'

**21:33** And you, son of man, prophesy and say, 'This is what the Lord God says to the sons of Ammon and concerning their reproach,' and you will say, 'Blade, blade, unsheathed for killing, polished so that you may put to death and flash,

**21:34** while vain things were seen for you, and lies were divined, so that you might be placed upon the necks of the wounded wicked, whose day has come, appointed in the time of iniquity.

**21:35** Return to your sheath. In the place in which you were created, in the land of your birth, I will judge you.

**21:36** And I will pour out My indignation upon you; in the fire of My fury I will blow upon you, and I will give you into the hands of men who are foolish and who fashion destruction.

**21:37** You will be food for the fire; your blood will be in the midst of the land; you will be handed over to oblivion, for I, the Lord, have spoken.'"

## Translator Notes

- **21:1** — Vulgate chapter 21 begins five verses earlier than the Hebrew Masoretic Text and most modern English Bibles (Vulgate 21:1-5 corresponds to MT 20:45-49, and Vulgate 21:6-37 to MT 21:1-32).
- **21:2** — Latin "stilla" literally means "drip" or "drop," a prophetic idiom for uttering words (repeated in 21:7). The Vulgate also distinguishes three southerly terms: "auster" (south), "africus" (southwest), and "meridianus" (southern).
- **21:15** — Vulgate-distinct reading: Latin "qui moves sceptrum filii mei, succidisti omne lignum" ("you who move the scepter of My son, you have cut down every tree") renders an obscure Hebrew line differently from the Masoretic Text ("Shall we rejoice? The rod of my son despises every tree"). Similarly, 21:17-18 follows the Vulgate's distinct clauses ("who had fled" and "when it overthrows the scepter").
- **21:24** — Vulgate-distinct reading: Latin "manu capiet conjecturam ... conjiciet" ("with his hand he will take a conjecture ... he will conjecture") depicts the king divining at the crossroads, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text instructs the prophet to carve a signpost.
- **21:28** — Vulgate-distinct reading: Latin "sabbatorum otium imitans" ("imitating the rest of the Sabbaths") connects the Hebrew phrase for sworn oaths with the Sabbath. In 21:32, the Vulgate likewise renders the Hebrew term for "ruin" as "iniquitatem" ("iniquity").
