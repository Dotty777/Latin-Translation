## Text

**16:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**16:2** "Son of man, make her abominations known to Jerusalem,

**16:3** and you shall say, 'This is what the Lord God says to Jerusalem: Your root and your birth are from the land of Canaan; your father was an Amorite, and your mother a Hittite.

**16:4** And when you were born, on the day of your birth, your umbilical cord was not cut, and you were not washed with water for health, nor salted with salt, nor wrapped in cloths.

**16:5** No eye spared you to do one of these things for you, having compassion on you; rather, you were cast out upon the face of the earth in the rejection of your soul on the day you were born.

**16:6** Yet passing by you, I saw you being trampled in your blood; and I said to you while you were in your blood, "Live!" Yes, I said to you, "In your blood, live!"

**16:7** I made you multiply like a sprout of the field; and you multiplied and grew tall, and you entered in and reached womanly adornment: your breasts grew full, and your hair grew, yet you were unclothed and full of shame.

**16:8** And I passed by you and saw you, and look, your time was the time of lovers; and I spread My cloak over you and covered your disgrace, and I swore to you and entered into a covenant with you, says the Lord God, and you became Mine.

**16:9** And I washed you with water, and I cleansed your blood from you, and I anointed you with oil.

**16:10** And I clothed you in multicolored garments, and I shod you with violet leather, and I wrapped you in fine linen, and I dressed you in fine fabrics;

**16:11** and I adorned you with adornment, and I placed bracelets on your hands and a necklace around your neck;

**16:12** and I placed a ring upon your face, and rings for your ears, and a crown of splendor upon your head.

**16:13** And you were adorned with gold and silver, and you were clothed in fine linen, brocade, and multicolored fabrics; you ate fine flour, honey, and oil, and you became exceedingly beautiful, and you advanced to royalty.

**16:14** And your name went out among the nations because of your loveliness, because you were perfect in My splendor which I had placed upon you, says the Lord God.

**16:15** And having confidence in your beauty, you committed fornication in your name, and you offered your fornication to every passerby, so that you might become his.

**16:16** And taking from your garments, you made for yourself high places stitched together on every side, and you committed fornication upon them such as has not been done, nor will be.

**16:17** And you took the vessels of your splendor from My gold and My silver which I gave to you, and you made for yourself male images, and you committed fornication with them.

**16:18** And you took your multicolored garments and covered them, and you set My oil and My incense before them.

**16:19** And My bread which I gave to you, fine flour and oil and honey with which I nourished you, you placed in their sight as a sweet aroma; and so it was done, says the Lord God.

**16:20** And you took your sons and your daughters whom you bore to Me, and you sacrificed them to them to be devoured. Is your fornication a small matter?

**16:21** You sacrificed My sons and gave them to them, consecrating them.

**16:22** And after all your abominations and fornications, you did not remember the days of your youth, when you were unclothed and full of shame, trampled in your blood.

**16:23** And it happened after all your wickedness ("Woe, woe to you!" says the Lord God),

**16:24** and you built for yourself a house of harlotry, and you made for yourself a booth of harlotry in all the public squares.

**16:25** At the head of every road you built a sign of your harlotry, and you made your splendor abominable, and you parted your feet for every passerby, and you multiplied your fornications;

**16:26** and you committed fornication with the sons of Egypt, your neighbors, great in flesh, and you multiplied your fornication to provoke Me.

**16:27** Look, I will stretch out My hand over you, and I will take away your justification, and I will give you over to the desires of those who hate you, the daughters of the Philistines, who blush at your wicked way.

**16:28** And you committed fornication with the sons of the Assyrians because you were not yet filled; and after you committed fornication, not even thus were you satisfied;

**16:29** and you multiplied your fornication in the land of Canaan with the Chaldeans, and not even thus were you satisfied.

**16:30** With what shall I cleanse your heart, says the Lord God, when you do all these works of a woman who is a harlot and brazen?

**16:31** Because you constructed your house of harlotry at the head of every road, and you made your high place in every public square; yet you did not become like a harlot increasing the price through disdain,

**16:32** but like an adulterous woman, who brings in strangers over her husband.

**16:33** To all harlots wages are given, yet you gave wages to all your lovers, and you kept giving gifts to them so that they would come in to you from every side to commit fornication with you.

**16:34** And it happened in you contrary to the custom of women in your fornications, and after you there will be no fornication; for in that you gave wages and did not receive wages, the opposite happened in you.

**16:35** Therefore, harlot, hear the word of the Lord.

**16:36** This is what the Lord God says: Because your bronze has been poured out and your disgrace has been uncovered in your fornications upon your lovers and upon the idols of your abominations, in the blood of your sons whom you gave to them,

**16:37** look, I will gather all your lovers with whom you mingled, and all whom you loved, together with all whom you hated; and I will gather them against you from every side, and I will uncover your disgrace before them, and they will see all your shamefulness.

**16:38** And I will judge you by the judgments of adulteresses and of those who shed blood, and I will give you over to the blood of fury and jealousy.

**16:39** And I will give you into their hands, and they will destroy your house of harlotry and tear down your booth of harlotry; and they will strip you of your garments and take away the vessels of your splendor, and leave you unclothed and full of disgrace;

**16:40** and they will bring a crowd against you, and they will stone you with stones and cut you down with their swords;

**16:41** and they will burn your houses with fire and execute judgments upon you in the sight of very many women. And you will cease committing fornication, and you will no longer give wages;

**16:42** and My indignation will rest upon you, and My jealousy will be taken away from you; and I will be calm and will be angry no more.

**16:43** Because you did not remember the days of your youth, and you provoked Me in all these things, therefore I also have brought your ways upon your head, says the Lord God, and I have not acted according to your wicked deeds in all your abominations.

**16:44** Look, everyone who speaks a common proverb will take it up against you, saying, "Like mother, so also is her daughter."

**16:45** You are the daughter of your mother, who cast away her husband and her sons; and you are the sister of your sisters, who cast away their husbands and their sons: your mother was a Hittite, and your father an Amorite.

**16:46** And your older sister is Samaria, she and her daughters, who dwell to your left; while your sister younger than you, who dwells to your right, is Sodom and her daughters.

**16:47** Yet you neither walked in their ways nor acted according to their wicked deeds a very little less; you have done almost more wicked deeds than they in all your ways.

**16:48** As I live, says the Lord God, your sister Sodom, she and her daughters, have not done as you and your daughters have done.

**16:49** Look, this was the iniquity of your sister Sodom: pride, fullness of bread and abundance, and the idleness of her and her daughters; and they did not extend a hand to the needy and the poor;

**16:50** and they were lifted up and committed abominations before Me; and I took them away, as you have seen.

**16:51** And Samaria did not commit half of your sins, but you surpassed them in your wicked deeds, and you justified your sisters by all your abominations which you practiced.

**16:52** Therefore you also bear your shame, you who surpassed your sisters by your sins, acting more wickedly than they; for they have been justified by you. Therefore you also be put to shame and bear your disgrace, you who justified your sisters.

**16:53** And I will turn, restoring them with the restoration of Sodom with her daughters, and with the restoration of Samaria and her daughters, and I will restore your return in the midst of them,

**16:54** so that you may bear your disgrace and be put to shame for all that you have done, comforting them.

**16:55** And your sister Sodom and her daughters will return to their former state, and Samaria and her daughters will return to their former state, and you and your daughters will return to your former state.

**16:56** Yet your sister Sodom was not heard in your mouth in the day of your pride,

**16:57** before your wickedness was uncovered, as at this time, to the reproach of the daughters of Syria and of all the daughters of the Philistines around you, who surround you on every side.

**16:58** You yourself have borne your wickedness and your disgrace, says the Lord God.

**16:59** For this is what the Lord God says: And I will do to you as you have despised the oath, so as to make the covenant void;

**16:60** yet I will remember My covenant with you in the days of your youth, and I will establish for you an everlasting covenant.

**16:61** And you will remember your ways and be put to shame when you receive your sisters who are older than you together with your younger ones; and I will give them to you as daughters, but not from your covenant.

**16:62** And I Myself will establish My covenant with you, and you will know that I am the Lord;

**16:63** so that you may remember and be put to shame, and may no longer open your mouth because of your shame, when I have been appeased toward you for all that you have done, says the Lord God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **16:6** — Latin conculcari ("being trampled") is a distinctly Vulgate reading (repeated in 16:22), whereas the Hebrew describes floundering or kicking in blood.
- **16:7** — Latin mundum muliebrem ("womanly adornment") uses a classical idiom for feminine finery, and confusione plena ("full of shame") reflects a distinctly Vulgate rendering of the verse's closing phrase.
- **16:27** — Latin justificationem tuam ("your justification") reflects the standard Vulgate translation of the Hebrew term for an ordinance or allotted portion.
- **16:30** — Latin In quo mundabo cor tuum ("With what shall I cleanse your heart?") is a notable Vulgate-distinct reading; the Hebrew reads "How weak is your heart!"
- **16:31** — Latin fastidio augens pretium ("increasing the price through disdain") distinctly portrays a harlot feigning reluctance to raise her fee, whereas the Hebrew speaks of scorning payment.
- **16:36** — Latin aes tuum ("your bronze") takes the underlying Hebrew noun from the root for bronze/copper, whereas the Hebrew is usually understood here as "your lust" or "your filthiness."
- **16:43** — Latin et non feci juxta scelera tua ("and I have not acted according to your wicked deeds") preserves a first-person reading, whereas the Hebrew is usually read in the second person ("Have you not committed lewdness...").
- **16:47** — Latin neque ... fecisti pauxillum minus: pene sceleratiora fecisti ("nor acted according to their wicked deeds a very little less; you have done almost more wicked deeds") is a distinctive Vulgate rendering of a difficult Hebrew phrase meaning "as if that were too small a thing, you acted even more corruptly."
- **16:50** — Latin sicut vidisti ("as you have seen") reads the second person singular, whereas the Masoretic Hebrew reads the first person ("as I saw fit").
