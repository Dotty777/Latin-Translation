## Text

**6:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**6:2** "Son of man, set your face toward the mountains of Israel, and you will prophesy to them,

**6:3** and you will say, 'Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord God. This is what the Lord God says to the mountains and hills, to the cliffs and valleys: Look, I will bring the sword upon you, and I will destroy your high places,

**6:4** and I will demolish your altars, and your images will be shattered, and I will cast down those of you who are killed before your idols;

**6:5** and I will place the corpses of the sons of Israel before the face of your images, and I will scatter your bones around your altars;

**6:6** in all your dwelling places the cities will be deserted, and the high places will be demolished and ruined; and your altars will perish and be shattered, and your idols will cease, and your shrines will be crushed, and your works will be wiped out;

**6:7** and one who is killed will fall in your midst, and you will know that I am the Lord.

**6:8** And I will leave among you those who escape the sword among the nations, when I have scattered you in the lands;

**6:9** and those of you who are delivered will remember Me among the nations to which they were led captive, because I have crushed their heart that fornicates and departs from Me, and their eyes that fornicate after their idols; and they will be displeasing to themselves over the evils that they have done in all their abominations.

**6:10** And they will know that I, the Lord, have not spoken in vain to do this evil to them.'"

**6:11** This is what the Lord God says: "Strike your hand and stamp your foot, and say, 'Alas, over all the abominations of the evils of the house of Israel, because by sword, famine, and plague they are about to fall!'

**6:12** The one who is far away will die by plague, and the one who is near will fall by the sword, and the one who is left and besieged will die by famine; and I will complete My indignation upon them.

**6:13** And you will know that I am the Lord, when those of you who are killed are in the midst of your idols, all around your altars, on every high hill, and on all the summits of the mountains, and under every shady tree, and under every leafy oak, the place where they burned fragrant incense to all their idols.

**6:14** And I will stretch out My hand over them, and I will make the land desolate and destitute, from the desert of Deblatha, in all their dwelling places; and they will know that I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **6:3** — Vulgate reads rupibus ("cliffs"), whereas the Hebrew has "ravines" or "watercourses".
- **6:4** — Vulgate renders Hebrew hammanim ("incense altars" or "sun-pillars") as simulacra ("images") here and in 6:5, and as delubra ("shrines") in 6:6.
- **6:9** — Vulgate reads active contrivi ("I have crushed"), whereas the Masoretic Hebrew has the passive or reflexive "I have been broken".
- **6:14** — Vulgate preserves the proper name Deblatha (reflecting Hebrew Diblah with its final ending, often emended by modern critics to Riblah) and reads "from the desert of Deblatha" rather than "from the wilderness toward Diblah".
