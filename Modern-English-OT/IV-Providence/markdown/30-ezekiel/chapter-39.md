## Text

**39:1** "Now you, son of man, prophesy against Gog, and you will say, 'This is what the Lord God says: Look, I am against you, Gog, the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal.

**39:2** And I will turn you around, and I will lead you out, and I will make you come up from the sides of the north, and I will bring you upon the mountains of Israel.

**39:3** And I will strike your bow in your left hand, and I will cast down your arrows from your right hand.

**39:4** Upon the mountains of Israel you will fall, and all your troops, and your peoples who are with you; I have given you to wild animals, to birds, and to every flying creature and the beasts of the earth to be devoured.

**39:5** Upon the face of the field you will fall, for I have spoken, says the Lord God.

**39:6** And I will send fire upon Magog, and upon those who live in the islands confidently; and they will know that I am the Lord.

**39:7** And I will make My holy name known in the midst of My people Israel, and I will not defile My holy name any longer; and the nations will know that I am the Lord, the Holy One of Israel.

**39:8** Look, it has come, and it is done, says the Lord God; this is the day of which I have spoken.

**39:9** And the inhabitants will go out from the cities of Israel, and they will kindle and burn up the weapons, shield and spears, bow and arrows, and hand staffs and lances; and they will burn them with fire for seven years.

**39:10** And they will not carry wood from the regions, nor cut any down from the forests, because they will burn the weapons with fire; and they will plunder those to whom they had been prey, and they will pillage their destroyers, says the Lord God.

**39:11** And it will be on that day: I will give Gog a renowned place, a burial ground in Israel, the Valley of the Travelers to the east of the sea, which will cause those passing by to be astonished; and there they will bury Gog and all his multitude, and it will be called the Valley of the Multitude of Gog.

**39:12** And the house of Israel will bury them, so that they may cleanse the land for seven months.

**39:13** And all the people of the land will bury him; and the day on which I have been glorified will be a renowned day for them, says the Lord God.

**39:14** And they will appoint men continually traversing the land, to bury and seek out those who had remained upon the face of the land, so that they may cleanse it; and after seven months they will begin to search.

**39:15** And they will go around traveling through the land; and whenever they see a human bone, they will set up a marker beside it, until the buriers bury it in the Valley of the Multitude of Gog.

**39:16** And the name of the city is Hamonah; and they will cleanse the land.'

**39:17** You therefore, son of man, this is what the Lord God says: Say to every winged creature, and to all birds, and to all the beasts of the field, 'Assemble, hurry, gather together from every side to My sacrifice which I am slaughtering for you, a great sacrifice upon the mountains of Israel, so that you may eat flesh and drink blood.

**39:18** You will eat the flesh of the mighty, and you will drink the blood of the princes of the earth: of rams, and of lambs, and of goats, and of bulls and fattened animals, and of all fat ones.

**39:19** And you will eat fat to fullness, and you will drink blood to drunkenness, from the sacrifice that I will slaughter for you;

**39:20** and you will be filled at My table with horse and mighty horseman, and with all men of war, says the Lord God.'

**39:21** And I will set My glory among the nations; and all the nations will see My judgment that I have executed, and My hand that I have laid upon them.

**39:22** And the house of Israel will know that I am the Lord their God, from that day and onward.

**39:23** And the nations will know that the house of Israel was taken captive in its iniquity, because they abandoned Me, and I hid My face from them, and handed them over into the hands of enemies, and all of them fell by the sword.

**39:24** According to their uncleanness and wickedness I did to them, and I hid My face from them.

**39:25** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: Now I will bring back the captivity of Jacob, and I will have mercy on the whole house of Israel, and I will take up zeal for My holy name.

**39:26** And they will bear their shame, and all the transgression by which they transgressed against Me, when they live in their land confidently, fearing no one,

**39:27** and when I have brought them back from the peoples, and gathered them from the lands of their enemies, and have been sanctified in them in the eyes of very many nations.

**39:28** And they will know that I am the Lord their God, because I carried them away into the nations, and gathered them upon their own land, and left no one of them behind there.

**39:29** And I will not hide My face from them any longer, because I have poured out My Spirit upon the whole house of Israel, says the Lord God."

## Translator Notes

- **39:4** — Following the Clementine punctuation ('feris, avibus'), 'feris' is translated as a distinct noun ("wild animals") rather than an adjective modifying 'avibus' ("birds of prey," Hebrew 'eit tzippor').
- **39:7** — Vulgate 'non polluam' is first-person active ("I will not defile"), where the Hebrew can be read permissively ("I will not let My holy name be profaned").
- **39:11** — Vulgate 'locum nominatum' ("a renowned place") reflects reading Hebrew 'sham' ("there") as 'shem' ("name/renown"), and 'obstupescere faciet praetereuntes' ("will cause those passing by to be astonished") diverges from the Hebrew "will block the travelers."
- **39:18** — Vulgate 'altilium, et pinguium omnium' ("fattened animals, and all fat ones") omits the place name "Bashan" found in the Hebrew ("fatlings of Bashan"), translating it instead as an adjective of fatness.
- **39:20** — Vulgate 'de equo, et equite forti' ("with horse and mighty horseman") attaches 'forti' ("mighty") directly to 'equite' ("horseman"), whereas the Hebrew lists "horses and charioteers, mighty men" separately.
