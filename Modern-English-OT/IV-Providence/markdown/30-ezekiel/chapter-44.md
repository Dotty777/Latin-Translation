## Text

**44:1** And he turned me back to the way of the outer gate of the sanctuary, which faced toward the east; and it was closed.

**44:2** And the Lord said to me, "This gate shall be closed; it shall not be opened, and no man shall pass through it, because the Lord, the God of Israel, has entered through it; and it shall be closed

**44:3** to the prince. The prince himself shall sit in it to eat bread before the Lord; he shall enter by the way of the gate of the vestibule, and by its way he shall go out."

**44:4** And he brought me by the way of the north gate in front of the house; and I saw, and look, the glory of the Lord filled the house of the Lord; and I fell on my face.

**44:5** And the Lord said to me, "Son of man, set your heart, and see with your eyes, and with your ears hear all that I speak to you concerning all the ceremonies of the house of the Lord and concerning all its laws; and you shall set your heart on the ways of the temple through all the exits of the sanctuary.

**44:6** And you shall say to the house of Israel that provokes Me, 'This is what the Lord God says: Let all your crimes be enough for you, house of Israel,

**44:7** because you bring in foreign sons, uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh, to be in My sanctuary and defile My house; and you offer My loaves, fat, and blood, and you break My covenant in all your crimes.

**44:8** And you have not kept the precepts of My sanctuary, and you have appointed keepers of My observances in My sanctuary for yourselves.

**44:9** This is what the Lord God says: No foreigner, uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh, shall enter My sanctuary, no foreign son who is in the midst of the sons of Israel.

**44:10** Moreover, the Levites who withdrew far from Me in the straying of the sons of Israel, and strayed from Me after their idols, and bore their iniquity,

**44:11** shall be custodians in My sanctuary, and doorkeepers of the gates of the house, and ministers of the house; they themselves shall slaughter the burnt offerings and the sacrifices of the people, and they themselves shall stand in their presence to minister to them.

**44:12** Because they ministered to them in the presence of their idols and became a stumbling block of iniquity to the house of Israel, therefore I have raised My hand against them, says the Lord God, and they shall bear their iniquity.

**44:13** And they shall not draw near to Me to perform the priesthood for Me, nor shall they approach any of My sanctuary beside the Holy of Holies; but they shall bear their disgrace and their crimes that they committed.

**44:14** And I will appoint them doorkeepers of the house in all its ministry and in all things that shall be done in it.

**44:15** But the priests and the Levites, the sons of Zadok, who kept the ceremonies of My sanctuary when the sons of Israel strayed from Me, they themselves shall approach Me to minister to Me; and they shall stand in My presence to offer to Me fat and blood, says the Lord God.

**44:16** They themselves shall enter My sanctuary, and they themselves shall approach My table, to minister to Me and to keep My ceremonies.

**44:17** And when they enter the gates of the inner court, they shall be clothed with linen garments; and nothing woolen shall come upon them when they minister in the gates of the inner court and inside.

**44:18** Linen headbands shall be on their heads, and linen undergarments shall be on their loins, and they shall not bind themselves in sweat.

**44:19** And when they go out into the outer court to the people, they shall take off their garments in which they had ministered and lay them away in the treasury chamber of the sanctuary; and they shall clothe themselves with other garments, and they shall not sanctify the people with their garments.

**44:20** And they shall not shave their head, nor let their hair grow long; but they shall surely trim their heads.

**44:21** And no priest shall drink wine when he is about to enter the inner court.

**44:22** And they shall not take as wives a widow or a divorced woman, but virgins of the offspring of the house of Israel; yet they may also take a widow who has been a widow of a priest.

**44:23** And they shall teach My people the difference between the holy and the defiled, and they shall show them the difference between the clean and the unclean.

**44:24** And when there is a dispute, they shall stand in My judgments and judge; they shall keep My laws and My precepts in all My solemn feasts, and they shall sanctify My Sabbaths.

**44:25** And they shall not go in to a dead person, so that they may not be defiled, except to a father and mother, and a son and daughter, and a brother, and a sister who has not had another husband, by whom they will be contaminated.

**44:26** And after he has been cleansed, seven days shall be counted for him.

**44:27** And on the day of his entrance into the sanctuary, to the inner court, to minister to Me in the sanctuary, he shall offer for his sin, says the Lord God.

**44:28** And they shall have no inheritance: I am their inheritance. And you shall give them no possession in Israel, for I am their possession.

**44:29** They themselves shall eat the sacrifice, both for sin and for guilt, and every vow in Israel shall be theirs.

**44:30** And the firstfruits of all the firstborn, and all the libations from all things that are offered, shall belong to the priests; and you shall give the firstfruits of your food to the priest, so that he may lay up a blessing for your house.

**44:31** The priests shall not eat anything that died of itself or was caught by a beast, whether of birds or of livestock.'"

## Translator Notes

- **44:3** — Following the Vulgate sentence division, 'to the prince' (Latin principi) concludes the clause of v. 2 ('it shall be closed to the prince'), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text begins a new clause in v. 3 ('As for the prince...').
- **44:15** — Here and in 43:19, the Vulgate reads 'the priests and the Levites' (Latin sacerdotes et Levitae), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'the Levitical priests.'
- **44:19** — The Vulgate renders the priestly rooms as the singular 'treasury chamber of the sanctuary' (Latin in gazophylacio sanctuarii), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'the holy chambers.'
- **44:23** — The Vulgate renders the opposite of 'holy' as 'defiled' (Latin pollutum), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has 'common/profane.'
- **44:28** — The Vulgate states negatively 'they shall have no inheritance' (Latin Non erit autem eis haereditas), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads affirmatively ('it shall be for them an inheritance').
