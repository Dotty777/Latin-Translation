## Text

**20:1** And it happened in the seventh year, in the fifth, on the tenth of the month, that men from the elders of Israel came to inquire of the Lord, and they sat before me.

**20:2** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**20:3** "Son of man, speak to the elders of Israel, and you will say to them, 'This is what the Lord God says: Have you really come to inquire of Me? As I live, I will not answer you, says the Lord God.'

**20:4** If you judge them, if you judge, son of man, show them the abominations of their fathers.

**20:5** And you will say to them, 'This is what the Lord God says: On the day when I chose Israel, and lifted up My hand for the offspring of the house of Jacob, and appeared to them in the land of Egypt, and lifted up My hand for them, saying, "I am the Lord your God,"

**20:6** on that day I lifted up My hand for them to lead them out of the land of Egypt into a land that I had provided for them, flowing with milk and honey, which is outstanding among all lands.

**20:7** And I said to them, "Let each one cast away the offenses of his eyes, and do not be defiled with the idols of Egypt; I am the Lord your God."

**20:8** And they provoked Me and were unwilling to listen to Me; not one cast aside the abominations of his eyes, nor did they abandon the idols of Egypt. And I said that I would pour out My indignation upon them and fulfill My anger against them in the midst of the land of Egypt.

**20:9** And I acted for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned before the nations in whose midst they were, and among whom I appeared to them to lead them out of the land of Egypt.

**20:10** Therefore I cast them out of the land of Egypt, and I led them out into the desert.

**20:11** And I gave them My precepts, and I showed them My judgments, by doing which a man will live in them.

**20:12** Moreover, I also gave them My Sabbaths, so that they would be a sign between Me and them, and they would know that I am the Lord who sanctifies them.

**20:13** And the house of Israel provoked Me in the desert: they did not walk in My precepts, and they cast aside My judgments, by doing which a man will live in them, and they greatly profaned My Sabbaths. Therefore I said that I would pour out My fury upon them in the desert and consume them;

**20:14** and I acted for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned before the nations from which I cast them out in their sight.

**20:15** I therefore lifted up My hand over them in the desert, that I would not bring them into the land that I gave them, flowing with milk and honey, the foremost of all lands,

**20:16** because they cast aside My judgments, and did not walk in My precepts, and profaned My Sabbaths; for their heart was going after idols.

**20:17** And My eye spared them, so that I would not kill them, nor did I consume them in the desert.

**20:18** But I said to their sons in the wilderness, "Do not walk in the precepts of your fathers, nor keep their judgments, nor be defiled with their idols.

**20:19** I am the Lord your God: walk in My precepts, keep My judgments and do them,

**20:20** and sanctify My Sabbaths, so that they may be a sign between Me and you, and you may know that I am the Lord your God."

**20:21** And the sons exasperated Me: they did not walk in My precepts, and they did not keep My judgments to do them, which, when a man has done, he will live in them, and they profaned My Sabbaths. And I threatened to pour out My fury upon them and fulfill My anger against them in the desert.

**20:22** But I turned away My hand and acted for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned before the nations from which I cast them out before their eyes.

**20:23** Again I lifted up My hand against them in the wilderness, to scatter them among the nations and winnow them into the lands,

**20:24** because they had not done My judgments, and had rejected My precepts, and had profaned My Sabbaths, and their eyes had been after the idols of their fathers.

**20:25** Therefore I also gave them precepts that were not good, and judgments in which they will not live.

**20:26** And I defiled them in their gifts, when they offered everything that opens the womb, on account of their sins; and they will know that I am the Lord.'

**20:27** For this reason, speak to the house of Israel, son of man, and you will say to them, 'This is what the Lord God says: Yet in this also your fathers blasphemed Me, when they had spurned Me with contempt;

**20:28** and when I had brought them into the land over which I lifted up My hand to give it to them, they saw every high hill and every leafy tree, and they sacrificed their victims there, and they gave there the provocation of their offering, and they placed there the aroma of their sweetness, and they poured out their libations.

**20:29** And I said to them, "What is the high place to which you go in?" And its name has been called High Place to this day.'

**20:30** Therefore say to the house of Israel, 'This is what the Lord God says: Surely in the way of your fathers you defile yourselves, and you commit fornication after their stumbling blocks,

**20:31** and in the offering of your gifts, when you pass your sons through the fire, you defile yourselves with all your idols until today; and will I answer you, house of Israel? As I live, says the Lord God, I will not answer you.

**20:32** Nor will the thought of your mind come about, you who say, "We will be like the nations and like the clans of the earth, to worship wood and stones."

**20:33** As I live, says the Lord God, with a strong hand, and with an outstretched arm, and with fury poured out, I will reign over you.

**20:34** And I will lead you out from the peoples, and I will gather you from the lands in which you have been scattered; with a mighty hand, and with an outstretched arm, and with fury poured out, I will reign over you.

**20:35** And I will bring you into the desert of the peoples, and I will enter into judgment with you there face to face.

**20:36** Just as I contended in judgment against your fathers in the desert of the land of Egypt, so I will judge you, says the Lord God.

**20:37** And I will subject you to My scepter, and I will bring you in the bonds of the covenant.

**20:38** And I will pick out from among you the transgressors and the ungodly, and I will lead them out of the land of their sojourning, and they will not enter the land of Israel; and you will know that I am the Lord.

**20:39** And as for you, house of Israel, this is what the Lord God says: Each of you, walk after your idols and serve them. But if even in this you do not listen to Me, and you defile My holy name any longer with your gifts and with your idols,

**20:40** on My holy mountain, on the high mountain of Israel, says the Lord God, there all the house of Israel will serve Me, all of them, I say, in the land in which they will be pleasing to Me; and there I will require your firstfruits and the beginning of your tithes, in all your consecrated things.

**20:41** As an aroma of sweetness I will accept you, when I have led you out from the peoples and have gathered you from the lands into which you have been scattered; and I will be sanctified in you before the eyes of the nations.

**20:42** And you will know that I am the Lord, when I have brought you to the land of Israel, into the land for which I lifted up My hand to give it to your fathers.

**20:43** And there you will remember your ways and all your wicked deeds, by which you have been defiled in them; and you will be displeasing to yourselves in your own sight for all your evils that you have done.

**20:44** And you will know that I am the Lord, when I have done good to you for the sake of My name, and not according to your evil ways, nor according to your most wicked deeds, house of Israel, says the Lord God.'"

**20:45** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**20:46** "Son of man, set your face against the way of the south, and let words drop toward the southwest, and prophesy to the forest of the southern field.

**20:47** And you will say to the southern forest, 'Hear the word of the Lord. This is what the Lord God says: "Look, I will kindle a fire in you, and I will burn up in you every green tree and every dry tree. The flame of the burning will not be extinguished, and every face from the south to the north will be burned up in it,

**20:48** and all flesh will see that I, the Lord, have kindled it, and it will not be extinguished."'"

**20:49** And I said, "Ah, ah, ah, Lord God! They themselves say of me, 'Does this man not speak in parables?'"

## Translator Notes

- **20:4** — Latin "Si judicas eos, si judicas" ("If you judge them, if you judge") renders the Hebrew interrogative ("Will you judge them?") as a conditional clause.
- **20:26** — Latin "propter delicta sua" ("on account of their sins") is a Vulgate-distinct reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "so that I might devastate them."
- **20:29** — The Vulgate translates both the common noun and the proper name of the Hebrew wordplay on Bamah into Latin as "excelsum" and "Excelsum" ("high place" and "High Place").
- **20:34** — The Clementine Vulgate repeats the closing clause "regnabo super vos" ("I will reign over you") from verse 33; this repetition does not appear in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **20:39** — Unlike the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("you shall no longer profane My holy name"), the Clementine Vulgate has no negation before "pollueritis" ("you defile"), making it part of the conditional clause leading into verse 40.
- **20:45** — In native Clementine Vulgate numbering (Clem1598-USFX), the southern forest prophecy closes chapter 20 as 20:45-49 (mapped to Saints.Bible 21:1-5), and chapter 21 begins with the sword against Jerusalem as 21:1-32 (mapped to Saints.Bible 21:6-37).
- **20:46** — Latin "stilla" literally means "drip" or "drop," a prophetic idiom for uttering words (repeated in 21:7). The Vulgate also distinguishes three southerly terms: "auster" (south), "africus" (southwest), and "meridianus" (southern).
