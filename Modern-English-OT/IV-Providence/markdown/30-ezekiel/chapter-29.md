## Text

**29:1** In the tenth year, in the tenth month, on the eleventh day of the month, the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**29:2** "Son of man, set your face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and you will prophesy concerning him and concerning all Egypt.

**29:3** Speak, and you will say, 'This is what the Lord God says: Look, I am against you, Pharaoh king of Egypt, great dragon, who lie in the midst of your rivers and say, "The river is mine, and I made myself."

**29:4** And I will put a bridle in your jaws, and I will fasten the fish of your rivers to your scales, and I will draw you out from the midst of your rivers, and all your fish will cling to your scales.

**29:5** And I will cast you out into the desert, and all the fish of your river; you will fall upon the face of the earth; you will not be collected, nor will you be gathered; I have given you to the beasts of the earth and to the birds of the sky to be devoured.

**29:6** And all the inhabitants of Egypt will know that I am the Lord, because you have been a staff of reed to the house of Israel:

**29:7** when they grasped you with the hand, you were broken, and you tore their whole shoulder; and when they leaned upon you, you were crushed, and you weakened all their loins.

**29:8** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: Look, I will bring a sword upon you, and I will kill from you man and beast.

**29:9** And the land of Egypt will become a desert and a desolation; and they will know that I am the Lord, because you have said, "The river is mine, and I made it."

**29:10** For this reason, look, I am against you and against your rivers; and I will make the land of Egypt into desolations, devastated by the sword, from the tower of Syene as far as the borders of Ethiopia.

**29:11** The foot of man will not pass through it, nor will the foot of beast walk in it, and it will not be inhabited for forty years.

**29:12** And I will make the land of Egypt deserted in the midst of deserted lands, and its cities in the midst of overthrown cities, and they will be desolate for forty years; and I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations, and I will winnow them into the lands.

**29:13** For this is what the Lord God says: After the end of forty years I will gather Egypt from the peoples among whom they had been scattered.

**29:14** And I will lead back the captivity of Egypt, and I will settle them in the land of Pathros, in the land of their birth, and there they will become a lowly kingdom.

**29:15** Among the other kingdoms it will be the lowliest, and it will no longer be lifted up above the nations; and I will diminish them so that they will not rule over the peoples.

**29:16** Nor will they any longer be a confidence to the house of Israel, teaching iniquity so that they flee and follow them; and they will know that I am the Lord God.'"

**29:17** And it happened in the twenty-seventh year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, that the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**29:18** "Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon made his army serve with a great service against Tyre; every head was made bald, and every shoulder was rubbed bare; and no payment was rendered to him, nor to his army, from Tyre, for the service with which he served Me against it.

**29:19** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: Look, I will place Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon in the land of Egypt; and he will take its multitude, and he will plunder its booty, and he will seize its spoils; and it will be payment for his army,

**29:20** and for the work with which he served against it: I have given him the land of Egypt because he labored for Me, says the Lord God.

**29:21** In that day a horn will sprout for the house of Israel, and to you I will give an open mouth in their midst, and they will know that I am the Lord."

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — Vulgate reads "eleventh day" (undecima die), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "twelfth day."
- **29:3** — Vulgate reads "and I made myself" (et ego feci memetipsum), emphasizing Pharaoh's boast of self-creation (contrast v. 9, et ego feci eum, "and I made it"), whereas the Hebrew can be read "I made it for myself."
- **29:10** — Vulgate renders Hebrew mi-migdol seveneh ("from Migdol to Syene") as "from the tower of Syene" (a turre Syenes), taking migdol as the common noun "tower," and uses the classical name "Ethiopia" (Aethiopiae) for Hebrew Cush.
- **29:16** — Vulgate reads "teaching iniquity so that they flee and follow them" (docentes iniquitatem ut fugiant, et sequantur eos), whereas the Hebrew reads "bringing iniquity to remembrance when they turn after them."
- **29:19** — Latin literally reads dabo Nabuchodonosor regem Babylonis in terra Aegypti ("I will give/place Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon in the land of Egypt"), making Nebuchadnezzar the direct object rather than the indirect object as in v. 20 (dedi ei terram Aegypti). In v. 18, the Vulgate also adds mihi ("served Me").
