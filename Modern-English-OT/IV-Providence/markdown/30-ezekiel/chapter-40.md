## Text

**40:1** In the twenty-fifth year of our exile, at the beginning of the year, on the tenth of the month, in the fourteenth year after the city was struck, on this very day, the hand of the Lord came upon me, and He brought me there.

**40:2** In visions of God He brought me into the land of Israel, and set me down on a very high mountain, on which was something like the building of a city facing toward the south.

**40:3** And He brought me in there, and look, a man whose appearance was like the appearance of bronze, and a linen cord was in his hand, and a measuring reed in his hand; and he was standing in the gate.

**40:4** And the same man spoke to me, "Son of man, see with your eyes, and hear with your ears, and set your heart on all that I will show you, for you have been brought here so that they may be shown to you. Declare all that you see to the house of Israel."

**40:5** And look, a wall on the outside around the house on every side, and in the man's hand was a measuring reed of six cubits and a handbreadth; and he measured the width of the building, one reed, and the height also, one reed.

**40:6** And he came to the gate that faced the eastern way, and went up by its steps, and he measured the threshold of the gate, one reed in width, that is, one threshold, one reed in width.

**40:7** And a chamber, one reed in length and one reed in width; and between the chambers, five cubits.

**40:8** And the threshold of the gate beside the vestibule of the gate on the inside, one reed.

**40:9** And he measured the vestibule of the gate, eight cubits, and its front, two cubits; and the vestibule of the gate was on the inside.

**40:10** Now the chambers of the gate toward the eastern way were three on this side and three on that side; there was one measurement for the three, and one measurement for the fronts on both sides.

**40:11** And he measured the width of the threshold of the gate, ten cubits, and the length of the gate, thirteen cubits.

**40:12** And a border in front of the chambers, one cubit, and one cubit was the boundary on each side; and the chambers were six cubits on this side and on that side.

**40:13** And he measured the gate from the roof of a chamber to its roof, a width of twenty-five cubits, doorway opposite doorway.

**40:14** And he made fronts for sixty cubits, and up to the front was the court of the gate on every side all around.

**40:15** And before the face of the gate that reached all the way to the face of the vestibule of the inner gate, fifty cubits.

**40:16** And slanted windows in the chambers and in their fronts, which were inside the gate on every side all around; and likewise there were also windows in the vestibules all around on the inside, and before the fronts a depiction of palm trees.

**40:17** And he brought me out to the outer court, and look, treasury chambers and a pavement laid with stone in the court all around: thirty treasury chambers around the pavement.

**40:18** And the pavement at the front of the gates, according to the length of the gates, was lower.

**40:19** And he measured the width from the face of the lower gate to the front of the inner court on the outside, one hundred cubits to the east and to the north.

**40:20** He also measured the gate of the outer court that faced the northern way, both in length and in width.

**40:21** And its chambers, three on this side and three on that side, and its front and its vestibule, according to the measurement of the first gate: its length fifty cubits, and its width twenty-five cubits.

**40:22** And its windows, and vestibule, and carvings were according to the measurement of the gate that faced toward the east; and its ascent was seven steps, and the vestibule was before it.

**40:23** And the gate of the inner court was opposite the northern gate and the eastern one; and he measured from gate to gate one hundred cubits.

**40:24** And he brought me out to the southern way, and look, a gate that faced toward the south; and he measured its front and its vestibule according to the previous measurements.

**40:25** And its windows, and vestibules all around, like the other windows: fifty cubits in length, and twenty-five cubits in width.

**40:26** And by seven steps one went up to it, and the vestibule was before its doors; and there were engraved palm trees, one on this side and the other on that side, on its front.

**40:27** And there was a gate of the inner court on the southern way; and he measured from gate to gate on the southern way, one hundred cubits.

**40:28** And he brought me into the inner court to the southern gate, and he measured the gate according to the previous measurements.

**40:29** Its chamber, and its front, and its vestibule with the same measurements, and its windows, and its vestibule all around: fifty cubits in length, and twenty-five cubits in width.

**40:31** And its vestibule was toward the outer court, and its palm trees were on the front; and there were eight steps by which one went up through it.

**40:32** And he brought me into the inner court by the eastern way, and he measured the gate according to the previous measurements.

**40:33** Its chamber, and its front, and its vestibule, as above, and its windows, and its vestibules all around: fifty cubits in length, and twenty-five cubits in width.

**40:34** And its vestibule, that is, of the outer court; and engraved palm trees were on its front, on this side and on that side; and its ascent was by eight steps.

**40:35** And he brought me to the gate that faced toward the north, and he measured according to the previous measurements.

**40:36** Its chamber, and its front, and its vestibule, and its windows all around: fifty cubits in length, and twenty-five cubits in width.

**40:37** And its vestibule faced toward the outer court, and there was an engraving of palm trees on its front, on this side and on that side; and its ascent was by eight steps.

**40:38** And through each treasury chamber was a doorway in the fronts of the gates; there they washed the burnt offering.

**40:39** And in the vestibule of the gate, two tables on this side and two tables on that side, so that the burnt offering may be sacrificed on them, and the offering for sin and for guilt.

**40:40** And at the outer side, which goes up to the doorway of the gate that leads to the north, were two tables; and at the other side, before the vestibule of the gate, two tables.

**40:41** Four tables on this side and four tables on that side: along the sides of the gate there were eight tables on which they sacrificed.

**40:42** And four tables for the burnt offering were built of squared stones, one and a half cubits in length, and one and a half cubits in width, and one cubit in height, on which they may place the instruments with which the burnt offering and the victim are sacrificed.

**40:43** And their rims were of one handbreadth, turned inward all around; and on the tables was the flesh of the offering.

**40:44** And outside the inner gate were the treasury chambers of the singers in the inner court, which was at the side of the gate facing toward the north, and their faces were toward the southern way; one was at the side of the eastern gate, facing the northern way.

**40:45** And he said to me, "This is the treasury chamber that faces the southern way; it will belong to the priests who keep watch in the charges of the temple.

**40:46** Moreover, the treasury chamber that faces toward the northern way will belong to the priests who keep watch for the service of the altar; these are the sons of Zadok, who from among the sons of Levi draw near to the Lord to minister to Him."

**40:47** And he measured the court, one hundred cubits in length and one hundred cubits in width, square; and the altar was before the face of the temple.

**40:48** And he brought me into the vestibule of the temple, and he measured the vestibule, five cubits on this side and five cubits on that side, and the width of the gate, three cubits on this side and three cubits on that side.

**40:49** And the length of the vestibule was twenty cubits, and the width eleven cubits, and by eight steps one went up to it. And there were columns on the fronts, one on this side and the other on that side.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**40:30** *(after `40:29`)* And a vestibule all around, twenty-five cubits in length and five cubits in width.

## Translator Notes

- **40:5** — Latin "sex cubitorum et palmo" literally gives a total length of six cubits and a handbreadth, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text specifies six long cubits, each a cubit plus a handbreadth; Latin "ædificii" also reads "of the building" rather than "of the wall."
- **40:9** — Throughout this chapter, the Vulgate consistently translates the Hebrew architectural term for the gate's jambs or pilasters as "frons" ("front").
- **40:17** — Latin "gazophylacia" literally denotes treasury chambers or storerooms, the Vulgate's consistent rendering for the temple courtyard chambers, distinct from "thalami" ("chambers") inside the gatehouses.
- **40:43** — Latin labia ("rims," literally "lips") renders a rare Hebrew term of uncertain meaning.
