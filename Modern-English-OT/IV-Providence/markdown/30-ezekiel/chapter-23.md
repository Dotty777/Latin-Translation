## Text

**23:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**23:2** "Son of man, there were two women, daughters of one mother;

**23:3** and they committed fornication in Egypt; in their youth they committed fornication. There their breasts were pressed, and the breasts of their puberty were crushed.

**23:4** Now their names were Oholah the older, and Oholibah her younger sister; and I possessed them, and they bore sons and daughters. Furthermore, as for their names, Samaria is Oholah, and Jerusalem is Oholibah.

**23:5** Therefore Oholah committed fornication against Me, and went mad over her lovers, over the Assyrians drawing near,

**23:6** clothed in violet, princes and magistrates, young men of desire, all of them horsemen, riders of horses.

**23:7** And she bestowed her fornications upon those chosen ones, all the sons of the Assyrians; and with all those over whom she went mad, she was defiled in their impurities.

**23:8** Moreover, she did not abandon her fornications that she had had in Egypt; for they also slept with her in her youth, and they crushed the breasts of her puberty, and poured out their fornication upon her.

**23:9** Therefore I delivered her into the hands of her lovers, into the hands of the sons of Assyria, over whose lust she went mad.

**23:10** They themselves uncovered her disgrace; they took away her sons and daughters, and her they killed with the sword. And the women became notorious, and they executed judgments upon her.

**23:11** When her sister Oholibah saw this, she went mad with lust more than she did, and her fornication, beyond the fornication of her sister,

**23:12** she shamelessly offered to the sons of the Assyrians, to commanders and magistrates coming to her, clothed in multicolored attire, to horsemen who rode on horses, and to young men, all of them outstanding in appearance.

**23:13** And I saw that the single way of both of them was defiled.

**23:14** And she increased her fornications; and when she saw men depicted on the wall, images of the Chaldeans portrayed in colors,

**23:15** and girded with belts around their waists, and with dyed turbans on their heads, the appearance of commanders, all of them, the likeness of the sons of Babylon and of the land of the Chaldeans in which they were born,

**23:16** she went mad over them with the craving of her eyes, and she sent messengers to them in Chaldea.

**23:17** And when the sons of Babylon came to her, to the bed of breasts, they defiled her with their debaucheries; and she was defiled by them, and her soul was satiated by them.

**23:18** She also laid bare her fornications, and uncovered her disgrace; and My soul withdrew from her, just as My soul had withdrawn from her sister.

**23:19** For she multiplied her fornications, remembering the days of her youth, in which she committed fornication in the land of Egypt.

**23:20** And she went mad with lust over intercourse with those whose flesh is like the flesh of donkeys, and like the emission of horses is their emission.

**23:21** And you revisited the wickedness of your youth, when your breasts were pressed in Egypt, and the breasts of your puberty were crushed.

**23:22** Therefore, Oholibah, this is what the Lord God says: 'Look, I will raise up all your lovers against you, with whom your soul has been satiated, and I will gather them against you all around:

**23:23** the sons of Babylon and all the Chaldeans, nobles, and tyrants, and princes, all the sons of the Assyrians, young men outstanding in appearance, all the commanders and magistrates, princes of princes, and renowned riders of horses.

**23:24** And they will come upon you equipped with chariot and wheel, a multitude of peoples; with breastplate, and shield, and helmet they will be armed against you on every side. And I will set judgment before them, and they will judge you by their judgments.

**23:25** And I will set My jealousy against you, which they exercise with you in fury: they will cut off your nose and your ears, and whatever remains will fall by the sword. They themselves will seize your sons and your daughters, and your last remnant will be devoured by fire.

**23:26** And they will strip you of your garments, and take away the vessels of your glory.

**23:27** And I will make your wickedness cease from you, and your fornication from the land of Egypt; and you will not lift up your eyes to them, and you will remember Egypt no more.'

**23:28** For this is what the Lord God says: 'Look, I will deliver you into the hands of those whom you hate, into the hands of those with whom your soul has been satiated.

**23:29** And they will deal with you in hatred, and take away all your labors, and leave you naked and full of disgrace; and the disgrace of your fornications will be revealed, your wickedness and your fornications.

**23:30** They have done these things to you, because you committed fornication after the nations, among whom you were defiled in their idols.

**23:31** You have walked in the way of your sister, and I will put her cup in your hand.'

**23:32** This is what the Lord God says: 'You will drink your sister's cup, deep and wide; you will become a mockery and a derision, which is most capacious.

**23:33** You will be filled with drunkenness and sorrow, with the cup of grief and sadness, the cup of your sister Samaria.

**23:34** And you will drink it, and drain it down to the dregs; and you will devour its fragments, and you will tear your breasts, for I have spoken,' says the Lord God.

**23:35** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: 'Because you have forgotten Me and have cast Me behind your body, you also bear your wickedness and your fornications.'"

**23:36** And the Lord said to me, saying: "Son of man, do you judge Oholah and Oholibah, and declare to them their wicked deeds?

**23:37** For they have committed adultery, and blood is on their hands, and they have committed fornication with their idols; moreover, even their own sons whom they bore to Me, they offered to them to be devoured.

**23:38** And this also they did to Me: they defiled My sanctuary on that day, and profaned My Sabbaths.

**23:39** And when they sacrificed their sons to their idols, and entered My sanctuary on that day to defile it, they did even these things in the midst of My house.

**23:40** They sent to men coming from far away, to whom they had sent a messenger; and so, look, they came, for whom you washed yourself, and painted around your eyes with antimony, and were adorned with women's finery.

**23:41** You sat on a most beautiful couch, and a table was adorned before you; My incense and My ointment you placed upon it.

**23:42** And the voice of an exulting multitude was in her; and among the men who were brought from a multitude of people and came from the desert, they put bracelets on their hands and splendid crowns on their heads.

**23:43** And I said to her who was worn out in adulteries: 'Now even this one will commit fornication in her fornication.'

**23:44** And they went in to her as to a prostitute woman; so they kept going in to Oholah and Oholibah, vile women.

**23:45** Therefore they are righteous men; these will judge them by the judgment of adulteresses and by the judgment of those who shed blood, because they are adulteresses, and blood is on their hands.

**23:46** For this is what the Lord God says: 'Bring a multitude against them, and deliver them over to tumult and to plunder.

**23:47** And let them be stoned with the stones of the peoples, and let them be pierced through with their swords; they will kill their sons and daughters, and burn their houses with fire.

**23:48** And I will remove wickedness from the land, and all women will learn not to act according to their wickedness.

**23:49** And they will place your wickedness upon you, and you will bear the sins of your idols; and you will know that I am the Lord God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **23:10** — The Vulgate reads plural "and the women became notorious" (factae sunt famosae mulieres), whereas the Hebrew text reads singular ("she became a byword among women").
- **23:17** — The Vulgate translates "to the bed of breasts" (ad cubile mammarum), reading the Hebrew consonantal term for "love" as "breasts," and renders "her soul was satiated by them" (saturata est) where the Hebrew means "alienated" (similarly in vv. 22 and 28).
- **23:23** — The Vulgate translates three titles as common nouns ("nobles, and tyrants, and princes") where the Hebrew text lists proper names of Mesopotamian tribes or regions (Pekod, Shoa, and Koa).
- **23:32** — In Latin, the feminine relative clause "which is most capacious" (quae est capacissima) attaches grammatically to feminine "derision" (subsannationem) because Latin "cup" (calix) is masculine, whereas in the Hebrew it refers to the large capacity of the feminine "cup."
- **23:42** — The Vulgate renders "came from the desert" (veniebant de deserto) where the Hebrew contains a disputed term (read either as "drunkards" or as "Sabeans"), and uses the masculine pronoun (eorum) for "their hands" and "their heads."
