## Text

**12:1** The burden of the word of the Lord upon Israel. The Lord, who stretches out heaven and lays the foundation of the earth and forms the spirit of man within him, says:

**12:2** "Look, I will make Jerusalem a lintel of drunkenness for all the surrounding peoples; and Judah also will be in the siege against Jerusalem.

**12:3** And it will be on that day: I will make Jerusalem a stone of burden for all the peoples; all who lift it will be torn with gashes, and all the kingdoms of the earth will be gathered against it.

**12:4** On that day," says the Lord, "I will strike every horse with stupor and its rider with madness; and over the house of Judah I will open My eyes, and I will strike every horse of the peoples with blindness.

**12:5** And the leaders of Judah will say in their heart, 'May the inhabitants of Jerusalem be strengthened for me in the Lord of hosts, their God!'

**12:6** On that day I will make the leaders of Judah like a furnace of fire among wood and like a torch of fire in hay; and they will devour to the right and to the left all the surrounding peoples, and Jerusalem will be inhabited again in its own place, in Jerusalem.

**12:7** And the Lord will save the tents of Judah as in the beginning, so that the house of David and the glory of the inhabitants of Jerusalem may not boast grandly against Judah.

**12:8** On that day the Lord will protect the inhabitants of Jerusalem; and the one among them who stumbles on that day will be like David, and the house of David like that of God, like the angel of the Lord in their sight.

**12:9** And it will be on that day: I will seek to crush all the nations that come against Jerusalem.

**12:10** And I will pour out upon the house of David and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem a spirit of grace and of prayers; and they will look to Me, whom they have pierced, and they will mourn for Him with mourning as over an only-begotten son, and they will grieve over Him as one customarily grieves at the death of a firstborn.

**12:11** On that day there will be great mourning in Jerusalem, like the mourning of Hadad-rimmon in the plain of Megiddo.

**12:12** And the land will mourn, families by families separately: the families of the house of David separately, and their wives separately;

**12:13** the families of the house of Nathan separately, and their wives separately; the families of the house of Levi separately, and their wives separately; the families of Shimei separately, and their wives separately;

**12:14** all the remaining families, families by families separately, and their wives separately."

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew sap (which can mean either "bowl/cup" or "threshold/lintel") as superliminare ("lintel"), giving superliminare crapulae ("a lintel of drunkenness").
- **12:5** — The Vulgate reads a passive jussive verb, Confortentur ("May ... be strengthened"), where the Hebrew has a noun ("are a strength to me").
- **12:8** — The Vulgate uses the genitive quasi Dei ("like that of God," referring back to "the house"), whereas the Hebrew reads "like God."
- **12:10** — The Vulgate reads ut doleri solet in morte primogeniti ("as one customarily grieves at the death of a firstborn"), making "at the death of" explicit where the Hebrew reads "like bitter weeping over a firstborn."
