## Text

**9:1** The burden of the word of the Lord in the land of Hadrach and of Damascus, its rest; for the eye of man and of all the tribes of Israel belongs to the Lord.

**9:2** Hamath also is within its borders, and Tyre and Sidon; for indeed they have greatly taken wisdom to themselves.

**9:3** And Tyre built her stronghold, and heaped up silver like soil, and gold like the mud of the streets.

**9:4** Look, the Lord will possess her, and He will strike her strength in the sea, and she herself will be devoured by fire.

**9:5** Ashkelon will see and be afraid, and Gaza, and she will grieve exceedingly, and Ekron, because her hope has been confounded; and the king will perish from Gaza, and Ashkelon will not be inhabited.

**9:6** And a separator will sit in Ashdod, and I will destroy the pride of the Philistines.

**9:7** And I will take away his blood from his mouth, and his abominations from between his teeth; and he also will be left for our God, and he will be like a leader in Judah, and Ekron like a Jebusite.

**9:8** And I will surround My house with those who serve as soldiers for Me, going and returning; and an oppressor will no longer pass over them, for now I have seen with My eyes.

**9:9** Exult greatly, daughter of Zion; shout for joy, daughter of Jerusalem: look, your king will come to you, righteous and a savior; He Himself is poor, and riding on a female donkey and on a colt, the foal of a female donkey.

**9:10** And I will destroy the chariot from Ephraim, and the horse from Jerusalem, and the bow of war will be shattered; and He will speak peace to the nations, and His authority will be from sea to sea, and from the rivers to the ends of the earth.

**9:11** You also, by the blood of Your covenant, have released Your prisoners from the pit in which there is no water.

**9:12** Turn back to the stronghold, prisoners of hope; today also I announce that I will repay double to you.

**9:13** For I have bent Judah for Myself like a bow; I have filled Ephraim; and I will rouse up your sons, Zion, against your sons, Greece, and I will make you like the sword of mighty men.

**9:14** And the Lord God will be seen over them, and His javelin will go out like lightning; and the Lord God will sound the trumpet, and will go in the whirlwind of the south.

**9:15** The Lord of hosts will protect them; and they will devour, and subdue with the stones of the sling; and drinking, they will become drunk as if from wine, and they will be filled like bowls, and like the horns of the altar.

**9:16** And the Lord their God will save them on that day, as the flock of His people, for holy stones will be lifted up over His land.

**9:17** For what is His goodness, and what is His beauty, except the grain of the elect, and wine bringing forth virgins?

## Translator Notes

- **9:6** — The Vulgate translates the rare Hebrew term mamzer ("mixed people" or "illegitimate one") as separator ("a separator"), reflecting an etymological interpretation of the word as one who separates or estranges.
- **9:8** — Rather than camping "because of an army, because of those passing back and forth," the Vulgate renders circumdabo domum meam ex his qui militant mihi euntes et revertentes ("I will surround My house with those who serve as soldiers for Me, going and returning"), portraying protective forces patrolling God's house.
- **9:9** — The Vulgate reads salvator ("a savior," active, matching the Septuagint) rather than the passive Hebrew "saved/victorious," translates "afflicted/humble" as pauper ("poor"), and reads the feminine asinam ("a female donkey") where the Hebrew has the masculine "donkey."
- **9:11** — Whereas the Hebrew has the first person ("I have set your prisoners free"), the Vulgate uses the second-person verb emisisti ("you have released"); the Latin does not name the one addressed.
- **9:17** — The Vulgate frames the verse as a single rhetorical question with nisi ("except") and renders Hebrew "grain [for] young men" as frumentum electorum ("the grain of the elect") alongside vinum germinans virgines ("wine bringing forth virgins").
