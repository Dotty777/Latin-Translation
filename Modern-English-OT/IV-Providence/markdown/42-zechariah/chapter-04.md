## Text

**4:1** And the angel who was speaking in me returned, and roused me like a man who is roused from his sleep.

**4:2** And he said to me, "What do you see?" And I said, "I have seen, and look, a lampstand entirely of gold, and its lamp on top of it, and its seven oil lamps on it, and seven funnels for the oil lamps that were on top of it,

**4:3** and two olive trees above it, one on the right of the lamp, and one on its left."

**4:4** And I answered and said to the angel who was speaking in me, saying, "What are these, my lord?"

**4:5** And the angel who was speaking in me answered and said to me, "Do you not know what these are?" And I said, "No, my lord."

**4:6** And he answered and said to me, saying, "This is the word of the Lord to Zerubbabel, saying, 'Not by an army, nor by strength, but by My Spirit,' says the Lord of hosts.

**4:7** 'Who are you, great mountain, before Zerubbabel? Into a plain! And he will bring out the primary stone, and he will make grace equal to its grace.'"

**4:8** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**4:9** "The hands of Zerubbabel founded this house, and his hands will complete it; and you will know that the Lord of hosts has sent me to you.

**4:10** For who has despised the small days? And they will rejoice and see the tin stone in the hand of Zerubbabel. These seven are the eyes of the Lord, which range throughout the whole earth."

**4:11** And I answered and said to him, "What are these two olive trees at the right of the lampstand and at its left?"

**4:12** And I answered a second time and said to him, "What are the two olive sprigs that are beside the two golden spouts in which are pouring pipes of gold?"

**4:13** And he said to me, saying, "Do you not know what these are?" And I said, "No, my lord."

**4:14** And he said, "These are the two sons of oil who stand beside the Ruler of the whole earth."

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — Latin "in me" literally translates the Hebrew preposition denoting interior prophetic speech, often rendered "with me" in modern versions.
- **4:2** — The Vulgate distinguishes the main top "lamp" ("lampas", for Hebrew "bowl") from the seven "oil lamps" ("lucernae") and reads "seven funnels" ("septem infusoria") where the Hebrew text doubles the number ("seven and seven pipes").
- **4:7** — The Vulgate uniquely renders the Hebrew phrase for shouts of "Grace, grace to it!" as "he will make grace equal to its grace" ("exaequabit gratiam gratiae ejus"), deriving the Hebrew noun from a root meaning to level or equalize.
- **4:10** — The Vulgate has the plural "small days" ("dies parvos", vs. Hebrew "day of small things") and renders the plumb line literally as "the tin stone" ("lapidem stanneum"), referring to a weighted tin plumb bob.
- **4:12** — Latin "spicae" literally means "ears" or "spikes" (here fruiting olive sprigs), and "in which are pouring pipes of gold" ("in quibus sunt suffusoria ex auro") is a Vulgate-distinct rendering where the Hebrew text refers to pipes that empty out golden oil.
