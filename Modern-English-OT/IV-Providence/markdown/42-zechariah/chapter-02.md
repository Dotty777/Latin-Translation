## Text

**2:1** And I lifted up my eyes and saw, and look, a man, and in his hand was a measurers' cord.

**2:2** And I said, "Where are you going?" And he said to me, "To measure Jerusalem, and to see how great its width is and how great its length is."

**2:3** And look, the angel who was speaking in me was going out, and another angel was going out to meet him;

**2:4** and he said to him, "Run, speak to that young man, saying, 'Jerusalem will be inhabited without a wall, because of the multitude of people and livestock in its midst.

**2:5** And I will be to it, says the Lord, a wall of fire all around, and I will be in glory in its midst.'"

**2:6** "Oh! Oh! Flee from the land of the north," says the Lord, "for I have dispersed you into the four winds of heaven," says the Lord.

**2:7** Oh, Zion! Flee, you who dwell with the daughter of Babylon,

**2:8** for this is what the Lord of hosts says: "After glory He has sent Me to the nations that plundered you; for whoever touches you touches the pupil of My eye.

**2:9** For look, I raise My hand over them, and they will become plunder for those who were serving them; and you will know that the Lord of hosts has sent Me.

**2:10** Give praise and rejoice, daughter of Zion, for look, I am coming, and I will dwell in your midst," says the Lord.

**2:11** "And many nations will be joined to the Lord in that day, and they will be a people to Me, and I will dwell in your midst; and you will know that the Lord of hosts has sent Me to you."

**2:12** And the Lord will possess Judah as His portion in the sanctified land, and He will again choose Jerusalem.

**2:13** Let all flesh be silent before the face of the Lord, for He has risen up from His holy dwelling.

## Translator Notes

- **2:8** — The phrase Post gloriam ("After glory") is debated, understood either as "after restoring glory" or "afterward, in glory." Latin pupillam oculi mei ("the pupil of My eye") reads first-person "My eye," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has third-person "his eye." Native Vulgate 2:8 maps to Saints.Bible 2:12. Capitalization of 'Me' in 'He has sent Me' (against lowercase 'me' at 4:9 and 6:15) reflects a divine-envoy reading and is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **2:9** — Native Vulgate 2:9 maps to Saints.Bible 2:13. Capitalization of 'Me' in 'has sent Me' (against lowercase 'me' at 4:9 and 6:15) is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **2:10** — Native Vulgate 2:10 maps to Saints.Bible 2:14. Speaker identity ('I am coming') connects directly to the 'sent Me' question in 2:8-9 and 2:11.
- **2:11** — Native Vulgate 2:11 maps to Saints.Bible 2:15. Capitalization of 'Me' in 'has sent Me' (against lowercase 'me' at 4:9 and 6:15) is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **2:12** — Latin in terra sanctificata ("in the sanctified land") uses the passive participle "sanctified," distinct from the simple adjective "holy" (sancto) in 2:17.
