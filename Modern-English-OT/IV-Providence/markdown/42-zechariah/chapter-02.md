## Text

**2:1** And I lifted up my eyes and saw, and look, four horns.

**2:2** And I said to the angel who was speaking in me, "What are these?" And he said to me, "These are the horns that scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem."

**2:3** And the Lord showed me four craftsmen.

**2:4** And I said, "What are these coming to do?" And he spoke, saying, "These are the horns that scattered Judah, man by man, and none of them lifted up his head; and these have come to terrify them, so that they may cast down the horns of the nations that lifted up a horn over the land of Judah to disperse it."

**2:5** And I lifted up my eyes and saw, and look, a man, and in his hand was a measurers' cord.

**2:6** And I said, "Where are you going?" And he said to me, "To measure Jerusalem, and to see how great its width is and how great its length is."

**2:7** And look, the angel who was speaking in me was going out, and another angel was going out to meet him;

**2:8** and he said to him, "Run, speak to that young man, saying, 'Jerusalem will be inhabited without a wall, because of the multitude of people and livestock in its midst.

**2:9** And I will be to it, says the Lord, a wall of fire all around, and I will be in glory in its midst.'"

**2:10** "Oh! Oh! Flee from the land of the north," says the Lord, "for I have dispersed you into the four winds of heaven," says the Lord.

**2:11** Oh, Zion! Flee, you who dwell with the daughter of Babylon,

**2:12** for this is what the Lord of hosts says: "After glory He has sent Me to the nations that plundered you; for whoever touches you touches the pupil of My eye.

**2:13** For look, I raise My hand over them, and they will become plunder for those who were serving them; and you will know that the Lord of hosts has sent Me.

**2:14** Give praise and rejoice, daughter of Zion, for look, I am coming, and I will dwell in your midst," says the Lord.

**2:15** "And many nations will be joined to the Lord in that day, and they will be a people to Me, and I will dwell in your midst; and you will know that the Lord of hosts has sent Me to you."

**2:16** And the Lord will possess Judah as His portion in the sanctified land, and He will again choose Jerusalem.

**2:17** Let all flesh be silent before the face of the Lord, for He has risen up from His holy dwelling.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — This Latin text, like the Hebrew, numbers the vision of the four horns and four craftsmen 2:1-4 and the measuring-line vision 2:5-17 (1:18-21 and 2:1-13 in some other editions).
- **2:4** — Latin per singulos viros ("man by man") is a literal Vulgate rendering of a Hebrew idiom usually taken as "so that no man lifted his head."
- **2:12** — The phrase Post gloriam ("After glory") is debated, understood either as "after restoring glory" or "afterward, in glory." Latin pupillam oculi mei ("the pupil of My eye") reads first-person "My eye," whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has third-person "his eye."
- **2:16** — Latin in terra sanctificata ("in the sanctified land") uses the passive participle "sanctified," distinct from the simple adjective "holy" (sancto) in 2:17.
