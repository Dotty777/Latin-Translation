## Text

**10:1** Ask the Lord for rain in the late season, and the Lord will make snow, and He will give them a downpour of rain, for each one vegetation in the field.

**10:2** For the idols have spoken what is useless, and the diviners have seen falsehood, and the dreamers have spoken in vain; they offered empty comfort. Therefore they have been led away like a flock; they will be afflicted, because they have no shepherd.

**10:3** "Against the shepherds My fury is enraged, and I will punish the male goats; for the Lord of hosts has visited His flock, the house of Judah, and has made them like the horse of His glory in war.

**10:4** From him the cornerstone, from him the peg, from him the bow of battle, from him every exactor will go out together.

**10:5** And they will be like mighty men trampling the mud of the roads in battle; and they will fight, because the Lord is with them, and the riders of horses will be confounded.

**10:6** And I will strengthen the house of Judah, and I will save the house of Joseph, and I will bring them back, because I will have mercy on them; and they will be as they were when I had not cast them off, for I am the Lord their God, and I will hear them.

**10:7** And Ephraim will be like mighty men, and their heart will rejoice as from wine; and their children will see and rejoice, and their heart will exult in the Lord.

**10:8** I will whistle for them and gather them, because I have redeemed them; and I will multiply them just as they had been multiplied before.

**10:9** And I will sow them among the peoples, and from far away they will remember Me; and they will live with their children and return.

**10:10** And I will lead them back from the land of Egypt, and from the Assyrians I will gather them, and to the land of Gilead and of Lebanon I will lead them, and no room will be found for them.

**10:11** And He will pass through the strait of the sea, and He will strike the waves in the sea, and all the depths of the river will be confounded; and the pride of Assyria will be humbled, and the scepter of Egypt will depart.

**10:12** I will strengthen them in the Lord, and they will walk in His name," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **10:1** — The Vulgate reads nives ("snow" or "snows"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text refers to storm clouds or lightning and the Septuagint reads "visions."
- **10:7** — Latin Et erunt quasi fortes Ephraim takes indeclinable Ephraim as a collective plural subject ("And Ephraim will be like mighty men"), though it can also be read as genitive ("And they will be like the mighty men of Ephraim").
- **10:11** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew distress/straits concretely as in maris freto ("through the strait of the sea"), and reads confundentur ("will be confounded") where the Hebrew has "will dry up."
