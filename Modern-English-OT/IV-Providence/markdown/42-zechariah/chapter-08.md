## Text

**8:1** And there came the word of the Lord of hosts, saying:

**8:2** "This is what the Lord of hosts says: 'I have been zealous for Zion with great zeal, and with great indignation I have been zealous for her.'"

**8:3** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "I have returned to Zion, and I will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem; and Jerusalem will be called the city of truth, and the mountain of the Lord of hosts, the sanctified mountain."

**8:4** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "Old men and old women will yet dwell in the squares of Jerusalem, and a man's staff will be in his hand because of the multitude of days.

**8:5** And the squares of the city will be filled with young boys and girls, playing in its squares."

**8:6** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "If it seems difficult in the eyes of the remnant of this people in those days, will it really be difficult in My eyes?" says the Lord of hosts.

**8:7** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "Look, I will save My people from the land of the east and from the land of the setting of the sun.

**8:8** And I will bring them, and they will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem; and they will be a people to Me, and I will be God to them, in truth and in righteousness."

**8:9** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "Let your hands be strengthened, you who hear in these days these words through the mouth of the prophets, on the day on which the house of the Lord of hosts was founded, so that the temple might be built.

**8:10** For indeed, before those days there was no wage for men, nor was there a wage for livestock; neither for the one going in nor for the one going out was there peace because of tribulation; and I let loose all men, each one against his neighbor.

**8:11** But now I will not act toward the remnant of this people according to the former days," says the Lord of hosts,

**8:12** "but there will be a seed of peace: the vine will give its fruit, and the earth will give its produce, and the heavens will give their dew; and I will cause the remnant of this people to possess all these things.

**8:13** And it will be that just as you were a curse among the nations, house of Judah and house of Israel, so I will save you, and you will be a blessing. Do not be afraid; let your hands be strengthened."

**8:14** For this is what the Lord of hosts says: "Just as I planned to afflict you when your fathers provoked Me to anger," says the Lord,

**8:15** "and I did not show mercy, so I have turned and planned in these days to do good to the house of Judah and to Jerusalem. Do not be afraid.

**8:16** These therefore are the things that you will do: speak the truth, each one with his neighbor; render truth and the judgment of peace in your gates.

**8:17** And do not devise evil in your hearts, each one against his friend, and do not love a lying oath; for all these are things that I hate," says the Lord.

**8:18** And the word of the Lord of hosts came to me, saying,

**8:19** "This is what the Lord of hosts says: 'The fast of the fourth month, and the fast of the fifth, and the fast of the seventh, and the fast of the tenth will become joy and gladness and splendid festivals for the house of Judah. Only love truth and peace.'"

**8:20** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "Until peoples come and dwell in many cities,

**8:21** and the inhabitants go, one to another, saying, 'Let us go and implore the face of the Lord, and seek the Lord of hosts; I also will go.'

**8:22** And many peoples and strong nations will come to seek the Lord of hosts in Jerusalem, and to implore the face of the Lord."

**8:23** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "In those days, ten men from all the languages of the nations will take hold, and they will take hold of the hem of a Jewish man, saying, 'We will go with you, for we have heard that God is with you.'"

## Translator Notes

- **8:3** — The Vulgate includes exercituum ("of hosts") in the opening formula (where the Hebrew has only "the Lord") and renders the final title as mons sanctificatus ("the sanctified mountain") rather than "the mountain of holiness."
- **8:10** — The Vulgate reverses the Hebrew order of "going out and coming in" to introeunti ... exeunti ("going in ... going out") and translates Hebrew "from the foe" abstractly as prae tribulatione ("because of tribulation").
- **8:20** — The Vulgate reads usquequo veniant populi et habitent in civitatibus multis ("Until peoples come and dwell in many cities"), translating the Hebrew phrase as a temporal clause with a finite verb ("and dwell") rather than "It will yet be that peoples will come, even the inhabitants of many cities."
