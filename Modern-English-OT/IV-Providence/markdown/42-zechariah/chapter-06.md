## Text

**6:1** And I turned and lifted up my eyes and saw, and look, four chariots coming out from between two mountains; and the mountains were mountains of bronze.

**6:2** In the first chariot were red horses, and in the second chariot black horses,

**6:3** and in the third chariot white horses, and in the fourth chariot dappled and strong horses.

**6:4** And I answered and said to the angel who was speaking in me, "What are these, my lord?"

**6:5** And the angel answered and said to me, "These are the four winds of heaven, which go out to stand before the Ruler of all the earth."

**6:6** The chariot in which the black horses were went out into the land of the north, and the white ones went out after them, and the dappled ones went out to the land of the south.

**6:7** And those that were strongest went out and were seeking to go and range through all the earth. And He said, "Go, walk through the earth." And they walked through the earth.

**6:8** And He called to me and spoke to me, saying, "Look, those who go out into the land of the north have caused My Spirit to rest in the land of the north."

**6:9** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**6:10** "Take from the exile, from Heldai, and from Tobijah, and from Jedaiah; and you will come on that day, and enter the house of Josiah the son of Zephaniah, who came from Babylon.

**6:11** And you will take gold and silver, and make crowns, and place them on the head of Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest;

**6:12** and you will speak to him, saying, 'This is what the Lord of hosts declares, saying: Look, a man, Rising is His name, and beneath Him He will rise, and He will build the temple for the Lord.

**6:13** And He Himself will construct the temple for the Lord; and He Himself will bear glory, and He will sit and rule upon His throne; and He will be a priest upon His throne, and a counsel of peace will be between those two.'

**6:14** And the crowns will be for Helem, and Tobijah, and Jedaiah, and Hem the son of Zephaniah, as a memorial in the temple of the Lord.

**6:15** And those who are far off will come and build in the temple of the Lord; and you will know that the Lord of hosts has sent me to you. And this will happen if you earnestly listen to the voice of the Lord your God."

## Translator Notes

- **6:5** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew "ruchot" specifically as "winds" ("venti", rather than "spirits") and reads "to stand" ("ut stent") where the Hebrew says "after presenting themselves".
- **6:11** — The Vulgate reverses the Hebrew order to "gold and silver" ("aurum et argentum") and uses the Latin/Greek name form "Jesus" ("Jesu") for the high priest Joshua.
- **6:12** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew "Branch" ("Tsemach") and its cognate verb as "Rising" ("Oriens") and "will rise" ("orietur"), following the Septuagint "Anatole" (cf. Luke 1:78).
- **6:13** — Latin "et erit sacerdos super solio suo" may be read either as "He will be a priest upon His throne" (uniting royal and priestly offices in the Messiah) or "a priest will be upon his throne" (pointing to the two figures in "between those two").
- **6:14** — The Vulgate reads the name "Hem" where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "Hen" (meaning "favor" or "grace").
