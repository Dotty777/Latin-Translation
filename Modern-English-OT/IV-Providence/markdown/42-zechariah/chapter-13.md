## Text

**13:1** In that day there will be an open fountain for the house of David and for the inhabitants of Jerusalem, for the washing of the sinner and of the menstruating woman.

**13:2** "And it will happen in that day," says the Lord of hosts, "that I will destroy the names of the idols from the land, and they will not be remembered any longer; and I will remove the false prophets and the unclean spirit from the land."

**13:3** And it will happen that when anyone prophesies any longer, his father and his mother, who gave birth to him, will say to him, "You will not live, because you have spoken a lie in the name of the Lord." And his father and his mother, his parents, will pierce him through when he prophesies.

**13:4** And it will happen in that day that the prophets will be ashamed, each one because of his vision when he prophesies, nor will they cover themselves with a cloak of sackcloth in order to lie.

**13:5** But he will say, "I am not a prophet; I am a man who tills the land, because Adam has been my example from my youth."

**13:6** And it will be said to him, "What are these wounds in the middle of your hands?" And he will say, "With these I was wounded in the house of those who loved me."

**13:7** "Sword, rouse yourself against My shepherd, and against the man closely joined to Me," says the Lord of hosts. "Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered; and I will turn My hand toward the little ones."

**13:8** "And it will happen in all the land," says the Lord, "that two parts in it will be scattered and will perish, and the third part will be left in it.

**13:9** And I will lead the third part through the fire, and I will burn them as silver is burned, and I will test them as gold is tested. They will call on My name, and I will hear them. I will say, 'You are My people,' and they will say, 'The Lord is my God.'"

## Translator Notes

- **13:1** — Vulgate reads "peccatoris et menstruatae" ("of the sinner and of the menstruating woman"), using concrete personal nouns where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the abstract nouns "sin and impurity."
- **13:5** — A notable Vulgate-distinct reading: "quoniam Adam exemplum meum" ("because Adam has been my example"), taking Hebrew "adam" as the first man Adam tilling the ground, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "for a man acquired me" (or "sold me").
- **13:6** — Vulgate "in medio manuum tuarum" ("in the middle of your hands") translates literally the Hebrew idiom "between your hands" (often understood in Hebrew as the chest or back).
- **13:9** — Vulgate reads second-person "Populus meus es" ("You are My people") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has third-person "It is My people." The Latin collective singular pronouns ("Ipse... eum... ipse") referring to the remnant are rendered "they/them" for clarity in English.
