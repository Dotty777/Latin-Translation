## Text

**7:1** And it happened in the fourth year of King Darius that the word of the Lord came to Zechariah, on the fourth day of the ninth month, which is Chislev.

**7:2** And Sharezer and Regem-melech, and the men who were with him, sent to the house of God to implore the face of the Lord,

**7:3** so that they might speak to the priests of the house of the Lord of hosts and to the prophets, saying, "Must I weep in the fifth month, or should I sanctify myself, as I have already done for many years?"

**7:4** And the word of the Lord of hosts came to me, saying,

**7:5** "Speak to all the people of the land and to the priests, saying, 'When you fasted and mourned in the fifth and seventh months through these seventy years, did you really fast a fast for Me?

**7:6** And when you ate and drank, did you not eat for yourselves and drink for your own selves?

**7:7** Are these not the words that the Lord spoke by the hand of the former prophets, while Jerusalem was still inhabited so that she was wealthy, she herself and the cities around her, and while the south and the plains were inhabited?'"

**7:8** And the word of the Lord came to Zechariah, saying,

**7:9** "This is what the Lord of hosts declares, saying, 'Render true judgment, and practice mercy and compassions, each one with his brother.

**7:10** And do not oppress the widow, the orphan, the foreigner, or the poor person; and let no man devise evil against his brother in his heart.'"

**7:11** And they refused to pay attention, and turned away a withdrawing shoulder, and made their ears heavy so that they would not hear.

**7:12** And they made their heart like adamant so that they would not hear the law and the words that the Lord of hosts sent in His Spirit through the hand of the former prophets; and great indignation came from the Lord of hosts.

**7:13** And it happened that just as He spoke and they did not hear, "so they will cry out and I will not listen," says the Lord of hosts.

**7:14** "And I scattered them through all the kingdoms that they do not know, and the land was left desolate by them, because there was no one passing through and returning; and they turned the desirable land into a desert."

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew Bethel as a common noun phrase, "to the house of God" (ad domum Dei), and places viri ("the men") in the nominative, making Sharezer, Regem-melech, and his companions the subjects who sent to the temple rather than envoys sent from Bethel.
- **7:3** — Where the Hebrew verb denotes abstaining or separating oneself as in a fast, the Vulgate renders sanctificare me ("sanctify myself"), emphasizing ritual consecration.
- **7:13** — The Vulgate reads locutus est ("He spoke") rather than the Hebrew "He called," and renders the second clause in the future tense (clamabunt et non exaudiam, "they will cry out and I will not listen") rather than a past habitual action.
