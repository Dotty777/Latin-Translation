## Text

**4:1** Hear the word of the Lord, sons of Israel, because the Lord has a legal dispute with the inhabitants of the land; for there is no truth, nor is there mercy, nor is there knowledge of God in the land.

**4:2** Cursing, falsehood, murder, theft, and adultery have overflowed, and blood has touched blood.

**4:3** Because of this, the land will mourn, and everyone who lives in it will grow weak, together with the beast of the field and the bird of the sky; and even the fish of the sea will be gathered together.

**4:4** Yet let no one judge, and let no man be reproved; for your people are like those who oppose the priest.

**4:5** And you will fall down today, and the prophet will also fall down with you. At night I have caused your mother to be silent.

**4:6** My people have fallen silent because they had no knowledge. Since you have rejected knowledge, I will reject you so that you may not exercise the priesthood for Me. And you have forgotten the law of your God; I too will forget your children.

**4:7** According to their multitude, so they have sinned against Me; I will exchange their glory for disgrace.

**4:8** They will eat the sins of My people, and toward their iniquity they will lift up their souls.

**4:9** And it will be: like the people, so the priest; and I will call him to account for his ways, and I will repay his thoughts to him.

**4:10** And they will eat and not be satisfied; they have committed fornication and have not ceased, because they have abandoned the Lord by not taking heed.

**4:11** Fornication, wine, and drunkenness take away the heart.

**4:12** My people have inquired of their wood, and their staff has told them; for a spirit of fornications has deceived them, and they have committed fornication away from their God.

**4:13** Upon the tops of the mountains they were sacrificing, and upon the hills they were sending up incense, beneath an oak, a poplar, and a terebinth, because its shade was good; therefore your daughters will commit fornication, and your brides will be adulteresses.

**4:14** I will not call your daughters to account when they have committed fornication, nor your brides when they have committed adultery; for they themselves were consorting with prostitutes and sacrificing with effeminate men, and a people without understanding will be beaten.

**4:15** If you commit fornication, Israel, at least let Judah not become guilty; and do not enter Gilgal, and do not go up to Beth-aven, nor swear, "The Lord lives!"

**4:16** For like an unruly cow, Israel has turned aside; now the Lord will pasture them like a lamb in a wide place.

**4:17** Ephraim is a partner of idols; leave him alone.

**4:18** Their banquet is separated; in fornication they have committed fornication; his protectors have loved to bring disgrace.

**4:19** The wind has bound him in its wings, and they will be put to shame because of their sacrifices.

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — The Vulgate renders the closing verb literally as congregabuntur ("will be gathered together"), reflecting the primary sense of the Hebrew root for gathering rather than "be swept away."
- **4:5** — Unlike the Hebrew division (which attaches "by night" to the prophet's stumbling and reads "I will destroy your mother"), the Vulgate opens a new sentence with Nocte ("At night") and renders tacere feci ("I have caused to be silent"), connecting directly to Conticuit ("have fallen silent") in 4:6.
- **4:10** — The Vulgate reads et non cessaverunt ("and have not ceased") rather than the Hebrew "and will not increase," and translates the closing phrase literally with the gerund in non custodiendo ("by not taking heed").
- **4:14** — The Vulgate translates cum effeminatis ("with effeminate men") where the Hebrew has feminine cult prostitutes, and renders the closing verb concretely as vapulabit ("will be beaten").
- **4:18** — The Vulgate translates the opening clause as Separatum est convivium eorum ("Their banquet is separated") rather than "Their drinking is gone," and renders the difficult closing words as dilexerunt afferre ignominiam protectores ejus ("his protectors have loved to bring disgrace").
