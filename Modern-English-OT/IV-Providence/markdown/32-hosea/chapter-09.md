## Text

**9:1** Do not rejoice, Israel; do not exult like the peoples; for you have committed fornication away from your God; you have loved wages on all the threshing floors of wheat.

**9:2** The threshing floor and the winepress will not feed them, and the wine will lie to them.

**9:3** They will not dwell in the land of the Lord. Ephraim has returned to Egypt, and among the Assyrians he has eaten what is polluted.

**9:4** They will not pour out wine as a libation to the Lord, and they will not be pleasing to Him. Their sacrifices are like the bread of mourners; all who eat it will be defiled; for their bread is for their own soul; it will not enter into the house of the Lord.

**9:5** What will you do on the solemn day, on the day of the festival of the Lord?

**9:6** For look, they have set out from devastation: Egypt will gather them, Memphis will bury them; the nettle will inherit their desirable silver; burdock will be in their tents.

**9:7** The days of visitation have come; the days of retribution have come. Know, Israel, that the prophet is foolish, the spiritual man is mad, because of the multitude of your iniquity and the multitude of madness.

**9:8** The watchman of Ephraim is with my God; the prophet has become a snare of ruin on all his ways; insanity is in the house of his God.

**9:9** They have sinned deeply, as in the days of Gibeah. He will remember their iniquity and will punish their sins.

**9:10** Like grapes in the desert I found Israel; like the first fruits of the fig tree at its top I saw their fathers; but they themselves went in to Baal-peor, and alienated themselves to shame, and became abominable like the things that they loved.

**9:11** Ephraim has flown away like a bird; their glory, from birth, and from the womb, and from conception.

**9:12** And even if they bring up their sons, I will make them childless among men; moreover, woe to them when I depart from them!

**9:13** Ephraim, as I saw, was Tyre, founded in beauty; and Ephraim will lead out his sons to the killer.

**9:14** Give them, Lord. What will You give them? Give them a womb without children and dry breasts.

**9:15** All their evils are in Gilgal, for there I held them in hatred. Because of the wickedness of their devices, I will drive them out of My house; I will not continue to love them; all their princes are turning away.

**9:16** Ephraim has been struck; their root has dried up; they will by no means produce fruit; and even if they give birth, I will kill the most beloved ones of their womb.

**9:17** My God will cast them away, because they have not listened to Him, and they will be wanderers among the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **9:7** — Vulgate reads "amentiæ" ("madness") here and "insania" ("insanity") in 9:8 for a Hebrew noun meaning "hostility" or "enmity."
- **9:8** — Vulgate renders the Hebrew "fowler's snare" as "laqueus ruinæ" ("a snare of ruin") and makes "propheta" ("the prophet") the subject of that clause.
- **9:10** — Vulgate reads "in cacumine ejus" ("at its top"), taking the Hebrew term for "in its first season" in a spatial sense.
- **9:11** — Following the Clementine Vulgate punctuation and past tense "avolavit," the verb "has flown away" carries over by ellipsis to "their glory" in the second clause.
- **9:13** — Vulgate reads "Tyrus erat fundata in pulchritudine" ("was Tyre, founded in beauty"), presenting the comparison to Tyre as a direct metaphor and rendering Hebrew "meadow" or "pleasant place" as "beauty."
