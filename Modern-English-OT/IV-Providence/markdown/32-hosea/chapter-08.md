## Text

**8:1** Let a trumpet be in your throat, like an eagle over the house of the Lord, because they have transgressed My covenant and have rebelled against My law.

**8:2** They will call upon Me: "My God, we Israel have known You."

**8:3** Israel has cast off what is good; an enemy will pursue him.

**8:4** They themselves have reigned, and not from Me; they have become princes, and I did not know it; their silver and their gold they have made into idols for themselves, so that they might perish.

**8:5** Your calf has been cast off, Samaria; My fury is incensed against them. How long will they be unable to be cleansed?

**8:6** For it too is from Israel; a craftsman made it, and it is not a god; for the calf of Samaria will become spiders' webs.

**8:7** Because they will sow wind, and they will harvest a whirlwind; there is no standing stalk in it; the shoot will not produce flour; and even if it does produce it, foreigners will eat it.

**8:8** Israel has been devoured; now he has become among the nations like an unclean vessel.

**8:9** For they themselves have gone up to Assyria, a wild donkey solitary by itself; Ephraim has given gifts to lovers.

**8:10** Yet even when they have hired the nations for wages, now I will gather them, and they will rest for a little while from the burden of the king and of the princes.

**8:11** Because Ephraim has multiplied altars for sinning, altars have become an offense to him.

**8:12** I will write for him My manifold laws, which have been counted as foreign.

**8:13** They will offer sacrifices, they will slaughter meat and eat, and the Lord will not accept them; now He will remember their iniquity and will punish their sins; they themselves will turn back to Egypt.

**8:14** And Israel has forgotten his Maker and has built shrines, and Judah has multiplied fortified cities; and I will send fire into his cities, and it will devour his palaces.

## Translator Notes

- **8:4** — Vulgate renders the verbs intransitively ("They themselves have reigned... they have become princes") rather than causatively as in the Hebrew ("they set up kings... they appointed princes").
- **8:6** — Vulgate reads "in aranearum telas" ("spiders' webs") for the rare Hebrew term usually translated "splinters" or "broken pieces."
- **8:10** — Vulgate reads "quiescent paulisper" ("they will rest for a little while," agreeing with the Septuagint) and coordinates "regis et principum" ("of the king and of the princes") where the Hebrew has "the king of princes."
- **8:13** — Latin "visitabit" literally means "will visit" in the biblical sense of judicial inspection and punishment (recurring in 9:9 and linked to "visitation" in 9:7).
