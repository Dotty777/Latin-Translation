## Text

**13:1** When Ephraim spoke, dread fell upon Israel; and he transgressed in Baal, and died.

**13:2** And now they have continued to sin; and they have made for themselves a cast image from their silver as a likeness of idols; all of it is the handiwork of craftsmen. To these they themselves say, "Sacrifice human beings, you who worship calves."

**13:3** Therefore they will be like a morning cloud, and like morning dew passing away; like dust snatched by a whirlwind from the threshing floor, and like smoke from a chimney.

**13:4** But I am the Lord your God from the land of Egypt; and you will know no God apart from Me, and there is no savior except Me.

**13:5** I knew you in the desert, in a land of solitude.

**13:6** According to their pastures they were filled and were satisfied; and they lifted up their heart, and they forgot Me.

**13:7** And I will be to them like a lioness, like a leopard on the road of the Assyrians.

**13:8** I will meet them like a bear whose cubs have been snatched away, and I will rip open the inner parts of their liver, and I will devour them there like a lion; the wild beast of the field will tear them apart.

**13:9** Destruction is yours, Israel; only in Me is your help.

**13:10** Where is your king? Especially now let him save you in all your cities; and your judges, about whom you said, "Give me a king and princes."

**13:11** I will give you a king in My fury, and I will take him away in My indignation.

**13:12** Ephraim's iniquity is bound up; his sin is concealed.

**13:13** The pains of a woman in labor will come upon him; he himself is not a wise son, for now he will not stand in the crushing of children.

**13:14** From the hand of death I will deliver them; from death I will redeem them. I will be your death, death! Your bite I will be, hell! Consolation is concealed from My eyes.

**13:15** Because he himself will divide among brothers, the Lord will bring a burning wind rising from the desert, and He will dry up his springs and lay waste his fountain; and He Himself will plunder the treasury of every desirable vessel.

## Translator Notes

- **13:2** — Vulgate "quasi similitudinem idolorum" ("as a likeness of idols") and the imperative "Immolate homines, vitulos adorantes" ("Sacrifice human beings, you who worship calves") reflect distinctly Vulgate readings compared with the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("according to their skill, idols ... men who sacrifice kiss calves").
- **13:7** — Vulgate "in via Assyriorum" ("on the road of the Assyrians") takes the final Hebrew word as the nation Assyria, matching the Septuagint, whereas the Masoretic Text reads it as a verb ("I will watch/lurk").
- **13:13** — Vulgate "in contritione filiorum" ("in the crushing of children") renders the Hebrew childbirth term ("opening of the womb") literally through its root meaning "break" or "crush."
- **13:14** — Vulgate renders the central lines as affirmative declarations ("I will be your death ... Your bite I will be") rather than the questions of the Masoretic Text ("Where are your plagues ...?"), and reads "of death" ("mortis") in the first clause where the Hebrew has "Sheol."
- **13:15** — Vulgate "dividet" ("will divide") reflects a Hebrew root meaning "separate," whereas the Masoretic Text reads a wordplay on Ephraim's name ("will flourish/bear fruit").
