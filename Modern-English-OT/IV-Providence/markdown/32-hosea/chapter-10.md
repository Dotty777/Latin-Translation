## Text

**10:1** Israel is a leafy vine; the fruit was matched to it. According to the abundance of its fruit it multiplied altars; according to the fertility of its land it abounded in images.

**10:2** Their heart is divided; now they will perish. He Himself will shatter their images; He will lay waste their altars.

**10:3** For now they will say, "There is no king for us, for we do not fear the Lord; and what will a king do for us?"

**10:4** You speak words of a worthless vision, and you will strike a covenant; and judgment will sprout like bitterness upon the furrows of the field.

**10:5** The inhabitants of Samaria worshiped the cows of Beth-aven; for its people mourned over it, and its temple attendants exulted over it in its glory, because it has departed from it.

**10:6** For indeed it itself has been carried off to Assyria, a gift to the avenging king. Shame will seize Ephraim, and Israel will be put to shame in its own will.

**10:7** Samaria has caused her king to pass away like foam upon the face of the water.

**10:8** And the high places of the idol, the sin of Israel, will be destroyed; burdock and thistle will come up upon their altars, and they will say to the mountains, "Cover us," and to the hills, "Fall upon us."

**10:9** From the days of Gibeah Israel has sinned; there they stood. Battle against the sons of iniquity will not overtake them in Gibeah.

**10:10** According to My desire I will chastise them; peoples will be gathered against them when they are chastised for their two iniquities.

**10:11** Ephraim is a heifer taught to love threshing, and I passed over the beauty of her neck; I will ride upon Ephraim, Judah will plow, Jacob will break up the furrows for himself.

**10:12** Sow for yourselves in righteousness, and reap in the mouth of mercy. Renew for yourselves fallow ground; and it is time to seek the Lord, when He comes who will teach you righteousness.

**10:13** You have plowed ungodliness, you have reaped iniquity; you have eaten the fruit of falsehood, because you have trusted in your ways, in the multitude of your mighty men.

**10:14** An uproar will rise up among your people, and all your fortresses will be laid waste, just as Salmana was laid waste by the house of him who judged Baal in the day of battle, with the mother dashed to pieces upon the children.

**10:15** Thus has Bethel done to you, because of the malice of your wicked deeds.

## Translator Notes

- **10:4** — The Vulgate reads visionis inutilis ("a worthless vision") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "false oaths."
- **10:5** — The Vulgate renders the verbs as coluerunt ("worshiped") and exsultaverunt ("exulted"), whereas the Hebrew text is commonly read as trembling and wailing over the calf of Beth-aven.
- **10:6** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew title melekh yarev ("the great king" or "King Jareb") as regi ultori ("the avenging king").
- **10:12** — "In the mouth of mercy" (in ore misericordiae) is a literal Vulgate rendering of the Hebrew idiom meaning "according to mercy," and "who will teach you righteousness" reflects the Vulgate reading of the Hebrew verb yoreh as "teach" rather than "rain."
- **10:14** — A distinctively Vulgate reading: Salmana (the Latin form of Zalmunna) and "the house of him who judged Baal" (alluding to Gideon/Jerubbaal) correspond to "Shalman" and "Beth-arbel" in the Hebrew text.
