## Text

**12:1** Ephraim has surrounded Me with denial, and the house of Israel with deceit; but Judah went down as a witness with God, and is faithful with the holy ones.

**12:2** Ephraim feeds on wind and pursues the scorching heat; all day long he multiplies falsehood and devastation, and he entered into a covenant with the Assyrians and was carrying oil to Egypt.

**12:3** Therefore the judgment of the Lord is with Judah, and a visitation upon Jacob; according to his ways and according to his schemes He will repay him.

**12:4** In the womb he supplanted his brother, and in his strength he was directed with an angel.

**12:5** And he prevailed against the angel and was strengthened; he wept and pleaded with him. In Bethel He found him, and there He spoke with us.

**12:6** And the Lord God of hosts, the Lord is His memorial.

**12:7** And you will turn back to your God; keep mercy and justice, and hope in your God always.

**12:8** Canaan, in his hand is a deceitful balance; he has loved extortion.

**12:9** And Ephraim said, "Indeed I have become rich; I have found an idol for myself. All my labors will not find in me iniquity that I have sinned."

**12:10** And I am the Lord your God from the land of Egypt; I will yet make you dwell in tents, as in the days of the festival.

**12:11** And I spoke to the prophets, and I multiplied vision, and by the hand of the prophets I was likened.

**12:12** If Gilead is an idol, then in vain were they sacrificing bulls in Gilgal; for even their altars are like heaps upon the furrows of the field.

**12:13** Jacob fled into the region of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept watch.

**12:14** And by a prophet the Lord led Israel out of Egypt, and by a prophet he was kept.

**12:15** Ephraim has provoked Me to anger in his bitternesses; and his blood will come upon him, and his Lord will return his reproach to him.

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — The Vulgate reads testis descendit ("went down as a witness") where the Hebrew text reads "still rules" or "is unruly"; this verse is numbered 11:12 in most English Bibles.
- **12:4** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew "strove with God" as directus est cum angelo ("was directed with an angel," also understood in Vulgate usage as "succeeded with an angel").
- **12:8** — The Vulgate preserves Chanaan ("Canaan") as a proper name; in Hebrew the same word can also mean "merchant."
- **12:9** — The Vulgate reads idolum ("an idol") for Hebrew on ("wealth"), connecting it with aven ("idolatry" or "wickedness"), as also in 10:8 and 12:12.
- **12:11** — The Vulgate translates assimilatus sum passively ("I was likened," that is, represented through prophetic likenesses), whereas the Hebrew verb is active ("I gave parables").
