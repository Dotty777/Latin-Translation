## Text

**5:1** Hear this, priests, and pay attention, house of Israel, and house of the king, listen closely; for judgment belongs to you, because you have become a snare to the watch and a net spread out over Tabor.

**5:2** And you have turned sacrificial victims aside into the deep, and I am the instructor of them all.

**5:3** I know Ephraim, and Israel is not hidden from Me; for now Ephraim has committed fornication; Israel has been defiled.

**5:4** They will not give their thoughts so that they may return to their God, because a spirit of fornications is in their midst, and they have not known the Lord.

**5:5** And the arrogance of Israel will answer to his face, and Israel and Ephraim will fall in their iniquity; Judah will also fall with them.

**5:6** With their flocks and with their herds they will go to seek the Lord, and they will not find Him; He has withdrawn from them.

**5:7** They have broken faith with the Lord, because they have fathered foreign children; now a month will devour them together with their portions.

**5:8** Sound the horn in Gibeah, the trumpet in Ramah; wail in Beth-aven, behind your back, Benjamin.

**5:9** Ephraim will be in desolation on the day of rebuke; among the tribes of Israel I have shown faithfulness.

**5:10** The princes of Judah have become like those who take up a boundary; upon them I will pour out My anger like water.

**5:11** Ephraim is suffering false accusation, broken in judgment, because he began to go away after filth.

**5:12** And I am like a moth to Ephraim, and like decay to the house of Judah.

**5:13** And Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah his bond; and Ephraim went away to Assyria and sent to the avenging king. Yet he himself will not be able to heal you, nor will he be able to untie the bond from you.

**5:14** For I am like a lioness to Ephraim, and like a lion cub to the house of Judah. I, I will seize and depart; I will carry off, and there is no one who can rescue.

**5:15** Going away, I will return to My place until you grow faint and seek My face.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew place name Mizpah etymologically as a common noun, speculationi ("to the watch" or "lookout").
- **5:2** — Where the Hebrew is obscure ("the revolters have gone deep in slaughter"), the Vulgate reads victimas declinastis in profundum ("you have turned sacrificial victims aside into the deep") and renders the closing noun as an agent, eruditor ("instructor").
- **5:4** — The Vulgate takes cogitationes suas ("their thoughts") as the direct object of Non dabunt ("They will not give"), rather than making "their deeds" the subject as in the Hebrew text.
- **5:13** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew for "wound" twice as vinculum ("bond"), and translates Hebrew "King Jareb" (or "the great king") etymologically as regem ultorem ("the avenging king").
- **5:15** — The Vulgate uses second-person plural verbs and translates deficiatis ("until you grow faint") where the Hebrew has third-person plural "until they acknowledge their guilt."
