## Text

**2:1** Say to your brothers, "My People," and to your sister, "Having Obtained Mercy."

**2:2** Judge your mother, judge, because she is not My wife, and I am not her husband. Let her remove her fornications from her face, and her adulteries from between her breasts;

**2:3** or else I will strip her naked, and set her as on the day of her birth, and make her like a wilderness, and set her like a trackless land, and kill her with thirst.

**2:4** And I will not have mercy on her children, because they are children of fornications.

**2:5** For their mother has committed fornication; she who conceived them has been put to shame, for she said, "I will go after my lovers, who give bread to me, and my water, my wool and my flax, my oil and my drink."

**2:6** For this reason, look, I will fence in your way with thorns, and I will fence it in with a stone wall, and she will not find her paths.

**2:7** And she will pursue her lovers, and will not overtake them; and she will seek them, and will not find them; and she will say, "I will go and return to my former husband, because it was better for me then than now."

**2:8** And she did not know that I gave her grain, and wine, and oil, and multiplied silver for her, and gold, which they made for Baal.

**2:9** Therefore I will turn back and take My grain in its time, and My wine in its time. And I will rescue My wool and My flax, which covered her disgrace.

**2:10** And now I will uncover her folly before the eyes of her lovers, and no man will snatch her from My hand;

**2:11** and I will cause all her joy to cease, her festival, her new moon, her Sabbath, and all her festive times.

**2:12** And I will ruin her vineyard and her fig tree, about which she said, "These are my wages, which my lovers have given me"; and I will turn it into a forest, and the beast of the field will devour it.

**2:13** And I will visit upon her the days of the Baals, on which she burned incense, and adorned herself with her earring and her necklace. And she went after her lovers, and forgot Me, says the Lord.

**2:14** For this reason, look, I will allure her, and lead her into the wilderness, and speak to her heart.

**2:15** And I will give her vinedressers to her from that same place, and the valley of Achor to open hope; and she will sing there as in the days of her youth, and as in the days of her ascent from the land of Egypt.

**2:16** And it will be on that day, says the Lord: she will call Me "My husband," and she will no longer call Me "Baali."

**2:17** And I will remove the names of the Baals from her mouth, and she will no longer remember their name.

**2:18** And I will make a covenant with them on that day, with the beast of the field, and with the bird of the sky, and with the creeping thing of the earth; and I will shatter bow, and sword, and war from the earth, and I will make them sleep with confidence.

**2:19** And I will betroth you to Myself forever; and I will betroth you to Myself in righteousness and justice, and in mercy, and in compassions.

**2:20** And I will betroth you to Myself in faithfulness; and you will know that I am the Lord.

**2:21** And it will be on that day: I will hear, says the Lord, I will hear the heavens, and they will hear the earth.

**2:22** And the earth will hear the wheat, and the wine, and the oil, and these will hear Jezreel.

**2:23** And I will sow her for Myself in the earth, and I will have mercy on her who was Without Mercy.

**2:24** And I will say to Not My People, "You are My people"; and he will say, "You are my God."

## Translator Notes

- **2:9** — Latin "liberabo" literally means "I will rescue" or "deliver," preserving the vivid metaphor of snatching His gifts away; "ignominiam" ("disgrace") renders the Hebrew "nakedness," just as "stultitiam" ("folly") renders "lewdness" in 2:12.
- **2:15** — Distinctly Vulgate readings here include "vinitores ejus" ("her vinedressers," where the Hebrew reads "her vineyards"), "ad aperiendam spem" ("to open hope," vs. Hebrew "a door of hope"), and "canet" ("she will sing").
- **2:16** — Where the Hebrew uses second person ("you will call"), the Vulgate reads third person ("vocabit", "she will call," continuing in 2:19 with "she will no longer remember"). "Baali" means "my master" or "my Baal," contrasting with "My husband."
- **2:23** — In native 2:21-22 (Saints 2:23-24), Latin "exaudiam" ("I will hear" favorably) renders the Hebrew "answer." Native 2:23 maps to Saints 2:25, followed in native Vulgate by the restored closing verse 2:24.
- **2:24** — Stage 1 restored the closing verse of Hosea chapter 2 (Clementine Vulgate 2:24, previously absent from HEAD~1).
