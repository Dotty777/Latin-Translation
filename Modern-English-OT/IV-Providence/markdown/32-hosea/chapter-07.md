## Text

**7:1** When I wished to heal Israel, the iniquity of Ephraim was revealed, and the wickedness of Samaria, because they have practiced falsehood; and a thief has entered plundering; a bandit is outside.

**7:2** And so that they may not perhaps say in their hearts that I have remembered all their wickedness, now their own devices have surrounded them; they have been done before My face.

**7:3** In their wickedness they have gladdened the king, and in their falsehoods the princes.

**7:4** All of them are committing adultery, like an oven kindled by the baker; the city rested for a little while from the mixing of the leaven, until the whole was leavened.

**7:5** The day of our king: the princes began to rage from wine; he stretched out his hand with mockers.

**7:6** For they applied their heart like an oven while he was lying in wait for them; all night the one baking them slept; in the morning he himself is kindled like a fire of flame.

**7:7** All of them have been heated like an oven, and they have devoured their judges; all their kings have fallen; there is no one among them who cries out to Me.

**7:8** Ephraim himself was mingling among the peoples; Ephraim has become ash-baked bread that is not turned over.

**7:9** Foreigners have consumed his strength, and he himself did not know it; and even gray hairs have spread upon him, and he himself was unaware of it.

**7:10** And the pride of Israel will be humbled to his face; yet they have not returned to the Lord their God, and they have not sought Him in all these things.

**7:11** And Ephraim has become like a misled dove having no heart. They were calling upon Egypt; they went away to the Assyrians.

**7:12** And when they set out, I will spread My net over them; like a bird of the sky I will bring them down; I will strike them according to the report of their assembly.

**7:13** Woe to them, because they have departed from Me! They will be devastated, because they have rebelled against Me; and I redeemed them, yet they themselves have spoken falsehoods against Me.

**7:14** And they have not cried out to Me in their heart, but they were wailing on their beds; over wheat and wine they were ruminating; they have departed from Me.

**7:15** And I instructed them and strengthened their arms, yet against Me they devised wickedness.

**7:16** They turned back so that they might be without a yoke; they have become like a deceitful bow; their princes will fall by the sword from the rage of their tongue. This is their derision in the land of Egypt.

## Translator Notes

- **7:2** — Vulgate reads "ne forte dicant" ("so that they may not perhaps say"), rendering the Hebrew negation as a negative purpose clause rather than a simple statement ("they do not consider").
- **7:4** — Vulgate uniquely reads "civitas" ("the city"), taking the Hebrew word otherwise read as "from stirring [the fire]" as "the city."
- **7:10** — Vulgate reads "humiliabitur" ("will be humbled"), following the Septuagint in deriving the Hebrew verb from the root meaning "to humble" rather than "to testify."
- **7:14** — Vulgate renders the debated Hebrew verb ("gather together" or "gash themselves") as "ruminabant" ("they were ruminating," literally chewing the cud or brooding over).
- **7:16** — Vulgate reads "ut essent absque jugo" ("so that they might be without a yoke"), understanding the Hebrew consonantal text as "without a yoke" rather than "not upward" or "not to the Most High."
