## Text

**1:1** The word of the Lord, which came to Hosea, son of Beeri, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam, son of Joash, king of Israel.

**1:2** The beginning of the Lord's speaking in Hosea. And the Lord said to Hosea, "Go, take for yourself a wife of fornications, and have for yourself children of fornications, because the land, committing fornication, will commit fornication away from the Lord."

**1:3** And he departed and took Gomer, daughter of Diblaim; and she conceived and bore him a son.

**1:4** And the Lord said to him, "Call his name Jezreel, because yet a little while, and I will visit the blood of Jezreel upon the house of Jehu, and I will cause the kingdom of the house of Israel to cease.

**1:5** And on that day I will shatter the bow of Israel in the valley of Jezreel."

**1:6** And she conceived again and bore a daughter. And He said to him, "Call her name Without Mercy, because I will no longer continue to have mercy on the house of Israel, but in forgetfulness I will forget them.

**1:7** And on the house of Judah I will have mercy, and I will save them by the Lord their God; and I will not save them by bow and sword, or by war, or by horses, or by horsemen."

**1:8** And she weaned her who was Without Mercy. And she conceived and bore a son.

**1:9** And He said, "Call his name Not My People, because you are not My people, and I will not be yours."

**1:10** And the number of the sons of Israel will be like the sand of the sea, which is without measure and will not be numbered. And it will be, in the place where it will be said to them, "You are not My people," that it will be said to them, "Sons of the living God."

**1:11** And the sons of Judah and the sons of Israel will be assembled together; and they will appoint for themselves one head, and they will go up from the land, because great is the day of Jezreel.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — Latin "Principium loquendi Domino in Osee" ("the Lord's speaking in Hosea") literally preserves the preposition "in" (speaking within or through the prophet), and "committing fornication, will commit fornication" keeps the Vulgate's emphatic participle-and-verb construction.
- **1:4** — Latin "visitabo ... super" ("I will visit ... upon") is a literal Vulgate idiom for calling to account or punishing (cf. 2:15).
- **1:6** — The Vulgate translates the children's symbolic names into Latin ("Absque misericordia", "Without Mercy"; "Non populus meus", "Not My People" in 1:9) rather than transliterating the Hebrew. Its closing clause "oblivione obliviscar eorum" ("in forgetfulness I will forget them") is a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Hebrew is read as refusing forgiveness or as carrying away.
- **1:10** — In native Clementine Vulgate numbering, 1:10-11 conclude chapter 1 and chapter 2 runs 2:1-24 (mapped to Saints.Bible 2:1-2 and 2:3-25). The Vulgate also leaves "Filii Dei viventis" ("Sons of the living God") concise without an explicit verb.
