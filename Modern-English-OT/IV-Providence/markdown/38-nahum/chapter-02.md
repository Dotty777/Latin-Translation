## Text

**2:1** Look, on the mountains are the feet of one proclaiming good news and announcing peace. Celebrate your festivals, Judah, and pay your vows, for Belial will no longer pass through you; he has wholly perished.

**2:2** One has come up who will scatter before you, who will maintain the siege: watch the road, strengthen your loins, fortify your might greatly.

**2:3** For the Lord has restored the pride of Jacob, like the pride of Israel; because devastators have laid them waste, and have ruined their shoots.

**2:4** The shield of his mighty men is fiery, the men of the army are in scarlet; the reins of the chariot are fiery on the day of his preparation, and the drivers have been lulled into a stupor.

**2:5** On the roads they have been thrown into confusion; the chariots have collided in the squares; their appearance is like torches, like flashes of lightning darting about.

**2:6** He will remember his mighty men; they will fall on their roads; swiftly they will climb her walls, and the shelter will be prepared.

**2:7** The gates of the rivers have been opened, and the temple has been demolished to the ground.

**2:8** And the soldier has been led away captive, and her maidservants were being driven along, moaning like doves, murmuring in their hearts.

**2:9** And Nineveh, her waters are like a pool of waters; yet they themselves have fled. "Stand, stand!" and there is no one who turns back.

**2:10** Plunder the silver, plunder the gold: and there is no end to the riches from all the desirable vessels.

**2:11** She is laid waste, and split, and torn apart; and a melting heart, and a loosening of the knees, and a failing in all loins, and the faces of all of them are like the blackness of a pot.

**2:12** Where is the dwelling place of the lions, and the feeding ground of the lion cubs, to which the lion went so that the lion cub might enter there, and there is no one to terrify them?

**2:13** The lion seized enough for his cubs, and killed for his lionesses, and filled his caves with prey, and his lair with plunder.

**2:14** "Look, I am against you," says the Lord of hosts, "and I will burn your chariots up into smoke, and the sword will consume your young lions, and I will wipe out your prey from the earth, and the voice of your messengers will no longer be heard."

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — In Hebrew and most English Bibles, this verse is numbered 1:15. The Vulgate retains Belial as a proper name rather than translating it as 'the wicked one'.
- **2:2** — The Vulgate renders qui custodiat obsidionem ('who will maintain the siege') as a second relative clause describing the attacker, whereas the Hebrew shifts to an imperative addressed to Nineveh ('guard the rampart').
- **2:4** — The Vulgate has two notable readings here: 'the reins of the chariot are fiery' (igneae habenae currus; Hebrew 'the chariots flash with steel') and 'the drivers have been lulled into a stupor' (agitatores consopiti sunt; Hebrew 'the cypress spears are brandished').
- **2:8** — The Vulgate distinctly reads 'the soldier' (miles) for the difficult Hebrew hutzav, and 'murmuring in their hearts' (murmurantes in cordibus suis) where the Hebrew has 'beating their breasts'. Late Latin minabantur (passive of mino) means 'were being driven along'.
- **2:9** — The Vulgate reads 'her waters' (aquae ejus) where the Hebrew has 'from her days' ('of old').
