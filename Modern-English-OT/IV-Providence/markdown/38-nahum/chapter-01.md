## Text

**1:1** The burden concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.

**1:2** The Lord is a jealous God and takes vengeance; the Lord takes vengeance and possesses fury; the Lord takes vengeance against His foes, and He Himself is angry with His enemies.

**1:3** The Lord is patient and great in strength, and cleansing, He will not make one innocent. The Lord has His ways in the storm and the whirlwind, and the clouds are the dust of His feet.

**1:4** He rebukes the sea and dries it up, and brings all the rivers to a desert. Bashan and Carmel have grown weak, and the flower of Lebanon has wilted.

**1:5** The mountains have been shaken by Him, and the hills have been made desolate; and the earth has trembled before His face, and the world, and all who dwell in it.

**1:6** Before the face of His indignation, who will stand? And who will resist in the wrath of His fury? His indignation has been poured out like fire, and the rocks have been dissolved by Him.

**1:7** The Lord is good, and gives strength in the day of distress, and knows those who hope in Him.

**1:8** And with a flood passing through, He will make a full end of its place, and darkness will pursue His enemies.

**1:9** What are you devising against the Lord? He Himself will make a full end; a double distress will not rise up,

**1:10** for just as thorns entwine one another, so is their banquet as they drink together; they will be consumed like stubble full of dryness.

**1:11** From you will go forth one devising wickedness against the Lord, pondering transgression in his mind.

**1:12** This is what the Lord says: "If they are complete and so many, even so they will be shorn, and he will pass away; I have afflicted you, and I will not afflict you any longer.

**1:13** And now I will shatter his rod from your back, and I will burst your bonds."

**1:14** And the Lord will give a command concerning you: "No more will be sown from your name; from the house of your god I will destroy the carved image and the cast image; I will make your grave, because you are dishonored."

**1:15** Look, on the mountains are the feet of one proclaiming good news and announcing peace. Celebrate your festivals, Judah, and pay your vows, for Belial will no longer pass through you; he has wholly perished.

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew emphatic construction literally as 'cleansing, He will not make one innocent' (mundans non faciet innocentem), meaning He will by no means acquit the guilty. The Latin also reads plural 'His ways' (viae ejus) where the Hebrew has singular 'His way'.
- **1:7** — Where the Hebrew reads 'a stronghold' and 'those who take refuge in Him', the Vulgate renders 'gives strength' (confortans) and 'those who hope in Him' (sperantes in se).
- **1:12** — Latin pertransibit ('he will pass away') shifts abruptly to the singular after plural attondentur ('they will be shorn'), mirroring the Hebrew syntax.
- **1:13** — The Vulgate distinctly reads 'his rod from your back' (virgam ejus de dorso tuo), whereas the Hebrew has 'his yoke from upon you'.
- **1:15** — In native Clementine Vulgate numbering (Clem1598-USFX), this verse concludes chapter 1 as 1:15 (mapped to Saints.Bible 2:1, shifting 2:1-13 one verse relative to Saints 2:2-14). The Vulgate retains Belial as a proper name rather than translating it as 'the wicked one'.
