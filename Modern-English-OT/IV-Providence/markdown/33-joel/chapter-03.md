## Text

**3:1** And it will happen after these things: I will pour out My Spirit on all flesh, and your sons and your daughters will prophesy; your old men will dream dreams, and your young men will see visions.

**3:2** And even on My male servants and female servants in those days I will pour out My Spirit.

**3:3** And I will give portents in heaven and on earth: blood, and fire, and vapor of smoke.

**3:4** The sun will be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the day of the Lord comes, great and dreadful.

**3:5** And it will happen: everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved, because on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there will be salvation, just as the Lord has said, and among those who remain whom the Lord will call.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — This text follows the four-chapter division of Joel found in the Hebrew; in the three-chapter division, 3:1-5 is numbered 2:28-32.
- **3:2** — Latin servos meos ("My male servants") includes the possessive "My" (agreeing with the Septuagint and Acts 2:18), whereas the Hebrew text reads simply "the male servants."
- **3:3** — Latin vaporem fumi ("vapor of smoke") follows the Septuagint rather than Hebrew "columns of smoke."
