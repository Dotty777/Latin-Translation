## Text

**3:1** For look, in those days and at that time, when I reverse the captivity of Judah and Jerusalem,

**3:2** I will gather all the nations and lead them down into the Valley of Jehoshaphat; and I will contend with them there over My people and My inheritance Israel, whom they scattered among the nations, and they divided My land.

**3:3** And they cast a lot over My people; and they placed a boy in a brothel, and sold a girl for wine so that they might drink.

**3:4** Yet what is there between Me and you, Tyre and Sidon, and all the territory of the Philistines? Will you really pay back vengeance to Me? And if you avenge yourselves against Me, quickly, swiftly I will pay back recompense to you upon your head.

**3:5** For you took My silver and gold, and you brought My desirable and most beautiful things into your shrines.

**3:6** And you sold the sons of Judah and the sons of Jerusalem to the sons of the Greeks, so that you might put them far away from their borders.

**3:7** Look, I will rouse them from the place in which you sold them, and I will turn back your retribution onto your head.

**3:8** And I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hands of the sons of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans, a faraway nation, for the Lord has spoken.

**3:9** Cry this out among the nations: sanctify war, rouse the mighty ones; let all the men of war approach, let them go up.

**3:10** Cut your plows into swords, and your hoes into spears. Let the weak one say, "I am strong."

**3:11** Burst forth and come, all nations from every side, and gather together; there the Lord will cause your mighty ones to fall.

**3:12** Let the nations rise and go up into the Valley of Jehoshaphat, for there I will sit to judge all the nations on every side.

**3:13** Thrust in the sickles, because the harvest has ripened; come and go down, for the winepress is full, the winepresses overflow; for their wickedness has been multiplied.

**3:14** Peoples, peoples, in the valley of cutting down, for the day of the Lord is near in the valley of cutting down.

**3:15** The sun and the moon have been darkened, and the stars have withdrawn their brightness.

**3:16** And the Lord will roar from Zion, and from Jerusalem He will utter His voice, and the heavens and the earth will be shaken; and the Lord is the hope of His people and the strength of the sons of Israel.

**3:17** And you will know that I am the Lord your God, dwelling in Zion, My holy mountain; and Jerusalem will be holy, and foreigners will not pass through her anymore.

**3:18** And it will be in that day: the mountains will drip sweetness, and the hills will flow with milk, and waters will run through all the streams of Judah; and a spring will go out from the house of the Lord and water the torrent of thorns.

**3:19** Egypt will become a desolation, and Idumea a wilderness of destruction, because they acted unjustly against the sons of Judah and shed innocent blood in their land.

**3:20** And Judea will be inhabited forever, and Jerusalem for generation after generation.

**3:21** And I will cleanse their blood, which I had not cleansed; and the Lord will dwell in Zion.

## Translator Notes

- **3:11** — Vulgate "ibi occumbere faciet Dominus robustos tuos" makes the Lord the subject of a future threat of defeat against the enemy's warriors ("cause your mighty ones to fall"), whereas the Hebrew is commonly read as a prayer asking the Lord to bring down His own mighty ones.
- **3:14** — Vulgate reads "Populi, populi, in valle concisionis" ("Peoples, peoples, in the valley of cutting down"), taking the Hebrew term (which can mean "decision") as an act of cutting down or slaughter.
- **3:18** — Vulgate "dulcedinem" ("sweetness") and "torrentem spinarum" ("torrent of thorns") render the Hebrew terms for "sweet wine" and the place-name "Valley of Shittim" (acacias) literally.
- **3:19** — Latin "Idumaea" ("Idumea") is the Greco-Latin territorial name corresponding to Hebrew "Edom," just as "Judaea" ("Judea") in 4:20 corresponds to Hebrew "Judah."
- **3:21** — Vulgate "mundabo sanguinem eorum, quem non mundaveram" ("I will cleanse their blood, which I had not cleansed") renders the debated Hebrew verb literally as purification rather than "avenge" or "acquit."
