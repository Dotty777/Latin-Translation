## Text

**26:1** In the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, this word came from the Lord, saying:

**26:2** "This is what the Lord says: Stand in the courtyard of the house of the Lord, and you will speak to all the cities of Judah, from which they come to worship in the house of the Lord, all the words that I have commanded you to speak to them; do not hold back a word;

**26:3** perhaps they will listen and turn back, each one from his evil way, and I will relent of the harm that I intend to do to them because of the wickedness of their practices.

**26:4** And you will say to them, 'This is what the Lord says: If you do not listen to Me, to walk in My law which I have given to you,

**26:5** to listen to the words of My servants the prophets whom I have sent to you, rising up in the night and dispatching them, and you have not listened,

**26:6** I will make this house like Shiloh, and this city I will make a curse for all the nations of the earth.'"

**26:7** And the priests, and the prophets, and all the people heard Jeremiah speaking these words in the house of the Lord.

**26:8** And when Jeremiah had finished speaking everything that the Lord had commanded him to speak to the whole people, the priests, and the prophets, and all the people seized him, saying, "Let him surely die!

**26:9** Why has he prophesied in the name of the Lord, saying, 'This house will be like Shiloh, and this city will be made desolate because there is no inhabitant'?" And all the people gathered against Jeremiah in the house of the Lord.

**26:10** And the princes of Judah heard these words, and they went up from the house of the king into the house of the Lord, and they sat at the entrance of the new gate of the house of the Lord.

**26:11** And the priests and the prophets spoke to the princes and to all the people, saying, "A sentence of death is due to this man, because he has prophesied against this city, just as you have heard with your ears."

**26:12** And Jeremiah spoke to all the princes and to the whole people, saying, "The Lord sent me to prophesy to this house and to this city all the words that you have heard.

**26:13** Now therefore make your ways and your practices good, and listen to the voice of the Lord your God, and the Lord will relent of the harm that He has spoken against you.

**26:14** But as for me, here I am in your hands; do to me what is good and right in your eyes.

**26:15** However, know and understand that if you kill me, you will bring innocent blood against yourselves, and against this city and its inhabitants; for in truth the Lord sent me to you to speak all these words in your ears."

**26:16** And the princes and all the people said to the priests and to the prophets, "No sentence of death is due to this man, because he has spoken to us in the name of the Lord our God."

**26:17** Then men from the elders of the land rose up, and they spoke to the entire assembly of the people, saying:

**26:18** "Micah of Moresheth was a prophet in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah, and he spoke to all the people of Judah, saying, 'This is what the Lord of hosts says: Zion will be plowed like a field, and Jerusalem will become a heap of stones, and the mountain of the house, high places of the forests.'

**26:19** Did Hezekiah king of Judah and all Judah condemn him to death? Did they not fear the Lord and implore the face of the Lord, and the Lord relented of the harm that He had spoken against them? Therefore we are doing great harm against our souls."

**26:20** There was also a man prophesying in the name of the Lord, Uriah son of Shimei, of Kiriath-jearim, and he prophesied against this city and against this land according to all the words of Jeremiah.

**26:21** And King Jehoiakim, and all his powerful men and princes, heard these words, and the king sought to kill him; and Uriah heard, and was afraid, and fled, and entered Egypt.

**26:22** And King Jehoiakim sent men into Egypt, Elnathan son of Achbor and men with him, into Egypt,

**26:23** and they brought Uriah out of Egypt and brought him to King Jehoiakim, and he struck him with the sword and threw his corpse into the graves of the lowly common people.

**26:24** So the hand of Ahikam son of Shaphan was with Jeremiah, so that he was not handed over into the hands of the people for them to kill him.

## Translator Notes

- **26:3** — Latin pœniteat me mali is literally "it may repent me of the evil," an impersonal idiom rendered here "relent of the harm."
- **26:5** — Latin "de nocte consurgens, et dirigens" ("rising up in the night and dispatching") is the Vulgate's literal rendering of the Hebrew idiom for rising early and sending persistently.
- **26:8** — The Vulgate uses the third-person jussive "Morte moriatur" ("Let him surely die," literally "By death let him die") and third-person "prophetavit" ("has he prophesied") in v. 9, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text addresses Jeremiah in the second person.
- **26:23** — Latin "vulgi ignobilis" ("the lowly common people") expands the Hebrew expression "the sons of the people."
