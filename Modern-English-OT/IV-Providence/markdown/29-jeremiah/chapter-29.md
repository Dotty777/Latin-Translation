## Text

**29:1** The word of the Lord that came to Jeremiah the prophet against the Philistines, before Pharaoh struck Gaza.

**29:2** This is what the Lord says: "Look, waters are rising from the north, and they will be like an overflowing torrent, and they will cover the land and its fullness, the city and its inhabitants. People will cry out, and all the inhabitants of the land will wail,

**29:3** at the noise of the pomp of arms and of his warriors, at the commotion of his chariots and the multitude of his wheels. Fathers did not look back to their children, with hands grown limp,

**29:4** because of the coming of the day on which all the Philistines will be laid waste, and Tyre and Sidon will be shattered along with all their remaining helpers; for the Lord has ravaged the Philistines, the remnant of the island of Cappadocia.

**29:5** Baldness has come upon Gaza; Ashkelon has fallen silent, and the remnant of their valley: how long will you gash yourself?

**29:6** Sword of the Lord, how long will you not rest? Enter into your sheath, grow cool, and be silent.

**29:7** How will it rest, since the Lord has commanded it against Ashkelon and against its coastal regions, and has appointed it there?"

## Translator Notes

- **29:3** — The Vulgate reads pompæ armorum, et bellatorum ejus ("the pomp of arms and of his warriors") and multitudine ("multitude"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text (Jer 47:3) refers to the stamping of the hooves of his stallions and the rumbling of his wheels.
- **29:4** — The Vulgate identifies Hebrew Caphtor (Crete) with the proper name insulæ Cappadociæ ("the island of Cappadocia"). In this same verse, the Latin renders the Philistines with both Philisthiim and Palæstinos.
