## Text

**4:1** "If you return, Israel," says the Lord, "turn back to Me; if you remove your stumbling blocks from before My face, you will not be shaken.

**4:2** And you will swear, 'The Lord lives,' in truth, and in judgment, and in righteousness; and the nations will bless Him, and will praise Him."

**4:3** For the Lord says these things to the man of Judah and to Jerusalem: "Break up new ground for yourselves, and do not sow upon thorns.

**4:4** Be circumcised to the Lord, and remove the foreskins of your hearts, men of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem, so that My indignation may not go out like fire and be kindled, with no one to extinguish it, because of the wickedness of your thoughts."

**4:5** "Announce in Judah, and make it heard in Jerusalem: speak, and blow the trumpet in the land; cry out loudly and say, 'Gather together, and let us enter the fortified cities.'

**4:6** Lift up a standard in Zion; be strengthened, do not stand still, for I am bringing evil from the north, and great destruction."

**4:7** A lion has gone up from his lair, and a plunderer of nations has risen up; he has gone out from his place to make your land a desolation. Your cities will be laid waste, remaining without an inhabitant.

**4:8** Over this bind sackcloth around yourselves; mourn and wail, because the anger of the fury of the Lord has not turned away from us.

**4:9** "And it will be on that day," says the Lord, "the heart of the king will perish, and the heart of the princes, and the priests will be astounded, and the prophets will be dismayed."

**4:10** And I said, "Alas! Alas! Alas! Lord God, have You really deceived this people and Jerusalem, saying, 'There will be peace for you,' and look, the sword has reached even to the soul?"

**4:11** At that time it will be said to this people and to Jerusalem, "A scorching wind on the roads that are in the desert of the way of the daughter of My people, not for winnowing or for cleansing.

**4:12** A full wind from these will come to Me, and now I will speak My judgments with them."

**4:13** Look, he will rise up like clouds, and his chariots like a storm; his horses are swifter than eagles. Woe to us, because we have been laid waste!

**4:14** Wash your heart from wickedness, Jerusalem, so that you may be saved. How long will harmful thoughts lodge within you?

**4:15** For there is a voice of one announcing from Dan, and making known an idol from Mount Ephraim.

**4:16** Tell the nations, "Look, it has been heard in Jerusalem that watchmen are coming from a distant land and raising their voice against the cities of Judah;

**4:17** like watchmen of the fields they have been set over her all around, because she has provoked Me to anger," says the Lord.

**4:18** Your ways and your thoughts have done these things to you. This is your wickedness, because it is bitter, because it has touched your heart.

**4:19** My belly, my belly! I am in pain; the senses of my heart are troubled within me. I will not be silent, because my soul has heard the sound of the war horn, the shout of battle.

**4:20** Destruction upon destruction has been called out, and the whole land has been laid waste; suddenly my tents have been laid waste, in an instant my skins.

**4:21** How long will I see the one fleeing, and hear the sound of the war horn?

**4:22** "For My foolish people have not known Me; they are unwise and senseless sons. They are wise to do evil, but to do good they have not known how."

**4:23** I looked at the earth, and look, it was empty and nothing; and at the heavens, and there was no light in them.

**4:24** I saw the mountains, and look, they were shaking, and all the hills were thrown into turmoil.

**4:25** I looked intently, and there was no man, and every bird of the heavens had departed.

**4:26** I looked, and look, Carmel was a desert, and all its cities were destroyed before the face of the Lord, and before the face of the anger of His fury.

**4:27** For the Lord says these things: "The whole earth will be desolate, yet I will not make a complete end.

**4:28** The earth will mourn, and the heavens above will grieve, because I have spoken. I have purposed it, and I have not relented, nor have I turned away from it."

**4:29** At the sound of the horseman and the one shooting the arrow, every city has fled; they have entered the steep places and climbed up the cliffs. All the cities have been abandoned, and no man dwells in them.

**4:30** And you who are laid waste, what will you do? When you clothe yourself in scarlet, when you are adorned with a golden necklace and paint your eyes with antimony, in vain you beautify yourself. Your lovers have despised you; they will seek your life.

**4:31** For I have heard a voice like that of a woman in labor, distress like that of a woman in childbirth: the voice of the daughter of Zion dying away and spreading out her hands, "Woe to me, because my soul has fainted on account of those who have been killed!"

## Translator Notes

- **4:6** — Latin 'confortamini' ("be strengthened") derives the underlying Hebrew verb from the root for strength rather than "flee for safety."
- **4:15** — Latin 'idolum' ("an idol") translates Hebrew 'aven' in its sense of "idolatry" or "vanity" rather than "disaster" or "calamity."
- **4:21** — Latin 'fugientem' ("the one fleeing") reflects reading the Hebrew noun 'nes' ("standard" or "banner") from the verb meaning "to flee."
- **4:26** — The Vulgate treats Hebrew 'ha-karmel' ("the fruitful land") as the proper place name 'Carmelus' ("Carmel").
- **4:31** — Latin 'propter interfectos' ("on account of those who have been killed") is passive where the Masoretic Hebrew has active "before murderers."
