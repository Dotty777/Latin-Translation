## Text

**48:1** And it happened in the seventh month that Ishmael son of Nethaniah, son of Elishama, of the royal seed, and the nobles of the king, and ten men with him, came to Gedaliah son of Ahikam in Mizpah, and they ate bread there together in Mizpah.

**48:2** Then Ishmael son of Nethaniah rose up, and the ten men who were with him, and they struck Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, with the sword, and killed the one whom the king of Babylon had appointed over the land.

**48:3** Ishmael also struck down all the Jews who were with Gedaliah in Mizpah, and the Chaldeans who were found there, and the fighting men.

**48:4** Now on the second day after he had killed Gedaliah, while no one yet knew,

**48:5** men came from Shechem, and from Shiloh, and from Samaria, eighty men, with their beards shaved, and with their garments torn, and disheveled; and they had offerings and frankincense in their hand to offer in the house of the Lord.

**48:6** So Ishmael son of Nethaniah went out from Mizpah to meet them, walking along and weeping as he went; and when he had met them, he said to them, "Come to Gedaliah son of Ahikam."

**48:7** And when they had come to the middle of the city, Ishmael son of Nethaniah killed them near the middle of the cistern, he and the men who were with him.

**48:8** But ten men were found among them who said to Ishmael, "Do not kill us, for we have treasures in the field, of wheat, and of barley, and of oil, and of honey." And he stopped, and did not kill them with their brothers.

**48:9** Now the cistern into which Ishmael had thrown all the corpses of the men whom he struck down because of Gedaliah is the very one that King Asa made because of Baasha king of Israel; Ishmael son of Nethaniah filled it with those who were killed.

**48:10** And Ishmael led captive all the remnant of the people who were in Mizpah, the daughters of the king, and all the people who had remained in Mizpah, whom Nebuzaradan, commander of the troops, had entrusted to Gedaliah son of Ahikam; and Ishmael son of Nethaniah captured them and departed to cross over to the sons of Ammon.

**48:11** Now Johanan son of Kareah, and all the commanders of the fighting men who were with him, heard of all the evil that Ishmael son of Nethaniah had done,

**48:12** and having taken all the men, they set out to fight against Ishmael son of Nethaniah; and they found him by the many waters that are in Gibeon.

**48:13** And when all the people who were with Ishmael saw Johanan son of Kareah, and all the commanders of the fighting men who were with him, they rejoiced,

**48:14** and all the people whom Ishmael had captured in Mizpah turned around, and turning back, went to Johanan son of Kareah.

**48:15** But Ishmael son of Nethaniah fled with eight men from before Johanan, and went to the sons of Ammon.

**48:16** So Johanan son of Kareah, and all the commanders of the fighting men who were with him, took all the remnant of the common people whom he had brought back from Ishmael son of Nethaniah from Mizpah, after he struck down Gedaliah son of Ahikam: men strong for battle, and women, and children, and eunuchs, whom he had brought back from Gibeon.

**48:17** And they departed and stayed, sojourning in Chimham, which is near Bethlehem, to go on and enter Egypt,

**48:18** from before the Chaldeans; for they were afraid of them, because Ishmael son of Nethaniah had struck down Gedaliah son of Ahikam, whom the king of Babylon had placed in charge in the land of Judah.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**48:45** *(after `48:18`)* In the shadow of Heshbon stood those fleeing from the snare, because a fire has gone out from Heshbon and a flame from the midst of Sihon; and it will devour part of Moab and the crown of the head of the sons of tumult.

**48:46** *(after `48:18`)* Woe to you, Moab: you have perished, people of Chemosh, because your sons and your daughters have been taken into captivity.

**48:47** *(after `48:18`)* And I will turn back the captivity of Moab in the last days, says the Lord. Up to this point are the judgments of Moab.

## Translator Notes

- **48:5** — Latin squallentes ("disheveled" or "unkempt in mourning") describes the men's disheveled state of mourning, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "gashing themselves."
- **48:7** — Latin circa medium laci ("near the middle of the cistern") reflects the elliptical Hebrew phrase "into the midst of the cistern," without an explicit verb of throwing until 48:9.
- **48:9** — Latin propter Godoliam ("because of Gedaliah") interprets the difficult Hebrew phrase "by the hand/side of Gedaliah," where the Septuagint reads "a great cistern."
- **48:17** — Latin peregrinantes in Chamaam ("sojourning in Chimham") translates the Hebrew word geruth as a participle rather than treating "Geruth Chimham" as a compound place name.
- **48:45 *(additional)*** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "de laqueo" ("from the snare") and "partem Moab" ("part of Moab"), contrasting with the Hebrew "without strength" and "forehead of Moab."
