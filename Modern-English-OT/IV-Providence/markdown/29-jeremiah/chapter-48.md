## Text

**48:1** Concerning Moab. This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Woe upon Nebo, for it has been laid waste and put to shame! Kiriathaim has been captured; the strong one has been put to shame and has trembled.

**48:2** There is no longer exultation in Moab against Heshbon; they have plotted evil: 'Come, and let us wipe her out from being a nation.' Therefore in silence you will fall silent, and the sword will follow you.

**48:3** The sound of an outcry from Horonaim: devastation and great shattering!

**48:4** Moab has been shattered; proclaim the outcry to her little ones.

**48:5** For by the ascent of Luhith she will go up weeping in tears, for on the descent of Horonaim the enemies have heard the wailing of shattering.

**48:6** Flee, save your lives, and you will be like tamarisks in the desert;

**48:7** for because you have had confidence in your strongholds and in your treasures, you also will be captured; and Chemosh will go into exile, his priests and his princes together.

**48:8** And the plunderer will come upon every city, and no city will be saved; and the valleys will perish, and the plains will be ruined, for the Lord has spoken.

**48:9** Give a flower to Moab, for blossoming she will go forth; and her cities will be deserted and uninhabitable.

**48:10** Cursed is the one who does the work of the Lord deceitfully, and cursed is the one who holds back his sword from blood.

**48:11** Moab has been fertile from his youth, and has rested on his dregs; nor has he been poured from vessel to vessel, and he has not gone into exile; therefore his taste has remained in him, and his aroma has not been changed.

**48:12** Therefore look, the days are coming," says the Lord, "and I will send to him arrangers and tilters of wine flasks, and they will tip him over, and empty his vessels, and smash their flasks.

**48:13** And Moab will be put to shame by Chemosh, just as the house of Israel was put to shame by Bethel, in which it had confidence.

**48:14** How do you say, 'We are strong, and mighty men for battle'?

**48:15** Moab has been laid waste, and her cities have collapsed, and her chosen young men have gone down to the slaughter," says the King; the Lord of hosts is His name.

**48:16** The ruin of Moab is close to coming, and his calamity will rush on very swiftly.

**48:17** Console him, all you who are around him; and all you who know his name, say, "How has the strong rod been broken, the glorious staff?"

**48:18** Come down from glory and sit in thirst, dwelling of the daughter of Dibon, for the destroyer of Moab has come up against you; he has ruined your strongholds.

**48:19** Stand by the road and watch, dwelling of Aroer; question the one who is fleeing, and to the one who has escaped say, "What has happened?"

**48:20** Moab has been put to shame, for he has been conquered. Wail and cry out; proclaim in Arnon that Moab has been laid waste,

**48:21** and judgment has come to the land of the plain, upon Holon, and upon Jahaz, and upon Mephaath,

**48:22** and upon Dibon, and upon Nebo, and upon the house of Diblathaim,

**48:23** and upon Kiriathaim, and upon Beth-gamul, and upon Beth-meon,

**48:24** and upon Kerioth, and upon Bozrah, and upon all the cities of the land of Moab, those that are far and those that are near.

**48:25** "The horn of Moab has been cut off, and his arm has been shattered," says the Lord.

**48:26** Make him drunk, because he has raised himself up against the Lord; and Moab will strike his hand in his vomit, and he himself also will become an object of derision.

**48:27** For Israel was an object of derision to you, as if you had found him among thieves; therefore, because of your words that you have spoken against him, you will be led away captive.

**48:28** Leave the cities and dwell in the rock, inhabitants of Moab, and be like a dove nesting at the top of the mouth of a cleft.

**48:29** We have heard of the pride of Moab; he is exceedingly proud: his loftiness, and arrogance, and pride, and the haughtiness of his heart.

**48:30** "I know," says the Lord, "his boasting, and that his strength is not in proportion to it, nor has it attempted to act according to what it was able.

**48:31** Therefore I will wail over Moab, and to all Moab I will cry out, to the lamenting men of the clay wall;

**48:32** with the mourning of Jazer I will weep for you, vine of Sibmah. Your shoots have crossed the sea; they have reached as far as the sea of Jazer; upon your harvest and your vintage the plunderer has rushed.

**48:33** Gladness and exultation have been taken away from Carmel and from the land of Moab, and I have removed the wine from the winepresses; by no means will the treader of the grape sing the accustomed chant.

**48:34** From the outcry of Heshbon as far as Elealeh and Jahaz they have raised their voice; from Zoar as far as Horonaim, like a three-year-old heifer; the waters of Nimrim also will be very bad.

**48:35** And I will remove from Moab," says the Lord, "the one who offers on the high places and who sacrifices to his gods.

**48:36** Therefore My heart will resound like flutes for Moab, and My heart will give the sound of flutes for the men of the clay wall; because he did more than he could, therefore they have perished.

**48:37** For every head will be baldness, and every beard will be shaved; on all hands there will be binding, and upon every back, sackcloth;

**48:38** upon all the rooftops of Moab and in its squares, all is mourning; for I have shattered Moab like a useless vessel," says the Lord.

**48:39** How has she been conquered, and they have wailed? How has Moab lowered his neck and been put to shame? And Moab will become an object of derision and an example to all those around him.

**48:40** This is what the Lord says: "Look, like an eagle he will fly and will spread out his wings against Moab.

**48:41** Kerioth has been captured, and the strongholds have been seized; and the heart of the mighty men of Moab on that day will be like the heart of a woman in labor,

**48:42** and Moab will cease to be a people, because he has boasted against the Lord.

**48:43** Terror and pit and snare are upon you, inhabitant of Moab," says the Lord.

**48:44** "Whoever flees from the face of terror will fall into the pit, and whoever climbs up out of the pit will be caught in the snare; for I will bring upon Moab the year of their visitation," says the Lord.

**48:45** In the shadow of Heshbon stood those fleeing from the snare, because a fire has gone out from Heshbon and a flame from the midst of Sihon; and it will devour part of Moab and the crown of the head of the sons of tumult.

**48:46** Woe to you, Moab: you have perished, people of Chemosh, because your sons and your daughters have been taken into captivity.

**48:47** And I will turn back the captivity of Moab in the last days, says the Lord. Up to this point are the judgments of Moab.

## Translator Notes

- **48:2** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew Madmen (often taken as a Moabite town name) etymologically as silens conticesces ("in silence you will fall silent") and attaches contra Hesebon ("against Heshbon") to the opening clause.
- **48:9** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew tsits as florem ("flower") and the following verb as florens ("blossoming"); the Hebrew is obscure and has also been understood as "wings ... fly away" or "salt."
- **48:31** — Both here and in 31:36, the Vulgate translates the Moabite city name Kir-heres etymologically as muri fictilis ("of the clay wall").
- **48:33** — The Vulgate reads Hebrew karmel ("orchard" or "fruitful field") as the proper name Carmel (de Carmelo).
- **48:34** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew Eglath-shelishiyah (often taken as a place name) literally as the ablative phrase vitula conternante ("a three-year-old heifer").
- **48:45** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "de laqueo" ("from the snare") and "partem Moab" ("part of Moab"), contrasting with the Hebrew "without strength" and "forehead of Moab."
