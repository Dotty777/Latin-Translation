## Text

**25:1** The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah (that is the first year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon),

**25:2** which Jeremiah the prophet spoke to all the people of Judah and to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying:

**25:3** "From the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, until this day—this is the twenty-third year—the word of the Lord has come to me, and I have spoken to you, rising in the night and speaking, yet you have not listened.

**25:4** And the Lord sent to you all His servants the prophets, rising at daybreak and sending them; yet you did not listen, nor did you incline your ears to hear,

**25:5** when He said, 'Turn back, each one from his evil way and from your most wicked thoughts, and you will dwell in the land that the Lord gave to you and to your fathers, from everlasting to everlasting;

**25:6** and do not go after foreign gods to serve them and worship them, and do not provoke Me to anger with the works of your hands, and I will not afflict you.'

**25:7** 'Yet you did not listen to Me,' says the Lord, 'so that you provoked Me to anger with the works of your hands, to your own harm.'

**25:8** Therefore this is what the Lord of hosts says: 'Because you have not listened to My words,

**25:9** look, I will send and take all the families of the north,' says the Lord, 'and Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, My servant, and I will bring them against this land, and against its inhabitants, and against all the nations that are around it; and I will put them to death, and I will make them an astonishment, and a hissing, and everlasting desolations.

**25:10** And I will destroy from among them the voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, the sound of the millstone and the light of the lamp.

**25:11** And this entire land will become a desolation and an astonishment; and all these nations will serve the king of Babylon for seventy years.

**25:12** And when the seventy years have been fulfilled, I will visit upon the king of Babylon and upon that nation,' says the Lord, 'their iniquity, and upon the land of the Chaldeans, and I will make it everlasting desolations.

**25:13** And I will bring upon that land all My words that I have spoken against it, everything that is written in this book, whatever Jeremiah has prophesied against all the nations,

**25:14** for they have served them, although they were many nations and great kings; and I will repay them according to their works and according to the deeds of their hands.'"

**25:15** For this is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Take the cup of the wine of this fury from My hand, and you shall give drink from it to all the nations to which I will send you.

**25:16** And they will drink, and be thrown into turmoil, and go mad before the sword that I will send among them."

**25:17** And I took the cup from the hand of the Lord, and gave drink to all the nations to which the Lord sent me:

**25:18** to Jerusalem, and the cities of Judah, and its kings, and its princes, to give them over to desolation, and to astonishment, and to hissing, and to a curse, as it is this day;

**25:19** to Pharaoh king of Egypt, and his servants, and his princes, and all his people;

**25:20** and to all in general: all the kings of the land of Uz, and all the kings of the land of the Philistines, and Ashkelon, and Gaza, and Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod;

**25:21** and Idumea, and Moab, and the sons of Ammon;

**25:22** and all the kings of Tyre, and all the kings of Sidon, and the kings of the land of the islands who are across the sea;

**25:23** and Dedan, and Tema, and Buz, and all who have their hair clipped;

**25:24** and all the kings of Arabia, and all the kings of the west who dwell in the desert;

**25:25** and all the kings of Zimri, and all the kings of Elam, and all the kings of the Medes;

**25:26** also all the kings of the north, from near and from far, each one against his brother, and all the kingdoms of the earth that are upon its face; and the king of Sheshach will drink after them.

**25:27** And you shall say to them, "This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: 'Drink, and get drunk, and vomit, and fall, and do not rise before the sword that I will send among you.'"

**25:28** And when they refuse to take the cup from your hand to drink, you shall say to them, "This is what the Lord of hosts says: 'You will certainly drink;

**25:29** for look, in the city in which My name has been invoked I will begin to bring affliction, and will you be as innocent and exempt? You will not be exempt; for I am calling a sword upon all the inhabitants of the earth,' says the Lord of hosts."

**25:30** And you shall prophesy to them all these words, and you shall say to them: "The Lord will roar from on high, and from His holy dwelling He will utter His voice; roaring, He will roar over His beauty; a chant like that of treaders will be sung in chorus against all the inhabitants of the earth.

**25:31** The sound has reached to the ends of the earth, because the Lord has a judgment with the nations; He Himself enters into judgment with all flesh. 'The wicked I have handed over to the sword,' says the Lord."

**25:32** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "Look, affliction will go forth from nation to nation, and a great whirlwind will go forth from the farthest bounds of the earth.

**25:33** And those killed by the Lord on that day will be from one end of the earth to the other end of it; they will not be mourned, and they will not be gathered, nor will they be buried; they will lie as dung upon the face of the earth."

**25:34** Wail, shepherds, and cry out, and sprinkle yourselves with ashes, leaders of the flock, for your days have been completed for you to be killed, and your dispersions; and you will fall like precious vessels.

**25:35** And flight will perish from the shepherds, and deliverance from the leaders of the flock.

**25:36** The sound of the outcry of the shepherds, and the wailing of the leaders of the flock! For the Lord has laid waste their pastures,

**25:37** and the fields of peace have fallen silent before the wrath of the fury of the Lord.

**25:38** Like a lion He has abandoned His shelter, for their land has become a desolation before the wrath of the dove, and before the wrath of the fury of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **25:3** — Latin de nocte consurgens ("rising in the night") here and consurgens diluculo ("rising at daybreak") in 25:4 both render the Hebrew idiom for rising early and acting persistently.
- **25:14** — In native Clementine Vulgate order, chapter 25 continues directly with the cup of wrath (25:14-38; mapped to Saints.Bible 32:15-38, with 25:14 absent in Saints), while the Oracles against the Nations stand in chapters 46-51. Latin servierunt eis, cum essent gentes multae et reges magni literally renders the dative construction ('they served them, though they were many nations and great kings').
- **25:15** — Native Clementine Vulgate order places the prose/biographical and restoration chapters at 25:14-45:5 (mapped to Saints.Bible 32:15-51:35: Vulgate 25:15-38=Saints 32:15-38; 26=33; 27=34; 28=35; 29=36; 30=37; 31=38; 32=39; 33=40; 34=41; 35=42; 36=43; 37=44; 38=45; 39=46; 40=47; 41=48; 42=49; 43=50; 44=51:1-30; 45:1-5=51:31-35) and gathers the Oracles against the Nations in 46-51 (46=Saints 26; 47=29; 48=31; 49:1-6=30:17-21; 49:7-22=30:1-16; 49:23-27=30:29-33; 49:28-33=30:23-28; 49:34-39=25:14-19; 50=27; 51=28), promoting all 36 previously unplaced verses (2:1, 7:1, 8:11-12, 10:6-8,10, 11:7, 17:1-4, 29:16-20,30, 30:22, 33:25-26, 46:26, 48:45-47, 51:45-48, 52:2-3,15,28-30) into their native slots.
- **25:20** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew kol-ha-ereb ("all the mixed peoples") as universis generaliter ("all in general"), and uses the Greco-Latin name Ausitis (terrae Ausitidis) for the land of Uz.
- **25:24** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew ha-ereb ("the mixed peoples") as occidentis ("of the west"), connecting it with the Hebrew root for evening and west.
- **25:30** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew nawehu ("His fold" or "habitation") as decorem suum ("His beauty"), deriving it from the root meaning comely or beautiful.
- **25:38** — The Vulgate reads Hebrew hay-yonah ("the oppressor" or "oppressing sword") as its homograph meaning "dove," translating a facie irae columbae ("before the wrath of the dove").
