## Text

**23:1** "Woe to the shepherds who destroy and tear apart the flock of My pasture!" says the Lord.

**23:2** Therefore this is what the Lord God of Israel says to the shepherds who tend My people: "You have scattered My flock and driven them out, and have not visited them. Look, I will visit upon you the wickedness of your practices," says the Lord.

**23:3** "And I Myself will gather the remnant of My flock from all the lands to which I will have driven them, and I will bring them back to their own fields, and they will increase and multiply.

**23:4** And I will raise up shepherds over them, and they will tend them; they will no longer be afraid, nor will they be terrified, and none will be missing from the number," says the Lord.

**23:5** "Look, days are coming," says the Lord, "and I will raise up for David a righteous shoot; and a king will reign, and He will be wise, and He will carry out judgment and righteousness in the land.

**23:6** In those days Judah will be saved, and Israel will dwell confidently; and this is the name by which they will call Him: 'The Lord Our Righteous One.'"

**23:7** "Therefore, look, days are coming," says the Lord, "when they will no longer say, 'As the Lord lives, who led the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt,'

**23:8** but rather, 'As the Lord lives, who led out and brought the seed of the house of Israel from the land of the north, and from all the lands to which I had driven them,' and they will dwell in their own land."

**23:9** Concerning the prophets: my heart is crushed within me; all my bones tremble; I have become like a drunken man, and like a man drenched with wine, before the presence of the Lord and before His holy words.

**23:10** For the land is filled with adulterers; for because of the curse the land has mourned, and the fields of the wilderness have dried up; their course has become evil, and their strength is inconsistent.

**23:11** "For prophet and priest are defiled, and in My house I have found their evil," says the Lord.

**23:12** "Therefore their way will be like a slippery place in the darkness; for they will be pushed forward and fall down in it; for I will bring calamities upon them, the year of their visitation," says the Lord.

**23:13** "And among the prophets of Samaria I saw foolishness: they prophesied by Baal and deceived My people Israel.

**23:14** And among the prophets of Jerusalem I saw the likeness of adulterers and a path of falsehood; and they strengthened the hands of the most wicked, so that no one would turn back from his wickedness; all of them have become to Me like Sodom, and its inhabitants like Gomorrah."

**23:15** Therefore this is what the Lord of hosts says concerning the prophets: "Look, I will feed them with wormwood and give them gall to drink; for from the prophets of Jerusalem defilement has gone out over all the land."

**23:16** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "Do not listen to the words of the prophets who prophesy to you and deceive you; they speak a vision of their own heart, not from the mouth of the Lord.

**23:17** They say to those who blaspheme Me, 'The Lord has spoken: "Peace will be yours"'; and to everyone who walks in the depravity of his own heart they have said, 'No evil will come upon you.'"

**23:18** For who has been present in the council of the Lord, and has seen and heard His word? Who has considered His word and listened?

**23:19** Look, the whirlwind of the Lord's indignation will go out, and a storm bursting out will come upon the head of the ungodly.

**23:20** The fury of the Lord will not turn back until He carries out and until He fulfills the thought of His heart; in the last days you will understand His counsel.

**23:21** "I did not send the prophets, yet they ran; I did not speak to them, yet they prophesied.

**23:22** If they had stood in My council and had made My words known to My people, I would certainly have turned them back from their evil way and from their most wicked thoughts."

**23:23** "Do you think I am a God nearby," says the Lord, "and not a God far away?"

**23:24** "If a man hides himself in secret places, will I not see him?" says the Lord. "Do I not fill heaven and earth?" says the Lord.

**23:25** "I have heard what the prophets have said, prophesying falsehood in My name and saying, 'I have dreamed, I have dreamed!'

**23:26** How long is this in the heart of the prophets who foretell falsehood and prophesy the deceptions of their own heart,

**23:27** who want to make My people forget My name because of their dreams, which each one tells to his neighbor, just as their fathers forgot My name because of Baal?

**23:28** Let the prophet who has a dream tell the dream, and let the one who has My word speak My word truthfully. What does chaff have to do with wheat?" says the Lord.

**23:29** "Are not My words like fire," says the Lord, "and like a hammer shattering rock?"

**23:30** "Therefore, look, I am against the prophets," says the Lord, "who steal My words, each one from his neighbor."

**23:31** "Look, I am against the prophets," says the Lord, "who take up their own tongues and say, 'The Lord says.'"

**23:32** "Look, I am against the prophets who dream falsehoods," says the Lord, "who recounted them and led My people astray by their falsehood and by their miracles, when I had not sent them nor commanded them; who have not profited this people at all," says the Lord.

**23:33** "Therefore, if this people, or a prophet, or a priest asks you, saying, 'What is the burden of the Lord?' you will say to them, 'You are the burden, for I will cast you off,'" says the Lord.

**23:34** "And as for the prophet, and the priest, and the people who say, 'The burden of the Lord,' I will punish that man and his house."

**23:35** This is what each of you will say to his neighbor and to his brother: "What has the Lord answered? And what has the Lord spoken?"

**23:36** And "the burden of the Lord" will no longer be mentioned; for each one's own word will be a burden to him, and you have perverted the words of the living God, the Lord of hosts, our God.

**23:37** This is what you will say to the prophet: "What has the Lord answered you? And what has the Lord spoken?"

**23:38** But if you say, "The burden of the Lord," for this reason this is what the Lord says: "Because you have spoken this word, 'The burden of the Lord,' and I sent to you saying, 'Do not say, "The burden of the Lord,"'

**23:39** therefore, look, I will lift you up and carry you, and I will abandon you, and the city that I gave to you and to your fathers, away from My presence;

**23:40** and I will give you over to an everlasting reproach and to an eternal disgrace, which will never be blotted out by forgetfulness."

## Translator Notes

- **23:5** — Latin germen justum ('a righteous shoot') and rex ('a king') are lowercase; capitalization of 'He' reflects traditional messianic interpretation (cf. 33:15-16) and is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **23:6** — The Vulgate has the adjective justus ("The Lord Our Righteous One") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the noun "righteousness," and uses the active plural vocabunt ("they will call"). Capitalization of 'Him' reflects traditional messianic interpretation (cf. 33:16) and is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **23:14** — Vulgate similitudinem adulterantium ("the likeness of adulterers") is a distinctly Latin reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "a horrible thing: committing adultery."
- **23:22** — The Vulgate has the first-person singular avertissem ("I would certainly have turned them back"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the third-person plural ("they would have turned them back").
- **23:32** — Vulgate in miraculis suis ("by their miracles") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which speaks of the false prophets' "recklessness" or "boasting."
- **23:33** — Latin onus (like Hebrew massa) carries a wordplay on both "burden" and "oracle." Vulgate Vos estis onus ("You are the burden") agrees with the Septuagint against the Masoretic vocalization ("What burden?").
