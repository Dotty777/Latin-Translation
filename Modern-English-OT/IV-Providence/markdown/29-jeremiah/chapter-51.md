## Text

**51:1** This is what the Lord says: "Look, I will stir up against Babylon and against her inhabitants, who have lifted up their heart against Me, as it were a pestilent wind;

**51:2** and I will send winnowers into Babylon, and they will winnow her and lay waste her land, for they have come against her from every side on the day of her affliction.

**51:3** Let the one who bends his bow not bend it, and let the one in armor not go up; do not spare her young men; put all her army to death.

**51:4** And those put to death will fall in the land of the Chaldeans, and the wounded in her regions.

**51:5** For Israel and Judah have not been widowed from their God, the Lord of hosts, yet their land is filled with guilt against the Holy One of Israel."

**51:6** Flee from the midst of Babylon, and let each one save his own life; do not keep silent about her iniquity, for it is the time of vengeance from the Lord; He Himself will render recompense to her.

**51:7** Babylon was a golden cup in the hand of the Lord, intoxicating the whole earth; the nations drank from her wine, and therefore they were shaken.

**51:8** Suddenly Babylon has fallen and been shattered. Wail over her; take resin for her pain, if perhaps she may be healed.

**51:9** We treated Babylon, and she was not healed; let us abandon her, and let each one go to his own land; for her judgment has reached up to the heavens, and has been lifted up as far as the clouds.

**51:10** The Lord has brought forth our justice; come, and let us recount in Zion the work of the Lord our God.

**51:11** Sharpen the arrows, fill the quivers; the Lord has stirred up the spirit of the kings of the Medes, and His intent is against Babylon, to destroy her, for it is the vengeance of the Lord, the vengeance of His temple.

**51:12** Against the walls of Babylon lift up a standard, increase the watch, post watchmen, prepare ambushes; for the Lord has planned and has done whatever He spoke against the inhabitants of Babylon.

**51:13** You who dwell upon many waters, wealthy in treasures, your end has come, the foot-measure of your cutting off.

**51:14** The Lord of hosts has sworn by His own life: "Surely I will fill you with men like locusts, and a triumphant chant will be sung over you."

**51:15** He who made the earth by His strength prepared the world by His wisdom, and by His understanding stretched out the heavens.

**51:16** When He utters His voice, waters are multiplied in the sky; He who lifts up clouds from the end of the earth made lightning for the rain, and brought forth the wind from His treasuries.

**51:17** Every man has become foolish apart from knowledge; every metal-caster has been put to shame by a carved image, for their cast work is false, and there is no breath in them.

**51:18** The works are empty and worthy of laughter; in the time of their visitation they will perish.

**51:19** Not like these is the portion of Jacob, for He Himself is the One who made all things, and Israel is the scepter of His inheritance; the Lord of hosts is His name.

**51:20** "You crush weapons of war for Me, and with you I will crush nations, and with you I will destroy kingdoms;

**51:21** and with you I will crush the horse and its rider, and with you I will crush the chariot and its rider;

**51:22** and with you I will crush man and woman, and with you I will crush old man and boy, and with you I will crush young man and virgin;

**51:23** and with you I will crush the shepherd and his flock, and with you I will crush the farmer and his yoked team, and with you I will crush leaders and magistrates;

**51:24** and I will pay back to Babylon and to all the inhabitants of Chaldea all their evil that they have done in Zion, before your eyes," says the Lord.

**51:25** "Look, I am against you, destructive mountain," says the Lord, "who corrupt the entire earth; and I will stretch out My hand against you, and roll you down from the rocks, and make you a mountain of burning;

**51:26** and they will not take from you a stone for a corner, or a stone for foundations; but you will be ruined forever," says the Lord.

**51:27** Lift up a standard in the earth, sound the trumpet among the nations, consecrate the nations against her, proclaim against her to the kings of Ararat, Minni, and Ashkenaz; muster Taphsar against her, bring up horses like bristling locusts.

**51:28** Consecrate the nations against her, the kings of Media, its leaders and all its magistrates, and the entire land of its dominion.

**51:29** And the earth will be shaken and thrown into turmoil, for the plan of the Lord will awaken against Babylon, to make the land of Babylon desolate and uninhabitable.

**51:30** The mighty men of Babylon have ceased from battle; they have remained in the strongholds; their strength has been devoured, and they have become like women; her dwellings have been set on fire, her bars have been shattered.

**51:31** Runner will come to meet runner, and messenger to meet messenger, to report to the king of Babylon that his city has been captured from one end to the other.

**51:32** And the fords have been seized, and the marshes have been set ablaze with fire, and the fighting men have been thrown into turmoil.

**51:33** For this is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "The daughter of Babylon is like a threshing floor at the time of her threshing; yet a little while, and the time of her harvest will come."

**51:34** "Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has eaten me, he has devoured me; he has made me like an empty vessel; he has swallowed me up like a dragon; he has filled his belly with my delicacies, and has cast me out.

**51:35** May the iniquity against me and my flesh be upon Babylon," says the dwelling of Zion; "and my blood be upon the inhabitants of Chaldea," says Jerusalem.

**51:36** Therefore, this is what the Lord says: "Look, I will judge your cause and exact your vengeance; and I will make her sea desolate, and dry up her spring.

**51:37** And Babylon will become heaps, a dwelling of dragons, an astonishment and a hissing, because there is no inhabitant.

**51:38** Together they will roar like lions; they will shake out their manes like lion cubs.

**51:39** In their heat I will set out their drinks, and I will make them drunk so that they sink into a stupor, and sleep an everlasting sleep, and do not rise," says the Lord.

**51:40** "I will bring them down like lambs to the sacrifice, and like rams with young goats.

**51:41** How Sheshach has been captured, and the renowned one of the entire earth has been seized! How Babylon has become an astonishment among the nations!

**51:42** The sea has come up over Babylon; she has been covered by the multitude of its waves.

**51:43** Her cities have become an astonishment, an uninhabitable and desolate land, a land in which no one dwells, nor does a son of man pass through it.

**51:44** And I will punish Bel in Babylon, and cast out from his mouth what he had swallowed; and the nations will no longer stream to him, since even the wall of Babylon will collapse.

**51:45** Come out from the midst of her, My people, so that each one may save his life from the wrath of the fury of the Lord,

**51:46** and so that your heart may not grow faint and you may not fear the report that will be heard in the land; and a report will come in one year, and after this year a report, and wickedness in the land, and ruler over ruler.

**51:47** Therefore, look, days are coming, and I will punish the carved images of Babylon, and all her land will be put to shame, and all her slaughtered will fall in the midst of her.

**51:48** And the heavens and the earth, and all things that are in them, will offer praise over Babylon, because plunderers will come upon her from the north," says the Lord.

**51:49** And just as Babylon caused those killed to fall in Israel, so from Babylon those killed will fall in the entire earth.

**51:50** You who have fled the sword, come, do not stand still; remember the Lord from afar, and let Jerusalem come up upon your heart.

**51:51** We have been put to shame, for we have heard reproach; disgrace has covered our faces, because foreigners have come upon the sanctuary of the house of the Lord.

**51:52** "Therefore, look, the days are coming," says the Lord, "and I will punish her carved images, and throughout all her land the wounded will groan.

**51:53** Even if Babylon ascends to heaven and fortifies her strength on high, from Me her devastators will come," says the Lord.

**51:54** The sound of an outcry from Babylon, and great destruction from the land of the Chaldeans;

**51:55** for the Lord has devastated Babylon, and has destroyed the loud voice from her; and their waves will resound like many waters; their voice has given forth a sound;

**51:56** because a plunderer has come against her, that is, against Babylon, and her mighty men have been captured, and their bow has withered; for the Lord is a mighty avenger; He will surely repay.

**51:57** "And I will make her princes drunk, and her wise men, and her leaders, and her magistrates, and her mighty men; and they will sleep an everlasting sleep and will not wake up," says the King (the Lord of hosts is His name).

**51:58** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "That very broad wall of Babylon will be utterly undermined, and her lofty gates will be burned with fire; and the labors of the peoples will be for nothing, and those of the nations for the fire, and they will utterly perish."

**51:59** The word that Jeremiah the prophet commanded Seraiah the son of Neriah, the son of Mahseiah, when he went with King Zedekiah to Babylon in the fourth year of his reign; now Seraiah was chief of the prophecy.

**51:60** And Jeremiah wrote in one book all the evil that was to come upon Babylon, all these words that are written against Babylon.

**51:61** And Jeremiah said to Seraiah: "When you come to Babylon, and see, and read all these words,

**51:62** you will say: 'Lord, You have spoken against this place to destroy it, so that there is no one who dwells in it, from man to beast, and so that it becomes a perpetual desolation.'

**51:63** And when you have finished reading this book, you will tie a stone to it and throw it into the middle of the Euphrates,

**51:64** and you will say: 'Thus Babylon will sink, and will not rise from the face of the affliction that I am bringing upon her, and she will be dissolved.'" Thus far are the words of Jeremiah.

## Translator Notes

- **51:1** — Latin "qui cor suum levaverunt contra me" ("who have lifted up their heart against Me") translates the Hebrew phrase leb qamai literally rather than decoding it as an atbash cipher for Chaldea.
- **51:6** — Vulgate "nolite tacere" ("do not keep silent") reads the Hebrew verb damam as "be silent"; the verb can also mean "be cut off/perish."
- **51:13** — Latin "pedalis praecisionis tuae" literally means "the foot-measure of your cutting off," corresponding to the Hebrew "cubit" and evoking the set length at which a weaver's web is cut from the loom.
- **51:20** — The Vulgate reads the Hebrew noun mappetz ("war-club/hammer") as a finite verb ("collidis," "you crush") governing "weapons of war," and uses instrumental "in te" ("with you") throughout the following verses.
- **51:27** — The Vulgate transliterates the Hebrew title tiphsar ("marshal" or "military commander") as a proper name, Taphsar, paired with "numerate" ("number/muster").
- **51:37** — Latin "draconum" ("of dragons") renders the Hebrew word for "jackals" in the Masoretic Text, paralleling "draco" ("dragon") in verse 34.
- **51:38** — Vulgate "excutient comas" ("they will shake out their manes") reflects reading the Hebrew verb na'ar as "shake out" rather than "growl," supplying "manes" for the lion imagery.
- **51:41** — "Sheshach" is the standard modern English form of Latin "Sesach," an atbash cipher for Babylon.
- **51:56** — The Vulgate adds the explanatory gloss "id est" ("that is"), renders the verb for the bows as "emarcuit" ("has withered"), and translates Hebrew 'El gemulot ("God of recompenses") as "fortis ultor" ("mighty avenger").
- **51:59** — Vulgate "princeps prophetiae" ("chief of the prophecy") is a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has sar menuchah ("officer of the resting place").
