## Text

**51:1** The word that came through Jeremiah to all the Jews who were living in the land of Egypt, living in Migdol, and in Tahpanhes, and in Memphis, and in the land of Pathros, saying:

**51:2** "This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: You have seen all this evil that I have brought upon Jerusalem and upon all the cities of Judah; and look, they are deserted today, and there is no inhabitant in them,

**51:3** because of the wickedness that they did to provoke Me to anger, going to sacrifice and to worship foreign gods whom they did not know—neither they, nor you, nor your fathers.

**51:4** And I sent to you all My servants the prophets, rising in the night, sending and saying, 'Do not do an abominable thing of this kind, which I hate.'

**51:5** And they did not listen, nor did they incline their ear to turn from their evils and not sacrifice to foreign gods.

**51:6** And My indignation and My fury flared up and were kindled in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, and they have been turned into a wasteland and a devastation, as on this day.

**51:7** And now this is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Why are you doing this great evil against your own souls, so that man and woman, little child and nursing infant perish from among you, from the midst of Judah, and nothing remaining is left to you,

**51:8** provoking Me with the works of your hands, by sacrificing to foreign gods in the land of Egypt, into which you have entered to live there, so that you perish and become a curse and a reproach to all the nations of the earth?

**51:9** Have you forgotten the evils of your fathers, and the evils of the kings of Judah, and the evils of his wives, and your own evils, and the evils of your wives, which they did in the land of Judah and in the regions of Jerusalem?

**51:10** They have not been cleansed to this day, and they have not feared, and they have not walked in the law of the Lord and in My precepts, which I set before you and before your fathers.

**51:11** Therefore this is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Look, I will set My face against you for evil, and I will destroy all Judah.

**51:12** And I will take the remnant of Judah who have set their faces to enter the land of Egypt and live there, and they will all be consumed in the land of Egypt; they will fall by the sword and by famine, and they will be consumed from the least to the greatest; by the sword and by famine they will die, and they will become an oath, and a wonder, and a curse, and a reproach.

**51:13** And I will punish the inhabitants of the land of Egypt just as I punished Jerusalem, with sword, and famine, and plague;

**51:14** and there will be no one who escapes or remains from the remnant of the Jews who go to sojourn in the land of Egypt and to return to the land of Judah, toward which they lift up their souls to return and live there; they will not return, except those who have escaped."

**51:15** Then all the men who knew that their wives were sacrificing to foreign gods, and all the women, of whom a great multitude was standing there, and all the people living in the land of Egypt in Pathros, answered Jeremiah, saying:

**51:16** "As for the word that you have spoken to us in the name of the Lord, we will not listen to it from you;

**51:17** but we will surely do every word that will go out from our mouth, to sacrifice to the queen of heaven and to pour out libations to her, just as we and our fathers, our kings and our princes did in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem; and we were filled with bread, and it went well with us, and we saw no evil.

**51:18** But from the time when we ceased sacrificing to the queen of heaven and pouring out libations to her, we lack everything, and we have been consumed by sword and by famine.

**51:19** And if we sacrifice to the queen of heaven and pour out libations to her, did we make cakes for her to worship her and pour out libations to her without our husbands?"

**51:20** And Jeremiah said to all the people—against the men, and against the women, and against the whole populace—who had answered him a word, saying:

**51:21** "The sacrifice that you sacrificed in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem—you and your fathers, your kings and your princes, and the people of the land—did the Lord not remember these things, and did it not come up upon His heart?

**51:22** And the Lord could no longer bear it because of the wickedness of your pursuits and because of the abominations that you committed; and your land has become a desolation, and an astonishment, and a curse, because there is no inhabitant, as it is this day.

**51:23** Because you sacrificed to idols, and sinned against the Lord, and did not listen to the voice of the Lord, and did not walk in His law, and in His precepts, and in His testimonies, therefore these evils have come upon you, as it is this day."

**51:24** Then Jeremiah said to all the people and to all the women, "Hear the word of the Lord, all Judah who are in the land of Egypt.

**51:25** This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, speaks, saying: You and your wives have spoken with your mouth, and with your hands you have fulfilled it, saying, 'Let us perform our vows that we have vowed, to sacrifice to the queen of heaven and to pour out libations to her.' You have fulfilled your vows, and in deed you have carried them out.

**51:26** Therefore hear the word of the Lord, all Judah who live in the land of Egypt: Look, I have sworn by My great name, says the Lord, that My name will by no means any longer be called upon from the mouth of any Jewish man saying, 'The Lord God lives,' in all the land of Egypt.

**51:27** Look, I will watch over them for evil and not for good; and all the men of Judah who are in the land of Egypt will be consumed by sword and by famine, until they are completely consumed.

**51:28** And those who escape the sword will return from the land of Egypt to the land of Judah, a few men; and all the remnant of Judah, of those entering the land of Egypt to live there, will know whose word will be fulfilled, Mine or theirs.

**51:29** And this is a sign to you, says the Lord, that I will punish you in this place, so that you may know that My words will truly be fulfilled against you for evil:

**51:30** This is what the Lord says: Look, I will hand over Pharaoh Hophra, king of Egypt, into the hand of his enemies and into the hand of those seeking his life, just as I handed over Zedekiah, king of Judah, into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, his enemy and the one seeking his life."

**51:31** The word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch the son of Neriah, when he had written these words in a book from the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, saying:

**51:32** "This is what the Lord God of Israel says to you, Baruch:

**51:33** You have said, 'Woe to wretched me! For the Lord has added sorrow to my sorrow; I have toiled in my groaning, and I have not found rest.'

**51:34** This is what the Lord says: This is what you will say to him, 'Look, those whom I have built I am tearing down, and those whom I have planted I am uprooting, and this whole land;

**51:35** and are you seeking great things for yourself? Do not seek them, for look, I will bring evil upon all flesh, says the Lord, and I will give you your life for safety in all the places to which you may go.'"

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**51:45** *(after `51:35`)* Come out from the midst of her, My people, so that each one may save his life from the wrath of the fury of the Lord,

**51:46** *(after `51:35`)* and so that your heart may not grow faint and you may not fear the report that will be heard in the land; and a report will come in one year, and after this year a report, and wickedness in the land, and ruler over ruler.

**51:47** *(after `51:35`)* Therefore, look, days are coming, and I will visit judgment upon the carved images of Babylon, and all her land will be put to shame, and all her slaughtered will fall in the midst of her.

**51:48** *(after `51:35`)* And the heavens and the earth, and all things that are in them, will offer praise over Babylon, because plunderers will come upon her from the north, says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **51:1** — The Vulgate reads 'through Jeremiah' (per Jeremiam) rather than Hebrew 'to Jeremiah,' and follows the Septuagint in rendering Hebrew Noph as 'Memphis.' In this critical arrangement, chapter 51 corresponds to MT Jeremiah 44-45.
- **51:3** — Here and throughout the chapter (vv. 5, 8, 15, 17-19, 21, 23, 25), the Vulgate renders Hebrew 'burn incense' as 'sacrifice' (sacrificare), and in v. 23 explicitly adds 'to idols' (idolis).
- **51:10** — The Vulgate reads 'They have not been cleansed' (Non sunt mundati) where the Hebrew has 'they have not been contrite/humbled,' and 'in the law of the Lord' (in lege Domini) where the Hebrew has 'in my law.'
- **51:12** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew 'desolation/horror' as 'a wonder' (miraculum), meaning an astonishing spectacle. Likewise in v. 19 the Vulgate reads 'to worship her' (ad colendum eam) rather than Hebrew 'to portray her,' and in v. 25 uses past indicatives where the Hebrew uses ironic imperatives.
- **51:35** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew 'as spoil/booty' as 'for safety' (in salutem). In v. 34, masculine plural 'those whom' (quos) personalizes the Hebrew neuter 'what.'
