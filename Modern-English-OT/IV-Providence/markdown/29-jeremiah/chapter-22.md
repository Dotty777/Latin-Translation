## Text

**22:1** This is what the Lord says: "Go down to the house of the king of Judah, and there you shall speak this word,

**22:2** and you shall say, 'Hear the word of the Lord, king of Judah, you who sit on the throne of David: you and your servants and your people, who enter through these gates.

**22:3** This is what the Lord says: Execute judgment and justice, and deliver the one oppressed by force from the hand of the extortioner; and do not distress the stranger, the orphan, and the widow, nor oppress them unjustly, and do not shed innocent blood in this place.

**22:4** For if you truly carry out this word, then kings of the lineage of David sitting on his throne will enter through the gates of this house, riding chariots and horses, they themselves, and their servants, and their people.

**22:5** But if you do not listen to these words, I have sworn by Myself, says the Lord, that this house will become a desolation.'"

**22:6** For this is what the Lord says concerning the house of the king of Judah: "You are Gilead to Me, the summit of Lebanon; yet I will surely make you a desolation, uninhabitable cities!

**22:7** And I will consecrate against you a destroying man and his weapons; and they will cut down your choice cedars and hurl them into the fire.

**22:8** And many nations will pass through this city, and each one will say to his neighbor, 'Why has the Lord done so to this great city?'

**22:9** And they will answer, 'Because they abandoned the covenant of the Lord their God, and worshiped foreign gods, and served them.'"

**22:10** Do not weep for the dead one, nor mourn over him with weeping; lament for the one who goes away, because he will not return again, nor will he see the land of his birth.

**22:11** For this is what the Lord says concerning Shallum, the son of Josiah, king of Judah, who reigned in place of Josiah his father, who went out from this place: "He will not return here anymore,

**22:12** but in the place to which I carried him away, there he will die, and he will not see this land anymore."

**22:13** Woe to the one who builds his house in injustice, and his upper rooms not in judgment; he will oppress his friend for nothing, and he will not pay him his wages;

**22:14** who says, "I will build for myself a wide house and spacious upper rooms"; who opens windows for himself, and makes paneled ceilings of cedar, and paints with vermilion.

**22:15** Will you reign because you compare yourself to cedar? Did not your father eat and drink, and execute judgment and justice, then when it was well with him?

**22:16** He judged the cause of the poor and needy for his own good; was it not for this reason, because he knew Me?—says the Lord.

**22:17** But your eyes and heart are set on greed, and on shedding innocent blood, and on extortion, and on the course of evil work.

**22:18** Therefore this is what the Lord says concerning Jehoiakim, the son of Josiah, king of Judah: "They will not lament for him, 'Alas, brother!' and 'Alas, sister!' They will not cry aloud for him, 'Alas, lord!' and 'Alas, glorious one!'

**22:19** With the burial of a donkey he will be buried, decayed and cast out beyond the gates of Jerusalem."

**22:20** Go up to Lebanon and cry out, and in Bashan raise your voice; and cry out to those passing by, because all your lovers have been shattered.

**22:21** I spoke to you in your abundance, and you said, "I will not listen." This is your way from your youth, because you have not listened to My voice.

**22:22** The wind will shepherd all your shepherds, and your lovers will go into captivity; and then you will be put to shame and blush because of all your wickedness.

**22:23** You who sit in Lebanon and make your nest in the cedars, how did you groan when pains came upon you, like the pains of a woman in labor?

**22:24** "As I live, says the Lord, even if Jeconiah, the son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, were a ring on My right hand, yet from there I would pull him off,

**22:25** and I will give you into the hand of those who seek your life, and into the hand of those whose face you dread, and into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and into the hand of the Chaldeans;

**22:26** and I will cast you, and your mother who bore you, into a foreign land in which you were not born, and there you will die.

**22:27** And to the land toward which they lift up their soul to return there, they will not return."

**22:28** Is this man Jeconiah an earthen and shattered vessel? Is he a vessel without any pleasure? Why have he and his seed been cast away, and hurled into a land that they did not know?

**22:29** Land, land, land, hear the word of the Lord.

**22:30** This is what the Lord says: "Write this man down as childless, a man who will not prosper in his days; for there will not be a man from his seed who will sit on the throne of David and have power anymore in Judah."

## Translator Notes

- **22:6** — Latin si non posuero (literally, 'if I do not make') is a solemn oath idiom meaning 'I will surely make,' and inhabitabiles literally means 'uninhabitable' (Hebrew: 'uninhabited').
- **22:16** — In verses 15-16, the Vulgate attaches tunc cum bene erat ei ('then when it was well with him') to the question in verse 15 and renders verse 16 as in bonum suum: numquid non ideo quia cognovit me? ('for his own good; was it not for this reason, because he knew Me?'), differing syntactically from the Hebrew.
- **22:17** — Latin ad cursum mali operis ('on the course of evil work') reflects a Vulgate rendering of the Hebrew word for oppression/violence through the root meaning 'to run.'
- **22:19** — The Vulgate reads putrefactus ('decayed/rotted'), whereas the Hebrew reads 'dragged away.'
- **22:20** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew geographic name Abarim as a common participle, ad transeuntes ('to those passing by').
- **22:24** — Here and in verse 28, the Vulgate uses the fuller name Jechonias ('Jeconiah') where the Hebrew has Coniah, renders the signet simply as annulus ('a ring'), and reads the third-person pronoun eum ('him') before shifting to second-person te ('you') in verse 25.
