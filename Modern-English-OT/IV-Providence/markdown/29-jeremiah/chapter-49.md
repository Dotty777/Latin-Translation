## Text

**49:1** Concerning the sons of Ammon. This is what the Lord says: "Does Israel have no sons, or does he have no heir? Why then has Milcom possessed Gad by inheritance, and his people dwelt in its cities?

**49:2** Therefore, look, days are coming," says the Lord, "and I will cause the roar of battle to be heard over Rabbah of the sons of Ammon, and she will be shattered into tumult, and her daughters will be burned with fire, and Israel will possess his possessors," says the Lord.

**49:3** "Wail, Heshbon, for Ai has been laid waste; cry out, daughters of Rabbah: wrap yourselves in sackcloth, lament and go around among the hedges, for Milcom will be led into exile, his priests and his princes together.

**49:4** Why do you boast in the valleys? Your valley has flowed away, delicate daughter, who trusted in your treasures and said, 'Who will come to me?'

**49:5** Look, I will bring terror upon you," says the Lord God of hosts, "from all who are around you; and you will be scattered, each one away from your sight, and there will be no one to gather those who flee."

**49:6** "And after these things I will cause the captives of the sons of Ammon to return," says the Lord.

**49:7** Concerning Idumea. This is what the Lord of hosts says: "Is there no longer wisdom in Teman? Counsel has perished from the sons; their wisdom has become useless.

**49:8** Flee, and turn your backs; go down into the chasm, inhabitants of Dedan, for I have brought the destruction of Esau upon him, the time of his visitation.

**49:9** If grape-gatherers had come upon you, they would not have left a cluster; if thieves by night, they would have seized what was enough for themselves.

**49:10** But I have uncovered Esau; I have revealed his hidden places, and he will not be able to be concealed. His seed has been laid waste, as well as his brothers and his neighbors, and he will be no more.

**49:11** Leave your orphans; I will make them live, and your widows will hope in Me."

**49:12** For this is what the Lord says: "Look, those whose judgment was not to drink the cup will certainly drink; and you, will you be left as though innocent? You will not be innocent, but you will certainly drink.

**49:13** For by Myself I have sworn," says the Lord, "that Bozrah will become a desolation, and a reproach, and a wasteland, and a curse, and all its cities will become everlasting desolations."

**49:14** I have heard a report from the Lord, and an envoy has been sent to the nations: "Gather together, and come against it, and let us rise up for battle."

**49:15** "For look, I have made you small among the nations, contemptible among people.

**49:16** Your arrogance has deceived you, and the pride of your heart, you who dwell in the caverns of the rock and strive to grasp the height of the hill; though you lift your nest high like an eagle, from there I will bring you down," says the Lord.

**49:17** "And Idumea will become deserted; everyone who passes through it will be astonished and will hiss over all its wounds.

**49:18** Just as Sodom and Gomorrah and its neighboring towns were overthrown," says the Lord, "no man will dwell there, and no son of man will inhabit it.

**49:19** Look, like a lion he will come up from the pride of the Jordan to the strong beauty, for suddenly I will make him run to her. And who will be the chosen one whom I will appoint over her? For who is like Me? And who will withstand Me? And who is this shepherd who can resist My face?"

**49:20** Therefore hear the counsel of the Lord that He has formed concerning Edom, and His plans that He has devised concerning the inhabitants of Teman: surely the little ones of the flock will cast them down; surely they will shatter their dwelling with them.

**49:21** At the sound of their downfall the earth shook; the cry of its voice was heard at the Red Sea.

**49:22** Look, like an eagle he will rise up and fly away, and spread out his wings over Bozrah; and the heart of the mighty men of Idumea in that day will be like the heart of a woman in labor.

**49:23** Concerning Damascus. Hamath and Arpad are confounded, for they have heard a most evil report; they are troubled on the sea; because of anxiety it could not rest.

**49:24** Damascus has grown feeble, she has turned to flight; trembling has seized her, anguish and pangs have taken hold of her like a woman in labor.

**49:25** How have they abandoned the praiseworthy city, the city of joy?

**49:26** "Therefore her young men will fall in her squares, and all the men of battle will fall silent in that day," says the Lord of hosts.

**49:27** "And I will kindle a fire in the wall of Damascus, and it will devour the fortifications of Ben-hadad."

**49:28** Concerning Kedar and the kingdoms of Hazor, which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon struck. This is what the Lord says: "Arise, and go up against Kedar, and lay waste the sons of the east.

**49:29** They will seize their tents and their flocks; they will carry off for themselves their hides, and all their vessels, and their camels, and they will proclaim dread over them all around.

**49:30** Flee, depart hastily, stay in the chasms, you who dwell in Hazor," says the Lord, "for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has formed a counsel against you and has devised plans against you.

**49:31** Rise up, and go up against a quiet nation, and one dwelling confidently," says the Lord. "They have neither doors nor bars; they dwell alone.

**49:32** And their camels will become plunder, and the multitude of livestock a prey; and I will scatter them to every wind, those who have their hair clipped, and from every border of theirs I will bring ruin upon them," says the Lord.

**49:33** "And Hazor will become a dwelling place of dragons, deserted forever; no man will remain there, nor will a son of man inhabit it."

**49:34** The word of the Lord that came to Jeremiah the prophet against Elam, in the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, saying:

**49:35** "This is what the Lord of hosts says: Look, I will break the bow of Elam, and their foremost strength;

**49:36** and I will bring upon Elam four winds from the four quarters of heaven, and I will scatter them into all these winds, and there will not be a nation to which the fugitives of Elam do not come.

**49:37** And I will make Elam terrified before their enemies and in the sight of those who seek their life; and I will bring calamity upon them, the anger of My fury," says the Lord, "and I will send the sword after them until I consume them.

**49:38** And I will set My throne in Elam, and I will destroy kings and princes from there," says the Lord.

**49:39** "Yet in the last days I will cause the captives of Elam to return," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **49:2** — The Clementine Vulgate reads in tumultum dissipata ("shattered into tumult"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "a desolate mound" (some Latin manuscripts read tumulum, "mound"). Native Vulgate 49:1-6 maps to Saints.Bible 30:17-21.
- **49:7** — The Vulgate uses the Greco-Latin geographical name Idumæa (Idumea) in 30:1, 11, and 16 alongside Edom in 30:14. It also renders a filiis ("from the sons"), reading Hebrew mibbənim ("from the discerning") as "from the sons."
- **49:19** — Latin ad pulchritudinem robustam ("to the strong beauty") reflects Jerome's rendering of Hebrew neweh ("pasture/habitation") as "beauty" (as also in 27:7, decori justitiæ); likewise, ad illam ("to her") differs from the Hebrew "away from her."
- **49:20** — Latin si non ... nisi ... ("if not ... unless ...") reflects the Hebrew oath idiom expressing strong affirmation ("surely"). The Vulgate also reads plural dissipaverint cum eis habitaculum eorum ("they will shatter their dwelling with them") where Hebrew describes the pasture being appalled at them.
- **49:25** — The Vulgate reads an active plural without a negative (Quomodo dereliquerunt, "How have they abandoned") and urbem lætitiæ ("the city of joy") without a possessive pronoun, whereas Hebrew (Jer 49:25) reads "How is the city of praise not abandoned, the city of my joy."
- **49:33** — The Vulgate renders in habitaculum draconum ("a dwelling place of dragons"), a characteristic Latin rendering of Hebrew tannim ("jackals").
- **49:34** — In native Clementine Vulgate order, the oracle against Elam stands at 49:34-39 (mapped to Saints.Bible 25:14-19). The adopted Latin text (Clem1598-USFX) prints facum for factum ("came").
- **49:39** — Vulgate captivos ("the captives") renders the object personally, whereas the corresponding Hebrew idiom can mean "the fortunes" or "the captivity."
