## Text

**43:1** Now it happened, when Jeremiah had finished speaking to the people all the words of the Lord their God, for which the Lord their God had sent him to them, all these words,

**43:2** that Azariah son of Hoshaiah, and Johanan son of Kareah, and all the arrogant men spoke, saying to Jeremiah, "You are speaking a lie: the Lord our God has not sent you, saying, 'Do not enter Egypt to dwell there.'

**43:3** But Baruch son of Neriah is inciting you against us, to hand us over into the hands of the Chaldeans, to kill us and cause us to be carried away into Babylon."

**43:4** And Johanan son of Kareah, and all the commanders of the fighting men, and all the people did not listen to the voice of the Lord, to remain in the land of Judah.

**43:5** But Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the fighting men took all of the remnant of Judah who had returned from all the nations to which they had previously been scattered, to dwell in the land of Judah:

**43:6** the men, and the women, and the little ones, and the daughters of the king, and every soul whom Nebuzaradan, commander of the troops, had left with Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, and Jeremiah the prophet, and Baruch son of Neriah;

**43:7** and they entered the land of Egypt, because they did not obey the voice of the Lord, and they came as far as Tahpanhes.

**43:8** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah in Tahpanhes, saying,

**43:9** "Take large stones in your hand, and hide them in the vault that is beneath the brick wall at the gate of the house of Pharaoh in Tahpanhes, while the Jewish men look on,

**43:10** and say to them, 'This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Look, I will send and take Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, My servant; and I will set his throne upon these stones that I have hidden, and he will establish his royal seat upon them;

**43:11** and he will come and strike the land of Egypt: those for death, to death; and those for captivity, to captivity; and those for the sword, to the sword;

**43:12** and he will kindle a fire in the shrines of the gods of Egypt, and he will burn them and lead them captive; and he will wrap himself with the land of Egypt just as a shepherd wraps himself with his cloak, and he will go out from there in peace;

**43:13** and he will shatter the statues of the house of the sun that are in the land of Egypt, and he will burn the shrines of the gods of Egypt with fire.'"

## Translator Notes

- **43:9** — Latin in crypta quae est sub muro latericio ("in the vault that is beneath the brick wall") gives a distinctive architectural rendering of two rare Hebrew terms generally taken to mean "mortar" and "brick pavement."
- **43:10** — Latin solium suum ("his royal seat") renders a rare Hebrew word generally taken to mean "canopy" or "pavilion"; quos abscondi ("that I have hidden") keeps the first-person verb within the oracle.
- **43:12** — Latin succendet ("he will kindle") is third person singular, making Nebuchadnezzar the subject (with the Septuagint) rather than first person ("I will kindle") as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text. Latin amicietur ("he will wrap himself") interprets the Hebrew verb as clothing oneself rather than picking clean.
- **43:13** — Latin statuas domus solis ("the statues of the house of the sun") translates the place name Beth-shemesh (Heliopolis in Egypt) rather than transliterating it, and renders the Hebrew "pillars/obelisks" as "statues."
