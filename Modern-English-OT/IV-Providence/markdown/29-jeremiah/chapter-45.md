## Text

**45:1** The word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch the son of Neriah, when he had written these words in a book from the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, saying:

**45:2** "This is what the Lord God of Israel says to you, Baruch:

**45:3** You have said, 'Woe to wretched me! For the Lord has added sorrow to my sorrow; I have toiled in my groaning, and I have not found rest.'

**45:4** This is what the Lord says: This is what you will say to him, 'Look, those whom I have built I am tearing down, and those whom I have planted I am uprooting, and this whole land;

**45:5** and are you seeking great things for yourself? Do not seek them, for look, I will bring evil upon all flesh, says the Lord, and I will give you your life for safety in all the places to which you may go.'"

## Translator Notes

- **45:5** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew 'as spoil/booty' as 'for safety' (in salutem). In v. 34, masculine plural 'those whom' (quos) personalizes the Hebrew neuter 'what.'
