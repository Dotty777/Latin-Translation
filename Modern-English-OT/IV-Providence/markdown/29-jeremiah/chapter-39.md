## Text

**39:1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord in the tenth year of Zedekiah king of Judah; that is the eighteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar.

**39:2** At that time the army of the king of Babylon was besieging Jerusalem, and Jeremiah the prophet was confined in the court of the prison that was in the house of the king of Judah.

**39:3** For Zedekiah king of Judah had confined him, saying, "Why do you prophesy, saying, 'This is what the Lord says: Look, I will give this city into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he will capture it,

**39:4** and Zedekiah king of Judah will not escape from the hand of the Chaldeans, but he will be handed over into the hands of the king of Babylon, and his mouth will speak with his mouth, and his eyes will see his eyes,

**39:5** and he will lead Zedekiah to Babylon, and he will be there until I visit him, says the Lord; and if you fight against the Chaldeans, you will have no success'?"

**39:6** And Jeremiah said, "The word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**39:7** 'Look, Hanamel the son of Shallum, your cousin, will come to you, saying, "Buy for yourself my field that is in Anathoth, for it belongs to you by kinship to buy it."'

**39:8** And Hanamel the son of my uncle came to me, according to the word of the Lord, to the courtyard of the prison, and said to me, 'Take possession of my field that is in Anathoth, in the land of Benjamin, because the inheritance belongs to you, and you are the next of kin to take possession of it.' Then I understood that it was the word of the Lord.

**39:9** And I bought from Hanamel the son of my uncle the field that is in Anathoth, and I weighed out the silver for him: seven staters and ten pieces of silver.

**39:10** And I wrote in a document, and sealed it, and called witnesses, and weighed out the silver on a scale.

**39:11** And I took the sealed document of possession, and the stipulations, and the ratified terms, and the seals on the outside;

**39:12** and I gave the document of possession to Baruch the son of Neriah, the son of Mahseiah, before the eyes of Hanamel my cousin, before the eyes of the witnesses who were written in the document of purchase, and before the eyes of all the Jews who were sitting in the court of the prison.

**39:13** And I commanded Baruch before them, saying,

**39:14** 'This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Take these documents, this sealed document of purchase and this document that is open, and put them in a clay vessel, so that they may last for many days;

**39:15** for this is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Houses and fields and vineyards will again be possessed in this land.'

**39:16** And I prayed to the Lord, after I handed over the document of possession to Baruch the son of Neriah, saying,

**39:17** 'Alas! Alas! Alas! Lord God, look, You made heaven and earth by Your great strength and by Your outstretched arm; no word will be difficult for You,

**39:18** who show mercy to thousands and repay the iniquity of the fathers into the lap of their children after them; most mighty, great, and powerful, the Lord of hosts is Your name.

**39:19** Great in counsel and incomprehensible in thought, whose eyes are open upon all the ways of the sons of Adam, so that You may repay each one according to his ways and according to the fruit of his devices.

**39:20** You who set signs and wonders in the land of Egypt until this day, and in Israel, and among people, and made a name for Yourself, as it is this day.

**39:21** And You led Your people Israel out of the land of Egypt, with signs and with wonders, and with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm, and with great terror;

**39:22** and You gave them this land, which You swore to their fathers that You would give to them, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**39:23** And they entered and possessed it, and they did not obey Your voice, and they did not walk in Your law; all the things that You commanded them to do, they did not do, and all these evils have come upon them.

**39:24** Look, ramparts have been built up against the city so that it may be captured, and the city has been given into the hands of the Chaldeans who are fighting against it, because of the sword, and famine, and pestilence; and whatever You have spoken has happened, as You Yourself see.

**39:25** And You say to me, Lord God, "Buy the field with silver and call witnesses," although the city has been given into the hands of the Chaldeans?'"

**39:26** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, saying,

**39:27** "Look, I am the Lord God of all flesh. Will any word be difficult for Me?

**39:28** Therefore this is what the Lord says: Look, I will hand over this city into the hands of the Chaldeans and into the hands of the king of Babylon, and they will capture it.

**39:29** And the Chaldeans who are fighting against this city will come, and set it on fire, and burn it up, and the houses on whose rooftops they sacrificed to Baal and poured out drink offerings to foreign gods to provoke Me.

**39:30** For the sons of Israel and the sons of Judah were continually doing evil in My sight from their youth; the sons of Israel, who until now exasperate Me by the work of their hands," says the Lord.

**39:31** "For this city has been to Me in fury and in My indignation, from the day on which they built it until this day on which it will be removed from My presence,

**39:32** because of the wickedness of the sons of Israel and the sons of Judah, which they did, provoking Me to anger: they and their kings, their princes, and their priests, and their prophets, the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

**39:33** And they turned their backs to Me, and not their faces, though I taught them early in the morning and instructed them, and they were unwilling to listen so as to receive discipline.

**39:34** And they placed their idols in the house in which My name has been called upon, to defile it.

**39:35** And they built the high places of Baal that are in the valley of the son of Hinnom, to consecrate their sons and their daughters to Molech, which I did not command them, nor did it come up into My heart that they should do this abomination and lead Judah into sin.

**39:36** And now, because of these things, this is what the Lord God of Israel says concerning this city, of which you say that it will be handed over into the hands of the king of Babylon by the sword, and by famine, and by plague:

**39:37** Look, I will gather them from all the lands to which I drove them out in My fury, and in My anger, and in great indignation; and I will bring them back to this place, and I will make them dwell securely;

**39:38** and they will be a people to Me, and I will be God to them.

**39:39** And I will give them one heart and one way, so that they may fear Me all their days, and that it may be well with them and with their children after them.

**39:40** And I will make an everlasting covenant with them, and I will not cease to do good to them; and I will put the fear of Me in their heart, so that they will not depart from Me.

**39:41** And I will rejoice over them when I do good to them, and I will plant them in this land in truth, with all My heart and with all My soul.

**39:42** For this is what the Lord says: Just as I have brought all this great evil upon this people, so I will bring upon them all the good that I am speaking to them.

**39:43** And fields will be possessed in this land, of which you say that it is deserted, because neither man nor beast has remained, and that it has been given into the hands of the Chaldeans.

**39:44** Fields will be bought with money, and they will be written in a document, and a seal will be impressed, and a witness will be called, in the land of Benjamin and around Jerusalem, in the cities of Judah, and in the cities of the mountains, and in the cities of the plains, and in the cities that are toward the south, for I will turn back their captivity," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **39:9** — The Vulgate renders the purchase price with Greco-Roman weight and currency terms (septem stateres, et decem argenteos, "seven staters and ten pieces of silver"), where the Hebrew reads "seven shekels and ten of silver" (seventeen shekels).
- **39:11** — The Vulgate uses Roman legal terms (stipulationes, et rata, "stipulations and ratified terms") and translates the final phrase as signa forinsecus ("seals on the outside"), whereas the Hebrew refers to the open copy of the deed.
- **39:17** — The Vulgate opens with a threefold exclamation (Heu! heu! heu!) and translates the Semitic idiom non erit tibi difficile omne verbum literally ("no word will be difficult for You," similarly in 39:27), using verbum ("word") in the broad sense of "matter" or "thing."
- **39:19** — The Vulgate reads incomprehensibilis cogitatu ("incomprehensible in thought"), whereas the Hebrew reads "mighty in deed."
- **39:35** — The Vulgate reads ut initiarent ("to consecrate/initiate") their sons and daughters to Molech, without the Hebrew phrase "to cause to pass through [fire]."
