## Text

**39:1** In the ninth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and all his army came to Jerusalem and besieged it.

**39:2** And in the eleventh year of Zedekiah, in the fourth month, on the fifth day of the month, the city was broken open,

**39:3** and all the officials of the king of Babylon entered and sat in the Middle Gate: Nergal, Sharezer, Samgar-nebo, Sarsechim, Rab-saris, Nergal, Sharezer, Rab-mag, and all the rest of the officials of the king of Babylon.

**39:4** And when Zedekiah king of Judah and all the men of war saw them, they fled; and they went out of the city by night by the way of the king's garden and through the gate that was between the two walls, and they went out toward the way of the wilderness.

**39:5** But the army of the Chaldeans pursued them, and they captured Zedekiah in the plain of the wilderness of Jericho; and having captured him, they brought him to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, at Riblah, which is in the land of Hamath, and he spoke judgments to him.

**39:6** And the king of Babylon killed the sons of Zedekiah at Riblah before his eyes; and the king of Babylon killed all the nobles of Judah.

**39:7** He also gouged out the eyes of Zedekiah and bound him with shackles so that he might be led to Babylon.

**39:8** The Chaldeans also set fire to the king's house and the house of the common people, and they tore down the wall of Jerusalem.

**39:9** And the remnant of the people who had remained in the city, and the deserters who had defected to him, and the rest of the common people who had remained, Nebuzaradan the captain of the troops carried away into Babylon.

**39:10** And some of the poor among the common people, who had nothing at all, Nebuzaradan the captain of the troops left behind in the land of Judah, and he gave them vineyards and cisterns on that day.

**39:11** Now Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had given orders concerning Jeremiah to Nebuzaradan the captain of the troops, saying,

**39:12** "Take him and set your eyes upon him, and do him no harm; rather, just as he wishes, so do for him."

**39:13** Therefore Nebuzaradan the commander of the troops sent, along with Nebushazban, and Rab-saris, and Nergal, and Sharezer, and Rab-mag, and all the nobles of the king of Babylon;

**39:14** they sent and took Jeremiah from the courtyard of the prison, and handed him over to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, so that he might go into the house and live among the people.

**39:15** Now the word of the Lord had come to Jeremiah while he was confined in the courtyard of the prison, saying,

**39:16** "Go and speak to Ebed-melech the Ethiopian, saying, 'This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Look, I will bring My words upon this city for harm and not for good, and they will be before your sight on that day.

**39:17** And I will deliver you on that day, says the Lord, and you will not be handed over into the hands of the men whom you dread;

**39:18** but rescuing you, I will deliver you, and you will not fall by the sword; rather, your soul will be yours for safety, because you have had confidence in Me, says the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **39:2** — The Clementine Vulgate reads 'on the fifth day of the month' (quinta mensis), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text and Greek Septuagint read 'on the ninth day of the month.'
- **39:3** — The Clementine Vulgate divides the Babylonian name Nergal-sharezer into two separate names (Neregel, Sereser) in both occurrences and leaves the official titles Rab-saris and Rab-mag untranslated as proper names.
- **39:4** — Verses 39:4-13 are restored in native Clementine Vulgate chapter 39 (absent from Septuagint-aligned ch. 46).
- **39:10** — Vulgate reads cisternas ('cisterns'), whereas the Hebrew reads 'fields.'
- **39:13** — As in 39:3, the Clementine Vulgate divides Nergal-sharezer into two names (Neregel, Sereser) and leaves the official titles Rab-saris and Rab-mag untranslated as proper names; Latin also uses a resumptive double verb across 39:13-14 (Misit ... miserunt, "sent ... they sent").
- **39:14** — In native Clementine Vulgate order, 39:14 follows the restored verses 39:4-13 (absent in Saints.Bible chapter 46, where 46:14 directly follows 46:3).
- **39:18** — The Vulgate reads 'for safety' (in salutem), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'as a prize of war.'
