## Text

**11:1** The word that came from the Lord to Jeremiah, saying:

**11:2** "Hear the words of this covenant, and speak to the men of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem,

**11:3** and you will say to them, 'This is what the Lord God of Israel says: Cursed is the man who does not listen to the words of this covenant

**11:4** which I commanded your fathers on the day when I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the iron furnace, saying, "Listen to My voice, and do all things that I command you, and you will be a people for Me, and I will be God for you,"

**11:5** so that I may uphold the oath which I swore to your fathers, that I would give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as it is this day.'" And I answered and said, "Amen, Lord."

**11:6** And the Lord said to me, "Cry aloud all these words in the cities of Judah and outside Jerusalem, saying, 'Hear the words of this covenant, and do them.'

**11:7** for warning, I solemnly warned your fathers on the day I brought them out of the land of Egypt, up to this day; rising in the morning, I solemnly warned them and said, "Hear My voice."

**11:8** And they did not listen, nor did they incline their ear, but they went, each one in the perversity of his evil heart; and I brought upon them all the words of this covenant which I commanded that they should do, and they did not."

**11:9** And the Lord said to me, "A conspiracy has been found among the men of Judah and among the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

**11:10** They have turned back to the former iniquities of their fathers, who refused to listen to My words; and these therefore have gone after foreign gods to serve them; the house of Israel and the house of Judah have made void My covenant which I made with their fathers."

**11:11** For this reason this is what the Lord says: "Look, I will bring upon them evils from which they will not be able to come out; and they will cry out to Me, and I will not hear them.

**11:12** And the cities of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem will go and cry out to the gods to whom they offer libations, and they will not save them in the time of their affliction.

**11:13** For according to the number of your cities were your gods, Judah; and according to the number of the streets of Jerusalem, you have set up altars of shame, altars for offering libations to the Baals.

**11:14** You therefore, do not pray for this people, and do not take up praise and prayer for them, because I will not hear in the time of their cry to Me, in the time of their affliction.

**11:15** What is it that My beloved has committed many crimes in My house? Will holy flesh take away from you your wicked deeds, in which you have boasted?

**11:16** The Lord called your name a lush olive tree, beautiful, fruitful, lovely; at the sound of speech, a great fire blazed up in it, and its thickets were burned up.

**11:17** And the Lord of hosts, who planted you, has spoken evil against you, because of the evils of the house of Israel and of the house of Judah, which they have done to themselves to provoke Me, offering libations to the Baals."

**11:18** But You, Lord, showed it to me, and I knew; then You revealed to me their pursuits.

**11:19** And I was like a gentle lamb that is carried to the sacrifice; and I did not know that they had devised plans against me, saying, "Let us put wood into his bread, and let us blot him out from the land of the living, and let his name be remembered no more."

**11:20** But You, Lord Sabaoth, who judge justly and test the kidneys and hearts, let me see Your vengeance upon them; for to You I have revealed my cause.

**11:21** Therefore this is what the Lord says to the men of Anathoth, who seek your life and say, "You will not prophesy in the name of the Lord, and you will not die at our hands":

**11:22** therefore this is what the Lord of hosts says: "Look, I will bring a reckoning upon them: the young men will die by the sword; their sons and their daughters will die by famine.

**11:23** And there will be no remnant from them; for I will bring evil upon the men of Anathoth, the year of their visitation."

## Translator Notes

- **11:6** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew word for "streets" as foris Jerusalem ("outside Jerusalem").
- **11:12** — Here and in 11:13, 11:17 the Vulgate translates the Hebrew verb for burning incense as libare ("to offer libations").
- **11:16** — Following Clementine Vulgate punctuation, grandis modifies ignis ("a great fire blazed up") after ad vocem loquelae ("at the sound of speech"), where the Hebrew reads "with the roar of a great tempest He has set fire to it."
- **11:19** — The Vulgate reads Mittamus lignum in panem ejus ("Let us put wood into his bread"), where the Hebrew has "Let us destroy the tree with its bread," that is, its fruit.
- **11:20** — Here alone in chapters 9-11 the Vulgate transliterates the Hebrew title Sabaoth ("hosts/armies") as Domine Sabaoth rather than translating it with exercituum.
