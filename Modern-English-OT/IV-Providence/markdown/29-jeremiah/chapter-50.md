## Text

**50:1** The word which the Lord spoke concerning Babylon and concerning the land of the Chaldeans, by the hand of Jeremiah the prophet:

**50:2** "Declare among the nations, and make it heard; lift up a standard, proclaim, and do not conceal it; say, 'Babylon has been captured, Bel has been put to shame, Merodach has been conquered, her carved images have been put to shame, their idols have been overcome.'

**50:3** For a nation has come up against her from the north, which will make her land a desolation, and there will be no one to dwell in her, from man to beast; and they have moved and gone away.

**50:4** In those days and at that time," says the Lord, "the sons of Israel will come, they and the sons of Judah together; walking and weeping they will hasten, and they will seek the Lord their God.

**50:5** They will ask the way to Zion, their faces turned here; they will come and be joined to the Lord in an everlasting covenant that will not be blotted out by any forgetfulness.

**50:6** My people have become a lost flock; their shepherds have led them astray and made them wander on the mountains; they have passed from mountain to hill; they have forgotten their resting place.

**50:7** All who found them devoured them, and their enemies said, 'We have not sinned, because they have sinned against the Lord, the beauty of righteousness, and the hope of their fathers, the Lord.'

**50:8** Withdraw from the midst of Babylon, and go out from the land of the Chaldeans, and be like young goats before the flock.

**50:9** For look, I am stirring up and will bring against Babylon an assembly of great nations from the land of the north, and they will prepare themselves against her, and from there she will be captured; its arrow is like that of a mighty man, a killer; it will not return empty.

**50:10** And Chaldea will become plunder; all who lay her waste will be filled," says the Lord.

**50:11** "Because you exult and speak great things, plundering My inheritance; because you have been let loose like calves upon the grass, and have bellowed like bulls,

**50:12** your mother has been greatly put to shame, and she who bore you has been leveled to the dust; look, she will be the last among the nations, deserted, trackless, and parched.

**50:13** Because of the wrath of the Lord she will not be inhabited, but she will be reduced entirely to a desolation; everyone who passes through Babylon will be appalled and will hiss over all her plagues.

**50:14** Prepare yourselves against Babylon all around, all you who bend the bow; attack her, do not spare the arrows, for she has sinned against the Lord.

**50:15** Shout against her; everywhere she has given her hand; her foundations have fallen, her walls have been destroyed, for it is the vengeance of the Lord; take vengeance on her; just as she has done, do to her.

**50:16** Destroy the sower from Babylon, and the one holding the sickle in the time of harvest; from the face of the sword of the dove, each one will turn to his own people, and every individual will flee to his own land.

**50:17** Israel is a scattered flock; lions have driven him out. First, the king of Assyria devoured him; this last one, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, has crushed his bones."

**50:18** Therefore this is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Look, I will visit the king of Babylon and his land, just as I visited the king of Assyria;

**50:19** and I will bring Israel back to his dwelling place; and he will graze on Carmel and Bashan, and on Mount Ephraim and Gilead his soul will be satisfied.

**50:20** In those days and at that time," says the Lord, "the iniquity of Israel will be sought, and there will be none, and the sin of Judah, and it will not be found; for I will be merciful to those whom I leave behind.

**50:21** Go up against the land of those who rule, and visit upon its inhabitants; destroy and kill what is after them," says the Lord, "and do according to all that I have commanded you.

**50:22** The sound of war is in the land, and great destruction.

**50:23** How has the hammer of the whole earth been broken and crushed? How has Babylon been turned into a desert among the nations?

**50:24** I have ensnared you, and you have been captured, Babylon, and you did not know it; you have been found and seized, because you have provoked the Lord.

**50:25** The Lord has opened His treasury and has brought out the vessels of His wrath, for the Lord God of hosts has a work in the land of the Chaldeans.

**50:26** Come against her from the farthest borders; open up so that those who trample her may come out; lift stones from the road and gather them into heaps; and kill her, and let nothing remain.

**50:27** Destroy all her mighty men; let them go down to the slaughter; woe to them, for their day has come, the time of their visitation!"

**50:28** The voice of those fleeing and of those who have escaped from the land of Babylon, to declare in Zion the vengeance of the Lord our God, the vengeance of His temple.

**50:29** "Declare against Babylon to very many, to all who bend the bow; take your stand against her all around, and let no one escape; repay her according to her work; according to all that she has done, do to her, for she has lifted herself up against the Lord, against the Holy One of Israel.

**50:30** Therefore her young men will fall in her streets, and all her men of war will fall silent on that day," says the Lord.

**50:31** "Look, I am against you, proud one!" says the Lord God of hosts, "for your day has come, the time of your visitation.

**50:32** And the proud one will fall and collapse, and there will be no one to raise him up; and I will kindle a fire in his cities, and it will devour all things around him."

**50:33** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "The sons of Israel and the sons of Judah together endure oppression; all who captured them hold them fast; they refuse to let them go.

**50:34** Their Redeemer is mighty; the Lord of hosts is His name; in judgment He will defend their cause, so that He may terrify the earth and shake the inhabitants of Babylon.

**50:35** A sword against the Chaldeans," says the Lord, "and against the inhabitants of Babylon, and against the princes, and against her wise men.

**50:36** A sword against her diviners, who will become fools; a sword against her mighty men, who will be afraid.

**50:37** A sword against her horses and against her chariots, and against all the multitude that is in her midst, and they will become like women; a sword against her treasures, which will be plundered.

**50:38** A drought will be upon her waters, and they will dry up, for it is a land of carved images, and they boast in portents.

**50:39** Therefore dragons will dwell with fig-eating fauns, and ostriches will dwell in her; and she will no longer be inhabited forever, nor will she be built up through generation after generation.

**50:40** Just as the Lord overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah and her neighboring cities," says the Lord, "no man will dwell there, and no son of man will inhabit her.

**50:41** Look, a people is coming from the north, and a great nation, and many kings will rise up from the ends of the earth.

**50:42** They will take hold of bow and shield; they are cruel and merciless; their voice will sound like the sea, and they will mount upon horses, like a man prepared for battle against you, daughter of Babylon.

**50:43** The king of Babylon has heard the report of them, and his hands have grown feeble; anguish has seized him, pain like a woman in labor.

**50:44** Look, like a lion he will come up from the pride of the Jordan to the strong beauty, for suddenly I will make him run to her. And who will be the chosen one whom I will appoint over her? For who is like Me? And who will withstand Me? And who is this shepherd who can resist My face?"

**50:45** Therefore hear the counsel of the Lord which He has conceived in His mind against Babylon, and His thoughts which He has thought concerning the land of the Chaldeans: unless the little ones of the flocks drag them away, unless their dwelling place is destroyed with them.

**50:46** At the sound of the captivity of Babylon the earth has been shaken, and an outcry has been heard among the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **50:21** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew symbolic place names Merathaim and Pekod as common expressions: terram dominantium ("the land of those who rule") and the imperative verb visita ("visit").
- **50:26** — The Vulgate diverges notably from the Hebrew ("open her granaries; pile her up like heaps"), reading instead "open up so that those who trample her may come out; lift stones from the road and gather them into heaps."
- **50:39** — Latin dracones cum faunis ficariis ("dragons with fig-eating fauns") is a distinctive Vulgate rendering of Hebrew terms for desert animals.
- **50:44** — Latin ad pulchritudinem robustam ("to the strong beauty") reflects Jerome's rendering of Hebrew neweh ("pasture/habitation") as "beauty" (as also in native 50:7 / Saints 27:7, decori justitiae); likewise, ad illam ("to her") differs from the Hebrew "away from her."
- **50:45** — Latin nisi ... nisi ("unless ... unless") literally reproduces the Hebrew elliptical oath formula meaning "surely ... surely," leaving the implied consequence unstated.
