## Text

**30:1** This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord, saying:

**30:2** This is what the Lord God of Israel says: "Write for yourself in a book all the words that I have spoken to you.

**30:3** For look, days are coming, says the Lord, and I will reverse the reversal of My people Israel and Judah, says the Lord; and I will bring them back to the land that I gave to their fathers, and they will possess it."

**30:4** And these are the words that the Lord spoke to Israel and to Judah:

**30:5** For this is what the Lord says: "We have heard a voice of terror; dread, and there is no peace.

**30:6** Ask and see whether a male gives birth: why then have I seen the hand of every man upon his waist, like one in labor, and all faces have turned to pallor?

**30:7** Woe! For great is that day, and there is none like it; and it is a time of tribulation for Jacob, and out of it he will be saved.

**30:8** And it will be on that day, says the Lord of hosts: I will shatter his yoke from your neck, and I will burst his bonds, and foreigners will no longer rule over him;

**30:9** but they will serve the Lord their God, and David their king, whom I will raise up for them."

**30:10** "Therefore do not fear, My servant Jacob," says the Lord, "and do not be dismayed, Israel; for look, I will save you from a distant land, and your seed from the land of their captivity; and Jacob will return, and will rest, and will abound in all good things, and there will be no one whom he fears,

**30:11** for I am with you," says the Lord, "to save you. For I will make a complete end among all the nations in which I have scattered you; yet I will not make a complete end of you, but I will discipline you in judgment, so that you may not seem innocent to yourself."

**30:12** For this is what the Lord says: "Your fracture is incurable; your wound is most severe;

**30:13** there is no one who judges your case for binding up; there is no benefit of remedies for you.

**30:14** All your lovers have forgotten you, and they will not seek you; for with the blow of an enemy I have struck you, with cruel chastisement; because of the multitude of your iniquity, your sins have become hard.

**30:15** Why do you cry out over your crushing? Your pain is incurable; because of the multitude of your iniquity and because of your hard sins, I have done these things to you.

**30:16** Therefore all who eat you will be devoured, and all your enemies will be led into captivity; and those who lay you waste will be laid waste, and all who plunder you I will give as plunder.

**30:17** For I will draw a scar over you, and from your wounds I will heal you, says the Lord. For they have called you 'Outcast,' Zion: 'This is the one who had no one seeking her.'"

**30:18** This is what the Lord says: "Look, I will reverse the reversal of the tents of Jacob, and I will have mercy on his dwellings; and the city will be built upon its height, and the temple will be founded according to its order;

**30:19** and out of them will go forth praise, and the voice of those making merry. And I will multiply them, and they will not be diminished; and I will glorify them, and they will not be weakened.

**30:20** And his children will be as from the beginning, and his assembly will remain before Me, and I will punish all who afflict him.

**30:21** And his leader will be from him, and a prince will be brought forth from his midst; and I will bring him near, and he will approach Me. For who is this who would apply his heart to draw near to Me?" says the Lord,

**30:22** "and you will be a people to Me, and I will be God to you."

**30:23** Look, the whirlwind of the Lord, fury going forth, a rushing storm: it will come to rest upon the head of the wicked.

**30:24** The Lord will not turn back the anger of indignation until He carries out and completes the thought of His heart; in the last of the days you will understand these things.

## Translator Notes

- **30:3** — Latin convertam conversionem (literally "I will turn the turning" or "reverse the reversal," repeated in 37:18) is Jerome's etymological rendering of the Hebrew idiom for restoring fortunes or returning from captivity.
- **30:8** — The Vulgate preserves abrupt pronoun shifts (jugum ejus, "his yoke"; collo tuo, "your neck"; vincula ejus, "his bonds"; ei, "him") and uses dominabuntur ("will rule over") where the Hebrew has a verb meaning "enslave."
- **30:10** — Stage 1 restored Jeremiah 30:10-11 (absent from Septuagint-aligned ch. 37); its wording in Vulgate 30:10 differs in several Latin phrases (salvabo, terra longinqua, captivitatis eorum, cunctis affluet bonis) from the parallel oracle at 46:27.
- **30:11** — Latin ut non videaris tibi innoxius ('so that you may not seem innocent to yourself') is Jerome's distinctive rendering of the Hebrew idiom for not leaving unpunished.
- **30:14** — Vulgate dura facta sunt ("have become hard," echoed by dura peccata, "hard sins," in 37:16) differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which describes the sins as "numerous" or "mighty."
- **30:17** — Latin Obducam cicatricem tibi ("I will draw a scar over you") is a classical medical idiom meaning to close an open wound with scar tissue so that it heals.
- **30:18** — Vulgate in excelso suo ("upon its height") and templum juxta ordinem suum fundabitur ("the temple will be founded according to its order") are distinctive renderings compared to the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("on its mound" and "the citadel will stand in its rightful place").
