## Text

**6:1** Strengthen yourselves, sons of Benjamin, in the midst of Jerusalem; and in Tekoa sound the horn, and over Beth-haccherem raise a banner, because evil has appeared from the north, and great destruction.

**6:2** I have likened the daughter of Zion to a beautiful and delicate woman.

**6:3** Shepherds and their flocks will come to her; they have pitched tents in her all around; each one will pasture those who are under his hand.

**6:4** "Consecrate war against her! Rise up, and let us go up at noon! Woe to us, because the day has declined, because the shadows of evening have grown longer!

**6:5** Arise, and let us go up by night, and let us destroy her houses."

**6:6** For this is what the Lord of hosts says: "Cut down her timber, and cast up a mound around Jerusalem. This is the city of visitation; all oppression is in her midst.

**6:7** Just as a cistern makes its water cold, so she has made her wickedness cold. Iniquity and devastation will be heard in her; before Me continually are sickness and wound.

**6:8** Be instructed, Jerusalem, so that My soul may not depart from you, so that I may not make you desolate, an uninhabitable land."

**6:9** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "They will gather the remnant of Israel as in a vineyard, down to the grape cluster. Turn your hand back like a grape-gatherer to the basket."

**6:10** To whom shall I speak, and whom shall I solemnly warn so that he may hear? Look, their ears are uncircumcised, and they cannot hear; look, the word of the Lord has become a reproach to them, and they will not receive it.

**6:11** Therefore I am full of the fury of the Lord; I have grown weary of holding it in. "Pour it out upon the little child outside, and upon the gathering of young men together; for man with woman will be captured, the old man with the one full of days.

**6:12** And their houses will pass over to others, fields and wives alike, because I will stretch out My hand against the inhabitants of the land," says the Lord,

**6:13** "for indeed, from the least even to the greatest, all devote themselves to greed; and from prophet even to priest, all practice deceit.

**6:14** And they were healing the brokenness of the daughter of My people with disgrace, saying, 'Peace, peace!' and there was no peace.

**6:15** They were put to shame because they committed abomination; rather, with shame they were not ashamed, and they did not know how to blush. For this reason they will fall among those who fall; at the time of their visitation they will collapse," says the Lord.

**6:16** This is what the Lord says: "Stand on the roads and look, and ask about the ancient paths, which is the good way, and walk in it, and you will find refreshment for your souls." And they said, "We will not walk."

**6:17** "And I appointed watchmen over you: 'Listen to the sound of the trumpet!'" And they said, "We will not listen."

**6:18** "Therefore hear, nations, and know, congregation, what great things I will do to them.

**6:19** Hear, earth: look, I will bring evils upon this people, the fruit of their thoughts, because they have not listened to My words, and they have rejected My law.

**6:20** For what purpose do you bring Me frankincense from Sheba, and sweet-smelling cane from a distant land? Your burnt offerings are not acceptable, and your sacrifices have not pleased Me."

**6:21** Therefore this is what the Lord says: "Look, I will set ruins upon this people, and fathers and sons together will fall in them; neighbor and friend will perish."

**6:22** This is what the Lord says: "Look, a people is coming from the land of the north, and a great nation will rise up from the ends of the earth.

**6:23** It will seize arrow and shield; it is cruel and will show no mercy. Its voice will resound like the sea, and they will mount horses, prepared like a man for battle against you, daughter of Zion."

**6:24** We have heard the report of it; our hands have gone limp. Distress has seized us, pains like a woman in labor.

**6:25** Do not go out to the fields, and do not walk on the road, for there is the sword of the enemy, terror all around.

**6:26** Daughter of my people, wrap yourself in sackcloth and sprinkle yourself with ashes; make for yourself mourning as for an only son, a bitter lamentation, because suddenly the destroyer will come upon us.

**6:27** "I have appointed you a strong tester among My people, and you will know and test their way.

**6:28** All these are princes turning aside, walking fraudulently, bronze and iron; all of them are corrupt.

**6:29** The bellows have failed; in the fire the lead has been consumed. In vain has the refiner refined, for their acts of wickedness have not been consumed.

**6:30** Call them rejected silver, because the Lord has rejected them."

## Translator Notes

- **6:1** — The Vulgate reads 'Confortamini ... in medio Jerusalem' ("Strengthen yourselves ... in the midst of Jerusalem") rather than the Hebrew "Flee for safety ... out of the midst of Jerusalem."
- **6:2** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew verb as 'assimilavi' ("I have likened"), reading "I have likened the daughter of Zion to a beautiful and delicate woman," rather than "I will destroy."
- **6:7** — Following an alternate sense of the Hebrew root, the Vulgate renders 'frigidam fecit' ("makes/has made ... cold") rather than "keeps fresh" or "wells up."
- **6:14** — The Vulgate renders 'cum ignominia' ("with disgrace"), connecting the Hebrew term with shame rather than "lightly" or "superficially."
- **6:23** — The Vulgate reads 'Sagittam et scutum' ("arrow and shield") where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "bow and spear" (or "javelin").
