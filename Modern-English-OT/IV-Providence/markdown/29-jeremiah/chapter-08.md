## Text

**8:1** "At that time," says the Lord, "they will cast out the bones of the kings of Judah, and the bones of its princes, and the bones of the priests, and the bones of the prophets, and the bones of those who dwelt in Jerusalem, from their tombs;

**8:2** and they will spread them out before the sun, and the moon, and all the host of heaven, which they loved, and which they served, and after which they walked, and which they sought, and which they worshiped. They will not be gathered, and they will not be buried; they will become a dung heap upon the face of the earth.

**8:3** And they will choose death rather than life, all who remain of this most wicked family, in all the places that are left, to which I have cast them out," says the Lord of hosts.

**8:4** And you will say to them, "This is what the Lord says: Does one who falls not rise again? And does one who has turned away not return?

**8:5** Why then has this people in Jerusalem turned away with a contentious turning away? They have laid hold of falsehood, and have refused to return.

**8:6** I paid attention and listened: no one speaks what is good; there is no one who repents over his sin, saying, 'What have I done?' All have turned to their own course, like a horse rushing headlong into battle.

**8:7** The kite in the sky knows its time; the turtledove, the swallow, and the stork observe the time of their coming; but My people do not know the judgment of the Lord.

**8:8** How do you say, 'We are wise, and the law of the Lord is with us'? Truly, the lying pen of the scribes has produced falsehood!

**8:9** The wise have been put to shame; they are terrified and captured; for they have rejected the word of the Lord, and there is no wisdom in them.

**8:10** Therefore I will give their wives to outsiders, their fields to inheritors, for from the least even to the greatest, all pursue greed; from prophet even to priest, all practice falsehood."

**8:13** "Gathering, I will gather them," says the Lord. "There is no grape on the vines, and there are no figs on the fig tree; the leaf has fallen, and I have given them what has passed away."

**8:14** "Why are we sitting here? Assemble, and let us enter the fortified city, and let us be silent there; for the Lord our God has made us silent and has given us water of gall to drink, for we have sinned against the Lord.

**8:15** We waited for peace, and there was no good; for a time of healing, and look, terror!"

**8:16** From Dan the snorting of his horses was heard; at the sound of the neighing of his warriors the whole land trembled; and they came and devoured the land and its fullness, the city and its inhabitants.

**8:17** "For look, I will send among you basilisk serpents, against which there is no charm, and they will bite you," says the Lord.

**8:18** My sorrow is upon sorrow; within me my heart is grieving.

**8:19** Look, the sound of the cry of the daughter of my people from a distant land: "Is the Lord not in Zion? Or is her king not in her?" "Why then have they stirred Me to anger with their carved images and with foreign vanities?"

**8:20** "The harvest has passed, the summer has ended, and we have not been saved."

**8:21** Over the crushing of the daughter of my people I am crushed and saddened; astonishment has taken hold of me.

**8:22** Is there no resin in Gilead? Or is there no physician there? Why then has the scar of the daughter of my people not closed over?

**8:23** Who will give water to my head, and to my eyes a fountain of tears, and I will weep day and night for the slaughtered of the daughter of my people?

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**8:11** *(after `8:10`)* And they were healing the fracture of the daughter of My people shamefully, saying, "Peace, peace!" when there was no peace.

**8:12** *(after `8:10`)* They were put to shame, because they committed an abomination; yet in fact they were not ashamed with any shame, and they did not know how to blush. Therefore they will fall among those who collapse; at the time of their visitation they will collapse, says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **8:3** — Vulgate renders "eligent" actively ("they will choose") and construes "quae derelicta sunt" ("that are left") with the places of exile, contrasting with the passive Hebrew "death will be chosen."
- **8:5** — Vulgate "aversione contentiosa" ("with a contentious turning away") reflects a distinct understanding of the Hebrew phrase meaning "perpetual backsliding."
- **8:7** — Vulgate "milvus ... ciconia" ("kite ... stork") assigns different bird species from the Hebrew ("stork ... crane").
- **8:13** — A notably difficult verse; the Vulgate literally reads "Congregans congregabo" ("Gathering, I will gather") and "dedi eis quae praetergressa sunt" ("I have given them what has passed away").
- **8:16** — Vulgate "pugnatorum" ("warriors" or "fighters") translates Hebrew "mighty ones/stallions" and refers here to battle-horses, as indicated by "hinnituum" ("neighing").
- **8:11 *(additional)*** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "ad ignominiam" ("shamefully," literally "to disgrace"), rather than "lightly."
