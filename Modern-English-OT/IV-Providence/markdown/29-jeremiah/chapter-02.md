## Text

**2:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**2:2** "Go, and cry out in the ears of Jerusalem, saying, 'This is what the Lord says: I have remembered you, showing compassion on your youth and the love of your betrothal, when you followed Me in the desert, in a land that is not sown.

**2:3** Israel is holy to the Lord, the firstfruits of His produce; all who devour him incur guilt; evils will come upon them, says the Lord.'"

**2:4** Hear the word of the Lord, house of Jacob, and all the families of the house of Israel.

**2:5** This is what the Lord says: "What iniquity did your fathers find in Me, that they went far away from Me, and walked after emptiness, and became empty?

**2:6** And they did not say, 'Where is the Lord, who caused us to go up from the land of Egypt, who led us through the desert, through an uninhabitable and pathless land, through a land of thirst and the image of death, through a land in which no man walked, nor did any human dwell?'

**2:7** And I brought you into the land of Carmel, so that you might eat its fruit and its best things; and when you entered, you defiled My land, and you made My inheritance an abomination.

**2:8** The priests did not say, 'Where is the Lord?' and those holding the law did not know Me, and the shepherds transgressed against Me, and the prophets prophesied by Baal and followed idols.

**2:9** Therefore I will still contend with you in judgment," says the Lord, "and I will dispute with your sons.

**2:10** Cross over to the islands of Kittim and see, and send to Kedar and consider intensely, and see whether anything of this kind has happened:

**2:11** whether a nation has changed its gods, and indeed they themselves are not gods; yet My people have changed their glory into an idol.

**2:12** Be appalled, heavens, at this, and its gates, be utterly desolate," says the Lord.

**2:13** "For My people have done two evils: they have abandoned Me, the spring of living water, and dug for themselves cisterns, shattered cisterns, because they are unable to hold waters.

**2:14** Is Israel a slave, or a homeborn slave? Why then has he become prey?

**2:15** Lions have roared against him and given their voice; they have made his land a desolation. His cities have been burned up, and there is no one to dwell in them.

**2:16** Also the sons of Memphis and Tahpanhes have violated you all the way to the crown of your head.

**2:17** Has this not happened to you because you have abandoned the Lord your God at the time when He was leading you along the way?

**2:18** And now what do you want for yourself on the way of Egypt, to drink murky water? And what do you have to do with the way of the Assyrians, to drink the water of the river?

**2:19** Your wickedness will convict you, and your turning away will rebuke you. Know and see that it is evil and bitter for you to have abandoned the Lord your God, and that the fear of Me is not with you," says the Lord God of hosts.

**2:20** "From long ago you broke My yoke, you snapped My bonds, and you said, 'I will not serve.' For on every lofty hill and under every leafy tree, you stretched yourself out as a prostitute.

**2:21** Yet I planted you as a choice vine, entirely true seed. How then have you turned for Me into something corrupt, a foreign vine?

**2:22** If you wash yourself with natron and multiply for yourself the herb borith, you are stained in your iniquity before Me," says the Lord God.

**2:23** "How do you say, 'I am not polluted; I have not walked after the Baals'? See your ways in the valley; know what you have done: a swift runner unfolding her ways.

**2:24** A wild donkey accustomed to the wilderness, in the desire of her soul she has drawn in the wind of her love; no one will turn her away. All who seek her will not grow weary; in her menstrual time they will find her.

**2:25** Hold back your foot from bareness, and your throat from thirst. Yet you said, 'I have despaired; by no means will I do it, for indeed I have loved strangers, and after them I will walk.'

**2:26** Just as a thief is put to shame when he is caught, so the house of Israel have been put to shame, they themselves and their kings, the princes, and the priests, and their prophets,

**2:27** saying to wood, 'You are my father,' and to stone, 'You gave birth to me.' They have turned their back to Me and not their face; yet in the time of their affliction they will say, 'Arise and deliver us.'

**2:28** Where are your gods that you made for yourself? Let them arise and deliver you in the time of your affliction, for indeed according to the number of your cities were your gods, Judah.

**2:29** Why do you want to contend with Me in judgment? All of you have abandoned Me," says the Lord.

**2:30** "In vain have I struck your sons; they did not receive discipline. Your sword has devoured your prophets; like a ravaging lion

**2:31** is your generation." See the word of the Lord: "Have I become a wilderness to Israel, or a late-bearing land? Why then have My people said, 'We have departed; we will not come to You any longer'?

**2:32** Will a virgin forget her ornament, or a bride her breastband? Yet My people have forgotten Me for countless days.

**2:33** Why do you strive to show your way as good in order to seek love, you who moreover have also taught your ways your wicked deeds,

**2:34** and on your skirts has been found the blood of the souls of the poor and the innocent? I did not find them in ditches, but in all the things that I mentioned above.

**2:35** And you said, 'I am without sin and innocent, and therefore let Your fury turn away from me.' Look, I will contend with you in judgment, because you have said, 'I have not sinned.'

**2:36** How exceedingly worthless you have become, repeating your ways! And you will be put to shame by Egypt, just as you were put to shame by Assyria.

**2:37** For from her also you will go out, and your hands will be upon your head, because the Lord has crushed your confidence, and you will have nothing prosperous in it."

## Translator Notes

- **2:7** — Vulgate "terram Carmeli" ("the land of Carmel") renders Hebrew eretz hakkarmel ("a plentiful/orchard land") as a proper place name.
- **2:12** — Vulgate "portae ejus" ("its gates") reflects a distinct reading of the Hebrew consonants that the Masoretic Text vocalizes as a verb meaning "shudder" or "be horribly afraid."
- **2:16** — Vulgate "constupraverunt" ("have violated") emphasizes moral defilement and dishonor, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has a verb meaning to graze, shave, or break the crown of the head.
- **2:31** — The Vulgate joins "quasi leo vastator" (2:30) with "generatio vestra" (2:31) ("like a ravaging lion is your generation"), and translates Hebrew eretz ma'peliyah ("land of deep darkness") as "terra serotina" ("a late-bearing land"), reflecting the agricultural sense of the Hebrew root.
- **2:34** — Vulgate "in alis tuis" (literally "in your wings") calques the Hebrew idiom for the skirts of a garment. The following clause ("non in fossis ... sed in omnibus quae supra memoravi") is distinctly Vulgate, reading "in ditches" rather than "in breaking in" and expanding "all these" to "all the things that I mentioned above."
