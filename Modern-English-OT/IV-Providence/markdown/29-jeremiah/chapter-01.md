## Text

**1:1** The words of Jeremiah, son of Hilkiah, of the priests who were in Anathoth, in the land of Benjamin.

**1:2** The word of the Lord that came to him in the days of Josiah, son of Amon, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.

**1:3** And it came in the days of Jehoiakim, son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the completion of the eleventh year of Zedekiah, son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the exile of Jerusalem, in the fifth month.

**1:4** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**1:5** "Before I formed you in the womb, I knew you, and before you came out from the womb, I sanctified you, and I gave you as a prophet among the nations."

**1:6** And I said, "Ah, ah, ah, Lord God, look, I do not know how to speak, because I am a boy."

**1:7** And the Lord said to me, "Do not say, 'I am a boy,' for you will go to all things to which I send you, and you will speak everything, whatever I command you.

**1:8** Do not be afraid of their face, because I am with you to rescue you," says the Lord.

**1:9** And the Lord extended His hand and touched my mouth, and the Lord said to me, "Look, I have placed My words in your mouth.

**1:10** Look, today I have appointed you over nations and over kingdoms, to uproot, and to tear down, and to ruin, and to shatter, and to build, and to plant."

**1:11** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying, "What do you see, Jeremiah?" And I said, "I see a watching rod."

**1:12** And the Lord said to me, "You have seen well, because I will watch over My word to do it."

**1:13** And the word of the Lord came to me a second time, saying, "What do you see?" And I said, "I see a kindled pot, and its face is from the face of the north."

**1:14** And the Lord said to me, "From the north evil will be spread out upon all the inhabitants of the land,

**1:15** for look, I will call together all the families of the kingdoms of the north," says the Lord, "and they will come, and each one will set his throne at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem, and against all its walls all around, and against all the cities of Judah.

**1:16** And I will speak My judgments with them concerning all the wickedness of those who have abandoned Me, and have offered libations to foreign gods, and have worshiped the work of their own hands.

**1:17** You, therefore, gird your loins, and rise up, and speak to them all that I command you. Do not be terrified of their face, for I will not cause you to fear their look.

**1:18** For indeed today I have made you a fortified city, and an iron pillar, and a bronze wall against the whole land: the kings of Judah, its princes, and the priests, and the people of the land.

**1:19** And they will wage war against you, and they will not prevail, because I am with you," says the Lord, "to deliver you."

## Translator Notes

- **1:6** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew exclamation of distress (ahah) as a triple cry, "A, a, a" ("Ah, ah, ah").
- **1:11** — Vulgate "virgam vigilantem" ("a watching rod") translates Hebrew maqqel shaqed ("almond rod") by its wordplay sense to preserve the pun with "vigilabo" ("I will watch") in verse 12.
- **1:16** — Vulgate "libaverunt" ("offered libations") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which speaks of burning incense or making smoke offerings.
- **1:17** — Vulgate "nec enim timere te faciam vultum eorum" ("for I will not cause you to fear their look") gives a reassuring promise rather than the warning found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("or I will dismay you before them").
