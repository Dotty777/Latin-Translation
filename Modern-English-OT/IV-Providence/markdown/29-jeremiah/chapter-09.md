## Text

**9:1** Who will give me in the wilderness a lodging place of travelers, that I may leave my people and withdraw from them? For all of them are adulterers, an assembly of transgressors.

**9:2** "And they have stretched out their tongue like a bow of falsehood and not of truth; they have grown strong in the land, because they have gone out from evil to evil, and they have not known Me," says the Lord.

**9:3** "Let each one guard himself from his neighbor, and let him not have trust in any brother of his; for every brother will utterly supplant, and every friend will walk deceitfully.

**9:4** And a man will deride his brother, and they will not speak the truth; for they have taught their tongue to speak falsehood; they have labored to act wickedly.

**9:5** Your dwelling is in the midst of deceit; in deceit they have refused to know Me," says the Lord.

**9:6** Therefore this is what the Lord of hosts says: "Look, I will smelt and test them; for what else should I do because of the daughter of My people?

**9:7** Their tongue is a wounding arrow; it has spoken deceit. With his mouth he speaks peace with his friend, and secretly he sets an ambush for him.

**9:8** Will I not bring a reckoning for these things," says the Lord, "or will My soul not take vengeance on a nation of this kind?

**9:9** Over the mountains I will take up weeping and a lament, and over the fair places of the wilderness a dirge, since they have been burned up, because there is no man passing through, and they have not heard the voice of the owner; from the bird of the sky even to the beasts, they have migrated and withdrawn.

**9:10** And I will make Jerusalem into heaps of sand and lairs of dragons; and the cities of Judah I will make into a desolation, because there is no inhabitant."

**9:11** Who is the wise man who may understand this, and to whom the word of the mouth of the Lord may come, so that he may declare this: why the land has perished and has been scorched like a wilderness, because there is no one who passes through?

**9:12** And the Lord said, "Because they have abandoned My law which I gave them, and have not listened to My voice, and have not walked in it,

**9:13** and they have gone after the perversity of their heart and after the Baals, which they learned from their fathers."

**9:14** Therefore this is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Look, I will feed this people with wormwood, and I will give them water of gall to drink.

**9:15** And I will scatter them among the nations which neither they nor their fathers have known, and I will send the sword after them until they are consumed."

**9:16** This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Consider, and summon the mourning women, and let them come; and send to those who are wise, and let them hurry."

**9:17** Let them make haste and take up a lament over us; let our eyes pour down tears, and let our eyelids overflow with waters.

**9:18** For a voice of lamentation has been heard from Zion: "How have we been devastated and greatly put to shame? Because we have abandoned the land, since our tents have been thrown down."

**9:19** Hear therefore, women, the word of the Lord, and let your ears receive the utterance of His mouth, and teach your daughters a lament, and each woman her neighbor a dirge,

**9:20** for death has climbed up through our windows; it has entered our houses, to destroy the little children from outside, the young men from the streets.

**9:21** Speak, "This is what the Lord says: 'And the carcass of man will fall like dung upon the face of the region, and like hay behind the back of the reaper, and there is no one to gather it.'"

**9:22** This is what the Lord says: "Do not let the wise man boast in his wisdom, and do not let the strong man boast in his strength, and do not let the rich man boast in his riches;

**9:23** but in this let the one who boasts boast: to know and recognize Me, that I am the Lord who practices mercy, and judgment, and righteousness on the earth; for these things please Me," says the Lord.

**9:24** "Look, days are coming," says the Lord, "and I will bring a reckoning upon everyone who has a circumcised foreskin,

**9:25** upon Egypt, and upon Judah, and upon Edom, and upon the sons of Ammon, and upon Moab, and upon all who are clipped in their hair, dwelling in the wilderness; for all the nations have a foreskin, but the whole house of Israel are uncircumcised in heart."

## Translator Notes

- **9:2** — The Vulgate attaches the genitives mendacii et non veritatis ("of falsehood and not of truth") directly to arcum ("bow"), rather than making falsehood the subject of the following clause as in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **9:8** — Latin visitare super (literally "visit upon") is a recurring biblical idiom for judicial reckoning or punishment (cf. 9:24, 11:22), corresponding to visitationis ("visitation") in 10:15 and 11:23.
- **9:9** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew terms for "pastures" and "livestock" etymologically as speciosa ("fair places") and possidentis ("the owner/possessor").
- **9:10** — The Vulgate reads acervos arenae ("heaps of sand") and draconum ("dragons," as also in 10:22), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "heaps of ruins" and "jackals."
- **9:24** — Literally qui circumcisum habet praeputium ("who has a circumcised foreskin"), a distinctly Vulgate rendering of the Hebrew phrase referring to those circumcised outwardly in body while uncircumcised in heart.
