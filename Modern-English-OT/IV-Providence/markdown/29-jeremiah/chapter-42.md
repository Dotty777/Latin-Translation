## Text

**42:1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, saying:

**42:2** "Go to the house of the Rechabites and speak to them, and you will bring them into the house of the Lord, into one chamber of the treasuries, and you will give them wine to drink."

**42:3** And I took Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah, son of Habazziniah, and his brothers, and all his sons, and the entire house of the Rechabites,

**42:4** and I brought them into the house of the Lord, to the treasury chamber of the sons of Hanan son of Igdaliah, the man of God, which was next to the treasury chamber of the princes, above the treasury of Maaseiah son of Shallum, who was keeper of the vestibule;

**42:5** and I set before the sons of the house of the Rechabites bowls full of wine, and cups, and I said to them, "Drink wine."

**42:6** They answered, "We will not drink wine, because Jonadab son of Rechab, our father, commanded us, saying, 'You will not drink wine, you and your sons, forever;

**42:7** and you will not build a house, and you will not sow seed, and you will not plant vineyards nor have any; but you will dwell in tents all your days, so that you may live many days upon the face of the land in which you reside as foreigners.'

**42:8** Therefore we have obeyed the voice of Jonadab son of Rechab, our father, in all that he commanded us, so that we have not drunk wine all our days, we and our wives, our sons and our daughters,

**42:9** and have not built houses to dwell in; and we have not had a vineyard, or a field, or seed;

**42:10** but we have dwelt in tents, and we have been obedient according to all that Jonadab our father commanded us.

**42:11** However, when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up to our land, we said, 'Come, and let us enter Jerusalem from before the army of the Chaldeans and from before the army of Syria,' and we remained in Jerusalem."

**42:12** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, saying:

**42:13** "This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Go and say to the men of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, 'Will you not receive instruction, so that you obey My words? says the Lord.

**42:14** The words of Jonadab son of Rechab have prevailed, which he commanded his sons, that they should not drink wine, and they have not drunk to this day, because they obeyed the command of their father; but I have spoken to you, rising early in the morning and speaking, and you have not obeyed Me.

**42:15** And I sent to you all My servants the prophets, rising at dawn and sending them, and saying, "Turn back, each one from his most wicked way, and make your pursuits good, and do not follow foreign gods nor worship them, and you will dwell in the land that I gave to you and to your fathers." Yet you did not incline your ear, nor did you listen to Me.

**42:16** Therefore the sons of Jonadab son of Rechab have upheld the command of their father that he had commanded them; but this people has not obeyed Me.

**42:17** For this reason, this is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Look, I will bring upon Judah and upon all the inhabitants of Jerusalem all the affliction that I have spoken against them, because I spoke to them, and they did not listen; I called them, and they did not answer Me.'"

**42:18** But to the house of the Rechabites Jeremiah said, "This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Because you have obeyed the command of Jonadab your father, and have kept all his commandments, and have done all that he commanded you,

**42:19** therefore this is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: There will not be lacking a man from the lineage of Jonadab son of Rechab, standing in My presence all the days."

## Translator Notes

- **42:2** — The Vulgate reads 'in unam exedram thesaurorum' ('into one chamber of the treasuries'), specifying treasury chambers where the Hebrew simply has 'one of the chambers,' anticipating 'gazophylacium' ('treasury chamber') and 'thesaurum' ('treasury') in verse 4.
- **42:10** — The Vulgate reads 'et obedientes fuimus' ('and we have been obedient'), condensing the Hebrew paired verbs 'we have obeyed and done.'
- **42:11** — The Vulgate includes the possessive 'nostram' ('ad terram nostram,' 'to our land') where the Hebrew reads simply 'to the land,' and renders Hebrew 'Aram' as 'Syriae' ('of Syria').
- **42:19** — The Vulgate explicitly supplies 'de stirpe' ('from the lineage') before 'Jonadab son of Rechab,' clarifying the Hebrew phrasing 'for Jonadab son of Rechab.'
