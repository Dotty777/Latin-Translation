## Text

**36:1** And these are the words of the letter that Jeremiah the prophet sent from Jerusalem to the remnant of the elders of the exile, and to the priests, and to the prophets, and to all the people whom Nebuchadnezzar had led away from Jerusalem into Babylon,

**36:2** after Jeconiah the king, and the lady, and the eunuchs, and the princes of Judah and Jerusalem, and the craftsman and the locksmith had gone out from Jerusalem,

**36:3** by the hand of Elasah son of Shaphan and Gemariah son of Hilkiah, whom Zedekiah king of Judah sent to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, into Babylon, saying:

**36:4** "This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says to all the exiles whom I carried away from Jerusalem into Babylon:

**36:5** Build houses and live in them, and plant gardens and eat their fruit.

**36:6** Take wives and father sons and daughters; and give wives to your sons, and give your daughters to husbands, and let them bear sons and daughters; and multiply there, and do not be few in number.

**36:7** And seek the peace of the city to which I caused you to go into exile, and pray for it to the Lord, for in its peace there will be peace for you.

**36:8** For this is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Do not let your prophets who are in your midst deceive you, nor your diviners, and do not pay attention to your dreams that you yourselves dream;

**36:9** for they themselves prophesy falsely to you in My name, and I did not send them, says the Lord.

**36:10** For this is what the Lord says: When seventy years begin to be fulfilled in Babylon, I will visit you, and I will raise up My good word over you, so that I may bring you back to this place.

**36:11** For I Myself know the thoughts that I ponder concerning you, declares the Lord, thoughts of peace and not of affliction, so that I may give you an end and patience.

**36:12** And you will call upon Me, and you will go, and you will pray to Me, and I will listen to you.

**36:13** You will seek Me and find Me, when you seek Me with your whole heart.

**36:14** And I will be found by you, declares the Lord; and I will bring back your captivity, and I will gather you from all the nations and from all the places to which I drove you out, says the Lord, and I will make you return from the place to which I caused you to go into exile.

**36:15** Because you have said, 'The Lord has raised up prophets for us in Babylon':

**36:21** This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says concerning Ahab son of Kolaiah and concerning Zedekiah son of Maaseiah, who prophesy falsely to you in My name: Look, I will hand them over into the hands of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will strike them down before your eyes;

**36:22** and a curse will be taken up from them by all the exiles of Judah who are in Babylon, saying, 'May the Lord make you like Zedekiah and like Ahab, whom the king of Babylon roasted in the fire';

**36:23** because they have committed folly in Israel, and have committed adultery with the wives of their friends, and have spoken a word falsely in My name, which I did not command them. I am judge and witness, says the Lord."

**36:24** And to Shemaiah the Nehelamite you shall say:

**36:25** "This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Because you have sent letters in your own name to all the people who are in Jerusalem, and to Zephaniah son of Maaseiah the priest, and to all the priests, saying,

**36:26** 'The Lord has appointed you priest in place of Jehoiada the priest, so that you may be a leader in the house of the Lord over every man who is mad and prophesying, so that you may put him in the stocks and in prison;

**36:27** and now why have you not rebuked Jeremiah the Anathothite, who prophesies to you?

**36:28** For on this account he sent to us in Babylon, saying, "It is long: build houses and live in them, and plant gardens and eat their fruits."'"

**36:29** Therefore Zephaniah the priest read this letter in the ears of Jeremiah the prophet.

**36:30** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, saying:

**36:31** "Send to all the exiles, saying, 'This is what the Lord says concerning Shemaiah the Nehelamite: Because Shemaiah has prophesied to you, and I did not send him, and he has made you trust in a lie,

**36:32** therefore this is what the Lord says: Look, I will punish Shemaiah the Nehelamite and his offspring; he will not have a man living in the midst of this people, and he will not see the good that I will do for My people, declares the Lord, because he has spoken rebellion against the Lord.'"

## Translator Notes

- **36:2** — Latin domina ("the lady" or "mistress") refers to the queen mother. Latin faber et inclusor ("craftsman and locksmith") are collective singulars, with inclusor literally denoting an "encloser" (locksmith or metal-setter).
- **36:10** — Vulgate Cum coeperint impleri ("When seventy years begin to be fulfilled") is a distinctive reading differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("when seventy years are completed").
- **36:11** — Vulgate ut dem vobis finem et patientiam ("so that I may give you an end and patience") is a notable Vulgate reading, where patientiam ("patience" or "endurance") stands in place of Hebrew "hope" and finem ("end" or "outcome") in place of "future."
- **36:14** — Vulgate de loco ad quem ("from the place to which") reverses the prepositions of the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("to the place from which"), referring to returning out of Babylon rather than back to Jerusalem.
- **36:23** — Vulgate Ego sum judex et testis ("I am judge and witness") is a distinctive reading compared to the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("I am the one who knows and am witness").
