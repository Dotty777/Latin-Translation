## Text

**36:1** And it happened in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, that this word came to Jeremiah from the Lord, saying:

**36:2** "Take a scroll of a book, and write in it all the words that I have spoken to you against Israel and Judah, and against all the nations, from the day on which I spoke to you, from the days of Josiah until this day,

**36:3** if perhaps, when the house of Judah hears all the evils that I intend to do to them, each one may turn back from his most wicked way, and I will be merciful toward their iniquity and their sin."

**36:4** Therefore Jeremiah called Baruch son of Neriah, and Baruch wrote from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the Lord that He had spoken to him, in a scroll of a book.

**36:5** And Jeremiah commanded Baruch, saying, "I am confined, and I am not able to enter the house of the Lord.

**36:6** So you go in, and read from the scroll in which you wrote the words of the Lord from my mouth, in the hearing of the people in the house of the Lord, on a day of fasting; and moreover, in the hearing of all Judah who come from their cities, you will read to them,

**36:7** if perhaps their prayer may fall in the presence of the Lord, and each one may turn back from his most wicked way; for great is the fury and indignation that the Lord has spoken against this people."

**36:8** And Baruch son of Neriah did according to all that Jeremiah the prophet had commanded him, reading from the scroll the words of the Lord in the house of the Lord.

**36:9** Now it happened in the fifth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, in the ninth month, that they proclaimed a fast in the presence of the Lord to all the people in Jerusalem, and to the entire multitude that had streamed together from the cities of Judah into Jerusalem.

**36:10** And Baruch read from the scroll the words of Jeremiah in the house of the Lord, in the treasury of Gemariah son of Shaphan the scribe, in the upper courtyard, at the entrance of the New Gate of the house of the Lord, in the hearing of all the people.

**36:11** And when Micaiah son of Gemariah, son of Shaphan, had heard all the words of the Lord from the book,

**36:12** he went down into the king's house, to the treasury of the scribe, and look, all the officials were sitting there: Elishama the scribe, and Delaiah son of Shemaiah, and Elnathan son of Achbor, and Gemariah son of Shaphan, and Zedekiah son of Hananiah, and all the officials;

**36:13** and Micaiah reported to them all the words that he heard as Baruch read from the scroll in the ears of the people.

**36:14** Therefore all the officials sent Jehudi son of Nethaniah, son of Shelemiah, son of Cushi, to Baruch, saying, "Take in your hand the scroll from which you read in the hearing of the people, and come." So Baruch son of Neriah took the scroll in his hand and came to them.

**36:15** And they said to him, "Sit down and read these things in our ears." And Baruch read in their ears.

**36:16** So when they had heard all the words, they were stunned, each one toward his neighbor, and they said to Baruch, "We must report all these words to the king."

**36:17** And they questioned him, saying, "Tell us how you wrote all these words from his mouth."

**36:18** Then Baruch said to them, "From his mouth he was speaking all these words to me as though reading, and I was writing in the scroll with ink."

**36:19** And the officials said to Baruch, "Go and hide yourself, you and Jeremiah, and let no one know where you are."

**36:20** And they went in to the king in the court; however, they deposited the scroll in the treasury of Elishama the scribe, and they reported all the words in the hearing of the king.

**36:21** And the king sent Jehudi to fetch the scroll; and taking it from the treasury of Elishama the scribe, he read it in the hearing of the king and of all the officials who were standing around the king.

**36:22** Now the king was sitting in the winter house, in the ninth month, and a brazier full of burning coals was set before him.

**36:23** And when Jehudi had read three or four columns, he cut it with a scribe's knife and threw it into the fire that was on the brazier, until the entire scroll was consumed by the fire that was in the brazier.

**36:24** And the king and all his servants who heard all these words were not afraid, nor did they tear their garments.

**36:25** Nevertheless, Elnathan, Delaiah, and Gemariah opposed the king so that he would not burn the book, and he did not listen to them.

**36:26** And the king commanded Jerahmeel son of Amelech, and Seraiah son of Azriel, and Shelemiah son of Abdeel, to arrest Baruch the scribe and Jeremiah the prophet; but the Lord hid them.

**36:27** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah the prophet, after the king had burned the scroll and the words that Baruch had written from the mouth of Jeremiah, saying:

**36:28** "Again, take another scroll, and write in it all the former words that were in the first scroll, which Jehoiakim king of Judah burned.

**36:29** And to Jehoiakim king of Judah you will say, 'This is what the Lord says: You have burned that scroll, saying, "Why have you written in it, announcing, 'The king of Babylon will swiftly come and lay waste this land, and cause man and beast to cease from it'?"

**36:30** Therefore this is what the Lord says against Jehoiakim king of Judah: There will not be anyone from him to sit upon the throne of David, and his corpse will be cast out to the heat by day and to the frost by night.

**36:31** And I will visit their iniquities against him, and against his seed, and against his servants; and I will bring upon them, and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and upon the men of Judah, all the evil that I spoke to them, and they did not listen.'"

**36:32** Then Jeremiah took another scroll and gave it to Baruch son of Neriah the scribe, who wrote in it from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the book that Jehoiakim king of Judah had burned with fire; and in addition, many more words were added than there had been before.

## Translator Notes

- **36:10** — Latin in gazophylacio ("in the treasury") uses a Greek loanword for the temple and palace office chambers (also in vv. 12, 20, and 21), where the Hebrew has lishkah ("chamber").
- **36:18** — Latin loquebatur quasi legens ("was speaking ... as though reading") is a distinctive Vulgate rendering of the Hebrew verb for reciting or dictating aloud.
- **36:26** — Latin filio Amelech ("son of Amelech") treats the Hebrew phrase ben-hammelekh ("the king's son") as a personal proper name.
- **36:29** — Latin Festinus veniet ("will swiftly come") renders the emphatic Hebrew infinitive construction ("will surely come") with an adjective denoting haste.
- **36:32** — Latin multo plures quam antea fuerant ("many more ... than there had been before") differs from the Hebrew reading ("many similar words").
