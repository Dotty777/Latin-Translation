## Text

**17:1** The sin of Judah is written with an iron stylus, with a diamond point, engraved upon the breadth of their heart and on the horns of their altars.

**17:2** When their children remember their altars, and their sacred groves, and the leafy trees on the high mountains,

**17:3** sacrificing in the field: I will give your strength and all your treasures over to plunder; your high places, because of sins throughout all your borders.

**17:4** And you will be left alone, apart from your inheritance that I gave you, and I will make you serve your enemies in a land that you do not know; for you have kindled a fire in My fury: it will burn forever.

**17:5** This is what the Lord says: "Cursed is the man who trusts in man and sets flesh as his arm, and whose heart departs from the Lord.

**17:6** For he will be like tamarisks in the desert, and he will not see when good comes; but he will dwell in dryness in the desert, in a salty and uninhabitable land.

**17:7** Blessed is the man who trusts in the Lord, and the Lord will be his confidence.

**17:8** And he will be like a tree that is transplanted by the waters, which sends its roots toward the moisture, and it will not fear when the heat comes; and its leaf will be green, and in a time of drought it will not be anxious, nor will it ever cease to produce fruit.

**17:9** Perverse is the heart of all, and unsearchable; who will know it?

**17:10** I am the Lord, searching the heart and testing the kidneys, who give to each one according to his way and according to the fruit of his devices.

**17:11** The partridge has brooded over what she did not lay; he has made riches, and not with justice: in the middle of his days he will leave them, and at his end he will be a fool."

**17:12** A throne of glory on high from the beginning, the place of our sanctification.

**17:13** Expectation of Israel, Lord, all who abandon You will be put to shame; those who depart from You will be written in the earth, because they have abandoned the vein of living waters, the Lord.

**17:14** Heal me, Lord, and I will be healed; save me, and I will be saved, because You are my praise.

**17:15** Look, they themselves say to me, "Where is the word of the Lord? Let it come."

**17:16** And I have not been troubled, following You as Shepherd, and the day of man I have not desired, You know; what came out of my lips was right in Your sight.

**17:17** Do not be a terror to me; You are my hope in the day of affliction.

**17:18** Let those who persecute me be put to shame, and let me not be put to shame; let them be terrified, and let me not be terrified; bring upon them the day of affliction, and crush them with a double crushing.

**17:19** This is what the Lord says to me: "Go and stand in the gate of the sons of the people, through which the kings of Judah enter and go out, and in all the gates of Jerusalem;

**17:20** and you shall say to them, 'Hear the word of the Lord, kings of Judah, and all Judah, and all inhabitants of Jerusalem who enter through these gates.

**17:21** This is what the Lord says: Guard your souls, and do not carry burdens on the Sabbath day, nor bring them in through the gates of Jerusalem;

**17:22** and do not bring loads out of your houses on the Sabbath day, and you shall not do any work; sanctify the Sabbath day, as I commanded your fathers.

**17:23** And they did not listen, nor did they incline their ear, but they hardened their neck so that they would not listen to Me and would not receive discipline.

**17:24** And it will come to pass: if you listen to Me,' says the Lord, 'so that you do not bring loads in through the gates of this city on the Sabbath day, and if you sanctify the Sabbath day so that you do not do any work on it,

**17:25** kings and princes will enter through the gates of this city, sitting upon the throne of David and riding in chariots and on horses, they themselves and their princes, the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem; and this city will be inhabited forever.

**17:26** And they will come from the cities of Judah, and from around Jerusalem, and from the land of Benjamin, and from the plains, and from the hill country, and from the south, bringing burnt offering, and sacrificial victim, and sacrifice, and frankincense, and they will bring an offering into the house of the Lord.

**17:27** But if you do not listen to Me to sanctify the Sabbath day, and not to carry a load and not to bring one in through the gates of Jerusalem on the Sabbath day, I will kindle a fire in its gates, and it will devour the houses of Jerusalem, and it will not be extinguished.'"

## Translator Notes

- **17:1** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "super latitudinem" ("upon the breadth"), in contrast to the Hebrew "tablet."
- **17:6** — Vulgate 'myricæ' (plural, "tamarisks") renders the Hebrew desert shrub 'arar' as plural tamarisk bushes.
- **17:8** — Vulgate 'ad humorem' ("toward the moisture") renders Hebrew 'al-yubal' ("by the stream") more generally as moisture.
- **17:9** — Vulgate 'cor omnium' ("the heart of all") and 'inscrutabile' ("unsearchable," creating a wordplay with 'scrutans', "searching," in 17:10) differ from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("deceitful above all things and desperately sick").
- **17:10** — Latin 'renes' ("kidneys") is the biblical idiom for the innermost mind and affections.
- **17:16** — Vulgate 'te pastorem sequens' ("following You as Shepherd") applies "shepherd" to God rather than to Jeremiah, and 'diem hominis' ("the day of man") reads the Hebrew word for "woeful/incurable" as the word for "man."
