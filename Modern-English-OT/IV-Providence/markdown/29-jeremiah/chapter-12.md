## Text

**12:1** Righteous indeed are You, Lord, if I dispute with You; yet I will speak of just things to You: Why does the way of the ungodly prosper, and why is it well with all who transgress and act unjustly?

**12:2** You have planted them, and they have taken root; they thrive and bear fruit. You are near to their mouth, and far from their kidneys.

**12:3** And You, Lord, know me; You have seen me, and You have tested my heart with You. Gather them together like a flock for sacrifice, and consecrate them for the day of slaughter.

**12:4** How long will the land mourn, and the grass of every region dry up, because of the wickedness of those who dwell in it? Beast and bird have been consumed, because they have said, "He will not see our final end."

**12:5** If you have grown weary running with footmen, how will you be able to contend with horses? And though you have been secure in a land of peace, what will you do in the pride of the Jordan?

**12:6** For even your brothers and the house of your father, even they themselves have fought against you, and have cried out after you with a full voice; do not believe them when they speak good things to you.

**12:7** I have left My house; I have abandoned My inheritance; I have given My beloved soul into the hand of her enemies.

**12:8** My inheritance has become to Me like a lion in the forest; it has given voice against Me, therefore I have hated it.

**12:9** Is My inheritance a multicolored bird to Me? Is it a bird dyed all over? Come, gather together, all you beasts of the earth; hurry to devour.

**12:10** Many shepherds have demolished My vineyard; they have trampled My share; they have turned My desirable portion into a desert of solitude.

**12:11** They have reduced it to ruin, and it has mourned over Me; with desolation the whole land has been made desolate, because there is no one who reflects in his heart.

**12:12** Destroyers have come upon all the ways of the desert, for the sword of the Lord will devour; from one end of the land to the other, there is no peace for all flesh.

**12:13** They have sown wheat and reaped thorns; they have received an inheritance, and it will not profit them. You will be put to shame by your fruits because of the wrath of the fury of the Lord.

**12:14** This is what the Lord says against all My most wicked neighbors, who touch the inheritance that I distributed to My people Israel: "Look, I will uproot them from their land, and I will uproot the house of Judah from their midst.

**12:15** And after I have uprooted them, I will turn back and have mercy on them, and I will bring them back, each man to his own inheritance and each man to his own land.

**12:16** And it will be: if, being instructed, they learn the ways of My people, so that they swear by My name, 'The Lord lives!' just as they taught My people to swear by Baal, they will be built up in the midst of My people.

**12:17** But if they do not listen, I will uproot that nation with uprooting and destruction," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — Latin renibus ("kidneys") follows the biblical idiom treating the kidneys as the seat of innermost thoughts, motives, and conscience.
- **12:5** — The Vulgate keeps superbia Jordanis ("pride of the Jordan") literally for the dense, lion-haunted thicket along the river.
- **12:7** — The Vulgate uses the accusative phrase dilectam animam meam ("My beloved soul"), matching the Septuagint rather than the Hebrew construct "the beloved of my soul."
- **12:9** — The Vulgate presents two parallel questions about a multicolored and dyed bird (avis discolor ... avis tincta per totum), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text speaks of a speckled bird of prey surrounded by other birds of prey.
- **12:13** — The Vulgate reads hæreditatem acceperunt ("they have received an inheritance"), reflecting a different reading of the Hebrew verb that the Masoretic Text vocalizes as "they have worn themselves out."
