## Text

**3:1** "It is commonly said, 'If a man sends away his wife, and departing from him she marries another man, will he return to her again? Will that woman not be polluted and defiled?' Yet you have committed fornication with many lovers; nevertheless, return to Me," says the Lord, "and I will receive you.

**3:2** Lift up your eyes straight ahead, and see where you have not lain prostrate. You sat along the roads, waiting for them like a robber in the wilderness, and you have polluted the land with your fornications and with your wicked deeds.

**3:3** For this reason the drops of rain have been withheld, and there has been no late rain. The forehead of a prostitute woman has become yours; you refused to blush.

**3:4** Therefore, at least from now on, call to Me, 'My Father, You are the guide of my virginity;

**3:5** will You be angry forever, or will You persist to the end?' Look, you have spoken, and you have done evil things, and you have prevailed."

**3:6** And the Lord said to me in the days of King Josiah, "Have you seen what apostate Israel has done? She went off by herself onto every high mountain and under every leafy tree, and committed fornication there.

**3:7** And I said, after she had done all these things, 'Return to Me,' yet she did not return. And her unfaithful sister Judah saw

**3:8** that because apostate Israel had committed adultery, I had sent her away and given her a certificate of divorce; yet unfaithful Judah, her sister, did not fear, but went off and committed fornication herself as well.

**3:9** And by the ease of her fornication she defiled the land, and committed adultery with stone and wood.

**3:10** And in all these things her unfaithful sister Judah has not returned to Me with her whole heart, but in falsehood," says the Lord.

**3:11** And the Lord said to me, "Apostate Israel has justified her soul in comparison with unfaithful Judah.

**3:12** Go and proclaim these words toward the north, and you will say, 'Return, apostate Israel, says the Lord, and I will not turn My face away from you, for I am holy, says the Lord, and I will not be angry forever.

**3:13** Only acknowledge your iniquity, that you have acted unfaithfully against the Lord your God, and have scattered your ways to strangers under every leafy tree, and have not listened to My voice, says the Lord.'"

**3:14** "Turn back, returning sons," says the Lord, "for I am your husband; and I will take you, one from a city and two from a clan, and I will bring you into Zion.

**3:15** And I will give you shepherds according to My heart, and they will feed you with knowledge and teaching.

**3:16** And when you have multiplied and increased in the land in those days," says the Lord, "they will no longer say, 'The ark of the covenant of the Lord,' nor will it come upon the heart, nor will they remember it, nor will it be visited, nor will it be made again.

**3:17** At that time they will call Jerusalem the throne of the Lord, and all the nations will be gathered to her in the name of the Lord in Jerusalem, and they will not walk after the depravity of their most wicked heart.

**3:18** In those days the house of Judah will go to the house of Israel, and they will come together from the land of the north to the land that I gave to your fathers.

**3:19** But I said, 'How shall I place you among the sons and grant you a desirable land, the splendid inheritance of the armies of the nations?' And I said, 'You will call Me Father, and you will not cease to walk after Me.'

**3:20** Yet just as when a woman despises her lover, so the house of Israel has despised Me," says the Lord.

**3:21** A voice has been heard on the roads, the weeping and wailing of the sons of Israel, because they have made their way unrighteous; they have forgotten the Lord their God.

**3:22** "Turn back, returning sons, and I will heal your apostasies." "Look, we come to You, for You are the Lord our God.

**3:23** Truly the hills were deceitful, and the multitude of the mountains; truly in the Lord our God is the salvation of Israel.

**3:24** Shame has devoured the labor of our fathers from our youth: their flocks and their herds, their sons and their daughters.

**3:25** We will sleep in our shame, and our disgrace will cover us, because we have sinned against the Lord our God, we and our fathers, from our youth even to this day, and we have not listened to the voice of the Lord our God."

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Vulgate opens with 'Vulgo dicitur' ("It is commonly said"), reads 'mulier illa' ("that woman") rather than the Hebrew "that land," and adds 'et ego suscipiam te' ("and I will receive you") at the end of the verse.
- **3:2** — Latin 'in directum' ("straight ahead") and 'quasi latro' ("like a robber") are Vulgate-distinct renderings where the Masoretic Hebrew reads "to the bare heights" and "like an Arab."
- **3:4** — The Vulgate renders 'voca me' as an imperative ("call to Me") rather than a rhetorical question ("have you not called to me?"), and translates Hebrew "my youth" as 'virginitatis meae' ("my virginity").
- **3:12** — Latin 'sanctus' ("holy") is the Vulgate's rendering of Hebrew 'hasid' ("merciful" or "steadfastly loving").
- **3:14** — Latin 'filii revertentes' ("returning sons") translates Hebrew 'banim shovavim' ("wayward" or "backsliding sons") with a participle emphasizing return; the same phrase recurs in 3:22.
