## Text

**33:1** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah a second time, while he was still confined in the court of the prison, saying,

**33:2** "This is what the Lord says, who is about to do it, and to form it, and to prepare it; the Lord is His name:

**33:3** Call to Me, and I will hear you, and I will declare to you great and firm things that you do not know.

**33:4** For this is what the Lord God of Israel says concerning the houses of this city, and concerning the houses of the king of Judah, which have been destroyed, and concerning the ramparts, and concerning the sword

**33:5** of those who come to fight with the Chaldeans and to fill them with the corpses of the men whom I struck down in My fury and in My indignation, hiding My face from this city because of all their wickedness:

**33:6** Look, I will draw over them a scar and health, and I will cure them, and I will reveal to them the prayer of peace and truth.

**33:7** And I will reverse the reversal of Judah and the reversal of Jerusalem, and I will build them up as at the beginning.

**33:8** And I will cleanse them from all their iniquity in which they sinned against Me, and I will be merciful toward all their iniquities in which they abandoned Me and despised Me.

**33:9** And it will be to Me a name, and joy, and praise, and exultation to all the nations of the earth, which will hear of all the good things that I am about to do for them; and they will fear and be troubled at all the good things and at all the peace that I will make for them."

**33:10** This is what the Lord says: "There will again be heard in this place, which you say is deserted because there is neither man nor beast in the cities of Judah and outside Jerusalem, which are desolate, without man, and without inhabitant, and without livestock,

**33:11** the voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, the voice of those saying, 'Give thanks to the Lord of hosts, for the Lord is good, for His mercy is forever,' and of those bringing votive offerings into the house of the Lord; for I will bring back the return of the land as at the beginning," says the Lord.

**33:12** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "There will again be in this deserted place, without man and without beast, and in all its cities, a dwelling place of shepherds of resting flocks.

**33:13** In the cities of the mountains, and in the cities of the plains, and in the cities that are toward the south, and in the land of Benjamin, and around Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah, flocks will again pass by the hand of the one counting them," says the Lord.

**33:14** "Look, days are coming," says the Lord, "and I will raise up the good word that I spoke to the house of Israel and to the house of Judah.

**33:15** In those days and at that time I will cause a shoot of righteousness to sprout for David, and he will carry out judgment and righteousness in the land.

**33:16** In those days Judah will be saved, and Jerusalem will dwell confidently; and this is the name by which they will call him: 'The Lord Our Righteous One.'

**33:17** For this is what the Lord says: There will not perish from David a man to sit upon the throne of the house of Israel;

**33:18** and from the priests and from the Levites there will not perish a man from before My face to offer burnt offerings, and burn an offering, and slay sacrifices all days."

**33:19** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, saying:

**33:20** "This is what the Lord says: If My covenant with the day and My covenant with the night can be made void, so that there is not day and night in their time,

**33:21** then My covenant with My servant David will also be able to be made void, so that there is no son from him to reign on his throne, and with the Levites and priests, My ministers.

**33:22** Just as the stars of heaven cannot be counted and the sand of the sea cannot be measured, so I will multiply the seed of My servant David and the Levites, My ministers."

**33:23** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, saying:

**33:24** "Have you not seen what this people has spoken, saying, 'The two families that the Lord had chosen have been cast off'? And they have despised My people, because it is no longer a nation before them.

**33:25** This is what the Lord says: If I have not established My covenant between day and night, and the laws for heaven and earth,

**33:26** then indeed I will also cast off the seed of Jacob and of My servant David, so that I will not take from his seed rulers of the seed of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; for I will bring back their return, and I will have mercy on them."

## Translator Notes

- **33:4** — In verses 4-5, the Vulgate coordinates et ad munitiones, et ad gladium with the preceding ad phrases and attaches venientium as a genitive ("the sword of those who come"), resolving a syntactically difficult Hebrew passage.
- **33:6** — Latin obducam eis cicatricem is a medical idiom literally meaning "draw a scar over them," that is, heal over their wounds. The Vulgate also reads deprecationem ("prayer/supplication") where the Hebrew means "abundance," connecting the rare Hebrew term to a homonymous root meaning "to pray/entreat."
- **33:7** — Latin convertam conversionem mirrors the Hebrew cognate idiom (as in 37:3 and 37:18); the Vulgate keeps it distinct from convertam captivitatem ("turn back their captivity") in 39:44.
- **33:10** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew chutzot ("streets") literally as foris ("outside Jerusalem"), reflecting the root sense of "outside."
- **33:12** — The Vulgate's triple genitive pastorum accubantium gregum ("of shepherds of resting flocks") uses the intransitive participle accubantium modifying gregum, whereas the Hebrew uses a causative participle ("shepherds causing flocks to lie down").
- **33:14** — Verses 33:14-26 are restored in native Clementine Vulgate chapter 33 (absent from Septuagint-aligned ch. 40; 33:25-26 were previously stored as unplaced entries).
- **33:15** — Latin germen justitiae ('shoot of righteousness') parallels 23:5; lowercase 'he' preserves Latin case ambiguity (flagged alongside 23:5-6 for human editorial capitalization policy).
- **33:16** — Vulgate 33:16 reads 'Jerusalem' (parallel to 'Israel' in 23:6) and masculine eum ('him', where the Hebrew reads feminine 'her'), with adjective justus ('Our Righteous One'). Lowercase 'him' is flagged alongside 23:6 for human editorial capitalization policy.
- **33:18** — Clem1598-USFX prints holocautomata (variant holocaustomata) and sacrificum (orthographic variant for sacrificium, 'offering/sacrifice').
- **33:26** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "reducam conversionem eorum" ("I will bring back their return"), a literal rendering of the Hebrew idiom for restoring fortunes or returning from exile.
