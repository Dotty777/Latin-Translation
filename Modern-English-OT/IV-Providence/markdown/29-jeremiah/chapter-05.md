## Text

**5:1** "Go around the streets of Jerusalem, and look and consider, and search in her squares to see if you can find a man doing justice and seeking faithfulness, and I will be merciful to her.

**5:2** And even if they say, 'The Lord lives,' even in this they will swear falsely."

**5:3** Lord, Your eyes look upon faithfulness. You struck them, and they felt no pain; You crushed them, and they refused to receive discipline. They hardened their faces more than rock, and they were unwilling to return.

**5:4** Then I said, "Perhaps they are poor and foolish, not knowing the way of the Lord, the judgment of their God.

**5:5** Therefore I will go to the leading men and speak to them, for they themselves know the way of the Lord, the judgment of their God." Yet look, these all the more have broken the yoke together; they have snapped the bonds.

**5:6** Therefore a lion from the forest has struck them, a wolf at evening has ravaged them, a leopard is watching over their cities; everyone who goes out from them will be captured, because their transgressions have multiplied; their turnings away have grown strong.

**5:7** "On what ground will I be able to be merciful to you? Your sons have abandoned Me and swear by those who are not gods. I fed them to the full, and they committed adultery, and in the house of a prostitute they lived wantonly.

**5:8** They have become lustful horses and stallions; each one was neighing after his neighbor's wife.

**5:9** Shall I not bring a reckoning for these things," says the Lord, "and shall My soul not take vengeance on such a nation?

**5:10** Go up on her walls and destroy, yet do not make a complete end; remove her branches, for they are not the Lord's.

**5:11** For by transgression the house of Israel and the house of Judah have transgressed against Me," says the Lord.

**5:12** They have denied the Lord and said, "It is not He, nor will evil come upon us; we will not see sword and famine.

**5:13** The prophets have spoken into the wind, and no answer was in them; therefore these things will happen to them."

**5:14** This is what the Lord God of hosts says: "Because you have spoken this word, look, I am making My words in your mouth into fire, and this people into wood, and it will devour them.

**5:15** Look, I will bring upon you a nation from afar, house of Israel," says the Lord, "a strong nation, an ancient nation, a nation whose language you will not know, nor will you understand what it speaks.

**5:16** Its quiver is like an open tomb; all of them are mighty men.

**5:17** And it will eat your crops and your bread; it will devour your sons and your daughters; it will eat your flock and your herds; it will eat your vineyard and your fig tree; and it will crush with the sword your fortified cities in which you have confidence.

**5:18** Yet in those days," says the Lord, "I will not bring you to a complete end.

**5:19** And if you say, 'Why has the Lord our God done all these things to us?' you will say to them, 'Just as you have abandoned Me and served a foreign god in your land, so you will serve foreigners in a land not your own.'"

**5:20** Announce this to the house of Jacob, and make it heard in Judah, saying:

**5:21** "Hear, foolish people, who have no heart, who have eyes and do not see, and ears and do not hear.

**5:22** Will you not fear Me, then," says the Lord, "and will you not feel anguish before My face? It is I who placed the sand as a boundary for the sea, an everlasting decree that it will not pass beyond; they will be stirred up, and they will not prevail, and its waves will swell, and they will not cross over it.

**5:23** But this people has come to have an unbelieving and provoking heart; they have turned back and gone away.

**5:24** And they did not say in their heart, 'Let us fear the Lord our God, who gives us the early and the late rain in its season, preserving for us the fullness of the annual harvest.'

**5:25** Your iniquities have turned these things away, and your sins have withheld good from you;

**5:26** for wicked men have been found among My people, lying in wait like bird-catchers, setting snares and traps to catch men.

**5:27** Just as a trap is full of birds, so their houses are full of deceit; therefore they have become great and rich.

**5:28** They have grown stout and fat, and they have passed over My words most wickedly. They have not judged the cause of the widow, they have not set right the cause of the orphan, and they have not judged the right of the poor.

**5:29** Shall I not bring a reckoning for these things," says the Lord, "or shall My soul not take vengeance upon a nation of this kind?

**5:30** Shock and astonishing things have taken place in the land:

**5:31** the prophets were prophesying falsehood, and the priests were applauding with their hands, and My people loved such things. What then will happen at the end of it?"

## Translator Notes

- **5:3** — The Vulgate renders 'Domine, oculi tui respiciunt fidem' as a declarative statement ("Your eyes look upon faithfulness") rather than the rhetorical question found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **5:6** — The Vulgate reads 'lupus ad vesperam' ("a wolf at evening") and 'capietur' ("will be captured"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "a wolf of the deserts" and "will be torn to pieces."
- **5:13** — The Vulgate reads 'Prophetae fuerunt in ventum locuti' ("The prophets have spoken into the wind") rather than the Hebrew "The prophets will become wind."
- **5:28** — The Vulgate distinctively reads 'praeterierunt sermones meos pessime' ("passed over My words most wickedly," versus Hebrew "surpass in deeds of evil") and includes the additional clause 'Causam viduae non judicaverunt' ("They have not judged the cause of the widow").
- **5:31** — The Vulgate reads 'sacerdotes applaudebant manibus suis' ("the priests were applauding with their hands"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "the priests rule at their direction."
