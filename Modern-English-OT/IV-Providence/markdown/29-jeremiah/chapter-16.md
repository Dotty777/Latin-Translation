## Text

**16:1** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**16:2** "You shall not take a wife, and you shall not have sons and daughters in this place.

**16:3** For this is what the Lord says concerning the sons and daughters who are born in this place, and concerning their mothers who gave birth to them, and concerning their fathers from whose lineage they were born in this land:

**16:4** They will die by deaths of sicknesses; they will not be mourned, and they will not be buried. They will become a dunghill upon the face of the earth, and by sword and by famine they will be consumed; and their corpse will be food for the birds of the sky and for the beasts of the earth.

**16:5** For this is what the Lord says: Do not enter a house of feasting, neither go to mourn nor comfort them, for I have taken away My peace from this people," says the Lord, "mercy and compassions.

**16:6** And great and small will die in this land; they will not be buried, nor will they be mourned, and they will not cut themselves, nor will baldness be made for them.

**16:7** And they will not break bread among them for one who mourns, for comfort over the dead; and they will not give them a drink of the cup for comfort over their father and mother.

**16:8** And do not enter a house of feasting to sit with them and eat and drink.

**16:9** For this is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Look, I will take away from this place, before your eyes and in your days, the voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride.

**16:10** And when you declare to this people all these words, and they say to you, 'Why has the Lord spoken all this great evil against us? What is our iniquity, and what is our sin that we have sinned against the Lord our God?'

**16:11** you shall say to them, 'Because your fathers abandoned Me,' says the Lord, 'and went after foreign gods, and served them, and worshiped them, and abandoned Me, and did not keep My law.

**16:12** And you yourselves have also acted worse than your fathers; for look, each one walks after the perversity of his evil heart so that he does not listen to Me.

**16:13** And I will cast you out of this land into a land that you do not know, you and your fathers; and there you will serve foreign gods day and night, who will not give you rest.'

**16:14** Therefore, look, the days are coming," says the Lord, "and it will no longer be said, 'The Lord lives, who brought out the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt,'

**16:15** but, 'The Lord lives, who brought out the sons of Israel from the land of the north and from all the lands to which I cast them out.' And I will bring them back into their land, which I gave to their fathers.

**16:16** Look, I will send many fishermen," says the Lord, "and they will fish for them; and after these things I will send them many hunters, and they will hunt them from every mountain, and from every hill, and from the caverns of the rocks.

**16:17** For My eyes are upon all their ways; they are not hidden from My face, and their iniquity has not been concealed from My eyes.

**16:18** And first I will repay double for their iniquities and their sins, because they have defiled My land with the carcasses of their idols, and with their abominations they have filled My inheritance."

**16:19** Lord, my strength and my might and my refuge in the day of tribulation, to You the nations will come from the ends of the earth and will say, "Truly our fathers possessed a lie, a vanity that did not profit them."

**16:20** Can a man make gods for himself, when they themselves are not gods?

**16:21** "Therefore, look, I will show them this time; I will show them My hand and My power, and they will know that My name is the Lord."

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**17:1** *(after `16:21`)* The sin of Judah is written with an iron stylus, with a diamond point, engraved upon the breadth of their heart and on the horns of their altars.

**17:2** *(after `16:21`)* When their children remember their altars, and their sacred groves, and the leafy trees on the high mountains,

**17:3** *(after `16:21`)* sacrificing in the field: I will give your strength and all your treasures over to plunder; your high places, because of sins throughout all your borders.

**17:4** *(after `16:21`)* And you will be left alone, apart from your inheritance that I gave you, and I will make you serve your enemies in a land that you do not know; for you have kindled a fire in My fury: it will burn forever.

## Translator Notes

- **16:3** — Vulgate 'de quorum stirpe sunt nati' ("from whose lineage they were born") expands on the simpler Hebrew expression "who fathered them."
- **16:5** — Vulgate 'domum convivii' ("a house of feasting," also in 16:8) renders Hebrew 'bet marzeah' (a funerary banquet house or house of mourning) literally as a banquet house.
- **16:13** — Vulgate 'qui non dabunt vobis requiem' ("who will not give you rest") refers in the third-person plural to the foreign gods, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads first-person singular ("for I will show you no favor").
- **17:1 *(additional)*** — The Clementine Vulgate reads "super latitudinem" ("upon the breadth"), in contrast to the Hebrew "tablet."
