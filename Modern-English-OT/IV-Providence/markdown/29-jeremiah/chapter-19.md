## Text

**19:1** This is what the Lord says: "Go, and take a potter's earthenware flask from the elders of the people and from the elders of the priests,

**19:2** and go out to the valley of the son of Hinnom, which is near the entrance of the Pottery Gate; and you will proclaim there the words that I will speak to you.

**19:3** And you will say, 'Hear the word of the Lord, kings of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem. This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Look, I will bring affliction upon this place, so that the ears of everyone who hears it will tingle,

**19:4** because they have abandoned Me, and have made this place foreign, and have poured out libations in it to foreign gods whom they did not know, they and their fathers and the kings of Judah; and they have filled this place with the blood of the innocent,

**19:5** and they have built the high places of the Baals, to burn their sons with fire as a burnt offering to the Baals, things that I did not command or speak, nor did they come up into My heart.

**19:6** Therefore look, days are coming, says the Lord, and this place will no longer be called Topheth and the valley of the son of Hinnom, but the Valley of Slaughter.

**19:7** And I will ruin the counsel of Judah and Jerusalem in this place, and I will overthrow them by the sword in the sight of their enemies and by the hand of those who seek their lives; and I will give their corpses as food to the birds of the sky and to the beasts of the earth.

**19:8** And I will make this city an astonishment and a hissing; everyone who passes through it will be astonished and will hiss over its entire plague.

**19:9** And I will feed them with the flesh of their sons and with the flesh of their daughters, and each one will eat the flesh of his friend in the siege and in the distress in which their enemies and those who seek their lives will shut them in.'

**19:10** And you will shatter the flask in the eyes of the men who will go with you,

**19:11** and you will say to them, 'This is what the Lord of hosts says: So I will shatter this people and this city, just as a potter's vessel is shattered, which can no longer be restored; and they will be buried in Topheth, because there is no other place for burying.

**19:12** So I will do to this place, says the Lord, and to its inhabitants, and I will make this city like Topheth.

**19:13** And the houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings of Judah will be like the place of Topheth, unclean, all the houses on whose rooftops they sacrificed to all the host of heaven and poured out libations to foreign gods.'"

**19:14** Then Jeremiah came from Topheth, where the Lord had sent him to prophesy, and he stood in the court of the house of the Lord and said to all the people:

**19:15** "This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Look, I will bring upon this city and upon all its towns all the evils that I have spoken against it, because they have hardened their neck so that they would not listen to My words."

## Translator Notes

- **19:1** — The Vulgate lacks a conjunction and second verb before a senioribus, reading literally "take a potter's earthenware flask from the elders," whereas the Hebrew means "buy a potter's earthenware flask and [take along] some of the elders."
- **19:4** — The Vulgate reads libaverunt ("poured out libations," as also in 18:15), whereas the Hebrew refers to burning offerings/incense.
- **19:5** — The Vulgate uses the plural Baalim ("the Baals") twice in this verse, whereas the Hebrew has the singular Baal.
- **19:7** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew "make them fall" as subvertam ("overthrow"), linking verbally with subvertit in 20:16.
