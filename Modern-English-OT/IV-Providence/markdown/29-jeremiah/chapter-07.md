## Text

**7:2** Stand in the gate of the house of the Lord, and proclaim this word there, and say, "Hear the word of the Lord, all Judah, you who enter through these gates to worship the Lord.

**7:3** This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Make your ways and your pursuits good, and I will dwell with you in this place.

**7:4** Do not trust in words of falsehood, saying, 'The temple of the Lord, the temple of the Lord, it is the temple of the Lord!'

**7:5** For if you rightly direct your ways and your pursuits, if you execute justice between a man and his neighbor,

**7:6** if you do not oppress the sojourner, the orphan, and the widow, nor shed innocent blood in this place, and do not walk after foreign gods to your own harm,

**7:7** I will dwell with you in this place, in the land that I gave to your fathers from of old and forever.

**7:8** Look, you are trusting for yourselves in sayings of falsehood that will not benefit you:

**7:9** stealing, murdering, committing adultery, swearing falsely, pouring out drink offerings to the Baals, and going after foreign gods whom you do not know,

**7:10** and you have come and stood before Me in this house, in which My name has been invoked, and have said, 'We have been delivered, because we have done all these abominations.'

**7:11** Has this house, in which My name has been invoked, then become a cave of robbers in your eyes? I, I am; I have seen it, says the Lord.

**7:12** Go to My place in Shiloh, where My name dwelt from the beginning, and see what I did to it because of the wickedness of My people Israel.

**7:13** And now, because you have done all these works, says the Lord, and I spoke to you, rising early in the morning and speaking, and you did not listen, and I called you, and you did not answer,

**7:14** I will do to this house, in which My name has been invoked and in which you have confidence, and to the place that I gave to you and to your fathers, just as I did to Shiloh;

**7:15** and I will cast you away from My presence, just as I cast away all your brothers, the entire seed of Ephraim."

**7:16** "As for you, therefore, do not pray for this people, nor take up praise and prayer for them, and do not oppose Me, for I will not hear you.

**7:17** Do you not see what these people are doing in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?

**7:18** The children gather wood, and the fathers kindle the fire, and the women knead fat to make cakes for the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings to foreign gods, and to provoke Me to anger.

**7:19** Is it Me they are provoking to anger?" says the Lord. "Is it not themselves, to the shame of their own face?"

**7:20** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: "Look, My fury and My indignation are fanned into flame upon this place, upon men, and upon livestock, and upon the trees of the region, and upon the produce of the land, and it will burn, and will not be extinguished."

**7:21** This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Add your burnt offerings to your sacrifices, and eat the flesh;

**7:22** for I did not speak with your fathers, nor did I command them, on the day when I brought them out of the land of Egypt, concerning the matter of burnt offerings and sacrifices;

**7:23** but this word I commanded them, saying, 'Listen to My voice, and I will be God to you, and you will be a people to Me; and walk in every way that I have commanded you, so that it may go well with you.'

**7:24** And they did not listen, nor incline their ear; instead, they went away in the desires and in the perversity of their evil heart, and turned backward and not forward,

**7:25** from the day when their fathers came out of the land of Egypt even to this day. And I sent to you all My servants the prophets daily, rising at dawn and sending them;

**7:26** yet they did not listen to Me, nor incline their ear; instead, they hardened their neck and acted worse than their fathers."

**7:27** And you will speak all these words to them, and they will not listen to you; and you will call to them, and they will not answer you.

**7:28** And you will say to them, "This is the nation that did not listen to the voice of the Lord its God, nor accept discipline; faithfulness has perished and has been taken away from their mouth."

**7:29** "Cut off your hair and cast it away, and take up a lamentation directly, for the Lord has cast off and abandoned the generation of His fury;

**7:30** for the sons of Judah have done evil in My eyes," says the Lord. "They have placed their stumbling blocks in the house in which My name has been invoked, to defile it;

**7:31** and they have built the high places of Topheth, which is in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, to burn their sons and their daughters in fire, things that I did not command, nor think of in My heart.

**7:32** Therefore, look, days will come," says the Lord, "and it will no longer be called Topheth or the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, but the Valley of Slaughter; and they will bury in Topheth, because there is no room.

**7:33** And the corpses of this people will become food for the birds of the sky and the beasts of the earth, and there will be no one to drive them away.

**7:34** And I will cause to cease from the cities of Judah and from the streets of Jerusalem the voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride; for the land will become a desolation."

## Translator Notes

- **7:3** — Vulgate "habitabo vobiscum" ("I will dwell with you," repeated in 7:7) differs from the Masoretic Hebrew "I will let you dwell."
- **7:9** — Vulgate "libare Baalim" ("pour out drink offerings to the Baals") uses the plural "Baalim" and the verb for libations, whereas the Hebrew has "burn incense to Baal" (singular).
- **7:10** — Vulgate "eo quod fecerimus" ("because we have done") renders the clause causally within the people's speech, whereas the Hebrew expresses purpose ("in order to go on doing").
- **7:18** — Vulgate "conspergunt adipem" literally means "knead (or mix) fat," a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Hebrew has "knead dough."
- **7:29** — Vulgate "in directum" ("directly" or "straight ahead") reflects Jerome's rendering of the Hebrew term for "bare heights" (cf. 7:16 "laudem," "praise," for Hebrew "outcry").
