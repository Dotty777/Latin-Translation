## Text

**32:15** For this is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: "Take the cup of the wine of this fury from My hand, and you shall give drink from it to all the nations to which I will send you.

**32:16** And they will drink, and be thrown into turmoil, and go mad before the sword that I will send among them."

**32:17** And I took the cup from the hand of the Lord, and gave drink to all the nations to which the Lord sent me:

**32:18** to Jerusalem, and the cities of Judah, and its kings, and its princes, to give them over to desolation, and to astonishment, and to hissing, and to a curse, as it is this day;

**32:19** to Pharaoh king of Egypt, and his servants, and his princes, and all his people;

**32:20** and to all in general: all the kings of the land of Uz, and all the kings of the land of the Philistines, and Ashkelon, and Gaza, and Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod;

**32:21** and Edom, and Moab, and the sons of Ammon;

**32:22** and all the kings of Tyre, and all the kings of Sidon, and the kings of the land of the islands who are across the sea;

**32:23** and Dedan, and Tema, and Buz, and all who have their hair clipped;

**32:24** and all the kings of Arabia, and all the kings of the west who dwell in the desert;

**32:25** and all the kings of Zimri, and all the kings of Elam, and all the kings of the Medes;

**32:26** also all the kings of the north, from near and from far, each one against his brother, and all the kingdoms of the earth that are upon its face; and the king of Sheshach will drink after them.

**32:27** And you shall say to them, "This is what the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, says: 'Drink, and get drunk, and vomit, and fall, and do not rise before the sword that I will send among you.'"

**32:28** And when they refuse to take the cup from your hand to drink, you shall say to them, "This is what the Lord of hosts says: 'You will certainly drink;

**32:29** for look, in the city in which My name has been invoked I will begin to bring affliction, and will you be as innocent and exempt? You will not be exempt; for I am calling a sword upon all the inhabitants of the earth,' says the Lord of hosts."

**32:30** And you shall prophesy to them all these words, and you shall say to them: "The Lord will roar from on high, and from His holy dwelling He will utter His voice; roaring, He will roar over His beauty; a chant like that of treaders will be sung in chorus against all the inhabitants of the earth.

**32:31** The sound has reached to the ends of the earth, because the Lord has a judgment with the nations; He Himself enters into judgment with all flesh. 'The wicked I have handed over to the sword,' says the Lord."

**32:32** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "Look, affliction will go forth from nation to nation, and a great whirlwind will go forth from the farthest bounds of the earth.

**32:33** And those killed by the Lord on that day will be from one end of the earth to the other end of it; they will not be mourned, and they will not be gathered, nor will they be buried; they will lie as dung upon the face of the earth."

**32:34** Wail, shepherds, and cry out, and sprinkle yourselves with ashes, leaders of the flock, for your days have been completed for you to be killed, and your dispersions; and you will fall like precious vessels.

**32:35** And flight will perish from the shepherds, and deliverance from the leaders of the flock.

**32:36** The sound of the outcry of the shepherds, and the wailing of the leaders of the flock! For the Lord has laid waste their pastures,

**32:37** and the fields of peace have fallen silent before the wrath of the fury of the Lord.

**32:38** Like a lion He has abandoned His shelter, for their land has become a desolation before the wrath of the dove, and before the wrath of the fury of the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **32:20** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew kol-ha-ereb ("all the mixed peoples") as universis generaliter ("all in general"), and uses the Greco-Latin name Ausitis (terrae Ausitidis) for the land of Uz.
- **32:24** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew ha-ereb ("the mixed peoples") as occidentis ("of the west"), connecting it with the Hebrew root for evening and west.
- **32:30** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew nawehu ("His fold" or "habitation") as decorem suum ("His beauty"), deriving it from the root meaning comely or beautiful.
- **32:38** — The Vulgate reads Hebrew hay-yonah ("the oppressor" or "oppressing sword") as its homograph meaning "dove," translating a facie irae columbae ("before the wrath of the dove").
