## Text

**21:1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord, when King Zedekiah sent to him Pashhur the son of Malchijah and Zephaniah the son of Maaseiah, the priest, saying,

**21:2** "Inquire of the Lord for us, because Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, is waging war against us; perhaps the Lord will deal with us according to all His wonders, and he will withdraw from us."

**21:3** And Jeremiah said to them, "This is what you shall say to Zedekiah:

**21:4** 'This is what the Lord God of Israel says: Look, I will turn back the weapons of war that are in your hands, and with which you are fighting against the king of Babylon and the Chaldeans who are besieging you around the walls; and I will gather them in the middle of this city.

**21:5** And I Myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and with a strong arm, and in fury, and in indignation, and in great wrath.

**21:6** And I will strike the inhabitants of this city: people and beasts will die by a great pestilence.

**21:7** And after these things, says the Lord, I will give Zedekiah king of Judah, and his servants, and his people, and those who are left in this city from the plague, and the sword, and the famine, into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of those who seek their life; and he will strike them with the edge of the sword, and he will not be moved, nor will he spare, nor will he have mercy.'"

**21:8** And to this people you shall say, "This is what the Lord says: Look, I place before you the way of life and the way of death.

**21:9** Whoever dwells in this city will die by the sword, and by famine, and by plague; but whoever goes out and deserts to the Chaldeans who are besieging you will live, and his life will be to him like spoil.

**21:10** For I have set My face against this city for evil and not for good, says the Lord; it will be given into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will burn it up with fire."

**21:11** And to the house of the king of Judah: "Hear the words of the Lord,

**21:12** house of David. This is what the Lord says: Render judgment in the morning, and rescue the one oppressed by force from the hand of the extortioner, so that My indignation may not go out like fire and be kindled, and there be no one to extinguish it, because of the wickedness of your practices.

**21:13** Look, I am against you, dweller of the solid and level valley, says the Lord, you who say, 'Who will strike us? And who will enter our houses?'

**21:14** And I will punish you according to the fruit of your practices, says the Lord; and I will kindle a fire in its forest, and it will devour everything around it."

## Translator Notes

- **21:11** — The Vulgate reads the plural 'words' (verba), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has the singular 'word.'
- **21:12** — Latin calumniantis (and calumniatoris in 22:3, calumniam in 22:17) denotes one who oppresses or extorts under color of wrongful accusation, rendering the Hebrew term for extortion/oppression.
- **21:13** — The Vulgate reads vallis solidae atque campestris ('the solid and level valley') and Quis percutiet nos? ('Who will strike us?'), whereas the Hebrew reads 'inhabitant of the valley, rock of the plain' and 'Who will come down against us?'
