## Text

**13:1** This is what the Lord says to me: "Go and acquire for yourself a linen loincloth, and you shall put it upon your loins, and you shall not bring it into water."

**13:2** And I acquired a loincloth according to the word of the Lord, and I put it around my loins.

**13:3** And the word of the Lord came to me a second time, saying,

**13:4** "Take the loincloth that you acquired, which is around your loins, and rising up, go to the Euphrates, and hide it there in a crevice of a rock."

**13:5** And I went away and hid it at the Euphrates, just as the Lord had commanded me.

**13:6** And it happened after very many days that the Lord said to me: "Arise, go to the Euphrates, and take from there the loincloth that I commanded you to hide there."

**13:7** And I went to the Euphrates, and I dug and took the loincloth from the place where I had hidden it; and look, the loincloth had rotted away, so that it was fit for no use.

**13:8** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,

**13:9** "This is what the Lord says: In this way I will cause the pride of Judah and the great pride of Jerusalem to rot:

**13:10** this most wicked people, who refuse to listen to My words, and walk in the depravity of their heart, and have gone after foreign gods to serve them and to worship them; and they will be like this loincloth, which is fit for no use.

**13:11** For just as a loincloth clings to the loins of a man, so I joined the whole house of Israel and the whole house of Judah closely to Myself, says the Lord, so that they might be for Me a people, and a name, and a praise, and a glory; and they did not listen.

**13:12** Therefore you shall speak this word to them: 'This is what the Lord God of Israel says: Every flask will be filled with wine.' And they will say to you, 'Are we unaware that every flask will be filled with wine?'

**13:13** And you shall say to them, 'This is what the Lord says: Look, I will fill all the inhabitants of this land, and the kings of the lineage of David who sit upon his throne, and the priests, and the prophets, and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, with drunkenness.

**13:14** And I will scatter them, each man from his brother, and the fathers and the sons together, says the Lord. I will not spare, and I will not yield, nor will I have mercy, so as not to destroy them.'"

**13:15** Listen, and perceive with your ears; do not be lifted up, for the Lord has spoken.

**13:16** Give glory to the Lord your God before it grows dark, and before your feet stumble against the gloomy mountains; you will wait for light, and He will turn it into the shadow of death and into deep gloom.

**13:17** But if you do not listen to this, my soul will weep in secret in the face of pride; weeping, it will weep, and my eye will pour down tears, because the flock of the Lord has been taken captive.

**13:18** Say to the king and to the sovereign lady: "Humble yourselves, sit down, for the crown of your glory has come down from your head."

**13:19** The cities of the south have been shut up, and there is no one to open them; all Judah has been carried away in a complete deportation.

**13:20** Lift up your eyes and see, you who come from the north: where is the flock that was given to you, your splendid herd?

**13:21** What will you say when He visits you? For you yourself have taught them against you, and instructed them against your own head. Will not pangs seize you, like a woman in labor?

**13:22** And if you say in your heart, "Why have these things come upon me?" because of the multitude of your iniquity your private parts have been uncovered; the soles of your feet have been defiled.

**13:23** If an Ethiopian can change his skin, or a leopard his spots, then you also will be able to do good, when you have learned evil.

**13:24** And I will scatter them abroad like stubble that is snatched away by the wind in the desert.

**13:25** This is your lot, and the portion of your measure from Me, says the Lord, because you have forgotten Me, and have trusted in falsehood.

**13:26** Therefore I Myself also have laid bare your thighs before your face, and your disgrace has appeared:

**13:27** your adulteries, and your neighing, the crime of your fornication; upon the hills in the field I have seen your abominations. Woe to you, Jerusalem! You will not be made clean after Me; how much longer still?

## Translator Notes

- **13:14** — The Vulgate reads dispergam eos virum a fratre suo ("I will scatter them, each man from his brother"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "I will dash them one against another."
- **13:18** — Latin dominatrici ("sovereign lady" or "mistress") renders Hebrew gevirah, the official title of the queen mother in Judah.
- **13:20** — The Vulgate uses a second-person plural verb, qui venitis ab aquilone ("you who come from the north"), whereas the Masoretic Text refers in the third person to "those coming from the north."
- **13:26** — The Vulgate renders this clause as nudavi femora tua ("I have laid bare your thighs"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "I will lift up your skirts."
- **13:27** — The Vulgate divides the Hebrew phrase to read non mundaberis post me ("you will not be made clean after Me"), whereas the Masoretic Text reads "you will not be clean; after how much longer?"
