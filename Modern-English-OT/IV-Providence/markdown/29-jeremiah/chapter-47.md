## Text

**47:1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord after he was released from Ramah by Nebuzaradan, the commander of the troops, when he took him bound in chains among all those who were going into exile from Jerusalem and Judah and were being led to Babylon.

**47:2** So the captain of the troops took Jeremiah and said to him, "The Lord your God spoke this disaster against this place,

**47:3** and the Lord has brought it about and done just as He spoke, because you sinned against the Lord and did not listen to His voice; and this word has come about for you.

**47:4** Now therefore, look, I have released you today from the chains that are on your hands. If it pleases you to come with me to Babylon, come, and I will set my eyes upon you; but if it displeases you to come with me to Babylon, stay behind. Look, the whole land is before you; whatever you choose, and wherever it pleases you to go, proceed there,

**47:5** and do not come with me, but live with Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon has appointed over the cities of Judah; live therefore with him among the people, or wherever it pleases you to go, go." The commander of the troops also gave him provisions and small gifts, and let him go.

**47:6** So Jeremiah came to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam at Mizpah, and lived with him among the people who had been left in the land.

**47:7** And when all the commanders of the army who had been scattered throughout the regions, they and their associates, heard that the king of Babylon had put Gedaliah the son of Ahikam in charge of the land, and that he had entrusted to him men, women, and little children, and some of the poor of the land who had not been carried away to Babylon,

**47:8** they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah: Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, and Johanan and Jonathan the sons of Kareah, and Seraiah the son of Tanhumeth, and the sons of Ephai, who were from Netophathi, and Jezaniah the son of Maacathi, they and their men.

**47:9** And Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, swore to them and to their companions, saying, "Do not be afraid to serve the Chaldeans; live in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it will go well with you.

**47:10** Look, as for me, I am living in Mizpah so that I may answer the command of the Chaldeans who are sent to us; but as for you, gather the vintage, and the harvest, and the oil, and store them in your vessels, and remain in your cities that you hold."

**47:11** Moreover, all the Judeans who were in Moab, and among the sons of Ammon, and in Idumea, and in all the regions, when they heard that the king of Babylon had granted a remnant in Judea and that he had appointed Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, over them,

**47:12** returned, I say, all the Judeans, from all the places to which they had fled, and came into the land of Judah, to Gedaliah at Mizpah, and gathered wine and a very great harvest.

**47:13** Now Johanan the son of Kareah and all the commanders of the army who had been scattered in the regions came to Gedaliah at Mizpah,

**47:14** and said to him, "Know that Baalis, king of the sons of Ammon, has sent Ishmael the son of Nethaniah to strike down your life." But Gedaliah the son of Ahikam did not believe them.

**47:15** Then Johanan the son of Kareah spoke to Gedaliah privately in Mizpah, saying, "I will go and strike down Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, with no one knowing, so that he does not destroy your life, and all the Judeans who have been gathered to you are not scattered, and the remnant of Judah does not perish."

**47:16** And Gedaliah the son of Ahikam said to Johanan the son of Kareah, "Do not do this thing, for you are speaking a falsehood about Ishmael."

## Translator Notes

- **47:1** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew title 'captain of the guard' with the late Roman military title 'commander of the troops' (magister militiae, and 'captain of the troops', princeps militiae, in verse 2).
- **47:5** — The Vulgate resolves a difficult Hebrew clause ('while he had not yet turned back...') as a direct continuation of Nebuzaradan's speech: 'and do not come with me' (et mecum noli venire).
- **47:8** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew gentilics 'the Netophathite' and 'the Maacathite' as a place name ('from Netophathi', de Netophathi) and a patronymic ('the son of Maacathi', filius Maachathi).
- **47:10** — The Vulgate reads 'answer the command of the Chaldeans who are sent to us' (respondeam praecepto Chaldaeorum qui mittuntur ad nos) where the Hebrew reads 'stand before the Chaldeans who come to us,' and renders 'summer fruit' as 'harvest' (messem) here and in verse 12.
- **47:14** — The Vulgate translates with an imperative ('Know that...', Scito) rather than the Hebrew question ('Do you indeed know...?'), and similarly renders the Hebrew question in verse 15 as a negative purpose clause ('so that he does not...', ne).
