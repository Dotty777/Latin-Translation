## Text

**3:1** And I said, "Listen, ruler of Jacob, and leaders of the house of Israel: is it not for you to know justice,

**3:2** you who hate good and love evil, who take their skins by force from off them, and their flesh from off their bones;

**3:3** who have eaten the flesh of my people, and flayed their skin from off them, and broken their bones, and cut them to pieces as in a cauldron, and like flesh in the middle of a pot?

**3:4** Then they will cry out to the Lord, and He will not hear them, and He will hide His face from them at that time, just as they acted wickedly in their devices."

**3:5** The Lord says this concerning the prophets who lead My people astray, who bite with their teeth and proclaim peace, and if anyone does not put anything in their mouth, they sanctify war against him:

**3:6** "Therefore there will be night for you instead of vision, and darkness for you instead of divination; and the sun will go down over the prophets, and the day will grow dark over them.

**3:7** And those who see visions will be put to shame, and the diviners will be put to shame; and all of them will cover their faces, because there is no answer from God."

**3:8** Nevertheless, I have been filled with the strength of the Spirit of the Lord, with justice, and with power, to declare to Jacob his crime, and to Israel his sin.

**3:9** Listen to this, rulers of the house of Jacob, and judges of the house of Israel, who detest justice and pervert all that is upright,

**3:10** who build Zion with bloodshed, and Jerusalem with iniquity.

**3:11** Her rulers judged for bribes, and her priests taught for a wage, and her prophets divined for money; and they rested upon the Lord, saying, "Is not the Lord in our midst? Evils will not come upon us."

**3:12** Because of this, on your account, Zion will be plowed like a field, and Jerusalem will be like a heap of stones, and the mountain of the temple will become the high places of forests.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — The Clementine Vulgate reads singular princeps Jacob ("ruler of Jacob") alongside plural duces ("leaders"), whereas 3:9 and 3:11 use plural principes.
- **3:7** — The Vulgate reads vultos suos ("their faces," a variant plural form of vultus) where the Hebrew specifies covering the mustache or upper lip.
- **3:11** — The Vulgate places the verbs in the past imperfect (judicabant, docebant, divinabant, requiescebant, "rested") where the Hebrew uses imperfective/present forms and "leaned."
