## Text

**5:1** Now you will be laid waste, daughter of a robber. They have laid a siege against us; with a rod they will strike the cheek of the judge of Israel.

**5:2** And you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, are very small among the thousands of Judah; from you will go forth for Me One who is to be ruler in Israel, and His going forth is from the beginning, from the days of eternity.

**5:3** Because of this He will give them up until the time when she who is in labor gives birth, and the remnant of His brothers will return to the sons of Israel.

**5:4** And He will stand and shepherd in the strength of the Lord, in the majesty of the name of the Lord His God; and they will return, for now He will be magnified to the ends of the earth.

**5:5** And this One will be peace: when the Assyrian comes into our land, and when he treads upon our houses, then we will raise up against him seven shepherds and eight leading men;

**5:6** and they will shepherd the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod with its lances; and He will deliver us from Assyria when he comes into our land, and when he treads within our borders.

**5:7** And the remnant of Jacob will be in the midst of many peoples like dew from the Lord, and like drops upon the grass, which does not wait for a man, and does not await the sons of men.

**5:8** And the remnant of Jacob will be among the nations, in the midst of many peoples, like a lion among the beasts of the forests, and like a young lion among flocks of sheep; and when it passes through, and tramples down, and seizes, there is no one to rescue.

**5:9** Your hand will be exalted over your foes, and all your enemies will perish.

**5:10** "And it will be in that day," says the Lord, "I will remove your horses from your midst, and I will wipe out your chariots.

**5:11** And I will destroy the cities of your land, and I will tear down all your strongholds; and I will remove sorceries from your hand, and there will be no divinations in you;

**5:12** and I will cause your carved images and your statues to perish from your midst, and you will no longer worship the works of your hands;

**5:13** and I will uproot your groves from your midst, and I will shatter your cities.

**5:14** And I will execute vengeance, in fury and in indignation, upon all the nations that have not listened."

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — In native Clementine Vulgate numbering (Clem1598-USFX), this verse begins chapter 5 as 5:1 (mapped to Saints.Bible 4:14, shifting 5:1-11 one verse relative to Saints 4:14 and 5:1-10); filia latronis ('daughter of a robber') renders the Hebrew phrase 'daughter of troops.'
- **5:2** — Native Vulgate 5:2 maps to Saints.Bible 5:1. Latin qui sit dominator ('one who is to be ruler') and ejus ('his') are lowercase; capitalization of 'One' and 'His' reflects traditional messianic reading and is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **5:3** — Native Vulgate 5:3 maps to Saints.Bible 5:2. Capitalization of 'He'/'His' (referring to the ruler of 5:2) is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **5:4** — Vulgate convertentur ('they will return' or 'they will be converted') reflects the root for turning back, differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ('they will dwell securely'). Native Vulgate 5:4 maps to Saints.Bible 5:3. Capitalization of 'He'/'His' is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **5:5** — Native Vulgate 5:5 maps to Saints.Bible 5:4. Capitalization of 'One' (Latin iste, 'this one') reflects traditional messianic reading and is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **5:6** — Vulgate in lanceis ejus ('with its lances') stands where the Hebrew reads 'in its entrances.' Native Vulgate 5:6 maps to Saints.Bible 5:5. Capitalization of 'He' (Latin liberabit) is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **5:11** — In native Clementine Vulgate numbering, 5:10-11 are consecutive verses (mapped to Saints.Bible 5:9-10; 5:11 combines the clauses numbered 5:11-12 in some other editions).
- **5:13** — Vulgate lucos tuos ('your groves') renders Hebrew Asherim (Asherah poles) as sacred groves.
