## Text

**2:1** Woe to you who devise what is useless and work evil on your beds! In the morning light they do it, because their hand is against God.

**2:2** And they coveted fields and took them by force, and seized houses; and they were defrauding a man and his house, a man and his inheritance.

**2:3** Therefore the Lord says this: "Look, I am devising evil against this family, from which you will not remove your necks, and you will not walk proudly, because it is a most evil time."

**2:4** In that day a proverb will be taken up against you, and a song will be sung with sweetness by those who say, "By devastation we have been laid waste; the portion of my people has been changed; how will he depart from me, when he returns to divide our regions?"

**2:5** Because of this there will not be for you anyone casting the cord of the lot in the assembly of the Lord.

**2:6** Do not speak, you who speak; it will not drip upon these; shame will not overtake them.

**2:7** The house of Jacob says, "Has the Spirit of the Lord been shortened, or are His thoughts like these?" Are not My words good with him who walks uprightly?

**2:8** And on the contrary, My people has risen up as an adversary. From off the tunic you have taken away the cloak, and those who were passing by innocently you have turned to war.

**2:9** You have driven out the women of My people from the house of their delights; from their little ones you have taken away My praise forever.

**2:10** Arise and go, because you do not have rest here; because of its uncleanness it will be corrupted with the worst decay.

**2:11** If only I were not a man having a spirit, and spoke falsehood instead! I will drip to you for wine and for drunkenness; and this people will be the one upon whom it is dripped.

**2:12** With gathering I will gather all of you, Jacob; I will bring together into one the remnant of Israel; I will place him together like a flock in a sheepfold, like a herd in the middle of the pens; they will make an uproar from the multitude of men.

**2:13** For he who opens the way will go up before them; they will divide, and pass through the gate, and go in through it; and their king will pass on before them, and the Lord at their head.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew idiom for having power in one's hand literally as quoniam contra Deum est manus eorum ("because their hand is against God").
- **2:4** — The Vulgate reads cum suavitate ("with sweetness") where the Hebrew expresses a bitter wail, and construes the final clauses as a question about the returning divider of their lands (quomodo recedet a me, cum revertatur, qui regiones nostras dividat?).
- **2:11** — The Vulgate renders this difficult verse as a first-person optative lament (Utinam non essem...), keeps the literal verb stillo ("drip," as in 2:6) for prophetic utterance, and reads ebrietatem ("drunkenness") for the Hebrew "strong drink."
- **2:13** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew "breaker" as pandens iter ("he who opens the way") and has ingredientur per eam ("go in through it") where the Hebrew reads "go out through it."
