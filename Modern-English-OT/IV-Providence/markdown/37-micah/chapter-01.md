## Text

**1:1** The word of the Lord which came to Micah the Morashtite in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.

**1:2** Listen, all peoples, and let the earth pay attention, and its fullness; and let the Lord God be a witness against you, the Lord from His holy temple.

**1:3** For look, the Lord will go out from His place, and will come down, and will tread upon the high places of the earth.

**1:4** And the mountains will be consumed beneath Him, and the valleys will be split apart like wax before fire, and like waters that rush down headlong.

**1:5** All this is because of the crime of Jacob, and because of the sins of the house of Israel. What is the crime of Jacob? Is it not Samaria? And what are the high places of Judah? Is it not Jerusalem?

**1:6** And I will make Samaria like a heap of stones in a field when a vineyard is planted; and I will pull her stones down into the valley, and I will uncover her foundations.

**1:7** And all her carved images will be cut to pieces, and all her wages will be burned with fire, and I will bring all her idols to destruction, because they were gathered from the wages of a prostitute, and even to the wage of a prostitute they will return.

**1:8** Over this I will lament and wail; I will go stripped and naked; I will make a lamentation like that of dragons, and a mourning like that of ostriches,

**1:9** because her wound is hopeless, because it has come even to Judah; it has touched the gate of my people, even to Jerusalem.

**1:10** In Gath do not declare it; do not weep with tears; in the house of dust, sprinkle yourselves with dust.

**1:11** And pass on for yourselves, beautiful dwelling, put to shame with disgrace; she who dwells at the exit has not gone out; the neighboring house, which stood for itself, will receive lamentation from you.

**1:12** For she who dwells in bitterness has grown weak for what is good, because evil has come down from the Lord to the gate of Jerusalem.

**1:13** The uproar of a chariot of astonishment is for the inhabitant of Lachish; she is the beginning of sin to the daughter of Zion, because in you the crimes of Israel were found.

**1:14** Therefore she will give emissaries over the inheritance of Gath, houses of falsehood for a deception to the kings of Israel.

**1:15** I will yet bring an heir to you who dwell in Mareshah; the glory of Israel will come even to Adullam.

**1:16** Make yourself bald and shear your hair over the children of your delights; widen your baldness like an eagle, because they have been led away captive from you.

## Translator Notes

- **1:8** — The Vulgate reads spoliatus ("stripped") where the Hebrew has "barefoot," and renders the Hebrew desert creatures as draconum ("dragons," often translated "jackals" from the Hebrew) and struthionum ("ostriches").
- **1:10** — Across 1:10-14 the Vulgate translates several Hebrew place names literally to preserve their wordplays, here rendering Beth-le-aphrah as in domo pulveris ("in the house of dust"), along with lacrimis ne ploretis ("do not weep with tears").
- **1:11** — Continuing its literal translation of town names, the Vulgate renders Shaphir as habitatio pulchra ("beautiful dwelling"), Zaanan as in exitu ("at the exit"), and Beth-ezel as domus vicina ("the neighboring house"), and similarly renders Maroth in 1:12 as in amaritudinibus ("in bitterness").
- **1:14** — The Vulgate has a third-person verb dabit ("she will give") with emissarios ("emissaries"), translates Moresheth-gath as hæreditatem Geth ("the inheritance of Gath"), and renders Achzib as domus mendacii ("houses of falsehood").
