## Text

**4:1** And it will be: in the last of the days the mountain of the house of the Lord will be prepared at the summit of the mountains, and lofty above the hills, and peoples will flow to it,

**4:2** and many nations will hasten and say, "Come, let us go up to the mountain of the Lord and to the house of the God of Jacob; and He will teach us concerning His ways, and we will go in His paths, for from Zion the law will go forth, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem."

**4:3** And He will judge between many peoples, and He will rebuke strong nations even far away; and they will beat their swords into plowshares and their spears into hoes; nation will not take up sword against nation, and they will no longer learn to wage war.

**4:4** And each man will sit beneath his vine and beneath his fig tree, and there will be no one to frighten him, for the mouth of the Lord of hosts has spoken.

**4:5** For all the peoples will walk, each one in the name of his god; but we will walk in the name of the Lord our God, forever and beyond.

**4:6** "In that day," says the Lord, "I will assemble her who limps, and I will gather her whom I had cast out and her whom I had afflicted;

**4:7** and I will make her who limps into a remnant, and her who had been distressed into a mighty nation; and the Lord will reign over them on Mount Zion, from now on and forever."

**4:8** And you, cloudy tower of the flock of the daughter of Zion, even to you it will come, and the first authority will come, the kingdom of the daughter of Jerusalem.

**4:9** Now why are you constricted with grief? Do you have no king, or has your counselor perished, that pain has seized you like a woman in labor?

**4:10** Be in pain and strain, daughter of Zion, like a woman in labor, for now you will go forth from the city, and you will dwell in the country, and you will come all the way to Babylon; there you will be delivered, there the Lord will redeem you from the hand of your enemies.

**4:11** And now many nations have been gathered against you, who say, "Let her be stoned, and let our eye look upon Zion."

**4:12** But they themselves have not known the thoughts of the Lord, and have not understood His counsel, for He has gathered them like the hay of the threshing floor.

**4:13** Arise and thresh, daughter of Zion, for I will make your horn iron, and I will make your hooves bronze; and you will crush many peoples, and you will destroy their plunder for the Lord, and their strength for the Lord of the whole earth.

**4:14** Now you will be laid waste, daughter of a robber. They have laid a siege against us; with a rod they will strike the cheek of the judge of Israel.

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — Vulgate ligones literally means 'hoes' or 'mattocks,' where the Hebrew has 'pruning hooks.'
- **4:8** — Vulgate nebulosa ('cloudy' or 'misty') interprets Hebrew ophel ('hill' or 'citadel') as a term for mist or darkness modifying 'tower.'
- **4:9** — Vulgate moerore contraheris ('are you constricted with grief') is a distinctly Vulgate rendering where the Hebrew reads 'do you cry aloud.'
- **4:11** — Vulgate Lapidetur ('Let her be stoned') is a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'Let her be profaned.'
- **4:14** — In the Vulgate (following the Hebrew chapter division), this verse ends chapter 4 rather than beginning chapter 5; filia latronis ('daughter of a robber') renders the Hebrew phrase 'daughter of troops.'
