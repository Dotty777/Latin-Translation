## Text

**6:1** Hear what the Lord speaks: "Arise, contend in judgment against the mountains, and let the hills hear your voice."

**6:2** Let the mountains hear the judgment of the Lord, and the strong foundations of the earth; for the judgment of the Lord is with His people, and with Israel it will be adjudicated.

**6:3** My people, what have I done to you? Or how have I been burdensome to you? Answer Me.

**6:4** For I brought you out of the land of Egypt, and from the house of slaves I delivered you, and I sent before your face Moses, and Aaron, and Miriam.

**6:5** My people, remember, please, what Balak, king of Moab, planned, and what Balaam, son of Beor, answered him, from Shittim all the way to Gilgal, so that you might know the righteous acts of the Lord.

**6:6** What worthy thing should I offer to the Lord? Should I bend the knee to God on high? Should I offer to Him burnt offerings and year-old calves?

**6:7** Can the Lord be appeased with thousands of rams, or with many thousands of fat male goats? Should I give my firstborn for my wickedness, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?

**6:8** I will show you, man, what is good, and what the Lord requires from you: indeed, to practice justice, and to love mercy, and to walk carefully with your God.

**6:9** The voice of the Lord cries out to the city, and there will be salvation for those who fear Your name: hear, tribes, and who will approve of it?

**6:10** Still there is fire in the house of the wicked, treasures of iniquity, and a smaller measure full of wrath.

**6:11** Will I justify a wicked balance, and the deceitful weights of a pouch?

**6:12** By which its rich men have been filled with iniquity, and those dwelling in it were speaking falsehood, and their tongue is fraudulent in their mouth.

**6:13** And therefore I have begun to strike you with destruction because of your sins.

**6:14** You will eat, and you will not be satisfied, and your humiliation will be in your midst; and you will take hold, and you will not save, and those whom you do save, I will give to the sword.

**6:15** You will sow, and you will not reap; you will tread the olive, and you will not be anointed with oil; and new wine, and you will not drink wine.

**6:16** And you have kept the precepts of Omri, and every work of the house of Ahab, and you have walked in their desires, so that I might give you over to destruction, and those dwelling in it to hissing; and you will bear the reproach of My people.

## Translator Notes

- **6:7** — Vulgate in multis millibus hircorum pinguium ('with many thousands of fat male goats') is a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads 'with ten thousands of rivers of oil.'
- **6:8** — Vulgate Indicabo ('I will show,' first-person future) differs from Hebrew 'He has told,' and sollicitum ('carefully' or 'attentively') expresses conscientious watchfulness rather than 'humbly.'
- **6:9** — Vulgate salus erit timentibus nomen tuum ('there will be salvation for those who fear Your name') and tribus, et quis approbabit illud ('O tribes, and who will approve of it') reflect a distinctly Vulgate interpretation of a difficult Hebrew line.
- **6:10** — Vulgate Adhuc ignis ('Still there is fire') reads the Hebrew particle as the noun 'fire,' and irae plena ('full of wrath') stands where the Hebrew has 'accursed.'
- **6:13** — Vulgate coepi ('I have begun') translates the Hebrew verb from the root 'to begin' rather than from the homonymous root meaning 'to make sick' or 'strike grievously.'
