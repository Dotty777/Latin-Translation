## Text

**4:1** "For look, the day will come, burning like a furnace; and all the proud and all who do wickedness will be stubble; and the coming day will set them ablaze," says the Lord of hosts, "which will not leave them root or sprout.

**4:2** And for you who fear My name, the sun of righteousness will rise, and healing in its wings; and you will go out and leap like calves from the herd.

**4:3** And you will trample the wicked, when they become ashes under the sole of your feet, on the day when I act," says the Lord of hosts.

**4:4** "Remember the law of Moses My servant, which I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel, precepts and judgments.

**4:5** Look, I will send you Elijah the prophet, before the great and fearsome day of the Lord comes.

**4:6** And he will turn the heart of the fathers to the children, and the heart of the children to their fathers, or else I might come and strike the land with a curse."

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — In native Clementine Vulgate numbering (Clem1598-USFX), chapter 4 comprises 4:1-6 (mapped to Saints.Bible 3:19-24, where 4:4 = Saints 3:24 and 4:5-6 = Saints 3:22-23).
- **4:2** — Vulgate reads "from the herd" (de armento), whereas the Hebrew has "from the stall."
- **4:4** — In native Clementine Vulgate order (matching the Hebrew MT), the charge to remember the law of Moses stands at 4:4 before the Elijah prophecy in 4:5-6 (whereas Saints.Bible places Moses at 3:24 after Elijah at 3:22-23).
- **4:5** — Native Clementine Vulgate 4:5-6 place the Elijah prophecy after 4:4 (mapped to Saints.Bible 3:22-23, which place Elijah before Moses at 3:24); in 4:6 "curse" translates anathemate, a ban of utter destruction.
