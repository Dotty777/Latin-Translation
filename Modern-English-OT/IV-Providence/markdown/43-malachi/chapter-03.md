## Text

**3:1** "Look, I am sending My messenger, and he will prepare the way before My face; and at once the Sovereign whom you seek will come to His temple, and the messenger of the covenant whom you desire. Look, He is coming," says the Lord of hosts.

**3:2** And who will be able to think of the day of His coming, and who will stand to see Him? For He Himself is like a smelting fire, and like the herb of fullers;

**3:3** and He will sit smelting and cleansing silver, and He will purify the sons of Levi, and He will refine them like gold and like silver, and they will be the Lord's, offering sacrifices in righteousness.

**3:4** And the sacrifice of Judah and Jerusalem will please the Lord, as in the days of old, and as in the ancient years.

**3:5** "And I will draw near to you in judgment, and I will be a swift witness against sorcerers, and adulterers, and perjurers, and against those who defraud the hired worker of his wage, and widows and orphans, and oppress the foreigner, and have not feared Me," says the Lord of hosts.

**3:6** "For I am the Lord, and I do not change; and you, sons of Jacob, have not been consumed.

**3:7** For from the days of your fathers you have departed from My statutes, and have not kept them. Return to Me, and I will return to you," says the Lord of hosts. "And you have said, 'In what way will we return?'

**3:8** Will a man afflict God, that you are piercing Me? And you have said, 'In what way are we piercing You?' In tithes and in firstfruits.

**3:9** And in scarcity you are cursed, and you are piercing Me, the whole nation.

**3:10** Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse, and let there be food in My house; and test Me in this," says the Lord, "if I will not open for you the floodgates of heaven, and pour out for you a blessing even to abundance,

**3:11** and I will rebuke the devourer for your sake, and it will not ruin the fruit of your land, nor will the vine in the field be barren," says the Lord of hosts.

**3:12** "And all nations will call you blessed, for you will be a desirable land," says the Lord of hosts.

**3:13** "Your words have grown strong against Me," says the Lord.

**3:14** "And you have said, 'What have we spoken against You?' You have said, 'Fruitless is the one who serves God; and what profit is there because we have kept His precepts, and because we have walked sorrowfully before the Lord of hosts?

**3:15** Therefore now we call the arrogant blessed, since those who do wickedness have been built up, and they have put God to the test, and have been kept safe.'"

**3:16** Then those who feared the Lord spoke, each one with his neighbor; and the Lord paid attention and heard, and a book of remembrance was written before Him for those who fear the Lord and think on His name.

**3:17** "And they will be Mine," says the Lord of hosts, "on the day when I act, for a special possession; and I will spare them, just as a man spares his son who serves him.

**3:18** And you will turn back, and you will see what is between the righteous and the wicked, and between the one who serves God and the one who does not serve Him."

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Clem1598-USFX prints capitalized Dominator ('the Sovereign'); capitalization of 'Sovereign', 'His', and 'He' (versus lowercase 'he' for My messenger) is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **3:2** — Vulgate reads "think of the day of His coming" (cogitare) and "to see Him" (ad videndum eum), where the Hebrew has "endure the day of His coming" and "when He appears"; "herb of fullers" (herba fullonum) refers to an alkali plant used in laundering cloth.
- **3:8** — Vulgate translates the Hebrew verb for "rob/defraud" with "afflict" (affliget) and "pierce" (configitis, also in 3:9), reads "firstfruits" (primitiis) for Hebrew "contributions," and in 3:9 reads "in scarcity" (in penuria, cf. 2:2) where the Hebrew has "with a curse."
