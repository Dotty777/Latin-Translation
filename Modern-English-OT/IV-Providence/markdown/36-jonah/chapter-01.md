## Text

**1:1** And the word of the Lord came to Jonah, son of Amittai, saying,

**1:2** "Rise, and go to Nineveh, the large city, and preach in it, because its wickedness has gone up before Me."

**1:3** And Jonah rose to flee to Tarshish from the presence of the Lord, and he went down to Joppa; and he found a ship going to Tarshish, and paid its fare, and went down into it to go with them to Tarshish from the presence of the Lord.

**1:4** But the Lord sent a great wind onto the sea, and a great storm arose on the sea, and the ship was in danger of being shattered.

**1:5** And the sailors were afraid, and the men cried out to their own god, and they threw the goods that were in the ship into the sea so that it might be lightened of them; and Jonah went down to the interior of the ship and was sleeping in a heavy sleep.

**1:6** And the helmsman approached him and said to him, "Why are you weighed down with sleep? Rise, call upon your God, if perhaps God may think of us, and we may not perish."

**1:7** And each man said to his companion, "Come, and let us cast lots, and let us know why this evil is upon us." And they cast lots, and the lot fell on Jonah.

**1:8** And they said to him, "Tell us on whose account this evil is upon us: what is your work? What is your land, and where are you going? Or from what people are you?"

**1:9** And he said to them, "I am a Hebrew, and I fear the Lord God of heaven, who made the sea and the dry land."

**1:10** And the men feared with a great fear and said to him, "Why have you done this?" For the men had learned that he was fleeing from the presence of the Lord, because he had told them.

**1:11** And they said to him, "What shall we do to you so that the sea will grow calm for us?" For the sea kept going on and swelling up.

**1:12** And he said to them, "Pick me up and throw me into the sea, and the sea will grow calm for you; for I know that because of me this huge storm has come upon you."

**1:13** And the men were rowing to return to the dry land, and they could not, because the sea kept going on and swelling up over them.

**1:14** And they cried out to the Lord and said, "We implore You, Lord, let us not perish for the life of this man, and do not lay innocent blood upon us; because You, Lord, have done as You willed."

**1:15** And they picked up Jonah and threw him into the sea, and the sea stood still from its raging.

**1:16** And the men feared the Lord with a great fear, and they offered sacrifices to the Lord and made vows.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — The Vulgate reads "preach in it" (prædica in ea) and singular "its wickedness" (malitia ejus), whereas the Hebrew text reads "cry out against it" and plural "their wickedness."
- **1:8** — The Vulgate asks "and where are you going?" (et quo vadis?), whereas the Hebrew text asks "where do you come from?"
- **1:11** — Latin "ibat, et intumescebat" (literally "kept going on and swelling up," repeated in 1:13) reflects a Semitic idiom for a storm growing steadily fiercer, while depicting the sea specifically as swelling up.
