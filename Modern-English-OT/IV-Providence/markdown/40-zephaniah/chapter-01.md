## Text

**1:1** The word of the Lord that came to Zephaniah, the son of Cushi, the son of Gedaliah, the son of Amariah, the son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah, the son of Amon, king of Judah.

**1:2** "Gathering, I will gather all things from the face of the earth," says the Lord,

**1:3** "gathering man and beast, gathering the birds of the sky and the fish of the sea; and there will be ruins of the wicked, and I will destroy mankind from the face of the earth," says the Lord.

**1:4** "And I will stretch out My hand over Judah and over all who inhabit Jerusalem, and I will destroy from this place the remnant of Baal and the names of the temple keepers along with the priests,

**1:5** and those who worship the host of heaven on the rooftops, and who worship and swear by the Lord, and swear by Milcom,

**1:6** and those who turn away from behind the back of the Lord, and who have not sought the Lord nor searched for Him."

**1:7** Be silent before the face of the Lord God, for the day of the Lord is near; for the Lord has prepared a sacrifice; He has consecrated His invited ones.

**1:8** "And it will happen on the day of the Lord's sacrifice: I will punish the princes and the sons of the king and all who are clothed in foreign attire,

**1:9** and I will punish everyone who arrogantly steps over the threshold on that day, those who fill the house of the Lord their God with iniquity and deceit."

**1:10** "And on that day," says the Lord, "there will be the sound of an outcry from the Fish Gate, and wailing from the Second, and a great shattering from the hills.

**1:11** Wail, inhabitants of the Mortar: all the people of Canaan have fallen silent; all who are wrapped in silver have perished.

**1:12** And it will happen at that time: I will search Jerusalem with lamps, and I will punish the men fixed in their dregs, who say in their hearts, 'The Lord will not do good, and He will not do evil.'

**1:13** And their strength will become plunder, and their houses a wasteland; and they will build houses and not inhabit them, and they will plant vineyards and not drink their wine."

**1:14** Near is the great day of the Lord; it is near and exceedingly swift. The sound of the day of the Lord is bitter; there the mighty man will be distressed.

**1:15** That day is a day of wrath, a day of tribulation and anguish, a day of disaster and misery, a day of darkness and thick gloom, a day of cloud and whirlwind,

**1:16** a day of trumpet and blast against the fortified cities and against the high corners.

**1:17** "And I will distress people, and they will walk like the blind, because they have sinned against the Lord; and their blood will be poured out like soil, and their bodies like dung."

**1:18** Even their silver and their gold will not be able to deliver them on the day of the Lord's wrath; in the fire of His zeal the whole earth will be devoured, for He will make a complete end with haste of all who inhabit the earth.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew idiom (commonly understood as "utterly sweep away") literally as "Gathering, I will gather" (Congregans congregabo), continuing the participle into verse 3.
- **1:3** — The Vulgate reads "there will be ruins of the wicked" (ruinæ impiorum erunt), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "the stumbling blocks along with the wicked."
- **1:9** — The Vulgate specifies "arrogantly steps over the threshold" (arroganter ingreditur, where Hebrew has "leaps over") and identifies the house as "the house of the Lord their God" (where Hebrew has "the house of their master").
- **1:11** — "The Mortar" translates Latin Pila (the Jerusalem market quarter called Maktesh in Hebrew; similarly "the Second" in 1:10 translates Secunda, the Second Quarter of Jerusalem), and "wrapped in silver" (involuti argento) reflects a Vulgate-distinct rendering where the Hebrew has "those who weigh out silver."
- **1:16** — Latin angulos ("corners," here and in 3:6) refers to the fortified corner towers or battlements of city walls.
