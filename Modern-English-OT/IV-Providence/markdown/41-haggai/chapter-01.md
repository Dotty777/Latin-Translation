## Text

**1:1** In the second year of King Darius, in the sixth month, on the first day of the month, the word of the Lord came through the hand of Haggai the prophet to Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua, son of Jehozadak, the high priest, saying:

**1:2** This is what the Lord of hosts declares, saying: "This people says, 'The time has not yet come for the house of the Lord to be built.'"

**1:3** And the word of the Lord came through the hand of Haggai the prophet, saying:

**1:4** "Is it really time for you to live in paneled houses, while this house is desolate?"

**1:5** And now this is what the Lord of hosts says: "Apply your hearts to your ways.

**1:6** You sowed much, and brought in little; you ate, and were not satisfied; you drank, and were not made drunk; you covered yourselves, and were not warmed; and the one who gathered wages put them into a pierced bag."

**1:7** This is what the Lord of hosts says: "Apply your hearts to your ways;

**1:8** go up to the mountain, bring wood, and build the house; and it will be acceptable to Me, and I will be glorified," says the Lord.

**1:9** "You looked for more, and look, it became less; and you brought it into the house, and I blew it away. For what reason?" says the Lord of hosts. "Because My house is desolate, and you hurry, each one to his own house.

**1:10** Because of this, the heavens above you have been restrained from giving dew, and the earth has been restrained from giving its yield;

**1:11** and I have called a drought upon the land, and upon the mountains, and upon the wheat, and upon the wine, and upon the oil, and whatever the ground brings forth, and upon people, and upon livestock, and upon all the labor of the hands."

**1:12** And Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, and Joshua, son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and all the remnant of the people listened to the voice of the Lord their God and the words of Haggai the prophet, just as the Lord their God had sent him to them; and the people feared before the face of the Lord.

**1:13** And Haggai, a messenger of the Lord from among the messengers of the Lord, spoke to the people, saying, "I am with you," says the Lord.

**1:14** And the Lord roused the spirit of Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua, son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of the remnant from all the people; and they entered and were doing work in the house of the Lord of hosts, their God.

**1:15** On the twenty-fourth day of the month, in the sixth month, in the second year of King Darius.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — The Vulgate spells the high priest's name Jesus (the Graeco-Latin form of Hebrew Joshua), Shealtiel as Salathiel, and Jehozadak as Josedec; standard modern English spellings are used here.
- **1:5** — Latin Ponite corda vestra super vias vestras literally means "place/set your hearts upon your ways," a Hebraism preserved in the Vulgate for giving careful thought to one's conduct (similarly in 1:7; 2:15, 18).
- **1:6** — Latin inebriati literally means "made drunk" or "saturated," used idiomatically in the Vulgate for drinking one's fill in abundance rather than sinful drunkenness.
- **1:13** — Latin nuntius Domini de nuntiis Domini ("a messenger of the Lord from among the messengers of the Lord," or possibly "from the messages of the Lord") is a Vulgate-distinct rendering of the Hebrew phrase meaning "the Lord's messenger in the Lord's commission."
