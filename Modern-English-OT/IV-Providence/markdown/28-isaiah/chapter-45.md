## Text

**45:1** This is what the Lord says to My anointed, Cyrus, whose right hand I have grasped, so that I may subdue nations before his face and turn the backs of kings, and open doors before him, and the gates will not be shut:

**45:2** "I will go before you, and I will humble the glorious ones of the earth; I will crush bronze gates and break iron bars in pieces;

**45:3** and I will give you hidden treasures and the concealed things of secret places, so that you may know that I am the Lord, who calls your name, the God of Israel,

**45:4** for the sake of My servant Jacob, and Israel, My chosen one; and I have called you by your name; I have likened you, and you have not known Me.

**45:5** I am the Lord, and there is no one else; outside Me there is no god; I have girded you, and you have not known Me,

**45:6** so that those from the rising of the sun and those from the west may know that apart from Me there is none; I am the Lord, and there is no other,

**45:7** forming light and creating darkness, making peace and creating evil; I am the Lord, doing all these things.

**45:8** Shed dew, heavens, from above, and let the clouds rain down the Righteous One; let the earth be opened and bring forth the Savior, and let righteousness arise at the same time: I, the Lord, have created Him."

**45:9** Woe to the one who contradicts his molder, a potsherd from the earthen vessels of the earth! Will the clay say to its potter, "What are you making, and your work is without hands?"

**45:10** Woe to the one who says to a father, "What are you fathering?" and to a woman, "What are you in labor with?"

**45:11** This is what the Lord says, the Holy One of Israel, its fashioner: "Ask Me about things to come; concerning My sons and concerning the work of My hands, command Me.

**45:12** I made the earth, and I Myself created man upon it; My hands stretched out the heavens, and I commanded all their host.

**45:13** I have raised him up for righteousness, and I will direct all his ways; he himself will build My city and will release My captives, not for a price nor for gifts," says the Lord God of hosts.

**45:14** This is what the Lord says: "The labor of Egypt, and the trade of Ethiopia, and the Sabeans, tall men, will cross over to you and will be yours; they will walk behind you, bound in manacles they will go, and they will bow down to you and plead with you: 'Only in you is God, and there is no god apart from you.'"

**45:15** Truly You are a hidden God, the God of Israel, the Savior.

**45:16** They have been put to shame, and all of them have been humiliated; together the makers of errors have gone away into shame.

**45:17** Israel has been saved in the Lord with an eternal salvation; you will not be put to shame, and you will not be humiliated forever and ever.

**45:18** For this is what the Lord says, who creates the heavens, He who is God, forming the earth and making it, He Himself its fashioner; He did not create it in vain; He formed it to be inhabited: "I am the Lord, and there is no other.

**45:19** I have not spoken in secret, in a dark place of the earth; I did not say to the offspring of Jacob in vain, 'Seek Me.' I am the Lord, speaking righteousness, declaring what is right.

**45:20** Assemble and come, and draw near together, you who have been saved from the nations: they have no knowledge, those who lift up the wood of their carved image and pray to a god that does not save.

**45:21** Declare, and come, and take counsel together. Who made this heard from the beginning, foretold it from that time? Is it not I, the Lord, and there is no other god apart from Me? A righteous God and one who saves: there is none besides Me.

**45:22** Turn to Me, and you will be saved, all the ends of the earth, for I am God, and there is no other.

**45:23** By Myself I have sworn; a word of righteousness will go out from My mouth and will not return: that to Me every knee will bow, and every tongue will swear."

**45:24** "Therefore in the Lord," one will say, "are my acts of righteousness and dominion." To Him they will come, and all who resist Him will be put to shame.

**45:25** In the Lord all the offspring of Israel will be justified and will be praised.

## Translator Notes

- **45:1** — Latin christo meo ("My anointed") agrees with the Septuagint against the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("His anointed"), and dorsa regum vertam ("turn the backs of kings") differs from the Hebrew "ungird the loins of kings."
- **45:4** — Latin assimilavi te (literally "I have likened you") is Jerome's rendering of the Hebrew verb for giving an honorary title or surname.
- **45:7** — Latin malum ("evil"), contrasted here with pacem ("peace"), refers to calamity, adversity, or physical hardship rather than moral evil.
- **45:8** — Unlike the Hebrew abstract nouns ("righteousness" and "salvation"), the Vulgate renders justum ("the Righteous One"), Salvatorem ("the Savior"), and eum ("Him") personally, giving this verse its classic messianic form; Latin germinet literally means "sprout" or "bud forth."
- **45:24** — Latin Ergo ("Therefore") and meæ sunt justitiæ et imperium ("my acts of righteousness and dominion") represent a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Hebrew reads "Only in the LORD, one shall say of me, are righteousness and strength."
