## Text

**12:1** And you will say on that day: "I will give thanks to You, Lord, because You were angry with me; Your fury has turned back, and You have comforted me.

**12:2** Look, God is my Savior; I will act confidently and I will not be afraid, because the Lord is my strength and my praise, and He has become salvation to me."

**12:3** You will draw waters with joy from the springs of the Savior.

**12:4** And you will say on that day: "Give thanks to the Lord and call upon His name; make His works known among the peoples; remember that His name is exalted.

**12:5** Sing to the Lord, because He has acted magnificently; declare this throughout the whole earth.

**12:6** Exult and praise, dwelling of Zion, because the Holy One of Israel is great in your midst."

## Translator Notes

- **12:2** — Vulgate reads salvator ("Savior") here and in 12:3 where the Hebrew has "salvation," while using in salutem ("salvation") at the end of 12:2; fiducialiter agam literally means "I will act confidently."
- **12:4** — Vulgate mementote ("remember") frames the command as recollection rather than the Hebrew causative "make mention" or "proclaim."
- **12:6** — Vulgate reads habitatio Sion ("dwelling of Zion"), an abstract or collective noun, rather than the Hebrew "inhabitant of Zion."
