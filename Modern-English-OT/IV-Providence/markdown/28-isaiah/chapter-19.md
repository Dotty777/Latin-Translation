## Text

**19:1** The burden of Egypt. Look, the Lord will ascend upon a light cloud and will enter Egypt, and the idols of Egypt will be shaken at His presence, and the heart of Egypt will melt in its midst.

**19:2** "And I will cause the Egyptians to clash against the Egyptians; and a man will fight against his brother, and a man against his friend, city against city, kingdom against kingdom.

**19:3** And the spirit of Egypt will be shattered within its inward parts, and I will cast down its counsel; and they will consult their idols, and their diviners, and mediums, and soothsayers.

**19:4** And I will hand Egypt over into the hand of cruel masters, and a strong king will rule over them," says the Lord God of hosts.

**19:5** And the water from the sea will dry up, and the river will become desolate and parched.

**19:6** And the rivers will fail; the streams of the embankments will dwindle and dry up; the reed and the rush will wither.

**19:7** The bed of the stream will be laid bare from its source, and every irrigated sowing will dry up, wither, and be no more.

**19:8** And the fishermen will grieve, and all who cast a hook into the river will mourn; and those who spread a net over the surface of the waters will languish.

**19:9** Those who worked flax will be put to shame, combing and weaving fine fabrics.

**19:10** And its irrigated grounds will droop: all who made ponds to catch fish.

**19:11** Foolish are the princes of Tanis; the wise counselors of Pharaoh have given unwise counsel. How will you say to Pharaoh, "I am a son of wise men, a son of ancient kings"?

**19:12** Where are your wise men now? Let them announce to you and declare what the Lord of hosts has planned against Egypt.

**19:13** The princes of Tanis have become foolish; the princes of Memphis have languished; they have deceived Egypt, the corner of its peoples.

**19:14** The Lord has mixed a spirit of dizziness in its midst; and they have made Egypt stagger in all its work, just as a drunken and vomiting man staggers.

**19:15** And there will be no work for Egypt that can make head and tail, the one bending down and the one restraining.

**19:16** In that day Egypt will be like women; and they will be stunned and will fear at the shaking of the hand of the Lord of hosts, which He Himself will shake over it.

**19:17** And the land of Judah will be a terror to Egypt; everyone who remembers it will be terrified because of the counsel of the Lord of hosts, which He Himself has planned against it.

**19:18** In that day there will be five cities in the land of Egypt speaking the language of Canaan and swearing by the Lord of hosts; one will be called "City of the Sun."

**19:19** In that day there will be an altar of the Lord in the midst of the land of Egypt, and a monument of the Lord beside its border.

**19:20** It will be for a sign and a witness to the Lord of hosts in the land of Egypt; for they will cry out to the Lord because of distress, and He will send them a savior and a defender who will deliver them.

**19:21** And the Lord will be known by Egypt, and the Egyptians will know the Lord in that day; and they will worship Him with sacrifices and with gifts; and they will vow vows to the Lord and fulfill them.

**19:22** And the Lord will strike Egypt with a plague, and He will heal it; and they will return to the Lord, and He will be appeased toward them, and He will heal them.

**19:23** In that day there will be a road from Egypt to Assyria; and the Assyrian will go into Egypt, and the Egyptian into Assyria, and the Egyptians will serve Assyria.

**19:24** In that day Israel will be a third with the Egyptian and the Assyrian, a blessing in the midst of the earth,

**19:25** which the Lord of hosts has blessed, saying, "Blessed be My people of Egypt, and the work of My hands for the Assyrian, while My inheritance is Israel."

## Translator Notes

- **19:10** — The Vulgate reads "its irrigated grounds will droop: all who made ponds to catch fish" for a difficult Hebrew verse that may mean "her weavers [or pillars] will be crushed, and all who work for wages will be grieved."
- **19:11** — Latin "Taneos" (Tanis, also in 19:13) is the Greco-Latin name for the Egyptian city called Zoan in Hebrew.
- **19:15** — The Vulgate construes "head and tail" as direct objects and renders the Hebrew botanical pair ("palm branch and reed") as "the one bending down and the one restraining" (incurvantem et refrenantem).
- **19:18** — Latin "Civitas solis" ("City of the Sun," i.e., Heliopolis) reflects the Hebrew textual reading Ir ha-Cheres (attested also in the Dead Sea Scrolls), whereas the standard Masoretic Text reads Ir ha-Heres ("City of Destruction").
- **19:23** — Latin "servient Aegyptii Assur" ("the Egyptians will serve Assyria") takes Assyria as the object of service, as the Septuagint does; the Hebrew can also mean "the Egyptians will worship with the Assyrians."
