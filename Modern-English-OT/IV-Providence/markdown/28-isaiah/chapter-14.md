## Text

**14:1** Its time is near to come, and its days will not be prolonged. For the Lord will have mercy on Jacob, and He will still choose from Israel, and He will make them rest upon their own ground; the stranger will be joined to them and will cling to the house of Jacob.

**14:2** And peoples will take them and bring them to their own place; and the house of Israel will possess them upon the land of the Lord as servants and maidservants; and they will be taking captive those who had taken them captive, and they will subdue their oppressors.

**14:3** And it will be on that day, when God gives you rest from your toil, and from your turmoil, and from the harsh servitude in which you previously served,

**14:4** you will take up this parable against the king of Babylon, and you will say: "How has the oppressor ceased; how has tribute come to rest?

**14:5** The Lord has shattered the staff of the wicked, the rod of the rulers,

**14:6** striking the peoples in indignation with an incurable blow, subduing the nations in fury, persecuting cruelly.

**14:7** The whole earth has come to rest and grown silent; it has rejoiced and exulted;

**14:8** the fir trees also have been glad over you, and the cedars of Lebanon: 'Since you fell asleep, no one will come up to cut us down.'

**14:9** The underworld below has been thrown into turmoil to meet your coming; it has roused the giants for you. All the princes of the earth have risen from their thrones, all the princes of the nations.

**14:10** All of them will answer and say to you: 'You too have been wounded just like us; you have become like us.'

**14:11** Your pride has been dragged down to the underworld, your corpse has fallen; beneath you the moth will be spread out, and worms will be your covering.

**14:12** How have you fallen from heaven, Lucifer, who rose in the morning? Have you crashed down to the earth, you who wounded the nations?

**14:13** You who said in your heart: 'I will ascend into heaven, above the stars of God I will exalt my throne; I will sit on the mountain of the covenant, on the sides of the north;

**14:14** I will ascend above the height of the clouds, I will be like the Most High'?

**14:15** Yet you will be dragged down to the underworld, into the depth of the pit.

**14:16** Those who see you will lean toward you and look closely at you: 'Is this really the man who threw the earth into turmoil, who shook kingdoms,

**14:17** who made the world a desert and destroyed its cities, who did not open the prison for his captives?'

**14:18** All the kings of the nations, every one of them, have slept in glory, each man in his own house;

**14:19** but you have been cast out from your tomb like a useless shoot, defiled and wrapped up with those who were killed by the sword and went down to the foundations of the pit, like a rotting corpse.

**14:20** You will not have fellowship, not even with them in burial; for you have destroyed your own land, you have killed your own people; the seed of the most wicked will not be named forever.

**14:21** Prepare his sons for slaughter because of the iniquity of their fathers; they will not rise up, nor inherit the earth, nor fill the face of the world with cities."

**14:22** "And I will rise up against them," says the Lord of hosts, "and I will destroy the name of Babylon, and the remnant, and shoot, and posterity," says the Lord,

**14:23** "and I will turn it into a possession of the hedgehog, and into marshes of waters, and I will sweep it with a broom, grinding it down," says the Lord of hosts.

**14:24** The Lord of hosts has sworn, saying: "Surely, just as I have thought, so it will be; and as I have considered in mind,

**14:25** so it will come about: that I will crush the Assyrian in My land, and on My mountains I will trample him; and his yoke will be taken away from them, and his burden will be lifted from their shoulder.

**14:26** This is the plan that I have devised over the whole earth, and this is the hand stretched out over all the nations."

**14:27** For the Lord of hosts has decreed, and who will be able to nullify it? And His hand is stretched out, and who will turn it back?

**14:28** In the year in which King Ahaz died, this burden came:

**14:29** Do not rejoice, all of you, Philistia, because the rod of the one who struck you has been broken to pieces; for from the root of the serpent a basilisk will come out, and its seed will swallow up the bird.

**14:30** And the firstborn of the poor will feed, and the poor will rest confidently; and I will make your root perish in famine, and I will kill your remnant.

**14:31** Wail, gate; cry out, city; all Philistia has been laid prostrate; for smoke will come from the north, and there is no one who will escape its column.

**14:32** And what will be answered to the messengers of the nation? That the Lord has founded Zion, and in Him the poor of His people will hope.

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — In the Clementine Vulgate, the opening sentence ("Its time is near to come, and its days will not be prolonged") begins chapter 14 rather than concluding 13:22 as in the Hebrew text.
- **14:9** — Latin Infernus (and inferos in 14:11, infernum in 14:15) literally denotes the lower realm or underworld (translating Hebrew Sheol); Vulgate gigantes ("giants," following the Septuagint) corresponds to Hebrew repha'im ("shades/departed spirits").
- **14:11** — Vulgate reads concidit cadaver tuum ("your corpse has fallen") and tinea ("moth"), diverging from the Hebrew "the sound of your harps" and "maggots."
- **14:12** — Latin Lucifer literally means "light-bearer" (the morning star), corresponding to Hebrew helel ("shining one"), with qui mane oriebaris ("who rose in the morning") corresponding to "son of the dawn." In 14:13, Vulgate monte testamenti ("mountain of the covenant") corresponds to Hebrew "mount of assembly."
- **14:29** — Vulgate regulus denotes the basilisk (a venomous serpent), and absorbens volucrem ("will swallow up the bird") is a distinctly Vulgate reading diverging from the Hebrew "flying fiery serpent."
