## Text

**11:1** And a shoot will go forth from the root of Jesse, and a flower will rise from his root.

**11:2** And the Spirit of the Lord will rest upon Him: the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and strength, the spirit of knowledge and piety,

**11:3** and the spirit of the fear of the Lord will fill Him. He will not judge according to the sight of the eyes, nor will He reprove according to the hearing of the ears;

**11:4** but He will judge the poor in righteousness, and will reprove in equity on behalf of the meek of the earth; and He will strike the earth with the rod of His mouth, and with the breath of His lips He will kill the wicked one.

**11:5** And righteousness will be the belt of His loins, and faithfulness the sash of His waist.

**11:6** The wolf will dwell with the lamb, and the leopard will lie down with the young goat; the calf, and the lion, and the sheep will stay together, and a little boy will lead them.

**11:7** The calf and the bear will graze; their young will rest together; and the lion will eat straw like the ox.

**11:8** And the nursing infant will delight itself over the hole of the asp; and the one who has been weaned will put his hand into the den of the basilisk.

**11:9** They will not harm, and they will not kill, in all My holy mountain, for the earth has been filled with the knowledge of the Lord, like the covering waters of the sea.

**11:10** In that day the root of Jesse, who stands as a sign for the peoples: Him the nations will implore, and His tomb will be glorious.

**11:11** And it will come to pass in that day: the Lord will extend His hand a second time to take possession of the remainder of His people, which will be left from the Assyrians, and from Egypt, and from Pathros, and from Ethiopia, and from Elam, and from Shinar, and from Hamath, and from the islands of the sea.

**11:12** And He will lift up a sign to the nations, and will gather the fugitives of Israel, and will assemble the dispersed of Judah from the four quarters of the earth.

**11:13** And the jealousy of Ephraim will be taken away, and the adversaries of Judah will perish; Ephraim will not envy Judah, and Judah will not fight against Ephraim.

**11:14** And they will fly upon the shoulders of the Philistines by the sea; together they will plunder the sons of the east; Idumea and Moab will be at the command of their hand, and the sons of Ammon will be obedient.

**11:15** And the Lord will lay waste the tongue of the sea of Egypt, and He will lift up His hand over the river in the strength of His Spirit; and He will strike it into seven streams, so that they may cross through it with shoes on.

**11:16** And there will be a way for My remaining people who will be left from the Assyrians, just as there was for Israel in that day when they went up from the land of Egypt.

## Translator Notes

- **11:2** — Together with 11:3, the Vulgate distinguishes 'pietatis' ("piety") from 'timoris Domini' ("the fear of the Lord," which "will fill Him"), giving seven distinct terms where the Hebrew repeats "fear of the Lord." Capitalization of 'Him' (referring to the shoot from the root of Jesse in 11:1) is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **11:3** — Capitalization of 'Him'/'He' (referring to the shoot of Jesse) is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **11:4** — Capitalization of 'He'/'His' (referring to the shoot of Jesse) is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **11:5** — Capitalization of 'His' (referring to the shoot of Jesse) is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **11:6** — Latin reads 'ovis' ("sheep") in 11:6 and 'vitulus' ("calf") in 11:7 where the Hebrew has "fatling" and "cow"; in 11:8 'reguli' designates the basilisk.
- **11:10** — Latin 'sepulchrum ejus' ("His tomb") is a Vulgate-distinct reading where the Hebrew text has "his resting place." Capitalization of 'Him'/'His' (referring to the root of Jesse) is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **11:14** — Latin uses 'Idumaea' (Idumea), the Greco-Latin name for Edom, and renders the Hebrew "westward" literally as 'per mare' ("by the sea").
