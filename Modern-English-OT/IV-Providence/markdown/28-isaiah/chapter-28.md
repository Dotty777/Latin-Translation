## Text

**28:1** Woe to the crown of pride, to the drunkards of Ephraim, and to the falling flower, the glory of its exultation, who were on the summit of the richest valley, going astray from wine.

**28:2** Look, the Lord is mighty and strong, like an onslaught of hail, a shattering whirlwind, like an onslaught of many waters overflowing and sent forth over a wide land.

**28:3** With feet the crown of pride of the drunkards of Ephraim will be trampled down.

**28:4** And the falling flower of the glory of its exultation, which is on the summit of the valley of the rich, will be like an early fruit before the ripeness of autumn, which, when an onlooker has gazed upon it, as soon as he holds it in his hand, he will devour it.

**28:5** In that day the Lord of hosts will be a crown of glory and a garland of exultation to the remnant of His people;

**28:6** and a spirit of judgment to the one sitting over judgment, and strength to those returning from war to the gate.

**28:7** But these also knew nothing because of wine, and went astray because of drunkenness; priest and prophet knew nothing because of drunkenness; they were swallowed up by wine, they went astray in drunkenness, they did not know the seer, they were ignorant of judgment.

**28:8** For all tables were filled with vomit and filth, so that there was no longer any place.

**28:9** Whom will He teach knowledge? And whom will He make to understand what is heard? Those weaned from milk, pulled away from the breasts.

**28:10** For command, command again; command, command again; wait, wait again; wait, wait again; a little there, a little there.

**28:11** For with speech of the lip and with another tongue He will speak to this people.

**28:12** To whom He said, "This is My rest; refresh the weary; and this is My refreshment," and they were unwilling to listen.

**28:13** And the word of the Lord will be to them: "Command, command again; command, command again; wait, wait again; wait, wait again; a little there, a little there," so that they may go, and fall backward, and be crushed, and be ensnared, and be captured.

**28:14** Because of this, hear the word of the Lord, mocking men, who rule over My people who are in Jerusalem.

**28:15** For you have said, "We have struck a covenant with death, and with hell we have made a pact; when the overflowing scourge passes through, it will not come upon us, because we have set falsehood as our hope, and by falsehood we have been protected."

**28:16** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: "Look, I will place in the foundations of Zion a stone, a tested stone, a cornerstone, precious, founded in the foundation; let the one who believes not hurry.

**28:17** And I will set judgment by weight, and justice by measure; and hail will overturn the hope of falsehood, and waters will overflow the protection.

**28:18** And your covenant with death will be blotted out, and your pact with hell will not stand; when the overflowing scourge passes through, you will be to it for trampling.

**28:19** Whenever it passes through, it will carry you away, for in the morning at daybreak it will pass through, by day and by night; and only vexation alone will give understanding to what is heard.

**28:20** For the bed is made so narrow that one falls off, and the short cloak cannot cover both."

**28:21** For just as on the mountain of divisions the Lord will stand up, just as in the valley that is in Gibeon He will be angry, so that He may do His work, strange is His work; so that He may perform His work, foreign is His work from Him.

**28:22** And now do not mock, so that your bonds may not perhaps be tightened; for I have heard of a completion and a shortening from the Lord God of hosts, upon the whole earth.

**28:23** Give ear, and hear my voice; pay attention, and hear my speech.

**28:24** Will the one plowing plow all day in order to sow? Will he break up and hoe his soil?

**28:25** When he has leveled its surface, will he not sow black cumin and scatter cumin, and set wheat in rows, and barley, and millet, and vetch within its borders?

**28:26** And He will instruct him in judgment; his God will teach him.

**28:27** For black cumin will not be threshed with saws, nor will a wagon wheel go around over cumin; rather, black cumin will be beaten out with a rod, and cumin with a staff.

**28:28** Bread, however, will be crushed; yet the one threshing will not thresh it forever, nor bruise it with a wagon wheel, nor crush it with his hooves.

**28:29** And this has gone forth from the Lord God of hosts, so that He might make counsel wonderful and magnify justice.

## Translator Notes

- **28:2** — Vulgate makes Dominus the nominative subject ("the Lord is mighty and strong"), whereas the Hebrew describes a mighty and strong agent belonging to the Lord.
- **28:10** — Vulgate renders the Hebrew rhythmic repetition (tsav latsav ... qav laqav) with paired Latin imperatives (manda, remanda; exspecta, reexspecta) and translates both final adverbs identically as ibi ("there").
- **28:20** — Vulgate presents the proverb as a bed too narrow for two so that one falls off and a short cloak unable to cover both (alter ... utrumque), differing from the Hebrew image of one person unable to stretch out or wrap up.
- **28:21** — Vulgate translates the Hebrew place name Har Perazim ("Mount Perazim") literally into Latin as in monte divisionum ("on the mountain of divisions").
- **28:28** — Vulgate ungulis suis (literally "with his hooves") renders Hebrew parashaw ("his horses"), referring to the hooves of the farmer's threshing animals.
