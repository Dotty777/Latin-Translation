## Text

**25:1** Lord, You are my God; I will exalt You, and I will give thanks to Your name: for You have done wondrous things, faithful plans of old. Amen.

**25:2** Because You have turned the city into a heap, the strong city into a ruin, the house of foreigners, so that it is not a city and will not be built forever.

**25:3** For this reason a strong people will praise You; the city of mighty nations will fear You:

**25:4** because You have become strength to the poor, strength to the needy in his distress, a hope from the whirlwind, a shade from the heat; for the breath of the mighty is like a whirlwind driving against a wall.

**25:5** Like heat in drought, You will humble the uproar of foreigners; and as with heat under a scorching cloud, You will make the offshoot of the strong wither.

**25:6** And the Lord of hosts will make for all peoples on this mountain a banquet of rich foods, a banquet of vintage, of rich foods full of marrow, of vintage cleared of dregs.

**25:7** And on this mountain He will cast down headlong the face of the bond bound over all peoples, and the web which He has woven over all nations.

**25:8** He will cast down death headlong forever; and the Lord God will take away the tear from every face, and He will take away the reproach of His people from the whole earth: for the Lord has spoken.

**25:9** And one will say on that day, "Look, this is our God; we have waited for Him, and He will save us; this is the Lord, we have waited patiently for Him: we will exult and be glad in His salvation."

**25:10** For the hand of the Lord will rest on this mountain; and Moab will be threshed beneath Him, just as straw is crushed in a wagon.

**25:11** And he will stretch out his hands beneath Him just as one swimming stretches out to swim; and He will humble his glory with the dashing of his hands.

**25:12** And the fortifications of your lofty walls will collapse, and will be humbled, and will be pulled down to the earth, all the way to the dust.

## Translator Notes

- **25:1** — Vulgate closes the verse with the transliteration "Amen" (Amen), rendering the Hebrew noun meaning "firmness" or "truth."
- **25:4** — Vulgate reads "a hope from the whirlwind" (spes a turbine) where the Hebrew has "a refuge from the storm"; in 25:5 Vulgate likewise has "under a scorching cloud" (sub nube torrente) and "offshoot" (propaginem) for Hebrew "by the shadow of a cloud" and "song."
- **25:7** — Vulgate renders Hebrew "will swallow up" as "will cast down headlong" (praecipitabit, also in 25:8) and uses the active clause "which He has woven" (quam orditus est) where the Hebrew has a passive participle ("which is woven").
- **25:10** — Vulgate reads "beneath Him" (sub eo, repeated in 25:11, referring to the Lord) and "in a wagon" (in plaustro), whereas the Hebrew reads "in its place" and "in the water of a dung-pit."
