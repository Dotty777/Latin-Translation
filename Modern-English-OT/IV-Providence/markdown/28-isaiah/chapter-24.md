## Text

**24:1** Look, the Lord will devastate the earth, and will strip it bare, and will afflict its face, and will scatter its inhabitants.

**24:2** And it will be: as the people, so the priest; and as the servant, so his master; as the maidservant, so her mistress; as the buyer, so the one who sells; as the lender, so the one who takes a loan; as the one who demands repayment, so the one who owes.

**24:3** With devastation the earth will be devastated, and with plundering it will be despoiled; for the Lord has spoken this word.

**24:4** The earth has mourned and wasted away, and has grown weak; the world has wasted away, the loftiness of the people of the earth has grown weak.

**24:5** And the earth has been defiled by its inhabitants, because they have transgressed the laws, have altered the ordinance, have broken the everlasting covenant.

**24:6** Because of this a curse will devour the earth, and its inhabitants will sin; and therefore its cultivators will go mad, and few people will be left.

**24:7** The vintage has mourned, the vine has grown weak, all who were rejoicing in heart have groaned;

**24:8** the joy of tambourines has ceased, the sound of those rejoicing has grown quiet, the sweetness of the harp has fallen silent.

**24:9** With a song they will not drink wine; the drink will be bitter to those drinking it.

**24:10** The city of emptiness has been shattered, every house has been shut, with no one entering.

**24:11** There will be an outcry over wine in the streets; all gladness has been deserted, the joy of the earth has been carried away.

**24:12** Desolation has been left in the city, and calamity will crush the gates.

**24:13** For these things will be in the midst of the earth, in the midst of the peoples, just as if a few olives that have remained are shaken from an olive tree, and grape clusters when the vintage has been finished.

**24:14** These will lift up their voice and will praise; when the Lord has been glorified, they will shout aloud from the sea.

**24:15** Because of this, in teachings glorify the Lord; in the islands of the sea, the name of the Lord, the God of Israel.

**24:16** From the ends of the earth we have heard praises, the glory of the Righteous One. And I said, "My secret to me, my secret to me. Woe to me! The treacherous have acted treacherously, and with the treachery of transgressors they have acted treacherously."

**24:17** Terror, and pit, and snare are upon you, you who are an inhabitant of the earth.

**24:18** And it will be: the one who flees from the sound of terror will fall into the pit, and the one who extricates himself from the pit will be caught by the snare; because the floodgates from on high have been opened, and the foundations of the earth will be violently shaken.

**24:19** With shattering the earth will be shattered, with crushing the earth will be crushed, with shaking the earth will be shaken;

**24:20** with swaying the earth will be swayed like a drunkard, and it will be carried off like a tent of a single night; and its iniquity will weigh it down, and it will collapse, and it will not rise again.

**24:21** And it will be: on that day the Lord will make a visitation upon the host of heaven on high, and upon the kings of the earth who are upon the earth;

**24:22** and they will be gathered together in the gathering of a single bundle into a pit, and they will be shut up there in prison, and after many days they will be visited.

**24:23** And the moon will blush, and the sun will be put to shame, when the Lord of hosts reigns on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, and is glorified in the sight of His elders.

## Translator Notes

- **24:6** — Vulgate reads "will sin" (peccabunt) and "will go mad" (insanient), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text has "bear guilt" and "are scorched."
- **24:14** — Vulgate has the temporal clause "when the Lord has been glorified" (cum glorificatus fuerit Dominus) and hinnient (literally "will neigh," Jerome's literal rendering of the Hebrew verb used both for neighing and for a ringing cry).
- **24:15** — Vulgate reads "in teachings" (in doctrinis), taking the Hebrew term meaning "in the lights" (understood of the regions of light, the east) in the sense of illuminations or doctrines.
- **24:16** — Vulgate reads "My secret to me" (Secretum meum mihi), taking the Hebrew razi-li in the sense of "secret" or "mystery" rather than "leanness" ("I waste away"). Clem1598-USFX prints capitalized Justi ('of the Righteous One'); capitalization is subject to edition-wide policy.
- **24:22** — Vulgate reads "of a single bundle" (unius fascis) where the Hebrew reads "of a prisoner"; similarly in 24:20 Vulgate has "carried off like a tent of a single night" (auferetur quasi tabernaculum unius noctis) for Hebrew "sways like a hut."
