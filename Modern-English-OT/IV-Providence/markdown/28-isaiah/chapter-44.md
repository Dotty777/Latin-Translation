## Text

**44:1** "And now hear, Jacob, My servant, and Israel, whom I have chosen."

**44:2** This is what the Lord, who makes and forms you, your helper from the womb, says: "Do not be afraid, My servant Jacob, and most upright one, whom I have chosen.

**44:3** For I will pour out waters upon the thirsty one, and streams upon the dry ground; I will pour out My Spirit upon your seed, and My blessing upon your offspring;

**44:4** and they will sprout among the grasses, like willows beside waters flowing past.

**44:5** This one will say, 'I belong to the Lord,' and that one will call by the name of Jacob; and another will write with his hand, 'To the Lord,' and will be likened in the name of Israel."

**44:6** This is what the Lord, the King of Israel and his Redeemer, the Lord of hosts, says: "I am the first, and I am the last, and apart from Me there is no god.

**44:7** Who is like Me? Let him call out and declare it, and let him lay out the order to Me from the time I established the ancient people; let them declare to them the things that are coming and the things that are to be.

**44:8** Do not be afraid, and do not be troubled: from that time I made you hear and I declared it; you are My witnesses. Is there a god apart from Me, or a fashioner whom I do not know?

**44:9** The shapers of an idol are all nothing, and their most cherished things will not profit them. They themselves are their witnesses, because they do not see and do not understand, so that they may be put to shame.

**44:10** Who has formed a god and cast a carved image useful for nothing?

**44:11** Look, all its associates will be put to shame, for the craftsmen are from among men; they will all assemble, they will stand and be terrified, and they will be put to shame together.

**44:12** The ironworker worked with a file; in glowing coals and with hammers he formed it, and he worked with the arm of his strength; he will grow hungry and lose strength, he will not drink water and will grow weary.

**44:13** The woodworker stretched out a rule, formed it with a plane, made it with squares, and rounded it with a compass, and made the image of a man, like a handsome human being dwelling in a house;

**44:14** he cut down cedars, he took a holm oak and an oak that had stood among the trees of the forest; he planted a pine tree, which the rain nourished:

**44:15** and it became a fire for men; he took from them and grew warm, and he kindled a fire and baked loaves; but from the rest he crafted a god and worshiped it; he made a carved image and bowed down before it.

**44:16** Half of it he burned in the fire, and with half of it he ate meat; he cooked a stew and was satisfied, and he grew warm and said, 'Aha! I have grown warm; I have seen the fire!'

**44:17** but the rest of it he made into a god and a carved image for himself; he bows down before it, and worships it, and pleads, saying, 'Deliver me, because you are my god!'

**44:18** They have not known, nor have they understood; for they have been plastered over so that their eyes may not see, and so that they may not understand with their heart.

**44:19** They do not reflect in their mind, nor do they know, nor do they perceive, so as to say, 'Half of it I burned in the fire, and I baked loaves over its coals; I cooked meat and ate it, and from the rest of it shall I make an idol? Shall I fall down before a block of wood?'

**44:20** Part of it is ashes; a foolish heart has worshiped it, and he will not deliver his own soul, nor will he say, 'Perhaps there is a lie in my right hand.'

**44:21** Remember these things, Jacob, and Israel, because you are My servant. I have formed you; you are My servant, Israel; do not forget Me.

**44:22** I have blotted out your iniquities like a cloud, and your sins like a mist; return to Me, because I have redeemed you."

**44:23** Give praise, heavens, because the Lord has shown mercy; shout for joy, ends of the earth; resound with praise, mountains, forest and every tree in it, because the Lord has redeemed Jacob, and Israel will glory.

**44:24** This is what the Lord, your Redeemer and your Fashioner from the womb, says: "I am the Lord, making all things, stretching out the heavens alone, establishing the earth, and there is no one with Me;

**44:25** making void the signs of diviners, and turning soothsayers into madness; turning the wise backward, and making their knowledge foolish;

**44:26** raising up the word of His servant, and fulfilling the counsel of His messengers; I who say to Jerusalem, 'You will be inhabited,' and to the cities of Judah, 'You will be built,' and I will raise up her waste places;

**44:27** who say to the deep, 'Be made desolate, and I will dry up your rivers';

**44:28** who say to Cyrus, 'You are My shepherd, and you will fulfill all My will'; who say to Jerusalem, 'You will be built,' and to the temple, 'You will be founded.'"

## Translator Notes

- **44:2** — Latin rectissime ("most upright one") translates the Hebrew poetic name Jeshurun by its meaning rather than transliterating it, and the Vulgate punctuates ab utero ("from the womb") with auxiliator tuus ("your helper").
- **44:8** — Latin formator ("fashioner") interprets the underlying Hebrew consonants in the sense of forming or shaping, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "rock."
- **44:18** — Latin obliti sunt is the passive of oblino ("to plaster or smear over"), not obliviscor ("to forget"), portraying the idolaters as coated over so that their eyes cannot see.
- **44:20** — The Vulgate reads Pars ejus cinis est; cor insipiens adoravit illud ("Part of it is ashes; a foolish heart has worshiped it") and Forte ("Perhaps"), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("He feeds on ashes; a deluded heart has led him astray ... Is there not...?").
- **44:21** — The Vulgate reads an active warning ne obliviscaris mei ("do not forget Me"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads a passive promise ("you will not be forgotten by Me").
