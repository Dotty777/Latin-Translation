## Text

**60:1** Rise up, be illumined, Jerusalem, because your light has come, and the glory of the Lord has risen upon you.

**60:2** For look, darkness will cover the earth, and gloom the peoples; but upon you the Lord will rise, and His glory will be seen in you.

**60:3** And nations will walk in your light, and kings in the brightness of your rising.

**60:4** Lift up your eyes all around and see: all these have been gathered together, they have come to you; your sons will come from afar, and your daughters will rise from the side.

**60:5** Then you will see and overflow; your heart will marvel and be enlarged, when the multitude of the sea is turned to you, when the strength of the nations comes to you.

**60:6** A flood of camels will cover you, the dromedaries of Midian and Ephah; all from Sheba will come, bringing gold and frankincense, and announcing praise to the Lord.

**60:7** All the flock of Kedar will be gathered to you; the rams of Nebaioth will minister to you; they will be offered upon My acceptable altar, and I will glorify the house of My majesty.

**60:8** Who are these who fly like clouds, and like doves to their windows?

**60:9** For the islands wait for Me, and the ships of the sea at the first, so that I may bring your sons from afar, their silver and their gold with them, for the name of the Lord your God, and for the Holy One of Israel, because He has glorified you.

**60:10** And the sons of foreigners will build your walls, and their kings will minister to you; for in My indignation I struck you, and in My reconciliation I have had mercy on you.

**60:11** And your gates will be opened continually; day and night they will not be shut, so that the strength of the nations may be brought to you, and their kings may be led in.

**60:12** For the nation and the kingdom that will not serve you will perish, and the nations will be laid waste with desolation.

**60:13** The glory of Lebanon will come to you, the fir, and the box tree, and the pine together, to adorn the place of My sanctuary; and I will glorify the place of My feet.

**60:14** And the sons of those who humbled you will come to you bowed down, and all who spoke against you will bow down to the footprints of your feet; and they will call you the City of the Lord, Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

**60:15** Because you have been abandoned and hated, and there was no one who passed through you, I will make you the pride of the ages, a joy for generation and generation;

**60:16** and you will nurse from the milk of the nations, and be suckled at the breast of kings; and you will know that I am the Lord who saves you, and your Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.

**60:17** Instead of bronze I will bring gold, and instead of iron I will bring silver, and instead of wood, bronze, and instead of stones, iron; and I will make your visitation peace, and your overseers righteousness.

**60:18** Iniquity will no longer be heard in your land, nor devastation and destruction within your borders; and salvation will occupy your walls, and praise your gates.

**60:19** You will no longer have the sun to shine by day, nor will the brightness of the moon illumine you; but the Lord will be an everlasting light for you, and your God your glory.

**60:20** Your sun will no longer set, and your moon will not be diminished, because the Lord will be an everlasting light for you, and the days of your mourning will be completed.

**60:21** And your people will all be righteous; they will inherit the land forever, the shoot of My planting, the work of My hand, for glorifying.

**60:22** The least will become a thousand, and the little one a most mighty nation. I, the Lord, will suddenly do this in its time.

## Translator Notes

- **60:1** — Vulgate 'illuminare' is a passive imperative ("be illumined"), differing from the active imperative ("shine") in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **60:4** — Vulgate 'de latere surgent' ("will rise from the side") diverges from the Hebrew, which describes daughters being carried on the hip or side.
- **60:9** — Vulgate 'naves maris' ("ships of the sea") follows Jerome's frequent rendering of "Tarshish" as "the sea" in Isaiah, and uses the first-person verb 'adducam' ("I may bring").
- **60:17** — Vulgate 'visitationem tuam' ("your visitation," i.e., oversight) translates the Hebrew noun commonly rendered "your overseers" or "your governance."
- **60:18** — Vulgate 'occupabit salus muros tuos' ("salvation will occupy your walls") differs from the Hebrew, which reads "you shall call your walls Salvation."
