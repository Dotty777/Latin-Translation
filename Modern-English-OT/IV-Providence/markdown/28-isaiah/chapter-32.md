## Text

**32:1** Look, a king will reign in righteousness, and princes will preside in judgment.

**32:2** And a man will be like one who is hidden from the wind and conceals himself from a storm, like streams of waters in thirst, and the shadow of a jutting rock in a desert land.

**32:3** The eyes of those who see will not grow dim, and the ears of those who hear will listen carefully.

**32:4** And the heart of fools will understand knowledge, and the tongue of stammerers will speak swiftly and plainly.

**32:5** The one who is senseless will no longer be called a prince, nor will the deceitful man be called greater;

**32:6** for the fool will speak foolish things, and his heart will commit iniquity, to carry out hypocrisy, and to speak deceitfully to the Lord, and to make empty the soul of the hungry, and to take away drink from the thirsty.

**32:7** The instruments of the deceitful man are most wicked; for he himself has contrived designs to destroy the meek with speech of falsehood, when the poor man spoke judgment.

**32:8** But the prince will devise the things that are worthy of a prince, and he himself will stand over leaders.

**32:9** Wealthy women, rise up and hear my voice; confident daughters, receive my speech with your ears.

**32:10** For after days and a year, you will be troubled, confident ones; for the grape harvest is finished, the gathering will no longer come.

**32:11** Be astonished, wealthy ones; be troubled, confident ones; strip yourselves and be put to shame; gird your loins.

**32:12** Lament upon your breasts, over the desirable region, over the fruitful vineyard.

**32:13** Upon the soil of my people thorns and briers will come up; how much more upon all the houses of joy of the exulting city!

**32:14** For the house has been abandoned, the multitude of the city has been deserted, darkness and groping have come upon the caves forever, a joy of wild donkeys, a pasture of flocks.

**32:15** Until the Spirit is poured out upon us from on high, and the desert will become Carmel, and Carmel will be reckoned as a forest.

**32:16** And judgment will dwell in the wilderness, and righteousness will sit in Carmel.

**32:17** And the work of righteousness will be peace, and the service of righteousness will be silence and security forever.

**32:18** And my people will sit in the beauty of peace, and in tents of confidence, and in opulent rest.

**32:19** But hail will be in the coming down of the forest, and in humiliation the city will be humiliated.

**32:20** Blessed are you who sow upon all waters, sending in the foot of the ox and the donkey.

## Translator Notes

- **32:8** — Vulgate super duces ("over leaders") takes Hebrew nedivot ("noble things") as a personal plural denoting nobles or rulers.
- **32:14** — Vulgate tenebrae et palpatio ("darkness and groping") interprets Hebrew ophel wa-bachan ("hill/citadel and watchtower") through roots meaning "darkness" and "testing/feeling."
- **32:15** — The Vulgate retains the Hebrew word carmel untranslated here and in verse 16; besides referring to Mount Carmel, the term means a fertile orchard or garden land. Latin has bare spiritus (without Dei or Domini), which can be rendered 'the Spirit' or 'a spirit' (or 'wind'); capitalization is subject to edition-wide editorial policy.
- **32:18** — Vulgate in pulchritudine pacis ("in the beauty of peace") derives Hebrew naweh ("dwelling/abode") from the root meaning "to be beautiful."
