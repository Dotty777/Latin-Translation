## Text

**62:1** For the sake of Zion I will not keep silent, and for the sake of Jerusalem I will not rest, until her righteous one goes forth like brightness, and her savior is kindled like a torch.

**62:2** And the nations will see your righteous one, and all the kings your renowned one; and a new name will be called for you, which the mouth of the Lord will name.

**62:3** And you will be a crown of glory in the hand of the Lord, and a diadem of the kingdom in the hand of your God.

**62:4** You will no longer be called "Abandoned," and your land will no more be called "Desolate"; but you will be called "My Will Is in Her," and your land "Inhabited," because the Lord has been well pleased in you, and your land will be inhabited.

**62:5** For a young man will dwell with a virgin, and your sons will dwell in you; and a bridegroom will rejoice over a bride, and your God will rejoice over you.

**62:6** Upon your walls, Jerusalem, I have appointed watchmen; all day and all night perpetually they will not keep silent. You who remember the Lord, do not keep silent,

**62:7** and give Him no silence, until He establishes and until He makes Jerusalem a praise on the earth.

**62:8** The Lord has sworn by His right hand and by the arm of His strength: "Never again will I give your wheat as food to your enemies, and foreign sons will not drink your wine for which you have labored;

**62:9** for those who gather it will eat and praise the Lord, and those who carry it in will drink in My holy courts."

**62:10** Pass through, pass through the gates, prepare the way for the people; make the road level, pick out the stones, and lift up a standard to the peoples.

**62:11** Look, the Lord has made it heard at the ends of the earth: "Say to the daughter of Zion, 'Look, your Savior comes; look, His reward is with Him, and His work is before Him.'"

**62:12** And they will call them "Holy People, Redeemed by the Lord"; but you will be called "Sought-Out City," and not "Abandoned."

## Translator Notes

- **62:1** — Instead of the abstract Hebrew nouns "her righteousness" and "her salvation," Vulgate uses personal masculine nouns here ("her righteous one," "her savior") and in 62:2 ("your righteous one," "your renowned one"), and uses the passive verb "is kindled" (accendatur).
- **62:4** — Vulgate translates rather than transliterates the Hebrew symbolic names, rendering Hephzibah ("My delight is in her") as "My Will Is in Her" (Voluntas mea in ea) and Beulah ("Married") as "Inhabited" (Inhabitata), continuing with "will dwell" (habitabit/habitabunt) rather than "marry" in 62:5.
- **62:8** — Latin preserves the elliptical biblical oath formula literally ("If I again give your wheat... and if foreign sons drink your wine..."), which expresses an emphatic negation ("Never again will I give...").
- **62:12** — Following the Clementine Vulgate punctuation (Quaesita civitas, et non Derelicta), "City" is grouped with "Sought-Out" rather than with "Not Abandoned" as in the Hebrew text.
