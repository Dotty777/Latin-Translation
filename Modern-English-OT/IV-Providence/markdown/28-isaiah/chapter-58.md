## Text

**58:1** Cry out, do not cease; like a trumpet, raise your voice high, and announce to My people their crimes, and to the house of Jacob their sins.

**58:2** For indeed they seek Me from day to day, and they desire to know My ways, like a nation that has practiced righteousness and has not abandoned the judgment of its God. They ask Me for judgments of righteousness; they desire to draw near to God.

**58:3** "Why have we fasted, and You did not look? We have humbled our souls, and You did not know?" Look, on the day of your fast your will is found, and you demand payment from all your debtors.

**58:4** Look, you fast for disputes and contentions, and you strike wickedly with the fist. Do not fast as you have up to this day, so that your outcry may be heard on high.

**58:5** Is such the fast that I have chosen, for a man to afflict his soul for a day? Is it to twist his head like a circle, and to spread out sackcloth and ashes? Will you call this a fast, and a day acceptable to the Lord?

**58:6** Is not this rather the fast that I have chosen? Loosen the bonds of wickedness, untie the oppressive bundles, let those who are crushed go free, and break apart every burden;

**58:7** break your bread for the hungry, and bring the needy and wandering into your house; when you see someone naked, cover him, and do not despise your flesh.

**58:8** Then your light will break forth like the morning, and your healing will swiftly rise; and your righteousness will go before your face, and the glory of the Lord will gather you.

**58:9** Then you will call, and the Lord will hear; you will cry out, and He will say, "Here I am." If you remove the chain from your midst, and cease stretching out the finger and speaking what does not profit,

**58:10** when you pour out your soul to the hungry and fill the afflicted soul, your light will rise in the darkness, and your darkness will be like noon.

**58:11** And the Lord will give you rest always, and He will fill your soul with splendors, and He will deliver your bones; and you will be like a watered garden, and like a spring of waters whose waters will not fail.

**58:12** And the desolate places of the ages will be built in you; you will raise up the foundations of generation and generation; and you will be called a builder of hedges, turning paths into quietness.

**58:13** If you turn away your foot from the Sabbath, from doing your will on My holy day, and call the Sabbath delightful, and the holy day of the Lord glorious, and glorify Him while you do not follow your ways, and your will is not found so as to speak a word,

**58:14** then you will take delight in the Lord, and I will lift you up above the heights of the earth, and I will feed you with the inheritance of Jacob your father; for the mouth of the Lord has spoken.

## Translator Notes

- **58:3** — Vulgate 'omnes debitores vestros repetitis' ("you demand payment from all your debtors") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which refers to oppressing all your workers.
- **58:5** — Vulgate 'contorquere quasi circulum caput suum' ("twist his head like a circle") renders the Hebrew word for "reed" or "bulrush" as a circle or hoop.
- **58:11** — Vulgate contains three distinctive readings here: 'requiem tibi dabit' ("give you rest," vs. Hebrew "guide you"), 'splendoribus' ("with splendors," vs. Hebrew "in scorched places"), and 'liberabit' ("deliver," vs. Hebrew "strengthen").
- **58:12** — Vulgate 'ædificator sepium, avertens semitas in quietem' ("builder of hedges, turning paths into quietness") diverges from the Hebrew, which reads "repairer of the breach, restorer of streets to dwell in."
