## Text

**9:1** The people who were walking in darkness have seen a great light; for those living in the region of the shadow of death, light has risen upon them.

**9:2** You have multiplied the nation, and have not magnified the joy. They will rejoice before You, like those who rejoice at the harvest, as victors exult after plunder has been taken, when they divide the spoils.

**9:3** For You have overcome the yoke of its burden, and the rod of its shoulder, and the scepter of its taskmaster, as in the day of Midian.

**9:4** For every plundering by violence with tumult, and a garment mixed with blood, will be for burning, and food for fire.

**9:5** For a little child has been born to us, and a son has been given to us, and sovereignty has come upon His shoulder; and His name will be called Wonderful, Counselor, God, Mighty, Father of the Age to Come, Prince of Peace.

**9:6** His dominion will be multiplied, and of peace there will be no end; He will sit upon the throne of David and over his kingdom, to establish it and strengthen it in judgment and righteousness, from now on and forever. The zeal of the Lord of hosts will do this.

**9:7** The Lord sent a word into Jacob, and it fell upon Israel.

**9:8** And all the people of Ephraim will know, and the inhabitants of Samaria, saying in pride and greatness of heart:

**9:9** "The bricks have fallen, but we will build with squared stones; they have cut down the sycamores, but we will substitute cedars."

**9:10** And the Lord will raise up the adversaries of Rezin over him, and will turn his enemies into tumult:

**9:11** Syria from the east, and the Philistines from the west; and they will devour Israel with a full mouth. In all these things His fury has not been turned away, but His hand is still stretched out.

**9:12** And the people have not returned to the One striking them, and they have not sought the Lord of hosts.

**9:13** And the Lord will destroy from Israel head and tail, the one bowing down and the one restraining, in one day.

**9:14** The aged and honorable man, he is the head; and the prophet teaching falsehood, he is the tail.

**9:15** And those who call this people blessed will be deceivers, and those who are called blessed will be thrown headlong.

**9:16** Because of this the Lord will not rejoice over its young men, and He will not have mercy on its orphans and widows; for everyone is a hypocrite and wicked, and every mouth has spoken folly. In all these things His fury has not been turned away, but His hand is still stretched out.

**9:17** For ungodliness has been kindled like fire; it will devour the brier and the thorn, and will be kindled in the thickness of the forest, and the pride of smoke will roll upward.

**9:18** In the anger of the Lord of hosts the land has been thrown into turmoil, and the people will be like food for fire; a man will not spare his own brother.

**9:19** And he will turn aside to the right, and will be hungry; and he will eat on the left, and will not be satisfied; each one will devour the flesh of his own arm: Manasseh will devour Ephraim, and Ephraim Manasseh; together they themselves are against Judah.

**9:20** In all these things His fury has not been turned away, but His hand is still stretched out.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — Vulgate Isaiah 9:1-20 corresponds to 9:2-21 in the Hebrew Masoretic Text and most modern English versions, since Vulgate 8:23 corresponds to Hebrew 9:1.
- **9:2** — Latin 'et non magnificasti laetitiam' ("and have not magnified the joy") follows the Hebrew written text (Ketiv, "not") rather than the read tradition (Qere, "for it"); the Latin also specifies 'victores' ("victors").
- **9:5** — The Clementine Vulgate separates the first four titles with commas ('Admirabilis, Consiliarius, Deus, Fortis'), and 'Pater futuri saeculi' ("Father of the Age to Come") is a Vulgate-distinct rendering corresponding to Hebrew "Everlasting Father."
- **9:13** — Latin 'incurvantem et refrenantem' ("the one bowing down and the one restraining") is a distinctly Vulgate rendering where the Hebrew text has "palm branch and reed."
