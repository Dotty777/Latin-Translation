# Isaiah 49 — Saints Modern English (Latin / Clementine Vulgate)

## Text

**49:1** Hear, islands, and pay attention, peoples from afar: the Lord called me from the womb; from the belly of my mother He remembered my name.

**49:2** And He made my mouth like a sharp sword; in the shadow of His hand He protected me, and He made me like a chosen arrow; in His quiver He hid me.

**49:3** And He said to me, "You are My servant, Israel, for in you I will glory."

**49:4** And I said, "I have labored for nothing; without cause and in vain I have spent my strength; therefore my judgment is with the Lord, and my work is with my God."

**49:5** And now the Lord says, who forms me from the womb as a servant for Himself, so that I may bring Jacob back to Him, and Israel will not be gathered; and I have been glorified in the eyes of the Lord, and my God has become my strength.

**49:6** And He said, "It is too little that you should be a servant for Me to raise up the tribes of Jacob and to turn back the dregs of Israel; look, I have given you as a light of the nations, so that you may be My salvation all the way to the end of the earth."

**49:7** This is what the Lord, the Redeemer of Israel, its Holy One, says to the despised soul, to the abhorred nation, to the servant of masters: "Kings will see, and princes will rise up, and they will worship because of the Lord, for He is faithful, and the Holy One of Israel, who has chosen you."

**49:8** This is what the Lord says: "In a favorable time I have heard you, and in the day of salvation I have helped you; and I have preserved you, and I have given you as a covenant of the people, so that you might raise up the land and possess the devastated inheritances;

**49:9** so that you might say to those who are bound, 'Go out,' and to those who are in darkness, 'Be revealed.' Along the ways they will feed, and on all the plains will be their pastures.

**49:10** They will neither hunger nor thirst, and the heat and the sun will not strike them, for He who has mercy on them will guide them, and at the springs of waters He will give them drink.

**49:11** And I will make all My mountains into a way, and My paths will be raised up.

**49:12** Look, these will come from afar, and look, those from the north and the sea, and these from the southern land."

**49:13** Give praise, heavens, and exult, earth; shout praise joyfully, mountains, for the Lord has comforted His people, and He will have mercy on His poor.

**49:14** And Zion said, "The Lord has abandoned me, and the Lord has forgotten me."

**49:15** Can a woman forget her infant, so that she does not have mercy on the son of her womb? Even if she should forget, I still will not forget you.

**49:16** Look, on My hands I have inscribed you; your walls are before My eyes always.

**49:17** Your builders have come; those who destroy you and lay you waste will go out from you.

**49:18** Lift up your eyes all around and see: all these have gathered together; they have come to you. "As I live," says the Lord, "surely with all these you will be clothed as with an ornament, and you will put them around yourself like a bride;

**49:19** for your deserts, and your desolate places, and the land of your ruin will now be too narrow because of the inhabitants; and those who were swallowing you up will be driven far away."

**49:20** Still the children of your barrenness will say in your ears, "The place is too narrow for me; make room for me so that I may dwell."

**49:21** And you will say in your heart, "Who bore these for me? I was barren and not giving birth, exiled and captive; and who raised these? I was abandoned and alone; and where were these?"

**49:22** This is what the Lord God says: "Look, I will lift up My hand to the nations, and to the peoples I will raise up My standard; and they will bring your sons in their arms, and they will carry your daughters on their shoulders.

**49:23** And kings will be your foster fathers, and queens your nurses; with their faces lowered to the ground they will bow down before you, and they will lick the dust of your feet. And you will know that I am the Lord, on account of whom those who wait for Him will not be put to shame."

**49:24** Can prey be taken from the mighty? Or can what has been captured by the strong be saved?

**49:25** For this is what the Lord says: "Indeed, even the captives will be taken from the mighty, and what has been carried off by the strong will be saved. And those who have judged you I will judge, and your children I will save.

**49:26** And I will feed your enemies with their own flesh, and as with new wine, they will become drunk with their own blood; and all flesh will know that I am the Lord who saves you, and your Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob."

## Translator Notes

- **49:5** — Latin "et Israel non congregabitur" ("and Israel will not be gathered") follows the written Hebrew consonantal text (Ketiv, "not"), whereas most modern versions follow the marginal reading (Qere, "to Him": "that Israel might be gathered to Him").
- **49:6** — Latin "faeces Israel" literally reads "the dregs of Israel" (the remnant left at the bottom), differing from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "the preserved ones of Israel."
- **49:9** — Latin "in omnibus planis" ("on all the plains") differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text, which reads "on all the bare heights."
- **49:12** — Latin "de terra australi" ("from the southern land") translates geographically what the Hebrew Masoretic Text presents as a place name, "the land of Sinim."
- **49:17** — Latin "Venerunt structores tui" ("Your builders have come") reads the Hebrew consonants as "your builders" rather than the Masoretic Text's "your children," and renders the verb as "have come" rather than "hasten."
