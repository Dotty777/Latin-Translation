## Text

**42:1** Here is My servant, I will uphold him; My chosen one, My soul has delighted in him; I have put My Spirit upon him; he will bring forth judgment to the nations.

**42:2** He will not cry out, nor will he show partiality, nor will his voice be heard outside.

**42:3** A cracked reed he will not crush, and smoking flax he will not extinguish; in truth he will bring out judgment.

**42:4** He will not be sad nor turbulent, until he sets judgment on the earth; and the islands will wait for his law.

**42:5** This is what the Lord God says, who creates the heavens and stretches them out; who forms the earth and the things that sprout from it; who gives breath to the people who are on it, and spirit to those who tread on it:

**42:6** "I, the Lord, have called you in righteousness, and taken hold of your hand, and kept you; and I have given you as a covenant of the people, as a light of the nations,

**42:7** so that you might open the eyes of the blind, and bring out the prisoner from confinement, from the prison house those sitting in darkness.

**42:8** I am the Lord, this is My name; I will not give My glory to another, nor My praise to carved images.

**42:9** The things that were first, look, they have come; I also announce new things; before they arise, I will make them heard to you."

**42:10** Sing to the Lord a new song, His praise from the ends of the earth, you who go down to the sea, and its fullness; islands, and their inhabitants.

**42:11** Let the desert and its cities be lifted up. Kedar will dwell in houses; give praise, inhabitants of the rock; from the top of the mountains they will cry out.

**42:12** They will ascribe glory to the Lord, and they will declare His praise in the islands.

**42:13** The Lord will go out like a mighty one; like a man of war He will stir up zeal; He will shout aloud and cry out; over His enemies He will show Himself strong.

**42:14** I have always been silent, I have kept still, I have been patient; like a woman in labor I will speak; I will scatter and swallow up together.

**42:15** I will make the mountains and hills desolate, and I will dry up all their grass; and I will turn the rivers into islands, and dry out the pools.

**42:16** And I will lead the blind on a way that they do not know, and on paths that they have not known I will make them walk; I will turn darkness before them into light, and crooked places into straight; these words I have done for them, and I have not abandoned them.

**42:17** They have been turned backward, let them be confounded with confusion, those who trust in a carved image, who say to a molten image, "You are our gods."

**42:18** You deaf, hear, and you blind, look in order to see.

**42:19** Who is blind, except My servant, and deaf, except the one to whom I have sent My messengers? Who is blind, except the one who has been sold? And who is blind, except the servant of the Lord?

**42:20** You who see many things, will you not observe them? You who have open ears, will you not hear?

**42:21** And the Lord willed to sanctify him, and to magnify the law, and to exalt it.

**42:22** But this people itself is plundered and laid waste; all of them are a snare of young men, and in prison houses they have been hidden; they have been made into prey, and there is no one who rescues; into plunder, and there is no one who says, "Restore."

**42:23** Who is there among you who will hear this, pay attention, and listen to what is to come?

**42:24** Who gave Jacob up to plunder, and Israel to those who lay waste? Was it not the Lord Himself, against whom we have sinned? And they were unwilling to walk in His ways, and they did not listen to His law.

**42:25** And He poured out upon him the indignation of His fury and fierce war; and He burned him all around, and he did not know; and He set him on fire, and he did not understand.

## Translator Notes

- **42:2** — The Vulgate 'neque accipiet personam' (literally, 'nor will he accept a person,' the biblical idiom for showing partiality) diverges from the Hebrew 'nor lift up [his voice].'
- **42:4** — The Vulgate reads 'Non erit tristis, neque turbulentus' ('He will not be sad nor turbulent'), differing from the Hebrew 'he will not grow faint or be crushed.'
- **42:11** — The Vulgate punctuates 'In domibus habitabit Cedar' ('Kedar will dwell in houses') as a separate clause, whereas the Hebrew links 'the villages where Kedar dwells' to the preceding call; Latin 'Cedar' is rendered with the standard modern English spelling 'Kedar.'
- **42:14** — The Vulgate 'loquar; dissipabo, et absorbebo simul' ('I will speak; I will scatter and swallow up together') diverges from the Hebrew imagery of groaning, gasping, and panting.
- **42:19** — The Vulgate reads 'ad quem nuntios meos misi' ('the one to whom I have sent My messengers') and 'qui venundatus est' ('the one who has been sold'), diverging from the Hebrew 'my messenger whom I send' and 'the one at peace / dedicated one.'
