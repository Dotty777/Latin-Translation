## Text

**16:1** Send forth the lamb, Lord, the ruler of the earth, from the rock of the desert to the mountain of the daughter of Zion.

**16:2** And it will be: like a fleeing bird and young birds flying away from the nest, so will the daughters of Moab be at the crossing of Arnon.

**16:3** Take counsel, convene a council; make your shadow like night at midday; hide those who flee, and do not betray the wanderers.

**16:4** My fugitives will dwell with you; Moab, be their hiding place from the face of the destroyer: for the dust has come to an end, the wretched one has been consumed, he who trampled the earth has ceased.

**16:5** And a throne will be prepared in mercy, and one will sit upon it in truth in the tent of David, judging and seeking justice, and swiftly rendering what is just.

**16:6** We have heard of the pride of Moab: it is exceedingly proud; its pride, and its arrogance, and its indignation are greater than its strength.

**16:7** Therefore Moab will wail for Moab; all of it will wail: to those who rejoice over the walls of baked brick, speak of their wounds.

**16:8** For the outskirts of Heshbon are desolate, and the lords of the nations have cut down the vine of Sibmah: its shoots reached as far as Jazer, they wandered in the desert; its branches were abandoned, they crossed the sea.

**16:9** Over this I will weep with the weeping of Jazer for the vine of Sibmah; I will drench you with my tears, Heshbon and Elealeh, for the voice of the treaders has rushed upon your vintage and upon your harvest.

**16:10** And gladness and exultation will be taken away from Carmel, and in the vineyards no one will exult or shout for joy. The one who was accustomed to tread will not tread wine in the winepress; I have taken away the voice of the treaders.

**16:11** Over this my belly will sound like a harp for Moab, and my inward parts for the wall of baked brick.

**16:12** And it will be: when it appears that Moab has labored upon its high places, it will enter its sanctuary to plead, and it will not prevail.

**16:13** This is the word that the Lord spoke to Moab from that time.

**16:14** And now the Lord has spoken, saying, "In three years, like the years of a hired worker, the glory of Moab over all the numerous people will be taken away, and it will be left small and meager, by no means numerous."

## Translator Notes

- **16:1** — Vulgate-distinct reading: "Emitte agnum, Domine, dominatorem terrae, de petra deserti" addresses the Lord with a singular imperative, places "the ruler of the earth" in apposition to "the lamb," and translates Hebrew Sela literally as "the rock."
- **16:4** — Vulgate-distinct readings: "profugi mei" ("My fugitives") is addressed to Moab, while "pulvis" ("the dust") and "miser" ("the wretched one") differ from the Hebrew Masoretic Text ("the extortioner" and "destruction").
- **16:7** — Latin "muros cocti lateris" ("the walls of baked brick") translates the Hebrew place name Kir-hareseth literally (as also in 16:11), and "his qui laetantur ... loquimini plagas suas" differs markedly from the Hebrew ("for the raisin cakes ... moan, utterly stricken").
- **16:10** — The Vulgate renders Hebrew karmel ("orchard" or "fruitful field") as the proper name "Carmel."
