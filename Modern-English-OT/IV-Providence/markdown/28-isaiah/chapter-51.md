## Text

**51:1** Listen to Me, you who pursue what is righteous and seek the Lord; look to the rock from which you were cut out, and to the hollow of the pit from which you were carved out.

**51:2** Look to Abraham, your father, and to Sarah, who gave birth to you; for I called him when he was one, and I blessed him and multiplied him.

**51:3** Therefore the Lord will comfort Zion, and He will comfort all her ruins; He will make her desert like delights, and her wilderness like the garden of the Lord. Joy and gladness will be found in her, thanksgiving and the voice of praise.

**51:4** Pay attention to Me, My people, and My tribe, listen to Me; for a law will go out from Me, and My judgment will rest as a light for the peoples.

**51:5** My righteous one is near, My savior has gone out, and My arms will judge the peoples; the islands will wait for Me, and they will wait upon My arm.

**51:6** Lift up your eyes to heaven, and look beneath the earth below; for the heavens will dissolve like smoke, and the earth will be worn away like a garment, and its inhabitants will perish like these; but My salvation will be forever, and My righteousness will not fail.

**51:7** Listen to Me, you who know what is righteous, My people, with My law in their heart: do not fear the reproach of men, and do not dread their blasphemies;

**51:8** for the worm will eat them just like a garment, and the moth will devour them just like wool; but My salvation will be forever, and My righteousness for generations of generations.

**51:9** Rise up, rise up, put on strength, arm of the Lord! Rise up as in ancient days, in the generations of the ages. Was it not You who struck the proud one, who wounded the dragon?

**51:10** Was it not You who dried up the sea, the water of the mighty abyss, who made the depth of the sea a way so that those set free might cross over?

**51:11** And now those who have been redeemed by the Lord will return and come into Zion praising, with everlasting gladness upon their heads; they will hold joy and gladness, and sorrow and groaning will flee.

**51:12** I, I Myself will comfort you. Who are you, that you should be afraid of a mortal man, and of a son of man who will dry up just like grass?

**51:13** And you have forgotten the Lord, your Maker, who stretched out the heavens and laid the foundation of the earth; and you have been terrified continually all day long before the face of the fury of the one who oppressed you and had prepared to destroy. Where now is the fury of the oppressor?

**51:14** Quickly the one advancing to open will come; and he will not kill to utter destruction, nor will his bread fail.

**51:15** But I am the Lord your God, who stir up the sea, and its waves swell; the Lord of hosts is My name.

**51:16** I have placed My words in your mouth, and in the shadow of My hand I have protected you, so that you may plant the heavens and lay the foundation of the earth, and say to Zion, "You are My people."

**51:17** Lift yourself up, lift yourself up, rise up, Jerusalem, you who have drunk from the hand of the Lord the cup of His wrath; down to the bottom of the cup of stupor you have drunk, and you have drunk deeply down to the dregs.

**51:18** There is no one to uphold her out of all the sons whom she bore, and there is no one to take hold of her hand out of all the sons whom she nurtured.

**51:19** There are two things that have come upon you; who will grieve over you? Devastation and shattering, and famine and the sword; who will comfort you?

**51:20** Your sons have been cast down; they have slept at the head of all the streets like an ensnared oryx, full of the indignation of the Lord, of the rebuke of your God.

**51:21** Therefore listen to this, poor little one, and drunk, not from wine.

**51:22** Your Ruler, the Lord, and your God, who will fight for His people, says this: "Look, I have taken from your hand the cup of stupor, the bottom of the cup of My indignation; you will not drink it anymore.

**51:23** And I will place it in the hand of those who humiliated you and said to your soul, 'Bow down, so that we may pass over'; and you laid your body like the ground, and like a way for those passing over."

## Translator Notes

- **51:3** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew place-name Eden according to its etymological meaning, delicias ('delights').
- **51:5** — The Vulgate renders justus meus ('My righteous one') and salvator meus ('My savior') as personal masculine nouns, where the Hebrew Masoretic Text uses the abstract nouns 'righteousness' and 'salvation.'
- **51:9** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew name Rahab by its sense as superbum ('the proud one').
- **51:14** — Following the Latin strictly, the Vulgate speaks of one advancing swiftly to open who will not kill to utter destruction, whereas the Hebrew describes a crouching captive soon to be released who will not die in the pit.
