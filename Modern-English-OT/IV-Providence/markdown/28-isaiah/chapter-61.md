## Text

**61:1** The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because the Lord has anointed me; He sent me to announce to the meek, to heal those crushed in heart, and to proclaim pardon to the captives and opening to those confined;

**61:2** to proclaim a year favorable to the Lord and a day of vengeance for our God; to comfort all who mourn,

**61:3** to provide for the mourners of Zion and give them a crown instead of ashes, the oil of joy instead of mourning, a cloak of praise instead of a spirit of grief; and they will be called in her mighty ones of righteousness, the planting of the Lord for glorification.

**61:4** And they will build the places deserted from ages past, and they will raise up the ancient ruins, and they will restore the deserted cities, laid waste for generation after generation.

**61:5** And strangers will stand and pasture your flocks, and the sons of foreigners will be your farmers and your vinedressers.

**61:6** But you will be called priests of the Lord; "Ministers of our God," it will be said to you; you will eat the strength of the nations, and in their glory you will take pride.

**61:7** Instead of your double shame and disgrace, they will praise their portion; because of this, in their land they will possess a double share; everlasting gladness will be theirs.

**61:8** For I, the Lord, love justice and hate robbery in a burnt offering; and I will give their work in truth, and I will make a perpetual covenant with them.

**61:9** And they will know their seed among the nations, and their offspring in the midst of the peoples; all who see them will recognize them, that these are the seed whom the Lord has blessed.

**61:10** Rejoicing, I will rejoice in the Lord, and my soul will exult in my God, because He has clothed me with garments of salvation and has wrapped me in a robe of righteousness, like a bridegroom adorned with a crown, and like a bride ornamented with her jewels.

**61:11** For just as the earth brings forth its sprout, and just as a garden makes its seed sprout, so the Lord God will make righteousness and praise sprout before all the nations.

## Translator Notes

- **61:1** — Vulgate reads "the Spirit of the Lord" (without "God" as in the Hebrew), "heal" (mederer) rather than Hebrew "bind up," "pardon" (indulgentiam, a legal term for amnesty or remission), and literally "opening to those confined" (clausis apertionem), preserving the ambiguity between opening a prison and opening eyes.
- **61:3** — Vulgate uniquely includes "in her" (in ea, referring to Zion) and renders the Hebrew term for "oaks" or "terebinths" as "mighty ones" (fortes justitiae), concluding with the gerund "for glorification" (ad glorificandum).
- **61:7** — Vulgate connects "double" (duplici) to "your shame" ("Instead of your double shame and disgrace"), whereas the Hebrew text reads "Instead of your shame, a double portion."
- **61:8** — Vulgate reads "robbery in a burnt offering" (rapinam in holocausto), interpreting the Hebrew noun as "burnt offering" rather than "wrongdoing," and translates the following term literally as "their work" (opus eorum) rather than "recompense."
