## Text

**27:1** In that day the Lord will visit upon Leviathan, the bar serpent, and upon Leviathan, the twisted serpent, with His hard, great, and strong sword, and He will kill the sea monster that is in the sea.

**27:2** In that day the vineyard of pure wine will sing to Him.

**27:3** "I am the Lord who keeps her; suddenly I will give her drink. So that no visitation may perhaps be made against her, night and day I keep her.

**27:4** Indignation is not in Me. Who will make Me a thorn and a bramble in battle? I will stride upon it; I will burn it altogether.

**27:5** Or will he rather take hold of My strength? He will make peace with Me; peace he will make with Me."

**27:6** Those who enter with a rush to Jacob: Israel will blossom and sprout, and they will fill the face of the world with seed.

**27:7** Has He struck him according to the blow of the one striking him? Or just as He killed those of his who were put to death, so was he killed?

**27:8** In measure against measure, when she has been cast out, You will judge her; He has meditated in His hard spirit through the day of heat.

**27:9** Therefore, upon this, iniquity will be forgiven to the house of Jacob; and this is all the fruit: that his sin be taken away, when he has made all the stones of the altar like shattered stones of ash; the groves and the shrines will not stand.

**27:10** For the fortified city will be desolate; the beautiful one will be abandoned and left like a desert; there the calf will graze, and there it will lie down, and it will consume its tops.

**27:11** In dryness her harvests will be crushed. Women are coming and teaching her; for it is not a wise people; therefore He who made it will not have mercy on it, and He who formed it will not spare it.

**27:12** And it will be: in that day the Lord will strike from the channel of the river to the torrent of Egypt; and you will be gathered one by one, sons of Israel.

**27:13** And it will be: in that day a blast will be sounded on a great trumpet; and those who had been lost will come from the land of the Assyrians, and those who had been cast out in the land of Egypt, and they will worship the Lord on the holy mountain in Jerusalem.

## Translator Notes

- **27:1** — Vulgate renders Hebrew bariach ("fleeing" or "swift") as vectem ("bar" or "bolt"), giving "the bar serpent."
- **27:2** — Vulgate reads vinea meri ("the vineyard of pure wine") as the nominative subject singing to the Lord (cantabit ei), whereas the Hebrew has a plural imperative, "Sing of her!"
- **27:4** — Vulgate translates the Hebrew dative suffix ("to me") as a direct-object accusative (dabit me spinam et veprem), yielding "Who will make Me a thorn and a bramble in battle?" rather than "Who would give me thorns and briers?"
- **27:8** — Vulgate translates Hebrew hagah (which can mean "remove" or "meditate") as meditatus est ("He has meditated") and qadim ("east wind") as aestus ("heat").
- **27:11** — Vulgate renders Hebrew qetsirah ("its boughs") as messes ("her harvests") and me'irot otah ("set fire to it") as docentes eam ("teaching her").
