## Text

**52:1** Rise up, rise up, put on your strength, Zion! Put on the garments of your glory, Jerusalem, city of the Holy One, for the uncircumcised and the unclean will no longer pass through you.

**52:2** Shake yourself from the dust, rise up; sit down, Jerusalem! Loosen the bonds of your neck, captive daughter of Zion.

**52:3** For the Lord says this: "You were sold for nothing, and without silver you will be redeemed."

**52:4** For the Lord God says this: "My people went down into Egypt at the beginning to be a settler there, and Assyria oppressed them without any cause.

**52:5** And now what do I have here," says the Lord, "since My people have been taken away for nothing? Their rulers act unjustly," says the Lord, "and continually all day long My name is blasphemed.

**52:6** Because of this, My people will know My name on that day, for I Myself who was speaking, look, here I am."

**52:7** How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of the one announcing and proclaiming peace, announcing good, proclaiming salvation, saying to Zion, "Your God will reign!"

**52:8** The voice of your watchmen: they have lifted up their voice, together they will praise, for eye to eye they will see when the Lord brings Zion back.

**52:9** Rejoice and praise together, desolate places of Jerusalem, for the Lord has comforted His people; He has redeemed Jerusalem.

**52:10** The Lord has prepared His holy arm in the eyes of all the nations, and all the ends of the earth will see the salvation of our God.

**52:11** Depart, depart; go out from there, do not touch what is polluted; go out from the midst of it; be cleansed, you who carry the vessels of the Lord.

**52:12** For you will not go out in tumult, nor will you hurry in flight; for the Lord will go before you, and the God of Israel will gather you.

**52:13** Look, My servant will understand; he will be exalted and lifted up, and he will be exceedingly high.

**52:14** Just as many were astounded at you, so inglorious will his appearance be among men, and his form among the sons of men.

**52:15** he will sprinkle many nations; before him kings will hold their mouths shut, for those to whom it has not been told about him have seen, and those who have not heard have contemplated.

## Translator Notes

- **52:1** — The Vulgate reads civitas Sancti ('city of the Holy One,' masculine singular genitive) rather than the Hebrew 'the holy city.'
- **52:8** — The Vulgate has the transitive verb converterit Dominus Sion ('when the Lord brings Zion back'), whereas the Hebrew reads 'when the Lord returns to Zion.'
- **52:12** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew term for 'rearguard' literally from its verbal root as congregabit vos ('will gather you').
- **52:14** — The Vulgate reads inglorius ... inter viros ... inter filios hominum ('inglorious ... among men ... among the sons of men'), where the Hebrew describes his appearance as marred more than any man.
- **52:15** — The Vulgate reads quibus non est narratum de eo ('those to whom it has not been told about him') with the perfect-tense verbs viderunt ('have seen') and contemplati sunt ('have contemplated'), diverging from the Hebrew future-tense 'what had not been told them they will see, and what they had not heard they will understand.'
