## Text

**10:1** Woe to those who enact unjust laws, and who, writing, have written unrighteousness,

**10:2** to oppress the poor in judgment and do violence to the cause of the humble of My people, so that widows might be their plunder and they might rob the orphans.

**10:3** What will you do in the day of visitation, and of calamity coming from afar? To whose help will you flee for refuge? And where will you leave your glory,

**10:4** so that you may not be bowed down under a bond, and fall with those who are killed? Over all these things His fury has not been turned away, but His hand is still stretched out.

**10:5** Woe to Assyria! He himself is the rod of My fury and the staff; in their hand is My indignation.

**10:6** To a deceitful nation I will send him, and against the people of My fury I will command him, to carry off spoils and seize plunder, and to place it for trampling like the mud of the streets.

**10:7** Yet he himself will not think so, and his heart will not reckon it this way; rather, his heart will be for crushing and for the extermination of not a few nations.

**10:8** For he will say:

**10:9** "Are not my princes altogether kings? As Carchemish is, is not Calno also? And as Arpad is, is not Hamath? As Damascus is, is not Samaria?

**10:10** Just as my hand has found the kingdoms of the idol, so also their images from Jerusalem and from Samaria.

**10:11** Just as I have done to Samaria and to its idols, will I not also do to Jerusalem and to its images?"

**10:12** And it will come to pass, when the Lord has fulfilled all His works on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, I will punish the fruit of the arrogant heart of the king of Assyria, and the glory of the loftiness of his eyes.

**10:13** For he has said: "In the strength of my hand I have acted, and in my wisdom I have understood; and I have removed the boundaries of the peoples, and have plundered their princes, and like a mighty one I have pulled down those seated on high.

**10:14** And my hand has found the strength of the peoples like a nest; and just as eggs that have been abandoned are gathered, so I myself have gathered the whole earth; and there was none who moved a wing, or opened a mouth, or chirped."

**10:15** Will the axe boast against the one who cuts with it? Or will the saw exalt itself against the one by whom it is drawn? It is as if a rod were to raise itself against the one raising it, and a staff were to exalt itself, which is indeed wood.

**10:16** Because of this, the Sovereign, the Lord of hosts, will send leanness among his fat ones; and beneath his glory, once kindled, it will burn like the burning of fire.

**10:17** And the light of Israel will be in fire, and its Holy One in flame; and his thorn and brier will be kindled and devoured in one day.

**10:18** And the glory of his forest and of his fruitful field will be consumed from soul even to flesh; and he will be a fugitive in terror.

**10:19** And the remnant of the trees of his forest will be numbered because of their fewness, and a boy will write them down.

**10:20** And it will come to pass in that day: the remainder of Israel, and those who have fled from the house of Jacob, will no longer lean upon the one who strikes them, but will lean upon the Lord, the Holy One of Israel, in truth.

**10:21** A remnant will return; the remnant, I say, of Jacob, to the mighty God.

**10:22** For even if your people, Israel, are like the sand of the sea, a remnant will return from it; a shortened completion will overflow with righteousness.

**10:23** For the Lord God of hosts will bring about a completion and a shortening in the midst of all the earth.

**10:24** Because of this, this is what the Lord God of hosts says: "Do not be afraid of Assyria, My people, dweller in Zion: he will strike you with a rod, and will lift up his staff over you, in the way of Egypt.

**10:25** For yet a little, a very brief while, and the indignation and My fury will be completed upon their wickedness."

**10:26** And the Lord of hosts will raise up a scourge against him, according to the blow against Midian at the rock of Oreb; and His rod over the sea, and He will lift it up in the way of Egypt.

**10:27** And it will come to pass in that day: his burden will be taken away from your shoulder and his yoke from your neck, and the yoke will rot away before the face of the oil.

**10:28** He will come to Aiath, he will pass through Migron, at Michmash he will deposit his baggage.

**10:29** They have passed over in haste: "Geba is our lodging." Ramah has been stunned; Gibeah of Saul has fled.

**10:30** Neigh with your voice, daughter of Gallim; pay attention, Laishah; poor little Anathoth!

**10:31** Madmenah has departed; inhabitants of Gebim, take courage!

**10:32** There is still a day to halt at Nob; he will shake his hand against the mountain of the daughter of Zion, the hill of Jerusalem.

**10:33** Look, the Sovereign, the Lord of hosts, will shatter the small flask in terror; and those lofty in stature will be cut down, and the exalted will be humbled.

**10:34** And the thickets of the forest will be overturned with iron; and Lebanon will fall along with the lofty ones.

## Translator Notes

- **10:15** — Latin 'qui utique lignum est' ("which is indeed wood") is a distinctly Vulgate reading where the Hebrew has "what is not wood."
- **10:18** — Latin uses 'carmeli' as a common noun for "fruitful field" (orchard), and 'terrore profugus' ("a fugitive in terror") is a Vulgate-distinct rendering where the Hebrew has "as when a sick man wastes away."
- **10:22** — Latin 'consummatio abbreviata' ("a shortened completion," echoed in 10:23 'consummationem et abbreviationem') renders the Hebrew words for "destruction" and "decreed" by their root senses "finishing" and "cut short"; compare Romans 9:28.
- **10:27** — Latin 'a facie olei' ("before the face of the oil") renders the Hebrew word for fatness/oil specifically as "oil."
- **10:33** — Latin 'lagunculam' ("small flask") is a notable Vulgate-distinct rendering where the Hebrew text has "boughs" or "branches."
