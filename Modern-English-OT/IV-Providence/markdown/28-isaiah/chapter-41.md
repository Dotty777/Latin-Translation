## Text

**41:1** Let the islands be silent before Me, and let the nations renew their strength; let them come forward, and then let them speak; let us draw near to judgment together.

**41:2** Who has raised up the Righteous One from the east, called him to follow Him? He will give nations in his sight, and He will subdue kings; He will give them like dust to his sword, as stubble swept away by the wind to his bow.

**41:3** He will pursue them, he will pass on in peace; a path will not appear at his feet.

**41:4** Who has worked and done these things, calling the generations from the beginning? I am the Lord; I am the first and the last.

**41:5** The islands saw and were afraid; the ends of the earth were astounded; they drew near and came forward.

**41:6** Each one will help his neighbor, and will say to his brother, "Be strong."

**41:7** The coppersmith, striking with a hammer, strengthened the one who was forging at that time, saying, "It is good for soldering," and he strengthened it with nails so that it would not be moved.

**41:8** And you, Israel, My servant, Jacob whom I have chosen, seed of Abraham My friend,

**41:9** in whom I took hold of you from the ends of the earth, and from its distant places I called you, and said to you, "You are My servant; I have chosen you and have not cast you away."

**41:10** Do not fear, for I am with you; do not turn aside, for I am your God; I have strengthened you, and I have helped you, and the right hand of My Righteous One has upheld you.

**41:11** Look, all who fight against you will be confounded and ashamed; they will be as though they did not exist, and the men who speak against you will perish.

**41:12** You will seek them and will not find them, the men who rebel against you; the men who wage war against you will be as though they did not exist and like destruction.

**41:13** For I am the Lord your God, taking hold of your hand and saying to you, "Do not fear; I have helped you."

**41:14** "Do not fear, worm of Jacob, you who are dead from Israel; I have helped you," says the Lord, and your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel.

**41:15** I have made you like a new threshing wagon, having serrated teeth; you will thresh the mountains and crush them, and you will make the hills like dust.

**41:16** You will winnow them, and the wind will carry them away, and the whirlwind will scatter them; and you will exult in the Lord; in the Holy One of Israel you will rejoice.

**41:17** The needy and the poor seek water, and there is none; their tongue has dried up with thirst. I, the Lord, will hear them; the God of Israel, I will not abandon them.

**41:18** I will open rivers on the sloping hills, and springs in the midst of the plains; I will turn the desert into pools of water, and the pathless land into streams of water.

**41:19** I will set in the wilderness the cedar, and the thorn, and the myrtle, and the olive tree; I will place in the desert the fir, the elm, and the box tree together,

**41:20** so that they may see, and know, and consider, and understand together, that the hand of the Lord has done this, and the Holy One of Israel has created it.

**41:21** "Bring your case near," says the Lord; "bring it forward, if perhaps you have anything," says the King of Jacob.

**41:22** Let them come forward and declare to us whatever things are to come; declare the former things that were, and we will set our heart on them and know their outcome; and make known to us the things that are to come.

**41:23** Announce the things that are to come in the future, and we will know that you are gods; also do good or evil, if you can, and let us speak and see together.

**41:24** Look, you are from nothing, and your work is from that which does not exist; an abomination is the one who has chosen you.

**41:25** I have raised up one from the north, and he will come from the rising of the sun; he will call on My name, and he will bring magistrates like clay, and like a potter treading down the soil.

**41:26** Who has announced it from the outset so that we may know, and from the beginning so that we may say, "You are righteous"? There is neither anyone announcing, nor foretelling, nor hearing your words.

**41:27** The first will say to Zion, "Look, they are here," and to Jerusalem I will give an evangelist.

**41:28** And I looked, and there was not even one among these who would take counsel and, when questioned, answer a word.

**41:29** Look, they are all unjust, and their works are vain; their images are wind and emptiness.

## Translator Notes

- **41:2** — The Vulgate reads 'Justum' ('the Righteous One') and 'vocavit eum ut sequeretur se' ('called him to follow Him'), diverging from the Hebrew 'whom righteousness/victory meets at his feet.'
- **41:4** — The Vulgate 'primus et novissimus ego sum' ('I am the first and the last') differs from the Hebrew 'I am the first, and with the last I am He.'
- **41:7** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew word for 'anvil' as 'tunc temporis' ('at that time'), reading it from the homonym meaning 'time.'
- **41:10** — Distinctive Vulgate readings here include 'ne declines' ('do not turn aside' vs. Hebrew 'do not be dismayed'), perfect-tense verbs ('have strengthened ... have helped ... has upheld'), and 'dextera Justi mei' ('the right hand of My Righteous One' vs. Hebrew 'My righteous right hand').
- **41:14** — The Vulgate reads 'qui mortui estis ex Israel' ('you who are dead from Israel'), vocalizing the Hebrew word for 'men' as 'dead.'
