## Text

**66:1** This is what the Lord says: "Heaven is My throne, and the earth is the footstool of My feet. What is this house that you will build for Me? And what is this place of My rest?

**66:2** All these things My hand has made, and all these came into being," says the Lord. "Yet to whom will I look, except to the poor one, and the one contrite in spirit, and the one who trembles at My words?

**66:3** The one who sacrifices an ox is like one who kills a man; the one who slaughters a beast is like one who dashes out the brains of a dog; the one who offers an offering is like one who offers pig's blood; the one who remembers frankincense is like one who blesses an idol. All these things they have chosen in their ways, and their soul has delighted in their abominations.

**66:4** Therefore I also will choose their delusions, and I will bring upon them the things they feared; because I called, and there was no one who answered; I spoke, and they did not hear; and they did evil in My eyes, and they chose the things I did not want."

**66:5** Hear the word of the Lord, you who tremble at His word. Your brothers who hate you and cast you out because of My name have said, "Let the Lord be glorified, and we will look on your gladness." Yet they themselves will be put to shame.

**66:6** The voice of the people from the city, a voice from the temple, the voice of the Lord repaying retribution to His enemies.

**66:7** Before she was in labor, she gave birth; before her delivery came, she gave birth to a male child.

**66:8** Who has ever heard such a thing? And who has seen anything like this? Will a land be in labor in one day, or will a nation be born at once, since Zion has been in labor and has given birth to her sons?

**66:9** "Will I, who cause others to give birth, Myself not give birth?" says the Lord. "If I am the one who grants childbearing to the rest, will I be barren?" says the Lord your God.

**66:10** Be glad with Jerusalem and exult in her, all you who love her; rejoice with her in joy, all of you who mourn over her,

**66:11** so that you may nurse and be filled from the breast of her comfort; so that you may draw milk and overflow with delights from her manifold glory.

**66:12** For this is what the Lord says: "Look, I will turn upon her like a river of peace, and like an overflowing torrent the glory of the nations, which you will nurse on: at the breasts you will be carried, and upon the knees they will caress you.

**66:13** Just as when a mother caresses someone, so I will comfort you, and in Jerusalem you will be comforted.

**66:14** You will see, and your heart will rejoice, and your bones will sprout like grass; and the hand of the Lord will be known to His servants, and He will be indignant toward His enemies.

**66:15** For look, the Lord will come in fire, and His chariots like a whirlwind, to render His fury in indignation and His rebuke in a flame of fire;

**66:16** for in fire the Lord will execute judgment, and by His sword against all flesh; and those killed by the Lord will be multiplied;

**66:17** those who were sanctifying themselves and considering themselves clean in the gardens behind the door inside, who were eating pig's flesh, and abomination, and the mouse: together they will be consumed," says the Lord.

**66:18** "Yet I am coming to gather their works and their thoughts, together with all nations and tongues; and they will come and see My glory.

**66:19** And I will set a sign among them, and I will send from among those who have been saved to the nations on the sea, to Africa and Lydia, aiming the arrow; to Italy and Greece, to the islands far away, to those who have not heard about Me and have not seen My glory. And they will proclaim My glory to the nations;

**66:20** and they will bring all your brothers from all the nations as a gift to the Lord, on horses, and in chariots, and in litters, and on mules, and in carriages, to My holy mountain Jerusalem," says the Lord, "just as when the sons of Israel bring an offering in a clean vessel into the house of the Lord.

**66:21** And I will take some from among them to be priests and Levites," says the Lord.

**66:22** "For just as the new heavens and the new earth, which I make to stand before Me," says the Lord, "so will your seed and your name stand.

**66:23** And there will be month after month, and Sabbath after Sabbath: all flesh will come to worship before My face," says the Lord.

**66:24** "And they will go out and see the corpses of the men who transgressed against Me; their worm will not die, and their fire will not be extinguished; and they will be even to satiety of sight for all flesh."

## Translator Notes

- **66:3** — Vulgate excerebret ("dashes out the brains of") differs from the Hebrew ("breaks the neck of"), and recordatur thuris ("remembers frankincense") literally renders the Hebrew idiom for making a memorial offering of frankincense.
- **66:6** — Vulgate reads "The voice of the people" (Vox populi), whereas the Hebrew reads "A sound of uproar."
- **66:17** — Vulgate reads "behind the door inside" (post januam intrinsecus), whereas the Hebrew reads "following one in the center."
- **66:18** — Vulgate resolves the broken Hebrew syntax by making "their works and their thoughts" the direct object of "to gather" (ut congregem), alongside "all nations and tongues."
- **66:19** — Vulgate identifies the Hebrew names Tarshish, Pul, Lud, Tubal, and Javan as "the sea" (mare), "Africa," "Lydia," "Italy," and "Greece," and reads "aiming the arrow" (tendentes sagittam) where the Hebrew has "drawing the bow."
- **66:24** — Vulgate renders the rare Hebrew term deraon ("abhorrence") etymologically as usque ad satietatem visionis ("even to satiety of sight"), conveying being sated to disgust at the sight.
