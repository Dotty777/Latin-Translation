## Text

**43:1** And now this is what the Lord says, who creates you, Jacob, and forms you, Israel: "Do not be afraid, because I have redeemed you, and I have called you by your name; you are Mine.

**43:2** When you pass through the waters, I will be with you, and the rivers will not cover you; when you walk in the fire, you will not be burned up, and the flame will not burn in you.

**43:3** For I am the Lord your God, the Holy One of Israel, your Savior; I have given Egypt as your propitiation, Ethiopia and Seba for you.

**43:4** Since you became honorable in My eyes, and glorious, I have loved you, and I will give men for you, and peoples for your life.

**43:5** Do not be afraid, because I am with you; from the east I will bring your seed, and from the west I will gather you.

**43:6** I will say to the north, 'Give,' and to the south, 'Do not hold back: bring My sons from afar, and My daughters from the ends of the earth.'

**43:7** And everyone who calls upon My name, for My glory I created him, I formed him, and I made him.

**43:8** Bring out the people who are blind, yet have eyes; deaf, yet they have ears.

**43:9** All the nations have been gathered together, and the tribes have been assembled. Who among you can declare this, and will make us hear the first things? Let them present their witnesses, let them be justified, and let them hear and say, 'It is true.'

**43:10** You are My witnesses," says the Lord, "and My servant whom I have chosen, so that you may know and believe Me, and understand that I am He; before Me no god was formed, and after Me there will be none.

**43:11** I am, I am the Lord, and apart from Me there is no savior.

**43:12** I have declared and saved; I have made it heard, and there was no stranger among you: you are My witnesses," says the Lord, "and I am God.

**43:13** And from the beginning I am He, and there is no one who can rescue from My hand. I will work, and who will turn it back?"

**43:14** This is what the Lord, your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, says: "For your sake I have sent to Babylon, and I have brought down all the bars, and the Chaldeans boasting in their ships.

**43:15** I am the Lord, your Holy One, who creates Israel, your King."

**43:16** This is what the Lord says, who provided a way in the sea and a path in the rushing waters,

**43:17** who brought out chariot and horse, the army and the mighty warrior; together they fell asleep, and they will not rise again; they were crushed like flax, and they were extinguished:

**43:18** "Do not remember the former things, and do not look upon the things of old.

**43:19** Look, I am doing new things, and now they will spring up; surely you will know them: I will place a way in the desert, and rivers in the trackless waste.

**43:20** The beast of the field will glorify Me, the dragons and the ostriches, because I have given waters in the desert, rivers in the trackless waste, to give drink to My people, My chosen one.

**43:21** This people I have formed for Myself; they will recount My praise.

**43:22** You have not called upon Me, Jacob, nor have you labored over Me, Israel.

**43:23** You have not offered Me the ram of your burnt offering, and with your sacrifices you have not glorified Me; I have not made you serve with an offering, nor have I caused you labor with frankincense.

**43:24** You have not bought calamus for Me with silver, and with the fat of your sacrifices you have not saturated Me; yet you have made Me serve with your sins, you have caused Me labor with your iniquities.

**43:25** I am, I Myself am the one who blots out your iniquities for My own sake, and I will not remember your sins.

**43:26** Remind Me, and let us be judged together; recount whatever you have, so that you may be justified.

**43:27** Your first father sinned, and your interpreters have transgressed against Me;

**43:28** and I have profaned the holy princes; I have given Jacob to utter destruction, and Israel to reviling."

## Translator Notes

- **43:3** — Latin propitiationem renders Hebrew kopher ("ransom") with a sacrificial term meaning "propitiation" or "atonement"; Latin Aethopiam (for Aethiopiam) and Saba correspond to Ethiopia (Hebrew Cush) and Seba.
- **43:7** — The Vulgate reads the active qui invocat nomen meum ("who calls upon My name"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads a passive ("who is called by My name").
- **43:14** — The Vulgate reads vectes universos ("all the bars," taking the Hebrew term as gate or prison bars rather than "fugitives") and in navibus suis gloriantes ("boasting in their ships").
- **43:19** — Latin nova ("new things") is plural and utique cognoscetis ea is an emphatic affirmation ("surely you will know them"), differing from the singular and rhetorical question in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
