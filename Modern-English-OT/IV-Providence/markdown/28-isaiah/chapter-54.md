## Text

**54:1** "Praise, barren woman, you who do not give birth; sing out praise and cry out joyfully, you who were not giving birth, for the children of the desolate woman are many, more than those of her who has a husband," says the Lord.

**54:2** "Enlarge the place of your tent, and stretch out the skins of your dwellings; do not hold back; make your cords long, and make your stakes firm.

**54:3** For to the right and to the left you will press forward, and your offspring will inherit the nations and will inhabit the desolate cities.

**54:4** Do not fear, for you will not be put to shame, nor will you blush; for you will not feel disgrace, because you will forget the shame of your youth, and the reproach of your widowhood you will remember no longer.

**54:5** For He who made you will rule over you, the Lord of hosts is His name, and your Redeemer is the Holy One of Israel; He will be called the God of all the earth.

**54:6** For the Lord has called you like a woman abandoned and grieving in spirit, and like a wife cast off from youth," your God has said.

**54:7** "For a brief instant I abandoned you, and with great compassions I will gather you.

**54:8** In a moment of indignation I hid My face from you for a little while, and with everlasting mercy I have had compassion on you," your Redeemer, the Lord, has said.

**54:9** "This is to Me just as in the days of Noah, to whom I swore that I would not bring the waters of Noah upon the earth anymore; so I have sworn that I will not be angry with you and will not rebuke you.

**54:10** For the mountains will be moved, and the hills will tremble; yet My mercy will not depart from you, and the covenant of My peace will not be moved," the Lord, who has compassion on you, has said.

**54:11** "Poor little one, torn by the storm without any comfort, look, I will lay your stones in order, and I will lay your foundations in sapphires;

**54:12** and I will make your battlements of jasper, and your gates of carved stones, and all your borders of desirable stones,

**54:13** all your children taught by the Lord, and an abundance of peace for your children.

**54:14** And in righteousness you will be founded; depart far from oppression, for you will not fear, and from terror, for it will not draw near to you.

**54:15** Look, a resident alien will come who was not with Me; one who was once a stranger to you will be joined to you.

**54:16** Look, I have created the smith who blows on the coals in the fire and brings forth an instrument for his work; and I have created the killer to destroy.

**54:17** No instrument that has been fashioned against you will succeed, and every tongue resisting you in judgment you will judge. This is the inheritance of the servants of the Lord, and their righteousness is with Me," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **54:1** — Latin hinni literally means 'neigh,' Jerome's vivid rendering of the Hebrew verb tsahal, which conveys both the neighing of horses and a shrill shout of joy.
- **54:9** — The Vulgate reads in diebus Noe ('in the days of Noah') and cui juravi ('to whom I swore'), where the Hebrew Masoretic Text begins 'like the waters of Noah.'
- **54:11** — The Vulgate renders the setting of the stones as per ordinem ('in order') rather than Hebrew 'in antimony' (dark mortar), and in 54:12 reads jaspidem ('jasper') and lapides sculptos ('carved stones').
- **54:15** — Reading the Hebrew root in the sense of 'sojourning' rather than 'attacking,' the Vulgate turns this verse into a promise that a resident alien (accola) and former stranger (advena) will come and be joined to Zion.
