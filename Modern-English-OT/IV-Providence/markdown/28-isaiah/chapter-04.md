## Text

**4:1** And seven women will take hold of one man on that day, saying, "We will eat our own bread and cover ourselves with our own clothing; only let your name be called over us; take away our reproach."

**4:2** On that day, the shoot of the Lord will be in magnificence and glory, and the fruit of the earth will be lofty, and an exultation for those who have been saved from Israel.

**4:3** And it will be: everyone who has been left in Zion and who remains in Jerusalem will be called holy, everyone who is written in life in Jerusalem.

**4:4** If the Lord has washed away the filth of the daughters of Zion and washed the blood of Jerusalem from her midst, in the spirit of judgment and the spirit of burning.

**4:5** And the Lord will create over every place of Mount Zion, and where He has been invoked, a cloud by day and smoke, and the brightness of a flaming fire in the night; for over all glory there will be protection.

**4:6** And a tabernacle will be for shade by day from the heat, and for security and a hiding place from the whirlwind and from the rain.

## Translator Notes

- **4:3** — Latin 'scriptus est in vita' ('written in life') uses the ablative case rather than the Hebrew 'written for life,' expressing inscription among the living.
- **4:4** — Latin 'Si' ('If') literally reproduces the Hebrew conditional particle that in context carries a temporal sense ('when').
- **4:5** — Latin 'ubi invocatus est' ('where He has been invoked') is a distinctly Vulgate rendering of the Hebrew term usually translated 'her assemblies.'
