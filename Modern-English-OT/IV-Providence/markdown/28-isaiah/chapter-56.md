## Text

**56:1** This is what the Lord says: "Keep justice, and practice righteousness, for My salvation is near to come, and My righteousness to be revealed."

**56:2** Blessed is the man who does this, and the son of man who will take hold of it, keeping the Sabbath so that he does not defile it, keeping his hands so that he does not do any evil.

**56:3** And let not the son of the foreigner who clings to the Lord speak, saying, "The Lord will surely divide me from His people," and let not the eunuch say, "Look, I am a dry tree."

**56:4** For this is what the Lord says to the eunuchs: "Those who keep My Sabbaths, and choose the things that I have willed, and hold fast to My covenant,

**56:5** to them I will give in My house and within My walls a place, and a name better than sons and daughters; I will give them an everlasting name that will not perish.

**56:6** And the sons of the foreigner who cling to the Lord, to worship Him, and to love His name, to be servants to Him, everyone who keeps the Sabbath so that he does not defile it, and who holds fast to My covenant,

**56:7** I will bring them to My holy mountain, and I will make them joyful in My house of prayer; their burnt offerings and their sacrifices will be pleasing to Me on My altar, for My house will be called a house of prayer for all peoples."

**56:8** The Lord God, who gathers the dispersed of Israel, says: "I will still gather to him his gathered ones."

**56:9** All you beasts of the field, come to devour, all the beasts of the forest.

**56:10** Its watchmen are all blind; all of them know nothing: mute dogs unable to bark, seeing empty things, sleeping, and loving dreams.

**56:11** And the most imprudent dogs have not known satisfaction; the shepherds themselves have been ignorant of understanding: all of them have turned aside to their own way, each one to his own greed, from the highest to the last.

## Additional Verses (Not in Saints Numbering)

**56:12** *(after `56:11`)* "Come, let us get wine, and let us be filled with drunkenness; and just as it is today, so it will also be tomorrow, and much more."

## Translator Notes

- **56:5** — Vulgate locum ('a place') renders Hebrew yad (lit. 'hand,' often translated 'monument' or 'memorial').
- **56:8** — Vulgate congregabo ad eum congregatos ejus ('I will gather to him his gathered ones') takes 'his gathered ones' as the direct object, whereas the Hebrew reads 'besides those already gathered to him.'
- **56:10** — Vulgate videntes vana ('seeing empty things') and amantes somnia ('loving dreams') interpret Hebrew terms more commonly rendered 'dreaming/babbling' and 'loving to slumber.'
- **56:11** — Clementine Vulgate imprudentissimi ('most imprudent') is a variant of impudentissimi ('most shameless'), where the Hebrew reads 'strong of appetite' (greedy); a summo usque ad novissimum ('from the highest to the last') expands the Hebrew phrase meaning 'without exception.'
- **56:12 *(additional)*** — The Latin presents the shepherds' boast directly without an introductory verb and reads plural sumamus ("let us get") with ebrietate ("drunkenness").
