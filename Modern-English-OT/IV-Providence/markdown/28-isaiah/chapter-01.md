## Text

**1:1** The vision of Isaiah, son of Amoz, which he saw regarding Judah and Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.

**1:2** Hear, heavens, and listen with your ears, earth, for the Lord has spoken: "I have nourished and exalted sons, yet they themselves have despised Me.

**1:3** The ox knows its owner, and the donkey the feeding trough of its master; yet Israel has not known Me, and My people have not understood."

**1:4** Woe to a sinful nation, a people heavy with iniquity, a wicked seed, depraved sons! They have abandoned the Lord; they have blasphemed the Holy One of Israel; they have turned backward in estrangement.

**1:5** "Where should I strike you further, as you add transgression? The whole head is faint, and the whole heart is grieving.

**1:6** From the sole of the foot to the crown of the head, there is no soundness in it; wound, and bruise, and swelling sore have not been bandaged, nor treated with medicine, nor soothed with oil.

**1:7** Your land is desolate; your cities are burned with fire; in your presence foreigners devour your country, and it will be laid desolate as in an enemy devastation.

**1:8** And the daughter of Zion will be left like a shelter in a vineyard, and like a hut in a cucumber field, and like a city that is being devastated."

**1:9** Unless the Lord of hosts had left us seed, we would have been like Sodom, and we would resemble Gomorrah.

**1:10** Hear the word of the Lord, rulers of Sodom; listen with your ears to the law of our God, people of Gomorrah.

**1:11** "Of what use to Me is the multitude of your sacrifices?" says the Lord. "I am full; I have not desired burnt offerings of rams, and the fat of fattened animals, and the blood of calves and lambs and male goats.

**1:12** When you came before My sight, who sought these things from your hands, that you should walk in My courts?

**1:13** Do not offer sacrifice in vain anymore; incense is an abomination to Me. New moon and sabbath, and other festivals, I will not endure; your assemblies are wicked.

**1:14** My soul hates your new moons and your solemn festivals; they have become burdensome to Me; I have grown weary enduring them.

**1:15** And when you stretch out your hands, I will turn My eyes away from you, and when you multiply prayer, I will not listen, for your hands are full of blood.

**1:16** Wash yourselves, be clean; remove the evil of your thoughts from My eyes; cease acting perversely,

**1:17** learn to do good; seek justice, come to the aid of the oppressed, render justice for the orphan, defend the widow.

**1:18** And come, and reprove Me," says the Lord. "If your sins are like scarlet, they will be whitened like snow; and if they are red like crimson, they will be like white wool.

**1:19** If you are willing and listen to Me, you will eat the good things of the land.

**1:20** But if you refuse and provoke Me to anger, the sword will devour you, for the mouth of the Lord has spoken."

**1:21** How has the faithful city, full of justice, become a prostitute? Righteousness lived in her, but now murderers.

**1:22** Your silver has been turned into dross; your wine has been mixed with water.

**1:23** Your rulers are unfaithful, companions of thieves. All of them love gifts; they pursue rewards. They do not render justice for the orphan, and the case of the widow does not come before them.

**1:24** Because of this the Lord, the God of hosts, the Mighty One of Israel, says: "Alas! I will take comfort over My adversaries, and I will be avenged on My enemies.

**1:25** And I will turn My hand toward you, and I will smelt away your dross to purity, and I will remove all your tin.

**1:26** And I will restore your judges as they were before, and your counselors as in ancient times; after these things you will be called the city of the righteous one, the faithful city."

**1:27** Zion will be redeemed in justice, and they will bring her back in righteousness.

**1:28** And He will crush the wicked and sinners together, and those who have abandoned the Lord will be consumed.

**1:29** For they will be put to shame because of the idols to which they sacrificed, and you will blush over the gardens that you had chosen,

**1:30** when you become like an oak with its leaves falling, and like a garden without water.

**1:31** And your strength will be like the ash of flax fibers, and your work like a spark; and both will be set on fire together, and there will be no one to extinguish it.

## Translator Notes

- **1:5** — Vulgate "percutiam" ("should I strike", first-person singular active) presents God speaking directly, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text has a passive ("will you still be struck?"). Latin "super quo" can mean either "where [on the body]" or "on what account."
- **1:9** — Vulgate "semen" ("seed", matching the Septuagint and Romans 9:29) differs from the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "survivors" or "remnant."
- **1:18** — Vulgate "arguite me" ("reprove Me" or "accuse Me", an active imperative with God as object) is a distinctive Latin reading compared to the Hebrew reciprocal "let us reason together."
- **1:19** — All Clementine witnesses unanimously read present-form comeditis (an orthographic/morphological variant for future comedetis); rendered 'you will eat' in keeping with the future-perfect conditional protasis.
- **1:26** — Vulgate "civitas justi" uses the singular substantive ("city of the righteous one" or "of what is right") rather than the abstract noun "city of righteousness" in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **1:27** — Vulgate "et reducent eam in justitia" ("and they will bring her back in righteousness") reads a finite verb with Zion as object, contrasting with the Hebrew Masoretic Text's "and her repentant ones with righteousness."
