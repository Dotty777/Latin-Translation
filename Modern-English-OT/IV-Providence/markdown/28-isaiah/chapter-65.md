## Text

**65:1** Those who did not ask before have sought Me; those who did not seek Me have found Me. I said, "Here I am, here I am," to a nation that did not call on My name.

**65:2** I have stretched out My hands all day long to an unbelieving people who walk in a way that is not good, after their own thoughts;

**65:3** a people who provoke Me to anger before My face continually, who offer sacrifices in gardens and make offerings on bricks;

**65:4** who dwell in tombs and sleep in the shrines of idols; who eat pig's flesh, and profane broth is in their vessels;

**65:5** who say, "Withdraw from me; do not come near me, because you are unclean." These will be smoke in My fury, a fire burning all day long.

**65:6** Look, it is written before Me: "I will not keep silent, but I will repay, and I will pay back into their bosom,

**65:7** your iniquities and the iniquities of your fathers together," says the Lord, "who sacrificed on the mountains and reproached Me on the hills; and I will measure back their former work into their bosom."

**65:8** This is what the Lord says: "Just as when a grape is found in a cluster, and it is said, 'Do not destroy it, for it is a blessing,' so I will do for the sake of My servants, so that I will not destroy the whole.

**65:9** And I will bring forth a seed from Jacob, and from Judah one who possesses My mountains; and My chosen ones will inherit it, and My servants will dwell there.

**65:10** And the plains will become folds for flocks, and the Valley of Achor a resting place for herds, for My people who have sought Me.

**65:11** And you who have abandoned the Lord, who have forgotten My holy mountain, who set a table for Fortune and pour drink offerings on it:

**65:12** I will number you for the sword, and all of you will collapse in slaughter, because I called and you did not answer; I spoke and you did not hear; and you did evil in My eyes, and you chose the things I did not want."

**65:13** Therefore, this is what the Lord God says: "Look, My servants will eat, and you will be hungry; look, My servants will drink, and you will be thirsty;

**65:14** look, My servants will be glad, and you will be put to shame; look, My servants will give praise out of exultation of heart, and you will cry out from sorrow of heart, and from brokenness of spirit you will wail,

**65:15** and you will leave your name as an oath for My chosen ones; and the Lord God will put you to death, and He will call His servants by another name:

**65:16** by which the one who is blessed on the earth will be blessed in God, amen, and the one who swears in the earth will swear by God, amen: because the former distresses have been handed over to forgetfulness, and because they are hidden from My eyes.

**65:17** For look, I am creating new heavens and a new earth; and the former things will not be in memory, and they will not rise up upon the heart.

**65:18** Rather, you will rejoice and exult forever in the things that I create; for look, I am creating Jerusalem to be an exultation, and her people a joy.

**65:19** And I will exult in Jerusalem, and I will rejoice in My people; and there will no longer be heard in it the voice of weeping and the voice of outcry.

**65:20** There will no longer be there an infant of days, nor an old man who does not complete his days, for the child of a hundred years will die, and the sinner of a hundred years will be cursed.

**65:21** And they will build houses and dwell in them; and they will plant vineyards and eat their fruits.

**65:22** They will not build and another dwell there; they will not plant and another eat: for according to the days of a tree will be the days of My people, and the works of their hands will grow old.

**65:23** My chosen ones will not labor in vain, nor bring forth children in turmoil, for they are the seed of the blessed of the Lord, and their descendants are with them.

**65:24** And it will happen that before they cry out, I will answer; while they are still speaking, I will hear.

**65:25** The wolf and the lamb will graze together, the lion and the ox will eat straw, and for the serpent, dust will be its bread. They will not harm nor kill in all My holy mountain," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **65:1** — Vulgate uses active verbs ("have sought Me ... have found [Me]," Quæsierunt me ... invenerunt), whereas the Hebrew uses passive forms ("I was sought ... I was found").
- **65:5** — Vulgate reads "because you are unclean" (quia immundus es), contrasting with the Hebrew ("for I am too holy for you").
- **65:11** — Vulgate renders Hebrew Gad as "Fortune" (fortunæ) and omits the second pagan deity name (Hebrew Meni, "Destiny"), reading instead "pour drink offerings on it" (super eam, referring to the table).
- **65:16** — Vulgate transliterates the Hebrew title "God of truth" (Elohei amen) directly as "in God, amen" (in Deo, amen).
- **65:22** — Vulgate places "My chosen ones" (Electi mei) at the start of verse 23 and makes "the works of their hands" the subject of inveterabunt ("will grow old," i.e., endure to great age), unlike the Hebrew ("my chosen will long enjoy the work of their hands").
- **65:25** — Vulgate reads "the lion and the ox" (leo et bos), whereas the Hebrew reads "the lion like the ox."
