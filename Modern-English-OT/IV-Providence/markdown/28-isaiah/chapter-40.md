## Text

**40:1** "Take comfort, take comfort, My people," says your God.

**40:2** Speak to the heart of Jerusalem and call to her, because her wickedness is finished, her iniquity has been forgiven; she has received double from the hand of the Lord for all her sins.

**40:3** A voice of one crying out in the desert: "Prepare the way of the Lord; make straight the paths of our God in the wasteland.

**40:4** Every valley will be raised up, and every mountain and hill will be brought low; and what is crooked will become straight, and rough places will become level roads,

**40:5** and the glory of the Lord will be revealed, and all flesh will see together that the mouth of the Lord has spoken."

**40:6** A voice of one saying, "Cry out!" And I said, "What will I cry out?" "All flesh is grass, and all its glory is like a flower of the field.

**40:7** The grass has dried up, and the flower has fallen, because the breath of the Lord has blown upon it. Truly the people are grass;

**40:8** the grass has dried up, and the flower has fallen, but the word of our Lord remains forever."

**40:9** Climb up onto a high mountain, you who proclaim good news to Zion; raise your voice with strength, you who proclaim good news to Jerusalem; raise it up, do not be afraid. Say to the cities of Judah, "Here is your God;

**40:10** look, the Lord God will come with strength, and His arm will rule; look, His reward is with Him, and His work is before Him.

**40:11** Like a shepherd He will feed His flock; in His arm He will gather the lambs, and lift them up in His bosom; those with young He Himself will carry."

**40:12** Who has measured the waters in a cupped hand, and weighed the heavens with a span? Who has weighed out the mass of the earth with three fingers, and balanced the mountains with a weight, and the hills on a scale?

**40:13** Who has assisted the Spirit of the Lord? Or who has been His counselor and shown Him?

**40:14** With whom did He take counsel, who instructed Him, and taught Him the path of justice, and educated Him in knowledge, and showed Him the way of prudence?

**40:15** Look, the nations are like a drop of a bucket, and have been counted like the smallest weight on a scale; look, the islands are like tiny dust.

**40:16** And Lebanon will not be enough for kindling, and its animals will not be enough for a burnt offering.

**40:17** All nations are as if they did not exist before Him, and by Him they have been counted as nothing and emptiness.

**40:18** To whom, then, have you made God similar? Or what image will you set for Him?

**40:19** Has a craftsman cast a carved image? Or has a goldsmith shaped it with gold, and a silversmith with silver plates?

**40:20** He has chosen strong and undecaying wood; a skilled artisan seeks how to set up an idol that will not move.

**40:21** Do you not know? Have you not heard? Has it not been announced to you from the beginning? Have you not understood the foundations of the earth?

**40:22** He who sits above the circle of the earth, and its inhabitants are like locusts; who stretches out the heavens like nothing, and spreads them out like a tent to dwell in;

**40:23** who renders the searchers of secrets as if they did not exist; He has made the judges of the earth like emptiness.

**40:24** And indeed their stock is neither planted, nor sown, nor rooted in the earth; suddenly He blew upon them, and they dried up, and a whirlwind will carry them off like stubble.

**40:25** "And to whom have you likened Me, and made Me equal?" says the Holy One.

**40:26** Lift up your eyes on high and see who created these things: He who leads out their army by number and calls them all by name; because of the abundance of His strength and might and power, not even one was left behind.

**40:27** Why do you say, Jacob, and speak, Israel, "My way is hidden from the Lord, and my judgment has passed away from my God"?

**40:28** Do you not know, or have you not heard? The Lord is the everlasting God, who created the ends of the earth; He will not faint, nor will He grow tired, nor is there any searching out of His wisdom.

**40:29** It is He who gives power to the weary, and to those who are not, He multiplies strength and might.

**40:30** Boys will faint and grow tired, and young men will fall in weakness;

**40:31** but those who hope in the Lord will renew strength; they will take up wings like eagles; they will run and not grow tired, they will walk and not faint.

## Translator Notes

- **40:1** — The Vulgate pairs passive/middle consolamini with the vocative popule meus ("Take comfort, My people"), whereas the Hebrew is commonly taken transitively ("Comfort my people").
- **40:2** — Latin malitia ("wickedness") is a Vulgate-distinct reading where the Masoretic Text reads "her warfare" or "hard service."
- **40:8** — The Clementine Vulgate reads Domini nostri ("of our Lord"), whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads "of our God."
- **40:22** — Latin velut nihilum ("like nothing") is a Vulgate-distinct rendering where the Masoretic Text has ka-doq ("like a thin curtain/gauze").
- **40:23** — Latin secretorum scrutatores ("searchers of secrets") reflects Jerome's etymological rendering of Hebrew rozenim ("princes/rulers") via the root for "secret."
- **40:29** — Latin his qui non sunt ("to those who are not") is a distinctive Vulgate rendering where the Masoretic Text reads "to him who has no might."
