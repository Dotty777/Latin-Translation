## Text

**26:1** On that day this song will be sung in the land of Judah: "Zion is the city of our strength; a savior will be set in it as a wall and a bulwark.

**26:2** Open the gates, and let a righteous nation enter, keeping the truth.

**26:3** The old error has departed: You will keep peace; peace, because we have hoped in You.

**26:4** You have hoped in the Lord through eternal ages, in the Lord God, strong forever.

**26:5** For He will bow down those who dwell on high; He will humble the lofty city: He will humble it all the way to the earth, He will pull it down all the way to the dust.

**26:6** The foot will trample it, the feet of the poor, the steps of the needy."

**26:7** The path of the righteous is straight; straight is the footpath of the righteous for walking.

**26:8** And in the path of Your judgments, Lord, we have waited patiently for You: Your name and Your remembrance are in the desire of the soul.

**26:9** My soul has desired You in the night, and indeed with my spirit in my inmost heart I will keep watch for You from early morning. When You carry out Your judgments on the earth, the inhabitants of the world will learn righteousness.

**26:10** Let us show mercy to the wicked, and he will not learn righteousness; in the land of the saints he has done unjust things, and he will not see the glory of the Lord.

**26:11** Lord, let Your hand be lifted up, and let them not see; let the envious peoples see and be put to shame, and let fire devour Your enemies.

**26:12** Lord, You will give peace to us: for You have worked all our works for us.

**26:13** Lord our God, lords besides You have possessed us; in You alone let us remember Your name.

**26:14** Let the dying not live, let the giants not rise again: therefore You have visited and crushed them, and You have destroyed all memory of them.

**26:15** You have shown indulgence to the nation, Lord, You have shown indulgence to the nation: have You been glorified? You have extended far all the borders of the earth.

**26:16** Lord, in anguish they have sought You; in the tribulation of murmuring Your instruction was for them.

**26:17** Just as she who conceives, when she draws near to childbirth, cries out in pain in her pangs, so have we become before Your face, Lord.

**26:18** We have conceived, and as it were been in labor, and have given birth to wind. We have not brought about deliverances on the earth; therefore the inhabitants of the earth have not fallen.

**26:19** Your dead will live, those of mine who were killed will rise again. Awake and give praise, you who dwell in the dust, for Your dew is a dew of light, and You will pull down the land of the giants into ruin.

**26:20** Go, My people, enter into your inner rooms; shut your doors upon yourself, hide yourself for a brief moment, until the indignation passes by.

**26:21** For look, the Lord will go out from His place to visit the iniquity of the inhabitant of the earth against him; and the earth will reveal its blood, and will no longer cover those who were killed upon it.

## Translator Notes

- **26:1** — Vulgate explicitly names "Zion" (Sion) and reads personal "a savior" (salvator) where the Hebrew has "We have a strong city; He sets salvation as walls and rampart."
- **26:3** — Vulgate reads "The old error has departed" (Vetus error abiit) for the Hebrew "a steadfast mind"; in 26:4 Vulgate likewise uses the past indicative "You have hoped" (Sperastis) where the Hebrew has an imperative ("Trust").
- **26:11** — Vulgate renders the clause with subjunctives ("let Your hand be lifted up, and let them not see") and "the envious peoples" (zelantes populi) where the Hebrew has "Your hand is lifted up, yet they do not see; let them see Your zeal for the people."
- **26:15** — Vulgate reads "You have shown indulgence to the nation" (Indulsisti genti) and frames the next clause as a rhetorical question ("have You been glorified?", numquid glorificatus es?), where the Hebrew reads "You have increased the nation ... You are glorified."
- **26:19** — Vulgate reads "You will pull down the land of the giants into ruin" (terram gigantum detrahes in ruinam) where the Hebrew has "the earth will give birth to the shades"; gigantes ("giants," as in 26:14) renders Hebrew rephaim.
