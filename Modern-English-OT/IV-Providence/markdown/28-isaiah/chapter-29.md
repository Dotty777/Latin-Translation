## Text

**29:1** Woe to Ariel, Ariel, the city that David conquered! Year has been added to year; the festivals have run their course.

**29:2** And I will besiege Ariel with a rampart, and she will be sorrowful and mourning, and she will be to Me like Ariel.

**29:3** And I will encircle like a sphere all around you, and I will cast up an embankment against you, and I will set fortifications for your siege.

**29:4** You will be brought low; from the earth you will speak, and from the ground your speech will be heard; and your voice will be like that of a divining spirit from the earth, and from the ground your speech will mutter.

**29:5** And the multitude of those winnowing you will be like fine dust, and like passing ash the multitude of those who prevailed against you;

**29:6** and it will happen suddenly, at once. She will be visited by the Lord of hosts with thunder, and shaking of the earth, and a great voice of whirlwind and tempest, and of a flame of devouring fire.

**29:7** And the multitude of all the nations that fought against Ariel, and all who waged war, and besieged, and prevailed against her, will be like a dream of a night vision.

**29:8** And just as one who is hungry dreams, and eats, yet when he has awakened, his soul is empty; and just as one who is thirsty dreams and drinks, and after he has awakened, he is weary and still thirsty, and his soul is empty: so will be the multitude of all the nations that fought against Mount Zion.

**29:9** Be astounded and marvel; waver and totter; be drunk, and not from wine; stagger, and not from drunkenness.

**29:10** For the Lord has mixed for you a spirit of deep sleep; He will close your eyes; He will cover your prophets and your leaders, who see visions.

**29:11** And the vision of all will be to you like the words of a sealed book, which, when they give it to one who knows letters, they will say, "Read this," and he will answer, "I cannot, for it is sealed."

**29:12** And the book will be given to one who does not know letters, and it will be said to him, "Read," and he will answer, "I do not know letters."

**29:13** And the Lord said: "Because this people draws near with its mouth, and with its lips glorifies Me, yet its heart is far from Me, and they have feared Me through the commandment and teachings of men,

**29:14** therefore, look, I will proceed to cause astonishment to this people with a great and astounding wonder; for wisdom will perish from its wise ones, and the understanding of its prudent ones will be hidden."

**29:15** Woe to you who are deep in heart, to hide counsel from the Lord; whose works are in darkness, and they say, "Who sees us? And who knows us?"

**29:16** This thought of yours is perverse, as if the clay should think against the potter, and the work should say to its maker, "You did not make me," and the thing formed should say to the one who formed it, "You do not understand."

**29:17** Will not Lebanon, yet in a little while and in a short time, be turned into Carmel, and Carmel be reckoned as a forest?

**29:18** And in that day the deaf will hear the words of the book, and out of darkness and gloom the eyes of the blind will see.

**29:19** And the meek will increase joy in the Lord, and poor people will exult in the Holy One of Israel;

**29:20** for the one who was prevailing has come to an end, the mocker is finished, and all who were watching over iniquity have been cut down,

**29:21** who were making people sin by a word, and were tripping up the one who reproves at the gate, and turned aside in vain from the righteous one.

**29:22** Because of this, this is what the Lord, who redeemed Abraham, says to the house of Jacob: "Jacob will not now be put to shame, nor will his face now blush;

**29:23** rather, when he sees his children, the works of My hands in his midst, sanctifying My name, they will even sanctify the Holy One of Jacob, and they will proclaim the God of Israel;

**29:24** and those going astray in spirit will know understanding, and the mutterers will learn the law."

## Translator Notes

- **29:1** — Vulgate translates Hebrew chanah ("encamped") as expugnavit ("conquered") and renders the following verb in the passive indicative (additus est, "has been added") rather than as an imperative.
- **29:3** — Vulgate renders Hebrew kaddur ("in a circle") literally as quasi sphaeram ("like a sphere").
- **29:5** — Vulgate translates Hebrew zarayik ("your strangers/foes") from the root for winnowing as ventilantium te ("those winnowing you"), and mots ("chaff") as favilla ("ash").
- **29:17** — Vulgate leaves the Hebrew term carmel ("orchard" or "fruitful field," also the name of Mount Carmel) untranslated alongside Libanus (Lebanon).
- **29:23** — Vulgate renders Hebrew ya'aritsu ("they will stand in awe of") as praedicabunt ("they will proclaim"), and in 29:24 renders leqach ("instruction") as legem ("the law").
