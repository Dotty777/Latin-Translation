## Text

**22:1** The burden of the Valley of Vision. What is the matter with you also, that you too have all gone up onto the rooftops?

**22:2** Full of clamor, crowded city, exulting city: those of you who were killed were not killed by the sword, nor did they die in war.

**22:3** All your princes fled together and were harshly bound; all who were found were fettered together; they fled far away.

**22:4** Therefore I said: "Withdraw from me; I will weep bitterly; do not insist on comforting me over the devastation of the daughter of my people,

**22:5** for it is a day of slaughter, and of trampling, and of weeping, from the Lord God of hosts, in the Valley of Vision, searching the wall and magnificent upon the mountain."

**22:6** And Elam took up the quiver, the chariot of a mounted man, and the shield laid bare the wall.

**22:7** And your choice valleys will be full of chariots, and horsemen will set their stations at the gate.

**22:8** And the covering of Judah will be uncovered, and on that day you will see the armory of the house of the forest.

**22:9** And you will see the breaches of the city of David, that they have multiplied; and you gathered the waters of the lower pool,

**22:10** and you counted the houses of Jerusalem, and you tore down houses to fortify the wall.

**22:11** And you made a reservoir between the two walls for the water of the old pool; and you did not look up to Him who had made it, and you did not see its Maker from afar.

**22:12** And on that day the Lord God of hosts will call to weeping, and to mourning, to baldness, and to a belt of sackcloth;

**22:13** and look, joy and gladness, killing calves and slaughtering rams, eating meat and drinking wine: "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we will die."

**22:14** And the voice of the Lord of hosts was revealed in my ears: "Certainly this iniquity will not be forgiven you until you die," says the Lord God of hosts.

**22:15** This is what the Lord God of hosts says: "Go, enter to him who dwells in the tabernacle, to Shebna, the overseer of the temple, and you will say to him:

**22:16** 'What do you have here, or as who are you here? For you have carved out a tomb for yourself here; you have diligently carved out a monument on high, a tabernacle for yourself in the rock.

**22:17** Look, the Lord will cause you to be carried away, just as a rooster is carried away; and like a cloak, so He will lift you up.

**22:18** He will crown you with crowns, with tribulation; like a ball He will throw you into a wide and spacious land; there you will die, and there will be the chariot of your glory, the disgrace of the house of your master.

**22:19** And I will drive you out from your station, and from your office I will depose you.

**22:20** And it will be on that day: I will call My servant Eliakim, the son of Hilkiah,

**22:21** and I will clothe him with your tunic, and with your belt I will strengthen him, and I will give your authority into his hand; and he will be like a father to those who inhabit Jerusalem and to the house of Judah.

**22:22** And I will place the key of the house of David upon his shoulder; and he will open, and there will be no one to shut; and he will shut, and there will be no one to open.

**22:23** And I will fasten him as a peg in a trustworthy place, and he will become a throne of glory for the house of his father.

**22:24** And they will hang upon him all the glory of the house of his father: various kinds of vessels, every small vessel, from the vessels of bowls to every vessel of musicians.'"

**22:25** "On that day," says the Lord of hosts, "the peg that had been fastened in a trustworthy place will be taken away, and it will be broken, and it will fall, and what had hung upon it will perish," for the Lord has spoken.

## Translator Notes

- **22:5** — A Vulgate-distinct reading: scrutans murum, et magnificus super montem ("searching the wall and magnificent upon the mountain"), whereas the Hebrew depicts breaking down walls and crying out to the mountain.
- **22:6** — The Vulgate renders parietem nudavit clypeus ("the shield laid bare the wall"), translating Hebrew Qir as the common noun "wall" (parietem) and making "shield" (clypeus) the subject rather than "Kir uncovered the shield."
- **22:14** — The Latin uses the literal Semitic oath idiom Si dimittetur ("If it will be forgiven..."), an elliptical curse-oath expressing emphatic negation ("Certainly it will not be forgiven"), and distinctly includes vox ("the voice") of the Lord of hosts.
- **22:15** — The Vulgate renders Shebna's title as qui habitat in tabernaculo ("who dwells in the tabernacle") and praepositum templi ("the overseer of the temple"), whereas the Hebrew refers to the royal steward over the palace.
- **22:17** — The Vulgate distinctly renders Hebrew gaver in its later sense as gallus gallinaceus ("rooster") rather than "strong man," and in 22:18 reads Coronas coronabit te tribulatione ("He will crown you with crowns, with tribulation").
