## Text

**5:1** I will sing to my beloved the song of my cousin concerning His vineyard. A vineyard was made for my beloved on a horn, the son of oil.

**5:2** And He hedged it in, and picked the stones out of it, and planted it as a choice vineyard; and He built a tower in the midst of it, and constructed a winepress in it; and He waited for it to produce grapes, and it produced wild grapes.

**5:3** Now therefore, inhabitants of Jerusalem and men of Judah, judge between Me and My vineyard.

**5:4** What is there that I ought to have done further for My vineyard, and did not do for it? Or was it that I waited for it to produce grapes, and it produced wild grapes?

**5:5** And now I will show you what I will do to My vineyard: I will take away its hedge, and it will be for plundering; I will tear down its wall, and it will be for trampling.

**5:6** And I will make it desolate; it will not be pruned and it will not be dug; and briers and thorns will come up, and I will command the clouds that they rain no rain upon it.

**5:7** For the vineyard of the Lord of hosts is the house of Israel, and the man of Judah is His delightful shoot; and I waited for it to produce judgment, and look, iniquity; and righteousness, and look, an outcry.

**5:8** Woe to you who join house to house, and link field to field up to the boundary of the place! Will you alone dwell in the midst of the land?

**5:9** "These things are in My ears," says the Lord of hosts, "unless many houses become desolate, great and beautiful, without an inhabitant.

**5:10** For ten acres of vineyards will produce one small flask, and thirty measures of seed will produce three measures."

**5:11** Woe to you who rise up in the morning to pursue drunkenness, and to drink until evening, so that you are inflamed with wine!

**5:12** Harp and lyre, tambourine and flute, and wine are at your banquets; and you do not regard the work of the Lord, nor do you consider the works of His hands.

**5:13** Therefore My people have been led captive, because they had no knowledge, and their nobles have perished from hunger, and their multitude has become parched with thirst.

**5:14** Therefore hell has enlarged its soul, and has opened its mouth without any limit; and her mighty ones, and her people, and her lofty and glorious ones will go down to it.

**5:15** And mankind will be bowed down, and man will be humbled, and the eyes of the lofty will be cast down.

**5:16** And the Lord of hosts will be exalted in judgment; and the holy God will be sanctified in righteousness.

**5:17** And lambs will graze according to their order, and strangers will eat the desolate places that have been turned into fertility.

**5:18** Woe to you who drag iniquity with cords of vanity, and sin as with a cart rope,

**5:19** who say, "Let His work hurry and come quickly, so that we may see; and let the counsel of the Holy One of Israel draw near and come, and we will know it!"

**5:20** Woe to you who call evil good, and good evil; setting darkness as light, and light as darkness; setting bitter for sweet, and sweet for bitter!

**5:21** Woe to you who are wise in your own eyes, and discerning before yourselves.

**5:22** Woe to you who are powerful for drinking wine, and mighty men for mixing drunkenness,

**5:23** who justify the wicked for bribes, and take away the righteousness of the righteous man from him!

**5:24** Because of this, just as a tongue of fire devours stubble and the heat of the flame burns it up, so their root will be like ashes, and their shoot will rise like dust; for they have cast away the law of the Lord of hosts, and have blasphemed the speech of the Holy One of Israel.

**5:25** Therefore the fury of the Lord grew angry against His people, and He stretched out His hand over them and struck them; and the mountains were shaken, and their corpses became like dung in the middle of the streets. In all these things His fury has not turned away, but His hand is still stretched out.

**5:26** And He will lift up a standard among the nations far away, and He will whistle to it from the ends of the earth; and look, in haste it will come swiftly.

**5:27** There is none fainting nor growing weary in it; it will neither slumber nor sleep, neither will the belt of its loins be loosened, nor will the strap of its sandal be broken.

**5:28** Its arrows are sharp, and all its bows are drawn. The hooves of its horses are like flint, and its wheels are like the rush of a tempest.

**5:29** Its roar is like a lion's; it will roar like young lions; and it will snarl, and grasp the prey, and embrace it, and there will be no one to rescue.

**5:30** And it will resound over them on that day like the sound of the sea; we will look upon the land, and look, darkness of distress, and the light has been darkened in its gloom.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Latin 'patruelis mei' ('of my cousin') translates a Hebrew kinsman term often rendered 'my beloved,' and 'in cornu filio olei' ('on a horn, the son of oil') preserves the Hebrew idiom for a fertile hilltop.
- **5:7** — Latin 'exspectavi' ('I waited') uses the first person singular directly as the Lord's voice, whereas the Hebrew has the third person ('he looked').
- **5:9** — Latin 'nisi' ('unless') literally renders the Hebrew elliptical oath formula meaning 'surely,' and in 5:10 Jerome converts the Hebrew measures into Roman equivalents ('jugera,' 'lagunculam,' and 'modii').
- **5:17** — Latin 'deserta in ubertatem versa' ('the desolate places that have been turned into fertility') is a distinctly Vulgate rendering where the Hebrew reads 'the ruins of the fat ones.'
- **5:25** — The Clementine Vulgate prints 'adversus' as an orthographic variant for 'aversus' ('turned away').
