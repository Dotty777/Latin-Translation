## Text

**2:1** The Lord says this: "For three crimes of Moab, and for four, I will not turn it back, because it burned the bones of the king of Idumea to ashes.

**2:2** And I will send fire into Moab, and it will devour the dwellings of Kerioth; and Moab will die with uproar, with the blare of the trumpet.

**2:3** And I will destroy the judge from its midst, and I will kill all its princes with him," says the Lord.

**2:4** The Lord says this: "For three crimes of Judah, and for four, I will not turn it back, because it rejected the law of the Lord and did not keep His commandments; for their idols had deceived them, after which their fathers had gone.

**2:5** And I will send fire into Judah, and it will devour the dwellings of Jerusalem."

**2:6** The Lord says this: "For three crimes of Israel, and for four, I will not turn it back, because it sold the righteous for silver, and the poor for sandals.

**2:7** They crush the heads of the poor upon the dust of the earth, and turn aside the way of the humble; and a son and his father went to the girl, to violate My holy name.

**2:8** And upon pledged garments they reclined beside every altar, and they were drinking the wine of the condemned in the house of their God.

**2:9** Yet I wiped out the Amorite from before their face, whose height was the height of cedars, and he himself was strong like an oak; and I crushed his fruit from above, and his roots beneath.

**2:10** It is I who made you come up from the land of Egypt, and led you in the desert for forty years, so that you might possess the land of the Amorite.

**2:11** And I raised up prophets from your sons, and Nazirites from your young men. Is it not so, sons of Israel?" says the Lord.

**2:12** "And you will give the Nazirites wine to drink, and you will command the prophets, saying, 'Do not prophesy.'

**2:13** Look, I will creak beneath you, just as a wagon loaded with hay creaks.

**2:14** And flight will perish from the swift, and the strong will not retain his strength, and the mighty will not save his life;

**2:15** and the one holding the bow will not stand, and the one swift on his feet will not be saved, and the rider of a horse will not save his life;

**2:16** and the mighty in heart among the strong will flee naked in that day," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **2:1** — Latin usque ad cinerem reads "to ashes," whereas the Hebrew text reads "to lime."
- **2:4** — Latin idola sua reads "their idols," a Vulgate-distinct rendering where the Hebrew text reads "their lies."
- **2:12** — The Clementine Vulgate uses future-tense verbs (propinabitis, mandabitis, "you will give ... to drink," "you will command"), whereas the Hebrew text uses past-tense verbs.
- **2:13** — Latin stridebo subter vos, sicut stridet plaustrum onustum foeno ("I will creak beneath you, just as a wagon loaded with hay creaks") preserves a distinctively Vulgate wording where Hebrew refers to pressing down or groaning like a cart loaded with sheaves.
