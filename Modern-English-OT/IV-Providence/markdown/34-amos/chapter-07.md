## Text

**7:1** The Lord God showed me these things: and look, a maker of the locust at the beginning of the sprouting shoots of the late rain, and look, the late rain after the king's shearing.

**7:2** And it happened, when it had finished eating the grass of the land, I said, "Lord God, be merciful, I plead; who will raise up Jacob, for he is very small?"

**7:3** The Lord had compassion over this: "It will not be," said the Lord.

**7:4** The Lord God showed me these things: and look, the Lord God was calling judgment to fire; and it devoured the vast abyss, and ate the portion at the same time.

**7:5** And I said, "Lord God, cease, I plead; who will raise up Jacob, for he is very small?"

**7:6** The Lord had compassion over this: "Even this will not be," said the Lord God.

**7:7** The Lord showed me these things: and look, the Lord standing upon a plastered wall, and in His hand a mason's trowel.

**7:8** And the Lord said to me, "What do you see, Amos?" And I said, "A mason's trowel." And the Lord said, "Look, I will place a trowel in the midst of My people Israel; I will no longer plaster over them.

**7:9** And the high places of the idol will be demolished, and the sanctuaries of Israel will be made desolate, and I will rise up against the house of Jeroboam with the sword."

**7:10** And Amaziah, the priest of Bethel, sent to Jeroboam, king of Israel, saying, "Amos has rebelled against you in the midst of the house of Israel; the land will not be able to bear all his words.

**7:11** For Amos says these things: 'Jeroboam will die by the sword, and Israel will go away captive from its land.'"

**7:12** And Amaziah said to Amos, "You who see, go: flee into the land of Judah, and eat bread there, and you will prophesy there.

**7:13** And in Bethel you will no longer prophesy, for it is the king's sanctuary, and it is the house of the kingdom."

**7:14** And Amos answered and said to Amaziah, "I am not a prophet, and I am not a son of a prophet, but I am a herdsman, plucking sycamores.

**7:15** And the Lord took me while I was following the flock, and the Lord said to me, 'Go, prophesy to My people Israel.'

**7:16** And now hear the word of the Lord: you say, 'You will not prophesy against Israel, and you will not drip upon the house of the idol.'

**7:17** Because of this, the Lord says these things: 'Your wife will act as a prostitute in the city, and your sons and your daughters will fall by the sword, and your ground will be measured with a line; and you will die in a polluted land, and Israel will go away captive from its land.'"

## Translator Notes

- **7:1** — Latin reads "maker of the locust" (fictor locustae) and "late rain" (serotini imbris ... serotinus), whereas the Hebrew refers to forming locusts as the late crop begins to sprout; tonsionem can mean either shearing flocks or mowing grass.
- **7:2** — Latin reads "who will raise up Jacob" (quis suscitabit Jacob, also in 7:5) and "had compassion" (misertus est, 7:3, 6), where the Hebrew has "how can Jacob stand?" and "relented."
- **7:7** — Latin reads "plastered wall" (murum litum) and "mason's trowel" (trulla caementarii), continuing the plastering image in 7:8 with "plaster over" (superinducere), where the Hebrew speaks of a plumb line and passing by or sparing.
- **7:9** — Latin reads "of the idol" (idoli, and "house of the idol" in 7:16), where the Hebrew has the name "Isaac."
