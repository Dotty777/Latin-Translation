## Text

**9:1** I saw the Lord standing upon the altar, and He said, "Strike the hinge, and let the lintels shake: for greed is on the head of all, and the last of them I will kill with the sword; there will be no flight for them. They will flee, and the one who flees from among them will not be saved.

**9:2** If they descend all the way to hell, from there My hand will bring them out; and if they ascend all the way to heaven, from there I will pull them down.

**9:3** And if they are hidden on the summit of Carmel, from there, searching, I will take them away; and if they conceal themselves from My eyes in the depth of the sea, there I will command the serpent, and it will bite them.

**9:4** And if they go away into captivity before their enemies, there I will command the sword, and it will kill them; and I will set My eyes upon them for evil, and not for good."

**9:5** And the Lord God of hosts, who touches the earth, and it will melt, and all who dwell in it will mourn; and all of it will rise like a stream, and flow down like the river of Egypt.

**9:6** He who builds His ascent in heaven, and has founded His bundle upon the earth; who calls the waters of the sea, and pours them out upon the face of the earth: the Lord is His name.

**9:7** "Are you not like the sons of the Ethiopians to Me, sons of Israel?" says the Lord. "Did I not bring Israel up from the land of Egypt, and the Philistines from Cappadocia, and the Syrians from Cyrene?"

**9:8** "Look, the eyes of the Lord God are upon the sinning kingdom, and I will crush it from the face of the earth; yet I will not completely crush the house of Jacob," says the Lord.

**9:9** "For look, I Myself will command, and I will shake the house of Israel among all the nations, just as wheat is shaken in a sieve, and not a pebble will fall to the ground.

**9:10** All the sinners of My people will die by the sword, who say, 'Evil will not draw near, and will not come upon us.'"

**9:11** "In that day I will raise up the tent of David that has fallen, and I will rebuild the openings of its walls, and I will restore the things that had collapsed; and I will rebuild it as in ancient days,

**9:12** so that they may possess the remnant of Idumea and all the nations, because My name has been called upon them," says the Lord who does these things.

**9:13** "Look, days are coming," says the Lord, "and the plowman will overtake the reaper, and the treader of grapes the one casting seed; and the mountains will drip sweetness, and all the hills will be cultivated.

**9:14** And I will turn back the captivity of My people Israel; and they will build deserted cities, and inhabit them; and they will plant vineyards, and drink their wine; and they will make gardens, and eat their fruits.

**9:15** And I will plant them upon their own ground, and I will no longer uproot them from their land, which I have given to them," says the Lord your God.

## Translator Notes

- **9:1** — Latin reads "hinge" (cardinem), "lintels" (superliminaria), and "for greed is on the head of all" (avaritia enim in capite omnium), where the Hebrew speaks of striking the capitals so the thresholds shake and shattering them on the head of all.
- **9:6** — Latin reads "His bundle" (fasciculum suum), rendering a Hebrew term often translated "His vault" or "His band."
- **9:7** — The Vulgate names "Cappadocia" and "Cyrene" where the Hebrew reads Caphtor and Kir, and renders Hebrew Cushites as "Ethiopians" (Aethiopum).
- **9:12** — Latin reads "the remnant of Idumea" (reliquias Idumaeae, corresponding to Hebrew Edom), whereas the Septuagint cited in Acts 15:17 reads "the remnant of mankind."
- **9:13** — Latin reads "will drip sweetness" (stillabunt ... dulcedinem) and "all the hills will be cultivated" (omnes colli culti erunt), where the Hebrew speaks of dripping sweet wine and all the hills melting or flowing.
