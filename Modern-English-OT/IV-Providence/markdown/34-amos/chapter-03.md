## Text

**3:1** Hear the word that the Lord has spoken against you, sons of Israel, against the whole family that I brought out from the land of Egypt, saying:

**3:2** "You alone have I known out of all the families of the earth; therefore I will visit upon you all your iniquities."

**3:3** Will two walk together, unless they have agreed?

**3:4** Will a lion roar in the forest, unless it has prey? Will a lion cub give voice from its den, unless it has caught something?

**3:5** Will a bird fall into a snare on the ground without a bird-catcher? Will a snare be taken away from the ground before it has caught anything?

**3:6** Will a trumpet blare in a city, and the people not be terrified? Will there be evil in a city that the Lord has not done?

**3:7** For the Lord God does not do a thing, unless He has revealed His secret to His servants the prophets.

**3:8** A lion will roar; who will not fear? The Lord God has spoken; who will not prophesy?

**3:9** Make it heard in the dwellings of Ashdod, and in the dwellings of the land of Egypt, and say: "Assemble upon the mountains of Samaria, and see the many acts of madness in its midst, and those suffering oppression in its inner chambers."

**3:10** "And they did not know how to do what is right," says the Lord, "storing up iniquity and plunder in their dwellings."

**3:11** Therefore this is what the Lord God says: "The land will be afflicted and surrounded, and your strength will be stripped from you, and your dwellings will be plundered."

**3:12** This is what the Lord says: "Just as if a shepherd rescues from the mouth of a lion two legs, or the tip of an ear, so will the sons of Israel be rescued, who dwell in Samaria on the corner of a couch, and on a bed of Damascus."

**3:13** "Hear, and bear witness in the house of Jacob," says the Lord God of hosts;

**3:14** "for in the day when I begin to visit the transgressions of Israel, I will visit upon him, and upon the altars of Bethel; and the horns of the altar will be cut off, and they will fall to the ground.

**3:15** And I will strike the winter house with the summer house, and the ivory houses will perish, and many dwellings will be demolished," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **3:2** — Latin visitabo ("I will visit," also in 3:14) follows the biblical idiom of judicial inspection, meaning to call to account and punish.
- **3:5** — Latin absque aucupe ("without a bird-catcher") and auferetur laqueus ("will a snare be taken away") reflect Vulgate readings distinct from the Hebrew phrasing concerning a lure and a trap springing up.
- **3:6** — Latin malum ("evil") can denote calamity or misfortune as well as moral evil.
- **3:11** — Latin Tribulabitur et circuietur terra ("The land will be afflicted and surrounded") renders the Hebrew clause ("an adversary shall surround the land") as two passive verbs with "the land" as subject.
- **3:12** — Latin in Damasci grabato ("on a bed of Damascus") takes the difficult Hebrew phrase as naming Damascus rather than denoting silken cushions or part of a bed.
