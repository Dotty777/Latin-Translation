## Text

**4:1** Hear this word, fat cows who are on the mountain of Samaria, who defraud the needy and crush the poor, who say to your masters, "Bring it, and we will drink."

**4:2** The Lord God has sworn by His holiness, "Look, days will come upon you, and they will lift you up on poles, and your remnants in boiling pots.

**4:3** And you will go out through the openings, one opposite another, and you will be cast into Armon," says the Lord.

**4:4** "Come to Bethel and act wickedly; to Gilgal, and multiply transgression; and bring your sacrifices in the morning, your tithes every three days.

**4:5** And sacrifice a praise offering from what is leavened, and proclaim freewill offerings, and announce them; for so you have desired, sons of Israel," says the Lord God.

**4:6** "Therefore I also gave you numbness of teeth in all your cities, and lack of bread in all your places; yet you did not return to Me," says the Lord.

**4:7** "I also withheld the rain from you when three months still remained until the harvest; and I sent rain upon one city, and upon another city I did not send rain; one part was rained upon, and the part upon which I did not send rain dried up.

**4:8** And two and three cities came to one city to drink water, and they were not satisfied; yet you did not return to Me," says the Lord.

**4:9** "I struck you with a scorching wind and with mildew; the caterpillar devoured the multitude of your gardens and your vineyards, your olive groves and your fig orchards; yet you did not return to Me," says the Lord.

**4:10** "I sent death among you in the way of Egypt; I struck your young men with the sword, even to the capture of your horses, and I caused the stench of your camps to rise into your nostrils; yet you did not return to Me," says the Lord.

**4:11** "I overthrew you as God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah, and you became like a firebrand snatched from the blaze; yet you did not return to Me," says the Lord.

**4:12** "Therefore I will do these things to you, Israel; and after I have done these things to you, prepare yourself to meet your God, Israel."

**4:13** For look, He who forms the mountains, and creates the wind, and announces His speech to man, making the morning mist, and treading upon the heights of the earth: the Lord God of hosts is His name.

## Translator Notes

- **4:1** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew "cows of Bashan" as "fat cows" (vaccae pingues), interpreting the place name Bashan as denoting richness or fatness.
- **4:2** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew fishing imagery ("with hooks ... with fishhooks") as "on poles" (in contis) and "in boiling pots" (in ollis ferventibus).
- **4:3** — The Vulgate preserves "Armon" (in Armon), corresponding to the obscure Hebrew term Harmon, whose location and meaning are uncertain.
- **4:6** — Where the Hebrew has "cleanness of teeth" (an idiom for famine), the Vulgate reads "numbness of teeth" (stuporem dentium).
- **4:13** — The Vulgate reads "announces His speech to man" (annuntians homini eloquium suum) and "making the morning mist" (faciens matutinam nebulam), where the Hebrew has "declares to man what his thought is" and "who makes dawn into darkness."
