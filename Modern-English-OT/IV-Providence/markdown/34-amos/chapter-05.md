## Text

**5:1** Hear this word which I lift up over you as a lamentation: The house of Israel has fallen, and will not rise again.

**5:2** The virgin Israel has been cast down onto her own land; there is no one to raise her up.

**5:3** For this is what the Lord God says: "The city from which a thousand went out will have a hundred left in it, and the one from which a hundred went out will have ten left in it, in the house of Israel."

**5:4** For this is what the Lord says to the house of Israel: "Seek Me, and you will live.

**5:5** And do not seek Bethel, and do not enter Gilgal, and you will not cross over to Beersheba; for Gilgal will be led captive, and Bethel will be useless."

**5:6** Seek the Lord and live (otherwise the house of Joseph may be burned like fire, and it will devour, and there will be no one to quench Bethel,

**5:7** you who turn judgment into wormwood and leave righteousness on the ground),

**5:8** Him who makes Arcturus and Orion, and turns darkness into morning, and changes day into night; who calls the waters of the sea and pours them out over the face of the earth; the Lord is His name;

**5:9** who smiles destruction upon the strong and brings devastation upon the mighty.

**5:10** They have hated the one who rebukes in the gate, and they have detested the one who speaks perfectly.

**5:11** Therefore, because you were plundering the poor man and taking choice plunder from him, you will build houses of squared stone, and you will not live in them; you will plant most lovely vineyards, and you will not drink their wine.

**5:12** For I know your many crimes and your mighty sins: enemies of the righteous one, taking a bribe, and pressing down the poor in the gate.

**5:13** Therefore the prudent one will keep silent in that time, for the time is evil.

**5:14** Seek good and not evil, so that you may live; and the Lord God of hosts will be with you, just as you have said.

**5:15** Hate evil and love good, and establish judgment in the gate; perhaps the Lord God of hosts will have mercy on the remnant of Joseph.

**5:16** Therefore this is what the Lord God of hosts, the Ruler, says: "In all the public squares there will be lamentation, and in all places that are outside it will be said, 'Woe, woe!' And they will call the farmer to mourning, and to lamentation those who know how to lament.

**5:17** And in all the vineyards there will be lamentation, for I will pass through your midst," says the Lord.

**5:18** Woe to those who desire the day of the Lord! For what purpose is it to you? This day of the Lord is darkness, and not light.

**5:19** Just as if a man flees from the face of a lion, and a bear meets him; and he enters a house, and leans with his hand upon the wall, and a snake bites him.

**5:20** Is not the day of the Lord darkness, and not light, and gloom, and no brightness in it?

**5:21** "I hate and have cast aside your festivals, and I will not take in the aroma of your assemblies.

**5:22** And if you offer Me burnt offerings and your gifts, I will not accept them; and I will not look upon the vowed offerings of your fattened animals.

**5:23** Take away from Me the uproar of your songs, and I will not listen to the melodies of your lyre.

**5:24** And judgment will be revealed like water, and righteousness like a mighty torrent.

**5:25** Did you present to Me sacrifices and an offering in the desert for forty years, house of Israel?

**5:26** And you carried the tabernacle for your Moloch, and the image of your idols, the star of your god, which you made for yourselves.

**5:27** And I will cause you to go into exile beyond Damascus," says the Lord; God of hosts is His name.

## Translator Notes

- **5:1** — Following the Clementine Vulgate verse division and punctuation, "the house of Israel" begins the lament as the subject of "has fallen," whereas the Hebrew joins "house of Israel" to the opening summons and places "has fallen" in verse 2.
- **5:6** — The Vulgate renders the warning passively with "the house of Joseph" as subject ("may be burned like fire," comburatur ut ignis) and places verses 6b-7 in parentheses so that the accusative participles of verse 8 ("Him who makes...") connect directly to "the Lord" in verse 6.
- **5:8** — The Vulgate names the constellation "Arcturus" (Arcturum) where the Hebrew reads "Pleiades."
- **5:9** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew verb ("flashes") as "smiles" (subridet) and reads "the mighty" (potentem) where the Hebrew has "fortress."
- **5:24** — The Vulgate renders the Hebrew verb ("let roll down") as "will be revealed" (revelabitur), deriving it from the similar Hebrew root meaning "to uncover or reveal."
- **5:26** — The Vulgate reads "the tabernacle for your Moloch" (with dative Moloch vestro) and "the image of your idols," where the Hebrew names the deities "Sikkuth your king and Kiyyun your images."
