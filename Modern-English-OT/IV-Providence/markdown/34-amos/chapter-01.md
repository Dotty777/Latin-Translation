## Text

**1:1** The words of Amos, who was among the shepherds from Tekoa, which he saw concerning Israel in the days of Uzziah, king of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam, son of Joash, king of Israel, two years before the earthquake.

**1:2** And he said: "The Lord will roar from Zion, and from Jerusalem He will give His voice; and the beautiful places of the shepherds have mourned, and the summit of Carmel has dried up."

**1:3** This is what the Lord says: "For three crimes of Damascus, and for four, I will not turn it back, because they threshed Gilead with iron wagons.

**1:4** And I will send fire into the house of Hazael, and it will devour the houses of Ben-hadad.

**1:5** And I will break the bar of Damascus, and I will destroy the inhabitant from the plain of the idol, and the one holding the scepter from the house of pleasure; and the people of Syria will be carried away to Cyrene," says the Lord.

**1:6** This is what the Lord says: "For three crimes of Gaza, and for four, I will not turn it back, because they carried away a complete captivity, to shut it up in Idumea.

**1:7** And I will send fire upon the wall of Gaza, and it will devour its dwellings.

**1:8** And I will destroy the inhabitant from Ashdod, and the one holding the scepter from Ashkelon; and I will turn My hand against Ekron, and the rest of the Philistines will perish," says the Lord God.

**1:9** This is what the Lord says: "For three crimes of Tyre, and for four, I will not turn it back, because they shut up a complete captivity in Idumea, and did not remember the covenant of brothers.

**1:10** And I will send fire upon the wall of Tyre, and it will devour its dwellings."

**1:11** This is what the Lord says: "For three crimes of Edom, and for four, I will not turn it back, because he pursued his brother with the sword, and violated his mercy, and held his fury further, and kept his indignation until the end.

**1:12** I will send fire into Teman, and it will devour the dwellings of Bozrah."

**1:13** This is what the Lord says: "For three crimes of the sons of Ammon, and for four, I will not turn it back, because he cut open the pregnant women of Gilead to enlarge his border.

**1:14** And I will kindle a fire on the wall of Rabbah, and it will devour its dwellings with wailing in the day of war, and with a whirlwind in the day of upheaval.

**1:15** And Milcom will go into captivity, he and his princes together," says the Lord.

## Translator Notes

- **1:2** — Latin speciosa pastorum ("the beautiful places of the shepherds") translates Hebrew "pastures of the shepherds" via the root meaning "beautiful," and the Vulgate shifts to past-tense verbs (luxerunt, exsiccatus est) after the initial futures.
- **1:3** — Latin non convertam eum (literally "I will not turn him/it back") recurs across the oracles in 1:3-2:6 and may denote either not revoking the decreed punishment or not turning the offending nation back to repentance.
- **1:5** — The Vulgate translates the Hebrew place names Biq'at-Aven and Beth-Eden literally as "the plain of the idol" (campo idoli) and "the house of pleasure" (domo voluptatis), and renders Hebrew Kir as Cyrenen (Cyrene).
- **1:6** — Here and in 1:9 and 2:1 the Vulgate uses the Greco-Roman territorial name Idumaea (Idumea) for Hebrew Edom, whereas 1:11 uses Edom.
- **1:15** — The Vulgate reads the proper name Melchom (Milcom, the national deity of the Ammonites), whereas the Masoretic vocalization reads "their king."
