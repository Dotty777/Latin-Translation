## Text

**8:1** The Lord God showed me these things: and look, a hook for fruit.

**8:2** And He said, "What do you see, Amos?" And I said, "A hook for fruit." And the Lord said to me, "The end has come upon My people Israel; I will no longer pass by them."

**8:3** "And the hinges of the temple will creak in that day," says the Lord God: "Many will die; in every place silence will be cast."

**8:4** Hear this, you who crush the poor, and cause the needy of the land to fail,

**8:5** saying, "When will the month pass, and we will sell merchandise? And the Sabbath, and we will open the grain, so that we may diminish the measure, and increase the shekel, and substitute deceitful scales,

**8:6** so that we may possess the needy for silver and the poor for sandals, and sell the refuse of the grain?"

**8:7** The Lord has sworn by the pride of Jacob: "Surely I will not forget all their works to the end."

**8:8** Will the land not tremble over this, and every inhabitant of it mourn, and all of it rise like a river, and be cast out, and flow down like the stream of Egypt?

**8:9** "And it will be in that day," says the Lord God, "the sun will set at noon, and I will make the earth grow dark on a day of light;

**8:10** and I will turn your festivals into mourning, and all your songs into lamentation, and I will bring sackcloth upon every back of yours, and baldness upon every head; and I will make it like the mourning for an only son, and its end like a bitter day."

**8:11** "Look, days are coming," the Lord will say, "and I will send a famine into the land: not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but of hearing the word of the Lord."

**8:12** And they will stagger from sea to sea, and from the north to the east; they will go around seeking the word of the Lord, and will not find it.

**8:13** In that day the beautiful virgins and the young men will faint from thirst,

**8:14** who swear by the offense of Samaria, and say, "Your god lives, Dan, and the way of Beersheba lives"; and they will fall, and will not rise up again.

## Translator Notes

- **8:1** — Latin reads "a hook for fruit" (uncinus pomorum, also in 8:2), whereas the Hebrew refers to a basket of summer fruit.
- **8:3** — Latin reads "the hinges of the temple will creak" (stridebunt cardines templi), where the Hebrew reads "the songs of the temple/palace will wail."
- **8:7** — "Surely I will not forget" renders the literal Latin conditional oath Si oblitus fuero ("If I have forgotten [may it not be]"), and "to the end" (usque ad finem) renders the Hebrew for "forever."
- **8:11** — The Clementine Vulgate reads the future tense dicet Dominus ("the Lord will say") rather than the present tense "says the Lord."
