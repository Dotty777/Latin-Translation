## Text

**3:1** King Nebuchadnezzar made a golden statue, sixty cubits in height and six cubits in width, and set it up on the plain of Dura, in the province of Babylon.

**3:2** Therefore King Nebuchadnezzar sent to assemble the satraps, magistrates, and judges, the commanders, rulers, and prefects, and all the princes of the regions, so that they might come together for the dedication of the statue that King Nebuchadnezzar had erected.

**3:3** Then the satraps, magistrates, and judges, the commanders, rulers, and nobles who had been appointed to offices of authority, and all the princes of the regions were assembled, so that they might come together for the dedication of the statue that King Nebuchadnezzar had erected. And they were standing before the statue that King Nebuchadnezzar had set in place.

**3:4** And a herald cried out loudly, "To you it is declared, peoples, tribes, and languages:

**3:5** at the hour in which you hear the sound of the trumpet, the pipe, the lyre, the harp, the psaltery, and the ensemble, and of every kind of music, fall down and worship the golden statue that King Nebuchadnezzar has set up.

**3:6** But if anyone does not fall prostrate and worship, in that very hour he will be thrown into the furnace of burning fire."

**3:7** Therefore, after these things, as soon as all the peoples heard the sound of the trumpet, the pipe, the lyre, the harp, the psaltery, and the ensemble, and of all kinds of music, all the peoples, tribes, and languages fell down and worshiped the golden statue that King Nebuchadnezzar had set up.

**3:8** And immediately at that very time, Chaldean men came forward and accused the Jews.

**3:9** And they said to King Nebuchadnezzar, "King, live forever!

**3:10** You, king, laid down a decree that every person who hears the sound of the trumpet, the pipe, the lyre, the harp, the psaltery, and the ensemble, and of every kind of music, should prostrate himself and worship the golden statue;

**3:11** and if anyone does not fall down and worship, he is to be thrown into the furnace of burning fire.

**3:12** Now there are Jewish men whom you appointed over the affairs of the region of Babylon: Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. These men, king, have despised your decree; they do not revere your gods, and they do not worship the golden statue that you have erected."

**3:13** Then Nebuchadnezzar, in fury and in anger, commanded that Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego be brought in; and immediately they were brought before the king.

**3:14** And King Nebuchadnezzar, speaking out, said to them, "Is it true, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, that you do not revere my gods and do not worship the golden statue that I have set up?

**3:15** Now therefore, if you are ready, at whatever hour you hear the sound of the trumpet, the pipe, the lyre, the harp, the psaltery, and the ensemble, and of all kinds of music, prostrate yourselves and worship the statue that I have made. But if you do not worship, in that very hour you will be thrown into the furnace of burning fire; and who is the god who will rescue you from my hand?"

**3:16** Answering, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego said to King Nebuchadnezzar, "It is not necessary for us to answer you concerning this matter.

**3:17** For look, our God, whom we revere, is able to rescue us from the furnace of burning fire and to deliver us from your hands, king.

**3:18** But if He is unwilling, let it be known to you, king, that we do not revere your gods, and we do not worship the golden statue that you have erected."

**3:19** Then Nebuchadnezzar was filled with fury, and the look of his face was altered toward Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego; and he commanded that the furnace be heated seven times more than it was accustomed to be heated.

**3:20** And he ordered the strongest men from his army to bind the feet of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego and throw them into the furnace of burning fire.

**3:21** And immediately those men, bound, with their trousers, turbans, shoes, and garments, were thrown into the middle of the furnace of burning fire.

**3:22** For the king's order was urgent, and the furnace had been heated excessively; furthermore, the flame of the fire killed those men who had thrown in Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego.

**3:23** But these three men, that is, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, fell into the middle of the furnace of burning fire, bound fast.

**3:24** And they were walking in the middle of the flame, praising God and blessing the Lord.

**3:25** And Azariah, standing, prayed in this way, and opening his mouth in the middle of the fire, he said:

**3:26** "Blessed are You, Lord God of our fathers, and praiseworthy and glorious is Your name forever,

**3:27** for You are just in all that You have done to us, and all Your works are true, and Your ways are right, and all Your judgments are true.

**3:28** For You have executed true judgments according to all that You have brought upon us and upon the holy city of our fathers, Jerusalem; for in truth and in judgment You have brought all these things because of our sins.

**3:29** For we have sinned, and have acted wickedly in departing from You, and have transgressed in all things;

**3:30** and we have not listened to Your commandments, nor observed them, nor done as You had commanded us so that it might go well with us.

**3:31** Therefore, all that You have brought upon us, and everything that You have done to us, You have done in true judgment;

**3:32** and You have handed us over into the hands of our enemies, who are wicked, utterly evil, and transgressors, and to an unjust king, the most evil beyond all the earth.

**3:33** And now we cannot open our mouth; we have become a shame and a reproach to Your servants and to those who revere You.

**3:34** Do not, we plead, hand us over forever for the sake of Your name, and do not annul Your covenant,

**3:35** and do not take away Your mercy from us, for the sake of Abraham Your beloved, and Isaac Your servant, and Israel Your holy one,

**3:36** to whom You spoke, promising that You would multiply their seed like the stars of heaven and like the sand that is on the shore of the sea;

**3:37** for, Lord, we have been diminished more than all the nations, and we are lowly throughout the whole earth today because of our sins.

**3:38** And at this time there is no prince, or leader, or prophet, nor burnt offering, nor sacrifice, nor oblation, nor incense, nor place of firstfruits before You,

**3:39** so that we might find Your mercy; yet with a contrite soul and a spirit of humility may we be accepted.

**3:40** As with a burnt offering of rams and bulls, and as with thousands of fat lambs, so let our sacrifice be in Your sight today, that it may please You, for there is no shame for those who trust in You.

**3:41** And now we follow You with our whole heart; and we fear You, and we seek Your face.

**3:42** Do not put us to shame, but deal with us according to Your gentleness and according to the multitude of Your mercy.

**3:43** And rescue us by Your wonders, and give glory to Your name, Lord;

**3:44** and let all who show evils to Your servants be put to shame; let them be put to shame by all Your power, and let their strength be crushed;

**3:45** and let them know that You alone are the Lord God, and glorious over the whole world."

**3:46** And the king's attendants who had thrown them in did not stop heating the furnace with naphtha, tow, pitch, and brushwood,

**3:47** and the flame poured out forty-nine cubits above the furnace,

**3:48** and it burst out and burned those of the Chaldeans whom it found near the furnace.

**3:49** But the angel of the Lord descended with Azariah and his companions into the furnace, and drove the flame of the fire out of the furnace,

**3:50** and made the middle of the furnace like a blowing wind of dew, and the fire did not touch them at all, nor distress them, nor bring any trouble upon them.

**3:51** Then these three, as from one mouth, were praising, and glorifying, and blessing God in the furnace, saying:

**3:52** "Blessed are You, Lord God of our fathers, and praiseworthy, and glorious, and supremely exalted forever. And blessed is the holy name of Your glory, and praiseworthy and supremely exalted throughout all ages.

**3:53** Blessed are You in the holy temple of Your glory, and supremely praiseworthy and supremely glorious forever.

**3:54** Blessed are You on the throne of Your kingdom, and supremely praiseworthy and supremely exalted forever.

**3:55** Blessed are You, who look upon the depths and sit above the cherubim, and praiseworthy and supremely exalted forever.

**3:56** Blessed are You in the expanse of heaven, and praiseworthy and glorious forever.

**3:57** Bless the Lord, all works of the Lord; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:58** Bless the Lord, angels of the Lord; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:59** Bless the Lord, heavens; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:60** Bless the Lord, all waters that are above the heavens; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:61** Bless the Lord, all powers of the Lord; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:62** Bless the Lord, sun and moon; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:63** Bless the Lord, stars of heaven; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:64** Bless the Lord, all rain and dew; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:65** Bless the Lord, all spirits of God; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:66** Bless the Lord, fire and heat; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:67** Bless the Lord, cold and heat; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:68** Bless the Lord, dews and hoarfrost; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:69** Bless the Lord, frost and cold; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:70** Bless the Lord, ice and snows; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:71** Bless the Lord, nights and days; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:72** Bless the Lord, light and darkness; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:73** Bless the Lord, lightning and clouds; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:74** Let the earth bless the Lord; let it praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:75** Bless the Lord, mountains and hills; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:76** Bless the Lord, all things sprouting on the earth; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:77** Bless the Lord, springs; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:78** Bless the Lord, seas and rivers; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:79** Bless the Lord, whales and all things that move in the waters; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:80** Bless the Lord, all birds of heaven; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:81** Bless the Lord, all beasts and livestock; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:82** Bless the Lord, sons of men; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:83** Let Israel bless the Lord; let it praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:84** Bless the Lord, priests of the Lord; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:85** Bless the Lord, servants of the Lord; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:86** Bless the Lord, spirits and souls of the just; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:87** Bless the Lord, holy and humble of heart; praise and supremely exalt Him forever.

**3:88** Bless the Lord, Hananiah, Azariah, Mishael; praise and supremely exalt Him forever, for He has rescued us from the underworld and saved us from the hand of death, and delivered us from the middle of the burning flame, and rescued us from the middle of the fire.

**3:89** Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy is forever.

**3:90** Bless the Lord God of gods, all devout ones; praise and give thanks to Him, for His mercy is for all ages."

**3:91** Then King Nebuchadnezzar was astonished, and rose up hastily, and said to his nobles, "Did we not throw three men shackled into the middle of the fire?" And answering the king, they said, "Truly, king."

**3:92** He answered and said, "Look, I see four men unbound and walking in the middle of the fire, and there is no harm in them, and the appearance of the fourth is like a son of God."

**3:93** Then Nebuchadnezzar approached the door of the furnace of burning fire and said, "Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, servants of the Most High God, come out and come here." And immediately Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego came out from the middle of the fire.

**3:94** And having assembled, the satraps, magistrates, judges, and powerful men of the king observed those men, how the fire had held no power over their bodies, and the hair of their head had not been singed, and their trousers had not been altered, and the smell of fire had not passed through them.

**3:95** And Nebuchadnezzar broke out and said, "Blessed be their God, namely, the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, who sent His angel and rescued His servants who believed in Him; and they altered the king's word and handed over their bodies so that they would not serve and would not worship any god except their own God.

**3:96** Therefore this decree has been laid down by me: that every people, tribe, and language, whichever speaks blasphemy against the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, is to perish, and its house is to be laid waste; for there is no other god who is able to save in this way."

**3:97** Then the king promoted Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego in the province of Babylon.

**3:98** King Nebuchadnezzar, to all peoples, nations, and languages who live in the entire earth: may peace be multiplied to you.

**3:99** The Most High God has worked signs and wonders with me. Therefore it has pleased me to proclaim

**3:100** His signs, because they are great, and His wonders, because they are mighty; and His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and His dominion is from generation to generation.

## Translator Notes

- **3:5** — Latin 'symphoniæ' retains the Greek loanword 'symphonia', which Jerome in his Commentary on Daniel understood as a harmonious musical ensemble rather than a single instrument, though modern scholars often identify the underlying Aramaic term as a bagpipe.
- **3:17** — Latin 'Ecce enim' ('For look') is a direct affirmation, whereas the Aramaic Masoretic Text begins conditionally ('if').
- **3:20** — Following the Greek Theodotion text, the Vulgate specifies 'ligatis pedibus' ('to bind the feet'), whereas the Aramaic text states only that the men were to be bound.
- **3:24** — Verses 3:24-90 preserve the Prayer of Azariah and the Song of the Three Young Men, translated in the Vulgate from the Greek Theodotion text and absent from the Aramaic Masoretic Text.
- **3:40** — The Vulgate reads 'ut placeat tibi' ('that it may please You') where the Greek has 'to follow You wholly'; likewise in 3:44, 'in omni potentia tua' ('by all Your power') refers to God's power rather than the enemies being stripped of their own power.
- **3:65** — The Vulgate reads 'omnes spiritus Dei' ('all spirits of God'), whereas the Greek Theodotion text has simply 'all winds' (or 'all spirits') without 'of God.'
- **3:88** — Latin 'infernus' translates Greek 'Hades', denoting the underworld or realm of the dead, as reflected in the parallel phrase 'from the hand of death.'
- **3:92** — Latin 'filio Dei' has the singular 'Dei' ('a son of God'), as does the Greek Theodotion text, whereas the Aramaic Masoretic Text has the plural 'bar elahin' ('a son of the gods').
- **3:94** — Latin 'sarabala' transliterates the Aramaic/Greek loanword for wide Persian trousers (translated 'braccis' in 3:21). In 3:95, 'verbum regis immutaverunt' ('altered the king's word') literally preserves the idiom for disobeying or overturning a royal command.
- **3:98** — In native Clementine Vulgate numbering, Nebuchadnezzar's opening epistolary greeting is 3:98-100 (corresponding to Saints.Bible 4:1-3), previously appended to English 4:37.
