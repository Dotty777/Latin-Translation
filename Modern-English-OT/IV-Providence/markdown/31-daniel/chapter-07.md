## Text

**7:1** In the first year of Belshazzar king of Babylon, Daniel saw a dream, and the vision of his head was on his bed; and writing down the dream, he encompassed it in a brief account, and touching on it in summary, he said:

**7:2** "I was seeing in my vision by night, and look, the four winds of heaven were fighting upon the great sea.

**7:3** And four huge beasts were coming up out of the sea, different from one another.

**7:4** The first was like a lioness, and it had the wings of an eagle. I was watching until its wings were plucked off, and it was lifted up from the earth and stood on its feet like a man; and the heart of a man was given to it.

**7:5** And look, another beast, like a bear, stood on one side; and three rows were in its mouth and in its teeth, and they were saying to it in this way: 'Rise, eat abundant flesh.'

**7:6** After these things I was watching, and look, another like a leopard, and it had wings like a bird, four upon itself; and four heads were on the beast, and authority was given to it.

**7:7** After these things I was watching in the vision of the night, and look, a fourth beast, terrifying and astonishing and exceedingly strong. It had great iron teeth, eating and shattering, and trampling the remainder with its feet; moreover, it was unlike the other beasts that I had seen before it, and it had ten horns.

**7:8** I was observing the horns, and look, another little horn sprang up from the middle of them, and three of the first horns were plucked out from before its face; and look, eyes like the eyes of a man were in this horn, and a mouth speaking enormous things.

**7:9** I was watching until thrones were set in place, and the Ancient of Days sat down. His garment was shining white like snow, and the hair of His head was like pure wool; His throne was flames of fire, its wheels were kindled fire.

**7:10** A fiery and rushing river was flowing out from before His face. Thousands of thousands were ministering to Him, and ten thousand times a hundred thousand were standing before Him; the court sat, and the books were opened.

**7:11** I was watching because of the sound of the grand words which that horn was speaking, and I saw that the beast was killed, and its body perished and was handed over to be burned with fire;

**7:12** and that the authority of the other beasts was also taken away, and periods of life were appointed for them until a time and a time.

**7:13** Therefore I was watching in the vision of the night, and look, with the clouds of heaven one like a Son of Man was coming, and He came all the way to the Ancient of Days, and they presented Him in His sight.

**7:14** And He gave Him authority, and honor, and a kingdom, and all peoples, tribes, and languages will serve Him. His authority is an eternal authority, which will not be taken away, and His kingdom is one that will not be destroyed.

**7:15** My spirit shuddered; I, Daniel, was terrified at these things, and the visions of my head troubled me.

**7:16** I approached one of those standing by and sought the truth from him concerning all these things. And he told me the interpretation of the words and taught me:

**7:17** 'These four great beasts are four kingdoms that will arise from the earth.

**7:18** But the holy ones of the Most High God will receive the kingdom, and they will possess the kingdom forever, and for ages of ages.'

**7:19** After this I wanted to learn carefully about the fourth beast, which was very different from all of them and exceedingly terrifying; its teeth and claws were iron, and it was eating and shattering, and trampling the remainder with its feet;

**7:20** and about the ten horns that it had on its head, and about the other one that had sprung up, before which three horns had fallen; and about that horn which had eyes and a mouth speaking grand things, and was greater than the rest.

**7:21** I was watching, and look, that horn was making war against the holy ones and prevailing over them,

**7:22** until the Ancient of Days came and gave judgment to the holy ones of the Exalted One, and the time arrived, and the holy ones took possession of the kingdom.

**7:23** And he spoke in this way: 'The fourth beast will be a fourth kingdom on earth, which will be greater than all kingdoms, and it will devour the whole earth, and trample it, and shatter it.

**7:24** Moreover, the ten horns of that kingdom will be ten kings; and another will arise after them, and he himself will be more powerful than the former ones, and he will humble three kings.

**7:25** And he will speak words against the Exalted One, and he will crush the holy ones of the Most High; and he will think that he can change times and laws, and they will be handed over into his hand until a time, and times, and half a time.

**7:26** And the court will sit, so that power may be taken away, and be crushed, and perish to the end.

**7:27** And let the kingdom, and the authority, and the greatness of the kingdom that is beneath the whole heaven, be given to the people of the holy ones of the Most High, whose kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and all kings will serve Him and obey.'

**7:28** Up to this point is the end of the word. I, Daniel, was greatly troubled by my thoughts, and my face was changed in me; yet I kept the word in my heart."

## Translator Notes

- **7:4** — Vulgate 'leaena' distinctly reads 'lioness' (feminine, matching Latin 'bestia'), whereas the Aramaic has 'lion.'
- **7:5** — Vulgate 'tres ordines ... in ore ejus, et in dentibus ejus' ('three rows in its mouth and in its teeth') differs distinctly from the Aramaic 'three ribs in its mouth between its teeth.'
- **7:10** — Vulgate 'decies millies centena millia' literally states 'ten thousand times a hundred thousand' (one billion), whereas the Aramaic reads 'ten thousand times ten thousand' (one hundred million).
- **7:14** — Vulgate 'Et dedit ei' uses an active verb ('And He gave Him,' with the Ancient of Days as implied subject), whereas the Aramaic reads passively 'was given.'
- **7:19** — Vulgate 'dentes et ungues ejus ferrei' makes both the fourth beast's teeth and claws iron, omitting the Aramaic detail that its claws were bronze; similarly, 7:17, 7:23, and 7:24 read 'four kingdoms,' 'greater,' and 'more powerful' where the Aramaic reads 'four kings' and 'different.'
