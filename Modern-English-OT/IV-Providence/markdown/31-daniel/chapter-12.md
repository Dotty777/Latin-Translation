## Text

**12:1** "At that time, however, Michael the great prince, who stands for the sons of your people, will rise up; and a time will come such as has not been from the time when nations began to exist until that time. And at that time your people will be saved, everyone who is found written in the book.

**12:2** And many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth will awaken, some to eternal life, and others to disgrace, so that they may always see.

**12:3** But those who are learned will shine like the radiance of the expanse, and those who instruct many toward righteousness, like stars for perpetual eternities.

**12:4** But you, Daniel, close up the words and seal the book until the appointed time; very many will pass through, and knowledge will be manifold."

**12:5** And I, Daniel, saw, and look, as it were, two others were standing: one on this side upon the bank of the river, and another on that side on the other bank of the river.

**12:6** And I said to the man who was clothed in linen, who was standing above the waters of the river, "How long until the end of these wonders?"

**12:7** And I heard the man who was clothed in linen, who was standing above the waters of the river, when he had raised his right hand and his left hand toward heaven and had sworn by Him who lives forever: "For a time, and times, and half a time. And when the scattering of the hand of the holy people has been completed, all these things will be completed."

**12:8** And I heard, and I did not understand. And I said, "My lord, what will be after these things?"

**12:9** And he said, "Go, Daniel, because the words are closed up and sealed until the predetermined time.

**12:10** Many will be chosen, and whitened, and tested like fire; and the wicked will act wickedly, nor will any of the wicked understand; the learned, however, will understand.

**12:11** And from the time when the perpetual sacrifice has been taken away and the abomination has been set up for desolation, there will be one thousand two hundred ninety days.

**12:12** Blessed is the one who waits and reaches one thousand three hundred thirty-five days.

**12:13** But you, go until the predetermined time; and you will rest, and you will stand in your lot at the end of the days."

## Translator Notes

- **12:1** — The Clementine Vulgate reads simply "a time will come such as has not been" (et veniet tempus quale non fuit), without the qualifier "of distress" found in the Hebrew Masoretic Text.
- **12:2** — The Vulgate renders the rare Hebrew word dera'on ("abhorrence/contempt") as ut videant semper ("so that they may always see"), reflecting Jerome's derivation of the term from the Hebrew root for "seeing."
- **12:6** — The Vulgate reads first-person Et dixi ("And I said"), making Daniel the questioner, whereas the Hebrew Masoretic Text reads third-person "and he/one said."
- **12:7** — Latin dispersio manus populi sancti ("the scattering of the hand of the holy people") literally reproduces the Hebrew idiom "hand" for power or strength, though Latin manus can also denote a "band" or "company."
- **12:10** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew yitbararu as Eligentur ("will be chosen," taking the root in the sense "select" rather than "purify") and reads quasi ignis probabuntur ("tested like fire," nominative ignis) where the Hebrew has "will be refined."
