## Text

**8:1** In the third year of the reign of King Belshazzar, a vision appeared to me. I, Daniel, after what I had seen at the beginning,

**8:2** saw in my vision, while I was in the fortress of Susa, which is in the region of Elam; and I saw in the vision that I was by the gate of Ulai.

**8:3** And I lifted up my eyes and saw, and look, one ram was standing before the marsh, having high horns, and one was higher than the other and growing up. Afterward,

**8:4** I saw the ram thrusting with its horns toward the west, and toward the north, and toward the south, and all the beasts could not resist it nor be delivered from its hand; and it acted according to its will and was exalted.

**8:5** And I was considering; and look, a buck of the goats was coming from the west over the face of the whole earth, and it was not touching the earth. Now the buck had a prominent horn between its eyes.

**8:6** And it came all the way to that horned ram which I had seen standing before the gate, and ran at it in the rush of its strength.

**8:7** And when it had approached close to the ram, it became enraged against it and struck the ram, and shattered its two horns, and the ram could not resist it; and when it had thrown it to the ground, it trampled it, and no one was able to deliver the ram from its hand.

**8:8** Now the buck of the goats became exceedingly great; and when it had grown, the great horn was broken, and four horns arose beneath it toward the four winds of heaven.

**8:9** And out of one of them one small horn came forth, and it became great toward the south, and toward the east, and toward the strength.

**8:10** And it was exalted as far as the strength of heaven, and threw down some of the strength and of the stars, and trampled them.

**8:11** And even as far as the Prince of the strength it was exalted; and from Him it took away the perpetual sacrifice, and threw down the place of His sanctuary.

**8:12** And power was given to it against the perpetual sacrifice because of sins; and truth will be cast down upon the earth, and it will act and prosper.

**8:13** Then I heard one of the holy ones speaking, and one holy one said to another, I do not know whom, who was speaking, "How long will the vision be, and the perpetual sacrifice, and the sin of the desolation that has taken place, and the sanctuary and the strength be trampled?"

**8:14** And he said to him, "Until evening and morning, two thousand three hundred days; and the sanctuary will be cleansed."

**8:15** Now it happened, while I, Daniel, was seeing the vision and seeking understanding, look, there stood in my sight something like the appearance of a man.

**8:16** And I heard the voice of a man amid Ulai, and he called out and said, "Gabriel, make this vision understood."

**8:17** And he came and stood beside where I was standing; and when he had come, terrified, I fell down upon my face, and he said to me, "Understand, son of man, for at the time of the end the vision will be fulfilled."

**8:18** And while he was speaking to me, I collapsed face down to the ground; and he touched me and set me standing in my place,

**8:19** and said to me, "I will show you the things that are to come at the end of the curse, for the time has its end.

**8:20** The ram that you saw having horns is the king of the Medes and of the Persians.

**8:21** Furthermore, the buck of the goats is the king of the Greeks; and the great horn that was between its eyes, he is the first king.

**8:22** Now as for the fact that, when it was broken, four arose in its place: four kings will rise up from his nation, but not in his strength.

**8:23** And after their reign, when iniquities have increased, a king shameless in face and understanding riddles will rise up;

**8:24** and his strength will be reinforced, yet not by his own power; and beyond what can be believed, he will lay waste to all things, and he will prosper and act. And he will kill the mighty and the people of the holy ones

**8:25** according to his will, and deceit will be directed in his hand; and he will exalt his heart, and in the abundance of all things he will put very many to death; and he will rise up against the Prince of princes, and without a hand he will be crushed.

**8:26** And the vision of evening and morning that has been spoken is true; you, therefore, seal up the vision, because it will be after many days."

**8:27** And I, Daniel, grew faint and was sick for days; and when I had risen, I was doing the king's work, and I was astonished at the vision, and there was no one to interpret it.

## Translator Notes

- **8:2** — The Vulgate reads super portam Ulai ("by/over the gate of Ulai") here and ante portam ("before the gate") in 8:6, as well as ante paludem ("before the marsh") in 8:3, where the Hebrew has "canal/stream" (uval).
- **8:9** — The Vulgate translates Hebrew "the glorious [land]" here and "the host" in 8:10-13 consistently with fortitudo ("strength"); in 8:12 it renders "host" with robur ("power").
- **8:11** — Latin principem fortitudinis ('prince of the strength/host') may refer to God Himself or to an angelic prince; capitalization reflects the divine-referent reading.
- **8:13** — Latin alteri nescio cui ("to another, I do not know whom") is Jerome's idiomatic rendering of the rare Hebrew term palmoni ("a certain one").
- **8:16** — The Clementine Vulgate reads feminine istam visionem ("this vision") without an explicit personal object, whereas the Hebrew reads "make this man understand the vision."
- **8:25** — Latin in copia rerum omnium ("in the abundance of all things") is a distinctly Vulgate rendering of the Hebrew phrase meaning "without warning" or "in security." Capitalization of 'Prince of princes' (principem principum) reflects the divine-referent reading.
