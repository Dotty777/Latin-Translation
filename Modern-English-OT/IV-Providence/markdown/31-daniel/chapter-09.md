## Text

**9:1** In the first year of Darius the son of Ahasuerus, of the seed of the Medes, who ruled over the kingdom of the Chaldeans,

**9:2** in year one of his reign, I, Daniel, understood in the books the number of the years about which the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah the prophet, that seventy years of the desolation of Jerusalem would be fulfilled.

**9:3** And I set my face toward the Lord my God to petition and to plead, in fastings, sackcloth, and ashes.

**9:4** And I prayed to the Lord my God, and confessed, and said, "I plead, Lord God, great and fearsome, keeping covenant and mercy for those who love You and keep Your commandments:

**9:5** we have sinned, we have committed iniquity, we have acted wickedly, and we have departed; and we have turned aside from Your commandments and judgments.

**9:6** We have not obeyed Your servants the prophets, who spoke in Your name to our kings, our princes, our fathers, and all the people of the land.

**9:7** To You, Lord, belongs righteousness, but to us shame of face, as it is today to the man of Judah, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and to all Israel, to those who are near and to those who are far away in all the lands to which You drove them out because of their iniquities in which they sinned against You.

**9:8** Lord, to us belongs shame of face, to our kings, our princes, and our fathers, who have sinned.

**9:9** But to You, the Lord our God, belong mercy and pardon, because we have departed from You,

**9:10** and we have not listened to the voice of the Lord our God, so as to walk in His law, which He set before us through His servants the prophets.

**9:11** And all Israel have transgressed Your law and have turned aside so as not to listen to Your voice; and the curse and the execration that is written in the book of Moses, the servant of God, has dripped upon us, because we have sinned against Him.

**9:12** And He has established His words, which He spoke against us and against our princes who judged us, so as to bring upon us a great evil, such as has never been under all heaven, according to what has been done in Jerusalem.

**9:13** Just as it is written in the law of Moses, all this evil has come upon us; and we have not petitioned Your face, Lord our God, so as to turn back from our iniquities and to reflect upon Your truth.

**9:14** And the Lord kept watch over the evil and brought it upon us. Righteous is the Lord our God in all His works that He has done, for we have not listened to His voice.

**9:15** And now, Lord our God, who brought Your people out of the land of Egypt with a strong hand and made a name for Yourself as on this day: we have sinned, we have committed iniquity.

**9:16** Lord, according to all Your righteousness, let Your anger and Your fury be turned away, I plead, from Your city Jerusalem and Your holy mountain. For because of our sins and the iniquities of our fathers, Jerusalem and Your people are a reproach to all around us.

**9:17** Now therefore hear, our God, the prayer of Your servant and his petitions, and show Your face upon Your sanctuary, which is deserted, for Your own sake.

**9:18** Incline Your ear, my God, and hear; open Your eyes and see our desolation, and the city upon which Your name has been called; for not in our righteous deeds do we lay down petitions before Your face, but in Your many compassions.

**9:19** Hear, Lord; be appeased, Lord; pay attention and act; do not delay, for Your own sake, my God, because Your name has been called upon the city and upon Your people."

**9:20** And while I was still speaking, and praying, and confessing my sins and the sins of my people Israel, and laying down my petitions in the sight of my God for the holy mountain of my God,

**9:21** while I was still speaking in prayer, look, the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision from the beginning, flying swiftly, touched me at the time of the evening sacrifice.

**9:22** And he taught me, and spoke to me, and said, "Daniel, now I have come forth so that I might teach you and you might understand.

**9:23** From the outset of your petitions a word went forth, and I have come to make it known to you, because you are a man of desires; you, therefore, consider the word and understand the vision.

**9:24** Seventy weeks have been shortened upon your people and upon your holy city, so that transgression may be finished, and sin may receive an end, and iniquity may be blotted out, and everlasting righteousness may be brought in, and vision and prophecy may be fulfilled, and the Holy One of holies may be anointed.

**9:25** Know, therefore, and consider: from the going forth of the word that Jerusalem be built again until Christ the leader, there will be seven weeks and sixty-two weeks; and the square and the walls will be built again in distress of times.

**9:26** And after sixty-two weeks, Christ will be put to death, and the people who will deny Him will not be His. And a people with a leader who is to come will destroy the city and the sanctuary; and its end will be devastation, and after the end of the war, decreed desolation.

**9:27** And he will confirm a covenant for many for one week; and in the half of the week victim and sacrifice will cease; and there will be in the temple the abomination of desolation; and until the consummation and the end, the desolation will continue."

## Translator Notes

- **9:23** — Latin vir desideriorum ("a man of desires") translates Hebrew ish chamudot ("greatly beloved" or "precious man") literally.
- **9:24** — The Vulgate reads abbreviatae sunt ("have been shortened"), where the Hebrew verb means "decreed" (literally "cut"), and the masculine Sanctus sanctorum ("the Holy One of holies"), not the neuter sanctum sanctorum used elsewhere for the most holy place.
- **9:25** — Latin christum ducem ("Christ the leader") renders Hebrew mashiach nagid ("anointed leader/prince") with christus ("anointed one/Christ") and joins the seven and sixty-two weeks as a single span.
- **9:26** — The Vulgate reads et non erit ejus populus qui eum negaturus est ("and the people who will deny Him will not be His"), where the Hebrew reads "and shall have nothing," and concludes with vastitas ("devastation") and post finem belli ("after the end of the war").
- **9:27** — The Vulgate uses intransitive deficiet ("will cease/fail") rather than a causative verb and reads in templo ("in the temple") where the Hebrew has "on the wing of abominations."
