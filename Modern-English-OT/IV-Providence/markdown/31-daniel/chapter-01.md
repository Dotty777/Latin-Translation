## Text

**1:1** In the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem and laid siege to it.

**1:2** And the Lord delivered into his hand Jehoiakim, king of Judah, and a portion of the vessels of the house of God; and he carried them away to the land of Shinar, into the house of his god, and he brought the vessels into the treasure house of his god.

**1:3** And the king told Ashpenaz, the overseer of the eunuchs, to bring in from the sons of Israel, and from the royal seed and that of the rulers,

**1:4** youths in whom there was no blemish, handsome in form and educated in all wisdom, discerning in knowledge and trained in learning, and who were able to stand in the king's palace, so that he might teach them the literature and language of the Chaldeans.

**1:5** And the king appointed for them an allowance for each day from his own food, and from the wine from which he himself drank, so that, having been nourished for three years, afterward they might stand in the presence of the king.

**1:6** So there were among them, from the sons of Judah, Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.

**1:7** And the overseer of the eunuchs gave them names: to Daniel, Belteshazzar; to Hananiah, Shadrach; to Mishael, Meshach; and to Azariah, Abednego.

**1:8** But Daniel resolved in his heart that he would not be defiled from the king's table, nor from the wine of his drinking; and he asked the overseer of the eunuchs that he might not be contaminated.

**1:9** Now God gave Daniel favor and mercy in the sight of the chief of the eunuchs.

**1:10** And the chief of the eunuchs said to Daniel, "I fear my lord the king, who appointed food and drink for you; if he sees your faces thinner than the other young men of your own age, you will condemn my head to the king."

**1:11** And Daniel said to Malasar, whom the chief of the eunuchs had appointed over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah:

**1:12** "Test us, I beg you, your servants, for ten days, and let legumes be given to us to eat, and water to drink;

**1:13** and examine our faces and the faces of the youths who eat the royal food; and just as you see, you will deal with your servants."

**1:14** Having heard a word of this kind, he tested them for ten days.

**1:15** Now after ten days, their faces appeared better and plumper than all the youths who were eating the royal food.

**1:16** So Malasar kept taking away the rations and the wine of their drinking, and giving them legumes.

**1:17** Now to these youths God gave knowledge and learning in every book and wisdom; and to Daniel, understanding of all visions and dreams.

**1:18** Therefore, when the days were completed after which the king had said that they should be brought in, the overseer of the eunuchs brought them into the presence of Nebuchadnezzar.

**1:19** And when the king had spoken to them, none were found among them all like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah; and they stood in the presence of the king.

**1:20** And in every matter of wisdom and understanding about which the king questioned them, he found in them tenfold above all the soothsayers and magicians who were in his entire kingdom.

**1:21** Now Daniel remained until the first year of King Cyrus.

## Translator Notes

- **1:3** — Latin tyrannorum (literally 'of rulers' or 'of lords/tyrants') renders Hebrew partemim ('nobles'), using Late Latin tyrannus in its older sense of a governing prince or lord.
- **1:7** — The Vulgate renders Daniel's Babylonian name as Baltassar (identical in spelling to the form it later uses for King Belshazzar), here standardized to Belteshazzar.
- **1:8** — The Vulgate reads de mensa regis ('from the king's table') where the Hebrew specifies patbag hammelek ('the king's royal delicacies/food').
- **1:11** — The Vulgate treats the Hebrew title hammelsar ('the steward' or 'overseer') as the personal name Malasar (also in 1:16).
- **1:20** — Latin invenit in eis decuplum super literally reads 'he found in them tenfold above'.
