## Text

**13:1** Now there was a man living in Babylon, and his name was Joakim;

**13:2** and he took a wife named Susanna, the daughter of Hilkiah, very beautiful and God-fearing;

**13:3** for her parents, being righteous, had instructed their daughter according to the law of Moses.

**13:4** Now Joakim was very rich, and he had an orchard next to his house; and the Jews used to flock to him, because he was the most honored of all.

**13:5** And two elders from the people were appointed judges in that year, concerning whom the Lord spoke: "Wickedness went forth from Babylon from the elder judges, who seemed to govern the people."

**13:6** These men frequented the house of Joakim, and all who had legal disputes came to them.

**13:7** Now when the people had returned home around noon, Susanna would go in and walk about in her husband's orchard.

**13:8** And the elders saw her going in and walking about every day, and they burned with lust for her;

**13:9** and they perverted their minds and turned away their eyes so as not to look to heaven nor remember righteous judgments.

**13:10** So both of them were wounded with passion for her, yet they did not disclose their pain to one another;

**13:11** for they were ashamed to reveal their lust to each other, wanting to lie with her.

**13:12** And every day they watched all the more eagerly to see her. And one said to the other,

**13:13** "Let us go home, for it is the hour for the midday meal." And going out, they parted from one another.

**13:14** And when they had turned back, they came to the same place; and questioning each other about the reason, they confessed their lust. And then together they set a time when they might find her alone.

**13:15** Now it happened, while they were watching for a suitable day, that she went in once, as yesterday and the day before, with only two young women, and wanted to bathe in the orchard, for it was hot;

**13:16** and no one was there except the two elders, who were hidden and watching her.

**13:17** So she said to the young women, "Bring me oil and soaps, and close the doors of the orchard so that I may bathe."

**13:18** And they did as she had commanded: they closed the doors of the orchard and went out through the back door to bring what she had ordered, and they did not know that the elders were hidden inside.

**13:19** Now when the young women had gone out, the two elders rose up, ran to her, and said,

**13:20** "Look, the doors of the orchard are closed, and no one sees us, and we are in lust for you; therefore consent to us and lie with us.

**13:21** But if you refuse, we will give testimony against you that a young man was with you, and for this reason you sent the young women away from you."

**13:22** Susanna groaned and said, "Distress surrounds me on every side; for if I do this, it is death for me, yet if I do not do it, I will not escape your hands.

**13:23** But it is better for me to fall into your hands without doing this deed than to sin in the sight of the Lord."

**13:24** And Susanna cried out with a loud voice; and the elders also cried out against her.

**13:25** And one of them ran to the doors of the orchard and opened them.

**13:26** Therefore, when the attendants of the house heard the outcry in the orchard, they rushed in through the back door to see what was happening.

**13:27** Now after the elders spoke, the servants were deeply ashamed, because no report of this kind had ever been spoken about Susanna. And the next day came.

**13:28** And when the people had come to her husband Joakim, the two elders also came, full of wicked intent against Susanna, in order to put her to death.

**13:29** And they said before the people, "Send for Susanna, daughter of Hilkiah, wife of Joakim." And immediately they sent for her.

**13:30** And she came with her parents, her children, and all her relatives.

**13:31** Now Susanna was very delicate and beautiful in appearance.

**13:32** But those wicked men ordered that she be unveiled (for she was veiled), so that at least in this way they might take their fill of her beauty.

**13:33** So her own people and all who knew her were weeping.

**13:34** Then the two elders stood up in the middle of the people and placed their hands on her head.

**13:35** Weeping, she looked up to heaven, for her heart had confidence in the Lord.

**13:36** And the elders said, "While we were walking alone in the orchard, this woman came in with two young women, closed the doors of the orchard, and sent the young women away from her.

**13:37** Then a young man who had been hiding came to her and lay with her.

**13:38** Now while we were in a corner of the orchard, seeing the wickedness, we ran to them and saw them having intercourse together.

**13:39** And as for him, we were unable to catch him, because he was stronger than we were, and after opening the doors he leaped out.

**13:40** But when we seized this woman, we asked who the young man was, and she refused to tell us. We are witnesses to this matter."

**13:41** The crowd believed them as elders and judges of the people, and they condemned her to death.

**13:42** Then Susanna cried out with a loud voice and said, "Eternal God, who know hidden things, who know all things before they happen,

**13:43** You know that they have borne false testimony against me; and look, I am dying, though I have done none of these things that these men have maliciously fabricated against me."

**13:44** And the Lord heard her voice.

**13:45** And while she was being led away to death, the Lord roused the holy spirit of a younger boy, whose name was Daniel;

**13:46** and he cried out with a loud voice, "I am clean from the blood of this woman."

**13:47** And all the people turned toward him and said, "What is this word that you have spoken?"

**13:48** Standing in the middle of them, he said, "Are you so foolish, sons of Israel, that without judging or learning what is true, you have condemned a daughter of Israel?

**13:49** Return to the judgment, because they have spoken false testimony against her."

**13:50** So the people returned in haste, and the elders said to him, "Come and sit in our midst and tell us, because God has given you the honor of old age."

**13:51** And Daniel said to them, "Separate them far from one another, and I will judge them."

**13:52** So when they had been separated from one another, he called one of them and said to him, "You who have grown old in evil days, now your sins that you committed before have come upon you,

**13:53** passing unjust judgments, oppressing the innocent, and releasing the guilty, though the Lord says, 'You must not kill the innocent and the righteous.'

**13:54** Now then, if you saw her, say under what tree you saw them conversing with one another." And he said, "Under a mastic tree."

**13:55** Then Daniel said, "Very well, you have lied against your own head; for look, the angel of God, having received the sentence from Him, will split you in two."

**13:56** And once he was removed, he ordered the other one to come and said to him, "Seed of Canaan and not of Judah, beauty has deceived you, and lust has perverted your heart.

**13:57** This is how you used to treat the daughters of Israel, and out of fear they spoke with you; but a daughter of Judah did not tolerate your wickedness.

**13:58** Now then, tell me under what tree you caught them talking with one another." And he said, "Under a holm oak."

**13:59** Then Daniel said to him, "Very well, you too have lied against your own head; for the angel of the Lord is waiting, holding a sword, to cut you in two and put both of you to death."

**13:60** Then the entire assembly cried out with a loud voice and blessed God, who saves those who hope in Him.

**13:61** And they rose up against the two elders (for Daniel had convicted them out of their own mouth of having spoken false testimony), and they did to them just as they had wickedly acted against their neighbor,

**13:62** so that they might act according to the law of Moses. And they put them to death, and innocent blood was saved on that day.

**13:63** Now Hilkiah and his wife praised God for their daughter Susanna, together with her husband Joakim and all their relatives, because nothing shameful was found in her.

**13:64** And Daniel became great in the sight of the people from that day onward.

**13:65** And King Astyages was gathered to his fathers, and Cyrus the Persian received his kingdom.

## Translator Notes

- **13:1** — In native Clementine Vulgate order, the narrative of Susanna forms chapter 13 (65 verses); previously 28 scattered verses were interleaved as unplaced entries after Daniel 1:21.
- **13:17** — Latin smigmata (apparatus variant smegmata) denotes cleansing soaps or washing ointments.
- **13:45** — Latin spiritum sanctum pueri junioris ('the holy spirit of a younger boy') is kept lowercase here because the genitive pueri junioris modifies spiritum sanctum as the boy's own spirit.
- **13:55** — In Latin, Daniel plays on the sound of schino ("mastic tree") in the preceding verse and scindet ("will split").
- **13:57** — The Latin phrase "spoke with you" (loquebantur vobis) is used here as a euphemism for intimate relations.
- **13:59** — Daniel's verdict ("cut you in two," Latin secet) reflects the wordplay on prino ("holm oak") in the preceding verse.
- **13:65** — In Clementine Vulgate numbering, the transition from Astyages to Cyrus closes chapter 13 as 13:65 rather than opening chapter 14.
