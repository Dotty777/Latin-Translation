## Text

**2:1** In the second year of the reign of Nebuchadnezzar, Nebuchadnezzar saw a dream, and his spirit was terrified, and his dream fled from him.

**2:2** Then the king commanded that the soothsayers, the magicians, the sorcerers, and the Chaldeans be summoned to disclose his dreams to the king. When they had come, they stood before the king.

**2:3** And the king said to them, "I saw a dream, and confused in mind I do not know what I saw."

**2:4** And the Chaldeans answered the king in Syriac, "O king, live forever! Tell the dream to your servants, and we will disclose its interpretation."

**2:5** And answering, the king said to the Chaldeans, "The matter has slipped from me; unless you disclose to me the dream and its interpretation, you will perish and your houses will be confiscated.

**2:6** But if you recount the dream and its interpretation, you will receive rewards, gifts, and great honor from me. Therefore disclose to me the dream and its interpretation."

**2:7** They answered a second time and said, "Let the king tell the dream to his servants, and we will disclose its interpretation."

**2:8** The king answered and said, "I know for certain that you are buying time, knowing that the matter has slipped from me.

**2:9** Therefore, if you do not disclose the dream to me, there is one sentence concerning you: that you have also fabricated a deceptive interpretation full of fraud to speak to me until the time passes. So tell me the dream, that I may know that you also speak its true interpretation."

**2:10** Then answering before the king, the Chaldeans said, "There is no man on earth who can fulfill your demand, O king; moreover, no king, however great and mighty, asks a matter of this kind from any soothsayer, magician, or Chaldean.

**2:11** For the matter that you seek, O king, is hard, and no one will be found who can disclose it in the king's sight, except the gods, whose dwelling is not with men."

**2:12** When he heard this, the king, in fury and great anger, commanded that all the wise men of Babylon should perish.

**2:13** And when the decree went out, the wise men were being put to death, and Daniel and his companions were sought out so that they might perish.

**2:14** Then Daniel inquired concerning the law and the decree from Arioch, commander of the king's military force, who had gone out to put to death the wise men of Babylon.

**2:15** And he questioned him, who had received authority from the king, for what reason so cruel a decree had gone out from the presence of the king. So when Arioch had explained the matter to Daniel,

**2:16** Daniel went in and asked the king to give him time to disclose the solution to the king.

**2:17** And he went into his house and explained the matter to Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, his companions,

**2:18** so that they might seek mercy from the presence of the God of heaven concerning this secret, and that Daniel and his companions might not perish with the rest of the wise men of Babylon.

**2:19** Then the mystery was revealed to Daniel through a vision in the night; and Daniel blessed the God of heaven,

**2:20** and speaking, he said, "May the name of the Lord be blessed from age even to age, because wisdom and strength are His.

**2:21** And He Himself changes times and ages; He removes kingdoms and establishes them; He gives wisdom to the wise and knowledge to those who understand instruction.

**2:22** He Himself reveals deep and hidden things, and knows what is set in darkness, and the light is with Him.

**2:23** To You, God of our fathers, I give thanks and praise You, because You have given me wisdom and strength, and now You have shown me what we asked of You, for You have opened to us the king's matter."

**2:24** After this Daniel went in to Arioch, whom the king had appointed to destroy the wise men of Babylon, and spoke to him thus: "Do not destroy the wise men of Babylon; bring me into the king's presence, and I will recount the solution to the king."

**2:25** Then Arioch hurried and brought Daniel in to the king, and said to him, "I have found a man from the sons of the exile of Judah who will declare the solution to the king."

**2:26** The king answered and said to Daniel, whose name was Belteshazzar, "Do you truly think you are able to disclose to me the dream that I saw and its interpretation?"

**2:27** And answering before the king, Daniel said, "The mystery about which the king asks, wise men, magicians, soothsayers, and diviners cannot disclose to the king;

**2:28** but there is a God in heaven who reveals mysteries, who has disclosed to you, King Nebuchadnezzar, what is to come in the last times. Your dream and the visions of your head on your bed are of this sort:

**2:29** You, O king, began to think on your bed about what would happen after this; and the One who reveals mysteries has shown you what is to come.

**2:30** To me also this secret has been revealed, not because of wisdom that is in me more than in all the living, but so that the interpretation might be made clear to the king, and that you might know the thoughts of your mind.

**2:31** You, O king, were watching, and look, there was as it were one huge statue; that statue, great and towering in height, stood opposite you, and its appearance was terrifying.

**2:32** The head of this statue was of finest gold, its chest and arms of silver, and its belly and thighs of bronze,

**2:33** while its legs were of iron; part of its feet was of iron, and part was of baked clay.

**2:34** You kept watching in this way until a stone was cut from a mountain without hands, and it struck the statue on its feet of iron and clay, and shattered them.

**2:35** Then the iron, the clay, the bronze, the silver, and the gold were crushed together and reduced as if to the fine dust of a summer threshing floor, which was carried away by the wind, and no place was found for them; but the stone that had struck the statue became a great mountain and filled the whole earth.

**2:36** This is the dream; we will also tell its interpretation before you, O king.

**2:37** You are a king of kings, and the God of heaven has given you kingdom, strength, dominion, and glory;

**2:38** and all places in which the sons of men dwell, and the wild beasts of the field, and also the birds of heaven, He has given into your hand, and He has set all things under your authority; you therefore are the golden head.

**2:39** And after you another kingdom will arise, inferior to you, of silver; and another, a third kingdom, of bronze, which will rule over the whole earth.

**2:40** And the fourth kingdom will be like iron: just as iron shatters and subdues all things, so it will shatter and crush all these.

**2:41** Moreover, because you saw part of the feet and toes to be potter's clay and part iron, the kingdom will be divided; yet it will arise from the rootstock of iron, just as you saw iron mixed with clay made of mud.

**2:42** And as the toes of the feet were partly iron and partly baked clay, the kingdom will be partly solid and partly brittle.

**2:43** And whereas you saw iron mixed with clay made of mud, they will indeed mingle with human seed, yet they will not cling to one another, just as iron cannot be mixed with clay.

**2:44** Now in the days of those kingdoms the God of heaven will raise up a kingdom that will never be destroyed, and its kingdom will not be handed over to another people; instead, it will shatter and consume all these kingdoms, and it itself will stand forever.

**2:45** Just as you saw that a stone was cut from the mountain without hands and shattered the clay, the iron, the bronze, the silver, and the gold, the great God has shown the king what is to come afterward; and the dream is true, and its interpretation is trustworthy."

**2:46** Then King Nebuchadnezzar fell on his face and paid homage to Daniel, and commanded that they offer sacrifices and incense to him.

**2:47** Then speaking, the king said to Daniel, "Truly your God is the God of gods, and the Lord of kings, and the One who reveals mysteries, since you were able to open this secret."

**2:48** Then the king raised Daniel to high rank and gave him many great gifts; and he appointed him ruler over all the provinces of Babylon, and prefect of the magistrates over all the wise men of Babylon.

**2:49** And Daniel requested of the king, and he appointed Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego over the affairs of the province of Babylon; but Daniel himself remained at the doors of the king.

## Translator Notes

- **2:4** — Latin syriace ("in Syriac") renders the Aramaic term for the Aramaic language.
- **2:5** — Latin Sermo recessit a me ("The matter has slipped from me") and domus vestræ publicabuntur ("your houses will be confiscated" to the public treasury) are distinctive Vulgate renderings of the Aramaic.
- **2:17** — Latin spellings Ananiæ, Misaëli, and Azariæ are rendered with standard modern English Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.
- **2:18** — Latin alternates between sacramentum ("secret," vv. 18, 30, 47) and mysterium ("mystery," vv. 19, 27-29, 47).
- **2:20** — Latin reads nomen Domini ("the name of the Lord") where the Aramaic reads "the name of God."
- **2:26** — Latin spelling Baltassar is rendered with standard modern English Belteshazzar.
- **2:27** — Latin aruspices (Roman entrail-inspecting diviners) is rendered "diviners."
- **2:35** — Latin favillam literally means "fine ash/dust" rather than "chaff."
- **2:39** — Latin explicitly adds argenteum ("of silver") to describe the second kingdom.
- **2:41** — Latin de plantario ferri ("from the rootstock/seedling of iron") differs from Aramaic "firmness of iron."
- **2:42** — Latin contritum literally means "crushed" or "broken," rendered "brittle" in contrast with solidum ("solid").
- **2:49** — Latin spellings Sidrach, Misach, and Abdenago are rendered with standard modern English Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego.
