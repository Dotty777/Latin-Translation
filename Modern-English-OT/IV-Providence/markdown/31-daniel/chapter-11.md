## Text

**11:1** "Now I, from the first year of Darius the Mede, was standing so that he might be strengthened and fortified.

**11:2** And now I will declare the truth to you. Look, three more kings will stand in Persia, and the fourth will be enriched with exceedingly great wealth above all; and when he has grown strong through his riches, he will stir up all against the kingdom of Greece.

**11:3** Then a mighty king will rise, and he will rule with great power, and he will do what pleases him.

**11:4** And when he has stood, his kingdom will be shattered and divided toward the four winds of heaven, yet not to his descendants, nor according to his power with which he ruled; for his kingdom will be torn apart even for outsiders, apart from these.

**11:5** And the king of the south will be strengthened; and one of his princes will prevail over him, and he will rule with authority, for great will be his dominion.

**11:6** And after the end of years they will make an alliance; and the daughter of the king of the south will come to the king of the north to make friendship, and she will not retain the strength of the arm, nor will her seed stand; and she herself will be handed over, and her young men who brought her, and those who were strengthening her in the times.

**11:7** And a planting from the sprout of her roots will stand; and he will come with an army and enter the province of the king of the north, and he will mistreat them and prevail.

**11:8** Moreover, he will lead captive into Egypt their gods as well, and carved images, and also precious vessels of silver and gold; he himself will prevail against the king of the north.

**11:9** And the king of the south will enter into the kingdom, and he will return to his own land.

**11:10** Now his sons will be provoked, and they will gather a multitude of very many armies; and he will come hastening and overflowing, and he will return, and be stirred up, and clash with his strength.

**11:11** And the king of the south, provoked, will go out and fight against the king of the north; and he will prepare an exceedingly great multitude, and the multitude will be given into his hand.

**11:12** And he will capture the multitude, and his heart will be lifted up, and he will cast down many thousands, yet he will not prevail.

**11:13** For the king of the north will turn back and prepare a multitude much greater than before; and at the end of the times and years he will come hastening with a great army and exceedingly great wealth.

**11:14** And in those times many will rise up against the king of the south; the sons of the transgressors of your people will also exalt themselves in order to fulfill the vision, and they will fall.

**11:15** And the king of the north comes, and he will heap up a rampart and capture the most fortified cities; and the arms of the south will not hold out, and his chosen ones will rise up to resist, and there will be no strength.

**11:16** And he who comes against him will do according to his pleasure, and there will be no one who stands before his face; and he will stand in the renowned land, and it will be consumed in his hand.

**11:17** And he will set his face to come to hold his entire kingdom, and he will do upright things with him; and he will give him a daughter of women in order to overthrow it, yet she will not stand, nor will she be his.

**11:18** And he will turn his face toward the islands and capture many; and he will make the prince of his reproach cease, and his reproach will be turned back upon him.

**11:19** And he will turn his face toward the dominion of his own land, and he will stumble and fall, and will not be found.

**11:20** And in his place will stand one most vile and unworthy of royal splendor; and in a few days he will be shattered, not in fury, nor in battle.

**11:21** And in his place will stand a despised one, and royal honor will not be bestowed on him; and he will come secretly and obtain the kingdom through deceit.

**11:22** And the arms of the one fighting will be overcome before his face and will be shattered, and moreover the leader of the covenant as well.

**11:23** And after friendships, he will practice deceit with him; and he will go up and prevail with a small people.

**11:24** And he will enter abundant and fertile cities, and he will do things that his fathers and the fathers of his fathers did not do; he will scatter plunder, and spoil, and their riches, and against the strongest cities he will devise plans, and this until a time.

**11:25** And his strength and his heart will be stirred up against the king of the south with a great army; and the king of the south will be provoked to war with many and exceedingly strong auxiliary forces, yet they will not stand, because they will devise plots against him.

**11:26** And those who eat bread with him will crush him, and his army will be overwhelmed, and very many will fall killed.

**11:27** The heart of the two kings will also be to do evil, and at one table they will speak falsehood; yet they will not succeed, because the end is still for another time.

**11:28** And he will return to his own land with much wealth, and his heart will be against the holy covenant; and he will act, and return to his own land.

**11:29** At the appointed time he will return and come toward the south; and the last will not be like the former.

**11:30** And triremes and the Romans will come against him, and he will be struck, and he will return and become indignant against the covenant of the sanctuary, and will act; and he will return and plot against those who have abandoned the covenant of the sanctuary.

**11:31** And arms from him will stand, and they will defile the sanctuary of strength, and take away the perpetual sacrifice; and they will place the abomination for desolation.

**11:32** And the wicked against the covenant will pretend deceitfully; but the people who know their God will prevail and act.

**11:33** And the learned among the people will teach very many; and they will fall by the sword, and by flame, and by captivity, and by plunder for days.

**11:34** And when they have fallen, they will be lifted up with a little help; and very many will join themselves to them deceitfully.

**11:35** And some of the educated will fall, so that they may be refined, and chosen, and made white until the predetermined time, because there will still be another time.

**11:36** And the king will do according to his will, and he will exalt himself and magnify himself against every god, and against the God of gods he will speak great things; and he will prosper until the wrath is completed, for the decree has indeed been accomplished.

**11:37** And he will not regard the God of his fathers; and he will be given to the desires of women, nor will he care for any of the gods, because he will rise up against all things.

**11:38** However, he will revere the god Maozim in his place; and a god whom his fathers did not know he will worship with gold, and silver, and precious stone, and precious things.

**11:39** And he will act to fortify Maozim with a foreign god whom he has acknowledged; and he will multiply glory, and he will give them authority over many, and he will divide the land for nothing.

**11:40** And at the predetermined time the king of the south will do battle against him, and the king of the north will come against him like a storm with chariots, and with horsemen, and with a great fleet; and he will enter the lands, and crush them, and pass through.

**11:41** And he will enter into the glorious land, and many will fall; however, these alone will be saved from his hand: Edom, and Moab, and the chief part of the sons of Ammon.

**11:42** And he will stretch out his hand against the lands, and the land of Egypt will not escape.

**11:43** And he will rule over the treasures of gold and silver, and over all the precious things of Egypt; he will also pass through Libya and Ethiopia.

**11:44** And a report will trouble him from the east and from the north; and he will come with a great multitude in order to crush and kill very many.

**11:45** And he will pitch his tent at Apadno between the seas, upon the renowned and holy mountain; and he will come even to its summit, and no one will help him."

## Translator Notes

- **11:9** — Latin places rex austri in the nominative ("And the king of the south will enter into the kingdom"), making him the subject, whereas the Hebrew is usually read with the king of the north entering the kingdom of the king of the south.
- **11:30** — Latin explicitly renders Hebrew "ships of Kittim" as trieres, et Romani ("triremes and the Romans"), and translates the closing clause as cogitabit adversum eos ("plot against those") rather than "show regard for those."
- **11:37** — Latin et erit in concupiscentiis feminarum ("and he will be given to the desires of women") is an affirmative statement, whereas the Hebrew places "the desire of women" under the surrounding negation.
- **11:38** — Latin transliterates Hebrew ma'uzzim ("fortresses") as the proper name Maozim here and in 11:39; in 11:39 Latin gratuito ("for nothing") also differs from Hebrew "for a price."
- **11:45** — Latin transliterates Hebrew appadno ("his palace/pavilion") as the place name Apadno, and renders the following phrase usque ad summitatem ejus ("even to its summit") rather than "to his end."
