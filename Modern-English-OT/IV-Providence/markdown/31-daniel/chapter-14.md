## Text

**14:1** Now Daniel was a table companion of the king, and honored above all his friends.

**14:2** There was also an idol among the Babylonians named Bel; and each day twelve artabas of fine flour, forty sheep, and six amphoras of wine were spent on him.

**14:3** The king also revered him, and went every day to worship him; but Daniel worshiped his own God. And the king said to him, "Why do you not worship Bel?"

**14:4** He answered and said to him, "Because I do not revere handmade idols, but the living God, who created heaven and earth and has authority over all flesh."

**14:5** And the king said to him, "Does Bel not seem to you to be a living god? Or do you not see how much he eats and drinks every day?"

**14:6** And Daniel said, smiling, "Do not be deceived, king; for this one is clay on the inside and bronze on the outside, and he has never eaten at any time."

**14:7** And the king grew angry and summoned his priests, and said to them, "Unless you tell me who it is that eats these provisions, you will die.

**14:8** But if you show that Bel eats these things, Daniel will die, because he has blasphemed against Bel." And Daniel said to the king, "Let it be done according to your word."

**14:9** Now there were seventy priests of Bel, besides their wives, little ones, and children. And the king came with Daniel into the temple of Bel.

**14:10** And the priests of Bel said, "Look, we are going outside; and you, king, set out the food and mix the wine, and close the door and seal it with your ring.

**14:11** And when you come in during the morning, if you do not find everything eaten by Bel, we will surely die, or else Daniel, who has lied against us."

**14:12** Now they treated it with contempt, because they had made a hidden entrance under the table, and through it they always came in and devoured them.

**14:13** So it happened, after they had gone out, that the king set the foods before Bel. Daniel gave orders to his servants, and they brought ashes, and he sifted them throughout the entire temple before the king; and going out, they closed the door, and sealing it with the king's ring, they departed.

**14:14** Now the priests entered during the night according to their custom, along with their wives and children, and ate everything and drank.

**14:15** And the king rose at first dawn, and Daniel with him.

**14:16** And the king said, "Are the seals intact, Daniel?" And he answered, "Intact, king."

**14:17** And immediately, when he had opened the door, the king looked at the table and cried out with a loud voice, "You are great, Bel, and there is no deceit at all in you!"

**14:18** And Daniel laughed and held the king back so that he would not go inside, and said, "Look at the floor: notice whose footprints these are."

**14:19** And the king said, "I see footprints of men, and of women, and of little children." And the king grew angry.

**14:20** Then he seized the priests, and their wives, and their children; and they showed him the hidden little doors through which they used to enter and consume what was on the table.

**14:21** Therefore the king put them to death, and handed Bel over into the power of Daniel, who overthrew him and his temple.

**14:22** And there was a great dragon in that place, and the Babylonians revered it.

**14:23** And the king said to Daniel, "Look, now you cannot say that this one is not a living god; so worship him."

**14:24** And Daniel said, "I worship the Lord my God, because He Himself is the living God; but this one is not a living god.

**14:25** As for you, king, give me authority, and I will kill the dragon without sword or club." And the king said, "I give it to you."

**14:26** So Daniel took pitch, fat, and hair, and boiled them together; he made them into lumps and put them into the dragon's mouth, and the dragon burst open. And he said, "Look at the one you were worshiping."

**14:27** When the Babylonians heard this, they were intensely indignant; and gathering together against the king, they said, "The king has become a Jew: he has destroyed Bel, killed the dragon, and put the priests to death."

**14:28** And when they came to the king, they said, "Hand Daniel over to us; otherwise we will kill you and your house."

**14:29** So the king saw that they were rushing upon him violently; and compelled by necessity, he handed Daniel over to them.

**14:30** They threw him into the den of lions, and he was there for six days.

**14:31** Now there were seven lions in the den, and two bodies and two sheep were given to them daily; and at that time these were not given to them, so that they would devour Daniel.

**14:32** Now Habakkuk the prophet was in Judea, and he had cooked a stew and crumbled bread into a bowl, and he was going into the field to carry it to the reapers.

**14:33** And the angel of the Lord said to Habakkuk, "Carry the meal that you have into Babylon to Daniel, who is in the den of lions."

**14:34** And Habakkuk said, "Lord, I have not seen Babylon, and I do not know the den."

**14:35** And the angel of the Lord took hold of him by the crown of his head, and carried him by the hair of his head, and set him down in Babylon above the den in the rush of his spirit.

**14:36** And Habakkuk cried out, saying, "Daniel, servant of God, take the meal that God has sent to you."

**14:37** And Daniel said, "You have remembered me, God, and You have not forsaken those who love You."

**14:38** And rising up, Daniel ate. Meanwhile, the angel of the Lord immediately restored Habakkuk to his own place.

**14:39** So the king came on the seventh day to mourn for Daniel; and he came to the den and looked in, and look, Daniel was sitting in the midst of the lions.

**14:40** And the king cried out with a loud voice, saying, "You are great, Lord God of Daniel!" And he drew him up out of the den of lions.

**14:41** As for those who had been the cause of his destruction, he threw them into the den, and they were devoured in an instant before him.

**14:42** Then the king said, "Let all who dwell in the entire earth stand in awe of the God of Daniel; for He Himself is the savior, working signs and wonders on earth, who delivered Daniel from the den of lions."

## Translator Notes

- **14:1** — In native Clementine Vulgate order, Bel and the Dragon forms chapter 14 (42 verses); previously 5 scattered verses were interleaved as unplaced entries after Daniel 1:21.
- **14:2** — Latin artabae (Persian/Egyptian dry grain measures) and amphorae (liquid measures) are preserved literally.
- **14:35** — Latin in impetu spiritus sui ('in the rush of his spirit') refers to the angel's breath/spirit or wind (lowercase).
- **14:42** — Stage 1 restored Clementine Vulgate Daniel 14:42; distinct from Daniel 6:27-28 despite overlapping praise wording.
