## Text

**4:4** I, Nebuchadnezzar, was at peace in my house and thriving in my palace.

**4:5** I saw a dream that terrified me, and my thoughts on my bed and the visions of my head disturbed me.

**4:10** The vision of my head on my couch: I was watching, and look, a tree in the middle of the earth, and its height was immense.

**4:11** The tree was great and strong, and its tallness touched heaven; the sight of it reached all the way to the ends of the entire earth.

**4:12** Its leaves were most beautiful and its fruit exceedingly abundant, and in it was food for all. Beneath it lived animals and beasts, and in its branches the birds of heaven made their dwelling, and from it all flesh fed.

**4:13** I was watching in the vision of my head on my bed, and look, a watcher and a holy one descended from heaven.

**4:14** He cried out loudly and spoke in this way: "Cut down the tree and lop off its branches; shake off its leaves and scatter its fruits. Let the beasts that are beneath it flee, and the birds from its branches.

**4:15** Nevertheless, leave the shoot of its roots in the earth, and let it be bound with a band of iron and bronze among the grasses that are outside; and let him be moistened with the dew of heaven, and let his portion be with the wild beasts in the grass of the earth.

**4:16** Let his heart be changed from a human one, and let the heart of a wild beast be given to him, and let seven times change over him.

**4:17** It is decreed by the sentence of the watchers, and it is the word of the holy ones and the petition, until the living come to know that the Most High rules in the kingdom of men, and He will give it to whomever He wills, and He will appoint the lowliest man over it."

**4:18** This dream I, King Nebuchadnezzar, saw. You, therefore, Belteshazzar, tell the interpretation quickly, because all the wise men of my kingdom are unable to declare the explanation to me; but you are able, because the spirit of the holy gods is in you.

**4:19** Then Daniel, whose name was Belteshazzar, began to think silently within himself for about one hour, and his thoughts were disturbing him. And the king answered and said, "Belteshazzar, do not let the dream and its interpretation disturb you." Belteshazzar answered and said, "My lord, may the dream be for those who hate you, and its interpretation for your enemies.

**4:20** The tree that you saw, lofty and sturdy, whose height reaches to heaven, and the sight of it across all the earth,

**4:21** and its branches most beautiful and its fruit exceedingly abundant, and food for all in it, the beasts of the field living beneath it and the birds of heaven dwelling in its branches:

**4:22** it is you, king, who have been made great and have grown strong; and your greatness has increased and reached all the way to heaven, and your dominion to the ends of the entire earth.

**4:23** And as for the king seeing a watcher and a holy one descend from heaven and say, 'Cut down the tree and destroy it; yet leave the shoot of its roots in the earth, and let it be bound with iron and bronze among the grasses outside, and let him be sprinkled with the dew of heaven, and let his pasture be with the wild beasts, until seven times change over him':

**4:24** this is the interpretation of the sentence of the Most High, which has come upon my lord the king:

**4:25** They will cast you out from among men, and your dwelling will be with beasts and wild animals, and you will eat grass like an ox, and you will be drenched with the dew of heaven. Seven times will also change over you, until you know that the Most High rules over the kingdom of men and gives it to whomever He wills.

**4:26** And as for his commanding that the shoot of its roots, that is, of the tree, should be left: your kingdom will remain yours after you have come to know that dominion is heavenly.

**4:27** For this reason, king, let my counsel be pleasing to you, and redeem your sins with almsgiving, and your iniquities with acts of mercy toward the poor; perhaps He will forgive your offenses."

**4:28** All of these things came upon King Nebuchadnezzar.

**4:29** After the end of twelve months, he was walking about in the palace of Babylon.

**4:30** And the king answered and said, "Is this not great Babylon, which I myself built as a house of the kingdom, in the strength of my might and in the glory of my splendor?"

**4:31** And while the word was still in the mouth of the king, a voice rushed down from heaven: "To you it is spoken, King Nebuchadnezzar: Your kingdom will pass away from you,

**4:32** and they will cast you out from among men, and your dwelling will be with beasts and wild animals; you will eat grass like an ox, and seven times will change over you, until you know that the Most High rules in the kingdom of men and gives it to whomever He wills."

**4:33** In that same hour the word was fulfilled upon Nebuchadnezzar, and he was cast out from among men, and he ate grass like an ox, and his body was soaked with the dew of heaven, until his hair grew in the likeness of eagles and his nails like those of birds.

**4:34** Therefore, after the end of the days, I, Nebuchadnezzar, lifted my eyes to heaven, and my reason was restored to me; and I blessed the Most High, and praised and glorified Him who lives forever, because His dominion is an everlasting dominion and His kingdom is from generation to generation.

**4:36** At that very time my reason returned to me, and I attained the honor and splendor of my kingdom; and my form returned to me, and my nobles and my magistrates sought me out, and I was restored in my kingdom, and greater magnificence was added to me.

**4:37** King Nebuchadnezzar, to all peoples, nations, and languages who live in the entire earth: may peace be multiplied to you. The Most High God has worked signs and wonders with me. Therefore it has pleased me to proclaim His signs, because they are great, and His wonders, because they are mighty; and His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and His dominion is from generation to generation. Now therefore, I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise and magnify and glorify the King of heaven, because all His works are true and His ways are judgments, and He is able to humble those who walk in pride.

## Translator Notes

- **4:15** — Latin germen radicum ejus literally means "the shoot of its roots" (similarly in 4:23 and 4:26), where the Aramaic text refers to the stump of the tree's roots.
- **4:16** — Latin septem tempora mutentur super eum literally means "let seven times change over him" (also in 4:23, 4:25, and 4:32), reflecting the Aramaic verb's dual sense of passing and alternating.
- **4:18** — The Vulgate spells both Daniel's Babylonian name (Belteshazzar, here and in 4:19, 5:12) and the king's name in chapter 5 (Belshazzar) identically as Baltassar, following the Greek Baltasar; standard modern English spellings are used to distinguish them.
- **4:27** — Vulgate eleemosynis redime ("redeem with almsgiving") and forsitan ignoscet delictis tuis ("perhaps He will forgive your offenses") differ from the Aramaic, which has "by righteousness" and "perhaps there may be a lengthening of your prosperity."
- **4:37** — This verse renders the Latin 3:98-100 (Nebuchadnezzar's proclamation and praise, English 4:1-3) followed by the Latin 4:37 (the king's final praise).
- **4:6 *(additional)*** — The Vulgate uses Baltassar for both Daniel's Babylonian name (Belteshazzar) and the king in chapter five (Belshazzar).
