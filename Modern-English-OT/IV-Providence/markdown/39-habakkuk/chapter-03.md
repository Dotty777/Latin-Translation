## Text

**3:1** A prayer of the prophet Habakkuk, for ignorances.

**3:2** Lord, I have heard Your report, and I was afraid. Lord, Your work, in the midst of the years bring it to life; in the midst of the years You will make it known; when You are angry, You will remember mercy.

**3:3** God will come from the south, and the Holy One from Mount Paran; His glory has covered the heavens, and the earth is full of His praise.

**3:4** His radiance will be like the light; horns are in His hands; there His strength is hidden.

**3:5** Before His face death will go, and the devil will go forth before His feet.

**3:6** He stood and measured the earth; He looked and dissolved the nations, and the mountains of the age were crushed; the hills of the world were bowed down by the paths of His eternity.

**3:7** Because of iniquity I saw the tents of Ethiopia; the skins of the land of Midian will be troubled.

**3:8** Were You angry with the rivers, Lord? Or was Your fury against the rivers, or Your indignation against the sea? For You will ride upon Your horses, and Your chariots are salvation.

**3:9** Rousing, You will rouse Your bow, the oaths to the tribes that You have spoken; You will split the rivers of the earth.

**3:10** The mountains saw You and were in pain; a torrent of waters passed by; the deep gave its voice; the height lifted up its hands.

**3:11** The sun and the moon stood still in their dwelling; in the light of Your arrows they will go, in the radiance of Your flashing spear.

**3:12** In rage You will trample the earth; in fury You will astound the nations.

**3:13** You went forth for the salvation of Your people, for salvation with Your anointed; You struck the head from the house of the wicked; You laid bare its foundation all the way to the neck.

**3:14** You cursed his scepters, the head of his warriors, coming like a whirlwind to scatter me; their exultation was like that of one who devours the poor in secret.

**3:15** You made a way in the sea for Your horses, in the mud of many waters.

**3:16** I heard, and my belly was greatly troubled; at the voice my lips trembled. Let rottenness enter into my bones and swarm beneath me, so that I may rest in the day of tribulation, so that I may go up to our girded people.

**3:17** For the fig tree will not bloom, and there will be no sprout on the vines; the work of the olive will deceive, and the fields will not bring food; the flock will be cut off from the sheepfold, and there will be no herd in the stalls.

**3:18** Yet I will rejoice in the Lord, and I will exult in God my Jesus.

**3:19** God the Lord is my strength, and He will make my feet like those of deer; and the Victor will lead me, singing in psalms, upon my high places.

## Translator Notes

- **3:1** — Vulgate 'pro ignorantiis' ("for ignorances") interprets the obscure Hebrew musical term 'al shigyonot' ("according to Shigionoth") through the verb root meaning to err in ignorance.
- **3:4** — Vulgate 'cornua in manibus ejus' ("horns are in His hands") translates Hebrew 'qarnayim' literally as "horns" rather than "rays" and places them "in His hands."
- **3:5** — Vulgate 'mors' ("death") and 'diabolus' ("the devil") are distinctive Latin renderings of Hebrew 'dever' ("pestilence") and 'reshef' ("plague" or "fiery bolt").
- **3:7** — Vulgate uses the proper name 'Æthiopiæ' ("Ethiopia") for Hebrew 'Kushan', and renders Hebrew 'tachat awen' ("under affliction") as 'Pro iniquitate' ("Because of iniquity").
- **3:16** — Vulgate 'scateat' ("swarm") and 'ut ascendam ad populum accinctum nostrum' ("so that I may go up to our girded people") diverge significantly from the Hebrew, which speaks of trembling in place and an invading people coming up.
- **3:18** — Vulgate 'in Deo Jesu meo' ("in God my Jesus") renders Hebrew 'yishi' ("my salvation") with the proper name Jesus.
- **3:19** — Vulgate 'victor in psalmis canentem' ("the Victor ... singing in psalms") integrates into the closing sentence what in Hebrew is a musical subscription ("For the choirmaster: with stringed instruments").
