JONAH 1

1:1  LA: Et factum est verbum Domini ad Jonam, filium Amathi, dicens :
     EN: The word of the Lord came to Jonah son of Amittai, saying:

1:2  LA: Surge, et vade in Niniven, civitatem grandem, et prædica in ea, quia ascendit malitia ejus coram me.
     EN: “Rise and go to Nineveh, the great city, and preach in it, for its wickedness has come up before Me.”

1:3  LA: Et surrexit Jonas, ut fugeret in Tharsis a facie Domini, et descendit in Joppen : et invenit navem euntem in Tharsis, et dedit naulum ejus, et descendit in eam ut iret cum eis in Tharsis a facie Domini.
     EN: Jonah rose to flee to Tarshish from the Lord's presence and went down to Joppa. He found a ship going to Tarshish, paid its fare, and went down into it to travel with them to Tarshish, away from the Lord's presence.

1:4  LA: Dominus autem misit ventum magnum in mare : et facta est tempestas magna in mari, et navis periclitabatur conteri.
     EN: But the Lord sent a great wind upon the sea, and a great storm arose on the sea; the ship was in danger of breaking apart.

1:5  LA: Et timuerunt nautæ, et clamaverunt viri ad deum suum, et miserunt vasa quæ erant in navi, in mare, ut alleviaretur ab eis ; et Jonas descendit ad interiora navis, et dormiebat sopore gravi.
     EN: The sailors were afraid, and each cried to his own god. They threw the equipment that was in the ship into the sea to lighten it for themselves. But Jonah had gone down into the inner part of the ship and was sleeping deeply.

1:6  LA: Et accessit ad eum gubernator, et dixit ei : Quid tu sopore deprimeris ? surge, invoca Deum tuum, si forte recogitet Deus de nobis, et non pereamus.
     EN: The helmsman approached him and said, “Why are you sunk in sleep? Get up, call upon your God! Perhaps God will take thought for us, and we will not perish.”

1:7  LA: Et dixit vir ad collegam suum : Venite et mittamus sortes, et sciamus quare hoc malum sit nobis. Et miserunt sortes, et cecidit sors super Jonam.
     EN: They said to one another, “Come, let us cast lots and learn why this disaster has come upon us.” They cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah.

1:8  LA: Et dixerunt ad eum : Indica nobis cujus causa malum istud sit nobis : quod est opus tuum ? quæ terra tua, et quo vadis ? vel ex quo populo es tu ?
     EN: They said to him, “Tell us on whose account this disaster has come upon us. What is your work? What is your country, and where are you going? And from what people are you?”

1:9  LA: Et dixit ad eos : Hebræus ego sum, et Dominum Deum cæli ego timeo, qui fecit mare et aridam.
     EN: He said to them, “I am a Hebrew, and I fear the Lord, the God of heaven, who made the sea and the dry land.”

1:10  LA: Et timuerunt viri timore magno, et dixerunt ad eum : Quid hoc fecisti ? cognoverunt enim viri quod a facie Domini fugeret, quia indicaverat eis.
      EN: The men were greatly afraid and said to him, “What have you done?” For the men knew that he was fleeing from the Lord's presence, because he had told them.

1:11  LA: Et dixerunt ad eum : Quid faciemus tibi, et cessabit mare a nobis ? quia mare ibat, et intumescebat.
      EN: They said to him, “What shall we do to you so that the sea will grow calm for us?” For the sea kept swelling.

1:12  LA: Et dixit ad eos : Tollite me, et mittite in mare, et cessabit mare a vobis : scio enim ego quoniam propter me tempestas hæc grandis venit super vos.
      EN: He said to them, “Pick me up and throw me into the sea, and the sea will grow calm for you. For I know that this great storm has come upon you because of me.”

1:13  LA: Et remigabant viri ut reverterentur ad aridam, et non valebant, quia mare ibat, et intumescebat super eos.
      EN: The men rowed to return to dry land, but they could not, for the sea kept swelling against them.

1:14  LA: Et clamaverunt ad Dominum, et dixerunt : Quæsumus, Domine, ne pereamus in anima viri istius, et ne des super nos sanguinem innocentem : quia tu, Domine, sicut voluisti, fecisti.
      EN: They cried to the Lord and said, “We beg You, Lord, do not let us perish for this man's life, and do not lay innocent blood upon us, for You, Lord, have done as You willed.”

1:15  LA: Et tulerunt Jonam, et miserunt in mare : et stetit mare a fervore suo.
      EN: They picked Jonah up and threw him into the sea, and the sea ceased its raging.

1:16  LA: Et timuerunt viri timore magno Dominum : et immolaverunt hostias Domino, et voverunt vota.
      EN: The men feared the Lord with great fear; they offered sacrifices to the Lord and made vows.

JONAH 2

2:1  LA: Et præparavit Dominus piscem grandem ut deglutiret Jonam : et erat Jonas in ventre piscis tribus diebus et tribus noctibus.
     EN: The Lord prepared a great fish to swallow Jonah, and Jonah was in the fish's belly three days and three nights.

2:2  LA: Et oravit Jonas ad Dominum Deum suum de ventre piscis,
     EN: Jonah prayed to the Lord his God from the fish's belly,

2:3  LA: et dixit : [Clamavi de tribulatione mea ad Dominum, et exaudivit me ; de ventre inferi clamavi, et exaudisti vocem meam.
     EN: and said, “In my affliction I cried to the Lord, and He answered me; from the belly of the underworld I cried, and You heard my voice.

2:4  LA: Et projecisti me in profundum in corde maris, et flumen circumdedit me : omnes gurgites tui, et fluctus tui super me transierunt.
     EN: You cast me into the depths, into the heart of the sea, and the current surrounded me; all Your surging waters and waves passed over me.

2:5  LA: Et ego dixi : Abjectus sum a conspectu oculorum tuorum ; verumtamen rursus videbo templum sanctum tuum.
     EN: I said, ‘I have been cast away from the sight of Your eyes; yet I will see Your holy temple again.’

2:6  LA: Circumdederunt me aquæ usque ad animam : abyssus vallavit me, pelagus operuit caput meum.
     EN: Waters surrounded me to my very life; the abyss enclosed me, the deep covered my head.

2:7  LA: Ad extrema montium descendi ; terræ vectes concluserunt me in æternum : et sublevabis de corruptione vitam meam, Domine Deus meus.
     EN: I descended to the extremities of the mountains; the earth's bars shut me in forever. Yet You will raise my life from corruption, Lord my God.

2:8  LA: Cum angustiaretur in me anima mea, Domini recordatus sum : ut veniat ad te oratio mea, ad templum sanctum tuum.
     EN: When my soul was distressed within me, I remembered the Lord, that my prayer might come to You, to Your holy temple.

2:9  LA: Qui custodiunt vanitates frustra, misericordiam suam derelinquunt.
     EN: Those who vainly cling to vanities abandon their own mercy.

2:10  LA: Ego autem in voce laudis immolabo tibi : quæcumque vovi, reddam pro salute Domino.]
      EN: But with a voice of praise I will sacrifice to You; whatever I have vowed, I will repay to the Lord for salvation.”

2:11  LA: Et dixit Dominus pisci, et evomuit Jonam in aridam.
      EN: The Lord spoke to the fish, and it vomited Jonah onto dry land.

JONAH 3

3:1  LA: Et factum est verbum Domini ad Jonam secundo, dicens :
     EN: The word of the Lord came to Jonah a second time, saying:

3:2  LA: Surge, et vade in Niniven, civitatem magnam, et prædica in ea prædicationem quam ego loquor ad te.
     EN: “Rise and go to Nineveh, the great city, and preach in it the preaching that I speak to you.”

3:3  LA: Et surrexit Jonas, et abiit in Niniven juxta verbum Domini : et Ninive erat civitas magna, itinere trium dierum.
     EN: Jonah rose and went to Nineveh according to the Lord's word. Now Nineveh was a great city, a journey of three days.

3:4  LA: Et cœpit Jonas introire in civitatem itinere diei unius : et clamavit, et dixit : Adhuc quadraginta dies, et Ninive subvertetur.
     EN: Jonah began to enter the city, going a day's journey, and he cried out, “Forty more days, and Nineveh will be overthrown!”

3:5  LA: Et crediderunt viri Ninivitæ in Deum, et prædicaverunt jejunium, et vestiti sunt saccis, a majore usque ad minorem.
     EN: The people of Nineveh believed in God, proclaimed a fast, and put on sackcloth, from the greatest to the least.

3:6  LA: Et pervenit verbum ad regem Ninive : et surrexit de solio suo, et abjecit vestimentum suum a se, et indutus est sacco, et sedit in cinere.
     EN: The word reached the king of Nineveh. He rose from his throne, took off his robe, put on sackcloth, and sat in ashes.

3:7  LA: Et clamavit, et dixit in Ninive ex ore regis et principum ejus, dicens : Homines, et jumenta, et boves, et pecora non gustent quidquam : nec pascantur, et aquam non bibant.
     EN: He had a proclamation made in Nineveh by the authority of the king and his princes, saying, “Let neither people nor beasts, neither cattle nor sheep, taste anything; let them neither feed nor drink water.

3:8  LA: Et operiantur saccis homines et jumenta, et clament ad Dominum in fortitudine : et convertatur vir a via sua mala, et ab iniquitate quæ est in manibus eorum.
     EN: Let people and beasts be covered with sackcloth, and let them cry mightily to the Lord. Let each turn from his evil way and from the injustice in their hands.

3:9  LA: Quis scit si convertatur et ignoscat Deus, et revertatur a furore iræ suæ, et non peribimus ?
     EN: Who knows whether God may turn and forgive, and turn away from His furious anger, so that we will not perish?”

3:10  LA: Et vidit Deus opera eorum, quia conversi sunt de via sua mala : et misertus est Deus super malitiam quam locutus fuerat ut faceret eis, et non fecit.
      EN: God saw their deeds, that they had turned from their evil way. God relented over the calamity He had said He would bring upon them, and He did not bring it.

JONAH 4

4:1  LA: Et afflictus est Jonas afflictione magna, et iratus est :
     EN: Jonah was greatly distressed and became angry.

4:2  LA: et oravit ad Dominum, et dixit : Obsecro, Domine, numquid non hoc est verbum meum cum adhuc essem in terra mea ? propter hoc præoccupavi ut fugerem in Tharsis : scio enim quia tu Deus clemens et misericors es, patiens et multæ miserationis, et ignoscens super malitia.
     EN: He prayed to the Lord and said, “Please, Lord, was this not what I said while I was still in my own country? That is why I made haste to flee to Tarshish, for I knew that You are a gracious and merciful God, patient and rich in compassion, and forgiving in the face of calamity.

4:3  LA: Et nunc, Domine, tolle, quæso, animam meam a me, quia melior est mihi mors quam vita.
     EN: Now, Lord, I beg You, take my life from me, for death is better for me than life.”

4:4  LA: Et dixit Dominus : Putasne bene irasceris tu ?
     EN: The Lord said, “Do you think you are right to be angry?”

4:5  LA: Et egressus est Jonas de civitate, et sedit contra orientem civitatis : et fecit sibimet umbraculum ibi, et sedebat subter illud in umbra, donec videret quid accideret civitati.
     EN: Jonah went out of the city and sat to the east of it. He made himself a shelter there and sat beneath it in the shade until he could see what would happen to the city.

4:6  LA: Et præparavit Dominus Deus hederam, et ascendit super caput Jonæ, ut esset umbra super caput ejus, et protegeret eum (laboraverat enim) : et lætatus est Jonas super hedera lætitia magna.
     EN: The Lord God prepared an ivy plant, and it grew up over Jonah's head, to give shade over his head and protect him, for he had been suffering. Jonah rejoiced greatly over the ivy.

4:7  LA: Et paravit Deus vermen ascensu diluculi in crastinum : et percussit hederam, et exaruit.
     EN: At the rise of dawn the next day God prepared a worm, and it struck the ivy, and it withered.

4:8  LA: Et cum ortus fuisset sol, præcepit Dominus vento calido et urenti : et percussit sol super caput Jonæ, et æstuabat : et petivit animæ suæ ut moreretur, et dixit : Melius est mihi mori quam vivere.
     EN: When the sun had risen, the Lord commanded a hot and scorching wind. The sun struck Jonah's head, and he sweltered; he asked for his life to end and said, “It is better for me to die than to live.”

4:9  LA: Et dixit Dominus ad Jonam : Putasne bene irasceris tu super hedera ? Et dixit : Bene irascor ego usque ad mortem.
     EN: The Lord said to Jonah, “Do you think you are right to be angry over the ivy?” He said, “I am right to be angry, even to death.”

4:10  LA: Et dixit Dominus : Tu doles super hederam in qua non laborasti, neque fecisti ut cresceret ; quæ sub una nocte nata est, et sub una nocte periit :
      EN: The Lord said, “You grieve over the ivy for which you did not labor and which you did not make grow, which came into being in one night and perished in one night.

4:11  LA: et ego non parcam Ninive, civitati magnæ, in qua sunt plus quam centum viginti millia hominum qui nesciunt quid sit inter dexteram et sinistram suam, et jumenta multa ?
      EN: And shall I not spare Nineveh, the great city, in which there are more than a hundred and twenty thousand people who do not know their right hand from their left, and many beasts?”
