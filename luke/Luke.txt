LUKE 1

1:1  LA: Quoniam quidem multi conati sunt ordinare narrationem, quæ in nobis completæ sunt, rerum:
     EN: Since many have undertaken to arrange an account of the things accomplished among us,

1:2  LA: sicut tradiderunt nobis, qui ab initio ipsi viderunt, et ministri fuerunt sermonis:
     EN: just as those who from the beginning saw them themselves and were ministers of the word handed them down to us,

1:3  LA: visum est et mihi, assecuto omnia a principio diligenter, ex ordine tibi scribere, optime Theophile,
     EN: it seemed good to me also, having carefully followed everything from the beginning, to write to you in order, most excellent Theophilus,

1:4  LA: ut cognoscas eorum verborum, de quibus eruditus es, veritatem.
     EN: so that you may know the truth of the words in which you have been instructed.

1:5  LA: Fuit in diebus Herodis, regis Judææ, sacerdos quidam nomine Zacharias de vice Abia, et uxor illius de filiabus Aaron, et nomen ejus Elisabeth.
     EN: There was in the days of Herod, the king of Judea, a certain priest named Zechariah, of the course of Abijah: and his wife was of the daughters of Aaron, and her name Elizabeth.

1:6  LA: Erant autem justi ambo ante Deum, incedentes in omnibus mandatis et justificationibus Domini sine querela.
     EN: They were both righteous before God, walking blamelessly in all the Lord’s commandments and ordinances.

1:7  LA: Et non erat illis filius, eo quod esset Elisabeth sterilis, et ambo processissent in diebus suis.
     EN: And they had no son, for that Elizabeth was barren: and they both were well advanced in years.

1:8  LA: Factum est autem, cum sacerdotio fungeretur in ordine vicis suæ ante Deum,
     EN: And it happened, when he executed the priestly function in the order of his course before God,

1:9  LA: secundum consuetudinem sacerdotii, sorte exiit ut incensum poneret, ingressus in templum Domini:
     EN: According to the custom of the priestly office, it was his lot to offer incense, going into the temple of the Lord.

1:10  LA: et omnis multitudo populi erat orans foris hora incensi.
      EN: And all the multitude of the people was praying without, at the hour of incense.

1:11  LA: Apparuit autem illi angelus Domini, stans a dextris altaris incensi.
      EN: And there appeared to him an angel of the Lord, standing on the right side of the altar of incense.

1:12  LA: Et Zacharias turbatus est videns, et timor irruit super eum.
      EN: And Zechariah seeing him, was troubled: and fear fell upon him.

1:13  LA: Ait autem ad illum angelus: Ne timeas, Zacharia, quoniam exaudita est deprecatio tua: et uxor tua Elisabeth pariet tibi filium, et vocabis nomen ejus Joannem:
      EN: But the angel said to him: Do not fear, Zechariah, for your prayer is heard: and your wife Elizabeth shall bear you a son. And you shall call his name John.

1:14  LA: et erit gaudium tibi, et exsultatio, et multi in nativitate ejus gaudebunt:
      EN: And you shall have joy and gladness: and many shall rejoice in his nativity.

1:15  LA: erit enim magnus coram Domino: et vinum et siceram non bibet, et Spiritu Sancto replebitur adhuc ex utero matris suæ:
      EN: For he will be great before the Lord. He will drink neither wine nor strong drink, and will be filled with the Holy Spirit even from his mother’s womb.

1:16  LA: et multos filiorum Israël convertet ad Dominum Deum ipsorum:
      EN: And he shall convert many of the children of Israel to the Lord their God.

1:17  LA: et ipse præcedet ante illum in spiritu et virtute Eliæ: ut convertat corda patrum in filios, et incredulos ad prudentiam justorum, parare Domino plebem perfectam.
      EN: He will go before him in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of fathers toward their children and the unbelieving to the understanding of the righteous, to prepare a people made ready for the Lord.

1:18  LA: Et dixit Zacharias ad angelum: Unde hoc sciam? ego enim sum senex, et uxor mea processit in diebus suis.
      EN: And Zechariah said to the angel: Whereby shall I know this? For I am an old man, and my wife is advanced in years.

1:19  LA: Et respondens angelus dixit ei: Ego sum Gabriel, qui asto ante Deum: et missus sum loqui ad te, et hæc tibi evangelizare.
      EN: And the angel answered him: I am Gabriel, who stand before God and am sent to speak to you and to bring you these good news.

1:20  LA: Et ecce eris tacens, et non poteris loqui usque in diem quo hæc fiant, pro eo quod non credidisti verbis meis, quæ implebuntur in tempore suo.
      EN: And behold, you shall be dumb and shall not be able to speak until the day wherein these things will happen: because you have not believed my words, which shall be fulfilled in their time.

1:21  LA: Et erat plebs exspectans Zachariam: et mirabantur quod tardaret ipse in templo.
      EN: And the people were waiting for Zechariah: and they wondered that he tarried so long in the temple.

1:22  LA: Egressus autem non poterat loqui ad illos, et cognoverunt quod visionem vidisset in templo. Et ipse erat innuens illis, et permansit mutus.
      EN: And when he came out, he could not speak to them: and they understood that he had seen a vision in the temple. And he made signs to them and remained dumb.

1:23  LA: Et factum est, ut impleti sunt dies officii ejus, abiit in domum suam:
      EN: And it happened, after the days of his office were accomplished, he departed to his own house.

1:24  LA: post hos autem dies concepit Elisabeth uxor ejus, et occultabat se mensibus quinque, dicens:
      EN: And after those days, Elizabeth his wife conceived and hid herself five months, saying:

1:25  LA: Quia sic fecit mihi Dominus in diebus, quibus respexit auferre opprobrium meum inter homines.
      EN: Thus has the Lord dealt with me in the days wherein he has had regard to take away my reproach among men.

1:26  LA: In mense autem sexto, missus est angelus Gabriel a Deo in civitatem Galilææ, cui nomen Nazareth,
      EN: In the sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent by God to a city of Galilee named Nazareth,

1:27  LA: ad virginem desponsatam viro, cui nomen erat Joseph, de domo David: et nomen virginis Maria.
      EN: to a virgin betrothed to a man named Joseph, of the house of David; and the virgin’s name was Mary.

1:28  LA: Et ingressus angelus ad eam dixit: Ave gratia plena: Dominus tecum: benedicta tu in mulieribus.
      EN: Entering, the angel said to her: Hail, full of grace; the Lord is with you; blessed are you among women.

1:29  LA: Quæ cum audisset, turbata est in sermone ejus, et cogitabat qualis esset ista salutatio.
      EN: When she heard this, she was troubled at his word and considered what kind of greeting this might be.

1:30  LA: Et ait angelus ei: Ne timeas, Maria: invenisti enim gratiam apud Deum.
      EN: The angel said to her: Do not fear, Mary, for you have found grace with God.

1:31  LA: Ecce concipies in utero, et paries filium, et vocabis nomen ejus Jesum:
      EN: Behold, you will conceive in your womb and bear a son, and you shall call his name Jesus.

1:32  LA: hic erit magnus, et Filius Altissimi vocabitur, et dabit illi Dominus Deus sedem David patris ejus: et regnabit in domo Jacob in æternum,
      EN: He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give him the throne of David his father, and he will reign over the house of Jacob forever,

1:33  LA: et regni ejus non erit finis.
      EN: and of his kingdom there will be no end.

1:34  LA: Dixit autem Maria ad angelum: Quomodo fiet istud, quoniam virum non cognosco?
      EN: But Mary said to the angel: How will this be, since I do not know a man?

1:35  LA: Et respondens angelus dixit ei: Spiritus Sanctus superveniet in te, et virtus Altissimi obumbrabit tibi. Ideoque et quod nascetur ex te sanctum, vocabitur Filius Dei.
      EN: The angel answered her: The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. Therefore the holy one to be born of you will also be called the Son of God.

1:36  LA: Et ecce Elisabeth cognata tua, et ipsa concepit filium in senectute sua: et hic mensis sextus est illi, quæ vocatur sterilis:
      EN: And behold, Elizabeth your relative has also conceived a son in her old age, and this is the sixth month for her who is called barren,

1:37  LA: quia non erit impossibile apud Deum omne verbum.
      EN: for no word will be impossible with God.

1:38  LA: Dixit autem Maria: Ecce ancilla Domini: fiat mihi secundum verbum tuum. Et discessit ab illa angelus.
      EN: Mary said: Behold the handmaid of the Lord; let it be done to me according to your word. And the angel departed from her.

1:39  LA: Exsurgens autem Maria in diebus illis, abiit in montana cum festinatione, in civitatem Juda:
      EN: And Mary rising up in those days, went into the hill country with haste into a city of Juda.

1:40  LA: et intravit in domum Zachariæ, et salutavit Elisabeth.
      EN: And she entered into the house of Zechariah and saluted Elizabeth.

1:41  LA: Et factum est, ut audivit salutationem Mariæ Elisabeth, exsultavit infans in utero ejus: et repleta est Spiritu Sancto Elisabeth:
      EN: When Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting, the infant leaped in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit.

1:42  LA: et exclamavit voce magna, et dixit: Benedicta tu inter mulieres, et benedictus fructus ventris tui.
      EN: She cried out with a loud voice and said: Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb.

1:43  LA: Et unde hoc mihi, ut veniat mater Domini mei ad me?
      EN: And how is this granted to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?

1:44  LA: Ecce enim ut facta est vox salutationis tuæ in auribus meis, exsultavit in gaudio infans in utero meo.
      EN: For behold, when the sound of your greeting reached my ears, the infant in my womb leaped for joy.

1:45  LA: Et beata, quæ credidisti, quoniam perficientur ea, quæ dicta sunt tibi a Domino.
      EN: And blessed are you who believed, for the things spoken to you by the Lord will be fulfilled.

1:46  LA: Et ait Maria: Magnificat anima mea Dominum:
      EN: And Mary said: My soul magnifies the Lord,

1:47  LA: et exsultavit spiritus meus in Deo salutari meo.
      EN: and my spirit has rejoiced in God my savior,

1:48  LA: Quia respexit humilitatem ancillæ suæ: ecce enim ex hoc beatam me dicent omnes generationes,
      EN: because he has looked upon the lowliness of his handmaid. For behold, from now on all generations will call me blessed,

1:49  LA: quia fecit mihi magna qui potens est: et sanctum nomen ejus,
      EN: because the Mighty One has done great things for me, and holy is his name.

1:50  LA: et misericordia ejus a progenie in progenies timentibus eum.
      EN: His mercy is from generation to generation for those who fear him.

1:51  LA: Fecit potentiam in brachio suo: dispersit superbos mente cordis sui.
      EN: He has shown power with his arm; he has scattered the proud in the thought of their hearts.

1:52  LA: Deposuit potentes de sede, et exaltavit humiles.
      EN: He has brought down the powerful from their seat and exalted the lowly.

1:53  LA: Esurientes implevit bonis: et divites dimisit inanes.
      EN: He has filled the hungry with good things and sent the rich away empty.

1:54  LA: Suscepit Israël puerum suum, recordatus misericordiæ suæ:
      EN: He has taken up Israel his servant, remembering his mercy,

1:55  LA: sicut locutus est ad patres nostros, Abraham et semini ejus in sæcula.
      EN: as he spoke to our fathers, to Abraham and his seed forever.

1:56  LA: Mansit autem Maria cum illa quasi mensibus tribus: et reversa est in domum suam.
      EN: And Mary abode with her about three months. And she returned to her own house.

1:57  LA: Elisabeth autem impletum est tempus pariendi, et peperit filium.
      EN: Now Elizabeth’s full time of being delivered had come: and she brought forth a son.

1:58  LA: Et audierunt vicini et cognati ejus quia magnificavit Dominus misericordiam suam cum illa, et congratulabantur ei.
      EN: And her neighbors and kinsfolks heard that the Lord had showed his great mercy towards her: and they congratulated with her.

1:59  LA: Et factum est in die octavo, venerunt circumcidere puerum, et vocabant eum nomine patris sui Zachariam.
      EN: And it happened that on the eighth day they came to circumcise the child: and they called him by his father’s name Zechariah.

1:60  LA: Et respondens mater ejus, dixit: Nequaquam, sed vocabitur Joannes.
      EN: And his mother answered: Not so. But he shall be called John.

1:61  LA: Et dixerunt ad illam: Quia nemo est in cognatione tua, qui vocetur hoc nomine.
      EN: And they said to her: There is none of your kindred that is called by this name.

1:62  LA: Innuebant autem patri ejus, quem vellet vocari eum.
      EN: And they made signs to his father, how he would have him called.

1:63  LA: Et postulans pugillarem scripsit, dicens: Joannes est nomen ejus. Et mirati sunt universi.
      EN: And demanding a writing table, he wrote, saying: John is his name. And they all wondered.

1:64  LA: Apertum est autem illico os ejus, et lingua ejus, et loquebatur benedicens Deum.
      EN: And immediately his mouth was opened and his tongue loosed: and he spoke, blessing God.

1:65  LA: Et factus est timor super omnes vicinos eorum: et super omnia montana Judææ divulgabantur omnia verba hæc:
      EN: And fear came upon all their neighbours: and all these things were noised abroad over all the hill country of Judea.

1:66  LA: et posuerunt omnes qui audierant in corde suo, dicentes: Quis, putas, puer iste erit? etenim manus Domini erat cum illo.
      EN: And all they that had heard them laid them up in their heart, saying: What an one, think you, shall this child be? For the hand of the Lord was with him.

1:67  LA: Et Zacharias pater ejus repletus est Spiritu Sancto: et prophetavit, dicens:
      EN: And Zechariah his father was filled with the Holy Spirit. And he prophesied, saying:

1:68  LA: Benedictus Dominus Deus Israël, quia visitavit, et fecit redemptionem plebis suæ:
      EN: Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, for he has visited and brought redemption to his people,

1:69  LA: et erexit cornu salutis nobis in domo David pueri sui,
      EN: and has raised up a horn of salvation for us in the house of David his servant,

1:70  LA: sicut locutus est per os sanctorum, qui a sæculo sunt, prophetarum ejus:
      EN: as he spoke through the mouth of his holy prophets from of old:

1:71  LA: salutem ex inimicis nostris, et de manu omnium qui oderunt nos:
      EN: salvation from our enemies and from the hand of all who hate us,

1:72  LA: ad faciendam misericordiam cum patribus nostris: et memorari testamenti sui sancti:
      EN: to show mercy to our fathers and to remember his holy covenant,

1:73  LA: jusjurandum, quod juravit ad Abraham patrem nostrum, daturum se nobis
      EN: the oath that he swore to Abraham our father, that he would grant us,

1:74  LA: ut sine timore, de manu inimicorum nostrorum liberati, serviamus illi
      EN: delivered from the hand of our enemies, to serve him without fear,

1:75  LA: in sanctitate et justitia coram ipso, omnibus diebus nostris.
      EN: in holiness and righteousness before him all our days.

1:76  LA: Et tu puer, propheta Altissimi vocaberis: præibis enim ante faciem Domini parare vias ejus,
      EN: And you, child, will be called a prophet of the Most High, for you will go before the face of the Lord to prepare his ways,

1:77  LA: ad dandam scientiam salutis plebi ejus in remissionem peccatorum eorum
      EN: to give his people knowledge of salvation in the forgiveness of their sins,

1:78  LA: per viscera misericordiæ Dei nostri, in quibus visitavit nos, oriens ex alto:
      EN: through the tender mercy of our God, by which the dawn from on high has visited us,

1:79  LA: illuminare his qui in tenebris et in umbra mortis sedent: ad dirigendos pedes nostros in viam pacis.
      EN: to shine on those who sit in darkness and the shadow of death, and to guide our feet into the way of peace.

1:80  LA: Puer autem crescebat, et confortabatur spiritu: et erat in desertis usque in diem ostensionis suæ ad Israël.
      EN: And the child grew and was strengthened in spirit: and was in the deserts until the day of his manifestation to Israel.

LUKE 2

2:1  LA: Factum est autem in diebus illis, exiit edictum a Cæsare Augusto ut describeretur universus orbis.
     EN: And it happened that in those days there went out a decree from Caesar Augustus that the whole world should be enrolled.

2:2  LA: Hæc descriptio prima facta est a præside Syriæ Cyrino:
     EN: This enrolling was first made by Cyrinus, the governor of Syria.

2:3  LA: et ibant omnes ut profiterentur singuli in suam civitatem.
     EN: And all went to be enrolled, everyone into his own city.

2:4  LA: Ascendit autem et Joseph a Galilæa de civitate Nazareth in Judæam, in civitatem David, quæ vocatur Bethlehem: eo quod esset de domo et familia David,
     EN: And Joseph also went up from Galilee, out of the city of Nazareth, into Judea, to the city of David, which is called Bethlehem: because he was of the house and family of David.

2:5  LA: ut profiteretur cum Maria desponsata sibi uxore prægnante.
     EN: To be enrolled with Mary his betrothed wife, who was with child.

2:6  LA: Factum est autem, cum essent ibi, impleti sunt dies ut pareret.
     EN: And it happened that when they were there, her days were accomplished that she should be delivered.

2:7  LA: Et peperit filium suum primogenitum, et pannis eum involvit, et reclinavit eum in præsepio: quia non erat eis locus in diversorio.
     EN: She bore her firstborn son, wrapped him in cloths, and laid him in a manger, because there was no place for them in the lodging place.

2:8  LA: Et pastores erant in regione eadem vigilantes, et custodientes vigilias noctis super gregem suum.
     EN: And there were in the same country shepherds watching and keeping the night watches over their flock.

2:9  LA: Et ecce angelus Domini stetit juxta illos, et claritas Dei circumfulsit illos, et timuerunt timore magno.
     EN: And behold an angel of the Lord stood by them and the brightness of God shone round about them: and they feared with a great fear.

2:10  LA: Et dixit illis angelus: Nolite timere: ecce enim evangelizo vobis gaudium magnum, quod erit omni populo:
      EN: And the angel said to them: Do not fear; for, behold, I bring you good news of great joy that shall be to all the people:

2:11  LA: quia natus est vobis hodie Salvator, qui est Christus Dominus, in civitate David.
      EN: For, this day is born to you a Savior, who is Christ the Lord, in the city of David.

2:12  LA: Et hoc vobis signum: invenietis infantem pannis involutum, et positum in præsepio.
      EN: And this shall be a sign to you. You shall find the infant wrapped in swaddling clothes and laid in a manger.

2:13  LA: Et subito facta est cum angelo multitudo militiæ cælestis laudantium Deum, et dicentium:
      EN: And suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly army, praising God and saying:

2:14  LA: Gloria in altissimis Deo, et in terra pax hominibus bonæ voluntatis.
      EN: Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace to people of good will.

2:15  LA: Et factum est, ut discesserunt ab eis angeli in cælum: pastores loquebantur ad invicem: Transeamus usque Bethlehem, et videamus hoc verbum, quod factum est, quod Dominus ostendit nobis.
      EN: And it happened, after the angels departed from them into heaven, the shepherds said one to another: Let us go over to Bethlehem and let us see this word that has happen, which the Lord has showed to us.

2:16  LA: Et venerunt festinantes: et invenerunt Mariam, et Joseph, et infantem positum in præsepio.
      EN: And they came with haste: and they found Mary and Joseph, and the infant lying in the manger.

2:17  LA: Videntes autem cognoverunt de verbo, quod dictum erat illis de puero hoc.
      EN: And seeing, they understood of the word that had been spoken to them concerning this child.

2:18  LA: Et omnes qui audierunt, mirati sunt: et de his quæ dicta erant a pastoribus ad ipsos.
      EN: And all that heard wondered: and at those things that were told them by the shepherds.

2:19  LA: Maria autem conservabat omnia verba hæc, conferens in corde suo.
      EN: But Mary kept all these words, pondering them in her heart.

2:20  LA: Et reversi sunt pastores glorificantes et laudantes Deum in omnibus quæ audierant et viderant, sicut dictum est ad illos.
      EN: And the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all the things they had heard and seen, as it was told to them.

2:21  LA: Et postquam consummati sunt dies octo, ut circumcideretur puer, vocatum est nomen ejus Jesus, quod vocatum est ab angelo priusquam in utero conciperetur.
      EN: And after eight days were accomplished, that the child should be circumcised, his name was called JESUS, which was called by the angel before he was conceived in the womb.

2:22  LA: Et postquam impleti sunt dies purgationis ejus secundum legem Moysi, tulerunt illum in Jerusalem, ut sisterent eum Domino,
      EN: When the days of her purification according to the Law of Moses were completed, they brought him to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord,

2:23  LA: sicut scriptum est in lege Domini: Quia omne masculinum adaperiens vulvam, sanctum Domino vocabitur:
      EN: As it is written in the law of the Lord: Every male opening the womb shall be called holy to the Lord:

2:24  LA: et ut darent hostiam secundum quod dictum est in lege Domini, par turturum, aut duos pullos columbarum.
      EN: And to offer a sacrifice, just as it is written in the law of the Lord, a pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons:

2:25  LA: Et ecce homo erat in Jerusalem, cui nomen Simeon, et homo iste justus, et timoratus, exspectans consolationem Israël: et Spiritus Sanctus erat in eo.
      EN: And behold there was a man in Jerusalem named Simeon: and this man was just and devout, waiting for the consolation of Israel. And the Holy Spirit was in him.

2:26  LA: Et responsum acceperat a Spiritu Sancto, non visurum se mortem, nisi prius videret Christum Domini.
      EN: And he had received an answer from the Holy Spirit, that he should not see death before he had seen the Christ of the Lord.

2:27  LA: Et venit in spiritu in templum. Et cum inducerent puerum Jesum parentes ejus, ut facerent secundum consuetudinem legis pro eo,
      EN: And he came by the Spirit into the temple. And when his parents brought in the child Jesus, to do for him according to the custom of the law,

2:28  LA: et ipse accepit eum in ulnas suas: et benedixit Deum, et dixit:
      EN: He also took him into his arms and blessed God and said

2:29  LA: Nunc dimittis servum tuum Domine, secundum verbum tuum in pace:
      EN: Now you dismiss your servant, Lord, in peace, according to your word,

2:30  LA: quia viderunt oculi mei salutare tuum,
      EN: for my eyes have seen your salvation,

2:31  LA: quod parasti ante faciem omnium populorum:
      EN: which you have prepared before the face of all peoples:

2:32  LA: lumen ad revelationem gentium, et gloriam plebis tuæ Israël.
      EN: a light for revelation to the nations, and the glory of your people Israel.

2:33  LA: Et erat pater ejus et mater mirantes super his quæ dicebantur de illo.
      EN: His father and mother were marveling at the things being said about him.

2:34  LA: Et benedixit illis Simeon, et dixit ad Mariam matrem ejus: Ecce positus est hic in ruinam et in resurrectionem multorum in Israël, et in signum cui contradicetur:
      EN: Simeon blessed them and said to Mary his mother: Behold, this one is set for the fall and rising of many in Israel, and as a sign that will be opposed;

2:35  LA: et tuam ipsius animam pertransibit gladius ut revelentur ex multis cordibus cogitationes.
      EN: and a sword will pierce your own soul, so that the thoughts of many hearts may be revealed.

2:36  LA: Et erat Anna prophetissa, filia Phanuel, de tribu Aser: hæc processerat in diebus multis, et vixerat cum viro suo annis septem a virginitate sua.
      EN: And there was one Anna, a prophetess, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Aser. She was far advanced in years and had lived with her husband seven years from her virginity.

2:37  LA: Et hæc vidua usque ad annos octoginta quatuor: quæ non discedebat de templo, jejuniis et obsecrationibus serviens nocte ac die.
      EN: And she was a widow until fourscore and four years: who departed not from the temple, by fastings and prayers serving night and day.

2:38  LA: Et hæc, ipsa hora superveniens, confitebatur Domino: et loquebatur de illo omnibus, qui exspectabant redemptionem Israël.
      EN: Now she, at the same hour, coming in, confessed to the Lord: and spoke of him to all that looked for the redemption of Israel.

2:39  LA: Et ut perfecerunt omnia secundum legem Domini, reversi sunt in Galilæam in civitatem suam Nazareth.
      EN: And after they had performed all things according to the law of the Lord, they returned into Galilee, to their city Nazareth.

2:40  LA: Puer autem crescebat, et confortabatur plenus sapientia: et gratia Dei erat in illo.
      EN: And the child grew and waxed strong, full of wisdom: and the grace of God was in him.

2:41  LA: Et ibant parentes ejus per omnes annos in Jerusalem, in die solemni Paschæ.
      EN: And his parents went every year to Jerusalem, at the solemn day of the pasch.

      EN: And he charged him that he should tell no one, but: Go, show yourself to the priest and offer for your cleansing just as Moses commanded, for a testimony to them.

5:15  LA: Perambulabat autem magis sermo de illo: et conveniebant turbæ multæ ut audirent, et curarentur ab infirmitatibus suis.
      EN: But the fame of him went abroad the more: and great multitudes came together to hear and to be healed by him of their infirmities.

5:16  LA: Ipse autem secedebat in desertum, et orabat.
      EN: And he retired into the desert; and prayed.

5:17  LA: Et factum est in una dierum, et ipse sedebat docens. Et erant pharisæi sedentes, et legis doctores, qui venerant ex omni castello Galilææ, et Judææ, et Jerusalem: et virtus Domini erat ad sanandum eos.
      EN: And it happened on a certain day, as he sat teaching, that there were also Pharisees and doctors of the law sitting by, that had come out of every town of Galilee and Judea and Jerusalem: and the power of the Lord was to heal them.

5:18  LA: Et ecce viri portantes in lecto hominem, qui erat paralyticus: et quærebant eum inferre, et ponere ante eum.
      EN: And behold, men brought in a bed a man who had the palsy: and they sought means to bring him in and to lay him before him.

5:19  LA: Et non invenientes qua parte illum inferrent præ turba, ascenderunt supra tectum, et per tegulas summiserunt eum cum lecto in medium ante Jesum.
      EN: And when they could not find by what way they might bring him in, because of the multitude, they went up upon the roof and let him down through the tiles with his bed into the midst before Jesus.

5:20  LA: Quorum fidem ut vidit, dixit: Homo, remittuntur tibi peccata tua.
      EN: Whose faith when he saw, he said: Man, your sins are forgiven you.

5:21  LA: Et cœperunt cogitare scribæ et pharisæi, dicentes: Quis est hic, qui loquitur blasphemias? quis potest dimittere peccata, nisi solus Deus?
      EN: And the scribes and Pharisees began to think, saying: Who is this who speaks blasphemies? Who can forgive sins, but God alone?

5:22  LA: Ut cognovit autem Jesus cogitationes eorum, respondens, dixit ad illos: Quid cogitatis in cordibus vestris?
      EN: And when Jesus knew their thoughts, answering he said to them: What is it you think in your hearts?

5:23  LA: Quid est facilius dicere: Dimittuntur tibi peccata: an dicere: Surge, et ambula?
      EN: Which is easier to say: Your sins are forgiven you; or to say: Arise and walk?

5:24  LA: Ut autem sciatis quia Filius hominis habet potestatem in terra dimittendi peccata, (ait paralytico) tibi dico, surge, tolle lectum tuum, et vade in domum tuam.
      EN: But that you may know that the Son of Man has the power on earth to forgive sins (he says to the sick of the palsy), I say to you, Arise, take up your bed and go into your house.

5:25  LA: Et confestim consurgens coram illis, tulit lectum in quo jacebat: et abiit in domum suam, magnificans Deum.
      EN: And immediately rising up before them, he took up the bed on which he lay: and he went away to his own house, glorifying God.

5:26  LA: Et stupor apprehendit omnes, et magnificabant Deum. Et repleti sunt timore, dicentes: Quia vidimus mirabilia hodie.
      EN: And all were astonished: and they glorified God. And they were filled with fear, saying: We have seen wonderful things to-day.

5:27  LA: Et post hæc exiit, et vidit publicanum nomine Levi, sedentem ad telonium, et ait illi: Sequere me.
      EN: And after these things, he went forth and saw a tax collector named Levi, sitting at the receipt of custom: and he said to him: Follow me.

5:28  LA: Et relictis omnibus, surgens secutus est eum.
      EN: And leaving all things, he rose up and followed him.

5:29  LA: Et fecit ei convivium magnum Levi in domo sua: et erat turba multa publicanorum, et aliorum qui cum illis erant discumbentes.
      EN: And Levi made him a great feast in his own house: And there was a great company of tax collectors and of others that were at table with them.

5:30  LA: Et murmurabant pharisæi et scribæ eorum, dicentes ad discipulos ejus: Quare cum publicanis et peccatoribus manducatis et bibitis?
      EN: But the Pharisees and scribes murmured, saying to his disciples: Why do you eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?

5:31  LA: Et respondens Jesus, dixit ad illos: Non egent qui sani sunt medico, sed qui male habent.
      EN: And Jesus answered them: They that are whole need not the physician: but they that are sick.

5:32  LA: Non veni vocare justos, sed peccatores ad pœnitentiam.
      EN: I came not to call the just, but sinners to repentance.

5:33  LA: At illi dixerunt ad eum: Quare discipuli Joannis jejunant frequenter, et obsecrationes faciunt, similiter et pharisæorum: tui autem edunt et bibunt?
      EN: And they said to him: Why do the disciples of John fast often and make prayers, and the disciples of the Pharisees in like manner; but yours eat and drink?

5:34  LA: Quibus ipse ait: Numquid potestis filios sponsi, dum cum illis est sponsus, facere jejunare?
      EN: To whom he said: Can you make the children of the bridegroom fast while the bridegroom is with them?

5:35  LA: Venient autem dies, cum ablatus fuerit ab illis sponsus: tunc jejunabunt in illis diebus.
      EN: But the days will come when the bridegroom shall be taken away from them: then shall they fast in those days.

5:36  LA: Dicebat autem et similitudinem ad illos: Quia nemo commissuram a novo vestimento immittit in vestimentum vetus: alioquin et novum rumpit, et veteri non convenit commissura a novo.
      EN: And he spoke also a similitude to them: That no one puts a piece from a new garment upon an old garment: otherwise he both tears the new, and the piece taken from the new agrees not with the old.

5:37  LA: Et nemo mittit vinum novum in utres veteres: alioquin rumpet vinum novum utres, et ipsum effundetur, et utres peribunt:
      EN: And no one puts new wine into old bottles: otherwise the new wine will break the bottles; and it will be spilled and the bottles will be lost.

5:38  LA: sed vinum novum in utres novos mittendum est, et utraque conservantur.
      EN: But new wine must be put into new bottles: and both are preserved.

5:39  LA: Et nemo bibens vetus, statim vult novum: dicit enim: Vetus melius est.
      EN: And no one drinking old has immediately a mind to new: for he says: The old is better.

LUKE 6

6:1  LA: Factum est autem in sabbato secundo, primo, cum transiret per sata, vellebant discipuli ejus spicas, et manducabant confricantes manibus.
     EN: And it happened on the second first sabbath that, as he went through the grain fields, his disciples plucked the ears and did eat, rubbing them in their hands.

6:2  LA: Quidam autem pharisæorum, dicebant illis: Quid facitis quod non licet in sabbatis?
     EN: And some of the Pharisees said to them: Why do you that which is not lawful on the sabbath days?

6:3  LA: Et respondens Jesus ad eos, dixit: Nec hoc legistis quod fecit David, cum esurisset ipse, et qui cum illo erant?
     EN: And Jesus answering them, said: Have you not read so much as this, what David did, when himself was hungry and they that were with him:

6:4  LA: quomodo intravit in domum Dei, et panes propositionis sumpsit, et manducavit, et dedit his qui cum ipso erant: quos non licet manducare nisi tantum sacerdotibus?
     EN: How he went into the house of God and took and ate the bread of proposition and gave to them that were with him, which is not lawful to eat but only for the priests?

6:5  LA: Et dicebat illis: Quia dominus est Filius hominis etiam sabbati.
     EN: And he said to them: The Son of Man is Lord also of the sabbath.

6:6  LA: Factum est autem in alio sabbato, ut intraret in synagogam, et doceret. Et erat ibi homo, et manus ejus dextra erat arida.
     EN: And it happened also, on another sabbath, that he entered into the synagogue and taught. And there was a man whose right hand was withered.

6:7  LA: Observabant autem scribæ et pharisæi si in sabbato curaret, ut invenirent unde accusarent eum.
     EN: And the scribes and Pharisees watched if he would heal on the sabbath: that they might find an accusation against him.

6:8  LA: Ipse vero sciebat cogitationes eorum: et ait homini qui habebat manum aridam: Surge, et sta in medium. Et surgens stetit.
     EN: But he knew their thoughts and said to the man who had the withered hand: Arise and stand forth in the midst. And rising he stood forth.

6:9  LA: Ait autem ad illos Jesus: Interrogo vos si licet sabbatis benefacere, an male: animam salvam facere, an perdere?
     EN: Then Jesus said to them: I ask you, if it be lawful on the sabbath days to do good or to do evil? To save life or to destroy?

6:10  LA: Et circumspectis omnibus dixit homini: Extende manum tuam. Et extendit: et restituta est manus ejus.
      EN: And looking round about on them all, he said to the man: Stretch forth your hand. And he stretched it forth. And his hand was restored.

6:11  LA: Ipsi autem repleti sunt insipientia, et colloquebantur ad invicem, quidnam facerent Jesu.
      EN: And they were filled with madness: and they talked one with another, what they might do to Jesus.

6:12  LA: Factum est autem in illis diebus, exiit in montem orare, et erat pernoctans in oratione Dei.
      EN: And it happened in those days, that he went out into a mountain to pray: and he passed the whole night in the prayer of God.

6:13  LA: Et cum dies factus esset, vocavit discipulos suos: et elegit duodecim ex ipsis (quos et apostolos nominavit):
      EN: And when day had come, he called to him his disciples: and he chose twelve of them (whom also he named apostles):

6:14  LA: Simonem, quem cognominavit Petrum, et Andream fratrem ejus, Jacobum, et Joannem, Philippum, et Bartholomæum,
      EN: Simon, whom he surnamed Peter, and Andrew his brother, James and John, Philip and Bartholomew,

6:15  LA: Matthæum, et Thomam, Jacobum Alphæi, et Simonem, qui vocatur Zelotes,
      EN: Matthew and Thomas, James the son of Alphaeus, and Simon who is called Zelotes,

6:16  LA: et Judam Jacobi, et Judam Iscariotem, qui fuit proditor.
      EN: And Jude the brother of James, and Judas Iscariot, who was the traitor.

6:17  LA: Et descendens cum illis, stetit in loco campestri, et turba discipulorum ejus, et multitudo copiosa plebis ab omni Judæa, et Jerusalem, et maritima, et Tyri, et Sidonis,
      EN: And coming down with them, he stood in a plain place: and the company of his disciples and a very great multitude of people from all Judea and Jerusalem and the sea coast, both of Tyre and Sidon,

6:18  LA: qui venerant ut audirent eum, et sanarentur a languoribus suis. Et qui vexabantur a spiritibus immundis, curabantur.
      EN: Who had come to hear him and to be healed of their diseases. And they that were troubled with unclean spirits were cured.

6:19  LA: Et omnis turba quærebat eum tangere: quia virtus de illo exibat, et sanabat omnes.
      EN: And all the multitude sought to touch him: for virtue went out from him and healed all.

6:20  LA: Et ipse elevatis oculis in discipulis suis, dicebat: Beati pauperes, quia vestrum est regnum Dei.
      EN: And he, lifting up his eyes on his disciples, said: Blessed are you poor: for yours is the kingdom of God.

6:21  LA: Beati qui nunc esuritis, quia saturabimini. Beati qui nunc fletis, quia ridebitis.
      EN: Blessed are you that hunger now: for you shall be filled. Blessed are you that weep now: for you shall laugh.

6:22  LA: Beati eritis cum vos oderint homines, et cum separaverint vos, et exprobraverint, et ejicerint nomen vestrum tamquam malum propter Filium hominis.
      EN: Blessed shall you be when men shall hate you, and when they shall separate you and shall reproach you and cast out your name as evil, for the Son of Man’s sake.

6:23  LA: Gaudete in illa die, et exsultate: ecce enim merces vestra multa est in cælo: secundum hæc enim faciebant prophetis patres eorum.
      EN: Be glad in that day and rejoice: for behold, your reward is great in heaven, For according to these things did their fathers to the prophets.

6:24  LA: Verumtamen væ vobis divitibus, quia habetis consolationem vestram.
      EN: But woe to you that are rich: for you have your consolation.

6:25  LA: Væ vobis, qui saturati estis: quia esurietis. Væ vobis, qui ridetis nunc: quia lugebitis et flebitis.
      EN: Woe to you that are filled: for you shall hunger. Woe to you that now laugh: for you shall mourn and weep.

6:26  LA: Væ cum benedixerint vobis homines: secundum hæc enim faciebant pseudoprophetis patres eorum.
      EN: Woe to you when men shall bless you: for according to these things did their fathers to the false prophets.

6:27  LA: Sed vobis dico, qui auditis: diligite inimicos vestros, benefacite his qui oderunt vos.
      EN: But I say to you that hear: Love your enemies. Do good to them that hate you.

6:28  LA: Benedicite maledicentibus vobis, et orate pro calumniantibus vos.
      EN: Bless them that curse you and pray for them that calumniate you.

6:29  LA: Et qui te percutit in maxillam, præbe et alteram. Et ab eo qui aufert tibi vestimentum, etiam tunicam noli prohibere.
      EN: And to him that strikes you on the one cheek, offer also the other. And him that takes away from you your cloak, forbid not to take your tunic also.

6:30  LA: Omni autem petenti te, tribue: et qui aufert quæ tua sunt, ne repetas.
      EN: Give to everyone that asks you: and of him that takes away your goods, ask them not again.

6:31  LA: Et prout vultis ut faciant vobis homines, et vos facite illis similiter.
      EN: And as you would that men should do to you, do you also to them in like manner.

6:32  LA: Et si diligitis eos qui vos diligunt, quæ vobis est gratia? nam et peccatores diligentes se diligunt.
      EN: And if you love them that love you, what thanks are to you? For sinners also love those that love them.

6:33  LA: Et si benefeceritis his qui vobis benefaciunt, quæ vobis est gratia? siquidem et peccatores hoc faciunt.
      EN: And if you do good to them who do good to you, what thanks are to you? For sinners also do this.

6:34  LA: Et si mutuum dederitis his a quibus speratis recipere, quæ gratia est vobis? nam et peccatores peccatoribus fœnerantur, ut recipiant æqualia.
      EN: And if you lend to them of whom you hope to receive, what thanks are to you? For sinners also lend to sinners, for to receive as much.

6:35  LA: Verumtamen diligite inimicos vestros: benefacite, et mutuum date, nihil inde sperantes: et erit merces vestra multa, et eritis filii Altissimi, quia ipse benignus est super ingratos et malos.
      EN: But love you your enemies: do good, and lend, hoping for nothing by it: and your reward shall be great, and you shall be the sons of the Highest. For he is kind to the unthankful and to the evil.

6:36  LA: Estote ergo misericordes sicut et Pater vester misericors est.
      EN: Be therefore merciful, as your Father also is merciful.

6:37  LA: Nolite judicare, et non judicabimini: nolite condemnare, et non condemnabimini. Dimitte, et dimittemini.
      EN: Judge not: and you shall not be judged. Condemn not: and you shall not be condemned. Forgive: and you shall be forgiven.

6:38  LA: Date, et dabitur vobis: mensuram bonam, et confertam, et coagitatam, et supereffluentem dabunt in sinum vestrum. Eadem quippe mensura, qua mensi fueritis, remetietur vobis.
      EN: Give: and it shall be given to you: good measure and pressed down and shaken together and running over shall they give into your bosom. For with the same measure that you shall mete also, it shall be measured to you again.

6:39  LA: Dicebat autem illis et similitudinem: Numquid potest cæcus cæcum ducere? nonne ambo in foveam cadunt?
      EN: And he spoke also to them a similitude: Can the blind lead the blind? Do they not both fall into the ditch?

6:40  LA: Non est discipulus super magistrum: perfectus autem omnis erit, si sit sicut magister ejus.
      EN: The disciple is not above his master: but everyone shall be perfect, if he be as his master.

6:41  LA: Quid autem vides festucam in oculo fratris tui, trabem autem, quæ in oculo tuo est, non consideras?
      EN: And why see you the mote in your brother’s eye: but the beam that is in your own eye you consider not?

6:42  LA: aut quomodo potes dicere fratri tuo: Frater, sine ejiciam festucam de oculo tuo: ipse in oculo tuo trabem non videns? Hypocrita, ejice primum trabem de oculo tuo: et tunc perspicies ut educas festucam de oculo fratris tui.
      EN: Or how can you say to your brother: Brother, let me pull the mote out of your eye, when you yourself see not the beam in your own eye? Hypocrite, cast first the beam out of your own eye: and then shall you see clearly to take out the mote from your brother’s eye.

6:43  LA: Non est enim arbor bona, quæ facit fructus malos: neque arbor mala, faciens fructum bonum.
      EN: For there is no good tree that brings forth evil fruit: nor an evil tree that brings forth good fruit.

6:44  LA: Unaquæque enim arbor de fructu suo cognoscitur. Neque enim de spinis colligunt ficus: neque de rubo vindemiant uvam.
      EN: For every tree is known by its fruit. For men do not gather figs from thorns: nor from a bramble bush do they gather the grape.

6:45  LA: Bonus homo de bono thesauro cordis sui profert bonum: et malus homo de malo thesauro profert malum. Ex abundantia enim cordis os loquitur.
      EN: A good man out of the good treasure of his heart brings forth that which is good: and an evil man out of the evil treasure brings forth that which is evil. For out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaks.

6:46  LA: Quid autem vocatis me Domine, Domine: et non facitis quæ dico?
      EN: And why call you me, Lord, Lord; and do not the things which I say?

6:47  LA: Omnis qui venit ad me, et audit sermones meos, et facit eos, ostendam vobis cui similis sit:
      EN: Everyone that comes to me and hears my words and does them, I will show you to whom he is like.

6:48  LA: similis est homini ædificanti domum, qui fodit in altum, et posuit fundamentum super petram: inundatione autem facta, illisum est flumen domui illi, et non potuit eam movere: fundata enim erat super petram.
      EN: He is like to a man building a house, who digged deep and laid the foundation upon a rock. And when a flood came, the stream beat vehemently upon that house: and it could not shake it: for it was founded on a rock.

6:49  LA: Qui autem audit, et non facit, similis est homini ædificanti domum suam super terram sine fundamento: in quam illisus est fluvius, et continuo cecidit: et facta est ruina domus illius magna.
      EN: But he that hears and does not is like to a man building his house upon the earth without a foundation: against which the stream beat vehemently. And immediately it fell: and the ruin of that house was great.

LUKE 7

7:1  LA: Cum autem implesset omnia verba sua in aures plebis, intravit Capharnaum.
     EN: And when he had finished all his words in the hearing of the people, he entered into Capernaum.

7:2  LA: Centurionis autem cujusdam servus male habens, erat moriturus: qui illi erat pretiosus.
     EN: And the servant of a certain centurion who was dear to him, being sick, was ready to die.

7:3  LA: Et cum audisset de Jesu, misit ad eum seniores Judæorum, rogans eum ut veniret et salvaret servum ejus.
     EN: And when he had heard of Jesus, he sent to him the ancients of the Jews, desiring him to come and heal his servant.

7:4  LA: At illi cum venissent ad Jesum, rogabant eum sollicite, dicentes ei: Quia dignus est ut hoc illi præstes:
     EN: And when they came to Jesus, they begged him earnestly, saying to him: He is worthy that you should do this for him.

7:5  LA: diligit enim gentem nostram, et synagogam ipse ædificavit nobis.
     EN: For he loves our nation: and he has built us a synagogue.

7:6  LA: Jesus autem ibat cum illis. Et cum jam non longe esset a domo, misit ad eum centurio amicos, dicens: Domine, noli vexari: non enim sum dignus ut sub tectum meum intres:
     EN: And Jesus went with them. And when he was now not far from the house, the centurion sent his friends to him, saying: Lord, trouble not yourself; for I am not worthy that you should enter under my roof.

7:7  LA: propter quod et meipsum non sum dignum arbitratus ut venirem ad te: sed dic verbo, et sanabitur puer meus.
     EN: For which cause neither did I think myself worthy to come to you: but say the word, and my servant shall be healed.

7:8  LA: Nam et ego homo sum sub potestate constitutus, habens sub me milites: et dico huic, Vade, et vadit: et alii, Veni, et venit: et servo meo, Fac hoc, et facit.
     EN: For I also am a man subject to authority, having under me soldiers: and I say to one, Go, and he goes: and to another, Come, and he comes; and to my servant, Do this, and he does it.

7:9  LA: Quo audito Jesus miratus est: et conversus sequentibus se turbis, dixit: Amen dico vobis, nec in Israël tantam fidem inveni.
     EN: Which Jesus hearing, marvelled: and turning about to the multitude that followed him, he said: Amen, I say to you, I have not found so great faith, not even in Israel.

7:10  LA: Et reversi, qui missi fuerant, domum, invenerunt servum, qui languerat, sanum.
      EN: And they who were sent, being returned to the house, found the servant whole who had been sick.

7:11  LA: Et factum est: deinceps ibat in civitatem quæ vocatur Naim: et ibant cum eo discipuli ejus et turba copiosa.
      EN: And it happened afterwards that he went into a city that is called Nain: and there went with him his disciples and a great multitude.

7:12  LA: Cum autem appropinquaret portæ civitatis, ecce defunctus efferebatur filius unicus matris suæ: et hæc vidua erat: et turba civitatis multa cum illa.
      EN: And when he came near to the gate of the city, behold a dead man was carried out, the only son of his mother: and she was a widow. And a great multitude of the city was with her.

7:13  LA: Quam cum vidisset Dominus, misericordia motus super eam, dixit illi: Noli flere.
      EN: Whom when the Lord had seen, being moved with mercy towards her, he said to her: Weep not.

7:14  LA: Et accessit, et tetigit loculum. (Hi autem qui portabant, steterunt.) Et ait: Adolescens, tibi dico, surge.
      EN: And he came near and touched the bier. And they that carried it stood still. And he said: Young man, I say to you, arise.

7:15  LA: Et resedit qui erat mortuus, et cœpit loqui. Et dedit illum matri suæ.
      EN: And he that was dead sat up and began to speak. And he gave him to his mother.

7:16  LA: Accepit autem omnes timor: et magnificabant Deum, dicentes: Quia propheta magnus surrexit in nobis: et quia Deus visitavit plebem suam.
      EN: And there came a fear upon them all: and they glorified God saying: A great prophet has risen up among us: and, God has visited his people.

7:17  LA: Et exiit hic sermo in universam Judæam de eo, et in omnem circa regionem.
      EN: And this rumour of him went forth throughout all Judea and throughout all the country round about.

7:18  LA: Et nuntiaverunt Joanni discipuli ejus de omnibus his.
      EN: And John’s disciples told him of all these things.

7:19  LA: Et convocavit duos de discipulis suis Joannes, et misit ad Jesum, dicens: Tu es qui venturus es, an alium exspectamus?
      EN: And John called to him two of his disciples and sent them to Jesus, saying: Are you the one who is to come? Or do we await another?

7:20  LA: Cum autem venissent ad eum viri, dixerunt: Joannes Baptista misit nos ad te dicens: Tu es qui venturus es, an alium exspectamus?
      EN: And when the men had come to him, they said: John the Baptist has sent us to you, saying: Are you the one who is to come? Or do we await another?

7:21  LA: (In ipsa autem hora multos curavit a languoribus, et plagis, et spiritibus malis, et cæcis multis donavit visum.)
      EN: (And in that same hour, he cured many of their diseases and hurts and evil spirits: and to many that were blind he gave sight.)

7:22  LA: Et respondens, dixit illis: Euntes renuntiate Joanni quæ audistis et vidistis: quia cæci vident, claudi ambulant, leprosi mundantur, surdi audiunt, mortui resurgunt, pauperes evangelizantur:
      EN: And answering, he said to them: Go and relate to John what you have heard and seen: the blind see, the lame walk, the lepers are made clean, the deaf hear, the dead rise again, to the poor the gospel is preached.

7:23  LA: et beatus est quicumque non fuerit scandalizatus in me.
      EN: And blessed is anyone who does not stumble because of me.

      EN: And when the messengers of John were departed, he began to speak to the multitudes concerning John. What went you out into the desert to see? A reed shaken with the wind?

7:25  LA: Sed quid existis videre? hominem mollibus vestibus indutum? Ecce qui in veste pretiosa sunt et deliciis, in domibus regum sunt.
      EN: But what went you out to see? A man clothed in soft garments? Behold they that are in costly apparel and live delicately are in the houses of kings.

7:26  LA: Sed quid existis videre? prophetam? Utique dico vobis, et plus quam prophetam:

     EN: And other some fell among thorns. And the thorns growing up with it, choked it.

     EN: And his disciples asked him what this parable might be.

      EN: Now the parable is this: The seed is the word of God.

      EN: And they by the way side are they that hear: then the devil comes and takes the word out of their heart, lest believing they should be saved.

      EN: For there is not anything secret that shall not be made manifest, nor hidden that shall not be known and come abroad.

      EN: And it was told him: Your mother and your brothers stand without, desiring to see you.

      EN: Who answered them: My mother and my brothers are they who hear the word of God and do it.

      EN: And they did so and made them all sit down.

      EN: And taking the five loaves and the two fishes, he looked up to heaven and blessed them: and he broke and distributed to his disciples, to set before the multitude.

      EN: And they did all eat and were filled. And there were taken up of fragments that remained to them, twelve baskets.

       EN: And if Satan also be divided against himself, how shall his kingdom stand? Because you say that through Beelzebub I cast out demons.

11:19  LA: Si autem ego in Beelzebub ejicio dæmonia: filii vestri in quo ejiciunt? ideo ipsi judices vestri erunt.
       EN: Now if I cast out demons by Beelzebub, by whom do your children cast them out? Therefore, they shall be your judges.

       EN: But if I by the finger of God cast out demons, doubtless the kingdom of God has come upon you.

11:21  LA: Cum fortis armatus custodit atrium suum, in pace sunt ea quæ possidet.
       EN: When a strong man armed keeps his court, those things are in peace which he possesses.

       EN: But if a stronger than he come upon him and overcome him, he will take away all his armour wherein he trusted and will distribute his spoils.

11:23  LA: Qui non est mecum, contra me est: et qui non colligit mecum, dispergit.
       EN: He that is not with me is against me; and he that gathers not with me scatters.

       EN: When the unclean spirit has gone out of a man, he walks through places without water, seeking rest: and not finding, he says: I will return into my house from where I came out.

       EN: And when he has come, he finds it swept and garnished.

       EN: Then he goes and takes with him seven other spirits more wicked than himself: and entering in they dwell there. And the last state of that man becomes worse than the first.

       EN: And it happened, as he spoke these things, a certain woman from the crowd, lifting up her voice, said to him: Blessed is the womb that bore you and the paps that gave you suck.

11:28  LA: At ille dixit: Quinimmo beati, qui audiunt verbum Dei et custodiunt illud.
       EN: But he said: Yes rather, blessed are they who hear the word of God and keep it.

11:29  LA: Turbis autem concurrentibus cœpit dicere: Generatio hæc, generatio nequam est: signum quærit, et signum non dabitur ei, nisi signum Jonæ prophetæ.
       EN: And the multitudes running together, he began to say: This generation is a wicked generation. It asks a sign: and a sign shall not be given it, but the sign of Jonah the prophet.

11:30  LA: Nam sicut fuit Jonas signum Ninivitis, ita erit et Filius hominis generationi isti.
       EN: For as Jonah was a sign to the Ninevites; so shall the Son of Man also be to this generation.

       EN: The queen of the south shall rise in the judgment with the men of this generation and shall condemn them: because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon. And behold more than Solomon here.

11:32  LA: Viri Ninivitæ surgent in judicio cum generatione hac, et condemnabunt illam: quia pœnitentiam egerunt ad prædicationem Jonæ, et ecce plus quam Jonas hic.
       EN: The men of Nineveh shall rise in the judgment with this generation and shall condemn it; Because they repented at the preaching of Jonah. And behold more than Jonah here.

11:33  LA: Nemo lucernam accendit, et in abscondito ponit, neque sub modio: sed supra candelabrum, ut qui ingrediuntur, lumen videant.
       EN: No man lights a candle and puts it in a hidden place, nor under a bushel: but upon a candlestick, that they that come in may see the light.

       EN: The light of your body is your eye. If your eye be single, your whole body will be lightsome: but if it be evil, your body also will be darksome.

11:35  LA: Vide ergo ne lumen quod in te est, tenebræ sint.
       EN: Take care therefore that the light which is in you do not be darkness.

11:36  LA: Si ergo corpus tuum totum lucidum fuerit, non habens aliquam partem tenebrarum, erit lucidum totum, et sicut lucerna fulgoris illuminabit te.
       EN: If then your whole body be lightsome, having no part of darkness: the whole shall be lightsome and, as a bright lamp, shall enlighten you.

       EN: And as he was speaking, a certain Pharisee prayed him that he would dine with him. And he going in, sat down to eat.

       EN: And the Pharisee began to say, thinking within himself, why he was not washed before dinner.

       EN: And the Lord said to him: Now you, Pharisees, make clean the outside of the cup and of the platter: but your inside is full of rapine and iniquity.

11:41  LA: Verumtamen quod superest, date eleemosynam: et ecce omnia munda sunt vobis.

       EN: But woe to you, Pharisees, because you tithe mint and rue and every herb and pass over judgment and the charity of God. Now these things you ought to have done, and not to leave the other undone.

11:43  LA: Væ vobis, pharisæis, quia diligitis primas cathedras in synagogis, et salutationes in foro.
       EN: Woe to you, Pharisees, because you love the uppermost seats in the synagogues and salutations in the marketplace.

11:44  LA: Væ vobis, quia estis ut monumenta, quæ non apparent, et homines ambulantes supra, nesciunt.
       EN: Woe to you, because you are as tombs that appear not: and men that walk over are not aware.

11:45  LA: Respondens autem quidam ex legisperitis, ait illi: Magister, hæc dicens etiam contumeliam nobis facis.
       EN: And one of the lawyers answering, says to him: Master, in saying these things, you reproach us also.

11:46  LA: At ille ait: Et vobis legisperitis væ: quia oneratis homines oneribus, quæ portare non possunt, et ipsi uno digito vestro non tangitis sarcinas.
       EN: But he said: Woe to you lawyers also, because you load men with burdens which they cannot bear and you yourselves touch not the packs with one of your fingers.

11:47  LA: Væ vobis, qui ædificatis monumenta prophetarum: patres autem vestri occiderunt illos.
       EN: Woe to you who build the monuments of the prophets: and your fathers killed them.

11:48  LA: Profecto testificamini quod consentitis operibus patrum vestrorum: quoniam ipsi quidem eos occiderunt, vos autem ædificatis eorum sepulchra.
       EN: Truly you bear witness that you consent to the doings of your fathers. For they indeed killed them: and you build their tombs.

       EN: From the blood of Abel to the blood of Zacharias, who was slain between the altar and the temple. Yes I say to you: It shall be required of this generation.

11:52  LA: Væ vobis, legisperitis, quia tulistis clavem scientiæ: ipsi non introistis, et eos qui introibant, prohibuistis.
       EN: Woe to you lawyers, for you have taken away the key of knowledge. You yourselves have not entered in: and those that were entering in, you have hindered.

11:53  LA: Cum autem hæc ad illos diceret, cœperunt pharisæi et legisperiti graviter insistere, et os ejus opprimere de multis,
       EN: And as he was saying these things to them, the Pharisees and the lawyers began violently to urge him and to oppress his mouth about many things,

11:54  LA: insidiantes ei, et quærentes aliquid capere de ore ejus, ut accusarent eum.
       EN: Lying in wait for him and seeking to catch something from his mouth, that they might accuse him.

LUKE 12

12:1  LA: Multis autem turbis circumstantibus, ita ut se invicem conculcarent, cœpit dicere ad discipulos suos: Attendite a fermento pharisæorum, quod est hypocrisis.
      EN: And when great multitudes stood about him, so that they trod one upon another, he began to say to his disciples: Beware you of the leaven of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy.

12:2  LA: Nihil autem opertum est, quod non reveletur: neque absconditum, quod non sciatur.
      EN: For there is nothing covered that shall not be revealed: nor hidden that shall not be known.

12:3  LA: Quoniam quæ in tenebris dixistis, in lumine dicentur: et quod in aurem locuti estis in cubiculis, prædicabitur in tectis.
      EN: For whatever things you have spoken in darkness shall be published in the light: and that which you have spoken in the ear in the chambers shall be preached on the housetops.

12:4  LA: Dico autem vobis amicis meis: Ne terreamini ab his qui occidunt corpus, et post hæc non habent amplius quid faciant.
      EN: And I say to you, my friends: Do not be afraid of them who kill the body and after that have no more that they can do.

12:5  LA: Ostendam autem vobis quem timeatis: timete eum qui, postquam occiderit, habet potestatem mittere in gehennam: ita dico vobis, hunc timete.
      EN: But I will show you whom you shall fear: Fear you him who, after he has killed, has power to cast into Gehenna. Yes, I say to you: Fear him.

12:6  LA: Nonne quinque passeres veneunt dipondio, et unus ex illis non est in oblivione coram Deo?
      EN: Are not five sparrows sold for a dupondius? Yet not one of them is forgotten before God.

12:7  LA: sed et capilli capitis vestri omnes numerati sunt. Nolite ergo timere: multis passeribus pluris estis vos.
      EN: Yes, the very hairs of your head are all numbered. Do not fear therefore: you are of more value than many sparrows.

12:8  LA: Dico autem vobis: Omnis quicumque confessus fuerit me coram hominibus, et Filius hominis confitebitur illum coram angelis Dei:
      EN: And I say to you: Whoever shall confess me before men, him shall the Son of Man also confess before the angels of God.

12:9  LA: qui autem negaverit me coram hominibus, negabitur coram angelis Dei.
      EN: But he that shall deny me before men shall be denied before the angels of God.

12:10  LA: Et omnis qui dicit verbum in Filium hominis, remittetur illi: ei autem qui in Spiritum Sanctum blasphemaverit, non remittetur.
       EN: And whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man, it shall be forgiven him: but to him that shall blaspheme against the Holy Spirit, it shall not be forgiven.

12:11  LA: Cum autem inducent vos in synagogas, et ad magistratus, et potestates, nolite solliciti esse qualiter, aut quid respondeatis, aut quid dicatis.
       EN: And when they shall bring you into the synagogues and to magistrates and powers, do not be anxious how or what you shall answer, or what you shall say.

12:12  LA: Spiritus enim Sanctus docebit vos in ipsa hora quid oporteat vos dicere.
       EN: For the Holy Spirit shall teach you in the same hour what you must say.

12:13  LA: Ait autem ei quidam de turba: Magister, dic fratri meo ut dividat mecum hæreditatem.
       EN: And one of the multitude said to him: Master, speak to my brother that he divide the inheritance with me.

12:14  LA: At ille dixit illi: Homo, quis me constituit judicem, aut divisorem super vos?
       EN: But he said to him: Man, who has appointed me judge or divider over you?

12:15  LA: Dixitque ad illos: Videte, et cavete ab omni avaritia: quia non in abundantia cujusquam vita ejus est ex his quæ possidet.
       EN: And he said to them: Take care and beware of all covetousness: for a man’s life does not consist in the abundance of things which he possesses.

12:16  LA: Dixit autem similitudinem ad illos, dicens: Hominis cujusdam divitis uberes fructus ager attulit:
       EN: And he spoke a similitude to them, saying: The land of a certain rich man brought forth plenty of fruits.

12:17  LA: et cogitabat intra se dicens: Quid faciam, quia non habeo quo congregem fructus meos?
       EN: And he thought within himself, saying: What shall I do, because I have no room where to bestow my fruits?

12:18  LA: Et dixit: Hoc faciam: destruam horrea mea, et majora faciam: et illuc congregabo omnia quæ nata sunt mihi, et bona mea,
       EN: And he said: This will I do: I will pull down my barns and will build greater: and into them will I gather all things that are grown to me and my goods.

12:19  LA: et dicam animæ meæ: Anima, habes multa bona posita in annos plurimos: requiesce, comede, bibe, epulare.
       EN: And I will say to my soul: Soul, you have much goods laid up for many years. Take your rest: eat, drink, make good cheer.

12:20  LA: Dixit autem illi Deus: Stulte, hac nocte animam tuam repetunt a te: quæ autem parasti, cujus erunt?
       EN: But God said to him: You fool, this night do they require your soul of you. And whose shall those things be which you have provided?

12:21  LA: Sic est qui sibi thesaurizat, et non est in Deum dives.
       EN: So is he that lays up treasure for himself and is not rich towards God.

12:22  LA: Dixitque ad discipulos suos: Ideo dico vobis, nolite solliciti esse animæ vestræ quid manducetis, neque corpori quid induamini.
       EN: And he said to his disciples: Therefore I say to you: Do not be anxious for your life, what you shall eat, nor for your body, what you shall put on.

       EN: Life is more than food, and the body more than clothing.

12:25  LA: Quis autem vestrum cogitando potest adjicere ad staturam suam cubitum unum?
       EN: And which of you by taking thought can add to his stature one cubit?

       EN: If then you do not be able to do so much as the least thing, why are you anxious for the rest?

12:27  LA: Considerate lilia quomodo crescunt: non laborant, neque nent: dico autem vobis, nec Salomon in omni gloria sua vestiebatur sicut unum ex istis.
       EN: Consider the lilies, how they grow: they labor not, neither do they spin. But I say to you, not even Solomon in all his glory was clothed like one of these.

12:28  LA: Si autem fœnum, quod hodie est in agro, et cras in clibanum mittitur, Deus sic vestit: quanto magis vos pusillæ fidei?
       EN: Now, if God clothe in this manner the grass that is to-day in the field and to-next day is cast into the oven: how much more you, O you of little faith?

12:29  LA: Et vos nolite quærere quid manducetis, aut quid bibatis: et nolite in sublime tolli:
       EN: And seek not what you shall eat or what you shall drink: and do not be lifted up on high.

       EN: For all these things do the nations of the world seek. But your Father knows that you need these things.

12:31  LA: Verumtamen quærite primum regnum Dei, et justitiam ejus: et hæc omnia adjicientur vobis.
       EN: But seek you first the kingdom of God and his justice: and all these things shall be added to you.

12:32  LA: Nolite timere pusillus grex, quia complacuit Patri vestro dare vobis regnum.
       EN: Do not fear, little flock, for it has pleased your Father to give you a kingdom.

12:33  LA: Vendite quæ possidetis, et date eleemosynam. Facite vobis sacculos, qui non veterascunt, thesaurum non deficientem in cælis: quo fur non appropriat, neque tinea corrumpit.
       EN: Sell what you possess and give alms. Make to yourselves bags which grow not old, a treasure in heaven which fails not: where no thief approaches, nor moth corrupts.

12:34  LA: Ubi enim thesaurus vester est, ibi et cor vestrum erit.
       EN: For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also.

12:35  LA: Sint lumbi vestri præcincti, et lucernæ ardentes in manibus vestris,
       EN: Let your loins be girt and lamps burning in your hands.

12:36  LA: et vos similes hominibus exspectantibus dominum suum quando revertatur a nuptiis: ut, cum venerit et pulsaverit, confestim aperiant ei.
       EN: And you yourselves like to men who wait for their lord, when he shall return from the wedding; that when he comes and knocks, they may open to him immediately.

       EN: Blessed are those servants whom the Lord, when he comes, shall find watching. Amen, I say to you that he will gird himself and make them sit down to table and passing will minister to them.

       EN: I say to you, you shall not go out from there until you pay the very last mite.

LUKE 13

13:1  LA: Aderant autem quidam ipso in tempore, nuntiantes illi de Galilæis, quorum sanguinem Pilatus miscuit cum sacrificiis eorum.
      EN: And there were present, at that very time, some that told him of the Galileans, whose blood Pilate had mingled with their sacrifices.

      EN: And he answered them: Think you that these Galileans were sinners above all the men of Galilee, because they suffered such things?

13:3  LA: Non, dico vobis: sed nisi pœnitentiam habueritis, omnes similiter peribitis.
      EN: No, I say to you: but unless you shall repent, you shall all likewise perish.

      EN: Or those eighteen upon whom the tower fell in Siloam and slew them: think you that they also were debtors above all the men that dwelt in Jerusalem?

13:5  LA: Non, dico vobis: sed si pœnitentiam non egeritis, omnes similiter peribitis.
      EN: No, I say to you: but except you repent, you shall all likewise perish.

13:6  LA: Dicebat autem et hanc similitudinem: Arborem fici habebat quidam plantatam in vinea sua, et venit quærens fructum in illa, et non invenit.
      EN: He spoke also this parable: A certain man had a fig tree planted in his vineyard: and he came seeking fruit on it and found none.

      EN: And he said to the dresser of the vineyard: Behold, for these three years I come seeking fruit on this fig tree and I find none. Cut it down therefore. Why burdens it the ground?

13:8  LA: At ille respondens, dicit illi: Domine dimitte illam et hoc anno, usque dum fodiam circa illam, et mittam stercora,

      EN: And if happily it bear fruit: but if not, then after that you shall cut it down.

       EN: And he was teaching in their synagogue on their sabbath.

       EN: And behold there was a woman who had a spirit of infirmity eighteen years. And she was bowed together: neither could she look upwards at all.

       EN: Whom when Jesus saw, he called her to him and said to her: Woman, you are delivered from your infirmity.

       EN: And he laid his hands upon her: and immediately she was made straight and glorified God.

       EN: And when he said these things, all his adversaries were ashamed: and all the people rejoiced for all the things that were gloriously done by him.

       EN: He said therefore: To what is the kingdom of God like, and to what shall I resemble it?

13:20  LA: Et iterum dixit: Cui simile æstimabo regnum Dei?
       EN: And again he said: To what shall I esteem the kingdom of God to be like?

13:21  LA: Simile est fermento, quod acceptum mulier abscondit in farinæ sata tria, donec fermentaretur totum.

       EN: And he went through the cities and towns teaching and making his journey to Jerusalem.

13:23  LA: Ait autem illi quidam: Domine, si pauci sunt, qui salvantur? Ipse autem dixit ad illos:
       EN: And a certain man said to him: Lord, are they few that are saved? But he said to them:

       EN: Then you shall begin to say: We have eaten and drunk in your presence: and you have taught in our streets.

13:27  LA: Et dicet vobis: Nescio vos unde sitis: discedite a me omnes operarii iniquitatis.
       EN: And he shall say to you: I do not know you, from where you are. Depart from me, all you workers of iniquity.

13:28  LA: Ibi erit fletus et stridor dentium: cum videritis Abraham, et Isaac, et Jacob, et omnes prophetas in regno Dei, vos autem expelli foras.
       EN: There shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth; when you shall see Abraham and Isaac and Jacob and all the prophets, in the kingdom of God: and you yourselves thrust out.

       EN: And there shall come from the east and the west and the north and the south: and shall sit down in the kingdom of God.

13:30  LA: Et ecce sunt novissimi qui erunt primi, et sunt primi qui erunt novissimi.
       EN: And behold, they are last that shall be first: and they are first that shall be last.

       EN: And he said to them: Go and tell that fox: Behold, I cast out demons and do cures, to-day and to-next day, and the third day I am consummated.

13:33  LA: Verumtamen oportet me hodie et cras et sequenti die ambulare: quia non capit prophetam perire extra Jerusalem.
       EN: Nevertheless, I must walk to-day and to-next day and the day following, because it cannot be that a prophet perish, out of Jerusalem.

       EN: Jerusalem, Jerusalem, that kill the prophets; and stone them that are sent to you, how often would I have gathered your children as the bird does her brood under her wings, and you would not?

       EN: Behold your house shall be left to you desolate. And I say to you that you shall not see me until the time come when you shall say: Blessed is he that comes in the name of the Lord.

LUKE 14

14:1  LA: Et factum est cum intraret Jesus in domum cujusdam principis pharisæorum sabbato manducare panem, et ipsi observabant eum.
      EN: And it happened, when Jesus went into the house of one of the chief of the Pharisees, on the sabbath day, to eat bread, that they watched him.

14:2  LA: Et ecce homo quidam hydropicus erat ante illum.
      EN: And behold, there was a certain man before him that had the dropsy.

14:3  LA: Et respondens Jesus dixit ad legisperitos et pharisæos, dicens: Si licet sabbato curare?
      EN: And Jesus answering, spoke to the lawyers and Pharisees, saying: Is it lawful to heal on the sabbath day?

14:4  LA: At illi tacuerunt. Ipse vero apprehensum sanavit eum, ac dimisit.
      EN: But they held their peace. But he taking him, healed him and sent him away.

14:5  LA: Et respondens ad illos dixit: Cujus vestrum asinus, aut bos in puteum cadet, et non continuo extrahet illum die sabbati?
      EN: And answering them, he said: Which of you shall have an donkey or an ox fall into a pit and will not immediately draw him out, on the sabbath day?

14:6  LA: Et non poterant ad hæc respondere illi.
      EN: And they could not answer him to these things.

14:7  LA: Dicebat autem et ad invitatos parabolam, intendens quomodo primos accubitus eligerent, dicens ad illos:
      EN: And he spoke a parable also to them that were invited, marking how they chose the first seats at the table, saying to them:

14:8  LA: Cum invitatus fueris ad nuptias, non discumbas in primo loco, ne forte honoratior te sit invitatus ab illo.
      EN: When you are invited to a wedding, sit not down in the first place, lest one more honorable than you be invited by him:

14:9  LA: Et veniens is, qui te et illum vocavit, dicat tibi: Da huic locum: et tunc incipias cum rubore novissimum locum tenere.
      EN: And he that invited you and him, come and say to you: Give this man place. And then you begin with shame to take the lowest place.

14:10  LA: Sed cum vocatus fueris, vade, recumbe in novissimo loco: ut, cum venerit qui te invitavit, dicat tibi: Amice, ascende superius. Tunc erit tibi gloria coram simul discumbentibus:
       EN: But when you are invited, go, sit down in the lowest place; that when he who invited you comes, he may say to you: Friend, go up higher. Then shall you have glory before them that sit at table with you.

14:11  LA: quia omnis, qui se exaltat, humiliabitur: et qui se humiliat, exaltabitur.
       EN: Because everyone that exalts himself shall be humbled: and he that humbles himself shall be exalted.

14:12  LA: Dicebat autem et ei, qui invitaverat: Cum facis prandium, aut cœnam, noli vocare amicos tuos, neque fratres tuos, neque cognatos, neque vicinos divites: ne forte te et ipsi reinvitent, et fiat tibi retributio;
       EN: And he said to him also that had invited him: When you make a dinner or a supper, call not your friends nor your brothers nor your kinsmen nor your neighbours who are rich; lest they also invite you again, and a recompense be made to you.

14:13  LA: sed cum facis convivium, voca pauperes, debiles, claudos, et cæcos:
       EN: But when you make a feast, call the poor, the maimed, the lame and the blind.

14:14  LA: et beatus eris, quia non habent retribuere tibi: retribuetur enim tibi in resurrectione justorum.
       EN: And you shall be blessed, because they have not wherewith to make you recompense: for recompense shall be made you at the resurrection of the just.

14:15  LA: Hæc cum audisset quidam de simul discumbentibus, dixit illi: Beatus qui manducabit panem in regno Dei.
       EN: When one of them that sat at table with him had heard these things, he said to him: Blessed is he that shall eat bread in the kingdom of God.

14:16  LA: At ipse dixit ei: Homo quidam fecit cœnam magnam, et vocavit multos.
       EN: But he said to him: A certain man made a great supper and invited many.

14:17  LA: Et misit servum suum hora cœnæ dicere invitatis ut venirent, quia jam parata sunt omnia.
       EN: And he sent his servant at the hour of supper to say to them that were invited, that they should come: for now all things are ready.

14:18  LA: Et cœperunt simul omnes excusare. Primus dixit ei: Villam emi, et necesse habeo exire, et videre illam: rogo te, habe me excusatum.
       EN: And they began all at once to make excuse. The first said to him: I have bought a farm and I must needs go out and see it. I pray you, hold me excused.

14:19  LA: Et alter dixit: Juga boum emi quinque, et eo probare illa: rogo te, habe me excusatum.
       EN: And another said: I have bought five yoke of oxen and I go to try them. I pray you, hold me excused.

14:20  LA: Et alius dixit: Uxorem duxi, et ideo non possum venire.
       EN: And another said: I have married a wife; and therefore I cannot come.

14:21  LA: Et reversus servus nuntiavit hæc domino suo. Tunc iratus paterfamilias, dixit servo suo: Exi cito in plateas et vicos civitatis: et pauperes, ac debiles, et cæcos, et claudos introduc huc.
       EN: And the servant returning, told these things to his lord. Then the master of the house, being angry, said to his servant: Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city; and bring in here the poor and the feeble and the blind and the lame.

14:22  LA: Et ait servus: Domine, factum est ut imperasti, et adhuc locus est.
       EN: And the servant said: Lord, it is done as you have commanded; and yet there is room.

14:23  LA: Et ait dominus servo: Exi in vias, et sæpes: et compelle intrare, ut impleatur domus mea.
       EN: And the Lord said to the servant: Go out into the highways and hedges, and compel them to come in, that my house may be filled.

14:24  LA: Dico autem vobis quod nemo virorum illorum qui vocati sunt, gustabit cœnam meam.
       EN: But I say to you that none of those men that were invited shall taste of my supper.

14:25  LA: Ibant autem turbæ multæ cum eo: et conversus dixit ad illos:
       EN: And there went great multitudes with him. And turning, he said to them:

14:26  LA: Si quis venit ad me, et non odit patrem suum, et matrem, et uxorem, et filios, et fratres, et sorores, adhuc autem et animam suam, non potest meus esse discipulus.
       EN: If any man come to me, and hate not his father and mother and wife and children and brothers and sisters, yes and his own life also, he cannot be my disciple.

14:27  LA: Et qui non bajulat crucem suam, et venit post me, non potest meus esse discipulus.
       EN: And whoever does not carry his cross and come after me cannot be my disciple.

14:28  LA: Quis enim ex vobis volens turrim ædificare, non prius sedens computat sumptus, qui necessarii sunt, si habeat ad perficiendum,
       EN: For which of you, having a mind to build a tower, does not first sit down and reckon the charges that are necessary, whether he have wherewithal to finish it:

14:29  LA: ne, posteaquam posuerit fundamentum, et non potuerit perficere, omnes qui vident, incipiant illudere ei,
       EN: Lest, after he has laid the foundation and is not able to finish it, all that see it begin to mock him,

14:30  LA: dicentes: Quia hic homo cœpit ædificare, et non potuit consummare?
       EN: Saying: This man began to build and was not able to finish.

14:31  LA: Aut quis rex iturus committere bellum adversus alium regem, non sedens prius cogitat, si possit cum decem millibus occurrere ei, qui cum viginti millibus venit ad se?
       EN: Or, what king, about to go to make war against another king, does not first sit down and think whether he be able, with ten thousand, to meet him that, with twenty thousand, comes against him?

14:32  LA: Alioquin adhuc illo longe agente, legationem mittens rogat ea quæ pacis sunt.
       EN: Or else, while the other is yet afar off, sending an embassy, he desires conditions of peace.

14:33  LA: Sic ergo omnis ex vobis, qui non renuntiat omnibus quæ possidet, non potest meus esse discipulus.
       EN: So likewise everyone of you that does not renounce all that he possesses cannot be my disciple.

14:34  LA: Bonum est sal: si autem sal evanuerit, in quo condietur?
       EN: Salt is good. But if the salt shall lose its savour, wherewith shall it be seasoned?

14:35  LA: Neque in terram, neque in sterquilinium utile est, sed foras mittetur. Qui habet aures audiendi, audiat.
       EN: It is neither profitable for the land nor for the dunghill: but shall be cast out. He that has ears to hear, let him hear.

LUKE 15

      EN: Now the tax collectors and sinners drew near to him to hear him.

      EN: And the Pharisees and the scribes murmured, saying: This man receives sinners and eats with them.

15:3  LA: Et ait ad illos parabolam istam dicens:
      EN: And he spoke to them this parable, saying:

15:4  LA: Quis ex vobis homo, qui habet centum oves, et si perdiderit unam ex illis, nonne dimittit nonaginta novem in deserto, et vadit ad illam quæ perierat, donec inveniat eam?
      EN: What man of you that has a hundred sheep, and if he shall lose one of them, does he not leave the ninety-nine in the desert and go after that which was lost, until he find it?

      EN: And when he has found it, lay it upon his shoulders, rejoicing?

15:6  LA: et veniens domum convocat amicos et vicinos, dicens illis: Congratulamini mihi, quia inveni ovem meam, quæ perierat.
      EN: And coming home, call together his friends and neighbours, saying to them: Rejoice with me, because I have found my sheep that was lost?

      EN: I say to you that in this way there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents than over ninety-nine righteous people who have no need of repentance.

      EN: And when she has found it, call together her friends and neighbours, saying: Rejoice with me, because I have found the groat which I had lost.

15:10  LA: Ita, dico vobis, gaudium erit coram angelis Dei super uno peccatore pœnitentiam agente.

       EN: And he would gladly have filled his belly with the husks the swine did eat: and no one gave to him.

       EN: Coming to himself, he said: How many hired workers in my father’s house have bread in abundance, while I perish here with hunger!

15:19  LA: jam non sum dignus vocari filius tuus: fac me sicut unum de mercenariis tuis.

15:21  LA: Dixitque ei filius: Pater, peccavi in cælum, et coram te: jam non sum dignus vocari filius tuus.
       EN: The son said to him: Father, I have sinned against heaven and before you; I am no longer worthy to be called your son.

       EN: for this son of mine was dead and has come to life again; he was lost and has been found. And they began to feast.

       EN: And he called one of the servants, and asked what these things meant.

15:27  LA: Isque dixit illi: Frater tuus venit, et occidit pater tuus vitulum saginatum, quia salvum illum recepit.
       EN: And he said to him: Your brother has come and your father has killed the fatted calf, because he has received him safe.

15:28  LA: Indignatus est autem, et nolebat introire. Pater ergo illius egressus, cœpit rogare illum.
       EN: And he was angry and would not go in. His father therefore coming out began to entreat him.

15:29  LA: At ille respondens, dixit patri suo: Ecce tot annis servio tibi, et numquam mandatum tuum præterivi: et numquam dedisti mihi hædum ut cum amicis meis epularer.
       EN: And he answered to his father: Behold, for so many years do I serve you and I have never transgressed your commandment: and yet you have never given me a kid to make merry with my friends.

       EN: But as soon as this your son has come, who has devoured his substance with prostitutes, you have killed for him the fatted calf.

15:31  LA: At ipse dixit illi: Fili, tu semper mecum es, et omnia mea tua sunt:
       EN: But he said to him: Son, you are always with me, and all that is mine is yours.

15:32  LA: epulari autem, et gaudere oportebat, quia frater tuus hic mortuus erat, et revixit; perierat, et inventus est.
       EN: But it was fitting to feast and rejoice, for this brother of yours was dead and has come to life again; he was lost and has been found.

LUKE 16

16:1  LA: Dicebat autem et ad discipulos suos: Homo quidam erat dives, qui habebat villicum: et hic diffamatus est apud illum quasi dissipasset bona ipsius.
      EN: And he said also to his disciples: There was a certain rich man who had a steward: and the same was accused to him, that he had wasted his goods.

16:2  LA: Et vocavit illum, et ait illi: Quid hoc audio de te? redde rationem villicationis tuæ: jam enim non poteris villicare.
      EN: And he called him and said to him: How is it that I hear this of you? Give an account of your stewardship: for now you can be steward no longer.

16:3  LA: Ait autem villicus intra se: Quid faciam, quia dominus meus aufert a me villicationem? Fodere non valeo, mendicare erubesco.
      EN: And the steward said within himself: What shall I do, because my lord takes away from me the stewardship? To dig I am not able; to beg I am ashamed.

16:4  LA: Scio quid faciam, ut, cum amotus fuero a villicatione, recipiant me in domos suas.
      EN: I know what I will do, that when I shall be removed from the stewardship, they may receive me into their houses.

      EN: Therefore, calling together everyone of his lord’s debtors, he said to the first: How much do you owe my lord?

      EN: But he said: An hundred barrels of oil. And he said to him: Take your bill and sit down quickly and write fifty.

23:35  LA: Et stabat populus spectans, et deridebant eum principes cum eis, dicentes: Alios salvos fecit, se salvum faciat, si hic est Christus Dei electus.
       EN: And the people stood beholding. And the rulers with them derided him, saying: He saved others: let him save himself, if he be Christ, the elect of God.

23:36  LA: Illudebant autem ei et milites accedentes, et acetum offerentes ei,
       EN: And the soldiers also mocked him, coming to him and offering him vinegar,

23:37  LA: et dicentes: Si tu es rex Judæorum, salvum te fac.
       EN: And saying: If you be the king of the Jews, save yourself.

23:38  LA: Erat autem et superscriptio scripta super eum litteris græcis, et latinis, et hebraicis: Hic est rex Judæorum.
       EN: And there was also a superscription written over him in letters of Greek and Latin and Hebrew THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS.

23:39  LA: Unus autem de his, qui pendebant, latronibus, blasphemabat eum, dicens: Si tu es Christus, salvum fac temetipsum et nos.
       EN: And one of those robbers who were hung blasphemed him, saying: If you be Christ, save yourself and us.

23:40  LA: Respondens autem alter increpabat eum, dicens: Neque tu times Deum, quod in eadem damnatione es.
       EN: But the other answering, rebuked him, saying: Neither do you fear God, seeing; you are under the same condemnation?

23:41  LA: Et nos quidem juste, nam digna factis recipimus: hic vero nihil mali gessit.
       EN: And we indeed justly: for we receive the due reward of our deeds. But this man has done no evil.

23:42  LA: Et dicebat ad Jesum: Domine, memento mei cum veneris in regnum tuum.
       EN: And he said to Jesus: Lord, remember me when you shall come into your kingdom.

23:43  LA: Et dixit illi Jesus: Amen dico tibi: hodie mecum eris in paradiso.
       EN: Jesus said to him: Amen, I say to you: today you will be with me in paradise.

23:44  LA: Erat autem fere hora sexta, et tenebræ factæ sunt in universam terram usque ad horam nonam.
       EN: And it was almost the sixth hour: and there was darkness over all the earth until the ninth hour.

23:45  LA: Et obscuratus est sol, et velum templi scissum est medium.
       EN: And the sun was darkened, and the veil of the temple was rent in the midst.

23:46  LA: Et clamans voce magna Jesus ait: Pater, in manus tuas commendo spiritum meum. Et hæc dicens, expiravit.
       EN: Crying out with a loud voice, Jesus said: Father, into your hands I commend my spirit. And having said these things, he breathed his last.

23:47  LA: Videns autem centurio quod factum fuerat, glorificavit Deum, dicens: Vere hic homo justus erat.
       EN: Now, the centurion, seeing what was done, glorified God, saying: Indeed this was a just man.

23:48  LA: Et omnis turba eorum, qui simul aderant ad spectaculum istud, et videbant quæ fiebant, percutientes pectora sua revertebantur.
       EN: And all the multitude of them that had come together to that sight and saw the things that were done returned, striking their breasts.

23:49  LA: Stabant autem omnes noti ejus a longe, et mulieres, quæ secutæ eum erant a Galilæa, hæc videntes.
       EN: And all his acquaintance and the women that had followed him from Galilee stood afar off, beholding these things.

23:50  LA: Et ecce vir nomine Joseph, qui erat decurio, vir bonus et justus:
       EN: And behold there was a man named Joseph who was a counsellor, a good and a just man,

23:51  LA: hic non consenserat consilio, et actibus eorum: ab Arimathæa civitate Judææ, qui exspectabat et ipse regnum Dei:
       EN: (The same had not consented to their counsel and doings) of Arimathea, a city of Judea: who also himself looked for the kingdom of God.

23:52  LA: hic accessit ad Pilatum et petiit corpus Jesu:
       EN: This man went to Pilate and begged the body of Jesus.

23:53  LA: et depositum involvit sindone, et posuit eum in monumento exciso, in quo nondum quisquam positus fuerat.
       EN: And taking him down, he wrapped him in fine linen and laid him in a tomb that was hewed in stone, wherein never yet any man had been laid.

23:54  LA: Et dies erat parasceves, et sabbatum illucescebat.
       EN: And it was the day of the Parasceve: and the sabbath drew on.

23:55  LA: Subsecutæ autem mulieres, quæ cum eo venerant de Galilæa, viderunt monumentum, et quemadmodum positum erat corpus ejus.
       EN: And the women that had come with him from Galilee, following after, saw the tomb and how his body was laid.

23:56  LA: Et revertentes paraverunt aromata, et unguenta: et sabbato quidem siluerunt secundum mandatum.
       EN: And returning, they prepared spices and ointments: and on the sabbath day they rested, according to the commandment.

LUKE 24

24:1  LA: Una autem sabbati valde diluculo venerunt ad monumentum, portantes quæ paraverant aromata:
      EN: And on the first day of the week, very early in the morning, they came to the tomb, bringing the spices which they had prepared.

24:2  LA: et invenerunt lapidem revolutum a monumento.
      EN: And they found the stone rolled back from the tomb.

24:3  LA: Et ingressæ non invenerunt corpus Domini Jesu.
      EN: And going in, they found not the body of the Lord Jesus.

24:4  LA: Et factum est, dum mente consternatæ essent de isto, ecce duo viri steterunt secus illas in veste fulgenti.
      EN: And it happened, as they were astonished in their mind at this, behold, two men stood by them, in shining apparel.

24:5  LA: Cum timerent autem, et declinarent vultum in terram, dixerunt ad illas: Quid quæritis viventem cum mortuis?
      EN: And as they were afraid and bowed down their countenance towards the ground, they said to them: Why seek you the living with the dead?

24:6  LA: non est hic, sed surrexit: recordamini qualiter locutus est vobis, cum adhuc in Galilæa esset,
      EN: He is not here, but has risen. Remember how he spoke to you, when he was yet in Galilee,

24:7  LA: dicens: Quia oportet Filium hominis tradi in manus hominum peccatorum, et crucifigi, et die tertia resurgere.
      EN: Saying: The Son of Man must be delivered into the hands of sinful men and be crucified and the third day rise again.

24:8  LA: Et recordatæ sunt verborum ejus.
      EN: And they remembered his words.

24:9  LA: Et regressæ a monumento nuntiaverunt hæc omnia illis undecim, et ceteris omnibus.
      EN: And going back from the tomb, they told all these things to the eleven and to all the rest.

24:10  LA: Erat autem Maria Magdalene, et Joanna, et Maria Jacobi, et ceteræ quæ cum eis erant, quæ dicebant ad apostolos hæc.
       EN: And it was Mary Magdalen and Joanna and Mary of James and the other women that were with them, who told these things to the apostles.

24:11  LA: Et visa sunt ante illos sicut deliramentum verba ista, et non crediderunt illis.
       EN: And these words seemed to them as idle tales: and they did not believe them.

24:12  LA: Petrus autem surgens cucurrit ad monumentum: et procumbens vidit linteamina sola posita, et abiit secum mirans quod factum fuerat.
       EN: But Peter rising up, ran to the tomb and, stooping down, he saw the linen cloths laid by themselves: and went away wondering in himself at that which had happen.

24:13  LA: Et ecce duo ex illis ibant ipsa die in castellum, quod erat in spatio stadiorum sexaginta ab Jerusalem, nomine Emmaus.
       EN: And behold, that same day two of them were going to a village named Emmaus, sixty stadia from Jerusalem.

24:14  LA: Et ipsi loquebantur ad invicem de his omnibus quæ acciderant.
       EN: And they talked together of all these things which had happened.

24:15  LA: Et factum est, dum fabularentur, et secum quærerent: et ipse Jesus appropinquans ibat cum illis:
       EN: And it happened that while they talked and reasoned with themselves, Jesus himself also, drawing near, went with them.

24:16  LA: oculi autem illorum tenebantur ne eum agnoscerent.
       EN: But their eyes were held, that they should not know him.

24:17  LA: Et ait ad illos: Qui sunt hi sermones, quos confertis ad invicem ambulantes, et estis tristes?
       EN: And he said to them: What are these discourses that you hold one with another as you walk and are sad?

24:18  LA: Et respondens unus, cui nomen Cleophas, dixit ei: Tu solus peregrinus es in Jerusalem, et non cognovisti quæ facta sunt in illa his diebus?
       EN: And the one of them, whose name was Clopas, answered him: Are you only a stranger in Jerusalem, and have not known the things that have been done there in these days?

24:19  LA: Quibus ille dixit: Quæ? Et dixerunt: De Jesu Nazareno, qui fuit vir propheta, potens in opere et sermone coram Deo et omni populo:
       EN: To whom he said: What things? And they said: Concerning Jesus of Nazareth, who was a prophet, mighty in work and word before God and all the people.

24:20  LA: et quomodo eum tradiderunt summi sacerdotes et principes nostri in damnationem mortis, et crucifixerunt eum:
       EN: And how our chief priests and princes delivered him to be condemned to death and crucified him.

24:21  LA: nos autem sperabamus quia ipse esset redempturus Israël: et nunc super hæc omnia, tertia dies est hodie quod hæc facta sunt.
       EN: But we hoped that it was he that should have redeemed Israel. And now besides all this, to-day is the third day since these things were done.

24:22  LA: Sed et mulieres quædam ex nostris terruerunt nos, quæ ante lucem fuerunt ad monumentum,
       EN: Yes and certain women also of our company affrighted us who, before it was light, were at the tomb,

24:23  LA: et non invento corpore ejus, venerunt, dicentes se etiam visionem angelorum vidisse, qui dicunt eum vivere.
       EN: And not finding his body, came, saying that they had also seen a vision of angels, who say that he is alive.

24:24  LA: Et abierunt quidam ex nostris ad monumentum: et ita invenerunt sicut mulieres dixerunt, ipsum vero non invenerunt.
       EN: And some of our people went to the tomb and found it so as the women had said: but him they found not.

24:25  LA: Et ipse dixit ad eos: O stulti, et tardi corde ad credendum in omnibus quæ locuti sunt prophetæ!
       EN: Then he said to them: O foolish and slow of heart to believe in all things, Which the prophets have spoken.

24:26  LA: Nonne hæc oportuit pati Christum, et ita intrare in gloriam suam?
       EN: Was it not necessary for the Christ to suffer these things and so enter his glory?

24:27  LA: Et incipiens a Moyse, et omnibus prophetis, interpretabatur illis in omnibus scripturis quæ de ipso erant.
       EN: Beginning with Moses and all the prophets, he interpreted for them the things concerning himself in all the Scriptures.

24:28  LA: Et appropinquaverunt castello quo ibant: et ipse se finxit longius ire.
       EN: And they drew near to the town where they were going: and he made as though he would go farther.

24:29  LA: Et coëgerunt illum, dicentes: Mane nobiscum, quoniam advesperascit, et inclinata est jam dies. Et intravit cum illis.
       EN: But they constrained him, saying: Stay with us, because it is towards evening and the day is now far spent. And he went in with them.

24:30  LA: Et factum est, dum recumberet cum eis, accepit panem, et benedixit, ac fregit, et porrigebat illis.
       EN: As he reclined at table with them, he took bread, blessed and broke it, and offered it to them.

       EN: Their eyes were opened, and they recognized him; and he vanished from their sight.

24:32  LA: Et dixerunt ad invicem: Nonne cor nostrum ardens erat in nobis dum loqueretur in via, et aperiret nobis Scripturas?
       EN: They said to one another: Was not our heart burning within us while he spoke on the way and opened the Scriptures to us?

24:33  LA: Et surgentes eadem hora regressi sunt in Jerusalem: et invenerunt congregatos undecim, et eos qui cum illis erant,

24:34  LA: dicentes: Quod surrexit Dominus vere, et apparuit Simoni.
       EN: Saying: The Lord has risen indeed and has appeared to Simon.

24:35  LA: Et ipsi narrabant quæ gesta erant in via, et quomodo cognoverunt eum in fractione panis.
       EN: And they told what things were done in the way: and how they knew him in the breaking of bread.

24:36  LA: Dum autem hæc loquuntur, stetit Jesus in medio eorum, et dicit eis: Pax vobis: ego sum, nolite timere.
       EN: Now, while they were speaking these things, Jesus stood in the middle of them and says to them: Peace be to you. It is I: Do not fear.

24:37  LA: Conturbati vero et conterriti, existimabant se spiritum videre.
       EN: But they being troubled and frightened, supposed that they saw a spirit.

24:38  LA: Et dixit eis: Quid turbati estis, et cogitationes ascendunt in corda vestra?
       EN: And he said to them: Why are you troubled, and why do thoughts arise in your hearts?

       EN: Look at my hands and feet, for it is I myself. Touch and see, for a spirit does not have flesh and bones, as you see that I have.

24:40  LA: Et cum hoc dixisset, ostendit eis manus et pedes.
       EN: And when he had said this, he showed them his hands and feet.

24:41  LA: Adhuc autem illis non credentibus, et mirantibus præ gaudio, dixit: Habetis hic aliquid quod manducetur?
       EN: But while they yet believed not and wondered for joy, he said: Have you here anything to eat?

24:42  LA: At illi obtulerunt ei partem piscis assi et favum mellis.
       EN: And they offered him a piece of a broiled fish and a honeycomb.

24:43  LA: Et cum manducasset coram eis, sumens reliquias dedit eis.
       EN: And when he had eaten before them, taking the remains, he gave to them.

24:44  LA: Et dixit ad eos: Hæc sunt verba quæ locutus sum ad vos cum adhuc essem vobiscum, quoniam necesse est impleri omnia quæ scripta sunt in lege Moysi, et prophetis, et Psalmis de me.

       EN: Then he opened their understanding so that they might understand the Scriptures,

       EN: and said to them: Thus it is written, and thus it was necessary for the Christ to suffer and to rise from the dead on the third day,

       EN: He led them out to Bethany and, lifting up his hands, blessed them.

       EN: As he blessed them, he withdrew from them and was carried into heaven.

24:52  LA: Et ipsi adorantes regressi sunt in Jerusalem cum gaudio magno:
       EN: They worshiped and returned to Jerusalem with great joy,

24:53  LA: et erant semper in templo, laudantes et benedicentes Deum. Amen.
       EN: and were always in the temple, praising and blessing God. Amen.
