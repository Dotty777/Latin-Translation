## Text

**1:1** In the beginning God created heaven and the earth.

**1:2** Now the earth was empty and vacant, and darkness was over the face of the abyss, and the Spirit of God was borne over the waters.

**1:3** And God said, "Let there be light." And light came to be.

**1:4** And God saw the light, that it was good; and He divided the light from the darkness.

**1:5** And He called the light Day, and the darkness Night. And evening and morning came to be, one day.

**1:6** God also said, "Let there be an expanse in the midst of the waters, and let it divide waters from waters."

**1:7** And God made the expanse, and divided the waters that were under the expanse from those that were above the expanse. And it came to be so.

**1:8** And God called the expanse Heaven. And evening and morning came to be, the second day.

**1:9** Then God said, "Let the waters that are under heaven be gathered into one place, and let the dry land appear." And it came to be so.

**1:10** And God called the dry land Earth, and the gatherings of the waters He called Seas. And God saw that it was good.

**1:11** And He said, "Let the earth sprout green vegetation producing seed, and the fruit-bearing tree producing fruit according to its kind, whose seed is in itself upon the earth." And it came to be so.

**1:12** And the earth brought forth green vegetation bearing seed according to its kind, and the tree producing fruit, each having seed according to its species. And God saw that it was good.

**1:13** And evening and morning came to be, the third day.

**1:14** Then God said, "Let there be light-bearers in the expanse of heaven to divide the day from the night, and let them serve for signs and seasons, and for days and years,

**1:15** so that they may shine in the expanse of heaven and give light to the earth." And it came to be so.

**1:16** And God made the two great light-bearers: the greater light-bearer to govern the day, and the lesser light-bearer to govern the night, and the stars.

**1:17** And He set them in the expanse of heaven to shine over the earth,

**1:18** and to govern the day and the night, and to divide the light from the darkness. And God saw that it was good.

**1:19** And evening and morning came to be, the fourth day.

**1:20** God also said, "Let the waters bring forth creeping creatures with living soul, and flying creatures above the earth beneath the expanse of heaven."

**1:21** And God created the great sea creatures, and every living and moving soul that the waters had brought forth according to their species, and every flying creature according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.

**1:22** And He blessed them, saying, "Increase and multiply, and fill the waters of the sea, and let the birds multiply upon the earth."

**1:23** And evening and morning came to be, the fifth day.

**1:24** God also said, "Let the earth bring forth the living soul according to its kind: livestock, and creeping creatures, and wild beasts of the earth according to their species." And it came to be so.

**1:25** And God made the wild beasts of the earth according to their species, and the livestock, and every creeping creature of the earth according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.

**1:26** And He said, "Let Us make man according to Our image and likeness, and let him rule over the fish of the sea, and the flying creatures of heaven, and the wild beasts, and the whole earth, and every creeping creature that moves upon the earth."

**1:27** And God created man according to His own image; according to the image of God He created him; male and female He created them.

**1:28** And God blessed them and said, "Increase and multiply, and fill the earth and subdue it, and have dominion over the fish of the sea, and the flying creatures of heaven, and all living creatures that move upon the earth."

**1:29** And God said, "Look, I have given you every seed-bearing plant upon the earth, and all trees that have in themselves seed of their own kind, so that they may be food for you,

**1:30** and for all the animals of the earth, and for every bird of heaven, and for all things that move upon the earth and in which there is a living soul, so that they may have something to eat." And it came to be so.

**1:31** And God saw all the things that He had made, and they were very good. And evening and morning came to be, the sixth day.

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Latin cælum is singular ("heaven"), unlike Hebrew plural hashshamayim ("the heavens").
- **1:2** — Latin inanis et vacua literally means "empty and vacant"; ferebatur ("was borne" or "moved") renders the Spirit's motion over the waters.
- **1:6** — Latin firmamentum denotes a solid vault or expanse; rendered "expanse" to maintain modern vocabulary.
- **1:21** — The Saints Latin text reads mutabilem (a Clementine textual spelling/variant of motabilem, "moving"); rendered "moving" in keeping with the Latin swimming-creatures clause.
- **1:29** — Latin ecce ("look") introduces the grant of plant food; rendered "Look" rather than archaic "behold."
