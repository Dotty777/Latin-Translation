## Text

**1:1** In the beginning the Word existed, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.

**1:2** This One was in the beginning with God.

**1:3** All things came into being through Him, and without Him nothing came into being that has been made.

**1:4** In Him there was life, and the life was the light of mankind;

**1:5** and the light shines in the darkness, and the darkness did not grasp it.

**1:6** There was a man who was sent from God, whose name was John.

**1:7** This man came for testimony, so that he might bear witness about the light, that all might believe through him.

**1:8** That man was not the light, but came so that he might bear witness about the light.

**1:9** That was the true light, which enlightens every person coming into this world.

**1:10** He was in the world, and the world was made through Him, yet the world did not know Him.

**1:11** He came to His own possessions, and His own people did not receive Him.

**1:12** Yet to as many as received Him, He gave authority to become sons of God, to those who believe in His name,

**1:13** who were born not from bloodlines, nor from the will of the flesh, nor from the will of a husband, but from God.

**1:14** And the Word became flesh and made His dwelling among us; and we saw His glory, glory as of the only-begotten from the Father, full of grace and truth.

**1:15** John bears witness about Him and cries out, saying, "This was the One of whom I said, 'The One who is going to come after me has been placed ahead of me, because He existed before me.'"

**1:16** And from His fullness all of us have received, even grace in place of grace;

**1:17** because the Law was given through Moses, while grace and truth came into being through Jesus Christ.

**1:18** No one has ever seen God; the only-begotten Son, who is in the bosom of the Father, He Himself has made Him known.

**1:19** And this is the testimony of John, when the Jews sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem to him to ask him, "Who are you?"

**1:20** And he confessed and did not deny, and he confessed, "I am not the Christ."

**1:21** And they asked him, "What then? Are you Elijah?" And he said, "I am not." "Are you the Prophet?" And he answered, "No."

**1:22** Therefore they said to him, "Who are you, so that we may give an answer to those who sent us? What do you say about yourself?"

**1:23** He said, "I am the voice of one crying out in the wilderness, 'Make straight the way of the Lord,' just as Isaiah the prophet said."

**1:24** And those who had been sent were from the Pharisees.

**1:25** And they asked him and said to him, "Why then do you baptize, if you are neither the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the Prophet?"

**1:26** John answered them, saying, "I baptize in water; yet there has stood in your midst One whom you do not know.

**1:27** He Himself is the One who is going to come after me, who has been placed ahead of me, the strap of whose sandal I am not worthy to untie."

**1:28** These things happened in Bethany across the Jordan, where John was baptizing.

**1:29** On the next day John saw Jesus coming toward him, and he said, "Look, the Lamb of God; look, the One who takes away the sin of the world!

**1:30** This is the One about whom I said, 'After me comes a man who has been placed ahead of me, because He existed before me.'

**1:31** And I did not know Him, yet so that He might be revealed in Israel, for this reason I came baptizing in water."

**1:32** And John bore witness, saying, "I saw the Spirit descending like a dove from heaven, and He remained upon Him.

**1:33** And I did not know Him; yet the One who sent me to baptize in water, that One said to me, 'The One upon whom you see the Spirit descending and remaining upon Him, this is the One who baptizes in the Holy Spirit.'

**1:34** And I have seen and borne witness that this is the Son of God."

**1:35** On the next day John was standing again with two of his disciples.

**1:36** And looking at Jesus as He walked, he says, "Look, the Lamb of God!"

**1:37** And the two disciples heard him speaking, and they followed Jesus.

**1:38** Then Jesus turned and, seeing them following Him, says to them, "What are you seeking?" They said to Him, "Rabbi (which translated means Teacher), where are You staying?"

**1:39** He says to them, "Come and see." They came and saw where He was staying, and they remained with Him that day; now the hour was about the tenth.

**1:40** Now Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, was one of the two who had heard from John and had followed Him.

**1:41** He first finds his own brother Simon and says to him, "We have found the Messiah (which is translated Christ)."

**1:42** And he brought him to Jesus. Looking intently at him, Jesus said, "You are Simon, son of Jonah; you will be called Cephas," which is translated Peter.

**1:43** On the following day He wanted to depart for Galilee, and He finds Philip. And Jesus says to him, "Follow Me."

**1:44** Now Philip was from Bethsaida, the city of Andrew and Peter.

**1:45** Philip finds Nathanael and says to him, "We have found the One about whom Moses wrote in the Law, and the prophets also: Jesus, son of Joseph, from Nazareth."

**1:46** And Nathanael said to him, "From Nazareth can anything good be?" Philip says to him, "Come and see."

**1:47** Jesus saw Nathanael coming toward Him and says about him, "Look, truly an Israelite in whom there is no deceit!"

**1:48** Nathanael says to Him, "From where do You know me?" Jesus answered and said to him, "Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you."

**1:49** Nathanael answered Him and said, "Rabbi, You are the Son of God; You are the King of Israel!"

**1:50** Jesus answered and said to him, "Because I said to you, 'I saw you under the fig tree,' you believe; you will see greater things than these."

**1:51** And He says to him, "Truly, truly, I say to you, you will see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending above the Son of Man."

## Translator Notes

- **1:1** — Latin Verbum renders Greek Logos ("Word," denoting speech, expression, or divine reason); erat ("was/existed") is existential in the opening clause.
- **1:2** — Latin Hoc is neuter, agreeing grammatically with neuter Verbum ("the Word"), referring to the Word Himself.
- **1:3** — Latin punctuation attaches quod factum est ("that has been made") to verse 3 rather than verse 4.
- **1:5** — Latin comprehenderunt carries the dual sense of "grasped/understood" and "overtook/overcame."
- **1:9** — Latin reads in hunc mundum ("into this world") rather than simply "into the world."
- **1:12** — Latin filios literally reads "sons" rather than "children."
- **1:13** — Latin ex sanguinibus is literally plural ("from bloods"), rendered "from bloodlines."
- **1:18** — The Vulgate reads unigenitus Filius ("the only-begotten Son") rather than the Alexandrian Greek reading "only-begotten God."
- **1:27** — The Vulgate includes qui ante me factus est ("who has been placed ahead of me"), absent from the earliest Alexandrian Greek manuscripts.
- **1:28** — Latin in Bethania ("in Bethany") is followed here rather than the textual variant "Bethabara."
- **1:29** — The Vulgate doubles the exclamation: Ecce agnus Dei, ecce qui tollit ("Look, the Lamb of God; look, the One who takes away").
- **1:42** — The Vulgate reads filius Jona ("son of Jonah") rather than the Alexandrian Greek "son of John."
- **1:51** — Latin Amen, amen dico vobis is rendered "Truly, truly, I say to you" in modern vocabulary, and plural vobis ("to you") addresses those present.
