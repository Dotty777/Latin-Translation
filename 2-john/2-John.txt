2 JOHN 1

1:1  LA: Senior Electæ dominæ, et natis ejus, quos ego diligo in veritate, et non ego solus, sed et omnes qui cognoverunt veritatem,
     EN: The elder to the chosen lady and her children, whom I love in truth—and not I alone, but also all who have known the truth—

1:2  LA: propter veritatem, quæ permanet in nobis, et nobiscum erit in æternum.
     EN: because of the truth that remains in us and will be with us forever.

1:3  LA: Sit vobiscum gratia, misericordia, pax a Deo Patre, et a Christo Jesu Filio Patris in veritate, et caritate.
     EN: May grace, mercy, and peace be with you from God the Father and from Christ Jesus, the Father’s Son, in truth and love.

1:4  LA: Gavisus sum valde, quoniam inveni de filiis tuis ambulantes in veritate, sicut mandatum accepimus a Patre.
     EN: I rejoiced greatly that I found some of your children walking in truth, just as we received a commandment from the Father.

1:5  LA: Et nunc rogo te domina, non tamquam mandatum novum scribens tibi, sed quod habuimus ab initio, ut diligamus alterutrum.
     EN: And now, lady, I ask you—not as though writing a new commandment to you, but the one we have had from the beginning—that we love one another.

1:6  LA: Et hæc est caritas, ut ambulemus secundum mandata ejus. Hoc est enim mandatum, ut quemadmodum audistis ab initio, in eo ambuletis.
     EN: And this is love: that we walk according to his commandments. For this is the commandment, that you walk in it, just as you heard from the beginning.

1:7  LA: Quoniam multi seductores exierunt in mundum, qui non confitentur Jesum Christum venisse in carnem: hic est seductor, et antichristus.
     EN: For many deceivers have gone out into the world who do not confess that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh. Such a person is the deceiver and the antichrist.

1:8  LA: Videte vosmetipsos, ne perdatis quæ operati estis: sed ut mercedem plenam accipiatis.
     EN: Watch yourselves, so that you do not lose what you have worked for, but receive a full reward.

1:9  LA: Omnis qui recedit, et non permanet in doctrina Christi, Deum non habet: qui permanet in doctrina, hic et Patrem et Filium habet.
     EN: Everyone who departs and does not remain in the teaching of Christ does not have God. Whoever remains in the teaching has both the Father and the Son.

1:10  LA: Si quis venit ad vos, et hanc doctrinam non affert, nolite recipere eum in domum, nec Ave ei dixeritis.
      EN: If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not receive him into your house or give him a greeting.

1:11  LA: Qui enim dicit illi Ave, communicat operibus ejus malignis.
      EN: For whoever greets him shares in his evil works.

1:12  LA: Plura habens vobis scribere, nolui per cartam et atramentum: spero enim me futurum apud vos, et os ad os loqui: ut gaudium vestrum plenum sit.
      EN: Although I have much to write to you, I did not wish to do so with paper and ink. For I hope to be with you and speak face to face, so that your joy may be complete.

1:13  LA: Salutant te filii sororis tuæ Electæ.
      EN: The children of your chosen sister greet you.
