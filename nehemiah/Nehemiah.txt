NEHEMIAH 1

1:1  LA: Verba Nehemiæ filii Helchiæ. Et factum est in mense Casleu, anno vigesimo, et ego eram in Susis castro.
     EN: The words of Nehemiah son of Hilkiah. In the month of Chislev, in the twentieth year, I was in the citadel of Susa.

1:2  LA: Et venit Hanani, unus de fratribus meis, ipse et viri ex Juda : et interrogavi eos de Judæis qui remanserant, et supererant de captivitate, et Jerusalem.
     EN: Hanani, one of my brothers, came with some men from Judah. I asked them about the Jews who remained, the survivors of the captivity, and about Jerusalem.

1:3  LA: Et dixerunt mihi : Qui remanserunt, et relicti sunt de captivitate ibi in provincia, in afflictione magna sunt, et in opprobrio : et murus Jerusalem dissipatus est, et portæ ejus combustæ sunt igni.
     EN: They said to me, “Those who remain there in the province, left over from the captivity, are in great distress and disgrace. Jerusalem's wall is broken down, and its gates have been burned with fire.”

1:4  LA: Cumque audissem verba hujuscemodi, sedi, et flevi, et luxi diebus multis : jejunabam, et orabam ante faciem Dei cæli :
     EN: When I heard these words, I sat down, wept, and mourned for many days. I fasted and prayed before the God of heaven,

1:5  LA: et dixi : Quæso, Domine Deus cæli fortis, magne atque terribilis, qui custodis pactum et misericordiam cum his qui te diligunt, et custodiunt mandata tua :
     EN: saying, “Please, Lord, God of heaven, mighty, great, and awesome, who keeps covenant and mercy with those who love You and keep Your commandments,

1:6  LA: fiant aures tuæ auscultantes, et oculi tui aperti, ut audias orationem servi tui, quam ego oro coram te hodie nocte et die pro filiis Israël servis tuis : et confiteor pro peccatis filiorum Israël, quibus peccaverunt tibi : ego et domus patris mei peccavimus,
     EN: let Your ears be attentive and Your eyes open to hear Your servant's prayer, which I pray before You today, night and day, for the Israelites, Your servants. I confess the sins of the Israelites, which they have committed against You. I and my father's house have sinned.

1:7  LA: vanitate seducti sumus, et non custodivimus mandatum tuum, et cæremonias, et judicia quæ præcepisti Moysi famulo tuo.
     EN: We have been led astray by vanity and have not kept Your commandment, the observances, or the judgments that You commanded Your servant Moses.

1:8  LA: Memento verbi quod mandasti Moysi servo tuo, dicens : Cum transgressi fueritis, ego dispergam vos in populos :
     EN: Remember the word You commanded Your servant Moses, saying, ‘When you transgress, I will scatter you among the peoples.

1:9  LA: et si revertamini ad me, et custodiatis præcepta mea, et faciatis ea : etiamsi abducti fueritis ad extrema cæli, inde congregabo vos, et reducam in locum quem elegi ut habitaret nomen meum ibi.
     EN: But if you return to Me and keep My commands and do them, even if you have been carried away to the ends of heaven, from there I will gather you and bring you back to the place I have chosen for My name to dwell.’

1:10  LA: Et ipsi servi tui, et populus tuus, quos redemisti in fortitudine tua magna, et in manu tua valida.
      EN: They are Your servants and Your people, whom You redeemed by Your great strength and Your mighty hand.

1:11  LA: Obsecro, Domine, sit auris tua attendens ad orationem servi tui, et ad orationem servorum tuorum, qui volunt timere nomen tuum : et dirige servum tuum hodie, et da ei misericordiam ante virum hunc. Ego enim eram pincerna regis.
      EN: Please, Lord, let Your ear be attentive to Your servant's prayer and to the prayer of Your servants who desire to fear Your name. Guide Your servant today and grant him mercy before this man.” For I was the king's cupbearer.

NEHEMIAH 2

2:1  LA: Factum est autem in mense Nisan, anno vigesimo Artaxerxis regis : et vinum erat ante eum, et levavi vinum, et dedi regi : et eram quasi languidus ante faciem ejus.
     EN: In the month of Nisan, in the twentieth year of King Artaxerxes, wine was before him. I took up the wine and gave it to the king, and I looked as though I were ill in his presence.

2:2  LA: Dixitque mihi rex : Quare vultus tuus tristis est, cum te ægrotum non videam ? non est hoc frustra, sed malum nescio quod in corde tuo est. Et timui valde, ac nimis :
     EN: The king said to me, “Why is your face sad, when I do not see that you are sick? This is not without cause; some trouble I do not know is in your heart.” I was exceedingly afraid

2:3  LA: et dixi regi : Rex, in æternum vive : quare non mœreat vultus meus, quia civitas domus sepulchrorum patris mei deserta est, et portæ ejus combustæ sunt igni ?
     EN: and said to the king, “May the king live forever! Why should my face not mourn, when the city that holds my father's tombs lies desolate and its gates have been burned with fire?”

2:4  LA: Et ait mihi rex : Pro qua re postulas ? Et oravi Deum cæli,
     EN: The king said to me, “What are you asking for?” I prayed to the God of heaven

2:5  LA: et dixi ad regem : Si videtur regi bonum, et si placet servus tuus ante faciem tuam, ut mittas me in Judæam ad civitatem sepulchri patris mei, et ædificabo eam.
     EN: and said to the king, “If it seems good to the king, and if your servant finds favor in your presence, send me to Judah, to the city of my father's tomb, and I will rebuild it.”

2:6  LA: Dixitque mihi rex, et regina quæ sedebat juxta eum : Usque ad quod tempus erit iter tuum, et quando reverteris ? Et placuit ante vultum regis, et misit me : et constitui ei tempus.
     EN: The king said to me, with the queen sitting beside him, “How long will your journey last, and when will you return?” It pleased the king, and he sent me; I set a time for him.

2:7  LA: Et dixi regi : Si regi videtur bonum, epistolas det mihi ad duces regionis trans flumen, ut traducant me, donec veniam in Judæam :
     EN: I said to the king, “If it seems good to the king, let letters be given me for the governors of the region beyond the River, so that they may let me pass until I reach Judah;

2:8  LA: et epistolam ad Asaph custodem saltus regis, ut det mihi ligna, ut tegere possim portas turris domus, et muros civitatis, et domum quam ingressus fuero. Et dedit mihi rex juxta manum Dei mei bonam mecum.
     EN: also a letter to Asaph, keeper of the king's forest, to give me timber to roof the gates of the tower of the house, the city walls, and the house I shall enter.” The king granted this according to the good hand of my God upon me.

2:9  LA: Et veni ad duces regionis trans flumen, dedique eis epistolas regis. Miserat autem rex mecum principes militum, et equites.
     EN: I came to the governors of the region beyond the River and gave them the king's letters. The king had also sent army commanders and horsemen with me.

2:10  LA: Et audierunt Sanaballat Horonites, et Tobias servus Ammanites : et contristati sunt afflictione magna, quod venisset homo qui quæreret prosperitatem filiorum Israël.
      EN: When Sanballat the Horonite and Tobiah the Ammonite servant heard, they were deeply distressed that a man had come to seek the prosperity of the Israelites.

2:11  LA: Et veni Jerusalem, et eram ibi tribus diebus.
      EN: I came to Jerusalem and remained there three days.

2:12  LA: Et surrexi nocte ego, et viri pauci mecum, et non indicavi cuiquam quid Deus dedisset in corde meo ut facerem in Jerusalem : et jumentum non erat mecum, nisi animal cui sedebam.
      EN: I rose by night, with a few men accompanying me. I told no one what God had put in my heart to do in Jerusalem. There was no beast with me except the animal I rode.

2:13  LA: Et egressus sum per portam vallis nocte, et ante fontem draconis, et ad portam stercoris, et considerabam murum Jerusalem dissipatum, et portas ejus consumptas igni.
      EN: I went out by night through the Valley Gate, past the Dragon Spring, and to the Dung Gate, examining Jerusalem's broken wall and its gates consumed by fire.

2:14  LA: Et transivi ad portam fontis, et ad aquæductum regis, et non erat locus jumento cui sedebam ut transiret.
      EN: I passed on to the Fountain Gate and the king's aqueduct, but there was no room for the animal I rode to pass.

2:15  LA: Et ascendi per torrentem nocte, et considerabam murum, et reversus veni ad portam vallis, et redii.
      EN: I went up the torrent bed by night and examined the wall. Then I turned back, came to the Valley Gate, and returned.

2:16  LA: Magistratus autem nesciebant quo abiissem, aut quid ego facerem : sed et Judæis, et sacerdotibus, et optimatibus, et magistratibus, et reliquis qui faciebant opus, usque ad id loci nihil indicaveram.
      EN: The officials did not know where I had gone or what I was doing. Until then I had told nothing to the Jews, the priests, the nobles, the officials, or the others doing the work.

2:17  LA: Et dixi eis : Vos nostis afflictionem in qua sumus : quia Jerusalem deserta est, et portæ ejus consumptæ sunt igni : venite, et ædificemus muros Jerusalem, et non simus ultra opprobrium.
      EN: I said to them, “You know the distress we are in: Jerusalem lies desolate, and its gates have been consumed by fire. Come, let us rebuild Jerusalem's walls and no longer be a disgrace.”

2:18  LA: Et indicavi eis manum Dei mei, quod esset bona mecum, et verba regis quæ locutus esset mihi, et aio : Surgamus, et ædificemus. Et confortatæ sunt manus eorum in bono.
      EN: I told them of my God's hand, how it had been good to me, and the words the king had spoken to me. I said, “Let us rise and build.” Their hands were strengthened for the good work.

2:19  LA: Audierunt autem Sanaballat Horonites, et Tobias servus Ammanites, et Gosem Arabs, et subsannaverunt nos, et despexerunt, dixeruntque : Quæ est hæc res quam facitis ? numquid contra regem vos rebellatis ?
      EN: When Sanballat the Horonite, Tobiah the Ammonite servant, and Geshem the Arab heard, they mocked and despised us, saying, “What is this thing you are doing? Are you rebelling against the king?”

2:20  LA: Et reddidi eis sermonem, dixique ad eos : Deus cæli ipse nos juvat, et nos servi ejus sumus : surgamus et ædificemus : vobis autem non est pars, et justitia, et memoria in Jerusalem.
      EN: I answered them, “The God of heaven Himself helps us, and we are His servants. Let us rise and build. But you have no share, right, or memorial in Jerusalem.”

NEHEMIAH 3

3:1  LA: Et surrexit Eliasib sacerdos magnus, et fratres ejus sacerdotes, et ædificaverunt portam gregis : ipsi sanctificaverunt eam, et statuerunt valvas ejus, et usque ad turrim centum cubitorum sanctificaverunt eam, usque ad turrim Hananeel.
     EN: Eliashib the high priest and his fellow priests rose and built the Sheep Gate. They consecrated it and set up its doors, and consecrated it as far as the tower of a hundred cubits, as far as the Tower of Hananel.

3:2  LA: Et juxta eum ædificaverunt viri Jericho : et juxta eum ædificavit Zachur filius Amri.
     EN: Beside him the men of Jericho built, and beside him Zaccur son of Imri built.

3:3  LA: Portam autem piscium ædificaverunt filii Asnaa : ipsi texerunt eam, et statuerunt valvas ejus, et seras, et vectes. Et juxta eos ædificavit Marimuth filius Uriæ, filii Accus.
     EN: The sons of Hassenaah built the Fish Gate. They roofed it and set up its doors, bolts, and bars. Beside them Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, built.

3:4  LA: Et juxta eum ædificavit Mosollam filius Barachiæ, filii Mesezebel : et juxta eos ædificavit Sadoc filius Baana.
     EN: Beside him Meshullam son of Berechiah, son of Meshezabel, built, and beside them Zadok son of Baanah built.

3:5  LA: Et juxta eos ædificaverunt Thecueni : optimates autem eorum non supposuerunt colla sua in opere Domini sui.
     EN: Beside them the Tekoites built, but their nobles would not bend their necks to the work of their Lord.

3:6  LA: Et portam veterem ædificaverunt Jojada filius Phasea, et Mosollam filius Besodia : ipsi texerunt eam, et statuerunt valvas ejus, et seras, et vectes.
     EN: Joiada son of Paseah and Meshullam son of Besodeiah built the Old Gate. They roofed it and set up its doors, bolts, and bars.

3:8  LA: Et juxta eum ædificavit Eziel filius Araia aurifex : et juxta eum ædificavit Ananias filius pigmentarii : et dimiserunt Jerusalem usque ad murum plateæ latioris.
     EN: Beside him Uzziel son of Harhaiah, a goldsmith, built, and beside him Hananiah, the perfumer's son, built. They left Jerusalem as far as the wall of the broader street.

3:9  LA: Et juxta eum ædificavit Raphaia filius Hur, princeps vici Jerusalem.
     EN: Beside him Rephaiah son of Hur, ruler of the district of Jerusalem, built.

3:10  LA: Et juxta eum ædificavit Jedaia filius Haromaph contra domum suam : et juxta eum ædificavit Hattus filius Haseboniæ.
      EN: Beside him Jedaiah son of Harumaph built opposite his house, and beside him Hattush son of Hashabneiah built.

3:11  LA: Mediam partem vici ædificavit Melchias filius Herem, et Hasub filius Phahath Moab, et turrim furnorum.
      EN: Malchijah son of Harim and Hasshub son of Pahath-moab built half the district and the Tower of the Ovens.

3:12  LA: Et juxta eum ædificavit Sellum filius Alohes, princeps mediæ partis vici Jerusalem, ipse et filiæ ejus.
      EN: Beside him Shallum son of Hallohesh, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, built, together with his daughters.

3:13  LA: Et portam vallis ædificavit Hanun, et habitatores Zanoë : ipsi ædificaverunt eam, et statuerunt valvas ejus, et seras, et vectes, et mille cubitos in muro usque ad portam sterquilinii.
      EN: Hanun and the inhabitants of Zanoah built the Valley Gate. They built it and set up its doors, bolts, and bars, and built a thousand cubits of wall as far as the Dung Gate.

3:14  LA: Et portam sterquilinii ædificavit Melchias filius Rechab, princeps vici Bethacharam : ipse ædificavit eam, et statuit valvas ejus, et seras, et vectes.
      EN: Malchijah son of Rechab, ruler of the district of Beth-haccherem, built the Dung Gate. He built it and set up its doors, bolts, and bars.

3:15  LA: Et portam fontis ædificavit Sellum filius Cholhoza, princeps pagi Maspha : ipse ædificavit eam, et texit, et statuit valvas ejus, et seras, et vectes, et muros piscinæ Siloë in hortum regis, et usque ad gradus qui descendunt de civitate David.
      EN: Shallum son of Col-hozeh, ruler of the district of Mizpah, built the Fountain Gate. He built it, roofed it, and set up its doors, bolts, and bars, and built the walls of the pool of Siloam toward the king's garden, as far as the steps descending from the City of David.

3:16  LA: Post eum ædificavit Nehemias filius Azboc, princeps dimidiæ partis vici Bethsur, usque contra sepulchrum David, et usque ad piscinam quæ grandi opere constructa est, et usque ad domum fortium.
      EN: After him Nehemiah son of Azbuk, ruler of half the district of Beth-zur, built as far as the place opposite David's tomb, as far as the pool constructed with great labor, and as far as the house of the mighty men.

3:17  LA: Post eum ædificaverunt Levitæ, Rehum filius Benni : post eum ædificavit Hasebias princeps dimidiæ partis vici Ceilæ in vico suo.
      EN: After him the Levites built: Rehum son of Bani. After him Hashabiah, ruler of half the district of Keilah, built in his district.

3:18  LA: Post eum ædificaverunt fratres eorum : Bavai filius Enadad, princeps dimidiæ partis Ceilæ :
      EN: After him their brothers built: Bavvai son of Henadad, ruler of half of Keilah.

3:19  LA: et ædificavit juxta eum Azer filius Josue, princeps Maspha, mensuram secundam, contra ascensum firmissimi anguli.
      EN: Beside him Ezer son of Jeshua, ruler of Mizpah, built a second section, opposite the ascent to the strongest corner.

3:20  LA: Post eum in monte ædificavit Baruch filius Zachai mensuram secundam, ab angulo usque ad portam domus Eliasib sacerdotis magni.
      EN: After him Baruch son of Zaccai built a second section on the hill, from the corner to the entrance of the house of Eliashib the high priest.

3:21  LA: Post eum ædificavit Merimuth filius Uriæ filii Haccus, mensuram secundam, a porta domus Eliasib, donec extenderetur domus Eliasib.
      EN: After him Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, built a second section, from the entrance of Eliashib's house to the far end of Eliashib's house.

3:22  LA: Et post eum ædificaverunt sacerdotes, viri de campestribus Jordanis.
      EN: After him the priests, men of the Jordan plains, built.

3:23  LA: Post eum ædificavit Benjamin et Hasub contra domum suam : et post eum ædificavit Azarias filius Maasiæ filii Ananiæ contra domum suam.
      EN: After him Benjamin and Hasshub built opposite their house. After him Azariah son of Maaseiah, son of Ananiah, built opposite his house.

3:24  LA: Post eum ædificavit Bennui filius Henadad mensuram secundam, a domo Azariæ usque ad flexuram, et usque ad angulum.
      EN: After him Binnui son of Henadad built a second section, from Azariah's house to the bend and as far as the corner.

3:25  LA: Phalel filius Ozi contra flexuram, et turrim quæ eminet de domo regis excelsa, id est, in atrio carceris : post eum Phadaia filius Pharos.
      EN: Palal son of Uzai built opposite the bend and the tower projecting from the king's upper house, that is, in the prison courtyard. After him Pedaiah son of Parosh built.

3:26  LA: Nathinæi autem habitabant in Ophel usque contra portam aquarum ad orientem, et turrim quæ prominebat.
      EN: The Nethinim lived on Ophel, as far as the place opposite the Water Gate to the east and the projecting tower.

3:27  LA: Post eum ædificaverunt Thecueni mensuram secundam e regione, a turre magna et eminente usque ad murum templi.
      EN: After him the Tekoites built a second section opposite it, from the great projecting tower as far as the Temple wall.

3:28  LA: Sursum autem a porta equorum ædificaverunt sacerdotes, unusquisque contra domum suam.
      EN: Above the Horse Gate the priests built, each opposite his own house.

3:29  LA: Post eos ædificavit Sadoc filius Emmer contra domum suam. Et post eum ædificavit Semaia filius Secheniæ, custos portæ orientalis.
      EN: After them Zadok son of Immer built opposite his house. After him Shemaiah son of Shecaniah, keeper of the East Gate, built.

3:30  LA: Post eum ædificavit Hanania filius Selemiæ, et Hanun filius Seleph sextus, mensuram secundam : post eum ædificavit Mosollam filius Barachiæ, contra gazophylacium suum. Post eum ædificavit Melchias filius aurificis usque ad domum Nathinæorum, et scruta vendentium contra portam judicialem, et usque ad cœnaculum anguli.
      EN: After him Hananiah son of Shelemiah and Hanun, the sixth son of Zalaph, built a second section. After him Meshullam son of Berechiah built opposite his storeroom. After him Malchijah, a goldsmith's son, built as far as the house of the Nethinim and the dealers in secondhand goods, opposite the Gate of Judgment and as far as the upper room at the corner.

3:31  LA: Post eum ædificavit Hanania filius Selemiæ, et Hanun filius Seleph sextus, mensuram secundam : post eum ædificavit Mosollam filius Barachiæ, contra gazophylacium suum. Post eum ædificavit Melchias filius aurificis usque ad domum Nathinæorum, et scruta vendentium contra portam judicialem, et usque ad cœnaculum anguli.
      EN: After him Hananiah son of Shelemiah and Hanun, the sixth son of Zalaph, built a second section. After him Meshullam son of Berechiah built opposite his storeroom. After him Malchijah, a goldsmith's son, built as far as the house of the Nethinim and the dealers in secondhand goods, opposite the Gate of Judgment and as far as the upper room at the corner.

3:32  LA: Et inter cœnaculum anguli in porta gregis, ædificaverunt aurifices et negotiatores.
      EN: Between the upper room at the corner and the area at the Sheep Gate, the goldsmiths and merchants built.

3:33  LA: Factum est autem, cum audisset Sanaballat quod ædificaremus murum, iratus est valde : et motus nimis subsannavit Judæos,
      EN: When Sanballat heard that we were building the wall, he became very angry. Deeply agitated, he mocked the Jews

3:34  LA: et dixit coram fratribus suis, et frequentia Samaritanorum : Quid Judæi faciunt imbecilles ? num dimittent eos gentes ? num sacrificabunt, et complebunt in una die ? numquid ædificare poterunt lapides de acervis pulveris, qui combusti sunt ?
      EN: and said before his brothers and the gathering of Samaritans, “What are these feeble Jews doing? Will the nations leave them alone? Will they sacrifice and finish in a single day? Can they build with the burned stones from the heaps of dust?”

3:35  LA: Sed et Tobias Ammanites, proximus ejus, ait : Ædificent : si ascenderit vulpes, transiliet murum eorum lapideum.
      EN: Tobiah the Ammonite, who was beside him, said, “Let them build! If a fox climbs up, it will leap over their stone wall.”

3:36  LA: Audi, Deus noster, quia facti sumus despectui : converte opprobrium super caput eorum, et da eos in despectionem in terra captivitatis.
      EN: Hear, our God, for we have become an object of contempt. Turn their reproach back upon their heads and give them over to contempt in a land of captivity.

3:37  LA: Ne operias iniquitatem eorum, et peccatum eorum coram facie tua non deleatur, quia irriserunt ædificantes.
      EN: Do not cover their iniquity, and let their sin not be blotted out before You, because they have mocked the builders.

NEHEMIAH 4

4:1  LA: Factum est autem, cum audisset Sanaballat, et Tobias, et Arabes, et Ammanitæ, et Azotii, quod obducta esset cicatrix muri Jerusalem, et quod cœpissent interrupta concludi, irati sunt nimis.
     EN: When Sanballat, Tobiah, the Arabs, the Ammonites, and the people of Ashdod heard that the breach in Jerusalem's wall was healing and the gaps were beginning to close, they became very angry.

4:2  LA: Et congregati sunt omnes pariter ut venirent, et pugnarent contra Jerusalem, et molirentur insidias.
     EN: They all gathered together to come and fight against Jerusalem and devise an ambush.

4:3  LA: Et oravimus Deum nostrum, et posuimus custodes super murum die ac nocte contra eos.
     EN: We prayed to our God and posted guards on the wall against them, day and night.

4:4  LA: Dixit autem Judas : Debilitata est fortitudo portantis, et humus nimia est, et nos non poterimus ædificare murum.
     EN: Judah said, “The strength of the burden-bearers has failed, and there is too much rubble. We cannot build the wall.”

4:5  LA: Et dixerunt hostes nostri : Nesciant, et ignorent donec veniamus in medium eorum, et interficiamus eos, et cessare faciamus opus.
     EN: Our enemies said, “Let them neither know nor notice until we come among them, kill them, and put an end to the work.”

4:6  LA: Factum est autem venientibus Judæis qui habitabant juxta eos, et dicentibus nobis per decem vices, ex omnibus locis quibus venerant ad nos,
     EN: When the Jews living near them came and spoke to us ten times, from all the places from which they came to us,

4:7  LA: statui in loco post murum per circuitum populum in ordinem cum gladiis suis, et lanceis, et arcubus.
     EN: I stationed the people in order behind the wall all around, with their swords, spears, and bows.

4:8  LA: Et perspexi atque surrexi : et aio ad optimates et magistratus, et ad reliquam partem vulgi : Nolite timere a facie eorum : Domini magni et terribilis mementote, et pugnate pro fratribus vestris, filiis vestris, et filiabus vestris, et uxoribus vestris, et domibus vestris.
     EN: I looked, rose, and said to the nobles, the officials, and the rest of the people, “Do not be afraid of them. Remember the great and awesome Lord, and fight for your brothers, sons, daughters, wives, and homes.”

4:9  LA: Factum est autem, cum audissent inimici nostri nuntiatum esse nobis, dissipavit Deus consilium eorum. Et reversi sumus omnes ad muros, unusquisque ad opus suum.
     EN: When our enemies heard that we had been informed, God frustrated their plan. We all returned to the walls, each to his own work.

4:10  LA: Et factum est a die illa, media pars juvenum eorum faciebat opus, et media parata erat ad bellum : et lanceæ, et scuta, et arcus, et loricæ, et principes post eos in omni domo Juda.
      EN: From that day onward, half their young men did the work, and half were ready for battle, with spears, shields, bows, and armor. The leaders stood behind them throughout the house of Judah.

4:11  LA: Ædificantium in muro, et portantium onera, et imponentium : una manu sua faciebat opus, et altera tenebat gladium :
      EN: Those who built on the wall, carried burdens, and loaded them did the work with one hand and held a sword in the other.

4:12  LA: ædificentium enim unusquisque gladio erat accinctus renes. Et ædificabant, et clangebant buccina juxta me.
      EN: Each builder had a sword fastened at his waist. They built, and they sounded the trumpet beside me.

4:13  LA: Et dixi ad optimates, et ad magistratus, et ad reliquam partem vulgi : Opus grande est et latum, et nos separati sumus in muro procul alter ab altero :

      EN: You performed signs and wonders against Pharaoh, all his servants, and all the people of that land, for You knew that they had acted arrogantly against them. You made a name for Yourself, as it is to this day.

13:9  LA: præcepique et emundaverunt gazophylacia : et retuli ibi vasa domus Dei, sacrificium, et thus.
      EN: I gave orders, and they purified the storerooms. I brought back there the vessels of God's house, the offering, and the incense.

13:10  LA: Et cognovi quod partes Levitarum non fuissent datæ, et fugisset unusquisque in regionem suam de Levitis, et cantoribus, et de his qui ministrabant :
       EN: I learned that the Levites' portions had not been given, and that each of the Levites, singers, and other ministers had fled to his own district.

13:11  LA: et egi causam adversus magistratus, et dixi : Quare dereliquimus domum Dei ? et congregavi eos, et feci stare in stationibus suis.
       EN: I took up the case against the officials and said, “Why have we abandoned God's house?” I gathered them and stationed them at their posts.

13:12  LA: Et omnis Juda apportabat decimam frumenti, vini, et olei, in horrea.
       EN: All Judah brought the tithe of grain, wine, and oil into the storehouses.

13:13  LA: Et constituimus super horrea Selemiam sacerdotem, et Sadoc scribam, et Phadaiam de Levitis, et juxta eos Hanan filium Zachur, filium Mathaniæ : quoniam fideles comprobati sunt, et ipsis creditæ sunt partes fratrum suorum.
       EN: We appointed over the storehouses Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe, and Pedaiah from the Levites, with Hanan son of Zaccur, son of Mattaniah, beside them. They had proved faithful, and the portions of their brothers were entrusted to them.

13:14  LA: Memento mei, Deus meus, pro hoc, et ne deleas miserationes meas quas feci in domo Dei mei, et in cæremoniis ejus.
       EN: Remember me for this, my God, and do not blot out the acts of mercy I have done for the house of my God and its observances.

13:15  LA: In diebus illis vidi in Juda calcantes torcularia in sabbato, portantes acervos, et onerantes super asinos vinum, et uvas, et ficus, et omne onus, et inferentes in Jerusalem, die sabbati. Et contestatus sum ut in die qua vendere liceret, venderent.
       EN: In those days I saw people in Judah treading winepresses on the Sabbath, carrying heaps of produce, and loading donkeys with wine, grapes, figs, and every kind of burden, and bringing them into Jerusalem on the Sabbath day. I warned them to sell on a day when selling was permitted.

13:16  LA: Et Tyrii habitaverunt in ea, inferentes pisces, et omnia venalia : et vendebant in sabbatis filiis Juda in Jerusalem.
       EN: Tyrians living there brought fish and all kinds of merchandise and sold them to the people of Judah in Jerusalem on the Sabbaths.

13:17  LA: Et objurgavi optimates Juda, et dixi eis : Quæ est hæc res mala quam vos facitis, et profanatis diem sabbati ?
       EN: I rebuked the nobles of Judah and said to them, “What is this evil you are doing, profaning the Sabbath day?

13:18  LA: numquid non hæc fecerunt patres nostri, et adduxit Deus noster super nos omne malum hoc, et super civitatem hanc ? et vos additis iracundiam super Israël violando sabbatum.
       EN: Did not our fathers do these things, so that our God brought all this evil upon us and upon this city? Now you add to the anger against Israel by violating the Sabbath.”

13:19  LA: Factum est autem, cum quievissent portæ Jerusalem in die sabbati, dixi : et clauserunt januas, et præcepi ut non aperirent eas usque post sabbatum : et de pueris meis constitui super portas, ut nullus inferret onus in die sabbati.
       EN: When Jerusalem's gates were at rest on the Sabbath day, I gave the word and they closed the doors. I ordered that they should not open them until after the Sabbath. I stationed some of my servants at the gates so that no one could bring in a burden on the Sabbath day.

13:20  LA: Et manserunt negotiatores, et vendentes universa venalia, foris Jerusalem semel et bis.
       EN: The merchants and sellers of all kinds of merchandise spent the night outside Jerusalem once or twice.

13:21  LA: Et contestatus sum eos, et dixi eis : Quare manetis ex adverso muri ? si secundo hoc feceritis, manum mittam in vos. Itaque ex tempore illo non venerunt in sabbato.
       EN: I warned them and said, “Why do you stay opposite the wall? If you do this again, I will lay hands on you.” From that time on they did not come on the Sabbath.

13:22  LA: Dixi quoque Levitis ut mundarentur, et venirent ad custodiendas portas, et sanctificandam diem sabbati : et pro hoc ergo memento mei, Deus meus, et parce mihi secundum multitudinem miserationum tuarum.
       EN: I also told the Levites to purify themselves and come to guard the gates and sanctify the Sabbath day. Remember me for this also, my God, and spare me according to the abundance of Your mercies.

13:23  LA: Sed et in diebus illis vidi Judæos ducentes uxores Azotidas, Ammonitidas, et Moabitidas.
       EN: In those days I also saw Jews marrying women of Ashdod, Ammon, and Moab.

13:24  LA: Et filii eorum ex media parte loquebantur azotice, et nesciebant loqui judaice, et loquebantur juxta linguam populi et populi.
       EN: Half their children spoke the language of Ashdod and did not know how to speak the language of Judah; they spoke according to the language of one people or another.

13:25  LA: Et objurgavi eos, et maledixi. Et cecidi ex eis viros, et decalvavi eos, et adjuravi in Deo ut non darent filias suas filiis eorum, et non acciperent de filiabus eorum filiis suis et sibimetipsis, dicens :
       EN: I rebuked and cursed them. I struck some of the men, made them bald, and put them under oath by God not to give their daughters to those men's sons or take any of those men's daughters for their sons or themselves, saying,

13:26  LA: Numquid non in hujuscemodi re peccavit Salomon rex Israël ? et certe in gentibus multis non erat rex similis ei, et dilectus Deo suo erat, et posuit eum Deus regem super omnem Israël : et ipsum ergo duxerunt ad peccatum mulieres alienigenæ.
       EN: “Did not Solomon king of Israel sin in this matter? Among many nations there was no king like him; he was beloved by his God, and God made him king over all Israel. Yet foreign women led even him into sin.

13:27  LA: Numquid et nos inobedientes faciemus omne malum grande hoc ut prævaricemur in Deo nostro, et ducamus uxores peregrinas ?
       EN: Shall we too act disobediently and commit all this great evil, transgressing against our God and marrying foreign wives?”

13:28  LA: De filiis autem Jojada filii Eliasib sacerdotis magni, gener erat Sanaballat Horonites, quem fugavi a me.
       EN: Among the sons of Joiada son of Eliashib the high priest was a son-in-law of Sanballat the Horonite, whom I drove away from me.

13:29  LA: Recordare, Domine Deus meus, adversum eos qui polluunt sacerdotium, jusque sacerdotale et Leviticum.
       EN: Remember, Lord my God, against those who defile the priesthood and the priestly and Levitical law.

13:30  LA: Igitur mundavi eos ab omnibus alienigenis, et constitui ordines sacerdotum et Levitarum, unumquemque in ministerio suo :
       EN: I purified them from all foreigners and established the orders of the priests and Levites, each in his ministry,

13:31  LA: et in oblatione lignorum in temporibus constitutis, et in primitivis : memento mei, Deus meus, in bonum. Amen.
       EN: and for the wood offering at appointed times and for the firstfruits. Remember me, my God, for good. Amen.
