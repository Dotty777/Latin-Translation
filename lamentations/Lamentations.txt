LAMENTATIONS 1

1:1  LA: Quomodo sedet sola civitas plena populo ! Facta est quasi vidua domina gentium ; princeps provinciarum facta est sub tributo.
     EN: Aleph. How the city once full of people sits alone! The mistress of nations has become like a widow; the princess of provinces has been subjected to tribute.

1:2  LA: Plorans ploravit in nocte, et lacrimæ ejus in maxillis ejus : non est qui consoletur eam ex omnibus caris ejus ; omnes amici ejus spreverunt eam, et facti sunt ei inimici.
     EN: Beth. She weeps and weeps in the night, her tears upon her cheeks. Among all those dear to her there is no one to comfort her. All her friends have scorned her and become her enemies.

1:3  LA: Migravit Judas propter afflictionem, et multitudinem servitutis ; habitavit inter gentes, nec invenit requiem : omnes persecutores ejus apprehenderunt eam inter angustias.
     EN: Ghimel. Judah has gone away because of affliction and great servitude. She has lived among the nations and found no rest. All her pursuers have caught her amid her straits.

1:4  LA: Viæ Sion lugent, eo quod non sint qui veniant ad solemnitatem : omnes portæ ejus destructæ, sacerdotes ejus gementes ; virgines ejus squalidæ, et ipsa oppressa amaritudine.
     EN: Daleth. The roads of Zion mourn because no one comes to the feast. All her gates are destroyed, her priests groan, her maidens are disheveled, and she herself is weighed down with bitterness.

1:5  LA: Facti sunt hostes ejus in capite ; inimici ejus locupletati sunt : quia Dominus locutus est super eam propter multitudinem iniquitatum ejus. Parvuli ejus ducti sunt in captivitatem ante faciem tribulantis.
     EN: He. Her foes have become the head; her enemies have grown rich, because the Lord has spoken against her for her many iniquities. Her little ones have been led into captivity before the oppressor.

1:6  LA: Et egressus est a filia Sion omnis decor ejus ; facti sunt principes ejus velut arietes non invenientes pascua, et abierunt absque fortitudine ante faciem subsequentis.
     EN: Vau. All her beauty has departed from the daughter of Zion. Her princes have become like rams finding no pasture, and they have gone without strength before their pursuer.

1:7  LA: Recordata est Jerusalem dierum afflictionis suæ, et prævaricationis, omnium desiderabilium suorum, quæ habuerat a diebus antiquis, cum caderet populus ejus in manu hostili, et non esset auxiliator : viderunt eam hostes, et deriserunt sabbata ejus.
     EN: Zain. Jerusalem has remembered the days of her affliction and transgression, all the precious things she had from ancient days, when her people fell into the enemy's hand and there was no helper. Her enemies saw her and mocked her Sabbaths.

1:8  LA: Peccatum peccavit Jerusalem, propterea instabilis facta est ; omnes qui glorificabant eam spreverunt illam, quia viderunt ignominiam ejus : ipsa autem gemens conversa est retrorsum.
     EN: Heth. Jerusalem has grievously sinned; therefore she has become unsteady. All who honored her have scorned her because they have seen her disgrace. She herself groans and turns away.

1:9  LA: Sordes ejus in pedibus ejus, nec recordata est finis sui ; deposita est vehementer, non habens consolatorem. Vide, Domine, afflictionem meam, quoniam erectus est inimicus.
     EN: Teth. Her filth is on her feet, and she did not remember her end. She has been brought very low, with no one to comfort her. Look, Lord, upon my affliction, for the enemy has risen high.

1:10  LA: Manum suam misit hostis ad omnia desiderabilia ejus, quia vidit gentes ingressas sanctuarium suum, de quibus præceperas ne intrarent in ecclesiam tuam.
      EN: Jod. The enemy has stretched out his hand to all her precious things, for she has seen nations enter her sanctuary, those whom You had commanded not to enter Your assembly.

1:11  LA: Omnis populus ejus gemens, et quærens panem ; dederunt pretiosa quæque pro cibo ad refocillandam animam. Vide, Domine, et considera quoniam facta sum vilis !
      EN: Caph. All her people groan and seek bread. They have given every precious thing for food to restore their life. Look, Lord, and consider how despised I have become!

1:12  LA: O vos omnes qui transitis per viam, attendite, et videte si est dolor sicut dolor meus ! quoniam vindemiavit me, ut locutus est Dominus, in die iræ furoris sui.
      EN: Lamed. O all you who pass along the road, look closely and see whether there is pain like my pain! For He has harvested me like a vineyard, as the Lord declared, on the day of His burning anger.

1:13  LA: De excelso misit ignem in ossibus meis, et erudivit me : expandit rete pedibus meis, convertit me retrorsum ; posuit me desolatam, tota die mœrore confectam.
      EN: Mem. From on high He sent fire into my bones and instructed me. He spread a net for my feet and turned me back. He made me desolate, worn down with sorrow all day long.

1:14  LA: Vigilavit jugum iniquitatum mearum ; in manu ejus convolutæ sunt, et impositæ collo meo. Infirmata est virtus mea : dedit me Dominus in manu de qua non potero surgere.
      EN: Nun. The yoke of my iniquities has kept watch. In His hand they have been intertwined and laid upon my neck. My strength has failed; the Lord has delivered me into a hand from which I cannot rise.

1:15  LA: Abstulit omnes magnificos meos Dominus de medio mei ; vocavit adversum me tempus ut contereret electos meos. Torcular calcavit Dominus virgini filiæ Juda.
      EN: Samech. The Lord has removed all my mighty ones from my midst. He has summoned a time against me to crush my chosen men. The Lord has trodden the winepress for the virgin daughter of Judah.

1:16  LA: Idcirco ego plorans, et oculus meus deducens aquas, quia longe factus est a me consolator, convertens animam meam. Facti sunt filii mei perditi, quoniam invaluit inimicus.
      EN: Ain. Therefore I weep, and my eye pours out water, because the comforter who restores my soul has gone far from me. My children have been lost because the enemy has prevailed.

1:17  LA: Expandit Sion manus suas ; non est qui consoletur eam. Mandavit Dominus adversum Jacob in circuitu ejus hostes ejus ; facta est Jerusalem quasi polluta menstruis inter eos.
      EN: Phe. Zion has spread out her hands; there is no one to comfort her. The Lord has commanded Jacob's enemies to surround him. Jerusalem has become among them like a woman defiled by menstruation.

1:18  LA: Justus est Dominus, quia os ejus ad iracundiam provocavi. Audite, obsecro, universi populi, et videte dolorem meum : virgines meæ et juvenes mei abierunt in captivitatem.
      EN: Sade. The Lord is righteous, for I provoked His word to anger. Listen, I beg you, all peoples, and see my pain. My maidens and young men have gone into captivity.

1:19  LA: Vocavi amicos meos, et ipsi deceperunt me ; sacerdotes mei et senes mei in urbe consumpti sunt, quia quæsierunt cibum sibi ut refocillarent animam suam.
      EN: Coph. I called to my friends, but they deceived me. My priests and elders perished in the city while seeking food for themselves to restore their lives.

1:20  LA: Vide, Domine, quoniam tribulor : conturbatus est venter meus, subversum est cor meum in memetipsa, quoniam amaritudine plena sum. Foris interfecit gladius, et domi mors similis est.
      EN: Res. Look, Lord, for I am distressed. My belly is troubled, my heart overturned within me, because I am full of bitterness. Outside the sword has killed, and at home there is a similar death.

1:21  LA: Audierunt quia ingemisco ego, et non est qui consoletur me ; omnes inimici mei audierunt malum meum, lætati sunt quoniam tu fecisti : adduxisti diem consolationis, et fient similes mei.
      EN: Sin. They have heard that I groan, and there is no one to comfort me. All my enemies have heard of my misfortune and rejoiced because You have done it. You have brought the day of consolation, and they will become like me.

1:22  LA: Ingrediatur omne malum eorum coram te : et vindemia eos, sicut vindemiasti me propter omnes iniquitates meas : multi enim gemitus mei, et cor meum mœrens.]
      EN: Thau. Let all their evil come before You. Harvest them as You harvested me for all my iniquities, for my groans are many and my heart is sorrowful.

LAMENTATIONS 2

2:1  LA: Quomodo obtexit caligine in furore suo Dominus filiam Sion ; projecit de cælo in terram inclytam Israël, et non est recordatus scabelli pedum suorum in die furoris sui !
     EN: Aleph. How the Lord has covered the daughter of Zion with darkness in His fury! He has cast the glory of Israel from heaven to earth and has not remembered His footstool on the day of His fury.

2:2  LA: Præcipitavit Dominus, nec pepercit omnia speciosa Jacob : destruxit in furore suo munitiones virginis Juda, et dejecit in terram ; polluit regnum et principes ejus.
     EN: Beth. The Lord has cast down without sparing all the beautiful things of Jacob. In His fury He has destroyed the fortifications of the virgin of Judah and hurled them to the ground. He has profaned the kingdom and its princes.

2:3  LA: Confregit in ira furoris sui omne cornu Israël ; avertit retrorsum dexteram suam a facie inimici, et succendit in Jacob quasi ignem flammæ devorantis in gyro.
     EN: Ghimel. In His burning anger He has broken every horn of Israel. He has drawn back His right hand before the enemy and kindled a blazing fire in Jacob, devouring all around.

2:4  LA: Tetendit arcum suum quasi inimicus, firmavit dexteram suam quasi hostis, et occidit omne quod pulchrum erat visu in tabernaculo filiæ Sion ; effudit quasi ignem indignationem suam.
     EN: Daleth. He has bent His bow like an enemy, braced His right hand like a foe, and killed everything lovely to see in the tent of the daughter of Zion. He has poured out His indignation like fire.

2:5  LA: Factus est Dominus velut inimicus, præcipitavit Israël : præcipitavit omnia mœnia ejus, dissipavit munitiones ejus, et replevit in filia Juda humiliatum et humiliatam.
     EN: He. The Lord has become like an enemy; He has cast Israel down. He has cast down all its walls, demolished its fortifications, and filled the daughter of Judah with humiliated men and women.

2:6  LA: Et dissipavit quasi hortum tentorium suum ; demolitus est tabernaculum suum. Oblivioni tradidit Dominus in Sion festivitatem et sabbatum ; et in opprobrium, et in indignationem furoris sui, regem et sacerdotem.
     EN: Vau. He has torn apart His tent like a garden and demolished His tabernacle. The Lord has consigned feast and Sabbath in Zion to oblivion, and king and priest to disgrace and the indignation of His fury.

2:7  LA: Repulit Dominus altare suum ; maledixit sanctificationi suæ : tradidit in manu inimici muros turrium ejus. Vocem dederunt in domo Domini sicut in die solemni.
     EN: Zain. The Lord has rejected His altar and cursed His holy place. He has delivered the walls of her towers into the enemy's hand. They have raised their voices in the Lord's house as on a feast day.

2:8  LA: Cogitavit Dominus dissipare murum filiæ Sion ; tetendit funiculum suum, et non avertit manum suam a perditione : luxitque antemurale, et murus pariter dissipatus est.
     EN: Heth. The Lord has determined to tear down the wall of the daughter of Zion. He has stretched out His measuring line and has not drawn His hand back from destruction. The outer rampart has mourned, and the wall has been torn down with it.

2:9  LA: Defixæ sunt in terra portæ ejus, perdidit et contrivit vectes ejus ; regem ejus et principes ejus in gentibus : non est lex, et prophetæ ejus non invenerunt visionem a Domino.
     EN: Teth. Her gates have sunk into the ground. He has destroyed and broken her bars; her king and princes are among the nations. There is no law, and her prophets have found no vision from the Lord.

2:10  LA: Sederunt in terra, conticuerunt senes filiæ Sion ; consperserunt cinere capita sua, accincti sunt ciliciis : abjecerunt in terram capita sua virgines Jerusalem.
      EN: Jod. The elders of the daughter of Zion sit on the ground in silence. They have sprinkled their heads with ashes and clothed themselves in sackcloth. The maidens of Jerusalem have bowed their heads to the ground.

2:11  LA: Defecerunt præ lacrimis oculi mei, conturbata sunt viscera mea ; effusum est in terra jecur meum super contritione filiæ populi mei, cum deficeret parvulus et lactens in plateis oppidi.
      EN: Caph. My eyes fail from tears, my inward parts are troubled, and my liver is poured out on the ground over the crushing of the daughter of my people, while child and nursing infant faint in the town's squares.

2:12  LA: Matribus suis dixerunt : Ubi est triticum et vinum ? cum deficerent quasi vulnerati in plateis civitatis, cum exhalarent animas suas in sinu matrum suarum.
      EN: Lamed. They said to their mothers, “Where are the wheat and wine?” as they fainted like wounded people in the city squares, as they breathed out their lives in their mothers' laps.

2:13  LA: Cui comparabo te, vel cui assimilabo te, filia Jerusalem ? cui exæquabo te, et consolabor te, virgo, filia Sion ? magna est enim velut mare contritio tua : quis medebitur tui ?
      EN: Mem. To whom shall I compare you, or to whom liken you, daughter of Jerusalem? With whom shall I match you, to comfort you, virgin daughter of Zion? For your ruin is great as the sea. Who will heal you?

2:14  LA: Prophetæ tui viderunt tibi falsa et stulta ; nec aperiebant iniquitatem tuam, ut te ad pœnitentiam provocarent ; viderunt autem tibi assumptiones falsas, et ejectiones.
      EN: Nun. Your prophets have seen false and foolish things for you. They did not uncover your iniquity to move you to repentance, but saw false proclamations and expulsions for you.

2:15  LA: Plauserunt super te manibus omnes transeuntes per viam ; sibilaverunt et moverunt caput suum super filiam Jerusalem : Hæccine est urbs, dicentes, perfecti decoris, gaudium universæ terræ ?
      EN: Samech. All who pass along the road clap their hands at you. They hiss and shake their heads over the daughter of Jerusalem, saying, “Is this the city of perfect beauty, the joy of the whole earth?”

2:16  LA: Aperuerunt super te os suum omnes inimici tui : sibilaverunt, et fremuerunt dentibus, et dixerunt : Devorabimus : en ista est dies quam exspectabamus ; invenimus, vidimus.
      EN: Phe. All your enemies open their mouths against you. They hiss and gnash their teeth, saying, “We will devour her! This is the day we waited for; we have found it, we have seen it.”

2:17  LA: Fecit Dominus quæ cogitavit ; complevit sermonem suum, quem præceperat a diebus antiquis : destruxit, et non pepercit, et lætificavit super te inimicum, et exaltavit cornu hostium tuorum.
      EN: Ain. The Lord has done what He planned. He has fulfilled His word, which He commanded in ancient days. He has destroyed without sparing, made the enemy rejoice over you, and raised high the horn of your foes.

2:18  LA: Clamavit cor eorum ad Dominum super muros filiæ Sion : Deduc quasi torrentem lacrimas per diem et noctem ; non des requiem tibi, neque taceat pupilla oculi tui.
      EN: Sade. Their heart cried to the Lord over the walls of the daughter of Zion: Pour down tears like a torrent, day and night. Give yourself no rest, and let the pupil of your eye not be silent.

2:19  LA: Consurge, lauda in nocte, in principio vigiliarum ; effunde sicut aquam cor tuum ante conspectum Domini : leva ad eum manus tuas pro anima parvulorum tuorum, qui defecerunt in fame in capite omnium compitorum.
      EN: Coph. Rise, give praise in the night at the beginning of the watches. Pour out your heart like water before the Lord. Lift your hands to Him for the lives of your little ones who have fainted from hunger at the head of every street.

2:20  LA: Vide, Domine, et considera quem vindemiaveris ita. Ergone comedent mulieres fructum suum, parvulos ad mensuram palmæ ? si occiditur in sanctuario Domini sacerdos et propheta ?
      EN: Res. Look, Lord, and consider whom You have harvested like this. Shall women eat their own fruit, little ones a handbreadth long? Shall priest and prophet be killed in the Lord's sanctuary?

2:21  LA: Jacuerunt in terra foris puer et senex ; virgines meæ et juvenes mei ceciderunt in gladio : interfecisti in die furoris tui, percussisti, nec misertus es.
      EN: Sin. Child and old man lie outside on the ground. My maidens and young men have fallen by the sword. You have killed on the day of Your fury; You have struck without mercy.

2:22  LA: Vocasti quasi ad diem solemnem, qui terrerent me de circuitu ; et non fuit in die furoris Domini qui effugeret, et relinqueretur : quos educavi et enutrivi, inimicus meus consumpsit eos.]
      EN: Thau. You have summoned those who terrify me on every side as though to a feast day. On the day of the Lord's fury no one escaped or survived. Those I reared and nourished, my enemy has consumed.

LAMENTATIONS 3

3:1  LA: Ego vir videns paupertatem meam in virga indignationis ejus.
     EN: Aleph. I am the man who sees my poverty under the rod of His indignation.

3:2  LA: Me minavit, et adduxit in tenebras, et non in lucem.
     EN: Aleph. He has driven me and brought me into darkness, not into light.

3:3  LA: Tantum in me vertit et convertit manum suam tota die.
     EN: Aleph. Against me alone He turns His hand again and again, all day long.

3:4  LA: Vetustam fecit pellem meam et carnem meam ; contrivit ossa mea.
     EN: Beth. He has aged my skin and my flesh; He has broken my bones.

3:5  LA: Ædificavit in gyro meo, et circumdedit me felle et labore.
     EN: Beth. He has built around me and surrounded me with gall and hardship.

3:6  LA: In tenebrosis collocavit me, quasi mortuos sempiternos.
     EN: Beth. He has placed me in dark places, like those forever dead.

3:7  LA: Circumædificavit adversum me, ut non egrediar ; aggravavit compedem meum.
     EN: Ghimel. He has built a wall against me so that I cannot escape; He has made my fetter heavy.

3:8  LA: Sed et cum clamavero, et rogavero, exclusit orationem meam.
     EN: Ghimel. Even when I cry out and plead, He shuts out my prayer.

3:9  LA: Conclusit vias meas lapidibus quadris ; semitas meas subvertit.
     EN: Ghimel. He has blocked my ways with squared stones and overturned my paths.

3:10  LA: Ursus insidians factus est mihi, leo in absconditis.
      EN: Daleth. He has become to me a bear lying in wait, a lion in hiding.

3:11  LA: Semitas meas subvertit, et confregit me ; posuit me desolatam.
      EN: Daleth. He has overturned my paths and broken me; He has left me desolate.

3:12  LA: Tetendit arcum suum, et posuit me quasi signum ad sagittam.
      EN: Daleth. He has bent His bow and set me as a target for an arrow.

3:13  LA: Misit in renibus meis filias pharetræ suæ.
      EN: He. He has driven the daughters of His quiver into my kidneys.

3:14  LA: Factus sum in derisum omni populo meo, canticum eorum tota die.
      EN: He. I have become a laughingstock to all my people, their song all day long.

3:15  LA: Replevit me amaritudinibus ; inebriavit me absinthio.
      EN: He. He has filled me with bitterness and made me drunk with wormwood.

3:16  LA: Et fregit ad numerum dentes meos ; cibavit me cinere.
      EN: Vau. He has broken my teeth one by one and fed me ashes.

3:17  LA: Et repulsa est a pace anima mea ; oblitus sum bonorum.
      EN: Vau. My soul has been driven from peace; I have forgotten good things.

3:18  LA: Et dixi : Periit finis meus, et spes mea a Domino.
      EN: Vau. I said, “My end has perished, and my hope from the Lord.”

3:19  LA: Recordare paupertatis, et transgressionis meæ, absinthii et fellis.
      EN: Zain. Remember my poverty and my transgression, the wormwood and the gall.

3:20  LA: Memoria memor ero, et tabescet in me anima mea.
      EN: Zain. I will surely remember, and my soul will waste away within me.

3:21  LA: Hæc recolens in corde meo, ideo sperabo.
      EN: Zain. Recalling these things in my heart, therefore I will hope.

3:25  LA: Bonus est Dominus sperantibus in eum, animæ quærenti illum.
      EN: Teth. The Lord is good to those who hope in Him, to the soul that seeks Him.

3:26  LA: Bonum est præstolari cum silentio salutare Dei.
      EN: Teth. It is good to wait in silence for God's salvation.

3:27  LA: Bonum est viro cum portaverit jugum ab adolescentia sua.
      EN: Teth. It is good for a man to have borne the yoke from his youth.

3:28  LA: Sedebit solitarius, et tacebit, quia levavit super se.
      EN: Jod. He will sit alone and be silent, because he has lifted it upon himself.

3:30  LA: Dabit percutienti se maxillam : saturabitur opprobriis.
      EN: Jod. He will offer his cheek to the one who strikes him; he will be filled with reproaches.

3:31  LA: Quia non repellet in sempiternum Dominus.
      EN: Caph. For the Lord will not reject forever.

3:32  LA: Quia si abjecit, et miserebitur, secundum multitudinem misericordiarum suarum.
      EN: Caph. Even if He has cast off, He will also show pity, according to the abundance of His mercies.

3:33  LA: Non enim humiliavit ex corde suo et abjecit filios hominum.
      EN: Caph. For He has not humbled and cast off the children of humanity from His heart.

3:34  LA: Ut conteret sub pedibus suis omnes vinctos terræ.
      EN: Lamed. To crush beneath His feet all the prisoners of the earth,

3:35  LA: Ut declinaret judicium viri in conspectu vultus Altissimi.
      EN: Lamed. to bend a man's judgment in the presence of the Most High,

3:36  LA: Ut perverteret hominem in judicio suo ; Dominus ignoravit.
      EN: Lamed. to wrong a man in his case—the Lord has not known this.

3:37  LA: Quis est iste qui dixit ut fieret, Domino non jubente ?
      EN: Mem. Who is this who said that a thing should be done, without the Lord commanding it?

3:38  LA: Ex ore Altissimi non egredientur nec mala nec bona ?
      EN: Mem. Do not both evils and good things come from the mouth of the Most High?

3:39  LA: Quid murmuravit homo vivens, vir pro peccatis suis ?
      EN: Mem. Why does a living man grumble, a man over his own sins?

3:40  LA: Scrutemur vias nostras, et quæramus, et revertamur ad Dominum.
      EN: Nun. Let us examine our ways and search them out, and return to the Lord.

3:41  LA: Levemus corda nostra cum manibus ad Dominum in cælos.
      EN: Nun. Let us lift our hearts with our hands to the Lord in heaven.

3:42  LA: Nos inique egimus, et ad iracundiam provocavimus ; idcirco tu inexorabilis es.
      EN: Nun. We have acted wickedly and provoked anger; therefore You are unappeasable.

3:43  LA: Operuisti in furore, et percussisti nos ; occidisti, nec pepercisti.
      EN: Samech. You have covered us in fury and struck us; You have killed without sparing.

3:44  LA: Opposuisti nubem tibi, ne transeat oratio.
      EN: Samech. You have set a cloud before Yourself so that prayer cannot pass through.

3:45  LA: Eradicationem et abjectionem posuisti me in medio populorum.
      EN: Samech. You have made me an uprooted and rejected thing among the peoples.

3:46  LA: Aperuerunt super nos os suum omnes inimici.
      EN: Phe. All our enemies have opened their mouths against us.

3:47  LA: Formido et laqueus facta est nobis vaticinatio, et contritio.
      EN: Phe. Prophecy and ruin have become terror and a snare for us.

3:48  LA: Divisiones aquarum deduxit oculus meus, in contritione filiæ populi mei.
      EN: Phe. My eye pours down streams of water over the crushing of the daughter of my people.

3:49  LA: Oculus meus afflictus est, nec tacuit, eo quod non esset requies.
      EN: Ain. My eye has been afflicted and has not fallen silent, because there is no rest,

3:50  LA: Donec respiceret et videret Dominus de cælis.
      EN: Ain. until the Lord looks down and sees from heaven.

3:51  LA: Oculus meus deprædatus est animam meam in cunctis filiabus urbis meæ.
      EN: Ain. My eye has ravaged my soul over all the daughters of my city.

3:52  LA: Venatione ceperunt me quasi avem inimici mei gratis.
      EN: Sade. My enemies have hunted and caught me like a bird, without cause.

3:53  LA: Lapsa est in lacum vita mea, et posuerunt lapidem super me.
      EN: Sade. My life has fallen into the pit, and they have placed a stone over me.

3:54  LA: Inundaverunt aquæ super caput meum ; dixi : Perii.
      EN: Sade. Waters have flooded over my head; I said, “I am lost.”

3:55  LA: Invocavi nomen tuum, Domine, de lacu novissimo.
      EN: Coph. I called upon Your name, Lord, from the deepest pit.

3:56  LA: Vocem meam audisti ; ne avertas aurem tuam a singultu meo et clamoribus.
      EN: Coph. You heard my voice; do not turn Your ear from my sobbing and cries.

3:57  LA: Appropinquasti in die quando invocavi te ; dixisti : Ne timeas.
      EN: Coph. You drew near on the day I called upon You; You said, “Do not be afraid.”

3:58  LA: Judicasti, Domine, causam animæ meæ, redemptor vitæ meæ.
      EN: Res. You have judged my soul's cause, Lord, Redeemer of my life.

3:59  LA: Vidisti, Domine, iniquitatem illorum adversum me : judica judicium meum.
      EN: Res. You have seen their iniquity against me, Lord; judge my case.

3:60  LA: Vidisti omnem furorem, universas cogitationes eorum adversum me.
      EN: Res. You have seen all their fury, all their schemes against me.

3:61  LA: Audisti opprobrium eorum, Domine, omnes cogitationes eorum adversum me.
      EN: Sin. You have heard their reproach, Lord, all their schemes against me,
