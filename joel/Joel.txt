JOEL — LATIN–ENGLISH (CLEMENTINE VULGATE)

Verse numbers follow the Saints (Greek/Hebrew) column; 'vulgate_reference' in verses.json gives the Vulgate's own number where it differs.

JOEL 1

1:1  LA: Verbum Domini, quod factum est ad Joël, filium Phatuel.
     EN: The word of the Lord that came to Joel son of Pethuel.

1:2  LA: [Audite hoc, senes, et auribus percipite, omnes habitatores terræ : si factum est istud in diebus vestris, aut in diebus patrum vestrorum ?
     EN: Hear this, elders; give ear, all inhabitants of the land. Has such a thing happened in your days or in the days of your fathers?

1:3  LA: Super hoc filiis vestris narrate, et filii vestri filiis suis, et filii eorum generationi alteræ.
     EN: Tell your children about it, and let your children tell their children, and their children another generation.

1:4  LA: Residuum erucæ comedit locusta, et residuum locustæ comedit bruchus, et residuum bruchi comedit rubigo.
     EN: What the caterpillar left, the locust ate; what the locust left, the young locust ate; what the young locust left, the blight ate.

1:5  LA: Expergiscimini, ebrii, et flete et ululate, omnes qui bibitis vinum in dulcedine, quoniam periit ab ore vestro.
     EN: Wake up, drunkards, and weep; howl, all who drink wine with delight, for it has vanished from your mouth.

1:6  LA: Gens enim ascendit super terram meam, fortis et innumerabilis : dentes ejus ut dentes leonis, et molares ejus ut catuli leonis.
     EN: For a nation has come up against My land, strong and beyond number. Its teeth are like a lion's teeth, and its fangs like a lion cub's.

1:7  LA: Posuit vineam meam in desertum, et ficum meam decorticavit ; nudans spoliavit eam, et projecit : albi facti sunt rami ejus.
     EN: It has made My vineyard a waste and stripped the bark from My fig tree. It has laid it utterly bare and cast it away; its branches have become white.

1:8  LA: Plange quasi virgo accincta sacco super virum pubertatis suæ.
     EN: Lament like a virgin clothed in sackcloth over the husband of her youth.

1:9  LA: Periit sacrificium et libatio de domo Domini ; luxerunt sacerdotes, ministri Domini.
     EN: Sacrifice and libation have vanished from the house of the Lord; the priests, the Lord's ministers, have mourned.

1:10  LA: Depopulata est regio, luxit humus, quoniam devastatum est triticum, confusum est vinum, elanguit oleum.
      EN: The countryside has been devastated, the soil has mourned, for the wheat is ruined, the wine has failed, and the oil has dwindled.

1:11  LA: Confusi sunt agricolæ, ululaverunt vinitores super frumento et hordeo, quia periit messis agri.
      EN: The farmers have been confounded; the vinegrowers have howled over wheat and barley, for the harvest of the field has perished.

1:12  LA: Vinea confusa est, et ficus elanguit ; malogranatum, et palma, et malum, et omnia ligna agri aruerunt, quia confusum est gaudium a filiis hominum.
      EN: The vine has failed and the fig tree withered. Pomegranate, palm, apple tree, and all the trees of the field have dried up, for joy has been confounded among the children of humankind.

1:13  LA: Accingite vos, et plangite, sacerdotes : ululate, ministri altaris ; ingredimini, cubate in sacco, ministri Dei mei, quoniam interiit de domo Dei vestri sacrificium et libatio.
      EN: Gird yourselves and lament, priests; howl, ministers of the altar. Go in and lie in sackcloth, ministers of my God, for sacrifice and libation have vanished from the house of your God.

1:14  LA: Sanctificate jejunium, vocate cœtum, congregate senes, omnes habitatores terræ in domum Dei vestri, et clamate ad Dominum :
      EN: Consecrate a fast, summon an assembly, gather the elders and all the land's inhabitants into the house of your God, and cry to the Lord:

1:15  LA: A, a, a, diei ! quia prope est dies Domini, et quasi vastitas a potente veniet.
      EN: “Ah, ah, ah, for the day! For the day of the Lord is near, and it will come like devastation from the Mighty One.”

1:16  LA: Numquid non coram oculis vestris alimenta perierunt de domo Dei nostri, lætitia et exsultatio ?
      EN: Has food not vanished before your eyes, and gladness and exultation from the house of our God?

1:17  LA: Computruerunt jumenta in stercore suo, demolita sunt horrea, dissipatæ sunt apothecæ, quoniam confusum est triticum.
      EN: The beasts of burden have rotted in their dung; the granaries are demolished, the storehouses broken down, for the wheat has failed.

1:18  LA: Quid ingemuit animal, mugierunt greges armenti ? quia non est pascua eis ; sed et greges pecorum disperierunt.
      EN: Why has the animal groaned, why have the herds of cattle lowed? Because they have no pasture; even the flocks of sheep have perished.

1:19  LA: Ad te, Domine, clamabo, quia ignis comedit speciosa deserti, et flamma succendit omnia ligna regionis.
      EN: To You, Lord, I will cry, for fire has consumed the wilderness's lovely places, and flame has set ablaze all the trees of the countryside.

1:20  LA: Sed et bestiæ agri, quasi area sitiens imbrem, suspexerunt ad te, quoniam exsiccati sunt fontes aquarum, et ignis devoravit speciosa deserti.]
      EN: Even the wild beasts, like a threshing floor thirsting for rain, have looked up to You, for the springs of water have dried up, and fire has devoured the wilderness's lovely places.

JOEL 2

2:1  LA: Canite tuba in Sion, ululate in monte sancto meo, conturbentur omnes habitatores terræ : quia venit dies Domini, quia prope est.
     EN: Sound the trumpet in Zion, howl on My holy mountain; let all inhabitants of the land be troubled, for the day of the Lord is coming, for it is near.

2:2  LA: Dies tenebrarum et caliginis, dies nubis et turbinis ; quasi mane expansum super montes populus multus et fortis : similis ei non fuit a principio, et post eum non erit usque in annos generationis et generationis.
     EN: A day of darkness and gloom, a day of cloud and whirlwind! Like dawn spread over the mountains is a people numerous and strong. Its like has not been from the beginning, nor will there be its like afterward through the years of generation upon generation.

2:3  LA: Ante faciem ejus ignis vorans, et post eum exurens flamma. Quasi hortus voluptatis terra coram eo, et post eum solitudo deserti, neque est qui effugiat eum.
     EN: Before it is a devouring fire, and behind it a burning flame. The land before it is like a garden of delight, and behind it a desolate wilderness; no one escapes it.

2:4  LA: Quasi aspectus equorum, aspectus eorum ; et quasi equites, sic current.
     EN: Their appearance is like the appearance of horses, and like horsemen they run.

2:5  LA: Sicut sonitus quadrigarum super capita montium exilient, sicut sonitus flammæ ignis devorantis stipulam, velut populus fortis præparatus ad prælium.
     EN: Like the sound of four-horse chariots they leap over the mountaintops, like the sound of a fiery flame devouring straw, like a strong people prepared for battle.

2:6  LA: A facie ejus cruciabuntur populi ; omnes vultus redigentur in ollam.
     EN: Before it peoples will writhe in torment; every face will become like a cooking pot.

2:7  LA: Sicut fortes current ; quasi viri bellatores ascendent murum : viri in viis suis gradientur, et non declinabunt a semitis suis.
     EN: Like mighty men they will run; like warriors they will climb the wall. Each will march on his own way, and they will not turn from their paths.

2:8  LA: Unusquisque fratrem suum non coarctabit, singuli in calle suo ambulabunt ; sed et per fenestras cadent, et non demolientur.
     EN: No one will crowd his brother; each will walk along his own track. They will even plunge through windows and will not be shattered.

2:9  LA: Urbem ingredientur, in muro current, domos conscendent, per fenestras intrabunt quasi fur.
     EN: They will enter the city, run upon the wall, climb the houses, and enter through the windows like a thief.

2:10  LA: A facie ejus contremuit terra, moti sunt cæli, sol et luna obtenebrati sunt, et stellæ retraxerunt splendorem suum.
      EN: Before it the earth has trembled, the heavens have shaken, the sun and moon have darkened, and the stars have withdrawn their brightness.

2:11  LA: Et Dominus dedit vocem suam ante faciem exercitus sui, quia multa sunt nimis castra ejus, quia fortia et facientia verbum ejus : magnus enim dies Domini, et terribilis valde, et quis sustinebit eum ?
      EN: The Lord has raised His voice before His army, for His camps are exceedingly numerous, for they are strong and carry out His word. Great indeed is the day of the Lord, and very terrible; who will endure it?

2:12  LA: Nunc ergo, dicit Dominus, convertimini ad me in toto corde vestro, in jejunio, et in fletu, et in planctu.
      EN: “Now therefore,” says the Lord, “return to Me with your whole heart, with fasting and weeping and lamentation.”

2:13  LA: Et scindite corda vestra, et non vestimenta vestra ; et convertimini ad Dominum Deum vestrum, quia benignus et misericors est, patiens et multæ misericordiæ, et præstabilis super malitia.
      EN: Rend your hearts and not your garments, and return to the Lord your God, for He is kind and merciful, patient and rich in mercy, and ready to relent over calamity.

2:14  LA: Quis scit si convertatur, et ignoscat, et relinquat post se benedictionem, sacrificium et libamen Domino Deo vestro ?
      EN: Who knows whether He may turn and forgive, and leave a blessing behind Him, a sacrifice and libation for the Lord your God?

2:15  LA: Canite tuba in Sion, sanctificate jejunium, vocate cœtum :
      EN: Sound the trumpet in Zion, consecrate a fast, summon an assembly.

2:16  LA: congregate populum, sanctificate ecclesiam, coadunate senes, congregate parvulos, et sugentes ubera ; egrediatur sponsus de cubili suo, et sponsa de thalamo suo.
      EN: Gather the people, consecrate the congregation, bring the elders together, gather the little ones and those nursing at the breast. Let the bridegroom leave his room and the bride her chamber.

2:17  LA: Inter vestibulum et altare plorabunt sacerdotes, ministri Domini, et dicent : Parce, Domine, parce populo tuo ; et ne des hæreditatem tuam in opprobrium, ut dominentur eis nationes. Quare dicunt in populis : Ubi est Deus eorum ?]
      EN: Between the porch and the altar the priests, the Lord's ministers, will weep and say, “Spare, Lord, spare Your people, and do not give Your inheritance over to reproach, for nations to rule over them. Why do they say among the peoples, ‘Where is their God?’”

2:18  LA: [Zelatus est Dominus terram suam, et pepercit populo suo.
      EN: The Lord was zealous for His land and spared His people.

2:19  LA: Et respondit Dominus, et dixit populo suo : Ecce ego mittam vobis frumentum, et vinum, et oleum, et replebimini eis ; et non dabo vos ultra opprobrium in gentibus.
      EN: The Lord answered and said to His people, “See, I will send you grain and wine and oil, and you will be filled with them. I will no longer make you a reproach among the nations.

2:20  LA: Et eum qui ab aquilone est procul faciam a vobis, et expellam eum in terram inviam et desertam : faciem ejus contra mare orientale, et extremum ejus ad mare novissimum : et ascendet fœtor ejus, et ascendet putredo ejus, quia superbe egit.
      EN: I will drive the one from the north far from you and expel him into a trackless and deserted land, his front toward the eastern sea and his rear toward the farthest sea. His stench will rise, and his rot will rise, because he acted proudly.”

2:21  LA: Noli timere, terra : exsulta, et lætare, quoniam magnificavit Dominus ut faceret.
      EN: Do not fear, land; exult and rejoice, for the Lord has done great things.

2:22  LA: Nolite timere, animalia regionis, quia germinaverunt speciosa deserti ; quia lignum attulit fructum suum, ficus et vinea dederunt virtutem suam.
      EN: Do not fear, animals of the countryside, for the wilderness's lovely places have sprouted; the tree has borne its fruit, the fig tree and vine have yielded their strength.

2:23  LA: Et, filii Sion, exsultate, et lætamini in Domino Deo vestro, quia dedit vobis doctorem justitiæ, et descendere faciet ad vos imbrem matutinum et serotinum, sicut in principio.
      EN: Children of Zion, exult and rejoice in the Lord your God, for He has given you a teacher of righteousness, and He will make the early and late rain come down to you, as at the beginning.

2:24  LA: Et implebuntur areæ frumento, et redundabunt torcularia vino et oleo.
      EN: The threshing floors will be filled with grain, and the presses will overflow with wine and oil.

2:25  LA: Et reddam vobis annos, quos comedit locusta, bruchus, et rubigo, et eruca : fortitudo mea magna quam misi in vos.
      EN: “I will restore to you the years that the locust, young locust, blight, and caterpillar ate, My great force that I sent against you.

2:26  LA: Et comedetis vescentes, et saturabimini ; et laudabitis nomen Domini Dei vestri, qui fecit mirabilia vobiscum ; et non confundetur populus meus in sempiternum.
      EN: You will eat your fill and be satisfied, and praise the name of the Lord your God, who has worked wonders with you. My people will never be put to shame.

2:27  LA: Et scietis quia in medio Israël ego sum, et ego Dominus Deus vester, et non est amplius ; et non confundetur populus meus in æternum.]
      EN: You will know that I am in the midst of Israel, that I am the Lord your God and there is no other. My people will never be put to shame.

JOEL 3

3:1  LA: [Et erit post hæc : effundam spiritum meum super omnem carnem, et prophetabunt filii vestri et filiæ vestræ : senes vestri somnia somniabunt, et juvenes vestri visiones videbunt.
     EN: After these things I will pour out My Spirit upon all flesh. Your sons and your daughters will prophesy; your elders will dream dreams, and your young men will see visions.

3:2  LA: Sed et super servos meos et ancillas in diebus illis effundam spiritum meum.
     EN: Even upon My male servants and female servants I will pour out My Spirit in those days.

3:3  LA: Et dabo prodigia in cælo et in terra, sanguinem, et ignem, et vaporem fumi.
     EN: I will give portents in heaven and on earth: blood and fire and vapor of smoke.

3:4  LA: Sol convertetur in tenebras, et luna in sanguinem, antequam veniat dies Domini magnus et horribilis.
     EN: The sun will turn to darkness and the moon to blood before the great and dreadful day of the Lord comes.

3:5  LA: Et erit : omnis qui invocaverit nomen Domini, salvus erit : quia in monte Sion et in Jerusalem erit salvatio, sicut dixit Dominus, et in residuis quos Dominus vocaverit.]
     EN: Everyone who calls upon the name of the Lord will be saved, for on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there will be salvation, as the Lord has said, and among the survivors whom the Lord has called.

JOEL 4

4:1  LA: Quia ecce in diebus illis, et in tempore illo, cum convertero captivitatem Juda et Jerusalem,
     EN: For see, in those days and at that time, when I reverse the captivity of Judah and Jerusalem,

4:2  LA: congregabo omnes gentes, et deducam eas in vallem Josaphat ; et disceptabo cum eis ibi super populo meo, et hæreditate mea Israël, quos disperserunt in nationibus, et terram meam diviserunt.
     EN: I will gather all nations and bring them down to the valley of Jehoshaphat. There I will plead My case with them over My people and My inheritance Israel, whom they scattered among the nations, and over My land, which they divided.

4:3  LA: Et super populum meum miserunt sortem ; et posuerunt puerum in prostibulo, et puellam vendiderunt pro vino ut biberent.
     EN: They cast lots over My people; they placed a boy in a brothel and sold a girl for wine to drink.

4:4  LA: Verum quid mihi et vobis, Tyrus et Sidon, et omnis terminus Palæstinorum ? numquid ultionem vos reddetis mihi ? et si ulciscimini vos contra me, cito velociter reddam vicissitudinem vobis super caput vestrum.
     EN: Indeed, what have you to do with Me, Tyre and Sidon and all the territory of the Philistines? Will you repay Me with vengeance? If you take vengeance against Me, swiftly and speedily I will return your recompense upon your own head.

4:5  LA: Argentum enim meum et aurum tulistis, et desiderabilia mea et pulcherrima intulistis in delubra vestra.
     EN: For you took My silver and gold and brought My precious and most beautiful things into your shrines.

4:6  LA: Et filios Juda et filios Jerusalem vendidistis filiis Græcorum, ut longe faceretis eos de finibus suis.
     EN: You sold the children of Judah and the children of Jerusalem to the children of the Greeks, to remove them far from their own borders.

4:7  LA: Ecce ego suscitabo eos de loco in quo vendidistis eos, et convertam retributionem vestram in caput vestrum.
     EN: See, I will rouse them from the place to which you sold them, and I will return your repayment upon your own head.

4:8  LA: Et vendam filios vestros et filias vestras in manibus filiorum Juda, et venundabunt eos Sabæis, genti longinquæ, quia Dominus locutus est.
     EN: I will sell your sons and daughters into the hands of the children of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans, a distant nation, for the Lord has spoken.”

4:9  LA: Clamate hoc in gentibus, sanctificate bellum, suscitate robustos : accedant, ascendant omnes viri bellatores.
     EN: Proclaim this among the nations: Consecrate a war, rouse the strong; let all warriors approach and come up.

4:10  LA: Concidite aratra vestra in gladios, et ligones vestros in lanceas. Infirmus dicat : Quia fortis ego sum.
      EN: Cut your plows into swords and your hoes into spears. Let the weak say, “I am strong.”

4:11  LA: Erumpite, et venite, omnes gentes de circuitu, et congregamini ; ibi occumbere faciet Dominus robustos tuos.
      EN: Break forth and come, all surrounding nations, and gather together. There the Lord will cause your strong ones to fall.

4:12  LA: Consurgant, et ascendant gentes in vallem Josaphat, quia ibi sedebo ut judicem omnes gentes in circuitu.
      EN: “Let the nations rise and come up to the valley of Jehoshaphat, for there I will sit to judge all the surrounding nations.”

4:13  LA: Mittite falces, quoniam maturavit messis ; venite, et descendite, quia plenum est torcular, exuberant torcularia : quia multiplicata est malitia eorum.
      EN: Put in the sickles, for the harvest is ripe; come and go down, for the winepress is full, the presses overflow, for their wickedness has multiplied.

4:14  LA: Populi, populi, in valle concisionis, quia juxta est dies Domini in valle concisionis.
      EN: Peoples, peoples in the valley of cutting down! For the day of the Lord is near in the valley of cutting down.

4:15  LA: Sol et luna obtenebrati sunt, et stellæ retraxerunt splendorem suum.
      EN: The sun and moon have darkened, and the stars have withdrawn their brightness.

4:16  LA: Et Dominus de Sion rugiet, et de Jerusalem dabit vocem suam, et movebuntur cæli et terra ; et Dominus spes populi sui, et fortitudo filiorum Israël.
      EN: The Lord will roar from Zion and raise His voice from Jerusalem; heaven and earth will shake. The Lord is the hope of His people and the strength of the children of Israel.

4:17  LA: Et scietis quia ego Dominus Deus vester, habitans in Sion monte sancto meo ; et erit Jerusalem sancta, et alieni non transibunt per eam amplius.
      EN: “You will know that I am the Lord your God, dwelling on Zion, My holy mountain. Jerusalem will be holy, and strangers will no longer pass through her.

4:18  LA: Et erit in die illa : stillabunt montes dulcedinem, et colles fluent lacte, et per omnes rivos Juda ibunt aquæ ; et fons de domo Domini egredietur, et irrigabit torrentem spinarum.
      EN: On that day the mountains will drip sweetness, the hills will flow with milk, and waters will run through all Judah's streambeds. A spring will go out from the Lord's house and water the torrent of thorns.

4:19  LA: Ægyptus in desolationem erit, et Idumæa in desertum perditionis, pro eo quod inique egerint in filios Juda, et effuderint sanguinem innocentem in terra sua.
      EN: Egypt will become a desolation, and Edom a wilderness of destruction, because they acted unjustly against the children of Judah and shed innocent blood in their land.

4:20  LA: Et Judæa in æternum habitabitur, et Jerusalem in generationem et generationem.
      EN: Judea will be inhabited forever, and Jerusalem from generation to generation.

4:21  LA: Et mundabo sanguinem eorum, quem non mundaveram ; et Dominus commorabitur in Sion.]
      EN: I will cleanse their blood, which I had not cleansed, and the Lord will dwell in Zion.”
