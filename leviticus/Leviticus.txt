LEVITICUS 1

1:1  LA: Vocavit autem Moysen, et locutus est ei Dominus de tabernaculo testimonii, dicens :
     EN: The Lord called Moses and spoke to him from the tabernacle of the testimony, saying:

1:2  LA: Loquere filiis Israël, et dices ad eos : Homo, qui obtulerit ex vobis hostiam Domino de pecoribus, id est, de bobus et ovibus offerens victimas,
     EN: Speak to the children of Israel and say to them: When anyone among you brings the Lord a sacrificial offering from the livestock, offering victims from the cattle or the sheep,

1:3  LA: si holocaustum fuerit ejus oblatio, ac de armento : masculum immaculatum offeret ad ostium tabernaculi testimonii, ad placandum sibi Dominum :
     EN: if his offering is a whole burnt offering from the herd, he shall offer an unblemished male at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony, to obtain the Lord's favor.

1:4  LA: ponetque manum super caput hostiæ, et acceptabilis erit, atque in expiationem ejus proficiens.
     EN: He shall lay his hand on the head of the sacrificial animal, and it shall be accepted and serve to make expiation for him.

1:5  LA: Immolabitque vitulum coram Domino, et offerent filii Aaron sacerdotes sanguinem ejus, fundentes per altaris circuitum, quod est ante ostium tabernaculi :
     EN: He shall slaughter the calf before the Lord, and Aaron's sons, the priests, shall present its blood, pouring it around the altar that stands before the entrance of the tabernacle.

1:6  LA: detractaque pelle hostiæ, artus in frusta concident.
     EN: After removing the animal's skin, they shall cut its limbs into pieces.

1:7  LA: Et subjicient in altari ignem, strue lignorum ante composita :
     EN: They shall put fire upon the altar, having first arranged a pile of wood,

1:8  LA: et membra quæ sunt cæsa, desuper ordinantes, caput videlicet, et cuncta quæ adhærent jecori,
     EN: and arrange the cut-up parts above it, namely the head and everything adhering to the liver.

1:9  LA: intestinis et pedibus lotis aqua : adolebitque ea sacerdos super altare in holocaustum et suavem odorem Domino.
     EN: After the entrails and feet have been washed with water, the priest shall burn them on the altar as a whole burnt offering and a pleasing fragrance to the Lord.

1:10  LA: Quod si de pecoribus oblatio est, de ovibus sive de capris holocaustum, masculum absque macula offeret :
     EN: If his offering is from the flock, a whole burnt offering of sheep or goats, he shall offer an unblemished male.

1:11  LA: immolabitque ad latus altaris, quod respicit ad aquilonem, coram Domino : sanguinem vero illius fundent super altare filii Aaron per circuitum :
     EN: He shall slaughter it before the Lord at the north side of the altar, and Aaron's sons shall pour its blood all around upon the altar.

1:12  LA: dividentque membra, caput, et omnia quæ adhærent jecori, et ponent super ligna, quibus subjiciendus est ignis :
     EN: They shall divide the limbs, the head, and everything adhering to the liver, and place them on the wood beneath which the fire is to be put.

1:13  LA: intestina vero et pedes lavabunt aqua. Et oblata omnia adolebit sacerdos super altare in holocaustum et odorem suavissimum Domino.
     EN: They shall wash the entrails and feet with water. The priest shall present all of it and burn it on the altar as a whole burnt offering and a most pleasing fragrance to the Lord.

1:14  LA: Si autem de avibus, holocausti oblatio fuerit Domino, de turturibus, aut pullis columbæ,
     EN: If the whole burnt offering to the Lord is of birds, from turtledoves or young pigeons,

1:15  LA: offeret eam sacerdos ad altare : et retorto ad collum capite, ac rupto vulneris loco, decurrere faciet sanguinem super crepidinem altaris :
     EN: the priest shall bring it to the altar. He shall twist its head back at the neck, open the wound, and let the blood run down upon the edge of the altar.

1:16  LA: vesiculam vero gutturis, et plumas projiciet prope altare ad orientalem plagam, in loco in quo cineres effundi solent,
     EN: He shall throw its crop and feathers beside the altar on the east side, in the place where the ashes are usually poured out.

1:17  LA: confringetque ascellas ejus, et non secabit, neque ferro dividet eam, et adolebit super altare, lignis igne supposito. Holocaustum est et oblatio suavissimi odoris Domino.
     EN: He shall break its wings, but shall not cut it apart or divide it with a blade. He shall burn it on the altar, with fire placed beneath the wood. It is a whole burnt offering and an offering of most pleasing fragrance to the Lord.

LEVITICUS 2

2:1  LA: Anima cum obtulerit oblationem sacrificii Domino, simila erit ejus oblatio ; fundetque super eam oleum, et ponet thus,
     EN: When a person brings the Lord a sacrificial offering, his offering shall be fine flour. He shall pour oil over it and put frankincense on it,

2:2  LA: ac deferet ad filios Aaron sacerdotes : quorum unus tollet pugillum plenum similæ et olei, ac totum thus, et ponet memoriale super altare in odorem suavissimum Domino.
     EN: and bring it to Aaron's sons, the priests. One of them shall take a full handful of the flour and oil, with all the frankincense, and place the memorial portion on the altar as a most pleasing fragrance to the Lord.

2:3  LA: Quod autem reliquum fuerit de sacrificio, erit Aaron et filiorum ejus, Sanctum sanctorum de oblationibus Domini.
     EN: What remains of the sacrifice shall belong to Aaron and his sons, a most holy portion of the Lord's offerings.

2:4  LA: Cum autem obtuleris sacrificium coctum in clibano : de simila, panes scilicet absque fermento, conspersos oleo, et lagana azyma oleo lita.
     EN: When you offer a sacrifice baked in an oven, it shall be of fine flour: unleavened loaves mixed with oil and unleavened wafers spread with oil.

2:5  LA: Si oblatio tua fuerit de sartagine, similæ conspersæ oleo et absque fermento,
     EN: If your offering is prepared in a pan, from fine flour mixed with oil and without leaven,

2:6  LA: divides eam minutatim, et fundes super eam oleum.
     EN: you shall divide it into small pieces and pour oil over it.

2:7  LA: Sin autem de craticula fuerit sacrificium, æque simila oleo conspergetur :
     EN: If the sacrifice is prepared on a griddle, the fine flour shall likewise be mixed with oil.

2:8  LA: quam offerens Domino, trades manibus sacerdotis.
     EN: When you offer it to the Lord, you shall place it in the priest's hands.

2:9  LA: Qui cum obtulerit eam, tollet memoriale de sacrificio, et adolebit super altare in odorem suavitatis Domino :
     EN: When he has presented it, he shall take the memorial portion from the sacrifice and burn it on the altar as a pleasing fragrance to the Lord.

2:10  LA: quidquid autem reliquum est, erit Aaron, et filiorum ejus, Sanctum sanctorum de oblationibus Domini.
     EN: Whatever remains shall belong to Aaron and his sons, a most holy portion of the Lord's offerings.

2:11  LA: Omnis oblatio quæ offeretur Domino, absque fermento fiet, nec quidquam fermenti ac mellis adolebitur in sacrificio Domino.
     EN: Every offering presented to the Lord shall be made without leaven. No leaven or honey shall be burned in sacrifice to the Lord.

2:12  LA: Primitias tantum eorum offeretis ac munera : super altare vero non imponentur in odorem suavitatis.
     EN: You may offer them only as firstfruits and gifts; they shall not be placed upon the altar as a pleasing fragrance.

2:13  LA: Quidquid obtuleris sacrificii, sale condies, nec auferes sal fœderis Dei tui de sacrificio tuo : in omni oblatione tua offeres sal.
     EN: Whatever sacrifice you bring, you shall season with salt. You shall not withhold the salt of your God's covenant from your sacrifice; with every offering you shall offer salt.

2:14  LA: Si autem obtuleris munus primarum frugum tuarum Domino de spicis adhuc virentibus, torrebis igni, et confringes in morem farris, et sic offeres primitias tuas Domino,
     EN: If you bring the Lord a gift of the firstfruits of your crops, from ears of grain still green, you shall roast them in the fire and crush them into coarse meal, and thus offer your firstfruits to the Lord,

2:15  LA: fundens supra oleum, et thus imponens, quia oblatio Domini est :
     EN: pouring oil over them and putting frankincense on them, because it is the Lord's offering.

2:16  LA: de qua adolebit sacerdos in memoriam muneris partem farris fracti, et olei, ac totum thus.
     EN: From it the priest shall burn some of the crushed grain and oil, and all the frankincense, as a memorial of the gift.

LEVITICUS 3

3:1  LA: Quod si hostia pacificorum fuerit ejus oblatio, et de bobus voluerit offerre, marem sive feminam, immaculata offeret coram Domino.
     EN: If his offering is a peace offering, and he wishes to offer it from the cattle, whether male or female, he shall offer an unblemished animal before the Lord.

3:2  LA: Ponetque manum super caput victimæ suæ, quæ immolabitur in introitu tabernaculi testimonii, fundentque filii Aaron sacerdotes sanguinem per altaris circuitum.
     EN: He shall lay his hand on the head of his victim, which shall be slaughtered at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony, and Aaron's sons, the priests, shall pour the blood around the altar.

3:3  LA: Et offerent de hostia pacificorum in oblationem Domino, adipem qui operit vitalia, et quidquid pinguedinis est intrinsecus :
     EN: From the peace offering they shall present as an offering to the Lord the fat that covers the internal organs and all the fat within,

3:4  LA: duos renes cum adipe quo teguntur ilia, et reticulum jecoris cum renunculis.
     EN: the two kidneys with the fat that covers the flanks, and the membrane of the liver together with the kidneys.

3:5  LA: Adolebuntque ea super altare in holocaustum, lignis igne supposito, in oblationem suavissimi odoris Domino.
     EN: They shall burn these upon the altar as a whole burnt offering, with fire placed beneath the wood, as an offering of most pleasing fragrance to the Lord.

3:6  LA: Si vero de ovibus fuerit ejus oblatio et pacificorum hostia, sive masculum obtulerit, sive feminam, immaculata erunt.
     EN: If his offering is a peace offering from the sheep, whether he offers a male or a female, the animal shall be unblemished.

3:7  LA: Si agnum obtulerit coram Domino,
     EN: If he offers a lamb before the Lord,

3:8  LA: ponet manum suam super caput victimæ suæ : quæ immolabitur in vestibulo tabernaculi testimonii : fundentque filii Aaron sanguinem ejus per circuitum altaris.
     EN: he shall lay his hand upon the head of his victim, which shall be slaughtered at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony, and Aaron's sons shall pour its blood around the altar.

3:9  LA: Et offerent de pacificorum hostia sacrificium Domino : adipem et caudam totam
     EN: From the peace offering they shall bring a sacrifice to the Lord: the fat and the whole tail,

3:10  LA: cum renibus, et pinguedinem quæ operit ventrem atque universa vitalia, et utrumque renunculum cum adipe qui est juxta ilia, reticulumque jecoris cum renunculis.
     EN: together with the kidneys, the fat that covers the belly and all the internal organs, both kidneys with the fat beside the flanks, and the membrane of the liver together with the kidneys.

3:11  LA: Et adolebit ea sacerdos super altare in pabulum ignis et oblationis Domini.
     EN: The priest shall burn these upon the altar as food for the fire and for the Lord's offering.

3:12  LA: Si capra fuerit ejus oblatio, et obtulerit eam Domino,
     EN: If his offering is a goat, and he presents it to the Lord,

3:13  LA: ponet manum suam super caput ejus : immolabitque eam in introitu tabernaculi testimonii, et fundent filii Aaron sanguinem ejus per altaris circuitum.
     EN: he shall lay his hand on its head and slaughter it at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony. Aaron's sons shall pour its blood around the altar.

3:14  LA: Tollentque ex ea in pastum ignis dominici, adipem qui operit ventrem, et qui tegit universa vitalia :
     EN: They shall take from it, as food for the Lord's fire, the fat that covers the belly and all the internal organs,

3:15  LA: duos renunculos cum reticulo, quod est super eos juxta ilia, et arvinam jecoris cum renunculis :
     EN: the two kidneys with the membrane over them beside the flanks, and the fat of the liver together with the kidneys.

3:16  LA: adolebitque ea super altare sacerdos in alimoniam ignis, et suavissimi odoris. Omnis adeps, Domini erit
     EN: The priest shall burn these upon the altar as food for the fire and as a most pleasing fragrance. All fat shall belong to the Lord,

3:17  LA: jure perpetuo in generationibus, et cunctis habitaculis vestris : nec sanguinem nec adipem omnino comedetis.
     EN: by a perpetual ordinance throughout your generations and in all your dwellings. You shall eat neither blood nor any fat.

LEVITICUS 4

4:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

4:2  LA: Loquere filiis Israël : Anima, quæ peccaverit per ignorantiam, et de universis mandatis Domini, quæ præcepit ut non fierent, quippiam fecerit :
     EN: Speak to the children of Israel: When a person sins through ignorance and does any of the things that the Lord's commandments forbid,

4:3  LA: si sacerdos, qui unctus est, peccaverit, delinquere faciens populum, offeret pro peccato suo vitulum immaculatum Domino :
     EN: if the anointed priest sins, causing the people to transgress, he shall offer an unblemished calf to the Lord for his sin.

4:4  LA: et adducet illum ad ostium tabernaculi testimonii coram Domino, ponetque manum super caput ejus, et immolabit eum Domino.
     EN: He shall bring it before the Lord to the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony, lay his hand upon its head, and slaughter it to the Lord.

4:5  LA: Hauriet quoque de sanguine vituli, inferens illum in tabernaculum testimonii.
     EN: He shall also take some of the calf's blood and bring it into the tabernacle of the testimony.

4:6  LA: Cumque intinxerit digitum in sanguine, asperget eo septies coram Domino contra velum sanctuarii.
     EN: Dipping his finger in the blood, he shall sprinkle it seven times before the Lord, toward the veil of the sanctuary.

4:7  LA: Ponetque de eodem sanguine super cornua altaris thymiamatis gratissimi Domino, quod est in tabernaculo testimonii : omnem autem reliquum sanguinem fundet in basim altaris holocausti in introitu tabernaculi.
     EN: He shall put some of the same blood on the horns of the altar of incense most pleasing to the Lord, which is in the tabernacle of the testimony. All the remaining blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of whole burnt offering, at the entrance of the tabernacle.

4:8  LA: Et adipem vituli auferet pro peccato, tam eum qui vitalia operit quam omnia quæ intrinsecus sunt :
     EN: He shall remove the fat of the calf offered for sin, both the fat covering the internal organs and all that is within,

4:9  LA: duos renunculos et reticulum quod est super eos juxta ilia, et adipem jecoris cum renunculis,
     EN: the two kidneys and the membrane over them beside the flanks, and the fat of the liver together with the kidneys,

4:10  LA: sicut aufertur de vitulo hostiæ pacificorum : et adolebit ea super altare holocausti.
     EN: as it is removed from the calf of the peace offering; and he shall burn these upon the altar of whole burnt offering.

4:11  LA: Pellem vero et omnes carnes, cum capite et pedibus et intestinis et fimo,
     EN: But the skin and all the flesh, with the head, feet, entrails, and dung,

4:12  LA: et reliquo corpore, efferet extra castra in locum mundum, ubi cineres effundi solent : incendetque ea super lignorum struem, quæ in loco effusorum cinerum cremabuntur.
     EN: and the rest of the body, he shall carry outside the camp to a clean place where the ashes are usually poured out. He shall set them on fire upon a pile of wood; they shall be burned in the place where the ashes are poured out.

4:13  LA: Quod si omnis turba Israël ignoraverit, et per imperitiam fecerit quod contra mandatum Domini est,
     EN: If the whole congregation of Israel has acted in ignorance and through lack of understanding has done what is contrary to the Lord's command,

4:14  LA: et postea intellexerit peccatum suum, offeret pro peccato suo vitulum, adducetque eum ad ostium tabernaculi.
     EN: and afterward recognizes its sin, it shall offer a calf for its sin and bring it to the entrance of the tabernacle.

4:15  LA: Et ponent seniores populi manus super caput ejus coram Domino. Immolatoque vitulo in conspectu Domini,
     EN: The elders of the people shall lay their hands upon its head before the Lord. When the calf has been slaughtered in the Lord's sight,

4:16  LA: inferet sacerdos, qui unctus est, de sanguine ejus in tabernaculum testimonii,
     EN: the anointed priest shall bring some of its blood into the tabernacle of the testimony,

4:17  LA: tincto digito aspergens septies contra velum.
     EN: and, dipping his finger in it, sprinkle it seven times toward the veil.

4:18  LA: Ponetque de eodem sanguine in cornibus altaris, quod est coram Domino in tabernaculo testimonii : reliquum autem sanguinem fundet juxta basim altaris holocaustorum, quod est in ostio tabernaculi testimonii.
     EN: He shall put some of the same blood on the horns of the altar that is before the Lord in the tabernacle of the testimony. The remaining blood he shall pour out beside the base of the altar of whole burnt offerings, which is at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony.

4:19  LA: Omnemque ejus adipem tollet, et adolebit super altare :
     EN: He shall remove all its fat and burn it upon the altar,

4:20  LA: sic faciens et de hoc vitulo quomodo fecit et prius : et rogante pro eis sacerdote, propitius erit eis Dominus.
     EN: doing with this calf as he did with the first. When the priest intercedes for them, the Lord will be gracious to them.

4:21  LA: Ipsum autem vitulum efferet extra castra, atque comburet sicut et priorem vitulum : quia est pro peccato multitudinis.
     EN: But the calf itself he shall carry outside the camp and burn, as he burned the first calf, because it is for the sin of the congregation.

4:22  LA: Si peccaverit princeps, et fecerit unum e pluribus per ignorantiam, quod Domini lege prohibetur :
     EN: If a ruler sins and through ignorance does any of the many things forbidden by the Lord's law,

4:23  LA: et postea intellexerit peccatum suum, offeret hostiam Domino, hircum de capris immaculatum.
     EN: and afterward recognizes his sin, he shall offer the Lord an unblemished male goat as a sacrificial victim.

4:24  LA: Ponetque manum suam super caput ejus : cumque immolaverit eum loco ubi solet mactari holocaustum coram Domino, quia pro peccato est,
     EN: He shall lay his hand upon its head. When he has slaughtered it before the Lord in the place where whole burnt offerings are usually slaughtered, because it is an offering for sin,

4:25  LA: tinget sacerdos digitum in sanguine hostiæ pro peccato, tangens cornua altaris holocausti, et reliquum fundens ad basim ejus.
     EN: the priest shall dip his finger in the blood of the sin offering, touch the horns of the altar of whole burnt offering, and pour the rest at its base.

4:26  LA: Adipem vero adolebit supra, sicut in victimis pacificorum fieri solet : rogabitque pro eo sacerdos, et pro peccato ejus, et dimittetur ei.
     EN: He shall burn the fat upon it, as is usually done with peace offerings. The priest shall intercede for him and for his sin, and he will be forgiven.

4:27  LA: Quod si peccaverit anima per ignorantiam, de populo terræ, ut faciat quidquam de his, quæ Domini lege prohibentur, atque delinquat,
     EN: If a person from among the people of the land sins through ignorance, doing anything forbidden by the Lord's law and thus transgressing,

4:28  LA: et cognoverit peccatum suum, offeret capram immaculatam.
     EN: and becomes aware of his sin, he shall offer an unblemished female goat.

4:29  LA: Ponetque manum super caput hostiæ quæ pro peccato est, et immolabit eam in loco holocausti.
     EN: He shall lay his hand upon the head of the sin offering and slaughter it in the place of whole burnt offering.

4:30  LA: Tolletque sacerdos de sanguine in digito suo : et tangens cornua altaris holocausti, reliquum fundet ad basim ejus.
     EN: The priest shall take some of the blood on his finger, touch the horns of the altar of whole burnt offering, and pour the rest at its base.

4:31  LA: Omnem autem adipem auferens, sicut auferri solet de victimis pacificorum, adolebit super altare in odorem suavitatis Domino : rogabitque pro eo, et dimittetur ei.
     EN: Removing all the fat, as it is usually removed from peace offerings, he shall burn it upon the altar as a pleasing fragrance to the Lord. He shall intercede for the person, and he will be forgiven.

4:32  LA: Sin autem de pecoribus obtulerit victimam pro peccato, ovem scilicet immaculatam :
     EN: If instead he offers a victim from the flock for sin, namely an unblemished ewe,

4:33  LA: ponet manum super caput ejus, et immolabit eam in loco ubi solent cædi holocaustorum hostiæ.
     EN: he shall lay his hand upon its head and slaughter it in the place where victims for whole burnt offerings are usually slaughtered.

4:34  LA: Sumetque sacerdos de sanguine ejus digito suo, et tangens cornua altaris holocausti, reliquum fundet ad basim ejus.
     EN: The priest shall take some of its blood on his finger, touch the horns of the altar of whole burnt offering, and pour the rest at its base.

4:35  LA: Omnem quoque adipem auferens, sicut auferri solet adeps arietis, qui immolatur pro pacificis, cremabit super altare in incensum Domini : rogabitque pro eo, et pro peccato ejus, et dimittetur ei.
     EN: Removing all the fat, as the fat is usually removed from a ram sacrificed as a peace offering, he shall burn it upon the altar as an offering by fire to the Lord. He shall intercede for the person and for his sin, and he will be forgiven.

LEVITICUS 5

5:1  LA: Si peccaverit anima, et audierit vocem jurantis, testisque fuerit quod aut ipse vidit, aut conscius est : nisi indicaverit, portabit iniquitatem suam.
     EN: If a person sins after hearing an oath pronounced, being a witness to something he himself has seen or knows about, and does not disclose it, he shall bear his iniquity.

5:2  LA: Anima quæ tetigerit aliquid immundum, sive quod occisum a bestia est, aut per se mortuum, aut quodlibet aliud reptile : et oblita fuerit immunditiæ suæ, rea est, et deliquit :
     EN: A person who touches anything unclean, whether something killed by a beast, something that has died of itself, or any other creeping creature, and forgets his uncleanness, is guilty and has transgressed.

5:3  LA: et si tetigerit quidquam de immunditia hominis juxta omnem impuritatem, qua pollui solet, oblitaque cognoverit postea, subjacebit delicto.
     EN: If he touches any human uncleanness, whatever the impurity by which a person is usually defiled, and forgets it but afterward becomes aware of it, he shall be liable for the offense.

5:4  LA: Anima, quæ juraverit, et protulerit labiis suis, ut vel male quid faceret, vel bene, et idipsum juramento et sermone firmaverit, oblitaque postea intellexerit delictum suum,
     EN: If a person swears and declares with his lips that he will do something, whether evil or good, confirming it by an oath and a spoken pledge, and forgets it but afterward recognizes his offense,

5:5  LA: agat pœnitentiam pro peccato,
     EN: he shall repent of the sin,

5:6  LA: et offerat de gregibus agnam sive capram, orabitque pro ea sacerdos et pro peccato ejus.
     EN: and offer a ewe lamb or a female goat from the flocks. The priest shall pray for the person and for his sin.

5:7  LA: Sin autem non potuerit offerre pecus, offerat duos turtures, vel duos pullos columbarum Domino, unum pro peccato, et alterum in holocaustum,
     EN: If he cannot offer an animal from the flock, he shall offer the Lord two turtledoves or two young pigeons, one for sin and the other as a whole burnt offering.

5:8  LA: dabitque eos sacerdoti : qui primum offerens pro peccato, retorquebit caput ejus ad pennulas, ita ut collo hæreat, et non penitus abrumpatur.
     EN: He shall give them to the priest, who shall first offer the one for sin, twisting its head back toward its little wings so that it remains attached to the neck and is not completely torn off.

5:9  LA: Et asperget de sanguine ejus parietem altaris ; quidquid autem reliquum fuerit, faciet distillare ad fundamentum ejus, quia pro peccato est.
     EN: He shall sprinkle some of its blood against the side of the altar, and let whatever remains drip down to its base, because it is for sin.

5:10  LA: Alterum vero adolebit in holocaustum, ut fieri solet : rogabitque pro eo sacerdos et pro peccato ejus, et dimittetur ei.
     EN: He shall burn the other as a whole burnt offering in the customary way. The priest shall intercede for the person and for his sin, and he will be forgiven.

5:11  LA: Quod si non quiverit manus ejus duos offerre turtures, aut duos pullos columbarum, offeret pro peccato suo similæ partem ephi decimam : non mittet in eam oleum, nec thuris aliquid imponet, quia pro peccato est.
     EN: If his means do not allow him to offer two turtledoves or two young pigeons, he shall offer a tenth of an ephah of fine flour for his sin. He shall not put oil in it or place any frankincense upon it, because it is for sin.

5:12  LA: Tradetque eam sacerdoti : qui plenum ex ea pugillum hauriens, cremabit super altare in monimentum ejus qui obtulerit,
     EN: He shall hand it to the priest, who shall take a full handful of it and burn it upon the altar as a memorial of the one who offered it,

5:13  LA: rogans pro illo et expians : reliquam vero partem ipse habebit in munere.
     EN: interceding for him and making expiation. The priest himself shall receive the remaining portion as a gift.

5:14  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

5:15  LA: Anima si prævaricans cæremonias, per errorem, in his quæ Domino sunt sanctificata, peccaverit, offeret pro delicto suo arietem immaculatum de gregibus, qui emi potest duobus siclis, juxta pondus sanctuarii :

     EN: The priest shall intercede for him before the Lord, and he will be forgiven for each of the deeds by which he sinned.

     EN: This is the offering that Aaron and his sons must bring to the Lord on the day of their anointing. They shall offer a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a perpetual sacrifice, half of it in the morning and half in the evening.

6:14 (Vg 6:21)  LA: quæ in sartagine oleo conspersa frigetur. Offeret autem eam calidam in odorem suavissimum Domino

     EN: by the priest who succeeds his father by right, and all of it shall be burned upon the altar.

6:16 (Vg 6:23)  LA: Omne enim sacrificium sacerdotum igne consumetur, nec quisquam comedet ex eo.
     EN: For every sacrifice of the priests shall be consumed by fire, and no one shall eat any of it.

     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

     EN: Speak to Aaron and his sons: This is the law of the sin offering. It shall be slaughtered before the Lord in the place where the whole burnt offering is presented. It is most holy.

     EN: The priest who offers it shall eat it in a holy place, in the court of the tabernacle.

     EN: Whatever touches its flesh shall be made holy. If a garment is sprinkled with its blood, it shall be washed in a holy place.

     EN: An earthenware vessel in which it has been cooked shall be broken. If it is a bronze vessel, it shall be scoured and washed with water.

6:22 (Vg 6:29)  LA: Omnis masculus de genere sacerdotali vescetur de carnibus ejus, quia Sanctum sanctorum est.
     EN: Every male of the priestly line may eat its flesh, because it is most holy.

     EN: But a victim slaughtered for sin, whose blood is brought into the tabernacle of the testimony to make expiation in the sanctuary, shall not be eaten; it shall be burned with fire.

LEVITICUS 7

7:1  LA: Hæc quoque lex hostiæ pro delicto, Sancta sanctorum est :
     EN: This also is the law of the guilt offering: it is most holy.

7:2  LA: idcirco ubi immolabitur holocaustum, mactabitur et victima pro delicto : sanguis ejus per gyrum altaris fundetur.
     EN: Therefore, in the place where the whole burnt offering is slaughtered, the victim for guilt shall also be slaughtered. Its blood shall be poured around the altar.

7:3  LA: Offerent ex ea caudam et adipem qui operit vitalia :
     EN: From it they shall offer the tail and the fat covering the internal organs,

7:4  LA: duos renunculos, et pinguedinem quæ juxta ilia est, reticulumque jecoris cum renunculis.
     EN: the two kidneys, the fat beside the flanks, and the membrane of the liver together with the kidneys.

7:5  LA: Et adolebit ea sacerdos super altare : incensum est Domini pro delicto.
     EN: The priest shall burn these upon the altar. It is the Lord's offering by fire for guilt.

7:6  LA: Omnis masculus de sacerdotali genere, in loco sancto vescetur his carnibus, quia Sanctum sanctorum est.
     EN: Every male of the priestly line may eat this meat in a holy place, because it is most holy.

7:7  LA: Sicut pro peccato offertur hostia, ita et pro delicto : utriusque hostiæ lex una erit : ad sacerdotem, qui eam obtulerit, pertinebit.
     EN: As a victim is offered for sin, so also for guilt. The same law shall apply to both offerings: it shall belong to the priest who offers it.

7:8  LA: Sacerdos qui offert holocausti victimam, habebit pellem ejus.
     EN: The priest who offers the victim for a whole burnt offering shall have its skin.

7:9  LA: Et omne sacrificium similæ, quod coquitur in clibano, et quidquid in craticula, vel in sartagine præparatur, ejus erit sacerdotis a quo offertur :
     EN: Every sacrifice of fine flour baked in an oven, and whatever is prepared on a griddle or in a pan, shall belong to the priest who offers it.

7:10  LA: sive oleo conspersa, sive arida fuerint, cunctis filiis Aaron mensura æqua per singulos dividetur.
     EN: Whether mixed with oil or dry, it shall be divided among all Aaron's sons, an equal portion to each.

7:11  LA: Hæc est lex hostiæ pacificorum quæ offertur Domino.
     EN: This is the law of the peace offering presented to the Lord.

7:12  LA: Si pro gratiarum actione oblatio fuerit, offerent panes absque fermento conspersos oleo, et lagana azyma uncta oleo, coctamque similam, et collyridas olei admistione conspersas :
     EN: If the offering is for thanksgiving, they shall offer unleavened loaves mixed with oil, unleavened wafers spread with oil, cooked fine flour, and cakes mixed with oil,

7:13  LA: panes quoque fermentatos cum hostia gratiarum, quæ immolatur pro pacificis :
     EN: as well as leavened loaves, with the thanksgiving victim sacrificed as a peace offering.

7:14  LA: ex quibus unus pro primitiis offeretur Domino, et erit sacerdotis qui fundet hostiæ sanguinem,
     EN: One of these shall be offered to the Lord as a firstfruits offering, and it shall belong to the priest who pours out the victim's blood.

7:15  LA: cujus carnes eadem comedentur die, nec remanebit ex eis quidquam usque mane.
     EN: Its meat shall be eaten that same day; none of it shall remain until morning.

7:16  LA: Si voto, vel sponte quispiam obtulerit hostiam, eadem similiter edetur die : sed et si quid in crastinum remanserit, vesci licitum est :
     EN: If someone offers a victim in fulfillment of a vow or of his own free will, it shall likewise be eaten that same day; but if any remains for the next day, it may be eaten.

7:17  LA: quidquid autem tertius invenerit dies, ignis absumet.
     EN: Whatever the third day finds remaining, fire shall consume.

7:18  LA: Si quis de carnibus victimæ pacificorum die tertio comederit, irrita fiet oblatio, nec proderit offerenti : quin potius quæcumque anima tali se edulio contaminaverit, prævaricationis rea erit.
     EN: If anyone eats the meat of a peace offering on the third day, the offering shall become invalid and shall not benefit the one who offers it. Indeed, any person who defiles himself with such food shall be guilty of transgression.

7:19  LA: Caro, quæ aliquid tetigerit immundum, non comedetur, sed comburetur igni : qui fuerit mundus, vescetur ex ea.
     EN: Meat that has touched anything unclean shall not be eaten but shall be burned with fire. Anyone who is clean may eat the meat.

7:20  LA: Anima polluta quæ ederit de carnibus hostiæ pacificorum, quæ oblata est Domino, peribit de populis suis.
     EN: A defiled person who eats meat from the peace offering presented to the Lord shall perish from among his people.

7:21  LA: Et quæ tetigerit immunditiam hominis, vel jumenti, sive omnis rei quæ polluere potest, et comederit de hujuscemodi carnibus, interibit de populis suis.
     EN: A person who touches human uncleanness, the uncleanness of an animal, or anything capable of defiling, and then eats such meat, shall perish from among his people.

7:22  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

7:23  LA: Loquere filiis Israël : Adipem ovis, et bovis, et capræ non comedetis.
     EN: Speak to the children of Israel: You shall not eat the fat of sheep, cattle, or goats.

7:24  LA: Adipem cadaveris morticini, et ejus animalis, quod a bestia captum est, habebitis in varios usus.
     EN: You may use for various purposes the fat of an animal that has died of itself or has been taken by a wild beast.

7:25  LA: Si quis adipem, qui offerri debet in incensum Domini, comederit, peribit de populo suo.
     EN: If anyone eats fat that ought to be offered by fire to the Lord, he shall perish from among his people.

7:26  LA: Sanguinem quoque omnis animalis non sumetis in cibo, tam de avibus quam de pecoribus.
     EN: Nor shall you take the blood of any animal as food, whether of birds or livestock.

7:27  LA: Omnis anima, quæ ederit sanguinem, peribit de populis suis.
     EN: Every person who eats blood shall perish from among his people.

7:28  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

7:29  LA: Loquere filiis Israël, dicens : Qui offert victimam pacificorum Domino, offerat simul et sacrificium, id est, libamenta ejus.
     EN: Speak to the children of Israel and say: Whoever offers the Lord a peace offering shall bring with it its sacrifice, that is, its accompanying offerings.

7:30  LA: Tenebit manibus adipem hostiæ, et pectusculum : cumque ambo oblata Domino consecraverit, tradet sacerdoti,
     EN: He shall hold the victim's fat and breast in his hands. When he has consecrated both by presenting them to the Lord, he shall hand them to the priest,

7:31  LA: qui adolebit adipem super altare, pectusculum autem erit Aaron et filiorum ejus.
     EN: who shall burn the fat upon the altar; the breast shall belong to Aaron and his sons.

7:32  LA: Armus quoque dexter de pacificorum hostiis cedet in primitias sacerdotis.
     EN: The right shoulder of the peace offerings shall also fall to the priest as a firstfruits portion.

7:33  LA: Qui obtulerit sanguinem et adipem filiorum Aaron, ipse habebit et armum dextrum in portione sua.
     EN: The one among Aaron's sons who presents the blood and fat shall himself receive the right shoulder as his portion.

7:34  LA: Pectusculum enim elevationis, et armum separationis, tuli a filiis Israël de hostiis eorum pacificis, et dedi Aaron sacerdoti, et filiis ejus, lege perpetua, ab omni populo Israël.
     EN: For I have taken from the children of Israel the breast that is lifted up and the shoulder that is set apart from their peace offerings, and have given them to Aaron the priest and his sons, by a perpetual law binding on all the people of Israel.

7:35  LA: Hæc est unctio Aaron et filiorum ejus in cæremoniis Domini die qua obtulit eos Moyses, ut sacerdotio fungerentur,
     EN: This is the anointing of Aaron and his sons in the Lord's sacred rites, on the day Moses presented them to exercise the priesthood.

7:36  LA: et quæ præcepit eis dari Dominus a filiis Israël religione perpetua in generationibus suis.
     EN: The Lord commanded the children of Israel to give it to them as a perpetual religious obligation throughout their generations.

7:37  LA: Ista est lex holocausti, et sacrificii pro peccato atque delicto, et pro consecratione et pacificorum victimis,
     EN: This is the law of the whole burnt offering, of sacrifice for sin and guilt, of consecration, and of peace offerings,

7:38  LA: quam constituit Dominus Moysi in monte Sinai, quando mandabit filiis Israël ut offerrent oblationes suas Domino in deserto Sinai.
     EN: which the Lord established for Moses on Mount Sinai, when He commanded the children of Israel to bring their offerings to the Lord in the wilderness of Sinai.

LEVITICUS 8

8:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

8:14  LA: Obtulit et vitulum pro peccato : cumque super caput ejus posuisset Aaron et filii ejus manus suas,

     EN: he slaughtered it, took some of the blood, and, dipping his finger in it, touched the horns all around the altar. After purifying and sanctifying it, he poured the remaining blood at its base.

8:16  LA: Adipem vero qui erat super vitalia, et reticulum jecoris, duosque renunculos, cum arvinulis suis, adolevit super altare :
     EN: He burned upon the altar the fat covering the internal organs, the membrane of the liver, and the two kidneys with their fat.

8:17  LA: vitulum cum pelle, et carnibus, et fimo, cremans extra castra, sicut præceperat Dominus.
     EN: The calf, with its skin, flesh, and dung, he burned outside the camp, as the Lord had commanded.

8:18  LA: Obtulit et arietem in holocaustum : super cujus caput cum imposuissent Aaron et filii ejus manus suas,
     EN: He also presented the ram as a whole burnt offering. When Aaron and his sons had laid their hands upon its head,

8:19  LA: immolavit eum, et fudit sanguinem ejus per circuitum altaris.
     EN: he slaughtered it and poured its blood around the altar.

8:20  LA: Ipsumque arietem in frusta concidens, caput ejus, et artus, et adipem adolevit igni,
     EN: Cutting the ram itself into pieces, he burned its head, limbs, and fat in the fire,

     EN: after first washing the entrails and feet. He burned the entire ram together upon the altar, because it was a whole burnt offering of most pleasing fragrance to the Lord, as He had commanded him.

8:22  LA: Obtulit et arietem secundum in consecratione sacerdotum, posueruntque super caput ejus Aaron et filii ejus manus suas :
     EN: He also presented the second ram for the consecration of the priests, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands upon its head.

8:24  LA: Obtulit et filios Aaron : cumque de sanguine arietis immolati tetigisset extremum auriculæ singulorum dextræ, et pollices manus ac pedis dextri, reliquum fudit super altare per circuitum :
     EN: He also brought Aaron's sons forward. After touching the tip of each one's right ear, the right thumb, and the right great toe with the blood of the slaughtered ram, he poured the rest all around upon the altar.

8:25  LA: adipem vero, et caudam, omnemque pinguedinem quæ operit intestina, reticulumque jecoris, et duos renes cum adipibus suis et armo dextro separavit.
     EN: He set apart the fat, the tail, all the fat covering the entrails, the membrane of the liver, the two kidneys with their fat, and the right shoulder.

8:26  LA: Tollens autem de canistro azymorum, quod erat coram Domino, panem absque fermento, et collyridam conspersam oleo, laganumque, posuit super adipes, et armum dextrum,

8:27  LA: tradens simul omnia Aaron et filiis ejus. Qui postquam levaverunt ea coram Domino,
     EN: He handed all of these together to Aaron and his sons. After they had lifted them up before the Lord,

8:28  LA: rursum suscepta de manibus eorum, adolevit super altare holocausti, eo quod consecrationis esset oblatio, in odorem suavitatis, sacrificii Domino.
     EN: he received them again from their hands and burned them upon the altar of whole burnt offering, because it was an offering of consecration, a sacrifice of pleasing fragrance to the Lord.

8:29  LA: Tulitque pectusculum, elevans illud coram Domino, de ariete consecrationis in partem suam, sicut præceperat ei Dominus.
     EN: He took the breast of the ram of consecration and lifted it up before the Lord as his own portion, as the Lord had commanded him.

8:30  LA: Assumensque unguentum, et sanguinem qui erat in altari, aspersit super Aaron et vestimenta ejus, et super filios illius ac vestes eorum.
     EN: Taking the anointing oil and the blood that was upon the altar, he sprinkled Aaron and his garments, and his sons and their garments.

8:31  LA: Cumque sanctificasset eos in vestitu suo, præcepit eis, dicens : Coquite carnes ante fores tabernaculi, et ibi comedite eas ; panes quoque consecrationis edite, qui positi sunt in canistro, sicut præcepit mihi Dominus, dicens : Aaron et filii ejus comedent eos :
     EN: When he had sanctified them in their clothing, he commanded them, saying: Cook the meat before the entrance of the tabernacle and eat it there. Eat also the bread of consecration placed in the basket, as the Lord commanded me, saying: Aaron and his sons shall eat it.

8:32  LA: quidquid autem reliquum fuerit de carne et panibus, ignis absumet.
     EN: Whatever remains of the meat and bread, fire shall consume.

8:33  LA: De ostio quoque tabernaculi non exibitis septem diebus, usque ad diem quo complebitur tempus consecrationis vestræ ; septem enim diebus finitur consecratio :
     EN: You shall not go out from the entrance of the tabernacle for seven days, until the day when the time of your consecration is completed; for the consecration is completed in seven days,

8:34  LA: sicut et impræsentiarum factum est, ut ritus sacrificii compleretur.
     EN: as has been done at this time, so that the rite of sacrifice might be fulfilled.

8:35  LA: Die ac nocte manebitis in tabernaculo observantes custodias Domini, ne moriamini : sic enim mihi præceptum est.
     EN: Day and night you shall remain in the tabernacle, keeping the Lord's charge, so that you do not die; for this is what I have been commanded.

8:36  LA: Feceruntque Aaron et filii ejus cuncta quæ locutus est Dominus per manum Moysi.
     EN: Aaron and his sons did everything the Lord had spoken through Moses.

9:8  LA: Statimque Aaron accedens ad altare, immolavit vitulum pro peccato suo :

9:9  LA: cujus sanguinem obtulerunt ei filii sui : in quo tingens digitum, tetigit cornua altaris, et fudit residuum ad basim ejus.

9:10  LA: Adipemque, et renunculos, ac reticulum jecoris, quæ sunt pro peccato, adolevit super altare, sicut præceperat Dominus Moysi :

9:11  LA: carnes vero et pellem ejus extra castra combussit igni.

9:12  LA: Immolavit et holocausti victimam : obtuleruntque ei filii sui sanguinem ejus, quem fudit per altaris circuitum.

9:13  LA: Ipsam etiam hostiam in frusta concisam, cum capite et membris singulis obtulerunt ; quæ omnia super altare cremavit igni,

9:15  LA: Et pro peccato populi offerens, mactavit hircum : expiatoque altari,

     EN: he made the whole burnt offering,

9:17  LA: addens in sacrificio libamenta, quæ pariter offeruntur, et adolens ea super altare, absque cæremoniis holocausti matutini.

9:18  LA: Immolavit et bovem atque arietem, hostias pacificas populi : obtuleruntque ei filii sui sanguinem, quem fudit super altare in circuitum.
     EN: He also slaughtered the ox and the ram, the people's peace offerings. His sons presented the blood to him, and he poured it all around upon the altar.

9:19  LA: Adipem autem bovis, et caudam arietis, renunculosque cum adipibus suis, et reticulum jecoris,

     EN: And behold, fire came forth from the Lord and consumed the whole burnt offering and the fat upon the altar. When the crowds saw this, they praised the Lord and fell upon their faces.

LEVITICUS 10

     EN: Aaron's sons Nadab and Abihu took their censers, put fire in them, and placed incense upon it, offering unauthorized fire before the Lord, which had not been commanded them.

     EN: Fire came forth from the Lord and consumed them, and they died before the Lord.

     EN: Moses said to Aaron: This is what the Lord has spoken: I will be shown holy in those who approach Me, and I will be glorified in the sight of all the people. When Aaron heard this, he remained silent.

10:4  LA: Vocatis autem Moyses Misaële et Elisaphan filiis Oziel, patrui Aaron, ait ad eos : Ite, et tollite fratres vestros de conspectu sanctuarii, et asportate extra castra.
     EN: Moses called Mishael and Elzaphan, sons of Uzziel, Aaron's uncle, and said to them: Go and take your kinsmen away from before the sanctuary, and carry them outside the camp.

     EN: They went immediately and carried them away just as they lay, clothed in linen tunics, and took them outside, as they had been commanded.

10:6  LA: Locutusque est Moyses ad Aaron, et ad Eleazar, et Ithamar, filios ejus : Capita vestra nolite nudare, et vestimenta nolite scindere, ne forte moriamini, et super omnem cœtum oriatur indignatio. Fratres vestri, et omnis domus Israël, plangant incendium quod Dominus suscitavit :
     EN: Moses said to Aaron and to his sons Eleazar and Ithamar: Do not uncover your heads or tear your garments, lest you die and wrath arise against the whole assembly. Let your kinsmen and the whole house of Israel mourn the burning that the Lord has brought about.

     EN: But you shall not go out through the entrance of the tabernacle, or you will perish, for the holy anointing oil is upon you. They did everything according to Moses' command.

10:8  LA: Dixit quoque Dominus ad Aaron :
     EN: The Lord also said to Aaron:

10:9  LA: Vinum, et omne quod inebriare potest, non bibetis tu et filii tui, quando intratis in tabernaculum testimonii, ne moriamini : quia præceptum sempiternum est in generationes vestras :
     EN: You and your sons shall drink neither wine nor anything that can intoxicate when you enter the tabernacle of the testimony, so that you do not die. This is a perpetual command throughout your generations,

10:10  LA: et ut habeatis scientiam discernendi inter sanctum et profanum, inter pollutum et mundum ;
     EN: so that you may know how to distinguish between the holy and the profane, between the defiled and the clean,

10:11  LA: doceatisque filios Israël omnia legitima mea quæ locutus est Dominus ad eos per manum Moysi.
     EN: and teach the children of Israel all My ordinances, which the Lord has spoken to them through Moses.

10:12  LA: Locutusque est Moyses ad Aaron, et ad Eleazar, et Ithamar, filios ejus, qui erant residui : Tollite sacrificium, quod remansit de oblatione Domini, et comedite illud absque fermento juxta altare, quia Sanctum sanctorum est.
     EN: Moses said to Aaron and to his remaining sons, Eleazar and Ithamar: Take the sacrifice left over from the Lord's offering and eat it without leaven beside the altar, because it is most holy.

10:13  LA: Comedetis autem in loco sancto : quod datum est tibi et filiis tuis de oblationibus Domini, sicut præceptum est mihi.
     EN: You shall eat it in a holy place, for it has been given to you and your sons from the Lord's offerings, as I have been commanded.

10:14  LA: Pectusculum quoque quod oblatum est, et armum qui separatus est, edetis in loco mundissimo tu et filii tui, et filiæ tuæ tecum : tibi enim ac liberis tuis reposita sunt de hostiis salutaribus filiorum Israël :
     EN: The breast that has been offered and the shoulder that has been set apart you shall eat in a completely clean place, you and your sons and daughters with you; for they have been reserved for you and your children from the peace offerings of the children of Israel,

10:15  LA: eo quod armum et pectus, et adipes qui cremantur in altari, elevaverunt coram Domino, et pertineant ad te, et ad filios tuos, lege perpetua, sicut præcepit Dominus.
     EN: because they have lifted up the shoulder and the breast, with the fat burned upon the altar, before the Lord. They belong to you and your sons by a perpetual law, as the Lord has commanded.

10:16  LA: Inter hæc, hircum, qui oblatus fuerat pro peccato, cum quæreret Moyses, exustum reperit : iratusque contra Eleazar et Ithamar filios Aaron, qui remanserant, ait :
     EN: Meanwhile, Moses searched for the male goat that had been offered for sin and found that it had been burned. Angry with Aaron's remaining sons, Eleazar and Ithamar, he said:

10:17  LA: Cur non comedistis hostiam pro peccato in loco sancto, quæ Sancta sanctorum est, et data vobis ut portetis iniquitatem multitudinis, et rogetis pro ea in conspectu Domini,
     EN: Why did you not eat the sin offering in a holy place? It is most holy and was given to you so that you might bear the congregation's iniquity and intercede for them in the Lord's sight,

10:18  LA: præsertim cum de sanguine illius non sit illatum intra sancta, et comedere debueritis eam in Sanctuario, sicut præceptum est mihi ?
     EN: especially since none of its blood was brought into the holy place, and you ought to have eaten it in the sanctuary, as I was commanded.

10:19  LA: Respondit Aaron : Oblata est hodie victima pro peccato, et holocaustum coram Domino : mihi autem accidit quod vides ; quomodo potui comedere eam, aut placere Domino in cæremoniis mente lugubri ?
     EN: Aaron answered: Today the sin offering and the whole burnt offering have been presented before the Lord, but what you see has happened to me. How could I have eaten it or pleased the Lord in the sacred rites with a grieving heart?

10:20  LA: Quod cum audisset Moyses, recepit satisfactionem.
     EN: When Moses heard this, he accepted the explanation.

LEVITICUS 11

11:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen et Aaron, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:

11:2  LA: Dicite filiis Israël : Hæc sunt animalia quæ comedere debetis de cunctis animantibus terræ :
     EN: Tell the children of Israel: These are the animals you may eat from all the living creatures of the earth:

11:3  LA: omne quod habet divisam ungulam, et ruminat in pecoribus, comedetis.
     EN: among the livestock, you may eat every animal that has a divided hoof and chews the cud.

11:4  LA: Quidquid autem ruminat quidem, et habet ungulam, sed non dividit eam, sicut camelus et cetera, non comedetis illud, et inter immunda reputabitis.
     EN: But any animal that chews the cud and has a hoof without dividing it, such as the camel and the others of this kind, you shall not eat; you shall count it among the unclean.

11:5  LA: Chœrogryllus qui ruminat, ungulamque non dividit, immundus est.
     EN: The hyrax, which chews the cud but does not divide the hoof, is unclean.

11:6  LA: Lepus quoque : nam et ipse ruminat, sed ungulam non dividit.
     EN: So also is the hare, for it too chews the cud but does not divide the hoof.

11:7  LA: Et sus : qui cum ungulam dividat, non ruminat.
     EN: And so is the pig, which divides the hoof but does not chew the cud.

11:8  LA: Horum carnibus non vescemini, nec cadavera contingetis, quia immunda sunt vobis.
     EN: You shall not eat their flesh or touch their carcasses, because they are unclean for you.

11:9  LA: Hæc sunt quæ gignuntur in aquis, et vesci licitum est : omne quod habet pinnulas et squamas, tam in mari quam in fluminibus et stagnis, comedetis.
     EN: These are the creatures born in the waters that you may eat: everything that has fins and scales, whether in the sea, rivers, or pools, you may eat.

11:10  LA: Quidquid autem pinnulas et squamas non habet, eorum quæ in aquis moventur et vivunt, abominabile vobis,
     EN: But whatever among the creatures moving and living in the waters does not have fins and scales shall be abhorrent to you

11:11  LA: execrandumque erit : carnes eorum non comedetis, et morticina vitabitis.
     EN: and detestable. You shall not eat their flesh, and you shall avoid their carcasses.

11:12  LA: Cuncta quæ non habent pinnulas et squamas in aquis, polluta erunt.
     EN: All creatures in the waters that lack fins and scales shall be unclean.

11:13  LA: Hæc sunt quæ de avibus comedere non debetis, et vitanda sunt vobis : aquilam, et gryphem, et haliæetum,
     EN: These are the birds you must not eat and must avoid: the eagle, the griffon, and the sea eagle,

11:14  LA: et milvum ac vulturem juxta genus suum,
     EN: the kite and the vulture according to its kind,

11:15  LA: et omne corvini generis in similitudinem suam,
     EN: and every bird of the raven kind according to its likeness,

11:16  LA: struthionem, et noctuam, et larum, et accipitrem juxta genus suum :
     EN: the ostrich, the night owl, the gull, and the hawk according to its kind,

11:17  LA: bubonem, et mergulum, et ibin,
     EN: the eagle owl, the diving bird, and the ibis,

11:18  LA: et cygnum, et onocrotalum, et porphyrionem,
     EN: the swan, the pelican, and the purple gallinule,

11:19  LA: herodionem, et charadrion juxta genus suum, upupam quoque, et vespertilionem.
     EN: the heron and the plover according to its kind, and also the hoopoe and the bat.

11:20  LA: Omne de volucribus quod graditur super quatuor pedes, abominabile erit vobis.
     EN: Every winged creature that walks on four feet shall be abhorrent to you.

11:21  LA: Quidquid autem ambulat quidem super quatuor pedes, sed habet longiora retro crura, per quæ salit super terram,
     EN: But whatever walks on four feet and has longer hind legs with which it leaps upon the earth,

11:22  LA: comedere debetis, ut est bruchus in genere suo, et attacus atque ophiomachus, ac locusta, singula juxta genus suum.
     EN: you may eat: the bruchus according to its kind, the attacus, the ophiomachus, and the locust, each according to its kind.

11:23  LA: Quidquid autem ex volucribus quatuor tantum habet pedes, execrabile erit vobis :
     EN: But every winged creature that has only four feet shall be detestable to you.

11:24  LA: et quicumque morticina eorum tetigerit, polluetur, et erit immundus usque ad vesperum :
     EN: Whoever touches their carcasses shall be defiled and remain unclean until evening.

11:25  LA: et si necesse fuerit ut portet quippiam horum mortuum, lavabit vestimenta sua, et immundus erit usque ad occasum solis.
     EN: If he must carry any of these dead creatures, he shall wash his garments and remain unclean until sunset.

11:26  LA: Omne animal quod habet quidem ungulam, sed non dividit eam, nec ruminat, immundum erit : et qui tetigerit illud, contaminabitur.
     EN: Every animal that has a hoof but does not divide it and does not chew the cud shall be unclean, and whoever touches it shall be defiled.

11:27  LA: Quod ambulat super manus ex cunctis animantibus, quæ incedunt quadrupedia, immundum erit : qui tetigerit morticina eorum, polluetur usque ad vesperum.
     EN: Among all the animals that walk on four feet, whatever walks on its paws shall be unclean. Whoever touches their carcasses shall be defiled until evening.

11:28  LA: Et qui portaverit hujuscemodi cadavera, lavabit vestimenta sua, et immundus erit usque ad vesperum : quia omnia hæc immunda sunt vobis.
     EN: Whoever carries such carcasses shall wash his garments and remain unclean until evening, because all these are unclean for you.

11:29  LA: Hæc quoque inter polluta reputabuntur de his quæ moventur in terra, mustela et mus et crocodilus, singula juxta genus suum,
     EN: These also shall be counted among the unclean creatures that move upon the earth: the weasel, the mouse, and the crocodile, each according to its kind,

11:30  LA: mygale, et chamæleon, et stellio, et lacerta, et talpa.
     EN: the shrew, the chameleon, the spotted lizard, the lizard, and the mole.

11:31  LA: Omnia hæc immunda sunt. Qui tetigerit morticina eorum, immundus erit usque ad vesperum :
     EN: All these are unclean. Whoever touches their carcasses shall be unclean until evening.

11:32  LA: et super quod ceciderit quidquam de morticinis eorum, polluetur, tam vas ligneum et vestimentum, quam pelles et cilicia : et in quocumque fit opus, tingentur aqua, et polluta erunt usque ad vesperum, et sic postea mundabuntur.
     EN: Anything upon which one of their carcasses falls shall be defiled, whether a wooden vessel, a garment, skins, or sackcloth. Whatever object is used for work shall be put into water; it shall be defiled until evening and afterward shall be clean.

11:33  LA: Vas autem fictile, in quod horum quidquam intro cecidit, polluetur, et idcirco frangendum est.
     EN: An earthenware vessel into which any of these falls shall be defiled and must therefore be broken.

11:34  LA: Omnis cibus, quem comedetis, si fusa fuerit super eum aqua, immundus erit : et omne liquens quod bibitur de universo vase, immundum erit.
     EN: Any food that you eat upon which water has been poured shall be unclean, and any liquid drunk from any such vessel shall be unclean.

11:35  LA: Et quidquid de morticinis hujuscemodi ceciderit super illud, immundum erit : sive clibani, sive chytropodes, destruentur, et immundi erunt.
     EN: Anything upon which one of these carcasses falls shall be unclean. Whether ovens or cooking stands, they shall be destroyed and shall be unclean.

11:36  LA: Fontes vero et cisternæ, et omnis aquarum congregatio munda erit. Qui morticinum eorum tetigerit, polluetur.
     EN: But springs, cisterns, and every collection of water shall be clean. Whoever touches their carcasses shall be defiled.

11:37  LA: Si ceciderit super sementem, non polluet eam.
     EN: If a carcass falls upon seed for sowing, it shall not defile it.

11:38  LA: Si autem quispiam aqua sementem perfuderit, et postea morticinis tacta fuerit, illico polluetur.
     EN: But if someone pours water over the seed and it afterward comes into contact with a carcass, it shall immediately be defiled.

11:39  LA: Si mortuum fuerit animal, quod licet vobis comedere, qui cadaver ejus tetigerit, immundus erit usque ad vesperum :
     EN: If an animal that you are permitted to eat dies, whoever touches its carcass shall be unclean until evening.

11:40  LA: et qui comederit ex eo quippiam, sive portaverit, lavabit vestimenta sua, et immundus erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: Whoever eats any of it or carries it shall wash his garments and remain unclean until evening.

11:41  LA: Omne quod reptat super terram, abominabile erit, nec assumetur in cibum.
     EN: Everything that creeps upon the earth shall be abhorrent and shall not be taken as food.

11:42  LA: Quidquid super pectus quadrupes graditur, et multos habet pedes, sive per humum trahitur, non comedetis, quia abominabile est.
     EN: Whatever goes upon its breast on four feet, has many feet, or drags itself along the ground, you shall not eat, because it is abhorrent.

11:43  LA: Nolite contaminare animas vestras, nec tangatis quidquam eorum, ne immundi sitis.
     EN: Do not defile yourselves or touch any of these things, lest you become unclean.

11:44  LA: Ego enim sum Dominus Deus vester : sancti estote, quia ego sanctus sum. Ne polluatis animas vestras in omni reptili quod movetur super terram.
     EN: For I am the Lord your God. Be holy, because I am holy. Do not defile yourselves with any creeping creature that moves upon the earth.

11:45  LA: Ego enim sum Dominus, qui eduxi vos de terra Ægypti, ut essem vobis in Deum. Sancti eritis, quia ego sanctus sum.
     EN: For I am the Lord, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God. You shall be holy, because I am holy.

11:46  LA: Ista est lex animantium ac volucrum, et omnis animæ viventis, quæ movetur in aqua, et reptat in terra,
     EN: This is the law of animals and winged creatures, and of every living creature that moves in the water or creeps upon the earth,

11:47  LA: ut differentias noveritis mundi et immundi, et sciatis quid comedere et quid respuere debeatis.
     EN: so that you may know the distinction between clean and unclean, and know what you may eat and what you must reject.

LEVITICUS 12

12:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

12:2  LA: Loquere filiis Israël, et dices ad eos : Mulier, si suscepto semine pepererit masculum, immunda erit septem diebus juxta dies separationis menstruæ.
     EN: Speak to the children of Israel and say to them: If a woman, having received seed, gives birth to a male child, she shall be unclean for seven days, as in the days of her menstrual separation.

12:3  LA: Et die octavo circumcidetur infantulus :
     EN: On the eighth day the infant shall be circumcised.

12:4  LA: ipsa vero triginta tribus diebus manebit in sanguine purificationis suæ. Omne sanctum non tanget, nec ingredietur in sanctuarium, donec impleantur dies purificationis suæ.
     EN: She shall remain thirty-three days in the blood of her purification. She shall touch nothing holy and shall not enter the sanctuary until the days of her purification are completed.

12:5  LA: Sin autem feminam pepererit, immunda erit duabus hebdomadibus juxta ritum fluxus menstrui, et sexaginta sex diebus manebit in sanguine purificationis suæ.
     EN: But if she gives birth to a female child, she shall be unclean for two weeks, according to the rule for menstrual flow, and shall remain sixty-six days in the blood of her purification.

12:6  LA: Cumque expleti fuerint dies purificationis suæ, pro filio sive pro filia, deferet agnum anniculum in holocaustum, et pullum columbæ sive turturem pro peccato, ad ostium tabernaculi testimonii, et tradet sacerdoti,
     EN: When the days of her purification for a son or a daughter are completed, she shall bring a year-old lamb as a whole burnt offering and a young pigeon or a turtledove for sin to the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony, and hand them to the priest.

12:7  LA: qui offeret illa coram Domino, et orabit pro ea, et sic mundabitur a profluvio sanguinis sui : ista est lex parientis masculum aut feminam.
     EN: He shall offer them before the Lord and pray for her, and thus she shall be cleansed from the flow of her blood. This is the law for a woman who gives birth to a male or female child.

12:8  LA: Quod si non invenerit manus ejus, nec potuerit offerre agnum, sumet duos turtures vel duos pullos columbarum, unum in holocaustum, et alterum pro peccato : orabitque pro ea sacerdos, et sic mundabitur.
     EN: If her means are insufficient and she cannot offer a lamb, she shall take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, one for a whole burnt offering and the other for sin. The priest shall pray for her, and thus she shall be cleansed.

LEVITICUS 13

13:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, et Aaron, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:

13:2  LA: Homo, in cujus cute et carne ortus fuerit diversus color, sive pustula, aut quasi lucens quippiam, id est, plaga lepræ, adducetur ad Aaron sacerdotem, vel ad unum quemlibet filiorum ejus.
     EN: A person on whose skin and flesh a discoloration, a swelling, or something like a bright spot appears, that is, a lesion of leprous disease, shall be brought to Aaron the priest or to any one of his sons.

13:3  LA: Qui cum viderit lepram in cute, et pilos in album mutatos colorem, ipsamque speciem lepræ humiliorem cute et carne reliqua : plaga lepræ est, et ad arbitrium ejus separabitur.
     EN: When the priest sees the leprous disease in the skin, the hairs changed to white, and the affected area lower than the rest of the skin and flesh, it is a lesion of leprous disease. The person shall be separated according to the priest’s judgment.

13:4  LA: Sin autem lucens candor fuerit in cute, nec humilior carne reliqua, et pili coloris pristini, recludet eum sacerdos septem diebus :
     EN: But if there is a bright white spot in the skin, not lower than the rest of the flesh, and the hairs retain their original color, the priest shall isolate him for seven days.

13:5  LA: et considerabit die septimo : et si quidem lepra ultra non creverit, nec transierit in cute priores terminos, rursum recludet eum septem diebus aliis.
     EN: On the seventh day he shall examine him. If the leprous disease has not increased further or spread in the skin beyond its former limits, he shall isolate him again for another seven days.

13:6  LA: Et die septimo contemplabitur : si obscurior fuerit lepra, et non creverit in cute, mundabit eum, quia scabies est : lavabitque homo vestimenta sua, et mundus erit.
     EN: On the seventh day he shall inspect him. If the leprous disease has become fainter and has not spread in the skin, he shall pronounce him clean, because it is a scaly eruption. The person shall wash his garments and be clean.

13:7  LA: Quod si postquam a sacerdote visus est, et redditus munditiæ, iterum lepra creverit : adducetur ad eum,
     EN: But if, after the priest has examined him and restored him to cleanness, the leprous disease spreads again, he shall be brought to him

13:8  LA: et immunditiæ condemnabitur.
     EN: and pronounced unclean.

13:9  LA: Plaga lepræ si fuerit in homine, adducetur ad sacerdotem,
     EN: If a person has a lesion of leprous disease, he shall be brought to the priest,

13:10  LA: et videbit eum. Cumque color albus in cute fuerit, et capillorum mutaverit aspectum, ipsa quoque caro viva apparuerit :
     EN: who shall examine him. If there is a white discoloration in the skin that has changed the appearance of the hair, and raw flesh itself has appeared,

13:11  LA: lepra vetustissima judicabitur, atque inolita cuti. Contaminabit itaque eum sacerdos, et non recludet, quia perspicuæ immunditiæ est.
     EN: it shall be judged a longstanding leprous disease that has taken root in the skin. The priest shall therefore pronounce him unclean and shall not isolate him, because his uncleanness is evident.

13:12  LA: Sin autem effloruerit discurrens lepra in cute, et operuerit omnem cutem a capite usque ad pedes, quidquid sub aspectum oculorum cadit,
     EN: But if the leprous disease breaks out and spreads over the skin, covering all the skin from head to foot, wherever the eyes can see,

13:13  LA: considerabit eum sacerdos, et teneri lepra mundissima judicabit : eo quod omnis in candorem versa sit, et idcirco homo mundus erit.
     EN: the priest shall examine him and judge that he has a clean form of leprous disease, because it has all turned white; therefore the person shall be clean.

13:14  LA: Quando vero caro vivens in eo apparuerit,
     EN: But whenever raw flesh appears in him,

13:15  LA: tunc sacerdotis judicio polluetur, et inter immundos reputabitur : caro enim viva, si lepra aspergitur, immunda est.
     EN: he shall then be declared defiled by the priest's judgment and counted among the unclean, for raw flesh affected by leprous disease is unclean.

13:16  LA: Quod si rursum versa fuerit in alborem, et totum hominem operuerit,
     EN: But if it turns white again and covers the entire person,

13:17  LA: considerabit eum sacerdos, et mundum esse decernet.
     EN: the priest shall examine him and declare him clean.

13:18  LA: Caro autem et cutis in qua ulcus natum est, et sanatum,
     EN: If a boil has appeared in the flesh and skin and has healed,

13:19  LA: et in loco ulceris cicatrix alba apparuerit, sive subrufa, adducetur homo ad sacerdotem.
     EN: and a white or reddish scar appears in the place of the boil, the person shall be brought to the priest.

13:20  LA: Qui cum viderit locum lepræ humiliorem carne reliqua, et pilos versos in candorem, contaminabit eum : plaga enim lepræ orta est in ulcere.
     EN: When the priest sees that the affected place is lower than the surrounding flesh and that the hairs have turned white, he shall pronounce him unclean, for a lesion of leprous disease has arisen in the boil.

13:21  LA: Quod si pilus coloris est pristini, et cicatrix subobscura, et vicina carne non est humilior, recludet eum septem diebus :
     EN: But if the hair has its original color, the scar is somewhat faint, and it is not lower than the neighboring flesh, he shall isolate him for seven days.

13:22  LA: et si quidem creverit, adjudicabit eum lepræ ;
     EN: If it spreads, he shall judge him to have leprous disease.

13:23  LA: sin autem steterit in loco suo, ulceris est cicatrix, et homo mundus erit.
     EN: But if it stays in its place, it is the scar of the boil, and the person shall be clean.

13:24  LA: Caro autem et cutis, quam ignis exusserit, et sanata albam sive rufam habuerit cicatricem,
     EN: If flesh and skin burned by fire have healed and have a white or red scar,

13:25  LA: considerabit eam sacerdos : et ecce versa est in alborem, et locus ejus reliqua cute est humilior, contaminabit eum, quia plaga lepræ in cicatrice orta est.
     EN: the priest shall examine it. If it has turned white and its place is lower than the surrounding skin, he shall pronounce the person unclean, because a lesion of leprous disease has arisen in the scar.

13:26  LA: Quod si pilorum color non fuerit immutatus, nec humilior plaga carne reliqua, et ipsa lepræ species fuerit subobscura, recludet eum septem diebus,
     EN: But if the color of the hairs has not changed, the lesion is not lower than the surrounding flesh, and the appearance of the leprous disease is somewhat faint, he shall isolate him for seven days.

13:27  LA: et die septimo contemplabitur : si creverit in cute lepra, contaminabit eum.
     EN: On the seventh day he shall inspect him. If the leprous disease has spread in the skin, he shall pronounce him unclean.

13:28  LA: Sin autem in loco suo candor steterit non satis clarus, plaga combustionis est, et idcirco mundabitur, quia cicatrix est combusturæ.
     EN: But if the white area has stayed in its place and is not very bright, it is a burn mark; therefore he shall be pronounced clean, because it is a scar from the burn.

13:29  LA: Vir, sive mulier, in cujus capite vel barba germinaverit lepra, videbit eos sacerdos.
     EN: If leprous disease appears on the head or beard of a man or woman, the priest shall examine them.

13:30  LA: Et si quidem humilior fuerit locus carne reliqua, et capillus flavus, solitoque subtilior, contaminabit eos, quia lepra capitis ac barbæ est.
     EN: If the affected place is lower than the surrounding flesh and the hair is yellow and thinner than usual, he shall pronounce them unclean, because it is leprous disease of the head or beard.

13:31  LA: Sin autem viderit locum maculæ æqualem vicinæ carni, et capillum nigrum : recludet eum septem diebus,
     EN: But if he sees that the affected place is level with the neighboring flesh and the hair is black, he shall isolate the person for seven days.

13:32  LA: et die septimo intuebitur. Si non creverit macula, et capillus sui coloris est, et locus plagæ carni reliquæ æqualis :
     EN: On the seventh day he shall inspect him. If the affected area has not spread, the hair retains its own color, and the lesion is level with the surrounding flesh,

13:33  LA: radetur homo absque loco maculæ, et includetur septem diebus aliis.
     EN: the person shall be shaved except at the affected place, and shall be isolated for another seven days.

13:34  LA: Si die septimo visa fuerit stetisse plaga in loco suo, nec humilior carne reliqua, mundabit eum : lotisque vestibus suis, mundus erit.
     EN: If on the seventh day the lesion is seen to have stayed in its place and is not lower than the surrounding flesh, he shall pronounce him clean. After washing his garments, the person shall be clean.

13:35  LA: Sin autem post emundationem rursus creverit macula in cute,
     EN: But if after his cleansing the affected area spreads again in the skin,

13:36  LA: non quæret amplius utrum capillus in flavum colorem sit immutatus, quia aperte immundus est.
     EN: the priest shall no longer inquire whether the hair has changed to yellow, because he is plainly unclean.

13:37  LA: Porro si steterit macula, et capilli nigri fuerint, noverit hominem sanatum esse, et confidenter eum pronuntiet mundum.
     EN: But if the affected area has stayed in place and the hairs are black, the priest shall know that the person is healed and shall confidently pronounce him clean.

13:38  LA: Vir, sive mulier, in cujus cute candor apparuerit,
     EN: If a white discoloration appears on the skin of a man or woman,

13:39  LA: intuebitur eos sacerdos. Si deprehenderit subobscurum alborem lucere in cute, sciat non esse lepram, sed maculam coloris candidi, et hominem mundum.
     EN: the priest shall inspect them. If he finds a somewhat faint whiteness showing in the skin, he shall know that it is not leprous disease but a white discoloration, and that the person is clean.

13:40  LA: Vir, de cujus capite capilli fluunt, calvus et mundus est :
     EN: A man whose hair falls out from his head is bald and clean.

13:41  LA: et si a fronte ceciderint pili, recalvaster et mundus est.
     EN: If the hair falls out from his forehead, he is bald at the front and clean.

13:42  LA: Sin autem in calvitio sive in recalvatione albus vel rufus color fuerit exortus,
     EN: But if a white or red discoloration appears in his bald crown or receding hairline,

13:43  LA: et hoc sacerdos viderit, condemnabit eum haud dubiæ lepræ, quæ orta est in calvitio.
     EN: and the priest sees it, he shall judge him to have definite leprous disease that has arisen in the bald area.

13:44  LA: Quicumque ergo maculatus fuerit lepra, et separatus est ad arbitrium sacerdotis,
     EN: Anyone, therefore, who is marked by leprous disease and has been separated according to the priest's judgment

13:45  LA: habebit vestimenta dissuta, caput nudum, os veste contectum, contaminatum ac sordidum se clamabit.
     EN: shall wear torn garments, have his head uncovered and his mouth covered by his clothing, and cry out that he is defiled and unclean.

13:46  LA: Omni tempore quo leprosus est et immundus, solus habitabit extra castra.
     EN: All the time that he has leprous disease and is unclean, he shall live alone outside the camp.

13:47  LA: Vestis lanea sive linea, quæ lepram habuerit,
     EN: A woolen or linen garment that has leprous contamination,

13:48  LA: in stamine atque subtegmine, aut certe pellis, vel quidquid ex pelle confectum est,
     EN: whether in the warp or the weft, or a skin or anything made of skin,

13:49  LA: si alba vel rufa macula fuerit infecta, lepra reputabitur, ostendeturque sacerdoti :
     EN: if it is affected by a white or red mark, shall be regarded as leprous and shown to the priest.

13:50  LA: qui consideratam recludet septem diebus :
     EN: After examining it, he shall isolate it for seven days.

13:51  LA: et die septimo rursus aspiciens, si deprehenderit crevisse, lepra perseverans est : pollutum judicabit vestimentum, et omne in quo fuerit inventa :
     EN: On the seventh day he shall look at it again. If he finds that it has spread, it is persistent leprous contamination. He shall judge the garment and whatever else it is found in to be defiled,

13:52  LA: et idcirco comburetur flammis.
     EN: and therefore it shall be burned in the flames.

13:53  LA: Quod si eam viderit non crevisse,
     EN: But if he sees that it has not spread,

13:54  LA: præcipiet, et lavabunt id in quo lepra est, recludetque illud septem diebus aliis.
     EN: he shall order the affected object to be washed, and shall isolate it for another seven days.

13:55  LA: Et cum viderit faciem quidem pristinam non reversam, nec tamen crevisse lepram, immundum judicabit, et igne comburet, eo quod infusa sit in superficie vestimenti, vel per totum, lepra.
     EN: When he sees that its former appearance has not returned, although the leprous contamination has not spread, he shall judge it unclean and burn it with fire, because the contamination has penetrated the surface of the garment or spread through the whole of it.

13:56  LA: Sin autem obscurior fuerit locus lepræ, postquam vestis est lota, abrumpet eum, et a solido dividet.
     EN: But if the affected place is fainter after the garment has been washed, he shall tear that part out and separate it from the sound material.

13:57  LA: Quod si ultra apparuerit in his locis, quæ prius immaculata erant, lepra volatilis et vaga, debet igne comburi.
     EN: If afterward a shifting and spreading leprous contamination appears in places that were previously unmarked, it must be burned with fire.

13:58  LA: Si cessaverit, lavabit aqua ea, quæ pura sunt, secundo, et munda erunt.
     EN: If it has ceased, he shall wash the clean objects with water a second time, and they shall be clean.

13:59  LA: Ista est lex lepræ vestimenti lanei et linei, staminis, atque subtegminis, omnisque supellectilis pelliceæ, quomodo mundari debeat, vel contaminari.
     EN: This is the law concerning leprous contamination in a woolen or linen garment, in warp or weft, and in any leather article: how it is to be pronounced clean or unclean.

LEVITICUS 14

14:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

14:2  LA: Hic est ritus leprosi, quando mundandus est. Adducetur ad sacerdotem :
     EN: This is the rite for a person with leprous disease when he is to be cleansed. He shall be brought to the priest,

14:3  LA: qui egressus de castris, cum invenerit lepram esse mundatam,
     EN: who shall go outside the camp. When he finds that the leprous disease has cleared,

14:4  LA: præcipiet ei, qui purificatur, ut offerat duos passeres vivos pro se, quibus vesci licitum est, et lignum cedrinum, vermiculumque et hyssopum.
     EN: he shall command the person being purified to offer on his own behalf two living sparrows of a kind permitted as food, cedarwood, scarlet material, and hyssop.

14:5  LA: Et unum ex passeribus immolari jubebit in vase fictili super aquas viventes :
     EN: He shall order one of the sparrows to be slaughtered in an earthenware vessel over fresh running water.

14:6  LA: alium autem vivum cum ligno cedrino, et cocco et hyssopo, tinget in sanguine passeris immolati,
     EN: The other, living sparrow, together with the cedarwood, scarlet material, and hyssop, he shall dip in the blood of the slaughtered sparrow.

14:7  LA: quo asperget illum, qui mundandus est, septies, ut jure purgetur : et dimittet passerem vivum, ut in agrum avolet.
     EN: With this he shall sprinkle the person being cleansed seven times, so that he may be duly purified; and he shall release the living sparrow to fly away into the open country.

14:8  LA: Cumque laverit homo vestimenta sua, radet omnes pilos corporis, et lavabitur aqua : purificatusque ingredietur castra, ita dumtaxat ut maneat extra tabernaculum suum septem diebus,
     EN: The person shall wash his garments, shave all the hair of his body, and bathe in water. When purified, he may enter the camp, but he must remain outside his own tent for seven days.

14:9  LA: et die septimo radet capillos capitis, barbamque et supercilia, ac totius corporis pilos. Et lotis rursum vestibus et corpore,
     EN: On the seventh day he shall shave the hair of his head, his beard, his eyebrows, and all the hair of his body. After washing his garments and body again,

14:10  LA: die octavo assumet duos agnos immaculatos, et ovem anniculam absque macula, et tres decimas similæ in sacrificium, quæ conspersa sit oleo, et seorsum olei sextarium.
     EN: on the eighth day he shall take two unblemished male lambs, an unblemished year-old ewe, three tenths of fine flour mixed with oil as a sacrifice, and a sextarius of oil separately.

14:11  LA: Cumque sacerdos purificans hominem, statuerit eum, et hæc omnia coram Domino in ostio tabernaculi testimonii,
     EN: The priest who purifies the person shall place him and all these things before the Lord at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony.

14:12  LA: tollet agnum et offeret eum pro delicto, oleique sextarium : et oblatis ante Dominum omnibus,
     EN: He shall take one lamb and offer it for guilt, together with the sextarius of oil. After presenting all these things before the Lord,

14:13  LA: immolabit agnum, ubi solet immolari hostia pro peccato, et holocaustum, id est, in loco sancto. Sicut enim pro peccato, ita et pro delicto ad sacerdotem pertinet hostia : Sancta sanctorum est.
     EN: he shall slaughter the lamb where the sin offering and the whole burnt offering are usually slaughtered, that is, in a holy place. For the guilt offering, like the sin offering, belongs to the priest; it is most holy.

14:14  LA: Assumensque sacerdos de sanguine hostiæ, quæ immolata est pro delicto, ponet super extremum auriculæ dextræ ejus qui mundatur, et super pollices manus dextræ et pedis :
     EN: The priest shall take some of the blood of the victim slaughtered for guilt and put it on the tip of the right ear of the person being cleansed, on the thumb of his right hand, and on his right great toe.

14:15  LA: et de olei sextario mittet in manum suam sinistram,
     EN: He shall pour some of the sextarius of oil into his own left hand,

14:16  LA: tingetque digitum dextrum in eo, et asperget coram Domino septies.
     EN: dip his right finger in it, and sprinkle it seven times before the Lord.

14:17  LA: Quod autem reliquum est olei in læva manu, fundet super extremum auriculæ dextræ ejus qui mundatur, et super pollices manus ac pedis dextri, et super sanguinem qui effusus est pro delicto,
     EN: The oil remaining in his left hand he shall pour on the tip of the right ear of the person being cleansed, on his right thumb and right great toe, over the blood that was poured out for guilt,

14:18  LA: et super caput ejus.
     EN: and upon his head.

14:19  LA: Rogabitque pro eo coram Domino, et faciet sacrificium pro peccato : tunc immolabit holocaustum,
     EN: He shall intercede for him before the Lord and make the sin offering. Then he shall slaughter the whole burnt offering

14:20  LA: et ponet illud in altari cum libamentis suis, et homo rite mundabitur.
     EN: and place it upon the altar with its accompanying offerings, and the person shall be cleansed according to the rite.

14:21  LA: Quod si pauper est, et non potest manus ejus invenire quæ dicta sunt pro delicto, assumet agnum ad oblationem, ut roget pro eo sacerdos, decimamque partem similæ conspersæ oleo in sacrificium, et olei sextarium,
     EN: But if the person is poor and his means cannot provide the things specified for guilt, he shall take one lamb as an offering, so that the priest may intercede for him, a tenth of fine flour mixed with oil as a sacrifice, and a sextarius of oil,

14:22  LA: duosque turtures sive duos pullos columbæ, quorum unus sit pro peccato, et alter in holocaustum :
     EN: and two turtledoves or two young pigeons, one for sin and the other as a whole burnt offering.

14:23  LA: offeretque ea die octavo purificationis suæ sacerdoti, ad ostium tabernaculi testimonii coram Domino.
     EN: On the eighth day of his purification he shall bring them to the priest before the Lord, at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony.

14:24  LA: Qui suscipiens agnum pro delicto et sextarium olei, levabit simul :
     EN: The priest shall receive the lamb for guilt and the sextarius of oil and lift them up together.

14:25  LA: immolatoque agno, de sanguine ejus ponet super extremum auriculæ dextræ illius qui mundatur, et super pollices manus ejus ac pedis dextri :
     EN: After slaughtering the lamb, he shall put some of its blood on the tip of the right ear of the person being cleansed, on the thumb of his right hand, and on his right great toe.

14:26  LA: olei vero partem mittet in manum suam sinistram,
     EN: He shall pour some of the oil into his own left hand,

14:27  LA: in quo tingens digitum dextræ manus asperget septies coram Domino :
     EN: and, dipping the finger of his right hand in it, sprinkle it seven times before the Lord.

14:28  LA: tangetque extremum dextræ auriculæ illius qui mundatur, et pollices manus ac pedis dextri, in loco sanguinis qui effusus est pro delicto :
     EN: He shall touch the tip of the right ear of the person being cleansed, his right thumb, and his right great toe, at the place of the blood poured out for guilt.

14:29  LA: reliquam autem partem olei, quæ est in sinistra manu, mittet super caput purificati, ut placet pro eo Dominum :
     EN: The remaining portion of the oil in his left hand he shall put upon the head of the person being purified, to obtain the Lord's favor for him.

14:30  LA: et turturem sive pullum columbæ offeret,
     EN: He shall offer the turtledoves or young pigeons,

14:31  LA: unum pro delicto, et alterum in holocaustum cum libamentis suis.
     EN: one for guilt and the other as a whole burnt offering with its accompanying offerings.

14:32  LA: Hoc est sacrificium leprosi, qui habere non potest omnia in emundationem sui.
     EN: This is the sacrifice for a person with leprous disease who cannot provide everything required for his cleansing.

14:33  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen et Aaron, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:

14:34  LA: Cum ingressi fueritis terram Chanaan, quam ego dabo vobis in possessionem, si fuerit plaga lepræ in ædibus,
     EN: When you have entered the land of Canaan, which I will give you as a possession, if a leprous contamination appears in a house,

14:35  LA: ibit cujus est domus, nuntians sacerdoti, et dicet : Quasi plaga lepræ videtur mihi esse in domo mea.
     EN: the owner of the house shall go and report it to the priest, saying: Something like a leprous contamination seems to me to be in my house.

14:36  LA: At ille præcipiet ut efferant universa de domo, priusquam ingrediatur eam, et videat utrum leprosa sit, ne immunda fiant omnia quæ in domo sunt. Intrabitque postea ut consideret lepram domus :
     EN: The priest shall order everything to be taken out of the house before he enters it to see whether it is contaminated, so that nothing in the house may become unclean. Afterward he shall enter to examine the contamination in the house.

14:37  LA: et cum viderit in parietibus illius quasi valliculas pallore sive rubore deformes, et humiliores superficie reliqua,
     EN: If he sees in its walls something like hollows, disfigured with a pale or reddish color and lower than the surrounding surface,

14:38  LA: egredietur ostium domus, et statim claudet illam septem diebus.
     EN: he shall go out through the door of the house and immediately close it up for seven days.

14:39  LA: Reversusque die septimo, considerabit eam : si invenerit crevisse lepram,
     EN: Returning on the seventh day, he shall examine it. If he finds that the contamination has spread,

14:40  LA: jubebit erui lapides in quibus lepra est, et projici eos extra civitatem in locum immundum :
     EN: he shall order the stones in which it appears to be removed and thrown outside the city into an unclean place,

14:41  LA: domum autem ipsam radi intrinsecus per circuitum, et spargi pulverem rasuræ extra urbem in locum immundum,
     EN: and the whole interior of the house to be scraped, with the scrapings scattered outside the city in an unclean place.

14:42  LA: lapidesque alios reponi pro his qui ablati fuerint, et luto alio liniri domum.
     EN: Other stones shall replace those removed, and the house shall be coated with fresh plaster.

14:43  LA: Sin autem postquam eruti sunt lapides, et pulvis erasus, et alia terra lita,
     EN: But if, after the stones have been removed, the dust scraped away, and fresh plaster applied,

14:44  LA: ingressus sacerdos viderit reversam lepram, et parietes respersos maculis, lepra est perseverans, et immunda domus :
     EN: the priest enters and sees that the contamination has returned and the walls are covered with spots, it is a persistent leprous contamination, and the house is unclean.

14:45  LA: quam statim destruent, et lapides ejus ac ligna, atque universum pulverem projicient extra oppidum in locum immundum.
     EN: They shall immediately demolish it and throw its stones, wood, and all its dust outside the town into an unclean place.

14:46  LA: Qui intraverit domum quando clausa est, immundus erit usque ad vesperum :
     EN: Anyone who enters the house while it is closed up shall be unclean until evening.

14:47  LA: et qui dormierit in ea, et comederit quippiam, lavabit vestimenta sua.
     EN: Anyone who sleeps in it or eats anything there shall wash his garments.

14:48  LA: Quod si introiens sacerdos viderit lepram non crevisse in domo, postquam denuo lita fuerit, purificabit eam reddita sanitate :
     EN: But if the priest enters and sees that the contamination has not spread in the house after it was plastered again, he shall pronounce it clean, since it has been restored to soundness.

14:49  LA: et in purificationem ejus sumet duos passeres, lignumque cedrinum, et vermiculum atque hyssopum :
     EN: For its purification he shall take two sparrows, cedarwood, scarlet material, and hyssop.

14:50  LA: et immolato uno passere in vase fictili super aquas vivas,
     EN: After slaughtering one sparrow in an earthenware vessel over fresh running water,

14:51  LA: tollet lignum cedrinum, et hyssopum, et coccum, et passerem vivum, et tinget omnia in sanguine passeris immolati, atque in aquis viventibus, et asperget domum septies,
     EN: he shall take the cedarwood, hyssop, scarlet material, and living sparrow, dip them all in the blood of the slaughtered sparrow and in the fresh running water, and sprinkle the house seven times.

14:52  LA: purificabitque eam tam in sanguine passeris quam in aquis viventibus, et in passere vivo, lignoque cedrino et hyssopo atque vermiculo.
     EN: He shall purify it with the sparrow's blood and the fresh running water, with the living sparrow, the cedarwood, hyssop, and scarlet material.

14:53  LA: Cumque dimiserit passerem avolare in agrum libere, orabit pro domo, et jure mundabitur.
     EN: When he has released the sparrow to fly freely into the open country, he shall pray for the house, and it shall be duly cleansed.

14:54  LA: Ista est lex omnis lepræ et percussuræ,
     EN: This is the law concerning every leprous disease and affliction,

14:55  LA: lepræ vestium et domorum,
     EN: the leprous contamination of garments and houses,

14:56  LA: cicatricis et erumpentium papularum, lucentis maculæ, et in varias species, coloribus immutatis,
     EN: scars, erupting swellings, bright spots, and the various appearances produced by changes of color,

14:57  LA: ut possit sciri quo tempore mundum quid, vel immundum sit.
     EN: so that it may be known when a thing is clean or unclean.

LEVITICUS 15

15:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen et Aaron, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:

15:2  LA: Loquimini filiis Israël, et dicite eis : Vir, qui patitur fluxum seminis, immundus erit.
     EN: Speak to the children of Israel and tell them: A man who suffers a flow of semen shall be unclean.

15:3  LA: Et tunc judicabitur huic vitio subjacere, cum per singula momenta adhæserit carni ejus, atque concreverit fœdus humor.
     EN: He shall be judged to suffer from this condition when foul fluid continually clings to his flesh and congeals there.

15:4  LA: Omne stratum, in quo dormierit, immundum erit, et ubicumque sederit.
     EN: Every bed on which he sleeps shall be unclean, and every place where he sits.

15:5  LA: Si quis hominum tetigerit lectum ejus, lavabit vestimenta sua, et ipse lotus aqua, immundus erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: Anyone who touches his bed shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

15:6  LA: Si sederit ubi ille sederat, et ipse lavabit vestimenta sua : et lotus aqua, immundus erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: Anyone who sits where that man has sat shall likewise wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

15:7  LA: Qui tetigerit carnem ejus, lavabit vestimenta sua : et ipse lotus aqua, immundus erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: Anyone who touches his flesh shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

15:8  LA: Si salivam hujuscemodi homo jecerit super eum qui mundus est, lavabit vestimenta sua : et lotus aqua, immundus erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: If such a man spits upon someone who is clean, that person shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

15:9  LA: Sagma, super quo sederit, immundum erit :
     EN: A saddle on which he sits shall be unclean.

15:10  LA: et quidquid sub eo fuerit, qui fluxum seminis patitur, pollutum erit usque ad vesperum. Qui portaverit horum aliquid, lavabit vestimenta sua : et ipse lotus aqua, immundus erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: Whatever has been beneath the man who suffers a flow of semen shall be defiled until evening. Anyone who carries one of these things shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

15:11  LA: Omnis, quem tetigerit qui talis est, non lotis ante manibus, lavabit vestimenta sua, et lotus aqua, immundus erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: Anyone whom such a man touches without first washing his hands shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

15:12  LA: Vas fictile quod tetigerit confringetur : vas autem ligneum lavabitur aqua.
     EN: An earthenware vessel that he touches shall be broken, but a wooden vessel shall be washed with water.

15:13  LA: Si sanatus fuerit qui hujuscemodi sustinet passionem, numerabit septem dies post emundationem sui, et lotis vestibus et toto corpore in aquis viventibus, erit mundus.
     EN: If the man suffering this condition is healed, he shall count seven days after his cleansing. After washing his garments and his entire body in fresh running water, he shall be clean.

15:14  LA: Die autem octavo sumet duos turtures, aut duos pullos columbæ, et veniet in conspectum Domini ad ostium tabernaculi testimonii, dabitque eos sacerdoti :
     EN: On the eighth day he shall take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, come before the Lord to the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony, and give them to the priest.

15:15  LA: qui faciet unum pro peccato et alterum in holocaustum : rogabitque pro eo coram Domino, ut emundetur a fluxi seminis sui.
     EN: The priest shall offer one for sin and the other as a whole burnt offering, and shall intercede for him before the Lord so that he may be cleansed from his flow of semen.

15:16  LA: Vir de quo egreditur semen coitus, lavabit aqua omne corpus suum : et immundus erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: A man from whom the seed of intercourse comes forth shall wash his entire body with water and shall be unclean until evening.

15:17  LA: Vestem et pellem, quam habuerit, lavabit aqua, et immunda erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: He shall wash with water the garment or skin that he has used, and it shall be unclean until evening.

15:18  LA: Mulier, cum qua coierit, lavabitur aqua, et immunda erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: The woman with whom he has had intercourse shall bathe in water and shall be unclean until evening.

15:19  LA: Mulier, quæ redeunte mense patitur fluxum sanguinis, septem diebus separabitur.
     EN: A woman who has a flow of blood when her monthly period returns shall be separated for seven days.

15:20  LA: Omnis qui tetigerit eam, immundus erit usque ad vesperum :
     EN: Anyone who touches her shall be unclean until evening.

15:21  LA: et in quo dormierit vel sederit diebus separationis suæ, polluetur.
     EN: Anything on which she sleeps or sits during the days of her separation shall be defiled.

15:22  LA: Qui tetigerit lectum ejus, lavabit vestimenta sua : et ipse lotus aqua, immundus erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: Anyone who touches her bed shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

15:23  LA: Omne vas, super quo illa sederit, quisquis attigerit, lavabit vestimenta sua : et ipse lotus aqua, pollutus erit usque ad vesperum.
     EN: Anyone who touches any object on which she has sat shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be defiled until evening.

15:24  LA: Si coierit cum ea vir tempore sanguinis menstrualis, immundus erit septem diebus : et omne stratum, in quo dormierit, polluetur.
     EN: If a man has intercourse with her during her menstrual bleeding, he shall be unclean for seven days, and every bed on which he sleeps shall be defiled.

15:25  LA: Mulier, quæ patitur multis diebus fluxum sanguinis non in tempore menstruali, vel quæ post menstruum sanguinem fluere non cessat, quamdiu subjacet huic passioni, immunda erit quasi sit in tempore menstruo.
     EN: A woman who suffers a flow of blood for many days outside the time of menstruation, or whose bleeding does not stop after her menstrual period, shall be unclean for as long as she suffers this condition, just as during her menstrual period.

15:26  LA: Omne stratum, in quo dormierit, et vas in quo sederit, pollutum erit.
     EN: Every bed on which she sleeps and every object on which she sits shall be defiled.

15:27  LA: Quicumque tetigerit ea, lavabit vestimenta sua : et ipse lotus aqua, immundus erit usque ad vesperam.
     EN: Anyone who touches them shall wash his garments and bathe in water, and shall be unclean until evening.

15:28  LA: Si steterit sanguis, et fluere cessaverit, numerabit septem dies purificationis suæ :
     EN: If the blood stops and ceases to flow, she shall count seven days for her purification.

15:29  LA: et die octavo offeret pro se sacerdoti duos turtures, aut duos pullos columbarum, ad ostium tabernaculi testimonii :
     EN: On the eighth day she shall bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons on her own behalf to the priest, at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony.

15:30  LA: qui unum faciet pro peccato, et alterum in holocaustum, rogabitque pro ea coram Domino, et pro fluxu immunditiæ ejus.
     EN: He shall offer one for sin and the other as a whole burnt offering, and shall intercede for her before the Lord concerning her unclean discharge.

15:31  LA: Docebitis ergo filios Israël ut caveant immunditiam, et non moriantur in sordibus suis, cum polluerint tabernaculum meum quod est inter eos.
     EN: Therefore you shall teach the children of Israel to guard against uncleanness, so that they do not die in their defilement by defiling My tabernacle, which is among them.

15:32  LA: Ista est lex ejus, qui patitur fluxum seminis, et qui polluitur coitu,
     EN: This is the law for a man who suffers a flow of semen or is defiled by intercourse,

15:33  LA: et quæ menstruis temporibus separatur, vel quæ jugi fluit sanguine, et hominis qui dormierit cum ea.
     EN: for a woman separated during her monthly periods or suffering a continuous flow of blood, and for the man who lies with her.

LEVITICUS 16

16:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen post mortem duorum filiorum Aaron, quando offerentes ignem alienum interfecti sunt :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses after the death of Aaron's two sons, when they were killed while offering unauthorized fire.

16:2  LA: et præcepit ei, dicens : Loquere ad Aaron fratrem tuum, ne omni tempore ingrediatur sanctuarium, quod est intra velum coram propitiatorio quo tegitur arca, ut non moriatur (quia in nube apparebo super oraculum),
     EN: He commanded him, saying: Tell your brother Aaron not to enter at any time he pleases into the sanctuary inside the veil, before the atonement cover that covers the ark, lest he die, for I will appear in a cloud above the place of divine utterance.

16:3  LA: nisi hæc ante fecerit : vitulum pro peccato offeret, et arietem in holocaustum.
     EN: He may enter only after doing this: he shall offer a calf for sin and a ram as a whole burnt offering.

16:4  LA: Tunica linea vestietur, feminalibus lineis verenda celabit : accingetur zona linea, cidarim lineam imponet capiti : hæc enim vestimenta sunt sancta : quibus cunctis, cum lotus fuerit, induetur.
     EN: He shall put on a linen tunic, cover his private parts with linen undergarments, fasten a linen sash around himself, and put a linen turban on his head. For these are holy garments, and he shall put all of them on after bathing.

16:5  LA: Suscipietque ab universa multitudine filiorum Israël duos hircos pro peccato, et unum arietem in holocaustum.
     EN: From the whole congregation of the children of Israel he shall receive two male goats for sin and one ram as a whole burnt offering.

16:6  LA: Cumque obtulerit vitulum, et oraverit pro se et pro domo sua,
     EN: When he has presented the calf and prayed for himself and his household,

16:7  LA: duos hircos stare faciet coram Domino in ostio tabernaculi testimonii :
     EN: he shall make the two male goats stand before the Lord at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony.

16:8  LA: mittensque super utrumque sortem, unam Domino, alteram capro emissario :
     EN: He shall cast lots over the two, one for the Lord and the other for the goat to be sent away.

16:9  LA: cujus exierit sors Domino, offeret illum pro peccato :
     EN: The goat whose lot falls to the Lord he shall offer for sin.

16:10  LA: cujus autem in caprum emissarium, statuet eum vivum coram Domino, ut fundat preces super eo, et emittat eum in solitudinem.
     EN: But the one whose lot falls to be the goat sent away he shall place alive before the Lord, to pour out prayers over it and send it into the wilderness.

16:11  LA: His rite celebratis, offeret vitulum, et rogans pro se, et pro domo sua, immolabit eum :
     EN: When these rites have been duly performed, he shall present the calf and slaughter it, interceding for himself and his household.

16:12  LA: assumptoque thuribulo, quod de prunis altaris impleverit, et hauriens manu compositum thymiama in incensum, ultra velum intrabit in sancta :
     EN: He shall take a censer filled with coals from the altar and a handful of blended incense to burn, and enter beyond the veil into the holy place.

16:13  LA: ut, positis super ignem aromatibus, nebula eorum et vapor operiat oraculum quod est supra testimonium, et non moriatur.
     EN: He shall put the spices upon the fire, so that their cloud and vapor cover the place of divine utterance above the testimony, and he does not die.

16:14  LA: Tollet quoque de sanguine vituli, et asperget digito septies contra propitiatorium ad orientem.
     EN: He shall also take some of the calf's blood and sprinkle it with his finger seven times toward the atonement cover on the east side.

16:15  LA: Cumque mactaverit hircum pro peccato populi, inferet sanguinem ejus intra velum, sicut præceptum est de sanguine vituli, ut aspergat e regione oraculi,
     EN: When he has slaughtered the male goat for the people's sin, he shall bring its blood inside the veil, as was commanded concerning the calf's blood, to sprinkle it facing the place of divine utterance

16:16  LA: et expiet sanctuarium ab immunditiis filiorum Israël, et a prævaricationibus eorum, cunctisque peccatis. Juxta hunc ritum faciet tabernaculo testimonii, quod fixum est inter eos, in medio sordium habitationis eorum.
     EN: and purify the sanctuary from the uncleanness of the children of Israel, their transgressions, and all their sins. He shall perform the same rite for the tabernacle of the testimony, which stands among them amid the defilement of their dwelling place.

16:17  LA: Nullus hominum sit in tabernaculo, quando pontifex sanctuarium ingreditur, ut roget pro se, et pro domo sua, et pro universo cœtu Israël, donec egrediatur.
     EN: No one shall be in the tabernacle from the time the high priest enters the sanctuary to intercede for himself, his household, and the whole assembly of Israel, until he comes out.

16:18  LA: Cum autem exierit ad altare quod coram Domino est, oret pro se, et sumptum sanguinem vituli atque hirci fundat super cornua ejus per gyrum :
     EN: When he comes out to the altar that is before the Lord, he shall pray for himself and take some of the calf's and the goat's blood, pouring it upon the horns all around it.

16:19  LA: aspergensque digito septies, expiet, et sanctificet illud ab immunditiis filiorum Israël.
     EN: Sprinkling it seven times with his finger, he shall purify and sanctify it from the uncleanness of the children of Israel.

16:20  LA: Postquam emundaverit sanctuarium, et tabernaculum, et altare, tunc offerat hircum viventem :
     EN: After cleansing the sanctuary, the tabernacle, and the altar, he shall bring forward the living male goat.

16:21  LA: et posita utraque manu super caput ejus, confiteatur omnes iniquitates filiorum Israël, et universa delicta atque peccata eorum : quæ imprecans capiti ejus, emittet illum per hominem paratum, in desertum.
     EN: Laying both hands upon its head, he shall confess all the iniquities of the children of Israel and all their offenses and sins. Invoking these upon its head, he shall send it into the wilderness by a man appointed for the task.

16:22  LA: Cumque portaverit hircus omnes iniquitates eorum in terram solitariam, et dimissus fuerit in deserto,
     EN: When the goat has carried all their iniquities into a solitary land and has been released in the wilderness,

16:23  LA: revertetur Aaron in tabernaculum testimonii, et depositis vestibus, quibus prius indutus erat, cum intraret sanctuarium, relictisque ibi,
     EN: Aaron shall return to the tabernacle of the testimony and take off the garments he wore when he entered the sanctuary, leaving them there.

16:24  LA: lavabit carnem suam in loco sancto, indueturque vestibus suis. Et postquam egressus obtulerit holocaustum suum, ac plebis, rogabit tam pro se quam pro populo :
     EN: He shall bathe his body in a holy place and put on his own garments. After coming out and offering his whole burnt offering and the people's, he shall intercede both for himself and for the people.

16:25  LA: et adipem, qui oblatus est pro peccatis, adolebit super altare.
     EN: The fat that was offered for sins he shall burn upon the altar.

16:26  LA: Ille vero, qui dimiserit caprum emissarium, lavabit vestimenta sua, et corpus aqua, et sic ingredietur in castra.
     EN: The man who released the goat sent away shall wash his garments and bathe his body in water, and then enter the camp.

16:27  LA: Vitulum autem, et hircum, qui pro peccato fuerant immolati, et quorum sanguis illatus est in sanctuarium, ut expiatio compleretur, asportabunt foras castra, et comburent igni tam pelles quam carnes eorum, ac fimum :
     EN: The calf and the male goat slaughtered for sin, whose blood was brought into the sanctuary to complete the expiation, shall be carried outside the camp. Their skins, flesh, and dung shall be burned with fire.

16:28  LA: et quicumque combusserit ea, lavabit vestimenta sua et carnem aqua, et sic ingredietur in castra.
     EN: Whoever burns them shall wash his garments and bathe his body in water, and then enter the camp.

16:29  LA: Eritque vobis hoc legitimum sempiternum : mense septimo, decima die mensis, affligetis animas vestras, nullumque opus facietis, sive indigena, sive advena qui peregrinatur inter vos.
     EN: This shall be a perpetual ordinance for you: in the seventh month, on the tenth day of the month, you shall afflict yourselves and do no work, whether native-born or a foreigner residing among you.

16:30  LA: In hac die expiatio erit vestri, atque mundatio ab omnibus peccatis vestris : coram Domino mundabimini.
     EN: On this day there shall be expiation for you and cleansing from all your sins. You shall be cleansed before the Lord.

16:31  LA: Sabbatum enim requietionis est, et affligetis animas vestras religione perpetua.
     EN: For it is a sabbath of rest, and you shall afflict yourselves as a perpetual religious observance.

16:32  LA: Expiabit autem sacerdos, qui unctus fuerit, et cujus manus initiatæ sunt ut sacerdotio fungatur pro patre suo : indueturque stola linea et vestibus sanctis,
     EN: The priest who has been anointed and whose hands have been consecrated to exercise the priesthood in his father's place shall make expiation. He shall put on the linen robe and holy garments,

16:33  LA: et expiabit sanctuarium et tabernaculum testimonii atque altare, sacerdotes quoque et universum populum.
     EN: and make expiation for the sanctuary, the tabernacle of the testimony, and the altar, and also for the priests and all the people.

16:34  LA: Eritque vobis hoc legitimum sempiternum, ut oretis pro filiis Israël, et pro cunctis peccatis eorum semel in anno. Fecit igitur sicut præceperat Dominus Moysi.
     EN: This shall be a perpetual ordinance for you, that once a year you intercede for the children of Israel and for all their sins. He therefore did as the Lord had commanded Moses.

LEVITICUS 17

17:1  LA: Et locutus est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

17:2  LA: Loquere Aaron et filiis ejus, et cunctis filiis Israël, dicens ad eos : Iste est sermo quem mandavit Dominus, dicens :
     EN: Speak to Aaron, his sons, and all the children of Israel, and tell them: This is the command the Lord has given:

17:3  LA: Homo quilibet de domo Israël, si occiderit bovem aut ovem, sive capram, in castris vel extra castra,
     EN: Any man of the house of Israel who kills an ox, a sheep, or a goat, inside or outside the camp,

17:4  LA: et non obtulerit ad ostium tabernaculi oblationem Domino, sanguinis reus erit : quasi si sanguinem fuderit, sic peribit de medio populi sui.
     EN: and does not bring it to the entrance of the tabernacle as an offering to the Lord, shall be guilty of bloodshed. As though he had shed blood, he shall perish from among his people.

17:5  LA: Ideo sacerdoti offerre debent filii Israël hostias suas, quas occident in agro, ut sanctificentur Domino ante ostium tabernaculi testimonii, et immolent eas hostias pacificas Domino.
     EN: Therefore the children of Israel must bring the victims they kill in the open country to the priest, so that they may be consecrated to the Lord before the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony and sacrificed as peace offerings to the Lord.

17:6  LA: Fundetque sacerdos sanguinem super altare Domini ad ostium tabernaculi testimonii, et adolebit adipem in odorem suavitatis Domino :
     EN: The priest shall pour the blood upon the Lord's altar at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony and burn the fat as a pleasing fragrance to the Lord.

17:7  LA: et nequaquam ultra immolabunt hostias suas dæmonibus, cum quibus fornicati sunt. Legitimum sempiternum erit illis et posteris eorum.
     EN: They shall no longer sacrifice their victims to demons, with whom they have prostituted themselves. This shall be a perpetual ordinance for them and their descendants.

17:8  LA: Et ad ipsos dices : Homo de domo Israël, et de advenis qui peregrinantur apud vos, qui obtulerit holocaustum sive victimam,
     EN: You shall say to them: Any man of the house of Israel or of the foreigners residing among you who presents a whole burnt offering or a sacrificial victim,

17:9  LA: et ad ostium tabernaculi testimonii non adduxerit eam, ut offeratur Domino, interibit de populo suo.
     EN: and does not bring it to the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony to be offered to the Lord, shall perish from among his people.

17:10  LA: Homo quilibet de domo Israël et de advenis qui peregrinantur inter eos, si comederit sanguinem, obfirmabo faciem meam contra animam illius, et disperdam eam de populo suo,
     EN: If any man of the house of Israel or of the foreigners residing among them eats blood, I will set My face firmly against that person and destroy him from among his people.

17:11  LA: quia anima carnis in sanguine est : et ego dedi illum vobis, ut super altare in eo expietis pro animabus vestris, et sanguis pro animæ piaculo sit.
     EN: For the life of the flesh is in the blood, and I have given it to you so that you may make expiation for yourselves with it upon the altar, and so that the blood may serve as an atonement for life.

17:12  LA: Idcirco dixi filiis Israël : Omnis anima ex vobis non comedet sanguinem, nec ex advenis qui peregrinantur apud vos.
     EN: Therefore I said to the children of Israel: No person among you shall eat blood, nor shall any of the foreigners residing among you.

17:13  LA: Homo quicumque de filiis Israël, et de advenis qui peregrinantur apud vos, si venatione atque aucupio ceperit feram, vel avem, quibus vesci licitum est, fundat sanguinem ejus, et operiat illum terra.
     EN: If any of the children of Israel or of the foreigners residing among you catches a wild animal or bird in hunting or fowling, of a kind permitted as food, he shall pour out its blood and cover it with earth.

17:14  LA: Anima enim omnis carnis in sanguine est : unde dixi filiis Israël : Sanguinem universæ carnis non comedetis, quia anima carnis in sanguine est : et quicumque comederit illum, interibit.
     EN: For the life of all flesh is in its blood. Therefore I said to the children of Israel: You shall not eat the blood of any flesh, because the life of the flesh is in the blood, and whoever eats it shall perish.

17:15  LA: Anima, quæ comederit morticinum, vel captum a bestia, tam de indigenis, quam de advenis, lavabit vestimenta sua et semetipsum aqua, et contaminatus erit usque ad vesperum : et hoc ordine mundus fiet.
     EN: Any person, whether native-born or a foreigner, who eats an animal that has died of itself or has been taken by a wild beast shall wash his garments and bathe in water. He shall be defiled until evening, and in this way shall become clean.

17:16  LA: Quod si non laverit vestimenta sua et corpus, portabit iniquitatem suam.
     EN: But if he does not wash his garments and body, he shall bear his iniquity.

LEVITICUS 18

18:1  LA: Locutus est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

18:2  LA: Loquere filiis Israël, et dices ad eos : Ego Dominus Deus vester :
     EN: Speak to the children of Israel and tell them: I am the Lord your God.

18:3  LA: juxta consuetudinem terræ Ægypti, in qua habitastis, non facietis : et juxta morem regionis Chanaan, ad quam ego introducturus sum vos, non agetis, nec in legitimis eorum ambulabitis.
     EN: You shall not act according to the custom of the land of Egypt, where you lived, or according to the practice of the land of Canaan, into which I am about to bring you. You shall not walk in their ordinances.

18:4  LA: Facietis judicia mea, et præcepta mea servabitis, et ambulabitis in eis. Ego Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: You shall carry out My judgments, keep My commands, and walk in them. I am the Lord your God.

18:5  LA: Custodite leges meas atque judicia, quæ faciens homo, vivet in eis. Ego Dominus.
     EN: Keep My laws and judgments; the person who carries them out shall live by them. I am the Lord.

18:6  LA: Omnis homo ad proximam sanguinis sui non accedet, ut revelet turpitudinem ejus. Ego Dominus.
     EN: No man shall approach a close blood relative to uncover her nakedness. I am the Lord.

18:7  LA: Turpitudinem patris tui et turpitudinem matris tuæ non discooperies : mater tua est : non revelabis turpitudinem ejus.
     EN: You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father or your mother. She is your mother; you shall not uncover her nakedness.

18:8  LA: Turpitudinem uxoris patris tui non discooperies : turpitudo enim patris tui est.
     EN: You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's wife, for it is your father's nakedness.

18:9  LA: Turpitudinem sororis tuæ ex patre sive ex matre, quæ domi vel foris genita est, non revelabis.
     EN: You shall not uncover the nakedness of your sister, whether your father's daughter or your mother's, whether born at home or away.

18:10  LA: Turpitudinem filiæ filii tui vel neptis ex filia non revelabis : quia turpitudo tua est.
     EN: You shall not uncover the nakedness of your son's daughter or your granddaughter through your daughter, for it is your own nakedness.

18:11  LA: Turpitudinem filiæ uxoris patris tui, quam peperit patri tuo, et est soror tua, non revelabis.
     EN: You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's wife's daughter, whom she bore to your father and who is your sister.

18:12  LA: Turpitudinem sororis patris tui non discooperies : quia caro est patris tui.
     EN: You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's sister, because she is your father's flesh.

18:13  LA: Turpitudinem sororis matris tuæ non revelabis, eo quod caro sit matris tuæ.
     EN: You shall not uncover the nakedness of your mother's sister, because she is your mother's flesh.

18:14  LA: Turpitudinem patrui tui non revelabis, nec accedes ad uxorem ejus, quæ tibi affinitate conjungitur.
     EN: You shall not uncover your paternal uncle's nakedness or approach his wife, who is joined to you by marriage.

18:15  LA: Turpitudinem nurus tuæ non revelabis, quia uxor filii tui est : nec discooperies ignominiam ejus.
     EN: You shall not uncover your daughter-in-law's nakedness, because she is your son's wife; you shall not expose her shame.

18:16  LA: Turpitudinem uxoris fratris tui non revelabis : quia turpitudo fratris tui est.
     EN: You shall not uncover the nakedness of your brother's wife, because it is your brother's nakedness.

18:17  LA: Turpitudinem uxoris tuæ et filiæ ejus non revelabis. Filiam filii ejus, et filiam filiæ illius non sumes, ut reveles ignominiam ejus : quia caro illius sunt, et talis coitus incestus est.
     EN: You shall not uncover the nakedness of your wife and her daughter. You shall not take her son's daughter or her daughter's daughter to expose her shame, because they are her flesh, and such intercourse is incestuous.

18:18  LA: Sororem uxoris tuæ in pellicatum illius non accipies, nec revelabis turpitudinem ejus adhuc illa vivente.
     EN: You shall not take your wife's sister as her rival or uncover her nakedness while your wife is still alive.

18:19  LA: Ad mulierem quæ patitur menstrua non accedes, nec revelabis fœditatem ejus.
     EN: You shall not approach a woman who is menstruating or uncover her uncleanness.

18:20  LA: Cum uxore proximi tui non coibis, nec seminis commistione maculaberis.
     EN: You shall not have intercourse with your neighbor's wife or defile yourself by the mingling of seed.

18:21  LA: De semine tuo non dabis ut consecretur idolo Moloch, nec pollues nomen Dei tui. Ego Dominus.
     EN: You shall not give any of your offspring to be consecrated to the idol Moloch, nor shall you profane the name of your God. I am the Lord.

18:22  LA: Cum masculo non commiscearis coitu femineo, quia abominatio est.
     EN: You shall not have intercourse with a male as with a woman, because it is an abomination.

18:23  LA: Cum omni pecore non coibis, nec maculaberis cum eo. Mulier non succumbet jumento, nec miscebitur ei, quia scelus est.
     EN: You shall not have intercourse with any animal or defile yourself with it. A woman shall not lie beneath an animal or mate with it, because it is a wicked act.

18:24  LA: Nec polluamini in omnibus his quibus contaminatæ sunt universæ gentes, quas ego ejiciam ante conspectum vestrum,
     EN: Do not defile yourselves with any of these things by which all the nations that I will drive out before your eyes have been defiled,

18:25  LA: et quibus polluta est terra : cujus ego scelera visitabo, ut evomat habitatores suos.
     EN: and by which the land has been defiled. I will visit punishment upon its wicked deeds, so that it vomits out its inhabitants.

18:26  LA: Custodite legitima mea atque judicia, et non faciatis ex omnibus abominationibus istis, tam indigena quam colonus qui peregrinantur apud vos.
     EN: Keep My ordinances and judgments, and do none of these abominations, whether native-born or a settler residing among you.

18:27  LA: Omnes enim execrationes istas fecerunt accolæ terræ qui fuerunt ante vos, et polluerunt eam.
     EN: For the inhabitants of the land who were there before you did all these detestable things and defiled it.

18:28  LA: Cavete ergo ne et vos similiter evomat, cum paria feceritis, sicut evomuit gentem, quæ fuit ante vos.
     EN: Take care, therefore, that it does not likewise vomit you out when you do the same things, as it vomited out the nation that was there before you.

18:29  LA: Omnis anima, quæ fecerit de abominationibus his quippiam, peribit de medio populi sui.
     EN: Every person who does any of these abominations shall perish from among his people.

18:30  LA: Custodite mandata mea. Nolite facere quæ fecerunt hi qui fuerunt ante vos, et ne polluamini in eis. Ego Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: Keep My commandments. Do not do what those before you did, and do not defile yourselves with these things. I am the Lord your God.

LEVITICUS 19

19:1  LA: Locutus est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

19:2  LA: Loquere ad omnem cœtum filiorum Israël, et dices ad eos : Sancti estote, quia ego sanctus sum, Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: Speak to the whole assembly of the children of Israel and tell them: Be holy, because I, the Lord your God, am holy.

19:3  LA: Unusquisque patrem suum, et matrem suam timeat. Sabbata mea custodite. Ego Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: Each of you shall revere his father and mother. Keep My sabbaths. I am the Lord your God.

19:4  LA: Nolite converti ad idola, nec deos conflatiles faciatis vobis. Ego Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: Do not turn to idols or make cast-metal gods for yourselves. I am the Lord your God.

19:5  LA: Si immolaveritis hostiam pacificorum Domino, ut sit placabilis,
     EN: If you sacrifice a peace offering to the Lord to obtain His favor,

19:6  LA: eo die quo fuerit immolata, comedetis eam, et die altero : quidquid autem residuum fuerit in diem tertium, igne comburetis.
     EN: you shall eat it on the day it is sacrificed and on the following day. Whatever remains until the third day you shall burn with fire.

19:7  LA: Si quis post biduum comederit ex ea, profanus erit, et impietatis reus :
     EN: If anyone eats of it after two days, he shall be profane and guilty of impiety.

19:8  LA: portabitque iniquitatem suam, quia sanctum Domini polluit, et peribit anima illa de populo suo.
     EN: He shall bear his iniquity because he has defiled what is holy to the Lord, and that person shall perish from among his people.

19:9  LA: Cumque messueris segetes terræ tuæ, non tondebis usque ad solum superficiem terræ, nec remanentes spicas colliges,
     EN: When you reap the crops of your land, you shall not cut the surface of the field bare to the ground or gather the ears of grain left behind.

19:10  LA: neque in vinea tua racemos et grana decidentia congregabis : sed pauperibus et peregrinis carpenda dimittes. Ego Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: Nor shall you gather the leftover clusters and fallen grapes in your vineyard; you shall leave them for the poor and foreigners to pick. I am the Lord your God.

19:11  LA: Non facietis furtum. Non mentiemini, nec decipiet unusquisque proximum suum.
     EN: You shall not steal. You shall not lie, and none of you shall deceive his neighbor.

19:12  LA: Non perjurabis in nomine meo, nec pollues nomen Dei tui. Ego Dominus.
     EN: You shall not swear falsely by My name or profane the name of your God. I am the Lord.

19:13  LA: Non facies calumniam proximo tuo nec vi opprimes eum. Non morabitur opus mercenarii tui apud te usque mane.
     EN: You shall not defraud your neighbor or oppress him by force. The wages of your hired worker shall not remain with you until morning.

19:14  LA: Non maledices surdo, nec coram cæco pones offendiculum : sed timebis Dominum Deum tuum, quia ego sum Dominus.
     EN: You shall not curse the deaf or put an obstacle before the blind, but you shall fear the Lord your God, because I am the Lord.

19:15  LA: Non facies quod iniquum est, nec injuste judicabis. Non consideres personam pauperis, nec honores vultum potentis. Juste judica proximo tuo.
     EN: You shall do nothing unjust or judge unfairly. You shall not favor the poor or show deference to the powerful. Judge your neighbor justly.

19:16  LA: Non eris criminator, nec susurro in populo. Non stabis contra sanguinem proximi tui. Ego Dominus.
     EN: You shall not be a slanderer or a whisperer among the people. You shall not take a stand against your neighbor's blood. I am the Lord.

19:17  LA: Non oderis fratrem tuum in corde tuo, sed publice argue eum, ne habeas super illo peccatum.
     EN: You shall not hate your brother in your heart, but shall rebuke him openly, so that you do not bear sin on his account.

19:18  LA: Non quæras ultionem, nec memor eris injuriæ civium tuorum. Diliges amicum tuum sicut teipsum. Ego Dominus.
     EN: You shall not seek vengeance or remember the wrong done by your fellow citizens. You shall love your friend as yourself. I am the Lord.

19:19  LA: Leges meas custodite. Jumentum tuum non facies coire cum alterius generis animantibus. Agrum tuum non seres diverso semine. Veste, quæ ex duobus texta est, non indueris.
     EN: Keep My laws. You shall not mate your livestock with animals of another kind. You shall not sow your field with different kinds of seed. You shall not wear a garment woven of two materials.

19:20  LA: Homo, si dormierit cum muliere coitu seminis, quæ sit ancilla etiam nubilis, et tamen pretio non redempta, nec libertate donata : vapulabunt ambo, et non morientur, quia non fuit libera.
     EN: If a man lies with a woman in intercourse with emission of seed, and she is a slave woman of marriageable age but has not been redeemed with money or granted freedom, both shall be beaten, but they shall not die, because she was not free.

19:21  LA: Pro delicto autem suo offeret Domino ad ostium tabernaculi testimonii arietem :
     EN: For his offense he shall bring the Lord a ram at the entrance of the tabernacle of the testimony.

19:22  LA: orabitque pro eo sacerdos, et pro peccato ejus coram Domino, et repropitiabitur ei, dimitteturque peccatum.
     EN: The priest shall pray for him and for his sin before the Lord. Favor shall be restored to him, and the sin shall be forgiven.

19:23  LA: Quando ingressi fueritis terram, et plantaveritis in ea ligna pomifera, auferetis præputia eorum : poma, quæ germinant, immunda erunt vobis, nec edetis ex eis.
     EN: When you enter the land and plant fruit trees in it, you shall remove their foreskins. The fruit they produce shall be unclean for you, and you shall not eat any of it.

19:24  LA: Quarto autem anno omnis fructus eorum sanctificabitur, laudabilis Domino.
     EN: In the fourth year all their fruit shall be consecrated as an offering of praise to the Lord.

19:25  LA: Quinto autem anno comedetis fructus, congregantes poma, quæ proferunt. Ego Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: In the fifth year you shall eat the fruit, gathering what they produce. I am the Lord your God.

19:26  LA: Non comedetis cum sanguine. Non augurabimini, nec observabitis somnia.
     EN: You shall not eat anything with blood. You shall not practice augury or observe dreams as omens.

19:27  LA: Neque in rotundum attondebitis comam, nec radetis barbam.
     EN: You shall not cut your hair in a circle or shave your beard.

19:28  LA: Et super mortuo non incidetis carnem vestram, neque figuras aliquas aut stigmata facietis vobis. Ego Dominus.
     EN: You shall not cut your flesh for the dead or make any figures or tattooed marks upon yourselves. I am the Lord.

19:29  LA: Ne prostituas filiam tuam, ne contaminetur terra et impleatur piaculo.
     EN: Do not prostitute your daughter, lest the land be defiled and filled with guilt.

19:30  LA: Sabbata mea custodite, et sanctuarium meum metuite. Ego Dominus.
     EN: Keep My sabbaths and revere My sanctuary. I am the Lord.

19:31  LA: Non declinetis ad magos, nec ab ariolis aliquid sciscitemini, ut polluamini per eos. Ego Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: Do not turn to magicians or inquire of diviners, and so become defiled through them. I am the Lord your God.

19:32  LA: Coram cano capite consurge, et honora personam senis : et time Dominum Deum tuum. Ego sum Dominus.
     EN: Rise before a gray head and honor the presence of an old person, and fear the Lord your God. I am the Lord.

19:33  LA: Si habitaverit advena in terra vestra, et moratus fuerit inter vos, non exprobretis ei :
     EN: If a foreigner lives in your land and remains among you, do not reproach him.

19:34  LA: sed sit inter vos quasi indigena, et diligetis eum quasi vosmetipsos : fuistis enim et vos advenæ in terra Ægypti. Ego Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: Let him be among you as one native-born, and love him as yourselves, for you too were foreigners in the land of Egypt. I am the Lord your God.

19:35  LA: Nolite facere iniquum aliquid in judicio, in regula, in pondere, in mensura.
     EN: You shall do nothing unjust in judgment, in a measuring rule, in weight, or in measure.

19:36  LA: Statera justa, et æqua sint pondera, justus modius, æquusque sextarius. Ego Dominus Deus vester, qui eduxi vos de terra Ægypti.
     EN: Let your balance be just, your weights fair, your modius correct, and your sextarius accurate. I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt.

19:37  LA: Custodite omnia præcepta mea, et universa judicia, et facite ea. Ego Dominus.
     EN: Keep all My commands and all My judgments, and carry them out. I am the Lord.

LEVITICUS 20

20:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

20:2  LA: Hæc loqueris filiis Israël : Homo de filiis Israël, et de advenis qui habitant in Israël, si quis dederit de semine suo idolo Moloch, morte moriatur : populus terræ lapidabit eum.
     EN: You shall tell the children of Israel: If any man of the children of Israel or of the foreigners dwelling in Israel gives any of his offspring to the idol Moloch, he shall surely be put to death. The people of the land shall stone him.

20:3  LA: Et ego ponam faciem meam contra illum : succidamque eum de medio populi sui, eo quod dederit de semine suo Moloch, et contaminaverit sanctuarium meum, ac polluerit nomen sanctum meum.
     EN: I will set My face against him and cut him off from among his people, because he has given his offspring to Moloch, defiled My sanctuary, and profaned My holy name.

20:4  LA: Quod si negligens populus terræ, et quasi parvipendens imperium meum, dimiserit hominem qui dedit de semine suo Moloch, nec voluerit eum occidere :
     EN: But if the people of the land are negligent and, as though despising My command, let the man who gave his offspring to Moloch go free and refuse to kill him,

20:5  LA: ponam faciem meam super hominem illum, et super cognationem ejus, succidamque et ipsum, et omnes qui consenserunt ei ut fornicarentur cum Moloch, de medio populi sui.
     EN: I will set My face against that man and his family. I will cut off from among their people both him and all who have agreed with him to prostitute themselves with Moloch.

20:6  LA: Anima, quæ declinaverit ad magos et ariolos, et fornicata fuerit cum eis, ponam faciem meam contra eam, et interficiam illam de medio populi sui.
     EN: If a person turns to magicians and diviners and prostitutes himself with them, I will set My face against that person and kill him from among his people.

20:7  LA: Sanctificamini et estote sancti, quia ego sum Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: Sanctify yourselves and be holy, because I am the Lord your God.

20:8  LA: Custodite præcepta mea, et facite ea : ego Dominus qui sanctifico vos.
     EN: Keep My commands and carry them out. I am the Lord, who sanctifies you.

20:9  LA: Qui maledixerit patri suo, aut matri, morte moriatur : patri matrique maledixit : sanguis ejus sit super eum.
     EN: Anyone who curses his father or mother shall surely be put to death. He has cursed his father or mother; his blood shall be upon him.

20:10  LA: Si mœchatus quis fuerit cum uxore alterius, et adulterium perpetraverit cum conjuge proximi sui, morte moriantur et mœchus et adultera.
     EN: If a man commits adultery with another man's wife and perpetrates adultery with his neighbor's spouse, both the adulterer and the adulteress shall surely be put to death.

20:11  LA: Qui dormierit cum noverca sua, et revelaverit ignominiam patris sui, morte moriantur ambo : sanguis eorum sit super eos.
     EN: If a man lies with his stepmother and exposes his father's shame, both shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.

20:12  LA: Si quis dormierit cum nuru sua, uterque moriatur, quia scelus operati sunt : sanguis eorum sit super eos.
     EN: If a man lies with his daughter-in-law, both shall die, because they have committed a wicked act; their blood shall be upon them.

20:13  LA: Qui dormierit cum masculo coitu femineo, uterque operatus est nefas : morte moriantur : sit sanguis eorum super eos.
     EN: If a man lies with a male as with a woman, both have committed a forbidden act. They shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.

20:14  LA: Qui supra uxorem filiam, duxerit matrem ejus, scelus operatus est : vivus ardebit cum eis, nec permanebit tantum nefas in medio vestri.
     EN: If a man, in addition to his wife, marries her mother, he has committed a wicked act. He shall be burned alive with them, so that so great a forbidden act does not remain among you.

20:15  LA: Qui cum jumento et pecore coierit, morte moriatur : pecus quoque occidite.
     EN: If a man has intercourse with a beast or livestock, he shall surely be put to death; kill the animal also.

20:16  LA: Mulier, quæ succubuerit cuilibet jumento, simul interficietur cum eo : sanguis eorum sit super eos.
     EN: A woman who lies beneath any animal shall be killed together with it; their blood shall be upon them.

20:17  LA: Qui acceperit sororem suam filiam patris sui, vel filiam matris suæ, et viderit turpitudinem ejus, illaque conspexerit fratris ignominiam, nefariam rem operati sunt : occidentur in conspectu populi sui, eo quod turpitudinem suam mutuo revelaverint, et portabunt iniquitatem suam.
     EN: If a man takes his sister, his father's daughter or his mother's daughter, and sees her nakedness while she sees her brother's shame, they have committed a forbidden act. They shall be killed in the sight of their people, because they have uncovered each other's nakedness, and they shall bear their iniquity.

20:18  LA: Qui coierit cum muliere in fluxu menstruo, et revelaverit turpitudinem ejus, ipsaque aperuerit fontem sanguinis sui, interficientur ambo de medio populi sui.
     EN: If a man has intercourse with a woman during her menstrual flow and uncovers her nakedness, and she opens the source of her blood, both shall be killed from among their people.

20:19  LA: Turpitudinem materteræ et amitæ tuæ non discooperies : qui hoc fecerit, ignominiam carnis suæ nudavit ; portabunt ambo iniquitatem suam.
     EN: You shall not uncover the nakedness of your mother's sister or your father's sister. Anyone who does this has exposed the shame of his own flesh; both shall bear their iniquity.

20:20  LA: Qui coierit cum uxore patrui vel avunculi sui, et revelaverit ignominiam cognationis suæ, portabunt ambo peccatum suum : absque liberis morientur.
     EN: If a man has intercourse with the wife of his paternal or maternal uncle and exposes the shame of his kin, both shall bear their sin; they shall die childless.

20:21  LA: Qui duxerit uxorem fratris sui, rem facit illicitam : turpitudinem fratris sui revelavit : absque liberis erunt.
     EN: If a man takes his brother's wife, he does what is unlawful. He has uncovered his brother's nakedness; they shall be childless.

20:22  LA: Custodite leges meas, atque judicia, et facite ea : ne et vos evomat terra quam intraturi estis et habitaturi.
     EN: Keep My laws and judgments and carry them out, so that the land you are about to enter and inhabit does not vomit you out also.

20:23  LA: Nolite ambulare in legitimis nationum, quas ego expulsurus sum ante vos. Omnia enim hæc fecerunt, et abominatus sum eas.
     EN: Do not walk in the ordinances of the nations that I am about to drive out before you. For they have done all these things, and I have abhorred them.

20:24  LA: Vobis autem loquor. Possidete terram eorum, quam dabo vobis in hæreditatem, terram fluentem lacte et melle. Ego Dominus Deus vester, qui separavi vos a ceteris populis.
     EN: But to you I say: Take possession of their land, which I will give you as an inheritance, a land flowing with milk and honey. I am the Lord your God, who has separated you from the other peoples.

20:25  LA: Separate ergo et vos jumentum mundum ab immundo, et avem mundam ab immunda : ne polluatis animas vestras in pecore, et avibus, et cunctis quæ moventur in terra, et quæ vobis ostendi esse polluta.
     EN: You too, therefore, shall separate the clean animal from the unclean and the clean bird from the unclean. Do not defile yourselves with livestock, birds, or any of the creatures that move upon the earth which I have shown you to be unclean.

20:26  LA: Eritis mihi sancti, quia sanctus sum ego Dominus, et separavi vos a ceteris populis, ut essetis mei.
     EN: You shall be holy to Me, because I, the Lord, am holy, and I have separated you from the other peoples to be Mine.

20:27  LA: Vir, sive mulier, in quibus pythonicus, vel divinationis fuerit spiritus, morte moriantur : lapidibus obruent eos : sanguis eorum sit super illos.
     EN: A man or woman who has a spirit of mediumship or divination shall surely be put to death. They shall stone them; their blood shall be upon them.

LEVITICUS 21

21:1  LA: Dixit quoque Dominus ad Moysen : Loquere ad sacerdotes filios Aaron, et dices ad eos : Ne contaminetur sacerdos in mortibus civium suorum,
     EN: The Lord also said to Moses: Speak to the priests, Aaron's sons, and tell them: A priest shall not defile himself through the death of any of his fellow citizens,

21:2  LA: nisi tantum in consanguineis, ac propinquis, id est, super patre et matre, et filio, et filia, fratre quoque,
     EN: except for his blood relatives and close kin, namely his father, mother, son, daughter, or brother,

21:3  LA: et sorore virgine quæ non est nupta viro :
     EN: and his virgin sister who has not married a man.

21:4  LA: sed nec in principe populi sui contaminabitur.
     EN: He shall not defile himself even for a ruler of his people.

21:5  LA: Non radent caput, nec barbam, neque in carnibus suis facient incisuras.
     EN: They shall not shave their heads or beards, or make cuts in their flesh.

21:6  LA: Sancti erunt Deo suo, et non polluent nomen ejus : incensum enim Domini, et panes Dei sui offerunt, et ideo sancti erunt.
     EN: They shall be holy to their God and shall not profane His name, for they present the Lord's offerings by fire and the bread of their God; therefore they shall be holy.

21:7  LA: Scortum et vile prostibulum non ducent uxorem, nec eam quæ repudiata est a marito : quia consecrati sunt Deo suo,
     EN: They shall not marry a prostitute or a woman degraded by prostitution, or a woman divorced by her husband, because they are consecrated to their God

21:8  LA: et panes propositionis offerunt. Sint ergo sancti, quia et ego sanctus sum, Dominus qui sanctifico eos.
     EN: and offer the bread of the Presence. Therefore let them be holy, because I, the Lord who sanctifies them, am holy.

21:9  LA: Sacerdotis filia si deprehensa fuerit in stupro, et violaverit nomen patris sui, flammis exuretur.
     EN: If a priest's daughter is found in sexual misconduct and dishonors her father's name, she shall be burned in the flames.

21:10  LA: Pontifex, id est, sacerdos maximus inter fratres suos, super cujus caput fusum est unctionis oleum, et cujus manus in sacerdotio consecratæ sunt, vestitusque est sanctis vestibus, caput suum non discooperiet, vestimenta non scindet :
     EN: The high priest, that is, the chief priest among his brothers, upon whose head the anointing oil has been poured, whose hands have been consecrated to the priesthood, and who has been clothed in holy garments, shall not uncover his head or tear his garments.

21:11  LA: et ad omnem mortuum non ingredietur omnino : super patre quoque suo et matre non contaminabitur.
     EN: He shall not enter where any dead person is, and shall not defile himself even for his father or mother.

21:12  LA: Nec egredietur de sanctis, ne polluat sanctuarium Domini, quia oleum sanctæ unctionis Dei sui super eum est. Ego Dominus.
     EN: He shall not leave the holy place, lest he profane the Lord's sanctuary, because the holy anointing oil of his God is upon him. I am the Lord.

21:13  LA: Virginem ducet uxorem :
     EN: He shall marry a virgin.

21:14  LA: viduam autem et repudiatam, et sordidam, atque meretricem non accipiet, sed puellam de populo suo :
     EN: He shall not take a widow, a divorced woman, a defiled woman, or a prostitute, but a maiden from his own people,

21:15  LA: ne commisceat stirpem generis sui vulgo gentis suæ : quia ego Dominus, qui sanctifico eum.
     EN: so that he does not mingle the offspring of his lineage with the common people of his nation; for I am the Lord, who sanctifies him.

21:16  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

21:17  LA: Loquere ad Aaron : Homo de semine tuo per familias qui habuerit maculam, non offeret panes Deo suo,
     EN: Speak to Aaron: No man among your descendants throughout their families who has a bodily blemish shall offer bread to his God

21:18  LA: nec accedet ad ministerium ejus : si cæcus fuerit, si claudus, si parvo vel grandi, vel torto naso,
     EN: or approach His service: whether he is blind or lame, has a small, large, or crooked nose,

21:19  LA: si fracto pede, si manu,
     EN: a broken foot or hand,

21:20  LA: si gibbus, si lippus, si albuginem habens in oculo, si jugem scabiem, si impetiginem in corpore, vel herniosus.
     EN: a curved back, inflamed eyes, a white spot in his eye, a persistent scaly condition, a skin eruption on his body, or a hernia.

21:21  LA: Omnis qui habuerit maculam de semine Aaron sacerdotis, non accedet offerre hostias Domino, nec panes Deo suo :
     EN: Anyone among the descendants of Aaron the priest who has a bodily blemish shall not approach to offer sacrificial victims to the Lord or bread to his God.

21:22  LA: vescetur tamen panibus qui offeruntur in sanctuario,
     EN: Nevertheless, he may eat the bread offered in the sanctuary,

21:23  LA: ita dumtaxat, ut intra velum non ingrediatur, nec accedat ad altare, quia maculam habet, et contaminare non debet sanctuarium meum. Ego Dominus qui sanctifico eos.
     EN: provided that he does not enter inside the veil or approach the altar, because he has a bodily blemish and must not defile My sanctuary. I am the Lord, who sanctifies them.

21:24  LA: Locutus est ergo Moyses ad Aaron, et ad filios ejus, et ad omnem Israël cuncta quæ fuerant sibi imperata.
     EN: Moses therefore spoke to Aaron, his sons, and all Israel everything that he had been commanded.

LEVITICUS 22

22:1  LA: Locutus quoque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord also spoke to Moses, saying:

22:2  LA: Loquere ad Aaron et ad filios ejus, ut caveant ab his quæ consecrata sunt filiorum Israël, et non contaminent nomen sanctificatorum mihi, quæ ipsi offerunt. Ego Dominus.
     EN: Speak to Aaron and his sons, that they take care concerning the things consecrated by the children of Israel and not profane the name of the things consecrated to Me which the people offer. I am the Lord.

22:3  LA: Dic ad eos, et ad posteros eorum : Omnis homo qui accesserit de stirpe vestra ad ea quæ consecrata sunt, et quæ obtulerunt filii Israël Domino, in quo est immunditia, peribit coram Domino. Ego sum Dominus.
     EN: Tell them and their descendants: Any man of your line who, while unclean, approaches the consecrated things that the children of Israel have offered to the Lord shall perish before the Lord. I am the Lord.

22:4  LA: Homo de semine Aaron, qui fuerit leprosus, aut patiens fluxum seminis, non vescetur de his quæ sanctificata sunt mihi, donec sanetur. Qui tetigerit immundum super mortuo, et ex quo egreditur semen quasi coitus,
     EN: A man among Aaron's descendants who has leprous disease or suffers a flow of semen shall not eat the things consecrated to Me until he is healed. Anyone who touches something unclean through contact with the dead, or from whom semen comes forth as in intercourse,

22:5  LA: et qui tangit reptile, et quodlibet immundum cujus tactus est sordidus,
     EN: or who touches a creeping creature or any unclean thing whose touch defiles,

22:6  LA: immundus erit usque ad vesperum, et non vescetur his quæ sanctificata sunt : sed cum laverit carnem suam aqua,
     EN: shall be unclean until evening and shall not eat the consecrated things. But when he has bathed his body in water

22:7  LA: et occubuerit sol, tunc mundatus vescetur de sanctificatis, quia cibus illius est.
     EN: and the sun has set, he shall then be clean and may eat the consecrated things, because they are his food.

22:8  LA: Morticinum et captum a bestia non comedent, nec polluentur in eis. Ego sum Dominus.
     EN: They shall not eat an animal that has died of itself or has been taken by a wild beast, nor defile themselves with these things. I am the Lord.

22:9  LA: Custodiant præcepta mea, ut non subjaceant peccato, et moriantur in sanctuario, cum polluerint illud. Ego Dominus qui sanctifico eos.
     EN: Let them keep My commands, so that they do not become liable for sin and die in the sanctuary by defiling it. I am the Lord, who sanctifies them.

22:10  LA: Omnis alienigena non comedet de sanctificatis ; inquilinus sacerdotis et mercenarius non vescentur ex eis.
     EN: No outsider shall eat the consecrated things; a priest's lodger and hired worker shall not eat them.

22:11  LA: Quem autem sacerdos emerit, et qui vernaculus domus ejus fuerit, his comedent ex eis.
     EN: But a person the priest has bought, or a slave born in his household, may eat them.

22:12  LA: Si filia sacerdotis cuilibet ex populo nupta fuerit, de his quæ sanctificata sunt, et de primitiis non vescetur.
     EN: If a priest's daughter marries any man from the people, she shall not eat the consecrated things or the firstfruits.

22:13  LA: Sin autem vidua, vel repudiata, et absque liberis reversa fuerit ad domum patris sui : sicut puella consueverat, aletur cibis patris sui. Omnis alienigena comedendi ex eis non habet potestatem.
     EN: But if she becomes widowed or divorced and, having no children, returns to her father's house, she shall be nourished with her father's food as she was accustomed to be as a girl. No outsider has the right to eat these things.

22:14  LA: Qui comederit de sanctificatis per ignorantiam, addet quintam partem cum eo quod comedit, et dabit sacerdoti in sanctuarium.
     EN: Anyone who eats the consecrated things through ignorance shall add a fifth to what he ate and give it to the priest for the sanctuary.

22:15  LA: Nec contaminabunt sanctificata filiorum Israël, quæ offerunt Domino :
     EN: They shall not defile the consecrated things that the children of Israel offer to the Lord,

22:16  LA: ne forte sustineant iniquitatem delicti sui, cum sanctificata comederint. Ego Dominus qui sanctifico eos.
     EN: lest they bear the iniquity of their offense when they eat the consecrated things. I am the Lord, who sanctifies them.

22:17  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

22:18  LA: Loquere ad Aaron et filios ejus, et ad omnes filios Israël, dicesque ad eos : Homo de domo Israël, et de advenis qui habitant apud vos, qui obtulerit oblationem suam, vel vota solvens, vel sponte offerens, quidquid illud obtulerit in holocaustum Domini,
     EN: Speak to Aaron, his sons, and all the children of Israel, and tell them: If a man of the house of Israel or of the foreigners dwelling among you brings his offering, whether fulfilling a vow or offering freely, whatever he brings as a whole burnt offering to the Lord,

22:19  LA: ut offeratur per vos, masculus immaculatus erit ex bobus, et ovibus, et ex capris :
     EN: to be offered by you it shall be an unblemished male from the cattle, sheep, or goats.

22:20  LA: si maculam habuerit, non offeretis, neque erit acceptabile.
     EN: If it has a blemish, you shall not offer it, and it shall not be acceptable.

22:21  LA: Homo qui obtulerit victimam pacificorum Domino, vel vota solvens, vel sponte offerens, tam de bobus quam de ovibus, immaculatum offeret ut acceptabile sit : omnis macula non erit in eo.
     EN: A man who offers a peace offering to the Lord, whether fulfilling a vow or offering freely, from either the cattle or the sheep, shall offer an unblemished animal so that it may be acceptable. There shall be no blemish in it.

22:22  LA: Si cæcum fuerit, si fractum, si cicatricem habens, si papulas, aut scabiem, aut impetiginem : non offeretis ea Domino, nec adolebitis ex eis super altare Domini.
     EN: If it is blind, injured, scarred, or has swellings, a scaly condition, or a skin eruption, you shall not offer such animals to the Lord or burn any part of them upon the Lord's altar.

22:23  LA: Bovem et ovem, aure et cauda amputatis, voluntarie offerre potes, votum autem ex eis solvi non potest.
     EN: You may offer an ox or a sheep with an ear or tail cut off as a freewill offering, but a vow cannot be fulfilled with such animals.

22:24  LA: Omne animal, quod vel contritis, vel tusis, vel sectis ablatisque testiculis est, non offeretis Domino, et in terra vestra hoc omnino ne faciatis.
     EN: You shall not offer the Lord any animal whose testicles have been crushed, bruised, cut, or removed. You shall not do this at all in your land.

22:25  LA: De manu alienigenæ non offeretis panes Deo vestro, et quidquid aliud dare voluerit, quia corrupta, et maculata sunt omnia : non suscipietis ea.
     EN: You shall not offer bread to your God from a foreigner's hand, or whatever else he wishes to give, for all these are damaged and blemished; you shall not accept them.

22:26  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

22:27  LA: Bos, ovis et capra, cum genita fuerint, septem diebus erunt sub ubere matris suæ : die autem octavo, et deinceps, offerri poterunt Domino.
     EN: When an ox, a sheep, or a goat is born, it shall remain at its mother's udder for seven days. From the eighth day onward it may be offered to the Lord.

22:28  LA: Sive illa bos, sive ovis, non immolabuntur una die cum fœtibus suis.
     EN: Whether a cow or a ewe, it shall not be slaughtered on the same day as its young.

22:29  LA: Si immolaveritis hostiam pro gratiarum actione Domino, ut possit esse placabilis,
     EN: If you sacrifice a thanksgiving offering to the Lord to obtain His favor,

22:30  LA: eodem die comedetis eam : non remanebit quidquam in mane alterius diei. Ego Dominus.
     EN: you shall eat it that same day. Nothing shall remain until the following morning. I am the Lord.

22:31  LA: Custodite mandata mea, et facite ea. Ego Dominus.
     EN: Keep My commandments and carry them out. I am the Lord.

22:32  LA: Ne polluatis nomen meum sanctum, ut sanctificer in medio filiorum Israël. Ego Dominus qui sanctifico vos,
     EN: Do not profane My holy name, so that I may be shown holy among the children of Israel. I am the Lord, who sanctifies you

22:33  LA: et eduxi de terra Ægypti, ut essem vobis in Deum. Ego Dominus.
     EN: and brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God. I am the Lord.

LEVITICUS 23

23:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

23:2  LA: Loquere filiis Israël, et dices ad eos : Hæ sunt feriæ Domini, quas vocabitis sanctas.
     EN: Speak to the children of Israel and tell them: These are the Lord's appointed feasts, which you shall proclaim holy.

23:3  LA: Sex diebus facietis opus : dies septimus, quia sabbati requies est, vocabitur sanctus : omne opus non facietis in eo : sabbatum Domini est in cunctis habitationibus vestris.
     EN: For six days you shall work. The seventh day, because it is the sabbath rest, shall be called holy. You shall do no work on it; it is the Lord's sabbath in all your dwellings.

23:4  LA: Hæ sunt ergo feriæ Domini sanctæ, quas celebrare debetis temporibus suis.
     EN: These, then, are the Lord's holy feasts, which you must celebrate at their appointed times.

23:5  LA: Mense primo, quartadecima die mensis ad vesperum, Phase Domini est :
     EN: In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month at evening, is the Lord's Passover.

23:6  LA: et quintadecima die mensis hujus, solemnitas azymorum Domini est. Septem diebus azyma comedetis.
     EN: On the fifteenth day of that month is the Lord's solemn feast of Unleavened Bread. For seven days you shall eat unleavened bread.

23:7  LA: Dies primus erit vobis celeberrimus, sanctusque : omne opus servile non facietis in eo,
     EN: The first day shall be a day of great celebration and holy to you; you shall do no servile work on it.

23:8  LA: sed offeretis sacrificium in igne Domino septem diebus. Dies autem septimus erit celebrior et sanctior : nullumque servile opus facietis in eo.
     EN: For seven days you shall offer the Lord a sacrifice by fire. The seventh day shall be a day of greater celebration and holiness; you shall do no servile work on it.

23:9  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

23:10  LA: Loquere filiis Israël, et dices ad eos : Cum ingressi fueritis terram, quam ego dabo vobis, et messueritis segetem, feretis manipulos spicarum, primitias messis vestræ, ad sacerdotem :
     EN: Speak to the children of Israel and tell them: When you enter the land that I will give you and reap its crop, you shall bring sheaves of grain, the firstfruits of your harvest, to the priest.

23:11  LA: qui elevabit fasciculum coram Domino, ut acceptabile sit pro vobis, altero die sabbati, et sanctificabit illum.
     EN: On the day after the sabbath he shall lift up the sheaf before the Lord, so that it may be accepted on your behalf, and shall consecrate it.

23:12  LA: Atque in eodem die quo manipulus consecratur, cædetur agnus immaculatus anniculus in holocaustum Domini.
     EN: On the same day that the sheaf is consecrated, an unblemished year-old lamb shall be slaughtered as a whole burnt offering to the Lord.

23:13  LA: Et libamenta offerentur cum eo, duæ decimæ similæ conspersæ oleo in incensum Domini, odoremque suavissimum : liba quoque vini, quarta pars hin.
     EN: With it shall be offered accompanying offerings: two tenths of fine flour mixed with oil as an offering by fire and a most pleasing fragrance to the Lord, and a drink offering of wine, a quarter of a hin.

23:14  LA: Panem, et polentam, et pultes non comedetis ex segete, usque ad diem qua offeretis ex ea Deo vestro. Præceptum est sempiternum in generationibus, cunctisque habitaculis vestris.
     EN: You shall not eat bread, roasted meal, or porridge from the crop until the day when you offer some of it to your God. This is a perpetual command throughout your generations and in all your dwellings.

23:15  LA: Numerabitis ergo ab altero die sabbati, in quo obtulistis manipulum primitiarum, septem hebdomadas plenas,
     EN: From the day after the sabbath, on which you offered the sheaf of firstfruits, you shall count seven full weeks,

23:16  LA: usque ad alteram diem expletionis hebdomadæ septimæ, id est, quinquaginta dies : et sic offeretis sacrificium novum Domino
     EN: until the day after the completion of the seventh week, that is, fifty days. Then you shall offer the Lord a new sacrifice

23:17  LA: ex omnibus habitaculis vestris, panes primitiarum duos de duabus decimis similæ fermentatæ, quos coquetis in primitias Domini.
     EN: from all your dwellings: two firstfruits loaves made from two tenths of leavened fine flour, which you shall bake as firstfruits for the Lord.

23:18  LA: Offeretisque cum panibus septem agnos immaculatos anniculos, et vitulum de armento unum, et arietes duos, et erunt in holocaustum cum libamentis suis, in odorem suavissimum Domini.
     EN: With the loaves you shall offer seven unblemished year-old lambs, one calf from the herd, and two rams. They shall be a whole burnt offering with their accompanying offerings, as a most pleasing fragrance to the Lord.

23:19  LA: Facietis et hircum pro peccato, duosque agnos anniculos hostias pacificorum.
     EN: You shall also offer a male goat for sin and two year-old lambs as peace offerings.

23:20  LA: Cumque elevaverit eos sacerdos cum panibus primitiarum coram Domino, cedent in usum ejus.
     EN: When the priest has lifted them up before the Lord with the firstfruits loaves, they shall fall to his use.

23:21  LA: Et vocabitis hunc diem celeberrimum, atque sanctissimum : omne opus servile non facietis in eo. Legitimum sempiternum erit in cunctis habitaculis, et generationibus vestris.
     EN: You shall proclaim this day a day of great celebration and most holy. You shall do no servile work on it. This shall be a perpetual ordinance in all your dwellings and throughout your generations.

23:22  LA: Postquam autem messueritis segetem terræ vestræ, nec secabitis eam usque ad solum, nec remanentes spicas colligetis : sed pauperibus et peregrinis dimittetis eas. Ego sum Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: When you reap the crop of your land, you shall not cut it bare to the ground or gather the ears of grain left behind; you shall leave them for the poor and foreigners. I am the Lord your God.

23:23  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

23:24  LA: Loquere filiis Israël : Mense septimo, prima die mensis, erit vobis sabbatum, memoriale, clangentibus tubis, et vocabitur sanctum :
     EN: Speak to the children of Israel: In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, you shall have a sabbath, a memorial with trumpets sounding, and it shall be called holy.

23:25  LA: omne opus servile non facietis in eo, et offeretis holocaustum Domino.
     EN: You shall do no servile work on it, and you shall offer the Lord a whole burnt offering.

23:26  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

23:27  LA: Decimo die mensis hujus septimi, dies expiationum erit celeberrimus, et vocabitur sanctus : affligetisque animas vestras in eo, et offeretis holocaustum Domino.
     EN: On the tenth day of this seventh month, the Day of Expiation shall be a day of great observance and shall be called holy. You shall afflict yourselves on it and offer the Lord a whole burnt offering.

23:28  LA: Omne opus servile non facietis in tempore diei hujus : quia dies propitiationis est, ut propitietur vobis Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: You shall do no servile work during this day, because it is a day of propitiation, so that the Lord your God may be gracious to you.

23:29  LA: Omnis anima, quæ afflicta non fuerit die hac, peribit de populis suis :
     EN: Every person who does not afflict himself on this day shall perish from among his people.

23:30  LA: et quæ operis quippiam fecerit, delebo eam de populo suo.
     EN: Any person who does any work, I will blot out from among his people.

23:31  LA: Nihil ergo operis facietis in eo : legitimum sempiternum erit vobis in cunctis generationibus, et habitationibus vestris.
     EN: Therefore you shall do no work on it. This shall be a perpetual ordinance for you throughout all your generations and in all your dwellings.

23:32  LA: Sabbatum requietionis est, et affligetis animas vestras die nono mensis : a vespera usque ad vesperam celebrabitis sabbata vestra.
     EN: It is a sabbath of rest, and you shall afflict yourselves on the ninth day of the month. From evening until evening you shall observe your sabbaths.

23:33  LA: Et locutus est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

23:34  LA: Loquere filiis Israël : A quintodecimo die mensis hujus septimi, erunt feriæ tabernaculorum septem diebus Domino.
     EN: Speak to the children of Israel: From the fifteenth day of this seventh month, there shall be the Feast of Tabernacles for seven days to the Lord.

23:35  LA: Dies primus vocabitur celeberrimus atque sanctissimus : omne opus servile non facietis in eo.
     EN: The first day shall be called a day of great celebration and most holy. You shall do no servile work on it.

23:36  LA: Et septem diebus offeretis holocausta Domino. Dies quoque octavus erit celeberrimus, atque sanctissimus, et offeretis holocaustum Domino : est enim cœtus atque collectæ : omne opus servile non facietis in eo.
     EN: For seven days you shall offer whole burnt offerings to the Lord. The eighth day also shall be a day of great celebration and most holy, and you shall offer a whole burnt offering to the Lord, for it is a gathering and an assembly. You shall do no servile work on it.

23:37  LA: Hæ sunt feriæ Domini, quas vocabitis celeberrimas atque sanctissimas, offeretisque in eis oblationes Domino, holocausta et libamenta juxta ritum uniuscujusque diei :
     EN: These are the Lord's feasts, which you shall proclaim as days of great celebration and most holy. On them you shall bring offerings to the Lord, whole burnt offerings and accompanying offerings according to the rite of each day,

23:38  LA: exceptis sabbatis Domini, donisque vestris, et quæ offeretis ex voto, vel quæ sponte tribuetis Domino.
     EN: in addition to the Lord's sabbaths, your gifts, the offerings you make in fulfillment of vows, and what you freely give to the Lord.

23:39  LA: A quintodecimo ergo die mensis septimi, quando congregaveritis omnes fructus terræ vestræ, celebrabitis ferias Domini septem diebus : die primo et die octavo erit sabbatum, id est, requies.
     EN: From the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when you have gathered all the produce of your land, you shall celebrate the Lord's feast for seven days. On the first day and the eighth day there shall be a sabbath, that is, rest.

23:40  LA: Sumetisque vobis die primo fructus arboris pulcherrimæ, spatulasque palmarum, et ramos ligni densarum frondium, et salices de torrente, et lætabimini coram Domino Deo vestro.
     EN: On the first day you shall take the fruit of a most beautiful tree, palm fronds, branches of thickly leaved trees, and willows from the stream, and rejoice before the Lord your God.

23:41  LA: Celebrabitisque solemnitatem ejus septem diebus per annum : legitimum sempiternum erit in generationibus vestris. Mense septimo festa celebrabitis,
     EN: You shall celebrate His solemn feast for seven days each year. It shall be a perpetual ordinance throughout your generations. In the seventh month you shall celebrate the feast,

23:42  LA: et habitabitis in umbraculis septem diebus : omnis, qui de genere est Israël, manebit in tabernaculis,
     EN: and live in shelters for seven days. Everyone of Israel's line shall stay in tabernacles,

23:43  LA: ut discant posteri vestri quod in tabernaculis habitare fecerim filios Israël, cum educerem eos de terra Ægypti. Ego Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: so that your descendants may learn that I made the children of Israel live in tabernacles when I brought them out of the land of Egypt. I am the Lord your God.

23:44  LA: Locutusque est Moyses super solemnitatibus Domini ad filios Israël.
     EN: Moses spoke to the children of Israel concerning the Lord's solemn feasts.

LEVITICUS 24

24:1  LA: Et locutus est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

24:2  LA: Præcipe filiis Israël, ut afferant tibi oleum de olivis purissimum, ac lucidum, ad concinnandas lucernas jugiter,
     EN: Command the children of Israel to bring you the purest, clearest olive oil to keep the lamps in order continually,

24:3  LA: extra velum testimonii in tabernaculo fœderis. Ponetque eas Aaron a vespere usque ad mane coram Domino, cultu rituque perpetuo in generationibus vestris.
     EN: outside the veil of the testimony in the tabernacle of the covenant. Aaron shall set them before the Lord from evening until morning, as a perpetual observance and rite throughout your generations.

24:4  LA: Super candelabrum mundissimum ponentur semper in conspectu Domini.
     EN: They shall always be set upon the most pure lampstand in the Lord's sight.

24:5  LA: Accipies quoque similam, et coques ex ea duodecim panes, qui singuli habebunt duas decimas :
     EN: You shall also take fine flour and bake twelve loaves from it, each containing two tenths.

24:6  LA: quorum senos altrinsecus super mensam purissimam coram Domino statues :
     EN: You shall place them in two rows of six upon the most pure table before the Lord.

24:7  LA: et pones super eos thus lucidissimum, ut sit panis in monimentum oblationis Domini.
     EN: Upon them you shall put the clearest frankincense, so that the bread may be a memorial of the Lord's offering.

24:8  LA: Per singula sabbata mutabuntur coram Domino suscepti a filiis Israël fœdere sempiterno :
     EN: Every sabbath they shall be replaced before the Lord, received from the children of Israel as a perpetual covenant.

24:9  LA: eruntque Aaron et filiorum ejus, ut comedant eos in loco sancto : quia Sanctum sanctorum est de sacrificiis Domini jure perpetuo.
     EN: They shall belong to Aaron and his sons, who shall eat them in a holy place, because they are a most holy portion of the Lord's sacrifices by a perpetual ordinance.

24:10  LA: Ecce autem egressus filius mulieris Israëlitidis, quem pepererat de viro ægyptio inter filios Israël, jurgatus est in castris cum viro Israëlita.
     EN: Now the son of an Israelite woman, whom she had borne to an Egyptian man, went out among the children of Israel and quarreled in the camp with an Israelite man.

24:11  LA: Cumque blasphemasset nomen, et maledixisset ei, adductus est ad Moysen. (Vocabatur autem mater ejus Salumith, filia Dabri de tribu Dan.)
     EN: When he blasphemed the Name and cursed it, he was brought to Moses. His mother's name was Shelomith, daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.

24:12  LA: Miseruntque eum in carcerem, donec nossent quid juberet Dominus.
     EN: They put him in custody until they learned what the Lord would command.

24:13  LA: Qui locutus est ad Moysen,
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses,

24:14  LA: dicens : Educ blasphemum extra castra, et ponant omnes qui audierunt, manus suas super caput ejus, et lapidet eum populus universus.
     EN: saying: Bring the blasphemer outside the camp. Let all who heard him lay their hands upon his head, and let the whole people stone him.

24:15  LA: Et ad filios Israël loqueris : Homo, qui maledixerit Deo suo, portabit peccatum suum ;
     EN: You shall tell the children of Israel: A person who curses his God shall bear his sin.

24:16  LA: et qui blasphemaverit nomen Domini, morte moriatur : lapidibus opprimet eum omnis multitudo, sive ille civis, sive peregrinus fuerit. Qui blasphemaverit nomen Domini, morte moriatur.
     EN: Anyone who blasphemes the Lord's name shall surely be put to death. The whole congregation shall overwhelm him with stones, whether he is a citizen or a foreigner. Anyone who blasphemes the Lord's name shall surely be put to death.

24:17  LA: Qui percusserit, et occiderit hominem, morte moriatur.
     EN: Anyone who strikes and kills a human being shall surely be put to death.

24:18  LA: Qui percusserit animal, reddet vicarium, id est, animam pro anima.
     EN: Anyone who strikes an animal shall give a replacement, that is, life for life.

24:19  LA: Qui irrogaverit maculam cuilibet civium suorum, sicut fecit, sic fiet ei :
     EN: If a person inflicts an injury upon any of his fellow citizens, as he has done, so shall it be done to him.

24:20  LA: fracturam pro fractura, oculum pro oculo, dentem pro dente restituet : qualem inflixerit maculam, talem sustinere cogetur.
     EN: He shall render fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth. Whatever injury he has inflicted, he shall be compelled to suffer the same.

24:21  LA: Qui percusserit jumentum, reddet aliud. Qui percusserit hominem, punietur.
     EN: Anyone who strikes an animal shall give another. Anyone who strikes a human being shall be punished.

24:22  LA: Æquum judicium sit inter vos, sive peregrinus, sive civis peccaverit : quia ego sum Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: Let there be equal judgment among you, whether a foreigner or a citizen has sinned, because I am the Lord your God.

24:23  LA: Locutusque est Moyses ad filios Israël : et eduxerunt eum, qui blasphemaverat, extra castra, ac lapidibus oppresserunt. Feceruntque filii Israël sicut præceperat Dominus Moysi.
     EN: Moses spoke to the children of Israel. They brought the man who had blasphemed outside the camp and overwhelmed him with stones. The children of Israel did as the Lord had commanded Moses.

LEVITICUS 25

25:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen in monte Sinai, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai, saying:

25:2  LA: Loquere filiis Israël, et dices ad eos : Quando ingressi fueritis terram quam ego dabo vobis, sabbatizes sabbatum Domino.
     EN: Speak to the children of Israel and tell them: When you enter the land that I will give you, you shall keep a sabbath to the Lord.

25:3  LA: Sex annis seres agrum tuum, et sex annis putabis vineam tuam, colligesque fructus ejus :
     EN: For six years you shall sow your field, and for six years prune your vineyard and gather its produce.

25:4  LA: septimo autem anno sabbatum erit terræ, requietionis Domini : agrum non seres, et vineam non putabis.
     EN: But in the seventh year the land shall have a sabbath, a rest to the Lord. You shall not sow your field or prune your vineyard.

25:5  LA: Quæ sponte gignet humus, non metes : et uvas primitiarum tuarum non colliges quasi vindemiam : annus enim requietionis terræ est :
     EN: You shall not reap what the ground produces of itself or gather your first grapes as a vintage, for it is a year of rest for the land.

25:6  LA: sed erunt vobis in cibum, tibi et servo tuo, ancillæ et mercenario tuo, et advenæ qui peregrinantur apud te :
     EN: But its produce shall be food for you, for your male and female slaves, your hired worker, and the foreigner residing with you.

25:7  LA: jumentis tuis et pecoribus, omnia quæ nascuntur præbebunt cibum.
     EN: Everything that grows shall also provide food for your beasts and livestock.

25:8  LA: Numerabis quoque tibi septem hebdomadas annorum, id est, septies septem, quæ simul faciunt annos quadraginta novem :
     EN: You shall count seven weeks of years, that is, seven times seven, which together make forty-nine years.

25:9  LA: et clanges buccina mense septimo, decima die mensis, propitiationis tempore, in universa terra vestra.
     EN: In the seventh month, on the tenth day of the month, at the time of propitiation, you shall sound the horn throughout your land.

25:10  LA: Sanctificabisque annum quinquagesimum, et vocabis remissionem cunctis habitatoribus terræ tuæ : ipse est enim jubilæus. Revertetur homo ad possessionem suam, et unusquisque rediet ad familiam pristinam :
     EN: You shall sanctify the fiftieth year and proclaim release to all the inhabitants of your land, for it is the jubilee. Each person shall return to his possession, and everyone shall return to his original family,

25:11  LA: quia jubilæus est, et quinquagesimus annus. Non seretis neque metetis sponte in agro nascentia, et primitias vindemiæ non colligetis,
     EN: because it is the jubilee and the fiftieth year. You shall not sow or reap what grows of itself in the field, or gather the firstfruits of the vintage,

25:12  LA: ob sanctificationem jubilæi : sed statim oblata comedetis.
     EN: because of the jubilee's holiness. Instead, you shall eat what the land provides directly.

25:13  LA: Anno jubilæi, redient omnes ad possessiones suas.
     EN: In the jubilee year all shall return to their possessions.

25:14  LA: Quando vendes quippiam civi tuo, vel emes ab eo, ne contristes fratrem tuum, sed juxta numerum annorum jubilæi emes ab eo,
     EN: When you sell anything to your fellow citizen or buy from him, do not wrong your brother; you shall buy from him according to the number of years reckoned from the jubilee,

25:15  LA: et juxta supputationem frugum vendet tibi.
     EN: and he shall sell to you according to the reckoning of harvests.

25:16  LA: Quanto plures anni remanserint post jubilæum, tanto crescet et pretium : et quanto minus temporis numeraveris, tanto minoris et emptio constabit : tempus enim frugum vendet tibi.
     EN: The more years remain following a jubilee, the higher the price shall be; the less time you count, the lower the purchase price shall be, for he is selling you a period of harvests.

25:17  LA: Nolite affligere contribules vestros, sed timeat unusquisque Deum suum, quia ego Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: Do not oppress your fellow tribesmen, but let each one fear his God, because I am the Lord your God.

25:18  LA: Facite præcepta mea, et judicia custodite, et implete ea : ut habitare possitis in terra absque ullo pavore,
     EN: Carry out My commands, keep My judgments, and fulfill them, so that you may live in the land without any fear,

25:19  LA: et gignat vobis humus fructus suos, quibus vescamini usque ad saturitatem, nullius impetum formidantes.
     EN: and the ground may produce its fruit for you, which you shall eat until satisfied, fearing no one's attack.

25:20  LA: Quod si dixeritis : Quid comedemus anno septimo, si non severimus, neque collegerimus fruges nostras ?
     EN: If you say: What shall we eat in the seventh year if we do not sow or gather our crops?

25:21  LA: dabo benedictionem meam vobis anno sexto, et faciet fructus trium annorum :
     EN: I will give you My blessing in the sixth year, and it shall produce fruit for three years.

25:22  LA: seretisque anno octavo, et comedetis veteres fruges usque ad nonum annum : donec nova nascantur, edetis vetera.
     EN: You shall sow in the eighth year and eat the old produce until the ninth year. Until the new produce grows, you shall eat the old.

25:23  LA: Terra quoque non vendetur in perpetuum, quia mea est, et vos advenæ et coloni mei estis :
     EN: The land shall not be sold in perpetuity, because it is Mine, and you are foreigners and settlers with Me.

25:24  LA: unde cuncta regio possessionis vestræ sub redemptionis conditione vendetur.
     EN: Therefore all the land you possess shall be sold subject to redemption.

25:25  LA: Si attenuatus frater tuus vendiderit possessiunculam suam, et voluerit propinquus ejus, potest redimere quod ille vendiderat.
     EN: If your brother becomes poor and sells his small holding, and his relative is willing, he may redeem what his brother sold.

25:26  LA: Sin autem non habuerit proximum, et ipse pretium ad redimendum potuerit invenire,
     EN: But if he has no close relative and can himself find the price of redemption,

25:27  LA: computabuntur fructus ex eo tempore quo vendidit : et quod reliquum est, reddet emptori, sicque recipiet possessionem suam.
     EN: the harvests from the time of the sale shall be reckoned. He shall repay the balance to the buyer and thus regain his possession.

25:28  LA: Quod si non invenerit manus ejus ut reddat pretium, habebit emptor quod emerat, usque ad annum jubilæum. In ipso enim omnis venditio redibit ad dominum et ad possessorem pristinum.
     EN: But if his means do not allow him to repay the price, the buyer shall keep what he bought until the jubilee year. In that year every property sold shall return to its original owner and possessor.

25:29  LA: Qui vendiderit domum intra urbis muros, habebit licentiam redimendi, donec unus impleatur annus.
     EN: Anyone who sells a house inside a city's walls shall have the right to redeem it until one full year has passed.

25:30  LA: Si non redemerit, et anni circulus fuerit evolutus, emptor possidebit eam, et posteri ejus in perpetuum, et redimi non poterit, etiam in jubilæo.
     EN: If he does not redeem it and the year's cycle has elapsed, the buyer and his descendants shall possess it in perpetuity, and it cannot be redeemed even in the jubilee.

25:31  LA: Sin autem in villa domus, quæ muros non habet, agrorum jure vendetur : si ante redempta non fuerit, in jubilæo revertetur ad dominum.
     EN: But if the house is in a village that has no walls, it shall be sold under the law governing fields. If it has not been redeemed earlier, it shall return to its owner in the jubilee.

25:32  LA: Ædes Levitarum quæ in urbibus sunt, semper possunt redimi :
     EN: The houses of the Levites in their cities may always be redeemed.

25:33  LA: si redemptæ non fuerint, in jubilæo revertentur ad dominos, quia domus urbium Levitarum pro possessionibus sunt inter filios Israël.
     EN: If they have not been redeemed, they shall return to their owners in the jubilee, because the houses in the Levites' cities are their possessions among the children of Israel.

25:34  LA: Suburbana autem eorum non veneant, quia possessio sempiterna est.
     EN: But their surrounding lands shall not be sold, because they are a perpetual possession.

25:35  LA: Si attenuatus fuerit frater tuus, et infirmus manu, et susceperis eum quasi advenam et peregrinum, et vixerit tecum,
     EN: If your brother becomes poor and his means fail, and you receive him as a foreigner and a sojourner to live with you,

25:36  LA: ne accipias usuras ab eo, nec amplius quam dedisti : time Deum tuum, ut vivere possit frater tuus apud te.
     EN: you shall not take interest from him or more than you gave. Fear your God, so that your brother may live with you.

25:37  LA: Pecuniam tuam non dabis ei ad usuram, et frugum superabundantiam non exiges.
     EN: You shall not lend him your money at interest or demand an increase on produce.

25:38  LA: Ego Dominus Deus vester, qui eduxi vos de terra Ægypti, ut darem vobis terram Chanaan, et essem vester Deus.
     EN: I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan and to be your God.

25:39  LA: Si paupertate compulsus vendiderit se tibi frater tuus, non eum opprimes servitute famulorum,
     EN: If your brother, compelled by poverty, sells himself to you, you shall not oppress him with the servitude of slaves.

25:40  LA: sed quasi mercenarius et colonus erit : usque ad annum jubilæum operabitur apud te,
     EN: He shall be with you as a hired worker and a settler, and shall work with you until the jubilee year.

25:41  LA: et postea egredietur cum liberis suis, et revertetur ad cognationem, ad possessionem patrum suorum.
     EN: Afterward he shall go out with his children and return to his kindred and the possession of his fathers.

25:42  LA: Mei enim servi sunt, et ego eduxi eos de terra Ægypti : non veneant conditione servorum :
     EN: For they are My servants, and I brought them out of the land of Egypt. They shall not be sold under the conditions of slaves.

25:43  LA: ne affligas eum per potentiam, sed metuito Deum tuum.
     EN: Do not use your power to oppress him, but fear your God.

25:44  LA: Servus et ancilla sint vobis de nationibus quæ in circuitu vestro sunt :
     EN: Your male and female slaves shall be from the nations around you.

25:45  LA: et de advenis qui peregrinantur apud vos, vel qui ex his nati fuerint in terra vestra, hos habebitis famulos :
     EN: You may also have slaves from the foreigners residing among you or from those born to them in your land.

25:46  LA: et hæreditario jure transmittetis ad posteros, ac possidebitis in æternum : fratres autem vestros filios Israël ne opprimatis per potentiam.
     EN: You shall pass them on to your descendants by hereditary right and possess them in perpetuity. But do not use your power to oppress your brothers, the children of Israel.

25:47  LA: Si invaluerit apud vos manus advenæ atque peregrini, et attenuatus frater tuus vendiderit se ei, aut cuiquam de stirpe ejus :
     EN: If a foreigner or sojourner among you prospers and your brother becomes poor and sells himself to him or to anyone of his family,

25:48  LA: post venditionem potest redimi. Qui voluerit ex fratribus suis, redimet eum,
     EN: he may be redeemed after the sale. Any of his brothers who wishes may redeem him,

25:49  LA: et patruus, et patruelis, et consanguineus, et affinis. Sin autem et ipse potuerit, redimet se,
     EN: as may his paternal uncle, his paternal cousin, a blood relative, or a relative by marriage. If he is able, he may redeem himself,

25:50  LA: supputatis dumtaxat annis a tempore venditionis suæ usque ad annum jubilæum : et pecunia, qua venditus fuerat, juxta annorum numerum, et rationem mercenarii supputata.
     EN: provided that the years from the time of his sale to the jubilee year are counted, and the money for which he was sold is reckoned according to the number of years and the rate for a hired worker.

25:51  LA: Si plures fuerint anni qui remanent usque ad jubilæum, secundum hos reddet et pretium :
     EN: If many years remain until the jubilee, he shall repay the price in proportion to them.

25:52  LA: si pauci, ponet rationem cum eo juxta annorum numerum, et reddet emptori quod reliquum est annorum,
     EN: If few remain, he shall settle accounts with the buyer according to the number of years and repay him for the years remaining,

25:53  LA: quibus ante servivit mercedibus imputatis : non affliget eum violenter in conspectu tuo.
     EN: crediting the wages for the years he has already served. The buyer shall not oppress him by force in your sight.

25:54  LA: Quod si per hæc redimi non potuerit, anno jubilæo egredietur cum liberis suis.
     EN: If he cannot be redeemed by these means, he shall go out with his children in the jubilee year.

25:55  LA: Mei enim sunt servi filii Israël, quos eduxi de terra Ægypti.
     EN: For the children of Israel are My servants, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt.

LEVITICUS 26

26:1  LA: Ego Dominus Deus vester : non facietis vobis idolum, et sculptile, nec titulos erigetis, nec insignem lapidem ponetis in terra vestra, ut adoretis eum. Ego enim sum Dominus Deus vester.
     EN: I am the Lord your God. You shall not make an idol or a carved image for yourselves, erect pillars, or set an engraved stone in your land to worship it, for I am the Lord your God.

26:2  LA: Custodite sabbata mea, et pavete ad sanctuarium meum. Ego Dominus.
     EN: Keep My sabbaths and stand in awe of My sanctuary. I am the Lord.

26:3  LA: Si in præceptis meis ambulaveritis, et mandata mea custodieritis, et feceritis ea, dabo vobis pluvias temporibus suis,
     EN: If you walk in My commands, keep My commandments, and carry them out, I will give you rain at its proper times,

26:4  LA: et terra gignet germen suum, et pomis arbores replebuntur.
     EN: and the land shall produce its growth, and the trees shall be filled with fruit.

26:5  LA: Apprehendet messium tritura vindemiam, et vindemia occupabit sementem : et comedetis panem vestrum in saturitate, et absque pavore habitabitis in terra vestra.
     EN: The threshing of your harvest shall reach the vintage, and the vintage shall overtake the sowing. You shall eat your bread until satisfied and live without fear in your land.

26:6  LA: Dabo pacem in finibus vestris : dormietis, et non erit qui exterreat. Auferam malas bestias, et gladius non transibit terminos vestros.
     EN: I will give peace within your borders. You shall sleep, and there shall be no one to terrify you. I will remove harmful beasts, and the sword shall not pass through your territory.

26:7  LA: Persequemini inimicos vestros, et corruent coram vobis.
     EN: You shall pursue your enemies, and they shall fall before you.

26:8  LA: Persequentur quinque de vestris centum alienos, et centum de vobis decem millia : cadent inimici vestri gladio in conspectu vestro.
     EN: Five of you shall pursue a hundred foreigners, and a hundred of you ten thousand. Your enemies shall fall by the sword before your eyes.

26:9  LA: Respiciam vos, et crescere faciam : multiplicabimini, et firmabo pactum meum vobiscum.
     EN: I will look upon you and make you increase. You shall multiply, and I will establish My covenant with you.

26:10  LA: Comedetis vetustissima veterum, et vetera novis supervenientibus projicietis.
     EN: You shall eat the oldest of your old stores, and cast out the old when the new comes in.

26:11  LA: Ponam tabernaculum meum in medio vestri, et non abjiciet vos anima mea.
     EN: I will place My tabernacle among you, and My soul shall not reject you.

26:12  LA: Ambulabo inter vos, et ero Deus vester, vosque eritis populus meus.
     EN: I will walk among you and be your God, and you shall be My people.

26:13  LA: Ego Dominus Deus vester, qui eduxi vos de terra Ægyptiorum, ne serviretis eis : et qui confregi catenas cervicum vestrarum, ut incederetis erecti.
     EN: I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of the Egyptians so that you would not serve them, and who broke the chains from your necks so that you might walk upright.

26:14  LA: Quod si non audieritis me, nec feceritis omnia mandata mea,
     EN: But if you do not listen to Me or carry out all My commandments,

26:15  LA: si spreveritis leges meas, et judicia mea contempseritis, ut non faciatis ea quæ a me constituta sunt, et ad irritum perducatis pactum meum :
     EN: if you reject My laws and despise My judgments, refusing to do what I have established and making My covenant void,

26:16  LA: ego quoque hæc faciam vobis : visitabo vos velociter in egestate, et ardore, qui conficiat oculos vestros, et consumat animas vestras. Frustra seretis sementem, quæ ab hostibus devorabitur.
     EN: I in turn will do these things to you: I will swiftly visit you with want and burning fever that wastes your eyes and consumes your lives. You shall sow your seed in vain, for it shall be devoured by your enemies.

26:17  LA: Ponam faciem meam contra vos, et corruetis coram hostibus vestris, et subjiciemini his qui oderunt vos : fugietis, nemine persequente.
     EN: I will set My face against you, and you shall fall before your enemies and be subjected to those who hate you. You shall flee when no one pursues.

26:18  LA: Sin autem nec sic obedieritis mihi, addam correptiones vestras septuplum propter peccata vestra,
     EN: If even then you do not obey Me, I will increase your chastisements sevenfold because of your sins,

26:19  LA: et conteram superbiam duritiæ vestræ. Daboque vobis cælum desuper sicut ferrum, et terram æneam.
     EN: and crush the pride of your stubbornness. I will make the sky above you like iron and the earth like bronze.

26:20  LA: Consumetur incassum labor vester, non proferet terra germen, nec arbores poma præbebunt.
     EN: Your labor shall be spent in vain. The earth shall not produce its growth, and the trees shall not yield fruit.

26:21  LA: Si ambulaveritis ex adverso mihi, nec volueritis audire me, addam plagas vestras in septuplum propter peccata vestra :
     EN: If you walk in opposition to Me and refuse to listen to Me, I will increase your plagues sevenfold because of your sins.

26:22  LA: immittamque in vos bestias agri, quæ consumant vos, et pecora vestra, et ad paucitatem cuncta redigant, desertæque fiant viæ vestræ.
     EN: I will send wild beasts against you, which shall consume you and your livestock and reduce everything to a few, and your roads shall become deserted.

26:23  LA: Quod si nec sic volueritis recipere disciplinam, sed ambulaveritis ex adverso mihi :
     EN: If even then you refuse to accept discipline and walk in opposition to Me,

26:24  LA: ego quoque contra vos adversus incedam, et percutiam vos septies propter peccata vestra,
     EN: I too will walk against you in opposition and strike you seven times because of your sins.

26:25  LA: inducamque super vos gladium ultorem fœderis mei. Cumque confugeritis in urbes, mittam pestilentiam in medio vestri, et trademini in manibus hostium,
     EN: I will bring upon you the sword that avenges My covenant. When you take refuge in the cities, I will send pestilence among you, and you shall be delivered into your enemies' hands,

26:26  LA: postquam confregero baculum panis vestri : ita ut decem mulieres in uno clibano coquant panes, et reddant eos ad pondus : et comedetis, et non saturabimini.
     EN: after I have broken your staff of bread, so that ten women bake bread in one oven and give it back by weight. You shall eat and not be satisfied.

26:27  LA: Sin autem nec per hæc audieritis me, sed ambulaveritis contra me :
     EN: If even through these things you do not listen to Me but walk against Me,

26:28  LA: et ego incedam adversus vos in furore contrario, et corripiam vos septem plagis propter peccata vestra :
     EN: I too will walk against you in opposing fury and chastise you with seven plagues because of your sins,

26:29  LA: ita ut comedatis carnes filiorum vestrorum et filiarum vestrarum.
     EN: so that you eat the flesh of your sons and daughters.

26:30  LA: Destruam excelsa vestra, et simulacra confringam. Cadetis inter ruinas idolorum vestrorum, et abominabitur vos anima mea,
     EN: I will destroy your high places and shatter your images. You shall fall among the ruins of your idols, and My soul shall abhor you,

26:31  LA: in tantum ut urbes vestras redigam in solitudinem, et deserta faciam sanctuaria vestra, nec recipiam ultra odorem suavissimum.
     EN: so that I reduce your cities to desolation and make your sanctuaries deserted, and no longer receive the most pleasing fragrance.

26:32  LA: Disperdamque terram vestram, et stupebunt super ea inimici vestri, cum habitatores illius fuerint.
     EN: I will lay waste your land, and your enemies shall be astonished at it when they become its inhabitants.

26:33  LA: Vos autem dispergam in gentes, et evaginabo post vos gladium, eritque terra vestra deserta, et civitates vestræ dirutæ.
     EN: I will scatter you among the nations and unsheathe the sword behind you. Your land shall be deserted and your cities destroyed.

26:34  LA: Tunc placebunt terræ sabbata sua cunctis diebus solitudinis suæ : quando fueritis
     EN: Then the land shall enjoy its sabbaths throughout all the days of its desolation. When you are

26:35  LA: in terra hostili, sabbatizabit, et requiescet in sabbatis solitudinis suæ, eo quod non requieverit in sabbatis vestris quando habitabatis in ea.
     EN: in an enemy land, it shall keep sabbath and rest during the sabbaths of its desolation, because it did not rest during your sabbaths when you lived in it.

26:36  LA: Et qui de vobis remanserint, dabo pavorem in cordibus eorum in regionibus hostium, terrebit eos sonitus folii volantis, et ita fugient quasi gladium : cadent, nullo persequente,
     EN: As for those of you who remain, I will put terror into their hearts in the lands of their enemies. The sound of a fluttering leaf shall terrify them, and they shall flee as from a sword. They shall fall when no one pursues,

26:37  LA: et corruent singuli super fratres suos, quasi bella fugientes, nemo vestrum inimicis audebit resistere.
     EN: and stumble over one another as though fleeing from battle. None of you shall dare to resist your enemies.

26:38  LA: Peribitis inter gentes, et hostilis vos terra consumet.
     EN: You shall perish among the nations, and an enemy land shall consume you.

26:39  LA: Quod si et de iis aliqui remanserint, tabescent in iniquitatibus suis, in terra inimicorum suorum, et propter peccata patrum suorum et sua affligentur :
     EN: If any of them remain, they shall waste away in their iniquities in the land of their enemies, and shall be afflicted because of their fathers' sins and their own,

26:40  LA: donec confiteantur iniquitates suas, et majorum suorum, quibus prævaricati sunt in me, et ambulaverunt ex adverso mihi.
     EN: until they confess their iniquities and those of their ancestors, by which they transgressed against Me and walked in opposition to Me.

26:41  LA: Ambulabo igitur et ego contra eos, et inducam illos in terram hostilem, donec erubescat incircumcisa mens eorum : tunc orabunt pro impietatibus suis.
     EN: I too, therefore, will walk against them and bring them into an enemy land until their uncircumcised mind is put to shame. Then they shall pray concerning their impieties.

26:42  LA: Et recordabor fœderis mei, quod pepigi cum Jacob, et Isaac, et Abraham. Terræ quoque memor ero :
     EN: I will remember My covenant, which I made with Jacob, Isaac, and Abraham. I will also remember the land,

26:43  LA: quæ cum relicta fuerit ab eis, complacebit sibi in sabbatis suis, patiens solitudinem propter illos. Ipsi vero rogabunt pro peccatis suis, eo quod abjecerint judicia mea, et leges meas despexerint.
     EN: which, when abandoned by them, shall delight in its sabbaths, enduring desolation because of them. They themselves shall plead concerning their sins, because they cast aside My judgments and despised My laws.

26:44  LA: Et tamen etiam cum essent in terra hostili, non penitus abjeci eos, neque sic despexi ut consumerentur, et irritum facerent pactum meum cum eis. Ego enim sum Dominus Deus eorum,
     EN: Yet even when they were in an enemy land, I did not utterly reject them or so despise them that they would be consumed and make My covenant with them void. For I am the Lord their God,

26:45  LA: et recordabor fœderis mei pristini, quando eduxi eos de terra Ægypti in conspectu gentium, ut essem Deus eorum. Ego Dominus. Hæc sunt judicia atque præcepta et leges quas dedit Dominus inter se et filios Israël in monte Sinai per manum Moysi.
     EN: and I will remember My former covenant, when I brought them out of the land of Egypt in the sight of the nations to be their God. I am the Lord. These are the judgments, commands, and laws that the Lord gave between Himself and the children of Israel on Mount Sinai through Moses.

26:46 (Vg 26:45)  LA: et recordabor fœderis mei pristini, quando eduxi eos de terra Ægypti in conspectu gentium, ut essem Deus eorum. Ego Dominus. Hæc sunt judicia atque præcepta et leges quas dedit Dominus inter se et filios Israël in monte Sinai per manum Moysi.
     EN: and I will remember My former covenant, when I brought them out of the land of Egypt in the sight of the nations to be their God. I am the Lord. These are the judgments, commands, and laws that the Lord gave between Himself and the children of Israel on Mount Sinai through Moses.

LEVITICUS 27

27:1  LA: Locutusque est Dominus ad Moysen, dicens :
     EN: The Lord spoke to Moses, saying:

27:2  LA: Loquere filiis Israël, et dices ad eos : Homo qui votum fecerit, et spoponderit Deo animam suam, sub æstimatione dabit pretium.
     EN: Speak to the children of Israel and tell them: A person who makes a vow and pledges himself to God shall give a payment according to an assessment.

27:3  LA: Si fuerit masculus a vigesimo anno usque ad sexagesimum annum, dabit quinquaginta siclos argenti ad mensuram sanctuarii :
     EN: If he is a male from twenty to sixty years old, he shall give fifty silver shekels according to the sanctuary standard.

27:4  LA: si mulier, triginta.
     EN: If a woman, she shall give thirty.

27:5  LA: A quinto autem anno usque ad vigesimum, masculus dabit viginti siclos : femina, decem.
     EN: From five to twenty years old, a male shall give twenty shekels and a female ten.

27:6  LA: Ab uno mense usque ad annum quintum, pro masculo dabuntur quinque sicli : pro femina, tres.
     EN: From one month to five years old, five shekels shall be given for a male and three for a female.

27:7  LA: Sexagenarius et ultra masculus dabit quindecim siclos : femina, decem.
     EN: A male sixty years old or older shall give fifteen shekels, and a female ten.

27:8  LA: Si pauper fuerit, et æstimationem reddere non valebit, stabit coram sacerdote : et quantum ille æstimaverit, et viderit eum posse reddere, tantum dabit.
     EN: If the person is poor and cannot pay the assessment, he shall stand before the priest. He shall give as much as the priest assesses and sees that he is able to pay.

27:9  LA: Animal autem, quod immolari potest Domino, si quis voverit, sanctum erit,
     EN: If anyone vows an animal that may be sacrificed to the Lord, it shall be holy

27:10  LA: et mutari non poterit, id est, nec melius malo, nec pejus bono : quod si mutaverit, et ipsum quod mutatum est, et illud pro quo mutatum est, consecratum erit Domino.
     EN: and may not be exchanged, neither a better one for a bad one nor a worse one for a good one. If he does exchange it, both the substitute and the animal for which it was substituted shall be consecrated to the Lord.

27:11  LA: Animal immundum, quod immolari Domino non potest, si quis voverit, adducetur ante sacerdotem :
     EN: If anyone vows an unclean animal that cannot be sacrificed to the Lord, it shall be brought before the priest,

27:12  LA: qui judicans utrum bonum an malum sit, statuet pretium.
     EN: who shall judge whether it is good or bad and establish its price.

27:13  LA: Quod si dare voluerit is qui offert, addet supra æstimationem quintam partem.
     EN: If the one offering it wishes to pay for it, he shall add a fifth to the assessment.

27:14  LA: Homo si voverit domum suam, et sanctificaverit Domino, considerabit eam sacerdos utrum bona an mala sit, et juxta pretium, quod ab eo fuerit constitutum, venundabitur :
     EN: If a person vows his house and consecrates it to the Lord, the priest shall examine whether it is good or bad, and it shall be sold according to the price he establishes.

27:15  LA: sin autem ille qui voverat, voluerit redimere eam, dabit quintam partem æstimationis supra, et habebit domum.
     EN: But if the one who vowed it wishes to redeem it, he shall add a fifth to the assessed value and have the house.

27:16  LA: Quod si agrum possessionis suæ voverit, et consecraverit Domino, juxta mensuram sementis æstimabitur pretium : si triginta modiis hordei seritur terra, quinquaginta siclis venundetur argenti.
     EN: If he vows a field from his own possession and consecrates it to the Lord, its price shall be assessed according to the amount of seed it takes. If the land is sown with thirty modii of barley, it shall be sold for fifty silver shekels.

27:17  LA: Si statim ab anno incipientis jubilæi voverit agrum, quanto valere potest, tanto æstimabitur.
     EN: If he vows the field at the beginning of the jubilee year, it shall be assessed at its full value.

27:18  LA: Sin autem post aliquantum temporis, supputabit sacerdos pecuniam juxta annorum, qui reliqui sunt, numerum usque ad jubilæum, et detrahetur ex pretio.
     EN: But if he does so some time afterward, the priest shall reckon the money according to the number of years remaining until the jubilee, and the price shall be reduced.

27:19  LA: Quod si voluerit redimere agrum ille qui voverat, addet quintam partem æstimatæ pecuniæ, et possidebit eum.
     EN: If the one who vowed the field wishes to redeem it, he shall add a fifth to the assessed sum and possess it.

27:20  LA: Sin autem noluerit redimere, sed alteri cuilibet fuerit venundatus, ultra eum qui voverat redimere non poterit.
     EN: But if he refuses to redeem it and it is sold to someone else, the one who vowed it shall no longer be able to redeem it.

27:21  LA: Quia cum jubilæi venerit dies, sanctificatus erit Domino, et possessio consecrata ad jus pertinet sacerdotum.
     EN: For when the day of jubilee comes, it shall be sanctified to the Lord, and the consecrated possession belongs by right to the priests.

27:22  LA: Si ager emptus est, et non de possessione majorum sanctificatus fuerit Domino,
     EN: If a field that was purchased, rather than one held from ancestors, is consecrated to the Lord,

27:23  LA: supputabit sacerdos juxta annorum numerum usque ad jubilæum, pretium : et dabit ille qui voverat eum, Domino.
     EN: the priest shall reckon its price according to the number of years until the jubilee, and the one who vowed it shall give that amount to the Lord.

27:24  LA: In jubilæo autem revertetur ad priorem dominum, qui vendiderat eum, et habuerat in sorte possessionis suæ.
     EN: At the jubilee it shall return to the former owner who sold it and held it as his allotted possession.

27:25  LA: Omnis æstimatio siclo sanctuarii ponderabitur. Siclus viginti obolos habet.
     EN: Every assessment shall be weighed by the sanctuary shekel. A shekel contains twenty obols.

27:26  LA: Primogenita, quæ ad Dominum pertinent, nemo sanctificare poterit et vovere : sive bos, sive ovis fuerit, Domini sunt.
     EN: No one may consecrate or vow the firstborn that already belong to the Lord. Whether an ox or a sheep, they are the Lord's.

27:27  LA: Quod si immundum est animal, redimet qui obtulit, juxta æstimationem tuam, et addet quintam partem pretii : si redimere noluerit, vendetur alteri quantocumque a te fuerit æstimatum.
     EN: If the animal is unclean, the one who offered it shall redeem it according to your assessment and add a fifth of the price. If he refuses to redeem it, it shall be sold to another for whatever value you assess.

27:28  LA: Omne quod Domino consecratur, sive homo fuerit, sive animal, sive ager, non vendetur, nec redimi poterit. Quidquid semel fuerit consecratum, Sanctum sanctorum erit Domino :
     EN: Anything devoted to the Lord, whether a human being, an animal, or a field, shall not be sold or redeemed. Whatever has once been devoted shall be most holy to the Lord.

27:29  LA: et omnis consecratio, quæ offertur ab homine, non redimetur, sed morte morietur.
     EN: Every devoted offering made by a person shall not be redeemed, but shall surely be put to death.

27:30  LA: Omnes decimæ terræ, sive de frugibus, sive de pomis arborum, Domini sunt, et illi sanctificantur.
     EN: All the tithes of the land, whether from the crops or the fruit of trees, belong to the Lord and are consecrated to Him.

27:31  LA: Si quis autem voluerit redimere decimas suas, addet quintam partem earum.
     EN: If anyone wishes to redeem his tithes, he shall add a fifth of their value.

27:32  LA: Omnium decimarum bovis et ovis et capræ, quæ sub pastoris virga transeunt, quidquid decimum venerit, sanctificabitur Domino.
     EN: In all the tithes of cattle, sheep, and goats that pass beneath the shepherd's rod, every tenth animal shall be consecrated to the Lord.

27:33  LA: Non eligetur nec bonum nec malum, nec altero commutabitur, si quis mutaverit : et quod mutatum est, et pro quo mutatum est, sanctificabitur Domino, et non redimetur.
     EN: Neither good nor bad shall be selected, and it shall not be exchanged for another. If anyone exchanges it, both the substitute and the animal for which it was substituted shall be consecrated to the Lord and shall not be redeemed.

27:34  LA: Hæc sunt præcepta, quæ mandavit Dominus Moysi ad filios Israël in monte Sinai.
     EN: These are the commands that the Lord gave Moses for the children of Israel on Mount Sinai.
