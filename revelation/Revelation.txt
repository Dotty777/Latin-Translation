REVELATION 1

1:1  LA: Apocalypsis Jesu Christi, quam dedit illi Deus palam facere servis suis, quæ oportet fieri cito: et significavit, mittens per angelum suum servo suo Joanni,
     EN: The revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave him to make known to his servants what must happen soon. He made it known by sending his angel to his servant John,

1:2  LA: qui testimonium perhibuit verbo Dei, et testimonium Jesu Christi, quæcumque vidit.
     EN: who bore witness to the word of God and the testimony of Jesus Christ—to everything he saw.

1:3  LA: Beatus qui legit, et audit verba prophetiæ hujus, et servat ea, quæ in ea scripta sunt: tempus enim prope est.
     EN: Blessed is the one who reads and hears the words of this prophecy and keeps what is written in it, for the time is near.

1:4  LA: Joannes septem ecclesiis, quæ sunt in Asia. Gratia vobis, et pax ab eo, qui est, et qui erat, et qui venturus est: et a septem spiritibus qui in conspectu throni ejus sunt:
     EN: John, to the seven churches in Asia: grace to you and peace from him who is, who was, and who is to come, and from the seven spirits before his throne,

1:5  LA: et a Jesu Christo, qui est testis fidelis, primogenitus mortuorum, et princeps regum terræ, qui dilexit nos, et lavit nos a peccatis nostris in sanguine suo,
     EN: and from Jesus Christ, the faithful witness, the firstborn of the dead, and the ruler of the kings of the earth, who loved us and washed us from our sins in his blood,

1:6  LA: et fecit nos regnum, et sacerdotes Deo et Patri suo: ipsi gloria et imperium in sæcula sæculorum. Amen.
     EN: and made us a kingdom and priests to his God and Father—to him be glory and dominion through ages of ages. Amen.

1:7  LA: Ecce venit cum nubibus, et videbit eum omnis oculus, et qui eum pupugerunt. Et plangent se super eum omnes tribus terræ. Etiam: amen.
     EN: Behold, he comes with the clouds, and every eye will see him, including those who pierced him; and all the tribes of the earth will mourn over him. Yes, amen.

1:8  LA: Ego sum alpha et omega, principium et finis, dicit Dominus Deus: qui est, et qui erat, et qui venturus est, omnipotens.
     EN: “I am the Alpha and the Omega, the beginning and the end,” says the Lord God, who is, who was, and who is to come, the Almighty.

1:9  LA: Ego Joannes frater vester, et particeps in tribulatione, et regno, et patientia in Christo Jesu: fui in insula, quæ appellatur Patmos, propter verbum Dei, et testimonium Jesu:
     EN: I, John, your brother and companion in affliction, in the kingdom, and in endurance in Christ Jesus, was on the island called Patmos because of the word of God and the testimony of Jesus.

1:10  LA: fui in spiritu in dominica die, et audivi post me vocem magnam tamquam tubæ,
     EN: I was in the spirit on the Lord’s day, and I heard behind me a loud voice like a trumpet,

1:11  LA: dicentis: Quod vides, scribe in libro: et mitte septem ecclesiis, quæ sunt in Asia, Epheso, et Smyrnæ, et Pergamo, et Thyatiræ, et Sardis, et Philadelphiæ, et Laodiciæ.
     EN: saying, “Write what you see in a book and send it to the seven churches in Asia: to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea.”

1:12  LA: Et conversus sum ut viderem vocem, quæ loquebatur mecum: et conversus vidi septem candelabra aurea:
     EN: I turned to see the voice speaking with me, and when I turned, I saw seven golden lampstands,

1:13  LA: et in medio septem candelabrorum aureorum, similem Filio hominis vestitum podere, et præcinctum ad mamillas zona aurea:
     EN: and among the seven golden lampstands one like the Son of Man, clothed in a robe reaching to his feet, with a golden sash around his chest.

1:14  LA: caput autem ejus, et capilli erant candidi tamquam lana alba, et tamquam nix, et oculi ejus tamquam flamma ignis:
     EN: His head and his hair were white like white wool and like snow, and his eyes were like a flame of fire.

1:15  LA: et pedes ejus similes auricalco, sicut in camino ardenti, et vox illius tamquam vox aquarum multarum:
     EN: His feet were like burnished bronze, as in a blazing furnace, and his voice was like the sound of many waters.

1:16  LA: et habebat in dextera sua stellas septem: et de ore ejus gladius utraque parte acutus exibat: et facies ejus sicut sol lucet in virtute sua.
     EN: He held seven stars in his right hand, and from his mouth came a sword sharp on both edges. His face was like the sun shining in its strength.

1:17  LA: Et cum vidissem eum, cecidi ad pedes ejus tamquam mortuus. Et posuit dexteram suam super me, dicens: Noli timere: ego sum primus, et novissimus,
     EN: When I saw him, I fell at his feet as though dead. He laid his right hand on me and said, “Do not fear. I am the first and the last,

1:18  LA: et vivus, et fui mortuus, et ecce sum vivens in sæcula sæculorum: et habeo claves mortis, et inferni.
     EN: and the living one. I was dead, and behold, I am alive through ages of ages, and I hold the keys of death and the underworld.

1:19  LA: Scribe ergo quæ vidisti, et quæ sunt, et quæ oportet fieri post hæc.
     EN: Write, then, what you have seen, what is, and what must happen after these things.

1:20  LA: Sacramentum septem stellarum, quas vidisti in dextera mea, et septem candelabra aurea: septem stellæ, angeli sunt septem ecclesiarum: et candelabra septem, septem ecclesiæ sunt.
     EN: As for the mystery of the seven stars you saw in my right hand and the seven golden lampstands: the seven stars are the angels of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands are the seven churches.”

REVELATION 2

2:1  LA: Angelo Ephesi ecclesiæ scribe: Hæc dicit, qui tenet septem stellas in dextera sua, qui ambulat in medio septem candelabrorum aureorum:
     EN: “To the angel of the church of Ephesus write: These things says the one who holds the seven stars in his right hand, who walks among the seven golden lampstands:

2:2  LA: Scio opera tua, et laborem, et patientiam tuam, et quia non potes sustinere malos: et tentasti eos, qui se dicunt apostolos esse, et non sunt: et invenisti eos mendaces:
     EN: I know your works, your toil, and your endurance, and that you cannot bear evil people. You have tested those who say they are apostles and are not, and have found them to be liars.

2:3  LA: et patientiam habes, et sustinuisti propter nomen meum, et non defecisti.
     EN: You have endurance, and you have borne hardship for my name and have not grown weary.

2:4  LA: Sed habeo adversum te, quod caritatem tuam primam reliquisti.
     EN: But I have this against you: you have abandoned your first love.

2:5  LA: Memor esto itaque unde excideris: et age pœnitentiam, et prima opera fac: sin autem, venio tibi, et movebo candelabrum tuum de loco suo, nisi pœnitentiam egeris.
     EN: Remember, then, from where you have fallen; repent and do the works you did at first. Otherwise I am coming to you and will move your lampstand from its place, unless you repent.

2:6  LA: Sed hoc habes, quia odisti facta Nicolaitarum, quæ et ego odi.
     EN: Yet you have this in your favor: you hate the deeds of the Nicolaitans, which I also hate.

2:7  LA: Qui habet aurem, audiat quid Spiritus dicat ecclesiis: Vincenti dabo edere de ligno vitæ, quod est in paradiso Dei mei.
     EN: Let the one who has an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who conquers I will give food from the tree of life, which is in the paradise of my God.

2:8  LA: Et angelo Smyrnæ ecclesiæ scribe: Hæc dicit primus, et novissimus, qui fuit mortuus, et vivit:
     EN: To the angel of the church of Smyrna write: These things says the first and the last, who was dead and lives:

2:9  LA: Scio tribulationem tuam, et paupertatem tuam, sed dives es: et blasphemaris ab his, qui se dicunt Judæos esse, et non sunt, sed sunt synagoga Satanæ.
     EN: I know your affliction and your poverty—yet you are rich—and you are slandered by those who say they are Jews and are not, but are a synagogue of Satan.

2:10  LA: Nihil horum timeas quæ passurus es. Ecce missurus est diabolus aliquos ex vobis in carcerem ut tentemini: et habebitis tribulationem diebus decem. Esto fidelis usque ad mortem, et dabo tibi coronam vitæ.
     EN: Do not fear anything you are about to suffer. Behold, the devil is about to cast some of you into prison so that you may be tested, and you will have affliction for ten days. Be faithful unto death, and I will give you the crown of life.

2:11  LA: Qui habet aurem, audiat quid Spiritus dicat ecclesiis: Qui vicerit, non lædetur a morte secunda.
     EN: Let the one who has an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. The one who conquers will not be harmed by the second death.

2:12  LA: Et angelo Pergami ecclesiæ scribe: Hæc dicit qui habet rhomphæam utraque parte acutam:
     EN: To the angel of the church of Pergamum write: These things says the one who has the sword sharp on both edges:

2:13  LA: Scio ubi habitas, ubi sedes est Satanæ: et tenes nomen meum, et non negasti fidem meam. Et in diebus illis Antipas testis meus fidelis, qui occisus est apud vos ubi Satanas habitat.
     EN: I know where you dwell, where Satan’s throne is. You hold fast to my name and have not denied my faith, even in those days of Antipas, my faithful witness, who was killed among you, where Satan dwells.

2:14  LA: Sed habeo adversus te pauca: quia habes illic tenentes doctrinam Balaam, qui docebat Balac mittere scandalum coram filiis Israël, edere, et fornicari:
     EN: But I have a few things against you: you have there those who hold to the teaching of Balaam, who taught Balak to place a stumbling block before the children of Israel, to eat and to commit sexual immorality.

2:15  LA: ita habes et tu tenentes doctrinam Nicolaitarum.
     EN: So you too have those who hold to the teaching of the Nicolaitans.

2:16  LA: Similiter pœnitentiam age: si quominus veniam tibi cito, et pugnabo cum illis in gladio oris mei.
     EN: Repent likewise; otherwise I will come to you soon and fight against them with the sword of my mouth.

2:17  LA: Qui habet aurem, audiat quid Spiritus dicat ecclesiis: Vincenti dabo manna absconditum, et dabo illi calculum candidum: et in calculo nomen novum scriptum, quod nemo scit, nisi qui accipit.
     EN: Let the one who has an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who conquers I will give the hidden manna, and I will give him a white stone, with a new name written on the stone that no one knows except the one who receives it.

2:18  LA: Et angelo Thyatiræ ecclesiæ scribe: Hæc dicit Filius Dei, qui habet oculos tamquam flammam ignis, et pedes ejus similes auricalco:
     EN: To the angel of the church of Thyatira write: These things says the Son of God, whose eyes are like a flame of fire and whose feet are like burnished bronze:

2:19  LA: Novi opera tua, et fidem, et caritatem tuam, et ministerium, et patientiam tuam, et opera tua novissima plura prioribus.
     EN: I know your works, your faith, your love, your service, and your endurance, and that your latter works are greater than your first.

2:20  LA: Sed habeo adversus te pauca: quia permittis mulierem Jezabel, quæ se dicit propheten, docere, et seducere servos meos, fornicari, et manducare de idolothytis.
     EN: But I have a few things against you: you allow the woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess, to teach and deceive my servants into committing sexual immorality and eating things sacrificed to idols.

2:21  LA: Et dedi illi tempus ut pœnitentiam ageret: et non vult pœnitere a fornicatione sua.
     EN: I gave her time to repent, but she is unwilling to repent of her sexual immorality.

2:22  LA: Ecce mittam eam in lectum: et qui mœchantur cum ea, in tribulatione maxima erunt, nisi pœnitentiam ab operibus suis egerint.
     EN: Behold, I will cast her onto a bed, and those who commit adultery with her will be in great affliction, unless they repent of their deeds.

2:23  LA: Et filios ejus interficiam in morte, et scient omnes ecclesiæ, quia ego sum scrutans renes, et corda: et dabo unicuique vestrum secundum opera sua. Vobis autem dico,
     EN: I will strike her children dead, and all the churches will know that I am the one who searches the kidneys and hearts; and I will give to each of you according to your works. But to you I say,

2:24  LA: et ceteris qui Thyatiræ estis: quicumque non habent doctrinam hanc, et qui non cognoverunt altitudines Satanæ, quemadmodum dicunt, non mittam super vos aliud pondus:
     EN: and to the rest who are in Thyatira, all who do not hold this teaching and have not known what they call the depths of Satan: I will lay no other burden upon you.

2:25  LA: tamen id quod habetis, tenete donec veniam.
     EN: Only hold fast to what you have until I come.

2:26  LA: Et qui vicerit, et custodierit usque in finem opera mea, dabo illi potestatem super gentes,
     EN: To the one who conquers and keeps my works until the end, I will give authority over the nations,

2:27  LA: et reget eas in virga ferrea, et tamquam vas figuli confringentur,
     EN: and he will rule them with an iron rod, and they will be shattered like a potter’s vessel,

2:28  LA: sicut et ego accepi a Patre meo: et dabo illi stellam matutinam.
     EN: just as I also received from my Father. And I will give him the morning star.

2:29  LA: Qui habet aurem, audiat quid Spiritus dicat ecclesiis.
     EN: Let the one who has an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

REVELATION 3

3:1  LA: Et angelo ecclesiæ Sardis scribe: Hæc dicit qui habet septem spiritus Dei, et septem stellas: Scio opera tua, quia nomen habes quod vivas, et mortuus es.
     EN: To the angel of the church of Sardis write: These things says the one who has the seven spirits of God and the seven stars: I know your works, that you have a name for being alive, but you are dead.

3:2  LA: Esto vigilans, et confirma cetera, quæ moritura erant. Non enim invenio opera tua plena coram Deo meo.
     EN: Be watchful and strengthen what remains, which was about to die. For I do not find your works complete before my God.

3:3  LA: In mente ergo habe qualiter acceperis, et audieris, et serva, et pœnitentiam age. Si ergo non vigilaveris, veniam ad te tamquam fur et nescies qua hora veniam ad te.
     EN: Remember, then, how you received and heard; keep it and repent. If you do not keep watch, I will come to you like a thief, and you will not know at what hour I will come to you.

3:4  LA: Sed habes pauca nomina in Sardis qui non inquinaverunt vestimenta sua: et ambulabunt mecum in albis, quia digni sunt.
     EN: But you have a few names in Sardis who have not defiled their garments, and they will walk with me in white, because they are worthy.

3:5  LA: Qui vicerit, sic vestietur vestimentis albis, et non delebo nomen ejus de libro vitæ, et confitebor nomen ejus coram Patre meo, et coram angelis ejus.
     EN: The one who conquers will thus be clothed in white garments, and I will not erase his name from the book of life. I will confess his name before my Father and before his angels.

3:6  LA: Qui habet aurem, audiat quid Spiritus dicat ecclesiis.
     EN: Let the one who has an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

3:7  LA: Et angelo Philadelphiæ ecclesiæ scribe: Hæc dicit Sanctus et Verus, qui habet clavem David: qui aperit, et nemo claudit: claudit, et nemo aperit:
     EN: To the angel of the church of Philadelphia write: These things says the Holy and True One, who holds the key of David, who opens and no one shuts, who shuts and no one opens:

3:8  LA: Scio opera tua. Ecce dedi coram te ostium apertum, quod nemo potest claudere: quia modicam habes virtutem, et servasti verbum meum, et non negasti nomen meum.
     EN: I know your works. Behold, I have set before you an open door that no one can shut, because you have little strength, yet you have kept my word and have not denied my name.

3:9  LA: Ecce dabo de synagoga Satanæ, qui dicunt se Judæos esse, et non sunt, sed mentiuntur: ecce faciam illos ut veniant, et adorent ante pedes tuos: et scient quia ego dilexi te,
     EN: Behold, I will give you some from the synagogue of Satan, those who say they are Jews and are not, but lie. Behold, I will make them come and bow down before your feet, and they will know that I have loved you.

3:10  LA: quoniam servasti verbum patientiæ meæ, et ego servabo te ab hora tentationis, quæ ventura est in orbem universum tentare habitantes in terra.
     EN: Because you have kept the word of my endurance, I too will keep you from the hour of trial that is to come upon the whole world, to test those who dwell on the earth.

3:11  LA: Ecce venio cito: tene quod habes, ut nemo accipiat coronam tuam.
     EN: Behold, I am coming soon. Hold fast to what you have, so that no one may take your crown.

3:12  LA: Qui vicerit, faciam illum columnam in templo Dei mei, et foras non egredietur amplius: et scribam super eum nomen Dei mei, et nomen civitatis Dei mei novæ Jerusalem, quæ descendit de cælo a Deo meo, et nomen meum novum.
     EN: The one who conquers I will make a pillar in the temple of my God, and he will never go out again. I will write upon him the name of my God and the name of the city of my God, the new Jerusalem, which comes down from heaven from my God, and my new name.

3:13  LA: Qui habet aurem, audiat quid Spiritus dicat ecclesiis.
     EN: Let the one who has an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

3:14  LA: Et angelo Laodiciæ ecclesiæ scribe: Hæc dicit: Amen, testis fidelis et verus, qui est principium creaturæ Dei.
     EN: To the angel of the church of Laodicea write: These things says the Amen, the faithful and true witness, who is the beginning of God’s creation:

3:15  LA: Scio opera tua: quia neque frigidus es, neque calidus: utinam frigidus esses, aut calidus:
     EN: I know your works, that you are neither cold nor hot. Would that you were cold or hot!

3:16  LA: sed quia tepidus es, et nec frigidus, nec calidus, incipiam te evomere ex ore meo:
     EN: But because you are lukewarm, neither cold nor hot, I will begin to vomit you out of my mouth.

3:17  LA: quia dicis: Quod dives sum, et locupletatus, et nullius egeo: et nescis quia tu es miser, et miserabilis, et pauper, et cæcus, et nudus.
     EN: For you say, ‘I am rich, I have grown wealthy, and I need nothing,’ and you do not know that you are wretched, pitiable, poor, blind, and naked.

3:18  LA: Suadeo tibi emere a me aurum ignitum probatum, ut locuples fias, et vestimentis albis induaris, et non appareat confusio nuditatis tuæ, et collyrio inunge oculos tuos ut videas.
     EN: I counsel you to buy from me gold tested by fire, so that you may become rich and be clothed in white garments, and the shame of your nakedness may not appear; and anoint your eyes with eye salve, so that you may see.

3:19  LA: Ego quos amo, arguo, et castigo. Æmulare ergo, et pœnitentiam age.
     EN: Those whom I love, I rebuke and discipline. Be zealous, then, and repent.

3:20  LA: Ecce sto ad ostium, et pulso: si quis audierit vocem meam, et aperuerit mihi januam, intrabo ad illum, et cœnabo cum illo, et ipse mecum.
     EN: Behold, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door to me, I will enter and dine with him, and he with me.

3:21  LA: Qui vicerit, dabo ei sedere mecum in throno meo: sicut et ego vici, et sedi cum Patre meo in throno ejus.
     EN: To the one who conquers I will grant a seat with me on my throne, just as I also conquered and sat down with my Father on his throne.

3:22  LA: Qui habet aurem, audiat quid Spiritus dicat ecclesiis.
     EN: Let the one who has an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches.”

REVELATION 4

4:1  LA: Post hæc vidi: et ecce ostium apertum in cælo, et vox prima, quam audivi tamquam tubæ loquentis mecum, dicens: Ascende huc, et ostendam tibi quæ oportet fieri post hæc.
     EN: After these things I looked, and behold, an open door in heaven. The first voice I had heard, like a trumpet speaking with me, said, “Come up here, and I will show you what must happen after these things.”

4:2  LA: Et statim fui in spiritu: et ecce sedes posita erat in cælo, et supra sedem sedens.
     EN: Immediately I was in the spirit, and behold, a throne stood in heaven, and someone was seated upon the throne.

4:3  LA: Et qui sedebat similis erat aspectui lapidis jaspidis, et sardinis: et iris erat in circuitu sedis similis visioni smaragdinæ.
     EN: The one seated there was like jasper and carnelian in appearance, and around the throne was a rainbow like emerald in appearance.

4:4  LA: Et in circuitu sedis sedilia viginti quatuor: et super thronos viginti quatuor seniores sedentes, circumamicti vestimentis albis, et in capitibus eorum coronæ aureæ.
     EN: Around the throne were twenty-four seats, and on the thrones sat twenty-four elders, clothed in white garments, with golden crowns upon their heads.

4:5  LA: Et de throno procedebant fulgura, et voces, et tonitrua: et septem lampades ardentes ante thronum, qui sunt septem spiritus Dei.
     EN: From the throne came flashes of lightning, voices, and thunder. Seven lamps burned before the throne; these are the seven spirits of God.

4:6  LA: Et in conspectu sedis tamquam mare vitreum simile crystallo: et in medio sedis, et in circuitu sedis quatuor animalia plena oculis ante et retro.
     EN: Before the throne was something like a glassy sea, like crystal. In the midst of the throne and around the throne were four living creatures, full of eyes in front and behind.

4:7  LA: Et animal primum simile leoni, et secundum animal simile vitulo, et tertium animal habens faciem quasi hominis, et quartum animal simile aquilæ volanti.
     EN: The first living creature was like a lion, the second like a calf, the third had a face like a human being, and the fourth was like a flying eagle.

4:8  LA: Et quatuor animalia, singula eorum habebant alas senas: et in circuitu, et intus plena sunt oculis: et requiem non habebant die ac nocte, dicentia: Sanctus, Sanctus, Sanctus Dominus Deus omnipotens, qui erat, et qui est, et qui venturus est.
     EN: Each of the four living creatures had six wings, and they were full of eyes around and within. Day and night they never rested, saying, “Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God Almighty, who was, who is, and who is to come.”

4:9  LA: Et cum darent illa animalia gloriam, et honorem, et benedictionem sedenti super thronum, viventi in sæcula sæculorum,
     EN: Whenever those living creatures gave glory, honor, and blessing to the one seated upon the throne, who lives through ages of ages,

4:10  LA: procidebant viginti quatuor seniores ante sedentem in throno, et adorabant viventem in sæcula sæculorum, et mittebant coronas suas ante thronum, dicentes:
     EN: the twenty-four elders fell down before the one seated upon the throne and worshiped him who lives through ages of ages. They cast their crowns before the throne, saying,

4:11  LA: Dignus es Domine Deus noster accipere gloriam, et honorem, et virtutem: quia tu creasti omnia, et propter voluntatem tuam erant, et creata sunt.
     EN: “You are worthy, O Lord our God, to receive glory, honor, and power, because you created all things; through your will they existed and were created.”

REVELATION 5

5:1  LA: Et vidi in dextera sedentis supra thronum, librum scriptum intus et foris, signatum sigillis septem.
     EN: I saw in the right hand of the one seated upon the throne a book written within and on the outside, sealed with seven seals.

5:2  LA: Et vidi angelum fortem, prædicantem voce magna: Quis est dignus aperire librum, et solvere signacula ejus?
     EN: I saw a mighty angel proclaiming with a loud voice, “Who is worthy to open the book and break its seals?”

5:3  LA: Et nemo poterat neque in cælo, neque in terra, neque subtus terram aperire librum, neque respicere illum.
     EN: No one in heaven, on earth, or under the earth was able to open the book or look upon it.

5:4  LA: Et ego flebam multum, quoniam nemo dignus inventus est aperire librum, nec videre eum.
     EN: I wept greatly because no one was found worthy to open the book or look upon it.

5:5  LA: Et unus de senioribus dixit mihi: Ne fleveris: ecce vicit leo de tribu Juda, radix David, aperire librum, et solvere septem signacula ejus.
     EN: One of the elders said to me, “Do not weep. Behold, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has conquered, so that he may open the book and break its seven seals.”

5:6  LA: Et vidi: et ecce in medio throni et quatuor animalium, et in medio seniorum, Agnum stantem tamquam occisum, habentem cornua septem, et oculos septem: qui sunt septem spiritus Dei, missi in omnem terram.
     EN: I looked, and behold, in the midst of the throne and the four living creatures, and in the midst of the elders, a Lamb stood as though slain, with seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven spirits of God sent into all the earth.

5:7  LA: Et venit: et accepit de dextera sedentis in throno librum.
     EN: He came and took the book from the right hand of the one seated upon the throne.

5:8  LA: Et cum aperuisset librum, quatuor animalia, et viginti quatuor seniores ceciderunt coram Agno, habentes singuli citharas, et phialas aureas plenas odoramentorum, quæ sunt orationes sanctorum:

     EN: saying to the sixth angel, who held the trumpet, “Release the four angels bound at the great river Euphrates.”

11:1  LA: Et datus est mihi calamus similis virgæ, et dictum est mihi: Surge, et metire templum Dei, et altare, et adorantes in eo:
     EN: A reed like a rod was given to me, and I was told, “Rise and measure God’s temple, the altar, and those worshiping in it.

11:2  LA: atrium autem, quod est foris templum, ejice foras, et ne metiaris illud: quoniam datum est gentibus, et civitatem sanctam calcabunt mensibus quadraginta duobus:
     EN: But leave out the court outside the temple and do not measure it, because it has been given to the nations, and they will trample the holy city for forty-two months.

11:3  LA: et dabo duobus testibus meis, et prophetabunt diebus mille ducentis sexaginta, amicti saccis.
     EN: I will give to my two witnesses, and they will prophesy for a thousand two hundred and sixty days, clothed in sackcloth.”

11:4  LA: Hi sunt duæ olivæ et duo candelabra in conspectu Domini terræ stantes.
     EN: These are the two olive trees and the two lampstands standing before the Lord of the earth.

11:5  LA: Et si quis voluerit eos nocere, ignis exiet de ore eorum, et devorabit inimicos eorum: et si quis voluerit eos lædere, sic oportet eum occidi.
     EN: If anyone wishes to harm them, fire will come from their mouths and devour their enemies. If anyone wishes to injure them, he must be killed in this way.

11:6  LA: Hi habent potestatem claudendi cælum, ne pluat diebus prophetiæ ipsorum: et potestatem habent super aquas convertendi eas in sanguinem, et percutere terram omni plaga quotiescumque voluerint.
     EN: They have authority to shut heaven, so that no rain falls during the days of their prophecy. They have authority over the waters, to turn them into blood, and to strike the earth with every plague as often as they wish.

11:7  LA: Et cum finierint testimonium suum, bestia, quæ ascendit de abysso, faciet adversum eos bellum, et vincet illos, et occidet eos.
     EN: When they have finished their testimony, the beast that rises from the abyss will wage war against them, conquer them, and kill them.

11:8  LA: Et corpora eorum jacebunt in plateis civitatis magnæ, quæ vocatur spiritualiter Sodoma, et Ægyptus, ubi et Dominus eorum crucifixus est.
     EN: Their bodies will lie in the streets of the great city that is spiritually called Sodom and Egypt, where their Lord also was crucified.

11:9  LA: Et videbunt de tribubus, et populis, et linguis, et gentibus corpora eorum per tres dies et dimidium: et corpora eorum non sinent poni in monumentis:
     EN: People from the tribes, peoples, tongues, and nations will look upon their bodies for three and a half days and will not allow their bodies to be placed in tombs.

11:10  LA: et inhabitantes terram gaudebunt super illos, et jucundabuntur: et munera mittent invicem, quoniam hi duo prophetæ cruciaverunt eos, qui habitabant super terram.
     EN: Those who dwell upon the earth will rejoice over them and make merry, and they will send gifts to one another, because these two prophets tormented those who dwelt upon the earth.

11:11  LA: Et post dies tres et dimidium, spiritus vitæ a Deo intravit in eos. Et steterunt super pedes suos, et timor magnus cecidit super eos qui viderunt eos.
     EN: After three and a half days, a spirit of life from God entered them. They stood upon their feet, and great fear fell upon those who saw them.

11:12  LA: Et audierunt vocem magnam de cælo, dicentem eis: Ascendite huc. Et ascenderunt in cælum in nube: et viderunt illos inimici eorum.
     EN: They heard a loud voice from heaven saying to them, “Come up here.” They went up into heaven in a cloud, and their enemies saw them.

11:13  LA: Et in illa hora factus est terræmotus magnus, et decima pars civitatis cecidit: et occisa sunt in terræmotu nomina hominum septem millia: et reliqui in timorem sunt missi, et dederunt gloriam Deo cæli.
     EN: In that hour a great earthquake occurred, and a tenth of the city fell. Seven thousand people were killed in the earthquake; the rest were struck with fear and gave glory to the God of heaven.

11:14  LA: Væ secundum abiit: et ecce væ tertium veniet cito.
     EN: The second woe has passed; behold, the third woe will come soon.

11:15  LA: Et septimus angelus tuba cecinit: et factæ sunt voces magnæ in cælo dicentes: Factum est regnum hujus mundi, Domini nostri et Christi ejus, et regnabit in sæcula sæculorum. Amen.
     EN: The seventh angel sounded his trumpet, and loud voices arose in heaven, saying, “The kingdom of this world has become the kingdom of our Lord and of his Christ, and he will reign through ages of ages. Amen.”

11:16  LA: Et viginti quatuor seniores, qui in conspectu Dei sedent in sedibus suis, ceciderunt in facies suas, et adoraverunt Deum, dicentes:
     EN: The twenty-four elders who sit upon their thrones before God fell upon their faces and worshiped God, saying,

11:17  LA: Gratias agimus tibi, Domine Deus omnipotens, qui es, et qui eras, et qui venturus es: quia accepisti virtutem tuam magnam, et regnasti.
     EN: “We give you thanks, Lord God Almighty, who are, who were, and who are to come, because you have taken your great power and have reigned.

11:18  LA: Et iratæ sunt gentes, et advenit ira tua et tempus mortuorum judicari, et reddere mercedem servis tuis prophetis, et sanctis, et timentibus nomen tuum pusillis et magnis, et exterminandi eos qui corruperunt terram.
     EN: The nations grew angry, and your wrath has come, and the time for the dead to be judged, to give the reward to your servants the prophets, to the saints and those who fear your name, small and great, and to destroy those who corrupted the earth.”

11:19  LA: Et apertum est templum Dei in cælo: et visa est arca testamenti ejus in templo ejus, et facta sunt fulgura, et voces, et terræmotus, et grando magna.
     EN: God’s temple in heaven was opened, and the ark of his covenant was seen in his temple. There were flashes of lightning, voices, an earthquake, and great hail.

REVELATION 12

12:1  LA: Et signum magnum apparuit in cælo: mulier amicta sole, et luna sub pedibus ejus, et in capite ejus corona stellarum duodecim:
     EN: A great sign appeared in heaven: a woman clothed with the sun, with the moon beneath her feet and a crown of twelve stars upon her head.

12:2  LA: et in utero habens, clamabat parturiens, et cruciabatur ut pariat.
     EN: She was with child and cried out in labor, tormented as she was about to give birth.

12:3  LA: Et visum est aliud signum in cælo: et ecce draco magnus rufus habens capita septem, et cornua decem: et in capitibus ejus diademata septem,
     EN: Another sign was seen in heaven: behold, a great fiery-red dragon with seven heads and ten horns, and seven diadems upon its heads.

12:4  LA: et cauda ejus trahebat tertiam partem stellarum cæli, et misit eas in terram: et draco stetit ante mulierem, quæ erat paritura, ut cum peperisset, filium ejus devoraret.
     EN: Its tail drew along a third of the stars of heaven and cast them upon the earth. The dragon stood before the woman who was about to give birth, so that when she had given birth it might devour her son.

12:5  LA: Et peperit filium masculum, qui recturus erat omnes gentes in virga ferrea: et raptus est filius ejus ad Deum, et ad thronum ejus,
     EN: She gave birth to a male child, who was to rule all the nations with an iron rod, and her son was caught up to God and to his throne.

12:6  LA: et mulier fugit in solitudinem ubi habebat locum paratum a Deo, ut ibi pascant eam diebus mille ducentis sexaginta.
     EN: The woman fled into the wilderness, where she had a place prepared by God, so that they might feed her there for a thousand two hundred and sixty days.

12:7  LA: Et factum est prælium magnum in cælo: Michaël et angeli ejus præliabantur cum dracone, et draco pugnabat, et angeli ejus:
     EN: A great battle took place in heaven. Michael and his angels fought against the dragon, and the dragon and its angels fought back.

12:8  LA: et non valuerunt, neque locus inventus est eorum amplius in cælo.
     EN: They did not prevail, and no place was found for them in heaven any longer.

12:9  LA: Et projectus est draco ille magnus, serpens antiquus, qui vocatur diabolus, et Satanas, qui seducit universum orbem: et projectus est in terram, et angeli ejus cum illo missi sunt.
     EN: That great dragon was cast down, the ancient serpent called the devil and Satan, who deceives the whole world. It was cast down to the earth, and its angels were cast down with it.

12:10  LA: Et audivi vocem magnam in cælo dicentem: Nunc facta est salus, et virtus, et regnum Dei nostri, et potestas Christi ejus: quia projectus est accusator fratrum nostrorum, qui accusabat illos ante conspectum Dei nostri die ac nocte.
     EN: I heard a loud voice in heaven saying, “Now have come the salvation, power, and kingdom of our God, and the authority of his Christ, because the accuser of our brothers has been cast down, who accused them before our God day and night.

12:11  LA: Et ipsi vicerunt eum propter sanguinem Agni, et propter verbum testimonii sui, et non dilexerunt animas suas usque ad mortem.
     EN: They conquered him through the blood of the Lamb and through the word of their testimony, and they did not love their lives even unto death.

12:12  LA: Propterea lætamini cæli, et qui habitatis in eis. Væ terræ, et mari, quia descendit diabolus ad vos habens iram magnam, sciens quod modicum tempus habet.
     EN: Therefore rejoice, heavens, and you who dwell in them. Woe to the earth and the sea, because the devil has come down to you in great wrath, knowing that he has little time.”

12:13  LA: Et postquam vidit draco quod projectus esset in terram, persecutus est mulierem, quæ peperit masculum:
     EN: When the dragon saw that it had been cast down to the earth, it pursued the woman who had given birth to the male child.

12:14  LA: et datæ sunt mulieri alæ duæ aquilæ magnæ ut volaret in desertum in locum suum, ubi alitur per tempus et tempora, et dimidium temporis a facie serpentis.
     EN: The two wings of a great eagle were given to the woman, so that she might fly into the wilderness to her place, where she is nourished for a time, times, and half a time, away from the serpent’s face.

12:15  LA: Et misit serpens ex ore suo post mulierem, aquam tamquam flumen, ut eam faceret trahi a flumine.
     EN: The serpent poured water like a river from its mouth after the woman, so that it might have her swept away by the river.

12:16  LA: Et adjuvit terra mulierem, et aperuit terra os suum, et absorbuit flumen, quod misit draco de ore suo.
     EN: The earth helped the woman: the earth opened its mouth and swallowed the river that the dragon had poured from its mouth.

12:17  LA: Et iratus est draco in mulierem: et abiit facere prælium cum reliquis de semine ejus, qui custodiunt mandata Dei, et habent testimonium Jesu Christi.
     EN: The dragon grew angry with the woman and went away to wage war against the rest of her offspring, who keep God’s commandments and hold the testimony of Jesus Christ.

12:18  LA: Et stetit supra arenam maris.
     EN: And it stood upon the sand of the sea.

REVELATION 13

13:1  LA: Et vidi de mari bestiam ascendentem habentem capita septem, et cornua decem, et super cornua ejus decem diademata, et super capita ejus nomina blasphemiæ.
     EN: I saw a beast rising from the sea, with seven heads and ten horns, ten diadems upon its horns, and names of blasphemy upon its heads.

13:2  LA: Et bestia, quam vidi, similis erat pardo, et pedes ejus sicut pedes ursi, et os ejus sicut os leonis. Et dedit illi draco virtutem suam, et potestatem magnam.
     EN: The beast I saw was like a leopard, its feet were like a bear’s feet, and its mouth was like a lion’s mouth. The dragon gave it its power and great authority.

13:3  LA: Et vidi unum de capitibus suis quasi occisum in mortem: et plaga mortis ejus curata est. Et admirata est universa terra post bestiam.
     EN: I saw one of its heads as though slain to death, but its deadly wound was healed. The whole earth marveled and followed the beast.

13:4  LA: Et adoraverunt draconem, qui dedit potestatem bestiæ: et adoraverunt bestiam, dicentes: Quis similis bestiæ? et quis poterit pugnare cum ea?
     EN: They worshiped the dragon, who gave authority to the beast, and they worshiped the beast, saying, “Who is like the beast, and who will be able to fight against it?”

13:5  LA: Et datum est ei os loquens magna et blasphemias: et data est ei potestas facere menses quadraginta duos.
     EN: A mouth speaking arrogant things and blasphemies was given to it, and authority was given to it to act for forty-two months.

13:6  LA: Et aperuit os suum in blasphemias ad Deum, blasphemare nomen ejus, et tabernaculum ejus, et eos qui in cælo habitant.
     EN: It opened its mouth in blasphemies against God, to blaspheme his name, his dwelling, and those who dwell in heaven.

13:7  LA: Et est datum illi bellum facere cum sanctis, et vincere eos. Et data est illi potestas in omnem tribum, et populum, et linguam, et gentem,
     EN: It was permitted to wage war against the saints and conquer them. Authority was given to it over every tribe, people, tongue, and nation,

13:8  LA: et adoraverunt eam omnes, qui inhabitant terram: quorum non sunt scripta nomina in libro vitæ Agni, qui occisus est ab origine mundi.
     EN: and all who dwell upon the earth worshiped it, whose names are not written in the book of life of the Lamb who was slain from the beginning of the world.

13:9  LA: Si quis habet aurem, audiat.
     EN: If anyone has an ear, let him hear.

13:10  LA: Qui in captivitatem duxerit, in captivitatem vadet: qui in gladio occiderit, oportet eum gladio occidi. Hic est patientia, et fides sanctorum.
     EN: Whoever leads others into captivity will go into captivity; whoever kills with the sword must be killed with the sword. Here is the endurance and faith of the saints.

13:11  LA: Et vidi aliam bestiam ascendentem de terra, et habebat cornua duo similia Agni, et loquebatur sicut draco.
     EN: I saw another beast rising from the earth. It had two horns like the Lamb’s, but spoke like a dragon.

13:12  LA: Et potestatem prioris bestiæ omnem faciebat in conspectu ejus: et fecit terram, et habitantes in ea, adorare bestiam primam, cujus curata est plaga mortis.
     EN: It exercised all the authority of the first beast in its presence, and it made the earth and those dwelling upon it worship the first beast, whose deadly wound had been healed.

13:13  LA: Et fecit signa magna, ut etiam ignem faceret de cælo descendere in terram in conspectu hominum.
     EN: It performed great signs, even making fire descend from heaven to earth before people’s eyes.

13:14  LA: Et seduxit habitantes in terra propter signa, quæ data sunt illi facere in conspectu bestiæ, dicens habitantibus in terra, ut faciant imaginem bestiæ, quæ habet plagam gladii, et vixit.
     EN: It deceived those dwelling upon the earth through the signs it was permitted to perform in the beast’s presence, telling those dwelling upon the earth to make an image of the beast that has the sword wound and lived.

13:15  LA: Et datum est illi ut daret spiritum imagini bestiæ, et ut loquatur imago bestiæ: et faciat ut quicumque non adoraverint imaginem bestiæ, occidantur.
     EN: It was permitted to give breath to the beast’s image, so that the beast’s image might speak and cause all who would not worship the beast’s image to be killed.

13:16  LA: Et faciet omnes pusillos, et magnos, et divites, et pauperes, et liberos, et servos habere caracterem in dextera manu sua, aut in frontibus suis:
     EN: It will cause everyone, small and great, rich and poor, free and slave, to have a mark on their right hand or upon their foreheads,

13:17  LA: et nequis possit emere, aut vendere, nisi qui habet caracterem, aut nomen bestiæ, aut numerum nominis ejus.
     EN: so that no one may buy or sell unless he has the mark, the beast’s name, or the number of its name.

13:18  LA: Hic sapientia est. Qui habet intellectum, computet numerum bestiæ. Numerus enim hominis est: et numerus ejus sexcenti sexaginta sex.
     EN: Here is wisdom. Let the one who has understanding calculate the beast’s number, for it is the number of a human being, and its number is six hundred and sixty-six.

REVELATION 14

14:1  LA: Et vidi: et ecce Agnus stabat supra montem Sion, et cum eo centum quadraginta quatuor millia, habentes nomen ejus, et nomen Patris ejus scriptum in frontibus suis.
     EN: I looked, and behold, the Lamb stood upon Mount Zion, and with him a hundred and forty-four thousand who had his name and his Father’s name written upon their foreheads.

14:2  LA: Et audivi vocem de cælo, tamquam vocem aquarum multarum, et tamquam vocem tonitrui magni: et vocem, quam audivi, sicut citharœdorum citharizantium in citharis suis.
     EN: I heard a sound from heaven like the sound of many waters and like the sound of mighty thunder. The sound I heard was like harpists playing their harps.

14:3  LA: Et cantabant quasi canticum novum ante sedem, et ante quatuor animalia, et seniores: et nemo poterat dicere canticum, nisi illa centum quadraginta quatuor millia, qui empti sunt de terra.
     EN: They sang what seemed a new song before the throne, the four living creatures, and the elders. No one could utter the song except those hundred and forty-four thousand who were purchased from the earth.

14:4  LA: Hi sunt, qui cum mulieribus non sunt coinquinati: virgines enim sunt. Hi sequuntur Agnum quocumque ierit. Hi empti sunt ex hominibus primitiæ Deo, et Agno:
     EN: These are the ones who have not been defiled with women, for they are virgins. They follow the Lamb wherever he goes. They were purchased from among humanity as firstfruits for God and the Lamb,

14:5  LA: et in ore eorum non est inventum mendacium: sine macula enim sunt ante thronum Dei.
     EN: and no lie was found in their mouths, for they are unblemished before God’s throne.

14:6  LA: Et vidi alterum angelum volantem per medium cæli, habentem Evangelium æternum, ut evangelizaret sedentibus super terram, et super omnem gentem, et tribum, et linguam, et populum:
     EN: I saw another angel flying through midheaven, holding an eternal gospel to proclaim to those who sit upon the earth and to every nation, tribe, tongue, and people.

14:7  LA: dicens magna voce: Timete Dominum, et date illi honorem, quia venit hora judicii ejus: et adorate eum, qui fecit cælum, et terram, mare, et fontes aquarum.
     EN: He said with a loud voice, “Fear the Lord and give him honor, because the hour of his judgment has come. Worship him who made heaven and earth, the sea and the springs of water.”

14:8  LA: Et alius angelus secutus est dicens: Cecidit, cecidit Babylon illa magna: quæ a vino iræ fornicationis suæ potavit omnes gentes.
     EN: Another angel followed, saying, “Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great, who gave all the nations drink from the wine of the wrath of her fornication.”

14:9  LA: Et tertius angelus secutus est illos, dicens voce magna: Si quis adoraverit bestiam, et imaginem ejus, et acceperit caracterem in fronte sua, aut in manu sua:
     EN: A third angel followed them, saying with a loud voice, “If anyone worships the beast and its image and receives a mark upon his forehead or his hand,

14:10  LA: et hic bibet de vino iræ Dei, quod mistum est mero in calice iræ ipsius, et cruciabitur igne, et sulphure in conspectu angelorum sanctorum, et ante conspectum Agni:
     EN: he too will drink from the wine of God’s wrath, mixed with pure wine in the cup of his anger, and will be tormented with fire and sulfur before the holy angels and before the Lamb.

14:11  LA: et fumus tormentorum eorum ascendet in sæcula sæculorum: nec habent requiem die ac nocte, qui adoraverunt bestiam, et imaginem ejus, et si quis acceperit caracterem nominis ejus.
     EN: The smoke of their torments will rise through ages of ages. Those who have worshiped the beast and its image, and anyone who has received the mark of its name, have no rest day or night.”

14:12  LA: Hic patientia sanctorum est, qui custodiunt mandata Dei, et fidem Jesu.
     EN: Here is the endurance of the saints, who keep God’s commandments and the faith of Jesus.

14:13  LA: Et audivi vocem de cælo, dicentem mihi: Scribe: Beati mortui qui in Domino moriuntur. Amodo jam dicit Spiritus, ut requiescant a laboribus suis: opera enim illorum sequuntur illos.
     EN: I heard a voice from heaven saying to me, “Write: Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord. From now on, says the Spirit, they are to rest from their labors, for their works follow them.”

14:14  LA: Et vidi: et ecce nubem candidam, et super nubem sedentem similem Filio hominis, habentem in capite suo coronam auream, et in manu sua falcem acutam.
     EN: I looked, and behold, a white cloud, and seated upon the cloud one like the Son of Man, with a golden crown upon his head and a sharp sickle in his hand.

14:15  LA: Et alius angelus exivit de templo, clamans voce magna ad sedentem super nubem: Mitte falcem tuam, et mete, quia venit hora ut metatur, quoniam aruit messis terræ.
     EN: Another angel came out of the temple, crying with a loud voice to the one seated upon the cloud, “Put forth your sickle and reap, because the hour to reap has come, for the earth’s harvest has dried.”

14:16  LA: Et misit qui sedebat super nubem, falcem suam in terram, et demessa est terra.
     EN: The one seated upon the cloud put forth his sickle upon the earth, and the earth was reaped.

14:17  LA: Et alius angelus exivit de templo, quod est in cælo, habens et ipse falcem acutam.
     EN: Another angel came out of the temple in heaven, also holding a sharp sickle.

14:18  LA: Et alius angelus exivit de altari, qui habebat potestatem supra ignem: et clamavit voce magna ad eum qui habebat falcem acutam, dicens: Mitte falcem tuam acutam, et vindemia botros vineæ terræ: quoniam maturæ sunt uvæ ejus.
     EN: Another angel came from the altar, who had authority over the fire. He called with a loud voice to the one holding the sharp sickle, saying, “Put forth your sharp sickle and gather the clusters of the earth’s vine, because its grapes are ripe.”

14:19  LA: Et misit angelus falcem suam acutam in terram, et vindemiavit vineam terræ, et misit in lacum iræ Dei magnum:
     EN: The angel put forth his sharp sickle upon the earth, gathered the earth’s vine, and cast it into the great winepress of God’s wrath.

14:20  LA: et calcatus est lacus extra civitatem, et exivit sanguis de lacu usque ad frenos equorum per stadia mille sexcenta.
     EN: The winepress was trodden outside the city, and blood came from the winepress as high as the horses’ bridles, for a thousand six hundred stadia.

REVELATION 15

15:1  LA: Et vidi aliud signum in cælo magnum et mirabile, angelos septem, habentes plagas septem novissimas: quoniam in illis consummata est ira Dei.
     EN: I saw another sign in heaven, great and wonderful: seven angels holding the seven last plagues, for in them God’s wrath was brought to completion.

15:2  LA: Et vidi tamquam mare vitreum mistum igne, et eos, qui vicerunt bestiam, et imaginem ejus, et numerum nominis ejus, stantes super mare vitreum, habentes citharas Dei:
     EN: I saw what seemed a glassy sea mingled with fire, and those who had conquered the beast, its image, and the number of its name, standing upon the glassy sea, holding God’s harps.

15:3  LA: et cantantes canticum Moysi servi Dei, et canticum Agni, dicentes: Magna et mirabilia sunt opera tua, Domine Deus omnipotens: justæ et veræ sunt viæ tuæ, Rex sæculorum.
     EN: They sang the song of Moses, God’s servant, and the song of the Lamb, saying, “Great and wonderful are your works, Lord God Almighty; just and true are your ways, King of the ages.

15:4  LA: Quis non timebit te, Domine, et magnificabit nomen tuum? quia solus pius es: quoniam omnes gentes venient, et adorabunt in conspectu tuo, quoniam judicia tua manifesta sunt.
     EN: Who will not fear you, Lord, and magnify your name? For you alone are holy. All nations will come and worship before you, because your judgments have been made manifest.”

15:5  LA: Et post hæc vidi: et ecce apertum est templum tabernaculi testimonii in cælo,
     EN: After these things I looked, and behold, the temple of the tabernacle of testimony was opened in heaven.

15:6  LA: et exierunt septem angeli habentes septem plagas de templo, vestiti lino mundo et candido, et præcincti circa pectora zonis aureis.
     EN: The seven angels holding the seven plagues came out of the temple, clothed in clean, bright linen, with golden sashes around their chests.

15:7  LA: Et unum de quatuor animalibus dedit septem angelis septem phialas aureas, plenas iracundiæ Dei viventis in sæcula sæculorum.
     EN: One of the four living creatures gave the seven angels seven golden bowls full of the wrath of God, who lives through ages of ages.

15:8  LA: Et impletum est templum fumo a majestate Dei, et de virtute ejus: et nemo poterat introire in templum, donec consummarentur septem plagæ septem angelorum.
     EN: The temple was filled with smoke from God’s majesty and his power, and no one could enter the temple until the seven plagues of the seven angels were completed.

REVELATION 16

16:1  LA: Et audivi vocem magnam de templo, dicentem septem angelis: Ite, et effundite septem phialas iræ Dei in terram.
     EN: I heard a loud voice from the temple saying to the seven angels, “Go and pour out the seven bowls of God’s wrath upon the earth.”

16:2  LA: Et abiit primus, et effudit phialam suam in terram, et factum est vulnus sævum et pessimum in homines, qui habebant caracterem bestiæ, et in eos qui adoraverunt imaginem ejus.
     EN: The first went and poured out his bowl upon the earth. A cruel and grievous sore came upon the people who had the beast’s mark and upon those who worshiped its image.

16:3  LA: Et secundus angelus effudit phialam suam in mare, et factus est sanguis tamquam mortui: et omnis anima vivens mortua est in mari.
     EN: The second angel poured out his bowl into the sea, and it became blood like that of a dead person, and every living soul in the sea died.

16:4  LA: Et tertius effudit phialam suam super flumina, et super fontes aquarum, et factus est sanguis.
     EN: The third poured out his bowl upon the rivers and the springs of water, and they became blood.

     EN: When the thousand years are completed, Satan will be released from his prison. He will go out and deceive the nations upon the four corners of the earth, Gog and Magog, and gather them for battle; their number is like the sand of the sea.

20:9 (Vg 20:8)  LA: Et ascenderunt super latitudinem terræ, et circuierunt castra sanctorum, et civitatem dilectam.
     EN: They went up across the breadth of the earth and surrounded the camp of the saints and the beloved city.

20:9  LA: Et descendit ignis a Deo de cælo, et devoravit eos: et diabolus, qui seducebat eos, missus est in stagnum ignis, et sulphuris, ubi et bestia
     EN: Fire came down from God out of heaven and devoured them. The devil who deceived them was cast into the lake of fire and sulfur, where also the beast

20:10  LA: et pseudopropheta cruciabuntur die ac nocte in sæcula sæculorum.
     EN: and the false prophet will be tormented day and night through ages of ages.

20:11  LA: Et vidi thronum magnum candidum, et sedentem super eum, a cujus conspectu fugit terra, et cælum, et locus non est inventus eis.
     EN: I saw a great white throne and the one seated upon it. From his presence earth and heaven fled, and no place was found for them.

20:12  LA: Et vidi mortuos, magnos et pusillos, stantes in conspectu throni, et libri aperti sunt: et alius liber apertus est, qui est vitæ: et judicati sunt mortui ex his, quæ scripta erant in libris, secundum opera ipsorum:
     EN: I saw the dead, great and small, standing before the throne, and books were opened. Another book was opened, which is the book of life. The dead were judged by what was written in the books, according to their works.

20:13  LA: et dedit mare mortuos, qui in eo erant: et mors et infernus dederunt mortuos suos, qui in ipsis erant: et judicatum est de singulis secundum opera ipsorum.
     EN: The sea gave up the dead who were in it, and Death and the Underworld gave up their dead who were in them. Each was judged according to his works.

20:14  LA: Et infernus et mors missi sunt in stagnum ignis. Hæc est mors secunda.
     EN: The Underworld and Death were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death.

20:15  LA: Et qui non inventus est in libro vitæ scriptus, missus est in stagnum ignis.
     EN: Anyone not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire.

REVELATION 21

21:1  LA: Et vidi cælum novum et terram novam. Primum enim cælum, et prima terra abiit, et mare jam non est.
     EN: I saw a new heaven and a new earth. For the first heaven and the first earth had passed away, and the sea is no more.

21:2  LA: Et ego Joannes vidi sanctam civitatem Jerusalem novam descendentem de cælo a Deo, paratam sicut sponsam ornatam viro suo.
     EN: I, John, saw the holy city, the new Jerusalem, descending from heaven from God, prepared like a bride adorned for her husband.

21:3  LA: Et audivi vocem magnam de throno dicentem: Ecce tabernaculum Dei cum hominibus, et habitabit cum eis. Et ipsi populus ejus erunt, et ipse Deus cum eis erit eorum Deus:
     EN: I heard a loud voice from the throne saying, “Behold, God’s dwelling is with humanity, and he will dwell with them. They will be his people, and God himself will be with them as their God.

21:4  LA: et absterget Deus omnem lacrimam ab oculis eorum: et mors ultra non erit, neque luctus, neque clamor, neque dolor erit ultra, quia prima abierunt.
     EN: God will wipe every tear from their eyes, and death will be no more. Mourning, crying, and pain will be no more, because the former things have passed away.”

21:5  LA: Et dixit qui sedebat in throno: Ecce nova facio omnia. Et dixit mihi: Scribe, quia hæc verba fidelissima sunt, et vera.
     EN: The one seated upon the throne said, “Behold, I make all things new.” He said to me, “Write, because these words are utterly trustworthy and true.”

21:6  LA: Et dixit mihi: Factum est: ego sum alpha et omega, initium et finis. Ego sitienti dabo de fonte aquæ vitæ, gratis.
     EN: He said to me, “It is done. I am the Alpha and the Omega, the beginning and the end. To the thirsty I will give freely from the spring of the water of life.

21:7  LA: Qui vicerit, possidebit hæc: et ero illi Deus, et ille erit mihi filius.
     EN: The one who conquers will possess these things, and I will be his God, and he will be my son.

21:8  LA: Timidis autem, et incredulis, et execratis, et homicidis, et fornicatoribus, et veneficis, et idolatris, et omnibus mendacibus, pars illorum erit in stagno ardenti igne et sulphure: quod est mors secunda.
     EN: But as for the cowardly, the unbelieving, the accursed, murderers, the sexually immoral, sorcerers, idolaters, and all liars, their portion will be in the lake burning with fire and sulfur, which is the second death.”

21:9  LA: Et venit unus de septem angelis habentibus phialas plenas septem plagis novissimis, et locutus est mecum, dicens: Veni, et ostendam tibi sponsam, uxorem Agni.
     EN: One of the seven angels holding bowls full of the seven last plagues came and spoke with me, saying, “Come, and I will show you the bride, the wife of the Lamb.”

21:10  LA: Et sustulit me in spiritu in montem magnum et altum, et ostendit mihi civitatem sanctam Jerusalem descendentem de cælo a Deo,
     EN: He carried me away in the spirit to a great and high mountain and showed me the holy city Jerusalem descending from heaven from God,

21:11  LA: habentem claritatem Dei: et lumen ejus simile lapidi pretioso tamquam lapidi jaspidis, sicut crystallum.
     EN: having God’s glory. Its light was like a precious stone, like jasper, clear as crystal.

21:12  LA: Et habebat murum magnum, et altum, habentem portas duodecim: et in portis angelos duodecim, et nomina inscripta, quæ sunt nomina duodecim tribuum filiorum Israël:
     EN: It had a great, high wall with twelve gates, and at the gates twelve angels, with names inscribed that are the names of the twelve tribes of the children of Israel.

21:13  LA: ab oriente portæ tres, et ab aquilone portæ tres, et ab austro portæ tres, et ab occasu portæ tres.
     EN: On the east were three gates, on the north three gates, on the south three gates, and on the west three gates.

21:14  LA: Et murus civitatis habens fundamenta duodecim, et in ipsis duodecim nomina duodecim apostolorum Agni.
     EN: The city’s wall had twelve foundations, and upon them the twelve names of the Lamb’s twelve apostles.

21:15  LA: Et qui loquebatur mecum, habebat mensuram arundineam auream, ut metiretur civitatem, et portas ejus, et murum.
     EN: The one speaking with me held a golden measuring reed, to measure the city, its gates, and its wall.

21:16  LA: Et civitas in quadro posita est, et longitudo ejus tanta est quanta et latitudo: et mensus est civitatem de arundine aurea per stadia duodecim millia: et longitudo, et altitudo, et latitudo ejus æqualia sunt.
     EN: The city lies foursquare, its length as great as its width. He measured the city with the golden reed: twelve thousand stadia. Its length, height, and width are equal.

21:17  LA: Et mensus est murum ejus centum quadraginta quatuor cubitorum, mensura hominis, quæ est angeli.
     EN: He measured its wall: a hundred and forty-four cubits, by a human measure, which is also the angel’s.

21:18  LA: Et erat structura muri ejus ex lapide jaspide: ipsa vero civitas aurum mundum simile vitro mundo.
     EN: Its wall was built of jasper, while the city itself was pure gold, like clear glass.

21:19  LA: Et fundamenta muri civitatis omni lapide pretioso ornata. Fundamentum primum, jaspis: secundum, sapphirus: tertium, calcedonius: quartum, smaragdus:
     EN: The foundations of the city’s wall were adorned with every precious stone. The first foundation was jasper; the second, sapphire; the third, chalcedony; the fourth, emerald;

21:20  LA: quintum, sardonyx: sextum, sardius: septimum, chrysolithus: octavum, beryllus: nonum, topazius: decimum, chrysoprasus: undecimum, hyacinthus: duodecimum, amethystus.
     EN: the fifth, sardonyx; the sixth, carnelian; the seventh, chrysolite; the eighth, beryl; the ninth, topaz; the tenth, chrysoprase; the eleventh, jacinth; the twelfth, amethyst.

21:21  LA: Et duodecim portæ, duodecim margaritæ sunt, per singulas: et singulæ portæ erant ex singulis margaritis: et platea civitatis aurum mundum, tamquam vitrum perlucidum.
     EN: The twelve gates were twelve pearls, each gate made from a single pearl. The city’s street was pure gold, like transparent glass.

21:22  LA: Et templum non vidi in ea: Dominus enim Deus omnipotens templum illius est, et Agnus.
     EN: I saw no temple in it, for the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple.

21:23  LA: Et civitas non eget sole neque luna ut luceant in ea, nam claritas Dei illuminavit eam, et lucerna ejus est Agnus.
     EN: The city has no need of sun or moon to shine in it, for God’s glory has illuminated it, and its lamp is the Lamb.

21:24  LA: Et ambulabunt gentes in lumine ejus: et reges terræ afferent gloriam suam et honorem in illam.
     EN: The nations will walk in its light, and the kings of the earth will bring their glory and honor into it.

21:25  LA: Et portæ ejus non claudentur per diem: nox enim non erit illic.
     EN: Its gates will not be shut by day, for there will be no night there.

21:26  LA: Et afferent gloriam et honorem gentium in illam.
     EN: They will bring the glory and honor of the nations into it.

21:27  LA: Non intrabit in eam aliquod coinquinatum, aut abominationem faciens et mendacium, nisi qui scripti sunt in libro vitæ Agni.
     EN: Nothing defiled will enter it, nor anyone practicing abomination and falsehood, but only those written in the Lamb’s book of life.

REVELATION 22

22:1  LA: Et ostendit mihi fluvium aquæ vitæ, splendidum tamquam crystallum, procedentem de sede Dei et Agni.
     EN: He showed me a river of the water of life, shining like crystal, flowing from the throne of God and of the Lamb.

22:2  LA: In medio plateæ ejus, et ex utraque parte fluminis, lignum vitæ, afferens fructus duodecim per menses singulos, reddens fructum suum et folia ligni ad sanitatem gentium.
     EN: In the midst of its street and on either side of the river was the tree of life, bearing twelve fruits, yielding its fruit each month; and the tree’s leaves were for the healing of the nations.

22:3  LA: Et omne maledictum non erit amplius: sed sedes Dei et Agni in illa erunt, et servi ejus servient illi.
     EN: There will be no curse anymore. The thrones of God and of the Lamb will be in it, and his servants will serve him.

22:4  LA: Et videbunt faciem ejus: et nomen ejus in frontibus eorum.
     EN: They will see his face, and his name will be upon their foreheads.

22:5  LA: Et nox ultra non erit: et non egebunt lumine lucernæ, neque lumine solis, quoniam Dominus Deus illuminabit illos, et regnabunt in sæcula sæculorum.
     EN: There will be no night anymore, and they will need neither lamplight nor sunlight, because the Lord God will illuminate them, and they will reign through ages of ages.

22:6  LA: Et dixit mihi: Hæc verba fidelissima sunt, et vera. Et Dominus Deus spirituum prophetarum misit angelum suum ostendere servis suis quæ oportet fieri cito.
     EN: He said to me, “These words are utterly trustworthy and true. The Lord God of the spirits of the prophets sent his angel to show his servants what must happen soon.

22:7  LA: Et ecce venio velociter. Beatus, qui custodit verba prophetiæ libri hujus.
     EN: And behold, I am coming swiftly. Blessed is the one who keeps the words of the prophecy of this book.”

22:8  LA: Et ego Joannes, qui audivi, et vidi hæc. Et postquam audissem, et vidissem, cecidi ut adorarem ante pedes angeli, qui mihi hæc ostendebat:
     EN: I, John, am the one who heard and saw these things. When I had heard and seen them, I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel who showed me these things.

22:9  LA: et dixit mihi: Vide ne feceris: conservus enim tuus sum, et fratrum tuorum prophetarum, et eorum qui servant verba prophetiæ libri hujus: Deum adora.
     EN: He said to me, “See that you do not do this! For I am a fellow servant of yours and of your brothers the prophets, and of those who keep the words of the prophecy of this book. Worship God.”

22:10  LA: Et dicit mihi: Ne signaveris verba prophetiæ libri hujus: tempus enim prope est.
     EN: He said to me, “Do not seal up the words of the prophecy of this book, for the time is near.

22:11  LA: Qui nocet, noceat adhuc: et qui in sordibus est, sordescat adhuc: et qui justus est, justificetur adhuc: et sanctus, sanctificetur adhuc.
     EN: Let the one who does harm still do harm; let the one who is in filth still become filthy; let the just still be justified; and let the holy still be sanctified.

22:12  LA: Ecce venio cito, et merces mea mecum est, reddere unicuique secundum opera sua.
     EN: Behold, I am coming soon, and my reward is with me, to repay each according to his works.

22:13  LA: Ego sum alpha et omega, primus et novissimus, principium et finis.
     EN: I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end.

22:14  LA: Beati, qui lavant stolas suas in sanguine Agni: ut sit potestas eorum in ligno vitæ, et per portas intrent in civitatem.
     EN: Blessed are those who wash their robes in the blood of the Lamb, so that they may have the right to the tree of life and may enter the city through its gates.

22:15  LA: Foris canes, et venefici, et impudici, et homicidæ, et idolis servientes, et omnis qui amat et facit mendacium.
     EN: Outside are the dogs, the sorcerers, the unchaste, the murderers, those serving idols, and everyone who loves and practices falsehood.

22:16  LA: Ego Jesus misi angelum meum testificari vobis hæc in ecclesiis. Ego sum radix, et genus David, stella splendida et matutina.
     EN: I, Jesus, have sent my angel to bear witness to you about these things in the churches. I am the Root and offspring of David, the bright morning star.”

22:17  LA: Et spiritus, et sponsa dicunt: Veni. Et qui audit, dicat: Veni. Et qui sitit, veniat: et qui vult, accipiat aquam vitæ, gratis.
     EN: The Spirit and the bride say, “Come.” Let the one who hears say, “Come.” Let the one who thirsts come; let the one who wishes take the water of life freely.

22:18  LA: Contestor enim omni audienti verba prophetiæ libri hujus: si quis apposuerit ad hæc, apponet Deus super illum plagas scriptas in libro isto.
     EN: For I solemnly testify to everyone who hears the words of the prophecy of this book: if anyone adds to these things, God will add to him the plagues written in this book.

22:19  LA: Et si quis diminuerit de verbis libri prophetiæ hujus, auferet Deus partem ejus de libro vitæ, et de civitate sancta, et de his quæ scripta sunt in libro isto:
     EN: If anyone takes away from the words of the book of this prophecy, God will take away his share in the book of life, in the holy city, and in the things written in this book.

22:20  LA: dicit qui testimonium perhibet istorum. Etiam venio cito: amen. Veni, Domine Jesu.
     EN: The one who bears witness to these things says, “Yes, I am coming soon.” Amen. Come, Lord Jesus.

22:21  LA: Gratia Domini nostri Jesu Christi cum omnibus vobis. Amen.
     EN: The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you. Amen.
