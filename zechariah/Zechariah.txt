ZECHARIAH 1

1:1  LA: In mense octavo, in anno secundo Darii regis, factum est verbum Domini ad Zachariam filium Barachiæ filii Addo prophetam, dicens :
     EN: In the eighth month, in the second year of King Darius, the word of the Lord came to Zechariah the prophet, son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, saying:

1:2  LA: Iratus est Dominus super patres vestros iracundia.
     EN: The Lord was fiercely angry with your fathers.

1:3  LA: Et dices ad eos : [Hæc dicit Dominus exercituum : Convertimini ad me, ait Dominus exercituum, et convertar ad vos, dicit Dominus exercituum.
     EN: Say to them: Thus says the Lord of hosts: Return to Me, says the Lord of hosts, and I will return to you, says the Lord of hosts.

1:4  LA: Ne sitis sicut patres vestri, ad quos clamabant prophetæ priores, dicentes : Hæc dicit Dominus exercituum : Convertimini de viis vestris malis, et de cogitationibus vestris pessimis : et non audierunt, neque attenderunt ad me, dicit Dominus.
     EN: Do not be like your fathers, to whom the earlier prophets cried out, saying, “Thus says the Lord of hosts: Turn from your evil ways and your wicked designs.” But they did not listen or pay attention to Me, says the Lord.

1:5  LA: Patres vestri, ubi sunt ? et prophetæ numquid in sempiternum vivent ?
     EN: Where are your fathers? And will the prophets live forever?

1:6  LA: Verumtamen verba mea, et legitima mea, quæ mandavi servis meis prophetis, numquid non comprehenderunt patres vestros, et conversi sunt, et dixerunt : Sicut cogitavit Dominus exercituum facere nobis secundum vias nostras, et secundum adinventiones nostras, fecit nobis ?]
     EN: Yet did not My words and My statutes, which I commanded My servants the prophets, overtake your fathers? They turned back and said, “As the Lord of hosts planned to deal with us according to our ways and our deeds, so He has dealt with us.”

1:7  LA: In die vigesima et quarta undecimi mensis Sabath, in anno secundo Darii, factum est verbum Domini ad Zachariam filium Barachiæ filii Addo prophetam, dicens :
     EN: On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, Shebat, in the second year of Darius, the word of the Lord came to Zechariah the prophet, son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, saying:

1:8  LA: Vidi per noctem, et ecce vir ascendens super equum rufum, et ipse stabat inter myrteta, quæ erant in profundo, et post eum equi rufi, varii, et albi.
     EN: I saw during the night, and look, a man was riding a red horse. He stood among the myrtle trees in the hollow, and behind him were red, dappled, and white horses.

1:9  LA: Et dixi : Quid sunt isti, domine mi ? Et dixit ad me angelus qui loquebatur in me : Ego ostendam tibi quid sint hæc.
     EN: I said, “What are these, my lord?” The angel who was speaking within me said to me, “I will show you what these are.”

1:10  LA: Et respondit vir qui stabat inter myrteta, et dixit : Isti sunt quos misit Dominus ut perambulent terram.
      EN: The man who stood among the myrtle trees answered and said, “These are the ones the Lord has sent to walk about the earth.”

1:11  LA: Et responderunt angelo Domini, qui stabat inter myrteta, et dixerunt : Perambulavimus terram, et ecce omnis terra habitatur, et quiescit.
      EN: They answered the angel of the Lord who stood among the myrtle trees and said, “We have walked about the earth, and look, the whole earth is inhabited and at rest.”

1:12  LA: Et respondit angelus Domini, et dixit : Domine exercituum, usquequo tu non misereberis Jerusalem, et urbium Juda, quibus iratus es ? iste jam septuagesimus annus est.
      EN: The angel of the Lord answered and said, “Lord of hosts, how long will You withhold mercy from Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, with which You have been angry? This is now the seventieth year.”

1:13  LA: Et respondit Dominus angelo qui loquebatur in me verba bona, verba consolatoria.
      EN: The Lord answered the angel who was speaking within me with good words, comforting words.

1:14  LA: Et dixit ad me angelus qui loquebatur in me : Clama, dicens : [Hæc dicit Dominus exercituum : Zelatus sum Jerusalem et Sion zelo magno,
      EN: The angel who was speaking within me said to me: Cry out, saying: Thus says the Lord of hosts: I am jealous for Jerusalem and Zion with great jealousy,

1:15  LA: et ira magna ego irascor super gentes opulentas, quia ego iratus sum parum, ipsi vero adjuverunt in malum.
      EN: and I am fiercely angry with the prosperous nations, for I was a little angry, but they helped to make the harm worse.

1:16  LA: Propterea hæc dicit Dominus : Revertar ad Jerusalem in misericordiis, et domus mea ædificabitur in ea, ait Dominus exercituum, et perpendiculum extendetur super Jerusalem.]
      EN: Therefore thus says the Lord: I will return to Jerusalem with mercies, and My house will be built in her, says the Lord of hosts, and the measuring line will be stretched over Jerusalem.

1:17  LA: Adhuc clama, dicens : [Hæc dicit Dominus exercituum : Adhuc affluent civitates meæ bonis, et consolabitur adhuc Dominus Sion, et eliget adhuc Jerusalem.]
      EN: Cry out again, saying: Thus says the Lord of hosts: My cities will again overflow with good things, and the Lord will again comfort Zion and again choose Jerusalem.

ZECHARIAH 2

2:1  LA: Et levavi oculos meos, et vidi, et ecce quatuor cornua.
     EN: I lifted my eyes and saw, and look, there were four horns.

2:2  LA: Et dixi ad angelum qui loquebatur in me : Quid sunt hæc ? Et dixit ad me : Hæc sunt cornua quæ ventilaverunt Judam, et Israël, et Jerusalem.
     EN: I said to the angel who was speaking within me, “What are these?” He said to me, “These are the horns that scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.”

2:3  LA: Et ostendit mihi Dominus quatuor fabros.
     EN: The Lord showed me four craftsmen.

2:4  LA: Et dixi : Quid isti veniunt facere ? Qui ait, dicens : Hæc sunt cornua quæ ventilaverunt Judam per singulos viros, et nemo eorum levavit caput suum : et venerunt isti deterrere ea, ut dejiciant cornua gentium, quæ levaverunt cornu super terram Juda ut dispergerent eam.
     EN: I said, “What have these come to do?” He answered, saying, “These are the horns that scattered Judah, man by man, so that none of them lifted his head. These craftsmen have come to terrify them and cast down the horns of the nations that raised their horn against the land of Judah to scatter it.”

2:5  LA: Et levavi oculos meos, et vidi, et ecce vir, et in manu ejus funiculus mensorum.
     EN: I lifted my eyes and saw, and look, there was a man with a surveyor's cord in his hand.

2:6  LA: Et dixi : Quo tu vadis ? Et dixit ad me : Ut metiar Jerusalem, et videam quanta sit latitudo ejus, et quanta longitudo ejus.
     EN: I said, “Where are you going?” He said to me, “To measure Jerusalem and see how great its breadth and how great its length will be.”

2:7  LA: Et ecce angelus qui loquebatur in me egrediebatur, et angelus alius egrediebatur in occursum ejus :
     EN: And look, the angel who was speaking within me was going out, and another angel came out to meet him.

2:8  LA: et dixit ad eum : Curre, loquere ad puerum istum, dicens : Absque muro habitabitur Jerusalem, præ multitudine hominum et jumentorum in medio ejus.
     EN: He said to him, “Run, speak to that young man, saying: Jerusalem will be inhabited without a wall, because of the multitude of people and livestock within her.

2:9  LA: Et ego ero ei, ait Dominus, murus ignis in circuitu, et in gloria ero in medio ejus.
     EN: And I, says the Lord, will be a wall of fire all around her, and I will be glory in her midst.”

2:10  LA: [O, o, fugite de terra aquilonis, dicit Dominus, quoniam in quatuor ventos cæli dispersi vos, dicit Dominus.
      EN: Oh, oh! Flee from the land of the north, says the Lord, for I have scattered you to the four winds of heaven, says the Lord.

2:11  LA: O Sion ! fuge, quæ habitas apud filiam Babylonis :
      EN: O Zion, flee, you who dwell with daughter Babylon!

2:12  LA: quia hæc dicit Dominus exercituum : Post gloriam misit me ad gentes quæ spoliaverunt vos : qui enim tetigerit vos, tangit pupillam oculi mei :
      EN: For thus says the Lord of hosts: After glory He sent me to the nations that plundered you, for whoever touches you touches the pupil of My eye.

2:13  LA: quia ecce ego levo manum meam super eos, et erunt prædæ his qui serviebant sibi : et cognoscetis quia Dominus exercituum misit me.
      EN: For look, I am raising My hand against them, and they will become plunder for those who served them. You will know that the Lord of hosts has sent me.

2:14  LA: Lauda et lætare, filia Sion, quia ecce ego venio, et habitabo in medio tui, ait Dominus.
      EN: Sing praise and be glad, daughter Zion, for look, I am coming and will dwell in your midst, says the Lord.

2:15  LA: Et applicabuntur gentes multæ ad Dominum in die illa, et erunt mihi in populum, et habitabo in medio tui : et scies quia Dominus exercituum misit me ad te.
      EN: Many nations will join themselves to the Lord on that day and become My people, and I will dwell in your midst. You will know that the Lord of hosts has sent me to you.

2:16  LA: Et possidebit Dominus Judam partem suam in terra sanctificata, et eliget adhuc Jerusalem.
      EN: The Lord will possess Judah as His portion in the holy land and will again choose Jerusalem.

2:17  LA: Sileat omnis caro a facie Domini, quia consurrexit de habitaculo sancto suo.]
      EN: Let all flesh be silent before the Lord, for He has risen from His holy dwelling.

ZECHARIAH 3

3:1  LA: Et ostendit mihi Dominus Jesum sacerdotem magnum, stantem coram angelo Domini : et Satan stabat a dextris ejus ut adversaretur ei.
     EN: The Lord showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the Lord, and Satan stood at his right hand to oppose him.

3:2  LA: Et dixit Dominus ad Satan : Increpet Dominus in te, Satan ! et increpet Dominus in te, qui elegit Jerusalem ! numquid non iste torris est erutus de igne ?
     EN: The Lord said to Satan, “May the Lord rebuke you, Satan! May the Lord who chose Jerusalem rebuke you! Is this not a burning brand snatched from the fire?”

3:3  LA: Et Jesus erat indutus vestibus sordidis, et stabat ante faciem angeli.
     EN: Joshua was clothed in filthy garments and stood before the angel.

3:4  LA: Qui respondit, et ait ad eos qui stabant coram se, dicens : Auferte vestimenta sordida ab eo. Et dixit ad eum : Ecce abstuli a te iniquitatem tuam, et indui te mutatoriis.
     EN: The angel answered and said to those who stood before him, “Remove his filthy garments.” And he said to Joshua, “Look, I have taken away your iniquity and clothed you in a change of garments.”

3:5  LA: Et dixit : Ponite cidarim mundam super caput ejus. Et posuerunt cidarim mundam super caput ejus, et induerunt eum vestibus : et angelus Domini stabat.
     EN: He said, “Put a clean turban on his head.” They put a clean turban on his head and clothed him with garments, while the angel of the Lord stood by.

3:6  LA: Et contestabatur angelus Domini Jesum, dicens :
     EN: The angel of the Lord solemnly charged Joshua, saying:

3:7  LA: [Hæc dicit Dominus exercituum : Si in viis meis ambulaveris, et custodiam meam custodieris, tu quoque judicabis domum meam, et custodies atria mea, et dabo tibi ambulantes de his qui nunc hic assistunt.
     EN: Thus says the Lord of hosts: If you walk in My ways and keep My charge, you too will govern My house and keep My courts, and I will give you companions from among those who now stand here.

3:8  LA: Audi, Jesu sacerdos magne, tu et amici tui, qui habitant coram te, quia viri portendentes sunt : ecce enim ego adducam servum meum Orientem.
     EN: Listen, Joshua, high priest, you and your companions who dwell in your presence, for they are men who serve as signs. For look, I will bring My servant, the Rising One.

3:9  LA: Quia ecce lapis quem dedi coram Jesu : super lapidem unum septem oculi sunt : ecce ego cælabo sculpturam ejus, ait Dominus exercituum, et auferam iniquitatem terræ illius in die una.
     EN: For look at the stone that I have set before Joshua: upon the one stone are seven eyes. Look, I will engrave its engraving, says the Lord of hosts, and remove the iniquity of that land in one day.

3:10  LA: In die illa, dicit Dominus exercituum, vocabit vir amicum suum subter vitem et subter ficum.]
      EN: On that day, says the Lord of hosts, each man will invite his friend beneath his vine and beneath his fig tree.

ZECHARIAH 4

4:1  LA: Et reversus est angelus qui loquebatur in me, et suscitavit me quasi virum qui suscitatur de somno suo.
     EN: The angel who was speaking within me returned and awakened me like a man awakened from his sleep.

4:2  LA: Et dixit ad me : Quid tu vides ? Et dixi : Vidi, et ecce candelabrum aureum totum, et lampas ejus super caput ipsius, et septem lucernæ ejus super illud, et septem infusoria lucernis quæ erant super caput ejus.
     EN: He said to me, “What do you see?” I said, “I saw—and look, a lampstand all of gold, with its reservoir on top of it, its seven lamps upon it, and seven channels for the lamps on its top.

4:3  LA: Et duæ olivæ super illud : una a dextris lampadis, et una a sinistris ejus.
     EN: And there are two olive trees beside it, one to the right of the reservoir and one to its left.”

4:4  LA: Et respondi, et aio ad angelum qui loquebatur in me, dicens : Quid sunt hæc, domine mi ?
     EN: I answered the angel who was speaking within me, saying, “What are these, my lord?”

4:5  LA: Et respondit angelus qui loquebatur in me, et dixit ad me : Numquid nescis quid sunt hæc ? Et dixi : Non, domine mi.
     EN: The angel who was speaking within me answered and said to me, “Do you not know what these are?” I said, “No, my lord.”

4:6  LA: Et respondit, et ait ad me, dicens : Hoc est verbum Domini ad Zorobabel, dicens : Non in exercitu, nec in robore, sed in spiritu meo, dicit Dominus exercituum.
     EN: He answered and said to me: This is the word of the Lord to Zerubbabel, saying: Not by an army or by strength, but by My Spirit, says the Lord of hosts.

4:7  LA: Quis tu, mons magne, coram Zorobabel ? In planum : et educet lapidem primarium, et exæquabit gratiam gratiæ ejus.
     EN: What are you, great mountain, before Zerubbabel? Become a plain! He will bring out the chief stone and match its beauty with beauty.

4:8  LA: Et factum est verbum Domini ad me, dicens :
     EN: The word of the Lord came to me, saying:

4:9  LA: Manus Zorobabel fundaverunt domum istam, et manus ejus perficient eam : et scietis quia Dominus exercituum misit me ad vos.
     EN: Zerubbabel's hands have laid the foundations of this house, and his hands will finish it. You will know that the Lord of hosts has sent me to you.

4:10  LA: Quis enim despexit dies parvos ? Et lætabuntur, et videbunt lapidem stanneum in manu Zorobabel. Septem isti oculi sunt Domini, qui discurrunt in universam terram.
      EN: For who has despised the little days? They will rejoice and see the tin plummet in Zerubbabel's hand. These seven are the eyes of the Lord, which range through the whole earth.

4:11  LA: Et respondi, et dixi ad eum : Quid sunt duæ olivæ istæ, ad dexteram candelabri, et ad sinistram ejus ?
      EN: I answered and said to him, “What are these two olive trees to the right and left of the lampstand?”

4:12  LA: Et respondi secundo, et dixi ad eum : Quid sunt duæ spicæ olivarum quæ sunt juxta duo rostra aurea in quibus sunt suffusoria ex auro ?
      EN: I spoke a second time and said to him, “What are the two olive branches beside the two golden spouts in which are the golden funnels?”

4:13  LA: Et ait ad me, dicens : Numquid nescis quid sunt hæc ? Et dixi : Non, domine mi.
      EN: He said to me, “Do you not know what these are?” I said, “No, my lord.”

4:14  LA: Et dixit : Isti sunt duo filii olei, qui assistunt Dominatori universæ terræ.
      EN: He said, “These are the two sons of oil who stand before the Ruler of the whole earth.”

ZECHARIAH 5

5:1  LA: Et conversus sum, et levavi oculos meos, et vidi, et ecce volumen volans.
     EN: I turned and lifted my eyes and saw, and look, there was a flying scroll.

5:2  LA: Et dixit ad me : Quid tu vides ? Et dixi : Ego video volumen volans : longitudo ejus viginti cubitorum, et latitudo ejus decem cubitorum.
     EN: He said to me, “What do you see?” I said, “I see a flying scroll; its length is twenty cubits and its width ten cubits.”

5:3  LA: Et dixit ad me : Hæc est maledictio quæ egreditur super faciem omnis terræ : quia omnis fur, sicut ibi scriptum est, judicabitur, et omnis jurans ex hoc similiter judicabitur.
     EN: He said to me, “This is the curse that goes out over the face of the whole earth. For every thief will be judged as it is written there, and everyone who swears will likewise be judged by it.

5:4  LA: Educam illud, dicit Dominus exercituum : et veniet ad domum furis, et ad domum jurantis in nomine meo mendaciter : et commorabitur in medio domus ejus, et consumet eam, et ligna ejus, et lapides ejus.
     EN: I will send it out, says the Lord of hosts, and it will enter the thief's house and the house of the one who swears falsely by My name. It will remain within his house and consume it, its timber and its stones.”

5:5  LA: Et egressus est angelus qui loquebatur in me, et dixit ad me : Leva oculos tuos, et vide quid est hoc quod egreditur.
     EN: The angel who was speaking within me came out and said to me, “Lift your eyes and see what this is that is coming out.”

5:6  LA: Et dixi : Quidnam est ? Et ait : Hæc est amphora egrediens. Et dixit : Hæc est oculus eorum in universa terra.
     EN: I said, “What is it?” He said, “This is an amphora coming out.” And he said, “This is their eye throughout the whole earth.”

5:7  LA: Et ecce talentum plumbi portabatur, et ecce mulier una sedens in medio amphoræ.
     EN: And look, a talent of lead was being lifted up, and there was a woman sitting inside the amphora.

5:8  LA: Et dixit : Hæc est impietas. Et projecit eam in medio amphoræ, et misit massam plumbeam in os ejus.
     EN: He said, “This is Wickedness.” He threw her into the amphora and put the leaden mass over its mouth.

5:9  LA: Et levavi oculos meos, et vidi : et ecce duæ mulieres egredientes : et spiritus in alis earum, et habebant alas quasi alas milvi, et levaverunt amphoram inter terram et cælum.
     EN: I lifted my eyes and saw, and look, two women were coming out, with wind in their wings. They had wings like a kite's wings, and they lifted the amphora between earth and heaven.

5:10  LA: Et dixi ad angelum qui loquebatur in me : Quo istæ deferunt amphoram ?
      EN: I said to the angel who was speaking within me, “Where are they carrying the amphora?”

5:11  LA: Et dixit ad me : Ut ædificetur ei domus in terra Sennaar, et stabiliatur, et ponatur ibi super basem suam.
      EN: He said to me, “To build a house for it in the land of Shinar, so that it may be established and set there upon its base.”

ZECHARIAH 6

6:1  LA: Et conversus sum, et levavi oculos meos, et vidi : et ecce quatuor quadrigæ egredientes de medio duorum montium : et montes, montes ærei.
     EN: I turned and lifted my eyes and saw, and look, four chariots were coming out from between two mountains; the mountains were mountains of bronze.

6:2  LA: In quadriga prima equi rufi, et in quadriga secunda equi nigri,
     EN: The first chariot had red horses, and the second chariot black horses;

6:3  LA: et in quadriga tertia equi albi, et in quadriga quarta equi varii et fortes.
     EN: the third chariot had white horses, and the fourth chariot dappled and strong horses.

6:4  LA: Et respondi, et dixi ad angelum qui loquebatur in me : Quid sunt hæc, domine mi ?
     EN: I answered and said to the angel who was speaking within me, “What are these, my lord?”

6:5  LA: Et respondit angelus, et ait ad me : Isti sunt quatuor venti cæli, qui egrediuntur ut stent coram Dominatore omnis terræ.
     EN: The angel answered and said to me, “These are the four winds of heaven, going out to stand before the Ruler of the whole earth.

6:6  LA: In qua erant equi nigri, egrediebantur in terram aquilonis : et albi egressi sunt post eos, et varii egressi sunt ad terram austri.
     EN: The chariot with the black horses was going out toward the northern land, and the white horses went out after them, while the dappled horses went out toward the southern land.”

6:7  LA: Qui autem erant robustissimi, exierunt, et quærebant ire et discurrere per omnem terram. Et dixit : Ite, perambulate terram : et perambulaverunt terram.
     EN: The strongest horses went out and sought to go and range through the whole earth. He said, “Go, walk about the earth.” And they walked about the earth.

6:8  LA: Et vocavit me, et locutus est ad me, dicens : Ecce qui egrediuntur in terram aquilonis, requiescere fecerunt spiritum meum in terra aquilonis.
     EN: He called me and spoke to me, saying, “Look, those going out toward the northern land have brought My Spirit to rest in the northern land.”

6:9  LA: Et factum est verbum Domini ad me, dicens :
     EN: The word of the Lord came to me, saying:

6:10  LA: Sume a transmigratione, ab Holdai, et a Tobia, et ab Idaia : et venies tu in die illa, et intrabis domum Josiæ filii Sophoniæ, qui venerunt de Babylone.
      EN: Take offerings from the exiles, from Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah, who have come from Babylon. Go that day and enter the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah.

6:11  LA: Et sumes aurum et argentum, et facies coronas, et pones in capite Jesu filii Josedec, sacerdotis magni :
      EN: Take gold and silver, make crowns, and place them on the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.

6:12  LA: et loqueris ad eum, dicens : Hæc ait Dominus exercituum, dicens : Ecce vir, Oriens nomen ejus, et subter eum orietur, et ædificabit templum Domino.
      EN: Speak to him, saying: Thus says the Lord of hosts: Look, a man whose name is the Rising One! He will rise from his own place, and he will build a temple for the Lord.

6:13  LA: Et ipse exstruet templum Domino : et ipse portabit gloriam, et sedebit, et dominabitur super solio suo : et erit sacerdos super solio suo, et consilium pacis erit inter illos duos.
      EN: He himself will build a temple for the Lord; he will bear glory and sit and rule upon his throne. He will be a priest upon his throne, and a counsel of peace will be between the two of them.

6:14  LA: Et coronæ erunt Helem, et Tobiæ, et Idaiæ, et Hem filio Sophoniæ, memoriale in templo Domini.
      EN: The crowns will belong to Helem, Tobijah, Jedaiah, and Hen son of Zephaniah, as a memorial in the Lord's temple.

6:15  LA: Et qui procul sunt, venient, et ædificabunt in templo Domini : et scietis quia Dominus exercituum misit me ad vos. Erit autem hoc, si auditu audieritis vocem Domini Dei vestri.
      EN: Those who are far away will come and build in the Lord's temple, and you will know that the Lord of hosts has sent me to you. This will happen if you faithfully listen to the voice of the Lord your God.

ZECHARIAH 7

7:1  LA: Et factum est in anno quarto Darii regis, factum est verbum Domini ad Zachariam, in quarta mensis noni, qui est Casleu.
     EN: In the fourth year of King Darius, the word of the Lord came to Zechariah on the fourth day of the ninth month, which is Chislev.

7:2  LA: Et miserunt ad domum Dei Sarasar et Rogommelech, et viri qui erant cum eo, ad deprecandam faciem Domini :
     EN: They sent Sharezer and Regem-melech, and the men who were with him, to the house of God to entreat the Lord's favor,

7:3  LA: ut dicerent sacerdotibus domus Domini exercituum, et prophetis, loquentes : Numquid flendum est mihi in quinto mense, vel sanctificare me debeo, sicut jam feci multis annis ?
     EN: and to speak to the priests of the house of the Lord of hosts and to the prophets, saying, “Should I weep in the fifth month or consecrate myself, as I have done for so many years?”

7:4  LA: Et factum est verbum Domini exercituum ad me, dicens :
     EN: The word of the Lord of hosts came to me, saying:

7:5  LA: Loquere ad omnem populum terræ, et ad sacerdotes, dicens : Cum jejunaretis, et plangeretis in quinto et septimo per hos septuaginta annos, numquid jejunium jejunastis mihi ?
     EN: Speak to all the people of the land and to the priests, saying: When you fasted and mourned in the fifth and seventh months during these seventy years, was it truly for Me that you fasted?

7:6  LA: et cum comedistis et bibistis, numquid non vobis comedistis et vobismetipsis bibistis ?
     EN: And when you ate and drank, was it not for yourselves that you ate and for yourselves that you drank?

7:7  LA: numquid non sunt verba quæ locutus est Dominus in manu prophetarum priorum, cum adhuc Jerusalem habitaretur ut esset opulenta, ipsa et urbes in circuitu ejus, et ad austrum, et in campestribus habitaretur ?
     EN: Are these not the words the Lord spoke through the earlier prophets, when Jerusalem was still inhabited and prosperous, with her surrounding cities, and the south and the lowlands were inhabited?

7:8  LA: Et factum est verbum Domini ad Zachariam, dicens :
     EN: The word of the Lord came to Zechariah, saying:

7:9  LA: Hæc ait Dominus exercituum, dicens : Judicium verum judicate, et misericordiam et miserationes facite, unusquisque cum fratre suo.

       EN: The land will mourn, family by family apart: the families of the house of David apart, and their wives apart;

12:13  LA: familiæ domus Nathan seorsum, et mulieres eorum seorsum : familiæ domus Levi seorsum, et mulieres eorum seorsum : familiæ Semei seorsum, et mulieres eorum seorsum :
       EN: the families of the house of Nathan apart, and their wives apart; the families of the house of Levi apart, and their wives apart; the families of Shimei apart, and their wives apart;

12:14  LA: omnes familiæ reliquæ, familiæ et familiæ seorsum, et mulieres eorum seorsum.]
       EN: all the remaining families, family by family apart, and their wives apart.

ZECHARIAH 13

13:1  LA: In die illa erit fons patens domui David et habitantibus Jerusalem, in ablutionem peccatoris et menstruatæ.
      EN: On that day a fountain will be opened for the house of David and Jerusalem's inhabitants, for the washing of the sinner and the menstruating woman.

13:2  LA: Et erit in die illa, dicit Dominus exercituum : disperdam nomina idolorum de terra, et non memorabuntur ultra : et pseudoprophetas, et spiritum immundum auferam de terra.
      EN: On that day, says the Lord of hosts, I will destroy the names of the idols from the land, and they will no longer be remembered. I will remove the false prophets and the unclean spirit from the land.

13:3  LA: Et erit : cum prophetaverit quispiam ultra, dicent ei pater ejus et mater ejus, qui genuerunt eum : Non vives, quia mendacium locutus es in nomine Domini : et configent eum pater ejus et mater ejus, genitores ejus, cum prophetaverit.
      EN: If anyone still prophesies, his father and mother who brought him forth will say to him, “You will not live, because you have spoken lies in the Lord's name.” His father and mother who begot him will pierce him when he prophesies.

13:4  LA: Et erit : in die illa confundentur prophetæ, unusquisque ex visione sua cum prophetaverit : nec operientur pallio saccino, ut mentiantur :
      EN: On that day the prophets will be ashamed, each of his own vision when he prophesies. They will not clothe themselves in a sackcloth mantle in order to lie,

13:5  LA: sed dicet : Non sum propheta : homo agricola ego sum, quoniam Adam exemplum meum ab adolescentia mea.
      EN: but each will say, “I am no prophet; I am a farmer, for Adam has been my example since my youth.”

13:6  LA: Et dicetur ei : Quid sunt plagæ istæ in medio manuum tuarum ? Et dicet : His plagatus sum in domo eorum qui diligebant me.]
      EN: He will be asked, “What are these wounds between your hands?” He will say, “I was wounded with these in the house of those who loved me.”

13:7  LA: Framea, suscitare super pastorem meum, et super virum cohærentem mihi, dicit Dominus exercituum : percute pastorem, et dispergentur oves : et convertam manum meam ad parvulos.
      EN: Sword, awaken against My shepherd, against the man joined to Me, says the Lord of hosts. Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered, and I will turn My hand toward the little ones.

13:8  LA: Et erunt in omni terra, dicit Dominus : partes duæ in ea dispergentur, et deficient : et tertia pars relinquetur in ea.
      EN: In all the land, says the Lord, two parts in it will be scattered and perish, and a third part will remain in it.

13:9  LA: Et ducam tertiam partem per ignem, et uram eos sicut uritur argentum, et probabo eos sicut probatur aurum. Ipse vocabit nomen meum, et ego exaudiam eum. Dicam : Populus meus es : et ipse dicet : Dominus Deus meus.
      EN: I will bring the third part through fire, burn them as silver is refined, and test them as gold is tested. They will call upon My name, and I will hear them. I will say, “You are My people,” and they will say, “The Lord is my God.”

ZECHARIAH 14

14:1  LA: Ecce venient dies Domini, et dividentur spolia tua in medio tui.
      EN: Look, the days of the Lord will come, and your plunder will be divided in your midst.

14:2  LA: Et congregabo omnes gentes ad Jerusalem in prælium : et capietur civitas, et vastabuntur domus, et mulieres violabuntur : et egredietur media pars civitatis in captivitatem, et reliquum populi non auferetur ex urbe.
      EN: I will gather all nations to battle against Jerusalem. The city will be captured, the houses plundered, and the women raped. Half the city will go into captivity, but the rest of the people will not be removed from the city.

14:3  LA: Et egredietur Dominus, et præliabitur contra gentes illas, sicut præliatus est in die certaminis.
      EN: The Lord will go forth and fight against those nations as He fought on the day of battle.

14:4  LA: Et stabunt pedes ejus in die illa super montem Olivarum, qui est contra Jerusalem ad orientem : et scindetur mons Olivarum ex media parte sui ad orientem et ad occidentem, prærupto grandi valde : et separabitur medium montis ad aquilonem, et medium ejus ad meridiem.
      EN: On that day His feet will stand upon the Mount of Olives, which faces Jerusalem on the east. The Mount of Olives will split through its middle toward the east and west, into a very great chasm; half the mountain will move toward the north and half toward the south.

14:5  LA: Et fugietis ad vallem montium eorum, quoniam conjungetur vallis montium usque ad proximum : et fugietis sicut fugistis a facie terræmotus in diebus Oziæ regis Juda : et veniet Dominus Deus meus, omnesque sancti cum eo.
      EN: You will flee to the valley of their mountains, for the valley of the mountains will reach as far as the nearby place. You will flee as you fled from the earthquake in the days of Uzziah king of Judah. The Lord my God will come, and all the holy ones with Him.

14:6  LA: Et erit in die illa : non erit lux, sed frigus et gelu.
      EN: On that day there will be no light, but cold and frost.

14:7  LA: Et erit dies una quæ nota est Domino, non dies neque nox : et in tempore vesperi erit lux.
      EN: There will be one day known to the Lord, neither day nor night, and at evening time there will be light.

14:8  LA: Et erit in die illa : exibunt aquæ vivæ de Jerusalem : medium earum ad mare orientale, et medium earum ad mare novissimum : in æstate et in hieme erunt.
      EN: On that day living waters will go out from Jerusalem, half toward the eastern sea and half toward the western sea; they will flow in summer and in winter.

14:9  LA: Et erit Dominus rex super omnem terram : in die illa erit Dominus unus, et erit nomen ejus unum.
      EN: The Lord will be king over all the earth. On that day the Lord will be one, and His name one.

14:10  LA: Et revertetur omnis terra usque ad desertum, de colle Remmon ad austrum Jerusalem : et exaltabitur, et habitabit in loco suo, a porta Benjamin usque ad locum portæ prioris, et usque ad portam angulorum, et a turre Hananeel usque ad torcularia regis.
       EN: All the land will become like the desert, from the hill of Rimmon south of Jerusalem. Jerusalem will be raised up and remain in her place, from the Benjamin Gate to the site of the former gate, as far as the Corner Gate, and from the Tower of Hananel to the king's winepresses.

14:11  LA: Et habitabunt in ea, et anathema non erit amplius, sed sedebit Jerusalem secura.
       EN: People will dwell in her, and there will no longer be a ban of destruction; Jerusalem will rest secure.

14:12  LA: Et hæc erit plaga qua percutiet Dominus omnes gentes quæ pugnaverunt adversus Jerusalem : tabescet caro uniuscujusque stantis super pedes suos : et oculi ejus contabescent in foraminibus suis, et lingua eorum contabescet in ore suo.
       EN: This will be the plague with which the Lord strikes all the nations that fought against Jerusalem: the flesh of each one will waste away as he stands on his feet; his eyes will waste away in their sockets, and their tongues will waste away in their mouths.

14:13  LA: In die illa erit tumultus Domini magnus in eis : et apprehendet vir manum proximi sui, et conseretur manus ejus super manum proximi sui.
       EN: On that day a great tumult from the Lord will be among them. Each man will seize his neighbor's hand, and his hand will grapple with his neighbor's hand.

14:14  LA: Sed et Judas pugnabit adversus Jerusalem : et congregabuntur divitiæ omnium gentium in circuitu, aurum, et argentum, et vestes multæ satis.
       EN: Judah too will fight against Jerusalem. The wealth of all the surrounding nations will be gathered: gold, silver, and garments in great abundance.

14:15  LA: Et sic erit ruina equi, et muli, et cameli, et asini, et omnium jumentorum quæ fuerint in castris illis, sicut ruina hæc.
       EN: A plague like this will fall upon the horse, mule, camel, donkey, and all the beasts in those camps.

14:16  LA: Et omnes qui reliqui fuerint de universis gentibus quæ venerunt contra Jerusalem, ascendent ab anno in annum ut adorent regem, Dominum exercituum, et celebrent festivitatem tabernaculorum.
       EN: All who remain of all the nations that came against Jerusalem will go up year after year to worship the King, the Lord of hosts, and celebrate the Festival of Tabernacles.

14:17  LA: Et erit : qui non ascenderit de familiis terræ ad Jerusalem ut adoret regem, Dominum exercituum, non erit super eos imber.
       EN: If any of the families of the earth do not go up to Jerusalem to worship the King, the Lord of hosts, there will be no rain upon them.

14:18  LA: Quod etsi familia Ægypti non ascenderit et non venerit, nec super eos erit : sed erit ruina, qua percutiet Dominus omnes gentes quæ non ascenderint ad celebrandam festivitatem tabernaculorum.
       EN: If the family of Egypt does not go up and does not come, there will be none upon them either; instead there will be the plague with which the Lord strikes all the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Festival of Tabernacles.

14:19  LA: Hoc erit peccatum Ægypti, et hoc peccatum omnium gentium quæ non ascenderint ad celebrandam festivitatem tabernaculorum.
       EN: This will be Egypt's punishment for sin and the punishment for sin of all the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Festival of Tabernacles.

14:20  LA: In die illa, erit quod super frenum equi est, sanctum Domino : et erunt lebetes in domo Domini quasi phialæ coram altari.
       EN: On that day, what is upon the horse's bridle will be holy to the Lord, and the cooking pots in the Lord's house will be like the bowls before the altar.

14:21  LA: Et erit omnis lebes in Jerusalem et in Juda sanctificatus Domino exercituum : et venient omnes immolantes, et sument ex eis, et coquent in eis : et non erit mercator ultra in domo Domini exercituum in die illo.
       EN: Every cooking pot in Jerusalem and Judah will be consecrated to the Lord of hosts. All who offer sacrifice will come, take some of them, and cook in them. There will no longer be a merchant in the house of the Lord of hosts on that day.
