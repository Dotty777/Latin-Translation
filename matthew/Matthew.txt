MATTHEW — LATIN–ENGLISH (CLEMENTINE VULGATE)
Captured from the Saints.Bible staging Parallel Reader, 2 November 2026.
Verse numbers follow the Saints (Greek) column; 'vulgate_reference' in verses.json gives the Vulgate's own number where it differs.

MATTHEW 1

1:1  LA: Liber generationis Jesu Christi filii David, filii Abraham.
     EN: The book of the genealogy of Jesus Christ, son of David, son of Abraham.

1:2  LA: Abraham genuit Isaac. Isaac autem genuit Jacob. Jacob autem genuit Judam, et fratres ejus.
     EN: Abraham fathered Isaac; Isaac fathered Jacob; Jacob fathered Judah and his brothers.

1:3  LA: Judas autem genuit Phares, et Zaram de Thamar. Phares autem genuit Esron. Esron autem genuit Aram.
     EN: Judah fathered Perez and Zerah by Tamar; Perez fathered Hezron; Hezron fathered Aram.

1:4  LA: Aram autem genuit Aminadab. Aminadab autem genuit Naasson. Naasson autem genuit Salmon.
     EN: Aram fathered Amminadab; Amminadab fathered Nahshon; Nahshon fathered Salmon.

1:5  LA: Salmon autem genuit Booz de Rahab. Booz autem genuit Obed ex Ruth. Obed autem genuit Jesse. Jesse autem genuit David regem.
     EN: Salmon fathered Boaz by Rahab; Boaz fathered Obed by Ruth; Obed fathered Jesse; Jesse fathered King David.

1:6  LA: David autem rex genuit Salomonem ex ea quæ fuit Uriæ.
     EN: King David fathered Solomon by her who had been Uriah’s wife.

1:7  LA: Salomon autem genuit Roboam. Roboam autem genuit Abiam. Abias autem genuit Asa.
     EN: Solomon fathered Rehoboam; Rehoboam fathered Abijah; Abijah fathered Asa.

1:8  LA: Asa autem genuit Josophat. Josophat autem genuit Joram. Joram autem genuit Oziam.
     EN: Asa fathered Jehoshaphat; Jehoshaphat fathered Joram; Joram fathered Uzziah.

1:9  LA: Ozias autem genuit Joatham. Joatham autem genuit Achaz. Achaz autem genuit Ezechiam.
     EN: Uzziah fathered Jotham; Jotham fathered Ahaz; Ahaz fathered Hezekiah.

1:10  LA: Ezechias autem genuit Manassen. Manasses autem genuit Amon. Amon autem genuit Josiam.
     EN: Hezekiah fathered Manasseh; Manasseh fathered Amon; Amon fathered Josiah.

1:11  LA: Josias autem genuit Jechoniam, et fratres ejus in transmigratione Babylonis.
     EN: Josiah fathered Jeconiah and his brothers at the deportation to Babylon.

1:12  LA: Et post transmigrationem Babylonis: Jechonias genuit Salathiel. Salathiel autem genuit Zorobabel.
     EN: After the deportation to Babylon, Jeconiah fathered Shealtiel; Shealtiel fathered Zerubbabel.

1:13  LA: Zorobabel autem genuit Abiud. Abiud autem genuit Eliacim. Eliacim autem genuit Azor.
     EN: Zerubbabel fathered Abiud; Abiud fathered Eliakim; Eliakim fathered Azor.

1:14  LA: Azor autem genuit Sadoc. Sadoc autem genuit Achim. Achim autem genuit Eliud.
     EN: Azor fathered Zadok; Zadok fathered Achim; Achim fathered Eliud.

1:15  LA: Eliud autem genuit Eleazar. Eleazar autem genuit Mathan. Mathan autem genuit Jacob.
     EN: Eliud fathered Eleazar; Eleazar fathered Matthan; Matthan fathered Jacob.

1:16  LA: Jacob autem genuit Joseph virum Mariæ, de qua natus est Jesus, qui vocatur Christus.
     EN: Jacob fathered Joseph, the husband of Mary, of whom Jesus was born, who is called Christ.

1:17  LA: Omnes itaque generationes ab Abraham usque ad David, generationes quatuordecim: et a David usque ad transmigrationem Babylonis, generationes quatuordecim: et a transmigratione Babylonis usque ad Christum, generationes quatuordecim.
     EN: So all the generations from Abraham to David were fourteen generations; from David to the deportation to Babylon, fourteen generations; and from the deportation to Babylon to Christ, fourteen generations.

1:18  LA: Christi autem generatio sic erat: cum esset desponsata mater ejus Maria Joseph, antequam convenirent inventa est in utero habens de Spiritu Sancto.
     EN: Now the birth of Christ took place in this way. When his mother Mary was betrothed to Joseph, before they came together she was found to be with child by the Holy Spirit.

1:19  LA: Joseph autem vir ejus cum esset justus, et nollet eam traducere, voluit occulte dimittere eam.
     EN: Joseph her husband, being righteous and unwilling to expose her to disgrace, intended to dismiss her secretly.

1:20  LA: Hæc autem eo cogitante, ecce angelus Domini apparuit in somnis ei, dicens: Joseph, fili David, noli timere accipere Mariam conjugem tuam: quod enim in ea natum est, de Spiritu Sancto est.
     EN: But as he considered these things, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream, saying: Joseph, son of David, do not fear to take Mary as your wife, for what has been conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit.

1:21  LA: Pariet autem filium: et vocabis nomen ejus Jesum: ipse enim salvum faciet populum suum a peccatis eorum.
     EN: She will bear a son, and you shall call his name Jesus, for he will save his people from their sins.

1:22  LA: Hoc autem totum factum est, ut adimpleretur quod dictum est a Domino per prophetam dicentem:
     EN: All this took place so that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet might be fulfilled:

1:23  LA: Ecce virgo in utero habebit, et pariet filium: et vocabunt nomen ejus Emmanuel, quod est interpretatum Nobiscum Deus.
     EN: Behold, the virgin will conceive and bear a son, and they will call his name Emmanuel, which means, God with us.

1:24  LA: Exsurgens autem Joseph a somno, fecit sicut præcepit ei angelus Domini, et accepit conjugem suam.
     EN: When Joseph awoke from sleep, he did as the angel of the Lord commanded him and took his wife.

1:25  LA: Et non cognoscebat eam donec peperit filium suum primogenitum: et vocavit nomen ejus Jesum.
     EN: And he did not know her until she bore her firstborn son, and he called his name Jesus.

MATTHEW 2

2:1  LA: Cum ergo natus esset Jesus in Bethlehem Juda in diebus Herodis regis, ecce magi ab oriente venerunt Jerosolymam,
     EN: When Jesus therefore was born in Bethlehem of Juda, in the days of king Herod, behold, there came wise men from the East to Jerusalem,

2:2  LA: dicentes: Ubi est qui natus est rex Judæorum? vidimus enim stellam ejus in oriente, et venimus adorare eum.
     EN: Saying: Where is he that is born king of the Jews? For we have seen his star in the East, and have come to adore him.

2:3  LA: Audiens autem Herodes rex, turbatus est, et omnis Jerosolyma cum illo.
     EN: And king Herod hearing this, was troubled, and all Jerusalem with him.

2:4  LA: Et congregans omnes principes sacerdotum, et scribas populi, sciscitabatur ab eis ubi Christus nasceretur.
     EN: And assembling together all the chief priests and the scribes of the people, he inquired of them where Christ should be born.

2:5  LA: At illi dixerunt: In Bethlehem Judæ: sic enim scriptum est per prophetam:
     EN: But they said to him: In Bethlehem of Juda. For so it is written by the prophet:

2:6  LA: Et tu Bethlehem terra Juda, nequaquam minima es in principibus Juda: ex te enim exiet dux, qui regat populum meum Israël.
     EN: And you Bethlehem the land of Juda are not the least among the princes of Juda: for out of you shall come forth the captain that shall rule my people Israel.

2:7  LA: Tunc Herodes clam vocatis magis diligenter didicit ab eis tempus stellæ, quæ apparuit eis:
     EN: Then Herod, privately calling the wise men learned diligently of them the time of the star which appeared to them;

2:8  LA: et mittens illos in Bethlehem, dixit: Ite, et interrogate diligenter de puero: et cum inveneritis, renuntiate mihi, ut et ego veniens adorem eum.
     EN: And sending them into Bethlehem, said: Go and diligently inquire after the child, and when you have found him, bring me word again, that I also may come and adore him.

2:9  LA: Qui cum audissent regem, abierunt, et ecce stella, quam viderant in oriente, antecedebat eos, usque dum veniens staret supra, ubi erat puer.
     EN: Who having heard the king, went their way; and behold the star which they had seen in the East, went before them, until it came and stood over where the child was.

2:10  LA: Videntes autem stellam gavisi sunt gaudio magno valde.
     EN: And seeing the star they rejoiced with exceeding great joy.

2:11  LA: Et intrantes domum, invenerunt puerum cum Maria matre ejus, et procidentes adoraverunt eum: et apertis thesauris suis obtulerunt ei munera, aurum, thus, et myrrham.
     EN: And entering into the house, they found the child with Mary his mother, and falling down they adored him: and opening their treasures, they offered him gifts; gold, frankincense, and myrrh.

2:12  LA: Et responso accepto in somnis ne redirent ad Herodem, per aliam viam reversi sunt in regionem suam.
     EN: And having received an answer in sleep that they should not return to Herod, they went back another way into their country.

2:13  LA: Qui cum recessissent, ecce angelus Domini apparuit in somnis Joseph, dicens: Surge, et accipe puerum, et matrem ejus, et fuge in Ægyptum, et esto ibi usque dum dicam tibi. Futurum est enim ut Herodes quærat puerum ad perdendum eum.
     EN: And after they were departed, behold an angel of the Lord appeared in sleep to Joseph, saying: Arise, and take the child and his mother, and fly into Egypt: and be there until I shall tell you. For it will happen that Herod will seek the child to destroy him.

2:14  LA: Qui consurgens accepit puerum et matrem ejus nocte, et secessit in Ægyptum:
     EN: Who arose, and took the child and his mother by night, and retired into Egypt:

2:15  LA: et erat ibi usque ad obitum Herodis: ut adimpleretur quod dictum est a Domino per prophetam dicentem: Ex Ægypto vocavi filium meum.
     EN: And he was there until the death of Herod: that it might be fulfilled which the Lord spoke by the prophet, saying: Out of Egypt have I called my son.

2:16  LA: Tunc Herodes videns quoniam illusus esset a magis, iratus est valde, et mittens occidit omnes pueros, qui erant in Bethlehem, et in omnibus finibus ejus, a bimatu et infra secundum tempus, quod exquisierat a magis.
     EN: Then Herod perceiving that he was deluded by the wise men, was exceeding angry: and sending killed all the menchildren that were in Bethlehem, and in all the borders of it, from two years old and under, according to the time which he had diligently inquired of the wise men.

2:17  LA: Tunc adimpletum est quod dictum est per Jeremiam prophetam dicentem:
     EN: Then was fulfilled that which was spoken by Jeremiah the prophet, saying:

2:18  LA: Vox in Rama audita est ploratus, et ululatus multus: Rachel plorans filios suos, et noluit consolari, quia non sunt.
     EN: A voice in Rama was heard, lamentation and great mourning; Rachel bewailing her children, and would not be comforted, because they are not.

2:19  LA: Defuncto autem Herode, ecce angelus Domini apparuit in somnis Joseph in Ægypto,
     EN: But when Herod was dead, behold an angel of the Lord appeared in sleep to Joseph in Egypt,

2:20  LA: dicens: Surge, et accipe puerum, et matrem ejus, et vade in terram Israël: defuncti sunt enim qui quærebant animam pueri.
     EN: Saying: Arise, and take the child and his mother, and go into the land of Israel. For they are dead that sought the life of the child.

2:21  LA: Qui consurgens, accepit puerum, et matrem ejus, et venit in terram Israël.
     EN: Who arose, and took the child and his mother, and came into the land of Israel.

2:22  LA: Audiens autem quod Archelaus regnaret in Judæa pro Herode patre suo, timuit illo ire: et admonitus in somnis, secessit in partes Galilææ.
     EN: But hearing that Archelaus reigned in Judea in the room of Herod his father, he was afraid to go there: and being warned in sleep retired into the quarters of Galilee.

2:23  LA: Et veniens habitavit in civitate quæ vocatur Nazareth: ut adimpleretur quod dictum est per prophetas: Quoniam Nazaræus vocabitur.
     EN: And coming he dwelt in a city called Nazareth: that it might be fulfilled which was said by the prophets: That he shall be called a Nazarene.

MATTHEW 3

3:1  LA: In diebus autem illis venit Joannes Baptista prædicans in deserto Judææ,
     EN: And in those days comes John the Baptist preaching in the desert of Judea.

3:2  LA: et dicens: Pœnitentiam agite: appropinquavit enim regnum cælorum.
     EN: and saying: Repent, for the kingdom of heaven has drawn near.

3:3  LA: Hic est enim, qui dictus est per Isaiam prophetam dicentem: Vox clamantis in deserto: Parate viam Domini; rectas facite semitas ejus.
     EN: For this is the one spoken of through Isaiah the prophet, saying: A voice crying in the wilderness: Prepare the way of the Lord; make his paths straight.

3:4  LA: Ipse autem Joannes habebat vestimentum de pilis camelorum, et zonam pelliceam circa lumbos suos: esca autem ejus erat locustæ, et mel silvestre.
     EN: John himself wore clothing of camel’s hair, with a leather belt around his waist; his food was locusts and wild honey.

3:5  LA: Tunc exibat ad eum Jerosolyma, et omnis Judæa, et omnis regio circa Jordanem;
     EN: Then went out to him Jerusalem and all Judea, and all the country about Jordan:

3:6  LA: et baptizabantur ab eo in Jordane, confitentes peccata sua.
     EN: And were baptized by him in the Jordan, confessing their sins.

3:7  LA: Videns autem multos pharisæorum, et sadducæorum, venientes ad baptismum suum, dixit eis: Progenies viperarum, quis demonstravit vobis fugere a ventura ira?
     EN: And seeing many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his baptism, he said to them: You brood of vipers, who has showed you to flee from the wrath to come?

3:8  LA: Facite ergo fructum dignum pœnitentiæ.
     EN: Therefore bear fruit worthy of repentance.

3:9  LA: Et ne velitis dicere intra vos: Patrem habemus Abraham. Dico enim vobis quoniam potens est Deus de lapidibus istis suscitare filios Abrahæ.
     EN: And think not to say within yourselves, We have Abraham for our father. For I tell you that God is able of these stones to raise up children to Abraham.

3:10  LA: Jam enim securis ad radicem arborum posita est. Omnis ergo arbor, quæ non facit fructum bonum, excidetur, et in ignem mittetur.
     EN: For now the axe is laid to the root of the trees. Every tree therefore that does not yield good fruit, shall be cut down, and cast into the fire.

3:11  LA: Ego quidem baptizo vos in aqua in pœnitentiam: qui autem post me venturus est, fortior me est, cujus non sum dignus calceamenta portare: ipse vos baptizabit in Spiritu Sancto, et igni.
     EN: I indeed baptize you in water for repentance, but the one coming after me is mightier than I; I am not worthy to carry his sandals. He will baptize you in the Holy Spirit and fire.

3:12  LA: Cujus ventilabrum in manu sua: et permundabit aream suam: et congregabit triticum suum in horreum, paleas autem comburet igni inextinguibili.
     EN: Whose fan is in his hand, and he will thoroughly cleanse his floor and gather his wheat into the barn; but the chaff he will burn with unquenchable fire.

3:13  LA: Tunc venit Jesus a Galilæa in Jordanem ad Joannem, ut baptizaretur ab eo.
     EN: Then comes Jesus from Galilee to the Jordan, to John, to be baptized by him.

3:14  LA: Joannes autem prohibebat eum, dicens: Ego a te debeo baptizari, et tu venis ad me?
     EN: But John stayed him, saying: I ought to be baptized by you, and do you come to me?

3:15  LA: Respondens autem Jesus, dixit ei: Sine modo: sic enim decet nos implere omnem justitiam. Tunc dimisit eum.
     EN: Jesus answered him: Allow it now, for in this way it is fitting for us to fulfill all righteousness. Then he allowed him.

3:16  LA: Baptizatus autem Jesus, confestim ascendit de aqua, et ecce aperti sunt ei cæli: et vidit Spiritum Dei descendentem sicut columbam, et venientem super se.
     EN: And Jesus being baptized, immediately came out of the water: and lo, the heavens were opened to him: and he saw the Spirit of God descending as a dove, and coming upon him.

3:17  LA: Et ecce vox de cælis dicens: Hic est Filius meus dilectus, in quo mihi complacui.
     EN: And behold a voice from heaven saying: This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.

MATTHEW 4

4:1  LA: Tunc Jesus ductus est in desertum a Spiritu, ut tentaretur a diabolo.
     EN: Then Jesus was led by the spirit into the desert, to be tempted by the devil.

4:2  LA: Et cum jejunasset quadraginta diebus, et quadraginta noctibus, postea esuriit.
     EN: And when he had fasted forty days and forty nights, afterwards he was hungry.

4:3  LA: Et accedens tentator dixit ei: Si Filius Dei es, dic ut lapides isti panes fiant.
     EN: And the tempter coming said to him: If you be the Son of God, command that these stones be made bread.

4:4  LA: Qui respondens dixit: Scriptum est: Non in solo pane vivit homo, sed in omni verbo, quod procedit de ore Dei.
     EN: Who answered: It is written, Not in bread alone does man live, but in every word that proceeds from the mouth of God.

4:5  LA: Tunc assumpsit eum diabolus in sanctam civitatem, et statuit eum super pinnaculum templi,
     EN: Then the devil took him up into the holy city, and set him upon the pinnacle of the temple,

4:6  LA: et dixit ei: Si Filius Dei es, mitte te deorsum. Scriptum est enim: Quia angelis suis mandavit de te, et in manibus tollent te, ne forte offendas ad lapidem pedem tuum.
     EN: And said to him: If you be the Son of God, cast yourself down, for it is written: That he has given his angels charge over you, and in their hands shall they bear you up, lest you dash your foot against a stone.

4:7  LA: Ait illi Jesus: Rursum scriptum est: Non tentabis Dominum Deum tuum.
     EN: Jesus said to him: It is written again: You shall not tempt the Lord your God.

4:8  LA: Iterum assumpsit eum diabolus in montem excelsum valde: et ostendit ei omnia regna mundi, et gloriam eorum,
     EN: Again the devil took him up into a very high mountain, and showed him all the kingdoms of the world, and the glory of them,

4:9  LA: et dixit ei: Hæc omnia tibi dabo, si cadens adoraveris me.
     EN: And said to him: All these will I give you, if falling down you will adore me.

4:10  LA: Tunc dicit ei Jesus: Vade Satana: Scriptum est enim: Dominum Deum tuum adorabis, et illi soli servies.
     EN: Then Jesus says to him: Begone, Satan: for it is written: The Lord your God shall you adore, and him only shall you serve.

4:11  LA: Tunc reliquit eum diabolus: et ecce angeli accesserunt, et ministrabant ei.
     EN: Then the devil left him; and behold angels came and ministered to him.

4:12  LA: Cum autem audisset Jesus quod Joannes traditus esset, secessit in Galilæam:
     EN: And when Jesus had heard that John was delivered up, he retired into Galilee:

4:13  LA: et, relicta civitate Nazareth, venit, et habitavit in Capharnaum maritima, in finibus Zabulon et Nephthalim:
     EN: And leaving the city Nazareth, he came and dwelt in Capernaum on the sea coast, in the borders of Zebulun and of Nephthalim;

4:14  LA: ut adimpleretur quod dictum est per Isaiam prophetam:
     EN: That it might be fulfilled which was said by Isaiah the prophet:

4:15  LA: Terra Zabulon, et terra Nephthalim, via maris trans Jordanem, Galilæa gentium:
     EN: Land of Zebulun and land of Nephthalim, the way of the sea beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles:

4:16  LA: populus, qui sedebat in tenebris, vidit lucem magnam: et sedentibus in regione umbræ mortis, lux orta est eis.
     EN: The people that sat in darkness, has seen great light: and to them that sat in the region of the shadow of death, light is sprung up.

4:17  LA: Exinde cœpit Jesus prædicare, et dicere: Pœnitentiam agite: appropinquavit enim regnum cælorum.
     EN: From that time Jesus began to preach, and to say: Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is near.

4:18  LA: Ambulans autem Jesus juxta mare Galilææ, vidit duos fratres, Simonem, qui vocatur Petrus, et Andream fratrem ejus, mittentes rete in mare (erant enim piscatores),
     EN: And Jesus walking by the sea of Galilee, saw two brothers, Simon who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea (for they were fishers).

4:19  LA: et ait illis: Venite post me, et faciam vos fieri piscatores hominum.
     EN: And he says to them: Come after me, and I will make you to be fishers of men.

4:20  LA: At illi continuo relictis retibus secuti sunt eum.
     EN: And they immediately leaving their nets, followed him.

4:21  LA: Et procedens inde, vidit alios duos fratres, Jacobum Zebedæi, et Joannem fratrem ejus, in navi cum Zebedæo patre eorum, reficientes retia sua: et vocavit eos.
     EN: And going on from there, he saw other two brothers, James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, in a boat with Zebedee their father, mending their nets: and he called them.

4:22  LA: Illi autem statim relictis retibus et patre, secuti sunt eum.
     EN: And they immediately left their nets and father, and followed him.

4:23  LA: Et circuibat Jesus totam Galilæam, docens in synagogis eorum, et prædicans Evangelium regni: et sanans omnem languorem, et omnem infirmitatem in populo.
     EN: And Jesus went about all Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom: and healing every kind of sickness and every infirmity, among the people.

4:24  LA: Et abiit opinio ejus in totam Syriam, et obtulerunt ei omnes male habentes, variis languoribus, et tormentis comprehensos, et qui dæmonia habebant, et lunaticos, et paralyticos, et curavit eos:
     EN: And his fame went throughout all Syria, and they presented to him all sick people that were taken with various diseases and torments, and such as were possessed by demons, and those afflicted by the moon, and those that had the palsy, and he cured them:

4:25  LA: et secutæ sunt eum turbæ multæ de Galilæa, et Decapoli, et de Jerosolymis, et de Judæa, et de trans Jordanem.
     EN: And much people followed him from Galilee, and from Decapolis, and from Jerusalem, and from Judea, and from beyond the Jordan.

MATTHEW 5

5:1  LA: Videns autem Jesus turbas, ascendit in montem, et cum sedisset, accesserunt ad eum discipuli ejus,
     EN: Seeing the crowds, Jesus went up the mountain; and when he had sat down, his disciples came to him.

5:2  LA: et aperiens os suum docebat eos dicens:
     EN: And opening his mouth, he taught them, saying:

5:3  LA: Beati pauperes spiritu: quoniam ipsorum est regnum cælorum.
     EN: Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

5:4 (Vg 5:5)  LA: Beati qui lugent: quoniam ipsi consolabuntur.
     EN: Blessed are those who mourn, for they will be comforted.

5:5 (Vg 5:4)  LA: Beati mites: quoniam ipsi possidebunt terram.
     EN: Blessed are the meek, for they will possess the earth.

5:6  LA: Beati qui esuriunt et sitiunt justitiam: quoniam ipsi saturabuntur.
     EN: Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they will be filled.

5:7  LA: Beati misericordes: quoniam ipsi misericordiam consequentur.
     EN: Blessed are the merciful, for they will obtain mercy.

5:8  LA: Beati mundo corde: quoniam ipsi Deum videbunt.
     EN: Blessed are the pure in heart, for they will see God.

5:9  LA: Beati pacifici: quoniam filii Dei vocabuntur.
     EN: Blessed are the peacemakers, for they will be called children of God.

5:10  LA: Beati qui persecutionem patiuntur propter justitiam: quoniam ipsorum est regnum cælorum.
     EN: Blessed are those who suffer persecution for righteousness, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

5:11  LA: Beati estis cum maledixerint vobis, et persecuti vos fuerint, et dixerint omne malum adversum vos mentientes, propter me:
     EN: Blessed are you when they revile you, persecute you, and falsely speak every evil against you because of me.

5:12  LA: gaudete, et exsultate, quoniam merces vestra copiosa est in cælis. Sic enim persecuti sunt prophetas, qui fuerunt ante vos.
     EN: Rejoice and be glad, for your reward is great in heaven; for so they persecuted the prophets who were before you.

5:13  LA: Vos estis sal terræ. Quod si sal evanuerit, in quo salietur? ad nihilum valet ultra, nisi ut mittatur foras, et conculcetur ab hominibus.
     EN: You are the salt of the earth. But if salt loses its strength, with what will it be salted? It is no longer good for anything except to be thrown outside and trampled by people.

5:14  LA: Vos estis lux mundi. Non potest civitas abscondi supra montem posita,
     EN: You are the light of the world. A city set on a mountain cannot be hidden.

5:15  LA: neque accendunt lucernam, et ponunt eam sub modio, sed super candelabrum, ut luceat omnibus qui in domo sunt.
     EN: Nor do people light a lamp and put it under a measuring basket, but on a lampstand, so that it shines for everyone in the house.

5:16  LA: Sic luceat lux vestra coram hominibus: ut videant opera vestra bona, et glorificent Patrem vestrum, qui in cælis est.
     EN: Let your light shine before people in this way, so that they may see your good works and glorify your Father who is in heaven.

5:17  LA: Nolite putare quoniam veni solvere legem aut prophetas: non veni solvere, sed adimplere.
     EN: Do not think that I came to abolish the Law or the Prophets. I did not come to abolish, but to fulfill.

5:18  LA: Amen quippe dico vobis, donec transeat cælum et terra, jota unum aut unus apex non præteribit a lege, donec omnia fiant.
     EN: For amen, I say to you: until heaven and earth pass away, not one iota or one stroke will pass from the Law until all things take place.

5:19  LA: Qui ergo solverit unum de mandatis istis minimis, et docuerit sic homines, minimus vocabitur in regno cælorum: qui autem fecerit et docuerit, hic magnus vocabitur in regno cælorum.
     EN: Therefore whoever breaks one of these least commandments and teaches people to do so will be called least in the kingdom of heaven; but whoever does and teaches them will be called great in the kingdom of heaven.

5:20  LA: Dico enim vobis, quia nisi abundaverit justitia vestra plus quam scribarum et pharisæorum, non intrabitis in regnum cælorum.
     EN: For I say to you: unless your righteousness exceeds that of the scribes and Pharisees, you will not enter the kingdom of heaven.

5:21  LA: Audistis quia dictum est antiquis: Non occides: qui autem occiderit, reus erit judicio.
     EN: You have heard that it was said to those of old: You shall not kill; and whoever kills will be liable to judgment.

5:22  LA: Ego autem dico vobis: quia omnis qui irascitur fratri suo, reus erit judicio. Qui autem dixerit fratri suo, raca: reus erit concilio. Qui autem dixerit, fatue: reus erit gehennæ ignis.
     EN: But I say to you: everyone who is angry with his brother will be liable to judgment. Whoever says to his brother, Raca, will be liable to the council; and whoever says, Fool, will be liable to the fire of Gehenna.

5:23  LA: Si ergo offers munus tuum ad altare, et ibi recordatus fueris quia frater tuus habet aliquid adversum te:
     EN: Therefore, if you are offering your gift at the altar and there remember that your brother has something against you,

5:24  LA: relinque ibi munus tuum ante altare, et vade prius reconciliari fratri tuo: et tunc veniens offeres munus tuum.
     EN: leave your gift there before the altar, and first go and be reconciled to your brother; then come and offer your gift.

5:25  LA: Esto consentiens adversario tuo cito dum es in via cum eo: ne forte tradat te adversarius judici, et judex tradat te ministro: et in carcerem mittaris.
     EN: Come to terms quickly with your adversary while you are on the way with him, lest your adversary hand you over to the judge, and the judge hand you over to the officer, and you be thrown into prison.

5:26  LA: Amen dico tibi, non exies inde, donec reddas novissimum quadrantem.
     EN: Amen, I say to you: you will not come out of there until you have paid the last quadrans.

5:27  LA: Audistis quia dictum est antiquis: Non mœchaberis.
     EN: You have heard that it was said to those of old: You shall not commit adultery.

5:28  LA: Ego autem dico vobis: quia omnis qui viderit mulierem ad concupiscendum eam, jam mœchatus est eam in corde suo.
     EN: But I say to you: everyone who looks at a woman to desire her has already committed adultery with her in his heart.

5:29  LA: Quod si oculus tuus dexter scandalizat te, erue eum, et projice abs te: expedit enim tibi ut pereat unum membrorum tuorum, quam totum corpus tuum mittatur in gehennam.
     EN: If your right eye causes you to stumble, tear it out and throw it away from you. For it is better for you that one of your members perish than that your whole body be thrown into Gehenna.

5:30  LA: Et si dextra manus tua scandalizat te, abscide eam, et projice abs te: expedit enim tibi ut pereat unum membrorum tuorum, quam totum corpus tuum eat in gehennam.
     EN: And if your right hand causes you to stumble, cut it off and throw it away from you. For it is better for you that one of your members perish than that your whole body go into Gehenna.

5:31  LA: Dictum est autem: Quicumque dimiserit uxorem suam, det ei libellum repudii.
     EN: It was also said: Whoever dismisses his wife, let him give her a certificate of divorce.

5:32  LA: Ego autem dico vobis: quia omnis qui dimiserit uxorem suam, excepta fornicationis causa, facit eam mœchari: et qui dimissam duxerit, adulterat.
     EN: But I say to you: everyone who dismisses his wife, except on account of fornication, causes her to commit adultery; and whoever marries a dismissed woman commits adultery.

5:33  LA: Iterum audistis quia dictum est antiquis: Non perjurabis: reddes autem Domino juramenta tua.
     EN: Again, you have heard that it was said to those of old: You shall not swear falsely, but shall fulfill your oaths to the Lord.

5:34  LA: Ego autem dico vobis, non jurare omnino, neque per cælum, quia thronus Dei est:
     EN: But I say to you: do not swear at all, either by heaven, for it is the throne of God,

5:35  LA: neque per terram, quia scabellum est pedum ejus: neque per Jerosolymam, quia civitas est magni regis:
     EN: or by the earth, for it is the footstool of his feet, or by Jerusalem, for it is the city of the great King.

5:36  LA: neque per caput tuum juraveris, quia non potes unum capillum album facere, aut nigrum.
     EN: Nor shall you swear by your head, because you cannot make one hair white or black.

5:37  LA: Sit autem sermo vester, est, est: non, non: quod autem his abundantius est, a malo est.
     EN: But let your speech be, Yes, yes; No, no. Whatever goes beyond these is from evil.

5:38  LA: Audistis quia dictum est: Oculum pro oculo, et dentem pro dente.
     EN: You have heard that it was said: An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.

5:39  LA: Ego autem dico vobis, non resistere malo: sed si quis te percusserit in dexteram maxillam tuam, præbe illi et alteram:
     EN: But I say to you: do not resist an evil person. If someone strikes you on your right cheek, offer him the other also.

5:40  LA: et ei, qui vult tecum judicio contendere, et tunicam tuam tollere, dimitte ei et pallium:
     EN: And if someone wishes to contend with you in court and take your tunic, let him have your cloak also.

5:41  LA: et quicumque te angariaverit mille passus, vade cum illo et alia duo.
     EN: And whoever compels you to go one thousand paces, go with him another two thousand.

5:42  LA: Qui petit a te, da ei: et volenti mutuari a te, ne avertaris.
     EN: Give to the one who asks you, and do not turn away from the one who wishes to borrow from you.

5:43  LA: Audistis quia dictum est: Diliges proximum tuum, et odio habebis inimicum tuum.
     EN: You have heard that it was said: You shall love your neighbor and hate your enemy.

5:44  LA: Ego autem dico vobis: diligite inimicos vestros, benefacite his qui oderunt vos, et orate pro persequentibus et calumniantibus vos:
     EN: But I say to you: love your enemies, do good to those who hate you, and pray for those who persecute and slander you,

5:45  LA: ut sitis filii Patris vestri, qui in cælis est: qui solem suum oriri facit super bonos et malos: et pluit super justos et injustos.
     EN: so that you may be children of your Father who is in heaven, who makes his sun rise on the good and the evil, and sends rain on the righteous and the unrighteous.

5:46  LA: Si enim diligitis eos qui vos diligunt, quam mercedem habebitis? nonne et publicani hoc faciunt?
     EN: For if you love those who love you, what reward will you have? Do not even tax collectors do this?

5:47  LA: Et si salutaveritis fratres vestros tantum, quid amplius facitis? nonne et ethnici hoc faciunt?
     EN: And if you greet only your brothers, what more are you doing? Do not even the Gentiles do this?

5:48  LA: Estote ergo vos perfecti, sicut et Pater vester cælestis perfectus est.
     EN: Therefore be perfect, as your heavenly Father is perfect.

MATTHEW 6

6:1  LA: Attendite ne justitiam vestram faciatis coram hominibus, ut videamini ab eis: alioquin mercedem non habebitis apud Patrem vestrum qui in cælis est.
     EN: Take care not to practice your righteousness before people in order to be seen by them; otherwise you will have no reward with your Father who is in heaven.

6:2  LA: Cum ergo facis eleemosynam, noli tuba canere ante te, sicut hypocritæ faciunt in synagogis, et in vicis, ut honorificentur ab hominibus. Amen dico vobis, receperunt mercedem suam.
     EN: Therefore, when you give alms, do not sound a trumpet before you, as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and streets, so that people may honor them. Amen, I say to you: they have received their reward.

6:3  LA: Te autem faciente eleemosynam, nesciat sinistra tua quid faciat dextera tua:
     EN: But when you give alms, do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing,

6:4  LA: ut sit eleemosyna tua in abscondito, et Pater tuus, qui videt in abscondito, reddet tibi.
     EN: so that your almsgiving may be in secret; and your Father, who sees in secret, will repay you.

6:5  LA: Et cum oratis, non eritis sicut hypocritæ qui amant in synagogis et in angulis platearum stantes orare, ut videantur ab hominibus: amen dico vobis, receperunt mercedem suam.
     EN: And when you pray, do not be like the hypocrites, who love to stand praying in synagogues and on street corners, so that people may see them. Amen, I say to you: they have received their reward.

6:6  LA: Tu autem cum oraveris, intra in cubiculum tuum, et clauso ostio, ora Patrem tuum in abscondito: et Pater tuus, qui videt in abscondito, reddet tibi.
     EN: But when you pray, enter your inner room, shut the door, and pray to your Father in secret; and your Father, who sees in secret, will repay you.

6:7  LA: Orantes autem, nolite multum loqui, sicut ethnici, putant enim quod in multiloquio suo exaudiantur.
     EN: When you pray, do not speak at length like the Gentiles, for they think that they will be heard because of their many words.

6:8  LA: Nolite ergo assimilari eis: scit enim Pater vester, quid opus sit vobis, antequam petatis eum.
     EN: Therefore do not be like them, for your Father knows what you need before you ask him.

6:9  LA: Sic ergo vos orabitis: Pater noster, qui es in cælis, sanctificetur nomen tuum.
     EN: Therefore pray in this way: Our Father, who is in heaven, may your name be sanctified.

6:10  LA: Adveniat regnum tuum; fiat voluntas tua, sicut in cælo et in terra.
     EN: May your kingdom come; may your will be done on earth as it is in heaven.

6:11  LA: Panem nostrum supersubstantialem da nobis hodie,
     EN: Give us today our supersubstantial bread,

6:12  LA: et dimitte nobis debita nostra, sicut et nos dimittimus debitoribus nostris.
     EN: and forgive us our debts, as we also forgive our debtors.

6:13  LA: Et ne nos inducas in tentationem, sed libera nos a malo. Amen.
     EN: And do not lead us into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

6:14  LA: Si enim dimiseritis hominibus peccata eorum: dimittet et vobis Pater vester cælestis delicta vestra.
     EN: For if you forgive people their sins, your heavenly Father will also forgive you your offenses.

6:15  LA: Si autem non dimiseritis hominibus: nec Pater vester dimittet vobis peccata vestra.
     EN: But if you do not forgive people, neither will your Father forgive you your sins.

6:16  LA: Cum autem jejunatis, nolite fieri sicut hypocritæ, tristes. Exterminant enim facies suas, ut appareant hominibus jejunantes. Amen dico vobis, quia receperunt mercedem suam.
     EN: When you fast, do not become gloomy like the hypocrites. For they disfigure their faces so that people may see that they are fasting. Amen, I say to you: they have received their reward.

6:17  LA: Tu autem, cum jejunas, unge caput tuum, et faciem tuam lava,
     EN: But when you fast, anoint your head and wash your face,

6:18  LA: ne videaris hominibus jejunans, sed Patri tuo, qui est in abscondito: et Pater tuus, qui videt in abscondito, reddet tibi.
     EN: so that your fasting may be seen by your Father who is in secret, rather than by people; and your Father, who sees in secret, will repay you.

6:19  LA: Nolite thesaurizare vobis thesauros in terra: ubi ærugo, et tinea demolitur: et ubi fures effodiunt, et furantur.
     EN: Do not store up treasures for yourselves on earth, where rust and moth destroy and where thieves break in and steal.

6:20  LA: Thesaurizate autem vobis thesauros in cælo, ubi neque ærugo, neque tinea demolitur, et ubi fures non effodiunt, nec furantur.
     EN: But store up treasures for yourselves in heaven, where neither rust nor moth destroys and where thieves do not break in or steal.

6:21  LA: Ubi enim est thesaurus tuus, ibi est et cor tuum.
     EN: For where your treasure is, there your heart is also.

6:22  LA: Lucerna corporis tui est oculus tuus. Si oculus tuus fuerit simplex, totum corpus tuum lucidum erit.
     EN: Your eye is the lamp of your body. If your eye is clear, your whole body will be full of light.

6:23  LA: Si autem oculus tuus fuerit nequam, totum corpus tuum tenebrosum erit. Si ergo lumen, quod in te est, tenebræ sunt: ipsæ tenebræ quantæ erunt?
     EN: But if your eye is evil, your whole body will be dark. If, then, the light in you is darkness, how great will that darkness be!

6:24  LA: Nemo potest duobus dominis servire: aut enim unum odio habebit, et alterum diliget: aut unum sustinebit, et alterum contemnet. Non potestis Deo servire et mammonæ.
     EN: No one can serve two masters. For either he will hate one and love the other, or he will hold to one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and mammon.

6:25  LA: Ideo dico vobis, ne solliciti sitis animæ vestræ quid manducetis, neque corpori vestro quid induamini. Nonne anima plus est quam esca, et corpus plus quam vestimentum?
     EN: Therefore I say to you: do not be anxious about your life, what you will eat, or about your body, what you will wear. Is not life more than food, and the body more than clothing?

6:26  LA: Respicite volatilia cæli, quoniam non serunt, neque metunt, neque congregant in horrea: et Pater vester cælestis pascit illa. Nonne vos magis pluris estis illis?
     EN: Look at the birds of the sky: they neither sow nor reap nor gather into barns, and your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not worth much more than they?

6:27  LA: Quis autem vestrum cogitans potest adjicere ad staturam suam cubitum unum?
     EN: Which of you by worrying can add one cubit to his stature?

6:28  LA: Et de vestimento quid solliciti estis? Considerate lilia agri quomodo crescunt: non laborant, neque nent.
     EN: And why are you anxious about clothing? Consider how the lilies of the field grow: they neither labor nor spin.

6:29  LA: Dico autem vobis, quoniam nec Salomon in omni gloria sua coopertus est sicut unum ex istis.
     EN: Yet I say to you that even Solomon in all his glory was not clothed like one of these.

6:30  LA: Si autem fœnum agri, quod hodie est, et cras in clibanum mittitur, Deus sic vestit, quanto magis vos modicæ fidei?
     EN: But if God so clothes the grass of the field, which is here today and thrown into the oven tomorrow, how much more you, people of little faith?

6:31  LA: Nolite ergo solliciti esse, dicentes: Quid manducabimus, aut quid bibemus, aut quo operiemur?
     EN: Therefore do not be anxious, saying: What will we eat? or What will we drink? or With what will we be clothed?

6:32  LA: hæc enim omnia gentes inquirunt. Scit enim Pater vester, quia his omnibus indigetis.
     EN: For the nations seek all these things, and your Father knows that you need them all.

6:33  LA: Quærite ergo primum regnum Dei, et justitiam ejus: et hæc omnia adjicientur vobis.
     EN: Therefore seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you.

6:34  LA: Nolite ergo solliciti esse in crastinum. Crastinus enim dies sollicitus erit sibi ipsi: sufficit diei malitia sua.
     EN: Therefore do not be anxious about tomorrow, for tomorrow will be anxious for itself. Each day has enough trouble of its own.

MATTHEW 7

7:1  LA: Nolite judicare, ut non judicemini.
     EN: Judge not, that you may not be judged.

7:2  LA: In quo enim judicio judicaveritis, judicabimini: et in qua mensura mensi fueritis, remetietur vobis.
     EN: For with what judgment you judge, you shall be judged: and with what measure you mete, it shall be measured to you again.

7:3  LA: Quid autem vides festucam in oculo fratris tui, et trabem in oculo tuo non vides?
     EN: And why see you the mote that is in your brother’s eye; and see not the beam that is in your own eye?

7:4  LA: aut quomodo dicis fratri tuo: Sine ejiciam festucam de oculo tuo, et ecce trabs est in oculo tuo?
     EN: Or how say you to your brother: Let me cast the mote out of your eye; and behold a beam is in your own eye?

7:5  LA: Hypocrita, ejice primum trabem de oculo tuo, et tunc videbis ejicere festucam de oculo fratris tui.
     EN: You hypocrite, cast out first the beam out of your own eye, and then shall you see to cast out the mote out of your brother’s eye.

7:6  LA: Nolite dare sanctum canibus: neque mittatis margaritas vestras ante porcos, ne forte conculcent eas pedibus suis, et conversi dirumpant vos.
     EN: Give not that which is holy to dogs; neither cast you your pearls before swine, lest they trample them under their feet, and turning upon you, they tear you.

7:7  LA: Petite, et dabitur vobis: quærite, et invenietis: pulsate, et aperietur vobis.
     EN: Ask, and it shall be given you: seek, and you shall find: knock, and it shall be opened to you.

7:8  LA: Omnis enim qui petit, accipit: et qui quærit, invenit: et pulsanti aperietur.
     EN: For everyone that asks, receives: and he that seeks, finds: and to him that knocks, it shall be opened.

7:9  LA: Aut quis est ex vobis homo, quem si petierit filius suus panem, numquid lapidem porriget ei?
     EN: Or what man is there among you, of whom if his son shall ask bread, will he reach him a stone?

7:10  LA: aut si piscem petierit, numquid serpentem porriget ei?
     EN: Or if he shall ask him a fish, will he reach him a serpent?

7:11  LA: Si ergo vos, cum sitis mali, nostis bona data dare filiis vestris: quanto magis Pater vester, qui in cælis est, dabit bona petentibus se?
     EN: If you then being evil, know how to give good gifts to your children: how much more will your Father who is in heaven, give good things to them that ask him?

7:12  LA: Omnia ergo quæcumque vultis ut faciant vobis homines, et vos facite illis. Hæc est enim lex, et prophetæ.
     EN: All things therefore whatever you would that men should do to you, do you also to them. For this is the law and the prophets.

7:13  LA: Intrate per angustam portam: quia lata porta, et spatiosa via est, quæ ducit ad perditionem, et multi sunt qui intrant per eam.
     EN: Enter you in at the narrow gate: for wide is the gate, and broad is the way that leads to destruction, and many there are who go in at that.

7:14  LA: Quam angusta porta, et arcta via est, quæ ducit ad vitam: et pauci sunt qui inveniunt eam!
     EN: How narrow is the gate, and narrow is the way that leads to life: and few there are that find it!

7:15  LA: Attendite a falsis prophetis, qui veniunt ad vos in vestimentis ovium, intrinsecus autem sunt lupi rapaces:
     EN: Beware of false prophets, who come to you in the clothing of sheep, but inwardly they are ravening wolves.

7:16  LA: a fructibus eorum cognoscetis eos. Numquid colligunt de spinis uvas, aut de tribulis ficus?
     EN: By their fruits you shall know them. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles?

7:17  LA: Sic omnis arbor bona fructus bonos facit: mala autem arbor malos fructus facit.
     EN: Even so every good tree brings forth good fruit, and the evil tree brings forth evil fruit.

7:18  LA: Non potest arbor bona malos fructus facere: neque arbor mala bonos fructus facere.
     EN: A good tree cannot bring forth evil fruit, neither can an evil tree bring forth good fruit.

7:19  LA: Omnis arbor, quæ non facit fructum bonum, excidetur, et in ignem mittetur.
     EN: Every tree that brings not forth good fruit, shall be cut down, and shall be cast into the fire.

7:20  LA: Igitur ex fructibus eorum cognoscetis eos.
     EN: Therefore by their fruits you shall know them.

7:21  LA: Non omnis qui dicit mihi, Domine, Domine, intrabit in regnum cælorum: sed qui facit voluntatem Patris mei, qui in cælis est, ipse intrabit in regnum cælorum.
     EN: Not everyone that says to me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven: but he that does the will of my Father who is in heaven, he shall enter into the kingdom of heaven.

7:22  LA: Multi dicent mihi in illa die: Domine, Domine, nonne in nomine tuo prophetavimus, et in nomine tuo dæmonia ejecimus, et in nomine tuo virtutes multas fecimus?
     EN: Many will say to me in that day: Lord, Lord, have not we prophesied in your name, and cast out demons in your name, and done many miracles in your name?

7:23  LA: Et tunc confitebor illis: Quia numquam novi vos: discedite a me, qui operamini iniquitatem.
     EN: And then will I profess to them, I never knew you: depart from me, you that work iniquity.

7:24  LA: Omnis ergo qui audit verba mea hæc, et facit ea, assimilabitur viro sapienti, qui ædificavit domum suam supra petram,
     EN: Everyone therefore that hears these my words, and does them, shall be likened to a wise man that built his house upon a rock,

7:25  LA: et descendit pluvia, et venerunt flumina, et flaverunt venti, et irruerunt in domum illam, et non cecidit: fundata enim erat super petram.
     EN: And the rain fell, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and they beat upon that house, and it fell not, for it was founded on a rock.

7:26  LA: Et omnis qui audit verba mea hæc, et non facit ea, similis erit viro stulto, qui ædificavit domum suam super arenam:
     EN: And everyone that hears these my words and does them not, shall be like a foolish man that built his house upon the sand,

7:27  LA: et descendit pluvia, et venerunt flumina, et flaverunt venti, et irruerunt in domum illam, et cecidit, et fuit ruina illius magna.
     EN: And the rain fell, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and they beat upon that house, and it fell, and great was the fall of it.

7:28  LA: Et factum est: cum consummasset Jesus verba hæc, admirabantur turbæ super doctrina ejus.
     EN: And it happened when Jesus had fully ended these words, the people were in admiration at his doctrine.

7:29  LA: Erat enim docens eos sicut potestatem habens, et non sicut scribæ eorum, et pharisæi.
     EN: For he was teaching them as one having power, and not as their scribes and Pharisees.

MATTHEW 8

8:1  LA: Cum autem descendisset de monte, secutæ sunt eum turbæ multæ:
     EN: And when he had come down from the mountain, great multitudes followed him:

8:2  LA: et ecce leprosus veniens, adorabat eum, dicens: Domine, si vis, potes me mundare.
     EN: And behold a leper came and adored him, saying: Lord, if you will, you can make me clean.

8:3  LA: Et extendens Jesus manum, tetigit eum, dicens: Volo: mundare. Et confestim mundata est lepra ejus.
     EN: And Jesus stretching forth his hand, touched him, saying: I will, be made clean. And immediately his leprosy was cleansed.

8:4  LA: Et ait illi Jesus: Vide, nemini dixeris: sed vade, ostende te sacerdoti, et offer munus, quod præcepit Moyses, in testimonium illis.
     EN: And Jesus says to him: See you tell no one: but go, show yourself to the priest, and offer the gift which Moses commanded, for a testimony to them.

8:5  LA: Cum autem introisset Capharnaum, accessit ad eum centurio, rogans eum,
     EN: And when he had entered into Capernaum, there came to him a centurion, imploring him,

8:6  LA: et dicens: Domine, puer meus jacet in domo paralyticus, et male torquetur.
     EN: And saying, Lord, my servant lies at home sick of the palsy, and is grievously tormented.

8:7  LA: Et ait illi Jesus: Ego veniam, et curabo eum.
     EN: And Jesus says to him: I will come and heal him.

8:8  LA: Et respondens centurio, ait: Domine, non sum dignus ut intres sub tectum meum: sed tantum dic verbo, et sanabitur puer meus.
     EN: And the centurion, making answer, said: Lord, I am not worthy that you should enter under my roof; but only say the word, and my servant shall be healed.

8:9  LA: Nam et ego homo sum sub potestate constitutus, habens sub me milites, et dico huic: Vade, et vadit: et alii: Veni, et venit: et servo meo: Fac hoc, et facit.
     EN: For I also am a man subject to authority, having under me soldiers; and I say to this, Go, and he goes, and to another Come, and he comes, and to my servant, Do this, and he does it.

8:10  LA: Audiens autem Jesus miratus est, et sequentibus se dixit: Amen dico vobis, non inveni tantam fidem in Israël.
     EN: And Jesus hearing this, marvelled; and said to them that followed him. Amen, I say to you, I have not found so great faith in Israel.

8:11  LA: Dico autem vobis, quod multi ab oriente et occidente venient, et recumbent cum Abraham, et Isaac, et Jacob in regno cælorum:
     EN: And I say to you that many shall come from the east and the west, and shall sit down with Abraham, and Isaac and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven:

MATTHEW 12

     EN: At that time Jesus went through the grain on the sabbath: and his disciples being hungry, began to pluck the ears, and to eat.

12:2  LA: Pharisæi autem videntes, dixerunt ei: Ecce discipuli tui faciunt quod non licet facere sabbatis.
     EN: And the Pharisees seeing them, said to him: Behold your disciples do that which is not lawful to do on the sabbath days.

12:3  LA: At ille dixit eis: Non legistis quid fecerit David, quando esuriit, et qui cum eo erant:
     EN: But he said to them: Have you not read what David did when he was hungry, and they that were with him:

     EN: How he entered into the house of God, and did eat the loaves of proposition, which it was not lawful for him to eat, nor for them that were with him, but for the priests only?

12:5  LA: aut non legistis in lege quia sabbatis sacerdotes in templo sabbatum violant, et sine crimine sunt?
     EN: Or have you not read in the law, that on the sabbath days the priests in the temple break the sabbath, and are without blame?

12:6  LA: Dico autem vobis, quia templo major est hic.
     EN: But I tell you that there is here a greater than the temple.

12:7  LA: Si autem sciretis, quid est: Misericordiam volo, et non sacrificium: numquam condemnassetis innocentes:
     EN: And if you knew what this means: I will have mercy, and not sacrifice: you would never have condemned the innocent.

12:8  LA: dominus enim est Filius hominis etiam sabbati.
     EN: For the Son of Man is Lord even of the sabbath.

12:9  LA: Et cum inde transisset, venit in synagogam eorum.
     EN: And when he had passed from there, he came into their synagogue.

     EN: And behold there was a man who had a withered hand, and they asked him, saying: Is it lawful to heal on the sabbath days? that they might accuse him.

12:11  LA: Ipse autem dixit illis: Quis erit ex vobis homo, qui habeat ovem unam, et si ceciderit hæc sabbatis in foveam, nonne tenebit et levabit eam?
     EN: But he said to them: What man shall there be among you, that has one sheep: and if the same fall into a pit on the sabbath day, will he not take hold on it and lift it up?

12:12  LA: Quanto magis melior est homo ove? itaque licet sabbatis benefacere.
     EN: How much better is a man than a sheep? Therefore it is lawful to do a good deed on the sabbath days.

12:13  LA: Tunc ait homini: Extende manum tuam. Et extendit, et restituta est sanitati sicut altera.
     EN: Then he says to the man: Stretch forth your hand; and he stretched it forth, and it was restored to health even as the other.

12:14  LA: Exeuntes autem pharisæi, consilium faciebant adversus eum, quomodo perderent eum.
     EN: And the Pharisees going out made a consultation against him, how they might destroy him.

12:15  LA: Jesus autem sciens recessit inde: et secuti sunt eum multi, et curavit eos omnes:
     EN: But Jesus knowing it, retired from there: and many followed him, and he healed them all.

12:16  LA: et præcepit eis ne manifestum eum facerent.
     EN: And he charged them that they should not make him known.

12:17  LA: Ut adimpleretur quod dictum est per Isaiam prophetam, dicentem:
     EN: That it might be fulfilled which was spoken by Isaiah the prophet, saying:

12:18  LA: Ecce puer meus, quem elegi, dilectus meus, in quo bene complacuit animæ meæ. Ponam spiritum meum super eum, et judicium gentibus nuntiabit.
     EN: Behold my servant whom I have chosen, my beloved in whom my soul has been well pleased. I will put my spirit upon him, and he shall show judgment to the Gentiles.

     EN: He shall not contend, nor cry out, neither shall any man hear his voice in the streets.

12:20  LA: arundinem quassatam non confringet, et linum fumigans non extinguet, donec ejiciat ad victoriam judicium:
     EN: The bruised reed he shall not break: and smoking flax he shall not extinguish: until he send forth judgment to victory.

12:21  LA: et in nomine ejus gentes sperabunt.
     EN: And in his name the Gentiles shall hope.

12:22  LA: Tunc oblatus est ei dæmonium habens, cæcus, et mutus, et curavit eum ita ut loqueretur, et videret.
     EN: Then was offered to him one possessed with a devil, blind and dumb: and he healed him, so that he spoke and saw.

     EN: But the Pharisees hearing it, said: This man casts not out demons but by Beelzebub the prince of the demons.

     EN: And Jesus knowing their thoughts, said to them: Every kingdom divided against itself shall be made desolate: and every city or house divided against itself shall not stand.

     EN: And if I by Beelzebub cast out demons, by whom do your children cast them out? Therefore they shall be your judges.

     EN: But if I by the Spirit of God cast out demons, then is the kingdom of God come upon you.

     EN: Or how can anyone enter into the house of the strong, and rifle his goods, unless he first bind the strong? and then he will rifle his house.

     EN: He that is not with me, is against me: and he that gathers not with me, scatters.

     EN: Therefore I say to you: Every sin and blasphemy shall be forgiven men, but the blasphemy of the Spirit shall not be forgiven.

     EN: And whoever shall speak a word against the Son of Man, it shall be forgiven him: but he that shall speak against the Holy Spirit, it shall not be forgiven him neither in this world, nor in the world to come.

     EN: Either make the tree good and its fruit good: or make the tree evil, and its fruit evil. For by the fruit the tree is known.

     EN: O generation of vipers, how can you speak good things, whereas you are evil? for out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaks.

12:35  LA: Bonus homo de bono thesauro profert bona: et malus homo de malo thesauro profert mala.
     EN: A good man out of a good treasure brings forth good things: and an evil man out of an evil treasure brings forth evil things.

12:36  LA: Dico autem vobis quoniam omne verbum otiosum, quod locuti fuerint homines, reddent rationem de eo in die judicii.
     EN: But I say to you, that every idle word that men shall speak, they shall render an account for it in the day of judgment.

12:37  LA: Ex verbis enim tuis justificaberis et ex verbis tuis condemnaberis.
     EN: For by your words you shall be justified, and by your words you shall be condemned.

12:38  LA: Tunc responderunt ei quidam de scribis et pharisæis, dicentes: Magister, volumus a te signum videre.
     EN: Then some of the scribes and Pharisees answered him, saying: Master, we would see a sign from you.

12:39  LA: Qui respondens ait illis: Generatio mala et adultera signum quærit: et signum non dabitur ei, nisi signum Jonæ prophetæ.
     EN: Who answered them: An evil and adulterous generation seeks a sign: and a sign shall not be given it, but the sign of Jonah the prophet.

     EN: The men of Nineveh shall rise in judgment with this generation, and shall condemn it: because they repented at the preaching of Jonah. And behold a greater than Jonah here.

12:42  LA: Regina austri surget in judicio cum generatione ista, et condemnabit eam: quia venit a finibus terræ audire sapientiam Salomonis, et ecce plus quam Salomon hic.
     EN: The queen of the south shall rise in judgment with this generation, and shall condemn it: because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and behold a greater than Solomon here.

12:43  LA: Cum autem immundus spiritus exierit ab homine, ambulat per loca arida, quærens requiem, et non invenit.
     EN: And when an unclean spirit has gone out of a man he walks through dry places seeking rest, and finds none.

12:44  LA: Tunc dicit: Revertar in domum meam, unde exivi. Et veniens invenit eam vacantem, scopis mundatam, et ornatam.
     EN: Then he says: I will return into my house from where I came out. And coming he finds it empty, swept, and garnished.

12:45  LA: Tunc vadit, et assumit septem alios spiritus secum nequiores se, et intrantes habitant ibi: et fiunt novissima hominis illius pejora prioribus. Sic erit et generationi huic pessimæ.
     EN: Then he goes, and takes with him seven other spirits more wicked than himself, and they enter in and dwell there: and the last state of that man is made worse than the first. So shall it be also to this wicked generation.

12:46  LA: Adhuc eo loquente ad turbas, ecce mater ejus et fratres stabant foris, quærentes loqui ei.
     EN: As he was yet speaking to the multitudes, behold his mother and his brothers stood without, seeking to speak to him.

12:47  LA: Dixit autem ei quidam: Ecce mater tua, et fratres tui foris stant quærentes te.
     EN: And one said to him: Behold your mother and your brothers stand without, seeking you.

12:48  LA: At ipse respondens dicenti sibi, ait: Quæ est mater mea, et qui sunt fratres mei?
     EN: But he answering him that told him, said: Who is my mother, and who are my brothers?

12:49  LA: Et extendens manum in discipulos suos, dixit: Ecce mater mea, et fratres mei.
     EN: And stretching forth his hand towards his disciples, he said: Behold my mother and my brothers.

12:50  LA: Quicumque enim fecerit voluntatem Patris mei, qui in cælis est, ipse meus frater, et soror, et mater est.
     EN: For whoever shall do the will of my Father, that is in heaven, he is my brother, and sister, and mother.

MATTHEW 13

13:1  LA: In illo die exiens Jesus de domo, sedebat secus mare.
     EN: The same day Jesus going out of the house, sat by the sea side.

13:2  LA: Et congregatæ sunt ad eum turbæ multæ, ita ut naviculam ascendens sederet: et omnis turba stabat in littore,
     EN: And great multitudes were gathered together to him, so that he went up into a boat and sat: and all the multitude stood on the shore.

13:3  LA: et locutus est eis multa in parabolis, dicens: Ecce exiit qui seminat, seminare.
     EN: And he spoke to them many things in parables, saying: Behold the sower went forth to sow.

13:4  LA: Et dum seminat, quædam ceciderunt secus viam, et venerunt volucres cæli, et comederunt ea.
     EN: And while he sows some fell by the way side, and the birds of the air came and ate them up.

13:5  LA: Alia autem ceciderunt in petrosa, ubi non habebant terram multam: et continuo exorta sunt, quia non habebant altitudinem terræ:
     EN: And other some fell upon stony ground, where they had not much earth: and they sprung up immediately, because they had no deepness of earth.

13:6  LA: sole autem orto æstuaverunt; et quia non habebant radicem, aruerunt.
     EN: And when the sun was up they were scorched: and because they had not root, they withered away.

13:7  LA: Alia autem ceciderunt in spinas: et creverunt spinæ, et suffocaverunt ea.
     EN: And others fell among thorns: and the thorns grew up and choked them.

13:8  LA: Alia autem ceciderunt in terram bonam: et dabant fructum, aliud centesimum, aliud sexagesimum, aliud trigesimum.
     EN: And others fell upon good ground: and they brought forth fruit, some a hundred fold, some sixty fold, and some thirty fold.

13:9  LA: Qui habet aures audiendi, audiat.
     EN: He that has ears to hear, let him hear.

13:10  LA: Et accedentes discipuli dixerunt ei: Quare in parabolis loqueris eis?
     EN: And his disciples came and said to him: Why speak you to them in parables?

13:11  LA: Qui respondens, ait illis: Quia vobis datum est nosse mysteria regni cælorum: illis autem non est datum.
     EN: Who answered them: Because to you it is given to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven: but to them it is not given.

13:12  LA: Qui enim habet, dabitur ei, et abundabit: qui autem non habet, et quod habet auferetur ab eo.
     EN: For he that has, to him shall be given, and he shall abound: but he that has not, from him shall be taken away that also which he has.

13:13  LA: Ideo in parabolis loquor eis: quia videntes non vident, et audientes non audiunt, neque intelligunt.
     EN: Therefore do I speak to them in parables: because seeing they see not, and hearing they hear not, neither do they understand.

13:14  LA: Et adimpletur in eis prophetia Isaiæ, dicentis: Auditu audietis, et non intelligetis: et videntes videbitis, et non videbitis.
     EN: And the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled in them, who says: By hearing you shall hear, and shall not understand: and seeing you shall see, and shall not perceive.

13:15  LA: Incrassatum est enim cor populi hujus, et auribus graviter audierunt, et oculos suos clauserunt: nequando videant oculis, et auribus audiant, et corde intelligant, et convertantur, et sanem eos.
     EN: For the heart of this people is grown gross, and with their ears they have been dull of hearing, and their eyes they have shut: lest at any time they should see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and be converted, and I should heal them.

13:16  LA: Vestri autem beati oculi quia vident, et aures vestræ quia audiunt.
     EN: But blessed are your eyes, because they see, and your ears, because they hear.

13:17  LA: Amen quippe dico vobis, quia multi prophetæ et justi cupierunt videre quæ videtis, et non viderunt: et audire quæ auditis, et non audierunt.
     EN: For, amen, I say to you, many prophets and just men have desired to see the things that you see, and have not seen them: and to hear the things that you hear and have not heard them.

13:18  LA: Vos ergo audite parabolam seminantis.
     EN: Hear you therefore the parable of the sower.

13:19  LA: Omnis qui audit verbum regni, et non intelligit, venit malus, et rapit quod seminatum est in corde ejus: hic est qui secus viam seminatus est.
     EN: When anyone hears the word of the kingdom, and understands it not, there comes the wicked one, and catches away that which was sown in his heart: this is he that received the seed by the way side.

13:20  LA: Qui autem super petrosa seminatus est, hic est qui verbum audit, et continuo cum gaudio accipit illud:
     EN: And he that received the seed upon stony ground, is he that hears the word, and immediately receives it with joy.

13:21  LA: non habet autem in se radicem, sed est temporalis: facta autem tribulatione et persecutione propter verbum, continuo scandalizatur.
     EN: But he has no root in himself and lasts only for a time. When trouble and persecution arise because of the word, he immediately stumbles.

13:22  LA: Qui autem seminatus est in spinis, hic est qui verbum audit, et sollicitudo sæculi istius, et fallacia divitiarum suffocat verbum, et sine fructu efficitur.
     EN: And he that received the seed among thorns, is he that hears the word, and the care of this world and the deceitfulness of riches chokes up the word, and he becomes fruitless.

13:23  LA: Qui vero in terram bonam seminatus est, hic est qui audit verbum, et intelligit, et fructum affert, et facit aliud quidem centesimum, aliud autem sexagesimum, aliud vero trigesimum.
     EN: But he that received the seed upon good ground, is he that hears the word, and understands, and bears fruit, and yields the one an hundredfold, and another sixty, and another thirty.

13:24  LA: Aliam parabolam proposuit illis, dicens: Simile factum est regnum cælorum homini, qui seminavit bonum semen in agro suo:
     EN: Another parable he proposed to them, saying: The kingdom of heaven is likened to a man that sowed good seed in his field.

13:25  LA: cum autem dormirent homines, venit inimicus ejus, et superseminavit zizania in medio tritici, et abiit.
     EN: But while men were asleep, his enemy came and oversowed weeds among the wheat and went his way.

13:26  LA: Cum autem crevisset herba, et fructum fecisset, tunc apparuerunt et zizania.
     EN: And when the blade was sprung up, and had brought forth fruit, then appeared also the weeds.

13:27  LA: Accedentes autem servi patrisfamilias, dixerunt ei: Domine, nonne bonum semen seminasti in agro tuo? unde ergo habet zizania?
     EN: And the servants of the good man of the house coming said to him. Sir, did you not sow good seed in your field? From where then has it weeds?

13:28  LA: Et ait illis: Inimicus homo hoc fecit. Servi autem dixerunt ei: Vis, imus, et colligimus ea?
     EN: And he said to them: An enemy has done this. And the servants said to him: Will you that we go and gather it up?

13:29  LA: Et ait: Non: ne forte colligentes zizania, eradicetis simul cum eis et triticum.
     EN: And he said: No, lest gathering up the weeds, you root up the wheat also together with it.

13:30  LA: Sinite utraque crescere usque ad messem, et in tempore messis dicam messoribus: Colligite primum zizania, et alligate ea in fasciculos ad comburendum: triticum autem congregate in horreum meum.
     EN: Suffer both to grow until the harvest, and in the time of the harvest I will say to the reapers: Gather up first the weeds, and bind it into bundles to burn, but the wheat gather you into my barn.

     EN: And they sent to him their disciples with the Herodians, saying: Master, we know that you are a true speaker and teach the way of God in truth. Neither care you for any man: for you do not regard the person of men.

     EN: Tell us therefore what do you think? Is it lawful to give tribute to Caesar, or not?

22:18  LA: Cognita autem Jesus nequitia eorum, ait: Quid me tentatis, hypocritæ?
     EN: But Jesus knowing their wickedness, said: Why do you tempt me, you hypocrites?

22:19  LA: ostendite mihi numisma census. At illi obtulerunt ei denarium.
     EN: Show me the coin of the tribute. And they offered him a denarius.

22:20  LA: Et ait illis Jesus: Cujus est imago hæc, et superscriptio?
     EN: And Jesus says to them: Whose image and inscription is this?

22:21  LA: Dicunt ei: Cæsaris. Tunc ait illis: Reddite ergo quæ sunt Cæsaris, Cæsari: et quæ sunt Dei, Deo.
     EN: They say to him: Caesar’s. Then he says to them: Render therefore to Caesar the things that are Caesar’s; and to God, the things that are God’s.

22:22  LA: Et audientes mirati sunt, et relicto eo abierunt.
     EN: And hearing this, they wondered and, leaving him, went their ways.

22:23  LA: In illo die accesserunt ad eum sadducæi, qui dicunt non esse resurrectionem: et interrogaverunt eum,
     EN: That day there came to him the Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection; and asked him,

22:24  LA: dicentes: Magister, Moyses dixit: Si quis mortuus fuerit non habens filium, ut ducat frater ejus uxorem illius, et suscitet semen fratri suo.
     EN: Saying: Master, Moses said: If a man die having no son, his brother shall marry his wife and raise up issue to his brother.

22:25  LA: Erant autem apud nos septem fratres: et primus, uxore ducta, defunctus est: et non habens semen, reliquit uxorem suam fratri suo.
     EN: Now there were with us seven brothers: and the first having married a wife, died; and not having issue, left his wife to his brother.

22:26  LA: Similiter secundus, et tertius usque ad septimum.
     EN: In like manner the second and the third and so on, to the seventh.

22:27  LA: Novissime autem omnium et mulier defuncta est.
     EN: And last of all the woman died also.

22:28  LA: In resurrectione ergo cujus erit de septem uxor? omnes enim habuerunt eam.
     EN: At the resurrection therefore, whose wife of the seven shall she be? For they all had her.

22:29  LA: Respondens autem Jesus, ait illis: Erratis nescientes Scripturas, neque virtutem Dei.
     EN: And Jesus answered them: You err, not knowing the Scriptures nor the power of God.

22:30  LA: In resurrectione enim neque nubent, neque nubentur: sed erunt sicut angeli Dei in cælo.
     EN: For in the resurrection they shall neither marry nor be married, but shall be as the angels of God in heaven.

22:31  LA: De resurrectione autem mortuorum non legistis quod dictum est a Deo dicente vobis:
     EN: And concerning the resurrection of the dead, have you not read that which was spoken by God, saying to you:

22:32  LA: Ego sum Deus Abraham, et Deus Isaac, et Deus Jacob? Non est Deus mortuorum, sed viventium.
     EN: I am the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob? He is not the God of the dead but of the living.

22:33  LA: Et audientes turbæ, mirabantur in doctrina ejus.
     EN: And the multitudes hearing it were in admiration at his doctrine.

22:34  LA: Pharisæi autem audientes quod silentium imposuisset sadducæis, convenerunt in unum:
     EN: But the Pharisees, hearing that he had silenced the Sadducees, came together.

22:35  LA: et interrogavit eum unus ex eis legis doctor, tentans eum:
     EN: And one of them, a doctor of the law, asked him, tempting him:

22:36  LA: Magister, quod est mandatum magnum in lege?
     EN: Master, which is the great commandment in the law?

22:37  LA: Ait illi Jesus: Diliges Dominum Deum tuum ex toto corde tuo, et in tota anima tua, et in tota mente tua.
     EN: Jesus said to him: You shall love the Lord your God with your whole heart and with your whole soul and with your whole mind.

22:38  LA: Hoc est maximum, et primum mandatum.
     EN: This is the greatest and the first commandment.

22:39  LA: Secundum autem simile est huic: Diliges proximum tuum, sicut teipsum.
     EN: And the second is like to this: You shall love your neighbour as yourself.

22:40  LA: In his duobus mandatis universa lex pendet, et prophetæ.
     EN: On these two commandments depends the whole law and the prophets.

22:41  LA: Congregatis autem pharisæis, interrogavit eos Jesus,
     EN: And the Pharisees being gathered together, Jesus asked them,

22:42  LA: dicens: Quid vobis videtur de Christo? cujus filius est? Dicunt ei: David.
     EN: Saying: What think you of Christ? Whose son is he? They say to him: David’s.

22:43  LA: Ait illis: Quomodo ergo David in spiritu vocat eum Dominum, dicens:
     EN: He says to them: How then does David in spirit call him Lord, saying:

22:44  LA: Dixit Dominus Domino meo: Sede a dextris meis, donec ponam inimicos tuos scabellum pedum tuorum?
     EN: The Lord said to my Lord: Sit on my right hand, until I make your enemies your footstool?

22:45  LA: Si ergo David vocat eum Dominum, quomodo filius ejus est?
     EN: If David then call him Lord, how is he his son?

22:46  LA: Et nemo poterat ei respondere verbum: neque ausus fuit quisquam ex illa die eum amplius interrogare.
     EN: And no one was able to answer him a word: neither dared any man from that day forth ask him any more questions.

MATTHEW 23

23:1  LA: Tunc Jesus locutus est ad turbas, et ad discipulos suos,
     EN: Then Jesus spoke to the multitudes and to his disciples,

23:2  LA: dicens: Super cathedram Moysi sederunt scribæ et pharisæi.
     EN: Saying: The scribes and the Pharisees have sitten on the chair of Moses.

23:3  LA: Omnia ergo quæcumque dixerint vobis, servate, et facite: secundum opera vero eorum nolite facere: dicunt enim, et non faciunt.
     EN: All things therefore whatever they shall say to you, observe and do: but according to their works do you not. For they say, and do not.

23:4  LA: Alligant enim onera gravia, et importabilia, et imponunt in humeros hominum: digito autem suo nolunt ea movere.
     EN: For they bind heavy and insupportable burdens and lay them on men’s shoulders: but with a finger of their own they will not move them.

23:5  LA: Omnia vero opera sua faciunt ut videantur ab hominibus: dilatant enim phylacteria sua, et magnificant fimbrias.
     EN: And all their works they do for to be seen of men. For they make their phylacteries broad and enlarge their fringes.

23:6  LA: Amant autem primos recubitus in cœnis, et primas cathedras in synagogis,
     EN: And they love the first places at feasts and the first chairs in the synagogues,

23:7  LA: et salutationes in foro, et vocari ab hominibus Rabbi.
     EN: And salutations in the market place, and to be called by men, Rabbi.

23:8  LA: Vos autem nolite vocari Rabbi: unus est enim magister vester, omnes autem vos fratres estis.
     EN: But do not be called Rabbi. For one is your master: and all you are brothers.

23:9  LA: Et patrem nolite vocare vobis super terram: unus est enim pater vester qui in cælis est.
     EN: And call none your father upon earth; for one is your father, who is in heaven.

23:10  LA: Nec vocemini magistri: quia magister vester unus est, Christus.
     EN: Neither be called masters: for one is your master, Christ.

23:11  LA: Qui major est vestrum, erit minister vester.
     EN: He that is the greatest among you shall be your servant.

23:12  LA: Qui autem se exaltaverit, humiliabitur: et qui se humiliaverit, exaltabitur.
     EN: And whoever shall exalt himself shall be humbled: and he that shall humble himself shall be exalted.

23:13  LA: Væ autem vobis scribæ et pharisæi hypocritæ, quia clauditis regnum cælorum ante homines! vos enim non intratis, nec introëuntes sinitis intrare.
     EN: But woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you shut the kingdom of heaven against men: for you yourselves do not enter in and those that are going in, you suffer not to enter.

23:15  LA: Væ vobis scribæ et pharisæi hypocritæ, quia circuitis mare, et aridam, ut faciatis unum proselytum, et cum fuerit factus, facitis eum filium gehennæ duplo quam vos.
     EN: Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you go round about the sea and the land to make one proselyte. And when he is made, you make him the child of Gehenna twofold more than yourselves.

23:16  LA: Væ vobis duces cæci, qui dicitis: Quicumque juraverit per templum, nihil est: qui autem juraverit in auro templi, debet.
     EN: Woe to you, blind guides, that say, Whoever shall swear by the temple, it is nothing; but he that shall swear by the gold of the temple is a debtor.

23:17  LA: Stulti et cæci: quid enim majus est? aurum, an templum, quod sanctificat aurum?
     EN: You foolish and blind: for whether is greater, the gold or the temple that sanctifies the gold?

23:18  LA: Et quicumque juraverit in altari, nihil est: quicumque autem juraverit in dono, quod est super illud, debet.
     EN: And whoever shall swear by the altar, it is nothing; but whoever shall swear by the gift that is upon it is a debtor.

23:19  LA: Cæci: quid enim majus est, donum, an altare, quod sanctificat donum?
     EN: You blind: for whether is greater, the gift or the altar that sanctifies the gift?

23:20  LA: Qui ergo jurat in altari, jurat in eo, et in omnibus quæ super illud sunt.
     EN: He therefore that swears by the altar swears by it and by all things that are upon it.

23:21  LA: Et quicumque juraverit in templo, jurat in illo, et in eo qui habitat in ipso:
     EN: And whoever shall swear by the temple swears by it and by him that dwells in it.

23:22  LA: et qui jurat in cælo, jurat in throno Dei, et in eo qui sedet super eum.
     EN: And he that swears by heaven swears by the throne of God and by him that sits thereon.

23:23  LA: Væ vobis scribæ et pharisæi hypocritæ, qui decimatis mentham, et anethum, et cyminum, et reliquistis quæ graviora sunt legis, judicium, et misericordiam, et fidem! hæc oportuit facere, et illa non omittere.
     EN: Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites; because you tithe mint and anise and cummin and have left the weightier things of the law: judgment and mercy and faith. These things you ought to have done and not to leave those undone.

23:24  LA: Duces cæci, excolantes culicem, camelum autem glutientes.
     EN: Blind guides, who strain out a gnat and swallow a camel.

23:25  LA: Væ vobis scribæ et pharisæi hypocritæ, quia mundatis quod deforis est calicis et paropsidis; intus autem pleni estis rapina et immunditia!
     EN: Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites; because you make clean the outside of the cup and of the dish, but within you are full of rapine and uncleanness.

23:26  LA: Pharisæe cæce, munda prius quod intus est calicis, et paropsidis, ut fiat id, quod deforis est, mundum.
     EN: You blind Pharisee, first make clean the inside of the cup and of the dish, that the outside may become clean.

23:27  LA: Væ vobis scribæ et pharisæi hypocritæ, quia similes estis sepulchris dealbatis, quæ a foris parent hominibus speciosa, intus vero pleni sunt ossibus mortuorum, et omni spurcitia!
     EN: Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites; because you are like to whitewashed tombs, which outwardly appear to men beautiful but within are full of dead men’s bones and of all filthiness.

23:28  LA: Sic et vos a foris quidem paretis hominibus justi: intus autem pleni estis hypocrisi et iniquitate.
     EN: So you also outwardly indeed appear to men just: but inwardly you are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.

23:29  LA: Væ vobis scribæ et pharisæi hypocritæ, qui ædificatis sepulchra prophetarum, et ornatis monumenta justorum,
     EN: Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, that build the tombs of the prophets and adorn the monuments of the just,

23:30  LA: et dicitis: Si fuissemus in diebus patrum nostrorum, non essemus socii eorum in sanguine prophetarum!
     EN: And say: If we had been in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.

23:31  LA: itaque testimonio estis vobismetipsis, quia filii estis eorum, qui prophetas occiderunt.
     EN: Therefore you are witnesses against yourselves, that you are the sons of them that killed the prophets.

23:32  LA: Et vos implete mensuram patrum vestrorum.
     EN: Fill you up then the measure of your fathers.

23:33  LA: Serpentes, genimina viperarum, quomodo fugietis a judicio gehennæ?
     EN: You serpents, generation of vipers, how will you flee from the judgment of Gehenna?

23:34  LA: Ideo ecce ego mitto ad vos prophetas, et sapientes, et scribas, et ex illis occidetis, et crucifigetis, et ex eis flagellabitis in synagogis vestris, et persequemini de civitate in civitatem:
     EN: Therefore behold I send to you prophets and wise men and scribes: and some of them you will put to death and crucify: and some you will flog in your synagogues and persecute from city to city.

23:35  LA: ut veniat super vos omnis sanguis justus, qui effusus est super terram, a sanguine Abel justi usque ad sanguinem Zachariæ, filii Barachiæ, quem occidistis inter templum et altare.
     EN: That upon you may come all the just blood that has been shed upon the earth, from the blood of Abel the just, even to the blood of Zacharias the son of Barachias, whom you killed between the temple and the altar.

23:36  LA: Amen dico vobis, venient hæc omnia super generationem istam.
     EN: Amen, I say to you, all these things shall come upon this generation.

23:37  LA: Jerusalem, Jerusalem, quæ occidis prophetas, et lapidas eos, qui ad te missi sunt, quoties volui congregare filios tuos, quemadmodum gallina congregat pullos suos sub alas, et noluisti?
     EN: Jerusalem, Jerusalem, you that kill the prophets and stone them that are sent to you, how often would I have gathered together your children, as the hen does gather her chickens under her wings, and you would not?

23:38  LA: Ecce relinquetur vobis domus vestra deserta.
     EN: Behold, your house shall be left to you, desolate.

23:39  LA: Dico enim vobis, non me videbitis amodo, donec dicatis: Benedictus, qui venit in nomine Domini.
     EN: For I say to you, you shall not see me from now on until you say: Blessed is he that comes in the name of the Lord.

MATTHEW 24

24:1  LA: Et egressus Jesus de templo, ibat. Et accesserunt discipuli ejus, ut ostenderent ei ædificationes templi.
     EN: And Jesus being come out of the temple, went away. And his disciples came to show him the buildings of the temple.

24:2  LA: Ipse autem respondens dixit illis: Videtis hæc omnia? amen dico vobis, non relinquetur hic lapis super lapidem, qui non destruatur.
     EN: And he answered them: Do you see all these things? Amen, I say to you, there shall not be left here a stone upon a stone that shall not be destroyed.

24:3  LA: Sedente autem eo super montem Oliveti, accesserunt ad eum discipuli secreto, dicentes: Dic nobis, quando hæc erunt? et quod signum adventus tui, et consummationis sæculi?
     EN: And when he was sitting on mount Olivet, the disciples came to him privately, saying: Tell us when shall these things be? And what shall be the sign of your coming and of the consummation of the world?

24:4  LA: Et respondens Jesus, dixit eis: Videte ne quis vos seducat:
     EN: And Jesus answered them: Take care that no one seduce you.

24:5  LA: multi enim venient in nomine meo, dicentes: Ego sum Christus: et multos seducent.
     EN: For many will come in my name saying, I am Christ. And they will seduce many.

24:6  LA: Audituri enim estis prælia, et opiniones præliorum. Videte ne turbemini: oportet enim hæc fieri, sed nondum est finis:
     EN: And you shall hear of wars and rumours of wars. See that you do not be troubled. For these things must happen: but the end is not yet.

24:7  LA: consurget enim gens in gentem, et regnum in regnum, et erunt pestilentiæ, et fames, et terræmotus per loca:
     EN: For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom: And there shall be pestilences and famines and earthquakes in places.

24:8  LA: hæc autem omnia initia sunt dolorum.
     EN: Now all these are the beginnings of sorrows.

24:9  LA: Tunc tradent vos in tribulationem, et occident vos: et eritis odio omnibus gentibus propter nomen meum.
     EN: Then shall they deliver you up to be afflicted and shall put you to death: and you shall be hated by all nations for my name’s sake.

24:10  LA: Et tunc scandalizabuntur multi, et invicem tradent, et odio habebunt invicem.
     EN: Then many will stumble, betray one another, and hate one another.

24:11  LA: Et multi pseudoprophetæ surgent, et seducent multos.
     EN: And many false prophets shall rise and shall seduce many.

24:12  LA: Et quoniam abundavit iniquitas, refrigescet caritas multorum:
     EN: And because iniquity has abounded, the charity of many shall grow cold.

24:13  LA: qui autem perseveraverit usque in finem, hic salvus erit.
     EN: But he that shall persevere to the end, he shall be saved.

24:14  LA: Et prædicabitur hoc Evangelium regni in universo orbe, in testimonium omnibus gentibus: et tunc veniet consummatio.
     EN: And this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in the whole world, for a testimony to all nations: and then shall the consummation come.

24:15  LA: Cum ergo videritis abominationem desolationis, quæ dicta est a Daniele propheta, stantem in loco sancto, qui legit, intelligat:
     EN: When therefore you shall see the abomination of desolation, which was spoken of by Daniel the prophet, standing in the holy place: he that reads let him understand.

24:16  LA: tunc qui in Judæa sunt, fugiant ad montes:
     EN: Then they that are in Judea, let them flee to the mountains:

24:17  LA: et qui in tecto, non descendat tollere aliquid de domo sua:
     EN: And he that is on the housetop, let him not come down to take anything out of his house:

24:18  LA: et qui in agro, non revertatur tollere tunicam suam.
     EN: And he that is in the field, let him not go back to take his tunic.

24:19  LA: Væ autem prægnantibus et nutrientibus in illis diebus!
     EN: And woe to them that are with child and that give suck in those days.

24:20  LA: Orate autem ut non fiat fuga vestra in hieme, vel sabbato:
     EN: But pray that your flight do not be in the winter or on the sabbath.

24:21  LA: erit enim tunc tribulatio magna, qualis non fuit ab initio mundi usque modo, neque fiet.
     EN: For there shall be then great tribulation, such as has not been from the beginning of the world until now, neither shall be.

24:22  LA: Et nisi breviati fuissent dies illi, non fieret salva omnis caro: sed propter electos breviabuntur dies illi.
     EN: And unless those days had been shortened, no flesh should be saved: but for the sake of the elect those days shall be shortened.

24:23  LA: Tunc si quis vobis dixerit: Ecce hic est Christus, aut illic: nolite credere.
     EN: Then if any man shall say to you, Lo here is Christ, or there: do not believe him.

24:24  LA: Surgent enim pseudochristi, et pseudoprophetæ: et dabunt signa magna, et prodigia, ita ut in errorem inducantur (si fieri potest) etiam electi.
     EN: For there shall arise false Christs and false prophets and shall show great signs and wonders, so much as to deceive (if possible) even the elect.

24:25  LA: Ecce prædixi vobis.
     EN: Behold I have told it to you, beforehand.

24:26  LA: Si ergo dixerint vobis: Ecce in deserto est, nolite exire; Ecce in penetralibus, nolite credere.
     EN: If therefore they shall say to you, Behold he is in the desert: go not out. Behold he is in the closets: believe it not.

24:27  LA: Sicut enim fulgur exit ab oriente, et paret usque in occidentem: ita erit et adventus Filii hominis.
     EN: For as lightning comes out of the east and appears even into the west: so shall also the coming of the Son of Man be.

24:28  LA: Ubicumque fuerit corpus, illic congregabuntur et aquilæ.
     EN: Wherever the body shall be, there shall the eagles also be gathered together.

24:29  LA: Statim autem post tribulationem dierum illorum sol obscurabitur, et luna non dabit lumen suum, et stellæ cadent de cælo, et virtutes cælorum commovebuntur:
     EN: And immediately after the tribulation of those days, the sun shall be darkened and the moon shall not give her light and the stars shall fall from heaven and the powers of heaven shall be moved.

24:30  LA: et tunc parebit signum Filii hominis in cælo: et tunc plangent omnes tribus terræ: et videbunt Filium hominis venientem in nubibus cæli cum virtute multa et majestate.
     EN: And then shall appear the sign of the Son of Man in heaven. And then shall all tribes of the earth mourn: and they shall see the Son of Man coming in the clouds of heaven with much power and majesty.

24:31  LA: Et mittet angelos suos cum tuba, et voce magna: et congregabunt electos ejus a quatuor ventis, a summis cælorum usque ad terminos eorum.
     EN: And he shall send his angels with a trumpet and a great voice: and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from the farthest parts of the heavens to the utmost bounds of them.

24:32  LA: Ab arbore autem fici discite parabolam: cum jam ramus ejus tener fuerit, et folia nata, scitis quia prope est æstas:
     EN: And from the fig tree learn a parable: When the branch of it is now tender and the leaves come forth, you know that summer is near.

24:33  LA: ita et vos cum videritis hæc omnia, scitote quia prope est, in januis.
     EN: So you also, when you shall see all these things, know you that it is near, even at the doors.

24:34  LA: Amen dico vobis, quia non præteribit generatio hæc, donec omnia hæc fiant.
     EN: Amen, I say to you that this generation shall not pass until all these things be done.

24:35  LA: Cælum et terra transibunt, verba autem mea non præteribunt.
     EN: Heaven and earth shall pass: but my words shall not pass.

24:36  LA: De die autem illa et hora nemo scit, neque angeli cælorum, nisi solus Pater.
     EN: But of that day and hour no one knows: no, not the angels of heaven, but the Father alone.

24:37  LA: Sicut autem in diebus Noë, ita erit et adventus Filii hominis:
     EN: And as in the days of Noah, so shall also the coming of the Son of Man be.

24:38  LA: sicut enim erant in diebus ante diluvium comedentes et bibentes, nubentes et nuptum tradentes, usque ad eum diem, quo intravit Noë in arcam,
     EN: For, as in the days before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, even until that day in which Noah entered into the ark:

24:39  LA: et non cognoverunt donec venit diluvium, et tulit omnes: ita erit et adventus Filii hominis.
     EN: And they knew not until the flood came and took them all away: so also shall the coming of the Son of Man be.

24:40  LA: Tunc duo erunt in agro: unus assumetur, et unus relinquetur.
     EN: Then two shall be in the field. One shall be taken and one shall be left.

24:41  LA: Duæ molentes in mola: una assumetur, et una relinquetur.
     EN: Two women shall be grinding at the mill. One shall be taken and one shall be left.

24:42  LA: Vigilate ergo, quia nescitis qua hora Dominus vester venturus sit.
     EN: Watch you therefore, because you do not know what hour your Lord will come.

24:43  LA: Illud autem scitote, quoniam si sciret paterfamilias qua hora fur venturus esset, vigilaret utique, et non sineret perfodi domum suam.
     EN: But this know you, that, if the goodman of the house knew at what hour the thief would come, he would certainly watch and would not suffer his house to be broken open.

24:44  LA: Ideo et vos estote parati: quia qua nescitis hora Filius hominis venturus est.
     EN: Therefore be also ready, because at what hour you do not know the Son of Man will come.

24:45  LA: Quis, putas, est fidelis servus, et prudens, quem constituit dominus suus super familiam suam ut det illis cibum in tempore?
     EN: Who, do you think, is the faithful and prudent servant whom his master has appointed over his household to give them food at the proper time?

24:46  LA: Beatus ille servus, quem cum venerit dominus ejus, invenerit sic facientem.
     EN: Blessed is that servant, whom when his lord shall come he shall find so doing.

24:47  LA: Amen dico vobis, quoniam super omnia bona sua constituet eum.
     EN: Amen, I say to you: he shall place him over all his goods.

24:48  LA: Si autem dixerit malus servus ille in corde suo: Moram fecit dominus meus venire:
     EN: But if that evil servant shall say in his heart: My lord is long a coming:

24:49  LA: et cœperit percutere conservos suos, manducet autem et bibat cum ebriosis:
     EN: And shall begin to strike his fellow servants and shall eat and drink with drunkards:

24:50  LA: veniet dominus servi illius in die qua non sperat, et hora qua ignorat:
     EN: The lord of that servant shall come in a day that he hopes not and at an hour that he does not know:

24:51  LA: et dividet eum, partemque ejus ponet cum hypocritis: illic erit fletus et stridor dentium.
     EN: And shall separate him and appoint his portion with the hypocrites. There shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.

MATTHEW 25

25:1  LA: Tunc simile erit regnum cælorum decem virginibus: quæ accipientes lampades suas exierunt obviam sponso et sponsæ.
     EN: Then shall the kingdom of heaven be like to ten virgins, who taking their lamps went out to meet the bridegroom and the bride.

25:2  LA: Quinque autem ex eis erant fatuæ, et quinque prudentes:
     EN: And five of them were foolish and five wise.

25:3  LA: sed quinque fatuæ, acceptis lampadibus, non sumpserunt oleum secum:
     EN: But the five foolish, having taken their lamps, did not take oil with them.

25:4  LA: prudentes vero acceperunt oleum in vasis suis cum lampadibus.
     EN: But the wise took oil in their vessels with the lamps.

25:5  LA: Moram autem faciente sponso, dormitaverunt omnes et dormierunt.
     EN: And the bridegroom tarrying, they all slumbered and slept.

25:6  LA: Media autem nocte clamor factus est: Ecce sponsus venit, exite obviam ei.
     EN: And at midnight there was a cry made: Behold the bridegroom comes. Go forth to meet him.

25:7  LA: Tunc surrexerunt omnes virgines illæ, et ornaverunt lampades suas.
     EN: Then all those virgins arose and trimmed their lamps.

25:8  LA: Fatuæ autem sapientibus dixerunt: Date nobis de oleo vestro, quia lampades nostræ extinguuntur.
     EN: And the foolish said to the wise: Give us of your oil, for our lamps are going out.

25:9  LA: Responderunt prudentes, dicentes: Ne forte non sufficiat nobis, et vobis, ite potius ad vendentes, et emite vobis.
     EN: The wise answered, saying: Lest perhaps there do not be enough for us and for you, go rather to them that sell and buy for yourselves.

25:10  LA: Dum autem irent emere, venit sponsus: et quæ paratæ erant, intraverunt cum eo ad nuptias, et clausa est janua.
     EN: Now while they went to buy the bridegroom came: and they that were ready went in with him to the marriage. And the door was shut.

25:11  LA: Novissime vero veniunt et reliquæ virgines, dicentes: Domine, domine, aperi nobis.
     EN: But at last came also the other virgins, saying: Lord, Lord, open to us.

25:12  LA: At ille respondens, ait: Amen dico vobis, nescio vos.
     EN: But he answered: Amen, I say to you, I do not know you.

25:13  LA: Vigilate itaque, quia nescitis diem, neque horam.
     EN: Watch you therefore, because you do not know the day nor the hour.

25:14  LA: Sicut enim homo peregre proficiscens, vocavit servos suos, et tradidit illis bona sua.
     EN: For even as a man going into a far country called his servants and delivered to them his goods;

25:15  LA: Et uni dedit quinque talenta, alii autem duo, alii vero unum, unicuique secundum propriam virtutem: et profectus est statim.
     EN: And to one he gave five talents, and to another two, and to another one, to everyone according to his proper ability: and immediately he took his journey.

25:16  LA: Abiit autem qui quinque talenta acceperat, et operatus est in eis, et lucratus est alia quinque.
     EN: And he that had received the five talents went his way and traded with the same and gained other five.

25:17  LA: Similiter et qui duo acceperat, lucratus est alia duo.
     EN: And in like manner he that had received the two gained other two.

25:18  LA: Qui autem unum acceperat, abiens fodit in terram, et abscondit pecuniam domini sui.
     EN: But he that had received the one, going his way, digged into the earth and hid his lord’s money.

25:19  LA: Post multum vero temporis venit dominus servorum illorum, et posuit rationem cum eis.
     EN: But after a long time the lord of those servants came and reckoned with them.

25:20  LA: Et accedens qui quinque talenta acceperat, obtulit alia quinque talenta, dicens: Domine, quinque talenta tradidisti mihi, ecce alia quinque superlucratus sum.
     EN: And he that had received the five talents coming, brought other five talents, saying: Lord, you did deliver to me five talents. Behold I have gained other five over and above.

25:21  LA: Ait illi dominus ejus: Euge serve bone, et fidelis: quia super pauca fuisti fidelis, super multa te constituam; intra in gaudium domini tui.
     EN: His lord said to him: Well done, good and faithful servant, because you have been faithful over a few things, I will place you over many things. Enter you into the joy of your lord.

25:22  LA: Accessit autem et qui duo talenta acceperat, et ait: Domine, duo talenta tradidisti mihi, ecce alia duo lucratus sum.
     EN: And he also that had received the two talents came and said: Lord, you deliveredst two talents to me. Behold I have gained other two.

25:23  LA: Ait illi dominus ejus: Euge serve bone, et fidelis: quia super pauca fuisti fidelis, super multa te constituam; intra in gaudium domini tui.
     EN: His lord said to him: Well done, good and faithful servant: because you have been faithful over a few things, I will place you over many things. Enter you into the joy of your lord.

25:24  LA: Accedens autem et qui unum talentum acceperat, ait: Domine, scio quia homo durus es; metis ubi non seminasti, et congregas ubi non sparsisti:
     EN: But he that had received the one talent, came and said: Lord, I know that you are a hard man; you reap where you have not sown and gather where you have not strewed.

25:25  LA: et timens abii, et abscondi talentum tuum in terra: ecce habes quod tuum est.
     EN: And being afraid, I went and hid your talent in the earth. Behold here you have that which is yours.

25:26  LA: Respondens autem dominus ejus, dixit ei: Serve male, et piger, sciebas quia meto ubi non semino, et congrego ubi non sparsi:
     EN: And his lord answered him: Wicked and slothful servant, you knew that I reap where I sow not and gather where I have not strewed.

25:27  LA: oportuit ergo te committere pecuniam meam numulariis, et veniens ego recepissem utique quod meum est cum usura.
     EN: You ought therefore to have committed my money to the bankers: and at my coming I should have received my own with usury.

25:28  LA: Tollite itaque ab eo talentum, et date ei qui habet decem talenta:
     EN: Take you away therefore the talent from him and give it him that has ten talents.

25:29  LA: omni enim habenti dabitur, et abundabit: ei autem qui non habet, et quod videtur habere, auferetur ab eo.
     EN: For to everyone that has shall be given, and he shall abound: but from him that has not, that also which he seems to have shall be taken away.

25:30  LA: Et inutilem servum ejicite in tenebras exteriores: illic erit fletus, et stridor dentium.
     EN: And the unprofitable servant, cast you out into the exterior darkness. There shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.

25:31  LA: Cum autem venerit Filius hominis in majestate sua, et omnes angeli cum eo, tunc sedebit super sedem majestatis suæ:
     EN: And when the Son of Man shall come in his majesty, and all the angels with him, then shall he sit upon the seat of his majesty.

25:32  LA: et congregabuntur ante eum omnes gentes, et separabit eos ab invicem, sicut pastor segregat oves ab hædis:
     EN: And all nations shall be gathered together before him: and he shall separate them one from another, as the shepherd separates the sheep from the goats:

25:33  LA: et statuet oves quidem a dextris suis, hædos autem a sinistris.
     EN: And he shall set the sheep on his right hand, but the goats on his left.

25:34  LA: Tunc dicet rex his qui a dextris ejus erunt: Venite benedicti Patris mei, possidete paratum vobis regnum a constitutione mundi:
     EN: Then shall the king say to them that shall be on his right hand: Come, you blessed of my Father, possess you the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world.

25:35  LA: esurivi enim, et dedistis mihi manducare: sitivi, et dedistis mihi bibere: hospes eram, et collegistis me:
     EN: For I was hungry, and you gave me to eat: I was thirsty, and you gave me to drink: I was a stranger, and you took me in:

25:36  LA: nudus, et cooperuistis me: infirmus, et visitastis me: in carcere eram, et venistis ad me.
     EN: Naked, and you covered me: sick, and you visited me: I was in prison, and you came to me.

25:37  LA: Tunc respondebunt ei justi, dicentes: Domine, quando te vidimus esurientem, et pavimus te: sitientem, et dedimus tibi potum?
     EN: Then shall the just answer him, saying: Lord, when did we see you hungry and fed you: thirsty and gave you drink?

25:38  LA: quando autem te vidimus hospitem, et collegimus te: aut nudum, et cooperuimus te?
     EN: Or when did we see you a stranger and took you in? Or naked and covered you?

25:39  LA: aut quando te vidimus infirmum, aut in carcere, et venimus ad te?
     EN: Or when did we see you sick or in prison and came to you?

25:40  LA: Et respondens rex, dicet illis: Amen dico vobis, quamdiu fecistis uni ex his fratribus meis minimis, mihi fecistis.
     EN: And the king answering shall say to them: Amen, I say to you, as long as you did it to one of these my least brothers, you did it to me.

25:41  LA: Tunc dicet et his qui a sinistris erunt: Discedite a me maledicti in ignem æternum, qui paratus est diabolo, et angelis ejus:
     EN: Then he shall say to them also that shall be on his left hand: Depart from me, you cursed, into everlasting fire, which was prepared for the devil and his angels.

25:42  LA: esurivi enim, et non dedistis mihi manducare: sitivi, et non dedistis mihi potum:
     EN: For I was hungry and you gave me not to eat: I was thirsty and you gave me not to drink.

25:43  LA: hospes eram, et non collegistis me: nudus, et non cooperuistis me: infirmus, et in carcere, et non visitastis me.
     EN: I was a stranger and you took me not in: naked and you covered me not: sick and in prison and you did not visit me.

25:44  LA: Tunc respondebunt ei et ipsi, dicentes: Domine, quando te vidimus esurientem, aut sitientem, aut hospitem, aut nudum, aut infirmum, aut in carcere, et non ministravimus tibi?
     EN: Then they also shall answer him, saying: Lord, when did we see you hungry or thirsty or a stranger or naked or sick or in prison and did not minister to you?

25:45  LA: Tunc respondebit illis, dicens: Amen dico vobis: Quamdiu non fecistis uni de minoribus his, nec mihi fecistis.
     EN: Then he shall answer them, saying: Amen: I say to you, as long as you did it not to one of these least, neither did you do it to me.

25:46  LA: Et ibunt hi in supplicium æternum: justi autem in vitam æternam.
     EN: And these shall go into everlasting punishment: but the just, into life everlasting.

MATTHEW 26

26:1  LA: Et factum est: cum consummasset Jesus sermones hos omnes, dixit discipulis suis:
     EN: And it happened, when Jesus had ended all these words, he said to his disciples:

26:2  LA: Scitis quia post biduum Pascha fiet, et Filius hominis tradetur ut crucifigatur.
     EN: You know that after two days shall be the pasch: and the Son of Man shall be delivered up to be crucified.

26:3  LA: Tunc congregati sunt principes sacerdotum, et seniores populi, in atrium principis sacerdotum, qui dicebatur Caiphas:
     EN: Then were gathered together the chief priests and ancients of the people, into the court of the high priest, who was called Caiaphas:

26:4  LA: et consilium fecerunt ut Jesum dolo tenerent, et occiderent.
     EN: And they consulted together that by subtilty they might apprehend Jesus and put him to death.

26:5  LA: Dicebant autem: Non in die festo, ne forte tumultus fieret in populo.
     EN: But they said: Not on the festival day, lest there should be a tumult among the people.

26:6  LA: Cum autem Jesus esset in Bethania in domo Simonis leprosi,
     EN: And when Jesus was in Bethania, in the house of Simon the leper,

26:7  LA: accessit ad eum mulier habens alabastrum unguenti pretiosi, et effudit super caput ipsius recumbentis.
     EN: There came to him a woman having an alabaster box of precious ointment and poured it on his head as he was at table.

26:8  LA: Videntes autem discipuli, indignati sunt, dicentes: Ut quid perditio hæc?
     EN: And the disciples seeing it had indignation, saying: To what purpose is this waste?

26:9  LA: potuit enim istud venundari multo, et dari pauperibus.
     EN: For this might have been sold for much and given to the poor.

26:10  LA: Sciens autem Jesus, ait illis: Quid molesti estis huic mulieri? opus enim bonum operata est in me.
     EN: And Jesus knowing it, said to them: Why do you trouble this woman? For she has wrought a good work upon me.

26:11  LA: Nam semper pauperes habetis vobiscum: me autem non semper habetis.
     EN: For the poor you have always with you: but me you have not always.

26:12  LA: Mittens enim hæc unguentum hoc in corpus meum, ad sepeliendum me fecit.
     EN: For she in pouring this ointment on my body has done it for my burial.

26:13  LA: Amen dico vobis, ubicumque prædicatum fuerit hoc Evangelium in toto mundo, dicetur et quod hæc fecit in memoriam ejus.
     EN: Amen, I say to you, wherever this gospel shall be preached in the whole world, that also which she has done shall be told for a memory of her.

26:14  LA: Tunc abiit unus de duodecim, qui dicebatur Judas Iscariotes, ad principes sacerdotum:
     EN: Then went one of the twelve, who was called Judas Iscariot, to the chief priests.

26:15  LA: et ait illis: Quid vultis mihi dare, et ego vobis eum tradam? At illi constituerunt ei triginta argenteos.
     EN: And said to them: What will you give me, and I will deliver him to you? But they appointed him thirty pieces of silver.

26:16  LA: Et exinde quærebat opportunitatem ut eum traderet.
     EN: And from thenceforth he sought opportunity to betray him.

26:17  LA: Prima autem die azymorum accesserunt discipuli ad Jesum, dicentes: Ubi vis paremus tibi comedere Pascha?
     EN: And on the first day of the Azymes, the disciples came to Jesus, saying: Where will you that we prepare for you to eat the pasch?

26:18  LA: At Jesus dixit: Ite in civitatem ad quemdam, et dicite ei: Magister dicit: Tempus meum prope est, apud te facio Pascha cum discipulis meis.
     EN: But Jesus said: Go into the city to a certain man and say to him: The master says, My time is near. With you I make the pasch with my disciples.

26:19  LA: Et fecerunt discipuli sicut constituit illis Jesus, et paraverunt Pascha.
     EN: And the disciples did as Jesus appointed to them: and they prepared the pasch.

26:20  LA: Vespere autem facto, discumbebat cum duodecim discipulis suis.
     EN: But when it was evening, he sat down with his twelve disciples.

26:21  LA: Et edentibus illis, dixit: Amen dico vobis, quia unus vestrum me traditurus est.
     EN: And while they were eating, he said: Amen, I say to you that one of you is about to betray me.

26:22  LA: Et contristati valde, cœperunt singuli dicere: Numquid ego sum Domine?
     EN: And they being very much troubled began everyone to say: Is it I, Lord?

26:23  LA: At ipse respondens, ait: Qui intingit mecum manum in paropside, hic me tradet.
     EN: But he answered: He that dips his hand with me in the dish, he shall betray me.

26:24  LA: Filius quidem hominis vadit, sicut scriptum est de illo: væ autem homini illi, per quem Filius hominis tradetur! bonum erat ei, si natus non fuisset homo ille.
     EN: The Son of Man indeed goes, as it is written of him. But woe to that man by whom the Son of Man shall be betrayed. It were better for him, if that man had not been born.

26:25  LA: Respondens autem Judas, qui tradidit eum, dixit: Numquid ego sum Rabbi? Ait illi: Tu dixisti.
     EN: And Judas that betrayed him answered: Is it I, Rabbi? He says to him: You have said it.

26:26  LA: Cœnantibus autem eis, accepit Jesus panem, et benedixit, ac fregit, deditque discipulis suis, et ait: Accipite, et comedite: hoc est corpus meum.
     EN: While they were eating, Jesus took bread, blessed and broke it, gave it to his disciples, and said: Take and eat; this is my body.

26:27  LA: Et accipiens calicem, gratias egit: et dedit illis, dicens: Bibite ex hoc omnes.
     EN: And taking a cup, he gave thanks and gave it to them, saying: Drink from this, all of you.

26:28  LA: Hic est enim sanguis meus novi testamenti, qui pro multis effundetur in remissionem peccatorum.
     EN: For this is my blood of the new covenant, which will be poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins.

26:29  LA: Dico autem vobis: non bibam amodo de hoc genimine vitis usque in diem illum, cum illud bibam vobiscum novum in regno Patris mei.
     EN: And I say to you, I will not drink from now on of this fruit of the vine until that day when I shall drink it with you new in the kingdom of my Father.

26:30  LA: Et hymno dicto, exierunt in montem Oliveti.
     EN: And a hymn being said, they went out to mount Olivet.

26:31  LA: Tunc dicit illis Jesus: Omnes vos scandalum patiemini in me in ista nocte. Scriptum est enim: Percutiam pastorem, et dispergentur oves gregis.
     EN: Then Jesus said to them: You will all stumble because of me this night. For it is written: I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will be scattered.

26:32  LA: Postquam autem resurrexero, præcedam vos in Galilæam.
     EN: But after I shall be risen again, I will go before you into Galilee.

26:33  LA: Respondens autem Petrus, ait illi: Et si omnes scandalizati fuerint in te, ego numquam scandalizabor.
     EN: Peter answered him: Even if everyone stumbles because of you, I will never stumble.

26:34  LA: Ait illi Jesus: Amen dico tibi, quia in hac nocte, antequam gallus cantet, ter me negabis.
     EN: Jesus said to him: Amen, I say to you that in this night before the rooster crow, you will deny me thrice.

26:35  LA: Ait illi Petrus: Etiamsi oportuerit me mori tecum, non te negabo. Similiter et omnes discipuli dixerunt.
     EN: Peter says to him: Yes, though I should die with you, I will not deny you. And in like manner said all the disciples.

26:36  LA: Tunc venit Jesus cum illis in villam, quæ dicitur Gethsemani, et dixit discipulis suis: Sedete hic donec vadam illuc, et orem.
     EN: Then Jesus came with them into a country place which is called Gethsemani. And he said to his disciples: Sit you here, until I go yonder and pray.

26:37  LA: Et assumpto Petro, et duobus filiis Zebedæi, cœpit contristari et mœstus esse.
     EN: And taking with him Peter and the two sons of Zebedee, he began to grow sorrowful and to be sad.

26:38  LA: Tunc ait illis: Tristis est anima mea usque ad mortem: sustinete hic, et vigilate mecum.
     EN: Then he says to them: My soul is sorrowful even to death. Stay you here and watch with me.

26:39  LA: Et progressus pusillum, procidit in faciem suam, orans, et dicens: Pater mi, si possibile est, transeat a me calix iste: verumtamen non sicut ego volo, sed sicut tu.
     EN: And going a little further, he fell upon his face, praying and saying: My Father, if it be possible, let this chalice pass from me. Nevertheless, not as I will but as you will.

26:40  LA: Et venit ad discipulos suos, et invenit eos dormientes, et dicit Petro: Sic non potuistis una hora vigilare mecum?
     EN: And he comes to his disciples and finds them asleep. And he says to Peter: What? Could you not watch one hour with me?

26:41  LA: Vigilate, et orate ut non intretis in tentationem. Spiritus quidem promptus est, caro autem infirma.
     EN: Watch you: and pray that you enter not into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh weak.

26:42  LA: Iterum secundo abiit, et oravit, dicens: Pater mi, si non potest hic calix transire nisi bibam illum, fiat voluntas tua.
     EN: Again the second time, he went and prayed, saying: My Father, if this chalice may not pass away, but I must drink it, your will be done.

26:43  LA: Et venit iterum, et invenit eos dormientes: erant enim oculi eorum gravati.
     EN: And he comes again and finds them sleeping: for their eyes were heavy.

26:44  LA: Et relictis illis, iterum abiit, et oravit tertio, eumdem sermonem dicens.
     EN: And leaving them, he went again: and he prayed the third time, saying the same word.

26:45  LA: Tunc venit ad discipulos suos, et dicit illis: Dormite jam, et requiescite: ecce appropinquavit hora, et Filius hominis tradetur in manus peccatorum.
     EN: Then he comes to his disciples and says to them: Sleep you now and take your rest. Behold the hour is near: and the Son of Man shall be betrayed into the hands of sinners.

26:46  LA: Surgite, eamus: ecce appropinquavit qui me tradet.
     EN: Rise: let us go. Behold he is near that will betray me.

     EN: As he yet spoke, behold Judas, one of the twelve, came, and with him a great multitude with swords and clubs, sent from the chief priests and the ancients of the people.

26:48  LA: Qui autem tradidit eum, dedit illis signum, dicens: Quemcumque osculatus fuero, ipse est, tenete eum.
     EN: And he that betrayed him gave them a sign, saying: Whomever I shall kiss, that is he. Hold him fast.

26:49  LA: Et confestim accedens ad Jesum, dixit: Ave Rabbi. Et osculatus est eum.
     EN: And immediately coming to Jesus, he said: Hail, Rabbi. And he kissed him.

26:50  LA: Dixitque illi Jesus: Amice, ad quid venisti? Tunc accesserunt, et manus injecerunt in Jesum, et tenuerunt eum.
     EN: And Jesus said to him: Friend, to what are you come? Then they came up and laid hands on Jesus and held him.

26:51  LA: Et ecce unus ex his qui erant cum Jesu, extendens manum, exemit gladium suum, et percutiens servum principis sacerdotum amputavit auriculam ejus.
     EN: And behold one of them that were with Jesus, stretching forth his hand, drew out his sword: and striking the servant of the high priest, cut off his ear.

26:52  LA: Tunc ait illi Jesus: Converte gladium tuum in locum suum: omnes enim, qui acceperint gladium, gladio peribunt.
     EN: Then Jesus says to him: Put up again your sword into its place: for all that take the sword shall perish with the sword.

     EN: Think you that I cannot ask my Father, and he will give me immediately more than twelve legions of angels?

26:54  LA: Quomodo ergo implebuntur Scripturæ, quia sic oportet fieri?
     EN: How then shall the scriptures be fulfilled, that so it must be done?

26:55  LA: In illa hora dixit Jesus turbis: Tamquam ad latronem existis cum gladiis et fustibus comprehendere me: quotidie apud vos sedebam docens in templo, et non me tenuistis.
     EN: In that same hour, Jesus said to the multitudes: You have come out, as it were to a robber, with swords and clubs to apprehend me. I sat daily with you, teaching in the temple: and you laid not hands on me.

26:56  LA: Hoc autem totum factum est, ut adimplerentur Scripturæ prophetarum. Tunc discipuli omnes, relicto eo, fugerunt.
     EN: Now all this was done that the scriptures of the prophets might be fulfilled. Then the disciples, all leaving him, fled.

26:57  LA: At illi tenentes Jesum, duxerunt ad Caipham principem sacerdotum, ubi scribæ et seniores convenerant.
     EN: But they holding Jesus led him to Caiaphas the high priest, where the scribes and the ancients were assembled.

26:58  LA: Petrus autem sequebatur eum a longe, usque in atrium principis sacerdotum. Et ingressus intro, sedebat cum ministris, ut videret finem.
     EN: And Peter followed him afar off, even to the court of the high priest, And going in, he sat with the servants, that he might see the end.

26:59  LA: Principes autem sacerdotum, et omne concilium, quærebant falsum testimonium contra Jesum, ut eum morti traderent:
     EN: And the chief priests and the whole council sought false witness against Jesus, that they might put him to death.

26:60  LA: et non invenerunt, cum multi falsi testes accessissent. Novissime autem venerunt duo falsi testes,
     EN: And they found not, whereas many false witnesses had come in. And last of all there came two false witnesses:

26:61  LA: et dixerunt: Hic dixit: Possum destruere templum Dei, et post triduum reædificare illud.
     EN: And they said: This man said, I am able to destroy the temple of God and after three days to rebuild it.

26:62  LA: Et surgens princeps sacerdotum, ait illi: Nihil respondes ad ea, quæ isti adversum te testificantur?
     EN: And the high priest rising up, said to him: Answer you nothing to the things which these witness against you?

26:63  LA: Jesus autem tacebat. Et princeps sacerdotum ait illi: Adjuro te per Deum vivum, ut dicas nobis si tu es Christus Filius Dei.
     EN: But Jesus held his peace. And the high priest said to him: I adjure you by the living God, that you tell us if you be the Christ the Son of God.

     EN: And they brought him bound and delivered him to Pontius Pilate the governor.

     EN: Saying: I have sinned in betraying innocent blood. But they said: What is that to us? Look you to it.

     EN: And casting down the pieces of silver in the temple, he departed and went and hung himself with an halter.

     EN: But the chief priests having taken the pieces of silver, said: It is not lawful to put them into the corbona, because it is the price of blood.

27:7  LA: Consilio autem inito, emerunt ex illis agrum figuli, in sepulturam peregrinorum.
     EN: And after they had consulted together, they bought with them the potter’s field, to be a burying place for strangers.

     EN: For this cause that field was called Haceldama, that is, the field of blood, even to this day.

27:9  LA: Tunc impletum est quod dictum est per Jeremiam prophetam, dicentem: Et acceperunt triginta argenteos pretium appretiati, quem appretiaverunt a filiis Israël:
     EN: Then was fulfilled that which was spoken by Jeremiah the prophet, saying: And they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him that was prized, whom they prized of the children of Israel.

27:10  LA: et dederunt eos in agrum figuli, sicut constituit mihi Dominus.
     EN: And they gave them to the potter’s field, as the Lord appointed to me.

27:11  LA: Jesus autem stetit ante præsidem, et interrogavit eum præses, dicens: Tu es rex Judæorum? Dicit illi Jesus: Tu dicis.
     EN: And Jesus stood before the governor, and the governor asked him, saying: Are you the king of the Jews? Jesus says to him: You say it.

27:12  LA: Et cum accusaretur a principibus sacerdotum et senioribus, nihil respondit.
     EN: And when he was accused by the chief priests and ancients, he answered nothing.

27:13  LA: Tunc dicit illi Pilatus: Non audis quanta adversum te dicunt testimonia?
     EN: Then Pilate says to him: Do not you hear how great testimonies they allege against you?

27:14  LA: Et non respondit ei ad ullum verbum, ita ut miraretur præses vehementer.
     EN: And he answered him to never a word, so that the governor wondered exceedingly.

27:15  LA: Per diem autem solemnem consueverat præses populo dimittere unum vinctum, quem voluissent:
     EN: Now upon the solemn day the governor was accustomed to release to the people one prisoner, whom they would.

27:16  LA: habebat autem tunc vinctum insignem, qui dicebatur Barabbas.
     EN: And he had then a notorious prisoner that was called Barabbas.

27:17  LA: Congregatis ergo illis, dixit Pilatus: Quem vultis dimittam vobis: Barabbam, an Jesum, qui dicitur Christus?
     EN: They therefore being gathered together, Pilate said: Whom will you that I release to you: Barabbas, or Jesus that is called Christ?

27:18  LA: Sciebat enim quod per invidiam tradidissent eum.
     EN: For he knew that for envy they had delivered him.

     EN: And as he was sitting in the place of judgment, his wife sent to him, saying: Have you nothing to do with that just man; for I have suffered many things this day in a dream because of him.

27:20  LA: Principes autem sacerdotum et seniores persuaserunt populis ut peterent Barabbam, Jesum vero perderent.
     EN: But the chief priests and ancients persuaded the people that they should ask Barabbas and make Jesus away.

27:21  LA: Respondens autem præses, ait illis: Quem vultis vobis de duobus dimitti? At illi dixerunt: Barabbam.
     EN: And the governor answered them: Whether will you of the two to be released to you? But they said: Barabbas.

27:22  LA: Dicit illis Pilatus: Quid igitur faciam de Jesu, qui dicitur Christus?
     EN: Pilate says to them: What shall I do then with Jesus that is called Christ?

27:23  LA: Dicunt omnes: Crucifigatur. Ait illis præses: Quid enim mali fecit? At illi magis clamabant dicentes: Crucifigatur.
     EN: They say all: Let him be crucified. The governor said to them: Why, what evil has he done? But they cried out the more, saying: Let him be crucified.

27:24  LA: Videns autem Pilatus quia nihil proficeret, sed magis tumultus fieret: accepta aqua, lavit manus coram populo, dicens: Innocens ego sum a sanguine justi hujus: vos videritis.
     EN: And Pilate seeing that he prevailed nothing, but that rather a tumult was made, taking water washed his hands before the people, saying: I am innocent of the blood of this just man. Look you to it.

27:25  LA: Et respondens universus populus, dixit: Sanguis ejus super nos, et super filios nostros.
     EN: And all the people answered: His blood be upon us and upon our children.

27:26  LA: Tunc dimisit illis Barabbam: Jesum autem flagellatum tradidit eis ut crucifigeretur.
     EN: Then he released to them Barabbas: and having flogged Jesus, delivered him to them to be crucified.

27:27  LA: Tunc milites præsidis suscipientes Jesum in prætorium, congregaverunt ad eum universam cohortem:
     EN: Then the soldiers of the governor, taking Jesus into the hall, gathered together to him the whole band.

27:28  LA: et exuentes eum, chlamydem coccineam circumdederunt ei,
     EN: And stripping him, they put a scarlet cloak about him.

27:29  LA: et plectentes coronam de spinis, posuerunt super caput ejus, et arundinem in dextera ejus. Et genu flexo ante eum, illudebant ei, dicentes: Ave rex Judæorum.
     EN: And platting a crown of thorns, they put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand. And bowing the knee before him, they mocked him, saying: Hail, King of the Jews.

27:30  LA: Et exspuentes in eum, acceperunt arundinem, et percutiebant caput ejus.
     EN: And spitting upon him, they took the reed and struck his head.

27:31  LA: Et postquam illuserunt ei, exuerunt eum chlamyde, et induerunt eum vestimentis ejus, et duxerunt eum ut crucifigerent.
     EN: And after they had mocked him, they took off the cloak from him and put on him his own garments and led him away to crucify him.

     EN: And going out, they found a man of Cyrene, named Simon: him they forced to take up his cross.

27:33  LA: Et venerunt in locum qui dicitur Golgotha, quod est Calvariæ locus.
     EN: And they came to the place that is called Golgotha, which is the place of Calvary.

27:34  LA: Et dederunt ei vinum bibere cum felle mistum. Et cum gustasset, noluit bibere.
     EN: And they gave him wine to drink mingled with gall. And when he had tasted, he would not drink.

27:35  LA: Postquam autem crucifixerunt eum, diviserunt vestimenta ejus, sortem mittentes: ut impleretur quod dictum est per prophetam dicentem: Diviserunt sibi vestimenta mea, et super vestem meam miserunt sortem.
     EN: And after they had crucified him, they divided his garments, casting lots; that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, saying: They divided my garments among them; and upon my vesture they cast lots.

27:36  LA: Et sedentes servabant eum.
     EN: And they sat and watched him.

27:37  LA: Et imposuerunt super caput ejus causam ipsius scriptam: Hic est Jesus rex Judæorum.
     EN: And they put over his head his cause written: THIS IS JESUS THE KING OF THE JEWS.

     EN: Then were crucified with him two thieves: one on the right hand and one on the left.

     EN: And they that passed by blasphemed him, wagging their heads,

     EN: In like manner also the chief priests, with the scribes and ancients, mocking said:

     EN: He saved others: himself he cannot save. If he be the king of Israel, let him now come down from the cross: and we will believe him.

     EN: He trusted in God: let him now deliver him if he will have him. For he said: I am the Son of God.

     EN: And the same thing the thieves also that were crucified with him reproached him with.

     EN: Now from the sixth hour, there was darkness over the whole earth, until the ninth hour.

     EN: And immediately one of them running took a sponge and filled it with vinegar and put it on a reed and gave him to drink.

27:49  LA: Ceteri vero dicebant: Sine, videamus an veniat Elias liberans eum.
     EN: And the others said: Let be. Let us see whether Elijah will come to deliver him.

     EN: And Jesus again crying with a loud voice, yielded up the ghost.

     EN: And the graves were opened: and many bodies of the saints that had slept arose,

     EN: He went to Pilate and asked the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded that the body should be delivered.

     EN: And laid it in his own new monument, which he had hewed out in a rock. And he rolled a great stone to the door of the monument and went his way.

     EN: And there was there Mary Magdalen and the other Mary, sitting over against the tomb.
