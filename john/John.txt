JOHN 1

1:1  LA: In principio erat Verbum, et Verbum erat apud Deum, et Deus erat Verbum.
     EN: In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.

1:2  LA: Hoc erat in principio apud Deum.
     EN: He was in the beginning with God.

1:3  LA: Omnia per ipsum facta sunt: et sine ipso factum est nihil, quod factum est.
     EN: All things were made through him, and without him nothing was made that was made.

1:4  LA: In ipso vita erat, et vita erat lux hominum:
     EN: In him was life, and the life was the light of humanity.

1:5  LA: et lux in tenebris lucet, et tenebræ eam non comprehenderunt.
     EN: The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness did not comprehend it.

1:6  LA: Fuit homo missus a Deo, cui nomen erat Joannes.
     EN: There was a man sent from God whose name was John.

1:7  LA: Hic venit in testimonium ut testimonium perhiberet de lumine, ut omnes crederent per illum.
     EN: He came as a witness, to bear witness to the light, so that all might believe through him.

1:8  LA: Non erat ille lux, sed ut testimonium perhiberet de lumine.
     EN: He was not the light, but came to bear witness to the light.

1:9  LA: Erat lux vera, quæ illuminat omnem hominem venientem in hunc mundum.
     EN: The true light was there, which enlightens every person coming into this world.

1:10  LA: In mundo erat, et mundus per ipsum factus est, et mundus eum non cognovit.
     EN: He was in the world, and the world was made through him, and the world did not know him.

1:11  LA: In propria venit, et sui eum non receperunt.
     EN: He came to his own, and his own did not receive him.

1:12  LA: Quotquot autem receperunt eum, dedit eis potestatem filios Dei fieri, his qui credunt in nomine ejus:
     EN: But to all who received him, he gave authority to become children of God, to those who believe in his name,

1:13  LA: qui non ex sanguinibus, neque ex voluntate carnis, neque ex voluntate viri, sed ex Deo nati sunt.
     EN: who were born not of bloods, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of a man, but of God.

1:14  LA: Et Verbum caro factum est, et habitavit in nobis: et vidimus gloriam ejus, gloriam quasi unigeniti a Patre plenum gratiæ et veritatis.
     EN: And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us. We saw his glory, glory as of the Only-begotten from the Father, full of grace and truth.

1:15  LA: Joannes testimonium perhibet de ipso, et clamat dicens: Hic erat quem dixi: Qui post me venturus est, ante me factus est: quia prior me erat.
     EN: John bears witness to him and cries out: This was the one of whom I said, The one coming after me has been placed before me, because he was before me.

1:16  LA: Et de plenitudine ejus nos omnes accepimus, et gratiam pro gratia:
     EN: From his fullness we have all received, and grace for grace,

1:17  LA: quia lex per Moysen data est, gratia et veritas per Jesum Christum facta est.
     EN: for the Law was given through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ.

1:18  LA: Deum nemo vidit umquam: unigenitus Filius, qui est in sinu Patris, ipse enarravit.
     EN: No one has ever seen God. The only-begotten Son, who is in the bosom of the Father, has made him known.

1:19  LA: Et hoc est testimonium Joannis, quando miserunt Judæi ab Jerosolymis sacerdotes et Levitas ad eum ut interrogarent eum: Tu quis es?
     EN: This is John’s testimony, when the Jews sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem to ask him: Who are you?

1:20  LA: Et confessus est, et non negavit, et confessus est: Quia non sum ego Christus.
     EN: He confessed and did not deny; he confessed: I am not the Christ.

1:21  LA: Et interrogaverunt eum: Quid ergo? Elias es tu? Et dixit: Non sum. Propheta es tu? Et respondit: Non.
     EN: They asked him: What, then? Are you Elijah? He said: I am not. Are you the Prophet? He answered: No.

1:22  LA: Dixerunt ergo ei: Quis es ut responsum demus his qui miserunt nos? quid dicis de teipso?
     EN: So they said to him: Who are you, so that we may give an answer to those who sent us? What do you say about yourself?

1:23  LA: Ait: Ego vox clamantis in deserto: Dirigite viam Domini, sicut dixit Isaias propheta.
     EN: He said: I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness, Make straight the way of the Lord, as Isaiah the prophet said.

1:24  LA: Et qui missi fuerant, erant ex pharisæis.
     EN: Those who had been sent were from the Pharisees.

1:25  LA: Et interrogaverunt eum, et dixerunt ei: Quid ergo baptizas, si tu non es Christus, neque Elias, neque propheta?
     EN: They asked him: Why, then, do you baptize, if you are not the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the Prophet?

1:26  LA: Respondit eis Joannes, dicens: Ego baptizo in aqua: medius autem vestrum stetit, quem vos nescitis.
     EN: John answered them: I baptize in water, but in your midst stands one whom you do not know.

1:27  LA: Ipse est qui post me venturus est, qui ante me factus est: cujus ego non sum dignus ut solvam ejus corrigiam calceamenti.
     EN: He is the one coming after me, who has been placed before me, whose sandal strap I am not worthy to untie.

1:28  LA: Hæc in Bethania facta sunt trans Jordanem, ubi erat Joannes baptizans.
     EN: These things happened in Bethany across the Jordan, where John was baptizing.

1:29  LA: Altera die vidit Joannes Jesum venientem ad se, et ait: Ecce agnus Dei, ecce qui tollit peccatum mundi.
     EN: The next day John saw Jesus coming toward him and said: Behold the Lamb of God; behold the one who takes away the sin of the world.

1:30  LA: Hic est de quo dixi: Post me venit vir qui ante me factus est: quia prior me erat:
     EN: This is the one of whom I said: After me comes a man who has been placed before me, because he was before me.

1:31  LA: et ego nesciebam eum, sed ut manifestetur in Israël, propterea veni ego in aqua baptizans.
     EN: I did not know him, but so that he might be revealed to Israel, I came baptizing in water.

1:32  LA: Et testimonium perhibuit Joannes, dicens: Quia vidi Spiritum descendentem quasi columbam de cælo, et mansit super eum.
     EN: John bore witness, saying: I saw the Spirit descending from heaven like a dove, and he remained upon him.

1:33  LA: Et ego nesciebam eum: sed qui misit me baptizare in aqua, ille mihi dixit: Super quem videris Spiritum descendentem, et manentem super eum, hic est qui baptizat in Spiritu Sancto.
     EN: I did not know him, but the one who sent me to baptize in water said to me: The one upon whom you see the Spirit descending and remaining is the one who baptizes in the Holy Spirit.

1:34  LA: Et ego vidi: et testimonium perhibui quia hic est Filius Dei.
     EN: I have seen and have borne witness that this is the Son of God.

1:35  LA: Altera die iterum stabat Joannes, et ex discipulis ejus duo.
     EN: The next day John was again standing there with two of his disciples.

1:36  LA: Et respiciens Jesum ambulantem, dicit: Ecce agnus Dei.
     EN: Looking at Jesus as he walked, he said: Behold the Lamb of God.

1:37  LA: Et audierunt eum duo discipuli loquentem, et secuti sunt Jesum.
     EN: The two disciples heard him speaking and followed Jesus.

1:38  LA: Conversus autem Jesus, et videns eos sequentes se, dicit eis: Quid quæritis? Qui dixerunt ei: Rabbi (quod dicitur interpretatum Magister), ubi habitas?
     EN: Jesus turned and saw them following him, and said: What do you seek? They said to him: Rabbi—which means Teacher—where do you dwell?

1:39  LA: Dicit eis: Venite et videte. Venerunt, et viderunt ubi maneret, et apud eum manserunt die illo: hora autem erat quasi decima.
     EN: He said to them: Come and see. They came and saw where he was staying, and stayed with him that day. It was about the tenth hour.

1:40  LA: Erat autem Andreas, frater Simonis Petri, unus ex duobus qui audierant a Joanne, et secuti fuerant eum.
     EN: Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, was one of the two who heard John and followed Jesus.

1:41  LA: Invenit hic primum fratrem suum Simonem, et dicit ei: Invenimus Messiam (quod est interpretatum Christus).
     EN: He first found his brother Simon and said to him: We have found the Messiah—which means Christ.

1:42  LA: Et adduxit eum ad Jesum. Intuitus autem eum Jesus, dixit: Tu es Simon, filius Jona; tu vocaberis Cephas, quod interpretatur Petrus.
     EN: He brought him to Jesus. Looking at him, Jesus said: You are Simon, son of Jonah; you will be called Cephas—which means Peter.

1:43  LA: In crastinum voluit exire in Galilæam, et invenit Philippum. Et dicit ei Jesus: Sequere me.
     EN: The next day he wished to go into Galilee, and found Philip. Jesus said to him: Follow me.

1:44  LA: Erat autem Philippus a Bethsaida, civitate Andreæ et Petri.
     EN: Philip was from Bethsaida, the city of Andrew and Peter.

1:45  LA: Invenit Philippus Nathanaël, et dicit ei: Quem scripsit Moyses in lege, et prophetæ, invenimus Jesum filium Joseph a Nazareth.
     EN: Philip found Nathanael and said to him: We have found the one of whom Moses wrote in the Law, and the prophets: Jesus, son of Joseph, from Nazareth.

1:46  LA: Et dixit ei Nathanaël: A Nazareth potest aliquid boni esse? Dicit ei Philippus: Veni et vide.
     EN: Nathanael said to him: Can anything good be from Nazareth? Philip said to him: Come and see.

1:47  LA: Vidit Jesus Nathanaël venientem ad se, et dicit de eo: Ecce vere Israëlita, in quo dolus non est.
     EN: Jesus saw Nathanael coming toward him and said of him: Behold, truly an Israelite in whom there is no deceit.

1:48  LA: Dicit ei Nathanaël: Unde me nosti? Respondit Jesus, et dixit ei: Priusquam te Philippus vocavit, cum esses sub ficu, vidi te.
     EN: Nathanael said to him: How do you know me? Jesus answered: Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you.

1:49  LA: Respondit ei Nathanaël, et ait: Rabbi, tu es Filius Dei, tu es rex Israël.
     EN: Nathanael answered him: Rabbi, you are the Son of God; you are the King of Israel.

1:50  LA: Respondit Jesus, et dixit ei: Quia dixi tibi: Vidi te sub ficu, credis; majus his videbis.
     EN: Jesus answered him: Because I said to you, I saw you under the fig tree, you believe; you will see greater things than these.

1:51  LA: Et dicit ei: Amen, amen dico vobis, videbitis cælum apertum, et angelos Dei ascendentes, et descendentes supra Filium hominis.
     EN: And he said to him: Amen, amen, I say to you all: you will see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of Man.

JOHN 2

2:1  LA: Et die tertia nuptiæ factæ sunt in Cana Galilææ, et erat mater Jesu ibi.
     EN: And the third day, there was a marriage in Cana of Galilee: and the mother of Jesus was there.

2:2  LA: Vocatus est autem et Jesus, et discipuli ejus, ad nuptias.
     EN: And Jesus also was invited, and his disciples, to the marriage.

2:3  LA: Et deficiente vino, dicit mater Jesu ad eum: Vinum non habent.
     EN: And the wine failing, the mother of Jesus says to him: They have no wine.

2:4  LA: Et dicit ei Jesus: Quid mihi et tibi est, mulier? nondum venit hora mea.
     EN: Jesus said to her: What is this to me and to you, woman? My hour has not yet come.

2:5  LA: Dicit mater ejus ministris: Quodcumque dixerit vobis, facite.
     EN: His mother says to the waiters: Whatever he shall say to you, do you.

2:6  LA: Erant autem ibi lapideæ hydriæ sex positæ secundum purificationem Judæorum, capientes singulæ metretas binas vel ternas.
     EN: Now there were set there six waterpots of stone, according to the manner of the purifying of the Jews, containing two or three measures apiece.

2:7  LA: Dicit eis Jesus: Implete hydrias aqua. Et impleverunt eas usque ad summum.
     EN: Jesus says to them: Fill the waterpots with water. And they filled them up to the brim.

2:8  LA: Et dicit eis Jesus: Haurite nunc, et ferte architriclino. Et tulerunt.
     EN: And Jesus says to them: Draw out now and carry to the chief steward of the feast. And they carried it.

2:9  LA: Ut autem gustavit architriclinus aquam vinum factam, et non sciebat unde esset, ministri autem sciebant, qui hauserant aquam: vocat sponsum architriclinus,
     EN: And when the chief steward had tasted the water made wine and knew not from where it was, but the waiters knew who had drawn the water: the chief steward calls the bridegroom,

2:10  LA: et dicit ei: Omnis homo primum bonum vinum ponit et cum inebriati fuerint, tunc id, quod deterius est. Tu autem servasti bonum vinum usque adhuc.
     EN: And says to him: Every man at first sets forth good wine, and when men have well drunk, then that which is worse. But you have kept the good wine until now.

2:11  LA: Hoc fecit initium signorum Jesus in Cana Galilææ; et manifestavit gloriam suam, et crediderunt in eum discipuli ejus.
     EN: Jesus did this as the beginning of his signs in Cana of Galilee, and revealed his glory; and his disciples believed in him.

2:12  LA: Post hoc descendit Capharnaum ipse, et mater ejus, et fratres ejus, et discipuli ejus: et ibi manserunt non multis diebus.
     EN: After this, he went down to Capernaum, he and his mother and his brothers and his disciples: and they remained there not many days.

2:13  LA: Et prope erat Pascha Judæorum, et ascendit Jesus Jerosolymam:
     EN: And the pasch of the Jews was near: and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.

2:14  LA: et invenit in templo vendentes boves, et oves, et columbas, et numularios sedentes.
     EN: And he found in the temple them that sold oxen and sheep and doves, and the changers of money sitting.

2:15  LA: Et cum fecisset quasi flagellum de funiculis, omnes ejecit de templo, oves quoque, et boves, et numulariorum effudit æs, et mensas subvertit.
     EN: And when he had made, as it were, a scourge of little cords, he drove them all out of the temple, the sheep also and the oxen: and the money of the changers he poured out, and the tables he overthrew.

2:16  LA: Et his qui columbas vendebant, dixit: Auferte ista hinc, et nolite facere domum patris mei, domum negotiationis.
     EN: And to them that sold doves he said: Take these things from here, and make not the house of my Father a house of traffic.

2:17  LA: Recordati sunt vero discipuli ejus quia scriptum est: Zelus domus tuæ comedit me.
     EN: And his disciples remembered, that it was written: The zeal of your house has eaten me up.

2:18  LA: Responderunt ergo Judæi, et dixerunt ei: Quod signum ostendis nobis, quia hæc facis?
     EN: The Jews, therefore, answered, and said to him: What sign do you show to us, seeing you do these things?

2:19  LA: Respondit Jesus, et dixit eis: Solvite templum hoc, et in tribus diebus excitabo illud.
     EN: Jesus answered them: Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.

2:20  LA: Dixerunt ergo Judæi: Quadraginta et sex annis ædificatum est templum hoc, et tu in tribus diebus excitabis illud?
     EN: The Jews then said: Six and forty years was this temple in building; and will you raise it up in three days?

2:21  LA: Ille autem dicebat de templo corporis sui.
     EN: But he was speaking about the temple of his body.

2:22  LA: Cum ergo resurrexisset a mortuis, recordati sunt discipuli ejus, quia hoc dicebat, et crediderunt scripturæ et sermoni quem dixit Jesus.
     EN: When therefore he had risen again from the dead, his disciples remembered that he had said this: and they believed the scripture and the word that Jesus had said.

2:23  LA: Cum autem esset Jerosolymis in Pascha in die festo, multi crediderunt in nomine ejus, videntes signa ejus, quæ faciebat.
     EN: Now when he was at Jerusalem, at the pasch, upon the festival day, many believed in his name, seeing his signs which he did.

2:24  LA: Ipse autem Jesus non credebat semetipsum eis, eo quod ipse nosset omnes,
     EN: But Jesus did not trust himself to them: for that he knew all men,

2:25  LA: et quia opus ei non erat ut quis testimonium perhiberet de homine: ipse enim sciebat quid esset in homine.
     EN: And because he needed not that any should give testimony of man: for he knew what was in man.

JOHN 3

3:1  LA: Erat autem homo ex pharisæis, Nicodemus nomine, princeps Judæorum.
     EN: And there was a man of the Pharisees, named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews.

3:2  LA: Hic venit ad Jesum nocte, et dixit ei: Rabbi, scimus quia a Deo venisti magister, nemo enim potest hæc signa facere, quæ tu facis, nisi fuerit Deus cum eo.
     EN: This man came to Jesus by night and said to him: Rabbi, we know that you are come a teacher from God; for no one can do these signs which you do, unless God be with him.

3:3  LA: Respondit Jesus, et dixit ei: Amen, amen dico tibi, nisi quis renatus fuerit denuo, non potest videre regnum Dei.
     EN: Jesus answered him: Amen, amen, I say to you: unless someone is born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.

3:4  LA: Dicit ad eum Nicodemus: Quomodo potest homo nasci, cum sit senex? numquid potest in ventrem matris suæ iterato introire et renasci?
     EN: Nicodemus says to him: How can a man be born when he is old? Can he enter a second time into his mother’s womb and be born again?

3:5  LA: Respondit Jesus: Amen, amen dico tibi, nisi quis renatus fuerit ex aqua, et Spiritu Sancto, non potest introire in regnum Dei.
     EN: Jesus answered: Amen, amen, I say to you: unless someone is born anew of water and the Holy Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God.

3:6  LA: Quod natum est ex carne, caro est: et quod natum est ex spiritu, spiritus est.
     EN: What is born of the flesh is flesh, and what is born of the spirit is spirit.

3:7  LA: Non mireris quia dixi tibi: oportet vos nasci denuo.
     EN: Do not marvel that I said to you: You all must be born again.

3:8  LA: Spiritus ubi vult spirat, et vocem ejus audis, sed nescis unde veniat, aut quo vadat: sic est omnis qui natus est ex spiritu.
     EN: The Spirit breathes where he wills, and you hear his voice, but you do not know where he comes from or where he goes. So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit.

3:9  LA: Respondit Nicodemus, et dixit ei: Quomodo possunt hæc fieri?
     EN: Nicodemus answered him: How can these things be done?

3:10  LA: Respondit Jesus, et dixit ei: Tu es magister in Israël, et hæc ignoras?
     EN: Jesus answered him: Are you a master in Israel, and know not these things?

3:11  LA: Amen, amen dico tibi, quia quod scimus loquimur, et quod vidimus testamur, et testimonium nostrum non accipitis.
     EN: Amen, amen, I say to you that we speak what we know and we testify what we have seen: and you receive not our testimony.

3:12  LA: Si terrena dixi vobis, et non creditis: quomodo, si dixero vobis cælestia, credetis?
     EN: If I have spoken to you earthly things, and you believe not: how will you believe, if I shall speak to you heavenly things?

3:13  LA: Et nemo ascendit in cælum, nisi qui descendit de cælo, Filius hominis, qui est in cælo.
     EN: No one has ascended into heaven except the one who descended from heaven, the Son of Man, who is in heaven.

3:14  LA: Et sicut Moyses exaltavit serpentem in deserto, ita exaltari oportet Filium hominis:
     EN: And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so the Son of Man must be lifted up,

3:15  LA: ut omnis qui credit in ipsum, non pereat, sed habeat vitam æternam.
     EN: so that everyone who believes in him may not perish, but may have eternal life.

3:16  LA: Sic enim Deus dilexit mundum, ut Filium suum unigenitum daret: ut omnis qui credit in eum, non pereat, sed habeat vitam æternam.
     EN: For God so loved the world that he gave his only-begotten Son, so that everyone who believes in him may not perish, but may have eternal life.

3:17  LA: Non enim misit Deus Filium suum in mundum, ut judicet mundum, sed ut salvetur mundus per ipsum.
     EN: For God did not send his Son into the world to judge the world, but so that the world might be saved through him.

3:18  LA: Qui credit in eum, non judicatur; qui autem non credit, jam judicatus est: quia non credit in nomine unigeniti Filii Dei.
     EN: Whoever believes in him is not judged; but whoever does not believe has already been judged, because he does not believe in the name of the only-begotten Son of God.

3:19  LA: Hoc est autem judicium: quia lux venit in mundum, et dilexerunt homines magis tenebras quam lucem: erant enim eorum mala opera.
     EN: This is the judgment: the light has come into the world, and people loved the darkness more than the light, for their works were evil.

3:20  LA: Omnis enim qui male agit, odit lucem, et non venit ad lucem, ut non arguantur opera ejus:
     EN: For everyone who does evil hates the light and does not come to the light, so that his works may not be exposed.

3:21  LA: qui autem facit veritatem, venit ad lucem, ut manifestentur opera ejus, quia in Deo sunt facta.
     EN: But whoever does the truth comes to the light, so that his works may be revealed as done in God.

3:22  LA: Post hæc venit Jesus et discipuli ejus in terram Judæam: et illic demorabatur cum eis, et baptizabat.
     EN: After these things, Jesus and his disciples came into the land of Judea: and there he abode with them and baptized.

3:23  LA: Erat autem et Joannes baptizans, in Ænnon, juxta Salim: quia aquæ multæ erant illic, et veniebant et baptizabantur.
     EN: And John also was baptizing in Ennon near Salim: because there was much water there. And they came and were baptized.

3:24  LA: Nondum enim missus fuerat Joannes in carcerem.
     EN: For John was not yet cast into prison.

3:25  LA: Facta est autem quæstio ex discipulis Joannis cum Judæis de purificatione.
     EN: And there arose a question between some of John’s disciples and the Jews, concerning purification.

3:26  LA: Et venerunt ad Joannem, et dixerunt ei: Rabbi, qui erat tecum trans Jordanem, cui tu testimonium perhibuisti, ecce hic baptizat, et omnes veniunt ad eum.
     EN: And they came to John and said to him: Rabbi, he that was with you beyond the Jordan, to whom you gave testimony: behold, he baptizes and all men come to him.

3:27  LA: Respondit Joannes, et dixit: Non potest homo accipere quidquam, nisi fuerit ei datum de cælo.
     EN: John answered: A man cannot receive anything, unless it be given him from heaven.

3:28  LA: Ipsi vos mihi testimonium perhibetis, quod dixerim: Non sum ego Christus: sed quia missus sum ante illum.
     EN: You yourselves do bear me witness that I said, I am not Christ, but that I am sent before him.

3:29  LA: Qui habet sponsam, sponsus est: amicus autem sponsi, qui stat, et audit eum, gaudio gaudet propter vocem sponsi. Hoc ergo gaudium meum impletum est.
     EN: He that has the bride is the bridegroom: but the friend of the bridegroom, who stands and hears Him, rejoices with joy because of the bridegroom’s voice. This my joy therefore is fulfilled.

3:30  LA: Illum oportet crescere, me autem minui.
     EN: He must increase: but I must decrease.

3:31  LA: Qui desursum venit, super omnes est. Qui est de terra, de terra est, et de terra loquitur. Qui de cælo venit, super omnes est.
     EN: He that comes from above is above all. He that is of the earth, of the earth he is, and of the earth he speaks. He that comes from heaven is above all.

3:32  LA: Et quod vidit, et audivit, hoc testatur: et testimonium ejus nemo accipit.
     EN: And what he has seen and heard, that he testifies: and no one receives his testimony.

3:33  LA: Qui accepit ejus testimonium signavit, quia Deus verax est.
     EN: He that has received his testimony has set to his seal that God is true.

3:34  LA: Quem enim misit Deus, verba Dei loquitur: non enim ad mensuram dat Deus spiritum.
     EN: For he whom God has sent speaks the words of God: for God does not give the Spirit by measure.

3:35  LA: Pater diligit Filium et omnia dedit in manu ejus.
     EN: The Father loves the Son: and he has given all things into his hand.

3:36  LA: Qui credit in Filium, habet vitam æternam; qui autem incredulus est Filio, non videbit vitam, sed ira Dei manet super eum.
     EN: Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life; but whoever disbelieves the Son will not see life, and the wrath of God remains upon him.

JOHN 4

4:1  LA: Ut ergo cognovit Jesus quia audierunt pharisæi quod Jesus plures discipulos facit, et baptizat, quam Joannes
     EN: When Jesus therefore understood that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus makes more disciples and baptizes more than John,

4:2  LA: (quamquam Jesus non baptizaret, sed discipuli ejus),
     EN: (Though Jesus himself did not baptize, but his disciples),

4:3  LA: reliquit Judæam, et abiit iterum in Galilæam.
     EN: He left Judea and went again into Galilee.

4:4  LA: Oportebat autem eum transire per Samariam.
     EN: And he was of necessity to pass through Samaria.

4:5  LA: Venit ergo in civitatem Samariæ, quæ dicitur Sichar, juxta prædium quod dedit Jacob Joseph filio suo.
     EN: He comes therefore to a city of Samaria, which is called Sichar, near the land which Jacob gave to his son Joseph.

4:6  LA: Erat autem ibi fons Jacob. Jesus ergo fatigatus ex itinere, sedebat sic supra fontem. Hora erat quasi sexta.
     EN: Now Jacob’s well was there. Jesus therefore, being wearied with his journey, sat thus on the well. It was about the sixth hour.

4:7  LA: Venit mulier de Samaria haurire aquam. Dicit ei Jesus: Da mihi bibere.
     EN: There comes a woman of Samaria, to draw water. Jesus says to her: Give me to drink.

4:8  LA: (Discipuli enim ejus abierant in civitatem ut cibos emerent.)
     EN: For his disciples had gone into the city to buy foods.

4:9  LA: Dicit ergo ei mulier illa Samaritana: Quomodo tu, Judæus cum sis, bibere a me poscis, quæ sum mulier Samaritana? non enim coutuntur Judæi Samaritanis.
     EN: Then that Samaritan woman says to him: How do you, being a Jew; ask of me to drink, who am a Samaritan woman? For the Jews do not communicate with the Samaritans.

4:10  LA: Respondit Jesus, et dixit ei: Si scires donum Dei, et quis est qui dicit tibi: Da mihi bibere, tu forsitan petisses ab eo, et dedisset tibi aquam vivam.
     EN: Jesus answered to her: If you did know the gift of God and who he is that says to you: Give me to drink; you perhaps would have asked of him, and he would have given you living water.

4:11  LA: Dicit ei mulier: Domine, neque in quo haurias habes, et puteus altus est: unde ergo habes aquam vivam?
     EN: The woman says to him: Sir, you have nothing wherein to draw, and the well is deep. From where then have you living water?

4:12  LA: Numquid tu major es patre nostro Jacob, qui dedit nobis puteum, et ipse ex eo bibit, et filii ejus, et pecora ejus?
     EN: Are you greater than our father Jacob, who gave us the well and drank of it, himself and his children and his cattle?

4:13  LA: Respondit Jesus, et dixit ei: Omnis qui bibit ex aqua hac, sitiet iterum; qui autem biberit ex aqua quam ego dabo ei, non sitiet in æternum:
     EN: Jesus answered to her: Whoever drinks of this water shall thirst again: but he that shall drink of the water that I will give him shall not thirst forever.

4:14  LA: sed aqua quam ego dabo ei, fiet in eo fons aquæ salientis in vitam æternam.
     EN: But the water that I will give him shall become in him a fountain of water, springing up into life everlasting.

4:15  LA: Dicit ad eum mulier: Domine, da mihi hanc aquam, ut non sitiam, neque veniam huc haurire.
     EN: The woman said to him: Sir, give me this water, that I may not thirst, nor come here to draw.

4:16  LA: Dicit ei Jesus: Vade, voca virum tuum, et veni huc.
     EN: Jesus says to her: Go, call your husband, and come here.

4:17  LA: Respondit mulier, et dixit: Non habeo virum. Dicit ei Jesus: Bene dixisti, quia non habeo virum;
     EN: The woman answered: I have no husband. Jesus said to her: You have said well: I have no husband.

4:18  LA: quinque enim viros habuisti, et nunc, quem habes, non est tuus vir: hoc vere dixisti.
     EN: For you have had five husbands: and he whom you now have is not your husband. This, you have said truly.

4:19  LA: Dicit ei mulier: Domine, video quia propheta es tu.
     EN: The woman says to him: Sir, I perceive that you are a prophet.

4:20  LA: Patres nostri in monte hoc adoraverunt, et vos dicitis, quia Jerosolymis est locus ubi adorare oportet.
     EN: Our fathers adored on this mountain: and you say that at Jerusalem is the place where men must adore.

4:21  LA: Dicit ei Jesus: Mulier, crede mihi, quia venit hora, quando neque in monte hoc, neque in Jerosolymis adorabitis Patrem.
     EN: Jesus says to her: Woman, believe me that the hour comes, when you shall neither on this mountain, nor in Jerusalem, adore the Father.

4:22  LA: Vos adoratis quod nescitis: nos adoramus quod scimus, quia salus ex Judæis est.
     EN: You adore that which you do not know: we adore that which we know. For salvation is of the Jews.

4:23  LA: Sed venit hora, et nunc est, quando veri adoratores adorabunt Patrem in spiritu et veritate. Nam et Pater tales quærit, qui adorent eum.
     EN: But the hour comes and now is, when the true adorers shall adore the Father in spirit and in truth. For the Father also seeks such to adore him.

4:24  LA: Spiritus est Deus: et eos qui adorant eum, in spiritu et veritate oportet adorare.
     EN: God is a spirit: and they that adore him must adore him in spirit and in truth.

4:25  LA: Dicit ei mulier: Scio quia Messias venit (qui dicitur Christus): cum ergo venerit ille, nobis annuntiabit omnia.
     EN: The woman says to him: I know that the Messias comes (who is called Christ): therefore, when he has come, he will tell us all things.

4:26  LA: Dicit ei Jesus: Ego sum, qui loquor tecum.
     EN: Jesus says to her: I am he, who am speaking with you.

4:27  LA: Et continuo venerunt discipuli ejus, et mirabantur quia cum muliere loquebatur. Nemo tamen dixit: Quid quæris? aut, Quid loqueris cum ea?
     EN: And immediately his disciples came. And they wondered that he talked with the woman. Yet no one said: What seek you? Or: Why talk you with her?

4:28  LA: Reliquit ergo hydriam suam mulier, et abiit in civitatem, et dicit illis hominibus:
     EN: The woman therefore left her waterpot and went her way into the city and says to the men there:

4:29  LA: Venite, et videte hominem qui dixit mihi omnia quæcumque feci: numquid ipse est Christus?
     EN: Come and see a man who told me everything I have ever done. Could he be the Christ?

4:30  LA: Exierunt ergo de civitate et veniebant ad eum.
     EN: They went therefore out of the city and came to him.

4:31  LA: Interea rogabant eum discipuli, dicentes: Rabbi, manduca.
     EN: In the mean time, the disciples prayed him, saying: Rabbi, eat.

4:32  LA: Ille autem dicit eis: Ego cibum habeo manducare, quem vos nescitis.
     EN: But he said to them: I have food to eat that you do not know.

4:33  LA: Dicebant ergo discipuli ad invicem: Numquid aliquis attulit ei manducare?
     EN: The disciples therefore said one to another: Has any man brought him to eat?

4:34  LA: Dicit eis Jesus: Meus cibus est ut faciam voluntatem ejus qui misit me, ut perficiam opus ejus.
     EN: Jesus said to them: My food is to do the will of the one who sent me, so that I may complete his work.

4:35  LA: Nonne vos dicitis quod adhuc quatuor menses sunt, et messis venit? Ecce dico vobis: levate oculos vestros, et videte regiones, quia albæ sunt jam ad messem.
     EN: Do not you say: There are yet four months, and then the harvest comes? Behold, I say to you, lift up your eyes, and see the countries. For they are white already to harvest.

4:36  LA: Et qui metit, mercedem accipit, et congregat fructum in vitam æternam: ut et qui seminat, simul gaudeat, et qui metit.
     EN: And he that reaps receives wages and gathers fruit to life everlasting: that both he that sows and he that reaps may rejoice together.

4:37  LA: In hoc enim est verbum verum: quia alius est qui seminat, et alius est qui metit.
     EN: For in this is the saying true: That it is one man that sows, and it is another that reaps.

4:38  LA: Ego misi vos metere quod vos non laborastis: alii laboraverunt, et vos in labores eorum introistis.
     EN: I have sent you to reap that in which you did not labor. Others have labored: and you have entered into their labors.

4:39  LA: Ex civitate autem illa multi crediderunt in eum Samaritanorum, propter verbum mulieris testimonium perhibentis: Quia dixit mihi omnia quæcumque feci.
     EN: Now of that city many of the Samaritans believed in him, for the word of the woman giving testimony: He told me all things whatever I have done.

4:40  LA: Cum venissent ergo ad illum Samaritani, rogaverunt eum ut ibi maneret. Et mansit ibi duos dies.
     EN: So when the Samaritans had come to him, they desired that he would tarry there. And he abode there two days.

4:41  LA: Et multo plures crediderunt in eum propter sermonem ejus.
     EN: And many more believed in him, because of his own word.

4:42  LA: Et mulieri dicebant: Quia jam non propter tuam loquelam credimus: ipsi enim audivimus, et scimus quia hic est vere Salvator mundi.
     EN: And they said to the woman: We now believe, not for your saying: for we ourselves have heard him and know that this is indeed the Savior of the world.

4:43  LA: Post duos autem dies exiit inde, et abiit in Galilæam.
     EN: Now after two days, he departed from there and went into Galilee.

4:44  LA: Ipse enim Jesus testimonium perhibuit, quia propheta in sua patria honorem non habet.
     EN: For Jesus himself gave testimony that a prophet has no honor in his own country.

4:45  LA: Cum ergo venisset in Galilæam, exceperunt eum Galilæi, cum omnia vidissent quæ fecerat Jerosolymis in die festo: et ipsi enim venerant ad diem festum.
     EN: And when he had come into Galilee, the Galileans received him, having seen all the things he had done at Jerusalem on the festival day: for they also went to the festival day.

4:46  LA: Venit ergo iterum in Cana Galilææ, ubi fecit aquam vinum. Et erat quidam regulus, cujus filius infirmabatur Capharnaum.
     EN: He came again therefore into Cana of Galilee, where he made the water wine. And there was a certain ruler, whose son was sick at Capernaum.

4:47  LA: Hic cum audisset quia Jesus adveniret a Judæa in Galilæam, abiit ad eum, et rogabat eum ut descenderet, et sanaret filium ejus: incipiebat enim mori.
     EN: He having heard that Jesus had come from Judea into Galilee, went to him and prayed him to come down and heal his son: for he was at the point of death.

4:48  LA: Dixit ergo Jesus ad eum: Nisi signa et prodigia videritis, non creditis.
     EN: Jesus therefore said to him: Unless you see signs and wonders, you believe not.

4:49  LA: Dicit ad eum regulus: Domine, descende priusquam moriatur filius meus.
     EN: The ruler says to him: Lord, come down before that my son die.

4:50  LA: Dicit ei Jesus: Vade, filius tuus vivit. Credidit homo sermoni quem dixit ei Jesus, et ibat.
     EN: Jesus says to him: Go your way. Your son lives. The man believed the word which Jesus said to him and went his way.

4:51  LA: Jam autem eo descendente, servi occurrerunt ei, et nuntiaverunt dicentes, quia filius ejus viveret.
     EN: And as he was going down, his servants met him: and they brought word, saying, that his son lived.

4:52  LA: Interrogabat ergo horam ab eis in qua melius habuerit. Et dixerunt ei: Quia heri hora septima reliquit eum febris.
     EN: He asked therefore of them the hour wherein he grew better. And they said to him: Yesterday at the seventh hour, the fever left him.

4:53  LA: Cognovit ergo pater, quia illa hora erat in qua dixit ei Jesus: Filius tuus vivit; et credidit ipse et domus ejus tota.
     EN: The father therefore knew that it was at the same hour that Jesus said to him: Your son lives. And himself believed, and his whole house.

4:54  LA: Hoc iterum secundum signum fecit Jesus, cum venisset a Judæa in Galilæam.
     EN: This is again the second miracle that Jesus did, when he had come out of Judea into Galilee.

JOHN 5

5:1  LA: Post hæc erat dies festus Judæorum, et ascendit Jesus Jerosolymam.
     EN: After these things was a festival day of the Jews: and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.

5:2  LA: Est autem Jerosolymis probatica piscina, quæ cognominatur hebraice Bethsaida, quinque porticus habens.
     EN: Now there is at Jerusalem a pond, called Probatica, which in Hebrew is named Bethsaida, having five porches.

5:3  LA: In his jacebat multitudo magna languentium, cæcorum, claudorum, aridorum, exspectantium aquæ motum.
     EN: In these lay a great multitude of the sick, blind, lame, and withered, waiting for the movement of the water.

5:5  LA: Erat autem quidam homo ibi triginta et octo annos habens in infirmitate sua.
     EN: And there was a certain man there that had been eight and thirty years under his infirmity.

5:6  LA: Hunc autem cum vidisset Jesus jacentem, et cognovisset quia jam multum tempus haberet, dicit ei: Vis sanus fieri?
     EN: Him when Jesus had seen lying, and knew that he had been now a long time, he says to him: Will you be made well?

5:7  LA: Respondit ei languidus: Domine, hominem non habeo, ut, cum turbata fuerit aqua, mittat me in piscinam: dum venio enim ego, alius ante me descendit.
     EN: The infirm man answered him: Sir, I have no one, when the water is troubled, to put me into the pond. For while I am coming, another goes down before me.

5:8  LA: Dicit ei Jesus: Surge, tolle grabatum tuum et ambula.
     EN: Jesus says to him: Arise, take up your bed and walk.

5:9  LA: Et statim sanus factus est homo ille: et sustulit grabatum suum, et ambulabat. Erat autem sabbatum in die illo.
     EN: And immediately the man was made well: and he took up his bed and walked. And it was the sabbath that day.

5:10  LA: Dicebant ergo Judæi illi qui sanatus fuerat: Sabbatum est, non licet tibi tollere grabatum tuum.
     EN: The Jews therefore said to him that was healed: It is the sabbath. It is not lawful for you to take up your bed.

5:11  LA: Respondit eis: Qui me sanum fecit, ille mihi dixit: Tolle grabatum tuum et ambula.
     EN: He answered them: He that made me whole, he said to me: Take up your bed and walk.

5:12  LA: Interrogaverunt ergo eum: Quis est ille homo qui dixit tibi: Tolle grabatum tuum et ambula?
     EN: They asked him therefore: Who is that man who said to you: Take up your bed and walk?

5:13  LA: Is autem qui sanus fuerat effectus, nesciebat quis esset. Jesus enim declinavit a turba constituta in loco.
     EN: But he who was healed knew not who it was: for Jesus went aside from the multitude standing in the place.

5:14  LA: Postea invenit eum Jesus in templo, et dixit illi: Ecce sanus factus es; jam noli peccare, ne deterius tibi aliquid contingat.
     EN: Afterwards, Jesus finds him in the temple and says to him: Behold you are made well: sin no more, lest some worse thing happen to you.

5:15  LA: Abiit ille homo, et nuntiavit Judæis quia Jesus esset, qui fecit eum sanum.
     EN: The man went his way and told the Jews that it was Jesus who had made him whole.

5:16  LA: Propterea persequebantur Judæi Jesum, quia hæc faciebat in sabbato.
     EN: Therefore did the Jews persecute Jesus, because he did these things on the sabbath.

5:17  LA: Jesus autem respondit eis: Pater meus usque modo operatur, et ego operor.
     EN: But Jesus answered them: My Father works until now; and I work.

5:18  LA: Propterea ergo magis quærebant eum Judæi interficere: quia non solum solvebat sabbatum, sed et patrem suum dicebat Deum, æqualem se faciens Deo. Respondit itaque Jesus, et dixit eis:
     EN: Hereupon therefore the Jews sought the more to kill him, because he did not only break the sabbath but also said God was his Father, making himself equal to God. Then Jesus answered, and said to them:

5:19  LA: Amen, amen dico vobis: non potest Filius a se facere quidquam, nisi quod viderit Patrem facientem: quæcumque enim ille fecerit, hæc et Filius similiter facit.
     EN: Amen, amen, I say to you, the Son cannot do anything of himself, but what he sees the Father doing: for what things soever he does, these the Son also does in like manner.

5:20  LA: Pater enim diligit Filium, et omnia demonstrat ei quæ ipse facit: et majora his demonstrabit ei opera, ut vos miremini.
     EN: For the Father loves the Son and shows him all things which himself does: and greater works than these will he show him, that you may wonder.

5:21  LA: Sicut enim Pater suscitat mortuos, et vivificat, sic et Filius, quos vult, vivificat.
     EN: For as the Father raises up the dead and gives life: so the Son also gives life to whom he will.

5:22  LA: Neque enim Pater judicat quemquam: sed omne judicium dedit Filio,
     EN: For neither does the Father judge any man: but has given all judgment to the Son.

5:23  LA: ut omnes honorificent Filium, sicut honorificant Patrem; qui non honorificat Filium, non honorificat Patrem, qui misit illum.
     EN: That all men may honor the Son, as they honor the Father. He who honors not the Son honors not the Father who has sent him.

5:24  LA: Amen, amen dico vobis, quia qui verbum meum audit, et credit ei qui misit me, habet vitam æternam, et in judicium non venit, sed transiit a morte in vitam.
     EN: Amen, amen, I say to you that he who hears my word and believes him that sent me has life everlasting: and comes not into judgment, but is passed from death to life.

5:25  LA: Amen, amen dico vobis, quia venit hora, et nunc est, quando mortui audient vocem Filii Dei: et qui audierint, vivent.
     EN: Amen, amen, I say to you, that the hour comes, and now is, when the dead shall hear the voice of the Son of God: and they that hear shall live.

5:26  LA: Sicut enim Pater habet vitam in semetipso, sic dedit et Filio habere vitam in semetipso:
     EN: For as the Father has life in himself, so he has given to the Son also to have life in himself.

5:27  LA: et potestatem dedit ei judicium facere, quia Filius hominis est.
     EN: And he has given him power to do judgment, because he is the Son of Man.

5:28  LA: Nolite mirari hoc, quia venit hora in qua omnes qui in monumentis sunt audient vocem Filii Dei:
     EN: Do not marvel at this, for the hour is coming when all who are in the tombs will hear the voice of the Son of God,

5:29  LA: et procedent qui bona fecerunt, in resurrectionem vitæ; qui vero mala egerunt, in resurrectionem judicii.
     EN: and those who have done good will come forth to a resurrection of life, but those who have done evil to a resurrection of judgment.

5:30  LA: Non possum ego a meipso facere quidquam. Sicut audio, judico: et judicium meum justum est, quia non quæro voluntatem meam, sed voluntatem ejus qui misit me.
     EN: I cannot of myself do anything. As I hear, so I judge. And my judgment is just: because I seek not my own will, but the will of him that sent me.

5:31  LA: Si ego testimonium perhibeo de meipso, testimonium meum non est verum.
     EN: If I bear witness of myself, my witness is not true.

5:32  LA: Alius est qui testimonium perhibet de me: et scio quia verum est testimonium, quod perhibet de me.
     EN: There is another that bears witness of me: and I know that the witness which he bears witness of me is true.

5:33  LA: Vos misistis ad Joannem, et testimonium perhibuit veritati.
     EN: You sent to John: and he gave testimony to the truth.

5:34  LA: Ego autem non ab homine testimonium accipio: sed hæc dico ut vos salvi sitis.
     EN: But I receive not testimony from man: but I say these things, that you may be saved.

5:35  LA: Ille erat lucerna ardens et lucens: vos autem voluistis ad horam exsultare in luce ejus.
     EN: He was a burning and a shining light: and you were willing for a time to rejoice in his light.

5:36  LA: Ego autem habeo testimonium majus Joanne. Opera enim quæ dedit mihi Pater ut perficiam ea: ipsa opera, quæ ego facio, testimonium perhibent de me, quia Pater misit me:
     EN: But I have a greater testimony than that of John: for the works which the Father has given me to perfect, the works themselves which I do, give testimony of me, that the Father has sent me.

5:37  LA: et qui misit me Pater, ipse testimonium perhibuit de me: neque vocem ejus umquam audistis, neque speciem ejus vidistis:
     EN: And the Father himself who has sent me has given testimony of me: neither have you heard his voice at any time, nor seen his shape.

5:38  LA: et verbum ejus non habetis in vobis manens: quia quem misit ille, huic vos non creditis.
     EN: And you have not his word abiding in you: for whom he has sent, him you believe not.

5:39  LA: Scrutamini Scripturas, quia vos putatis in ipsis vitam æternam habere: et illæ sunt quæ testimonium perhibent de me:
     EN: Search the scriptures: for you think in them to have life everlasting. And the same are they that give testimony of me.

5:40  LA: et non vultis venire ad me ut vitam habeatis.
     EN: And you will not come to me that you may have life.

5:41  LA: Claritatem ab hominibus non accipio.
     EN: I receive not glory from men.

5:42  LA: Sed cognovi vos, quia dilectionem Dei non habetis in vobis.
     EN: But I know you, that you have not the love of God in you.

5:43  LA: Ego veni in nomine Patris mei, et non accipitis me; si alius venerit in nomine suo, illum accipietis.
     EN: I am come in the name of my Father, and you receive me not: if another shall come in his own name, him you will receive.

5:44  LA: Quomodo vos potestis credere, qui gloriam ab invicem accipitis, et gloriam quæ a solo Deo est, non quæritis?
     EN: How can you believe, who receive glory one from another: and the glory which is from God alone, you do not seek?

5:45  LA: Nolite putare quia ego accusaturus sim vos apud Patrem: est qui accusat vos Moyses, in quo vos speratis.
     EN: Think not that I will accuse you to the Father. There is one that accuses you, Moses, in whom you trust.

5:46  LA: Si enim crederetis Moysi, crederetis forsitan et mihi: de me enim ille scripsit.
     EN: For if you did believe Moses, you would perhaps believe me also: for he wrote of me.

5:47  LA: Si autem illius litteris non creditis, quomodo verbis meis credetis?
     EN: But if you do not believe his writings, how will you believe my words?

JOHN 6

6:1  LA: Post hæc abiit Jesus trans mare Galilææ, quod est Tiberiadis:
     EN: After these things Jesus went over the sea of Galilee, which is that of Tiberias.

6:2  LA: et sequebatur eum multitudo magna, quia videbant signa quæ faciebat super his qui infirmabantur.
     EN: And a great multitude followed him, because they saw the miracles which he did on them that were diseased.

6:3  LA: Subiit ergo in montem Jesus et ibi sedebat cum discipulis suis.
     EN: Jesus therefore went up into a mountain: and there he sat with his disciples.

6:4  LA: Erat autem proximum Pascha dies festus Judæorum.
     EN: Now the pasch, the festival day of the Jews, was near.

6:5  LA: Cum sublevasset ergo oculos Jesus, et vidisset quia multitudo maxima venit ad eum, dixit ad Philippum: Unde ememus panes, ut manducent hi?
     EN: When Jesus therefore had lifted up his eyes and seen that a very great multitude comes to him, he said to Philip: From where shall we buy bread, that these may eat?

6:6  LA: Hoc autem dicebat tentans eum: ipse enim sciebat quid esset facturus.
     EN: And this he said to try him: for he himself knew what he would do.

6:7  LA: Respondit ei Philippus: Ducentorum denariorum panes non sufficiunt eis, ut unusquisque modicum quid accipiat.
     EN: Philip answered him: Two hundred pennyworth of bread is not sufficient for them that everyone may take a little.

6:8  LA: Dicit ei unus ex discipulis ejus, Andreas, frater Simonis Petri:
     EN: One of his disciples, Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, says to him:

6:9  LA: Est puer unus hic qui habet quinque panes hordeaceos et duos pisces: sed hæc quid sunt inter tantos?
     EN: There is a boy here that has five barley loaves and two fishes. But what are these among so many?

6:10  LA: Dixit ergo Jesus: Facite homines discumbere. Erat autem fœnum multum in loco. Discubuerunt ergo viri, numero quasi quinque millia.
     EN: Then Jesus said: Make the men sit down. Now, there was much grass in the place. The men therefore sat down, in number about five thousand.

6:11  LA: Accepit ergo Jesus panes: et cum gratias egisset, distribuit discumbentibus: similiter et ex piscibus quantum volebant.
     EN: And Jesus took the loaves: and when he had given thanks, he distributed to them that were set down. In like manner also of the fishes, as much as they would.

6:12  LA: Ut autem impleti sunt, dixit discipulis suis: Colligite quæ superaverunt fragmenta, ne pereant.
     EN: And when they were filled, he said to his disciples: gather up the fragments that remain, lest they be lost.

6:13  LA: Collegerunt ergo, et impleverunt duodecim cophinos fragmentorum ex quinque panibus hordeaceis, quæ superfuerunt his qui manducaverant.
     EN: They gathered up therefore and filled twelve baskets with the fragments of the five barley loaves which remained over and above to them that had eaten.

6:14  LA: Illi ergo homines cum vidissent quod Jesus fecerat signum, dicebant: Quia hic est vere propheta, qui venturus est in mundum.
     EN: Now those men, when they had seen what a miracle Jesus had done, said: This is of a truth the prophet that is to come into the world.

6:15  LA: Jesus ergo cum cognovisset quia venturi essent ut raperent eum, et facerent eum regem, fugit iterum in montem ipse solus.
     EN: Jesus therefore, when he knew that they would come to take him by force and make him king, fled again into the mountain himself alone.

JOHN 7

7:1  LA: Post hæc autem ambulabat Jesus in Galilæam: non enim volebat in Judæam ambulare, quia quærebant eum Judæi interficere.
     EN: After these things, Jesus walked in Galilee: for he would not walk in Judea, because the Jews sought to kill him.

7:2  LA: Erat autem in proximo dies festus Judæorum, Scenopegia.
     EN: Now the Jews' feast of tabernacles was near.

7:3  LA: Dixerunt autem ad eum fratres ejus: Transi hinc, et vade in Judæam, ut et discipuli tui videant opera tua, quæ facis.
     EN: And his brothers said to him: Pass from here and go into Judea, that your disciples also may see your works which you do.

7:4  LA: Nemo quippe in occulto quid facit, et quærit ipse in palam esse: si hæc facis, manifesta teipsum mundo.
     EN: For there is no one that does anything in secret, and he himself seeks to be known openly. If you do these things, manifest yourself to the world.

7:5  LA: Neque enim fratres ejus credebant in eum.
     EN: For neither did his brothers believe in him.

7:6  LA: Dicit ergo eis Jesus: Tempus meum nondum advenit: tempus autem vestrum semper est paratum.
     EN: Then Jesus said to them: My time is not yet come; but your time is always ready.

7:7  LA: Non potest mundus odisse vos: me autem odit, quia ego testimonium perhibeo de illo quod opera ejus mala sunt.
     EN: The world cannot hate you: but me it hates, because I give testimony of it, that the works of it are evil,

7:8  LA: Vos ascendite ad diem festum hunc, ego autem non ascendo ad diem festum istum: quia meum tempus nondum impletum est.
     EN: Go up to this festival day: but I go not up to this festival day, because my time is not yet accomplished.

7:9  LA: Hæc cum dixisset, ipse mansit in Galilæa.
     EN: When he had said these things, he himself stayed in Galilee.

7:10  LA: Ut autem ascenderunt fratres ejus, tunc et ipse ascendit ad diem festum non manifeste, sed quasi in occulto.
     EN: But after his brothers had gone up, then he also went up to the feast, not openly, but, as it were, in secret.

7:11  LA: Judæi ergo quærebant eum in die festo, et dicebant: Ubi est ille?
     EN: The Jews therefore sought him on the festival day and said: Where is he?

7:12  LA: Et murmur multum erat in turba de eo. Quidam enim dicebant: Quia bonus est. Alii autem dicebant: Non, sed seducit turbas.
     EN: And there was much murmuring among the multitude concerning him. For some said: He is a good man. And others said: No, but he misleads the people.

7:13  LA: Nemo tamen palam loquebatur de illo propter metum Judæorum.
     EN: Yet no one spoke openly of him, for fear of the Jews.

7:14  LA: Jam autem die festo mediante, ascendit Jesus in templum, et docebat.
     EN: Now, about the midst of the feast, Jesus went up into the temple and taught.

7:15  LA: Et mirabantur Judæi, dicentes: Quomodo hic litteras scit, cum non didicerit?
     EN: And the Jews wondered, saying: How does this man know letters, having never learned?

7:16  LA: Respondit eis Jesus, et dixit: Mea doctrina non est mea, sed ejus qui misit me.
     EN: Jesus answered them and said: My doctrine is not mine, but his that sent me.

7:17  LA: Si quis voluerit voluntatem ejus facere, cognoscet de doctrina, utrum ex Deo sit, an ego a meipso loquar.
     EN: If any man will do the will of him, he shall know of the doctrine, whether it be of God, or whether I speak of myself.

7:18  LA: Qui a semetipso loquitur, gloriam propriam quærit; qui autem quærit gloriam ejus qui misit eum, hic verax est, et injustitia in illo non est.
     EN: He that speaks of himself seeks his own glory: but he that seeks the glory of him that sent him, he is true and there is no injustice in him.

7:19  LA: Nonne Moyses dedit vobis legem: et nemo ex vobis facit legem?
     EN: Did not Moses give you the law, and yet none of you keeps the law?

7:20  LA: Quid me quæritis interficere? Respondit turba, et dixit: Dæmonium habes: quis te quærit interficere?
     EN: Why seek you to kill me? The multitude answered: You have a devil. Who seeks to kill you?

7:21  LA: Respondit Jesus et dixit eis: Unum opus feci, et omnes miramini:
     EN: Jesus answered them: One work I have done: and you all wonder.

7:22  LA: propterea Moyses dedit vobis circumcisionem (non quia ex Moyse est, sed ex patribus), et in sabbato circumciditis hominem.
     EN: Therefore, Moses gave you circumcision (not because it is of Moses, but of the fathers): and on the sabbath day you circumcise a man.

7:23  LA: Si circumcisionem accipit homo in sabbato, ut non solvatur lex Moysi: mihi indignamini quia totum hominem sanum feci in sabbato?
     EN: If a man receive circumcision on the sabbath day, that the law of Moses may not be broken: are you angry at me, because I have healed the whole man on the sabbath day?

7:24  LA: Nolite judicare secundum faciem, sed justum judicium judicate.
     EN: Judge not according to the appearance: but judge just judgment.

7:25  LA: Dicebant ergo quidam ex Jerosolymis: Nonne hic est, quem quærunt interficere?
     EN: Some therefore of Jerusalem said: Is not this he whom they seek to kill?

7:26  LA: et ecce palam loquitur, et nihil ei dicunt. Numquid vere cognoverunt principes quia hic est Christus?
     EN: And behold, he speaks openly: and they say nothing to him. Have the rulers known for a truth that this is the Christ?

7:27  LA: Sed hunc scimus unde sit: Christus autem cum venerit, nemo scit unde sit.
     EN: But we know this man, from where he is: but when the Christ comes, no one knows, from where he is.

7:28  LA: Clamabat ergo Jesus in templo docens, et dicens: Et me scitis, et unde sim scitis: et a meipso non veni, sed est verus qui misit me, quem vos nescitis.
     EN: Jesus therefore cried out in the temple, teaching and saying: You both know me, and you know from where I am. And I am not come of myself: but he that sent me is true, whom you do not know.

7:29  LA: Ego scio eum: quia ab ipso sum, et ipse me misit.
     EN: I know him, because I am from him: and he has sent me.

7:30  LA: Quærebant ergo eum apprehendere: et nemo misit in illum manus, quia nondum venerat hora ejus.
     EN: They sought therefore to apprehend him: and no one laid hands on him, because his hour was not yet come.

     EN: But of the people many believed in him and said: When the Christ comes, shall he do more miracles than this man does?

     EN: The Pharisees heard the people murmuring these things concerning him: and the rulers and Pharisees sent ministers to apprehend him.

     EN: Jesus therefore said to them: Yet a little while I am with you: and then I go to him that sent me.

     EN: You shall seek me and shall not find me: and where I am, there you cannot come.

     EN: The Jews therefore said among themselves: Whither will he go, that we shall not find him? Will he go to the dispersed among the Gentiles and teach the Gentiles?

7:36  LA: quis est hic sermo, quem dixit: Quæretis me, et non invenietis: et ubi sum ego, vos non potestis venire?
     EN: What is this saying that he has said: You shall seek me and shall not find me? And: Where I am, you cannot come?

7:37  LA: In novissimo autem die magno festivitatis stabat Jesus, et clamabat dicens: Si quis sitit, veniat ad me et bibat.
     EN: And on the last, and great day of the festivity, Jesus stood and cried, saying: If any man thirst, let him come to me and drink.

7:38  LA: Qui credit in me, sicut dicit Scriptura, flumina de ventre ejus fluent aquæ vivæ.
     EN: He that believes in me, as the scripture says: Out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water.

7:39  LA: Hoc autem dixit de Spiritu, quem accepturi erant credentes in eum: nondum enim erat Spiritus datus, quia Jesus nondum erat glorificatus.
     EN: Now this he said of the Spirit which they should receive who believed in him: for yet the Spirit was not given, because Jesus was not yet glorified.

7:40  LA: Ex illa ergo turba cum audissent hos sermones ejus, dicebant: Hic est vere propheta.
     EN: Of that multitude therefore, when they had heard these words of his, some said: This is the prophet indeed.

7:41  LA: Alii dicebant: Hic est Christus. Quidam autem dicebant: Numquid a Galilæa venit Christus?
     EN: Others said: This is the Christ. But some said: Does the Christ come out of Galilee?

7:42  LA: nonne Scriptura dicit: Quia ex semine David, et de Bethlehem castello, ubi erat David, venit Christus?
     EN: Does not the scripture say: That Christ comes of the seed of David and from Bethlehem the town where David was?

7:43  LA: Dissensio itaque facta est in turba propter eum.
     EN: So there arose a dissension among the people because of him.

7:44  LA: Quidam autem ex ipsis volebant apprehendere eum: sed nemo misit super eum manus.
     EN: And some of them would have apprehended him: but no one laid hands upon him.

7:45  LA: Venerunt ergo ministri ad pontifices et pharisæos. Et dixerunt eis illi: Quare non adduxistis illum?
     EN: The ministers therefore came to the chief priests and the Pharisees. And they said to them: Why have you not brought him?

7:46  LA: Responderunt ministri: Numquam sic locutus est homo, sicut hic homo.
     EN: The ministers answered: Never did man speak like this man.

7:47  LA: Responderunt ergo eis pharisæi: Numquid et vos seducti estis?
     EN: The Pharisees therefore answered them: Are you also seduced?

7:48  LA: numquid ex principibus aliquis credidit in eum, aut ex pharisæis?
     EN: Has anyone of the rulers believed in him, or of the Pharisees?

7:49  LA: sed turba hæc, quæ non novit legem, maledicti sunt.
     EN: But this multitude, that knows not the law, are accursed.

7:50  LA: Dixit Nicodemus ad eos, ille qui venit ad eum nocte, qui unus erat ex ipsis:
     EN: Nicodemus said to them (he that came to him by night, who was one of them):

7:51  LA: Numquid lex nostra judicat hominem, nisi prius audierit ab ipso, et cognoverit quid faciat?
     EN: Does our law judge any man, unless it first hear him and know what he does?

7:52  LA: Responderunt, et dixerunt ei: Numquid et tu Galilæus es? scrutare Scripturas, et vide quia a Galilæa propheta non surgit.
     EN: They answered him: Are you also a Galilean? Search the scriptures, and see that out of Galilee a prophet rises not.

7:53  LA: Et reversi sunt unusquisque in domum suam.
     EN: And each went back to his own house.

     EN: In the Law Moses commanded us to stone such women. What, then, do you say?

8:6  LA: Hoc autem dicebant tentantes eum, ut possent accusare eum. Jesus autem inclinans se deorsum, digito scribebat in terra.
     EN: They said this to test him, so that they might accuse him. But Jesus bent down and wrote on the ground with his finger.

8:7  LA: Cum ergo perseverarent interrogantes eum, erexit se, et dixit eis: Qui sine peccato est vestrum, primus in illam lapidem mittat.
     EN: When they persisted in questioning him, he straightened up and said to them: Let the one among you who is without sin be the first to throw a stone at her.

8:8  LA: Et iterum se inclinans, scribebat in terra.
     EN: And bending down again, he wrote on the ground.

8:9  LA: Audientes autem unus post unum exibant, incipientes a senioribus: et remansit solus Jesus, et mulier in medio stans.
     EN: On hearing this, they went out one by one, beginning with the elders. Jesus was left alone, with the woman standing in the midst.

8:10  LA: Erigens autem se Jesus, dixit ei: Mulier, ubi sunt qui te accusabant? nemo te condemnavit?
     EN: Jesus straightened up and said to her: Woman, where are those who accused you? Has no one condemned you?

8:11  LA: Quæ dixit: Nemo, Domine. Dixit autem Jesus: Nec ego te condemnabo: vade, et jam amplius noli peccare.
     EN: She said: No one, Lord. Jesus said: Neither will I condemn you. Go, and sin no more.

8:12  LA: Iterum ergo locutus est eis Jesus, dicens: Ego sum lux mundi: qui sequitur me, non ambulat in tenebris, sed habebit lumen vitæ.
     EN: Again therefore, Jesus spoke to them, saying: I am the light of the world. He that follows me walks not in darkness, but shall have the light of life.

8:13  LA: Dixerunt ergo ei pharisæi: Tu de teipso testimonium perhibes; testimonium tuum non est verum.
     EN: The Pharisees therefore said to him: You give testimony of yourself. Your testimony is not true.

8:14  LA: Respondit Jesus, et dixit eis: Et si ego testimonium perhibeo de meipso, verum est testimonium meum: quia scio unde veni et quo vado; vos autem nescitis unde venio aut quo vado.
     EN: Jesus answered them: Although I give testimony of myself, my testimony is true: for I know from where I came, and where I go: but you do not know from where I come, or where I go.

8:15  LA: Vos secundum carnem judicatis: ego non judico quemquam;
     EN: You judge according to the flesh: I judge not any man.

8:16  LA: et si judico ego, judicium meum verum est, quia solus non sum: sed ego et qui misit me, Pater.
     EN: And if I do judge, my judgment is true: because I am not alone, but I and the Father that sent me.

8:17  LA: Et in lege vestra scriptum est, quia duorum hominum testimonium verum est.
     EN: And in your law it is written that the testimony of two men is true.

8:18  LA: Ego sum qui testimonium perhibeo de meipso, et testimonium perhibet de me qui misit me, Pater.
     EN: I am one that give testimony of myself: and the Father that sent me gives testimony of me.

8:19  LA: Dicebant ergo ei: Ubi est Pater tuus? Respondit Jesus: Neque me scitis, neque Patrem meum: si me sciretis, forsitan et Patrem meum sciretis.
     EN: They said therefore to him: Where is your Father? Jesus answered: Neither me do you know, nor my Father. If you did know me, perhaps you would know my Father also.

8:20  LA: Hæc verba locutus est Jesus in gazophylacio, docens in templo: et nemo apprehendit eum, quia necdum venerat hora ejus.
     EN: These words Jesus spoke in the treasury, teaching in the temple: and no one laid hands on him, because his hour was not yet come.

8:21  LA: Dixit ergo iterum eis Jesus: Ego vado, et quæretis me, et in peccato vestro moriemini. Quo ego vado, vos non potestis venire.
     EN: Again therefore Jesus said to them: I go: and you shall seek me. And you shall die in your sin. Whither I go, you cannot come.

8:22  LA: Dicebant ergo Judæi: Numquid interficiet semetipsum, quia dixit: Quo ego vado, vos non potestis venire?
     EN: The Jews therefore said: Will he kill himself, because he said: Whither I go, you cannot come?

8:23  LA: Et dicebat eis: Vos de deorsum estis, ego de supernis sum. Vos de mundo hoc estis, ego non sum de hoc mundo.
     EN: And he said to them: You are from beneath: I am from above. You are of this world: I am not of this world.

8:24  LA: Dixi ergo vobis quia moriemini in peccatis vestris: si enim non credideritis quia ego sum, moriemini in peccato vestro.
     EN: Therefore I told you that you will die in your sins; for unless you believe that I am, you will die in your sin.

8:25  LA: Dicebant ergo ei: Tu quis es? Dixit eis Jesus: Principium, qui et loquor vobis.
     EN: So they said to him: Who are you? Jesus said to them: The Beginning, I who also speak to you.

8:26  LA: Multa habeo de vobis loqui, et judicare; sed qui me misit, verax est; et ego quæ audivi ab eo, hæc loquor in mundo.
     EN: Many things I have to speak and to judge of you. But he that sent me, is true: and the things I have heard of him, these same I speak in the world.

8:27  LA: Et non cognoverunt quia Patrem ejus dicebat Deum.
     EN: And they understood not that he called God his Father.

8:28  LA: Dixit ergo eis Jesus: Cum exaltaveritis Filium hominis, tunc cognoscetis quia ego sum, et a meipso facio nihil, sed sicut docuit me Pater, hæc loquor:
     EN: Jesus therefore said to them: When you shall have lifted up, the Son of Man, then shall you know that I am he and that I do nothing of myself. But as the Father has taught me, these things I speak.

8:29  LA: et qui me misit, mecum est, et non reliquit me solum: quia ego quæ placita sunt ei, facio semper.
     EN: And he that sent me is with me: and he has not left me alone. For I do always the things that please him.

8:30  LA: Hæc illo loquente, multi crediderunt in eum.
     EN: When he spoke these things, many believed in him.

8:31  LA: Dicebat ergo Jesus ad eos, qui crediderunt ei, Judæos: Si vos manseritis in sermone meo, vere discipuli mei eritis,
     EN: Then Jesus said to those Jews who believed him: If you continue in my word, you shall be my disciples indeed.

8:32  LA: et cognoscetis veritatem, et veritas liberabit vos.
     EN: And you shall know the truth: and the truth shall make you free.

8:33  LA: Responderunt ei: Semen Abrahæ sumus, et nemini servivimus umquam: quomodo tu dicis: Liberi eritis?
     EN: They answered him: We are the seed of Abraham: and we have never been slaves to any man. How say you: You shall be free?

8:34  LA: Respondit eis Jesus: Amen, amen dico vobis: quia omnis qui facit peccatum, servus est peccati.
     EN: Jesus answered them: Amen, amen, I say to you that whoever commits sin is the servant of sin.

8:35  LA: Servus autem non manet in domo in æternum: filius autem manet in æternum.
     EN: Now the servant abides not in the house forever: but the son abides forever.

8:36  LA: Si ergo vos filius liberaverit, vere liberi eritis.
     EN: If therefore the son shall make you free, you shall be free indeed.

8:37  LA: Scio quia filii Abrahæ estis: sed quæritis me interficere, quia sermo meus non capit in vobis.
     EN: I know that you are the children of Abraham: but you seek to kill me, because my word has no place in you.

8:38  LA: Ego quod vidi apud Patrem meum, loquor: et vos quæ vidistis apud patrem vestrum, facitis.
     EN: I speak that which I have seen with my Father: and you do the things that you have seen with your father.

8:39  LA: Responderunt, et dixerunt ei: Pater noster Abraham est. Dicit eis Jesus: Si filii Abrahæ estis, opera Abrahæ facite.
     EN: They answered him: Abraham is our father. Jesus says to them: If you be the children of Abraham, do the works of Abraham.

8:40  LA: Nunc autem quæritis me interficere, hominem, qui veritatem vobis locutus sum, quam audivi a Deo: hoc Abraham non fecit.
     EN: But now you seek to kill me, a man who have spoken the truth to you, which I have heard of God. This Abraham did not.

8:41  LA: Vos facitis opera patris vestri. Dixerunt itaque ei: Nos ex fornicatione non sumus nati: unum patrem habemus Deum.
     EN: You do the works of your father. They said therefore to him: We are not born of fornication: we have one Father, even God.

8:42  LA: Dixit ergo eis Jesus: Si Deus pater vester esset, diligeretis utique et me; ego enim ex Deo processi, et veni: neque enim a meipso veni, sed ille me misit.
     EN: Jesus therefore said to them: If God were your Father, you would indeed love me. For from God I proceeded and came. For I came not of myself: but he sent me.

8:43  LA: Quare loquelam meam non cognoscitis? Quia non potestis audire sermonem meum.
     EN: Why do you not know my speech? Because you cannot hear my word.

8:44  LA: Vos ex patre diabolo estis: et desideria patris vestri vultis facere. Ille homicida erat ab initio, et in veritate non stetit: quia non est veritas in eo: cum loquitur mendacium, ex propriis loquitur, quia mendax est, et pater ejus.
     EN: You are of your father the devil: and the desires of your father you will do. He was a murderer from the beginning: and he stood not in the truth, because truth is not in him. When he speaks a lie, he speaks of his own: for he is a liar, and the father of it.

8:45  LA: Ego autem si veritatem dico, non creditis mihi.
     EN: But if I say the truth, you believe me not.

8:46  LA: Quis ex vobis arguet me de peccato? si veritatem dico vobis, quare non creditis mihi?
     EN: Which of you shall convince me of sin? If I say the truth to you, why do you not believe me?

8:47  LA: Qui ex Deo est, verba Dei audit. Propterea vos non auditis, quia ex Deo non estis.
     EN: He that is of God hears the words of God. Therefore you hear them not, because you are not of God.

8:48  LA: Responderunt ergo Judæi, et dixerunt ei: Nonne bene dicimus nos quia Samaritanus es tu, et dæmonium habes?
     EN: The Jews therefore answered him: Do not we say well that you are a Samaritan and have a devil?

8:49  LA: Respondit Jesus: Ego dæmonium non habeo: sed honorifico Patrem meum, et vos inhonorastis me.
     EN: Jesus answered: I have not a devil: but I honor my Father. And you have dishonoured me.

8:50  LA: Ego autem non quæro gloriam meam: est qui quærat, et judicet.
     EN: But I seek not my own glory: there is one that seeks and judges.

8:51  LA: Amen, amen dico vobis: si quis sermonem meum servaverit, mortem non videbit in æternum.
     EN: Amen, amen, I say to you: If any man keep my word, he shall not see death forever.

8:52  LA: Dixerunt ergo Judæi: Nunc cognovimus quia dæmonium habes. Abraham mortuus est, et prophetæ; et tu dicis: Si quis sermonem meum servaverit, non gustabit mortem in æternum.
     EN: The Jews therefore said: Now we know that you have a devil. Abraham is dead, and the prophets: and you say: If any man keep my word, he shall not taste death forever.

8:53  LA: Numquid tu major es patre nostro Abraham, qui mortuus est? et prophetæ mortui sunt. Quem teipsum facis?
     EN: Are you greater than our father Abraham who is dead? And the prophets are dead. Whom do you make yourself?

8:54  LA: Respondit Jesus: Si ego glorifico meipsum, gloria mea nihil est: est Pater meus, qui glorificat me, quem vos dicitis quia Deus vester est,
     EN: Jesus answered: If I glorify myself, my glory is nothing. It is my Father that glorifies me, of whom you say that he is your God.

8:55  LA: et non cognovistis eum: ego autem novi eum. Et si dixero quia non scio eum, ero similis vobis, mendax. Sed scio eum, et sermonem ejus servo.
     EN: And you have not known him: but I know him. And if I shall say that I know him not, I shall be like to you, a liar. But I do know him and do keep his word.

8:56  LA: Abraham pater vester exsultavit ut videret diem meum: vidit, et gavisus est.
     EN: Abraham your father rejoiced that he might see my day: he saw it and was glad.

8:57  LA: Dixerunt ergo Judæi ad eum: Quinquaginta annos nondum habes, et Abraham vidisti?
     EN: The Jews therefore said to him: You are not yet fifty years old. And have you seen Abraham?

8:58  LA: Dixit eis Jesus: Amen, amen dico vobis, antequam Abraham fieret, ego sum.
     EN: Jesus said to them: Amen, amen, I say to you: before Abraham came to be, I am.

8:59  LA: Tulerunt ergo lapides, ut jacerent in eum: Jesus autem abscondit se, et exivit de templo.
     EN: They took up stones therefore to cast at him. But Jesus hid himself and went out of the temple.

JOHN 9

9:1  LA: Et præteriens Jesus vidit hominem cæcum a nativitate:
     EN: And Jesus passing by, saw a man who was blind from his birth.

9:2  LA: et interrogaverunt eum discipuli ejus: Rabbi, quis peccavit, hic, aut parentes ejus, ut cæcus nasceretur?
     EN: And his disciples asked him: Rabbi, who has sinned, this man or his parents, that he should be born blind?

9:3  LA: Respondit Jesus: Neque hic peccavit, neque parentes ejus: sed ut manifestentur opera Dei in illo.
     EN: Jesus answered: Neither has this man sinned, nor his parents; but that the works of God should be made manifest in him.

9:4  LA: Me oportet operari opera ejus qui misit me, donec dies est: venit nox, quando nemo potest operari:
     EN: I must work the works of him that sent me, while it is day: the night comes, when no one can work.

9:5  LA: quamdiu sum in mundo, lux sum mundi.
     EN: As long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world.

9:6  LA: Hæc cum dixisset, exspuit in terram, et fecit lutum ex sputo, et linivit lutum super oculos ejus,
     EN: When he had said these things, he spat on the ground and made clay of the spittle and spread the clay upon his eyes,

9:7  LA: et dixit ei: Vade, lava in natatoria Siloë (quod interpretatur Missus). Abiit ergo, et lavit, et venit videns.
     EN: And said to him: Go, wash in the pool of Siloam, which is interpreted, Sent. He went therefore and washed: and he came seeing.

9:8  LA: Itaque vicini, et qui viderant eum prius quia mendicus erat, dicebant: Nonne hic est qui sedebat, et mendicabat? Alii dicebant: Quia hic est.
     EN: The neighbours, therefore, and they who had seen him before that he was a beggar, said: Is not this he that sat and begged? Some said: This is he.

9:9  LA: Alii autem: Nequaquam, sed similis est ei. Ille vero dicebat: Quia ego sum.
     EN: But others said: No, but he is like him. But he said: I am he.

9:10  LA: Dicebant ergo ei: Quomodo aperti sunt tibi oculi?
     EN: They said therefore to him: How were your eyes opened?

9:11  LA: Respondit: Ille homo qui dicitur Jesus, lutum fecit: et unxit oculos meos, et dixit mihi: Vade ad natatoria Siloë, et lava. Et abii, et lavi, et video.
     EN: He answered: That man that is called Jesus made clay and anointed my eyes and said to me: Go to the pool of Siloam and wash. And I went: I washed: and I see.

9:12  LA: Et dixerunt ei: Ubi est ille? Ait: Nescio.
     EN: And they said to him: Where is he? He says: I know not.

9:13  LA: Adducunt eum ad pharisæos, qui cæcus fuerat.
     EN: They bring him that had been blind to the Pharisees.

9:14  LA: Erat autem sabbatum quando lutum fecit Jesus, et aperuit oculos ejus.
     EN: Now it was the sabbath, when Jesus made the clay and opened his eyes.

9:15  LA: Iterum ergo interrogabant eum pharisæi quomodo vidisset. Ille autem dixit eis: Lutum mihi posuit super oculos, et lavi, et video.
     EN: Again therefore the Pharisees asked him how he had received his sight. But he said to them: He put clay upon my eyes: and I washed: and I see.

9:16  LA: Dicebant ergo ex pharisæis quidam: Non est hic homo a Deo, qui sabbatum non custodit. Alii autem dicebant: Quomodo potest homo peccator hæc signa facere? Et schisma erat inter eos.
     EN: Some therefore of the Pharisees said: This man is not of God, who keeps not the sabbath. But others said: How can a man that is a sinner do such miracles? And there was a division among them.

9:17  LA: Dicunt ergo cæco iterum: Tu quid dicis de illo qui aperuit oculos tuos? Ille autem dixit: Quia propheta est.
     EN: They say therefore to the blind man again: What say you of him that has opened your eyes? And he said: He is a prophet.

9:18  LA: Non crediderunt ergo Judæi de illo, quia cæcus fuisset et vidisset, donec vocaverunt parentes ejus, qui viderat:
     EN: The Jews then did not believe concerning him, that he had been blind and had received his sight, until they called the parents of him that had received his sight,

9:19  LA: et interrogaverunt eos, dicentes: Hic est filius vester, quem vos dicitis quia cæcus natus est? quomodo ergo nunc videt?
     EN: And asked them, saying: Is this your son, who you say was born blind? How then does he now see?

9:20  LA: Responderunt eis parentes ejus, et dixerunt: Scimus quia hic est filius noster, et quia cæcus natus est:
     EN: His parents answered them and said: We know that this is our son and that he was born blind:

9:21  LA: quomodo autem nunc videat, nescimus: aut quis ejus aperuit oculos, nos nescimus; ipsum interrogate: ætatem habet, ipse de se loquatur.
     EN: But how he now sees, we know not: or who has opened his eyes, we know not. Ask himself: he is of age: Let him speak for himself.

9:22  LA: Hæc dixerunt parentes ejus, quoniam timebant Judæos: jam enim conspiraverunt Judæi, ut si quis eum confiteretur esse Christum, extra synagogam fieret.
     EN: These things his parents said, because they feared the Jews: for the Jews had already agreed among themselves that if any man should confess him to be Christ, he should be put out of the synagogue.

9:23  LA: Propterea parentes ejus dixerunt: Quia ætatem habet, ipsum interrogate.
     EN: Therefore did his parents say: He is of age. Ask himself.

9:24  LA: Vocaverunt ergo rursum hominem qui fuerat cæcus, et dixerunt ei: Da gloriam Deo: nos scimus quia hic homo peccator est.
     EN: They therefore called the man again that had been blind and said to him: Give glory to God. We know that this man is a sinner.

9:25  LA: Dixit ergo eis ille: Si peccator est, nescio; unum scio, quia cæcus cum essem, modo video.
     EN: He said therefore to them: If he be a sinner, I know not. One thing I know, that whereas I was blind, now I see.

9:26  LA: Dixerunt ergo illi: Quid fecit tibi? quomodo aperuit tibi oculos?
     EN: They said then to him: What did he to you? How did he open your eyes?

9:27  LA: Respondit eis: Dixi vobis jam, et audistis: quod iterum vultis audire? numquid et vos vultis discipuli ejus fieri?
     EN: He answered them: I have told you already, and you have heard. Why would you hear it again? Will you also become his disciples?

9:28  LA: Maledixerunt ergo ei, et dixerunt: Tu discipulus illius sis: nos autem Moysi discipuli sumus.
     EN: They reviled him therefore and said: Be his disciple; but we are the disciples of Moses.

9:29  LA: Nos scimus quia Moysi locutus est Deus; hunc autem nescimus unde sit.
     EN: We know that God spoke to Moses: but as to this man, we know not from where he is.

9:30  LA: Respondit ille homo, et dixit eis: In hoc enim mirabile est quia vos nescitis unde sit, et aperuit meos oculos:
     EN: The man answered them: why, herein is a wonderful thing, that you do not know from where he is, and he has opened my eyes.

9:31  LA: scimus autem quia peccatores Deus non audit: sed si quis Dei cultor est, et voluntatem ejus facit, hunc exaudit.
     EN: Now we know that God does not hear sinners: but if a man be a server of God and does his will, him he hears.

9:32  LA: A sæculo non est auditum quia quis aperuit oculos cæci nati.
     EN: From the beginning of the world it has not been heard, that any man has opened the eyes of one born blind.

9:33  LA: Nisi esset hic a Deo, non poterat facere quidquam.
     EN: Unless this man were of God, he could not do anything.

9:34  LA: Responderunt, et dixerunt ei: In peccatis natus es totus, et tu doces nos? Et ejecerunt eum foras.
     EN: They answered him: You were wholly born in sins; and do you teach us? And they cast him out.

9:35  LA: Audivit Jesus quia ejecerunt eum foras: et cum invenisset eum, dixit ei: Tu credis in Filium Dei?
     EN: Jesus heard that they had cast him out. And when he had found him, he said to him: Do you believe in the Son of God?

9:36  LA: Respondit ille, et dixit: Quis est, Domine, ut credam in eum?
     EN: He answered, and said: Who is he, Lord, that I may believe in him?

9:37  LA: Et dixit ei Jesus: Et vidisti eum, et qui loquitur tecum, ipse est.
     EN: And Jesus said to him: You have both seen him; and it is he that talks with you.

9:38  LA: At ille ait: Credo, Domine. Et procidens adoravit eum.
     EN: And he said: I believe, Lord. And falling down, he adored him.

9:39  LA: Et dixit Jesus: In judicium ego in hunc mundum veni: ut qui non vident videant, et qui vident cæci fiant.
     EN: And Jesus said: For judgment I am come into this world: that they who see not may see; and they who see may become blind.

9:40  LA: Et audierunt quidam ex pharisæis qui cum ipso erant, et dixerunt ei: Numquid et nos cæci sumus?
     EN: And some of the Pharisees, who were with him, heard: and they said to him: Are we also blind?

9:41  LA: Dixit eis Jesus: Si cæci essetis, non haberetis peccatum. Nunc vero dicitis, Quia videmus: peccatum vestrum manet.
     EN: Jesus said to them: If you were blind, you should not have sin: but now you say: We see. Your sin remains.

JOHN 10

10:1  LA: Amen , amen dico vobis: qui non intrat per ostium in ovile ovium, sed ascendit aliunde, ille fur est et latro.
     EN: Amen, amen, I say to you: He that enters not by the door into the sheepfold but climbs up another way, the same is a thief and a robber.

10:2  LA: Qui autem intrat per ostium, pastor est ovium.
     EN: But he that enters in by the door is the shepherd of the sheep.

10:3  LA: Huic ostiarius aperit, et oves vocem ejus audiunt, et proprias oves vocat nominatim, et educit eas.
     EN: To him the porter opens: and the sheep hear his voice. And he calls his own sheep by name and leads them out.

10:4  LA: Et cum proprias oves emiserit, ante eas vadit: et oves illum sequuntur, quia sciunt vocem ejus.
     EN: And when he has let out his own sheep, he goes before them: and the sheep follow him, because they know his voice.

10:5  LA: Alienum autem non sequuntur, sed fugiunt ab eo: quia non noverunt vocem alienorum.
     EN: But a stranger they follow not, but fly from him, because they know not the voice of strangers.

10:6  LA: Hoc proverbium dixit eis Jesus: illi autem non cognoverunt quid loqueretur eis.
     EN: This proverb Jesus spoke to them. But they understood not what he spoke to them.

10:7  LA: Dixit ergo eis iterum Jesus: Amen, amen dico vobis, quia ego sum ostium ovium.
     EN: Jesus therefore said to them again: Amen, amen, I say to you, I am the door of the sheep.

10:8  LA: Omnes quotquot venerunt, fures sunt, et latrones, et non audierunt eos oves.
     EN: All, as many as have come, are thieves and robbers: and the sheep heard them not.

10:9  LA: Ego sum ostium. Per me si quis introierit, salvabitur: et ingredietur, et egredietur, et pascua inveniet.
     EN: I am the door. By me, if any man enter in, he shall be saved: and he shall go in and go out, and shall find pastures.

10:10  LA: Fur non venit nisi ut furetur, et mactet, et perdat. Ego veni ut vitam habeant, et abundantius habeant.
     EN: The thief comes not, but for to steal and to kill and to destroy. I am come that they may have life and may have it more abundantly.

10:11  LA: Ego sum pastor bonus. Bonus pastor animam suam dat pro ovibus suis.
     EN: I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd gives his life for his sheep.

10:12  LA: Mercenarius autem, et qui non est pastor, cujus non sunt oves propriæ, videt lupum venientem, et dimittit oves, et fugit: et lupus rapit, et dispergit oves;
     EN: But the hireling and he that is not the shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, sees the wolf coming and leaves the sheep and flees: and the wolf catches and scatters the sheep,

10:13  LA: mercenarius autem fugit, quia mercenarius est, et non pertinet ad eum de ovibus.
     EN: And the hireling flees, because he is a hireling: and he has no care for the sheep.

10:14  LA: Ego sum pastor bonus: et cognosco meas, et cognoscunt me meæ.
     EN: I am the good shepherd: and I know mine, and mine know me.

10:15  LA: Sicut novit me Pater, et ego agnosco Patrem: et animam meam pono pro ovibus meis.
     EN: As the Father knows me, and I know the Father: and I lay down my life for my sheep.

     EN: Now is the judgment of the world: now shall the prince of this world be cast out.

12:32  LA: Et ego, si exaltatus fuero a terra, omnia traham ad meipsum.
     EN: And I, if I be lifted up from the earth, will draw all things to myself.

12:33  LA: (Hoc autem dicebat, significans qua morte esset moriturus.)
     EN: (Now this he said, signifying what death he should die.)

     EN: The multitude answered him: We have heard out of the law that Christ abides forever. And how say you: The Son of Man must be lifted up? Who is this Son of Man?

12:35  LA: Dixit ergo eis Jesus: Adhuc modicum, lumen in vobis est. Ambulate dum lucem habetis, ut non vos tenebræ comprehendant; et qui ambulat in tenebris, nescit quo vadat.
     EN: Jesus therefore said to them: Yet a little while, the light is among you. Walk while you have the light, that the darkness overtake you not. And he that walks in darkness knows not where he goes.

12:36  LA: Dum lucem habetis, credite in lucem, ut filii lucis sitis. Hæc locutus est Jesus, et abiit et abscondit se ab eis.
     EN: Whilst you have the light, believe in the light, that you may be the children of light. These things Jesus spoke: and he went away and hid himself from them.

12:37  LA: Cum autem tanta signa fecisset coram eis, non credebant in eum;
     EN: And whereas he had done so many miracles before them, they believed not in him:

12:38  LA: ut sermo Isaiæ prophetæ impleretur, quem dixit: Domine, quis credidit auditui nostro? et brachium Domini cui revelatum est?
     EN: That the saying of Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, which he said: Lord, who has believed our hearing? And to whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed?

12:39  LA: Propterea non poterant credere, quia iterum dixit Isaias:
     EN: Therefore they could not believe, because Isaiah said again:

12:40  LA: Excæcavit oculos eorum, et induravit cor eorum ut non videant oculis, et non intelligant corde, et convertantur, et sanem eos.
     EN: He has blinded their eyes and hardened their heart, that they should not see with their eyes, nor understand with their heart and be converted: and I should heal them.

12:41  LA: Hæc dixit Isaias, quando vidit gloriam ejus, et locutus est de eo.
     EN: These things said Isaiah, when he saw his glory, and spoke of him.

12:42  LA: Verumtamen et ex principibus multi crediderunt in eum: sed propter pharisæos non confitebantur, ut e synagoga non ejicerentur.
     EN: However, many of the chief men also believed in him: but because of the Pharisees they did not confess him, that they might not be cast out of the synagogue.

12:43  LA: Dilexerunt enim gloriam hominum magis quam gloriam Dei.
     EN: For they loved the glory of men more than the glory of God.

12:44  LA: Jesus autem clamavit, et dixit: Qui credit in me, non credit in me, sed in eum qui misit me.
     EN: But Jesus cried and said: He that believes in me does not believe in me, but in him that sent me.

12:45  LA: Et qui videt me, videt eum qui misit me.
     EN: And he that sees me, sees him that sent me.

12:46  LA: Ego lux in mundum veni, ut omnis qui credit in me, in tenebris non maneat.
     EN: I am come, a light into the world, that whoever believes in me may not remain in darkness.

12:47  LA: Et si quis audierit verba mea, et non custodierit, ego non judico eum; non enim veni ut judicem mundum, sed ut salvificem mundum.
     EN: And if any man hear my words and keep them not, I do not judge him for I came not to judge the world, but to save the world.

12:48  LA: Qui spernit me et non accipit verba mea, habet qui judicet eum. Sermo quem locutus sum, ille judicabit eum in novissimo die.
     EN: He that despises me and receives not my words has one that judges him. The word that I have spoken, the same shall judge him in the last day.

12:49  LA: Quia ego ex meipso non sum locutus, sed qui misit me, Pater, ipse mihi mandatum dedit quid dicam et quid loquar.
     EN: For I have not spoken of myself: but the Father who sent me, he gave me commandment what I should say and what I should speak.

12:50  LA: Et scio quia mandatum ejus vita æterna est: quæ ergo ego loquor, sicut dixit mihi Pater, sic loquor.
     EN: And I know that his commandment is life everlasting. The things therefore that I speak, even as the Father said to me, so do I speak.

JOHN 13

     EN: Before the festival day of the pasch, Jesus knowing that his hour had come, that he should pass out of this world to the Father: having loved his own who were in the world, he loved them to the end.

13:2  LA: Et cœna facta, cum diabolus jam misisset in cor ut traderet eum Judas Simonis Iscariotæ:
     EN: And when supper was done (the devil having now put into the heart of Judas, the son of Simon Iscariot, to betray him),

13:3  LA: sciens quia omnia dedit ei Pater in manus, et quia a Deo exivit, et ad Deum vadit:
     EN: Knowing that the Father had given him all things into his hands and that he came from God and goes to God,

     EN: He rises from supper and lays aside his garments and, having taken a towel, girded himself.

13:5  LA: Deinde mittit aquam in pelvim, et cœpit lavare pedes discipulorum, et extergere linteo, quo erat præcinctus.
     EN: After that, he puts water into a basin and began to wash the feet of the disciples and to wipe them with the towel wherewith he was girded.

13:6  LA: Venit ergo ad Simonem Petrum. Et dicit ei Petrus: Domine, tu mihi lavas pedes?
     EN: He comes therefore to Simon Peter. And Peter says to him: Lord, do you wash my feet?

13:7  LA: Respondit Jesus, et dixit ei: Quod ego facio, tu nescis modo: scies autem postea.
     EN: Jesus answered him: What I do, you do not know now; but you shall know hereafter.

13:8  LA: Dicit ei Petrus: Non lavabis mihi pedes in æternum. Respondit ei Jesus: Si non lavero te, non habebis partem mecum.
     EN: Peter says to him: You shall never wash my feet, Jesus answered him: If I wash you not, you shall have no part with me.

13:9  LA: Dicit ei Simon Petrus: Domine, non tantum pedes meos, sed et manus, et caput.
     EN: Simon Peter says to him: Lord, not only my feet, but also my hands and my head.

13:10  LA: Dicit ei Jesus: Qui lotus est, non indiget nisi ut pedes lavet, sed est mundus totus. Et vos mundi estis, sed non omnes.
     EN: Jesus says to him: He that is washed needs not but to wash his feet, but is clean wholly. And you are clean, but not all.

13:11  LA: Sciebat enim quisnam esset qui traderet eum; propterea dixit: Non estis mundi omnes.
     EN: For he knew who he was that would betray him; therefore he said: You are not all clean.

13:12  LA: Postquam ergo lavit pedes eorum, et accepit vestimenta sua, cum recubuisset iterum, dixit eis: Scitis quid fecerim vobis?
     EN: Then after he had washed their feet and taken his garments, being set down again, he said to them: Know you what I have done to you?

13:13  LA: Vos vocatis me Magister et Domine, et bene dicitis: sum etenim.
     EN: You call me Master and Lord. And you say well: for so I am.

13:14  LA: Si ergo ego lavi pedes vestros, Dominus et Magister, et vos debetis alter alterius lavare pedes.
     EN: If then I being your Lord and Master, have washed your feet; you also ought to wash one another’s feet.

13:15  LA: Exemplum enim dedi vobis, ut quemadmodum ego feci vobis, ita et vos faciatis.
     EN: For I have given you an example, that as I have done to you, so you do also.

13:16  LA: Amen, amen dico vobis: non est servus major domino suo: neque apostolus major est eo qui misit illum.
     EN: Amen, amen, I say to you: The servant is not greater than his lord: neither is the apostle greater than he that sent him.

13:17  LA: Si hæc scitis, beati eritis si feceritis ea.
     EN: If you know these things, you shall be blessed if you do them.

13:18  LA: Non de omnibus vobis dico: ego scio quos elegerim; sed ut adimpleatur Scriptura: Qui manducat mecum panem, levabit contra me calcaneum suum.
     EN: I speak not of you all: I know whom I have chosen. But that the scripture may be fulfilled: He that eats bread with me shall lift up his heel against me,

13:19  LA: Amodo dico vobis, priusquam fiat: ut cum factum fuerit, credatis quia ego sum.
     EN: At present I tell you, before it happen: that when it will happen, you may believe that I am he.

13:20  LA: Amen, amen dico vobis: qui accipit si quem misero, me accipit; qui autem me accipit, accipit eum qui me misit.
     EN: Amen, amen, I say to you, he that receives whomever I send receives me: and he that receives me receives him that sent me.

13:21  LA: Cum hæc dixisset Jesus, turbatus est spiritu: et protestatus est, et dixit: Amen, amen dico vobis, quia unus ex vobis tradet me.
     EN: When Jesus had said these things, he was troubled in spirit; and he testified, and said: Amen, amen, I say to you, one of you shall betray me.

13:22  LA: Aspiciebant ergo ad invicem discipuli, hæsitantes de quo diceret.
     EN: The disciples therefore looked one upon another, doubting of whom he spoke.

13:23  LA: Erat ergo recumbens unus ex discipulis ejus in sinu Jesu, quem diligebat Jesus.
     EN: Now there was leaning on Jesus’ bosom one of his disciples, whom Jesus loved.

13:24  LA: Innuit ergo huic Simon Petrus, et dixit ei: Quis est, de quo dicit?
     EN: Simon Peter therefore beckoned to him and said to him: Who is it of whom he speaks?

13:25  LA: Itaque cum recubuisset ille supra pectus Jesu, dicit ei: Domine, quis est?
     EN: He therefore, leaning on the breast of Jesus, says to him: Lord, who is it?

13:26  LA: Respondit Jesus: Ille est cui ego intinctum panem porrexero. Et cum intinxisset panem, dedit Judæ Simonis Iscariotæ.
     EN: Jesus answered: He it is to whom I shall reach bread dipped. And when he had dipped the bread, he gave it to Judas Iscariot, the son of Simon.

13:27  LA: Et post buccellam, introivit in eum Satanas. Et dixit ei Jesus: Quod facis, fac citius.
     EN: And after the morsel, Satan entered into him. And Jesus said to him: That which you do, do quickly.

13:28  LA: Hoc autem nemo scivit discumbentium ad quid dixerit ei.
     EN: Now no one at the table knew to what purpose he said this to him.

13:29  LA: Quidam enim putabant, quia loculos habebat Judas, quod dixisset ei Jesus: Eme ea quæ opus sunt nobis ad diem festum: aut egenis ut aliquid daret.
     EN: For some thought, because Judas had the purse, that Jesus had said to him: Buy those things which we need for the festival day: or that he should give something to the poor.

13:30  LA: Cum ergo accepisset ille buccellam, exivit continuo. Erat autem nox.
     EN: He therefore, having received the morsel, went out immediately. And it was night.

13:31  LA: Cum ergo exisset, dixit Jesus: Nunc clarificatus est Filius hominis, et Deus clarificatus est in eo.
     EN: When he therefore had gone out, Jesus said: Now is the Son of Man glorified; and God is glorified in him.

13:32  LA: Si Deus clarificatus est in eo, et Deus clarificabit eum in semetipso: et continuo clarificabit eum.
     EN: If God be glorified in him, God also will glorify him in himself: and immediately will he glorify him.

13:33  LA: Filioli, adhuc modicum vobiscum sum. Quæretis me; et sicut dixi Judæis, quo ego vado, vos non potestis venire: et vobis dico modo.
     EN: Little children, yet a little while I am with you. You shall seek me. And as I said to the Jews: Whither I go you cannot come; so I say to you now.

13:34  LA: Mandatum novum do vobis: ut diligatis invicem: sicut dilexi vos, ut et vos diligatis invicem.
     EN: A new commandment I give to you: That you love one another, as I have loved you, that you also love one another.

13:35  LA: In hoc cognoscent omnes quia discipuli mei estis, si dilectionem habueritis ad invicem.
     EN: By this shall all men know that you are my disciples, if you have love one for another.

13:36  LA: Dicit ei Simon Petrus: Domine, quo vadis? Respondit Jesus: Quo ego vado non potes me modo sequi: sequeris autem postea.
     EN: Simon Peter says to him: Lord, where do you go? Jesus answered: Whither I go, you can not follow me now: but you shall follow hereafter.

13:37  LA: Dicit ei Petrus: Quare non possum te sequi modo? animam meam pro te ponam.
     EN: Peter says to him: Why cannot I follow you now? I will lay down my life for you.

13:38  LA: Respondit ei Jesus: Animam tuam pro me pones? amen, amen dico tibi: non cantabit gallus, donec ter me neges.
     EN: Jesus answered him: Will you lay down your life for me? Amen, amen, I say to you, the rooster shall not crow, until you deny me thrice.

JOHN 14

14:1  LA: Non turbetur cor vestrum. Creditis in Deum, et in me credite.
     EN: Do not let your heart be troubled. You believe in God; believe also in me.

14:2  LA: In domo Patris mei mansiones multæ sunt; si quominus dixissem vobis: quia vado parare vobis locum.
     EN: In my Father’s house are many dwelling places. If it were otherwise, I would have told you, for I am going to prepare a place for you.

14:3  LA: Et si abiero, et præparavero vobis locum, iterum venio, et accipiam vos ad meipsum: ut ubi sum ego, et vos sitis.
     EN: And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again and receive you to myself, so that where I am, you may be also.

14:4  LA: Et quo ego vado scitis, et viam scitis.
     EN: And where I go you know: and the way you know.

14:5  LA: Dicit ei Thomas: Domine, nescimus quo vadis: et quomodo possumus viam scire?
     EN: Thomas says to him: Lord, we know not where you go. And how can we know the way?

14:6  LA: Dicit ei Jesus: Ego sum via, et veritas, et vita. Nemo venit ad Patrem, nisi per me.
     EN: Jesus said to him: I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.

14:7  LA: Si cognovissetis me, et Patrem meum utique cognovissetis: et amodo cognoscetis eum, et vidistis eum.
     EN: If you had known me, you would without doubt have known my Father also: and from now on you shall know him. And you have seen him.

14:8  LA: Dicit ei Philippus: Domine, ostende nobis Patrem, et sufficit nobis.
     EN: Philip says to him: Lord, show us the Father; and it is enough for us.

14:9  LA: Dicit ei Jesus: Tanto tempore vobiscum sum, et non cognovistis me? Philippe, qui videt me, videt et Patrem. Quomodo tu dicis: Ostende nobis Patrem?
     EN: Jesus says to him: Have I been so long a time with you and have you not known me? Philip, he that sees me sees the Father also. How say you: Show us the Father?

14:10  LA: Non creditis quia ego in Patre, et Pater in me est? Verba quæ ego loquor vobis, a meipso non loquor. Pater autem in me manens, ipse fecit opera.
     EN: Do you not believe that I am in the Father and the Father in me? The words that I speak to you, I speak not of myself. But the Father who abides in me, he does the works.

14:11  LA: Non creditis quia ego in Patre, et Pater in me est?
     EN: Do you not believe that I am in the Father and the Father in me?

14:12  LA: alioquin propter opera ipsa credite. Amen, amen dico vobis, qui credit in me, opera quæ ego facio, et ipse faciet, et majora horum faciet: quia ego ad Patrem vado.
     EN: Otherwise believe for the very works’ sake. Amen, amen, I say to you, he that believes in me, the works that I do, he also shall do: and greater than these shall he do: because I go to the Father.

14:13  LA: Et quodcumque petieritis Patrem in nomine meo, hoc faciam: ut glorificetur Pater in Filio.
     EN: And whatever you shall ask the Father in my name, that will I do: that the Father may be glorified in the Son.

14:14  LA: Si quid petieritis me in nomine meo, hoc faciam.
     EN: If you shall ask me anything in my name, that I will do.

14:15  LA: Si diligitis me, mandata mea servate:
     EN: If you love me, keep my commandments.

14:16  LA: et ego rogabo Patrem, et alium Paraclitum dabit vobis, ut maneat vobiscum in æternum,
     EN: And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Paraclete, to remain with you forever,

14:17  LA: Spiritum veritatis, quem mundus non potest accipere, quia non videt eum, nec scit eum: vos autem cognoscetis eum, quia apud vos manebit, et in vobis erit.
     EN: the Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot receive because it neither sees him nor knows him. But you will know him, because he will remain with you and will be in you.

14:18  LA: Non relinquam vos orphanos: veniam ad vos.
     EN: I will not leave you orphans: I will come to you.

14:19  LA: Adhuc modicum, et mundus me jam non videt. Vos autem videtis me: quia ego vivo, et vos vivetis.
     EN: Yet a little while and the world sees me no more. But you see me: because I live, and you shall live.

14:20  LA: In illo die vos cognoscetis quia ego sum in Patre meo, et vos in me, et ego in vobis.
     EN: In that day you shall know that I am in my Father: and you in me, and I in you.

14:21  LA: Qui habet mandata mea, et servat ea: ille est qui diligit me. Qui autem diligit me, diligetur a Patre meo: et ego diligam eum, et manifestabo ei meipsum.
     EN: He that has my commandments and keeps them; he it is that loves me. And he that loves me shall be loved of my Father: and I will love him and will manifest myself to him.

14:22  LA: Dicit ei Judas, non ille Iscariotes: Domine, quid factum est, quia manifestaturus es nobis teipsum, et non mundo?
     EN: Judas says to him, not the Iscariot: Lord, how is it that you will manifest yourself to us, and not to the world?

14:23  LA: Respondit Jesus, et dixit ei: Si quis diligit me, sermonem meum servabit, et Pater meus diliget eum, et ad eum veniemus, et mansionem apud eum faciemus;
     EN: Jesus answered him: If anyone love me, he will keep my word. And my Father will love him and we will come to him and will make our abode with him.

14:24  LA: qui non diligit me, sermones meos non servat. Et sermonem, quem audistis, non est meus: sed ejus qui misit me, Patris.
     EN: He that loves me not keeps not my words. And the word which you have heard is not mine; but the Father’s who sent me.

14:25  LA: Hæc locutus sum vobis apud vos manens.
     EN: These things have I spoken to you, abiding with you.

14:26  LA: Paraclitus autem Spiritus Sanctus, quem mittet Pater in nomine meo, ille vos docebit omnia, et suggeret vobis omnia quæcumque dixero vobis.
     EN: But the Paraclete, the Holy Spirit whom the Father will send in my name, will teach you all things and remind you of everything I shall have said to you.

14:27  LA: Pacem relinquo vobis, pacem meam do vobis: non quomodo mundus dat, ego do vobis. Non turbetur cor vestrum, neque formidet.
     EN: Peace I leave with you: my peace I give to you: not as the world gives, do I give to you. Let not your heart be troubled: nor let it be afraid.

14:28  LA: Audistis quia ego dixi vobis: Vado, et venio ad vos. Si diligeretis me, gauderetis utique, quia vado ad Patrem: quia Pater major me est.
     EN: You heard that I said to you: I am going away and coming to you. If you loved me, you would indeed rejoice that I am going to the Father, because the Father is greater than I.

14:29  LA: Et nunc dixi vobis priusquam fiat: ut cum factum fuerit, credatis.
     EN: And now I have told you before it happen: that when it will happen, you may believe.

14:30  LA: Jam non multa loquar vobiscum: venit enim princeps mundi hujus, et in me non habet quidquam.
     EN: I will not now speak many things with you. For the prince of this world comes: and in me he has not anything.

14:31  LA: Sed ut cognoscat mundus quia diligo Patrem, et sicut mandatum dedit mihi Pater, sic facio. Surgite, eamus hinc.
     EN: But that the world may know that I love the Father: and as the Father has given me commandment, so do I. Arise, let us go from here.

JOHN 15

15:1  LA: Ego sum vitis vera, et Pater meus agricola est.
     EN: I am the true vine: and my Father is the husbandman.

15:2  LA: Omnem palmitem in me non ferentem fructum, tollet eum, et omnem qui fert fructum, purgabit eum, ut fructum plus afferat.
     EN: Every branch in me that bears not fruit, he will take away: and everyone that bears fruit, he will purge it, that it may bring forth more fruit.

15:3  LA: Jam vos mundi estis propter sermonem quem locutus sum vobis.
     EN: Now you are clean, by reason of the word which I have spoken to you.

15:4  LA: Manete in me, et ego in vobis. Sicut palmes non potest ferre fructum a semetipso, nisi manserit in vite, sic nec vos, nisi in me manseritis.
     EN: Abide in me: and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, unless it abide in the vine, so neither can you, unless you abide in me.

15:5  LA: Ego sum vitis, vos palmites: qui manet in me, et ego in eo, hic fert fructum multum, quia sine me nihil potestis facere.
     EN: I am the vine: you the branches. He that abides in me, and I in him, the same bears much fruit: for without me you can do nothing.

15:6  LA: Si quis in me non manserit, mittetur foras sicut palmes, et arescet, et colligent eum, et in ignem mittent, et ardet.
     EN: If anyone abide not in me, he shall be cast forth as a branch and shall wither: and they shall gather him up and cast him into the fire: and he burns.

15:7  LA: Si manseritis in me, et verba mea in vobis manserint, quodcumque volueritis petetis, et fiet vobis.
     EN: If you abide in me and my words abide in you, you shall ask whatever you will: and it shall be done to you.

15:8  LA: In hoc clarificatus est Pater meus, ut fructum plurimum afferatis, et efficiamini mei discipuli.
     EN: In this is my Father glorified: that you bring forth very much fruit and become my disciples.

15:9  LA: Sicut dilexit me Pater, et ego dilexi vos. Manete in dilectione mea.
     EN: As the Father has loved me, I also have loved you. Abide in my love.

15:10  LA: Si præcepta mea servaveritis, manebitis in dilectione mea, sicut et ego Patris mei præcepta servavi, et maneo in ejus dilectione.
     EN: If you keep my commandments, you shall abide in my love: as I also have kept my Father’s commandments and do abide in his love.

15:11  LA: Hæc locutus sum vobis: ut gaudium meum in vobis sit, et gaudium vestrum impleatur.
     EN: These things I have spoken to you, that my joy may be in you, and your joy may be filled.

15:12  LA: Hoc est præceptum meum, ut diligatis invicem, sicut dilexi vos.
     EN: This is my commandment, that you love one another, as I have loved you.

15:13  LA: Majorem hac dilectionem nemo habet, ut animam suam ponat quis pro amicis suis.
     EN: Greater love than this no one has, that a man lay down his life for his friends.

15:14  LA: Vos amici mei estis, si feceritis quæ ego præcipio vobis.
     EN: You are my friends, if you do the things that I command you.

15:15  LA: Jam non dicam vos servos: quia servus nescit quid faciat dominus ejus. Vos autem dixi amicos: quia omnia quæcumque audivi a Patre meo, nota feci vobis.
     EN: I will not now call you servants: for the servant knows not what his lord does. But I have called you friends: because all things, whatever I have heard of my Father, I have made known to you.

15:16  LA: Non vos me elegistis, sed ego elegi vos, et posui vos ut eatis, et fructum afferatis, et fructus vester maneat: ut quodcumque petieritis Patrem in nomine meo, det vobis.
     EN: You have not chosen me: but I have chosen you; and have appointed you, that you should go and should bring forth fruit; and your fruit should remain: that whatever you shall ask of the Father in my name, he may give it you.

15:17  LA: Hæc mando vobis: ut diligatis invicem.
     EN: These things I command you, that you love one another.

15:18  LA: Si mundus vos odit, scitote quia me priorem vobis odio habuit.
     EN: If the world hate you, know you that it has hated me before you.

15:19  LA: Si de mundo fuissetis, mundus quod suum erat diligeret: quia vero de mundo non estis, sed ego elegi vos de mundo, propterea odit vos mundus.
     EN: If you had been of the world, the world would love its own: but because you are not of the world, but I have chosen you out of the world, therefore the world hates you.

15:20  LA: Mementote sermonis mei, quem ego dixi vobis: non est servus major domino suo. Si me persecuti sunt, et vos persequentur; si sermonem meum servaverunt, et vestrum servabunt.
     EN: Remember my word that I said to you: The servant is not greater than his master. If they have persecuted me, they will also persecute you. If they have kept my word, they will keep yours also.

15:21  LA: Sed hæc omnia facient vobis propter nomen meum: quia nesciunt eum qui misit me.
     EN: But all these things they will do to you for my name’s sake: because they know not him that sent me.

15:22  LA: Si non venissem, et locutus fuissem eis, peccatum non haberent: nunc autem excusationem non habent de peccato suo.
     EN: If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not have sin: but now they have no excuse for their sin.

15:23  LA: Qui me odit, et Patrem meum odit.
     EN: He that hates me hates my Father also.

15:24  LA: Si opera non fecissem in eis quæ nemo alius fecit, peccatum non haberent: nunc autem et viderunt, et oderunt et me, et Patrem meum.
     EN: If I had not done among them the works that no other man has done, they would not have sin: but now they have both seen and hated both me and my Father.

15:25  LA: Sed ut adimpleatur sermo, qui in lege eorum scriptus est: Quia odio habuerunt me gratis.
     EN: But that the word may be fulfilled which is written in their law: they hated me without cause.

15:26  LA: Cum autem venerit Paraclitus, quem ego mittam vobis a Patre, Spiritum veritatis, qui a Patre procedit, ille testimonium perhibebit de me;
     EN: But when the Paraclete comes, whom I will send you from the Father, the Spirit of truth, who proceeds from the Father, he shall give testimony of me.

15:27  LA: et vos testimonium perhibebitis, quia ab initio mecum estis.
     EN: And you shall give testimony, because you are with me from the beginning.

JOHN 16

16:1  LA: Hæc locutus sum vobis, ut non scandalizemini.
     EN: I have spoken these things to you so that you may not stumble.

16:2  LA: Absque synagogis facient vos: sed venit hora, ut omnis qui interficit vos arbitretur obsequium se præstare Deo.
     EN: They will put you out of the synagogues: yes, the hour comes, that whoever kills you will think that he does a service to God.

16:3  LA: Et hæc facient vobis, quia non noverunt Patrem, neque me.
     EN: And these things will they do to you; because they have not known the Father nor me.

16:4  LA: Sed hæc locutus sum vobis, ut cum venerit hora eorum, reminiscamini quia ego dixi vobis.
     EN: But these things I have told you, that when their hour shall come, you may remember that I told you.

16:5  LA: Hæc autem vobis ab initio non dixi, quia vobiscum eram. Et nunc vado ad eum qui misit me; et nemo ex vobis interrogat me: Quo vadis?
     EN: But I told you not these things from the beginning, because I was with you. And now I go to him that sent me, and none of you asks me: Whither do you go?

16:6  LA: sed quia hæc locutus sum vobis, tristitia implevit cor vestrum.
     EN: But because I have spoken these things to you, sorrow has filled your heart.

16:7  LA: Sed ego veritatem dico vobis: expedit vobis ut ego vadam: si enim non abiero, Paraclitus non veniet ad vos; si autem abiero, mittam eum ad vos.
     EN: But I tell you the truth: it is expedient to you that I go. For if I go not, the Paraclete will not come to you: but if I go, I will send him to you.

16:8  LA: Et cum venerit ille, arguet mundum de peccato, et de justitia, et de judicio.
     EN: And when he has come, he will convince the world of sin and of justice and of judgment.

16:9  LA: De peccato quidem, quia non crediderunt in me.
     EN: Of sin: because they believed not in me.

16:10  LA: De justitia vero, quia ad Patrem vado, et jam non videbitis me.
     EN: And of justice: because I go to the Father: and you shall see me no longer.

16:11  LA: De judicio autem, quia princeps hujus mundi jam judicatus est.
     EN: And of judgment: because the prince of this world is already judged.

16:12  LA: Adhuc multa habeo vobis dicere, sed non potestis portare modo.
     EN: I have yet many things to say to you: but you cannot bear them now.

16:13  LA: Cum autem venerit ille Spiritus veritatis, docebit vos omnem veritatem: non enim loquetur a semetipso, sed quæcumque audiet loquetur, et quæ ventura sunt annuntiabit vobis.
     EN: But when he, the Spirit of truth, comes, he will teach you all truth; for he will not speak from himself, but will speak whatever he hears, and will announce to you the things to come.

16:14  LA: Ille me clarificabit, quia de meo accipiet, et annuntiabit vobis.
     EN: He shall glorify me: because he shall receive of mine and shall show it to you.

16:15  LA: Omnia quæcumque habet Pater, mea sunt. Propterea dixi: quia de meo accipiet, et annuntiabit vobis.
     EN: All things whatever the Father has are mine. Therefore I said that he shall receive of mine and show it to you.

16:16  LA: Modicum, et jam non videbitis me; et iterum modicum, et videbitis me: quia vado ad Patrem.
     EN: A little while, and now you shall not see me: and again a little while, and you shall see me: because I go to the Father.

16:17  LA: Dixerunt ergo ex discipulis ejus ad invicem: Quid est hoc quod dicit nobis: Modicum, et non videbitis me; et iterum modicum, et videbitis me, et quia vado ad Patrem?
     EN: Then some of his disciples said one to another: What is this that he says to us: A little while, and you shall not see me: and again a little while, and you shall see me, and, Because I go to the Father?

16:18  LA: Dicebant ergo: Quid est hoc quod dicit: Modicum? nescimus quid loquitur.
     EN: They said therefore: What is this that he says, A little while? We know not what he speaks.

16:19  LA: Cognovit autem Jesus, quia volebant eum interrogare, et dixit eis: De hoc quæritis inter vos quia dixi: Modicum, et non videbitis me; et iterum modicum, et videbitis me.
     EN: And Jesus knew that they wished to ask him. And he said to them: Of this do you inquire among yourselves, because I said: A little while, and you shall not see me; and again a little while, and you shall see me?

16:20  LA: Amen, amen dico vobis: quia plorabitis, et flebitis vos, mundus autem gaudebit; vos autem contristabimini, sed tristitia vestra vertetur in gaudium.
     EN: Amen, amen, I say to you, that you shall lament and weep, but the world shall rejoice: and you shall be made sorrowful, but your sorrow shall be turned into joy.

16:21  LA: Mulier cum parit, tristitiam habet, quia venit hora ejus; cum autem pepererit puerum, jam non meminit pressuræ propter gaudium, quia natus est homo in mundum.
     EN: A woman, when she is in labor, has sorrow, because her hour has come; but when she has brought forth the child, she remembers no more the anguish, for joy that a man is born into the world.

16:22  LA: Et vos igitur nunc quidem tristitiam habetis, iterum autem videbo vos, et gaudebit cor vestrum: et gaudium vestrum nemo tollet a vobis.
     EN: So also you now indeed have sorrow: but I will see you again and your heart shall rejoice. And your joy no one shall take from you.

16:23  LA: Et in illo die me non rogabitis quidquam. Amen, amen dico vobis: si quid petieritis Patrem in nomine meo, dabit vobis.
     EN: And in that day you shall not ask me anything. Amen, amen, I say to you: if you ask the Father anything in my name, he will give it you.

16:24  LA: Usque modo non petistis quidquam in nomine meo: petite, et accipietis, ut gaudium vestrum sit plenum.
     EN: Hitherto, you have not asked anything in my name. Ask, and you shall receive; that your joy may be full.

16:25  LA: Hæc in proverbiis locutus sum vobis. Venit hora cum jam non in proverbiis loquar vobis, sed palam de Patre annuntiabo vobis:
     EN: These things I have spoken to you in proverbs. The hour comes when I will no longer speak to you in proverbs, but will show you plainly of the Father.

16:26  LA: in illo die in nomine meo petetis: et non dico vobis quia ego rogabo Patrem de vobis:
     EN: In that day, you shall ask in my name: and I say not to you that I will ask the Father for you.

16:27  LA: ipse enim Pater amat vos, quia vos me amastis, et credidistis, quia ego a Deo exivi.
     EN: For the Father himself loves you, because you have loved me and have believed that I came out from God.

16:28  LA: Exivi a Patre, et veni in mundum: iterum relinquo mundum, et vado ad Patrem.
     EN: I came forth from the Father and am come into the world: again I leave the world and I go to the Father.

16:29  LA: Dicunt ei discipuli ejus: Ecce nunc palam loqueris, et proverbium nullum dicis:
     EN: His disciples say to him: Behold, now you speak plainly and speak no proverb.

16:30  LA: nunc scimus quia scis omnia, et non opus est tibi ut quis te interroget: in hoc credimus quia a Deo existi.
     EN: Now we know that you know all things and you need not that any man should ask you. By this we believe that you came forth from God.

16:31  LA: Respondit eis Jesus: Modo creditis?
     EN: Jesus answered them: Do you now believe?

16:32  LA: ecce venit hora, et jam venit, ut dispergamini unusquisque in propria, et me solum relinquatis: et non sum solus, quia Pater mecum est.
     EN: Behold, the hour comes, and it is now come, that you shall be scattered every man to his own and shall leave me alone. And yet I am not alone, because the Father is with me.

16:33  LA: Hæc locutus sum vobis, ut in me pacem habeatis. In mundo pressuram habebitis: sed confidite, ego vici mundum.
     EN: These things I have spoken to you, that in me you may have peace. In the world you shall have distress. But have confidence. I have overcome the world.

JOHN 17

17:1  LA: Hæc locutus est Jesus: et sublevatis oculis in cælum, dixit: Pater, venit hora: clarifica Filium tuum, ut Filius tuus clarificet te:
     EN: These things Jesus spoke: and lifting up his eyes to heaven, he said: Father, the hour has come. Glorify your Son, that your Son may glorify you.

17:2  LA: sicut dedisti ei potestatem omnis carnis, ut omne, quod dedisti ei, det eis vitam æternam.
     EN: As you have given him power over all flesh, that he may give eternal life to all whom you have given him.

17:3  LA: Hæc est autem vita æterna: ut cognoscant te, solum Deum verum, et quem misisti Jesum Christum.
     EN: Now this is eternal life: That they may know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom you have sent.

17:4  LA: Ego te clarificavi super terram: opus consummavi, quod dedisti mihi ut faciam:
     EN: I have glorified you on the earth; I have finished the work which you gave me to do.

17:5  LA: et nunc clarifica me tu, Pater, apud temetipsum, claritate quam habui, priusquam mundus esset, apud te.
     EN: And now glorify you me, O Father, with yourself, with the glory which I had, before the world was, with you.

17:6  LA: Manifestavi nomen tuum hominibus, quos dedisti mihi de mundo: tui erant, et mihi eos dedisti: et sermonem tuum servaverunt.
     EN: I have manifested your name to the men whom you have given me out of the world. Yours they were: and to me you gave them. And they have kept your word.

17:7  LA: Nunc cognoverunt quia omnia quæ dedisti mihi, abs te sunt:
     EN: Now they have known that all things which you have given me are from you:

17:8  LA: quia verba quæ dedisti mihi, dedi eis: et ipsi acceperunt, et cognoverunt vere quia a te exivi, et crediderunt quia tu me misisti.
     EN: Because the words which you gave me, I have given to them. And they have received them and have known in very deed that I came out from you: and they have believed that you did send me.

17:9  LA: Ego pro eis rogo; non pro mundo rogo, sed pro his quos dedisti mihi: quia tui sunt:
     EN: I pray for them. I pray not for the world, but for them whom you have given me: because they are yours.

17:10  LA: et mea omnia tua sunt, et tua mea sunt: et clarificatus sum in eis.
     EN: And all my things are yours, and yours are mine: and I am glorified in them.

17:11  LA: Et jam non sum in mundo, et hi in mundo sunt, et ego ad te venio. Pater sancte, serva eos in nomine tuo, quos dedisti mihi: ut sint unum, sicut et nos.
     EN: And now I am not in the world, and these are in the world, and I come to you. Holy Father, keep them in your name whom you have given me: that they may be one, as we also are.

17:12  LA: Cum essem cum eis, ego servabam eos in nomine tuo. Quos dedisti mihi, custodivi: et nemo ex eis periit, nisi filius perditionis, ut Scriptura impleatur.
     EN: While I was with them, I kept them in your name. Those whom you gave me have I kept: and none of them is lost, but the son of perdition: that the scripture may be fulfilled.

17:13  LA: Nunc autem ad te venio: et hæc loquor in mundo, ut habeant gaudium meum impletum in semetipsis.
     EN: And now I come to you: and these things I speak in the world, that they may have my joy filled in themselves.

17:14  LA: Ego dedi eis sermonem tuum, et mundus eos odio habuit, quia non sunt de mundo, sicut et ego non sum de mundo.
     EN: I have given them your word, and the world has hated them: because they are not of the world, as I also am not of the world.

17:15  LA: Non rogo ut tollas eos de mundo, sed ut serves eos a malo.
     EN: I pray not that you should take them out of the world, but that you should keep them from evil.

17:16  LA: De mundo non sunt, sicut et ego non sum de mundo.
     EN: They are not of the world, as I also am not of the world.

17:17  LA: Sanctifica eos in veritate. Sermo tuus veritas est.
     EN: Sanctify them in truth. Your word is truth.

17:18  LA: Sicut tu me misisti in mundum, et ego misi eos in mundum:
     EN: As you have sent me into the world, I also have sent them into the world.

17:19  LA: et pro eis ego sanctifico meipsum: ut sint et ipsi sanctificati in veritate.
     EN: And for them do I sanctify myself, that they also may be sanctified in truth.

17:20  LA: Non pro eis autem rogo tantum, sed et pro eis qui credituri sunt per verbum eorum in me:
     EN: And not for them only do I pray, but for them also who through their word shall believe in me.

17:21  LA: ut omnes unum sint, sicut tu Pater in me, et ego in te, ut et ipsi in nobis unum sint: ut credat mundus, quia tu me misisti.
     EN: That they all may be one, as you, Father, in me, and I in you; that they also may be one in us: that the world may believe that you have sent me.

17:22  LA: Et ego claritatem, quam dedisti mihi, dedi eis: ut sint unum, sicut et nos unum sumus.
     EN: And the glory which you have given me, I have given to them: that, they may be one, as we also are one.

17:23  LA: Ego in eis, et tu in me: ut sint consummati in unum: et cognoscat mundus quia tu me misisti, et dilexisti eos, sicut et me dilexisti.
     EN: I in them, and you in me: that they may be made perfect in one: and the world may know that you have sent me and have loved them, as you have also loved me.

17:24  LA: Pater, quos dedisti mihi, volo ut ubi sum ego, et illi sint mecum: ut videant claritatem meam, quam dedisti mihi: quia dilexisti me ante constitutionem mundi.
     EN: Father, I will that where I am, they also whom you have given me may be with me: that they may see my glory which you have given me, because you have loved me before the creation of the world.

17:25  LA: Pater juste, mundus te non cognovit, ego autem te cognovi: et hi cognoverunt, quia tu me misisti.
     EN: Just Father, the world has not known you: but I have known you. And these have known that you have sent me.

17:26  LA: Et notum feci eis nomen tuum, et notum faciam: ut dilectio, qua dilexisti me, in ipsis sit, et ego in ipsis.
     EN: And I have made known your name to them and will make it known: that the love wherewith you have loved me may be in them, and I in them.

JOHN 18

18:1  LA: Hæc cum dixisset Jesus, egressus est cum discipulis suis trans torrentem Cedron, ubi erat hortus, in quem introivit ipse, et discipuli ejus.
     EN: When Jesus had said these things, he went forth with his disciples over the brook Kidron, where there was a garden, into which he entered with his disciples.

18:2  LA: Sciebat autem et Judas, qui tradebat eum, locum: quia frequenter Jesus convenerat illuc cum discipulis suis.
     EN: And Judas also, who betrayed him, knew the place: because Jesus had often resorted there together with his disciples.

18:3  LA: Judas ergo cum accepisset cohortem, et a pontificibus et pharisæis ministros, venit illuc cum laternis, et facibus, et armis.
     EN: Judas therefore having received a band of soldiers and servants from the chief priests and the Pharisees, comes there with lanterns and torches and weapons.

18:4  LA: Jesus itaque sciens omnia quæ ventura erant super eum, processit, et dixit eis: Quem quæritis?
     EN: Jesus therefore, knowing all things that should come upon him, went forth and said to them: Whom seek you?

18:5  LA: Responderunt ei: Jesum Nazarenum. Dicit eis Jesus: Ego sum. Stabat autem et Judas, qui tradebat eum, cum ipsis.
     EN: They answered him: Jesus of Nazareth. Jesus says to them: I am he. And Judas also, who betrayed him, stood with them.

18:6  LA: Ut ergo dixit eis: Ego sum: abierunt retrorsum, et ceciderunt in terram.
     EN: As soon therefore as he had said to them: I am he; they went backward and fell to the ground.

18:7  LA: Iterum ergo interrogavit eos: Quem quæritis? Illi autem dixerunt: Jesum Nazarenum.
     EN: Again therefore he asked them: Whom seek you? And they said: Jesus of Nazareth.

18:8  LA: Respondit Jesus: Dixi vobis, quia ego sum: si ergo me quæritis, sinite hos abire.
     EN: Jesus answered: I have told you that I am he. If therefore you seek me, let these go their way,

18:9  LA: Ut impleretur sermo, quem dixit: Quia quos dedisti mihi, non perdidi ex eis quemquam.
     EN: That the word might be fulfilled which he said: Of them whom you have given me, I have not lost anyone.

18:10  LA: Simon ergo Petrus habens gladium eduxit eum: et percussit pontificis servum, et abscidit auriculam ejus dexteram. Erat autem nomen servo Malchus.
     EN: Then Simon Peter, having a sword, drew it and struck the servant of the high priest and cut off his right ear. And the name of the servant was Malchus.

18:11  LA: Dixit ergo Jesus Petro: Mitte gladium tuum in vaginam. Calicem, quem dedit mihi Pater, non bibam illum?
     EN: Jesus therefore said to Peter: Put up your sword into the scabbard. The chalice which my father has given me, shall I not drink it?

18:12  LA: Cohors ergo, et tribunus, et ministri Judæorum comprehenderunt Jesum, et ligaverunt eum.
     EN: Then the band and the tribune and the servants of the Jews took Jesus and bound him.

18:13  LA: Et adduxerunt eum ad Annam primum: erat enim socer Caiphæ, qui erat pontifex anni illius.
     EN: And they led him away to Annas first, for he was father-in-law to Caiaphas, who was the high priest of that year.

18:14  LA: Erat autem Caiphas, qui consilium dederat Judæis: Quia expedit unum hominem mori pro populo.
     EN: Now Caiaphas was he who had given the counsel to the Jews: That it was expedient that one man should die for the people.

18:15  LA: Sequebatur autem Jesum Simon Petrus, et alius discipulus. Discipulus autem ille erat notus pontifici, et introivit cum Jesu in atrium pontificis.
     EN: And Simon Peter followed Jesus: and so did another disciple. And that disciple was known to the high priest and went in with Jesus into the court of the high priest.

18:16  LA: Petrus autem stabat ad ostium foris. Exivit ergo discipulus alius, qui erat notus pontifici, et dixit ostiariæ: et introduxit Petrum.
     EN: But Peter stood at the door without. The other disciple therefore, who was known to the high priest, went out and spoke to the portress and brought in Peter.

18:17  LA: Dicit ergo Petro ancilla ostiaria: Numquid et tu ex discipulis es hominis istius? Dicit ille: Non sum.
     EN: The maid therefore that was portress says to Peter: Are not you also one of this man’s disciples? He says: I am not.

18:18  LA: Stabant autem servi et ministri ad prunas, quia frigus erat, et calefaciebant se: erat autem cum eis et Petrus stans, et calefaciens se.
     EN: Now the servants and ministers stood at a fire of coals, because it was cold, and warmed themselves. And with them was Peter also, standing and warming himself.

18:19  LA: Pontifex ergo interrogavit Jesum de discipulis suis, et de doctrina ejus.
     EN: The high priest therefore asked Jesus of his disciples and of his doctrine.

18:20  LA: Respondit ei Jesus: Ego palam locutus sum mundo: ego semper docui in synagoga, et in templo, quo omnes Judæi conveniunt, et in occulto locutus sum nihil.
     EN: Jesus answered him: I have spoken openly to the world. I have always taught in the synagogue and in the temple, where all the Jews resort: and in secret I have spoken nothing.

18:21  LA: Quid me interrogas? interroga eos qui audierunt quid locutus sim ipsis: ecce hi sciunt quæ dixerim ego.
     EN: Why ask you me? Ask them who have heard what I have spoken to them. Behold they know what things I have said.

18:22  LA: Hæc autem cum dixisset, unus assistens ministrorum dedit alapam Jesu, dicens: Sic respondes pontifici?
     EN: And when he had said these things, one of the servants standing by gave Jesus a blow, saying: Answer you the high priest so?

18:23  LA: Respondit ei Jesus: Si male locutus sum, testimonium perhibe de malo: si autem bene, quid me cædis?
     EN: Jesus answered him: If I have spoken evil, give testimony of the evil; but if well, why strike you me?

18:24  LA: Et misit eum Annas ligatum ad Caipham pontificem.
     EN: And Annas sent him bound to Caiaphas the high priest.

18:25  LA: Erat autem Simon Petrus stans, et calefaciens se. Dixerunt ergo ei: Numquid et tu ex discipulis ejus es? Negavit ille, et dixit: Non sum.
     EN: And Simon Peter was standing and warming himself. They said therefore to him: Are not you also one of his disciples? He denied it and said: I am not.

18:26  LA: Dicit ei unus ex servis pontificis, cognatus ejus, cujus abscidit Petrus auriculam: Nonne ego te vidi in horto cum illo?
     EN: One of the servants of the high priest (a kinsman to him whose ear Peter cut off) says to him: Did not I see you in the garden with him?

18:27  LA: Iterum ergo negavit Petrus: et statim gallus cantavit.
     EN: Again therefore Peter denied: and immediately the rooster crew.

18:28  LA: Adducunt ergo Jesum a Caipha in prætorium. Erat autem mane: et ipsi non introierunt in prætorium, ut non contaminarentur, sed ut manducarent Pascha.
     EN: Then they led Jesus from Caiaphas to the governor’s hall. And it was morning: and they went not into the hall, that they might not be defiled, but that they might eat the pasch.

18:29  LA: Exivit ergo Pilatus ad eos foras, et dixit: Quam accusationem affertis adversus hominem hunc?
     EN: Pilate therefore went out to them, and said: What accusation bring you against this man?

18:30  LA: Responderunt, et dixerunt ei: Si non esset hic malefactor, non tibi tradidissemus eum.
     EN: They answered him: If he were not a malefactor, we would not have delivered him up to you.

18:31  LA: Dixit ergo eis Pilatus: Accipite eum vos, et secundum legem vestram judicate eum. Dixerunt ergo ei Judæi: Nobis non licet interficere quemquam.
     EN: Pilate therefore said to them: Take him you, and judge him according to your law. The Jews therefore said to him: It is not lawful for us to put any man to death.

18:32  LA: Ut sermo Jesu impleretur, quem dixit, significans qua morte esset moriturus.
     EN: That the word of Jesus might be fulfilled, which he said, signifying what death he should die.

18:33  LA: Introivit ergo iterum in prætorium Pilatus: et vocavit Jesum, et dixit ei: Tu es rex Judæorum?
     EN: Pilate therefore went into the hall again and called Jesus and said to him: Are you the king of the Jews?

18:34  LA: Respondit Jesus: A temetipso hoc dicis, an alii dixerunt tibi de me?
     EN: Jesus answered: Say you this thing of yourself, or have others told it you of me?

18:35  LA: Respondit Pilatus: Numquid ego Judæus sum? gens tua et pontifices tradiderunt te mihi: quid fecisti?
     EN: Pilate answered: Am I a Jew? Your own nation and the chief priests have delivered you up to me. What have you done?

18:36  LA: Respondit Jesus: Regnum meum non est de hoc mundo. Si ex hoc mundo esset regnum meum, ministri mei utique decertarent ut non traderer Judæis: nunc autem regnum meum non est hinc.
     EN: Jesus answered: My kingdom is not of this world. If my kingdom were of this world, my servants would certainly strive that I should not be delivered to the Jews: but now my kingdom is not from here.

18:37  LA: Dixit itaque ei Pilatus: Ergo rex es tu? Respondit Jesus: Tu dicis quia rex sum ego. Ego in hoc natus sum, et ad hoc veni in mundum, ut testimonium perhibeam veritati: omnis qui est ex veritate, audit vocem meam.
     EN: Pilate therefore said to him: Are you a king then? Jesus answered: You say that I am a king. For this was I born, and for this came I into the world; that I should give testimony to the truth. Everyone that is of the truth hears my voice.

18:38  LA: Dicit ei Pilatus: Quid est veritas? Et cum hoc dixisset, iterum exivit ad Judæos, et dicit eis: Ego nullam invenio in eo causam.
     EN: Pilate says to him: What is truth? And when he said this, he went out again to the Jews and says to them: I find no cause in him.

18:39  LA: Est autem consuetudo vobis ut unum dimittam vobis in Pascha: vultis ergo dimittam vobis regem Judæorum?
     EN: But you have a custom that I should release one to you at the Pasch. Will you, therefore, that I release to you the king of the Jews?

18:40  LA: Clamaverunt ergo rursum omnes, dicentes: Non hunc, sed Barabbam. Erat autem Barabbas latro.
     EN: Then cried they all again, saying: Not this man, but Barabbas. Now Barabbas was a robber.

JOHN 19

19:1  LA: Tunc ergo apprehendit Pilatus Jesum, et flagellavit.
     EN: Then therefore Pilate took Jesus and flogged him.

19:2  LA: Et milites plectentes coronam de spinis, imposuerunt capiti ejus: et veste purpurea circumdederunt eum.
     EN: And the soldiers platting a crown of thorns, put it upon his head: and they put on him a purple garment.

19:3  LA: Et veniebant ad eum, et dicebant: Ave, rex Judæorum: et dabant ei alapas.
     EN: And they came to him and said: Hail, king of the Jews. And they gave him blows.

19:4  LA: Exivit ergo iterum Pilatus foras, et dicit eis: Ecce adduco vobis eum foras, ut cognoscatis quia nullam invenio in eo causam.
     EN: Pilate therefore went forth again and says to them: Behold, I bring him forth to you, that you may know that I find no cause in him.

19:5  LA: (Exivit ergo Jesus portans coronam spineam, et purpureum vestimentum.) Et dicit eis: Ecce homo.
     EN: (Jesus therefore came forth, bearing the crown of thorns and the purple garment.) And he says to them: Behold the Man.

19:6  LA: Cum ergo vidissent eum pontifices et ministri, clamabant, dicentes: Crucifige, crucifige eum. Dicit eis Pilatus: Accipite eum vos, et crucifigite: ego enim non invenio in eo causam.
     EN: When the chief priests, therefore, and the servants had seen him, they cried out, saying: Crucify him, Crucify him. Pilate says to them: Take him you, and crucify him: for I find no cause in him.

19:7  LA: Responderunt ei Judæi: Nos legem habemus, et secundum legem debet mori, quia Filium Dei se fecit.
     EN: The Jews answered him: We have a law; and according to the law he ought to die, because he made himself the Son of God.

19:8  LA: Cum ergo audisset Pilatus hunc sermonem, magis timuit.
     EN: When Pilate therefore had heard this saying, he feared the more.

19:9  LA: Et ingressus est prætorium iterum: et dixit ad Jesum: Unde es tu? Jesus autem responsum non dedit ei.
     EN: And he entered into the hall again; and he said to Jesus: From where are you? But Jesus gave him no answer.

19:10  LA: Dicit ergo ei Pilatus: Mihi non loqueris? nescis quia potestatem habeo crucifigere te, et potestatem habeo dimittere te?
     EN: Pilate therefore says to him: Speak you not to me? Do you not know that I have power to crucify you, and I have power to release you?

19:11  LA: Respondit Jesus: Non haberes potestatem adversum me ullam, nisi tibi datum esset desuper. Propterea qui me tradidit tibi, majus peccatum habet.
     EN: Jesus answered: You would have no authority against me unless it had been given you from above. Therefore the one who handed me over to you has the greater sin.

19:12  LA: Et exinde quærebat Pilatus dimittere eum. Judæi autem clamabant dicentes: Si hunc dimittis, non es amicus Cæsaris. Omnis enim qui se regem facit, contradicit Cæsari.
     EN: And from then on Pilate sought to release him. But the Jews cried out, saying: If you release this man, you are not Caesar’s friend. For whoever makes himself a king speaks against Caesar.

19:13  LA: Pilatus autem cum audisset hos sermones, adduxit foras Jesum: et sedit pro tribunali, in loco qui dicitur Lithostrotos, hebraice autem Gabbatha.
     EN: Now when Pilate had heard these words, he brought Jesus forth and sat down in the judgment seat, in the place that is called Lithostrotos, and in Hebrew Gabbatha.

19:14  LA: Erat autem parasceve Paschæ, hora quasi sexta, et dicit Judæis: Ecce rex vester.
     EN: And it was the parasceve of the pasch, about the sixth hour: and he says to the Jews: Behold your king.

19:15  LA: Illi autem clamabant: Tolle, tolle, crucifige eum. Dicit eis Pilatus: Regem vestrum crucifigam? Responderunt pontifices: Non habemus regem, nisi Cæsarem.
     EN: But they cried out: Away with him: Away with him: Crucify him. Pilate says to them: shall I crucify your king? The chief priests answered: We have no king but Caesar.

19:16  LA: Tunc ergo tradidit eis illum ut crucifigeretur. Susceperunt autem Jesum, et eduxerunt.
     EN: Then therefore he delivered him to them to be crucified. And they took Jesus and led him forth.

19:17  LA: Et bajulans sibi crucem exivit in eum, qui dicitur Calvariæ locum, hebraice autem Golgotha:
     EN: And bearing his own cross, he went forth to the place which is called Calvary, but in Hebrew Golgotha.

19:18  LA: ubi crucifixerunt eum, et cum eo alios duos hinc et hinc, medium autem Jesum.
     EN: Where they crucified him, and with him two others, one on each side, and Jesus in the midst.

19:19  LA: Scripsit autem et titulum Pilatus, et posuit super crucem. Erat autem scriptum: Jesus Nazarenus, Rex Judæorum.
     EN: And Pilate wrote a title also: and he put it upon the cross. And the writing was: JESUS OF NAZARETH, THE KING OF THE JEWS.

19:20  LA: Hunc ergo titulum multi Judæorum legerunt: quia prope civitatem erat locus, ubi crucifixus est Jesus, et erat scriptum hebraice, græce, et latine.
     EN: This title therefore many of the Jews did read: because the place where Jesus was crucified was near to the city. And it was written in Hebrew, in Greek, and in Latin.

19:21  LA: Dicebant ergo Pilato pontifices Judæorum: Noli scribere: Rex Judæorum: sed quia ipse dixit: Rex sum Judæorum.
     EN: Then the chief priests of the Jews said to Pilate: Write not: The King of the Jews. But that he said: I am the King of the Jews.

19:22  LA: Respondit Pilatus: Quod scripsi, scripsi.
     EN: Pilate answered: What I have written, I have written.

19:23  LA: Milites ergo cum crucifixissent eum, acceperunt vestimenta ejus (et fecerunt quatuor partes, unicuique militi partem) et tunicam. Erat autem tunica inconsutilis, desuper contexta per totum.
     EN: The soldiers therefore, when they had crucified him, took his garments, (and they made four parts, to every soldier a part) and also his tunic. Now the tunic was without seam, woven from the top throughout.

19:24  LA: Dixerunt ergo ad invicem: Non scindamus eam, sed sortiamur de illa cujus sit. Ut Scriptura impleretur, dicens: Partiti sunt vestimenta mea sibi: et in vestem meam miserunt sortem. Et milites quidem hæc fecerunt.
     EN: They said then one to another: Let us not cut it but let us cast lots for it, whose it shall be; that the scripture might be fulfilled, saying: They have parted my garments among them, and upon my vesture they have cast lots. And the soldiers indeed did these things.

19:25  LA: Stabant autem juxta crucem Jesu mater ejus, et soror matris ejus, Maria Cleophæ, et Maria Magdalene.
     EN: Now there stood by the cross of Jesus, his mother and his mother’s sister, Mary of Clopas, and Mary Magdalen.

19:26  LA: Cum vidisset ergo Jesus matrem, et discipulum stantem, quem diligebat, dicit matri suæ: Mulier, ecce filius tuus.
     EN: When Jesus saw his mother and the disciple whom he loved standing there, he said to his mother: Woman, behold your son.

19:27  LA: Deinde dicit discipulo: Ecce mater tua. Et ex illa hora accepit eam discipulus in sua.
     EN: Then he said to the disciple: Behold your mother. And from that hour the disciple took her into his own home.

19:28  LA: Postea sciens Jesus quia omnia consummata sunt, ut consummaretur Scriptura, dixit: Sitio.
     EN: After this, Jesus knew that everything had been accomplished. To fulfill Scripture, he said, “I thirst.”

19:29  LA: Vas ergo erat positum aceto plenum. Illi autem spongiam plenam aceto, hyssopo circumponentes, obtulerunt ori ejus.
     EN: Now there was a vessel set there, full of vinegar. And they, putting a sponge full of vinegar about hyssop, put it to his mouth.

19:30  LA: Cum ergo accepisset Jesus acetum, dixit: Consummatum est. Et inclinato capite tradidit spiritum.
     EN: When Jesus had received the vinegar, he said: It is consummated. And bowing his head, he handed over his spirit.

19:31  LA: Judæi ergo (quoniam parasceve erat) ut non remanerent in cruce corpora sabbato (erat enim magnus dies ille sabbati), rogaverunt Pilatum ut frangerentur eorum crura, et tollerentur.
     EN: Then the Jews (because it was the parasceve), that the bodies might not remain upon the cross on the sabbath day (for that was a great sabbath day), begged Pilate that their legs might be broken: and that they might be taken away.

19:32  LA: Venerunt ergo milites: et primi quidem fregerunt crura, et alterius, qui crucifixus est cum eo.
     EN: The soldiers therefore came: and they broke the legs of the first, and of the other that was crucified with him.

19:33  LA: Ad Jesum autem cum venissent, ut viderunt eum jam mortuum, non fregerunt ejus crura,
     EN: But after they had come to Jesus, when they saw that he was already dead, they did not break his legs.

19:34  LA: sed unus militum lancea latus ejus aperuit, et continuo exivit sanguis et aqua.
     EN: But one of the soldiers opened his side with a spear, and immediately blood and water came out.

19:35  LA: Et qui vidit, testimonium perhibuit: et verum est testimonium ejus. Et ille scit quia vera dicit: ut et vos credatis.
     EN: And he that saw it has given testimony: and his testimony is true. And he knows that he says true: that you also may believe.

19:36  LA: Facta sunt enim hæc ut Scriptura impleretur: Os non comminuetis ex eo.
     EN: For these things were done that the scripture might be fulfilled: You shall not break a bone of him.

19:37  LA: Et iterum alia Scriptura dicit: Videbunt in quem transfixerunt.
     EN: And again another scripture says: They shall look on him whom they pierced.

19:38  LA: Post hæc autem rogavit Pilatum Joseph ab Arimathæa (eo quod esset discipulus Jesu, occultus autem propter metum Judæorum), ut tolleret corpus Jesu. Et permisit Pilatus. Venit ergo, et tulit corpus Jesu.
     EN: And after these things, Joseph of Arimathea (because he was a disciple of Jesus, but secretly for fear of the Jews), begged Pilate that he might take away the body of Jesus. And Pilate gave leave. He came therefore and took away the body of Jesus.

19:39  LA: Venit autem et Nicodemus, qui venerat ad Jesum nocte primum, ferens mixturam myrrhæ et aloës, quasi libras centum.
     EN: Nicodemus, who had first come to Jesus by night, also came, bringing a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about a hundred Roman pounds.

19:40  LA: Acceperunt ergo corpus Jesu, et ligaverunt illud linteis cum aromatibus, sicut mos est Judæis sepelire.
     EN: They took therefore the body of Jesus and bound it in linen cloths, with the spices, as the manner of the Jews is to bury.

19:41  LA: Erat autem in loco, ubi crucifixus est, hortus: et in horto monumentum novum, in quo nondum quisquam positus erat.
     EN: Now there was in the place where he was crucified a garden: and in the garden a new tomb, wherein no one yet had been laid.

19:42  LA: Ibi ergo propter parasceven Judæorum, quia juxta erat monumentum, posuerunt Jesum.
     EN: There, therefore, because of the parasceve of the Jews, they laid Jesus: because the tomb was near.

JOHN 20

20:1  LA: Una autem sabbati, Maria Magdalene venit mane, cum adhuc tenebræ essent, ad monumentum: et vidit lapidem sublatum a monumento.
     EN: On the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene came early to the tomb, while it was still dark, and saw that the stone had been taken away from the tomb.

20:2  LA: Cucurrit ergo, et venit ad Simonem Petrum, et ad alium discipulum, quem amabat Jesus, et dicit illis: Tulerunt Dominum de monumento, et nescimus ubi posuerunt eum.
     EN: So she ran and came to Simon Peter and the other disciple whom Jesus loved, and said to them: They have taken the Lord out of the tomb, and we do not know where they have laid him.

20:3  LA: Exiit ergo Petrus, et ille alius discipulus, et venerunt ad monumentum.
     EN: Peter and the other disciple went out and came to the tomb.

20:4  LA: Currebant autem duo simul, et ille alius discipulus præcucurrit citius Petro, et venit primus ad monumentum.
     EN: The two were running together, but the other disciple ran ahead faster than Peter and came first to the tomb.

20:5  LA: Et cum se inclinasset, vidit posita linteamina: non tamen introivit.
     EN: Bending down, he saw the linen cloths lying there, but did not go in.

20:6  LA: Venit ergo Simon Petrus sequens eum, et introivit in monumentum, et vidit linteamina posita,
     EN: Then Simon Peter came, following him, and entered the tomb; and he saw the linen cloths lying there,

20:7  LA: et sudarium, quod fuerat super caput ejus, non cum linteaminibus positum, sed separatim involutum in unum locum.
     EN: and the cloth that had been upon his head, not lying with the linen cloths, but rolled up separately in one place.

20:8  LA: Tunc ergo introivit et ille discipulus qui venerat primus ad monumentum: et vidit, et credidit:
     EN: Then that disciple, who had come first to the tomb, also entered; and he saw and believed.

20:9  LA: nondum enim sciebant Scripturam, quia oportebat eum a mortuis resurgere.
     EN: For they did not yet understand the Scripture, that he must rise from the dead.

20:10  LA: Abierunt ergo iterum discipuli ad semetipsos.
     EN: Then the disciples returned to their own.

20:11  LA: Maria autem stabat ad monumentum foris, plorans. Dum ergo fleret, inclinavit se, et prospexit in monumentum:
     EN: But Mary stood outside at the tomb, weeping. As she wept, she bent down and looked into the tomb,

20:12  LA: et vidit duos angelos in albis sedentes, unum ad caput, et unum ad pedes, ubi positum fuerat corpus Jesu.
     EN: and saw two angels in white sitting where Jesus’ body had been laid, one at the head and one at the feet.

20:13  LA: Dicunt ei illi: Mulier, quid ploras? Dicit eis: Quia tulerunt Dominum meum: et nescio ubi posuerunt eum.
     EN: They said to her: Woman, why are you weeping? She said to them: Because they have taken away my Lord, and I do not know where they have laid him.

20:14  LA: Hæc cum dixisset, conversa est retrorsum, et vidit Jesum stantem: et non sciebat quia Jesus est.
     EN: After saying this, she turned around and saw Jesus standing there, but did not know that it was Jesus.

20:15  LA: Dicit ei Jesus: Mulier, quid ploras? quem quæris? Illa existimans quia hortulanus esset, dicit ei: Domine, si tu sustulisti eum, dicito mihi ubi posuisti eum, et ego eum tollam.
     EN: Jesus said to her: Woman, why are you weeping? Whom do you seek? Thinking that he was the gardener, she said to him: Sir, if you have carried him away, tell me where you have laid him, and I will take him.

20:16  LA: Dicit ei Jesus: Maria. Conversa illa, dicit ei: Rabboni (quod dicitur Magister).
     EN: Jesus said to her: Mary. She turned and said to him: Rabboni—which means Teacher.

20:17  LA: Dicit ei Jesus: Noli me tangere, nondum enim ascendi ad Patrem meum: vade autem ad fratres meos, et dic eis: Ascendo ad Patrem meum, et Patrem vestrum, Deum meum, et Deum vestrum.
     EN: Jesus said to her: Do not touch me, for I have not yet ascended to my Father. But go to my brothers and tell them: I ascend to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God.

20:18  LA: Venit Maria Magdalene annuntians discipulis: Quia vidi Dominum, et hæc dixit mihi.
     EN: Mary Magdalene came and announced to the disciples: I have seen the Lord, and he said these things to me.

20:19  LA: Cum ergo sero esset die illo, una sabbatorum, et fores essent clausæ, ubi erant discipuli congregati propter metum Judæorum: venit Jesus, et stetit in medio, et dixit eis: Pax vobis.
     EN: When evening came on that day, the first day of the week, and the doors where the disciples were gathered were shut for fear of the Jews, Jesus came and stood in the midst and said to them: Peace be with you.

20:20  LA: Et cum hoc dixisset, ostendit eis manus et latus. Gavisi sunt ergo discipuli, viso Domino.
     EN: After saying this, he showed them his hands and side. Then the disciples rejoiced at seeing the Lord.

20:21  LA: Dixit ergo eis iterum: Pax vobis. Sicut misit me Pater, et ego mitto vos.
     EN: He said to them again: Peace be with you. As the Father has sent me, I also send you.

20:22  LA: Hæc cum dixisset, insufflavit, et dixit eis: Accipite Spiritum Sanctum:
     EN: Having said this, he breathed upon them and said: Receive the Holy Spirit.

20:23  LA: quorum remiseritis peccata, remittuntur eis: et quorum retinueritis, retenta sunt.
     EN: Whose sins you forgive are forgiven them, and whose sins you retain are retained.

20:24  LA: Thomas autem unus ex duodecim, qui dicitur Didymus, non erat cum eis quando venit Jesus.
     EN: Thomas, one of the twelve, who is called Didymus, was not with them when Jesus came.

20:25  LA: Dixerunt ergo ei alii discipuli: Vidimus Dominum. Ille autem dixit eis: Nisi videro in manibus ejus fixuram clavorum, et mittam digitum meum in locum clavorum, et mittam manum meam in latus ejus, non credam.
     EN: The other disciples therefore said to him: We have seen the Lord. But he said to them: Unless I see the mark of the nails in his hands, put my finger into the place of the nails, and put my hand into his side, I will not believe.

20:26  LA: Et post dies octo, iterum erant discipuli ejus intus, et Thomas cum eis. Venit Jesus januis clausis, et stetit in medio, et dixit: Pax vobis.
     EN: After eight days his disciples were again inside, and Thomas was with them. Jesus came while the doors were shut, stood in the midst, and said: Peace be with you.

20:27  LA: Deinde dicit Thomæ: Infer digitum tuum huc, et vide manus meas, et affer manum tuam, et mitte in latus meum: et noli esse incredulus, sed fidelis.
     EN: Then he said to Thomas: Bring your finger here, and see my hands; bring your hand and put it into my side. Do not be unbelieving, but faithful.

20:28  LA: Respondit Thomas, et dixit ei: Dominus meus et Deus meus.
     EN: Thomas answered and said to him: My Lord and my God.

20:29  LA: Dixit ei Jesus: Quia vidisti me, Thoma, credidisti: beati qui non viderunt, et crediderunt.
     EN: Jesus said to him: Because you have seen me, Thomas, you have believed. Blessed are those who have not seen and have believed.

20:30  LA: Multa quidem et alia signa fecit Jesus in conspectu discipulorum suorum, quæ non sunt scripta in libro hoc.
     EN: Jesus also did many other signs in the sight of his disciples that are not written in this book.

20:31  LA: Hæc autem scripta sunt ut credatis, quia Jesus est Christus Filius Dei: et ut credentes, vitam habeatis in nomine ejus.
     EN: But these are written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that believing you may have life in his name.

JOHN 21

21:1  LA: Postea manifestavit se iterum Jesus discipulis ad mare Tiberiadis. Manifestavit autem sic:
     EN: After this, Jesus showed himself again to the disciples at the sea of Tiberias. And he showed himself after this manner.

21:2  LA: erant simul Simon Petrus, et Thomas, qui dicitur Didymus, et Nathanaël, qui erat a Cana Galilææ, et filii Zebedæi, et alii ex discipulis ejus duo.
     EN: There were together: Simon Peter and Thomas, who is called Didymus, and Nathanael, who was of Cana of Galilee, and the sons of Zebedee and two others of his disciples.

21:3  LA: Dicit eis Simon Petrus: Vado piscari. Dicunt ei: Venimus et nos tecum. Et exierunt, et ascenderunt in navim: et illa nocte nihil prendiderunt.
     EN: Simon Peter says to them: I go a fishing. They say to him: We also come with you. And they went forth and entered into the boat: and that night they caught nothing.

21:4  LA: Mane autem facto stetit Jesus in littore: non tamen cognoverunt discipuli quia Jesus est.
     EN: But when the morning had come, Jesus stood on the shore: yet the disciples knew not that it was Jesus.

21:5  LA: Dixit ergo eis Jesus: Pueri, numquid pulmentarium habetis? Responderunt ei: Non.
     EN: Jesus said to them: Children, do you have anything to eat with bread? They answered him: No.

21:6  LA: Dicit eis: Mittite in dexteram navigii rete, et invenietis. Miserunt ergo: et jam non valebant illud trahere præ multitudine piscium.
     EN: He says to them: Cast the net on the right side of the boat; and you shall find. They cast therefore: and now they were not able to draw it, for the multitude of fishes.

21:7  LA: Dixit ergo discipulus ille, quem diligebat Jesus, Petro: Dominus est. Simon Petrus cum audisset quia Dominus est, tunica succinxit se (erat enim nudus) et misit se in mare.
     EN: That disciple therefore whom Jesus loved said to Peter: It is the Lord. Simon Peter, when he heard that it was the Lord, girt his tunic about him (for he was naked) and cast himself into the sea.

21:8  LA: Alii autem discipuli navigio venerunt (non enim longe erant a terra, sed quasi cubitis ducentis), trahentes rete piscium.
     EN: But the other disciples came in the boat (for they were not far from the land, but as it were two hundred cubits) dragging the net with fishes.

21:9  LA: Ut ergo descenderunt in terram, viderunt prunas positas, et piscem superpositum, et panem.
     EN: As soon then as they came to land they saw hot coals lying, and a fish laid thereon, and bread.

21:10  LA: Dicit eis Jesus: Afferte de piscibus, quos prendidistis nunc.
     EN: Jesus says to them: Bring here of the fishes which you have now caught.

21:11  LA: Ascendit Simon Petrus et traxit rete in terram, plenum magnis piscibus centum quinquaginta tribus. Et cum tanti essent, non est scissum rete.
     EN: Simon Peter went up and drew the net to land, full of great fishes, one hundred and fifty-three. And although there were so many, the net was not broken.

21:12  LA: Dicit eis Jesus: Venite, prandete. Et nemo audebat discumbentium interrogare eum: Tu quis es? scientes, quia Dominus est.
     EN: Jesus says to them: Come and dine. And none of them who were at table, dared ask him: Who are you? Knowing that it was the Lord.

21:13  LA: Et venit Jesus, et accipit panem, et dat eis, et piscem similiter.
     EN: And Jesus comes and takes bread and gives them: and fish in like manner.

21:14  LA: Hoc jam tertio manifestatus est Jesus discipulis suis cum resurrexisset a mortuis.
     EN: This is now the third time that Jesus was manifested to his disciples, after he had risen from the dead.

21:15  LA: Cum ergo prandissent, dicit Simoni Petro Jesus: Simon Joannis, diligis me plus his? Dicit ei: Etiam Domine, tu scis quia amo te. Dicit ei: Pasce agnos meos.
     EN: When they had eaten, Jesus said to Simon Peter: Simon, son of John, do you love me more than these? He said to him: Yes, Lord; you know that I love you. He said to him: Feed my lambs.

21:16  LA: Dicit ei iterum: Simon Joannis, diligis me? Ait illi: Etiam Domine, tu scis quia amo te. Dicit ei: Pasce agnos meos.
     EN: He said to him a second time: Simon, son of John, do you love me? He said to him: Yes, Lord; you know that I love you. He said to him: Feed my lambs.

21:17  LA: Dicit ei tertio: Simon Joannis, amas me? Contristatus est Petrus, quia dixit ei tertio: Amas me? et dixit ei: Domine, tu omnia nosti, tu scis quia amo te. Dixit ei: Pasce oves meas.
     EN: He said to him a third time: Simon, son of John, do you love me? Peter was grieved because he said to him a third time, Do you love me? And he said to him: Lord, you know all things; you know that I love you. Jesus said to him: Feed my sheep.

21:18  LA: Amen, amen dico tibi: cum esses junior, cingebas te, et ambulabas ubi volebas: cum autem senueris, extendes manus tuas, et alius te cinget, et ducet quo tu non vis.
     EN: Amen, amen, I say to you: when you were younger, you girded yourself and walked where you wished; but when you grow old, you will stretch out your hands, and another will gird you and lead you where you do not wish.

21:19  LA: Hoc autem dixit significans qua morte clarificaturus esset Deum. Et cum hoc dixisset, dicit ei: Sequere me.
     EN: He said this to signify by what death he would glorify God. After saying this, he said to him: Follow me.

21:20  LA: Conversus Petrus vidit illum discipulum, quem diligebat Jesus, sequentem, qui et recubuit in cœna super pectus ejus, et dixit: Domine, quis est qui tradet te?
     EN: Peter turning about, saw that disciple whom Jesus loved following, who also leaned on his breast at supper and said: Lord, who is he that shall betray you?

21:21  LA: Hunc ergo cum vidisset Petrus, dixit Jesu: Domine, hic autem quid?
     EN: Him therefore when Peter had seen, he says to Jesus: Lord, and what shall this man do?

21:22  LA: Dicit ei Jesus: Sic eum volo manere donec veniam, quid ad te? tu me sequere.
     EN: Jesus said to him: Thus I will that he remain until I come; what is that to you? You follow me.

21:23  LA: Exiit ergo sermo iste inter fratres quia discipulus ille non moritur. Et non dixit ei Jesus: Non moritur, sed: Sic eum volo manere donec veniam, quid ad te?
     EN: So this saying spread among the brothers, that this disciple would not die. Yet Jesus did not say to him, He will not die, but: Thus I will that he remain until I come; what is that to you?

21:24  LA: Hic est discipulus ille qui testimonium perhibet de his, et scripsit hæc: et scimus quia verum est testimonium ejus.
     EN: This is the disciple who bears witness to these things and wrote them, and we know that his testimony is true.

21:25  LA: Sunt autem et alia multa quæ fecit Jesus: quæ si scribantur per singula, nec ipsum arbitror mundum capere posse eos, qui scribendi sunt, libros.
     EN: There are also many other things that Jesus did. If each were written down, I think that even the world itself could not contain the books that would be written.
