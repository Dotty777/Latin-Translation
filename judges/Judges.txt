JUDGES 1

1:1  LA: Post mortem Josue, consuluerunt filii Israël Dominum, dicentes : Quis ascendet ante nos contra Chananæum, et erit dux belli ?
     EN: After Joshua's death, the children of Israel consulted the Lord, saying, “Who will go up ahead of us against the Canaanites and lead the war?”

1:2  LA: Dixitque Dominus : Judas ascendet : ecce tradidi terram in manus ejus.
     EN: The Lord said, “Judah will go up. See, I have delivered the land into his hands.”

1:3  LA: Et ait Judas Simeoni fratri suo : Ascende mecum in sortem meam, et pugna contra Chananæum, ut et ego pergam tecum in sortem tuam. Et abiit cum eo Simeon.
     EN: Judah said to his brother Simeon, “Come up with me into my allotted territory and fight against the Canaanites, so that I too may go with you into your allotted territory.” And Simeon went with him.

1:4  LA: Ascenditque Judas, et tradidit Dominus Chananæum ac Pherezæum in manus eorum : et percusserunt in Bezec decem millia virorum.
     EN: Judah went up, and the Lord delivered the Canaanites and Perizzites into their hands. They struck down ten thousand men at Bezek.

1:5  LA: Inveneruntque Adonibezec in Bezec, et pugnaverunt contra eum, ac percusserunt Chananæum et Pherezæum.
     EN: They found Adoni-bezek at Bezek, fought against him, and struck down the Canaanites and Perizzites.

1:6  LA: Fugit autem Adonibezec : quem persecuti comprehenderunt, cæsis summitatibus manuum ejus ac pedum.
     EN: Adoni-bezek fled, but they pursued and captured him, cutting off the extremities of his hands and feet.

1:7  LA: Dixitque Adonibezec : Septuaginta reges amputatis manuum ac pedum summitatibus colligebant sub mensa mea ciborum reliquias : sicut feci, ita reddidit mihi Deus. Adduxeruntque eum in Jerusalem, et ibi mortuus est.
     EN: Adoni-bezek said, “Seventy kings, with the extremities of their hands and feet cut off, used to gather scraps of food beneath my table. As I have done, so God has repaid me.” They brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.

1:8  LA: Oppugnantes ergo filii Juda Jerusalem, ceperunt eam, et percusserunt in ore gladii, tradentes cunctam incendio civitatem.
     EN: The children of Judah attacked Jerusalem, captured it, and struck it with the edge of the sword, surrendering the whole city to fire.

1:9  LA: Et postea descendentes pugnaverunt contra Chananæum, qui habitabat in montanis, et ad meridiem, et in campestribus.
     EN: Afterward they went down and fought against the Canaanites who lived in the hill country, in the south, and in the plains.

1:10  LA: Pergensque Judas contra Chananæum, qui habitabat in Hebron (cujus nomen fuit antiquitus Cariath Arbe), percussit Sesai, et Ahiman, et Tholmai :
      EN: Judah went against the Canaanites living in Hebron, whose former name was Kiriath-arba, and struck down Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.

1:11  LA: atque inde profectus abiit ad habitatores Dabir, cujus nomen vetus erat Cariath Sepher, id est, civitas litterarum.
      EN: Setting out from there, he went against the inhabitants of Debir, whose old name was Kiriath-sepher, that is, City of Letters.

1:12  LA: Dixitque Caleb : Qui percusserit Cariath Sepher, et vastaverit eam, dabo ei Axam filiam meam uxorem.
      EN: Caleb said, “Whoever strikes Kiriath-sepher and lays it waste, I will give him my daughter Achsah as his wife.”

1:13  LA: Cumque cepisset eam Othoniel filius Cenez frater Caleb minor, dedit ei Axam filiam suam conjugem.
      EN: When Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother, had captured it, Caleb gave him his daughter Achsah as his wife.

1:14  LA: Quam pergentem in itinere monuit vir suus ut peteret a patre suo agrum. Quæ cum suspirasset sedens in asino, dixit ei Caleb : Quid habes ?
      EN: As she was traveling on her way, her husband urged her to ask her father for a field. When she sighed as she sat on her donkey, Caleb said to her, “What is troubling you?”

1:15  LA: At illa respondit : Da mihi benedictionem, quia terram arentem dedisti mihi : da et irriguam aquis. Dedit ergo ei Caleb irriguum superius, et irriguum inferius.
      EN: She answered, “Give me a blessing. Since you have given me dry land, give me land watered by streams as well.” So Caleb gave her the upper watered land and the lower watered land.

1:16  LA: Filii autem Cinæi cognati Moysi ascenderunt de civitate palmarum cum filiis Juda, in desertum sortis ejus, quod est ad meridiem Arad, et habitaverunt cum eo.
      EN: The descendants of the Kenite, Moses' relative, went up from the City of Palms with the children of Judah into the wilderness of his allotted territory, south of Arad, and lived with him.

1:17  LA: Abiit autem Judas cum Simeone fratre suo, et percusserunt simul Chananæum qui habitabat in Sephaath, et interfecerunt eum. Vocatumque est nomen urbis, Horma, id est, anathema.
      EN: Judah went with his brother Simeon, and together they struck down and killed the Canaanites living in Zephath. The city was named Hormah, that is, Devoted to Destruction.

1:18  LA: Cepitque Judas Gazam cum finibus suis, et Ascalonem, atque Accaron cum terminis suis.
      EN: Judah captured Gaza with its territory, Ashkelon, and Ekron with its borders.

1:19  LA: Fuitque Dominus cum Juda, et montana possedit : nec potuit delere habitatores vallis, quia falcatis curribus abundabant.
      EN: The Lord was with Judah, and he took possession of the hill country. But he could not destroy the inhabitants of the valley, because they had many scythed chariots.

1:20  LA: Dederuntque Caleb Hebron, sicut dixerat Moyses, qui delevit ex ea tres filios Enac.
      EN: They gave Hebron to Caleb, as Moses had said, and he destroyed the three sons of Anak there.

1:21  LA: Jebusæum autem habitatorem Jerusalem non deleverunt filii Benjamin : habitavitque Jebusæus cum filiis Benjamin in Jerusalem, usque in præsentem diem.
      EN: But the children of Benjamin did not destroy the Jebusites who inhabited Jerusalem. The Jebusites have lived with the children of Benjamin in Jerusalem to the present day.

1:22  LA: Domus quoque Joseph ascendit in Bethel, fuitque Dominus cum eis.
      EN: The house of Joseph also went up against Bethel, and the Lord was with them.

1:23  LA: Nam cum obsiderent urbem, quæ prius Luza vocabatur,
      EN: While they were besieging the city, which was formerly called Luz,

1:24  LA: viderunt hominem egredientem de civitate, dixeruntque ad eum : Ostende nobis introitum civitatis, et faciemus tecum misericordiam.
      EN: they saw a man leaving the city and said to him, “Show us the way into the city, and we will deal mercifully with you.”

1:25  LA: Qui cum ostendisset eis, percusserunt urbem in ore gladii : hominem autem illum, et omnem cognationem ejus, dimiserunt.
      EN: When he showed them, they struck the city with the edge of the sword, but released that man and all his relatives.

1:26  LA: Qui dimissus, abiit in terram Hetthim, et ædificavit ibi civitatem, vocavitque eam Luzam : quæ ita appellatur usque in præsentem diem.
      EN: After his release, he went into the land of the Hittites, built a city there, and called it Luz. That is its name to the present day.

1:27  LA: Manasses quoque non delevit Bethsan, et Thanac cum viculis suis, et habitatores Dor, et Jeblaam, et Mageddo cum viculis suis, cœpitque Chananæus habitare cum eis.
      EN: Manasseh also did not destroy Beth-shean and Taanach with their villages, or the inhabitants of Dor, Ibleam, and Megiddo with their villages. The Canaanites began to live with them.

1:28  LA: Postquam autem confortatus est Israël, fecit eos tributarios, et delere noluit.
      EN: When Israel grew strong, however, they made them pay tribute and were unwilling to destroy them.

1:29  LA: Ephraim etiam non interfecit Chananæum, qui habitabat in Gazer, sed habitavit cum eo.
      EN: Ephraim likewise did not kill the Canaanites living in Gezer, but lived with them.

1:30  LA: Zabulon non delevit habitatores Cetron, et Naalol : sed habitavit Chananæus in medio ejus, factusque est ei tributarius.
      EN: Zebulun did not destroy the inhabitants of Kitron and Nahalol. The Canaanites lived among them and became subject to their tribute.

1:31  LA: Aser quoque non delevit habitatores Accho, et Sidonis, Ahalab, et Achazib, et Helba, et Aphec, et Rohob :
      EN: Asher also did not destroy the inhabitants of Acco, Sidon, Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, and Rehob.

1:32  LA: habitavitque in medio Chananæi habitatoris illius terræ, nec interfecit eum.
      EN: He lived among the Canaanites who inhabited that land and did not kill them.

1:33  LA: Nephthali quoque non delevit habitatores Bethsames, et Bethanath : et habitavit inter Chananæum habitatorem terræ, fueruntque ei Bethsamitæ et Bethanitæ tributarii.
      EN: Naphtali also did not destroy the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath, but lived among the Canaanites who inhabited the land. The people of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath became subject to his tribute.

1:34  LA: Arctavitque Amorrhæus filios Dan in monte, nec dedit eis locum ut ad planiora descenderent :
      EN: The Amorites confined the children of Dan to the hill country and gave them no room to descend into the more level country.

1:35  LA: habitavitque in monte Hares, quod interpretatur testaceo, in Ajalon et Salebim. Et aggravata est manus domus Joseph, factusque est ei tributarius.
      EN: They lived on Mount Heres, which means Earthen, in Aijalon and Shaalbim. But the hand of the house of Joseph grew heavy upon them, and they became subject to its tribute.

1:36  LA: Fuit autem terminus Amorrhæi ab ascensu Scorpionis, petra, et superiora loca.
      EN: The boundary of the Amorites ran from the Ascent of the Scorpion, from the rock, and through the higher places.

JUDGES 2

2:1  LA: Ascenditque angelus Domini de Galgalis ad Locum flentium, et ait : Eduxi vos de Ægypto, et introduxi in terram, pro qua juravi patribus vestris : et pollicitus sum ut non facerem irritum pactum meum vobiscum in sempiternum,
     EN: The angel of the Lord went up from Gilgal to the Place of Weeping and said, “I brought you out of Egypt and led you into the land for which I swore to your fathers. I promised never to make My covenant with you void,

2:2  LA: ita dumtaxat ut non feriretis fœdus cum habitatoribus terræ hujus, sed aras eorum subverteretis : et noluistis audire vocem meam : cur hoc fecistis ?
     EN: provided that you made no covenant with the inhabitants of this land, but overthrew their altars. Yet you refused to listen to My voice. Why have you done this?

2:3  LA: Quam ob rem nolui delere eos a facie vestra : ut habeatis hostes, et dii eorum sint vobis in ruinam.
     EN: For this reason I have been unwilling to destroy them before you, so that you may have enemies, and their gods may bring you to ruin.”

2:4  LA: Cumque loqueretur angelus Domini hæc verba ad omnes filios Israël, elevaverunt ipsi vocem suam, et fleverunt.
     EN: When the angel of the Lord spoke these words to all the children of Israel, they raised their voices and wept.

2:5  LA: Et vocatum est nomen loci illius, Locus flentium, sive lacrimarum : immolaveruntque ibi hostias Domini.
     EN: That place was named the Place of Weeping, or of Tears, and there they offered sacrifices to the Lord.

2:6  LA: Dimisit ergo Josue populum, et abierunt filii Israël unusquisque in possessionem suam, ut obtinerent eam :
     EN: Joshua had dismissed the people, and the children of Israel had gone, each to his own possession, to occupy it.

2:7  LA: servieruntque Domino cunctis diebus ejus, et seniorum, qui longo post eum vixerunt tempore, et noverant omnia opera Domini quæ fecerat cum Israël.
     EN: They served the Lord throughout his lifetime and that of the elders who lived a long time after him and knew all the works the Lord had done for Israel.

2:8  LA: Mortuus est autem Josue filius Nun, famulus Domini, centum et decem annorum,
     EN: Joshua son of Nun, the Lord's servant, died at the age of one hundred and ten.

2:9  LA: et sepelierunt eum in finibus possessionis suæ in Thamnathsare in monte Ephraim, a septentrionali plaga montis Gaas.
     EN: They buried him within the boundaries of his possession, at Timnath-serah in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.

2:10  LA: Omnisque illa generatio congregata est ad patres suos : et surrexerunt alii, qui non noverant Dominum, et opera quæ fecerat cum Israël.
      EN: That entire generation was gathered to its fathers. Others arose who did not know the Lord or the works He had done for Israel.

2:11  LA: Feceruntque filii Israël malum in conspectu Domini, et servierunt Baalim.
      EN: The children of Israel did evil in the Lord's sight and served the Baals.

2:12  LA: Ac dimiserunt Dominum Deum patrum suorum, qui eduxerat eos de terra Ægypti, et secuti sunt deos alienos, deosque populorum, qui habitabant in circuitu eorum, et adoraverunt eos : et ad iracundiam concitaverunt Dominum,
      EN: They abandoned the Lord, the God of their fathers, who had brought them out of the land of Egypt, and followed foreign gods, the gods of the peoples living around them, and worshiped them. They provoked the Lord to anger,

2:13  LA: dimittentes eum, et servientes Baal et Astaroth.
      EN: abandoning Him and serving Baal and Ashtaroth.

2:14  LA: Iratusque Dominus contra Israël, tradidit eos in manus diripientium : qui ceperunt eos, et vendiderunt hostibus qui habitabant per gyrum : nec potuerunt resistere adversariis suis,
      EN: The Lord grew angry with Israel and delivered them into the hands of plunderers, who captured them and sold them to the enemies living around them. They could not resist their adversaries.

2:15  LA: sed quocumque pergere voluissent, manus Domini super eos erat, sicut locutus est, et juravit eis, et vehementer afflicti sunt.
      EN: Wherever they wished to go, the Lord's hand was against them, as He had said and sworn to them, and they suffered severely.

2:16  LA: Suscitavitque Dominus judices, qui liberarent eos de vastantium manibus : sed nec eos audire voluerunt,
      EN: The Lord raised up judges to rescue them from the hands of those who ravaged them, but they were unwilling to listen even to them,

2:17  LA: fornicantes cum diis alienis, et adorantes eos. Cito deseruerunt viam, per quam ingressi fuerant patres eorum : et audientes mandata Domini, omnia fecere contraria.
      EN: prostituting themselves to foreign gods and worshiping them. They quickly abandoned the way their fathers had followed; though they heard the Lord's commandments, they did everything to the contrary.

2:18  LA: Cumque Dominus judices suscitaret, in diebus eorum flectebatur misericordia, et audiebat afflictorum gemitus, et liberabat eos de cæde vastantium.
      EN: Whenever the Lord raised up judges, during their days He was moved with mercy, heard the groans of the afflicted, and rescued them from the slaughter of their ravagers.

2:19  LA: Postquam autem mortuus esset judex, revertebantur, et multo faciebant pejora quam fecerant patres eorum, sequentes deos alienos, servientes eis, et adorantes illos. Non dimiserunt adinventiones suas, et viam durissimam per quam ambulare consueverunt.
      EN: But after a judge died, they turned back and did far worse things than their fathers had done, following foreign gods, serving them, and worshiping them. They did not abandon their practices or the stubborn way they were accustomed to follow.

2:20  LA: Iratusque est furor Domini in Israël, et ait : Quia irritum fecit gens ista pactum meum, quod pepigeram cum patribus eorum, et vocem meam audire contempsit :
      EN: The Lord's fury blazed against Israel, and He said, “Because this nation has made void My covenant, which I established with their fathers, and has scorned to listen to My voice,

2:21  LA: et ego non delebo gentes, quas dimisit Josue, et mortuus est :
      EN: I too will not destroy the nations Joshua left behind when he died,

2:22  LA: ut in ipsis experiar Israël, utrum custodiant viam Domini, et ambulent in ea, sicut custodierunt patres eorum, an non.
      EN: so that through them I may test Israel, to see whether they keep the Lord's way and walk in it as their fathers kept it, or not.”

2:23  LA: Dimisit ergo Dominus omnes nationes has, et cito subvertere noluit, nec tradidit in manus Josue.
      EN: So the Lord left all these nations; He was unwilling to overthrow them quickly and did not deliver them into Joshua's hands.

JUDGES 3

3:1  LA: Hæ sunt gentes quas Dominus dereliquit, ut erudiret in eis Israëlem, et omnes qui non noverant bella Chananæorum :
     EN: These are the nations the Lord left behind to train Israel through them, all those who had not known the wars of the Canaanites,

3:2  LA: ut postea discerent filii eorum certare cum hostibus, et habere consuetudinem præliandi :
     EN: so that afterward their children might learn to fight their enemies and become accustomed to warfare:

3:3  LA: quinque satrapas Philisthinorum, omnemque Chananæum, et Sidonium, atque Hevæum, qui habitabat in monte Libano, de monte Baal Hermon usque ad introitum Emath.
     EN: the five rulers of the Philistines, all the Canaanites, the Sidonians, and the Hivites who lived on Mount Lebanon, from Mount Baal-hermon to the entrance of Hamath.

3:4  LA: Dimisitque eos, ut in ipsis experiretur Israëlem, utrum audiret mandata Domini quæ præceperat patribus eorum per manum Moysi, an non.
     EN: He left them to test Israel through them, to see whether they would obey the Lord's commandments, which He had commanded their fathers through Moses, or not.

3:5  LA: Itaque filii Israël habitaverunt in medio Chananæi, et Hethæi, et Amorrhæi, et Pherezæi, et Hevæi, et Jebusæi :
     EN: So the children of Israel lived among the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.

3:6  LA: et duxerunt uxores filias eorum, ipsique filias suas filiis eorum tradiderunt, et servierunt diis eorum.
     EN: They married their daughters, gave their own daughters to their sons, and served their gods.

3:7  LA: Feceruntque malum in conspectu Domini, et obliti sunt Dei sui, servientes Baalim et Astaroth.
     EN: They did evil in the Lord's sight and forgot their God, serving the Baals and Ashtaroth.

3:8  LA: Iratusque contra Israël Dominus, tradidit eos in manus Chusan Rasathaim regis Mesopotamiæ, servieruntque ei octo annis.
     EN: The Lord grew angry with Israel and delivered them into the hands of Cushan-rishathaim king of Mesopotamia, and they served him for eight years.

3:9  LA: Et clamaverunt ad Dominum, qui suscitavit eis salvatorem, et liberavit eos, Othoniel videlicet filium Cenez, fratrem Caleb minorem :
     EN: They cried to the Lord, who raised up a savior for them and rescued them: Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother.

3:10  LA: fuitque in eo spiritus Domini, et judicavit Israël. Egressusque est ad pugnam, et tradidit Dominus in manus ejus Chusan Rasathaim regem Syriæ, et oppressit eum.
      EN: The spirit of the Lord was in him, and he judged Israel. He went out to battle, and the Lord delivered Cushan-rishathaim king of Syria into his hands, and he subdued him.

3:11  LA: Quievitque terra quadraginta annis, et mortuus est Othoniel filius Cenez.
      EN: The land had rest for forty years, and Othniel son of Kenaz died.

3:12  LA: Addiderunt autem filii Israël facere malum in conspectu Domini : qui confortavit adversum eos Eglon regem Moab, quia fecerunt malum in conspectu ejus.
      EN: The children of Israel again did evil in the Lord's sight. He strengthened Eglon king of Moab against them, because they had done evil in His sight.

3:13  LA: Et copulavit ei filios Ammon, et Amalec : abiitque et percussit Israël, atque possedit urbem palmarum.
      EN: He joined the children of Ammon and Amalek to him; Eglon went and struck Israel and took possession of the City of Palms.

3:14  LA: Servieruntque filii Israël Eglon regi Moab decem et octo annis.
      EN: The children of Israel served Eglon king of Moab for eighteen years.

3:15  LA: Et postea clamaverunt ad Dominum, qui suscitavit eis salvatorem vocabulo Aod, filium Gera, filii Jemini, qui utraque manu pro dextera utebatur. Miseruntque filii Israël per illum munera Eglon regi Moab.
      EN: Afterward they cried to the Lord, who raised up a savior for them named Ehud, son of Gera, a Benjaminite, who used either hand as his right. Through him the children of Israel sent gifts to Eglon king of Moab.

3:16  LA: Qui fecit sibi gladium ancipitem, habentem in medio capulum longitudinis palmæ manus, et accinctus est eo subter sagum in dextro femore.
      EN: He made himself a double-edged sword with a handle in the middle the length of the palm of a hand, and fastened it under his cloak on his right thigh.

3:17  LA: Obtulitque munera Eglon regi Moab. Erat autem Eglon crassus nimis.
      EN: He presented the gifts to Eglon king of Moab. Eglon was extremely fat.

3:18  LA: Cumque obtulisset ei munera, prosecutus est socios, qui cum eo venerant.
      EN: When he had presented the gifts to him, he accompanied the companions who had come with him on their way.

3:19  LA: Et reversus de Galgalis, ubi erant idola, dixit ad regem : Verbum secretum habeo ad te, o rex. Et ille imperavit silentium : egressisque omnibus qui circa eum erant,
      EN: Returning from Gilgal, where the idols were, he said to the king, “I have a secret message for you, O king.” The king commanded silence, and when everyone around him had gone out,

3:20  LA: ingressus est Aod ad eum : sedebat autem in æstivo cœnaculo solus : dixitque : Verbum Dei habeo ad te. Qui statim surrexit de throno.
      EN: Ehud came in to him. He was sitting alone in a cool upper room. Ehud said, “I have a message from God for you.” At once he rose from his throne.

3:21  LA: Extenditque Aod sinistram manum, et tulit sicam de dextro femore suo, infixitque eam in ventre ejus
      EN: Ehud reached out his left hand, drew the dagger from his right thigh, and thrust it into his belly

3:22  LA: tam valide, ut capulus sequeretur ferrum in vulnere, ac pinguissimo adipe stringeretur. Nec eduxit gladium, sed ita ut percusserat, reliquit in corpore : statimque per secreta naturæ alvi stercora proruperunt.
      EN: with such force that the handle followed the blade into the wound and was held fast by the thick fat. He did not draw the sword out, but left it in the body just as he had thrust it in; immediately the contents of the bowels burst out through the body's private parts.

3:23  LA: Aod autem clausis diligentissime ostiis cœnaculi, et obfirmatis sera,
      EN: Ehud carefully closed the doors of the upper room and fastened them with a bar,

3:24  LA: per posticum egressus est. Servique regis ingressi viderunt clausas fores cœnaculi, atque dixerunt : Forsitan purgat alvum in æstivo cubiculo.
      EN: and went out through the back entrance. The king's servants came in, saw that the doors of the upper room were closed, and said, “Perhaps he is relieving himself in the cool chamber.”

3:25  LA: Expectantesque diu donec erubescerent, et videntes quod nullus aperiret, tulerunt clavem : et aperientes invenerunt dominum suum in terra jacentem mortuum.
      EN: They waited a long time, until they were embarrassed. Seeing that no one opened the doors, they took the key, and on opening them found their lord lying dead on the ground.

3:26  LA: Aod autem, dum illi turbarentur, effugit, et pertransiit locum idolorum, unde reversus fuerat. Venitque in Seirath :
      EN: While they were in confusion, Ehud escaped and passed beyond the place of the idols from which he had returned. He came to Seirah,

3:27  LA: et statim insonuit buccina in monte Ephraim, descenderuntque cum eo filii Israël, ipso in fronte gradiente.
      EN: and immediately sounded a trumpet in the hill country of Ephraim. The children of Israel went down with him, and he marched at their head.

3:28  LA: Qui dixit ad eos : Sequimini me : tradidit enim Dominus inimicos nostros Moabitas in manus nostras. Descenderuntque post eum, et occupaverunt vada Jordanis quæ transmittunt in Moab : et non dimiserunt transire quemquam :
      EN: He said to them, “Follow me, for the Lord has delivered our enemies the Moabites into our hands.” They went down after him and occupied the fords of the Jordan leading across to Moab, and allowed no one to cross.

3:29  LA: sed percusserunt Moabitas in tempore illo, circiter decem millia, omnes robustos et fortes viros. Nullus eorum evadere potuit.
      EN: At that time they struck down about ten thousand Moabites, all sturdy and valiant men. None of them could escape.

3:30  LA: Humiliatusque est Moab in die illo sub manu Israël : et quievit terra octoginta annis.
      EN: Moab was humbled that day under Israel's hand, and the land had rest for eighty years.

3:31  LA: Post hunc fuit Samgar filius Anath, qui percussit de Philisthiim sexcentos viros vomere : et ipse quoque defendit Israël.
      EN: After him came Shamgar son of Anath, who struck down six hundred Philistine men with a plowshare. He too defended Israel.

JUDGES 4

4:1  LA: Addideruntque filii Israël facere malum in conspectu Domini post mortem Aod,

      EN: Gaal son of Ebed cried out, “Who is Abimelech, and what is Shechem, that we should serve him? Is he not Jerubbaal's son, and has he not appointed his servant Zebul ruler over the men of Hamor, Shechem's father? Why, then, should we serve him?

9:30  LA: Zebul enim princeps civitatis, auditis sermonibus Gaal filii Obed, iratus est valde,
      EN: For Zebul, the city's ruler, heard the words of Gaal son of Ebed and became very angry.

      EN: He secretly sent messengers to Abimelech, saying, “Gaal son of Ebed has come to Shechem with his brothers and is attacking the city against you.

      EN: Now arise at night with the people who are with you, and hide in the fields.

9:33  LA: et primo mane, oriente sole, irrue super civitatem. Illo autem egrediente adversum te cum populo suo, fac ei quod potueris.
      EN: At daybreak, when the sun rises, fall upon the city. When he comes out against you with his people, do to him what you can.”

      EN: Abimelech arose at night with his entire army and set ambushes near Shechem in four places.

9:35  LA: Egressusque est Gaal filius Obed, et stetit in introitu portæ civitatis. Surrexit autem Abimelech, et omnis exercitus cum eo, de insidiarum loco.
      EN: Gaal son of Ebed went out and stood at the entrance to the city gate. Abimelech and his entire army arose from the place of ambush.

      EN: When Gaal saw the people, he said to Zebul, “Look, a multitude is coming down from the mountains.” He answered him, “You are seeing the shadows of the mountains as though they were men's heads, and this mistake deceives you.”

9:37  LA: Rursumque Gaal ait : Ecce populus de umbilico terræ descendit, et unus cuneus venit per viam quæ respicit quercum.

      EN: Zebul said to him, “Where now is that mouth of yours with which you said, ‘Who is Abimelech, that we should serve him?’ Are these not the people you despised? Go out and fight against them.”

9:39  LA: Abiit ergo Gaal, spectante Sichimorum populo, et pugnavit contra Abimelech,
      EN: So Gaal went out, with the people of Shechem watching, and fought against Abimelech,

9:40  LA: qui persecutus est eum fugientem, et in urbem compulit : cecideruntque ex parte ejus plurimi, usque ad portam civitatis.
      EN: who pursued him as he fled and drove him into the city. Very many of his men fell, all the way to the city gate.

9:41  LA: Et Abimelech sedit in Ruma : Zebul autem Gaal et socios ejus expulit de urbe, nec in ea passus est commorari.
      EN: Abimelech stayed at Arumah, while Zebul drove Gaal and his companions out of the city and would not allow them to remain in it.

9:42  LA: Sequenti ergo die, egressus est populus in campum. Quod cum nuntiatum esset Abimelech,
      EN: On the next day the people went out into the fields. When Abimelech was told this,

9:43  LA: tulit exercitum suum, et divisit in tres turmas, tendens insidias in agris. Vidensque quod egrederetur populus de civitate, surrexit, et irruit in eos
      EN: he took his army, divided it into three companies, and set an ambush in the fields. Seeing the people come out of the city, he arose and fell upon them

9:44  LA: cum cuneo suo, oppugnans et obsidens civitatem : duæ autem turmæ palantes per campum adversarios persequebantur.
      EN: with his company, attacking and besieging the city, while the two other companies pursued the adversaries scattered through the fields.

9:45  LA: Porro Abimelech omni die illo oppugnabat urbem : quam cepit, interfectis habitatoribus ejus, ipsaque destructa, ita ut sal in ea dispergeret.
      EN: Abimelech attacked the city all that day. He captured it, killed its inhabitants, and destroyed it, even scattering salt in it.

9:46  LA: Quod cum audissent qui habitabant in turre Sichimorum, ingressi sunt fanum dei sui Berith, ubi fœdus cum eo pepigerant, et ex eo locus nomen acceperat : qui erat munitus valde.
      EN: When those living in Shechem's tower heard this, they entered the shrine of their god Berith, where they had made a covenant with him, from which the place had received its name. It was strongly fortified.

9:47  LA: Abimelech quoque audiens viros turris Sichimorum pariter conglobatos,
      EN: Abimelech also heard that the men of Shechem's tower had gathered together.

9:48  LA: ascendit in montem Selmon cum omni populo suo : et arrepta securi, præcidit arboris ramum, impositumque ferens humero, dixit ad socios : Quod me videtis facere, cito facite.
      EN: He went up Mount Zalmon with all his people, seized an axe, and cut off a tree branch. Carrying it on his shoulder, he said to his companions, “Quickly do what you see me doing.”

9:49  LA: Igitur certatim ramos de arboribus præcidentes, sequebantur ducem. Qui circumdantes præsidium, succenderunt : atque ita factum est, ut fumo et igne mille homines necarentur, viri pariter et mulieres, habitatorum turris Sichem.
      EN: Competing with one another, they cut branches from the trees and followed their leader. They surrounded the stronghold and set it on fire. So a thousand people, men and women alike, from among the inhabitants of Shechem's tower, were killed by smoke and fire.

9:50  LA: Abimelech autem inde proficiscens venit ad oppidum Thebes, quod circumdans obsidebat exercitu.
      EN: Abimelech set out from there and came to the town of Thebez, which he surrounded and besieged with his army.

9:51  LA: Erat autem turris excelsa in media civitate, ad quam confugerant simul viri ac mulieres, et omnes principes civitatis, clausa firmissime janua, et super turris tectum stantes per propugnacula.
      EN: There was a high tower in the middle of the city, to which the men and women and all the city's leaders had fled together. They shut the door securely and stood on the tower's roof along its defenses.

9:52  LA: Accedensque Abimelech juxta turrim, pugnabat fortiter : et appropinquans ostio, ignem supponere nitebatur :
      EN: Abimelech approached the tower and fought fiercely. Coming near the door, he tried to set fire beneath it,

9:53  LA: et ecce una mulier fragmen molæ desuper jaciens, illisit capiti Abimelech, et confregit cerebrum ejus.
      EN: when a woman threw down a fragment of a millstone from above. It struck Abimelech's head and shattered his brain.

9:54  LA: Qui vocavit cito armigerum suum, et ait ad eum : Evagina gladium tuum, et percute me, ne forte dicatur quod a femina interfectus sim. Qui jussa perficiens, interfecit eum.
      EN: He quickly called his armor-bearer and said to him, “Draw your sword and strike me, lest it be said that I was killed by a woman.” He obeyed the command and killed him.

9:55  LA: Illoque mortuo, omnes qui cum eo erant de Israël, reversi sunt in sedes suas :
      EN: When he was dead, all the Israelites who had been with him returned to their homes.

9:56  LA: et reddidit Deus malum quod fecerat Abimelech contra patrem suum, interfectis septuaginta fratribus suis.
      EN: God repaid the evil Abimelech had done against his father by killing his seventy brothers.

9:57  LA: Sichimitis quoque quod operati erant, retributum est, et venit super eos maledictio Joatham filii Jerobaal.
      EN: The Shechemites too were repaid for what they had done, and the curse of Jotham son of Jerubbaal came upon them.

JUDGES 10

10:1  LA: Post Abimelech surrexit dux in Israël Thola filius Phua patrui Abimelech, vir de Issachar, qui habitavit in Samir montis Ephraim :
      EN: After Abimelech, Tola, the son of Abimelech's paternal uncle Puah, arose as leader in Israel. He was a man of Issachar and lived in Shamir in the hill country of Ephraim.

10:2  LA: et judicavit Israëlem viginti et tribus annis, mortuusque est, ac sepultus in Samir.
      EN: He judged Israel for twenty-three years, then died and was buried in Shamir.

10:3  LA: Huic successit Jair Galaadites, qui judicavit Israël per viginti et duos annos,
      EN: He was succeeded by Jair the Gileadite, who judged Israel for twenty-two years.

10:4  LA: habens triginta filios sedentes super triginta pullos asinarum, et principes triginta civitatum, quæ ex nomine ejus sunt appellatæ Havoth Jair, id est, oppida Jair, usque in præsentem diem, in terra Galaad.
      EN: He had thirty sons who rode on thirty donkey colts and ruled thirty cities. These were named after him Havvoth-jair, that is, Towns of Jair, as they are called to the present day, in the land of Gilead.

10:5  LA: Mortuusque est Jair, ac sepultus in loco cui est vocabulum Camon.
      EN: Jair died and was buried in the place called Kamon.

10:6  LA: Filii autem Israël peccatis veteribus jungentes nova, fecerunt malum in conspectu Domini, et servierunt idolis, Baalim et Astaroth, et diis Syriæ ac Sidonis et Moab et filiorum Ammon et Philisthiim : dimiseruntque Dominum, et non coluerunt eum.
      EN: The children of Israel added new sins to their old ones and did evil in the Lord's sight. They served idols, the Baals and Ashtaroth, and the gods of Syria, Sidon, Moab, the children of Ammon, and the Philistines. They abandoned the Lord and did not worship Him.

10:7  LA: Contra quos Dominus iratus, tradidit eos in manus Philisthiim et filiorum Ammon.
      EN: The Lord grew angry with them and delivered them into the hands of the Philistines and the children of Ammon.

10:8  LA: Afflictique sunt, et vehementer oppressi per annos decem et octo, omnes qui habitabant trans Jordanem in terra Amorrhæi, qui est in Galaad :
      EN: All those who lived beyond the Jordan in the land of the Amorites in Gilead were afflicted and severely oppressed for eighteen years,

10:9  LA: in tantum ut filii Ammon, Jordane transmisso, vastarent Judam et Benjamin et Ephraim : afflictusque est Israël nimis.
      EN: so that the children of Ammon crossed the Jordan and ravaged Judah, Benjamin, and Ephraim. Israel was afflicted exceedingly.

10:10  LA: Et clamantes ad Dominum, dixerunt : Peccavimus tibi, quia dereliquimus Dominum Deum nostrum, et servivimus Baalim.
       EN: They cried to the Lord and said, “We have sinned against You, because we have abandoned the Lord our God and served the Baals.”

10:11  LA: Quibus locutus est Dominus : Numquid non Ægyptii et Amorrhæi, filiique Ammon et Philisthiim,
       EN: The Lord said to them, “Did not the Egyptians and Amorites, the children of Ammon and the Philistines,

10:12  LA: Sidonii quoque et Amalec et Chanaan oppresserunt vos, et clamastis ad me, et erui vos de manu eorum ?
       EN: and also the Sidonians, Amalek, and Canaan oppress you? You cried to Me, and I rescued you from their hands.

10:13  LA: Et tamen reliquistis me, et coluistis deos alienos : idcirco non addam ut ultra vos liberem :
       EN: Yet you abandoned Me and worshiped foreign gods. Therefore I will no longer rescue you.

10:14  LA: ite, et invocate deos quos elegistis : ipsi vos liberent in tempore angustiæ.
       EN: Go and call upon the gods you have chosen. Let them rescue you in the time of distress.”

10:15  LA: Dixeruntque filii Israël ad Dominum : Peccavimus, redde tu nobis quidquid tibi placet : tantum nunc libera nos.
       EN: The children of Israel said to the Lord, “We have sinned. Repay us with whatever pleases You; only rescue us now.”

10:16  LA: Quæ dicentes, omnia de finibus suis alienorum deorum idola projecerunt, et servierunt Domino Deo : qui doluit super miseriis eorum.
       EN: As they said this, they cast out all the idols of foreign gods from their territory and served the Lord God, who grieved over their miseries.

10:17  LA: Itaque filii Ammon conclamantes in Galaad fixere tentoria, contra quos congregati filii Israël in Maspha castrametati sunt.
       EN: The children of Ammon rallied with a shout and pitched their tents in Gilead. The children of Israel assembled against them and camped in Mizpah.

10:18  LA: Dixeruntque principes Galaad singuli ad proximos suos : Qui primus ex nobis contra filios Ammon cœperit dimicare, erit dux populi Galaad.
       EN: The leaders of Gilead said to one another, “Whoever is the first among us to begin fighting the children of Ammon shall be the leader of the people of Gilead.”

JUDGES 11

11:1  LA: Fuit illo tempore Jephte Galaadites vir fortissimus atque pugnator, filius mulieris meretricis, qui natus est de Galaad.
      EN: At that time Jephthah the Gileadite was a very mighty man and a warrior. He was the son of a prostitute, born to Gilead.

11:2  LA: Habuit autem Galaad uxorem, de qua suscepit filios : qui postquam creverant, ejecerunt Jephte, dicentes : Hæres in domo patris nostri esse non poteris, quia de altera matre natus es.
      EN: Gilead also had a wife who bore him sons. When they had grown up, they drove Jephthah out, saying, “You cannot be an heir in our father's house, because you were born of another mother.”

11:3  LA: Quos ille fugiens atque devitans, habitavit in terra Tob : congregatique sunt ad eum viri inopes, et latrocinantes, et quasi principem sequebantur.
      EN: Fleeing and avoiding them, he lived in the land of Tob. Destitute men and robbers gathered around him and followed him as their leader.

11:4  LA: In illis diebus pugnabant filii Ammon contra Israël.
      EN: In those days the children of Ammon were fighting against Israel.

11:5  LA: Quibus acriter instantibus perrexerunt majores natu de Galaad, ut tollerent in auxilium sui Jephte de terra Tob :
      EN: When they pressed hard, the elders of Gilead went to bring Jephthah from the land of Tob to help them.

11:6  LA: dixeruntque ad eum : Veni et esto princeps noster, et pugna contra filios Ammon.
      EN: They said to him, “Come and be our leader, and fight against the children of Ammon.”

11:7  LA: Quibus ille respondit : Nonne vos estis, qui odistis me, et ejecistis de domo patris mei ? et nunc venistis ad me necessitate compulsi.
      EN: He answered them, “Are you not the ones who hated me and drove me out of my father's house? Now you have come to me, compelled by necessity.”

11:8  LA: Dixeruntque principes Galaad ad Jephte : Ob hanc igitur causam nunc ad te venimus, ut proficiscaris nobiscum, et pugnes contra filios Ammon, sisque dux omnium qui habitant in Galaad.
      EN: The leaders of Gilead said to Jephthah, “This is precisely why we have now come to you: that you may go with us, fight against the children of Ammon, and be leader of all who live in Gilead.”

11:9  LA: Jephte quoque dixit eis : Si vere venistis ad me, ut pugnem pro vobis contra filios Ammon, tradideritque eos Dominus in manus meas, ego ero vester princeps ?
      EN: Jephthah also said to them, “If you have truly come to me so that I may fight for you against the children of Ammon, and the Lord delivers them into my hands, shall I be your leader?”

11:10  LA: Qui responderunt ei : Dominus, qui hæc audit, ipse mediator ac testis est quod nostra promissa faciemus.
       EN: They answered him, “The Lord, who hears these things, is Himself mediator and witness that we will carry out our promises.”

11:11  LA: Abiit itaque Jephte cum principibus Galaad, fecitque eum omnis populus principem sui. Locutusque est Jephte omnes sermones suos coram Domino in Maspha.
       EN: So Jephthah went with the leaders of Gilead, and all the people made him their leader. Jephthah spoke all his words before the Lord in Mizpah.

11:12  LA: Et misit nuntios ad regem filiorum Ammon, qui ex persona sua dicerent : Quid mihi et tibi est, quia venisti contra me, ut vastares terram meam ?
       EN: He sent messengers to the king of the children of Ammon to say on his behalf, “What is there between you and me, that you have come against me to ravage my land?”

11:13  LA: Quibus ille respondit : Quia tulit Israël terram meam, quando ascendit de Ægypto, a finibus Arnon usque Jaboc atque Jordanem : nunc ergo cum pace redde mihi eam.
       EN: He answered them, “Because Israel took my land when they came up from Egypt, from the borders of the Arnon to the Jabbok and the Jordan. Now restore it to me in peace.”

11:14  LA: Per quos rursum mandavit Jephte, et imperavit eis ut dicerent regi Ammon :
       EN: Jephthah again sent a message through them and commanded them to say to the king of Ammon,

11:15  LA: Hæc dicit Jephte : Non tulit Israël terram Moab, nec terram filiorum Ammon :
       EN: “Thus says Jephthah: Israel did not take the land of Moab or the land of the children of Ammon.

11:16  LA: sed quando de Ægypto conscenderunt, ambulavit per solitudinem usque ad mare Rubrum, et venit in Cades.
       EN: When they came up from Egypt, Israel traveled through the wilderness to the Red Sea and came to Kadesh.

11:17  LA: Misitque nuntios ad regem Edom, dicens : Dimitte me ut transeam per terram tuam. Qui noluit acquiescere precibus ejus. Misit quoque ad regem Moab, qui et ipse transitum præbere contempsit. Mansit itaque in Cades,
       EN: They sent messengers to the king of Edom, saying, ‘Allow me to pass through your land.’ He was unwilling to grant their request. They also sent to the king of Moab, who likewise refused to grant passage. So Israel remained in Kadesh,

11:18  LA: et circuivit ex latere terram Edom et terram Moab : venitque contra orientalem plagam terræ Moab, et castrametatus est trans Arnon : nec voluit intrare terminos Moab. (Arnon quippe confinium est terræ Moab.)
       EN: and went around the side of the land of Edom and the land of Moab. They came opposite the eastern part of the land of Moab and camped beyond the Arnon, without wishing to enter Moab's territory, for the Arnon is the boundary of the land of Moab.

11:19  LA: Misit itaque Israël nuntios ad Sehon regem Amorrhæorum, qui habitabat in Hesebon, et dixerunt ei : Dimitte ut transeam per terram tuam usque ad fluvium.
       EN: Israel sent messengers to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and they said to him, ‘Allow me to pass through your land as far as the river.’

11:20  LA: Qui et ipse Israël verba despiciens, non dimisit eum transire per terminos suos : sed infinita multitudine congregata, egressus est contra eum in Jasa, et fortiter resistebat.
       EN: He too scorned Israel's words and did not allow them to pass through his territory. Gathering an immense multitude, he came out against them at Jahaz and resisted fiercely.

11:21  LA: Tradiditque eum Dominus in manus Israël cum omni exercitu suo : qui percussit eum, et possedit omnem terram Amorrhæi habitatoris regionis illius,
       EN: The Lord delivered him and his whole army into Israel's hands. Israel struck him and took possession of all the land of the Amorites who inhabited that region,

11:22  LA: et universos fines ejus, de Arnon usque Jaboc, et de solitudine usque ad Jordanem.
       EN: and all its territory, from the Arnon to the Jabbok and from the wilderness to the Jordan.

11:23  LA: Dominus ergo Deus Israël subvertit Amorrhæum, pugnante contra illum populo suo Israël, et tu nunc vis possidere terram ejus ?
       EN: The Lord, the God of Israel, overthrew the Amorites as His people Israel fought against them, and now you wish to possess their land?

11:24  LA: nonne ea quæ possidet Chamos deus tuus, tibi jure debentur ? quæ autem Dominus Deus noster victor obtinuit, in nostram cedent possessionem :
       EN: Do not the things that Chemosh your god possesses rightfully belong to you? What the Lord our God has gained by victory will become our possession.

11:25  LA: nisi forte melior es Balac filio Sephor rege Moab ; aut docere potes, quod jurgatus sit contra Israël, et pugnaverit contra eum,
       EN: Unless perhaps you are better than Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, or can show that he quarreled with Israel and fought against them,

11:26  LA: quando habitavit in Hesebon et viculis ejus, et in Aroër et villis illius, vel in cunctis civitatibus juxta Jordanem, per trecentos annos. Quare tanto tempore nihil super hac repetitione tentastis ?
       EN: while they lived in Heshbon and its villages, in Aroer and its settlements, and in all the cities beside the Jordan for three hundred years. Why have you attempted nothing to reclaim this land during so long a time?

       EN: When those remaining of Benjamin saw this, they fled into the wilderness and headed toward the rock called Rimmon. During that flight, as they scattered and went in different directions, the Israelites killed five thousand men. They pursued them farther and killed another two thousand as well.

20:46  LA: Et sic factum est, ut omnes qui ceciderant de Benjamin in diversis locis essent viginti quinque millia pugnatores ad bella promptissimi.
       EN: Thus all those of Benjamin who had fallen in various places numbered twenty-five thousand warriors, men very well prepared for battle.

20:47  LA: Remanserunt itaque de omni numero Benjamin, qui evadere et fugere in solitudinem potuerunt, sexcenti viri : sederuntque in petra Remmon mensibus quatuor.
       EN: Of Benjamin's entire number, six hundred men remained who had been able to escape and flee into the wilderness. They stayed at the rock of Rimmon for four months.

20:48  LA: Regressi autem filii Israël, omnes reliquias civitatis a viris usque ad jumenta gladio percusserunt, cunctasque urbes et viculos Benjamin vorax flamma consumpsit.
       EN: The children of Israel returned and struck with the sword everything remaining of the city, from men to livestock, and devouring flame consumed all the cities and villages of Benjamin.

JUDGES 21

21:1  LA: Juraverunt quoque filii Israël in Maspha, et dixerunt : Nullus nostrum dabit filiis Benjamin de filiabus suis uxorem.
      EN: The children of Israel also swore at Mizpah, saying, “None of us will give any of his daughters as a wife to the children of Benjamin.”

21:2  LA: Veneruntque omnes ad domum Dei in Silo, et in conspectu ejus sedentes usque ad vesperam, levaverunt vocem, et magno ululatu cœperunt flere, dicentes :
      EN: They all came to the house of God in Shiloh and sat in His presence until evening. They raised their voices and began to weep with loud wailing, saying,

21:3  LA: Quare, Domine Deus Israël, factum est hoc malum in populo tuo, ut hodie una tribus auferretur ex nobis ?
      EN: “Why, Lord, God of Israel, has this evil happened among Your people, that today one tribe should be taken away from us?”

21:4  LA: Altera autem die diluculo consurgentes, exstruxerunt altare : obtuleruntque ibi holocausta, et pacificas victimas, et dixerunt :
      EN: On the next day they arose at dawn and built an altar. They offered burnt offerings and peace offerings there and said,

21:5  LA: Quis non ascendit in exercitu Domini de universis tribubus Israël ? grandi enim juramento se constrinxerant, cum essent in Maspha, interfici eos qui defuissent.
      EN: “Who from all the tribes of Israel did not come up with the Lord's army?” For when they were in Mizpah, they had bound themselves by a solemn oath that those who were absent should be killed.

21:6  LA: Ductique pœnitentia filii Israël super fratre suo Benjamin, cœperunt dicere : Ablata est tribus una de Israël :
      EN: The children of Israel were moved with regret for their brother Benjamin and began to say, “One tribe has been taken away from Israel.

21:7  LA: unde uxores accipient ? omnes enim in commune juravimus, non daturos nos his filias nostras.
      EN: Where will they obtain wives? For all of us swore together that we would not give them our daughters.”

21:8  LA: Idcirco dixerunt : Quis est de universis tribubus Israël, qui non ascendit ad Dominum in Maspha ? Et ecce inventi sunt habitatores Jabes Galaad in illo exercitu non fuisse.
      EN: Therefore they said, “Who from all the tribes of Israel did not go up to the Lord at Mizpah?” It was found that the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead had not been in that army.

21:9  LA: (Eo quoque tempore cum essent in Silo, nullus ex eis ibi repertus est.)
      EN: At that time also, when they were in Shiloh, none of them was found there.

21:10  LA: Miserunt itaque decem millia viros robustissimos, et præceperunt eis : Ite, et percutite habitatores Jabes Galaad in ore gladii, tam uxores quam parvulos eorum.
       EN: They sent ten thousand very strong men and commanded them, “Go and strike the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead with the edge of the sword, their wives and little children as well.

21:11  LA: Et hoc erit quod observare debebitis : omne generis masculini, et mulieres quæ cognoverunt viros, interficite ; virgines autem reservate.
       EN: This is what you must observe: kill every male and every woman who has known a man, but spare the virgins.”

21:12  LA: Inventæque sunt de Jabes Galaad quadringentæ virgines, quæ nescierunt viri thorum : et adduxerunt eas ad castra in Silo, in terram Chanaan.
       EN: Four hundred virgins who had not known a man's bed were found in Jabesh-gilead. They brought them to the camp in Shiloh, in the land of Canaan.

21:13  LA: Miseruntque nuntios ad filios Benjamin, qui erant in petra Remmon, et præceperunt eis, ut eos susciperent in pace.
       EN: They sent messengers to the children of Benjamin at the rock of Rimmon and instructed them to receive them in peace.

21:14  LA: Veneruntque filii Benjamin in illo tempore, et datæ sunt eis uxores de filiabus Jabes Galaad : alias autem non repererunt, quas simili modo traderent.
       EN: The children of Benjamin came at that time, and wives were given to them from the daughters of Jabesh-gilead. But they found no others to hand over in the same way.

21:15  LA: Universusque Israël valde doluit, et egit pœnitentiam super interfectione unius tribus ex Israël.
       EN: All Israel grieved deeply and felt remorse over the destruction of one tribe from Israel.

21:16  LA: Dixeruntque majores natu : Quid faciemus reliquis, qui non acceperunt uxores ? omnes in Benjamin feminæ conciderunt,
       EN: The elders said, “What shall we do for those remaining who have not received wives? All the women in Benjamin have fallen,

21:17  LA: et magna nobis cura, ingentique studio providendum est, ne una tribus deleatur ex Israël.
       EN: and we must take great care and make every effort to ensure that one tribe is not destroyed from Israel.

21:18  LA: Filias enim nostras eis dare non possumus, constricti juramento et maledictione qua diximus : Maledictus qui dederit de filiabus suis uxorem Benjamin.
       EN: We cannot give them our daughters, because we are bound by the oath and curse in which we said, ‘Cursed is the one who gives any of his daughters as a wife to Benjamin.’”

21:19  LA: Ceperuntque consilium, atque dixerunt : Ecce solemnitas Domini est in Silo anniversaria, quæ sita est ad septentrionem urbis Bethel, et ad orientalem plagam viæ, quæ de Bethel tendit ad Sichimam, et ad meridiem oppidi Lebona.
       EN: They took counsel and said, “There is an annual solemn festival of the Lord at Shiloh, which is situated north of the city of Bethel, east of the road from Bethel to Shechem, and south of the town of Lebonah.”

21:20  LA: Præceperuntque filiis Benjamin, atque dixerunt : Ite, et latitate in vineis.
       EN: They commanded the children of Benjamin, saying, “Go and hide in the vineyards.

21:21  LA: Cumque videritis filias Silo ad ducendos choros ex more procedere, exite repente de vineis, et rapite ex eis singuli uxores singulas, et pergite in terram Benjamin.
       EN: When you see the daughters of Shiloh come out to dance as usual, suddenly emerge from the vineyards, and each of you seize one of them as his wife. Then go into the land of Benjamin.

21:22  LA: Cumque venerint patres earum, ac fratres, et adversum vos queri cœperint atque jurgari, dicemus eis : Miseremini eorum : non enim rapuerunt eas jure bellantium atque victorum : sed rogantibus ut acciperent, non dedistis, et a vestra parte peccatum est.
       EN: When their fathers and brothers come and begin to complain and quarrel with you, we will say to them, ‘Have pity on them. They did not seize the women by the right of warriors and conquerors; rather, when they asked to receive them, you did not give them, and the sin is on your side.’”

21:23  LA: Feceruntque filii Benjamin ut sibi fuerat imperatum : et juxta numerum suum, rapuerunt sibi de his quæ ducebant choros, uxores singulas : abieruntque in possessionem suam ædificantes urbes, et habitantes in eis.
       EN: The children of Benjamin did as they had been commanded. According to their number, each seized a wife for himself from among the women who were dancing. They went into their own possession, rebuilt the cities, and lived in them.

21:24  LA: Filii quoque Israël reversi sunt per tribus et familias in tabernacula sua. In diebus illis non erat rex in Israël : sed unusquisque quod sibi rectum videbatur, hoc faciebat.
       EN: The children of Israel also returned by their tribes and families to their tents. In those days there was no king in Israel, and everyone did what seemed right to himself.

       EN: The children of Israel also returned by their tribes and families to their tents. In those days there was no king in Israel, and everyone did what seemed right to himself.
