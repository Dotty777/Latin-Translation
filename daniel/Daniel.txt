DANIEL 1

1:1  LA: Anno tertio regni Joakim regis Juda, venit Nabuchodonosor, rex Babylonis, in Jerusalem, et obsedit eam :
     EN: In the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and besieged it.

1:2  LA: et tradidit Dominus in manu ejus Joakim, regem Juda, et partem vasorum domus Dei : et asportavit ea in terram Sennaar in domum dei sui, et vasa intulit in domum thesauri dei sui.
     EN: The Lord delivered Jehoiakim king of Judah into his hand, together with some of the vessels of the house of God. He carried them away to the land of Shinar, to the house of his god, and brought the vessels into his god’s treasury.

1:3  LA: Et ait rex Asphenez præposito eunuchorum ut introduceret de filiis Israël, et de semine regio et tyrannorum,
     EN: The king told Ashpenaz, chief of the eunuchs, to bring in some of the Israelites, from the royal line and the ruling families:

1:4  LA: pueros in quibus nulla esset macula, decoros forma, et eruditos omni sapientia, cautos scientia, et doctos disciplina, et qui possent stare in palatio regis, ut doceret eos litteras et linguam Chaldæorum.
     EN: young men without any blemish, handsome in appearance, instructed in every kind of wisdom, discerning in knowledge, trained in learning, and able to serve in the king’s palace. He was to teach them the literature and language of the Chaldeans.

1:5  LA: Et constituit eis rex annonam per singulos dies de cibis suis, et de vino unde bibebat ipse, ut enutriti tribus annis, postea starent in conspectu regis.
     EN: The king assigned them a daily allowance from his own food and from the wine he himself drank, so that, after being nourished for three years, they might serve in the king’s presence.

1:6  LA: Fuerunt ergo inter eos de filiis Juda, Daniel, Ananias, Misaël, et Azarias.
     EN: Among them were these men of Judah: Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.

1:7  LA: Et imposuit eis præpositus eunuchorum nomina : Danieli, Baltassar ; Ananiæ, Sidrach ; Misaëli, Misach ; et Azariæ, Abdenago.
     EN: The chief of the eunuchs gave them names: Daniel he called Belteshazzar; Hananiah, Shadrach; Mishael, Meshach; and Azariah, Abednego.

1:8  LA: Proposuit autem Daniel in corde suo ne pollueretur de mensa regis, neque de vino potus ejus : et rogavit eunuchorum præpositum ne contaminaretur.
     EN: Daniel resolved in his heart not to defile himself with the king’s table or with the wine he drank. He asked the chief of the eunuchs that he might not be defiled.

1:9  LA: Dedit autem Deus Danieli gratiam et misericordiam in conspectu principis eunuchorum.
     EN: God granted Daniel favor and compassion in the sight of the chief of the eunuchs.

1:10  LA: Et ait princeps eunuchorum ad Danielem : Timeo ego dominum meum regem, qui constituit vobis cibum et potum : qui si viderit vultus vestros macilentiores præ ceteris adolescentibus coævis vestris, condemnabitis caput meum regi.
      EN: The chief of the eunuchs said to Daniel, “I fear my lord the king, who has assigned your food and drink. If he sees your faces thinner than those of the other young men your age, you will make me answer to the king with my life.”

1:11  LA: Et dixit Daniel ad Malasar, quem constituerat princeps eunuchorum super Danielem, Ananiam, Misaëlem, et Azariam :
      EN: Daniel said to Malasar, whom the chief of the eunuchs had appointed over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah,

1:12  LA: Tenta nos, obsecro, servos tuos, diebus decem, et dentur nobis legumina ad vescendum, et aqua ad bibendum :
      EN: “Please test your servants for ten days. Let us be given legumes to eat and water to drink.

1:13  LA: et contemplare vultus nostros, et vultus puerorum, qui vescuntur cibo regio : et sicut videris, facies cum servis tuis.
      EN: Then compare our faces with the faces of the young men who eat the king’s food, and deal with your servants according to what you see.”

1:14  LA: Qui, audito sermone hujuscemodi, tentavit eos diebus decem.
      EN: Hearing this proposal, he tested them for ten days.

1:15  LA: Post dies autem decem, apparuerunt vultus eorum meliores, et corpulentiores præ omnibus pueris, qui vescebantur cibo regio.
      EN: After ten days their faces looked better and fuller than those of all the young men who ate the king’s food.

1:16  LA: Porro Malasar tollebat cibaria, et vinum potus eorum : dabatque eis legumina.
      EN: So Malasar took away their food and the wine they were to drink and gave them legumes.

1:17  LA: Pueris autem his dedit Deus scientiam et disciplinam, in omni libro et sapientia : Danieli autem intelligentiam omnium visionum et somniorum.
      EN: God gave these young men knowledge and learning in every kind of literature and wisdom; to Daniel He also gave understanding of every vision and dream.

1:18  LA: Completis itaque diebus, post quos dixerat rex ut introducerentur, introduxit eos præpositus eunuchorum in conspectu Nabuchodonosor.
      EN: When the days were completed after which the king had said they should be brought in, the chief of the eunuchs brought them before Nebuchadnezzar.

1:19  LA: Cumque eis locutus fuisset rex, non sunt inventi tales de universis, ut Daniel, Ananias, Misaël, et Azarias : et steterunt in conspectu regis.
      EN: The king spoke with them, and among them all none were found like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah. They entered the king’s service.

1:20  LA: Et omne verbum sapientiæ et intellectus, quod sciscitatus est ab eis rex, invenit in eis decuplum super cunctos ariolos et magos qui erant in universo regno ejus.
      EN: In every matter of wisdom and understanding about which the king questioned them, he found them ten times better than all the diviners and magicians throughout his kingdom.

1:21  LA: Fuit autem Daniel usque ad annum primum Cyri regis.
      EN: Daniel remained until the first year of King Cyrus.

1:22  LA: Ingemuit Susanna, et ait : Angustiæ sunt mihi undique : si enim hoc egero, mors mihi est : si autem non egero, non effugiam manus vestras.
      EN: Susanna groaned and said, “I am trapped on every side. If I do this, it is death for me; if I do not, I will not escape your hands.

1:23  LA: Sed melius est mihi absque opere incidere in manus vestras, quam peccare in conspectu Domini.
      EN: But it is better for me to fall into your hands without doing it than to sin in the sight of the Lord.”

1:24  LA: Et dixit rex Danieli : Ecce nunc non potes dicere quia iste non sit deus vivens : adora ergo eum.
      EN: The king said to Daniel, “Now look, you cannot say that this is not a living god. Worship it, then.”

1:25  LA: Dixitque Daniel : Dominum Deum meum adoro, quia ipse est Deus vivens : iste autem non est deus vivens.
      EN: Daniel said, “I worship the Lord my God, for He is the living God. But this is no living god.

1:26  LA: Tu autem, rex, da mihi potestatem, et interficiam draconem absque gladio et fuste. Et ait rex : Do tibi.
      EN: Give me permission, O king, and I will kill the dragon without sword or club.” The king said, “I give you permission.”

1:27  LA: Tulit ergo Daniel picem, et adipem, et pilos, et coxit pariter : fecitque massas, et dedit in os draconis, et diruptus est draco. Et dixit : Ecce quem colebatis.
      EN: Daniel took pitch, fat, and hair and boiled them together. He made lumps of the mixture and put them into the dragon’s mouth, and the dragon burst apart. He said, “Look at what you worshiped!”

1:28  LA: Cumque venisset populus ad Joakim virum ejus, venerunt et duo presbyteri, pleni iniqua cogitatione adversus Susannam ut interficerent eam.
      EN: When the people came to her husband Joakim, the two elders also came, full of a wicked plan against Susanna, to put her to death.

1:29  LA: Et dixerunt coram populo : Mittite ad Susannam filiam Helciæ uxorem Joakim. Et statim miserunt.
      EN: They said before the people, “Send for Susanna, daughter of Hilkiah, the wife of Joakim.” At once they sent for her.

1:30  LA: Et venit cum parentibus, et filiis, et universis cognatis suis.
      EN: She came with her parents, children, and all her relatives.

1:31  LA: Porro Susanna erat delicata nimis, et pulchra specie.
      EN: Susanna was very delicate and beautiful in appearance.

1:32  LA: At iniqui illi jusserunt ut discooperiretur (erat enim cooperta), ut vel sic satiarentur decore ejus.
      EN: Those wicked men ordered her to be unveiled, for she was veiled, so that even in this way they might satisfy themselves with her beauty.

1:33  LA: Flebant igitur sui, et omnes qui noverant eam.
      EN: Her family and all who knew her were weeping.

1:34  LA: Consurgentes autem duo presbyteri in medio populi, posuerunt manus suas super caput ejus.
      EN: The two elders rose in the midst of the people and laid their hands on her head.

1:35  LA: Quæ flens suspexit ad cælum : erat enim cor ejus fiduciam habens in Domino.
      EN: Weeping, she looked up to heaven, for her heart trusted in the Lord.

1:35a  LA: Exclamavit autem voce magna Susanna, et dixit : Deus æterne, qui absconditorum es cognitor, qui nosti omnia antequam fiant,
       EN: But Susanna cried out with a loud voice and said, “Eternal God, who know hidden things, who know everything before it happens,

1:36  LA: Et dixerunt presbyteri : Cum deambularemus in pomario soli, ingressa est hæc cum duabus puellis : et clausit ostia pomarii, et dimisit a se puellas.
      EN: The elders said, “As we were walking alone in the orchard, this woman entered with two servant girls. She closed the doors of the orchard and sent the servant girls away.

1:37  LA: Venitque ad eam adolescens, qui erat absconditus, et concubuit cum ea.
      EN: A young man who had been hiding came to her and lay with her.

1:38  LA: Porro nos cum essemus in angulo pomarii, videntes iniquitatem, cucurrimus ad eos, et vidimus eos pariter commisceri.
      EN: We were in a corner of the orchard. When we saw the wickedness, we ran toward them and saw them lying together.

1:39  LA: Et illum quidem non quivimus comprehendere, quia fortior nobis erat, et apertis ostiis exilivit :
      EN: We could not seize him, because he was stronger than we were. He opened the doors and sprang out.

1:40  LA: hanc autem cum apprehendissemus, interrogavimus, quisnam esset adolescens, et noluit indicare nobis : hujus rei testes sumus.
      EN: But we seized this woman and asked who the young man was, and she refused to tell us. We are witnesses of this.”

1:41  LA: Credidit eis multitudo quasi senibus et judicibus populi, et condemnaverunt eam ad mortem.
      EN: The crowd believed them as elders and judges of the people, and they condemned her to death.

1:42  LA: Porro illos, qui perditionis ejus causa fuerant, intromisit in lacum, et devorati sunt in momento coram eo.
      EN: But those who had caused his intended destruction he threw into the den, and they were devoured in a moment before him.

1:44  LA: Exaudivit autem Dominus vocem ejus.
      EN: The Lord heard her voice.

1:45  LA: Exaudivit autem Dominus vocem ejus.
      EN: The Lord heard her voice.

1:48  LA: Qui cum staret in medio eorum, ait : Sic fatui filii Israël, non judicantes, neque quod verum est cognoscentes, condemnastis filiam Israël ?
      EN: Standing in their midst, he said, “Are you so foolish, children of Israel, that without examining the case or learning the truth you have condemned a daughter of Israel?

1:51  LA: Et dixit ad eos Daniel : Separate illos ab invicem procul, et dijudicabo eos.
      EN: Daniel said to them, “Separate them far from one another, and I will examine them.”

1:52  LA: Cum ergo divisi essent alter ab altero, vocavit unum de eis, et dixit ad eum : Inveterate dierum malorum, nunc venerunt peccata tua, quæ operabaris prius :
      EN: When they had been separated from one another, he called one of them and said to him, “You who have grown old in evil days, now your sins have come upon you, the sins you committed before:

1:53  LA: judicans judicia injusta, innocentes opprimens, et dimittens noxios, dicente Domino : Innocentem et justum non interficies.
      EN: rendering unjust judgments, oppressing the innocent, and releasing the guilty, although the Lord says, ‘You shall not kill the innocent and the just.’

1:54  LA: Nunc ergo, si vidisti eam, dic sub qua arbore videris eos colloquentes sibi. Qui ait : Sub schino.
      EN: Now, then, if you saw her, tell me under what tree you saw them conversing together.” He said, “Under a mastic tree.”

1:55  LA: Dixit autem Daniel : Recte mentitus es in caput tuum : ecce enim angelus Dei, accepta sententia ab eo, scindet te medium.
      EN: Daniel said, “You have told a fine lie against your own head! For behold, the angel of God has received His sentence and will split you in two.”

1:56  LA: Et, amoto eo, jussit venire alium, et dixit ei : Semen Chanaan, et non Juda, species decepit te, et concupiscentia subvertit cor tuum :
      EN: After sending him away, he ordered the other to come and said to him, “Offspring of Canaan and not of Judah, beauty has deceived you and desire has perverted your heart.

1:57  LA: sic faciebatis filiabus Israël, et illæ timentes loquebantur vobis : sed filia Juda non sustinuit iniquitatem vestram.
      EN: This is how you treated the daughters of Israel, and in fear they would talk with you. But a daughter of Judah has not tolerated your wickedness.

1:58  LA: Nunc ergo, dic mihi sub qua arbore comprehenderis eos loquentes sibi. Qui ait : Sub prino.
      EN: Now, then, tell me under what tree you caught them conversing together.” He said, “Under a holm oak.”

1:59  LA: Dixit autem ei Daniel : Recte mentitus es et tu in caput tuum : manet enim angelus Domini, gladium habens, ut secet te medium, et interficiat vos.
      EN: Daniel said to him, “You too have told a fine lie against your own head! For the angel of the Lord waits with a sword to cut you in two and destroy you both.”

1:60  LA: Exclamavit itaque omnis cœtus voce magna, et benedixerunt Deum, qui salvat sperantes in se.
      EN: The whole assembly cried out with a loud voice and blessed God, who saves those who hope in Him.

1:61  LA: Exclamavit itaque omnis cœtus voce magna, et benedixerunt Deum, qui salvat sperantes in se.
      EN: The whole assembly cried out with a loud voice and blessed God, who saves those who hope in Him.

1:62  LA: Exclamavit itaque omnis cœtus voce magna, et benedixerunt Deum, qui salvat sperantes in se.
      EN: The whole assembly cried out with a loud voice and blessed God, who saves those who hope in Him.

DANIEL 2

2:1  LA: In anno secundo regni Nabuchodonosor, vidit Nabuchodonosor somnium, et conterritus est spiritus ejus, et somnium ejus fugit ab eo.
     EN: In the second year of Nebuchadnezzar’s reign, Nebuchadnezzar had a dream. His spirit was terrified, and his dream escaped him.

2:2  LA: Præcepit autem rex ut convocarentur arioli, et magi, et malefici, et Chaldæi, ut indicarent regi somnia sua. Qui cum venissent, steterunt coram rege.
     EN: The king ordered the diviners, magicians, sorcerers, and Chaldeans to be summoned to tell the king his dreams. They came and stood before the king.

2:3  LA: Et dixit ad eos rex : Vidi somnium, et mente confusus ignoro quid viderim.
     EN: The king said to them, “I have had a dream, and my mind is so confused that I do not know what I saw.”

2:4  LA: Responderuntque Chaldæi regi syriace : Rex, in sempiternum vive ! dic somnium servis tuis, et interpretationem ejus indicabimus.
     EN: The Chaldeans answered the king in Syriac, “O king, live forever! Tell your servants the dream, and we will explain its meaning.”

2:5  LA: Et respondens rex ait Chaldæis : Sermo recessit a me : nisi indicaveritis mihi somnium, et conjecturam ejus, peribitis vos, et domus vestræ publicabuntur.
     EN: The king answered the Chaldeans, “The matter has escaped me. Unless you tell me the dream and its interpretation, you will perish and your houses will be confiscated.

2:6  LA: Si autem somnium, et conjecturam ejus narraveritis, præmia, et dona, et honorem multum accipietis a me. Somnium igitur, et interpretationem ejus indicate mihi.
     EN: But if you recount the dream and its interpretation, you will receive rewards, gifts, and great honor from me. Tell me, then, the dream and its meaning.”

2:7  LA: Responderunt secundo, atque dixerunt : Rex somnium dicat servis suis, et interpretationem illius indicabimus.
     EN: They answered a second time, “Let the king tell his servants the dream, and we will explain its meaning.”

2:8  LA: Respondit rex, et ait : Certe novi quod tempus redimitis, scientes quod recesserit a me sermo.
     EN: The king replied, “I know for certain that you are buying time, because you know that the matter has escaped me.

2:9  LA: Si ergo somnium non indicaveritis mihi, una est de vobis sententia, quod interpretationem quoque fallacem, et deceptione plenam composueritis, ut loquamini mihi donec tempus pertranseat. Somnium itaque dicite mihi, ut sciam quod interpretationem quoque ejus veram loquamini.
     EN: If you do not tell me the dream, there is only one sentence for you, because you have also prepared a false and deceitful interpretation to tell me until the time passes. Tell me the dream, so that I may know that the interpretation you give is also true.”

2:10  LA: Respondentes ergo Chaldæi coram rege, dixerunt : Non est homo super terram, qui sermonem tuum, rex, possit implere : sed neque regum quisquam magnus et potens verbum hujuscemodi sciscitatur ab omni ariolo, et mago, et Chaldæo.
      EN: The Chaldeans answered before the king, “There is no one on earth who can fulfill your demand, O king. Nor has any king, however great and powerful, asked such a thing of any diviner, magician, or Chaldean.

2:11  LA: Sermo enim, quem tu quæris, rex, gravis est : nec reperietur quisquam qui indicet illum in conspectu regis, exceptis diis, quorum non est cum hominibus conversatio.
      EN: The thing you ask, O king, is difficult. No one can be found to tell it before the king except the gods, whose dwelling is not among mortals.”

2:12  LA: Quo audito, rex, in furore et in ira magna, præcepit ut perirent omnes sapientes Babylonis.
      EN: Hearing this, the king, in fury and great anger, ordered all the wise men of Babylon to be put to death.

2:13  LA: Et egressa sententia, sapientes interficiebantur : quærebanturque Daniel et socii ejus, ut perirent.
      EN: The decree went out, and the wise men were being killed. Daniel and his companions were also sought out, to be put to death.

2:14  LA: Tunc Daniel requisivit de lege atque sententia ab Arioch principe militiæ regis, qui egressus fuerat ad interficiendos sapientes Babylonis.
      EN: Then Daniel inquired about the law and sentence from Arioch, commander of the king’s army, who had gone out to kill the wise men of Babylon.

2:15  LA: Et interrogavit eum, qui a rege potestatem acceperat, quam ob causam tam crudelis sententia a facie regis esset egressa. Cum ergo rem indicasset Arioch Danieli,
      EN: He asked this man, who had received authority from the king, why such a cruel sentence had gone out from the king. When Arioch explained the matter to Daniel,

2:16  LA: Daniel ingressus rogavit regem ut tempus daret sibi ad solutionem indicandam regi.
      EN: Daniel went in and asked the king to give him time to make the explanation known to the king.

2:17  LA: Et ingressus est domum suam, Ananiæque et Misaëli et Azariæ, sociis suis, indicavit negotium,
      EN: He went home and told the matter to his companions Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah,

2:18  LA: ut quærerent misericordiam a facie Dei cæli super sacramento isto, et non perirent Daniel et socii ejus cum ceteris sapientibus Babylonis.
      EN: so that they might seek mercy from the God of heaven concerning this mystery, and Daniel and his companions might not perish with the rest of the wise men of Babylon.

2:19  LA: Tunc Danieli mysterium per visionem nocte revelatum est : et benedixit Daniel Deum cæli,
      EN: Then the mystery was revealed to Daniel in a vision during the night, and Daniel blessed the God of heaven.

2:20  LA: et locutus ait : Sit nomen Domini benedictum a sæculo et usque in sæculum : quia sapientia et fortitudo ejus sunt.
      EN: He said, “May the name of the Lord be blessed from age to age, for wisdom and power belong to Him.

2:21  LA: Et ipse mutat tempora, et ætates : transfert regna, atque constituit : dat sapientiam sapientibus, et scientiam intelligentibus disciplinam.
      EN: He changes times and seasons; He removes kingdoms and establishes them. He gives wisdom to the wise and knowledge to those who understand learning.

2:22  LA: Ipse revelat profunda et abscondita, et novit in tenebris constituta : et lux cum eo est.
      EN: He reveals deep and hidden things and knows what lies in darkness, and light is with Him.

2:23  LA: Tibi, Deus patrum nostrorum, confiteor, teque laudo, quia sapientiam et fortitudinem dedisti mihi, et nunc ostendisti mihi quæ rogavimus te, quia sermonem regis aperuisti nobis.
      EN: I give You thanks and praise You, God of our fathers, because You have given me wisdom and power. Now You have shown me what we asked of You, for You have disclosed the king’s matter to us.”

2:24  LA: Post hæc Daniel ingressus ad Arioch, quem constituerat rex ut perderet sapientes Babylonis, sic ei locutus est : Sapientes Babylonis ne perdas : introduc me in conspectu regis, et solutionem regi narrabo.
      EN: After this Daniel went to Arioch, whom the king had appointed to destroy the wise men of Babylon, and said to him, “Do not destroy the wise men of Babylon. Bring me before the king, and I will tell the king the explanation.”

2:25  LA: Tunc Arioch festinus introduxit Danielem ad regem, et dixit ei : Inveni hominem de filiis transmigrationis Juda, qui solutionem regi annuntiet.
      EN: Arioch quickly brought Daniel to the king and said to him, “I have found a man among the exiles of Judah who will make the explanation known to the king.”

2:26  LA: Respondit rex, et dixit Danieli, cujus nomen erat Baltassar : Putasne vere potes mihi indicare somnium, quod vidi, et interpretationem ejus ?
      EN: The king said to Daniel, whose name was Belteshazzar, “Can you really tell me the dream I saw and its meaning?”

2:27  LA: Et respondens Daniel coram rege, ait : Mysterium, quod rex interrogat, sapientes, magi, arioli, et aruspices nequeunt indicare regi :
      EN: Daniel answered before the king, “The mystery about which the king asks cannot be told to the king by wise men, magicians, diviners, or soothsayers.

2:28  LA: sed est Deus in cælo revelans mysteria, qui indicavit tibi, rex Nabuchodonosor, quæ ventura sunt in novissimis temporibus. Somnium tuum, et visiones capitis tui in cubili tuo hujuscemodi sunt.
      EN: But there is a God in heaven who reveals mysteries. He has shown you, King Nebuchadnezzar, what will happen in the last days. Your dream and the visions in your head as you lay on your bed were these:

2:29  LA: Tu, rex, cogitare cœpisti in strato tuo, quid esset futurum post hæc : et qui revelat mysteria, ostendit tibi quæ ventura sunt.
      EN: As you lay on your bed, O king, you began to think about what would happen afterward, and He who reveals mysteries showed you what is to come.

2:30  LA: Mihi quoque non in sapientia, quæ est in me plus quam in cunctis viventibus, sacramentum hoc revelatum est : sed ut interpretatio regi manifesta fieret, et cogitationes mentis tuæ scires.
      EN: This mystery was revealed to me because the interpretation was to be made known to the king and you were to understand the thoughts of your mind, not because I have greater wisdom than all other living people.

2:31  LA: Tu, rex, videbas, et ecce quasi statua una grandis : statua illa magna, et statura sublimis stabat contra te, et intuitus ejus erat terribilis.
      EN: You were looking, O king, and there appeared a single great statue. That statue was immense and exceedingly tall. It stood before you, and its appearance was terrifying.

2:32  LA: Hujus statuæ caput ex auro optimo erat, pectus autem et brachia de argento, porro venter et femora ex ære,
      EN: The statue’s head was of the finest gold, its chest and arms of silver, its belly and thighs of bronze,

2:33  LA: tibiæ autem ferreæ : pedum quædam pars erat ferrea, quædam autem fictilis.
      EN: and its legs of iron. Its feet were partly iron and partly earthenware.

2:34  LA: Videbas ita, donec abscissus est lapis de monte sine manibus : et percussit statuam in pedibus ejus ferreis et fictilibus, et comminuit eos.
      EN: You watched until a stone was cut from a mountain without hands. It struck the statue on its feet of iron and earthenware and shattered them.

2:35  LA: Tunc contrita sunt pariter ferrum, testa, æs, argentum, et aurum, et redacta quasi in favillam æstivæ areæ, quæ rapta sunt vento, nullusque locus inventus est eis : lapis autem, qui percusserat statuam, factus est mons magnus, et implevit universam terram.
      EN: Then the iron, earthenware, bronze, silver, and gold were crushed together and became like dust from a summer threshing floor, carried away by the wind until no place was found for them. But the stone that struck the statue became a great mountain and filled the whole earth.

2:36  LA: Hoc est somnium : interpretationem quoque ejus dicemus coram te, rex.
      EN: This is the dream. We will also tell its interpretation before you, O king.

2:37  LA: Tu rex regum es : et Deus cæli regnum, et fortitudinem, et imperium, et gloriam dedit tibi :
      EN: You are king of kings, and the God of heaven has given you the kingdom, power, dominion, and glory.

2:38  LA: et omnia, in quibus habitant filii hominum, et bestiæ agri : volucres quoque cæli dedit in manu tua, et sub ditione tua universa constituit : tu es ergo caput aureum.
      EN: He has given into your hand every place where human beings and the beasts of the field dwell, as well as the birds of the sky, and has placed everything under your rule. You, then, are the head of gold.

2:39  LA: Et post te consurget regnum aliud minus te argenteum : et regnum tertium aliud æreum, quod imperabit universæ terræ.
      EN: After you another kingdom will arise, of silver and inferior to yours; then a third kingdom, of bronze, which will rule over the whole earth.

2:40  LA: Et regnum quartum erit velut ferrum : quomodo ferrum comminuit, et domat omnia, sic comminuet, et conteret omnia hæc.
      EN: A fourth kingdom will be like iron. Just as iron crushes and subdues everything, so it will crush and shatter all these things.

2:41  LA: Porro quia vidisti pedum, et digitorum partem testæ figuli, et partem ferream, regnum divisum erit : quod tamen de plantario ferri orietur, secundum quod vidisti ferrum mistum testæ ex luto.
      EN: Because you saw the feet and toes partly of potter’s earthenware and partly of iron, the kingdom will be divided, though it will spring from a rootstock of iron, as you saw iron mixed with clay earthenware.

2:42  LA: Et digitos pedum ex parte ferreos, et ex parte fictiles : ex parte regnum erit solidum, et ex parte contritum.
      EN: The toes of the feet were partly iron and partly earthenware: the kingdom will be partly strong and partly broken.

2:43  LA: Quod autem vidisti ferrum mistum testæ ex luto, commiscebuntur quidem humano semine, sed non adhærebunt sibi, sicut ferrum misceri non potest testæ.
      EN: As you saw iron mixed with clay earthenware, they will indeed mingle through human offspring, but they will not hold together, just as iron cannot mix with earthenware.

2:44  LA: In diebus autem regnorum illorum suscitabit Deus cæli regnum, quod in æternum non dissipabitur, et regnum ejus alteri populo non tradetur : comminuet autem, et consumet universa regna hæc, et ipsum stabit in æternum.
      EN: In the days of those kingdoms the God of heaven will raise up a kingdom that will never be destroyed, and His kingdom will not be handed over to another people. It will crush and consume all these kingdoms, but it will itself stand forever.

2:45  LA: Secundum quod vidisti, quod de monte abscissus est lapis sine manibus, et comminuit testam, et ferrum, et æs, et argentum, et aurum, Deus magnus ostendit regi quæ ventura sunt postea : et verum est somnium, et fidelis interpretatio ejus.
      EN: As you saw a stone cut from the mountain without hands, which crushed the earthenware, iron, bronze, silver, and gold, the great God has shown the king what is to happen afterward. The dream is true, and its interpretation is trustworthy.”

2:46  LA: Tunc rex Nabuchodonosor cecidit in faciem suam, et Danielem adoravit, et hostias, et incensum præcepit ut sacrificarent ei.
      EN: Then King Nebuchadnezzar fell on his face and paid homage to Daniel, and he ordered sacrifices and incense to be offered to him.

2:47  LA: Loquens ergo rex, ait Danieli : Vere Deus vester Deus deorum est, et Dominus regum, et revelans mysteria : quoniam tu potuisti aperire hoc sacramentum.
      EN: The king said to Daniel, “Truly your God is the God of gods and Lord of kings, and a revealer of mysteries, since you have been able to disclose this mystery.”

2:48  LA: Tunc rex Danielem in sublime extulit, et munera multa et magna dedit ei : et constituit eum principem super omnes provincias Babylonis, et præfectum magistratuum super cunctos sapientes Babylonis.
      EN: Then the king raised Daniel to high rank and gave him many great gifts. He appointed him ruler over all the provinces of Babylon and chief of the officials over all the wise men of Babylon.

2:49  LA: Daniel autem postulavit a rege, et constituit super opera provinciæ Babylonis Sidrach, Misach, et Abdenago : ipse autem Daniel erat in foribus regis.
      EN: At Daniel’s request, the king appointed Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego over the affairs of the province of Babylon. Daniel himself remained at the king’s court.

DANIEL 3

3:1  LA: Nabuchodonosor rex fecit statuam auream, altitudine cubitorum sexaginta, latitudine cubitorum sex, et statuit eam in campo Dura, provinciæ Babylonis.
     EN: King Nebuchadnezzar made a golden statue, sixty cubits high and six cubits wide, and set it up on the plain of Dura in the province of Babylon.

3:2  LA: Itaque Nabuchodonosor rex misit ad congregandos satrapas, magistratus, et judices, duces, et tyrannos, et præfectos, omnesque principes regionum, ut convenirent ad dedicationem statuæ quam erexerat Nabuchodonosor rex.
     EN: King Nebuchadnezzar sent word to gather the satraps, magistrates, judges, commanders, rulers, governors, and all the provincial officials, so that they might come to the dedication of the statue King Nebuchadnezzar had erected.

3:3  LA: Tunc congregati sunt satrapæ, magistratus, et judices, duces, et tyranni, et optimates, qui erant in potestatibus constituti, et universi principes regionum, ut convenirent ad dedicationem statuæ, quam erexerat Nabuchodonosor rex. Stabant autem in conspectu statuæ, quam posuerat Nabuchodonosor rex :
     EN: Then the satraps, magistrates, judges, commanders, rulers, nobles who held authority, and all the provincial officials gathered for the dedication of the statue King Nebuchadnezzar had erected. They stood before the statue King Nebuchadnezzar had set up.

3:4  LA: et præco clamabat valenter : Vobis dicitur populis, tribubus, et linguis :
     EN: A herald cried out loudly, “This command is given to you, peoples, tribes, and languages:

3:5  LA: in hora qua audieritis sonitum tubæ, et fistulæ, et citharæ, sambucæ, et psalterii, et symphoniæ, et universi generis musicorum, cadentes adorate statuam auream, quam constituit Nabuchodonosor rex.
     EN: At the moment you hear the sound of the trumpet, pipe, lyre, triangular harp, psaltery, ensemble, and every kind of musical instrument, fall down and worship the golden statue King Nebuchadnezzar has set up.

3:6  LA: Si quis autem non prostratus adoraverit, eadem hora mittetur in fornacem ignis ardentis.
     EN: Whoever does not fall down and worship will immediately be thrown into a furnace of blazing fire.”

3:7  LA: Post hæc igitur, statim ut audierunt omnes populi sonitum tubæ, fistulæ, et citharæ, sambucæ, et psalterii, et symphoniæ, et omnis generis musicorum, cadentes omnes populi, tribus, et linguæ adoraverunt statuam auream, quam constituerat Nabuchodonosor rex.
     EN: Accordingly, as soon as all the peoples heard the sound of the trumpet, pipe, lyre, triangular harp, psaltery, ensemble, and every kind of musical instrument, all the peoples, tribes, and languages fell down and worshiped the golden statue King Nebuchadnezzar had set up.

3:8  LA: Statimque in ipso tempore accedentes viri Chaldæi accusaverunt Judæos :
     EN: At that very time some Chaldean men came forward and accused the Jews.

3:9  LA: dixeruntque Nabuchodonosor regi : Rex, in æternum vive !
     EN: They said to King Nebuchadnezzar, “O king, live forever!

3:10  LA: tu, rex, posuisti decretum, ut omnis homo, qui audierit sonitum tubæ, fistulæ, et citharæ, sambucæ, et psalterii, et symphoniæ, et universi generis musicorum, prosternat se, et adoret statuam auream :
      EN: You, O king, issued a decree that everyone who heard the sound of the trumpet, pipe, lyre, triangular harp, psaltery, ensemble, and every kind of musical instrument should fall down and worship the golden statue,

3:11  LA: si quis autem non procidens adoraverit, mittatur in fornacem ignis ardentis.
      EN: and that whoever did not fall down and worship should be thrown into a furnace of blazing fire.

3:12  LA: Sunt ergo viri Judæi, quos constituisti super opera regionis Babylonis, Sidrach, Misach, et Abdenago : viri isti contempserunt, rex, decretum tuum : deos tuos non colunt, et statuam auream, quam erexisti, non adorant.
      EN: But there are Jewish men whom you appointed over the affairs of the province of Babylon: Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. These men have disregarded your decree, O king. They do not serve your gods or worship the golden statue you erected.”

3:13  LA: Tunc Nabuchodonosor, in furore et in ira, præcepit ut adducerentur Sidrach, Misach, et Abdenago : qui confestim adducti sunt in conspectu regis.
      EN: Then Nebuchadnezzar, in fury and anger, ordered Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego to be brought in. They were immediately brought before the king.

3:14  LA: Pronuntiansque Nabuchodonosor rex, ait eis : Verene Sidrach, Misach, et Abdenago, deos meos non colitis, et statuam auream, quam constitui, non adoratis ?
      EN: King Nebuchadnezzar addressed them: “Is it true, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, that you do not serve my gods or worship the golden statue I have set up?

3:15  LA: nunc ergo si estis parati, quacumque hora audieritis sonitum tubæ, fistulæ, citharæ, sambucæ, et psalterii, et symphoniæ, omnisque generis musicorum, prosternite vos, et adorate statuam, quam feci : quod si non adoraveritis, eadem hora mittemini in fornacem ignis ardentis : et quis est Deus, qui eripiet vos de manu mea ?
      EN: Now, then, if you are ready, at whatever moment you hear the sound of the trumpet, pipe, lyre, triangular harp, psaltery, ensemble, and every kind of musical instrument, fall down and worship the statue I have made. But if you do not worship, you will immediately be thrown into a furnace of blazing fire. And who is the God who will rescue you from my hand?”

3:16  LA: Respondentes Sidrach, Misach, et Abdenago, dixerunt regi Nabuchodonosor : Non oportet nos de hac re respondere tibi.
      EN: Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego answered King Nebuchadnezzar, “We do not need to answer you about this matter.

3:17  LA: Ecce enim Deus noster, quem colimus, potest eripere nos de camino ignis ardentis, et de manibus tuis, o rex, liberare.
      EN: For behold, our God, whom we serve, can rescue us from the furnace of blazing fire and deliver us from your hands, O king.

3:18  LA: Quod si noluerit, notum sit tibi, rex, quia deos tuos non colimus, et statuam auream, quam erexisti, non adoramus.
      EN: But if He is unwilling, let it be known to you, O king, that we will not serve your gods or worship the golden statue you have erected.”

3:19  LA: Tunc Nabuchodonosor repletus est furore, et aspectus faciei illius immutatus est super Sidrach, Misach, et Abdenago : et præcepit ut succenderetur fornax septuplum quam succendi consueverat.
      EN: Then Nebuchadnezzar was filled with fury, and the expression on his face changed toward Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. He ordered the furnace to be heated seven times hotter than usual.

3:20  LA: Et viris fortissimis de exercitu suo jussit ut ligatis pedibus Sidrach, Misach, et Abdenago, mitterent eos in fornacem ignis ardentis.
      EN: He commanded the strongest men in his army to bind the feet of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego and throw them into the furnace of blazing fire.

3:21  LA: Et confestim viri illi vincti, cum braccis suis, et tiaris, et calceamentis, et vestibus, missi sunt in medium fornacis ignis ardentis :
      EN: At once those men were bound and thrown into the midst of the furnace of blazing fire, with their trousers, turbans, shoes, and other clothing,

3:22  LA: nam jussio regis urgebat. Fornax autem succensa erat nimis : porro viros illos, qui miserant Sidrach, Misach, et Abdenago, interfecit flamma ignis.
      EN: for the king’s command was urgent. The furnace had been heated beyond measure, and the fiery flame killed the men who threw in Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego.

3:23  LA: Viri autem hi tres, id est, Sidrach, Misach, et Abdenago, ceciderunt in medio camino ignis ardentis, colligati.
      EN: These three men, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, fell bound into the midst of the furnace of blazing fire.

      EN: They walked about in the midst of the flame, praising God and blessing the Lord.

      EN: Azariah stood and prayed in this way, opening his mouth in the midst of the fire and saying:

      EN: “Blessed are You, Lord, God of our fathers, and Your name is worthy of praise and glory forever.

      EN: For You are just in everything You have done to us. All Your works are true, Your ways upright, and all Your judgments true.

3:28  LA: Judicia enim vera fecisti juxta omnia, quæ induxisti super nos, et super civitatem sanctam patrum nostrorum Jerusalem : quia in veritate et in judicio induxisti omnia hæc propter peccata nostra.
      EN: You have rendered true judgments in everything You brought upon us and upon Jerusalem, the holy city of our fathers. For in truth and justice You brought all this upon us because of our sins.

3:29  LA: Peccavimus enim, et inique egimus recedentes a te, et deliquimus in omnibus :
      EN: For we have sinned and acted wickedly by departing from You; we have done wrong in everything.

      EN: We have neither listened to Your commandments nor observed them, nor have we done as You commanded us so that it might go well with us.

3:31  LA: Omnia ergo, quæ induxisti super nos, et universa quæ fecisti nobis, in vero judicio fecisti ;
      EN: Everything You have brought upon us, then, and everything You have done to us, You have done in true judgment.

      EN: You have delivered us into the hands of our enemies, wicked, utterly evil, and faithless, and into the hands of an unjust king, more evil than any in all the earth.

      EN: As with burnt offerings of rams and bulls, and with thousands of fat lambs, so may our sacrifice be before You today, that it may please You, for those who trust in You are not put to shame.

DANIEL 7

7:2  LA: Videbam in visione mea nocte : et ecce quatuor venti cæli pugnabant in mari magno.

DANIEL 8

8:1  LA: Anno tertio regni Baltassar regis, visio apparuit mihi. Ego Daniel, post id quod videram in principio,

     EN: I saw the ram thrusting its horns toward the west, the north, and the south. No beasts could resist it or escape from its power. It did as it pleased and became great.

8:6  LA: Et venit usque ad arietem illum cornutum, quem videram stantem ante portam, et cucurrit ad eum in impetu fortitudinis suæ.
     EN: It came to that horned ram I had seen standing before the gate and rushed at it with all the force of its strength.

8:7  LA: Cumque appropinquasset prope arietem, efferatus est in eum, et percussit arietem : et comminuit duo cornua ejus, et non poterat aries resistere ei : cumque eum misisset in terram, conculcavit, et nemo quibat liberare arietem de manu ejus.
     EN: As it drew near the ram, it became savage toward it and struck the ram, shattering its two horns. The ram could not resist it. The goat threw it to the ground and trampled it, and no one could rescue the ram from its power.

8:8  LA: Hircus autem caprarum magnus factus est nimis : cumque crevisset, fractum est cornu magnum, et orta sunt quatuor cornua subter illud per quatuor ventos cæli.
     EN: The male goat became exceedingly great, but when it had grown strong, the great horn was broken, and four horns arose beneath it toward the four winds of heaven.

      EN: It grew great as far as the forces of heaven. It cast down some of those forces and some of the stars and trampled them.

8:11  LA: Et usque ad principem fortitudinis magnificatum est : et ab eo tulit juge sacrificium, et dejecit locum sanctificationis ejus.
      EN: It grew great even against the commander of those forces. It took the continual sacrifice away from him and cast down the place of his sanctuary.

8:12  LA: Robur autem datum est ei contra juge sacrificium propter peccata : et prosternetur veritas in terra, et faciet, et prosperabitur.
      EN: Strength was given to it against the continual sacrifice because of sins. Truth will be cast down to the earth, and it will act and prosper.

      EN: I heard one of the holy ones speaking, and one holy one said to another who was speaking—I do not know who—‘How long will the vision last, concerning the continual sacrifice and the sin of desolation that has occurred? How long will the sanctuary and the forces be trampled?’

      EN: He said to him, ‘Until evening and morning, two thousand three hundred days; then the sanctuary will be cleansed.’

      EN: As I, Daniel, saw the vision and sought understanding, behold, something like the form of a man stood before me.

      EN: I heard a man’s voice from amid Ulai, calling out, ‘Gabriel, make this vision understood.’

      EN: The ram you saw with horns is the king of the Medes and Persians.

      EN: His strength will grow mighty, though not by his own power. Beyond belief he will lay everything waste; he will prosper and accomplish his purpose. He will kill the mighty and the people of the holy ones

8:25  LA: secundum voluntatem suam, et dirigetur dolus in manu ejus : et cor suum magnificabit, et in copia rerum omnium occidet plurimos : et contra principem principum consurget, et sine manu conteretur.
      EN: according to his will, and deceit will succeed in his hand. He will exalt himself in his heart and kill very many amid an abundance of all things. He will rise against the prince of princes and be crushed without a hand being raised.

8:26  LA: Et visio vespere et mane, quæ dicta est, vera est : tu ergo visionem signa, quia post multos dies erit.
      EN: The vision of evening and morning that was spoken is true. Seal the vision, therefore, because it concerns many days to come.’

DANIEL 9

9:3  LA: Et posui faciem meam ad Dominum Deum meum rogare et deprecari in jejuniis, sacco, et cinere.
     EN: I turned my face to the Lord my God, to seek Him and implore Him with fasting, sackcloth, and ashes.

9:8  LA: Domine, nobis confusio faciei, regibus nostris, principibus nostris, et patribus nostris, qui peccaverunt.
     EN: Lord, shame of face belongs to us, to our kings, our rulers, and our fathers, who have sinned.

9:9  LA: Tibi autem Domino Deo nostro misericordia et propitiatio, quia recessimus a te,
     EN: But mercy and forgiveness belong to You, the Lord our God, for we have departed from You

9:10  LA: et non audivimus vocem Domini Dei nostri ut ambularemus in lege ejus, quam posuit nobis per servos suos prophetas.
      EN: and have not listened to the voice of the Lord our God, to walk in His law, which He set before us through His servants the prophets.

9:11  LA: Et omnis Israël prævaricati sunt legem tuam, et declinaverunt ne audirent vocem tuam : et stillavit super nos maledictio et detestatio quæ scripta est in libro Moysi servi Dei, quia peccavimus ei.
      EN: All Israel has transgressed Your law and turned aside rather than listen to Your voice. The curse and imprecation written in the book of Moses, the servant of God, have poured down upon us, because we have sinned against Him.

9:12  LA: Et statuit sermones suos, quos locutus est super nos et super principes nostros, qui judicaverunt nos, ut superinduceret in nos magnum malum, quale numquam fuit sub omni cælo, secundum quod factum est in Jerusalem.
      EN: He has fulfilled the words He spoke against us and against our rulers who judged us, bringing upon us a great calamity. Nowhere under all heaven has anything happened like what has happened in Jerusalem.

9:13  LA: Sicut scriptum est in lege Moysi, omne malum hoc venit super nos : et non rogavimus faciem tuam, Domine Deus noster, ut reverteremur ab iniquitatibus nostris, et cogitaremus veritatem tuam.
      EN: Just as it is written in the law of Moses, all this calamity has come upon us. Yet we have not sought Your favor, Lord our God, by turning back from our iniquities and considering Your truth.

9:14  LA: Et vigilavit Dominus super malitiam, et adduxit eam super nos. Justus Dominus Deus noster in omnibus operibus suis, quæ fecit : non enim audivimus vocem ejus.
      EN: The Lord watched over the calamity and brought it upon us. The Lord our God is just in all the works He has done, for we have not listened to His voice.

9:15  LA: Et nunc Domine Deus noster, qui eduxisti populum tuum de terra Ægypti in manu forti, et fecisti tibi nomen secundum diem hanc : peccavimus, iniquitatem fecimus.
      EN: Now, Lord our God, who brought Your people out of the land of Egypt with a mighty hand and made for Yourself a name that endures to this day: we have sinned, we have committed iniquity.

9:16  LA: Domine, in omnem justitiam tuam avertatur, obsecro, ira tua et furor tuus a civitate tua Jerusalem, et monte sancto tuo. Propter peccata enim nostra, et iniquitates patrum nostrorum, Jerusalem et populus tuus in opprobrium sunt omnibus per circuitum nostrum.
      EN: Lord, in accordance with all Your justice, I beg You, let Your anger and fury turn away from Your city Jerusalem and Your holy mountain. For because of our sins and the iniquities of our fathers, Jerusalem and Your people have become a reproach to all those around us.

9:17  LA: Nunc ergo exaudi, Deus noster, orationem servi tui, et preces ejus : et ostende faciem tuam super sanctuarium tuum, quod desertum est propter temetipsum.
      EN: Now, therefore, our God, hear the prayer and supplications of Your servant, and for Your own sake show Your face over Your desolate sanctuary.

9:18  LA: Inclina, Deus meus, aurem tuam, et audi : aperi oculos tuos, et vide desolationem nostram, et civitatem super quam invocatum est nomen tuum : neque enim in justificationibus nostris prosternimus preces ante faciem tuam, sed in miserationibus tuis multis.
      EN: Incline Your ear, my God, and hear; open Your eyes and see our desolation and the city over which Your name is invoked. For we lay our prayers before You on the strength of Your many mercies, not on the strength of our righteous deeds.

9:19  LA: Exaudi, Domine ; placare Domine : attende et fac : ne moreris propter temetipsum, Deus meus, quia nomen tuum invocatum est super civitatem et super populum tuum.
      EN: Hear, Lord. Be appeased, Lord. Listen and act. Do not delay, for Your own sake, my God, because Your name is invoked over Your city and Your people.”

9:20  LA: Cumque adhuc loquerer, et orarem, et confiterer peccata mea, et peccata populi mei Israël, et prosternerem preces meas in conspectu Dei mei, pro monte sancto Dei mei :
      EN: While I was still speaking and praying, confessing my sins and the sins of my people Israel, and laying my prayers before my God for the holy mountain of my God,

9:21  LA: adhuc me loquente in oratione, ecce vir Gabriel, quem videram in visione a principio, cito volans tetigit me in tempore sacrificii vespertini.
      EN: while I was still speaking in prayer, behold, the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision at the beginning, flew swiftly and touched me at the time of the evening sacrifice.

9:22  LA: Et docuit me, et locutus est mihi, dixitque : Daniel, nunc egressus sum ut docerem te, et intelligeres.
      EN: He instructed me, speaking to me in these words: “Daniel, I have now come forth to teach you, so that you may understand.

9:23  LA: Ab exordio precum tuarum egressus est sermo : ego autem veni ut indicarem tibi, quia vir desideriorum es : tu ergo animadverte sermonem, et intellige visionem.
      EN: At the beginning of your prayers the word went out, and I have come to tell you, because you are a man greatly beloved. Attend, then, to the word and understand the vision.

9:24  LA: Septuaginta hebdomades abbreviatæ sunt super populum tuum et super urbem sanctam tuam, ut consummetur prævaricatio, et finem accipiat peccatum, et deleatur iniquitas, et adducatur justitia sempiterna, et impleatur visio et prophetia, et ungatur Sanctus sanctorum.
      EN: Seventy weeks have been shortened for your people and your holy city, so that transgression may be brought to completion, sin come to an end, iniquity be wiped away, everlasting justice be brought in, vision and prophecy be fulfilled, and the Most Holy One be anointed.

9:25  LA: Scito ergo, et animadverte : ab exitu sermonis, ut iterum ædificetur Jerusalem, usque ad christum ducem, hebdomades septem, et hebdomades sexaginta duæ erunt : et rursum ædificabitur platea, et muri in angustia temporum.
      EN: Know, then, and understand: from the going forth of the word that Jerusalem is to be rebuilt until an anointed ruler, there will be seven weeks and sixty-two weeks. The street and the walls will be rebuilt in troubled times.

9:26  LA: Et post hebdomades sexaginta duas occidetur christus : et non erit ejus populus qui eum negaturus est. Et civitatem et sanctuarium dissipabit populus cum duce venturo : et finis ejus vastitas, et post finem belli statuta desolatio.
      EN: After the sixty-two weeks an anointed one will be killed, and the people who will deny him will not be his. A people, together with a ruler who is to come, will destroy the city and the sanctuary. Its end will be devastation, and after the end of the war desolation is decreed.

9:27  LA: Confirmabit autem pactum multis hebdomada una : et in dimidio hebdomadis deficiet hostia et sacrificium : et erit in templo abominatio desolationis : et usque ad consummationem et finem perseverabit desolatio.
      EN: He will confirm a covenant with many for one week. In the middle of the week the offering and sacrifice will cease, and the abomination of desolation will be in the temple. Desolation will persist until the consummation and the end.”

DANIEL 10

10:1  LA: Anno tertio Cyri regis Persarum, verbum revelatum est Danieli cognomento Baltassar, et verbum verum, et fortitudo magna : intellexitque sermonem : intelligentia enim est opus in visione.
      EN: In the third year of Cyrus king of Persia, a word was revealed to Daniel, who was also called Belteshazzar. The word was true, and its power great. He understood the message, for understanding is needed in the vision.

10:2  LA: In diebus illis ego Daniel lugebam trium hebdomadarum diebus :
      EN: In those days I, Daniel, was mourning for three full weeks.

10:3  LA: panem desiderabilem non comedi, et caro et vinum non introierunt in os meum, sed neque unguento unctus sum, donec complerentur trium hebdomadarum dies.
      EN: I ate no desirable bread; meat and wine did not enter my mouth, and I did not anoint myself with ointment until the three full weeks were completed.

10:4  LA: Die autem vigesima et quarta mensis primi, eram juxta fluvium magnum, qui est Tigris.
      EN: On the twenty-fourth day of the first month, I was beside the great river, the Tigris.

10:5  LA: Et levavi oculos meos, et vidi : et ecce vir unus vestitus lineis, et renes ejus accincti auro obrizo :
      EN: I lifted my eyes and looked, and behold, a man clothed in linen, with his waist girded with refined gold.

10:6  LA: et corpus ejus quasi chrysolithus, et facies ejus velut species fulguris, et oculi ejus ut lampas ardens : et brachia ejus, et quæ deorsum sunt usque ad pedes, quasi species æris candentis : et vox sermonum ejus ut vox multitudinis.
      EN: His body was like chrysolite, his face like the appearance of lightning, and his eyes like a burning torch. His arms and his lower limbs down to his feet had the appearance of glowing bronze, and the sound of his words was like the sound of a multitude.

10:7  LA: Vidi autem ego Daniel solus visionem : porro viri qui erant mecum non viderunt, sed terror nimius irruit super eos, et fugerunt in absconditum.
      EN: I, Daniel, alone saw the vision. The men who were with me did not see it, but overwhelming terror fell upon them, and they fled into hiding.

10:8  LA: Ego autem relictus solus vidi visionem grandem hanc : et non remansit in me fortitudo, sed et species mea immutata est in me, et emarcui, nec habui quidquam virium.
      EN: Left alone, I saw this great vision. No strength remained in me; my appearance changed, I wasted away, and I had no strength at all.

10:9  LA: Et audivi vocem sermonum ejus : et audiens jacebam consternatus super faciem meam, et vultus meus hærebat terræ.
      EN: I heard the sound of his words, and as I heard it I lay stunned on my face, with my face pressed to the earth.

10:10  LA: Et ecce manus tetigit me, et erexit me super genua mea, et super articulos manuum mearum.
       EN: And behold, a hand touched me and raised me onto my knees and the knuckles of my hands.

10:11  LA: Et dixit ad me : Daniel vir desideriorum, intellige verba quæ ego loquor ad te, et sta in gradu tuo : nunc enim sum missus ad te. Cumque dixisset mihi sermonem istum, steti tremens.
       EN: He said to me, “Daniel, man greatly beloved, understand the words I am speaking to you and stand upright where you are, for I have now been sent to you.” When he said these words to me, I stood trembling.

10:12  LA: Et ait ad me : Noli metuere, Daniel : quia ex die primo, quo posuisti cor tuum ad intelligendum ut te affligeres in conspectu Dei tui, exaudita sunt verba tua : et ego veni propter sermones tuos.
       EN: He said to me, “Do not fear, Daniel, for from the first day you set your heart on understanding and humbled yourself before your God, your words were heard. I have come because of your words.

10:13  LA: Princeps autem regni Persarum restitit mihi viginti et uno diebus : et ecce Michaël, unus de principibus primis, venit in adjutorium meum, et ego remansi ibi juxta regem Persarum.
       EN: But the prince of the kingdom of Persia resisted me for twenty-one days. Then Michael, one of the foremost princes, came to help me, and I remained there beside the king of Persia.

10:14  LA: Veni autem ut docerem te quæ ventura sunt populo tuo in novissimis diebus, quoniam adhuc visio in dies.
       EN: I have come to teach you what will happen to your people in the last days, for the vision still concerns days to come.”

10:15  LA: Cumque loqueretur mihi hujuscemodi verbis, dejeci vultum meum ad terram, et tacui.
       EN: While he was speaking these words to me, I lowered my face to the earth and was silent.

10:16  LA: Et ecce quasi similitudo filii hominis tetigit labia mea : et aperiens os meum locutus sum, et dixi ad eum, qui stabat contra me : Domine mi, in visione tua dissolutæ sunt compages meæ, et nihil in me remansit virium.
       EN: And behold, one in the likeness of a son of man touched my lips. I opened my mouth and spoke, saying to the one who stood before me, “My lord, at your vision my joints gave way, and no strength remained in me.

10:17  LA: Et quomodo poterit servus domini mei loqui cum domino meo ? nihil enim in me remansit virium, sed et halitus meus intercluditur.
       EN: How can my lord’s servant speak with my lord? For no strength remains in me, and even my breath is cut off.”

10:18  LA: Rursum ergo tetigit me quasi visio hominis, et confortavit me,
       EN: Then one in the appearance of a man touched me again and strengthened me.

10:19  LA: et dixit : Noli timere, vir desideriorum : pax tibi : confortare, et esto robustus. Cumque loqueretur mecum, convalui, et dixi : Loquere, domine mi, quia confortasti me.
       EN: He said, “Do not fear, man greatly beloved. Peace be with you; take courage and be strong.” As he spoke with me, I grew strong and said, “Speak, my lord, for you have strengthened me.”

10:20  LA: Et ait : Numquid scis quare venerim ad te ? et nunc revertar ut prælier adversum principem Persarum. Cum ego egrederer, apparuit princeps Græcorum veniens.
       EN: He said, “Do you know why I have come to you? Now I will return to fight against the prince of Persia. As I was going out, the prince of Greece appeared, coming toward me.

10:21  LA: Verumtamen annuntiabo tibi quod expressum est in scriptura veritatis : et nemo est adjutor meus in omnibus his, nisi Michaël princeps vester.
       EN: But I will tell you what is set down in the writing of truth. No one helps me in all these matters except Michael, your prince.

DANIEL 11

11:1  LA: Ego autem ab anno primo Darii Medi stabam ut confortaretur et roboraretur.
      EN: From the first year of Darius the Mede, I stood by to strengthen and support him.

11:2  LA: Et nunc veritatem annuntiabo tibi. Ecce adhuc tres reges stabunt in Perside, et quartus ditabitur opibus nimiis super omnes : et cum invaluerit divitiis suis, concitabit omnes adversum regnum Græciæ.
      EN: Now I will tell you the truth. Behold, three more kings will arise in Persia, and a fourth will become far richer than all of them. When he has grown strong through his riches, he will stir up everyone against the kingdom of Greece.

11:3  LA: Surget vero rex fortis, et dominabitur potestate multa, et faciet quod placuerit ei.
      EN: A mighty king will arise, rule with great power, and do as he pleases.

11:4  LA: Et cum steterit, conteretur regnum ejus, et dividetur in quatuor ventos cæli : sed non in posteros ejus, neque secundum potentiam illius, qua dominatus est : lacerabitur enim regnum ejus etiam in externos, exceptis his.
      EN: When he has arisen, his kingdom will be shattered and divided toward the four winds of heaven. It will not belong to his descendants, nor have the power with which he ruled, for his kingdom will be torn apart and given to outsiders apart from these.

11:5  LA: Et confortabitur rex austri : et de principibus ejus prævalebit super eum, et dominabitur ditione : multa enim dominatio ejus.
      EN: The king of the south will grow strong, but one of his princes will prevail over him and rule with dominion, for his dominion will be great.

11:6  LA: Et post finem annorum fœderabuntur : filiaque regis austri veniet ad regem aquilonis facere amicitiam, et non obtinebit fortitudinem brachii, nec stabit semen ejus : et tradetur ipsa, et qui adduxerunt eam adolescentes ejus, et qui confortabant eam in temporibus.
      EN: After some years they will make an alliance. The daughter of the king of the south will come to the king of the north to establish friendship. But she will not retain the strength of her arm, nor will her offspring endure. She will be handed over, together with the young men who brought her and those who supported her at those times.

11:7  LA: Et stabit de germine radicum ejus plantatio : et veniet cum exercitu, et ingredietur provinciam regis aquilonis : et abutetur eis, et obtinebit.
      EN: A shoot from her roots will arise. He will come with an army and enter the province of the king of the north, deal harshly with them, and prevail.

11:8  LA: Insuper et deos eorum, et sculptilia, vasa quoque pretiosa argenti et auri, captiva ducet in Ægyptum : ipse prævalebit adversus regem aquilonis.
      EN: He will also carry away their gods and carved images, and their precious vessels of silver and gold, as captives to Egypt. He will prevail against the king of the north.

11:9  LA: Et intrabit in regnum rex austri, et revertetur ad terram suam.
      EN: The king of the south will enter the kingdom and return to his own land.

11:10  LA: Filii autem ejus provocabuntur, et congregabunt multitudinem exercituum plurimorum : et veniet properans, et inundans : et revertetur, et concitabitur, et congredietur cum robore ejus.
       EN: His sons will be provoked and gather a multitude of very great armies. He will come swiftly, sweeping in like a flood; he will return, be stirred up, and engage his forces.

11:11  LA: Et provocatus rex austri egredietur, et pugnabit adversus regem aquilonis, et præparabit multitudinem nimiam, et dabitur multitudo in manu ejus.
       EN: The king of the south, provoked, will go out and fight against the king of the north. He will muster a vast multitude, and the multitude will be given into his hand.

11:12  LA: Et capiet multitudinem, et exaltabitur cor ejus, et dejiciet multa millia, sed non prævalebit.
       EN: He will capture the multitude, and his heart will be exalted. He will cast down many thousands, but he will not prevail.

11:13  LA: Convertetur enim rex aquilonis, et præparabit multitudinem multo majorem quam prius : et in fine temporum annorumque veniet properans cum exercitu magno, et opibus nimiis.
       EN: For the king of the north will return and muster a multitude far greater than before. After a period of years he will come swiftly with a great army and immense resources.

11:14  LA: Et in temporibus illis multi consurgent adversus regem austri : filii quoque prævaricatorum populi tui extollentur ut impleant visionem, et corruent.
       EN: In those times many will rise against the king of the south. The children of the transgressors among your people will also rise up to fulfill the vision, but they will fall.

11:15  LA: Et venit rex aquilonis, et comportabit aggerem, et capiet urbes munitissimas : et brachia austri non sustinebunt, et consurgent electi ejus ad resistendum, et non erit fortitudo.
       EN: The king of the north will come, heap up a siege ramp, and capture strongly fortified cities. The forces of the south will not withstand him. His chosen men will arise to resist, but they will have no strength.

11:16  LA: Et faciet veniens super eum juxta placitum suum, et non erit qui stet contra faciem ejus : et stabit in terra inclyta, et consumetur in manu ejus.
       EN: The one who comes against him will do as he pleases, and no one will stand before him. He will take his stand in the renowned land, and it will be consumed in his hand.

11:17  LA: Et ponet faciem suam ut veniat ad tenendum universum regnum ejus, et recta faciet cum eo : et filiam feminarum dabit ei, ut evertat illud : et non stabit, nec illius erit.
       EN: He will set his face on coming to seize his whole kingdom and will make fair terms with him. He will give him a daughter of women to overthrow it, but she will not stand by him or belong to him.

11:18  LA: Et convertet faciem suam ad insulas, et capiet multas : et cessare faciet principem opprobrii sui, et opprobrium ejus convertetur in eum.
       EN: He will turn his face toward the islands and capture many. He will put an end to the prince who reproaches him, and his reproach will turn back upon him.

11:19  LA: Et convertet faciem suam ad imperium terræ suæ, et impinget, et corruet, et non invenietur.
       EN: He will turn his face toward the dominion of his own land, but he will stumble, fall, and no longer be found.

11:20  LA: Et stabit in loco ejus vilissimus, et indignus decore regio : et in paucis diebus conteretur, non in furore, nec in prælio.
       EN: In his place will arise a most contemptible man, unworthy of royal splendor. Within a few days he will be crushed, neither in anger nor in battle.

11:21  LA: Et stabit in loco ejus despectus, et non tribuetur ei honor regius : et veniet clam, et obtinebit regnum in fraudulentia.
       EN: In his place will arise a despised man to whom royal honor will not be given. He will come secretly and obtain the kingdom by deceit.

11:22  LA: Et brachia pugnantis expugnabuntur a facie ejus, et conterentur : insuper et dux fœderis.
       EN: The forces of the combatant will be overcome before him and crushed, and so will the leader of the covenant.

11:23  LA: Et post amicitias, cum eo faciet dolum : et ascendet, et superabit in modico populo.
       EN: After entering friendship with him, he will act deceitfully. He will rise and gain the upper hand with a small people.

11:24  LA: Et abundantes, et uberes urbes ingredietur : et faciet quæ non fecerunt patres ejus, et patres patrum ejus : rapinas, et prædam, et divitias eorum dissipabit, et contra firmissimas cogitationes inibit : et hoc usque ad tempus.
       EN: He will enter prosperous and fruitful cities and do what neither his fathers nor his fathers’ fathers did. He will scatter their plunder, booty, and riches, and will devise plans against the strongest cities, but only for a time.

11:25  LA: Et concitabitur fortitudo ejus, et cor ejus adversum regem austri in exercitu magno : et rex austri provocabitur ad bellum multis auxiliis, et fortibus nimis : et non stabunt, quia inibunt adversus eum consilia.
       EN: His strength and courage will be stirred up against the king of the south with a great army. The king of the south will be provoked to war with many exceedingly powerful forces. But they will not stand, because plots will be devised against him.

11:26  LA: Et comedentes panem cum eo, conterent illum, exercitusque ejus opprimetur : et cadent interfecti plurimi.
       EN: Those who eat bread with him will crush him, his army will be overwhelmed, and very many will fall slain.

11:27  LA: Duorum quoque regum cor erit ut malefaciant, et ad mensam unam mendacium loquentur : et non proficient, quia adhuc finis in aliud tempus.
       EN: The hearts of both kings will be set on doing evil. They will speak lies at one table, but they will not succeed, for the end still belongs to another time.

11:28  LA: Et revertetur in terram suam cum opibus multis : et cor ejus adversum testamentum sanctum, et faciet, et revertetur in terram suam.
       EN: He will return to his own land with many riches. His heart will be against the holy covenant; he will act and return to his own land.

11:29  LA: Statuto tempore revertetur, et veniet ad austrum : et non erit priori simile novissimum.
       EN: At the appointed time he will return and come to the south, but this last occasion will not be like the first.

11:30  LA: Et venient super eum trieres, et Romani : et percutietur, et revertetur, et indignabitur contra testamentum sanctuarii, et faciet : reverteturque, et cogitabit adversum eos qui dereliquerunt testamentum sanctuarii.
       EN: Triremes and Romans will come against him, and he will be struck down. He will return, rage against the covenant of the sanctuary, and act. He will return and devise plans against those who have abandoned the covenant of the sanctuary.

11:31  LA: Et brachia ex eo stabunt, et polluent sanctuarium fortitudinis, et auferent juge sacrificium : et dabunt abominationem in desolationem.
       EN: Forces from him will arise and defile the fortified sanctuary. They will remove the continual sacrifice and set up the abomination for desolation.

11:32  LA: Et impii in testamentum simulabunt fraudulenter : populus autem sciens Deum suum, obtinebit, et faciet.
       EN: Those who act wickedly against the covenant will practice deceitful pretense. But the people who know their God will prevail and act.

11:33  LA: Et docti in populo docebunt plurimos : et ruent in gladio, et in flamma, et in captivitate, et in rapina dierum.
       EN: The learned among the people will teach many, yet for days they will fall by sword and flame, by captivity and plunder.

11:34  LA: Cumque corruerint, sublevabuntur auxilio parvulo : et applicabuntur eis plurimi fraudulenter.
       EN: When they fall, they will be supported with a little help, and many will attach themselves to them deceitfully.

11:35  LA: Et de eruditis ruent, ut conflentur, et eligantur, et dealbentur usque ad tempus præfinitum : quia adhuc aliud tempus erit.
       EN: Some of the learned will fall, so that they may be refined, chosen, and made white until the appointed time, for there will still be another time.

11:36  LA: Et faciet juxta voluntatem suam rex, et elevabitur, et magnificabitur adversus omnem deum : et adversus Deum deorum loquetur magnifica, et dirigetur, donec compleatur iracundia : perpetrata quippe est definitio.
       EN: The king will do as he wills. He will exalt himself and magnify himself against every god; against the God of gods he will speak boastful things. He will prosper until wrath is completed, for the determination has been made.

11:37  LA: Et Deum patrum suorum non reputabit : et erit in concupiscentiis feminarum, nec quemquam deorum curabit, quia adversum universa consurget.
       EN: He will disregard the God of his fathers and be given to desires for women. He will pay no attention to any of the gods, for he will rise against them all.

11:38  LA: Deum autem Maozim in loco suo venerabitur : et deum, quem ignoraverunt patres ejus, colet auro, et argento, et lapide pretioso, rebusque pretiosis.
       EN: In his place he will honor the god Maozim. With gold, silver, precious stones, and costly things, he will worship a god whom his fathers did not know.

11:39  LA: Et faciet ut muniat Maozim cum deo alieno, quem cognovit, et multiplicabit gloriam, et dabit eis potestatem in multis, et terram dividet gratuito.
       EN: He will act to fortify Maozim with the foreign god whom he has acknowledged. He will multiply glory, give them authority over many, and distribute the land freely.

11:40  LA: Et in tempore præfinito præliabitur adversus eum rex austri, et quasi tempestas veniet contra illum rex aquilonis in curribus, et in equitibus, et in classe magna, et ingredietur terras, et conteret, et pertransiet.
       EN: At the appointed time the king of the south will make war against him, and the king of the north will come against him like a storm, with chariots, horsemen, and a great fleet. He will enter lands, crush them, and pass through.

11:41  LA: Et introibit in terram gloriosam, et multæ corruent : hæ autem solæ salvabuntur de manu ejus, Edom, et Moab, et principium filiorum Ammon.
       EN: He will enter the glorious land, and many lands will fall. These alone will escape from his hand: Edom, Moab, and the foremost of the Ammonites.

11:42  LA: Et mittet manum suam in terras : et terra Ægypti non effugiet.
       EN: He will stretch out his hand against the lands, and the land of Egypt will not escape.

11:43  LA: Et dominabitur thesaurorum auri, et argenti, et in omnibus pretiosis Ægypti : per Libyam quoque, et Æthiopiam transibit.
       EN: He will gain dominion over the treasures of gold and silver and all the precious things of Egypt. He will also pass through Libya and Ethiopia.

11:44  LA: Et fama turbabit eum ab oriente et ab aquilone : et veniet in multitudine magna ut conterat et interficiat plurimos.
       EN: Reports from the east and the north will trouble him. He will come with a great multitude to crush and kill very many.

11:45  LA: Et figet tabernaculum suum Apadno inter maria, super montem inclytum et sanctum : et veniet usque ad summitatem ejus, et nemo auxiliabitur ei.
       EN: He will pitch his tent, Apadno, between the seas, upon the renowned and holy mountain. He will come to its summit, and no one will help him.

DANIEL 12

12:1  LA: In tempore autem illo consurget Michaël princeps magnus, qui stat pro filiis populi tui : et veniet tempus quale non fuit ab eo ex quo gentes esse cœperunt usque ad tempus illud. Et in tempore illo salvabitur populus tuus, omnis qui inventus fuerit scriptus in libro.
      EN: At that time Michael will arise, the great prince who stands for the children of your people. A time will come such as has never been since nations began to exist until that time. At that time your people will be saved, everyone who is found written in the book.

12:2  LA: Et multi de his qui dormiunt in terræ pulvere evigilabunt, alii in vitam æternam, et alii in opprobrium ut videant semper.
      EN: Many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth will awaken, some to everlasting life and others to reproach, to behold it forever.

12:3  LA: Qui autem docti fuerint, fulgebunt quasi splendor firmamenti : et qui ad justitiam erudiunt multos, quasi stellæ in perpetuas æternitates.
      EN: Those who have learning will shine like the brightness of the firmament, and those who instruct many in justice like stars through everlasting ages.

12:4  LA: Tu autem Daniel, claude sermones, et signa librum usque ad tempus statutum : plurimi pertransibunt, et multiplex erit scientia.
      EN: But you, Daniel, close up the words and seal the book until the appointed time. Very many will pass through, and knowledge will be manifold.

12:5  LA: Et vidi ego Daniel, et ecce quasi duo alii stabant : unus hinc super ripam fluminis, et alius inde ex altera ripa fluminis.
      EN: I, Daniel, looked, and behold, two others appeared to be standing there, one on this bank of the river and the other on the opposite bank.

12:6  LA: Et dixi viro qui erat indutus lineis, qui stabat super aquas fluminis : Usquequo finis horum mirabilium ?
      EN: I said to the man clothed in linen, who stood above the waters of the river, ‘How long until these wonders are completed?’

12:7  LA: Et audivi virum qui indutus erat lineis, qui stabat super aquas fluminis, cum elevasset dexteram et sinistram suam in cælum, et jurasset per viventem in æternum, quia in tempus, et tempora, et dimidium temporis. Et cum completa fuerit dispersio manus populi sancti, complebuntur universa hæc.
      EN: I heard the man clothed in linen, who stood above the waters of the river, as he raised his right and left hands to heaven and swore by Him who lives forever: ‘For a time, times, and half a time. When the scattering of the strength of the holy people is completed, all these things will be completed.’

12:8  LA: Et ego audivi, et non intellexi. Et dixi : Domine mi, quid erit post hæc ?
      EN: I heard, but did not understand. I said, ‘My lord, what will happen after these things?’

12:9  LA: Et ait : Vade, Daniel, quia clausi sunt signatique sermones usque ad præfinitum tempus.
      EN: He said, ‘Go, Daniel, for the words are closed and sealed until the appointed time.

12:10  LA: Eligentur, et dealbabuntur, et quasi ignis probabuntur multi : et impie agent impii, neque intelligent omnes impii : porro docti intelligent.
       EN: Many will be chosen, made white, and tested as by fire. The wicked will act wickedly, and none of the wicked will understand, but the learned will understand.

12:11  LA: Et a tempore cum ablatum fuerit juge sacrificium, et posita fuerit abominatio in desolationem, dies mille ducenti nonaginta.
       EN: From the time the continual sacrifice is removed and the abomination is set up for desolation, there will be one thousand two hundred and ninety days.

12:12  LA: Beatus qui exspectat, et pervenit usque ad dies mille trecentos triginta quinque.
       EN: Blessed is the one who waits and reaches one thousand three hundred and thirty-five days.

12:13  LA: Tu autem vade ad præfinitum : et requiesces, et stabis in sorte tua in finem dierum.
       EN: But you, go on to the appointed end. You will rest and stand in your allotted place at the end of days.’”
