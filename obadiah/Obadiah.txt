OBADIAH 1

1:1  LA: Visio Abdiæ. [Hæc dicit Dominus Deus ad Edom : Auditum audivimus a Domino, et legatum ad gentes misit : surgite, et consurgamus adversus eum in prælium.
     EN: The vision of Obadiah. Thus says the Lord God to Edom: We have heard a report from the Lord, and He has sent an envoy to the nations: “Rise, and let us rise against him in battle.”

1:2  LA: Ecce parvulum dedi te in gentibus : contemptibilis tu es valde.
     EN: “See, I have made you small among the nations; you are greatly despised.

1:3  LA: Superbia cordis tui extulit te, habitantem in scissuris petrarum, exaltantem solium tuum ; qui dicis in corde tuo : Quis detrahet me in terram ?
     EN: The pride of your heart has raised you up, you who dwell in clefts of the rocks and set your throne high, who say in your heart, ‘Who will bring me down to the ground?’

1:4  LA: Si exaltatus fueris ut aquila, et si inter sidera posueris nidum tuum, inde detraham te, dicit Dominus.
     EN: Though you are raised as high as an eagle, though you set your nest among the stars, from there I will bring you down,” says the Lord.

1:5  LA: Si fures introissent ad te, si latrones per noctem, quomodo conticuisses ? nonne furati essent sufficientia sibi ? Si vindemiatores introissent ad te, numquid saltem racemum reliquissent tibi ?
     EN: “If thieves had come to you, if robbers by night, how would you have been silenced? Would they not have stolen only enough for themselves? If grape gatherers had come to you, would they at least have left you a cluster?

1:6  LA: Quomodo scrutati sunt Esau ; investigaverunt abscondita ejus ?
     EN: How they have searched Esau! They have investigated his hidden things.

1:7  LA: Usque ad terminum emiserunt te : omnes viri fœderis tui illuserunt tibi : invaluerunt adversum te viri pacis tuæ, qui comedunt tecum, ponent insidias subter te ; non est prudentia in eo.
     EN: They have sent you as far as the border; all your allies have deceived you. Those at peace with you have prevailed against you; those who eat with you will set snares beneath you. There is no understanding in him.

1:8  LA: Numquid non in die illa, dicit Dominus, perdam sapientes de Idumæa, et prudentiam de monte Esau ?
     EN: On that day,” says the Lord, “will I not destroy the wise from Edom and understanding from the mountain of Esau?

1:9  LA: Et timebunt fortes tui a meridie, ut intereat vir de monte Esau.
     EN: Your mighty men from the south will be afraid, so that man may perish from the mountain of Esau.

1:10  LA: Propter interfectionem, et propter iniquitatem in fratrem tuum Jacob, operiet te confusio, et peribis in æternum.
      EN: Because of slaughter and because of the wrong done to your brother Jacob, shame will cover you, and you will perish forever.

1:11  LA: In die cum stares adversus eum, quando capiebant alieni exercitum ejus, et extranei ingrediebantur portas ejus, et super Jerusalem mittebant sortem, tu quoque eras quasi unus ex eis.
      EN: On the day when you stood against him, when strangers captured his army and foreigners entered his gates and cast lots over Jerusalem, you too were like one of them.

1:12  LA: Et non despicies in die fratris tui, in die peregrinationis ejus : et non lætaberis super filios Juda in die perditionis eorum : et non magnificabis os tuum in die angustiæ.
      EN: You shall not look down on your brother's day, the day of his exile; you shall not rejoice over the children of Judah on the day of their destruction, nor boast with your mouth on the day of distress.

1:13  LA: Neque ingredieris portam populi mei in die ruinæ eorum ; neque despicies et tu in malis ejus in die vastitatis illius. Et non emitteris adversus exercitum ejus in die vastitatis illius,
      EN: You shall not enter My people's gate on the day of their ruin, nor shall you too look down on his misfortunes on the day of his devastation. You shall not launch out against his army on the day of his devastation,

1:14  LA: neque stabis in exitibus ut interficias eos qui fugerint, et non concludes reliquos ejus in die tribulationis.
      EN: nor stand at the exits to kill those who flee, nor hem in his survivors on the day of affliction.

1:15  LA: Quoniam juxta est dies Domini super omnes gentes : sicut fecisti, fiet tibi ; retributionem tuam convertet in caput tuum.
      EN: For the day of the Lord is near upon all nations. As you have done, it will be done to you; He will turn your repayment back upon your head.

1:16  LA: Quomodo enim bibistis super montem sanctum meum, bibent omnes gentes jugiter : et bibent, et absorbebunt, et erunt quasi non sint.
      EN: For as you drank on My holy mountain, so all nations will drink continually; they will drink and swallow, and will be as though they had never been.

1:17  LA: Et in monte Sion erit salvatio, et erit sanctus ; et possidebit domus Jacob eos qui se possederant.
      EN: But on Mount Zion there will be salvation, and it will be holy; the house of Jacob will possess those who had possessed them.

1:18  LA: Et erit domus Jacob ignis, et domus Joseph flamma, et domus Esau stipula : et succendentur in eis, et devorabunt eos, et non erunt reliquiæ domus Esau, quia Dominus locutus est.
      EN: The house of Jacob will be a fire, the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau stubble. They will set them ablaze and devour them, and there will be no survivor of the house of Esau, for the Lord has spoken.

1:19  LA: Et hæreditabunt hi, qui ad austrum sunt, montem Esau, et qui in campestribus, Philisthiim : et possidebunt regionem Ephraim et regionem Samariæ, et Benjamin possidebit Galaad.
      EN: Those in the south will inherit the mountain of Esau, and those in the plains the Philistines. They will possess the region of Ephraim and the region of Samaria, and Benjamin will possess Gilead.

1:20  LA: Et transmigratio exercitus hujus filiorum Israël, omnia loca Chananæorum usque ad Sareptam : et transmigratio Jerusalem, quæ in Bosphoro est, possidebit civitates austri.
      EN: The exiles of this army of the children of Israel will possess all the places of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath; and Jerusalem's exiles who are in the Bosporus will possess the cities of the south.

1:21  LA: Et ascendent salvatores in montem Sion judicare montem Esau, et erit Domino regnum.]
      EN: Saviors will ascend Mount Zion to judge the mountain of Esau, and the kingdom will belong to the Lord.”
