JOSHUA 1

1:1  LA: Et factum est post mortem Moysi servi Domini, ut loqueretur Dominus ad Josue filium Nun, ministrum Moysi, et diceret ei :
     EN: After the death of Moses, the Lord's servant, the Lord spoke to Joshua son of Nun, Moses' assistant, and said to him:

1:2  LA: Moyses servus meus mortuus est : surge, et transi Jordanem istum tu, et omnis populus tecum, in terram quam ego dabo filiis Israël.
     EN: “My servant Moses is dead. Rise, cross this Jordan, you and all the people with you, into the land that I will give to the children of Israel.

1:3  LA: Omnem locum, quem calcaverit vestigium pedis vestri, vobis tradam, sicut locutus sum Moysi.
     EN: Every place on which the sole of your foot treads I will hand over to you, as I told Moses.

1:4  LA: A deserto et Libano usque ad fluvium magnum Euphraten, omnis terra Hethæorum usque ad mare magnum contra solis occasum erit terminus vester.
     EN: From the wilderness and Lebanon to the great river Euphrates, all the land of the Hittites, as far as the Great Sea toward the setting sun, will be your territory.

1:5  LA: Nullus poterit vobis resistere cunctis diebus vitæ tuæ : sicut fui cum Moyse, ita ero tecum : non dimittam, nec derelinquam te.
     EN: No one will be able to resist you throughout all the days of your life. As I was with Moses, so I will be with you. I will not abandon you or forsake you.

1:6  LA: Confortare, et esto robustus : tu enim sorte divides populo huic terram, pro qua juravi patribus suis, ut traderem eam illis.
     EN: Take courage and be strong, for you will divide by lot among this people the land that I swore to their fathers I would hand over to them.

1:7  LA: Confortare igitur, et esto robustus valde, ut custodias, et facias omnem legem, quam præcepit tibi Moyses servus meus : ne declines ab ea ad dexteram vel ad sinistram, ut intelligas cuncta quæ agis.
     EN: Take courage, then, and be very strong, so that you observe and carry out the whole law that My servant Moses commanded you. Do not turn from it to the right or to the left, so that you understand everything you do.

1:8  LA: Non recedat volumen legis hujus ab ore tuo : sed meditaberis in eo diebus ac noctibus, ut custodias et facias omnia quæ scripta sunt in eo : tunc diriges viam tuam, et intelliges eam.
     EN: This scroll of the law must not depart from your mouth. Meditate on it day and night, so that you observe and carry out everything written in it. Then you will set your way straight and understand it.

1:9  LA: Ecce præcipio tibi : confortare, et esto robustus. Noli metuere, et noli timere : quoniam tecum est Dominus Deus tuus in omnibus ad quæcumque perrexeris.
     EN: Look, I command you: take courage and be strong. Do not be afraid or terrified, for the Lord your God is with you in everything, wherever you go.”

1:10  LA: Præcepitque Josue principibus populi, dicens : Transite per medium castrorum, et imperate populo, ac dicite :
      EN: Joshua commanded the leaders of the people, saying, “Pass through the middle of the camp and give the people these orders:

1:11  LA: Præparate vobis cibaria : quoniam post diem tertium transibitis Jordanem, et intrabitis ad possidendam terram quam Dominus Deus vester daturus est vobis.
      EN: ‘Prepare provisions for yourselves, for after the third day you will cross the Jordan and enter to take possession of the land that the Lord your God is going to give you.’”

1:12  LA: Rubenitis quoque et Gaditis, et dimidiæ tribui Manasse, ait :
      EN: He also said to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh:

1:13  LA: Mementote sermonis, quem præcepit vobis Moyses famulus Domini, dicens : Dominus Deus vester dedit vobis requiem, et omnem terram.
      EN: “Remember the words that Moses, the Lord's servant, commanded you: ‘The Lord your God has given you rest and all the land.’

1:14  LA: Uxores vestræ, et filii, ac jumenta manebunt in terra, quam tradidit vobis Moyses trans Jordanem : vos autem transite armati ante fratres vestros, omnes fortes manu, et pugnate pro eis,
      EN: Your wives, children, and livestock will remain in the land that Moses handed over to you beyond the Jordan. But you, all the mighty warriors, must cross armed ahead of your brothers and fight for them,

1:15  LA: donec det Dominus requiem fratribus vestris sicut et vobis dedit, et possideant ipsi quoque terram quam Dominus Deus vester daturus est eis : et sic revertimini in terram possessionis vestræ, et habitabitis in ea, quam vobis dedit Moyses famulus Domini trans Jordanem contra solis ortum.
      EN: until the Lord gives your brothers rest, as He has given you, and they too possess the land that the Lord your God is going to give them. Then return to the land of your possession and live in it, the land that Moses, the Lord's servant, gave you beyond the Jordan toward the rising sun.”

1:16  LA: Responderuntque ad Josue, atque dixerunt : Omnia quæ præcepisti nobis, faciemus : et quocumque miseris, ibimus.
      EN: They answered Joshua and said, “We will do everything you have commanded us, and wherever you send us, we will go.

1:17  LA: Sicut obedivimus in cunctis Moysi, ita obediemus et tibi : tantum sit Dominus Deus tuus tecum, sicut fuit cum Moyse.
      EN: As we obeyed Moses in everything, so we will also obey you. Only may the Lord your God be with you, as He was with Moses.

1:18  LA: Qui contradixerit ori tuo, et non obedierit cunctis sermonibus, quos præceperis ei, moriatur. Tu tantum confortare, et viriliter age.
      EN: Whoever contradicts your word and does not obey everything you command him must die. Only take courage and act bravely.”

JOSHUA 2

2:1  LA: Misit igitur Josue filius Nun de Setim duos viros exploratores in abscondito : et dixit eis : Ite, et considerate terram, urbemque Jericho. Qui pergentes ingressi sunt domum mulieris meretricis, nomine Rahab, et quieverunt apud eam.
     EN: Then Joshua son of Nun secretly sent two men from Shittim as scouts. He said to them, “Go and examine the land and the city of Jericho.” They went and entered the house of a woman named Rahab, a prostitute, and lodged with her.

2:2  LA: Nuntiatumque est regi Jericho, et dictum : Ecce viri ingressi sunt huc per noctem de filiis Israël, ut explorarent terram.
     EN: It was reported to the king of Jericho: “Look, men from the children of Israel have come here during the night to explore the land.”

2:3  LA: Misitque rex Jericho ad Rahab, dicens : Educ viros, qui venerunt ad te, et ingressi sunt domum tuam : exploratores quippe sunt, et omnem terram considerare venerunt.
     EN: The king of Jericho sent word to Rahab: “Bring out the men who came to you and entered your house, for they are scouts and have come to examine the whole land.”

2:4  LA: Tollensque mulier viros, abscondit, et ait : Fateor, venerunt ad me, sed nesciebam unde essent :
     EN: The woman took the men and hid them. She said, “I admit they came to me, but I did not know where they were from.

2:5  LA: cumque porta clauderetur in tenebris, et illi pariter exierunt ; nescio quo abierunt : persequimini cito, et comprehendetis eos.
     EN: When the gate was being closed at dark, they also went out. I do not know where they went. Pursue them quickly, and you will catch them.”

2:6  LA: Ipsa autem fecit ascendere viros in solarium domus suæ, operuitque eos stipula lini, quæ ibi erat.
     EN: But she had made the men go up to the roof of her house and had covered them with the flax stalks that were there.

2:7  LA: Hi autem, qui missi fuerant, secuti sunt eos per viam, quæ ducit ad vadum Jordanis : illisque egressis statim porta clausa est.
     EN: Those who had been sent pursued them along the road leading to the ford of the Jordan. As soon as they had gone out, the gate was closed.

2:8  LA: Necdum obdormierant qui latebant, et ecce mulier ascendit ad eos, et ait :
     EN: The men in hiding had not yet fallen asleep when the woman went up to them and said:

2:9  LA: Novi quod Dominus tradiderit vobis terram : etenim irruit in nos terror vester, et elanguerunt omnes habitatores terræ.
     EN: “I know that the Lord has handed the land over to you. Terror of you has fallen upon us, and all the inhabitants of the land have lost heart.

2:10  LA: Audivimus quod siccaverit Dominus aquas maris Rubri ad vestrum introitum, quando egressi estis ex Ægypto : et quæ feceritis duobus Amorrhæorum regibus, qui erant trans Jordanem, Sehon et Og, quos interfecistis.
      EN: We have heard that the Lord dried up the waters of the Red Sea for your entry when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites beyond the Jordan, Sihon and Og, whom you killed.

2:11  LA: Et hæc audientes pertimuimus, et elanguit cor nostrum, nec remansit in nobis spiritus ad introitum vestrum : Dominus enim Deus vester ipse est Deus in cælo sursum et in terra deorsum.
      EN: Hearing these things, we were terrified. Our hearts failed, and no spirit remained in us at your coming, for the Lord your God is Himself God in heaven above and on earth below.

2:12  LA: Nunc ergo jurate mihi per Dominum, ut quomodo ego misericordiam feci vobiscum, ita et vos faciatis cum domo patris mei : detisque mihi verum signum,
      EN: Now therefore swear to me by the Lord that, as I have shown mercy to you, you too will show mercy to my father's household. Give me a trustworthy sign

2:13  LA: ut salvetis patrem meum et matrem, et fratres ac sorores meas, et omnia quæ illorum sunt, et eruatis animas nostras a morte.
      EN: that you will save my father and mother, my brothers and sisters, and everything belonging to them, and rescue our lives from death.”

2:14  LA: Qui responderunt ei : Anima nostra sit pro vobis in mortem, si tamen non prodideris nos : cumque tradiderit nobis Dominus terram, faciemus in te misericordiam et veritatem.
      EN: They answered her, “Let our lives answer for yours in death, provided you do not betray us. When the Lord hands the land over to us, we will treat you with mercy and faithfulness.”

2:15  LA: Demisit ergo eos per funem de fenestra : domus enim ejus hærebat muro.
      EN: So she lowered them by a rope from the window, for her house adjoined the wall.

2:16  LA: Dixitque ad eos : Ad montana conscendite, ne forte occurrant vobis revertentes : ibique latitate tribus diebus, donec redeant, et sic ibitis per viam vestram.
      EN: She said to them, “Go up into the hills, or those returning may meet you. Hide there for three days until they return, and then go on your way.”

2:17  LA: Qui dixerunt ad eam : Innoxii erimus a juramento hoc, quo adjurasti nos :
      EN: They said to her, “We will be free of guilt under this oath that you have made us swear

2:18  LA: si ingredientibus nobis terram, signum fuerit funiculus iste coccineus, et ligaveris eum in fenestra, per quam demisisti nos : et patrem tuum ac matrem, fratresque et omnem cognationem tuam congregaveris in domum tuam.
      EN: if, when we enter the land, this scarlet cord serves as a sign, and you tie it in the window through which you lowered us, and gather your father and mother, your brothers, and all your relatives into your house.

2:19  LA: Qui ostium domus tuæ egressus fuerit, sanguis ipsius erit in caput ejus, et nos erimus alieni. Cunctorum autem sanguis, qui tecum in domo fuerint, redundabit in caput nostrum, si eos aliquis tetigerit.
      EN: Whoever goes out through the door of your house, his blood will be on his own head, and we will be free of blame. But the blood of everyone with you in the house will fall upon our heads if anyone touches them.

2:20  LA: Quod si nos prodere volueris, et sermonem istum proferre in medium, erimus mundi ab hoc juramento, quo adjurasti nos.
      EN: But if you choose to betray us and make this matter public, we will be free of this oath that you have made us swear.”

2:21  LA: Et illa respondit : Sicut locuti estis, ita fiat : dimittensque eos ut pergerent, appendit funiculum coccineum in fenestra.
      EN: She answered, “Let it be as you have said.” She sent them away to continue their journey and hung the scarlet cord in the window.

2:22  LA: Illi vero ambulantes pervenerunt ad montana, et manserunt ibi tres dies, donec reverterentur qui fuerant persecuti : quærentes enim per omnem viam, non repererunt eos.
      EN: They went on and reached the hills. They stayed there three days, until their pursuers returned; for although these had searched along the whole road, they had not found them.

2:23  LA: Quibus urbem ingressis, reversi sunt, et descenderunt exploratores de monte : et, transmisso Jordane, venerunt ad Josue filium Nun, narraveruntque ei omnia quæ acciderant sibi,
      EN: When the pursuers had entered the city, the scouts returned and came down from the mountain. They crossed the Jordan and came to Joshua son of Nun, and told him everything that had happened to them.

2:24  LA: atque dixerunt : Tradidit Dominus omnem terram hanc in manus nostras, et timore prostrati sunt cuncti habitatores ejus.
      EN: They said, “The Lord has handed this whole land into our hands, and all its inhabitants are overcome with fear.”

JOSHUA 3

3:1  LA: Igitur Josue de nocte consurgens movit castra : egredientesque de Setim, venerunt ad Jordanem ipse et omnes filii Israël, et morati sunt ibi tres dies.
     EN: Joshua rose during the night and moved the camp. Setting out from Shittim, he and all the children of Israel came to the Jordan, and they stayed there for three days.

3:2  LA: Quibus evolutis transierunt præcones per castrorum medium,
     EN: When these days had passed, heralds went through the middle of the camp

3:3  LA: et clamare cœperunt : Quando videritis arcam fœderis Domini Dei vestri, et sacerdotes stirpis Leviticæ portantes eam, vos quoque consurgite, et sequimini præcedentes :
     EN: and began to proclaim: “When you see the ark of the covenant of the Lord your God and the priests of Levitical descent carrying it, you too must rise and follow those going ahead.

3:4  LA: sitque inter vos et arcam spatium cubitorum duum millium : ut procul videre possitis, et nosse per quam viam ingrediamini : quia prius non ambulastis per eam : et cavete ne appropinquetis ad arcam.
     EN: Let there be a distance of two thousand cubits between you and the ark, so that you can see it from afar and know the way you must take, because you have not traveled it before. Take care not to approach the ark.”

3:5  LA: Dixitque Josue ad populum : Sanctificamini : cras enim faciet Dominus inter vos mirabilia.
     EN: Joshua said to the people, “Consecrate yourselves, for tomorrow the Lord will do wonders among you.”

3:6  LA: Et ait ad sacerdotes : Tollite arcam fœderis, et præcedite populum. Qui jussa complentes, tulerunt, et ambulaverunt ante eos.
     EN: He said to the priests, “Take up the ark of the covenant and go ahead of the people.” Carrying out his orders, they took it up and walked ahead of them.

3:7  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad Josue : Hodie incipiam exaltare te coram omni Israël : ut sciant quod sicut cum Moyse fui, ita et tecum sim.
     EN: The Lord said to Joshua, “Today I will begin to exalt you before all Israel, so that they may know that, as I was with Moses, so I am with you.

3:8  LA: Tu autem præcipe sacerdotibus, qui portant arcam fœderis, et dic eis : Cum ingressi fueritis partem aquæ Jordanis, state in ea.
     EN: Command the priests carrying the ark of the covenant and say to them, ‘When you have entered the edge of the Jordan's water, stand in it.’”

3:9  LA: Dixitque Josue ad filios Israël : Accedite huc, et audite verbum Domini Dei vestri.
     EN: Joshua said to the children of Israel, “Come here and hear the word of the Lord your God.”

3:10  LA: Et rursum : In hoc, inquit, scietis quod Dominus Deus vivens in medio vestri est, et disperdet in conspectu vestro Chananæum et Hethæum, Hevæum et Pherezæum, Gergesæum quoque et Jebusæum, et Amorrhæum.
      EN: Then he said, “By this you will know that the Lord, the living God, is among you and will destroy before your eyes the Canaanites, Hittites, Hivites, Perizzites, Girgashites, Jebusites, and Amorites.

3:11  LA: Ecce arca fœderis Domini omnis terræ antecedet vos per Jordanem.
      EN: Look, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth will go ahead of you through the Jordan.

3:12  LA: Parate duodecim viros de tribubus Israël, singulos per singulas tribus.
      EN: Choose twelve men from the tribes of Israel, one from each tribe.

3:13  LA: Et cum posuerint vestigia pedum suorum sacerdotes qui portant arcam Domini Dei universæ terræ in aquis Jordanis, aquæ quæ inferiores sunt, decurrent atque deficient : quæ autem desuper veniunt, in una mole consistent.
      EN: When the priests carrying the ark of the Lord, the God of the whole earth, set the soles of their feet in the waters of the Jordan, the waters below will flow away and fail, but those coming from above will stand in a single mass.”

3:14  LA: Igitur egressus est populus de tabernaculis suis, ut transiret Jordanem : et sacerdotes, qui portabant arcam fœderis, pergebant ante eum.
      EN: So the people came out of their tents to cross the Jordan, and the priests carrying the ark of the covenant went ahead of them.

3:15  LA: Ingressisque eis Jordanem, et pedibus eorum in parte aquæ tinctis (Jordanis autem ripas alvei sui tempore messis impleverat),
      EN: When they entered the Jordan and their feet were dipped into the edge of the water—the Jordan had filled the banks of its channel at harvest time—

3:16  LA: steterunt aquæ descendentes in loco uno, et ad instar montis intumescentes apparebant procul, ab urbe quæ vocatur Adom usque ad locum Sarthan : quæ autem inferiores erant, in mare Solitudinis (quod nunc vocatur Mortuum) descenderunt, usquequo omnino deficerent.
      EN: the descending waters stood in one place and appeared from afar, swelling like a mountain, from the city called Adam as far as the place called Zarethan. But the waters below flowed down into the Sea of the Wilderness, which is now called the Dead Sea, until they failed completely.

3:17  LA: Populus autem incedebat contra Jericho : et sacerdotes qui portabant arcam fœderis Domini, stabant super siccam humum in medio Jordanis accincti, omnisque populus per arentem alveum transibat.
      EN: The people advanced opposite Jericho, while the priests carrying the ark of the Lord's covenant stood ready on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan, and all the people crossed through the dry riverbed.

JOSHUA 4

4:1  LA: Quibus transgressis, dixit Dominus ad Josue :
     EN: When they had crossed, the Lord said to Joshua:

4:2  LA: Elige duodecim viros singulos per singulas tribus :
     EN: “Choose twelve men, one from each tribe,

4:3  LA: et præcipe eis ut tollant de medio Jordanis alveo, ubi steterunt pedes sacerdotum, duodecim durissimos lapides, quos ponetis in loco castrorum, ubi fixeritis hac nocte tentoria.
     EN: and command them to take twelve very hard stones from the middle of the Jordan's bed, where the priests' feet stood. You will put them at the camping place where you pitch your tents tonight.”

4:4  LA: Vocavitque Josue duodecim viros, quos elegerat de filiis Israël, singulos de singulis tribubus,
     EN: Joshua called the twelve men whom he had chosen from the children of Israel, one from each tribe,

4:5  LA: et ait ad eos : Ite ante arcam Domini Dei vestri ad Jordanis medium, et portate inde singuli singulos lapides in humeris vestris, juxta numerum filiorum Israël,
     EN: and said to them, “Go ahead of the ark of the Lord your God into the middle of the Jordan, and each of you carry one stone from there on your shoulder, according to the number of the children of Israel,

4:6  LA: ut sit signum inter vos : et quando interrogaverint vos filii vestri cras, dicentes : Quid sibi volunt isti lapides ?
     EN: to be a sign among you. When your children ask you in time to come, ‘What do these stones mean?’

4:7  LA: respondebitis eis : Defecerunt aquæ Jordanis ante arcam fœderis Domini, cum transiret eum : idcirco positi sunt lapides isti in monimentum filiorum Israël usque in æternum.
     EN: you will answer them, ‘The waters of the Jordan failed before the ark of the Lord's covenant when it crossed over. Therefore these stones have been set up as a memorial for the children of Israel forever.’”

4:8  LA: Fecerunt ergo filii Israël sicut præcepit eis Josue, portantes de medio Jordanis alveo duodecim lapides, ut Dominus ei imperarat, juxta numerum filiorum Israël, usque ad locum in quo castrametati sunt, ibique posuerunt eos.
     EN: The children of Israel did as Joshua commanded them. They carried twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan's bed, as the Lord had ordered him, according to the number of the children of Israel, to the place where they camped, and put them there.

4:9  LA: Alios quoque duodecim lapides posuit Josue in medio Jordanis alveo, ubi steterunt sacerdotes qui portabant arcam fœderis : et sunt ibi usque in præsentem diem.
     EN: Joshua also set twelve other stones in the middle of the Jordan's bed, where the priests carrying the ark of the covenant had stood. They are there to this day.

4:10  LA: Sacerdotes autem qui portabant arcam, stabant in Jordanis medio, donec omnia complerentur, quæ Josue, ut loqueretur ad populum, præceperat Dominus, et dixerat ei Moyses. Festinavitque populus, et transiit.
      EN: The priests carrying the ark stood in the middle of the Jordan until everything was completed that the Lord had commanded Joshua to tell the people and Moses had told him. The people hurried across.

4:11  LA: Cumque transissent omnes, transivit et arca Domini, sacerdotesque pergebant ante populum.
      EN: When everyone had crossed, the ark of the Lord also crossed, and the priests went ahead of the people.

4:12  LA: Filii quoque Ruben, et Gad, et dimidia tribus Manasse, armati præcedebant filios Israël, sicut eis præceperat Moyses :
      EN: The children of Reuben and Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh also went armed ahead of the children of Israel, as Moses had commanded them.

4:13  LA: et quadraginta pugnatorum millia per turmas, et cuneos, incedebant per plana atque campestria urbis Jericho.
      EN: Forty thousand fighting men, in companies and formations, advanced across the plains and open country of the city of Jericho.

4:14  LA: In die illo magnificavit Dominus Josue coram omni Israël, ut timerent eum, sicut timuerant Moysen, dum adviveret.
      EN: On that day the Lord made Joshua great before all Israel, so that they revered him as they had revered Moses while he was alive.

4:15  LA: Dixitque ad eum :
      EN: He said to him:

4:16  LA: Præcipe sacerdotibus, qui portant arcam fœderis, ut ascendant de Jordane.
      EN: “Command the priests carrying the ark of the covenant to come up out of the Jordan.”

4:17  LA: Qui præcepit eis, dicens : Ascendite de Jordane.
      EN: He commanded them, saying, “Come up out of the Jordan.”

4:18  LA: Cumque ascendissent portantes arcam fœderis Domini, et siccam humum calcare cœpissent, reversæ sunt aquæ in alveum suum, et fluebant sicut ante consueverant.
      EN: When those carrying the ark of the Lord's covenant had come up and begun to tread on dry ground, the waters returned to their riverbed and flowed as they had done before.

4:19  LA: Populus autem ascendit de Jordane decimo die mensis primi, et castrametati sunt in Galgalis contra orientalem plagam urbis Jericho.
      EN: The people came up out of the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month and camped at Gilgal, opposite the eastern side of the city of Jericho.

4:20  LA: Duodecim quoque lapides, quos de Jordanis alveo sumpserant, posuit Josue in Galgalis,
      EN: Joshua also set up at Gilgal the twelve stones that they had taken from the Jordan's bed.

4:21  LA: et dixit ad filios Israël : Quando interrogaverint filii vestri cras patres suos, et dixerint eis : Quid sibi volunt lapides isti ?
      EN: He said to the children of Israel, “When your children ask their fathers in time to come, ‘What do these stones mean?’

4:22  LA: docebitis eos, atque dicetis : Per arentem alveum transivit Israël Jordanem istum,
      EN: you will teach them and say, ‘Israel crossed this Jordan through a dry riverbed,

4:23  LA: siccante Domino Deo vestro aquas ejus in conspectu vestro, donec transiretis,
      EN: while the Lord your God dried up its waters before you until you had crossed, just as He had done before at the Red Sea, which He dried up until we had crossed,

4:24  LA: ut discant omnes terrarum populi fortissimam Domini manum, ut et vos timeatis Dominum Deum vestrum omni tempore.
      EN: so that all the peoples of the earth might learn of the Lord's mighty hand, and you too might fear the Lord your God at all times.’”

JOSHUA 5

5:1  LA: Postquam ergo audierunt omnes reges Amorrhæorum, qui habitabant trans Jordanem ad occidentalem plagam, et cuncti reges Chanaan, qui propinqua possidebant magni maris loca, quod siccasset Dominus fluenta Jordanis coram filiis Israël donec transirent, dissolutum est cor eorum, et non remansit in eis spiritus, timentium introitum filiorum Israël.
     EN: When all the kings of the Amorites living beyond the Jordan to the west, and all the kings of Canaan who held the places near the Great Sea, heard that the Lord had dried up the streams of the Jordan before the children of Israel until they crossed, their hearts melted, and no spirit remained in them, for they feared the coming of the children of Israel.

5:2  LA: Eo tempore ait Dominus ad Josue : Fac tibi cultros lapideos, et circumcide secundo filios Israël.
     EN: At that time the Lord said to Joshua, “Make stone knives for yourself and circumcise the children of Israel a second time.”

5:3  LA: Fecit quod jusserat Dominus, et circumcidit filios Israël in colle præputiorum.
     EN: He did as the Lord had ordered and circumcised the children of Israel on the Hill of the Foreskins.

5:4  LA: Hæc autem causa est secundæ circumcisionis : omnis populus, qui egressus est de Ægypto generis masculini, universi bellatores viri, mortui sunt in deserto per longissimos viæ circuitus,
     EN: This was the reason for the second circumcision: all the males who came out of Egypt, all the fighting men, had died in the wilderness during the very long wanderings of the journey.

5:5  LA: qui omnes circumcisi erant. Populus autem qui natus est in deserto,
     EN: They had all been circumcised. But the people born in the wilderness

     EN: during the forty years of travel through the vast desert were uncircumcised, until those who had not listened to the Lord's voice were consumed, those to whom He had previously sworn that He would not show them the land flowing with milk and honey.

5:7  LA: Horum filii in locum successerunt patrum, et circumcisi sunt a Josue : quia sicut nati fuerant, in præputio erant, nec eos in via aliquis circumciderat.
     EN: Their sons succeeded their fathers in their place and were circumcised by Joshua, because they still had their foreskins, just as they were born; no one had circumcised them on the journey.

5:8  LA: Postquam autem omnes circumcisi sunt, manserunt in eodem castrorum loco, donec sanarentur.
     EN: After they had all been circumcised, they remained at the same camping place until they healed.

5:9  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad Josue : Hodie abstuli opprobrium Ægypti a vobis. Vocatumque est nomen loci illius Galgala, usque in præsentem diem.
     EN: The Lord said to Joshua, “Today I have taken away from you the disgrace of Egypt.” That place has been called Gilgal to this day.

5:10  LA: Manseruntque filii Israël in Galgalis, et fecerunt Phase quartadecima die mensis ad vesperum in campestribus Jericho :
      EN: The children of Israel stayed at Gilgal and celebrated the Passover on the fourteenth day of the month, in the evening, on the plains of Jericho.

5:11  LA: et comederunt de frugibus terræ die altero, azymos panes, et polentam ejusdem anni.
      EN: On the next day they ate some of the land's produce: unleavened bread and roasted meal from that year's crop.

5:12  LA: Defecitque manna postquam comederunt de frugibus terræ, nec usi sunt ultra cibo illo filii Israël, sed comederunt de frugibus præsentis anni terræ Chanaan.
      EN: The manna ceased after they ate some of the land's produce. The children of Israel no longer used that food, but ate some of the produce of that year's crop in the land of Canaan.

5:13  LA: Cum autem esset Josue in agro urbis Jericho, levavit oculos, et vidit virum stantem contra se, evaginatum tenentem gladium : perrexitque ad eum, et ait : Noster es, an adversariorum ?
      EN: When Joshua was in the countryside of the city of Jericho, he raised his eyes and saw a man standing opposite him, holding a drawn sword. He went up to him and said, “Are you one of us or one of our adversaries?”

5:14  LA: Qui respondit : Nequaquam : sed sum princeps exercitus Domini, et nunc venio.
      EN: He answered, “No. I am the commander of the Lord's army, and now I have come.” Joshua fell facedown on the ground and, bowing in reverence, said, “What does my lord say to his servant?”

5:15  LA: Solve, inquit, calceamentum tuum de pedibus tuis : locus enim, in quo stas, sanctus est. Fecitque Josue ut sibi fuerat imperatum.
      EN: He said, “Take your footwear off your feet, for the place where you stand is holy.” Joshua did as he had been commanded.

JOSHUA 6

6:1  LA: Jericho autem clausa erat atque munita, timore filiorum Israël, et nullus egredi audebat aut ingredi.
     EN: Jericho was shut up and fortified for fear of the children of Israel, and no one dared go out or come in.

6:2  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad Josue : Ecce dedi in manu tua Jericho, et regem ejus, omnesque fortes viros.
     EN: The Lord said to Joshua, “Look, I have given Jericho into your hand, with its king and all its mighty men.

6:3  LA: Circuite urbem cuncti bellatores semel per diem : sic facietis sex diebus.
     EN: All you fighting men must march around the city once a day. Do this for six days.

6:5  LA: Cumque insonuerit vox tubæ longior atque concisior, et in auribus vestris increpuerit, conclamabit omnis populus vociferatione maxima, et muri funditus corruent civitatis, ingredienturque singuli per locum contra quem steterint.
     EN: When the trumpet's longer, sharper blast sounds and rings in your ears, all the people must shout with a very loud cry. The city's walls will collapse to their foundations, and each person will enter at the place opposite where he stands.”

6:6  LA: Vocavit ergo Josue filius Nun sacerdotes, et dixit ad eos : Tollite arcam fœderis : et septem alii sacerdotes tollant septem jubilæorum buccinas, et incedant ante arcam Domini.
     EN: Joshua son of Nun called the priests and said to them, “Take up the ark of the covenant, and let seven other priests take seven jubilee trumpets and walk ahead of the ark of the Lord.”

6:7  LA: Ad populum quoque ait : Ite, et circuite civitatem, armati, præcedentes arcam Domini.
     EN: He also said to the people, “Go and march around the city, armed, ahead of the ark of the Lord.”

6:8  LA: Cumque Josue verba finisset, et septem sacerdotes septem buccinis clangerent ante arcam fœderis Domini,
     EN: When Joshua had finished speaking and the seven priests were sounding seven trumpets before the ark of the Lord's covenant,

6:9  LA: omnisque præcederet armatus exercitus, reliquum vulgus arcam sequebatur, ac buccinis omnia concrepabant.
     EN: with the whole armed army going ahead, the rest of the people followed the ark, and everything resounded with trumpets.

6:10  LA: Præceperat autem Josue populo, dicens : Non clamabitis, nec audietur vox vestra, neque ullus sermo ex ore vestro egredietur, donec veniat dies in quo dicam vobis : Clamate, et vociferamini.
      EN: Joshua had commanded the people, saying, “You must not shout; your voices must not be heard, nor must a word come out of your mouths until the day when I tell you, ‘Shout and raise a cry!’”

6:11  LA: Circuivit ergo arca Domini civitatem semel per diem, et reversa in castra mansit ibi.
      EN: So the ark of the Lord went around the city once a day and returned to the camp, where it remained.

6:12  LA: Igitur Josue de nocte consurgente, tulerunt sacerdotes arcam Domini,
      EN: Joshua rose during the night, and the priests took up the ark of the Lord.

6:13  LA: et septem ex eis septem buccinas, quarum in jubilæo usus est : præcedebantque arcam Domini ambulantes atque clangentes : et armatus populus ibat ante eos, vulgus autem reliquum sequebatur arcam, et buccinis personabat.
      EN: Seven of them took seven trumpets of the kind used at the jubilee, and went ahead of the ark of the Lord, walking and sounding them. The armed people went ahead of them, while the rest of the people followed the ark amid the sound of trumpets.

6:14  LA: Circuieruntque civitatem secundo die semel, et reversi sunt in castra. Sic fecerunt sex diebus.
      EN: On the second day they marched around the city once and returned to the camp. They did this for six days.

6:15  LA: Dies autem septimo, diluculo consurgentes, circuierunt urbem, sicut dispositum erat, septies.
      EN: On the seventh day they rose at dawn and marched around the city seven times, as had been arranged.

6:16  LA: Cumque septimo circuitu clangerent buccinis sacerdotes, dixit Josue ad omnem Israël : Vociferamini : tradidit enim vobis Dominus civitatem.
      EN: At the seventh circuit, when the priests sounded the trumpets, Joshua said to all Israel, “Shout! For the Lord has handed the city over to you.

6:17  LA: Sitque civitas hæc anathema, et omnia quæ in ea sunt, Domino : sola Rahab meretrix vivat, cum universis qui cum ea in domo sunt : abscondit enim nuntios quos direximus.
      EN: Let this city and everything in it be devoted to the Lord for destruction. Only Rahab the prostitute must live, with everyone who is with her in the house, because she hid the messengers we sent.

6:18  LA: Vos autem cavete ne de his, quæ præcepta sunt, quippiam contingatis, et sitis prævaricationis rei, et omnia castra Israël sub peccato sint atque turbentur.
      EN: But take care not to touch any of the things covered by this command, lest you be guilty of transgression and bring sin and trouble upon the whole camp of Israel.

6:19  LA: Quidquid autem auri et argenti fuerit, et vasorum æneorum ac ferri, Domino consecretur, repositum in thesauris ejus.
      EN: Whatever gold and silver there is, and vessels of bronze and iron, must be consecrated to the Lord and deposited in His treasuries.”

6:20  LA: Igitur omni populo vociferante, et clangentibus tubis, postquam in aures multitudinis vox sonitusque increpuit, muri illico corruerunt : et ascendit unusquisque per locum qui contra se erat : ceperuntque civitatem,
      EN: Then all the people shouted and the trumpets sounded. When the blast and the sound rang in the ears of the multitude, the walls immediately collapsed. Each person went up at the place opposite him, and they captured the city.

6:21  LA: et interfecerunt omnia quæ erant in ea, a viro usque ad mulierem, ab infante usque ad senem. Boves quoque et oves et asinos in ore gladii percusserunt.
      EN: They killed everything in it, from man to woman, from infant to old person. They also struck down oxen, sheep, and donkeys with the edge of the sword.

6:22  LA: Duobus autem viris, qui exploratores missi fuerant, dixit Josue : Ingredimini domum mulieris meretricis, et producite eam, et omnia quæ illius sunt, sicut illi juramento firmastis.
      EN: Joshua said to the two men who had been sent as scouts, “Enter the prostitute's house and bring her out, with everything belonging to her, as you pledged to her by oath.”

6:23  LA: Ingressique juvenes eduxerunt Rahab, et parentes ejus, fratres quoque, et cunctam supellectilem ac cognationem illius, et extra castra Israël manere fecerunt.
      EN: The young men went in and brought out Rahab and her parents, her brothers, all her belongings, and her relatives. They made them stay outside the camp of Israel.

6:24  LA: Urbem autem, et omnia quæ erant in ea, succenderunt, absque auro et argento, et vasis æneis, ac ferro, quæ in ærarium Domini consecrarunt.
      EN: They burned the city and everything in it, except the gold and silver, the bronze vessels, and the iron, which they consecrated to the Lord's treasury.

6:25  LA: Rahab vero meretricem, et domum patris ejus, et omnia quæ habebat, fecit Josue vivere, et habitaverunt in medio Israël, usque in præsentem diem : eo quod absconderit nuntios, quos miserat ut explorarent Jericho. In tempore illo, imprecatus est Josue, dicens :
      EN: But Joshua spared Rahab the prostitute, her father's household, and everything she had. They lived among Israel, as they do to this day, because she hid the messengers he had sent to explore Jericho. At that time Joshua pronounced this curse:

6:26  LA: Maledictus vir coram Domino, qui suscitaverit et ædificaverit civitatem Jericho. In primogenito suo fundamenta illius jaciat, et in novissimo liberorum ponat portas ejus.
      EN: “Cursed before the Lord be the man who raises up and rebuilds the city of Jericho. At the cost of his firstborn may he lay its foundations, and at the cost of his youngest child may he set up its gates.”

6:27  LA: Fuit ergo Dominus cum Josue, et nomen ejus vulgatum est in omni terra.
      EN: The Lord was with Joshua, and his name became known throughout the whole land.

JOSHUA 7

7:1  LA: Filii autem Israël prævaricati sunt mandatum, et usurpaverunt de anathemate. Nam Achan filius Charmi filii Zabdi filii Zare de tribu Juda tulit aliquid de anathemate : iratusque est Dominus contra filios Israël.
     EN: But the children of Israel transgressed the command and appropriated some of what was devoted to destruction. Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took something from what was devoted to destruction, and the Lord became angry with the children of Israel.

7:2  LA: Cumque mitteret Josue de Jericho viros contra Hai, quæ est juxta Bethaven, ad orientalem plagam oppidi Bethel, dixit eis : Ascendite, et explorate terram. Qui præcepta complentes exploraverunt Hai.
     EN: Joshua sent men from Jericho toward Ai, which is near Beth-aven, east of the town of Bethel, and said to them, “Go up and explore the land.” They carried out his orders and explored Ai.

7:3  LA: Et reversi dixerunt ei : Non ascendat omnis populus, sed duo vel tria millia virorum pergant, et deleant civitatem : quare omnis populus frustra vexabitur contra hostes paucissimos ?
     EN: They returned and said to him, “Do not let all the people go up. Let two or three thousand men go and destroy the city. Why should all the people be troubled needlessly against so few enemies?”

7:4  LA: Ascenderunt ergo tria millia pugnatorum. Qui statim terga vertentes,
     EN: So three thousand fighting men went up. They immediately turned their backs

7:5  LA: percussi sunt a viris urbis Hai, et corruerunt ex eis triginta sex homines : persecutique sunt eos adversarii de porta usque ad Sabarim, et ceciderunt per prona fugientes : pertimuitque cor populi, et instar aquæ liquefactum est.
     EN: and were struck down by the men of the city of Ai. Thirty-six of them fell. Their adversaries pursued them from the gate as far as Shebarim, and they fell while fleeing down the slopes. The people's hearts were terrified and melted like water.

7:6  LA: Josue vero scidit vestimenta sua, et pronus cecidit in terram coram arca Domini usque ad vesperam, tam ipse quam omnes senes Israël : miseruntque pulverem super capita sua,
     EN: Joshua tore his clothes and fell facedown on the ground before the ark of the Lord until evening, both he and all the elders of Israel. They put dust on their heads,

7:7  LA: et dixit Josue : Heu Domine Deus, quid voluisti traducere populum istum Jordanem fluvium, ut traderes nos in manus Amorrhæi, et perderes ? utinam ut cœpimus, mansissemus trans Jordanem.
     EN: and Joshua said, “Alas, Lord God! Why did You choose to bring this people across the river Jordan, only to hand us over to the Amorites and destroy us? If only we had remained beyond the Jordan, as we were at first!

7:8  LA: Mi Domine Deus, quid dicam, videns Israëlem hostibus suis terga vertentem ?
     EN: My Lord God, what can I say, when I see Israel turning its back to its enemies?

7:9  LA: Audient Chananæi, et omnes habitatores terræ, et pariter conglobati circumdabunt nos, atque delebunt nomen nostrum de terra : et quid facies magno nomini tuo ?
     EN: The Canaanites and all the inhabitants of the land will hear of it. They will gather together and surround us and wipe our name from the earth. What will You do for Your great name?”

7:10  LA: Dixitque Dominus ad Josue : Surge : cur jaces pronus in terra ?
      EN: The Lord said to Joshua, “Rise! Why are you lying facedown on the ground?

7:11  LA: Peccavit Israël, et prævaricatus est pactum meum : tuleruntque de anathemate, et furati sunt atque mentiti, et absconderunt inter vasa sua.
      EN: Israel has sinned and transgressed My covenant. They have taken some of what was devoted to destruction; they have stolen, lied, and hidden it among their belongings.

7:12  LA: Nec poterit Israël stare ante hostes suos, eosque fugiet : quia pollutus est anathemate. Non ero ultra vobiscum, donec conteratis eum qui hujus sceleris reus est.
      EN: Israel will not be able to stand before its enemies, but will flee from them, because it has been defiled by what was devoted to destruction. I will no longer be with you until you crush the one guilty of this crime.

7:13  LA: Surge, sanctifica populum, et dic eis : Sanctificamini in crastinum : hæc enim dicit Dominus Deus Israël : Anathema in medio tui est, Israël : non poteris stare coram hostibus tuis, donec deleatur ex te qui hoc contaminatus est scelere.
      EN: Rise, consecrate the people, and say to them, ‘Consecrate yourselves for tomorrow, for thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: What is devoted to destruction is among you, Israel. You will not be able to stand before your enemies until the one defiled by this crime is destroyed from among you.

7:14  LA: Accedetisque mane singuli per tribus vestras : et quamcumque tribum sors invenerit, accedet per cognationes suas, et cognatio per domos, domusque per viros.
      EN: In the morning you must come forward, each according to your tribes. Whichever tribe the lot identifies must come forward by its clans, and the clan by its households, and the household by its men.

7:15  LA: Et quicumque ille in hoc facinore fuerit deprehensus, comburetur igni cum omni substantia sua : quoniam prævaricatus est pactum Domini, et fecit nefas in Israël.
      EN: Whoever is caught in this offense must be burned with fire, together with all his possessions, because he transgressed the Lord's covenant and committed a wicked deed in Israel.’”

7:16  LA: Surgens itaque Josue mane, applicuit Israël per tribus suas, et inventa est tribus Juda.
      EN: Joshua rose in the morning and brought Israel forward by its tribes, and the tribe of Judah was identified.

7:17  LA: Quæ cum juxta familias suas esset oblata, inventa est familia Zare. Illam quoque per domos offerens, reperit Zabdi :
      EN: When it had been brought forward according to its families, the family of Zerah was identified. Bringing it forward by its households, he identified Zabdi.

7:18  LA: cujus domum in singulos dividens viros, invenit Achan filium Charmi filii Zabdi filii Zare de tribu Juda.
      EN: Dividing his household into individual men, he identified Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah.

7:19  LA: Et ait Josue ad Achan : Fili mi, da gloriam Domino Deo Israël, et confitere, atque indica mihi quid feceris, ne abscondas.
      EN: Joshua said to Achan, “My son, give glory to the Lord, the God of Israel, and confess. Tell me what you have done; do not hide it.”

7:20  LA: Responditque Achan Josue, et dixit ei : Vere ego peccavi Domino Deo Israël, et sic et sic feci.
      EN: Achan answered Joshua, “It is true: I have sinned against the Lord, the God of Israel. This is what I did.

7:21  LA: Vidi enim inter spolia pallium coccineum valde bonum, et ducentos siclos argenti, regulamque auream quinquaginta siclorum : et concupiscens abstuli, et abscondi in terra contra medium tabernaculi mei, argentumque fossa humo operui.
      EN: Among the spoils I saw a very fine scarlet cloak, two hundred shekels of silver, and a gold bar weighing fifty shekels. I coveted them, took them, and hid them in the ground in the middle of my tent; I buried the silver in a hole in the earth.”

7:22  LA: Misit ergo Josue ministros : qui currentes ad tabernaculum illius, repererunt cuncta abscondita in eodem loco, et argentum simul.
      EN: Joshua sent attendants, who ran to his tent and found everything hidden in that same place, together with the silver.

7:23  LA: Auferentesque de tentorio, tulerunt ea ad Josue, et ad omnes filios Israël, projeceruntque ante Dominum.
      EN: They took them from the tent and brought them to Joshua and all the children of Israel, and laid them before the Lord.

7:24  LA: Tollens itaque Josue Achan filium Zare, argentumque et pallium, et auream regulam, filios quoque et filias ejus, boves et asinos et oves, ipsumque tabernaculum, et cunctam supellectilem (et omnis Israël cum eo), duxerunt eos ad vallem Achor :
      EN: Then Joshua, with all Israel, took Achan son of Zerah, the silver, the cloak, the gold bar, his sons and daughters, his oxen, donkeys, and sheep, the tent itself, and all his belongings, and brought them to the Valley of Achor.

7:25  LA: ubi dixit Josue : Quia turbasti nos, exturbet te Dominus in die hac. Lapidavitque eum omnis Israël : et cuncta quæ illius erant, igne consumpta sunt.
      EN: There Joshua said, “Because you have brought trouble on us, may the Lord drive you out in trouble today.” All Israel stoned him, and everything belonging to him was consumed by fire.

7:26  LA: Congregaveruntque super eum acervum magnum lapidum, qui permanet usque in præsentem diem. Et aversus est furor Domini ab eis. Vocatumque est nomen loci illius, vallis Achor, usque hodie.
      EN: They piled a great heap of stones over him, which remains to this day. The Lord's fury turned away from them. That place has been called the Valley of Achor to this day.

JOSHUA 8

8:1  LA: Dixit autem Dominus ad Josue : Ne timeas, neque formides : tolle tecum omnem multitudinem pugnatorum, et consurgens ascende in oppidum Hai. Ecce tradidi in manu tua regem ejus et populum, urbemque et terram.
     EN: The Lord said to Joshua, “Do not be afraid or terrified. Take the whole multitude of fighting men with you, rise, and go up to the town of Ai. Look, I have handed over to you its king, its people, its city, and its land.

8:2  LA: Faciesque urbi Hai, et regi ejus, sicut fecisti Jericho, et regi illius : prædam vero, et omnia animantia diripietis vobis : pone insidias urbi post eam.
     EN: You must do to the city of Ai and its king as you did to Jericho and its king. But you may plunder the spoil and all the livestock for yourselves. Set an ambush behind the city.”

8:3  LA: Surrexitque Josue, et omnis exercitus bellatorum cum eo, ut ascenderent in Hai : et electa triginta millia virorum fortium misit nocte,
     EN: Joshua rose, and the whole army of fighting men with him, to go up to Ai. He chose thirty thousand strong men and sent them by night.

8:4  LA: præcepitque eis, dicens : Ponite insidias post civitatem, nec longius recedatis : et eritis omnes parati.
     EN: He commanded them, saying, “Set an ambush behind the city. Do not go too far away, and all of you be ready.

8:5  LA: Ego autem, et reliqua multitudo, quæ mecum est, accedemus ex adverso contra urbem. Cumque exierint contra nos, sicut ante fecimus, fugiemus, et terga vertemus,
     EN: I and the rest of the multitude with me will approach the city from the front. When they come out against us, we will flee and turn our backs, as we did before,

8:6  LA: donec persequentes ab urbe longius protrahantur : putabunt enim nos fugere sicut prius.
     EN: until their pursuit draws them far away from the city. They will think that we are fleeing as before.

8:7  LA: Nobis ergo fugientibus, et illis persequentibus, consurgetis de insidiis, et vastabitis civitatem : tradetque eam Dominus Deus vester in manus vestras.
     EN: While we flee and they pursue, you must rise from the ambush and lay waste the city. The Lord your God will hand it over to you.

8:8  LA: Cumque ceperitis, succendite eam, et sic omnia facietis, ut jussi.
     EN: When you have captured it, burn it. Do everything as I have ordered.”

8:9  LA: Dimisitque eos, et perrexerunt ad locum insidiarum, sederuntque inter Bethel et Hai, ad occidentalem plagam urbis Hai : Josue autem nocte illa in medio mansit populi,
     EN: He sent them off, and they went to the place of ambush and took up positions between Bethel and Ai, west of the city of Ai. Joshua stayed that night among the people.

8:10  LA: surgensque diluculo recensuit socios, et ascendit cum senioribus in fronte exercitus, vallatus auxilio pugnatorum.
      EN: Rising at dawn, he mustered his companions and went up with the elders at the front of the army, surrounded by the support of the fighting men.

8:11  LA: Cumque venissent et ascendissent ex adverso civitatis, steterunt ad septentrionalem urbis plagam, inter quam et eos erat vallis media.
      EN: They came up opposite the city and halted on its northern side, with a valley between it and them.

8:12  LA: Quinque autem millia viros elegerat, et posuerat in insidiis inter Bethel et Hai ex occidentali parte ejusdem civitatis :
      EN: He had chosen five thousand men and placed them in ambush between Bethel and Ai, on the western side of that city.

8:14  LA: Quod cum vidisset rex Hai, festinavit mane, et egressus est cum omni exercitu civitatis, direxitque aciem contra desertum, ignorans quod post tergum laterent insidiæ.
      EN: When the king of Ai saw this, he hurried out in the morning with the city's whole army and drew up a battle line facing the wilderness, unaware that an ambush lay hidden behind him.

8:15  LA: Josue vero et omnis Israël cesserunt loco, simulantes metum, et fugientes per solitudinis viam.
      EN: Joshua and all Israel gave ground, pretending fear and fleeing along the road into the wilderness.

8:16  LA: At illi vociferantes pariter, et se mutuo cohortantes, persecuti sunt eos. Cumque recessissent a civitate,
      EN: The others shouted together and urged one another on, pursuing them. When they had gone away from the city,

8:17  LA: et ne unus quidem in urbe Hai et Bethel remansisset qui non persequeretur Israël (sicut eruperant aperta oppida relinquentes),
      EN: and not even one man remained in Ai or Bethel who was not pursuing Israel—for they had rushed out, leaving the towns open—

8:18  LA: dixit Dominus ad Josue : Leva clypeum, qui in manu tua est, contra urbem Hai, quoniam tibi tradam eam.
      EN: the Lord said to Joshua, “Raise the shield in your hand toward the city of Ai, for I will hand it over to you.”

8:19  LA: Cumque elevasset clypeum ex adverso civitatis, insidiæ, quæ latebant, surrexerunt confestim : et pergentes ad civitatem, ceperunt, et succenderunt eam.
      EN: When he raised the shield toward the city, those hidden in the ambush immediately rose. They went to the city, captured it, and set it on fire.
