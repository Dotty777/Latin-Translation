MICAH 1

1:1  LA: Verbum Domini, quod factum est ad Michæam Morasthiten, in diebus Joathan, Achaz, et Ezechiæ, regum Juda, quod vidit super Samariam et Jerusalem.
     EN: The word of the Lord that came to Micah of Moresheth in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.

1:2  LA: [Audite, populi omnes, et attendat terra, et plenitudo ejus : et sit Dominus Deus vobis in testem, Dominus de templo sancto suo.
     EN: Hear, all peoples; let the earth and all that fills it pay attention. Let the Lord God be a witness against you, the Lord from His holy temple.

1:3  LA: Quia ecce Dominus egredietur de loco suo, et descendet, et calcabit super excelsa terræ.
     EN: For see, the Lord will go out from His place, descend, and tread upon the heights of the earth.

1:4  LA: Et consumentur montes subtus eum, et valles scindentur sicut cera a facie ignis, et sicut aquæ quæ decurrunt in præceps.
     EN: The mountains will melt beneath Him, and the valleys split apart like wax before fire, like waters rushing down a steep slope.

1:5  LA: In scelere Jacob omne istud, et in peccatis domus Israël. Quod scelus Jacob ? nonne Samaria ? et quæ excelsa Judæ ? nonne Jerusalem ?
     EN: All this is because of Jacob's crime and the sins of the house of Israel. What is Jacob's crime? Is it not Samaria? And what are Judah's high places? Are they not Jerusalem?

1:6  LA: Et ponam Samariam quasi acervum lapidum in agro, cum plantatur vinea ; et detraham in vallem lapides ejus, et fundamenta ejus revelabo.
     EN: “I will make Samaria like a heap of stones in a field when a vineyard is planted. I will drag her stones down into the valley and uncover her foundations.

1:7  LA: Et omnia sculptilia ejus concidentur, et omnes mercedes ejus comburentur igne, et omnia idola ejus ponam in perditionem, quia de mercedibus meretricis congregata sunt, et usque ad mercedem meretricis revertentur.
     EN: All her carved images will be broken, all her wages burned with fire; I will bring all her idols to destruction, for they were gathered from a prostitute's wages, and to a prostitute's wages they will return.”

1:8  LA: Super hoc plangam, et ululabo ; vadam spoliatus, et nudus ; faciam planctum velut draconum, et luctum quasi struthionum :
     EN: Over this I will lament and howl; I will go stripped and naked. I will make a lament like dragons and a mourning like ostriches,

1:9  LA: quia desperata est plaga ejus, quia venit usque ad Judam ; tetigit portam populi mei usque ad Jerusalem.
     EN: for her wound is beyond hope; it has reached Judah, touched the gate of my people, as far as Jerusalem.

1:10  LA: In Geth nolite annuntiare ; lacrimis ne ploretis ; in domo pulveris pulvere vos conspergite.
      EN: Do not announce it in Gath; do not weep with tears. In the house of dust, sprinkle yourselves with dust.

1:11  LA: Et transite vobis, habitatio pulchra, confusa ignominia : non est egressa quæ habitat in exitu : planctum domus vicina accipiet ex vobis, quæ stetit sibimet.
      EN: Pass on, beautiful dwelling, confounded with disgrace. She who dwells at the exit has not gone out; the neighboring house, which stood by itself, will receive lamentation from you.

1:12  LA: Quia infirmata est in bonum, quæ habitat in amaritudinibus ; quia descendit malum a Domino in portam Jerusalem.
      EN: For she who dwells in bitterness has become weak in hope of good, for calamity has come down from the Lord to Jerusalem's gate.

1:13  LA: Tumultus quadrigæ stuporis habitanti Lachis : principium peccati est filiæ Sion, quia in te inventa sunt scelera Israël.
      EN: The clamor of a bewildering chariot for the inhabitant of Lachish! She is the beginning of sin for the daughter of Zion, for in you the crimes of Israel were found.

1:14  LA: Propterea dabit emissarios super hæreditatem Geth, domus mendacii in deceptionem regibus Israël.
      EN: Therefore she will send messengers concerning the inheritance of Gath; houses of falsehood will deceive the kings of Israel.

1:15  LA: Adhuc hæredem adducam tibi quæ habitas in Maresa ; usque ad Odollam veniet gloria Israël.
      EN: “I will yet bring an heir to you who dwell in Mareshah; the glory of Israel will come as far as Adullam.”

1:16  LA: Decalvare, et tondere super filios deliciarum tuarum ; dilata calvitium tuum sicut aquila, quoniam captivi ducti sunt ex te.]
      EN: Make yourself bald and shear yourself for the children of your delight. Widen your baldness like an eagle, for they have been led captive away from you.

MICAH 2

2:1  LA: Væ qui cogitatis inutile, et operamini malum in cubilibus vestris ! In luce matutina faciunt illud, quoniam contra Deum est manus eorum.
     EN: Woe to you who devise what is worthless and work evil on your beds! At morning light they carry it out, because their hand is against God.

2:2  LA: Et concupierunt agros, et violenter tulerunt : et rapuerunt domos, et calumniabantur virum, et domum ejus : virum, et hæreditatem ejus.
     EN: They coveted fields and seized them by force; they stole houses and oppressed a man and his household, a man and his inheritance.

2:3  LA: Idcirco hæc dicit Dominus : Ecce ego cogito super familiam istam malum, unde non auferetis colla vestra, et non ambulabitis superbi : quoniam tempus pessimum est.
     EN: Therefore thus says the Lord: “See, I am devising calamity against this family, from which you will not remove your necks, and you will not walk proudly, for it is a very evil time.

2:4  LA: In die illa sumetur super vos parabola, et cantabitur canticum cum suavitate, dicentium : Depopulatione vastati sumus ; pars populi mei commutata est : quomodo recedet a me, cum revertatur, qui regiones nostras dividat ?
     EN: On that day a proverb will be taken up against you, and a song sung sweetly by those who say, ‘We have been utterly devastated; my people's portion has been exchanged. How will it depart from me, when the one who divides our territories returns?’

2:5  LA: Propter hoc non erit tibi mittens funiculum sortis in cœtu Domini.
     EN: Therefore you will have no one to cast the measuring cord for an allotment in the assembly of the Lord.

2:6  LA: Ne loquamini loquentes ; non stillabit super istos, non comprehendet confusio.
     EN: ‘Do not speak,’ you who speak! He will not pour out speech upon these; shame will not overtake them.

2:7  LA: Dicit domus Jacob : Numquid abbreviatus est spiritus Domini, aut tales sunt cogitationes ejus ? nonne verba mea bona sunt cum eo qui recte graditur ?
     EN: The house of Jacob says, ‘Has the Lord's Spirit been shortened, or are these His thoughts?’ Are My words not good to the one who walks uprightly?

2:8  LA: et e contrario populus meus in adversarium consurrexit. Desuper tunica pallium sustulistis : et eos qui transibant simpliciter convertistis in bellum.
     EN: But on the contrary My people have risen as an enemy. You took the cloak from over the tunic, and those who passed by harmlessly you turned toward war.

2:9  LA: Mulieres populi mei ejecistis de domo deliciarum suarum ; a parvulis earum tulistis laudem meam in perpetuum.
     EN: You drove the women of My people from their delightful houses; from their little ones you took My praise forever.

2:10  LA: Surgite, et ite, quia non habetis hic requiem : propter immunditiam ejus corrumpetur putredine pessima.
      EN: Rise and go, for you have no rest here; because of its uncleanness it will be corrupted with the worst decay.

2:11  LA: Utinam non essem vir habens spiritum, et mendacium potius loquerer ! Stillabo tibi in vinum et in ebrietatem ; et erit super quem stillatur populus iste.
      EN: Would that I were not a man possessing the spirit and spoke falsehood instead! I will pour out speech to you about wine and drunkenness, and this people will be the one upon whom it is poured.

2:12  LA: Congregatione congregabo, Jacob, totum te ; in unum conducam reliquias Israël : pariter ponam illum quasi gregem in ovili, quasi pecus in medio caularum : tumultuabuntur a multitudine hominum.
      EN: I will surely gather all of you, Jacob; I will bring Israel's remnant together. I will place them together like a flock in a fold, like livestock in the midst of pens; they will be noisy with the multitude of people.

2:13  LA: Ascendet enim pandens iter ante eos : divident, et transibunt portam, et ingredientur per eam : et transibit rex eorum coram eis, et Dominus in capite eorum.]
      EN: For one opening the way will go up before them; they will break through, pass the gate, and enter through it. Their king will pass before them, and the Lord at their head.”

MICAH 3

3:1  LA: Et dixi : Audite, princeps Jacob, et duces domus Israël : numquid non vestrum est scire judicium,
     EN: I said: “Hear, prince of Jacob and leaders of the house of Israel! Is it not your part to know justice,

3:2  LA: qui odio habetis bonum, et diligitis malum ; qui violenter tollitis pelles eorum desuper eis, et carnem eorum desuper ossibus eorum ;
     EN: you who hate good and love evil, who violently tear their skin from them and their flesh from their bones,

3:3  LA: qui comederunt carnem populi mei, et pellem eorum desuper excoriaverunt, et ossa eorum confregerunt, et conciderunt sicut in lebete, et quasi carnem in medio ollæ ?
     EN: who have eaten My people's flesh, stripped their skin from them, broken their bones, and chopped them up as in a cauldron, like flesh in the midst of a pot?”

3:4  LA: Tunc clamabunt ad Dominum, et non exaudiet eos, et abscondet faciem suam ab eis in tempore illo, sicut nequiter egerunt in adinventionibus suis.
     EN: Then they will cry to the Lord, and He will not answer them. He will hide His face from them at that time, as they acted wickedly in their contrivances.

3:5  LA: Hæc dicit Dominus super prophetas, qui seducunt populum meum : qui mordent dentibus suis, et prædicant pacem ; et si quis non dederit in ore eorum quippiam, sanctificant super eum prælium.
     EN: Thus says the Lord concerning the prophets who lead My people astray, who bite with their teeth and proclaim peace, but consecrate a war against anyone who puts nothing in their mouths:

3:6  LA: Propterea nox vobis pro visione erit, et tenebræ vobis pro divinatione ; et occumbet sol super prophetas, et obtenebrabitur super eos dies.
     EN: “Therefore you will have night instead of vision and darkness instead of divination. The sun will set upon the prophets, and the day will grow dark over them.

3:7  LA: Et confundentur qui vident visiones, et confundentur divini ; et operient omnes vultos suos, quia non est responsum Dei.
     EN: Those who see visions will be ashamed, and the diviners confounded; they will all cover their faces, because there is no answer from God.”

3:8  LA: Verumtamen ego repletus sum fortitudine spiritus Domini, judicio, et virtute, ut annuntiem Jacob scelus suum, et Israël peccatum suum.
     EN: But I am filled with the strength of the Lord's Spirit, with justice and might, to declare to Jacob his crime and to Israel his sin.

3:9  LA: Audite hoc, principes domus Jacob, et judices domus Israël, qui abominamini judicium, et omnia recta pervertitis :
     EN: Hear this, princes of the house of Jacob and judges of the house of Israel, who abhor justice and pervert everything straight,

3:10  LA: qui ædificatis Sion in sanguinibus, et Jerusalem in iniquitate.
      EN: who build Zion with bloodshed and Jerusalem with iniquity.

3:11  LA: Principes ejus in muneribus judicabant, et sacerdotes ejus in mercede docebant, et prophetæ ejus in pecunia divinabant : et super Dominum requiescebant, dicentes : Numquid non Dominus in medio nostrum ? non venient super nos mala.
      EN: Her princes judged for gifts, her priests taught for a fee, and her prophets divined for money. Yet they leaned upon the Lord, saying, “Is the Lord not among us? No evils will come upon us.”

3:12  LA: Propter hoc, causa vestri, Sion quasi ager arabitur, et Jerusalem quasi acervus lapidum erit, et mons templi in excelsa silvarum.]
      EN: Therefore, because of you, Zion will be plowed like a field, Jerusalem will become a heap of stones, and the mountain of the temple wooded heights.

MICAH 4

4:1  LA: Et erit : in novissimo dierum erit mons domus Domini præparatus in vertice montium, et sublimis super colles : et fluent ad eum populi,
     EN: In the last days the mountain of the Lord's house will be established on the summit of the mountains, raised above the hills, and peoples will stream to it.

4:2  LA: et properabunt gentes multæ, et dicent : Venite, ascendamus ad montem Domini, et ad domum Dei Jacob : et docebit nos de viis suis, et ibimus in semitis ejus, quia de Sion egredietur lex, et verbum Domini de Jerusalem.
     EN: Many nations will hasten and say, “Come, let us go up to the mountain of the Lord, to the house of the God of Jacob. He will teach us His ways, and we will walk in His paths, for the law will go out from Zion and the Lord's word from Jerusalem.”

4:3  LA: Et judicabit inter populos multos, et corripiet gentes fortes usque in longinquum : et concident gladios suos in vomeres, et hastas suas in ligones : non sumet gens adversus gentem gladium, et non discent ultra belligerare.
     EN: He will judge between many peoples and correct strong nations far away. They will cut their swords into plowshares and their spears into hoes; nation will not take up sword against nation, nor will they learn warfare any longer.

4:4  LA: Et sedebit vir subtus vitem suam et subtus ficum suam, et non erit qui deterreat, quia os Domini exercituum locutum est.
     EN: Each will sit beneath his vine and beneath his fig tree, with no one to terrify him, for the mouth of the Lord of hosts has spoken.

4:5  LA: Quia omnes populi ambulabunt unusquisque in nomine dei sui ; nos autem ambulabimus in nomine Domini Dei nostri, in æternum et ultra.]
     EN: For all peoples will walk, each in the name of his god; but we will walk in the name of the Lord our God forever and beyond.

4:6  LA: [In die illa, dicit Dominus, congregabo claudicantem, et eam quam ejeceram colligam, et quam afflixeram :
     EN: “On that day,” says the Lord, “I will gather the lame one, assemble her whom I drove out and whom I afflicted.

4:7  LA: et ponam claudicantem in reliquias, et eam quæ laboraverat, in gentem robustam : et regnabit Dominus super eos in monte Sion, ex hoc nunc et usque in æternum.
     EN: I will make the lame one a remnant and her who suffered a strong nation, and the Lord will reign over them on Mount Zion, from now and forever.

4:8  LA: Et tu, turris gregis nebulosa filiæ Sion, usque ad te veniet, et veniet potestas prima, regnum filiæ Jerusalem.
     EN: And you, clouded tower of the flock of the daughter of Zion, to you it will come: the former dominion will come, the kingdom of the daughter of Jerusalem.

4:9  LA: Nunc quare mœrore contraheris ? numquid rex non est tibi, aut consiliarius tuus periit, quia comprehendit te dolor sicut parturientem ?
     EN: Now why are you contracted with grief? Have you no king, or has your counselor perished, that pain has seized you like a woman giving birth?

4:10  LA: Dole et satage, filia Sion, quasi parturiens, quia nunc egredieris de civitate, et habitabis in regione, et venies usque ad Babylonem : ibi liberaberis, ibi redimet te Dominus de manu inimicorum tuorum.
      EN: Suffer and struggle, daughter of Zion, like a woman giving birth, for now you will leave the city, dwell in the countryside, and come as far as Babylon. There you will be freed; there the Lord will redeem you from the hand of your enemies.

4:11  LA: Et nunc congregatæ sunt super te gentes multæ, quæ dicunt : Lapidetur, et aspiciat in Sion oculus noster.
      EN: Now many nations have gathered against you, saying, ‘Let her be stoned, and let our eye look upon Zion.’

4:12  LA: Ipsi autem non cognoverunt cogitationes Domini, et non intellexerunt consilium ejus, quia congregavit eos quasi fœnum areæ.
      EN: But they have not known the Lord's thoughts or understood His plan, for He has gathered them like hay upon a threshing floor.

4:13  LA: Surge, et tritura, filia Sion, quia cornu tuum ponam ferreum, et ungulas tuas ponam æreas ; et comminues populos multos, et interficies Domino rapinas eorum, et fortitudinem eorum Domino universæ terræ.]
      EN: Rise and thresh, daughter of Zion, for I will make your horn iron and your hoofs bronze. You will crush many peoples and devote their plunder to destruction for the Lord, their strength to the Lord of all the earth.”

4:14  LA: Nunc vastaberis, filia latronis. Obsidionem posuerunt super nos : in virga percutient maxillam judicis Israël.]
      EN: Now you will be devastated, daughter of a robber. They have laid siege against us; with a rod they will strike the cheek of Israel's judge.

MICAH 5

5:1  LA: [Et tu, Bethlehem Ephrata, parvulus es in millibus Juda ; ex te mihi egredietur qui sit dominator in Israël, et egressus ejus ab initio, a diebus æternitatis.
     EN: “And you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, are little among Judah's thousands. From you will come forth for Me one who is to rule in Israel, whose coming forth is from the beginning, from the days of eternity.”

5:2  LA: Propter hoc dabit eos usque ad tempus in quo parturiens pariet, et reliquiæ fratrum ejus convertentur ad filios Israël.
     EN: Therefore He will give them up until the time when she who is in labor gives birth, and the remnant of his brothers will return to the children of Israel.

5:3  LA: Et stabit, et pascet in fortitudine Domini, in sublimitate nominis Domini Dei sui : et convertentur, quia nunc magnificabitur usque ad terminos terræ.
     EN: He will stand and pasture in the strength of the Lord, in the majesty of the name of the Lord his God. They will return, for now he will be magnified to the ends of the earth.

5:4  LA: Et erit iste pax : cum venerit Assyrius in terram nostram, et quando calcaverit domibus nostris, et suscitabimus super eum septem pastores et octo primates homines ;
     EN: He will be peace. When the Assyrian comes into our land and treads in our houses, we will raise against him seven shepherds and eight leading men.

5:5  LA: et pascent terram Assur in gladio, et terram Nemrod in lanceis ejus, et liberabit ab Assur cum venerit in terram nostram, et cum calcaverit in finibus nostris.
     EN: They will pasture the land of Assyria with the sword and the land of Nimrod with its spears; he will rescue us from Assyria when it comes into our land and treads within our borders.

5:6  LA: Et erunt reliquiæ Jacob in medio populorum multorum quasi ros a Domino, et quasi stillæ super herbam, quæ non exspectat virum, et non præstolatur filios hominum.
     EN: The remnant of Jacob will be among many peoples like dew from the Lord, like drops upon grass, which does not wait for man or look to the children of humankind.

5:7  LA: Et erunt reliquiæ Jacob in gentibus, in medio populorum multorum, quasi leo in jumentis silvarum, et quasi catulus leonis in gregibus pecorum, qui cum transierit, et conculcaverit, et ceperit, non est qui eruat.
     EN: The remnant of Jacob will be among the nations, in the midst of many peoples, like a lion among forest beasts, like a lion cub among flocks of sheep; when it passes through, tramples, and seizes, there is no one to rescue.

5:8  LA: Exaltabitur manus tua super hostes tuos, et omnes inimici tui interibunt.
     EN: Your hand will be raised above your foes, and all your enemies will perish.

5:9  LA: Et erit in die illa, dicit Dominus : auferam equos tuos de medio tui, et disperdam quadrigas tuas.
     EN: “On that day,” says the Lord, “I will remove your horses from your midst and destroy your chariots.

5:10  LA: Et perdam civitates terræ tuæ, et destruam omnes munitiones tuas : et auferam maleficia de manu tua, et divinationes non erunt in te :
      EN: I will destroy the cities of your land and demolish all your fortifications. I will remove sorceries from your hand, and there will be no divinations among you.

MICAH 6

6:1  LA: Audite quæ Dominus loquitur : Surge, contende judicio adversum montes, et audiant colles vocem tuam.
     EN: Hear what the Lord says: “Rise, plead your case against the mountains, and let the hills hear your voice.”

6:2  LA: Audiant montes judicium Domini, et fortia fundamenta terræ ; quia judicium Domini cum populo suo, et cum Israël dijudicabitur.
     EN: Let the mountains and the strong foundations of the earth hear the Lord's case, for the Lord has a case against His people, and He will enter into judgment with Israel.

6:3  LA: Popule meus, quid feci tibi ? aut quid molestus fui tibi ? Responde mihi.
     EN: “My people, what have I done to you, or how have I troubled you? Answer Me.

6:4  LA: Quia eduxi te de terra Ægypti, et de domo servientium liberavi te, et misi ante faciem tuam Moysen, et Aaron, et Mariam.
     EN: For I brought you out of the land of Egypt and freed you from the house of slavery; I sent Moses, Aaron, and Miriam before you.

6:5  LA: Popule meus, memento, quæso, quid cogitaverit Balach, rex Moab, et quid responderit ei Balaam, filius Beor, de Setim usque ad Galgalam, ut cognosceres justitias Domini.
     EN: My people, remember, I ask you, what Balak king of Moab devised and what Balaam son of Beor answered him, from Shittim to Gilgal, that you might know the Lord's righteous deeds.”

6:6  LA: Quid dignum offeram Domino ? curvabo genu Deo excelso ? Numquid offeram ei holocautomata et vitulos anniculos ?
     EN: What worthy thing shall I offer the Lord? Shall I bend the knee to God on high? Shall I offer Him burnt offerings and year-old calves?

6:7  LA: numquid placari potest Dominus in millibus arietum, aut in multis millibus hircorum pinguium ? numquid dabo primogenitum meum pro scelere meo, fructum ventris mei pro peccato animæ meæ ?
     EN: Can the Lord be appeased with thousands of rams or with many thousands of fat goats? Shall I give my firstborn for my crime, the fruit of my womb for the sin of my soul?

6:8  LA: Indicabo tibi, o homo, quid sit bonum, et quid Dominus requirat a te : utique facere judicium, et diligere misericordiam, et sollicitum ambulare cum Deo tuo.
     EN: I will show you, O man, what is good and what the Lord requires of you: to do justice, love mercy, and walk attentively with your God.

6:9  LA: Vox Domini ad civitatem clamat, et salus erit timentibus nomen tuum : audite, tribus, et quis approbabit illud ?
     EN: The Lord's voice cries to the city, and salvation will belong to those who fear Your name. Hear, tribes; and who will approve it?

6:10  LA: Adhuc ignis in domo impii thesauri iniquitatis, et mensura minor iræ plena.
      EN: There is still fire in the wicked man's house, treasures of iniquity, and a diminished measure full of wrath.

6:11  LA: Numquid justificabo stateram impiam, et saccelli pondera dolosa ?
      EN: Shall I justify a wicked balance and a bag of deceitful weights?

6:12  LA: In quibus divites ejus repleti sunt iniquitate, et habitantes in ea loquebantur mendacium, et lingua eorum fraudulenta in ore eorum.
      EN: By these her rich people were filled with iniquity; those dwelling in her spoke lies, and their tongue was deceitful in their mouth.

6:13  LA: Et ego ergo cœpi percutere te perditione super peccatis tuis.
      EN: Therefore I too have begun to strike you with destruction for your sins.

6:14  LA: Tu comedes, et non saturaberis, et humiliatio tua in medio tui : et apprehendes, et non salvabis, et quos salvaveris, in gladium dabo.
      EN: You will eat and not be satisfied, and your humiliation will be within you. You will take hold but not save, and those whom you save I will give to the sword.

6:15  LA: Tu seminabis, et non metes : tu calcabis olivam, et non ungeris oleo ; et mustum, et non bibes vinum.
      EN: You will sow and not reap; you will tread olives and not anoint yourself with oil, and tread new wine but not drink the wine.

6:16  LA: Et custodisti præcepta Amri, et omne opus domus Achab, et ambulasti in voluntatibus eorum : ut darem te in perditionem, et habitantes in ea in sibilum, et opprobrium populi mei portabitis.]
      EN: You kept the precepts of Omri and every practice of the house of Ahab and walked according to their wishes, so that I would give you over to destruction and her inhabitants to hissing; you will bear the reproach of My people.

MICAH 7

7:1  LA: Væ mihi, quia factus sum sicut qui colligit in autumno racemos vindemiæ ! non est botrus ad comedendum, præcoquas ficus desideravit anima mea.
     EN: Woe to me, for I have become like one gathering clusters after the autumn vintage! There is no bunch to eat; my soul has longed for early figs.

7:2  LA: Periit sanctus de terra, et rectus in hominibus non est : omnes in sanguine insidiantur ; vir fratrem suum ad mortem venatur.
     EN: The holy one has perished from the land; there is no upright person among humankind. They all lie in wait for blood; each hunts his brother to death.

7:3  LA: Malum manuum suarum dicunt bonum : princeps postulat, et judex in reddendo est ; et magnus locutus est desiderium animæ suæ, et conturbaverunt eam.
     EN: They call the evil of their hands good. The prince demands, the judge is intent on payment, and the great man has spoken the desire of his soul; they have thrown it into confusion.

7:4  LA: Qui optimus in eis est, quasi paliurus, et qui rectus, quasi spina de sepe. Dies speculationis tuæ, visitatio tua venit : nunc erit vastitas eorum.
     EN: The best among them is like a thornbush, and the upright like a thorn from a hedge. The day of your watching, your visitation, has come; now will be their devastation.

7:5  LA: Nolite credere amico, et nolite confidere in duce : ab ea quæ dormit in sinu tuo custodi claustra oris tui.
     EN: Do not trust a friend or put confidence in a leader; guard the doors of your mouth from her who sleeps in your embrace.

7:6  LA: Quia filius contumeliam facit patri, et filia consurgit adversus matrem suam : nurus adversus socrum suam, et inimici hominis domestici ejus.]
     EN: For a son insults his father, a daughter rises against her mother, a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law, and a man's enemies are those of his own household.

7:7  LA: [Ego autem ad Dominum aspiciam ; exspectabo Deum, salvatorem meum : audiet me Deus meus.
     EN: But I will look toward the Lord; I will wait for God my Savior. My God will hear me.

7:8  LA: Ne læteris, inimica mea, super me, quia cecidi : consurgam cum sedero in tenebris : Dominus lux mea est.
     EN: Do not rejoice over me, my enemy, because I have fallen. I will rise; when I sit in darkness, the Lord is my light.

7:9  LA: Iram Domini portabo, quoniam peccavi ei, donec causam meam judicet, et faciat judicium meum. Educet me in lucem : videbo justitiam ejus.
     EN: I will bear the Lord's anger, for I have sinned against Him, until He judges my case and renders judgment for me. He will lead me out into the light; I will see His righteousness.

7:10  LA: Et aspiciet inimica mea, et operietur confusione, quæ dicit ad me : Ubi est Dominus Deus tuus ? Oculi mei videbunt in eam : nunc erit in conculcationem ut lutum platearum.
      EN: My enemy will see and be covered with shame, she who says to me, “Where is the Lord your God?” My eyes will look upon her; now she will be trampled like the mud of the streets.

7:11  LA: Dies, ut ædificentur maceriæ tuæ ; in die illa longe fiet lex.
      EN: A day for your walls to be built! On that day the law will be carried far away.

7:12  LA: In die illa et usque ad te veniet de Assur, et usque ad civitates munitas, et a civitatibus munitis usque ad flumen, et ad mare de mari, et ad montem de monte.
      EN: On that day one will come to you from Assyria and as far as the fortified cities, from the fortified cities to the river, from sea to sea, and from mountain to mountain.

7:13  LA: Et terra erit in desolationem propter habitatores suos, et propter fructum cogitationum eorum.]
      EN: The land will become a desolation because of its inhabitants and because of the fruit of their thoughts.

7:14  LA: [Pasce populum tuum in virga tua, gregem hæreditatis tuæ, habitantes solos, in saltu, in medio Carmeli. Pascentur Basan et Galaad juxta dies antiquos.
      EN: Pasture Your people with Your rod, the flock of Your inheritance, dwelling alone in a forest in the midst of Carmel. They will pasture in Bashan and Gilead as in the days of old.

7:15  LA: Secundum dies egressionis tuæ de terra Ægypti, ostendam ei mirabilia.
      EN: “As in the days of your departure from the land of Egypt, I will show him wonders.”

7:16  LA: Videbunt gentes, et confundentur super omni fortitudine sua. Ponent manum super os, aures eorum surdæ erunt.
      EN: Nations will see and be ashamed of all their strength. They will put a hand over their mouth; their ears will become deaf.

7:17  LA: Lingent pulverem sicut serpentes ; velut reptilia terræ perturbabuntur in ædibus suis. Dominum Deum nostrum formidabunt, et timebunt te.
      EN: They will lick dust like serpents; like creeping things of the earth they will be disturbed in their dwellings. They will dread the Lord our God and fear You.

7:18  LA: Quis, Deus, similis tui, qui aufers iniquitatem, et transis peccatum reliquiarum hæreditatis tuæ ? Non immittet ultra furorem suum, quoniam volens misericordiam est.
      EN: Who, O God, is like You, who take away iniquity and pass over the sin of the remnant of Your inheritance? He will not send forth His fury again, for He delights in mercy.

7:19  LA: Revertetur, et miserebitur nostri ; deponet iniquitates nostras, et projiciet in profundum maris omnia peccata nostra.
      EN: He will return and have mercy on us; He will put away our iniquities and cast all our sins into the depths of the sea.

7:20  LA: Dabis veritatem Jacob, misericordiam Abraham, quæ jurasti patribus nostris a diebus antiquis.]
      EN: You will give faithfulness to Jacob and mercy to Abraham, which You swore to our fathers from the days of old.
