1 SAMUEL 1

1:1  LA: Fuit vir unus de Ramathaimsophim, de monte Ephraim, et nomen ejus Elcana, filius Jeroham, filii Eliu, filii Thohu, filii Suph, Ephrathæus :
     EN: There was a man from Ramathaim-zophim in the hill country of Ephraim, named Elkanah, son of Jeroham, son of Elihu, son of Tohu, son of Zuph, an Ephrathite.

1:2  LA: et habuit duas uxores, nomen uni Anna, et nomen secundæ Phenenna. Fueruntque Phenennæ filii : Annæ autem non erant liberi.
     EN: He had two wives: one was named Hannah, and the second Peninnah. Peninnah had children, but Hannah had none.

1:3  LA: Et ascendebat vir ille de civitate sua statutis diebus, ut adoraret et sacrificaret Domino exercituum in Silo. Erant autem ibi duo filii Heli, Ophni et Phinees, sacerdotes Domini.
     EN: This man would go up from his city at the appointed times to worship and sacrifice to the Lord of hosts at Shiloh. The two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were there as priests of the Lord.

1:4  LA: Venit ergo dies, et immolavit Elcana, deditque Phenennæ uxori suæ, et cunctis filiis ejus et filiabus, partes :
     EN: When the day came, Elkanah offered sacrifice and gave portions to his wife Peninnah and all her sons and daughters.

1:5  LA: Annæ autem dedit partem unam tristis, quia Annam diligebat. Dominus autem concluserat vulvam ejus.
     EN: But to Hannah he sadly gave one portion, because he loved Hannah. The Lord, however, had closed her womb.

1:6  LA: Affligebat quoque eam æmula ejus, et vehementer angebat, in tantum ut exprobraret quod Dominus conclusisset vulvam ejus :
     EN: Her rival also tormented her and distressed her greatly, even taunting her because the Lord had closed her womb.

1:7  LA: sicque faciebat per singulos annos : cum redeunte tempore ascenderent ad templum Domini, et sic provocabat eam : porro illa flebat, et non capiebat cibum.
     EN: She did this year after year: whenever the time returned for them to go up to the Lord's temple, she provoked her in this way. Hannah would weep and take no food.

1:8  LA: Dixit ergo ei Elcana vir suus : Anna, cur fles ? et quare non comedis ? et quam ob rem affligitur cor tuum ? numquid non ego melior tibi sum, quam decem filii ?
     EN: Her husband Elkanah said to her, “Hannah, why are you crying? Why do you not eat, and why is your heart distressed? Am I not better to you than ten sons?”

1:9  LA: Surrexit autem Anna postquam comederat et biberat in Silo. Et Heli sacerdote sedente super sellam ante postes templi Domini,
     EN: Hannah rose after she had eaten and drunk at Shiloh. While Eli the priest was sitting on his seat before the doorposts of the Lord's temple,

1:10  LA: cum esset Anna amaro animo, oravit ad Dominum, flens largiter,
      EN: Hannah, bitter in spirit, prayed to the Lord, weeping freely.

1:11  LA: et votum vovit, dicens : Domine exercituum, si respiciens videris afflictionem famulæ tuæ, et recordatus mei fueris, nec oblitus ancillæ tuæ, dederisque servæ tuæ sexum virilem : dabo eum Domino omnibus diebus vitæ ejus, et novacula non ascendet super caput ejus.
      EN: She made a vow, saying, “Lord of hosts, if You look upon Your servant's affliction and remember me, and do not forget Your handmaid, but give Your servant a male child, I will give him to the Lord all the days of his life, and no razor shall touch his head.”

1:12  LA: Factum est autem, cum illa multiplicaret preces coram Domino, ut Heli observaret os ejus.
      EN: As she continued praying before the Lord, Eli watched her mouth.

1:13  LA: Porro Anna loquebatur in corde suo, tantumque labia illius movebantur, et vox penitus non audiebatur. Æstimavit ergo eam Heli temulentam,
      EN: Hannah was speaking in her heart; only her lips moved, and no sound at all could be heard. Eli therefore thought she was drunk

1:14  LA: dixitque ei : Usquequo ebria eris ? digere paulisper vinum, quo mades.
      EN: and said to her, “How long will you remain drunk? Take a little time to digest the wine that has soaked you.”

1:15  LA: Respondens Anna : Nequaquam, inquit, domine mi : nam mulier infelix nimis ego sum : vinumque et omne quod inebriare potest, non bibi, sed effudi animam meam in conspectu Domini.
      EN: Hannah answered, “No, my lord! I am a deeply unhappy woman. I have drunk neither wine nor anything that can intoxicate, but I have poured out my soul in the Lord's presence.

1:16  LA: Ne reputes ancillam tuam quasi unam de filiabus Belial : quia ex multitudine doloris et mœroris mei locuta sum usque in præsens.
      EN: Do not regard your handmaid as one of the daughters of Belial. It is out of the abundance of my pain and sorrow that I have been speaking until now.”

1:17  LA: Tunc Heli ait ei : Vade in pace : et Deus Israël det tibi petitionem tuam quam rogasti eum.
      EN: Then Eli said to her, “Go in peace, and may the God of Israel grant the petition you have asked of Him.”

1:18  LA: Et illa dixit : Utinam inveniat ancilla tua gratiam in oculis tuis. Et abiit mulier in viam suam, et comedit, vultusque illius non sunt amplius in diversa mutati.
      EN: She said, “May your handmaid find favor in your eyes.” The woman went on her way and ate, and her expression no longer kept changing.

1:19  LA: Et surrexerunt mane, et adoraverunt coram Domino : reversique sunt, et venerunt in domum suam Ramatha. Cognovit autem Elcana Annam uxorem suam : et recordatus est ejus Dominus.
      EN: They rose early and worshiped before the Lord, then returned and came to their home at Ramah. Elkanah knew his wife Hannah, and the Lord remembered her.

1:20  LA: Et factum est post circulum dierum, concepit Anna, et peperit filium : vocavitque nomen ejus Samuel, eo quod a Domino postulasset eum.
      EN: In the course of time Hannah conceived and bore a son. She named him Samuel, because she had asked the Lord for him.

1:21  LA: Ascendit autem vir ejus Elcana, et omnis domus ejus, ut immolaret Domino hostiam solemnem, et votum suum.
      EN: Her husband Elkanah and his whole household went up to offer the customary sacrifice to the Lord and fulfill his vow.

1:22  LA: Et Anna non ascendit : dixit enim viro suo : Non vadam donec ablactetur infans, et ducam eum, ut appareat ante conspectum Domini, et maneat ibi jugiter.
      EN: But Hannah did not go up. She said to her husband, “I will not go until the child is weaned. Then I will bring him, so that he may appear before the Lord and remain there always.”

1:23  LA: Et ait ei Elcana vir suus : Fac quod bonum tibi videtur, et mane donec ablactes eum : precorque ut impleat Dominus verbum suum. Mansit ergo mulier, et lactavit filium suum, donec amoveret eum a lacte.
      EN: Her husband Elkanah said to her, “Do what seems good to you, and stay until you have weaned him. I pray that the Lord will fulfill His word.” So the woman stayed and nursed her son until she took him from the breast.

1:24  LA: Et adduxit eum secum, postquam ablactaverat, in vitulis tribus, et tribus modiis farinæ, et amphora vini, et adduxit eum ad domum Domini in Silo. Puer autem erat adhuc infantulus :
      EN: After she had weaned him, she brought him with her, along with three calves, three modii of flour, and an amphora of wine, and brought him to the house of the Lord at Shiloh. The boy was still a very small child.

1:25  LA: et immolaverunt vitulum, et obtulerunt puerum Heli.
      EN: They slaughtered a calf and presented the boy to Eli.

1:26  LA: Et ait Anna : Obsecro mi domine, vivit anima tua, domine : ego sum illa mulier, quæ steti coram te hic orans Dominum.
      EN: Hannah said, “Please, my lord, as you live, my lord, I am the woman who stood here beside you, praying to the Lord.

1:27  LA: Pro puero isto oravi, et dedit mihi Dominus petitionem meam quam postulavi eum.
      EN: I prayed for this boy, and the Lord granted the petition I asked of Him.

1:28  LA: Idcirco et ego commodavi eum Domino cunctis diebus quibus fuerit commodatus Domino. Et adoraverunt ibi Dominum. Et oravit Anna, et ait :
      EN: Therefore I too have lent him to the Lord for all the days that he shall be lent to the Lord.” They worshiped the Lord there. And Hannah prayed and said:

1 SAMUEL 2

2:1  LA: Exultavit cor meum in Domino, et exaltatum est cornu meum in Deo meo ; dilatatum est os meum super inimicos meos : quia lætata sum in salutari tuo.
     EN: “My heart has rejoiced in the Lord, and my horn is lifted high in my God. My mouth is opened wide against my enemies, because I have rejoiced in Your salvation.

2:2  LA: Non est sanctus, ut est Dominus, neque enim est alius extra te, et non est fortis sicut Deus noster.
     EN: There is none holy as the Lord is holy; indeed, there is no other besides You, and none strong like our God.

2:3  LA: Nolite multiplicare loqui sublimia gloriantes ; recedant vetera de ore vestro : quia Deus scientiarum Dominus est, et ipsi præparantur cogitationes.
     EN: Do not keep speaking lofty words in your boasting; let the old boasts leave your mouths. For the Lord is the God of knowledge, and thoughts are laid ready before Him.

2:4  LA: Arcus fortium superatus est, et infirmi accincti sunt robore.
     EN: The bow of the mighty has been overcome, and the weak have girded themselves with strength.

2:5  LA: Repleti prius, pro panibus se locaverunt : et famelici saturati sunt, donec sterilis peperit plurimos : et quæ multos habebat filios, infirmata est.
     EN: Those once well fed have hired themselves out for bread, and the hungry have been satisfied, until the barren woman has borne many children, and she who had many sons has grown weak.

2:6  LA: Dominus mortificat et vivificat ; deducit ad inferos et reducit.
     EN: The Lord puts to death and gives life; He brings down to the realm of the dead and brings back again.

2:7  LA: Dominus pauperem facit et ditat, humiliat et sublevat.
     EN: The Lord makes poor and makes rich; He brings low and raises up.

2:8  LA: Suscitat de pulvere egenum, et de stercore elevat pauperem : ut sedeat cum principibus, et solium gloriæ teneat. Domini enim sunt cardines terræ, et posuit super eos orbem.
     EN: He raises the needy from the dust and lifts the poor from the dung heap, to sit with princes and occupy a throne of glory. For the foundations of the earth belong to the Lord, and upon them He has set the world.

2:9  LA: Pedes sanctorum suorum servabit, et impii in tenebris conticescent : quia non in fortitudine sua roborabitur vir.
     EN: He will guard the feet of His holy ones, and the wicked will fall silent in darkness; for no man will prevail by his own strength.

2:10  LA: Dominum formidabunt adversarii ejus : et super ipsos in cælis tonabit. Dominus judicabit fines terræ, et dabit imperium regi suo, et sublimabit cornu christi sui.]
      EN: His adversaries will dread the Lord, and from heaven He will thunder against them. The Lord will judge the ends of the earth, give power to His king, and lift high the horn of His anointed.”

2:11  LA: Et abiit Elcana Ramatha, in domum suam : puer autem erat minister in conspectu Domini ante faciem Heli sacerdotis.
      EN: Elkanah went home to Ramah, but the boy ministered in the Lord's presence under Eli the priest.

2:12  LA: Porro filii Heli, filii Belial, nescientes Dominum,
      EN: Now Eli's sons were sons of Belial, knowing neither the Lord

2:13  LA: neque officium sacerdotum ad populum : sed quicumque immolasset victimam, veniebat puer sacerdotis, dum coquerentur carnes, et habebat fuscinulam tridentem in manu sua,
      EN: nor the priests' duty toward the people. Whenever anyone offered a sacrifice, the priest's servant would come while the meat was cooking, with a three-pronged fork in his hand,

2:14  LA: et mittebat eam in lebetem, vel in caldariam, aut in ollam, sive in cacabum : et omne quod levabat fuscinula, tollebat sacerdos sibi : sic faciebant universo Israëli venientium in Silo.
      EN: and thrust it into the cauldron, kettle, pot, or pan. Whatever the fork brought up, the priest took for himself. This is what they did to all the Israelites who came to Shiloh.

2:15  LA: Etiam antequam adolerent adipem, veniebat puer sacerdotis, et dicebat immolanti : Da mihi carnem, ut coquam sacerdoti : non enim accipiam a te carnem coctam, sed crudam.
      EN: Even before they burned the fat, the priest's servant would come and say to the person offering sacrifice, “Give me meat to cook for the priest. I will not accept cooked meat from you, only raw.”

2:16  LA: Dicebatque illi immolans : Incendatur primum juxta morem hodie adeps, et tolle tibi quantumcumque desiderat anima tua. Qui respondens aiebat ei : Nequaquam : nunc enim dabis, alioquin tollam vi.
      EN: The person offering sacrifice would say to him, “Let the fat first be burned today according to custom; then take as much as you desire.” But he would answer, “No! You must give it now, or I will take it by force.”

2:17  LA: Erat ergo peccatum puerorum grande nimis coram Domino : quia retrahebant homines a sacrificio Domini.
      EN: The young men's sin was therefore very great before the Lord, because they were turning people away from the Lord's sacrifice.

2:18  LA: Samuel autem ministrabat ante faciem Domini, puer accinctus ephod lineo.
      EN: But Samuel ministered before the Lord, a boy wearing a linen ephod.

2:19  LA: Et tunicam parvam faciebat ei mater sua, quam afferebat statutis diebus, ascendens cum viro suo, ut immolaret hostiam solemnem.
      EN: His mother would make him a little tunic and bring it at the appointed times, when she went up with her husband to offer the customary sacrifice.

2:20  LA: Et benedixit Heli Elcanæ et uxori ejus : dixitque ei : Reddat tibi Dominus semen de muliere hac, pro fœnore quod commodasti Domino. Et abierunt in locum suum.
      EN: Eli blessed Elkanah and his wife and said to him, “May the Lord give you offspring from this woman in return for the loan you have lent to the Lord.” Then they went home.

2:21  LA: Visitavit ergo Dominus Annam, et concepit, et peperit tres filios, et duas filias : et magnificatus est puer Samuel apud Dominum.
      EN: The Lord visited Hannah, and she conceived and bore three sons and two daughters. Meanwhile the boy Samuel grew great in the Lord's presence.

2:22  LA: Heli autem erat senex valde, et audivit omnia quæ faciebant filii sui universo Israëli, et quomodo dormiebant cum mulieribus quæ observabant ad ostium tabernaculi :
      EN: Eli was very old. He heard everything his sons were doing to all Israel, and how they slept with the women who kept watch at the entrance of the tabernacle.

2:23  LA: et dixit eis : Quare facitis res hujuscemodi quas ego audio, res pessimas, ab omni populo ?
      EN: He said to them, “Why do you do such things—the very wicked things I hear about from all the people?

2:24  LA: Nolite, filii mei : non enim est bona fama quam ego audio, ut transgredi faciatis populum Domini.
      EN: No, my sons! The report I hear is not good: you are causing the Lord's people to transgress.

2:25  LA: Si peccaverit vir in virum, placari ei potest Deus : si autem in Dominum peccaverit vir, quis orabit pro eo ? Et non audierunt vocem patris sui : quia voluit Dominus occidere eos.
      EN: If one man sins against another, God can be appeased on his behalf. But if a man sins against the Lord, who will pray for him?” Yet they did not listen to their father's voice, because the Lord willed to put them to death.

2:26  LA: Puer autem Samuel proficiebat atque crescebat, et placebat tam Domino quam hominibus.
      EN: But the boy Samuel advanced and grew, and was pleasing both to the Lord and to people.

2:27  LA: Venit autem vir Dei ad Heli, et ait ad eum : Hæc dicit Dominus : Numquid non aperte revelatus sum domui patris tui, cum essent in Ægypto in domo Pharaonis ?
      EN: A man of God came to Eli and said to him, “Thus says the Lord: Did I not plainly reveal Myself to your father's house when they were in Egypt, in Pharaoh's house?

2:28  LA: Et elegi eum ex omnibus tribubus Israël mihi in sacerdotem, ut ascenderet ad altare meum, et adoleret mihi incensum, et portaret ephod coram me : et dedi domui patris tui omnia de sacrificiis filiorum Israël.
      EN: I chose him from all the tribes of Israel as My priest, to go up to My altar, burn incense to Me, and wear the ephod before Me. I gave your father's house all the offerings from the sacrifices of the Israelites.

2:29  LA: Quare calce abjecistis victimam meam, et munera mea quæ præcepi ut offerrentur in templo : et magis honorasti filios tuos quam me, ut comederetis primitias omnis sacrificii Israël populi mei ?
      EN: Why have you kicked aside My sacrifice and My offerings, which I commanded to be offered in the temple? You have honored your sons more than Me, so that you might eat the first portions of every sacrifice of My people Israel.

2:30  LA: Propterea ait Dominus Deus Israël : Loquens locutus sum, ut domus tua, et domus patris tui, ministraret in conspectu meo usque in sempiternum. Nunc autem dicit Dominus : Absit hoc a me : sed quicumque glorificaverit me, glorificabo eum : qui autem contemnunt me, erunt ignobiles.
      EN: Therefore the Lord, the God of Israel, says: I solemnly declared that your house and your father's house would minister in My presence forever. But now the Lord says: Far be this from Me! Whoever honors Me, I will honor; but those who despise Me will be held in contempt.

2:31  LA: Ecce dies veniunt, et præcidam brachium tuum, et brachium domus patris tui, ut non sit senex in domo tua.
      EN: Look, the days are coming when I will cut off your arm and the arm of your father's house, so that no old man will remain in your house.

2:32  LA: Et videbis æmulum tuum in templo, in universis prosperis Israël : et non erit senex in domo tua omnibus diebus.
      EN: You will see your rival in the temple amid all Israel's prosperity, and there will never be an old man in your house.

2:33  LA: Verumtamen non auferam penitus virum ex te ab altari meo : sed ut deficiant oculi tui, et tabescat anima tua : et pars magna domus tuæ morietur cum ad virilem ætatem venerit.
      EN: Yet I will not entirely remove every man of yours from My altar, so that your eyes may fail and your soul waste away. A large part of your house will die upon reaching manhood.

2:34  LA: Hoc autem erit tibi signum, quod venturum est duobus filiis tuis, Ophni et Phinees : in die uno morientur ambo.
      EN: This will be your sign: what is coming upon your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas. On one day they will both die.

2:35  LA: Et suscitabo mihi sacerdotem fidelem, qui juxta cor meum et animam meam faciet : et ædificabo ei domum fidelem, et ambulabit coram christo meo cunctis diebus.
      EN: I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest who will act according to My heart and My soul. I will build him a faithful house, and he will walk before My anointed all his days.

2:36  LA: Futurum est autem, ut quicumque remanserit in domo tua, veniat ut oretur pro eo, et offerat nummum argenteum, et tortam panis, dicatque : Dimitte me, obsecro, ad unam partem sacerdotalem, ut comedam buccellam panis.
      EN: Then whoever remains in your house will come so that prayer may be made for him, and will offer a silver coin and a loaf of bread, saying, ‘Please admit me to one of the priestly duties, so that I may eat a morsel of bread.’”

1 SAMUEL 3

3:1  LA: Puer autem Samuel ministrabat Domino coram Heli, et sermo Domini erat pretiosus in diebus illis : non erat visio manifesta.
     EN: The boy Samuel ministered to the Lord under Eli. The word of the Lord was precious in those days; there was no manifest vision.

3:2  LA: Factum est ergo in die quadam, Heli jacebat in loco suo, et oculi ejus caligaverant, nec poterat videre :
     EN: One day Eli was lying in his place. His eyes had grown dim, and he could not see.

3:3  LA: lucerna Dei antequam extingueretur, Samuel dormiebat in templo Domini, ubi erat arca Dei.
     EN: Before God's lamp went out, Samuel was sleeping in the Lord's temple, where the ark of God was.

3:4  LA: Et vocavit Dominus Samuel. Qui respondens, ait : Ecce ego.
     EN: The Lord called Samuel, and he answered, “Here I am.”

3:5  LA: Et cucurrit ad Heli, et dixit : Ecce ego : vocasti enim me. Qui dixit : Non vocavi : revertere, et dormi. Et abiit, et dormivit.
     EN: He ran to Eli and said, “Here I am, for you called me.” But Eli said, “I did not call. Go back and sleep.” So he went and slept.

3:6  LA: Et adjecit Dominus rursum vocare Samuelem. Consurgensque Samuel, abiit ad Heli, et dixit : Ecce ego, quia vocasti me. Qui respondit : Non vocavi te, fili mi : revertere et dormi.
     EN: Again the Lord called Samuel. Samuel rose, went to Eli, and said, “Here I am, because you called me.” He answered, “I did not call you, my son. Go back and sleep.”

3:7  LA: Porro Samuel necdum sciebat Dominum, neque revelatus fuerat ei sermo Domini.
     EN: Samuel did not yet know the Lord, nor had the word of the Lord yet been revealed to him.

3:8  LA: Et adjecit Dominus, et vocavit adhuc Samuelem tertio. Qui consurgens abiit ad Heli,
     EN: The Lord called Samuel yet a third time. He rose and went to Eli,

3:9  LA: et ait : Ecce ego, quia vocasti me. Intellexit ergo Heli quia Dominus vocaret puerum : et ait ad Samuelem : Vade, et dormi : et si deinceps vocaverit te, dices : Loquere, Domine, quia audit servus tuus. Abiit ergo Samuel, et dormivit in loco suo.
     EN: and said, “Here I am, because you called me.” Then Eli understood that the Lord was calling the boy, and said to Samuel, “Go and sleep. If He calls you again, say, ‘Speak, Lord, for Your servant is listening.’” Samuel went and slept in his place.

3:10  LA: Et venit Dominus, et stetit : et vocavit, sicut vocaverat secundo : Samuel, Samuel. Et ait Samuel : Loquere, Domine, quia audit servus tuus.
      EN: The Lord came and stood there, and called as He had called the second time, “Samuel, Samuel!” Samuel said, “Speak, Lord, for Your servant is listening.”

3:11  LA: Et dixit Dominus ad Samuelem : Ecce ego facio verbum in Israël, quod quicumque audierit, tinnient ambæ aures ejus.
      EN: The Lord said to Samuel, “Look, I am doing something in Israel that will make both ears of everyone who hears it ring.

3:12  LA: In die illa suscitabo adversum Heli omnia quæ locutus sum super domum ejus : incipiam, et complebo.
      EN: On that day I will bring against Eli everything I have spoken concerning his house. I will begin it, and I will complete it.

3:13  LA: Prædixi enim ei quod judicaturus essem domum ejus in æternum propter iniquitatem, eo quod noverat indigne agere filios suos, et non corripuerit eos.
      EN: For I foretold to him that I would judge his house forever because of its iniquity: he knew that his sons were acting shamefully and did not restrain them.

3:14  LA: Idcirco juravi domui Heli quod non expietur iniquitas domus ejus victimis et muneribus usque in æternum.
      EN: Therefore I have sworn to Eli's house that the iniquity of his house shall never be atoned for by sacrifices or offerings.”

3:15  LA: Dormivit autem Samuel usque mane, aperuitque ostia domus Domini. Et Samuel timebat indicare visionem Heli.
      EN: Samuel slept until morning and opened the doors of the Lord's house. But Samuel was afraid to tell Eli the vision.

3:16  LA: Vocavit ergo Heli Samuelem, et dixit : Samuel fili mi ? Qui respondens ait : Præsto sum.
      EN: Then Eli called Samuel and said, “Samuel, my son!” He answered, “I am here.”

3:17  LA: Et interrogavit eum : Quis est sermo, quem locutus est Dominus ad te ? oro te ne celaveris me : hæc faciat tibi Deus, et hæc addat, si absconderis a me sermonem ex omnibus verbis quæ dicta sunt tibi.
      EN: Eli asked him, “What is the word the Lord spoke to you? Please do not hide it from me. May God do these things to you and add more besides if you hide from me a word of anything that was said to you.”

3:18  LA: Indicavit itaque ei Samuel universos sermones, et non abscondit ab eo. Et ille respondit : Dominus est : quod bonum est in oculis suis faciat.
      EN: So Samuel told him everything and hid nothing from him. Eli answered, “He is the Lord. Let Him do what is good in His eyes.”

3:19  LA: Crevit autem Samuel, et Dominus erat cum eo, et non cecidit ex omnibus verbis ejus in terram.
      EN: Samuel grew, and the Lord was with him; not one of his words fell to the ground.

3:20  LA: Et cognovit universus Israël, a Dan usque Bersabee, quod fidelis Samuel propheta esset Domini.
      EN: All Israel, from Dan to Beersheba, knew that Samuel was a faithful prophet of the Lord.

3:21  LA: Et addidit Dominus ut appareret in Silo, quoniam revelatus fuerat Dominus Samueli in Silo juxta verbum Domini. Et evenit sermo Samuelis universo Israëli.
      EN: The Lord continued to appear at Shiloh, for the Lord had revealed Himself to Samuel at Shiloh through the word of the Lord. And Samuel's word came to all Israel.

1 SAMUEL 4

4:1  LA: Et factum est in diebus illis, convenerunt Philisthiim in pugnam : et egressus est Israël obviam Philisthiim in prælium, et castrametatus est juxta lapidem Adjutorii. Porro Philisthiim venerunt in Aphec,

      EN: Now the king walks before you. I have grown old and gray, and my sons are with you. I have lived before you from my youth until this day; here I stand.

       EN: The Lord sent Jerubbaal, Bedan, Jephthah, and Samuel, and rescued you from the hand of your enemies all around, and you lived in safety.

12:13  LA: Nunc ergo præsto est rex vester, quem elegistis et petistis : ecce dedit vobis Dominus regem.
       EN: Now here is your king, whom you chose and requested. Look, the Lord has given you a king.

12:14  LA: Si timueritis Dominum, et servieritis ei, et audieritis vocem ejus, et non exasperaveritis os Domini, eritis et vos, et rex qui imperat vobis, sequentes Dominum Deum vestrum :
       EN: If you fear the Lord, serve Him, listen to His voice, and do not defy the Lord's command, both you and the king who rules you will follow the Lord your God.

       EN: Is it not wheat harvest today? I will call upon the Lord, and He will send thunder and rain. Then you will know and see that you have done a great evil for yourselves in the Lord's sight by asking for a king over you.”

       EN: Samuel cried out to the Lord, and the Lord sent thunder and rain that day.

       EN: All the people greatly feared the Lord and Samuel, and all the people said to Samuel, “Pray to the Lord your God for your servants, so that we do not die. For we have added to all our sins the evil of asking for a king.”

       EN: Samuel said to the people, “Do not be afraid. You have done all this evil, yet do not turn away from following the Lord; serve the Lord with all your heart.

       EN: Do not turn aside after empty things that can neither benefit you nor rescue you, because they are empty.

12:22  LA: Et non derelinquet Dominus populum suum propter nomen suum magnum : quia juravit Dominus facere vos sibi populum.
       EN: The Lord will not abandon His people, for the sake of His great name, because the Lord has sworn to make you His people.

       EN: As for me, far be it from me to sin against the Lord by ceasing to pray for you. I will teach you the good and upright way.

12:24  LA: Igitur timete Dominum, et servite ei in veritate, et ex toto corde vestro : vidistis enim magnifica quæ in vobis gesserit.
       EN: Therefore fear the Lord and serve Him faithfully with all your heart, for you have seen the great things He has done among you.

12:25  LA: Quod si perseveraveritis in malitia, et vos et rex vester pariter peribitis.
       EN: But if you persist in wickedness, both you and your king will perish together.”

      EN: Hebrews also crossed the Jordan into the land of Gad and Gilead. Saul was still at Gilgal, and all the people who followed him were terrified.

      EN: He waited seven days, according to Samuel's appointment, but Samuel did not come to Gilgal, and the people slipped away from him.

13:9  LA: Ait ergo Saul : Afferte mihi holocaustum et pacifica. Et obtulit holocaustum.
      EN: Saul said, “Bring me the burnt offering and the peace offerings.” He offered the burnt offering.

       EN: Just as he finished offering the burnt offering, Samuel arrived. Saul went out to meet and greet him.

       EN: Samuel said to him, “What have you done?” Saul answered, “I saw that the people were slipping away from me, that you had not come within the appointed days, and that the Philistines had gathered at Michmash.

13:12  LA: dixi : Nunc descendent Philisthiim ad me in Galgala, et faciem Domini non placavi. Necessitate compulsus, obtuli holocaustum.
       EN: I said, ‘Now the Philistines will come down against me at Gilgal, and I have not appeased the Lord.’ Compelled by necessity, I offered the burnt offering.”

13:13  LA: Dixitque Samuel ad Saul : Stulte egisti, nec custodisti mandata Domini Dei tui quæ præcepit tibi. Quod si non fecisses, jam nunc præparasset Dominus regnum tuum super Israël in sempiternum :
       EN: Samuel said to Saul, “You have acted foolishly and have not kept the commandments the Lord your God gave you. Had you not done this, the Lord would now have established your kingdom over Israel forever.

13:14  LA: sed nequaquam regnum tuum ultra consurget. Quæsivit Dominus sibi virum juxta cor suum : et præcepit ei Dominus ut esset dux super populum suum, eo quod non servaveris quæ præcepit Dominus.

13:20  LA: Descendebat ergo omnis Israël ad Philisthiim, ut exacueret unusquisque vomerem suum, et ligonem, et securim, et sarculum.
       EN: All Israel therefore went down to the Philistines, each to sharpen his plowshare, mattock, axe, and hoe.

1 SAMUEL 14

14:1  LA: Et accidit quadam die ut diceret Jonathas filius Saul ad adolescentem armigerum suum : Veni, et transeamus ad stationem Philisthinorum, quæ est trans locum illum. Patri autem suo hoc ipsum non indicavit.
      EN: One day Jonathan son of Saul said to his young armor-bearer, “Come, let us cross over to the Philistine outpost on the other side of that place.” But he did not tell his father about it.

14:2  LA: Porro Saul morabatur in extrema parte Gabaa sub malogranato, quæ erat in Magron : et erat populus cum eo quasi sexcentorum virorum.
      EN: Saul was staying at the edge of Gibeah, under the pomegranate tree in Migron. About six hundred men were with him.

14:3  LA: Et Achias filius Achitob fratris Ichabod filii Phinees, qui ortus fuerat ex Heli sacerdote Domini in Silo, portabat ephod. Sed et populus ignorabat quo isset Jonathas.
      EN: Ahijah wore the ephod. He was the son of Ahitub, the brother of Ichabod, son of Phinehas, who was born to Eli, the Lord's priest at Shiloh. The people did not know where Jonathan had gone.

14:4  LA: Erant autem inter ascensus per quos nitebatur Jonathas transire ad stationem Philisthinorum, eminentes petræ ex utraque parte, et quasi in modum dentium scopuli hinc et inde prærupti : nomen uni Boses, et nomen alteri Sene :
      EN: Between the ascents by which Jonathan was trying to cross to the Philistine outpost, rocks projected on either side, with steep crags here and there like teeth. One was called Bozez and the other Seneh.

14:5  LA: unus scopulus prominens ad aquilonem ex adverso Machmas, et alter ad meridiem contra Gabaa.
      EN: One crag projected northward, facing Michmash, and the other southward, facing Gibeah.

14:6  LA: Dixit autem Jonathas ad adolescentem armigerum suum : Veni, transeamus ad stationem incircumcisorum horum, si forte faciat Dominus pro nobis : quia non est Domino difficile salvare, vel in multis, vel in paucis.
      EN: Jonathan said to his young armor-bearer, “Come, let us cross to the outpost of these uncircumcised men. Perhaps the Lord will act for us, for it is not difficult for the Lord to save by many or by few.”

14:7  LA: Dixitque ei armiger suus : Fac omnia quæ placent animo tuo : perge quo cupis, et ero tecum ubicumque volueris.
      EN: His armor-bearer said to him, “Do everything that pleases your heart. Go where you wish, and I will be with you wherever you choose.”

14:8  LA: Et ait Jonathas : Ecce nos transimus ad viros istos. Cumque apparuerimus eis,
      EN: Jonathan said, “We will cross over to these men and show ourselves to them.

      EN: If they say to us, ‘Stay until we come to you,’ we will stand where we are and not go up to them.

       EN: All the Israelites who had hidden in the hill country of Ephraim heard that the Philistines had fled and joined their own people in battle. About ten thousand men were with Saul.

       EN: All the people of the land came into a forest where there was honey on the surface of the ground.

       EN: The people entered the forest and saw honey flowing, but no one put his hand to his mouth, because the people feared the oath.

       EN: Jonathan had not heard his father put the people under the oath. He stretched out the end of the staff in his hand, dipped it into the honeycomb, and brought his hand to his mouth, and his eyes brightened.

       EN: One of the people said, “Your father strictly bound the people by an oath, saying, ‘Cursed is the man who eats bread today.’” Now the people were exhausted.

14:29  LA: Dixitque Jonathas : Turbavit pater meus terram : vidistis ipsi quia illuminati sunt oculi mei, eo quod gustaverim paululum de melle isto :

       EN: They turned upon the spoil, took sheep, oxen, and calves, and slaughtered them on the ground. The people ate them with the blood.

30:10  LA: Persecutus est autem David ipse, et quadringenti viri : substiterant enim ducenti, qui lassi transire non poterant torrentem Besor.
       EN: David pursued with four hundred men, for two hundred had stopped, too exhausted to cross the torrent of Besor.

30:11  LA: Et invenerunt virum ægyptium in agro, et adduxerunt eum ad David : dederuntque ei panem ut comederet, et biberet aquam,
       EN: They found an Egyptian man in the field and brought him to David. They gave him bread to eat and water to drink,

30:12  LA: sed et fragmen massæ caricarum, et duas ligaturas uvæ passæ. Quæ cum comedisset, reversus est spiritus ejus, et refocillatus est : non enim comederat panem, neque biberat aquam, tribus diebus et tribus noctibus.
       EN: also a piece of a dried-fig cake and two clusters of raisins. When he had eaten, his spirit returned and he was revived, for he had eaten no bread and drunk no water for three days and three nights.

30:13  LA: Dixit itaque ei David : Cujus es tu ? vel unde ? et quo pergis ? Qui ait : Puer ægyptius ego sum, servus viri Amalecitæ : dereliquit autem me dominus meus, quia ægrotare cœpi nudiustertius.
       EN: David said to him, “To whom do you belong? Where are you from, and where are you going?” He said, “I am an Egyptian servant, the slave of an Amalekite man. My master abandoned me because I fell ill three days ago.

30:14  LA: Siquidem nos erupimus ad australem plagam Cerethi, et contra Judam, et ad meridiem Caleb, et Siceleg succendimus igni.
       EN: We raided the southern region of the Cherethites, the territory of Judah, and the south of Caleb, and we burned Ziklag with fire.”

30:15  LA: Dixitque ei David : Potes me ducere ad cuneum istum ? Qui ait : Jura mihi per Deum quod non occidas me, et non tradas me in manus domini mei, et ego ducam te ad cuneum istum. Et juravit ei David.
       EN: David said to him, “Can you lead me to this band?” He said, “Swear to me by God that you will neither kill me nor hand me over to my master, and I will lead you to this band.” David swore to him.

30:16  LA: Qui cum duxisset eum, ecce illi discumbebant super faciem universæ terræ comedentes et bibentes, et quasi festum celebrantes diem, pro cuncta præda et spoliis quæ ceperant de terra Philisthiim et de terra Juda.
       EN: He led him there, and they were spread out over the whole countryside, eating, drinking, and celebrating as on a feast day because of all the spoil and plunder they had taken from the land of the Philistines and the land of Judah.

30:17  LA: Et percussit eos David a vespere usque ad vesperam alterius diei, et non evasit ex eis quisquam, nisi quadringenti viri adolescentes, qui ascenderant camelos et fugerant.
       EN: David struck them from evening until the evening of the next day. None of them escaped except four hundred young men who mounted camels and fled.

30:18  LA: Eruit ergo David omnia quæ tulerant Amalecitæ, et duas uxores suas eruit.
       EN: David recovered everything the Amalekites had taken, and he rescued his two wives.

30:19  LA: Nec defuit quidquam a parvo usque ad magnum, tam de filiis quam de filiabus, et de spoliis, et quæcumque rapuerant : omnia reduxit David.
       EN: Nothing was missing, from the least to the greatest, whether sons, daughters, spoil, or anything they had carried away. David brought everything back.

30:20  LA: Et tulit universos greges et armenta, et minavit ante faciem suam : dixeruntque : Hæc est præda David.
       EN: He took all the flocks and herds and drove them before him. They said, “This is David's spoil.”

30:21  LA: Venit autem David ad ducentos viros qui lassi substiterant, nec sequi potuerant David, et residere eos jusserat in torrente Besor : qui egressi sunt obviam David et populo qui erat cum eo. Accedens autem David ad populum, salutavit eos pacifice.
       EN: David came to the two hundred men who had stopped from exhaustion and could not follow him, whom he had ordered to remain at the torrent of Besor. They came out to meet David and the people with him. David approached the people and greeted them peaceably.

30:22  LA: Respondensque omnis vir pessimus et iniquus de viris qui ierant cum David, dixit : Quia non venerunt nobiscum, non dabimus eis quidquam de præda quam eruimus : sed sufficiat unicuique uxor sua et filii : quos cum acceperint, recedant.
       EN: Every thoroughly wicked and unjust man among those who had gone with David said, “Since they did not come with us, we will give them none of the spoil we recovered. Let each be satisfied with his wife and children; let them take them and leave.”

30:23  LA: Dixit autem David : Non sic facietis, fratres mei, de his quæ tradidit nobis Dominus, et custodivit nos, et dedit latrunculos qui eruperant adversum nos, in manus nostras :
       EN: David said, “You shall not act like this, my brothers, with what the Lord has given us. He guarded us and delivered into our hands the raiders who attacked us.

30:24  LA: nec audiet vos quisquam super sermone hoc : æqua enim pars erit descendentis ad prælium, et remanentis ad sarcinas, et similiter divident.
       EN: No one will listen to you on this matter. The share of the one who goes down to battle will be equal to the share of the one who remains with the baggage. They will divide it alike.”

30:25  LA: Et factum est hoc ex die illa et deinceps, constitutum et præfinitum, et quasi lex in Israël usque in diem hanc.
       EN: From that day onward this was established and decreed as a law in Israel, down to this day.

30:26  LA: Venit ergo David in Siceleg, et misit dona de præda senioribus Juda proximis suis, dicens : Accipite benedictionem de præda hostium Domini :
       EN: David came to Ziklag and sent gifts from the spoil to the elders of Judah, his neighbors, saying, “Accept a blessing from the spoil of the Lord's enemies.”

1 SAMUEL 31

31:1  LA: Philisthiim autem pugnabant adversum Israël : et fugerunt viri Israël ante faciem Philisthiim, et ceciderunt interfecti in monte Gelboë.
      EN: The Philistines fought against Israel. The men of Israel fled before the Philistines and fell slain on Mount Gilboa.

31:2  LA: Irrueruntque Philisthiim in Saul et in filios ejus, et percusserunt Jonathan, et Abinadab, et Melchisua filios Saul :
      EN: The Philistines fell upon Saul and his sons, and struck down Saul's sons Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua.

31:3  LA: totumque pondus prælii versum est in Saul, et consecuti sunt eum viri sagittarii, et vulneratus est vehementer a sagittariis.
      EN: The full weight of the battle turned against Saul. The archers overtook him, and he was severely wounded by them.

31:4  LA: Dixitque Saul ad armigerum suum : Evagina gladium tuum, et percute me : ne forte veniant incircumcisi isti, et interficiant me, illudentes mihi. Et noluit armiger ejus : fuerat enim nimio terrore perterritus. Arripuit itaque Saul gladium, et irruit super eum.
      EN: Saul said to his armor-bearer, “Draw your sword and strike me, lest these uncircumcised men come and kill me, mocking me.” His armor-bearer refused, for he was overcome with terror. Saul took his sword and fell upon it.

31:5  LA: Quod cum vidisset armiger ejus, videlicet quod mortuus esset Saul, irruit etiam ipse super gladium suum, et mortuus est cum eo.
      EN: When his armor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, he too fell upon his sword and died with him.

31:6  LA: Mortuus est ergo Saul, et tres filii ejus, et armiger illius, et universi viri ejus in die illa pariter.
      EN: Thus Saul, his three sons, his armor-bearer, and all his men died together that day.

31:7  LA: Videntes autem viri Israël qui erant trans vallem et trans Jordanem, quod fugissent viri Israëlitæ, et quod mortuus esset Saul et filii ejus, reliquerunt civitates suas, et fugerunt : veneruntque Philisthiim, et habitaverunt ibi.
      EN: The Israelites across the valley and beyond the Jordan saw that the men of Israel had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead. They abandoned their cities and fled, and the Philistines came and lived in them.

31:8  LA: Facta autem die altera, venerunt Philisthiim ut spoliarent interfectos, et invenerunt Saul et tres filios ejus jacentes in monte Gelboë.
      EN: The next day the Philistines came to strip the slain and found Saul and his three sons lying on Mount Gilboa.

31:9  LA: Et præciderunt caput Saul, et spoliaverunt eum armis : et miserunt in terram Philisthinorum per circuitum, ut annuntiaretur in templo idolorum, et in populis.
      EN: They cut off Saul's head and stripped off his armor. They sent throughout the land of the Philistines to announce it in the temple of their idols and among the people.

31:10  LA: Et posuerunt arma ejus in templo Astaroth, corpus vero ejus suspenderunt in muro Bethsan.
       EN: They placed his armor in the temple of Ashtaroth and hung his body on the wall of Beth-shan.

31:11  LA: Quod cum audissent habitatores Jabes Galaad, quæcumque fecerant Philisthiim Saul,
       EN: When the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead heard everything the Philistines had done to Saul,

31:12  LA: surrexerunt omnes viri fortissimi, et ambulaverunt tota nocte, et tulerunt cadaver Saul, et cadavera filiorum ejus, de muro Bethsan : veneruntque Jabes Galaad, et combusserunt ea ibi :
       EN: all the bravest men rose and traveled all night. They took Saul's corpse and the corpses of his sons from the wall of Beth-shan, came to Jabesh-gilead, and burned them there.

31:13  LA: et tulerunt ossa eorum, et sepelierunt in nemore Jabes, et jejunaverunt septem diebus.
       EN: They took their bones, buried them in the grove at Jabesh, and fasted seven days.
