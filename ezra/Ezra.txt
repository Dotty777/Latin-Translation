EZRA 1

1:1  LA: In anno primo Cyri regis Persarum, ut compleretur verbum Domini ex ore Jeremiæ, suscitavit Dominus spiritum Cyri regis Persarum : et traduxit vocem in omni regno suo, etiam per scripturam, dicens :
     EN: In the first year of Cyrus king of the Persians, that the Lord's word from Jeremiah's mouth might be fulfilled, the Lord stirred up the spirit of Cyrus king of the Persians. He sent a proclamation throughout his kingdom, also in writing, saying:

1:2  LA: Hæc dicit Cyrus rex Persarum : Omnia regna terræ dedit mihi Dominus Deus cæli, et ipse præcepit mihi ut ædificarem ei domum in Jerusalem, quæ est in Judæa.
     EN: “Thus says Cyrus king of the Persians: The Lord, the God of heaven, has given me all the kingdoms of the earth, and He has commanded me to build Him a house in Jerusalem, which is in Judah.

1:3  LA: Quis est in vobis de universo populo ejus ? Sit Deus illius cum ipso. Ascendat in Jerusalem, quæ est in Judæa, et ædificet domum Domini Dei Israël : ipse est Deus qui est in Jerusalem.
     EN: Who among you belongs to any of His people? May his God be with him. Let him go up to Jerusalem in Judah and build the house of the Lord, the God of Israel. He is the God who is in Jerusalem.

1:4  LA: Et omnes reliqui in cunctis locis ubicumque habitant, adjuvent eum viri de loco suo argento et auro, et substantia, et pecoribus, excepto quod voluntarie offerunt templo Dei, quod est in Jerusalem.
     EN: As for all who remain in any place, wherever they dwell, let the men of each one's place help him with silver, gold, possessions, and livestock, besides what they offer freely for God's Temple in Jerusalem.”

1:5  LA: Et surrexerunt principes patrum de Juda et Benjamin, et sacerdotes, et Levitæ, et omnis cujus Deus suscitavit spiritum, ut ascenderent ad ædificandum templum Domini, quod erat in Jerusalem.
     EN: The heads of the ancestral houses of Judah and Benjamin, the priests and Levites, and everyone whose spirit God had stirred arose to go up and build the Lord's Temple in Jerusalem.

1:6  LA: Universique qui erant in circuitu, adjuverunt manus eorum in vasis argenteis et aureis, in substantia et jumentis, in supellectili, exceptis his quæ sponte obtulerant.
     EN: All those around them strengthened their hands with silver and gold vessels, possessions, beasts of burden, and furnishings, besides what they offered freely.

1:7  LA: Rex quoque Cyrus protulit vasa templi Domini, quæ tulerat Nabuchodonosor de Jerusalem, et posuerat ea in templo dei sui.
     EN: King Cyrus also brought out the vessels of the Lord's Temple that Nebuchadnezzar had taken from Jerusalem and placed in the temple of his god.

1:8  LA: Protulit autem ea Cyrus rex Persarum per manum Mithridatis filii Gazabar, et annumeravit ea Sassabasar principi Juda.
     EN: Cyrus king of the Persians brought them out through Mithredath son of Gazabar and counted them out to Sheshbazzar, prince of Judah.

1:9  LA: Et hic est numerus eorum : phialæ aureæ triginta, phialæ argenteæ mille, cultri viginti novem, scyphi aurei triginta,
     EN: This was their number: thirty gold bowls, a thousand silver bowls, twenty-nine knives, thirty gold cups,

1:10  LA: scyphi argentei secundi quadringenti decem, vasa alia mille.
      EN: four hundred ten silver cups of a second grade, and a thousand other vessels.

1:11  LA: Omnia vasa aurea et argentea quinque millia quadringenta : universa tulit Sassabasar cum his qui ascendebant de transmigratione Babylonis in Jerusalem.
      EN: All the gold and silver vessels numbered five thousand four hundred. Sheshbazzar brought them all with those going up from the Babylonian exile to Jerusalem.

EZRA 3

3:1  LA: Jamque venerat mensis septimus, et erant filii Israël in civitatibus suis : congregatus est ergo populus quasi vir unus in Jerusalem.
     EN: The seventh month had now come, and the children of Israel were in their cities. The people gathered as one man in Jerusalem.

3:2  LA: Et surrexit Josue filius Josedec, et fratres ejus sacerdotes, et Zorobabel filius Salathiel, et fratres ejus, et ædificaverunt altare Dei Israël ut offerrent in eo holocautomata, sicut scriptum est in lege Moysi viri Dei.
     EN: Jeshua son of Jozadak and his fellow priests, and Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and his kinsmen, arose and built the altar of the God of Israel, to offer burnt offerings upon it, as written in the law of Moses, the man of God.

3:3  LA: Collocaverunt autem altare Dei super bases suas, deterrentibus eos per circuitum populis terrarum : et obtulerunt super illud holocaustum Domino mane et vespere.
     EN: They set God's altar upon its foundations, though the peoples of the surrounding lands frightened them, and offered a burnt offering to the Lord upon it morning and evening.

3:4  LA: Feceruntque solemnitatem tabernaculorum, sicut scriptum est, et holocaustum diebus singulis per ordinem secundum præceptum opus diei in die suo.
     EN: They celebrated the Feast of Tabernacles as written, and the daily burnt offering in order, according to the command, each day's work on its own day.

3:5  LA: Et post hæc holocaustum juge, tam in calendis quam in universis solemnitatibus Domini quæ erant consecratæ, et in omnibus in quibus ultro offerebatur munus Domino.
     EN: Afterward they offered the continual burnt offering, both at the new moons and at all the Lord's consecrated feasts, and on every occasion when a gift was freely offered to the Lord.

3:6  LA: A primo die mensis septimi cœperunt offerre holocaustum Domino : porro templum Dei nondum fundatum erat.
     EN: From the first day of the seventh month they began to offer burnt offerings to the Lord, although the foundation of God's Temple had not yet been laid.

3:7  LA: Dederunt autem pecunias latomis et cæmentariis : cibum quoque, et potum, et oleum Sidoniis Tyriisque, ut deferrent ligna cedrina de Libano ad mare Joppe, juxta quod præceperat Cyrus rex Persarum eis.
     EN: They gave money to stonecutters and masons, and food, drink, and oil to the Sidonians and Tyrians to bring cedar timber from Lebanon to the sea at Joppa, as Cyrus king of the Persians had commanded them.

3:8  LA: Anno autem secundo adventus eorum ad templum Dei in Jerusalem, mense secundo, cœperunt Zorobabel filius Salathiel, et Josue filius Josedec, et reliqui de fratribus eorum sacerdotes, et Levitæ, et omnes qui venerant de captivitate in Jerusalem, et constituerunt Levitas a viginti annis et supra, ut urgerent opus Domini.
     EN: In the second year of their coming to God's Temple in Jerusalem, in the second month, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Jeshua son of Jozadak, the rest of their fellow priests and Levites, and all who had come to Jerusalem from captivity began the work. They appointed Levites twenty years old and upward to press on the Lord's work.

3:9  LA: Stetitque Josue et filii ejus et fratres ejus, Cedmihel et filii ejus, et filii Juda, quasi vir unus, ut instarent super eos qui faciebant opus in templo Dei : filii Henadad, et filii eorum, et fratres eorum Levitæ.
     EN: Jeshua, his sons and kinsmen, Kadmiel and his sons, and the descendants of Judah stood together as one man to oversee those working on God's Temple, along with the descendants of Henadad, their sons and their fellow Levites.

3:10  LA: Fundato igitur a cæmentariis templo Domini, steterunt sacerdotes in ornatu suo cum tubis, et Levitæ filii Asaph in cymbalis, ut laudarent Deum per manus David regis Israël.
      EN: When the masons had laid the foundation of the Lord's Temple, the priests stood in their attire with trumpets, and the Levites, Asaph's descendants, with cymbals, to praise God according to the direction of David king of Israel.

3:11  LA: Et concinebant in hymnis, et confessione Domino : Quoniam bonus, quoniam in æternum misericordia ejus super Israël. Omnis quoque populus vociferabatur clamore magno in laudando Dominum, eo quod fundatum esset templum Domini.
      EN: They sang together in hymns and thanksgiving to the Lord: “For He is good, for His mercy toward Israel endures forever.” All the people also shouted with a great cry, praising the Lord because the foundation of the Lord's Temple had been laid.

3:12  LA: Plurimi etiam de sacerdotibus et Levitis, et principes patrum, et seniores, qui viderant templum prius cum fundatum esset, et hoc templum, in oculis eorum, flebant voce magna : et multi vociferantes in lætitia, elevabant vocem.
      EN: Many of the priests and Levites, the heads of the ancestral houses, and the elders who had seen the former Temple wept aloud when this Temple too had been founded before their eyes. Many others shouted for joy and raised their voices.

3:13  LA: Nec poterat quisquam agnoscere vocem clamoris lætantium, et vocem fletus populi : commistim enim populus vociferabatur clamore magno, et vox audiebatur procul.
      EN: No one could distinguish the sound of the joyful shouting from the people's weeping, for the people cried out together with a great shout, and the sound was heard far away.

EZRA 4

4:1  LA: Audierunt autem hostes Judæ et Benjamin, quia filii captivitatis ædificarent templum Domino Deo Israël :
     EN: The enemies of Judah and Benjamin heard that the former captives were building a Temple to the Lord, the God of Israel.

4:2  LA: et accedentes ad Zorobabel, et ad principes patrum, dixerunt eis : Ædificemus vobiscum, quia ita ut vos, quærimus Deum vestrum : ecce nos immolavimus victimas a diebus Asor Haddan regis Assur, qui adduxit nos huc.
     EN: They approached Zerubbabel and the heads of the ancestral houses and said, “Let us build with you, for we seek your God as you do. We have offered sacrifices since the days of Esarhaddon king of Assyria, who brought us here.”

4:3  LA: Et dixit eis Zorobabel, et Josue, et reliqui principes patrum Israël : Non est vobis et nobis ut ædificemus domum Deo nostro, sed nos ipsi soli ædificabimus Domino Deo nostro, sicut præcepit nobis Cyrus rex Persarum.
     EN: Zerubbabel, Jeshua, and the other heads of Israel's ancestral houses said to them, “It is not for you and us to build a house for our God together. We alone will build for the Lord our God, as Cyrus king of the Persians commanded us.”

4:4  LA: Factum est igitur ut populus terræ impediret manus populi Judæ, et turbaret eos in ædificando.
     EN: So the people of the land hindered the hands of the people of Judah and troubled them as they built.

4:5  LA: Conduxerunt autem adversus eos consiliatores, ut destruerent consilium eorum omnibus diebus Cyri regis Persarum, et usque ad regnum Darii regis Persarum.
     EN: They hired counselors against them to defeat their plan throughout the days of Cyrus king of the Persians and until the reign of Darius king of the Persians.

4:6  LA: In regno autem Assueri, in principio regni ejus, scripserunt accusationem adversus habitatores Judæ et Jerusalem.
     EN: In the reign of Ahasuerus, at the beginning of his reign, they wrote an accusation against the inhabitants of Judah and Jerusalem.

4:7  LA: Et in diebus Artaxerxis scripsit Beselam, Mithridates, et Thabeel, et reliqui qui erant in consilio eorum, ad Artaxerxem regem Persarum : epistola autem accusationis scripta erat syriace, et legebatur sermone syro.
     EN: In the days of Artaxerxes, Bishlam, Mithredath, Tabeel, and the rest of their council wrote to Artaxerxes king of the Persians. The letter of accusation was written in Syriac and read in the Syriac language.

4:8  LA: Reum Beelteem, et Samsai scriba, scripserunt epistolam unam de Jerusalem Artaxerxi regi, hujuscemodi :
     EN: Rehum Beelteem and Shimshai the scribe wrote a letter of this kind concerning Jerusalem to King Artaxerxes:

4:9  LA: Reum Beelteem, et Samsai scriba, et reliqui consiliatores eorum, Dinæi, et Apharsathachæi, Terphalæi, Apharsæi, Erchuæi, Babylonii, Susanechæi, Dievi, et Ælamitæ,
     EN: Rehum Beelteem, Shimshai the scribe, the rest of their counselors, the Dinaeans, Apharsathachites, Tarpelites, Apharsites, Erechites, Babylonians, Susanchites, Dehavites, and Elamites,

4:10  LA: et ceteri de gentibus, quas transtulit Asenaphar magnus et gloriosus, et habitare eas fecit in civitatibus Samariæ, et in reliquis regionibus trans flumen in pace
      EN: and the rest of the nations whom the great and glorious Asenaphar had brought over and settled in the cities of Samaria and the other regions beyond the River, in peace—

4:11  LA: (hoc est exemplar epistolæ, quam miserunt ad eum), Artaxerxi regi, servi tui, viri qui sunt trans fluvium, salutem dicunt.
      EN: this is the copy of the letter they sent him—“To King Artaxerxes, your servants, the men beyond the River, send greetings.

4:12  LA: Notum sit regi quia Judæi, qui ascenderunt a te ad nos, venerunt in Jerusalem civitatem rebellem et pessimam, quam ædificant exstruentes muros ejus, et parietes componentes.
      EN: Let it be known to the king that the Jews who came up from you to us have come to Jerusalem, that rebellious and very wicked city. They are building it, erecting its walls and putting its ramparts together.

4:13  LA: Nunc igitur notum sit regi, quia si civitas illa ædificata fuerit, et muri ejus instaurati, tributum, et vectigal, et annuos reditus non dabunt, et usque ad reges hæc noxa perveniet.
      EN: Now let it be known to the king that if this city is built and its walls restored, they will not pay tribute, toll, or annual revenues, and the loss will reach even the kings.

4:14  LA: Nos autem memores salis, quod in palatio comedimus, et quia læsiones regis videre nefas ducimus, idcirco misimus et nuntiavimus regi,
      EN: We remember the salt we eat in the palace, and we regard it as wrong to see injury done to the king. Therefore we have sent word to the king,

4:15  LA: ut recenseas in libris historiarum patrum tuorum, et invenies scriptum in commentariis : et scies quoniam urbs illa, urbs rebellis est, et nocens regibus et provinciis, et bella concitantur in ea ex diebus antiquis : quam ob rem et civitas ipsa destructa est.
      EN: so that you may search the historical books of your fathers. You will find it written in the records and learn that this city is rebellious and harmful to kings and provinces, and that wars have been stirred up in it from ancient days. For this reason the city itself was destroyed.

4:16  LA: Nuntiamus nos regi, quoniam si civitas illa ædificata fuerit, et muri ipsius instaurati, possessionem trans fluvium non habebis.
      EN: We inform the king that if this city is built and its walls restored, you will have no possession beyond the River.”

4:17  LA: Verbum misit rex ad Reum Beelteem, et Samsai scribam, et ad reliquos, qui erant in consilio eorum habitatores Samariæ, et ceteris trans fluvium, salutem dicens et pacem.
      EN: The king sent word to Rehum Beelteem, Shimshai the scribe, and the rest of their council who dwelt in Samaria, and to the others beyond the River, sending greetings and peace:

4:18  LA: Accusatio, quam misistis ad nos, manifeste lecta est coram me,
      EN: “The accusation you sent us has been plainly read before me.

4:19  LA: et a me præceptum est : et recensuerunt, inveneruntque quoniam civitas illa a diebus antiquis adversum reges rebellat, et seditiones, et prælia concitantur in ea :
      EN: I issued a command, and a search was made. They found that this city has rebelled against kings from ancient days, and that sedition and battles are stirred up in it.

4:20  LA: nam et reges fortissimi fuerunt in Jerusalem, qui et dominati sunt omni regioni quæ trans fluvium est : tributum quoque et vectigal, et reditus accipiebant.
      EN: Very powerful kings have also ruled in Jerusalem, governing the whole region beyond the River and receiving tribute, toll, and revenues.

4:21  LA: Nunc ergo audite sententiam : prohibeatis viros illos, ut urbs illa non ædificetur donec si forte a me jussum fuerit.
      EN: Now hear the decision: restrain these men, so that this city is not built until I should give the command.

4:22  LA: Videte ne negligenter hoc impleatis, et paulatim crescat malum contra reges.
      EN: See that you do not carry this out negligently, lest the harm against the kings gradually increase.”

4:23  LA: Itaque exemplum edicti Artaxerxis regis lectum est coram Reum Beelteem, et Samsai scriba, et consiliariis eorum : et abierunt festini in Jerusalem ad Judæos, et prohibuerunt eos in brachio et robore.
      EN: The copy of King Artaxerxes's decree was read before Rehum Beelteem, Shimshai the scribe, and their counselors. They hurried to the Jews in Jerusalem and stopped them by force and strength.

4:24  LA: Tunc intermissum est opus domus Domini in Jerusalem, et non fiebat usque ad annum secundum regni Darii regis Persarum.
      EN: Then work on the Lord's house in Jerusalem ceased, and it was not resumed until the second year of Darius king of the Persians.

EZRA 5

5:1  LA: Prophetaverunt autem Aggæus propheta, et Zacharias filius Addo, prophetantes ad Judæos qui erant in Judæa et Jerusalem, in nomine Dei Israël.
     EN: The prophets Haggai and Zechariah son of Iddo prophesied to the Jews in Judah and Jerusalem in the name of the God of Israel.

5:2  LA: Tunc surrexerunt Zorobabel filius Salathiel, et Josue filius Josedec, et cœperunt ædificare templum Dei in Jerusalem, et cum eis prophetæ Dei adjuvantes eos.
     EN: Then Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua son of Jozadak arose and began to build God's Temple in Jerusalem, with God's prophets helping them.

5:3  LA: In ipso autem tempore venit ad eos Thathanai, qui erat dux trans flumen, et Stharbuzanai, et consiliarii eorum : sicque dixerunt eis : Quis dedit vobis consilium ut domum hanc ædificaretis, et muros ejus instauraretis ?
     EN: At that time Tattenai, governor beyond the River, Shethar-bozenai, and their counselors came to them and said, “Who advised you to build this house and restore its walls?”

5:4  LA: Ad quod respondimus eis, quæ essent nomina hominum auctorum ædificationis illius.
     EN: We answered them with the names of the men responsible for that building.

5:5  LA: Oculus autem Dei eorum factus est super senes Judæorum, et non potuerunt inhibere eos. Placuitque ut res ad Darium referretur, et tunc satisfacerent adversus accusationem illam.
     EN: But the eye of their God was upon the elders of the Jews, and they could not stop them. It was agreed that the matter should be referred to Darius, and that they should then answer this accusation.

5:6  LA: Exemplar epistolæ, quam misit Thathanai dux regionis trans flumen, et Stharbuzanai, et consiliatores ejus Arphasachæi, qui erant trans flumen, ad Darium regem.
     EN: This is a copy of the letter Tattenai, governor of the region beyond the River, Shethar-bozenai, and his Apharsachite counselors beyond the River sent to King Darius.

5:7  LA: Sermo, quem miserant ei, sic scriptus erat : Dario regi pax omnis.
     EN: The message they sent him was written as follows: “All peace to King Darius.

5:8  LA: Notum sit regi, isse nos ad Judæam provinciam, ad domum Dei magni, quæ ædificatur lapide impolito, et ligna ponuntur in parietibus : opusque illud diligenter exstruitur, et crescit in manibus eorum.
     EN: Let it be known to the king that we went to the province of Judah, to the house of the great God. It is being built of unpolished stone, and timber is being set in the walls. The work is being diligently constructed and progresses in their hands.

5:9  LA: Interrogavimus ergo senes illos, et ita diximus eis : Quis dedit vobis potestatem ut domum hanc ædificaretis, et muros hos instauraretis ?
     EN: We questioned those elders, saying, ‘Who gave you authority to build this house and restore these walls?’

5:10  LA: Sed et nomina eorum quæsivimus ab eis, ut nuntiaremus tibi : scripsimusque nomina eorum virorum, qui sunt principes in eis.
      EN: We also asked them their names, to inform you, and wrote down the names of the men who are their leaders.

5:11  LA: Hujuscemodi autem sermonem responderunt nobis dicentes : Nos sumus servi Dei cæli et terræ, et ædificamus templum, quod erat exstructum ante hos annos multos, quodque rex Israël magnus ædificaverat, et exstruxerat.
      EN: They answered us in these words: ‘We are servants of the God of heaven and earth, and we are rebuilding the Temple that was built many years ago, which a great king of Israel built and erected.

5:12  LA: Postquam autem ad iracundiam provocaverunt patres nostri Deum cæli, tradidit eos in manus Nabuchodonosor regis Babylonis Chaldæi, domum quoque hanc destruxit, et populum ejus transtulit in Babylonem.
      EN: But after our fathers provoked the God of heaven to anger, He delivered them into the hands of Nebuchadnezzar, the Chaldean king of Babylon. He destroyed this house and carried its people away to Babylon.

5:13  LA: Anno autem primo Cyri regis Babylonis, Cyrus rex proposuit edictum ut domus Dei hæc ædificaretur.
      EN: In the first year of Cyrus king of Babylon, King Cyrus issued a decree that this house of God should be built.

5:14  LA: Nam et vasa templi Dei aurea et argentea, quæ Nabuchodonosor tulerat de templo, quod erat in Jerusalem, et asportaverat ea in templum Babylonis, protulit Cyrus rex de templo Babylonis, et data sunt Sassabasar vocabulo, quem et principem constituit,
      EN: King Cyrus also brought out from the temple of Babylon the gold and silver vessels of God's Temple, which Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the Temple in Jerusalem and carried to the temple of Babylon. They were given to a man named Sheshbazzar, whom he appointed governor.

5:15  LA: dixitque ei : Hæc vasa tolle, et vade, et pone ea in templo, quod est in Jerusalem, et domus Dei ædificetur in loco suo.
      EN: He said to him, “Take these vessels, go, and place them in the Temple in Jerusalem, and let God's house be built on its site.”

5:16  LA: Tunc itaque Sassabasar ille venit et posuit fundamenta templi Dei in Jerusalem, et ex eo tempore usque nunc ædificatur, et necdum completum est.
      EN: Then this Sheshbazzar came and laid the foundations of God's Temple in Jerusalem. From that time until now it has been under construction and is not yet complete.’

5:17  LA: Nunc ergo si videtur regi bonum, recenseat in bibliotheca regis, quæ est in Babylone, utrumnam a Cyro rege jussum fuerit ut ædificaretur domus Dei in Jerusalem, et voluntatem regis super hac re mittat ad nos.
      EN: Now, if it seems good to the king, let a search be made in the king's archive in Babylon to determine whether King Cyrus commanded that God's house in Jerusalem be built, and let the king send us his will concerning this matter.”

EZRA 6

6:1  LA: Tunc Darius rex præcepit : et recensuerunt in bibliotheca librorum, qui erant repositi in Babylone.
     EN: Then King Darius gave a command, and a search was made in the archive of books stored in Babylon.

6:2  LA: Et inventum est in Ecbatanis, quod est castrum in Medena provincia, volumen unum : talisque scriptus erat in eo commentarius :
     EN: A scroll was found at Ecbatana, a fortress in the province of Media, and this record was written in it:

6:3  LA: Anno primo Cyri regis, Cyrus rex decrevit ut domus Dei ædificaretur, quæ est in Jerusalem, in loco ubi immolent hostias, et ut ponant fundamenta supportantia altitudinem cubitorum sexaginta, et latitudinem cubitorum sexaginta,
     EN: “In the first year of King Cyrus, King Cyrus decreed that God's house in Jerusalem should be built on the site where sacrifices are offered, and that foundations be laid to support a height of sixty cubits and a width of sixty cubits,

6:4  LA: ordines de lapidibus impolitis tres, et sic ordines de lignis novis : sumptus autem de domo regis dabuntur.
     EN: with three courses of unpolished stones, and likewise courses of new timber. The expenses shall be paid from the king's house.

6:5  LA: Sed et vasa templi Dei aurea et argentea, quæ Nabuchodonosor tulerat de templo Jerusalem, et attulerat ea in Babylonem, reddantur, et referantur in templum in Jerusalem in locum suum, quæ et posita sunt in templo Dei.
     EN: The gold and silver vessels of God's Temple, which Nebuchadnezzar took from the Temple in Jerusalem and brought to Babylon, shall also be returned and brought back to their place in the Temple in Jerusalem; they were also placed in God's Temple.

6:6  LA: Nunc ergo Thathanai dux regionis, quæ est trans flumen, Stharbuzanai, et consiliarii vestri Apharsachæi, qui estis trans flumen, procul recedite ab illis,
     EN: “Now, Tattenai, governor of the region beyond the River, Shethar-bozenai, and your Apharsachite counselors beyond the River, keep far away from them.

6:7  LA: et dimittite fieri templum Dei illud a duce Judæorum, et a senioribus eorum, ut domum Dei illam ædificent in loco suo.
     EN: Allow this Temple of God to be built by the governor of the Jews and their elders, that they may build this house of God on its site.

6:8  LA: Sed et a me præceptum est quid oporteat fieri a presbyteris Judæorum illis ut ædificetur domus Dei, scilicet ut de arca regis, id est, de tributis quæ dantur de regione trans flumen, studiose sumptus dentur viris illis, ne impediatur opus.
     EN: I also command what those elders of the Jews must do so that God's house may be built: the expenses are to be paid diligently to these men from the king's treasury, that is, from the tribute paid by the region beyond the River, so that the work is not hindered.

6:9  LA: Quod si necesse fuerit, et vitulos, et agnos, et hædos in holocaustum Deo cæli, frumentum, sal, vinum, et oleum, secundum ritum sacerdotum, qui sunt in Jerusalem, detur eis per singulos dies, ne sit in aliquo querimonia.
     EN: Whatever is needed—young bulls, lambs, and young goats for burnt offerings to the God of heaven, and grain, salt, wine, and oil according to the requirements of the priests in Jerusalem—shall be given them daily, so that there is no cause for complaint.

6:10  LA: Et offerant oblationes Deo cæli, orentque pro vita regis, et filiorum ejus.
      EN: Let them offer oblations to the God of heaven and pray for the life of the king and his sons.

6:11  LA: A me ergo positum est decretum : ut omnis homo qui hanc mutaverit jussionem, tollatur lignum de domum ipsius, et erigatur, et configatur in eo, domus autem ejus publicetur.
      EN: I have also issued this decree: whoever alters this command shall have a timber taken from his house, erected, and himself fastened upon it; his house shall be confiscated.

6:12  LA: Deus autem, qui habitare fecit nomen suum ibi, dissipet omnia regna, et populum qui extenderit manum suam ut repugnet, et dissipet domum Dei illam, quæ est in Jerusalem. Ego Darius statui decretum, quod studiose impleri volo.
      EN: May the God who has made His name dwell there overthrow every kingdom and people that stretches out its hand to oppose and destroy this house of God in Jerusalem. I, Darius, have established this decree and desire it to be carried out diligently.”

6:13  LA: Igitur Thathanai dux regionis trans flumen, et Stharbuzanai, et consiliarii ejus, secundum quod præceperat Darius rex, sic diligenter executi sunt.
      EN: Tattenai, governor of the region beyond the River, Shethar-bozenai, and his counselors diligently carried out what King Darius had commanded.

6:14  LA: Seniores autem Judæorum ædificabant, et prosperabantur juxta prophetiam Aggæi prophetæ, et Zachariæ filii Addo : et ædificaverunt et construxerunt, jubente Deo Israël, et jubente Cyro, et Dario, et Artaxerxe regibus Persarum :
      EN: The elders of the Jews built and prospered according to the prophecy of Haggai the prophet and Zechariah son of Iddo. They built and completed the work at the command of the God of Israel and at the command of Cyrus, Darius, and Artaxerxes, kings of the Persians.

6:15  LA: et compleverunt domum Dei istam, usque ad diem tertium mensis Adar, qui est annus sextus regni Darii regis.
      EN: They completed this house of God by the third day of the month Adar, in the sixth year of King Darius's reign.

6:16  LA: Fecerunt autem filii Israël sacerdotes et Levitæ, et reliqui filiorum transmigrationis, dedicationem domus Dei in gaudio.
      EN: The children of Israel, the priests, the Levites, and the rest of those who had returned from exile celebrated the dedication of God's house with joy.

6:17  LA: Et obtulerunt in dedicationem domus Dei, vitulos centum, arietes ducentos, agnos quadringentos, hircos caprarum pro peccato totius Israël duodecim, juxta numerum tribuum Israël.
      EN: For the dedication of God's house they offered a hundred young bulls, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs, and twelve male goats as a sin offering for all Israel, according to the number of Israel's tribes.

6:18  LA: Et statuerunt sacerdotes in ordinibus suis, et Levitas in vicibus suis, super opera Dei in Jerusalem, sicut scriptum est in libro Moysi.
      EN: They appointed the priests in their divisions and the Levites in their courses for God's service in Jerusalem, as written in the book of Moses.

6:19  LA: Fecerunt autem filii Israël transmigrationis Pascha, quartadecima die mensis primi.
      EN: The children of Israel who had returned from exile celebrated the Passover on the fourteenth day of the first month.

6:20  LA: Purificati enim fuerant sacerdotes et Levitæ quasi unus : omnes mundi ad immolandum Pascha universis filiis transmigrationis, et fratribus suis sacerdotibus, et sibi.
      EN: For the priests and Levites had purified themselves as one; all were clean to slaughter the Passover for all the returned exiles, for their fellow priests, and for themselves.

6:21  LA: Et comederunt filii Israël, qui reversi fuerant de transmigratione, et omnes qui se separaverant a coinquinatione gentium terræ ad eos, ut quærerent Dominum Deum Israël.
      EN: The children of Israel who had returned from exile ate it, together with all who had separated themselves from the uncleanness of the nations of the land and joined them to seek the Lord, the God of Israel.

6:22  LA: Et fecerunt solemnitatem azymorum septem diebus in lætitia, quoniam lætificaverat eos Dominus, et converterat cor regis Assur ad eos, ut adjuvaret manus eorum in opere domus Domini Dei Israël.
      EN: They celebrated the Feast of Unleavened Bread with joy for seven days, for the Lord had made them glad and turned the heart of the king of Assyria toward them, to strengthen their hands in the work of the house of the Lord, the God of Israel.

EZRA 7

7:1  LA: Post hæc autem verba in regno Artaxerxis regis Persarum, Esdras filius Saraiæ, filii Azariæ, filii Helciæ,
     EN: After these events, in the reign of Artaxerxes king of the Persians, Ezra son of Seraiah, son of Azariah, son of Hilkiah,

7:2  LA: filii Sellum, filii Sadoc, filii Achitob,
     EN: son of Shallum, son of Zadok, son of Ahitub,

7:3  LA: filii Amariæ, filii Azariæ, filii Maraioth,
     EN: son of Amariah, son of Azariah, son of Meraioth,

7:4  LA: filii Zarahiæ, filii Ozi, filii Bocci,
     EN: son of Zerahiah, son of Uzzi, son of Bukki,

7:5  LA: filii Abisue, filii Phinees, filii Eleazar, filii Aaron sacerdotis ab initio.
     EN: son of Abishua, son of Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron, the priest from the beginning—

7:6  LA: Ipse Esdras ascendit de Babylone, et ipse scriba velox in lege Moysi, quam Dominus Deus dedit Israël : et dedit ei rex secundum manum Domini Dei ejus super eum, omnem petitionem ejus.
     EN: this Ezra came up from Babylon. He was a skilled scribe in the law of Moses, which the Lord God had given Israel. The king granted him every request, according to the hand of the Lord his God upon him.

7:7  LA: Et ascenderunt de filiis Israël, et de filiis sacerdotum, et de filiis Levitarum, et de cantoribus, et de janitoribus, et de Nathinæis, in Jerusalem, anno septimo Artaxerxis regis.
     EN: Some of the children of Israel, the priests, the Levites, the singers, the gatekeepers, and the Nethinim came up to Jerusalem in the seventh year of King Artaxerxes.

7:8  LA: Et venerunt in Jerusalem mense quinto, ipse est annus septimus regis.
     EN: They came to Jerusalem in the fifth month, in that seventh year of the king.

7:9  LA: Quia in primo die mensis primi cœpit ascendere de Babylone, et in primo die mensis quinti venit in Jerusalem, juxta manum Dei sui bonam super se.
     EN: For on the first day of the first month he began the journey up from Babylon, and on the first day of the fifth month he reached Jerusalem, according to the good hand of his God upon him.

7:10  LA: Esdras enim paravit cor suum, ut investigaret legem Domini, et faceret et doceret in Israël præceptum et judicium.
      EN: Ezra had prepared his heart to investigate the Lord's law, to carry it out, and to teach statute and judgment in Israel.

7:11  LA: Hoc est autem exemplar epistolæ edicti, quod dedit rex Artaxerxes Esdræ sacerdoti, scribæ erudito in sermonibus et præceptis Domini, et cæremoniis ejus in Israël.
      EN: This is a copy of the written decree King Artaxerxes gave Ezra the priest, a scribe learned in the Lord's words and commandments and His ordinances for Israel:

7:12  LA: Artaxerxes rex regum Esdræ sacerdoti scribæ legis Dei cæli doctissimo, salutem.
      EN: “Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, a most learned scribe in the law of the God of heaven: greetings.

7:13  LA: A me decretum est, ut cuicumque placuerit in regno meo de populo Israël, et de sacerdotibus ejus, et de Levitis, ire in Jerusalem, tecum vadat.
      EN: I decree that anyone in my kingdom from the people of Israel, their priests, and their Levites who wishes to go to Jerusalem may go with you.

7:14  LA: A facie enim regis, et septem consiliatorum ejus, missus es, ut visites Judæam et Jerusalem in lege Dei tui, quæ est in manu tua :
      EN: For you are sent by the king and his seven counselors to visit Judah and Jerusalem according to the law of your God, which is in your hand,

7:15  LA: et ut feras argentum et aurum quod rex, et consiliatores ejus, sponte obtulerunt Deo Israël, cujus in Jerusalem tabernaculum est.
      EN: and to carry the silver and gold that the king and his counselors have freely offered to the God of Israel, whose dwelling is in Jerusalem.

7:16  LA: Et omne argentum et aurum quodcumque inveneris in universa provincia Babylonis, et populus offerre voluerit, et de sacerdotibus quæ sponte obtulerint domui Dei sui, quæ est in Jerusalem,
      EN: All the silver and gold you find throughout the province of Babylon that the people wish to offer, and whatever the priests freely offer to the house of their God in Jerusalem,

7:17  LA: libere accipe, et studiose eme de hac pecunia vitulos, arietes, agnos, et sacrificia, et libamina eorum, et offer ea super altare templi Dei vestri, quod est in Jerusalem.
      EN: accept freely. With this money diligently buy young bulls, rams, lambs, and their offerings and libations, and offer them upon the altar of your God's Temple in Jerusalem.

7:18  LA: Sed et si quid tibi et fratribus tuis placuerit de reliquo argento et auro ut faciatis, juxta voluntatem Dei vestri facite.
      EN: Whatever you and your kinsmen see fit to do with the remaining silver and gold, do according to your God's will.

7:19  LA: Vasa quoque, quæ dantur tibi in ministerium domus Dei tui, trade in conspectu Dei in Jerusalem.
      EN: Deliver the vessels given you for the service of your God's house before God in Jerusalem.

7:20  LA: Sed et cetera, quibus opus fuerit in domum Dei tui, quantumcumque necesse est ut expendas, dabitur de thesauro, et de fisco regis,
      EN: Whatever else is needed for your God's house, however much you must spend, will be provided from the royal store and treasury,

7:21  LA: et a me. Ego Artaxerxes rex, statui atque decrevi omnibus custodibus arcæ publicæ, qui sunt trans flumen, ut quodcumque petierit a vobis Esdras sacerdos, scriba legis Dei cæli, absque mora detis,
      EN: and from me. I, King Artaxerxes, establish and decree to all keepers of the public treasury beyond the River: whatever Ezra the priest, scribe of the law of the God of heaven, asks of you, give without delay,

7:22  LA: usque ad argenti talenta centum, et usque ad frumenti coros centum, et usque ad vini batos centum, et usque ad batos olei centum, sal vero absque mensura.
      EN: up to a hundred talents of silver, a hundred cors of grain, a hundred baths of wine, and a hundred baths of oil, and salt without measure.

7:23  LA: Omne quod ad ritum Dei cæli pertinet, tribuatur diligenter in domo Dei cæli : ne forte irascatur contra regnum regis, et filiorum ejus.
      EN: Let everything belonging to the worship of the God of heaven be diligently provided in the house of the God of heaven, lest He become angry with the kingdom of the king and his sons.

7:24  LA: Vobis quoque notum facimus de universis sacerdotibus, et Levitis, et cantoribus, et janitoribus, Nathinæis, et ministris domus Dei hujus, ut vectigal, et tributum, et annonas non habeatis potestatem imponendi super eos.
      EN: We also inform you that you have no authority to impose toll, tribute, or provisions upon any priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, Nethinim, or servants of this house of God.

7:25  LA: Tu autem Esdra, secundum sapientiam Dei tui, quæ est in manu tua, constitue judices et præsides, ut judicent omni populo qui est trans flumen, his videlicet qui noverunt legem Dei tui : sed et imperitos docete libere.
      EN: And you, Ezra, according to the wisdom of your God that is in your hand, appoint judges and governors to judge all the people beyond the River, namely those who know your God's law. Freely teach those who do not know it.

7:26  LA: Et omnis qui non fecerit legem Dei tui, et legem regis, diligenter, judicium erit de eo sive in mortem, sive in exilium, sive in condemnationem substantiæ ejus, vel certe in carcerem.
      EN: Whoever does not observe your God's law and the king's law shall be diligently judged, whether by death, exile, confiscation of his possessions, or imprisonment.”

7:27  LA: Benedictus Dominus Deus patrum nostrorum, qui dedit hoc in corde regis ut glorificaret domum Domini quæ est in Jerusalem,
      EN: Blessed be the Lord, the God of our fathers, who put this in the king's heart, to glorify the Lord's house in Jerusalem,

7:28  LA: et in me inclinavit misericordiam suam coram rege et consiliatoribus ejus, et universis principibus regis potentibus : et ego confortatus manu Domini Dei mei, quæ erat in me, congregavi de Israël principes qui ascenderent mecum.
      EN: and inclined His mercy toward me before the king, his counselors, and all the king's mighty princes. Strengthened by the hand of the Lord my God upon me, I gathered leaders from Israel to go up with me.

     EN: Of the descendants of Phinehas, Gershom; of the descendants of Ithamar, Daniel; of the descendants of David, Hattush.
