# Numbers study prose — draft for review

Every chapter of Numbers (1–36) now carries its own interpretive study in the Study Workspace, verse by verse, on staging site 248834710. All thirty-six chapter records have status **Draft for review**; nothing has been sent to production.

## What each verse carries

For each of the 1,287 verse keys (the site's keys follow the Hebrew/Greek chapter divisions where they differ from the English, see below):

- **received** — one sentence stating what the verse says, in the Hebrew-column wording.
- **explain** — a paragraph on how the Hebrew, Greek, Latin and King James columns relate: what they share, where the Greek departs from the Hebrew, where the Latin or King James follows one or the other, and what the site's own source notes say about the difference. Uncertain Hebrew terms (the stones and jewellery of 31:50, the last word of 35:3, the place-names of the itinerary) are reported as uncertain.
- **shared** — three short statements all four columns agree on.
- **instructions** — a Knowledge line (stated), a Wisdom line (reflection), and a Command line wherever the verse contains a command. 2,693 instructions in all, 119 of them Commands.
- **entries** — links into the lexicon catalogue, 79 distinct entries (YHWH 272 verses; Moses 204; high priest, used for Aaron, Eleazar and Phinehas, 146; tabernacle 62; Egypt 32; altar 32; incense 25; Canaan 24; Jordan 22; Joshua 17; Sinai 13; savor 13; Joseph 12; ark 11; Elohim 11; redeemer (the blood-avenger) 9; Miriam 9; grapevine 9; Jacob 8; angel of the Lord 8; Jericho 8; Passover lamb 7; rock 6; lampstand 6; Spirit 6; Baal 6; bronze serpent, manna, Og, David, Abraham, El Shaddai …).
- **lost** — what the Greek loses from the Hebrew, what the Greek has in its place or adds, and what the Latin and King James do with the difference. Recurring differences are carried by shared constants so the wording is the same wherever the same difference recurs (Tent of Meeting / Tent of Testimony; grain offering / sacrifice; fire offering / offering; young bull / calf; between the evenings / toward evening; plains of Moab / western side of Moab; contribution / firstfruits; well-being / salvation offerings; Latin "a tenth of a tenth"; the Greek's reversed tribe orders).
- A **chapter note** summarising the chapter and listing the Hebrew–Greek differences in it.

## For the reviewer

- **Chapter and verse divisions.** The site's keys follow the Hebrew and Greek, not the English: chapter 16 has 35 verses and 17 has 28 (English 16:36–50 = 17:1–15, English 17:1–13 = 17:16–28); English 29:40 is 30:1 and English 30:1–16 is 30:2–17; chapter 6 has 26 keys because the Greek places the Hebrew's 6:27 before the blessing. Chapter 26 has 65 keys with the source aligning by tribe where the Greek reorders them; chapter 1 likewise reorders Judah–Gad. The chapter notes say so, and a NUM/ORDER constant carries the note into `lost`.
- **Where the Greek changes the sense, not just the word.** "My gifts, My donations … at My feasts" for "My offering, My food … at its appointed time" (28:2); "the Lord will cleanse her" for "will forgive her" throughout the vow law (30:6, 9, 13), and at 30:16 "after the day … his sin" for "after he has heard … her guilt"; "who know evil and good" added to the condemned generation (32:11); "the one set apart" for "the Kenizzite" (32:12); "act lawlessly against the congregation" for "destroy this whole people" (32:15); a long added clause on moving the eastern tribes' households into Canaan (32:30); "destroy the inhabitants" for "dispossess" (33:52–55); two thousand cubits at 35:4 where the Hebrew has one thousand; "commit murder against the land" for "pollute the land" (35:33); the propitiation formula added at 29:11; Aaron added to Phinehas's descent and the trumpets in "their hands" (31:6).
- **Place-names.** The Greek translates many (Marah "Bitternesses", Kibroth-hattaavah "Graves of Desire", Eshcol "Cluster", Havvoth-jair "villages of Jair", Pi-hahiroth "mouth of Eiroth", Iye-abarim "Gai, on the other side") and respells others (the stations of 33, the chiefs of 34, the kings of 31:8); it inserts a Paran stage at 33:36 and drops Nebo at 32:38, Atroth and Jogbehah at 32:35. The prose reports each as the Greek's reading; the Latin and King James follow the Hebrew form in nearly every case.
- **The hard chapters.** 31:17–18 (the Midianite boys and women killed, the girls kept "for yourselves") and 33:52–56 are reported as the text has them in every column; the Wisdom lines say plainly that the text does not say less than it says and that it is not a pattern for anyone. 31:40 (thirty-two persons as YHWH's tribute) is explained as servants given to the priests, not sacrifices, following the source note.
- **The Hebrew's own difficulties.** 35:3 (the last word: livelihood or living creatures, Rashi's "needs"); 35:4–5 (a thousand and two thousand cubits); 31:50 (the jewellery terms); 34:7 (Mount Hor in the north is not Aaron's); 30:7 ("a rash utterance", which the Greek lacks). Each is marked as disputed or uncertain.
- **Lexicon gaps.** No catalogue entries exist for Aaron, Eleazar or Phinehas (so `high-priest` is used), Balaam, Korah, the Levites as a tribe, the Nazirite, sin or peace offerings, the red heifer, cities of refuge, Jubilee, vows, the blood-avenger as such (`redeemer` is used), Zelophehad's daughters, Caleb, Midian, Moab, Edom, Sihon (Og exists), Hormah, Kadesh, Mount Hor, Pisgah, Peor, the ban, or the itinerary's stations. Adding entries and re-merging is a small job.
- **Voice.** As for Genesis, Exodus and Leviticus: Wisdom lines are reflective, one sentence or two, and point forward only to what later Scripture plainly does with the verse (the bronze serpent and John 3, the rock and 1 Corinthians 10, the refuge and Hebrews 6, Balaam's star, the Aaronic blessing, the two witnesses). Commands are quoted in the Hebrew-column wording.
