PHILEMON 1

1:1  LA: Paulus vinctus Christi Jesu, et Timotheus frater, Philemoni dilecto, et adjutori nostro,
     EN: Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother, to Philemon, our beloved fellow worker,

1:2  LA: et Appiæ sorori carissimæ, et Archippo commilitoni nostro, et ecclesiæ, quæ in domo tua est.
     EN: to our dearest sister Apphia, to Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the church in your house:

1:3  LA: Gratia vobis, et pax a Deo Patre nostro, et Domino Jesu Christo.
     EN: grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

1:4  LA: Gratias ago Deo meo, semper memoriam tui faciens in orationibus meis,
     EN: I thank my God, always remembering you in my prayers,

1:5  LA: audiens caritatem tuam, et fidem, quam habes in Domino Jesu, et in omnes sanctos:
     EN: as I hear of your love and the faith you have in the Lord Jesus and toward all the saints,

1:6  LA: ut communicatio fidei tuæ evidens fiat in agnitione omnis operis boni, quod est in vobis in Christo Jesu.
     EN: that the sharing of your faith may become evident in the recognition of every good work that is in you in Christ Jesus.

1:7  LA: Gaudium enim magnum habui, et consolationem in caritate tua: quia viscera sanctorum requieverunt per te, frater.
     EN: For I have had great joy and comfort in your love, because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through you, brother.

1:8  LA: Propter quod multam fiduciam habens in Christo Jesu imperandi tibi quod ad rem pertinet:
     EN: Therefore, although I have great boldness in Christ Jesus to command you to do what is fitting,

1:9  LA: propter caritatem magis obsecro, cum sis talis, ut Paulus senex, nunc autem et vinctus Jesu Christi:
     EN: I appeal instead for love’s sake, since you are such a man as Paul, an old man and now also a prisoner of Jesus Christ.

1:10  LA: obsecro te pro meo filio, quem genui in vinculis, Onesimo,
      EN: I appeal to you for my son Onesimus, whom I have fathered in chains.

1:11  LA: qui tibi aliquando inutilis fuit, nunc autem et mihi et tibi utilis,
      EN: He was once useless to you, but is now useful to both me and you.

1:12  LA: quem remisi tibi. Tu autem illum, ut mea viscera, suscipe:
      EN: I have sent him back to you. Receive him as my own heart.

1:13  LA: quem ego volueram mecum detinere, ut pro te mihi ministraret in vinculis Evangelii:
      EN: I had wished to keep him with me, so that he might serve me in your place while I am in chains for the gospel.

1:14  LA: sine consilio autem tuo nihil volui facere, uti ne velut ex necessitate bonum tuum esset, sed voluntarium.
      EN: But I wanted to do nothing without your consent, so that your good deed might be voluntary and not, as it were, compelled.

1:15  LA: Forsitan enim ideo discessit ad horam a te, ut æternum illum reciperes:
      EN: For perhaps he was separated from you for a time so that you might receive him forever,

1:16  LA: jam non ut servum, sed pro servo carissimum fratrem, maxime mihi: quanto autem magis tibi et in carne, et in Domino?
      EN: no longer as a slave, but in place of a slave, a dearly loved brother—especially to me, but how much more to you, both in the flesh and in the Lord!

1:17  LA: Si ergo habes me socium, suscipe illum sicut me:
      EN: So if you consider me your partner, receive him as you would me.

1:18  LA: si autem aliquid nocuit tibi, aut debet, hoc mihi imputa.
      EN: If he has wronged you in anything or owes you anything, charge that to me.

1:19  LA: Ego Paulus scripsi mea manu: ego reddam, ut non dicam tibi, quod et teipsum mihi debes:
      EN: I, Paul, have written this with my own hand: I will repay it—to say nothing of the fact that you owe me your very self.

1:20  LA: ita, frater. Ego te fruar in Domino: refice viscera mea in Domino.
      EN: Yes, brother, let me have this benefit from you in the Lord. Refresh my heart in the Lord.

1:21  LA: Confidens in obedientia tua scripsi tibi: sciens quoniam et super id, quod dico, facies.
      EN: Confident of your obedience, I have written to you, knowing that you will do even more than I say.

1:22  LA: Simul autem et para mihi hospitium: nam spero per orationes vestras donari me vobis.
      EN: At the same time, prepare a lodging for me, for I hope that through your prayers I will be granted to you.

1:23  LA: Salutat te Epaphras concaptivus meus in Christo Jesu,
      EN: Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, greets you,

1:24  LA: Marcus, Aristarchus, Demas, et Lucas, adjutores mei.
      EN: as do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, my fellow workers.

1:25  LA: Gratia Domini nostri Jesu Christi cum spiritu vestro. Amen.
      EN: The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen.
