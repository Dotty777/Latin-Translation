HOSEA 1

1:1  LA: Verbum Domini, quod factum est ad Osee, filium Beeri, in diebus Oziæ, Joathan, Achaz, Ezechiæ, regum Juda ; et in diebus Jeroboam, filii Joas, regis Israël.
     EN: The word of the Lord that came to Hosea son of Beeri in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel.

1:2  LA: Principium loquendi Domino in Osee. Et dixit Dominus ad Osee : Vade, sume tibi uxorem fornicationum, et fac tibi filios fornicationum, quia fornicans fornicabitur terra a Domino.
     EN: The beginning of the Lord's speaking through Hosea. The Lord said to Hosea, “Go, take for yourself a wife of prostitution, and have children of prostitution, for the land will prostitute itself utterly, turning away from the Lord.”

1:3  LA: Et abiit, et accepit Gomer, filiam Debelaim : et concepit, et peperit ei filium.
     EN: He went and took Gomer daughter of Diblaim, and she conceived and bore him a son.

1:4  LA: Et dixit Dominus ad eum : Voca nomen ejus Jezrahel, quoniam adhuc modicum, et visitabo sanguinem Jezrahel super domum Jehu, et quiescere faciam regnum domus Israël.
     EN: The Lord said to him, “Name him Jezreel, for in a little while I will call the house of Jehu to account for the blood of Jezreel, and I will put an end to the kingdom of the house of Israel.

1:5  LA: Et in illa die conteram arcum Israël in valle Jezrahel.
     EN: On that day I will break the bow of Israel in the valley of Jezreel.”

1:6  LA: Et concepit adhuc, et peperit filiam. Et dixit ei : Voca nomen ejus, Absque misericordia, quia non addam ultra misereri domui Israël, sed oblivione obliviscar eorum.
     EN: She conceived again and bore a daughter. He said to him, “Name her Without Mercy, for I will no longer have mercy on the house of Israel, but will forget them utterly.

1:7  LA: Et domui Juda miserebor, et salvabo eos in Domino Deo suo ; et non salvabo eos in arcu et gladio, et in bello, et in equis, et in equitibus.
     EN: Yet I will have mercy on the house of Judah and save them by the Lord their God; I will not save them by bow or sword or battle, by horses or horsemen.”

1:8  LA: Et ablactavit eam quæ erat Absque misericordia. Et concepit, et peperit filium.
     EN: She weaned the one called Without Mercy, then conceived and bore a son.

1:9  LA: Et dixit : Voca nomen ejus, Non populus meus, quia vos non populus meus, et ego non ero vester.
     EN: He said, “Name him Not My People, for you are not My people, and I will not be yours.

HOSEA 2

2:1  LA: Et erit numerus filiorum Israël quasi arena maris, quæ sine mensura est, et non numerabitur. Et erit in loco ubi dicetur eis : Non populus meus vos : dicetur eis : Filii Dei viventis.
     EN: Yet the number of the children of Israel will be like the sand of the sea, which is beyond measure and cannot be counted. In the place where they are told, ‘You are not My people,’ they will be told, ‘Children of the living God.’

2:2  LA: Et congregabuntur filii Juda et filii Israël pariter ; et ponent sibimet caput unum, et ascendent de terra, quia magnus dies Jezrahel.
     EN: The children of Judah and the children of Israel will gather together; they will appoint one head over themselves and come up from the land, for great is the day of Jezreel.

2:3  LA: Dicite fratribus vestris : Populus meus ; et sorori vestræ : Misericordiam consecuta.
     EN: Say to your brothers, ‘My People,’ and to your sister, ‘She Who Has Received Mercy.’

2:4  LA: [Judicate matrem vestram, judicate, quoniam ipsa non uxor mea, et ego non vir ejus. Auferat fornicationes suas a facie sua, et adulteria sua de medio uberum suorum ;
     EN: Bring a case against your mother, bring a case, for she is not My wife, and I am not her husband. Let her remove her prostitution from her face and her adulteries from between her breasts,

2:5  LA: ne forte expoliem eam nudam, et statuam eam secundum diem nativitatis suæ, et ponam eam quasi solitudinem, et statuam eam velut terram inviam, et interficiam eam siti.
     EN: lest I strip her naked and leave her as on the day of her birth, make her like a wilderness, leave her like a trackless land, and kill her with thirst.

2:6  LA: Et filiorum illius non miserebor, quoniam filii fornicationum sunt.
     EN: I will have no mercy on her children, for they are children of prostitution.

2:7  LA: Quia fornicata est mater eorum, confusa est quæ concepit eos ; quia dixit : Vadam post amatores meos, qui dant panes mihi, et aquas meas, lanam meam, et linum meum, oleum meum, et potum meum.
     EN: For their mother prostituted herself; she who conceived them disgraced herself. She said, ‘I will go after my lovers, who give me my bread and water, my wool and flax, my oil and drink.’

2:8  LA: Propter hoc ecce ego sepiam viam tuam spinis, et sepiam eam maceria, et semitas suas non inveniet.
     EN: Therefore, see, I will hedge your path with thorns and enclose it with a wall, and she will not find her paths.

2:9  LA: Et sequetur amatores suos, et non apprehendet eos ; et quæret eos, et non inveniet : et dicet : Vadam, et revertar ad virum meum priorem, quia bene mihi erat tunc magis quam nunc.
     EN: She will pursue her lovers but not catch them; she will seek them but not find them. Then she will say, ‘I will go and return to my former husband, for I was better off then than now.’

2:10  LA: Et hæc nescivit, quia ego dedi ei frumentum, et vinum, et oleum, et argentum multiplicavi ei, et aurum, quæ fecerunt Baal.
      EN: She did not know that it was I who gave her grain and wine and oil, and lavished silver and gold on her, which they made into a Baal.

2:11  LA: Idcirco convertar, et sumam frumentum meum in tempore suo, et vinum meum in tempore suo. Et liberabo lanam meam et linum meum, quæ operiebant ignominiam ejus.
      EN: Therefore I will turn back and take My grain at its proper time and My wine at its proper time. I will recover My wool and flax, which covered her shame.

2:12  LA: Et nunc revelabo stultitiam ejus in oculis amatorum ejus ; et vir non eruet eam de manu mea ;
      EN: Now I will expose her folly before the eyes of her lovers, and no man will rescue her from My hand.

2:13  LA: et cessare faciam omne gaudium ejus, solemnitatem ejus, neomeniam ejus, sabbatum ejus, et omnia festa tempora ejus.
      EN: I will put an end to all her joy, her solemn celebration, her new moon, her sabbath, and all her appointed feasts.

2:14  LA: Et corrumpam vineam ejus, et ficum ejus, de quibus dixit : Mercedes hæ meæ sunt, quas dederunt mihi amatores mei ; et ponam eam in saltum, et comedet eam bestia agri.
      EN: I will ruin her vineyard and her fig tree, of which she said, ‘These are my wages, which my lovers gave me.’ I will turn it into a thicket, and the wild beast will eat it.

2:15  LA: Et visitabo super eam dies Baalim, quibus accendebat incensum, et ornabatur in aure sua, et monili suo. Et ibat post amatores suos, et mei obliviscebatur, dicit Dominus.
      EN: I will call her to account for the days of the Baals, when she burned incense and adorned herself with her earring and necklace. She went after her lovers and forgot Me, says the Lord.

2:16  LA: Propter hoc ecce ego lactabo eam, et ducam eam in solitudinem, et loquar ad cor ejus.
      EN: Therefore, see, I will coax her, lead her into the wilderness, and speak to her heart.

2:17  LA: Et dabo ei vinitores ejus ex eodem loco, et vallem Achor, ad aperiendam spem ; et canet ibi juxta dies juventutis suæ, et juxta dies ascensionis suæ de terra Ægypti.
      EN: From that same place I will give her her vinegrowers and the valley of Achor, to open up hope. There she will sing as in the days of her youth, as in the days when she came up from the land of Egypt.

2:18  LA: Et erit in die illa, ait Dominus : vocabit me, Vir meus, et non vocabit me ultra Baali.
      EN: On that day, says the Lord, she will call Me ‘My Husband,’ and will no longer call Me ‘My Baal.’

2:19  LA: Et auferam nomina Baalim de ore ejus, et non recordabitur ultra nominis eorum.
      EN: I will remove the names of the Baals from her mouth, and she will no longer remember their name.

2:20  LA: Et percutiam cum eis fœdus in die illa, cum bestia agri, et cum volucre cæli, et cum reptili terræ ; et arcum, et gladium, et bellum conteram de terra, et dormire eos faciam fiducialiter.
      EN: On that day I will make a covenant with them, with the wild beast, the bird of the sky, and the creeping creature of the earth. I will break bow and sword and warfare from the land and make them sleep in safety.

2:21  LA: Et sponsabo te mihi in sempiternum ; et sponsabo te mihi in justitia, et judicio, et in misericordia, et in miserationibus.
      EN: I will betroth you to Myself forever; I will betroth you to Myself in righteousness and justice, in mercy and compassion.

2:22  LA: Et sponsabo te mihi in fide ; et scies quia ego Dominus.
      EN: I will betroth you to Myself in faithfulness, and you will know that I am the Lord.

2:23  LA: Et erit in die illa : exaudiam, dicit Dominus, exaudiam cælos, et illi exaudient terram.
      EN: On that day I will answer, says the Lord; I will answer the heavens, and they will answer the earth.

2:24  LA: Et terra exaudiet triticum, et vinum, et oleum, et hæc exaudient Jezrahel.
      EN: The earth will answer the wheat and wine and oil, and these will answer Jezreel.

2:25  LA: Et seminabo eam mihi in terra, et miserebor ejus quæ fuit Absque misericordia.
      EN: I will sow her for Myself in the land, and I will have mercy on her who was Without Mercy.

HOSEA 3

3:1  LA: Et dixit Dominus ad me : Adhuc vade, et dilige mulierem dilectam amico et adulteram, sicut diligit Dominus filios Israël, et ipsi respiciunt ad deos alienos, et diligunt vinacia uvarum.
     EN: The Lord said to me, “Go again and love a woman loved by a companion and committing adultery, just as the Lord loves the children of Israel, though they turn toward foreign gods and love grape skins.”

3:2  LA: Et fodi eam mihi quindecim argenteis, et coro hordei, et dimidio coro hordæi.
     EN: I obtained her for myself for fifteen pieces of silver, a cor of barley, and half a cor of barley.

3:3  LA: Et dixi ad eam : Dies multos exspectabis me ; non fornicaberis, et non eris viro ; sed et ego exspectabo te.
     EN: I said to her, “You will wait for me many days. You will not prostitute yourself or belong to a man; I too will wait for you.”

3:4  LA: Quia dies multos sedebunt filii Israël sine rege, et sine principe, et sine sacrificio, et sine altari, et sine ephod, et sine theraphim.
     EN: For the children of Israel will remain many days without king or prince, without sacrifice or altar, without ephod or teraphim.

3:5  LA: Et post hæc revertentur filii Israël, et quærent Dominum Deum suum, et David regem suum : et pavebunt ad Dominum, et ad bonum ejus, in novissimo dierum.
     EN: Afterward the children of Israel will return and seek the Lord their God and David their king; in the last days they will turn in trembling to the Lord and to His goodness.

HOSEA 4

4:1  LA: Audite verbum Domini, filii Israël, quia judicium Domino cum habitatoribus terræ : non est enim veritas, et non est misericordia, et non est scientia Dei in terra.
     EN: Hear the word of the Lord, children of Israel, for the Lord has a case against the inhabitants of the land. There is no truth, no mercy, and no knowledge of God in the land.

4:2  LA: Maledictum, et mendacium, et homicidium, et furtum, et adulterium inundaverunt, et sanguis sanguinem tetigit.
     EN: Cursing and lying, murder and theft and adultery have flooded it, and blood has touched blood.

4:3  LA: Propter hoc lugebit terra, et infirmabitur omnis qui habitat in ea, in bestia agri, et in volucre cæli ; sed et pisces maris congregabuntur.
     EN: Therefore the land will mourn, and everyone dwelling in it will languish, along with the wild beast and the bird of the sky; even the fish of the sea will be gathered together.

4:4  LA: Verumtamen unusquisque non judicet, et non arguatur vir : populus enim tuus sicut hi qui contradicunt sacerdoti.
     EN: Yet let no one judge, and let no man be reproved, for your people are like those who contradict a priest.

4:5  LA: Et corrues hodie, et corruet etiam propheta tecum. Nocte tacere feci matrem tuam.
     EN: You will fall today, and the prophet too will fall with you. At night I have silenced your mother.

4:6  LA: Conticuit populus meus, eo quod non habuerit scientiam : quia tu scientiam repulisti, repellam te, ne sacerdotio fungaris mihi ; et oblita es legis Dei tui, obliviscar filiorum tuorum et ego.
     EN: My people have fallen silent because they had no knowledge. Because you rejected knowledge, I will reject you from serving Me as priest; because you have forgotten the law of your God, I too will forget your children.

4:7  LA: Secundum multitudinem eorum sic peccaverunt mihi : gloriam eorum in ignominiam commutabo.
     EN: As their numbers grew, so they sinned against Me; I will change their glory into disgrace.

4:8  LA: Peccata populi mei comedent, et ad iniquitatem eorum sublevabunt animas eorum.
     EN: They will eat the sins of My people and raise their souls toward their iniquity.

4:9  LA: Et erit sicut populus, sic sacerdos ; et visitabo super eum vias ejus, et cogitationes ejus reddam ei.
     EN: People and priest will fare alike. I will call him to account for his ways and repay him for his designs.

4:10  LA: Et comedent, et non saturabuntur ; fornicati sunt, et non cessaverunt : quoniam Dominum dereliquerunt in non custodiendo.
      EN: They will eat and not be satisfied; they have prostituted themselves and have not stopped, for they abandoned the Lord by failing to keep watch.

4:11  LA: Fornicatio, et vinum, et ebrietas auferunt cor.
      EN: Prostitution, wine, and drunkenness take away the heart.

4:12  LA: Populus meus in ligno suo interrogavit, et baculus ejus annuntiavit ei ; spiritus enim fornicationum decepit eos, et fornicati sunt a Deo suo.
      EN: My people have consulted their wood, and their staff has given them an answer. For a spirit of prostitution has deceived them, and they have prostituted themselves away from their God.

4:13  LA: Super capita montium sacrificabant, et super colles ascendebant thymiama ; subtus quercum, et populum, et terebinthum, quia bona erat umbra ejus ; ideo fornicabuntur filiæ vestræ, et sponsæ vestræ adulteræ erunt.
      EN: They sacrificed on the mountaintops and sent incense up on the hills, beneath oak and poplar and terebinth, because its shade was good. Therefore your daughters will prostitute themselves, and your brides will commit adultery.

4:14  LA: Non visitabo super filias vestras cum fuerint fornicatæ, et super sponsas vestras cum adulteraverint, quoniam ipsi cum meretricibus conversabantur, et cum effeminatis sacrificabant ; et populus non intelligens vapulabit.
      EN: I will not call your daughters to account when they prostitute themselves, or your brides when they commit adultery, for the men themselves associated with prostitutes and sacrificed with effeminate men; and a people without understanding will be beaten.

4:15  LA: Si fornicaris tu, Israël, non delinquat saltem Juda ; et nolite ingredi in Galgala, et ne ascenderitis in Bethaven, neque juraveritis : Vivit Dominus !
      EN: Though you prostitute yourself, Israel, let Judah at least not offend. Do not enter Gilgal or go up to Beth-aven, and do not swear, ‘As the Lord lives!’

4:16  LA: Quoniam sicut vacca lasciviens declinavit Israël ; nunc pascet eos Dominus, quasi agnum in latitudine.
      EN: For Israel has turned aside like a wanton cow; now the Lord will pasture them like a lamb in an open place.

4:17  LA: Particeps idolorum Ephraim : dimitte eum.
      EN: Ephraim is a partner of idols; leave him alone.

4:18  LA: Separatum est convivium eorum ; fornicatione fornicati sunt : dilexerunt afferre ignominiam protectores ejus.
      EN: Their drinking party has broken apart; they have prostituted themselves utterly. Her protectors have loved to bring disgrace.

4:19  LA: Ligavit eum spiritus in alis suis, et confundentur a sacrificiis suis.]
      EN: The wind has bound him in its wings, and they will be put to shame because of their sacrifices.

HOSEA 5

5:1  LA: Audite hoc, sacerdotes, et attendite, domus Israël, et domus regis, auscultate : quia vobis judicium est, quoniam laqueus facti estis speculationi, et rete expansum super Thabor.
     EN: Hear this, priests; pay attention, house of Israel; give ear, royal house, for judgment concerns you. You have become a snare for the lookout and a net spread over Tabor.

5:2  LA: Et victimas declinastis in profundum ; et ego eruditor omnium eorum.
     EN: You have diverted sacrificial victims into the depths; and I am the instructor of them all.

5:3  LA: Ego scio Ephraim, et Israël non est absconditus a me : quia nunc fornicatus est Ephraim ; contaminatus est Israël.
     EN: I know Ephraim, and Israel is not hidden from Me, for now Ephraim has prostituted himself; Israel is defiled.

5:4  LA: Non dabunt cogitationes suas ut revertantur ad Deum suum, quia spiritus fornicationum in medio eorum, et Dominum non cognoverunt.
     EN: They will not direct their thoughts toward returning to their God, for a spirit of prostitution is among them, and they have not known the Lord.

5:5  LA: Et respondebit arrogantia Israël in facie ejus, et Israël et Ephraim ruent in iniquitate sua : ruet etiam Judas cum eis.
     EN: Israel's arrogance will answer him to his face; Israel and Ephraim will fall in their iniquity, and Judah too will fall with them.

5:6  LA: In gregibus suis et in armentis suis vadent ad quærendum Dominum, et non invenient : ablatus est ab eis.
     EN: With their flocks and herds they will go to seek the Lord, but they will not find Him; He has withdrawn from them.

5:7  LA: In Dominum prævaricati sunt, quia filios alienos genuerunt : nunc devorabit eos mensis, cum partibus suis.
     EN: They have dealt treacherously with the Lord, for they have begotten foreign children. Now a month will devour them together with their allotted portions.

5:8  LA: Clangite buccina in Gabaa, tuba in Rama ; ululate in Bethaven, post tergum tuum, Benjamin.
     EN: Sound the horn in Gibeah, the trumpet in Ramah; howl in Beth-aven! Behind your back, Benjamin!

5:9  LA: Ephraim in desolatione erit in die correptionis ; in tribubus Israël ostendi fidem.
     EN: Ephraim will become a desolation on the day of correction; among the tribes of Israel I have shown faithfulness.

5:10  LA: Facti sunt principes Juda quasi assumentes terminum ; super eos effundam quasi aquam iram meam.
      EN: The princes of Judah have become like those who move a boundary; upon them I will pour out My wrath like water.

5:11  LA: Calumniam patiens est Ephraim, fractus judicio, quoniam cœpit abire post sordes.
      EN: Ephraim suffers oppression, shattered by judgment, because he began to follow filth.

5:12  LA: Et ego quasi tinea Ephraim, et quasi putredo domui Juda.
      EN: I am like a moth to Ephraim and like rot to the house of Judah.

5:13  LA: Et vidit Ephraim languorem suum, et Juda vinculum suum ; et abiit Ephraim ad Assur, et misit ad regem ultorem : et ipse non poterit sanare vos, nec solvere poterit a vobis vinculum.
      EN: Ephraim saw his sickness and Judah his bondage. Ephraim went to Assyria and sent to the avenging king, but he will not be able to heal you or release you from bondage.

5:14  LA: Quoniam ego quasi leæna Ephraim, et quasi catulus leonis domui Juda. Ego, ego capiam, et vadam ; tollam, et non est qui eruat.
      EN: For I am like a lioness to Ephraim and like a lion cub to the house of Judah. I, I will seize and go away; I will carry off, and there is no one to rescue.

5:15  LA: Vadens revertar ad locum meum, donec deficiatis, et quæratis faciem meam.]
      EN: I will go back to My place until you fail and seek My face.

     EN: In their affliction they will rise early to Me: “Come, let us return to the Lord,

     EN: He will bring us to life after two days; on the third day He will raise us, and we will live in His sight. We will know and press on to know the Lord. His coming forth is prepared like the dawn, and He will come to us like rain, the early and late rain upon the earth.”

HOSEA 7

7:1  LA: Cum sanare vellem Israël, revelata est iniquitas Ephraim, et malitia Samariæ, quia operati sunt mendacium ; et fur ingressus est spolians, latrunculus foris.
     EN: When I wished to heal Israel, Ephraim's iniquity and Samaria's wickedness were uncovered, for they practiced deceit. A thief came in to plunder, and a robber lurked outside.

7:2  LA: Et ne forte dicant in cordibus suis, omnem malitiam eorum me recordatum, nunc circumdederunt eos adinventiones suæ : coram facie mea factæ sunt.
     EN: Lest they say in their hearts that I have remembered all their wickedness, now their own contrivances have surrounded them; they have taken place before My face.

7:3  LA: In malitia sua lætificaverunt regem, et in mendaciis suis principes.
     EN: With their wickedness they have delighted the king, and with their lies the princes.

7:4  LA: Omnes adulterantes, quasi clibanus succensus a coquente ; quievit paululum civitas a commistione fermenti, donec fermentaretur totum.
     EN: They are all adulterers, like an oven fired by a baker. The city rested a little from the mixing of the leaven until the whole was leavened.

7:5  LA: Dies regis nostri : cœperunt principes furere a vino ; extendit manum suam cum illusoribus.
     EN: The day of our king: the princes began to rage from wine; he stretched out his hand with scoffers.

7:6  LA: Quia applicuerunt quasi clibanum cor suum, cum insidiaretur eis ; tota nocte dormivit coquens eos : mane ipse succensus quasi ignis flammæ.
     EN: For they brought their heart near like an oven while he lay in ambush for them. All night the one baking them slept; in the morning he himself was kindled like a blazing fire.

7:7  LA: Omnes calefacti sunt quasi clibanus, et devoraverunt judices suos : omnes reges eorum ceciderunt ; non est qui clamat in eis ad me.
     EN: They are all heated like an oven and have devoured their judges. All their kings have fallen; there is no one among them who calls to Me.

7:8  LA: Ephraim in populis ipse commiscebatur ; Ephraim factus est subcinericius panis, qui non reversatur.
     EN: Ephraim himself mixed among the peoples; Ephraim has become a cake baked under ashes that is not turned over.

7:9  LA: Comederunt alieni robur ejus, et ipse nescivit ; sed et cani effusi sunt in eo, et ipse ignoravit.
     EN: Foreigners have eaten away his strength, and he did not know it. Gray hairs have also spread upon him, and he was unaware.

7:10  LA: Et humiliabitur superbia Israël in facie ejus ; nec reversi sunt ad Dominum Deum suum, et non quæsierunt eum in omnibus his.
      EN: Israel's pride will be humbled to his face, yet they have not returned to the Lord their God or sought Him amid all these things.

7:11  LA: Et factus est Ephraim quasi columba seducta non habens cor. Ægyptum invocabant ; ad Assyrios abierunt.
      EN: Ephraim has become like a deceived dove without sense. They called upon Egypt; they went to the Assyrians.

7:12  LA: Et cum profecti fuerint, expandam super eos rete meum : quasi volucrem cæli detraham eos ; cædam eos secundum auditionem cœtus eorum.

14:5  LA: Sanabo contritiones eorum ; diligam eos spontanee : quia aversus est furor meus ab eis.
      EN: I will heal their brokenness; I will love them freely, for My fury has turned away from them.

14:6  LA: Ero quasi ros ; Israël germinabit sicut lilium, et erumpet radix ejus ut Libani.
      EN: I will be like dew; Israel will sprout like a lily, and his root will shoot out like Lebanon's.

14:7  LA: Ibunt rami ejus, et erit quasi oliva gloria ejus, et odor ejus ut Libani.
      EN: His branches will spread; his glory will be like an olive tree, and his fragrance like Lebanon's.

14:8  LA: Convertentur sedentes in umbra ejus ; vivent tritico, et germinabunt quasi vinea ; memoriale ejus sicut vinum Libani.
      EN: Those dwelling in his shade will return; they will live on wheat and sprout like a vine. His remembrance will be like the wine of Lebanon.

14:9  LA: Ephraim, quid mihi ultra idola ? Ego exaudiam, et dirigam eum ego ut abietem virentem ; ex me fructus tuus inventus est.
      EN: Ephraim, what have I any longer to do with idols? I will answer him and guide him like a green fir tree; from Me your fruit is found.

14:10  LA: Quis sapiens, et intelliget ista ? intelligens, et sciet hæc ? quia rectæ viæ Domini, et justi ambulabunt in eis ; prævaricatores vero corruent in eis.]
       EN: Who is wise and will understand these things, discerning and will know them? For the Lord's ways are straight, and the righteous will walk in them, but transgressors will fall in them.
