ZEPHANIAH 1

1:1  LA: Verbum Domini quod factum est ad Sophoniam, filium Chusi, filii Godoliæ, filii Amariæ, filii Ezeciæ, in diebus Josiæ, filii Amon, regis Judæ.
     EN: The word of the Lord that came to Zephaniah son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah.

1:2  LA: [Congregans congregabo omnia a facie terræ, dicit Dominus :
     EN: I will surely gather everything away from the face of the earth, says the Lord:

1:3  LA: congregans hominem et pecus, congregans volatilia cæli et pisces maris : et ruinæ impiorum erunt, et disperdam homines a facie terræ, dicit Dominus.
     EN: gathering away human beings and livestock, gathering away the birds of heaven and the fish of the sea. The wicked will fall, and I will destroy human beings from the face of the earth, says the Lord.

1:4  LA: Et extendam manum meam super Judam et super omnes habitantes Jerusalem : et disperdam de loco hoc reliquias Baal, et nomina ædituorum cum sacerdotibus ;
     EN: I will stretch out My hand against Judah and against all who inhabit Jerusalem. I will destroy from this place the remnants of Baal and the names of the temple attendants together with the priests,

1:5  LA: et eos qui adorant super tecta militiam cæli, et adorant et jurant in Domino, et jurant in Melchom ;
     EN: and those who worship the host of heaven on the rooftops, and those who worship and swear by the Lord while also swearing by Milcom,

1:6  LA: et qui avertuntur de post tergum Domini, et qui non quæsierunt Dominum, nec investigaverunt eum.
     EN: and those who turn away from following the Lord, and those who have not sought the Lord or inquired after Him.

1:7  LA: Silete a facie Domini Dei, quia juxta est dies Domini : quia præparavit Dominus hostiam ; sanctificavit vocatos suos.
     EN: Be silent before the Lord God, for the day of the Lord is near. The Lord has prepared a sacrifice; He has consecrated His invited guests.

1:8  LA: Et erit : in die hostiæ Domini, visitabo super principes, et super filios regis, et super omnes qui induti sunt veste peregrina ;
     EN: On the day of the Lord's sacrifice, I will call to account the princes, the king's sons, and all who are clothed in foreign dress.

1:9  LA: et visitabo super omnem qui arroganter ingreditur super limen in die illa, qui complent domum Domini Dei sui iniquitate et dolo.
     EN: On that day I will call to account everyone who steps arrogantly over the threshold, those who fill the house of the Lord their God with iniquity and deceit.

1:10  LA: Et erit in die illa, dicit Dominus, vox clamoris a porta piscium, et ululatus a Secunda, et contritio magna a collibus.
      EN: On that day, says the Lord, there will be the sound of an outcry from the Fish Gate, a wailing from the Second Quarter, and a great crashing from the hills.

1:11  LA: Ululate, habitatores Pilæ : conticuit omnis populus Chanaan, disperierunt omnes involuti argento.
      EN: Wail, inhabitants of the Mortar! All the people of Canaan have fallen silent; all who were wrapped in silver have perished.

1:12  LA: Et erit in tempore illo : scrutabor Jerusalem in lucernis, et visitabo super viros defixos in fæcibus suis, qui dicunt in cordibus suis : Non faciet bene Dominus, et non faciet male.
      EN: At that time I will search Jerusalem with lamps and call to account the men settled on their dregs, who say in their hearts, “The Lord will do neither good nor harm.”

1:13  LA: Et erit fortitudo eorum in direptionem, et domus eorum in desertum : et ædificabunt domos, et non habitabunt ; et plantabunt vineas, et non bibent vinum earum.
      EN: Their resources will be plundered and their houses a wasteland. They will build houses but not inhabit them; they will plant vineyards but not drink their wine.

1:14  LA: Juxta est dies Domini magnus : juxta est, et velox nimis. Vox diei Domini amara : tribulabitur ibi fortis.
      EN: The great day of the Lord is near—near and coming very swiftly. The sound of the day of the Lord is bitter; there the mighty man will be distressed.

1:15  LA: Dies iræ dies illa, dies tribulationis et angustiæ, dies calamitatis et miseriæ, dies tenebrarum et caliginis, dies nebulæ et turbinis,
      EN: That day is a day of wrath, a day of distress and anguish, a day of calamity and misery, a day of darkness and gloom, a day of cloud and whirlwind,

1:16  LA: dies tubæ et clangoris super civitates munitas, et super angulos excelsos.
      EN: a day of trumpet and battle cry against fortified cities and lofty towers.

1:17  LA: Et tribulabo homines, et ambulabunt ut cæci, quia Domino peccaverunt ; et effundetur sanguis eorum sicut humus, et corpora eorum sicut stercora.
      EN: I will distress human beings, and they will walk like the blind, because they have sinned against the Lord. Their blood will be poured out like dust and their bodies like dung.

1:18  LA: Sed et argentum eorum et aurum eorum non poterit liberare eos in die iræ Domini : in igne zeli ejus devorabitur omnis terra, quia consummationem cum festinatione faciet cunctis habitantibus terram.]
      EN: Neither their silver nor their gold will be able to deliver them on the day of the Lord's wrath. In the fire of His jealousy the whole earth will be devoured, for He will swiftly make an end of all who inhabit the earth.

ZEPHANIAH 2

2:1  LA: Convenite , congregamini, gens non amabilis,
     EN: Come together, gather yourselves, unlovable nation,

2:2  LA: priusquam pariat jussio quasi pulverem transeuntem diem, antequam veniat super vos ira furoris Domini, antequam veniat super vos dies indignationis Domini.
     EN: before the decree brings forth a day passing like dust, before the Lord's furious anger comes upon you, before the day of the Lord's indignation comes upon you.

2:3  LA: Quærite Dominum, omnes mansueti terræ, qui judicium ejus estis operati ; quærite justum, quærite mansuetum, si quomodo abscondamini in die furoris Domini.
     EN: Seek the Lord, all you meek of the earth who have practiced His judgment. Seek what is just; seek what is meek, that perhaps you may be hidden on the day of the Lord's fury.

2:4  LA: Quia Gaza destructa erit, et Ascalon in desertum : Azotum in meridie ejicient, et Accaron eradicabitur.
     EN: For Gaza will be destroyed and Ashkelon become a wasteland. They will drive out Ashdod at noon, and Ekron will be uprooted.

2:5  LA: Væ qui habitatis funiculum maris, gens perditorum ! verbum Domini super vos, Chanaan, terra Philisthinorum ; et disperdam te, ita ut non sit inhabitator.
     EN: Woe to you who inhabit the seacoast, a nation of the lost! The word of the Lord is against you, Canaan, land of the Philistines: I will destroy you until no inhabitant remains.

2:6  LA: Et erit funiculus maris requies pastorum, et caulæ pecorum ;
     EN: The seacoast will become a resting place for shepherds and folds for flocks.

2:7  LA: et erit funiculus ejus qui remanserit de domo Juda : ibi pascentur, in domibus Ascalonis ad vesperam requiescent, quia visitabit eos Dominus Deus eorum, et avertet captivitatem eorum.
     EN: The coast will belong to those who remain of the house of Judah. They will pasture there and rest in the houses of Ashkelon at evening, for the Lord their God will visit them and reverse their captivity.

2:8  LA: Audivi opprobrium Moab, et blasphemias filiorum Ammon, quæ exprobraverunt populo meo, et magnificati sunt super terminos eorum.
     EN: I have heard Moab's taunts and the insults of the children of Ammon, with which they reproached My people and vaunted themselves against their borders.

2:9  LA: Propterea vivo ego, dicit Dominus exercituum, Deus Israël, quia Moab ut Sodoma erit, et filii Ammon quasi Gomorrha : siccitas spinarum, et acervi salis, et desertum usque in æternum : reliquiæ populi mei diripient eos, et residui gentis meæ possidebunt illos.
     EN: Therefore, as I live, says the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, Moab will become like Sodom and the children of Ammon like Gomorrah: a dry growth of thorns, heaps of salt, and a wasteland forever. The remnant of My people will plunder them, and the survivors of My nation will possess them.

2:10  LA: Hoc eis eveniet pro superbia sua, quia blasphemaverunt et magnificati sunt super populum Domini exercituum.
      EN: This will happen to them for their pride, because they insulted the people of the Lord of hosts and vaunted themselves against them.

2:11  LA: Horribilis Dominus super eos, et attenuabit omnes deos terræ : et adorabunt eum viri de loco suo, omnes insulæ gentium.
      EN: The Lord will be terrifying to them, and He will reduce all the gods of the earth to nothing. People will worship Him, each from their own place—all the islands of the nations.

2:12  LA: Sed et vos, Æthiopes, interfecti gladio meo eritis.
      EN: You too, Ethiopians, will be slain by My sword.

2:13  LA: Et extendet manum suam super aquilonem, et perdet Assur, et ponet speciosam in solitudinem, et in invium, et quasi desertum.
      EN: He will stretch out His hand against the north and destroy Assyria. He will make the beautiful city a solitude, a trackless place, like a desert.

2:14  LA: Et accubabunt in medio ejus greges, omnes bestiæ gentium ; et onocrotalus et ericius in liminibus ejus morabuntur : vox cantantis in fenestra, corvus in superliminari, quoniam attenuabo robur ejus.
      EN: Flocks will lie down within her, all the beasts of the nations. Pelican and hedgehog will dwell upon her thresholds; a voice will sing at the window, and a raven will be on the lintel, for I will strip away her strength.

2:15  LA: Hæc est civitas gloriosa habitans in confidentia, quæ dicebat in corde suo : Ego sum, et extra me non est alia amplius : quomodo facta est in desertum cubile bestiæ ? omnis qui transit per eam sibilabit, et movebit manum suam.]
      EN: This is the glorious city that dwelt securely, that said in her heart, “I am, and there is no other besides me.” How has she become a wilderness, a lair for beasts? Everyone who passes by her will hiss and wave his hand.

ZEPHANIAH 3

3:1  LA: Væ provocatrix, et redempta civitas, columba !
     EN: Woe, rebellious and redeemed city, you dove!

3:2  LA: non audivit vocem, et non suscepit disciplinam ; in Domino non est confisa, ad Deum suum non appropinquavit.
     EN: She has not listened to the voice or accepted instruction. She has not trusted in the Lord or drawn near to her God.

3:3  LA: Principes ejus in medio ejus quasi leones rugientes ; judices ejus lupi vespere, non relinquebant in mane.
     EN: Her princes within her are like roaring lions; her judges are evening wolves that left nothing for the morning.

3:4  LA: Prophetæ ejus vesani, viri infideles ; sacerdotes ejus polluerunt sanctum, injuste egerunt contra legem.
     EN: Her prophets are mad, faithless men. Her priests have defiled the holy place and acted unjustly against the law.

3:5  LA: Dominus justus in medio ejus non faciet iniquitatem ; mane, mane judicium suum dabit in lucem, et non abscondetur ; nescivit autem iniquus confusionem.
     EN: The just Lord in her midst will do no iniquity. Morning after morning He will bring His judgment to light, and it will not be hidden; but the wicked person has known no shame.

3:6  LA: Disperdidi gentes, et dissipati sunt anguli earum ; desertas feci vias eorum, dum non est qui transeat ; desolatæ sunt civitates eorum, non remanente viro, neque ullo habitatore.
     EN: I have destroyed nations, and their towers have been demolished. I have made their roads deserted, with no one passing through. Their cities have been laid waste, with no man remaining and no inhabitant at all.

3:7  LA: Dixi : Attamen timebis me, suscipies disciplinam ; et non peribit habitaculum ejus, propter omnia in quibus visitavi eam : verumtamen diluculo surgentes corruperunt omnes cogitationes suas.
     EN: I said, “Surely you will fear Me and accept instruction; then her dwelling will not perish because of all the things for which I called her to account.” Yet they rose early and corrupted all their designs.

3:8  LA: Quapropter exspecta me, dicit Dominus, in die resurrectionis meæ in futurum : quia judicium meum ut congregem gentes, et colligam regna, et effundam super eos indignationem meam, omnem iram furoris mei : in igne enim zeli mei devorabitur omnis terra.]
     EN: Therefore wait for Me, says the Lord, for the coming day when I rise. For My judgment is to gather nations and assemble kingdoms, and to pour out upon them My indignation, all My furious anger. For in the fire of My jealousy the whole earth will be devoured.

3:9  LA: [Quia tunc reddam populis labium electum, ut invocent omnes in nomine Domini, et serviant ei humero uno.
     EN: For then I will restore to the peoples a chosen speech, so that they may all call upon the name of the Lord and serve Him shoulder to shoulder.

3:10  LA: Ultra flumina Æthiopiæ, inde supplices mei ; filii dispersorum meorum deferent munus mihi.
      EN: From beyond the rivers of Ethiopia will come My suppliants; the children of My scattered people will bring Me an offering.

3:11  LA: In die illa non confunderis super cunctis adinventionibus tuis, quibus prævaricata es in me, quia tunc auferam de medio tui magniloquos superbiæ tuæ, et non adjicies exaltari amplius in monte sancto meo.
      EN: On that day you will not be ashamed of all your deeds by which you betrayed Me, for then I will remove from your midst those who boast in your pride, and you will no longer exalt yourself upon My holy mountain.

3:12  LA: Et derelinquam in medio tui populum pauperem et egenum : et sperabunt in nomine Domini.
      EN: I will leave in your midst a poor and needy people, and they will hope in the name of the Lord.

3:13  LA: Reliquiæ Israël non facient iniquitatem, nec loquentur mendacium, et non invenietur in ore eorum lingua dolosa, quoniam ipsi pascentur, et accubabunt, et non erit qui exterreat.
      EN: The remnant of Israel will do no iniquity or speak lies, and no deceitful tongue will be found in their mouth. They will feed and lie down, with no one to frighten them.

3:14  LA: Lauda, filia Sion ; jubila, Israël : lætare, et exsulta in omni corde, filia Jerusalem.
      EN: Sing praise, daughter Zion! Shout for joy, Israel! Be glad and exult with all your heart, daughter Jerusalem!

3:15  LA: Abstulit Dominus judicium tuum ; avertit inimicos tuos. Rex Israël Dominus in medio tui : non timebis malum ultra.
      EN: The Lord has removed the judgment against you; He has turned away your enemies. The King of Israel, the Lord, is in your midst; you will fear evil no longer.

3:16  LA: In die illa dicetur Jerusalem : Noli timere ; Sion : Non dissolvantur manus tuæ.
      EN: On that day it will be said to Jerusalem, “Do not fear,” and to Zion, “Do not let your hands grow weak.”

3:17  LA: Dominus Deus tuus in medio tui fortis, ipse salvabit : gaudebit super te in lætitia, silebit in dilectione sua, exsultabit super te in laude.
      EN: The Lord your God is mighty in your midst; He Himself will save. He will rejoice over you with gladness; He will be silent in His love; He will exult over you with praise.

3:18  LA: Nugas, qui a lege recesserant, congregabo, quia ex te erant : ut non ultra habeas super eis opprobrium.
      EN: I will gather the worthless ones who had departed from the law, because they belonged to you, so that you may no longer bear reproach on their account.

3:19  LA: Ecce ego interficiam omnes qui afflixerunt te in tempore illo : et salvabo claudicantem, et eam quæ ejecta fuerat congregabo : et ponam eos in laudem, et in nomen, in omni terra confusionis eorum,
      EN: Look, at that time I will slay all who afflicted you. I will save the lame and gather her who was cast out. I will make them an object of praise and renown in every land of their shame,

3:20  LA: in tempore illo quo adducam vos, et in tempore quo congregabo vos. Dabo enim vos in nomen, et in laudem omnibus populis terræ, cum convertero captivitatem vestram coram oculis vestris, dicit Dominus.]
      EN: at that time when I bring you back, at the time when I gather you. For I will give you renown and praise among all the peoples of the earth, when I reverse your captivity before your eyes, says the Lord.
