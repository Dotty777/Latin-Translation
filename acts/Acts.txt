ACTS — LATIN–ENGLISH (CLEMENTINE VULGATE)

ACTS 1

1:1  LA: Primum quidem sermonem feci de omnibus, o Theophile, quæ cœpit Jesus facere et docere
     EN: In my first account, Theophilus, I wrote about all that Jesus began to do and teach

1:2  LA: usque in diem qua præcipiens Apostolis per Spiritum Sanctum, quos elegit, assumptus est:
     EN: until the day he was taken up, after giving instructions through the Holy Spirit to the apostles he had chosen.

1:3  LA: quibus et præbuit seipsum vivum post passionem suam in multis argumentis, per dies quadraginta apparens eis, et loquens de regno Dei.
     EN: To them he also presented himself alive after his suffering, with many proofs, appearing to them over forty days and speaking about the kingdom of God.

1:4  LA: Et convescens, præcepit eis ab Jerosolymis ne discederent, sed exspectarent promissionem Patris, quam audistis (inquit) per os meum:
     EN: While eating with them, he instructed them not to leave Jerusalem, but to await the Father’s promise, “which,” he said, “you heard from my mouth.

1:5  LA: quia Joannes quidem baptizavit aqua, vos autem baptizabimini Spiritu Sancto non post multos hos dies.
     EN: For John baptized with water, but you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit not many days from now.”

1:6  LA: Igitur qui convenerant, interrogabant eum, dicentes: Domine, si in tempore hoc restitues regnum Israël?
     EN: So those who had gathered asked him, “Lord, will you restore the kingdom to Israel at this time?”

1:7  LA: Dixit autem eis: Non est vestrum nosse tempora vel momenta quæ Pater posuit in sua potestate:
     EN: He said to them, “It is not for you to know the times or seasons that the Father has set by his own authority.

1:8  LA: sed accipietis virtutem supervenientis Spiritus Sancti in vos, et eritis mihi testes in Jerusalem, et in omni Judæa, et Samaria, et usque ad ultimum terræ.
     EN: But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes upon you, and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, in all Judea and Samaria, and to the end of the earth.”

1:9  LA: Et cum hæc dixisset, videntibus illis, elevatus est: et nubes suscepit eum ab oculis eorum.
     EN: When he had said these things, he was lifted up as they watched, and a cloud received him out of their sight.

1:10  LA: Cumque intuerentur in cælum euntem illum, ecce duo viri astiterunt juxta illos in vestibus albis,
      EN: While they were looking intently into heaven as he went, behold, two men stood beside them in white clothing.

1:11  LA: qui et dixerunt: Viri Galilæi, quid statis aspicientes in cælum? Hic Jesus, qui assumptus est a vobis in cælum, sic veniet quemadmodum vidistis eum euntem in cælum.
      EN: They said, “Men of Galilee, why do you stand looking into heaven? This Jesus, who was taken up from you into heaven, will come in the same way as you saw him go into heaven.”

1:12  LA: Tunc reversi sunt Jerosolymam a monte qui vocatur Oliveti, qui est juxta Jerusalem, sabbati habens iter.
      EN: Then they returned to Jerusalem from the mount that is called Olivet, which is near Jerusalem, within a sabbath day’s journey.

1:13  LA: Et cum introissent in cœnaculum, ascenderunt ubi manebant Petrus, et Joannes, Jacobus, et Andreas, Philippus, et Thomas, Bartholomæus, et Matthæus, Jacobus Alphæi, et Simon Zelotes, et Judas Jacobi.
      EN: And when they had come in, they went up into an upper room, where abode Peter and John, James and Andrew, Philip and Thomas, Bartholomew and Matthew, James of Alphaeus and Simon Zelotes and Jude the brother of James.

1:14  LA: Hi omnes erant perseverantes unanimiter in oratione cum mulieribus, et Maria matre Jesu, et fratribus ejus.
      EN: All these were persevering with one mind in prayer with the women, and Mary the mother of Jesus, and with his brothers.

1:15  LA: In diebus illis, exsurgens Petrus in medio fratrum, dixit (erat autem turba hominum simul, fere centum viginti):
      EN: In those days Peter rising up in the middle of the brothers, said (now the number of persons together was about a hundred and twenty):

1:16  LA: Viri fratres, oportet impleri Scripturam quam prædixit Spiritus Sanctus per os David de Juda, qui fuit dux eorum qui comprehenderunt Jesum:
      EN: Men, brothers, the scripture must needs be fulfilled, which the Holy Spirit spoke before by the mouth of David concerning Judas, who was the leader of them that apprehended Jesus:

1:17  LA: qui connumeratus erat in nobis, et sortitus est sortem ministerii hujus.
      EN: Who was numbered with us, and had obtained part of this ministry.

1:18  LA: Et hic quidem possedit agrum de mercede iniquitatis, et suspensus crepuit medius: et diffusa sunt omnia viscera ejus.
      EN: And he indeed has possessed a field of the reward of iniquity, and being hung, burst asunder in the midst: and all his heart gushed out.

1:19  LA: Et notum factum est omnibus habitantibus Jerusalem, ita ut appellaretur ager ille, lingua eorum, Haceldama, hoc est, ager sanguinis.
      EN: And it became known to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem: so that the same field was called in their tongue, Haceldama, that is to say, The field of blood.

1:20  LA: Scriptum est enim in libro Psalmorum: Fiat commoratio eorum deserta, et non sit qui inhabitet in ea: et episcopatum ejus accipiat alter.
      EN: For it is written in the book of Psalms: Let their habitation become desolate, and let there be none to dwell in it. And his bishopric let another take.

1:21  LA: Oportet ergo ex his viris qui nobiscum sunt congregati in omni tempore quo intravit et exivit inter nos Dominus Jesus,
      EN: Therefore of these men who have companied with us, all the time that the Lord Jesus came in and went out among us,

1:22  LA: incipiens a baptismate Joannis usque in diem qua assumptus est a nobis, testem resurrectionis ejus nobiscum fieri unum ex istis.
      EN: Beginning from the baptism of John, until the day wherein he was taken up from us, one of these must be made a witness with us of his resurrection.

1:23  LA: Et statuerunt duos, Joseph, qui vocabatur Barsabas, qui cognominatus est Justus, et Mathiam.
      EN: And they appointed two, Joseph, called Barsabas, who was surnamed Justus, and Matthias.

1:24  LA: Et orantes dixerunt: Tu Domine, qui corda nosti omnium, ostende quem elegeris ex his duobus unum,
      EN: And praying, they said: You, Lord, who know the heart of all men, show whether of these two you have chosen,

1:25  LA: accipere locum ministerii hujus et apostolatus, de quo prævaricatus est Judas ut abiret in locum suum.
      EN: To take the place of this ministry and apostleship, from which Judas has by transgression fallen, that he might go to his own place.

1:26  LA: Et dederunt sortes eis, et cecidit sors super Mathiam: et annumeratus est cum undecim Apostolis.
      EN: And they gave them lots, and the lot fell upon Matthias, and he was numbered with the eleven apostles.

ACTS 2

2:1  LA: Et cum complerentur dies Pentecostes, erant omnes pariter in eodem loco:
     EN: When the days of Pentecost were being fulfilled, they were all together in the same place.

2:2  LA: et factus est repente de cælo sonus, tamquam advenientis spiritus vehementis, et replevit totam domum ubi erant sedentes.
     EN: Suddenly a sound came from heaven like a mighty wind rushing in, and it filled the whole house where they were sitting.

2:3  LA: Et apparuerunt illis dispertitæ linguæ tamquam ignis, seditque supra singulos eorum:
     EN: Divided tongues, as of fire, appeared to them, and a tongue rested on each of them.

2:4  LA: et repleti sunt omnes Spiritu Sancto, et cœperunt loqui variis linguis, prout Spiritus Sanctus dabat eloqui illis.
     EN: They were all filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in various tongues, as the Holy Spirit gave them utterance.

2:5  LA: Erant autem in Jerusalem habitantes Judæi, viri religiosi ex omni natione quæ sub cælo est.
     EN: Now there were Jews living in Jerusalem, devout men from every nation under heaven.

2:6  LA: Facta autem hac voce, convenit multitudo, et mente confusa est, quoniam audiebat unusquisque lingua sua illos loquentes.
     EN: When this sound occurred, a crowd gathered and was bewildered, because each person heard them speaking in his own language.

2:7  LA: Stupebant autem omnes, et mirabantur, dicentes: Nonne ecce omnes isti qui loquuntur, Galilæi sunt?
     EN: They were all astonished and wondered, saying, “Look, are not all these who are speaking Galileans?

2:8  LA: et quomodo nos audivimus unusquisque linguam nostram in qua nati sumus?
     EN: How is it, then, that each of us has heard our own language, in which we were born?”

2:9  LA: Parthi, et Medi, et Ælamitæ, et qui habitant Mespotamiam, Judæam, et Cappadociam, Pontum, et Asiam,
     EN: Parthians and Medes and Elamites and inhabitants of Mesopotamia, Judea, and Cappadocia, Pontus and Asia,

2:10  LA: Phrygiam, et Pamphyliam, Ægyptum, et partes Libyæ quæ est circa Cyrenen: et advenæ Romani,
      EN: Phrygia and Pamphylia, Egypt and the parts of Libya about Cyrene, and strangers of Rome,

2:11  LA: Judæi quoque, et Proselyti, Cretes, et Arabes: audivimus eos loquentes nostris linguis magnalia Dei.
      EN: Jews also, and proselytes, Cretes, and Arabians: we have heard them speak in our own tongues the wonderful works of God.

2:12  LA: Stupebant autem omnes, et mirabantur ad invicem, dicentes: Quidnam vult hoc esse?
      EN: And they were all astonished, and wondered, saying one to another: What means this?

2:13  LA: Alii autem irridentes dicebant: Quia musto pleni sunt isti.
      EN: But others mocking, said: These men are full of new wine.

2:14  LA: Stans autem Petrus cum undecim, levavit vocem suam, et locutus est eis: Viri Judæi, et qui habitatis Jerusalem universi, hoc vobis notum sit, et auribus percipite verba mea.
      EN: But Peter standing up with the eleven, lifted up his voice, and spoke to them: You men of Judea, and all you that dwell in Jerusalem, be this known to you and with your ears receive my words.

2:15  LA: Non enim, sicut vos æstimatis, hi ebrii sunt, cum sit hora diei tertia:
      EN: For these are not drunk, as you suppose, seeing it is but the third hour of the day:

2:16  LA: sed hoc est quod dictum est per prophetam Joël:
      EN: But this is that which was spoken of by the prophet Joel:

2:17  LA: Et erit in novissimis diebus, dicit Dominus, effundam de Spiritu meo super omnem carnem: et prophetabunt filii vestri et filiæ vestræ, et juvenes vestri visiones videbunt, et seniores vestri somnia somniabunt.
      EN: And it will happen, in the last days, (says the Lord), I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh: and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy: and your young men shall see visions, and your old men shall dream dreams.

2:18  LA: Et quidem super servos meos, et super ancillas meas, in diebus illis effundam de Spiritu meo, et prophetabunt:
      EN: And upon my servants indeed and upon my handmaids will I pour out in those days of my spirit: and they shall prophesy.

2:19  LA: et dabo prodigia in cælo sursum, et signa in terra deorsum, sanguinem, et ignem, et vaporem fumi:
      EN: And I will show wonders in the heaven above, and signs on the earth beneath: blood and fire, and vapour of smoke.

2:20  LA: sol convertetur in tenebras, et luna in sanguinem, antequam veniat dies Domini magnus et manifestus.
      EN: The sun shall be turned into darkness and the moon into blood, before the great and manifest day of the Lord shall come.

2:21  LA: Et erit: omnis quicumque invocaverit nomen Domini, salvus erit.
      EN: And it will happen, that whoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.

2:22  LA: Viri Israëlitæ, audite verba hæc: Jesum Nazarenum, virum approbatum a Deo in vobis, virtutibus, et prodigiis, et signis, quæ fecit Deus per illum in medio vestri, sicut et vos scitis:
      EN: You men of Israel, hear these words: Jesus of Nazareth, a man approved of God among you by miracles and wonders and signs, which God did by him, in the middle of you, as you also know:

2:23  LA: hunc, definito consilio et præscientia Dei traditum, per manus iniquorum affligentes interemistis:
      EN: This same being delivered up, by the determinate counsel and foreknowledge of God, you by the hands of wicked men have crucified and slain.

2:24  LA: quem Deus suscitavit, solutis doloribus inferni, juxta quod impossibile erat teneri illum ab eo.
      EN: Whom God has raised up, having loosed the sorrows of the underworld, as it was impossible that he should be holden by it.

2:25  LA: David enim dicit in eum: Providebam Dominum in conspectu meo semper: quoniam a dextris est mihi, ne commovear:
      EN: For David says concerning him: I foresaw the Lord before my face: because he is at my right hand, that I may not be moved.

2:26  LA: propter hoc lætatum est cor meum, et exsultavit lingua mea, insuper et caro mea requiescet in spe:
      EN: For this my heart has been glad, and my tongue has rejoiced: moreover my flesh also shall rest in hope.

2:27  LA: quoniam non derelinques animam meam in inferno, nec dabis sanctum tuum videre corruptionem.
      EN: Because you will not leave my soul in the underworld: nor suffer your Holy One to see corruption.

2:28  LA: Notas mihi fecisti vias vitæ: et replebis me jucunditate cum facie tua.
      EN: You have made known to me the ways of life: you shall make me full of joy with your countenance.

2:29  LA: Viri fratres, liceat audenter dicere ad vos de patriarcha David, quoniam defunctus est, et sepultus: et sepulchrum ejus est apud nos usque in hodiernum diem.
      EN: You men, brothers, let me freely speak to you of the patriarch David: that he died and was buried; and his tomb is with us to this present day.

2:30  LA: Propheta igitur cum esset, et sciret quia jurejurando jurasset illi Deus de fructu lumbi ejus sedere super sedem ejus:
      EN: Whereas therefore he was a prophet and knew that God has sworn to him with an oath, that of the fruit of his loins one should sit upon his throne.

2:31  LA: providens locutus est de resurrectione Christi, quia neque derelictus est in inferno, neque caro ejus vidit corruptionem.
      EN: Foreseeing this, he spoke of the resurrection of Christ. For neither was he left in the underworld: neither did his flesh see corruption.

2:32  LA: Hunc Jesum resuscitavit Deus, cujus omnes nos testes sumus.
      EN: This Jesus has God raised again, whereof all we are witnesses.

2:33  LA: Dextera igitur Dei exaltatus, et promissione Spiritus Sancti accepta a Patre, effudit hunc, quem vos videtis et auditis.
      EN: Being exalted therefore by the right hand of God and having received of the Father the promise of the Holy Spirit, he has poured forth this which you see and hear.

2:34  LA: Non enim David ascendit in cælum: dixit autem ipse: Dixit Dominus Domino meo: Sede a dextris meis,
      EN: For David ascended not into heaven; but he himself said: The Lord said to my Lord: Sit you on my right hand,

2:35  LA: donec ponam inimicos tuos scabellum pedum tuorum.
      EN: Until I make your enemies your footstool.

2:36  LA: Certissime sciat ergo omnis domus Israël, quia et Dominum eum et Christum fecit Deus hunc Jesum, quem vos crucifixistis.
      EN: “Therefore let the whole house of Israel know with certainty that God has made this Jesus whom you crucified both Lord and Christ.”

2:37  LA: His autem auditis, compuncti sunt corde, et dixerunt ad Petrum et ad reliquos Apostolos: Quid faciemus, viri fratres?
      EN: When they heard this, they were pierced to the heart and said to Peter and the other apostles, “Brothers, what shall we do?”

2:38  LA: Petrus vero ad illos: Pœnitentiam, inquit, agite, et baptizetur unusquisque vestrum in nomine Jesu Christi in remissionem peccatorum vestrorum: et accipietis donum Spiritus Sancti.
      EN: Peter said to them, “Repent, and let each of you be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.

2:39  LA: Vobis enim est repromissio, et filiis vestris, et omnibus qui longe sunt, quoscumque advocaverit Dominus Deus noster.
      EN: For the promise is for you, for your children, and for all who are far away—as many as the Lord our God calls.”

2:40  LA: Aliis etiam verbis plurimis testificatus est, et exhortabatur eos, dicens: Salvamini a generatione ista prava.
      EN: With many other words he bore witness and urged them, saying, “Be saved from this crooked generation.”

2:41  LA: Qui ergo receperunt sermonem ejus, baptizati sunt: et appositæ sunt in die illa animæ circiter tria millia.
      EN: Those who received his word were baptized, and about three thousand souls were added that day.

2:42  LA: Erant autem perseverantes in doctrina Apostolorum, et communicatione fractionis panis, et orationibus.
      EN: They persevered in the apostles’ teaching, in the fellowship of the breaking of bread, and in prayers.

2:43  LA: Fiebat autem omni animæ timor: multa quoque prodigia et signa per Apostolos in Jerusalem fiebant, et metus erat magnus in universis.
      EN: Fear came upon every soul. Many wonders and signs were also done through the apostles in Jerusalem, and great fear was among them all.

2:44  LA: Omnes etiam qui credebant, erant pariter, et habebant omnia communia.
      EN: All who believed were together and held everything in common.

2:45  LA: Possessiones et substantias vendebant, et dividebant illa omnibus, prout cuique opus erat.
      EN: They sold their possessions and goods and distributed them to everyone, according to each person’s need.

2:46  LA: Quotidie quoque perdurantes unanimiter in templo, et frangentes circa domos panem, sumebant cibum cum exsultatione et simplicitate cordis,
      EN: Each day they continued with one accord in the temple and broke bread in their homes, sharing food with gladness and simplicity of heart,

2:47  LA: collaudantes Deum et habentes gratiam ad omnem plebem. Dominus autem augebat qui salvi fierent quotidie in idipsum.
      EN: praising God and enjoying favor with all the people. And the Lord added daily to their number those who were being saved.

ACTS 3

3:1  LA: Petrus autem et Joannes ascendebant in templum ad horam orationis nonam.
     EN: Now Peter and John went up into the temple at the ninth hour of prayer.

3:2  LA: Et quidam vir, qui erat claudus ex utero matris suæ, bajulabatur: quem ponebant quotidie ad portam templi, quæ dicitur Speciosa, ut peteret eleemosynam ab introëuntibus in templum.
     EN: And a certain man who was lame from his mother’s womb was carried: whom they laid every day at the gate of the temple, which is called Beautiful, that he might ask alms of them that went into the temple.

3:3  LA: Is cum vidisset Petrum et Joannem incipientes introire in templum, rogabat ut eleemosynam acciperet.
     EN: He, when he had seen Peter and John, about to go into the temple, asked to receive an alms.

3:4  LA: Intuens autem in eum Petrus cum Joanne, dixit: Respice in nos.
     EN: But Peter with John, fastening his eyes upon him, said: Look upon us.

3:5  LA: At ille intendebat in eos, sperans se aliquid accepturum ab eis.
     EN: But he looked earnestly upon them, hoping that he should receive something of them.

3:6  LA: Petrus autem dixit: Argentum et aurum non est mihi: quod autem habeo, hoc tibi do: in nomine Jesu Christi Nazareni surge, et ambula.
     EN: But Peter said: Silver and gold I have none; but what I have, I give you. In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, arise and walk.

3:7  LA: Et apprehensa manu ejus dextera, allevavit eum, et protinus consolidatæ sunt bases ejus et plantæ.
     EN: And taking him by the right hand, he lifted him up: and immediately his feet and soles received strength.

3:8  LA: Et exiliens stetit, et ambulabat: et intravit cum illis in templum ambulans, et exiliens, et laudans Deum.
     EN: And he leaping up, stood and walked and went in with them into the temple, walking and leaping and praising God.

3:9  LA: Et vidit omnis populus eum ambulantem et laudantem Deum.
     EN: And all the people saw him walking and praising God.

3:10  LA: Cognoscebant autem illum, quod ipse erat qui ad eleemosynam sedebat ad Speciosam portam templi: et impleti sunt stupore et extasi in eo quod contigerat illi.
      EN: And they knew him, that it was he who sat begging alms at the Beautiful gate of the temple: and they were filled with wonder and amazement at that which had happened to him.

3:11  LA: Cum teneret autem Petrum et Joannem, cucurrit omnis populus ad eos ad porticum quæ appellatur Salomonis, stupentes.
      EN: And as he held Peter and John, all the people ran to them, to the porch which is called Solomon’s, greatly wondering.

3:12  LA: Videns autem Petrus, respondit ad populum: Viri Israëlitæ, quid miramini in hoc, aut nos quid intuemini, quasi nostra virtute aut potestate fecerimus hunc ambulare?
      EN: But Peter seeing, answered to the people: You men of Israel, why wonder you at this? Or why look you upon us, as if by our strength or power we had made this man to walk?

3:13  LA: Deus Abraham, et Deus Isaac, et Deus Jacob, Deus patrum nostrorum glorificavit filium suum Jesum, quem vos quidem tradidistis, et negastis ante faciem Pilati, judicante illo dimitti.
      EN: The God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob, the God of our fathers, has glorified his Son Jesus, whom you indeed delivered up and denied before the face of Pilate, when he judged he should be released.

3:14  LA: Vos autem sanctum et justum negastis, et petistis virum homicidam donari vobis:
      EN: But you denied the Holy One and the Just: and desired a murderer to be granted to you.

3:15  LA: auctorem vero vitæ interfecistis, quem Deus suscitavit a mortuis, cujus nos testes sumus.
      EN: But the author of life you killed, whom God has raised from the dead: of which we are witnesses.

3:16  LA: Et in fide nominis ejus, hunc quem vos vidistis et nostis, confirmavit nomen ejus: et fides, quæ per eum est, dedit integram sanitatem istam in conspectu omnium vestrum.
      EN: And in the faith of his name, this man, whom you have seen and known, has his name strengthened. And the faith which is by him has given this perfect soundness in the sight of you all.

3:17  LA: Et nunc, fratres, scio quia per ignorantiam fecistis, sicut et principes vestri.
      EN: And now, brothers, I know that you did it through ignorance: as did also your rulers.

3:18  LA: Deus autem, quæ prænuntiavit per os omnium prophetarum, pati Christum suum, sic implevit.
      EN: But those things which God before had showed by the mouth of all the prophets, that his Christ should suffer, he has so fulfilled.

3:19  LA: Pœnitemini igitur et convertimini, ut deleantur peccata vestra:
      EN: Be penitent, therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out.

3:20  LA: ut cum venerint tempora refrigerii a conspectu Domini, et miserit eum qui prædicatus est vobis, Jesum Christum,
      EN: That when the times of refreshment shall come from the presence of the Lord, and he shall send him who has been preached to you, Jesus Christ.

3:21  LA: quem oportet quidem cælum suscipere usque in tempora restitutionis omnium quæ locutus est Deus per os sanctorum suorum a sæculo prophetarum.
      EN: Whom heaven indeed must receive, until the times of the restitution of all things, which God has spoken by the mouth of his holy prophets, from the beginning of the world.

3:22  LA: Moyses quidem dixit: Quoniam prophetam suscitabit vobis Dominus Deus vester de fratribus vestris, tamquam me: ipsum audietis juxta omnia quæcumque locutus fuerit vobis.
      EN: For Moses said: A prophet shall the Lord your God raise up to you of your brothers, like to me: him you shall hear according to all things whatever he shall speak to you.

3:23  LA: Erit autem: omnis anima quæ non audierit prophetam illum, exterminabitur de plebe.
      EN: And it shall be, that every soul which will not hear that prophet shall be destroyed from among the people.

3:24  LA: Et omnes prophetæ, a Samuel et deinceps, qui locuti sunt, annuntiaverunt dies istos.
      EN: And all the prophets, from Samuel and afterwards, who have spoken, have told of these days.

3:25  LA: Vos estis filii prophetarum, et testamenti quod disposuit Deus ad patres nostros, dicens ad Abraham: Et in semine tuo benedicentur omnes familiæ terræ.
      EN: You are the children of the prophets and of the testament which God made to our fathers, saying to Abraham: And in your seed shall all the kindreds of the earth be blessed.

3:26  LA: Vobis primum Deus suscitans filium suum, misit eum benedicentem vobis: ut convertat se unusquisque a nequitia sua.
      EN: To you first, God, raising up his Son, has sent him to bless you: that everyone may convert himself from his wickedness.

ACTS 4

4:1  LA: Loquentibus autem illis ad populum, supervenerunt sacerdotes, et magistratus templi, et sadducæi,
     EN: And as they were speaking to the people the priests and the officer of the temple and the Sadducees came upon them,

4:2  LA: dolentes quod docerent populum, et annuntiarent in Jesu resurrectionem ex mortuis:
     EN: Being grieved that they taught the people and preached in Jesus the resurrection from the dead:

4:3  LA: et injecerunt in eos manus, et posuerunt eos in custodiam in crastinum: erat enim jam vespera.
     EN: And they laid hands upon them and put them in hold until the next day: for it was now evening.

4:4  LA: Multi autem eorum qui audierant verbum, crediderunt: et factus est numerus virorum quinque millia.
     EN: But many of them who had heard the word believed: and the number of the men was made five thousand.

4:5  LA: Factum est autem in crastinum, ut congregarentur principes eorum, et seniores, et scribæ, in Jerusalem:
     EN: And it happened on the next day, that their princes and ancients and scribes were gathered together in Jerusalem.

4:6  LA: et Annas princeps sacerdotum, et Caiphas, et Joannes, et Alexander, et quotquot erant de genere sacerdotali.
     EN: And Annas the high priest and Caiaphas and John and Alexander: and as many as were of the kindred of the high priest.

4:7  LA: Et statuentes eos in medio, interrogabant: In qua virtute, aut in quo nomine fecistis hoc vos?
     EN: And setting them in the midst, they asked: By what power or by what name, have you done this?

4:8  LA: Tunc repletus Spiritu Sancto Petrus, dixit ad eos: Principes populi, et seniores, audite:
     EN: Then Peter, filled with the Holy Spirit, said to them: You princes of the people and ancients, hear.

4:9  LA: si nos hodie dijudicamur in benefacto hominis infirmi, in quo iste salvus factus est,
     EN: If we this day are examined concerning the good deed done to the infirm man, by what means he has been made well:

4:10  LA: notum sit omnibus vobis, et omni plebi Israël, quia in nomine Domini nostri Jesu Christi Nazareni, quem vos crucifixistis, quem Deus suscitavit a mortuis, in hoc iste astat coram vobis sanus.
      EN: Be it known to you all and to all the people of Israel, that by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ of Nazareth, whom you crucified, whom God has raised from the dead, even by him, this man stands here before you, whole.

4:11  LA: Hic est lapis qui reprobatus est a vobis ædificantibus, qui factus est in caput anguli:
      EN: This is the stone which was rejected by you the builders, which has become the head of the corner.

4:12  LA: et non est in alio aliquo salus. Nec enim aliud nomen est sub cælo datum hominibus, in quo oporteat nos salvos fieri.
      EN: Neither is there salvation in any other. For there is no other name under heaven given to men, whereby we must be saved.

4:13  LA: Videntes autem Petri constantiam, et Joannis, comperto quod homines essent sine litteris, et idiotæ, admirabantur, et cognoscebant eos quoniam cum Jesu fuerant:
      EN: Now seeing the constancy of Peter and of John, understanding that they were illiterate and ignorant men, they wondered: and they knew them that they had been with Jesus.

4:14  LA: hominem quoque videntes stantem cum eis, qui curatus fuerat, nihil poterant contradicere.
      EN: Seeing the man also who had been healed, standing with them, they could say nothing against it.

4:15  LA: Jusserunt autem eos foras extra concilium secedere: et conferebant ad invicem,
      EN: But they commanded them to go aside out of the council: and they conferred among themselves,

4:16  LA: dicentes: Quid faciemus hominibus istis? quoniam quidem notum signum factum est per eos omnibus habitantibus Jerusalem: manifestum est, et non possumus negare.
      EN: Saying: What shall we do to these men? For indeed a miracle has been done by them, known to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem. It is manifest: and we cannot deny it.

4:17  LA: Sed ne amplius divulgetur in populum, comminemur eis ne ultra loquantur in nomine hoc ulli hominum.
      EN: But that it may be no farther spread among the people, let us threaten them that they speak no more in this name to any man.

4:18  LA: Et vocantes eos, denuntiaverunt ne omnino loquerentur neque docerent in nomine Jesu.
      EN: And calling them, they charged them not to speak at all, nor teach in the name of Jesus.

4:19  LA: Petrus vero et Joannes respondentes, dixerunt ad eos: Si justum est in conspectu Dei vos potius audire quam Deum, judicate.
      EN: But Peter and John answered them: If it be just, in the sight of God, to hear you rather than God, judge you.

4:20  LA: Non enim possumus quæ vidimus et audivimus non loqui.
      EN: For we cannot but speak the things which we have seen and heard.

4:21  LA: At illi comminantes dimiserunt eos, non invenientes quomodo punirent eos propter populum: quia omnes clarificabant id quod factum fuerat in eo quod acciderat.
      EN: But they, threatening, sent them away, not finding how they might punish them, because of the people: for all men glorified what had been done, in that which had happen.

4:22  LA: Annorum enim erat amplius quadraginta homo, in quo factum fuerat signum istud sanitatis.
      EN: For the man was above forty years old, in whom that miraculous cure had been wrought.

4:23  LA: Dimissi autem venerunt ad suos, et annuntiaverunt eis quanta ad eos principes sacerdotum et seniores dixissent.
      EN: And being let go, they came to their own company and related all that the chief priests and ancients had said to them.

4:24  LA: Qui cum audissent, unanimiter levaverunt vocem ad Deum, et dixerunt: Domine, tu es qui fecisti cælum et terram, mare et omnia quæ in eis sunt:
      EN: Who having heard it, with one accord lifted up their voice to God and said: Lord, you are he that did make heaven and earth, the sea and all things that are in them.

4:25  LA: qui Spiritu Sancto per os patris nostri David pueri tui dixisti: Quare fremuerunt gentes, et populi meditati sunt inania?
      EN: Who, by the Holy Spirit, by the mouth of our father David, your servant, have said: Why did the Gentiles rage: and the people meditate vain things?

4:26  LA: Astiterunt reges terræ, et principes convenerunt in unum adversus Dominum, et adversus Christum ejus.
      EN: The kings of the earth stood up: and the princes assembled together against the Lord and his Christ.

4:27  LA: Convenerunt enim vere in civitate ista adversus sanctum puerum tuum Jesum, quem unxisti, Herodes et Pontius Pilatus, cum gentibus et populis Israël,
      EN: For of a truth there assembled together in this city against your holy child Jesus, whom you have anointed, Herod, and Pontius Pilate, with the Gentiles and the people of Israel,

4:28  LA: facere quæ manus tua et consilium tuum decreverunt fieri.
      EN: To do what your hand and your counsel decreed to be done.

4:29  LA: Et nunc, Domine, respice in minas eorum, et da servis tuis cum omni fiducia loqui verbum tuum,
      EN: And now, Lord, behold their threatenings: and grant to your servants that with all confidence they may speak your word,

4:30  LA: in eo quod manum tuam extendas ad sanitates, et signa, et prodigia fieri per nomen sancti filii tui Jesu.
      EN: By stretching forth your hand to cures and signs and wonders, to be done by the name of your holy Son, Jesus.

4:31  LA: Et cum orassent, motus est locus in quo erant congregati: et repleti sunt omnes Spiritu Sancto, et loquebantur verbum Dei cum fiducia.
      EN: And when they had prayed, the place was moved wherein they were assembled: and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit: and they spoke the word of God with confidence.

4:32  LA: Multitudinis autem credentium erat cor unum et anima una: nec quisquam eorum quæ possidebat, aliquid suum esse dicebat, sed erant illis omnia communia.
      EN: And the multitude of believers had but one heart and one soul. Neither did anyone say that anything of the things which he possessed was his own: but all things were common to them.

4:33  LA: Et virtute magna reddebant Apostoli testimonium resurrectionis Jesu Christi Domini nostri: et gratia magna erat in omnibus illis.
      EN: And with great power did the Apostles give testimony of the resurrection of Jesus Christ our Lord: and great grace was in them all.

4:34  LA: Neque enim quisquam egens erat inter illos. Quotquot enim possessores agrorum aut domorum erant, vendentes afferebant pretia eorum quæ vendebant,
      EN: For neither was there anyone needy among them. For as many as were owners of lands or houses sold them and brought the price of the things they sold,

4:35  LA: et ponebant ante pedes Apostolorum. Dividebatur autem singulis prout cuique opus erat.
      EN: And laid it down before the feet of the apostles. And distribution was made to everyone, just as he had need.

4:36  LA: Joseph autem, qui cognominatus est Barnabas ab Apostolis (quod est interpretatum, Filius consolationis), Levites, Cyprius genere,
      EN: And Joseph, who, by the apostles, was surnamed Barnabas (which is, by interpretation, The son of consolation), a Levite, a Cyprian born,

4:37  LA: cum haberet agrum, vendidit eum, et attulit pretium, et posuit ante pedes Apostolorum.
      EN: Having land, sold it and brought the price and laid it at the feet of the Apostles.

ACTS 5

5:1  LA: Vir autem quidam nomine Ananias, cum Saphira uxore suo vendidit agrum,
     EN: But a certain man named Ananias, with Saphira his wife, sold a piece of land,

5:2  LA: et fraudavit de pretio agri, conscia uxore sua: et afferens partem quamdam, ad pedes Apostolorum posuit.
     EN: And by fraud kept back part of the price of the land, his wife being privy to it: and bringing a certain part of it, laid it at the feet of the apostles.

5:3  LA: Dixit autem Petrus: Anania, cur tentavit Satanas cor tuum, mentiri te Spiritui Sancto, et fraudare de pretio agri?
     EN: But Peter said: Ananias, why has Satan tempted your heart, that you should lie to the Holy Spirit and by fraud keep part of the price of the land?

5:4  LA: nonne manens tibi manebat, et venundatum in tua erat potestate? quare posuisti in corde tuo hanc rem? non es mentitus hominibus, sed Deo.
     EN: Whilst it remained, did it not remain to you? And after it was sold, was it not in your power? Why have you conceived this thing in your heart? You have not lied to men, but to God.

5:5  LA: Audiens autem Ananias hæc verba, cecidit, et expiravit. Et factus est timor magnus super omnes qui audierunt.
     EN: And Ananias, hearing these words, fell down and breathed his last. And there came great fear upon all that heard it.

5:6  LA: Surgentes autem juvenes amoverunt eum, et efferentes sepelierunt.
     EN: And the young men rising up, removed him, and carrying him out, buried him.

5:7  LA: Factum est autem quasi horarum trium spatium, et uxor ipsius, nesciens quod factum fuerat, introivit.
     EN: And it was about the space of three hours after, when his wife, not knowing what had happened, came in.

5:8  LA: Dixit autem ei Petrus: Dic mihi mulier, si tanti agrum vendidistis? At illa dixit: Etiam tanti.
     EN: And Peter said to her: Tell me, woman, whether you sold the land for so much? And she said: Yes, for so much.

5:9  LA: Petrus autem ad eam: Quid utique convenit vobis tentare Spiritum Domini? Ecce pedes eorum qui sepelierunt virum tuum ad ostium, et efferent te.
     EN: And Peter said to her: Why have you agreed together to tempt the spirit of the Lord? Behold the feet of them who have buried your husband are at the door: and they shall carry you out,

5:10  LA: Confestim cecidit ante pedes ejus, et expiravit. Intrantes autem juvenes invenerunt illam mortuam: et extulerunt, et sepelierunt ad virum suum.
      EN: Immediately, she fell down before his feet and breathed his last. And the young men coming in found her dead: and carried her out and buried her by her husband.

5:11  LA: Et factus est timor magnus in universa ecclesia, et in omnes qui audierunt hæc.
      EN: And there came great fear upon the whole church and upon all that heard these things.

5:12  LA: Per manus autem Apostolorum fiebant signa et prodigia multa in plebe. Et erant unanimiter omnes in porticu Salomonis.
      EN: And by the hands of the apostles were many signs and wonders wrought among the people. And they were all with one accord in Solomon’s porch.

5:13  LA: Ceterorum autem nemo audebat se conjungere illis: sed magnificabat eos populus.
      EN: But of the rest no one dared join himself to them: but the people magnified them.

5:14  LA: Magis autem augebatur credentium in Domino multitudo virorum ac mulierum,
      EN: And the multitude of men and women who believed in the Lord was more increased:

5:15  LA: ita ut in plateas ejicerent infirmos, et ponerent in lectulis et grabatis, ut, veniente Petro, saltem umbra illius obumbraret quemquam illorum, et liberarentur ab infirmitatibus suis.
      EN: Insomuch that they brought forth the sick into the streets and laid them on beds and couches, that, when Peter came, his shadow at the least might overshadow any of them and they might be delivered from their infirmities.

5:16  LA: Concurrebat autem et multitudo vicinarum civitatum Jerusalem, afferentes ægros, et vexatos a spiritibus immundis: qui curabantur omnes.
      EN: And there came also together to Jerusalem a multitude out of the neighbouring cities, bringing sick persons and such as were troubled with unclean spirits: who were all healed.

5:17  LA: Exsurgens autem princeps sacerdotum, et omnes qui cum illo erant (quæ est hæresis sadducæorum), repleti sunt zelo:
      EN: Then the high priest rising up, and all they that were with him (which is the heresy of the Sadducees) were filled with envy.

5:18  LA: et injecerunt manus in Apostolos, et posuerunt eos in custodia publica.
      EN: And they laid hands on the apostles and put them in the common prison.

5:19  LA: Angelus autem Domini per noctem aperiens januas carceris, et educens eos, dixit:
      EN: But an angel of the Lord by night, opening the doors of the prison and leading them out, said:

5:20  LA: Ite, et stantes loquimini in templo plebi omnia verba vitæ hujus.
      EN: Go, and standing speak in the temple to the people all the words of this life.

5:21  LA: Qui cum audissent, intraverunt diluculo in templum, et docebant. Adveniens autem princeps sacerdotum, et qui cum eo erant, convocaverunt concilium, et omnes seniores filiorum Israël: et miserunt ad carcerem ut adducerentur.
      EN: Who having heard this, early in the morning, entered into the temple and taught. And the high priest coming, and they that were with him, called together the council and all the ancients of the children of Israel: and they sent to the prison to have them brought.

5:22  LA: Cum autem venissent ministri, et aperto carcere non invenissent illos, reversi nuntiaverunt,
      EN: But when the ministers came and opening the prison found them not there, they returned and told,

5:23  LA: dicentes: Carcerem quidem invenimus clausum cum omni diligentia, et custodes stantes ante januas: aperientes autem neminem intus invenimus.
      EN: Saying: The prison indeed we found shut with all diligence, and the keepers standing before the doors; but opening it, we found no one within.

5:24  LA: Ut autem audierunt hos sermones magistratus templi et principes sacerdotum, ambigebant de illis quidnam fieret.
      EN: Now when the officer of the temple and the chief priests heard these words, they were in doubt concerning them, what would happen.

5:25  LA: Adveniens autem quidam, nuntiavit eis: Quia ecce viri quos posuistis in carcerem, sunt in templo, stantes et docentes populum.
      EN: But one came and told them: Behold, the men whom you put in prison are in the temple, standing and teaching the people.

5:26  LA: Tunc abiit magistratus cum ministris, et adduxit illos sine vi: timebant enim populum ne lapidarentur.
      EN: Then went the officer with the ministers and brought them without violence: for they feared the people, lest they should be stoned.

5:27  LA: Et cum adduxissent illos, statuerunt in concilio: et interrogavit eos princeps sacerdotum,
      EN: And when they had brought them, they set them before the council. And the high priest asked them,

5:28  LA: dicens: Præcipiendo præcepimus vobis ne doceretis in nomine isto, et ecce replestis Jerusalem doctrina vestra: et vultis inducere super nos sanguinem hominis istius.
      EN: Saying: Commanding, we commanded you that you should not teach in this name. And behold, you have filled Jerusalem with your doctrine: and you wish to bring the blood of this man upon us.

5:29  LA: Respondens autem Petrus et Apostoli, dixerunt: Obedire oportet Deo magis quam hominibus.
      EN: But Peter and the apostles answered: We ought to obey God rather than men.

5:30  LA: Deus patrum nostrorum suscitavit Jesum, quem vos interemistis, suspendentes in ligno.
      EN: The God of our fathers has raised up Jesus, whom you put to death, hanging him upon a tree.

5:31  LA: Hunc principem et salvatorem Deus exaltavit dextera sua ad dandam pœnitentiam Israëli, et remissionem peccatorum:
      EN: Him has God exalted with his right hand, to be Prince and Savior, to give repentance to Israel and forgiveness of sins.

5:32  LA: et nos sumus testes horum verborum, et Spiritus Sanctus, quem dedit Deus omnibus obedientibus sibi.
      EN: And we are witnesses of these things: and the Holy Spirit, whom God has given to all that obey him.

5:33  LA: Hæc cum audissent, dissecabantur, et cogitabant interficere illos.
      EN: When they had heard these things, they were cut to the heart: and they thought to put them to death.

5:34  LA: Surgens autem quidam in concilio pharisæus, nomine Gamaliel, legis doctor, honorabilis universæ plebi, jussit foras ad breve homines fieri,
      EN: But one in the council rising up, a Pharisee, named Gamaliel, a doctor of the law, respected by all the people, commanded the men to be put forth a little while.

5:35  LA: dixitque ad illos: Viri Israëlitæ, attendite vobis super hominibus istis quid acturi sitis.
      EN: And he said to them: You men of Israel, take care to yourselves what you intend to do, as touching these men.

5:36  LA: Ante hos enim dies extitit Theodas, dicens se esse aliquem, cui consensit numerus virorum circiter quadringentorum: qui occisus est, et omnes qui credebant ei, dissipati sunt, et redacti ad nihilum.
      EN: For before these days rose up Theodas, affirming himself to be somebody, to whom a number of men, about four hundred, joined themselves. Who was slain: and all that believed him were scattered and brought to nothing.

5:37  LA: Post hunc extitit Judas Galilæus in diebus professionis, et avertit populum post se: et ipse periit, et omnes quotquot consenserunt ei, dispersi sunt.
      EN: After this man, rose up Judas of Galilee, in the days of the enrolling, and drew away the people after him. He also perished: and all, even as many as consented to him, were dispersed.

5:38  LA: Et nunc itaque dico vobis, discedite ab hominibus istis, et sinite illos: quoniam si est ex hominibus consilium hoc aut opus, dissolvetur:
      EN: And now, therefore, I say to you: Refrain from these men and let them alone. For if this council or this work be of men, it will come to nought:

5:39  LA: si vero ex Deo est, non poteritis dissolvere illud, ne forte et Deo repugnare inveniamini. Consenserunt autem illi.
      EN: But if it be of God, you cannot overthrow it, lest you be found even to fight against God. And they consented to him.

5:40  LA: Et convocantes Apostolos, cæsis denuntiaverunt ne omnino loquerentur in nomine Jesu, et dimiserunt eos.
      EN: And calling in the apostles, after they had flogged them, they charged them that they should not speak at all in the name of Jesus. And they dismissed them.

5:41  LA: Et illi quidem ibant gaudentes a conspectu concilii, quoniam digni habiti sunt pro nomine Jesu contumeliam pati.
      EN: And they indeed went from the presence of the council, rejoicing that they were accounted worthy to suffer reproach for the name of Jesus.

5:42  LA: Omni autem die non cessabant in templo et circa domos, docentes et evangelizantes Christum Jesum.
      EN: And every day they ceased not, in the temple and from house to house, to teach and preach Christ Jesus.

ACTS 6

6:1  LA: In diebus illis, crescente numero discipulorum, factum est murmur Græcorum adversus Hebræos, eo quod despicerentur in ministerio quotidiano viduæ eorum.
     EN: And in those days, the number of the disciples increasing, there arose a murmuring of the Greeks against the Hebrews, for that their widows were neglected in the daily ministration.

6:2  LA: Convocantes autem duodecim multitudinem discipulorum, dixerunt: Non est æquum nos derelinquere verbum Dei, et ministrare mensis.
     EN: Then the twelve, calling together the multitude of the disciples, said: It is not reason that we should leave the word of God and serve tables.

6:3  LA: Considerate ergo, fratres, viros ex vobis boni testimonii septem, plenos Spiritu Sancto et sapientia, quos constituamus super hoc opus.
     EN: Therefore, brothers, look you out among you seven men of good reputation, full of the Holy Spirit and wisdom, whom we may appoint over this business.

6:4  LA: Nos vero orationi et ministerio verbi instantes erimus.
     EN: But we will give ourselves continually to prayer and to the ministry of the word.

6:5  LA: Et placuit sermo coram omni multitudine. Et elegerunt Stephanum, virum plenum fide et Spiritu Sancto, et Philippum, et Prochorum, et Nicanorem, et Timonem, et Parmenam, et Nicolaum advenam Antiochenum.
     EN: And the saying was liked by all the multitude. And they chose Stephen, a man full of faith and of the Holy Spirit, and Philip and Prochorus and Nicanor, and Timon and Parmenas and Nicolas, a proselyte of Antioch.

6:6  LA: Hos statuerunt ante conspectum Apostolorum: et orantes imposuerunt eis manus.
     EN: These they set before the apostles: and they praying, imposed hands upon them.

6:7  LA: Et verbum Domini crescebat, et multiplicabatur numerus discipulorum in Jerusalem valde: multa etiam turba sacerdotum obediebat fidei.
     EN: And the word of the Lord increased: and the number of the disciples was multiplied in Jerusalem exceedingly. A great multitude also of the priests obeyed the faith.

6:8  LA: Stephanus autem plenus gratia et fortitudine, faciebat prodigia et signa magna in populo.
     EN: And Stephen, full of grace and fortitude, did great wonders and signs among the people.

6:9  LA: Surrexerunt autem quidam de synagoga quæ appellatur Libertinorum, et Cyrenensium, et Alexandrinorum, et eorum qui erant a Cilicia, et Asia, disputantes cum Stephano:
     EN: Now there arose some, of that which is called the synagogue of the Libertines and of the Cyrenians and of the Alexandrians and of them that were of Cilicia and Asia, disputing with Stephen.

6:10  LA: et non poterant resistere sapientiæ, et Spiritui qui loquebatur.
      EN: And they were not able to resist the wisdom and the spirit that spoke.

6:11  LA: Tunc summiserunt viros, qui dicerent se audivisse eum dicentem verba blasphemiæ in Moysen et in Deum.
      EN: Then they suborned men to say they had heard him speak words of blasphemy against Moses and against God.

6:12  LA: Commoverunt itaque plebem, et seniores, et scribas: et concurrentes rapuerunt eum, et adduxerunt in concilium,
      EN: And they stirred up the people and the ancients and the scribes. And running together, they took him and brought him to the council.

6:13  LA: et statuerunt falsos testes, qui dicerent: Homo iste non cessat loqui verba adversus locum sanctum, et legem:
      EN: And they set up false witnesses, who said: This man ceases not to speak words against the holy place and the law.

6:14  LA: audivimus enim eum dicentem quoniam Jesus Nazarenus hic destruet locum istum, et mutabit traditiones quas tradidit nobis Moyses.
      EN: For we have heard him say that this Jesus of Nazareth shall destroy this place and shall change the traditions which Moses delivered to us.

6:15  LA: Et intuentes eum omnes qui sedebant in concilio, viderunt faciem ejus tamquam faciem angeli.
      EN: And all that sat in the council, looking on him, saw his face as if it had been the face of an angel.

ACTS 7

7:1  LA: Dixit autem princeps sacerdotum: Si hæc ita se habent?
     EN: Then the high priest said: Are these things so?

7:2  LA: Qui ait: Viri fratres et patres, audite: Deus gloriæ apparuit patri nostro Abrahæ cum esset in Mesopotamia, priusquam moraretur in Charan,
     EN: Who said: You men, brothers and fathers, hear. The God of glory appeared to our father Abraham, when he was in Mesopotamia, before he dwelt in Charan.

7:3  LA: et dixit ad illum: Exi de terra tua, et de cognatione tua, et veni in terram quam monstravero tibi.
     EN: And said to him: Go forth out of your country and from your kindred: and come into the land which I shall show you.

7:4  LA: Tunc exiit de terra Chaldæorum, et habitavit in Charan. Et inde, postquam mortuus est pater ejus, transtulit illum in terram istam, in qua nunc vos habitatis.
     EN: Then he went out of the land of the Chaldeans and dwelt in Charan. And from there, after his father was dead, he removed him into this land, wherein you now dwell.

7:5  LA: Et non dedit illi hæreditatem in ea, nec passum pedis: sed repromisit dare illi eam in possessionem, et semini ejus post ipsum, cum non haberet filium.
     EN: And he gave him no inheritance in it: no, not the pace of a foot. But he promised to give it him in possession, and to his seed after him, when yet he had no child.

7:6  LA: Locutus est autem ei Deus: Quia erit semen ejus accola in terra aliena, et servituti eos subjicient, et male tractabunt eos annis quadringentis:
     EN: And God said to him: That his seed should sojourn in a strange country, and that they should bring them under bondage and treat them evil four hundred years.

7:7  LA: et gentem cui servierint, judicabo ego, dixit Dominus: et post hæc exibunt, et servient mihi in loco isto.
     EN: And the nation which they shall serve will I judge (said the Lord): and after these things they shall go out and shall serve me in this place.

7:8  LA: Et dedit illi testamentum circumcisionis: et sic genuit Isaac, et circumcidit eum die octavo: et Isaac, Jacob: et Jacob, duodecim patriarchas.
     EN: And he gave him the covenant of circumcision. And so he fathered Isaac and circumcised him the eighth day: and Isaac fathered Jacob: and Jacob, the twelve patriarchs.

7:9  LA: Et patriarchæ æmulantes, Joseph vendiderunt in Ægyptum: et erat Deus cum eo,
     EN: And the patriarchs, through envy, sold Joseph into Egypt. And God was with him,

7:10  LA: et eripuit eum ex omnibus tribulationibus ejus, et dedit ei gratiam et sapientiam in conspectu pharaonis regis Ægypti: et constituit eum præpositum super Ægyptum, et super omnem domum suam.

      EN: And Ananias went his way and entered into the house. And laying his hands upon him, he said: Brother Saul, the Lord Jesus has sent me, he that appeared to you in the way as you came, that you may receive your sight and be filled with the Holy Spirit.

9:18  LA: Et confestim ceciderunt ab oculis ejus tamquam squamæ, et visum recepit: et surgens baptizatus est.
      EN: And immediately there fell from his eyes as it were scales: and he received his sight. And rising up, he was baptized.

9:19  LA: Et cum accepisset cibum, confortatus est. Fuit autem cum discipulis qui erant Damasci per dies aliquot.
      EN: And when he had taken food, he was strengthened. And he was with the disciples that were at Damascus, for some days.

9:20  LA: Et continuo in synagogis prædicabat Jesum, quoniam hic est Filius Dei.
      EN: And immediately he preached Jesus in the synagogues, that he is the son of God.

9:21  LA: Stupebant autem omnes qui audiebant, et dicebant: Nonne hic est qui expugnabat in Jerusalem eos qui invocabant nomen istud: et huc ad hoc venit, ut vinctos illos duceret ad principes sacerdotum?
      EN: And all that heard him were astonished and said: Is not this he who persecuted in Jerusalem those that called upon this name and came here for that intent, that he might carry them bound to the chief priests?

9:22  LA: Saulus autem multo magis convalescebat, et confundebat Judæos qui habitabant Damasci, affirmans quoniam hic est Christus.
      EN: But Saul increased much more in strength and confounded the Jews who dwelt at Damascus, affirming that this is the Christ.

9:23  LA: Cum autem implerentur dies multi, consilium fecerunt in unum Judæi ut eum interficerent.
      EN: And when many days were passed, the Jews consulted together to kill him.

9:24  LA: Notæ autem factæ sunt Saulo insidiæ eorum. Custodiebant autem et portas die ac nocte, ut eum interficerent.
      EN: But their lying in wait was made known to Saul. And they watched the gates also day and night, that they might kill him.

9:25  LA: Accipientes autem eum discipuli nocte, per murum dimiserunt eum, submittentes in sporta.
      EN: But the disciples, taking him in the night, conveyed him away by the wall, letting him down in a basket.

9:26  LA: Cum autem venisset in Jerusalem, tentabat se jungere discipulis, et omnes timebant eum, non credentes quod esset discipulus.
      EN: And when he had come into Jerusalem, he essayed to join himself to the disciples: and they all were afraid of him, not believing that he was a disciple.

9:27  LA: Barnabas autem apprehensum illum duxit ad Apostolos: et narravit illis quomodo in via vidisset Dominum, et quia locutus est ei, et quomodo in Damasco fiducialiter egerit in nomine Jesu.
      EN: But Barnabas took him and brought him to the apostles and told them how he had seen the Lord, and that he had spoken to him: and how in Damascus he had dealt confidently in the name of Jesus.

9:28  LA: Et erat cum illis intrans et exiens in Jerusalem, et fiducialiter agens in nomine Domini.
      EN: And he was with them, coming in and going out in Jerusalem and dealing confidently in the name of the Lord.

9:29  LA: Loquebatur quoque gentibus, et disputabat cum Græcis: illi autem quærebant occidere eum.
      EN: He spoke also to the Gentiles and disputed with the Greeks. But they sought to kill him.

9:30  LA: Quod cum cognovissent fratres, deduxerunt eum Cæsaream, et dimiserunt Tarsum.
      EN: Which when the brothers had known, they brought him down to Cæsarea and sent him away to Tarsus.

9:31  LA: Ecclesia quidem per totam Judæam, et Galilæam, et Samariam habebat pacem, et ædificabatur ambulans in timore Domini, et consolatione Sancti Spiritus replebatur.
      EN: Now, the church had peace throughout all Judea and Galilee and Samaria: and was edified, walking in the fear of the Lord: and was filled with the consolation of the Holy Spirit.

9:32  LA: Factum est autem, ut Petrus dum pertransiret universos, deveniret ad sanctos qui habitabant Lyddæ.
      EN: And it happened that Peter, as he passed through, visiting all, came to the saints who dwelt at Lydda.

9:33  LA: Invenit autem ibi hominem quemdam, nomine Æneam, ab annis octo jacentem in grabato, qui erat paralyticus.
      EN: And he found there a certain man named Eneas, who had kept his bed for eight years, who was ill of the palsy.

9:34  LA: Et ait illi Petrus: Ænea, sanat te Dominus Jesus Christus: surge, et sterne tibi. Et continuo surrexit.
      EN: And Peter said to him: Eneas, the Lord Jesus Christ heals you. Arise and make your bed. And immediately he arose.

9:35  LA: Et viderunt eum omnes qui habitabant Lyddæ et Saronæ: qui conversi sunt ad Dominum.
      EN: And all that dwelt at Lydda and Saron saw him: who were converted to the Lord.

9:36  LA: In Joppe autem fuit quædam discipula, nomine Tabitha, quæ interpretata dicitur Dorcas. Hæc erat plena operibus bonis et eleemosynis quas faciebat.
      EN: And in Joppe there was a certain disciple named Tabitha, which by interpretation is called Dorcas. This woman was full of good works and almsdeeds which she did.

9:37  LA: Factum est autem in diebus illis ut infirmata moreretur. Quam cum lavissent, posuerunt eam in cœnaculo.
      EN: And it happened in those days that she was sick and died. Whom when they had washed, they laid her in an upper chamber.

9:38  LA: Cum autem prope esset Lydda ad Joppen, discipuli, audientes quia Petrus esset in ea, miserunt duos viros ad eum, rogantes: Ne pigriteris venire ad nos.
      EN: And since Lydda was near to Joppe, the disciples, hearing that Peter was there, sent to him two men, desiring him that he would not be slack to come to them.

9:39  LA: Exsurgens autem Petrus, venit cum illis. Et cum advenisset, duxerunt illum in cœnaculum: et circumsteterunt illum omnes viduæ flentes, et ostendentes ei tunicas et vestes quas faciebat illis Dorcas.
      EN: And Peter rising up went with them. And when he had come, they brought him into the upper chamber. And all the widows stood about him, weeping and shewing him the tunics and garments which Dorcas made them.

9:40  LA: Ejectis autem omnibus foras, Petrus ponens genua oravit: et conversus ad corpus, dixit: Tabitha, surge. At illa aperuit oculos suos: et viso Petro, resedit.
      EN: And they all being put forth, Peter, kneeling down, prayed. And turning to the body, he said: Tabitha, arise. And she opened her eyes and, seeing Peter, sat up.

9:41  LA: Dans autem illi manum, erexit eam. Et cum vocasset sanctos et viduas, assignavit eam vivam.
      EN: And giving her his hand, he lifted her up. And when he had called the saints and the widows, he presented her alive.

9:42  LA: Notum autem factum est per universam Joppen: et crediderunt multi in Domino.
      EN: And it was made known throughout all Joppe. And many believed in the Lord.

9:43  LA: Factum est autem ut dies multos moraretur in Joppe, apud Simonem quemdam coriarium.
      EN: And it happened that he abode many days in Joppe, with one Simon a tanner.

ACTS 10

10:1  LA: Vir autem quidam erat in Cæsarea, nomine Cornelius, centurio cohortis quæ dicitur Italica,
      EN: And there was a certain man in Cæsarea, named Cornelius, a centurion of that which is called the Italian band:

10:2  LA: religiosus, ac timens Deum cum omni domo sua, faciens eleemosynas multas plebi, et deprecans Deum semper.
      EN: A religious man, and fearing God with all his house, giving much alms to the people and always praying to God.

10:3  LA: Is vidit in visu manifeste, quasi hora diei nona, angelum Dei introëuntem ad se, et dicentem sibi: Corneli.
      EN: This man saw in a vision manifestly, about the ninth hour of the day, an angel of God coming in to him and saying to him: Cornelius.

10:4  LA: At ille intuens eum, timore correptus, dixit: Quid est, domine? Dixit autem illi: Orationes tuæ et eleemosynæ tuæ ascenderunt in memoriam in conspectu Dei.
      EN: And he, beholding him, being seized with fear, said: What is it, Lord? And he said to him: Your prayers and your alms are ascended for a memorial in the sight of God.

10:5  LA: Et nunc mitte viros in Joppen, et accersi Simonem quemdam, qui cognominatur Petrus:
      EN: And now send men to Joppe: and call here one Simon, who is surnamed Peter.

10:6  LA: hic hospitatur apud Simonem quemdam coriarium, cujus est domus juxta mare: hic dicet tibi quid te oporteat facere.
      EN: He lodges with one Simon a tanner, whose house is by the sea side. He will tell you what you must do.

10:7  LA: Et cum discessisset angelus qui loquebatur illi, vocavit duos domesticos suos, et militem metuentem Dominum ex his qui illi parebant.
      EN: And when the angel who spoke to him was departed, he called two of his household servants and a soldier who feared the Lord, of them that were under him.

10:8  LA: Quibus cum narrasset omnia, misit illos in Joppen.
      EN: To whom when he had related all, he sent them to Joppe.

10:9  LA: Postera autem die, iter illis facientibus, et appropinquantibus civitati, ascendit Petrus in superiora ut oraret circa horam sextam.
      EN: And on the next day, while they were going on their journey and drawing near to the city, Peter went up to the higher parts of the house to pray, about the sixth hour.

10:10  LA: Et cum esuriret, voluit gustare. Parantibus autem illis, cecidit super eum mentis excessus:
       EN: And being hungry, he was desirous to taste somewhat. And as they were preparing, there came upon him an ecstasy of mind.

10:11  LA: et vidit cælum apertum, et descendens vas quoddam, velut linteum magnum, quatuor initiis submitti de cælo in terram,
       EN: And he saw the heaven opened and a certain vessel descending, as it were a great linen sheet let down by the four corners from heaven to the earth:

10:12  LA: in quo erant omnia quadrupedia, et serpentia terræ, et volatilia cæli.
       EN: Wherein were every kind of four-footed beasts and creeping things of the earth and fowls of the air.

10:13  LA: Et facta est vox ad eum: Surge, Petre: occide, et manduca.
       EN: And there came a voice to him: Arise, Peter. Kill and eat.

10:14  LA: Ait autem Petrus: Absit Domine, quia numquam manducavi omne commune et immundum.
       EN: But Peter said: Far be it from me. For I never did eat anything that is common and unclean.

10:15  LA: Et vox iterum secundo ad eum: Quod Deus purificavit, tu commune ne dixeris.
       EN: And the voice spoke to him again the second time: That which God has cleansed, do not you call common.

10:16  LA: Hoc autem factum est per ter: et statim receptum est vas in cælum.
       EN: And this was done thrice. And immediately the vessel was taken up into heaven.

10:17  LA: Et dum intra se hæsitaret Petrus quidnam esset visio quam vidisset, ecce viri qui missi erant a Cornelio, inquirentes domum Simonis astiterunt ad januam.
       EN: Now, while Peter was doubting within himself what the vision that he had seen should mean, behold the men who were sent from Cornelius, inquiring for Simon’s house, stood at the gate.

10:18  LA: Et cum vocassent, interrogabant, si Simon qui cognominatur Petrus illic haberet hospitium.
       EN: And when they had called, they asked if Simon, who is surnamed Peter, were lodged there.

10:19  LA: Petro autem cogitante de visione, dixit Spiritus ei: Ecce viri tres quærunt te.
       EN: And as Peter was thinking of the vision, the Spirit said to him: Behold three men seek you.

10:20  LA: Surge itaque, descende, et vade cum eis nihil dubitans: quia ego misi illos.
       EN: Arise, therefore: get you down and go with them, doubting nothing: for I have sent them.

10:21  LA: Descendens autem Petrus ad viros, dixit: Ecce ego sum, quem quæritis: quæ causa est, propter quam venistis?
       EN: Then Peter, going down to the men, said: Behold, I am he whom you seek. What is the cause for which you have come?

10:22  LA: Qui dixerunt: Cornelius centurio, vir justus et timens Deum, et testimonium habens ab universa gente Judæorum, responsum accepit ab angelo sancto accersire te in domum suam, et audire verba abs te.
       EN: Who said: Cornelius, a centurion, a just man and one that fears God, and having good testimony from all the nation of the Jews, received an answer of an holy angel, to send for you into his house And to hear words of you.

10:23  LA: Introducens ergo eos, recepit hospitio. Sequenti autem die, surgens profectus est cum illis, et quidam ex fratribus ab Joppe comitati sunt eum.
       EN: Then bringing them in, he lodged them. And the day following, he arose and went with them: and some of the brothers from Joppe accompanied him.

10:24  LA: Altera autem die introivit Cæsaream. Cornelius vero exspectabat illos, convocatis cognatis suis et necessariis amicis.
       EN: And the next day after, he entered into Cæsarea. And Cornelius waited for them, having called together his kinsmen and special friends.

10:25  LA: Et factum est cum introisset Petrus, obvius venit ei Cornelius, et procidens ad pedes ejus adoravit.
       EN: And it happened that when Peter had come in, Cornelius came to meet him and falling at his feet adored.

10:26  LA: Petrus vero elevavit eum, dicens: Surge: et ego ipse homo sum.
       EN: But Peter lifted him up, saying: Arise: I myself also am a man.

10:27  LA: Et loquens cum illo intravit, et invenit multos qui convenerant:
       EN: And talking with him, he went in and found many that had come together.

10:28  LA: dixitque ad illos: Vos scitis quomodo abominatum sit viro Judæo conjungi aut accedere ad alienigenam: sed mihi ostendit Deus neminem communem aut immundum dicere hominem.
       EN: And he said to them: you know how abominable it is for a man that is a Jew to keep company or to come to one of another nation: but God has showed to me, to call no one common or unclean.

10:29  LA: Propter quod sine dubitatione veni accersitus. Interrogo ergo, quam ob causam accersistis me?
       EN: For which cause, making no doubt, I came when I was sent for. I ask, therefore, for what cause you have sent for me?

10:30  LA: Et Cornelius ait: A nudiusquarta die usque ad hanc horam, orans eram hora nona in domo mea, et ecce vir stetit ante me in veste candida, et ait:
       EN: And Cornelius said: Four days ago, to this hour, I was praying in my house, at the ninth hour and behold a man stood before me in white apparel and said:

10:31  LA: Corneli, exaudita est oratio tua, et eleemosynæ tuæ commemoratæ sunt in conspectu Dei.
       EN: Cornelius, your prayer is heard and your alms are had in remembrance in the sight of God.

10:32  LA: Mitte ergo in Joppen, et accersi Simonem qui cognominatur Petrus: hic hospitatur in domo Simonis coriarii juxta mare.
       EN: Send therefore to Joppe: and call here Simon, who is surnamed Peter. He lodges in the house of Simon a tanner, by the sea side.

10:33  LA: Confestim ergo misi ad te: et tu benefecisti veniendo. Nunc ergo omnes nos in conspectu tuo adsumus audire omnia quæcumque tibi præcepta sunt a Domino.
       EN: Immediately therefore I sent to you: and you have done well in coming. Now, therefore, all we are present in your sight to hear all things whatever are commanded you by the Lord.

10:34  LA: Aperiens autem Petrus os suum, dixit: In veritate comperi quia non est personarum acceptor Deus;
       EN: And Peter opening his mouth, said: in very deed I perceive that God is not a respecter of persons.

10:35  LA: sed in omni gente qui timet eum, et operatur justitiam, acceptus est illi.
       EN: But in every nation, he that fears him and works justice is acceptable to him.

10:36  LA: Verbum misit Deus filiis Israël, annuntians pacem per Jesum Christum (hic est omnium Dominus).
       EN: God sent the word to the children of Israel, preaching peace by Jesus Christ (He is Lord of all).

10:37  LA: Vos scitis quod factum est verbum per universam Judæam: incipiens enim a Galilæa post baptismum quod prædicavit Joannes,
       EN: You know the word which has been published through all Judea: for it began from Galilee, after the baptism which John preached.

10:38  LA: Jesum a Nazareth: quomodo unxit eum Deus Spiritu Sancto, et virtute, qui pertransiit benefaciendo, et sanando omnes oppressos a diabolo, quoniam Deus erat cum illo.
       EN: Jesus of Nazareth: how God anointed him with the Holy Spirit and with power, who went about doing good and healing all that were oppressed by the devil, for God was with him.

10:39  LA: Et nos testes sumus omnium quæ fecit in regione Judæorum, et Jerusalem, quem occiderunt suspendentes in ligno.
       EN: And we are witnesses of all things that he did in the land of the Jews and in Jerusalem: whom they killed, hanging him upon a tree.

10:40  LA: Hunc Deus suscitavit tertia die, et dedit eum manifestum fieri,
       EN: Him God raised up the third day and gave him to be made manifest,

10:41  LA: non omni populo, sed testibus præordinatis a Deo: nobis, qui manducavimus et bibimus cum illo postquam resurrexit a mortuis.
       EN: Not to all the people, but to witnesses preordained by God, even to us, who did eat and drink with him, after he arose again from the dead.

10:42  LA: Et præcepit nobis prædicare populo, et testificari, quia ipse est qui constitutus est a Deo judex vivorum et mortuorum.
       EN: And he commanded us to preach to the people and to testify that it is he who was appointed by God to be judge of the living and of the dead.

10:43  LA: Huic omnes prophetæ testimonium perhibent remissionem peccatorum accipere per nomen ejus omnes qui credunt in eum.
       EN: To him all the prophets give testimony, that by his name all receive forgiveness of sins, who believe in him.

10:44  LA: Adhuc loquente Petro verba hæc, cecidit Spiritus Sanctus super omnes qui audiebant verbum.
       EN: While Peter was yet speaking these words, the Holy Spirit fell on all them that heard the word.

10:45  LA: Et obstupuerunt ex circumcisione fideles qui venerant cum Petro, quia et in nationes gratia Spiritus Sancti effusa est.
       EN: And the faithful of the circumcision, who came with Peter, were astonished for that the grace of the Holy Spirit was poured out upon the Gentiles also.

10:46  LA: Audiebant enim illos loquentes linguis, et magnificantes Deum.
       EN: For they heard them speaking with tongues and magnifying God.

10:47  LA: Tunc respondit Petrus: Numquid aquam quis prohibere potest ut non baptizentur hi qui Spiritum Sanctum acceperunt sicut et nos?
       EN: Then Peter answered: Can any man forbid water, that these should not be baptized, who have received the Holy Spirit, as well as we?

10:48  LA: Et jussit eos baptizari in nomine Domini Jesu Christi. Tunc rogaverunt eum ut maneret apud eos aliquot diebus.
       EN: And he commanded them to be baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ. Then they desired him to tarry with them some days.

ACTS 11

11:1  LA: Audierunt autem Apostoli et fratres qui erant in Judæa, quoniam et gentes receperunt verbum Dei.
      EN: And the apostles and brothers, who were in Judea, heard that the Gentiles also had received the word of God.

11:2  LA: Cum autem ascendisset Petrus Jerosolymam, disceptabant adversus illum qui erant ex circumcisione,
      EN: And when Peter had come up to Jerusalem, they that were of the circumcision contended with him,

11:3  LA: dicentes: Quare introisti ad viros præputium habentes, et manducasti cum illis?
      EN: Saying: Why did you go in to men uncircumcised and did eat with them?

11:4  LA: Incipiens autem Petrus exponebat illis ordinem, dicens:
      EN: But Peter began and declared to them the matter in order, saying:

11:5  LA: Ego eram in civitate Joppe orans, et vidi in excessu mentis visionem, descendens vas quoddam velut linteum magnum quatuor initiis summitti de cælo, et venit usque ad me.
      EN: I was in the city of Joppe praying: and I saw in an ecstasy of mind a vision, a certain vessel descending, as it were a great sheet let down from heaven by four corners. And it came even to me.

11:6  LA: In quod intuens considerabam, et vidi quadrupedia terræ, et bestias, et reptilia, et volatilia cæli.
      EN: Into which looking, I considered and saw fourfooted creatures of the earth and beasts and creeping things and fowls of the air.

11:7  LA: Audivi autem et vocem dicentem mihi: Surge, Petre: occide, et manduca.
      EN: And I heard also a voice saying to me: Arise, Peter. Kill and eat.

11:8  LA: Dixi autem: Nequaquam Domine: quia commune aut immundum numquam introivit in os meum.
      EN: And I said: Not so, Lord: for nothing common or unclean has ever entered into my mouth.

11:9  LA: Respondit autem vox secundo de cælo: Quæ Deus mundavit, tu ne commune dixeris.
      EN: And the voice answered again from heaven: What God has made clean, do not you call common.

11:10  LA: Hoc autem factum est per ter: et recepta sunt omnia rursum in cælum.
       EN: And this was done three times. And all were taken up again into heaven.

11:11  LA: Et ecce viri tres confestim astiterunt in domo in qua eram, missi a Cæsarea ad me.
       EN: And behold, immediately there were three men come to the house wherein I was, sent to me from Cæsarea.

11:12  LA: Dixit autem Spiritus mihi ut irem cum illis, nihil hæsitans. Venerunt autem mecum et sex fratres isti, et ingressi sumus in domum viri.
       EN: And the Spirit said to me that I should go with them, nothing doubting. And these six brothers went with me also: and we entered into the man’s house.

11:13  LA: Narravit autem nobis quomodo vidisset angelum in domo sua, stantem et dicentem sibi: Mitte in Joppen, et accersi Simonem qui cognominatur Petrus,
       EN: And he told us how he had seen an angel in his house, standing and saying to him: Send to Joppe and call here Simon, who is surnamed Peter,

11:14  LA: qui loquetur tibi verba in quibus salvus eris tu, et universa domus tua.
       EN: Who shall speak to you words whereby you shall be saved, and all your house.

11:15  LA: Cum autem cœpissem loqui, cecidit Spiritus Sanctus super eos, sicut et in nos in initio.
       EN: And when I had begun to speak, the Holy Spirit fell upon them, as upon us also in the beginning.

11:16  LA: Recordatus sum autem verbi Domini, sicut dicebat: Joannes quidem baptizavit aqua, vos autem baptizabimini Spiritu Sancto.
       EN: And I remembered the word of the Lord, how that he said: John indeed baptized with water but you shall be baptized with the Holy Spirit.

11:17  LA: Si ergo eamdem gratiam dedit illis Deus, sicut et nobis qui credidimus in Dominum Jesum Christum: ego quis eram, qui possem prohibere Deum?
       EN: If then God gave them the same grace as to us also who believed in the Lord Jesus Christ: who was I, that could withstand God?

11:18  LA: His auditis, tacuerunt: et glorificaverunt Deum, dicentes: Ergo et gentibus pœnitentiam dedit Deus ad vitam.
       EN: Having heard these things, they held their peace and glorified God, saying: God then has also to the Gentiles given repentance, to life.

11:19  LA: Et illi quidem qui dispersi fuerant a tribulatione quæ facta fuerat sub Stephano, perambulaverunt usque Phœnicen, et Cyprum, et Antiochiam, nemini loquentes verbum, nisi solis Judæis.
       EN: Now they who had been dispersed by the persecution that arose on occasion of Stephen went about as far as Phenice and Cyprus and Antioch, speaking the word to none, but to the Jews only.

11:20  LA: Erant autem quidam ex eis viri Cyprii et Cyrenæi, qui cum introissent Antiochiam, loquebantur et ad Græcos, annuntiantes Dominum Jesum.
       EN: But some of them were men of Cyprus and Cyrene, who, when they had entered into Antioch, spoke also to the Greeks, preaching the Lord Jesus.

11:21  LA: Et erat manus Domini cum eis: multusque numerus credentium conversus est ad Dominum.
       EN: And the hand of he Lord was with them: and a great number believing, were converted to the Lord.

11:22  LA: Pervenit autem sermo ad aures ecclesiæ quæ erat Jerosolymis super istis: et miserunt Barnabam usque ad Antiochiam.
       EN: And the news came to the ears of the church that was at Jerusalem, touching these things: and they sent Barnabas as far as Antioch.

11:23  LA: Qui cum pervenisset, et vidisset gratiam Dei, gavisus est: et hortabatur omnes in proposito cordis permanere in Domino:
       EN: Who, when he had come and had seen the grace of God, rejoiced. And he exhorted them all with purpose of heart to continue in the Lord.

11:24  LA: quia erat vir bonus, et plenus Spiritu Sancto, et fide. Et apposita est multa turba Domino.
       EN: For he was a good man and full of the Holy Spirit and of faith. And a great multitude was added to the Lord.

11:25  LA: Profectus est autem Barnabas Tarsum, ut quæreret Saulum: quem cum invenisset, perduxit Antiochiam.
       EN: And Barnabas went to Tarsus to seek Saul: whom, when he had found, he brought to Antioch.

11:26  LA: Et annum totum conversati sunt ibi in ecclesia: et docuerunt turbam multam, ita ut cognominarentur primum Antiochiæ discipuli, christiani.
       EN: And they conversed there in the church a whole year: and they taught a great multitude, so that at Antioch the disciples were first named Christians.

11:27  LA: In his autem diebus supervenerunt ab Jerosolymis prophetæ Antiochiam:
       EN: And in these days there came prophets from Jerusalem to Antioch.

       EN: Confirming the souls of the disciples and exhorting them to continue in the faith: and that through many tribulations we must enter into the kingdom of God.

14:23  LA: Et cum constituissent illis per singulas ecclesias presbyteros, et orassent cum jejunationibus, commendaverunt eos Domino, in quem crediderunt.
       EN: And when they had ordained to them priests in every church and had prayed with fasting, they commended them to the Lord, in whom they believed.

14:24  LA: Transeuntesque Pisidiam, venerunt in Pamphyliam,
       EN: And passing through Pisidia, they came into Pamphylia.

14:25  LA: et loquentes verbum Domini in Perge, descenderunt in Attaliam:
       EN: And having spoken the word of the Lord in Perge, they went down into Attalia.

14:26  LA: et inde navigaverunt Antiochiam, unde erant traditi gratiæ Dei in opus quod compleverunt.
       EN: And from there they sailed to Antioch, from where they had been delivered to the grace of God, to the work which they accomplished.

14:27  LA: Cum autem venissent, et congregassent ecclesiam, retulerunt quanta fecisset Deus cum illis, et quia aperuisset gentibus ostium fidei.
       EN: And when they had come and had assembled the church, they related what great things God had done with them and how he had opened the door of faith to the Gentiles.

14:28  LA: Morati sunt autem tempus non modicum cum discipulis.
       EN: And they abode no small time with the disciples.

ACTS 15

15:1  LA: Et quidam descendentes de Judæa docebant fratres: Quia nisi circumcidamini secundum morem Moysi, non potestis salvari.
      EN: And some, coming down from Judea, taught the brothers: That, except you be circumcised after the manner of Moses, you cannot be saved.

15:2  LA: Facta ergo seditione non minima Paulo et Barnabæ adversus illos, statuerunt ut ascenderent Paulus et Barnabas, et quidam alii ex aliis ad Apostolos et presbyteros in Jerusalem super hac quæstione.
      EN: And when Paul and Barnabas had no small contest with them, they determined that Paul and Barnabas and certain others of the other side should go up to the apostles and priests to Jerusalem, about this question.

15:3  LA: Illi ergo deducti ab ecclesia pertransibant Phœnicen et Samariam, narrantes conversionem gentium: et faciebant gaudium magnum omnibus fratribus.
      EN: They therefore, being brought on their way by the church, passed through Phenice and Samaria, relating the conversion of the Gentiles. And they caused great joy to all the brothers.

15:4  LA: Cum autem venissent Jerosolymam, suscepti sunt ab ecclesia, et ab Apostolis et senioribus, annuntiantes quanta Deus fecisset cum illis.
      EN: And when they had come to Jerusalem, they were received by the church and by the apostles and ancients, declaring how great things God had done with them.

15:5  LA: Surrexerunt autem quidam de hæresi pharisæorum, qui crediderunt, dicentes quia oportet circumcidi eos, præcipere quoque servare legem Moysi.
      EN: But there arose of the sect of the Pharisees some that believed, saying: They must be circumcised and be commanded to observe the law of Moses.

15:6  LA: Conveneruntque Apostoli et seniores videre de verbo hoc.
      EN: And the apostles and ancients assembled to consider of this matter.

15:7  LA: Cum autem magna conquisitio fieret, surgens Petrus dixit ad eos: Viri fratres, vos scitis quoniam ab antiquis diebus Deus in nobis elegit, per os meum audire gentes verbum Evangelii et credere.
      EN: After much discussion, Peter stood up and said to them, “Brothers, you know that long ago God chose among us that the Gentiles should hear the word of the gospel through my mouth and believe.

15:8  LA: Et qui novit corda Deus, testimonium perhibuit, dans illis Spiritum Sanctum, sicut et nobis,
      EN: And God, who knows hearts, bore witness to them, giving them the Holy Spirit just as he did to us.

15:9  LA: et nihil discrevit inter nos et illos, fide purificans corda eorum.
      EN: He made no distinction between us and them, cleansing their hearts by faith.

15:10  LA: Nunc ergo quid tentatis Deum, imponere jugum super cervices discipulorum quod neque patres nostri, neque nos portare potuimus?
       EN: Now, then, why do you test God by placing upon the disciples’ necks a yoke that neither our fathers nor we have been able to bear?

15:11  LA: sed per gratiam Domini Jesu Christi credimus salvari, quemadmodum et illi.
       EN: Rather, we believe that we are saved through the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, just as they are.”

15:12  LA: Tacuit autem omnis multitudo: et audiebant Barnabam et Paulum narrantes quanta Deus fecisset signa et prodigia in gentibus per eos.
       EN: And all the multitude held their peace: and they heard Barnabas and Paul telling what great signs and wonders God had wrought among the Gentiles by them.

15:13  LA: Et postquam tacuerunt, respondit Jacobus, dicens: Viri fratres, audite me.
       EN: And after they had held their peace, James answered, saying: Men, brothers, hear me.

15:14  LA: Simon narravit quemadmodum primum Deus visitavit sumere ex gentibus populum nomini suo.
       EN: Simon has related how God first visited to take of the Gentiles, a people to his name.

15:15  LA: Et huic concordant verba prophetarum: sicut scriptum est:
       EN: And to this agree the words of the prophets, as it is written:

15:16  LA: Post hæc revertar, et reædificabo tabernaculum David quod decidit: et diruta ejus reædificabo, et erigam illud:
       EN: After these things I will return and will rebuild the tabernacle of David, which is fallen down: and the ruins of it I will rebuild. And I will set it up:

15:17  LA: ut requirant ceteri hominum Dominum, et omnes gentes super quas invocatum est nomen meum, dicit Dominus faciens hæc.
       EN: That the residue of men may seek after the Lord, and all nations upon whom my name is invoked, says the Lord, who does these things.

15:18  LA: Notum a sæculo est Domino opus suum.
       EN: To the Lord was his own work known from the beginning of the world.

15:19  LA: Propter quod ego judico non inquietari eos qui ex gentibus convertuntur ad Deum,
       EN: For which cause I judge that they who from among the Gentiles are converted to God are not to be disquieted:

15:20  LA: sed scribere ad eos ut abstineant se a contaminationibus simulacrorum, et fornicatione, et suffocatis, et sanguine.
       EN: But that we write to them, that they refrain themselves from the pollutions of idols and from sexual immorality and from things strangled and from blood.

15:21  LA: Moyses enim a temporibus antiquis habet in singulis civitatibus qui eum prædicent in synagogis, ubi per omne sabbatum legitur.
       EN: For Moses of old time has in every city them that preach him in the synagogues, where he is read every sabbath.

15:22  LA: Tunc placuit Apostolis et senioribus cum omni ecclesia eligere viros ex eis, et mittere Antiochiam cum Paulo et Barnaba: Judam, qui cognominabatur Barsabas, et Silam, viros primos in fratribus:
       EN: Then it pleased the apostles and ancients, with the whole church, to choose men of their own company and to send to Antioch with Paul and Barnabas, namely, Judas, who was surnamed Barsabas, and Silas, chief men among the brothers.

15:23  LA: scribentes per manus eorum: Apostoli et seniores fratres, his qui sunt Antiochiæ, et Syriæ, et Ciliciæ, fratribus ex gentibus, salutem.
       EN: Writing by their hands: The apostles and ancients, brothers, to the brothers of the Gentiles that are at Antioch and in Syria and Cilicia, greeting.

15:24  LA: Quoniam audivimus quia quidam ex nobis exeuntes, turbaverunt vos verbis, evertentes animas vestras, quibus non mandavimus,
       EN: Since we have heard that some going out from us have troubled you with words, subverting your souls, to whom we gave no commandment:

15:25  LA: placuit nobis collectis in unum eligere viros, et mittere ad vos cum carissimis nostris Barnaba et Paulo,
       EN: It has seemed good to us, being assembled together, to choose out men and to send them to you, with our well beloved Barnabas and Paul:

15:26  LA: hominibus qui tradiderunt animas suas pro nomine Domini nostri Jesu Christi.
       EN: Men that have given their lives for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

15:27  LA: Misimus ergo Judam et Silam, qui et ipsi vobis verbis referent eadem.
       EN: We have sent therefore Judas and Silas, who themselves also will, by word of mouth, tell you the same things.

15:28  LA: Visum est enim Spiritui Sancto et nobis nihil ultra imponere vobis oneris quam hæc necessaria:
       EN: “For it has seemed good to the Holy Spirit and to us to lay upon you no burden beyond these necessary things:

15:29  LA: ut abstineatis vos ab immolatis simulacrorum, et sanguine, et suffocato, et fornicatione: a quibus custodientes vos, bene agetis. Valete.
       EN: that you abstain from things sacrificed to idols, from blood, from what has been strangled, and from sexual immorality. If you keep yourselves from these, you will do well. Farewell.”

15:30  LA: Illi ergo dimissi, descenderunt Antiochiam: et congregata multitudine tradiderunt epistolam.
       EN: They therefore, being dismissed, went down to Antioch and, gathering together the multitude, delivered the epistle.

15:31  LA: Quam cum legissent, gavisi sunt super consolatione.
       EN: Which when they had read, they rejoiced for the consolation.

15:32  LA: Judas autem et Silas, et ipsi cum essent prophetæ, verbo plurimo consolati sunt fratres, et confirmaverunt.
       EN: But Judas and Silas, being prophets also themselves, with many words comforted the brothers and confirmed them.

15:33  LA: Facto autem ibi aliquanto tempore, dimissi sunt cum pace a fratribus ad eos qui miserant illos.
       EN: And after they had spent some time there, they were let go with peace by the brothers to them that had sent them.

15:34  LA: Visum est autem Silæ ibi remanere: Judas autem solus abiit Jerusalem.
       EN: But it seemed good to Silas to remain there: and Judas alone departed to Jerusalem.

15:35  LA: Paulus autem et Barnabas demorabantur Antiochiæ, docentes et evangelizantes cum aliis pluribus verbum Domini.
       EN: And Paul and Barnabas continued at Antioch, teaching and preaching, with many others, the word of the Lord.

15:36  LA: Post aliquot autem dies, dixit ad Barnabam Paulus: Revertentes visitemus fratres per universas civitates in quibus prædicavimus verbum Domini, quomodo se habeant.
       EN: And after some days, Paul said to Barnabas: Let us return and visit our brothers in all the cities wherein we have preached the word of the Lord, to see how they do.

15:37  LA: Barnabas autem volebat secum assumere et Joannem, qui cognominabatur Marcus.
       EN: And Barnabas would have taken with them John also, that was surnamed Mark.

15:38  LA: Paulus autem rogabat eum (ut qui discessisset ab eis de Pamphylia, et non isset cum eis in opus) non debere recipi.
       EN: But Paul desired that he (as having departed from them out of Pamphylia and not gone with them to the work) might not be received.

15:39  LA: Facta est autem dissensio, ita ut discederent ab invicem, et Barnabas quidem, assumpto Marco, navigaret Cyprum.
       EN: And there arose a dissension so that they departed one from another. And Barnabas indeed, taking Mark, sailed to Cyprus.

15:40  LA: Paulus vero, electo Sila, profectus est, traditus gratiæ Dei a fratribus.
       EN: But Paul, choosing Silas, departed, being delivered by the brothers to the grace of God.

15:41  LA: Perambulabat autem Syriam et Ciliciam, confirmans ecclesias: præcipiens custodire præcepta Apostolorum et seniorum.
       EN: And he went through Syria and Cilicia, confirming the churches, commanding them to keep the precepts of the apostles and the ancients.

ACTS 16

16:1  LA: Pervenit autem Derben et Lystram. Et ecce discipulus quidam erat ibi nomine Timotheus, filius mulieris Judææ fidelis, patre gentili.
      EN: And he came to Derbe and Lystra. And behold, there was a certain disciple there named Timothy, the son of a Jewish woman that believed: but his father was a Gentile.

16:2  LA: Huic testimonium bonum reddebant qui in Lystris erant et Iconio fratres.
      EN: To this man the brothers that were in Lystra and Iconium gave a good testimony.

16:3  LA: Hunc voluit Paulus secum proficisci: et assumens circumcidit eum propter Judæos qui erant in illis locis. Sciebant enim omnes quod pater ejus erat gentilis.
      EN: Him Paul would have to go along with him: and taking him, he circumcised him, because of the Jews who were in those places. For they all knew that his father was a Gentile.

16:4  LA: Cum autem pertransirent civitates, tradebant eis custodiri dogmata quæ erant decreta ab Apostolis et senioribus qui erant Jerosolymis.
      EN: And as they passed through the cities, they delivered to them the decrees for to keep, that were decreed by the apostles and ancients who were at Jerusalem.

16:5  LA: Et ecclesiæ quidem confirmabantur fide, et abundabunt numero quotidie.
      EN: And the churches were confirmed in faith and increased in number daily.

16:6  LA: Transeuntes autem Phrygiam et Galatiæ regionem, vetati sunt a Spiritu Sancto loqui verbum Dei in Asia.
      EN: And when they had passed through Phrygia and the country of Galatia, they were forbidden by the Holy Spirit to preach the word in Asia.

16:7  LA: Cum venissent autem in Mysiam, tentabant ire in Bithyniam: et non permisit eos Spiritus Jesu.
      EN: And when they had come into Mysia, they attempted to go into Bithynia: and the Spirit of Jesus did not allow them.

16:8  LA: Cum autem pertransissent Mysiam, descenderunt Troadem:
      EN: And when they had passed through Mysia, they went down to Troas.

16:9  LA: et visio per noctem Paulo ostensa est: vir Macedo quidam erat stans et deprecans eum, et dicens: Transiens in Macedoniam, adjuva nos.
      EN: And a vision was showed to Paul in the night, which was a man of Macedonia standing and imploring him and saying: Pass over into Macedonia and help us.

16:10  LA: Ut autem visum vidit, statim quæsivimus proficisci in Macedoniam, certi facti quod vocasset nos Deus evangelizare eis.
       EN: And as soon as he had seen the vision, immediately we sought to go into Macedonia: being assured that God had called us to preach the gospel to them.

16:11  LA: Navigantes autem a Troade, recto cursu venimus Samothraciam, et sequenti die Neapolim:
       EN: And sailing from Troas, we came with a straight course to Samothracia, and the day following to Neapolis.

16:12  LA: et inde Philippos, quæ est prima partis Macedoniæ civitas, colonia. Eramus autem in hac urbe diebus aliquot, conferentes.
       EN: And from there to Philippi, which is the chief city of part of Macedonia, a colony. And we were in this city some days conferring together.

16:13  LA: Die autem sabbatorum egressi sumus foras portam juxta flumen, ubi videbatur oratio esse: et sedentes loquebamur mulieribus quæ convenerant.
       EN: And upon the Sabbath day, we went forth without the gate by a river side, where it seemed that there was prayer: and sitting down, we spoke to the women that were assembled.

16:14  LA: Et quædam mulier nomine Lydia, purpuraria civitatis Thyatirenorum, colens Deum, audivit: cujus Dominus aperuit cor intendere his quæ dicebantur a Paulo.
       EN: And a certain woman named Lydia, a seller of purple, of the city of Thyatira, one that worshipped God, did hear: whose heart the Lord opened to attend to those things which were said by Paul.

16:15  LA: Cum autem baptizata esset, et domus ejus, deprecata est, dicens: Si judicastis me fidelem Domino esse, introite in domum meam, et manete. Et coëgit nos.
       EN: And when she was baptized, and her household, she begged us, saying: If you have judged me to be faithful to the Lord, come into my house and abide there. And she constrained us.

16:16  LA: Factum est autem euntibus nobis ad orationem, puellam quamdam habentem spiritum pythonem obviare nobis, quæ quæstum magnum præstabat dominis suis divinando.
       EN: And it happened, as we went to prayer, a certain girl having a pythonical spirit met us, who brought to her masters much gain by divining.

16:17  LA: Hæc subsecuta Paulum et nos, clamabat dicens: Isti homines servi Dei excelsi sunt, qui annuntiant vobis viam salutis.
       EN: This same following Paul and us, cried out, saying: These men are the servants of the Most High God, who preach to you the way of salvation.

16:18  LA: Hoc autem faciebat multis diebus. Dolens autem Paulus, et conversus, spiritui dixit: Præcipio tibi in nomine Jesu Christi exire ab ea. Et exiit eadem hora.
       EN: And this she did many days. But Paul being grieved, turned and said to the spirit: I command you, in the name of Jesus Christ, to go out from her. And he went out the same hour.

16:19  LA: Videntes autem domini ejus quia exivit spes quæstus eorum, apprehendentes Paulum et Silam, perduxerunt in forum ad principes:
       EN: But her masters, seeing that the hope of their gain had gone, apprehending Paul and Silas, brought them into the market place to the rulers.

16:20  LA: et offerentes eos magistratibus, dixerunt: Hi homines conturbant civitatem nostram, cum sint Judæi:
       EN: And presenting them to the magistrates, they said: These men disturb our city, being Jews:

16:21  LA: et annuntiant morem quem non licet nobis suscipere neque facere, cum simus Romani.
       EN: And preach a fashion which it is not lawful for us to receive nor observe, being Romans.

16:22  LA: Et cucurrit plebs adversus eos: et magistratus, scissis tunicis eorum, jusserunt eos virgis cædi.
       EN: And the people ran together against them: and the magistrates, rending off their clothes, commanded them to be beaten with rods.

16:23  LA: Et cum multas plagas eis imposuissent, miserunt eos in carcerem, præcipientes custodi ut diligenter custodiret eos.
       EN: And when they had laid many stripes upon them, they cast them into prison, charging the gaoler to keep them diligently.

16:24  LA: Qui cum tale præceptum accepisset, misit eos in interiorem carcerem, et pedes eorum strinxit ligno.
       EN: Who having received such a charge, thrust them into the inner prison and made their feet fast in the stocks.

16:25  LA: Media autem nocte Paulus et Silas orantes, laudabant Deum: et audiebant eos qui in custodia erant.
       EN: And at midnight, Paul and Silas, praying, praised God. And they that were in prison heard them.

16:26  LA: Subito vero terræmotus factus est magnus, ita ut moverentur fundamenta carceris. Et statim aperta sunt omnia ostia: et universorum vincula soluta sunt.
       EN: And suddenly there was a great earthquake, so that the foundations of the prison were shaken. And immediately all the doors were opened and the bands of all were loosed.

16:27  LA: Expergefactus autem custos carceris, et videns januas apertas carceris, evaginato gladio volebat se interficere, æstimans fugisse vinctos.
       EN: And the keeper of the prison, awakening out of his sleep and seeing the doors of the prison open, drawing his sword, would have killed himself, supposing that the prisoners had been fled.

16:28  LA: Clamavit autem Paulus voce magna, dicens: Nihil tibi mali feceris: universi enim hic sumus.
       EN: But Paul cried with a loud voice, saying: Do yourself no harm, for we all are here.

16:29  LA: Petitoque lumine, introgressus est: et tremefactus procidit Paulo et Silæ ad pedes:
       EN: Then calling for a light, he went in: and trembling, fell down at the feet of Paul and Silas.

16:30  LA: et producens eos foras, ait: Domini, quid me oportet facere, ut salvus fiam?
       EN: And bringing them out, he said: Masters, what must I do, that I may be saved?

16:31  LA: At illi dixerunt: Crede in Dominum Jesum, et salvus eris tu, et domus tua.
       EN: But they said: believe in the Lord Jesus: and you shall be saved, and your house.

16:32  LA: Et locuti sunt ei verbum Domini cum omnibus qui erant in domo ejus.
       EN: And they preached the word of the Lord to him and to all that were in his house.

16:33  LA: Et tollens eos in illa hora noctis, lavit plagas eorum: et baptizatus est ipse, et omnis domus ejus continuo.
       EN: And he, taking them the same hour of the night, washed their stripes: and himself was baptized, and all his house immediately.

16:34  LA: Cumque perduxisset eos in domum suam, apposuit eis mensam, et lætatus est cum omni domo sua credens Deo.
       EN: And when he had brought them into his own house, he laid the table for them: and rejoiced with all his house, believing God.

16:35  LA: Et cum dies factus esset, miserunt magistratus lictores, dicentes: Dimitte homines illos.
       EN: And when the day had come, the magistrates sent the serjeants, saying: Let those men go.

16:36  LA: Nuntiavit autem custos carceris verba hæc Paulo: Quia miserunt magistratus ut dimittamini: nunc igitur exeuntes, ite in pace.
       EN: And the keeper of the prison told these words to Paul: The magistrates have sent to let you go. Now therefore depart. And go in peace.

16:37  LA: Paulus autem dixit eis: Cæsos nos publice, indemnatos homines Romanos, miserunt in carcerem: et nunc occulte nos ejiciunt? Non ita: sed veniant,
       EN: But Paul said to them: They have beaten us publicly, uncondemned, men that are Romans, and have cast us into prison. And now do they thrust us out privately? Not so: but let them come.

16:38  LA: et ipsi nos ejiciant. Nuntiaverunt autem magistratibus lictores verba hæc. Timueruntque audito quod Romani essent:
       EN: And let us out themselves. And the serjeants told these words to the magistrates. And they were afraid: hearing that they were Romans.

16:39  LA: et venientes deprecati sunt eos, et educentes rogabant ut egrederentur de urbe.
       EN: And coming, they begged them: and bringing them out, they desired them to depart out of the city.

16:40  LA: Exeuntes autem de carcere, introierunt ad Lydiam: et visis fratribus consolati sunt eos, et profecti sunt.
       EN: And they went out of the prison and entered into the house of Lydia: and having seen the brothers, they comforted them and departed.

ACTS 17

17:1  LA: Cum autem perambulassent Amphipolim et Apolloniam, venerunt Thessalonicam, ubi erat synagoga Judæorum.
      EN: And when they had passed through Amphipolis and Apollonia, they came to Thessalonica, where there was a synagogue of the Jews.

17:2  LA: Secundum consuetudinem autem Paulus introivit ad eos, et per sabbata tria disserebat eis de Scripturis,
      EN: And Paul, according to his custom, went in to them. And for three sabbath days he reasoned with them out of the scriptures:

17:3  LA: adaperiens et insinuans quia Christum oportuit pati, et resurgere a mortuis: et quia hic est Jesus Christus, quem ego annuntio vobis.
      EN: Declaring and insinuating that the Christ was to suffer and to rise again from the dead; and that this is Jesus Christ, whom I preach to you.

17:4  LA: Et quidam ex eis crediderunt et adjuncti sunt Paulo et Silæ: et de colentibus gentilibusque multitudo magna, et mulieres nobiles non paucæ.
      EN: And some of them believed and were associated to Paul and Silas: and of those that served God and of the Gentiles a great multitude: and of noble women not a few.

17:5  LA: Zelantes autem Judæi, assumentesque de vulgo viros quosdam malos, et turba facta, concitaverunt civitatem: et assistentes domui Jasonis quærebant eos producere in populum.
      EN: But the Jews, moved with envy and taking to them some wicked men of the vulgar sort and making a tumult, set the city in an uproar: and besetting Jason’s house, sought to bring them out to the people.

17:6  LA: Et cum non invenissent eos, trahebant Jasonem et quosdam fratres ad principes civitatis, clamantes: Quoniam hi qui urbem concitant, et huc venerunt,
      EN: And not finding them, they drew Jason and certain brothers to the rulers of the city, crying: They that set the city in an uproar have come here also:

17:7  LA: quos suscepit Jason, et hi omnes contra decreta Cæsaris faciunt, regem alium dicentes esse, Jesum.
      EN: Whom Jason has received. And these all do contrary to the decrees of Caesar, saying that there is another king, Jesus.

17:8  LA: Concitaverunt autem plebem et principes civitatis audientes hæc.
      EN: And they stirred up the people: and the rulers of the city, hearing these things,

17:9  LA: Et accepta satisfactione a Jasone et a ceteris, dimiserunt eos.
      EN: And having taken satisfaction of Jason and of the rest, they let them go.

17:10  LA: Fratres vero confestim per noctem dimiserunt Paulum et Silam in Berœam. Qui cum venissent, in synagogam Judæorum introierunt.
       EN: But the brothers immediately sent away Paul and Silas by night to Berea. Who, when they had come there, went into the synagogue of the Jews.

17:11  LA: Hi autem erant nobiliores eorum qui sunt Thessalonicæ, qui susceperunt verbum cum omni aviditate, quotidie scrutantes Scripturas, si hæc ita se haberent.
       EN: Now these were more noble than those in Thessalonica, who received the word with all eagerness, daily searching the scriptures, whether these things were so.

17:12  LA: Et multi quidem crediderunt ex eis, et mulierum gentilium honestarum, et viri non pauci.
       EN: And many indeed of them believed: and of honorable women that were Gentiles and of men, not a few.

17:13  LA: Cum autem cognovissent in Thessalonica Judæi quia et Berœæ prædicatum est a Paulo verbum Dei, venerunt et illuc commoventes, et turbantes multitudinem.
       EN: And when the Jews of Thessalonica had knowledge that the word of God was also preached by Paul at Berea, they came there also, stirring up and troubling the multitude.

17:14  LA: Statimque tunc Paulum dimiserunt fratres, ut iret usque ad mare: Silas autem et Timotheus remanserunt ibi.
       EN: And then immediately the brothers sent away Paul, to go to the sea: but Silas and Timothy remained there.

17:15  LA: Qui autem deducebant Paulum, perduxerunt eum usque Athenas, et accepto mandato ab eo ad Silam et Timotheum ut quam celeriter venirent ad illum, profecti sunt.
       EN: And they that conducted Paul brought him as far as Athens: and receiving a commandment from him to Silas and Timothy, that they should come to him with all speed, they departed.

       EN: That they should seek God, if perhaps they may feel after him or find him, although he do not be far from each of us.

       EN: So Paul went out from among them.

ACTS 23

23:1  LA: Intendens autem in concilium Paulus, ait: Viri fratres, ego omni conscientia bona conversatus sum ante Deum usque in hodiernum diem.
      EN: And Paul, looking upon the council, said: Men, brothers, I have conversed with all good conscience before God until this present day.

23:2  LA: Princeps autem sacerdotum Ananias præcepit astantibus sibi percutere os ejus.
      EN: And the high priest, Ananias, commanded them that stood by him to strike him on the mouth.

23:3  LA: Tunc Paulus dixit ad eum: Percutiet te Deus, paries dealbate. Et tu sedens judicas me secundum legem, et contra legem jubes me percuti?
      EN: Then Paul said to him: God shall strike you, you whitewashed wall. For, sit you to judge me according to the law and, contrary to the law, command me to be struck?

23:4  LA: Et qui astabant dixerunt: Summum sacerdotem Dei maledicis.
      EN: And they that stood by said: Do you revile the high priest of God?

23:5  LA: Dixit autem Paulus: Nesciebam, fratres, quia princeps est sacerdotum. Scriptum est enim: Principem populi tui non maledices.
      EN: And Paul said: I knew not, brothers, that he is the high priest. For it is written: You shall not speak evil of the prince of your people.

23:6  LA: Sciens autem Paulus quia una pars esset sadducæorum, et altera pharisæorum, exclamavit in concilio: Viri fratres, ego pharisæus sum, filius pharisæorum: de spe et resurrectione mortuorum ego judicor.
      EN: And Paul, knowing that the one part were Sadducees and the other Pharisees, cried out in the council: Men, brothers, I am a Pharisee, the son of Pharisees: concerning the hope and resurrection of the dead I am called in question.

23:7  LA: Et cum hæc dixisset, facta est dissensio inter pharisæos et sadducæos, et soluta est multitudo.
      EN: And when he had so said, there arose a dissension between the Pharisees and the Sadducees. And the multitude was divided.

23:8  LA: Sadducæi enim dicunt non esse resurrectionem, neque angelum, neque spiritum: pharisæi autem utraque confitentur.
      EN: For the Sadducees say that there is no resurrection, neither angel, nor spirit: but the Pharisees confess both.

23:9  LA: Factus est autem clamor magnus. Et surgentes quidam pharisæorum, pugnabant, dicentes: Nihil mali invenimus in homine isto: quid si spiritus locutus est ei, aut angelus?
      EN: And there arose a great cry. And some of the Pharisees rising up, strove, saying: We find no evil in this man. What if a spirit has spoken to him, or an angel?

23:10  LA: Et cum magna dissensio facta esset, timens tribunus ne discerperetur Paulus ab ipsis, jussit milites descendere, et rapere eum de medio eorum, ac deducere eum in castra.
       EN: And when there arose a great dissension, the tribune, fearing lest Paul should be pulled in pieces by them, commanded the soldiers to go down and to take him by force from among them and to bring him into the castle.

23:11  LA: Sequenti autem nocte assistens ei Dominus, ait: Constans esto: sicut enim testificatus es de me in Jerusalem, sic te oportet et Romæ testificari.
       EN: And the night following, the Lord standing by him, said: Be constant: for as you have testified of me in Jerusalem, so must you bear witness also at Rome.

23:12  LA: Facta autem die collegerunt se quidam ex Judæis, et devoverunt, se dicentes neque manducaturos, neque bibituros donec occiderent Paulum.
       EN: And when day had come, some of the Jews gathered together and bound themselves under a curse, saying that they would neither eat nor drink until they killed Paul.

23:13  LA: Erant autem plus quam quadraginta viri qui hanc conjurationem fecerant:
       EN: And they were more than forty men that had made this conspiracy.

23:14  LA: qui accesserunt ad principes sacerdotum et seniores, et dixerunt: Devotione devovimus nos nihil gustaturos, donec occidamus Paulum.
       EN: Who came to the chief priests and the ancients and said: We have bound ourselves under a great curse that we will eat nothing until we have slain Paul.

23:15  LA: Nunc ergo vos notum facite tribuno cum concilio, ut producat illum ad vos, tamquam aliquid certius cognituri de eo. Nos vero priusquam appropiet, parati sumus interficere illum.
       EN: Now therefore do you with the council signify to the tribune, that he bring him forth to you, as if you meant to know something more certain touching him. And we, before he come near, are ready to kill him.

23:16  LA: Quod cum audisset filius sororis Pauli insidias, venit, et intravit in castra, nuntiavitque Paulo.
       EN: Which when Paul’s sister’s son had heard, of their lying in wait, he came and entered into the castle and told Paul.

23:17  LA: Vocans autem Paulus ad se unum ex centurionibus, ait: Adolescentem hunc perduc ad tribunum, habet enim aliquid indicare illi.
       EN: And Paul, calling to him one of the centurions, said: Bring this young man to the tribune: for he has some thing to tell him.

23:18  LA: Et ille quidem assumens eum duxit ad tribunum, et ait: Vinctus Paulus rogavit me hunc adolescentem perducere ad te, habentem aliquid loqui tibi.
       EN: And he, taking him, brought him to the tribune and said: Paul, the prisoner, desired me to bring this young man to you, who has some thing to say to you.

23:19  LA: Apprehendens autem tribunus manum illius, secessit cum eo seorsum, et interrogavit illum: Quid est quod habes indicare mihi?
       EN: And the tribune, taking him by the hand, went aside with him privately and asked him: What is it that you have to tell me?

23:20  LA: Ille autem dixit: Judæis convenit rogare te ut crastina die producas Paulum in concilium, quasi aliquid certius inquisituri sint de illo:
       EN: And he said: The Jews have agreed to desire you that you would bring forth Paul to-next day into the council, as if they meant to inquire some thing more certain touching him.

23:21  LA: tu vero ne credideris illis: insidiantur enim ei ex eis viri amplius quam quadraginta, qui se devoverunt non manducare, neque bibere donec interficiant eum: et nunc parati sunt, exspectantes promissum tuum.
       EN: But do not you give credit to them: for there lie in wait for him more than forty men of them, who have bound themselves by oath neither to eat nor to drink, until they have killed him. And they are now ready, looking for a promise from you.

23:22  LA: Tribunus igitur dimisit adolescentem, præcipiens ne cui loqueretur quoniam hæc nota sibi fecisset.
       EN: The tribune therefore dismissed the young man, charging him that he should tell no one that he had made known these things to him.

23:23  LA: Et vocatis duobus centurionibus, dixit illis: Parate milites ducentos ut eant usque Cæsaream, et equites septuaginta, et lancearios ducentos a tertia hora noctis,
       EN: Then having called two centurions, he said to them: Make ready two hundred soldiers to go as far as Cæsarea: and seventy horsemen and two hundred spearmen, for the third hour of the night.

23:24  LA: et jumenta præparate ut imponentes Paulum, salvum perducerent ad Felicem præsidem.
       EN: And provide beasts, that they may set Paul on and bring him safe to Felix the governor.

23:25  LA: (Timuit enim ne forte raperent eum Judæi, et occiderent, et ipse postea calumniam sustineret, tamquam accepturus pecuniam.)
       EN: (For he feared lest the Jews might take him away by force and kill him: and he should afterwards be slandered, as if he was to take money.) And he wrote a letter after this manner:

23:26  LA: Scribens epistolam continentem hæc: Claudius Lysias optimo præsidi Felici, salutem.
       EN: Claudius Lysias to the most excellent governor, Felix, greeting:

23:27  LA: Virum hunc comprehensum a Judæis, et incipientem interfici ab eis, superveniens cum exercitu eripui, cognito quia Romanus est.
       EN: This man, being taken by the Jews and ready to be killed by them, I rescued, coming in with an army, understanding that he is a Roman.

23:28  LA: Volensque scire causam quam objiciebant illi, deduxi eum in concilium eorum.
       EN: And meaning to know the cause which they objected to him, I brought him forth into their council.

23:29  LA: Quem inveni accusari de quæstionibus legis ipsorum, nihil vero dignum morte aut vinculis habentem criminis.
       EN: Whom I found to be accused concerning questions of their law; but having nothing laid to his charge worthy of death or of bands.

23:30  LA: Et cum mihi perlatum esset de insidiis quas paraverant illi, misi eum ad te, denuntians et accusatoribus ut dicant apud te. Vale.
       EN: And when I was told of ambushes that they had prepared for him, I sent him to you, signifying also to his accusers to plead before you. Farewell.

23:31  LA: Milites ergo secundum præceptum sibi assumentes Paulum, duxerunt per noctem in Antipatridem.
       EN: Then the soldiers, just as it was commanded them, taking Paul, brought him by night to Antipatris.

23:32  LA: Et postera die dimissis equitibus ut cum eo irent, reversi sunt ad castra.
       EN: And the next day, leaving the horsemen to go with him, they returned to the castle.

23:33  LA: Qui cum venissent Cæsaream, et tradidissent epistolam præsidi, statuerunt ante illum et Paulum.
       EN: Who, when they had come to Cæsarea and had delivered the letter to the governor, did also present Paul before him.

23:34  LA: Cum legisset autem, et interrogasset de qua provincia esset, et cognoscens quia de Cilicia:
       EN: And when he had read it and had asked of what province he was and understood that he was of Cilicia:

23:35  LA: Audiam te, inquit, cum accusatores tui venerint. Jussitque in prætorio Herodis custodiri eum.
       EN: I will hear you, said he, when your accusers come. And he commanded him to be kept in Herod’s judgment hall.

ACTS 24

24:1  LA: Post quinque autem dies descendit princeps sacerdotum Ananias, cum senioribus quibusdam, et Tertullo quodam oratore, qui adierunt præsidem adversus Paulum.
      EN: And after five days, the high priest, Ananias, came down with some ancients and one Tertullus, an orator, who went to the governor against Paul.

24:2  LA: Et citato Paulo cœpit accusare Tertullus, dicens: Cum in multa pace agamus per te, et multa corrigantur per tuam providentiam,
      EN: And Paul being called for, Tertullus began to accuse him, saying: Whereas, through you we live in much peace and many things are rectified by your providence,

24:3  LA: semper et ubique suscipimus, optime Felix, cum omni gratiarum actione.
      EN: We accept it always and in all places, most excellent Felix, with all thanksgiving.

24:4  LA: Ne diutius autem te protraham, oro, breviter audias nos pro tua clementia.
      EN: But that I be no further tedious to you, I desire you of your clemency to hear us in a few words.

24:5  LA: Invenimus hunc hominem pestiferum, et concitantem seditiones omnibus Judæis in universo orbe, et auctorem seditionis sectæ Nazarenorum:
      EN: We have found this to be a pestilent man and raising seditions among all the Jews throughout the world: and author of the sedition of the sect of the Nazarenes.

24:6  LA: qui etiam templum violare conatus est, quem et apprehensum voluimus secundum legem nostram judicare.
      EN: Who also has gone about to profane the temple: whom, we having apprehended, would also have judged according to our law.

24:7  LA: Superveniens autem tribunus Lysias, cum vi magna eripuit eum de manibus nostris,
      EN: But Lysias the tribune, coming upon us with great violence, took him away out of our hands;

24:8  LA: jubens accusatores ejus ad te venire: a quo poteris ipse judicans, de omnibus istis cognoscere, de quibus nos accusamus eum.
      EN: Commanding his accusers to come to you. Of whom you may yourself, by examination, have knowledge of all these things whereof we accuse him.

24:9  LA: Adjecerunt autem et Judæi, dicentes hæc ita se habere.
      EN: And the Jews also added and said that these things were so.

24:10  LA: Respondit autem Paulus (annuente sibi præside dicere): Ex multis annis te esse judicem genti huic sciens, bono animo pro me satisfaciam.
       EN: Then Paul answered (the governor making a sign to him to speak): Knowing that for many years you have been judge over this nation, I will with good courage answer for myself.

24:11  LA: Potes enim cognoscere quia non plus sunt mihi dies quam duodecim, ex quo ascendi adorare in Jerusalem:
       EN: For you may understand that there are yet but twelve days since I went up to adore in Jerusalem:

24:12  LA: et neque in templo invenerunt me cum aliquo disputantem, aut concursum facientem turbæ, neque in synagogis, neque in civitate:
       EN: And neither in the temple did they find me disputing with any man or causing any concourse of the people: neither in the synagogues, nor in the city.

24:13  LA: neque probare possunt tibi de quibus nunc me accusant.
       EN: Neither can they prove to you the things whereof they now accuse me.

24:14  LA: Confiteor autem hoc tibi, quod secundum sectam quam dicunt hæresim, sic deservio Patri et Deo meo, credens omnibus quæ in lege et prophetis scripta sunt:
       EN: But this I confess to you that according to the way which they call a heresy, so do I serve the Father and my God, believing all things which are written in the law and the prophets:

24:15  LA: spem habens in Deum, quam et hi ipsi exspectant, resurrectionem futuram justorum et iniquorum.
       EN: Having hope in God, which these also themselves look for, that there shall be a resurrection of the just and unjust.

24:16  LA: In hoc et ipse studeo sine offendiculo conscientiam habere ad Deum et ad homines semper.
       EN: And herein do I endeavour to have always a conscience without offence, towards God and towards men.

24:17  LA: Post annos autem plures eleemosynas facturus in gentem meam, veni, et oblationes, et vota,
       EN: Now after many years, I came to bring alms to my nation and offerings and vows.

24:18  LA: in quibus invenerunt me purificatum in templo: non cum turba, neque cum tumultu.
       EN: In which I was found purified in the temple: neither with multitude nor with tumult.

24:19  LA: Quidam autem ex Asia Judæi, quos oportebat apud te præsto esse, et accusare si quid haberent adversum me:
       EN: But certain Jews of Asia, who ought to be present before you and to accuse, if they had anything against me:

24:20  LA: aut hi ipsi dicant si quid invenerunt in me iniquitatis cum stem in concilio,
       EN: Or let these men themselves say if they found in me any iniquity, when standing before the council,

24:21  LA: nisi de una hac solummodo voce qua clamavi inter eos stans: Quoniam de resurrectione mortuorum ego judicor hodie a vobis.
       EN: Except it be for this one voice only that I cried, standing among them: Concerning the resurrection of the dead am I judged this day by you.

24:22  LA: Distulit autem illos Felix, certissime sciens de via hac, dicens: Cum tribunus Lysias descenderit, audiam vos.
       EN: And Felix put them off, having most certain knowledge of this way, saying: When Lysias the tribune shall come down, I will hear you.

24:23  LA: Jussitque centurioni custodire eum, et habere requiem, nec quemquam de suis prohibere ministrare ei.
       EN: And he commanded a centurion to keep him: and that he should be easy and that he should not prohibit any of his friends to minister to him.

24:24  LA: Post aliquot autem dies veniens Felix cum Drusilla uxore sua, quæ erat Judæa, vocavit Paulum, et audivit ab eo fidem quæ est in Christum Jesum.
       EN: And after some days, Felix, coming with Drusilla his wife, who was a Jew, sent for Paul and heard of him the faith that is in Christ Jesus.

24:25  LA: Disputante autem illo de justitia, et castitate, et de judicio futuro, tremefactus Felix, respondit: Quod nunc attinet, vade: tempore autem opportuno accersam te:
       EN: And as he treated of justice and chastity and of the judgment to come, Felix, being terrified, answered: For this time, go your way: but when I have a convenient time, I will send for you.

24:26  LA: simul et sperans quod pecunia ei daretur a Paulo, propter quod et frequenter accersens eum, loquebatur cum eo.
       EN: Hoping also also that money should be given him by Paul: for which cause also oftentimes sending for him, he spoke with him.

24:27  LA: Biennio autem expleto, accepit successorem Felix Portium Festum. Volens autem gratiam præstare Judæis Felix, reliquit Paulum vinctum.
       EN: But when two years were ended, Felix had for successor Portius Festus. And Felix being willing to show the Jews a pleasure, left Paul bound.

ACTS 25

25:1  LA: Festus ergo cum venisset in provinciam, post triduum ascendit Jerosolymam a Cæsarea.
      EN: Now when Festus had come into the province, after three days, he went up to Jerusalem from Cæsarea.

25:2  LA: Adieruntque eum principes sacerdotum et primi Judæorum adversus Paulum: et rogabant eum,
      EN: And the chief priests and principal men of the Jews went to him against Paul: and they begged him,

25:3  LA: postulantes gratiam adversus eum, ut juberet perduci eum in Jerusalem, insidias tendentes ut interficerent eum in via.
      EN: Requesting favor against him, that he would command him to be brought to Jerusalem, laying wait to kill him in the way.

25:4  LA: Festus autem respondit servari Paulum in Cæsarea: se autem maturius profecturum.
      EN: But Festus answered: That Paul was kept in Cæsarea: and that he himself would very shortly depart there.

25:5  LA: Qui ergo in vobis, ait, potentes sunt, descendentes simul, si quod est in viro crimen, accusent eum.
      EN: Let them, therefore, says he, among you that are able, go down with me and accuse him, if there be any crime in the man.

25:6  LA: Demoratus autem inter eos dies non amplius quam octo aut decem, descendit Cæsaream, et altera die sedit pro tribunali, et jussit Paulum adduci.
      EN: And having tarried among them no more than eight or ten days, he went down to Cæsarea. And the next day, he sat in the judgment seat and commanded Paul to be brought.

25:7  LA: Qui cum perductus esset, circumsteterunt eum, qui ab Jerosolyma descenderant Judæi, multas et graves causas objicientes, quas non poterant probare:
      EN: Who being brought, the Jews stood about him, who had come down from Jerusalem, objecting many and grievious causes, which they could not prove:

25:8  LA: Paulo rationem reddente: Quoniam neque in legem Judæorum, neque in templum, neque in Cæsarem quidquam peccavi.
      EN: Paul making answer for himself: Neither against the law of the Jews, nor against the temple, nor against Caesar, have I offended in anything.

25:9  LA: Festus autem volens gratiam præstare Judæis, respondens Paulo, dixit: Vis Jerosolymam ascendere, et ibi de his judicari apud me?
      EN: But Festus, willing to show the Jews a pleasure, answering Paul, said: Will you go up to Jerusalem and there be judged of these things before me?

25:10  LA: Dixit autem Paulus: Ad tribunal Cæsaris sto: ibi me oportet judicari: Judæis non nocui, sicut tu melius nosti.
       EN: Then Paul said: I stand at Caesar’s judgment seat, where I ought to be judged. To the Jews I have done no injury, as you very well know.

25:11  LA: Si enim nocui, aut dignum morte aliquid feci, non recuso mori: si vero nihil est eorum quæ hi accusant me, nemo potest me illis donare. Cæsarem appello.
       EN: For if I have injured them or have committed anything worthy of death, I refuse not to die. But if there be none of these things whereof they accuse me, no one may deliver me to them. I appeal to Caesar.

25:12  LA: Tunc Festus cum concilio locutus, respondit: Cæsarem appellasti? ad Cæsarem ibis.
       EN: Then Festus, having conferred with the council, answered: Have you appealed to Caesar? To Caesar shall you go.

25:13  LA: Et cum dies aliquot transacti essent, Agrippa rex et Bernice descenderunt Cæsaream ad salutandum Festum.
       EN: And after some days, king Agrippa and Bernice came down to Cæsarea, to greet Festus.

25:14  LA: Et cum dies plures ibi demorarentur, Festus regi indicavit de Paulo, dicens: Vir quidam est derelictus a Felice vinctus,
       EN: And as they tarried there many days, Festus told the king of Paul, saying: A certain man was left prisoner by Felix.

25:15  LA: de quo cum essem Jerosolymis, adierunt me principes sacerdotum et seniores Judæorum, postulantes adversus illum damnationem.
       EN: About whom, when I was at Jerusalem, the chief priests and the ancients of the Jews came to me, desiring condemnation against him.

25:16  LA: Ad quos respondi: Quia non est Romanis consuetudo damnare aliquem hominem priusquam is qui accusatur præsentes habeat accusatores, locumque defendendi accipiat ad abluenda crimina.
       EN: To whom I answered: it is not the custom of the Romans to condemn any man, before that he who is accused have his accusers present and have liberty to make his answer, to clear himself of the things laid to his charge.

25:17  LA: Cum ergo huc convenissent sine ulla dilatione, sequenti die sedens pro tribunali, jussi adduci virum.
       EN: When therefore they had come here, without any delay, on the day following, sitting in the judgment seat, I commanded the man to be brought.

25:18  LA: De quo, cum stetissent accusatores, nullam causam deferebant, de quibus ego suspicabar malum.
       EN: Against whom, when the accusers stood up, they brought no accusation of this which I thought ill of:

25:19  LA: Quæstiones vero quasdam de sua superstitione habebant adversus eum, et de quodam Jesu defuncto, quem affirmabat Paulus vivere.
       EN: But had certain questions of their own superstition against him, and of one Jesus deceased, whom Paul affirmed to be alive.

25:20  LA: Hæsitans autem ego de hujusmodi quæstione, dicebam si vellet ire Jerosolymam, et ibi judicari de istis.
       EN: I therefore being in a doubt of this manner of question, asked him whether he would go to Jerusalem and there be judged of these things.

25:21  LA: Paulo autem appellante ut servaretur ad Augusti cognitionem, jussi servari eum, donec mittam eum ad Cæsarem.
       EN: But Paul, appealing to be reserved to the hearing of Augustus, I commanded him to be kept, until I might send him to Caesar.

25:22  LA: Agrippa autem dixit ad Festum: Volebam et ipse hominem audire. Cras, inquit, audies eum.
       EN: And Agrippa said to Festus: I would also hear the man, myself. To-next day, said he, you shall hear him.

25:23  LA: Altera autem die cum venisset Agrippa et Bernice cum multa ambitione, et introissent in auditorium cum tribunis et viris principalibus civitatis, jubente Festo, adductus est Paulus.
       EN: And on the next day, when Agrippa and Bernice had come with great pomp and had entered into the hall of audience with the tribunes and principal men of the city, at Festus’ commandment, Paul was brought forth.

25:24  LA: Et dicit Festus: Agrippa rex, et omnes qui simul adestis nobiscum viri, videtis hunc de quo omnis multitudo Judæorum interpellavit me Jerosolymis, petentes et acclamantes non oportere eum vivere amplius.
       EN: And Festus says: King Agrippa and all you men who are here present with us, you see this man, about whom all the multitude of the Jews dealt with me at Jerusalem, requesting and crying out that he ought not to live any longer.

25:25  LA: Ego vere comperi nihil dignum morte eum admisisse. Ipso autem hoc appellante ad Augustum, judicavi mittere.
       EN: Yet have I found nothing that he has committed worthy of death. But since he himself has appealed to Augustus, I have determined to send him.

25:26  LA: De quo quid certum scribam domino, non habeo. Propter quod produxi eum ad vos, et maxime ad te, rex Agrippa, ut interrogatione facta habeam quid scribam.
       EN: Of whom I have nothing certain to write to my lord. For which cause, I have brought him forth before you, and especially before you, O king Agrippa, that, examination being made, I may have what to write.

25:27  LA: Sine ratione enim mihi videtur mittere vinctum, et causas ejus non significare.
       EN: For it seems to me unreasonable to send a prisoner and not to signify the things laid to his charge.

ACTS 26

26:1  LA: Agrippa vero ad Paulum ait: Permittitur tibi loqui pro temetipso. Tunc Paulus extenta manu cœpit rationem reddere:
      EN: Then Agrippa said to Paul: You are permitted to speak for yourself. Then Paul, stretching forth his hand, began to make his answer.

26:2  LA: De omnibus quibus accusor a Judæis, rex Agrippa, æstimo me beatum apud te cum sim defensurus me hodie,
      EN: I think myself happy, O king Agrippa, that I am to answer for myself this day before you, touching all the things whereof I am accused by the Jews.

26:3  LA: maxime te sciente omnia, et quæ apud Judæos sunt consuetudines et quæstiones: propter quod obsecro patienter me audias.
      EN: Especially as you know all, both customs and questions, that are among the Jews. Therefore I implore you to hear me patiently.

26:4  LA: Et quidem vitam meam a juventute, quæ ab initio fuit in gente mea in Jerosolymis, noverunt omnes Judæi:
      EN: And my life indeed from my youth, which was from the beginning among my own nation in Jerusalem, all the Jews do know:

26:5  LA: præscientes me ab initio (si velint testimonium perhibere) quoniam secundum certissimam sectam nostræ religionis vixi pharisæus.
      EN: Having known me from the beginning (if they will give testimony) that according to the most sure sect of our religion I lived, a Pharisee.

26:6  LA: Et nunc, in spe quæ ad patres nostros repromissionis facta est a Deo, sto judicio subjectus:
      EN: And now for the hope of the promise that was made by God to the fathers, do I stand subject to judgment:

26:7  LA: in quam duodecim tribus nostræ nocte ac die deservientes, sperant devenire. De qua spe accusor a Judæis, rex.
      EN: To which, our twelve tribes, serving night and day, hope to come. For which hope, O king, I am accused by the Jews.

26:8  LA: Quid incredibile judicatur apud vos, si Deus mortuos suscitat?
      EN: Why should it be thought a thing incredible that God should raise the dead?

26:9  LA: Et ego quidem existimaveram me adversus nomen Jesu Nazareni debere multa contraria agere,
      EN: And I indeed did formerly think that I ought to do many things contrary to the name of Jesus of Nazareth.

26:10  LA: quod et feci Jerosolymis, et multos sanctorum ego in carceribus inclusi, a principibus sacerdotum potestate accepta: et cum occiderentur, detuli sententiam.

ACTS 27

      EN: And the day following, we came to Sidon. And Julius, treating Paul courteously, permitted him to go to his friends and to take care of himself.

      EN: And there, the centurion, finding a ship of Alexandria sailing into Italy, removed us into it.

      EN: And with much ado sailing by it, we came into a certain place, which is called Good-havens, near to which was the city of Thalassa.

27:9  LA: Multo autem tempore peracto, et cum jam non esset tuta navigatio eo quod et jejunium jam præteriisset, consolabatur eos Paulus,

       EN: But the centurion believed the pilot and the master of the ship, more than those things which were said by Paul.

       EN: And the south wind gently blowing, thinking that they had obtained their purpose, when they had loosed from Asson, they sailed close by Crete.

       EN: But not long after, there arose against it a tempestuous wind, called Euroaquilo.

       EN: And when the ship was caught and could not bear up against the wind, giving up the ship to the winds, we were driven.

       EN: And the third day they cast out with their own hands the tackling of the ship.
