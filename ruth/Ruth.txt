RUTH 1

1:1  LA: In diebus unius judicis, quando judices præerant, facta est fames in terra. Abiitque homo de Bethlehem Juda, ut peregrinaretur in regione Moabitide cum uxore sua ac duobus liberis.
     EN: In the days of one of the judges, when judges ruled, a famine arose in the land. A man from Bethlehem in Judah went to live as a foreigner in the region of Moab with his wife and two children.

1:2  LA: Ipse vocabatur Elimelech, et uxor ejus Noëmi : et duo filii, alter Mahalon, et alter Chelion, Ephrathæi de Bethlehem Juda. Ingressique regionem Moabitidem, morabantur ibi.
     EN: His name was Elimelech, and his wife's name Naomi. His two sons were named Mahlon and Chilion. They were Ephrathites from Bethlehem in Judah. They entered the region of Moab and stayed there.

1:3  LA: Et mortuus est Elimelech maritus Noëmi : remansitque ipsa cum filiis.
     EN: Elimelech, Naomi's husband, died, and she was left with her sons.

1:4  LA: Qui acceperunt uxores Moabitidas, quarum una vocabatur Orpha, altera vero Ruth. Manseruntque ibi decem annis,
     EN: They took Moabite wives, one named Orpah and the other Ruth. They remained there ten years,

1:5  LA: et ambo mortui sunt, Mahalon videlicet et Chelion : remansitque mulier orbata duobus liberis ac marito.
     EN: and both died, Mahlon and Chilion. The woman was left bereaved of her two children and her husband.

1:6  LA: Et surrexit ut in patriam pergeret cum utraque nuru sua de regione Moabitide : audierat enim quod respexisset Dominus populum suum, et dedisset eis escas.
     EN: She arose to leave the region of Moab and return to her homeland with both her daughters-in-law, for she had heard that the Lord had looked upon His people and given them food.

1:7  LA: Egressa est itaque de loco peregrinationis suæ, cum utraque nuru : et jam in via revertendi posita in terram Juda,
     EN: So she left the place where she had lived as a foreigner with both her daughters-in-law. Once they were on the road back to the land of Judah,

1:8  LA: dixit ad eas : Ite in domum matris vestræ : faciat vobiscum Dominus misericordiam, sicut fecistis cum mortuis, et mecum.
     EN: she said to them, “Go to your mothers' homes. May the Lord show you mercy, as you have shown it to the dead and to me.

1:9  LA: Det vobis invenire requiem in domibus virorum quos sortituræ estis. Et osculata est eas. Quæ elevata voce flere cœperunt,
     EN: May He grant you rest in the homes of the husbands who will be yours.” She kissed them, and they raised their voices and began to weep,

1:10  LA: et dicere : Tecum pergemus ad populum tuum.
      EN: saying, “We will go with you to your people.”

1:11  LA: Quibus illa respondit : Revertimini, filiæ meæ, cur venitis mecum ? num ultra habeo filios in utero meo, ut viros ex me sperare possitis ?
      EN: She answered them, “Return, my daughters. Why are you coming with me? Do I still have sons in my womb, so that you can hope for husbands from me?

1:12  LA: Revertimini, filiæ meæ, et abite : jam enim senectute confecta sum, nec apta vinculo conjugali : etiamsi possem hac nocte concipere, et parere filios,
      EN: Return, my daughters, and go. I am already worn out with age and no longer fit for the bond of marriage. Even if I could conceive this very night and bear sons,

1:13  LA: si eos expectare velitis donec crescant, et annos pubertatis impleant, ante eritis vetulæ quam nubatis. Nolite, quæso, filiæ meæ : quia vestra angustia magis me premit, et egressa est manus Domini contra me.
      EN: and you were willing to wait for them until they grew up and reached the years of maturity, you would be old women before you married. Please, my daughters, do not do this, for your distress weighs more heavily upon me, and the hand of the Lord has gone out against me.”

1:14  LA: Elevata igitur voce, rursum flere cœperunt : Orpha osculata est socrum, ac reversa est ; Ruth adhæsit socrui suæ :
      EN: They raised their voices and began to weep again. Orpah kissed her mother-in-law and returned, but Ruth clung to her mother-in-law.

1:15  LA: cui dixit Noëmi : En reversa est cognata tua ad populum suum, et ad deos suos, vade cum ea.
      EN: Naomi said to her, “See, your sister-in-law has returned to her people and her gods. Go with her.”

1:16  LA: Quæ respondit : Ne adverseris mihi ut relinquam te et abeam : quocumque enim perrexeris, pergam, et ubi morata fueris, et ego pariter morabor. Populus tuus populus meus, et Deus tuus Deus meus.
      EN: She answered, “Do not oppose me by making me leave you and go away. Wherever you go, I will go, and wherever you stay, I too will stay. Your people are my people, and your God my God.

1:17  LA: Quæ te terra morientem susceperit, in ea moriar : ibique locum accipiam sepulturæ. Hæc mihi faciat Dominus, et hæc addat, si non sola mors me et te separaverit.
      EN: In the land that receives you when you die, I will die, and there I will receive a burial place. May the Lord do these things to me, and add these things also, if anything but death separates you and me.”

1:18  LA: Videns ergo Noëmi quod obstinato animo Ruth decrevisset secum pergere, adversari noluit, nec ad suos ultra reditum persuadere :
      EN: Seeing that Ruth had firmly resolved to go with her, Naomi would not oppose her or persuade her any further to return to her own people.

1:19  LA: profectæque sunt simul, et venerunt in Bethlehem. Quibus urbem ingressis, velox apud cunctos fama percrebruit : dicebantque mulieres : Hæc est illa Noëmi.
      EN: They traveled together and came to Bethlehem. When they entered the city, the news quickly spread among everyone, and the women said, “This is that Naomi.”

1:20  LA: Quibus ait : Ne vocetis me Noëmi (id est, pulchram), sed vocate me Mara (id est, amaram), quia amaritudine valde replevit me Omnipotens.
      EN: She said to them, “Do not call me Naomi—that is, beautiful—but call me Mara—that is, bitter—for the Almighty has filled me with great bitterness.

1:21  LA: Egressa sum plena, et vacuam reduxit me Dominus. Cur ergo vocatis me Noëmi, quam Dominus humiliavit, et afflixit Omnipotens ?
      EN: I went out full, and the Lord has brought me back empty. Why, then, do you call me Naomi, when the Lord has humbled me and the Almighty has afflicted me?”

1:22  LA: Venit ergo Noëmi cum Ruth Moabitide nuru sua, de terra peregrinationis suæ : ac reversa est in Bethlehem, quando primum hordea metebantur.
      EN: So Naomi came from the land where she had lived as a foreigner with Ruth the Moabite, her daughter-in-law. She returned to Bethlehem as the barley harvest was beginning.

RUTH 2

2:1  LA: Erat autem viro Elimelech consanguineus, homo potens, et magnarum opum, nomine Booz.
     EN: Her husband Elimelech had a kinsman named Boaz, a powerful man of great wealth.

2:2  LA: Dixitque Ruth Moabitis ad socrum suam : Si jubes, vadam in agrum, et colligam spicas quæ fugerint manus metentium, ubicumque clementis in me patrisfamilias reperero gratiam. Cui illa respondit : Vade, filia mea.
     EN: Ruth the Moabite said to her mother-in-law, “If you permit it, I will go into the field and gather the ears of grain that escape the reapers' hands, wherever I find favor with a landowner who is kind to me.” She answered, “Go, my daughter.”

2:3  LA: Abiit itaque et colligebat spicas post terga metentium. Accidit autem ut ager ille haberet dominum nomine Booz, qui erat de cognatione Elimelech.
     EN: So she went and gathered ears of grain behind the reapers. It happened that the owner of that field was named Boaz, who was of Elimelech's family.

2:4  LA: Et ecce, ipse veniebat de Bethlehem, dixitque messoribus : Dominus vobiscum. Qui responderunt ei : Benedicat tibi Dominus.
     EN: Just then he came from Bethlehem and said to the reapers, “The Lord be with you.” They answered him, “May the Lord bless you.”

2:5  LA: Dixitque Booz juveni, qui messoribus præerat : Cujus est hæc puella ?
     EN: Boaz said to the young man in charge of the reapers, “Whose young woman is this?”

2:6  LA: Cui respondit : Hæc est Moabitis, quæ venit cum Noëmi, de regione Moabitide,
     EN: He answered, “She is the Moabite who came with Naomi from the region of Moab.

2:7  LA: et rogavit ut spicas colligeret remanentes, sequens messorum vestigia : et de mane usque nunc stat in agro, et ne ad momentum quidem domum reversa est.
     EN: She asked to gather the ears left behind, following in the reapers' footsteps. She has stood in the field from morning until now and has not returned home even for a moment.”

2:8  LA: Et ait Booz ad Ruth : Audi, filia, ne vadas in alterum agrum ad colligendum, nec recedas ab hoc loco : sed jungere puellis meis,
     EN: Boaz said to Ruth, “Listen, my daughter. Do not go into another field to gather grain, and do not leave this place. Stay with my young women,

2:9  LA: et ubi messuerint, sequere. Mandavi enim pueris meis, ut nemo molestus sit tibi : sed etiam si sitieris, vade ad sarcinulas, et bibe aquas, de quibus et pueri bibunt.
     EN: and follow wherever they reap. I have commanded my young men that no one is to trouble you. And if you are thirsty, go to the supplies and drink the water that the young men drink.”

2:10  LA: Quæ cadens in faciem suam et adorans super terram, dixit ad eum : Unde mihi hoc, ut invenirem gratiam ante oculos tuos, et nosse me dignareris peregrinam mulierem ?
      EN: She fell upon her face and bowed to the ground, saying to him, “How have I found favor in your eyes, that you should deign to notice me, a foreign woman?”

2:11  LA: Cui ille respondit : Nuntiata sunt mihi omnia quæ feceris socrui tuæ post mortem viri tui : et quod reliqueris parentes tuos, et terram in qua nata es, et veneris ad populum, quem antea nesciebas.
      EN: He answered, “Everything you have done for your mother-in-law after your husband's death has been reported to me: how you left your parents and the land where you were born and came to a people you did not know before.

2:12  LA: Reddat tibi Dominus pro opere tuo, et plenam mercedem recipias a Domino Deo Israël, ad quem venisti, et sub cujus confugisti alas.
      EN: May the Lord repay you for your work, and may you receive a full reward from the Lord, the God of Israel, to whom you have come and beneath whose wings you have taken refuge.”

2:13  LA: Quæ ait : Inveni gratiam apud oculos tuos, domine mi, qui consolatus es me, et locutus es ad cor ancillæ tuæ, quæ non sum similis unius puellarum tuarum.
      EN: She said, “I have found favor in your eyes, my lord, for you have comforted me and spoken to the heart of your servant, though I am not like one of your young women.”

2:14  LA: Dixitque ad eam Booz : Quando hora vescendi fuerit, veni huc, et comede panem, et intinge buccellam tuam in aceto. Sedit itaque ad messorum latus, et congessit polentam sibi, comeditque et saturata est, et tulit reliquias.
      EN: Boaz said to her, “When it is time to eat, come here, eat bread, and dip your morsel in the vinegar.” So she sat beside the reapers and heaped up hulled barley for herself. She ate and was satisfied, and took away what remained.

2:15  LA: Atque inde surrexit, ut spicas ex more colligeret. Præcepit autem Booz pueris suis, dicens : Etiamsi vobiscum metere voluerit, ne prohibeatis eam :
      EN: Then she arose to gather ears of grain as usual. Boaz commanded his young men, “Even if she wishes to reap with you, do not prevent her.

2:16  LA: et de vestris quoque manipulis projicite de industria, et remanere permittite, ut absque rubore colligat, et colligentem nemo corripiat.
      EN: Deliberately cast some from your own sheaves and let it remain, so that she may gather without shame. Let no one rebuke her as she gathers.”

2:17  LA: Collegit ergo in agro usque ad vesperam : et quæ collegerat virga cædens et excutiens, invenit hordei quasi ephi mensuram, id est, tres modios.
      EN: She gathered in the field until evening. Beating and shaking out what she had gathered with a rod, she found about an ephah of barley, that is, three modii.

2:18  LA: Quos portans reversa est in civitatem, et ostendit socrui suæ : insuper protulit, et dedit ei de reliquiis cibi sui, quo saturata fuerat.
      EN: She carried them back into the city and showed her mother-in-law. She also brought out and gave her what remained of the food with which she had been satisfied.

2:19  LA: Dixitque ei socrus sua : Ubi hodie collegisti, et ubi fecisti opus ? sit benedictus qui misertus est tui. Indicavitque ei apud quem fuisset operata : et nomen dixit viri, quod Booz vocaretur.
      EN: Her mother-in-law said to her, “Where did you gather today, and where did you work? Blessed be the one who had mercy on you.” She told her with whom she had worked, and said that the man's name was Boaz.

2:20  LA: Cui respondit Noëmi : Benedictus sit a Domino : quoniam eamdem gratiam, quam præbuerat vivis, servavit et mortuis. Rursumque ait : Propinquus noster est homo.
      EN: Naomi answered her, “May he be blessed by the Lord, for he has kept toward the dead the same kindness he showed to the living.” She added, “The man is our kinsman.”

2:21  LA: Et ait Ruth : Hoc quoque, inquit, præcepit mihi, ut tamdiu messoribus ejus jungerer, donec omnes segetes meterentur.
      EN: Ruth said, “He also commanded me to stay with his reapers until all the crops are harvested.”

2:22  LA: Cui dixit socrus : Melius est, filia mea, ut cum puellis ejus exeas ad metendum, ne in alieno agro quispiam resistat tibi.
      EN: Her mother-in-law said, “It is better, my daughter, for you to go out to reap with his young women, so that no one opposes you in another field.”

2:23  LA: Juncta est itaque puellis Booz : et tamdiu cum eis messuit, donec hordea et triticum in horreis conderentur.
      EN: So she stayed with Boaz's young women and reaped with them until the barley and wheat were stored in the granaries.

RUTH 3

3:1  LA: Postquam autem reversa est ad socrum suam, audivit ab ea : Filia mea, quæram tibi requiem, et providebo ut bene sit tibi.
     EN: After she returned to her mother-in-law, she heard her say, “My daughter, I will seek rest for you and provide for your well-being.

3:2  LA: Booz iste, cujus puellis in agro juncta es, propinquus noster est, et hac nocte aream hordei ventilat.
     EN: This Boaz, whose young women you joined in the field, is our kinsman. Tonight he is winnowing barley on the threshing floor.

3:3  LA: Lavare igitur, et ungere, et induere cultioribus vestimentis, et descende in aream : non te videat homo, donec esum potumque finierit.
     EN: Wash, anoint yourself, put on finer clothes, and go down to the threshing floor. Do not let the man see you until he has finished eating and drinking.

3:4  LA: Quando autem ierit ad dormiendum, nota locum in quo dormiat : veniesque et discooperies pallium, quo operitur a parte pedum, et projicies te, et ibi jacebis : ipse autem dicet quid agere debeas.
     EN: When he goes to sleep, note the place where he lies. Then go, uncover the cloak covering his feet, and lie down there. He himself will tell you what you should do.”

3:5  LA: Quæ respondit : Quidquid præceperis, faciam.
     EN: She answered, “I will do whatever you command.”

3:6  LA: Descenditque in aream, et fecit omnia quæ sibi imperaverat socrus.
     EN: She went down to the threshing floor and did everything her mother-in-law had commanded her.

3:7  LA: Cumque comedisset Booz, et bibisset, et factus esset hilarior, issetque ad dormiendum juxta acervum manipulorum, venit abscondite, et discooperto pallio, a pedibus ejus se projecit.
     EN: When Boaz had eaten and drunk and become cheerful, he went to sleep beside the heap of sheaves. She came secretly, uncovered the cloak, and lay down at his feet.

3:8  LA: Et ecce, nocte jam media expavit homo, et conturbatus est : viditque mulierem jacentem ad pedes suos,
     EN: In the middle of the night the man was startled and disturbed, and saw a woman lying at his feet.

3:9  LA: et ait illi : Quæ es ? Illaque respondit : Ego sum Ruth ancilla tua : expande pallium tuum super famulam tuam, quia propinquus es.
     EN: He said to her, “Who are you?” She answered, “I am Ruth, your servant. Spread your cloak over your servant, for you are a kinsman.”

3:10  LA: Et ille : Benedicta, inquit, es a Domino, filia, et priorem misericordiam posteriore superasti : quia non est secuta juvenes, pauperes sive divites.
      EN: He said, “May you be blessed by the Lord, my daughter. You have surpassed your earlier kindness with this later kindness, for you have not gone after young men, whether poor or rich.

3:11  LA: Noli ergo metuere, sed quidquid dixeris mihi, faciam tibi. Scit enim omnis populus, qui habitat intra portas urbis meæ, mulierem te esse virtutis.
      EN: Do not be afraid. Whatever you tell me, I will do for you, for all the people who dwell within the gates of my city know that you are a woman of virtue.

3:12  LA: Nec abnuo me propinquum, sed est alius me propinquior.
      EN: I do not deny that I am a kinsman, but there is another more closely related than I.

3:13  LA: Quiesce hac nocte : et facto mane, si te voluerit propinquitatis jure retinere, bene res acta est : sin autem ille noluerit, ego te absque ulla dubitatione suscipiam, vivit Dominus. Dormi usque mane.
      EN: Rest tonight. In the morning, if he wishes to take you by right of kinship, the matter is well settled. But if he is unwilling, I will certainly take you myself, as the Lord lives. Sleep until morning.”

3:14  LA: Dormivit itaque ad pedes ejus, usque ad noctis abscessum. Surrexit itaque antequam homines se cognoscerent mutuo, et dixit Booz : Cave ne quis noverit quod huc veneris.
      EN: She slept at his feet until the night passed. Then she arose before people could recognize one another, and Boaz said, “Take care that no one knows you came here.”

3:15  LA: Et rursum : Expande, inquit, pallium tuum, quo operiris, et tene utraque manu. Qua extendente, et tenente, mensus est sex modios hordei, et posuit super eam. Quæ portans ingressa est civitatem,
      EN: He added, “Spread out the cloak you are wearing and hold it with both hands.” As she spread it out and held it, he measured six modii of barley and placed them upon her. Carrying them, she entered the city

3:16  LA: et venit ad socrum suam. Quæ dixit ei : Quid egisti, filia ? Narravitque ei omnia, quæ sibi fecisset homo.
      EN: and came to her mother-in-law, who said to her, “What did you do, my daughter?” She told her everything the man had done for her,

3:17  LA: Et ait : Ecce sex modios hordei dedit mihi, et ait : Nolo vacuam te reverti ad socrum tuam.
      EN: and said, “See, he gave me six modii of barley and said, ‘I do not want you to return to your mother-in-law empty-handed.’”

3:18  LA: Dixitque Noëmi : Expecta, filia, donec videamus quem res exitum habeat : neque enim cessabit homo, nisi compleverit quod locutus est.
      EN: Naomi said, “Wait, my daughter, until we see how the matter turns out. The man will not rest until he has fulfilled what he said.”

RUTH 4

4:1  LA: Ascendit ergo Booz ad portam, et sedit ibi. Cumque vidisset propinquum præterire, de quo prius sermo habitus est, dixit ad eum : Declina paulisper, et sede hic : vocans eum nomine suo. Qui divertit, et sedit.
     EN: Boaz went up to the gate and sat there. When he saw the kinsman they had spoken about passing by, he called him by name and said, “Turn aside a moment and sit here.” He turned aside and sat down.

4:2  LA: Tollens autem Booz decem viros de senioribus civitatis, dixit ad eos : Sedete hic.
     EN: Boaz took ten men from among the city's elders and said to them, “Sit here.”

4:3  LA: Quibus sedentibus, locutus est ad propinquum : Partem agri fratris nostri Elimelech vendet Noëmi, quæ reversa est de regione Moabitide :
     EN: When they were seated, he said to the kinsman, “Naomi, who has returned from the region of Moab, will sell the portion of the field belonging to our brother Elimelech.

4:4  LA: quod audire te volui, et tibi dicere coram cunctis sedentibus, et majoribus natu de populo meo. Si vis possidere jure propinquitatis, eme, et posside : sin autem displicet tibi, hoc ipsum indica mihi, ut sciam quid facere debeam : nullus enim est propinquus, excepto te, qui prior es, et me, qui secundus sum. At ille respondit : Ego agrum emam.
     EN: I wanted you to hear this and to tell you in the presence of all who sit here and the elders of my people. If you wish to possess it by right of kinship, buy it and take possession. But if this does not please you, tell me, so that I may know what I should do. For there is no kinsman except you, who are first, and me, who am second.” He answered, “I will buy the field.”

4:5  LA: Cui dixit Booz : Quando emeris agrum de manu mulieris, Ruth quoque Moabitidem, quæ uxor defuncti fuit, debes accipere : ut suscites nomen propinqui tui in hæreditate sua.
     EN: Boaz said to him, “When you buy the field from the woman's hand, you must also take Ruth the Moabite, who was the dead man's wife, to raise up your kinsman's name in his inheritance.”

4:6  LA: Qui respondit : Cedo juri propinquitatis : neque enim posteritatem familiæ meæ delere debeo : tu meo utere privilegio, quo me libenter carere profiteor.
     EN: He answered, “I yield the right of kinship, for I must not destroy the posterity of my family. Use my privilege yourself; I freely declare that I relinquish it.”

4:7  LA: Hic autem erat mos antiquitus in Israël inter propinquos, ut siquando alter alteri suo juri cedebat, ut esset firma concessio, solvebat homo calceamentum suum, et dabat proximo suo : hoc erat testimonium cessionis in Israël.
     EN: This was the custom in Israel in former times between kinsmen: whenever one yielded his right to another, the man would remove his sandal and give it to his neighbor to confirm the grant. This was the testimony of a transfer in Israel.

4:8  LA: Dixit ergo propinquo suo Booz : Tolle calceamentum tuum. Quod statim solvit de pede suo.
     EN: So Boaz said to his kinsman, “Remove your sandal.” He immediately took it off his foot.

4:9  LA: At ille majoribus natu, et universo populo : Testes vos, inquit, estis hodie, quod possederim omnis quæ fuerunt Elimelech, et Chelion, et Mahalon, tradente Noëmi ;
     EN: Boaz said to the elders and all the people, “You are witnesses today that I have taken possession of everything that belonged to Elimelech, Chilion, and Mahlon, with Naomi handing it over,

4:10  LA: et Ruth Moabitidem, uxorem Mahalon, in conjugium sumpserim, ut suscitem nomen defuncti in hæreditate sua, ne vocabulum ejus de familia sua ac fratribus et populo deleatur. Vos, inquam, hujus rei testes estis.
      EN: and that I have taken Ruth the Moabite, Mahlon's wife, in marriage, to raise up the dead man's name in his inheritance, so that his name may not be erased from his family, his brothers, and his people. You, I say, are witnesses of this.”

4:11  LA: Respondit omnis populus, qui erat in porta, et majores natu : Nos testes sumus : faciat Dominus hanc mulierem, quæ ingreditur domum tuam, sicut Rachel et Liam, quæ ædificaverunt domum Israël : ut sit exemplum virtutis in Ephratha, et habeat celebre nomen in Bethlehem :
      EN: All the people at the gate and the elders answered, “We are witnesses. May the Lord make this woman who enters your house like Rachel and Leah, who built the house of Israel, so that she may be an example of virtue in Ephrathah and have a renowned name in Bethlehem.

4:12  LA: fiatque domus tua sicut domus Phares, quem Thamar peperit Judæ, de semine quod tibi dederit Dominus ex hac puella.
      EN: May your house become like the house of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah, through the offspring the Lord gives you by this young woman.”

4:13  LA: Tulit itaque Booz Ruth, et accepit uxorem : ingressusque est ad eam, et dedit illi Dominus ut conciperet, et pareret filium.
      EN: So Boaz took Ruth and received her as his wife. He went in to her, and the Lord granted her to conceive and bear a son.

4:14  LA: Dixeruntque mulieres ad Noëmi : Benedictus Dominus, qui non est passus ut deficeret successor familiæ tuæ, et vocaretur nomen ejus in Israël :
      EN: The women said to Naomi, “Blessed be the Lord, who has not allowed your family to lack a successor. May his name be spoken in Israel,

4:15  LA: et habeas qui consoletur animam tuam, et enutriat senectutem : de nuru enim tua natus est, quæ te diligit, et multo tibi melior est, quam si septem haberes filios.
      EN: and may you have one to comfort your soul and nourish your old age. For he was born of your daughter-in-law who loves you, who is far better to you than if you had seven sons.”

4:16  LA: Susceptumque Noëmi puerum posuit in sinu suo, et nutricis ac gerulæ fungebatur officio.
      EN: Naomi took the child, laid him in her lap, and performed the duties of nurse and caregiver.

4:17  LA: Vicinæ autem mulieris congratulantes ei, et dicentes : Natus est filius Noëmi : vocaverunt nomen ejus Obed : hic est pater Isai, patris David.
